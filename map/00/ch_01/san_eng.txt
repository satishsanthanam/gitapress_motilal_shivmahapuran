### **CHAPTER ONE - (*Greatness of Śivapurāṇa<sup>*</sup>*) - **

[fn *: The Chapters (1-7) on the glory of Śivapurāṇa are taken from Skandapurāṇa.]

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
हे हे सूत महाप्राज्ञ सर्वसिद्धान्तवित् प्रभो ।
आख्याहि मे कथासारं पुराणानां विशेषतः ॥ १॥

English
*Śaunaka*<sup>1</sup> *said* :—  O Sūta of great intellect, O my lord, the knower of all Philosophical principles, please narrate to me the essence of the Purāṇas in detail. (1.)

[fn 1: Śaunaka was the chief of the sages at the great sacrifice in Naimisa forest to whom the Mahābhārata and the Purāṇas were recited by the Sūta in the reign of Adhisīmakṛṣṇa, the great-grandson of Janamejaya and the sixth in generation from Arjuna in the Paurava line. —Vā 1.12; 99, 255-8; Padma I. 1. 19.]

---

Sanskrit
सदाचारश्च सद्भक्तिर्विवेको वर्धते कथम् ।
स्वविकारनिरासश्च सज्जनैः क्रियते कथम् ॥ २॥

English
How do good conduct, good devotion and power of discrimination flourish ? How are base feelings dispelled by good men ? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवाश्चासुरतां प्राप्ताः प्रायो घोरे कलाविह ।
तस्य संशोधने किं हि विद्यते परमायनम् ॥ ३॥

English
In this terrible Kali age all living beings have almost become demoniac in character. What is the effective mode of remedying the same ? (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यदस्ति वस्तु परमं श्रेयसां श्रेय उत्तमम् ।
पावनं पावनानां च साधनं तद्वदाधुना ॥ ४॥

English
Now tell me about the greatest means to achieve the most perfect weal, the holiest of the holy modes. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
येन तत्साधनेनाशु शुध्यत्यात्मा विशेषतः ।
शिवप्राप्तिर्भवेत्तात सदा निर्मलचेतसः ॥ ५॥

English
What is that, the practice of which particularly purifies the soul ? What is that which enables a man of unsullied mind to attain Śiva ? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
धन्यस्त्वं मुनिशार्दूल श्रवणप्रीतिलालसः ।
अतो विचार्य सुधिया वच्मि शास्त्रं महोत्तमम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Sūta*<sup>2</sup> *said* :— O foremost among sages, you are blessed indeed as you are desirous of hearing. Hence I shall ponder over the greatest of the Sacred lore intelligently and tell you. (6.)

[fn 2: The Sūtas (Vā I. 32-3; Padma I. I. 27-28) preserved the genealogies of Gods, sages, and glorious monarchs as well as the traditions of great men. The Sūta here is not a caste that is described by Manu (X.11.17) as the offspring of a Kṣatriya father and Brahman mother. He is a venerable Brāhmaṇa who has preserved ballads, songs, genealogies of Gods, sages and glorious Kings.—Pargiter : Ancient Indian Historical Tradition Ch. II ; also Pusalkar : Studies in Epics and Purāṇas of India, Intro. P. 29. He is described as the disciple of Vyāsa.—SP. I.4.7.]

शिवतोषकरं दिव्यं श‍ृणु वत्स रसायनम् ॥ ७॥

English
O dear, listen to that divine panacea evolved out of all religious tenets, heightening true devotion and conducive to the pleasure of Śiva. (7.)

English
It is destructive of the great fear of the Python of Kāla (Death). O sage, it is the noble Śiva Purāṇa³ formerly narrated by Śiva Himself. (8.)

[fn 3: For the nomenclature and authenticity of this Purāṇa see Introduction.]


---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमारस्य मुनेरुपदेशात् परादरात् ।
व्यासेनोक्तं तु सङ्क्षेपात् कलिजानां हिताय च ॥ ९॥

English
For the benefit of the people in the age of Kali, the sage Vyāsa⁴ has abridged it out of great respect for the sage Sanatkumāra⁵ on being instructed by him. (9.)

[fn 4. According to the Pauranic tradition, Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana Vyāsa, the son of Satyavati, composed the eighteen purānas or superintended their compilation.—Mat. 53.70.]

[fn 5. The purānas were first compiled by Brahmā (Vā I. 60-61). Sanatkumāra, a son of Brahmā (ŚP I. 4. 8-9; I. 5. 17) inherited them from his father and imparted them to Vyāsa who in turn abridged them in 18 compendiums.]

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मादपरं किञ्चित्पुराणाच्छैवतो मुने ।
न विद्यते मनःशुद्ध्यै कलिजानां विशेषतः ॥ १०॥

English
O sage, there is nothing other than Śiva Purāṇa for the purification of the mind especially of the people of the Kali age.⁶ (10.)

[fn 6. The beginning of the Kali age has been discussed by Dr. Fleet (JRAS, 1911, PP. 479, 675, 686) and he has pointed out that it began on the day on which Lord Kṛṣṇa died, which the chronology of the Mahābhārata places, as he shows, some twenty years after the great battle and that it was then that Yudhiṣṭhira abdicated and Parikṣit began to reign.—Pargiter : Dynasties of the Kali Age.—Intro. P. X.]

Sanskrit
जन्मान्तरे भवेत् पुण्यं महद्यस्य सुधीमतः ।
तस्य प्रीतिर्भवेत्तत्र महाभाग्यवतो मुने ॥ ११॥

English
It is only the intelligent and the highly fortunate man who has accumulated great merits in his previous birth who will be drawn towards it. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छिवपुराणं हि परमं शास्त्रमुत्तमम् ।
शिवरूपं क्षितौ ज्ञेयं सेवनीयं च सर्वथा ॥ १२॥

English
This Śivapurāṇa is the greatest and the noblest of the sacred lore. It is the form of Śiva and as such is to be served and realised in this world. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पठनाच्छ्रवणादस्य भक्तिमान्नरसत्तमः ।
सद्यः शिवपदप्राप्तिं लभते सर्वसाधनात् ॥ १३॥

English
By reading this and listening to it the good man becomes very pious. By all means he instantly attains Śiva's region. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात् सर्वप्रयत्नेन काङ्क्षितं पठनं नृभिः ।
तथास्य श्रवणं प्रेम्णा सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ १४॥

English
Hence every endeavour of men to read this is desirable. Loving care to listen to it yields all desired results. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराणश्रवणाच्छम्भोर्निष्पापो जायते नरः ।
भुक्त्वा भोगान् सुविपुलान् शिवलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥ १५॥

English
By listening to this Purāṇa of Śiva a man becomes sinless. After enjoying all extensive worldly pleasures he will attain the region of Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
राजसूयेन यत्पुण्यमग्निष्टोमशतेन च ।
तत् पुण्यं लभते शम्भोः कथाश्रवणमात्रतः ॥ १६॥

English
Merely by listening to the story of Śiva a man secures that merit which results from the performance of Rājasūya$^{7}$ and a hundred Agniṣṭomas.$^{8}$ (16.)


[fn 7. Rājasūya is a great sacrifice performed by a universal monarch (in which the tributary princes also take part) at the time of his coronation as a mark of his undisputed sovereignty.]

[fn 8. Agniṣṭoma is a sacrificial rite extending over several days in spring and forming an essential part of the Jyotiṣṭoma.]



---

Sanskrit
ये श‍ृण्वन्ति मुने शैवं पुराणं शास्त्रमुत्तमम् ।
ते मनुष्या न मन्तव्या रुद्रा एव न संशयः ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, those who listen to Śivapurāṇa the noblest of Sacred lore, cease to be mere human beings. They must be undoubtedly considered as manifestations of Rudra, a form of Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वतां तत् पुराणं हि तथा कीर्तयतां च तत् ।
पादाम्बुजरजांस्येव तीर्थानि मुनयो विदुः ॥ १८॥

English
Sages consider the dust in the feet of those who habitually listen to that Purāṇa and recite it, on a par with holy centres. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
गन्तुं निःश्रेयसं स्थानं येऽभिवाञ्छन्ति देहिनः ।

English
May those who wish to attain the seat of salvation, listen always to the holy Śivapurāṇa with great devotion. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा श्रोतुं यद्यशक्तो भवेत् स मुनिसत्तम ।
नियतात्मा प्रतिदिनं श‍ृणुयाद्वा मुहूर्तकम् ॥ २०॥

English
O noblest among sages, if he is unable to listen to it always, let him hear it for a short while every day with his mind fully controlled. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

पुण्यमासादिषु मुने श‍ृणुयाच्छिवपुराणकम् ॥ २१॥

English
If any one is unable to listen to it every day, O sage, let him listen to Śivapurāṇa in the holy months. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Those who listen to that Purāṇa even for a Muhūrta (48 minutes), half that period, one fourth of that period or even for a moment will not suffer from mishaps. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O lord of sages, the man who listens to that Purāṇa crosses the ocean of worldly existence after burning the great forest of Karma (binding actions). (23.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, the merit that accrues from all gifts and all Sacrifices becomes stabilised after listening to Śivapurāṇa. (24.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Particularly in the age of Kali there is no greater virtue conducive to the achievement of liberation by men, O sage, than listening to Śivapurāṇa. (25.)

---

Sanskrit

English

[fn 9. Kalpadruma is a mythological tree supposed to grant all desires.]
---

Sanskrit

English
For the benefit of the evil-minded persons of the Kali age, bereft of virtuous conduct, Lord Śiva has produced the nectar in the form of Śivapurāṇa. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
The sanctifying story of Śivapurāṇa must always be resorted to, definitely so. (29.)

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

[fn 10. In Vedic times the Aśvamedha sacrifice was performed by kings desirous of offspring but subsequently it was performed by them for the achievement of universal supremacy. A horse was turned loose to wander at will for a year, attended by a guardian; when the horse entered a foreign country, the ruler was bound either to submit or to fight. In this way the horse returned at the end of a year, the guardian obtaining or enforcing the submission of princes whom he brought in this train. After the successful return of the horse, the horse was sacrificed amidst great rejoicings. It is said that the horse was sometimes not immolated but kept bound during the ceremony.]

[fn 11. Vājapeya is one of the seven forms of the Soma-sacrifice offered by kings or Brāhmans aspiring to the highest position, and preceding the Rājasūya and the Bīhaspatisava.]

Sanskrit
शैवं पुराणममलं शैवसर्वस्वमादरात् ।
सेवनीयं प्रयत्नेन परत्रेह सुखेप्सुना ॥ ४७॥

English
The holy Śivapurāṇa, the sole possession of a devotee of Śiva, should assiduously be resorted to by a person who desires for happiness here and hereafter. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्वर्गप्रदं शैवं पुराणममलं परम् ।
श्रोतव्यं सर्वदा प्रीत्या पठितव्यं विशेषतः ॥ ४८॥

English
The holy Śivapurāṇa that accords the four aims of life (virtue, wealth, love and salvation) must be heard and read with great devotion always. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदेतिहासशास्त्रेषु परं श्रेयस्करं महत् ।
शैवं पुराणं विज्ञेयं सर्वथा हि मुमुक्षुभिः ॥ ४९॥

English
The Śivapurāṇa, the greatest harbinger of the perfect welfare among the Vedas, Itihāsas and other sacred texts must be thoroughly understood by those who seek salvation. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवं पुराणमिदमात्मविदां वरिष्ठं
सेव्यं सदा परमवस्तु सता समर्च्यम् ।
तापत्रयाभिशमनं सुखदं सदैव
प्राणप्रियं विधिहरीशमुखामराणाम् ॥ ५०॥

English
This Śivapurāṇa is the greatest resort of the knowers of Ātman (Spiritual Seekers) for ever; it is the noblest object worthy of adoration of good men ; it suppresses the three types of distresses (*i. e.* physical illness, extraneous attacks and divine calamities) ; it accords happiness always ; and it is very pleasing to all Devas led by Brahmā, Hari and Īśa. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
वन्दे शिवपुराणं हि सर्वदाहं प्रसन्नधीः ।
शिवः प्रसन्नतां यायाद् दद्यात्स्वपदयो रतिम् ॥ ५१॥

English
With the mind extremely delighted I bow unto Śivapurāṇa for ever. May Śiva be pleased and bestow on me a devotion to His feet. (51.)
