### **CHAPTER TWO - (*The liberation of Devarāja*)**

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
सूत सूत महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं परमार्थवित् ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा दिव्या श्राविता कृपया हि नः ॥ १॥

English
*Śaunaka said :—*  O Sūta, thou art the most blessed and the most fortunate knower of the greatest Truth. Thou hast narrated to us, out of great compassion, this divine wonderful tale. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अघौघविध्वंसकरी मनःशुद्धिविधायिनि ।
शिवसन्तोषजननी कथेयं नः श्रुताद्भुता ॥ २॥

English
This wonderful narrative that destroys hosts of sins, purifies the mind, and propitiates Lord Śiva has been heard by us. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्कथासमानं न भुवि किञ्चित् परात्परम् ।
निश्चयेनेति विज्ञातमस्माभिः कृपया तव ॥ ३॥

English
Thanks to thy compassion we have decisively realised that there is nothing so fine and nice as this tale. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
के के विशुध्यन्त्यनया कथया पापिनः कलौ ।
वद तान् कृपया सूत कृतार्थं भुवनं कुरु ॥ ४॥

English
Who are those among sinners in the Kali age who get sanctified by this story ? Please enlighten us. Make the whole world gratified. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
ये मानवाः पापकृतो दुराचाररताः खलाः ।
कामादिनिरता नित्यं तेऽपि शुध्यन्त्यनेन वै ॥ ५॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  Men who habitually commit sins, wicked persons indulging in vicious activities and persons of lecherous disposition become pure hereby. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानयज्ञः परोऽयं वै भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदः सदा ।
शोधनः सर्वपापानां शिवसन्तोषकारकः ॥ ६॥

English
This is a great Jñānayajña (sacrificial rite of wisdom) ; it yields worldly enjoyment as well as salvation ; it dispels all sins and delights Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तृष्णाकुलाः सत्यहीनाः पितृमातृविदूषकाः ।
दाम्भिका हिंसका ये च तेऽपि शुध्यन्त्यनेन वै ॥ ७॥

English
Men overwhelmed by the thirst of covetousness, those devoid of truthfulness, those who decry even their parents, haughty vain fellows and persons prone to violent activities become sanctified by this. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स्ववर्णाश्रमधर्मेभ्यो वर्जिता मत्सरान्विताः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेऽनेन सम्पुनन्ति कलावपि ॥ ८॥

English
Those who never practise the duties of their Varṇas and Āśramas (castes and walks of life) and those of malicious temperament become sanctified thanks to the Jñānayajña even in the Kali age. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
छलच्छद्मकरा ये च ये च क्रूराः सुनिर्दयाः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेऽनेन सम्पुनन्ति कलावपि ॥ ९॥

English
Those who habitually practise deception and those who are ruthless and of cruel disposition are sanctified by this Jñānayajña even in the Kali age. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मस्वपुष्टाः सततं व्यभिचाररताश्च ये ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेऽनेन सम्पुनन्ति कलावपि ॥ १०॥

English
Those who misappropriate the wealth of brahmins and thereby nourish themselves and those who indulge in heinous crimes of adultery become sanctified by this Jñānayajña even in the Kali age. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा पापरता ये च ये शठाश्च दुराशयाः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेऽनेन सम्पुनन्ति कलावपि ॥ ११॥

English
Those who always indulge in sinful actions and those who are roguish persons of wicked mind become sanctified by this Jñānayajña even in the Kali age. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
मलिना दुर्धियोऽशान्ता देवताद्रव्यभोजिनः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेऽनेन सम्पुनन्ति कलावपि ॥ १२॥

English
Men of unclean habits and wicked minds, men who know no peace and men who swallow temple and trust properties become sanctified by this Jñānayajña even in the Kali age. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराणस्यास्य पुण्यं सन्महापातकनाशनम् ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं चैव शिवसन्तोषहेतुकम् ॥ १३॥

English
The merit accruing from this Purāṇa destroys great sins, yields worldly enjoyments and salvation and delights Lord Śiva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रैवोदाहरन्तीममितिहासं पुरातनम् ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण पापहानिर्भवत्यलम् ॥ १४॥

English
In this context an ancient anecdote is cited as an example, the mere hearing of which, removes all sins utterly. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
आसीत् किरातनगरे ब्राह्मणो ज्ञानदुर्बलः ।
दरिद्रो रसविक्रेता वेदधर्मपराङ्मुखः ॥ १५॥

English
In the city of Kirātas there lived a brahmin extremely poor and deficient in (brahmanical) knowledge. He used to sell various kinds of beverage and was averse to the worship of gods or to virtuous activities. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्यास्नानपरिभ्रष्टो वैश्यवृतिपरायणः ।
देवराज इति ख्यातो विश्र्वस्तजनवञ्चकः ॥ १६॥

English
He never practised the daily Sandhyā prayers or ablutions. His practice resembled a Vaiśya’s mode of living. He never hesitated to deceive credulous persons. His name was Devarāja. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
स विप्रान् क्षत्रियान् वैश्यान् शूद्रांश्चापि तथापरान् ।
हत्वा नानामिषेणैव तत्तद्धनमपाहरत् ॥ १७॥

English
Either by killing or by using various deceitful means he used to rob Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas, Śūdras and others. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अधर्माद् बहुवित्तानि पश्चात्तेनार्जितानि वै ।
न धर्माय धनं तस्य स्वल्पञ्चापीह पापिनः ॥ १८॥

English
Thus by foul means much wealth was later accumulated by him. But the sinner that he was, not even the slightest part of his wealth was utilised in virtuous acts. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदैकतडागे स स्नातुं यातो महीसुरः ।
वेश्यां शोभावतीं नाम दृष्ट्वा तत्रातिविह्वलः ॥ १९॥

English
Once that brahmin went to a lake to take his bath. There he saw a harlot called Śobhāvatī and was much agitated at her sight. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स्ववशं धनिनं विप्रं ज्ञात्वा हृष्टाथ सुन्दरी ।
वार्तालापेन तच्चित्तं प्रीतिमत्समजायत ॥ २०॥

English
The beautiful woman was extremely delighted on coming to know that a rich brahmin had become her willing slave. The brahmin's heart was filled with love due to her pleasant talk. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियं कर्तुं स तां मेने पतिं कर्तुं च सा तथा ।
एवं कामवशौ भूत्वा बहुकालं विजह्रतुः ॥ २१॥

English
He decided to make her his wife and she consented to have him as her husband. Thus in mutual love they sported for a long time. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
आसने शयने पाने भोजने क्रीडने तथा ।
दम्पतीव सदा द्वौ तु ववृताते परस्परम् ॥ २२॥

English
Sitting, lying, eating, drinking and playing together they were not at all different from any other wedded couple. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मात्रा पित्रा तथा पत्न्या वारितोऽपि पुनः पुनः ।
नामन्यत वचस्तेषां पापवृत्तिपरायणः ॥ २३॥

English
Dissuaded again and again by his mother, father, first wife and others though he was, he never paid heed to their words but continued his sinful activities. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदेर्ष्यावशाद् रात्रौ मातरं पितरं वधूम् ।
प्रसुप्तान् न्यवधीद् दुष्टो धनं तेषां तथाहरत् ॥ २४॥

English
Once he became so enraged as to kill his mother, father and wedded wife at dead of night while they were asleep and took possession of their wealth. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मनीनं धनं यच्च पित्रादीनां तथा धनम् ।
वेश्यायै दत्तवान् सर्वं कामी तद्गतमानसः ॥ २५॥

English
Enamoured of the courtesan he handed over to her his own wealth and also the wealth that he looted from his father, mother and first wife. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽभक्ष्यभक्षकः पापी मदिरापानलालसः ।
एकपात्रे सदाभौक्षीत् सवेश्यो ब्राह्मणाधमः ॥ २६॥

English
In the company of this harlot he used to eat all sorts of forbidden food, became an addict to wine and spirituous liquors and partook of his food from the same plate as his concubine. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिद्दैवयोगेन प्रतिष्ठानमुपागतः ।
शिवालयं ददर्शासौ तत्र साधुजनावृतम् ॥ २७॥

English
Once, by chance, he came to the city of Pratiṣṭhāna.$^{13}$ He saw a Siva temple where saintly men had congregated. (27.)

[fn 13: Pratiṣṭhāna : There are references to two towns of the same name : (1) a town at the confluence of the Ganges and Yamunā and capital of the early kings of the lunar race, (2) a town on the Godāvari and capital of Sālivahana. The latter town can be identified with the modern Paithan in the Aurangabad district. It was known as Paithinasipuri : SA 11. vii. 14. 34. 37.
]
---

Sanskrit
स्थित्वा तत्र च विप्रोऽसौ ज्वरेणातिप्रपीडितः ।
शुश्राव सततं शैवीं कथां विप्रमुखोद्गताम् ॥ २८॥

English
During his stay there, he was afflicted by an acute fever. He heard the discourse on Siva conducted by a brahmin. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
देवराजश्च मासान्ते ज्वरेणापीडितो मृतः ।
बद्धो यमभटैः पाशैर्नीतो यमपुरं बलात् ॥ २९॥

English
The brahmin Devarāja suffering from fever died at the end of a month. He was bound with nooses by Yama's attendants and forcibly taken to Yama's city. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तावच्छिवगणाः शुभ्रास्त्रिशूलाञ्चितपाणयः ।
भस्मभासितसर्वाङ्गा रुद्राक्षाञ्चितविग्रहाः ॥ ३०॥

शिवलोकात् समागत्य क्रुद्धा यमपुरीं ययुः ।
ताडयित्वा तु तद्दूतांस्तर्जयित्वा पुनः पुनः ॥ ३१॥

देवराजं समामोच्य विमाने परमाद्भुते ।
उपवेश्य यदा दूताः कैलासं गन्तुमुत्सुकाः ॥ ३२॥

तदा यमपुरीमध्ये महाकोलाहलोऽभवत् ।
धर्मराजस्तु तं श्रुत्वा स्वालयाद् बहिरागमत् ॥ ३३॥

English
In the mean while Siva's attendants dressed in white, smeared with ashes all over the body, wearing garlands of Rudrākṣa and wielding tridents in their hands started furiously from Sivaloka and reached Yama's city. They threatened the attendants of Yama (the God of death) and thrashed them. Releasing Devarāja from their clutches they seated him in a wonderful aerial chariot. When they were about to start to Kailāsa a great tumult arose in the middle of Yama’s city on hearing which Dharmarāja (the God of Death) himself came out of his palace. (30-33.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वाथा चतुरो दूतान् साक्षाद् रुद्रानिवापरान् ।
पूजयामास धर्मज्ञो धर्मराजो यथाविधि ॥ ३४॥

English
On seeing the four messengers who appeared like replicas of Rudra Himself, Dharmarāja the knower of virtues honoured them in accordance with the custom. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानेन चक्षुषा सर्वं वृत्तान्तं ज्ञातवान् यमः ।
न भयात् पृष्टवान् किञ्चिच्छम्भोर्दूतान् महात्मनः ॥ ३५॥

English
Yama came to know of everything through his vision of wisdom. Out of fear he did not question the noble attendants of Śiva. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजिताः प्रार्थितास्ते वै कैलासमगमंस्तदा ।
ददुः शिवाय साम्बाय तं दयावारिराशये ॥ ३६॥

English
Being duly honoured and adored by Yama, they went to Kailāsa and handed over the brahmin to Śiva, the very ocean of mercy and to the divine mother Pārvatī. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या शिवपुराणस्य कथा परमपावानी ।
यस्याः श्रवणमात्रेण पापीयानपि मुक्तिभाक् ॥ ३७॥

English
Blessed indeed is the story of Śivapurāṇa, the holiest of holy stories, a mere hearing of which qualifies even the greatest sinner for salvation. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवमहास्थानं परं धाम परं पदम् ।
यदाहुर्वेदविद्वांसः सर्वलोकोपरि स्थितम् ॥ ३८॥

English
The great seat of Sadāśiva is the greatest abode and the noblest of positions which Vedic scholars have extolled as stationed above all Lokas (worlds). (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया वैश्याः शूद्रा अन्येऽपि प्राणिनः ।
हिंसिता धनलोभेन बहवो येन पापिना ॥ ३९॥

मातृपितृवधूहन्ता वेश्यागामी च मद्यपः ।
देवराजो द्विजस्तत्र गत्वा मुक्तोऽभवत् क्षणात् ॥ ४०॥

English
Devarāja the base brahmin, addicted to wine, enamoured of a vile harlot, slayer of his own father, mother and wife and who out of greed for money had killed many brahmins, kṣatriyas, vaiśyas and śūdras and others became a liberated soul instantaneously on reaching that supreme Loka. (39-40.)
