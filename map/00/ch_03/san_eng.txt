### **CHAPTER THREE - *(Cañculā’s disillusion and detachment)***

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
सूत सूत महाभाग सर्वज्ञोऽसि महामते ।
त्वत्प्रसादात् कृतार्थोऽहं कृतार्थोऽहं पुनः पुनः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O Śaunaka, please listen I shall tell you, though it is a great secret, since you are the foremost among Vedic scholars and a leading devotee of Śiva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इतिहासमिमं श्रुत्वा मनो मेऽतीव मोदते ।
अन्यामपि कथां शम्भोर्वद प्रेमविवर्धिनीम् ॥ २॥

English
My mind rejoices much on hearing this old anecdote. Please narrate another story equally increasing devotion to Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नामृतं पिबतां लोके मुक्तिः क्वापि सभाज्यते ।
शम्भोः कथासुधापानं प्रत्यक्षं मुक्तिदायकम् ॥ ३॥

English
Nowhere in the world are those who drink nectar honoured with liberation. But in regard to the nectar of the story of Śiva it is different. When drunk, it straightway accords salvation. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या धन्या कथा शम्भोस्त्वं धन्यो धन्य एव च ।
यदाकर्णनमात्रेण शिवलोकं व्रजेन्नरः ॥ ४॥

English
Thou art blessed, blessed indeed. Blessed, blessed is the story of Śiva on hearing which a man attains Śivaloka. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्रनिकटे देशे ग्रामो बाष्कलसंज्ञकः ।
वसन्ति यत्र पापिष्ठा वेदधर्मोज्झिता जनाः ॥ ६॥

English
There is a seaside village “Bāskala”$^{14}$ where sinful people bereft of Vedic virtue reside. (6.)

[fn 14. Bāskala grāma—Cf. SK III 111. 32 50. It has not been possible to identify and locate this village.]

---

Sanskrit
दुष्टा दुर्विषयात्मानो निर्दैवा जिह्मवृत्तयः ।
कृषीवलाः शस्त्रधराः परस्त्रीभोगिनः खलाः ॥ ७॥

English
They are wicked debauchees with deceptive means of livelihood, atheists, farmers bearing weapons and adulterous rogues. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानवैराग्यसद्धर्मं न जानन्ति परं हि ते ।
कुकथाश्रवणाढ्येषु निरताः पशुबुद्धयः ॥ ८॥

English
They know not anything about true knowledge, detachment or true virtue. They are brutish in their mental make-up and take a great deal of interest in listening to evil gossips and slander. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्ये वर्णाश्च कुधियः स्वधर्मविमुखाः खलाः ।
कुकर्मनिरता नित्यं सदा विषयिणश्च ते ॥ ९॥

English
People of different castes are equally roguish never paying attention to their duties. Always drawn to worldly pleasures they are ever engrossed in one evil action or another. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियः सर्वाश्च कुटिलाः स्वैरिण्यः पापलालसाः ।
कुधियो व्यभिचारिण्यः सद्वृत्ताचारवर्जिताः ॥ १०॥

English
All the women too are equally crooked, whorish and sinful. Evil-tempered, loose in morals they are devoid of good behaviour and disciplined life. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कुजनसंवासे ग्रामे बाष्कलसंज्ञिते ।
तत्रैको बिन्दुगो नाम विप्र आसीन्महाधमः ॥ ११॥

English
In the village “Bāskala” peopled by wicked people, there was a base brahmin called Binduga. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स दुरात्मा महापापी सुदारोऽपि कुमार्गगः ।
वेश्यापतिर्बभूवाथ कामाकुलितमानसः ॥ १२॥

English
He was a wicked sinner traversing evil paths. Although he had a beautiful wife he was enamoured of a prostitute. His passion for her completely upset his mind. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपत्नीं चञ्चुलां नाम हित्वा नित्यं सुधर्मिणीम् ।
रेमे स वेश्यया दुष्टः स्मरबाणप्रपीडितः ॥ १३॥

English
He forsook his devoted wife Cañculā and indulged in sexual dalliance with the prostitute overwhelmed by Cupid’s arrows. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कालो व्यतीयाय महांस्तस्य कुकर्मिणः ।
सा स्वधर्मभयात् क्लेशात् स्मरार्तापि च चञ्चुला ॥ १४॥

English
Many years thus elapsed without any abatement in his evil action. Afraid of violating her chastity Cañculā, though smitten by Cupid bore her distress (calmly for a short while). (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्याङ्गना सापि प्ररूढनवयौवना ।
अविषह्यस्मरावेशा स्वधर्माद्विरराम ह ॥ १५॥

English
But later on as her youthful health and boisterous virility increased, cupid's onslaught became extremely unbearable for her and she ceased from strictly adhering to her virtuous conduct. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
जारेण सङ्गता रात्रौ रेमे पापेन गुप्ततः ।
पतिदृष्टिं वञ्चयित्वा भ्रष्टसत्त्वा कुमार्गगा ॥ १६॥

English
Unknown to her husband she began to indulge in sexual intercourse with her sinful paramour at night. Fallen thus from Sāttvic virtues she went ahead along her evil ways. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्तां दुराचारां स्वपत्निं चञ्चुलां मुने ।
जारेण सङ्गतां रात्रौ ददर्श स्मरविह्वलाम् ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, once he saw his wife amorously indulging in sexual intercourse with her paramour at night. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तां दूषितां पत्नीं कुकर्मासक्तमानसाम् ।
जारेण सङ्गतां रात्रौ क्रोधाद् दुद्राव वेगतः ॥ १८॥

English
Seeing his wife thus defiled by the paramour at night he furiously rushed at them. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तमागतं गृहे दुष्टमाज्ञाय बिन्दुगं खलः ।
पलायितो द्रुतं जारो वेगतश्छद्मवान् स वै ॥ १९॥

English
When the roguish deceitful paramour knew that the wicked Binduga had returned to the house he fled from the scene immediately. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ स बिन्दुगः पत्नीं गृहीत्वा सुदुराशयः ।
मुष्टिबन्धेन सन्तर्ज्य पुनः पुनरताडयत् ॥ २०॥

English
The wicked Binduga caught hold of his wife and with threats and abuses fisted her again and again. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सा नारी ताडिता भर्त्रा चञ्चुला स्वैरिणी खला ।
कुपिता निर्भया प्राह स्वपतिं बिन्दुगं खलम् ॥ २१॥

English
The whorish wicked woman Cañculā thus beaten by her husband became infuriated and spoke to her wicked husband. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
चञ्चुलोवाच ।
भवान् प्रतिदिनं कामं रमते वेश्यया कुधीः ।
मां विहाय स्वप्त्नीं च युवतीं पतिसेविनीम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Cañculā said :—*  Foulminded that you are, you indulge in sexual intercourse with the harlot every day. You have discarded me your wife, ever ready to serve you with my youthful body. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
रूपवत्या युवत्याश्च कामाकुलितचेतसः ।
विना पतिविहारं स्यात् का गतिर्मे भवान् वदेत् ॥ २३॥

English
I am a youthful maiden endowed with beauty and mentally agitated by lust. Tell me what other course can I take when I am denied the amorous sport with my husband. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं महारूपवती नवयौवनविह्वला ।
कथं सहे कामदुःखं तव सङ्गं विनार्तधीः ॥ २४॥

English
I am very beautiful and agitated with flush of fresh youth. Deprived of sexual intercourse with you I am extremely distressed. How can I bear the pangs of passion ? (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः स तया मूर्खो मूढधीर्ब्राह्मणोऽधमः ।
प्रोवाच बिन्दुगः पापी स्वधर्मविमुखः खलः ॥ २५॥

English
*Sūta said :—* That base brahmin Binduga, when addressed thus by his wife, foolish and averse to his own duties said to her. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
बिन्दुग उवाच ।
सत्यमेतत्त्वयोक्तं हि कामव्याकुलचेतसा ।
हितं वक्ष्यामि तस्मात्ते श‍ृणु कान्ते भयं त्यज ॥ २६॥

English
*Binduga said :—*  True indeed is what you have said with your mind agitated by passion. Please listen, my dear wife, I shall tell you something that will be of benefit to you. You need not be afraid. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
जारैर्विहर नित्यं त्वं चेतसा निर्भयेन वै ।
धनमाकर्ष तेभ्यो हि दत्त्वा तेभ्यः परां रतिम् ॥ २७॥

English
You go ahead with your sexual sports with any number of paramours. No fear need enter your mind. Extract as much of wealth as you can from them and give them enough sexual pleasure. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्धनं देहि सर्वं मे वेश्यासंसक्तचेतसः ।
महत्स्वार्थं भवेन्नूनं तवापि च ममापि च ॥ २८॥

English
You must hand over all the amount to me. You know that I am enamoured of my concubine. Thus our mutual interests will be assured. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति भर्तृवचः श्रुत्वा चञ्चुला तद्वधूश्च सा ।
तथेति भर्तृवचनं प्रतिजग्राह हृष्टधीः ॥ २९॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  His wife Cañculā on hearing these words of her husband became extremely delighted and assented to his vicious proposal. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वैवं समयं तौ वै दम्पती दुष्टमानसौ ।
कुकर्मनिरतौ जातौ निर्भयेन कुचेतसा ॥ ३०॥

English
Having thus entered into their nefarious mutual contract the two wicked persons —the husband and the wife— fearlessly went ahead with their evil actions. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं तयोस्तु दम्पत्योर्दुराचारप्रवृत्तयोः ।
महान् कालो व्यतीयाय निष्फलो मूढचेतसोः ॥ ३१॥

English
A great deal of time was thus wasted by the foolish couple indulging in their vicious activities. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विप्रः स कुमतिर्बिन्दुगो वृषलीपतिः ।
कालेन निधनं प्राप्तो जगाम नरकं खलः ॥ ३२॥

English
The wicked Binduga, the brahmin with a Śūdra woman for his concubine, died after some years and fell into Hell. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
भुक्त्वा नरकदुःखानि बह्वहानि स मूढधीः ।
विन्ध्येऽभवत् पिशाचो हि गिरौ पापी भयङ्करः ॥ ३३॥

English
The foolish fellow endured distress and torture in Hell for many days. He then became a ghost in the Vindhya mountain range continuing to be terribly sinful. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मृते भर्तरि तस्मिन्वै दुराचारेऽथ बिन्दुगे ।
उवास स्वगृहे पुत्रैश्चिरकालं विमूढधीः ॥ ३४॥

एवं विहरती जारैः सा नारी चञ्चुलाह्वया ।
आसीत् कामरता प्रीता किञ्चिदुत्क्रान्तयौवना ॥ ३५॥

English
After the death of her husband the wicked Binduga, the woman Cañculā continued to stay in her house with her sons. The woman foolishly continued her amorous dalliance with her paramours till she no longer retained her youthful charms. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा दैवयोगेन सम्प्राप्ते पुण्यपर्वणि ।
सा नारी बन्धुभिः सार्धं गोकर्णं क्षेत्रमाययौ ॥ ३६॥

English
Due to divine intercession it chanced that on an auspicious occasion she happened to go to the Gokarṇa$^{15}$ temple in the company of her kinsmen. (36.)

[fn 15. Gokarṇa : lit. 'cow's ear'. It is a place of pilgrimage sacred to Śiva, on the west coast, near Mangalore. It has the temple of Mahādeva, supposed to have been established by Rāvaṇa.]
---

Sanskrit
प्रसङ्गात् सा तदा गत्वा कस्मिंश्चित् तीर्थपाथसि ।
सस्नौ सामान्यतो यत्र तत्र बभ्राम बन्धुभिः ॥ ३७॥

English
Casually moving about here and there with her kinsmen she happened to take her bath in a holy pond as a normal routine affair. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
देवालयेऽथ कस्मिंश्चिद्दैवज्ञमुखतः शुभाम् ।
शुश्राव सत्कथां शम्भोः पुण्यां पौराणिकीं च सा ॥ ३८॥

English
In a certain temple a scholar of divine wisdom was conducting a discourse on the holy Śivapurāṇa story some of which she happened to hear. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

सन्तप्तलोहपरिघं क्षिपन्ति स्मरमन्दिरे ॥ ३९॥

इति पौराणिकेनोक्तां श्रुत्वा वैराग्यवर्धिनीम् ।
कथामासीद्भयोद्विग्ना चकम्पे तत्र सा च वै ॥ ४०॥

English

This narrative made by the Paurāṇika to increase detachment, made the woman tremble with fear. (39-40.)

---

Sanskrit
कथा समाप्तौ सा नारी निर्गतेषु जनेषु च ।
भीता रहसि तं प्राह शैवं संवाचकं द्विजम् ॥ ४१॥

English
At the end of the discourse when all the people dispersed, the terrified woman approached the scholarly brahmin and spoke to him in confidence. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
चञ्चुलोवाच ।

श्रुत्वा मामुद्धर स्वामिन् कृपां कृत्वातुलामपि ॥ ४२॥

English
*Cañculā said :—*  O noble sir, please listen to the ignoble activities which I performed without knowing my real duties. O lord, on hearing the same you will please take pity on me and lift me up. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

नीतं पौंश्चल्यतः सर्वं यौवनं मदनान्धया ॥ ४३॥

English
O lord, with a mind utterly deluded I have committed very great sin. Blinded by lust I spent the whole of my youth in incontinent prostitution. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Today on hearing your learned discourse abounding in the sentiments of non-attachment I have become extremely terrified and I tremble much. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Fie upon me, the foolish sinner of a woman deluded by lust, censurable, clinging to worldly pleasures and averse to my own duties. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Unknowingly a great sin that produces excessive distress has been committed by me for a fleeting glimpse of an evanescent pleasure, a criminal action. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Alas, I do not know which terrible goal this will lead me to. My mind has always been turned to evil ways. Which wise man will come to my succour there ? (47.)

---

Sanskrit
मरणे यमदूताँस्तान् कथं द्रक्ष्ये भयङ्करान् ।

English
At the time of death how shall I face the terrible messengers of Yama ? How shall I feel when they tie nooses forcibly round my neck ? (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं सहिष्ये नरके खण्डशो देहकृन्तनम् ।
यातनां तत्र महतीं दुःखदां च विशेषतः ॥ ४९॥

English
How shall I endure in Hell the mincing of my body to pieces ? How shall I endure the special torture that is excessively painful ? (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
I bewail my lot. How can I peacefully proceed with the activity of my sense-organs during the day ? Agitated with misery how shall I get peaceful sleep during the night ? (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

यद्दुःखं देहिनो घोरं तस्मात् कोटिगुणं मम ॥ ५३॥

English
O Brahmin, my present pain is millions of times more than that of a man stuck to the stake or hurled from a high mountain-top. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वमेधशतं कृत्वा गङ्गां स्नात्वा शतं समाः ।
न शुद्धिर्जायते प्रायो मत्पापस्य गरीयसः ॥ ५४॥

English
My sin is so great that it cannot be washed away even if I take ablutions in the Gaṅgā for a hundred years or even if I perform a hundred sacrifices. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि कं वा शरणमाश्रये ।
कस्त्रायते मां लोकेऽस्मिन् पतन्तीं नरकार्णवे ॥ ५५॥

English
What shall I do ? Where shall I go ? Whom shall I resort to ? I am falling into the ocean of Hell. Who can save me in this world ? (55.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव मे गुरुर्ब्रह्मंस्त्वं माता त्वं पितासि च ।
उद्धरोद्धर मां दीनां त्वामेव शरणं गताम् ॥ ५६॥

English
O noble sir, thou art my preceptor. Thou art my mother. Thou art my father. I seek refuge in Thee. I am in a pitiable plight. Lift me; lift me. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति सञ्जातनिर्वेदां पतितां चरणद्वये ।
उत्थाप्य कृपया धीमान् बभाषे ब्राह्मणः स हि ॥ ५७॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  The intelligent Brahmin mercifully lifted up Cañculā who had become disgusted (with worldly affairs) and had fallen at his feet. That Brahmin then spoke (as follows). (57.)
