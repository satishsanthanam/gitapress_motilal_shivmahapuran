### **CHAPTER FOUR - ### *Cañculā's Salvation***

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मण उवाच ।
दिष्ट्या काले प्रबुद्धासि शिवानुग्रहतो वराम् ।
इमां शिवपुराणस्य श्रुत्वा वैराग्यवत्कथाम् ॥ १॥

मा भैषीर्द्विजपत्नि त्वं शिवस्य शरणं व्रज ।
शिवानुग्रहतः सर्वं पापं सद्यो विनश्यति ॥ २॥

English
*The Brahmin said :—*  O Brahmin lady, fortunately you have realised at the proper time on hearing the story of Śivapurāṇa that is conducive to non-attachment. Do not be afraid. Seek refuge in Śiva. All sins perish instantaneously by Śiva's grace. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
वक्ष्यामि ते परं वस्तु शिवकीर्तिसमन्वितम् ।
भविष्यति गतिर्येन सर्वदा ते सुखावहा ॥ ३॥

English
I shall explain to you that great object attached to the glorification of Śiva whereby your course hereafter will be pleasant always. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्कथाश्रवणादेव जाता ते मतिरीदृशी ।
पश्चात्तापान्विता शुद्धा वैराग्यं विषयेषु च ॥ ४॥

English
It is by listening to the excellent story that your mind has now turned to the pure path of repentance and detachment towards worldly pleasures. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चात्तापः पापकृतां पापानां निष्कृतिः परा ।
सर्वेषां वर्णितं सद्भिः सर्वपापविशोधनम् ॥ ५॥

English
Repentance is the only way of acquittance for all sinners. Saintly men have extolled it as the only way of expiation for all sins. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चात्तापेनैव शुद्धिः प्रायश्चित्तं करोति सः ।
यथोपदिष्टं सद्भिर्हि सर्वपापविशोधनम् ॥ ६॥

English
Purity can be realised by repentance alone. If the sinner expiates in the manner advised by saintly men it removes all sins. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रायश्चित्तमधीकृत्य विधिवन्निर्भयः पुमान् ।
न याति सुगतिं प्रायः पश्चात्तापी न संशयः ॥ ७॥

English
After due expiation he becomes free from fear. By repentance he attains salvation undoubtedly. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छिवपुराणस्य कथाश्रवणतो यथा ।
जायते चित्तशुद्धिर्हि न तथान्यैरुपायतः ॥ ८॥

English
The mental purity that one derives on hearing the story of Śivapurāṇa cannot be gained by any other means. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शोध्यमानं दर्पणं हि यथा भवति निर्मलम् ।
तथैतत्कथया चेतो विशुद्धिं यात्यसंशयम् ॥ ९॥

English
As a mirror becomes free from dirt on being wiped with a cloth, so is the mind undoubtedly purified by listening to this story. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
विशुद्धे चेतसि शिवो नृणां तिष्ठति साम्बिकः ।
ततो याति विशुद्धात्मा साम्बशम्भोः परं पदम् ॥ १०॥

English
Accompanied by Ambā, Śiva stays in the minds of pure men. The sanctified soul thereupon attains the region of Śiva and Ambā. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः सर्वस्य वर्गस्यैतत्कथासाधनं मतम् ।
एतदर्थं महादेवो निर्ममे त्वाग्रहादिमाम् ॥ ११॥

English
Hence this story is the means of realising the fourfold aim of life. It is for this that Mahādeva earnestly created this. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कथया सिध्यति ध्यानमनया गिरिजापतेः ।
ध्यानाज्ज्ञानं परं तस्मात् कैवल्यं भवति ध्रुवम् ॥ १२॥

English
Listening to the story of Pārvatī's consort (Śiva) brings about steady contemplation. Contemplation leads to perfect knowledge which certainly brings in salvation. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
असिद्धशङ्करध्यानः कथामेव श‍ृणोति यः ।
स प्राप्यान्यभवे ध्यानं शम्भोर्याति परां गतिम् ॥ १३॥

English
A person who listens to the story in this birth though he be unable to meditate, realises the same in the next birth after which he reaches the goal of Śiva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्कथाश्रवणतः कृत्वा ध्यानमुमापतेः ।
ते पश्चात्तापिनः पापा बहवः सिद्धिमागताः ॥ १४॥

English
Many repentant sinners have meditated upon Śiva after hearing this story and have achieved salvation. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां श्रेयसां बीजं सत्कथाश्रवणं नृणाम् ।
यथावर्त्म समाराध्यं भवबन्धगदापहम् ॥ १५॥

English
Listening to the excellent story is the cause of beatitude for all men. Properly entertained, it dispels the ailment of worldly bondage. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कथाश्रवणतः शम्भोर्मननाच्च ततो हृदा ।
निदिध्यासनतश्चैव चित्तशुद्धिर्भवत्यलम् ॥ १६॥

English
Listening to the story of Śiva, constant meditations thereon and repeated musings certainly purify the mind. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो भक्तिर्महेशस्य पुत्राभ्यां भवति ध्रुवम् ।
तदनुग्रहतो दिव्या ततो मुक्तिर्न संशयः ॥ १७॥

English
That (the purity of the mind) leads the meditator to a devotion of Maheśa and his two sons (Gaṇeśa and Kārtikeya). With their blessings one undoubtedly attains liberation. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्विहीनः पशुर्ज्ञेयो मायाबन्धनसक्तधीः ।
संसारबन्धनान्नैव मुक्तो भवति स ध्रुवम् ॥ १८॥

English
A person devoid of that devotion with his mind entangled in the bondage of ignorance is a brute. He can never be liberated from the worldly bondage. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो हि द्विजपत्नि त्वं विषयेभ्यो निवृत्तधीः ।
श‍ृणु शम्भोः कथां चैतां भक्त्या परमपावनीम् ॥ १९॥

English
Hence O Brahmin lady, you turn away from worldly pleasures. Listen to the sanctifying story of Śiva with devotion. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वन्त्याः सत्कथामेतां शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ।
शुद्धिमेष्यति चेतस्ते ततो मुक्तिमवाप्स्यसि ॥ २०॥

English
Your mind, as you listen to the excellent story of Śiva, the Supreme Soul, will become pure and thereafter you will realise liberation. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायतः शिवपादाब्जं चेतसा निर्मलेन वै ।
एकेन जन्मना मुक्तिः सत्यं सत्यं वदाम्यहम् ॥ २१॥

English
Liberation is assured in this very birth to a person who meditates on the lotus-like feet of Śiva, with a pure mind. Truth, I am saying the truth. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स द्विजवरो वरः शैवः कृपार्द्रधीः ।
तूष्णीं बभूव शुद्धात्मा शिवध्यानपरायणः ॥ २२॥

English
*Sūta said:*  After saying this, that excellent brahmin with his mind melting with pity ceased talking and turned his attention to the meditation on Śiva with the purity of the Soul. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ बिन्दुगपत्नि सा चञ्चुलाह्वा प्रसन्नधीः ।
इत्युक्ता तेन विप्रेण समासीद्बाष्पलोचना ॥ २३॥

English
The wife of Binduga, called Cañculā, when thus addressed by the brahmin, became delighted and her eyes brimmed with tears. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पपातारं द्विजेन्द्रस्य पादयोस्तस्य हृष्टधीः ।
चञ्चुला साञ्जलिः सा च कृतार्थास्मीत्यभाषत ॥ २४॥

English
With great delight in her heart she fell at the brahmin's feet. Cañculā with her palms joined together said "I am blessed". (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सोत्थाय सातङ्का साञ्जलिर्गद्गदाक्षरम् ।
तमुवाच महाशैवं द्विजं वैराग्ययुक् सुधीः ॥ २५॥

English
Afterwards she rose up with great mental agitation. With her hands joined together, her words faltering in excitement, the woman of good intellect in her detached mood said to the brahmin, the great devotee of Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
चञ्चुलोवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् शैववर स्वामिन् धन्यस्त्वं परमार्थदृक् ।
परोपकारनिरतो वर्णनीयः सुसाधुषु ॥ २६॥

English
*Cañculā said :—*  O my lord, great brahmin devotee of Śiva, you are blessed. You are endowed with the vision of Truth. You are devoted to rendering help to others. You are to be described among great saintly men. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्धरोद्धर मां साधो पतन्तीं नरकार्णवे ।
श्रुत्वा यां सुकथां शैवीं पुराणार्थविजृम्भिताम् ॥ २७॥

विरक्तधीरहं जाता विषयेभ्यश्च सर्वतः ।
सुश्रद्धा महती ह्येतत्पुराणश्रवणेऽधुना ॥ २८॥

English
O saintly one, I am about to fall into the ocean of Hell. Save me. I am now faithfully eager to listen to the Purāṇa. On hearing its excellent story I became detached from worldly pleasures. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा साञ्जलिः सा वै सम्प्राप्य तदनुग्रहम् ।
तत्पुराणं श्रोतुकामातिष्ठत्तत्सेवने रता ॥ २९॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  So saying with reverence she got the blessings of  the brahmin. Desirous of hearing the Purāṇa she stayed there rendering service to him. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शैववरो विप्रस्तस्मिन्नेव स्थले सुधीः ।
सत्कथां श्रावयामास तत्पुराणस्य तां स्त्रियम् ॥ ३०॥

English
The intelligent brahmin devotee narrated the Purāṇic story to the woman on the spot. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं तस्मिन् महाक्षेत्रे तस्मादेव द्विजोत्तमात् ।
कथां शिवपुराणस्य सा शुश्राव महोत्तमाम् ॥ ३१॥

English
In this manner she listened to the excellent story of Śivapurāṇa in that holy centre from that excellent brahmin. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तिज्ञानविरागाणां वर्धिनीं मुक्तिदायिनीम् ।
बभूव सुकृतार्था सा श्रुत्वा तां सत्कथां पराम् ॥ ३२॥

English
On hearing that excellent story that heightened devotion, knowledge and detachment and yielded liberation, she became greatly blessed. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्गुरोस्तस्य कृपया शुद्धचित्ता च सा द्रुतम् ।
शिवानुग्रहतः शम्भोः रूपध्यानमवाप ह ॥ ३३॥

English
Favoured by the good preceptor she quickly gained purity of mind. By the blessings of Śiva she could meditate on Śiva’s forms and features. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं सद्गुरुमाश्रित्य सा प्राप्तशिवसन्मतिः ।
दध्यौ मुहुर्मुहुः शम्भोश्चिदानन्दमयं वपुः ॥ ३४॥

English
Thus, resorting to the good preceptor, her mind was drawn towards Śiva. She constantly meditated on the sentient blissful body of Śiva. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नात्वा तीर्थजले नित्यं जटावल्कलधारिणी ।
भस्मोद्धूलितसर्वाङ्गी रुद्राक्षकृतभूषणा ॥ ३५॥

शिवनामजपासक्ता वाग्यता मितभोजना ।
गुरूपदिष्टमार्गेण सा शिवं समतोषयत् ॥ ३६॥

English
She wore barks of trees and had her hair matted. She smeared ashes over her body. She wore garlands of Rudrākṣa beads. Every day she took her ablutions in the sacred water. She regularly repeated Śiva’s names. She regulated her speech and diet. She propitiated Lord Śiva in the manner advised by the preceptor. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं तस्याश्चञ्चुलायाः कुर्वन्त्या ध्यानमुत्तमम् ।
बहुकालो व्यतीयाय शम्भोस्तत्रैव शौनक ॥ ३७॥

English
O Śaunaka, thus for a long time Cañculā continued her meditation on Lord Śiva. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ कालेन पूर्णेन भक्तित्रिकसमन्विता ।
समुत्ससर्ज देहं स्वमनायासेन चञ्चुला ॥ ३८॥

English
When the stipulated period was over, Cañculā in her practice of the three-fold$^{16}$ devotion cast-off her body without any difficulty. (38.)

[fn 16. The three kinds of devotion are :—(1) the devotion of hearing (śravaṇa), (2) of glorifying (Kīrtana) and (3) of deliberating (manana) the attributes of God. ŚP. VS. 3. 21-25]


---

Sanskrit
विमानं द्रुतमायान्तं प्रेषितं त्रिपुरारिणा ।
दिव्यं स्वगणसंयुक्तं नानाशोभासमन्वितम् ॥ ३९॥

English
The divine aerial chariot shining in brilliant colours, sent by Tripurāri$^{17}$ (Śiva) Himself, accompanied by His attendants, arrived there quickly. (39.)

[fn 17. Śiva is called Tripurāri, the slayer of Tripura, for he killed the demon Tripura who presided over three cities of gold, silver and iron in the sky, air and earth built for demons by Maya.]
---

Sanskrit
अथ तत्र समारूढा महेशानुचरैर्वरैः ।
नीता शिवपुरीं सद्यो ध्वस्तसर्वमला च सा ॥ ४०॥

English
With her dirt and sin removed she mounted the aerial chariot and was immediately taken to Śiva’s city by the lord’s noble attendants. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यरूपधरा दिव्या दिव्यावयवशालिनी ।
चन्द्रार्धशेखरा गौरी विलसद्दिव्यभूषणा ॥ ४१॥

English
She assumed a divine form. Her limbs were divine in their features. She assumed the form of Gauri with the crescent moon as her coronet and divine ornaments shining brilliantly. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र महादेवं सा ददर्श त्रिलोचनम् ।
विष्णुब्रह्मादिभिर्देवैः सेव्यमानं सनातनम् ॥ ४२॥

English
She saw the three-eyed Mahādeva, the eternal, being served devotedly by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

उपास्यमानं सद्भक्त्या कोटिसूर्यसमप्रभम् ॥ ४३॥

English
He had the brilliance of ten million suns and was reverently served by Gaṇeśa, Bhṛṅgi, Nandīśa Virabhadreśvara and others. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
नीलग्रीवं पञ्चवक्त्रं त्र्यम्बकं चन्द्रशेखरम् ।
वामाङ्गे बिभ्रतं गौरीं विद्युत्पुञ्जसमप्रभाम् ॥ ४४॥

English
His neck had a blue hue; he had five faces, three eyes, the crescent moon as crest-ornament and his left side was apportioned to Gauri who had the brilliance of lightning. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्पूरगौरं गौरीशं सर्वालङ्कारधारिणम् ।
सितभस्मलसद्देहं सितवस्त्रं महोज्ज्वलम् ॥ ४५॥

English
He was white in complexion like camphor and wore all ornaments. Besmeared with white ashes all over the body and clad in white cloth he shone brilliantly. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वैवं शङ्करं नारी सा मुमोदाति चञ्चुला ।
सुसम्भ्रमान्महाप्रीता प्रणनाम पुनः पुनः ॥ ४६॥

English
The woman Cañculā became highly delighted on seeing Śaṅkara. In her flutter of delight she bowed again and again to Him. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
साञ्जलिः सा मुदा प्रेम्णा सन्तुष्टा च विनीतका ।
आनन्दाश्रुजलैर्युक्ता रोमहर्षसमन्विता ॥ ४७॥

English
She joined her palms in reverence with great pleasure, love and humility. In her great delight she shed tears of joy and had feelings of horripilation. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा वै करुणया पार्वत्या शङ्करेण च ।
समानीतोपकण्ठं हि सुदृष्ट्या च विलोकिता ॥ ४८॥

English
With sympathy she was allowed to approach Pārvatī and Śaṅkara who gracefully looked at her. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

दिव्यसौख्यान्विता तत्र चञ्चुला बिन्दुगप्रिया ॥ ४९॥

English
Cañculā, the beloved wife of Binduga, thus attained a divine form and was blessed with divine pleasures and made a chaperon by Pārvatī. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

लब्ध्वा निवासमचलं लेभे सुखमनाहतम् ॥ ५०॥

English
In that permanent abode of excellent bliss and sublime lustre she acquired a permanent residence and unobstructed pleasure. (50.)
