### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(Binduga's Salvation)***

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
सूत सूत महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं शिवसक्तधीः ।
श्रावितेयं कथास्माकमद्भुता भक्तिवर्धिनी ॥ १॥

तत्र गत्वा किं चकार चञ्चुला प्राप्तसद्गतिः ।
तत् त्वं वद विशेषेण तत्पतेश्च महामते ॥ २॥

English
*Śaunaka said :—* O Sūta, the fortunate Sūta, thou art blessed with thy mind engrossed in Śiva. The story that thou hast narrated to us is wonderful and conducive to the increase of devotion. What did the woman Cañculā do after obtaining her salvation? O intelligent one, please tell me in detail the story of her husband too. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सा कदाचिदुमां देवीमुपगम्य प्रणम्य च ।
सुतुष्टाव करौ बद्ध्वा परमानन्दसम्प्लुता ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—* Once she approached goddess Umā Pārvatī.$^{18}$ She bowed and offered prayers to her with palms joined in her flutter of delight. (3.)

[fn 18. In the Pauranic Mythology, Pārvatī is the daughter of Himālaya and the wife of Śiva. In the cult of Śakti and Tantras, she has been identified with Prakṛti itself. Almost all the Purāṇas speak of her as Prakṛti and her three Guṇas Sattva, Rajas and Tamas are the three Gods : Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
चञ्चुलोवाच ।
गिरिजे स्कन्दमातस्त्वं सेविता सर्वदा नरैः ।
सर्वासौख्यप्रदे शम्भुप्रिये ब्रह्मस्वरूपिणि ॥ ४॥

English
*Cañculā said :—* O mother of Skanda, daughter of mountain, Thou art always served by men. O beloved of Śiva, the bestower of all pleasures, having the form of Supreme Brahman, (4.)

---

Sanskrit

त्वमाद्या प्रकृतिस्सूक्ष्मा सच्चिदानन्दरूपिणी ॥ ५॥

English
Thou art worthy of being served by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others. Thou art both endowed with and devoid of attributes. Thou art the subtle primordial Prakṛti, with Existence, Knowledge and Bliss for thy forms. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिस्थितिलयकरी त्रिगुणा त्रिसुरालया ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशानां सुप्रतिष्ठाकरा परा ॥ ६॥

English
Thou createst, maintainest and annihilatest. Thou hast the three Guṇas. Thou art the refuge of the three types of divine beings. Thou sustainest Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा महेशीं तां चञ्चुला प्राप्तसद्गतिः ।
विरराम नतस्कन्धा प्रेमपूर्णाश्रुलोचना ॥ ७॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  Offering thus her prayers to the Goddess, Cañculā who had attained salvation ceased to talk with shoulders stooping and eyes brimming with tears of love. (7.)

---

Sanskrit

तामुवाच महाप्रीत्या चञ्चुलां भक्तवत्सला ॥ ८॥

English
Pārvatī, the beloved of Śiva, ever favouring her devotees, was greatly moved by pity and said to Cañculā lovingly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  O Cañculā, my friend, I am pleased to hear your prayer. O beautiful woman, what is the boon you crave from me? Tell me. There is nothing that I cannot give you. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ता सा गिरिजया चञ्चुला सुप्रणम्य ताम् ।
पर्यपृच्छत सुप्रीत्या साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तका ॥ १०॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  Thus urged by Girijā, Cañculā bowed to her. She asked her, bending her head and joining her palms together with great devotion. (10.)

---

Sanskrit

तेन युक्ता यथाहं वै भवामि गिरिजेऽनघे ॥ ११॥

English
*Cañculā said :—*  O Celestial Girijā, I do not know where my husband is at present, nor where he is to go. O benignant favourite of the distressed, please make such arrangements as would enable me to join him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit

महादेवि महेशानि भर्ता मे वृषलीपतिः ।

English
O great goddess Maheśānī, my husband had a Śūdra woman as his concubine. He died before me. I do not know what befell that sinner. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।

प्रत्युवाच सुसम्प्रीत्या गिरिजा नयवत्सला ॥ १३॥

English
*Śūta said:—* On hearing these words of Cañculā Pārvatī, the daughter of Himālaya, who is fond of justice, replied lovingly. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिजोवाच ।
सूते भर्ता बिन्दुगाह्वो महापापी दुराशयः ।
वेश्याभोगी महामूढो मृत्वा स नरकं गतः ॥ १४॥

English
*Girijā said:—*  O daughter, your wicked sinful husband Binduga, the foolish wretch enamoured of prostitutes has been to hell after his death. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भुक्त्वा नरकदुःखानि विविधान्यमिताः समाः ।
पापशेषेण पापात्मा विन्ध्ये जातः पिशाचकः ॥ १५॥

English
He underwent the various tortures of hell for many years and has now become a Piśāca due to the residue of sins, in the Vindhya mountains. (15.)

---

Sanskrit

तत्रैव वातभुग्दुष्टः सर्वकष्टवहः सदा ॥ १६॥

English
Even now that wicked fellow is undergoing various painful tortures. He, in the form of a Piśāca, has only wind for his diet and is suffering from all sorts of miseries. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।

पतिदुःखेन महता दुःखितासीत्तदा किल ॥ १७॥

English
*Śūta said:—*  On hearing these words of Gaurī, Cañculā of auspicious rites was overwhelmed by the pain at the news of her husband's distress. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

पुनः पप्रच्छ सा नारी हृदयेन विदूयता ॥ १८॥

English
She somehow steadied her mind, bowed to Maheśvarī and with a worried heart asked the goddess. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Cañculā said:—* O Maheśvarī, O great goddess, be kind to me. Please redeem my husband, a wicked perpetrator of evil actions though he be. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
*Śūta said:—* On hearing these words of the woman, Pārvatī, favourably disposed to her devotees, replied to her chaperon Cañculā, delighted in her heart. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English

English

English

Sanskrit
स पिशाचो महापुण्यं श्रुत्वा शिवपुराणकम् ।
विधूय कलुषं सर्वं जहौ पैशाचिकं वपुः ॥ ५०॥

English
The Piśāca too, on hearing the holy Śivapurāṇa, cast-off all his sins and discarded his ghostly body. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यरूपो बभूवाशु गौरवर्णः सितांशुकः ।
सर्वालङ्कारदीप्ताङ्गस्त्रिनेत्रश्चन्द्रशेखरः ॥ ५१॥

English
He assumed the divine form of the three-eyed moon-crested God (Śiva), white in complexion, clad in white cloth, with the body illuminated and embellished by all ornaments. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यं दिव्यवपुर्भूत्वा तया स निजकान्तया ।
जगौ स्वयमपि श्रीमांश्चरितं पार्वतीपतेः ॥ ५२॥

English
Taking up the divine body, the glorious Binduga accompanied by his wife sang the story of Pārvatī’s consort. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वधूमिति सन्दृष्ट्वा सर्वे देवर्षयश्च ते ।
बभूवुर्विस्मिताश्चित्ते परमानन्दसम्युताः ॥ ५३॥

English
On seeing his wife thus, all the divine sages had a welcome surprise and were highly delighted in their minds. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सुकृतार्था महेशस्य श्रुत्वा चरितमद्भुतम् ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुः प्रीत्या शंसन्तः शाङ्करं यशः ॥ ५४॥

English
Gratified on hearing the wonderful story of Śiva they returned to their respective abodes delightedly glorifying Śiva. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
बिन्दुगः सोऽपि दिव्यात्मा सुविमानस्थितः सुखी ।
स्वकान्तापार्श्वगः श्रीमाञ्छुशुभेऽतीव खस्थितः ॥ ५५॥

English
Binduga in his divine form ascended the aerial chariot with great pleasure. High up in the sky, with his wife at his side he shone brilliantly. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ गायन् महेशस्य सुगुणान् सुमनोहरान् ।
सतुम्बुरुर्जगामाशु सकान्तः शाङ्करं पदम् ॥ ५६॥

English
Singing the pleasing attributes of Śiva he hastened to Śiva’s region accompanied by Tumburu and his own wife. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
सुसत्कृतो महेशेन पार्वत्या च स बिन्दुगः ।
स्वगणश्च कृतः प्रीत्या साभवद्गिरिजासखी ॥ ५७॥

English
Binduga was welcomed by Śiva and Pārvatī and was lovingly made their attendant. His wife became the chaperon of Girijā. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिँल्लोके परानन्दे घनज्योतिषि शाश्वते ।
लब्ध्वा निवासमचलं लभेते परमं सुखम् ॥ ५८॥

English
In that permanent abode of excellent bliss and sublime lustre he acquired an unassailable residence and unobstructed pleasure. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येतत् कथितं पुण्यमितिहासमघापहम् ।
शिवाशिवपरानन्दं निर्मलं भक्तिवर्धनम् ॥ ५९॥

English
Thus I have narrated this holy anecdote that removes sins, is highly delightful to Śiva and Pārvatī in pure and heightening devotion. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या कीर्तयेद्वा समाहितः ।
स भुक्त्वा विपुलान् भोगानन्ते मुक्तिमवाप्नुयात् ॥ ६०॥

English
He who listens to this account with devotion and recites this piously shall enjoy immense pleasures and obtain liberation. (60.)
