### **CHAPTER SIX - *(Rules for listening to Śivapurāṇa)***

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
सूत सूत महाप्राज्ञ व्यासशिष्य नमोऽस्तु ते ।
धन्यस्त्वं शैववर्योऽसि वर्णनीयमहद्गुणः ॥ १॥

श्रीमच्छिवपुराणस्य श्रवणस्य विधिं वद ।
येन सर्वं लभेच्छ्रोता सम्पूर्णं फलमुत्तमम् ॥ २॥

English
*Śaunaka said :—*  O Sūta, O highly intelligent disciple of Vyāsa, obeisance to thee. Thou art blessed and the foremost among Śiva’s devotees. Thy attributes are highly praiseworthy. Please tell me about the rules for listening to Śivapurāṇa whereby the listener shall obtain all excellent fruits. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अथ ते सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि सम्पूर्णफलहेतवे ।
विधिं शिवपुराणस्य शौनक श्रवणे मुने ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O sage Śaunaka, I shall tell you the rules for listening to Śivapurāṇa so that the entire fruit may be derived by their observance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दैवज्ञं च समाहूय सन्तोष्य च जनान्वितः ।
मुहूर्तं शोधयेच्छुद्धं निर्विघ्नेन समाप्तये ॥ ४॥

English
The householder must invite an astrologer and propitiate him to fix an auspicious day for the beginning, so that it may conclude without obstacles in the middle. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वार्ता प्रेष्या प्रयत्नेन देशे देशे च सा शुभा ।
भविष्यति कथा शैवी आगन्तव्यं शुभार्थिभिः ॥ ५॥

English
News must be circulated in different localities that the auspicious discourse is to take place and all who seek welfare must be present. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
दुरे हरिकथाः केचिद् दुरे शङ्करकीर्तनाः ।
स्त्रियः शूद्रादयो ये च बोधस्तेषां भवेद्यतः ॥ ६॥

English
Women, Śūdra and others who are far removed from holy discourses and stay away from singing glories of Śiva shall attend this discourse whence they may have some enlightenment. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
देशे देशे शाम्भवा ये कीर्तनश्रवणोत्सुकाः ।
तेषामानयनं कार्यं तत्प्रकारार्थमादरात् ॥ ७॥

English
Wherever there are devotees of Śiva, eager to listen to the songs of praise in the neighbourhood, they must also be invited with due reverence. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
भविष्यति समाजोऽत्र साधूनां परमोत्सवः ।
पारायणे पुराणस्य शैवस्य परमाद्भुतः ॥ ८॥

English
Thus there shall be a great festive gathering of saintly men at the discourse of Śivapurāṇa, a wonderful congregation. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमच्छिवपुराणाह्वरसपानाय चादरात् ।
आयान्त्वरं भवन्तश्च कृपया प्रेमतत्पराः ॥ ९॥

English
With devotion, may all of you be pleased to join us for imbibing the sweet juice of Śivapurāṇa, with due reverence. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नावकाशो यदि प्रेम्णागन्तव्यं दिनमेककम् ।
सर्वथागमनं कार्यं दुर्लभा च क्षणस्थितिः ॥ १०॥

English
If you do not have sufficient leisure, please grace the assembly at least for a day. By all means, do come, even for a short stay or a while. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषामाह्वानमेवं हि कार्यं सविनयं मुदा ।
आगतानां च तेषां हि सर्वथा कार्य आदरः ॥ ११॥

English
Thus all should be invited humbly. Those who come should be hospitably received in all respects. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवालये च तीर्थे वा वने वापि गृहेऽथवा ।
कार्यं शिवपुराणस्य श्रवणस्थलमुत्तमम् ॥ १२॥

English
An excellent spot for the discourse on Śivapurāṇa must be selected in a temple of Śiva, or in a holy centre or in a park or in a private house. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्यं संशोधनं भूमेर्लेपनं धातुमण्डनम् ।
विचित्रा रचना दिव्या महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ १३॥

English
The ground must be scrubbed, cleaned and smeared with cowdung. It must be decorated with metallic materials attended with all festivities. The whole arrangement must be divinely exquisite and pleasing to diverse tastes. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहोपस्करमुद्धृत्य निखिलं तदयोग्यकम् ।
एकान्ते गृहकोणे चादृश्ये यत्नान्निवेशयेत् ॥ १४॥

English
All the rubbish must be removed and all unnecessary things must be hidden in a corner away from the public view. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तव्यो मण्डपोऽत्युच्चैः कदलीस्तम्भमण्डितः ।
फलपुष्पादिभिस्सम्यग्विष्वग्वैतानराजितः ॥ १५॥

English
A high platform must be constructed, richly decorated with stumps of plantain trees. The whole place should be covered with a canopy. Fruits and flowers should be profusely used. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्द्दिक्षु ध्वजारोपस्सपताकः सुशोभनः ।
सुभक्तिः सर्वथा कार्या सर्वानन्दविधायिनी ॥ १६॥

English
Flags and banners should be hoisted in the four quarters. They should be neatly arranged to be pleasing to everyone. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्कल्प्यमासनं दिव्यं शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ।
वक्तुश्चापि तथा दिव्यमासनं सुखसाधनम् ॥ १७॥

English
A seat must be assigned to Śiva, the Supreme soul. A comfortable seat shall be assigned to the orator. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रोतॄणां कल्पनीयानि सुस्थलानि यथार्हतः ।
अन्येषां च स्थलान्येव साधारणतया मुने ॥ १८॥

English
Good places shall be reserved for the regular listeners as befitting their position. O sage, for the other casual visitors, seats with ordinary comfort shall be set apart. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विवाहे यादृशं चित्तं तादृशं कार्यमेव हि ।
अन्या चिन्ता विनिर्वार्या सर्वा शौनक लौकिकी ॥ १९॥

English
People must be in as pleasant a mood as on marriage occasions : all worldly worries and anxieties must be avoided. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
उदङ्मुखो भवेद्वक्ता श्रोता प्राग्वदनस्तथा ।
व्युत्क्रमः पादयोर्ज्ञेयो विरोधो नास्ति कश्चनः ॥ २०॥

English
The discourser faces the north and the listeners the east. There is no fear of the criss-crossings of the feet. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा पूर्वदिग्ज्ञेया पूज्यपूजकमध्यतः ।
अथवा सम्मुखे वक्तुः श्रोतॄणामाननं स्मृतम् ॥ २१॥

English
Or the discourser faces the east as the worshipper Or let the discourser and the recipient face each other. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यासासनसमारूढो यदा पौराणिको द्विजः ।
असमाप्तौ प्रसङ्गस्य नमस्कुर्यान्न कस्यचित् ॥ २२॥

English
As long as he is seated in the seat of the discourcer, the Purāṇist does not bow to any one before the conclusion of the discourse. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
बालो युवाथ वृद्धो वा दरिद्रो वापि दुर्बलः ।
पुराणज्ञः सदा वन्द्यः पूज्यश्च सुकृतार्थिभिः ॥ २३॥

English
Whether he is a boy or a youth, an old man, an indigent person, or a weakling, the scholar well-versed in the Purāṇa is worthy of honour from all those who seek merit. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नीचबुद्धिं न कुर्वीत पुराणज्ञे कदाचन ।
यस्य वक्त्रोद्गता वाणी कामधेनुः शरीरिणाम् ॥ २४॥

English
Never shall anyone show demeaning disrespect towards a Purāṇa-scholar, the speech from whose mouth is no less than the divine cow Kāmadhenu for all persons. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरवः सन्ति बहवो जन्मतो गुणतश्च वै ।
परो गुरुः पुराणज्ञस्तेषां मध्ये विशेषतः ॥ २५॥

English
Either as the cause of birth or of attributes there are many who may be termed “Guru” (Elder, preceptor). Among them the Purāṇic scholar is the greatest Guru. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भवकोटिसहस्रेषु भूत्वा भूत्वावसीदताम् ।
यो ददाति परां मुक्तिं कोऽन्यस्तस्मात् परो गुरुः ॥ २६॥

English
Who can be a greater Guru than the person who bestows the highest salvation on those who are disheartened due to the millions of births ? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराणज्ञः शुचिर्दक्षः शान्तो विजितमत्सरः ।
साधुः कारुण्यवान् वाग्मी वदेत् पुण्यकथामिमाम् ॥ २७॥

English
The person who undertakes to conduct a discourse on this sanctifying tale shall be well-versed in Purāṇas, pure, skilful, quiet, free from malice, saintly, sympathetic and eloquent. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
आसूर्योदयमारभ्य सार्धत्रिप्रहरान्तकम् ।
कथा शिवपुराणस्य वाच्या सम्यक् सुधीमता ॥ २८॥

English
The intelligent discourcer shall start the narration of the story of Śivapurāṇa at sunrise and continue it for two and a half Praharas ($2\frac{1}{2} \times 3 = 7\frac{1}{2}$ Hrs) earnestly. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ये धूर्ता ये च दुर्वृत्ता ये चान्ये विजिगीषवः ।
तेषां कुटिलवृत्तीनामग्रे नैव वदेत् कथाम् ॥ २९॥

English
This story shall not be narrated before rogues, wicked persons of crooked professions and those bent on conquering others in disputes and arguments. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
न दुर्जनसमाकीर्णे न तु दस्युसमावृते ।
देशे न धूर्तसदने वदेत् पुण्यकथामिमाम् ॥ ३०॥

English
The discourse on this holy story shall not be conducted in a place infested by wicked men, or surrounded by thieves or in the house of a rogue. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कथाविरामः कर्तव्यो मध्यान्हे हि मुहूर्तकम् ।
मलमूत्रोत्सर्जनार्थं तत्कथाकीर्तनान्नरैः ॥ ३१॥

English
The orator shall have an interval of a Muhūrta (forty-eight minutes) at midday for the sake of answering calls of nature. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
वक्त्रा क्षौरं हि सङ्कार्यं दिनादर्वाग्व्रताप्तये ।
कार्यं सङ्क्षेपतो नित्यकर्म सर्वं प्रयत्नतः ॥ ३२॥

English
The discourcer must have his share on the day previous to the discourse so that his vow be maintained. During the days of discourse he shall perform all his daily routine (Sandhyā etc.) briefly. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वक्तुः पार्श्वे सहायार्थमन्यः स्थाप्यस्तथाविधः ।
पण्डितः संशयच्छेत्ता लोकबोधनतत्परः ॥ ३३॥

English
Another scholar equally well-versed in Purāṇas should be sitting near the discourcer to help him. He must be competent to clear doubts and eager to enlighten the people. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
कथाविघ्नविनाशार्थं गणनाथं प्रपूजयेत् ।
कथाधीशं शिवं भक्त्या पुस्तकं च विशेषतः ॥ ३४॥

English
In order to ward off obstacles to the discourse, Gananātha²⁰ should be worshipped. The lord of the story Śiva and the book, Śivapurāṇa, too must be worshipped with piety. (34.)

[fn 20. Gananātha : It is an epithet of Śiva and also of Ganesa . But as the worship of Śiva is mentioned separately in the following line of this verse, the term Gananātha here signifies Ganesa, the son of Śiva and Pārvatī (See V. 54 of this chapter). He is invariably propitiated at the beginning of any important undertaking.]
---

Sanskrit
कथां शिवपुराणस्य श‍ृणुयादादरात्सुधीः ।
श्रोता सुविधिना शुद्धः शुद्धचित्तः प्रसन्नधीः ॥ ३५॥

English
The story of Śivapurāṇa must be listened to with careful attention. The recipient must be intelligent, pure in mind, delighted at the heart and a follower of conventions. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेककर्मविभ्रान्तः कामादिषड्विकारवान् ।
स्त्रैणः पाखण्डवादी च वक्ता श्रोता न पुण्यभाक् ॥ ३६॥

English
If either the discourses or the recipient indulges in too many extraneous activities, is a victim of any of the six base feelings of lust, anger etc,²¹ is enamoured of women or is a heretic he cannot gain any merit. (36.)

[fn 21. Ṣadvikāras : Six causes of perturbation are the following : lust (kāma), anger (krodha), greed (lobha), pride (mada), delusion (moha), envy (matsara).]
---

Sanskrit
लोकचिन्तां धनागारपुत्रचिन्तां व्युदस्य च ।
कथाचित्तः शुद्धमतिः स लभेत् फलमुत्तमम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Casting off the worries of worldly affairs and those of wealth, house and sons if any one of pure mind concentrates his attention on the discourse he will secure the excellent fruit. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रद्धाभक्तिसमायुक्ता नान्यकार्येषु लालसाः ।
वाग्यताः शुचयोऽव्यग्राः श्रोतारः पुण्यभागिनः ॥ ३८॥

English
The recipients who are endowed with faith and piety, do not eagerly pursue other activities and are unruffled, pure and restrained in speech derive great merit. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अभक्ता ये कथां पुण्यां श‍ृण्वन्तीमां नराधमाः ।
तेषां श्रवणजं नास्ति फलं दुःखं भवे भवे ॥ ३९॥

English
Base men of impious nature who listen to this holy story do not have any special merit derived out of it. They will have misery in every birth. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
असम्पूज्य पुराणं ये यथाशक्त्या ह्युपायनैः ।
श‍ृण्वन्तीमां कथां मूढाः स्युर्दरिद्रा न पावनाः ॥ ४०॥

English
Those who do not honour this Purāṇa with presents according to their capacity are fools. Even if they listen to the story they will not be sanctified. They will become indigent. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
कथायां कथ्यमानायां गच्छन्त्यन्यत्र ये नराः ।
भोगान्तरे प्रणश्यन्ति तेषां दारादिसम्पदः ॥ ४१॥

English
Those who walk out of congregation in the middle of the discourse will have the adverse effect: they will face the destruction of their wives and wealth in the midst of enjoyment. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सोष्णीषमस्तका ये च श‍ृण्वन्तीमां कथां नराः ।
तत्पुत्राश्च प्रजायन्ते पापिनः कुलदूषकाः ॥ ४२॥

English
The sons and descendants of the people who attend the discourse with turbaned head, become sinners defiling the whole race. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ताम्बूलं भक्षयन्तो ये श‍ृण्वन्तीमां कथां नराः ।
स्वविष्ठां खादयन्त्येतान्नरके यमकिङ्कराः ॥ ४३॥

English
The attendants of Yama in hell force the people who chewed betel leaves while attending the discourse, to eat their own faeces. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ये च तुङ्गासनारूढाः श‍ृण्वन्तीमां कथां नराः ।
भुक्त्वा ते नरकान् सर्वांस्ततः काका भवन्ति हि ॥ ४४॥

English
Those who listen to the story seated on a more elevated seat fall into hell and after undergoing the tortures there are reborn as crows. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ये वीराद्यासनारूढाः श‍ृण्वन्तीमां कथां शुभाम् ।
भुक्तवा ते नरकान् सर्वान्विषवृक्षा भवन्ति वै ॥ ४५॥

English
Those who listen to this auspicious story seated in the Vīra pose$^{22}$ fall into hell and after undergoing the tortures of hell are reborn as poisonous plants. (45.)

[fn 22. Virāsana also called Paryāṅka bandha. It is a particular kind of posture practised by ascetics in meditation setting on the hams.]

---

Sanskrit
असम्प्रणम्य वक्तारं कथां श‍ृण्वन्ति ये नराः ।
भुक्त्वा ते नरकान् सर्वान् भवन्त्यर्जुनपादपाः ॥ ४६॥

English
Those who listen to the story without bowing to the discourcer at first fall into hell and after undergoing the tortures of hell are reborn as Arjuna trees. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अनातुराः शयाना ये श‍ृण्वन्तीमां कथां नराः ।
भुक्त्वा ते नरकान् सर्वान् भवन्त्यजगरादयः ॥ ४७॥

English
Those who, not being sick, listen to the story lying down, fall into hell and are reborn as pythons etc. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
वक्तुः समासनारूढा ये श‍ृण्वन्ति कथामिमाम् ।
गुरुतल्पसमं पापं प्राप्यते नारकैः सदा ॥ ४८॥

English
Those who listen to the story seated on the same level as the discourcer become as sinful as the defiler of the preceptor's bed and fall into hell. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ये निन्दन्ति च वक्तारं कथां चेमां सुपावनीम् ।
भवन्ति शुनका भुक्त्वा दुःखं जन्मशतं हि ते ॥ ४९॥

English
Those who speak ill of the discourcer or of this sacred story are born as dogs and lead miserable lives in hundred births. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
कथायां वर्तमानायां दुर्वादं ये वदन्ति हि ।
भुक्त्वा ते नरकान् घोरान् भवन्ति गर्दभास्ततः ॥ ५०॥

English
Those who begin to argue and dispute while the discourse is being held fall into hell and after undergoing the tortures there are reborn as donkeys. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिन्नापि श‍ृण्वन्ति कथामेतां सुपावनीम् ।
भुक्त्वा ते नरकान् घोरान् भवन्ति वनसूकराः ॥ ५१॥

English
Those who never listen to this sanctifying story fall into hell. After experiencing the tortures there they are reborn as wild boars. (51.)

Sanskrit
कथायां कीर्त्यमानायां विघ्नं कुर्वन्ति ये खलाः ।
कोट्यब्दं नरकान् भुक्त्वा भवन्ति ग्रामसूकराः ॥ ५२॥

English
The rogues who create hindrances even as the discourse is being held fall into hell. After undergoing the tortures there for millions of years they are reborn as village-boars. (52.)

Sanskrit
एवं विचार्य शुद्धात्मा श्रोता वक्तृसुभक्तिमान् ।
कथाश्रवणहेतोर्हि भवेत् प्रीत्योद्यतः सुधीः ॥ ५३॥

English
Realising all these, the listener shall always be pure, devoted to the discourcer and intelligent enough to listen to the story with devotion. (53.)

Sanskrit
कथाविघ्नविनाशार्थं गणेशं पूजयेत् पुरा ।
नित्यं सम्पाद्य सङ्क्षेपात् प्रायश्चित्तं समाचरेत् ॥ ५४॥

English
For warding off obstacles to the discourse Lord Gaṇeśa should be worshipped at first. Every day at the end of the discourse he shall briefly perform expiatory rites (for omissions and commissions). (54.)

English
He shall worship the nine planets$^{23}$ and the deities in the "Sarvatobhadra" array. He shall worship the book according to the rites of Śiva's adoration. (55.)

At the conclusion of the worship he shall offer prayer to the book identified directly with Śiva, humbly and piously joining his palms in reverence. (56.)

Sanskrit

English
This wish of mine must be fulfilled by Thee. May this narration of the story be concluded without obstacles. (58.)

Sanskrit

English
I am immersed in the middle of the ocean of worldly existence. Please lift me up from it, miserable wretch that I am, with my limbs caught in by the crocodiles of Karman (Action) : O Śaṅkara, I am Thy slave." (59.)

Sanskrit

English
The householder shall thus pray to Śivapurāṇa identified directly with Śiva, in words evoking pity. Then he shall begin the worship of the discourser. (60.)

Sanskrit

English
He shall adore the discourser too in the same manner as in the rite of the worship of Śiva and propitiate him with flowers, cloths, ornaments, incense lamps etc. (61.)

Sanskrit

English
In the presence of the discourser he shall take vow and observe all restraints with a pure mind and the same shall be maintained till the conclusion to the extent of his capacity. (62.)

Sanskrit

English
