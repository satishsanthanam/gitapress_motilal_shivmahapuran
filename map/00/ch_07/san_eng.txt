### **CHAPTER SEVEN - (Description of Do's and Don'ts to those who take up listening to the Śivapurāṇa as a rite and that of the worship of the discourser).**

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
सूत सूत महाप्राज्ञ धन्यस्त्वं शैवपुङ्गव ।
श्रावितेयं कथास्माकमद्भुतेयं शुभावहा ॥ १॥

पुंसां शिवपुराणस्य श्रवणव्रतिनां मुने ।
सर्वलोकहितार्थाय दयया नियमं वद ॥ २॥

English
*Śaunaka said :—*  O Sūta, Sūta of great intellect, thou art foremost among devotees of Śiva and the most blessed. Thou hast narrated this wonderfully auspicious story, O sage, please tell me the rules governing those who perform the rite of listening to Śivapurāṇa, for the benefit of the whole world. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
नियमं श‍ृणु सद्भक्त्या पुंसां तेषां च शौनक ।
नियमात् सत्कथां श्रुत्वा निर्विघ्नफलमुत्तमम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O Śaunaka, listen with devotion to the rules governing those persons. If you hear the excellent story with due observance of the rules, the fruit is excellent and there is no obstacle in the achievement of the fruit. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पुंसां दीक्षाविहीनानां नाधिकारः कथाश्रवे ।
श्रोतुकामैरतो वक्तुर्दीक्षा ग्राह्या च तैर्मुने ॥ ४॥

English
Persons devoid of initiation are not entitled to listen to the story. Hence those who wish to listen must take initiation, O sage, from the discourses. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचर्यमधस्सुप्तिः पत्रावल्यां च भोजनम् ।
कथासमाप्तौ भुक्तिं च कुर्यान्नित्यं कथाव्रती ॥ ५॥

English
The devotee who takes up this rite shall take his daily meal only at the end of the daily discourse. He must observe Brahmacarya (celibacy) during those days. He must lie on the ground and take food only in the Patrāvalli (a number of leaves stitched together to serve the purpose of a plate). (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आसमाप्तपुराणं हि समुपोष्य सुशक्तिमान् ।
श‍ृणुयाद्भक्तितः शुद्धः पुराणं शैवमुत्तमम् ॥ ६॥

English
The man who has the strength in abundance shall observe fast till the conclusion of the whole Purāṇa and listen to the excellent Śivapurāṇa with great devotion and purity. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
घृतपानं पयःपानं कृत्वा वा श‍ृणुयात् सुखम् ।
फलाहारेण वा श्राव्यमेकभुक्तं न वा हि तत् ॥ ७॥

English
He may drink only milk or ghee throughout and listen to the story with pleasure. He may live on fruit diet or take a single meal or even eschew that and proceed with the listening rite. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एकवारं हविष्यान्नं भुञ्ज्यादेतत्कथाव्रती ।
सुखसाध्यं यथा स्यात्तच्छ्रवणं कार्यमेव च ॥ ८॥

English
Or he may take Haviṣyānna (cooked rice soaked in ghee and sacrificially offered) once a day and maintain the rite. The diet part is according to convenience and comfort but the listening shall be strictly maintained. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
भोजनं सुकरं मन्ये कथासु श्रवणप्रदम् ।
नोपवासो वरश्चेत् स्यात् कथाश्रवणविघ्नकृत् ॥ ९॥

English
If there is more facility in hearing let the devotee take food. If observing fast causes hindrance to listening to the story it is not to be recommended. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
गरिष्ठं द्विदलं दग्धं निष्पावांश्च मसूरिकाम् ।
भावदुष्टं पर्युषितं जग्ध्वा नित्यं कथाव्रती ॥ १०॥

वार्ताकं च कलिन्दं च चिचिण्डं मूलकं तथा ।
कूष्माण्डं नालिकेरं च मूलं जग्ध्वा कथाव्रती ॥ ११॥

पलाण्डुं लशुनं हिङ्गुं गृञ्जं मादकं हि तत् ।
वस्तून्यामिषसंज्ञानि वर्जयेद्यः कथाव्रती ॥ १२॥

English
The householder taking the rite shall avoid heavy indigestible pulses like Niṣpāva, Maśūrikā etc., stale food, defiled food, brinjals, gourds, radish, pumpkins, cocoa-nuts, garlic, onion, asafoetida, intoxicating beverages and all kinds of meat. (10-12.)

---

Sanskrit
कामादिषड्विकारं च द्विजानां च विनिन्दनम् ।
पतिव्रतासतां निन्दां वर्जयेद्यः कथाव्रती ॥ १३॥

English
He shall avoid the six base feelings of lust, anger etc., he shall not despise brahmins and bear ill will towards chaste ladies and good men. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
रजस्वलां न पश्येच्च पतितान्न वदेत् कथाम् ।
द्विजद्विषो वेदवर्ज्यान्न वदेद्यः कथाव्रती ॥ १४॥

English
He shall not look at women in their menstrual period. He shall not converse with fallen people, nor talk to haters of brahmins or unbelievers in the Vedas. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यं शौचं दयां मौनमार्जवं विनयं तथा ।
औदार्यं मनसश्चैव कुर्यान्नित्यं कथाव्रती ॥ १५॥

English
The house-holder shall practise and strictly adhere to truthfulness, purity, mercy, restraint in speech, straightforwardness, humility, liberalmindedness and other virtues. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्कामश्च सकामश्च नियमाच्छृणुयात् कथाम् ।
सकामः काममाप्नोति निष्कामो मोक्षमाप्नुयात् ॥ १६॥

English
The householder may listen to the story with any specific desire cherished in his mind or absolutely free from any desire. If he has any desire it will be fulfilled; if he is free from desire he shall attain salvation. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
दरिद्रश्च क्षयी रोगी पापी निर्भाग्य एव च ।
अनपत्योऽपि पुरुषः श‍ृणुयात् सत्कथामिमाम् ॥ १७॥

English
An indigent person, a consumptive, a sinner, an unfortunate person and a person having no child shall hear this excellent story. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
काकवन्ध्यादयः सप्तविधा अपि खलस्त्रियः ।
स्रवद्गर्भा च या नारी ताभ्यां श्राव्या कथा परा ॥ १८॥

English
The seven types of wicked women like Kākavandhyā (a woman having a single child) and those suffering from miscarriages shall hear this story. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां श्रवणं कार्यं स्त्रीभिः पुम्भिश्च यत्नतः ।
एतच्छिवपुराणस्य विधिना च कथां मुने ॥ १९॥

English
Whether women or men, all must hear the story of Śivapurāṇa, O sage, in the manner prescribed. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छिवपुराणस्य पारायणदिनानि वै ।
अत्युत्तमानि बोध्यानि कोटियज्ञसमानि च ॥ २०॥

English
The days of discourse on Śivapurāṇa must be considered very excellent, even on a par with millions of sacrifices. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतेषु विधिना दत्तं यदल्पमपि वस्तु हि ।
दिवसेषु वरिष्ठेषु तदक्षय्यफलं लभेत् ॥ २१॥

English
Gifts duly bestowed on these excellent days, even though they may not be much in quantity, yield everlasting benefit. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वा व्रतविधिं श्रुत्वेमां परमां कथाम् ।
परानन्दयुतः श्रीमानुद्यापनमथाचरेत् ॥ २२॥

English
Observing the rites thus, and listening to the great story the flourishing house-holder shall delightedly perform the Udyāpana rite (at the end of completion). (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदुद्यापनविधिश्चतुर्दश्याः समो मतः ।
कार्यस्तद्वद्धनाढ्यैश्च तदुक्तफलकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ २३॥

English
This Udyāpana rite is on a par with the Caturdaśī rite (observed on the fourteenth day of the lunar month). Rich men who wish to secure the fruits thereof must perform it likewise. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अकिञ्चनेषु भक्तेषु प्रायो नोद्यापनग्रहः ।
श्रवणेनैव पूतास्ते निष्कामाः शाम्भवा मताः ॥ २४॥

English
Indigent devotees usually do not and need not perform the Udyāpana rite. They are sanctified by the listening alone. Pious devotees of Śiva are free from desires. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शिवपुराणस्य पारायणमखोत्सवे ।
समाप्ते श्रोतृभिर्भक्त्या पूजा कार्या प्रयत्नतः ॥ २५॥

English
After the festive celebration of the sacrifice of the discourse on Śivapurāṇa is thus concluded, the listeners shall perform the worship. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवपूजनवत् सम्यक् पुस्तकस्य पुरो मुने ।
पूजा कार्या सुविधिना वक्तुश्च तदनन्तरम् ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, due worship must be performed in front of the book in the manner of the worship of Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पुस्तकाच्छादनार्थं हि नवीनं चासनं शुभम् ।
समर्चयेद् दृढं दिव्यं बन्धनार्थं च सूत्रकम् ॥ २७॥

English
A fine new cloth to cover the book and a strong silken cord to tie it up must be given. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराणार्थं प्रयच्छन्ति ये सूत्रं वसनं नवम् ।
भोगिनो ज्ञानसम्पन्नास्ते भवन्ति भवे भवे ॥ २८॥

English
Those who give silken cord and new cloth for the book of Purāṇa become yogins endowed with knowledge in every birth they take. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
वक्त्रे दद्यान्महार्हाणि वस्तूनि विविधानि च ।
वस्त्रभूषणपात्राणि दिव्यं बहु विशेषतः ॥ २९॥

English
Many kinds of valuable objects, cloth, ornaments, vessels and much wealth in particular should be given to the discourser. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
आसनार्थं प्रयच्छन्ति पुराणस्य च ये नराः ।
कम्बलाजिनवासांसि मञ्चं फलकमेव च ॥ ३०॥

स्वर्गलोकं समासाद्य भुक्त्वा भोगान् यथेप्सितान् ।
स्थित्वा ब्रह्मपदे कल्पं यान्ति शैवपदं ततः ॥ ३१॥

English
Those who give carpets, deer skins, cloth, elevated couches and planks to keep the volume of Purāṇa on, attain heaven, enjoy all desirable pleasures, stay in Brahmā's region for the duration of a Kalpa and finally attain Śiva's region. (30-31.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वा विधानेन पुस्तकस्य प्रपूजनम् ।
वक्तुश्च मुनिशार्दूल महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ ३२॥

सहायार्थं स्थापितस्य पण्डितस्य प्रपूजनम् ।
कुर्यात्तदनुसारेण किञ्चिदूनं धनादिभिः ॥ ३३॥

English
After performing the worship of the book as stipulated, O foremost among sages, and also that of the discourser with great eclat, the scholar who had been appointed assistant should be duly honoured in the same manner but with a smaller sum of money. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
समागतेभ्यो विप्रेभ्यो दद्यादन्नं धनादिकम् ।
महोत्सवः प्रकर्तव्यो गीतैर्वाद्यैश्च नर्तनैः ॥ ३४॥

English
Food and monetary gifts and other things must be given to the brahmin visitors. A great festival must be celebrated with vocal and instrumental music and performance of dances. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
विरक्तश्च भवेच्छ्रोता परेऽहनि विशेषतः ।
गीता वाच्या शिवेनोक्ता रामचन्द्राय या मुने ॥ ३५॥

English
The listener shall gradually become detached and especially on the next day, O sage, the holy Gītā narrated by Śiva to Rāmacandra must be read. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहस्थश्चेद्भवेच्छ्रोता कर्तव्यस्तेन धीमता ।
होमः शुद्धेन हविषा कर्मणस्तस्य शान्तये ॥ ३६॥

English
If the listener is a householder he must perform Homa with pure Havis (holy ghee) for tranquilising the rite. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रसंहितया होमः प्रतिश्लोकेन वा मुने ।
गायत्र्यास्तन्मयत्वाच्च पुराणस्यास्य तत्त्वतः ॥ ३७॥

English
The Homa must be performed with Rudrasaṃhitā or with each verse of Gāyatri, for in fact, this Purāṇa is identical with it, (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा मूलमन्त्रेण पञ्चवर्णेन शैवतः ।
होमाशक्तौ बुधो हौम्यं हविर्दद्याद् द्विजाय तत् ॥ ३८॥

English
or with the Mūlamantra of Śiva of five syllables. If he is incompetent to perform Homa let him give the ghee-offering to a brahmin. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
दोषयोः प्रशमार्थं च न्यूनताधिकताख्ययोः ।
पठेच्च श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या शिवनामसहस्रकम् ॥ ३९॥

English
In order to suppress the defects of deficiency and excess he shall either read or listen with devotion to the thousand names of Śiva. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन स्यात् सफलं सर्वं सफलं नात्र संशयः ।
यतो नास्त्यधिकं त्वस्मात् त्रैलोक्ये वस्तु किञ्चन ॥ ४०॥

English
Undoubtedly, thus, every thing shall be fruitful and the fruit too shall be excellent since there is no greater thing in the three worlds than this. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादशमितान् विप्रान् भोजयेन्मधुपायसैः ।
दद्यात् तेभ्यो दक्षिणां च व्रतपूर्णत्वसिद्धये ॥ ४१॥

English
He shall feed eleven brahmins with honey and milk puddings. He must give them Dakṣiṇā also to complete the rite. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तौ पलत्रयमितस्वर्णेन सुन्दरं मुने ।
सिंहं विधाय तत्रास्य पुराणस्य शुभाक्षरम् ॥ ४२॥

लेखितं लिखितं वापि संस्थाप्य विधिना पुमान् ।
सम्पूज्यावाहनाद्यैश्च ह्युपचारैः सदक्षिणम् ॥ ४३॥

वस्त्रभूषणगन्धाद्यैः पूजिताय यतात्मने ।
आचार्याय सुधीर्दद्याच्छिवसन्तोषहेतवे ॥ ४४॥

English
If he is competent, O sage, he must make an image of a lion with three Palas of gold and either engrave the name of this Purāṇa on it or affix a label with the name written on it. He must worship his preceptor of great restraint with the gifts of cloth, ornaments, scents etc., and hand them over to him for propitiating Śiva. (42-44.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन दानप्रभावेण पुराणस्यास्य शौनक ।
सम्प्राप्यानुग्रहं शैवं मुक्तः स्याद्भवबन्धनात् ॥ ४५॥

English
O Śaunaka, by the power of this gift and of the Purāṇa he shall secure the blessings of Śiva and be freed from the bondage of worldly existence. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृते विधाने च श्रीमच्छिवपुराणकम् ।
सम्पूर्णफलदं स्याद्वै भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायकम् ॥ ४६॥

English
If these rites are performed, the Sivapurāṇa shall yield entire fruit, enjoyment of worldly pleasures and salvation. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कथितं सर्वं किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ।
श्रीमच्छिवपुराणस्य माहात्म्यं सर्वकामदम् ॥ ४७॥

English
Thus I have narrated to you the greatness of Sivapurāṇa that bestows every cherished desire. What else do you wish to hear ? (47.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमच्छिवपुराणं तु पुराणतिलकं स्मृतम् ।
महच्छिवप्रियं रम्यं भवरोगनिवारणम् ॥ ४८॥

English
The Sivapurāṇa holds the mark of distinction among all Purāṇas. It is highly pleasing to Śiva. It wards off the ailment of worldly existence. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ते जन्मभाजः खलु जीवलोके
ये वै सदा ध्यायन्ति विश्वनाथम् ।
वाणी गुणान् स्तौति कथां श‍ृणोति
श्रोत्रद्वयं ते भवमुत्तरन्ति ॥ ४९॥

English
Those who are always engaged in the meditation of Śiva, those whose tongue adores the attributes of Śiva, and those whose ears listen to the story of Śiva, cross the ocean of worldly existence. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
सकलगुणविभेदैर्नित्यमस्पष्टरूपं
जगति च बहिरन्तर्भासमानं महिम्ना ।
मनसि च बहिरन्तर्वाङ्मनोवृत्तिरूपं
परमशिवमनन्तानन्दसान्द्रं प्रपद्ये ॥ ५०॥

English
I seek refuge in Śiva the great, of infinite thickset bliss, Śiva whose form is unaffected by all the three *Guṇas*, Śiva who manifests Himself within and without this world, within and without the mind, Śiva whose form is variously evolved by mental ideas and verbal expressions. (50.)

Sanskrit
इति श्रीस्कान्दे महापुराणे सनत्कुमारसंहितायां श्रीशिवपुराणश्रवणव्रतिनां विधिनिषेधपुस्तकवक्तृपूजनवर्णनं नाम सप्तमोऽध्यायः ॥ ०.७॥
