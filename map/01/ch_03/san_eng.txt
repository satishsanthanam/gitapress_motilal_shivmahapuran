### **CHAPTER THREE - *(The deliberation on the achievable and the means of achievement)* - *Vyāsa said :—***

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः सौतं प्रोचुस्ते परमर्षयः ।
वेदान्तसारसर्वस्वं पुराणं श्रावयाद्भुतम् ॥ १॥

English
On hearing the words of Sūta, the great sages said, “Please narrate the wonderful Purāṇa that fully treats of the essence of Vedānta”. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा मुनीनां स वचनं सुप्रहर्षितः ।
संस्मरञ्छङ्करं सूतः प्रोवाच मुनिसत्तमान् ॥ २॥

English
Very delighted at the request of the sages Sūta meditated on Śiva and spoke to them. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श‍ृण्वन्तु ऋषयः सर्वे स्मृत्वा शिवमनामयम् ।
पुराणप्रवणं शैवं पुराणं वेदसारजम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  Contemplating on Śiva free from ailments may ye all hear this Śivapurāṇa, the foremost among Purāṇas, that amplifies the essence of the Vedas. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र गीतं त्रिकं प्रीत्या भक्तिज्ञानविरागकम् ॥ ४॥

वेदान्तवेद्यं सद्वस्तु विशेषेण प्रवर्णितम् ॥ ५॥

English
Where the trio, Bhakti (Piety) Jñāna (Wisdom) and Vairāgya (non-attachment) has been proclaimed and the object which is knowable only through Vedānta, has been particularly described. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वन्तु ऋषयः सर्वे पुराणं वेदसारजम् ।
पुरा कालेन महता कल्पेऽतीते पुनः पुनः ॥ ६॥

अस्मिन्नुपस्थिते कल्पे प्रवृत्ते सृष्टिकर्मणि ।
मुनीनां षट्कुलीनानां ब्रुवतामितरेतरम् ॥ ७॥

इदं परमिदं नेति विवादः सुमहानभूत् ।
तेऽभिजग्मुर्विधातारं ब्रह्माणं प्रष्टुमव्ययम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  May ye all hear the Purāṇa that embibes the essence of the Vedas. Formerly, when many Kalpas (Aeons) elapsed and this Kalpa started with the process of creation, a great dispute arose among the sages of six clans who held divergent views as to which is great and which is not. They approached Brahmā the Creator, to ask him about the imperishable. (6-8.)

---

Sanskrit
वाग्भिर्विनयगर्भाभिः सर्वे प्राञ्जलयोऽब्रुवन् ।
त्वं हि सर्वजगद्धाता सर्वकारणकारणम् ॥ ९॥

कः पुमान् सर्वतत्त्वेभ्यः पुराणः परतः परः ।
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
यतो वाचो निवर्तन्ते अप्राप्य मनसा सह ॥ १०॥

यस्मात् सर्वमिदं ब्रह्मविष्णुरुद्रेन्द्रपूर्वकम् ।
सह भूतेन्द्रियैः सर्वैः प्रथमं सम्प्रसूयते ॥ ११॥

एष देवो महादेवः सर्वज्ञो जगदीश्वरः ।
अयं तु परया भक्त्या दृश्यते नान्यथा क्वचित् ॥ १२॥

English
All of them with palms joined in reverence addressed him with words couched in humility—“Thou art the creator of the entire universe, the cause of all causes. Who is that Being older than all Principles, the greatest of the great? *Brahmā said :—* “That from whom words recede, not approaching him even with the mind ; that from whom this entire universe beginning with Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra and Indra, along with all elements and all sense-organs, is evolved at first ; he is the lord Mahādeva the omniscient, the lord of the universe. He can be realised by supreme devotion and not by other means. (9-12.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रो हरिर्हरश्चैव तथान्ये च सुरेश्वराः ।
भक्त्या परमया तस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ १३॥

English
Rudra, Hari, Hara and other lords of Devas are ever desirous of seeing Him, moved by great devotion. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुनात्र किमुक्तेन शिवे भक्त्या विमुच्यते ।
प्रसादाद्देवताभक्तिः प्रसादो भक्तिसम्भवः ।
यथेहाङ्कुरतो बीजं बीजतो वा यथाङ्कुरः ॥ १४॥

English
Of what avail is a verbose statement ? One is liberated by devotion unto Śiva. Devotion to the deity is due to His Grace; and His grace is due to devotion just as the seed gives rise to the sprout and the sprout produces the seed. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादीशप्रसादार्थं यूयं गत्वा भुवं द्विजाः ।
दीर्घसत्रं समाकृध्वं यूयं वर्षसहस्रकम् ॥ १५॥

English
Hence, O Brahmins, all of you descend to the earth, to propitiate the Lord. You have to perform a sacrifice of long duration for a thousand years. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अमुष्यैवाध्वरेशस्य शिवस्यैव प्रसादतः ।
वेदोक्तविद्यासारं तु ज्ञायते साध्यसाधनम् ॥ १६॥

English
It is by the grace of Śiva alone who will be the presiding deity of this sacrifice that the means of achievement of the Achievable can be realised and that is the essence of the Vidyā (mystic learning) mentioned in the Vedas. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनयः ऊचुः ।
अथ किं परमं साध्यं किं वा तत्साधनं परम् ।
साधकः कीदृशस्तत्र तदिदं ब्रूहि तत्त्वतः ॥ १७॥

English
*The sages said :—* What is that great Achievable ? What is that great means of achievement ? Of what sort is the performer of the rite ? Please mention these precisely. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
साध्यं शिवपदप्राप्तिः साधनं तस्य सेवनम् ।
साधकस्तत्प्रसादाद्यो नित्यादिफलनिस्पृहः ॥ १८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  The attainment of Śiva's region is the Achievable. Means of achievement is the service rendered unto Him. Sādhaka (the performer of the rite) is the person who is free from desire even for permanence which attitude is the result of His grace. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्म कृत्वा तु वेदोक्तं तदर्पितमहाफलम् ।
परमेशपदप्राप्तिः सालोक्यादिक्रमात्ततः ॥ १९॥

English
Rites mentioned in the Vedas should be performed with the fruits thereof dedicated to Him. Thence, through Sālokya$^{33}$ he attains the feet of the great Lord. (19.)

[fn 33. The devotee attains exemption from further transmigration and his identification with the deity, gradually through four stages ; viz. Sālokya (being in the same world with the deity), (Sāmlpya (nearness to the deity), Sāyujya (intimate union with the deity) and Sārūpya (assimilation to the deity). *SP.* adds Sārṣṭi (9.26) (equality in rank, condition or power) as one of the grades of Mukti.]
---

Sanskrit
तत्तद्भक्त्यनुसारेण सर्वेषां परमं फलम् ।
तत्साधनं बहुविधं साक्षादीशेन बोधितम् ॥ २०॥

English
All attain the great fruit according to the standard in devotion achieved. The ways of achieving these standards are manifold as expounded by Īśa Himself. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्क्षिप्य तत्र वः सारं साधनं प्रब्रवीम्यहम् ।
श्रोत्रेण श्रवणं तस्य वचसा कीर्तनं तथा ॥ २१॥

मनसा मननं तस्य महासाधनमुच्यते ।
श्रोतव्यः कीर्तितव्यश्च मन्तव्यश्च महेश्वरः ॥ २२॥

English
I shall condense the same and tell you the essential means. Listening to the glory of Śiva, glorifying him by means of words, and deliberation in the mind, these constitute the greatest of the means. Maheśvara is to be heard, glorified and meditated upon. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुतिः प्रमाणं नः साधनेनामुना परम् ।
साध्यं व्रजत सर्वार्थसाधनैकपरायणाः ॥ २३॥

English
Thus Śruti³⁴ is our authority. Resorting solely to this great means, all of you attain the Achievable. (23.)

[fn 34. The word Śruti in the Purāṇas does not mean 'sacred tradition' but simply 'tradition'.—Pargiter AIHT. Ch. II.]
---

Sanskrit
प्रत्यक्षं चक्षुषा दृष्ट्वा तत्र लोकः प्रवर्तते ।
अप्रत्यक्षं हि सर्वत्र ज्ञात्वा श्रोत्रेण चेष्टते ॥ २४॥

English
Regarding visible things people see with their eyes and begin their activity. Concerning the invisible everywhere, they know through the ears and activise themselves. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छ्रवणमेवादौ श्रुत्वा गुरुमुखाद् बुधः ।
ततः संसाधयेदन्यत् कीर्तनं मननं सुधीः ॥ २५॥

English
Hence Śravaṇa (listening) is the first rite. The intelligent scholar must listen to the oral explanation of the preceptor and then practise the other rites.—Kīrtana (glorifying) and Manana (deliberation). (25.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रमान्मननपर्यन्ते साधनेऽस्मिन् सुसाधिते ।
शिवयोगो भवेत्तेन सालोक्यादिक्रमाच्छनैः ॥ २६॥

सर्वाङ्गव्याधयः पश्चात् सर्वानन्दश्च लीयते ।
अभ्यासात् क्लेशमेतद् वै पश्चादाद्यन्तमङ्गलम् ॥ २७॥

English
When all the means upto Manana are well exercised, Śivayoga (unification with Śiva) results gradually through Sālokya etc. All the ailments of the body are nullified and supreme bliss is realised. Painful indeed is the process but later on everything becomes auspicious from beginning to end. (26-27.)
