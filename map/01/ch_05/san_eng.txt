### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(The greatness of the phallic emblem of Śiva.)***

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रवणादित्रिकेऽशक्तो लिङ्गं वेरं च शाङ्करम् ।
संस्थाप्य नित्यमभ्यर्च्य तरेत् संसारसागरम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  A person incompetent to perform the three rites of Śravaṇa etc. shall fix the phallic emblem or the image of Śiva and worship them every day. He can thus cross the ocean of worldly existence. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अपि द्रव्यं वहेदेव यथाबलमवञ्चयन् ।
अर्पयेल्लिङ्गवेरार्थमर्चयेदपि सन्ततम् ॥ २॥

English
As far as he can afford, the devotee shall make gifts of wealth too without deceiving others. He shall offer them to the phallic emblem or the image of Śiva. He must worship them constantly. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्डपं गोपुरं तीर्थं मठं क्षेत्रं तथोत्सवम् ।
वस्त्रं गन्धं च माल्यं च धूपं दीपं च भक्तितः ॥ ३॥

विविधान्नं च नैवेद्यमपूपव्यञ्जनैर्युतम् ।
छत्रं ध्वजं च व्यजनं चामरं चापि साङ्गकम् ॥ ४॥

राजोपचारवत् सर्वं धारयेल्लिङ्गवेरयोः ।
प्रदक्षिणां नमस्कारं यथाशक्ति जपं तथा ॥ ५॥

आवाहनादि सर्गान्तं नित्यं कुर्यात् सुभक्तितः ।
इत्थमभ्यर्च्ययन् देवं लिङ्गे वेरे च शाङ्करे ॥ ६॥

सिद्धिमेति शिवप्रीत्या हित्वापि श्रवणादिकम् ।
लिङ्गवेरार्चनामात्रान्मुक्ताः पुर्वे महाजनाः ॥ ७॥

English
The worship must be performed elaborately. Construction of platforms, ornamental portals, monasteries, temples, holy centres, etc., offerings of cloth, scents, garlands, incense, lamps, with due piety; oblations of various cooked rice, pancakes, pies etc. with side dishes ; umbrellas, fans, chowries with all paraphernalia—everything shall be maintained in the worship of Śiva. In fact, all royal homage shall be paid. Circumambulation and obeisance with Japas according to capacity shall be performed. All the different usual rites in worships like invocation shall be maintained with due devotion. A person who worships the phallic emblem or the image in this manner will attain salvation even without Śravaṇa etc. Many noble men of yore have been liberated solely by this simple worship. (3-7.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
वेरमात्रे तु सर्वत्र पूज्यन्ते देवतागणाः ।
लिङ्गे वेरे च सर्वत्र कथं सम्पूज्यते शिवः ॥ ८॥

English
*Sages said :—*  Everywhere the deities are worshipped only in their image. How is it that Śiva is worshipped both in the image and the phallus ? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अहो मुनीश्वराः पुण्यं प्रश्नमेतन्महाद्भुतम् ।
अत्र वक्ता महादेवो नान्योऽस्ति पुरुषः क्वचित् ॥ ९॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O sages, this question is holy and wondrous. Here the speaker is Śiva Himself and not any ordinary person. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेनोक्तं प्रवक्ष्यामि क्रमाद् गुरुमुखाच्छ्रुतम् ।
शिवैको ब्रह्मरूपत्वान्निष्कलः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १०॥

English
I shall tell you what Śiva Himself had said and what I heard from my own preceptor. Śiva alone is glorified as Niṣkala (nameless and formless) since He is identical with supreme Brahman. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
रूपित्वात् सकलस्तद्वत्तस्मात् सकलनिष्कलः ।
निष्कलत्वान्निराकारं लिङ्गं तस्य समागतम् ॥ ११॥

English
He is also Sakala as He has an embodied form. He is both Sakala and Niṣkala. It is in his Niṣkala aspect that the Liṅga$^{37}$ is appropriate. (11.)

[fn 37. Śiva-liṅga : the phallic emblem of Śiva which is universally worshipped.]
---

Sanskrit
सकलत्वात् तथा वेरं साकारं तस्य सङ्गतम् ।
सकलाकलरूपत्वाद् ब्रह्मशब्दाभिधः परः ॥ १२॥

अपि लिङ्गे च वेरे च नित्यमभ्यर्च्यते जनैः ।
अब्रह्मत्वात्तदन्येषां निष्कलत्वं न हि क्वचित् ॥ १३॥

English
In the Sakala aspect the worship of his embodied form is appropriate. Since He has the Sakala and Niṣkala aspects He is worshipped both in the phallic and in the embodied form by the people and is called the highest Brahman. Other deities, not being Brahman, have no Niṣkala aspect anywhere. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्ते निष्कले लिङ्गे नाराध्यन्ते सुरेश्वराः ।
अब्रह्मत्वाच्च जीवत्वात्तथान्ये देवतागणाः ॥ १४॥

English
Hence the deities are not worshipped in the formless phallic symbol. The other deities are both non-Brahman and individual souls. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तूष्णीं सकलमात्रत्वादर्च्यन्ते वेरमात्रके ।
जीवत्वं शङ्करान्येषां ब्रह्मत्वं शङ्करस्य च ॥ १५॥

English
In view of their being embodied alone they are worshipped solely in the bodily form. Śaṅkara has Brahmatva and the others Jivatva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदान्तसारसंसिद्धं प्रणवार्थे प्रकाशनात् ।
एवमेव पुरा पृष्टो मन्दरे नन्दिकेश्वरः ॥ १६॥

English
This has been explained in the meaning of the Praṇava (Om), the essence of Vedānta, by Nandikeśvara$^{38}$ when asked by Sanatkumāra, the intelligent son of Brahmā, at the mountain Mandara. (16.)


[fn 38. Nandikeśvara : One of the attendants of Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमारमुनिना ब्रह्मपुत्रेण धीमता ।
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
शिवान्यदेववश्यानां सर्वेषामपि सर्वतः ॥ १७॥

वेरमात्रं च पूजार्थं श्रुतं दृष्टं च भूरिशः ।
शिवमात्रस्य पूजायां लिङ्गं वेरं च दृश्यते ॥ १८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  The embodied form alone is often observed in the worship of the deities other than Śiva. But both the phallic and the embodied forms are seen only in the worship of Śiva. Hence O benevolent one, please tell me precisely making me understand the truth. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
अतस्तद् ब्रूहि कल्याण तत्त्वं मे साधुबोधनम् ।
नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
अनुत्तरमिमं प्रश्नं रहस्यं ब्रह्मलक्षणम् ॥ १९॥

English
*Nandikeśvara said :—*  It is impossible to answer this question without revealing the secret of Brahman. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
कथयामि शिवेनोक्तं भक्तियुक्तस्य तेऽनघ ।
शिवस्य ब्रह्मरूपत्वान्निष्कलत्वाच्च निष्कलम् ॥ २०॥

लिङ्गं तस्यैव पूजायां सर्ववेदेषु सम्मतम् ।
तस्यैव सकलत्वाच्च तथा सकलनिष्कलम् ॥ २१॥

सकलं च तथा वेरं पूजायां लोकसम्मतम् ।
शिवान्येषां च जीवत्वात् सकलत्वाच्च सर्वतः ॥ २२॥

वेरमात्रं च पूजायां सम्मतं वेदनिर्णये ।
स्वाविर्भावे च देवानां सकलं रूपमेव हि ॥ २३॥

शिवस्य लिङ्गं वेरं च दर्शने दृश्यते खलु ।
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
उक्तं त्वया महाभाग लिङ्गवेरप्रचारणम् ॥ २४॥

English
O sinless one, since you are pious I shall tell you what Śiva Himself has said. Since Śiva has the bodiless aspect in virtue of His being the supreme Brahman, the Niṣkala linga, in conformity with the Vedic implication, is used only in His worship. Since He has an embodied form as well, His embodied form is also worshipped and accepted by all people. According to the decision in the Vedas, the embodied form alone is to be used in the worship of other deities who are only individual souls embodied. Devas have only the embodied aspect in their manifestation. In sacred literature both the phallic and the embodied forms are mentioned for Śiva. (20-24.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्य च तदन्येषां विभज्य परमार्थतः ।
तस्मात्तदेव परमं लिङ्गं वेरादिसम्भवम् ॥ २५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  O Fortunate one, you have explained the worship of phallus and image distinctly for Śiva and the other deities. Hence, O lord of Yogins, I wish to hear the feature of the manifestation of the phallic aspect of Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रोतुमिच्छामि योगीन्द्र लिङ्गाविर्भावलक्षणम् ।
नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु वत्स भवत्प्रीत्या वक्ष्यामि परमार्थतः ॥ २६॥

पुरा कल्पे महाकाले प्रपन्ने लोकविश्रुते ।
अयुध्येतां महात्मानौ ब्रह्मविष्णू परस्परम् ॥ २७॥

English
*Nandikeśvara said :—*  O dear one, out of love for you I shall tell you the truth. Long-long ago, in the famous first Kalpa,³⁹ the noble souls Brahmā and Viṣṇu fought each other. (26-27.)

[fn 39. The term Kalpa in a precise sense means a vast cosmic period but this seems to have been a later application of it, when the scheme of cosmological time was developed. It is often used in a simpler and unspecialized way to mean 'a period of time', 'an age.' This seems to have been its earlier signification, as where it is said 'Purā Kalpe, mahākāle' in old time, long, long ago. In such texts Purākalpa is often used loosely and has the general sense of 'Old time'.]
---

Sanskrit
तयोर्मानं निराकर्तुं तन्मध्ये परमेश्वरः ।
निष्कलस्तम्भरूपेण स्वरूपं समदर्शयत् ॥ २८॥

English
In order to eradicate their arrogance lord Parameśvara showed his unembodied Niṣkala form in the form of a column in their midst. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्वलिङ्गचिह्नत्वात् स्तम्भतो निष्कलं शिवः ।
स्वलिङ्गं दर्शयामास जगतां हितकाम्यया ॥ २९॥

English
He showed his phallus emblem separate, evolved out of the column, with a desire to bless the worlds. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाप्रभृति लोकेषु निष्कलं लिङ्गमैश्वरम् ।
सकलं च तथा वेरं शिवस्यैव प्रकल्पितम् ॥ ३०॥

English
From that time onwards the divine phallus and the embodied image, both, were assigned to Śiva alone. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवान्येषां तु देवानां वेरमात्रं प्रकल्पितम् ।
तत्तद् वेरं तु देवानां तत्तद्भोगप्रदं शुभम् ।
शिवस्य लिङ्गवेरत्वं भोगमोक्षप्रदं शुभम् ॥ ३१॥

English
The embodied form alone was assigned to deities other than Śiva. The different types of the embodied forms of the different Devas yield only enjoyments. In regard to Śiva the phallic emblem and the embodied form together bestow auspicious enjoyment and salvation. (31.)
