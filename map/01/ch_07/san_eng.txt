### **CHAPTER SEVEN - (Śiva manifesting himself as a column of fire in the battlefield)**

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
वत्सकाः स्वस्ति वः कच्चिद्वर्तते मम शासनात् ।
जगच्च देवतावंशः स्वस्वकर्मणि किं न वा ॥ १॥

English
Iśvara said :—  Dear children, hail to ye. I hope the universe and the race of the deities, under my suzerainty, flourish in their respective duties. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रागेव विदितं युद्धं ब्रह्मविष्ण्वोर्मया सुराः ।
भवतामभितापेन पौनरुक्त्येन भाषितम् ॥ २॥

English
O gods, the fight between Brahmā and Viṣṇu is already known to me. This agitation on your part is like a redundant speech. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सस्मितया माध्व्या कुमार परिभाषया ।
समतोषयदम्बायाः स पतिस्तत्सुरव्रजम् ॥ ३॥

English
Thus the consort of Ambā consoled the concourse of devas with honeylike speech sweetened with a smile in the manner of appeasing children. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ युद्धाङ्गणं गन्तुं हरिधात्रोरधीश्वरः ।
आज्ञापयद्गणेशानां शतं तत्रैव संसदि ॥ ४॥

English
In that very assembly the lord announced his desire to go to the battlefield of Hari and Brahmā and accordingly issued His directive to a hundred of the commanders of his attendants. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो वाद्यं बहुविधं प्रयाणाय परेशितुः ।
गणेश्वराश्च सन्नद्धा नानावाहनभूषणाः ॥ ५॥

प्रणवाकारमाद्यन्तं पञ्चमण्डलमण्डितम् ।
आरुरोह रथं भद्रमम्बिकापतिरीश्वरः ।
ससूनुगणमिन्द्राद्याः सर्वेऽप्यनुययुः सुराः ॥ ६॥

English
Different kinds of musical instruments were played to announce the start of the journey of the Lord. The commanders of the attendants were in readiness fully bedecked in their ornaments, seated in their respective vehicles. The lord, consort of Ambikā, mounted the holy chariot shaped like Omkāra from front to the back and embellished in five circular rings. He was accompanied by his sons and Ganas. All the devas, Indra and others, followed. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रध्वजव्यजनचामरपुष्पवर्ष-
सङ्गीतनृत्यनिवहैरपि वाद्यवर्गैः ।
सम्मानितः पशुपतिः परया च देव्या
साकं तयोः समरभूमिमगात् ससैन्यः ॥ ७॥

English
Honoured suitably by the display of banners of various colours, fans, chowries, scattered flowers, music, dance and the instrument players, and accompanied by the great goddess (Pārvatī), Paśupati (Śiva) went to the battle-field with the whole army. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
समीक्ष्य तु तयोर्युद्धं निगूढोऽभ्रं समास्थितः ।
समाप्तवाद्यनिर्घोषः शान्तोरुगणनिःस्वनः ॥ ८॥

English
On espying the battle, the lord vanished in the firmament. The play of the music stopped and the tumult of the Gaṇas subsided. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ब्रह्माच्युतौ वीरौ हन्तुकामौ परस्परम् ।
माहेश्वरेण चास्त्रेण तथा पाशुपतेन च ॥ ९॥

English
There in the battlefield Brahmā and Acyuta desirous of killing each other were awaiting the result of the Māheśvara and the Paśupata weapons hurled by them. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्त्रज्वालैरथो दग्धं ब्रह्मविष्ण्वोर्जगत्त्रयम् ।
ईशोऽपि तं निरीक्ष्याथ ह्यकालप्रलयं भृशम् ॥ १०॥

महानलस्तम्भविभीषणाकृति-
र्बभूव तन्मध्यतले स निष्कलः ॥ ११॥

English
The flames emitted by the two weapons of Brahmā and Viṣṇu burned the three worlds. On seeing this imminent untimely dissolution the bodiless form of Śiva assumed the terrific form of a huge column of fire in their midst. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
ते अस्त्रे चापि सज्वाले लोकसंहरणक्षमे ।
निपेततुः क्षणेनैव ह्याविर्भूते महानले ॥ १२॥

English
The two weapons of fiery flame potential enough to destroy the entire world fell into the huge column of fire that manifested itself there instantaneously. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तदद्भुतं चित्रमस्त्रशान्तिकरं शुभम् ।
किमेतदद्भुताकारमित्यूचुश्च परस्परम् ॥ १३॥

English
Seeing that auspicious wonderful phenomenon assuaging the weapons they asked each other "What is this wonderful form ?" (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीन्द्रियमिदं स्तम्भमग्निरूपं किमुत्थितम् ।
अस्योर्ध्वमपि चाधश्च आवयोर्लक्ष्यमेव हि ॥ १४॥

English
"What is this column of fire that has risen up ? It is beyond the range of senses. We have to find out its top and bottom." (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इति व्यवस्थितौ वीरौ मिलितौ वीरमानिनौ ।
तत्परौ तत्परीक्षार्थं प्रतस्थातेऽथ सत्वरम् ॥ १५॥

English
Jointly deciding like this, the two heroes proud of their prowess immediately set about assiduously in their quest. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आवयोर्मिश्रयोस्तत्र कार्यमेकं न सम्भवेत् ।
इत्युक्त्वा सूकरतनुर्विष्णुस्तस्यादिमीयिवान् ॥ १६॥

तथा ब्रह्मा हंसतनुस्तदन्तं वीक्षितुं ययौ ।
भित्त्वा पातालनिलयं गत्वा दूरतरं हरिः ॥ १७॥

नापश्यत्तस्य संस्थानं स्तम्भस्यानलवर्चसः ।
श्रान्तः स सूकरहरिः प्राप पूर्वं रणाङ्गणम् ॥ १८॥

English
"Nothing will turn up if we are together". Saying this, Viṣṇu assumed the form of a Boar and went in search of the root. Brahmā in the form of a swan went up in search of the top. Piercing through the netherworlds and going very far below, Viṣṇu could not see the root of the fiery column. Utterly exhausted, Viṣṇu in the form of a Boar returned to the former battle-ground. (16-18.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ गच्छंस्तु व्योम्ना च विधिस्तात पिता तव ।
ददर्श केतकीपुष्पं किञ्चिद्विच्युतमद्भुतम् ॥ १९॥

English
Dear one, your father, Brahmā who went high up in the sky saw a certain bunch of Ketakī flower of mysterious nature falling from above. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अतिसौरभ्यमम्लानं बहुवर्षच्युतं तथा ।
अन्वीक्ष्य च तयोः कृत्यं भगवान् परमेश्वरः ॥ २०॥

परिहासं तु कृतवान्कम्पनाच्चलितं शिरः ।
तस्मात्तावनुगृह्णातुं च्युतं केतकमुत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing the mutual fight of Brahmā and Viṣṇu, lord Śiva laughed. When his head shook, the Ketakī flower dropped down. Although it had been in its downward course for many years, neither its fragrance nor its lustre had been diminished even a bit. The flower had been intended to bless them. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
किं त्वं पतसि पुष्पेश पुष्पराट् केन वै धृतः ।
आदिमस्याप्रमेयस्य स्तम्भमध्याच्च्युतश्चिरम् ॥ २२॥

न सम्पश्यामि तस्मात्त्वं जह्याशामन्तदर्शने ।
अस्यान्तस्य च सेवार्थं हंसमूर्तिरिहागतः ॥ २३॥

English
(Brahmā said) "O lord of flowers, by whom had you been worn? Why do you fall? I have come here to seek out the top, in the form of a swan." (The flower replied) "I am falling down from the middle of this primordial column that is inscrutable. It has taken me a long time. Hence I do not see how you can see the top." (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
इतः परं सखे मेऽद्य त्वया कर्तव्यमीप्सितम् ।
मया सह त्वया वाच्यमेतद्विष्णोश्च सन्निधौ ॥ २४॥

स्तम्भान्तो वीक्षितो धात्रा तत्र साक्ष्यहमच्युत ।
इत्युक्त्वा केतकं तत्र प्रणनाम पुनः पुनः ।
असत्यमपि शस्तं स्यादापदीत्यनुशासनम् ॥ २५॥

English
"Dear friend, hereafter you must do as I desire. In the presence of Viṣṇu you must say like this. O Acyuta, the top of the column has been seen by Brahmā. I am the witness for the same." Saying this he bowed to the Ketakī flower again and again. Even falsehood is recommended in times of danger. So say the authoritative texts. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
समीक्ष्य तत्राच्युतमायतश्रमं
प्रनष्टहर्षं तु ननर्त हर्षात् ।
उवाच चैनं परमार्थमच्युतं
षण्ढात्तवादः स विधिस्ततोऽच्युतम् ॥ २६॥

English
(Returning to the original place) on seeing Viṣṇu there, utterly exhausted and lacking pleasure, Brahmā danced with joy. Viṣṇu, in the manner of a eunuch admitting his inability (to a woman), told him the truth (that he could not see the bottom). But Brahmā told Viṣṇu like this. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तम्भाग्रमेतत् समुदीक्षितं हरे
तत्रैव साक्षी ननु केतकं त्विदम् ।
ततोऽवदत्तत्र हि केतकं मृषा
तथेति तद्धातृवचस्तदन्तिके ॥ २७॥

हरिश्च तत्सत्यमितीव चिन्तयं-
श्चकार तस्मै विधये नमः स्वयम् ।
षोडशैरुपचारैश्च पूजयामास तं विधिम् ॥ २८॥

English
"O Hari, the top of this column has been seen by me. This Ketakī flower is my witness." The Ketaka flower repeated the falsehood endorsing the words of Brahmā in his presence. Hari, taking it to be true, made obeisance to Brahmā. He worshipped Brahmā with all the sixteen means of service and homage.⁴² (27-28.)

[fn 42. Śoṭaśopacāra: The sixteen acts of homage to a deity are mentioned in *ŚP* 11. 25-29. They are differently enumerated elsewhere : आसन् स्वागतं पाथमध्यमाचमनीषकम् । मधुपक्षोचमस्नानं वसनामरथानि च । गन्धपुष्पे पूषदीपी नैवेद्यं वन्दनं तथा । Tantrasāra enumerates 64 Upacāras.]
---

Sanskrit
विधिं प्रहर्तुं शठमग्नलिङ्गतः
स ईश्वरस्तत्र बभूव साकृतिः ।
समुत्थितः स्वामिविलोकनात् पुनः
प्रकम्पपाणिः परिगृह्य तत्पदम् ॥ २९॥

English
The Lord taking up a visible form in order to chastise Brahmā who practised trickery, came out of the column of fire. On seeing the lord, Viṣṇu stood up and with his hands shaking with fear caught hold of the lord's feet. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
आद्यन्तहीनवपुषि त्वयि मोहबुद्ध्या
भूयान् विमर्श इह नावति कामनोत्थः ।
स त्वं प्रसीद करुणाकर कश्मलं नौ
मृष्टं क्षमस्व विहितं भवतैव केल्या ॥ ३०॥

English
It is out of ignorance and delusion about you whose body is without a beginning or an end that we indulged in this quest prompted by our own desire. Hence O, Sympathetic Being, forgive us for our fault. In fact, it is but another form of your divine sport. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
वत्स प्रसन्नोऽस्मि हरे यतस्त्व-
मीशत्वमिच्छन्नपि सत्यवाक्यम् ।
ब्रूयास्ततस्ते भविता जनेषु
साम्यं मया सत्कृतिरप्यलप्सि ॥ ३१॥

English
# *Īśvara said*  "O dear Hari, I am pleased with you, because you strictly adhered to truth in spite of your desire to be a lord. Hence among the general public you will have a footing equal to mine. You will be honoured too likewise. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
इतः परं ते पृथगात्मनश्च
क्षेत्रप्रतिष्ठोत्सवपूजनं च ॥ ३२॥

English
Hereafter you will be separate from me having separate temple, installation of idols, festivals and worship." (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इति देवः पुरा प्रीतः सत्येन हरये परम् ।
ददौ स्वसाम्यमत्यर्थं देवसङ्घे च पश्यति ॥ ३३॥

English
Thus, formerly, the lord was delighted by the truthfulness of Hari and offered him a footing equal to his own even as the assembly of the devas was witnessing the same. (33.)
