### **CHAPTER NINE - # (The Proclamation of Śiva as Maheśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
तत्रान्तरे तौ च नाथं प्रणम्य विधिमाधवौ ।
बद्धाञ्जलिपुटौ तूष्णीं तस्थतुर्दक्षवामगौ ॥ १॥

English
Nandikeśvara said :— In the mean time Brahmā and Viṣṇu had been standing silently on either side of the lord with the palms joined in reverence. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र संस्थाप्य तौ देवं सकुटुम्बं वरासने ।
पूजयामासतुः पूज्यं पुण्यैः पुरुषवस्तुभिः ॥ २॥

English
Then they installed the lord with all the members of His family on a splendid seat and worshipped Him with all holy personal things. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पौरुषं प्राकृतं वस्तु ज्ञेयं दीर्घाल्पकालिकम् ।
हारनूपुरकेयूरकिरीटमणिकुण्डलैः ॥ ३॥

यज्ञसूत्रोत्तरीयस्रक्क्षौममाल्याङ्गुलीयकैः ।
पुष्पताम्बूलकर्पूरचन्दनागुरुलेपनैः ॥ ४॥

धूपदीपसितच्छत्रव्यजनध्वजचामरैः ।
अन्यैर्दिव्योपहारैश्च वाङ्मनोतीतवैभवैः ॥ ५॥

पतियोग्यैः पश्वलभ्यैस्तौ समार्चयतां पतिम् ।
यद्यच्छ्रेष्ठतमं वस्तु पतियोग्यं हितध्वज ॥ ६॥

English
The personal things constitute those natural things of long and short duration such as necklaces, anklets, bracelets coronets, ear-rings, sacrificial threads, upper cloth of lace border, garlands, silk cloth chokers, rings, flowers, betel leaves, camphor, sandal paste, Aguru unguents, incense, lamps, white umbrella, fans, banners, chowries and other divine offerings whose greatness cannot be expressed or even thought of. Both of them adored the lord with all these things worthy of the lord and inaccessible to Paśu (the animal i.e. the individual soul). All excellent things are worthy of the lord, O brahmin.* (3-6.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वस्त्वखिलमीशोऽपि पारम्पर्यचिकीर्षया ।
सभ्यानां प्रददौ हृष्टः पृथक् तत्र यथाक्रमम् ॥ ७॥

English
In order to set up a precedence the delighted lord handed over all those articles to the attendants assembled according to the order of priority. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
कोलाहलो महानासीत्तत्र तद्वस्तु गृह्यताम् ।
तत्रैव ब्रह्मविष्णुभ्यां चार्चितः शङ्करः पुरा ॥ ८॥

प्रसन्नः प्राह तौ नम्रौ सस्मितं भक्तिवर्धनः ।
ईश्वर उवाच ।
तुष्टोऽहमद्य वां वत्सौ पूजयास्मिन् महादिने ॥ ९॥

दिनमेतत्ततः पुण्यं भविष्यति महत्तरम् ।
शिवरात्रिरिति ख्याता तिथिरेषा मम प्रिया ॥ १०॥

English
The bustle of those who came to receive them was too much. It was there that Brahmā and Viṣṇu adored Śaṅkara at first. When they stood there humbly, the gratified lord spoke smilingly heightening their devotion. Iśvara said :— Dear children, I am delighted at your worship on this holy day. Henceforth this day will be famous as “Śivarātrī” the holiest of holy days pleasing to me. (8-10.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्काले तु यः कुर्यात् पूजां मल्लिङ्गवेरयोः ।
कुर्यात् स जगतः कृत्यं स्थितिसर्गादिकं पुमान् ॥ ११॥

English
He who performs the worship of my phallic emblem and the embodied image on this day will be competent to perform the task of creation and the maintenance etc. of the universe. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवरात्रावहोरात्रं निराहारो जितेन्द्रियः ।
अर्चयेद्वा यथान्यायं यथाबलमवञ्चकः ॥ १२॥

English
The devotee shall observe fast on Śivarātrī, both during the day and the night. He shall perfectly restrain his sense-organs. He shall adore (with flowers) to the extent of his strength. He shall not deceive any one. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्फलं मम पूजायां वर्षमेकं निरन्तरम् ।
तत्फलं लभते सद्यः शिवरात्रौ मदर्चनात् ॥ १३॥

English
By the worship on Śivarātrī day the devotee attains that fruit which usually accrues to one who continuously worships me for a year. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्धर्मवृद्धिकालोऽयं चन्द्रकाल इवाम्बुधेः ।
प्रतिष्ठाद्युत्सवो यत्र मामको मङ्गलायनः ॥ १४॥

English
This is the time when the virtue of devotion to me increases like the tide in the ocean at the rise of the moon. Festivities like the installation of my idols etc. on that day are very auspicious. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्पुनः स्तम्भरूपेण स्वाविरासमहं पुरा ।
स कालो मार्गशीर्षे तु स्यादार्द्राऋक्षमर्भकौ ॥ १५॥

English
The day on which I manifested myself in the form of a column of fire is the Ārdrā star in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa (November-December), O children. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्द्रायां मार्गशीर्षे तु यः पश्येन्मामुमासखम् ।
मद्वेरमपि वा लिङ्गं स गुहादपि मे प्रियः ॥ १६॥

English
He who sees me on the day of Ārdrā star in the month of Mārgaśīrṣa in the company of Umā and worships my phallic emblem or embodied image is dearer to me than even Guha (Kārtikeya). (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अलं दर्शनमात्रेण फलं तस्मिन् दिने शुभे ।
अभ्यर्चनं चेदधिकं फलं वाचामगोचरम् ॥ १७॥

English
On that auspicious day the vision alone accords ample results. If he worships too, the result cannot be adequately described. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
रणरङ्गतलेऽमुष्मिन् यदहं लिङ्गवर्ष्मणा ।
जृम्भितो लिङ्गवत्तस्माल्लिङ्गस्थानमिदं भवेत् ॥ १८॥

English
Since I manifested myself in the form of phallic emblem in the field of battle, this place will be known as Liṅgasthāna. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अनाद्यन्तमिदं स्तम्भमणुमात्रं भविष्यति ।
दर्शनार्थं हि जगतां पूजनार्थं हि पुत्रकौ ॥ १९॥

English
O sons, this column without root or top will henceforth be diminutive in size for the sake of the vision and worship of the world. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
भोगावहमिदं लिङ्गं भुक्तिमुक्त्येकसाधनम् ।
दर्शनस्पर्शनध्यानाज्जन्तूनां जन्ममोचनम् ॥ २०॥

English
The phallic emblem confers enjoyment. It is the only means of worldly enjoyment and salvation. Viewed, touched or meditated upon, it wards off all future births of the living beings. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अनलाचलसङ्काशं यदिदं लिङ्गमुत्थितम् ।
अरुणाचलमित्येव तदिदं ख्यातिमेष्यति ॥ २१॥

English
Since the phallic emblem rose high resembling a mountain of fire, this shall be famous as Ruddy (Aruṇa) mountain.⁴⁴ (21.)

[fn 44. Aruṇācala : The Aruṇa mountain lies to the west of Kailāsa and is the abode of Śiva (Vā. 47. 17-18; Br. II. 18. 18; Sk. III. 59-61; IV 9. 13. 21. 37; also Kern : M. I. P. 3; See Awasthi : Studies in Skanda Puraṇa P. 54.]
---

Sanskrit
अत्र तीर्थं च बहुधा भविष्यति महत्तरम् ।
मुक्तिरप्यत्र जन्तूनां वासेन मरणेन च ॥ २२॥

English
Many holy centres will spring up here. A residence or death in this holy place ensures liberation. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
रथोत्सवादिकल्याणं जनावासं तु सर्वतः ।
अत्र दत्तं हुतं जप्तं सर्वं कोटिगुणं भवेत् ॥ २३॥

English
The celebration of chariot festivals, the congregation of devotees, the presentation of ordinary as well as sacrificial gifts and offering of prayers at this place shall be millionfold efficacious. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्क्षेत्रादपि सर्वस्मात् क्षेत्रमेतन्महत्तरम् ।
अत्र संस्मृतिमात्रेण मुक्तिर्भवति देहिनाम् ॥ २४॥

English
Of all my sectors this sector shall be the greatest. A mere remembrance of me at this place shall accord salvation to all souls. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मान्महत्तरमिदं क्षेत्रमत्यन्तशोभनम् ।
सर्वकल्याणसम्पूर्णं सर्वमुक्तिकरं शुभम् ॥ २५॥

English
Hence this sector shall be greater than all other sectors, very auspicious, full of all sorts of welfare and according salvation to everyone. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्चयित्वात्र मामेव लिङ्गे लिङ्गिनमीश्वरम् ।
सालोक्यं चैव सामीप्यं सारूप्यं सार्ष्टिरेव च ॥ २६॥

सायुज्यमिति पञ्चैते क्रियादीनां फलं मतम् ।
सर्वेऽपि यूयं सकलं प्राप्स्यथाशु मनोरथम् ॥ २७॥

English
Worshipping me in my supreme phallic form at this place and performing the other sacred rites shall accord the five types of salvation—Sālokya, Sāmīpya, Sārūpya, Sārṣṭi and Sāyujya. May all of you achieve all your cherished desires. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
इत्यनुगृह्य भगवान् विनीतौ विधिमाधवौ ।
यत्पूर्वं प्रहतं युद्धे तयोः सैन्यं परस्परम् ॥ २८॥

तदुत्थापयदत्यर्थं स्वशक्त्यामृतधारया ।
तयोर्मौढ्यं च वैरं च व्यपनेतुमुवाच तौ ॥ २९॥

English
*Nandikeśvara said :—*  Thus blessing Brahmā and Viṣṇu who had been made humble, the lord resuscitated by His nectar-like power all the soldiers of the two deities that had been killed in the battle before and spoke to them in order to remove their foolishness and mutual enmity. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
सकलं निष्कलं चेति स्वरूपद्वयमस्ति मे ।
नान्यस्य कस्यचित्तस्मादन्यः सर्वोऽप्यनीश्वरः ॥ ३०॥

English
I have two forms : the manifest and the unmanifest. No one else has these two forms. Hence all else are non-Iśvaras. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरस्तात् स्तम्भरूपेण पश्चाद् रूपेण चार्भकौ ।
ब्रह्मत्वं निष्कलं प्रोक्तमीशत्वं सकलं तथा ॥ ३१॥

द्वयं ममैव संसिद्धं न मदन्यस्य कस्यचित् ।
तस्मादीशत्वमन्येषां युवयोरपि न क्वचित् ॥ ३२॥

English
Dear sons, first in the form of the column and afterwards in this embodied form I have expounded to you my formless Brahma-hood, and embodied Iśa-hood. These two are present only in me and not in anyone else. Hence no one else, not even you too can claim Iśatva (Iśa-hood). (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
तदज्ञानेन वां वृत्तमीशमानं महाद्भुतम् ।
तन्निराकर्तुमत्रैवमुत्थितोऽहं रणक्षितौ ॥ ३३॥

English
It is out of your ignorance of this fact that you were swept away by your false prestige and pride of being Iśa, surprising as it is. I rose up in the middle of the battle-field for quelling the same. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
त्यजतं मानमात्मीयं मयीशे कुरुतं मतिम् ।
मत्प्रसादेन लोकेषु सर्वोऽप्यर्थः प्रकाशते ॥ ३४॥

English
Cast off your false pride. Fix your thought in me as your lord. It is out of my favour that all the objects in the world are illuminated. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरूक्तिर्व्यञ्जकं तत्र प्रमाणं वा पुनः पुनः ।
ब्रह्मतत्त्वमिदं गूढं भवत्प्रीत्या भणाम्यहम् ॥ ३५॥

English
The statement of the preceptor is the reminder and the authority on all occasions. This secret truth of Brahman I am revealing to you out of love. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अहमेव परं ब्रह्म मत्स्वरूपं कलाकलम् ।
ब्रह्मत्वादीश्वरश्चाहं कृत्यं मेऽनुग्रहादिकम् ॥ ३६॥

English
I am the supreme Brahman. My form is both manifest and unmanifest in view of my Brahma-hood and Īśvaratva. My duty is blessing etc. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहत्त्वाद् बृंहणत्वाच्च ब्रह्माहं ब्रह्मकेशवौ ।
समत्वाद्व्यापकत्वाच्च तथैवात्माहमर्भकौ ॥ ३७॥

English
O Brahmā and Viṣṇu, I am Brahman because of Bṛhatva (huge size) and Bṛṛhaṇatva (causing to grow). O children, similarly I am Ātman due to Samatva (equality) and Vyāpakatva (Pervasiveness). (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अनात्मानः परे सर्वे जीवा एव न संशयः ।
अनुग्रहाद्यं सर्गान्तं जगत्कृत्यं च पञ्चकम् ॥ ३८॥

ईशत्वादेव मे नित्यं न मदन्यस्य कस्यचित् ।
आदौ ब्रह्मत्वबुद्ध्यर्थं निष्कलं लिङ्गमुत्थितम् ॥ ३९॥

English
All others are Anātmans, individual souls undoubtedly. There are five activities$^{45}$ in respect of the universe beginning with Anugraha$^{46}$ (liberation) and ending with Sarga (creation). Therefore these activities devolve on me because I am Īśa and not on anyone else. It is to make my Brahmatva understood that my phallic emblem rose up. (38-39.)

[fn 45. In respect of creation, *ŚP* speaks of different five activities in the Vāyavīya Saṃhitā 9. 4-5.]

[fn 46. The text reads 'anugrahādyam Sargāntam' *i. e.* beginning with liberation and ending with creation. But correctly it should be anugrahāntam Sargādyam' *i. e.* beginning with creation and ending with liberation. The correct process of activities is mentioned in the following Chapter, Verses 3-5.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्मादज्ञातमीशत्वं व्यक्तं द्योतयितुं हि वाम् ।
सकलोऽहमतो जातः साक्षादीशस्तु तत्क्षणात् ॥ ४०॥

English
In order to clarify my Īśatva, unknown hitherto, I have manifested myself immediately in the embodied form of Īśa. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सकलत्वमतो ज्ञेयमीशत्वं मयि सत्वरम् ।
यदिदं निष्कलं स्तम्भं मम ब्रह्मत्वबोधकम् ॥ ४१॥

English
The Īśatva in me is to be known as the embodied form and this symbolic column is indicative of my Brahmatva. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गलक्षणयुक्तत्वान्मम लिङ्गं भवेदिदम् ।
तदिदं नित्यमभ्यर्यं युवाभ्यामत्र पुत्रकौ ॥ ४२॥

English
Since it has all the characteristic features of my phallic emblem, it shall be my symbol. O sons, you shall worship it every day. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
मदात्मकमिदं नित्यं मम सान्निध्यकारणम् ।
महत्पूज्यमिदं नित्यमभेदाल्लिङ्गलिङ्गिनोः ॥ ४३॥

English
The phallic symbol and the symbolised Śiva are non-different. Hence this phallic emblem is identical with me. It brings devotees quite near to me. It is worthy of worship therefore. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र प्रतिष्ठितं येन मदीयं लिङ्गमीदृशम् ।
तत्र प्रतिष्ठितः सोऽहमप्रतिष्ठोऽपि वत्सकौ ॥ ४४॥

English
O dear sons, if phallic emblem of this sort in installed I can be considered installed, though my idol is not installed. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

द्वितीये स्थापिते लिङ्गे मदैक्यं फलमेव हि ॥ ४५॥

English
In respect of creation, *ŚP* speaks of different five activities in the Vāyavīya Saṃhitā 9. 4-5. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
