### **CHAPTER TEN - *(The Evanescence of Śiva after expounding the Five-fold duties and the Omkāra mantra to Brahmā and Viṣṇu)***

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मविष्णू ऊचतुः
सर्गादिपञ्चकृत्यस्य लक्षणं ब्रूहि नौ प्रभो ।
शिव उवाच ।
मत्कृत्यबोधनं गुह्यं कृपया प्रब्रवीमि वाम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā and Viṣṇu said :—*  O Lord, please tell us the characteristic feature of the five-fold duties beginning with creation. *Śiva said :—* I shall tell you the great secret of the five-fold duties, out of compassion for you. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिः स्थितिश्च संहारस्तिरोभावोऽप्यनुग्रहः ।
पञ्चैव मे जगत्कृत्यं नित्यसिद्धमजाच्युतौ ॥ २॥

English
O Brahmā and Viṣṇu, the permanent cycle of the five-fold duties consists of creation, maintenance, annihilation, concealment, and blessing. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्गः संसारसंरम्भस्तत्प्रतिष्ठा स्थितिर्मता ।
संहारो मर्दनं तस्य तिरोभावस्तदुत्क्रमः ॥ ३॥

English
Sarga is the creation of the world ; Sthiti is its maintenance; Saṃhāra is the annihilation; Tirobhāva is the removal and concealment (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मोक्षोऽनुग्रहस्तन्मे कृत्यमेवं हि पञ्चकम् ।
कृत्यमेतद्वहत्यन्यस्तूष्णीं गोपुरबिम्बवत् ॥ ४॥

English
Liberation (from the cycle of birth and death) is blessing. These five are my activities but are carried on by others silently as in the case of the statue at the Portal. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्गादि यच्चतुःकृत्यं संसारपरिजृम्भणम् ।
पञ्चमं मुक्तिहेतुर्वै नित्यं मयि च सुस्थिरम् ॥ ५॥

English
The first four activities concern the evolution of the world and the fifth one is the cause of salvation. All these constitute my prerogatives. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिदं पञ्चभूतेषु दृश्यते मामकैर्जनैः ।
सृष्टिर्भूमौ स्थितिस्तोये संहारः पावके तथा ॥ ६॥

तिरोभावोऽनिले तद्वदनुग्रह इहाम्बरे ।
सृज्यते धरया सर्वमद्भिः सर्वं प्रवर्धते ॥ ७॥

अर्द्यते तेजसा सर्वं वायुना चापनीयते ।
व्योम्नानुगृह्यते सर्वं ज्ञेयमेवं हि सूरिभिः ॥ ८॥

English
These activities are observed in the five elements by devotees—Sarga (creation) in the Earth, Sthiti (maintenance) in the waters, Saṃhāra (annihilation) in the fire, Tirobhāva (concealment) in the wind and Anugraha (liberation, the blessed state) in the firmament. Everything is created by the Earth; everything flourishes by virtue of the waters; everything is urged by the fire, everything is removed by the wind and everything is blessed by the firmament. Thus intelligent men must know the same. (6-8.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चकृत्यमिदं वोढुं ममास्ति मुखपञ्चकम् ।
चतुर्दिक्षु चतुर्वक्त्रं तन्मध्ये पञ्चमं मुखम् ॥ ९॥

English
In order to look after these five-fold activities I have five faces, four in the four quarters and the fifth in the middle. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
युवाभ्यां तपसा लब्धमेतत्कृत्यद्वयं सुतौ ।
सृष्टिस्थित्यभिधं भाग्यं मत्तः प्रीतादतिप्रियम् ॥ १०॥

English
O sons, in view of your austerities you two have received the first two activities:—creation and maintenance. You have gratified me and are blessed therefore. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा रुद्रमहेशाभ्यामन्यत्कृत्यद्वयं परम् ।
अनुग्रहाख्यं केनापि लब्धुं नैव हि शक्यते ॥ ११॥

English
Similarly, the other two activities (annihilation and concealment) have been assigned to Rudra and Maheśa. The fifth one of Anugraha (liberation) cannot be taken up by any other. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सर्वं पौर्विकं कर्म युवाभ्यां कालविस्मृतम् ।
न तद् रुद्रमहेशाभ्यां विस्मृतं कर्म तादृशम् ॥ १२॥

English
All this previous arrangement has been forgotten by both of you due to lapse of time, not so by Rudra and Maheśa. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
रूपे वेषे च कृत्ये च वाहने चासने तथा ।
आयुधादौ च मत्साम्यमस्माभिस्तत्कृते कृतम् ॥ १३॥

English
I have assigned them my equality in form, dress, activity, vehicle, seat, weapons etc. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्ध्यानविरहाद्वत्सौ मौढ्यं वामेवमागतम् ।
मज्ज्ञाने सति मैवं स्यान्मानं रूपं महेशवत् ॥ १४॥

English
O dear sons, your delusion was the result of your not meditating upon me. If you had retained my knowledge you would not have embibed this false pride of being Maheśa yourselves. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मान्मज्ज्ञानसिद्ध्यर्थं मन्त्रमोङ्कारनामकम् ।
इतः परं प्रजपतं मामकं मानभञ्जनम् ॥ १५॥

English
Hence, hereafter, both of you shall start reciting the mantra Oṅkāra to acquire knowledge of me. It shall quell your false pride as well. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
उपादिशं निजं मन्त्रमोङ्कारमुरुमङ्गलम् ।
ऊँकारो मन्मुखाज्जज्ञे प्रथमं मत्प्रबोधकः ॥ १६॥

English
I have taught this great auspicious mantra. Oṅkāra came out of my mouth. Originally it indicated me. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वाचकोऽयमहं वाच्यो मन्त्रोऽयं हि मदात्मकः ।
तदनुस्मरणं नित्यं ममानुस्मरणं भवेत् ॥ १७॥

English
It is the indicator and I am the indicated. This mantra is identical with me. The repetition of this mantra is verily my repeated remembrance. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अकार उत्तरात् पूर्वमुकारः पश्चिमाननात् ।
मकारो दक्षिणमुखाद् बिन्दुः प्राङ्मुखतस्तथा ॥ १८॥

नादो मध्यमुखादेवं पञ्चधासौ विजृम्भितः ।
एकीभूतः पुनस्तद्वदोमित्येकाक्षरोऽभवत् ॥ १९॥

English
The syllable “A” came first from northern face; the syllable “U” from the western; the syllable “M” from the southern and the Bindu (dot) from the eastern face. The Nāda (mystical sound) came from the middle face. Thus the complete set cropped up in five-fold form. Then all of them united in the syllable of “Om”. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
नामरूपात्मकं सर्वं वेदभूतकुलद्वयम् ।
व्याप्तमेतेन मन्त्रेण शिवशक्त्योश्च बोधकः ॥ २०॥

English
The two sets of created beings—Nāma (name) and Rūpa (form) are pervaded by this mantra. It indicates Śiva and Śakti. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मात् पञ्चाक्षरं जज्ञे बोधकं सकलस्य तत् ।
अकारादिक्रमेणैव नकारादि यथाक्रमम् ॥ २१॥

English
From this also is born the five-syllabled mantra (Namaśśivāya). It indicates all knowledge. The syllables “NA” etc. follow the order of the syllables “A” etc. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मात् पञ्चाक्षराज्जाता मातृकाः पञ्चभेदतः ।
तस्माच्छिरश्चतुर्वक्त्रात्त्रिपाद्गायत्रिरेव हि ॥ २२॥

English
From the five-syllabled mantra the five mothers were born. The Śiromantra is born of that. The three-footed Gāyatri also came out of the four faces. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदः सर्वस्ततो जज्ञे ततो वै मन्त्रकोटयः ।
तत्तन्मन्त्रेण तत्सिद्धिः सर्वसिद्धिरितो भवेत् ॥ २३॥

English
The entire set of Vedas and crores of mantras were born of that. Different things are achieved through different mantras but everything is achieved through Oṅkāra alone. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेन मन्त्रकन्देन भोगो मोक्षश्च सिद्ध्यति ।
सकला मन्त्रराजानः साक्षाद्भोगप्रदाः शुभाः ॥ २४॥

English
By this root-mantra, the very enjoyment as well as salvation is achieved. All the royal mantras are auspicious and directly accord enjoyment. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
पुनस्तयोस्तत्र तिरः पटं गुरुः
प्रकल्प्य मन्त्रं च समादिशत् परम् ।
निधाय तच्छीर्ष्णि कराम्बुजं शनै-
रुदङ्मुखं संस्थितयोः सहाम्बिकः ॥ २५॥

English
*Nandikeśvara* said :—  The lord in the company of his consort Ambikā, assumed the role of the preceptor for both of them. He screened them and placed his lotus-like hand on their heads as they faced the north and slowly taught them the great mantra. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिरुच्चार्याग्रहीन्मन्त्रं यन्त्रतन्त्रोक्तिपूर्वकम् ।
शिष्यौ च तौ दक्षिणायामात्मानं च समार्पयत् ॥ २६॥

प्रबद्धहस्तौ किल तौ तदन्तिके
तमूचतुर्देववरं जगद्गुरुम् ॥ २७॥

English
The two disciples received the mantra by repeating it thrice, along with the requisite Yantra and Tantra$^{47}$ duly expounded. By way of fees, the disciples dedicated themselves. Thereafter standing near him with hands clasped in reverence they addressed the lord, the preceptor of the universe. (26-27.)

[fn 47. The rites of worship are performed in accompaniment with Tantra, Yantra and Mantra appliances. Yantra is a mystical diagram possessed of occult powers. Tantra is a ritual, the chief peculiarity of which is the worship of the female energy of Śiva, personified in the person of his Śakti. This special energy, the Śakti of Śiva is concerned with sexual intercourse and magic power. Mantra is a magical formula.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माच्युतावूचतुः
नमो निष्कलरूपाय नमो निष्कलतेजसे ।
नमः सकलनाथाय नमस्ते सकलात्मने ॥ २८॥

नमः प्रणववाच्याय नमः प्रणवलिङ्गिने ।
नमः सृष्ट्यादिकर्त्रे च नमः पञ्चमुखाय ते ॥ २९॥

पञ्चब्रह्मस्वरूपाय पञ्चकृत्याय ते नमः ।
आत्मने ब्रह्मणे तुभ्यमनन्तगुणशक्तये ॥ ३०॥

सकलाकलरूपाय शम्भवे गुरवे नमः ।
इति स्तुत्वा गुरुं पद्यैर्ब्रह्मा विष्णुश्च नेमतुः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Brahmā and Viṣṇu* said :—  (The prayer):—Obeisance to Thee of the bodiless form. Obeisance to Thee of the formless lustre. Obeisance to Thee the lord of everything. Obeisance to Thee the soul of everything or of the embodied form. Obeisance to Thee stated by the Praṇava. Obeisance to Thee having Praṇava as Thy symbol. Obeisance to Thee the author of creation etc. Obeisance to Thee of five faces. Obeisance to Thee identical with Pañcabrahma form. Obeisance to Thee of five-fold functions. Obeisance to Thee the Ātman, the Brahman, of endless attributes and power. Obeisance to Śiva the preceptor, possessed of both embodied and bodiless forms.” After eulogising the preceptor in verses Brahmā and Viṣṇu bowed to him. (28-31.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
वत्सकौ सर्वतत्त्वं च कथितं दर्शितं च वाम् ।
जपतं प्रणवं मन्त्रं देवीदिष्टं मदात्मकम् ॥ ३२॥

English
*Iśvara* said :—  O dear sons, the truthful extract of every-thing has been narrated to you with demonstration. You shall recite as directed by the Goddess this Om mantra which is identical with me. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानं च सुस्थिरं भाग्यं सर्वं भवति शाश्वतम् ।
आर्द्रायां च चतुर्दश्यां तज्जप्यं त्वक्षयं भवेत् ॥ ३३॥

English
Your knowledge shall be stabilised. Permanent fortune shall stand by you. On the Caturdaśī day and on the day with Ārdrā star, the recital of this mantra will give you everlasting efficacy. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यगत्या महार्द्रायामेकं कोटिगुणं भवेत् ।
मृगशीर्षान्तिमो भागः पुनर्वस्वादिमस्तथा ॥ ३४॥

आर्द्रासमं सदा ज्ञेयं पूजाहोमादितर्पणे ।
दर्शनं तु प्रभाते च प्रातःसङ्गवकालयोः ॥ ३५॥

English
The recital of this mantra at the time when the transit of the sun is in the Ārdrā star is million-fold efficacious. In the context of worship, Homa and Tarpaṇa, the last quarter of the star Mṛgasīras and the first quarter of Punarvasu must always be considered on a par with Ārdrā. The Vision is to be had at early dawn and within three muhūrtas (two hours twentyfour minutes) thereafter. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्दशी तथा ग्राह्या निशीथव्यापिनी भवेत् ।
प्रदोषव्यापिनी चैव परयुक्ता प्रशस्यते ॥ ३६॥

English
Caturdaśī is to be taken when it continues up to midnight. If it is only upto the early part of the night and joined with another thereafter, it is also recommended. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गं वेरं च मे तुल्यं यजतां लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ।
तस्माल्लिङ्गं परं पूज्यं वेरादपि मुमुक्षुभिः ॥ ३७॥

English
Although I consider the phallic and the embodied form to be equal, the phallic form is excellent for those who worship. Hence for those who seek salvation the latter is preferable to the former. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गमोङ्कारमन्त्रेण वेरं पञ्चाक्षरेण तु ।
स्वयमेव हि सद्द्रव्यैः प्रतिष्ठाप्यं परैरपि ॥ ३८॥

पूजयेदुपचारैश्च मत्पदं सुलभं भवेत् ।
इति शास्य तथा शिष्यौ तत्रैवान्तर्हितः शिवः ॥ ३९॥

English
The others too shall install the phallic form with Omkāra mantra and the embodied form with the five-syllabled mantra, with excellent articles of worship and adore with due homage. It will be easy for them to attain my region. Having thus instructed His disciples Śiva vanished there itself. (38-39.)
