### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - *(The mode of worshipping the phallic form and making gifts)***

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः
कथं लिङ्गं प्रतिष्ठाप्यं कथं वा तस्य लक्षणम् ।
कथं वा तत्समभ्यर्च्यं देशे काले च केन हि ॥ १॥

English
*The sages said :—*  How is the phallic form of Śiva to be installed ? What are the characteristic features of the form ? How is it to be worshipped ? What is the appropriate time and place for worship. What sort of performer he must be ? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
युष्मदर्थं प्रवक्ष्यामि बुद्ध्यतामवधानतः ।
अनुकूले शुभे काले पुण्ये तीर्थे तटे तथा ॥ २॥

यथेष्टं लिङ्गमारोप्यं यत्र स्यान्नित्यमर्चनम् ।
पार्थिवेन तथाप्येन तैजसेन यथारुचि ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  I shall tell you everything for your sake, please listen attentively. The time must be convenient and auspicious. The place must be a holy centre. It can be on the bank of a river or anywhere facilitating a daily worship. It can be of Pārthiva (Earth), Āpya (Watery) or Taijasa (fiery) type. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पक्षणसंयुक्तं लिङ्गं पूजाफलं लभेत् ।
सर्वलक्षणसंयुक्तं सद्यः पूजाफलप्रदम् ॥ ४॥

English
If it has all the characteristics mentioned in the sacred texts, the devotee derives the fruit of worship. If it has all characteristics, it accords the fruit of worship instantaneously. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
चरे विशिष्यते सूक्ष्मं स्थावरे स्थूलमेव हि ।
सलक्षणं सपीठं च स्थापयेच्छिवनिर्मितम् ॥ ५॥

English
A subtle one is recommended if it be mobile and a gross one if it is stationary. The phallic emblem of good characteristics shall be set up in the seat of the same sort. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्डलं चतुरस्रं वा त्रिकोणमथवा तथा ।
खट्वाङ्गवन्मध्यसूक्ष्मं लिङ्गपीठं महाफलम् ॥ ६॥

English
The seat can be circular, square or triangular in shape. The one shaped like a cot in the middle is of middle efficacy. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं मृच्छिलादिभ्यो लिङ्गं लोहादिभिः कृतम् ।
येन लिङ्गं तेन पीठं स्थावरे हि विशिष्यते ॥ ७॥

English
At first, the emblem was made of earth or rock; then it used to be made with the metals. If it is stationary, the emblem and the Pīṭha should be of the one and the same material. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गं पीठं चरे त्वेकं लिङ्गं बाणकृतं विना ।
लिङ्गप्रमाणं कर्तॄणां द्वादशाङ्गुलमुत्तमम् ॥ ८॥

English
Save the one which the asura Bāṇa worshipped, both the emblem and the seat shall be unitary, if emblem be mobile. The length of the emblem shall be of the measure of twelve fingers of the devotee. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
न्यूनं चेत्फलमल्पं स्यादधिकं नैव दुष्यते ।
कर्तुरेकाङ्गुलन्यूनं चरेऽपि च तथैव हि ॥ ९॥

English
If it is shorter it is less efficacious; if it is longer there is no harm. A shortage by the breadth of the finger of the devotee in regard to the mobile one is similarly harmless. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
आदौ विमानं शिल्पेन कार्यं देवगणैर्युतम् ।
तत्र गर्भगृहे रम्ये दृढे दर्पणसन्निभे ॥ १०॥

भूषिते नवरत्नैश्च दिग्द्वारे च प्रधानके ।
नीलं रक्तं च वैडूर्यं श्यामं मारकतं तथा ॥ ११॥

मुक्ताप्रवालगोमेदवज्राणि नवरत्नकम् ।
मध्ये लिङ्गं महद् द्रव्यं निःक्षिपेत्सहवैदिके ॥ १२॥

English
A Vimāna (chariot-like structure) of artistic beauty shall be made at first wherein the divine attendants shall be represented. In its firm and beautiful sanctum sanctorum shining like a mirror studded with the nine precious gems—sapphire, lapis lazuli, Emerald, pearl, coral, Gomedaka, diamonds and rubies, the emblem shall be installed on the altar. (10-12.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पूज्य लिङ्गं सद्याद्यैः पञ्चस्थाने यथाक्रमम् ।
अग्नौ च हुत्वा बहुधा हविषा सकुलं च माम् ॥ १३॥

अभ्यर्च्य गुरुमाचार्यमर्थैः कामैश्च बान्धवम् ।
दद्यादैश्वर्यमर्थिभ्यो जडमप्यजडं तथा ॥ १४॥

स्थावरं जङ्गमं जीवं सर्वं सन्तोष्य यत्नतः ।
सुवर्णपूरिते श्वभ्रे नवरत्नैश्च पूरिते ॥ १५॥

सद्यादि ब्रह्म चोच्चार्य ध्यात्वा देवं परं शुभम् ।
उदीर्य च महामन्त्रमोङ्कारं नादघोषितम् ॥ १६॥

लिङ्गं तत्र प्रतिष्ठाप्य लिङ्गं पीठेन योजयेत् ।
लिङ्गं सपीठं निक्षिप्य नित्यलेपेन बन्धयेत् ॥ १७॥

English
The emblems shall be worshipped with the mantras beginning with “Sadyo”$^{48}$ in five different places in order. Sacrificial offerings shall be made in the fire. Śiva and the gods of His family shall be adored. The preceptor is given monetary gifts. Kinsmen are propitiated with whatever they desire. Money is distributed among the mendicants. All objects sentient or otherwise, and all living beings movable or immovable are duly gratified. The cavity is filled with gems. Mantras “Sadyo” etc. are recited. The auspicious supreme lord is meditated upon. The great mantra Omkāra resonant with its mystical sound is repeated. The liṅga is then united with the Pīṭha (pedestal). The two are then welded together. (13-17.)

[fn 48. VS. 29. 36.]
---

Sanskrit
एवं वेरं च संस्थाप्यं तत्रैव परमं शुभम् ।
पञ्चाक्षरेण वेरं तु उत्सवार्थं बहिस्तथा ॥ १८॥

English
Similarly the embodied image shall also be fixed there auspiciously. For the sake of festivals the embodied image shall be installed outside with the five-syllabled mantra. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
वेरं गुरुभ्यो गृह्णीयात्साधुभिः पूजितं तु वा ।
एवं लिङ्गे च वेरे च पूजा शिवपदप्रदा ॥ १९॥

English
The embodied image shall be taken from the preceptors or it must be one that has been worshipped by saintly men. Such an adoration of the embodied image and the phallic emblem accords the region of Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्च द्विविधं प्रोक्तं स्थावरं जङ्गमं तथा ।
स्थावरं लिङ्गमित्याहुस्तरुगुल्मादिकं तथा ॥ २०॥

English
The phallic emblem is of two varieties : the stationary and the mobile. Trees, hedges etc. represent the stationary. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
जङ्गमं लिङ्गमित्याहुः कृमिकीटादिकं तथा ।
स्थावरस्य च शुश्रूषा जङ्गमस्य च तर्पणम् ॥ २१॥

English
Worms, insects etc. represent the mobile. For the stationary one, tending and similar service is recommended. For the mobile one Tarpaṇa (propitiation) is recommended. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्तत्सुखानुरागेण शिवपूजां विदुर्बुधाः ।
पीठमम्बामयं सर्वं शिवलिङ्गं च चिन्मयम् ॥ २२॥

English
With a love for the happiness of different beings Śiva Pujā shall be performed—so say the wise men. The pedestal represents Śiva's consort—Pārvatī and his phallic emblem represents the sentient being. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा देवीमुमामङ्के धृत्वा तिष्ठति शङ्करः ।
तथा लिङ्गमिदं पीठं धृत्वा तिष्ठति सन्ततम् ॥ २३॥

English
Just as lord Śiva remains ever in close embrace of the Goddess Pārvatī, so also the phallic emblem holds on to the pedestal, for ever. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्थाप्य महालिङ्गं पूजयेदुपचारकैः ।
नित्यपूजा यथाशक्ति ध्वजादिकरणं तथा ॥ २४॥

English
Such is the installation of Śiva's great phallic emblem which shall be worshipped with due homage. The daily worship shall be made in accordance with one's capacity; so also the fixation of banner etc. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इति संस्थापयेल्लिङ्गं साक्षाच्छिवपदप्रदम् ।
अथवा चरलिङ्गं तु षोडशैरुपचारकैः ॥ २५॥

पूजयेच्च यथान्यायं क्रमाच्छिवपदप्रदम् ।
आवाहनं चासनं च अर्घ्यं पाद्यं तथैव च ॥ २६॥

तदङ्गाचमनं चैव स्नानमभ्यङ्गपूर्वकम् ।
वस्त्रं गन्धं तथा पुष्पं धूपं दीपं निवेदनम् ॥ २७॥

नीराजनं च ताम्बूलं नमस्कारो विसर्जनम् ।
अथवार्घ्यादिकं कृत्वा नैवेद्यान्तं यथाविधि ॥ २८॥

अथाभिषेकं नैवेद्यं नमस्कारं च तर्पणम् ।
यथाशक्ति सदा कुर्यात्क्रमाच्छिवपदप्रदम् ॥ २९॥

English
The devotee shall install the phallic emblem and it will accord directly the region of Śiva. Or the devotee shall worship the mobile emblem with the sixteen types of homage and services as prescribed. It accords the region of Śiva gradually. The sixteen types of service are$^{49}$ :—invocation (Āvāhana); offering the seat (Āsana); water offering (Arghya); washing of the feet (Pādya); water for rinsing the mouth as a mystical rite (Ācamana); oil bath (Abhyanga snāna); (25-29.)

[fn 49. The sixteen acts of homage to a deity are slightly different in other texts ; Compare आसनं स्वागतं पाथमध्यमाचमनीयकम् । मधुपर्काचमरनान-वसनाभरथादि च । गन्धपुष्पे धूपदीपौ नैवेद्यं बन्दनं तथा Tantrasāra mentions 64 Upacāras.]
---


Sanskrit
अथवा मानुषे लिङ्गेऽप्यार्षे दैवे स्वयम्भुवि ।
स्थापितेऽपूर्वके लिङ्गे सोपचारं यथा तथा ॥ ३०॥

English
Or he shall perform all the sixteen rites in the phallic emblem of human, saintly or godly origin, or in one naturally risen up (Svayambhū) or in one of very extraordinary nature installed duly. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजोपकरणे दत्ते यत्किञ्चित्फलमश्नुते ।
प्रदक्षिणानमस्कारैः क्रमाच्छिवपदप्रदम् ॥ ३१॥

English
If the devotee makes gifts of articles of worship he will get some benefit or other. By circumambulation and obeisance he will attain Śiva's region gradually. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गदर्शनमात्रं वा नियमेन शिवप्रदम् ।
मृत्पिष्टगोशकृत्पुष्पैः करवीरेण वा फलैः ॥ ३२॥

गुडेन नवनीतेन भस्मनान्नैर्यथारुचि ।
लिङ्गं यत्नेन कृत्वान्ते यजेत्तदनुसारतः ॥ ३३॥

English
Regular vision of the phallic emblem accords benefit. Or the devotee can make a phallic emblem out of clay, cowdung, flowers, Karavīra fruit, jaggery, butter, ashes or cooked rice as he likes and worship it according to the prescribed rules. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
अङ्गुष्ठादावपि तथा पूजामिच्छन्ति केचन ।
लिङ्गकर्मणि सर्वत्र निषेधोऽस्ति न कर्हिचित् ॥ ३४॥

English
Some authorities have recommended the worship of the phallic emblem on the thumb etc. In these rites of phallic worship, there is no sort of prohibition whatsoever. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वत्र फलदाता हि प्रयासानुगुणं शिवः ।
अथवा लिङ्गदानं वा लिङ्गमौल्यमथापि वा ॥ ३५॥

English
Everywhere Śiva accords benefit as befitting the endeavour put in. Or he shall make gifts of the phallic emblem or the value of its construction. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रद्धया शिवभक्ताय दत्तं शिवपदप्रदम् ।
अथवा प्रणवं नित्यं जपेद्दशसहस्रकम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Whatever is given to a devotee of Śiva with sincere faith accords Śiva's region. Or the devotee can repeat the Praṇava mantra ten thousand times every day. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्ययोश्च सहस्रं वा ज्ञेयं शिवपदप्रदम् ।
जपकाले मकारान्तं मनःशुद्धिकरं भवेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
Repetition of Om (Praṇava mantra) a thousand times at dawn and at dusk is known to be according Śiva's region. At the time of the repeated utterance (Japa) of the mantra, (Om) ending with "M" purifies the mind. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
समाधौ मानसं प्रोक्तमुपांशुसार्वकालिकम् ।
समानप्रणवं चेमं बिन्दुनादयुतं विदुः ॥ ३८॥

English
At the time of Samādhi (meditation) the repetition of Omkāra must be mental. Muttering of it in low voice can be practised at all times. The same with Bindu (dot) and Nāda (sound) is also of the same efficacy. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ पञ्चाक्षरं नित्यं जपेदयुतमादरात् ।
सन्ध्ययोश्च सहस्रं वा ज्ञेयं शिवपदप्रदम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Or the devotee can with due reverence repeat the five-syllabled mantra ten thousand times every day or a thousand times at dawn and at dusk. It accords the region of Śiva. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणवेनादिसंयुक्तं ब्राह्मणानां विशिष्यते ।
दीक्षायुक्तं गुरोर्ग्राह्यं मन्त्रं ह्यथ फलाप्तये ॥ ४०॥

English
Repetition of the five-syllabled mantra (Namaśśivāya) by a brahmin is specially efficacious with the Om (Praṇava) prefixed. A mantra must be received from a preceptor with proper initiation for the acquisition of the desired fruit. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भस्नानं मन्त्रदीक्षां मातृकान्यासमेव च ।
ब्राह्मणः सत्यपूतात्मा गुरुर्ज्ञानी विशिष्यते ॥ ४१॥

English
The ceremonial ablution when the sun is in transit to the Zodiac Kumbha, initiation for mantras, the Nyāsa of Mātṛkās;$^{50}$ a brahmin, a person with soul purified by truth; a preceptor of perfect knowledge—all these are splendid. (41.)

[fn 50. Nyāsas are particular diagrams which are closely associated with the divine mothers and are written in characters to which a magical power is ascribed. These are the personified energies of the principal deities connected with the worship of Śiva. They are reckoned sometimes 7, sometimes 8, 9 or 16 in number.]
---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit
अथवाक्षरलक्षं वा ज्ञेयं शिवपदप्रदम् ।
सहस्रं तु सहस्राणां सहस्रेण दिनेन हि ॥ ४५॥

English

Or if the devotee repeats it a thousand times every day and completes a million times in a thousand days, he can achieve whatever he desires. He shall feed brahmins every day. A brahmin shall repeat the Gāyatri a thousand and eight times every day in the morning. He shall attain Śiva's region gradually. He shall repeat Vedic verses and hymns with the observance of restraints. (45-47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The Daśārṇa mantra shall be repeated either 99 times or nine hundred times or nine thousand nine hundred times. (48.)

----

Sanskrit
वेदपारायणं चैव ज्ञेयं शिवपदप्रदम् ।
अन्यान्बहुतरान्मन्त्राञ्जपेदक्षरलक्षतः ॥ ४९॥

English
The regular study of the Vedas accords Śiva's region. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाक्षरांस्तथा मन्त्राञ्जपेदक्षरकोटितः ।
ततः परं जपेच्चैव सहस्रं भक्तिपूर्वकम् ॥ ५०॥

English
If the mantra consists of only one syllable it shall first be repeated a crore times and thereafter a thousand times with great devotion. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कुर्याद्यथाशक्ति क्रमाच्छिवपदं लभेत् ।
नित्यं रुचिकरं त्वेकं मन्त्रमामरणान्तिकम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Doing thus according to one's capacity one shall gradually attain Śiva's region. It is the duty of every one to repeat a mantra pleasing to him every day till his death. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रमोमिति जपेत्सर्वाभीष्टं शिवाज्ञया ।
पुष्पारामादिकं वापि तथा सम्मार्जनादिकम् ॥ ५२॥

शिवाय शिवकार्यार्थे कृत्वा शिवपदं लभेत् ।
शिवक्षेत्रे तथा वासं नित्यं कुर्याच्च भक्तितः ॥ ५३॥

English
If a man repeats "Om" a thousand times he shall get all his desires fulfilled at the bidding of Śiva. If he plants a flower-garden for the sake of Śiva or even renders service by sweeping and cleaning Śiva's temple and precincts he shall attain Śiva's region. The devotee shall reside for ever in Śiva's temple with great devotion. (52-53.)

Sanskrit

English
It yields worldly enjoyment and salvation to every one sentient or insentient. Hence an intelligent man shall reside in a temple of Śiva till death. (54.)

Sanskrit
लिङ्गाद्धस्तशतं पुण्यं क्षेत्रे मानुषके विदुः ।
सहस्रारत्निमात्रं तु पुण्यं क्षेत्रे तथार्षके ॥ ५५॥

English
In a temple built by ordinary man, the space upto a hundred hastas (1 hasta 30 cms) from the phallic image is holy. In a temple dedicated to sages, the space upto a thousand Aratnis (1 Aratni 45 cms) from the phallic image is holy. In a temple dedicated to sages, the space upto a thousand Aratnis (1 Aratni=45 cms) from the emblem is holy. (55.)

Sanskrit
दैवलिङ्गे तथा ज्ञेयं सहस्रारत्निमानतः ।
धनुःप्रमाणसाहस्रं पुण्यं क्षेत्रे स्वयम्भुवि ॥ ५६॥

English
If the phallic emblem had been installed by gods the space upto a thousand Aratnis is holy. In a temple where phallic emblem is self-risen, the space upto a thousand Dhanuḥ Pramāṇas (a dhanuḥ pramāṇa=4 hastas) is holy. (56.)

Sanskrit
द्वाहं दशाहं मास्यं वा सपिण्डीकरणं तु वा ।
आब्दिकं वा शिवक्षेत्रे क्षेत्रे पिण्डमथापि वा ॥ ५९॥

सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः सद्यः शिवपदं लभेत् ।
अथवा सप्तरात्रं वा वसेद्वा पञ्चरात्रकम् ॥ ६०॥

त्रिरात्रमेकरात्रं वा क्रमाच्छिवपदं लभेत् ।
स्ववर्णानुगुणं लोके स्वाचारात्प्राप्नुते नरः ॥ ६१॥

English
The rites of obsequies of the second day or the tenth day, the offerings of monthly Piṇḍas, the rite of Sapiṇḍikaraṇa or the annual Śrāddha shall be performed in a holy centre. He will instantly attain Śiva's region. By staying there for seven, five or three nights or a single night he will attain Śiva's region gradually. He will obtain results according to his conduct and befitting his caste. (59-61.)

Sanskrit
सर्वं कृतमकामेन साक्षाच्छिवपदप्रदम् ।
प्रातर्मध्याह्नसायाह्नमहस्त्रिष्वेकतः क्रमात् ॥ ६३॥

प्रातर्विधिकरं ज्ञेयं मध्याह्नं कामिकं तथा ।
सायाह्नं शान्तिकं ज्ञेयं रात्रावपि तथैव हि ॥ ६४॥

English
Anything done with no specific desire in view yields the region of Śiva directly. Of the three periods of time, ordained rites shall be performed in the morning, rites for the fulfilment of desires in the midday and rites for the suppression of the evil in the evening. The same thing holds good for nights too. (63-64.)
