### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - (The nature of bondage and liberation and the Glorification of the phallic emblem of Śiva) - The sages said :—**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
बन्धमोक्षस्वरूपं हि ब्रूहि सर्वार्थवित्तम ।
सूत उवाच ।
बन्धं मोक्षं तथोपायं वक्ष्येऽहं श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ १॥

English
O foremost among those who know everything, please explain the nature of bondage and liberation. Sūta said :— I shall explain bondage, liberation and the means of liberation. Please listen attentively. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्याद्यष्टबन्धेन बद्धो जीवः स उच्यते ।
प्रकृत्याद्यष्टबन्धेन निर्मुक्तो मुक्त उच्यते ॥ २॥

English
A Jīva is said to be in bondage if he is tied up by the noose of eightfold primary essences, Prakṛti etc. When freed from them he is called liberated. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्यादिवशीकारो मोक्ष इत्युच्यते स्वतः ।
बद्धजीवस्तु निर्मुक्तो मुक्तजीवः स कथ्यते ॥ ३॥

English
Perfect control and subjugation of Prakṛti and its offshoots is Salvation. A Jīva in bondage when freed from it is called a liberated soul. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्यग्रे ततो बुद्धिरहङ्कारो गुणात्मकः ।
पञ्चतन्मात्रमित्येतत्प्रकृत्याद्यष्टकं विदुः ॥ ४॥

English
The set of eight that binds is :—Prakṛti, Buddhi (cosmic intellect), Ahaṅkāra (cosmic ego) of the nature of attributes, and the five Tannātrās (cosmic principles of Ether etc.) (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्याद्यष्टजो देहो देहजं कर्म उच्यते ।
पुनश्च कर्मजो देहो जन्म कर्म पुनः पुनः ॥ ५॥

English
The body is evolved out of these eight. The body carries on activities. The activities generate the body. Thus birth and activities continue in a series. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शरीरं त्रिविधं ज्ञेयं स्थूलं सूक्ष्मं च कारणम् ।
स्थूलं व्यापारदं प्रोक्तं सूक्ष्ममिन्द्रियभोगदम् ॥ ६॥

कारणं त्वात्मभोगार्थं जीवकर्मानुरूपतः ।
सुखं दुःखं पुण्यपापैः कर्मभिः फलमश्नुते ॥ ७॥

English
The body is of three types: the gross, the subtle and the causal. The gross body is responsible for all activities; the subtle body yields the enjoyment of pleasures through the senses. The causal body is for the sake of experiencing the good and bad results of the activities of the Jīva. The Jīva experiences happiness as a result of virtue and misery as a result of sin. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्धि कर्मरज्ज्वा हि बद्धो जीवः पुनः पुनः ।
शरीरत्रयकर्मभ्यां चक्रवद् भ्राम्यते सदा ॥ ८॥

English
The Jīva bound by the rope of activities revolves round and round for ever like a wheel by means of the three types of body and their activities. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रभ्रमनिवृत्यर्थं चक्रकर्तारमीडयेत् ।
प्रकृत्यादिमहाचक्रं प्रकृतेः परतः शिवः ॥ ९॥

English
The creator of the wheel must be worshipped for the cessation of the revolution of the wheel. The Prakṛti etc. constitute the great wheel and Śiva is beyond the Prakṛti. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रकर्ता महेशो हि प्रकृतेः परतो यतः ।
पिबति वाथ वमति जीवाबालो जलं यथा ॥ १०॥

शिवस्तथा प्रकृत्यादि वशीकृत्याधितिष्ठति ।
सर्वं वशीकृतं यस्मात्तस्माच्छिव इति स्मृतः ।
शिव एव हि सर्वज्ञः परिपूर्णश्च निःस्पृहः ॥ ११॥

English
The creator of the wheel is the Lord Śiva. He is beyond the Prakṛti. Just as a boy drinks or spits out water as he pleases so also Śiva keeps Prakṛti etc. just as he pleases. He is called Śiva because he has brought it under his control. (Vaśikṛta). Śiva alone is omniscient, perfect and free from desire. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वज्ञता तृप्तिरनादिबोधः
स्वतन्त्रता नित्यमलुप्तशक्तिः ।
अनन्तशक्तिश्च महेश्वरस्य
यन्मानसैश्वर्यमवैति वेदः ॥ १२॥

English
The mental prowess of Maheśvara which Vedas alone can comprehend consists of omniscience, satiety, beginningless understanding, independence, never failing and and infinite power. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः शिवप्रसादेन प्रकृत्यादि वशं भवेत् ।
शिवप्रसादलाभार्थं शिवमेव प्रपूजयेत् ॥ १३॥

English
Hence Prakṛti etc. come under control due to Śiva's grace. One shall worship Śiva alone for the acquisition of Śiva's grace. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
निःस्पृहस्य च पूर्णस्य तस्य पूजा कथं भवेत् ।
शिवोद्देशकृतं कर्म प्रसादजनकं भवेत् ॥ १४॥

English
If one were to ask "How can there be a self-less worship of a perfect being?" the answer is "An activity done with dedication to Śiva shall cause pleasure to him". (14.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गे वेरे भक्तजने शिवमुद्दिश्य पूजयेत् ।
कायेन मनसा वाचा धनेनापि प्रपूजयेत् ॥ १५॥

English
Keeping Śiva in view the devotee shall worship the phallic or the embodied image of Śiva, or his devotee. He shall worship his devotee by means of the body, mind, speech and money spent. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजया तु महेशो हि प्रकृतेः परमः शिवः ।
प्रसादं कुरुते सत्यं पूजकस्य विशेषतः ॥ १६॥

English
Śiva, the great lord, who is beyond Prakṛti is delighted at the worship and specially blesses the worshipper. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवप्रसादात्कर्माद्यं क्रमेण स्ववशं भवेत् ।
कर्मारभ्य प्रकृत्यन्तं यदा सर्वं वशं भवेत् ॥ १७॥

तदा मुक्त इति प्रोक्तः स्वात्मारामो विराजते ।
प्रसादात्परमेशस्य कर्मदेहो यदा वशः ॥ १८॥

तदा वै शिवलोके तु वासः सालोक्यमुच्यते ।
सामीप्यं याति साम्बस्य तन्मात्रे च वशं गते ॥ १९॥

English
The Karma etc. come under control gradually due to Śiva's grace. Beginning with Karma and ending with Prakṛti when everything comes under control, the Jīva is called liberated and he shines as a self-realised person. By the grace of Śiva, when this body which is resultant from activities (Karmadeha) comes under control, the devotee attains residence in Śivaloka. This is called Sālokya form of liberation. When the subtle elements come under control, the devotee attains nearness to Śiva. (17-19.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा तु शिवसायुज्यमायुधाद्यैः क्रियादिभिः ।
महाप्रसादलाभे च बुद्धिश्चापि वशा भवेत् ॥ २०॥

English
Then he attains similarity with Śiva by means of weapons and activities. This is called Sārūpya. When the devotee acquires the great favour, the cosmic intellect too comes under control. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
बुद्धिस्तु कार्यं प्रकृतेस्तत्सार्ष्टिरिति कथ्यते ।
पुनर्महाप्रसादेन प्रकृतिर्वशमेष्यति ॥ २१॥

English
The cosmic intellect is only an effect of the Prakṛti. The control of Intellect is called Sārṣṭi—a form of liberation wherein the devotee has the same rank and power as Śiva. Then due to a further great favour of Śiva, the Prakṛti comes under control. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्य मानसैश्वर्यं तदाऽयत्नं भविष्यति ।
सार्वज्ञाद्यं शिवैश्वर्यं लब्ध्वा स्वात्मनि राजते ॥ २२॥

तत्सायुज्यमिति प्राहुर्वेदागमपरायणाः ।
एवं क्रमेण मुक्तिः स्याल्लिङ्गादौ पूजया स्वतः ॥ २३॥

English
The mental prowess of Śiva becomes his without any difficulty. On acquiring the omniscience and prosperity of Śiva, the devotee becomes resplendent in his soul. This is called Sāyujya (complete identity) by persons well-versed in the Vedas and Āgamas (Traditional Sacred Texts). It is in this order that one gets salvation by the worship of the phallic image of Śiva. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः शिवप्रसादार्थं क्रियाद्यैः पूजयेच्छिवम् ।
शिवक्रिया शिवतपः शिवमन्त्रजपः सदा ॥ २४॥

English
Hence the devotee shall worship Śiva by performing sacred rites etc. for the acquisition of Śiva's favour. Śiva's sacred rites, Śiva's penance, and the Japas of Śiva mantras always. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवज्ञानं शिवध्यानमुत्तरोत्तरमभ्यसेत् ।
आसुप्तेरामृतेः कालं नयेद्वै शिवचिन्तया ॥ २५॥

English
Knowledge of Śiva and meditation on Him shall be practised more and more. The time till retirement to bed, the time till death shall be spent in contemplating over Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्यादिभिश्च कुसुमैरर्चयेच्छिवमेष्यति ॥

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
लिङ्गादौ शिवपूजाया विधानं ब्रूहि सुव्रत ॥ २६॥

सूत उवाच ।
लिङ्गानां च क्रमं वक्ष्ये यथावच्छृणुत द्विजाः ।
तदेव लिङ्गं प्रथमं प्रणवं सार्वकामिकम् ॥ २७॥

English
He shall adore Śiva by means of the "Sadyo" mantras and flowers. He will attain welfare. *The sages said:—* O excellent one of good rites, please explain the rules governing worship of Śiva in the phallic and other forms. *Sūta said:—* I shall explain, O brahmins, the procedure of the wor- ship of the phallic form, please listen. The first phallic form is the Praṇava that confers all desires. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
सूक्ष्मप्रणवरूपं हि सूक्ष्मरूपं तु निष्कलम् ।
स्थूललिङ्गं हि सकलं तत्पञ्चाक्षरमुच्यते ॥ २८॥

English
It is called Sūkṣma Praṇava (the subtle one) if it is Niṣkala. The Sthūla (gross one) is Sakala and it consists of five constituent syllables. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोः पूजा तपः प्रोक्तं साक्षान्मोक्षप्रदे उभे ।
पौरुषप्रकृतिभूतानि लिङ्गानि सुबहूनि च ॥ २९॥

English
The worship of these two is called a penance. Both of them accord salvation. There are many phallic emblems of Pauruṣa Prakṛti. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तानि विस्तरतो वक्तुं शिवो वेत्ति न चापरः ।
भूविकाराणि लिङ्गानि ज्ञातानि प्रब्रवीमि वः ॥ ३०॥

English
Śiva alone can explain them in detail. No one else. Such as are evolved of Earthly material are known to me which I shall explain to you all. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयम्भूलिङ्गं प्रथमं बिन्दुलिङ्गं द्वितीयकम् ।
प्रतिष्ठितं चरं चैव गुरुलिङ्गं तु पञ्चमम् ॥ ३१॥

English
These are of five types: (1) Svayambhū, (2) Bindu, (3) Pratiṣṭhita, (4) Cara, (5) Guru Liṅga. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवर्षितपसा तुष्टः सान्निध्यार्थं तु तत्र वै ।
पृथिव्यन्तर्गतः शर्वो बीजं वै नादरूपतः ॥ ३२॥

स्थावराङ्कुरवद्भूमिमुद्भिद्य व्यक्त एव सः ।
स्वयम्भूतं जातमिति स्वयम्भूरिति तं विदुः ॥ ३३॥

English
When he is gladdened by the austerities of devas and sages, Śiva in the form of Nāda assumes the form of a seed under the ground and suddenly piercing the ground above like a germinating sprout manifests Himself outside and makes His presence felt. Since this emblem is self-raised it is called Svayambhū. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्लिङ्गपूजया ज्ञानं स्वयमेव प्रवर्धते ।
सुवर्णरजतादौ वा पृथिव्यां स्थण्डिलेऽपि वा ॥ ३४॥

स्वहस्ताल्लिखितं लिङ्गं शुद्धप्रणवमन्त्रकम् ।
यन्त्रलिङ्गं समालिख्य प्रतिष्ठावाहनं चरेत् ॥ ३५॥

English
By worshipping it the devotee gains increasing knowledge automatically. In a gold or silver plate or on the ground or an altar, the devotee draws the picture of the phallic emblem, the pure Praṇava mantra and shall invoke it with the rites of Pratiṣṭhā and Āvāhana. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
बिन्दुनादमयं लिङ्गं स्थावरं जङ्गमं च यत् ।
भावनामयमेतद्धि शिवदृष्टं न संशयः ॥ ३६॥

English
The Bindu and Nāda forms, the stationary or mobile ones are conceptual but belong to Śiva, undoubtedly. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र विश्वस्यते शम्भुस्तत्र तस्मै फलप्रदः ।
स्वहस्ताल्लिखिते यन्त्रे स्थावरादावकृत्रिमे ॥ ३७॥

आवाह्य पूजयेच्छम्भुं षोडशैरुपचारकैः ।
स्वयमैश्वर्यमाप्नोति ज्ञानमभ्यासतो भवेत् ॥ ३८॥

English
Wherever Śiva is sincerely believed to be present, the lord bestows on the devotee the benefit through that alone. The devotee can invoke the lord in a natural immobile thing—a rock or a stump—or an engraved picture and worship Śiva by the sixteen Upacāras (services and homage). He will attain supreme power of the lord and by practice gain knowledge. (37-38.)

---

Sanskrit
देवैश्च ऋषिभिश्चापि स्वात्मसिद्ध्यर्थमेव हि ।
समन्त्रेणात्महस्तेन कृतं यच्छुद्धमण्डले ॥ ३९॥

शुद्धभावनया चैव स्थापितं लिङ्गमुत्तम् ।
तल्लिङ्गं पौरुषं प्राहुस्तत्प्रतिष्ठितमुच्यते ॥ ४०॥

English
If the image is installed with pure mind in a pure altar either by the Gods or the sages for the realisation of the soul, it is called Pauruṣa and it comes under the category of the installed phallic image of Śiva. (39-40.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्लिङ्गपूजया नित्यं पौरुषैश्वर्यमाप्नुयात् ।
महद्भिर्ब्राह्मणैश्चापि राजभिश्च महाधनैः ॥ ४१॥

शिल्पिना कल्पितं लिङ्गं मन्त्रेण स्थापितं च यत् ।
प्रतिष्ठितं प्राकृतं हि प्राकृतैश्वर्यभोगदम् ॥ ४२॥

English
By a regular worship of this phallic image, the devotee will obtain all Pauruṣa Aiśvaryas (human riches). If great brahmins or rich kings install a linga prepared by the artisans, it is called Pratiṣṭhita and Prākṛta. It accords enjoyment of Prākṛta Aiśvarya (Natural riches) to the worshipper. (41-42.)

---

Sanskrit
यदूर्जितं च नित्यं च तद्धि पौरुषमुच्यते ।
यद् दुर्बलमनित्यं च तद्धि प्राकृतमुच्यते ॥ ४३॥

English
That which is forceful and permanent is called Pauruṣa. That which is weak and temporary is called Prākṛta. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गं नाभिस्तथा जिह्वा नासाग्रं च शिखा क्रमात् ।
कट्यादिषु त्रिलोकेषु लिङ्गमाध्यात्मिकं चरम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The spiritual cum mobile form is represented by the constituents of the body, viz. the penis, navel, tongue, the tip of the nose, hips etc. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वतं पौरुषं प्रोक्तं भूतलं प्राकृतं विदुः ।
वृक्षादि पौरुषं ज्ञेयं गुल्मादि प्राकृतं विदुः ॥ ४५॥

English
The mountain comes under the Pauruṣa class and the surface of the world under the Prākṛta class. Trees etc. are Pauruṣa and creepers etc. are Prākṛta. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
षाष्टिकं प्राकृतं ज्ञेयं शालिगोधूमपौरुषम् ।
ऐश्वर्यं पौरुषं विद्यादणिमाद्यष्टसिद्धिदम् ॥ ४६॥

English
The Śāṣṭika rice is Prākṛta but rice of the Śāli variety and wheat are Pauruṣa. The Aiśvarya is Pauruṣa. It bestows eightfold siddhis viz. Aṇimā etc. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सुस्त्रीधनादिविषयं प्राकृतं प्राहुरास्तिकाः ।
प्रथमं चरलिङ्गेषु रसलिङ्गं प्रकथ्यते ॥ ४७॥

English
The Prākṛta liṅga bestows good women, riches etc. according to the believers. Now, first of all I shall mention the Rasaliṅga from among Caraliṅgas. (Rasaliṅga is mentioned as the foremost among mobile liṅgas). (47.)

---

Sanskrit
रसलिङ्गं ब्राह्मणानां सर्वाभीष्टप्रदं भवेत् ।
बाणलिङ्गं क्षत्रियाणां महाराज्यप्रदं शुभम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Rasaliṅga is a bestower of all wishes to the brahmins. The auspicious Bāṇaliṅga is a bestower of vast kingdoms to the Kṣatriyas. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वर्णलिङ्गं तु वैश्यानां महाधनपतित्वदम् ।
शिलालिङ्गं तु शूद्राणां महाशुद्धिकरं शुभम् ॥ ४९॥

English
A gold liṅga bestows the ownership of vast wealth on the Vaiśyas. A Śilāliṅga (a liṅga made of rock) bestows great purity on the Śūdras. (49.)

---

यथायोग्यं प्रकुर्वीत क्रियादीन् शिवभक्तिमान् ॥ १५३॥

English
The true devotee of Śiva shall observe the sacred rites etc. in accordance with the place, time, physical ability, possession of wealth as befitting his state. (153.)

---

Sanskrit
न्यायार्जितसुवित्तेन वसेत्प्राज्ञः शिवस्थले ।
जीवहिंसादिरहितमतिक्लेशविवर्जितम् ॥ १५४॥

English
The intelligent devotee shall take up his residence in a holy centre of Śiva, desist from violence to living beings, without exposing himself to undue strain, and spending only such wealth as he earns by legitimate means. (154.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाक्षरेण जप्तं च तोयमन्नं विदुः सुखम् ।
अथवाहुर्दरिद्रस्य भिक्षान्नं ज्ञानदं भवेत् ॥ १५५॥

English
Even water sanctified by the five-syllabled mantra is conducive to happiness like cooked food. Even the alms begged and acquired by an indigent devotee is conducive to perfect knowledge. (155.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवभक्तस्य भिक्षान्नं शिवभक्तिविवर्धनम् ।
शम्भुसत्रमिति प्राहुर्भिक्षान्नं शिवयोगिनः ॥ १५६॥

English
Charitable food of a devotee of Śiva increases devotion to Śiva. Śivayogins call such charitable food sacrificial offerings to Śiva. (156.)

---

Sanskrit
येन केनाप्युपायेन यत्र कुत्रापि भूतले ।
शुद्धान्नभुक्सदा मौनी रहस्यं न प्रकाशयेत् ॥ १५७॥

English
The devotee of Śiva shall always be scrupulous about the purity of his food, wherever he stays and whatever means of sustenance he has. He shall observe silence and shall not disclose the secret. (157.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकाशयेत्तु भक्तानां शिवमाहात्म्यमेव हि ।
रहस्यं शिवमन्त्रस्य शिवो जानाति नापरः ॥ १५८॥

English
To the devotees he shall expound the greatness of Śiva. Only Śiva can know the secret of Śivamantra. No one else. (158.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवभक्तो वसेन्नित्यं शिवलिङ्गं समाश्रितः ।
स्थाणुलिङ्गाश्रयेणैव स्थाणुर्भवति भूसुराः ॥ १५९॥

English
The devotee of Śiva shall always resort to the phallic emblem of Śiva. O brahmins, one becomes Śiva by resorting to stationary phallic emblem. (159.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजया चरलिङ्गस्य क्रमान्मुक्तो भवेद् ध्रुवम् ।
सर्वमुक्तं समासेन साध्यसाधनमुत्तमम् ॥ १६०॥

English
By worshipping the mobile phallic image the liberation is certainly gradual. Thus I have mentioned the achievable and the excellent means of achievement. (160.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यासेन यत्पुरा प्रोक्तं यच्छ्रुतं हि मया पुरा ।
भद्रमस्तु हि वोऽस्माकं शिवभक्तिर्दृढास्तु सा ॥ १६१॥

English
What has been mentioned formerly by Vyāsa and what has been heard by me before, has been mentioned to you. Welfare attend ye all. May our devotion to Śiva be stable and firm. (161.)

---

Sanskrit
य इमं पठतेऽध्यायं यः श‍ृणोति नरः सदा ।
शिवज्ञानं स लभते शिवस्य कृपया बुधाः ॥ १६२॥

English
O scholars, whoever reads this chapter by Śiva's grace and whoever listens to this always shall acquire the knowledge of Śiva. (162.)
