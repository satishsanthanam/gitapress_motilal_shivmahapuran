### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - (Glorification of the worship of Śiva's Earthen phallic image)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत चिरञ्जीव धन्यस्त्वं शिवभक्तिमान् ।
सम्यगुक्तस्त्वया लिङ्गमहिमा सत्फलप्रदः ॥ १॥

यत्र पार्थिवमाहेशलिङ्गस्य महिमाधुना ।
सर्वोत्कृष्टश्च कथितो व्यासतो ब्रूहि तं पुनः ॥ २॥

English
The sages said :— O Sūta, Sūta, be long-lived. Thou art a blessed devotee of Śiva. The greatness of Śiva's phallic image in according excellent benefit has been well explained by you. Now speak about the greatness of Earthen phallic image of Śiva which is far superior to all others. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श‍ृणुध्वमृषयः सर्वे सद्भक्त्यादरतोऽखिलाः ।
शिवपार्थिवलिङ्गस्य महिमा प्रोच्यते मया ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O sages, please listen all of you with great devotion and respect. Now I am going to speak on the greatness of earthly phallic image of Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
उक्तेष्वेतेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ।
तस्य पूजनतो विप्रा बहवः सिद्धिमागताः ॥ ४॥

English
The Earthly phallic image of Śiva is the most excellent of all such images of Śiva. Many brahmins have achieved great things by worshipping it. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिर्ब्रह्मा च ऋषयः सप्रजापतयस्तथा ।
सम्पूज्य पार्थिवं लिङ्गं प्रापुः सर्वेप्सितं द्विजाः ॥ ५॥

English
O brahmins, Hari, Brahmā, Prajāpati and other sages have attained all they desired by worshipping this Earthly phallic image. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
देवासुरमनुष्याश्च गन्धर्वोरगराक्षसाः ।
अन्येऽपि बहवः सिद्धिं तं सम्पूज्य गताः पराम् ॥ ६॥

English
Devas, Asuras, men, Gandharvas, serpents, Rākṣasas and many others have attained greatness after worshipping it. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कृते रत्नमयं लिङ्गं त्रेतायां हेमसम्भवम् ।
द्वापरे पारदं श्रेष्ठं पार्थिवं तु कलौ युगे ॥ ७॥

English
The phallic emblem of Śiva made of precious gems was considered the best in the Kṛta age; of pure gold in the Dvāpara; of mercury in the Tretā and of earth in the Kali age. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टमूर्तिषु सर्वासु मूर्तिर्वै पार्थिवी वरा ।
अनन्यपूजिता विप्रास्तपस्तस्मान्महत्फलम् ॥ ८॥

English
Among the eight$^{89}$ cosmic bodies of Śiva, the Earthen body is the best. Since it is not worshipped by any one else O Brāhmaṇas! it yields great benefit. (8.)

[fn 89. ŚB (6.1. 3. 1-18) gives the following version of the eight forms of Śiva : "When the life-principle became manifest it had no name, so it cried. Prajāpati asked the reason and being informed that the child wanted a name, first gave him the name Rudra, then Śarva, Paśupati, Ugra, Aśani, Bhava, Mahādeva and Iśāna. This was the conception from which the purāṇa writers developed the Aṣṭamūrti conception of Śiva. The fact is that the eight forms of Śiva symbolise the five gross material elements (ether, air, fire, water, and earth), two opposite principles of Prāṇa and Apāṇa (heat and cold represented by the sun and the moon) and the principle of mind (मंगल) which is the eighth.]
---

Sanskrit
यथा सर्वेषु देवेषु ज्येष्ठः श्रेष्ठः महेश्वरः ।
एवं सर्वेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमुच्यते ॥ ९॥

English
Just as Śiva is the oldest and the most excellent of all deities, so also his earthly phallic image is the most excellent of all. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा नदीषु सर्वासु ज्येष्ठा श्रेष्ठा सुरापगा ।
तथा सर्वेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमुच्यते ॥ १०॥

English
Just as the celestial river Gaṅgā is the oldest and the most excellent of all rivers, so also is the earthen phallic image of Śiva the most excellent of all. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा सर्वेषु मन्त्रेषु प्रणवो हि महान्स्मृतः ।
तथेदं पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमाराध्यं पूज्यमेव हि ॥ ११॥

English
Just as the Praṇava is considered the greatest of all mantras, so also the earthen phallic image of Śiva that is worthy to be worshipped, is the most excellent of all. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा सर्वेषु वर्णेषु ब्राह्मणः श्रेष्ठ उच्यते ।
तथा सर्वेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमुच्यते ॥ १२॥

English
Just as the brahmin is spoken of as the most excellent of all Varṇas so also is the earthen phallic image of Śiva the most excellent of all other phallic images. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा पुरीषु सर्वासु काशी श्रेष्ठतमा स्मृता ।
तथा सर्वेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमुच्यते ॥ १३॥

English
Just as Kāśī is considered the most excellent of all holy cities, so also the earthly phallic image of Śiva is spoken of as the most excellent of all other phallic images. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा व्रतेषु सर्वेषु शिवरात्रिव्रतं परम् ।
तथा सर्वेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमुच्यते ॥ १४॥

English
Just as the rite of Śivarātri is the greatest of all sacred rites so also the earthly phallic image of Śiva is the most excellent of all other phallic images. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा देवीषु सर्वासु शैवी शक्तिः परा स्मृता ।
तथा सर्वेषु लिङ्गेषु पार्थिवं श्रेष्ठमुच्यते ॥ १५॥

English
Just as Śiva's energy is considered the greatest of all goddesses so also the earthen phallic emblem of Śiva is spoken of as the most excellent of all. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्य पार्थिवं लिङ्गं योऽन्यदेवं प्रपूजयेत् ।
वृथा भवति सा पूजा स्नानदानादिकं वृथा ॥ १६॥

English
Discarding the worship of the earthen phallic image if any one were to worship another deity, that worship becomes fruitless. Ceremonial ablutions, charitable gifts etc. are of no avail. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवाराधनं पुण्यं धन्यमायुर्विवर्धनम् ।
तुष्टिदं पुष्टिदं श्रीदं कार्यं साधकसत्तमैः ॥ १७॥

English
The propitiation of the earthen phallic image is sanctifying, bestower of bliss, longevity, satiety, nourishment and fortune. It must be observed by all good aspirants. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यथालब्धोपचारैश्च भक्तिश्रद्धासमन्वितः ।
पूजयेत्पार्थिवं लिङ्गं सर्वकामार्थसिद्धिदम् ॥ १८॥

English
A devotee endowed with unflinching faith shall worship the earthen phallic image with such modes of service as are easily available. It accords the achievement of all desired objects. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यः कृत्वा पार्थिवं लिङ्गं पूजयेच्छुभवेदिकम् ।
इहैव धनवाञ्छ्रीमानन्ते रुद्रोऽभिजायते ॥ १९॥

English
He who worships the earthen phallic image after constructing an auspicious altar becomes affluent and glorious here itself and becomes Rudra in the end. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिसन्ध्यं योऽर्चयंल्लिङ्गं कृत्वा बिल्वेन पार्थिवम् ।
दशैकादशकं यावत्तस्य पुण्यफलं श‍ृणु ॥ २०॥

English
He who worships the earthen phallic image in the three junctures of the threefold division of the day every day gains the bliss for twentyone future births. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेनैव स्वदेहेन रुद्रलोके महीयते ।
पापहं सर्वमर्त्यानां दर्शनात्स्पर्शनादपि ॥ २१॥

English
He is honoured in Rudraloka with this body alone. His body dispels the sins of every man by mere sight or touch. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवन्मुक्तः स वै ज्ञानी शिव एव न संशयः ।
तस्य दर्शनमात्रेण भुक्तिर्मुक्तिश्च जायते ॥ २२॥

English
He is a living liberated soul, he is wise, he is Śiva, there is no doubt. A mere sight of him accords enjoyment of worldly pleasures and salvation. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवं यः पूजयेन्नित्यं कृत्वा लिङ्गं तु पार्थिवम् ।
यावज्जीवनपर्यन्तं स याति शिवमन्दिरम् ॥ २३॥

मृडेनाप्रमितान्वर्षाञ्छिवलोके हि तिष्ठति ।
सकामः पुनरागत्य राजेन्द्रो भारते भवेत् ॥ २४॥

English
He who worships the earthen phallic emblem of Śiva every day stays in Śivaloka for so many years of Śiva, as he had been visiting Śiva's temple in his life. If he had any wish he would be reborn in the land of Bharata as a sovereign monarch. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्कामः पूजयेन्नित्यं पार्थिवं लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ।
शिवलोके सदा तिष्ठेत्तस्य सायुज्यमाप्नुयात् ॥ २५॥

English
If a man without any desire worships every day the excellent earthen phallic image, he shall stay in Śiva's region for ever. He shall attain the Sāyujya type of salvation. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवं शिवलिङ्गं च विप्रो यदि न पूजयेत् ।
स याति नरकं घोरं शूलप्रोतं सुदारुणम् ॥ २६॥

English
If a brahmin does not worship the earthen phallic image he shall fall in the terrible hell with a terrible trident pierced through his body. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाकथञ्चिद्विधिना रम्यं लिङ्गं प्रकारयेत् ।
पञ्चसूत्रविधानं च पार्थिवे न विचारयेत् ॥ २७॥

English
By any means the phallic image shall be made beautiful. The Pañcasatra rite shall be performed with the earthen phallic image. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अखण्डं तद्धि कर्तव्यं न विखण्डं प्रकारयेत् ।
विखण्डं तु प्रकुर्वाणो नैव पूजाफलं लभेत् ॥ २८॥

English
The earthen phallic image shall be made as a single whole. Making it piecemeal i.e if the image is made joining two or more pieces, he will never derive the merit of worship. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
रत्नजं हेमजं लिङ्गं पारदं स्फाटिकं तथा ।
पार्थिवं पुष्परागोत्थमखण्डं तु प्रकारयेत् ॥ २९॥

English
Whether it is made of gems, gold, mercury, crystals or Pusparāga it shall be a single whole. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अखण्डं तु चरं लिङ्गं द्विखण्डमचरं स्मृतम् ।
खण्डाखण्डविचारोऽयं सचराचरयोः स्मृतः ॥ ३०॥

English
All mobile phallic emblems shall be a single whole. Stationary phallic images shall be made of two pieces. This is the rule about broken and unbroken phallic images both immobile or mobile. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदिका तु महाविद्या लिङ्गं देवो महेश्वरः ।
अतो हि स्थावरे लिङ्गे स्मृता श्रेष्ठा द्विखण्डता ॥ ३१॥

English
The pedestal is the great Māyā; the phallic image is lord Śiva. Hence in immobile image two-piece construction is recommended. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विखण्डं स्थावरं लिङ्गं कर्तव्यं हि विधानतः ।
अखण्डं जङ्गमं प्रोक्तं शैवसिद्धान्तवेदिभिः ॥ ३२॥

English
This has been mentioned by those who know the principles of Śaiva cult that a stationary phallic image shall be made of two pieces. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विखण्डं तु चरं लिङ्गं कुर्वन्त्यज्ञानमोहिताः ।
नैव सिद्धान्तवेत्तारो मुनयः शास्त्रकोविदाः ॥ ३३॥

English
Only those who are deluded by ignorance make the mobile phallic image of two pieces. The sages who know the Śaiva cult; and are well versed in Śaiva Sacred texts do not enjoin that. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अखण्डं स्थावरं लिङ्गं द्विखण्डं चरमेव च ।
ये कुर्वन्ति नरा मूढा न पूजाफलभागिनः ॥ ३४॥

English
Those who make a stationary phallic image as a single whole and a mobile one pieced are fools. They never reap the benefit of worship. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छास्त्रोक्तविधिना अखण्डं चरसंज्ञकम् ।
द्विखण्डं स्थावरं लिङ्गं कर्तव्यं परया मुदा ॥ ३५॥

English
Hence, one shall make with very great pleasure the mobile one as a single whole and the stationary one as two-pieced according to rules laid down in the sacred texts. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अखण्डे तु चरे पूजा सम्पूर्णफलदायिनी ।
द्विखण्डे तु चरे पूजा महाहानिप्रदा स्मृता ॥ ३६॥

English
The worship of an unbroken mobile image yields full benefit while the worship of two-pieced mobile image brings about great harm. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अखण्डे स्थावरे पूजा न कामफलदायिनी ।
प्रत्यवायकरी नित्यमित्युक्तं शास्त्रवेदिभिः ॥ ३७॥

English
This has been stated by those who know the lore that the worship of a stationary image of a single piece not only withholds the cherished desire but is also full of hazards. (37.)

---
