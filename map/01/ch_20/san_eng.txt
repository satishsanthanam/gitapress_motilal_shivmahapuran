### **CHAPTER TWENTY - *(The mode of worshipping an earthen phallic image by chanting Vedic mantras)***

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच -
अथ वैदिकभक्तानां पार्थिवार्चा निगद्यते ।
वैदिकेनैव मार्गेण भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायिनी ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—  Now, the mode of worshipping an earthen phallic image according to the Vedic rites is being explained. It yields worldly pleasures and salvation to the Vedic worshippers. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत्रोक्तविधिना स्नात्वा सन्ध्यां कृत्वा यथाविधि ।
ब्रह्मयज्ञं विधायादौ ततस्तर्पणमाचरेत् ॥ २॥

English
The devotee shall take bath in accordance with the rules prescribed in the sacred code. He shall duly perform his Sandhyā prayers. After performing the Brahma Yajña, one of the five daily sacrifices, he shall perform Tarpaṇa (a rite of offering water oblation to the manes). (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नैत्यिकं सकलं कामं विधायानन्तरं पुमान् ।
शिवस्मरणपूर्वं हि भस्मरुद्राक्षधारकः ॥ ३॥

वेदोक्तविधिना सम्यक् सम्पूर्णफलसिद्धये ।
पूजयेत्परया भक्त्या पार्थिवं लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ॥ ४॥

English
After finishing the daily rites he shall apply ashes and wear Rudrākṣa, all along remembering Lord Śiva. With great devotion he shall then worship the excellent earthen phallic image according to Vedic injunctions in order to realise the full benefit. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
नदीतीरे तडागे च पर्वते काननेऽपि च ।
शिवालये शुचौ देशे पार्थिवार्चा विधीयते ॥ ५॥

English
The worship of the earthen phallic image shall be performed on the bank of a river or a tank or on the top of a mountain or in a forest, or in a Śiva temple. It must be in a clean place. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धप्रदेशसम्भूतां मृदमाहृत्य यत्नतः ।
शिवलिङ्गं प्रकल्पेत सावधानतया द्विजाः ॥ ६॥

English
O brahmins, he shall bring clay from a clean place and carefully make the phallic image. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्रे गौरा स्मृता शोणा बाहुजे पीतवर्णका ।
वैश्ये कृष्णा पादजाते ह्यथवा यत्र या भवेत् ॥ ७॥

English
White clay is to be used by a brahmin; red clay by a Kṣatriya; yellow clay by a Vaiśya and black clay by a Śūdra. Anything available shall be used if the specified clay is not found. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्गृह्य मृत्तिकां लिङ्गनिर्माणार्थं प्रयत्नतः ।
अतीव शुभदेशे च स्थापयेत्तां मृदं शुभाम् ॥ ८॥

English
After taking the clay he shall place it in an auspicious place for making the image. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
संशोध्य च जलेनापि पिण्डीकृत्य शनैः शनैः ।
विधीयेत शुभं लिङ्गं पार्थिवं वेदमार्गतः ॥ ९॥

English
After washing the clay clean with water and kneading it slowly he shall prepare a good earthen phallic image according to the Vedic direction. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सम्पूजयेद्भक्त्या भुक्तिमुक्तिफलाप्तये ।
तत्प्रकारमहं वच्मि श‍ृणुध्वं सविधानतः ॥ १०॥

English
Then he shall worship it with devotion for the sake of enjoying worldly pleasures here and salvation here after. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः शिवाय मन्त्रेणार्चनद्रव्यं च प्रोक्षयेत् ।
भूरसीति च मन्त्रेण क्षेत्रसिद्धिं प्रकारयेत् ॥ ११॥

English
The material of worship shall be sprinkled with water, chanting the formula “Namaḥ Śivāya”$^{90}$ With the mantra “Bhūrasi⁹¹” etc. the achievement of the sanctity of a holy centre (Kṣetra Siddhi) shall be effected. (11.)

[fn 90. VS. 16.41.]
---

Sanskrit
आपोऽस्मानिति मन्त्रेण जलसंस्कारमाचरेत् ।
नमस्ते रुद्रमन्त्रेण स्फाटिकाबन्धमुच्यते ॥ १२॥

English
Water shall be sanctified with the mantra “Āposmān⁹²” etc. The rite of “Phāṭikābandha” shall be performed with “Namaste Rudra⁹³” mantra (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भवायेति मन्त्रेण क्षेत्रशुद्धिं प्रकारयेत् ।
कुर्यात्पञ्चामृतस्यापि नमःपूर्वेण प्रोक्षणम् ॥ १३॥

English
The purity of the place of worship shall be heightened with the mantra “Śambhavāya⁹⁴” etc. The sprinkling of water over Pañcāmṛta⁹⁵ shall be performed with the word Namah prefixed. (13.)

[fn 95. Five kinds of food viz. milk, curd, butter, honey and sugar are called Pañcāmṛta.]
---

Sanskrit
नीलग्रीवाय मन्त्रेण नमःपूर्वेण भक्तिमान् ।
चरेच्छङ्करलिङ्गस्य प्रतिष्ठापनमुत्तमम् ॥ १४॥

English
The excellent installation of the phallic image of Śiva shall be made devoutly with the mantra “Namah Nilagrīvāya⁹⁶” (obeisance to the blue-necked). (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तितस्तत एतत्ते रुद्रावेति च मन्त्रतः ।
आसनं रमणीयं वै दद्याद्वैदिकमार्गकृत् ॥ १५॥

English
The worshipper following the Vedic path shall make devoutly the offer of a beautiful seat with the mantra “Etatte rudrāya⁹⁷” etc. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मानो महान्तमिति च मन्त्रेणावाहनं चरेत् ।
या ते रुद्रेण मन्त्रेण सञ्चरेदुपवेशनम् ॥ १६॥

English
The invocation (Āvāhana) shall be performed with the mantra “Mā no mahāntam⁹⁸” etc. The seating (Upaveśana) shall be performed with the mantra “Yā te rudreṇa⁹⁹”. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्रेण यामिषुमिति न्यासं कुर्याच्छिवस्य च ।
अध्यवोचदिति प्रेम्णाधिवासं मनुनाचरेत् ॥ १७॥

English
With the mantra “Yāmiṣum¹⁰⁰” etc. the Nyāsa (ritualistic touching of the body in various parts) shall be performed. The offering of fragrance shall be performed endearingly with the mantra ‘Adhyavocat’¹⁰¹ etc. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मनुनासौ जीव इति देवतान्यासमाचरेत् ।
असौ योऽवसर्पतीति चाचरेदुपसर्पणम् ॥ १८॥

English
The Nyāsa of the deity shall be performed with the mantra “Asau Jīva¹⁰²” etc. The rite of approaching the deity (upasarpaṇa) shall be performed with the mantra “Asau Yovasarpati¹⁰³” etc. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नमोऽस्तु नीलग्रीवायेति पाद्यं मनुनाहरेत् ।
अर्घ्यं च रुद्रगायत्र्याचमनं त्र्यम्बकेण च ॥ १९॥

English
The water used for washing the feet (Pādya) shall be offered with the mantra. “Namostu Nilagrīvāya¹⁰⁴” (obeisance to the blue-necked). The water for the respectful reception (Arghya) shall be offered with the Rudragāyatri$^{105}$ mantra and the sipping water (Ācaṇana) with the Tryambaka$^{106}$ mantra. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पयः पृथिव्यां मन्त्रेण पयसा स्नानमाचरेत् ।
दधिक्राव्णेति मन्त्रेण दधिस्नानं च कारयेत् ॥ २०॥

English
The ceremonial ablution with milk shall be performed with the mantra “Payaḥ Pṛthivyām$^{107}$ etc. The ceremonial ablution with curd shall be performed with the mantra “Dadhi Kṛāṇaḥ$^{108}$” etc. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
घृतस्नानं खलु घृतं घृतयावेति मन्त्रतः ।
मधुव्वाता मधुनक्तं मधुमान्न इति त्र्यृचा ॥ २१॥

मधुखण्डस्नपनं प्रोक्तमिति पञ्चामृतं स्मृतम् ।
अथवा पाद्यमन्त्रेण स्नानं पञ्चामृतेन च ॥ २२॥

English
The ceremonial ablution with ghee shall be performed with the mantra “Ghṛtam Ghṛtayāvā$^{109}$ etc. The ceremonial ablution with honey and Sugar candy shall be performed with three hymns beginning with “Madhuvātā$^{110}$, Madhu Naktam$^{111}$, Madhumānnaḥ$^{112}$”. Thus the Pañcāmṛta ablution is explained. Or the ablution with Pañcāmṛta can be performed with the Pādya mantra Namostu Nilagrīvāya$^{113}$. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
मानस्तोके इति प्रेम्णा मन्त्रेण कटिबन्धनम् ।
नमो धृष्णवे इति वा उत्तरीयं च धारयेत् ॥ २३॥

English
The tying of the waistband (Kaṭibandhana) shall be performed devoutly with the mantra “Mā nastoke$^{114}$” etc. The piece of cloth to be worn on the upper part of the body shall be offered with the mantra “Namo Dhṛṣṇave$^{115}$” etc. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
या ते हेतिरिति प्रेम्णा ऋक्चतुष्केण वैदिकः ।
शिवाय विधिना भक्तश्चरेद्वस्त्रसमर्पणम् ॥ २४॥

English
The pious follower of Vedic rites shall make an offering of cloth (vastrasamarpaṇa) duly to Śiva with the four hymns beginning with “Yā te heti$^{116}$ etc. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः श्वभ्य इति प्रेम्णा गन्धं दद्यादृचा सुधीः ।
नमस्तक्षभ्य इति चाक्षतान्मन्त्रेण चार्पयेत् ॥ २५॥

English
The intelligent devotee shall offer scents devoutly with the mantra “Namaḥ Śvabhyaḥ$^{117}$” etc. He shall offer Akṣatas (raw rice grains) with the mantra “Namastakṣabhyaḥ$^{118}$” etc. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः पार्याय इति वा पुष्पं मन्त्रेण चार्पयेत् ।
नमः पर्ण्णाय इति वा बिल्वपत्रसमर्पणम् ॥ २६॥

English
Flower offerings shall be made with the mantra “Namaḥ Pāryāya$^{119}$” etc. Bilva leaves shall be offered with the mantra “Namaḥ Parṇāya$^{120}$” etc. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः कपर्दिने चेति धूपं दद्याद्यथाविधि ।
दीपं दद्याद्यथोक्तं तु नम आशव इत्यृचा ॥ २७॥

English
The incense shall be offered with the mantra “Namah Kapardine ca$^{121}$” etc. in accordance with the rules. The lamp shall be offered in the prescribed manner with the mantra “Namah Āśave$^{122}$” etc. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नमो ज्येष्ठाय मन्त्रेण दद्यान्नैवेद्यमुत्तमम् ।
मनुना त्र्यम्बकमिति पुनराचमनं स्मृतम् ॥ २८॥

English
The excellent Naivedya shall be offered with the mantra “Namo Jyeṣṭhāya”$^{123}$ etc. Ācamana shall be offered again with the mantra “Tryambakam$^{124}$” etc. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
इमा रुद्रायेति ऋचा कुर्यात्फलसमर्पणम् ।
नमो व्रज्यायेति ऋचा सकलं शम्भवेऽर्पयेत् ॥ २९॥

English
Fruit shall be offered with the mantra “Imā Rudrāya$^{125}$”. Everything shall be dedicated to Śiva with the mantra “Namo Vrajyāya$^{126}$” etc. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मानो महान्तमिति च मानस्तोके इति ततः ।
मन्त्रद्वयेनैकादशाक्षतै रुद्रान्प्रपूजयेत् ॥ ३०॥

English
We shall make an offering of eleven raw rice grains to the eleven Rudras$^{127}$ with the two mantras “Mā No Mahāntam$^{128}$” etc. and “Mā Nastoke$^{129}$” etc. (30.)

[fn 127. The names of eleven Rudras are variously mentioned in the Purāṇas. According to MP they are : Ajaikapād, Ahirbudhnya, Hara, Virūpākṣa, Raivata, Bahurūpa, Tryambaka, Savitā, Jayanta, Pināki: Aparājita. In the VP, the first three are the same; the rest are substituted by Nirṛta, Īśvara, Bhuvana, Aṅgāraka, Ardhaketu, Mṛtyu, Sarpa, Kapālin.]
---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यगर्भ इति त्र्यृचा दक्षिणां हि समर्पयेत् ।
देवस्यत्वेति मन्त्रेण ह्यभिषेकं चरेद्बुधः ॥ ३१॥

English
The scholarly devotee shall offer sacrificial fee (Dakṣiṇā)$^{130}$ with the three mantras beginning with “Hiraṇya-garbha” etc. and shall perform ablution (Abhiṣeka) with the mantra “Devasya tvā$^{131}$” etc. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
दीपमन्त्रेण वा शम्भोर्नीराजनविधिं चरेत् ।
पुष्पाञ्जलिं चरेद्भक्त्या इमा रुद्राय च त्र्यृचा ॥ ३२॥

English
The rite of waving lights Nirājana for Śiva shall be performed with the mantra for the lamp (Namah Āśave*). Puṣpāñjali (offering of handful of flowers) shall be performed with devotion with the hymn Imā rudrāya$^{132}$” etc. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
मानो महान्तमिति च चरेत्प्राज्ञः प्रदक्षिणाम् ।
मानस्तोकेति मन्त्रेण साष्टाङ्गं प्रणमेत्सुधीः ॥ ३३॥

English
The wise devotee shall then perform the Pradakṣiṇā (circumambulation) with the mantra “Mā No Mahāntam$^{133}$” and the intelligent one shall perform Sāṣṭāṅga (eight limbs touching the ground) prostration with the mantra “Mā Nastoke$^{134}$” etc. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एष ते इति मन्त्रेण शिवमुद्रां प्रदर्शयेत् ।
यतो यत इत्यभयां ज्ञानाख्यां त्र्यम्बकेण च ॥ ३४॥

English
He shall show the “Śiva Mudrā” with the mantra “Eṣa te$^{136}$”; the Abhayamudrā with the mantra “Yato Yataḥ$^{136}$” etc. and the Jñāna Mudrā with the Tryambaka$^{137}$ mantra. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः सेनेति मन्त्रेण महामुद्रां प्रदर्शयेत् ।
दर्शयेद्धेनुमुद्रां च नमो गोभ्य ऋचानया ॥ ३५॥

English
The Mahāmudrā shall be shown with the mantra “Namah Senā$^{138}$” etc. He shall then show the Dhenumudrā with the mantra “Namo Gobhyaḥ” etc. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्च मुद्राः प्रदर्श्याथ शिवमन्त्रजपं चरेत् ।
शतरुद्रियमन्त्रेण जपेद् वेदविचक्षणः ॥ ३६॥

English
After showing all these five Mudrās he shall perform the “Śiva Mantra$^{139}$ Japa”. The devotee well versed in the Vedas shall recite the “Śatarudriya” mantra. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पञ्चाङ्गपाठं च कुयाद् वेदविचक्षणः ।
देवागात्विति मन्त्रेण कुर्याच्छम्भोर्विसर्जनम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Pañcāṅgapāṭha shall then be performed by the Vedic scholar. Then Visarjana (Ritualistic farewell) shall be performed with the mantra “Devā gātu$^{140}$” etc. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तः शिवपूजाया व्यासतो वैदिको विधिः ।
समासतश्च श‍ृणुत वैदिकं विधिमुत्तमम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus the Vedic rite of the worship of Śiva has been explained in detail. Now listen to the excellent Vedic rite in brief. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋचा सद्योजातमिति मृदाहरणमाचरेत् ।
वामदेवाय इति च जलप्रक्षेपमाचरेत् ॥ ३९॥

English
The clay shall be brought with the mantra “Sadyo Jātam$^{141}$”. The sprinkling of water shall be performed with the mantra “Vāmadevāya$^{142}$”. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अघोरेण च मन्त्रेण लिङ्गनिर्माणमाचरेत् ।
तत्पुरुषाय मन्त्रेणाह्वानं कुर्याद्यथाविधि ॥ ४०॥

English
The phallic image shall be prepared with the Aghora$^{143}$ mantra. The Āhvāna (invocation) shall be performed with the mantra “Tatpuruṣāya$^{144}$”. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
संयोजयेद्वेदिकायामीशानमनुना हरम् ।
अन्यत्सर्वं विधानं च कुर्यात्सङ्क्षेपतः सुधीः ॥ ४१॥

English
The phallic image of Hara shall be fixed to the pedestal with the Īśāna$^{145}$ mantra. The intelligent devotee shall perform all other rites in brief. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाक्षरेण मन्त्रेण गुरुदत्तेन वा तथा ।
कुर्यात्पूजां षोडशोपचारेण विधिवत्सुधीः ॥ ४२॥

English
With the five-syllabled mantra or any other mantra taught by the preceptor the intelligent devotee shall perform, as prescribed by the rule, the adoration with due observance of the sixteen Upacāras (and the following prayer). (42.)

---

Sanskrit
भवाय भवनाशाय महादेवाय धीमहि ।
उग्राय उग्रनाशाय शर्वाय शशिमौलिने ॥ ४३॥

English
“We meditate upon Bhava, the destroyer of worldly existence, on the great lord, on Ugra, the annihilator of terrible sins, on Śarva the moon-crested". (43.)

---

Sanskrit

सुभक्त्या च भ्रमं त्यक्त्वा भक्त्यैव फलदः शिवः ॥ ४४॥

English
The intelligent devotee shall perform the worship of Śiva with this mantra or with the Vedic mantra with great devotion and abandoning errors. Śiva accords benefits when with devotion he is propitiated. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Notwithstanding the Vedic mode of worship as stated above, O brahmins, we now proceed to explain the common procedure of Śiva's worship. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

तां श‍ृणुध्वं मुनिश्रेष्ठाः सर्वकामप्रदायिनीम् ॥ ४६॥

English
This mode of worship of Śiva's earthen phallic image is the muttering of the names of Śiva. O excellent sages, it yields all cherished desires. Please listen to me. I shall explain it. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
