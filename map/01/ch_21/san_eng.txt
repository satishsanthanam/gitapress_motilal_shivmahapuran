### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - *(The number of phallic images of Śiva used in worship for fulfilment of desires)***

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग व्यासशिष्य नमोऽस्तु ते ।
सम्यगुक्तं त्वया तात पार्थिवार्चाविधानकम् ॥ १॥

कामनाभेदमाश्रित्य सङ्ख्यां ब्रूहि विधानतः ।
शिवपार्थिवलिङ्गानां कृपया दीनवत्सल ॥ २॥

English
*The sages said :—*  O Sūta, O Sūta the fortunate, disciple of Vyāsa, obeisance be to Thee. Thou hast clearly explained the procedure of the worship of the earthen phallic images. Now kindly explain the number of phallic images as based on the wishes, one may have. Thou art favourably disposed to the distressed and the miserable. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श‍ृणुध्वमृषयः सर्वे पार्थिवार्चाविधानकम् ।
यस्यानुष्ठानमात्रेण कृतकृत्यो भवेन्नरः ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O sages, you listen to the rules of procedure in the worship of earthen phallic image, by following which a man reaps full satisfaction. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अकृत्वा पार्थिवं लिङ्गं योऽन्यदेवं प्रपूजयेत् ।
वृथा भवति सा पूजा दमदानादिकं वृथा ॥ ४॥

English
If any one worships another deity without making the earthen phallic image, his worship shall be fruitless. His restraint and charitable gifts go in vain. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्ख्या पार्थिवलिङ्गानां यथाकामं निगद्यते ।
सङ्ख्या सद्यो मुनिश्रेष्ठ निश्चयेन फलप्रदा ॥ ५॥

English
The number of earthen phallic images in regard to different desires is being stipulated which will, O foremost among sages, certainly yield the benefit. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमावाहनं तत्र प्रतिष्ठा पूजनं पृथक् ।
लिङ्गाकारं समं तत्र सर्वं ज्ञेयं पृथक्पृथक् ॥ ६॥

English
The first invocation, installation and worship are of Śiva assumed the form of a goat while Dakṣa became a deer and escaped. A devotee who imitates the sound of a terror-struck goat in the presence of the phallic image of Śiva pleases the God. all separate. Only the shape of the phallic image is the same. Everything else is different. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्यार्थी पुरुषः प्रीत्या सहस्रमितपार्थिवम् ।
पूजयेच्छिवलिङ्गं हि निश्चयात्तत्फलप्रदम् ॥ ७॥

English
A person who seeks learning shall with pleasure make a thousand earthen phallic images and offer worship. Certainly he will get that benefit. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
नरः पार्थिवलिङ्गानां धनार्थी च तदर्धकम् ।
पुत्रार्थी सार्धसाहस्रं वस्त्रार्थी शतपञ्चकम् ॥ ८॥

English
A person desirous of wealth shall make five hundred earthen phallic images; wishing for a son—a thousand five hundred; wishing for garments—five hundred. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मोक्षार्थी कोटिगुणितं भूकामश्च सहस्रकम् ।
दयार्थी च त्रिसाहस्रं तीर्थार्थी द्विसहस्रकम् ॥ ९॥

English
A person desirous of salvation—a crore; desirous of lands—a thousand; craving for mercy—three thousand; desirous of a holy centre—two thousand. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सुहृत्कामी त्रिसाहस्रं वश्यार्थी शतमष्टकम् ।
मारणार्थी सप्तशतं मोहनार्थी शताष्टकम् ॥ १०॥

English
A person desirous of friends—three thousand; desirous of the power of controlling—eight hundred; desirous of bringing about the death of a person—seven hundred; desirous of enchanting—eight hundred. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्चाटनपरश्चैव सहस्रं च यथोक्ततः ।
स्तम्भनार्थी सहस्रं तु द्वेषणार्थी तदर्धकम् ॥ ११॥

English
A person desirous of sweeping off his foes—a thousand; desirous of numbifying—a thousand; desirous of kindling hatred—five hundred. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
निगडान्मुक्तिकामस्तु सहस्रं सार्धमुत्तमम् ।
महाराजभये पञ्चशतं ज्ञेयं विचक्षणैः ॥ १२॥

English
A person desirous of freeing himself from fett rs—a thousand five hundred. If there is fear from a great king—five hundred. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
चौरादिसङ्कटे ज्ञेयं पार्थिवानां शतद्वयम् ।
डाकिन्यादिभये पञ्चशतमुक्तं च पार्थिवम् ॥ १३॥

English
If there is danger from thieves, robbers etc.—two hundred; if there is the evil influence of Dākinī$^{150}$ and other foul spirits—five hundred. (13.)

[fn 150. A female imp or fiend attendant upon Kāli and feeding on human flesh. The Dākinīs are also called Asrapās, 'blood-drinkers'.]
---

Sanskrit
दारिद्रत्ये पञ्चसाहस्रमयुतं सर्वकामदम् । प्लेअसे चेच्क्
अथ नित्यविधिं वक्ष्ये श‍ृणुध्वं मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ १४॥

English
In poverty—five thousand. If ten thousand such are made, all wishes will be fulfilled. O great sages, I shall now mention the daily procedure. Please listen. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एकं पापहरं प्रोक्तं द्विलिङ्गं चार्थसिद्धिदम् ।
त्रिलिङ्गं सर्वकामानां कारणं परमीरितम् ॥ १५॥

English
One such is said to remove sins. Two confer wealth. Three are mentioned as the cause for the fulfilment of all desires. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तरोत्तरमेवं स्यात्पूर्वोक्तगणनावधि ।
मतान्तरमथो वक्ष्ये सङ्ख्यायां मुनिभेदतः ॥ १६॥

English
Above this, more and more benefits accrue until the stipulated number is reached. I shall now mention another opinion coming from a different sage. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गानामयुतं कृत्वा पार्थिवानां सुबुद्धिमान् ।
निर्भयो हि भवेन्नूनं महाराजभयं हरेत् ॥ १७॥

English
An intelligent person can certainly remain fearless after making such ten thousand images. It removes the fear from great kings. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
कारागृहादिमुक्त्यर्थमयुतं कारयेद् बुधः ।
डाकिन्यादिभये सप्तसहस्रं कारयेत्तथा ॥ १८॥

English
A sensible man shall cause ten thousand such to be made for freedom from imprisonment. When there is the fear of the evil influence of Dākinī and other evil spirits he shall cause seven thousand such to be made. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अपुत्रः पञ्चपञ्चाशत् सहस्राणि प्रकारयेत् ।
लिङ्गानामयुतेनैव कन्यकासन्ततिं लभेत् ॥ १९॥

English
A person having no sons shall cause fifty-five thousand such to be made. One shall get daughters by causing ten thousand such to be made. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गानामयुतेनैव विष्ण्वाद्यैश्वर्यमाप्नुयात् ।
लिङ्गानां प्रयुतेनैव ह्यतुलां श्रियमाप्नुयात् ॥ २०॥

English
A devotee shall achieve the prosperity and splendour of Viṣṇu and others by making ten thousand images. He shall derive unrivalled glory and wealth by making one million images. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिमेकां तु लिङ्गानां यः करोति नरो भुवि ।
शिव एव भवेत्सोऽपि नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ २१॥

English
Surely if a man makes a crore he shall become Śiva Himself. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्चा पार्थिवलिङ्गानां कोटियज्ञफलप्रदा ।
भुक्तिदा मुक्तिदा नित्यं ततः कामार्थिनां नृणाम् ॥ २२॥

English
The worship of earthen phallic images accords the benefit of a crore sacrifices. It gives all worldly pleasures and salvation to those who desire them. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
विना लिङ्गार्चनं यस्य कालो गच्छति नित्यशः ।
महाहानिर्भवेत्तस्य दुर्वृत्तस्य दुरात्मनः ॥ २३॥

English
He who spends his time in vain without worship of such images will incur great loss. He is no better than a wicked, evil-souled man. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एकतः सर्वदानानि व्रतानि विविधानि च ।
तीर्थानि नियमा यज्ञा लिङ्गार्चा चैकतः स्मृता ॥ २४॥

English
If the worship of such images is weighed against all the charitable gifts, sacred rites, holy centres, restraints and sacrifices, both will be found equal. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कलौ लिङ्गार्चनं श्रेष्ठं यथा लोके प्रदृश्यते ।
तथान्यन्नास्ति शास्त्राणामेष सिद्धान्तनिश्चयः ॥ २५॥

English
In the age of Kali the worship of the phallic image is excellent as is evident from what we see in the world. There is nothing else. This is the conclusion of all sacred texts and religious cults. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं लिङ्गं विविधापन्निवारणम् ।
पूजयित्वा नरो नित्यं शिवसायुज्यमाप्नुयात् ॥ २६॥

English
The phallic image yields worldly pleasures and salvation. It wards off different sorts of mishaps. By worshipping it, man attains identity with Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनाममयं लिङ्गं नित्यं पूज्यं महर्षिभिः ।
यतश्च सर्वलिङ्गेषु तस्मात्पूज्यं विधानतः ॥ २७॥

English
Since the phallic image is enjoined to be worshipped even by the sages, it shall be worshipped by every one in the manner stipulated. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Based on sizes the images are of three types—Excellent (Uttama), normal (Madhyama) and inferior (Nīca). O foremost of sages, I shall explain them, please listen. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
A phallic image, four angulas (inches) in height, with a splendid pedestal is mentioned as the most excellent by sages who are well-versed in sacred lore. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
He who worships many such images every day with great devotion and faith can achieve the fulfilment of any desire conceived in his heart. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

English
In the four Vedas, nothing else is mentioned so holy as the worship of the phallic image. This is the conclusion arrived at in all sacred lores. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

(36-38.)

English
Then he shall repeat the five-syllabled mantra, Śatarudriya, many Śaiva hymns and read Pañcāṅga and perform circumambulation. After obeisance he shall bid farewell to the phallic image. Thus have I mentioned the worship of Śiva with due devotion. (51-52.)

Sanskrit
रात्रावुदङ्मुखः कुर्याद् देवकार्यं सदैव हि ।
शिवार्चनं सदाप्येवं शुचिः कुर्यादुदङ्मुखः ॥ ५३॥

न प्राचीमग्रतः शम्भोर्नोदीचीं शक्तिसंहिताम् ।
न प्रतीचीं यतः पृष्ठमतो ग्राह्यं समाश्रयेत् ॥ ५४॥

English
Divine rites shall always be performed facing the north in the night. Similarly Śiva's worship shall always be performed facing the north, not the east. Śaktisamhitā shall not be recited facing the north or the west since it is the back. (53-54.)

Sanskrit
विना भस्मत्रिपुण्ड्रेण विना रुद्राक्षमालया ।
बिल्वपत्रं विना नैव पूजयेच्छङ्करं बुधः ॥ ५५॥

भस्माप्राप्तौ मुनिश्रेष्ठाः प्रवृत्ते शिवपूजने ।
तस्मान्मृदापि कर्तव्यं ललाटे च त्रिपुण्ड्रकम् ॥ ५६॥

English
Śiva shall not be worshipped without Tripuṇḍra, Rudrākṣa and Bilvapatra. O best of sages! when the worship is on, if the ash is not available, Tripuṇḍra, (three lines on the forehead) shall be drawn with the white clay. (55-56.)
