### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - *(The glorification of the Rudrākṣa and of the names of Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग व्यासशिष्य नमोऽस्तु ते ।
तदेव व्यासतो ब्रूहि भस्ममाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ॥ १॥

तथा रुद्राक्षमाहात्म्यं नाममाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ।
त्रितयं ब्रूहि सुप्रीत्या ममानन्दय मानसम् ॥ २॥

English
*The sages said :—*  O Sūta, Sūta the fortunate disciple of Vyāsa, obeisance to thee. Please explain again the glorification of the holy ashes, of the Rudrākṣa and of Śiva's names. Lovingly explaining the three, please delight our minds. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टं भवद्भिश्च लोकानां हितकारकम् ।
भवन्तो वै महाधन्याः पवित्राः कुलभूषणाः ॥ ३॥

येषां चैव शिवः साक्षाद् दैवतं परमं शुभम् ।
सदाशिवकथा लोके वल्लभा भवतां सदा ॥ ४॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  It is good that you have referred to this matter that is highly beneficent to the world. You are blessed, holy and ornaments to your families since you own Śiva as your sole great favourite deity. The anecdotes of Śiva are dear to you all for ever. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
ते धन्याश्च कृतार्थाश्च सफलं देहधारणम् ।
उद्धृतं च कुलं तेषां ये शिवं समुपासते ॥ ५॥

English
Those who adore Śiva are blessed and content. Their birth is fruitful and their family is elevated. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनाम मुखे यस्य सदा शिवशिवेति च ।
पापानि न स्पृशन्त्येव खदिराङ्गारकं यथा ॥ ६॥

English
Sins never touch those from whose mouth the names Sadāśiva, Śiva etc. come out for ever, as they do not touch the burning charcoal of the khadira wood. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशिवाय नमस्तुभ्यं मुखं व्याहरते यदा ।
तन्मुखं पावनं तीर्थं सर्वपापविनाशनम् ॥ ७॥

English
When a mouth utters "Obeisance to Thee, holy Śiva" that mouth (face) is on a par with holy centres destroying all sins. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मुखं च तथा यो वै पश्यति प्रीतिमान्नरः ।
तीर्थजन्यं फलं तस्य भवतीति सुनिश्चितम् ॥ ८॥

English
It is certain that the benefit of making pilgrimages to holy centres accrues to one who lovingly looks at His holy face. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र त्रयं सदा तिष्ठेदेतच्छुभतरं द्विजाः ।
तस्य दर्शनमात्रेण वेणीस्नानफलं लभेत् ॥ ९॥

English
O brahmins, the place where these three are found is the most auspicious one. A mere contact of the place accords the benefit of taking a holy dip in the sacred Trivenī (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनाम विभूतिश्च तथा रुद्राक्ष एव च ।
एतत्त्रयं महापुण्यं त्रिवेणीसदृशं स्मृतम् ॥ १०॥

English
Śiva's name, the ashes and the Rudrākṣa beads—the three are very holy and are on a par with Trivenī* (the confluence of the three holy rivers). (10.)

[fn *The place of confluence (Prayāga, now Allahabad) of the Ganges with the yamunā and the subterranean Sarasvatī.]
---

Sanskrit
एतत्त्रयं शरीरे च यस्य तिष्ठति नित्यशः ।
तस्यैव दर्शनं लोके दुर्लभं पापहारकम् ॥ ११॥

English
The sight of the persons who have these three in their bodies is a rare occurrence. But when obtained it removes all sins. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दर्शनं यथा वेणी नोभयोरन्तरं मनाक् ।
एवं यो न विजानाति स पापिष्ठो न संशयः ॥ १२॥

English
There is no difference at all between these two—a sight of the holy man and a bath in the Trivenī. He who does not realise this is undoubtedly a sinner. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विभूतिर्यस्य नो भाले नाङ्गे रुद्राक्षधारणम् ।
नास्ये शिवमयी वाणी तं त्यजेदधमं यथा ॥ १३॥

English
The man who has no ashes on his forehead, has not worn Rudrākṣa on his body and does not utter names of Śiva shall be shunned as one does a base man. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवं नाम यथा गङ्गा विभूतिर्यमुना मता ।
रुद्राक्षं विधिजा प्रोक्ता सर्वपापविनाशिनी ॥ १४॥

English
As said by Brahmā, Śiva’s name is on a par with Gaṅgā, the ash is equal to Yamunā and Rudrākṣa destroys all sins (and is equal to Sarasvatī). (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शरीरे च त्रयं यस्य तत्फलं चैकतः स्थितम् ।
एकतो वेणिकायाश्च स्नानजं तु फलं बुधैः ॥ १५॥

तदेवं तुलितं पूर्वं ब्रह्मणा हितकारिणा ।
समानं चैव तज्जातं तस्माद् धार्यं सदा बुधैः ॥ १६॥

English
Brahmā wishing to bestow beneficence weighed one against the other. He put on one side the benefit achieved by a person in whose body the three things were present. On the other side he put the blessedness achieved by those who took their bath in the holy Trivenī. Both were found equal. Hence scholars shall wear these always. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिनं हि समारभ्य ब्रह्मविष्ण्वादिभिः सुरैः ।
धार्यते त्रितयं तच्च दर्शनात्पापहारकम् ॥ १७॥

English
From that time onwards Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other Devas wear these three. Their very sight dispels sins. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋष्य ऊचुः ।
ईदृशं हि फलं प्रोक्तं नामादित्रितयोद्भवम् ।
तन्माहात्म्यं विशेषेण वक्तुमर्हसि सुव्रत ॥ १८॥

English
*The sages said :—*  O righteous one, you have explained the benefit of the three things: Śiva’s name etc. Please explain it vividly. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
ऋषयो हि महाप्राज्ञाः सच्छैवा ज्ञानिनां वराः ।
तन्माहात्म्यं हि सद्भक्त्या श‍ृणुतादरतो द्विजाः ॥ १९॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O brahmanical sages, you are all good devotees of Śiva, gifted with knowledge and great intellect. You are the foremost among the wise. Please listen with reverence to their greatness. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सुगूढमपि शास्त्रेषु पुराणेषु श्रुतिष्वपि ।
भवत्स्नेहान्मया विप्राः प्रकाशः क्रियतेऽधुना ॥ २०॥

English
O brahmins, it is mysteriously hidden in sacred texts, Vedas and Purāṇas. Out of love for you I reveal the same to you now. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्तत्त्रितयमाहात्म्यं सञ्जानाति द्विजोत्तमाः ।
महेश्वरं विना सर्वं ब्रह्माण्डे सदसत्परम् ॥ २१॥

English
O foremost among the brahmins ! Who ever does know the real greatness of the three except Śiva who is beyond all in the whole universe ? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
वच्म्यहं नाममाहात्म्यं यथाभक्ति समासतः ।
श‍ृणुत प्रीतितो विप्राः सर्वपापहरं परम् ॥ २२॥

English
Briefly I shall explain the greatness of the names as prompted by my devotion. O brahmins, do you lovingly listen to his greatness: the destroyer of all sins. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेति नामदावाग्नेर्महापातकपर्वताः ।
भस्मीभवन्त्यनायासात्सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ २३॥

English
Mountainous heaps of great sins are destroyed as in a blazing forest fire when the names of Śiva are repeated. They are reduced to ashes without any difficulty. It is true, undoubtedly true. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पापमूलानि दुःखानि विविधान्यपि शौनक ।
शिवनामैकनश्यानि नान्यनश्यानि सर्वथा ॥ २४॥

English
O Śaunaka, different sorts of miseries with sins as their roots can be quelled only by muttering Śiva's names, and not by anything else entirely. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स वैदिकः स पुण्यात्मा स धन्यः स बुधो मतः ।
शिवनामजपासक्तो यो नित्यं भुवि मानवः ॥ २५॥

English
The man who is devotedly attached to the Japas of Śiva's names in the world, is really a follower of the Vedas, a meritorious soul and a blessed scholar. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भवन्ति विविधा धर्मास्तेषां सद्यः फलोन्मुखाः ।
येषां भवति विश्वासः शिवनामजपे मुने ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, instantaneously fruitful are the different sacred rites of those who have full faith in the efficacy of the Japas of Śiva's names. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पातकानि विनश्यन्ति यावन्ति शिवनामतः ।
भुवि तावन्ति पापानि क्रियन्ते न नरैर्मुने ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, so many sins are not committed by men in the world as are and can be destroyed by Śiva's names. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्महत्यादिपापानां राशीनप्रमितान्मुने ।
शिवनाम द्रुतं प्रोक्तं नाशयत्यखिलान्नरैः ॥ २८॥

English
O sage, Śiva's names repeated by men, immediately destroy the countless heaps of sins such as the slaughter of a brahmin. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनामतरीं प्राप्य संसाराब्धिं तरन्ति ये ।
संसारमूलपापानि तानि नश्यन्त्यसंशयम् ॥ २९॥

English
Those who cross the ocean of worldly existence by resorting to the raft of the names of Śiva do definitely destroy those sins that are the root-cause of worldly existence. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
संसारमूलभूतानां पातकानां महामुने ।
शिवनामकुठारेण विनाशो जायते ध्रुवम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O great sage, the destruction of sins that are the roots of worldly existence is certainly effected by the axe of Śiva's names. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनामामृतं पेयं पापदावानलार्दितैः ।
पापदावाग्नितप्तानां शान्तिस्तेन विना न हि ॥ ३१॥

English
The nectar of Śiva's names shall be drunk by those who are distressed and scorched by the conflagration of sins. Without it, the people who are scorched by the conflagration cannot have any peace. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेति नामपीयूषवर्षधारापरिप्लुताः ।
संसारदवमध्येऽपि न शोचन्ति कदाचन ॥ ३२॥

English
Those who are drenched by the nectarine downpour of Śiva's names never feel ill at ease even in the middle of the conflagration of worldly existence. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनाम्नि महद्भक्तिर्जाता येषां महात्मनाम् ।
तद्विधानां तु सहसा मुक्तिर्भवति सर्वथा ॥ ३३॥

English
The noble souls who have acquired great devotion to the names of Śiva, and those like them, attain perfect liberation instantaneously. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेकजन्मभिर्येन तपस्तप्तं मुनीश्वर ।
शिवनाम्नि भवेद्भक्तिः सर्वपापापहारिणी ॥ ३४॥

English
O lord of sages, devotion to the names of Śiva, that destroys all sins can be acquired only by him who has performed penances in the course of many births. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्यासाधारणी शम्भुनाम्नि भक्तिरखण्डिता ।
तस्यैव मोक्षः सुलभो नान्यस्येति मतिर्मम ॥ ३५॥

English
Salvation is easy of access only to him who has extraordinary and unbroken devotion for the names of Śiva. I believe in this. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वाप्यनेकपापानि शिवनामजपादरः ।
सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तो भवत्येव न संशयः ॥ ३६॥

English
Even if he has committed many sins, a person who has reverence for the Japa of Śiva's names, becomes certainly free from all sins. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
भवन्ति भस्मसाद् वृक्षा दवदग्धा यथा वने ।
तथा तावन्ति दग्धानि पापानि शिवनामतः ॥ ३७॥

English
Just as the trees in a forest are burnt and reduced to ashes by the forest fire, so also are the sins destroyed by Śiva's names. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यो नित्यं भस्मपूताङ्गः शिवनामजपादरः ।
स तरत्येव संसारमघोरमपि शौनक ॥ ३८॥

English
O Śaunaka, he who regularly sanctifies his body by the holy ashes and who performs the Japa of Śiva's names crosses even the terrible ocean of worldly existence. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मस्वहरणं कृत्वा हत्वापि ब्राह्मणान्बहून् ।
न लिप्यते नरः पापैः शिवनामजपादरः ॥ ३९॥

English
A person who undertakes the Japa of Śiva's names is not sullied by sins even after misappropriating a brahmin's wealth and killing many brahmins. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
विलोक्य वेदानखिलान् शिवनामजपः परः ।
संसारतरणोपाय इति पूर्वैर्विनिश्चितम् ॥ ४०॥

English
After going through all the Vedas it has been decided by our ancestors that the noblest means of crossing the ocean of worldly existence is the performance of the Japa of Śiva's names. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्त्या मुनिश्रेष्ठाः श्लोकेनैकेन वच्म्यहम् ।
शिवाभिधानमाहात्म्यं सर्वपापापहारणम् ॥ ४१॥

English
O excellent sages, why should I say much? By means of a single verse I shall mention the greatness and efficacy of the names of Śiva or the destruction of all sins. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
पापानां हरणे शम्भोर्नाम्नः शक्तिर्हि यावती ।
शक्नोति पातकं तावत्कर्तुं नापि नरः क्वचित् ॥ ४२॥

English
The power of the names of Śiva in destroying sins is more than the ability of men to commit them. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनामप्रभावेण लेभे सद्गतिमुत्तमाम् ।
इन्द्रद्युम्ननृपः पूर्वं महापापयुतो मुने ॥ ४३॥

English
O sage, formerly the king Indradyumna who was a great sinner, attained the excellent goal of the good through the influence of Śiva's names. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा काचिद् द्विजा योषाऽसौ मुने बहुपापिनी ।
शिवनामप्रभावेण लेभे सद्गतिमुत्तमाम् ॥ ४४॥

English
O sage, similarly a brahmin woman too of very sinful activities attained the excellent goal of the good through the influence of Śiva's names. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तं वो द्विजश्रेष्ठा नाममाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ।
श‍ृणुध्वं भस्ममाहात्म्यं सर्वपावनपावनम् ॥ ४५॥

English
O excellent brahmins, thus I have told you about the surpassing excellence of the names. Now please listen to the greatness of holy ashes, the most sacred of all. (45.)

---
