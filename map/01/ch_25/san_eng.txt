### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - *(The greatness of Rudrākṣa)***

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
शौनकर्षे महाप्राज्ञ शिवरूप महापते ।
श‍ृणु रुद्राक्षमाहात्म्यं समासात् कथयाम्यहम् ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said :-  O sage Śaunaka, highly intelligent, of the form of Śiva, noble-minded, please listen to the greatness of Rudrākṣa. I shall explain it briefly. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवप्रियतमो ज्ञेयो रुद्राक्षः परपावनः ।
दर्शनात् स्पर्शनाज्जाप्यात् सर्वपापहरः स्मृतः ॥ २॥

English
Rudrākṣa is a favourite bead of Śiva. It is highly sanctifying. It removes all sins by sight, contact and Japas. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा रुद्राक्षमहिमा देव्यग्रे कथितो मुने ।
लोकोपकरणार्थाय शिवेन परमात्मना ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, formerly the greatness of Rudrākṣa was declared to the Goddess by Śiva, the supreme soul, for rendering help to the worlds. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
श्रूयतां तु महेशानि रुद्राक्षमहिमा शिवे ।
कथयामि तव प्रीत्या भक्तानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ४॥

English
Śiva said :-  O Śivā, Maheśāni, be pleased to hear the greatness of Rudrākṣa. I speak out love for you from a desire for the benefit of the devotees of Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यवर्षसहस्राणि महेशानि पुनः पुरा ।
तपः प्रकुर्वतस्त्रस्तं मनः संयम्य वै मम ॥ ५॥

स्वतन्त्रेण परेशेन लोकोपकृतिकारिणा ।
लीलया परमेशानि चक्षुरुन्मीलितं मया ॥ ६॥

पुटाभ्यां चारुचक्षुर्भ्यां पतिता जलबिन्दवः ।
तत्राश्रुबिन्दुतो जाता वृक्षा रुद्राक्षसंज्ञकाः ॥ ७॥

English
O Maheśāni, formerly I had been performing penance for thousands of divine years. Although I had controlled it rigorously, my mind was in flutter. Out of sport, I being self-possessed just opened my eyes, O Goddess, from a desire of helping the worlds. Drops of tears fell from my beautiful half-closed eyes. From those tear-drops there cropped up the Rudrākṣa plants. (5-7.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थावरत्वमनुप्राप्य भक्तानुग्रहकारणात् ।
ते दत्ता विष्णुभक्तेभ्यश्चतुर्वर्णेभ्य एव च ॥ ८॥

English
They became immobile. In order to bless the devotees they were given to the four Varṇas devoted to the worship of Viṣṇu (8.)

---

Sanskrit
भूमौ गौडोद्भवांश्चक्रे रुद्राक्षाञ्छिववल्लभान् ।
मथुरायामयोध्यायां लङ्कायां मलये तथा ॥ ९॥

सह्याद्रौ च तथा काश्यां देशेष्वन्येषु वा तथा ।
परानसह्यपापौघभेदनाञ्छ्रुतिनोदनान् ॥ १०॥

English
Rudrākṣas grown in Gauḍa¹⁶⁵ land became great favourites of Śiva. They were grown in Mathurā, Laṅkā, Ayodhyā, Malaya¹⁶⁶, Sahya¹⁶⁷ mountain, Kāśī and other places. They are competent to break asunder the clustered sins unbearable to the others, as the sacred texts have declared. (9-10.)

[fn 165. Gauḍa deśa, according to Skandapurāṇa, was the central part of Bengal extending from Varṇa to the borders of Orissa :

ब्रह्मदेशं समारभ्य अनेनेशान्तगः शिवे ।
गौहदेशः समाख्यातुः सर्वविषाविशारदः ॥]

[fn 166. Malaya : a mountain range on the west of Malabar, the western ghats, abounding in sandal trees.]

[fn 167. Sahya : It is one of the seven principal ranges, the other six being Mahendra, Malaya, Sūktimat, Rikṣa, Vindhya and Pāripatra or Pāriyātra.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणाः क्षत्रिया वैश्याः शूद्रा जाता ममाज्ञया ।
रुद्राक्षास्ते पृथिव्यां तु तज्जातीयाः शुभाक्षकाः ॥ ११॥

English
At my bidding they were classified into Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas and Śūdras. These Rudrākṣas are of auspicious nature. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
श्वेतरक्ताः पीतकृष्णा वर्णा ज्ञेयाः क्रमाद् बुधैः ।
स्वजातीयं नृभिर्धार्यं रुद्राक्षं वर्णतः क्रमात् ॥ १२॥

English
The colours of the four types of Rudrākṣas are respectively white, red, yellow and black. All people shall wear the Rudrākṣa of their own Varṇa. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्णैस्तु तत्फलं धार्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिफलेप्सुभिः ।
शिवभक्तैर्विशेषेण शिवयोः प्रीतये सदा ॥ १३॥

English
If they desire their benefit, namely worldly pleasures and salvation and if the devotees of Śiva wish to gratify Śiva they must wear the Rudrākṣa. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
धात्रीफलप्रमाणं यच्छ्रेष्ठमेतदुदाहृतम् ।
बदरीफलमात्रं तु मध्यमं सम्प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ १४॥

English
A Rudrākṣa of the size of an Emblic myrobalan (Dhātrīphala) is mentioned as the most excellent; one of the size of the fruit of the jujube tree (Badarīphala) is spoken of as the middling. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अधमं चणमात्रं स्यात् प्रक्रियैषा परोच्यते ।
श‍ृणु पार्वति सुप्रीत्या भक्तानां हितकाम्यया ॥ १५॥

English
O Pārvatī, lovingly listen to this from a desire for the benefit of the devotees. The meanest of Rudrākṣas is of the size of a gram according to this excellent classification. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
बदरीफलमात्रं च यत् स्यात् किल महेश्वरि ।
तथापि फलदं लोके सुखसौभाग्यवर्धनम् ॥ १६॥

English
O Maheśvari, even the Rudrākṣa which is only of the size of the fruit of the jujube accords the benefit and heightens happiness and good fortune. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
धात्रीफलसमं यत् स्यात् सर्वारिष्टविनाशनम् ।
गुञ्जया सदृशं यत् स्यात् सर्वार्थफलसाधनम् ॥ १७॥

English
That which is of the size of the emblic myrobalan is conducive to the destruction of all distresses. That which is of the size of a Guñjā (the berry) is conducive to the achievement of the fruit of all desires. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा यथा लघुः स्याद्वै तथाधिकफलप्रदः ।
एकैकतः फलं प्रोक्तं दशांशैरधिकं बुधैः ॥ १८॥

English
The lighter the Rudrākṣa, the more fruitful it is. Each of these is fruitful and that of a weight of one tenth is considered by scholars as the most fruitful. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्राक्षधारणं प्रोक्तं पापनाशनहेतवे ।
तस्माच्च धारणीयो वै सर्वार्थसाधनो ध्रुवम् ॥ १९॥

English
The wearing of Rudrākṣa is recommended for the sake of destroying sins. Hence that which is conducive to the achievement of every object has to be worn certainly. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा च दृश्यते लोके रुद्राक्षः फलदः शुभः ।
न तथा दृश्यतेऽन्या च मालिका परमेश्वरि ॥ २०॥

English
O Parameśvari, no other necklace or garland is observed in the world to be so auspicious and fruitful as the Rudrākṣa. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
समाः स्निग्धा दृढाः स्थूलाः कण्टकैः संयुताः शुभाः ।
रुद्राक्षाः कामदा देवि भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदाः सदा ॥ २१॥

English
O Goddess, Rudrākṣas of even size, glossy, firm, thick and having many thornlike protrusions yield desires and bestow worldly pleasures and salvation for ever. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कृमिदुष्टं छिन्नभिन्नं कण्टकैर्हीनमेव च ।
व्रणयुक्तमवृत्तं च रुद्राक्षान् षड् विवर्जयेत् ॥ २२॥

English
Six types of Rudrākṣas shall be discarded :— that which is defiled by worms, is cut and broken, has no thorn-like protrusions, has cracks and is not circular. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयमेव कृतद्वारं रुद्राक्षं स्यादिहोत्तमम् ।
यत्तु पौरुषयत्नेन कृतं तन्मध्यमं भवेत् ॥ २३॥

English
That which has a natural hole from end to end is the most excellent; that which is bored through by human effort is the middling one. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्राक्षधारणं प्रोक्तं महापातकनाशनम् ।
रुद्रसङ्ख्याशतं धृत्वा रुद्ररूपो भवेन्नरः ॥ २४॥

English
The wearing of Rudrākṣa is spoken of as conducive to the destruction of great sins. If eleven hundred Rudrākṣas are worn on the person, the man assumes the form of Rudra. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादशशतानीह धृत्वा यत्फलमाप्यते ।
तत्फलं शक्यते नैव वक्तुं वर्षशतैरपि ॥ २५॥

English
Even in hundreds of years it is impossible to describe adequately the benefit derived by wearing eleven hundred and fifty Rudrākṣas. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शतार्धेन युतैः पञ्चशतैर्वै मुकुटं मतम् ।
रुद्राक्षैर्विरचेत्सम्यग्भक्तिमान्पुरुषो वरः ॥ २६॥

English
A devout man shall make a coronet consisting of five hundred and fifty Rudrākṣas. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिभिः शतैः षष्टियुक्तैस्त्रिरावृत्त्या तथा पुनः ।
रुद्राक्षैरुपवीतं च निर्मीयाद्भक्तितत्परः ॥ २७॥

English
A person of pious nature shall make three circular strings in the manner of the sacred thread, each having three hundred and sixty beads. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिखायां च त्रयं प्रोक्तं रुद्रक्षाणां महेश्वरि ।
कर्णयोः षट् च षट् चैव वामदक्षिणयोस्तथा ॥ २८॥

English
O Maheśvari, three Rudrākṣas must be worn on the tuft and six in each of the ears right and left. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
शतमेकोत्तरं कण्ठे बाह्वोर्वै रुद्रसङ्ख्यया ।
कूर्परद्वारयोस्तत्र मणिबन्धे तथा पुनः ॥ २९॥

उपवीते त्रयं धार्यं शिवभक्तिरतैर्नरैः ।
शेषानुर्वरितान्पञ्च सम्मितान्धारयेत्कटौ ॥ ३०॥

English
Hundred and one Rudrākṣas shall be worn round the neck; eleven Rudrākṣas shall be worn round each of the arms, elbows and wrists. Devotees of Śiva shall have three Rudrākṣas in the sacred thread and round the hips five Rudrākṣas shall be tied. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सङ्ख्या धृता येन रुद्राक्षाः परमेश्वरि ।
तद्रूपं तु प्रणम्यं हि स्तुत्यं सर्वैर्महेशवत् ॥ ३१॥

English
O Parameśvari, the person by whom so many Rudrākṣas are worn is worthy of being bowed to and adored by all like Maheśa. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूतं स्थितं ध्याने यदा कृत्वासने जनम् ।
शिवेति व्याहरंश्चैव दृष्ट्वा पापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३२॥

English
Such a person while in contemplation shall be duly seated and addressed "O Śiva". Seeing him, every one is freed from sins. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शताधिकसहस्रस्य विधिरेष प्रकीर्तितः ।
तदभावे प्रकारोऽन्यः शुभः सम्प्रोच्यते मया ॥ ३३॥

English
This is the rule regarding eleven hundred Rudrākṣas. If so many are not available, another auspicious procedure I mention to you. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
शिखायामेकरुद्राक्षं शिरसा त्रिंशतं वहेत् ।
पञ्चाशच्च गले दध्याद् बाह्वोः षोडश षोडश ॥ ३४॥

मणिबन्धे द्वादश द्विस्कन्धे पञ्चशतं वहेत् ।
अष्टोत्तरशतैर्माल्यमुपवीतं प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ३५॥

एवं सहस्ररुद्राक्षान्धारयेद्यो दृढव्रतः ।
तं नमन्ति सुराः सर्वे यथा रुद्रस्तथैव सः ॥ ३६॥

English
One Rudrākṣa shall be worn on the tuft, thirty on the head, fifty round the neck; sixteen in each of the arms; twelve round each of the wrists; five hundred on the shoulders, and three strings each having hundred and eight in the manner of the sacred thread. He who wears in all a thousand Rudrākṣas and is of firm resolve in performing rites is bowed to by all Devas like Rudra himself. (34-36.)

---

Sanskrit
एकं शिखायां रुद्राक्षं चत्वारिंशत्तु मस्तके ।
द्वात्रिंशत्कण्ठदेशे तु वक्षस्यष्टोत्तरं शतम् ॥ ३७॥

एकैकं कर्णयोः षट् षट् बाह्वोः षोडश षोडश ।
करयो रविमानेन द्विगुणेन मुनीश्वर ॥ ३८॥

सङ्ख्या प्रीतिधृता येन सोऽपि शैवजनः परः ।
शिववत्पूजनीयो हि वन्द्यः सर्वैरभीक्ष्णशः ॥ ३९॥

English
One Rudrākṣa shall be worn on the tuft, forty on the forehead, thirty-two round the neck; hundred and eight over the chest; six in each of the ears; sixteen round each of the arms; O lord of sages, according to the measurement of the forearms, twelve or twice that number shall be worn there. A person who wears so many, out of love, is a great devotee of Śiva. He shall be worshipped like Śiva. He is worthy of being always honoured by all. (37-39.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरसीशानमन्त्रेण कर्णे तत्पुरुषेण च ।
अघोरेण गले धार्यं तेनैव हृदयेऽपि च ॥ ४०॥

English
It shall be worn on the head repeating Īśāna¹⁶⁸ mantra; on the ears with Tripuruṣa¹⁶⁹ mantra; round the neck with Aghora$^{170}$ mantra and on the chest also likewise. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अघोरबीजमन्त्रेण करयोर्धारयेत्सुधीः ।
पञ्चदशाक्षग्रथितां वामदेवेन चोदरे ॥ ४१॥

English
The wise devotee shall wear the Rudrākṣa round the forearms with Aghora Bija mantra. A string of fifteen beads shall be worn on the stomach with Vāmadeva mantra.$^{171}$ (41.)


---

Sanskrit
पञ्चब्रह्मभिरङ्गैश्च त्रिमालां पञ्च सप्त च ।
अथ वा मूलमन्त्रेण सर्वानक्षांस्तु धारयेत् ॥ ४२॥

English
With five mantras—Sadyojāta etc. three, five or seven garlands shall be worn. Or all beads shall be worn with the Mūla mantra$^{172}$. (42.)

[fn 172. The five-syllabled mantra "Namaḥ Śivāya" is the basic mantra of Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
मद्यं मांसं तु लशुनं पलाण्डुं शिग्रुमेव च ।
श्लेष्मान्तकं विड्वराहं भक्षणे वर्जयेत्ततः ॥ ४३॥

English
A devotee of Śiva shall refrain from eating meat, garlic, onion, red garlic, potherb, Śleṣmātaka, pig of rubbish and liquors. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
वलक्षं रुद्राक्षं द्विजतनुभिरेवेह विहितं
सुरक्तं क्षत्राणां प्रमुदितमुमे पीतमसकृत् ।
ततो वैश्यैर्धार्यं प्रतिदिवसभावश्यकमहो
तथा कृष्णं शूद्रैः श्रुतिगदितमार्गोऽयमगजे ॥ ४४॥

English
O Umā, daughter of the mountain, the white Rudrākṣa shall be worn by the brahmin, the red by the Kṣatriya, the yellow by the Vaiśya, the black by the Śūdra. This is the path indicated by the Vedas. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्णी वनी गृहयतीर्नियमेन दध्या-
देतद्रहस्यपरमो न हि जातु तिष्ठेत् ।
रुद्राक्षधारणमिदं सुकृतैश्च लभ्यं
त्यक्त्वेदमेतदखिलान्नरकान् प्रयान्ति ॥ ४५॥

English
Whether he is a householder, forest-dweller, ascetic or of any Order, none shall go out of this secret advice. Only by great merits can the opportunity to wear the Rudrākṣa be obtained. If he misses it he will go to hell. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
आदावामलकास्ततो लघुतरा
रुग्णास्ततः कण्टकैः
सन्दष्टाः कृमिभिस्तनूपकरण-
च्छिद्रेण हीनास्तथा ।
धार्या नैव शुभेप्सुभिश्चणकवद्
रुद्राक्षमप्यन्ततो
रुद्राक्षो मम लिङ्गमङ्गलमुमे
सूक्ष्मं प्रशस्तं सदा ॥ ४६॥

English
The Rudrākṣas of the size of an Emblic myrobalan and those of lighter weight but depressed with thorns, those eaten by worms or without holes and those characterized by other defects shall not be worn by those wishing for auspicious results. They shall avoid small ones of the size of gram. O Umā, Rudrākṣa is an auspicious complement to my phallic image. The small one is always praiseworthy. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाश्रमाणां वर्णानां स्त्रीशूद्राणां शिवाज्ञया ।
धार्याः सदैव रुद्राक्षा यतीनां प्रणवेन हि ॥ ४७॥

English
People of all Varṇas and Āśramas even women and Śūdras can wear Rudrākṣa at the bidding of Śiva. The ascetics shall wear it with the Praṇava. (47.)

Thus, O lord of sages, whatever has been asked has now been explained. The greatness of ash and Rudrākṣa bestows the luxuriant fulfilment of all desires. (92.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद्यः श‍ृणुयान्नित्यं माहात्म्यं परमं शुभम् ।
रुद्राक्षभस्मनोर्भक्त्या सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ ९३॥

English
He who regularly listens to the highly auspicious greatness of ash and Rudrākṣa with devotion shall attain the fulfilment of all desires. (93.)

---

Sanskrit
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा पुत्रपौत्रादिसंयुतः ।
लभेत्परत्र सन्मोक्षं शिवस्यातिप्रियो भवेत् ॥ ९४॥

English
He will enjoy all happiness here. He will be blessed with sons and grandsons. In the next world he will attain salvation. He will be a great favourite of Śiva. (94.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्येश्वरसंहितेयं कथिता वो मुनीश्वराः ।
सर्वसिद्धिप्रदा नित्यं मुक्तिदा शिवशासनात् ॥ ९५॥

English
O lordly sages, thus the compendium of Vidyesvara-samhita has been narrated to you all. As ordered by Śiva it bestows achievement of everything and salvation. (95.)

---
