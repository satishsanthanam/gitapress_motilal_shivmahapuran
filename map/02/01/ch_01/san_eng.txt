### **CHAPTER ONE - *(The inquiry of the sages)***

---

Sanskrit
१

विश्वोद्भवस्थितिलयादिषु हेतुमेकं
गौरीपतिं विदिततत्त्वमनन्तकीर्तिम् ।
मायाश्रयं विगतमायमचिन्त्यरूपं
बोधस्वरूपममलं हि शिवं नमामि ॥ १॥

English
I bow to Śiva the consort of Gauri, the sole cause of the origin, sustenance, dissolution of the universe, who has understood the reality, who is of endless renown, who is the support of Māyā but is free from its influence, whose form is incomprehensible, who is unsullied and who is perfect knowledge itself. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वन्दे शिवं तं प्रकृतेरनादिं
प्रशान्तमेकं पुरुषोत्तमं हि ।
स्वमायया कृत्स्नमिदं हि सृष्ट्वा
नभोवदन्तर्बहिरास्थितो यः ॥ २॥

English
I salute Śiva who is prior to Prakṛti, who is calm and tranquil, the only excellent Puruṣa, who has created this visible universe and who stays both within and without like ether. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वन्देऽन्तरस्थं निजगूढरूपं
शिवं स्वतः स्रष्टुमिदं विचष्टे ।
जगन्ति नित्यं परितो भ्रमन्ति
यत्सन्निधौ चुम्बकलोहवत्तम् ॥ ३॥

English
I salute Śiva, of unmanifest form, who having extended himself by way of creation stands in the middle of it while the worlds move around him like iron filings round the magnet. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
जगतः पितरं शम्भुं जगतो मातरं शिवाम् ।
तत्पुत्रश्च गणाधीशं नत्वैतद्वर्णयामहे ॥ ४॥

English
*Vyāsa said :—*  I describe this after bowing to Śambhu, the father of the universe, Śivā the mother of the universe and Gaṇādhīsa their son. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा मुनयः सर्वे नैमिषारण्यवासिनः ।
पप्रच्छुर्वरया भक्त्या सूतं ते शौनकादयः ॥ ५॥

English
Once Śaunaka and other sages living in Naimiṣa forest asked Sūta with full devotion. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
विद्येश्वरसंहितायाः श्रुता सा सत्कथा शुभा ।
साध्यसाधनखण्डाख्या रम्याद्या भक्तवत्सला ॥ ६॥

English
*The sages said :—*  The good and auspicious story of Vidyēśvarasaṃhitā has been heard by us. This first delightful compendium, "On the achievable and the means of achievement" is lovingly disposed to the devotees. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत सूत महाभाग चिरञ्जीव सुखी भव ।
यच्छ्रावयसि नस्तात शाङ्करीं परमां कथाम् ॥ ७॥

English
Sūta, O blessed Sūta, live long. Be happy. You will please narrate to us, O dear, the great anecdotes of Śiva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पिबन्तस्त्वन्मुखाम्भोजच्युतं ज्ञानामृतं वयम् ।
अवितृप्ताः पुनः किञ्चित्प्रष्टुमिच्छामहेऽनघ ॥ ८॥

English
O sinless one, drinking the nectar of knowledge poured out from your lotus-mouth we are never satiated. Hence we would like to inquire of you something more. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यासप्रसादात्सर्वज्ञो प्राप्तोऽसि कृतकृत्यताम् ।
नाज्ञातं विद्यते किञ्चिद्भूतं भव्यं भवच्च यत् ॥ ९॥

English
O omniscient one, by the favour of Vyāsa you have realised contentment. There is nothing not known to you whether of the past, present or future. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरोर्व्यासस्य सद्भक्त्या समासाद्य कृपां पराम् ।
सर्वं ज्ञातं विशेषेण सर्वं सार्थं कृतं जनुः ॥ १०॥

English
In return for your excellent devotion you have gained the great favour of your preceptor Vyāsa. You have understood everything. You have made your life highly noble and purposeful. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं कथय प्राज्ञ शिवरूपमनुत्तमम् ।
दिव्यानि वै चरित्राणि शिवयोरप्यशेषतः ॥ ११॥

English
Now, O wise one, please explain the excellent form of Śiva. Please narrate the divine anecdote of Śiva and Pārvatī without omitting anything. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अगुणो गुणतां याति कथं लोके महेश्वरः ।
शिवतत्त्वं वयं सर्वे न जानीमो विचारतः ॥ १२॥

English
Maheśvara is Aguṇa (free from attributes). How does He take up the Saguṇa from in the world ? We do not know the true nature of Śiva, despite our great deliberation. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टेः पूर्वं कथं शम्भुः स्वरूपेणावतिष्ठते ।
सृष्टिमध्ये स हि कथं क्रीडन्संवर्तते प्रभुः ॥ १३॥

English
Before the origin of creation how does lord Śiva maintain His form ? In the midst of creation how does He maintain His sport ? (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदन्ते च कथं देवः स तिष्ठति महेश्वरः ।
कथं प्रसन्नतां याति शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ॥ १४॥

English
How does lord Maheśvara stand at the moment of dissolution ? How is Śaṅkara who blesses the world with happiness propitiated ? (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स प्रसन्नो महेशानः किं प्रयच्छति सत्फलम् ।
स्वभक्तेभ्यः परेभ्यश्च तत्सर्वं कथयस्व नः ॥ १५॥

English
What benefit does the great Lord confer when He is pleased with His own devotees and others ? Please tell us. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्यः प्रसन्नो भगवान्भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुम ।
भक्तप्रयासं स महान्न पश्यति दयापरः ॥ १६॥

English
We have heard that the lord becomes pleased instantaneously. The merciful lord is unable to bear the stress and strain that His devotee undergoes. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा विष्णुर्महेशश्च त्रयो देवाः शिवाङ्गजाः ।
महेशस्तत्र पूर्णांशः स्वयमेव शिवोऽपरः ॥ १७॥

English
The three deities Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa are born of Śiva. Among them Maheśa when he has all the substrata of elements is Śiva himself as distinct from Maheśa$^{175}$. (17.)

[fn 175. According to this statement Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Maheśa are the three forms of Śiva. In the Kūrma Purāṇā, (II. 37. 70-71) there occurs a slightly modified version : Agni (Tamas), Brahmā (Rajas) and Viṣṇu (Sattva) are the three forms of Rudra while another form, full and attributeless is Śiva himself.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्याविर्भावमाख्याहि चरितानि विशेषतः ।
उमाविर्भावमाख्याहि तद्विवाहं तथा प्रभो ॥ १८॥

English
Please explain His manifestation and tell us about His various activities. Please tell us about the birth of Umā and her marriage too, O lord. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्गार्हस्थ्यं विशेषेण तथा लीलाः परा अपि ।
एतत्सर्वं तदन्यच्च कथनीयं त्वयानघ ॥ १९॥

English
Their domestic life and their divine sports shall also be narrated to us. O sinless one, please tell us all about it and anything else that shall be told. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
इति पृष्टस्तदा तैस्तु सूतो हर्षसमन्वितः ।
स्मृत्वा शम्भुपदाम्भोजं प्रत्युवाच मुनीश्वरान् ॥ २०॥

English
*Vyāsa said :—*  Being thus requested Sūta was delighted. Remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva he replied to the sages. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सम्यक् पृष्टं भवद्भिश्च धन्या यूयं मुनीश्वराः ।
सदाशिवकथायां वो यज्जाता नैष्ठिकी मतिः ॥ २१॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O lordly sages, what you have asked for is very nice. You are all blessed inasmuch as your minds are drawn towards Sadāśiva’s anecdotes. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवकथाप्रश्नः पुरुषांस्त्रीन्पुनाति हि ।
वक्तारं पृच्छकं श्रोतॄञ्जाह्नवीसलिलं यथा ॥ २२॥

English
Like the holy waters of the Gaṅgā the inquiry into the anecdotes of Sadāśiva sanctifies the three persons : the narrator, the inquirer and the hearer. (22.)

---

English
O brahmins, except for the slayer of animals, who can be averse to hear the narrative of the attributes of Śiva, that highly delights three types of people always ? (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भोर्गुणानुवादात्को विरज्येत पुमान्द्विजाः ।
विना पशुघ्नं त्रिविधजनानन्दकरात्सदा । २३॥

गीयमानो वितृष्णैश्च भवरोगौषधोऽपि हि ।
मनःश्रोत्राभिरामश्च यतः सर्वार्थदः स वै ॥ २४॥

English
When it is being recited by persons who have no attachment or desire, it is verily an antidote for all ailments of worldly existence, for it is highly delightful to the ear and the heart while at the same time it bestows all objects. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कथयामि यथाबुद्धि भवत्प्रश्नानुसारतः ।
शिवलीलां प्रयत्नेन द्विजास्तां श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ २५॥

English
O brahmins, I shall explain Śiva’s sports in the light of your enquiry as far as my intelligence enables me to do so. Please listen respectfully. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भवद्भिः पृच्छ्यते यद्वत्तत्तथा नारदेन वै ।
पृष्टं पित्रे प्रेरितेन हरिणा शिवरूपिणा ॥ २६॥

English
Induced by lord Viṣṇu, a manifestation of Śiva, Nārada had also put the same question to his father Brahmā as you are asking me now. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा श्रुत्वा सुतवचः शिवभक्तः प्रसन्नधीः ।
जगौ शिवयशः प्रीत्या हर्षयन्मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ २७॥

English
On hearing the words of his son, Brahmā, a devotee of Śiva, was delighted in his mind. Out of love he sang the glory of Śiva heightening the pleasure of the excellent sage (Nārada). (27.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
सूतोक्तमिति तद्वाक्यमाकर्ण्य द्विजसत्तमाः ।
पप्रच्छुस्तत्सुसंवादं कुतूहलसमन्विताः ॥ २८॥

English
*Vyāsa said :—*  The learned brahmins, on hearing the words of Sūta became eager to know more of that conversation and so asked him. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग शैवोत्तम महामते ।
श्रुत्वा तव वचो रम्यं चेतो नः सकुतूहलम् ॥ २९॥

English
*The sages said:—*  O Sūta, O blessed Sūta, of great intellect and foremost among the devotees of Śiva, on hearing your most delightful words our minds have become very eager to know more. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कदा बभूव सुखकृद्विधिनारदयोर्महान् ।
संवादो यत्र गिरिशसुलीला भवमोचनी ॥ ३०॥

विधिनारदसंवादपूर्वकं शाङ्करं यशः ।
ब्रूहि नस्तात तत्प्रीत्या तत्तत्प्रश्नानुसारतः ॥ ३१॥

English
Dear one, please tell us lovingly when this highly pleasant conversation between Brahmā and Nārada took place, wherein Śiva's glory was sung and the divine sport of Lord Śiva, destructive of worldly existence, had been discussed. What were the questions and how were they answered, please explain. (30-31.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां मुनीनां भावितात्मनाम् ।
सूतः प्रोवाच सुप्रीतस्तत्संवादानुसारतः ॥ ३२॥

English
On hearing these words of the sages of noble mind Sūta was pleased much and narrated everything pertaining to the conversation referred to. (32.)
