### **CHAPTER TWO - *(Indra sends Kāmadeva to disturb the penance of Nārada)* - *Sūta said:—***

---

Sanskrit
२

सूत उवाच ।
एकस्मिन्समये विप्रा नारदो मुनिसत्तमः ।
ब्रह्मपुत्रो विनीतात्मा तपोऽर्थं मन आदधे ॥ १॥

English
O brahmins, once Nārada the excellent sage, son of Brahmā was inclined to perform penance controlling himself very much. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमशैलगुहा काचिदेका परमशोभना ।
यत्समीपे सुरनदी सदा वहति वेगतः ॥ २॥

English
There is a very beautiful cave in the Himālaya mountain near which the celestial river flows rapidly. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राश्रमो महादिव्यो नानाशोभासमन्वितः ।
तपोऽर्थं स ययौ तत्र नारदो दिव्यदर्शनः ॥ ३॥

English
There was a great hermitage of divine splendour which was resplendent in many ways. Nārada endowed with divine vision went there to perform the penance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा मुनिशार्दूलस्तेपे स सुचिरं तपः ।
बध्वासनं दृढं मौनी प्राणानायम्य शुद्धधीः ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing the hermitage (very convenient for penance) the leading sage performed the penance for a long time, seated firmly and steadily, keeping silent, controlling the breath and retaining the purity of the intellect. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रे मुनिः समाधिं तमहम्ब्रह्मेति यत्र ह ।
विज्ञानं भवति ब्रह्मसाक्षात्कारकरं द्विजाः ॥ ५॥

English
O brahmins, the sage performed meditation and contemplation wherein the realisation "I am Brahman" is generated leading to the direct perception of Brahman. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं तपति तस्मिन्वै नारदे मुनिसत्तमे ।
चकम्पेऽथ शुनासीरो मनस्सन्तापविह्वलः ॥ ६॥

English
When the great sage Nārada was thus performing penance, the mind of Indra became excessively agitated and he trembled. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसीति विचिन्त्यासौ मुनिर्मे राज्यमिच्छति ।
तद्विघ्नकरणार्थं हि हरिर्यत्नमियेष सः ॥ ७॥

English
Thinking “This sage is yearning for my kingdom” Indra wanted to spoil it. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सस्मार स स्मरं शक्रश्चेतसा देवनायकः ।
आजगाम द्रुतं कामः समधीर्महिषीयुतः ॥ ८॥

English
Indra, the leader of Devas, remembered Kāmadeva (Cupid) who arrived there immediately, accompanied by his Queen (Rati) and spring (his friend). (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथागतं स्मरं दृष्ट्वा सम्बोध्य सुरराट् प्रभुः ।
उवाच तं प्रपश्याशु स्वार्थे कुटिलशेमुषिः ॥ ९॥

English
The king of Devas, endowed with crooked intelligence to achieve his interests, saw that Kāma had arrived and addressed him thus. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्र उवाच ।
मित्रवर्य महावीर सर्वदा हितकारक ।
श‍ृणु प्रीत्या वचो मे त्वं कुरु साहाय्यमात्मना ॥ १०॥

English
*Indra said:—*  O friend, of great prowess, always doing what is beneficent to me, please hear lovingly what I am going to say. Render me your help. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वद्बलान्मे बहूनाञ्च तपोगर्वो विनाशितः ।
मद्राज्यस्थिरता मित्र त्वदनुग्रहतः सदा ॥ ११॥

English
Strongly supported by you I have destroyed the pride of many ascetics O friend, the stability of my kingdom is always due to your blessing. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमशैलगुहायां हि मुनिस्तपति नारदः ।
मनसोद्दिश्य विश्वेशं महासंयमवान्दृढः ॥ १२॥

English
Nārada, the sage, is performing a penance in the Himālaya mountain directing his mind towards the Lord of the universe with great mental control and firm resolve. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
याचेन्न विधितो राज्यं स ममेति विशङ्कितः ।
अद्यैव गच्छ तत्र त्वं तत्तपोविघ्नमाचर ॥ १३॥

English
I now fear lest he should beg of Brahmā my kingdom. You must go there now itself and hinder his penance. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याज्ञप्तो महेन्द्रेण स कामः समधुप्रियः ।
जगाम तत्स्थलं गर्वादुपायं स्वं चकार ह ॥ १४॥

English
Being thus commanded by Indra, Kāmadeva, accompanied by his wife (Rati) and Madhu, his friend, went haughtily to that place. He then prepared his own means of attack. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
रचयामास तत्राशु स्वकलाः सकला अपि ।
वसन्तोऽपि स्वप्रभावं चकार विविधं मदात् ॥ १५॥

English
He employed all his arts there immediately. Spring too haughtily spread his prowess of diverse nature. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
न बभूव मुनेश्चेतो विकृतं मुनिसत्तमाः ।
भ्रष्टो बभूव तद्गर्वो महेशानुग्रहेण ह ॥ १६॥

English
O great sages, the mind of the sage (Nārada) did not waver. Only the arrogance of these fellows suffered a setback and that too by the favour of Maheśa. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणुतादरतस्तत्र कारणं शौनकादयः ।
ईश्वरानुग्रहेणात्र न प्रभावः स्मरस्य हि ॥ १७॥

English
Please listen to the reason thereof, O Śaunaka and other sages! By the controlling power of the lord, Kāma could not exercise any influence. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रैव शम्भुनाकारि सुतपश्च स्मरारिणा ।
अत्रैव दग्धस्तेनाशु कामो मुनितपोपहः ॥ १८॥

English
It was in this very place that Śiva, the indefatigable enemy of Kāma, had formerly performed a great penance. It was here that Kāma was reduced to ashes—Kāma who used to spoil the penances of sages. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
कामजीवनहेतोर्हि रत्या सम्प्रार्थितैः सुरैः ।
सम्प्रार्थित उवाचेदं शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ॥ १९॥

English
Rati wanted the resuscitation of Kāma and requested the Devas. They appealed to lord Śiva, the benefactor of the whole world who said thus : (19.)

---

Sanskrit
कञ्चित्समयमासाद्य जीविष्यति सुराः स्मरः ।
परन्त्विह स्मरोपायश्चलिष्यति न कश्चन ॥ २०॥

English
"O Gods, after some time Kāma will come to life again. But none of his tricks will succeed here. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इह यावद्दृष्यते भूर्जनैः स्थित्वामराः सदा ।
कामबाणप्रभावोऽत्र न चलिष्यत्यसंशयम् ॥ २१॥

English
Whatever space all round this spot is visible to persons here, will be out of the influence of Kāma for ever, O Devas. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इति शम्भूक्तितः कामो मिथ्यात्मगतिकस्तदा ।
नारदे स जगामाशु दिवमिन्द्रसमीपतः ॥ २२॥

English
It was due to this statement of Śiva that Kāma's viles did not prevail upon Nārada. From Śiva's abode he went to Indra. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आचख्यौ सर्ववृत्तान्तं प्रभावं च मुनेः स्मरः ।
तदाज्ञया ययौ स्थानं स्वकीयं समधुप्रियः ॥ २३॥

English
Kāma then narrated everything about the sage and commended his power. At Indra's bidding Kāma returned to his own place. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
विस्मितोऽभूत्सुराधीशः प्रशशंसाथ नारदम् ।
तद्वृत्तान्तानभिज्ञो हि मोहितः शिवमायया ॥ २४॥

English
Deluded by Śiva's Māyā (power of illusion) Indra was unaware of the true facts and was greatly surprised and he admired Nārada. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्ज्ञेया शाम्भवी माया सर्वेषां प्राणिनामिह ।
भक्तं विनार्पितात्मानं तया सम्मोह्यते जगत् ॥ २५॥

English
Śiva's Māyā is incomprehensible to all. The whole universe is deluded by it. Only the true devotees of dedicated souls escape. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदोऽपि चिरं तस्थौ तत्रेशानुग्रहेण ह ।
पूर्णं मत्वा तपस्तत्स्वं विरराम ततो मुनिः ॥ २६॥

English
Backed by Śiva's blessings Nārada stayed in the hermitage for a long time. Then realising that his penance was complete, the sage concluded the same. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कामाज्जयं निजं मत्वा गर्वितोऽभून्मुनीश्वरः ।
वृथैव विगतज्ञानः शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ २७॥

English
Thinking that he had conquered Kāma he was puffed with pride. He was devoid of true knowledge and deluded by Śiva's Māyā. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या धन्या महामाया शाम्भवी मुनिसत्तमाः ।
तद्गतिं न हि पश्यन्ति विष्णुब्रह्मादयोऽपि हि ॥ २८॥

English
O great sages, blessed and very blessed is Śiva's Māyā. Even Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others do not know the the turn it takes. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तया सम्मोहितोऽतीव नारदो मुनिसत्तमः ।
कैलासं प्रययौ शीघ्रं स्ववृत्तं गदितुं मदी ॥ २९॥

English
In that state of delusion and puffed up arrogance, the great sage Nārada went to Kailāsa to expatiate on his own achievement. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रं नत्वाब्रवीत्सर्वं स्ववृत्तं गर्ववान्मुनिः ।
मत्त्वात्मानं महात्मानं स्वप्रभुञ्च स्मरञ्जयम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Bowing down to Rudra, the sage arrogantly spoke of his exploits with the conviction that he was equal to the noble-souled lord, the conqueror of Kāma, i.e., Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा शङ्करः प्राह नारदं भक्तवत्सलः ।
स्वमायामोहितं हेत्वनभिज्ञं भ्रष्टचेतसम् ॥ ३१॥

English
On hearing it, Śiva who is favourably disposed to His devotees, advised Nārada who was ignorant of the real cause, whose mind had strayed and who had been deluded by His (Śiva's) Māyā. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
हे तात नारद प्राज्ञ धन्यस्त्वं श‍ृणु मद्वचः ।
वाच्यमेवं न कुत्रापि हरेरग्रे विशेषतः ॥ ३२॥

English
*Rudra said :—*  "Dear Nārada, O wise sage, you are blessed. But please listen to me. Never speak like this anywhere else, especially in the presence of Viṣṇu. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पृच्छमानोऽपि न ब्रूयाः स्ववृत्तं मे यदुक्तवान् ।
गोप्यं गोप्यं सर्वथा हि नैव वाच्यं कदाचन ॥ ३३॥

English
Even when you are asked you should not mention your achievements as you have done just now. These should be guarded as close secrets and should never be expressed. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
शास्म्यहं त्वां विशेषेण मम प्रियतमो भवान् ।
विष्णुभक्तो यतस्त्वं हि तद्भक्तोऽतीव मेऽनुगः ॥ ३४॥

English
I bid you specifically like this because you are a great favourite of mine. Since you are a devotee of Viṣṇu you are my follower as all his devotees are.” (34.)

---

English
*Sūla said :—*  Lord Rudra, the cause of creation, advised him in many ways like this. But Nārada who was still under the influence of Śiva’s Māyā did not take up this wholesome advice. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
शास्ति स्मेत्थञ्च बहुशो रुद्रः सूतिकरः प्रभुः ।
नारदो न हितं मेने शिवमायाविमोहितः । ३५॥

प्रबला भाविनी कर्मगतिर्ज्ञेया विचक्षणैः ।
न निवार्या जनैः कैश्चिदपीच्छा सैव शाङ्करी ॥ ३६॥

English
The future course of actions shall be considered inevitable by sensible persons. The will of Śiva cannot be warded off by any one. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स मुनिवर्यो हि ब्रह्मलोकं जगाम ह ।
विधिं नत्वाब्रवीत्कामजयं स्वस्य तपोबलात् ॥ ३७॥

English
Then the great sage went to Brahmā’s world. After saluting Brahmā he told him about his conquest of Kāma as a result of his penance. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य विधिः सोऽथ स्मृत्वा शम्भुपदाम्बुजम् ।
विज्ञाय कारणं सर्वं निषिषेध सुतं तदा ॥ ३८॥

English
On hearing that, Brahmā remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and knew thereby the true cause. He then forbade his son. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
मेने हितं न विध्युक्तं नारदो ज्ञानिसत्तमः ।
शिवमायामोहितश्च रूढचित्तमदाङ्कुरः ॥ ३९॥

English
Although foremost among the wise, Nārada did not take up the advice of Brahmā as he had been deluded by Śiva’s Māyā. The sprout of arrogance had been so fixed in his mind. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेच्छा यादृशी लोके भवत्येव हि सा तदा ।
तदधीनं जगत्सर्वं वचस्तन्त्यां स्थितं यतः ॥ ४०॥

English
Everything will take place in the world in the manner Śiva wills. It is true that the entire universe is dependent on His will. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदोऽथ ययौ शीघ्रं विष्णुलोकं विनष्टधीः ।
मदाङ्कुरमना वृत्तं गदितुं स्वं तदग्रतः ॥ ४१॥

English
Nārada hastened to Viṣṇuloka in the same state of senseless arrogance, to boast of his exploits in the presence of Viṣṇu. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
आगच्छन्तं मुनिं दृष्ट्वा नारदं विष्णुरादरात् ।
उत्थित्वाग्रे गतोऽरं तं शिश्लेष ज्ञातहेतुकः ॥ ४२॥

English
When Viṣṇu saw Nārada approaching, he could guess the purpose of his visit. He stood up and received him cordially. He walked forward and embraced him lovingly. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वासने समुपावेश्य स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ।
हरिः प्राह वचस्तथ्यं नारदं मदनाशनम् ॥ ४३॥

English
He made Nārada sit comfortably. After remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva, He frankly uttered these words intended to quell the arrogance of Nārada. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
कुत आगम्यते तात किमर्थमिह चागतः ।
धन्यस्त्वं मुनिशार्दूल तीर्थोऽहं तु तवागमात् ॥ ४४॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “O dear Nārada, foremost among sages, you are blessed. I am sanctified by your visit. May I know where you come from and why you have come?” (44.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुवाक्यमिति श्रुत्वा नारदो गर्वितो मुनिः ।
स्ववृत्तं सर्वमाचष्ट समदं मदमोहितः ॥ ४५॥

English
On hearing these words of Viṣṇu, the sage Nārada felt elated. He narrated his story in the same haughty manner. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा मुनिवचो विष्णुः समदं कारणं ततः ।
ज्ञातवानखिलं स्मृत्वा शिवपादाम्बुजं हृदि ॥ ४६॥

English
On hearing the arrogant words of the sage, Viṣṇu remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva again and understood the true cause. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तुष्टाव गिरिशं भक्त्या शिवात्मा शैवराड्ढरिः ।
साञ्जलिर्विसुधीर्नम्रमस्तकः परमेश्वरम् ॥ ४७॥

English
Viṣṇu, a leading devotee of Śiva, with his soul dedicated to Śiva, bowed his head and eulogised Parameśvara, the lord of the holy mountain, with his palms joined in reverence. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
देव देव महादेव प्रसीद परमेश्वर ।
धन्यस्त्वं शिव धन्या ते माया सर्वविमोहिनी ॥ ४८॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “O Lord, O Lord Mahādeva, Parameśvara, be pleased. O Śiva thou art blessed. Thy Māyā enchants everyone.” (48.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यादि स स्तुतिं कृत्वा शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
निमील्य नयने ध्यात्वा विरराम पदाम्बुजम् ॥ ४९॥

English
Having thus chanted the prayer to Śiva, the supreme Ātman, he closed his eyes and meditated on His lotus-like feet and stopped. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्कर्तव्यं शङ्करस्य स ज्ञात्वा विश्वपालकः ।
शिवशासनतः प्राह हृदाथ मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ ५०॥

English
On coming to know what Śiva was about to do, through Śiva’s bidding, he addressed the great sage pleasantly. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
धन्यस्त्वं मुनिशार्दूल तपोनिधिरुदारधीः ।
भक्तित्रिकं न यस्यास्ति काममोहादयो मुने ॥ ५१॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O foremost among sages, you are blessed. You are the storehouse of austerities and large-hearted. O sage, lust and delusion rise only in the heart of that man who is devoid of the three types of devotion.$^{176}$ (51.)

---

Sanskrit
विकारास्तस्य सद्यो वै भवन्त्यखिलदुःखदाः ।
नैष्ठिको ब्रह्मचारी त्वं ज्ञानवैराग्यवान्सदा ॥ ५२॥

English
Base passions that bring in their wake all sorts of miseries crop up in him instantly. But you are vowed to perpetual celibacy. You are ever endowed with knowledge and devoted to non-attachment. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं कामविकारी स्याज्जन्मनाविकृतः सुधीः ।
इत्याद्युक्तं वचो भूरि श्रुत्वा स मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ५३॥

विजहास हृदा नत्वा प्रत्युवाच वचो हरिम् ।
नारद उवाच ।
किम्प्रभावः स्मरः स्वामिन्कृपा यद्यस्ति ते मयि ॥ ५४॥

इत्युक्त्वा हरिमानम्य ययौ यादृच्छिको मुनिः ॥ ५५॥

English
Unaffected by passion and highly intelligent by nature how can you be swayed by lust?" On hearing words like these, the great sage laughed within himself but spoke to Viṣṇu humbly. *Nārada said :—* "O lord, what can Kāma do to me if you remain favourable to me ?" Saying so, the sage who had paid a casual visit bowed to Viṣṇu and left. (53-55.)
