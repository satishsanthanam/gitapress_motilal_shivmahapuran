### **CHAPTER THREE - *(Nārada attends the Svayaṃvara of a virgin and is discomfited)***

---

Sanskrit
३

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग व्यासशिष्य नमोऽस्तु ते ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा तात वर्णिता कृपया हि नः ॥ १॥

मुनौ गते हरिस्तात किञ्चकार ततः परम् ।
नारदोऽपि गतः कुत्र तन्मे व्याख्यातुमर्हसि ॥ २॥

English
*The sages said :—*  Sūta, O blessed Sūta, the disciple of Vyāsa, our obeisance to thee. It is due to thy grace that this wonderful story has been narrated to us, O dear one. Now tell us in detail what Viṣṇu did after Nārada had left the place? And where did Nārada go? (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां सूतः पौराणिकोत्तमः ।
प्रत्युवाच शिवं स्मृत्वा नानासूतिकरं बुधः ॥ ३॥

English
*Vyāsa said :—*  On hearing these words of the sages, Sūta$^{177}$ the wise and excellent scholar of Purāṇas remembered Śiva, the cause of different kinds of creation and replied. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
मुनौ यदृच्छया विष्णुर्गते तस्मिन्हि नारदे ।
शिवेच्छया चकाराशु मायां मायाविशारदः ॥ ४॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  When Nārada went away casually Viṣṇu, skilful in wielding his Māyā, spread his Māyā, as Śiva had willed. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिमार्गस्य मध्ये तु विरेचे नगरं महत् ।
शतयोजनविस्तारमद्भुतं सुमनोहरम् ॥ ५॥

English
On the path taken by the sage He created a big wonderful city. It was a hundred Yojanas in extent and surprisingly beautiful. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वलोकादधिकं रम्यं नानावस्तुविराजितम् ।
नरनारीविहाराढ्यं चतुर्वर्णाकुलं परम् ॥ ६॥

English
It was far more beautiful than heaven. Many articles were displayed there. Men and women of all the four castes stayed there. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र राजा शीलनिधिर्नामैश्वर्यसमन्वितः ।
सुतास्वयम्वरोद्युक्तो महोत्सवसमन्वितः ॥ ७॥

English
The wealthy and prosperous king of that city named Śīlanidhi was preparing for the gorgeous celebration of the voluntary wooing (Svayamvara)$^{178}$ of his daughter. (7.)

[fn 178. This was an ancient custom amongst the kings of Kṣatriya caste to hold a public assembly of suitors for the selection of a husband for their daughters.]

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्दिग्भ्यः समायातैः संयुतं नृपनन्दनैः ।
नानावेषैः सुशोभैश्च तत्कन्यावरणोत्सुकैः ॥ ८॥

English
Brilliant princes coming from all the four quarters eager to court the princess had thronged there dressed in diverse ways. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एतादृशं पुरं दृष्ट्वा मोहं प्राप्तोऽथ नारदः ।
कौतुकी तन्नृपद्वारं जगाम मदनैधितः ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing such a splendid city Nārada$^{179}$ was enchanted. With his love kindled, he eagerly went to the palace threshold. (9.)

[fn 179. Nārada is one of the ten mind-born sons of Brahmā having sprung from his thigh. He is celebrated as a divine sage and is associated with another sage Parvata. He is represented as the messenger from the Gods to men and vice versa and as being very fond of promoting discords among Gods and men; hence he is called Kalipriya.]
---

Sanskrit

उपवेश्यार्चयाञ्चक्रे रत्नसिंहासने वरे ॥ १०॥

English
When the sage reached the palace the king Śīlanidhi adored him, having offered him a seat on the splendid throne studded with precious gems. (10.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He called his daughter Śrīmati and asked her to kneel down at the feet of Nārada. (11.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Being struck with wonder on seeing the girl, Nārada said—"O king, who is this lovely girl comparable to celestial damsels?" (12.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On hearing the words of the sage, the king replied with his palms joined in reverence—"O sage, this is my daughter Śrīmati. (13.)

---

Sanskrit

English
She has attained the marriageable age. She is in search of a qualified bridegroom. She has all charms and accomplishments and her Svayamvara is imminent. (14.)

---

Sanskrit

कीदृशं तनयेयं मे वरमाप्स्यति तद्वद ॥ १५॥

English
O sage, kindly foretell her destiny, everything that is in her horoscope. Please tell me what sort of a husband she will get." (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तो मुनिशार्दूलस्तामिच्छुः कामविह्वलः ।
समाभाष्य स राजानं नारदो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ १६॥

English
By the time these words were spoken Nārada had become an agitated victim of love and desired her. Addressing the king, he said thus :— (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतेयं तव भूपाल सर्वलक्षणलक्षिता ।
महाभाग्यवती धन्या लक्ष्मीरिव गुणालया ॥ १७॥

English
"O great king, this daughter of yours is endowed with all characteristics : She is highly fortunate and blessed like Lakṣmī. She is an abode of all qualities. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेश्वरोऽजितो वीरो गिरीशसदृशो विभुः ।
अस्याः पतिर्ध्रुवं भावी कामजित्सुरसत्तमः ॥ १८॥

English
Her future husband will certainly be a splendid God, lord of all, unvanquished, heroic, on a par with Śiva, and Vying with Kāmadeva”. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा नृपमामन्त्र्य ययौ यादृच्छिको मुनिः ।
बभूव कामविवशः शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ १९॥

English
Having said this, the casual visitor Nārada took leave of the king. Deluded by Śivā’s Māyā he was extremely oppressed by love. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

स्वयंवरे नृपालानामेकं मां वृणुयात्कथम् ॥ २०॥

English
The sage began to muse—“How shall I get her ? How shall she woo me amongst the princes in the Svayamvara hall. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English
A comely appearance appeals to all women in every respect. Only by seeing a charming personality will she become enamoured. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Thinking thus, Nārada who was agitated by love, went to Viṣṇuloka somehow to acquire Viṣṇu’s form to captivate her. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He saluted Viṣṇu and said—“I shall tell you secretly my affairs entirely.” (23.)

English
When Viṣṇu who did everything according to Śiva’s wish agreed and asked him to narrate, the sage said :— (24.)

Sanskrit

English

English

विप्ररूपधरौ रुद्रगणौ ज्ञानविशारदौ ॥ ५३॥

English
Immediately the two attendants of Rudra, of perfect wisdom, disguised as brahmins spoke to Nārada. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
गणावूचतुः
हे नारद मुने त्वं हि वृथा मदनमोहितः ।
तल्लिप्सुः स्वमुखं पश्य वानरस्येव गर्हितम् ॥ ५४॥

English
*The attendants said :—*  O sage Nārada, being deluded by love, you are desirous of getting her. Your effort is in vain. See, your face is as despicable as that of a monkey. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य तयोर्वाक्यं नारदो विस्मितोऽभवत् ।
मुखं ददर्श मुकुरे शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ ५५॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  Oh hearing their words Nārada was surprised. Deluded by Śiva's māyā he looked into a mirror. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वमुखं वानरस्येव दृष्ट्वा चुक्रोध सत्वरम् ।
शापं ददौ तयोस्तत्र गणयोर्मोहितो मुनिः ॥ ५६॥

English
On seeing his face like that of a monkey he became infuriated. The deluded sage cursed the two attendants. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
युवां ममोपहासं वै चक्रतुर्ब्राह्मणस्य हि ।
भवेतां राक्षसौ विप्रवीर्यजौ वै तदाकृती ॥ ५७॥

English
Since you had mocked at me, you will become demons born of brahminical semen and of that form. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा हरगणावित्थं स्वशापं ज्ञानिसत्तमौ ।
न किञ्चिदूचतुस्तौ हि मुनिमाज्ञाय मोहितम् ॥ ५८॥

स्वस्थानं जग्मतुर्विप्रा उदासीनौ शिवस्तुतिम् ।
चक्रतुर्मन्यमानौ वै शिवेच्छां सकलां सदा ॥ ५९॥

English
On hearing the curse, the two attendants of perfect wisdom remained silent because they knew that the sage was deluded. O brahmins, they returned to their abode and sitting there quietly went on eulogising Śiva. They considered everything as Śiva's will. (58-59.)
