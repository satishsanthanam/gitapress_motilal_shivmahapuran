### **CHAPTER FOUR - *Nārada goes to Vaikuṇṭha and curses Viṣṇu there)***

---

Sanskrit
४

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाप्राज्ञ वर्णिता ह्यद्भुता कथा ।
धन्या तु शाम्भवी माया तदधीनं चराचरम् ॥ १॥

गतयोर्गणयोः शम्भोः स्वयमात्मेच्छया विभोः ।
किञ्चकार मुनिः क्रुद्धो नारदः स्मरविह्वलः ॥ २॥

English
*The sages said :—*  Sūta, O Sūta of great intellect, a wonderful tale has been narrated by you. Blessed indeed is the Māyā of Śiva. All mobile and immobile things depend upon it. When the two attendants of lord Rudra had left at their own will what did the infuriated Nārada, the sage disquieted by Kāma-deva, do ? (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
विमोहितो मुनिर्दत्त्वा तयोः शापं यथोचितम् ।
जले मुखं निरीक्ष्याथ स्वरूपं गिरिशेच्छया ॥ ३॥

शिवेच्छया न प्रबुद्धः स्मृत्वा हरिकृतच्छलम् ।
क्रोधं दुर्विषहं कृत्वा विष्णुलोकं जगाम ह ॥ ४॥

उवाच वचनं क्रुद्धः समिद्ध इव पावकः ।
दुरुक्तिगर्भितं व्यङ्गं नष्टज्ञानः शिवेच्छया ॥ ५॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  After cursing the two attendants of Śiva suitably, the sage still under the earlier delusion looked into the water and saw that his face was quite normal. It was also due to Śiva's will. He did not wake from the delusion still again due to Śiva's will. Thereupon recollecting that it might have been a deception of Hari, he became unbearably infuriated and went to Viṣṇuloka. There he angrily poured abusive words blazing like kindled fire since his wisdom had vanished due to Śiva's will. (3-5.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
हे हरे त्वं महादुष्टः कपटी विश्वमोहनः ।
परोत्साहं न सहसे मायावी मलिनाशयः ॥ ६॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Viṣṇu, you are extremely wicked, deceptive enchanter of the world. You are unable to brook others' enthusiastic success. You dabble in illusory tactics and your intentions are always dirty. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मोहिनीरूपमादाय कपटं कृतवान्पुरा ।
असुरेभ्योऽपाययस्त्वं वारुणीममृतं न हि ॥ ७॥

English
Formerly you assumed the form of an enchantress$^{180}$ and showed your deceptive power. You made the demons drink liquor and not the nectar. (7.)

[fn 180. It refers to the form assumed by Viṣṇu at the time of cheating the demons of nectar.]

---

Sanskrit
चेत्पिबेन्न विषं रुद्रो दयां कृत्वा महेश्वरः ।
भवेन्नष्टाखिला माया तव व्याजरते हरे ॥ ८॥

English
If out of pity Śiva had not drunk poison$^{181}$, O Viṣṇu, all your illusory tactics would have been quelled since you take pleasure only in deception. (8.)

[fn 181. It refers to Śiva's swallowing the poison produced at the churning of the ocean.]
---

Sanskrit
गतिः सकपटा तेऽतिप्रिया विष्णो विशेषतः ।
साधुस्वभावो न भवान्स्वतन्त्रः प्रभुणा कृतः ॥ ९॥

English
O Viṣṇu, a deceptive path is extremely attractive to you. You had never been of saintly nature, but the lord made you free from control. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं समुचितं नैव शिवेन परमात्मना ।
तत्प्रभावबलं ध्यात्वा स्वतन्त्रकृतिकारकः ॥ १०॥

त्वद्गतिं सुसमाज्ञाय पश्चात्तापमवाप सः ।
विप्रं सर्वोपरि प्राह स्वोक्तवेदप्रमाणकृत् ॥ ११॥

English
What is done by Śiva the supreme Ātman does not seem proper. Thinking of your influence and strength when you act independently and seeing the way you go He has now repented. He has announced that a brahmin is superior to all, thereby making the Vedas pronounced by Him authoritative. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
तज्ज्ञात्वाहं हरे त्वाद्य शिक्षयिष्यामि तद्बलात् ।
यथा न कुर्याः कुत्रापीदृशं कर्म कदाचन ॥ १२॥

English
O Viṣṇu, knowing that, I shall now teach you through that power so that hereafter you will never do such things. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि निर्भयस्त्वं हि सङ्गं नापस्तरस्विना ।
इदानीं लप्स्यसे विष्णो फलं स्वकृतकर्मणः ॥ १३॥

English
You are fearless because till now you have not come into clash with an equally powerful person. Now you will derive, O Viṣṇu, the fruit of your own "deeds". (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थमुक्त्वा हरिं सोऽथ मुनिर्मायाविमोहितः ।
शशाप क्रोधनिर्विण्णो ब्रह्मतेजः प्रदर्शयन् ॥ १४॥

English
After saying this, the sage still under the influence of Māyā furiously cursed Viṣṇu, thereby exhibiting the superiority of his brahminical power. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीकृते व्याकुलं विष्णो मामकार्षीर्विमोहकः ।
अन्वकार्षीः स्वरूपेण येन कापट्यकार्यकृत् ॥ १५॥

तद्रूपेण मनुष्यस्त्वं भव तद्दुःखभुग्घरे ।
यन्मुखं कृतवान्मे त्वं ते भवन्तु सहायिनः ॥ १६॥

English
O Viṣṇu, the enchanter that you are, you made me distressed for the sake of woman. O Hari, you shall experience misery in that human form which you imitated while proceeding with your deceptive tactics. Your allies will be those whose face you assigned to me. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं स्त्रीवियोगजं दुःखं लभस्व परदुःखदः ।
मनुष्यगतिकः प्रायो भवाज्ञानविमोहितः ॥ १७॥

English
O inflictor of miseries upon others, you shall get the misery of separation from a woman. You shall have the travails of a human being deluded by ignorance." (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इति शप्त्वा हरिं मोहान्नारदोऽज्ञानमोहितः ।
विष्णुर्जग्राह तं शापं प्रशंसन् शाम्भवीमजाम् ॥ १८॥

English
Thus Nārada, deluded himself by ignorance, cursed Hari. Viṣṇu quietly accepted the cause praising the Māyā of Śambhu. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुर्महालीलो निश्चकर्ष विमोहिनीम ।
स्वमायां मोहितो ज्ञानी नारदोऽप्यभवद्यया ॥ १९॥

English
Thereafter Śiva, of great divine sport withdrew his enchanting Māyā whereby Nārada became wise (as before) and free from delusion. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्हितायां मायायां पूर्ववन्मतिमानभूत् ।
नारदो विस्मितमनाः प्राप्तबोधो निराकुलः ॥ २०॥

पश्चात्तापमवाप्याति निनिन्द स्वं मुहुर्मुहुः ।
प्रशशंस तदा मायां शाम्भवीं ज्ञानिमोहिनीम् ॥ २१॥

English
When the Māyā vanished he became as intelligent as before regaining perfect knowledge and becoming free from distress. He was surprised (at his own action in the meantime). He cursed himself after repenting again and again. He praised the Māyā of Śiva which could enchant even wise people. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ज्ञात्वा मुनिःसर्वं मायाविभ्रममात्मनः ।
अपतत्पादयोर्विष्णोर्नारदो वैष्णवोत्तमः ॥ २२॥

English
On realising his mistakes due to illusion, Nārada, the most excellent of the devotees of Viṣṇu, fell at his feet. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
हर्युपस्थापितः प्राह वचनं नष्टदुर्मतिः ।
मया दुरुक्तयः प्रोक्ता मोहितेन कुबुद्धिना ॥ २३॥

English
Consoled by Hari and freed from wicked ideas he said—“Being deluded and evil-minded I have spoken many wicked words to you. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
दत्तः शापोऽपि ते नाथ वितथं कुरु तं प्रभो ।
महत्पापमकार्षं हि यास्यामि निरयं ध्रुवम् ॥ २४॥

English
O lord, I heaped curses on you. O master, please make them ineffective. I have committed a great sin. Certainly I will be falling into a hell. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कमुपायं हरे कुर्यां दासोऽहं ते तमादिश ।
येन पापकुलं नश्येन्निरयो न भवेन्मम ॥ २५॥

English
O Hari, I am your slave. Please direct me what to do whereby I may destroy my sins and prevent my downfall into hell.” (25.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा स पुनर्विष्णोः पादयोर्मुनिसत्तमः ।
पपात सुमतिर्भक्त्या पश्चात्तापमुपागतः ॥ २६॥

English
Saying thus, the excellent sage once again fell at Viṣṇu’s feet and with the mind purified repented sincerely. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुस्तमुत्थाप्य बभाषे सूनृतं वचः ।
विष्णुरुवाच ।
न खेदं कुरु मे भक्तवरस्त्वं नात्र संशयः ॥ २७॥

English
Thereupon Viṣṇu lifted him up and spoke affably and courteously. *Viṣṇu said :—* “Do not be sorry too much. Undoubtedly you are my true devotee. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु तात प्रवक्ष्यामि सुहितं तव निश्चयात् ।
निरयस्ते न भविता शिवः शं ते विधास्यति ॥ २८॥

English
Dear sage, now listen. I shall tell you what is certainly beneficial to you. You will not fall into hell. Śiva will make you happy. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यदकार्षीः शिववचो वितथं मदमोहितः ।
स दत्तवानीदृशं ते फलं कर्मफलप्रदः ॥ २९॥

English
Deluded by your haughtiness you disobeyed the instructions of Śiva. The true bestower of fruits according to the actions, He has given you this result. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेच्छयाखिलं जातं कुर्वित्थं निश्चितां मतिम् ।
गर्वापहर्ता स स्वामी शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ॥ ३०॥

English
Be sure in your mind that everything has happened in accordance with Śiva’s wish. That lord Śiva, the supreme lord, removes haughtiness. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
परं ब्रह्म परात्मा स सच्चिदानन्दबोधनः ।
निर्गुणो निर्विकारी च रजःसत्त्वतमःपरः ॥ ३१॥

English
He is the supreme Brahman; the supreme Ātman, Existence, Knowledge and Bliss. He is free from the three Guṇas, changes and deviations. He is beyond Rajas, Sattva and Tamas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
स एवादाय मायां स्वां त्रिधा भवति रूपतः ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशात्मा निर्गुणोऽनिर्गुणोऽपि सः ॥ ३२॥

English
He is both Saguṇa and Nirguṇa (with and without attributes). He Himself availing of his own Māyā manifests into three forms—Brahmā, Viṣṇu, and Maheśa. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गुणत्वे शिवाह्वो हि परमात्मा महेश्वरः ।
परं ब्रह्माव्ययोऽनन्तो महादेवेति गीयते ॥ ३३॥

English
In his attributeless pure form He is glorified as Śiva, the supreme Ātman, Maheśvara, the supreme Brahman, the undecaying, the endless, and Mahādeva. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सेवया विधिः स्रष्टा पालको जगतामहम् ।
स्वयं सर्वस्य संहारी रुद्ररूपेण सर्वदा ॥ ३४॥

English
Serving him, Brahmā becomes the creator and I the sustainer of the worlds. He himself in the manifestation as Rudra is the annihilator always. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
साक्षी शिवस्वरूपेण मायाभिन्नः स निर्गुणः ।
स्वेच्छाचारी संविहारी भक्तानुग्रहकारकः ॥ ३५॥

English
Different from Māyā, the pure Being in the form of Śiva is the Sākṣin (cosmic witness) and moving about according to His Will and indulging in divine sport He blesses his devotees. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु त्वं नारद मुने सदुपायं सुखप्रदम् ।
सर्वपापापहर्तारं भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं सदा ॥ ३६॥

English
O sage Nārada, please listen to a good remedy that bestows happiness, removes all sins and yields worldly pleasures and salvation. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
त्यक्त्वा स्वसंशयं सर्वं गायन् शङ्करसद्यशः ।
शतनामशिवस्तोत्रं सदानन्यमतिर्जप ॥ ३७॥

English
Cast off all your doubts. Sing the songs of noble glory of Śiva. With your mind not turning to anything else, always repeat the hundred names of Śiva and his hymns. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्जपित्वा द्रुतं सर्वं तव पापं विनश्यति ।
इत्युक्त्वा नारदं विष्णुः पुनः प्राह दयान्वितः ॥ ३८॥

English
By his Japa all of your sins will perish instantaneously. After saying this to Nārada, Viṣṇu continued mercifully. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
मुने न कुरु शोकं त्वं त्वया किञ्चित्कृतं न हि ।
स्वेच्छया कृतवान्शम्भुरिदं सर्वं न संशयः ॥ ३९॥

English
"O sage, do not be grief-striken. Nothing has been perpetrated by you. It was Śiva who did everything. There is no doubt in this. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अहार्षित्त्वन्मतिं दिव्यां कामक्लेशमदात्स ते ।
त्वन्मुखाद्दापयाञ्चक्रे शापं मे स महेश्वरः ॥ ४०॥

English
It was lord Maheśvara who deluded your splendid intellect and made you suffer on account of love. It was he who made you His mouthpiece and cursed me. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं स्वचरितं लोके प्रकटीकृतवान् स्वयम् ।
मृत्युञ्जयः कालकालो भक्तोद्धारपरायणः ॥ ४१॥

English
In this manner the great Conqueror of Death, Kāla of Kāla, always devoted to the uplift of his devotees, made His own conduct of life manifest in the world. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

सर्वशक्तिप्रदो मेऽस्ति स एव परमेश्वरः ॥ ४२॥

English
There is no other lord and master so loving and pleasure-inspiring unto me as Śiva. The same Parameśvara bestows all power on me. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

तद्यशः श‍ृणु गाय त्वं कुरु नित्यं तदर्चनम् ॥ ४३॥

English
O sage, perform His adoration. Worship him always. Hear and sing his glory. Perpetually pay Him homage. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He who approaches Śiva by means of his body, mind and speech is a great scholar. He is called a living liberated soul. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The name Śiva blazing like the forest conflagration reduces mountainous heaps of great sins to ashes without any difficulty. True, it is undoubtedly true. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The different kinds of miseries arising from sins shall be destroyed only through the worship of Śiva, and not through other means. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---
