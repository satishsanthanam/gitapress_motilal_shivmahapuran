### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(Nārada goes to Kāśī)***

---

Sanskrit
५

सूत उवाच ।
अन्तर्हिते हरौ विप्रा नारदो मुनिसत्तमः ।
विचचार महीं पश्यन् शिवलिङ्गानि भक्तितः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O brahmins, when Viṣṇu vanished, the excellent sage Nārada roamed over the Earth seeing Śiva Liṅgas (in the various holy centres) with piety. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्या अटनं कृत्वा शिवरूपाण्यनेकशः ।
ददर्श प्रीतितो विप्रा भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदानि सः ॥ २॥

English
In the course of his wanderings over the Earth, O brahmins, with his mind full of devotional pleasure he saw many forms of Śiva that confer worldly pleasures and salvation on the devotees. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तं विचरन्तं कौ नारदं दिव्यदर्शनम् ।
ज्ञात्वा शम्भुगणौ तौ तु सुचित्तमुपजग्मतुः ॥ ३॥

English
On knowing that Nārada of divine vision was wandering over the Earth, the two attendants of Śiva approached him who by that time had become pure in mind. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरसा सुप्रणम्याशु गणावूचतुरादरात् ।
गृहीत्वा चरणौ तस्य शापोद्धारेच्छया च तौ ॥ ४॥

English
They bowed to him and touched his feet. With a desire to secure release from the curse they spoke to him respectfully. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणावूचतुः
ब्रह्मपुत्र सुरर्षे हि श‍ृणु प्रीत्यावयोर्वचः ।
तवापराधकर्तारावावां विप्रौ न वस्तुतः ॥ ५॥

English
*The attendants of Śiva said :—*  O celestial sage, son of Brahmā, please hear our words. We who formerly offended you are really not brahmins. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आवां हरगणौ विप्र तवागस्कारिणौ मुने ।
स्वयम्वरे राजपुत्र्या मायामोहितचेतसा ॥ ६॥

त्वया दत्तश्च नौ शापः परेशप्रेरितेन ह ।
ज्ञात्वा कुसमयं तत्र मौनमेव हि जीवनम् ॥ ७॥

English
O brahminical sage, we, your former offenders, are the attendants of Śiva. Induced by Śiva you had cursed us when your mind was deluded by the illusory infatuation for the princess at the Svayamvara. Realising that the occasion was inopportune we kept quiet then. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वकर्मणः फलं प्राप्तं कस्यापि न हि दूषणम् ।
सुप्रसन्नो भव विभो कुर्वनुग्रहमद्य नौ ॥ ८॥

English
We reaped the fruit of our own action. No one is to be blamed for it. O lord, be pleased. Bless us now. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
वच आकर्ण्य गणयोरिति भक्त्युक्तमादरात् ।
प्रत्युवाच मुनिः प्रीत्या पश्चात्तापमवाप्य सः ॥ ९॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing the words of the attendants uttered with devotion and respect, the sage replied lovingly, repenting (for his previous fury). (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
श‍ृणुतं मे महादेवगणौ मान्यतमौ सताम् ।
वचनं सुखदं मोहनिर्मुक्तं च यथार्थकम् ॥ १०॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O attendants of Lord Śiva, most worthy of the respect of good people, please listen to my words now free from delusion. They are true and shall make you happy. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा मम मतिर्भ्रष्टासीच्छिवेच्छावशाद् ध्रुवम् ।
सर्वथा मोहमापन्नः शप्तवान्वां कुशेमुषिः ॥ ११॥

English
Formerly my mind had been depraved. Certainly it was Śiva’s will. In that state of delusion and crookedness of the mind I had unfortunately cursed both of you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यदुक्तं तत्तथा भावि तथापि श‍ृणुतां गणौ ।
शापोद्धारमहं वच्मि क्षमेथामघमद्य मे ॥ १२॥

English
What I have said is bound to happen. Still, O Gaṇas (attendants) listen. I shall tell you the way of redemption from the curse. Please forgive my sin now. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वीर्यान्मुनिवरस्याप्त्वा राक्षसेशत्वमादिशम् ।
स्यातां विभवसंयुक्तौ बलिनौ सुप्रतापिनौ ॥ १३॥

सर्वब्रह्माण्डराजानौ शिवभक्तौ जितेन्द्रियौ ।
शिवापरतनोर्मृत्युं प्राप्य स्वं पदमाप्स्यथः ॥ १४॥

English
You will be born as demons from the semen virile of a great sage and due to his power you will secure the commanding position of the king of demons endowed with prosperity, strength and valorous exploits. You will rule over whole of the universe as devotees of Śiva with your sense conquered. You will gain your former position after courting death at the hands of a manifestation of Śiva. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य मुनेर्वाक्यं नारदस्य महात्मनः ।
उभौ हरगणौ प्रीतौ स्वं पदं जग्मतुर्मुदा ॥ १५॥

English
*Śūta said :—*  On hearing these words of the noble-souled Nārada, the two attendants of Śiva became delighted and went back to their abode joyfully. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदोऽपि परं प्रीतो ध्यायन् शिवमनन्यधीः ।
विचचार महीं पश्यन् शिवतीर्थान्यभीक्ष्णशः ॥ १६॥

English
Nārada too was delighted. Meditating exclusively on Śiva he continued his wanderings over the Earth seeing the various holy centres of Śiva personally. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
काशीं प्राप्याथ स मुनिः सर्वोपरिविराजिताम् ।
शिवप्रियां शम्भुसुखप्रदां शम्भुस्वरूपिणीम् ॥ १७॥

दृष्ट्वा काशीं कृतार्थोऽभूत्काशीनाथं ददर्श ह ।
आनर्च परमप्रीत्या परमानन्दसंयुतः ॥ १८॥

English
Reaching Kāśī that excelled all other cities in holiness, which is a favourite resort of Śiva, which easily bestows the favour of Śiva and which is identical with Śiva, the sage became contented. He saw Śiva, the lord of Kāśī and worshipped Him with very great pleasure and love. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
समुदः सेव्य तां काशीं कृतार्थो मुनिसत्तमः ।
नमन्संवर्णयन्भक्त्या संस्मरन्प्रेमविह्वलः ॥ १९॥

English
While staying at Kāśī, the excellent sage became contented; he bowed to the lord, described his glory piously, and remembered him with the flutter of love. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मलोकं जगामाथ शिवस्मरणसन्मतिः ।
शिवतत्त्वं विशेषेण ज्ञातुमिच्छुः स नारदः ॥ २०॥

English
Nārada then went to the region of Brahmā, his mind being highly purified by remembering Śiva. He was eager to know further the principles of Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नत्वा तत्र विधिं भक्त्या स्तुत्वा च विविधैस्तवैः ।
पप्रच्छ शिवसत्तत्त्वं शिवसंयुक्तमानसः ॥ २१॥

English
There he bowed to Brahmā with devotion and culogised him with various prayers. With his mind riveted to Śiva he asked him the good principles of Śiva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन्ब्रह्मस्वरूपज्ञ पितामह जगत्प्रभो ।
त्वत्प्रसादान्मया सर्वं विष्णोर्माहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ॥ २२॥

भक्तिमार्गं ज्ञानमार्गं तपोमार्गं सुदुस्तरम् ।
दानमार्गं च तीर्थानां मार्गं च श्रुतवानहम् ॥ २३॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, knower of the form of Brahman, O Pitāmaha, the lord of the universe, by your grace I have heard the greatness of Viṣṇu entirely and also the path of devotion, of knowledge, of austere penance, of charitable gifts and of holy centres. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
न ज्ञातं शिवतत्त्वं च पूजाविधिमतः क्रमात् ।
चरित्रं विविधं तस्य निवेदय मम प्रभो ॥ २४॥

English
But I have not understood the principle of Śiva. Hence, O lord, please explain the rules of His worship and also the various activities of the lord. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गुणोऽपि शिवस्तात सगुणः शङ्करः कथम् ।
शिवतत्त्वं न जानामि मोहितः शिवमायया ॥ २५॥

English
O dear sage, how can Śiva who is free form attributes become full of attributes? Since I am deluded by Śiva's Māyā, I do not know the principle of Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टेः पूर्वं कथं शम्भुः स्वरूपेण प्रतिष्ठितः ।
सृष्टिमध्ये स हि कथं क्रीडन्संवर्तते प्रभुः ॥ २६॥

English
How did Śiva remain in His pure form before Creation? In the middle of creation how does He sport about? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तदन्ते च कथं देवः स तिष्ठति महेश्वरः ।
कथं प्रसन्नतां याति शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ॥ २७॥

English
At the time of dissolution how does He remain? How is He, the benefactor of the world, propitiated? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तुष्टश्च स्वभक्तेभ्यः परेभ्यश्च महेश्वरः ।
किं फलं यच्छति विधे तत्सर्वं कथयस्व मे ॥ २८॥

English
O Brahmā, when propitiated what benefit does He bestow on His devotees and on others? Please satisfy me on all these enquiries (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्यः प्रसन्नो भगवान्भवतीत्यनुसंश्रुतम् ।
भक्तप्रयासं स महान्न पश्यति दयापरः ॥ २९॥

English
I have heard that the lord becomes delighted immediately. The merciful Great God cannot bear the stress and strain of His devotees.$^{185}$ (29.)

[fn 185. Verses 29-32 on this page are the same as verses 16-19 in RSI. Ch. 1.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा विष्णुर्महेशश्च त्रयो देवाः शिवांशजाः ।
महेशस्तत्र पूर्णांशः स्वयमेव शिवः परः ॥ ३०॥

English
The three deities Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa are born as parts of Śiva. Maheśa, having all the parts of Śiva, is Śiva Himself. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याविर्भावमाख्याहि चरितानि विशेषतः ।
उमाविर्भावमाख्याहि तद्विवाहं तथा विभो ॥ ३१॥

English
Please tell me all about His manifestation and especially His exploits. O lord, please narrate the manifestation of Umā and her marriage. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्गार्हस्थ्यं विशेषेण तथा लीलाः परा अपि ।
एतत्सर्वं तथान्यच्च कथनीयं त्वयानघ ॥ ३२॥

English
Their domestic life, especially their great divine sports and other things which are worthy of mention should be narrated to me, O sinless one. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
तदुत्पत्तिं विवाहं च शिवायास्तु विशेषतः ।
प्रब्रूहि मे प्रजानाथ गुहजन्म तथैव च ॥ ३३॥

English
Pārvati’s birth and her marriage as well as Guha’s birth shall be narrated in detail, O lord of people. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुभ्यश्च श्रुतं पूर्वं न तृप्तोऽस्मि जगत्प्रभो ।
अतस्त्वां शरणं प्राप्तः कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ ३४॥

English
O lord of universe, this I have heard from many, before, but I am not satisfied. Hence I have sought refuge in you. Please have mercy on me. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य नारदस्याङ्गजस्य हि ।
उवाच वचनं तत्र ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ॥ ३५॥

English
On hearing these words of Nārada his own son, Brahmā, the grandfather of the world, said this. (35.)
