### **CHAPTER SEVEN - (The dispute between Brahmā and Viṣṇu)**

---

Sanskrit
७

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
सुप्ते नारायणे देवे नाभौ पङ्कजमुत्तमम् ।
आविर्बभूव सहसा बृहद्वै शङ्करेच्छया ॥ १॥

English
### Brahmā said:-  When lord Nārāyaṇa continued to sleep, an excellent lotus of huge size came out of his navel as desired by Śiva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्तयष्टिकायुक्तं कर्णिकारसमप्रभम् ।
अनन्तयोजनायाममनन्तोच्छ्रायसंयुतम् ॥ २॥

English
It was many Yojanas wide and high. It had an endless stalk. The pericarp was of a brilliant hue. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिसूर्यप्रतीकाशं सुन्दरं तत्त्वसंयुतम् ।
अत्यद्भुतं महारम्यं दर्शनीयमनुत्तमम् ॥ ३॥

English
It was very beautiful with the brilliance of ten million suns. It was wonderful, excellent and worthy of vision containing Tattvas. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा यत्नं पूर्ववत्स शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ।
दक्षिणाङ्गान्निजान्मां वै साम्बः शम्भुरजीजनत् ॥ ४॥

English
Exerting himself as before, Śiva, the great lord, with Pārvatī as his better half created me from His right limb. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स मायामोहितं कृत्वा मां महेशो द्रुतं मुने ।
तन्नाभिपङ्कजादाविर्भावयामास लीलया ॥ ५॥

English
O sage, having deluded me with His illusion immediately, Śiva in the course of His sport, produced me through the umbilical lotus of Viṣṇu. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पद्मात्ततो जज्ञे पुत्रोऽहं हेमगर्भकः ।
चतुर्मुखो रक्तवर्णस्त्रिपुण्ड्राङ्कितमस्तकः ॥ ६॥

English
Thus it was that I came to be known as Lotus-born and conceived in a golden womb. I had four faces, red complexion and Tripundra-marked forehead. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मायामोहितश्चाहं नाविदं कमलं विना ।
स्वदेहजनकं तात पितरं ज्ञानदुर्बलः ॥ ७॥

English
Deluded by His illusion and weakened in knowledge, O dear one, I did not know who the progenitor of my body was, other than the lotus. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
कोऽहं वा कुत आयातः किं कार्यं तु मदीयकम् ।
कस्य पुत्रोऽहमुत्पन्नः केनैव निर्मितोऽधुना ॥ ८॥

English
"Who am I ? Whence did I come ? What is my duty ? To whom was I born a son ? By whom have I been created ?" (8.)

---

Sanskrit
इति संशयमापन्नं बुद्धिर्मां समपद्यत ।
किमर्थं मोहमायामि तज्ज्ञानं सुकरं खलु ॥ ९॥

एतत्कमलपुष्पस्य पत्रारोहस्थलं ह्यधः ।
मत्कर्ता च स वै तत्र भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ १०॥

इति बुद्धिं समास्थाय कमलादवरोहयम् ।
नाले नाले गतस्तत्र वर्षाणां शतकं मुने ॥ ११॥

English
My intellect became confused with these doubts. Then I thought "Why shall I be under delusion ? It is easy to gain that knowledge. The place of growth of this lotus is below. My progenitor will undoubtedly be there." Thinking thus I descended from the lotus. O sage, for a hundred years the downward trend continued. (9-11.)

---

Sanskrit
न लब्धं तु मया तत्र कमलस्थानमुत्तमम् ।
संशयं च पुनः प्राप्तः कमले गन्तुमुत्सुकः ॥ १२॥

English
The source of the lotus was not attained by me. In the doubt-tormented state I became eager to go up on to the top of the lotus. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
आरुरोहाथ कमलं नालमार्गेण वै मुने ।
कुड्मलं कमलस्याथ लब्धवान्न विमोहितः ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, I climbed up to the lotus by the stalk. But the upper part of the lotus I could not reach. I was disappointed. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
नालमार्गेण भ्रमतो गतं वर्षशतं पुनः ।
क्षणमात्रं तदा तत्र ततस्तिष्ठन्विमोहितः ॥ १४॥

English
Another hundred years elapsed in my wandering up the lotus. I stopped a while in that confounded state. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा वाणी समुत्पन्ना तपेति परमा शुभा ।
शिवेच्छया परा व्योम्नो मोहविध्वंसिनी मुने ॥ १५॥

English
Then, O sage, by the will of Śiva, an auspicious voice "Perform Penance" was heard from the sky which dispelled my delusion. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा व्योमवचनं द्वादशाब्दं प्रयत्नतः ।
पुनस्तप्तं तपो घोरं द्रष्टुं स्वजनकं तदा ॥ १६॥

English
On hearing the voice of the sky I exerted myself for twelve years in performing a terrible penance in order to see my progenitor. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा हि भगवान्विष्णुश्चतुर्बाहुः सुलोचनः ।
मय्येवानुग्रहं कर्तुं द्रुतमाविर्बभूव ह ॥ १७॥

English
At the same time, the four-armed lord Viṣṇu of beautiful eyes suddenly appeared before me in order to bless me. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचक्रायुधकरो गदापद्मधरः परः ।
घनश्यामलसर्वाङ्गः पीताम्बरधरः परः ॥ १८॥

English
The great lord was holding the conch, the discus, the mace and the lotus in his hands. He was wearing the yellow silken cloth and had cloud-blue complexion all over his body. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मुकुटादिमहाभूषः प्रसन्नमुखपङ्कजः ।
कोटिकन्दर्पसङ्काशःसन्दृष्टो मोहितेन सः ॥ १९॥

English
He had a crown. He was bedecked in great ornaments. His lotus-like face beamed with pleasure. Such was the lord resembling ten million Cupids that I saw still not out of delusion. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा सुन्दरं रूपं विस्मयं परमं गतः ।
कालाभं काञ्चनाभं च सर्वात्मानं चतुर्भुजम् ॥ २०॥

तथाभूतमहं दृष्ट्वा सदसन्मयमात्मना ।
नारायणं महाबाहुं हर्षितो ह्यभवं तदा ॥ २१॥

English
At the sight of that beautiful form I was struck with wonder. On seeing the four-armed Nārāyaṇa, shining like Kāla, of golden hue, the immanent soul of all in that form, of large arms depicting the Sat and Asat in Himself I became delighted. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
मायया मोहितः शम्भोस्तदा लीलात्मनः प्रभोः ।
अविज्ञाय स्वजनकं तमवोचं प्रहर्षितः ॥ २२॥

English
Deluded by the illusion of Śiva, the sportive lord, I could not recognise my progenitor in him. I addressed him with delight. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
कस्त्वं वदेति हस्तेन समुत्थाप्य सनातनम् ।
तदा हस्तप्रहारेण तीव्रेण सुदृढेन तु ॥ २३॥

English
"Who are you? Please tell me", saying this I tried to wake the Eternal Being. [When he did not wake up] I tried to wake him up with fiercer and firmer beatings of the hand. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रबुद्ध्योत्थाय शयनात्समासीनः क्षणं वशी ।
ददर्श निद्राविक्लिन्न नीरजामललोचनः ॥ २४॥

English
Then the lord who had self-control woke up from his bed and sat. He looked up with his pure eyes resembling a wet lotus, due to sleep. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मामत्र संस्थितं भासाध्यासितो भगवान्हरिः ।
आह चोत्थाय ब्रह्माणं हसन्मां मधुरं सकृत् ॥ २५॥

English
As I stood there quietly, the lord Viṣṇu spread his brilliance over me. Standing up he smiled once and spoke these sweet words. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
स्वागतं स्वागतं वत्स पितामह महाद्युते ।
निर्भयो भव दास्येऽहं सर्वान्कामान्न संशयः ॥ २६॥

English
*Viṣṇu said*:— "Welcome, welcome to you, dear child, O Pitāmaha of great brilliance. Do not be afraid. Undoubtedly I shall confer on you all that you desire. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा स्मितपूर्वं सुरर्षभः ।
रजसा बद्धवैरश्च तमवोचं जनार्दनम् ॥ २७॥

English
O foremost among gods, on hearing these words uttered with a smile I told Viṣṇu with my inimical attitude roused by the Rajoguṇa. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
भाषसे वत्स वत्सेति सर्वसंहारकारणम् ।
मामिहाति स्मितं कृत्वा गुरुः शिष्यमिवानघ ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said* :—  "O faultless one, how is it that you speak of me trivially as "Dear child", me who am the cause of annihilation of everything, as a preceptor addresses his disciple? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तारं जगतां साक्षात्प्रकृतेश्च प्रवर्तकम् ।
सनातनमजं विष्णुं विरिञ्चिं विष्णुसम्भवम् ॥ २९॥

विश्वात्मानं विधातारं धातारं पङ्कजेक्षणम् ।
किमर्थं भाषसे मोहाद्वक्तुमर्हसि सत्वरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
"I am the creator of worlds, the direct activiser of Prakṛti, unborn, the eternal, all-pervasive Brahmā. I am born of Viṣṇu. I am the soul of universe, the originator, creator, and the lotus-eyed. You must explain to me quickly why you speak like this. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदो मां वक्ति नियमात्स्वयम्भुवमजं विभुम् ।
पितामहं स्वराजं च परमेष्ठिनमुत्तमम् ॥ ३१॥

English
The Vedas speak of me invariably as self-born, unborn, all-pervasive, grandfather, self-governed and the excellent supreme Being. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य हरिर्वाक्यं मम क्रुद्धो रमापतिः ।
सोऽपि मामाह जाने त्वां कर्तारमिति लोकतः ॥ ३२॥

विष्णुरुवाच ।
कर्तुं धर्तुं भवानङ्गादवतीर्णो ममाव्ययात् ।
विस्मृतोऽसि जगन्नाथं नारायणमनामयम् ॥ ३३॥

पुरुषं परमात्मानं पुरुहूतं पुरुष्टुतम् ।
विष्णुमच्युतमीशानं विश्वस्य प्रभवोद्भवम् ॥ ३४॥

नारायणं महाबाहुं सर्वव्यापकमीश्वरम् ।
मन्नाभिपद्मतस्त्वं हि प्रसूतो नात्र संशयः ॥ ३५॥

English
On hearing these words of Hari, the lord of Lakṣmī became angry and told me thus :— *Viṣṇu said :—* “I know you as the creator of the world. For the sake of creation and support you are descended from my undecaying limbs. You have forgotten me, who am a lord of universe, abiding in waters the salubrious, the supreme soul, invoked by many, praised by many, All-pervasive, imperishable, ruler, the source and origin of universe, the long-armed and the omnipresent lord. There is no doubt in this that you are born of the lotus from my umbilicus. (32-35.)

---

Sanskrit

श‍ृणु सत्यं चतुर्वक्त्र सर्वदेवेश्वरो ह्यहम् ॥ ३६॥

English
“Of course, it is not your fault. I have exercised my power of illusion over you. O four-faced one, listen to the truth. I am the lord of all Gods. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
“I am the creator, sustainer and destroyer. There is no powerful person equal to me. O Pitāmaha, I am the supreme Brahman, the greatest Truth. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
सुश्वेतो ह्यनलप्रख्यो विश्वतः पक्षसंयुतः ।
मनोऽनिलजवो भूत्वा गतोर्ध्वं चोर्ध्वतः पुरा ॥ ५५॥

English
Very white of complexion and endowed with wings on either side I flew up and up with the speed of the mind and wind. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणोऽपि विश्वात्मा सुश्वेतो ह्यभवत्तदा ।
दशयोजनविस्तीर्णं शतयोजनमायतम् ॥ ५६॥

मेरुपर्वतवर्ष्माणं गौरतीक्ष्णोग्रदंष्ट्रिणम् ।
कालादित्यसमाभासं दीर्घघोणं महास्वनम् ॥ ५७॥

ह्रस्वपादं विचित्राङ्गं जैत्रं दृढमनौपमम् ।
वाराहाकारमास्थाय गतवांस्तदधौ जवात् ॥ ५८॥

English
Nārāyaṇa, the soul of the universe too, became white then. His body was ten yojanas wide and a hundred yojanas long, as huge as the mountain Meru. He had white sharp teeth. His brilliance resembled the sun at the time of dissolution. His snort was long and his roar tremendous. His feet were short. His limbs were of diverse colours. His form as the boar was of matchless firmness which assured his eagerness to be victorious, and he went down quickly. (56-58.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वर्षसहस्रं च चरन्विष्णुरधो गतः ।
तदाप्रभृति लोकेषु श्वेतवाराहसंज्ञकः ॥ ५९॥

English
For a thousand years his downward course continued. From that time onwards Viṣṇu came to be called “Śvetavārāha” (white Boar) in all the worlds. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पो बभूव देवर्षे नराणां कालसंज्ञकः ।
बभ्राम बहुधा विष्णुः प्रभविष्णुरधोगतः ॥ ६०॥

English
A Kalpa had elapsed according to human calculation when Viṣṇu thus went down and wandered in his eagerness to come out victorious. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
नापश्यदल्पमप्यस्य मूलं लिङ्गस्य सूकरः ।
तावत्कालं गतश्चोर्ध्वमहमप्यरिसूदन ॥ ६१॥

English
The Boar did not find even the smallest trace of the root of the Liṅga. O, destroyer of enemies, I too spent the same time in going up. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्वरं सर्वयत्नेन तस्यान्तं ज्ञातुमिच्छया ।
श्रान्तो न दृष्ट्वा तस्यान्तमहं कालादधोगतः ॥ ६२॥

English
From a desire to know its top as quickly as possible I exerted myself and was exhausted. Unable to see the top I came down after some time. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव भगवान्विष्णुः श्रान्तः कमललोचनः ।
सर्वदेवनिभस्तूर्णमुत्थितः स महावपुः ॥ ६३॥

English
Similarly, lord Viṣṇu, the lotus-eyed, too became weary. Appearing like the lord of everything in his huge body he too rose up. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
समागतो मया सार्धं प्रणिपत्य भवं मुहुः ।
मायया मोहितः शम्भोस्तस्थौ संविग्नमानसः ॥ ६४॥

English
As soon as he came up, we bowed to Śiva again and again. He stood aside with a gloomy mind as he too was deluded by the illusion of Śiva. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
पृष्ठतः पार्श्वतश्चैव ह्यग्रतः परमेश्वरम् ।
प्रणिपत्य मया सार्धं सस्मार किमिदं त्विति ॥ ६५॥

English
We bowed down to Liṅga at His back, sides and in front. He mused within himself “What can this be?” (65.)

---

Sanskrit
अनिर्देश्यं च तद्रूपमनाम कर्मवर्जितम् ।
अलिङ्गं लिङ्गतां प्राप्तं ध्यानमार्गेऽप्यगोचरम् ॥ ६६॥

English
“That form can’t be directly expressed. It is without action and name. Without any sex-distinction it has become a liṅga. It is beyond the path of meditation. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्थं चित्तं तदा कृत्वा नमस्कारपरायणौ ।
बभूवतुरुभावावामहं हरिरपि ध्रुवम् ॥ ६७॥

English
Both of us, Hari and I, with the peace of our minds, became eager to perform obeisance. (67.)

---

Sanskrit
जानीवो न हि ते रूपं योऽसि सोऽसि महाप्रभो ।
नमोऽस्तु ते महेशान रूपं दर्शय नौ त्वरम् ॥ ६८॥

English
“We do not know Thy true form, what Thou art Thou art, O great lord. Obeisance be to Thee, O Maheśāna. Please hurry up to reveal Thy form to us.” (68.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शरच्छतान्यासन्नमस्कारं प्रकुर्वतोः ।
आवयोर्मुनिशार्दूल मदमास्थितयोस्तदा ॥ ६९॥

English
Thus performing obeisance and prayer to quell our earlier pride, O foremost of sages, we spent a hundred autumns therein. (69.)
