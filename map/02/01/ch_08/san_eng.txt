### **CHAPTER EIGHT - *(The description of the body of Śabdabrahman)***

---

Sanskrit
८

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं तयोर्मुनिश्रेष्ठ दर्शनं काङ्क्षमाणयोः ।
विगर्वयोश्च सुरयोः सदा नौ स्थितयोर्मुने ॥ १॥

दयालुरभवच्छम्भुर्दीनानां प्रतिपालकः ।
गर्विणां गर्वहर्ता च सर्वेषां प्रभुरव्ययः ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O most excellent sage, we were eager to have a vision of the lord. Our haughtiness had been curbed. O sage, we waited there patiently. Śiva, the protector of the distressed, remover of the haughtiness of the haughty and the undecaying lord of everything took mercy on us. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा समभवत्तत्र नादो वै शब्दलक्षणः ।
ओमोमिति सुरश्रेष्ठात्सुव्यक्तः प्लुतलक्षणः ॥ ३॥

English
There arose the sound “Om Om” in the prolated accent.$^{190}$ It was very clear. The divine sound in the form of a word came out from the most excellent of Gods. (3.)

[fn 190. The pluta is a prolated vowel, as in Om, often marked with the figure three (मौष्ठव), as it contains three syllabic instant in pronouncing it.]
---

Sanskrit
किमिदं त्विति सञ्चिन्त्य मया तिष्ठन्महास्वनः ।
विष्णुः सर्वसुराराध्यो निर्वैरस्तुष्टचेतसा ॥ ४॥

लिङ्गस्य दक्षिणे भागे तथापश्यत्सनातनम् ।
आद्यं वर्णमकाराख्यमुकारं चोत्तरे ततः ॥ ५॥

English
“What shall be this great sound?” thinking like this I stood perplexed. Viṣṇu who is worthy of respect from all the Gods, who is free from all inimical thoughts, saw with the delightful heart, the eternal being’s manifestation on the right side of the linga. First, he saw the syllable “A” and he saw the syllable “U” thereafter. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
मकारं मध्यतश्चैव नादमन्तेऽस्य चोमिति ।
सूर्यमण्डलवद् दृष्ट्वा वर्णमाद्यं तु दक्षिणे ॥ ६॥

उत्तरे पावकप्रख्यमुकारमृषिसत्तम ।
शीतांशुमण्डलप्रख्यं मकारं तस्य मध्यतः ॥ ७॥

तस्योपरि तदापश्यच्छुद्धस्फटिकसुप्रभम् ।
तुरीयातीतममलं निष्कलं निरुपद्रवम् ॥ ८॥

निर्द्वन्द्वं केवलं शून्यं बाह्याभ्यन्तरवर्जितम् ।
सबाह्याभ्यन्तरे चैव बाह्याभ्यन्तरसंस्थितम् ॥ ९॥

आदिमध्यान्तरहितमानन्दस्यादिकारणम् ।
सत्यमानन्दममृतं परं ब्रह्मपरायणम् ॥ १०॥

English
He saw the syllable “M” in the middle and Nāda (the mystical sound) in the form “Om” in the end. He saw the first syllable on the right like the blazing sphere of the sun. O foremost of sages, thereafter he saw the syllable “U” dazzling like fire. In the middle he saw the syllable “M” glittering like the lunar sphere. Above that what he saw was the supreme Brahman, the greatest refuge. It had the lustre of the pure crystal. It was the pure Being beyond the Fourth (Turiya), the unsullied & free from extraneous harassment. It was free from mutually clashing opposites. It was single (isolated), void, free from exterior and interior though stationed in the exterior and the interior, devoid of beginning, middle and end, the primordial cause of Bliss, the truth, The Bliss and the Nectar. (6-10.)

Sanskrit
कुत एवात्र सम्भूतः परीक्षावोऽग्निसम्भवम् ।
अधो गमिष्याम्यनलस्तम्भस्यानुपमस्य च ॥ ११॥

वेदशब्दोभयावेशं विश्वात्मानं व्यचिन्तयत् ।
तदाभवदृषिस्तत्र ऋषेः सारतमः स्मृतः ॥ १२॥

English
Viṣṇu thus meditated on the universal soul enveloped by the two Vedic sounds and wished to examine the source whence the Fire-column arose and to go deep down the unequalled fiery column. Then there came a sage who told him the essence of the truth. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
तेनैव ऋषिणा विष्णुर्ज्ञातवान्परमेश्वरम् ।
महादेवं परं ब्रह्म शब्दब्रह्मतनुं परम् ॥ १३॥

English
Viṣṇu realised that the sage himself was the great lord and the supreme Brahman embodied in the Śabda Brahman. (i.e. the mystic syllable Om). (13.)

---

Sanskrit
चिन्तया रहितो रुद्रो वाचो यन्मनसा सह ।
अप्राप्य तन्निवर्तन्ते वाच्यस्त्वेकाक्षरेण सः ॥ १४॥

English
The Brahman is Rudra free from worries. The words and the mind are incapable of comprehending it; without reaching it they return. It can be expressed by the single-syllabled mantra “Om”. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाक्षरेण तद्वाक्यमृतं परमकारणम् ।
सत्यमानन्दममृतं परं ब्रह्म परात्परम् ॥ १५॥

English
The supreme Brahman, the Truth, the Bliss, the Amṛta, the greatest of the great and the ultimate cause can be expressed by the single-syllabled mantra. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाक्षरादकाराख्याद्भगवान्बीजकोऽण्डजः ।
एकाक्षरादुकाराख्याद्धरिः परमकारणम् ॥ १६॥

English
The single syllable “A” is the source of the lord Brahmā. The single syllable “U” is the source of Viṣṇu, the ultimate cause. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाक्षरान्मकाराख्याद्भगवान्नीललोहितः ।
सर्गकर्ता त्वकाराख्यो ह्युकाराख्यस्तु मोहकः ॥ १७॥

English
The single syllable “M” is the source of Rudra. The creator is expressed by the letter “A”. The enchanter is expressed by the letter “U” (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मकाराख्यस्तु यो नित्यमनुग्रहकरोऽभवत् ।
मकाराख्यो विभुर्बीजी ह्यकारो बीज उच्यते ॥ १८॥

English
The being expressed by the letter “M” blesses always. It is all-pervasive and progenitor; the letter “A” is the seed. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
उकाराख्यो हरिर्योनिः प्रधानपुरुषेश्वरः ।
बीजी च बीजं तद्योनिर्नादाख्यश्च महेश्वरः ॥ १९॥

English
The being expressed by the letter “U” is Viṣṇu. It is the source, the receptacle, the lord of primordial nature and primordial being, the progenitor, the seed, source and sound. All these constitute Lord Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
बीजी विभज्य चात्मानं स्वेच्छया तु व्यवस्थितः ।
अस्य लिङ्गादभूद् बीजमकारो बीजिनः प्रभोः ॥ २०॥

English
The progenitor is stationed after dividing itself. From the linga of the progenitor, the lord, arose the seed—the syllable “A” (20.)

---

Sanskrit
उकारयोनौ निःक्षिप्तमवर्धत समन्ततः ।
सौवर्णमभवच्चाण्डमावेद्यं तदलक्षणम् ॥ २१॥

English
The Bija being deposited in the Yoni, the letter “U” began to increase all round. It became a golden egg. It was something known which could not be delineated. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेकाब्दं तथा चाप्सु दिव्यमण्डं व्यवस्थितम् ।
ततो वर्षसहस्रान्ते द्विधा कृतमजोद्भवम् ॥ २२॥

English
The divine egg floated in the waters for many years. Then at the end of a thousand years, it split into two giving birth to Brahmā. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अण्डमप्सु स्थितं साक्षाद् व्याघातेनेश्वरेण तु ।
तथास्य सुशुभं हैमं कपालं चोर्ध्वसंस्थितम् ॥ २३॥

जज्ञे सा द्यौस्तदपरं पृथिवी पञ्चलक्षणा ।
तस्मादण्डाद्भवो जज्ञे ककाराख्यश्चतुर्मुखः ॥ २४॥

English
The egg floating in the waters on being hit by Īśvara split into two. The auspicious golden upper lid became the upper region and the lower one became the Earth of five characteristics. From (the inner part of) the egg was born the four-faced lord (Brahmā) expressed by the letter “KA” (23-24.)

Sanskrit
स स्रष्टा सर्वलोकानां स एव त्रिविधः प्रभुः ।
एवमोमोमिति प्रोक्तमित्याहुर्यजुषां वराः ॥ २५॥

English
He is the creator of all the worlds. He alone is the lord manifesting in three forms. Persons well-versed in the Yajurveda call it Om Om. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यजुषां वचनं श्रुत्वा ऋचः सामानि सादरम् ।
एवमेव हरे ब्रह्मन्नित्याहुश्चावयोस्तदा ॥ २६॥

English
On hearing the words of the Yajurveda, both the Ṛgveda and the Sāmaveda respectfully called us then Viṣṇu and Brahmā. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

मन्त्रैर्महेश्वरं देवं तुष्टाव सुमहोदयम् ॥ २७॥

English
Then realising the lord of the Gods we eulogised, as far as we could, Lord Śiva, the cause of great achievement. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरेऽन्यच्च रूपमद्भुतसुन्दरम् ।
ददर्श च मया सार्धं भगवान्विश्वपालकः ॥ २८॥

English
Viṣṇu, the protector of the universe, in the meantime, saw another wonderfully beautiful form, along with me. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चवक्त्रं दशभुजं गौरकर्पूरवन्मुने ।
नानाकान्तिसमायुक्तं नानाभूषणभूषितम् ॥ २९॥

महोदारं महावीर्यं महापुरुषलक्षणम् ।
तं दृष्ट्वा परमं रूपं कृतार्थोऽभून्मया हरिः ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing that wonderful form, Viṣṇu and I became satisfied. The form had five faces, ten arms, and a complexion white as camphor, O sage. It had diverse brilliant features. It was decorated in different ornaments. It was highly liberal and endowed with great prowess. It had all the characteristics of a great man. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रसन्नो भगवान्महेशः परमेश्वरः ।
दिव्यं शब्दमयं रूपमाख्याय प्रहसन्स्थितः ॥ ३१॥

English
Thereafter, the lord Śiva was pleased. Revealing his form embedded in letters He laughingly stood before us. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अकारस्तस्य मूर्धा हि ललाटो दीर्घ उच्यते ।
इकारो दक्षिणं नेत्रमीकारो वामलोचनम् ॥ ३२॥

English
The short letter “A” is His head. The long letter “Ā” is His forehead. The letter “I” is His right eye and the letter “Ī” His left eye. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
उकारो दक्षिणं श्रोत्रमूकारो वाम उच्यते ।
ऋकारो दक्षिणं तस्य कपोलं परमेष्ठिनः ॥ ३३॥

English
The letter “U” is His right ear and the letter “Ū” His left ear. The letter “R” is the right cheek of that great lord. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
वामं कपोलमॄकारो लृ लॄ नासापुटे उभे ।
एकारश्चोष्ठ ऊर्ध्वश्च ह्यैकारस्त्वधरो विभोः ॥ ३४॥

English
“R̥” is His left cheek. The two letters “I” “Ī”, are His nostrils. The letter “E” is His upper lip and the letter “Al” is His lower lip. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ओकारश्च तथौकारो दन्तपङ्क्तिद्वयं क्रमात् ।
अमस्तु तालुनी तस्य देवदेवस्य शूलिनः ॥ ३५॥

English
The letter “O” and the letter “AU” are respectively the two rows of his teeth. The letters “AM” and “AH” [Anusvāra and Visarga] are his palates. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The five letters beginning with KA [ie KA, KHA, GA, GHA and N A] are His five hands on the right side. The five letters beginning with CA [i.e. CA, CH, JA, JHA and NA] are His hands on the left side. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

पकार उदरं तस्य फकारः पार्श्व उच्यते ॥ ३७॥

English
Similarly the five letters beginning with TA and the five letters beginning with TA constitute His legs. The letter PA is His belly and the letter PHA is His right side. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

मकारो हृदयं शम्भोर्महादेवस्य योगिनः ॥ ३८॥

English
The letter BA is His left side. The letter BHA is His shoulder. The letter MA is the heart of the great yogin Mahādeva. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The letters YA, RA, LA, VA, ŚA, ṢA and SA are the seven Dhātus (vital secretions) of the lord. The letter HA is His umbilicus and the letter KṢA is His nose. (39.)

Sanskrit

English
Viṣṇu and I became contented on seeing this letter-embedded form of the Saguṇa manifestation of Nirguṇa lord in the company of Umā. (40.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
ओङ्कारप्रभवं मन्त्रं कलापञ्चकसंयुतम् ।
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशं शुभाष्टत्रिंशदक्षरम् ॥ ४२॥

मेधाकारमभूद्भूयः सर्वधर्मार्थसाधकम् ।
गायत्रीप्रभवं मन्त्रं सहितं वश्यकारकम् ॥ ४३॥

चतुर्विंशतिवर्णाढ्यं चतुष्कालमनुत्तमम् ।
अथ पञ्चसितं मन्त्रं कलाष्टकसमायुतम् ॥ ४४॥

आभिचारिकमत्यर्थं प्रायस्त्रिंशच्छुभाक्षरम् ।
यजुर्वेदसमायुक्तं पञ्चविंशच्छुभाक्षरम् ॥ ४५॥

कलाष्टकसमायुक्तं सुश्वेतं शान्तिकं तथा ।
त्रयोदशकलायुक्तं बालाद्यैः सह लेहितम् ॥ ४६॥

बभूवुरस्य चोत्पत्तिवृद्धिसंहारकारणम् ।
वर्णा एकाधिकाः षष्टिरस्य मन्त्रवरस्य तु ॥ ४७॥

English
The mantra beginning with Omkāra with its Kalās five in number, consisting of the auspicious thirty-eight syllables, being pure as crystal, increases intelligence and is an effective medium of accomplishing sacred rites. The mantras in the Gāyatri metre of twenty-four syllables and having four Kalās are conducive to enjoyment. The five-syllabled mantra of eight Kalās consisting of thirty syllables is employed for black magic. Mantras of Yajurveda consisting of twenty-five syllables and eight Kalās are used for conciliatory purpose. The mantra of thirteen Kalās consisting of sixty-one syllables is conducive to outcome, increase and destruction. (42-47.)

(50-53.)

इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां प्रथमखण्डे सृष्ट्युपाख्याने शब्दब्रह्मतनुवर्णनं नामाष्टमोऽध्यायः ॥ २.१.८॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥ 
॥ द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां प्रथमः सृष्टिखण्डः
