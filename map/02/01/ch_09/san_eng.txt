### **CHAPTER NINE - (Description of Śivatattva)**

---

Sanskrit
९

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथाकर्ण्य नुतिं विष्णुकृतां स्वस्य महेश्वरः ।
प्रादुर्बभूव सुप्रीतः सवामः करुणानिधिः ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said :—  On hearing his own eulogy from the mouth of Viṣṇu, the delighted Śiva, the store-house of kindness, revealed Himself to us along with his consort. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चवक्त्रस्त्रिनयनो भालचन्द्रो जटाधरः ।
गौरवर्णो विशालाक्षो भस्मोद्धूलितविग्रहः ॥ २॥

दशबाहुर्नीलगलः सर्वाभरणभूषितः ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरो भस्मत्रिपुण्ड्राङ्कितमस्तकः ॥ ३॥

English
He had five faces and three eyes, and the crescent moon on his forehead. He wore matted hair. He was white-complexioned and had wide eyes. His body had been dusted with the ashes. He had ten arms. His neck was blue in colour. He was bedecked with all ornaments. He was very handsome with respect to every limb. Three ash-lines marked His forehead. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा तादृशं देवं सवामं परमेश्वरम् ।
तुष्टाव पुनरिष्टाभिर्वाग्भिर्विष्णुर्मया सह ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing lord Śiva accompanied by His beautiful consort, Viṣṇu along with me eulogised Him again with appropriate words. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
निगमं श्वासरूपेण ददौ तस्मै ततो हरः ।
विष्णवे च प्रसन्नात्मा महेशः करुणाकरः ॥ ५॥

ततो ज्ञानमदात्तस्मै रहस्यं परमात्मने ।
परमात्मा पुनर्मह्यं दत्तवान्कृपया मुने ॥ ६॥

English
Śiva, the merciful, who was delighted breathed the Vedas into Viṣṇu and conferred Perfect Knowledge on him, the secret of the supreme Ātman. O sage, thereafter, out of sympathy, the supreme Ātman conferred these on me too. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्प्राप्य निगमं विष्णुः पप्रच्छ पुनरेव तम् ।
कृतार्थः साञ्जलिर्नत्वा मया सह महेश्वरम् ॥ ७॥

English
After receiving the Vedas, Viṣṇu was satisfied and bowing to Him with palms joined in reverence along with me, he asked the lord Śiva. (7.)

---

English
Viṣṇu said :—  “O Lord, How are you propitiated? How shall I worship you, O lord? How shall I meditate on you? How are you impressed by any one? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
कथं च तुष्यसे देव मया पूज्यः कथं प्रभो ।
कथं ध्यानं प्रकर्तव्यं कथं व्रजसि वश्यताम् । ८॥

किं कर्तव्यं महादेव ह्यावाभ्यां तव शासनात् ।
सदा सदाज्ञापय नौ प्रीत्यर्थं कुरु शङ्कर ॥ ९॥

English
O Great God, tell us what at Thy bidding shall we ever do? Please command us, O Śiva, do this to favour us. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वं महाराज कृपां कृत्वावयोः प्रभो ।
कथनीयं तथान्यच्च विज्ञाय स्वानुगौ शिव ॥ १०॥

English
O Great lord, be merciful to tell us all these things. O Śiva, we are your followers. Taking this into mind, you will enlighten us on these and other similar points too. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येतद्वचनं श्रुत्वा प्रसन्नो भगवान्हरः ।
उवाच वचनं प्रीत्या सुप्रसन्नः कृपानिधिः ॥ ११॥

English
Bramhā said :—  On hearing these words, the lord Śiva was delighted. The merciful lord then spoke lovingly. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशिव उवाच ।
भक्त्या च भवतोर्नूनं प्रीतोऽहं सुरसत्तमौ ।
पश्यन्तं मां महादेवं भयं सर्वं विमुञ्चताम् ॥ १२॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O foremost among gods, I am delighted by your devotion. Look upon me as a great deity. Cost off all your fears. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
मम लिङ्गं सदा पूज्यं ध्येयं चैतादृशं मम ।
इदानीं दृश्यते यद्वत्तथा कार्यं प्रयत्नतः ॥ १३॥

English
Worship my linga and do always meditate upon the form which you see just before you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजितो लिङ्गरूपेण प्रसन्नो विविधं फलम् ।
दास्यामि सर्वलोकेभ्यो मनोऽभीष्टान्यनेकशः ॥ १४॥

English
When I am worshipped in the phallic form I will be delighted and will bestow different benefits upon all people, all that they wish for in their minds. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा दुःखं भवेत्तत्र युवयोः सुरसत्तमौ ।
पूजिते मम लिङ्गे च तदा स्याद् दुःखनाशनम् ॥ १५॥

English
O foremost among the deities, whenever any misery befalls you, it shall be destroyed when my linga is worshipped. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
युवां प्रसूतौ प्रकृतेर्मदीयाया महाबलौ ।
सव्यापसव्यागात्राभ्यां मम सर्वेश्वरस्य हि ॥ १६॥

English
O strong ones, you two are born of my own Prakṛti, out of my left and right sides. I am the lord of everything. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं मे दक्षिणात्पार्श्वाद् ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ।
वामपार्श्वाच्च विष्णुस्त्वं समुत्पन्नः परात्मनः ॥ १७॥

English
This Brahmā, grandfather for all people, is born of my right side. You, Viṣṇu, are born of my left side. I am the supreme Ātman. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रीतोऽहं युवयोः सम्यग्वरं दद्यां यथेप्सितम् ।
मयि भक्तिर्दृढा भूयाद्युवयोरभ्यनुज्ञया ॥ १८॥

पार्थिवीं चैव मन्मूर्तिं विधाय कुरुतं युवाम् ।
सेवां च विविधां प्राज्ञौ कृत्वा सुखमवाप्स्यथः ॥ १९॥

English
Delighted I shall confer on you boons and whatever you desire. May your devotion to me be steady. With my permission you can make my form in clay and perform adoration. After rendering different kinds of service like this sensibly you shall attain happiness. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मन्सृष्टिं कुरु त्वं हि मदाज्ञापरिपालकः ।
वत्स वत्स हरे त्वं च पालयैवं चराचरम् ॥ २०॥

English
O Brahmā, strictly adhering to my direction you carry on the work of creation. Dear child, dear Hari, you shall sustain the mobile and the immobile beings. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा नौ प्रभुस्ताभ्यां पूजाविधिमदाच्छुभाम् ।
येनैव पूजितः शम्भुः फलं यच्छत्यनेकशः ॥ २१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Saying thus, the lord presented to us the auspicious mode of His worship, adored duly by means of which Śiva confers many benefits. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भोर्मया च सहितो हरिः ।
प्रत्युवाच महेशानं प्रणिपत्य कृताञ्जलिः ॥ २२॥

English
On hearing the words of Śiva along with me, Viṣṇu bowed to Śiva with palms joined in reverence and said. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
यदि प्रीतिः समुत्पन्ना यदि देयो वरश्च नौ ।
भक्तिर्भवतु नौ नित्यं त्वयि चाव्यभिचारिणी ॥ २३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “If you are pleased, if a boon is to be given to us, may our devotion to you be perpetual and unstraying. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमप्यवतरस्वाद्य लीलया निर्गुणोऽपि हि ।
सहायं कुरु नौ तात त्वं परः परमेश्वरः ॥ २४॥

English
Although you are Nirguṇa, be pleased to incarnate in the course of your divine sports and help us. Dear lord, you are great lord, the supreme. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
आवयोर्देवदेवेश विवादमपि शोभनम् ।
इहागतो भवान्यस्माद्विवादशमनाय नौ ॥ २५॥

English
O lord of lords, even our dispute has turned out to be auspicious, now that you have come here to suppress the same". (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा पुनः प्राह हरो हरिम् ।
प्रणिपत्य स्थितं मूर्ध्ना कृताञ्जलिपुटः स्वयम् ॥ २६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words Śiva told Viṣṇu who stood there with the head bent down and with palms joined in reverence. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमहेश उवाच ।
प्रलयस्थितिसर्गाणां कर्ताहं सगुणोऽगुणः ।
परब्रह्म निर्विकारी सच्चिदानन्दलक्षणः ॥ २७॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  Although Nirguṇa, I am Saguṇa too and the author of dissolution, maintenance and creation. I am the supreme Brahman without decay and change. Existence, Knowledge and Bliss are my characteristics. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिधा भिन्नो ह्यहं विष्णो ब्रह्मविष्णुहराख्यया ।
सर्गरक्षालयगुणैर्निष्कलोऽहं सदा हरे ॥ २८॥

English
Truly, I am Niṣkala (Nirguna) for ever, O Hari. For the activities of creation, maintenance and dissolution I manifest myself in the three forms of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Hara, O Viṣṇu. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुतोऽहं यत्त्वया विष्णो ब्रह्मणा मेऽवतारणे ।
प्रार्थनां तां करिष्यामि सत्यां यद्भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २९॥

English
O Viṣṇu, since you, along with Brahmā, have eulogised me and prayed for my incarnation, I shall make that request true, favourably disposed towards my devotees that I am. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्रूपं परमं ब्रह्मन्नीदृशं भवदङ्गतः ।
प्रकटीभविता लोके नाम्ना रुद्रः प्रकीर्तितः ॥ ३०॥

English
A great form similar to this, O Brahmā, shall become manifest in the world through your body. He will be called Rudra. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मदंशात्तस्य सामर्थ्यं न्यूनं नैव भविष्यति ।
योऽहं सोऽहं न भेदोऽस्ति पूजाविधिविधानतः ॥ ३१॥

English
His capacity will never be less, since He will be my own part and parcel. He is I. I am he. In the modes of worship too there is no difference. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा च ज्योतिषः सङ्गाज्जलादेः स्पर्शता न वै ।
तथा ममागुणस्यापि संयोगाद् बन्धनं न हि ॥ ३२॥

English
As heat etc. in water and other things due to the contact of fire is not permanent in water etc., similarly my Nirguṇa aspect is not affected by the external contact. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवरूपं ममैतच्च रुद्रोऽपि शिववत्तदा ।
न तत्र परभेदो वै कर्तव्यश्च महामुने ॥ ३३॥

English
This form of mine as Śiva is that of Rudra too. O great sage, no one shall make any difference in it. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्तुतो ह्येकरूपं हि द्विधा भिन्नं जगत्युत ।
अतो भेदो न विज्ञेयः शिवे रुद्रे कदाचन ॥ ३४॥

English
The same form appears split into two in the universe. Hence Śiva and Rudra shall not be considered different. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्णस्य यथैकस्य वस्तुत्वं नैव गच्छति ।
अलङ्कृतिकृते देव नामभेदो न वस्तुतः ॥ ३५॥

English
A piece of gold turned into an ornament does not cease to be gold. There may be difference in name but not in the material content. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
यथैकस्या मृदो भेदो नानापात्रे न वस्तुतः ।
कारणस्यैव कार्ये न सन्निधानं निदर्शनम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Just as the difference of clay and the various objects made of it is not a material one, so also in this case. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञातव्यं बुधवर्यैश्च निर्मलज्ञानिभिः सुरौ ।
एवं ज्ञात्वा भवद्भ्यां तु न दृश्यं भेदकारणम् ॥ ३७॥

English
This shall be known by all scholars and Gods of unsullied knowledge. If you realise this, you will not be seeing the cause of difference. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्तुवत् सर्वदृश्यं च शिवरूपं मतं मम ।
अहं भवानजश्चैव रुद्रो योऽयं भविष्यति ॥ ३८॥

एकरूपा न भेदस्तु भेदे वै बन्धनं भवेत् ।
तथापि च मदीयं हि शिवरूपं सनातनम् ॥ ३९॥

English
I think that we all should see the from of Śiva as the basic material. Myself, you, Brahmā and Rudra who will be manifesting himself are of the same form. There is no difference. If there had been difference that would have been bondage. Yet the eternal Śiva-form is mine alone. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
मूलीभूतं सदोक्तं च सत्यज्ञानमनन्तकम् ।
एवं ज्ञात्वा सदा ध्येयं मनसा चैव तत्त्वतः ॥ ४०॥

English
That pure form is spoken of as the main root, the Truth, the Knowledge, the Endless. Realising this too, it must be meditated upon in the true manner in your mind. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रूयतां चैव भो ब्रह्मन् यद्गोप्यं कथ्यते मया ।
भवन्तौ प्रकृतेर्यातौ नायं वै प्रकृतेः पुनः ॥ ४१॥

English
O Brahmā, another secret which I am going to unfold to you may be listened to. You two are born of Prakṛti but not this one (Rudra). (41.)

---

Sanskrit
मदाज्ञा जायते तत्र ब्रह्मणो भ्रुकुटेरहम् ।
गुणेष्वपि यथा प्रोक्तस्तामसः प्रकृतो हरः ॥ ४२॥

वैकारिकश्च विज्ञेयो योऽहङ्कार उदाहृतः ।
नामतो वस्तुतो नैव तामसः परिचक्ष्यते ॥ ४३॥

English
My command is carried to that place through Brahmā's eyebrows. I am therefore spoken of as Tāmasa and Prākṛta, Hara in respect to the Guṇas alone and shall be known as Vaikārika too which is actually the Ahaṅkāra (the Ego). That is called Tāmasa only in name and not in reality. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मात्कारणाद् बह्मन्करणीयमिदं त्वया ।
सृष्टिकर्ता भव ब्रह्मन्सृष्टेश्च पालको हरिः ॥ ४४॥

English
For this reason, O Brahmā, this shall be carried out by you. O Brahmā, you shall be the creator and Hari the protector. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
मदीयश्च तथांशो यो लयकर्ता भविष्यति ।
इयं या प्रकृतिर्देवी ह्युमाख्या परमेश्वरी ॥ ४५॥

English
My would-be part shall be the cause of dissolution. This goddess Umā, Parameśvari is the Prakṛti. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यास्तु शक्तिर्वाग्देवी ब्रह्माणं सा भजिष्यति ।
अन्या शक्तिः पुनस्तत्र प्रकृतेः सम्भविष्यति ॥ ४६॥

English
Her Śakti, the goddess of speech, shall resort to Brahmā. Another Śakti also will be arising out of the Prakṛti. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
समाश्रयिष्यते विष्णुं लक्ष्मीरूपेण सा तदा ।
पुनश्च काली नाम्ना सा मदंशं प्राप्स्यति ध्रुवम् ॥ ४७॥

English
That Śakti will resort to Viṣṇu in the form of Lakṣmī. Another Śakti Kālī will surely share my part. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतीरूपेण सा तत्र कार्यार्थे सम्भविष्यति ।
एवं देव्यास्तथा प्रोक्ताः शक्तयः परमाः शुभाः ॥ ४८॥

English
She will be born in the form of Brilliance for effective work. Thus I have told you of the great auspicious Śaktis of the Goddess. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिस्थितिलयानां हि कार्यं तासां क्रमाद ध्रुवम् ।
एतस्याः प्रकृतेरंशा मत्प्रियायाः सुरोत्तम ॥ ४९॥

English
Their activities are respectively creation, maintenance and dissolution. O foremost among Gods, they are the parts of Prakṛti, my beloved. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं च लक्ष्मीमुपाश्रित्य कार्यं कर्तुमिहार्हसि ।
ब्रह्मंस्त्वं च गिरां देवीं प्रकृत्यंशामवाप्य च ॥ ५०॥

सृष्टिकार्यं हृदा कर्तुं मन्निर्देशादिहार्हसि ।
अहं कालीं समाश्रित्य मत्प्रियांशां परात्पराम् ॥ ५१॥

रुद्ररूपेण प्रलयं करिष्ये कार्यमुत्तमम् ।
चतुर्वर्णमयं लोकं तत्सर्वैराश्रमैर्ध्रुवम् ॥ ५२॥

तदन्यैर्विविधैः कार्यैः कृत्वा सुखमवाप्स्यथः ।
ज्ञानविज्ञानसंयुक्तो लोकानां हितकारकः ॥ ५३॥

English
O Viṣṇu, you shall carry on your activities with the co-operation of Lakṣmī. O Brahmā, with the co- operation of the goddess of speech, the part of Prakṛti, you shall carry on joyfully the activity of creation, according to my direction. I shall have the co-operation of Kālī, the part of my beloved, the greatest of the great and shall carry out the excellent activity of dissolution in the form of Rudra. You shall be happy after the creation of the world consisting of the four Varṇas and their ancillaries—the four Āśramas (stages of life) and various sorts of other incidental activities. You shall contribute to the welfare of the world making use of your knowledge and perfect wisdom. (50-53.)
