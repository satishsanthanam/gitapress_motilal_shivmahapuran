### **CHAPTER TEN - *(Description of Parama Śivatattva)***

---

Sanskrit
१०

परमेश्वर उवाच ।
अन्यच्छृणु हरे विष्णो शासनं मम सुव्रत ।
सदा सर्वेषु लोकेषु मान्यः पूज्यो भविष्यसि ॥ १॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  O Viṣṇu of good rites, O Hari, listen to another pronouncement of mine. You will be worthy of honour and worship in all the worlds for ever. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणा निर्मिते लोके यदा दुःखं प्रजायते ।
तदा त्वं सर्वदुःखानां नाशाय तत्परो भव ॥ २॥

English
Whenever a misery befalls the world created by Brahmā, you shall be eager for the destruction of all miseries. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सहायं ते करिष्यामि सर्वकार्ये च दुःसहे ।
तव शत्रून्हनिष्यामि दुःसाध्यान्परमोत्कटान् ॥ ३॥

English
In all difficult and unbearable activities I shall help you. I shall kill your indefatigable and fierce enemies. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विविधानवतारांश्च गृहीत्वा कीर्तिमुत्तमाम् ।
विस्तारय हरे लोके तारणाय परो भव ॥ ४॥

English
O Viṣṇu, spread your glory in the worlds far and wide by taking up various incarnations. Strive for their succour. I am always Saguṇa when I become Rudra with this body. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
गुणरूपो ह्यहं रुद्रो ह्यनेन वपुषा सदा ।
कार्यं करिष्ये लोकानां तवाशक्यं न संशयः ॥ ५॥

English
Certainly I shall perform your activities for the sake of the worlds if they are impossible for you. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रध्येयो भवांश्चैव भवद्ध्येयो हरस्तथा ।
युवयोरन्तरं नैव तव रुद्रस्य किञ्चन ॥ ६॥

English
You are to be meditated upon by Rudra and Rudra is to be meditated upon by you. There is no difference between you and Rudra. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्तुतश्चापि चैकत्वं वरतोऽपि तथैव च ।
लीलयापि महाविष्णो सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ७॥

English
O Great Viṣṇu, your identity is due to inherent nature, the boons conferred and your divine sports. True, it is certainly true. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रभक्तो नरो यस्तु तव निन्दां करिष्यति ।
तस्य पुण्यं च निखिलं द्रुतं भस्म भविष्यति ॥ ८॥

English
If any devotee of Rudra were to censure you, he will have all his merits reduced to ashes quickly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नरके पतनं तस्य त्वद्द्वेषात्पुरुषोत्तम ।
मदाज्ञया भवेद्विष्णो सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ९॥

English
O Viṣṇu, the most excellent of all persons, by hating you he will certainly fall into hell. That is my directive. True, it is certainly true. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकेऽस्मिन्मुक्तिदो नृणां भुक्तिदश्च विशेषतः ।
ध्येयः पूज्यश्च भक्तानां निग्रहानुग्रहौ कुरु ॥ १०॥

English
In this world, be the bestower of worldly pleasures and salvation to men particularly. Worthy of being honoured and worshipped by devotees, perform the activities of curbing and blessing. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा मां च धातारं हस्ते धृत्वा स्वयं हरिम् ।
कथयामास दुःखेषु सहायो भव सर्वदा ॥ ११॥

English
Saying this and holding me, the creator, and Viṣṇu by the hand He continued—“Always render help in distress. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाध्यक्षश्च सर्वेषु भक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायकः ।
भव त्वं सर्वदा श्रेष्ठः सर्वकामप्रसाधकः ॥ १२॥

English
Be the presiding deity of all. Bestow worldly pleasures and salvation for ever. Be the most excellent accomplisher of the achievement of all desires. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां प्राणरूपश्च भव त्वं च ममाज्ञया ।
सङ्कटे भजनीयो हि स रुद्रो मत्तनुर्हरे ॥ १३॥

English
You will assume the form of the vital airs in everyone at my bidding. O Hari, in the times of distress, Rudra my manifestation, shall be worshipped. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां यः समाश्रितो नूनं मामेव स समाश्रितः ।
अन्तरं यश्च जानाति निरये पतति ध्रुवम् ॥ १४॥

English
He who has sought refuge in you has certainly sought refuge in me. He who differentiates will certainly fall into Hell. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
आयुर्बलं श‍ृणुष्वाद्य त्रिदेवानां विशेषतः ।
सन्देहोऽत्र न कर्तव्यो ब्रह्मविष्णुहरात्मनाम् ॥ १५॥

English
Listen to the span of life of the deities—Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Hara. There shall not be any doubt in this respect. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्युगसहस्राणि ब्रह्मणो दिनमुच्यते ।
रात्रिश्च तावती तस्य मानमेतत्क्रमेण ह ॥ १६॥

English
A thousand sets of the four-yuga periods constitute one day of Brahmā. The period of night is also similar. Further measurement of time is based on this calculation. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां त्रिंशद्दिनैर्मासो द्वादशैस्तैश्च वत्सरः ।
शतवर्षप्रमाणेन ब्रह्मायुः परिकीर्तितम् ॥ १७॥

English
Thirty such days (days and nights) constitute one month and twelve months, one year. The span of life of Brahmā is hundred such years. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणो वर्षमात्रेण दिनं वैष्णवमुच्यते ।
सोऽपि वर्षशतं यावदात्ममानेन जीवति ॥ १८॥

English
One year of Brahmā constitutes one day of Viṣṇu. Viṣṇu lives for hundred years in his own calculation. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
वैष्णवेन तु वर्षेण दिनं रौद्रं भवेद् ध्रुवम् ।
हरो वर्षशते याते नररूपेण संस्थितः ॥ १९॥

English
One year of Viṣṇu constitutes one day of Rudra. When a hundred year period passes, Rudra assumes the form of Nara (supreme Man). (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यावदुच्छ्वसितं वक्त्रे सदाशिवसमुद्भवम् ।
पश्चाच्छक्तिं समभ्येति यावन्निःश्वसितं भवेत् ॥ २०॥

English
He stays like that as long as the breath is retained by Sadāśiva. When He exhales he merges into Śakti. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
निःश्वासोच्छ्वसितानां च सर्वेषामेव देहिनाम् ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुहराणां च गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् ॥ २१॥

एकविंशसहस्राणि शतैः षड्भिः शतानि च ।
अहोरात्राणि चोक्तानि प्रमाणं सुरसत्तमौ ॥ २२॥

English
In the case of all living beings, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Hara, Gandharvas, serpents, Rākṣasas, etc., twenty one thousand six hundred respirations constitute the period of one day and one night, O foremost among Devas. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
षड्भिरुच्छ्वासनिःश्वासैः पलमेकं प्रवर्तितम् ।
घटी षष्टिपला प्रोक्ता सा षष्ट्या च दिनं निशा ॥ २३॥

निःश्वासोच्छ्वासितानां च परिसङ्ख्या न विद्यते ।
सदाशिवसमुत्थानमेतस्मात्सोऽक्षयः स्मृतः ॥ २४॥

English
Six respirations constitute the period of time one Pala. Sixty such Palas constitute one Ghaṭī. Sixty Ghaṭīs constitute one day and one night. ($6 \times 60 \times 60 = 21600$). There is no limit to the number of respirations of Sadāśiva. Hence He is undecaying. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं रूपं त्वया तावद्रक्षणीयं ममाज्ञया ।
तावत्सृष्टेश्च कार्यं वै कर्तव्यं विविधैर्गुणैः ॥ २५॥

English
It is my directive that you should preserve this form and maintain all the activities of the created worlds by means of these different Guṇas so long.” (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भोर्मया च भगवान्हरिः ।
प्रणिपत्य च विश्वेशं प्राह मन्दतरं वशी ॥ २६॥

English
On hearing these words of Śiva the lord Viṣṇu, of controlled senses, spoke slowly to Śiva after duly bowing to Him. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
शङ्कर श्रूयतामेतत्कृपासिन्धो जगत्पते ।
सर्वमेतत्करिष्यामि भवदाज्ञावशानुगः ॥ २७॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “O Śaṅkara, the ocean of mercy, the lord of the universe, be pleased to hear. I shall do all these things strictly adhering to your behests. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मम ध्येयः सदा त्वं च भविष्यसि न चान्यथा ।
भवतः सर्वसामर्थ्यं लब्धं चैव पुरा मया ॥ २८॥

English
I shall always meditate upon you. I would not act otherwise. Your omnipotency has already been experienced by me. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणमात्रमपि स्वामिंस्तव ध्यानं परं मम ।
चेतसो दूरतो नैव सङ्गच्छतु कदाचन ॥ २९॥

English
O lord, let not the meditation of your form be ever far removed from my mind even for a moment. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मम भक्तश्च यः स्वामिँस्तव निन्दां करिष्यति ।
तस्य वै निरये वासं प्रयच्छ नियतं ध्रुवम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O lord, if anyone of my devotees were to censure you, you will please assign perpetual residence in hell to him. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वद्भक्तो यो भवेत् स्वामिन्मम प्रियतरो हि सः ।
एवं वै यो विजानाति तस्य मुक्तिर्न दुर्लभा ॥ ३१॥

English
O lord, whoever be your devotee shall also be my favourite. He who knows and realises this shall not find salvation inaccessible to him. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
महिमा च मदीयोऽद्य वर्धितो भवता ध्रुवम् ।
कदाचिदगुणश्चैव जायते क्षम्यतामिति ॥ ३२॥

English
My greatness has been further heightened by you certainly. If ever I am found deficient in qualities I may be excused. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तदा शम्भुस्तदीयं हि श्रुत्वा वचनमुत्तमम् ।
उवाच विष्णुं सुप्रीत्या क्षम्या तेऽगुणता मया ॥ ३३॥

English
(Brahmā said :—) Then, on hearing the excellent words of Viṣṇu, Śiva said to him “Of course the deficiency shall be excused lovingly.” (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्त्वा हरिं नौ स कराभ्यां परमेश्वरः ।
पस्पर्श सकलाङ्गेषु कृपया तु कृपानिधिः ॥ ३४॥

English
After saying this mercifully the lord, the ocean of mercy, stroked us all over the body with His hands. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
आदिश्य विविधान्धर्मान्सर्वदुःखहरो हरः ।
ददौ वराननेकांश्चावयोर्हितचिकीर्षया ॥ ३५॥

English
With a desire to do what is wholesome to us He instructed us in various sacred rites and conferred on us many boons. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स भगवान् शम्भुः कृपया भक्तवत्सलः ।
दृष्ट्या सम्पश्यतोः शीघ्रं तत्रैवान्तरधीयत ॥ ३६॥

English
Even as we were watching, the lord Śiva, favourably disposed towards devotees, vanished immediately. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाप्रभृति लोकेऽस्मिँल्लिङ्गपूजाविधिः स्मृतः ।
लिङ्गे प्रतिष्ठितः शम्भुर्भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायकः ॥ ३७॥

English
The rite of the worship of the liṅga is instituted ever since in the world. Śiva installed in the liṅga bestows worldly pleasures and salvation. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गवेदिर्महादेवी लिङ्गं साक्षान्महेश्वरः ।
लयनाल्लिङ्गमित्युक्तं तत्रैव निखिलं जगत् ॥ ३८॥

English
The great goddess is the pedestal for the liṅga. The liṅga is Lord Śiva Himself. Since the whole universe finally merges into in, it is called Liṅga. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तु लैङ्गं पठेन्नित्यमाख्यानं लिङ्गसन्निधौ ।
षण्मासाच्छिवरूपो वै नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ३९॥

English
He who reads regularly this anecdote of the Liṅga in the vicinity of the liṅga assumes the form of Śiva within six months. There need be no hesitation in this respect. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तु लिङ्गसमीपे तु कार्यं किञ्चित्करोति च ।
तस्य पुण्यफलं वक्तुं न शक्नोमि महामुने ॥ ४०॥

English
O great sage, I cannot adequately express the blessedness accruing to the person who carries on any activity whatsoever in the vicinity of the liṅga. (40.)

---
