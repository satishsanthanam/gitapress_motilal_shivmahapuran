### **CHAPTER TWELVE - *(Consideration of the essential and the non-essential in the worship)***

---

Sanskrit
१२

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन्प्रजापते तात धन्यस्त्वं शिवसक्तधीः ।
एतदेव पुनः सम्यग्ब्रूहि मे विस्तराद्विधे ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O dear father Brahmā, with your mind fixed on Śiva, you are blessed indeed. Please explain this again still more precisely. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एकस्मिन्समये तात ऋषीनाहूय सर्वतः ।
निर्जरांश्चावदं प्रीत्या सुवचः पद्मसम्भवः ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  I, the lotus-born, once called together all the sages and all the Gods and addressed them lovingly with these good words. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि नित्यसुखे श्रद्धा यदि सिद्धेश्च कामुकाः ।
आगन्तव्यं मया सार्धं तीरं क्षीरपयोनिधेः ॥ ३॥

English
If you have faith in permanent happiness, if you desire the achievement of the same, all of you shall come along with me to the shores of the milk-ocean.$^{200}$ (3.)

[fn 200. According to the Paurāṇic concept, the turbulent and foamy sea known as the southern China Sea which surrounds Sākadvīpa (identified with Malaya, Siam, Indo-China and Southern China) on three sides was called ‘the sea of milk’ or Kṣīra Samudra : cp. SM. Ali : Geography of the Purāṇas.]

English

English
On seeing Brahmā and other deities standing there, Viṣṇu remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and spoke these noble words. (6.)

English

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
# *The lord said :—*  O Brahmā, hear. You and these devas have already heard this. Yet I shall repeat it to you and to the devas. (11.)

Sanskrit

ब्रह्मन्देवैः समस्तैश्च बहुधा कार्यतत्परैः ॥ १२॥

सेव्यः सेव्यः सदा देवः शङ्करः सर्वदुःखहा ।

English
It has been seen. It is being seen. Then why is it being asked now? O Brahmā, Lord Śiva, the destroyer of all miseries, shall be served always by all who wish to achieve things. He Himself has told me as well as Brahmā particularly about this. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit

English
His worship shall never be forsaken by those who wish to attain happiness. A wonderful example has been narrated to and seen by you all. (14.)

Sanskrit

English
When they abandoned worshipping the lord of the Devas—Maheśvara in the form of the Liṅga, the sons of Tāra$^{201}$ along with their kinsmen perished. (15.)

[fn 201. Tāraputras—the children of Daitya Tāraka who was conquered by Indra with the help of Skanda—the son of Śiva. The episode is the central theme of Kālidāsa’s Kumārasambhava.]

Sanskrit
मया च मोहितास्ते वै मायया दूरतः कृताः ।
सर्वे विनष्टाः प्रध्वस्ताः शिवेन रहिता यदा ॥ १६॥

English
They had been enchanted by me. By my illusion they were driven far by me. When they were devoid of Śiva, they were all destroyed and exterminated. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सदा पूजनीयो लिङ्गमूर्तिधरो हरः ।
सेवनीयो विशेषेण श्रद्धया देवसत्तम ॥ १७॥

English
Hence Śiva in the form of phallic image shall be worshipped always. He, the foremost among deities, shall be served with special faith. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शर्वलिङ्गार्चनादेव देवा दैत्याश्च सत्तमाः ।
अहं त्वं च तथा ब्रह्मन्कथं तद्विस्मृतं त्वया ॥ १८॥

English
It is by the worship of the liṅga of Śiva that all good men, devas, daityas, I and you, O Brahmā, are sustained. How is it that it was forgotten by you? (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्लिङ्गमर्चयेन्नित्यं येन केनापि हेतुना ।
तस्मात् ब्रह्मन्सुरैः शर्वः सर्वकामफलेप्सया ॥ १९॥

English
Hence, O Brahmā, His liṅga shall be regularly worshipped whatever may be the aim. Śiva shall be worshipped whatever the desire may be. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

यन्मुहूर्तं क्षणं वापि शिवं नैव समर्चयेत् ॥ २०॥

English
If an hour or even a moment is spent without the worship of Śiva, it is a loss. It is an imperfection, a great foible, blindness, stupidity and foolishness. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

भवसंस्मरणा ये च न ते दुःखस्य भाजनाः ॥ २१॥

English
Those who are devotedly attached to Śiva, those whose minds are turned towards Śiva and those who constantly remember Śiva, never become victims of misery. (21.)

Sanskrit

आरोग्यं च शरीरं च प्रतिष्ठां चाप्यलौकिकीम् ।

अन्ते मुक्तिफलं चैव भक्तिं वा परमेशितुः ।
पूर्वपुण्यातिरेकेण तेऽर्चयन्ति सदाशिवम् ॥ २४॥

English
Those who desire magnificent buildings, beautiful ornaments, beautiful women, wealth to satiety, sons and grandsons, health, splendid body, extraordinary status, heavenly happiness and final salvation or profound devotion to the great lord shall duly worship Śiva by virtue of their merit accumulated by them. (22-24.)

Sanskrit

English
"O sages and devas, be pleased to hear with love and pleasure. I am going to explain lovingly the mode of worshipping Śiva that confers worldly pleasures and salvation. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Śiva is always present near a person set in meditation. There is no necessity for any atonement or expiation for a person of true knowledge. (47.)

---

बाह्यं स्थूलं समुद्दिष्टं सूक्ष्ममाभ्यन्तरं मतम् ॥ ५१॥

English
O brahmins, linga is of two types: the exterior and the interior. The exterior is gross and the interior is subtle. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मयज्ञरता ये च स्थूललिङ्गार्चने रताः ।
असतां भावनार्थाय सूक्ष्मेण स्थूलविग्रहाः ॥ ५२॥

English
Those who are engaged in ritualistic sacrifices and do regularly worship the gross linga are unable to steady the mind by meditating upon the subtle and hence they use the gross linga. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
आध्यात्मिकं तु यल्लिङ्गं प्रत्यक्षं यस्य नो भवेत् ।
स तल्लिङ्गे तथा स्थूले कल्पयेच्च न चान्यथा ॥ ५३॥

English
He who has not mastered the linga of the mind, the subtle one, must perform the worship in the gross linga and not otherwise. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानिनां सूक्ष्मममलं भावात्प्रत्यक्षमव्ययम् ।
यथा स्थूलमयुक्तानामुत्कृष्टादौ प्रकल्पितम् ॥ ५४॥

English
The pure undying subtle linga is ever perceived by the masters of true knowledge in the same manner as the gross one is thought to be very excellent by those who are not yogins. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो विचारतो नास्ति ह्यन्यत्तत्वार्थवादिनः ।
निष्कलं सकलं चित्ते सर्वं शिवमयं जगत् ॥ ५५॥

English
If we consider properly there is nothing else for the real interpreter. Whatever is Niṣkala or Sakala is of the form of Śiva in the whole universe. This must be constantly thought of in the mind. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं ज्ञानविमुक्तानां नास्ति दोषविकल्पना ।
विधिश्चैव तथा नास्ति विहिताविहिते तथा ॥ ५६॥

English
Even if they are devoid of the ultimate perfect knowledge, no defect or deficiency can be ascribed to them. Rules regarding what shall be done and what shall not be done are not binding on them. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा जलेषु कमलं सलिलैर्नावलिप्यते ।
तथा ज्ञानी गृहे तिष्ठन्कर्मणा नावबध्यते ॥ ५७॥

English
The knower, of course, is not at all bound by actions, even if he continues the householder's life just as the lotus standing in water is not contaminated by the water. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ज्ञानं समुत्पन्नं यावन्नैव नरस्य वै ।
तावच्च कर्मणा देवं शिवमाराधयेन्नरः ॥ ५८॥

English
Till the realisation of perfect knowledge a man should continue the ritualistic worship of Śiva. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्ययार्थं च जगतामेकस्थोऽपि दिवाकरः ।
एकोऽपि बहुधा दृष्टो जलाधारादिवस्तुषु ॥ ५९॥

दृश्यते श्रूयते लोके यद्यत्सदसदात्मकम् ।
तत्तत्सर्वं सुरा वित्त परं ब्रह्म शिवात्मकम् ॥ ६०॥

English
In order to convince the world, the rituals must be continued. Just as the sun is reflected in many vessels with water, in the same manner, O devas, know that the supreme Brahman, Śiva, assumes the forms of whatever is seen or heard in the world, real or unreal. (59-60.)

---

Sanskrit
भेदो जलानां लोकेऽस्मिन्प्रतिभावेऽविचारतः ।
एवमाहुस्तथा चान्ये सर्वे वेदार्थतत्त्वगाः ॥ ६१॥

English
There is difference in the vessels but not in the water that they contain. This is what those who know the real meaning of the Vedas say. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
हृदि संसारिणः साक्षात्सकलः परमेश्वरः ।
इति विज्ञानयुक्तस्य किं तस्य प्रतिमादिभिः ॥ ६२॥

English
"Lord Śiva is within the heart of beings in this world." Of what avail are the idols to those who have this real knowledge? (62.)

---

Sanskrit
इति विज्ञानहीनस्य प्रतिमाकल्पना शुभा ।
पदमुच्चैः समारोढुं पुंसो ह्यालम्बनं स्मृतम् ॥ ६३॥

English
Having an idol is very auspicious for a person who has no such knowledge. It is a ladder that enables him to climb to a higher position. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
आलम्बनं विना तस्य पदमुच्चैः सुदुष्करम् ।
निर्गुणप्राप्तये नॄणां प्रतिमालम्बनं स्मृतम् ॥ ६४॥

English
It is very difficult to climb to a position without a support. The idol is only a means to achieve the Nirguṇa Śiva. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
सगुणान्निर्गुणप्राप्तिर्भवतीति सुनिश्चितम् ।
एवं च सर्वदेवानां प्रतिमा प्रत्ययावहा ॥ ६५॥

English
The attainment of the Nirguṇa through a Saguṇa is certainly possible. In this manner, the symbols of all lords are conducive to a steady faith and belief. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
देवश्चायं महीयान्वै तस्यार्थे पूजनं त्विदम् ।
गन्धचन्दनपुष्पादि किमर्थं प्रतिमां विना ॥ ६६॥

English
This lord is very great and this is the mode of worship of that lord. If there is no idol, of what avail are scents, sandal paste, flowers, etc.? (66.)

---

Sanskrit
तावच्च प्रतिमा पूज्या यावद्विज्ञानसम्भवः ।
ज्ञानाभावे न पूज्येत पतनं तस्य निश्चितम् ॥ ६७॥

English
Till the realisation of true knowledge, the idol shall necessarily be worshipped. If any one does not worship the idol before he attains perfect knowledge, his downfall is sure. (67.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मात्कारणाद्विप्राः श्रूयतां परमार्थतः ।
स्वजात्युक्तं तु यत्कर्म कर्तव्यं तत्प्रयत्नतः ॥ ६८॥

English
O brahmins, hear the true statement of facts. For the same reason as mentioned before, the duties of your own caste shall be performed assiduously. (68.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र यत्र यथा भक्तिः कर्तव्यं पूजनादिकम् ।
विना पूजनदानादि पातकं न च दूरतः ॥ ६९॥

English
Worship shall be performed where devotion is directed. Without worship and charitable gifts, sin cannot be kept at bay. (69.)

---

Sanskrit
यावच्च पातकं देहे तावत्सिद्धिर्न जायते ।
गते च पातके तस्य सर्वं च सफलं भवेत् ॥ ७०॥

English
As long as there is a vestige of sin in the body, achievement need not be expected. When the sin is wiped off, all rites will bear fruit. (70.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा च मलिने वस्त्रे रङ्गः शुभतरो न हि ।
क्षालने हि कृते शुद्धे सर्वो रङ्गः प्रसज्जते ॥ ७१॥

English
If there is dirt in the cloth the dyeing process cannot be carried out effectively. After the cloth is bleached any dye can be applied to it effectively. (71.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा च निर्मले देहे देवानां सम्यगर्चया ।
ज्ञानरङ्गः प्रजायेत तदा विज्ञानसम्भवः ॥ ७२॥

English
Similarly when the body is freed of its dirty stuff by proper worship of deities, the dye of knowledge can stick to it whence true knowledge will arise. (72.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञानस्य च सन्मूलं भक्तिरव्यभिचारिणी ।
ज्ञानस्यापि च सन्मूलं भक्तिरेवाऽभिधीयते ॥ ७३॥

English
The root of true knowledge is unswerving devotion. The root of knowledge too is devotion. (73.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्गत्या गुरुराप्येत गुरोर्मन्त्रादिपूजनम् ।
पूजनाज्जायते भक्तिर्भक्त्या ज्ञानं प्रजायते ॥ ७४॥

English
The root of devotion is good action and the worship of one's own favourite deity. The root of that is the good preceptor. A good preceptor is secured only through association with good people. (74.)

---

English
If one associates with good people, one will come across a preceptor. From the preceptor mantras and the modes of worship can be learned. Bhakti (devotion) is generated by worship and it gives birth to knowledge. (75.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञानं जायते ज्ञानात्परब्रह्मप्रकाशकम् ।
विज्ञानं च यदा जातं तदा भेदो निवर्तते ॥ ७६॥

English
Knowledge leads to perfect knowledge and realisation of the supreme Brahman. When there is perfect knowledge, differentiations cease altogether. (76.)

---

Sanskrit
भेदे निवृत्ते सकले द्वन्द्वदुःखविहीनता ।
द्वन्द्वदुःखविहीनस्तु शिवरूपो भवत्यसौ ॥ ७७॥

English
When differentiation ceases, the misery of mutually clashing opposites vanishes. He who is free from the tangle of opposites and the miseries attendant on them assumes the form of Śiva. (77.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वन्द्वाप्राप्तौ न जायेतां सुखदुःखे विजानतः ।
विहिताविहिते तस्य न स्यातां च सुरर्षयः ॥ ७८॥

English
O celestial sages, when the mutually clashing opposites do not afflict, a person endowed with true knowledge has neither happiness nor misery. Rules of do's and don'ts do not bind him. (78.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशो विरलो लोके गृहाश्रमविवर्जितः ।
यदि लोके भवत्यस्मिन्दर्शनात्पापहारकः ॥ ७९॥

English
Such a person who has not entered a household life is rare to meet with. If there is such a one he will quell all sins by his mere sight. (79.)

---

Sanskrit
तीर्थानि श्लाघयन्तीह तादृशं ज्ञानवित्तमम् ।
देवाश्च मुनयः सर्वे परब्रह्मात्मकं शिवम् ॥ ८०॥

English
Even the holy centres praise such a person of knowledge. Devas and all sages consider him the supreme Brahman, Śiva Himself. (80.)

---

Sanskrit
तादृशानि न तीर्थानि न देवा मृच्छिलामयाः ।
ते पुनन्त्युरुकालेन विज्ञानी दर्शनादपि ॥ ८१॥

English
The holy centres or the deities in the form of clay or rock idols are not equal to him. They take time in sanctifying persons. But a man of true knowledge purifies through his sheer vision. (81.)

---

Sanskrit
यावद्गृहाश्रमे तिष्ठेत्तावदाकारपूजनम् ।
कुर्याच्छ्रेष्ठस्य सुप्रीत्या सुरेषु खलु पञ्चसु ॥ ८२॥

English
As long as he continues the life of a householder he shall perform the worship of the idols of the most excellent of the five deities with pleasure. (82.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा च शिवः पूज्यो मूलमेकं विशिष्यते ।
मूले सिक्ते तथा शाखाः तृप्ताः सत्यखिलाः सुराः ॥ ८३॥

English
Or it is enough if Śiva alone is worshipped. The root is the most important. When the root is watered, O gods, the branches are well-cared for. (83.)

---

Sanskrit
शाखासु च सुतृप्तासु मूलं तृप्तं न कर्हिचित् ।
एवं सर्वेषु तृप्तेषु सुरेषु मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ ८४॥

English
O excellent sages, if the branches are taken care of, it does not necessarily mean that the root is cared for. When the deities are propitiated, the same analogy holds good. (84.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा शिवतृप्तिर्नो विज्ञेया सूक्ष्मबुद्धिभिः ।
शिवे च पूजिते देवाः पूजिताः सर्व एव हि ॥ ८५॥

तस्माच्च पूजयेद्देवं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम् ।
सर्वकामफलावाप्त्यै सर्वभूतहिते रतः ॥ ८६॥

English
Our aim shall be to propitiate Śiva if we are sensible. O gods, if Śiva is worshipped, all the gods are worshipped. Hence a person who wants to do good to all living beings shall worship Śiva, the benefactor of the world, for the attainment of all deires. (85-86.)
