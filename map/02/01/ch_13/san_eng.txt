### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - *(The mode of worshipping Śiva).***

---

Sanskrit
१३

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि पूजाविधिमनुत्तमम् ।
श्रूयतामृषयो देवाःसर्वकामसुखावहम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sages, O devas, listen. Now I shall explain a mode of worship than which there is no better one and which is conducive to the achievement of all happiness and cherished desires. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय संस्मरेत्साम्बकं शिवम् ।
कुर्यात्तत्प्रार्थनां भक्त्या साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तकः ॥ २॥

English
Getting up in the Brāhma Muhūrta within an hour before dawn one shall remember Śiva accompanied by his consort. With palms joined in great devotion and head bent down he shall offer prayers. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ देवेश उत्तिष्ठ हृदयेशय ।
उत्तिष्ठ त्वमुमास्वामिन्ब्रह्माण्डे मङ्गलं कुरु ॥ ३॥

English
O lord of devas, get up, get up. O lord stationed in the heart, get up. O lord of Umā, get up. Confer your auspicious blessings on the entire universe. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
जानामि धर्मं न च मे प्रवृत्ति-
र्जानाम्यधर्मं न च मे निवृत्तिः ।
त्वया महादेव हृदि स्थितेन
यथा नियुक्तोऽस्मि तथा करोमि ॥ ४॥

English
I know what is virtuous, but I am not inclined to work it up. I know what is unrighteous but I am unable to desist from it. O Mahādeva, I do everything as prompted by you, stationed in my heart. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा वचनं भक्त्या स्मृत्वा च गुरुपादके ।
बहिर्गच्छेद्दक्षिणाशां त्यागार्थं मलमूत्रयोः ॥ ५॥

English
After repeating these words of prayer and remembering the sandals of the preceptor he shall go out to the southern direction for answering the calls of nature. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
देहशुद्धिं ततः कृत्वा स मृज्जलविशोधनैः ।
हस्तौ पादौ च प्रक्षाल्य दन्तधावनमाचरेत् ॥ ६॥

English
Cleaning the body thereafter with earth and water and washing his hands and feet he shall clean the teeth. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
दिवानाथे त्वनुदिते कृत्वा वै दन्तधावनम् ।
मुखं षोडशवारं तु प्रक्षाल्याञ्जलिभिस्तथा ॥ ७॥

English
Cleaning of the teeth shall be completed before sunrise. He shall gargle sixteen times with so many mouthfuls of water. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
षष्ठ्याद्यामाश्च तिथयो नवम्यर्कदिने तथा ।
वर्ज्याः सुरर्षयो यत्नाद्भक्तेन रदधावने ॥ ८॥

English
O celestial sages, the Tithis of Śaṣṭhī, navamī as well as new moon days and sundays are forbidden for cleaning the teeth with tooth brush twigs. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
यथावकाशं सुस्नायान्नद्यादिष्वथवा गृहे ।
देशकालाविरुद्धं च स्नानं कार्यं नरेण च ॥ ९॥

English
Bath shall be taken at a convenient time in rivers or in the house itself. No man shall take bath against the conventions of locality or the convenience of the season. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
रवेर्दिने तथा श्राद्धे सङ्क्रान्तौ ग्रहणे तथा ।
महादाने तथा तीर्थे ह्युपवासदिने तथा ॥ १०॥

अशौचेऽप्यथवा प्राप्ते न स्नायादुष्णवारिणा ।
यथा साभिमुखं स्नायात्तीर्थादौ भक्तिमान्नरः ॥ ११॥

English
Hot water bath shall be avoided on sundays, Śrāddha days, Saṅkrānti days, at the times of eclipse, on days of Great Charity and fast, in holy centres and during the days of impurity due to death or birth in the family. In the holy ponds and rivers one shall take bath facing the east with great devotion. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
तैलाभ्यङ्गं च कुर्वीत वारान्दृष्ट्वा क्रमेण च ।
नित्यमभ्यङ्गके चैव वासितं वा न दूषितम् ॥ १२॥

English
Oil bath shall be taken on particular days of the week according to convention in the society. If one is accustomed to take oil bath everyday or if one is using scented oil breaking the convention, it is not faulty. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
श्राद्धे च ग्रहणे चैवोपवासे प्रतिपद्दिने ।
अथवा सार्षपं तैलं न दुष्येद् ग्रहणं विना ॥ १३॥

English
Otherwise one should avoid Śrāddha days, days of eclipse, fast days and the first day of the luner fortnight for oil baths. Except on the days of eclipse mustard oil can be used on other days. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
देशं कालं विचार्य्यैवं स्नानं कुर्याद्यथाविधि ।
उत्तराभिमुखश्चैव प्राङ्मुखोऽप्यथवा पुनः ॥ १४॥

English
Bath shall be taken after due consideration of the place and season duly. He shall face either the north or the east when taking bath. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्छिष्टेनैव वस्त्रेण न स्नायात्स कदाचन ।
शुद्धवस्त्रेण स स्नायात्तद्देवस्मृतिपूर्वकम् ॥ १५॥

English
He shall never take bath wearing another man's clothes. He shall take bath in pure clothes and shall think on his favourite deities. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
परधार्य्यं च नोच्छिष्टं रात्रौ च विधृतं च यत् ।
तेन स्नानं तथा कार्यं क्षालितं च परित्यजेत् ॥ १६॥

English
If he wears during the night another man's clothes, the same are not impure, hence there is no harm in taking bath with those clothes on but after taking bath they must be washed and returned. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तर्पणं च ततः कार्यं देवर्षिपितृतृप्तिदम् ।
धौतवस्त्रं ततो धार्यं पुनराचमनं चरेत् ॥ १७॥

English
After bath he shall perform water libation propitiating gods, sages and the manes. Thereafter washed and dried clothes shall be worn and Ācamana performed again. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शुचौ देशे ततो गत्वा गोमयाद्यपमार्जिते ।
आसनं च शुभं तत्र रचनीयं द्विजोत्तमाः ॥ १८॥

English
In a clean place washed and smeared with cow-dung, the devotee shall take his seat, O Brahmins. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धकाष्ठसमुत्पन्नं पूर्णं स्तरितमेव वा ।
चित्रासनं तथा कुर्यात्सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ १९॥

English
The seat shall be made of wood or a cloth-cover. A seat of diverse colours is conducive to the achievement of all desires. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यथायोग्यं पुनर्ग्राह्यं मृगचर्मादिकं च यत् ।
तत्रोपविश्य कुर्वीत त्रिपुण्ड्रं भस्मना सुधीः ॥ २०॥

English
Or he can have the hide of a deer for a seat. He shall sit on it and apply Tripuṇḍra with the ashes. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
जपस्तपस्तथा दानं त्रिपुण्ड्रात्सफलं भवेत् ।
अभावे भस्मनस्तत्र जलस्यादि प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ २१॥

English
Prayers, penance and charity shall be performed with due markings of Tripuṇḍra on the forehead for sure results. If ashes are not available marking may be done with holy water. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वा त्रिपुण्ड्रं च रुद्राक्षान्धारयेन्नरः ।
सम्पाद्य च स्वकं कर्म पुनराराधयेच्छिवम् ॥ २२॥

English
After marking Tripundra, on the forehead, the devotee shall wear Rudrākṣas. After daily rites are over, he shall begin the worship of Śiva. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनराचमनं कृत्वा त्रिवारं मन्त्रपूर्वकम् ।
एकं वाथ प्रकुर्याच्च गङ्गाबिन्दुरिति ब्रुवन् ॥ २३॥

English
Then he shall perform Ācamana, the sipping of water thrice with the requisite mantras or once, saying that it is a drop of Gaṅgā water. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नोदकं तथा तत्र शिवपूजार्थमाहरेत् ।
अन्यद्वस्तु च यत्किञ्चिद्यथाशक्ति समीपगम् ॥ २४॥

कृत्वा स्थेयं च तत्रैव धैर्यमास्थाय वै पुनः ।
अर्घपात्रं तथा चैकं जलगन्धाक्षतैर्युतम् ॥ २५॥

English
Rice cooked with water shall be brought for the worship of Śiva. Whatever other things he can bring shall also be brought and kept near. A vessel for Arghya with water and scented raw rice grains shall also be brought. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणांसे तथा स्थाप्यमुपचारस्य क्लृप्तये ।
गुरोश्च स्मरणं कृत्वा तदनुज्ञामवाप्य च ॥ २६॥

सङ्कल्पं विधिवत्कृत्वा कामनां च नियुज्य वै ।
पूजयेत्परया भक्त्या शिवं सपरिवारकम् ॥ २७॥

English
To complete the formalities of worship, the vessel shall be placed on the right shoulder. He shall think upon the preceptor and ritualistically take his permission for the worship. He shall perform the rite of Saṅkalpa (including the requisite mantras and statements about the pūjā, the day, month, year etc. and the purpose of the Pūjā) and aver his desire. He shall perform the worship of Śiva with His attendants devoutly. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
मुद्रामेकां प्रदर्श्यैव पूजयेद्विघ्नहारकम् ।
सिन्दुरादिपदार्थैश्च सिद्धिबुद्धिसमन्वितम् ॥ २८॥

लक्षलाभयुतं तत्र पूजयित्वा नमेत्पुनः ।
चतुर्थ्यन्तैर्नामपदैर्नमोऽन्तैः प्रणवादिभिः ॥ २९॥

English
Showing the mystic mudrā and using saffron and other materials he shall bow to and worship Gaṇeśa who confers benefits a hundred thousand times and is accompanied by his consorts Siddhi and Buddhi$^{202}$. He shall repeat his names ending in the dative case appended with Namaḥ and prefixed with Praṇava. (28-29.)

[fn 202. Siddhi and Buddhi are personified as the wives of Gaṇeśa, the son of Śiva and Pārvatī.]
---

Sanskrit
क्षमाप्यैनं तदा देवं भ्रात्रा चैव समन्वितम् ।
पूजयेत्परया भक्त्या नमस्कुर्यात्पुनः पुनः ॥ ३०॥

English
After craving for forgiveness of the deity, he shall be worshipped again in the company of his brother Kārtikeya with great devotion and shall be bowed to again and again. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारपालं सदा द्वारि तिष्ठन्तं च महोदरम् ।
पूजयित्वा ततः पश्चात्पूजयेद्गिरिजां सतीम् ॥ ३१॥

English
The big-bellied Gaṇeśa, the gate-keeper of the lord, shall be worshipped. Goddess Satī, Girijā shall be worshipped then. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्दनैः कुङ्कुमैश्चैव धूपैर्दीपैरनेकशः ।
नैवेद्यैर्विविधैश्चैव पूजयित्वा ततः शिवम् ॥ ३२॥

नमस्कृत्य पुनस्तत्र गच्छेच्च शिवसन्निधौ ।
यदि गेहे पार्थिवीं वा हैमीं वा राजतीं तथा ॥ ३३॥

धातुजन्यां तथैवान्यां पारदां वा प्रकल्पयेत् ।
नमस्कृत्य पुनस्तां च पूजयेद्भक्तितत्परः ॥ ३४॥

तस्यां तु पूजितायां वै सर्वे स्युः पूजितास्तथा ।
स्थापयेच्च मृदा लिङ्गं विधाय विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ ३५॥

English
After worshipping Śiva with sandal paste, saffron, incense, various lamps, and food-offerings of different sorts he shall bow down again. In the house the liṅga shall be made of clay, silver or any other metal or mercury. It shall be bowed to with devotion. If that is worshipped, all deities are worshipped. If the liṅga is made of clay it shall be installed duly. (32-35.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तव्यं सर्वथा तत्र नियमाः स्वगृहे स्थितैः ।
प्राणप्रतिष्ठां कुर्वीत भूतशुद्धिं विधाय च ॥ ३६॥

English
The householders shall perform every rite according to prescribed rules. After performing the purificatory rite of the Bhūtas, the installation of the idol shall be performed. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
दिक्पालान्पूजयेत्तत्र स्थापयित्वा शिवालये ।
गृहे शिवः सदा पूज्यो मूलमन्त्राभियोगतः ॥ ३७॥

तत्र तु द्वारपालानां नियमो नास्ति सर्वथा ।
गृहे लिङ्गं च यत्पूज्यं तस्मिन्सर्वं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ ३८॥

English
If the worship is performed in the temple of Śiva, the guardians of the quarters shall be installed and worshipped. In the house, Śiva shall be worshipped by the root mantra. It is not obligatory that the gatekeeper shall be worshipped. The linga that is worshipped by me can be worshipped in the house. Everything is installed in the same. (37-38.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजाकाले च साङ्गं वै परिवारेण संयुतम् ।
आवाह्य पूजयेद्देवं नियमोऽत्र न विद्यते ॥ ३९॥

English
At the time of worship, the lord shall be invoked along with his attendants and paraphernalia. But there is no hard and fast rule governing this aspect. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्य सन्निधिं कृत्वा स्वासनं परिकल्पयेत् ।
उदङ्मुखस्तदा स्थित्वा पुनराचमनं चरेत् ॥ ४०॥

English
He shall provide his own seat in the vicinity of Śiva. He shall face the north and perform the rite of Ācamana (sipping water). (40.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रक्षाल्य हस्तौ पश्चाद्वै प्राणायामं प्रकल्पयेत् ।
मूलमन्त्रेण तत्रैव दशावर्तं नयेन्नरः ॥ ४१॥

English
The devotee shall wash his hands and feet and perform Prāṇāyāma ten times with Mūlamantra. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चमुद्राः प्रकर्तव्याः पूजावश्यं करे स्थिता ।
एता मुद्राः प्रदर्श्यैव चरेत्पूजाविधिं नरः ॥ ४२॥

English
Five mystic Mudrās shall be shown with the hand before the worship. Only after showing the Mudrās shall the worship be performed. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
दीपं कृत्वा तदा तत्र नमस्कारं गुरोरथ ।
बध्वा पद्मासनं तत्र भद्रासनमथापि वा ॥ ४३॥

उत्तानासनकं कृत्वा पर्यङ्कासनकं तथा ।
यथासुखं तथा स्थित्वा प्रयोगं पुनरेव च ॥ ४४॥

कृत्वा पूजां पुरा जातां वट्टकेनैव तारयेत् ।
यदि वा स्वयमेवेह गृहे न नियमोऽस्ति च ॥ ४५॥

English
The lamp shall be shown then. Homage shall be paid to the preceptor. He shall then seat himself in the yogic poses of Padma, Bhadra, Uttāna or Paryāṅka whichever is convenient and perform the rites once again. After the worship he shall float it along with the cake. If the worship is performed in the house these rules are not binding. (43-45.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चाच्चैवार्घपात्रेण क्षालयेल्लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ।
अनन्यमानसो भूत्वा पूजाद्रव्यं निधाय च ॥ ४६॥

English
Afterwards the excellent linga shall be washed with the water from the vessel of Arghya itself after keeping all the material with the concentrated mind. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चाच्चावाहयेद्देवं मन्त्रेणानेन वै नरः ।
कैलासशिखरस्थं च पार्वतीपतिमुत्तमम् ॥ ४७॥

यथोक्तरूपिणं शम्भुं निर्गुणं गुणरूपिणम् ।
पञ्चवक्त्रं दशभुजं त्रिनेत्रं वृषभध्वजम् ॥ ४८॥

कर्पूरगौरं दिव्याङ्गं चन्द्रमौलिं कपर्दिनम् ।
व्याघ्रचर्मोत्तरीयं च गजचर्माम्बरं शुभम् ॥ ४९॥

वासुक्यादिपरीताङ्गं पिनाकाद्यायुधान्वितम् ।
सिद्धयोऽष्टौ च यस्याग्रे नृत्यन्तीह निरन्तरम् ॥ ५०॥

जयजयेति शब्दैश्च सेवितं भक्तपुञ्जकैः ।
तेजसा दुःसहेनैव दुर्लक्ष्यं देवसेवितम् ॥ ५१॥

शरण्यं सर्वसत्त्वानां प्रसन्नमुखपङ्कजम् ।
वेदैः शास्त्रैर्यथागीतं विष्णुब्रह्मनुतं सदा ॥ ५२॥

भक्तवत्सलमानन्दं शिवमावाहयाम्यहम् ।
एवं ध्यात्वा शिवं साम्बमासनं परिकल्पयेत् ॥ ५३॥

English
The lord shall be invoked then with the following mantra. "I am invoking Śiva, the blissful and favourably disposed to the devotees, Śiva seated on the summit of Kailāsa, the excellent lord of Pārvatī, Śambhu of the form as mentioned before, both with or without qualities possessed of five faces, ten hands, three eyes and the bull for banner, as white as camphor, of divine limbs, having crescent moon on the head, wearing matted hair, clad in the hide of an elephant and with the hide of the tiger as upper garment, with Vāsuki and other serpents turned round his body, holding Pināka and other weapons, having the eight Siddhis$^{203}$ (accomplishments) dancing constantly in front of Him, served by crowds of devotees crying loudly “Be victorious. Be victorious.” of unbearable sight due to excessive splendour, served by all devas, the sole refuge for all living beings, of beaming face shining like lotus and always eulogised by Viṣṇu and Brahmā as extolled by the Vedas and sacred texts.” After the meditation of Śiva along with his consort, the seat shall be arranged for.
