### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - *(Directions for the worship of Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
१४

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
व्यासशिष्य महाभाग कथय त्वं प्रमाणतः ।
कैः पुष्पैः पूजितः शम्भुः किं किं यच्छति वै फलम् ॥ १॥

English
*The sages said :—*  O disciple of Vyāsa, O fortunate one, please explain to us authoritatively the fruits granted by Śiva for the different worships with different flowers. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
शौनकाद्याश्च ऋषयः श‍ृणुतादरतोऽखिलम् ॥

कथयाम्यद्य सुप्रीत्या पुष्पार्पणविनिर्णयम् । २॥

एष एव विधिः पृष्टो नारदेन महर्षिणा ।
प्रोवाच परमप्रीत्या पुष्पार्पणविनिर्णयम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Śūla said :—*  O sages, Śaunaka and others, please listen attentively. I shall lovingly explain to you the mode of offering flowers which is the same as Brahmā explained to Nārada at his request. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
कमलैर्बिल्वपत्रैश्च शतपत्रैस्तथा पुनः ।
शङ्खपुष्पैस्तथा देवं लक्ष्मीकामोऽर्चयेच्छिवम् ॥ ४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  A person desirous of wealth shall worship lord Śiva with lotuses, Bilva leaves, petals of lotuses or with Śaṅkha flowers. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एतैश्च लक्षसङ्ख्याकैः पूजितश्चेद्भवेच्छिवः ।
पापहानिस्तथा विप्र लक्ष्मीः स्यान्नात्र संशयः ॥ ५॥

English
O Brahmin, if a devotee worships Śiva with a hundred flowers, his sins shall be wiped off and the devotee shall become rich. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
विंशतिः कमलानां तु प्रस्थमेकमुदाहृतम् ।
बिल्वो दलसहस्रेण प्रस्थार्धं परिभाषितम् ॥ ६॥

English
Twenty full lotuses constitute one prastha measure. A Thousand Bilva leaves constitute half a Prastha. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शतपत्रसहस्रेण प्रस्थार्धं परिभाषितम् ।
पलैः षोडशभिः प्रस्थः पलं टङ्कदशस्मृतः ॥ ७॥

English
Petals of lotuses, a thousand in number constitute half a prastha. Ten Tāṅka weight constitutes one pala and sixteen palas make one prastha. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेनैव तु मानेन तुलामारोपयेद्यदा ।
सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति निष्कामश्चेच्छिवो भवेत् ॥ ८॥

English
Flowers for worship shall be weighed in the balance according to this calculation. The worship thus duly performed shall accord all cherished desires. If the devotee worships with no specific desires he will become Śiva himself. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
राज्यस्य कामुको यो वै पार्थिवानां च पूजया ।
तोषयेच्छङ्करं देवं दशकोट्या मुनीश्वराः ॥ ९॥

लिङ्गं शिवं तथा पुष्पमखण्डं तण्डुलं तथा ।
चर्चितं चन्दनेनैव जलधारां तथा पुनः ॥ १०॥

प्रतिरूपं तथा मन्त्रं बिल्वीदलमनुत्तमम् ।
अथवा शतपत्रं च कमलं वा तथा पुनः ॥ ११॥

शङ्खपुष्पैस्तथा प्रोक्तं विशेषेण पुरातनैः ।
सर्वकामफलं दिव्यं परत्रेहापि सर्वथा ॥ १२॥

धूपं दीपं च नैवेद्यमर्घं चारार्तिकं तथा ।
प्रदक्षिणां नमस्कारं क्षमापनविसर्जने ॥ १३॥

कृत्वा साङ्गं तथा भोज्यं कृतं येन भवेदिह ।
तस्य वै सर्वथा राज्यं शङ्करः प्रददाति च ॥ १४॥

English
O lordly sages, a person desirous of obtaining a kingdom shall propitiate Lord Śiva with the worship of a hundred million earthen liṅgas. Lord Śiva confers a kingdom on the devotee certainly. He shall use Śivaliṅga for worship. Flowers shall be used. Unsplit rice grains mixed with sandal paste shall be used. The ceremonial ablution shall be performed. The manṛta used shall be pleasing. Bilva leaves are very excellent. Or he can use loose petals or full lotuses or Śaṅkha flowers according to ancient authorities. The worship is divine and accords pleasures and achievement of desires both here and hereafter. He shall not omit other items such as incense, lamps, food-offerings, Arghya, Ārārtika (waving of lights), Pradakṣiṇā, Namaskāra, Kṣamāpana (craving forgiveness and Visarjana) the ritualistic dismissal). At the end he shall feed other devotees. (9-14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधान्यकामुको यो वै तदर्धेनार्चयेत्पुमान् ।
कारागृहगतो यो वै लक्षेनैवार्चयेद्धरम् ॥ १५॥

English
A person who yearns for important positions shall worship half the former number. A person desiring release from prison shall worship a hundred thousand liṅgas of Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रोगग्रस्तो यदा स्याद्वै तदर्धेनार्चयेच्छिवम् ।
कन्याकामो भवेद्यो वै तदर्धेन शिवं पुनः ॥ १६॥

English
A person afflicted by ailments shall worship half that number. A person desiring a daughter shall worship half that number. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्याकामस्तथा यः स्यात्तदर्धेनार्चयेच्छिवम् ।
वाणीकामो भवेद्यो वै घृतेनैवार्चयेच्छिवम् ॥ १७॥

English
A person desirous of learning shall worship half that number. A person desirous of eloquence shall worship Śiva with ghee. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्चाटनार्थं शत्रूणां तन्मितेनैव पूजनम् ।
मारणे वै तु लक्षेण मोहने तु तदर्धतः ॥ १८॥

English
In order to drive out enemies, the number of worship is the same as before. For exterminating enemies, worship is for a hundred thousand times and for enchantment worship is half that number. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सामन्तानां जये चैव कोटिपूजा प्रशस्यते ।
राज्ञामयुतसङ्ख्यं च वशीकरणकर्मणि ॥ १९॥

English
For the conquest of vassal kings, worship for ten million times is recommended. or keeping vassal kings under influence the same for ten thousand times is recommended. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यशसे च तथा सङ्ख्या वाहनाद्यैः सहस्रिका ।
मुक्तिकामोऽर्चयेच्छम्भुं पञ्चकोट्या सुभक्तितः ॥ २०॥

English
For achieving glory with plenty of vehicles, worship for a thousand times shall be performed. A person desiring salvation shall worship Śiva five crores of times with deep devotion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानार्थी पूजयेत्कोट्या शङ्करं लोक शङ्करम् ।
शिवदर्शनकामो वै तदर्धेन प्रपूजयेत् ॥ २१॥

English
A person seeking knowledge shall worship Śiva, the benefactor of the world, ten million times. A person desiring Śiva's vision shall worship Him five million times. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा मृत्युञ्जयो जाप्यः कामनाफलरूपतः ।
पञ्चलक्षा जपा यर्हि प्रत्यक्षं तु भवेच्छिवः ॥ २२॥

English
The Mṛtyuñjaya mantra shall be repeated half a million times when Śiva shall be visible to the devotee and fulfil his desires. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
लक्षेण भजते कश्चिद् द्वितीये जातिसम्भवः ।
तृतीये कामनालाभश्चतुर्थे तं प्रपश्यति ॥ २३॥

English
If a person repeats the mantra a hundred thousand times and begins a second instalment he will be lifted to a higher caste. When he completes the third hundred thousand times all his worldly desires will be fulfilled. In the fourth Lakṣa he will be able to see the lord. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चमं च यदा लक्षं फलं यच्छत्यसंशयम् ।
अनेनैव तु मन्त्रेण दशलक्षे फलं भवेत् ॥ २४॥

English
When the fifty Lakṣa is completed, the lord will confer on him all benefits. When the same mantra is repeated a million times, the merit is tremendous. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्तिकामो भवेद्यो वै दर्भैश्च पूजनं चरेत् ।
लक्षसङ्ख्या तु सर्वत्र ज्ञातव्या ऋषिसत्तम ॥ २५॥

English
A person desirous of liberation shall worship him with Darbhas. O best of sages, the number everywhere is a hundred thousand times. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
आयुःकामो भवेद्यो वै दूर्वाभिः पूजनं चरेत् ।
पुत्रकामो भवेद्यो वै धत्तूरकुसुमैश्चरेत् ॥ २६॥

English
A person desirous of long life shall worship him with Dūrvā grass. A person desirous of sons shall worship him with Dhattūra flowers. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्तदण्डश्च धत्तूरः पूजने शुभदः स्मृतः ।
अगस्त्यकुसुमैश्चैव पूजकस्य महद्यशः ॥ २७॥

English
A Dhattūra plant with red stem is specially auspicious for worship. A worshipper using Agastya flowers will earn great fame. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
भुक्तिमुक्तिफलं तस्य तुलस्या पूजयेद्यदि ।
अर्कपुष्पैः प्रतापश्च कुब्जकह्लारकैस्तथा ॥ २८॥

English
Worldly pleasures and salvation will be secured by a person who worships with Tulasi. Great valour can be secured by worshipping with Arka or Kubjakalhāra flowers. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
जपाकुसुमपूजा तु शत्रूणां मृत्युदा स्मृता ।
रोगोच्चाटनकानीह करवीराणि वै क्रमात् ॥ २९॥

English
The worship with Japā flowers (China rose) brings about the death of enemies. Karavīra flowers drive away all ailments. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
बन्धुकैर्भूषणावाप्तिर्जात्या वाहान्न संशयः ।
अतसीपुष्पकैर्देवं विष्णुवल्लभतामियात् ॥॥ ३०॥

English
By worshipping with Bandhūka flowers the devotee will get ornaments; with Jāti flowers he will acquire good vehicles; with Atasi flowers he will attain favour of Viṣṇu. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शमीपत्रैस्तथा मुक्तिः प्राप्यते पुरुषेण च ।
मल्लिकाकुसुमैर्दत्तैः स्त्रियं शुभतरां शिवः ॥ ३१॥

English
With Śamī leaves he will secure salvation. With Mallikā flowers he will secure an auspicious woman. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
यूथिकाकुसुमैः शस्तैर्गृहं नैव विमुच्यते ।
कर्णिकारैस्तथा वस्त्रसम्पत्तिर्जायते नृणाम् ॥ ३२॥

English
With the splendid Yūthikā flowers he will not be deprived of a house. With Karṇikāra flowers he will secure plenty of garments. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गुण्डीकुसुमैर्लोके मनो निर्मलतां व्रजेत् ।
विल्वपत्रैस्तथा लक्षैः सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ ३३॥

English
With Nirguṇḍi flowers, his mind becomes pure in the world. A hundred thousand Bilva leaves used for worship will secure the fulfilment of all desires. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृङ्गारहारपुष्पैस्तु वर्धते सुखसम्पदा ।
ऋतुजातानि पुष्पाणि मुक्तिदानि न संशयः ॥ ३४॥

English
Use of lovely flowers in the form of garlands increases happiness and wealth. Use of seasonal flowers for worship yields liberation. There is no doubt in this. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
राजिकाकुसुमानीह शत्रूणां मृत्युदानि च ।
एषां लक्षं शिवे दद्याद्दद्याच्च विपुलं फलम् ॥ ३५॥

English
The flowers of Rājikā bring about the death of enemies. A hundred thousand Rājikā flowers shall be used for the worship of Śiva. The benefit accruing will be very great. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्यते कुसुमं तन्न यन्नैव शिववल्लभम् ।
चम्पकं केतकं हित्वा त्वन्यत्सर्वं समर्पयेत् ॥ ३६॥

English
Excepting the Campaka and the Ketaka there is no flower which does not appeal to Śiva. All other flowers can be used for worshipping Him. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः परं च धान्यानां पूजने शङ्करस्य च ।
प्रमाणं च फलं सर्वं प्रीत्या श‍ृणु च सत्तम ॥ ३७॥

English
Now, O excellent one, listen to the quantity of and the benefit accruing from grains and pulses in their use for worship of Śiva. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्दुलारोपणे नृणां लक्ष्मी वृद्धिः प्रजायते ।
अखण्डितविधौ विप्र सम्यग्भक्त्या शिवोपरि ॥ ३८॥

षट्केनैव तु प्रस्थानां तदर्धेन तथा पुनः ।
पलद्वयं तथा लक्षमानेन समुदाहृतम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Heaping up rice grains by way of worship causes prosperity. Six and a half prastha, and two palas of rice grains constitute a hundred thousand in number of grains. These shall be used in their unsplit form for the worship of Śiva. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजां रुद्रप्रधानेन कृत्वा वस्त्रं सुसुन्दरम् ।
शिवोपरि न्यसेत्तत्र तन्दुलार्पणमुत्तमम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Worship of Rudra shall be performed at first and a fine cloth shall be spread over the linga. The rice grains shall be put over the cloth at the time of worship. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
उपरि श्रीफलं त्वेकं गन्धपुष्पादिभिस्तथा ।
रोपयित्वा च धूपादि कृत्वा पूजाफलं भवेत् ॥॥ ४१॥

English
At the end of worship, a coconut fruit shall be placed with scents and flowers etc. and fumigated with incense. The devotee shall attain the benefit of worship. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजापत्यद्वयं रौप्यमाषसङ्ख्या च दक्षिणा ।
देया तदुपदेष्ट्रे हि शक्त्या वा दक्षिणा मता ॥ ४२॥

English
Silver coins and black gram shall be given as fee to the priest as much as for two Prājāpatya ceremonies. If the devotee cannot afford it he shall give according to his capacity. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
आदित्यसङ्ख्यया तत्र ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्ततः ।
लक्षपूजा तथा जाता साङ्गञ्च मन्त्रपूर्वकम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Thereafter twelve brahmins shall be fed. The whole of this then constitutes the Lakṣapūjā complete in its details and with requisite mantras. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
शतमष्टोत्तरं तत्र मन्त्रे विधिरुदाहृतः ।
तिलानां च पलं लक्षं महापातकनाशनम् ॥ ४४॥

एकादशपलैरेव लक्षमानमुदाहृतम् ।
पूर्ववत्पूजनं तत्र कर्तव्यं हितकाम्यया ॥ ४५॥

भोज्या वै ब्राह्मणास्तस्मादत्र कार्या नरेण हि ।
महापातकजं दुःखं तत्क्षणान्नश्यति ध्रुवम् ॥ ४६॥

English
The mantras shall be repeated hundred and eight times. That is the rule. A hundred thousand gingelly seeds used for worship destroy even great sins. Eleven Palas of gingelly seeds constitute a hundred thousand in number. The mode of worship is the same as before. Those who desire beneficent results shall perform the Pūjā. Brahmins shall be fed. Hence, only those who can afford shall perform this. Certainly all miseries due to great sins perish instantaneously. (44-46.)

---

Sanskrit
यवपूजा तथा प्रोक्ता लक्षेण परमा शिवे ।
प्रस्थानामष्टकं चैव तथा प्रस्थार्धकं पुनः ॥ ४७॥

पलद्वययुतं तत्र मानमेतत्पुरातनम् ।
यवपूजा च मुनिभिः स्वर्गसौख्यविवर्धिनी ॥ ४८॥

English
Performance of the worship of Śiva with a hundred thousand barley grains is highly efficacious. Eight and a half Prasthas and two Palas of barley grains constitute a hundred thousand in number according to ancient calculation. The worship with barley grains, the sages say, increases heavenly pleasures. (47-48.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राजापत्यं ब्राह्मणानां कर्तव्यं च फलेप्सुभिः ।
गोधूमान्नैस्तथा पूजा प्रशस्ता शङ्करस्य वै ॥ ४९॥

सन्ततिर्वर्धते तस्य यदि लक्षावधिः कृता ।
द्रोणार्धेन भवेल्लक्षं विधानं विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Brahmins desiring the benefit shall perform the rite of Prājāpatya. The worship of Śiva with wheat grains is highly praiseworthy. If a hundred thousand grains are used for worship, the devotee shall be blessed with a number of children. Half a Droṇa of wheat will constitute a hundred thousand in number of grains. The mode of worship is as before. (49-50.)

---

Sanskrit
मुद्गानां पूजने देवः शिवो यच्छति वै सुखम् ।
प्रस्थानां सप्तकेनैव प्रस्थार्धेनाथवा पुनः ॥ ५१॥

पलद्वययुतेनैव लक्षमुक्तं पुरातनैः ।
ब्राह्मणाश्च तथा भोज्या रुद्रसङ्ख्याप्रमाणतः ॥ ५२॥

English
Śiva accords happiness on being worshipped with green grams. Seven Prasthas and two Palas to seven and a half Prasthas of green grams constitute a hundred thousand in number. Eleven brahmins shall be fed. (51-52.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रियङ्गुपूजनादेव धर्माध्यक्षे परात्मनि ।
धर्मार्थकामा वर्धन्ते पूजा सर्वसुखावहा ॥ ५३॥

प्रस्थैकेन च तस्योक्तं लक्षमेकं पुरातनैः ।
