### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - *(The manifestation of Rudra)***

---

Sanskrit
१५

नारद उवाच ।
विधे विधे महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं सुरसत्तम ।
श्राविताद्याद्भुता शैवकथा परमपावनी ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O creator, O Brahmā the fortunate, you are blessed O foremost among Devas. A wonderfully sanctifying story of Śiva has been narrated by you, today. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राद्भुता महादिव्या लिङ्गोत्पत्तिः श्रुता शुभा ।
श्रुत्वा यस्याः प्रभावं च दुःखनाशो भवेदिह ॥ २॥

English
I have heard the wonderfully divine story of the origin of the Liṅga, the auspicious hearing of the efficacy of which destroys all miseries here. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्तरं च यज्जातं माहात्म्यं चरितं तथा ।
सृष्टेश्चैव प्रकारं च कथय त्वं विशेषतः ॥ ३॥

English
Please narrate what transpired thereafter, the grandeur of the created things and particularly the mode of creation. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
सम्यक् पृष्टं च भवता यज्जातं तदनन्तरम् ।
कथयिष्यामि सङ्क्षेपाद्यथा पूर्वं श्रुतं मया ॥ ४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  You have requested very pertinently. I shall briefly narrate what transpired later as I have heard before. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्हिते तदा देवे शिवरूपे सनातने ।
अहं विष्णुश्च विप्रेन्द्र अधिकं सुखमाप्तवान् ॥ ५॥

मया च विष्णुना रूपं हंसवाराहयोस्तदा ।
संवृतं तु ततस्ताभ्यां लोकसर्गावनेच्छया ॥ ६॥

English
When the eternal lord Śiva vanished, O chief of brahmins, Viṣṇu and I in a very happy mood withdrew our forms of Swan and Boar and wished for creation and sustenance of the worlds. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
विधे ब्रह्मन् महाप्राज्ञ संशयो हृदि मे महान् ।
कृपां कृत्वातुलां शीघ्रं तं नाशयितुमर्हसि ॥ ७॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Vidhi, O Brahmā, O wise one, I have a great doubt. Please remove the same. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हंसवाराहयो रूपं युवाभ्यां च धृतं कथम् ।
अन्यद्रूपं विहायैव किमत्र वद कारणम् ॥ ८॥

English
How is it that both of you assumed the forms of Swan and Boar instead of other forms? Please tell me the reason for the same. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येतद्वचनं श्रुत्वा नारदस्य महात्मनः ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं ब्रह्मा सादरमब्रवीत् ॥ ९॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing these words of the noble-souled Nārada, Brahmā spoke after remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
हंसस्य चोर्ध्वगमने गतिर्भवति निश्चला ।
तत्त्वातत्त्वविवेकोऽस्ति जलदुग्धविभागवत् ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  The swan has the power of going up steadily. It has the power of discriminating between the real and the unreal as in separating milk from water. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अज्ञानज्ञानयोस्तत्त्वं विवेचयति हंसकः ।
हंसरूपं धृतं तेन ब्रह्मणा सृष्टिकारिणा ॥ ११॥

English
The swan understands the distinction between ignorance and knowledge. Hence I (Brahmā) the Creator, assumed the form of Swan. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
विवेको नैव लब्धश्च यतो हंसो व्यलीयत ।
शिवस्वरूपतत्त्वस्य ज्योतिरूपस्य नारद ॥ १२॥

English
O Nārada ! But I failed to cognize the refulgent form of Śiva and therefore could not exercise my power of discrimination. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिप्रवृत्तिकामस्य कथं ज्ञानं प्रजायते ।
यतो लब्धो विवेकोऽपि न मया हंसरूपिणा ॥ १३॥

English
How can real knowledge dawn on one who is engaged in activities of creation ? Hence though in the form of Swan I could not attain the power of discrimination. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गमनेऽधो वराहस्य गतिर्भवति निश्चला ।
धृतं वाराहरूपं हि विष्णुना वनचारिणा ॥ १४॥

English
A boar has the power of steadily going deep below. Hence Viṣṇu, the wanderer in the forest, assumed the form of the boar. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा भवकल्पार्थं तद्रूपं हि प्रकल्पितम् ।
विष्णुना च वराहस्य भुवनावनकारिणा ॥ १५॥

English
Or Viṣṇu, the protector of all the worlds assumed the form of a Boar to start a new Kalpa (Aeon). (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्दिनं हि समारभ्य तद्रूपं धृतवान्हरिः ।
तद्दिनं प्रतिकल्पोऽसौ कल्पो वाराहसंज्ञकः ॥ १६॥

English
Since the day he assumed the form of a Boar, the aeon by the title of Vārāha has started. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिच्छा वा यदा जाता तस्य रूपस्य धारणे ।
तद्दिनं प्रतिकल्पोऽसौ कल्पो वाराहसंज्ञकः ॥ १७॥

English
Or the Vārāhakalpa can be considered to have started since the day we two decided to assume these forms. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रश्नोत्तरं दत्तं प्रस्तुतं श‍ृणु नारद ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं वक्ष्ये सृष्टिविधिं मुने ॥ १८॥

English
O Nārada, thus I have answered your relevant question. O sage, now listen. I shall resume the context. Remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva I shall explain to you the mode of Creation. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्हिते महादेवे त्वहं लोकपितामहः ।
तदीयं वचनं कर्तुमध्यायन्ध्यानतत्परः ॥ १९॥

English
When God Śiva vanished, I, Pitāmaha (grandfather) of the worlds fell into contemplation pondering on the means of carrying out His words of direction. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्कृत्य तदा शम्भुं ज्ञानं प्राप्य हरेस्तदा ।
आनन्दं परमं गत्वा सृष्टिं कर्तुं मनो दधे ॥ २०॥

English
Then after bowing down to Śiva, getting knowledge from Viṣṇu and attaining the highest bliss, I decided to start the work of creation. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुश्चापि तदा तत्र प्रणिपत्य सदाशिवम् ।
उपदिश्य च मां तात ह्यन्तर्धानमुपागतः ॥ २१॥

English
After bowing to Śiva and instructing me, O dear one, Viṣṇu too vanished. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

वैकुण्ठनगरं गत्वा तत्रोवास हरिः सदा ॥ २२॥

English
After getting the blessings of Śiva and going out of the cosmic egg, Viṣṇu made Vaikuṇṭha$^{207}$ his permanent abode. (22.)

[fn 207. It is Viṣṇu’s abode variously described as situated in the Nothern ocean or on the eastern peak of mount Meru.]

---

Sanskrit
अहं स्मृत्वा शिवं तत्र विष्णुं वै सृष्टिकाम्यया ।
पूर्वं सृष्टं जलं यच्च तत्राञ्जलिमुदाक्षिपम् ॥ २३॥

English
Desiring to create, I remembered Śiva and Viṣṇu. In the waters that had already been created I offered handfuls of water as libation. (23.)

---

Sanskrit

विराड्रूपमभूद्विप्र जलरूपमपश्यतः ॥ २४॥

English
Then the cosmic egg arose consisting of twenty-four Principles$^{208}$. O brahmin, then a splendid, huge form Virāṭ appeared and the form of waters was not seen. (24.)

[fn 208. According to the Paurāṇic account of creation, the cosmic Egg constituted of twenty-four tattvas was entirely material. In the beginning, it was a dead egg and it remained so until it was activated by the principle of Brahmā which having entered into it split the egg into two halves by the process of fission.]

English

Then again at the bidding of Lord Śiva the Bhūtādika Sarga (creation of the elements etc.) appeared. Thus five types of creation collectively called Vaikṛta were set in motion by me. (44.)

Sanskrit

English
Brahmā evolved three types of creation from Prakṛti. The first one was the creation of Mahat (the cosmic principle of intellect.) The second was that of the subtle elements. The third was Vaikārika of the nature of transformations and ramifications). Thus with five Vaikṛta types and three later Prākṛtas there were eight types of creation. (45-46.)
