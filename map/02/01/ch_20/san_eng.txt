### **CHAPTER TWENTY - (*Śiva goes to Kailāsa*)**

---

Sanskrit
२०

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
नारद त्वं श‍ृणु मुने शिवागमनमुत्तमम् ।
कैलासे पर्वतश्रेष्ठे कुबेरस्य तपोबलात् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O Nārada, hear the story of Śiva’s arrival at Kailāsa, the best of mountains, thanks to the power of penance of Kubera. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
निधिपत्ववरं दत्त्वा गत्वा स्वस्थानमुत्तमम् ।
विचिन्त्य हृदि विश्वेशः कुबेरवरदायकः ॥ २॥

English
“The lord of the Universe, after bestowing the boon of lordship of treasures upon Kubera, returned to His excellent abode and thought within Himself thus. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
विध्यङ्गजस्स्वरूपो मे पूर्णः प्रलयकार्यकृत् ।
तद्रूपेण गमिष्यामि कैलासं गुह्यकालयम् ॥ ३॥

English
My complete manifestation, born of Brahma can look after the activity of Dissolution. Now, assuming that form I shall go to Kailāsa, the residence of Guhyakas. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रो हृदयजो मे हि पूर्णांशो ब्रह्म निष्कलः ।
हरिब्रह्मादिभिः सेव्यो मदभिन्नो निरञ्जनः ॥ ४॥

English
Rudra, born of my heart, my perfect manifestation is the Single Supreme Brahman. He is worthy of being served by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others. He is not different from me. He is unsullied. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्स्वरूपेण तत्रैव सुहृद्भूत्वा विलास्यहम् ।
कुबेरस्य च वत्स्यामि करिष्यामि तपो महत् ॥ ५॥

English
In that form I shall become that friend of Kubera$^{231}$, shall remain near him and perform great penance.” (5.)

[fn 231. Kubera or Kuvera. He is the God of wealth and the chief of the Yakṣas and Guhyakas. His name Ku-bera or Ku-vera signifies his deformed body having three legs and eight teeth. He is married to Yakṣī, the daughter of the Dānava Mura! As an especial friend of Śiva he is called Śiva-sakhā. His capital Alakā on the Himālaya mountain is mentioned also in the RV.]
---

Sanskrit
इति सञ्चिन्त्य रुद्रोऽसौ शिवेच्छां गन्तुमुत्सुकः ।
ननाद तत्र ढक्कां स्वां सुगतिं नादरूपिणीम् ॥ ६॥

English
Thinking thus, Rudra, desirous of carrying out the wish of Śiva (the supreme Brahman) sounded his drum that gave out the divine Nāda. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रैलोक्यामानशे तस्या ध्वनिरुत्साहकारकः ।
आह्वानगतिसंयुक्तो विचित्रः सान्द्रशब्दकः ॥ ७॥

English
Its resonant, reverberating sound pervaded the three worlds heightening enthusiasm and called upon everyone in diverse ways. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा विष्णुब्रह्माद्याः सुराश्च मुनयस्तथा ।
आगमा निगमा मूर्ताः सिद्धा जग्मुश्च तत्र वै ॥ ८॥

English
On hearing that, Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other deities, sages, the persons well-versed in Āgamas, Nigamas and Siddhas. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरासुराद्याः सकलास्तत्र जग्मुश्च सोत्सवाः ।
सर्वेऽपि प्रमथा जग्मुर्यत्र कुत्रापि संस्थिताः ॥ ९॥

English
Devas and Asuras came there with great delight. The Pramathas* too reached that place from different quarters. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
गणपाश्च महाभागाः सर्वलोकनमस्कृताः ।
तेषां सङ्ख्यामहं वच्मि सावधानतया श‍ृणु ॥ १०॥

English
The leaders of Gaṇas revered by the whole world and of high fortune arrived there. I shall mention to you their number. Listen attentively. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्ययाच्छङ्खकर्णश्च गणकोट्या गणेश्वरः ।
दशभिः केकराक्षश्च विकृतोऽष्टाभिरेव च ॥ ११॥

English
The leader of the Gaṇas, Śaṅkhakarṇa came there with a crore of his Gaṇas; Kekarākṣa with ten crores and Vikṛta with eight crores. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुः षष्ट्या विशाखश्च नवभिः पारियात्रकः ।
षड्भिः सर्वान्तकः श्रीमान्दुन्दुभोऽष्टाभिरेव च ॥ १२॥

English
Viśākha with sixty-four crores; Pāriyātraka with nine crores, Sarvāntaka with six crores and the glorious Dunduma with eight crores. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
जालङ्को हि द्वादशभिः कोटिभिर्गणपुङ्गवः ।
सप्तभिः समदः श्रीमाँस्तथैव विकृताननः ॥ १३॥

English
Jālaṅka, the chief leader of Gaṇas, with twelve crores; the glorious Madana and Vikṛtānana with seven crores each. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चभिश्च कपाली हि षड्भिः सन्दारकः शुभः ।
कोटिकोटिभिरेवेह कण्डुकः कुण्डकस्तथा ॥ १४॥

English
Kapālin with five crores, the auspicious Sandāraka with six crores and Kaṇḍuka and Kuṇḍaka each with a crore. (14.)

---

English
Viṣṭambha and Candratāpana each with eight crores, the leader of Gaṇas Mahākeśa with a thousand crores. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्टम्भोऽष्टाभिरगमदष्टभिश्चन्द्रतापनः । १५॥

महाकेशः सहस्रेण कोटीनां गणपो वृतः । १६॥

कुण्डी द्वादशभिर्वाहस्तथा पर्वतकः शुभः ।
कालश्च कालकश्चैव महाकालः शतेन वै ॥ १७॥

English
Kuṇḍin, Vāha and the auspicious Parvataka with twelve crores each, Kāla, Kālaka and Mahākāla each with a hundred crores. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निकः शतकोट्या वै कोट्याभिमुख एव च ।
आदित्यमूर्धा कोट्या च तथा चैव धनावहः ॥ १८॥

English
Agnika with a hundred crores, Abhimukha with a crore, Ādityamūrdhā and Dhanāvaha each with a crore. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्नाहश्च शतेनैव कुमुदः कोटिभिस्तथा ।
अमोघः कोकिलश्चैव कोटिकोट्या सुमन्त्रकः ॥ १९॥

English
Sannāha and Kumuda with a hundred crores, Amogha, Kokila and Sumantraka each with a crore. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
काकपादोऽपरः षष्ट्या षष्ट्या सन्तानकः प्रभुः ।
महाबलश्च नवभिर्मधुपिङ्गश्च पिङ्गलः ॥ २०॥

English
Another (leader of Gaṇas) Kākapāda with six crores and the lord Santānaka with six crores, Mahābala, Madhupiṅga and Piṅgala each with nine crores. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नीलो नवत्या देवेशं पूर्णभद्रस्तथैव च ।
कोटीनां चैव सप्तानां चतुर्वक्त्रो महाबलः ॥ २१॥

English
Nila, Deveśa and Pūrṇabhadra each with ninety crores and the strong Caturvaktra with seven crores. (21.)

---

English
The lord of all (Śiva) reached there ready to go to Kailāsa surrounded by groups of crores, thousands, hundreds and twenties. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिकोटिसहस्राणां शतैर्विंशतिभिर्वृतः ।
तत्राजगाम सर्वेशः कैलासगमनाय वै ॥। २२॥

काष्ठागूढश्चतुःषष्ट्या सुकेशो वृषभस्तथा ।
कोटिभिः सप्तभिश्चैत्रो नकुलीशस्स्वयं प्रभुः ॥ २३॥

English
Kāṣṭhāgūḍha, Sukeśa and Vṛṣabha each with sixty-four crores. Caitra, Nakuliśa and Svayamprabhu each with seven crores. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकान्तकश्च दीप्तात्मा तथा दैत्यान्तकः प्रभुः ।
देवो भृङ्गी रिटिः श्रीमान्देवदेवप्रियस्तथा ॥ २४॥

अशनिर्भानुकश्चैव चतुःषष्ट्या सनातनः ।
नन्दीश्वरो गणाधीशः शतकोट्या महाबलः ॥ २५॥

English
Lokāntaka Diṭṭātmā and the lord Daityāntaka, lord Bhṛṅgīriṭi and the glorious Devadevapriya, Aśani Bhānuka and Sanātana each with sixty-four crores; Nandiśvarw the supreme chief of Gaṇas, and Mahābala each with hundred crores. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
एते चान्ये च गणपा असङ्ख्याता महाबलाः ।
सर्वे सहस्रहस्ताश्च जटामुकुटधारिणः ॥ २६॥

English
These and other leaders of Gaṇas were all powerful and innumerable. They had thousand hands, matted hair, crown etc. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

हारकुण्डलकेयूरमुकुटाद्यैरलङ्कृताः ॥ २७॥

English
They had crescent moon as their embellishing decoration ; they were blue-necked, three-eyed, adorned with necklaces, earings, crowns and other ornaments. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Lord of Gaṇas emulating Brahmā, Indra and Viṣṇu and shining with the brilliance of crores of suns and possessed of Aṇimā etc. reached there. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

जग्मुस्तत्र महाप्रीत्या शिवदर्शनलालसाः ॥ २९॥

English
The Gaṇa chiefs$^{232}$ and other noble souls of spotless splendour eagerly reached there desirous of seeing Śiva. (29.)

[fn 232. Lords of Gaṇas. Gaṇas are troops who generally appear in classes. Nine such classes are mentioned in the Puranas : They are (1) Ādityas (2) Viśvas or Viśvedevas (3) Vasus (4) Tuṣitas (5) Ābhāsvaras (6) Anilas (7) Mahārājikas (8) Sādhyas (9) Rudras. These are attached to Lord Śiva and serve under the command of Gaṇeśa, dwelling on Gaṇa-parvata identified with Kailāsa—a peak of the Himālaya mountain.]

Sanskrit

English
Reaching the spot they saw Śiva, bowed to and eulogised him. Viṣṇu and others bent their heads and joined their palms in reverence. (30.)

English

English

English
Dear friend, I am charmed by your love. I have become your friend. Go to your place fearlessly. O sincere friend, I shall always assist you. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भोः कुबेरः प्रीतमानसः ।
तदाज्ञया स्वकं धाम जगाम प्रमुदान्वितः ॥ ५३॥

English
On hearing these words of Śiva, Kubera was highly delighted. At His bidding he returned to his abode. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
स उवास गिरौ शम्भुः कैलासे पर्वतोत्तमे ।
सगणो योगनिरतः स्वच्छन्दो ध्यानतत्परः ॥ ५४॥

English
Śiva stayed on Kailāsa, the best of all mountains, along with his Gaṇas, practising Yoga and meditation at his own sweet will. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
क्वचिद्दध्यौ स्वमात्मानं क्वचिद्योगरतोऽभवत् ।
इतिहासं गणान्प्रीत्यावादीत्स्वच्छन्दमानसः ॥ ५५॥

English
In some places he meditated upon his own soul. In some places he practised Yoga. At times, of his own accord, he gave discourses on ancient historical tales. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
क्वचित्कैलासकुधरसुस्थानेषु महेश्वरः ।
विजहार गणैः प्रीत्या विविधेषु विहारवित् ॥ ५६॥

English
He, being an expert in divine sports, sported with his Gaṇas in the different regions of the Kailāsa hill. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं रुद्रस्वरूपोऽसौ शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ।
अकार्षीत्स्वगिरौ लीला नाना योगिवरोऽपि यः ॥ ५७॥

English
Thus lord Śiva who had assumed the form of Rudra performed divine sports on the mount Kailāsa though he was foremost among Yogins. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
नीत्वा कालं कियन्तं सोऽपत्नीकः परमेश्वरः ।
पश्चादवाप स्वां पत्नीं दक्षपत्नीसमुद्भवाम् ॥ ५८॥

English
Thus lord Śiva spent some time without his divine consort. After some time He married Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa Prajāpati. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
विजहार तया सत्या दक्षपुत्र्या महेश्वरः ।
सुखी बभूव देवर्षे लोकाचारपरायणः ॥ ५९॥

English
Lord Śiva sported with her. Following the conventions of the world, O celestial sage, he became happy. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं रुद्रावतारस्ते वर्णितोऽयं मुनीश्वर ।
कैलासागमनं चास्य सखित्वं निधिपस्य हि ॥ ६०॥

English
O sage, thus I have explained to you the manifestation of Śiva in the form of Rudra, his arrival at Kailāsa and his friendship of Kubera, the lord of treasures. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
तदन्तर्गतलीलापि वर्णिता ज्ञानवर्धिनी ।
इहामुत्र च या नित्यं सर्वकामफलप्रदा ॥ ६१॥

English
Thus I have explained the inner sport also which increases perfect knowledge and which confers the fulfilment of desires here and hereafter. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
इमां कथां पठेद्यस्तु श‍ृणुयाद्वा समाहितः ।
इह भुक्तिं समासाद्य लभेन्मुक्तिं परत्र सः ॥ ६२॥

English
He who reads or listens to this story attentively will enjoy all worldly pleasures here and attain salvation hereafter. (62.)
