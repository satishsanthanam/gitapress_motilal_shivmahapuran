### **CHAPTER TWO - *(The appearance of Cupid)***

---

Sanskrit
२

सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य नैमिषारण्यवासिनः ।
पप्रच्छ च मुनिश्रेष्ठः कथां पापप्रणाशिनीम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O residents of Naimiṣa* forest, after hearing his words, the excellent sage further requested him for more such stories that quell sins. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
विधे विधे महाभाग कथां शम्भोः शुभावहाम् ।
श‍ृण्वन् भवन्मुखाम्भोजान्न तृप्तोऽस्मि महाप्रभो ॥ २॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, O great lord, though continuously hearing the auspicious story of Śiva from your lotus-face I am never satiated. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः कथय तत्सर्वं शिवस्य चरितं शुभम् ।
सतीकीर्त्यन्वितं दिव्यं श्रोतुमिच्छामि विश्वकृत् ॥ ३॥

English
Please further narrate the auspicious story of Śiva entirely. I wish to hear that story in which Satī is glorified, O Brahman. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सती हि कथमुत्पन्ना दक्षदारेषु शोभना ।
कथं हरो मनश्चक्रे दाराहरणकर्मणि ॥ ४॥

English
How was the auspicious Satī born of Dakṣa’s wife? How did Śiva become inclined to marry her? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं वा दक्षकोपेन त्यक्तदेहा सती पुरा ।
हिमवत्तनया जाता भूयो वाकाशमागता ॥ ५॥

English
How did she cast off her body formerly, due to her rage with Dakṣa? How was she born as the daughter of Himālaya and how did she reach heaven again? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्याश्च तपोऽत्युग्रं विवाहश्च कथं त्वभूत् ।
कथमर्धशरीरस्था बभूव स्मरनाशिनः ॥ ६॥

English
How was her rigorous penance performed? How was her marriage celebrated? How did she happen to share half the body of Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वं समाचक्ष्व विस्तरेण महामते ।
नान्योऽस्ति संशयच्छेत्ता त्वत्समो न भविष्यति ॥ ७॥

English
Please explain all these points in detail, O intelligent one. There is none else to remove my doubts and none shall ever be like you. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु त्वं च मुने सर्वं सतीशिवयशः शुभम् ।
पावनं परमं दिव्यं गुह्याद् गुह्यतमं परम् ॥ ८॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O sage, listen to the auspicious glory of Satī and Śiva entirely. It is extremely sanctifying, divine and the greatest secret of all secrets. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छम्भुः पुरोवाच भक्तवर्याय विष्णवे ।
पृष्टस्तेन महाभक्त्या परोपकृतये मुने ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, Śiva himself narrated this formerly to Viṣṇu, the greatest of devotees for helping others, when requested by him. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सोऽपि मया पृष्टो विष्णुः शैववरः सुधीः ।
प्रीत्या मह्यं समाचख्यौ विस्तरान्मुनिसत्तम ॥ १०॥

English
Viṣṇu, the intelligent and the greatest of Śiva's devotees was asked by me and O great sage, he told me everything lovingly. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तत्कथयिष्यामि कथामेतां पुरातनीम् ।
शिवाशिवयशोयुक्तां सर्वकामफलप्रदाम् ॥ ११॥

English
Therefore I shall narrate this ancient story that confers the fulfilment of all desires since it glorifies Satī and Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा यदा शिवो देवो निर्गुणो निर्विकल्पकः ।
अरूपः शक्तिरहितश्चिन्मात्रः सदसत्परः ॥ १२॥

English
Originally when Śiva was separated from Śakti and was pure consciousness alone, He was attributeless, free from alternatives, devoid of forms and beyond the existent and non-existent. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अभवत्सगुणः सोऽपि द्विरूपः शक्तिमान्प्रभुः ।
सोमो दिव्याकृतिर्विप्र निर्विकारी परात्परः ॥ १३॥

English
He, the greatest of the great and of changeless form when united with Śakti, was filled with attributes and had specific forms and divine features. O brahmin, He was accompanied by Umā. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य वामाङ्गजो विष्णुर्ब्रह्माहं दक्षिणाङ्गजः ।
रुद्रो हृदयतो जातोऽभवच्च मुनिसत्तम ॥ १४॥

English
Viṣṇu was born of His left and I, Brahmā, of his right side, O great sage, Rudra was born of his heart. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिकर्ताभवं ब्रह्मा विष्णुः पालनकारकः ।
लयकर्ता स्वयं रुद्रस्त्रिधाभूतः सदाशिवः ॥ १५॥

English
I became the creator (Brahmā); Viṣṇu the cause of sustenance; Rudra the author of dissolution. Thus Sadāśiva, manifested himself in three forms. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मरीचिमत्रिं पुलहं पुलस्त्याङ्गिरसौ क्रतुम् ।
वसिष्ठं नारदं दक्षं भृगुं चेति महाप्रभून् ॥ १६॥

English
It was after worshipping Him that I, Brahmā, the grand-father of all the worlds, began the creation of all subjects including Devas, Asuras, human beings etc. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्ट्वा प्रजापतीन् दक्षप्रमुखान्सुरसत्तमान् ।
अमन्यं सुप्रसन्नोऽहं निजं सर्वमहोन्नतम् ॥ १७॥

English
After creating the guardians of the subjects, Prajāpatis, Dakṣa and other Devas, I considered myself loftier than others and was delighted. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माहं मानसान्पुत्रानसर्जं च यदा मुने ।
तदा मन्मनसो जाता चारुरूपा वराङ्गना ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, when I created Marīci, Atri, Pulaha, Pulastya, Aṅgiras, Kratu, Vasiṣṭha, Nārada, Dakṣa and Bhr̥gu, my mental sons of lordly stature, a beautiful woman of handsome features was born of my mind. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
नाम्ना सन्ध्या दिवाक्षान्ता सायं सन्ध्या जपन्तिका ।
अतीव सुन्दरी सुभ्रूर्मुनिचेतोविमोहिनी ॥ २०॥

English
She was variously called Sandhyā, Divakṣāntā, Sāyaṁ Sandhyā and Jayantikā. She was very beautiful with finely-shaped eyebrows capable of captivating the minds of even sages. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
न तादृशी देवलोके न मर्त्ये न रसातले ।
कालत्रयेऽपि वै नारी सम्पूर्णगुणशालिनी ॥ २१॥

English
Neither in human world nor in that of the Devas was there such a woman of complete perfection in all qualities. Nor was there such a woman in nether worlds in all the three times (past, present and future). (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वाहं तां समुत्थाय चिन्तयन् हृदि हृद्गताम् ।
दक्षादयश्च स्रष्टारो मरीच्याद्याश्च मत्सुताः ॥ २२॥

English
On seeing her I involuntarily got up. Various thoughts rose up in my heart. Dakṣa and others—the Prajāpatis, Marīci and others—all my sons, felt similarly. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं चिन्तयतो मे हि ब्रह्मणो मुनिसत्तम ।
मानसः पुरुषो मञ्जुराविर्भूतो महाद्भुतः ॥ २३॥

English
O best of sages, when I Brahmā, thought like this, a wonderfully Beautiful Being appeared as my mental creation. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
काञ्चनीकृतजाताभः पीनोरस्कः सुनासिकः ।
सुवृत्तोरुकटीजङ्घो नीलवेलितकेसरः ॥ २४॥

लग्नभ्रूयुगलो लोलः पूर्णचन्द्रनिभाननः ।
कपाटायतसद्वक्षो रोमराजीविराजितः ॥ २५॥

अभ्रमातङ्गकाकारः पीनो नीलसुवासकः ।
आरक्तपाणिनयनमुखपादकरोद्भवः ॥ २६॥

क्षीणमध्यश्चारुदन्तः प्रमत्तगजगन्धनः ।
प्रफुल्लपद्मपत्राक्षः केसरघ्राणतर्पणः ॥ २७॥

कम्बुग्रीवो मीनकेतुः प्रांशुर्मकरवाहनः ।
पञ्चपुष्पायुधो वेगी पुष्पकोदण्डमण्डितः ॥ २८॥

कान्तः कटाक्षपातेन भ्रामयन्नयनद्वयम् ।
सुगन्धिमारुतो तात श‍ृङ्गाररससेवितः ॥ २९॥

English
He had a golden complexion. His chest was stout and firm. His nose was fine. His thigh, hips and calves were round and plump. He had blue wavelets of hair. His eyebrows were thickset and tremulous. His face shone like the full moon. His hairy chest was broad like a door. He was as huge as the celestial elephant Airāvata. He was wearing a blue cloth. His hands, eyes, face, legs and fingers were red in colour. He had a slender waist. His teeth were fine. He smelt like an elephant in its rut. His eyes were like the petals of a full-brown lotus. He was fragrant like the filaments. His neck was like the conch. He had the emblem of a fish. He was tall. He had the Makara fish for his vehicle. He was armed with a bow and five flowers for his arrows. His loving glance was very attractive as he rolled his eyes here and there. O dear one, his very breath was a fragrant wind. He was accompanied by the sentiment of love. (24-29.)

---

Sanskrit
तं वीक्ष्य पुरुषं सर्वे दक्षाद्या मत्सुताश्च ते ।
औत्सुक्यं परमं जग्मुर्विस्मयाविष्टमानसाः ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing that Being, my sons, Dakṣa and others, were struck with wonder and became eager and inquisitive. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अभवद्विकृतं तेषां मत्सुतानां मनो द्रुतम् ।
धैर्यं नैवालभत्तात कामाकुलितचेतसाम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Their mind became deformed and confused immediately. Smitten with love they lost their mental courage. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मां सोऽपि वेधसं वीक्ष्य स्रष्टारं जगतां पतिम् ।
प्रणम्य पुरुषः प्राह विनयानतकन्धरः ॥ ३२॥

English
On seeing me the creator and the lord of the worlds, the person bowed down with his shoulders stooping by humility and said. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरुष उवाच ।
किं करिष्याम्यहं कर्म ब्रह्मंस्तत्र नियोजय ।
मान्योऽद्य पुरुषो यस्मादुचितः शोभितो विधे ॥ ३३॥

अभिमानं च योग्यं च स्थानं पत्नी च या मम ।
तन्मे वद त्रिलोकेश त्वं स्रष्टा जगतां पतिः ॥ ३४॥

English
*The person said :—*  “O Brahmā, what is the work I am to do ? Please assign me an honourable task, O Brahmā, suitable to and becoming me, O lord of the three worlds, you are the creator and hence the lord of all the worlds. Please tell me. What is my honourable and suitable place? Who is going to be my wife?" (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा पुरुषस्य महात्मनः ।
क्षणं न किञ्चित् प्रोवाच स स्रष्टा चातिविस्मितः ॥ ३५॥

अतो मनः सुसंयम्य सम्यगुत्सृज्य विस्मयम् ।
अवोचत्पुरुषं ब्रह्मा तत्कामं च समावहन् ॥ ३६॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing the words of the noble-souled person Kāma, the creator did not say anything for a short while in his surprised predicament. Then steadying his mind and abandoning his surprised look, Brahmā, already a victim of Kāma, spoke to the person thus :— (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अनेन त्वं स्वरूपेण पुष्पबाणैश्च पञ्चभिः ।
मोहयन् पुरुषान् स्त्रींश्च कुरु सृष्टिं सनातनीम् ॥ ३७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  In this form and with your five flower-arrows$^{236}$ you can enamour and captivate men and women and carry on the eternal task of creation. (37.)

[fn 236. Five flowers that are the missiles of love-God Kāma are stated to be arabinda (a white lotus), aśoka (Jonesia Aśoka), āmra (mango-oot), navamallikā (Jasmine) and nilotpala (a blue lotus).]
---

Sanskrit
अस्मिञ्जीवाश्च देवाद्यास्त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ।
एते सर्वे भविष्यन्ति न क्षमास्तव लङ्घने ॥ ३८॥

English
In this universe consisting of three worlds, mobile and immobile beings, none of the living beings including the Devas will be competent to defy you. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं वा वासुदेवो वा स्थाणुर्वा पुरुषोत्तम ।
भविष्यामस्तव वशे किमन्ये प्राणधारकाः ॥ ३९॥

English
O best of beings, not to speak of ordinary living beings even I Brahmā, Vāsudeva and Śiva will be in your control. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रच्छन्नरूपो जन्तूनां प्रविशन् हृदयं सदा ।
सुखहेतुः स्वयं भूत्वा सृष्टिं कुरु सनातनीम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Invisibly you enter the hearts of living beings, excite thrilling feelings of pleasure and carry on the activities of creation that is to last for ever. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्पुष्पबाणस्य सदा सुखलक्ष्यं मनोऽद्भुतम् ।
सर्वेषां प्राणिनां नित्यं सदा मदकरो भवान् ॥ ४१॥

English
The minds of all living beings will become an easy target of your five-flower arrows. You will be the cause of their elation. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कर्म कथितं सृष्टिप्रावर्तकं पुनः ।
नामान्येते वदिष्यन्ति सुता मे तव तत्त्वतः ॥ ४२॥

English
Thus I have assigned you the task of facilitating creation. These sons of mine will confer names and titles on you. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वाहं सुरश्रेष्ठ स्वसुतानां मुखानि च ।
आलोक्य स्वासने पाद्मे प्रोपविष्टोऽभवं क्षणम् ॥ ४३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O best of the celestials, after saying this and casting a meaningful glance at my sons I resumed my lotus-seat immediately. (43.)
