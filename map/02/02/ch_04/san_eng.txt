### **CHAPTER FOUR - *(Kāma's marriage)***

---

Sanskrit
४

नारद उवाच ।
विष्णुशिष्य महाप्राज्ञ विधे लोककर प्रभो ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा प्रोक्ता शिवलीलामृतान्विता ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O lord Brahmā, O Viṣṇu's disciple of great intellect, O creator of the world, you have narrated a wonderful story consisting of the nector of Śiva's divine sports. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः किमभवत्तात चरितं तद् वदाधुना ।
अहं श्रद्धान्वितः श्रोतुं यदि शम्भुकथाश्रयम् ॥ २॥

English
O dear one, what happened thereafter, please tell me now. I am all attention to a narrative based on Śiva's life. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
शम्भौ गते निजस्थाने वेधस्यन्तर्हिते मयि ।
दक्षः प्राहाथ कन्दर्पं संस्मरन् मम तद्वचः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When Śiva had gone back to His place and I, Brahmā had vanished from the scene, Dakṣa remembered my words and spoke to Kāma. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
मद्देहजेयं कन्दर्प सद्रूपगुणसंयुता ।
एनां गृह्णीष्व भार्यार्थं भवतः सदृशीं गुणैः ॥ ४॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  "O Kāma, this girl is born of my body. She is endowed with beauty and good qualities. She fits you admirably. Take her as your wife. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एषा तव महातेजाः सर्वदा सहचारिणी ।
भविष्यति यथाकामं धर्मतो वशवर्तिनी ॥ ५॥

English
This powerful girl shall ever be under your righteous control and shall be your constant companion as long as you wish." (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा प्रददौ तस्यै देहस्वेदाम्बुसम्भवाम् ।
कन्दर्पायाग्रतः कृत्वा नाम कृत्वा रतीति ताम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Saying so, he presented to him the girl born of his sweat after naming her Rati. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विवाह्य तां स्मरः सोऽपि मुमोदातीव नारद ।
दक्षजां तनयां रम्यां मुनीनामपि मोहिनीम् ॥ ७॥

English
O Nārada, after marrying the beautiful daughter of Dakṣa who could enchant even sages, Kāma rejoiced much. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तां वीक्ष्य मदनो रत्याख्यां स्वस्त्रियं शुभाम् ।
आत्मागुणेन विद्धोऽसौ मुमोह रतिरञ्जितः ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing his auspicious wife, Rati, Kāma was pierced by his own arrows and was overpowered by the pleasure of dalliance. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणप्रदाभवत्कान्ता गौरी मृगदृशी मुदा ।
लोलापाङ्ग्यथ तस्यैव भार्या च सदृशी रतौ ॥ ९॥

English
His wife of fair complexion, tremulous side-glances and fawn-eyes, admirably suited to his love of pleasure offered him ample sports. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्या भ्रूयुगलं वीक्ष्य संशयं मदनोऽकरोत् ।
उत्सादनं मत्कोदण्डं विधात्रास्यां निवेशितम् ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing her eyebrows the doubt arose in the mind of Kāma. — “These two have been fitted to her to excel my bow, by Brahmā who wants to undo it !” (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गतिं दृष्ट्वा कटाक्षाणामाशु तस्या द्विजोत्तम ।
आशु गन्तुं निजास्त्राणां श्रद्दधे न च चारुताम् ॥ ११॥

English
O best of Brahmins, On seeing her rapid-roving glances he did not retain his faith in his arrows in the matter of swift action. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याः स्वभावसौरभ्यं धीरश्वासानिलं तथा ।
आघ्राय मदनः श्रद्धां त्यक्तवान् मलयानिले ॥ १२॥

English
Inhaling the naturally sweet fragrance of her steady breath Kāma abandoned his faith in the Malaya breeze. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्णेन्दुसदृशं वक्त्रं दृष्ट्वा लक्ष्मसुलक्षितम् ।
न निश्चिकाय मदनो भेदं तन्मुखचन्द्रयोः ॥ १३॥

English
Seeing her face resembling the full moon with all characteristic marks, Kāma was unable to find any difference between her face and the moon. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्णपद्मकलिकातुल्यं तस्याः कुचद्वयम् ।
रेजे चूचुकयुग्मेन भ्रमरेणेव वेष्टितम् ॥ १४॥

English
Her pair of breasts resembled the buds of golden lotus with nipples shining like bees hovering round them. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
दृढपीनोन्नतं तस्याः स्तनमध्यं विलम्बिनीम् ।
आनाभिलम्बिनीं मालां तन्वीं चन्द्रायितां शुभाम् ॥ १५॥

ज्यां पुष्पधनुषः कामः षट्पदावलिसम्भ्रमाम् ।
विसस्मार च यस्मात्तां विसृज्यैनां निरीक्षते ॥ १६॥

English
Certainly Kāma had set aside and forgotten the string of his flowery bow with tumultuous buzzing hums of bees because his eyes were riveted to the auspicious necklace with eyelets of peacock’s tail suspended over her firm protruding plump breasts down to her umbilical part. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
गम्भीरनाभिरन्ध्रान्तश्चतुःपार्श्वत्वगावृतम् ।
आननाब्जेक्षणद्वन्द्वमारक्तकमलं यथा ॥ १७॥

English
His eyes covering the skin with their glances around her deep navel shone like red plums. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षीणमध्येन वपुषा निसर्गाष्टापदप्रभा ।
रुक्मवेदीव ददृशे कामेन रमणी हि सा ॥ १८॥

English
That lovely woman of slender waist with a natural golden complexion appeared like a golden platform to Kāma. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रम्भास्तम्भायतं स्निग्धं यदूरुयुगलं मृदु ।
निजशक्तिसमं कामो वीक्षाञ्चक्रे मनोहरम् ॥ १९॥

English
Kāma looked at her thighs lovely like the stump of a plantain as though they were his javelin. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
आरक्तपार्ष्णिपादाग्रं प्रान्तभागं पदद्वयम् ।
अनुरागमिवानेन मित्रं तस्या मनोभवः ॥ २०॥

English
The heels, the tips and the sides of her feet were reddish in tinge. With them she looked as the comrade of the Cupid. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याः करयुगं रक्तं नखरैः किंशुकोपमैः ।
वृत्ताभिरङ्गुलीभिश्च सूक्ष्माग्राभिर्मनोहरम् ॥ २१॥

English
Her red hands with nails like Kimśuka flowers and with well-rounded tapering fingers were very beautiful. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् बाहुयुगुलं कान्तं मृणालयुगलायतम् ।
मृदु स्निग्धं चिरं राजत्कान्तिलोहप्रवालवत् ॥ २२॥

English
Her arms were fine like the lotus-stalk. They were glossy and soft. They resembled corals putting forth beams of splendour. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नीलनीरदसङ्काशः केशपाशो मनोहरः ।
चमरीबालभरवद्विभाति स्म स्मरप्रिया ॥ २३॥

English
Her glossy hair resembled the blue cloud and the fluffy tail of the Camari dear. Thus shone the wife of Kāma. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एतादृशीं रतिं नाम्नीं प्रालेयाद्रिसमुद्भवाम् ।
गङ्गामिव महादेवो जग्राहोत्फुल्ललोचनः ॥ २४॥

चक्रपद्मां चारुबाहुं मृणालशकलान्विताम् ।
भ्रूयुग्मविभ्रमव्राततनूर्मिपरिराजिताम् ॥ २५॥

कटाक्षपाततुङ्गौघां स्वीयनेत्रोत्पलान्विताम् ।
तनुलोमाम्बुशैवालां मनोद्रुमविलासिनीम् ॥ २६॥

निम्ननाभिह्रदां क्षामां सर्वाङ्गरमणीयिकाम् ।
सर्वलावण्यसदनां शोभमानां रमामिव ॥ २७॥

English
Just as Lord Śiva accepted Gaṅgā oozing from the snowy mountain, Kāma married her. She carried a discus and a lotus in her hand. She had arms fine as the lotus-stalk. She had wavelets of her eyebows. Her side-glances rose up and down like gentle tides. She had eyes resembling a blue lotus. The curly locks of hair on her body were like the mossy growth in the river. She shone with her mind expanded like the tree. Her deep navel resembled the deep eddy. Thus shone Rati with her beautiful body. In fact she appeared to be the abode of beauty itself like Ramā (Goddess Lakṣmī). (24-27.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वादशाभरणैर्युक्तां श‍ृङ्गारैः षोडशैर्युताम् ।
मोहनीं सर्वलोकानां भासयन्तीं दिशो दश ॥ २८॥

English
She had twelve varieties of ornaments. She was an expert in the sixteen types of amorous gestures. She was capable of charming the whole world. She illuminated all the ten quarters. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
इति तां मदनो वीक्ष्य रतिं जग्राह सोत्सुकः ।
रागादुपस्थितां लक्ष्मीं हृषीकेश इवोत्तमाम् ॥ २९॥

English
Seeing Rati like this, Kāma eagerly accepted her just as Viṣṇu accepted Lakṣmī who approached him with love. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नोवाच च तदा दक्षं कामो मोदभवात्ततः ।
विस्मृत्य दारुणं शापं विधिदत्तं विमोहितः ॥ ३०॥

English
In his height of joy, the deluded Kāma forgot the terrible curse of Brahmā and so he had no occasion to mention about it to Dakṣa. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा महोत्सवस्तात बभूव सुखवर्धनः ।
दक्षः प्रीततरश्चासीन्मुमुदे तनया मम ॥ ३१॥

English
Great festivities heightening the pleasure of everyone ensued, O dear one. My son Dakṣa was more delighted than everyone else. He rejoiced. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कामोऽतीव सुखं प्राप्य सर्वदुःखक्षयं गतः ।
दक्षजापि रतिः कामं प्राप्य चापि जहर्ष ह ॥ ३२॥

रराज च तया सार्धं भिन्नश्चारुवचाः स्मरः ।
जीमूत इव सन्ध्यायां सौदामन्या मनोज्ञया ॥ ३३॥

इति रतिपतिरुच्चैर्मोहयुक्तो रतिं तां
हृदुपरि जगृहे वै योगदर्शीव विद्याम् ।
रतिरपि पतिमग्र्यं प्राप्य सा चापि रेजे
हरिमिव कमला वै पूर्णचन्द्रोपमास्या ॥ ३४॥

English
Having reached the acme of happiness Kāma thought all miseries were at an end. Dakṣa's daughter Rati was highly delighted on getting Kāma as her husband. The sweet-voiced Kāma rejoiced with her like the cloud at sunset mingled with sparkling lightning. Thus Kāma took Rati to his chest in his happy delusion like the Yogin his knowledge. Having secured a fine husband, Rati with face shining like the full moon shone like Lakṣmī having secured Hari. (32-34.)
