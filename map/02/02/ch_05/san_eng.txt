### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(The story of Sandhyā)***

---

Sanskrit
५

सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य ब्रह्मणो मुनिसत्तमः ।
स मुदोवाच संस्मृत्य शङ्करं प्रीतमानसः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing these words of Brahmā, the excellent sage remembered Śiva with a delighted heart and spoke joyfully. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे महाभाग विष्णुशिष्य महामते ।
अद्भुता कथिता लीला त्वया च शशिमौलिनः ॥ २॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, the fortunate disciple of Viṣṇu, O intelligent one, you have narrated the wonderful divine sports of the moon-crested lord. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीतदारे मदने हृष्टे हि स्वगृहं गते ।
दक्षे च स्वगृहं याते तथा हि त्वयि कर्तरि ॥ ३॥

मानसेषु च पुत्रेषु गतेषु स्वस्वधामसु ।
सन्ध्या कुत्र गता सा च ब्रह्मपुत्री पितृप्रसूः ॥ ४॥

English
After Kāma had married and gone to his residence when all of you, i.e. you the creator, Dakṣa and the mental sons, had all gone to your respective abodes, where did Sandhyā, the daughter of Brahmā and the mother of the Pīṭṭs go ? (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
किं चकार च केनैव पुरुषेण विवाहिता ।
एतत्सर्वं विशेषेण सन्ध्यायाश्चरितं वद ॥ ५॥

English
What did she do ? Who married her ? Please tell me all about it and particularly the account related to Sandhyā. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य ब्रह्मपुत्रस्य धीमतः ।
संस्मृत्य शङ्करं भक्त्या ब्रह्मा प्रोवाच तत्त्ववित् ॥ ६॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing these words of his intelligent son, Brahmā, who knew the real situation, remembered Śiva and said:— (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु त्वं च मुने सर्वं सन्ध्यायाश्चरितं शुभम् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा सर्वकामिन्यःसाध्व्यः स्युः सर्वदा मुने ॥ ७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage, listen to the auspicious story of Sandhyā, on hearing which ladies do always become chaste. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सा च सन्ध्या सुता मे हि मनोजाता पुराभवत् ।
तपस्तप्त्वा तनुं त्यक्त्वा सैव जाता त्वरुन्धती ॥ ८॥

English
That Sandhyā was my daughter mentally created by me formerly. She performed a penance, cast off her body and was reborn as Arundhati. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मेधातिथेः सुता भूत्वा मुनिश्रेष्ठस्य धीमती ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशानवचनाच्चरितव्रता ॥ ९॥

वव्रे पतिं महात्मानं वसिष्ठं शंसितव्रतम् ।
पतिव्रता च मुख्याभूद्वन्द्या पूज्या त्वभीषणा ॥ १०॥

English
She was born as the intelligent daughter of the excellent sage Medhātithi, performed sacred rites at the bidding of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva and chose as her husband the noble-souled Vasiṣṭha of praiseworthy rites. She of auspicious countenance became the foremost of chaste ladies and deserved honour and respect from everyone. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
कथं तया तपस्तप्तं किमर्थं कुत्र सन्ध्यया ।
कथं शरीरं सा त्यक्त्वाभवन्मेधातिथेः सुता ॥ ११॥

English
Nārada said :—  How did she perform penance? Why and where? How did she cast off her body and become the daughter of Medhātithi? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं वा विहितं देवैर्ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवैः पतिम् ।
वसिष्ठं तु महात्मानं संवव्रे शंसितव्रतम् ॥ १२॥

English
What did the deities Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva command her to do? and how did she choose the noble-souled Vasiṣṭha of praiseworthy rites as her husband? (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एतन्मे श्रोष्यमाणाय विस्तरेण पितामह ।
कौतूहलमरुन्धत्याश्चरितं ब्रूहि तत्त्वतः ॥ १३॥

English
I am eager to hear all these things. O Grand Father, tell me in detail the story of Sandhyā precisely. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अहं स्वतनयां सन्ध्यां दृष्ट्वा पूर्वमथात्मनः ।
कामायाशु मनोऽकार्षं त्यक्त्वा शिवभयाच्च सा ॥ १४॥

English
Brahmā said :- Formerly on seeing Sandhyā, my daughter, I cherished a love for her, which, being afraid of Śiva, I forsook. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्यायाश्चलितं चित्तं कामबाणविलोडितम् ।
ऋषीणामपि संरुद्धमानसानां महात्मनाम् ॥ १५॥

English
Sandhyā's mind too was shaken on being stirred by Kāma's arrows. The same had happened with the mind of the noble-souled sages who had so far curbed their minds. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
भर्गस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा सोपहासं च मां प्रति ।
आत्मनश्चलितत्वं वै ह्यमर्यादमृषीन्प्रति ॥ १६॥

कामस्य तादृशं भावं मुनिमोहकरं मुहुः ।
दृष्ट्वा सन्ध्या स्वयं तत्रोपयमायातिदुःखिता ॥ १७॥

English
She had heard the words of Śiva to me couched in mocking terms. She had realised that her mental aberration in regard to the sages was beyond decency. She had seen the attitude of Kāma culminating in the delusion of the sages; frequently. Hence Sandhyā was excessively distressed with respect to her marriage. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तु ब्रह्मणा शप्ते मदने च मया मुने ।
अन्तर्भूते मयि शिवे गते चापि निजास्पदे ॥ १८॥

आमर्षवशमापन्ना सा सन्ध्या मुनिसत्तम ।
मम पुत्री विचार्यैवं तदा ध्यानपराभवत् ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, then I cursed Kāma. Śiva left the place and I too disappeared. Thus her support was lost. So, O excellent sage, Sandhyā became furious. Then, my daughter considered all these things and meditated. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायन्ती क्षणमेवाशु पूर्वं वृत्तं मनस्विनी ।
इदं विममृशे सन्ध्या तस्मिन्काले यथोचितम् ॥ २०॥

English
Meditating on the recent events, she of great fortitude mused what befitted the situation. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्योवाच ।
उत्पन्नमात्रां मां दृष्ट्वा युवतीं मदनेरितः ।
अकार्षीत्सानुरागोऽयमभिलाषं पिता मम ॥ २१॥

English
Sandhyā said :—  Seeing me as a lady in the prime of my youth even at my nativity, my father, prompted by Kāma, cherished a lustful desire for me. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्यतां मानसानां च मुनीनां भावितात्मनाम् ।
दृष्ट्वैव माममर्यादं सकाममभवन्मनः ॥ २२॥

English
The minds of the sages, the mental sons, reputed to be pure in mind, on seeing me became lustful breaking the conventions. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ममापि मथितं चित्तं मदनेन दुरात्मना ।
येन दृष्ट्वा मुनीन्सर्वांश्चलितं मन्मनो भृशम् ॥ २३॥

English
My mind too was excessively stirred up by the wicked Kāma, as a result of which, on seeing those sages it too became excessively shaken. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
फलमेतस्य पापस्य मदनः स्वयमाप्तवान् ।
यस्तं शशाप कुपितः शम्भोरग्रे पितामहः ॥ २४॥

English
Of course Kāma reaped the fruits of his sinful misdeeds, for Brahmā became angry and cursed him in the presence of Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्नुयां फलमेतस्य पापस्य स्वघकारिणी ।
तच्छोधनफलमहमाशु चेच्छामि साधनम् ॥ २५॥

English
I too shall have to reap the fruits of my sin. I have committed a great sin. I wish to have a means for making amends. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यन्मां पिता भ्रातरश्च सकामामपरोक्षतः ।
दृष्ट्वा चक्रुः स्पृहां तस्मान्न मत्तः पापकृत्परा ॥ २६॥

English
Directly perceiving that I too had lustful feelings, the brothers and my father had a similar desire. Hence I am the worst sinner. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ममापि कामभावोऽभूदमर्यादं समीक्ष्य तान् ।
पत्या इव स्वके ताते सर्वेषु सहजेष्वषि ॥ २७॥

English
I too had the unconventional lustful feelings on seeing them, towards my own father and brothers as towards a husband. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
करिष्याम्यस्य पापस्य प्रायश्चित्तमहं स्वयम् ।
आत्मानमग्नौ होष्यामि वेदमार्गानुसारतः ॥ २८॥

English
I shall perform expiatory rites myself for my sin. Following the Vedic injunctions I shall consign myself to the fire. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
किं त्वेकां स्थापयिष्यामि मर्यादामिह भूतले ।
उत्पन्नमात्रा न यथा सकामाः स्युः शरीरिणः ॥ २९॥

English
But I shall set up the new limits in the world. No person shall be so lustful at the time of birth. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदर्थमहं कृत्वा तपः परमदारुणम् ।
मर्यादां स्थापयिष्यामि पश्चात्त्यक्ष्यामि जीवितम् ॥ ३०॥

English
For this purpose I shall perform a severe penance. Then I shall establish the new limits and afterwards I shall abandon this life. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मिन् शरीरे पित्रा मे ह्यभिलाषः स्वयं कृतः ।
भ्रातृभिस्तेन कायेन किञ्चिन्नास्ति प्रयोजनम् ॥ ३१॥

English
No purpose will be served with this body for which love was cherished by my father and brothers. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मया येन शरीरेण तातेषु सहजेषु च ।
उद्भावितः कामभावो न तत्सुकृतसाधनम् ॥ ३२॥

English
This body cannot be the means for achieving merit, for, it was through this body that lustful feelings were generated in my father and brothers. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सञ्चिन्त्य मनसा सन्ध्या शैलवरं ततः ।
जगाम चन्द्रभागाख्यं चन्द्रभागापगा यतः ॥ ३३॥

English
Thinking thus in her mind, Sandhyā went to the mountain Candrabhāga from which the river Candrabhāgā$^{237}$ flows. (33.)

[fn 237. Candrabhāgā, modern Cenab. It is called Asikni 'black' in the Rgveda, Akesines by Arrian and Sandabhāgā by Ptolemy. It rises from the foot of the Himālayas and flows in two rivulets: Candrā from a large snow-bed to the South-East of Bāra Lācha; Bhāgā from the north-west slope of the pass and both join at Tandi and the joint stream is known as Candrabhāgā. H. Dh. S. Vol. IV P. 742; Geo of the Purāṇas P. 114.]
---

Sanskrit
अथ तत्र गतां ज्ञात्वा सन्ध्यां गिरिवरं प्रति ।
तपसे नियतात्मानं ब्रह्मावोचमहं सुतम् ॥ ३४॥

वसिष्ठं संयतात्मानं सर्वज्ञं ज्ञानयोगिनम् ।
समीपे स्वे समासीनं वेदवेदाङ्गपारगम् ॥ ३५॥

English
On coming to know that she had gone to the mountain, I, Brahmā, told my son Vasiṣṭha, the omniscient, of purified mind due to penance, who had acquired spiritual knowledge who was seated near me and who had mastered the Vedas and the Vedāṅgas. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
वसिष्ठ पुत्र गच्छ त्वं सन्ध्यां जातां मनस्विनीम् ।
तपसे धृतकामां च दीक्षस्वैनां यथाविधि ॥ ३६॥

English
“O son Vasiṣṭha, approach Sandhyā, my daughter of great fortitude. She is desirous of performing a penance. Initiate her duly in the procedure of that. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्दाक्षमभवत्तस्याः पुरा दृष्ट्वैव कामुकान् ।
युष्मान्मां च तथात्मानं सकामां मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३७॥

English
O great sage, formerly seeing you all and me as her lovers and realising her own lustful feelings she had blushed. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Though not expressed and though not personified, your action then is considered by her as her first death. Now she wishes to put an end to her life. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Among those who observe limits and conventions she wants to lay down a limitation. The chaste lady has gone to the mountain Candrabhāga for performing the penance. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

तस्माद्यथोपदेशात्सा प्राप्नोत्विष्टं तथा कुरु ॥ ४०॥

English
She does not know the procedure of performing a penance. O dear, see that she realises her desire by means of your instructions. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, abandon this form of yours. Disguise yourself and approach her to demonstrate the mode of penance. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

---

English
Vasiṣṭha saw the celestial lake full of Gaṇas and resembling the Mānasa lake. He saw Sandhyā too on its bank. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
With her seated on its bank, the lake, full of splendid lotuses, appeared like the sky in the dusk with the moon rising and the stars twinkling. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

[fn 238. The lake Lohita lies at the foot of the mountain Lohita—Hemāṣṭṅga or Sarvopadha, situated on the north of the Hemakūta (Kailāsa) range. It is the source-lake of the Lauhitya identified with the modern river Brahmaputra.]
---

Sanskrit

English
From the ridges of that big mountain which appeared like a big fort wall, the river Candrabhāgā rose and flowed towards the Southern sea. The sage saw that too. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
That river breaks the western wing of the mountain Candrabhāga even as Gaṅgā of the mountain Himālaya and flows towards the sea. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Seeing Sandhyā on the bank of the lake Bṛhallohita on that mountain Candrabhāga, Vasiṣṭha asked her respectfully. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

किमर्थमागता भद्रे निर्जनं त्वं महीधरम् ।

English
*Vasiṣṭha said :—*  “O good lady, why have you come to this mountain devoid of men? Whose daughter are you? What is it that you intend to do? (50.)

---

Sanskrit

वदनं पूर्णचन्द्राभं निष्चेष्टं वा कथं तव ॥ ५१॥

English

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य वसिष्ठस्य महात्मनः ।
दृष्ट्वा च तं महात्मानं ज्वलन्तमिव पावकम् ॥ ५२॥

शरीरधृग्ब्रह्मचर्यं विलसन्तं जटाधरम् ।
सादरं प्रणिपत्याथ सन्ध्योवाच तपोधनम् ॥ ५३॥

English
On hearing the words of the noble-souled Vasiṣṭha and seeing him blazing like fire, shining like Brahmacarya (Celibacy) personified, Sandhyā bowed to the sage wearing matted hair and spoke to him respectfully. (52-53.)

Sanskrit

English
*Sandhyā said :—*  “O fearless (sage), know that the purpose for which I came to this mountain has already been achieved or rather will be achieved by your very sight. (54.)

Sanskrit

English
O sage, I came to this mountain devoid of men to perform penance. I am the daughter of Brahmā and am known as Sandhyā. (55.)

Sanskrit

English
If it be proper and not inconvenient for you please instruct me. This is what I expect of you. There is nothing to be kept secret in this. (56.)

Sanskrit

English
Without knowing the procedure of penance I have come to this penance grove. Due to this worry I am perplexed and my heart trembles”. (57.)

Sanskrit

English
On hearing her words, Vasiṣṭha, the most excellent among the knowers of Brahman, well-versed in every rite did not ask anything further. (58.)

Sanskrit

English
After remembering Śiva favourably disposed to the devotees he addressed the lady who had controlled herself and was preparing for the penance. (59.)

Sanskrit

English
*Vasiṣṭha said :—*  He who is the supreme brilliance, He who is the greatest austerity, He who is the worthiest of worship—let that Śiva be meditated upon. (60.)

Sanskrit

English
Worship Him who is the most excellent of all Beings, the sole first cause of all the worlds and the principal cause of virtue, wealth, love and salvation. (61.)

English
O lady, worship lord Śiva, the lord of all Devas with the following mantra. By that, certainly you will achieve everything. (62.)

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
