### **CHAPTER SIX - *(The Hymn sung by Sandhyā. Sandhyā acquires the boon from Śiva.)***

---

Sanskrit
६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
सुतवर्य महाप्राज्ञ श‍ृणु सन्ध्यातपो महत् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा नश्यते पापसमूहस्तत्क्षणाद् ध्रुवम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O best of my sons, O intelligent one, listen to the description of the great penance of Sandhyā on hearing which sins are quelled instantly. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
उपदिश्य तपोभावं वसिष्ठे स्वगृहं गते ।
सन्ध्यापि तपसो भावं ज्ञात्वा मोदमवाप ह ॥ २॥

English
When Vasiṣṭha went back to his abode after instructing her in the rites of penance, Sandhyā was greatly pleased on learning the procedure of penance. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सानन्दमनसो वेषं कृत्वा तु यादृशम् ।
तपश्चर्तुं समारेभे बृहल्लोहिततीरगा ॥ ३॥

English
On the bank of the lake Bṛhallohita she began to perform penance after she had put on the dress of a person of blissful mind. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यथोक्तं तु वसिष्ठेन मन्त्रं तपसि साधनम् ।
मन्त्रेण तेन सद्भक्त्या पूजयामास शङ्करम् ॥ ४॥

English
She worshipped Śiva with the mantra taught by Vasiṣṭha as the adjunct of penance in the manner explained by him. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एकान्तमनसस्तस्याः कुर्वन्त्याः सुमहत्तपः ।
शम्भौ विन्यस्तचित्ताया गतमेकं चतुर्युगम् ॥ ५॥

English
A period of four Yugas elapsed during which she continued her great penance with mind fixed and duly concentrated on Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नोऽभूत्तदा शम्भुस्तपसा तेन तोषितः ।
अन्तर्बहिस्तथाकाशे दर्शयित्वा निजं वपुः ॥ ६॥

English
Propitiated by her penance Śiva was greatly delighted. He revealed Himself to her within and without as well as in the heaven. (6.)

---

English
Śiva became visible to her in the form in which she was meditating upon him. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्रूपं चिन्तयन्ती सा तेन प्रत्यक्षतां गतः । ७॥

अथ सा पुरतो दृष्ट्वा मनसा चिन्तितं प्रभुम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं शान्तं मुमोदातीव शङ्करम् ॥ ८॥

English
She rejoiced much on seeing in front of her, the lord Śiva with face beaming with delight, in the same form as she was meditating on. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ससाध्वसमहं वक्ष्ये किं कथं स्तौमि वा हरम् ।
इति चिन्तापरा भूत्वा न्यमीलयत चक्षुषी ॥ ९॥

English
"What shall I say ? How shall I eulogise?" in this agitation she closed her eyes with fear. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
निमीलिताक्ष्यास्तस्यास्तु प्रविश्य हृदयं हरः ।
दिव्यं ज्ञानं ददौ तस्यै वाचं दिव्ये च चक्षुषी ॥ १०॥

English
As she remained with eyes shut, Śiva entered her heart and blessed her with divine wisdom, divine speech and divine eyes. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यज्ञानं दिव्यचक्षुर्दिव्यां वाचमवाप सा ।
प्रत्यक्षं वीक्ष्य दुर्गेशं तुष्टाव जगतां पतिम् ॥ ११॥

English
She thus acquired divine wisdom, divine eyes and divine speech. Directly perceiving the lord of Durgā she eulogised the lord of the worlds. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्योवाच ।
निराकारं ज्ञानगम्यं परं य-
न्नैव स्थूलं नापि सूक्ष्मं न चोच्चम् ।
अन्तश्चिन्त्यं योगिभिस्तस्य रूपं
तस्मै तुभ्यं लोककर्त्रे नमोऽस्तु ॥ १२॥

English
*Sandhyā said:—*  That which has no specific form, that which can be known through perfect knowledge; that which is neither gross, nor subtle, nor high; that which is to be meditated upon by Yogins within themselves—obeisance be to Thee who art of this sort and the creator of the worlds. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं शान्तं निर्मलं निर्विकारं
ज्ञानागम्यं स्वप्रकाशेऽविकारम् ।
खाध्वप्रख्यं ध्वान्तमार्गात्परस्ताद्-
रूपं यस्य त्वां नमामि प्रसन्नम् ॥ १३॥

English
I bow to Thee, lord Śiva, whose form is like a road to heaven, beyond the path of darkness, and who art calm, pure, changeless incomprehensible through worldly knowledge, self-illuminated and unaltered. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एकं शुद्धं दीप्यमानं विनाजां
चिदानन्दं सहजं चाविकारि ।
नित्यानन्दं सत्यभूतिप्रसन्नं
यस्य श्रीदं रूपमस्मै नमस्ते ॥ १४॥

English
Obeisance to Thee whose form is solitary, pure, luminous, free from illusion, knowledge-cum-bliss, naturally undecaying, eternal bliss, delighted at the outcome of truth and prosperity and productive of glory. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्याकारोद्भावनीयं प्रभिन्नं
सत्त्वच्छन्दं ध्येयमात्मस्वरूपम् ।
सारं पारं पावनानां पवित्रं
तस्मै रूपं यस्य चैवं नमस्ते ॥ १५॥

English
Obeisance to Thee whose form can be imagined in the nature of Vidyā (Perfect Knowledge), which is different from insentient things, Sāttvika in will, that which should be meditated on as the form of Ātman, which is the utmost essence and which is the holiest of all sanctifying objects. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्त्वाकारं शुद्धरूपं मनोज्ञं
रत्नाकल्पं स्वच्छकर्पूरगौरम् ।
इष्टाभीती शूलमुण्डं दधानं
हस्तैर्नमो योगयुक्ताय तुभ्यम् ॥ १६॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, the Yogin whose Saguṇa form is pure, lovely, bedecked in jewels, as white and clean as camphor and which holds in its hand the desired boon, fearlessness, the trident and the scalp. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
गगनं भूर्दिशश्चैव सलिलं ज्योतिरेव च ।
पुनः कालश्च रूपाणि यस्य तुभ्यं नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १७॥

English
Obeisance to Thee whose forms are the sky, the earth, the quarters, the waters, the fire and the Eternal time. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधानपुरुषौ यस्य कायत्वेन विनिर्गतौ ।
तस्मादव्यक्तरूपाय शङ्कराय नमो नमः ॥ १८॥

English
Obeisance, obeisance to Śiva of unmanifest form from whom unmanifest primordial nature and Puruṣa issued forth as its effect. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यो ब्रह्मा कुरुते सृष्टिं यो विष्णुः कुरुते स्थितिम् ।
संहरिष्यति यो रुद्रस्तस्मै तुभ्यं नमो नमः ॥ १९॥

English
Obeisance, obeisance to Thee who createst this universe in the form of Brahmā, who sustainest it in the form of Viṣṇu and who destroyest it in the form of Rudra. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नमो नमः कारणकारणाय
दिव्यामृतज्ञानविभूतिदाय ।
समस्तलोकान्तरभूतिदाय
प्रकाशरूपाय परात्पराय ॥ २०॥

English
Obeisance, obeisance to the cause of causes, to the bestower of divine nectar, wisdom and prosperity; to the bestower of the prosperity of all other worlds, and the luminous greatest of the great. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्यापरं नो जगदुच्यते पदात्
क्षितिर्दिशः सूर्य इन्दुर्मनोजः ।
बहिर्मुखा नाभितश्चान्तरिक्षं
तस्मै तुभ्यं शम्भवे मे नमोऽस्तु ॥ २१॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, Śiva, beyond whose region no other world exists; from whose umbilical region arose the earth, the quarters, the sun, the moon, the cupid, the devas and the ether. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं परः परमात्मा च त्वं विद्या विविधा हरः ।
सद्ब्रह्म च परं ब्रह्म विचारणपरायणः ॥ २२॥

English
Thou art, the greatest supreme soul. Thou art Śiva, the various lores, the pure Brahman, the supreme Brahman and the utmost object of deliberation. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य नादिर्न मध्यं च नान्तमस्ति जगद्यतः ।
कथं स्तोष्यामि तं देवं वाङ्मनोऽगोचरं हरम् ॥ २३॥

English
How can I adequately eulogise lord Śiva who is inexpressible by words, is incomprehensible to the mind, is the cause of the world and has no beginning, no middle, no end. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य ब्रह्मादयो देव मुनयश्च तपोधनाः ।
न विवृण्वन्ति रूपाणि वर्णनीयः कथं स मे ॥ २४॥

English
How can he be described by me, whose forms even Brahmā and other Gods or sages of great austerity cannot describe. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रिया मया ते किं ज्ञेया निर्गुणस्य गुणाः प्रभो ।
नैव जानन्ति यद्रूपं सेन्द्रा अपि सुरासुराः ॥ २५॥

English
O lord, Thou art attributeless. How can Thy attributes be known to me, a mere woman? Even the Gods including Indra and Asuras do not know it. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्तुभ्यं महेशान नमस्तुभ्यं तपोमय ।
प्रसीद शम्भो देवेश भूयो भूयो नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २६॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, O Lord Śiva, obeisance to Thee, O personification of penance; O Śiva, lord of the Gods, be pleased, obeisance be to Thee again and again. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्याः संस्तुतः परमेश्वरः ।
सुप्रसन्नतरश्चाभूच्छङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Being thus eulogised and having heard her words Śiva favourably disposed to the devotees became highly pleased. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्याः शरीरं तु वल्कलाजिनसंयुतम् ।
परिच्छन्नं जटाव्रातैः पवित्रे मूर्ध्नि राजितैः ॥ २८॥

हिमानीतर्जिताम्भोजसदृशं वदनं तदा ।
निरीक्ष्य कृपयाविष्टो हरः प्रोवाच तामिदम् ॥ २९॥

English
Her body originally clad in barks of trees and deer-hide had by this time been completely covered by clusters of matted hair hanging down from the head and her face appeared like a lotus threatened by frost. On seeing her Śiva melted with pity and said to her. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
प्रीतोऽस्मि तपसा भद्रे भवत्याः परमेण वै ।
स्तवेन च शुभप्राज्ञे वरं वरय साम्प्रतम् ॥ ३०॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O gentle lady, I am delighted by your great penance and this eulogy. O auspiciously intelligent woman, you can choose your boon. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
येन ते विद्यते कार्यं वरेणास्मिन्मनोगतम् ।
तत्करिष्ये च भद्रं ते प्रसन्नोऽहं तव व्रतैः ॥ ३१॥

English
Whatever boon seems to be useful to you and is desired by you I shall grant it to you. I am delighted by your rites.” (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा महेशस्य प्रसन्नमनसस्तदा ।
सन्ध्योवाच सुप्रसन्ना प्रणम्य च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ३२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* On hearing these words of Śiva who was delighted, Sandhyā was highly pleased and she said after repeated obeisance. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्योवाच ।
यदि देयो वरः प्रीत्या वरयोग्यास्म्यहं यदि ।
यदि शुद्धास्म्यहं जाता तस्मात्पापान्महेश्वर ॥ ३३॥

यदि देव प्रसन्नोऽसि तपसा मम साम्प्रतम् ।
वृतस्तदायं प्रथमं वरो मम विधीयताम् ॥ ३४॥

English
*Sandhyā said :—*  O Lord Śiva, if I am to be favoured with the boon, if I am considered worthy of receiving a boon, if I am purified of that sin, if the lord is delighted with my penance, let the first boon chosen by me be granted. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पन्नमात्रा देवेश प्राणिनोऽस्मिन् भुवस्थले ।
न भवन्तु समेनैव सकामाः सम्भवन्तु वै ॥ ३५॥

English
Let no living being, O lord of the Gods, born in this atmosphere be full of lust at the time of its nativity. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्धि वृत्ता हि लोकेषु त्रिष्वपि प्रथिता यथा ।
भविष्यामि तथा नान्या वर एको वृतो मया ॥ ३६॥

English
This is another boon chosen by me that no other woman shall become so famous in the three worlds as I have become or shall become. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सकामा मम सृष्टिस्तु कुत्रचिन्न पतिष्यति ।
यो मे पतिर्भवेन्नाथ सोऽपि मेऽतिसुहृच्च वै ॥ ३७॥

English
No creation of mine shall become lustful or fall anywhere degraded. He who becomes my husband shall be my intimate friend of pure mind. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यो द्रक्ष्यति सकामो मां पुरुषस्तस्य पौरुषम् ।
गमिष्यति तदा नाशं स च क्लीबो भविष्यति ॥ ३८॥

English
Any person who looks at me with lustful eyes shall lose his manliness and become a eunuch. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्याः शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
उवाच सुप्रसन्नात्मा निष्पापायास्तयेरिते ॥ ३९॥

English
On hearing the words of that woman who had become freed of sin Śiva who is favourably disposed to His devotees and who was delighted at what she had said, spoke as follows. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि च सन्ध्ये त्वं त्वत्पापं भस्मतां गतम् ।
त्वयि त्यक्तो मया क्रोधः शुद्धा जाता तपःकरात् ॥ ४०॥

English
O lady Sandhyā, listen. Your sin has been reduced to ashes. I have abandoned my anger towards you. By this penance you have become pure. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यद्वृतं त्वया भद्रे दत्तं तदखिलं मया ।
सुप्रसन्नेन तपसा तव सन्ध्ये वरेण हि ॥ ४१॥

English
O gentle lady Sandhyā, whatever you have asked I grant you entirely. I am delighted by this excellent penance of yours. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं शैशवो भावः कौमाराख्यो द्वितीयकः ।
तृतीयो यौवनो भावश्चतुर्थो वार्धकस्तथा ॥ ४२॥

English
(In all living beings) the first stage shall be infancy, the second childhood, the third youth and the fourth stage shall be old age. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तृतीये त्वथ सम्प्राप्ते वयोभागे शरीरिणः ।
सकामाः स्युर्द्वितीयान्ते भविष्यति क्वचित् क्वचित् ॥ ४३॥

English
When the third stage in life is reached, the living beings shall become lustful. In some cases it shall be at the end of the second stage. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा तव मर्यादा जगति स्थापिता मया ।
उत्पन्नमात्रा न यथा सकामाः स्युः शरीरिणः ॥ ४४॥

English
This new limitation is imposed by me as a result of your penance. No living being shall be lustful at the time of its nativity. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं च लोके सतीभावं तादृशं समवाप्नुहि ।
त्रिषु लोकेषु नान्यस्या यादृशं सम्भविष्यति ॥ ४५॥

English
You will attain such a pure chastity as will not be attained by any other woman in the three worlds. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पश्यति सकामस्त्वां पाणिग्राहमृते तव ।
स सद्यः क्लीबतां प्राप्य दुर्बलत्वं गमिष्यति ॥ ४६॥

English
Excepting your husband whoever looks at you with lustful eyes shall immediately become impotent and weak. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिस्तव महाभागस्तपोरूपसमन्वितः ।
सप्तकल्पान्तजीवी च भविष्यति सह त्वया ॥ ४७॥

English
Your husband shall be one endowed with great fortune, penance and comely features. He shall live for a period of seven Kalpas along with you. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते ये वरा मत्तः प्रार्थितास्ते कृता मया ।
अन्यच्च ते वदिष्यामि पूर्वजन्मनि संस्थितम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Thus I have granted you all the boons requested by you. I shall tell you another incident that transpired in the previous birth. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

तदुपायं वदामि त्वां तत्कुरुष्व न संशयः ॥ ४९॥

English
That you would cast off your body in the fire has been foretold. I shall tell you the means thereof. You will certainly carry it out. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

कृत्स्नप्रज्वलिते वह्नावचिरात् क्रियतां त्वया ॥ ५०॥

English
Let that be performed by you at the twelve-year-sacrifice of the sage Medhātithi in the blazing sacrificial fire ere long. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छैलोपत्यकायां चन्द्रभागानदीतटे ।
मेधातिथिर्महायज्ञं कुरुते तापसाश्रमे ॥ ५१॥

English
In the ridge of this mountain, on the banks of this river Candrabhāgā, Medhātithi is performing a great penance in his hermitage. (51.)

तत्र गत्वा स्वयं छन्दं मुनिभिर्नोपलक्षिता ।
मत्प्रसादाद्वह्निजाता तस्य पुत्री भविष्यसि ॥ ५२॥

English
You go there unobserved by the sages. Thanks to my favour, you will become his fire-born daughter. (52.)

Sanskrit
यस्ते वरो वाञ्छनीयः स्वामी मनसि कश्चन ।
तं निधाय निजस्वान्ते त्यज वह्नौ वपुः स्वकम् ॥ ५३॥

English
If you have chosen in your mind a desirable bride-groom as your husband, you shall think of him while you consign your body into the fire. (53.)

Sanskrit
यदा त्वं दारुणं सन्ध्ये तपश्चरसि पर्वते ।
यावच्चतुर्युगं तस्य व्यतीते तु कृते युगे ॥ ५४॥

त्रेतायाः प्रथमे भागे जाता दक्षस्य कन्यकाः ।
यास्ताः शीलसमापन्ना यथायोग्यं विवाहिताः ॥ ५५॥

English
O Sandhyā, while you were performing severe penance—which had lasted for four yugas—in the earlier part of Tretā Yuga, after the Kṛta Yuga had elapsed, Dakṣa had begotten many chaste daughters who were also duly married. (54-55.)

Sanskrit
तन्मध्ये स ददौ कन्या विधवे सप्तविंशतिः ।
चन्द्रोऽन्याःसम्परित्यज्य रोहिण्यां प्रीतिमानभूत् ॥ ५६॥

English
He gave twenty seven of his daughters to the moon in marriage. But the moon had a special liking for only Rohiṇī and he neglected others. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्धेतोर्हि यदा चन्द्रः शप्तो दक्षेण कोपिना ।
तदा भवत्या निकटे सर्वे देवाः समागताः । ५७॥

न दृष्टाश्च त्वया सन्ध्ये ते देवा ब्रह्मणा सह ।
मयि विन्यस्तमनसा खं च दृष्ट्वा लभेत्पुनः ॥ ५८॥

चन्द्रस्य शापमोक्षार्थं जाता चन्द्रनदी तदा ।
सृष्टा धात्रा तदैवात्र मेधातिथिरुपस्थितः ॥ ५९॥

English
Hence, the moon was cursed by Dakṣa, the redemption being, when he sees the Ether he would find her there. At that time the Gods had come near you but since you were having your mind fixed in me, the Gods in the company of Brahmā were not seen by you. The river Candrabhāgā arose being created by Brahmā for the redemption of the moon from the curse. It was then that Medhātithi arrived here. (57-59.)

Sanskrit
तपसा तत्समो नास्ति न भूतो न भविष्यति ।
येन यज्ञः समारब्धो ज्योतिष्टोमो महाविधिः ॥ ६०॥

English
There is none equal to him in penance. There has never been such a person, nor will there ever be one. He has now started the sacrifice of Jyotiṣṭoma of many great rites. (60.)

Sanskrit
तत्र प्रज्वलितो वह्निस्तस्मिन्त्यज वपुः स्वकम् ।
इदानीं सुपवित्रा त्वं सम्पूर्णोऽस्तु पणस्तव ॥ ६१॥

English
In that blazing sacrificial fire you shall cast off your body. You are pure now. May your other desires be also fulfilled. (61.)

Sanskrit
एतन्मया स्थापितं स्वाकार्यार्थं भो तपस्विनि ।
तत्कुरुष्व महाभागे याहि यज्ञे महामुनेः ।
कृत्वा हितं च देवेशस्तत्रैवान्तरधीयत ॥ ६२॥

English
O Hermitess, these things have been ordained by me for my own end. O fortunate woman, do as I instruct you. Go to the sacrifice of that sage. Thus after instructing her for her welfare the lord vanished from the scene. (62.)
