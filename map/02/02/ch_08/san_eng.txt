### **CHAPTER EIGHT - *(The description of the form and features of Vasanta)***

मोहयस्व महादेवं रत्या सह महोद्यमः ॥ ५०॥

English
O Kāma, in the company of Rati and these companions, Vasanta and others, you shall exert yourself in charming Lord Śiva. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तां कामिनीं तात भावयिष्यामि यत्नतः ।
मनसा सुविचार्यैव या हरं मोहयिष्यति ॥ ५१॥

English
O dear one, I shall conceive and create that lovely woman who will finally captivate. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्तो मया कामः सुरज्येष्ठेन हर्षितः ।
ननाम चरणौ मेऽपि स पत्नीसहितस्तदा ॥ ५२॥

English
When Kāma was thus addressed by me (Brahmā), he was delighted and he fell at my feet along with his wife and offered obeisance. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षं प्रणम्य तान् सर्वान्मानसानभिवाद्य च ।
यत्रात्मा गतवान् शम्भुस्तत्स्थानं मन्मथो ययौ ॥ ५३॥

English
He bowed to Dakṣa and paid respects to my mental sons. He then went to the place where Śiva, the supreme Soul had gone. (53.)
