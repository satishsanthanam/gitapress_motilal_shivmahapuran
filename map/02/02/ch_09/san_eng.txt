### **CHAPTER NINE - *(The power of Kāma and the birth of his attendants)***

---

Sanskrit
९

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तस्मिन् गते सानुचरे शिवस्थानं च मन्मथे ।
चरित्रमभवच्चित्रं तच्छृणुष्व मुनीश्वर ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O great sage, when Kāma went to Śiva's abode along with his attendants an adversely surprising incident occurred to which listen please. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र महावीरो मन्मथो मोहकारकः ।
स्वप्रभावं ततानाशु मोहयामास प्राणिनः ॥ २॥

English
After going there, the heroic Kāma competent to enchant others spread all his wiles and charmed all living beings. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वसन्तोऽपि प्रभावं स्वं चकार हरमोहनम् ।
सर्वे वृक्षा एकदैव प्रफुल्ला अभवन्मुने ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, spring too showed his prowess in order to delude Śiva. All the trees simultaneously bloomed. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विविधान्कृतवान्यत्नान् रत्या सह मनोभवः ।
जीवाः सर्वे वशं याताः सगणेशः शिवो न हि ॥ ४॥

English
Kāma and Rati played many a trick. All living beings fell victims of their wiles but not Śiva, the lord of Gaṇas. (4.)

---

Sanskrit

जगाम स निजस्थानं निवृत्य विमदस्तदा ॥ ५॥

English
O sage, the efforts of Kāma who was accompanied by spring were futile. He returned to his residence being cured of his arrogance. (5.)

---

Sanskrit

उवाच मदनो मां चोदासीनो विमदो मुने ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, Kāma saluted me, and bereft of arrogance and completely despondent he told me in faltering voice. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
काम उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् शम्भुर्मोहनीयो न वै योगपरायणः ।
न शक्तिर्मम नान्यस्य तस्य शम्भोर्हि मोहने ॥ ७॥

English
*Kāma said :—*  O Brahmā, Śiva, an expert in Yogic practices cannot be charmed. Neither I nor anyone else has the power to enchant Śiva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit

रत्या सहाखिलास्ते च निष्फला अभवन् शिवे ॥ ८॥

English
O Brahmā, different tricks were tried by me and my friends as well as by Rati. All those became futile in regard to Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रयासान् विविधाँस्तात गदतस्तान्मुने मम ॥ ९॥

English
O Brahmā, listen to the different kinds of efforts undertaken by us in trying to enchant Him and the manner we did them I shall explain, O sage. (9.)

---

Sanskrit

उद्वेजयामि रुद्रं स्म नित्यं मोहनकारिणा ।
प्रयत्नतो महादेवं समाधिस्थं त्रिलोचनम् ॥ ११॥

English
When Śiva was in the state of trance with full control of senses, I tried to agitate him—the three-eyed lord Śiva, through the fragrant cool breeze that blew with force and that usually thrilled everyone. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वसायकांस्तथा पञ्च समादाय शरासनम् ।
तस्याभितो भ्रमतस्तु मोहयंस्तद्गणानहम् ॥ १२॥

English
I lifted up my bow and fitted my reputed five arrows. Going round him I tried to enchant him. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

अभवद्विकृतो नैव शङ्करः सगणः प्रभुः ॥ १३॥

English
Even as I entered the zone, the living beings fell into my power but lord Śiva and his Gaṇas were not moved at all. (13.)

---

Sanskrit

तत्रागतस्तदैवाहं सरतिः समधुर्विधे ॥ १४॥

English
O Brahmā, when Śiva went to the Himālayan ridge, Rati, Spring and I reached the place. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा मेरुं गतो रुद्रो यदा वा नागकेशरम् ।
कैलासं वा यदा यातस्तत्राहं गतवांस्तदा ॥ १५॥

English
Wherever He went whether on Meru$^{247}$ Nāgakeśara$^{248}$ or Kailāsa, I too went there immediately. (15.)

[fn 247. Meru is situated in the centre of the earth. It is described in the Purāṇas as the four-armed Svastika, evolving in four directions each with seven constituent members. It can be identified with the highland of Tartary, north of the Himālayas. It is variously called Su-meru, Hemādri (the Golden Mountain) Ratnasānu (jewel peak), Karṇikācala (lotus mountain), Amarādri, Deva-parvata, 'mountain of the Gods'. On its extent and identification with the Great Pamir knot of Asia, see the Geography of the Purāṇas Ch. III. PP. 47-52.]

[fn 248. Nāga-Keśara, the Nāga mountain which can be identified with the Farghana Valley on the basis of the produce of this region, the account of which is given by Huen-Tsang. Ibid. Ch. V. PP. 80-81.]

English

English

English

ययौ मारगणैः सार्धं शिवस्थानं मुनीश्वर ॥ ५५॥

English
O great sage, after saying thus Kama, accompanied by Vasanta and Rati started with his troops to the abode of Siva, despite the fear lurking in his mind. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्ववत् स्वप्रभावं च चक्रे मनसिजस्तदा ।
बहूपायं स हि मधुर्विविधां बुद्धिमावहन् ॥ ५६॥

English
Kama employed all his wiles as before. Vasanta too employed various means racking his brain in diverse ways. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
उपायं स चकाराति तत्र मारगणोऽपि च ।
मोहोऽभवन्न वै शम्भोरपि कश्चित्परात्मनः ॥ ५७॥

English
He used many tactics. His troops too tried their best. But Siva, the Great Soul, was not afflicted the least. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्त्य पुनरायातो मम स्थानं स्मरस्तदा ।
आसीन्मारगणोऽगर्वोऽहर्षो मेऽपि पुरस्थितः ॥ ५८॥

English
Kama then returned to my abode. I had great pride in his troops but now distress and discomfit stood facing me. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
कामः प्रोवाच मां तात प्रणम्य च निरुत्सवः ।
स्थित्वा मम पुरोऽगर्वो मारैश्च मधुना तदा ॥ ५९॥

English
O dear, bowing to me with despair and dejection while standing before me without pride and arrogance, along with his troops and Vasanta, Kama spoke to me in these words. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं पूर्वादधिकतः कर्म तन्मोहने विधे ।
नाभवत्तस्य मोहोऽपि कश्चिद्ध्यानरतात्मनः ॥ ६०॥

English
O Brahma, more efforts were put by us to enthral Him, but they all went in vain as He was absorbed in deep meditation. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
न दग्धा मे तनुश्चैव तत्र तेन दयालुना ।
कारणं पूर्वपुण्यं च निर्विकारः स वै प्रभुः ॥ ६१॥

English
There my body was not reduced to ashes because He is merciful. My previous merits too may have been the cause. As for the lord there is no affectation or change in Him. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
चेद्वरस्ते हरो भार्यां गृह्णीयादिति पद्मज ।
परोपायं कुरु तदा विगर्व इति मे मतिः ॥ ६२॥

English
O Brahmā, if you desire that Śiva should take a wife unto Himself, you should employ some means with modesty. This is what I think proper in the circumstances. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा सपरीवारो ययौ कामः स्वमाश्रमम् ।
प्रणम्य मां स्मरन् शम्भुं गर्वदं दीनवत्सलम् ॥ ६३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Saying this, Kāma returned to his abode along with his followers after saluting me and remembering Śiva, the destroyer of arrogance and the favourite of His devotees. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सर्वमहं दृष्ट्वा ज्ञात्वा तस्य च भावनाम् ।
विमुखोऽहं शम्भुमोहान्नियतं ते वदाम्यहम् ॥ २८॥
