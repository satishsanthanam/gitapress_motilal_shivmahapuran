### **CHAPTER TEN - (*Brahmā-Viṣṇu dialogue*)**

---

Sanskrit
१०

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं शिवसक्तधीः ।
कथितं सुचरित्रं ते शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, the fortunate, the dispenser of the fruits of our actions, you are a blessed devotee of Śiva, as your mind is fixed in him. You have narrated to me the good story of Śiva, the great soul. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
निजाश्रमे गते कामे सगणे सरतौ ततः ।
किमासीत्किमकार्षीस्त्वं तश्चरित्रं वदाधुना ॥ २॥

English
When Kāma returned to his hermitage with Rati and his followers what happened and what steps you took? Please narrate that now. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु नारद सुप्रीत्या चरित्रं शशिमौलिनः ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण निर्विकारो भवेन्नरः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada listen lovingly to the story of the moon-crested lord, a mere listening to which makes a man free from depravity and decay. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
निजाश्रमं गते कामे परिवारसमन्विते ।
यद् बभूव तदा जातं तच्चरित्रं निबोध मे ॥ ४॥

English
When Kāma returned to his abode with Rati and his followers what happened next, you can hear from me in full detail. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
नष्टोऽभून्नारद मदो विस्मयोऽभूच्च मे हृदि ।
निरानन्दस्य च मुनेऽपूर्णे निजमनोरथे ॥ ५॥

English
O sage Nārada, my arrogance was quashed when my desire remained unrealised. And surprise filled my dissatisfied and distressed heart. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अशोचं बहुधा चित्ते गृह्णीयात्स कथं स्त्रियम् ।
निर्विकारो जितात्मा स शङ्करो योगतत्परः ॥ ६॥

English
How will Śiva who is free from depravity, who has conquered himself and who is devoted to Yogic practices take up a wife unto himself? Thinking thus I bewailed a lot. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं विचार्य बहुधा तदाहं विमदो मुने ।
हरिं तं सोऽस्मरं भक्त्या शिवात्मानं स्वदेहदम् ॥ ७॥

English
Anxiously thinking all this about, O sage, I became free from haughtiness. I remembered Viṣṇu who is identical with Śiva and who is the cause of my origin. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्तवं च शुभस्तोत्रैर्दीनवाक्यसमन्वितैः ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा भगवानाशु बभूवाविर्हि मे पुरा ॥ ८॥

English
I eulogised Him with auspicious hymns supplemented by statements of my miserable predicament on hearing which the lord appeared before me immediately. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्भुजोऽरविन्दाक्षः शङ्खपद्मगदाधरः ।
लसत्पीतपटः श्यामतनुर्भक्तप्रियो हरिः ॥ ९॥

English
The lord Viṣṇu with four arms, lotus-like eyes, holding conch, lotus and mace in his hands and wearing the refulgent yellow robe, dark-complexioned and the beloved of the devotees. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा तादृशमहं सुशरण्यं मुहुर्मुहुः ।
अस्तवं च पुनः प्रेम्णा बाष्पगद्गदया गिरा ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing him in that form I eulogised him again with devotion and words choked with tears. I considered him as my sole refuge. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिराकर्ण्य तत्स्तोत्रं सुप्रसन्न उवाच माम् ।
दुःखहा निजभक्तानां ब्रह्माणं शरणं गतम् ॥ ११॥

English
On hearing this psalm of prayer, Viṣṇu, the destroyer of the miseries of his devotees, became delighted and spoke to me who sought refuge in him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिरुवाच ।
विधे ब्रह्मन् महाप्राज्ञ धन्यस्त्वं लोककारक ।
किमर्थं स्मरणं मेऽद्य कृतं च क्रियते नुतिः ॥ १२॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “O Brahmā of great intellect, you are the blessed creator of the world. Why did you remember me? Why do you laud me? (12.)

---

Sanskrit
किं जातं ते महद्दुःखं मदग्रे तद्वदाधुना ।
शमयिष्यामि तत्सर्वं नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १३॥

English
What great misery has befallen you? Tell me now. I shall quell it entirely. You need not entertain any doubt in this respect.” (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति विष्णोर्वचः श्रुत्वा किञ्चिदुच्छ्वसिताननः ।
अवोचं वचनं विष्णुं प्रणम्य सुकृताञ्जलिः ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of Viṣṇu I heaved a sigh of relief and raised my face. I spoke to Viṣṇu with due salutations and palms joined in reverence. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव रमानाथ मद्वार्तां श‍ृणु मानद ।
श्रुत्वा च करुणां कृत्वा हर दुःखं कमावह ॥ १५॥

English
O lord of Lakṣmī, lord of Gods, please listen to my submission, O bestower of Honour. On hearing it please take pity, remove my misery and bestow happiness on me. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रसम्मोहनार्थं हि कामं प्रेषितवानहम् ।
परिवारयुतं विष्णो समारमधुबान्धवम् ॥ १६॥

English
O Viṣṇu, I sent Kāma with his followers, Māras, Spring and others in order to fascinate Rudra. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रुस्ते विविधोपायान् निष्फला अभवंश्च ते ।
नाभवत्तस्य सम्मोहो योगिनः समदर्शिनः ॥ १७॥

English
They employed various means but in vain. He, the ascetic of equanimity, was not moved at all. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य वचो मे स हरिर्मां प्राह विस्मितः ।
विज्ञाताखिलदो ज्ञानी शिवतत्त्वविशारदः ॥ १८॥

English
On hearing these words of mine Viṣṇu the omniscient who is conversant in the principles of Śiva-cult was surprised and spoke to me thus. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
कस्माद्धेतोरिति मतिस्तव जाता पितामह ।
सर्वं विचार्य सुधिया ब्रह्मन् सत्यं हि तद्वद ॥ १९॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O Brahmā, how is it that such an idea entered into your mind? Considering everything sensibly tell me the truth. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात चरित्रं तत् तव माया विमोहिनी ।
तदधीनं जगत्सर्वं सुखदुःखादितत्परम् ॥ २०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Dear lord, hear the story. Your magic is very fascinating. The world is attracted by it. Happiness and misery are based on the same. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ययैव प्रेषितश्चाहं पापं कर्तुं समुद्यतः ।
आसं तच्छृणु देवेश वदामि तव शासनात् ॥ २१॥

English
Induced by that I resolved on committing the sin. Please listen. At your bidding I shall narrate it in detail. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिप्रारम्भसमये दश पुत्रा हि जज्ञिरे ।
दक्षाद्यास्तनया चैका वाग्भवाप्यतिसुन्दरी ॥ २२॥

English
At the beginning of the creation ten sons were born to me together with a very beautiful daughter originating from my speech.$^{250}$ (22.)

[fn 250. In regard to the number of Brahmā's sons the Purānas differ considerably. See Note 242.]
---

Sanskrit
धर्मो वक्षःस्थलात्कामो मनसोऽन्योऽपि देहतः ।
जातास्तत्र सुतां दृष्ट्वा मम मोहोऽभवद्धरे ॥ २३॥

English
Dharma originated from the heart and Kāma from the various parts of my body. O Viṣṇu, on seeing my daughter I was highly fascinated. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कुदृष्ट्या तां समद्राक्षं तव मायाविमोहितः ।
तत्क्षणाद्धर आगत्य मामनिन्दत्सुतानपि ॥ २४॥

English
I looked at her with a distorted vision since I had been deluded by your Māyā. Immediately Śiva came there and reproached me and my sons too. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
धिक्कारं कृतवान् सर्वान् निजं मत्वा परं प्रभुम् ।
ज्ञानिनं योगिनं नाथं भोगिनं विजितेन्द्रियम् ॥ २५॥

English
He rebuked us considering Himself the sole lord, possessed of supreme knowledge and adept in Yogic practices and an enjoyer with full control over all sense-organs. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रो भूत्वा मम हरेऽनिन्दन्मां च समक्षतः ।
इति दुःखं महन्मे हि तदुक्तं तव सन्निधौ ॥ २६॥

English
O Viṣṇu, my sorrow is that even after manifesting Himself as my son He reproached me face to face. I have mentioned it to you now. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
गृह्णीयाद्यदि पत्नीं स स्यां सुखी नष्टदुःखधीः ।
एतदर्थं समायातः शरणं तव केशव ॥ २७॥

English
If He were to take a wife unto Himself I shall become happy and forget all my miseries. O Keśava, it is for this purpose that I have sought refuge in you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचो मे हि ब्रह्मणो मधुसूदनः ।
विहस्य मां द्रुतं प्राह हर्षयन्भवकारकम् ॥ २८॥

English
On hearing these words of mine, Viṣṇu laughed and spoke immediately delighting me, the cause of entire creation. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
विधे श‍ृणु हि मद्वाक्यं सर्वं भ्रमनिवारणम् ।
सर्वं वेदागमादीनां सम्मतं परमार्थतः ॥ २९॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O Brahmā listen to my words in full. It will eradicate your frustration. It will be consistent with what is said in the Vedas and Āgamas and what is in conformity with reality. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
महामूढमतिश्चाद्य सञ्जातोऽसि कथं विधे ।
वेदवक्तापि निखिललोककर्ता हि दुर्मतिः ॥ ३०॥

English
O Brahmā, how is it that you became so utterly confused in the mind? It is improper for the reciter of the Vedas and the creator of the universe to be so wicked. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
जडतां त्यज मन्दात्मन् कुरु त्वं नेदृशीं मतिम् ।
किं ब्रुवन्त्यखिला वेदाः स्तुत्या तत्स्मर सद्धिया ॥ ३१॥

English
O slow-witted one, cast-off this sluggishness. Do not indulge in such foolish thoughts hereafter. What is it that the Vedas say by means of their hymns? Think on it with a pure mind. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रं जानासि दुर्बुद्धे स्वसुतं परमेश्वरम् ।
वेदवक्तापि विज्ञानं विस्मृतं तेऽखिलं विधे ॥ ३२॥

English
You foolishly think of Rudra, the great lord as your son. O Brahman, though the reciter of the Vedas you have forgotten all true knowledge. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करं सुरसामान्यं मत्वा द्रोहं करोषि हि ।
सुबुद्धिर्विगता तेऽद्याविर्भूता कुमतिस्तथा ॥ ३३॥

English
Considering Śiva on a par with ordinary Gods you are maliciously disposed towards Him. Your good intents have vanished and evil ones have cropped up. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्त्वसिद्धान्तमाख्यातं श‍ृणु सद्बुद्धिमावह ।
यथार्थं निगमाख्यातं निर्णीय भवकारकम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Listen to the first principle that had been narrated of old. Have clean conscience. It is the true Being that is glorified as the cause of all Creation. This is decisive. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः सर्वस्वकर्ता हि भर्ता हर्ता परात्परः ।
परब्रह्म परेशश्च निर्गुणो नित्य एव च ॥ ३५॥

English
Śiva is the creator of everything, the sustainer and destroyer. He is greater than the great. He is the supreme Brahman, the greatest lord, the attributeless, the eternal. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अनिर्देश्यो निर्विकारः परमात्माद्वयोऽच्युतः ।
अनन्तोऽन्तकरः स्वामी व्यापकः परमेश्वरः ॥ ३६॥

English
He cannot be defined. He is not subject to deterioration or decay. He is the supreme soul, without a second, unswerving and endless. He is the cause of dissolution, all-pervasive and great lord. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिस्थितिविनाशानां कर्ता त्रिगुणभाग्विभुः ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशाख्यो रजः सत्त्वतमः परः ॥ ३७॥

English
He is all-pervasive, possessed of three guṇas, for the causation of creation, sustenance and dissolution in the name of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa but really beyond Rajas, Śattva and Tamas—the three attributes. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
मायाभिन्नो निरीहश्च मायो मायाविशारदः ।
सगुणोऽपि स्वतन्त्रश्च निजानन्दोऽविकल्पकः ॥ ३८॥

English
He is distinct from illusion. He is free from desires. He is the creator of illusion yet uninfluenced by illusion. He is an adept. He is possessed of attributes yet independent of them. He is blissful in Himself. He is free from suspicions and alternatives. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मारामो हि निर्द्वन्द्वो भक्ताधीनःसुविग्रहः ।
योगी योगरतो नित्यं योगमार्गप्रदर्शकः ॥ ३९॥

English
He rests and relaxes in His own soul. He is free from the pair of opposites, such as happiness and unhappiness. He is subservient to His devotees in a fine physical body. He is a yogin devoted always to the practice of Yogas. He is guide to the path of Yoga. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्वापहारी लोकेशः सर्वदा दीनवत्सलः ।
एतादृशो हि यः स्वामी स्वपुत्रं मन्यसे हि तम् ॥ ४०॥

English
He is the lord of the worlds and the destroyer of arrogance. He is favourably disposed to the miserable. Such is the lord—our master whom you consider your son! (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशं त्यज कुज्ञानं शरणं व्रज तस्य वै ।
भज सर्वात्मना शम्भुं सन्तुष्टः शं विधास्यति ॥ ४१॥

English
Cast-off all these stupid notions. Seek refuge in Him. Worship Him exclusively. When He is propitiated He will bestow on you all that is auspicious and beneficent. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
गृह्णीयाच्छङ्करः पत्नीं विचारो हृदि चेत्तव ।
शिवामुद्दिश्य सुतपः कुरु ब्रह्मन् शिवं स्मरन् ॥ ४२॥

English
O Brahmā, if a thought surges in your heart that Śiva should take a wife unto Himself, you must perform penance directed to Śiva and think upon Śiva. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
कुरु ध्यानं शिवायात्स्वं काममुद्दिश्य तं हृदि ।
सा चेत्प्रसन्ना देवेशी सर्वं कार्यं विधास्यति ॥ ४३॥

English
Meditate upon Śiva with that desire cherished in your heart. If that Goddess is propitiated she will do everything. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वावतारं सगुणा यदि स्यान्मानुषी शिवा ।
कस्यचित्तनया लोके सा तत्पत्नी भवेद् ध्रुवम् ॥ ४४॥

English
If Śivā takes an incarnation as a human being in Her attributive aspect, as the daughter of a person in the world, She will definitely become His wife. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षमाज्ञापय ब्रह्मन् तपः कुर्य्यात्प्रयत्नतः ।
तामुत्पादयितुं पत्नीं शिवार्थं भक्तितः स्वतः ॥ ४५॥

English
O Brahmā, command Dakṣa. Let him carry out a penance strenuously with a great devotion to beget her to be given as a wife unto Śiva. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्ताधीनौ च तौ तात सुविज्ञेयौ शिवाशिवौ ।
स्वेच्छया सगुणौ जातौ परब्रह्मस्वरूपिणौ ॥ ४६॥

English
O dear, Śivā and Śiva are subservient to their devotees. This must be realised. Both of them being intrinsically the supreme Brahman can readily assume attributive form out of their own free will. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तत्क्षणं मेशः शिवं सस्मार स्वप्रभुम् ।
कृपया तस्य सम्प्राप्य ज्ञानमूचे च मां ततः ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying so, the lord of Lakṣmī thought upon his lord Śiva. Thanks to His favour, he received the real knowledge and spoke to me again. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
विधे स्मर पुरोक्तं यद्वचनं शङ्करेण च ।
प्रार्थितेन यदावाभ्यामुत्पन्नाभ्यां तदिच्छया ॥ ४८॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—*  O Brahmā remember the words spoken by Śiva formerly when requested, due to His own will, by us at the time of our nativity. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
विस्मृतं तव तत्सर्वं धन्या या शाम्भवी परा ।
तया सम्मोहितं सर्वं दुर्विज्ञेया शिवं विना ॥ ४९॥

English
Everything has been forgotten by you. Blessed indeed is the great illusion of Śiva which deludes everything. It is incomprehensible to all except Śiva. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा हि सगुणो जातः स्वेच्छया निर्गुणः शिवः ।
मामुत्पाद्य ततस्त्वां च स्वशक्त्या सुविहारकृत् ॥ ५०॥

उपादिदेश त्वां शम्भुः सृष्टिकार्यं तदा प्रभुः ।
तत्पालनं च मां ब्रह्मन् सोमः सूतिकरोऽव्ययः ॥ ५१॥

English
When Śiva devoid of attributes became, out of His own accord, full of attributes, He created me first and then you with His own power in the course of His divine sport. The lord Śiva assigned to you the work of creation. O Brahman, the imperishable Śiva, the cause of creation, entrusted me with the task of sustaining it. (50-51.)

Sanskrit

भव त्वमसि सर्वेशोऽवतारी गुणरूपधृक् ॥ ५२॥

इत्युक्तः प्राह स स्वामी विहस्य करुणान्वितः ।
दिवमुद्वीक्ष्य सुप्रीत्या नानालीलाविशारदः ॥ ५३॥

English
Then we requested Him—"O Śiva, the lord of all, be pleased to take an incarnation with all your attributes." Thus requested He laughed and spoke sympathetically, with his eyes raised to Heaven. Verily He is an adept in divine sports. (52-53.)
