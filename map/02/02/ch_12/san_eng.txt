### **CHAPTER TWELVE - *(Dakṣa granted the boon)***

---

Sanskrit
१२

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् शम्भुवर प्राज्ञ सम्यगुक्तं त्वयानघ ।
शिवाशिवचरित्रं च पावितं जन्म मे हितम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O Brahmā, the sinless and the intelligent one, you have splendidly narrated the story of Śivā and Śiva. My life has been sanctified. This is conducive to my benefit. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं वद दक्षस्तु तपः कृत्वा दृढव्रतः ।
कं वरं प्राप देव्यास्तु कथं सा दक्षजाभवत् ॥ २॥

English
Now please tell me, what the boon was that Dakṣa, with steady sacred rites and austere penance, secured from the Goddess, and how she became Dakṣa's daughter. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु नारद धन्यस्त्वं मुनिभिर्भक्तितोऽखिलैः ।
यथा तेपे तपो दक्षो वरं प्राप च सुव्रतः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O Nārada, listen. You are blessed. You are revered by all sages with devotion. Hear how with good sacred rites, Dakṣa performed penance and secured boons. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मदाज्ञप्तः सुधीर्दक्षः समाधाय महाधिपः ।
अयाद्यष्टुं च तां देवीं तत्कामो जगदम्बिकाम् ॥ ४॥

English
At my bidding, the intelligent Dakṣa the great chief controlled his mind and went to worship the Goddess, the mother of the universe, with that cherished desire. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षीरोदोत्तरतीरस्थां तां कृत्वा हृदयस्थिताम् ।
तपस्तप्तुं समारेभे द्रष्टुं प्रत्यक्षतोऽम्बिकाम् ॥ ५॥

English
He went to the northern shore of the ocean of milk$^{252}$ and began to perform the penance, keeping the mother of the universe in his heart. He wished to see the Goddess in person. (5.)

[fn 252. See Note 200 P. 224.]
---

Sanskrit
दिव्यवर्षेण दक्षस्तु सहस्राणां त्रयं समाः ।
तपश्चचार नियतः संयतात्मा दृढव्रतः ॥ ६॥

English
For three thousand divine years he performed the penance with good sacred rites, controlling his mind and keeping himself pure. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मारुताशी निराहारो जलाहारी च पर्णभुक् ।
एवं निनाय तं कालं चिन्तयन्तां जगन्मयीम् ॥ ७॥

English
For some years, he sustained himself on taking in only air, abstaining from food, for some years taking only water and for some years taking only leaves as food. Thus he spent the time meditating upon the Goddess in cosmic form. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्गाध्यानसमासक्तश्चिरं कालं तपोरतः ।
नियमैर्बहुभिर्देवीमाराधयति सुव्रतः ॥ ८॥

English
He was intensively devoted to the meditation of the Goddess. He was engaged in the penance for a long time. With sacred rites and various restraints he worshipped the Goddess. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो यमादियुक्तस्य दक्षस्य मुनिसत्तम ।
जगदम्बां पूजयतः प्रत्यक्षमभवच्छिवा ॥ ९॥

English
O excellent sage, then Śivā appeared in person to Dakṣa who maintained all restraints, Yama<sup>253</sup> etc. and worshipped the mother of the universe. (9.)

[fn 253. Yama=Self-restraint. It is the first of the eight means of attaining mental concentration. The rest are नियम, आसन, प्रायायाम, प्रत्याहार, पारखा, ध्यान and समाधि । For details see बोधसार pp. 121-128.

Yamas are usually said to be ten :

अक्षचयै दया भ्रान्तिदानं सत्यमकल्कता ।
अहिंस्रास्तेयमाधुर्यं दमरचेति यमाः स्तुताः ॥]
---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रत्यक्षतो दृष्ट्वा जगदम्बां जगन्मयीम् ।
कृतकृत्यमथात्मानं मेने दक्षः प्रजापतिः ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing the mother of the universe cosmic in form, Dakṣa the lord of the subjects considered himself well rewarded. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहस्थां कालिकां कृष्णां चारुवक्त्रां चतुर्भुजाम् ।
वरदाभयनीलाब्जखड्गहस्तां मनोहराम् ॥ ११॥

English
With various sorts of prayer he eulogised and bowed to the Goddess mother of the universe, Kālikā seated on a lion, dark-complexioned, with four arms and beautiful face, the bestower of the boon, the abode of safety, holding a blue lotus and the sword in her hands, comely with reddish eyes and with beautiful dishevelled hair. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
आरक्तनयनां चारुमुक्तकेशीं जगत्प्रसूम् ।
तुष्टाव वाग्भिश्चित्राभिः सुप्रणम्याथ सुप्रभाम् । १२॥

दक्ष उवाच ।
जगदबं महामाये जगदीशे महेश्वरि ।
कृपां कृत्वा नमस्तेऽस्तु दर्शितं स्ववपुर्मम ॥ १३॥

English
Dakṣa said:—  Obeisance to Thee, O great Goddess, mother of the universe, wielding the great illusion, the ruler of the universe. It is with great favour that Thou showed Thy own body to me. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसीद भगवत्याद्ये प्रसीद शिवरूपिणि ।
प्रसीद भक्तवरदे जगन्माये नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १४॥

English
Be pleased, O primordial Goddess, be pleased, O Goddess in the form of Śiva; be pleased, O bestower of boons to the devotees; obeisance be to Thee, O wielder of illusion over the universe. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति स्तुता महेशानी दक्षेण प्रयतात्मना ।
उवाच दक्षं ज्ञात्वापि स्वयं तस्येप्सितं मुने ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, thus eulogised by Dakṣa of purified soul, the Goddess spoke to Dakṣa, although she knew what his desire was. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
तुष्टाहं दक्ष भवतः सद्भक्त्या ह्यनया भृशम् ।
वरं वृष्णीष्व स्वाभीष्टं नादेयं विद्यते तव ॥ १६॥

English
*The Goddess said:—*  O Dakṣa I am very much delighted by your great devotion. Choose a boon according to your desire. There is nothing which shall not be granted to you. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
जगदम्बावचः श्रुत्वा ततो दक्षः प्रजापतिः ।
सुप्रहृष्टतरः प्राह नामं नामं च तां शिवाम् ॥ १७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of the mother of the universe, Dakṣa Prajāpati was very happy and he said to Śivā after bowing to her frequently. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
जगदम्ब महामाये यदि त्वं वरदा मम ।
मद्वचः श‍ृ णु सुप्रीत्या मम कामं प्रपूरय ॥ १८॥

English
*Dakṣa said:—*  O wielder of great illusion, O mother of the universe, if you wish to grant me any boon please listen to my words with pleasure. Be pleased to fulfil my desire. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मम स्वामी शिवो यो हि स जातो ब्रह्मणः सुतः ।
रुद्रनामा पूर्णरूपावतारः परमात्मनः ॥ १९॥

English
My lord and master Śiva has manifested Himself as Brahmā’s son in the name of Rudra. He is the perfect and fullfledged incarnation of the supreme soul. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तवावतारो नो जातः का तत्पत्नी भवेदतः ।
तं मोहय महेशानमवतीर्य क्षितौ शिवे ॥ २०॥

English
You have not so far incarnated. Who will be His wife? Hence O Śivā, take an incarnation on the Earth and fascinate the great lord. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वदृते तस्य मोहाय न शक्तान्या कदाचन ।
तस्मान्मम सुता भूत्वा हरजाया भवाधुना ॥ २१॥

English
Excepting you, no other lady will ever be competent to enthral Him. Hence be born as my daughter and become Śiva’s consort. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं कृत्वा सुलीलां च भव त्वं हरमोहिनी ।
ममैवैष वरो देवि सत्यमुक्तं तवाग्रतः ॥ २२॥

English
Exhibiting your divine sports as this, O Goddess, you be the enchantress of Śiva, this is the only boon I crave of you. I speak out the truth to you. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वार्थं न केवलं मेऽस्ति सर्वेषां जगतामपि ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवानां च ब्रह्मणा प्रेरितो ह्यहम् ॥ २३॥

English
This fulfils my own interests. Indeed it fulfils the interests of all the worlds as well as those of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. Hence I have been induced by Brahmā in this direction. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य प्रजेशस्य वचनं जगदम्बिका ।
प्रत्युवाच विहस्येति स्मृत्वा तं मनसा शिवम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Dakṣa the mother of the universe replied smilingly after thinking on Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
तात प्रजापते दक्ष श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।
सत्यं ब्रवीमि त्वद्भक्त्या सुप्रसन्नाखिलप्रदा ॥ २५॥

English
# *The Goddess said :—*  Dear one, O Dakṣa Prajāpati, listen to my weighty words. I tell you the truth. I am much delighted by your devotion. I shall bestow everything. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तव सुता दक्ष त्वज्जायायां महेश्वरी ।
भविष्यामि न सन्देहस्त्वद्भक्तिवशवर्तिनी ॥ २६॥

English
Subservient to your devotion, O Dakṣa, I, the great Goddess, shall be born of your wife as your daughter. There is no doubt in this. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा यत्नं करिष्यामि तपः कृत्वा सुदुःसहम् ।
हरजाया भविष्यामि तद्वरं प्राप्य चानघ ॥ २७॥

English
O sinless one, I shall perform a penance strenuously and shall become Śiva’s wife, after I have secured a boon from Him to that effect. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नान्यथा कार्यसिद्धिर्हि निर्विकारी च स प्रभुः ।
विधेर्विष्णोश्च संसेव्यः पूर्ण एव सदाशिवः ॥ २८॥

English
Otherwise there is no chance of the fulfilment of the object. The lord is free from all aberrations. He is the full incarnation of Sadāśiva, worthy of being served by Brahmā and Viṣṇu. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तस्य सदा दासी प्रिया जन्मनि जन्मनि ।
मम स्वामी स वै शम्भुर्नानारूपधरोऽपि ह ॥ २९॥

English
I am His slave for ever, His beloved in every birth (incarnation). Śiva who manifests Himself in many forms is indeed my master. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
वरप्रभावाद् भ्रुकुटेरवतीर्णो विधेः स च ।
अहं तद्वरतोऽपीहावतरिष्ये तदाज्ञया ॥ ३०॥

English
It was by His favour that He manifested through the eyebrows of Brahmā. I too shall incarnate by His favour and at His bidding. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
गच्छ स्वभवनं तात मया ज्ञाता तु दूतिका ।
हरजाया भविष्यामि भूत्वा ते तनयाचिरात् ॥ ३१॥

English
O dear one, go back to your residence. I have known my mission. Born as your daughter, ere long I shall be Śiva’s wife. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा सद्वचो दक्षं शिवाज्ञां प्राप्य चेतसि ।
पुनः प्रोवाच सा देवी स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ३२॥

English
Having spoken these splendid words, She sought and obtained Śiva’s permission through mental communion While thinking on Śiva’s lotus-like feet, the Goddess spoke as follows :— (32.)

---

Sanskrit
परन्तु प्रण आधेयो मनसा ते प्रजापते ।
श्रावयिष्यामि ते तं वै सत्यं जानीहि नो मृषा ॥ ३३॥

English
But O Prajāpati, you have to take a vow. It is a precondition. I shall tell you. It is true, never false, please understand. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा भवान् मयि पुनर्भवेन्मन्दादरस्तदा ।
देहं त्यक्ष्ये निजं सत्यं स्वात्मन्यस्म्यथ वेतरम् ॥ ३४॥

English
If in future you were to be less respectful to me I will cast off my body. I shall withdraw myself to my soul or take to another form. It is true. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एष दत्तस्तव वरः प्रतिसर्गं प्रजापते ।
अहं तव सुता भूत्वा भविष्यामि हरप्रिया ॥ ३५॥

English
O Dakṣa, this boon has been granted to you. At every creation I shall be born as your daughter and become the beloved of Śiva. (35.)

---

English
# *Brahmā said :—*  After speaking thus to Dakṣa the chief Prajāpati, the great Goddess immediately vanished even as Dakṣa was watching. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा महेशानी दक्षं मुख्यप्रजापतिम् ॥

अन्तर्दधे द्रुतं तत्र सम्यग् दक्षस्य पश्यतः । ३६॥

अन्तर्हितायां दुर्गायां स दक्षोऽपि निजाश्रमम् ।
जगाम च मुदं लेभे भविष्यति सुतेति सा ॥ ३७॥

English
When she had vanished, Dakṣa returned to his hermitage. He rejoiced because he knew that the great Goddess would become his daughter. (37.)
