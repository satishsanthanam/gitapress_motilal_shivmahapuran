### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - *(Nārada is cursed by Dakṣa)***

---

Sanskrit
१३

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे महाप्राज्ञ वद नो वदतां वर ।
दक्षे गृहं गते प्रीत्या किमभूत्तदनन्तरम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā of great intelligence, O eloquent one, please tell us what happened after Dakṣa went home with great delight ? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
दक्षः प्रजापतिर्गत्वा स्वाश्रमं हृष्टमानसः ।
सर्गं चकार बहुधा मानसं मम चाज्ञया ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Dakṣa Prajāpati returned with pleasure to his hermitage and began mental creations at my bidding. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तमबृंहितमालोक्य प्रजासर्गं प्रजापतिः ।
दक्षो निवेदयामास ब्रह्मणे जनकाय मे ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the creation, not increasing in size, Dakṣa Prajāpati informed me his father, Brahmā. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
ब्रह्मंस्तात प्रजानाथ वर्धन्ते न प्रजाः प्रभो
मया विरचिताः सर्वास्तावत्यो हि स्थिताः खलु ॥ ४॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  O Brahmā, lord of subjects, these subjects are not flourishing. They are conceived by me but they remain stationary. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
किं करोमि प्रजानाथ वर्धेयुः कथमात्मना ।
तदुपायं समाचक्ष्व प्रजाः कुर्यां न संशयः ॥ ५॥

English
O lord of subjects what shall I do ? How can they flourish themselves ? Please instruct me in the means thereof. I shall certainly create subjects. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
दक्ष प्रजापते तात श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।
तत्कुरुष्व सुरश्रेष्ठ शिवस्ते शं करिष्यति ॥ ६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Dakṣa Prajāpati, listen to my weighty words and carry out the direction. Śiva will bless you with welfare. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
या च पञ्चजनस्याङ्गसुता रम्या प्रजापतेः ।
असिक्नी नाम पत्नीत्वे प्रजेश प्रतिगृह्यताम् ॥ ७॥

English
O lord of subjects, let Asiknī, the beautiful daughter of Pañcajana, the lord of five tribes, be taken by you as your consort. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
वामव्यवायधर्मस्त्वं प्रजासर्गमिमं पुनः ।
तद्विधायां च कामिन्यां भूरिशो भावयिष्यसि ॥ ८॥

English
Indulging in sexual intercourse you can create subjects many in number in a beautiful woman like her. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
ततःसमुत्पादयितुं प्रजा मैथुनधर्मतः ।
उपयेमे वीरणस्य निदेशान्मे सुतां ततः ॥ ९॥

English
Then, in order to procreate subjects by way of coitus he married the daughter of Vīraṇa at my bidding. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्यां स्वपत्न्यां च वीरिण्यां स प्रजापतिः ।
हर्यश्वसंज्ञानयुतं दक्षः पुत्रानजीजनत् ॥ १०॥

English
Then in his wife Vīriṇī, Dakṣa Prajāpati begot sons named Haryaśvas.$^{254}$ (10.)

[fn 254. Haryaśvas were the sons of the patriarch Dakṣa, five thousand in number, begotten by him for the purpose of peopling the earth. The sage Nārada dissuaded them from producing offspring and they dispersed themselves through the regions and never returned. H.M. P. 120.]
---

Sanskrit
अपृथग्धर्मशीलास्ते सर्व आसन् सुता मुने ।
पितृभक्तिरता नित्यं वेदमार्गपरायणाः ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, all those sons were devoted to their father and followed the Vedic path. They did not have separate virtues and practices. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृप्रोक्ताः प्रजासर्गकरणार्थं ययुर्दिशम् ।
प्रतीचीं तपसे तात सर्वे दाक्षायणाः सुताः ॥ १२॥

English
Advised by their father, O dear one, the sons of Dakṣa went in the western direction for penance in order to create subjects (progeny). (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र नारायणसरस्तीर्थं परमपावनम् ।
सङ्गमो यत्र सञ्जातो दिव्यसिन्धुसमुद्रयोः ॥ १३॥

English
There they came to the holy lake Nārāyaṇa where the celestial Sindhu has its confluence with the ocean. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदुपस्पर्शनादेव प्रोत्पन्नमतयोऽभवन् ।
धर्मे पारमहंसे च विनिर्धूतमलाशयाः ॥ १४॥

English
On touching the holy water, their intellect was sharpened. The Dharma of holy ascetics eradicated all their impurities. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजाविवृद्धये ते वै तेपिरे तत्र सत्तमाः ।
दाक्षायणा दृढात्मानः पित्रादेशेसुयन्त्रिता ॥ १५॥

English
For making progenies flourish, the excellent sons of Dakṣa, fettered by the command of their father began to perform Tapas with steady resolve. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं च तान् नारद ज्ञात्वा तपतः सृष्टिहेतवे ।
अगमस्तत्र भूरीणि हार्दमाज्ञाय मापतेः ॥ १६॥

English
O Nārada, you came to know that they were performing penance for the sake of creation. You realised the intention of Viṣṇu and went there. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अदृष्ट्वा तं भुवः सृष्टिं कथं कर्तुं समुद्यताः ।
हर्यश्वा दक्षतनया इत्यवोचंस्तमादरत् ॥ १७॥

English
"O Haryaśvas, sons of Dakṣa, how is it that you have begun your attempts at creation without seeing the end of the earth?" So you asked them with respect. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तं निशम्याथ हर्यश्वास्ते त्वदुक्तमतन्द्रिताः ।
औत्पत्तिकधियः सर्वे स्वयं विममृशुर्भृशम् ॥ १८॥

English
They heard what you said eagerly. With their minds fixed on creation they deliberated on the proposal. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सुशास्त्रजनकादेशं यो न वेद निवर्तकम् ।
स कथं गुणविश्रम्भी कर्तुं सर्गमुपक्रमेत् ॥ १९॥

English
How can a person begin the work of creation putting faith in the Guṇas alone if he does not know the command of the father of Sacred Texts (which implies) turning back? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इति निश्चित्य ते पुत्राः सुधियश्चैकचेतसः ।
प्रणम्य तं परिक्रम्यायुर्मार्गमनिवर्तकम् ॥ २०॥

English
Having made up their minds unanimously, the intelligent sons bowed to you and circumambulated. They then proceeded ahead on a path never to turn back. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद त्वं मनः शम्भोर्लोकानन्यचरो मुने ।
निर्विकारो महेशानमनोवृत्तिकरस्तदा ॥ २१॥

English
O sage Nārada, with your mind fixed on Śiva, and desirous of carrying out His orders you went to various worlds without any mental aberration. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
काले गते बहुतरे मम पुत्रः प्रजापतिः ।
नाशं निशम्य पुत्राणां नारदादन्वतप्यत ॥ २२॥

English
When much time elapsed, my son Prajāpati heard that the extinction of his sons was due to Nārada and became distressed. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहुर्मुहुरुवाचेति सुप्रजत्वं शुचां पदम् ।
शुशोच बहुशो दक्षः शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ २३॥

English
He frequently mused like this—“A multitude of sons brings only disaster”. Dakṣa who was deluded by Śiva’s illusion bewailed thus in many ways. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अहमागत्य सुप्रीत्या सान्त्वयं दक्षमात्मजम् ।
शान्तिभावं प्रदर्श्यैव दैवं प्रबलमित्युत ॥ २४॥

English
I went over to him and consoled my son Dakṣa out of love and reminded him that fate is all powerful. I pointed out the way to calmness. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दक्षः पञ्चजन्यां मया स परिसान्त्वितः ।
सबलाश्वाभिधान् पुत्रान् सहस्रं चाप्यजीजनत् ॥ २५॥

English
On being consoled by me, Dakṣa begot a thousand sons named Sabalāśvas in the daughter of Pañcajana. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तेऽपि जग्मुस्तत्र सुताः पित्रादिष्टा दृढव्रताः ।
प्रजासर्गे यत्र सिद्धाः स्वपूर्वभ्रातरो ययुः ॥ २६॥

English
At the bidding of their father, they too reached the place where their elder brothers, the Siddhas, had gone with the same steady resolve in the creation of subjects. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तदुपस्पर्शनादेव नष्टाघा विमलाशयाः ।
तेपुर्महत्तपस्तत्र जपन्तो ब्रह्म सुव्रताः ॥ २७॥

English
At the very touch of waters of the Nārāyaṇa lake they too had their sins quelled and became purified. They performed penance, strenuously repeating many mantras and performing sacred rites. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजासर्गोद्यतांस्तान् वै ज्ञात्वा गत्वेति नारद ।
पूर्ववच्चागदो वाक्यं संस्मरन्नैश्वरीं गतिम् ॥ २८॥

English
O Nārada, you came to know that they too were attempting the creation of subjects and you told them as before, mindful of the way of Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रातृपन्थानमादिश्य त्वं मुनेऽमोघदर्शन ।
अयाश्चोर्ध्वगतिं तेऽपि भ्रातृमार्गं ययुः सुताः ॥ २९॥

English
O sage, of beneficent sight, you showed, them the path followed by their brothers. You went upto heaven and the sons of Dakṣa went the way of their brothers. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पातान् बहुशोऽपश्यत्तदैव स प्रजापतिः ।
विस्मितोऽभूत्स मे पुत्रो दक्षो मनसि दुःखितः ॥ ३०॥

English
At the very same time, my son Dakṣa Prajāpati saw many an ill omen. He was disagreeably surprised and felt distressed. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्ववत् त्वत्कृतं दक्षः शुश्राव चकितो भृशम् ।
पुत्रनाशं शुशोचाति पुत्रशोकविमूर्च्छितः ॥ ३१॥

English
As before, Dakṣa heard that the disappearance of his sons was brought about by you. He bewailed a lot. He was stunned, grief-stricken at the loss of his sons. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
चुक्रोध तुभ्यं दक्षोऽसौ दुष्टोऽयमिति चाब्रवीत् ।
आगतस्तत्र दैवात् त्वमनुग्रहकरस्तदा ॥ ३२॥

English
Dakṣa was furious. He called you a wicked fellow. Fate caused you to go there at the psychological moment in the guise of one who wanted to bless him. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शोकाविष्टः स दक्षो हि रोषविस्फुरिताधरः ।
उपलभ्य तमाहत्य धिग्धिक् प्रोच्य विगर्हयन् ॥ ३३॥

English
The grief-stricken Dakṣa approached you with his lips throbbing with fury, taunted you and reproached you saying “Fie, Fie” and spoke to you. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
किं कृतं तेऽधमश्रेष्ठ साधूनां साधुलिङ्गतः ।
भिक्षोमार्गोऽर्भकानां वै दर्शितोऽसाधुकारि नः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  O foremost among the base, disguised in the garb of a saint, what is it that you have done to those good people—my sons? To those engaged in good actions the accursed path of a mendicant has been pointed out by you. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋणैस्त्रिभिरमुक्तानां लोकयोरुभयोः कृतः ।
विघातः श्रेयसोऽमीषां निर्दयेन शठेन ते ॥ ३५॥

English
Ruthless rogue that you are, even when they were not free from the three debts$^{255}$ you put obstacles in the path of their progress both here as well as hereafter. (35.)

[fn 255. Reference is made to the three debts which every person belonging to first three varṇas owes to the ancient seers, the ancestors, and the Gods. He owes Brahmacarya or study of the Vedas to the Rāis, sacrifice and worship to the Gods, procreation of a son to the Manes. See Manu VI. 35. In later times two more debts—benevolence to mankind and hospitality to guests are added.]
---

Sanskrit
ऋणानि त्रीण्यपाकृत्य यो गृहात्प्रव्रजेत्पुमान् ।
मातरं पितरं त्यक्त्वा मोक्षमिच्छन् व्रजत्यधः ॥ ३६॥

English
He who renounces the world desiring salvation, without repaying the three debts and departs from the house forsaking his parents surely courts downfall. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्दयस्त्वं सुनिर्लज्जः शिशुधीभिद्यशोऽपहा ।
हरेः पार्षदमध्ये हि वृथा चरसि मूढधीः ॥ ३७॥

English
You are unkind, shameless, distorter of the tender intellect of children, and a destroyer of fame. Why do you, a foolish fellow, move about among the attendants of Viṣṇu in vain? (37.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहुर्मुहुरभद्रं त्वमचरो मेऽधमाधम ।
विभवेद् भ्रमतस्तेऽतः पदं लोकेषु न स्थिरम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Frequently you have committed offences against me, O basest of the base. Hence roaming ever in the worlds your feet will never be steady anywhere. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शशापेति शुचा दक्षस्त्वां तदा साधुसम्मतम् ।
बुबोध नेश्वरेच्छां स शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ ३९॥

शापं प्रत्यग्रहीच्च त्वं स मुने निर्विकारधीः ।
एष एव ब्रह्मसाधो सहते सोऽपि च स्वयम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Grief-stricken Dakṣa cursed you thus, you who are honoured by saints. It was Śiva’s power of delusion that prevented him from understanding the will of Īśvara. Without your mind being affected the least, you accepted the curse. All saintly Brahminical saints forbear thus. (39-40.)
