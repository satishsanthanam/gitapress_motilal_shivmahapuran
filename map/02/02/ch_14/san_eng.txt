### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - *(The birth of Sati and her childish sports)***

---

Sanskrit
१४

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवमुने लोकपितामहः ।
तत्रागममहं प्रीत्या ज्ञात्वा तच्चरितं द्रुतम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  In the mean time, O celestial sage I, the grandfather of the worlds, came there, on hearing the incident. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
असान्त्वयमहं दक्षं पूर्ववत्सुविचक्षणः ।
अकार्षं तेन सुस्नेहं तव सुप्रीतिमावहन् ॥ २॥

English
I consoled Dakṣa as before. Clever that I was I made him friendly with you. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वात्मजं मुनिवर्यं त्वां सुप्रीत्या देववल्लभम् ।
समाश्वास्य समादाय प्रत्यपद्ये स्वधाम ह ॥ ३॥

English
O best of sages, I consoled you—my own son, beloved of the Devas, and taking you with love effected conciliation. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रजापतिर्दक्षोऽनुनीतो मे निजस्त्रियाम् ।
जनयामास दुहितॄः सुभगाः षष्टिसम्मिताः ॥ ४॥

English
Then Dakṣa, consoled by me, begot of his wife sixty comely daughters. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तासां विवाहं कृतवान् धर्मादिभिरतन्द्रितः ।
तदेव श‍ृणु सुप्रीत्या प्रवदामि मुनीश्वर ॥ ५॥

English
Without any lassitude he performed their marriages with Dharma and others. O excellent sage, listen to that with pleasure. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ददौ दश सुता दक्षो धर्माय विधिवन्मुने ।
त्रयोदश कश्यपाय मुनये त्रिनवेन्दवे ॥ ६॥

English
Dakṣa gave ten of his daughters duly in marriage to Dharma, thirteen to Kaśyapa the sage, and twenty seven to the moon. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
भूताङ्गिरः कृशाश्श्वाभ्यां द्वे द्वे पुत्र्यौ प्रदत्तवान् ।
तार्क्ष्याय चापराः कन्याः प्रसूतिप्रसवैर्यतः ॥ ७॥

English
He gave two daughters each to Bhṛgu, Aṅgiras and Kṛśāśva. The other daughters were given to Tārkṣya. The sons and grandsons and descendants of these filled the three worlds. A detailed narration is not attempted here. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिलोकाः पूरितास्तन्नो वर्ण्यते व्यासतो भयात् । ८॥

केचिद्वदन्ति तां ज्येष्ठां मध्यमां चापरे शिवाम् ।
सर्वानन्तरजां केचित्कल्पभेदात् त्रयं च सत् ॥ ९॥

English
Some say that Śivā was the eldest of his daughters, some say that she was the middle and some say that she was the youngest. All the three opinions are correct in different Kalpas. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्तरं सुतोत्पत्तेः सपत्नीकः प्रजापतिः ।
दक्षो दधौ सुप्रीत्या तां मनसा जगदम्बिकाम् ॥ १०॥

English
After the birth of his daughter, Dakṣa Prajāpati and his wife meditated on the mother of the universe with pleasure. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः प्रेम्णा च तुष्टाव गिरा गद्गदया हि सः ।
भूयो भूयो नमस्कृत्य साञ्जलिर्विनयान्वितः ॥ ११॥

English
Then he lovingly eulogised her with words choked at the throat and repeatedly paid respects to her humbly joining the palms in reverence. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तुष्टा सा तदा देवी विचारं मनसीति च ।
चक्रेऽवतारं वीरिण्यां कुर्यां पणविपूर्तये ॥ १२॥

English
The Goddess, who was highly delighted, thought within himself. “I shall incarnate in Vīriṇī in order to keep my word”. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सोवास मनसि दक्षस्य जगदम्बिका ।
विललास तदातीव स दक्षो मुनिसत्तम ॥ १३॥

English
O excellent sage, then the mother of the universe spoke to Dakṣa in mind. Then Dakṣa shone forth splendidly. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सुमुहूर्तेन दक्षोऽपि स्वपत्न्यां निदधे मुदा ।
दक्षपत्न्यास्तदा चित्ते शिवोवास दयान्विता ॥ १४॥

English
In an auspicious hour he deposited his semen in his wife. Thus full of compassion, Śivā began to reside in the womb of Dakṣa’s consort. (14.)

---

English
All the characteristic signs of pregnancy appeared in her. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आविर्बभूव चिह्नानि दोहदस्याखिलानि वै । १५॥

विरेजे वीरिणी तात हृष्टचित्ताधिकं च सा ।
शिवावासप्रभावात्तु महामङ्गलरूपिणी ॥ १६॥

English
Dear one, thanks to the power of the presence of Śivā, Vīriṇī had an auspicious appearance and shone all the more with mental pleasure. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कुलस्य सम्प्रदायैश्च श्रुतेश्चित्तसमुन्नतेः ।
व्यधत्त सुक्रिया दक्षः प्रीत्या पुंसवनादिकाः ॥ १७॥

English
As befitting the loftiness of his mind, customs and manners current in his family and the injunctions of the Vedas, Dakṣa performed the rites of Puṃsavana$^{256}$ etc. out of affection. (17.)

[fn 256. Puṃsavana is a pre-natal rite through which a male child is produced. Compare पुमान् प्रसूयते येन कर्मैथा तेषु सबनमीरितम् । Saunaka quoted in V.M.S. Vol. I. P. 166. As to the time of its performance, the authorities differ considerably. It is performed in the third, fourth, sixth or even in the eighth month of pregnancy. Cf. H.S. PP 60-63.]
---

Sanskrit
उत्सवोऽतीव सञ्जातस्तदा तेषु च कर्मसु ।
वित्तं ददौ द्विजातिभ्यो यथाकामं प्रजापतिः ॥ १८॥

English
Great festivities accompanied those rites. Dakṣa presented liberal sums of money to the brahmins. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्मिन्नवसरे सर्वे हर्यादयः सुराः ।
ज्ञात्वा गर्भगतां देवीं वीरिण्यां ते मुदं ययुः ॥ १९॥

English
On coming to know that the Goddess had come to the womb of Vīrīṇī, Viṣṇu and other Devas became very gay. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य च सर्वे ते तुष्टुवुर्जगदम्बिकाम् ।
लोकोपकारकरिणीं प्रणम्य च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २०॥

English
They all approached her and paid frequent respects to the benefactress of the worlds, eulogising the mother of the universe. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा ततस्ते बहुधा प्रशंसां हृष्टमानसाः ।
दक्षप्रजापतेश्चैव वीरिण्याः स्वगृहं ययुः ॥ २१॥

English
Delighted in their hearts they praised Dakṣa and Vīrīṇī in various ways and went to their respective abodes. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
गतेषु नवमासेषु कारयित्वा च लौकिकीम् ।
गतिं शिवा च पूर्णे सा दशमे मासि नारद ॥ २२॥

आविर्बभूव पुरतो मातुः सद्यस्तदा मुने ।
मुहूर्ते सुखदे चन्द्रग्रहतारानुकूलके ॥ २३॥

English
O Nārada, nine months had passed with due observance of worldly conventions. In the tenth month, in an auspicious happy hour, when the moon, the stars and the planets were favourably disposed, Śivā suddenly appeared, O sage, in front of her mother. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यां तु जातमात्रायां सुप्रीतोऽसौ प्रजापतिः ।
शैवदेवीति तां मेने दृष्ट्वा तां तेजसोल्बणाम् ॥ २४॥

English
As soon as she was born, Dakṣa was highly pleased. On seeing her extremely brilliant he was convinced that she was the Goddess Śivā herself. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाभूत्पुष्पसद्वृष्टिर्मेघाश्च ववृषुर्जलम् ।
दिशः शान्ता द्रुतं तस्यां जातायां च मुनीश्वर ॥ २५॥

English
O excellent sage, when she was born there was a gentle shower from the clouds accompanied by that of flowers. The quarters became tranquil immediately. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अवादयन्त त्रिदशाः शुभवाद्यानि खे गताः ।
जज्ज्वलुश्चाग्नयः शान्ताः सर्वमासीत्सुमङ्गलम् ॥ २६॥

English
The devas gathered in the sky and played on musical instruments; sacrificial fires kindled calmly; everything indicated auspiciousness. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरिणीसम्भवां दृष्ट्वा दक्षस्तां जगदम्बिकाम् ।
नमस्कृत्य करौ बद्ध्वा बहु तुष्टाव भक्तितः ॥ २७॥

English
On seeing the mother of the universe born of Vīrīṇī, Dakṣa joined his palms in reverence, paid respects to her and eulogised her. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
महेशानि नमस्तुभ्यं जगदम्बे सनातनि ।
कृपां कुरु महादेवि सत्ये सत्यस्वरूपिणि ॥ २८॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  O Goddess, the eternal mother of the universe, obeisance to Thee. O great Goddess, the Truthful and truth-featured, be pleased. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवा शान्ता महामाया योगनिद्रा जगन्मयी ।
या प्रोच्यते वेदविद्भिर्नमामि त्वां हितावहाम् ॥ २९॥

English
I bow to Thee, the bestower of benefits, Thee who art auspicious, calm, great illusion, mystic slumber and identical with the universe. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यया धाता जगत्सृष्टौ नियुक्तस्तां पुराकरोत् ।
तां त्वां नमामि परमां जगद्धात्रीं महेश्वरीम् ॥ ३०॥

English
I bow to Thee, the great mother of the universe, the great Goddess, by whom formerly Brahmā had been directed to create the worlds which he carried out. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यया विष्णुर्जगत्स्थित्यै नियुक्तस्तां सदाकरोत् ।
तां त्वां नमामि परमां जगद्धात्रीं महेश्वरीम् ॥ ३१॥

English
I bow to Thee, great support of the universe, the great Goddess, by whom formerly Viṣṇu had been directed to sustain the universe which he has been doing always. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
यया रुद्रो जगन्नाशे नियुक्तस्तां सदाकरोत् ।
तां त्वां नमामि परमां जगद्धात्रीं महेश्वरीम् ॥ ३२॥

English
I bow to Thee the great mother of the universe, the great Goddess, by whom formerly Rudra had been directed to annihilate the universe which he has been doing always. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
रजःसत्त्वतमोरूपां सर्वकार्यकरीं सदा ।
त्रिदेवजननीं देवीं त्वां नमामि च तां शिवाम् ॥ ३३॥

English
I bow to Thee, Śivā, of Rājasika Sāttvika, and Tāmasika forms, performing every thing always, and the Goddess mother of the three deities. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्त्वां विचिन्तयेद् देवि विद्याविद्यात्मिकां पराम् ।
तस्य भुक्तिश्च मुक्तिश्च सदा करतले स्थिता ॥ ३४॥

English
O Goddess, enjoyment of worldly pleasures and salvation are always within the reach of that person who meditates on thee in the form of Vidyā and Avidyā, every day. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्त्वां प्रत्यक्षतो देवि शिवां पश्यति पावनीम् ।
तस्यावश्यं भवेन्मुक्तिर्विद्याविद्याप्रकाशिका ॥ ३५॥

English
O Goddess, he who directly perceives Thee, the sanctifying deity, will certainly attain salvation with the discrimination of Vidyā and Avidyā. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ये स्तुवन्ति जगन्मातर्भवानीमम्बिकेति च ।
जगन्मयीति दुर्गेति सर्वं तेषां भविष्यति ॥ ३६॥

English
O mother of the universe, those who eulogise Thee with the names<sup>257</sup> of Bhavānī, Ambikā, Jaganmāyā and Durgā will have everything. (36.)

[fn 257. The Goddess has a great variety of names referable to her various forms, attributes and actions but these names are not always used accurately and distinctively. As the wife of God Śiva she is Bhavānī, as the mother of the world she is Ambikā or jaganmātā (the reading jaganmātā for Jaganmāyā is preferable). In her terrible form she is Durgā, the inaccessible.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति स्तुता जगन्माता शिवा दक्षेण धीमता ।
तथोवाच तदा दक्षं यथा माता श‍ृणोति न ॥ ३७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Śivā, the mother of the universe, eulogised by Dakṣa the intelligent, said to Dakṣa without making it heard by her mother. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं सम्मोह्य तथ्यं च तथा दक्षः श‍ृणोतु तत् ।
नान्यस्तथा शिवा प्राह नानोतिः परमेश्वरी ॥ ३८॥

English
Śivā, the great Goddess and the source of future creation, made everyone deluded and said in such a manner that Dakṣa heard the truth and not anyone else. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

अहमाराधिता पूर्वं सुतार्थं ते प्रजापते ।

English
*The Goddess said :—*  O Prajāpati, I had been propitiated formerly for becoming your daughter. Your wish has been fulfilled now. You can carry on your activities of penance. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा तदा देवी दक्षं च निजमायया ।

English
Having spoken thus to Dakṣa, the Goddess assumed infancy through her illusory power and began to cry near her mother. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On heaving the cry, the woman spoke in agitation. The servant-maids too became pleasantly agitated. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing the comely form of Asiknī's daughter, the women rejoiced. The citizens raised shouts of Victory then. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

दक्षोऽसिक्नी मुदं लेभे शुभं दृष्ट्वा सुताननम् ॥ ४३॥

English
There were great festivities with songs and musical instruments. On seeing the unearthly face of their daughter the pleasure of Dakṣa and Asiknī knew no bounds. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षः श्रुतिकुलाचारं चक्रे च विधिवत्तदा ।
दानं ददौ द्विजातिभ्योऽन्येभ्यश्च द्रविणं तथा ॥ ४४॥

English
Dakṣa duly performed all the conventional ceremonies and the rites of the Vedas. He gave various gifts to the brahmins and money to others. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Songs and dances were performed everywhere in a befitting manner. Musical instruments played auspicious songs repeatedly. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Hari and other Gods came with their attendants, along with the sages and joined the festivities. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing the daughter of Dakṣa, the Goddess mother of the universe, they bowed and eulogised her with auspicious hymns. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
In their great delight they shouted cries of victory. They praised Dakṣa and Vīrīṇī in particular. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Her other names in the world were assigned afterwards. They are auspicious and quell miseries in particular. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
