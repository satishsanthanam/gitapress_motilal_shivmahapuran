### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - *(The Sacred rites of Nandā and Hymn to Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
१५

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथैकदा पितुः पार्श्वे तिष्ठन्तीं तां सतीमहम् ।
त्वया सह मुनेऽद्राक्षं सारभूतां त्रिलोकके ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* O sage, once I saw Satī standing near her father along with you. She is, as it were, the essence of the three worlds. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पित्रा नमस्कृतं वीक्ष्य सत्कृतं त्वां च मां सती ।
प्रणनाम मुदा भक्त्या लोकलीलानुसारिणी ॥ २॥

English
When she saw both of us honoured and bowed to by her father, Satī following the conventions of the world, saluted us with joy and reverence. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणामान्ते सतीं वीक्ष्य दक्षदत्तशुभासने ।
स्थितोऽहं नारद त्वं च विनतामहमागदम् ॥ ३॥

English
At the end of obeisance, O Nārada, you and I sat in the fine seat provided by Dakṣa. When she humbly bowed again, I spoke to her. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वामेव यः कामयते यं तु कामयसे सति ।
तमाप्नुहि पतिं देवं सर्वज्ञं जगदीश्वरम् ॥ ४॥

English
O Satī, secure, as your husband, the lord of the universe, (the omniscient Śiva) who desires only you and whom you too desire. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यो नान्यां जगृहे नापि गृह्णाति न ग्रहीष्यति ।
जायां स ते पतिर्भूयादनन्यसदृशः शुभे ॥ ५॥

English
O auspicious lady, you shall secure, as your husband, the person who has not taken, does not take, and will not be taking another wife. He will be unlike others. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा सुचिरं तां वै स्थित्वा दक्षालये पुनः ।
विसृष्टौ तेन संयातौ स्वस्थानं तौ च नारद ॥ ६॥

English
O Nārada, after saying this to Satī we stayed in Dakṣa's abode for a long time. We were bidden farewell by him and we went to our respective places. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षोऽभवच्च सुप्रीतस्तदाकर्ण्य गतज्वरः ।
आददे तनयां स्वां तां मत्वा हि परमेश्वरीम् ॥ ७॥

English
On hearing that, Dakṣa became delighted and free from all worries. Thinking that she was a great Goddess, he took her with him. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं विहारै रुचिरैः कौमारैर्भक्तवत्सला ।
जहाववस्थां कौमारीं स्वेच्छाधृतनराकृतिः ॥ ८॥

English
Thus with various charming girlish sports the Goddess who is favourably disposed to her devotees and who had assumed human form out of her own will passed the state of girlhood. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
बाल्यं व्यतीत्य सा प्राप किञ्चिद्यौवनतां सती ।
अतीव तपसाङ्गेन सर्वाङ्गेषु मनोहरा ॥ ९॥

English
After passing her girlhood and reaching the state of early youth she attained beauty in every limb which blazed forth brilliantly. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षस्तां वीक्ष्य लोकेशः प्रोद्भिन्नान्तर्वयःस्थिताम् ।
चिन्तयामास भर्गाय कथं दास्य इमां सुताम् ॥ १०॥

English
Dakṣa, the lord of worlds, on seeing her blooming in the proper age thought within—"How shall I give my daughter to Śiva ?" (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सापि स्वयं भर्गं प्राप्तुमैच्छत्तदान्वहम् ।
पितुर्मनोगतिं ज्ञात्वा मातुर्निकटमागता ॥ ११॥

English
She too desired to attain Śiva. Her desire grew every day. After knowing her father's idea, she approached her mother. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पप्रच्छाज्ञां तपोहेतोः शङ्करस्य विशालधीः ।
मातुः शिवाथ वीरिण्याः सा सती परमेश्वरी ॥ १२॥

English
Satī, the great Goddess of wide intellect, sought the permission of her mother to perform the penance with Śiva as the goal, for the happiness of her mother Vīriṇī. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सती महेशानं पतिं प्राप्तुं दृढव्रता ।
सा तमाराधयामास गृहे मातुरनुज्ञया ॥ १३॥

English
Firmly resolved in her desire to secure Śiva as her husband, she propitiated him in her own house with the permission of her mother. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
आश्विने मासि नन्दायां तिथावानर्च भक्तितः ।
गुडौदनैः सलवणैर्हरं नत्वा निनाय तम् ॥ १४॥

English
In the month of Āśvina, (September-October), on Nandā Tithis (i.e. first, sixth and eleventh days of lunar fortnight) she worshipped Śiva with great devotion, offering cooked rice with jaggery and salt. She spent a month like that. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्तिकस्य चतुर्दश्यामपूपैः पायसैरपि ।
समाकीर्णैः समाराध्य सस्मार परमेश्वरम् ॥ १५॥

English
On the Caturdaśī (14th day) of the month of Kārttika (October-November), she worshipped and meditated on Lord Śiva, offering sweet pies and puddings. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्गशीर्षेऽसिताष्टम्यां सतिलैः सयवौदनैः ।
पूजयित्वा हरं कीलैर्निनाय दिवसान् सती ॥ १६॥

English
On the eighth day in the dark half of Mārgaśīrṣa (November-December), Satī worshipped Śiva with cooked barley and gingelly seeds and spent the other days (in devotion). (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पौषे तु शुक्लसप्तम्यां कृत्वा जागरणं निशि ।
अपूजयच्छिवं प्रातः कृशरान्नेन सा सती ॥ १७॥

English
On the seventh day in the bright half of Pauṣa (December-January) Satī spent the night in keeping awake and worshipped Śiva in the morning with cooked rice and Kṛśara (jaggery mixed with gingelly seeds). (17.)

---

Sanskrit
माघे तु पौर्णमास्यां सा कृत्वा जागरणं निशि ।
आर्द्रवस्त्रा नदीतीरेऽकरोच्छङ्करपूजनम् ॥ १८॥

English
She kept awake in the full-moon night of Māgha (January-February) and worshipped Śiva on the banks of the river wearing wet clothes. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्यासितभूतायां कृत्वा जागरणं निशि ।
विशेषतः समानर्च शैलूषैः सर्वयामसु ॥ १९॥

English
On the fourteenth day of the dark half of Phālguna (February-March), she kept awake in the night and performed special worship of Śiva with Bilva fruits and leaves in every period of three hours. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
चैत्रे शुक्लचतुर्दश्यां पलाशैर्दमनैः शिवम् ।
अपूजयद्दिवारात्रौ संस्मरन् सा निनाय तम् ॥ २०॥

English
On the Caturdaśī day of the bright half of Caitra (March-April) she worshipped Śiva with Palāśa and Damana flowers day and night. She spent (the rest of) the month remembering Him. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
माघशुक्लतृतीयायां तिलाहारयवौदनैः ।
पूजयित्वा सती रुद्रं गव्यैर्मासं निनाय तम् ॥ २१॥

English
After worshipping Him with cooked barley and gingelly seeds on the third day of the bright half of Māgha (January-February), she spent the month on the products of milk obtained from a cow. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्येष्ठस्य पूर्णिमायां वै रात्रौ सम्पूज्य शङ्करम् ।
वसनैर्बृहतीपुष्पैर्निराहारा निनाय तम् ॥ २२॥

English
After worshipping Him with the offerings of cloths and Bṛhatī flowers on the full-moon night of Jyeṣṭha (May-June) she spent the whole month observing fast. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आषाढस्य चतुर्दश्यां शुक्लायां कृष्णवाससा ।
बृहतीकुसुमैः पूजा रुद्रस्याकारि वै तथा ॥ २३॥

English
On the Caturdaśī day in the bright half of Āṣāḍha (June-July) wearing a black cloth, she worshipped Rudra with Bṛhatī$^{259}$ flohers. (23.)

[fn 259. Bṛhatī—a plant, of which the flowers are used in the worship of Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
श्रावणस्य सिताष्टम्यां चतुर्दश्यां च सा शिवम् ।
यज्ञोपवीतैर्वासोभिः पवित्रैरप्यपूजयत् ॥ २४॥

English
On the eighth and fourteenth days in the bright half of Śrāvaṇa (July-August), she worshipped Śiva with holy sacred threads and cloths. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
भाद्रे कृष्णत्रयोदश्यां पुष्पैर्नानाविधैः फलैः ।
सम्पूज्य च चतुर्दश्यां चकार जलभोजनम् ॥ २५॥

English
After worshipping Śiva with various fruits and flowers on the thirteenth day in the dark half of Bhādra (August-September) she took only water on the fourteenth day. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाविधैः फलैः पुष्पैः सस्यैस्तत्कालसम्भवैः ।
चक्रे सुनियताहारा जपन्मासे शिवार्चनम् ॥ २६॥

English
Keeping strict control over her diet and repeating various mantras she worshipped Śiva with different fruits, flowers and leaves fresh and readily available. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वमासे सर्वदिने शिवार्चनरता सती ।
दृढव्रताभवद्देवी स्वेच्छाधृतनराकृतिः ॥ २७॥

English
The Goddess Satī who had assumed human form out of her will, became firmly devoted to the worship of Śiva on every day and month. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं नन्दाव्रतं कृत्स्नं समाप्य सुसमाहिता ।
दध्यौ शिवं सती प्रेम्णा निश्चलाभूदनन्यधीः ॥ २८॥

English
Concluding all the sacred rites of Nandā, Satī began to meditate on Śiva with concentrated devotion. She was steady and she never thought of any one else. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवा मुनयश्चाखिला मुने ।
विष्णुं मां च पुरस्कृत्य ययुर्द्रष्टुं सतीतपः ॥ २९॥

English
In the meantime, O sage, devas and sages with Viṣṇu and me at their head came to see the penance of Satī. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्टागत्य सती देवैर्मूर्ता सिद्धिरिवापरा ।
शिवध्यानमहामग्ना सिद्धावस्थां गता तदा ॥ ३०॥

English
On arrival, Satī was seen by the devas as an achievement in embodied form or as success incarnate. She was completely engrossed in meditating on Śiva. She had reached the stage of the enlightened seers. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रुः सर्वे सुराः सत्यै मुदा साञ्जलयो नतिम् ।
मुनयश्च नतस्कन्धा विष्ण्वाद्याः प्रीतमानसाः ॥ ३१॥

English
With palms joined in reverence, the devas paid respects to Satī joyfully. The sages bent down their shoulders in respect. Viṣṇu and others became delighted. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सर्वे सुप्रसन्नाः विष्ण्वाद्याश्च सुरर्षयः ।
प्रशशंसुस्तपस्तस्याः सत्यास्तस्मात्सविस्मयाः ॥ ३२॥

English
Viṣṇu and others and the celestial sages joyously praised Satī's penance. They were even surprised at that. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रणम्य तां देवीं पुनस्ते मुनयः सुराः ।
जग्मुर्गिरिवरं सद्यः कैलासं शिववल्लभम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Bowing again to the Goddess, the sages and devas went immediately to Kailāsa, the great mountain dear to Śiva. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सावित्रीसहितश्चाहं सह लक्ष्म्या मुदान्वितः ।
वासुदेवोऽपि भगवाञ्जगामाथ हरान्तिकम् ॥ ३४॥

English
The lord Viṣṇu approached Śiva with great joy, accompanied by Lakṣmī and I too along with Sāvitri, the Goddess of speech. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र प्रभुं दृष्ट्वा सुप्रणम्य ससम्भ्रमाः ।
तुष्टुवुर्विविधैः स्तोत्रैः करौ बद्ध्वा विनम्रकाः ॥ ३५॥

English
On arrival there, after paying respects to the lord with great excitement we lauded Him with various hymns with palms joined in reverence. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
नमो भगवते तुभ्यं यत एतच्चराचरम् ।
पुरुषाय महेशाय परेशाय महात्मने ॥ ३६॥

English
# *The Devas said :—*  Obeisance to Thee, O lord, from whom the mobile and the immobile beings have originated. Obeisance to the great Puruṣa, Maheśa, the supreme Īśa and the great Ātman. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
आदिबीजाय सर्वेषां चिद्रूपाय पराय च ।
ब्रह्मणे निर्विकाराय प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्य च ॥ ३७॥

English
Obeisance to the primordial seed of every one, the Cidrūpa (one possessed of the form of consciousness), the Puruṣa beyond Prakṛti. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं प्रतिपच्येदं येनेदं विचकास्ति हि ।
यस्मादिदं यतश्चेदं यस्येदं त्वं च यत्रतः ॥ ३८॥

English
Obeisance to Thee who createst this world, by whom this is illuminated, from whom this originated, by whom this is sustained, to whom this belongs and by whom everything is kept under control. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
योऽस्मात्परस्माच्च परो निर्विकारी महाप्रभुः ।
ईक्षते यः स्वात्मनीदं तं नताः स्म स्वयम्भुवम् ॥ ३९॥

English
We bow to that self-born deity who is beyond this and everything that is great, who is the undepraved great lord, who sees these within Himself. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अविद्धदृक् परः साक्षी सर्वात्मानेकरूपधृक् ।
आत्मभूतः परब्रह्म तपन्तं शरणं गताः ॥ ४०॥

English
We have sought refuge at His feet who is the supreme Brahman, who is the soul of everyone, who is the greatest witness with unbarred vision and who assumes various forms. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
न यस्य देवा ऋषयः सिद्धाश्च न विदुः पदम् ।
कः पुनर्जन्तुरपरो ज्ञातुमर्हति वेदितुम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Obeisance to Him whose region is not known by devas, sages or Siddhas. How then can other creatures realise it or express it ? (41.)

---

Sanskrit
दिदृक्षवो यस्य पदं मुक्तसङ्गाः सुसाधवः ।
चरितं सुगतिर्नस्त्वं सलोकव्रतमव्रणम् ॥ ४२॥

English
He is our goal supreme, seeking to see whose region great saints free from attachment perform unmutilated vow of Release.$^{260}$ (42.)

[fn 260. See Note No 23 P. 45. 'Salokavrata' is a vow of release. Sālokya is a stage of mukti—an exemption from further transmigration. The released person lives in the same world with the deity and does not migrate to the other world.]
---

Sanskrit
त्वज्जन्मादिविकारा नो विद्यन्ते केऽपि दुःखदाः ।
तथापि मायया त्वं हि गृह्णासि कृपया च तान् ॥ ४३॥

English
Thou hast no change like death, birth etc. that yields misery, yet by means of Māyā thou assumest all these. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मै नमः परेशाय तुभ्यमाश्चर्यकर्मणे ।
नमो गिरां विदूराय ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने ॥ ४४॥

English
Obeisance to Thee who art the great Īśa and the performer of miracles. Obeisance to Brahman, the great soul who is far removed from words. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
अरूपायोरुरूपाय परायानन्तशक्तये ।
त्रिलोकपतये सर्वसाक्षिणे सर्वगाय च ॥ ४५॥

English
Obeisance to the formless Being of immense form, the great, of unlimited power, the lord of the three worlds, the witness of all and all-pervasive. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
नम आत्मप्रदीपाय निर्वाणसुखसम्पदे ।
ज्ञानात्मने नमस्तेऽस्तु व्यापकायेश्वराय च ॥ ४६॥

English
Obeisance to the light of Ātman, richly endowed with the happiness of liberation, of the form of knowledge. Obeisance to Thee, the all-pervasive Lord. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
नैष्कर्म्येण सुलभ्याय कैवल्यपतये नमः ।
पुरुषाय परेशाय नमस्ते सर्वदाय च ॥ ४७॥

English
Obeisance to the lord of salvation who is accessible only through the cessation of worldly activities. Obeisance to Thee the great Puruṣa, the great lord, the bestower of all. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेत्रज्ञायात्मरूपाय सर्वप्रत्ययहेतवे ॥ ४८॥

English
Obeisance to conscious principle in the corporal frame, identical with Ātman, the cause of all perception. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाध्यक्षाय महते मूलप्रकृतये नमः ।
पुरुषाय परेशाय नमस्ते सर्वदाय च ॥ ४९॥

English
Obeisance to the original Prakṛti, the great presiding deity of everything. Obeisance to Thee the great Puruṣa, the great lord, the bestower of all. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिनेत्रायेषुवक्त्राय सदाभासाय ते नमः ।
सर्वेन्द्रियगुणद्रष्ट्रे निष्कारण नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ५०॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, the three-eyed, the five-faced and the ever-luminous. Obeisance to Thee who hast no cause and who seest all the qualities of the sense-organs. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिलोककारणायाथापवर्गाय नमो नमः ।
अपवर्गप्रदायाशु शरणागततारिणे ॥ ५१॥

English
Obeisance, obeisance to Thee, the cause of the three worlds and salvation. Obeisance to the quick bestower of liberation, and deliverer of those who seek refuge. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाम्नायागमानां चोदधये परमेष्ठिने ।
परायणाय भक्तानां गुणानां च नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ५२॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, the ocean of the knowledge of Vedic texts. Obeisance to Thee, the great lord and the ultimate goal of devotees and possessed of three attributes. (52.)
