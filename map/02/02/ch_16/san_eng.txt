### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - *(Prayer to Śiva offered by Brahmā and Viṣṇu)***

---

Sanskrit
१६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति स्तुतिं च हर्यादिकृतामाकर्ण्य शङ्करः ।
बभूवातिप्रसन्नो हि विजहास च सूतिकृत् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the song of praise offered by Viṣṇu and others, Śiva, the cause of protection and enjoyment became delighted and smiled broadly. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मविष्णू तु दृष्ट्वा तौ सस्त्रीकौ सङ्गतौ हरः ।
यथोचितं समाभाष्य पप्रच्छागमनं तयोः ॥ २॥

English
On seeing Brahmā and Viṣṇu in the company of their consorts, Śiva addressed them suitably and asked them the purpose of their visit. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
हे हरे हे विधे देवा मुनयश्चाद्य निर्भयाः ।
निजागमनहेतुं हि कथयध्वं सुतत्त्वतः ॥ ३॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O devas and sages please  tell me precisely and without fear the purpose of your visit. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
किमर्थमागता यूयं किं कार्यं चेह विद्यते ।
तत्सर्वं श्रोतुमिच्छामि भवत्स्तुत्या प्रसन्नधीः ॥ ४॥

English
I am delighted at the hymn sung by you all. I wish to hear why you have all come here and what is the work to be done here. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति पृष्टे हरेणाहं सर्वलोकपितामहः ।
मुनेऽवोचं महादेवं विष्णुना परिचोदितः ॥ ५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, when we were asked by Śiva thus, I, the grandfather of the worlds, spoke to Lord Śiva on being prompted by Viṣṇu. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
यदर्थमागतावावां तच्छृणु त्वं सुरर्षिभिः ॥ ६॥

English
O great lord, lord of Devas, O lord, the ocean of mercy, please listen why we both have come here in the company of devas and sages. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विशेषतस्तवैवार्थमागता वृषभध्वज ।
सहार्थिनः सदा योग्यमन्यथा न जगद्भवेत् ॥ ७॥

English
O bull-emblemed deity, we have come here particularly for your sake, along with these suppliants. Otherwise the universe would not be in a proper state always. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिद्भविष्यन्त्यसुरा मम वध्या महेश्वर ।
हरेर्वध्यास्तथा केचिद्भवन्तश्चापि केचन ॥ ८॥

English
O great lord, some demons are to be killed by me, some by Viṣṇu and some by you. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
केचित्त्वद्वीर्यजातस्य तनयस्य महाप्रभो ।
मायावध्याः प्रभो केचिद्भविष्यन्त्यसुराः सदा ॥ ९॥

English
O great lord, some of them would be killed by your son and some will be killed by me. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तवैव कृपया शम्भोः सुराणां सुखमुत्तमम् ।
नाशयित्वासुरान् घोराञ्जगत्स्वास्थ्यं सदाभयम् ॥ १०॥

English
The devas attain happiness only by your favour. The universe can attain peace and fearlessness only after destroying the demons. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
योगयुक्ते त्वयि सदा रागद्वेषविवर्जिते ।
दयामात्रैकनिरते न वध्या ह्यथवा तव ॥ ११॥

English
Or they cannot be killed by you in as much as you are always merciful, free from love and hate and engaged in Yoga. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
आराधितेषु तेष्वीश कथं सृष्टिस्तथा स्थितिः ।
अतश्च भविता युक्तं नित्यं नित्यं वृषध्वज ॥ १२॥

English
O lord, Vṛṣadhvaja,$^{262}$ how can the activities of creation, sustenance and dissolution be carried on properly for ever, if they are not killed? (12.)

[fn 262. Vṛṣadhvaja (or Vṛṣabhadhvaja) is an appellation of Śiva derived from the fact of his having the emblem of Bull known as Nandin.]
---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिस्थित्यन्तकर्माणि न कार्याणि यदा तदा ।
शरीरभेदमस्माकं मायायाश्च न युज्यते ॥ १३॥

English
The activities of creation, sustenance and dissolution are to be pursued by us now and then. The difference in our bodies is perceptible only through illusion. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्वरूपा हि वयं भिन्नाः कार्यस्य भेदतः ।
कार्यभेदो न सिद्धश्चेद्रूपभेदोऽप्रयोजनः ॥ १४॥

English
Though our real form is one, we are different in as much as our activities are different. If there is no functional difference, the difference in forms is meaningless. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एक एव त्रिधा भिन्नः परमात्मा महेश्वरः ।
मायायाः कारणादेव स्वतन्त्रो लीलया प्रभुः ॥ १५॥

English
The same supreme Ātman, lord Śiva manifests in three different ways due to Māyā. The lord is independent in his divine sports. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वामाङ्गजो हरिस्तस्य दक्षिणाङ्गभवो ह्यहम् ।
शिवस्य हृदयाज्जातस्त्वं हि पूर्णतनुः शिवः ॥ १६॥

English
Viṣṇu is born of his left limb. I am born of the right limb. You are born of the heart of Śiva and are his full-fledged incarnation. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं वयं त्रिधा भूताः प्रभो भिन्नस्वरूपिणः ।
शिवाशिवसुतास्तत्त्वं हृदा विद्धि सनातन ॥ १७॥

English
Thus, O lord, we have become three, with different forms. We are the sons of Śivā and Śiva which, O eternal one, you must note. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विष्णुश्च सस्त्रीकौ सञ्जातौ कार्यहेतुतः ।
लोककार्यकरौ प्रीत्या तव शासनतः प्रभो ॥ १८॥

English
Viṣṇu and I have become united with our wives for the performance of our function. O lord, with great pleasure we carry on our activities in the world at your bidding. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्विश्वहितार्थाय सुराणां सुखहेतवे ।
परिगृह्णीष्व भार्यार्थे रामामेकां सुशोभनाम् ॥ १९॥

English
Hence, for the benefit of the universe, for the happiness of the devas, you must accept an auspicious lady as your wife. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यच्छृणु महेशान पूर्ववृत्तं स्मृतं मया ।
यन्नो पुरः पुरा प्रोक्तं त्वयैव शिवरूपिणा ॥ २०॥

English
O great lord, please listen to another incident of bygone days, just recollected by me. You yourself as Śiva mentioned this to us formerly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्रूपं परमं ब्रह्मन्नीदृशं भवदङ्गतः ।
प्रकटीभविता लोके नाम्ना रुद्रः प्रकीर्तितः ॥ २१॥

English
“O Brahmā, this my great form, exactly as this, will be manifested through your limb. He will be known as Rudra in the world. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिकर्ताभवद् ब्रह्मा हरिः पालनकारकः ।
लयकारी भविष्यामि रुद्ररूपो गुणाकृतिः ॥ २२॥

English
Brahmā is the cause of creation, Viṣṇu is sustainer. I shall be the cause of dissolution in the form of Rudra, a Saguṇa form. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियं विवाह्य लोकस्य करिष्ये कार्यमुत्तमम् ।
इति संस्मृत्य स्वप्रोक्तं पूर्णं कुरु निजं पणम् ॥ २३॥

English
I shall marry a woman and perform the excellent function.” These are your words. Remembering these words please fulfil your promise. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
निदेशस्तव च स्वामिन्नहं सृष्टिकरो हरिः ।
पालको लयहेतुस्त्वमाविर्भूतः स्वयं शिवः ॥ २४॥

English
O lord, this is your own directive that I be the creator and Viṣṇu the protector. Śiva Himself has manifested in your form as the cause of dissolution. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां विना न समर्थौ हि आवां च स्वस्वकर्मणि ।
लोककार्यरतां तस्मादेकां गृह्णीष्व कामिनीम् ॥ २५॥

English
We two are unable to perform our duties without you. Hence take up a beloved consort who too will be engaged in the activities of creation. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा पद्मालया विष्णोः सावित्री च यथा मम ।
तथा सहचरीं शम्भो कान्तां गृह्णीष्व सम्प्रति ॥ २६॥

English
O Śiva, accept a beloved wife as a life companion in the same manner as Viṣṇu has taken the Goddess of the lotus (Lakṣmī) and I have taken the goddess of speech. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचो मे हि ब्रह्मणः पुरतो हरेः ।
स मां जगाद लोकेशः स्मेराननमुखो हरः ॥ २७॥

English
On hearing these words of mine—of Brahmā—in the presence of Viṣṇu, Śiva, the lord of worlds spoke to me with his face beaming with a smile. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
हे ब्रह्मन् हे हरे मे हि युवां प्रियतरौ सदा ।
दृष्ट्वा वां च ममानन्दो भवत्यतितरां खलु ॥ २८॥

English
Śiva said :—  O Brahmā, O Viṣṇu, both of you are always dear to me. On seeing you both, my delight is enhanced indeed. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
युवां सुरविशिष्टौ हि त्रिभुवस्वामिनौ किल ।
कथनं वां गरिष्ठेति भवकार्यरतात्मनोः ॥ २९॥

English
You two are the best among the devas. You are the masters of three worlds. What you say is indeed weighty since you two are engaged in Śiva's work. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
उचितं न सुरश्रेष्ठौ विवाहकरणं मम ।
तपोरतविरक्तस्य सदा विदितयोगिनः ॥ ३०॥

English
O best of Devas, it is not proper for me to marry as I am detached from the world and engaged in penance. I always practise Yoga. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यो निवृत्तिसुमार्गस्थः स्वात्मारामो निरञ्जनः ।
अवधूततनुर्ज्ञानी स्वद्रष्टा कामवर्जितः ॥ ३१॥

अविकारी ह्यभोगी च सदाशुचिरमङ्गलः ।
तस्य प्रयोजनं लोके कामिन्या किं वदाधुना ॥ ३२॥

English
Of what avail is a beloved to me in this world since I am in the path of abstinence delighting myself in my own soul, freed of attachment, unsullied, with the body of an ascetic, possessed of knowledge, seeing himself, free from aberrations and a non-reveller. Besides I am always unclean and inauspicious. Hence say now what can I do with a loving wife? (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
केवलं योगलग्नस्य ममानन्दः सदास्ति वै ।
ज्ञानहीनस्तु पुरुषो मनुते बहु कामकम् ॥ ३३॥

विवाहकरणं लोके विज्ञेयं परबन्धनम् ।
तस्मात्तस्य रुचिर्नो मे सत्यं सत्यं वदाम्यहम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Even as I am engaged in Yoga, I experience the mystic Bliss. Only a man devoid of perfect knowledge will make much of marriage and desire it. Actually it is a great bondage. Hence I am not interested in it. This is truth. I am telling you the truth. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
न स्वार्थं मे प्रवृत्तिर्हि सम्यक् स्वार्थविचिन्तनात् ।
तथापि तत्करिष्यामि भवदुक्तं जगद्धितम् ॥ ३५॥

English
None of my activities is pursued with self-interest. Yet I shall carry out what you have suggested for the benefit of the universe. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्वा वचो गरिष्ठं वा निजोक्तिपरिपूर्तये ।
करिष्यामि विवाहं वै भक्तवश्यः सदा ह्यहम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Considering your weighty words for the fulfilment of my promise and the goal of my task, I shall marry. I am always subservient to my devotees. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
परन्तु यादृशीं कान्तां ग्रहीष्यामि यथापणम् ।
तच्छृणुष्व हरे ब्रह्मन् युक्तमेव वचो मम ॥ ३७॥

English
O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, you must hear what sort of a wife I will be taking in accordance with that promise. What I say is indeed proper. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
या मे तेजः समर्था हि ग्रहीतुं स्याद्विभागशः ।
तां निदेशय भार्यार्थे योगिनीं कामरूपिणीम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Suggest a woman of comely features and Yogic practice who will be able to receive my semen virile in parts. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
योगयुक्ते मयि तथा योगिन्येव भविष्यति ।
कामासक्ते मयि तथा कामिन्येव भविष्यति ॥ ३९॥

English
She must be a Yogini when I practise Yoga and a loving woman when I indulge in love. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यमक्षरं वेदविदो निगदन्ति मनीषिणः ।
ज्योतीरूपं शिवं ते च चिन्तयिष्ये सनातनम् ॥ ४०॥

तच्चिन्तायां यदासक्तो ब्रह्मन् गच्छामि भाविनीम् ।
तत्र या विघ्नजननी न भवित्री हतास्तु मे ॥ ४१॥

English
Sometimes I will be thinking about Śiva, my own form of splendour, the eternal principle which the scholars well versed in the Vedas call Imperishable. When I go in trance, O Brahmā, in that meditation, damned be she who causes an impediment therein. (40-41.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं वा विष्णुरहं वापि शिवस्य ब्रह्मरूपिणः ।
अंशभूता महाभागा योग्यं तदनुचिन्तनम् ॥ ४२॥

English
You, Viṣṇu and I are the parts exceedingly of Brahman. So we are exceedingly fortunate. It is but proper to think about Him. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्चिन्तया विनोद्वाहं स्थास्यामि कमलासन ।
तस्माज्जायां प्रादिश त्वं मत्कर्मानुगतां सदा ॥ ४३॥

English
It is this worry that kept me unmarried, O lotus-seated (Brahmā). Hence, get me a wife who will follow my activities ever. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राप्येकं पणं मे त्वं श‍ृणु ब्रह्मँश्च मां प्रति ।
अविश्वासो मदुक्ते चेन्मया त्यक्ता भविष्यति ॥ ४४॥

English
There is another condition to which please also listen, O Brahmā. If she evinces a disbelief in me or in what I say, I shall abandon her. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वाहं स विष्णुर्हरस्य च ।
सस्मितं मोदितमनोऽवोचं चेति विनम्रकः ॥ ४५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Śiva in the presence of Viṣṇu, smilingly and joyously I spoke thus in humble spirits. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु नाथ महेशान मार्गिता यादृशी त्वया ।
निवेदयामि सुप्रीत्या तां स्त्रियं तादृशीं प्रभो ॥ ४६॥

English
O lord Śiva, I shall suggest such a woman as you desire for yourself. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
उमा सा भिन्नरूपेण सञ्जाता कार्यसाधिनी ।
सरस्वती तथा लक्ष्मीर्द्विधारूपा पुरा प्रभो ॥ ४७॥

English
She is Umā, O lord. Formerly she manifested herself in the forms of Sarasvatī and Lakṣmī in order to fulfil her task. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
पद्मा कान्ताभवद्विष्णोस्तथा मम सरस्वती ।
तृतीयरूपा सा नोऽभूल्लोककार्यहितैषिणी ॥ ४८॥

English
Lakṣmī became the wife of Viṣṇu and Sarasvatī mine. From her desire for the welfare of the world she has taken a third form. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षस्य तनया याभूत्सती नाम्ना तु सा विभो ।
सैवेदृशी भवेद्भार्या भवेद्धि हितकारिणी ॥ ४९॥

English
She is born now as Dakṣa’s daughter in the name of Satī. O lord, she will be an ideal wife rendering wholesome service. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
सा तपस्यति देवेश त्वदर्थं हि दृढव्रता ।
त्वां पतिं प्राप्तुकामा वै महातेजोवती सती ॥ ५०॥

English
O lord of devas, at present she is performing penance for securing you. She is firm in her austere rites. She desires you as her husband. Indeed she is highly brilliant. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
दातुं गच्छ वरं तस्यै कृपां कुरु महेश्वर ।
तां विवाहय सुप्रीत्या वरं दत्त्वा च तादृशम् ॥ ५१॥

English
O Lord Śiva, be merciful to her. Grant her the desired boon. Then lovingly marry her. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
हरेर्मम च देवानामियं वाञ्छास्ति शङ्कर ।
परिपूरय सद्दृष्ट्या पश्यामोत्सवमादरात् ॥ ५२॥

English
O Śiva, this is the desire of Viṣṇu, the devas and mine too. With a benignant look fulfil our desire. Let us see the wedding festivities with devotion. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
मङ्गलं परमं भूयात् त्रिलोकेषु सुखावहम् ।
सर्वज्वरो विनश्येद् वै सर्वेषां नात्र संशयः ॥ ५३॥

English
Let there be a happy and auspicious occasion (for that) in the three worlds. Let all ailments vanish. Let there be no doubt about it. (53.)

Sanskrit
अथवास्मद्वचःशेषेऽवदत्तं मधुसूदनः ।
लीलाजाकृतिमीशानं भक्तवत्सलमच्युतः ॥ ५४॥

English
Then at the conclusion of my speech, Viṣṇu, the slayer of Madhu demon,²⁶³ spoke to Śiva who assumes various forms during His divine sports and who is favourably disposed to his devotees. (54.)

[fn 263. The demons Madhu and Kaitabha sprang from the ear of Viṣṇu while he was asleep at the end of a Kalpa. As soon as born they tried to kill Brahmā who was lying on the lotus sprung from Viṣṇu's navel. Viṣṇu killed them and obtained the names Kaitabhajit and Madhu-sūdana.]

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणाकर शङ्कर ।
यदुक्तं ब्रह्मणा सर्वं मदुक्तं तन्न संशयः ॥ ५५॥

English
Viṣṇu said :— O great lord, the lord of devas, O Śiva the merciful, there is no doubt in this that what Brahmā has said constitutes what I have to say. (55.)

Sanskrit
तत्कुरुष्व महेशान कृपां कृत्वा ममोपरि ।
सनाथं कुरु सद्दृष्ट्या त्रिलोकं सुविवाह्य ताम् ॥ ५६॥

English
Hence, O great lord be merciful to me and carry out this request. Marrying her please make the three worlds blessed with a leader with benignant look. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा भगवान् विष्णुः तूष्णीमास मुने सुधीः ।
तथास्त्विति विहस्याह स प्रभुर्भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ५७॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O sage, after saying this, the intelligent lord Viṣṇu kept silent. The great lord Rudra, favourably disposed to His devotees, smilingly said, "So be it." (57.)

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वावां च सम्प्राप्य चाज्ञां स मुनिभिः सुरैः ।
अगच्छाव स्वेष्टदेशं सस्त्रीकौ परहर्षितौ ॥ ५८॥

English
Then both of us took leave of him and in a cheerful mood returned to our respective abodes along with our wives, the sages and the devas. (58.)
