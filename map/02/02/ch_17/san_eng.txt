### **CHAPTER SEVENTEEN - (Sati granted the boon)**

---

Sanskrit
१७

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा सर्वदेवैश्च कृता शम्भोर्नुतिः परा ।
शिवाच्च सा वरं प्राप्ता श‍ृणु ह्यादरतो मुने ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O sage, thus I have told you about the prayer to Śiva offered by all the devas. How Sati obtained a boon from Śiva, you shall now listen with respect. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो सती पुनः शुक्लपक्षेऽष्टम्यामुपोषिता ।
आश्विने मासि सर्वेशं पूजयामास भक्तितः ॥ २॥

English
Then, in the month of Āśvina (September-October), Sati observed a fast on the eighth day of the bright half and worshipped Śiva with great devotion. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
इति नन्दाव्रते पूर्णे नवम्यां दिनभागतः ।
तस्यास्तु ध्यानमग्नायाः प्रत्यक्षमभवद् हरः ॥ ३॥

English
When her Nandā rites were concluded on the ninth day (Navami), while she was engrossed in meditation, Śiva became visible to her. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरो गौरः पञ्चवक्त्रस्त्रिलोचनः ।
चन्द्रभालः प्रसन्नात्मा शितिकण्ठश्चतुर्भुजः ॥ ४॥

त्रिशूलब्रह्मकवराभयधृग्भस्मभास्वरः ।
स्वर्धुन्या विलसच्छीर्षः सकलाङ्गमनोहरः ॥ ५॥

महालावण्यधामा च कोटिचन्द्रसमाननः ।
कोटिस्मरसमा कान्तिः सर्वथा स्त्रीप्रियाकृतिः ॥ ६॥

English
He was fair-complexioned, handsome in appearance, had five faces$^{264}$ and three eyes$^{265}$. The crescent moon adorned His forehead. He was in a joyous mood. He had four arms and His neck was blue$^{266}$ in colour. He was holding trident and an amulet (Brahmakavaca) for protection. He was brilliant with dust. His head was comely with the celestial river Gaṅgā. He was gay in all parts of his body. He was the abode of great beauty. His face shone with the brilliance of ten million moons. His lustre matched that of ten million cupids. In every respect His features were such as appeal to all women. (4-6.)

[fn 264. On the five-faced God Śiva See Note No. 25 P. 34.]

[fn 265. On the three-eyed Śiva See Note No. 147 P. 141.]

[fn 266. Śiva is called the blue-necked (Nilakaṇṭha or Śitikaṇṭha) from swallowing the poison produced at the churning of the ocean.]
---

Sanskrit
प्रत्यक्षतो हरं वीक्ष्य सती सेदृग्विधं प्रभुम् ।
ववन्दे चरणौ तस्य सुलज्जावनतानना ॥ ७॥

English
On seeing Śiva directly in such a form she bent her head from shyness and she knelt at his feet. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्राह महादेवः सतीं तद्व्रतधारिणीम् ।
तामिच्छन्नपि भार्यार्थं तपश्चर्याफलप्रदः ॥ ८॥

English
Although He desired her to be his wife He wished to bestow on her the fruit of her penance. Thus He spoke to her in the state of her penance. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेव उवाच ।
दक्षनन्दिनि प्रीतोऽस्मि व्रतेनानेन सुव्रते ।
वरं वरय सन्दास्ये यत्तवाभिमतं भवेत् ॥ ९॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O daughter of Dakṣa, of good rites, I am delighted by these rites you have observed. Choose a boon. I shall grant it whatever it may be. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
जानन्नपीह तद्भावं महादेवो जगत्पतिः ।
जगौ वरं वृणीष्वेति तद्वाक्यश्रवणेच्छया ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Although Śiva, the lord of the universe, knew her desire, he said—“Choose a boon.” It was because He desired to hear her speak. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सापि त्रपावशा युक्ता वक्तुं नो हृदि यत्स्थितम् ।
शशाक सा त्वभीष्टं यत्तल्लज्जाच्छादितं पुनः ॥ ११॥

English
She too, who was highly bashful, could not speak out her mind as it was covered up by bashfulness. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रेममग्नाभवत्साति श्रुत्वा शिववचः प्रियम् ।
तज्ज्ञात्वा सुप्रसन्नोऽभूच्छङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १२॥

English
On realising that she was fully immersed in love on hearing Śiva’s pleasing words, Śaṅkāra who was favourably disposed to His devotees was highly pleased. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं ब्रूहि वरं ब्रूहि प्राहेति स पुनर्द्रुतम् ।
सतीभक्तिवशः शम्भुरन्तर्यामी सतां गतिः ॥ १३॥

English
He repeatedly urged her “Speak out the boon you wish to choose, speak out the boon.” Śiva, the immanent lord, the goal of the good was drawn to Satī by her devotion. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ त्रपां स्वां सन्धाय यदा प्राह हरं सती ।
यथेष्टं देहि वरद वरमित्यनिवारकम् ॥ १४॥

तदा वाक्यस्यावसानमनवेक्ष्य वृषध्वजः ।
भव त्वं मम भार्येति प्राह तां भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १५॥

English
Somehow suppressing her bashfulness when Satī spoke “As you please give unto me, O bestower of boons, the desired boon or the bridegroom of my desire, without any hindrance,” the full-emblemed deity Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees, did not wait for the completion of her request and said—“you be my wife.” (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य साभीष्टफलभावनम् ।
तूष्णीं तस्थौ प्रमुदिता वरं प्राप्य मनोगतम् ॥ १६॥

English
On hearing his words embracing the fruits of her cherished desire she kept silent. She was highly delighted on obtaining the boon (or bridegroom) stationed in her mind. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सकामस्य हरस्याग्रे स्थिता सा चारुहासिनी ।
अकरोन्निजभावांश्च हावान्कामविवर्धनान् ॥ १७॥

English
She stood smiling sweetly before Śiva who was full of love. She opened her inmost feelings through various subtle gestures that increased notions of love. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो भावान्समादाय श‍ृङ्गाराख्यो रसस्तदा ।
तयोश्चित्ते विवेशाशु कला हावा यथोदितम् ॥ १८॥

English
Taking up these notions, gestures and feelings, the flavour of love called Śṛṅgāra entered their hearts. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O celestial sage, by the advent of the flavour of love, a peculiar glow, in the usual manner of worldly sports, was manifest in them as in the star Citrā and the moon.²⁶⁷ (19.)

Sanskrit

English
In the presence of Śiva whose body shone with the brilliance of crystal, Satī who had the glossy brilliance of split collyrium, shone like a line of cloud near the moon. (20.)

Sanskrit

English
The delighted daughter of Dakṣa with her palms joined frequently in reverence, joyfully spoke to Śiva who is favourably disposed to His devotees. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English

English
