### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - *(Marriage of Śiva and Satī)***

---

Sanskrit
१८

नारद उवाच ।
रुद्रपार्श्वे त्वयि गते किमभूच्चरितं ततः ।
का वार्ता ह्यभवत्तात किं चकार हरः स्वयम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  When you approached Śiva, what was it that transpired? What were the events? What did Śiva Himself do? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथाहं शिवमानेतुं प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरम् ।
आसदं हि महादेवं हिमवद्गिरिसंस्थितम् ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* I approached lord Śiva who was staying in the Himā-layan mountains in order to bring Him (to the house of Dakṣa). I was in a joyous mood. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
मां वीक्ष्य लोकस्रष्टारमायान्तं वृषभध्वजः ।
मनसा संशयं चक्रे सतीप्राप्तौ मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing me, the creator of the world, approaching, the bull-emblemed Śiva had doubts about the acquisition of Satī. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रीत्या हरो लोके गतिमाश्रित्य लीलया ।
सत्या भक्त्या च मां क्षिप्रमुवाच प्राकृतो यथा ॥ ४॥

English
Due to His real affection or as a part of His divine sports in conformity with the conventions of the world or due to the devotion of Satī, Śiva immediately spoke to me like an ordinary man. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
किमकार्षीत्सुरज्येष्ठ सत्यर्थे त्वत्सुतः स माम् ।
कथयस्व यथा स्वान्तं न दीर्येन्मन्मथेन हि ॥ ५॥

English
*Śiva said :—* O eldest of devas, what did your son (Daṣka) do in the matter of Satī. Tell me lest my heart should be severed by the cupid. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
धावमानो विप्रयोगो मामेव च सतीं प्रति ।
अभिहन्ति सुरज्येष्ठ त्यक्त्वान्यां प्राणधारिणीम् ॥ ६॥

English
This anxiety of separation, O eldest of devas, running between Satī and me attacks only me, leaving the other, the woman, who very well sustains her life. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सतीति सततं ब्रह्मन् वद कार्यं करोम्यहम् ।
अभेदान्मम सा प्राप्या तद्विधे क्रियतां तथा ॥ ७॥

English
O Brahmā, respect the name Satī. Let me do what shall be done. She is not different from me. She has to be attained by me. O Brahmā, act accordingly. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति रुद्रोक्तवचनं लोकाचारसुगर्भितम् ।
श्रुत्वाहं नारदमुने सान्त्वयन्नगदं शिवम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage Nārada, on hearing the words of Śiva best speaking of His strict adherence to the conventions of the world I told Śiva, consoling Him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
सत्यर्थं यन्मम सुतो वदति स्म वृषध्वज ।
तच्छ्रणुष्व निजासाध्यं सिद्धमित्यवधारय ॥ ९॥

English
O bull-emblemed God, hear what my son told me regarding Satī. Rest assured that what you wanted to achieve has been achieved. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देया तस्मै मया पुत्री तदर्थं परिकल्पिता ।
ममाभीष्टमिदं कार्यं त्वद्वाक्यादधिकं पुनः ॥ १०॥

English
Dakṣa has said “My daughter shall go to Him. She has been intended for Him. This has been my desire.” Now that you also say, it is all the more necessary that it shall be carried out. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्पुत्र्याराधितः शम्भुरेतदर्थं स्वयं पुनः ।
सोऽप्यन्विष्यति मां यस्मात्तदा देया मया हरे ॥ ११॥

English
For this purpose Śiva had been propitiated by my daughter. Now He too seeks her. Hence She has to be offered to Him by me. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शुभे लग्ने सुमुहूर्ते समागच्छतु सेऽन्तिकम् ।
तदा दास्यामि तनयां भिक्षार्थं शम्भवे विधे ॥ १२॥

English
Let Him come to me in an auspicious conjunction of stars. Then, O Brahmā, I shall offer my daughter to Him in the form of Alms. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युवाच स मां दक्षस्तस्मात्त्वं वृषभध्वज ।
शुभे मुहूर्ते तद्वेश्म गच्छ तामानयस्व च ॥ १३॥

English
O bull-emblemed God, Dakṣa has told me so. Go to his house in an auspicious hour and bring her here. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति श्रुत्वा मम वचो लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ।
उवाच विहसन् रुद्रो मुने मां भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, on hearing these words of mine, Rudra, who is favourably disposed to His devotees, spoke with a smile, strictly adhering to the conventions of the world. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
गमिष्ये भवता सार्धं नारदेन च तद्गृहम् ।
अहमेव जगत्स्रष्टस्तस्मात्त्वं नारदं स्मर ॥ १५॥

English
Śiva said :—  I shall go to his house accompanied by you and Nārada. Hence, O creator of the universe, you remember Nārada. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मरीच्यादीन् स्वपुत्रांश्च मानसानपि संस्मर ।
तैः सार्धं दक्षनिलयं गमिष्ये सगणो विधे ॥ १६॥

English
Remember your mental as well as physical sons—Marīci and others. O Brahmā, with all my attendants and with them I shall go to Dakṣa's house. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याज्ञप्तोऽहमीशेन लोकाचारपरेण ह ।
संस्मरं नारदं त्वां च मरीच्यादीन्सुतांस्तथा ॥ १७॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Thus commanded by Śiva following the conventions of the world, I remembered you, Nārada and the other sons—Marīci etc. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः समागताः सर्वे मानसास्तनयास्त्वया ।
मम स्मरणमात्रेण हृष्टास्ते द्रुतमादरात् ॥ १८॥

English
Immediately after I remembered them, all of my mental sons and you arrived in a happy mood. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुः समागतस्तूर्णं स्मृतो रुद्रेण शैवराट् ।
स स्वसैन्यैः कमलया गरुडारूढ एव च ॥ १९॥

English
Remembered by Śiva, Viṣṇu, the foremost of Śiva's devotees, came there along with the Goddess Lakṣmī seated on Garuḍa<sup>269</sup> and accompanied by his army. (19.)

[fn 269. Garuḍa, the chief of birds, is descended from Kaśyapa and Vinatā—one of the daughters of Dakṣa. He is the Vehicle of lord Viṣṇu. He is represented as having the head, wings, talons and beak of an eagle and the body and limbs of a man. His face is white, his wings red and his body golden. For details, see Legends in the Mahābhārata PP. 1-153.]
---

Sanskrit
अथ चैत्रसिते पक्षे नक्षत्रे भगदैवते ।
त्रयोदश्यां दिने भानौ निर्गच्छत्स महेश्वरः ॥ २०॥

English
In the bright half of the month of Caitra (March-April) on the thirteenth day when the star was Uttarā Phālgunī on a Sunday, lord Śiva started. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वैः सुरगणैः सार्धं ब्रह्मविष्णुपुरःसरैः ।
तथा तैर्मुनिभिर्गच्छन् स बभौ पथि शङ्करः ॥ २१॥

English
Going ahead, with all the devas, led by Brahmā and Viṣṇu and accompanied by the sages, Śiva shone brilliantly. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्गे समुत्सवो जातो देवादीनां च गच्छताम् ।
तथा हरगणानां च सानन्दमनसामति ॥ २२॥

English
Great festivities were arranged by Devas and the attendants of Śiva who were in the happiest mood, on their way. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
गजगोव्याघ्रसर्पाश्च जटा चन्द्रकला तथा ।
जग्मुः सर्वे भूषणत्वं यथायोग्यं शिवेच्छया ॥ २३॥

English
The hides of elephant and tiger, the serpents, the crescent moon and the matted hair, all became fitting ornaments and embellishments at Śiva's will. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्षणेन बलिना बलीवर्देन वेगिना ।
सविष्णुप्रमुखः प्रीत्या प्राप दक्षालयं हरः ॥ २४॥

English
Then in a trice, Śiva reached Dakṣa's abode seated on his speedy bull and along with Viṣṇu and others. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दक्षो विनीतात्मा सम्प्रहृष्टतनूरुहः ।
प्रययौ सम्मुखं तस्य संयुक्तः सकलैर्निजैः ॥ २५॥

English
With great humility and boundless joy, Dakṣa along with his people welcomed Him. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे सुरगणास्तत्र स्वयं दक्षेण सत्कृताः ।
पार्श्वे श्रेष्ठं च मुनिभिरुपविष्टा यथाक्रमम् ॥ २६॥

English
The Devas and their attendants were honoured by Dakṣa. The sages were seated in their due order. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
परिवार्याखिलान्देवान्गणांश्च मुनिभिर्यथा ।
दक्षः समानयामास गृहाभ्यन्तरतः शिवम् ॥ २७॥

English
Then Dakṣa took Śiva within the house along with the devas and the sages. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दक्षः प्रसन्नात्मा स्वयं सर्वेश्वरं हरम् ।
समानर्च विधानेन दत्त्वासनमनुत्तमम् ॥ २८॥

English
The delighted Dakṣa worshipped lord Śiva, after offering him an excellent seat. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विष्णुं च मां विप्रान्सुरान्सर्वान्गणांस्तथा ।
पूजयामास सद्भक्त्या यथोचितविधानतः ॥ २९॥

English
He worshipped Viṣṇu, me, the brahmins, devas and the Gaṇas of Śiva, with great devotion and in a fitting manner. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा यथोचितां पूजां तेषां पूज्यादिभिस्तथा ।
चकार संविदं दक्षो मुनिभिर्मानसैः पुनः ॥ ३०॥

English
After performing the suitable worship, Dakṣa in the presence of respectable sages announced the marriage agreement. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो मां पितरं प्राह दक्षः प्रीत्या हि मत्सुतः ।
प्रणिपत्य त्वया कर्म कार्यं वैवाहिकं विभो ॥ ३१॥

English
Then Dakṣa, my son, knelt before me, his father, with pleasure and said—"O lord, the marriage rites shall be performed by you." (31.)

---

Sanskrit
वाढमित्यहमप्युक्त्वा प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ।
समुत्थाय ततोऽकार्षं तत्कार्यमखिलं तथा ॥ ३२॥

English
Saying 'Amen' I got up with a delightful heart and performed the preliminary rites. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शुभे मुहूर्ते हि लग्ने ग्रहबलान्विते ।
सतीं निजसुतां दक्षो ददौ हर्षेण शम्भवे ॥ ३३॥

English
Then in an auspicious conjunction of stars with the planets in a propitious position, Dakṣa joyfully gave his daughter Satī to Śiva. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्वाहविधिना सोऽपि पाणिं जग्राह हर्षितः ।
दाक्षायण्या वरतनोस्तदानीं वृषभध्वजः ॥ ३४॥

English
As a part of the rites of marriage the delighted Śiva grasped the hand of Satī of comely appearance. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं हरिस्त्वदाद्या वै मुनयश्च सुरा गणाः ।
नेमुः सर्वे संस्तुतिभिः तोषयामासुरीश्वरम् ॥ ३५॥

English
We all, Viṣṇu, I, you and other sages, bowed to Śiva and delighted Him with laudatory hymns. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
समुत्सवो महानासीन्नृत्यगानपुरःसरः ।
आनन्दं परमं जग्मुः सर्वे मुनिगणाः सुराः ॥ ३६॥

English
There were great festivities with songs and dances. The sages and the devas were in a gay mood. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
कन्यां दत्त्वा कृतार्थोऽभूत्तदा दक्षो हि मत्सुतः ।
शिवाशिवौ प्रसन्नौ च निखिलं मङ्गलालयम् ॥ ३७॥

English
After offering his daughter, Dakṣa, my son, was extremely satisfied, Satī and Śiva were in happy mood. Everything concluded auspiciously. (37.)
