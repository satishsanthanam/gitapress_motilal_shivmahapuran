### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - *(Description of Śiva's sports)***

---

Sanskrit
१९

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
कृत्वा दक्षः सुतादानं यौतकं विविधं ददौ ।
हराय सुप्रसन्नश्च द्विजेभ्यो विविधं धनम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After giving his daughter in marriage, Dakṣa gave her different articles in the form of dowry. Many gifts were given to Śiva. Dakṣa gave monetary gifts to the brahmins with great delight. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुमुपागत्य समुत्थाय कृताञ्जलिः ।
सार्धं कमलया चेदमुवाच गरुडध्वजः ॥ २॥

English
Then Viṣṇu stood up. Approaching Śiva with palms joined in reverence and accompanied by Lakṣmī, the Garuda-vehicled God Viṣṇu spoke thus. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
त्वं पिता जगतां तात सती माताखिलस्य च ॥ ३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—*  O great lord, O ocean of mercy, lord of devas, O dear one, you are the father and Satī is the mother of the world. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
युवां लीलावतारौ वै सतां क्षेमाय सर्वदा ।
खलानां निग्रहार्थाय श्रुतिरेषा सनातनी ॥ ४॥

English
You have taken incarnation out of sheer sport for the welfare of the good and suppression of the wicked—so says the eternal scripture. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्निग्धनीलाञ्जनश्यामशोभया शोभसे हर ।
दाक्षायण्या यथा चाहं प्रतिलोमेन पद्मया ॥ ५॥

English
You are fair-complexioned and Satī has the blue lustre of the glossy collyrium. I on the other hand am blue in hue and Lakṣmī is fair-complexioned. You two shine in juxtaposition with us two. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां वा नृणां रक्षां कुरु सत्यानया सताम् ।
संसारसारिणां शम्भो मङ्गलं सर्वदा तथा ॥ ६॥

English
O Śiva, along with this Satī, protect the good people and the devas. Similarly always bestow auspicious goodness upon the people of this world. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
य एनां साभिलाषो वै दृष्ट्वा श्रुत्वाथवा भवेत् ।
तं हन्याः सर्वभूतेश विज्ञप्तिरिति मे प्रभो ॥ ७॥

English
O lord of living beings, this is my humble submission. you shall kill the man, whoever it may be, who sees or hears her with lust in his mind. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचो विष्णोर्विहस्य परमेश्वरः ।
एवमस्त्विति सर्वज्ञः प्रोवाच मधुसूदनम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* On hearing these words of Viṣṇu, lord Śiva laughed. The omniscient lord told the slayer of Madhu, "Be it so." (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्थानं हरिरागत्य स्थित आसीन्मुनीश्वर ।
उत्सवं कारयामास जुगोप चरितं च तत् ॥ ९॥

English
O great sage, after this, Viṣṇu returned to his abode. He kept the incident quite secret but asked the people to continue the festivities. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं देवीं समागत्य गृह्योक्तविधिनाखिलम् ।
अग्निकार्यं यथोद्दिष्टमकार्षं च सुविस्तरम् ॥ १०॥

English
I approached the Goddess (Satī) and performed in detail all the sacrificial rites as laid down in the Gṛhya-sūtras. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवा शिवश्चैव यथाविधि प्रहृष्टवत् ।
अग्नेः प्रदक्षिणं चक्रे मदाचार्यद्विजाज्ञया ॥ ११॥

English
Then at my bidding in the capacity of the main priest, Śivā and Śiva duly and with great delight performed the circumambulation of the sacred fire.$^{270}$ (11.)

[fn 270. The circumambulation of the fire by the bride and the bridegroom is one of the rites in the Vedic nuptial ceremony. The bride and the bridegroom go round the fire while the husband recites the following formula: "To thee they have in the beginning carried round Sūryā with the bridal procession. Mayest thou give back, Agni, to the husband the wife together with offsprings." The fire plays an important role in the performance of Vedic Saṃskāras. See H.S. P. 219.]
---

Sanskrit
तदा महोत्सवस्तत्राद्भुतोऽभूद् द्विजसत्तम ।
सर्वेषां सुखदं वाद्यं गीतनृत्यपुरःसरम् ॥ १२॥

English
O excellent brahmin, then wonderfully great festivities were conducted with beatings of drums and playings on musical instruments accompanied by songs and dances pleasing everyone. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदानीमद्भुतं तत्र चरितं समभूदति ।
सुविस्मयकरं तात तच्छृणु त्वं वदामि ते ॥ १३॥

English
Then a surprisingly strange event occurred there. Dear one, listen to it. I shall tell you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्ज्ञेया शाम्भवी माया तया सम्मोहितं जगत् ।
सचराचरमत्यन्तं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥ १४॥

English
Śiva's power of illusion is inscrutable. The whole universe, the mobile or immobile, is deluded by it, Devas and Asuras. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
योऽहं शम्भुं मोहयितुं पुरैच्छं कपटेन ह ।
मां च तं शङ्करस्तात मोहयामास लीलया ॥ १५॥

English
Formerly I wished to delude Śiva by deceitful means. But now Śiva Himself has deluded me by means of His divine sports. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इच्छेत्परापकारं यः स तस्यैव भवेद् ध्रुवम् ।
इति मत्वापकारं नो कुर्यादन्यस्य पूरुषः ॥ १६॥

English
If a man wishes evil of others, he himself becomes the victim of the same. There is no doubt about it. Realising this, no man shall wish evil of anyone else. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रदक्षिणां प्रकुर्वन्त्या वह्नेः सत्याः पदद्वयम् ।
आविर्बभूव वसनात्तदद्राक्षमहं मुने ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, while going round the fire, the feet of Satī protruded out of the cloth that covered them. I looked at them. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मदनाविष्टचेताश्च भूत्वाङ्गानि व्यलोकयम् ।
अहं सत्या द्विजश्रेष्ठ शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ १८॥

English
My mind being afflicted by love I stared at the limbs of Satī. O excellent brahmin, I was deluded by Śiva's Māyā. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा यथाहं रम्याणि व्यैक्षमङ्गानि कौतुकात् ।
सत्या बभूव संहृष्टः कामार्तो हि तथा तथा ॥ १९॥

English
The more I stared at the beautiful limbs of Satī eagerly the more I became thrilled like a love-afflicted man. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अहमेवं तथा दृष्ट्वा दक्षजां च पतिव्रताम् ।
स्मराविष्टमना वक्त्रं द्रष्टुकामोऽभवं मुने ॥ २०॥

English
Staring thus at the chaste daughter of Dakṣa and being afflicted by the cupid, O sage, I craved to see her face. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
न शम्भोर्लज्जया वक्त्रं प्रत्यक्षं च विलोकितम् ।
न च सा लज्जयाविष्टा करोति प्रकटं मुखम् ॥ २१॥

English
Since she was bashful in the presence of Śiva I could not see her face. She did not show out her face on account of shyness. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तद्दर्शनार्थाय सदुपायं विचारयन् ।
धूम्रघोरेण कामार्तोऽकार्षं तच्च ततः परम् ॥ २२॥

English
Then I began to consider proper means whereby I could see the face. Afflicted much by the cupid, I pitched upon the production of airful smoke as the means thereof. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्द्रेन्धनानि भूरीणि क्षिप्त्वा तत्र विभावसौ ।
स्वल्पाज्याहुतिविन्यासादार्द्रद्रव्योद्भवस्तथा ॥ २३॥

प्रादुर्भूतस्ततो धूमो भूयांस्तत्र समन्ततः ।
तादृग् येन तमोभूतं वेदीभूमिविनिर्मितम् ॥ २४॥

English
I put many wet twigs into the fire. Only very little ghee did I pour into the fire. Much smoke arose out of the fire from the wet twigs, so much so that darkness enveloped the whole altar ground (and the neighbourhood). (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो धूमाकुले नेत्रे महेशः परमेश्वरः ।
हस्ताभ्यां छादयामास बहुलीलाकरः प्रभुः ॥ २५॥

English
Then lord Śiva, the supreme God, indulged in many sports, covered his eyes (apparently) afflicted by smoke. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो वस्त्रं समुत्क्षिप्य सतीवक्त्रमहं मुने ।
अवेक्षं किल कामार्तः प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥ २६॥

English
Then, O sage, afflicted by the cupid and delighted in the heart of hearts, I lifted her veil and stared into the face of Satī. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहुर्मुहुरहं तात पश्यामि स्म सतीमुखम् ।
अथेन्द्रियविकारं च प्राप्तवानस्मि सोऽवशः ॥ २७॥

English
I looked at the face of Satī many a time. I was helpless in curbing the onset of a sensuous organism. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मम रेतः प्रचस्कन्द ततस्तद्वीक्षणाद् द्रुतम् ।
चतुर्बिन्दुमितं भूमौ तुषारचयसन्निभम् ॥ २८॥

English
Four drops of my semen virile got displaced and fell on the ground like drops of dew as a result of staring into her face. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽहं शङ्कितो मौनी तत्क्षणं विस्मितो मुने ।
आच्छादये स्म तद्रेतो यथा कश्चिद् बुबोध न ॥ २९॥

English
O sage, then I was stunned into silence. I was surprised. I became suspicious. I covered up the semen drops lest anyone should see them. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तद्भगवान् शम्भुर्ज्ञात्वा दिव्येन चक्षुषा ।
रेतोऽवस्कन्दनात्तस्य कोपादेतदुवाच ह ॥ ३०॥

English
But the lord Śiva saw it by His divine vision. The trickling down of the semen excited His fury and He said— (30.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
किमेतद्विहितं पाप त्वया कर्म विगर्हितम् ।
विवाहे मम कान्ताया वक्त्रं दृष्टं न रागतः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  “O sinful wretch, what a despicable mess you have perpetrated? At the time of her marriage you have passionately gazed at the face of my beloved. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं वेत्सि शङ्करेणैतत्कर्म ज्ञातं न किञ्चन ।
त्रैलोक्येऽपि न मेऽज्ञातं गूढं तस्मात्कथं विधे ॥ ३२॥

English
You think that this blunder has not been known by me at all. There is nothing that is unknown to me in the three worlds. O Brahman, how can it then remain hidden? (32.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्किञ्चित् त्रिषु लोकेषु जङ्गमं स्थावरं तथा ।
तस्याहं मध्यगो मूढ तैलं यद्वत्तिलान्तिगम् ॥ ३३॥

English
O foolish fellow, just as the oil is latent in the gingelly seed so also I am present within everything in the three worlds whether mobile or immobile.” (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा प्रियविष्णुर्मां तदा विष्णुवचः स्मरन् ।
इयेष हन्तुं ब्रह्माणं शूलमुद्यम्य शङ्करः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Saying thus, and remembering the words of Viṣṇu, Śiva who dearly loved Viṣṇu lifted His trident and wished to kill me. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

मरीचिप्रमुखास्ते वै हाहाकारं च चक्रिरे ॥ ३५॥

English
O excellent brahmin, when the trident was lifted up by Him to kill me, Marīci$^{271}$ and others raised a hue and cry. (35.)

[fn 271. For the mind-born and physical sons of the creator, See Note No. 242 P. 305.]
---

Sanskrit

तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं तत्र प्रज्वलन्तं भयातुराः ॥ ३६॥

English
Then all the devas and the sages, extremely terrified, began to eulogise Him who was blazing there. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Devas said :—*  O lord, O great lord, favourably disposed to those who seek refuge, O Śiva, save me. O lord Śiva, be pleased. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Mysterious is your form, O lord, and mysterious are your divine sports. Your Māyā is enigmatic and complex. Everything and everyone except your devotee is deluded by it, O Lord. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus in many ways, the timid and frightened devas and the sages eulogised the lord of devas who was furious. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Seeing Dakṣa in front of Him in a state of excited suspicion, and remembering the request of Viṣṇu, lord Śiva spoke these displeasing words:— (42.)

---

Sanskrit

विष्णुना मेऽतिभक्तेन यदिदानीमुदीरितम् ।

English
*Lord Śiva said :—*  O patriarch Dakṣa what has just been requested by Viṣṇu my great devotee and agreed to by me shall be done here. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
“O lord, whoever stares at Satī lustfully shall be killed by you.” I shall make these words of Viṣṇu true by killing Brahmā. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Why did Brahmā stare at Satī lustfully? Moreover he has committed a sin by discharging his semen. Hence I shall kill him. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

English
*Brahmā said : —*  When the lord of Devas spoke thus furiously, all the people including Devas, sages and human beings trembled. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

अभूवं विकलोऽतीव तदाहं तद्विमोहितः ॥ ४७॥

English
There was a piteous cry of distress. Everywhere tense suspense prevailed. Then I who wanted to delude Him was myself deluded. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुर्महेशातिप्रियः कार्यविचक्षणः ।

English
Then the intelligent Viṣṇu, the great favourite of Śiva and very clever in managing all affairs bowed down and lauded Rudra who spoke as before. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

इदमूचे वारयंस्तं क्षिप्रं भूत्वा पुरः सरः ॥ ४९॥

English
Standing in front of Him and singing various songs of praise to Śiva who is favourably disposed towards His devotees He prevented Him and said thus : (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O lord Śiva, do not kill Brahmā, the creator and lord of the worlds. He has sought refuge in you and you are reputed to be favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in you. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

विज्ञप्तिं हृदि मे मत्वा कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ ५१॥

English
O lord, I am a great favourite of yours and am called the chief of Devotees. Keeping my submission in mind be merciful towards me. (51.)

---
