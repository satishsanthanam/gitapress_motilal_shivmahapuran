### **CHAPTER TWENTY - *(Sati's marriage festival)***

---

Sanskrit
२०

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे महाभाग शिवभक्त वरप्रभो ।
श्रावितं चरितं शम्भोरद्भुतं मङ्गलायनम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O lord Brahmā, the fortunate one, foremost of Śiva's devotees, you have narrated the wonderfully auspicious story of Śiva. O dear one, what happened after that? Please continue to narrate the story of the moon-crested Śiva and Satī, the wonderful story that quells all sins. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः किमभवत्तात कथ्यतां शशिमौलिनः ।
सत्याश्च चरितं दिव्यं सर्वाघौघविनाशनम् । २॥

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
निवृत्ते शङ्करे चास्मद्वधाद्भक्तानुकम्पिनि ।
अभवन्निर्भयाः सर्वे सुखिनः सुप्रसन्नकाः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When Śiva who is sympathetic towards His devotees, desisted from killing me, all became fearless, happy and pleased. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नतस्कन्धाः साञ्जलयः प्रणेमुर्निखिलाश्च ते ।
तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं भक्त्या चक्रुर्जयरवं मुदा ॥ ४॥

English
All of them bowed with stooping shoulders, and palms joined in reverence. They lauded Śiva with devotion. They shouted cries of victory with pleasure. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नेव कालेऽहं प्रसन्नो निर्भयो मुने ।
अस्तवं शङ्करं भक्त्या विविधैश्च शुभस्तवैः ॥ ५॥

English
At the same time, delighted and fearless, O sage, I eulogised Śiva with devotion by means of auspicious prayers. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तुष्टमनाः शम्भुर्बहुलीलाकरः प्रभुः ।
मुने मां समुवाचेदं सर्वेषां श‍ृण्वतां तदा ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, the lord Śiva who was delighted in His mind and who is an adept in many a divine sport spoke to me within the hearing of all. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् तात प्रसन्नोऽहं निर्भयस्त्वं भवाधुना ।
स्वशीर्षं स्पृश हस्तेन मदाज्ञां कुर्वसंशयम् ॥ ७॥

English
*Rudra said :—*  "Dear Brahmā, I am glad. You can be free from fear. You touch your head with your hand. Unhesitatingly carry out my behest." (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भोर्बहुलीलाकृतः प्रभोः ।
स्पृशन् स्वं कं तथा भूत्वा प्राणमं वृषभध्वजम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Lord Śiva adept in divine sports I touched my head and in the same manner bowed to Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
यावदेवमहं स्वं कं स्पृशामि निजपाणिना ।
तावत्तत्र स्थितं सद्यस्तद्रूपवृषवाहनम् ॥ ९॥

English
When I thus touched my head I assumed the shape of his vehicle, the bull. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो लज्जापरीताङ्गः स्थितश्चाहमधोमुखः ।
इन्द्राद्यैरमरैः सर्वैः सुदृष्टः सर्वतः स्थितैः ॥ १०॥

English
Then I was too much ashamed. I stood with my head bent down. Indra and other devas standing around saw me in that plight. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथाहं लज्जयाविष्टः प्रणिपत्य महेश्वरम् ।
प्रावोचं संस्तुतिं कृत्वा क्षम्यतां क्षम्यतामिति ॥ ११॥

English
Ashamed that I was, I repeatedly bowed to Him and after offering prayers spoke to Him again : “I may be excused. I may be excused.” (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य पापस्य शुद्ध्यर्थं प्रायश्चित्तं वद प्रभो ।
निग्रहं च तथा न्यायं येन पापं प्रयातु मे ॥ १२॥

English
“O lord, tell me the mode of atonement for my sin. Even killing is justifiable. May my sin be removed thereby.” (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तस्तु मया शम्भुरुवाच प्रणतं हि माम् ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा सर्वेशो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १३॥

English
Thus addressed by me, Śiva, the lord of all, who is favourable, delightedly told me as I stood bowing to Him. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरुवाच ।
अनेनैव स्वरूपेण मदधिष्ठितकेन हि ।
तपः कुरु प्रसन्नात्मा मदाराधनतत्परः ॥ १४॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  In this very form (of a bull) whereon I sit, you shall perform penance with pleasure in your heart and desire for propitiating me. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ख्यातिं यास्यसि सर्वत्र नाम्ना रुद्रशिरः क्षितौ ।
साधकः सर्वकृत्यानां तेजोभाजां द्विजन्मनाम् ॥ १५॥

English
You will acquire the glory of being called “The head of Rudra” in the world. You will be the accomplisher of rites for brahmins of great repute. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मनुष्याणामिदं कृत्यं यस्माद्वीर्यं त्वयाधुना ।
तस्मात्त्वं मानुषो भूत्वा विचरिष्यसि भूतले ॥ १६॥

English
Discharge of semen is the act of human beings and as you have done the same, you will be born as a man and be roaming over the earth. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्त्वां चानेन रूपेण दृष्ट्वा कौ विचरिष्यति ।
किमेतद् ब्रह्मणो मूर्ध्नि वदन्निति पुरान्तकः ॥ १७॥

ततस्ते चेष्टितं सर्वं कौतुकाच्छ्रोष्यतीति यः ।
परदारकृतात्त्यागान्मुक्तिं सद्यः स यास्यति ॥ १८॥

English
When you wander over the earth in this form, people will be asking, “What is there on the head of Brahmā?” and you shall reply “Śiva”. Any body who has committed the sin of outraging the modesty of another man’s wife will be free from that sin if he eagerly hears your story. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा यथा जनश्चैतत्कृत्यं ते कीर्तयिष्यति ।
तथा तथा विशुद्धिस्ते पापस्यास्य भविष्यति ॥ १९॥

English
Whenever people thus repeat your wicked action your sin will gradually subside and you will become pure. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदेव हि ते ब्रह्मन् प्रायश्चित्तं मयेरितम् ।
जनहास्यकरं लोके तव गर्हाकरं परम् ॥ २०॥

English
O Brahmā, this is the atonement I lay down for you, being laughed at by the people and ridiculed by them. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्च तव वीर्यं हि पतितं वेदिमध्यगम् ।
कामार्तस्य मया दृष्टं नैतद्धार्यं भविष्यति ॥ २१॥

English
The semen drops that fell in the middle of the altar-ground from you when you were excited by lust and seen by me will not be retained by any one. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्बिन्दुमितं रेतः पतितं यत्क्षितौ तव ।
तन्मितास्तोयदा व्योम्नि भवेयुः प्रलयङ्कराः ॥ २२॥

English
Four drops of your semen fell on the ground. Hence so many terrible clouds causing dissolution shall rise up in the sky. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र देवर्षीणां पुरो द्रुतम् ।
तद्रेतसः समभवंस्तन्मिताश्च बलाहकाः ॥ २३॥

English
In the meantime, (when Śiva said so) in front of the devas and the sages, so many clouds emanated from the semen drops. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
संवर्तकस्तथावर्तः पुष्करो द्रोण एव च ।
एते चतुर्विधास्तात महामेघा लयङ्कराः ॥ २४॥

English
O dear one, four types of great clouds that caused destruction are the Samvartaka, the Āvarta, the Puṣkara and the Droṇa.$^{273}$ (24.)

[fn 273. Samvartaka, Āvarta Puṣkara and Droṇa are the names of clouds that emerge at the advent of dissolution of the universe.]
---

Sanskrit
गर्जन्तश्चाथ मुञ्चन्तस्तोयानीषच्छिवेच्छया ।
फेलुर्व्योम्नि मुनिश्रेष्ठ तोयदास्ते कदारवाः ॥ २५॥

English
O excellent sage, those clouds rumbling and roaring with hideous sounds dropping showers at the slightest wish of Śiva burst asunder in the sky. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तैस्तु सञ्छादिते व्योम्नि सुगर्जद्भिश्च शङ्करः ।
प्रशान् दाक्षायणी देवी भृशं शान्तोऽभवद् द्रुतम् ॥ २६॥

English
When the sky was covered by those roaring clouds, Śiva and the Goddess Śivā were quite calm. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ चाहं वीतभयः शङ्करस्याज्ञया तदा ।
शेषं वैवाहिकं कर्म समाप्तिमनयं मुने ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, thereafter becoming fearless, I concluded the remaining rites of the marriage at the bidding of Śiva. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पपात पुष्पवृष्टिश्च शिवाशिवशिरस्कयोः ।
सर्वत्र च मुनिश्रेष्ठ मुदा देवगणोज्झिता ॥ २८॥

English
O excellent sage, a shower of flowers dropped by the devas with great pleasure fell on the heads of Śivā and Śiva and also on all their sides. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
वाद्यमानेषु वाद्येषु गायमानेषु तेषु च ।
पठत्सु विप्रवर्गेषु वेदान् भक्त्यान्वितेषु च ॥ २९॥

रम्भादिषु पुरन्ध्रीषु नृत्यमानासु सादरम् ।
महोत्सवो महानासीद्देवपत्नीषु नारद ॥ ३०॥

English
O Nārada, great festivities were conducted by the wives of the devas. Musical instruments were played, songs were sung, Vedic hymns were recited devoutly by groups of brahmins. The celestial damsels Rambhā and others danced zealously. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ कर्मवितानेशः प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ।
प्राह मां प्राञ्जलिं प्रीत्या लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ॥ ३१॥

English
Then the delightful lord, the lord of sacrificial rites, following the conventions of the world, spoke to me as I was standing with palms joined in reverence. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
हे ब्रह्मन् सुकृतं कर्म सर्वं वैवाहिकं च यत् ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि त्वमाचार्यो दद्यां ते दक्षिणां च काम् ॥ ३२॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O Brahmā, all the rites of marriage have been performed extremely well. I am pleased. You officiated as the priest. What shall I give you as the nuptial fee? (32.)

---

Sanskrit
याचस्व तां सुरज्येष्ठ यद्यपि स्यात्सुदुर्लभा ।
ब्रूहि शीघ्रं महाभाग नादेयं विद्यते मम ॥ ३३॥

English
O eldest of devas, you can demand it even if it be hard to get. Tell me quickly, O fortunate one. For there is nothing which cannot be granted by me. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः सोऽहं शङ्करस्य कृताञ्जलिः ।
मुनेऽवोचं विनीतात्मा प्रणम्येशं मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Brahmā said :* O sage, on hearing these words of Śiva I humbly bowed to Him repeatedly with palms joined in reverence and said :— (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश वरयोग्योऽस्म्यहं यदि ।
तत्कुरु त्वं महेशान सुप्रीत्या यद्वदाम्यहम् ॥ ३५॥

English
“O lord of Devas, if you are pleased, if I deserve your blessings, O lord, please do as I request you with pleasure. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेनैव तु रूपेण वेद्यामस्यां महेश्वर ।
त्वया स्थेयं सदैवात्र नृणां पापविशुद्धये ॥ ३६॥

English
O lord Śiva, for the purification of men from sins you will please stay for ever in this altar in this self-same form. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
येनास्य सन्निधौ कृत्वा स्वाश्रमं शशिशेखर ।
तपः कुर्यां विनाशाय स्वपापस्यास्य शङ्कर ॥ ३७॥

English
O moon-crested God, I shall make my hermitage in its vicinity and perform penance to destroy my sin. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
चैत्रशुक्लत्रयोदश्यां नक्षत्रे भगदैवते ।
सूर्यवारे च यो भक्त्या वीक्षेत भुवि मानवः ॥ ३८॥

तदैव तस्य पापानि प्रयान्तु हर सङ्क्षयम् ।
वर्धते विपुलं पुण्यं रोगा नश्यन्तु सर्वशः ॥ ३९॥

English
If anyone visits this holy site on the thirteenth day in the bright half of Caitra (March-April) when the star is Uttarāphālgunī and the day is Sunday, may all his sins be quelled O Śiva; may his merits increase and may his ailments disappear. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
या नारी दुर्भगा वन्ध्या काणा रूपविवर्जिता ।
सापि त्वद्दर्शनादेव निर्दोषा सम्भवेद् ध्रुवम् ॥ ४०॥

English
If a woman who is barren, one-eyed, ugly or unfortunate, visits this place she shall be freed from all these defects.” (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचो मे हि स्वात्मसर्वसुखावहम् ।
तथास्त्विति शिवः प्राह सुप्रसन्नेन चेतसा ॥ ४१॥

English
On hearing these words of mine, Śiva was pleased and He said “Let it be so”. This made me very happy. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
हिताय सर्वलोकस्य वेद्यां तस्यां व्यवस्थितः ।
स्थास्यामि सहितः पत्न्या सत्या त्वद्वचनाद्विधे ॥ ४२॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O, for the benefits of the people, I shall stay in this altar, with my wife Satī, in accordance with your words of request. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा भगवांस्तत्र सभार्यो वृषभध्वजः ।
उवाच वेदिमध्यस्थो मूर्तिं कृत्वांशरूपिणीम् ॥ ४३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this, the lord Śiva in the company of his wife stayed in the middle of the altar creating a partial image of Himself. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

पत्न्या सत्या गन्तुमना अभूत्स्वजनवत्सलः ॥ ४४॥

English
Taking leave of Dakṣa, Śiva, the great lord, desired to depart along with his wife Satī. He was so fond of His own men. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दक्षो विनयावनतः सुधीः ।
साञ्जलिर्नतकः प्रीत्या तुष्टाव वृषभध्वजम् ॥ ४५॥

English
In the meantime, the intelligent Dakṣa bowed humbly with palms joined in reverence and eulogised Śiva with devotion. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्ण्वादयः सुराः सर्वे मुनयश्च गणास्तदा ।
नत्वा संस्तूय विविधं चक्रुर्जयरवं मुदा ॥ ४६॥

English
Viṣṇu, the gods and the Gaṇas bowed to and lauded Him shouting cries of victory with pleasure. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

जगाम हिमवत्प्रस्थं वृषभस्थः स्वयं प्रभुः ॥ ४७॥

English
With the joyous consent of Dakṣa, Śiva seated Satī on the bull and then sitting Himself on it went to the Himālayan ridges. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

विरेजे वृषभस्था वै चन्द्रान्ते कालिका यथा ॥ ४८॥

English
Seated on the bull along with Śiva, the sweet smiling Satī of fine teeth shone like a black cloud near the moon. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्ण्वादयः सुराः सर्वे मरीच्याद्यास्तथर्षयः ।

English
Viṣṇu and other devas, Marīci and other sages, Dakṣa and the other people were all in a state of pleasant steady senselessness. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Some played on musical instruments, others sang sweetly the lustrous glory of Śiva. They all followed Śiva joyously. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Half the way Śiva took leave of Dakṣa with pleasure. Along with his followers Dakṣa returned to his abode thrilled by Śiva's love. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Viṣṇu and other Devas, though permitted to go, followed Śiva with devotion and great joy. (52.)

तैः सर्वैः सगणैः शम्भुः सत्या च स्वस्त्रिया युतः ।
प्राप स्वं धाम संहृष्टो हिमवद् गिरिशोभितम् ॥ ५३॥

English
With these, his wife and his attendants Śiva reached his abode in the beauteous surroundings of the Himālayas with very great delight. (53.)

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वाखिलान्देवान्मुनीनपि परांस्तथा ।
मुदा विसर्जयामास बहु सम्मान्य सादरम् ॥ ५४॥

English
After reaching his abode Śiva honoured the devas and the great sages and then bade farewell to them with respect. (54.)

Sanskrit
शम्भुमाभाष्य ते सर्वे विष्ण्वाद्या मुदिताननाः ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुर्नत्वा स्तुत्वा च मुनयः सुराः ॥ ५५॥

English
Taking leave of Śiva eulogising and bowing to Him, Viṣṇu, as also the Gods and sages with joyful beaming faces returned to their respective abodes. (55.)

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि मुदितोऽत्यर्थं स्वपत्न्या दक्षकन्यया ।
हिमवत्प्रस्थसंस्थो हि विजहार भवानुगः ॥ ५६॥

English
Śiva with boundless pleasure in the company of his wife—the daughter of Dakṣa, sported in the Himālayan region following the conventions of the world. (56.)

Sanskrit
ततः स शङ्करः सत्या सगणः सूतिकृन्मुने ।
प्राप स्वं धाम संहृष्टः कैलासं पर्वतोत्तमम् ॥ ५७॥

English
Then O sage, Śiva, the primordial creation, entered His residence in Kailāsa the best of mountains along with Satī and his attendants. (57.)

Sanskrit
एतद् वः सर्वमाख्यातं यथा तस्य पुराभवत् ।
विवाहो वृषयानस्य मनुस्वायम्भुवान्तरे ॥ ५८॥

English
Thus I have narrated to you all how the marriage of the bull-vehicled lord took place formerly in the Manvantara of Svāyambhuva Manu.274 (58.)

[fn 274. The time-durations become manifest as Manvantara, Yuga, Saṃvatsara and other relatively bigger and smaller units in the rotating wheel of time. The Purāṇas mention fourteen Manvantaras in order :

(1) स्वायम्भुव (2) स्वारोचिष (3) औत्तिमि (4) तामस (5) रैवत
(6) चाक्षुष (7) वैवस्वत (8) सावणि (9) दक्षसावणि (10) ब्रह्म-
सावणि (11) धर्मसावणि (12) रुद्रसावणि (13) रौञ्च-दैव सावणि
(14) इन्द्रसावणि ।

The fourteen Manvantaras derive their names from fourteen successive mythical progenitors and sovereigns of the earth. Svāyambhuva Manvantara is the first and is known after Svāyambhuva Manu who produced the ten Prajāpatis or Mahārṣis and is so called because he sprang from Svayambhu, the Self-existent Brahman.]

Sanskrit
विवाहसमये यज्ञे प्रारम्भे वा श‍ृणोति यः ।
एतदाख्यानमव्यग्रः सम्पूज्य वृषभध्वजम् ॥ ५९॥

तस्याविघ्नं भवेत्सर्वं कर्म वैवाहिकं च यत् ।
शुभाख्यमपरं कर्म निर्विघ्नं सर्वदा भवेत् ॥ ६०॥

English
If any one hears this narrative with concentrated attention after worshipping Śiva at marriages, sacrifices or other auspicious undertakings, all the rites—of marriage or other auspicious undertaking—will always conclude without obstacles. (59-60.)

Sanskrit
कन्या च सुखसौभाग्यशीलाचारगुणान्विता ।
साध्वी स्यात्पुत्रिणी प्रीत्या श्रुत्वाख्यानमिदं शुभम् ॥ ६१॥

English
The bride will be blessed with happiness, good fortune, good conduct, and good qualities. She will be chaste and produce sons on hearing this auspicious narration. (61.)
