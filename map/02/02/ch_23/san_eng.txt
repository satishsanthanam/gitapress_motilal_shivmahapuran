### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - *(Description of the Power of Devotion)***

---

Sanskrit
२३

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं कृत्वा विहारं वै शङ्करेण च सा सती ।
सन्तुष्टा साभवच्चाति विरागा समजायत ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After sporting about like this till satiety with Śiva, Satī became less attached. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्दिवसे देवी सती रहसि सङ्गता ।
शिवं प्रणम्य सद्भक्त्या न्यस्योच्चैः सुकृताञ्जलिः ॥ २॥

सुप्रसन्नं प्रभुं नत्वा सा दक्षतनया सती ।
उवाच साञ्जलिर्भक्त्या विनयावनता ततः ॥ ३॥

English
One day after delighting the lord with her devotion and obeisance Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa, spoke thus to Śiva. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
दीनोद्धर महायोगिन् कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ ४॥

English
*Satī said :—*  O great lord, lord of lords and ocean of mercy, O great Yogin, the uplifter of the distressed, take pity on me. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं परः पुरुषः स्वामी रजःसत्त्वतमः परः ।
निर्गुणः सगुणः साक्षी निर्विकारी महाप्रभुः ॥ ५॥

English
You are a great Puruṣa, the lord, beyond Sattva, Rajas and Tamas. You are both Saguṇa and Nirguṇa.$^{286}$ You are a great lord, a cosmic witness, and free from aberration. (5.)

[fn 286. Śiva is conceived as Saguṇa (possessed of attributes), a personal deity who responds to prayer, bestows grace or enters into history. He is conceived also as Nirguṇa when in the devotee's state of mental spiritual enlightenment (Jñāna) he is identical with his self]
---

Sanskrit
धन्याहं ते प्रिया जाता कामिनी सुविहारिणी ।
जातस्त्वं मे पतिः स्वामिन् भक्तवात्सल्यतो हर ॥ ६॥

English
I am blessed since I became your beloved wife sporting with you. O lord, you became my husband because of your love for your devotees. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतो बहुसमा नाथ विहारः परमस्त्वया ।
सन्तुष्टाहं महेशान निवृत्तं मे मनस्ततः ॥ ७॥

English
O lord, after sporting with you for many years I have become fully satiated and now my mind is turned away from it. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञातुमिच्छामि देवेश परं तत्त्वं सुखावहम् ।
येन संसारदुःखाद्वै तरेज्जीवोऽञ्जसा हर ॥ ८॥

English
O lord of gods, I wish to know the great pleasing principle whereby O, Śiva, all living beings surmount worldly miseries in a trice. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्कृत्वा विषयी जीवः स लभेत्परमं पदम् ।
संसारी न भवेन्नाथ तत्त्वं वद कृपां कुरु ॥ ९॥

English
O lord, please explain that activity which enables people, to obtain the supreme region and free themselves from worldly bondage. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्यपृच्छत्स्म सद्भक्त्या शङ्करं सा सती मुने ।
आदिशक्तिर्महेशानी जीवोद्धाराय केवलम् ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* O sage, the primordial Goddess asked Śiva thus only for the sake of uplifting worldly creatures. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
आकर्ण्य तच्छिवः स्वामी स्वेच्छयोपात्तविग्रहः ।
अवोचत्परमप्रीतः सतीं योगविरक्तधीः ॥ ११॥

English
On hearing that, lord Śiva whose mind is engrossed in the practice of Yoga and who assumes physical bodies out of his own accord, spoke to Sati. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि प्रवक्ष्यामि दाक्षायणि महेश्वरि ।
परं तत्त्वं तदेवानुशयी मुक्तो भवेद्यतः ॥ १२॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  O Goddess Sati, listen, I shall explain the great principle whereby the remorseful creature becomes a liberated soul. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
परतत्त्वं विजानीहि विज्ञानं परमेश्वरी ।
द्वितीयं स्मरणं यत्र नाहं ब्रह्मेति शुद्धधीः ॥ १३॥

English
O great Goddess, know that the perfect knowledge is the great principle—the consciousness that “I am Brahman” in the perfect intellect where nothing else is remembered. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दुर्लभं त्रिलोकेऽस्मिंस्तज्ज्ञाता विरलः प्रिये ।
यादृशो यः स दासोऽहं ब्रह्म साक्षात्परात्परः ॥ १४॥

English
This consciousness is very rare in the three worlds. O beloved, I am Brahman, the greatest of the great and very few are those who know my real nature. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्माता मम भक्तिश्च भुक्तिमुक्तिफलप्रदा ।
सुलभा मत्प्रसादाद्धि नवधा सा प्रकीर्तिता ॥ १५॥

English
Devotion to me is considered as the bestower of worldly pleasures and salvation. It is achievable only by my grace. It is nine-fold. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तौ ज्ञाने न भेदो हि तत्कर्तुः सर्वदा सुखम् ।
विज्ञानं न भवत्येव सति भक्तिविरोधिनः ॥ १६॥

English
There is no difference between devotion and perfect knowledge. A person who is engrossed in devotion enjoys perpetual happiness. Perfect knowledge never descends in a vicious person averse to devotion. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्त्याधीः सदाहं वै तत्प्रभावाद् गृहेष्वपि ।
नीचानां जातिहीनानां यामि देवि न संशयः ॥ १७॥

English
Attracted by devotion and as a result of its influence, O Goddess, I go even to the houses of the base-born and outcasts. There is no doubt about it. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सा भक्तिर्द्विविधा देवि सगुणा निर्गुणा मता ।
वैधी स्वाभाविकी या या वरा सा त्ववरा स्मृता ॥ १८॥

नैष्ठिक्यनैष्ठिकी भेदाद् द्विविधे द्विविधे हि ते ।
षड्विधा नैष्ठिकी ज्ञेया द्वितीयैकविधा स्मृता ॥ १९॥

विहिताविहिताभेदात्तामनेकां विदुर्बुधाः ।
तयोर्बहुविधत्वाच्च तत्त्वं त्वन्यत्र वर्णितम् ॥ २०॥

English
Devotion is variously classified as attributive and attributeless, as conventional and natural, greater and lesser, perpetual and non-perpetual. There are six further subdivisions of the perpetual devotion. Scholars further classify it into enjoined and non-enjoined. Thus devotions are manifold which have been explained elsewhere. (18-20.)

---

Sanskrit
ते नवाङ्गे उभे ज्ञेये वर्णिते मुनिभिः प्रिये ।
वर्णयामि नवाङ्गानि प्रेमतः श‍ृणु दक्षजे ॥ २१॥

English
O beloved, sages have explained that the different kinds of devotion have nine ancillary adjuncts. O daughter of Dakṣa, I shall narrate them to which you listen with love. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रवणं कीर्तनं चैव स्मरणं सेवनं तथा ।
दास्यं तथार्चनं देवि वन्दनं मम सर्वदा ॥ २२॥

सख्यमात्मार्पणं चेति नवाङ्गानि विदुर्बुधाः ।
उपाङ्गानि शिवे तस्या बहूनि कथितानि वै ॥ २३॥

English
According to scholars O Goddess, the nine ancillary adjuncts are :—listening, eulogising, remembering, serving, surrendering, worshipping, saluting, friendliness and dedication. O Śiva, its further subdivisions too have been explained. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु देवि नवाङ्गानां लक्षणानि पृथक् पृथक् ।
मम भक्तेर्मनो दत्त्वा भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदानि हि ॥ २४॥

कथादेर्नित्यसम्मानं कुर्वन्देहादिभिर्मुदा ।
स्थिरासनेन तत्पानं यत्तच्छ्रवणमुच्यते ॥ २५॥

English
O Goddess, listen to the characteristics of these nine adjuncts separately. By listening is meant the imbibing of my stories that bestow worldly pleasures and salvation, with great devotion, in steady posture. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
हृदाकाशेन सम्पश्यन् जन्मकर्माणि वै मम ।
प्रीत्योच्चोच्चारणं तेषामेतत्कीर्तनमुच्यते ॥ २६॥

English
After conceiving in the mind the details of my manifestations and activities, loudly and cheerfully, proclaiming the same in order to eulogise me is what is called eulogising. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यापकं देवि मां दृष्ट्वा नित्यं सर्वत्र सर्वदा ।
निर्भयत्वं सदा लोके स्मरणं तदुदाहृतम् ॥ २७॥

English
O Goddess, after realising me to be all-pervading a feeling of fearlessness is what is called remembering. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अरुणोदयमारभ्य सेवाकालेऽञ्चिता हृदा ।
निर्भयत्वं सदा लोके स्मरणं तदुदाहृतम् ॥ २८॥

English
The service rendered to the godhead commencing at the early dawn, with mind, speech, hands and feet is what is called serving. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा सेव्यानुकूल्येन सेवनं तद्धि गोगणैः ।
हृदयामृतभोगेन प्रियं दास्यमुदाहृतम् ॥ २९॥

English
Surrendering oneself in the service of the godhead who is worthy of being served and serving with all the sense-organs feeling hearty sense of elation is what is called surrendering. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा भृत्यानुकूल्येन विधिना मे परात्मने ।
अर्पणं षोडशानां वै पाद्यादीनां तदर्चनम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Offering sixteen types of service to me, the supreme soul, in accordance with one's capacity is called worshipping. The sixteen types of service are Pādya$^{287}$ etc. (30.)

[fn 287. On the sixteen acts of worship See Note 49 P. 69.]

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्रोच्चारणध्यानाभ्यां मनसा वचसा क्रमात् ।
यदष्टाङ्गेन भूस्पर्शं तद्वै वन्दनमुच्यते ॥ ३१॥

English
Meditating in the mind, repeating the mantras and touching the ground with eight limbs$^{288}$ is called saluting. (31.)

[fn 288. On the Aṣṭāṅga praṇāma See Note No. 41 P. 54.]
---

Sanskrit
मङ्गलामङ्गलं यद्यत्करोतीतीश्वरो हि मे ।
सर्वं तन्मङ्गलायेति विश्वासः सख्यलक्षणम् ॥ ३२॥

English
The belief—"Whatever god bestows on me, good or bad, is for my welfare"—is the characteristic sign of friendliness. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा देहादिकं तस्य प्रीत्यै सर्वं तदर्पणम् ।
निर्वाहाय च शून्यत्वं यत्तदात्मसमर्पणम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Dedicating everything, the body and other possessions, for the propitiation of the godhead and retaining nothing for oneself is called dedication. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
नवाङ्गानीति मद्भक्तेर्भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदानि च ।
मम प्रियाणि चातीव ज्ञानोत्पत्तिकराणि च ॥ ३४॥

English
These nine adjuncts to the devotion to me, cause perfect knowledge, bestow worldly pleasures and salvation and are pleasing to me. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
उपाङ्गानि च मद्भक्तेर्बहूनि कथितानि वै ।
बिल्वादिसेवनादीनि समूह्यानि विचारतः ॥ ३५॥

English
The further subdivisions in the adjuncts are numerous. Nurturing the Bilva tree etc. can be included therein. They shall be thought of by the devotee himself. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं साङ्गोपाङ्गभक्तिर्मम सर्वोत्तमा प्रिये ।
ज्ञानवैराग्यजननी मुक्तिदासी विराजते ॥ ३६॥

English
O beloved, thus my devotion with various adjuncts and ancillaries, is contributory to salvation since it is productive of perfect Knowledge and Detachment. It is the most excellent path. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वकर्मफलोत्पत्तिः सर्वदा त्वत्समप्रिया ।
यच्चित्ते सा स्थिता नित्यं सर्वदा सोऽति मत्प्रियः ॥ ३७॥

English
A true devotion is as endearing to me as to you. It is productive of the fruits of all rites for ever. He who has it in his mind is a great favourite of mine. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रैलोक्ये भक्तिसदृशः पन्था नास्ति सुखावहः ।
चतुर्युगेषु देवेशि कलौ तु सुविशेषतः ॥ ३८॥

English
There is no other path as easy and pleasing as devotion in the three worlds, O goddess of devas, in all the four Yugas generally and in the Kaliyuga particularly. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
कलौ तु ज्ञानवैराग्यौ वृद्धरूपौ निरुत्सवौ ।
ग्राहकाभावतो देवि जातौ जर्जरतामति ॥ ३९॥

English
Knowledge (Jñāna) and Detachment (Vairāgya) have grown old and have lost their lustre in the Kali Age. They have become decayed and worn-out as the people who can grasp them are rare. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
कलौ प्रत्यक्षफलदा भक्तिः सर्वयुगेष्वपि ।
तत्प्रभावादहं नित्यं तद्वशो नात्र संशयः ॥ ४०॥

English
In the Kali age as in all the four Yugas there is immediate and visible benefit in devotion. I am subservient to a devotee in view of the power of devotion. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
यो भक्तिमान्पुमाँल्लोके सदाहं तत्सहायकृत् ।
विघ्नहर्ता रिपुस्तस्य दण्ड्यो नात्र च संशयः ॥ ४१॥

English
I always assist a man endowed with devotion and remove his obstacles. A person devoid of devotion is worthy of being punished. There is no doubt about it. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तहेतोरहं देवि कालं क्रोधपरिप्लुतः ।
अदहं वह्निना नेत्रभवेन निजरक्षकः ॥ ४२॥

English
I am the protector of my devotees. For the protection of a devotee of mine I burnt the God of death, O goddess, in the fire emerging from my eyes. (42.)

---

English
For the sake of a devotee of mine I became very furious with the sun formerly. I over-powered him with my trident. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तहेतोरहं देवि रव्युपर्यभवं किल ।
अतिक्रोधान्वितः शूलं गृहीत्वान्वजयं पुरा । ४३॥

भक्तहेतोरहं देवि रावणं सगणं क्रुधा ।
त्यजामि स्म कृतो नैव पक्षपातो हि तस्य वै ॥ ४४॥

English
I was not a party to the evil actions of Rāvaṇa (though he was my devotee). For the sake of another devotee I discarded Rāvaṇa with all his followers. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तहेतोरहं देवि व्यासं हि कुमतिग्रहम् ।
काश्या न्यसारयं क्रोधाद्दण्डयित्वा च नन्दिना ॥ ४५॥

English
O goddess, for the sake of a devotee, I angrily expelled Vyāsa when he had a vicious thought, from Kāsi after punishing him duly through Nandin. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन देवेशि भक्त्याधीनः सदा ह्यहम् ।
तत्कर्तुः पुरुषस्यातिवशगो नात्र संशयः ॥ ४६॥

English
Why shall I say more, O Goddess? I am always subservient to a devotee, always under the control of a person who practises devotion. There is no doubt in this. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थमाकर्ण्य भक्तेस्तु महत्त्वं दक्षजा सती ।
जहर्षातीव मनसि प्रणनाम शिवं मुदा ॥ ४७॥

English
Brahmā said :—  On hearing this greatness of devotion, Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa, was delighted much and bowed to Śiva with pleasure. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः पप्रच्छ सद्भक्त्या तत्काण्डविषयं मुने ।
शास्त्रं सुखकरं लोके जीवोद्धारपरायणम् ॥ ४८॥

English
O sage, again she asked with great devotion more about the subject as explained in the Śāstras which is pleasing and conducive to the uplift of all creatures. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
सयन्त्रमन्त्रशास्त्रं च तन्माहात्म्यं विशेषतः ।
अन्यानि धर्मवस्तूनि जीवोद्धारकराणि हि ॥ ४९॥

English
She enquired about topics on virtue and righteous living, uplighting the creatures and the sacred lore on Yantras and Mantras²⁸⁹ together with their greatness. (49.)

[fn 289. On the explanation of Yantrā and Mantra, see Note No. 47 p. 66.]

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करोऽपि तदाकर्ण्य सतीप्रश्नं प्रहृष्टधीः ।
वर्णयामास सुप्रीत्या जीवोद्धाराय कृत्स्नशः ॥ ५०॥

English
On hearing the enquiry of Satī Śiva was delighted and He narrated them with pleasure in their entirety for raising the worldly creatures. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्त्रशास्त्रं सयन्त्रं हि सपञ्चाङ्गं महेश्वरः ।
बभाषे महिमानं च तत्तद्देववरस्य वै ॥ ५१॥

English
The sacred lore bearing on the subject, the glory and greatness of the illustrious lord, Śiva explained Himself with Yantras, with their five adjuncts. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सेतिहासकथां तेषां भक्तमाहात्म्यमेव च ।
सवर्णाश्रमधर्मांश्च नृपधर्मान् मुनीश्वर ॥ ५२॥

English
He told her legendary stories, the greatness of the votaries, the norms of peoples of different castes and stages in life and the duties of kings, O great sage. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतस्त्रीधर्ममाहात्म्यं वर्णाश्रममनश्वरम् ।
वैद्यशास्त्रं तथा ज्योतिःशास्त्रं जीवसुखावहम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Thus Satī and Śiva who are intrinsically the Supreme Brahman, who are the bestowers of happiness on the three worlds, who are omniscient, who are bent upon rendering help to the people, who appear as the personification of good qualities sported about in Kailāsa, in the Himālayas and other places. (55-56.)
