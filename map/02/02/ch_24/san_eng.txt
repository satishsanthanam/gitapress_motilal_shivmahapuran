### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - (Sati's test of Rāma's divinity)**

---

Sanskrit
२४

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे प्रजानाथ महाप्राज्ञ कृपाकर ।
श्रावितं शङ्करयशः सतीशङ्करयोः शुभम् ॥ १॥

इदानीं ब्रूहि सत्प्रीत्या परं तद्यश उत्तमम् ।
किमकार्ष्टां हि तत्स्थौ वै चरितं दम्पती शिवौ ॥ २॥

English
Nārada said :—  "O Brahmā, lord of subjects, of great mercy and lofty intellect, you have narrated the benevolent glory of Sati and Śiva. Now, please tell me more of their glory. What did the couple Śiva and Śivā do further, stationed on that (mountain) ? (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
सतीशिवचरित्रं च श‍ृणु मे प्रेमतो मुने ।
लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रित्य चिक्रीडाते सदान्वहम् ॥ ३॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O sage, listen to the story of Sati and Śiva. Having resorted to worldly conventions they continued their sports every day. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सती महादेवी वियोगमलभन्मुने ।
स्वपतः शङ्करस्येति वदन्त्येके सुबुद्धयः ॥ ४॥

English
Thereafter, according to a tradition, it is said that the great Goddess Sati was separated from her husband Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वागर्थाविव सम्पृक्तौ शक्तीशौ सर्वदा चितौ ।
कथं घटेत च तयोर्वियोगस्तत्त्वतो मुने ॥ ५॥

English
Śakti and Iśa are united for ever like a word and its meaning⁸⁹¹. O sage, how can a real separation of the two occur ? (5.)

[fn 291. For the similarity of idea and expression, compare Kālidāsa's Raghuvamśa 1.1. For the repetition of the same see SP. RS II. 25. 69.]
---

Sanskrit
लीलारुचित्वादथवा सङ्घटेताखिलं च तत् ।
कुरुते यद्यदीशौ च सतीशौ भवरीतिगौ ॥ ६॥

English
Or inasmuch as Sati and Śiva have sportive interest, whatever they do is proper. For, they follow the conventions of the world. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सा त्यक्ता दक्षजा दृष्ट्वा पतिना जनकाध्वरे ।
शम्भोरनादरात्तत्र देहं तत्याज सङ्गता ॥ ७॥

English
She was forsaken by her husband at the time of her father's sacrifice. In view of the disrespect shown to Śiva she cast-off her body there. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्हिमालये सैवाविर्भूता नामतः सती ।
पार्वतीति शिवं प्राप तप्त्वा भूरि विवाहतः ॥ ८॥

English
She was born again as Pārvati, daughter of the Himālayas. She performed penance for several years and attained Śiva as her husband. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य ब्रह्मणः स तु नारदः ।
पप्रच्छ च विधातारं शिवाशिवमहद्यशः ॥ ९॥

English
Sūta said :—  After hearing these words of Brahmā, Nārada asked the Creator about the glory of Śivā and Śiva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
विष्णुशिष्य महाभाग विधे मे वद विस्तरात् ।
शिवाशिवचरित्रं तद्भवाचारपरानुगम् ॥ १०॥

English
Nārada said :—
  O Brahmā, disciple of Viṣṇu, of great fortune, please explain in detail the story of Śivā and Śiva who followed the conventions of the world. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
किमर्थं शङ्करो जायां तत्याज प्राणतः प्रियाम् ।
तस्मादाचक्ष्व मे तात विचित्रमिति मन्महे ॥ ११॥

English
O dear, why did Śiva abandon His wife who was to Him dearer than his life? It looks rather strange. Hence please explain. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कुतोऽध्वरेऽजः पुत्रस्यानादरोऽभूच्छिवस्य ते ।
कथं तत्याज सा देहं गत्वा तत्र पितुः क्रतौ ॥ १२॥

English
Wherefore did your son Dakṣa disrespect Śiva at the time of sacrifice? How did she abandon her body at the sacrifice of her father? (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः किमभवत्तत्र किमकार्षीन्महेश्वरः ।
तत्सर्वं मे समाचक्ष्व श्रद्धायुक् तच्छ्रुतावहम् ॥ १३॥

English
What happened after that? What did Śiva do? Please explain everything to me. I am eager to listen to it. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात परप्रीत्या मुनिभिः सह नारद ।
सुतवर्य महाप्राज्ञ चरितं शशिमौलिनः ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear Nārada, of great intellect, the most excellent of my sons, listen with pleasure, along with the sages, to the story of the moon-crested lord. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्कृत्य महेशानं हर्यादिसुरसेवितम् ।
परब्रह्म प्रवक्ष्यामि तच्चरित्रं महाद्भुतम् ॥ १५॥

English
After bowing to lord Śiva who is the supreme Brahman and who is served by Viṣṇu and others, I begin to explain and narrate His story of wonderful significance. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेयं शिवलीला हि बहुलीलाकरः प्रभुः ।
स्वतन्त्रो निर्विकारी च सती सापि हि तद्विधा ॥ १६॥

English
Everything is a sport of Śiva. The lord indulges in many divine sports. He is independent and undecaying. Satī too is like that. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा कः समर्थो हि तत्कर्मकरणे मुने ।
परमात्मा परब्रह्म स एव परमेश्वरः ॥ १७॥

यं सदा भजते श्रीशोऽहं चापि सकलाः सुराः ।
मुनयश्च महात्मानः सिद्धाश्च सनकादयः ॥ १८॥

English
Otherwise, O sage, who can perform such wonderful deeds? Lord Śiva alone is the Supreme soul and the Supreme Brahman whom we all worship—I, Viṣṇu, all the devas, sages, the noble-souled Siddhas like Sanaka$^{292}$ and others. (17-18.)

[fn 292. Here the reference is to the mind-born sons of Brahmā-Sanaka, Sananda, Sanātana and Sanat who are called Siddhas or semi-divine beings of great purity and holiness]
---

Sanskrit
शेषः सदा यशो यस्य मुदा गायति नित्यशः ।
पारं न लभते तात स प्रभुः शङ्करः शिवः ॥ १९॥

English
O dear one, Śiva is that lord whose glory is sung for ever by Śeṣa$^{293}$ with great pleasure but is never exhausted. (19.)

[fn 293. Śeṣa, a thousand-headed serpent, is the emblem of eternity. He is the son of Kadru and the King of the Nāgas or snakes inhabiting Pātāla.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्यैव लीलया सर्वोऽयमिति तत्त्वविभ्रमः ।
तत्र दोषो न कस्यापि सर्वव्यापी स प्रेरकः ॥ २०॥

English
The erroneous perception of this visible world is due to His own sports. There none can be blamed. The all-pervasive lord is the inducer. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्समये रुद्रः सत्या त्रिभवने भवः ।
वृषमारुह्य पर्याटद्रसां लीलाविशारदः ॥ २१॥

English
Once Śiva accompanied by Satī and seated on His Bull wandered over the Earth, in one of his sportive activities. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्य दण्डकारण्यं पर्यटन् सागराम्बराम् ।
दर्शयन् तत्रगां शोभां सत्यै सत्यपणः प्रभुः ॥ २२॥

English
Wandering over the ocean-girt Earth He reached Daṇḍaka$^{294}$ forest where the lord of truthful stake and transaction pointed to Satī the beauty of the surrounding nature. (22.)

[fn 294. Daṇḍaka forest lay between the Narmadā and the Godāvari. According to the Padmapurāṇa (V. 34, 5 14-50) it was named after the third son of King Ikṣvāku called Daṇḍa or Daṇḍaka. Vālmiki’s Rāmāyaṇa describes it as “a wilderness over which separate hermitages are scattered while wild beasts and Rākṣasas everywhere abound.”]
---

Sanskrit
तत्र रामं ददर्शासौ लक्ष्मणेनान्वितं हरः ।
अन्विष्यन्तं प्रियां सीतां रावणेन हृतां छलात् ॥ २३॥

English
There Śiva saw Rāma who was searching for Sītā who was deceitfully abducted by Rāvaṇa. Lakṣmaṇa too was there. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
हा सीतेति प्रोच्चरन्तं विरहाविष्टमानसम् ।
यतस्ततश्च पश्यन्तं रुदन्तं हि मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २४॥

English
Due to the pangs of separation Rāma was crying out “Alas Sītā.” He was pitiably lamenting and glancing here and there. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
समिच्छन्तं च तत्प्राप्तिं पृच्छन्तं तद्गतिं हृदा ।
कुजादिभ्यो नष्टधियमत्रपं शोकविह्वलम् ॥ २५॥

English
Rāma was yearning for her redemption. He was musing over her whereabouts. Due to adverse position of planets like Mars etc. he had become forlorn and shamelessly grief-stricken. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यवंशोद्भवं वीरं भूपं दशरथात्मजम् ।
भरताग्रजमानन्दरहितं विगतप्रभम् ॥ २६॥

English
He was a heroic king of the solar race, son of Daśaratha, elder brother of Bharata. He had become cheerless and devoid of lustre. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्णकामो वराधीनं प्राणमत्स्म मुदा हरः ।
रामं भ्रमन्तं विपिने सलक्ष्मणमुदारधीः ॥ २७॥

English
The great liberal-minded lord Śiva who is Pūrṇakāma (one whose ambitions are fully realised) delightfully bowed to Rāma who was wandering in the forest in the company of Lakṣmaṇa and was in need of a favour. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
जयेत्युक्त्वान्यतोऽगच्छन्नदात्तस्मै स्वदर्शनम् ।
रामाय विपिने तस्मिन् शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २८॥

English
“Be victorious” said Śiva who is favourably disposed to His devotees. While He was going elsewhere in the forest He revealed Himself to Rāma. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
इतीदृशीं सती दृष्ट्वा शिवलीलां विमोहनीम् ।
सुविस्मिता शिवं प्राह शिवमायाविमोहिता ॥ २९॥

English
Satī was surprised at this charmingly strange sport of Śiva. She was deluded by Śiva’s Māyā and spoke to Him. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
देवदेव परब्रह्म सर्वेश परमेश्वर ।
सेवन्ते त्वां सदा सर्वे हरिब्रह्मादयः सुराः ॥ ३०॥

English
*Satī said:—* O lord, the lord of all, the Supreme Brahman, all the devas, Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others serve Thee always. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

वेदान्तवेद्यो यत्नेन निर्विकारी परप्रभुः ॥ ३१॥

English
Thou art worthy of being served and bowed to. Thou art worthy of being meditated upon always. Thou art known and realised only through the science of Metaphysics, after strenuous efforts. Thou art the great lord, the undecaying. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
काविमौ पुरुषौ नाथ विरहव्याकुलाकृती ।
विचरन्तौ वने क्लिष्टौ दीनौ वीरौ धनुर्धरौ ॥ ३२॥

English
O lord, who are these two persons apparently grief-stricken from pangs of separation? Though heroic archers they are greatly distressed. They seem to be roaming about in the forest. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

मुदितः सुप्रसन्नात्माभवो भक्त इवाधुना ॥ ३३॥

English
How is it that Thou becomest highly delighted and behavest like a devotee on seeing the elder of the two who resembles a blue lotus (in complexion) ? (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इति मे संशयं स्वामिन् शङ्कर छेत्तुमर्हसि ।
सेव्यस्य सेवके नैव घटते प्रणतिः प्रभो ॥ ३४॥

English
O lord Śiva, may this doubt of mine be kindly heard. O lord, the kneeling down of the master at the feet of a servant is not quite befitting. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
आदिशक्तिः सती देवी शिवा सा परमेश्वरी ।
शिवमायावशी भूत्वा पप्रच्छेत्थं शिवं प्रभुम् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  The great Goddess Satī the primordial Śakti, put this question to Śiva on being deluded by Śiva's illusion. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य वचः सत्याः शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ।
तदा विहस्य स प्राह सतीं लीलाविशारदः ॥ ३६॥

English
On hearing these words of Satī, lord Śiva laughed and said to Satī. He was shrewd in his divine sports. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
परमेश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि सति प्रीत्या यथार्थं वच्मि नच्छलम् ।
वरदानप्रभावात्तु प्रणामं चैवमादरात् ॥ ३७॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :*  "O Goddess Satī, listen with pleasure. I shall truly explain it. There is no deception. I bowed thus with respect due to the power of the boon (granted by me). (37.)

---

Sanskrit
रामलक्ष्मणनामानौ भ्रातरौ वीरसम्मतौ ।

English
O Goddess, they are two brothers Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa. They are heroic, intelligent sons of Daśaratha, born of the solar dynasty. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The fair-complexioned one is the younger brother Lakṣmaṇa. He is the partial incarnation of Śeṣa. The elder one is the complete incarnation of Viṣṇu. He is called Rāma. He is incapable of being harassed. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

इत्युक्त्वा विररामासौ शम्भुः सूतिकरः प्रभुः ॥ ४०॥

English
The lord has incarnated on the Earth for our welfare and the protection of the good." Saying thus Śiva, the lord, who causes prosperity to his votaries stopped. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
On realising that her mind was not convinced, Śiva, the eternal lord, who is shrewd in the divine sports which He indulges in, spoke these words:— (42.)

---

Sanskrit

श‍ृणु मद्वचनं देवि न विश्वसिति चेन्मनः ।

English
*Śiva said :*  O Goddess, if your mind is not convinced, listen to my words. You can test the divinity of Rāma yourself, using your own intelligence. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O beloved Satī, he is standing there beneath the Vaṭa tree. You can test him and proceed until your delusion is quelled. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Going there at Śiva’s bidding, Satī the Goddess thought—“How shall I test Rāma the forest-roamer. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Deciding like this she who was deluded by Śiva became Sītā and went there to test him. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति रामवचः श्रुत्वा चकितासीत्सती तदा ।
स्मृत्वा शिवोक्तं मत्वा चावितथं लज्जिता भृशम् ॥ ५१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Rāma, Satī was stunned. Remembering Śiva’s words and realising the truth of the same she felt ashamed. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
रामं विज्ञाय विष्णुं तं स्वरूपं संविधाय च ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदं चित्ते सत्युवाच प्रसन्नधीः ॥ ५२॥

English
Realising Rāma to be Viṣṇu she re-assumed her own original form. Remembering Śiva’s feet in her heart Satī spoke delightedly :— (52.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो मया गणैश्चैव पर्यटन् वसुधां प्रभुः ।
इहागच्छच्च विपिने स्वतन्त्रः परमेश्वरः ॥ ५३॥

English
Wandering over the earth along with me in the company of his attendants, the great lord Śiva came here in the forest. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
अपश्यदत्र स त्वां हि सीतान्वेषणतत्परम् ।
सलक्ष्मणं विरहिणं सीतया क्लिष्टमानसम् ॥ ५४॥

English
Here he saw you searching for Sītā in the company of Lakṣmaṇa. You were highly distressed on account of separation from Sītā. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
नत्वा त्वां स गतो मूले वटस्य स्थित एव हि ।
प्रशंसन् महिमानं ते वैष्णवं परमं मुदा ॥ ५५॥

English
At the root of the Vaṭa he came and bowed to you glorifying your greatness with pleasure. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्भुजं हरिं त्वां नो दृष्ट्वैव मुदितोऽभवत् ।
यथेदं रूपममलं पश्यन्नानन्दमाप्तवान् ॥ ५६॥

English
He was not so happy on seeing the four-armed Viṣṇu as on seeing this simple pure form of yours. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं शम्भोर्भ्रममानीय चेतसि ।
तदाज्ञया परीक्षां ते कृतवत्यस्मि राघव ॥ ५७॥

English
O Rāma, on hearing those words of Śiva, my mind became suspicious and at his bidding I desired to test your divinity. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञातं मे राम विष्णुस्त्वं दृष्टा ते प्रभुताखिला ।
निःसंशया तदापि तच्छृणु त्वं च महामते ॥ ५८॥

English
O Rāma, I have realised your Viṣṇuism. I have seen your over-all lordship. I am now free from doubts. But, still, O intelligent one, please listen to this. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं प्रणम्यस्त्वं तस्य सत्यं ब्रूहि ममाग्रतः ।
कुरु निःसंशयां त्वं मां शमलं प्राप्नुहि द्रुतम् ॥ ५९॥

English
How is it that you became worthy of being saluted by him? Please tell me the truth. Make me free from doubt. Thus you shall be happy. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्या रामश्चोत्फुल्ललोचनः ।
अस्मरत्स्वं प्रभुं शम्भुं प्रेमाभूद् हृदि चाधिकम् ॥ ६०॥

English
Brahmā said:—  On hearing her words Rāma became happy, his eyes shining with brilliance. He thought upon his lord Śiva. Emotions of love swelled in his heart. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्या विनाज्ञया शम्भुसमीपं नागमन्मुने ।
संवर्ण्य महिमानं च प्रावोचद्राघवः सतीम् ॥ ६१॥

English
O sage, without the specific permission of Śatī he did not go near Śiva. Describing his greatness Rāma spoke to Śatī again. (61.)
