### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - (Separation of Satī and Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
२५

श्रीराम उवाच ।
एकदा हि पुरा देवि शम्भुः परमसूतिकृत् ।
विश्वकर्माणमाहूय स्वलोके परतः परे ॥ १॥

तेन स्वधेनुशालायां कारयामास विस्तृतम् ।
रम्यं च भवनं सम्यक् तत्र सिंहासनं वरम् ॥ २॥

English
Rāma said:—  O Goddess, formerly once, Śiva, the creator supreme, called Viśvakarman²⁹⁵ to His highest region. He made him erect a large hall of great beauty in His cowshed, and an exquisite throne there. (1-2.)

[fn 295. In the Purāṇas Viśvakarman is invested with the powers and offices of the Vedic Tvaṣṭr. He is the great architect, executor of handicrafts, the builder of great cities. He is the son of Prabhāsa, the eighth Vasu, by his wife Yogasiddhā.]

---

Sanskrit
तत्र च्छत्रं महादिव्यं सर्वदाद्भुतमुत्तमम् ।
कारयामास विघ्नार्थं शङ्करो विश्वकर्मणा ॥ ३॥

English
Śiva, caused Viśvakarman to make an excellent, divine, wonderful umbrella for warding off obstacles. (3.)

Sanskrit

सिद्धगन्धर्वनागादीनुपदेवांश्च कृत्स्नशः ॥ ४॥

देवान् सर्वानागमांश्च विधिपुत्रान् मुनीनपि ।
देवीः सर्वा अप्सरोभिर्नानावस्तुसमन्विताः ॥ ५॥

English
He invited Indra and other gods, the Siddhas, Gandharvas, Nāgas, Upadeśas and Āgamas²⁹⁶, Brahmā with his sons, the sages and the celestial goddesses and nymphs who came there with various articles. (4-5.)

[fn 296. The Upadeśas (instructions) and the Āgamas (scriptures) are personified. They refer to the persons who impart instructions and are well versed in the scriptures.]

English
Sixteen virgins each of devas, sages, Siddhas and serpents were brought for the auspicious ceremony. (6.)

English
With a delightful mind He called Viṣṇu from Vaikuṇṭha. O Goddess, Śiva rejoiced at the perfect devotion of Viṣṇu. (10.)

Sanskrit

English
In an auspicious hour, the great lord made Viṣṇu sit on the exquisite throne and delightedly decorated him in every way. (11.)

Sanskrit

English
A beautiful coronet was fixed on Viṣṇu and the auspicious holy thread was tied to his waist. He was then coronated by lord Śiva in the Cosmic Hall. (12.)

Sanskrit

English
What was His own and even non-transferable, Śiva the independent and favourably disposed to His devotees, conferred on Viṣṇu and eulogised him. (13.)

Sanskrit

English
The lord who is favourably disposed to His devotees, revealing Himself independent but subservient to the boons granted by Him, spoke these words to Brahmā the creator of all worlds. (14.)

Sanskrit

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  Lord, may you all hear. From now onwards, at my bidding, this Viṣṇu has become worthy of my respect and that of all devas. Dear one, you too bow to him. May all the Vedas extol him at my bidding as they extol me. (15-16.)

Sanskrit
श्रीराम उवाच ।

English
*Rāma said:—*  So saying, Rudra, Himself bowed to Garuḍa-bannered Viṣṇu. The bestower of boons, He who is favourably disposed to His devotees, felt delighted by his devotion to Viṣṇu. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

मुनिसिद्धादिभिश्चैव वन्दितोऽभूद्धरिस्तदा ॥ १८॥

English
Then Viṣṇu was duly revered by Brahmā followed by devas, sages, Siddhas and others. (18.)

Sanskrit

English
Then the delighted Lord Śiva, favourably disposed towards his devotees, bestowed great boons on Viṣṇu and the other devas. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

त्वं कर्ता सर्वलोकानां भर्ता हर्ता मदाज्ञया ।

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  At my bidding you are now the creator, sustainer and destroyer of all the worlds. You are the bestower of virtue, wealth and love and the chastiser of people of evil predilection. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
जगदीशो जगत्पूज्यो महाबलपराक्रमः ।
अजेयस्त्वं रणे क्वापि ममापि हि भविष्यसि ॥ २१॥

English
You are the lord of the universe. You are worthy of the worship of the universe. You will be invincible in battle anywhere even against me. You will be endowed with great strength and valour. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
You take three Śaktis—will etc. conferred by me. You can have the power of exhibiting diverse sports and independence in the three worlds. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Viṣṇu, persons who hate you shall indeed be chastised and curbed by me with strenuous efforts. Salvation shall be given by me, O Viṣṇu, to your devotees. (23.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Accept this Māyā too which cannot be withstood by devas and others and by which the entire universe will be deluded and made insentient as it were. (24.)

Sanskrit

English
O Viṣṇu, you are my left hand, as Brahmā is my right hand. You shall be his progenitor and sustainer too. (25.)

English

धन्यस्त्वं परमेशान त्वत्समोऽद्य तथा पणः ।
न कोऽप्यन्यस्त्रिलोकेऽस्मिन् महायोगी महाप्रभुः ॥ ५४॥

English
*The celestial voice said :—*  O great Lord, you are blessed indeed. There is no other great Yogin or great lord in the three worlds, on a par with you. No one else can maintain that promise. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रुत्वा व्योमवचो देवी शिवं पप्रच्छ विप्रभा ।
कं पणं कृतवान्नाथ ब्रूहि मे परमेश्वर ॥ ५५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the celestial voice, the goddess, utterly lustrless asked Śiva—“O lord, please tell me, what is the promise that you made?” (55.)

---

Sanskrit
इति पृष्टोऽपि गिरिशः सत्या हितकरः प्रभुः ।
नोद्वाहं स्वं पणं तस्यै यत् हर्यग्रेऽकरोत्पुरा ॥ ५६॥

English
Even when asked, the lord who was benevolent to Satī did not reveal the vow which he took in the presence of Viṣṇu formerly. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सती शिवं ध्यात्वा स्वपतिं प्राणवल्लभम् ।
सर्वं बुबोध हेतुं तं प्रियत्यागमयं मुने ॥ ५७॥

English
Then O sage, meditating on Śiva, her own beloved husband, Satī understood the matter which meant the abandonment of her own self. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽतीव शुशोचाशु बुध्वा सा त्यागमात्मनः ।
शम्भुना दक्षजा तस्मान्निःश्वसन्ती मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ५८॥

English
After realising the abandonment of herself by Him, the daughter of Dakṣa was grieved much and began to heave sighs frequently. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्तस्याः समाज्ञाय गुप्तं चक्रे मनोभवम् ।
सत्यै पणं स्वकीयं हि कथा बह्वीर्वदन् प्रभुः ॥ ५९॥

English
But the lord Śiva kept the fact of His vow a secret from her and narrated many a tale to her. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्या प्राप स कैलासं कथयन् विविधाः कथाः ।
वरे स्थित्वा निजं रूपं दधौ योगी समाधिभृत् ॥ ६०॥

English
Thus, narrating tales to her on the way He reached Kailāsa along with her. There Śiva, the Yogin, entered a trance and meditated upon His real form. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तस्थौ सती धाम्नि महाव्याकुलमानसा ।
न बुबोध चरित्रं तत्कश्चिच्च शिवयोर्मुने ॥ ६१॥

English
Satī stayed in the abode, overwhelmed by grief. But O sage, no one could guess the conduct of Śiva and Śivā. (61.)

Sanskrit

स्वोपात्तदेहयोः प्रभ्वोर्लोकलीलानुसारिणोः ॥ ६२॥

English
O sage, great time elapsed even as the lord and the Goddess followed the conventions of the world through the physical bodies taken up by themselves. (62.)

Sanskrit

तज्ज्ञात्वा जगदम्बा हि सती तत्राजगाम सा ॥ ६३॥

English
Then Śiva the great enjoyer and protector stopped His meditation. On coming to know of it Satī, the mother of the universe, came there. (63.)

Sanskrit

English
The Goddess bowed to Him with a moaning heart. The benevolent Śiva offered her a seat in front of Himself. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
कथयामास सुप्रीत्या कथा वह्वीर्मनोरमाः ।

English
He narrated several interesting tales to her. By these divine sports He tried to entertain her and make her mind free from grief. (65.)

Sanskrit

नेत्याश्चर्यं शिवे तात मन्तव्यं परमेश्वरे ॥ ६६॥

English
She regained her previous happiness. He too did not forsake His vow. O dear, this need not be considered wonderful in the benign great lord Śiva. (66.)

Sanskrit

English
But O sage, some ignorant Pandits thus narrate the story of Śivā and Śiva and their separation. But how can there be a real separation between the two? (67.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Who knows the true life and conduct of Śivā and Śiva. They sport about of their own accord and make their own lives for ever. (68.)

Sanskrit

Śatī and Śiva are united together like words and their meanings²⁹⁷. Only if they wish, can their separation be even imagined. (69.)
