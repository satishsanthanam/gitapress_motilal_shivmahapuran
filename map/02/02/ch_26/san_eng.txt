### **CHAPTER TWENTYSIX - (The cause of estrangement between Dakṣa and Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
२६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
पुराभवच्च सर्वेषामध्वरो विधिना महान् ।
प्रयागे समवेतानां मुनीनां च महात्मनाम् ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said :  Formerly, a great sacrifice was performed by the sages and noble souls who assembled at Prayāga²⁹⁸. (1.)

[fn 298. Prayāga : See note No. 27 P. 35.]
---

Sanskrit
तत्र सिद्धाः समायाताः सनकाद्याः सुरर्षयः ।
सप्रजापतयो देवा ज्ञानिनो ब्रह्मदर्शिनः ॥ २॥

English
Siddhas, Sanaka and others, the celestial sages, devas with Prajāpatis and men of perfect knowledge who had realised the Brahman attended the function. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं समागतस्तत्र परिवारसमन्वितः ।
निगमैरागमैर्युक्तो मूर्तिमद्भिर्महाप्रभैः ॥ ३॥

English
I too attended the same along with my followers. All the Āgamas and Nigamas in brilliant embodied forms were present with me. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
समाजोऽभूद्विचित्रो हि तेषामुत्सवसंयुतः ।
ज्ञानवादोऽभवत्तत्र नानाशास्त्रसमुद्भवः ॥ ४॥

English
The assembly was variegated and of diverse character. They conducted discussions on epistemology from different sacred texts with great festivities. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे रुद्रः सभवानीगणः प्रभुः ।
त्रिलोकहितकृत्स्वामी तत्रागात्सूतिकृन्मुने ॥ ५॥

English
O sage, in the meantime, lord Śiva, accompanied by His attendants and Satī came there, the lord conferring benefit on the three worlds and affording them protection. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा शिवं सुराः सर्वे सिद्धाश्च मुनयस्तथा ।
अनमंस्तं प्रभुं भक्त्या तुष्टुवुश्च तथा ह्यहम् ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing the lord, devas, Siddhas and sages bowed to and eulogised Him with great devotion. I too joined them. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्थुः शिवाज्ञया सर्वे यथास्थानं मुदान्विताः ।
प्रभुदर्शनसन्तुष्टाः वर्णयन्तो निजं विधिम् ॥ ७॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva they sat in their respective places. They were excessively delighted on seeing the lord. They explained to Him the various activities they were engaged in. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे दक्षः प्रजापतिपतिः प्रभुः ।
आगमत्तत्र सुप्रीतः सुवर्चस्वी यदृच्छया ॥ ८॥

English
In the meantime, the lord Dakṣa, the lord of Prajāpatis, came there delighted by shedding lustre everywhere in the course of a casual visit. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मां प्रणम्य स दक्षो हि न्युष्टस्तत्र मदाज्ञया ।
ब्रह्माण्डाधिपतिर्मान्यो मानी तत्त्वबहिर्मुखः ॥ ९॥

English
After saluting me, Dakṣa sat there with my consent. Dakṣa, the lord of the universe, was a bit arrogant though worthy of honour, as he had no deep insight into Reality. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुतिभिः प्रणिपातैश्च दक्षः सर्वैः सुरर्षिभिः ।
पूजितो वरतेजस्वी करौ वध्वा विनम्रकैः ॥ १०॥

English
Dakṣa, of great splendour was honoured by the humble celestial sages with laudatory songs, obeisance, and the joining of palms in great reverence. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाविहारकृन्नाथः स्वतन्त्रः परमोतिकृत् ।
नानामत्तं तदा दक्षं स्वासनस्थो महेश्वरः ॥ ११॥

English
But lord Śiva who indulges in diverse sports, sat firmly and did not bow to Him. Naturally the lord who is the cause of protection is independent. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वानतं हरं तत्र स मे पुत्रोऽप्रसन्नधीः ।
अकुप्यत्सहसा रुद्रे तदा दक्षः प्रजापतिः ॥ १२॥

English
Seeing Śiva not bowing to him, my son became displeased. Dakṣa, the patriarch, was furious with Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रूरदृष्ट्या महागर्वो दृष्ट्वा रुद्रं महाप्रभुम् ।
सर्वान्संश्रावयन्नुच्चैरवोचज्ज्ञानवर्जितः ॥ १३॥

English
Haughty and devoid of perfect knowledge, Dakṣa looked cruelly at Śiva and spoke aloud within the hearing of all present. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षवाच ।
एते हि सर्वे च सुरासुरा भृशं
नमन्ति मां विप्रवरास्तथर्षयः ।
कथं ह्यसौ दुर्जनवन्महामना
त्वभूत्तु यः प्रेतपिशाचसंवृतः ॥ १४॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  “All these Suras and Asuras, brahmins and sages bow to me. How is it that this gentleman who is always surrounded by goblins and ghosts behaves like a wicked man ? (14.)

---

Sanskrit
श्मशानवासी निरपत्रपो ह्ययं
कथं प्रणामं न करोति मेऽधुना ।
लुप्तक्रियो भूतपिशाचसेवितो
मत्तोऽविधो नीतिविदूषकः सदा ॥ १५॥

English
“How is it that this shameless frequenter of cremation grounds does not bow to me now ? He is devoid of rites. He has cast off religious practices. He is surrounded by spirits and ghosts. He is elated and he spoils good policies and conventions. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पाखण्डिनो दुर्जनपापशीला
दृष्ट्वा द्विजं प्रोद्धतनिन्दकाञ्च ।
वध्वां सदासक्तरतिप्रवीण-
स्तस्मादमुं शप्तुमहं प्रवृत्तः ॥ १६॥

English
Heretics, wicked persons, who behave arrogantly on seeing a brahmin and despise him are on a par with one another. Besides, this person is always engrossed in the love of his wife. Hence I am going to curse him”. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवमुक्त्वा स महाखलस्तदा
रुषान्वितो रुद्रमिदं ह्यवोचत् ।
श‍ृण्वन्त्वमी विप्रवरास्तथा सुरा
वध्यं हि मे चार्हथ कर्तुमेतम् ॥ १७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying thus the furious rogue spoke to Śiva thus. *Dakṣa said :—* May all these brahmins and devas listen. May all of you deem him worthy of being killed by me. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
रुद्रो ह्ययं यज्ञबहिष्कृतो मे
वर्णेष्वतीतोऽथ विवर्णरूपः ।
देवैर्न भागं लभतां सहैव
श्मशानवासी कुलजन्महीनः ॥ १८॥

English
Let not this Śiva, a resident of cremation grounds, lacking in nobility of birth and pedigree, expelled by me from sacrifices, an outcaste and ugly-shaped, obtain his share along with the devas”. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति दक्षोक्तमाकर्ण्य भृग्वाद्या बहवो जनाः ।
अगर्हयन् दुष्टसत्त्वं रुद्रं मत्त्वामरैः समम् ॥ १९॥

नन्दी निशम्य तद्वाक्यं लोलाक्षोऽतिरुषान्वितः ।
अब्रवीत् त्वरितं दक्षं शापं दातुमना गणः ॥ २०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Dakṣa, Bhr̥gu$^{299}$ and others reproached Śiva. After duly saluting Śiva along with the devas, Nandin, the attendant of Śiva who had heard the words of Dakṣa, was very furious and rolled his eyes. With an intention to curse him, he immediately spoke to Dakṣa. (19-20.)

[fn 299. Bhr̥gu is one of the Prajāpatis and great sages and is regarded as the founder of the race of the Bhr̥gus or Bhārgavas, in which Jamadagni and Paraśurāma were born.]
---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
रे रे शठ महामूढ दक्ष दुष्टमते त्वया ।
यज्ञबाह्यो हि मे स्वामी महेशो हि कृतः कथम् ॥ २१॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—*  “O foolish Dakṣa, of roguish and wicked intention, how is it that you have expelled my lord Śiva from sacrifice ? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य स्मरणमात्रेण भवन्ति सफला मखाः ।
तीर्थानि च पवित्राणि सोऽयं शप्तो हरः कथम् ॥ २२॥

English
How is it that you cursed him whose thought makes all sacrifices fruitful and all sacred places holy ? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वृथा ते ब्रह्मचापल्याच्छप्तोऽयं दक्ष दुर्मते ।
वृथोपहसितश्चैवादुष्टो रुद्रो महाप्रभुः ॥ २३॥

English
O Dakṣa, of wicked intentions, in vain did you curse him by your inconsiderate rashness as a brahmin. The great lord Śiva who is free from defects, has in vain been ridiculed by you. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
येनेदं पाल्यते विश्वं सृष्टमन्ते विनाशितम् ।
शप्तोऽयं स कथं रुद्रो महेशो ब्राह्मणाधम ॥ २४॥

English
O vile brahmin, how is it that you cursed Śiva the great lord, by whom this universe is created, sustained and destroyed in the end ?” (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं निर्भत्सितस्तेन नन्दिना हि प्रजापतिः ।
नन्दिनं च शशापाथ दक्षो रोषसमन्वितः ॥ २५॥

English
Censured and rebuked thus by Nandin, Dakṣa the patriarch who was still furious cursed Nandin too. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
यूयं सर्वे रुद्रगणा वेदबाह्या भवन्तु वै ।
वेदमार्गपरित्यक्तास्तथा त्यक्ता महर्षिभिः ॥ २६॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  “You all, the attendants of Śiva, are expelled from Vedic rites. You will be abandoned by the followers of the Vedic path as well as by great sages. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पाखण्डवादनिरताः शिष्टाचारबहिष्कृताः ।
मदिरापाननिरता जटाभस्मास्थिधारिणः ॥ २७॥

English
“You all will be confirmed heretics, out of the conventions of society. You will indulge in drinking wine. Matted hair, ashes and bones will be your embellishments”. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति शप्तास्तथा तेन दक्षेण शिवकिङ्कराः ।
तच्छ्रुत्वातिरुषाविष्टोऽभवन्नन्दी शिवप्रियः ॥ २८॥

English
# *Brahmā said :—* Thus Śiva’s attendants were cursed by Dakṣa. On hearing that, Nandin the favourite of Śiva became furious. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्युवाच द्रुतं दक्षं गर्वितं तं महाखलम् ।
शिलादतनयो नन्दी तेजस्वी शिववल्लभः ॥ २९॥

English
Nandin, the brilliant son of Śilāda and favourite of Śiva, spoke immediately to Dakṣa who was excessively roguish and haughty. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
रे दक्ष शठ दुर्बुद्धे वृथैव शिवकिङ्कराः ।
शप्तास्ते ब्रह्मचापल्याच्छिवतत्त्वमजानता ॥ ३०॥

English
# *Nandīśvara said :—*  O roguish wicked Dakṣa, in vain did you curse Śiva’s attendants, you who do not know Śiva’s principles. You have exercised your indiscreet rashness on being a brahmin. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
भृग्वाद्यैर्दुष्टचित्तैश्च मूढैः स उपहासितः ।
महाप्रभुर्महेशानो ब्राह्मणत्वादहम्मते ॥ ३१॥

English
The great lord Śiva is ridiculed by the evil-minded fools Bhr̥gu and others apparently due to their egotism in being brahmins. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ये रुद्रविमुखाश्चात्र ब्राह्मणास्त्वादृशाः खलाः ।
रुद्रतेजःप्रभावत्वात्तेषां शापं ददाम्यहम् ॥ ३२॥

English
With the power of Śiva (backing me) I now heap curses on these brahmins here who are against Śiva and hence wicked like you. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदवादरता यूयं वेदतत्त्वबहिर्मुखाः ।
भवन्तु सततं विप्रा नान्यदस्तीति वादिनः ॥ ३३॥

English
You are engaged in discussing Vedas but you will be ignorant of Vedic principles. May these brahmins prattle that there is nothing else. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
कामात्मानः स्वर्गपराः क्रोधलोभमदान्विताः ।
भवन्तु सततं विप्रा भिक्षुका निरपत्रपाः ॥ ३४॥

English
May these brahmins indulging in lust, heavenly pleasures, anger, covetousness and pride be shameless beggars. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदमार्गं पुरस्कृत्य ब्राह्मणाः शूद्रयाजिनः ।
दरिद्रा वै भविष्यन्ति प्रतिग्रहरताः सदा ॥ ३५॥

English
These brahmins will be officiating in the sacrifices of Śūdras, following the Vedic path. They will be perpetually poor and eager to receive monetary gifts. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
असत्प्रतिग्रहाश्चैव सर्वे निरयगामिनः ।
भविष्यन्ति सदा दक्ष केचिद्वै ब्रह्मराक्षसाः ॥ ३६॥

English
Due to their acceptance of monetary gifts from undeserving persons they will fall into hell. O Dakṣa, some of them will become brahminical Rākṣasas. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
यः शिवं सुरसामान्यमुद्दिश्य परमेश्वरम् ।
द्रुह्यत्यजो दुष्टमतिः तत्त्वतां विमुखो भवेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
Brahmā who rivals with Lord Śiva, considering him on a par with ordinary devas and who has evil intentions too, will become averse to the true principles of Śaiva cult. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
कूटधर्मेषु गेहेषु सदा ग्राम्यसुखेच्छया ।
कर्मतन्त्रं वितनुतां वेदवादं च शाश्वतम् ॥ ३८॥

विनष्टानन्दकमुखो विस्मृतात्मगतिः पशुः ।
भ्रष्टकर्मानयरतो दक्षो बस्तमुखोऽचिरात् ॥ ३९॥

English
Dakṣa will become goat-faced ere long. He will be indulging in vulgar worldly lustful pleasures, and evil strategies. He will be laying down rules for rituals and perpetually discussing Vedic passages. His bright pleasing face will disappear. He will become individual soul strayed from his ultimate goal. He will fall from his holy rites and indulge in wicked deeds. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
शप्तास्ते कोपिना तत्र नन्दिना ब्राह्मणा यदा ।
हाहाकारो महानासीच्छप्तो दक्षेण चेश्वरः ॥ ४०॥

English
When the brahmins were cursed by the furious Nandin and Śiva was cursed by Dakṣa there was a great hue and cry. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्याहमत्यन्तमनिन्दं तं मुहुर्मुहुः ।
भृग्वादीनपि विप्रांश्च वेदसृट् शिवतत्त्ववित् ॥ ४१॥

English
On hearing that, I, the creator of the Vedas and the knower of the principles of Śiva rebuked him frequently and also the brahmins Bhṛgu and others. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

उवाच मधुरं वाक्यं बोधयंस्तं सदाशिवः ॥ ४२॥

English
On hearing the words of Nandin, the lord Sadāśiva laughed and spoke sweetly to him enlightening him further. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिव उवाच ।
श‍ृणु नन्दिन् महाप्राज्ञ न कर्तुं क्रोधमर्हसि ।
वृथा शप्तं ब्रह्मकुलं मत्वा शप्तं च मां भ्रमात् ॥ ४३॥

English
Sadāśiva said :— “O Nandin of great intellect, listen. Do not get angry. You have cursed the brahmins in vain, erroneously thinking that I have been cursed. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदो मन्त्राक्षरमयः साक्षात्सूक्तमयो भृशम् ।
सूक्ते प्रतिष्ठितो ह्यात्मा सर्वेषामपि देहिनाम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Vedas are in the form of syllables of verses and hymns. The Self is established in the Sūkta, whomsoever it may belong to. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादात्मविदो नित्यं त्वं मा शप रुषान्वितः ।
शप्या न वेदाः केनापि दुर्द्धियाऽपि कदाचन ॥ ४५॥

English
Hence do not angrily curse the knowers of the Self. The Vedas shall not be cursed by anyone, not even by the evil-minded. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं शप्तो न चेदानीं तत्त्वतो बोद्धुमर्हसि ।
शान्तो भव महाधीमन् सनकादिविबोधकः ॥ ४६॥

English
I have not been cursed now. You please understand the factual position. O intelligent one, be calm, enlighten Sanaka and others. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्ञोऽहं यज्ञकर्माहं यज्ञाङ्गानि च सर्वशः ।
यज्ञात्मा यज्ञनिरतो यज्ञबाह्योऽहमेव वै ॥ ४७॥

English
I am the sacrifice, the sacrificial rite, the ancillary adjuncts of the sacrifice, the Self of sacrifice and one engrossed in sacrifice. I am out of sacrifice too. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
कोऽयं कस्त्वमिमे के हि सर्वोऽहमपि तत्त्वतः ।
इति बुद्ध्या हि विमृश वृथा शप्तास्त्वया द्विजाः ॥ ४८॥

English
Who is this? Who are you? Who are these? In reality I am all. Consider everything in this light. In vain did you curse the brahmins. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्त्वज्ञानेन निर्हृत्य प्रपञ्चरचनां भव ।
बुधःस्वस्थो महाबुद्धे नन्दिन् क्रोधादिवर्जितः ॥ ४९॥

English
Extracting the fundamental basis of the construction of the universe through the knowledge of reality, be enlightened and self-assured, O intelligent one. Be free from anger and other emotions”. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं प्रबोधितस्तेन शम्भुना नन्दिकेश्वरः ।
विवेकपरमो भूत्वा शान्तोऽभूत्क्रोधवर्जितः ॥ ५०॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Thus exhorted by Śiva, Nandikeśvara became calm and free from anger and took up discrimination as the ultimate aim. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि तं प्रबोध्याशु स्वगणं प्राणवल्लभम् ।
सगणः स ययौ तस्मात्स्वस्थानं प्रमुदान्वितः ॥ ५१॥

English
After enlightening him and also his favourite Gaṇas, Śiva returned to His abode accompanied by his Gaṇas with great delight. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षोऽपि स रुषाविष्टस्तैर्द्विजैः परिवारितः ।
स्वस्थानं च ययौ चित्ते शिवद्रोहपरायणः ॥ ५२॥

English
Seething with fury and malice against Śiva, Dakṣa went to his abode along with the brahmins. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रं तदानीं परिशप्यमानं
संस्मृत्य दक्षः परया रुषान्वितः ।
श्रद्धां विहायैव स मूढबुद्धि-
र्निन्दापरोऽभूच्छिवपूजकानाम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Remembering the situation in which Śiva had been cursed and still furious against him, Dakṣa of confounded intellect forsook his faith and entertained enmity and disgust against the worshippers of Śiva. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तो दक्षदुर्बुद्धिः शम्भुना परमात्मना ।
परां दुर्धिषणां तस्य श‍ृणु तात वदाम्यहम् ॥ ५४॥

English
Thus I have narrated the crooked intellect of Dakṣa in regard to Śiva the great Self. O dear one, hear about his evil intention and thought. I shall tell you further. (54.)
