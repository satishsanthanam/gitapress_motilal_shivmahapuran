### **CHAPTER TWENTYSEVEN - *(The inauguration of Dakṣa's sacrifice)***

---

Sanskrit
२७

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एकदा तु मुने तेन यज्ञः प्रारम्भितो महान् ।
तत्राहूतास्तदा सर्वे दीक्षितेन सुरर्षयः ॥ १॥

महर्षयोऽखिलास्तत्र निर्जराश्च समागताः ।
यद्यज्ञकरणार्थं हि शिवमायाविमोहिताः ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Once a great sacrifice was started by Dakṣa, O sage. To partake in that sacrifice, the celestial and terrestrial sages and devas were invited by Śiva and they reached the place being deluded by Śiva's Māyā. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
अगस्त्यः कश्यपोऽत्रिश्च वामदेवस्तथा भृगुः ।
दधीचिर्भगवान् व्यासो भारद्वाजोऽथ गौतमः ॥ ३॥

पैलः पराशरो गर्गो भार्गवः ककुभः सितः ।
सुमन्तुत्रिककङ्काश्च वैशम्पायन एव च ॥ ४॥

एते चान्ये च बहवो मुनयो हर्षिता ययुः ।
मम पुत्रस्य दक्षस्य सदाराः ससुता मखम् ॥ ५॥

English
Agastya, Kaśyapa, Atri, Vāmadeva, Bhṛgu, Dadhīci, the revered Vyāsa, Bharadvāja, Gautama, Paila, Parāśara, Garga, Bhārgava, Kakubha, Sita Sumantu, Trika, Kaṅka, Vaiśampāyana and many others along with their sons and wives arrived at the sacrifice of Dakṣa—my son. (3-5.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा सर्वे सुरगणा लोकपाला महोदयाः ।
तथोपनिर्जराः सर्वे स्वोपकारबलान्विताः ॥ ६॥

English
All the devas, the guardians of the quarters of rising fortune, the subordinate devas with their offers of help and service attended the sacrifice. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यलोकात्समानीतो नुतोऽहं विश्वकारकः ।
ससुतः सपरीवारो मूर्तवेदादिसंयुतः ॥ ७॥

English
From Satyalōka, I, the creator of the universe, was duly lauded and taken there along with my sons, followers and the embodied forms of the Vedas etc. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
वैकुण्ठाच्च तथा विष्णुः सम्प्रार्थ्य विविधादरात् ।
सपार्षदपरीवारः समानीतो मखं प्रति ॥ ८॥

English
Viṣṇu was duly requested, respected and brought to the place of sacrifice from Vaikuṇṭha$^{300}$ along with his aide-de-camp and followers. (8.)

[fn 300. Vaikuṇṭha, also called Vāibhra, is the abode of Viṣṇu variously described as situated on the eastern peak of Mount Meru or in the Northern ocean.]

---

Sanskrit
एवमन्ये समायाता दक्षयज्ञं विमोहिताः ।
सत्कृतास्तेन दक्षेण सर्वे ते हि दुरात्मना ॥ ९॥

English
Similarly others too, equally deluded, came to the sacrifice. Then Dakṣa who was ill disposed towards Śiva received them hospitably. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
भवनानि महार्हाणि सुप्रभाणि महान्ति च ।
त्वष्ट्रा कृतानि दिव्यानि तेभ्यो दत्तानि तेन वै ॥ १०॥

English
Large divine mansions of great value and brilliant lustre were erected by Tvaṣṭr$^{301}$ and assigned to them by Dakṣa. (10.)

[fn 301. Tvaṣṭr is identified with Viśvakarman, the divine architect. See Note No. 295 P 389.]


---

Sanskrit
तेषु सर्वेषु धिष्ण्येषु यथायोग्यं च संस्थिताः ।
सन्मानिता अराजंस्ते सकला विष्णुना मया ॥ ११॥

English
In all those places they stationed themselves in a befitting manner after being duly honoured. They shone along with Viṣṇu and me. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्तमाने महायज्ञे तीर्थे कनखले तदा ।
ऋत्विजश्च कृतास्तेन भृग्वाद्याश्च तपोधनाः ॥ १२॥

English
In that sacrifice that was being performed in that holy place of Kanakhala,$^{302}$ Bhṛgu and other sages were made Ṛtviks by him (Dakṣa). (12.)

[fn 302. Kanakhala is a sacred town, near Haradvāra, on the Ganges where Dakṣa performed the great sacrifice in which Sati burnt herself. The river Gaṅgā is held very sacred at Kanakhala.]


---

Sanskrit
अधिष्ठाता स्वयं विष्णुः सह सर्वमरुद्गणैः ।
अहं तत्राभवं ब्रह्मा त्रयीविधिनिदर्शकः ॥ १३॥

English
Viṣṇu himself was the presiding officer along with the Maruts. I was the Brahmā (a special officiating deity) the director and guide for Vedic rituals. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव सर्वे दिक्पाला द्वारपालाश्च रक्षकाः ।
सायुधाः सपरीवाराः कुतूहलकराः सदा ॥ १४॥

English
The guardians of the quarters became the gate-keepers and watchmen. They were well-equipped in arms and had many attendants to assist them. They were very enthusiastic. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
उपतस्थे स्वयं यज्ञः सुरूपस्तस्य चाध्वरे ।
सर्वे महामुनिश्रेष्ठाः स्वयं वेदधराभवन् ॥ १५॥

English
In that altar, sacrifice itself was present in its beautiful embodied form. The excellent sages became the holders of the Vedas. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तनूनपादपि निजं चक्रे रूपं सहस्रशः ।
हविषां ग्रहणायाशु तस्मिन् यज्ञे महोत्सवे ॥ १६॥

English
The sacrificial fire evinced its diverse forms in a thousand ways, during the sacrificial festivities, in order to receive the sacrificial offerings of Dakṣa. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टाशीतिसहस्राणि जुह्वति सह ऋत्विजः ।
उद्गातारश्चतुःषष्टिसहस्राणि सुरर्षयः ॥ १७॥

अध्वर्यवोऽथ होतारस्तावन्तो नारदादयः ।
सप्तर्षयः समा गाथाः कुर्वन्ति स्म पृथक्पृथक् ॥ १८॥

English
There were eighty-six thousand Ṛtviks$^{303}$ in the performance of the sacrifice and sixtyfour thousand Udgātrs. The celestial sages Nārada and others acted as Adhvaryus and Hotṛs. They too were as many. The seven sages$^{304}$ (jointly and) severally repeated the Sāman hymns. (17-18.)

[fn 303. The priests (Rṛvijas) participating in the Vedic sacrifices are usually four in number. They are (1) Hotṛ, Adhvaryu, Udgāṭṛ and Brahman corresponding to the four Vedas—Ṛg, Yajus, Sāman and Atharvan respectively. Each of the priests has three companions or helpers, the total no. is sixteen viz. Hotṛ—Maitrāvaruṇa, Acchāvāka, Grāvastuṭ; Adhvaryu—Pratiprasthāṭṛ, Neṣṭr, Unnetṛ; Udgāṭṛ—Prastotṛ, Pratiharṭṛ, Subrahmaṇya and Brahman—Brahmanācchamsin, Agnīḍl ra, Potṛ. See Āśvalāyana Śrauta Sūtra IV. 1. 4-6.]
---

Sanskrit
गन्धर्वविद्याधरसिद्धसङ्घा-
नादित्यसङ्घान् सगणान् सयज्ञान् ।
सङ्ख्यावतान्नागचरान् समस्तान्
वव्रे स दक्षो हि महाध्वरे स्वे ॥ १९॥

English
In his great sacrifice Dakṣa extended his invitation to Gandharvas, Vidyādharas, Siddhas, Ādityas, all the innumerable Nāgas along with their followers and sacrificial ritualists. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजर्षिराजर्षिसुरर्षिसङ्घा
नृपाः समित्राः सचिवाः ससैन्याः ।
वसुप्रमुख्या गणदेवताश्च
सर्वे वृतास्तेन मखोपवेत्त्रा ॥ २०॥

English
Brahminical, Royal and celestial sages, kings, with their friends, ministers, armies etc, Vasus$^{305}$ and other chief Gaṇadevatas—all of them were invited by him in the sacrifice. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दीक्षायुक्तस्तदा दक्षः कृतकौतुकमङ्गलः ।
भार्यया सहितो रेजे कृतस्वस्त्ययनो भृशम् ॥ २१॥

English
With the proper initiation, tying of the holy thread round his wrist and Svastyayana$^{306}$ rites duly performed, Dakṣa along with his wife, shone well. (21.)

[fn 306. A set of Vedic Mantras recited for causing prosperity and good fortune.]


---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन् यज्ञे वृतः शम्भुर्न दक्षेण दुरात्मना ।
कपालीति विनिश्चित्य तस्य यज्ञार्हता न हि ॥ २२॥

English
Dakṣa, the evil-minded, did not invite Śiva for that sacrifice, deciding that He was not worthy of taking part in the sacrifice because He was a Kapālin$^{307}$. (22.)

[fn 307. Śiva is called Kapālin for He bears skulls of men (Kapāla) as ornament.]

---

Sanskrit
कपालिभार्येति सती दयिता स्वसुतापि च ।
नाहूता यज्ञविषये दक्षेणागुणदर्शिना ॥ २३॥

English
In view of the fact that Satī was the wife of Kapālin, she was not invited, though she was his beloved daughter, by Dakṣa who was blind to her qualities. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं प्रवर्तमाने हि दक्षयज्ञे महोत्सवे ।
स्वकार्यलग्नास्तत्रासन् सर्वे तेऽध्वरसम्मताः ॥ २४॥

English
While the great festivities in the sacrifice of Dakṣa were being celebrated those who had assembled for the same were engrossed in their respective activities. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरेऽदृष्ट्वा तत्र वै शङ्करं प्रभुम् ।
प्रोद्विग्नमानसः शैवो दधीचो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ २५॥

English
In the meantime, Dadhīci$^{308}$ a devotee of Śiva, realising that lord Śiva was not there became dispirited and spoke thus. (25.)

[fn 308 Dadhīci. Compare Mbh. XII, 20283, where he blames Dakṣa.]
---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
सर्वे श‍ृणुत मद्वाक्यं देवर्षिप्रमुखा मुदा ।
कस्मान्नैवागतः शम्भुरस्मिन् यज्ञे महोत्सवे ॥ २६॥

English
*Dadhīci said :—* O ye all ! celestial sages and others, pay heed to my words. Why has not Śiva taken part in the festivities of this sacrifice ? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एते सुरेशा मुनयो महत्तराः
सलोकपालाश्च समागता हि ।
तथापि यज्ञस्तु न शोभते भृशं
पिनाकिना तेन महात्मना विना ॥ २७॥

English
Of course, the chiefs of devas, the great sages and the guardians of the quarters have all come. Yet the sacrifice cannot be perfect and complete without the noble-souled, trident-holder Śiva. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
येनैव सर्वाण्यपि मङ्गलानि
भवन्ति शंसन्ति महाविपश्चितः ।
सोऽसौ न दृष्टोऽत्र पुमान् पुराणो
वृषध्वजो नीलगलः परेशः ॥ २८॥

English
The bull-bannered, blue-necked eternal Puruṣa the great Īśa is not seen here. Great scholars have affirmed, that all auspicious results happen due to Him alone. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अमङ्गलान्येव च मङ्गलानि
भवन्ति येनाधिगतानि दक्ष ।
त्रिपञ्चकेनाप्यथ मङ्गलानि
भवन्ति सद्यः परतः पराणि ॥ २९॥

English
O Dakṣa, if accepted by Triyambaka all inauspicious things become auspicious and fresh auspicious things take shape which will be greater than the greatest in a trice. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वयैव कर्तव्यमाह्वानं परमेशितुः ।
त्वरितं ब्रह्मणा वापि विष्णुना प्रभुविष्णुना ॥ ३०॥

English
Hence the invitation to the great Śiva shall be extended by you yourself immediately or by Brahmā or by Viṣṇu the lord. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रेण लोकपालैश्च द्विजैः सिद्धैः सहाधुना ।
सर्वथानयनीयोऽसौ शङ्करो यज्ञपूर्तये ॥ ३१॥

English
By all means Śiva shall be brought here by you along with Indra, the guardians of the quarters, the brahmins and the Siddhas in order to make the sacrifice complete and perfect. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वैर्भवद्भिर्गन्तव्यं यत्र देवो महेश्वरः ।
दाक्षायण्या समं शम्भुमानयध्वं त्वरान्विताः ॥ ३२॥

English
All of you shall go where He is stationed. Immediately bring Śiva along with Satī. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

अत्रागतेन देवेशाः साम्बेन परमात्मना ॥ ३३॥

English
O lords of Devas, everything shall be sanctified through Śiva, the supreme Self. If the consort of Śivā, the great Being, comes here, everything will be all right. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य स्मृत्या च नामोक्त्या समग्रं सुकृतं भवेत् ।
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन ह्यानेतव्यो वृषध्वजः ॥ ३४॥

English
Since all merits accrue from thinking upon Him and repeating His names, the bull-bannered deity shall be brought with all efforts. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

भविष्यत्यन्यथापूर्णः सत्यमेतद् ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ३५॥

English
If Śiva comes here, the sacrifice will become sanctified, or it will remain incomplete and imperfect. I am telling you the truth. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

उवाच त्वरितं मूढः प्रहसन्निव दुष्टधीः ॥ ३६॥

English

Sanskrit

English

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

English

English

English

निर्ययुः स्वाश्रमान् सद्यः शापं दत्त्वा तथैव च ॥ ४९॥

English
Then some important devotees of Śiva who were initiated in the Śaiva cult cursed Dakṣa and returned to their respective abodes. (49.)

Sanskrit
मुनौ विनिर्गते तस्मिन् मखादन्येषु दुष्टधीः ।
शिवद्रोही मुनीन् दक्षः प्रहसन्निदमब्रवीत् ॥ ५०॥

English
When the sage Dadhīci and others staged a walk-out, the evil-minded Dakṣa, inimical to Śiva, said mocking at them. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
गतः शिवप्रियो विप्रो दधीचो नाम नामतः ।
अन्ये तथाविधा ये च गतास्ते मम चाध्वरात् ॥ ५१॥

English
*Dakṣa said:—*  Gone is that brahmin favourite of Śiva named Dadhīci and others too of his ilk have gone out of my sacrifice. (51.)

Sanskrit
एतच्छुभतरं जातं सम्मतं मे हि सर्वथा ।
सत्यं ब्रवीमि देवेश सुराश्च मुनयस्तथा ॥ ५२॥

English
This has become good. I approve of this always. O Indra, devas and sages, I am telling you the truth. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
विनष्टचित्ता मन्दाश्च मिथ्यावादरताः खलाः ।
वेदबाह्या दुराचाराः त्याज्यास्ते मखकर्मणि ॥ ५३॥

English
They are slow-witted and senseless. They are rogues indulging in false deliberations and discussions. They are out of the Vedic circle. These men of evil conduct shall be eschewed from sacrificial rites. (53.)

Sanskrit
वेदवादरता यूयं सर्वे विष्णुपुरोगमाः ।
यज्ञं मे सफलं विप्राः सुराः कुर्वन्तु माचिरम् ॥ ५४॥

English
You all, brahmins, sages and devas with Viṣṇu at the head shall make my sacrifice fruitful. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य शिवमायाविमोहिताः ।
तन्मखे देवयजनं चक्रुः सर्वे सुरर्षयः ॥ ५५॥

इति तन्मखशापो हि वर्णितो मे मुनीश्वर ।
यज्ञविध्वंसयोगोऽपि प्रोच्यते श‍ृणु सादरम् ॥ ५६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words, the celestial sages deluded by Śiva’s Māyā performed the worship of the deities in that sacrifice. O great sage, I have thus explained how the sacrifice had been cursed. Now I shall explain how the sacrifice was destroyed. (55-56.)
