### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - *(Sati’s Journey)***

---

Sanskrit
२८

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
यदा ययुर्दक्षमखमुत्सवेन सुरर्षयः ।
तस्मिन्नेवान्तरे देवी पर्वते गन्धमादने ॥ १॥

धारागृहे वितानेन सखीभिः परिवारिता ।
दाक्षायणी महाक्रीडाश्चकार विविधाः सती ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  In the meantime when the celestial sages were on their way to Dakṣa’s sacrifice, with great eclat Sati the daughter of Dakṣa was engrossed in diverse sports, surrounded by her friends under the canopy of the fountain house on the mountain Gandhamādana$^{309}$. (1-2.)

[fn 303. The location of the Gandhamādana is highly controversial. According to the Paurāṇic account Gandhamādana is a mountain that forms the division between Ilāvṛta and Bhadrāśva to the east of Meru and is renowned for its fragrant forests.]

---

Sanskrit
क्रीडासक्ता तदा देवी ददर्शाथ मुदा सती ।
दक्षयज्ञे प्रयान्तीं तां रोहिण्यापृच्छ्य सत्वरम् ॥ ३॥

दृष्ट्वा सीमन्तया भूतां विजयां प्राह सा सती ।
स्वसखीं प्रवरां प्राणप्रियां सा हि हितावहाम् ॥ ४॥

English
While she was thus gaily sportive$^{310}$, Sati saw the moon in the company of Rohiṇī going to the sacrifice of Dakṣa. Sati asked Vijayā her maiden-in-chief, her beloved friend, wishing her all welfare. (3-4.)

[fn 310. Rohiṇī, according to Paurāṇic Mythology, was the daughter of Dakṣa and the favourite wife of the moon.]
---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
हे सखीप्रवरे प्राणप्रिये त्वं विजये मम ।
क्व गमिष्यति चन्द्रोऽयं रोहिण्या पृच्छ सत्वरम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Sati said :—*  O beloved friend Vijayā, where does this moon go now in a hurry in the company of Rohiṇī after taking leave of us? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तथोक्ता विजया सत्या गत्वा तत्सन्निधौ द्रुतम् ।
क्व गच्छसीति पप्रच्छ शशिनं तं यथोचितम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  When Satī thus asked her, Vijayā went near the moon and asked him “Where are you going ?” (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विजयोक्तमथाकर्ण्य स्वयात्रां पूर्वमादरात् ।
कथितं तेन तत्सर्वं दक्षयज्ञोत्सवादिकम् ॥ ७॥

English
On hearing what Vijayā said, the moon mentioned everything about the sacrificial festival of Dakṣa, with great respect. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा विजया देवीं त्वरिता जातसम्भ्रमा ।
कथयामास तत्सर्वं यदुक्तं शशिना सतीम् ॥ ८॥

English
On hearing what the moon told her, Vijayā was greatly agitated and mentioned it immediately to goddess Satī. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा कालिका देवी विस्मिताभूत्सती तदा ।
विमृश्य कारणं तत्राज्ञात्वा चेतस्यचिन्तयत् ॥ ९॥

English
On hearing it, Satī, the goddess Kālikā, was surprised. She thought over the possible reason, but not knowing it she mused like this. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षः पिता मे माता च वीरिणी नौ कुतः सती ।
आह्वानं न करोति स्म विस्मृता मां प्रियां सुताम् ॥ १०॥

English
“Dakṣa is my father. Vīriṇī is my mother. I am their beloved daughter Satī. Why did they not invite me ? Have they forgotten their own beloved daughter ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पृच्छेयं शङ्करं तत्र कारणं सर्वमादरात् ।
चिन्तयित्वेति सासीद्वै तत्र गन्तुं सुनिश्चया ॥ ११॥

English
I shall ask Śiva respectfully the reason for the same”. Thinking thus she decided to go to Him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दाक्षायणी देवी विजयां प्रवरां सखीम् ।
स्थापयित्वा द्रुतं तत्र समगच्छच्छिवान्तिकम् ॥ १२॥

English
Making her maiden-in-chief Vijayā wait there, Satī immediately went near Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ददर्श तं सभामध्ये संस्थितं बहुभिर्गणैः ।
नन्द्यादिभिर्महावीरैः प्रवरैर्यूथयूथपैः ॥ १३॥

English
She saw Him in the middle of the council-chamber surrounded by hosts of his attendants—Nandin and others of great valour. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तं प्रभुमीशानं स्वपतिं साथ दक्षजा ।
प्रष्टुं तत्कारणं शीघ्रं प्राप शङ्करसन्निधिम् ॥ १४॥

English
After seeing her husband, the lord Śiva, the daughter of Dakṣa came near Him quickly in order to ask Him the reason. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेन स्थापिता स्वाङ्के प्रीतियुक्तेन स्वप्रिया ।
प्रमोदिता वचोभिः सा बहुमानपुरःसरम् ॥ १५॥

English
Lovingly Śiva took his beloved on his lap and delighted her with pleasing words. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुर्महालीलः सर्वेशः सुखदः सताम् ।
सतीमुवाच त्वरितं गणमध्यस्थ आदरात् ॥ १६॥

English
Then Śiva, the lord of all, the bestower of happiness on the good, seated in the midst of his attendants told Satī indulging (as usual) in his great divine sports. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरुवाच ।
किमर्थमागतात्र त्वं सभामध्ये सविस्मया ।
कारणं तस्य सुप्रीत्या शीघ्रं वद सुमध्यमे ॥ १७॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  “O slender-waisted lady, why did you come here in the council-chamber and that too in a state of surprise ? Please tell me the reason.” (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्ता तदा तेन महेशेन मुनीश्वर ।
साञ्जलिः सुप्रणम्याशु सत्युवाच प्रभुं शिवा ॥ १८॥

English
*Brahmā continues the story—*  O great sages, Satī on thus being addressed by Śiva, bowed to the lord with palms joined in reverence and said:— (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
पितुर्मम महान् यज्ञो भवतीति मया श्रुतम् ।
तत्रोत्सवो महानस्ति समवेताः सुरर्षयः ॥ १९॥

English
*Satī said :—*  I have heard that my father is performing a great sacrifice. Great festivities are being conducted there. The celestial sages have assembled too. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुर्मम महायज्ञे कस्मात्तव न रोचते ।
गमनं देवदेवेश तत्सर्वं कथय प्रभो ॥ २०॥

English
O lord of devas, how is it that a visit to my father's great sacrifice does not appeal to you? Please explain to me fully, O lord. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सुहृदामेष वै धर्मः सुहृद्भिः सह सङ्गतिः ।
कुर्वन्ति यन्महादेव सुहृदः प्रीतिवर्धिनीम् ॥ २१॥

English
This is the duty of friends that they shall frequently associate with their friends. O great lord, friends always do what increases the pleasures of their friends. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन मया गच्छ सह प्रभो ।
यज्ञवाटं पितुर्मेऽद्य स्वामिन् प्रार्थनया मम ॥ २२॥

English
O lord, please come to my father's Sacrificial Hall along with me. O lord, let it be at my request. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तस्यास्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा सत्या देवो महेश्वरः ।
दक्षवागिषुहृद्विद्धो बभाषे सूनृतं वचः ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Satī, lord Śiva, wounded in the heart by the words of Dakṣa piercing like a dart, spoke these courteous and pleasing words. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
दक्षस्तव पिता देवि मम द्रोही विशेषतः ॥ २४॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  Dakṣa is very well your father, dear. But he is my particular enemy. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य ये मानिनः सर्वे ससुरर्षिमुखाः परे ।
ते मूढा यजनं प्राप्ताः पितुस्ते ज्ञानवर्जिताः ॥ २५॥

English
But the celestial sages who usually honour me have become confused now. Being devoid of true knowledge they are attending the sacrifice of your father. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अनाहूताश्च ये देवि गच्छन्ति परमन्दिरम् ।
अवमानं प्राप्नुवन्ति मरणादधिकं तथा ॥ २६॥

English
O gentle lady, those who go to another man's house without being invited attain disrespect which is more serious than even death. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
परालयं गतोऽपीन्द्रो लघुर्भवति तद्विधः ।
का कथा च परेषां वै रीढा यात्रा हि तद्विधा ॥ २७॥

English
Even the prosperous Indra and people like him going to another man's house in such a context become worthless. What then about others? A journey of such a nature is futile. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वया मया चापि दक्षस्य यजनं प्रति ।
न गन्तव्यं विशेषेण सत्यमुक्तं मया प्रिये ॥ २८॥

English
Hence you and I particularly shall not go to Dakṣa's sacrifice. O beloved, I have told you the truth. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तथारिभिर्न व्यथते ह्यर्दितोऽपि शरैर्जनः ।
स्वानां दुरुक्तिभिर्मर्मताडितः स यथा मतः ॥ २९॥

English
People wounded with arrows by enemies are not so pained as when their vulnerable points are hit by the taunting words of kinsmen. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्यादिभिर्गुणैः षड्भिरसदन्यैः सतां स्मृतौ ।
हतायां भूयसां धाम न पश्यन्ति खलाः प्रिये ॥ ३०॥

English
O beloved, the wicked people do not observe that their own status is being hit when they attack good men endowed with the six qualities of learning.” (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्ता सती तेन महेशेन महात्मना ।
उवाच रोषसंयुक्ता शिवं वाक्यविदां वरम् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus advised by the noble-souled Śiva, Satī was angry and spoke thus to Śiva, the foremost of fluent speakers. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
यज्ञःस्यात्सफलो येन स त्वं शम्भोऽखिलेश्वर ।
अनाहूतोऽसि तेनाद्य पित्रा मे दुष्टकारिणा ॥ ३२॥

English
*Satī said :—*  O Śiva, lord of all, you by whom sacrifice becomes fruitful have not been invited by my father, thus he has committed a foul deed. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सर्वं ज्ञातुमिच्छामि भव भावं दुरात्मनः ।
सुरर्षीणां च सर्वेषामागतानां दुरात्मनाम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Hence, O Śiva, I wish to know the trend of thought of that evil-minded person as well as that of the celestial sages and all other wicked persons assembled there. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्चाद्यैव गच्छामि स्वपितुर्यजनं प्रभो ।
अनुज्ञां देहि मे नाथ तत्र गन्तुं महेश्वर ॥ ३४॥

English
Hence O lord, I wish to go to the sacrifice of my father. O lord Śiva, please grant me permission to go there. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्तो भगवान् रुद्रस्तया देव्या शिवः स्वयम् ।
विज्ञाताखिलदृक् द्रष्टा सतीं सूतिकरोऽब्रवीत् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Lord Śiva, possessed of the perfect vision, realising everything and seeing all, and the cause of protection, being requested by the Goddess, spoke to her. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
यद्येवं ते रुचिर्देवि तत्र गन्तुमवश्यकम् ।
सुव्रते वचनान्मे त्वं गच्छ शीघ्रं पितुर्मखम् ॥ ३६॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  “O goddess, if this is what you wish, if you think it needful to go, O righteous one, you can immediately start for your father’s sacrifice with my willing permission. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एतं नन्दिनमारुह्य वृषभं सज्जमादरात् ।
महाराजोपचाराणि कृत्वा बहुगुणान्विता ॥ ३७॥

English
You can go in royal splendour mounting this bull richly caparisoned.” (37.)

---

Sanskrit
भूषितं वृषमारोहेत्युक्ता रुद्रेण सा सती ।
सुभूषिता सती युक्ता ह्यगमत्पितृमन्दिरम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Satī thus commanded to mount the decorated bull, bedecked herself and started for her father’s abode. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
महाराजोपचाराणि दत्तानि परमात्मना ।
सुच्छत्रचामरादीनि सद्वस्त्राभरणानि च ॥ ३९॥

English
The royal paraphernalia like the umbrella, chowries, silken clothes and ornaments were given to her by (Śiva) the great lord. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
गणाः षष्टिसहस्राणि रौद्रा जग्मुः शिवाज्ञया ।
कुतूहलयुताः प्रीता महोत्सवसमन्विताः ॥ ४०॥

English
Sixty thousand of the attendants of Śiva lovingly and enthusiastically went with her with great festivities. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोत्सवो महानासीद्यजने तत्र सर्वतः ।
सत्याः शिवप्रियायास्तु वामदेवगणैः कृतः ॥ ४१॥

English
The festivities performed by the attendants of Śiva to Satī, Śiva's beloved, were indeed very great. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
कुतूहलं गणाश्चक्रुः शिवयोर्यश उज्जगुः ।
बलात्ते पुप्लुवुः प्रीत्या महावीराः शिवप्रियाः ॥ ४२॥

सर्वथासीन्महाशोभा गमने जगदम्बिके ।
सुखारावः सम्बभूव पूरितं भुवनत्रयम् ॥ ४३॥

English
The heroic attendants, favourites of Śiva sang songs of praise of Śiva and Śivā and jumped in their joy with hearts of childish innocence. In every respect the departure of the mother of the universe was very glorious. The three worlds became filled with pleasing sounds. (42-43.)
