### **CHAPTER TWENTYNINE - *(Satī's statement)***

---

Sanskrit
२९

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
दाक्षायणी गता तत्र यत्र यज्ञो महाप्रभः ।
सुरासुरमुनीन्द्रादिकुतूहलसमन्वितः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Satī reached the place where the colourful sacrifice accompanied by the enthusiasm of devas, asuras, great sages etc. was in progress. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपितुर्भवनं तत्र नानाश्चर्यसमन्वितम् ।
ददर्श सुप्रभं चारु सुरर्षिगणसंयुतम् ॥ २॥

English
She saw her father's mansion abounding in wondrous things lustrous and beautiful as well as the groups of devas and sages. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारि स्थिता तदा देवी ह्यवरुह्य निजासनात् ।
नन्दिनोऽभ्यन्तरं शीघ्रमेकैवागच्छदध्वरम् ॥ ३॥

English
The Goddess stopped at the gate and descended from Nandin, the bull. She went all alone inside the place of sacrifice. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतां च सतीं दृष्ट्वासिक्नी माता यशस्विनी ।
अकरोदादरं तस्या भगिन्यश्च यथोचितम् ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing Satī, her glorious mother Asiknī and her sisters received her respectfully. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
नाकरोदादरं दक्षो दृष्ट्वा तामपि किञ्चन ।
नान्योऽपि तद्भयात्तत्र शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ ५॥

English
Even after seeing her, Dakṣa did not show any sign or gesture of love or respect. The others too deluded by Śiva's Māyā did not receive her out of fear of Dakṣa. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा मातरं देवी पितरं च सती मुने ।
अनमद्विस्मितात्यन्तं सर्वलोकपराभवात् ॥ ६॥

English
Satī then bowed to her parents, O sage. In her surprise (at the cool reception) she gazed at every one. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
भागानपश्यद्देवानां हर्यादीनां तदध्वरे ।
न शम्भुभागमकरोत् क्रोधं दुर्विषहं सती ॥ ७॥

English
In that sacrifice, Satī saw the shares allotted to the deities, Viṣṇu, and others but not to Śiva. She then fell into a great fury. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा दक्षं दहन्तीव रुषा पूर्णा सती भृशम् ।
क्रूरदृष्ट्या विलोक्यैव सर्वानप्यपमानिता ॥ ८॥

English
Slighted thus and hence very furious at everyone she directed her burning fiery look at Dakṣa and every one present there. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
अनाहूतस्त्वया कस्माच्छम्भुः परमशोभनः ।
येन पूतमिदं विश्वं समग्रं सचराचरम् ॥ ९॥

English
*Satī said :—*  How is it that Śiva who is highly auspicious and by whom the entire universe of the mobile and the immobile is sanctified, has not been invited by you? (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्ञो यज्ञविदां श्रेष्ठो यज्ञाङ्गो यज्ञदक्षिणः ।
यज्ञकर्ता च यः शम्भुस्तं विना च कथं मखः ॥ १०॥

English
What is that sacrifice without Śiva who is sacrifice Himself, the performer of sacrifice, the fee of sacrifice, the adjunct of sacrifice and the foremost of those who know sacrifice itself. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य स्मरणमात्रेण सर्वं पूतं भवत्यहो ।
विना तेन कृतं सर्वमपवित्रं भविष्यति ॥ ११॥

English
Every rite performed without Him will be impure but with Him or by the mere remembrance of Him becomes pure. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
द्रव्यमन्त्रादिकं सर्वं हव्यं कव्यं च यन्मयम् ।
शम्भुना हि विना तेन कथं यज्ञः प्रवर्तितः ॥ १२॥

English
The articles of offerings, the mantra, the Havya and Kavya$^{311}$, everything is identical with Śiva. How is it that the sacrifice is being performed without Him? (12.)

[fn 311. Havya-Kavya : Oblations both to the Gods and the spirits of the deceased ancestors.]

---

Sanskrit
किं शिवं सुरसामान्यं मत्वाकार्षीरनादरम् ।
भ्रष्टबुद्धिर्भवानद्य जातोऽसि जनकाधम ॥ १३॥

English
Did you disrespect Him, considering Him on a par with ordinary devas? You have become senseless and mean though you are my father. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुब्रह्मादयो देवा यं संसेव्य महेश्वरम् ।
प्राप्ताः स्वपदवीं सर्वे तं न जानासि रे हरम् ॥ १४॥

English
Ha, you do not know Śiva the great lord by serving whom Viṣṇu Brahmā and other devas have attained their position and status. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एते कथं समायाता विष्णुब्रह्मादयः सुराः ।
तव यज्ञे विना शम्भुं स्वप्रभुं मुनयस्तथा ॥ १५॥

English
How did Viṣṇu, Brahmā, other devas and the sages happen to be present at your sacrifice without their lord Śiva? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा परमेशानी विष्ण्वादीन्सकलान् प्रति ।
पृथक्पृथगवोचत्सा भर्त्सयन्ती भवात्मिका ॥ १६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* After saying this, Satī addressed Viṣṇu and others severally, taunting them. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्युवाच ।
हे विष्णो त्वं महादेवं किं न जानासि तत्त्वतः ।
सगुणं निर्गुणं चापि श्रुतयो यं वदन्ति ह ॥ १७॥

English
*Satī said :—*  “O Viṣṇu, don’t you know the real nature of Śiva whom the Vedas speak of as both full or devoid of attributes? (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यपि त्वां करं दत्त्वा बहुवारं महेश्वरः ।
अशिक्षयत्पुरा शाल्वप्रमुखाकृतिभिर्हरे ॥ १८॥

तदपि ज्ञानमायातं न ते चेतसि दुर्मते ।
भागार्थी दक्षयज्ञेऽस्मिन् शिवं स्वस्वामिनं विना ॥ १९॥

English
Although as the chieftain of king Śālva,$^{312}$ Śiva had caught hold of your hand and set you aright many a time, that admonition has not entered your brain, now that you have evinced a desire to partake of your share in Dakṣa’s sacrifice without inviting lord Śiva. (18-19.)

[fn 312. Śālva, the king of the country of Śālas (modern Rājasthan) was inimical to Viṣṇu.]
---

Sanskrit
पुरा पञ्चमुखो भूत्वा गर्वितोऽसि सदाशिवम् ।
कृतश्चतुर्मुखस्तेन विस्मृतोऽसि तदद्भुतम् ॥ २०॥

English
O Brahmā, you had five faces formerly. When you exhibited your haughtiness against Śiva, He made you four-faced. It is surprising that you have forgotten it. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्र त्वं किं न जानासि महादेवस्य विक्रमम् ।
भस्मीकृतः पविस्ते हि हरेण क्रूरकर्मणा ॥ २१॥

English
O Indra, don't you know the valour of the great lord? Śiva had once ruthlessly reduced your thunderbolt to ashes. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
हे सुराः किन्न जानीथ महादेवस्य विक्रमम् ।
अत्रे वसिष्ठ मुनयो युष्माभिः किं कृतं त्विह ॥ २२॥

English
O devas, don't you know the valour of Mahādeva, O Atri, O Vasiṣṭha, O, sages what have you all done here? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
भिक्षाटनं च कृतवान् पुरा दारुवने विभुः ।
शप्तो यद्भिक्षुको रुद्रो भवद्भिर्मुनिभिस्तदा ॥ २३॥

शप्तेनापि च रुद्रेण यत्कृतं विस्मृतं कथम् ।
तल्लिङ्गेनाखिलं दग्धं भुवनं सचराचरम् ॥ २४॥

English
Once the lord wandered (begging for alms) in Dāruvana$^{314}$. You, sages, cursed him in the guise of a mendicant. How is it that you have now forgotten what Śiva did on being cursed by you? The entire universe of the mobile and immobile was burnt by His Liṅga. (23-24.)

[fn 314. Dāruvana or Dārukāvana which contains the temple of Nāgeśa, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Śiva has been identified with Aundh in the Nizām's territory (Arch. Sur. Lists of Nizām's Territory XXXI. 21, 29). Another vana of the same name also stands at the following places: (1) In the Himālayas near Badrinath (Mbh. XIII. 25. 27) (2) Near Vijayesvara in Kāśmīr (H.C. 10.3). Due to these variations it is not possible to ascertain the exact locality of Dāruvana in the present context.]

Sanskrit

English

---

English

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Knowing Śiva to be of indecent dress and features, my dear daughter I did not invite him to the sacrifice in the presence of gods and sages. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Induced by Brahmā I gave you in marriage to the wicked haughty Śiva who does not know customs. I have been a sinner and slow-witted. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
अवकीर्य जटा भूतैः श्मशाने स कपालधृक् ।
तन्माल्यभस्म वा ज्ञात्वा प्रीत्यावसदुदारधीः ॥ ५२॥

ये मूर्द्धभिर्दधति तच्चरणोत्सृष्टमादरात् ।
निर्माल्यं मुनयो देवाः स शिवः परमेश्वरः ॥ ५३॥

English
Śiva who holds the skull in his hands resides in the cremation ground in the company of goblins. He wears matted hair. But sages and devas keep on their heads the dust from His feet. Such is the nature of lord Śiva, the great God. (52-53.)

In the Vedas two sorts of actions are ordained—direct and renunciatory. Scholars differentiate between these two and hold that they cannot be simultaneous and they cannot occur in a single entity. But in Śiva the great Brahman, these actions do not have any effect. (54-55.)
