### **CHAPTER THIRTY - (Description of Satī's casting off of her body and the subsequent disorder)**

---

Sanskrit
३०

नारद उवाच ।
मौनीभूता यदा सासीत्सती शङ्करवल्लभा ।
चरित्रं किमभूत्तत्र विधे तद्वद चादरात् ॥ १॥

English
Nārada said :—  When Satī, Śiva's beloved became silent what happened there ? O Brahmā please tell me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
मौनीभूता सती देवी स्मृत्वा स्वपतिमादरात् ।
क्षितावुदीच्यां सहसा निषसाद प्रशान्तधीः ॥ २॥

English
Brahmā said:-  Observing silence and remembering her lord with great respect, Satī the Goddess calmed down and sat on the ground in the northern wing. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
जलमाचम्य विधिवत् संवृता वाससा शुचिः ।
दृङ् निमील्य पतिं स्मृत्वा योगमार्गं समाविशत् ॥ ३॥

English
Having sipped water duly, covering up her body entirely with her cloth she closed her eyes and remembered her lord. She then entered the yogic trance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा समानावनिलौ प्राणापानौ सितानना ।
उत्थाप्योदानमथ च यत्नात्सा नाभिचक्रतः ॥ ४॥

हृदि स्थाप्योरसि धिया स्थितं कण्ठाद् भ्रुवोः सती ।
अनिन्दितानयन्मध्यं शङ्करप्राणवल्लभा ॥ ५॥

English
Keeping her face steady she balanced the winds Prāṇa and Apāna<sup>316</sup>. She then lifted up the wind Udāna from the umbilical region, stabilised it in the cardiac region took it through the throat and finally fixed it in the middle of the eyebrows. (4-5.)
[fn 316. The system of Yoga enjoins the control of vital airs viz. प्राण and अपान in some particular position.]
---

Sanskrit
एवं स्वदेहं सहसा दक्षकोपाज्जिहासती ।
दग्धे गात्रे वायुशुचिर्धारितं योगमार्गतः ॥ ६॥

ततः स्वभर्तुश्चरणं चिन्तयन्ती न चापरम् ।
अपश्यत्सा सती तत्र योगमार्गनिविष्टधीः ॥ ७॥

English
She desired to cast-off her body due to her anger with Dakṣa. She desired to burn off the body and retain the pure wind by yogic means. In this posture she remembered the feet of her lord and nothing else. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
हतकल्मषतद्देहः प्रापतच्च तदग्निना ।
भस्मसादभवत्सद्यो मुनिश्रेष्ठ तदिच्छया ॥ ८॥

English
Her body divested of its sins fell in the yogic fire and was reduced to ashes, O excellent sage, in accordance with her own wish. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पश्यतां च खे भूमौ वादोऽभूत्सुमहांस्तदा ।
हाहेति सोऽद्भुतश्चित्रः सुरादीनां भयावहः ॥ ९॥

English
The loud shouts and cries of "Hā, Hā" of those who witnessed it spread everywhere on the earth and rose up in the sky. Everything was surprisingly wonderful and terrifying to the devas and others. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
हन्त प्रिया परा शम्भोर्देवी दैवतमस्य हि ।
जहावसून् सती केन सुदुष्टेन प्रकोपिता ॥ १०॥

English
"Alas, Śiva's beloved Goddess, nay his deity, Satī has cast-off her life. Who is that wicked person who angered her ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो त्वनात्म्यं सुमहदस्य दक्षस्य पश्यत ।
चराचरं प्रजा यस्य यत्पुत्रस्य प्रजापतेः ॥ ११॥

English
See the unholy unspiritual misdeed of Dakṣa the patriarch, the son of Brahmā whose subjects are the mobile and immobile creatures of the world. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अहोऽद्य विमनाभूत्सा सती देवी मनस्विनी ।
वृषध्वजप्रियाभीक्ष्णं मानयोग्या सतां सदा ॥ १२॥

English
Alas, Satī, the noble beloved of the bull-bannered deity has become dispirited. She ought to have been honoured duly. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽयं दुर्मर्षहृदयो ब्रह्मध्रुक् स प्रजापतिः ।
महतीमपकीर्तिं हि प्राप्स्यति त्वखिले भवे ॥ १३॥

English
This patriarch of hardened heart, inimical to the Brahman, will definitely become infamous in the whole world. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्स्वाङ्गजां सुतां शम्भुद्विट् न्यषेधत्समुद्यताम् ।
महानरकभोगी स मृतये नोऽपराधतः ॥ १४॥

English
Since he refused to comply with the request of his own daughter he will be falling into a terrible hell after death due to his own guilt. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वदत्येवं जने सत्या दृष्ट्वासुत्यागमद्भुतम् ।
द्रुतं तत्पार्षदाः क्रोधादुदतिष्ठन्नुदायुधाः ॥ १५॥

English
When people were saying thus on seeing the self-immolation of Satī, her attendants rose up in anger with their weapons. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारि स्थिता गणाः सर्वे रसायुतमिता रुषा ।
शङ्करस्य प्रभावात्तेऽक्रुध्यन्नतिमहाबलाः ॥ १६॥

English
They had been waiting near the door numbering sixty thousand. Those powerful attendants of lord Śiva became furious. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारमकुर्वंस्ते धिग् धिग् नो नेति वादिनः ।
उच्चैः सर्वेऽसकृद्वीराः शङ्करस्य गणाधिपाः ॥ १७॥

English
Those attendants of Śiva shouted exclamations—Hā Hā, fie, fie, no, no, loudly and frequently. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारेण महता व्याप्तमासीद्दिगन्तरम् ।
सर्वे प्रापन् भयं देवा मुनयोऽन्येऽपि ते स्थिताः ॥ १८॥

English
The quarters were pervaded with the shouts of Hā, Hā. The devas and sages who had assembled there were struck with fear. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
गणाः सम्मन्त्र्य ते सर्वेऽभूवन् क्रुद्धा उदायुधाः ।
कुर्वन्तः प्रलयं वाद्यैः शस्त्रैर्व्याप्तं दिगन्तरम् ॥ १९॥

English
Consulting one another, the attendants lifted their weapons furiously and the atmosphere was pervaded with the sound of their arms. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शस्त्रैरघ्नन्निजाङ्गानि केचित्तत्र शुचाकुलाः ।
शिरोमुखानि देवर्षे सुतीक्ष्णैः प्राणनाशिभिः ॥ २०॥

English
O celestial sage, some of them excessively stricken with grief cut off their limbs with their weapons, some their heads, some their faces, with the sharp lethal weapons they had. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं ते विलयं प्राप्ता दाक्षायण्याः समं तदा ।
गणायुते द्वे च तदा तदद्भुतमिवाभवत् ॥ २१॥

English
Thus about twentythousand of those attendants courted death along with Satī. It was very surprising. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
गणा नाशावशिष्टा ये शङ्करस्य महात्मनः ।
दक्षं तं क्रोधितं हन्तुमुदतिष्ठन्नुदायुधाः ॥ २२॥

English
Such of the attendants of the noble-souled Śiva who survived, jumped up with their weapons to kill the furious Dakṣa. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषामापततां वेगं निशम्य भगवान् भृगुः ।
यज्ञघ्नघ्नेन यजुषा दक्षिणाग्नौ जुहोन्मुने ॥ २३॥

English
On seeing the force of their onslaught, O sages, the holy sage Bhrgu poured offering in the Dakṣiṇa fire with the Yajur mantra to quell the obstructors of sacrifice. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
हूयमाने च भृगुणा समुत्पेतुर्महासुराः ।
ऋभवो नाम प्रबला वीरास्तत्र सहस्रशः ॥ २४॥

English
While the sage Bhrgu$^{317}$ was pouring the offerings, thousands of powerful demons—Rbhus<sup>318</sup> rose up. (24.)

[fn 318. Rbhus : They are the sons of Sudhanvan, a descendant of Angiras, severally named Rbhu, Vibhu and Vāja. Through their assiduous performance of good works they obtained superhuman powers and became entitled to receive praise and adoration.]
---

Sanskrit
तैरलातायुधैस्तत्र प्रमथानां मुनीश्वर ।
अभूद्युद्धं सुविकटं श‍ृण्वतां रोमहर्षणम् ॥ २५॥

English
O excellent sage, a terrible fight ensued between Śiva's attendants and the demons who had firebrands for their weapons. Their hair stood on end when people heard the uproar. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋभुभिस्तैर्महावीरैर्हन्यमानाः समन्ततः ।
अयत्नयानाः प्रमथा उशद्भिर्ब्रह्मतेजसा ॥ २६॥

English
The attendants were killed by the Rbhus of powerful valour and favoured with Brahminical splendour. They were forced to run without difficulty. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शिवगणास्ते वै हता विद्राविता द्रुतम् ।
शिवेच्छया महाशक्त्या तदद्भुतमिवाभवत् ॥ २७॥

English
It had been the desire of Śiva of the great Śakti that the attendants were killed and routed quickly. It was a wonderful scene. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा ऋषयो देवाः शक्राद्याः समरुद्गणाः ।
विश्वेऽश्विनौ लोकपालास्तूष्णीं भूतास्तदाभवन् ॥ २८॥

English
On seeing this, the sages, Indra and other devas, the Maruts, the Viśvedevas, the Aśvins and the guardians of the quarters kept silent. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिद्विष्णुं प्रभुं तत्र प्रार्थयन्तः समन्ततः ।
उद्विग्ना मन्त्रयन्तश्च विघ्नाभावं मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २९॥

English
Some of them went to request and consult with Viṣṇu frequently for preventing obstacles. They were greatly agitated. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सुविचार्योदर्कफलं महोद्विग्नाः सुबुद्धयः ।
सुरविष्ण्वादयोऽभूवंस्तन्नाशाद्रावणान्मुहुः ॥ ३०॥

English
The sensible devas, Viṣṇu and others, became agitated on pondering over the results of Pramathas' destruction and routing. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूतस्तदा यज्ञो विघ्नो जातो दुरात्मनः ।
ब्रह्मबन्धोश्च दक्षस्य शङ्करद्रोहिणो मुने ॥ ३१॥

English
Such was the obstacle to the sacrifice of Dakṣa who rivalled with Śiva, who was wicked and who professed to be a kinsman of Brahmā, O sage. (31.)

---
