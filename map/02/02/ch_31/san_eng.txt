### **CHAPTER THIRTY-ONE - *(The Celestial Voice)***

---

Sanskrit
३१

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र नभोवाणी मुनीश्वर ।
अवोचच्छृण्वतां दक्षसुरादीनां यथार्थतः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O excellent sage, in the meantime a celestial voice arose, even as Dakṣa, the devas and others were listening. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
व्योमवाण्युवाच ।
रे रे दक्ष दुराचार दम्भाचारपरायण ।
किं कृतं ते महामूढ कर्म चानर्थकारकम् ॥ २॥

English
*The celestial Voice said:—*  O Dakṣa, of evil conduct, of haughty disposition, what is it that you have foolishly done now, this misdeed bringing in many an unhappy calamity in its wake ? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
न कृतं शैवराजस्य दधीचेर्वचनस्य हि ।
प्रमाणं तत्कृते मूढ सर्वानन्दकरं शुभम् ॥ ३॥

English
You never gave any credence to the words of Dadhīci, the king of devotees of Śiva. O fool, if it had been carried out, everything would have been auspicious and pleasing. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गतस्ते मखाद्विप्रः शापं दत्त्वा सुदुःसहम् ।
ततोऽपि बुद्धं किञ्चिन्नो त्वया मूढेन चेतसि ॥ ४॥

English
After cursing you terribly, the brahmin had walked out of the sacrificial hall in protest. Still you did not understand anything, fool that you were. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कृतः कथं नो वै स्वपुत्र्यास्त्वादरः परः ।
समागतायाः सत्याश्च मङ्गलाया गृहं स्वतः ॥ ५॥

English
How is it that you did not honour Satī your daughter, the auspicious lady who herself came to your house ? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सतीभवौ नार्चितौ हि किमिदं ज्ञानदुर्बल ।
ब्रह्मपुत्र इति वृथा गर्वितोऽसि विमोहितः ॥ ६॥

English
O you weak in knowledge, how is it that you did not worship Satī and Śiva ? In vain do you feel proud of being Brahmā's son. You are actually deluded. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येव सदाराध्या सर्वपुण्यफलप्रदा ।
त्रिलोकमाता कल्याणी शङ्करार्धाङ्गभागिनी ॥ ७॥

English
That Satī alone, who quells all sins, who is the mother of three worlds, who has occupied half the body of Śiva and who confers all welfare should be propitiated always. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येवार्चिता नित्यं सर्वसौभाग्यदायिनी ।
माहेश्वरी स्वभक्तानां सर्वमङ्गलदायिनी ॥ ८॥

English
That Satī alone when worshipped for ever confers good fortunes. She is the great goddess who bestows everything auspicious on her devotees. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येवार्चिता नित्यं संसारभयनाशिनी ।
मनोऽभीष्टप्रदा देवी सर्वोपद्रवहारिणी ॥ ९॥

English
That Satī alone when propitiated for ever destroys the fear from worldly existence. She is the goddess who confers what we desire and who removes all disorders. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येवार्चिता नित्यं कीर्तिसम्पत्प्रदायिनी ।
परमा परमेशानी भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायिनी ॥ १०॥

English
That Satī alone when propitiated for ever bestows fame and wealth. She is the great Goddess who confers worldly pleasures and salvation. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येव जगद्धात्री जगद्रक्षणकारिणी ।
अनादिशक्तिः कल्पान्ते जगत्संहारकारिणी ॥ ११॥

English
That Satī alone is the creator of the universe, the protectress of the universe, the cause of destruction of the universe at the end of the Kalpa. She is the primordial Śakti. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येव जगन्माता विष्णुमाता विलासिनी ।
ब्रह्मेन्द्रचन्द्रवह्न्यर्कदेवादिजननी स्मृता ॥ १२॥

English
That Satī alone is the Māyā of the universe, the mother of Viṣṇu, of diverse shining features, and the mother of Brahmā, Indra, the moon, the fire, the sun and the devas. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सत्येव तपोधर्मदानादिफलदायिनी ।
शम्भुशक्तिर्महादेवी दुष्टहन्त्री परात्परा ॥ १३॥

English
That Satī alone is the bestower of the fruits of penance, charitable gifts and virtuous actions. She is the Śakti of Śiva, the great Goddess, the destroyer of the wicked and the greatest of the great. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृग्विधा सती देवी यस्य पत्नी सदा प्रिया ।
तस्यै भागो न दत्तस्ते मूढेन कुविचारिणा ॥ १४॥

English
The due share has not been offered by you who are foolish and evil-minded to the lord whose ever beloved wife is the goddess Satī of such glorious features. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुर्हि परमेशानः सर्वस्वामी परात्परः ।
विष्णुब्रह्मादिसंसेव्यः सर्वकल्याणकारकः ॥ १५॥

English
Śiva indeed is the great lord, the lord of all, the greatest of the great, worthy of being served by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others and the cause of all welfare. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तप्यते हि तपः सिद्धैरेतद्दर्शनकाङ्क्षिभिः ।
युज्यते योगिभिर्योगैरेतद्दर्शनकाङ्क्षिभिः ॥ १६॥

English
Penances are performed by the Siddhas who desire to have a vision of Him. Yogic meditations and exercises are performed by Yogins who desire to have a vision of Him. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्तधनधान्यानां यागादीनां तथैव च ।
दर्शनं शङ्करस्यैव महत्फलमुदाहृतम् ॥ १७॥

English
Vision of Śiva yields great fruits, endless wealth and foodgrains and the fructification of all sacrifices. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव एव जगद्धाता सर्वविद्यापतिः प्रभुः ।
आदिविद्यावरस्वामी सर्वमङ्गलमङ्गलः ॥ १८॥

English
Śiva alone is the creator of the universe, the lord of all lores, the upholder of the primordial learning and the lord, the most auspicious of the auspicious. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छक्तेर्न कृतो यस्मात्सत्करोऽद्य त्वया खल ।
अत एवाध्वरस्यास्य विनाशो हि भविष्यति ॥ १९॥

English
Since you have not duly respected His Śakti, and since you are wicked, this sacrifice will definitely be destroyed. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अमङ्गलं भवत्येव पूजार्हाणामपूजया ।
पूज्यमाना च नासौ हि यतः पूज्यतमा शिवा ॥ २०॥

English
Inauspicious results befall one when those worthy of worship are not worshipped and they are quelled when they are worshipped, since Śivā is the worthiest of all worship. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रेणापि शिरसां शेषो यत्पादजं रजः ।
वहत्यहरहः प्रीत्या तस्य शक्तिः शिवा सती ॥ २१॥

English
That Śakti is Śivā, Sati, the dust from whose feet is worn everyday by Śeṣa with his thousand heads. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्पादपद्ममनिशं ध्यात्वा सम्पूज्य सादरम् ।
विष्णुर्विष्णुत्वमापन्नस्तस्य शम्भोः प्रिया सती ॥ २२॥

English
Sati is the beloved of Śiva by meditating upon whose lotus-like feet for ever and by worshipping which Viṣṇu attained his Viṣṇu-hood. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्पादपद्ममनिशं ध्यात्वा सम्पूज्य सादरम् ।
ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मत्वमापन्नस्तस्य शम्भोः प्रिया सती ॥ २३॥

English
Sati is the beloved of Śiva, by meditating upon whose lotus-like feet for ever and by worshipping which, Brahmā attained his Brahmā-hood. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्पादपद्ममनिशं ध्यात्वा सम्पूज्य सादरम् ।
इन्द्रादयो लोकपालाः प्रापुः स्वं स्वं परं पदम् ॥ २४॥

English
Śiva is the lord, by meditating upon whose lotus-like feet for ever and by worshipping which the guardians of quarters—Indra and others attained their respective positions. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्पिता शिवः शक्तिर्जगन्माता च सा सती ।
सत्कृतौ न त्वया मूढ कथं श्रेयो भविष्यति ॥ २५॥

English
Śiva is the father of the universe. That Sati is the mother of the universe. O fool, they were not honoured duly by you. How can you attain welfare? (25.)

---

Sanskrit
दौर्भाग्यं त्वयि सङ्क्रान्तं सङ्क्रान्तास्त्वयि चापदः ।
यौ चानाराधितौ भक्त्या भवानीशङ्करौ च तौ ॥ २६॥

English
Since Sati and Śiva are not propitiated by you, misfortune has befallen you. Miseries have attacked you. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अनभ्यर्च्य शिवं शम्भुं कल्याणं प्राप्नुयामिति ।
किमस्ति गर्वो दुर्वारः स गर्वोऽद्य विनश्यति ॥ २७॥

English
Do you feel proud enough to suppose that you can attain welfare without worshipping Śiva? That haughty pride will be quashed today. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेशविमुखो भूत्वा देवेष्वेतेषु कस्तव ।
करिष्यति सहायं तं न ते पश्यामि सर्वथा ॥ २८॥

English
I do not see anyone among these devas who will come to your assistance, since you are averse to the Lord. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि देवाः करिष्यन्ति साहाय्यमधुना तव ।
तदा नाशं समाप्स्यन्ति शलभा इव वह्निना ॥ २९॥

English
If the devas were to come to your assistance, they are sure to be destroyed like moths by fire. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्वलत्वद्य मुखं ते वै यज्ञध्वंसो भवत्विति ।
सहायास्तव यावन्तस्ते ज्वलन्त्वद्य सत्वरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Let your face burn. Let your sacrifice be quashed. Whoever comes as your assistant—let him be burnt today immediately. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अमराणां च सर्वेषां शपथोऽमङ्गलाय ते ।
करिष्यन्त्यद्य साहाय्यं यदेतस्य दुरात्मनः ॥ ३१॥

English
An oath on all the devas for your inauspiciousness, if they are to assist your wickedness. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गच्छन्त्वमराः स्वोकमेतदध्वरमण्डपात् ।
अन्यथा भवतो नाशो भविष्यत्यद्य सर्वथा ॥ ३२॥

English
Let all the devas depart quickly from this sacrificial altar. Otherwise you all will perish today without an escape. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गच्छन्त्वपरे सर्वे मुनिनागादयो मखात् ।
अन्यथा भवतां नाशो भविष्यत्यद्य सर्वथा ॥ ३३॥

English
Let all the sages, Nāgas and others leave this sacrifice. Otherwise, you all will perish today, without an escape. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गच्छ त्वं हरे शीघ्रमेतदध्वरमण्डपात् ।
अन्यथा भवतो नाशो भविष्यत्यद्य सर्वथा ॥ ३४॥

English
O Viṣṇu, come out of this sacrificial platform quickly. Otherwise you will perish today without an escape. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गच्छ त्वं विधे शीघ्रमेतदध्वरमण्डपात् ।
अन्यथा भवतो नाशो भविष्यत्यद्य सर्वथा ॥ ३५॥

English
O Brahmā, come out of this sacrificial platform quickly. Otherwise you will perish today, by all means. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वाध्वरशालायामखिलायां सुसंस्थितान् ॥

व्यरमत्सा नभोवाणी सर्वकल्याणकारिणी ॥ ३६॥

तच्छ्रुत्वा व्योमवचनं सर्वे हर्यादयः सुराः ।
अकार्षुर्विस्मयं तात मुनयश्च तथापरे ॥ ३७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying this to those who had gathered in the sacrificial hall, the celestial voice that conferred welfare stopped. O dear one, on hearing the astral voice Viṣṇu and other devas were surprised. The sages too were wonder-struck. (36-37.)
