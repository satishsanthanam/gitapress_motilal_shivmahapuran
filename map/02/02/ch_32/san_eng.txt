### **CHAPTER THIRTY-TWO - (*Vīrabhadra is born and Śiva advises him*)**

---

Sanskrit
३२

नारद उवाच ।
श्रुत्वा व्योमगिरं दक्षः किमकार्षीत्तदाबुधः ।
अन्ये च कृतवन्तः किं ततश्च किमभूद् वद ॥ १॥

पराजिताः शिवगणा भृगुमन्त्रबलेन वै ।
किमकार्षुः कुत्र गतास्तत्त्वं वद महामते ॥ २॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  On hearing the ethereal voice what did the unwise Dakṣa do ? What did the others do ? What happened thereafter ? Please narrate. O intelligent one, please tell me what those attendants of Śiva who were defeated by the power of Bhṛgu's mantras did and where they went. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रुत्वा व्योमगिरं सर्वे विस्मिताश्च सुरादयः ।
नावोचत्किञ्चिदपि ते तिष्ठन्तस्तु विमोहिताः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the voice of the Sky, the devas and others were stunned with surprise. They did not say anything. They stood perplexed and dazed. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पलायमाना ये वीरा भृगुमन्त्रबलेन ते ।
अवशिष्टाः शिवगणाः शिवं शरणमाययुः ॥ ४॥

English
The remaining attendants of Śiva who were defeated and routed by the power of Bhṛgu’s mantras fled and sought refuge in Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं निवेदयामासू रुद्रायामिततेजसे ।
चरित्रं च तथाभूतं सुप्रणम्यादराच्च ते ॥ ५॥

English
Bowing with great respect to Śiva of immeasurable splendour they narrated everything that transpired there. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गणा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव पाहि नः शरणागतान् ।
संश‍ृण्वादरतो नाथ सतीवार्तां च विस्तरात् ॥ ६॥

English
*Gaṇas said :—*  O lord of Devas, save us who have sought refuge in you. Please listen with condescension to the detailed description of the events connected with Sati. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्वितेन महेशान दक्षेण सुदुरात्मना ।
अपमानः कृतः सत्यानादरो निर्जरैस्तथा ॥ ७॥

English
O Lord, great disrespect was shown to Sati by the haughty and wicked Dakṣa. The devas too did not show due respect to her. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तुभ्यं भागमदान्नो स देवेभ्यश्च प्रदत्तवान् ।
दुर्वचांस्यवदत्प्रोच्चैर्दुष्टो दक्षः सुगर्वितः ॥ ८॥

English
He did not allot your share but gave it to all the devas. The extremely haughty Dakṣa wickedly and loudly spoke harsh words. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दृष्ट्वा न ते भागं यज्ञेऽकुप्यत्सती प्रभो ।
विनिन्द्य बहुशस्तातमधाक्षीत्स्वतनुं तदा ॥ ९॥

English
O lord, not seeing your share in the sacrifice Sati became angry. After censuring her father many times she burnt her body (in the yogic fire). (9.)

---

Sanskrit
गणास्त्वयुतसङ्ख्याका मृतास्तत्र विलज्जया ।
स्वाङ्गान्याच्छिद्य शस्त्रैश्च क्रुध्याम ह्यपरे वयम् ॥ १०॥

English
Gaṇas exceeding ten-thousand put an end to their lives out of shame by cutting off their limbs with weapons. We, the rest, became infuriated. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्यज्ञं ध्वंसितुं वेगात्सन्नद्धास्तु भयावहाः ।
तिरस्कृता हि भृगुणा स्वप्रभावाद्विरोधिना ॥ ११॥

English
Assuming a terrifying attitude we suddenly got ready to destroy the sacrifice. But we were repelled by Bhṛgu by means of his (spiritual) power. He opposed us. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ते वयं शरणं प्राप्तास्तव विश्वम्भर प्रभो ।
निर्भयान् कुरु नस्तस्माद् दयमान भवाद्भयात् ॥ १२॥

English
O lord, the sustainer of the world, we have now sought refuge in you. We are now grieved and fear-stricken. Please make us free from fear. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अपमानं विशेषेण तस्मिन् यज्ञे महाप्रभो ।
दक्षाद्यास्तेऽखिला दुष्टा अकुर्वन् गर्विता अति ॥ १३॥

English
O great lord, Dakṣa and the other wicked persons have shown great disrespect because they are very haughty. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तं निखिलं वृत्तं स्वेषां सत्याश्च शङ्कर ।
तेषां च मूढबुद्धीनां यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ १४॥

English
O bestower of honour we have told you all that happened to us and to Sati. Please deal with those deluded fools in the manner you deem fit. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य स्वगणानां वचः प्रभुः ।
सस्मार नारदं सर्वं ज्ञातुं तच्चरितं लघु ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of his attendants, the lord remembered you, Nārada, in order to know their activities. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतस्त्वं द्रुतं तत्र देवर्षे दिव्यदर्शनः ।
प्रणम्य शङ्करं भक्त्या साञ्जलिस्तत्र तस्थिवान् ॥ १६॥

English
O celestial sage, endowed with divine vision you reached the place and after bowing to Śiva with devotion you waited there with palms joined in reverence. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां प्रशस्याथ स स्वामी सत्या वार्तां च पृष्टवान् ।
दक्षयज्ञगताया वै परं च चरितं तथा ॥ १७॥

English
After praising you, the lord asked you about Sati's news at the sacrifice of Dakṣa and the incidents there. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
पृष्टेन शम्भुना तात त्वयाश्वेव शिवात्मना ।
तत्सर्वं कथितं वृत्तं जातं दक्षाध्वरे हि यत् ॥ १८॥

English
O dear, when you were thus asked by Śiva, you identified with Śiva and narrated to him in detail about what had happened in Dakṣa's sacrifice. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्येश्वरो वाक्यं मुने तत् त्वन्मुखोदितम् ।
चुकोपातिद्रुतं रुद्रो महारौद्रपराक्रमः ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, on hearing the words spoken by you, Śiva became furious in a trice, Śiva of great fury and valour. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पाट्यैकां जटां रुद्रो लोकसंहारकारकः ।
आस्फालयामास रुषा पर्वतस्य तदोपरि ॥ २०॥

English
Then Rudra, the destroyer of the world, plucked out a cluster of his matted hair and struck the top of the mountain with it. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तोदनाच्च द्विधा भूता सा जटा च मुने प्रभोः ।
सम्बभूव महारावो महाप्रलयभीषणः ॥ २१॥

English
O sage, the cluster of the matted hair of the lord split into two, on being struck on the mountain. A loud explosive sound was heard which was as terrific as the sound at the time of dissolution. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तज्जटायाः समुद्भूतो वीरभद्रो महाबलः ।
पूर्वभागेन देवर्षे महाभीमो गणाग्रणीः ॥ २२॥

English
O celestial sage, from the first half of that cluster of matted hair, rose up the powerful Vīrabhadra$^{319}$, the terrific leader of the Gaṇas. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स भूमिं विश्वतो वृत्य चात्यतिष्ठद्दशाङ्गुलम् ।
प्रलयानलसङ्काशः प्रोन्नतो दोःसहस्रवान् ॥ २३॥

English
He stood lofty with two thousand hands blazing like the consuming fire. He enveloped the world all round and towered over it ten inches more. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कोपनिःश्वासतस्तत्र महारुद्रस्य चेशितुः ।
जातं ज्वराणां शतकं सन्निपातास्त्रयोदश ॥ २४॥

English
From the furious breath of Śiva, the great Rudra, hundred fevers and thirteen humours came out. (24.)

---

Sanskrit

English
From the other half of the cluster of matted hair Mahākālī$^{320}$ was born. O dear one, she was very terrible and was surrounded by crores of goblins. (25.)

[fn 320. Mahākālī is represented with a black skin, a hideous or terrible countenance, dripping with snakes, hung round with skulls and human heads, and in all respects resembling a fury rather than a Goddess. H.M. P. 86.]

English
The ruthless fevers had embodied forms. They were capable of terrifying the world. They were blazing with their fiery splendour. (26.)

Sanskrit
प्रमथाश्चागमिष्यन्ति यदि विष्ण्वादयो भ्रमात् ।
नानाकर्षणमन्त्रेण ज्वालयानीय सत्वरम् ॥ ५३॥

English
If Viṣṇu and others, out of erroneous notions were to withstand, other gaṇas will come for your help. They shall be burnt by you after dragging them with mantras. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ये तत्रोल्लङ्घ्य शपथं मदीयं गर्विताः स्थिताः ।
ते हि मद्द्रोहिणोऽतस्तान् ज्वालयानलमालया ॥ ५४॥

English
Transgressing my injunctions many haughty persons are lingering there. They are also my enemies. So burn them with a series of blazing fires. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
सपत्नीकान्ससारांश्च दक्षयागस्थलस्थितान् ।
प्रज्वाल्य भस्मसात्कृत्वा पुनरायाहि सत्वरम् ॥ ५५॥

English
After reducing them to ashes, along with their wives, and all the paraphernalia at the sacrifice of Dakṣa, you shall return quickly. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र त्वयि गते देवा विश्वाद्या अपि सादरम् ।
स्तोष्यन्ति त्वां तदाप्याशु ज्वालया ज्वालयैव तान् ॥ ५६॥

English
It is possible that when you go there, devas and others may praise you. Still you shall burn them in the flames. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानपि कृतद्रोहान् ज्वालामालासमाकुलैः ।
ज्वालय ज्वलनैः शीघ्रं माध्यायाध्यायपालकम् ॥ ५७॥

English
Burn the devas too who have committed offence, in the blazing fire, after meditating on me, your protector. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षादीन्सकलांस्तत्र सपत्नीकान्सबान्धवान् ।
प्रज्वाल्य वीर दक्षं नु सलीलं सलिलं पिब ॥ ५८॥

English
After burning Dakṣa and all others along with their wives and kinsmen, without any effort, in a playful manner you shall drink waters. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा रोषताम्राक्षो वेदमर्यादपालकः ।
विरराम महावीरं कालारिः सकलेश्वरः ॥ ५९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying thus to Vīrabhadra the great hero, Śiva the lord of all, the slayer of Kāla, the protector of Vedic conventions, stopped, with his eyes still resembling copper (due to anger). (59.)
