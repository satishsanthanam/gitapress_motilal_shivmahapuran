### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - *(The devas witness bad omens at the place of sacrifice)***

---

Sanskrit
३४

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं प्रचलिते चास्मिन् वीरभद्रे गणान्विते ।
दुष्टचिह्नानि दक्षेण दृष्टानि विबुधैरपि ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When Vīrabhadra set off thus, bad omens were seen by Dakṣa and the devas. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पाता विविधाश्चासन् वीरभद्रे गणान्विते ।
त्रिविधा अपि देवर्षे यज्ञविध्वंससूचकाः ॥ २॥

English
O celestial sage, when Vīrabhadra accompanied by the Gaṇas proceeded thus, many portentous phenomena occurred at the sacrifice of Dakṣa including the three evil omens, boding the imminent destruction of Dakṣa’s sacrifice. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षवामाक्षिबाहूरुविस्पन्दः समजायत ।
नानाकष्टप्रदस्तात सर्वथाशुभसूचकः ॥ ३॥

English
The left eye, arm and thigh of Dakṣa throbbed. In every respect, it indicated everything inauspicious It was harassing to him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भूकम्पः समभूत्तत्र दक्षयागस्थले तदा ।
दक्षोऽपश्यच्च मध्याह्ने नक्षत्राण्यद्भुतानि च ॥ ४॥

English
There was an earthquake at the site of sacrifice. Dakṣa observed the mysterious phenomena of stars at noon. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दिशश्चासन्सुमलिनाः कर्बुरोऽभूद्दिवाकरः ।
परिवेषसहस्रेण सङ्क्रान्तश्च भयङ्करः ॥ ५॥

English
The quarters became dirty and gloomy. The sun appeared spotted and terrifying with thousands of circlets all round. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
नक्षत्राणि पतन्ति स्म विद्युदग्निप्रभाणि च ।
नक्षत्राणामभूद्वक्रा गतिश्चाधोमुखी तदा ॥ ६॥

English
Stars, brilliant like lightning and blazing fire were seen falling. Some of them went zigzag and some fell with face downwards. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
गृध्रा दक्षशिरः स्पृष्ट्वा समुद्भूताः सहस्रशः ।
आसीद् गृध्रपक्षच्छायैः सच्छायो यागमण्डपः ॥ ७॥

English
Thousands of vultures hovered above touching Dakṣa’s head. Shadows of these darkened the sacrificial platform. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ववाशिरे यागभूमौ क्रोष्टारो नेत्रकस्तदा ।
उल्कावृष्टिरभूत्तत्र श्वेतवृश्चिकसम्भवा ॥ ८॥

English
Jackals howled in the surroundings of the sacrificial ground. The evil star Netraka and meteors seemed to fall like white scorpions. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
खरा वाता ववुस्तत्र पांशुवृष्टिसमन्विताः ।
शलभाश्च समुद्भूता विवर्तानिलकम्पिताः ॥ ९॥

English
Rough winds raising a lot of dust blew there. Locusts and moths were tossed about by whirlwinds. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नीतश्च पवनैरूर्ध्वं स दक्षाध्वरमण्डपः ।
दैवान्वितेन दक्षेण यः कृतो नूतनोऽद्भुतः ॥ १०॥

English
The wonderfully new sacrificial platform erected by Dakṣa and the devas was thrown up by the winds. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
वेमुर्दक्षादयः सर्वे तदा शोणितमद्भुतम् ।
वेमुश्च मांसखण्डानि सशल्यानि मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ११॥

English
Surprisingly enough, Dakṣa and others vomitted blood, pieces of flesh and bones very frequently. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सकम्पाश्च बभूवुस्ते दीपा वातहता इव ।
दुःखिताश्चाभवन्सर्वे शस्त्रधाराहता इव ॥ १२॥

English
They became unsteady and tremulous like lamps blown by wind. They felt miserable as if struck with the sharp edges of weapons. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा निनादजातानि बाष्पवर्षाणि तत्क्षणे ।
प्रातस्तुषारवर्षीणि पद्मानीव वनान्तरे ॥ १३॥

दक्षाद्यक्षीणि जातानि ह्यकस्माद्विशदान्यपि ।
निशायां कमलाश्चैव कुमुदानीव सङ्गवे ॥ १४॥

English
The eyes of Dakṣa and others sometimes resembled the fading lotuses of the summer; sometimes they resembled the flowers in forests with dews trickling from them; sometimes they seemed like lotuses at night and sometimes like Kumuda flowers in the forenoon. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
असृग्ववर्ष देवश्च तिमिरेणावृता दिशः ।
दिग्दाहोऽभूद्विशेषेण त्रासयन् सकलाञ्जनान् ॥ १५॥

English
The deities seemed to shower blood; the quarters became enveloped in darkness; there was a peculiar blaze everywhere terrifying all. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
एवंविधान्यरिष्टानि ददृशुर्विबुधादयः ।
भयमापेदिरेऽत्यन्तं मुने विष्ण्वादिकास्तदा ॥ १६॥

English
O sage, devas and others saw such evil portents as these. Viṣṇu and others were struck with great fear. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
भुवि ते मूर्छिताः पेतुर्हा हताः स्म इतीरयन् ।
तरवस्तीरसञ्जाता नदीवेगहता इव ॥ १७॥

English
“Ha, we are doomed” saying thus they fell unconscious on the ground like trees on the edges of rivers when felled by the force of the current. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
पतित्वा ते स्थिता भूमौ क्रूराः सर्पा हता इव ।
कन्दुका इव ते भूयः पतिताः पुनरुत्थिताः ॥ १८॥

English
Fallen on the ground they remained motionless like cruel serpents struck dead. Sometimes those fallen bounced up like balls. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्ते तापसन्तप्ता रुरुदुः कुररी इव ।
रोदनध्वनिसङ्क्रातोरुक्तिप्रत्युक्तिका इव ॥ १९॥

English
Then due to extreme distress they cried like twittering sparrows. Their groans and their voices got confusingly mingled with each other. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सवैकुण्ठास्ततः सर्वे तदा कुण्ठितशक्तयः ।
स्वस्वोपकण्ठमाकण्ठं लुलुठुः कमठा इव ॥ २०॥

English
Everyone including Viṣṇu had their power blunted and impeded. They rolled and dashed against one another like tortoises. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र सञ्जाता चाशरीरवाक् ।
श्रावयत्यखिलान् देवान् दक्षं चैव विशेषतः ॥ २१॥

English
In the meantime a disembodied voice arose there within the hearing of the devas and that of Dakṣa particularly. The ethereal voice said. (21.)

---

English
Fie upon your life now, O Dakṣa. You are evil-minded and excessively foolish. Great misery caused by Śiva will inevitably befall you. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशवाण्युवाच ।
धिक् जन्म तव दक्षाद्य महामूढोऽसि पापधीःस् ॥

भविष्यति महद्दुःखमनिवार्यं हरोद्भवम् । २२॥

सहायिनोऽत्र ये मूढास्तव देवादयः स्थिताः ।
तेषामपि महद्दुःखं भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ २३॥

English
Certainly great misery will befall those foolish devas and others who are here crying out “Hā Hā”. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वाकाशवचनं दृष्ट्वारिष्टानि तानि च ।
दक्षः प्रापद्भयं चाति परे देवादयोऽपि ह ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing that voice of the sky, and seeing those ill omens, Dakṣa was terribly afraid. The others—devas etc.—too followed suit. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
वेपमानस्तदा दक्षो विकलश्चाति चेतसि ।
अगच्छच्छरणं विष्णोः स्वप्रभोरिन्दिरापतेः ॥ २५॥

English
Trembling miserably and utterly shaken, Dakṣa sought refuge in Viṣṇu, the consort of Lakṣmī and his own lord. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रणम्य भयाविष्टः संस्तूय च विचेतनः ।
अवोचद्देवदेवं तं विष्णुं स्वजनवत्सलम् ॥ २६॥

English
Making humble obeisance in his fright, and eulogising in his mental distress, he said thus to Viṣṇu endearing to his own men. (26.)
