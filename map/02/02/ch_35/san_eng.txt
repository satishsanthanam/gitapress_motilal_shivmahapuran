### **CHAPTER THIRTY-FIVE - (*Viṣṇu's statement*)**

---

Sanskrit
३५

दक्ष उवाच ।
देवदेव हरे विष्णो दीनबन्धो कृपानिधे ।
मम रक्षा विधातव्या भवता साध्वरस्य च ॥ १॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  O Hari, Viṣṇu, lord of devas, friend of the poor, storehouse of mercy, you must protect me and my sacrifice. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षकस्त्वं मखस्यैव मखकर्मा मखात्मकः ।
कृपा विधेया यज्ञस्य भङ्गो भवतु न प्रभो ॥ २॥

English
You are the guardian of sacrifice. You are identical with the activity of sacrifice, the performer of sacrifice; O lord, be merciful, let not the sacrifice be destroyed. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थं बहुविधां दक्षः कृत्वा विज्ञप्तिमादरात् ।
पपात पादयोस्तस्य भयव्याकुलमानसः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus submitting entreaties of diverse sorts with great respect, in his mental agitation due to fear, he fell at his feet. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्थाप्य तं ततो विष्णुर्दक्षं विक्लिन्नमानसम् ।
श्रुत्वा च तस्य तद्वाक्यं कुमतेरस्मरच्छिवम् ॥ ४॥

English
Viṣṇu raised Dakṣa of agitated mind and on hearing the words of that evil-minded one, Viṣṇu remembered Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृत्वा शिवं महेशानं स्वप्रभुं परमेश्वरम् ।
अवदच्छिवतत्त्वज्ञो दक्षं सम्बोधयन्हरिः ॥ ५॥

English
After remembering Śiva his own lord, Viṣṇu, the knower of Śiva's principles, said thus, addressing Dakṣa :— (5.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिरुवाच ।
श‍ृणु दक्ष प्रवक्ष्यामि तत्त्वतः श‍ृणु मे वचः ।
सर्वथा ते हितकरं महामन्त्रसुखप्रदम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  Listen, O Dakṣa, I shall explain everything true to fact; listen to my words which are as efficacious as mantras, beneficent and pleasing. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अवज्ञा हि कृता दक्ष त्वया तत्त्वमजानता ।
सकलाधीश्वरस्यैव शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ॥ ७॥

English
Not knowing the principle of Śiva, the great Self and lord of everything, you have insulted Him. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वरावज्ञया सर्वं कार्यं भवति सर्वथा ।
विफलं केवलं नैव विपत्तिश्च पदे पदे ॥ ८॥

English
By insulting Him, not only does every activity become futile in every respect but also it engenders adversities at every step. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अपूज्या यत्र पूज्यन्ते पूजनीयो न पूज्यते ।
त्रीणि तत्र भविष्यन्ति दारिद्र्यं मरणं भयम् ॥ ९॥

English
Poverty, death and fear, these three take place when people worthy of worship are not worshipped and when undeserving people are honoured. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन माननीयो वृषध्वजः ।
अमानितान्महेशाच्च महद्भयमुपस्थितम् ॥ १०॥

English
Hence with all efforts, the bull-bannered deity shall be respected and revered. A great terror has befallen us because lord Śiva has been dishonoured here. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि न वयं सर्वे प्रभवः प्रभवामहे ।
भवतो दुर्नयेनैव मया सत्यमुदीर्यते ॥ ११॥

English
Although we are all lords, we are unable to accomplish anything due to your misdeeds. It is a fact I am mentioning. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
विष्णोस्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा दक्षश्चिन्तापरोऽभवत् ।
विवर्णवदनो भूत्वा तूष्णीमासीद्भुवि स्थितः ॥ १२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Viṣṇu, Dakṣa began to ponder. He sat quietly on the ground with his face turned pale. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे वीरभद्रः सैन्यसमन्वितः ।
अगच्छदध्वरं रुद्रप्रेरितो गणनायकः ॥ १३॥

English
Meanwhile Vīrabhadra, the leader of the Gaṇas, sent by Rudra, came to the place of sacrifice accompanied by his army. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पृष्ठे केचित्समायाता गगने केचिदागताः ।
दिशश्च विदिशः सर्वे समावृत्य तथापरे ॥ १४॥

शर्वाज्ञया गणाः शूरा निर्भया रुद्रविक्रमाः ।
असङ्ख्याः सिंहनादान्वै कुर्वन्तो वीरसत्तमाः ॥ १५॥

English
Some of his attendants followed him closely; some came through the sky and others came from the different quarters and sub-quarters. At the bidding of Śiva, these innumerable heroic, fearless Gaṇas came there roaring like lions. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन नादेन महता नादितं भुवनत्रयम् ।
रजसा चावृतं व्योम तमसा चावृता दिशः ॥ १६॥

English
By that great sound, the three worlds echoed and the quarters were plunged in darkness. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तद्वीपान्विता पृथ्वी चचालाति भयाकुला ।
सशैलकानना तत्र चुक्षुभुः सकलाब्धयः ॥ १७॥

English
The whole earth containing the seven continents shook with fear. All the oceans, forests and mountains were excessively agitated. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूतं च तत्सैन्यं लोकक्षयकरं महत् ।
दृष्ट्वा च विस्मिताः सर्वे बभूवुरमरादयः ॥ १८॥

English
On seeing that vast army of destructive potentiality, devas and others were quite surprised. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सैन्योद्योगमथालोक्य दक्षश्चासृङ्मुखाकुलः ।
दण्डवत्पतितो विष्णुं सकलत्रोऽभ्यभाषत ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing the enterprising activity of the army, Dakṣa's face turned red with agitation. He fell at the feet of Viṣṇu straight like a staff, prostrated at his feet, along with his wife and said thus. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
भवद्बलेनैव मया यज्ञः प्रारम्भितो महान् ।
सत्कर्मसिद्धये विष्णो प्रमाणं त्वं महाप्रभो ॥ २०॥

English
*Dakṣa said :—*  Depending upon you, this great sacrifice was started by me. O Viṣṇu, great lord, you are the final authority in the matter of realising good rites. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णो त्वं कर्मणां साक्षी यज्ञानां प्रतिपालकः ।
धर्मस्य वेदगर्भस्य ब्रह्मणस्त्वं महाप्रभो ॥ २१॥

English
O Viṣṇu you are the cosmic witness of sacred rites and the protector of sacrifices. You are the saviour of Vedic virtue, O great lord. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्रक्षा विधातव्या यज्ञस्यास्य मम प्रभो ।
त्वदन्यः कः समर्थोऽस्ति यतस्त्वं सकलप्रभुः ॥ २२॥

English
Hence, O lord, you shall offer protection to my sacrifice here. Who else, other than you, is competent for it ? You are the lord of all. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
दक्षस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा विष्णुर्दीनतरं तदा ।
अवोचद् बोधयंस्तं वै शिवतत्त्वपराङ्मुखम् ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these piteous words of Dakṣa, Viṣṇu spoke enlightening Dakṣa who was averse to Śiva’s principles. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
मया रक्षा विधातव्या तव यज्ञस्य दक्ष वै ।
ख्यातो मम पणः सत्यो धर्मस्य परिपालनम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O Dakṣa, protection shall be offered to your sacrifice. My promise of protecting Dharma is truly well known. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सत्यं तु त्वयोक्तं हि किं तत्तस्य व्यतिक्रमः ।
श‍ृणु त्वं वच्म्यहं दक्ष क्रूरबुद्धिं त्यजाधुना ॥ २५॥

English
You have stated the truth, but you have transgressed the same. O Dakṣa listen, I shall tell you. Cast off your cruelty. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नैमिषेऽनिमिषक्षेत्रे यज्जातं वृत्तमद्भुतम् ।
तत् किं न स्मर्यते दक्ष विस्मृतं किं कुबुद्धिना ॥ २६॥

English
What transpired at Naimiṣa<sup>326</sup>, the holy place in a surprisingly mysterious manner is evidently not remembered by you, O Dakṣa. Did you forget it in your evil way ? (26.)

[fn 326. Naimiṣa, modern Nimsar, is a sacred region of Uttara Pradeśa in the district of Sitāpur on the bank of Gomati.]
---

Sanskrit
रुद्रकोपाच्च को ह्यत्र समर्थो रक्षणे तव ।
न यस्याभिमतं दक्ष यस्त्वां रक्षति दुर्मतिः ॥ २७॥

English
Who can save you from Rudra’s anger ? O Dakṣa, a person who protects you, the wicked one, finds no approval anywhere. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कर्म किमकर्मेति तन्न पश्यसि दुर्मते ।
समर्थं केवलं कर्म न भविष्यति सर्वदा ॥ २८॥

English
An evil-minded man does not see what is good and what is not. A sacred action or rite cannot be efficacious always. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वकर्म विद्धि तद्येन समर्थत्वेन जायते ।
न त्वन्यः कर्मणो दाता शं भवेदीश्वरं विना ॥ २९॥

English
You must know that to be your duty which is naturally efficacious. Except Śiva none can be the bestower of action. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वरस्य च यो भक्त्या शान्तस्तद्गतमानसः ।
कर्मणो हि फलं तस्य प्रयच्छति तदा शिवः ॥ ३०॥

English
Śiva bestows the fruit of the actions upon the person who is tranquil on account of devotion to the lord and whose mind is fixed in him alone. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
केवलं ज्ञानमाश्रित्य निरीश्वरपरा नराः ।
निरयं ते च गच्छन्ति कल्पकोटिशतानि च ॥ ३१॥

English
Depending upon Knowledge alone if they eschew devotion to God they will fall into hell and remain there for hundreds of crores of Kalpas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः कर्ममयैः पाशैर्बद्धा जन्मनि जन्मनि ।
निरयेषु प्रपच्यन्ते केवलं कर्मरूपिणः ॥ ३२॥

English
Those who solely depend on actions are bound by its nooses, are born in lives after lives and are finally scorched and tortured in hells. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं रुद्रगणाधीशो वीरभद्रोऽरिमर्दनः ।
रुद्रकोपाग्निसम्भूतः समायातोऽध्वराङ्गणे ॥ ३३॥

English
Here, Virabhadra, the chief of Rudra's attendants, who suppresses all his enemies and who is born of the fire of Rudra's anger has now come to the sacrificial yard. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अयमस्मद्विनाशार्थमागतोऽस्ति न संशयः ।
अशक्यमस्य नास्त्येव किमप्यस्तु तु वस्तुतः ॥ ३४॥

English
There is no doubt in this that he has come for destroying us. There is nothing impossible for him to do, whatever it may be really. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रज्वाल्यास्मानयं सर्वान् ध्रुवमेव महाप्रभुः ।
ततः प्रशान्तहृदयो भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ ३५॥

English
It is certain that this great lord will become tranquil only after burning us all. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमहादेवशपथं समुल्लङ्घ्य भ्रमान्मया ।
यतः स्थितं ततः प्राप्यं मया दुःखं त्वया सह ॥ ३६॥

English
Since by my mistake I have transgressed the affirmation of lord Siva, I too shall bear the miseries along with you. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिर्मम तु नास्त्येव दक्षाद्यैतन्निवारणे ।
शपथोल्लङ्घनादेव शिवद्रोही यतोऽस्म्यहम् ॥ ३७॥

English
O Daksa, I have no power to prevent this since I have become an enemy of Siva by transgressing his affirmation. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
कालत्रयेऽपि न यतो महेशद्रोहिणां सुखम् ।
ततोऽवश्यं मया प्राप्तं दुःखमद्य त्वया सह ॥ ३८॥

English
There can be no happiness to the enemies of Siva in the three periods of time; misery has of necessity been invited by me along with you. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदर्शनाभिधं चक्रमेतस्मिन्न लगिष्यति ।
शैवचक्रमिदं यस्मादशैवलयकारणम् ॥ ३९॥

English
My discus Sudarsana will not hit him. My discus is Saiva (belonging to Siva) and it can cause the death of only non-Saivas. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
विनापि वीरभद्रेण नामैतच्चक्रमैश्वरम् ।
हत्वा गमिष्यत्यधुना सत्वरं हरसन्निधौ ॥ ४०॥

English
Even if Virabhadra had not been here, this discus would have killed us and returned to Siva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवं शपथमुल्लङ्घ्य स्थितं मां चक्रमीदृशम् ।
असंहत्यैव सहसा कृपयैव स्थिरं परम् ॥ ४१॥

English
This discus has remained without killing me, although I had transgressed the avowal of Siva. It indicates that it is compassionate. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

गमिष्यत्यधुना शीघ्रं ज्वालामालासमाकुलम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Hereafter this discus will not stay with me. With its fiery effusions it will depart quickly. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रः पूजितोऽपि शीघ्रमस्माभिरादरात् ।
महाक्रोधसमाक्रान्तो नास्मान् संरक्षयिष्यति ॥ ४३॥

English
Even if he is worshipped and honoured by us with respect, Virabhadra will not protect us since he is extremely angry. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
अकाण्डप्रलयोऽस्माकमागतोऽद्य हि हा हहा ।

English
A sudden dissolution of every one of us is imminent. Alas, it has already set upon us. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
शरण्योऽस्माकमधुना नास्त्येव हि जगत्त्रये ।
शङ्करद्रोहिणो लोके कः शरण्यो भविष्यति ॥ ४५॥

English
There is none to offer us refuge in the three worlds. Who can be the refuge of an enemy of Siva in this world? (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even if the body undergoes destruction, the torture at the hands of Yama$^{327}$ is in store for us. It is impossible to bear as it generates much misery. (46.)

[fn 327. Yama : In Paurāṇic Mythology, Yama is the God who presides over the manes and rules the spirits of the dead. He is always represented as a terrible deity inflicting tortures, called yātanā, on departed sinful spirits.]
---

Sanskrit
शिवद्रोहिणमालोक्य दष्टदन्तो यमः स्वयम् ।
तप्ततैलकटाहेषु पातयत्येव नान्यथा ॥ ४७॥

English
On seeing an enemy of Śiva, Yama gnashes his teeth. He puts him in cauldrons of oil and not otherwise. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Actually I was preparing to leave after an open declaration. Still I did not leave immediately by the contagious sin of this wicked person. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even if we flee from this place, Virabhadra the devotee of Śiva, will drag and pull us by means of his weapons. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Whether it is heaven or earth, Pātāla or any where else, it is not difficult for the weapons of Virabhadra to gain access there. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Such is the power of everyone of the attendants of the trident-bearing Rudra. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Formerly at Kāśī, Kālabhairava had plucked off the fifth head of Brahmā playfully with the tip of his nail. (52.)

---

Sanskrit

वीरभद्रोऽपि सम्प्राप तदैवाध्वरमण्डपम् ॥ ५३॥

English
After saying this, Viṣṇu resumed his seat, his lotus-face showing signs of great fear. At the same time Virabhadra too reached the sacrificial platform. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं ब्रुवति गोविन्द आगतं सैन्यसागरम् ।
वीरभद्रेण सहितं ददृशुश्च सुरादयः ॥ ५४॥

English
While Viṣṇu was saying this, the gods and others saw the vast ocean of the army led by Virabhadra already come there. (54.)
