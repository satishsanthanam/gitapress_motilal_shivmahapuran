### **CHAPTER THIRTYSIX - *(The dialogue between Viṣṇu and Virabhadra)***

---

Sanskrit
३६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इन्द्रोऽपि प्रहसन् विष्णुमात्मवादरतं तदा ।
वज्रपाणिः सुरैः सार्धं योद्धुकामोऽभवत्तदा ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Indra mocked at Viṣṇu who was engrossed in his own arguments. He, the bearer of the thunderbolt, was desirous of fighting Virabhadra along with the other devas. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तदेन्द्रो गजमारूढो बस्तारूढोऽनलस्तथा ।
यमो महिषमारूढो निरृतिः प्रेतमेव च ॥ २॥

English
Then Indra rode on his elephant, the fire-god rode on a goat, Yama rode on his buffalo and Nirṛti rode on a ghost. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पाशी च मकरारूढो मृगारूढः सदागतिः ।
कुबेरः पुष्पकारूढः सन्नद्धोऽभूदतन्द्रितः ॥ ३॥

English
Varuṇa rode on a crocodile; the wind-god rode on a deer; Kubera sat in his chariot Puṣpaka and he was ready and alert. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तथान्ये सुरसङ्घाश्च यक्षचारणगुह्यकाः ।
आरुह्य वाहनान्येव स्वानि स्वानि प्रतापिनः ॥ ४॥

English
The others in the hosts of devas, Yakṣas, Cāraṇas and Guhyakas all very powerful, rode on their own respective vehicles. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषामुद्योगमालोक्य दक्षश्चासृङ्मुखस्तथा ।
तदन्तिकं समागत्य सकलत्रोऽभ्यभाषत ॥ ५॥

English
Seeing their enterprise, Dakṣa with blood rushing to his face in his excitement approached them along with his wife and spoke. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
युष्मद्बलेनैव मया यज्ञः प्रारम्भितो महान् ।
सत्कर्मसिद्धये यूयं प्रमाणाः स्युर्महाप्रभाः ॥ ६॥

English
*Dakṣa said:—*  Depending on your support and strength alone have I begun this great sacrifice. Such brilliant persons as you, are authorities in the achievement of all good actions. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा दक्षवचनं सर्वे देवाः सवासवाः ।
निर्ययुस्त्वरितं तत्र युद्धं कर्तुं समुद्यताः ॥ ७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Dakṣa, the gods including Indra set off immediately in their readiness to fight. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवगणाः सर्वे युयुधुस्ते बलान्विताः ।
शक्रादयो लोकपाला मोहिताः शिवमायया ॥ ८॥

English
Indra and other devas and the guardians of the quarters, deluded by Śiva’s Māyā fought with their full power. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां च गणानां च तदासीत्समरो महान् ।
तीक्ष्णतोमरनाराचैर्युयुधुस्ते परस्परम् ॥ ९॥

English
A great fight ensued between the devas and the gaṇas. Those powerful warriors fought with each other with sharp spikes, iron clubs etc. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नेदुः शङ्खाश्च भेर्यश्च तस्मिन् रणमहोत्सवे ।
महादुन्दुभयो नेदुः पटहा डिण्डिमादयः ॥ १०॥

English
Conchs were blown. Drums were beaten in that great war festival. Battle drums were sounded both big and small. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन शब्देन महता श्लाघ्यमानास्तदा सुराः ।
लोकपालैश्च सहिता जघ्नुस्तान् शिवकिङ्करान् ॥ ११॥

English
Being encouraged by that sound, the devas in the company of the guardians of the quarters hit and thrashed the attendants of Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्राद्यैर्लोकपालैश्च गणाः शम्भोः पराङ्मुखाः ।
कृताश्च मुनिशार्दूल भृगोर्मन्त्रबलेन च ॥ १२॥

English
O excellent sage, on account of the incantations of Bhṛgu, the attendants of Śiva were routed by Indra and other guardians of the quarters. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्चाटनं कृतं तेषां भृगुणा यज्वना तदा ।
यजनार्थं च देवानां तुष्ट्यर्थं दीक्षितस्य च ॥ १३॥

English
Their defeat was effected by Bhṛgu the sacrificial priest, for the continuance of the worship of the deities and for the satisfaction of Dakṣa who had been initiated in the sacrifice. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पराजितान् स्वकान् दृष्ट्वा वीरभद्रो रुषान्वितः ।
भूतप्रेतपिशाचांश्च कृत्वा तानेव पृष्ठतः ॥ १४॥

वृषभस्थान् पुरस्कृत्य स्वयं चैव महाबलः ।
महात्रिशूलमादाय पातयामास निर्जरान् ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing his people defeated, Virabhadra became infuriated. He asked the goblins, ghosts and spirits to keep back. The attendants riding on bulls were sent to the front ranks. Accompanied by his forces and wielding his great trident he felled the devas. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
देवान् यक्षान् साध्यगणान् गुह्यकान् चारणानपि ।
शूलघातैश्च ते सर्वे गणा वेगात् प्रजघ्निरे ॥ १६॥

English
The attendants of Śiva hit and thrashed the devas, the Yakṣas, Sādhyas, Guhyakas and Cāraṇas with their javelins, spears and tridents. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिद् द्विधा कृताः खड्गैर्मुद्गरैश्च विपोथिताः ।
अन्यैः शस्त्रैरपि सुरा गणैर्भिन्नास्तदाभवन् ॥ १७॥

English
Some were split with swords and smashed with iron-clubs. The devas were hit and smothered with various weapons by the attendants of Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पराजिताः सर्वे पलायनपरायणाः ।
परस्परं परित्यज्य गता देवास्त्रिविष्टपम् ॥ १८॥

English
Thus defeated, the devas left one another in lurch and fled to heaven. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
केवलं लोकपालास्ते शक्राद्यास्तस्थुरुत्सुकाः ।
सङ्ग्रामे दारुणे तस्मिन् धृत्वा धैर्यं महाबलाः ॥ १९॥

English
Only the guardians of the quarters, Indra and others, had the courage and strength to stay behind in that terrible battle with what little enthusiasm they had. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे मिलित्वा शक्राद्या देवास्तत्र रणाजिरे ।
बृहस्पतिं च पप्रच्छुर्विनयावनतास्तदा ॥ २०॥

English
Indra and others collectively approached Bṛhaspati in that battlefield and asked him humbly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकपाला ऊचुः ।
गुरो बृहस्पते तात महाप्राज्ञ दयानिधे ।
शीघ्रं वद पृच्छतो नः कुतोऽस्माकं जयो भवेत् ॥ २१॥

English
*The guardians of the quarters said :—*  O dear preceptor Bṛhaspati, intelligent and merciful, please tell us quickly how we can be victorious. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां स्मृत्वा शम्भुं प्रयत्नवान् ।
बृहस्पतिरुवाचेदं महेन्द्रं ज्ञानदुर्बलम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing their words, Bṛhaspati remembered Śiva and spoke to Indra who was confused and confounded to slove the difficulty. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।
यदुक्तं विष्णुना पूर्वं तत्सर्वं जातमद्य वै ।
तदेव विवृणोमीन्द्र सावधानतया श‍ृणु ॥ २३॥

English
*Bṛhaspati said :—*  O Indra, what Viṣṇu had said before has taken place now. I shall explain it further. Listen attentively. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्ति यश्चेश्वरः कश्चित् फलदः सर्वकर्मणाम् ।
कर्तारं भजते सोऽपि न स्वकर्तुः प्रभुर्हि सः ॥ २४॥

English
There is the presiding deity of sacrifices who dispenses the fruits of all sacrifices. He does it with reference to the performer. He is not independent of the performer. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
न मन्त्रौषधयः सर्वे नाभिचारा न लौकिकाः ।
न कर्माणि न वेदाश्च न मीमांसाद्वयं तथा ॥ २५॥

अन्यान्यपि च शास्त्राणि नानावेदयुतानि च ।
ज्ञातुं नेशं सम्भवन्ति वदन्त्येवं पुरातनाः ॥ २६॥

English
Neither Mantras nor medicinal herbs, nor black magic, nor worldly activities, nor the Vedas, nor the two systems of Mīmāṃsās, nor other sacred texts based on Vedic passages are able to know Śiva—so the ancient authorities say. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
न स्वज्ञेयो महेशानः सर्ववेदायुतेन सः ।
भक्तैरनन्यशरणैर्नान्यथेति महाश्रुतिः ॥ २७॥

English
Lord Śiva is not unknowable entirely. He can be known by devotees who have no other refuge though he is unknowable through thousands of Vedic passages without devotion. So says the great Vedic passage. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शान्त्या च परया दृष्ट्या सर्वथा निर्विकारया ।
तदनुग्रहतो नूनं ज्ञातव्यो हि सदाशिवः ॥ २८॥

English
Sadāśiva shall be known through his own blessings by mental tranquility and supreme vision without aberrations and distractions. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
परं तु संवदिष्यामि कार्याकार्य विवक्षितौ ।
सिद्धयंशं च सुरेशान तं श‍ृणु त्वं हिताय वै ॥ २९॥

English
But, O Lord of devas, in the matter of discussion about what shall be done and what not, I shall explain the aspect of fulfilment of our desire. Hear that in your own interest. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमिन्द्र बालिशो भूत्वा लोकपालैः सहाद्य वै ।
आगतो दक्षयज्ञं हि किं करिष्यसि विक्रमम् ॥ ३०॥

English
It is your childishness that prompted you to be present here in the sacrifice of Dakṣa along with the other guardians of quarters. What shall you do with the exhibition of your valour ? (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एते रुद्रसहायाश्च गणाः परमकोपनाः ।
आगता यज्ञविघ्नार्थं तं करिष्यन्त्यसंशयम् ॥ ३१॥

English
These infuriated assistants of Rudra have come here to stop the sacrifice and they will do it undoubtedly. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा न ह्युपायोऽत्र केषाञ्चिदपि तत्त्वतः ।
यज्ञविघ्नविनाशार्थं सत्यं सत्यं ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ३२॥

English
There is no remedy to prevent the destruction of the sacrifice. True, I am telling you the truth. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं बृहस्पतेर्वाक्यं श्रुत्वा ते हि दिवौकसः ।
चिन्तामापेदिरे सर्वे लोकपालाः सवासवाः ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Bṛhaspati the guardians of the quarters, dwellers in heaven including Indra fell athinking. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽब्रवीद् वीरभद्रो महावीरगणैर्वृतः ।
इन्द्रादीन् लोकपालांस्तान् स्मृत्वा मनसि शङ्करम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Then, remembering Śiva mentally Vīrabhadra surrounded by the heroic attendants of Śiva spoke to Indra and other guardians of the quarters. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्र उवाच ।
सर्वे यूयं बालिशत्वादवदानार्थमागताः ।
अवदानं प्रयच्छामि आगच्छत ममान्तिकम् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Vīrabhadra said :—*  “On account of your childishness you came here for this glorious act. I shall now offer you Avadāna (i.e. I shall cut you to pieces). Come near me. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
हे शक्र हे शुचे भानो हे शशिन् हे धनाधिप ।
हे पाशपाणे हे वायो निरृते यम शेष हे ॥ ३६॥

हे सुरासुरसङ्घा हीहैत यूयं विचक्षणाः ।
अवदानानि दास्यामि आतृप्त्याद्यासतां वरान् ॥ ३७॥

English
O Indra, O Agni, O Sun, O Moon, O Kubera, O Varuṇa, O Wind, O Nirṛti, O Yama, O Śeṣa, O Devas and Asuras, O clever ones, come here. I shall give you Avadānas. The boon will be tasted by you till you are satiated.” (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा सितैर्बाणैर्जघानाथ रुषान्वितः ।
निखिलांस्तान् सुरान् सद्यो वीरभद्रो गणाग्रणीः ।
तैर्बाणैर्निहताःसर्वे वासवाद्याः सुरेश्वराः ॥ ३८॥

English
*Brahmā said :*  Saying so, Vīrabhadra, the leader of the attendants became furious and hit all the devas with sharp arrows. Severely wounded by the arrows, Indra and other leaders of devas fled in all the ten directions. When the guardians of the quarters and other devas had fled, Vīrabhadra came very near the entrance of the sacrificial chamber along with the Gaṇas. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
पलायनपरा भूत्वा जग्मुस्ते च दिशो दश ।
गतेषु लोकपालेषु विद्रुतेषु सुरेषु च ॥

यज्ञवाटोपकण्ठे हि वीरभद्रोऽगमद् गणैः । ३९॥

तदा ते ऋषयः सर्वे सुभीता हि रमेश्वरम् ।
विज्ञप्तुकामाः सहसा शीघ्रमूचुर्नता भृशम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Then all the sages who had assembled there were terribly afraid and bowing to Viṣṇu and desirous of informing him said thus:— (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
देवदेव रमानाथ सर्वेश्वर महाप्रभो ।
रक्ष यज्ञं हि दक्षस्य यज्ञोऽसि त्वं न संशयः ॥ ४१॥

यज्ञकर्मा यज्ञरूपो यज्ञाङ्गो यज्ञरक्षकः ।
रक्ष यज्ञमतो रक्ष त्वत्तोऽन्यो न हि रक्षकः ॥ ४२॥

English
*The sages said :—*  “O lord of Lakṣmī, lord of devas, O great lord, lord of everyone, save the sacrifice of Dakṣa. Undoubtedly you are the sacrifice, the performer of sacrifice, the sacrifice embodied, ancillary to sacrifice and the protector of sacrifice. Please save, save the sacrifice. There is none else than you to protect it.” (41-42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषामृषीणां वचनं हरिः ।
योद्धुकामोऽभवद्विष्णुर्वीरभद्रेण तेन वै ॥ ४३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the sages Viṣṇu, desirous of fighting with Vīrabhadra went ahead. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्भुजः सुसन्नद्धो चक्रायुधधरः करैः ।
महाबलोऽमरगणैर्यज्ञवाटात्स निर्ययौ ॥ ४४॥

English
The powerful Viṣṇu, the four-armed discus-bearing Viṣṇu, fully equipped, came out of the sacrificial chamber along with the devas. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रः शूलपाणिर्नानागणसमन्वितः ।
ददर्श विष्णुं सन्नद्धं योद्धुकामं महाप्रभुम् ॥ ४५॥

English
The trident-wielding Vīrabhadra accompanied by the different Gaṇas saw Viṣṇu the great lord desirous of fighting and ready for it. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा वीरभद्रोऽभूद् भ्रुकुटीकुटिलाननः ।
कृतान्त इव पापिष्ठं मृगेन्द्र इव वारणम् ॥ ४६॥

English
On seeing him Vīrabhadra knit his eyebrows and his face became awful. He met him as the god of death meeting a sinner or the lion meeting an elephant. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तथाविधं हरिं दृष्ट्वा वीरभद्रोऽरिमर्दनः ।
अवदत्त्वरितः क्रुद्धो गणैर्वीरैः समावृतः ॥ ४७॥

English
On seeing Viṣṇu in such a manner Vīrabhadra the suppressor of enemies, surrounded by the Gaṇas became furious and said. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्र उवाच ।
रे रे हरे महादेवशपथोल्लङ्घनं त्वया ।
कथमद्य कृतं चित्ते गर्वः किमभवत्तव ॥ ४८॥

English
*Vīrabhadra said :*  O Viṣṇu, how is it that you set at nought the affirmation of lord Śiva ? Why were you haughty ? (48.)

---

Sanskrit
तव श्रीरुद्रशपथोल्लङ्घने शक्तिरस्ति किम् ।
को वा त्वमसि को वा ते रक्षऽकोऽस्ति जगत्त्रये ॥ ४९॥

English
Can you dare to transgress the affirmation of lord Śiva ? Who are you ? In the three worlds who is your saviour ? (49.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र त्वमागतः कस्माद्वयं तन्नैव विद्महे ।
दक्षस्य यज्ञपाता त्वं कथं जातोऽसि तद्वद ॥ ५०॥

English
Why did you come here? We do not know that. How did you become the guardian and saviour of Dakṣa’s sacrifice ? Explain it. (50.)

---

English
Haven't you seen what Sati did ? Haven't you heard what Dadhīci said ? (51.)

---
