### **CHAPTER THIRTYSEVEN - *(Destruction of Dakṣa’s sacrifice)***

---

Sanskrit
३७

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
वीरभद्रोऽथ युद्धे वै विष्णुना स महाबलः ।
संस्मृत्य शङ्करं चित्ते सर्वापद्विनिवारणम् ॥ १॥

आरुह्य स्यन्दनं दिव्यं सर्ववैरिविमर्दनः ।
गृहीत्वा परमास्त्राणि सिंहनादं जगर्ज ह ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Mentally meditating on Śiva, the remover of all adversities and seated in his divine chariot, the powerful Vīrabhadra took up all the great miraculous weapons for his fight with Viṣṇu and roared like a lion. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुश्चापि महाघोषं पाञ्चजन्याभिधं निजम् ।
दध्मौ बली महाशङ्खं स्वकीयान् हर्षयन्निव ॥ ३॥

English
Viṣṇu, the powerful, loudly blew his conch “Pāñca-janya” delighting his own people. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा शङ्खनिर्ह्रादं देवा ये च पलायिताः ।
रणं हित्वा गताः पूर्वं ते द्रुतं पुनराययुः ॥ ४॥

English
On hearing the sound of the conch, the devas who had fled before leaving off the battle-field returned quickly. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रगणैस्ते तु लोकपालाः सवासवाः ।
युद्धं चक्रुस्तथा सिंहनादं कृत्वा बलान्विताः ॥ ५॥

English
The guardians of the quarters including Indra roared like lions and fought forcefully with the Gaṇas of Virabhadra. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गणानां लोकपालानां द्वन्द्वयुद्धं भयावहम् ।
अभवत्तत्र तुमुलं गर्जतां सिंहनादतः ॥ ६॥

English
A noisy terrible fight ensued between the Gaṇas and the guardians of the quarters, both roaring like lions. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिना युयुधे शक्रोऽनलो वै चाश्मना तथा ।
कुबेरोऽपि हि कूष्माण्डपतिना युयुधे बली ॥ ७॥

English
Indra fought with Nandin; the fire-god with Aśman and the powerful Kubera fought with Kūṣmāṇḍapati. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तदेन्द्रेण हतो नन्दी वज्रेण शतपर्वणा ॥ ८॥

नन्दिना च हतः शक्रस्त्रिशूलेन स्तनान्तरे ॥ ९॥

English
Nandin was hit hard by Indra with the thunderbolt that had a hundred spikes. Indra was hurt in the middle of his chest by Nandin with the trident. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
बलिनौ द्वावपि प्रीत्या युयुधाते परस्परम् ।
नानाघातांश्च कुर्वन्तौ नन्दिशक्रौ जिगीषया ॥ १०॥

English
Nandin and Indra both equally powerful fought with each other gleefully, and hit each other in diverse ways with a desire to overpower each other. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्त्या जघान चाश्मानं शुचिः परमकोपनः ।
सोऽपि शूलेन तं वेगाच्छितधारेण पावकम् ॥ ११॥

English
The infuriated fire-god hit Aśman with his (spear). He too hit back the fire-god with his trident of very sharp point. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यमेन सह सङ्ग्रामं महालोको गणाग्रणीः ।
चकार तुमुलं वीरो महादेवं स्मरन्मुदा ॥ १२॥

English
Mahāloka, the heroic chieftain of the Gaṇas, remembered Lord Śiva with joy and fought with Yama. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नैरृतेन समागम्य चण्डश्च बलवत्तरः ।
युयुधे परमास्त्रैश्च नैरृतिं निविडम्बयन् ॥ १३॥

English
Caṇḍa, the brawny, grappled with Nairṛṭa and mortified him with many great miraculous weapons. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
वरुणेन समं वीरो मुण्डश्चैव महाबलः ।
युयुधे परया शक्त्या त्रिलोकीं विस्मयन्निव ॥ १४॥

English
The powerful hero Muṇḍa fought with Varuṇa surprising the three worlds with his great spear. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुना च हतो भृङ्गी स्वास्त्रेण परमौजसा ।
भृङ्गिणा च हतो वायुस्त्रिशूलेन प्रतापिना ॥ १५॥

English
Bhṛṅgī was struck by the wind god with his weapon of great force. Vāyu was struck (in return) by Bhṛṅgī with a powerful trident. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कुबेरेणैव सङ्गम्य कूष्माण्डपतिरादरात् ।
युयुधे बलवान् वीरो ध्यात्वा हृदि महेश्वरम् ॥ १६॥

English
Meditating on Lord Śiva in his heart, the strong and heroic Kūṣmāṇḍapati clashed with Kubera and fought terribly. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
योगिनीचक्रसंयुक्तो भैरवीनायको महान् ।
विदार्य देवानखिलान् पपौ शोणितमद्भुतम् ॥ १७॥

English
Splitting up all the Devas, the great leader of Bhairavī in collaboration with the circle of Yoginis, drank much of their blood. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेत्रपालास्तथा तत्र बुभुक्षुः सुरपुङ्गवान् ।
काली चापि विदार्यैव तान्पपौ रुधिरं बहु ॥ १८॥

English
Desirous of gobbling up the leading devas, Kālī split them and drank their blood. Kṣetrapāla too did the same. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुर्महातेजा युयुधे तैश्च शत्रुहा ।
चक्रं चिक्षेप वेगेन दहन्निव दिशो दश ॥ १९॥

English
Then Viṣṇu, the slayer of enemies and who was excessively brilliant, hurled his discus and fought with them. The discus seemed to burn the ten directions. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेत्रपालः समायान्तं चक्रमालोक्य वेगतः ।
तत्रागत्यागतो वीरश्चाग्रसत्सहसा बली ॥ २०॥

English
Kṣetrapāla saw the discus coming on. He ran to the place and bravely caught hold of it. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रं ग्रसितमालोक्य विष्णुः परपुरञ्जयः ।
मुखं तस्य परामृज्य तमुद्गालितवानरिम् ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing the discus held in his mouth, Viṣṇu the conqueror of enemies' cities caught hold of his throat and made him spit out the discus. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वचक्रमादाय महानुभावः
चुकोप चातीव भवैकभर्ता ।
महाबली तैर्युयुधे प्रवीरैः
सङ्क्रुद्धनानायुधधारकोऽस्त्रैः ॥ २२॥

English
Regaining his discus, Viṣṇu the sole sustainer of the world, of great dignity became very angry. In that infuriated state he took up different weapons and fought with the brave warriors. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रे महारणं विष्णुः तैः सार्धं युयुधे मुदा ।
नानायुधानि सङ्क्षिप्य तुमुलं भीमविक्रमम् ॥ २३॥

English
Viṣṇu fought a great battle with them by hurling many weapons and evincing boisterous display of his terrific exploits. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते भैरवाद्याश्च युयुधुस्तेन भूरिशः ।
नानास्त्राणि विमुञ्चन्तः सङ्कुद्धाः परमौजसा ॥ २४॥

English
Bhairava and others displayed their strength furiously by hurling several weapons and by fighting with him. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं तेषां रणं दृष्ट्वा हरिणातुलतेजसा ।
विनिवृत्य समागम्य तान्स्वयं युयुधे बली ॥ २५॥

English
Virabhadra saw their battle with Viṣṇu of unequalled splendour, returned and clashed with him in a great battle. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुर्महातेजाश्चक्रमुद्यम्य मूर्च्छितः ।
युयुधे भगवांस्तेन वीरभद्रेण माधवः ॥ २६॥

English
Then Viṣṇu of great brilliance lifted up his discus and fought with Virabhadra. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोः समभवद्युद्धं सुघोरं रोमहर्षणम् ।
महावीराब्धिपत्योस्तु नानास्त्रधरयोर्मुने ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, a terrible fight provoking harripilation took place between Mahābali and Varuṇa with various weapons. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोर्योगबलात्तस्य देहादेव सुदारुणाः ।
शङ्खचक्रगदाहस्ता असङ्ख्याताश्च जज्ञिरे ॥ २८॥

English
Thanks to the Yogic power of Viṣṇu, innumerable soldiers terrible and wielding conch, discus and mace in their hands emerged from his own body. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ते चापि युयुधुस्तेन वीरभद्रेण भाषता ।
विष्णुवत् बलवन्तो हि नानायुधधरा गणाः ॥ २९॥

English
They too fought against Virabhadra who continued to shout. These strong groups of warriors were as strong as Viṣṇu and had various weapons with them. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तान्सर्वानपि वीरोऽसौ नारायणसमप्रभान् ।
भस्मीचकार शूलेन हत्वा स्मृत्वा शिवं प्रभुम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Remembering Śiva, his lord and hitting them, who were as lustrous as Nārāyaṇa with his trident, he reduced them to ashes. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चोरसि तं विष्णुं लीलयैव रणाजिरे ।
जघान वीरभद्रो हि त्रिशूलेन महाबली ॥ ३१॥

English
The most powerful Virabhadra struck Viṣṇu in the chest playfully with his trident in the course of the battle. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन घातेन सहसा विहतः पुरुषोत्तमः ।
पपात च तदा भूमौ विसंज्ञोऽभून्मुने हरिः ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, hit suddenly by that blow, Viṣṇu Puruṣottama, fell unconscious on the ground. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जज्ञेऽद्भुतं तेजः प्रलयानलसन्निभम् ।
त्रैलोक्यदाहकं तीव्रं वीराणामपि भीकरम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Then arose a wonderful brilliance as terrible as the fire at the time of dissolution. It seemed to burn all the three worlds. It was severe and terrifying even to great heroes. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रोधरक्तेक्षणः श्रीमान् पुनरुत्थाय स प्रभुः ।
प्रहर्तुं चक्रमुद्यम्य ह्यतिष्ठत्पुरुषर्षभः ॥ ३४॥

English
That glorious lord, with eyes red by anger, got up again. The best of beings lifted up his discus and stood ready to strike. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य चक्रं महारौद्रं कालादित्यसमप्रभम् ।
व्यष्टम्भयददीनात्मा वीरभद्रः शिवः प्रभुः ॥ ३५॥

English
Virabhadra of no weak soul, nay, identical with lord Śiva, held his terrific discus luminous like black sun suspended and motionless. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
मुने शम्भोः प्रभावात्तु मायेशस्य महाप्रभोः ।
न चचाल हरेश्चक्रं करस्थं स्तम्भितं ध्रुवम् ॥ ३६॥

English
O sage, thanks to the power of Śiva, the great lord controlling Māyā, the Cakra held in the hand of Viṣṇu became stunned and motionless. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुर्गणेशेन वीरभद्रेण भाषता ।
अतिष्ठत् स्तम्भितस्तेन श‍ृङ्गवानिव निश्चलः ॥ ३७॥

English
Viṣṇu who was kept stunned by Virabhadra of eloquent words who was the lord of the Gaṇas, remained motionless like a mountain. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विष्णुः स्तम्भितो हि वीरभद्रेण नारद ।
यज्वोपमन्त्रणमनुं निःस्तम्भनकरं जपन् ॥ ३८॥

English
O Nārada, when he became benumbed and stunned, Viṣṇu repeated formulas for redemption from torpidity. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्तम्भननिर्मुक्तः शार्ङ्गधन्वा रमेश्वरः ।
शार्ङ्गं जग्राह स क्रुद्धः स्वधनुः सशरं मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, becoming freed from the stunned state, Viṣṇu became infuriated and took up his bow and arrows. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिभिश्च धर्षितं बाणैस्तेन शार्ङ्गधनुर्हरेः ।
वीरभद्रेण तत्तात त्रिधाभूत्तत्क्षणान्मुने ॥ ४०॥

English
O dear sage, Viṣṇu's bow was attacked with three arrows by Virabhadra whereupon it split into three in a trice. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुर्महावाण्या बोधितस्तं महागणम् ।
असह्यवर्चसं ज्ञात्वा ह्यन्तर्धातुं मनो दधे ॥ ४१॥

English
Then Viṣṇu was enlightened by the great voice that the great Gaṇas were invincible. He therefore thought of vanishing from the scene. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा च तत्सर्वमिदं भविष्यं
सतीकृतं दुष्प्रसहं परेषाम् ।
गताः स्वलोकं स्वगणान्वितास्तु
स्मृत्वा शिवं सर्वपतिं स्वतन्त्रम् ॥ ४२॥

English
On coming to know that all this was bound to happen brought about by Sati, so unbearable to all others, all of us went to our respective worlds after duly remembering Śiva, the independent lord of all. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

अचिन्तयं सुदुःखार्तो मया किं कार्यमद्य वै ॥ ४३॥

English
When I returned to Satyaloka the grief of my son afflicted me much. In my miserable plight I pondered over what shall be done by me. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing the disorder and utter destruction of the great sacrifice, the sacrifice itself being afraid assumed the form of a deer and fled. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Virabhadra seized it as it, was fleeing up the sky in the form of a deer and beheaded it. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English
Nandin plucked out the eyes of Bhaga who was felled over the ground with anger because it was he who winked at Dakṣa while cursing. (55.)

Sanskrit
विडम्बिता स्वधा तत्र सा स्वाहा दक्षिणा तथा ।
मन्त्रास्तन्त्रास्तथा चान्ये तत्रस्था गणनायकैः ॥ ५६॥

English
Svadhā, Svāhā, Dakṣiṇā Mantras and Tantras, all those who were there were molested and mortified by the leaders of Gaṇas. (56.)

Sanskrit
ववृषुस्ते पुरीषाणि वितानाग्नौ रुषा गणाः ।
अनिर्वाच्यं तदा चक्रुर्गणा वीरास्तमध्वरम् ॥ ५७॥

English
The Gaṇas furiously showered filth and rubbish on the sacrificial fire. The heroic Gaṇas made the sacrifice inexpressibly impure. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्वेद्यन्तरगतं निलीनं तद्भयाद् बलात् ।
आनिनाय समाज्ञाय वीरभद्रः स्वभूःसुतम् ॥ ५८॥

English
After coming to know that Dakṣa had hidden himself behind the altar due to his fright, Virabhadra dragged him out with force. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
कपोलेऽस्य गृहीत्वा तु खड्गेनोपहृतं शिरः ।
अभेद्यमभवत्तस्य तच्च योगप्रभावतः ॥ ५९॥

English
He was caught hold of by his cheeks, his head and was struck with the sword. Due to the yogic power of Dakṣa it could not be split. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
अभेद्यं तच्छिरो मत्वा शस्त्रास्त्रैश्च तु सर्वशः ।
करेण त्रोटयामास पद्भ्यामाक्रम्य चोरसि ॥ ६०॥

English
Thinking that his head could not be pierced or cut with weapons he kicked his chest with the foot and wrested the head with his hand. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छिरस्तस्य दुष्टस्य दक्षस्य हरवैरिणः ।
अग्निकुण्डे प्रचिक्षेप वीरभद्रो गणाग्रणीः ॥ ६१॥

English
Vīrabhadra the leader of the Gaṇas threw the head of the wicked Dakṣa, the enemy of Śiva, into the firepit. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
रेजे तदा वीरभद्रस्त्रिशूलं भ्रामयन्करे ।
क्रुद्धा रणाक्षसंवर्ताः प्रज्वाल्य पर्वतोपमाः ॥ ६२॥

English
Vīrabhadra whirling the trident in his hand looked splendid indeed. The angry Raṇākṣa and Saṃvarta looked like the blazing mountains. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
अनायासेन हत्वैतान् वीरभद्रस्ततोऽग्निना ।
ज्वालयामास सक्रोधो दीप्ताग्निः शलभानिव ॥ ६३॥

English
After killing them without difficulty Vīrabhadra in his fury burnt them in the fire like a blazing conflagration consuming moths. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रस्ततो दग्धान् दृष्ट्वा दक्षपुरोगमान् ।
अट्टाट्टहासमकरोत् पूरयंश्च जगत्त्रयम् ॥ ६४॥

English
Seeing Dakṣa and others entirely burnt, he laughed boisterously filling the three worlds with the sound. (64.)

English
He was surrounded by heroic glory. Then a divine shower of flowers originating from the celestial park fell over Vīrabhadra accompanied by his Gaṇas. (95.)

Sanskrit

English
Cool breezes blew gently fragrant and pleasing. Divine drums sounded simultaneously. (66.)

Sanskrit
कैलासं स ययौ वीरः कृतकार्यस्ततः परम् ।
विनाशितदृढध्वान्तो भानुमानिव सत्वरम् ॥ ६७॥

English
The hero who had accomplished his duties went to Kailāsa quickly like the sun who quells darkness. (67.)

Sanskrit

English
On seeing Vīrabhadra who had fulfilled his task, lord Śiva was pleased and he made him the presiding officer of his Gaṇas. (68.)
