### **CHAPTER THIRTYEIGHT - *(The dialogue between Kṣuva and Dadhīca)***

---

Sanskrit
३८

सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य विधेरमितधीमतः ।
पप्रच्छ नारदः प्रीत्या विस्मितस्तं द्विजोत्तमः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—*  After hearing these words of Brahmā of immeasurable intellect, Nārada the brahmin was surprised and he lovingly asked him thus. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
शिवं विहाय दक्षस्य सुरैर्यज्ञं हरिर्गतः ।
हेतुना केन तद् ब्रूहि यत्रावज्ञाभवत्ततः ॥ २॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  Please tell me for what reason did Viṣṇu go to the sacrifice of Dakṣa along with the devas but leaving off Śiva, for there so much of ignominy was in store for him ? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
जानाति किं स शम्भुं नो हरिः प्रलयविक्रमम् ।
रणं कथं च कृतवान् तद्गणैरबुधो यथा ॥ ३॥

English
Does not Viṣṇu know Śiva who has the power of dissolution ? Why did he fight with his Gaṇas like a foolish insensible man ? (3.)

---

Sanskrit
एष मे संशयो भूयांस्तं छिन्धि करुणानिधे ।
चरितं ब्रूहि शम्भोस्तु चित्तोत्साहकरं प्रभो ॥ ४॥

English
O Merciful one, this is my great doubt. Please clarify it O lord, narrate the story of Śiva to us that heightens the enthusiasm in our mind. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
द्विजवर्य श‍ृणु प्रीत्या चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
यत्पृच्छते कुर्वतश्च सर्वसंशयहारकम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O excellent brahmin, listen with pleasure to the story of the moon-crested lord which dispels our doubts. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीचस्य मुनेः शापाद् भ्रष्टज्ञानो हरिः पुरा ।
सामरो दक्षयज्ञं वै गतः क्षुवसहायकृत् ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, formerly Viṣṇu lost his knowledge by the curse of Dadhīca. He, therefore, went to Dakṣa's sacrifice along with the devas in order to help Kṣuva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
किमर्थं शप्तवान्विष्णुं दधीचो मुनिसत्तमः ।
कोऽपकारः कृतस्तस्य हरिणा तत्सहायिना ॥ ७॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  Why did the excellent sage Dadhīca curse Viṣṇu ? What harm was done by Viṣṇu to Dadhīca by helping Kṣuva ? (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
समुत्पन्नो महातेजा राजा क्षुव इति स्मृतः ।
अभून्मित्रं दधीचस्य मुनीन्द्रस्य महाप्रभोः ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  There was a king of great splendour named Kṣuva. He was the friend of Dadhīca, the sage of very great potentiality. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चिरात्तपःप्रसङ्गाद्वै वादः क्षुवदधीचयोः ।
महानर्थकरः ख्यातस्त्रिलोकेष्वभवत्पुरा ॥ ९॥

English
Formerly a great dispute, well known in the three worlds, took place between Kṣuva and Dadhīca in the context of their penance. This caused great disaster. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र त्रिवर्णतः श्रेष्ठो विप्र एव न संशयः ।
इति प्राह दधीचो हि शिवभक्तस्तु वेदवित् ॥ १०॥

English
Dadhīca, a great devotee of Śiva and a Vedic scholar said—A brahmin alone is the noble person in the three higher Varṇas. There is no doubt. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य दधीचस्य महामुने ।
क्षुवः प्राहेति नृपतिः श्रीमदेन विमोहितः ॥ ११॥

English
O great sage, on hearing the words of Dadhīca, the king Kṣuva deluded by his pride due to wealth and glory said thus :— (11.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुव उवाच ।
अष्टानां लोकपालानां वपुर्धारयते नृपः ।
तस्मान्नृपो वरिष्ठो हि वर्णाश्रमपतिः प्रभुः ॥ १२॥

English
*Kṣuva said :—*  A king holds in his body parts of the eight guardians of the worlds. Hence a king is the most excellent lord of all Varṇas and Āśramas. He is the supreme lord.$^{331}$ (12.)

[fn 331. The king embodies the essence of eight Lokapālas and as such he is a divine being. Cp. Manu. Ch. 7. He is authorised to maintain the system of four Varṇas and Āśramas but none of the sacred texts—Śrutis and Smṛtis—empowers him to rule over the Brāhmaṇa Varṇa. Cp. G. Dh. S. राजा सर्वस्येष्टे ब्राह्मणवर्जम् ।]
---

Sanskrit
सर्वदेवमयो राजा श्रुतिः प्राहेति तत्परा ।
महती देवता या सा सोऽहमेव ततो मुने ॥ १३॥

English
The Vedas say clearly that the king consists of all devas. Hence, O sage, I am that great deity. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्विप्राद्वरो राजा च्यावनेय विचार्यताम् ।
नावमन्तव्य एवातः पूज्योऽहं सर्वथा त्वया ॥ १४॥

English
Hence a king is nobler than a brahmin. Take the example of Cyavana. Hence I am not to be disrespected by you. I am to be honoured always. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रुत्वा तथा मतं तस्य क्षुवस्य मुनिसत्तमः ।
श्रुतिस्मृतिविरुद्धं तं चुकोपातीव भार्गवः ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing that opinion of Kṣuva, O excellent sage, which went contrary to Vedas and Smṛtis, Śukra became angry. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ क्रुद्धो महातेजा गौरवाच्चात्मनो मुने ।
अताडयत्क्षुवं मूर्ध्नि दधीचो वाममुष्टितः ॥ १६॥

English
Dadhīca of great splendour became angry because it affected his prestige, O sage. With his left fist he hit Kṣuva on his head. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्रेण तं च चिच्छेद दधीचं ताडितः क्षुवः ।
जगर्जातीव सङ्क्रुद्धो ब्रह्माण्डाधिपतिः कुधीः ॥ १७॥

English
Kṣuva on being hit struck Dadhīca with the thunderbolt. The king of evil mind became angry and roared loudly. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
पपात भूमौ निहतो तेन वज्रेण भार्गवः ।
शुक्रं सस्मार क्षुवकृद्भार्गवस्य कुलन्धरः ॥ १८॥

English
Dadhīca on being struck by the thunderbolt remembered Śukra who was one of his ancestors. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्रोऽथ सन्धयामास ताडितं च क्षुवेन तु ।
योगी दधीचस्य तदा देहमागत्य स द्रुतम् ॥ १९॥

English
Śukra of Yogic powers approached the body of Dadhīca who was hit by Kṣuva and rejoined the broken limbs. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्धाय पूर्ववद्देहं दधीचस्याह भार्गवः ।
शिवभक्ताग्रणीर्मृत्यञ्जयविद्याप्रवर्तकः ॥ २०॥

English
After rejoining the organs as before of Dadhīca, Śukra a leading devotee of Śiva, the initiator of Mṛtyuñjaya-vidyā said. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्र उवाच ।
दधीच तात सम्पूज्य शिवं सर्वेश्वरं प्रभुम् ।
महामृत्युञ्जयं मन्त्रं श्रौतमग्र्यं वदामि ते ॥ २१॥

English
*Śukra said :—*  Dear Dadhīca, after worshipping Śiva the lord of everyone, I am going to tell you the highly potential Vedic mantra Mahāmṛtyuñjaya. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे त्रैलोक्यं पितरं प्रभुम् ।
त्रिमण्डलस्य पितरं त्रिगुणस्य महेश्वरम् ॥ २२॥

English
We worship the three-eyed lord Śiva, the lord of the three worlds, the father of the three spheres, the lord of the three guṇas. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रितत्त्वस्य त्रिवह्नेश्च त्रिधाभूतस्य सर्वतः ।
त्रिदिवस्य त्रिबाहोश्च त्रिधाभूतस्य सर्वतः ॥ २३॥

त्रिदेवस्य महादेवः सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्द्धनम् ।
सर्वभूतेषु सर्वत्र त्रिगुणेषु कृतौ यथा ॥ २४॥

इन्द्रियेषु तथान्येषु देवेषु च गणेषु च ।
पुष्पे सुगन्धिवत्सूरः सुगन्धिरमरेश्वरः ॥ २५॥

English
Lord Śiva is the essence, the fragrance of the three Tattvas, three fires, of every thing that is trichotomised, of the three worlds, of the three arms and of the trinity. He is the nourisher. In all living beings, everywhere, in the three Guṇas, in the creation, in the sense-organs, in the devas and Gaṇas, he is the essence as the fragrance in a flower. He is the lord of devas. (23-25.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्टिश्च प्रकृतेर्यस्मात्पुरुषाद् वै द्विजोत्तम ।
महदादिविशेषान्तविकल्पश्चापि सुव्रत ॥ २६॥

विष्णोः पितामहस्यापि मुनीनां च महामुने ।
इन्द्रियाणां च देवानां तस्माद् वै पुष्टिवर्धनः ॥ २७॥

English
O excellent brahmin of good rites, He is called the nourisher because it is from Him the supreme Puruṣa Śiva that the Prakṛti, the different Tattvas from Mahat to the different Indriyas, Viṣṇu, Brahmā, the sages, Indra and the devas derive their nourishment. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
तं देवममृतं रुद्रं कर्मणा तपसापि वा ।
स्वाध्यायेन च योगेन ध्यानेन च प्रजायते ॥ २८॥

सत्येनान्येन सूक्ष्माग्रान्मृत्युपाशाद्भवः स्वयम् ।
बन्धमोक्षकरो यस्मादुर्वारुकमिव प्रभुः ॥ २९॥

English
Worship that immortal deity Śiva with sacred rites, penance, self-study of the Vedas, yogic practices, meditation, observance of truth and other means. You will be freed from the noose of Yama. The lord is the cause of both bondage and salvation. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
मृतसञ्जीवनीमन्त्रो मम सर्वोत्तमः स्मृतः ।
एवं जपपरः प्रीत्या नियमेन शिवं स्मरन् ॥ ३०॥

जप्त्वा हुत्वाभिमन्त्र्यैवं जलं पिब दिवानिशम् ।
शिवस्य सन्निधौ ध्यात्वा नास्ति मृत्युभयं क्वचित् ॥ ३१॥

English
In my opinion this Mṛtasañjīvanī mantra is the most excellent of all. Repeat these mantras regularly remembering Śiva with devotion. After Japa, Homa and recitation of the mantras observe fast, but you can drink water day and night. If the meditation is conducted in the presence of Śiva there is no fear of death from anywhere. (30-31.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा न्यासादिकं सर्वं सम्पूज्य विधिवच्छिवम् ।
संविधायेदं निर्व्यग्रः शङ्करं भक्तवत्सलम् ॥ ३२॥

ध्यानमस्य प्रवक्ष्यामि यथा ध्यात्वा जपेन्मनुम् ।
सिद्धमन्त्रो भवेद्धीमान् यावच्छम्भुप्रभावतः ॥ ३३॥

English
Nyāsa and other ritualistic rites shall be observed. Śiva shall be worshipped duly. Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees shall be propitiated. I shall also mention the observance of meditation. It is after this meditation that the mantra shall be repeated as long as the purpose is realised due to Śiva's power. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
हस्ताम्भोजयुगस्थकुम्भयुगलादुद्धृत्य तोयं शिरः
सिञ्चन्तं करयोर्युगेन दधतं स्वाङ्के सकुम्भौ करौ ।
अक्षस्रङ्मृगहस्तमम्बुजगतं मूर्धस्थचन्द्रस्रवत्-
पीयूषार्द्रतनुं भजे सगिरिजं त्र्यक्षं च मृत्युञ्जयम् ॥ ३४॥

English
I worship the three-eyed Lord Śiva, the conqueror of death who is accompanied by (Pārvatī); who pours water on his head from two vessels held in his lotus-like hands, by means of the other pair of hands; who has placed the two hands with the pots on the lap; who usually holds in his hands the Rudrākṣa garland and a deer and whose body is rendered cool and wet by the nectar exuding from the moon worn on the head. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
उपदिश्येति शुक्रः तं दधीचिं मुनिसत्तमम् ।
स्वस्थानमगमत्तात संस्मरन् शङ्करं प्रभुम् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After instructing the excellent sage Dadhīca thus and remembering lord Śiva, O dear, Śukra returned to his abode. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा दधीचो हि महामुनिः ।
वनं जगाम तपसे महाप्रीत्या शिवं स्मरन् ॥ ३६॥

English
On hearing his words, the great sage Dadhīca went to the forest for penance thinking upon Śiva with great pleasure. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Going there he performed penance repeating$^{332}$ the mantra named Mahāmṛtyuñjaya in accordance with the rules and remembering Śiva with great pleasure. (37.)

[fn 332. Lord Śiva is called “the Conqueror of Death”. The mantra for the propitiation of that God for the conquest of Death is also called “the Conqueror of Death”. The mantra runs as follows : “Tryambakam yajāmahe sugandhim puṣṭivardhanam. Urvārukamiva bandhanān mṛtyormukṣiya māmṛtāt.”]
---

Sanskrit
तन्मनुं सुचिरं जप्त्वा तपसाराध्य शङ्करम् ।
शिवं सन्तोषयामास महामृत्युञ्जयं हि सः ॥ ३८॥

English
After repeating the mantra for a long time and propitiating Śiva with penance, he delighted Śiva named Mahāmṛtyuñjaya—the conqueror of great death. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुः प्रसन्नात्मा तज्जपाद्भक्तवत्सलः ।

English
O great sage, Śiva who is favourably disposed towards his devotees became delighted by that Japa and appeared before him lovingly. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing his lord Śiva, the great sage was highly pleased. After bowing to him with devotion and in accordance with rules he eulogised him with palms joined in reverence. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रीत्या शिवस्तात प्रसन्नश्च्यावनिं मुने ।
वरं ब्रूहीति स प्राह सुप्रसन्नेन चेतसा ॥ ४१॥

English
O dear one, O sage, Śiva told the son of Cyavana (Dadhīca)—“Please tell me what boon (you require)” (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On hearing the words of Śiva, Dadhīca, the most excellent devotee, spoke to Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees, with palms joined in reverence and a formal salutation. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

देवदेव महादेव मह्यं देहि वरत्रयम् ।

English
*Dadhīca said :—*  O great lord, lord of Devas, please give me three boons viz. adamantine bones, impossibility of being killed and absence of distress. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words mentioned by him, the delightful great lord gave Dadhīca the three boons saying “so be it”. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

क्षुवस्थानं जगामाशु वेदमार्गे प्रतिष्ठितः ॥ ४५॥

English
After securing the three boons from Śiva, the great sage, who strictly followed the Vedic path, was delighted and went immediately to kṣuva’s abode. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्यावध्यत्वमुग्रात्स वज्रास्थित्वमदीनताम् ।
अताडयच्च राजेन्द्रं पादमूलेन मूर्धनि ॥ ४६॥

English
Having secured indestructibility, adamantine bones and absence of distress from Śiva, he kicked the king on the head with the root of his foot. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

क्रोधं कृत्वा विशेषेण विष्णुगौरवगर्वितः ॥ ४७॥

English
Kṣuva, the king who was haughty by the favour of Viṣṇu, became angry and hit Dadhīca on his chest with his thunderbolt. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रभावात्परमेशस्य धातृपुत्रो विसिस्मिये ॥ ४८॥

English
The thunderbolt was incompetent to destroy Dadhīca the noble-souled, thanks to the power of lord Śiva. The son of the creator (Kṣuva) was greatly surprised. (48.)

दृष्ट्वाप्यवध्यत्वमदीनतां च
वज्रस्य चात्यन्तपरप्रभावम् ।
क्षुवो दधीचस्य मुनीश्वरस्य
विसिस्मिये चेतसि धातृपुत्रः ॥ ४९॥

English
On thus seeing the indestructibility, absence of distress and adamantine bones of Dadhīca the great sage, Kṣuva, the son of the creator, became surprised at heart. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
आराधयामास हरिं मुकुन्द-
मिन्द्रानुजं काननमाशु गत्वा ।
प्रपन्नपालश्च पराजितो हि
दधीचमृत्युञ्जयसेवकेन ॥ ५०॥

English
Defeated thus by Dadhīca who was the server of Mṛtyuñjaya and who resorted to Śiva, Kṣuva went to the forest immediately and propitiated Viṣṇu, the younger brother of Indra. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजया तस्य सन्तुष्टो भगवान् मधुसूदनः ।
प्रददौ दर्शनं तस्मै दिव्यं वै गरुडध्वजः ॥ ५१॥

English
The lord Viṣṇu satisfied with his worship revealed himself to him in the divine form of the Garuḍa-bannered deity. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्येन दर्शनेनैव दृष्ट्वा देवं जनार्दनम् ।
तुष्टाव वाग्भिरिष्टाभिः प्रणम्य गरुडध्वजम् ॥ ५२॥

English
On seeing the lord with his divine vision, he bowed and eulogised him with pleasing words. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पूज्य चैवं त्रिदशेश्वराद्यैः
स्तुत्वा स्तुतं देवमजेयमीशम् ।
विज्ञापयामास निरीक्ष्य भक्त्या
जनार्दनाय प्रणिपत्य मूर्ध्ना ॥ ५३॥

English
After worshipping and eulogising the invincible lord, lauded by Indra and others, he devoutly bowed down his head and submitted thus to him. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ।
भगवन् ब्राह्मणः कश्चिद्दधीच इति विश्रुतः ।
धर्मवेत्ता विनीतात्मा सखा मम पुराभवत् ॥ ५४॥

English
# *The king said :—*  O Holy lord, formerly I had a humble friend, a certain brahmin renowned as Dadhīca who knew all virtuous acts. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
अवध्यः सर्वदा सर्वैः शङ्करस्य प्रभावतः ।
तमाराध्य महादेवं मृत्युञ्जयमनामयम् ॥ ५५॥

English
He cannot be killed by anyone at any time due to the power of Śiva. He got this boon after propitiating Śiva, the conqueror of death who is free from sickness. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
सावज्ञं वामपादेन मम मूर्ध्नि सदस्यपि ।
ताडयामास वेगेन स दधीचो महातपाः ॥ ५६॥

English
In the open assembly, that Dadhīca of great penance, contemptuously kicked me with his left leg on my head. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच मां च गर्वेण न बिभेमीति सर्वतः ।
मृत्युञ्जयाप्तसुवरो गर्वितो ह्यतुलं हरे ॥ ५७॥

English
O Viṣṇu, he told me haughtily—“I am not afraid of anybody”. Having obtained favours from Mṛtyuñjaya he is incomparably haughty. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ज्ञात्वा दधीचस्य ह्यवध्यत्वं महात्मनः ।
सस्मारास्य महेशस्य प्रभावमतुलं हरिः ॥ ५८॥

English
# *Brahmā said :—*  On coming to know of the indestructibility of Dadhīca of noble soul, Viṣṇu thought upon the unrivalled power of lord Śiva. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्मृत्वा हरिः प्राह क्षुवं विधिसुतं द्रुतम् ।
विप्राणां नास्ति राजेन्द्र भयमण्वपि कुत्रचित् ॥ ५९॥

English
Having thus remembered Śiva, Viṣṇu told Kṣuva the son of Brahmā immediately— “O noble king, brahmins need not be afraid of anything even a bit.” (59.)

---

Sanskrit
विशेषाद् रुद्रभक्तानां भयं नास्ति च भूपते ।
दुःखं करोति विप्रस्य शापार्थं स सुरस्य मे ॥ ६०॥

English
O king, especially the devotees of Śiva have no fear at all. He would curse me along with the devas and trouble me. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
भविता तस्य शापेन दक्षयज्ञे सुरेश्वरात् ।
विनाशो मम राजेन्द्र पुनरुत्थानमेव च ॥ ६१॥

English
O noble king, my destruction will also take place from the leader of Gaṇas at Dakṣa’s sacrifice due to his curse. Of course I will be rising up again. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्समेत्य राजेन्द्र सर्वयज्ञो न भूयते ।
करोमि यत्नं राजेन्द्र दधीचविजयाय ते ॥ ६२॥

English
O leader of kings, the completion of the sacrifice will not take place. Of course O king, I shall strive for your victory over Dadhīca. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा वाक्यं क्षुवः प्राह तथास्त्विति हरेर्नृपः ।
तस्थौ तत्रैव तत्प्रीत्या तत्कामोत्सुकमानसः ॥ ६३॥

English
On hearing the words of Viṣṇu, the king Kṣuva said—“So be it”. He remained there alone, eager to achieve his desire. (63.)
