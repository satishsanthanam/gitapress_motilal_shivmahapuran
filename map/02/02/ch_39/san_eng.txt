### **CHAPTER THIRTY-NINE - *(Description of the fight between Viṣṇu and Dadhīca)***

---

Sanskrit
३९

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
क्षुवस्य हितकृत्येन दधीचस्याश्रमं ययौ ।
विप्ररूपमथास्थाय भगवान् भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  In order to do something beneficent to Kṣuva, the lord Viṣṇu who is favourably disposed to his devotees assumed the form of a brahmin and went to the hermitage of Dadhīca (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीचं प्राह विप्रर्षिमभिवन्द्य जगद्गुरुः ।
क्षुवकार्यार्थमुद्युक्तः शैवेन्द्रं छलमाश्रितः ॥ २॥

English
The Preceptor of the Universe Viṣṇu resorting to deception for the realisation of Kṣuva’s purpose, bowed to the brahminical sage Dadhīca, the leader of devotees of Śiva, and spoke these words. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
भो भो दधीच विप्रर्षे भवार्चनरताव्यय ।
वरमेकं वृणे त्वत्तस्तद्भवान् दातुमर्हति ॥ ३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O Dadhīca, O brahminical sage, engrossed in the worship of Śiva, O unchanging one, I am soliciting a boon from you, which you will please grant. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
याचितो देवदेवेन दधीचः शैवसत्तमः ॥

क्षुवकार्यार्थिना शीघ्रं जगाद वचनं हरिम् ॥ ४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Dadhīca, the most excellent of the devotees of Śiva, thus requested by the lord of Devas on behalf of Kṣuva, immediately replied thus. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
ज्ञातं तवेप्सितं विप्र क्षुवकार्यार्थमागतः ।
भगवान् विप्ररूपेण मायी त्वमसि वै हरिः ॥ ५॥

English
*Dadhīca said :—*  O brahmin, your purpose has been understood by me. You have come here on behalf of Kṣuva. You are the lord Viṣṇu in the form of a brahmin due to your illusion. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतं भविष्यं देवेश वर्तमानं जनार्दन ।
ज्ञानं प्रसादाद् रुद्रस्य सदा त्रैकालिकं मम ॥ ६॥

English
O lord of Devas, O Viṣṇu, thanks to the grace of Śiva, the past, future and present, everything in the three periods of time is known to me. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां जानेऽहं हरिं विष्णुं द्विजत्वं त्यज सुव्रत ।
आराधितोऽसि भूपेन क्षुवेण खलबुद्धिना ॥ ७॥

English
I know you to be Viṣṇu, O observer of good rites, leave off your disguise of brahminhood. You have been propitiated by king Kṣuva of wicked intellect. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
जाने तवैव भगवन् भक्तवत्सलतां हरे ।
छलं त्यज स्वरूपं हि स्वीकुरु स्मर शङ्करम् ॥ ८॥

English
O lord Viṣṇu, I know your favouritism towards your devotees. Cast off this deception. Assume your own real form. Remember Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्ति चेत्कस्यचिद्भीतिर्भवार्चनरतस्य मे ।
वक्तुमर्हसि यत्नेन सत्यधारणपूर्वकम् ॥ ९॥

English
If any one is afraid of me in view of my being engrossed in the worship of Śiva, you will please tell me bearing in mind the true state of things. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
वदामि न मृषा क्वापि शिवस्मरणसक्तधीः ।
न बिभेमि जगत्यस्मिन्देवदैत्यादिकादपि ॥ १०॥

English
With my mind drawn to the memory of Śiva, I never tell a lie. I am not afraid of devas or demons in the whole universe. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
भयं दधीच सर्वत्र नष्टं च तव सुव्रत ।
भवार्चनरतो यस्माद्भवान्सर्वज्ञ एव च ॥ ११॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “O Dadhīca of good rites, all sorts of fear are at an end for you. Since you are actively engaged in the worship of Śiva you are omniscient. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
बिभेमीति सकृद्वक्तुमर्हसि त्वं नमस्तव ।
नियोगान्मम राजेन्द्र क्षुवात् प्रतिसहादहम् ॥ १२॥

English
You will please say for once that you are afraid. Obeisance to you. At my behest please bear with Kṣuva the leader of kings”. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं श्रुत्वापि तद्वाक्यं विष्णोः स तु महामुनिः ।
विहस्य निर्भयः प्राह दधीचः शैवसत्तमः ॥ १३॥

English
*Brahmā said :*  Even after hearing this request of Viṣṇu, the great sage Dadhīca, the most excellent of the devotees of Śiva, laughed and said fearlessly:— (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
न बिभेमि सदा क्वापि कुतश्चिदपि किञ्चन ।
प्रभावाद्देवदेवस्य शम्भोः साक्षात्पिनाकिनः ॥ १४॥

English
*Dadhīca said :—*  “I am not at all afraid of anything anywhere at any time, thanks to the power of the Pināka-wielding Śiva, the lord of Devas". (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततस्तस्य मुनेः श्रुत्वा वचनं कुपितो हरिः ।
चक्रमुद्यम्य सन्तस्थौ दिधक्षुर्मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ १५॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Then, on hearing the words of the sage, Viṣṇu became angry. He lifted his discus and stood as if he would burn the excellent sage. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अभवत्कुण्ठितं तत्र विप्रे चक्रं सुदारुणम् ।
प्रभावाच्च तदीशस्य नृपतेः सन्निधावपि ॥ १६॥

English
When hurled upon the brahmin, the terrible discus became blunted in the presence of the king, thanks to the power of Śiva. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तं कुण्ठितास्यं तच्चक्रं विष्णुं जगाद ह ।
दधीचः सस्मितं साक्षात्सदसद्व्यक्तिकारणम् ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing the discus blunted at the edges, Dadhīca smilingly spoke to Viṣṇu, the cause of discrimination between the existent and the non-existent. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
भगवन् भवता लब्धं पुरातीव सुदारुणम् ।
सुदर्शनमिति ख्यातं चक्रं विष्णोः प्रयत्नतः ।
भवस्य तच्छुभं चक्रं न जिघांसति मामिह ॥ १८॥

English
Dadhīca said :—  O lord Viṣṇu, formerly the famous terrible discus Sudarśana³³³ was secured by you with great effort. (18.)

[fn 333. It is a mythical weapon causing infallible destruction. It is so called because it is presided over by Brahmā.]
---

Sanskrit
भगवानथ क्रुद्धोऽस्मै सर्वास्त्राणि क्रमाद्धरिः ।
ब्रह्मास्त्राद्यैः शरैश्चास्त्रैः प्रयत्नं कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ १९॥

English
That is the auspicious discus of Śiva. It does not kill me here. O lord Viṣṇu, furious at me you can endeavour to hurl all the weapons such as Brahmāstra³³³ at me one by one. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
स तस्य वचनं श्रुत्वा दृष्ट्वा निर्वीर्यमानुषम् ।
ससर्जाथ क्रुधा तस्मै सर्वास्त्राणि क्रमाद्धरिः ॥ २०॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Viṣṇu, on hearing his words, thought that he was only a man devoid of virility and hence hurled at him all the weapons.

21 Thereupon the devas of crooked intellect rendered help to Viṣṇu who was engaged in fighting with a single brahmin. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
चिक्षिपुः स्वानि स्वान्याशु शस्त्राण्यस्त्राणि सर्वतः ।
दधीचोपरि वेगेन शक्राद्या हरिपाक्षिकाः ॥ २२॥

English
Indra and others, partisans of Viṣṇu, hurled with force their own different weapons from all round on Dadhīca. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कुशमुष्टिमथादाय दधीचः संस्मरन् शिवम् ।
ससर्ज सर्वदेवेभ्यो वज्रास्थिः सर्वतो वशी ॥ २३॥

English
Taking a fistful of Kuśa grass and remembering Śiva, Dadhīca of adamantine bones and self-control discharged it against all the Gods. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करस्य प्रभावात्तु कुशमुष्टिर्मुनेर्हि सा ।
दिव्यं त्रिशूलमभवत् कालाग्निसदृशं मुने ॥ २४॥

English
O sage, thanks to the power of Śiva, the fistful Kuśa grass of the sage became the divine trident equal in potentiality to the fire of the god of death. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्धुं देवान् मतिं चक्रे सायुधान् सशिखं च तत् ।
प्रज्वलत्सर्वतः शैवं युगान्ताग्न्यधिकप्रभम् ॥ २५॥

English
That trident of Śaiva nature blazing around with the lustre exceeding the fire at the close of the Yugas wanted to burn the armed Gods. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणेन्द्रमुख्यैस्तु देवैः क्षिप्तानि यानि च ।
आयुधानि समस्तानि प्रणेमुस्त्रिशिखं च तत् ॥ २६॥

English
All the weapons hurled by the devas of whom Nārāyaṇa and Indra were the most important, bowed down in reverence to that trident. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च दुद्रुवुः सर्वे ध्वस्तवीर्या दिवौकसः ।
तस्थौ तत्र हरिर्भीतः केवलं मायिनां वरः ॥ २७॥

English
Bereft of their virility, the heaven-dwelling devas fled. Viṣṇu, alone, the foremost of those who make use of Māyā, remained there but he was afraid. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ससर्ज भगवान् विष्णुः स्वदेहात्पुरुषोत्तमः ।
आत्मनः सदृशान् दिव्यान् लक्षलक्षायुतान् गणान् ॥ २८॥

English
Viṣṇu, the lord, created out of his body millions of divine beings like himself. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ते चापि युयुधुस्तत्र वीरा विष्णुगणास्ततः ।
मुनिनैकेन देवर्षे दधीचेन शिवात्मना ॥ २९॥

English
O celestial sage, those Viṣṇuganas of heroic power fought with the single sage Dadhīca identical with Śiva. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विष्णुगणांस्तान्वै नियुध्य बहुशो रणे ।
ददाह सहसा सर्वान् दधीचः शैवसत्तमः ॥ ३०॥

English
Then withstanding the entire hosts of Viṣṇuganas, Dadhīca, the most excellent of the devotees of Śiva, burnt them all in the battle. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तद्विस्मयार्थाय दधीचस्य मुनेर्हरिः ।
विश्वमूर्तिरभूच्छीघ्रं महामायाविशारदः ॥ ३१॥

English
Then, in order to confound the sage Dadhīca, Viṣṇu, clever in the use of illusion, became multi-formed. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य देहे हरेः साक्षादपश्यद् द्विजसत्तमः ।
दधीचो देवतादीनां जीवानां च सहस्रकम् ॥ ३२॥

English
In that body of Viṣṇu, the most excellent brahmin Dadhīca saw thousands of devas and living beings. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतानां कोटयश्चैव गणानां कोटयस्तथा ।
अण्डानां कोटयश्चैव विश्वमूर्तेस्तनौ तदा ॥ ३३॥

English
There were crores of Bhūtas, crores of Gaṇas and crores of universes in the body of multiformed Viṣṇu then. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वैतदखिलं तत्र च्यावनिः सततं तदा ।
विष्णुमाह जगन्नाथं जगत्स्तवमजं विभुम् ॥ ३४॥

English
On seeing all things spread there, Dadhīca, the son of Cyavana spoke to Viṣṇu, the all-pervading, unborn, lord of the universe, eulogised by the whole universe. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
मायां त्यज महाबाहो प्रतिभासो विचारतः ।
विज्ञातानि सहस्राणि दुर्विज्ञेयानि माधव ॥ ३५॥

English
*Dadhīca said:—*  O long-armed one, cast-off this illusion. On consideration, this is only a sham appearance. O Viṣṇu, thousands of inscrutable things are known to me also. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
मयि पश्य जगत्सर्वं त्वया युक्तमतन्द्रितः ।
ब्रह्माणं च तथा रुद्रं दिव्यां दृष्टिं ददामि ते ॥ ३६॥

English
I shall give you a divine vision. You will see in me the entire universe including you, Brahmā and Rudra. You shall then be alert. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा दर्शयामास स्वतनौ निखिलं मुनिः ।
ब्रह्माण्डं च्यावनिः शम्भुतेजसा पूर्णदेहकः ॥ ३७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying so, the saintly son of Cyavana, of body fully infused with the brilliance of Śiva, showed the entire universe in his body. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाह विष्णुं देवेशं दधीचः शैवसत्तमः ।
संस्मरन् शङ्करं चित्ते विहसन् विभयः सुधीः ॥ ३८॥

English
Remembering Śiva in his mind and laughing fearlessly, the intelligent Dadhīca, the most excellent of the devotees of Śiva spoke to Viṣṇu, the lord of Devas, thus. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
मायया त्वनया किं वा मन्त्रशक्त्याथ वा हरे ।
सत्कामनामिमां कृत्वा योद्धुमर्हसि यत्नतः ॥ ३९॥

English
*Dadhīca said :—*  O Viṣṇu, of what avail is this illusion of yours or the power of Mantra. You shall give up this illusion and fight in a straight manner. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतच्छुत्वा मुनेस्तस्य वचनं निर्भयस्तदा ।
शम्भुतेजोमयं विष्णुः चुकोपातीव तं मुनिम् ॥ ४०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of the sage, the fearless Viṣṇu became angry with the sage who had been infused with the brilliance of Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च दुद्रुवुर्भूयो देवं नारायणं च तम् ।
योद्धुकामाश्च मुनिना दधीचेन प्रतापिना ॥ ४१॥

English
The gods also rushed to the aid of lord Viṣṇu who was desirous of fighting with the sage Dadhīca of great valour. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्रागमन्मत्सङ्गमः क्षुवः ।
अवारयन्तं निश्चेष्टं पद्मयोनिं हरिं सुरान् ॥ ४२॥

निशम्य वचनं मे हि ब्राह्मणो न विनिर्जितः ।
जगाम निकटं तस्य प्रणनाम मुनिं हरिः ॥ ४३॥

English
In the meantime, Kṣuva of noble contact, came there. He prevented the motionless Brahmā, Viṣṇu and the gods from fighting. Even after hearing my words, the defeated Viṣṇu did not go near the sage, nor bowed to her. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुवो दीनतरो भूत्वा गत्वा तत्र मुनीश्वरम् ।
दधीचमभिवाद्यैव प्रार्थयामास विक्लवः ॥ ४४॥

English
Kṣuva who became agitated and distressed went near Dadhīca the great sage, bowed to him and requested thus. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुव उवाच ।
प्रसीद मुनिशार्दूल शिवभक्तशिरोमणे ।
प्रसीद परमेशान दुर्लक्ष्यो दुर्जनैः सदा ॥ ४५॥

English
*Kṣuva said :—*  O leader of sages, foremost of the devotees of Śiva, be pleased. Be pleased with Lakṣmī's consort who is difficult to be perceived by the wicked people. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य राज्ञः सुरगणस्य हि ।
अनुजग्राह तं विप्रो दधीचस्तपसां निधिः ॥ ४६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of the king on behalf of the devas, the brahmin Dadhīca, the storehouse of penance, blessed him. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दृष्ट्वा रमेशादीन् क्रोधविह्वलितो मुनिः ।
हृदि स्मृत्वा शिवं विष्णुं शशाप च सुरानपि ॥ ४७॥

English
Then, on seeing the lord of Lakṣmī Viṣṇu and others, the sage was angry. Remembering Śiva, he cursed Viṣṇu and the devas. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीच उवाच ।
रुद्रकोपाग्निना देवाः सदेवेन्द्रा मुनीश्वराः ।
ध्वस्ता भवन्तु देवेन विष्णुना च समं गणैः ॥ ४८॥

English
*Dadhīca said :—*  Let the gods with Indra, the sages and the lord Viṣṇu be burnt in the fire of the anger of Rudra. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं शप्त्वा सुरान् प्रेक्ष्य क्षुवमाह ततो मुनिः ।
देवैश्च पूज्यो राजेन्द्र नृपैश्चैव द्विजोत्तमः ॥ ४९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After cursing them he looked at Kṣuva$^{334}$ and said—“O leader of kings, a brahmin is worthy of being respected by devas, kings and the best of other castes. (49.)

[fn 334. The legend upholds the supremacy of the Brāhmaṇa over the other caste.]
