### **CHAPTER FORTY - *(Journey to Kailāsa and the vision of Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
४०

नारद उवाच
विधे विधे महाप्राज्ञ शैवतत्त्वप्रदर्शक ।
श्राविता रमणीप्राया शिवलीला महाद्भुता ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O Brahmā of great intellect, who are the guide for Śiva cult, you have narrated to me the wonderfully beautiful story of Śiva’s sport. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरेण वीरभद्रेण दक्षयज्ञं विनाश्य वै ।
कैलासाद्रौ गते तात किमभूत्तद्वदाधुना ॥ २॥

English
After destroying the sacrifice of Dakṣa when the heroic Virabhadra went to Kailāsa what happened there ? O dear, please tell me now. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ देवगणाः सर्वे मुनयश्च पराजिताः ।
रुद्रानीकैर्विभिन्नाङ्गा मम लोकं ययुस्तदा ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said:*  Defeated and mutilated by Śiva’s armies the gods and the sages came to my region. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयम्भुवे नमस्कृत्य मह्यं संस्तूय भूरिशः ।
तत्स्वक्लेशं विषेशेण कार्त्स्न्येनैव न्यवेदयन् ॥ ४॥

English
After making obeisance to me who am self-born, and eulogising me in various ways, they explained their distress entirely. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य ततोऽहं वै पुत्रशोकेन पीडितः ।
अचिन्तयमतिव्यग्रो दूयमानेन चेतसा ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing that, I was extremely pained for my son Dakṣa. In that mental anguish I thought. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कार्यं कार्यमद्याशु मया देवसुखावहम् ।
येन जीवतु दक्षोऽसौ मखः पूर्णो भवेत्सुरः ॥ ६॥

English
“What step shall I take to please the devas, whereby Dakṣa can be restored to life and whereby the sacrifice also be completed ?” (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विचार्य बहुधा नालभं शमहं मुने ।
विष्णुं तदा स्मरन् भक्त्या ज्ञानमाप तदोचितम् ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, even after thinking a lot I did not attain any peace of mind. Remembering lord Viṣṇu with devotion I knew what I shall do. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवैश्च मुनिभिर्विष्णोर्लोकमहं गतः ।
नत्वा नुत्वा च विविधैः स्तवैर्दुःखं न्यवदेयम् ॥ ८॥

English
Then I went to the world of Viṣṇu along with the gods and the sages. After bowing to and eulogising him with various hymns I informed him of my misery. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाध्वरः प्रपूर्णः स्याद्देव यज्ञकरश्च सः ।
सुखिनः स्युः सुराः सर्वे मुनयश्च तथा कुरु ॥ ९॥

English
Please make arrangements so that the sacrifice shall be completed, the gods and the sages be happy. As you know he was performing a sacrifice. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव रमानाथ विष्णो देवसुखावह ।
वयं त्वच्छरणं प्राप्ताः सदेवमुनयो ध्रुवम् ॥ १०॥

English
O lord of Lakṣmī, lord of gods, bestower of happiness to the gods, we, including the devas and the sages, have sought refuge in you. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचो मे हि ब्रह्मणः स रमेश्वरः ।
प्रत्युवाच शिवं स्मृत्वा शिवात्मा दीनमानसः ॥ ११॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing my words, the lord of Lakṣmī with his soul set on Śiva and mind free from distress, replied after duly remembering Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
तेजीयसि न सा भूता कृतागसि बुभूषताम् ।
तत्र क्षेमाय बहुधा बुभूषा हि कृतागसाम् ॥ १२॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  An aggression against a powerful person neither befits a weak aggressor nor leads to his welfare. (12.)<sup>336</sup>

[fn 336. The text of this verse is corrupt in all printed editions. We have translated it after emending the text in the following way :

तेजीयसि न सा भाति कृतागसि बुभूषता ।
तन्न क्षेमाय बहुषा बुभूषा हि कृतागसाम् ॥]
---

Sanskrit
कृतपापाः सुराः सर्वे शिवे हि परमेश्वरे ।
पराददुर्यज्ञभागं तस्य शम्भोर्विधे यतः ॥ १३॥

English
Thus, O Brahman, the gods have committed sin and offended Śiva, since they had partaken of His share in the sacrifice. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादयध्वं सर्वे हि यूयं शुद्धेन चेतसा ।
अथापरप्रसादं तं गृहीताङ्घ्रियुगं शिवम् ॥ १४॥

English
You shall propitiate Śiva by falling at his feet, with pure mind. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मिन् प्रकुपिते देवे विनश्यत्यखिलं जगत् ।
सलोकपालयज्ञस्य शासनाज्जीवितं द्रुतम् ॥ १५॥

English
At the bidding of the protector of the world whose fury annihilates everything, the resuscitation is certain and immediate. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तमाशु प्रियया देवं विहीनं च दुरुक्तिभिः ।
क्षमापयध्वं हृद्विद्धं दक्षेण सुदुरात्मना ॥ १६॥

English
That lord has been wounded in the heart by the wicked Dakṣa by harsh words. Crave the forgiveness of that lord who has lost his beloved now. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अयमेव महोपायस्तच्छान्त्यै केवलं विधे ।
शम्भोः सन्तुष्टये मन्ये सत्यमेवोदितं मया ॥ १७॥

English
O Brahman, this is the only remedy for tranquilising him, I think and also for pleasing him. I have mentioned that truth. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
नाहं न त्वं सुराश्चान्ये मुनयोऽपि तनूभृतः ।
यस्य तत्त्वं प्रमाणं च न विदुर्बलवीर्ययोः ॥ १८॥

English
Neither I nor you, nor the gods, nor the sages, nor any embodied being knows the reality and the extent of his strength and power. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मतन्त्रस्य तस्यापि परस्य परमात्मनः ।
क उपायं विधित्सेद्वै परं मूढं विरोधिनम् ॥ १९॥

English
Who else can lay down a remedy for Śiva who is independent and the greatest self—a remedy that quells delusion? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
चलिष्येऽहमपि ब्रह्मन् सर्वैः सार्धं शिवालयम् ।
क्षमापयामि गिरिशं कृतागश्च शिवे ध्रुवम् ॥ २०॥

English
I too have offended Śiva. I shall also come, O Brahmā, to Śiva's abode along with you all and crave the forgiveness of Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थमादिश्य विष्णुर्मां ब्रह्माणं सामरादिकम् ।
सार्धं देवैर्मतिं चक्रे तद्गिरौ गमनाय सः ॥ २१॥

English
Brahmā said :—  After commanding thus, me, Brahmā and the gods, Viṣṇu desired to go to his mountain along with the devas. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ययौ स्वधिष्ण्यनिलयं शिवस्याद्रिवरं शुभम् ।
कैलासं सामरमुनिप्रजेशादिमयो हरिः ॥ २२॥

English
Accompanied by the gods, sages, Brahmā and others Viṣṇu went to Kailāsa, the auspicious excellent mountainous abode of Śiva. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अतिप्रियं प्रभोर्नित्यं सुजुष्टं किन्नरादिभिः ।
नरेतरैरप्सरोभिर्योगसिद्धैर्महोन्नतम् ॥ २३॥

English
It was a favourite abode of the lord where Kinnaras, Apsaras, Siddhas and other divine beings stayed. It was very high. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नानामणिमयैः श‍ृङ्गैः शोभमानं समन्ततः ।
नानाधातुविचित्रं वै नानाद्रुमलताकुलम् ॥ २४॥

English
It was brilliant with many peaks full of precious gems all round. It was of variegated colour due to diverse minerals. It contained different trees and creepers. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नानामृगगणाकीर्णं नानापक्षिसमन्वितम् ।
नानाजलप्रस्रवणैरमरैः सिद्धयोषिताम् ॥ २५॥

रमणैर्विहरन्तीनां नानाकन्दरसानुभिः ।
द्रुमजातिभिरन्याभी राजितं राजतप्रभम् ॥ २६॥

English
Many kinds of deer roamed and many kinds of birds hovered there. The celestial and Siddha damsels sported about in different springs and pools along with their husbands and lovers. It contained many caves and ridges. It shone with various kinds of trees and had a silver lustre. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याघ्रादिभिर्महासत्त्वैर्निर्घुष्टं क्रूरतोज्झितम् ।
सर्वशोभान्वितं दिव्यं महाविस्मयकारकम् ॥ २७॥

English
It was infested with big animals, tigers and others who were free from cruelty. It was of divine nature endowed with shining brilliance. It inspired great surprise and wonder. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

सर्वपावनसङ्कर्त्र्या विष्णुपद्या सुनिर्मलम् ॥ २८॥

English
The river Gaṅgā originating from the holy abode of Satī, sanctifying everything flowed there and so the place was very clean. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing this mountain named Kailāsa, a great favourite of Śiva, Viṣṇu and other devas were surprised along with the excellent sages. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Near it, the gods saw Alakā$^{337}$, the beautiful and divine city of Kubera—a friend of Rudra. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Near that they saw the sylvan park Saugandhika which contained all kinds of trees. The sound originating from it was surprisingly divine. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Outskirting it are the two holy rivers Nandā$^{338}$ and Alakanandā that quelled sins by their mere sight. (32.)

[fn 338. Nandā, Alakanandā and Bhāgīrathī are three famous branches of Gaṅgā in the upper course in the Pauri-Garhwal region.]

Sanskrit

English
The celestial damsels descending to them from their world drank their waters. Emaciated for their sexual dalliance with their menfolk they entered them for their sports. (33.)

Sanskrit

English
The fig tree had steady shade all round. It had a number of suspended branches without hanging roots. Its height was a hundred Yojanas. It had no nests on it. It afforded protection from heat. (35.)

Sanskrit

English
