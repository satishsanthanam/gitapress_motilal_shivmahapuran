### **CHAPTER FORTYONE - *(Devas eulogise Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
४१

विष्ण्वादय ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव लौकिकाचारकृत्प्रभो ।
ब्रह्म त्वामीश्वरं शम्भुं जानीमः कृपया तव ॥ १॥

English
*Viṣṇu and others said :—*  O great lord, the lord of the gods and the prescriber of worldly conventions, we know you to be Śiva and Brahman, thanks to your favour. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
किं मोहयसि नस्तात मायया परया तव ।
दुर्ज्ञेयया सदा पुंसां मोहिन्या परमेश्वर ॥ २॥

English
O lord Śiva, why do you delude us by your illusion which is inscrutable and which deludes people always. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
।

प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्यापि जगतो योनिबीजयोः ।
परब्रह्म परस्त्वं च मनोवाचामगोचरः ॥ ३॥

English
You are the supreme Brahman, greater than Prakṛti and Puruṣa, the material and activating cause of the universe. You are incomprehensible and inexpressible. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव विश्वं सृजसि पास्यत्सि निजतन्त्रतः ।
स्वरूपां शिवशक्तिं हि क्रीडन्नूर्णपटो यथा ॥ ४॥

English
You alone create, sustain and annihilate the universe under your control like a spider (weaving its web). You sport about with Śivaśakti—your own manifestation. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव क्रतुमीशान ससर्जिथ दयापरः ।
दक्षेण सूत्रेण विभो सदा त्रय्यभिपत्तये ॥ ५॥

English
O Śiva, merciful that you are, you alone created the sacrifice through Dakṣa for the fulfilment of the Vedas. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयैव लोकेऽवसिताः सेतवो यान् धृतव्रताः ।
शुद्धान् श्रद्दधते विप्रा वेदमार्गविचक्षणाः ॥ ६॥

English
The delimitations which brahmins, experts in the Vedic path and rituals, believe in, end with you in the world. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तुस्त्वं मङ्गलानां हि स्वपरं तु सुखे विभो ।
अमङ्गलानां च हितं मिश्रं वाथ विपर्ययम् ॥ ७॥

English
O lord, the activities of auspicious nature result in happiness to the doer whereas inauspicious activities end in adverse, or in partially good and bad results. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वकर्मफलानां हि सदा दाता त्वमेव हि ।
सर्वे हि प्रोक्ताः पशवस्तत्पतिस्त्वं श्रुतिश्रुतः ॥ ८॥

English
You alone are the bestower of the fruits of all actions. You are the lord of glorious things according to the Vedas. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथग्धियः कर्मदृशोऽरुन्तुदाश्च दुराशयाः ।
वितुदन्ति परान् मूढा दुरुक्तैर्मत्सरान्विताः ॥ ९॥

English
Vulgar persons who observe sacrificial rites alone are acrimonious and wicked. With harsh words and jealousy these deluded persons inflict pain on others. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां दैववधानां भो भूयात्त्वच्च वधो विभो ।
भगवन्परमेशान कृपां कुरु परप्रभो ॥ १०॥

English
O lord, let not the destruction of these gods be carried out by you. O lord, great god, be merciful. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नमो रुद्राय शान्ताय ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने ।
कपर्दिने महेशाय ज्योत्स्नाय महते नमः ॥ ११॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva who is calm, the supreme and the highest soul, of matted hair, great lord and the bright one. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं हि विश्वसृजां स्रष्टा धाता त्वं प्रपितामहः ।
त्रिगुणात्मा निर्गुणश्च प्रकृतेः पुरुषात्परः ॥ १२॥

English
You are the creator of the creators of the universe. You are the sustainer and the forefather, possessed of three attributes and attributeless. You are greater than primordial nature and the supreme Being. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्ते नीलकण्ठाय वेधसे परमात्मने ।
विश्वाय विश्वबीजाय जगदानन्दहेतवे ॥ १३॥

English
Obeisance to Thee the blue-necked, the creator, the supreme soul, the universe, the speed of the universe and the cause of the bliss of the universe. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ओङ्कारस्त्वं वषट्कारः सर्वारम्भप्रवर्तकः ।
हन्तकारः स्वधाकारो हव्यकव्यान्नभुक् सदा ॥ १४॥

English
You are Omkāra, Vaṣaṭkāra,$^{339}$ the initiator of enterprises, Hantakāra, Svadhākāra<sup>*</sup> and the partaker of Havya and Kavya offerings always. (14.)

[fn 339. Vaṣaṭ or Vauṣaṭ is an exclamation uttered by the Hoṭṭ priest at the end of the sacrificial verse on hearing which the Adhvaryu priest casts the oblation offered to the deity into the fire.]

[fn *: Hantakāra and Svadhākāra are particular formulas of benediction. In the present context the three—Vaṣaṭkāra, Hantakāra and Svadhākāra are personified and described as identical with Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
कृतः कथं यज्ञभङ्गस्त्वया धर्मपरायण ।
ब्रह्मण्यस्त्वं महादेव कथं यज्ञहनो विभो ॥ १५॥

English
O righteous one, how is it that the sacrifice has been broken by you? O great God, you are a benefactor of brahmins. O lord, how can you be a destroyer of sacrificers? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणानां गवां चैव धर्मस्य प्रतिपालकः ।
शरण्योऽसि सदानन्त्यः सर्वेषां प्राणिनां प्रभो ॥ १६॥

English
You are the protector of virtue, brahmins and cows. O lord, you are the shelter for all living beings and worthy of being bowed to (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्ते भगवन् रुद्र भास्करामिततेजसे ।
नमो भवाय देवाय रसायाम्बुमयाय ते ॥ १७॥

English
Obeisance to you, O lord, having the splendour of innumerable suns. Obeisance to you, the Bhava, the lord in the form of flavour and fluid. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शर्वाय क्षितिरूपाय सदा सुरभिणे नमः ।
रुद्रायाग्निस्वरूपाय महातेजस्विने नमः ॥ १८॥

English
Obeisance to you who are every thing, who are in the form of fragrant earth. Obeisance to Him of great splendour, Him in the form of fire. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशाय वायवे तुभ्यं संस्पर्शाय नमो नमः ।
पशूनां पतये तुभ्यं यजमानाय वेधसे ॥ १९॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva who is wind in the subtle form of the principal of touch. Obeisance to you, the lord of individual souls, the priest presiding over sacrifice; and Vedhas (the creator). (19.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमाय व्योमरूपाय शब्दमात्राय ते नमः ।
महादेवाय सोमाय प्रवृत्ताय नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २०॥

English
Obeisance to you the terrible in the form of Ether with the principle of sound$^{340}$. Obeisance to the great lord Moon, or, one accompanied by Umā; obeisance to the Active. (20.)

[fn 340. Śiva symbolises the five elements viz. earth, water, fire, wind and ether.]
---

Sanskrit
उग्राय सूर्यरूपाय नमस्ते कर्मयोगिने ।
नमस्ते कालकालाय नमस्ते रुद्रमन्यवे ॥ २१॥

English
Obeisance to Ugra in the form of Sun; obeisance to you the detached performer of actions, the slayer of Kāla, and the furious Rudra. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः शिवाय भीमाय शङ्कराय शिवाय ते ।
उग्रोऽसि सर्वभूतानां नियन्ता यच्छिवोऽसि नः ॥ २२॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva, Bhīma, Ugra, the controller of living beings; you are Śiva to us. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मयस्कराय विश्वाय ब्रह्मणे ह्यार्तिनाशिने ।
अम्बिकापतये तुभ्यमुमायाः पतये नमः ॥ २३॥

English
Obeisance to the giver of pleasure to all-pervasive universal soul, the destroyer of distress; the consort of Umā. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शर्वाय सर्वरूपाय पुरुषाय परात्मने ।
सदसद्व्यक्तिहीनाय महतः कारणाय ते ॥ २४॥

English
Obeisance to the annihilator, the supreme Being in the form of all objects, the great soul who is indistinguishable from the existent and the non-existent, and is the cause of intellect. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
जाताय बहुधा लोके प्रभूताय नमो नमः ।
नीलाय नीलरुद्राय कद्रुद्राय प्रचेतसे ॥ २५॥

English
Obeisance, obeisance to one who is omniformed and the plentiful; obeisance to Nila, Nilarudra, Kadrudra and Pracetas. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
मीढुष्टमाय देवाय शिपिविष्टाय ते नमः ।
महीयसे नमस्तुभ्यं हन्त्रे देवारिणां सदा ॥ २६॥

English
Obeisance to the most bounteous lord who is pervaded by rays, who is the greatest, and the destroyer of the enemies of the gods. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ताराय च सुताराय तरुणाय सुतेजसे ।
हरिकेशाय देवाय महेशाय नमो नमः ॥ २७॥

English
Obeisance to Tāra (star), Sutāra (one that enables others to cross), Taruṇa (the ever young), and the brilliant. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां शम्भवे तुभ्यं विभवे परमात्मने ।
परमाय नमस्तुभ्यं कालकण्ठाय ते नमः ॥ २८॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva who is beneficent to the gods, the lord, the great soul, Obeisance to you the great; obeisance to you, the dark-necked God. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्याय परेशाय हिरण्यवपुषे नमः ।
भीमाय भीमरूपाय भीमकर्मरताय च ॥ २९॥

English
Obeisance to the golden one, the great lord, of golden body; obeisance to Bhima, Bhimarūpa, obeisance to one engaged in terrible deeds. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
भस्मदिग्धशरीराय रुद्राक्षाभरणाय च ।
नमो ह्रस्वाय दीर्घाय वामनाय नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ३०॥

English
Obeisance to one who has smeared his body with ashes, decorated himself with Rudrākṣa; and is of short long dwarfish height. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
दूरेवधाय ते देवाग्रेवधाय नमो नमः ।
धन्विने शूलिने तुभ्यं गदिने हलिने नमः ॥ ३१॥

English
Obeisance to you, O lord, who can kill at a dislance, in front, to one who has a bow, a trident, a mace and a ploughshare. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
नानायुधधरायैव दैत्यदानवनाशिने ।
सद्याय सद्यरूपाय सद्योजाताय वै नमः ॥ ३२॥

English
Obeisance to the wielder of many weapons, to the destroyer of Daityas and Dānavas, to Sadya, Sadyarūpa and Sadyojāta. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वामाय वामरूपाय वामनेत्राय ते नमः ।
अघोराय परेशाय विकटाय नमो नमः ॥ ३३॥

English
Obeisance to Vāma, Vāmarūpa, Vāmanetra, Aghora, the great lord and the Vikaṭa. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पुरुषाय नाथाय पुराणपुरुषाय च ।
पुरुषार्थप्रदानाय व्रतिने परमेष्ठिने ॥ ३४॥

English
Obeisance to Tatpuruṣa, to Nātha, the ancient Puruṣa, the bestower of the four aims of life, Vratin and Parameṣṭhin. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशानाय नमस्तुभ्यमीश्वराय नमो नमः ।
ब्रह्मणे ब्रह्मरूपाय नमः साक्षात्परात्मने ॥ ३५॥

English
Obeisance to you, Īśānas, Īśvara, Brahman, of the form of Brahman, the Supreme Soul. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
उग्रोऽसि सर्वदुष्टानां नियन्तासि शिवोऽसि नः ।
कालकूटाशिने तुभ्यं देवाद्यवनकारिणे ॥ ३६॥

English
You are fierce towards all wicked persons; to us you are Śiva the controller. Obeisance to you the swallower of Kālakūṭa poison<sup>341</sup>, the cause of protection of the Gods and others. (36.)

[fn 341. This refers to Śiva's swallowing of poison at the churning of the ocean.]

---

Sanskrit
वीराय वीरभद्राय रक्षद्वीराय शूलिने ।
महादेवाय महते पशूनां पतये नमः ॥ ३७॥

English
Obeisance to Vīra, Vīrabhadra, the protector of heroes, the trident-holder, the great lord of mankind. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरात्मने सुविद्याय श्रीकण्ठाय पिनाकिने ।
नमोऽनन्ताय सूक्ष्माय नमस्ते मृत्युमन्यवे ॥ ३८॥

English
Obeisance to Him of the heroic soul of perfect learning, Śrikaṇṭha, Pinākin, the endless, the subtle, the one whose anger is the cause of death. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
पराय परमेशाय परात्परतराय ते ।
परात्पराय विभवे नमस्ते विश्वमूर्तये ॥ ३९॥

English
Obeisance to the great lord, greater than the greatest, the greatest of the great, the all-pervading omni-formed lord. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
नमो विष्णुकलत्राय विष्णुक्षेत्राय भानवे ।
भैरवाय शरण्याय त्र्यम्बकाय विहारिणे ॥ ४०॥

English
Obeisance to Viṣṇukalatra, Viṣṇukṣetra, the sun, Bhairava, the refuge of the refugees, the three-eyed and the sportive. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
मृत्युञ्जयाय शोकाय त्रिगुणाय गुणात्मने ।
चन्द्रसूर्याग्निनेत्राय सर्वकारणसेतवे ॥ ४१॥

English
Obeisance to Mṛtyuñjaya, the cause of sorrow, of the form of three attributes, one with the moon, sun and fire as eyes, to the bridge of each and every cause. (41.)


---

Sanskrit
भवता हि जगत्सर्वं व्याप्तं स्वेनैव तेजसा ।
परब्रह्म निर्विकारी चिदानन्दः प्रकाशवान् ॥ ४२॥

English
The entire universe is pervaded by you with your own splendour; you are the great Brahman, the unchanging consciousness, bliss and light. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मविष्ण्विन्द्रचन्द्रादिप्रमुखाःसकलाः सुराः ।
मुनयश्चापरे त्वत्तः सम्प्रसूता महेश्वर ॥ ४३॥

English
O lord Śiva, all the Gods headed by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Indra, the moon, the sages and others are born of you. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यतो बिभर्षि सकलं विभज्य तनुमष्टधा ।
अष्टमूर्तिरितीशश्च त्वमाद्यः करुणामयः ॥ ४४॥

English
Since you hold everything by dividing your cosmic body into eight you are known as Aṣṭamūrti$^{348}$, you are the primordial Śiva, the merciful. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वद्भयाद्वाति वातोऽयं दहत्यग्निर्भयात्तव ।
सूर्यस्तपति ते भीत्या मृत्युर्धावति सर्वतः ॥ ४५॥

English
Afraid of you the wind blows, the fire blazes, the sun shines and death runs all round. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
दयासिन्धो महेशान प्रसीद परमेश्वर ।
रक्ष रक्ष सदैवास्मान् यस्मान्नष्टान् विचेतसः ॥ ४६॥

English
O Great Lord, the ocean of mercy, be pleased. Save us for ever, for we are otherwise doomed as we lack in fortitude. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षिताः सततं नाथ त्वयैव करुणानिधे ।
नानापद्भ्यो वयं शम्भो तथैवाद्य प्रपाहि नः ॥ ४७॥

English
O merciful lord, we have been protected always by you alone from different miseries. Similarly protect us now. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्ञस्योद्धरणं नाथ कुरु शीघ्रं प्रसादकृत् ।
असमाप्तस्य दुर्गेश दक्षस्य च प्रजापतेः ॥ ४८॥

English
O lord the blesser, O lord of Durgā, revive the incomplete sacrifice of Dakṣa Prajāpati immediately. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
भगोऽक्षिणी प्रपद्येत यजमानश्च जीवतु ।
पूष्णो दन्ताश्च रोहन्तु भृगोः श्मश्रूणि पूर्ववत् ॥ ४९॥

English
Let Bhaga regain his sight, let the initiated Dakṣa be restored to life, let the teeth of Pūṣan grow, let the moustaches of Bhṛgu appear as before. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
भवतानुगृहीतानां देवादीनां च सर्वशः ।
आरोग्यं भग्नगात्राणां शङ्कर त्वायुधाश्मभिः ॥ ५०॥

English
O Śiva, let the Gods and others whose bodies have been mutilated by weapons and stones, regain their previous normal health under your blessing. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्णभागोऽस्तु ते नाथावशिष्टेऽध्वरकर्मणि ।
रुद्रभागेन यज्ञस्ते कल्पितो नान्यथा क्वचित् ॥ ५१॥

इत्युक्त्वा सप्रजेशश्च रमेशश्च कृताञ्जलिः ।
दण्डवत्पतितो भूमौ क्षमापयितुमुद्यतः ॥ ५२॥

English
O lord, the entire share of yours will be allotted to you. Vasiṣṭha will officiate in the sacrifice, the sacrifice will have the share of Rudra, not otherwise. Saying so, Viṣṇu, the lord of Lakṣmī, along with Brahmā craved Śiva's forgiveness by prostrating on the ground. (51-52.)
