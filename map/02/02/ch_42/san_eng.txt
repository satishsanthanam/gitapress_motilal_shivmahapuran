### **CHAPTER FORTYTWO - (The removal of Dakṣa's misery)**

---

Sanskrit
४२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रीब्रह्मेशप्रजेशेन सदैव मुनिना च वै ।
अनुनीतः शम्भुरासीत्प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said:—  Lord Śiva was delighted on being conciliated and cajolled by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and the sages. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
आश्वास्य देवान् विष्ण्वादीन् विहस्य करुणानिधिः ।
उवाच परमेशानः कुर्वन् परमनुग्रहम् ॥ २॥

English
Consoling Viṣṇu and other devas and laughing, the merciful lord Śiva blessed them and spoke. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमहादेव उवाच ।
श‍ृणुतं सावधानेन मम वाक्यं सुरोत्तमौ ।
यथार्थं वच्मि वां तात वां क्रोधं सर्वदासहम् ॥ ३॥

English
Lord Śiva said :—  O excellent devas, both of you listen with attention. O dear ones, I shall state the truth. I have always borne your wrath. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नाघं तनौ तु बालानां वर्णये नानुचिन्तये ।
मम मायाभिभूतानां दण्डस्तत्र धृतो मया ॥ ४॥

English
I do not take into account the sin committed by my children. I have inflicted punishment on those who are afflicted by my illusion. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षस्य यज्ञभङ्गोऽयं न कृतश्च मया क्वचित् ।
परं द्वेष्टि परेषां यदात्मनस्तद्भविष्यति ॥ ५॥

English
The destruction of the sacrifice of Dakṣa was not done by me. If a person hates another, ultimately it recoils on him alone. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
परेषां क्लेदनं कर्म न कार्यं तत् कदाचन ।
परं द्वेष्टि परेषां यदात्मनस्तद्भविष्यति ॥ ६॥

English
No action that involves the affliction of others will be indulged in by me at any time. If anyone hates another it will recoil on him alone. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षस्य यज्ञशीर्ष्णो हि भवत्वजमुखं शिरः ।
मित्रनेत्रेण सम्पश्येद्यज्ञभागं भगः सुरः ॥ ७॥

English
Let the head (the sacrificial head) of Dakṣa be that of a goat. Let the god Bhaga receive his share in the sacrifice in conjunction with the sun. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पूषाभिधः सुरस्तात दद्भिर्जक्षतु पिष्टभुक् ।
याजमानैर्भग्नदन्तः सत्यमेतन्मयोदितम् ॥ ८॥

English
O dear ones, the god Pūṣan who used to grip the cooked offerings in a sacrifice with his teeth has been rendered broken-toothed and will remain as such. I have spoken the truth. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
बस्तश्मश्रुर्भवेदेव भृगुर्मम विरोधकृत् ।
देवाः प्रकृतिसर्वाङ्गा ये म उच्छेषणं ददुः ॥ ९॥

English
Bhṛgu who opposed me shall become goat-bearded. The gods who tried to uproot me shall have their physical bodies. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
बाहुभ्यामश्विनौ पूष्णो हस्ताभ्यां कृतवाहकः ।
भवन्त्वध्वर्यवश्चान्ये भवत्प्रीत्या मयोदितम् ॥ १०॥

English
Let the Adhvaryu priests be borne through the arms of Aśvins and the hands of Pūṣan. I have spoken thus out of love for you. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा परमेशानो विरराम दयान्वितः ।
चराचरपतिर्देवः सम्राट् वेदानुसारकृत् ॥ ११॥

English
Brahmā said:—  Saying thus, the lord Śiva though merciful, yet of imperial nature, the lord of the mobile and immobile and the follower of Vedic injunctions, stopped. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सर्वे सुराद्यास्ते श्रुत्वा शङ्करभाषितम् ।
साधु साध्विति सम्प्रोचुः परितुष्टाः सविष्ण्वजाः ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing his speech, Viṣṇu and other gods were delighted and sent out cries of approbation. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

भूयस्तद्देवयजनं ययौ च परया मुदा ॥ १३॥

एवं तेषां प्रार्थनया विष्णुप्रभृतिभिः सुरैः ।
ययौ कनखलं शम्भुर्यज्ञवाटं प्रजापतेः ॥ १४॥

English
Then at the invitation of Viṣṇu and other gods, Śiva went to Kanakhala at the sacrificial altar of Dakṣa Prajāpati. The celestial sages and I too accompanied him there. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रस्तदा ददर्शाथ वीरभद्रेण यत्कृतम् ।
प्रध्वंसं तं क्रतोस्तत्र देवर्षीणां विशेषतः ॥ १५॥

English
Then Rudra saw the extent of destruction carried out by Vīrabhadra, of the sacrifice and of the celestial sages. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वहा स्वधा तथा पूषा तुष्टिर्धृतिः सरस्वती ।
तथान्ये ऋषयः सर्वे पितरश्चाग्नयस्तथा ॥ १६॥

येऽन्ये च बहवस्तत्र यक्षगन्धर्वराक्षसाः ।
त्रोटिता लुञ्चिताश्चैव मृताः केचिद्रणाजिरे ॥ १७॥

English
Svāhā, Svadhā, Pūṣan, Tuṣṭi, Dhṛti, Sarasvatī, the sages, the manes, Agnis, many others like Yakṣas, Gandharvas, Rākṣasas who were mutilated, wounded or killed in the battle were seen by him laughingly. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्ञं तथाविधं दृष्ट्वा समाहूय गणाधिपम् ।
वीरभद्रं महावीर्यमुवाच प्रहसन् प्रभुः ॥ १८॥

English
On seeing this destruction of the sacrifice, He called the chieftain of Gaṇas, Vīrabhadra of great virility and spoke to him. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

महान् दण्डो धृतस्तात देवर्ष्यादिषु सत्वरम् ॥ १९॥

English
O Vīrabhadra of powerful arms, what is it that you have done? O dear, in your hurry you have inflicted very severe punishment on the celestial sages and others. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

यज्ञो विलक्षणस्तात यस्येदं फलमीदृशम् ॥ २०॥

English
Bring Dakṣa here quickly. O dear, he performed a sacrifice contrary to rules, whence this result arose. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

कबन्धमानयित्वाग्रे तस्य शम्भोरथाक्षिपत् ॥ २१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus commanded by Śiva, Vīrabhadra, hastened to bring the headless body of Dakṣa which he threw in front of Śiva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

शिरः कुत्रेति तेनोक्तं वीरभद्रोऽब्रवीत्प्रभुः ।
मया शिरो हुतं चाग्नौ तदानीमेव शङ्कर ॥ २३॥

English
O excellent sage, on seeing the headless body, Śiva, the benefactor of the worlds, spoke laughingly to Vīrabhadra—“Where is the head of Dakṣa?”, Vīrabhadra replied—“O lord Śiva, at that time itself, the head was consigned to fire by me.” (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit

देवान् तथाज्ञपत्प्रीत्या यदुक्तं तत्पुरा प्रभुः ॥ २४॥

विधाय कार्त्स्न्येन च तद्यदाह भगवान् भवः ।
मया विष्ण्वादयः सर्वे भृग्वादीनथ सत्वरम् ॥ २५॥

अथ प्रजापतेस्तस्य सवनीयपशोः शिरः ।
बस्तस्य सन्दधुः शम्भोः कायेनारं सुशासनात् ॥ २६॥

English
On hearing the words of Vīrabhadra, Śiva commanded the gods in the manner as mentioned before. After doing in accordance with what lord Śiva had said, Viṣṇu, the gods and I acquainted Bhṛgu with the same quickly. At the bidding of Śiva, they immediately joined the head of the sacrificial animal, the goat, with the body of Dakṣa. (24-26.)

---

Sanskrit

English
When the head was joined and Śiva looked at it, Dakṣa regained his life and awoke as if from sleep. (27.)

Sanskrit
अथ शक्रादयो देवा लोकपालाः सुचेतसः ।
तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं देवं प्रसन्नमुखपङ्कजम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Then Indra and other gods, the guardians of the quarters, of good mentality lauded lord Śiva whose lotus-like face beamed with brilliance. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नमनसः सर्वे देवास्तथा परे ।
सिद्धर्षयः प्रजेशाश्च तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं मुदा ॥ ५४॥

English
The other gods too of delighted minds, the Siddhas, the sages and the Prajāpatis lauded Śiva joyously. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
तथोपदेवनागाश्च सदस्या ब्राह्मणास्तथा ।
प्रणम्य परया भक्त्या तुष्टुवुश्च पृथक् पृथक् ॥ ५५॥

English
Then the gods of lower rank, Nāgas, and the brahmins, the members of the assembly, bowed with devotion and eulogised Śiva severally. (55.)
