### **CHAPTER FORTYTHREE - *(The Arrangement in Dakṣa's Sacrifice)***

---

Sanskrit
४३

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति स्तुतो रमेशेन मया चैव सुरर्षिभिः ।
तथान्यैश्च महादेवः प्रसन्नः सम्बभूव ह ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus eulogised by Viṣṇu, by me, by the gods, sages and others, the great lord became delighted. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुः कृपादृष्ट्या सर्वान् ऋषिसुरादिकान् ।
ब्रह्मविष्णू समाधाय दक्षमेतदुवाच ह ॥ २॥

English
After consoling Brahmā, Viṣṇu, the sages and the gods by His benign glance, Śiva spoke to Dakṣa. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेव उवाच ।
श‍ृणु दक्ष प्रवक्ष्यामि प्रसन्नोऽस्मि प्रजापते ।
भक्ताधीनः सदाहं वै स्वतन्त्रोऽप्यखिलेश्वरः ॥ ३॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—*  O Dakṣa, listen. I shall explain. O patriarch, I am delighted. Although I am independent lord of all, I am subservient to my devotees always. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनः सदा ।
उत्तरोत्तरतः श्रेष्ठास्तेषां दक्ष प्रजापते ॥ ४॥

English
Four kinds of meritorious persons worship me always. O patriarch Dakṣa, the latter are greater than the former. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी चैव चतुर्थकः ।
पूर्वे त्रयश्च सामान्याश्चतुर्थो हि विशिष्यते ॥ ५॥

English
They are—the distressed, the inquisitive, the fortune-seeker and the wise. The first three are ordinary and the fourth one is extraordinary person (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र ज्ञानी प्रियतरो मम रूपं च स स्मृतः ।
तस्मात्प्रियतरो नान्यः सत्यं सत्यं वदाम्यहम् ॥ ६॥

English
The wise among these four is a great favourite of mine. He is of my own form. None is dearer to me than the wise. It is the truth. I tell you the truth. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानगम्योऽहमात्मज्ञो वेदान्तश्रुतिपारगैः ।
विना ज्ञानेन मां प्राप्तुं यतन्ते चाल्पबुद्धयः ॥ ७॥

English
I am the knower of Self. I can be known through knowledge by those who have mastered Vedānta and the Vedas. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
न वेदैश्च न यज्ञैश्च न दानैस्तपसा क्वचित् ।
न शक्नुवन्ति मां प्राप्तुं मूढाः कर्मवशा नराः ॥ ८॥

English
Deluded men engrossed in rituals alone cannot attain me through the Vedas, sacrifices, gifts or austerities. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
केवलं कर्मणा त्वं स्म संसारं तर्तुमिच्छसि ।
अत एवाभवं रुष्टो यज्ञविध्वंसकारकः ॥ ९॥

English
You wished to cross the ocean of worldly existence by observance of rituals alone. That was why I became angry and caused the destruction of the sacrifice. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इतः प्रभृति भो दक्ष मत्वा मां परमेश्वरम् ।
बुद्ध्या ज्ञानपरो भूत्वा कुरु कर्म समाहितः ॥ १०॥

English
Hereafter, O Dakṣa, thinking upon me as the great Lord and giving more importance to knowledge you carry on rituals with care and attention. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यच्च श‍ृणु सद्बुद्ध्या वचनं मे प्रजापते ।
वच्मि गुह्यं धर्महेतोः सगुणत्वेऽप्यहं तव ॥ ११॥

English
O patriarch, listen to another statement of mine with a clear conscience. Although it is based on the qualitative aspect it is esoteric. For the sake of virtue I shall tell you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं ब्रह्मा च विष्णुश्च जगतः कारणं परम् ।
आत्मेश्वर उपद्रष्टा स्वयन्दृगविशेषणः ॥ १२॥

English
Brahmā, Viṣṇu and I constitute the chief cause of the universe. But I am the soul, the witness, self-seer and without attributes. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्ममायां समाविश्य सोऽहं गुणमयीं मुने ।
सृजन् रक्षन्हरन्विश्वं दधे संज्ञाः क्रियोचिताः ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, entering into my own illusion consisting of three attributes, I create, sustain and annihilate the universe and acquire designations consistent with the activities. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्वितीये परे तस्मिन् ब्रह्मण्यात्मनि केवले ।
अज्ञः पश्यति भेदेन भूतानि ब्रह्म चेश्वरम् ॥ १४॥

English
In that supreme, sole, universal God which is the pure Self, the ignorant sees different living beings, Brahman, Īśvara etc. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरःकरादिस्वाङ्गेषु कुरुते न यथा पुमान् ।
पारक्यशेमुषीं क्वापि भूतेष्वेवं हि मत्परः ॥ १५॥

English
Even as an ordinary man does not consider his head, hands and other limbs as separate from his own self so also my follower does not feel separateness about the living beings. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वभूतात्मनामेकभावनां यो न पश्यति ।
त्रिसुराणां भिदां दक्ष स शान्तिमधिगच्छति ॥ १६॥

English
He attains peace, who does not see any difference among the three deities who constitute the soul of all living beings and who have the same innate property, O Dakṣa. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यः करोति त्रिदेवेषु भेदबुद्धिं नराधमः ।
नरके स वसेन्नूनं यावदाचन्द्रतारकम् ॥ १७॥

English
The base man who differentiates the deities of the Trinity$^{343}$ certainly stays in hell as long as the moon and the stars shine. (17.)

[fn 343. Trinity is the emanation from the transcendent reality called Brahman. The trinitarian pattern of the cosmos is a single whole at its base. The three—Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra—exist in one and one in three and they are comprehended within that one Being who is supreme, secret and the soul of all things.]
---

Sanskrit
मत्परः पूजयेद्देवान् सर्वानपि विचक्षणः ।
स ज्ञानं लभते येन मुक्तिर्भवति शाश्वती ॥ १८॥

English
My devotee may worship the gods. Being so absorbed he will attain knowledge leading to eternal salvation. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विधिभक्तिं विना नैव भक्तिर्भवति वैष्णवी ।
विष्णुभक्तिं विना मे न भक्तिः क्वापि प्रजायते ॥ १९॥

English
Without devotion to Brahmā one cannot have the devotion to Viṣṇu; without devotion to Viṣṇu none will have devotion towards me. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा शङ्करः स्वामी सर्वेषां परमेश्वरः ।
सर्वेषां श‍ृण्वतां तत्रोवाच वाणीं कृपाकरः ॥ २०॥

English
After saying this, Śiva, the great lord, the merciful god spoke these words within the hearing of everyone. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिभक्तो हि मां निन्देत्तथा शैवो भवेद्यदि ।
तयोः शापा भवेयुस्ते तत्त्वप्राप्तिर्भवेन्न हि ॥ २१॥

English
"If a devotee of Viṣṇu hates me or if a devotee of Śiva hates Viṣṇu, both will incur curses and never realise reality". (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य महेशस्य वचनं सुखकारकम् ।
जहर्षुः सकलास्तत्र सुरमुन्यादयो मुने ॥ २२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these pleasing words of lord Śiva, O sage, the devas, sages and others were greatly delighted. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षोऽभवन्महाप्रीत्या शिवभक्तिरतस्तदा ।
सकुटुम्बः सुराद्यास्ते शिवं मत्वाखिलेश्वरम् ॥ २३॥

यथा येन कृता शम्भोः संस्तुतिः परमात्मनः ।
तथा तस्मै वरो दत्तः शम्भुना तुष्टचेतसा ॥ २४॥

English
With great joy, Dakṣa, his family and the gods realised Śiva as the lord of all and was engrossed in devotion to Śiva. Lord Śiva who was delighted in mind granted boons to all, as he received reverence to His great self. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञप्तः शिवेनाशु दक्षः शिवभक्तः प्रसन्नधीः ।
यज्ञं चकार सम्पूर्णं शिवानुग्रहतो मुने ॥ २५॥

English
Permitted by Śiva and with his blessings, O sage, Dakṣa the devotee of Śiva, with a delighted heart completed his sacrifice. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ददौ भागान्सुरेभ्यो हि पूर्णभागं शिवाय सः ।
दानं ददौ द्विजेभ्यश्च प्राप्तः शम्भोरनुग्रहः ॥ २६॥

English
He allotted the full share to Śiva and gave the gods their respective shares. He gave charitable gifts to the brahmins and secured the good blessings of Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवस्य सुमहत्तत्कर्म विधिपूर्वकम् ।
दक्षः समाप्य विधिवत्सहर्त्विग्भिः प्रजापतिः ॥ २७॥

English
Thus the patriarch, Dakṣa, in collaboration with the Rṛtviks, completed that great rite of the gods in accordance with the sacred injunction. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं दक्षमखः पूर्णोऽभवत्तत्र मुनीश्वर ।
शङ्करस्य प्रसादेन परब्रह्मस्वरूपिणः ॥ २८॥

English
O excellent sage, thus the sacrifice of Dakṣa was completed, thanks to the grace of Śiva who is identical with the supreme Brahman. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवर्षयः सर्वे शंसन्तः शाङ्करं यशः ।
स्वधामानि ययुस्तुष्टाः परेऽपि सुखतस्तदा ॥ २९॥

English
Then the celestial sages, sang the glory of Śiva and left for their abodes with delighted minds. Others too were pleased and left for their homes. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विष्णुश्च सुप्रीतावपि स्वं स्वं परं मुदा ।
गायन्तौ सुयशः शम्भोः सर्वमङ्गलदं सदा ॥ ३०॥

English
Viṣṇu and I, went to our regions, joyfully singing the ever auspicious glory of Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षसम्मानितः प्रीत्या महादेवोऽपि सद्गतिः ।
कैलासं स ययौ शैलं सुप्रीतः सगणो निजम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Lovingly honoured by Dakṣa, the great lord Śiva, the goal of the good, returned to Kailāsa along with his Gaṇas. He was greatly delighted. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्य स्वगिरिं शम्भुः सस्मार स्वप्रियां सतीम् ।
गणेभ्यः कथयामास प्रधानेभ्यश्च तत्कथाम् ॥ ३२॥

English
After returning to his mountain, Śiva remembered His beloved Satī and mentioned her story to the most important of his Gaṇas. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कालं निनाय विज्ञानी बहु तच्चरितं वदन् ।
लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रित्य दर्शयन् कामितां प्रभुः ॥ ३३॥

English
Narrating her story, lord Śiva passed many days. He then evinced the lover's humour according to the conventions of the world. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
नानीतिकारकः स्वामी परब्रह्म सतां गतिः ।
तस्य मोहः क्व वा शोकः क्व विकारः परो मुने ॥ ३४॥

English
O sage, the lord is never unjust. The supreme Brahman is the goal of the good. How can He be deluded? What sorrow has He? How can he have other aberrations? (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विष्णुश्च जानीवस्तद्भेदं न कदाचन ।
के परे मुनयो देवा मानुषाद्याश्च योगिनः ॥ ३५॥

English
Even Viṣṇu and I do not know His real secret. What then about others, the sages, gods, human beings and even Yogins. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
महिमा शाङ्करोऽनन्तो दुर्विज्ञेयो मनीषिभिः ।
भक्तज्ञातश्च सद्भक्त्या तत्प्रसादाद्विना श्रमम् ॥ ३६॥

English
The greatness of Śiva is endless and inscrutable even to the learned sages. It is known to the devotees without difficulty, thanks to good devotion and his favour. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एकोऽपि न विकारो हि शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
सन्दर्शयति लोकेभ्यः कृत्वा तां तादृशीं गतिम् ॥ ३७॥

English
There is no emotion or aberration at all in Śiva the supreme Being. He points out to the people of the world by his different actions, their respective goals. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्पठित्वा च संश्रुत्य सर्वलोकसुधीर्मुने ।
लभते सद्गतिं दिव्यामिहापि सुखमुत्तमम् ॥ ३८॥

English
O sage, by reading or listening to this, intelligent persons in the world secure good goal hereafter and excellent happiness in this world. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं दाक्षायणी हित्वा निजदेहं सती पुनः ।
जज्ञे हिमवतः पत्न्यां मेनायामिति विश्रुतम् ॥ ३९॥

English
After forsaking her body thus, Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa, was born as the daughter of Menā, the wife of Himavat. This is well known. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः कृत्वा तपस्तत्र शिवं वव्रे पतिं च सा ।
गौरी भूत्वार्धवामाङ्गी लीलाश्चक्रेऽद्भुताः शिवा ॥ ४०॥

English
After performing penance again she wooed Śiva as her husband. Attaining white complexion she performed many wonderful, divine sports and gained half the body of Śiva.³⁴⁴ (40.)

[fn 344. Ardhanārīśvara is the half-male and half-female form of Śiva. This form, most popular in ancient sculpture, symbolises the union and concord of the spirit and its energy.]
---

Sanskrit
इत्थं सतीचरित्रं ते वर्णितं परमाद्भुतम् ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं दिव्यं सर्वकामप्रदायकम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Thus I have described the fascinating story of Satī to you which confers worldly pleasures and salvation, which is divine and bestows all wishes. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं पवित्रं परपावनम् ।
स्वर्ग्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं पुत्रपौत्रफलप्रदम् ॥ ४२॥

English
This narrative is flawless, pure, sanctifying, conferring heavenly pleasures, glory, longevity and the pleasure of sons and grandsons. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद् भक्त्या श्रावयेद्भक्तिमान्नरान् ।
सर्वकर्मा लभेत्तात परत्र परमां गतिम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Whoever listens to this or recites this with devotion, O dear, will attain the greatest goal in every rite. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पठेत्पाठयेद्वापि समाख्यानमिदं शुभम् ।
सोऽपि भुक्त्वाखिलान् भोगानन्ते मोक्षमवाप्नुयात् ॥ ४४॥

English
He who reads or teaches this auspicious narrative will attain salvation on death after enjoying all worldly pleasures. (44.)
