### **CHAPTER ONE - *(The marriage of Himācala<sup>1</sup>)***

[fn 1. Himavat or Himācala is represented in two forms (1) the mobile and (2) immobile. The former is the subtle human form while the latter is the gross, stationary form identical with the mountain Himālayas

The present section recounts the marriage of Himavat with Menā in his mobile form. See RS III 1.15.]
---

Sanskrit
१

नारद उवाच ।
दाक्षायणी सती देवी त्यक्तदेहा पितुर्मखे ।
कथं गिरिसुता ब्रह्मन् बभूव जगदम्बिका ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, how did the goddess Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa who forsook her body in her father's sacrifice, become the daughter of Himācala, and the mother of the universe? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं कृत्वा तपोऽत्युग्रं पतिमाप शिवं च सा ।
एतन्मे पृच्छते सम्यक् कथय त्वं विशेषतः ॥ २॥

English
How could she secure Śiva as her husband after performing a severe penance? Please explain this clearly to me who ask you about it. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु त्वं मुनिशार्दूल शिवाचरितमुत्तमम् ।
पावनं परमं दिव्यं सर्वपापहरं शुभम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O foremost of sages, listen to the story of Śivā which is excellent, sanctifying, highly divine, auspicious and destructive of all sins. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा दाक्षायणी देवी हरेण सहिता मुदा ।
हिमाचले सुचिक्रीडे लीलया परमेश्वरी ॥ ४॥

मत्सुतेयमिति ज्ञात्वा सिषेवे मातृवर्चसा ।
हिमाचलप्रिया मेना सर्वर्द्धिभिरनिर्भरा ॥ ५॥

English
When the great goddess Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa, was sporting about on the Himālayas with Śiva, Menā,<sup>2</sup> the beloved of Himācala thought that she was her own daughter and loved her like a mother with all kinds of nourishments. (4-5.)

[fn 2. Menā or Menakā, the wife of Himavat and the mother of Pārvatī, was one of the three daughters of Svadhā, the wife of Kavi, a class of Pītrs. Svadhā was one of the sixty daughters of Dakṣa and Prasūtī who gave birth to Menā, Dhanyā and Kalāvatī.]
---

Sanskrit
यदा दाक्षायणी रुष्टा नादृता स्वतनुं जहौ ।
पित्रा दक्षेण तद्यज्ञे सङ्गता परमेश्वरी ॥ ६॥

तदैव मेनका तां सा हिमाचलप्रिया मुने ।
शिवलोकस्थितां देवीमारिराधयिषुस्तदा ॥ ७॥

English
When the great Goddess Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa who had been to her father's sacrifice and who did not receive his due attention became angry and cast off her body, at the very same time, O sage, Himācala's beloved Menā wanted to propitiate her in Śivaloka. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यामहं सुता स्यामित्यवधार्य सती हृदा ।
त्यक्तदेहा मनो दध्रे भवितुं हिमवत्सुता ॥ ८॥

English
Satī thought to herself :—“I shall be her daughter” and cast off her body in order to become the daughter of Himācala. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
समयं प्राप्य सा देवी सर्वदेवस्तुता पुनः ।
सती त्यक्ततनुः प्रीत्या मेनकातनयाभवत् ॥ ९॥

English
At the proper time Satī who had cast off her body and who was worshipped and eulogised by the gods became the daughter of Menā out of sheer joy. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नाम्ना सा पार्वती देवी तपः कृत्वा सुदुस्सहम् ।
नारदस्योपदेशाद्वै पतिं प्राप शिवं पुनः ॥ १०॥

English
On being advised by Nārada, the goddess who was named Pārvatī, performed a severe penance and thereby secured Śiva as her husband. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे महाप्राज्ञ वद मे वदतां वर ।
मेनकायाः समुत्पत्तिं विवाहचरितं तथा ॥ ११॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, of great intellect and foremost of eloquent gods, please tell me the origin and details of the marriage of Menā. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या हि मेनका देवी यस्यां जाता सुता सती ।
अतो मान्या च धन्या च सर्वेषां सा पतिव्रता ॥ १२॥

English
Blessed indeed is the gentle lady Menakā of whom Satī was born as a daughter. Hence that chaste lady is worthy of the honour and blessings of everyone. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु त्वं नारद मुने पार्वतीमातुरुद्भवम् ।
विवाहं चरितं चैव पावनं भक्तिवर्धनम् ॥ १३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage Nārada, you listen to the story of the origin of Pārvatī’s mother and her marriage and other details both sanctifying and conducive to the growth of devotion. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्त्युत्तरस्यां दिशि वै गिरीशो हिमवान्महान् ।
पर्वतो हि मुनिश्रेष्ठ महातेजाः समृद्धिभाक् ॥ १४॥

English
O excellent sage, there in the northern region is a mountain called Himavat who is the lord of mountains and has great splendour and prosperity. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वैरूप्यं तस्य विख्यातं जङ्गमस्थिरभेदतः ।
वर्णयामि समासेन तस्य सूक्ष्मस्वरूपकम् ॥ १५॥

English
His twofold aspects—that of a mobile nature and that of the immobile one—are well known. I succinctly describe his subtle form. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वापरौ तोयनिधी सुविगाह्य स्थितो हि यः ।
नानारत्नाकरो रम्यो मानदण्ड इव क्षितेः ॥ १६॥

English
He is beautiful and is the storehouse of multifarious gems. Extending from the eastern to the western ocean he appears like a measuring rod of the Earth.³ (16.)

[fn 3. For the similarity of ideas and verbal expression, compare Kālidāsa’s Kumārasambhava I. 1.]
---

Sanskrit
नानावृक्षसमाकीर्णो नानाश‍ृङ्गसुचित्रितः ।
सिंहव्याघ्रादिपशुभिः सेवितः सुखिभिः सदा ॥ १७॥

English
He abounds in various trees. Being of variegated shape and features he is adorned by many peaks on him. Lions, tigers and other animals frequent it. Many happy persons live there for ever. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तुषारनिधिरत्युग्रो नानाश्चर्यविचित्रितः ।
देवर्षिसिद्धमुनिभिः संश्रितः शिवसम्प्रियः ॥ १८॥

English
He is the storehouse of snow (and yet) very fierce. He is the resort of wonderful things. He is resorted to by the gods, sages and seers. He is a great favourite of Śiva. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तपःस्थानोऽतिपूतात्मा पावनश्च महात्मनाम् ।
तपस्सिद्धिप्रदोऽत्यन्तं नानाधात्वाकरः शुभः ॥ १९॥

English
He is of pure soul, an abode of austerities. He sanctifies even the great souls. He is the bestower of the benefit of austerities. He is the auspicious storehouse of multifarious minerals. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव दिव्यरूपो हि रम्यः सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरः ।
विष्ण्वंशोऽविकृतः शैलराजराजः सतां प्रियः ॥ २०॥

English
He is of a divine form. He is beautiful in every part. He is the unaffected part of Viṣṇu. He is the king of leading mountains and a great favourite of the good. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कुलस्थित्यै च स गिरिर्धर्मवर्द्धनहेतवे ।
स्वविवाहं कर्तुमैच्छत्पितृदेवहितेच्छया ॥ २१॥

English
Due to the desire for the benefit of the manes and the gods and for the stabilisation of his race as well as for the increase of virtue, Himācala wanted to marry. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे देवाः स्वार्थमाचिन्त्य कृत्स्नशः ।
ऊचुः पितॄन्समागत्य दिव्यान्प्रीत्या मुनीश्वर ॥ २२॥

English
At that time, considering their own interest entirely, O excellent sage, the gods approached the celestial forefathers and said lovingly to them. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः
सर्वे श‍ृणुत नो वाक्यं पितरः प्रीतमानसाः ।
कर्तव्यं तत्तथैवाशु देवकार्येप्सवो यदि ॥ २३॥

English
*The gods said :—*  “O ye forefathers, listen to our words with pleasure. If you desire that the affairs of the gods be fulfilled you must act accordingly soon. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
मेना नाम सुता या वो ज्येष्ठा मङ्गलरूपिणी ।
तां विवाह्य च सुप्रीत्या हिमाख्येन महीभृता ॥ २४॥

एवं सर्वमहालाभः सर्वेषां च भविष्यति ।
युष्माकममराणां च दुःखहानिः पदे पदे ॥ २५॥

English
Uniting your eldest daughter Menā of auspicious features with Himācala, a great benefit will accrue to everyone. At every step, the miseries of the gods and those of yours as well can be reduced”. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्यामरवचः पितरस्ते विमृश्य च ।
स्मृत्वा शापं सुतानां च प्रोचुरोमिति तद्वचः ॥ २६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the gods, pondering over them and remembering the curse incurred by their daughters, the forefathers said “Amen” to their proposals. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ददुर्मेनां सुविधिना हिमागाय निजात्मजाम् ।
समुत्सवो महानासीत्तद्विवाहे सुमङ्गले ॥ २७॥

English
They gave their daughter Menā to Himavat. In that auspicious marriage there were great festivities. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
हर्यादयोऽपि ते देवा मुनयश्चापरेऽखिलाः ।
आजग्मुस्तत्र संस्मृत्य वामदेवं भवं धिया ॥ २८॥

English
Viṣṇu, the other gods and the sages reached there with their hearts set on Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्सवं कारयामासुर्दत्त्वा दानान्यनेकशः ।
सुप्रशस्य पितॄन्दिव्यान्प्रशशंसुर्हिमाचलम् ॥ २९॥

English
Giving many charitable gifts they made the celebration a great success. They praised the celestial forefathers and the Himavat. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
महामोदान्विता देवास्ते सर्वे समुनीश्वराः ।
सञ्जग्मुः स्वस्वधामानि संस्मरन्तः शिवाशिवौ ॥ ३०॥

English
All the gods and the sages rejoiced and returned to their own abodes with their hearts set on Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यौतुकं बहु सम्प्राप्य सुविवाह्य प्रियां च ताम् ।
आजगाम स्वभवनं मुदमाप गिरीश्वरः ॥ ३१॥

English
After receiving many articles as gifts and marrying the beloved lady Menā, the lord of mountains returned to his abode and rejoiced. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
मेनया हि हिमागस्य सुविवाहो मुनीश्वर ।
प्रोक्तो मे सुखदः प्रीत्या किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ३२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O excellent sage, the pleasing details of the splendid marriage of Himavat with Menā have been recounted to you thus. What more do you wish to hear? (32.)
