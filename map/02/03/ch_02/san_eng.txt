### **CHAPTER TWO - (*Menā and others incur the imprecation of Sanaka etc.*)**

---

Sanskrit
२

नारद उवाच ।
विधे प्राज्ञ वदेदानीं मेनोत्पत्तिं समादरात् ।
अपि शापं समाचक्ष्व कुरु सन्देहभञ्जनम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, the intelligent one, please now tell me reverently about the origin of Menā as well as the imprecation. Please clear my doubts. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु नारद सुप्रीत्या मेनोत्पत्तिं विवेकतः ।
मुनिभिः सह वक्ष्येऽहं सुतवर्य महाबुध ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada and the sages, listen to the narrative of the origin of Menā. O excellent son, O great scholar, I shall mention it now. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षनामा मम सुतो यः पुरा कथितो मुने ।
तस्य जाताः सुताः षष्टिप्रमिताः सृष्टिकारणाः ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, I have already told you about my son Dakṣa. He had sixty daughters all of whom were the instruments of creation. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तासां विवाहमकरोत्स वरैः कश्यपादिभिः ।
विदितं ते समस्तं तत्प्रस्तुतं श‍ृणु नारद ॥ ४॥

English
He celebrated their marriages with Kaśyapa and other bridegrooms. You know all that already. O Nārada, now, listen to the present story. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तासां मध्ये स्वधानाम्नीं पितृभ्यो दत्तवान्सुताम् ।
तिस्रोऽभवन्सुतास्तस्याः सुभगा धर्ममूर्तयः ॥ ५॥

English
Goloka : It is a modern addition to the original series of fourteen lokas. It is identified with Gokula, a pastoral district on the Yamunā about Mathurā where Kṛṣṇa passed his boyhood with the cowherd. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तासां नामानि श‍ृणु मे पावनानि मुनीश्वर ।
सदा विघ्नहराण्येव महामङ्गलदानि च ॥ ६॥

English
O excellent sage, listen to their holy names which remove obstacles and confer blessings. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनानाम्नी सुता ज्येष्ठा मध्या धन्या कलावती ।
अन्त्या एताः सुताः सर्वाः पितॄणां मानसोद्भवाः ॥ ७॥

English
Menā was the eldest. Dhanyā was the middle. Kalāvatī was the youngest. All these were mentally conceived daughters of the forefathers. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अयोनिजाः स्वधायाश्च लोकतस्तत्सुता मताः ।
आसां प्रोच्य सुनामानि सर्वान्कामान् जनो लभेत् ॥ ८॥

English
They were not born of the womb of Svadhā. They were conventionally considered her children. On reciting their names, men can achieve their desires. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जगद्वन्द्याः सदा लोकमातरः परमोददाः ।
योगिन्यः परमा ज्ञाननिधानास्तास्त्रिलोकगाः ॥ ९॥

English
The mothers of the worlds are worthy of reverence of the entire universe always. They are the bestowers of great joy. They are great yoginīs, storehouses of knowledge. They pervade the three worlds. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्समये तिस्रो भगिन्यस्ता मुनीश्वर ।
श्वेतद्वीपं विष्णुलोकं जग्मुर्दर्शनहेतवे ॥ १०॥

English
O excellent sage, once the three sisters went to Śvetadvīpa (white island)⁴ in the world of Viṣṇu for sight-seeing purpose. (10.)

[fn 4. It has not been possible to identify this island. Colonel Wilford attempted to identify it with Britain. See. H. M. P. 315.]
---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा प्रणामं विष्णोश्च संस्तुतिं भक्तिसंयुताः ।
तस्थुस्तदाज्ञया तत्र सुसमाजो महानभूत् ॥ ११॥

English
After bowing to and eulogising Viṣṇu with great devotion they halted there at his bidding. A great concourse of people was held there. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव सनकाद्यास्तु सिद्धा ब्रह्मसुता मुने ।
गतास्तत्र हरिं नत्वा स्तुत्वा तस्थुस्तदाज्ञया ॥ १२॥

English
O sage, at the same time, Siddhas, sons of Brahmā—Sanaka and others came there. They bowed to and lauded Viṣṇu and stayed there at his bidding. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सनकाद्यान्मुनीन् दृष्ट्वोत्तस्थुस्ते सकला द्रुतम् ।
तत्रस्थान्संस्थितान्नत्वा देवाद्याँल्लोकवन्दितान् ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing those sages Sanaka and others and the persons who had assembled there, stood up. When they, the elders of gods respected by the people, sat they all bowed to them. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तिस्रो भगिन्यस्तास्तत्र नोत्तस्थुर्मोहिता मुने ।
मायया दैवविवशाः शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ॥ १४॥

English
Helpless by misfortune and deluded by lord Śiva's illusion O sage, the three sisters did not stand up. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मोहिनी सर्वलोकानां शिवमाया गरीयसी ।
तदधीनं जगत्सर्वं शिवेच्छा सा प्रकीर्त्यते ॥ १५॥

English
Śiva's illusion is weighty and capable of deluding the worlds. The entire universe is subservient to it. It is also called Śiva's Will. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रारब्धं प्रोच्यते सैव तन्नामानि ह्यनेकशः ।
शिवेच्छया भवत्येव नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १६॥

English
The same is also called an action that has begun to fructify. Its names are many. Everything takes place on Śiva's wish. There is nothing to be pondered over in this respect. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
भूत्वा तद्वशगास्ता वै न चक्रुरपि तन्नतिम् ।
विस्मिताः सम्प्रदृश्यैव संस्थितास्तत्र केवलम् ॥ १७॥

English
Becoming a victim thereof, the sisters did not make obeisance to them. They remained surprised and stunned thereafter seeing them. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तादृशीं तद्गतिं दृष्ट्वा सनकाद्या मुनीश्वराः ।
ज्ञानिनोऽपि परं चक्रुः क्रोधं दुर्विषहं च ते ॥ १८॥

English
On seeing such a behaviour on their part the great sages, Sanaka and others, despite being wise, became unbearably furious. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेच्छामोहितस्तत्र सक्रोधस्ता उवाच ह ।
सनत्कुमारो योगीशः शापं दण्डकरं ददत् ॥ १९॥

English
Himself deluded by Śiva's illusion Sanaka, a perfect Yogin, furiously told them giving a curse as punishment. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
यूयं तिस्रो भगिन्यश्च मूढाः सद्वयुनोज्झिताः ।
अज्ञातश्रुतितत्त्वा हि पितृकन्या अपि ध्रुवम् ॥ २०॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  Inspite of being the daughters of the forefathers, ye three sisters are foolish, bereft of wisdom and ignorant of the essence of the Vedas. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्युत्थानं कृतं नो यो नमस्कारोऽपि गर्विताः ।
मोहिता नरभावत्वात्स्वर्गाद् दूरा भवन्तु हि ॥ २१॥

English
You did not stand up nor did you pay any respects to us. You were haughty and deluded and so evinced a deluded disposition of human beings. Hence all of you shall leave heaven. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नरस्त्रियः सम्भवन्तु तिस्रोऽप्य ज्ञानमोहिताः ।
स्वकर्मणः प्रभावेण लभध्वं फलमीदृशम् ॥ २२॥

English
May the three sisters deluded by ignorance be born as human womenfolk. May ye reap this fruit as a result of the power of your own action. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य च साध्व्यस्तास्तिस्रोऽपि चकिता भृशम् ।
पतित्वा पादयोस्तस्य समूचूर्नतमस्तकाः ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing this, the chaste maidens got perplexed. The three fell at his feet and spoke with their heads bent down. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृतनया ऊचुः
मुनिवर्य दयासिन्धो प्रसन्नो भव चाधुना ।
त्वत्प्रणामं वयं मूढाः कुर्महे स्म न भावतः ॥ २४॥

English
*The daughters of the forefathers said :—*  “O excellent sage, ocean of mercy, be pleased now. Because we were mentally confounded we did not bow to you. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्तं च तत्फलं विप्र न ते दोषो महामुने ।
अनुग्रहं कुरुष्वात्र लभेम स्वर्गतिं पुनः ॥ २५॥

English
O Brahmin, the result thereof has been achieved by us. O great sage it is not your fault. Bless us now whereby we shall regain heavenly abode again.” (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
श्रुत्वा तद्वचनं तात प्रोवाच स मुनिस्तदा ।
शापोद्धारं प्रसन्नात्मा प्रेरितः शिवमायया ॥ २६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing their words, O dear, the sage spoke to them. He had been induced by Śiva’s illusion to give them redemption from the curse. He was a bit consoled. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच
पितॄणां तनयास्तिस्रः श‍ृणुत प्रीतमानसाः ।
वचनं मम शोकघ्नं सुखदं सर्वदैव वः ॥ २७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  “O ye three daughters of forefathers, listen with pleasure to my words that will dispel your sorrow and bestow happiness on you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोरंशस्य शैलस्य हिमाधारस्य कामिनी ।
ज्येष्ठा भवतु तत्कन्या भविष्यत्येव पार्वती ॥ २८॥

English
May the eldest among you become the wife of Himavat the mountain that is a part of Viṣṇu. Pārvatī shall be her daughter. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या प्रिया द्वितीया तु योगिनी जनकस्य च ।
तस्याः कन्या महालक्ष्मीर्नाम्ना सीता भविष्यति ॥ २९॥

English
The second daughter Dhanyā shall be the Yogini, the wife of Janaka. Her daughter shall be Mahālakṣmī in the name of Sītā. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषभानस्य वैश्यस्य कनिष्ठा च कलावती ।
भविष्यति प्रिया राधा तत्सुता द्वापरान्ततः ॥ ३०॥

English
The youngest Kalāvatī shall be the wife of the Vaiśya—Vṛṣabhāna. At the end of Dvāpara, Rādhā shall be her daughter. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनका योगिनी पत्या पार्वत्याश्च वरेण च ।
तेन देहेन कैलासं गमिष्यति परं पदम् ॥ ३१॥

English
The Yogini Menā shall attain the great region Kailāsa along with her body and in the company of her husband due to the boon of Pārvatī. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या च सीतया सीरध्वजो जनकवंशजः ।
जीवन्मुक्तो महायोगी वैकुण्ठं च गमिष्यति ॥ ३२॥

English
Janaka shall be blessed by Sītā born in Janaka's race and he shall be a living liberated soul. A great Yogin, he will attain Vaikuṇṭha. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कलावती वृषभानस्य कौतुकात्कन्यया सह ।
जीवन्मुक्ता च गोलोकं गमिष्यति न संशयः ॥ ३३॥

English
Kalāvatī by the virtue of Vṛṣabhāna shall become a living liberated soul and attain Goloka⁵ along with her daughter. There is no doubt about it. (33.)

[fn 5. Goloka : It is a modern addition to the original series of fourteen lokas. It is identified with Gokula, a pastoral district on the Yamunā about Mathurā where Kṛṣṇa passed his boyhood with the cowherd.]
---

Sanskrit
विना विपत्तिं महिमा केषां कुत्र भविष्यति ।
सुकर्मिणां गते दुःखे प्रभवेद् दुर्लभं सुखम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Without adversity how can one attain greatness? To persons of good rites, if misery vanishes happiness is likely to be difficult of access. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृणां तनयाः यूयं सर्वाः स्वर्गविलासिकाः ।
कर्मक्षयश्च युष्माकमभवद्विष्णुदर्शनात् ॥ ३५॥

English
Ye the daughters of forefathers shall shine in heaven. By the vision of Viṣṇu your evil actions have been quelled." (35.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा पुनरप्याह गतक्रोधो मुनीश्वरः ।
शिवं संस्मृत्य मनसा ज्ञानदं भुक्तिमुक्तिदम् ॥ ३६॥

English
After saying this, the sage was freed of his fury on thinking of Śiva, the bestower of wisdom, worldly pleasures and salvation. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अपरं श‍ृणुत प्रीत्या मद्वचः सुखदं सदा ।
धन्या यूयं शिवप्रीता मान्या पूज्या ह्यभीक्ष्णशः ॥ ३७॥

English
Listen further to my words always pleasing to you. You are all blessed by Śiva's pleasure. Hence you will be worthy of honour and respect immediately. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनायास्तनया देवी पार्वती जगदम्बिका ।
भविष्यति प्रिया शम्भोस्तपः कृत्वा सुदुस्सहम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Menā's daughter, goddess Pārvatī, the mother of the universe shall become Śiva's beloved after performing severe penance. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्यासुता स्मृता सीता रामपत्नी भविष्यति ।
लौकिकाचारमाश्रित्य रामेण विहरिष्यति ॥ ३९॥

English
Dhanyā's daughter Sītā will become Rāma's wife. Based on worldly conventions she will sport about with Rāma. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
कलावतीसुता राधा साक्षाद् गोलोकवासिनी ।
गुप्तस्नेहनिबद्धा सा कृष्णपत्नी भविष्यति ॥ ४०॥

English
Kalāvatī's daughter Rādhā, resident of Goloka⁶ shall become the wife of Kṛṣṇa united with him in secret love. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थमाभाष्य स मुनिर्भ्रातृभिः सह संस्तुतः ।
सनत्कुमारो भगवाँस्तत्रैवान्तर्हितोऽभवत् ॥ ४१॥

English
### *Brahmā said*  After saying this, that holy sage Sanatkumāra vanished there itself along with his brothers after he was eulogised duly. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तिस्रो भगिन्यस्तास्तात पितृणां मानसीः सुताः ।
गतपापाः सुखं प्राप्य स्वधाम प्रययुर्द्रुतम् ॥ ४२॥

English
The three sisters, the mentally conceived daughters of the forefathers were freed of their sins and attained happiness. They returned to their residence quickly. (42.)
