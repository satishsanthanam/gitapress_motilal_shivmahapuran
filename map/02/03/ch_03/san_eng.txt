### **CHAPTER THREE - (Hymn to Śiva by Viṣṇu and other gods)**

---

Sanskrit
३

नारद उवाच ।
विधे प्राज्ञ महाधीमन्वद मे वदतां वर ।
ततः परं किमभवच्चरितं विष्णुसद्गुरोः ॥ १॥

English
Nārada said:—  O Brahmā, the best of eloquent ones, of great wisdom, please tell me. What is the subsequent story of the good auspicious mountain ? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्भुतेयं कथा प्रोक्ता मेनापूर्वगतिः शुभा ।
विवाहश्च श्रुतः सम्यक् परमं चरितं वद ॥ २॥

English
You have narrated the wonderful story of the auspicious antecedents of Menā. The details of the marital rites too are heard. Please continue the subsequent narrative. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनां विवाह्य स गिरिः कृतवान् किं ततः परम् ।
पार्वती कथमुत्पन्ना तस्यां वै जगदम्बिका ॥ ३॥

English
After marrying Menā what did the mountain do afterwards ? How was Pārvatī, the mother of the universe, born of her ? (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तपः सुदुस्सहं कृत्वा कथं प्राप पतिं हरम् ।
एतत्सर्वं समाचक्ष्व विस्तराच्छाङ्करं यशः ॥ ४॥

English
How did she secure Śiva as her husband after performing a severe penance ? Narrate all these things in detail regarding the glory of Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
मुने त्वं श‍ृणु सुप्रीत्या शाङ्करं सुयशः शुभम् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा ब्रह्महा शुद्ध्येत्सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O sage, lovingly listen to the auspicious glory of Śiva on hearing which even a slayer of a brahmin becomes pure and attains all desires. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा मेनाविवाहं तु कृत्वागच्छद्गिरिर्गृहम् ।
तदा समुत्सवो जातस्त्रिषु लोकेषु नारद ॥ ६॥

English
It is a grove of Indra, lying to the north of Meru. Himācala bowed to them gladly and honoured them with devotion. He praised his own good fortune. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचलोऽपि सुप्रीतश्चकार परमोत्सवम् ।
भूसुरान् बन्धुवर्गांश्च परानानर्च सद्धिया ॥ ७॥

English
The delighted Himācala too celebrated a great festival. With good intention he worshipped and revered brahmins, kinsmen and others. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे द्विजाश्च सन्तुष्टा दत्त्वाशीर्वचनं वरम् ।
ययुस्तस्मै स्वस्वधाम बन्धुवर्गास्तथापरे ॥ ८॥

English
The contented brahmins returned to their respective abodes after blessing them. The kinsmen and the others also returned. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचलोऽपि सुप्रीतो मेनया सुखदे गृहे ।
रेमेऽन्यत्र च सुस्थाने नन्दनादिवनेष्वपि ॥ ९॥

English
The delighted Himacāla⁶ sported with Menā in his cosy abode, Nandana and other parks as well as in several nice places. (9.)

[fn 6. It is a grove of Indra, lying to the north of Meru.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे देवा मुने विष्ण्वादयोऽखिलाः ।
मुनयश्च महात्मानः प्रजग्मुर्भूधरान्तिके ॥ १०॥

English
At that time, O sage, Viṣṇu, the gods and the noble-sould sages approached the mountain. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तानागतान्देवान्प्रणनाम मुदा गिरिः ।
सम्मानं कृतवान् भक्त्या प्रशंसन् स्वविधिं महान् ॥ ११॥

English
On seeing the gods after their arrival, the noble (11.)

---

Sanskrit
साञ्जलिर्नतशीर्षो हि स तुष्टाव सुभक्तितः ।
रोमोद्गमो महानासीद्गिरेः प्रेमाश्रवोऽपतन् ॥ १२॥

English
With the head bent down and palms joined in reverence, he eulogised them with great devotion. Himācala's hair stood on end and tears of love fell from his eyes. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रणम्य सुप्रीतो हिमशैलः प्रसन्नधीः ।
उवाच प्रणतो भूत्वा मुने विष्ण्वादिकान्सुरान् ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, after bowing to them, the delighted Himācala spoke thus to Viṣṇu and other gods. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचल उवाच ।
अद्य मे सफलं जन्म सफलं सुमहत्तपः ।
अद्य मे सफलं ज्ञानमद्य मे सफलाः क्रियाः ॥ १४॥

English
*Himācala said:—*  Today my life has become fruitful. My good penance has become fruitful. Today my knowledge has become fruitful. Today my sacred rites have become fruitful. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्योऽहमद्य सञ्जातो धन्या मे सकला क्षितिः ।
धन्यं कुलं तथा दाराः सर्वं धन्यं न संशयः ॥ १५॥

English
I have become blessed today. My entire kingdom, my wife and family have become blessed. Everything has become blessed. There is no doubt about it. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यतः समागता यूयं मिलित्वा सर्व एकदा ।
मां निदेशयत प्रीत्योचितं मत्वा स्वसेवकम् ॥ १६॥

English
Wherefore have all of you come in a body? Lovingly command me, considering me your own servant. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा महीध्रस्य वचनं ते सुरास्तदा ।
ऊचुर्हर्यादयः प्रीताः सिद्धिं मत्वा स्वकार्यतः ॥ १७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Himācala, Viṣṇu and other gods considered their affair fulfilled and were delighted. They spoke. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
हिमाचल महाप्राज्ञ श‍ृण्वस्मद्वचनं हितम् ।
यदर्थमागताः सर्वे तद् ब्रूमः प्रीतितो वयम् ॥ १८॥

English
*Gods said:—*  "O Himācala of great intellect, please listen to our beneficent words. We shall gladly tell you why we have come. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
या पुरा जगदम्बोमा दक्षकन्याभवद्गिरे ।
रुद्रपत्नी हि सा भूत्वा चिक्रीडे सुचिरं भुवि ॥ १९॥

English
O Himācala, the mother of the universe Umā, who was born as Dakṣa's daughter, became Rudra's wife and sported for a long time on the earth. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृतोऽनादरं प्राप्य संस्मृत्य स्वपणं सती ।
जगाम स्वपदं त्यक्त्वा तच्छरीरं तदाम्बिका ॥ २०॥

English
On being disrespected by her father, Satī remembered her vow, abandoned her body and returned to her own region.

21-22 O Himācala, this story is well known in the world. You too know it. If this takes place it will be an asset to all gods as well as to you. The gods too will be under your control." (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्य भवतश्चापि स्युः सर्वे ते वशाः सुराः । २२॥

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां हर्यादीनां गिरीश्वरः ।
तथास्त्विति प्रसन्नात्मा प्रोवाच न च आदरात् ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Viṣṇu and others the delighted lord Himācala said—“So be it” and worshipped them with respect. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते च समादिश्य तद्विधिं परमादरात् ।
स्वयं जग्मुश्च शरणमुमायाः शङ्करस्त्रियः ॥ २४॥

English
After instructing him in the method to be followed with great devotion they approached Umā, the consort of Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सुस्थले मनसा स्थित्वा सस्मरुर्जगदम्बिकाम् ।
प्रणम्य बहुशस्तत्र तुष्टुवुः श्रद्धया सुराः ॥ २५॥

English
They stationed themselves in a good place and remembered the mother of the universe, and bowing to her repeatedly eulogised her with devotion. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देव्युमे जगतामम्ब शिवलोकनिवासिनि ।
सदाशिवप्रिये दुर्गे त्वां नमामो महेश्वरि ॥ २६॥

English
*The gods said:—*  O goddess Umā, mother of the universe, resident of Śivaloka, favourite of Śiva, O great goddess, O Durgā, we bow to you, (26.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशक्तिं पावनां शान्तां पुष्टिं परमपावनीम् ।
वयं नमामहे भक्त्या महदव्यक्तरूपिणीम् ॥ २७॥

English
With great devotion we bow to the illustrious Energy, the holy, the tranquil, the holy nourishment and the one with the forms of Mahat and the Avyakta. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवां शिवकरां शुद्धां स्थूलां सूक्ष्मां परायणाम् ।
अन्तर्विद्यासुविद्याभ्यां सुप्रीतां त्वां नमामहे ॥ २८॥

English
We worship you, Śiva the cause of welfare, the pure, the gross, the subtle, the great goal and the one delighted with the inner and good learning. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं श्रद्धा त्वं धृतिस्त्वं श्रीस्त्वमेव सर्वगोचरा ।
त्वं दीधितिस्सूर्यगता स्वप्रपञ्चप्रकाशिनी ॥ २९॥

English
You are faith, fortitude and prosperity. You alone have control over everything; you are the splendour and energy of the sun illuminating your own universe. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
या च ब्रह्माण्डसंस्थाने जगज्जीवेषु या जगत् ।
आप्याययति ब्रह्मादितृणान्तं तां नमामहे ॥ ३०॥

English
We bow to her who promotes robustness in all the beings of the universe from Brahmā to a blade of grass in the whole Cosmos. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
गायत्री त्वं वेदमाता त्वं सावित्री सरस्वती ।
त्वं वार्ता सर्वजगतां त्वं त्रयी धर्मरूपिणी ॥ ३१॥

English
You are Gāyatri, the mother of the Vedas, Sāvitri, Sarasvatī, the sustenance of all the universe; you are the triad of the Vedas having Dharma for its form. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
निद्रा त्वं सर्वभूतेषु क्षुधा तृप्तिस्त्वमेव हि ।
तृष्णा कान्तिश्छविस्तुष्टिः सर्वानन्दकरी सदा ॥ ३२॥

English
You are sleep in all living beings; you are hunger, satiety, thirst, splendour, brilliance and contentment. You are the delighter of every one for ever. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं लक्ष्मीः पुण्यकर्तॄणां त्वं ज्येष्ठा पापिनां सदा ।
त्वं शान्तिः सर्वजगतां त्वं धात्री प्राणपोषिणी ॥ ३३॥

English
To those who perform meritorious actions you are the goddess of fortune. To the sinners you are the eldest sister, the deity of Ignominy; you are peace for the universe, and the mother sustaining lives. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं तत्त्वरूपा भूतानां पञ्चानामपि सारकृत् ।
त्वं हि नीतिभृतां नीतिर्व्यवसायस्वरूपिणी ॥ ३४॥

English
You are the essential feature of five elements. You are Justice in those who uphold justice. You are endeavour personified. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गीतिस्त्वं सामवेदस्य ग्रन्थिस्त्वं यजुषां हुतिः ।
ऋग्वेदस्य तथा मात्राथर्वणस्य परा गतिः ॥ ३५॥

English
Of the Ṛgveda you are the invocation; of the Yajurveda you are the blending knot of the mantras; of Sāmaveda you are the song and of the Atharvaṇa Veda you are the measure of time, you are the final goal. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
समस्तगीर्वाणगणस्य शक्ति-
स्तमोमयी धातृगुणैकदृश्या ।
रजः प्रपञ्चात्तु भवैकरूपा
या नः श्रुता भव्यकरी स्तुतेह ॥ ३६॥

English
She who is the Tāmasika power of all the Gods, she who is visible in the Rājasika quality of the Creator, she who is heard by us as the benefactress and of the form of Śiva is eulogised here. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
संसारसागरकरालभवाङ्गदुःख-
निस्तारकारितरणिं च निवीतहीनाम् ।
अष्टाङ्गयोगपरिपालनकेलिदक्षां
विन्ध्यागवासनिरतां प्रणमाम तां वै ॥ ३७॥

English
Let us bow to her who is interested in residing on the Vindhya mountains;⁷ who is clever in the playful activity of affording protection to Aṣṭāṅga Yoga; who is devoid of cessation and who acts like a raft that enables the crossing of the ocean of worldly existence with its terrible miseries. (37.)

[fn 7. As Vindhyavāsinī, dweller in the Vindhyas, the Goddess is worshipped at a place where the Vindhyas approach the Ganges, near Mirzapur.]
---

Sanskrit
नासाक्षिवक्त्रभुजवक्षसि मानसे च
धृत्या सुखानि वितनोति सदैव जन्तोः ।

English
May she be pleased with us, for keeping up the sustenance of the world, she, who in the form of slumber that is extremely exhilarating to all born in the universe, extends pleasure in the nose, eyes, face, arms, chest and the mind. (38.)

Sanskrit
