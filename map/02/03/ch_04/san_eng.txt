### **CHAPTER FOUR - *(The Goddess consoles the Gods)***

---

Sanskrit
४

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थं देवैः स्तुता देवी दुर्गा दुर्गार्तिनाशिनी ।
आविर्बभूव देवानां पुरतो जगदम्बिका ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Thus eulogised by the Gods, the Goddess Durgā, the mother of the universe, the destroyer of impassable distress, appeared in front of them. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
रथे रत्नमये दिव्ये संस्थिता परमाद्भुते ।
किङ्किणीजालसंयुक्ते मृदुसंस्तरणे वरे ॥ २॥

English
She was seated in a wonderful divine gem-set chariot over which a soft cushion had been spread and which was decorated with tinkling ornaments. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिसूर्याधिकाभासरम्यावयवभासिनी ।
स्वतेजोराशिमध्यस्था वररूपासमच्छविः ॥ ३॥

English
She was shining with the brilliance of her limbs that surpassed even the lustre of a crore of suns. She was sur- (3.)

---


Sanskrit
अनूपमा महामाया सदाशिवविलासिनी ।
त्रिगुणा निर्गुणा नित्या शिवलोकनिवासिनी ॥ ४॥

English
She was the unequalled supreme illusion, the beautiful wife of Sadāśiva. She had all the three qualities and was devoid of attributes⁸ also, she had been staying in the region of Śiva. (4.)

[fn 8. As the personified energy of the gods Viṣṇu, Brahmā and Rudra, representing the three qualities Sattva, Rajas and Tamas, the Goddess is called 'Trigunā' i.e. possessed of three qualities. But as the personified energy of Śiva, who is the Supreme Being, devoid of attributes she is called Nirguṇā.]


---

Sanskrit
त्रिदेवजननी चण्डी शिवा सर्वार्तिनाशिनी ।
सर्वमाता महानिद्रा सर्वस्वजनतारिणी ॥ ५॥

English
She was the mother of the three deities⁹, Caṇḍī, Śivā, the destroyer of the distress of all, the mother of all supreme slumber and the redeemer of all her own people. (5.)

[fn 9. She is the mother-goddess of Viṣṇu, Brahmā and Rudra, the synthetic form of three qualities responsible for the creation, maintenance and dissolution of the universe.]
---

Sanskrit
तेजोराशेः प्रभावात्तु सा तु दृष्टा सुरैश्शिवा ।
तुष्टुवुस्तां पुनस्ते वै सुरा दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ ६॥

English
Śivā was seen by the gods through the power of huge column of brilliance. Again the gods eulogised her in order to have a sight of her. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवगणाः सर्वे विष्ण्वाद्या दर्शनेप्सवः ।
ददृशुर्जगदम्बां तां तत्कृपां प्राप्य तत्र हि ॥ ७॥

English
Then Viṣṇu and other gods who were desirous of seeing her saw the mother of the universe there itself after receiving her favour. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवानन्दसन्दोहः सर्वेषां त्रिदिवौकसाम् ।
पुनः पुनः प्रणेमुस्तां तुष्टुवुश्च विशेषतः ॥ ८॥

English
The dwellers of heaven were extremely pleased, they bowed to her again and again and particularly eulogised her. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
शिवे शर्वाणि कल्याणि जगदम्ब महेश्वरि ।
त्वां नताः सर्वथा देवा वयं सर्वार्तिनाशिनीम् ॥ ९॥

English
*The gods said :—*  O Śivā, O great Goddess, O mother of the universe we gods bow to you, the destroyer of all distress. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
न हि जानन्ति देवेशि वेदाः शास्त्राणि कृत्स्नशः ।
अतीतो महिमा ध्यानं तव वाङ्मनसोः शिवे ॥ १०॥

English
O Goddess, neither the Vedas nor the sacred texts know you perfectly. Your greatness, O Śivā, is beyond the scope of speech and mind and cannot even be meditated upon. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अतद्व्यावृत्तितस्त्वां वै चकितं चकितं सदा ।
अभिधत्ते श्रुतिरपि परेषां का कथा मता ॥ ११॥

English
Even the Vedas mention you, trembling with fright, by negating what you are not. What will be the matter in regard to others ? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
जानन्ति बहवो भक्तास्त्वत्कृपां प्राप्य भक्तितः ।
शरणागतभक्तानां न कुत्रापि भयादिकम् ॥ १२॥

English
Many devotees know the same after getting your favour through devotion. There is no cause for fear to the devotees who seek refuge in you. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञप्तिं श‍ृणु सुप्रीता यस्या दासाः सदाम्बिके ।
तव देवि महादेवि हीनतो वर्णयामहे ॥ १३॥

English
O great Goddess listen to our submission which we, your slaves for ever, are going to explain. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा दक्षसुता भूत्वा सञ्जाता हरवल्लभा ।
ब्रह्मणश्च परेषां वानाशयत्त्वमकं महत् ॥ १४॥

English
Formerly you were born as the daughter of Dakṣa and were married to Śiva. You destroyed the great misery of Brahmā and others. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृतोऽनादरं प्राप्यात्यजः पणवशात्तनुम् ।
स्वलोकमगमस्त्वं वालभद्दुखं हरोऽपि हि ॥ १५॥

English
Being disrespected by your father, you cast off your body in accordance with your vow. You then went to your own world and Śiva became miserable. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
न हि जातं प्रपूर्णं तद्देवकार्यं महेश्वरि ।
व्याकुला मुनयो देवाः शरणं त्वां गता वयम् ॥ १६॥

English
O great Goddess, the purpose of the gods has not been completely carried out. The sages are agitated. Hence we, Gods, have sought refuge in you. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्णं कुरु महेशानि निर्जराणां मनोरथम् ।
सनत्कुमारवचनं सफलं स्याद्यथा शिवे ॥ १७॥

English
O great Goddess, please fulfil the desire of the God, O Śivā, so that the words of Sanatkumāra may be fruitful. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अवतीर्य क्षितौ देवि रुद्रपत्नी पुनर्भव ।
लीलां कुरु यथायोग्यं प्राप्नुयुर्निर्जराः सुखम् ॥ १८॥

English
O Goddess, incarnating again on the earth please be the wife of Rudra (Śiva) again. Carry on your sports in a fitting manner and let the Gods be happy. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखी स्याद्देवि रुद्रोऽपि कैलासाचलसंस्थितः ।
सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनो दुःखं नश्यतु कृत्स्नशः ॥ १९॥

English
O Goddess, may Rudra too, the resident of Kailāsa be happy. Let all become happy. Let misery perish entirely. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति प्रोच्यामराः सर्वे विष्ण्वाद्याः प्रेमसङ्कुलाः ।
मौनमास्थाय सन्तस्थुर्भक्तिनम्रात्ममूर्तयः ॥ २०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Saying so, Viṣṇu and the other gods, full of loving devotion remained waiting silently and humbly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवापि सुप्रसन्नाभूदाकर्ण्यामरसंस्तुतिम् ।
आकलय्याथ तद्धेतुं संस्मृत्य स्वप्रभुं शिवम् ॥ २१॥

उवाचोमा तदा देवी सम्बोध्य विबुधांश्च तान् ।
विहस्य मापतिमुखान् सदया भक्तवत्सला ॥ २२॥

English
Śivā too was delighted on hearing the eulogy of the gods and ascertaining the course of the same after remembering her lord Śiva, the compassionate Umā addressed smilingly the gods, chief of whom was Viṣṇu. The Goddess, favourably disposed to her devotees, said:— (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
उमोवाच
हे हरे हे विधे देवा मुनयश्च गतव्यथाः ।
सर्वे श‍ृणुत मद्वाक्यं प्रसन्नाहं न संशयः ॥ २३॥

English
*Umā said :—*  O Viṣṇu, O, Brahmā, O Gods and sages who are free from sorrow and pain ye listen to my words. I am delighted undoubtedly. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
चरितं मम सर्वत्र त्रैलोक्यस्य सुखावहम् ।
कृतं मयैवं सकलं दक्षमोहादिकं च तत् ॥ २४॥

English
My activities are conducive to happiness everywhere in the three worlds. The delusion of Dakṣa and other things were carried out by me alone. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अवतारं करिष्यामि क्षितौ पूर्णं न संशयः ।
बहवो हेतवोऽप्यत्र तद्वदामि महादरात् ॥ २५॥

English
I shall take a full incarnation on the earth. There is no doubt in this. There are many reasons for the same. I shall mention them with respect. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा हिमाचलो देवा मेना चातिसुभक्तितः ।
सेवां मे चक्रतुस्तातजननीवत्सतीतनोः ॥ २६॥

English
Formerly, O gods, with great devotion Himācala and Menā rendered service to me in my life as Sati, like my parents. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं कुरुतः सेवां सुभक्त्या मम नित्यशः ।
मेना विशेषतस्तत्र सुतात्वे नात्र संशयः ॥ २७॥

English
Even now they continuously render me service and Menā particularly (does so). There is no doubt about my becoming their daughter. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रो गच्छतु यूयं चावतारं हिमवद्गृहे ।
अतश्चावतरिष्यामि दुःखनाशो भविष्यति ॥ २८॥

English
Just as you, Rudra too, desires my incarnation in the abode of Himavat. Hence I shall incarnate. That shall be the end of misery in the world. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे गच्छत धाम स्वं स्वं सुखं लभतां चिरम् ।
अवतीर्य सुता भूत्वा मेनाया दास्य उत्सुखम् ॥ २९॥

English
All of you return to your abodes. You shall be happy for a long time. After incarnating I shall give Menā full happiness. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
हरपत्नी भविष्यामि सुगुप्तं मतमात्मनः ।
अद्भुता शिवलीला हि ज्ञानिनामपि मोहिनी ॥ ३०॥

English
I shall become Śiva’s wife. But this desire is a great secret with me. Śiva’s divine sport is wonderful. It deludes even the wise. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्प्रभृति मे त्यक्ता स्वतनुर्दक्षजा सुराः ।
पितृतोऽनादरं दृष्ट्वा स्वामिनस्तत्क्रतौ गता ॥ ३१॥

तदाप्रभृति स स्वामी रुद्रः कालाग्निसंज्ञकः ।
दिगम्बरो बभूवाशु मच्चिन्तनपरायणः ॥ ३२॥

English
Ever since I cast off my body born of Dakṣa on seeing my lord’s disrespect at the hands of my father at the altar of sacrifice, my lord Rudra is tormented by thoughts about me. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
मम रोषं क्रतौ दृष्ट्वा पितुस्तत्र गता सती ।
अत्यजत्स्वतनुं प्रीत्या धर्मज्ञेति विचारतः ॥ ३३॥

योग्यभूत्सदनं त्यक्त्वा कृत्वा वेषमलौकिकम् ।
न सेहे विरहं सत्या मद्रूपाया महेश्वरः ॥ ३४॥

English
He saw my anger at the altar of my father’s sacrifice. Thinking that the virtuous lady had cast-off her body out of love for him he became a Yogin and abandoned home-life. He assumed an unearthly form and features. But he could not bear my separation. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
मम हेतोर्महादुःखी स बभूव कुवेषभृत् ।
अत्यजत्स तदारभ्य कामजं सुखमुत्तमम् ॥ ३५॥

English
On account of me he was much distressed. He put on an abnormal dress. Ever since he forsook the excellent pleasure of love. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यच्छृणुत हे विष्णो हे विधे मुनयः सुराः ।
महाप्रभोर्महेशस्य लीलां भुवनपालिनीम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Hear further, O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O sages and O gods, the divine sports of the supreme lord Śiva, that protect the universe. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
विधाय मालां सुप्रीत्या ममास्थ्नां विरहाकुलः ।
न शान्तिं प्राप कुत्रापि प्रबुद्धोऽप्येक एव सः ॥ ३७॥

English
Oppressed by the pangs of bereavement He wreathed a garland of my bones. Although He is the sole enlightened god He did not get peace anywhere. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इतस्ततो रुरोदोच्चैरनीश इव स प्रभुः ।
योग्यायोग्यं न बुबुधे भ्रमन्सर्वत्र सर्वदा ॥ ३८॥

English
Like a non-god, like a helpless creature he roamed about here and there and cried aloud. The lord Himself could not distinguish between the proper and the improper. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं लीलां हरोऽकार्षीद्दर्शयन्कामिनां प्रभुः ।
ऊचे कामुकवद्वाणीं विरहव्याकुलामिव ॥ ३९॥

English
The lord Śiva did this just to show the behaviour of a love-lorn lover. He blabbered like a lover in despair due to separation. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्तुतोऽविकृतोऽदीनोऽस्त्यजितः परमेश्वरः ।
परिपूर्णः शिवः स्वामी मायाधीशोऽखिलेश्वरः ॥ ४०॥

English
But really the supreme lord has no aberrations, is not distressed and remains unconquered. My master Śiva is perfect, lord of all and the controller of illusion. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा मोहतस्तस्य किं कामाच्च प्रयोजनम् ।
विकारेणापि केनाशु मायालिप्तो न स प्रभुः ॥ ४१॥

English
He is not tarnished by illusion. Of what avail are illusion, love and other emotions for Him? (41.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रोऽतीवेच्छति विभुः स मे कर्तुं करग्रहम् ।
अवतारं क्षितौ मेनाहिमाचलगृहे सुराः ॥ ४२॥

English
Rudra, the lord, is anxious to marry me and hence my incarnation on the Earth at the residence of Menā and Himācala, O gods. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
अतश्चावतरिष्यामि रुद्रसन्तोषहेतवे ।
हिमागपत्न्यां मेनायां लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रिता ॥ ४३॥

English
In order to propitiate Rudra, I shall incarnate as the daughter of Menā, the wife of Himācala, in accordance with the way of the world. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्ता रुद्रप्रिया भूत्वा तपः कृत्वा सुदुस्सहम् ।
देवकार्यं करिष्यामि सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ४४॥

English
After performing a severe penance as His devotee I shall become Rudra's beloved and then perform the work of the gods. This is truth, real truth, there is no doubt about it. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
गच्छत स्वगृहं सर्वे भवं भजत नित्यशः ।
तत्कृपातोऽखिलं दुःखं विनश्यति न संशयः ॥ ४५॥

English
All of you return to your abodes. Continuously worship Śiva. Undoubtedly your miseries will be quelled by His favour. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
भविष्यति कृपालोस्तु कृपया मङ्गलं सदा ।
वन्द्या पूज्या त्रिलोकेऽहं तज्जायेति च हेतुतः ॥ ४६॥

English
By the grace of merciful lord Śiva, you will achieve auspicious results. As the wife of that lord I shall be honoured and worshipped in the world. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा जगदम्बा सा देवानां पश्यतां तदा ।
अन्तर्दधे शिवा तात स्वं लोकं प्राप वै द्रुतम् ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear, even as the gods were watching, Śivā, the mother of the universe, vanished after saying this and returned immediately to her world. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्ण्वादयः सुराः सर्वे मुनयश्च मुदान्विताः ।
कृत्वा तद्दिशि सन्नामं स्वस्वधामानि संययुः ॥ ४८॥

English
After making obeisance to the direction in which she went, the delighted Viṣṇu and others, sages and the gods, returned to their abodes. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं दुर्गासुचरितं वर्णितं ते मुनीश्वर ।
सर्वदा सुखदं नॄणां भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायकम् ॥ ४९॥

English
O excellent sages, thus I have narrated to you the auspicious narrative of the goddess Durgā. It is always pleasing to men and it bestows worldly pleasures and salvation. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

पठेद्वा पाठयेद्वापि सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ ५०॥

English
Whoever hears or recites this with concentration, reads or teaches this, will obtain the fruits of all desires. (50.)
