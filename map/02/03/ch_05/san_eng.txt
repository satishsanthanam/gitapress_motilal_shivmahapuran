### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(Menā obtains the boon)***

---

Sanskrit
५

नारद उवाच ।
अन्तर्हितायां देव्यां तु दुर्गायां स्वगृहेषु च ।
गतेष्वमरवृन्देषु किमभूत्तदनन्तरम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  When the Goddess Durgā vanished and the gods returned to their abodes, what happened next? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं मेनागिरीशौ च तेपाते परमं तपः ।
कथं सुताभवत्तस्य मेनायां तात तद्वद ॥ २॥

English
O dear, how did Menā and the lord of the mountains perform the great penance? How did he beget a daughter of Menā. Please narrate. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
विप्रवर्य सुतश्रेष्ठ श‍ृणु तच्चरितं महत् ।
प्रणम्य शङ्करं भक्त्या वच्मि भक्तिविवर्धनम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O best of brahmins, O most excellent of my sons, listen to that great account. After bowing to Śiva with devotion I shall narrate that story which increases devotion. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
उपदिश्य गते तात सुरवृन्दे गिरीश्वरः ।
हर्यादौ मेनका चापि तेपाते परमं तपः ॥ ४॥

English
When Viṣṇu and other gods returned after instructing him, the lord of the mountains and Menā performed a great penance. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अहर्निशं शिवां शम्भुं चिन्तयन्तौ च दम्पती ।
सम्यगारेधतुर्नित्यं भक्तियुक्तेन चेतसा ॥ ५॥

English
Meditating on Śivā and Śiva day and night with devout mind, the couple worshipped them continuously. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिप्रियातीव मुदानर्च देवीं शिवेन सा ।
दानं ददौ द्विजेभ्यश्च सदा तत्तोषहेतवे ॥ ६॥

English
The beloved of the mountain worshipped the goddess along with Śiva, joyously. She gave charitable gifts always to the brahmins for their satisfaction. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
चैत्रमासं समारभ्य सप्तविंशतिवत्सरान् ।
शिवां सम्पूजयामासापत्यार्थिन्यन्वहं रता ॥ ७॥

English
Desirous of obtaining a child, she worshipped Śivā everyday for twenty-seven years beginning it in the month of March-April. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टम्यामुपवासं तु कृत्वादान्नवमीतिथौ ।
मोदकैर्बलिपिष्टैश्च पायसैर्गन्धपुष्पकैः ॥ ८॥

English
Observing a fast on the eighth day of the lunar fortnight, she made charitable gifts of sweets, offerings of oblation rice cakes, puddings and fragrant flowers on the ninth day. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गायामौषधिप्रस्थे कृत्वा मूर्तिं महीमयीम् ।
उमायाः पूजयामास नानावस्तुसमर्पणैः ॥ ९॥

English
She made clay idol of the Goddess and worshipped her by offering various things on the banks of the Gaṅgā in Auṣadhiprastha.$^{10}$ (9.)

[fn 10. It was the capital of Himavat. Cf. Kālidāsa's Kumāra 6. 33, 36. The name indicates that it was a market place for the mountainous herbs.]
---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्सा निराहारा कदाचित्सा धृतव्रता ।
कदाचित्पवनाहारा कदाचिज्जलभुघ्यभूत् ॥ १०॥

English
On some days she observed a complete fast. On some days she observed sacred rites. Some days wind alone constituted her food and some days she drank only water. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाविन्यस्तचेतस्का सप्तविंशतिवत्सरान् ।
निनाय मेनका प्रीत्या परं सा मृष्टवर्चसा ॥ ११॥

English
With her mind fixed on Śivā, Menā passed twenty seven years with pleasure and brilliant lustre. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तविंशतिवर्षान्ते जगन्माता जगन्मयी ।
सुप्रीताभवदत्यर्थमुमा शङ्करकामिनी ॥ १२॥

English
At the end of twenty-seven years, Umā the beloved of Śiva, the mother of the world and identical with the universe became highly delighted. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहाय मेनायाः पुरतः परमेश्वरी ।
आविर्बभूव सा देवी सन्तुष्टा तत्सुभक्तितः ॥ १३॥

English
The goddess Śivā, delighted by her good devotion appeared in front of Menā in order to bless her. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यावयवसंयुक्ता तेजोमण्डलमध्यगा ।
उवाच विहसन्ती सा मेनां प्रत्यक्षतां गता ॥ १४॥

English
Appearing to her in a form of divine limbs through a lustrous zone, she smilingly said to Menā. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
वरं ब्रूहि महासाध्वि यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ।
सुप्रसन्ना च तपसा तवाहं गिरिकामिनि ॥ १५॥

English
*The Goddess said :—*  O beloved of the mountain, I am delighted by your penance. O chaste lady, tell me what you desire in your mind. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्प्रार्थितं त्वया मेने तपोव्रतसमाधिना ।
दास्ये तेऽहं च तत्सर्वं वाञ्छितं यद्यदा भवेत् ॥ १६॥

English
O Menā, whatever is desired by you by penance, sacred rites and ecstatic contemplation I shall grant you and that too whenever you wish for it. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःसा मेनका देवीं प्रत्यक्षां कालिकां तदा ।
दृष्ट्वा च प्रणनामाथ वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ १७॥

English
Then seeing the goddess in her presence Menā bowed and spoke these words:— (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच
देवि प्रत्यक्षतो रूपं दृष्टं तव मयाधुना ।
त्वामहं स्तोतुमिच्छामि प्रसन्ना भव कालिके ॥ १८॥

English
*Menā said:—*  O Goddess, your form has been perceived by me directly, just now. I wish to eulogise you. Be pleased. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
अथ सा मेनयेत्युक्ता कालिका सर्वमोहिनी ।
बाहुभ्यां सुप्रसन्नात्मा मेनकां परिषस्वजे ॥ १९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On being thus requested by Menā, the goddess Umā, the enchantress of everyone, embraced Menā and was highly delighted. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्राप्तमहाज्ञाना मेनका कालिकां शिवम् ।
तुष्टाव वाग्भिरिष्टाभिर्भक्त्या प्रत्यक्षतां गताम् ॥ २०॥

English
Acquiring very great wisdom, Menā eulogised Śivā, who had appeared in person, by means of pleasing words with great devotion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
महामायां जगद्धात्रीं चण्डिकां लोकधारिणीम् ।
प्रणमामि महादेवीं सर्वकामार्थदायिनीम् ॥ २१॥

English
*Menā said:—*  I bow to the great goddess,¹¹ the bestower of all desires, I bow to her who wields great illusion, the creator and sustainer of the universe. (21.)

[fn 11. The Goddess Śivā is mentioned here under a variety of names, forms, attributes and actions. She is contemplated upon by the ascetics for the deliverance from the bondage of this world. She is propitiated for the satiation of desires with the bloody sacrifices performed according to Atharvanic rites.

She is represented in her milder and fiercer forms. In her milder form she is pleasing as the moon, gracious as the mother! In her terrible aspect she is furious as the fire and scorching as the sun]
---

Sanskrit
नित्यानन्दकरीं मायां योगनिद्रां जगत्प्रसूम् ।
प्रणमामि सदा सिद्धां शुभसारसमालिनीम् ॥ २२॥

English
I bow to her of contemplative sleep, and to her the wielder of great illusion and the cause of permanent bliss. I bow to the mother of the universe. I bow to Siddhā having the garland of auspicious lotuses. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

आकल्पं वनितानां च प्राणिनां बुद्धिरूपिणीम् ॥ २३॥

English
I bow to the grandmother, of perpetual bliss. I bow to the goddess who dispels the sorrow of the devotees, (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सा त्वं बन्धच्छेदहेतुर्यतीनां
कस्ते गेयो मादृशीभिः प्रभावः ।
हिंसां या वाथर्ववेदस्य सा त्वं
नित्यं कामं त्वं ममेष्टं विधेहि ॥ २४॥

English
You are the cause of the snapping of all fetters of ascetics. Which one of your powers can be sung by women like me? You are violence mentioned in the Atharvaveda. You (of such powerful means) fulfil my desire. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नित्यानित्यैर्भावहीनैः परास्तै-
स्तत्तन्मात्रैर्योज्यते भूतवर्गः ।
तेषां शक्तिस्त्वं सदा नित्यरूपा

English
The living beings are being united to the different principles of the nature of permanence and otherwise and those without substance are discarded. You are the inherent power of those permanent principles. In the proper time you become a woman of ability with Yogic powers. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
योनिर्धरित्री जगतां त्वमेव

English
You are the origin and the sustainer of the worlds. You are the eternal Prakṛti, the great, by whom even the Brahman is brought under control. O you, of noble nature, O mother, be pleased with me. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

English
You are the great power latent in fire; you are the burning power of the sun's rays; you are the pleasing power of the extensive moonlight. O Goddess, I bow to you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
To good women you manifest yourself as their beloved; to persons of perfect self-control and sublimation you manifest yourself as eternal; to the entire universe you manifest as desire; as of Viṣṇu you are the Māyā so you are of Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
You assume different forms as you please for the purpose of creation, sustenance and annihilation and give birth to the bodies of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. You, of such potentiality, be pleased. Obeisance to you again. (29.)

English

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

Sanskrit
इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां तृतीये पार्वतीखण्डे मेनावरलाभवर्णनं नाम पञ्चमोऽध्यायः ॥ २.३.५॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥ 
द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां तृतीयः पार्वतीखण्डे
