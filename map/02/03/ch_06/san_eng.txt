### **CHAPTER SIX - (Pārvatī's birth)**

---

Sanskrit
६

ब्रह्मोवाच
अथ सस्मरतुर्भक्त्या दम्पती तौ भवाम्बिकाम् ।
प्रसूतिहेतवे तत्र देवकार्यार्थमादरात् ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said:—  Then the couple, with great devotion remembered the goddess for her birth in order to carry out the work of the gods. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सा चण्डिका योगात्त्यक्तदेहा पुरा पितुः ।
ईहया भवितुं भूयः समैच्छद् गिरिदारतः ॥ २॥

English
Then the Goddess who formerly had cast off her body to spite her father, by means of her Yogic powers, desired to be born of the wife of the mountain. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यं विधातुं स्ववचः प्रसन्नाखिलकामदा ।
पूर्णांशाच्छैलचित्ते सा विवेशाथ महेश्वरी ॥ ३॥

English
In order to make her own words true, the great goddess, who bestows everything desired, delightedly entered the mind of the mountain with all her constituent elements. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विरराज ततः सोऽति प्रमदोऽपूर्वसुद्युतिः ।
हुताशन इवाधृष्यस्तेजोराशिर्महामनाः ॥ ४॥

English
Hence he shone with an extraordinary splendour and great joy. With a resplendent brilliance, he, of lofty mind, became invincible like a blazing fire. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो गिरिः स्वप्रियायां परिपूर्णं शिवांशकम् ।
समाधिमत्वात्समये समधत्त सुशङ्करे ॥ ५॥

English
Then, in a beneficent hour, the lord of the mountains deposited in his beloved the entire constituent element of Siva by means of ecstatic contemplation. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
समधत्त गिरेः पत्नी गर्भं देव्याः प्रसादतः ।
चित्ते स्थितायाः करुणाकरायाः सुखदं गिरेः ॥ ६॥

English
The wife of the mountain, by the grace of the goddess who had sympathetically stationed herself in the mind of the mountain, conceived. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिप्रिया सर्वजगन्निवासासंश्रयाधिकम् ।
विरेजे सुतरां मेना तेजोमण्डलगा सदा ॥ ७॥

English
The beloved of the mountain, Menā, shone all the more by the presence of the goddess who bore the entire universe. She appeared as if she was in a brilliant sphere. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखोदयं स्वभर्तुश्च मेना दौर्हृदलक्षणम् ।
दधौ निदानं देवानामानन्दस्येप्सितं शुभम् ॥ ८॥

English
Menā bore the characteristic signs of pregnancy which almost indicated the imminent rise in pleasure of her lord and served as the auspicious cause for the future bliss of the gods. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देहसादादसम्पूर्णभूषणा लोध्रसम्मुखा ।
स्वल्पभेन्दुक्षयेकासौ विचेष्यर्क्षा विभावरी ॥ ९॥

English
The weakness of her body did not allow her to wear ornaments. Her face became pale like the Lodhra flower. She resembled the night when there are very few stars and the moon is in a waning state.[13] (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाननं मृत्सुरभि नायं तृप्तिं गिरीश्वरः ।
मुने रहस्युपाघ्राय प्रेमाधिक्यं बभूव तत् ॥ १०॥

English
Kissing her face, emitting the fragrance of the earth in the course of his secret dalliance, the lord of the mountains, was not satiated. His love increased.[14] (10.)

---

Sanskrit
मेना स्पृहावती केषु न मे शंसति वस्तुषु ।
किञ्चिदिष्टं ह्रियापृच्छदनुवेलं सखीर्गिरिः ॥ ११॥

English
The lord of mountains asked Menā's friends frequently—"What are the desires of Menā? She herself does not express them out of bashfulness."[15] (11.)

---

Sanskrit
उपेत्य दौर्हृदं शैल्यं यद्वव्रेऽपश्यदाशु तत् ।
आनीतं नेष्टमस्याद्धा नासाध्यं त्रिदिवेऽपि हि ॥ १२॥

English
Whatever product of the mountain she wished to have in the course of her pregnancy she found brought to her. There was nothing which he, the lord of the mountain could not accomplish in the heaven too. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रचीयमानावयवा निस्तीर्य दौर्हृदव्यथाम् ।
रेजे मेना बाललता नद्धपत्राधिका यथा ॥ १३॥

English
Surmounting the difficulties of the early days of pregnancy, she grew more plump in her limbs. Menā then shone like a tender creeper putting forth more leaves and flowers. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिः सगर्भां महिषीममंस्त धरणीमिव ।
निधानगर्भामभ्यन्तर्लीनवह्निशमीमिव ॥ १४॥

English
The lord of the mountains considered his pregnant queen like the earth with a treasure within and like the Sami twig with latent fire in it.[16] (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रियाप्रीतेश्च मनसः स्वार्जितद्रविणस्य च ।
समुन्नतेः श्रुतेः प्राज्ञः क्रियाश्चक्रे यथोचिताः ॥ १५॥

English
The intelligent lord of mountains performed all the sacred rites befitting his love for his wife, the loftiness of his mind, the vastness of riches earned by him and the injunctions of the Vedas. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ददर्श काले मेनां स प्रतीतः प्रसवोन्मुखीम् ।
अभ्रितां च दिवं गर्भगृहे भिषगधिष्ठिते ॥ १६॥

दृष्ट्वा प्रियां शुभाङ्गीं वै मुमोदाति गिरीश्वरः ।
गर्भस्थजगदम्बां हि महातेजोवतीं तदा ॥ १७॥

English
At the proper time, he saw his wife Menā about to be delivered of the child, with delight, as one sees the sky enveloped with clouds. The lord of the mountains felt greatly rejoiced on seeing his wife of sound and auspicious in limbs the “labour-chamber” presided over by physicians. She felt very brilliant with the mother of the universe in her womb. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे देवा मुने विष्ण्वादयस्तथा ।
मुनयश्च समागम्य गर्भस्थां तुष्टुवुः शिवाम् ॥ १८॥

English
In the mean time, O sage, Viṣṇu, and other gods as well as the sages came there and eulogised Śivā who was in the womb. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः
दुर्गे जय जय प्राज्ञे जगदम्ब महेश्वरि ।
सत्यव्रते सत्यपरे त्रिसत्ये सत्यरूपिणी ॥ १९॥

English
*The gods said:—*  O Goddess, be victorious, O intelligent one, O mother of the universe, O great Goddess, O you of true rites, prone to truth, true in three things, O truth-formed. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यस्थे सत्यसुप्रीते सत्ययोने च सत्यतः ।
सत्यवक्त्रे सत्यनेत्रे प्रपन्नाः शरणं च ते ॥ २०॥

English
O you stationed in truth, we have sought refuge in you. O you delighted with truth, Origin of truth, Truth of Truth, of truthful sight. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवप्रिये महेशानि देवदुःखक्षयङ्करि ।
त्रैलोक्यमाता शर्वाणी व्यापिनी भक्तवत्सला ॥ २१॥

English
O beloved of Śiva, great goddess, O destroyer of the miseries of gods, you are the mother of the three worlds, consort of Śiva, pervasive and favourably disposed to your devotees. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
आविर्भूय त्रिलोकेशि देवकार्यं कुरुष्व ह ।
सनाथाः कृपया ते हि वयं सर्वे महेश्वरि ॥ २२॥

English
O goddess of the three worlds, manifest yourself and perform the function of the gods. O goddess, all of us are well protected only due to your favour. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्तः सर्वे च सुखिनो लभन्ते सुखमुत्तमम् ।
त्वां विना न हि किञ्चिद्वै शोभते त्रिभवेष्वपि ॥ २३॥

English
Happy persons attain their happiness only from you. Nothing shines in the three worlds without you. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्थं कृत्वा महेशान्या गर्भस्थाया बहुस्तुतिम् ।
प्रसन्नमनसो देवाः स्वं स्वं धाम ययुस्तदा ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Thus eulogising, in many ways, the great goddess stationed in the womb, the gods returned to their abodes, highly delighted in their minds. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यतीते नवमे मासे दशमे मासि पूर्णतः ।
गर्भस्था सा गतिं दध्रे कालिका जगदम्बिका ॥ २५॥

English
When nine months were completed, in the tenth month, the goddess, the mother of the universe, bore all the states of a child in the womb in the complete form. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सुसमयश्चासीच्छान्तभग्रहतारकः ।
नभः प्रसन्नतां यातं प्रकाशः सर्वदिक्षु हि ॥ २६॥

English
The time was good. The planets, stars and the luminary heavenly bodies were quiet; the sky was clear and there was brilliance in all the quarters. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मही मङ्गलभूयिष्ठा सवनग्रामसागरा ।
सरःस्रवन्तीवापीषु पुफुल्लुः पङ्कजानि वै ॥ २७॥

English
The earth consisting of forests, villages and oceans was very auspicious. Lotuses blossomed in lakes, rivers and tanks. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ववुश्च विविधा वाताः सुखस्पर्शा मुनीश्वर ।
मुमुदुः साधवः सर्वेऽसतां दुःखमभूद् द्रुतम् ॥ २८॥

English
O excellent sage, diverse winds gentle to the touch blew; good men rejoiced and bad people became unhappy. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
दुन्दुभीन्वादयामासुर्नभस्यागत्य निर्जराः ।
पुष्पवृष्टिरभूत्तत्र जगुर्गन्धर्वसत्तमाः ॥ २९॥

English
The gods stood in the sky and sounded big drums. A shower of flowers fell. Excellent Gandharvas sang sweet songs. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्याधरस्त्रियो व्योम्नि ननृतुश्चाप्सरास्तथा ।
तदोत्सवो महानासीद्देवादीनां नभःस्थले ॥ ३०॥

English
Vidyādhara women and the celestial nymphs danced in the sky; in the heavenly region great festivities were celebrated by the gods and others. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे देवी पूर्वशक्तिः शिवा सती ।
आविर्बभूव पुरतो मेनाया निजरूपतः ॥ ३१॥

English
At that time Śivā, Satī of perfect power formerly appeared in front of Menā in her real form. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
वसन्तर्तौ मधौ मासे नवम्यां मृगधिष्ण्यके ।
अर्धरात्रे समुत्पन्ना गङ्गेव शशिमण्डलात् ॥ ३२॥

English
She was born at midnight when the constellation Mṛgaśīras was in conjunction with the moon on the ninth day in the month of Madhu (March-April) in the spring season like the Gaṅgā from the moon's sphere. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
समये तत्स्वरूपेण मेनकाजठराच्छिवा ।
समुद्धूय समुत्पन्ना सा लक्ष्मीरिव सागरात् ॥ ३३॥

English
Coming out of the belly of Menā at the proper time in her real form, she resembled Lakṣmī coming out of the ocean. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तस्यां तु जातायां प्रसन्नोऽभूत्तदा भवः ।
अनुकूलो ववौ वायुर्गम्भीरो गन्धयुक् शुभः ॥ ३४॥

English
When she was born, Śiva was glad. A slow, fragrant and auspicious wind blew favourably. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूव पुष्पवृष्टिश्च तोयवृष्टिपुरस्सरम् ।
जज्वलुश्चाग्नयः शान्ता जगर्जुश्च तदा घनाः ॥ ३५॥

English
Along with the rain there was a shower of flowers. Fires calmly glowed and the clouds rumbled. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यां तु जायमानायां सर्वस्वं समपद्यत ।
हिमवन्नगरे तत्र सर्वं दुःखं क्षयं गतम् ॥ ३६॥

English
At the time of her birth, riches and prosperity flourished in the city of Himavat. All miseries perished. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे तत्र विष्ण्वाद्याः सकलाः सुराः ।
आजग्मुः सुखिनः प्रीत्या ददृशुर्जगदम्बिकाम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Viṣṇu and other gods reached there in time and saw the mother of the universe. They were delighted and happy. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तुष्टुवुस्तां शिवामम्बां कालिकां शिवकामिनीम् ।
दिव्यरूपां महामायां शिवलोकनिवासिनीम् ॥ ३८॥

English
They eulogised Śivā the mother of the universe, the beloved of Śiva, of great illusory power, of divine features and resident of Śiva's region. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
।

देवा ऊचुः
जगदम्ब महादेवि सर्वसिद्धिविधायिनि ।
देवकार्यकरी त्वं हि सदातस्त्वां नमामहे ॥ ३९॥

English
### *The Gods said:—*  O great goddess, O mother of the universe, O achiever of all accomplishments, you alone can carry out the work of the gods. Hence we bow to you always. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा कुरु कल्याणं देवानां भक्तवत्सले ।
मेनामनोरथः पूर्णः कृतः कुरु हरस्य च ॥ ४०॥

English
O you favourably disposed to the devotees, do everything conducive to the happiness of the Gods. You have fulfilled the desire of Menā. Now, you fulfil that of Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्थं स्तुत्वा शिवां देवा विष्ण्वाद्याः सुप्रणम्य ताम् ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुः प्रीताः शंसन्तस्तद्गतिं पराम् ॥ ४१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After eulogising Śivā thus, Viṣṇu and other gods bowed to her again delightedly and returned to their abodes praising her great divine ways. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तां तु दृष्ट्वा तथा जातां नीलोत्पलदलप्रभाम ।
श्यामां सा मेनका देवीं मुदमायाति नारद ॥ ४२॥

English
O Nārada, Menā rejoiced much on seeing goddess Umā of the splendour of the blue lotus as her daughter. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यरूपं विलोक्यानु ज्ञानमाप गिरिप्रिया ।
विज्ञाय परमेशानीं तुष्टावातिप्रहर्षिता ॥ ४३॥

English
On seeing her divine features, the beloved of the mountain attained perfect knowledge. After that realisation she eulogised the supreme Goddess with very great delight. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच
जगदम्ब महेशानि कृतातिकरुणा त्वया ।
आविर्भूता मम पुरो विलसन्ती यदम्बिके ॥ ४४॥

English
*Menā said :—*  “Great favour has been shown by you, O Goddess, O mother of the universe, inasmuch as you have manifested yourself in front of me brilliantly. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमाद्या सर्वशक्तीनां त्रिलोकजननी शिवे ।
शिवप्रिया सदा देवि सर्वदेवस्तुता परा ॥ ४५॥

English
You are the primordial one among all Energies. O Śivā, you are the mother of the three worlds. O Goddess you are the beloved of Śiva, you are great goddess eulogised by the gods. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपां कुरु महेशानि मम ध्यानस्थिता भव ।
एतद्रूपेण प्रत्यक्षं रूपं धेहि सुतासमम् ॥ ४६॥

English
O great Goddess, be pleased. Remain in my meditation in this form, but have the form of my daughter in public view” (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्या मेनाया भूधरस्त्रियाः ।
प्रत्युवाच शिवा देवी सुप्रसन्ना गिरिप्रियाम् ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Menā, the wife of the mountain, the delighted goddess Śivā replied to Menā, the beloved of the mountain thus. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच
हे मेने त्वं पुरा मां च सुसेवितवती रता ।
त्वद्भक्त्या सुप्रसन्नाहं वरं दातुं गतान्तिकम् ॥ ४८॥

English
*The Goddess said :—*  “O Menā, formerly you served me excellently. I am delighted by your devotion. I have come near you to grant you a boon. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं ब्रूहीति मद्वाणीं श्रुत्वा ते तद्वरो वृतः ।
सुता भव महादेवि सा मे देवहितं कुरु ॥ ४९॥

English
On hearing my words “Express your wish and choose a boon” you had chosen the boon “great Goddess, become my daughter. Do what is beneficent to the gods.” (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा दत्त्वा वरं तेऽहं गता स्वं पदमादरात् ।
समयं प्राप्य तनयाभवं ते गिरिकामिनि ॥ ५०॥

English
Accordingly granting you the boon I returned to my abode. O beloved of the mountain, I have become your daughter at the proper time. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यरूपं धृतं मेऽद्य यत्ते मत्स्मरणं भवेत् ।
अन्यथा मर्त्यभावेन तवाज्ञानं भवेन्मयि ॥ ५१॥

English
I have assumed the divine form now, so that you may be reminded of me. In a human form if I had appeared, it would have put you out of knowledge about me. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
युवां मां पुत्रिभावेन दिव्यभावेन वासकृत् ।
चिन्तयन्तौ कृतस्नेहौ यातास्थो मद्गतिं पराम् ॥ ५२॥

English
Constantly thinking of me in the form of your daughter, or in the divine form with love, both of you will surely attain my region, the great goal. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
देवकार्यं करिष्यामि लीलां कृत्वाद्भुतां क्षितौ ।
शम्भुपत्नी भविष्यामि तारयिष्यामि सज्जनान् ॥ ५३॥

English
I shall do the work of the gods showing my wonderfully divine sports. I shall become the wife of Śiva and redeem good men.” (53.)

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वासीच्छिवा तूष्णीमम्बिका स्वात्ममायया ।
पश्यन्त्यां मातरि प्रीत्या सद्योऽभूत्तनयातनुः ॥ ५४॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  After saying thus Śivā kept quiet. Even as the mother was watching with pleasure, she assumed the body of a daughter by her power of illusion. (54.)
