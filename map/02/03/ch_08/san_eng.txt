### **CHAPTER EIGHT - *(Nārada-Himālaya Conversation)***

---

Sanskrit
८

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एकदा त्वं शिवज्ञानी शिवलीलाविदां वरः ।
हिमाचलगृहं प्रीत्यागमस्त्वं शिवप्रेरितः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :*  Once, induced by Śiva, you went to the abode of Himācala lovingly, you who have the knowledge of Śiva and who are the foremost among those who know the divine sports of Śiva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा मुने गिरीशस्त्वां नत्वानर्च स नारद ।
आहूय च स्वतनयां त्वदङ्घ्र्योस्तामपातयत् ॥ २॥

English
O sage Nārada, on seeing you, the lord of the mountains bowed to you and worshipped you. He called his daughter and asked her to fall at your feet. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्नत्वा मुनीश त्वामुवाच हिमभूधरः ।
साञ्जलिः स्वविधिं मत्वा बहुसन्नतमस्तकः ॥ ३॥

English
O excellent sage, he bowed to you again. Himavat joined his palms in reverence and bent his head considering it his duty and spoke to you. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
हे मुने नारद ज्ञानिन्ब्रह्मपुत्रवर प्रभो ।
सर्वज्ञस्त्वं सकरुणः परोपकरणे रतः ॥ ४॥

English
*Himavat said :—*  O sage Nārada, of good knowledge, O lord, foremost among the sons of Brahmā, you are omniscient. You are sympathetic. You are engaged in rendering help to others. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्सुताजातकं ब्रूहि गुणदोषसमुद्भवम् ।
कस्य प्रिया भाग्यवती भविष्यति सुता मम ॥ ५॥

English
Please read the horoscope of my daughter and tell me about her good and bad fortune. Whose beloved wife will my fortunate daughter be ? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्तो मुनिवर्य त्वं गिरीशेन हिमाद्रिणा ।
विलोक्य कालिकाहस्तं सर्वाङ्गं च विशेषतः ॥ ६॥

अवोचस्त्वं गिरिं तात कौतुकी वाग्विशारदः ।
ज्ञानी विदितवृत्तान्तो नारदः प्रीतमानसः ॥ ७॥

English
*Bramhā said :—*  O excellent sage, being thus requested by Himavat the lord of mountains, you looked at Kālī's palm and the limbs as well. O dear, you are wise. You know many facts. You are eloquent in speech. You then spoke. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
एषा ते तनया मेने सुधांशोरिव वर्धिता ।
आद्या कला शैलराज सर्वलक्षणशालिनी ॥ ८॥

English
*Nārada said :*  "O Menā, O king of mountains, this daughter of yours has all auspicious signs. Like the first digit of the moon she will increase day by day. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपतेः सुखदात्यन्तं पित्रोः कीर्तिविवर्धिनी ।
महासाध्वी च सर्वासु महानन्दकरी सदा ॥ ९॥

English
She will delight her husband, and heighten the glory of her parents. She will be a great chaste lady. She will grant bliss to everyone always. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सुलक्षणानि सर्वाणि त्वत्सुतायाः करे गिरे ।
एका विलक्षणा रेखा तत्फलं श‍ृणु तत्त्वतः ॥ १०॥

English
I see all good signs in the plam of your daughter, O lord of mountains. There is an abnormal line also. Listen to the indication thereof. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
योगी नग्नोऽगुणोऽकामी मातृतातविवर्जितः ।
अमानोऽशिववेषश्च पतिरस्याः किलेदृशः ॥ ११॥

English
Her husband will be a naked Yogin, without any qualities. He will be free from lust. He will have neither mother nor father. He will be indifferent to honours. His dress and manners will be inauspicious. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्ते हि सत्यं मत्वा च दम्पती ।
मेना हिमाचलश्चापि दुःखितौ तौ बभूवतुः ॥ १२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing your words the couple thought them true. Both Menā and Himavat were much distressed. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाकर्ण्यवचस्ते हि तादृशं जगदम्बिका ।
लक्षणैस्तं शिवं मत्वा जहर्षाति मुने हृदि ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, on hearing your words, and inferring that indications referred to Śiva, Pārvati's joy knew no bounds. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
न मृषा नारदवचस्त्विति सञ्चिन्त्य सा शिवा ।
स्नेहं शिवपदद्वन्द्वे चकाराति हृदा तदा ॥ १४॥

English
Convinced that Nārada's words could not be false, Śivā turned her mind and love to Śiva's feet. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच दुखितः शैलस्त्वां तदा हृदि नारद ।
कमुपायं मुने कुर्यामतिदुःखमभूदिति ॥ १५॥

English
The lord of mountains who was very much grieved in mind spoke to you, "O Nārada, O sage, what is the way out ? What shall I do ? A great misery has befallen us". (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा त्वं मुने प्रात्थ महाकौतुककारकः ।
हिमाचलं शुभैर्वाक्यैर्हर्षयन्वाग्विशारदः ॥ १६॥

English
On hearing that, O sage, you who are eloquent in speech, delighted Himavat by your sweet words of auspicious import and spoke to console him. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
स्नेहाच्छृणु गिरे वाक्यं मम सत्यं मृषा न हि ।
कररेखा ब्रह्मलिपिर्न मृषा भवति ध्रुवम् ॥ १७॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  “O lord of mountains, listen to my words with affability. They are true. They cannot be false. The lines in the palm are the lines of Brahmā. They cannot be untrue. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तादृशोऽस्याः पतिः शैल भविष्यति न संशयः ।
तत्रोपायं श‍ृणु प्रीत्या यं कृत्वा लप्स्यसे सुखम् ॥ १८॥

English
O lord of mountains, there is no doubt that her husband will be such a person. You now hear what you have to do whereby you will be happy. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तादृशोऽस्ति वरः शम्भुः लीलारूपधरः प्रभुः ।
कुलक्षणानि सर्वाणि तत्र तुल्यानि सद्गुणैः ॥ १९॥

English
There is a bridegroom like that. He is lord Śiva who has sportively assumed a physical form. In Him all bad characteristics are equal to good characteristics. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभौ दोषो न दुःखाय दुःखदोऽत्यप्रभौ हि सः ।
रविपावकगङ्गानां तत्र ज्ञेया निदर्शना ॥ २०॥

English
In a majestic person a defect does not produce misery. It may well cause misery in a non-majestic person. Sun, fire and Gaṅgā may be cited as examples. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छिवाय कन्यां स्वां शिवां देहि विवेकतः ।
शिवः सर्वेश्वरः सेव्योऽविकारी प्रभुरव्ययः ॥ २१॥

English
Hence you give your daughter in marriage to Śiva. That will be a wise step. Lord Śiva who is the sole lord, unchanging and without any aberration is worthy of being resorted to. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शीघ्रप्रसादः स शिवः तां ग्रहीष्यत्यसंशयम् ।
तपःसाध्यो विशेषेण यदि कुर्याच्छिवा तपः ॥ २२॥

English
By performing penance, Śiva can be propitiated quickly and He will accept her undoubtedly. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा सुसमर्थो हि स शिवः सकलेश्वरः ।
कुलिशस्यापि विध्वंसी ब्रह्माधीनः सुखप्रदः ॥ २३॥

English
In every respect, Śiva, the lord of all, is the most suitable person. He cannot be slain even by thunderbolts. He can render Brahmā and others distressed.”$^{17}$ (23.)

[fn 17. Our translation is based on the following emended text :

“कुलिसं रय्यविध्वंसी ब्रह्मादीनामकप्रदः”]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा त्वं पुनस्तात कौतुकी ब्रह्मविन्मुने ।
शैलराजमवोचो हि हर्षयन्वचनैः शुभैः ॥ २४॥

English
O dear sage, after saying this you continued. You delighted him with auspicious words. You spoke to the king of the mountains. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
भाविनी दयिता शम्भोः सानुकूला सदा हरे ।
महासाध्वी सुव्रता च पित्रोः सुखविवर्धिनी ॥ २५॥

English
“O lord of mountains, she will be the wife of Śiva and will remain his favourite always. She will be a chaste lady of good rites. She will increase the pleasure of her parents. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भोश्चित्तं वशे चैषा करिष्यति तपस्विनी ।
स चाप्येनामृते योषां न ह्यन्यामुद्वहिष्यति ॥ २६॥

English
Performing a penance she will fascinate Śiva’s mind towards herself. He too will marry none else except her. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एतयोः सदृशं प्रेम न कस्याप्येव तादृशम् ।
भूतं वा भविता वापि नाधुना च प्रवर्तते ॥ २७॥

English
A love akin to this pair will not be found anywhere. Never in the past was it seen nor will it occur in future. Nor it is current now. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अनयोः सुरकार्याणि कर्तव्यानि मृतानि च ।
यानि यानि नगश्रेष्ठ जीवितानि पुनः पुनः ॥ २८॥

English
O best of mountains, the two will fulfil the work of the gods. They will resuscitate those who have breathed their last. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अनया कन्यया तेऽद्रे अर्धनारीश्वरो हरः ।
भविष्यति तथा हर्षदिनयोर्मिलितं पुनः ॥ २९॥

English
O lord of mountains, Śiva will become Ardhanā-rīśvara (half male and half female), with your daughter forming half the part of your body. Their meeting once again will be delightful. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
शरीरार्धं हरस्यैषा हरिष्यति सुता तव ।
तपः प्रभावात्सन्तोष्य महेशं सकलेश्वरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
After propitiating lord Śiva, the lord of all, by the power of her penance, your daughter will take away half the body of Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वर्णगौरी सुवर्णाभा तपसा तोष्य तं हरम् ।
विद्युद्गौरतमा चेयं तव पुत्री भविष्यति ॥ ३१॥

English
By propitating Śiva with her penance she will acquire the lustre of gold and will be known as Svarṇagauri. Your daughter will be as fair-complexioned as lightning. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
गौरीति नाम्ना कन्या तु ख्यातिमेषा गमिष्यति ।
सर्वदेवगणैः पूज्या हरिब्रह्मादिभिस्तथा ॥ ३२॥

English
This girl will be famous in the name of Gauri. She will deserve the respect of Viṣṇu, Brahmā and the other Gods. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नान्यस्मै त्वमिमां दातुमिहार्हसि नगोत्तम ।
इदं चोपांशु देवानां न प्रकाश्यं कदाचन ॥ ३३॥

English
O excellent mountain, you shall not give her to anyone else. This is a secret of the gods. This shall not be revealed to any one else. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा देवर्षे तव नारद ।
उवाच हिमवान्वाक्यं मुने त्वां वाग्विशारदः ॥ ३४॥

English
O celestial sage, Nārada, on hearing these words that you spoke O, sage, the eloquent Himavat spoke thus: (34.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच
हे मुने नारद प्राज्ञ विज्ञप्तिं काञ्चिदेव हि ।
करोमि तां श‍ृणु प्रीत्यातस्त्वं प्रमुदमावह ॥ ३५॥

English
*Himavat said:—*  O sage Nārada, O intelligent one, I have one submission to make. Please listen to it lovingly and make us delightful. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रूयते त्यक्तसङ्गः स महादेवो यतात्मवान् ।
तपश्चरति सन्नित्यं देवानामप्यगोचरः ॥ ३६॥

English
It is heard that the great God abhors all attachments. He has perfect self-control. He is ever busy in penance and is out of reach of even the Gods. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
स कथं ध्यानमार्गस्थः परब्रह्मार्पितं मनः ।
भ्रंशयिष्यति देवर्षे तत्र मे संशयो महान् ॥ ३७॥

English
O celestial sage, He is in the path of meditation. How can He withdraw His mind from the supreme Brahman? I have a great doubt in this respect. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षरं परमं ब्रह्म प्रदीपकलिकोपमम् ।
सदाशिवाख्यं स्वं रूपं निर्विकारमजात्परम् ॥ ३८॥

निर्गुणं सगुणं तच्च निर्विशेषं निरीहकम् ।
अतः पश्यति सर्वत्र न तु बाह्यं निरीक्षते ॥ ३९॥

English
The supreme Brahman is great and imperishable. It is like the streak of a lamp. It is termed Sadāśiva. It is without aberration. It is beyond Brahmā. It is both full and devoid of qualities. It has no special traits, no desires. It sees within$^{18}$ and not without. (38-39.)

[fn 18. We have adopted the reading अन्तः for अतः]
---

Sanskrit
इति स श्रूयते नित्यं किन्नराणां मुखान्मुने ।
इहागतानां सुप्रीत्या किं तन्मिथ्यावचो ध्रुवम् ॥ ४०॥

English
O sage, from the Kinnaras who come here, such are the things heard about Him. Can it be untrue? (40.)

---

Sanskrit
विशेषतः श्रूयते स साक्षान्नाम्ना तथा हरः ।
समयं कृतवान्पूर्वं तन्मया गदितं श‍ृणु ॥ ४१॥

English
This is also heard that Śiva had entered into a contract with the lady Satī. Listen to what I say? (41.)

---

Sanskrit
न त्वामृतेऽन्यां वरये दाक्षायणि सती प्रिये ।
भार्यार्थं न ग्रहीष्यामि सत्यमेतद्ब्रवीमि ते ॥ ४२॥

English
"O Satī, Dākṣāyaṇī, my beloved, I shall not take the hand of any woman except you as my wife. This is the truth that I say." (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सत्या समं तेन पुरैव समयः कृतः ।
तस्यां मृतायां स कथं स्वयमन्यां ग्रहीष्यति ॥ ४३॥

English
This was the agreement that He made with Satī formerly. She is dead. How then will He take another woman to be His wife. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा स गिरिस्तूष्णीमास तस्य पुरस्तव ।
तदाकर्ण्याथ देवर्षे त्वं प्रावोचः सुतत्त्वतः ॥ ४४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying these words, the lord of mountains kept quiet in your presence. O celestial sage, on hearing that, you spoke words that revealed the truth. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच
न वै कार्या त्वया चिन्ता गिरिराज महामते ।
एषा तव सुता काली दक्षजा ह्यभवत्पुरा ॥ ४५॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  "O lord of mountains, of great intellect, you need not worry. This daughter of yours, Pārvatī, was formerly the daughter of Dakṣa. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
सतीनामाभवत्तस्याः सर्वमङ्गलदं सदा ।
सती सा वै दक्षकन्या भूत्वा रुद्रप्रियाभवत् ॥ ४६॥

English
Satī was her auspicious name. Satī who was Dakṣa's daughter became Rudra's wife. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुर्यज्ञे तथा प्राप्यानादरं शङ्करस्य च ।
तं दृष्ट्वा कोपमाधायात्याक्षीद्देहं च सा सती ॥ ४७॥

English
Being dishonoured at the sacrifice of her father, and being the witness of Śiva's dishonour she was furious and she cast off her body. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनःसैव समुत्पन्ना तव गेहेऽम्बिका शिवा ।
पार्वती हरपत्नीयं भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ ४८॥

English
She herself is born in your house as Pārvatī. There is no doubt that she will become Śiva's wife." (48.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वं विस्तरात्त्वं प्रोक्तवान्भूभृते मुने ।
पूर्वरूपं चरित्रं च पार्वत्याः प्रीतिवर्धनम् ॥ ४९॥

तं सर्वं पूर्ववृत्तान्तं काल्या मुनिमुखाद्गिरिः ।
श्रुत्वा सपुत्रदारः स तदा निःसंशयोऽभवत् ॥ ५०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, all these details you mentioned to the lord of mountains. You told the previous history of Pārvatī that increased her pleasure and on hearing which, the lord of mountains, his wife and children were freed from all suspicions. (49-50.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः काली कथां श्रुत्वा नारदस्य मुखात्तदा ।
लज्जयाधोमुखी भूत्वा स्मितविस्तारितानना ॥ ५१॥

English
On hearing the story from Nārada, Pārvatī bent down her head in bashfulness but her smile heightened the beauty of her face. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
करेण तां तु संस्पृश्य श्रुत्वा तच्चरितं गिरिः ।
मूर्ध्नि शश्वत्तथाघ्राय स्वासनान्ते न्यवेशयत् ॥ ५२॥

English
On hearing the story, the lord of mountains stroked her fondly, kissed her on the head and placed her on his seat. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वं तां पुनर्दृष्ट्वाऽवोचस्तत्र स्थितां मुने ।
हर्षयन् गिरिराजं च मेनकां तनयैः सह ॥ ५३॥

English
O sage, on seeing her seated there you spoke again delighting the lord of mountains, Menakā and her sons. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहासनं तु किन्त्वस्याः शैलराज भवेदतः ।
शम्भोरूरौ सदैतस्या आसनं तु भविष्यति ॥ ५४॥

हरेरूर्वासनं प्राप्य तनया तव सन्ततम् ।
न यत्र कस्यचिद् दृष्टिर्मानसं वा गमिष्यति ॥ ५५॥

English
“O king of mountains, she will have a better throne than this. Śiva’s thigh will be her permanent abode. On getting the seat on the thigh of Śiva your daughter will go to the world where no eye or mind can reach.” (54-55.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति वचनमुदारं नारद त्वं गिरीशं
त्रिदिवमगम उक्त्वा तत्क्षणादेव प्रीत्या ।
गिरिपतिरपि चित्ते चारुसम्मोदयुक्तः
स्वगृहमगमदेवं सर्वसम्पत्समृद्धम् ॥ ५६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada, after saying this to the lord of mountains, you went to heaven immediately. The lord of mountains too, whose mind was filled with joy, returned to his palace endowed with all riches. (56.)
