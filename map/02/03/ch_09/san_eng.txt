### **CHAPTER NINE - *(The parents advise Pārvatī to propitiate Śiva. Śiva appears before Pārvatī in dream)***

---

Sanskrit
९

नारद उवाच ।
विधे तात त्वया शैववर प्राज्ञाद्भुता कथा ।
वर्णिता करुणां कृत्वा प्रीतिर्मे वर्धिताधिका ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, O dear one, O foremost among the devotees of Śiva, O intelligent one, taking pity on me you have narrated a wonderful story to me and have increased my pleasure thereby. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
विधे गते स्वकं धाम मयि वै दिव्यदर्शने ।
ततः किमभवत्तात कृपया तद्वदाधुना ॥ २॥

English
O Brahmā, when I, of divine vision, had gone to my abode what happened thereafter? Please tell me now. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
गते त्वयि मुने स्वर्गे कियत्काले गते सति ।
मेना प्राप्यैकदा शैलनिकटं प्रणनाम सा ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After you had gone to heaven, some time passed. Once Menā approached the lord of mountains and bowed to him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थित्वा सविनयं प्राह स्वनाथं गिरिकामिनी ।
तत्र शैलाधिनाथं सा प्राणप्रियसुता सती ॥ ४॥

English
After waiting there for some time with humility, the beloved of the mountain addressed her lord; the chaste lady who loved her daughter as ardently as her own life spoke to the lord of mountains thus. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच
मुनिवाक्यं न बुद्धं मे सम्यङ्नारीस्वभावतः ।
विवाहं कुरु कन्यायाःसुन्दरेण वरेण ह ॥ ५॥

English
*Menā said :—*  As but is natural to women, the words of the sage have not been understood by me well. (I think it is better) that you perform the marriage of our daughter with a handsome bridegroom. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा हि भवेत्तत्रोद्वाहोऽपूर्वसुखावहः ।
वरश्च गिरिजायास्तु सुलक्षणकुलोद्भवः ॥ ६॥

English
Let the bridegroom of Pārvatī be born of a good family endowed with good characteristic signs. In every respect that marriage will yield an unprecedented happiness. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राणप्रिया सुता मे हि सुखिता स्याद्यथा प्रिया ।
सद्वरं प्राप्य सुप्रीता तथा कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ७॥

English
Obeisance to you. Do everything necessary to make our daughter, as beloved to us as our own lives, very happy and delighted after being united with a good bridegroom. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वाश्रुमुखी मेना पत्यङ्घ्र्योः पतिता तदा ।
तामुत्थाप्य गिरिः प्राह यथावत्प्राज्ञसत्तमः ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this, with tears in her eyes Menā fell at the feet of her husband. Raising her, the lord of mountains, the most excellent among intelligent people, made a true statement. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
श‍ृणु त्वं मेनके देवि यथार्थं वच्मि तत्त्वतः ।
भ्रमं त्यज मुनेर्वाक्यं वितथं न कदाचन ॥ ९॥

English
*Himācala said :—*  O gentle lady Menakā, listen. I shall tell you the truth. Do not be under false impressions. The sage's statement will never be false. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि स्नेहः सुतायास्ते सुतां शिक्षय सादरम् ।
तपः कुर्याच्छङ्करस्य सा भक्त्या स्थिरचेतसा ॥ १०॥

English
If you feel affectionate towards your daughter, zealously instruct your daughter. Let her perform the penance with Śiva as the object, devotedly and steadily. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
चेत्प्रसन्नः शिवः काल्याः पाणिं गृह्णाति मेनके ।
सर्वं भूयाच्छुभं नश्येन्नारदोक्तममङ्गलम् ॥ ११॥

English
If Śiva is delighted, O Menakā, He will marry her. Everything shall be auspicious. The inauspicious features indicated by Nārada will perish. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अमङ्गलानि सर्वाणि मङ्गलानि सदा शिवे ।
तस्मात्सुतां शिवप्राप्त्यै तपसे शिक्षय द्रुतम् ॥ १२॥

English
All inauspicious things are auspicious in Sadāśiva. Hence immediately teach your daughter to hasten to perform the penance for attaining Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य गिरेर्वाक्यं मेना प्रीततराभवत् ।
सुतोपकण्ठमगमदुपदेष्टुं तपोरुचिम् ॥ १३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the lord of mountains, Menā was greatly delighted. She approached her daughter to advise her to take interest in penance. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सुताङ्गं सुकुमारं हि दृष्ट्वातीवाथ मेनका ।
विव्यथे नेत्रयुग्मे चाश्रुपूर्णेऽभवतां द्रुतम् ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing the tender limbs of her daughter, Menakā was greatly distressed. Her eyes welled up in tears immediately. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतां समुपदेष्टुं तन्न शशाक गिरिप्रिया ।
बुबुधे पार्वती तद्वै जननीङ्गितमाशु सा ॥ १५॥

English
The beloved of the lord of mountains was unable to advise her daughter to perform penance. Pārvatī understood the implied wish of her mother quickly. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा कालिका देवी सर्वज्ञा परमेश्वरी ।
उवाच जननीं सद्यः समाश्वास्य पुनः पुनः ॥ १६॥

English
Then the omniscient supreme goddess Pārvatī immediately spoke to her mother after consoling her again and again. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
मातः श‍ृणु महाप्राज्ञेऽद्यतनेऽजमुहूर्तके ।
रात्रौ दृष्टो मया स्वप्नस्तं वदामि कृपां कुरु ॥ १७॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  O mother, of great intelligence, listen in the early dawn to-day. At night I had a dream I shall tell you. Be pleased. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्रश्चैव तपस्वी मां सदयः प्रीतिपूर्वकम् ।
उपादिदेश सुतपः कर्तुं मातः शिवस्य वै ॥ १८॥

English
A brahmin sage advised me lovingly and compassionately to perform the penance of Śiva, O mother. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा मेनका शीघ्रं पतिमाहूय तत्र च ।
तत्स्वप्नं कथयामास सुतादृष्टमशेषतः ॥ १९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing that, Menakā called her husband there and told him the dream as seen by her daughter. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतास्वप्नमथाकर्ण्य मेनकातो गिरीश्वरः ।
उवाच परमप्रीतः प्रियां सम्बोधयन्गिरा ॥ २०॥

English
After hearing the dream of his daughter from Menakā, the lord of the mountains was pleased and he spoke thus to his wife. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरीश्वर उवाच
हे प्रियेऽपररात्रान्ते स्वप्नो दृष्टो मयापि हि ।
तं श‍ृणु त्वं महाप्रीत्या वच्म्यहं तं समादरात् ॥ २१॥

English
*The lord of the mountains said :—*  O dear, at the end of the latter half of the night, I too had a dream. Please listen to it lovingly. I shall zealously explain it. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्तपस्वी परमो नारदोक्तवराङ्गधृक् ।
पुरोपकण्ठं सुप्रीत्या तपः कर्तुं समागतः ॥ २२॥

English
A great saint of exquisite limbs, as mentioned by Nārada, arrived near my city with very great pleasure in order to perform penance there. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीत्वा स्वसुतां तत्रागमं प्रीततरोऽप्यहम् ।
मया ज्ञातः स वै शम्भुर्नारदोक्तवरः प्रभुः ॥ २३॥

English
Delighted much I took my daughter there with me. He was recognised as Lord Śiva, the bridegroom as mentioned by Nārada. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सेवार्थं तस्य तनयामुपदिश्य तपस्विनः ।
तं वै प्रार्थितवांस्तस्यां न तदाङ्गीचकार सः ॥ २४॥

English
Advising our daughter to render service to that saint I requested him to approve of it but He didn't. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अभूद्विवादः सुमहान्साङ्ख्यवेदान्तसम्मतः ।
ततस्तदाज्ञया तत्र संस्थितासीत्सुता मम ॥ २५॥

English
A great discussion took place (between her and Śiva based on Sāṅkhya and Vedānta).$^{19}$ Thereafter at His bidding my daughter stayed there. (25.)

[fn 19. Compare a dialogue between Śiva and Pārvatī who represent the different phases and aspects of Puruṣa and Prakṛti respectively. Cf. Ch. 13 of this section.]
---

Sanskrit
निधाय हृदि तं कामं सिषेवे भक्तितश्च सा ।
इति दृष्टं मया स्वप्नं प्रोक्तवांस्ते वरानने ॥ २६॥

English
Concealing her love in the heart she served Him with devotion. This is the dream I had, O bright-faced lady and I have told you all. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो मेने कियत्कालं परीक्ष्यं तत्फलं प्रिये ।
योग्यमस्तीदमेवेह बुध्यस्व त्वं मम ध्रुवम् ॥ २७॥

English
Hence, dear Menā, for some time the result of this has to be watched. Certainly know this to be the proper step for me. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा गिरिराजश्च मेनका वै मुनीश्वर ।
सन्तस्थतुः परीक्षन्तौ तत्फलं शुद्धचेतसौ ॥ २८॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O excellent sage, the lord of the mountains having thus explained to Menakā, both of them remained watching its result, pure in mind. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं व्यतीतेऽल्पदिने परमेशः सतां गतिः ।
सतीविरहसुव्यग्रो भ्रमन्सर्वत्र सूतिकृत् ॥ २९॥

तत्राजगाम सुप्रीत्या कियद्गणयुतः प्रभुः ।
तपः कर्तुं सतीप्रेमविरहाकुलमानसः ॥ ३०॥

English
When a few days passed by, lord Śiva, the goal of saintly men, the cause of protection and enjoyment wandering here and there in his flutter and excitement due to the separation from Sati, came there with pleasure accompanied by a few of his Gaṇas, in order to perform penance. The lord was completely agitated due to Sati's love and separation from her. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
तपश्चकार स्वं तत्र पार्वती सेवने रता ।
सखीभ्यां सहिता नित्यं प्रसन्नार्थमभूत्तदा ॥ ३१॥

English
He performed his penance there. Pārvatī engaged herself in His service continuously accompanied by two of her maids. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्धोऽपि मार्गणैः शम्भुर्विकृतिं नाप स प्रभुः ।
प्रेषितेन सुरैः स्वात्ममोहनार्थं स्मरेण वै ॥ ३२॥

English
Although the lord Śiva was hit and wounded by the arrows of Kāma who was sent thither by the gods to enchant Him, He was not swayed at all. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्ध्वा स्मरं च तत्रैव स्ववह्निनयनेन सः ।
स्मृत्वा मम वचः क्रुद्धो मह्यमन्तर्दधे ततः ॥ ३३॥

English
Burning Kāma there by His fiery eye, on remembering my words, the lord became angry with me and vanished from the scene. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कालेन कियता विनाश्य गिरिजामदम् ।
प्रसादितः सुतपसा प्रसन्नोऽभून्महेश्वरः ॥ ३४॥

English
After sometime, Lord Śiva quelled the pride of Pārvatī but he was propitiated by her again performing great penance. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
लौकिकाचारमाश्रित्य रुद्रो विष्णुप्रसादितः ।
कालीं विवाहयामास ततोऽभूद्बहुमङ्गलम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Following the conventions of the world, the lord married Pārvatī after being sponsored by Viṣṇu. Then everything auspicious ensued. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येतत्कथितं तात समासाच्चरितं विभोः ।
शङ्करस्य परं दिव्यं किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ३६॥

English
O dear, thus the story of the lord, the divine story of Śiva, has been narrated in brief. What is it that you wish to hear again ? (36.)
