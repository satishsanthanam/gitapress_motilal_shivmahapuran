### **CHAPTER TEN - *(Mars is born and is raised to the status of a Planet by Śiva's grace)***

---

Sanskrit
१०

नारद उवाच ।
विष्णुशिष्य महाभाग विधे शैववर प्रभो ।
शिवलीलामिमां व्यासात्प्रीत्या मे वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, the fortunate disciple of Viṣṇu, O lord, foremost among the devotees of Śiva, please narrate the divine sport of Śiva in detail to me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सतीविरहयुक्शम्भुः किं चक्रे चरितं तथा ।
तपः कर्तुं कदायातो हिमवत्प्रस्थमुत्तमम् ॥ २॥

English
What did Śiva, separated from Satī, do? When did He go to the excellent ridge of the Himavat to perform penance? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाशिवविवादोऽभूत्कथं कामक्षयश्च मे ।
तपः कृत्वा कथं प्राप शिवं शम्भुं च पार्वती ॥ ३॥

English
How did the discussion between Śivā and Śiva take place? How did Pārvatī attain Śiva by performing penance? (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सर्वमपरं चापि शिवसच्चरितं परम् ।
वक्तुमर्हसि मे ब्रह्मन्महानन्दकरं शुभम् ॥ ४॥

English
O Brahmā, these and other things, connected with the divine life of Śiva, pleasing and auspicious, you kindly narrate. (4.)

---

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing this inquiry of Nārada, Brahmā, the excellent lord of the worlds, remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and spoke. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गणानाभाष्य शोचंस्तां तद्गुणान्प्रेमवर्धनान् ।
वर्णयामास सुप्रीत्या दर्शयँल्लौकिकीं गतिम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O celestial sage, most excellent among the devotees of Śiva, listen to His glory that sanctifies, renders everything auspicious and increases devotion. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्य स्वगिरिं शम्भुः प्रियाविरहकातरः ।
सस्मार स्वप्रियां देवीं सतीं प्राणाधिकां हृदा ॥ ७॥

English
Returning to His mountain, Śiva in his excitement caused by his separation from his beloved, remembered Satī, who was dearer to Him than his very life. (7.)

---

English
Addressing His Gaṇas, He bewailed her and narrated her good qualities heightening love. In this way He showed the way of the world to the people. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
दिगम्बरो बभूवाथ त्यक्त्वा गार्हस्थ्यसद्गतिम् ।
पुनर्बभ्राम लोकान्वै सर्वान् लीलाविशारदः ॥ ९॥

English
Abandoning the polished manners of a householder, He cast off his dress and roamed about all the worlds, clever in divine sports that He was. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
न प्राप दर्शनं क्वापि सतीविरहदुःखितः ।
पुनश्च गिरिमायातः शङ्करो भक्तशङ्करः ॥ १०॥

समाधाय मनो यत्नात्समाधिं दुःखनाशनम् ।
चकार च ददर्शासौ स्वरूपं निजमव्ययम् ॥ ११॥

English
Not seeing her anywhere, the pangs of his separation from Satī increasing, Śiva, the benefactor of His devotees, returned to His mountain and entered into trance for the destruction of misery. Thereupon He saw His imperishable real form. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं चिरतरं स्थाणुस्तस्थौ ध्वस्तगुणत्रयः ।
निर्विकारी परं ब्रह्म मायाधीशः स्वयं प्रभुः ॥ १२॥

English
Thus Śiva remained for a long time eliminating the three attributes, and unaffected by aberrations. The lord Himself, the controller of illusion remained in the state of the Supreme Brahman. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः समाधिं तत्याज व्यतीयुर्ह्यमिताः समाः ।
यदा तदा बभूवाशु चरितं तद्वदामि वः ॥ १३॥

English
Then He gave up trance. Many years elapsed. What happened thereafter, I shall now recount to you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभोर्ललाटदेशात्तु यत्पृषच्छ्रमसम्भवम् ।
पपात धरणौ तत्र स बभूव शिशुर्द्रुतम् ॥ १४॥

English
The drops of sweat caused by exhaustion fell on the Earth from the lord's forehead and took the shape of a child immediately. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्भुजोऽरुणाकारो रमणीयाकृतिर्मुने ।
अलौकिकद्युतिः श्रीमाँस्तेजस्वी परदुस्सहः ॥ १५॥

English
O sage, the child was tawny-coloured and had four arms. He was comely in features. His brilliance was supermundane and unbearable to others. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रुरोद स शिशुस्तस्य पुरो हि परमेशितुः ।
प्राकृतात्मजवत्तत्र भवाचाररतस्य हि ॥ १६॥

English
Like a common child he cried in front of the Great lord who was engaged in worldly activities. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा विचार्य सुधिया धृत्वा सुस्त्रीतनुं क्षितिः ।
आविर्बभूव तत्रैव भयमानीय शङ्करात् ॥ १७॥

English
Afraid of Śiva, the Earth pondered deeply over it and appeared before him in the guise of a good lady. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तं बलं द्रुतमुत्थाय क्रोडायां निदधे वरम् ।
स्तन्यं सापाययत्प्रीत्या दुग्धं चोपरिसम्भवम् ॥ १८॥

English
She lifted up the child immediately and held him to her chest. Lovingly she suckled the child with her excellent breast milk that flowed over her body. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
चुचुम्ब तन्मुखं स्नेहात्स्मित्वा क्रीडयदात्मजम् ।
सत्यभावात्स्वयं माता परमेशहितावहा ॥ १९॥

English
She kissed the child's face lovingly and petted him smilingly. In the absence of Sati she herself acted as his mother in the interest of lord Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा चरितं शम्भुः कौतुकी सूतिकृत्कृती ।
अन्तर्यामी विहस्याथोवाच ज्ञात्वा रसां हरः ॥ २०॥

English
Śiva knew that she was the Earth. Śiva, the cause of protection and enjoyment, the immanent soul, on seeing her activities became contented and eagerly said to her laughingly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या त्वं धरणि प्रीत्या पालयैतं सुतं मम ।
त्वय्युद्भूतं श्रमजलान्महातेजस्विनो वरम् ॥ २१॥

English
"O Earth, you are blessed. Rear this child of mine lovingly, born of my glittering drops of sweat over you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
मम श्रमकभूर्बालो यद्यपि प्रियकृत्क्षिते ।
त्वन्नाम्ना स्याद्भवेत्ख्यातस्त्रितापरहितः सदा ॥ २२॥

English
Although the child is born of the sweat of my body, O Earth, he will be famous in the world after your name. He will be a bestower of pleasures and will be free from the three distresses always. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
असौ बालः कुदाता हि भविष्यति गुणी तव ।
ममापि सुखदाता हि गृहाणैनं यथारुचि ॥ २३॥

English
This boy of yours will be a bestower of lands and will have good qualities. He will make me too happy. Accept him with pleasure". (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा विररामाथ किञ्चिद्विरहमुक्तधीः ।
लोकाचारकरो रुद्रो निर्विकारी सतां प्रियः ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this He stopped. He was a bit relieved of His pangs of separation. Śiva, free from aberrations, and a lover of good men, acted thus only for following the worldly conventions. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अपि क्षितिर्जगामाशु शिवाज्ञामधिगम्य सा ।
स्वस्थानं ससुता प्राप सुखमात्यन्तिकं च वै ॥ २५॥

English
The Earth too, as Śiva bade her, returned to her abode along with the child. She was extremely happy. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
स बालो भौम इत्याख्यां प्राप्य भूत्वा युवा द्रुतम् ।
तस्यां काश्यां चिरं कालं सिषेवे शङ्करं प्रभुम् ॥ २६॥

English
The child acquired the name Bhauma (son of the Earth). He attained youth immediately. For a long time he worshipped lord Śiva at Kāśī. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेश्वरप्रसादेन ग्रहत्वं प्राप्य भूमिजः ।
दिव्यं लोकं जगामाशु शुक्रलोकात्परं वरम् ॥ २७॥

English
By the grace of lord Śiva, the son of the Earth, acquired the status of a planet. He went to the heavenly sphere beyond the region of Venus. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तं शम्भुचरितं सतीविरहसंयुतम् ।
तपस्याचरणं शम्भोः श‍ृणु चादरतो मुने ॥ २८॥

English
O sage, thus I have told you the story of Śiva and His separation from Satī. Now listen to the story of His performance of penance. (28.)
