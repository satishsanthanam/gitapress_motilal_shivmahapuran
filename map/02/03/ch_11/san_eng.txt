### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - (*Śiva and Himavat meet together*)**

---

Sanskrit
११

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
वर्धमाना गिरेः पुत्री सा शक्तिर्लोकपूजिता ।
अष्टवर्षा यदा जाता हिमालयगृहे सती ॥ १॥

तज्जन्म गिरिशो ज्ञात्वा सतीविरहकातरः ।
कृत्वा तमद्भुतामन्तर्मुमोदातीव नारद ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada, the daughter of the mountain, honoured in the three worlds, was brought up in the palace of Himācala. When she was eight years old, Śiva distressed by Satī's separation came to know of her birth. Keeping her wonderful memory within his heart He rejoiced much. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नेवान्तरे शम्भुर्लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ।
समाधातुं मनःसम्यक्तपः कर्तुं समैच्छत ॥ ३॥

English
In the meantime, following the conventions of the world, Śiva wished to perform penance in order to concentrate his mind properly. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कांश्चिद्गणवरान् शान्तान् नन्द्यादीनवगृह्य च ।
गङ्गावतारमगमद्धिमवत्प्रस्थमुत्तमम् ॥ ४॥

यत्र गङ्गा निपतिता पुरा ब्रह्मपुरात्स्रुता ।
सर्वाघौघविनाशाय पावनी परमा मुने ॥ ५॥

English
Taking some important Gaṇas of quiet nature, Nandin and others, with Him, He went to the excellent Himālayan ridge—Gaṅgāvatāra, O sage, where the great holy river Gaṅgā flowed from Brahmapura$^{20}$ formerly, in order to quell sins. (4-5.)

[fn 20. Cunningham (A. G. P. 299) identifies Brahmapura (the Po-loki-mo-pu-lo of Hwen Thsang: Waters, I. P. 324) with the capital city Vairāṭapaṭṭana of the hilly country lying between the Alakanandā and the Karnali rivers. The territory covered the districts of Garhwal and Kumaon (Cf. Br. S. ch. 14 and G.D. P. 40) and was stretched within 667 miles in circuit.]
---

Sanskrit
हरे ध्यानपरे तस्मिन्प्रमथा ध्यानतत्पराः ।
अभवन्केचिदपरे नन्दिभृङ्ग्यादयो गणाः ॥ ६॥

चेतो ज्ञानभवं नित्यं ज्योतीरूपं निरामयम् ।
जगन्मयं चिदानन्दं द्वैतहीनं निराश्रयम् ॥ ७॥

English
Staying there, Śiva of full self-control, started His activities of penance. With full concentration and alertness He thought on His own Self, the cause of mental knowledge, the eternal, the luminous, free from affliction, identical with the universe, consciousness and Bliss, without a second and having no support. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
सेवां चक्रुस्तदा केचिद्गणाः शम्भोः परात्मनः ।
नैवाकूजंस्तु मौना हि द्वारपाः केचनाभवन् ॥ ९॥

English
When Śiva began His meditation, the Pramathas also began their meditation as well as some Gaṇas, Nandin, Bhringi etc. Some of the Gaṇas rendered service to Śiva, the Supreme Self. Some of them became His gatekeepers. They observed silence and did not shout. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र जगाम हिमभूधरः ।
शङ्करस्यौषधिप्रस्थे श्रुत्वागमनमादरात् ॥ १०॥

English
In the meantime on hearing that Śiva had come to Auṣadhiprastha$^{21}$, the mountain Himavat too went there. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणनाम प्रभुं रुद्रं सगणो भूधरेश्वरः ।
समानर्च च सुप्रीतस्तुष्टाव स कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ११॥

English
Accompanied by his attendants, the lord of the mountains bowed to the lord Śiva, worshipped Him with pleasure and eulogised Him with palms joined in reverence. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव कपर्दिच्छङ्कर प्रभो ।
त्वयैव लोकनाथेन पालितं भुवनत्रयम् ॥ १२॥

English
*Lord Himavat said:—* O great god, lord of the gods, O lord Śiva, the three worlds are sustained by you alone who are lord of the worlds. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्ते देवदेवेश योगिरूपधराय च ।
निर्गुणाय नमस्तुभ्यं सगुणाय विहारिणे ॥ १३॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, O lord of gods, obeisance to the one who has assumed the form of a Yogin, obeisance to Thee that art possessed and devoid of attributes and obeisance to Thee who art sportive. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कैलासवासिने शम्भो सर्वलोकाटनाय च ।
नमस्ते परमेशाय लीलाकाराय शूलिने ॥ १४॥

English
O Śiva, obeisance to the resident of Kailāsa, obeisance to one who wanders all over the worlds, obeisance to thee the great lord, to the one indulging in divine sports, obeisance to the trident-holder. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
परिपूर्णगुणाधानविकाररहिताय ते ।
नमोऽनीहाय वीहाय धीराय परमात्मने ॥ १५॥

English
O lord, of complete and perfect qualities, obeisance to Thee, devoid of aberrations. Obeisance to Thee without aspirations. Obeisance to Thee without desires. Obeisance to the bold one, to the great soul. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अबहिर्भोगकाराय जनवत्सलते नमः ।
त्रिगुणाधीश मायेश ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने ॥ १६॥

English
O overlord of the three attributes, O lord of delusion, favourably disposed towards the people, obeisance unto you, who grant inner pleasures of the soul. Obeisance to Brahman, the great soul. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुब्रह्मादिसेव्याय विष्णुब्रह्मस्वरूपिणे ।
विष्णुब्रह्मैकदात्रे ते भक्तप्रिय नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १७॥

English
Obeisance to Thee, worthy of being served by Viṣṇu Brahmā and others; obeisance to Thee of the form of Viṣṇu and Brahmā; obeisance to Thee, the creator of Viṣṇu and Brahmā, obeisance to Thee O one favourably disposed to the devotees. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तपोरत तपःस्थान सुतपःफलदायिने ।
तपःप्रियाय शान्ताय नमस्ते ब्रह्मरूपिणे ॥ १८॥

English
O one engaged in penance, O one the venue of penance; obeisance to Thee the bestower of fruits of penance; obeisance to Thee who lovest penance; obeisance to Thee of the form of Brahman and quiescent. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यवहारकरायैव लोकाचारकराय ते ।
सगुणाय परेशाय नमोऽस्तु परमात्मने ॥ १९॥

English
Obeisance to Thee who lay down the principles of dealings and worldly conventions; obeisance to the great Śiva full of attributes; obeisance to Thee the great soul. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
लीला तव महेशानावेद्या साधुसुखप्रदा ।
भक्ताधीनस्वरूपोऽसि भक्तवश्यो हि कर्मकृत् ॥ २०॥

English
O great lord, your divine sports are incomprehensible. They bestow happiness on saintly men. Your nature is subservient to the devotees and you are under their control. You are the performer of all activities. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मम भाग्योदयात्तत्र त्वमागत इह प्रभो ।
सनाथ कृतवान्मां त्वं वर्णितो दीनवत्सलः ॥ २१॥

English
O lord, you have come here because my fortune is in its ascendancy. You have been described as a bestower of favours to the distressed. You have put me under your patronage and protection. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्य मे सफलं जन्म सफलं जीवनं मम ।
अद्य मे सफलं सर्वं यदत्र त्वं समागतः ॥ २२॥

English
Today my life has borne fruit, in fact everything connected with me has become fruitful since you have come here. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा मां दासमव्यग्रमाज्ञां देहि महेश्वर ।
त्वत्सेवां च महाप्रीत्या कुर्यामहमनन्यधीः ॥ २३॥

English
Knowing me to be your slave of great composure, O great lord, you can freely command me. With my mind not fascinated by other things I shall serve you with great pleasure. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य गिरीशस्य महेश्वरः ।
किञ्चिदुन्मील्य नेत्रे च ददर्श सगणं गिरिम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the lord of mountains, lord Śiva slightly opened his eyes and cast a glance on the lord of mountains who was accompanied by his attendants. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सगणं तं तथा दृष्ट्वा गिरिराजं वृषध्वजः ।
उवाच ध्यानयोगस्थः स्मयन्निव जगत्पतिः ॥ २५॥

English
On seeing the lord of mountains with his followers, the bull-bannered god Śiva, the lord of the universe permanently engaged in meditation and Yogic practice said smilingly. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
तव पृष्ठे तपस्तप्तुं रहस्यमहमागतः ।
यथा न कोऽपि निकटं समायातु तथा कुरु ॥ २६॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  I have come to perform penance in secret on your top. Make arrangements so that none should be able to come near me. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं महात्मा तपोधामा मुनीनां च सदाश्रयः ।
देवानां राक्षसानां च परेषां च महात्मनाम् ॥ २७॥

English
You are a noble soul, the abode of penance and permanent residence of sages, gods, demons and other great men. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा वासो द्विजादीनां गङ्गापूतश्च नित्यदा ।
परोपकारी सर्वेषां गिरीणामधिपः प्रभुः ॥ २८॥

English
You are the permanent residence of brahmins and others; you are always sanctified by Gaṅgā; you render help to others and you are the lord and king of all mountains. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तपश्चराम्यत्र गङ्गावतरणे स्थले ।
आश्रितस्तव सुप्रीतो गिरिराज यतात्मवान् ॥ २९॥

English
O king of mountains, delighted in resorting to you and controlling my senses and mind I am going to perform penance here at Gaṅgāvataraṇa.$^{22}$ (29.)

[fn 22. It is a sacred place celebrated in the Matsya and Vāyu Purāṇas where the river Gaṅgā emerges from the Vindu Sarovara through visible outlets and subterranean channels.]
---

Sanskrit
निर्विघ्नं मे तपश्चात्र हेतुना येन शैलप ।
सर्वथा हि गिरिश्रेष्ठ सुयत्नं कुरु साम्प्रतम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O lord of mountains, O best of mountains, now put forth all endeavour whereby my penance can be conducted without obstacles. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ममेदमेव परमं सेवनं पर्वतोत्तम ।
स्वगृहं गच्छ सत्प्रीत्या तत्सम्पादय यत्नतः ॥ ३१॥

English
O excellent Mountain, this alone is the greatest service that you can render. Please arrange for it with due effort. Please return to your abode with your mind full of pleasure. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा जगतां नाथस्तूष्णीमास स सूतिकृत् ।

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this, the lord of the worlds, the cause of protection and enjoyment kept silent. The lord of the mountains then spoke to Śiva with affection. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
पूजितोऽसि जगन्नाथ मया त्वं परमेश्वर ।
स्वागतेनाद्य विषये स्थितं त्वां प्रार्थयामि किम् ॥ ३३॥

English
*Himācala said :—*  O great lord of the universe, I have come myself and worshipped you. What shall I ask you who stay in my own kingdom? (33.)

---

Sanskrit
महता तपसा त्वं हि देवैर्यत्नपराश्रितैः ।
न प्राप्यसे महेशान स त्वं स्वयमुपस्थितः ॥ ३४॥

English
O great lord, you cannot be attained by great penance even by the gods who put forth great efforts. But you yourself have come here. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्तोऽप्यन्यतमो नास्ति न मत्तोऽन्योऽस्ति पुण्यवान् ।
भवानिति च मत्पृष्ठे तपसे समुपस्थितः ॥ ३५॥

English
There is none more fortunate than me; there is none more meritorious than me, since you have come to perform penance on my summit. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
देवेन्द्रादधिकं मन्ये स्वात्मानं परमेश्वर ।
सगणेन त्वयागत्य कृतोऽनुग्रहभागहम् ॥ ३६॥

English
O great lord, I consider myself greater than the god of gods. You have come here with your Gaṇas and made me blessed. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्विघ्नं कुरु देवेश स्वतन्त्रः परमं तपः ।
करिष्येऽहं तथा सेवां दासोऽहं ते सदा प्रभो ॥ ३७॥

English
O lord of gods, independently and without any obstacles perform your great penance. O lord, I am your slave always. I shall do all service to you. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

वृत्तान्तं तं समाचख्यौ प्रियायै च समादरात् ॥ ३८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this, the lord of the mountains returned at once to his abode and enthusiastically narrated everything to his beloved wife. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Nārada, calling together all his attendants with the members of their families, the lord of the mountains said emphatically. (39.)

Sanskrit
