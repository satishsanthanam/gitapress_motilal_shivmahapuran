### **CHAPTER TWELVE - (*Śiva-Himavat dialogue*)**

---

Sanskrit
१२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ शैलपतिर्हृष्टः सत्पुष्पफलसञ्चयम् ।
समादाय स्वतनयासहितोऽगाद्धरान्तिकम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said*  Then, the delighted lord of the mountains, took some fresh flowers and fruits with him and approached Śiva along with his daughter. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
स गत्वा त्रिजगन्नाथं प्रणम्य ध्यानतत्परम् ।
अर्पयामास तनयां कालीं तस्मै हृदाद्भुताम् ॥ २॥

English
Approaching the lord of the three worlds, engaged in meditation and bowing to Him he mentally dedicated to Him, his wonderful daughter. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
फलपुष्पादिकं सर्वं तत्तदग्रे निधाय सः ।
अग्रे कृत्वा सुतां शम्भुमिदमाह च शैलराट् ॥ ३॥

English
Placing the fruits and flowers in front of Him and making his daughter stand before Him, the lord of the mountains spoke to Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमगिरिरुवाच ।
भगवंस्तनया मे त्वां सेवितुं चन्द्रशेखरम् ।
समुत्सुका समानीता त्वदाराधनकाङ्क्षया ॥ ४॥

English
*Himācala said :—*  O lord, my daughter who is eager to serve you, the moon-crested lord, I have brought here with a desire to propitiate you. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सखीभ्यां सह नित्यं त्वां सेवतामेव शङ्करम् ।
अनुजानीहि तां नाथ मयि ते यद्यनुग्रहः ॥ ५॥

English
Let her serve you, the benefactor, for ever, along with two of her maids. O lord, if you wish to bless me, please permit her. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ तां शङ्करोऽपश्यत्प्रथमारूढयौवनाम् ।
फुल्लेन्दीवरपत्राभां पूर्णचन्द्रनिभाननाम् ॥ ६॥

समस्तलीलासंस्थानशुभवेषविजृम्भिताम् ।
कम्बुग्रीवां विशालाक्षीं चारुकर्णयुगोज्ज्वलाम् ॥ ७॥

मृणालायतपर्यन्तबाहुयुग्ममनोहराम् ।
राजीवकुड्मलप्रख्यौ घनपीनौदृढौस्तनौ ॥ ८॥

बिभ्रतीं क्षीणमध्यां च त्रिवलीमध्यराजिताम् ।
स्थलपद्मप्रतीकाशपादयुग्मविराजिताम् ॥ ९॥

ध्यानपञ्जरनिर्बद्धमुनिमानसमप्यलम् ।
दर्शनाद्भ्रंशने शक्तां योषिद्गणशिरोमणिम् ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Then Śiva looked at her in the first flush of her youth. Her complexion resembled the full blown blue lotus petals. Her face appeared as the full moon. Her auspicious dress and features were the repositories of all graceful charms. Her neck had the shape of the conchshell. Her eyes were wide and her ears shone exquisitely. On either side, her long-rounded arms resembling a lotus-stalk shone beautifully. Her two breasts resembling lotus-buds were stout, plump and firm. Her waist was slender and the curly locks of her hair shone well. Her feet resembled the land-lotus and were comely in appearance. She was competent to shake the minds of even the sages deeply engrossed in meditation, even at the very sight. She was a crest-jewel of all the maidens in the world. (6-10.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तां तादृशीं तात ध्यानिनां च मनोहराम् ।
विग्रहे तन्त्रमन्त्राणां वर्धिनीं कामरूपिणीम् ॥ ११॥

न्यमीलयदृशौ शीघ्रं दध्यौ स्वं रूपमुत्तमम् ।
परतत्त्वं महायोगी त्रिगुणात्परमव्ययम् ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing her in that exquisite form as increased the pleasure and love of even those who meditate, the great Yogin Śiva closed His eyes immediately and meditated upon His real form, the great principle that is beyond the three attributes and is imperishable. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
ववन्द शीर्ष्णा च पुनर्हिमाचलः
स संशयं प्रापददीनसत्त्वः ।
उवाच वाक्यं जगदेकबन्धुं
गिरीश्वरो वाक्यविदां वरिष्ठः ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing Śiva the lord of all, the chief of those devoted to penance, the lord with the moon as his ornament, who can be known through spiritual insight and who was sitting in the meditative posture closing His eyes, Himācala saluted Him again. Though he was not disheartened, he entertained some doubts. Thus he, the lord of mountains, foremost of the eloquent, spoke to Śiva, the sole kinsman of the universe. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचल उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणाकर शङ्कर ।
पश्य मां शरणं प्राप्तमुन्मील्य नयने विभो ॥ १५॥

English
*Himācala said :—*  O great lord of the gods, O Śiva, the merciful, O lord, open your eyes and look at me who have sought refuge in you. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव शर्व महेशान जगदानन्दकृत्प्रभो ।
त्वां नतोऽहं महादेव सर्वापद्विनिवर्तकम् ॥ १६॥

English
O Śiva, O great lord, the delighter of the universe, O great God, I bow to you who destroy all adversities. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
न त्वां जानन्ति देवेश वेदाः शास्त्राणि कृत्स्नशः ।
अतीतो महिमाध्वानं तव वाङ्मनसोः सदा ॥ १७॥

English
O lord of gods, the Vedas and the sacred lore do not know you entirely. Your greatness is beyond the sphere of words and minds, inexpressible by means of words and incomprehensible. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अतद्व्यावृत्तितस्त्वां वै चकितं चकितं सदा ।
अभिधत्ते श्रुतिः सर्वा परेषां का कथा मता ॥ १८॥

English
Not to speak of others, even the Vedas describe you with awe and timidity not positively but negating what you are not. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
जानन्ति बहवो भक्तास्त्वत्कृपां प्राप्य भक्तितः ।
शरणागत भक्तानां न कुत्रापि भ्रमादिकम् ॥ १९॥

English
Securing your grace through devotion, many devotees become acquainted with you. Seeking refuge in you they get correct knowledge about your real self. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञप्तिं श‍ृणु मत्प्रीत्या स्वदासस्य ममाधुना ।
तव देवाज्ञया तात दीनत्वाद्वर्णयामि हि ॥ २०॥

English
Please listen to my entreaty with a long heart. I am your slave. O dear lord, in humility I shall explain the same to you. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सभाग्योऽहं महादेव प्रसादात्तव शङ्कर ।
मत्वा स्वदासं मां नाथ कृपां कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २१॥

English
O great god Śiva, by your favour I feel most fortunate. O lord, consider me your slave and be sympathetic towards me. Obeisance to you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्यहं चागमिष्यामि दर्शनार्थं तव प्रभो ।
अनया सुतया स्वामिन्निदेशं दातुमर्हसि ॥ २२॥

English
O lord, I shall be visiting you daily along with my daughter. O lord, be pleased to command me accordingly. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्योन्मील्य नेत्रे महेश्वरः ।
त्यक्तध्यानः परामृश्य देवदेवोऽब्रवीद्वचः ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing his words, the great lord of the gods broke His meditation, opened His eyes, thought a little and spoke. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
आगन्तव्यं त्वया नित्यं दर्शनार्थं ममाचल ।
कुमारीं सदने स्थाप्य नान्यथा मम दर्शनम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  “O mountain, you shall come every day to see me, leaving your daughter in your abode. Otherwise I cannot be seen” (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
महेशवचनं श्रुत्वा शिवातातस्तथाविधम् ।
अचलः प्रत्युवाचेदं गिरिशं नतकन्धरः ॥ २५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of Śiva in that view, the father of Śivā the mountain, bowed his head and replied to Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचल उवाच ।
कस्मान्मयानया सार्धं नागन्तव्यं तदुच्यताम् ।
सेवने किमयोग्येयं नाहं वेद्म्यत्र कारणम् ॥ २६॥

English
*Himācala said:—*  “Let this be kindly mentioned why this girl cannot accompany me here. Is she unworthy of your service ? I do not know the reason thereof.” (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततोऽब्रवीद्गिरिं शम्भुः प्रहसन्वृषभध्वजः ।
लोकाचारं विशेषेण दर्शयन्हि कुयोगिनाम् ॥ २७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Particularly pointing out the worldly conduct of fake ascetics, the bull-bannered god Śiva laughingly spoke to the mountain. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरुवाच ।
इयं कुमारी सुश्रोणी तन्वी चन्द्रानना शुभा ।
नानेतव्या मत्समीपे वारयामि पुनः पुनः ॥ २८॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  This auspicious slender-bodied maiden of comely hips and moon-like face should not be brought near me. I forbid you again and again. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
मायारूपा स्मृता नारी विद्वद्भिर्वेदपारगैः ।
युवती तु विशेषेण विघ्नकर्त्री तपस्विनाम् ॥ २९॥

English
A woman is a phase of illusion. As the scholars who have mastered the Vedas say particularly, a young damsel is a hindrance to ascetics. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तपस्वी योगी च निर्लिप्तो मायया सदा ।
प्रयोजनं न युवत्या वै स्त्रिया किं मेऽस्ति भूधर ॥ ३०॥

English
O mountain, I am an ascetic, a yogin, never affected by illusion. Of what avail is a woman thrust on me? (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पुनर्न वक्तव्यं तपस्विवरसंश्रित ।
वेदधर्मप्रवीणस्त्वं यतो ज्ञानिवरो बुधः ॥ ३१॥

English
O friend resorted to by great ascetics, you shall not say so again, since you are an adept in Vedic religion, a scholar and one foremost among the wise. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
भवत्यचल तत्सङ्गाद्विषयोत्पत्तिराशु वै ।
विनश्यति च वैराग्यं ततो भ्रश्यति सत्तपः ॥ ३२॥

English
O mountain, by contact with a woman, worldliness springs up; non-attachment perishes and the virtuous penance is destroyed. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अतस्तपस्विना शैल न कार्या स्त्रीषु सङ्गतिः ॥

महाविषयमूलं सा ज्ञानवैराग्यनाशिनी ॥ ३३॥

English
Hence, O mountain, no ascetic shall have any truck with women. A woman is the root of all worldly attachments. She destroys all wisdom and detachment together. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याद्युक्त्वा बहुतरं महायोगी महेश्वरः ।
विरराम गिरीशं तं महायोगिवरः प्रभुः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Speaking these and many other similar things to the lord of mountains, lord Śiva the great Yogin, stopped. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य शम्भो-
र्निरामयं निःस्पृहं निष्ठुरं च ।
कालीतातश्चकितोऽभूत्सुरर्षे
तद्वत्किञ्चिद्व्याकुलश्चास तूष्णीम् ॥ ३५॥

English
On hearing these ruthless words of Śiva free from sickness and desire, the father of Pārvatī became nervous, O celestial sage, and a little agitated. But he kept quiet. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्विनोक्तं वचनं निशम्य
तथा गिरीशं चकितं विचार्य ।
अतः प्रणम्यैव शिवं भवानी
जगाद वाक्यं विशदन्तदानीम् ॥ ३६॥

English
On hearing the words of the ascetic and finding her father, the lord of the mountains, frightened, Pārvatī bowed to Śiva and spoke these words clearly. (36.)
