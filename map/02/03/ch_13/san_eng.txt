### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - (Śiva-Pārvatī dialogue)**

---

Sanskrit
१३

भवान्युवाच ।
किमुक्तं गिरिराजाय त्वया योगिंस्तपस्विना ।
तदुत्तरं श‍ृणु विभो मत्तो ज्ञानिविशारद ॥ १॥

English
Pārvatī said:—  O Yogin, O lord, wise and clever, please listen to the reply to what you, as an ascetic, said to the lord of mountains. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तपःशक्त्यान्वितः शम्भो करोषि विपुलं तपः ।
तव बुद्धिरियं जाता तपस्तप्तुं महात्मनः ॥ २॥

English
O Śiva, you perform this great penance because you possess the energy of penance. Your intellect is inclined to perform penance because you are a noble soul. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सा शक्तिः प्रकृतिर्ज्ञेया सर्वेषामपि कर्मणाम् ।
तया विरच्यते सर्वं पाल्यते च विनाश्यते ॥ ३॥

English
That energy is the Prakṛti, the cause of all activities. Everything is created, sustained and destroyed by it. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्त्वं का प्रकृतिःसूक्ष्मा भगवंस्तद्विमृश्यताम् ।
विना प्रकृत्या च कथं लिङ्गरूपी महेश्वरः ॥ ४॥

English
O lord, please ponder over who you are and who this subtle Prakṛti is. Without Prakṛti how can the great lord of the phallic form exist ? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्चनीयोऽसि वन्द्योऽसि ध्येयोऽसि प्राणिनां सदा ।
प्रकृत्या च विचार्येति हृदा सर्वं तदुच्यताम् ॥ ५॥

English
You are worthy of the worship, respect and meditation of all living beings for ever, thanks to Prakṛti. Thinking of this in your heart, please reply. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
पार्वत्यास्तद्वचः श्रुत्वा महोतिकरणे रतः ।
सुविहस्य प्रसन्नात्मा महेशो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ६॥

English
Brahmā said:—  On hearing these words of Pārvatī, the great lord engaged in the causation of great enjoyment and protection became delighted. He laughed and said. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
तपसा परमेणैव प्रकृतिं नाशयाम्यहम् ।
प्रकृत्या रहितः शम्भुरहं तिष्ठामि तत्त्वतः ॥ ७॥

English
The great lord said:—  I am destroying the Prakṛti with my great penance. I remain in reality without Prakṛti. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्च प्रकृतेः सद्भिर्न कार्यः सङ्ग्रहः क्वचित् ।
स्थातव्यं निर्विकारैश्च लोकाचारविवर्जितैः ॥ ८॥

English
Indeed Prakṛti should not be taken in by good people. They should remain unaffected eschewing all worldly conduct. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्ता शम्भुना तात लौकिकव्यवहारतः ।
सुविहस्य हृदा काली जगाद मधुरं वचः ॥ ९॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O dear one, this was said by Śiva in accordance with worldly conventions and dealings. Pārvatī thereupon laughed to herself and spoke these sweet words. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
काल्युवाच ।
यदुक्तं भवता योगिन्वचनं शङ्कर प्रभो ।
सा च किं प्रकृतिर्न स्यादतीतस्तां भवान्कथम् ॥ १०॥

English
Pārvatī said .—  O Yogin, O lord Śiva, based on what you said how can that Prakṛti cease to exist and how can you be considered beyond that Prakṛti ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद्विचार्य वक्तव्यं तत्त्वतो हि यथातथम् ।
प्रकृत्या सर्वमेतच्च बद्धमस्ति निरन्तरम् ॥ ११॥

English
You shall ponder over this and say with reference to the facts as they are. All these (the universe etc) are bound by Prakṛti continuously. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वया न वक्तव्यं न कार्यं किञ्चिदेव हि ।
वचनं रचनं सर्वं प्राकृतं विद्धि चेतसा ॥ १२॥

English
Hence you shall not say anything, not do anything. Know that speaking, doing etc. is a Prakṛta activity. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यच्छृणोषि यदश्नासि यत्पश्यसि करोषि यत् ।
तत्सर्वं प्रकृतेः कार्यं मिथ्यावादो निरर्थकः ॥ १३॥

English
What you hear, what you eat, what you see and what you do—all these are (essentially) the activities of Prakṛti. To say that it is unreal is meaningless. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृतेः परमश्चेत्त्वं किमर्थं तप्यसे तपः ।
त्वया शम्भोऽधुना ह्यस्मिन्गिरौ हिमवति प्रभो ॥ १४॥

English
O lord, if you are greater than Prakṛti, wherefore do you perform penance, O Śiva, now, on this mountain Himavat. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्या गिलितोऽसि त्वं न जानासि निजं हर ।
निजं जानासि चेदीश किमर्थं तप्यसे तपः ॥ १५॥

English
O Śiva, you have been swallowed by Prakṛti, you do not know your own situation. O lord, if you do not know your own situation why do you perform penance ? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वाग्वादेन च किं कार्यं मम योगिंस्त्वया सह ।
प्रत्यक्षे ह्यनुमानस्य न प्रमाणं विदुर्बुधाः ॥ १६॥

English
O yogin, what have I to do with an argument with you ? Scholars say that without perception inference has no authority at all. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रियाणां गोचरत्वं यावद्भवति देहिनाम् ।
तावत्सर्वं विमन्तव्यं प्राकृतं ज्ञानिभिर्धिया ॥ १७॥

English
As long as the embodied beings remain the objects of the sense-organs, everything is Prakṛta. Wise men consider it so. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन योगीश श‍ृणु मद्वचनं परम् ।
सा चाहं पुरुषोऽसि त्वं सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ १८॥

English
O lord of ascetics, a longwinded talk is of no avail. Listen to my emphatic statement. I am Prakṛti and you are Puruṣa. This is the truth. There is no doubt about it. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मदनुग्रहतस्त्वं हि सगुणो रूपवान्मतः ।
मां विना त्वं निरीहोऽसि न किञ्चित्कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ १९॥

English
With my blessings you become qualitative and embodied. Without me, you are attributeless and incompetent to perform any activity. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पराधीनःसदा त्वं हि नानाकर्मकरो वशी ।
निर्विकारी कथं त्वं हि न लिप्तश्च मया कथम् ॥ २०॥

English
Being always subservient to Prakṛti you perform all activities. Self-controlled, free from aberrations and untainted by me how can you perform them ? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृतेः परमोऽसि त्वं यदि सत्यं वचस्तव ।
तर्हि त्वया न भेतव्यं समीपे मम शङ्कर ॥ २१॥

English
If you are really superior to Prakṛti, if what you say is true, you need not be afraid to be near me, O Śiva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्याः साङ्ख्यशास्त्रोदितं शिवः ।
वेदान्तमतसंस्थो हि वाक्यमूचे शिवां प्रति ॥ २२॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Pārvatī based on the Sāṃkhya system, Śiva replied to her, upholding the Vedāntin's point of view... (22.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशिव उवाच ।
इत्येवं त्वं यदि ब्रूषे गिरिजे साङ्ख्यधारिणी ।
प्रत्यहं कुरु मे सेवामनिषिद्धां सुभाषिणि ॥ २३॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  O Pārvatī, O upholder of the Sāṃkhya system, if you say so, O sweet-voiced lady, you render me unforbidden service every day. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यहं ब्रह्म निर्लिप्तो मायया परमेश्वरः ।
वेदान्तवेद्यो मायेशस्त्वं करिष्यसि किं तदा ॥ २४॥

English
If I am the Brahman, the supreme lord, unsullied by illusion, comprehensible through spiritual knowledge and the master of illusion what will you do then? (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवमुक्त्वा गिरिजां वाक्यमूचे गिरिं प्रभुः ।
भक्तानुरञ्जनकरो भक्तानुग्रहकारकः ॥ २५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Having spoken to Pārvatī thus, the lord, the conciliator and the blesser of the devotees spoke to the mountain thus. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशिव उवाच ।
अत्रैव सोऽहं तपसा परेण
गिरे तव प्रस्थवरेऽतिरम्ये ।
चरामि भूमौ परमार्थभाव-
स्वरूपमानन्दमयं सुलोचयन् ॥ २६॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  O lord of mountains, here itself on your beautiful excellent ridge, I shall perform my penance showing to the world my real blissful form and nature. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्तप्तुमनुज्ञा मे दातव्या पर्वताधिप ।
अनुज्ञया विना किञ्चित्तपः कर्तुं न शक्यते ॥ २७॥

English
O lord of mountains, permission shall be given to me to perform penance. Without your permission it is not possible for me (or any one else) to perform any penance here. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य देवदेवस्य शूलिनः ।
प्रणम्य हिमवाञ्छम्भुमिदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Śiva, the lord of gods, Himavat bowed to Śiva and said. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमवानुवाच ।
त्वदीयं हि जगत्सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ।
किमप्यहं महादेव तुच्छो भूत्वा वदामि ते ॥ २९॥

English
*Himavat said:—*  The entire universe consisting of gods, Asuras and human beings, is yours. O great god, though insignificant, I blabber something to you. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्तो हिमवता शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ।
विहस्य गिरिराजं तं प्राह याहीति सादरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Thus addressed by Himavat, Śiva, the benefactor of the worlds, laughingly permitted him to go. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करेणाभ्यनुज्ञातःस्वगृहं हिमवान्ययौ ।
सार्धं गिरिजया वै स प्रत्यहं दर्शने स्थितः ॥ ३१॥

English
Permitted by Śiva, Himavat returned to his abode along with Pārvatī. He wanted to visit Him daily. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पित्रा विनापि सा काली सखीभ्यां सह नित्यशः ।
जगाम शङ्कराभ्याशं सेवायै भक्तितत्परा ॥ ३२॥

English
Even without her father but accompanied by her maids, Pārvatī approached Śiva everyday for serving Him with devotion. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
निषिषेध न तां कोऽपि गणो नन्दीश्वरादिकः ।
महेशशासनात्तात तच्छासनकरः शुचिः ॥ ३३॥

English
O dear, at the bidding of lord Śiva, none of the Gaṇas, Nandiśvara and others, purely carrying out the orders of Śiva, prevented her. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
साङ्ख्यवेदान्तमतयोः शिवयोः शिवदः सदा ।
संवादः सुखकृच्चोक्तोऽभिन्नयोः सुविचारतः ॥ ३४॥

English
The discourse of Śivā and Śiva who represented the principles of Sāṃkhya and Vedānta and who, if thoughtfully considered, are not different from each other, was very happy and pleasing for ever. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिराजस्य वचनात्तनयां तस्य शङ्करः ।
पार्श्वे समीपे जग्राह गौरवादपि गोपरः ॥ ३५॥

English
At the request of the lord of mountains, Śiva permitted Pārvatī to remain with Him being true to His words though with all gravity and seriousness. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाचेदं वचः कालीं सखीभ्यां सह गोपतिः ।
नित्यं मां सेवतां यातु निर्भीता ह्यत्र तिष्ठतु ॥ ३६॥

English
He, the lord of individual souls, said to Pārvatī in the company of her maids—“You can serve me everyday You can go (as you please). You can stay here fearlessly.” (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्त्वा तु तां देवीं सेवायै जगृहे हरः ।
निर्विकारो महायोगी नानालीलाकरः प्रभुः ॥ ३७॥

English
Saying this, He accepted the goddess in his service. Śiva is free from aberrations. He is a great Yogin, the lord who indulges in different kinds of divine sports. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमेव महद्धैर्यं धीराणां सुतपस्विनाम् ।
विघ्नवन्त्यपि सम्प्राप्य यद्विघ्नैर्न विहन्यते ॥ ३८॥

English
This is the supreme courage of great ascetics possessed of fortitude that though surrounded by obstacles they are not overpowered by them. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्वपुरमायातो गिरिराट् परिचारकैः ।
मुमोदातीव मनसि सप्रियः स मुनीश्वर ॥ ३९॥

English
Then, the lord of mountains, returned to his city and rejoiced in the company of his wife, the sages and attendants. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
हरश्च ध्यानयोगेन परमात्मानमादरात् ।
निर्विघ्नेन स्वमनसा त्वासीच्चिन्तयितुं स्थितः ॥ ४०॥

English
Śiva mentioned the Yoga of meditation on the great Ātman with His mind freed from obstacles. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
काली सखीभ्यां सहिता प्रत्यहं चन्द्रशेखरम् ।
सेवमाना महादेवं गमनागमने स्थिता ॥ ४१॥

English
Pārvatī, along with her maids, continued her daily service to the moon-crested lord, coming and going without any hindrance. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रक्षाल्य चरणौ शम्भोः पपौ तच्चरणोदकम् ।
वह्निशौचेन वस्त्रेण चक्रे तद्गात्रमार्जनम् ॥ ४२॥

English
She washed Śiva’s feet and drank that holy water. With a cloth heated in fire she wiped his body. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
षोडशेनोपचारेण सम्पूज्य विधिवद्धरम् ।
पुनःपुनः सुप्रणम्य ययौ नित्यं पितुर्गृहम् ॥ ४३॥

English
After worshipping Him with sixteen types of offerings duly, and bowing to Him repeatedly she used to return to her father’s abode. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं संसेवमानायाः शङ्करं ध्यानतत्परम् ।
व्यतीयाय महान्कालः शिवाया मुनिसत्तम ॥ ४४॥

English
O excellent sage, a long time elapsed as she continued her service to Him who was engrossed in meditation. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्सहिता काली सखीभ्यां शङ्कराश्रमे ।
वितेने सुन्दरं गानं सुतालं स्मरवर्धनम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Sometimes accompanied by her maids, she sang exquisite songs of good note that increased love in the hermitage of Śiva. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्कुशपुष्पाणि समिधं नयति स्वयम् ।
सखीभ्यां स्थानसंस्कारं कुर्वती न्यवसत्तदा ॥ ४६॥

English
Sometimes she brought Kuśa grass, flowers and sacrificial twigs. Sometimes, assisted by her maids, she scubbed and cleaned the place. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिन्नियता गेहे स्थिता चन्द्रभृतो भृशम् ।
वीक्षन्ती विस्मयामास सकामा चन्द्रशेखरम् ॥ ४७॥

English
Sometimes she stayed in the house of the moon-cre- scent lord, pure and holy. Sometimes she used to gaze at the lord lovingly and with surprise.

48-49 In the course of his penance sometimes the lord of the goblins thought about her as free from attachment. But as she was in her physical form He did not take her as His wife though she was near Him, though she was endowed with every feature of beauty, though she was capable of deluding even the sages. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेवः पुनर्दृष्ट्वा तथा तां संयतेन्द्रियाम् ।
स्वसेवने रतां नित्यं सदयः समचिन्तयत् ॥ ५०॥

English
On seeing her with perfect control over her sense-organs and engrossed in serving Him always, the lord mercifully thought. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
यदैवैषा तपश्चर्याव्रतं काली करिष्यति ।
तदा च तां ग्रहीष्यामि गर्वबीजविवर्जिताम् ॥ ५१॥

English
"I shall take her only when the last seed of ego goes away from her; when she herself performs a penance." (51.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति सञ्चिन्त्य भूतेशो द्रुतं ध्यानसमाश्रितः ।
महयोगीश्वरोऽभूद्वै महालीलाकरः प्रभुः ॥ ५२॥

English
Thinking thus, the lord of the Bhūtas reverted to meditation. The lord who could indulge in great sports became a great Yogin. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यानासक्तस्य तस्याथ शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
हृदि नासीन्मुने काचिदन्या चिन्ता व्यवस्थिता ॥ ५३॥

English
O sage, when Śiva, the great Ātman, sank into meditation, no other thought entered His mind. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
काली त्वनुदिनं शम्भुं सद्भक्त्या समसेवत ।
विचिन्तयन्ती सततं तस्य रूपं महात्मनः ॥ ५४॥

English
As for pārvatī, she served Him everyday with great devotion, always thinking on the form of that Great Soul. (54.)

Sanskrit
हरो ध्यानपरः कालीं नित्यं प्रैक्षत सुस्थिताम् ।
विस्मृत्य पूर्वचिन्तां तां पश्यन्नपि न पश्यति ॥ ५५॥

English
Śiva who was engrossed in meditation saw her every day in full composure. Forgetting His previous thoughts about her, He did not see her although He saw her. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवाः शक्राद्या मुनयश्च ते ।
ब्रह्माज्ञया स्मरं तत्र प्रेषयामासुरादरात् ॥ ५६॥

English
In the mean time Indra, other gods and the sages eagerly sent Kāma there at the bidding of Brahmā. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन कारयितुं योगं काल्या रुद्रेण कामतः ।
महावीर्येणासुरेण तारकेण प्रपीडिताः ॥ ५७॥

English
They had been harassed by the demon Tāraka, the demon of great strength. Hence they wanted to unite Parvatī and Śiva in love. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र स्मरः सर्वमुपायमकरोन्निजम् ।
चुक्षुभे न हरः किञ्चित्तं च भस्मीचकार ह ॥ ५८॥

English
After reaching there Kāma tried all his tricks but Śiva was not at all agitated. He reduced Kāma to ashes. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्यपि विगर्वाभून्मुने तस्य निदेशतः ।
ततस्तपो महत्कृत्वा शिवं प्राप पतिं सती ॥ ५९॥

English
O sage, Pārvatī too was divested of her ego. At his bidding she performed a penance and obtained Him as her husband. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवतुस्तौ सुप्रीतौ पार्वतीपरमेश्वरौ ।
चक्रतुर्देवकार्यं हि परोपकरणे रतौ ॥ ६०॥

English
Pārvatī and Śiva were very happy. Engrossed in helping others they carried out the work of the gods. (60.)
