### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - (The Birth of Tāraka and Vajrāṅga and their Penance)**

---

Sanskrit
१४

नारद उवाच ।
विष्णुशिष्य महाशैव सम्यगुक्तं त्वया विधे ।
चरितं परमं ह्येतच्छिवायाश्च शिवस्य च ॥ १॥

English
Nārada said:—  O Brahmā, great devotee of Śiva and disciple of Viṣṇu, this great story of Śivā and Śiva has been narrated very well by you. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्तारकासुरो ब्रह्मन्येन देवाः प्रपीडिताः ।
कस्य पुत्रस्य वै ब्रूहि तत्कथां च शिवाश्रयाम् ॥ २॥

English
Who was this Tāraka demon, O Brahmā, by whom the gods were harassed. Whose son was he? Narrate his story with reference to Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
भस्मीचकार स कथं शङ्करश्च स्मरं वशी ।
तदपि ब्रूहि सुप्रीत्याऽद्भुतं तच्चरितं विभोः ॥ ३॥

English
How did Śiva of full control reduce Kāma to ashes? Please narrate that too with pleasure. The story of the lord is indeed wonderful. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं शिवा तपोऽत्युग्रं चकार सुखहेतवे ।
कथं प्राप पतिं शम्भुमादिशक्तिर्जगत्परा ॥ ४॥

English
How did Śivā perform the severe penance for the sake of happiness? How did the primordial energy who is greater than the universe secure Śiva as her husband? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वमशेषेण विशेषेण महाबुध ।
ब्रूहि मे श्रद्दधानाय स्वपुत्राय शिवात्मने ॥ ५॥

English
O great scholar, narrate all these complete in every detail to me, your son, who has dedicated his soul to Śiva and who has developed full faith in Him. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
पुत्रवर्य महाप्राज्ञ सुरर्षे शंसितव्रत ।
वच्म्यहं शङ्करं स्मृत्वा सर्वं तच्चरितं श‍ृणु ॥ ६॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O celestial sage, of great intellect, O foremost of my sons, whose sacred rites are laudable, I explain the entire story after thinking on Śiva. Listen. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं तारकस्यैव भवं संश‍ृणु नारद ।
यद्वधार्थं महायत्नः कृतो देवैः शिवाश्रयैः ॥ ७॥

English
O Nārada, first of all, you hear the birth of Tāraka himself, to secure whose death great effort was made by the gods depending on Śiva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मम पुत्रो मरीचिर्यः कश्यपस्तस्य चात्मजः ।
त्रयोदशमितास्तस्य स्त्रियो दक्षसुताश्च याः ॥ ८॥

English
My son Marīci begot Kaśyapa who married thirteen daughters of Dakṣa. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
दितिर्ज्येष्ठा च तत्स्त्री हि सुषुवे सा सुतद्वयम् ।
हिरण्यकशिपुर्ज्येष्ठो हिरण्याक्षोऽनुजस्ततः ॥ ९॥

English
The eldest of them Diti bore two sons: Hiranyakaśipu the elder and Hiranyākṣa the younger. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तौ हतौ विष्णुना दैत्यौ नृसिंहक्रोडरूपतः ।
सुदुःखदौ ततो देवाः सुखमापुश्च निर्भयाः ॥ १०॥

English
When these two began to harass the gods, Viṣṇu assumed the forms of Man-lion and Boar and killed them. Then the gods became fearless and happy. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दितिश्च दुःखितासीत्सा कश्यपं शरणं गता ।
पुनःसंसेव्य तं भक्त्या गर्भमाधत्त सुव्रता ॥ ११॥

English
The distressed Diti sought refuge in Kaśyapa and serving him with devotion and observing the sacred rites she conceived. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्विज्ञाय महेन्द्रोऽपि लब्धच्छिद्रो महोद्यमी ।
तद्गर्भं व्यच्छिनत्तत्र प्रविश्य पविना मुहुः ॥ १२॥

English
On coming to know of it, Indra entered her womb forcibly and cut it off many a time with his thunderbolt. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्व्रतस्य प्रभावेण न तद्गर्भो ममार ह ।
स्वपन्त्या दैवयोगेन सप्त सप्ताभवन्सुताः ॥ १३॥

English
By the power of her sacred rites, the child in the womb did not die as she was sleeping at that time, by a stroke of good luck. They were cut into seven pieces and so she had seven sons. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा आसन्सुतास्ते च नामतो मरुतोऽखिलाः ।
स्वर्गं ययुस्तदेन्द्रेण देवराजात्मसात्कृताः ॥ १४॥

English
These sons became gods by the name of Maruts. They all went to heaven along with Indra and were taken as his own attendants by the king of gods. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्दितिः पतिं भेजेऽनुतप्ता निजकर्मतः ।
चकार सुप्रसन्नं तं मुनिं परमसेवया ॥ १५॥

English
Diti resorted again to her husband repenting for her action. She made the sage pleased by means of great service. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्यप उवाच
तपः कुरु शुचिर्भूत्वा ब्रह्मणश्चायुतं समाः ।
चेद्भविष्यति तत्पूर्वं भविता ते सुतस्तदा ॥ १६॥

English
*Kaśyapa said:—*  Be pure and perform penance for ten thousand years of Brahmā. When it is completed you will have a son. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा दित्या कृतं पूर्णं तत्तपः श्रद्धया मुने ।
ततः पत्युः प्राप्य गर्भं सुषुवे तादृशं सुतम् ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, the penance was completed by Diti who performed it with faith. Thereafter from him she conceived and delivered of a son. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्राङ्गनामा सोऽभूद्वै दितिपुत्रोऽमरोपमः ।
नामतुल्यतनुर्वीरः सुप्रताप्युद्भवाद्बली ॥ १८॥

English
That son of Diti named Vajrāṅga (of adamantine limbs) was on a par with the gods. Befitting his name, his body was strong and powerful even from his very birth. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
जननीशासनात्सद्यः स सुतो निर्जराधिपम् ।
बलाद्धृत्वा ददौ दण्डं विविधं निर्जरानपि ॥ १९॥

English
At the bidding of his mother, he immediately abducted Indra, the lord of gods, the other gods and punished them in various ways. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दितिः सुखमतीवाप दृष्ट्वा शक्रादिदुर्दशाम् ।
अमरा अपि शक्राद्या जग्मुर्दुःखं स्वकर्मतः ॥ २०॥

English
Seeing the distress of Indra and others, Diti became very happy. Indra and other gods became miserable due to their own actions. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाहं कश्यपेनाशु तत्रागत्य सुसामगीः ।
देवानत्याजयंस्तस्मात्सदा देवहिते रतः ॥ २१॥

English
Always engaged in the welfare of the gods, I went there accompanied by Kaśyapa. Employing gentle and peaceful words I got the gods released. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
देवान्मुक्त्वा स वज्राङ्गस्ततः प्रोवाच सादरम् ।
शिवभक्तोऽतिशुद्धात्मा निर्विकारः प्रसन्नधीः ॥ २२॥

English
Releasing the gods with respect, Vajrāṅga, a great devotee of Śiva, was delighted in his heart, and he of pure soul, without any aberration, spoke. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्राङ्ग उवाच
इन्द्रो दुष्टः प्रजाघाती मातुर्मे स्वार्थसाधकः ।
स फलं प्राप्तवानद्य स्वराज्यं हि करोतु सः ॥ २३॥

English
*Vajrāṅga said:—*  In order to achieve his interest, Indra killed the foetus of my mother. He has now tasted the fruit thereof. Well may he rule over his kingdom. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
मातुराज्ञावशाद्ब्रह्मन्कृतमेतन्मयाखिलम् ।
न मे भोगाभिलाषो वै कस्यचिद्भुवनस्य हि ॥ २४॥

English
O Brahmā, I did this only at the bidding of my mother. I have no desire for the enjoyments of any one of the worlds. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्त्वसारं विधे ब्रूहि मह्यं वेदविदांवर ।
येन स्यां सुसुखी नित्यं निर्विकारः प्रसन्नधीः ॥ २५॥

English
O Brahmā, foremost of those who know the Vedas, tell me the essence of real philosophy whereby I can ever remain happy, pleased in heart and free from aberrations. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वाहं मुनेऽवोचं सात्त्विको भाव उच्यते ।
तत्त्वसार इति प्रीत्या सृजाम्येकां वरां स्त्रियम् ॥ २६॥

English
On hearing that, O sage, I said—“Sāttvika feelings constitute the essence of real philosophy. I shall lovingly create an exquisite lady.” (26.)

---

Sanskrit
वराङ्गीं नाम तां दत्त्वा तस्मै दितिसुताय वै ।
अयां स्वधाम सुप्रीतः कश्यपस्तत्पितापि च ॥ २७॥

English
After offering her who was named Varāṅgī, to that son of Diti, I went to my abode in great delight. So also Kaśyapa, his father. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दैत्यः स वज्राङ्गः सात्त्विकं भावमाश्रितः ।
आसुरं भावमुत्सृज्य निर्वैरः सुखमाप्तवान् ॥ २८॥

English
Thereafter the demon eschewed his diabolical feelings and resorted to sublime thoughts. Since he was free from fiendish feelings he became happy. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
न बभूव वराङ्ग्या हि हृदि भावोऽथ सात्त्विकः ।
सकामा स्वपतिं भेजे श्रद्धया विविधं सती ॥ २९॥

English
But no sublime feeling entered in the heart of Varāṅgī. With chastity and faith she served her husband lovingly in diverse ways. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तत्सेवनादाशु सन्तुष्टोऽभून्महाप्रभुः ।
स वज्राङ्गः पतिस्तस्या उवाच वचनं तदा ॥ ३०॥

English
Her husband Vajrāṅga of great lordly status was glad very soon on account of her service. He then spoke thus:— (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्राङ्ग्युवाच ।
किमिच्छसि प्रिये ब्रूहि किं ते मनसि वर्तते ।
तच्छ्रुत्वानम्य तं प्राह सा पतिं स्वमनोरथम् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Vajrāṅga said:—*  O beloved, what do you wish? What is it that you cherish in your mind? On hearing that, she bowed to her husband and revealed her desire. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
वराङ्ग्युवाच ।
चेत् प्रसन्नोऽभवस्त्वं वै सुतं मे देहि सत्पते ।
महाबलं त्रिलोकस्य जेतारं हरिदुःखदम् ॥ ३२॥

English
*Varāṅgī said:—*  “O my good husband, if you are so pleased grant me a powerful son who will conquer three worlds and cause misery to Viṣṇu.” (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा प्रियावाक्यं विस्मितोऽभूत्स आकुलः ।
उवाच हृदि स ज्ञानी सात्त्विको वैरवर्जितः ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of his beloved, he was disagreeably surprised and vexed. He was free from inimical thoughts. With perfect wisdom and Sāttvika feelings in his heart he said:— (33.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रियेच्छति विरोधं वै सुरैर्मे न हि रोचते ।
किं कुर्यां हि क्व गच्छेयं कथं नश्येन्न मे पणः ॥ ३४॥

English
My beloved wishes enmity with the gods. It does not appeal to me. What shall I do? Where shall I go? How can my vow be preserved from destruction? (34.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रियामनोरथश्चैव पूर्णः स्यात्त्रिजगद्भवेत् ।
क्लेशयुङ्नितरां भूयो देवाश्च मुनयस्तथा ॥ ३५॥

English
If my wife’s wishes are fulfilled, the three worlds will be much distressed, so too the gods and the sages. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
न पूर्णः स्यात्प्रियाकामस्तदा मे नरको भवेत् ।
द्विधापि धर्महानिर्वै भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुवान् ॥ ३६॥

English
If my beloved’s desires are not fulfilled, I am sure to be cast into hell. In either case righteousness will be lost. This is what we have heard. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्राङ्ग इत्थं बभ्राम स मुने धर्मसङ्कटे ।
बलाबलं द्वयोस्तत्र विचिचिन्त च बुद्धितः ॥ ३७॥

English
O sage, thus Vajrāṅga whirled a lot in a dilemma. Intelligently he considered the corresponding strength and weakness of both the alternatives. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेच्छया स हि मुने वाक्यं मेने स्त्रियो बुधः ।
तथास्त्विति वचः प्राह प्रियां प्रति स दैत्यराट् ॥ ३८॥

English
O sage, as willed by Śiva, though intelligent the king of demons agreed to the proposal. He told his wife “So be it.” (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तदर्थमकरोत्तीव्रं तपोऽन्यद्दुष्करं स तु ।
मां समुद्दिश्य सुप्रीत्या बहुवर्षं जितेन्द्रियः ॥ ३९॥

English
For that purpose he performed another very difficult penance with great zeal with me as the object of worship, for number of years. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं दातुमगां तस्मै दृष्ट्वाहं तत्तपो महत् ।
वरं ब्रूहि ह्यवोचं तं सुप्रसन्नेन चेतसा ॥ ४०॥

English
On seeing the great penance I went to him for granting the boon. With a delighted mind I told him “speak out the boon you wish to have.” (40.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्राङ्गस्तु तदा प्रीतं मां दृष्ट्वा खे स्थितं विभुम् ।
सुप्रणम्य बहुस्तुत्वा वरं वव्रे प्रियाहितम् ॥ ४१॥

English
On seeing me in the firmament in the pleasant mood he worshipped and eulogised me as well as craved for the boon as desired by his wife. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्राङ्ग उवाच ।
सुतं देहि स्वमातुर्मे महाहितकरं प्रभो ।
महाबलं सुप्रतापं सुसमर्थं तपोनिधिम् ॥ ४२॥

English
*Vajrāṅga said :—*  O lord, give me a son who will be carrying out what is beneficent to his mother, who will be strong, valorous and efficient, who will be a storehouse of penance. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य च तद्वाक्यं तथास्त्वित्यब्रवं मुने ।
अयां स्वधाम तद्दत्त्वा विमनाः संस्मरच्छिवम् ॥ ४३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing his words, O sage, I said “So be it.” After granting the boon I returned to my abode thinking on Śiva, though a bit distressed. (43.)
