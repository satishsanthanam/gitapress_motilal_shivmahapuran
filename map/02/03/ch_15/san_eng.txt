### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - *(The penance and reign of Tārakāsura)***

---

Sanskrit
१५

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ सा गर्भमाधत्त वराङ्गी तत्पुरादरात् ।
स ववर्धाभ्यन्तरे हि बहुवर्षैः सुतेजसा ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Then that Varāṅgī devoted to him, conceived. The child within her body developed in many years with its brilliance. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सा समये पूर्णे वराङ्गी सुषुवे सुतम् ।
महाकायं महावीर्यं प्रज्वलन्तं दिशो दश ॥ २॥

English
That Varāṅgī, when the time was complete, delivered of a son of huge body and great strength dazzling the ten quarters. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव च महोत्पाता बभूवुर्दुःखहेतवः ।
जायमाने सुते तस्मिन्वराङ्ग्याः सुरदुःखदे ॥ ३॥

English
At the same time, several phenomena of evil portent forboding misery and distress happened, when the son of Varāṅgī was born making the gods miserable. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दिवि भुव्यन्तरिक्षे च सर्वलोकभयङ्कराः ।
अनर्थसूचकास्तात त्रिविधाः तान्ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ४॥

English
O dear, the phenomena of three varieties indicating great calamity and terrifying the worlds occurred in the sky, heaven and earth. I shall narrate them. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सोल्काश्चाशनयः पेतुर्महाशब्दा भयङ्कराः ।
उदयं चक्रुरुत्कृष्टाः केतवो दुःखदायकाः ॥ ५॥

English
With a terrifying noise, thunderbolts fell along with comets; shooting meteors rose up, making the world miserable. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
चचाल वसुधा साद्रिर्जज्वलुः सकला दिशः ।
चुक्षुभुः सरितः सर्वाः सागराश्च विशेषतः ॥ ६॥

English
The earth with all the mountains quaked; the quarters blazed; the rivers and oceans were particularly agitated. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
हूत्कारानीरयन् घोरान्खरस्पर्शो मरुद्ववौ ।
उन्मूलयन्महावृक्षान्वात्यानीको रजोध्वजः ॥ ७॥

English
The rough wind blew with a hissing noise. Gusts of wind with troops of tempests and dust for banner uprooted several trees. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सराह्वोःसूर्यविध्वोस्तु मुहुः परिधयोऽभवन् ।
महाभयस्य विप्रेन्द्र सूचकाःसुखहारकाः ॥ ८॥

English
O great brahmin, the misty haloes around the sun and the moon in the grip of Rāhu became the harbingers of great fear and unhappiness. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
महीध्रविवरेभ्यश्च निर्घाता भयसूचकाः ।
रथनिर्ह्रादतुल्याश्च जज्ञिरेऽवसरे ततः ॥ ९॥

English
At that time terrifying sounds that resembled those of the chariot issued forth from cracks and crevices in the mountains. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सृगालोलूकटङ्कारैर्वमन्त्यो मुखतोऽनलम् ।
अन्तर्ग्रामेषु विकटं प्रणेदुरशिवाः शिवाः ॥ १०॥

English
Within villages, inauspicious vixens howled hideously vomiting fires; as it were, through their mouths along with the hissing and twanging sounds of the hootings and howlings of owls and jackals. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यतस्ततो ग्रामसिंहा उन्नमय्य शिरोधराम् ।
सङ्गीतवद्रोदनवद्व्यमुञ्चन्विविधान्रवान् ॥ ११॥

English
Lifting up their necks, the dogs barked in diverse ways producing sounds of singing or lamenting here and there. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
खार्काररभसा मत्ताः खुरैर्घ्नन्तो रसां खराः ।
वरूथशः तदा तात पर्यधावन्नितस्ततः ॥ १२॥

English
O dear, groups of mad asses ran here and there braying loudly and digging the ground with their hoofs. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
खगा उदपतन्नीडाद्रासभत्रस्तमानसाः ।
क्रोशन्तो व्यग्रचित्ताश्च स्थितिमापुर्न कुत्रचित् ॥ १३॥

English
Terrified by the asses, birds flew up from their nests. In their excitement and flutter they honked and cronked. They did not find a peaceful perch anywhere. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शकृन्मूत्रमकार्षुश्च गोष्ठेऽरण्ये भयाकुलाः ।
बभ्रमुः स्थितिमापुर्नो पशवस्ताडिता इव ॥ १४॥

English
Beasts in sheds and forests roamed here and there in great fright as though beaten and driven about, passing urine and shitting dungs as they pleased. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
गावोऽत्रसन्नसृग्दोहा बाष्पनेत्रा भयाकुलाः ।
तोयदा अभवंस्तत्र भयदाः पूयवर्षिणः ॥ १५॥

English
Frightened cows sprayed blood through their udders; their eyes brimmed with tears, clouds showering putrid matter became terrifying. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यरुदन्प्रतिमास्तत्र देवानामुत्पतिष्णवः ।
विनानिलं द्रुमाः पेतुर्ग्रहयुद्धं बभूव खे ॥ १६॥

English
Idols and images of deities appeared to cry and fly up. Even when there was no gale, trees fell down. Planets in the sky clashed with one another. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यादिका बहूत्पाता जज्ञिरे मुनिसत्तम ।
अज्ञानिनो जनास्तत्र मेनिरे विश्वसम्प्लवम् ॥ १७॥

English
O excellent sage, these and similar portending phenomena occurred : Ignorant persons thought the submersion of the whole universe was imminent. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रजापतिर्नामाकरोत्तस्यासुरस्य वै ।
तारकेति विचार्यैव कश्यपो हि महौजसः ॥ १८॥

English
Then Kaśyapa Prajāpati thought well and named the powerful demon Tāraka. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
महावीरः स सहसा व्यज्यमानात्मपौरुषः ।
ववृधेऽत्यश्मसारेण कायेनाद्रिपतिर्यथा ॥ १९॥

English
That heroic demon, with his manliness and valour manifesting quickly grew and developed with his steely frame like the lord of mountains. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो स तारको दैत्यो महाबलपराक्रमः ।
तपः कर्तुं जनन्याश्चाज्ञां ययाचे महामनाः ॥ २०॥

English
Then the demon Tāraka, of great strength and exploit, endowed with a lofty mind, requested permission of his mother for performing penance. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्ताज्ञः स महामायी मायिनामपि मोहकः ।
सर्वदेवजयं कर्तुं तपोऽर्थं मन आदधे ॥ २१॥

English
The permission having been secured, that demon possessing great power of illusion and capable of deluding even experts in the magical art, thought of performing penance in order to conquer all the gods. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
मधोर्वनमुपागम्य गुर्वाज्ञाप्रतिपालकः ।
विधिमुद्दिश्य विधिवत्तपस्तेपे सुदारुणम् ॥ २२॥

English
Strictly adhering to the directions of his elders and preceptors he went to the forest of Madhu and performed a severe penance duly, having Brahmā as his objective. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ऊर्ध्वबाहुश्चैकपादो रविं पश्यन्स चक्षुषा ।
शतवर्षं तपश्चक्रे दृढचित्तो दृढव्रतः ॥ २३॥

English
For a hundred years he performed penance with his hands lifted up, standing on only one leg and gazing at the sun. With his mind steady and firm he observed all sacred rites. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अङ्गुष्ठेन भुवं स्पृष्ट्वा शतवर्षं च तादृशः ।
तेपे तपो दृढात्मा स तारकोऽसुरराट् प्रभुः ॥ २४॥

English
Then for a hundred years, the lord and king of Asuras, Tāraka performed the penance : stood steady touching the ground with the single big toe. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शतवर्षं जलं प्राश्नन् शतवर्षं च वायुभुक् ।
शतवर्षं जले तिष्ठञ्च्छतं च स्थण्डिलेऽतपत् ॥ २५॥

English
For hundred years he performed penance by drinking only water; another hundred years by sustaining himself on air alone, another hundred years standing in water and another hundred years standing on dry land. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शतवर्षं तथा चाग्नौ शतवर्षमधोमुखः ।
शतवर्षं तु हस्तस्य तलेन च भुवं स्थितः ॥ २६॥

English
A hundred years he performed the penance amidst fires, a hundred years in a topsy-turvy position and a hundred years supported on the ground by the palms of his hands. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शतवर्षं तु वृक्षस्य शाखामालम्ब्य वै मुने ।
पादाभ्यां शुचिधूमं हि पिबंश्चाधोमुखस्तथा ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, a hundred years he remained with his head down and feet up clinging fast to the branch of a tree and inhaling the pure smoke of the sacrificial fire. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कष्टतरं तेपे सुतपः स तु दैत्यराट् ।
काममुद्दिश्य विधिवच्छृण्वतामपि दुस्सहम् ॥ २८॥

English
Thus with ardour, the king of the demons performed the severe penance duly unbearable even to those who heard about it. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रैवं तपतस्तस्य महत्तेजो विनिस्सृतम् ।
शिरसः सर्वसंसर्पि महोपद्रवकृन्मुने ॥ २९॥

English
O sage, in the process of such a penance, a huge mass of light shot up from his head and spread all round. It caused great havoc. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तेनैव देवलोकास्ते दग्धप्राया बभूविरे ।
अभितो दुःखमापन्नाः सर्वे देवर्षयो मुने ॥ ३०॥

English
All the worlds of the gods were well nigh consumed by it alone. O sage, all the celestial sages were hard hit and distressed. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रश्च भयमापेदेऽधिकं देवेश्वरस्तदा ।
तपस्यत्यद्य कश्चिद्वै मत्पदं धर्षयिष्यति ॥ ३१॥

अकाण्डे चैव ब्रह्माण्डं संहरिष्यत्ययं प्रभुः ।
इति संशयमापन्ना निश्चयं नोपलेभिरे ॥ ३२॥

English
Indra, the lord of gods, was extremely terrified. He thought "Some one is performing a penance. Surely he will usurp my position. This master mind shall in a trice destroy the whole cosmos". All those who entertained similar doubts could not decide what to do. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सर्वे सुसम्मन्त्र्य मिथस्ते निर्जरर्षयः ।
मल्लोकमगमन्भीता दीना मां समुपस्थिताः ॥ ३३॥

English
Then all those gods and sages consulted one another and in their great fright they came to my world and approached me in a piteous plight. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मां प्रणम्य सुसंस्तूय सर्वे ते क्लिष्टचेतसः ।
कृतस्त्वञ्जलयो मह्यं वृत्तं सर्वं न्यवेदयन् ॥ ३४॥

English
Bowing to and eulogising me with palms joined in reverence, all of them explained everything to me distressed in mind that they were. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं सर्वं सुनिश्चित्य कारणं तस्य सद्धिया ।
वरं दातुं गतस्तत्र यत्र तप्यति सोऽसुरः ॥ ३५॥

English
Coming to a definite conclusion with adequate thought as to the reason for the same, I went where the demon was performing penance in order to grant him the boon. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अवोचं वचनं तं वै वरं ब्रूहीत्यहं मुने ।
तपस्तप्तं त्वया तीव्रं नादेयं विद्यते तव ॥ ३६॥

English
O sage, I told him thus—"Tell me what boon you want. A severe penance has been performed by you. There is nothing which cannot be granted to you". (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं मद्वचः श्रुत्वा तारकः स महासुरः ।
मां प्रणम्य सुसंस्तूय वरं वव्रेऽतिदारुणम् ॥ ३७॥

English
On hearing these words of mine, Tāraka, the great demon, bowed and eulogised me and requested for a terrible boon. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तारक उवाच ।
त्वयि प्रसन्ने वरदे किमसाध्यं भवेन्मम ।
अतो याचे वरं त्वत्तः श‍ृणु तन्मे पितामह ॥ ३८॥

English
*Tāraka said :—*  "O Pitāmaha, if you are glad and ready to grant me the boon what is it that cannot be achieved by me? Hence I request you for this boon. Please listen. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश यदि देयो वरो मम ।
देयं वरद्वयं मह्यं कृपां कृत्वा ममोपरि ॥ ३९॥

English
O lord of gods, if you are pleased and if a boon is to be given to me, be kind enough to grant me two boons. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वया च निर्मिते लोके सकलेऽस्मिन्महाप्रभो ।
मत्तुल्यो बलवान्नूनं न भवेत्कोऽपि वै पुमान् ॥ ४०॥

English
O great lord, there should certainly be no man equal to me in strength in this entire universe created by you. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
शिववीर्यसमुत्पन्नः पुत्रःसेनापतिर्यदा ।
भूत्वा शस्त्रं क्षिपेन्मह्यं तदा मे मरणं भवेत् ॥ ४१॥

English
If a son born of Śiva becomes the commander-in-chief of an army and discharges weapons against me, let my death occur then". (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तोऽथ तदा तेन दैत्येनाहं मुनीश्वर ।
वरं च तादृशं दत्त्वा स्वलोकमगमं द्रुतम् ॥ ४२॥

English
O excellent sage, thus requested by that demon, I granted him two boons and hastened back to my abode. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्योऽपि स वरं लब्ध्वा मनसेप्सितमुत्तमम् ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा शोणिताख्यपुरं गतः ॥ ४३॥

English
Securing the excellent boon in accordance with his cherished desire, the demon was very glad and went to the town Śoṇita$^{23}$. (43.)

[fn 23. It was the capital of the Asura territory, later on called Bāṇapura, as it was ruled by the powerful Asura Bāṇa, the devotee of Śiva. According to Dey (G. D. PP. 21, 189) Śoṇitapura is still called by that name, and is situated in Kamaun on the bank of the river Kedāra-Gaṅgā or Mandākinī about 6 miles from Uṣāmaṭha at a short distance from Guptakāśī".]
---

Sanskrit
अभिषिक्तस्तदा राज्ये त्रैलोक्यस्यासुरैः सह ।
शुक्रेण दैत्यगुरुणाज्ञया मे स महासुरः ॥ ४४॥

English
That great demon was crowned the king of the three worlds with the permission of Śukra, the preceptor of the demons. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तु स महादैत्योऽभवत् त्रैलोक्यनायकः ।
स्वाज्ञां प्रवर्तयामास पीडयन्सचराचरम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Then the great demon became the leader of the three worlds. He inaugurated his commanding position by harassing the mobile and immobile beings. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
राज्यं चकार विधिवत्त्रिलोकस्य स तारकः ।
प्रजाश्च पालयामास पीडयन्निर्जरादिकान् ॥ ४६॥

English
He duly established his suzerainty over the three worlds. He protected his subjects but inflicted pain on the gods and others. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स तारको दैत्यस्तेषां रत्नान्युपाददे ।
इन्द्रादिलोकपालानां स्वतो दत्तानि तद्भयात् ॥ ४७॥

English
Then the demon Tāraka seized gems and jewels of all the guardians of the quarters, Indra and others, offered under duress by them on being afraid of him. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रेणैरावतस्तस्य भयात्तस्मै समर्पितः ।
कुबेरेण तदा दत्ता निधयो नवसङ्ख्यकाः ॥ ४८॥

English
Afraid of him, Indra surrendered his Airāvata (white elephant) and Kubera all his nine treasures. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
वरुणेन हयाः शुभ्रा ऋषिभिः कामकृत्तथा ।
इन्द्रेणोच्चैः श्रवा दिव्यो भयात्तस्मै समर्पितः ॥ ४९॥

English
White horses were surrendered by Varuṇa, the wish-yielding cow Kāmadhenu by the sages, and the sun out of fear for him surrendered his divine horse Uccaiḥśravas. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र यत्र शुभं वस्तु दृष्टं तेनासुरेण हि ।
तत्तद्गृहीतं तरसा निस्सारस्त्रिभवोऽभवत् ॥ ५०॥

English
Wherever a fine article was espied by the demon, he seized it immediately. The three worlds became void of all valuable things. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्राश्च तथा रत्नान्यदुस्तस्मै भयान्मुने ।
अकृष्टपच्यासीत्पृथ्वी प्रजाः कामदुघाखिलाः ॥ ५१॥

English
O sage, the oceans tho offered him their gems on account of fear. The entire earth became exuberant in productivity without being tilled and yielded what his subjects desired. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यश्च तपते तद्वत्तद्दुःखं न यथा भवेत् ।
चन्द्रस्तु प्रभया दृश्यो वायुः सर्वानुकूल्यवान् ॥ ५२॥

English
The sun glowed gently and mildly as not to make him distressed. The moon was always visible with his brilliant light and the wind blew always favouring him. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां चैव यद्द्रव्यं पितॄणां च परस्य च ।
तत्सर्वं समुपादत्तमसुरेण दुरात्मना ॥ ५३॥

English
Whatever riches the gods possessed or the manes or others had, were forfeited by the wicked demon. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
वशीकृत्य स लोकांस्त्रीन्स्वयमिन्द्रो बभूव ह ।
अद्वितीयः प्रभुश्चासीद्राज्यं चक्रेऽद्भुतं वशी ॥ ५४॥

English
Bringing the three worlds under his control, he declared Indra himself. He became the undisputed lord and ruled over them with perfect self-control. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
निस्सार्य सकलान्देवान्दैत्यानस्थापयत्ततः ।
स्वयं नियोजयामास देवयोनिन् स्वकर्मणि ॥ ५५॥

English
Dismissing the gods he installed demons in their places. Some gods he engaged in his personal service. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तद्बाधिता देवाः सर्वे शक्रपुरोगमाः ।
मुने मां शरणं जग्मुरनाथा अतिविह्वलाः ॥ ५६॥

English
O sage, the gods harassed by him, led by Indra, sought refuge in me. They were helpless and extremely agitated. (56.)
