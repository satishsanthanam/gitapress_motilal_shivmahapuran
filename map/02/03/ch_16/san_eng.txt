### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - *(Brahmā consoles the gods harassed and frightened by the demon Tāraka)***

---

Sanskrit
१६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ ते निर्जराः सर्वे सुप्रणम्य प्रजेश्वरम् ।
तुष्टुवुः परया भक्त्या तारकेण प्रपीडिताः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  The gods terribly tormented by Tāraka, bowed to and eulogised me, the lord of subjects with great devotion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं श्रुत्वामरनुतिं यथार्थां हृदयङ्गमाम् ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा प्रत्यवोचं दिवौकसः ॥ २॥

English
On hearing the eulogy of the gods pleasing and true to facts I was highly pleased and replied to the heaven-dwellers thus. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वागतं स्वाधिकारा वै निर्विघ्नाः सन्ति वः सुराः ।
किमर्थमागता यूयमत्र सर्वे वदन्तु मे ॥ ३॥

English
O gods, welcome to you. I hope you are all fulfilling your duties without obstacles. Why have you all come here? Tell me. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा वचो मे ते नत्वा सर्वे दिवौकसः ।
मामूचुर्नतका दीनास्तारकेण प्रपीडिताः ॥ ४॥

English
On hearing my words those gods bowed to me duly and spoke. Being tormented by Tāraka they were in a piteous plight. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
लोकेश तारको दैत्यो वरेण तव दर्पितः ।
निरस्यास्मान्हठात्स्थानान्यग्रहीन्नो बलात् स्वयम् ॥ ५॥

English
*The gods said :—*  O lord of the worlds, thanks to the boon received from you. The demon Tāraka is very haughty. Driving us out with force he has taken possession of our positions. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
भवतः किमु न ज्ञातं दुःखं यन्नः उपस्थितम् ।
तद्दुःखं नाशय क्षिप्रं वयं ते शरणं गताः ॥ ६॥

English
Is it not known to you what misery has befallen us? Please dispel our misery quickly. We seek refuge in you. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अहर्निशं बाधतेऽस्मान् यत्र तत्रास्थितान्स वै ।
पलायमानाः पश्यामो यत्र तत्रापि तारकम् ॥ ७॥

English
He torments us wherever we happen to stay by day or at night. Wherever we flee we see Tāraka. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तारकान्नश्च यद्दुःखं सम्भूतं सकलेश्वर ।
तेन सर्वे वयं तात पीडिता विकला अति ॥ ८॥

English
O dear, lord of all, we are extremely harassed and agitated due to Tāraka. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निर्यमोऽथ वरुणो निरृतिर्वायुरेव च ।
अन्ये दिक्पतयश्चापि सर्वे यद्वशगामिनः ॥ ९॥

English
Agni, Yama, Varuṇa, Nirṛti, Vāyu and other guardians of the deities are under his control. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे मनुष्यधर्माणःसर्वैः परिकरैर्युताः ।
सेवन्ते तं महादैत्यं न स्वतन्त्राः कदाचन ॥ १०॥

English
None of them is ever independent. All serve him in the manner of human beings accompanied by their followers. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं तेनार्दिता देवा वशगास्तस्य सर्वदा ।
तदिच्छाकार्यनिरताः सर्वे तस्यानुजीविनः ॥ ११॥

English
Being harassed by him, the gods have become subservient of him. They are engaged in carrying out his wishes. All of us are his servants. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्यो वनिताः सर्वा ये चाप्यप्सरसां गणाः ।
सर्वांस्तानग्रहीद्दैत्यस्तारकोऽसौ महाबली ॥ १२॥

English
Our woman folk, the groups of heavenly nymphs have been captured by Tāraka, the powerful. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
न यज्ञाःसम्प्रवर्तन्ते न तपस्यन्ति तापसाः ।
दानधर्मादिकं किञ्चिन्न लोकेषु प्रवर्त्तते ॥ १३॥

English
No sacrifice is in the making. No ascetic is in penances. The charitable and virtuous activities are being seldom pursued in the worlds. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य सेनापतिः क्रौञ्चो महापाप्यस्ति दानवः ।
स पातालतलं गत्वा बाधते त्वनिशं प्रजाः ॥ १४॥

English
His commander-in-chief is a simple demon-Krauñca. He has now gone to the nether worlds and is harassing the people very much. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन नस्तारकेणेदं सकलं भुवनत्रयम् ।
हृतं हठाज्जगद्धातः पापेनाकरुणात्मना ॥ १५॥

English
The regions of our three worlds have been forcibly taken, O Brahmā, by this Tāraka of sinful and ruthless temperament. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वयं च तत्र यास्यामो यत्स्थानं त्वं विनिर्दिशेः ।
स्वस्थास्तद्वारितास्तेन लोकनाथ सुरारिणा ॥ १६॥

English
O lord of the worlds, we were in heaven but now that we have been turned out by that demon we shall go to any place which you may kindly suggest. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं नो गतिश्च शास्ता च धाता त्राता त्वमेव हि ।
वयं सर्वे तारकाख्यवह्नौ दग्धाः सुविह्वलाः ॥ १७॥

English
You are our final resort. You are our ruler, creator, and protector. But we are scorched in the fire of the name Tāraka. We are extremely agitated. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन क्रूरा उपाय नः सर्वे हतबलाः कृताः ।
विकारे सान्निपाते वा वीर्यवन्त्यौषधानि च ॥ १८॥

English
Our ruthless activities against him have turned out to be weak and ineffective, even as medicinal herbs of great potency are rendered ineffective in an ailment brought about by the combination of all deranged humours. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्रास्माकं जयाशा हि हरिचक्रे सुदर्शने ।
तत्कुण्ठितमभूत्तस्य कण्ठे पुष्पमिवार्पितम् ॥ १९॥

English
We had some hope of victory in Sudarśana the discus of Viṣṇu. But even that discus has become ineffective in his neck where it has fallen as though it were a floral offering to a deity. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्येतद्वचनं श्रुत्वा निर्जराणामहं मुने ।
प्रत्यवोचं सुरान्सर्वांस्तत्कालसदृशं वचः ॥ २०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage, on hearing these words of the gods, I told them befitting the occasion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
ममैव वचसा दैत्यस्तारकाख्यः समेधितः ।
न मत्तस्तस्य हननं युज्यते हि दिवौकसः ॥ २१॥

English
“O gods, the demon Tāraka has flourished, thanks to my words of blessings. His destruction through me does not seem proper. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो नैव वधो योग्यो यतो वृद्धिमुपागतः ।
विषवृक्षोऽपि संवर्ध्य स्वयं छेत्तुमसाम्प्रतम् ॥ २२॥

English
Improper is the destruction through that source wherefrom he has flourished. Even a poisonous tree tended and nurtured by one cannot be cut and felled down by oneself.²⁴ (22.)

[fn 24. For the similarity of idea and verbal expression, cp. Kālidāsa's Kumāra.]
---

Sanskrit
युष्माकं चाखिलं कार्यं कर्तुं योग्यो हि शङ्करः ।
किन्तु स्वयं न शक्तो हि प्रतिकर्तुं प्रचोदितः ॥ २३॥

English
Śiva is the most suitable agent to carry out your task. But I myself cannot do anything remedial in this case. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तारकाख्यस्तु पापेन स्वयमेष्यति सङ्क्षयम् ।
यथा यूयं संविदध्वमुपदेशकरस्त्वहम् ॥ २४॥

English
Tāraka will be destroyed by his own sin. How that shall be done you know from me. I shall advise you. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
न मया तारको वध्यो हरिणापि हरेण च ।
नान्येनापि सुरैर्वापि मद्वरात्सत्यमुच्यते ॥ २५॥

English
Thanks to the power of the boon granted by me. Tāraka cannot be killed by me or by Viṣṇu or by Śiva or by any one of the gods. It is true. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शिववीर्यसमुत्पन्नो यदि स्यात्तनयः सुराः ।
स एव तारकाख्यस्य हन्ता दैत्यस्य नापरः ॥ २६॥

English
O gods, if there is a son born of Śiva, he alone can kill the demon Tāraka. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यमुपायमहं वच्मि तं कुरुध्वं सुरोत्तमाः ।
महादेवप्रसादेन सिद्धिमेष्यति स ध्रुवम् ॥ २७॥

English
O best of gods, you carry out the remedy I am suggesting. By the grace of lord Śiva, it can be successfully accomplished. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सती दाक्षायणी पूर्वं त्यक्तदेहा तु याभवत् ।
सोत्पन्ना मेनकागर्भात्सा कथा विदिता हि वः ॥ २८॥

English
Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa, formerly cast off her body. She is now born of Menakā's womb. That event is already known to you all. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्या अवश्यं गिरिशः करिष्यति करग्रहम् ।
तत्कुरुध्वमुपायं च तथापि त्रिदिवौकसः ॥ २९॥

English
O gods, it is certain that lord Śiva will marry her. Still you shall pursue your endeavour. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा विधध्वं सुतरां तस्यां तु परियत्नतः ।
पार्वत्यां मेनकाजायां रेतःप्रतिनिपातने ॥ ३०॥

English
Make such arrangements as to ensure the discharge of semen into Pārvatī, the daughter of Menakā. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तमूर्ध्वरेतसं शम्भुं सैव प्रच्युतरेतसम् ।
कर्तुं समर्था नान्यास्ति तथा काप्यबला बलात् ॥ ३१॥

English
Śiva is a great Yogin who can make semen flow upwards in the body. Only Pārvatī can make him discharge the semen downwards, out of the body. There is no other woman capable of it. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सुता गिरिराजस्य साम्प्रतं प्रौढयौवना ।
तपस्यन्तं हिमगिरौ नित्यं संसेवते हरम् ॥ ३२॥

English
That daughter of the lord of the mountains is now in her prime of youth. She is serving Śiva in his penance on the Himālayas. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वाक्याद्धिमवतः काली स्वपितुर्हठतश्शिवा ।
सखीभ्यां सेवते सार्धं ध्यानस्थं परमेश्वरम् ॥ ३३॥

English
As a result of the tenacious pleadings of her father, she is serving Him in meditation. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तामग्रतोऽर्चमानां वै त्रैलोक्ये वरवर्णिनीम् ।
ध्यानासक्तो महेशो हि मनसापि न हीयते ॥ ३४॥

English
She is the most beautiful lady in the three worlds. She stands in front of Him and worships Him. Still lord Śiva who is engrossed in His meditation is not distracted by her presence. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
भार्यां समीहेत यथा स कालीं चन्द्रशेखरः ।
तथा विधध्वं त्रिदशा न चिरादेव यत्नतः ॥ ३५॥

English
It is your duty to ascertain means to make Him desire Pārvatī for His wife. O gods, do something in that direction very quickly. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थानं गत्वाथ दैत्यस्य तमहं तारकं ततः ।
निवारयिष्ये कुहठात्स्वस्थानं गच्छतामराः ॥ ३६॥

English
I shall go to the demon's abode and try to dissuade him from his obstinacy. O gods, you can go to your abode (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वाहं सुरान् शीघ्रं तारकाख्यासुरस्य वै ।
उपसङ्गम्य सुप्रीत्या समाभाष्येदमब्रवम् ॥ ३७॥

English
After saying thus to the gods I hastened to the demon Tāraka. I addressed him thus. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तेजोःसारमिमं स्वर्गं राज्यं त्वं परिपासि नः ।
यदर्थं सुतपस्तप्तं वाञ्छसि त्वं ततोऽधिकम् ॥ ३८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  You are ruling over our heaven which contains the essence of all brilliance. You are desirous of getting more than what you bargained for at the time of your penance. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
वरश्चाप्यवरो दत्तो न मया स्वर्गराज्यता ।
तस्मात्स्वर्गं परित्यज्य क्षितौ राज्यं समाचर ॥ ३९॥

English
I granted you a boon but not the kingdom of heaven. Hence leave off this region. You can rule over the earth. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
देवयोग्यानि तत्रैव कार्याणि निखिलान्यपि ।
भविष्यन्त्यसुरश्रेष्ठ नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ४०॥

English
O best of Asuras, even there you can achieve the fruit of your activities as here in Devaloka. There is nothing to hesitate in this matter. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वाहं च सम्बोध्यासुरं तं सकलेश्वरः ।
स्मृत्वा शिवां च सशिवं तत्रान्तर्धानमागतः ॥ ४१॥

English
After thus exhorting the demon to leave off heaven I, the lord of all, remembered Śiva and Śivā and vanished from the scene. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तारकोऽपि परित्यज्य स्वर्गं क्षितिमथाभ्यगात् ।
शोणिताख्यपुरे स्थित्वा सर्वराज्यं चकार सः ॥ ४२॥

English
Leaving the heaven, Tāraka descended to the earth. Stationed in the town Śoṇita, he ruled over the entire kingdom. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाःसर्वेऽपि तच्छुत्वा मद्वाक्यं सुप्रणम्य माम् ।
शक्रस्थानं ययुः प्रीत्या शक्रेण सुसमाहिताः ॥ ४३॥

English
On hearing my words, the gods bowed to me and went to Indra’s abode. They were duly received by Indra. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा मिलित्वा च विचार्य च परस्परम् ।
ते सर्वे मरुतः प्रीत्या मघवन्तं वचोऽब्रुवन् ॥ ४४॥

English
After reaching there and consulting one another, the gods in a body lovingly told Indra. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
शम्भोर्यथा शिवायां वै रुचिर्जायेत कामतः ।
मघवंस्ते प्रकर्तव्यं ब्रह्मोक्तं सर्वमेव तत् ॥ ४५॥

English
*The gods said :—* “O lord, you should carry out the suggestions of Brahmā and see that Śiva is lovingly inclined towards Śivā. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं सर्ववृत्तान्तं विनिवेद्य सुरेश्वरम् ।
जग्मुस्ते सर्वतो देवाः स्वं स्वं स्थानं मुदान्विताः ॥ ४६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After explaining every thing to the lord, the gods went in all directions to their respective abodes with great pleasure. (46.)
