### **CHAPTER SEVENTEEN - (The dialogue between Indra and Kāmadeva)**

---

Sanskrit
१७

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
गतेषु तेषु देवेषु शक्रः सस्मार वै स्मरम् ।
पीडितस्तारकेनाति दैत्येन च दुरात्मना ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said :—  When the gods had gone, Indra remembered Kāma. He was so afflicted by Tāraka, the wicked demon. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतस्तत्क्षणात्कामः सवसन्तो रतिप्रियः ।
सावलेपो युतो रत्या त्रैलोक्यविजयी प्रभुः ॥ २॥

English
In an instant, Kāma, the lover of Rati, came there along with Vasanta. He was accompanied by Rati too. Being powerful enough to conquer the three worlds he was very haughty. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणामं च ततः कृत्वा स्थित्वा तत्पुरतः स्मरः ।
महोन्नतमनास्तात साञ्जलिः शक्रमब्रवीत् ॥ ३॥

English
Making due obeisance standing in front of Indra, the lofty-minded Kāma joined his palms in reverence and said :— (3.)

---

Sanskrit
काम उवाच ।
किं कार्यं ते समुत्पन्नं स्मृतोऽहं केन हेतुना ।
तत् त्वं कथय देवेश तत्कर्तुं समुपागतः ॥ ४॥

English
Kāma said :—  “What is the matter that has cropped up now? Wherefore was I remembered? Please tell me. I am here to carry it out”. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य कन्दर्पस्य सुरेश्वरः ।
उवाच वचनं प्रीत्या युक्तं युक्तमिति स्तुवन् ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā said :—  On hearing the words of Kāma, Indra, the lord of the gods, said praising him lovingly saying “well done, very proper”. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्र उवाच ।
तव साधु समारम्भो यन्मे कार्यमुपस्थितम् ।
तत्कतुर्मुद्यतोऽसि त्वं धन्योऽसि मकरध्वज ॥ ६॥

English
Indra said :—  O Kāma you are blessed indeed, since you are in readiness to carry out the affair I have on hand. You have begun well. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रस्तुतं श‍ृणु मद्वाक्यं कथयामि तवाग्रतः ।
मदीयं चैव यत्कार्यं त्वदीयं तन्न चान्यथा ॥ ७॥

English
Listen to what is relevant to the context. I shall tell you everything. My job is equally your job and not otherwise. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मित्राणि मम सन्त्येव बहूनि सुमहान्ति च ।
परं तु स्मर सन्मित्रं त्वत्तुल्यं न हि कुत्रचित् ॥ ८॥

English
I have many friends and great friends at that. But, O Kāma, I have no other friend on a par with you anywhere. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जयार्थं मे द्वयं तात निर्मितं वज्रमुत्तमम् ।
वज्रं च निष्फलं स्याद्वै त्वं तु नैव कदाचन ॥ ९॥

English
O dear, for my conquest, the unparalleled thunderbolt has been made. Even that weapon may sometimes be ineffective but you are never so. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यतो हितं प्रजायेत ततः को नु प्रियः परः ।
तस्मान्मित्रवरस्त्वं हि मत्कार्यं कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ १०॥

English
Whō can be dearer than the person from whom one derives benefit? Hence you, my greatest friend, must carry out my task. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
मम दुःखं समुत्पन्नमसाध्यं चापि कालजम् ।
केनापि नैव तच्छक्यं दूरीकर्तुं त्वया विना ॥ ११॥

English
Time being accursed, a great irremediable misery has befallen me. None other than you can dispel it. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
दातुः परीक्षा दुर्भिक्षे रणे शूरस्य जायते ।
आपत्काले तु मित्रस्याशक्तौ स्त्रीणां कुलस्य हि ॥ १२॥

English
The test of a donor is at the time of famine; the test of a warrior is at the time of battle; the test of a friend is at the time of adversity and the test of a woman is in the financial weakness of the family. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विनये सङ्कटे प्राप्तेऽवितथस्य परोक्षतः ।
सुस्नेहस्य तथा तात नान्यथा सत्यमीरितम् ॥ १३॥

English
O dear, the test of a real friend is in the time of distress and is also based on what he does behind the back. It is not otherwise. This is truth. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्तायां वै ममापत्ताववार्यायां परेण हि ।
परीक्षा च त्वदीयाद्या मित्रवर्य भविष्यति ॥ १४॥

English
Now that an adversity has befallen me, which cannot be thwarted by anyone else, O dear friend, it shall be a test for you today. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
न केवलं मदीयं च कार्यमस्ति सुखावहम् ।
किं तु सर्वसुरादीनां कार्यमेतन्न संशयः ॥ १५॥

English
This is not a matter that brings pleasure to me alone. This is a matter that concerns all the gods and others too. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येतन्मघवद्वाक्यं श्रुत्वा तु मकरध्वजाः ।
उवाच प्रेमगम्भीरं वाक्यं सुस्मितपूर्वकम् ॥ १६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Indra the fish-bannered god Kāma spoke smilingly in words indicating love and gravity. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
काम उवाच ।
किमर्थमित्थं वदसि नोत्तरं वच्म्यहं तव ।
उपकृत्कृत्रिमं लोके दृश्यते कथ्यते न च ॥ १७॥

English
*Kāma said :—*  Why do you say like this? I make no answer to you. A helping unreal friend is neither seen nor spoken of in the world. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्कटे बहु यो ब्रूते स किं कार्यं करिष्यति ।
तथापि च महाराज कथयामि श‍ृणु प्रभो ॥ १८॥

English
He who speaks much at the time of adversity will not turn out much. Yet, O king, my lord, I shall say something. Please listen. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पदं ते कर्षितुं यो वै तपस्तपति दारुणम् ।
पातयिष्याम्यहं तं च शत्रुं ते मित्र सर्वथा ॥ १९॥

English
O dear friend, I shall cause the downfall of that enemy of yours who is performing a severe penance to usurp your position. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणेन भ्रंशयिष्यामि कटाक्षेण वरस्त्रियाः ।
देवर्षिदानवादींश्च नराणां गणना न मे ॥ २०॥

English
I shall topple gods, sages, demons and others through the side-glances of a beautiful woman. I do not at all take human beings into consideration. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्रं तिष्ठतु दूरे वै शस्त्राण्यन्यान्यनेकशः ।
किं ते कार्यं करिष्यन्ति मयि मित्र उपस्थिते ॥ २१॥

English
Let your thunderbolt and other weapons of innumerable varieties be set aside. What will they do when I, your friend, am present? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माणं वा हरिं वापि भ्रष्टं कुर्यां न संशयः ।
अन्येषां गणना नास्ति पातयेयं हरं त्वपि ॥ २२॥

English
I can undoubtedly make Brahmā and Viṣṇu go astray. Others are of no consideration. I shall make even Śiva fall. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चैव मृदवो बाणास्ते च पुष्पमया मम ।
चापस्त्रिधा पुष्पमयश्शिञ्जिनी भ्रमरार्जिता ॥ २३॥

English
I have only five arrows that are soft and flowery. My bow is of three types. That too is flowery. The bow-string consists of bees. (23.)

---

English
My support and strength is my beloved wife Ratī Spring is my minister. O god, I am having five forces. The moon, the storehouse of nectar, is my friend. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
बलं सुदयिता मे हि वसन्तः सचिवः स्मृतः ।
अहं पञ्चबलो देव मित्रं मम सुधानिधिः । २४॥

सेनाधिपश्च श‍ृङ्गारो हावभावाश्च सैनिकाः ।
सर्वे मे मृदवः शक्र अहं चापि तथाविधः ॥ २५॥

English
The sentiment of love is my commander-in-chief. The coquettish gestures and emotions are my soldiers. All these are soft and gentle. O Indra, I too am of that sort. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्येन पूर्यते कार्यं धीमांस्तत्तेन योजयेत् ।
मम योग्यं तु यत्कार्यं सर्वं तन्मे नियोजय ॥ २६॥

English
An intelligent man shall put together things that are mutually complementary. You shall therefore engage me in a task that accords with my capacity. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं तु वचस्तस्य श्रुत्वा शक्रः सुहर्षितः ।
उवाच प्रणमन्वाचा कामं कान्तासुखावहम् ॥ २७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing his words, Indra was much pleased. Pleasing Kāma, the bestower of cherished happiness, by means of his words, he spoke. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्र उवाच ।
यत्कार्यं मनसोद्दिष्टं मया तात मनोभव ।
कर्त्तुं तत् त्वं समर्थोऽसि नान्यस्मात्तस्य सम्भवः ॥ २८॥

English
*Indra said :—*   O dear Kāma, you are competent to carry out the task which I have conceived in my mind. It cannot be realised through anyone else. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु काम प्रवक्ष्यामि यथार्थं मित्रसत्तम ।
यदर्थे च स्पृहा जाता तव चाद्य मनोभव ॥ २९॥

English
O Kāma, foremost among my friends, listen. I shall explain truly for what I remembered you and desired your presence, O Kāma. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तारकाख्यो महादैत्यो ब्रह्मणो वरमद्भुतम् ।
अभूतजेयः सम्प्राप्य सर्वेषामपि दुःखदः ॥ ३०॥

English
Securing a wonderful boon from Brahmā, the great demon Tāraka has become invincible and a pest for everyone. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन सम्पीड्यते लोको नष्टा धर्मा ह्यनेकशः ।
दुःखिता निर्जराः सर्वे ऋषयश्च तथाखिलाः ॥ ३१॥

English
The entire world is harassed by him. Many virtuous rites are destroyed. The gods have become miserable and so also the sages. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवैश्च सकलैस्तेन कृतं युद्धं यथाबलम् ।
सर्वेषां चायुधान्यत्र विफलान्यभवन्पुरा ॥ ३२॥

English
He had been fought by the gods to the utmost of their ability formerly. But the weapons of all the gods became quite futile. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
भग्नः पाशो जलेशस्य हरिचक्रं सुदर्शनम् ।
तत्कुण्ठितमभूत्तस्य कण्ठे क्षिप्तं च विष्णुना ॥ ३३॥

English
The noose of Varuṇa, the god of waters, snapped. When hurled at his neck by Viṣṇu, the discus Sudarśana was blunted. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्य मरणं प्रोक्तं प्रजेशेन दुरात्मनः ।
शम्भोर्वीर्योद्भवाद्बालान्महायोगीश्वरस्य हि ॥ ३४॥

English
The death of this wicked demon has been foretold by Brahmā, the lord of the people, at the hands of the boy born of Śiva, the great Yogin. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्कार्यं त्वया साधु कर्तव्यं सुप्रयत्नतः ।
ततःस्यान्मित्रवर्याति देवानां नः परं सुखम् ॥ ३५॥

English
O dear friend, this task must be achieved by you diligently. Then we, the gods, can be very happy. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ममापि विहितं तस्मात्सर्वलोकसुखावहम् ।
मित्रधर्मं हृदि स्मृत्वा कर्तुमर्हसि साम्प्रतम् ॥ ३६॥

English
It will be beneficent to me. It will render the whole world happy. Realising the duties of a friend you are now to act. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुःस गिरिराजे हि तपः परममास्थितः ।
स प्रभुर्नापि कामेन स्वतन्त्रः परमेश्वरः ॥ ३७॥

English
Śiva is at present engaged in a great penance. The supreme lord is always independent. It is not to achieve any desire that He performs the penance. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्समीपे च देवार्थं पार्वती स्वसखीयुता ।
सेवमाना तिष्ठतीति पित्राज्ञप्ता मया श्रुतम् ॥ ३८॥

English
For the sake of gods, at the bidding of her father, Pārvatī is attending on Him, I hear. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा तस्यां रुचिस्तस्य शिवस्य नियतात्मनः ।
जायेत नितरां मार तथा कार्यं त्वया ध्रुवम् ॥ ३९॥

English
O Kāma, you shall certainly do everything necessary to bring about an interest in her in the mind of Śiva who has self-control. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इति कृत्वा कृती स्यास्त्वं सर्वं दुःखं विनङ्क्ष्यति ।
लोके स्थायी प्रतापस्ते भविष्यति न चान्यथा ॥ ४०॥

English
You will become contented after this. Your miseries will be destroyed. Your exploit will be permanently established in the world. Not otherwise. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्तः स तु कामो हि प्रफुल्लमुखपङ्कजः ।
प्रेम्णोवाचेति देवेशं करिष्यामि न संशयः ॥ ४१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On being thus addressed, Kāma was glad, his face beaming like a full blown lotus. He lovingly said to the lord of gods, “I shall undoubtedly do it.” (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा वचनं तस्मै तथेत्योमिति तद्वचः ।
अग्रहीत्तरसा कामः शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ ४२॥

यत्र योगीश्वरः साक्षात्तप्यते परमं तपः ।
जगाम तत्र सुप्रीतः सदारः सवसन्तकः ॥ ४३॥

English
After saying this when he said “So be it” he said “yes.” Kāma accepted it because he was deluded by Śiva’s illusion. Accompanied by his wife and Spring he went to the place where Śiva, the Yogin, was performing penance. (42-43.)
