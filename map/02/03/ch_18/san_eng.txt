### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - *(Description of the perturbation caused by Kāma)***

---

Sanskrit
१८

कामविकारवर्णनं
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तत्र गत्वा स्मरो गर्वी शिवमायाविमोहितः ।
मोहकः स मधोश्चादौ धर्मं विस्तारयन्स्थितः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After going there, the haughty Kāma, deluded by Śiva’s magic power, stationed himself, after first spreading the enchanting power of Spring all around. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वसन्तस्य च यो धर्मः प्रससार स सर्वतः ।
तपःस्थाने महेशस्यौषधिप्रस्थे मुनीश्वर ॥ २॥

English
The enchanting influence of Spring spread every- where around Oṣadhiprastha, the penance-grove of Śiva, the supreme lord, O excellent sage. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वनानि च प्रफुल्लानि पादपानां महामुने ।
आसन्विशेषतस्तत्र तत्प्रभावान्मुनीश्वर ॥ ३॥

English
O great sage, the groves bloomed with special exuberance, O excellent sage, due to his power. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पाणि सहकाराणामशोकवनिकासु वै ।
विरेजुस्सुस्मरोद्दीपकराणि सुरभीण्यपि ॥ ४॥

English
The fragrant flowers of Mango and Aśoka trees shone heightening feelings of love. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कैरवाणि च पुष्पाणि भ्रमराकलितानि च ।
बभूवुर्मदनावेशकराणि च विशेषतः ॥ ५॥

English
The water lilies with bees hovering on them proved to be the causes for the rise of love in the minds of everyone. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सुकामोद्दीपनकरं कोकिलाकलकूजितम् ।
आसीदति सुरम्यं हि मनोहरमतिप्रियम् ॥ ६॥

English
The sweet cooings of the cuckoos heightened emotions of love. They were exquisite and pleasing to the mind. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रमराणां तथा शब्दा विविधा अभवन्मुने ।
मनोहराश्च सर्वेषां कामोद्दीपकरा अपि ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, diverse sounds of the hummings of the bees rang sweet in the ears of everyone heightening temptations of love. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रस्य विशदा कान्तिर्विकीर्णा हि समन्ततः ।
कामिनां कामिनीनां च दूतिका इव साभवत् ॥ ८॥

English
The bright light of the moon scattered all around appeared to be the emissary of lovers and their beloveds. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मानिनां प्रेरणायासीत्तत्काले कालदीपिका ।
मारुतश्च सुखः साधो ववौ विरहिणोऽप्रियः ॥ ९॥

English
At that time the Kāladīpikā (brilliant lamp) induced reticent haughty persons to love. O good sir, the wind blew gently but distressed those who were separated from their beloveds. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वसन्तविस्तारो मदनावेशकारकः ।
वनौकसां तदा तत्र मुनीनां दुस्सहोऽत्यभूत् ॥ १०॥

English
Thus the vast diffusion of Spring caused the display of emotions of love. It was unbearable to the forest-dwelling sages. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अचेतसामपि तदा कामासक्तिरभून्मुने ।
सुचेतसां हि जीवानां सेति किं वर्ण्यते कथा ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, then, even the insentient beings had the emotions of love. What about the state of sentient ones? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं चकार स मधुः स्वप्रभावं सुदुस्सहम् ।
सर्वेषां चैव जीवानां कामोद्दीपनकारकः ॥ १२॥

English
Thus spring employed his unbearable power heightening the love of all living beings. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अकालनिर्मितं तात मधोर्वीक्ष्य हरस्तदा ।
आश्चर्यं परमं मेने स्वलीलात्ततनुः प्रभुः ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing the untimely display of spring, Śiva the lord, who had assumed a physical body indulging in divine sports, thought it surprising. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ लीलाकरस्तत्र तपः परमदुष्करम् ।
तताप स वशीशो हि हरो दुःखहरः प्रभुः ॥ १४॥

English
But He, the chief of the self-controlled and the remover of man's misery continued his severe penance. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वसन्ते प्रसृते तत्र कामो रतिसमन्वितः ।
चूतं बाणं समाकृष्य स्थितस्तद्वामपार्श्वतः ॥ १५॥

English
When spring spread everywhere, Kāma accompanied by Rati stood on his left side, with the arrow of mango blossom taken out and kept in readiness. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वप्रभावं वितस्तार मोहयन्सकलान् जनान् ।
रत्या युक्तं तदा कामं दृष्ट्वा को वा न मोहितः ॥ १६॥

English
Enchanting all people, he spread his influence. Who was not enchanted on seeing Kāma in the company of Rati? (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं प्रवृत्तसुरतौ श‍ृङ्गारोऽपि गणैः सह ।
हावभावयुतस्तत्र प्रविवेश हरान्तिकम् ॥ १७॥

English
Thus they initiated their dalliance. The sentiment of love too accompanied by coquettish gestures and emotions reached the vicinity of Śiva along with his attendants. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मदनः प्रकटस्तत्र न्यवसच्चित्तगो बहिः ।
न दृष्टवांस्तदा शम्भोश्छिद्रं येन प्रविश्यते ॥ १८॥

English
Kāma, usually stationed within the mind manifested himself outside. But he could not find any vulnerable loop-hole in Śiva whereby he could enter Him. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा चाप्राप्तविवरस्तस्मिन्योगिवरे स्मरः ।
महादेवे तदा सोऽभून्महाभयविमोहितः ॥ १९॥

English
When Kāma did not secure any entry within the great Yogin, he became deluded and frightened much through the magical power of Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्वलज्ज्वालाग्निसङ्काशभालनेत्रसमन्वितम् ।
ध्यानस्थं शङ्करं को वा समासादयितुं क्षमः ॥ २०॥

English
Who could gain access to Śiva in meditation, who could fix an eye in his forehead that resembled fire with shooting blazing flames? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र सखीभ्यां संयुता शिवा ।
जगाम शिवपूजार्थं नीत्वा पुष्पाण्यनेकशः ॥ २१॥

English
In the mean time Pārvatī came there along with her two maids and brought various kinds of flowers for Śiva’s worship. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यां यादृशं लोकैः सौन्दर्यं वर्ण्यते महत् ।
तत्सर्वमधिकं तस्यां पार्वत्यामस्ति निश्चितम् ॥ २२॥

English
Certainly Pārvatī had a greater beauty than the most exquisite lady described by people on the earth. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्तवाणि सुपुष्पाणि धृतानि च तया यदा ।
तत्सौन्दर्यं कथं वर्ण्यमपि वर्षशतैरपि ॥ २३॥

English
When she wore pretty flowers of the season how could her beauty be described even in a hundred years? (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा शिवसमीपे तु गता सा पर्वतात्मजा ।
तदैव शङ्करो ध्यानं त्यक्त्वा क्षणमवस्थितः ॥ २४॥

English
No sooner did she enter within the proximity of Śiva than He came out of his meditation for a short while. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छिद्रं प्राप्य मदनः प्रथमं हर्षणेन तु ।
बाणेन हर्षयामास पार्श्वस्थं चन्द्रशेखरम् ॥ २५॥

English
Profiting by that opportune moment, Kāma, by means of his arrow Harṣaṇa delighted the moon-crest god Śiva who was nearby. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृङ्गारैश्च तदा भावैः सहिता पार्वती हरम् ।
जगाम कामसाहाय्ये मुने सुरभिणा सह ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, in assistance to Kāma, Pārvatī reached the place near Śiva with emotions of love and accompanied by Spring. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैवाकृष्य तच्चापं रुच्यर्थं शूलधारिणः ।
द्रुतं पुष्पशरं तस्मै स्मरोऽमुञ्चत्सुसंयतः ॥ २७॥

English
In order to make the trident-bearing lord take interest in her, Kāma drew his bow very carefully and discharged his flowery arrow on Him. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा निरन्तरं नित्यमागच्छति तथा शिवम् ।
तं नमस्कृत्य तत्पूजां कृत्वा तत्पुरतः स्थिता ॥ २८॥

English
As was her usual practice she approached Śiva, bowed to Him, worshipped Him and stood in front of Him (awaiting further instructions). (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सा दृष्टा पार्वती तत्र प्रभुणा गिरिशेन हि ।
विवृण्वती तदाङ्गानि स्त्रीस्वभावात्सुलज्जया ॥ २९॥

English
Pārvatī was stared at by lord Śiva, while she was laying bare some of the limbs bashfully, as is natural to women in such circumstances. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सुसंस्मृत्य वरं तस्या विधिदत्तं पुरा प्रभुः ।
शिवोऽपि वर्णयामास तदङ्गानि मुदा मुने ॥ ३०॥

English
Remembering the boon granted to her by Brahmā formerly, O sage, lord Śiva began to describe her limbs joyfully. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
किं मुखं किं शशाङ्कश्च किं नेत्रे चोत्पले च किम् ।
भ्रुकुट्यौ धनुषी चैते कन्दर्पस्य महात्मनः ॥ ३१॥

English
Śiva said :— “Is this your face or the moon? Are these your eyes or lotus petals? These two eyebrows are the bows of Kāma of noble soul. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अधरः किं च बिम्बं किं किं नासा शुकचञ्चुका ।
किं स्वरः कोकिलालापः किं मध्यं चाथ वेदिका ॥ ३२॥

English
Is this your lower lip or Bimba fruit? Is this your nose or the beak of a parrot? Do I hear your voice or the cooing of the cuckoo? Is this your slender waist or the sacrificial altar? (32.)

---

Sanskrit
किं गतिर्वर्ण्यते ह्यस्याः किं रूपं वर्ण्यते मुहुः ।
पुष्पाणि किं च वर्ण्यन्ते वस्त्राणि च तथा पुनः ॥ ३३॥

English
How can her gait be described? How can her comely appearance be described? How can the flowers be described? How can the clothes be described? (33.)

---

Sanskrit
लालित्यं चारु यत्सृष्टौ तदेकत्र विनिर्मितम् ।
सर्वथा रमणीयानि सर्वाङ्गानि न संशयः ॥ ३४॥

English
Whatever is graceful and sweet in the creation has been incorporated here. Indeed, all her limbs are exquisite in every respect. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो धन्यतरा चेयं पार्वत्यद्भुतरूपिणी ।
एतत्समा न त्रैलोक्ये नारी कापि सुरूपिणी ॥ ३५॥

English
How blessed is this Pārvatī of mysteriously wonderful features. There is no other woman equal to her in beauty in the three worlds. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सुलावण्यनिधिश्चेयमद्भुताङ्गानि बिभ्रती ।
विमोहिनी मुनीनां च महासुखविवर्धीनी ॥ ३६॥

English
She is a storehouse of the finest beauty. She has wondrous beautiful limbs. She is an enchantress of even sages. She increases great happiness.” (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वर्णयित्वा तु तदङ्गानि मुहुर्मुहुः ।
विधिदत्तवराध्यासाद्धरस्तु विरराम ह ॥ ३७॥

English
Brahmā said:—  After describing her body again and again, recollecting the boon granted by Brahmā, Śiva stopped. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
हस्तं वस्त्रान्तरे यावदचालयत शङ्करः ।
स्त्रीस्वभावाच्च सा तत्र लज्जिता दूरतो गता ॥ ३८॥

English
When Śiva put His hand within her garment and moved it, she, as is natural to women, bashfully withdrew and kept aloof. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
विवृण्वती निजाङ्गानि पश्यन्ती च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
सुवीक्षणैर्महामोदात्सुस्मिताभूच्छिवा मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, then Pārvatī smilingly laid bare some parts of her body and cast graceful glances at Him with great pleasure. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं चेष्टां तदा दृष्ट्वा शम्भुर्मोहमुपागतः ।
उवाच वचनं चैवं महालीलो महेश्वरः ॥ ४०॥

English
On seeing these movements and gestures Śiva became fascinated. Lord Śiva indulging in great divine sports spoke these words. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्या दर्शनमात्रेण महानन्दो भवत्यलम् ।
यदालिङ्गनमेतस्याः कुर्यां किन्तु ततः सुखम् ॥ ४१॥

English
“I feel great pleasure on merely seeing her. What pleasure shall I derive by embracing her?” (41.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणमात्रं विचार्येत्थं सम्पूज्य गिरिजां ततः ।
प्रबुद्धः स महायोगी सुविरक्तो जगाविति ॥ ४२॥

English
Thinking thus only for a moment, the enlightened Śiva became detached, honoured Pārvatī and spoke. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
किं जातं चरितं चित्रं किमहं मोहमागतः ।
कामेन विकृतश्चाद्य भूत्वापि प्रभुरीश्वरः ॥ ४३॥

English
“How wonderful and mysterious is the situation that has arisen! How is it that I have been deluded and fascinated ? Though I am the lord and master, I have been perturbed by Kāma. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वरोऽहं यदीच्छेयं पराङ्गस्पर्शनं खलु ।
तर्हि कोऽन्योऽक्षमः क्षुद्रः किं किं नैव करिष्यति ॥ ४४॥

English
If I, the master, were to yearn for the touch of a woman's limbs what will not be done by other incompetent and insignificant creatures". (44.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वैराग्यमासाद्य पर्यङ्कासादनं च तत् ।
वारयामास सर्वात्मा परेशः किं पतेदिह ॥ ४५॥

English
Thus resuming detachment, lord Śiva forbade her sitting on the couch. How can there be a downfall for the great lord Śiva ? (45.)
