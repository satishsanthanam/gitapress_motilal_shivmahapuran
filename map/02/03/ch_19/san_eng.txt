### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - *(Kāma's destruction by Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
१९

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन्विधे महाभाग किं जातं तदनन्तरम् ।
कथय त्वं प्रसादेन तां कथां पापनाशिनीम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O Brahma, the most fortunate one, what happened then ? Be pleased to tell me that story that destroys our sins altogether. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रूयतां सा कथा तात यज्जातं तदनन्तरम् ।
तव स्नेहात्प्रवक्ष्यामि शिवलीलां मुदावहाम् ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O dear one, hear the story of what happened thereafter. Out of love for me I shall recount Śiva's sports that bring about joy. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
धैर्यस्य व्यसनं दृष्ट्वा महायोगी महेश्वरः ।
विचिचिन्त मनस्येवं विस्मितोऽति ततः परम् ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the dissipation of His courage, lord Śiva, the great Yogin, thought within Himself wondering much. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
किमु विघ्नाः समुत्पन्नाः कुर्वतस्तप उत्तमम् ।
केन मे विकृतं चित्तं कृतमत्र कुकर्मिणा ॥ ४॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  How is it that obstacles have cropped up while I am performing the great penance ? Who can be that wicked person who has made my mind highly perturbed ? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कुवर्णनं मया प्रीत्या परस्त्र्युपरि वै कृतम् ।
जातो धर्मविरोधोऽत्र श्रुतिसीमा विलङ्घिता ॥ ५॥

English
With love I have described in bad taste another man's woman. I have contravened rules of virtue and transgressed the bounds of the Vedas. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
विचिन्त्येत्थं महायोगी परमेशः सतां गतिः ।
दिशो विलोकयामास परितः शङ्कितस्तदा ॥ ६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After thinking like this, the great Yogin, the goal of the good, surveyed all round, his suspicion having been aroused. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
वामभागे स्थितं कामं ददर्शाकृष्टबाणकम् ।
स्वशरं क्षेप्तुकामं हि गर्वितं मूढचेतसम् ॥ ७॥

English
He saw Kāma stationed on His left side with his bow fully drawn and ready to discharge the arrow. Kāma was haughty and so was very senseless. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा तादृशं कामं गिरीशस्य परात्मनः ।
सञ्जातः क्रोधसम्मर्दस्तत्क्षणादपि नारद ॥ ८॥

English
O Nārada, on seeing Kāma in that attitude, instantaneously anger was aroused in lord Śiva, the supreme soul. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
कामः स्थितोऽन्तरिक्षे स धृत्वा तत्सशरं धनुः ।
चिक्षेपास्त्रं दुर्निवारममोघं शङ्करे मुने ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, standing high up in the air, holding the arrow and the bow, Kāma discharged his arrow, usually unerring on Śiva (9.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवामोघमस्त्रं तु मोघं तत्परमात्मनि ।
समशाम्यत्ततस्तस्मिन्सङ्क्रुद्धे परमेश्वरे ॥ १०॥

English
The infallible weapon became futile on the great lord. The furious weapon calmed down in regard to the great soul, Śiva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
मोघीभूते शिवे स्वेऽस्त्रे भयमापाशु मन्मथः ।
चकम्पे च पुरः स्थित्वा दृष्ट्वा मृत्युञ्जयं प्रभुम् ॥ ११॥

English
Kāma was frightened when his weapon failed. Standing there and seeing lord Śiva, the conqueror of death in front, he trembled. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सस्मार त्रिदशान्सर्वान्शक्रादीन्भयविह्वलः ।
स स्मरो मुनिशार्दूल स्वप्रयासे निरर्थके ॥ १२॥

English
O great sage, when his endeavour became futile, Kāma who was frightened much remembered Indra and all other gods. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
कामेन सुस्मृता देवाः शक्राद्यास्ते मुनीश्वर ।
आययुः सकलास्ते हि शम्भुं नत्वा च तुष्टुवुः ॥ १३॥

English
O great sage, remembered by Kāma, Indra and other gods came there, bowed to and eulogised Śiva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुतिं कुर्वत्सु देवेषु क्रुद्धस्याति हरस्य हि ।
तृतीयात्तस्य नेत्राद्वै निस्ससार ततो महान् ॥ १४॥

English
When the gods eulogised thus, a great flame of fire sprang up from the third eye of the infuriated Śiva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ललाटमध्यगात्तस्मात्सवह्निर्द्रुतसम्भवः ।
जज्वालोर्ध्वशिखो दीप्तः प्रलयाग्निसमप्रभः ॥ १५॥

English
That fire originating instantaneously from the eye in the middle of His forehead blazed with flames shooting up and resembling the fire of final dissolution in refulgence. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पत्य गगने तूर्णं निपत्य धरणी तले ।
भ्रामं भ्रामं स्वपरितः पपात मेदिनीं परि ॥ १६॥

English
After shooting up in the sky, it fell on the ground and rolled over the earth all round. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
भस्मसात्कृतवान्साधो मदनं तावदेव हि ।
यावच्च मरुतां वाचः क्षम्यतां क्षम्यतामिति ॥ १७॥

English
Even before the gods had the time to say "Let him be forgiven, let him be excused" it reduced Kāma to ashes. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
हते तस्मिन्स्मरे वीरे देव दुःखमुपागताः ।
रुरुदुर्विह्वलाश्चातिक्रोशन्तः किमभूदिति ॥ १८॥

English
When the heroic Kāma was thus slain, the gods became miserable. In their agitation they lamented much and saying "O what has happened?" they cried aloud. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
श्वेताङ्गा विकृतात्मा च गिरिराजसुता तदा ।
जगाम मन्दिरं स्वं च समादाय सखीजनम् ॥ १९॥

English
With pallid face and limbs, the extremely agitated daughter of the king of mountains returned to her palace taking the maids along with her. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणमात्रं रतिस्तत्र विसंज्ञा साभवत्तदा ।
भर्तृमृत्युजदुःखेन पतिता सा मृता इव ॥ २०॥

English
Due to the misery on account of the death of her husband, Rati fell down unconscious, as if dead. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
जातायां चैव संज्ञायां रतिरत्यन्तविह्वला ।
विललाप तदा तत्रोच्चरन्ती विविधं वचः ॥ २१॥

English
When she regained consciousness after a while, Rati in her great agitation lamented loudly and said:— (21.)

---

Sanskrit
रतिरुवाच ।
किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि किं कृतं दैवतैरिह ।
मत्स्वामिनं समाहूय नाशयामासुरुद्धतम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Rati said: —*  "What shall I do? Where can I go? What is it that the gods have done in making my husband a victim thus ? They have called him here and destroyed him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
हा हा नाथ स्मर स्वामिन्प्राणप्रिय सुखप्रद ।
इदं तु किमभूदत्र हा हा प्रिय प्रियेति च ॥ २३॥

English
O lord Kāma, dearer to me than my vital airs, O bestower of happiness, what has happened here ? Hā, Hā, my dear, my dear !” (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थं विलपती सा तु वदन्ती बहुधा वचः ।
हस्तौ पादौ तदास्फाल्य केशानत्रोटयत्तदा ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Lamenting thus and crying out various piteous words she beat with her hands, kicked with her legs and plucked her hairs. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्विलापं तदा श्रुत्वा तत्र सर्वे वनेचराः ।
अभवन्दुःखिताः सर्वे स्थावरा अपि नारद ॥ २५॥

English
O Nārada, on hearing her lamentation even the beasts and residents of the forest, nay all the immobile trees and bushes became miserable. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र देवाः शक्रादयोऽखिलाः ।
रतिमूचुः समाश्वास्य संस्मरन्तो महेश्वरम् ॥ २६॥

English
In the meantime Indra and other gods remembered lord Śiva and consoled Rati saying as follows:— (26.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
किञ्चिद्भस्म गृहीत्वा तु रक्ष यत्नाद्भयं त्यज ।
जीवयिष्यति स स्वामी लप्स्यसे त्वं पुनः प्रियम् ॥ २७॥

English
*The gods said:—*  Take some ashes and preserve them. With effort check your fear. The lord will resuscitate your lover. You will regain your lover again. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखदाता न कोऽप्यस्ति दुःखदाता न कश्चन ।
सर्वोऽपि स्वकृतं भुङ्क्ते देवान् शोचसि वै वृथा ॥ २८॥

English
There is none who gives us happiness or misery. All enjoy and experience the fruit of what they do. In vain do you curse the gods. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याश्वास्य रतिं देवाः सर्वे शिवमुपागताः ।
सुप्रसाद्य शिवं भक्त्या वचनं चेदमब्रुवन् ॥ २९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After consoling Rati thus, all the gods approached Śiva and propitiated Him. With great devotion they spoke these words to Him. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
भगवन् श्रूयतामेतद्वचनं नः शुभं प्रभो ।
कृपां कृत्वा महेशान शरणागतवत्सल ॥ ३०॥

English
*The gods said :—*  O lord, O great god, favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in you, be pleased to listen to these well intended words of ours. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सुविचारय सुप्रीत्या कृतिं कामस्य शङ्कर ।
कामेनैतत्कृतं यत्र न स्वार्थं तन्महेश्वर ॥ ३१॥

English
O Śiva, be pleased to ponder over the action of Kāma. O lord Śiva, there is no tinge of selfishness in what Kāma has done. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
दुष्टेन पीडितैर्देवैस्तारकेणाखिलैर्विभो ।
कर्म तत्कारितं नाथ नान्यथा विद्धि शङ्कर ॥ ३२॥

English
O lord, he had been induced to do so by all the gods harassed by the wicked Tāraka. O Śiva, please know that it is not otherwise. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
रतिरेकाकिनी देव विलापं दुःखिता सती ।
करोति गिरिश त्वं च तामाश्वासय सर्वद ॥ ३३॥

English
O lord, the chaste Rati is lonely and miserable now. O lord Śiva, she is in great lamentation, O bestower of everything, please console her. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
संहारं कर्तुकामोऽसि क्रोधेनानेन शङ्कर ।
दैवतैः सह सर्वेषां हतवांस्तं यदि स्मरम् ॥ ३४॥

English
If you have finally disposed off Kāma, O Śiva, you are desirous of annihilating all the gods by means of your fury. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःखं तस्या रतेर्दृष्ट्वा नष्टप्रायाश्च देवताः ।
तस्मात्त्वया च कर्तव्यं रत्याश्शोकापनोदनम् ॥ ३५॥

English
On seeing the distress of Rati, the gods are almost doomed. Hence you must remove the distress of Rati. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां प्रसन्नो भगवान् शिवः ।
देवानां सकलानां च वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ ३६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing their words, lord Śiva was pleased. He said this to all the gods. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
देवाश्च ऋषयः सर्वे मद्वचः श‍ृणुतादरात् ।
मत्कोपेन च यज्जातं तत्तथा नान्यथा भवेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O gods, O sages, all of you listen attentively to my words. What has happened, thanks to my fury, cannot be altered. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अनङ्गस्तावदेव स्यात्कामो रतिपतिः प्रभुः ।
यावच्चावतरेत्कृष्णो धरण्यां रुक्मिणीपतिः ॥ ३८॥

English
The lord Kāma, the husband of Rati, shall remain bodiless till Viṣṇu incarnates as Kṛṣṇa on the earth and marries Rukmiṇi. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारकायां यदा स्थित्वा पुत्रानुत्पादयिष्यति ।
तदा कृष्णस्तु रुक्मिण्यां काममुत्पादयिष्यति ॥ ३९॥

English
Kṛṣṇa will beget Kāma in Rukmiṇi when he goes to Dvārakā$^{25}$ and begins to procreate children. (39.)

[fn 25. This town, associated with Lord Kṛṣṇa, is situated in Kathiawar.]
---

Sanskrit
प्रद्युम्नं नाम तस्यैव भविष्यति न संशयः ।
जातमात्रं तु तं पुत्रं शम्बरः संहरिष्यति ॥ ४०॥

English
His name will certainly be Pradyumna. The demon Śambara will abduct the boy at the time of his very birth. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
हृत्वा प्रास्य समुद्रं तं शम्बरो दानवोत्तमः ।
मृतं ज्ञात्वा वृथा मूढो नगरं स्वं गमिष्यति ॥ ४१॥

English
After abducting the boy, the great demon, Śambara, will throw him in the sea. The foolish fellow will take him for dead and will return to his city. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तावच्च नगरं तस्य रते स्थेयं यथासुखम् ।
तत्रैव स्वपतेः प्राप्तिः प्रद्युम्नस्य भविष्यति ॥ ४२॥

English
O Rati, you shall stay in his city till then. There alone you will get back your husband Pradyumna. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र कामो मिलित्वा तं हत्वा शम्बरमाहवे ।
भविष्यति सुखी देवाः प्रद्युम्नाख्यः स्वकामिनीम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Kāma in the name of Pradyumna will regain his wife after killing Śambara in a battle. O gods, he will be happy thereafter. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तदीयं चैव यद् द्रव्यं नीत्वा स नगरं पुनः ।
गमिष्यति तया सार्धं देवाः सत्यं वचो मम ॥ ४४॥

English
After taking all the valuable properties of Śambara, O gods, he will go to the city again along with her. These words of mine are true. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचः शम्भोर्देवा ऊचुः प्रणम्य तम् ।
किञ्चिदुच्छ्वसिताश्चित्ते करौ बद्ध्वा नताङ्गकाः ॥ ४५॥

English
*Brahmā said —*  On hearing these words of Śiva, the gods heaved a sigh of relief. Joining their palms in reverence and bowing to Him they said :— (45.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
शीघ्रं जीवय कामं त्वं रक्ष प्राणान् रतेर्हर ॥ ४६॥

English
*The gods said :—*  “O great god, lord of the gods, O lord, the ocean of mercy, please resuscitate Kāma quickly. O Śiva, save the life of Rati.” (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्यामरवचः प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ।
पुनर्बभाषे करुणासागरः सकलेश्वरः ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the gods, great God became delighted. The lord of all, the ocean of mercy, spoke again. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
हे देवाः सुप्रसन्नोऽस्मि जीवयिष्यामि चान्तरे ।
कामः स मद्गणो भूत्वा विहरिष्यति नित्यशः ॥ ४८॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O gods, I am delighted. I shall resuscitate Kāma within myself. He will be one of my Gaṇas and will sport about always. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
नाख्येयमिदमाख्यानं कस्यचित्पुरतः सुराः ।
गच्छत स्वस्थलं दुखं नाशयिष्यामि सर्वतः ॥ ४९॥

English
O gods, this story should not be narrated in the presence of any one. All of you return to your abodes. I shall destroy all miseries. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे रुद्रो देवानां स्तुवतां तदा ।
सर्वे देवाः सुप्रसन्ना बभूवुर्गतविस्मयाः ॥ ५०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this Rudra vanished even as the gods were eulogising Him. The gods became delighted and free from mental suspense. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तां च समाश्वास्य रुद्रस्य वचने स्थिताः ।
उक्त्वा वचस्तदीयं च स्वं स्वं धाम ययुर्मुने ॥ ५१॥

English
O sage, abiding by the directions of Śiva and consoling Rati by means of the conciliatory words of Śiva, the gods returned to their respective places. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
कामपत्नी समादिष्टं नगरं सा गता तदा ।
प्रतीक्षमाणा तं कालं रुद्रादिष्टं मुनीश्वर ॥ ५२॥

English
O excellent sage, then Rati, the wife of Kāma went to the city and waited for the time mentioned by Śiva. (52.)

इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां तृतीये पार्वतीखण्डे कामनाशवर्णनं नामैकोनविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ २.३.१९॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥ 
द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां तृतीयः पार्वतीखण्डे
