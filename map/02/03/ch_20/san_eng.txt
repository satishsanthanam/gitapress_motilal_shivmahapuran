### **CHAPTER TWENTY - *(The story of the submarine fire)***

---

Sanskrit
२०

नारद उवाच ।
विधे नेत्रसमुद्भूतवह्निज्वाला हरस्य सा ।
गता कुत्र वद त्वं तच्चरितं शशिमौलिनः ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, please tell me “Where did the flame of fire emerging from the eye of Śiva go?” Please tell me also the further story of the moon-crested lord. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
यदा भस्म चकाराशु तृतीयनयनानलः ।
शम्भोः कामं प्रजज्वाल सर्वतो विफलस्तदा ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When the fire from the third eye of Śiva reduced Kāma to ashes it began to blaze all round without burning anything. (2.)

---

English
A huge hue and cry was raised in the three worlds consisting of the mobile and immobile creatures. Immediately the gods and sages sought refuge in me. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारो महानासीत् त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ।
सर्वे देवर्षयस्तात शरणं मां ययुर्द्रुतम् । ३॥

सर्वे निवेदयामासुस्तद्दुःखं मह्यमाकुलाः ।
सुप्रणम्य सुसंस्तूय करौ बद्ध्वा नताननाः ॥ ४॥

English
All of them in their agitation bowed to and eulogised me with their palms joined in reverence and the heads bent down. They intimated to me their grief. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वाहं शिवं स्मृत्वा तद्धेतुं सुविमृश्य च ।
गतस्तत्र विनीतात्मा त्रिलोकावनहेतवे ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing that I pondered over the reason for the same, and remembering Śiva humbly I went there in order to protect the three worlds. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्दग्धुकामः स शुचिर्ज्वालामालातिदीपितः ।
स्तम्भितोऽरं मया शम्भुप्रसादाप्तसुतेजसा ॥ ६॥

English
That fire, out to burn everything, very brilliant with its shooting flames, was thwarted by me as I had the capacity by Śiva’s grace. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ क्रोधमयं वह्निं दग्धुकामं जगत्त्रयम् ।
वाडवं तमकर्षं च सौम्यज्वालामुखं मुने ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, then I made that fire of fury, out to burn the three worlds, tender in its blaze and mare-like in shape. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तं वाडवतनुमहं समादाय शिवेच्छया ।
सागरं समगां लोकहिताय जगतां पतिः ॥ ८॥

English
Taking that fire mare-like in form, at the will of Śiva, I, the lord of the worlds, went to the sea shore, for the benefit of the worlds. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतं मां समालोक्य सागरस्साञ्जलिर्मुने ।
धृत्वा च पौरुषं रूपमागतः सन्निधिं मम ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, on seeing me arrived there, the sea took a human form and approached me with palms joined in reverence. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रणम्याथ मां सिन्धुः संस्तूय च यथाविधि ।
स मामुवाच सुप्रीत्या सर्वलोकपितामहम् ॥ १०॥

English
Bowing to and duly eulogising me, the grandfather of all the worlds, the ocean said lovingly. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सागर उवाच ।
किमर्थमागतोऽसि त्वं ब्रह्मन्नत्राखिलाधिप ।
तन्निदेशय सुप्रीत्या मत्वा मां च स्वसेवकम् ॥ ११॥

अथाहं सागरवचः श्रुत्वा प्रीतिपुरस्सरम् ।
प्रावोचं शङ्करं स्मृत्वा लौकिकं हितमावहन् ॥ १२॥

English
*The ocean said :—*  “O Brahmā, the lord of all, why have you come here? Please command me with pleasure knowing me to be your servant”. On hearing the words of the ocean I remembered Śiva. I spoke with love in order to benefit the world. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात महाधीमन्सर्वलोकहितावह ।
वच्म्यहं प्रीतितःसिन्धो शिवेच्छाप्रेरितो हृदा ॥ १३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear, intelligent one, causing the welfare of all the worlds, O ocean, induced by Śiva’s will, I shall explain to you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं क्रोधो महेशस्य वाडवात्मा महाप्रभुः ।
दग्ध्वा कामं द्रुतं सर्वं दग्धुकामोऽभवत्ततः ॥ १४॥

English
This is the fire of fury of lord Śiva, the great lord. It is in the form of a mare now. After burning Kāma it was about to burn everything. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रार्थितोऽहं सुरैः शीघ्रं पीडितैः शङ्करेच्छया ।
तत्रागत्य द्रुतं तं वै तात स्तम्भितवान् शुचिम् ॥ १५॥

English
At the will of Śiva I was requested by the gods who were harassed by it, and so I went there and suppressed the fire. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वाडवं रूपमाधत्त तमादायागतोऽत्र ह ।
निर्दिशामि जलाधार त्वामहं करुणाकरः ॥ १६॥

English
I gave it the form of a mare. I have brought it here. O ocean, I ask you to be merciful. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं क्रोधी महेशस्य वाडवं रूपमाश्रितः ।
ज्वालामुखस्त्वया धार्यो यावदाभूतसम्प्लवम् ॥ १७॥

English
This fury of lord Śiva, now in the form of a mare, you will bear till the final dissolution of all living beings. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यदात्राहं समागम्य वत्स्यामि सरितां पते ।
तदा त्वया परित्याज्यः क्रोधोऽयं शाङ्करोऽद्भुतः ॥ १८॥

English
O lord of rivers, when I shall come and stay here, you shall release it. This is Śiva’s wonderful fire of fury. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
भोजनं तोयमेतस्य तव नित्यं भविष्यति ।
यत्नादेवावधार्योऽयं यथा नोपैति चान्तरम् ॥ १९॥

English
His perpetual diet shall consist of your waters. This shall be preserved by you with effort lest it should go down. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तो हि मया सिन्धुरङ्गीचक्रे तदा ध्रुवम् ।
ग्रहीतुं वाडवं वह्निं रौद्रं चाशक्यमन्यतः ॥ २०॥

English
Thus requested by me, the ocean agreed. None else could have grasped Śiva’s fire of fury thus. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रविष्टो जलधौ स वाडवतनुः शुचिः ।
वार्यौघान्सुदहंस्तस्य ज्वालामालाभिदीपितः ॥ २१॥

English
That fire in the form of a mare entered the ocean and began to consume the currents of water. It blazed with all its shooting flames. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःसन्तुष्टचेतस्कः स्वं धामाहं गतो मुने ।
अन्तर्धानमगात्सिन्धुर्दिव्यरूपः प्रणम्य माम् ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, then, delighted in mind I returned to my abode. The ocean of divine form bowed to me and vanished. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वास्थ्यं प्राप जगत्सर्वं निर्मुक्तं तद्भवाद्भयात् ।
देवा बभूवुः सुखिनो मुनयश्च महामुने ॥ २३॥

English
O great sage, the entire universe, freed from the fear of that fire became normal. The gods and the sages became happy. (23.)
