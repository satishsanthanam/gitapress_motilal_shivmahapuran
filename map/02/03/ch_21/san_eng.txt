### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - *(Nārada instructs Pārvatī)***

---

Sanskrit
२१

नारद उवाच ।
विधे तात महाप्राज्ञ विष्णुशिष्य त्रिलोककृत् ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा प्रोक्ता शङ्करस्य महात्मनः ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O dear Brahmā, O disciple of Viṣṇu, of great intellect, O Creator of three worlds, this is a very wonderful story of the great soul Śiva that has been narrated. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
भस्मीभूते स्मरे शम्भुतृतीयनयनाग्निना ।
तस्मिन्प्रविष्टे जलधौ वद त्वं किमभूत्ततः ॥ २॥

English
When Kāma had been reduced to ashes by the fire from the third eye of Śiva and when that fire had been deposited in the ocean what happened thereafter? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
किं चकार ततो देवी पार्वती कुधरात्मजा ।
गता कुत्र सखीभ्यां सा तद्वदाद्य दयानिधे ॥ ३॥

English
What did Goddess Pārvatī, the daughter of the lord of mountains, do? O storehouse of mercy, please tell me now where she went along with her maids. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात महाप्राज्ञ चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
महोतिकारकस्यैव स्वामिनो मम चादरात् ॥ ४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear, of great intellect, listen to the story of the moon-crested lord, my master and the cause of great enjoyment and protection. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यदादहच्छम्भुनेत्रोद्भवो हि मदनं शुचिः ।
महाशब्दोऽद्भुतोऽभूद्वै येनाकाशः प्रपूरितः ॥ ५॥

English
A wonderfully loud sound arose covering the whole firmament when the fire issuing from Śiva’s eye burnt Kāma. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन शब्देन महता कामं दग्धं समीक्ष्य च ।
सखीभ्यां सह भीता सा ययौ स्वगृहमाकुला ॥ ६॥

English
On hearing that loud report and seeing Kāma burnt, Pārvatī was terribly frightened and she returned to her abode along with her maids. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन शब्देन हिमवान्परिवारसमन्वितः ।
विस्मितोऽभूदतिक्लिष्टः सुतां स्मृत्वा गतां ततः ॥ ७॥

English
Himavat along with his attendants and relatives was surprised on hearing that loud report. He was agitated on remembering that his daughter had gone there. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
जगाम शोकं शैलेशो सुतां दृष्ट्वातिविह्वलाम् ।
रुदन्तीं शम्भुविरहादाससादाचलेश्वरः ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing his daughter excessively agitated, the lord of the mountains was sorry. The lord of the mountains approached her gently as she was crying due to her separation from Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
आसाद्य पाणिना तस्या मार्जयन्नयनद्वयम् ।
मा बिभीहि शिवेऽरोदीरित्युक्त्वा तां तदाग्रहीत् ॥ ९॥

क्रोडे कृत्वा सुतां शीघ्रं हिमवानचलेश्वरः ।
स्वमालयमथानिन्ये सान्त्वयन्नतिविह्वलाम् ॥ १०॥

English
Approaching her and wiping off her eyes with his hand he said—“Dear daughter, do not be afraid, do not cry. He took her on his lap and consoled her. The lord of the mountains took her immediately to his palace. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्हिते स्मरं दग्ध्वा हरे तद्विरहाच्छिवा ।
विकलाभूद् भृशं सा वै लेभे शर्म न कुत्रचित् ॥ ११॥

English
When Śiva had vanished after burning Kāma, Pārvatī became extremely agitated due to His separation. She did not attain pleasure anywhere. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुर्गृहं तदा गत्वा मिलित्वा मातरं शिवा ।
पुनर्जातं तदा मेने स्वात्मानं सा धरात्मजा ॥ १२॥

English
Returning to her father’s abode and meeting her mother, Śivā, the daughter of the mountain, considered herself born again. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
निनिन्द च स्वरूपं सा हा हतास्मीत्यथाब्रवीत् ।
सखीभिर्बोधिता चापि न बुबोध गिरीन्द्रजा ॥ १३॥

English
She cursed her own beauty. She said to herself. “O, I am doomed”. The daughter of the lord of mountains did not regain composure though consoled and assuaged by the maids. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपती च पिबन्ती च सा स्नाती गच्छती शिवा ।
तिष्ठन्ती च सखीमध्ये न किञ्चित्सुखमाप ह ॥ १४॥

English
She did not achieve happiness and peace in sleeping, drinking, bathing, or sitting amidst her maids. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
धिक्स्वरूपं मदीयं च तथा जन्म च कर्म च ।
इति ब्रुवन्ती सततं स्मरन्ती हरचेष्टितम् ॥ १५॥

English
Remembering the various gestures and movements of Śiva, she muttered to herself ever and anon—“Fie upon my beauty. Fie on my birth and activity”. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सा पार्वती शम्भुविरहोत्क्लिष्टमानसा ।
सुखं न लेभे किञ्चिद्वाब्रवीच्छिवशिवेति च ॥ १६॥

English
Thus Pārvatī was much distressed in mind due to separation from Śiva. She did not at all feel happy. She always muttered “Śiva, Śiva.” (16.)

---

Sanskrit
निवसन्ती पितुर्गेहे पिनाकिगतचेतना ।
शुशोचाथ शिवा तात मुमोह च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ १७॥

English
O dear, with her consciousness centred round the Pināka-bearing lord, she continued to stay in the palace of her father. Śiva bewailed much and fainted frequently. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलाधिराजोऽप्यथ मेनकापि
मैनाकमुख्यास्तनयाश्च सर्वे ।
तां सान्त्वयामासुरदीनसत्त्वा
हरं विसस्मार तथापि नो सा ॥ १८॥

English
The lord of the mountains, Menakā, and their sons chief of whom was Maināka of undisturbed mind, tried to console her but still she did not forget Śiva. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवमुने धीमन्हिमवत्प्रस्तरे तदा ।
नियोजितो बलभिदागमस्त्वं कामचारतः ॥ १९॥

English
O celestial sage, O intelligent one, employed by Indra, the slayer of Bala, you came to Himālaya mountain roaming here and there as you pleased. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वं पूजितस्तेन भूधरेण महात्मना ।
कुशलं पृष्टवांस्तं वै तदाविष्टो वरासने ॥ २०॥

English
You were then worshipped by the noble-souled mountain. You enquired of his health and happiness and you were seated in a noble seat. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रोवाच शैलेशः कन्याचरितमादितः ।
हरसेवान्वितं कामदहनं च हरेण ह ॥ २१॥

English
Then the lord of the mountains told you the story of his daughter from her service to Śiva to the burning of Kāma by Him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वावोचो मुने त्वं तु तं शैलेशं शिवं भज ।
तमामन्त्र्योदतिष्ठस्त्वं संस्मृत्य मनसा शिवम् ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, on hearing that, you told the lord of the mountains—“Worship Śiva.” You stood up, remembered Śiva mentally and took leave of him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तं समुत्सृज्य रहसि कालीं तामगमस्त्वरा ।
लोकोपकारको ज्ञानी त्वं मुने शिववल्लभः ॥ २३॥

English
O sage, leaving him you hastened to meet Pārvatī secretly, you a favourite of Śiva, perfectly wise and engaged in helping the world. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
आसाद्य कालीं सम्बोध्य तद्धिते स्थित आदरात् ।
अवोचस्त्वं वचस्तथ्यं सर्वेषां ज्ञानिनां वरः ॥ २४॥

English
Approaching Pārvatī and addressing her, you spoke to her respectfully. You are foremost among the wise and you were interested in her welfare. Your words were true. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
श‍ृणु कालि वचो मे हि सत्यं वच्मि दयारतः ।
सर्वथा ते हितकरं निर्विकारं सुकामदम् ॥ २५॥

English
*Nārada said: —*  O Pārvatī, listen. I am sympathetic to you. I shall speak truly. My words will be beneficent to you in all respects. They will lead to the achievement of your desire. They are free from aberrations. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सेवितश्च महादेवस्त्वयेह तपसा विना ।
गर्ववत्या यदध्वंसीद्दीनानुग्रहकारकः ॥ २६॥

English
The great god has been served by you without austerities. You had some pride which He, the blesser of the distressed, eradicated. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विरक्तश्च स ते स्वामी महायोगी महेश्वरः ।
विसृष्टवान्स्मरं दग्ध्वा त्वां शिवे भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २७॥

English
O Śivā, after burning Kāma, lord Śiva though favourably disposed to His devotees, left you, since the lord is a great Yogin and so unattached to you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वं सुतपोयुक्ता चिरमाराधयेश्वरम् ।
तपसा संस्कृतां रुद्रः स द्वितीयां करिष्यति ॥ २८॥

English
Hence you shall propitiate Him by performing a great penance. Śiva will take you as His wife, after you have been sanctified by austerities. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं चापि शङ्करं शम्भुं न त्यक्ष्यसि कदाचन ।
नान्यं पतिं हठाद्देवि ग्रहीष्यसि शिवादृते ॥ २९॥

English
You will never forsake the auspicious Śiva. O goddess, you will not take any one other than Śiva as your husband. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्यवचस्ते हि मुने सा भूधरात्मजा ।
किञ्चिदुच्छ्वसिता काली प्राह त्वां साञ्जलिर्मुदा ॥ ३०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing your words, O sage, Pārvatī, the daughter of the mountain, heaved a sigh of relief and gladly spoke to you with palms joined in reverence. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोवाच ।
त्वं तु सर्वज्ञ जगतामुपकारकर प्रभो ।
रुद्रस्याराधनार्थाय मन्त्रं देहि मुने हि मे ॥ ३१॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  O sage, O omniscient one, you help all the worlds, please tell me a formula for the propitiation of lord Śiva. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
न सिध्यति क्रिया कापि सर्वेषां सद्गुरुं विना ।
मया श्रुता पुरा सत्यं श्रुतिरेषा सनातनी ॥ ३२॥

English
No sacred rite will ever fructify without a good preceptor. Truly this eternal statement of the Vedas was heard by me before. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्याः पार्वत्या मुनिसत्तमः ।
पञ्चाक्षरं शम्भुमन्त्रं विधिपूर्वमुपादिशः ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Pārvatī, O excellent sage, you taught her the five-syllabled mantra of Śiva* in accordance with the sacred law. (33.)

[fn *. The five-syllabled Mantra of Śiva : नमः शिवाय]
---

Sanskrit
अवोचश्च वचस्तां त्वं श्रद्धामुत्पादयन्मुने ।
प्रभावं मन्त्रराजस्य तस्य सर्वाधिकं मुने ॥ ३४॥

English
O sage, generating her faith you told her the supreme efficacy of the great formula thus. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि मनोरस्य प्रभावं परमाद्भुतम् ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण शङ्करः सुप्रसीदति ॥ ३५॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O goddess, listen to the wonderful efficacy of this formula on hearing which Śiva becomes excessively pleased. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्रोऽयं सर्वमन्त्राणामधिराजश्च कामदः ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदोऽत्यन्तं शङ्करस्य महाप्रियः ॥ ३६॥

English
This formula is a king of all formulas. It yields all cherished desires, bestows all worldly pleasures and salvation, and appeals much to Śiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सुभगे येन जप्तेन विधिना सोऽचिराद् द्रुतम् ।
आराधितस्ते प्रत्यक्षो भविष्यति शिवो ध्रुवम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Repeating this formula in accordance with the injunctions you shall propitiate Śiva. He will certainly appear before you. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
चिन्तयन्ती च तद्रूपं नियमस्था शराक्षरम् ।
जप मन्त्रं शिवे त्वं हि सन्तुष्यति शिवो द्रुतम् ॥ ३८॥

English
O Śivā, meditate on His form, observing all restraints. Repeat the five-syllabled mantra. Śiva will be pleased quickly. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कुरु तपः साध्वि तपःसाध्यो महेश्वरः ।
तपस्येव फलं सर्वैः प्राप्यते नान्यथा क्वचित् ॥ ३९॥

English
O chaste lady, perform the penance thus. Lord Śiva can be attained through penance. Every one attains the desired fruits in penance and not otherwise. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा तदा कालीं नारद त्वं शिवप्रियः ।
यादृच्छिकोऽगमस्त्वं तु स्वर्गं देवहिते रतः ॥ ४०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada, after saying thus to Pārvatī, you, the favourite of Śiva, went to heaven, a casual visitor engaged in the welfare of the gods. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वती च तदा श्रुत्वा वचनं तव नारद ।
सुप्रसन्ना तदा प्राप पञ्चाक्षरमनूत्तमम् ॥ ४१॥

English
O Nārada, on hearing your words and securing the excellent five-syllabled mantra, Pārvatī was extremely pleased. (41.)
