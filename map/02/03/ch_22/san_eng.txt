### **CHAPTER TWENTYTWO - *(Description of Pārvatī's penance)***

---

Sanskrit
२२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
त्वयि देवमुने याते पार्वती हृष्टमानसा ।
तपःसाध्यं हरं मेने तपोऽर्थं मन आदधे ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After your departure, O celestial sage, convinced that Śiva could be achieved by means of penance, Pārvatī became glad and decided to perform penance. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सख्यौ समादाय जयां च विजयां तथा ।
मातरं पितरं चैव सखीभ्यां पर्यपृच्छत ॥ २॥

English
She took her maids Jayā and Vijayā into confidence and through them made her parents acquainted. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं पितरं गत्वा हिमवन्तं नगेश्वरम् ।
पर्यपृच्छत्सुप्रणम्य विनयेन समन्विता ॥ ३॥

English
First she wanted her father Himavat, the lord of mountains, to hear her humble words. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सख्यावूचतुः -
हिमवन् श्रूयतां पुत्रीवचनं कथ्यतेऽधुना ॥

सा स्वयं चैव देहस्य रूपस्यापि तथा पुनः । ४॥

भवतो हि कुलस्यास्य साफल्यं कर्तुमिच्छति ।
तपसा साधनीयोऽसौ नान्यथा दृश्यतां व्रजेत् ॥ ५॥

English
*The maids said :—*  O Himavat, let the words of your daughter be heard through us. She wishes to make her body, beauty and family fruitful. This can be achieved through penance and not otherwise. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्च पर्वतश्रेष्ठ देयाज्ञा भवताधुना ।
तपः करोतु गिरिजा वनं गत्वेति सादरम् ॥ ६॥

English
O excellent mountain, the permission may kindly be given—"Let Pārvatī go to the forest and perform penance there".

*Brahmā said :—* (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं च तदा पृष्टः सखीभ्यां मुनिसत्तम ।
पार्वत्या सुविचार्याथ गिरिराजोऽब्रवीदिदम् ॥ ७॥

English
O excellent sage, thus requested by Pārvatī through the maids, the lord of the mountains carefully considered the proposal and said: (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
मह्यं च रोचतेऽत्यर्थं मेनायै रुच्यतां पुनः ।
यथेदं भवितव्यं च किमतः परमुत्तमम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Himācala said :—*  This appeals to me. I it appeals to Menā as it ought to be, what else can be a better course ? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
साफल्यं तु मदीयस्य कुलस्य च न संशयः ।
मात्रे तु रुच्यते चेद्वै ततः शुभतरं नु किम् ॥ ९॥

English
There is no doubt about it that my House will be fortunate. If her mother also likes this, what can be more auspicious than this ? (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं पित्रा प्रोक्तं श्रुत्वा तु ते तदा ।
जग्मतुर्मातरं सख्यौ तदाज्ञप्ते तया सह ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  After hearing the words spoken by her father and (in a way) commanded by him, the two maids went to her mother along with her. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तु मातरं तस्याः पार्वत्यास्ते च नारद ।
सुप्रणम्य करौ बध्वोचतुर्वचनमादरात् ॥ ११॥

English
O Nārada, approaching Pārvatī's mother, they bowed down and with palms joined in reverence they respectfully spoke thus. (11.)

---

English
*The maids said: —*  O mother, O gentle lady, please listen to the words of your daughter. Obeisance be to you. You will listen with pleasure and act accordingly. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सख्यावूचतुः
मातस्त्वं वचनं पुत्र्याः श‍ृणु देवि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

सुप्रसन्नतया तद्वै श्रुत्वा कर्तुमिहार्हसि । १२॥

तप्तुकामा तु ते पुत्री शिवार्थं परमं तपः ।
प्राप्तानुज्ञा पितुश्चैव तुभ्यं च परिपृच्छति ॥ १३॥

English
For the sake of attaining Śiva, your daughter wishes to perform a severe penance. She has secured the permission of her father. She now wants to seek it from you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं स्वरूपसाफल्यं कर्तुकामा पतिव्रते ।
त्वदाज्ञा यदि जायेत तप्यते च तथा तपः ॥ १४॥

English
O chaste lady, she is desirous of making her beauty fruitful. If your permission too is received, the penance can be performed. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा च ततःसख्यौ तूष्णीमास्तां मुनीश्वर ।
नाङ्गीचकार मेना सा तद्वाक्यं खिन्नमानसा ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  O excellent sage, after saying this, the maids became silent. Distressed in mind, Menā did not accept the proposal. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सा पार्वती प्राह स्वयमेवाथ मातरम् ।
करौ बद्ध्वा विनीतात्मा स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ १६॥

English
Then Pārvatī herself spoke to her mother joining her palms in humility and remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
मातस्तप्तुं गमिष्यामि प्रातः प्राप्तुं महेश्वरम् ।
अनुजानीहि मां गन्तुं तपसेऽद्य तपोवनम् ॥ १७॥

English
*Pārvatī said: —*  O mother, I shall be going in the morning for performing penance to achieve Śiva. Please permit me to go to the penance-grove for the task. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः पुत्र्या मेना दुःखमुपागता ।
सोपाहूय तदा पुत्रीमुवाच विकला सती ॥ १८॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  On hearing the words of her daughter, Menā was distressed. That chaste lady called her daughter near and spoke to her in dejection. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
दुःखितासि शिवे पुत्री तपस्तप्तुं पुरा यदि ।
तपश्चर गृहेऽद्य त्वं न बहिर्गच्छ पार्वति ॥ १९॥

English
*Menā said :—*  O daughter Śivā, if you are distressed, if you wish to perform penance, you can do it at home. O Pārvatī, do not go out. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
कुत्र यासि तपः कर्तुं देवाः सन्ति गृहे मम ।
तीर्थानि च समस्तानि क्षेत्राणि विविधानि च ॥ २०॥

English
Where do you wish to go for performing penance ? All the deities are in my house. All the holy centres and the different temples too are here. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तव्यो न हठः पुत्रि गन्तव्यं न बहिः क्वचित् ।
साधितं किं त्वया पूर्वं पुनः किं साधयिष्यसि ॥ २१॥

English
Do not be stubborn, dear daughter. You shall not go out of your home. What did you achieve when you went out previously? What are you going to achieve at present ? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शरीरं कोमलं वत्से तपस्तु कठिनं महत् ।
एतस्मात्तु त्वया कार्यं तपोऽत्र न बहिर्व्रज ॥ २२॥

English
Dear child, slender is your body and hard is the penance. Hence you shall perform penance here. You shall not go out. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीणां तपोवनगतिर्न श्रुता कामनार्थिनी ।
तस्मात्त्वं पुत्रि मा कार्षीः तपोऽर्थं गमनं प्रति ॥ २३॥

English
For a woman to go to a penance-grove for the realisation of her desire is what we have never heard of before. Hence, dear daughter, do not go out for penance. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं बहुधा पुत्री तन्मात्रा विनिवारिता ।
संवदे न सुखं किञ्चिद्विनाराध्य महेश्वरम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus, in various ways, the daughter was dissuaded by her mother. But she did not find any pleasure except in propitiating Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तपोनिषिद्धा तपसे वनं गन्तुं च मेनया ।
हेतुना तेन सोमेति नाम प्राप शिवा तदा ॥ २५॥

English
Pārvatī acquired the name Umā since she was prevented from going to forest by Menā and forbidden to perform penance. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तां दुःखितां ज्ञात्वा मेना शैलप्रिया शिवाम् ।
निदेशं सा ददौ तस्याः पार्वत्यास्तपसे मुने ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, on realising that Pārvatī was quite dejected, Menā, the beloved of the mountain, permitted her to perform penance. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मातुराज्ञां च सम्प्राप्य सुव्रता मुनिसत्तम ।
ततः स्वान्ते सुखं लेभे पार्वती स्मृतशङ्करा ॥ २७॥

English
O excellent sage, on getting permission from her mother, Pārvatī remembered Śiva and felt happy. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मातरं पितरं साथ प्रणिपत्य मुदा शिवा ।
सखीभ्यां च शिवं स्मृत्वा तपस्तप्तुं समुद्गता ॥ २८॥

English
Bowing to her parents with joy, Pārvatī remembered Śiva and set out for performing penance along with her maids. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वा मतान्यनेकानि वस्त्राणि विविधानि च ।
वल्कलानि धृतान्याशु मौञ्जीं बद्ध्वा तु शोभनाम् ॥ २९॥

English
Discarding all the fine clothes of her taste, she wore tree-barks and the fine girdle of Muñja grass. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वा हारं तथा चर्म मृगस्य परमं धृतम् ।
जगाम तपसे तत्र गङ्गावतरणं प्रति ॥ ३०॥

English
She eschewed necklace and wore the pure deer skin. She arrived at Gaṅgāvataraṇa$^{26}$ for performing penance. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुना कुर्वता ध्यानं यत्र दग्धो मनोभवः ।
गङ्गावतरणो नाम प्रस्थो हिमवतःस च ॥ ३१॥

English
The Gaṅgāvataraṇa was in the Himālayan ridge where Kāma was burnt by Śiva who was performing meditation. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हरशून्योऽथ ददृशे स प्रस्थो हिमभूभृतः ।
काल्या तत्रेत्य भोस्तात पार्वत्या जगदम्बया ॥ ३२॥

English
Oh dear, that Himālayan ridge devoid of Śiva was painfully seen by Pārvatī, the mother of the universe, the daughter of the mountain. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र स्थित्वा पुरा शम्भुः तप्तवान्दुस्तरं तपः ।
तत्र क्षणं तु सा स्थित्वा बभूव विरहार्दिता ॥ ३३॥

English
She stood for a while in the place where formerly Śiva had performed penance and became dispirited by the pangs of separation. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
हा हरेति शिवा तत्र रुदन्ती सा गिरेः सुता ।
विललापातिदुःखार्ता चिन्ताशोकसमन्विता ॥ ३४॥

English
Crying aloud “Alas O Śiva” she, the daughter of the mountain, lamented sorrowfully and anxiously. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चिरेण सा मोहं धैर्यात्संस्तभ्य पार्वती ।
नियमायाभवत्तत्र दीक्षिता हिमवत्सुता ॥ ३५॥

English
Suppressing the delusion with fortitude after a long time Pārvatī, the daughter of Himavat, got herself initiated for the observance of ritualistic activities. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तपश्चकार सा तत्र श‍ृङ्गितीर्थे महोत्तमे ।
गौरीशिखर नामासीत्तत्तपःकरणाद्धि तत् ॥ ३६॥

English
She performed penance in the excellent holy centre Śṛṅgītirtha which (later) acquired the title “Gauri-Śikhara”$^{27}$ due to her performance of penance thereon. (36.)

[fn 27. It is one of the famous peaks of the Himālayas.]
---

Sanskrit
सुन्दराश्च द्रुमास्तत्र पवित्राश्शिवया मुने ।
आरोपिताः परीक्षार्थं तपसः फलभागिनः ॥ ३७॥

English
O sage, many beautiful holy plants were laid there by Pārvatī for testing the fruitfulness of her penance. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
भूभिशुद्धिं ततः कृत्वा वेदीं निर्माय सुन्दरी ।
तथा तपःसमारब्धं मुनीनामपि दुष्करम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Neatly cleaning the ground, the beautiful lady built the altar. Then the penance, difficult to be performed even by the sages, was begun. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
विगृह्य मनसा सर्वाणीन्द्रियाणि सहाशु सा ।
समुपस्थानिके तत्र चकार परमं तपः ॥ ३९॥

English
Suppressing her sense-organs with her mind, she started the great penance in a place within the proximity. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ग्रीष्मे च परितो वह्निं प्रज्वलन्तं दिवानिशम् ।
कृत्वा तस्थौ च तन्मध्ये सततं जपती मनुम् ॥ ४०॥

English
In the summer she kept a perpetually blazing fire all round and remaining within continued muttering the mantra. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सततं चैव वर्षासु स्थण्डिले सुस्थिरासना ।
शिलापृष्ठे च संसिक्ता बभूव जलधारया ॥ ४१॥

English
In the rainy season she continuously remained sitting on the bare ground on the rock and got herself drenched by the downpour of rain. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शीते जलान्तरे शश्वत्तस्थौ सा भक्तितत्परा ।
अनाहारातपत्तत्र नीहारेषु निशासु च ॥ ४२॥

English
During the winter, with great devotion she remained in water throughout. During snowfall and in the nights too she performed her penance observing fast. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं तपः प्रकुर्वाणा पञ्चाक्षरजपे रता ।
दध्यौ शिवं शिवा तत्र सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Performing such austerities and engrossed in the muttering of the five-syllabled mantra, Pārvatī meditated on Śiva, the bestower of fruits of our cherished desires. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वारोपितान् शुभान्वृक्षान्सखीभिस्सिञ्चती मुदा ।
प्रत्यहं सावकाशे सा तत्रातिथ्यमकल्पयत् ॥ ४४॥

English
Everyday during leisure time she used to water the trees planted by her along with her maids and extended acts of hospitality. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
वातश्चैव तथा शीतवृष्टिश्च विविधा तथा ।
दुस्सहोऽपि तथा घर्मस्तया सेहे सुचित्तया ॥ ४५॥

English
Chill gusts of wind, cool showers, and unbearable heat she bore with equanimity. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःखं च विविधं तत्र गणितं न तयागतम् ।
केवलं मन आधाय शिवे सासीत्स्थिता मुने ॥ ४६॥

English
Different sorts of worries she did not mind at all. O sage, fixing her mind in Śiva alone she remained firm and steady. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं फलभोगेन द्वितीयं पर्णभोजनैः ।
तपः प्रकुर्वती देवी क्रमान्निन्येऽमिताः समाः ॥ ४७॥

English
The first year she spent in taking fruits, the second in taking leaves, in the course of her penance. She spent many years thus. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पर्णान्यपि शिवा निरस्य हिमवत्सुता ।
निराहाराभवद्देवी तपश्चरणसंरता ॥ ४८॥

English
Then Śivā, the daughter of Himavat, eschewed even the leaves. She did not take any food. She was engrossed in the performance of penance. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
आहारे त्यक्तपर्णाभूद्यस्माद्धिमवतः सुता ।
तेन देवैरपर्णेति कथिता नामतः शिवा ॥ ४९॥

English
Since she, the daughter of Himavat, eschewed leaves from her diet she was called Aparṇā by the gods. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
एकपादस्थिता सासीच्छिवं संस्मृत्य पार्वती ।
पञ्चाक्षरं जपन्ती च मनुं तेपे तपो महत् ॥ ५०॥

English
Then Pārvatī performed great penance standing on one leg and remembering Śiva, she continued muttering the five-syllabled mantra. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
चीरवल्कलसंवीता जटासङ्घातधारिणी ।
शिवचिन्तनसंसक्ता जिगाय तपसा मुनीन् ॥ ५१॥

English
Clad in barks of trees, wearing matted hair and eager in the meditation of Śiva, she surpassed even sages by her penance. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं तस्यास्तपस्यन्त्या चिन्तयन्त्या महेश्वरम् ।
त्रीणि वर्षसहस्राणि जग्मुः काल्यास्तपोवने ॥ ५२॥

English
Pārvatī thus spent three thousand years in the penance-grove performing penance and meditating on lord Śiva. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
षष्टिवर्षसहस्राणि यत्र तेपे तपो हरः ।
तत्र क्षणमथोषित्वा चिन्तयामास सा शिवा ॥ ५३॥

English
Remaining for a short while in the place where Śiva had performed penance for sixty thousand years, Pārvatī thought like this. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
नियमस्थां महादेव किं मां जानासि नाधुना ।
येनाहं सुचिरं तेन नानुयाता तपोरता ॥ ५४॥

English
Does not the Supreme lord know me observing these ritualistic activities now? Wherefore am I not followed by him though engaged in penance? (54.)

---

Sanskrit
लोके वेदे च गिरिशो मुनिभिर्गीयते सदा ।
शङ्करः स हि सर्वज्ञः सर्वात्मा सर्वदर्शनः ॥ ५५॥

English
In the Śāstras and the Vedas, lord Śiva is always sung in praise by the sages as the bestower of welfare, omniscient, all-pervading and all-seer. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वभूतिप्रदो देवः सर्वभावानुभावनः ।
भक्ताभीष्टप्रदो नित्यं सर्वक्लेशनिवारणः ॥ ५६॥

English
The lord is the bestower of all riches, the moulder of fine emotions, the bestower of the desires of devotees and the remover of their distress. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वकामान्परित्यज्य यदि चाहं वृषध्वजे ।
अनुरक्ता तदा सोऽत्र सम्प्रसीदतु शङ्करः ॥ ५७॥

English
If I am devoted to the bull-bannered lord, discarding all desires, may He be pleased with me. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि नारदतन्त्रोक्तमन्त्रो जप्तः शराक्षरः ।
सुभक्त्या विधिना नित्यं सम्प्रसीदतु शङ्करः ॥ ५८॥

English
If the mantra of the Nārada Tantra, consisting of five syllables has been continuously repeated by me with great devotion may He be pleased with me. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि भक्त्या शिवस्याहं निर्विकारा यथोदितम् ।
सर्वेश्वरस्य चात्यन्तं सम्प्रसीदतु शङ्करः ॥ ५९॥

English
If I am a devotee without aberrations of Śiva, the lord of all, may He be extremely pleased with me. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं चिन्तयती नित्यं तेपे सा सुचिरं तपः ।
अधोमुखी निर्विकारा जटावल्कलधारिणी ॥ ६०॥

English
Pondering frequently like this incessantly, she performed penance for a long time, with her face turned downwards, her apparel of bark and mind without any aberrations. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा तया तपस्तप्तं मुनीनामपि दुष्करम् ।
स्मृत्वा च पुरुषास्तत्र परमं विस्मयं गताः ॥ ६१॥

English
She performed penance difficult to be performed even by the sages, so much so that people were struck with surprise. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्तपोदर्शनार्थं हि समाजग्मुश्च तेऽखिलाः ।
धन्यान्निजान्मन्यमाना जगदुश्चेति सम्मताः ॥ ६२॥

English
All of them came there to witness her penance. Considering themselves blessed, they proclaimed thus approvingly. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
महतां धर्मवृद्धेषु गमनं श्रेय उच्यते ।
प्रमाणं तपसो नास्ति मान्यो धर्मःसदा बुधैः ॥ ६३॥

English
"To follow the standard of the virtuous personages is declared to be conducive to greatness. There is no delimitation in penance. Virtue shall be honoured by the wise always. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा दृष्ट्वा तपोऽस्यास्तु किमन्यैः क्रियते तपः ।
अस्मात्तपोऽधिकं लोके न भूतं न भविष्यति ॥ ६४॥

English
After seeing or hearing about the penance of this lady what penance will be pursued by a man? A penance greater than this has never been before, nor will it ever be". (64.)

---

Sanskrit
जल्पन्त इति ते सर्वे सुप्रशस्य शिवातपः ।
जग्मुः स्वं धाम मुदिताः कठिनाङ्गाश्च ये ह्यपि ॥ ६५॥

English
Saying thus, they praised the penance of Pārvatī and joyously returned to their abodes. Even persons of sturdy countenance praised her penance. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यच्छृणु महर्षे त्वं प्रभावं तपसोऽधुना ।
पार्वत्या जगदम्बायाः पराश्चर्यकरं महत् ॥ ६६॥

English
O sage, listen to another surprising influence of the penance of Pārvatī, the mother of the universe. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाश्रमगता ये च स्वभावेन विरोधिनः ।
तेऽप्यासँस्तत्प्रभावेण विरोधरहितास्तदा ॥ ६७॥

English
Even the naturally inimical beings in and around her hermitage became free from animosity due to her power. (67.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहा गावश्च सततं रागादिदोषसंयुताः ।
तन्महिम्ना च ते तत्र नाबाधन्त परस्परम् ॥ ६८॥

English
Lions and cows prone to the passions of love, hatred etc. ceased to harass one another, thanks to her greatness. (68.)

---

Sanskrit
अथान्ये च मुनिश्रेष्ठ मार्जारा मूषकादयः ।
निसर्गाद्वैरिणो यत्र विक्रियन्ते स्म न क्वचित् ॥ ६९॥

English
O excellent sage, creatures like cats, mice etc. who are born enemies to one another did not exhibit any bad characteristics there. (69.)

---

Sanskrit
वृक्षाश्च सफलास्तत्र तृणानि विविधानि च ।
पुष्पाणि च विचित्राणि तत्रासन्मुनिसत्तम ॥ ७०॥

English
O excellent sage, trees bore fruits, grasses grew in plenty and flowers of variegated nature and colour blossomed there. (70.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वनं च तदा सर्वं कैलासेनोपमान्वितम् ।
जातं च तपसस्तस्याः सिद्धिरूपमभूत्तदा ॥ ७१॥

English
The entire forest became comparable to Kailāsa as it were the achievement of her penance. (71.)
