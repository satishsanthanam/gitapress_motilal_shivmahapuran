### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - *(Śiva consents to marry Pārvatī)***

---

Sanskrit
२४

देवा ऊचुः ।
नमो रुद्राय देवाय मदनान्तकराय च ॥

स्तुत्याय भूरिभासाय त्रिनेत्राय नमो नमः ॥ १॥

English
*The gods said :—*  Obeisance to lord Śiva, obeisance to the destroyer of Kāma; obeisance, obeisance to one worthy of eulogy; to one of profuse splendour, to the three-eyed lord. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिपिविष्टाय भीमाय भीमाक्षाय नमोनमः ।
महादेवाय प्रभवे त्रिविष्टपतये नमः ॥ २॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva clad in skin; obeisance to the terrible, to the terrible-eyed, to great lord and to the lord of the three worlds. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं नाथः सर्वलोकानां पिता माता त्वमीश्वरः ।
शम्भुरीशः शङ्करोऽसि दयालुस्त्वं विशेषतः ॥ ३॥

English
You are the lord of worlds; you are the father, the mother and the lord; you are Śiva the benefactor. You are particularly compassionate. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं धाता सर्वजगतां त्रातुमर्हसि नः प्रभो ।
त्वां विना कः समर्थोऽस्ति दुःखनाशे महेश्वर ॥ ४॥

English
You are the creator of all the worlds; O lord, you shall save us. O great God, except you who else is competent to destroy misery ? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां सुराणां नन्दिकेश्वरः ।
कृपया परया युक्तो विज्ञप्तुं शम्भुमारभत् ॥ ५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the gods, Nandikeśvara who was endowed with great sympathy began to inform Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
विष्ण्वादयः सुरगणा मुनिसिद्धसङ्घा-
स्त्वां द्रष्टुमेव सुरवर्य विशेषयन्ति ।
कार्यार्थिनोऽसुरवरैः परिभर्त्स्यमानाः
सम्यक् पराभवपदं परमं प्रपन्नाः ॥ ६॥

English
*Nandikeśvara said :—*  O foremost among the gods, Viṣṇu, other gods, the sages and Siddhas eulogise you in order to see you. They are being threatened by Asuras. Hence they seek some remedy and resort to thy feet, the seat of great fearlessness. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वया हि सर्वेश त्रातव्या मुनयः सुराः ।
दीनबन्धुर्विशेषेण त्वमुक्तो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ७॥

English
Hence, O lord of all, the sages and the gods shall be protected by you. You have been particularly mentioned as the kinsman of the distressed and favourably disposed towards your devotees. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं दयावता शम्भुर्विज्ञप्तो नन्दिना भृशम् ।
शनैः शनैरुपरमद्ध्यानादुन्मील्य चाक्षिणी ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Śiva, who was thus informed by Nandin and who was extremely sympathetic, slowly reverted from His meditation and opened His eyes. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशोऽथोपरतः शम्भुस्तदा परमकोविदः ।
समाधेः परमात्मासौ सुरान्सर्वानुवाच ह ॥ ९॥

English
Then lord Śiva, the highly efficient great Ātman, reverted from His trance and spoke to the gods. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरुवाच ।
कस्माद्यूयं समायाता मत्समीपं सुरेश्वराः ।
हरिब्रह्मादयः सर्वे ब्रूत कारणमाशु तत् ॥ १०॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  “O great gods, Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others, why have you come near me? Mention the reason for the same.” (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचः शम्भोः सर्वे देवा मुदान्विताः ।
विष्णोर्विलोकयामासुर्मुखं विज्ञप्तिहेतवे ॥ ११॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Śiva, all the gods rejoiced. They looked at Viṣṇu as if to induce him to speak. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुर्महाभक्तो देवानां हितकारकः ।
मदीरितमुवाचेदं सुरकार्यं महत्तमम् ॥ १२॥

English
Then Viṣṇu, the great devotee and benefactor of the gods mentioned the matter of great importance of the gods as mentioned by me (before). (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तारकेण कृतं शम्भो देवानां परमाद्भुतम् ।
कष्टात्कष्टतरं देवा विज्ञप्तुं सर्व आगताः ॥ १३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  “O Śiva, all the gods have come here to submit to you their misery perpetrated mysteriously by Tāraka. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
हे शम्भो तव पुत्रेणौरसेन हि भविष्यति ।
निहतस्तारको दैत्यो नान्यथा मम भाषितम् ॥ १४॥

विचार्येत्थं महादेव कृपां कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ।
देवान्समुद्धर स्वामिन् कष्टात्तारकनिर्मितात् ॥ १५॥

English
O Śiva, the demon Tāraka will be killed only by your self-begotten son and not otherwise. Ponder over what I have said and take pity on me. Obeisance, O great lord, to you. O lord, redeem the gods from the misery brought about by Tāraka. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वया गिरिजा देव शम्भो
ग्रहीतव्या पाणिना दक्षिणेन ।
पाणिग्रहेणैव महानुभावां
दत्तां गिरीन्द्रेण च तां कुरुष्व ॥ १६॥

English
Hence, O lord Śiva, Pārvatī shall be accepted by you and grasped with your right hand. Accept her hand as offered in marriage by the lord of mountains. She is full of noble attributes. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोस्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा प्रसन्नो ह्यब्रवीच्छिवः ।
दर्शयन् सद्गतिं तेषां सर्वेषां योगतत्परः ॥ १७॥

English
On hearing those words of Viṣṇu, Śiva was pleased and said indicating to them the goal of the good, eager in Yogic feats as He was. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
यदा मे स्वीकृता देवी गिरिजा सर्वसुन्दरी ।
तदा सर्वे सुरेन्द्राश्च मुनयो ऋषयस्तदा ॥ १८॥

सकामाश्च भविष्यन्ति न क्षमाश्च परे पथि ।
जीवयिष्यति दुर्गा सा पाणिग्रहणतः स्मरम् ॥ १९॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  “If goddess Pārvatī, the most beautiful lady were to be accepted by me, she will be able to resuscitate Kāma on account of the marriage. Then all the gods, sages and ascetics will become lusty and incompetent in the great path of Yoga. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
मदनो हि मया दग्धः सर्वेषां कार्यसिद्धये ।
ब्रह्मणो वचनाद्विष्णो नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ २०॥

English
Kāma was burnt by me for the achievement of universal goal. It was at the suggestion of Brahmā, O Viṣṇu. No anxiety need be felt in this connection. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विमृश्य मनसा कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।
सुधीः सर्वैश्च देवेन्द्र हठं नो कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ २१॥

English
O lord of gods, O intelligent one, it is your duty not to be obdurate after considering the situation of what shall be done and what not. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्धे कामे मया विष्णो सुरकार्यं महत् कृतम् ।
सर्वे तिष्ठन्तु निष्कामा मया सह सुनिश्चितम् ॥ २२॥

English
O Viṣṇu, a great favour to the gods has been done by me when Kāma was burnt. May ye all stay free from lust certainly along with me. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाहं च सुराः सर्वे तथा यूयमयत्नतः ।
तपः परमसंयुक्ताः करिष्यध्वं सुदुष्करम् ॥ २३॥

English
Just as I, so also you, O gods, can without effort perform difficult tasks being endowed with the energy of great penance. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यूयं समाधिना तेन मदनेन विना सुराः ।
परमानन्दसंयुक्ता निर्विकारा भवन्तु वै ॥ २४॥

English
Kāma not being with you, you can be endowed with the supreme bliss and be free from aberrations by means of spiritual contemplation, O Gods. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरावृत्तं स्मरकृतं विस्मृतं यद् विधे हरे ।
महेन्द्र मुनयो देवा यत्तत्सर्वं विमृश्यताम् ॥ २५॥

English
O Brahmā, O Viṣṇu, O Indra, O sages and O gods, what had been done by Kāma formerly and forgotten by you may be recollected and pondered over. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
महाधनुर्धरेणैव मदनेन हठात्सुराः ।
सर्वेषां ध्यानविध्वंसः कृतस्तेन पुरामराः ॥ २६॥

English
O gods, meditation of everyone had been spoiled by the stubborn Kāma, the great archer formerly. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कामो हि नरकायैव तस्मात् क्रोधोऽभिजायते ।
क्रोधाद्भवति सम्मोहो मोहाच्च भ्रंशते तपः ॥ २७॥

English
Kāma leads to hell; lust to anger, anger to delusion and delusion destroys penance. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
कामक्रोधौ परित्याज्यौ भवद्भिः सुरसत्तमैः ।
सर्वैरेव च मन्तव्यं मद्वाक्यं नान्यथा क्वचित् ॥ २८॥

English
Anger and lust shall be eschewed by you, the best of gods. My words shall be heeded by you all and not otherwise. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं विश्राव्य भगवान् महादेवो वृषध्वजः ।
सुरान् प्रवाचयामास विधिविष्णू तथा मुनीन् ॥ २९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying thus, the bull-bannered lord Śiva expressed the wish that Brahmā, Viṣṇu, the gods and the sages, should speak. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तूष्णीम्भूतोऽभवच्छम्भुर्ध्यानमाश्रित्य वै पुनः ।
आस्ते पुरा यथा स्थाणुर्गणैश्च परिवारितः ॥ ३०॥

English
Śiva became quiet after resorting to meditation again. Śiva, as before, was surrounded by His Gaṇas. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वात्मानमात्मना शम्भुरात्मन्येव व्यचिन्तयत् ।
निरञ्जनं निराभासं निर्विकारं निरामयम् ॥ ३१॥

परात्परतरं नित्यं निर्ममं निरवग्रहम् ।
शब्दातीतं निर्गुणं च ज्ञानगम्यं परात्परम् ॥ ३२॥

एवं स्वरूपं परमं चिन्तयन् ध्यानमास्थितः ।
परमानन्दसम्मग्नो बभूव बहुसूतिकृत् ॥ ३३॥

English
Śiva thought within Himself His own Soul, the form that is unsullied, free from distortions, aberrations and ailments, the form which is greater than the greatest, eternal, free from sense of possession, free from obsessions, beyond the ken of sounds and words, devoid of attributes and knowable through perfect wisdom. Thinking upon His own features thus in His meditation, the lord, the cause of great enjoyment and protection became engrossed in supreme bliss. (31-33.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यानस्थितं च सर्वेशं दृष्ट्वा सर्वे दिवौकसः ।
हरिशक्रादयः सर्वे नन्दिनं प्रोचुरानताः ॥ ३४॥

English
On seeing Śiva again engaged in meditation all the dwellers of heaven, Viṣṇu, Indra and others, humbly told Nandin. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
किं वयं करवामाद्य विरक्तो ध्यानमास्थितः ।
शम्भुस्त्वं शङ्करसखः सर्वज्ञः शुचिसेवकः ॥ ३५॥

English
*The gods said :—*  What shall we do now? Śiva has become detached and has gone on meditation. You are a companion of Śiva and pure assistant. You are omniscient. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

तदुपायं समाचक्ष्व वयं त्वच्छरणं गताः ॥ ३६॥

English
O chief of the Gaṇas, we seek refuge in you. Please guide us. What is the remedy by which Śiva can be propitiated? (36.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रत्युवाच सुरांस्तान्स नन्दी शम्भुप्रियो गणः ॥ ३७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage, thus urged by the gods, Viṣṇu and others, Nandin, the favourite Gaṇa of Śiva replied to the gods. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

श‍ृणुध्वं वचनं मे हि शिवसन्तोषकारकम् ॥ ३८॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—*  O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O Indra, O gods and O sages, pay heed to my words gratifying to Śiva. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

अतिदीनतया सर्वे सुनुतिं कुरुतादरात् ॥ ३९॥

English
If you persist still in your wish that Śiva shall marry, you shall eulogise with respect and piteous request. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O gods, great lord cannot be made subservient by ordinary devotion. The supreme lord does even what shall not be done when moved by extraordinary devotion. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other gods, then act accordingly, otherwise go the way you have come. Tarry not. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage, on hearing his words, Viṣṇu and other gods, considering that it must be so, eulogised Śiva with pleasure. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O great lord, lord of the gods, O ocean of mercy, lift us up from the great distress. Save us who have sought refuge in you. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Viṣṇu accompanied by me spoke out many piteous words, remembering Śiva with great devotion. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Śiva was thus eulogised by the gods, Viṣṇu and me. He, the great lord, ceased His meditation due to His attachment for His devotees. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The delighted Śiva said heightening the pleasure of Viṣṇu and other gods, after glancing at them with merciful looks. Śiva is favourably disposed to His devotees. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---
