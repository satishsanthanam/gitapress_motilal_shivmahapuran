### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - *(The seven celestial sages test Parvatī)***

---

Sanskrit
२५

नारद उवाच ।
गतेषु तेषु देवेषु विधिविष्ण्वादिकेषु च ।
सर्वेषु मुनिषु प्रीत्या किं बभूव ततः परम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  When Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other gods had gone along with the sages what happened thereafter? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कृतं शम्भुना तात वरं दातुं समागतः ।
कियत्कालेन च कथं तद्वद प्रीतिमावह ॥ २॥

English
O dear one, what did Śiva do? Within what time did He go to grant the boon? How? Please tell me for my pleasure. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
गतेषु तेषु देवेषु ब्रह्मादिषु निजाश्रमम् ।
तत्तपः सुपरीक्षार्थं समाधिस्थोऽभवद्भवः ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When Brahmā and other gods had gone back to their respective abodes, Śiva entered into spiritual contemplation in order to test her penance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वात्मानमात्मना कृत्वा स्वात्मन्येव व्यचिन्तयत् ।
परात्परतरं स्वस्थं निर्मायं निरवग्रहम् ॥ ४॥

English
He meditated upon His own soul within Himself, the Ātman that is greater than the greatest, free from illusion and obsessions and stationed within itself. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वस्तुभूतो भगवानीश्वरो वृषभध्वजः ।
अविज्ञातगतिःसूतिःस हरः परमेश्वरः ॥ ५॥

English
The bull-bannered lord Śiva, the object of the expression That,* whose movements are unknown, is the cause of enjoyment and protection. Śiva is the lord Supreme. (5.)

[fn *. It refers to the formula "that are you", identifying the soul with the supreme soul.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
गिरिजा हि तदा तात तताप परमं तपः ।
तपसा तेन रुद्रोऽपि परं विस्मयमागतः ॥ ६॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear then, Pārvatī was engaged in great penance. Even Śiva wondered at that. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
समाधेश्चलितस्सोऽभूद्भक्ताधीनोऽपि नान्यथा ।
वसिष्ठादीन्मुनीन्सप्त सस्मार सूतिकृद्धरः ॥ ७॥

English
He became distracted from spiritual contemplation. A deity subservient to His devotees cannot be otherwise. Śiva, the cause of great enjoyment and protection remembered the seven celestial sages, Vasiṣṭha and others. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तापि मुनयः शीघ्रमाययुः स्मृतिमात्रतः ।
प्रसन्नवदनाः सर्वे वर्णयन्तो विधिं बहु ॥ ८॥

English
Immediately on being remembered, the seven sages$^{36}$ came there with faces beaming with delight and praising their good fate. (8.)

[fn 36. The seven sages viz. Marīci, Atri Aṅgiras, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu and Vasiṣṭha are represented by a group of seven stars called Ursa Major.]
---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य तं महेशानं तुष्टुवुर्हर्षनिर्भराः ।
वाण्या गद्गदया बद्धकरा विनतकन्धराः ॥ ९॥

English
Bowing to Him with folded arms and bent shoulders they eulogised lord Śiva with extreme pleasure by means of words choked with devotional feelings. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तर्षय ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
जाता वयं सुधन्या हि त्वया यदधुना स्मृताः ॥ १०॥

English
*The seven sages said:—*  “O great God, lord of gods, O lord, O ocean of mercy, we have become very well blessed since we have been remembered by you now. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
किमर्थं संस्मृता नाथ शासनं देहि तद्धि नः ।
स्वदाससदृशीं स्वामिन्कृपां कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ११॥

English
Why have we been remembered? We may kindly be commanded. O lord, have pity on us as on your own slaves. Obeisance be to you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य मुनीनां तु विज्ञप्तिं करुणानिधिः ।
प्रोवाच विहसन्प्रीत्या प्रोत्फुल्लनयनाम्बुजः ॥ १२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of the sages, lord Śiva, the storehouse of sympathy spoke lovingly and laughingly with eyes beaming like full-blown lotus. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
हे सप्तमुनयस्ताताः श‍ृणुतारं वचो मम ।
अस्मद्धितकरा यूयं सर्वज्ञानविचक्षणाः ॥ १३॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—*  O dear seven celestial sages, listen to my words. You are all my benefactors. You are clever and perfectly wise. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तपश्चरति देवेशी पार्वती गिरिजाधुना ।
गौरीशिखरसंज्ञे हि पर्वते दृढमानसा ॥ १४॥

English
The great Goddess Pārvatī, the daughter of the mountain is performing a penance now in the mountain called Gauriśikhara, with a steady mind. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मां पतिं प्राप्तुकामा हि सा सखीसेविता द्विजाः ।
सर्वान्कामान्विहायान्यान्परं निश्चयमागता ॥ १५॥

English
O brahmins, she is desirous of attaining me as her husband. She is being served by her maids. She has discarded all other desires. She is determined in her resolve. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गच्छत यूयं मच्छासनान्मुनिसत्तमाः ।
परीक्षां दृढतायास्तत्कुरुत प्रेमचेतसः ॥ १६॥

English
O excellent sages, you go there at my bidding. With love in mind, conduct the test of her resolve. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा छलसंयुक्तं वचनीयं वचश्च वः ।
न संशयः प्रकर्तव्यः शासनान्मम सुव्रताः ॥ १७॥

English
O virtuous ones of good rites, at my bidding, you need not hesitate to employ even deceitful and critical remarks. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याज्ञप्ताश्च मुनयो जग्मुस्तत्र द्रुतं हि ते ।
यत्र राजति सा दीप्ता जगन्माता नगात्मजा ॥ १८॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Thus commanded, those sages quickly went there where the mother of universe, the brilliant daughter of the mountain, shone with refulgence. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र दृष्ट्वा शिवा साक्षात्तपःसिद्धिरिवापरा ।
मूर्ता परमतेजस्का विलसन्ती सुतेजसा ॥ १९॥

English
There she was seen as the personified achievement of penance itself. She was shining brilliantly with great splendour. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
हृदा प्रणम्य तां ते तु ऋषयस्सप्त सुव्रताः ।
सन्नता वचनं प्रोचुः पूजिताश्च विशेषतः ॥ २०॥

English
Mentally bowing to her, the seven sages, of good rites, humbly spoke to her after being warmly welcomed and worshipped by her. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
श‍ृणु शैलसुते देवि किमर्थं तप्यते तपः ।
इच्छसि त्वं सुरं कं च किं फलं तद्वदाधुना ॥ २१॥

English
The sages said :—  O daughter of the mountain, listen. Why do you perform this penance? Which god do you wish to propitiate? For what purpose? Please tell us now. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्ता सा शिवा देवी गिरीन्द्रतनया द्विजैः ।
प्रत्युवाच वचः सत्यं सुगूढमपि तत्पुरः ॥ २२॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Thus addressed by the brahmins, the goddess Śivā, daughter of the lord of mountains, replied truly before them though it was a great secret. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
मुनीश्वराःसंश‍ृणुत मद्वाक्यं प्रीतितो हृदा ।
ब्रवीमि स्वविचारं वै चिन्तितो यो धिया स्वया ॥ २३॥

English
Pārvatī said :—  O great sages, listen to my words with hearty affection. I am saying only what I thought in my own way. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
करिष्यथ प्रहासं मे श्रुत्वा वाचो ह्यसम्भवाः ।
सङ्कोचो वर्णनाद्विप्रा भवत्येव करोमि किम् ॥ २४॥

English
On hearing my words you will laugh at me considering my proposal impossible. O brahmins, I hesitate in revealing it but what can I do? (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं मनो हि सुदृढमवशं परकर्मकृत् ।
जलोपरि महाभित्तिं चिकीर्षति महोन्नताम् ॥ २५॥

English
This mind of mine is resolute helplessly attempting at a great task. Verily it is trying to erect a high wall on the surface of water. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरर्षेः शासनं प्राप्य करोमि सुदृढं तपः ।
रुद्रः पतिर्भवेन्मे हि विधायेति मनोरथम् ॥ २६॥

English
At the bidding of the celestial sage I am performing this steady penance with the desire that Rudra be my husband. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अपक्षो मन्मनः पक्षी व्योम्नि उड्डीयते हठात् ।
तदाशां शङ्करः स्वामी पिपर्त्तु करुणानिधिः ॥ २७॥

English
The unfledged birding of my mind flies up tenaciously. May lord Śiva, the storehouse of mercy fulfil its desire. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्या विहस्य मुनयश्च ते ।
सम्मान्य गिरिजां प्रीत्या प्रोचुश्छलवचो मृषा ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing her words, the sages honoured Pārvatī mentally with pleasure but spoke these deceptive false words laughingly. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
न ज्ञातं तस्य चरितं वृथापण्डितमानिनः ।
देवर्षेः क्रूरमनसः सुज्ञा भूत्वाप्यगात्मजे ॥ २९॥

English
*The sages said :—*  O daughter of the mountain, although you are wise and intelligent, you are not able to see through the conduct of that celestial sage who professes to be a great scholar but who is cruel-minded. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदः कूटवादी च परचित्तप्रमन्थकः ।
तस्य वार्ताश्रवणतो हानिर्भवति सर्वथा ॥ ३०॥

English
Nārada is a quibbler. He misleads others. If his words are paid heed to, you stand to lose in every respect. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र त्वं श‍ृणु सद् बुद्ध्या चेतिहासं सुशोभितम् ।
क्रमात्त्वां बोधयन्तो हि प्रीत्या तमुपधारय ॥ ३१॥

English
Now listen to a true anecdote that sheds light thereon, with keen intellect. We are enlightening you out of love and affection, take it to heart. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मपुत्रो हि यो दक्षः सुषुवे पितुराज्ञया ।
स्वपत्न्यामयुतं पुत्रानयुङ्क्त तपसि प्रियान् ॥ ३२॥

English
Dakṣa, the son of Brahmā, at the bidding of his father, begot ten thousand sons of his wife. He dearly loved them and employed them in performing a great penance. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सुताः पश्चिमदिशि नारायणसरो गताः ।
तपोऽर्थे ते प्रतिज्ञाय नारदस्तत्र वै ययौ ॥ ३३॥

English
The sons went to the holy lake Nārāyaṇasaras$^{37}$ in the western zone for performing penance. Nosing it out, Nārada too went there. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
कूटोपदेशमाश्राव्य तत्र तान्नारदो मुनिः ।
तदाज्ञया च ते सर्वे पितुर्न गृहमाययुः ॥ ३४॥

English
Sage Nārada misled them with his deceptive instructions. At his instance, they never went back home to their father. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा कुपितो दक्षः पित्राश्वासितमानसः ।
उत्पाद्य पुत्रान्प्रायुङ्क्त सहस्रप्रमितांस्ततः ॥ ३५॥

English
On hearing this, Dakṣa was infuriated but his father consoled him. Thereafter he begot a thousand other sons and engaged them in penance. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तेऽपि तत्र गताः पुत्राः तपोऽर्थं पितुराज्ञया ।
नारदोऽपि ययौ तत्र पुनस्तत्स्वोपदेशकृत् ॥ ३६॥

English
The sons too went to the same place for penance at the bidding of their father. Nārada too went there, a self-appointed instructor for them. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ददौ तदुपदेशं ते तेभ्यो भ्रातृपथं ययुः ।
आययुर्न पितुर्गेहं भिक्षुवृत्तिरताश्च ते ॥ ३७॥

English
The Nārāyaṇa lake has not been identified. Probably it is a lake of the Nārāyaṇa hill, near Badrinath in Garhwal. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं नारदसद्वृत्तिर्विश्रुता शैलकन्यके ।
अन्यां श‍ृणु हि तद्वृत्तिं वैराग्यकरणीं नृणाम् ॥ ३८॥

English
O daughter of mountain, the good conduct of Nārada is thus well-known. Now hear about another activity of his in making men detached. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्याधरश्चित्रकेतुर्यो बभूव पुराकरोत् ।
स्वोपदेशमयं दत्त्वा तस्मै शून्यं च तद्गृहम् ॥ ३९॥

English
There was a Vidyādhara named Citraketu. The sage instructed him and made him detached from his house. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रह्लादाय स्वोपदेशान्हिरण्यकशिपोः परम् ।
दत्त्वा दुःखं ददौ चायं परबुद्धिप्रभेदकः ॥ ४०॥

English
He bestowed his instructions on Prahlāda and made him suffer much at the hands of Hiranyakaśipu. He is definitely a person who splits others' intellect. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिना निजविद्या यच्छ्राविता कर्णरोचना ।
स स्वगेहं विहायाशु भिक्षां चरति प्रायशः ॥ ४१॥

English
Whomsoever this sage advocates his philosophy, very pleasing to the ears, generally the same person discards his hearth and home and begins to beg for alms. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदो मलिनात्मा हि सर्वदोज्ज्वलदेहवान् ।
जानीमस्तं विशेषेण वयं तत्सहवासिनः ॥ ४२॥

English
Nārada has a dirty soul though he is endowed with a white brilliant complexion for ever. We know him particularly because we are his associates. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
बकं साधुं वर्णयन्ति न मत्स्यानत्ति सर्वथा ।
सहवासी विजानीयाच्चरित्रं सहवासिनाम् ॥ ४३॥

English
People from a distance may describe a stork as a gentle bird that does not prey on fish. But in fact an associate knows the conduct of his associates. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
लब्ध्वा तदुपदेशं हि त्वमपि प्राज्ञसम्मता ।
वृथैव मूर्खीभूता त्वं तपश्चरसि दुष्करम् ॥ ४४॥

English
You too who are honoured by the wise have followed his advice and thus become a fool. That is why you are performing this severe penance. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
यदर्थमीदृशं बाले करोषि विपुलं तपः ।
सोदासीनो निर्विकारो मदनारिर्न संशयः ॥ ४५॥

English
O young lady, He, for whose sake you are performing this elaborate penance is a perpetually indifferent person of no emotional disturbance. Undoubtedly He is an enemy of Kāma. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशं हि वरं लब्ध्वा किं सुखं सम्भविष्यति ।
विचारं कुरु देवेशि त्वमेव गिरिजात्मजे ॥ ४६॥

English
The trident-bearing Śiva has an inauspicious body, is free from shame and has no home or pedigree. He is naked and ill-featured. He associates with ghosts and goblins and the like. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
स धूर्तस्तव विज्ञानं विनाश्य निजमायया ।
मोहयामास सद्युक्त्या कारयामास वै तपः ॥ ४७॥

English
That rogue of a sage has destroyed your discretion with his deception. He has deluded you with apparently good arguments and made you perform this penance. (47.)

---

English
O great Goddess, daughter of the mountain, you alone think within yourself how much pleasure could be derived by getting such a bridegroom. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं दक्षजां साध्वीं विवाह्य सुधिया सतीम् ।
निर्वाहं कृतवान्नैव मूढः किञ्चिद्दिनानि हि ॥ ४९॥

English
At first he married Sati, the chaste daughter of Dakṣa, eagerly but the fool that he was he could not maintain the household even for a few days. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तां तथैव स वै दोषं दत्त्वात्याक्षीत् स्वयं प्रभुः ।
ध्यायन्स्वरूपमकलमशोकमरमत्सुखी ॥ ५०॥

English
He accused her and forsook her Himself. The lord went on meditating on His own form, free from stains and sorrows and sported happily. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
एकलः परनिर्वाणो ह्यसङ्गोऽद्वय एव च ।
तेन नार्याः कथं देवि निर्वाहः सम्भविष्यति ॥ ५१॥

English
He is single without a second and without attachment. He is after salvation, O gentle lady, how can a woman put up with him. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि शासनं प्राप्य गृहमायाहि दुर्मतिम् ।
त्यजास्माकं महाभागे भविष्यति च शं तव ॥ ५२॥

English
O blessed one, even now, at our bidding, return to your house. Cast off this foolish intention. You will benefit thereby. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
