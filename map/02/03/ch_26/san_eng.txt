### **CHAPTER TWENTYSIX - *(Pārvatī-Jaṭīla dialogue)***

---

Sanskrit
२६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
गतेषु तेषु मुनिषु स्वं लोकं शङ्करः स्वयम् ।
परीक्षितुं तपो देव्या ऐच्छत्सूतिकरः प्रभुः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* When those sages returned to their abodes, lord Śiva, the cause of great enjoyment and protection wanted to test the penance of the goddess. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
परीक्षाछद्मना शम्भुर्द्रष्टुं तां तुष्टमानसः ।
जटिलं रूपमास्थाय स ययौ पार्वतीवनम् ॥ २॥

English
Under the pretext of testing, Śiva wanted to see her. With a delighted mind He assumed the form of a Jaṭīla (an ascetic with matted hair) and went to the forest of penance of Pārvatī. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीव स्थविरो विप्रदेहधारी स्वतेजसा ।
प्रज्वलन्मनसा हृष्टो दण्डी छत्री बभूव सः ॥ ३॥

English
He took the form of a very old man with the body of a brahmin. His brilliance shone. He was delighted in mind. He had an umbrella and a staff (to support Him). (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापश्यत्स्थितां देवीं सखीभिः परिवारिताम् ।
वेदिकोपरि शुद्धां तां शिवामिव विधोः कलाम् ॥ ४॥

English
There He saw the goddess surrounded by her maids on the platform, as pure as the digit of the moon. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुर्निरीक्ष्य तां देवीं ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपवान् ।
उपकण्ठं ययौ प्रीत्या तदासौ भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ५॥

English
Śiva, who is favourably disposed towards His devotees, approached her with pleasure in the guise of a celibate. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतं तं तदा दृष्ट्वा ब्राह्मणं तेजसाद्भुतम् ।
अपूजयच्छिवा देवी सर्वपूजोपहारकैः ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing that brahmin of wonderful refulgence come, goddess Pārvatī worshipped Him with all the articles of worship. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सुसत्कृतं संविधाभिः पूजितं परया मुदा ।
पार्वती कुशलं प्रीत्या पप्रच्छ द्विजमादरात् ॥ ७॥

English
She worshipped him with great joy by means of well prepared and arranged articles of worship. Thereafter Pārvatī enquired after the health of the brahmin with respect. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपेण कस्त्वं हि कुत आगतः ।
इदं वनं भासयसे वद वेदविदां वर ॥ ८॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  Who are you and whence have you come in the guise of a Brahmacārin? You are making this forest refulgent by your splendour. Please speak, O foremost among Vedic scholars. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्र उवाच ।
अहमिच्छाभिगामी च वृद्धो विप्रतनुः सुधीः ।
तपस्वी सुखदोऽन्येषामुपकारी न संशयः ॥ ९॥

English
*The brahmin said :—*  I am an aged brahmin roaming about as I please. I am an intelligent ascetic bestowing happiness and helping others. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
का त्वं कस्यासि तनया किमर्थं विजने वने ।
तपश्चरसि दुर्धर्षं मुनिभिः प्रपदैरपि ॥ १०॥

English
Who are you? What is your parentage? Why do you perform penance in this isolated forest? Your penance cannot be surpassed even by the sages of eminent status. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
न बाला न च वृद्धासि तरुणी भासि शोभना ।
कथं पतिं विना तीक्ष्णं तपश्चरसि वै वने ॥ ११॥

English
You are neither a small girl nor an old woman. You appear to be an auspicious young woman. How is it that you are performing this penance even when you are unmarried. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
किं त्वं तपस्विनी भद्रे कस्यचित्सहचारिणी ।
तपस्वी स न पुष्णाति देवि त्वां च गतोऽन्यतः ॥ १२॥

English
O gentle lady, are you the wife of an ascetic who does not provide you with food and shelter and so leaving you has gone to another place ? (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वद कस्य कुले जाता कः पिता तव काभिधा ।
महासौभाग्यरूपा त्वं वृथा तव तपोरतिः ॥ १३॥

English
Tell me, in which family are you born ? Who is your father ? What are your undertakings ? You are very fortunate. Futile is your interest in penance. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
किं त्वं वेदप्रसूर्लक्ष्मीः किं सुरूपा सरस्वती ।
एतासु मध्ये का वा त्वं नाहं तर्कितुमुत्सहे ॥ १४॥

English
Are you the mother of the Vedas ? Are you Lakṣmī or Sarasvatī ? I dare not guess who you are ? (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
नाहं वेदप्रसूर्विप्र न लक्ष्मीश्च सरस्वती ।
अहं हिमाचलसुता साम्प्रतं नाम पार्वती ॥ १५॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  O brahmin, I am not the mother of the Vedas, nor Lakṣmī nor Sarasvatī. I am the daughter of Himācala and my name is Pārvatī. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा दक्षसुता जाता सती नामान्यजन्मनि ।
योगेन त्यक्तदेहाहं यत्पित्रा निन्दितः पतिः ॥ १६॥

English
Previously I had been born as Satī, the daughter of Dakṣa. By Yogic means I cast off my body since my husband was insulted by my father. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र जन्मनि सम्प्राप्तः शिवोऽपि विधिवैभवात् ।
मां त्यक्त्वा भस्मसात्कृत्य मन्मथं स जगाम ह ॥ १७॥

English
Even in this life, Śiva came to me but due to ill luck, He reduced Kāma to ashes, left me and went away. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रयाते शङ्करे तापोद्विजिताहं पितुर्गृहात् ।
आगता तपसे विप्र सुदृढा स्वर्णदीतटे ॥ १८॥

English
O brahmin, when Śiva went away, I came out of my father's house, being greatly dejected, to perform this steady penance on the banks of the celestial river.$^{39}$ (18.)

[fn 39. The celestial Gaṅgā is said to flow from the toe of Viṣṇu and to have been brought down from heaven, by the prayers of Bhagiratha to purify the ashes of the sixty thousand sons of king Sagara who had been burnt by the angry glance of the saga Kapila.]
---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा तपः कठोरं च सुचिरं प्राणवल्लभम् ।
न प्राप्याग्नौ विविक्षन्ती त्वां दृष्ट्वा संस्थिता क्षणम् ॥ १९॥

English
Even after performing this severe penance for a long time, I could not attain Him. I was just to consign myself to fire but on seeing you, I have stopped for a while. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
गच्छ त्वं प्रविशाम्यग्नौ शिवेनाङ्गीकृता न हि ।
यत्र यत्र जनुर्लप्स्ये वरिष्यामि शिवं वरम् ॥ २०॥

English
Now you can go. I shall enter fire since I have not been accepted by Śiva. Wherever I take birth I shall woo only Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा पार्वती वह्नौ तत्पुरः प्रविवेश सा ।
निषिध्यमाना पुरतो ब्राह्मणेन पुनः पुनः ॥ २१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying so, Pārvatī jumped into the fire in the presence of the brahmin although she was forbidden by Him again and again. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
वह्निप्रवेशं कुर्वत्याः पार्वत्यास्तत्प्रभावतः ।
बभूव तत्क्षणं सद्यो वह्निश्चन्दनपङ्कवत् ॥ २२॥

English
Even as she jumped into the fire, it became as cool as sandal paste due to her ascetic power. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणं तदन्तरे स्थित्वा ह्युत्पतन्तीं दिवं द्विजः ।
पुनः पप्रच्छ सहसा विहसन्सुतनुं शिवः ॥ २३॥

English
The brahmin stopped her standing on her way as she was trying to go away and asked her laughingly. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विज उवाच ।
अहो तपस्ते किं भद्रे न बुद्धं किञ्चिदेव हि ।
न दग्धो वह्निना देहो न च प्राप्तं मनीषितम् ॥ २४॥

English
*The Brahmin said :—*  O gentle lady, I cannot understand anything. Your penance is wonderful. Your body is not charred by the fire. Still your desire remains unsatiated so far. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः सत्यं निकामं वै वद देवि मनोरथम् ।
ममाग्रे विप्रवर्यस्य सर्वानन्दप्रदस्य हि ॥ २५॥

English
O gentle lady, let me know about your desire; I am a brahmin who can bestow pleasure upon everyone. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाविधि त्वया देवि कीर्त्यतां सर्वथात्मना ।
तस्मान्मैत्री च सञ्जाता कार्यं गोप्यं त्वया न हि ॥ २६॥

English
Please tell me everything truly and methodically. Since we have become friends nothing should be kept a secret from me. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
किमिच्छसि वरं देवि प्रष्टुमिच्छाम्यतः परम् ।
त्वय्येव तदसौ देवि फलं सर्वं प्रदृश्यते ॥ २७॥

English
I wish to ask you now. O gentle lady, whom do you wish to have as your husband? It is in you that the fruit of penance is seen. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
परार्थे च तपश्चेद्वै तिष्ठेत्तु तप एव तत् ।
रत्नं हस्ते समादाय हित्वा काचस्तु सञ्चितः ॥ २८॥

English
If your penance is for others or for the supreme object, wherefore should you perform it at all? You had a jewel in your hand, you cast it off and have taken up a base metal instead. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशं तव सौन्दर्यं कथं व्यर्थीकृतं त्वया ।
हित्वा वस्त्राण्यनेकानि चर्मादि च धृतं त्वया ॥ २९॥

English
Why have you rendered your beauty in vain by taking recourse to this penance that eschewing different sorts of fine clothes hide is worn by you. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सर्वं कारणं ब्रूहि तपसस्त्वस्य सत्यतः ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा विप्रवर्योऽहं यथा हर्षमवाप्नुयाम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Hence tell me the reason, truthfully, for this penance. Let me, a great brahmin, be pleased on hearing the same. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति पृष्टा तदा तेन सखीं प्रैरयताम्बिका ।
तन्मुखेनैव तत्सर्वं कथयामास सुव्रता ॥ ३१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus asked by him, Pārvatī urged her maid. She of good rites made everything narrated to him through her maid. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तया च प्रेरिता तत्र पार्वत्या विजयाभिधा ।
प्राणप्रिया सुव्रतज्ञा सखी जटिलमब्रवीत् ॥ ३२॥

English
Induced by Pārvatī, her bosom friend Vijayā who knew all about her good rites spoke to the ascetic. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सख्युवाच ।
श‍ृणु साधो प्रवक्ष्यामि पार्वतीचरितं परम् ।
हेतुं च तपसस्सर्वं यदि त्वं श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ३३॥

English
*The maid said :—*  O saintly sir, listen. I shall recount the story of Pārvatī as well as the reason for penance, if you wish to hear. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सखी मे गिरिराजस्य सुतेयं हिमभूभृतः ।
ख्याता वै पार्वती नाम्ना तस्या मातास्ति मेनका ॥ ३४॥

English
This my friend is the daughter of Himācala, lord of mountains. She is the daughter of Menakā named Kālī but famous as Pārvatī. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ऊढेयं न च केनापि न वाञ्छति शिवात्परम् ।
त्रीणि वर्षसहस्राणि तपश्चरणसाधिनी ॥ ३५॥

English
She is not married to anyone nor does she desire any other than Śiva for her husband. She has performed this penance for three thousand years. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तदर्थं मेऽनया सख्या प्रारब्धं तप ईदृशम् ।
तदत्र कारणं वक्ष्ये श‍ृणु साधो द्विजोत्तम ॥ ३६॥

English
It was for that purpose that my friend started penance like this. I shall tell you the reason. Listen, O excellent brahmin, O saintly one. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वेन्द्रप्रमुखान्देवान् हरिं ब्रह्माणमेव च ।
पतिं पिनाकपाणिं वै प्राप्तुमिच्छति पार्वती ॥ ३७॥

English
Leaving off Indra and other gods, Viṣṇu and Brahmā, Pārvatī wishes to attain the Pināka-bearing Śiva as her husband. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं सखी मदीया वै वृक्षानारोपयत्पुरा ।
तेषु सर्वेषु सञ्जातं फलपुष्पादिकं द्विज ॥ ३८॥

English
She my friend planted many trees before. O brahmin, all of them have put forth flowers and fruits. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
रूपसार्थाय जनककुलालङ्करणाय च ।
समुद्दिश्य महेशानं कामस्यानुग्रहाय च ॥ ३९॥

मत्सखी नारददेशात्तपस्तपति दारुणम् ।
मनोरथं कुतस्तस्या न फलिष्यति तापस ॥ ४०॥

English
My friend has been performing severe penance at the bidding of Nārada to make her beauty fruitful, to embellish her father's race and to bless Kāma. She has directed this penance to lord Śiva. O holy ascetic, how is it that her desire is not fulfilled. (39-40.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्ते पृष्टं द्विजश्रेष्ठ मत्सख्या मनसीप्सितम् ।
मया ख्यातं च तत्प्रीत्या किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ४१॥

English
O excellent brahmin, you enquired of her desire. I have just told you out of my love for her. What else do you wish to hear. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा विजयाया यथार्थतः ।
मुने स जटिलो रुद्रो विहसन्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ४२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these truthful words of Vijayā, O sage, Śiva who came disguised as an ascetic said laughingly. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
जटिल उवाच ।
सख्येदं कथितं तत्र परिहासोऽनुमीयते ।
यथार्थं चेत्तदा देवी स्वमुखेनाभिभाषताम् ॥ ४३॥

English
*The ascetic said :—*  The maid has said something, but I deduce only a huge joke therefrom. If it be true, let the gentle lady herself speak out. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्ते च तदा तेन जटिलेन द्विजन्मना ।
उवाच पार्वती देवी स्वमुखेनैव तं द्विजम् ॥ ४४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When these words were uttered by that brahmin ascetic, goddess Pārvatī spoke to the brahmin thus. (44.)
