### **CHAPTER TWENTYSEVEN - (Description of the fraudulent words of the Brahmacārin)**

---

Sanskrit
२७

पार्वत्युवाच ।
श‍ृणु द्विजेन्द्र जटिल मद्वृत्तं निखिलं खलु ।
सख्युक्तं मेऽद्य यत्सत्यं तत्तथैव न चान्यथा ॥ १॥

English
Pārvatī said : —  O great brahmin, listen to my story entirely. What my friend has said just now is the whole truth, not otherwise. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसा वचसा साक्षात्कर्मणा पतिभावतः ।
सत्यं ब्रवीमि नोऽसत्यं वृतो वै शङ्करो मया ॥ २॥

English
I am telling you the truth and not a lie. Śiva has been wooed by me, by mind, speech and action as well as by means of ascetic feelings. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
जानामि दुर्लभं वस्तु कथम्प्राप्यं मया भवेत् ।
तथापि मन औत्सुक्यात्तप्यतेऽद्य तपो मया ॥ ३॥

English
I know that it is an inaccessible object. How can I attain it? Still out of my eagerness I am performing this penance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा वचनं तस्मै स्थिता सा गिरिजा तदा ।
उवाच ब्राह्मणस्तत्र तच्छ्रुत्वा पार्वतीवचः ॥ ४॥

English
Brahmā said :—  After saying these words to him, the daughter of the mountain kept quiet. On hearing the words of Pārvatī the brahmin said. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मण उवाच ।
एतावत्कालपर्यन्तं ममेच्छा महती ह्यभूत् ।
किं वस्तु काङ्क्षती देवी कुरुते सुमहत्तपः ॥ ५॥

English
The brahmin said :—  So long I had been desirous of knowing what our gentle lady craves for that she performs this great penance. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तज्ज्ञात्वा निखिलं देवि श्रुत्वा त्वन्मुखपङ्कजात् ।
इतो गच्छाम्यहं स्थानाद्यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ६॥

English
O dear lady, I have now known it through your own words. I am now going away from this place. You can do as you please. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
न कथ्यते त्वया मह्यं मित्रत्वं निष्फलं भवेत् ।
यथा कार्यं तथा भावि कथनीयं सुखेन च ॥ ७॥

English
What should be done by you is not mentioned by me. To me your further friendship is useless. But this should be mentioned that your future should be happy. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा वचनं तस्य यावद्गन्तुमियेष सः ।
तावच्च पार्वती देवी प्रणम्योवाच तं द्विजम् ॥ ८॥

English
Brahmā said :—  After saying these words to her when he proposed to go, goddess Pārvatī bowed to and spoke to the brahmin. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
किं गमिष्यसि विप्रेन्द्र स्थितो भव हितं वद ।
इत्युक्ते च तया तत्र स्थित्वोवाच स दण्डधृक् ॥ ९॥

English
Pārvatī said :—  "O excellent brahmin, why do you go? Stay and tender me wholesome advice”. When she said thus, the staff-bearing brahmin stopped and spoke. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विज उवाच ।
यदि श्रोतुमना देवि मां स्थापयसि भक्तितः ।
वदामि तत्त्वं तत्सर्वं येन ते वयुनं भवेत् ॥ १०॥

English
*The brahmin said :—*  If you are stopping me with devotion, truly desirous of hearing then I shall explain everything whereby you may gain some wisdom. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
जानाम्यहं महादेवं सर्वथा गुरुधर्मतः ।
प्रवदामि यथार्थं हि सावधानतया श‍ृणु ॥ ११॥

English
I know Śiva through and through with all His weighty attributes. I shall tell you the truth. Listen with attention. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषध्वजो महादेवो भस्मदिग्धो जटाधरः ।
व्याघ्रचर्माम्बरधरः संवीतो गजकृत्तिना ॥ १२॥

English
The great lord is bull-bannered. His body is smeared with ashes. His hair is matted. He is clad in the hide of a tiger. He has covered His body with the hide of an elephant. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालधारी सर्पौघैः सर्वगात्रेषु वेष्टितः ।
विषदिग्धोऽभक्ष्यभक्षो विरूपाक्षो विभीषणः ॥ १३॥

English
He holds the skull. Serpents twine round His limbs. Poison has left a mark on his neck. He eats even forbidden stuffs. He has odd eyes and is definitely awful. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अव्यक्तजन्मा सततं गृहभोगविवर्जितः ।
दिगम्बरो दशभुजो भूतप्रेतान्वितस्सदा ॥ १४॥

English
His birth and pedigree cannot be traced. He is devoid of the enjoyment of a householder. He has ten arms. He is mostly naked and is ever accompanied by ghosts and goblins. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
केन वा कारणेन त्वं तं भर्तारं समीहसे ।
क्व ज्ञानं ते गतं देवि तद्वदाद्य विचारतः ॥ १५॥

English
What is the reason whereby you wish Him to be your husband? O gentle lady, where has your wisdom gone? Think well and tell me. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वं श्रुतं मया चैव व्रतं तस्य भयङ्करम् ।
श‍ृणु ते निगदाम्यद्य यदि ते श्रवणे रुचिः ॥ १६॥

English
A previous terrible activity of His has been heard by me. If you are interested in hearing I shall tell you. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षस्य दुहिता साध्वी सती वृषभवाहनम् ।
वव्रे पतिं पुरा दैवात्तत्सम्भोगः परिश्रुतः ॥ १७॥

English
Dakṣa’s daughter, the chaste lady Sati wooed Vṛṣabhavāhana (Śiva) as her husband. Fortunately their union was well known. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालिजायेति सती दक्षेण परिवर्जिता ।
यज्ञे भागप्रदानाय शम्भुश्चापि विवर्जितः ॥ १८॥

English
Sati was discarded by Dakṣa because she was the wife of the skull-bearing Śiva. Śiva too was eschewed in the allocation of shares in the sacrifice. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सा तथैवापमानेन भृशं कोपाकुला सती ।
तत्याजासून्प्रियाँस्तत्र तया त्यक्तश्च शङ्करः ॥ १९॥

English
On account of the insult Sati was infuriated and she discarded her dear life. Śiva too was abandoned by her. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं स्त्रीरत्नं तव पिता राजा निखिलभूभृताम् ।
तथाविधं पतिं कस्मादुग्रेण तपसेहसे ॥ २०॥

English
You are a jewel among women. Your father is the king of all mountains. Why do you crave for a husband like this and that too by means of a severe penance? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दत्त्वा सुवर्णमुद्रां च ग्रहीतुं काचमिच्छसि ।
हित्वा च चन्दनं शुभ्रं कर्दमं लेप्तुमिच्छसि ॥ २१॥

English
Handing over a gold coin you wish to buy a piece of glass. Setting aside the pure sandal paste you wish to smear mud over your body. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यतेजः परित्यज्य खद्योतद्युतिमिच्छसि ।
चीनांशुकं विहायैव चर्माम्बरमिहेच्छसि ॥ २२॥

English
Unmindful of the sunlight you wish to have the light of the glow worm. Throwing away the fine China$^{50}$ silk you wish to wear the hide. (22.)

[fn 50. The Chinese silken cloth is mentioned also by Kālidāsa in the Śākuntala 1. 34.]

---

Sanskrit
गृहवासं परित्यज्य वनवासं समीहसे ।
लोहमिच्छसि देवेशि त्यक्त्वा शेवधिमुत्तमम् ॥ २३॥

English
Discarding the life at home you yearn for a life in the forest, O madam, throwing away excellent treasure you wish a piece of iron in return. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रादिलोकपालांश्च हित्वा शिवमनुव्रता ।
नैतत्सूक्तं हि लोकेषु विरुद्धं दृश्यतेऽधुना ॥ २४॥

English
Leaving off the guardians of the quarters you run after Śiva. This is not well said It is against the conventions of the world. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व त्वं कमलपत्राक्षी क्वासौ वै त्रिविलोचनः ।
शशाङ्कवदना त्वं च पञ्चवक्त्रः शिवः स्मृतः ॥ २५॥

English
Where you with eyes like the petals of a lotus ? Where this three-eyed creature—Śiva ? You are moon-faced while Śiva is five-faced.* (25.)

[fn *: On the five-faced feature of Śiva see Note 25 P. 46]]
---

Sanskrit
वेणी शिरसि ते दिव्या सर्पिणीव विभासिता ।
जटाजूटं शिवस्यैव प्रसिद्धं परिचक्षते ॥ २६॥

English
On your head the divine plaited hair shines with glossy splendour like a serpent. But Śiva has only the matted hair to boast of. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्दनं च त्वदीयाङ्गे चिताभस्म शिवस्य च ।
क्व दुकूलं त्वदीयं वै शाङ्करं क्व गजाजिनम् ॥ २७॥

English
Sandal paste is applied on your body, while the ashes of the funeral pyre on that of Śiva. Where your silken garment and where the elephant-hide of Śiva. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व भूषणानि दिव्यानि क्व सर्पाः शङ्करस्य च ।
क्व चरा देवताःसर्वाः क्व च भूतबलिप्रियः ॥ २८॥

English
Where the divine ornaments and where the serpents of Śiva ? Where the deities that move about and where Śiva, fond of goblins and their oblations ? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व वा मृदङ्गवादश्च क्व च तड्डमरुस्तथा ।
क्व च भेरीकलापश्च क्व च श‍ृङ्गरवोऽशुभः ॥ २९॥

English
Where the pleasing sound of his tabor ? Where His peculiar drum called Damaru ? Where the set of fine drums and the inauspicious sound of his horn ? (29.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व च ढक्कामयः शब्दो गलनादः क्व चाशुभः ।
भवत्याश्च शिवस्यैव न युक्तं रूपमुत्तमम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Where the inauspicious sound of double drum and where the sound of his throat ? There is no matching beauty between you both. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि द्रव्यं भवेत्तस्य कथं स्यात्स दिगम्बरः ।
वाहनं च बलीवर्दः सामग्री कापि तस्य न ॥ ३१॥

English
If He had money to spare how could He have been a naked being ? His vehicle is a bull. He has no other appendages. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
वरेषु ये गुणाः प्रोक्ता नारीणां सुखदायकाः ।
तन्मध्ये हि विरूपाक्षे एकोऽपि न गुणः स्मृतः ॥ ३२॥

English
There is not even a single quality in the odd-eyed Śiva out of the innumerable qualities pleasing to women and expected in bride-grooms. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
तवापि कामो दयितो दग्धस्तेन हरेण च ।
अनादरस्तदा दृष्टो हित्वा त्वामन्यतो गतः ॥ ३३॥

English
Your friend Kāma was burnt by Śiva. He insulted you also by leaving you off and going elsewhere. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
जातिर्न दृश्यते तस्य विद्याज्ञानं तथैव च ।
सहायाश्च पिशाचा हि विषं कण्ठे हि दृश्यते ॥ ३४॥

English
His caste is not recognised. He has no learning or wisdom. His assistants are the ghosts. Poison is seen even in His throat. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाकी च सदा नित्यं विरागी च विशेषतः ।
तस्मात्त्वं हि हरेणैव मनो योक्तुं तु चार्हसि ॥ ३५॥

English
He also moves about in isolation. He is detached from everything particularly. Hence you cannot fix your mind in Him. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व च हारस्त्वदीयो वै क्व च तन्मुण्डमालिका ।
अङ्गरागः क्व ते दिव्यः चिताभस्म क्व तत्तनौ ॥ ३६॥

English
Where your necklace and where the garland of skulls that he wears? Where your rich divine unguent and where the ash from the funeral pyre that He has on His body? (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं विरुद्धं रूपादि तव देवि हरस्य च ।
मह्यं न रोचते ह्येतद्यदिच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ३७॥

English
O divine lady, everything concerning you and Śiva, such as form, features etc. is mutually discordant. I do not like your resolution. You can do whatever you please. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
असद्वस्तु च यत्किञ्चित् तत्सर्वं स्वयमीहसे ।
निवर्तय मनस्तस्मान्नो चेदिच्छसि तत्कुरु ॥ ३८॥

English
You yourself have evolved taste for all bad objects. Turn your mind from Him. If not, do whatever you please. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा तस्य विप्रस्य पार्वती ।
उवाच क्रुद्धमनसा शिवनिन्दापरं द्विजम् ॥ ३९॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  On hearing these words of that brahmin, Pārvatī said angrily to the brahmin who discredited Śiva. (39.)
