### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - *(Pārvatī sees Śiva's real form)***

---

Sanskrit
२८

पार्वत्युवाच ।
एतावद्धि मया ज्ञातं कश्चिदन्योऽयमागतः ।
इदानीं सकलं ज्ञातमवध्यस्त्वं विशेषतः ॥ १॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  So long I have been thinking that some one else has come. Now everything has become clear. You are a person who cannot be killed. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयोक्तं विदितं देव तदलीकं न चान्यथा ।
यदि त्वयोदितं स्याद्वै विरुद्धं नोच्यते त्वया ॥ २॥

English
O lord, what has been said by you is known. It is not otherwise. If what has been said by you is real, it cannot be called unreal. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिद्दृश्यते तादृक् वेषधारी महेश्वरः ।
स्वलीलया परब्रह्म स्वरागोपात्तविग्रहः ॥ ३॥

English
Sometimes lord Śiva is seen in that guise. But He is the supreme Brahman who, out of his own accord, takes up bodies in his own sports. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपेण प्रतारयितुमुद्यतः ।
आगतश्छलसंयुक्तं वचोऽवादीः कुयुक्तितः ॥ ४॥

English
You have now come in the form of a student ascetic for the sake of deceiving me. Using false arguments, you have spoken fraudulent words. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करस्य स्वरूपं तु जानामि सुविशेषतः ।
शिवतत्त्वमतो वच्मि सुविचार्य यथार्हतः ॥ ५॥

English
I know the real form of Śiva very particularly. I shall therefore explain Śiva's reality in the proper perspective after careful consideration. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्तुतो निर्गुणो ब्रह्म सगुणः कारणेन सः ।
कुतो जातिर्भवेत्तस्य निर्गुणस्य गुणात्मनः ॥ ६॥

English
He is in fact devoid of attributes. But for some reasons He takes up attributes. How can He have a birth, he who is really attributeless but takes up attributes? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स सर्वासां हि विद्यानामधिष्ठानं सदाशिवः ।
किं तस्य विद्यया कार्यं पूर्णस्य परमात्मनः ॥ ७॥

English
Sadāśiva is the support and receptacle of all lores. Of what avail is learning to Him who is perfect and the supreme soul? (7.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदा उच्छ्वासरूपेण पुरा दत्ताश्च विष्णवे ।
शम्भुना तेन कल्पादौ तत्समः कोऽस्ति सुप्रभुः ॥ ८॥

English
At the beginning of the Kalpa, all the Vedas were given by Śiva to Viṣṇu in the form of breath. Who can be a good lord equal to Him? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषामादिभूतस्य वयोमानं कुतस्ततः ।
प्रकृतिस्तु ततो जाता किं शक्तेस्तस्य कारणम् ॥ ९॥

English
How can He be measured in age, He who is primordial to everything and everyone. Even primordial nature (Prakṛti) is born of Him. Of what avail is then Energy to Him? (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ये भजन्ति च तं प्रीत्या शक्तीशं शङ्करं सदा ।
तस्मै शक्तित्रयं शम्भुः स ददाति सदाव्ययम् ॥ १०॥

English
With the threefold Energies,⁴¹ Śiva blesses those who worship Him always as the lord of Energies. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यैव भजनाज्जीवो मृत्युं जयति निर्भयः ।
तस्मान्मृत्युञ्जयन्नाम प्रसिद्धं भुवनत्रये ॥ ११॥

English
Every individual soul becomes fearless and conquers death by worshipping Him. Hence His designation 'the conqueror of death' is famous in all the three worlds. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यैव पक्षपातेन विष्णुर्विष्णुत्वमाप्नुयात् ।
ब्रह्मत्वं च यथा ब्रह्मा देवा देवत्वमेव च ॥ १२॥

English
Viṣṇu attains and retains his Viṣṇuhood by His favour. Similarly Brahmā his Brahmāhood and the gods their godhood.

13-14 Whenever the lord of the gods wants to see Śiva he has to propitiate His gate-keepers, the ghosts etc., otherwise his crown becomes shattered by batons. Really Śiva is a great lord. He has no need for many attendants. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्याणरूपिणस्तस्य सेवयेह न किं भवेत् ।
किं न्यूनं तस्य देवस्य मामिच्छति सदाशिवः ॥ १५॥

English
What is it that cannot befall one who serves the auspicious-featured Śiva. What is deficient in that lord? Does Sadāśiva like me? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तजन्मदरिद्रः स्यात्सेवेद्यो यदि शङ्करम् ।
तस्यैतत्सेवनाल्लोके लक्ष्मीः स्यादनपायिनी ॥ १६॥

English
Even if a person is perpetually poor for seven lives, after serving Śiva, his prosperity becomes unhampered. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यदग्रे सिद्धयोऽष्टौ च नित्यं नृत्यन्ति तोषितुम् ।
अवाङ्मुखाः सदा तत्र तद्धितं दुर्लभं कुतः ॥ १७॥

English
How can he find benefit inaccessible-he in whose presence the eight Siddhis⁴² (achievements) dance always for the sake of propitiation with speechless mouths or with lowered faces. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यस्य मङ्गलानीह सेवते शङ्करस्य न ।
यथापि मङ्गलं तस्य स्मरणादेव जायते ॥ १८॥

English
Although Śiva resorts to inauspicious things yet by thinking on Him everything becomes auspicious. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य पूजाप्रभावेण कामाः सिद्ध्यन्ति सर्वशः ।
कुतो विकारस्तस्यास्ति निर्विकारस्य सर्वदा ॥ १९॥

English
His worship fulfils all desires. How can there be aberration in Him who always remains in an unmodified state. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेति मङ्गलं नाम मुखे यस्य निरन्तरम् ।
तस्यैव दर्शनादन्ये पवित्राः सन्ति सर्वदा ॥ २०॥

English
People are sanctified by merely seeing the person in whose mouth the auspicious name “Śiva” is ever present. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यपूतं भवेद्भस्म चितायाश्च त्वयोदितम् ।
नित्यमस्याङ्गगं देवैः शिरोभिर्धार्यते कथम् ॥ २१॥

English
If, as you say, the ash from the funeral pyre is unholy, how is it that the same transferred to Śiva’s body is taken thence and worn on the head by the gods? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यो देवो जगतां कर्ता भर्ता हर्ता गुणान्वितः ।
निर्गुणः शिवसंज्ञश्च स विज्ञेयः कथं भवेत् ॥ २२॥

English
How can He be easily realised, He who is the deity that creates, sustains and annihilates the worlds, all the same who is devoid of attributes and is termed Śiva? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अगुणं ब्रह्मणो रूपं शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
तत्कथं हि विजानन्ति त्वादृशास्तद्बहिर्मुखाः ॥ २३॥

English
The form of Śiva, the supreme soul, is that of Brahman, devoid of attributes. How can people like you know it, people with extrovert faces? (23.)

---

Sanskrit
दुराचाराश्च पापाश्च वेभ्यस्ते विनिर्गताः ।
तत्त्वं ते नैव जानन्ति शिवस्यागुणरूपिणः ॥ २४॥

English
Persons of evil conduct, sinners and those who have gone astray from the path of the gods do not at all know the reality of Śiva of attributeless form. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनिन्दां करोतीह तत्त्वमज्ञाय यः पुमान् ।
आजन्मसञ्चितं पुण्यं भस्मीभवति तस्य तत् ॥ २५॥

English
If, out of ignorance of His reality, any one were to discredit Śiva, his merit hoarded ever since birth becomes reduced to ashes. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वया निन्दा कृता यात्र हरस्यामिततेजसः ।
त्वत्पूजा च कृता यन्मे तस्मात्पापं भजाम्यहम् ॥ २६॥

English
You have censured Śiva of immeasurable splendour and I have worshiped you, hence I have become sinful. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवविद्वेषिणं दृष्ट्वा सचैलं स्नानमाचरेत् ।
शिवविद्वेषिणं दृष्ट्वा प्रायश्चितं समाचरेत् ॥ २७॥

English
On seeing a person who hates Śiva one should take bath along with one’s clothes. On seeing a person who hates Śiva one should perform expiatory rites. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
रे रे दुष्ट त्वया चोक्तमहं जानामि शङ्करम् ।
निश्चयेन न विज्ञातश्शिव एव सनातनः ॥ २८॥

English
O wicked one, you profess knowledge of Śiva. But you should know that the eternal Śiva is not known at all. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा तथा भवेद्रुद्रो यथा वा बहुरूपवान् ।
ममाभीष्टतमो नित्यं निर्विकारी सतां प्रियः ॥ २९॥

English
Whatever may be the form or feature of Śiva, He is multiformed. Still He is my favourite. He is without aberration and beloved of the good. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्ब्रह्मापि न समः तस्य क्वापि महात्मनः ।
कुतोऽन्ये निर्जराद्याश्च कालाधीनाः सदैव ते ॥ ३०॥

English
Neither Viṣṇu nor Brahmā can equal that noble soul. How can then the gods and others be? They are always dependent on Time and are not eternal. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इति बुद्ध्या समालोक्य स्वया सत्या सुतत्त्वतः ।
शिवार्थं वनमागत्य करोमि विपुलं तपः ॥ ३१॥

English
After realising this with my sharp intellect factually, I have come to this forest and am performing the elaborate penance for attaining Śiva. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव परमेशानः सर्वेशो भक्तवत्सलः ।
सम्प्राप्तुं मेऽभिलाषो हि दीनानुग्रहकारकम् ॥ ३२॥

English
My ambition is to attain the supreme lord who is favourably disposed to His devotees and who blesses the distressed. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा गिरिजा सा हि गिरीश्वरसुता मुने ।
विरराम शिवं दध्यौ निर्विकारेण चेतसा ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying this, O sage, Pārvatī, the daughter of the mountain, stopped and meditated on Śiva with unaffected mind. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य वचो देव्या ब्रह्मचारी स वै द्विजः ।
पुनर्वचनमाख्यातुं यावदेव प्रचक्रमे ॥ ३४॥

उवाच गिरिजा तावत्स्वसखीं विजयां द्रुतम् ।
शिवसक्तमनोवृत्तिः शिवनिन्दापराङ्मुखी ॥ ३५॥

English
On hearing the words of the goddess when the brahmin ascetic began to say something, Pārvatī whose mind was fixed on Śiva and who was averse to hear any disparaging remarks about Śiva spoke immediately to Vijayā, her maid. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिजोवाच ।
वारणीयः प्रयत्नेन सख्ययं हि द्विजाधमः ।
पुनर्वक्तुमनाश्चैव शिवनिन्दां करिष्यति ॥ ३६॥

English
*Pārvatī said:—*  This base brahmin must be prevented strenuously. He is inclined to say something again. He will surely censure Śiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
न केवलं भवेत्पापं निन्दां कर्तुश्शिवस्य हि ।
यो वै श‍ृणोति तन्निन्दां पापभाक् स भवेदिह ॥ ३७॥

English
Not only does he who disparages Śiva incur sin but also he who hears the same.⁴³ (37.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनिन्दाकरो वध्यः सर्वथा शिवकिङ्करैः ।
ब्राह्मणश्चेत्स वै त्याज्यो गन्तव्यं तत्स्थलाद्द्रुतम् ॥ ३८॥

English
A person who disparages Śiva is definitely worthy of being killed by Śiva’s attendants. If it is a brahmin he must be dismissed or the hearer shall go away from that place immediately. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं दुष्टः पुनर्निन्दां करिष्यति शिवस्य हि ।
ब्राह्मणत्वादवध्यश्चेत्त्याज्योऽदृश्यश्च सर्वथा ॥ ३९॥

English
This wicked man will again disparage Śiva. Since he is a brahmin, he is not to be killed. He shall be abandoned. He shall not be seen at all. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वैतत्स्थलमद्यैव यास्यामोऽन्यत्र मा चिरम् ।
अथ सम्भाषणं न स्यादनेनाऽविदुषा पुनः ॥ ४०॥

English
Let us leave this place at once and go elsewhere. Do not tarry. Let there be no more talk with this ignorant man. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा चोमया यावत्पादमुत्क्षिप्यते मुने ।
असौ तावच्छिवः साक्षादालम्बे प्रियया स्वयम् ॥ ४१॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Saying this, O sage, even as Pārvatī was about to step ahead, the brahmin manifesting as Śiva clasped his beloved. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा स्वरूपं सुभगं शिवाध्यानं यथा तथा ।
दर्शयित्वा शिवायै तामुवाचावाङ्मुखीं शिवः ॥ ४२॥

English
After assuming the handsome form in the manner Pārvatī had meditated upon and manifesting it to Pārvatī, Śiva addressed her while she stood with her lowered head. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
कुत्र यास्यसि मां हित्वा न त्वं त्याज्या मया पुनः ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूहि नादेयं विद्यते तव ॥ ४३॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  “Where will you go, leaving me? You are not to be discarded again by me. I am delighted. Tell me what boon shall I grant you. There is nothing that cannot be given to you. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
From today I am your slave bought by you by performing penance.⁴⁴ I have been bought by your beauty. Even a moment appears like a Yuga. (44.)

---

English
Even in the three worlds I do not see a beloved like you. O Pārvatī, in every respect I am subservient to you. You can fulfil all your desires. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English

English
*Brahmā said :—*  When the lord of the gods spoke in this way, Pārvatī rejoiced. Whatever distress she had felt during penance she cast off as something old. (49.)

Sanskrit

O excellent sage, her weariness subsided. In fact, when the fruit is realised, the exertion felt during the process of undertaking perishes. (50.)
