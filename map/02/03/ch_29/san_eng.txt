### **CHAPTER TWENTYNINE - (*Śivā-Śiva dialogue*)**

---

Sanskrit
२९

नारद उवाच ।
ब्रह्मन् विधे महाभाग किं जातं तदनन्तरम् ।
तत्सर्वं श्रोतुमिच्छामि कथय त्वं शिवायशः ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, O fortunate one, what happened thereafter? I wish to hear everything. Please narrate the glory of Pārvatī to me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
देवर्षे श्रूयतां सम्यक् कथयामि कथां मुदा ।
तां महापापसंहर्त्रीं शिवभक्तिविवर्धिनीम् ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O celestial sage, let this be listened to. I shall resume the story joyfully, the story that quells all sins and increases devotion to Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वती वचनं श्रुत्वा हरस्स परमात्मनः ।
दृष्ट्वानन्दकरं रूपं जहर्षातीव च द्विज ॥ ३॥

English
O brahmin, on hearing the words of Śiva, the great Soul and on seeing His pleasant form and features Pārvatī was delighted much. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्युवाच महासाध्वी स्वोपकण्ठस्थितं विभुम् ।
अतीव सुखिता देवी प्रीत्युत्फुल्लानना शिवा ॥ ४॥

English
The highly chaste lady, goddess Pārvatī replied to the lord standing near with great pleasure and face beaming with love. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
त्वं नाथो मम देवेश त्वया किं विस्मृतं पुरा ।
दक्षयज्ञविनाशं हि यदर्थं कृतवान्हठात् ॥ ५॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—* O lord, O lord of gods, you are my husband. Has it been forgotten by you why you destroyed the sacrifice of Dakṣa formerly with great tenacity? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स त्वं साहं समुत्पन्ना मेनायां कार्यसिद्धये ।
देवानां देवदेवेश तारकाप्तासुखात्मनाम् ॥ ६॥

English
Listen, O lord of gods, I am born of Menā for the achievement of the task of the gods terrified to the quick by Tāraka. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश करोषि च कृपां यदि ।
पतिर्भव ममेशान मम वाक्यं कुरु प्रभो ॥ ७॥

English
O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if you are sympathetic O lord, become my husband. O lord do as I say. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुर्गेहे मया सम्यग्गम्यते त्वदनुज्ञया ।
प्रसिद्धं क्रियतां तद्वै विशुद्धं परमं यशः ॥ ८॥

English
With your permission I am going to my father's abode. Let your pure great glory be made well known. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
गन्तव्यं भवता नाथ हिमवत्पार्श्वतः प्रभो ।
याचस्व मां ततो भिक्षुर्भूत्वा लीलाविशारदः ॥ ९॥

English
O lord, you will please go to Himavat. Clever in divine sports, be the mendicant and beg of him, me as your alms. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा त्वया प्रकर्तव्यं लोकेषु ख्यापयन् यशः ।
पितुर्मे सफलं सर्वं कुरुष्वैवं गृहाश्रमम् ॥ १०॥

English
Spreading your glory in the worlds you shall make everything about my father fruitful. Thus you start your householder's life. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिभिर्बोधितः प्रीत्या स्वबन्धुपरिवारितः ।
करिष्यति न सन्देहस्तव वाक्यं पिता मम ॥ ११॥

English
There is no doubt that my father with his kinsmen will accede to your request as he has already been urged by the sages lovingly. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षकन्या पुराहं वै पित्रा दत्ता यदा तव ।
यथोक्तविधिना तत्र विवाहो न कृतस्त्वया ॥ १२॥

English
Formerly as Dakṣa's daughter I was offered to you by my father. But the marriage rites were not duly performed then. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
न ग्रहाः पूजितास्तेन दक्षेण जनकेन मे ।
ग्रहाणां विषयस्तेन सच्छिद्रोऽयं महानभूत् ॥ १३॥

English
The planets were not worshipped by my father. Therefore a great defect occurred in our marriage in regard to the planets. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्यथोक्तविधिना कर्तुमर्हसि मे प्रभो ।
विवाहं त्वं महादेव देवानां कार्यसिद्धये ॥ १४॥

English
Hence, O lord, you will celebrate marriage in accordance with the rules for the fulfilment of the task of the gods. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
विवाहस्य यथा रीतिः कर्तव्या सा तथा ध्रुवम् ।
जानातु हिमवान् सम्यक् कृतं पुत्र्या शुभं तपः ॥ १५॥

English
The customary procedures of the marriage shall certainly be followed. Let Himavat know that an auspicious penance has been performed well by his daughter. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा सुप्रसन्नः सदाशिवः ।
प्रोवाच वचनं प्रीत्या गिरिजां प्रहसन्निव ॥ १६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words, Sadāśiva was highly delighted. Laughingly and lovingly He spoke to Pārvatī. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि महेशानि परमं वचनं मम ।
यथोचितं सुमाङ्गल्यमविकारि तथा कुरु ॥ १७॥

English
*Śiva said—*  O great Goddess, listen to my important statement. See that our marriage rites are performed in the proper manner without deficiency. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मादिकानि भूतानि त्वनित्यानि वरानने ।
दृष्टं यत्सर्वमेतच्च नश्वरं विद्धि भामिनि ॥ १८॥

English
O sweet-faced one, all the living beings Brahmā and others are non-eternal. O beautiful lady, know all these visible things to be perishable. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एकोऽनेकत्वमापन्नो निर्गुणो हि गुणान्वितः ।
स्वज्योत्स्नया यो विभाति परज्योत्स्नान्वितोऽभवत् ॥ १९॥

English
Know that the single beings assumed manifold forms. The attributeless took over the attributes. That which is self-luminous had other lights imposed on it. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वतन्त्रः परतन्त्रश्च त्वया देवि कृतो ह्यहम् ।
सर्वकर्त्री च प्रकृतिर्महामाया त्वमेव हि ॥ २०॥

English
O gentle lady, I, the independent, have been made subservient by you. You alone are the great illusory power, the Primordial nature that creates. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मायामयं कृतमिदं च जगत्समग्रं
सर्वात्मना हि विधृतं परया स्वबुद्ध्या ।
सर्वात्मभिः सुकृतिभिः परमात्मभावैः
संसिक्तमात्मनि गुणैः परिवेष्टितञ्च ॥ २१॥

English
This entire universe has been made of illusion; it is held by the supreme soul with His great intellect. It is united and enveloped by the Gaṇas of the nature of pervading souls of meritorious deeds, akin to the nature of supreme soul. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
के ग्रहाः के ऋतुगणाः के वान्येऽपि त्वया ग्रहाः ।
किमुक्तं चाधुना देवि शिवार्थं वरवर्णिनि ॥ २२॥

English
What are these planets? What are these sets of seasons? What are those other planets? O gentle lady, what is said by you, O fair-complexioned one. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
गुणकार्यप्रभेदेनावाभ्यां प्रादुर्भवः कृतः ।
भक्तहेतोर्जगत्यस्मिन्भक्तवत्सलभावतः ॥ २३॥

English
We two have created the universe different in attributes and actions for the sake of the devotees and with a disposition favourable to them. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं हि वै प्रकृतिः सूक्ष्मा रजः सत्त्वतमोमयी ।
व्यापारदक्षा सततं सगुणा निर्गुणापि च ॥ २४॥

English
You are indeed the subtle primordial nature consisting of Rajas, Sattva and Tamas. You are capable of perpetual activity. You are both possessed and devoid of attributes. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषामिह भूतानामहमात्मा सुमध्यमे ।
निर्विकारी निरीहश्च भक्तेच्छोपात्तविग्रहः ॥ २५॥

English
O slender-waisted lady, of all living beings I am the soul without abberation without yearnings. I take up bodies at the requests and wishes of my devotees. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालयं न गच्छेयं जनकं तव शैलजे ।
ततस्त्वां भिक्षुको भूत्वा न याचेयं कथञ्चन ॥ २६॥

English
O daughter of the mountain, I will not go up to Himavat your father. I will not become a mendicant and beg of him for you. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
महागुणैर्गरिष्ठोऽपि महात्मापि गिरीन्द्रजे ।
देहीति वचनात्सद्यः पुरुषो याति लाघवम् ॥ २७॥

English
O daughter of the lord of mountains, even a weighty person endowed with great qualities, even a noble soul, is considered base immediately after uttering the words—"Please give me". (27.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं ज्ञात्वा तु कल्याणि किमस्माकं वदस्यथ ।
कार्यं त्वदाज्ञया भद्रे यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ २८॥

English
O benevolent lady, after knowing this what is it that you say is our duty? Gentle lady, do as you wish. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तेनोक्तापि महादेवी सा साध्वी कमलेक्षणा ।
जगाद शङ्करं भक्त्या सुप्रणम्य पुनः पुनः ॥ २९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Thus addressed, the great goddess, the chaste lady of lotus-like eyes told Śiva with devotion after bowing to Him again and again. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
त्वमात्मा प्रकृतिश्चाहं नात्र कार्या विचारणा ।
स्वतन्त्रौ भक्तवशगौ निर्गुणौ सगुणावपि ॥ ३०॥

English
*Pārvatī said:—*  "You are the cosmic soul and I am the cosmic nature. There is nothing to deliberate on this. We two are independent and subservient to the devotees. We two are both possessed and devoid of attributes. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रयत्नेन त्वया शम्भो कार्यं वाक्यं मम प्रभो ।
याचस्व मां हिमगिरेः सौभाग्यं देहि शङ्कर ॥ ३१॥

English
O lord Śiva, with effort, you will please do according to my request. O Śiva, beg of Himavat for me, You will bestow a fortune on him. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपां कुरु महेशान तव भक्तास्मि नित्यशः ।
तव पत्नी सदा नाथ ह्यहं जन्मनि जन्मनि ॥ ३२॥

English
O great lord, be sympathetic. I am your devotee for ever. I am your wife for ever in ever birth. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं ब्रह्म परमात्मा हि निर्गुणः प्रकृतेः परः ।
निर्विकारी निरीहश्च स्वतन्त्रः परमेश्वरः ॥ ३३॥

English
You are Brahman, the great soul, devoid of attributes greater than primordial nature, without abberation, free from yearnings, independent, great lord. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि सगुणोऽपीह भक्तोद्धारपरायणः ।
विहारी स्वात्मनि रतो नानालीलाविशारदः ॥ ३४॥

English
Still you are possessed of attributes too and enthusiastic in the uplift of the devotees. You sport about in your own soul engrossed in it and you are clever in your different sports. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा त्वामहं जाने महादेव महेश्वर ।
किमुक्तेन च सर्वज्ञ बहुना हि दयां कुरु ॥ ३५॥

English
O great lord, I know you in every respect. O omniscient, of what avail is a detailed talk. Take pity on me. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
विस्तारय यशो लोके कृत्वा लीलां महाद्भुताम् ।
यत्सुगीय जना नाथाञ्जसोत्तीर्णा भवाम्बुधिम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Spread your glory in the world indulging in your wonderful divine sports. Singing them, O lord, people can cross the ocean of worldly existence. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवमुक्त्वा गिरिजा सुप्रणम्य पुनः पुनः ।
विरराम महेशानं नतस्कन्धा कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ३७॥

English
Brahmā said:—  After saying these words to the great lord, Pārvatī stopped and bowed to Him frequently with shoulders stooping and palms joined in reverence. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवमुक्तः स तया महात्मा
महेश्वरो लोकविडम्बनाय ।
तथेति मत्वा प्रहसन् बभूव
मुदान्वितः कर्तुमनास्तदेव ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus addressed by her, Śiva of the noble soul, just to follow and imitate the worldly conventions thought that it should be so. Being desirous of doing so he rejoiced. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो ह्यन्तर्हितश्शम्भुर्बभूव सुप्रहर्षितः ।
कैलासं प्रययौ काल्या विरहाकृष्टमानसः ॥ ३९॥

English
Then Śiva vanished. With a great delight He went to Kailāsa but at His separation from Pārvatī his mind was distressed. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा महेशानो नन्द्यादिभ्यः स ऊचिवान् ।
वृत्तान्तं सकलं तं वै परमानन्दनिर्भरः ॥ ४०॥

English
After reaching there He mentioned the news to Nandin and others. He was very much delighted. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तेऽपि श्रुत्वा गणाः सर्वे भैरवाद्याश्च सर्वशः ।
बभूवुः सुखिनोऽत्यन्तं विदधुः परमोत्सवम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Those Gaṇas, Bhairava and others were greatly pleased. They celebrated the occasion with great festivities. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
महत्सुमङ्गलं तत्र बभूवातीव नारद ।
सर्वेषां दुःखनाशोऽभूद्रुद्रः प्रापापि सम्मुदम् ॥ ४२॥

English
O Nārada, all went on auspiciously. All misery was at an end. Śiva too was in a pleasant mood. (42.)
