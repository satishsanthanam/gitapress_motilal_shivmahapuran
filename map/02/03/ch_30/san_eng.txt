### **CHAPTER THIRTY - (The Celebration of Pārvatī's Return)**

---

Sanskrit
३०

नारद उवाच ।
विधे तात महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं परमार्थदृक् ।
अद्भुतेयं कथाश्रावि त्वदनुग्रहतो मया ॥ १॥

English
Nārada said:—  O Brahmā, O dear, of great fortune, you have the real vision and are blessed. This wonderful story was heard by me, thanks to your blessings. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
गते हरे स्वशैले हि पार्वती सर्वमङ्गला ।
किं चकार गता कुत्र तन्मे वद महामते ॥ २॥

English
When Śiva returned to His mountain, O intelligent one, what did Pārvatī all auspicious do and where did she go? Please tell me. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु सुप्रीतितस्तात यज्जातं तदनन्तरम् ।
हरे गते निजस्थाने तद् वदामि शिवं स्मरन् ॥ ३॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O dear, listen with pleasure to what happened thereafter when Śiva returned to His place. I shall mention it, remembering Śiva, (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्यपि सखीयुक्ता रूपं कृत्वा तु सार्थकम् ।
जगाम स्वपितुर्गेहं महादेवेति वादिनी ॥ ४॥

English
Accompanied by her maids and assuming meaningful dress and features she returned to her father's house repeating the name of lord Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्यागमनं श्रुत्वा मेना च स हिमाचलः ।
दिव्यं यानं समारुह्य प्रययौ हर्षविह्वलः ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing that Pārvatī was returning, Menā and Himavat excessively delighted went ahead seated in a divine vehicle.

6-7 The chief priest, the citizens, the maids, the kinsmen and also others accompanied them. All the brothers with Maināka, the eldest, accompanied them highly delighted and crying shouts of victory. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
संस्थाप्य मङ्गलघटं राजवर्त्मनि राजिते ।
चन्दनागरुकस्तूरीफलशाखासमन्विते ॥ ८॥

सुपुरोधाब्राह्मणैश्च मुनिभिर्ब्रह्मवादिभिः ।
नारीभिर्नर्तकीभिश्च गजेन्द्रादिसुशोभितैः ॥ ९॥

परितः परितो रम्भास्तम्भवृन्दसमन्विते ।
पतिपुत्रवतीयोषित्समूहैर्दीपहस्तकैः ॥ १०॥

द्विजवृन्दैश्च संयुक्ते कुर्वद्भिर्मङ्गलध्वनिम् ।
नानाप्रकारवाद्यैश्च शङ्खध्वनिभिरन्विते ॥ ११॥

एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दुर्गाजगाम स्वपुरान्तिकम् ।
विशन्ती नगरं देवी ददर्श पितरौ पुनः ॥ १२॥

English
The auspicious waterpot was placed in the main highway decorated with sandal paste, aguru, musk and branches of trees with fruits. The priests, brahmins and sages reciting the Vedas, dancing girls, all went ahead seated on lofty elephants to receive her. All round stumps of plantain trees were fixed. Women along with their sons and husbands held lamps in their hands. Brahmins were shouting mantras etc. in an auspicious voice. Various instruments were played. Conch shells were sounded. In the meantime Pārvatī reached the outskirts of the city. Entering the city she saw her parents again. (8-12.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रसन्नौ प्रधावन्तौ हर्षविह्वलमानसौ ।
दृष्ट्वा काली सुप्रहृष्टा स्वालिभिः प्रणनाम तौ ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing the parents rushing at her in their great delight, she gladly bowed to them along with her maids. (13.)

---

Sanskrit

English
They gave her their blessings and embraced her. Saying “O darling,” they shed tears in their excitement of love. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्वकीया अप्यस्या अन्या नार्योऽपि सम्मुदा ।
भ्रातृस्त्रियोऽपि सुप्रीत्या दृढालिङ्गनमादधुः ॥ १५॥

English
Women near and dear as also the wives of her brothers embraced her closely with great pleasure. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
साधितं हि त्वया सम्यक् सुकार्यं कुलतारणम् ।
त्वत्सदाचरणेनापि पाविताः स्माखिला वयम् ॥ १६॥

English
“A great task has been well accomplished by you. It has saved the whole family. All of us are sanctified by your noble conduct”. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सर्वे सुप्रशंस्य प्रणेमुस्तां प्रहर्षिताः ।
चन्दनैः सुप्रसूनैश्च समानर्चुः शिवां मुदा ॥ १७॥

English
Praising her with these and similar words they bowed to her with great delight. They worshipped her with sandal paste and sweet scented flowers in great joy. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

पुष्पवृष्टिं शुभां चक्रुर्नत्वा तां तुष्टुवुः स्तवैः ॥ १८॥

English
At that time the gods, seated in their aerial chariots in the sky, showered auspicious flowers, bowed to and eulogised her with hymns. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

पुरं प्रवेशयामासुः सर्वे विप्रादयो मुदा ॥ १९॥

English
Then the Brahmins and others joyfully took you within the city in a resplendent chariot. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विप्राः पुरोधाश्च सख्योऽन्याश्च स्त्रियः शिवम् ।
गृहं प्रवेशयामासुर्बहुमानपुरस्सरम् ॥ २०॥

English
Then the brahmins, the maids and other women took her within the house with due honour. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियो निर्मञ्छनं चक्रुर्विप्रा युयुजुराशिषः ।
हिमवान्मेनका माता मुमोदाति मुनीश्वर ॥ २१॥

English
O great sage, ladies performed her ceremonial ablution; the brahmins offered benedictions. Himvat and Menakā rejoiced much. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स हिमवान् तात सुप्रहृष्टाः प्रसन्नधीः ।
सम्मान्य सकलान्प्रीत्या स्नातुं गङ्गां जगाम ह ॥ २२॥

English
Himavat considered his household life fruitful. A daughter is far better than an ignoble son. He praised you too, Nārada, saying “Well done, Well done”. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणेभ्यश्च बन्दिभ्यः पर्वतेन्द्रो धनं ददौ ।
मङ्गलं पाठयामास स द्विजेभ्यो महोत्सवम् ॥ २३॥

English
The lord of the mountains gave monetary gifts to brahmins and lords. He made the brahmins recite auspicious hymns, as part of the festivities. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्वकन्यया हृष्टौ पितरौ भ्रातरस्तथा ।
जामयश्च महाप्रीत्या समूषुः प्राङ्गणे मुने ॥ २४॥

English
The parents delighted with their daughter; the brothers and the sisters gathered joyfully in the courtyard, O sage. (24.)

---

English
The happy and delighted Himavat, honoured everyone. Then he went to the Gaṅgā for his bath. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शम्भुः सुलीलो भक्तवत्सलः ।
सुनर्तकनटो भूत्वा मेनकासन्निधिं ययौ ॥ २६॥

English
In the meantime, Śiva, favourably disposed to His disciples and prone to divine sports, assumed the guise of a dancer and approached Menakā. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृङ्गं वामे करे धृत्वा दक्षिणे डमरुं तथा ।
पृष्ठे कन्थां रक्तवासा नृत्यगानविशारदः ॥ २७॥

ततस्सुनटरूपोऽसौ मेनाया प्राङ्गणे मुदा ।
चक्रे सुनृत्यं विविधं गानं चातिमनोहरम् ॥ २८॥

English
He held the blowing horn in his left and the drum in his right hand. He wore a red cloth and had the wallet suspended behind his back. In the guise of a dancer with the skill of dancing and singing, he danced well and sang many songs in sweet voice. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृङ्गं च डमरुं तत्र वादयामास सुध्वनिम् ।
महतीं विविधां तत्र स चकार मनोहराम् ॥ २९॥

English
He blew the horn and played on the drum in very sweet tunes. Everything was very pleasant.

30 All the citizens men, women, children and old folks assembled there to witness the performance. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा सुगीतं तद्दृष्ट्वा सुनृत्यं च मनोहरम् ।
सहसा मुमुहुः सर्वे मेनापि च तदा मुने ॥ ३१॥

English
O sage, on hearing the sweet songs, and seeing the delightful dance, the people entered into raptures of ecstasy.

32-35 Pārvatī became unconscious. She saw Śiva’s handsome form, bearing trident and other symbols before her vision. He had smeared the ashes all over His body. He was wearing a garland of bones. His face was beaming with his shining three eyes. He had the sacred thread of a serpent. Exquisitely white in complexion, the handsome lord Śiva, the friend of the distressed, the ocean of mercy was repeating the words “Choose the boon (or the bridegroom).” On seeing Him thus in her mind she bowed to Him. Mentally she had chosen the boon when she had said, “Be my husband”. (31.)

Sanskrit

अन्तर्धाय पुनस्तत्र सुननर्त स भिक्षुकः ॥ ३६॥

English
And He had granted her the auspicious boon with pleasure and vanished. The mendicant now continued the dance. (36.)

English
On hearing the details from Menā he became very angry. He ordered his attendants to drive out the dancer. (41.)

English
But O excellent sage, none of them could push him out as he was hot to the touch like a blazing fire and very brilliant. (42.)
