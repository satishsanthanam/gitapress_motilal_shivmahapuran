### **CHAPTER THIRTYONE - (Description of Śiva's magic)**

---

Sanskrit
३१

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तयोर्भक्तिं शिवे ज्ञात्वा परामव्यभिचारिणीम् ।
सर्वे शक्रादयो देवाश्चिचिन्तुरिति नारद ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said:—  On knowing their undistracted great devotion to Śiva, O Nārada, Indra and other gods, thought like this. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
एकान्तभक्त्या शैलश्चेत्कन्यां तस्मै प्रदास्यति ।
ध्रुवं निर्वाणतां सद्यः स प्राप्स्यति च भारते ॥ २॥

English
The Gods said :—  If the mountain were to give his daughter to Śiva with singleminded devotion he will attain salvation immediately and will disappear from Bhārata.⁴⁵ (2.)

[fn 45. Bhārata, the land of Bharatas, derives its name from the Bharatas, an ancient powerful Aryan tribe mentioned in the Ṛgveda.]
---

Sanskrit
अनन्तरत्नाधारश्चेत्पृथ्वीं त्यक्त्वा प्रयास्यति ।
रत्नगर्भाभिधा भूमिर्मिथ्यैव भविता ध्रुवम् ॥ ३॥

English
The mountain is the storehouse of endless gems. If he were to leave off the Earth and go, the name of the Earth—Ratnagarbhā (having gems in the womb)—shall be a misnomer. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थावरत्वं परित्यज्य दिव्यरूपं विधाय सः ।
कन्यां शूलभृते दत्त्वा शिवलोकं गमिष्यति ॥ ४॥

English
He will cast off his immobile aspect and assume a divine form. He will give his daughter to the Trident bearing deity and will go to Śiva's region. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेवस्य सारूप्यं लप्स्यते नात्र संशयः ।
तत्र भुक्त्वा वरान्भोगांस्ततो मोक्षमवाप्स्यति ॥ ५॥

English
He will undoubtedly attain mergence into lord Śiva. having enjoyed pleasures there, he will attain salvation. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्यालोच्य सुराः सर्वे कृत्वा चामन्त्रणं मिथः ।
प्रस्थापयितुमैच्छंस्ते गुरुं तत्र सुविस्मिताः ॥ ६॥

English
Brahmā said:—  Thinking like this and consulting one another they, in their bewilderment, decided to send god Bṛhaspati there. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शक्रादयो देवाः सर्वे गुरुनिकेतनम् ।
जग्मुः प्रीत्या सविनया नारद स्वार्थसाधकाः ॥ ७॥

English
O Nārada, then Indra and other gods, went to Bṛhaspati's abode lovingly with humility, in their eagerness to achieve their self-interest. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र गुरुं नत्वा सर्वे देवाः सवासवाः ।
चक्रुर्निवेदनं तस्मै गुरवे वृत्तमादरात् ॥ ८॥

English
Reaching there, all the gods including Indra bowed to Bṛhaspati and submitted every detail to him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
गुरो हिमालयगृहं गच्छास्मत्कार्यसिद्धये ।
तत्र गत्वा प्रयत्नेन कुरु निन्दां च शूलिनः ॥ ९॥

English
*The gods said :—*  O revered preceptor, please go to the abode of Himavat for the fulfilment of our task. After going there, you shall make disparaging remarks about the trident-bearing deity. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पिनाकिना विना दुर्गा वरं नान्यं वरिष्यति ।
अनिच्छया सुतां दत्त्वा फलं तूर्णं लभिष्यति ॥ १०॥

कालेनैवाधुना शैल इदानीं भुवि तिष्ठतु ।
अनेकरत्नाधारं तं स्थापय त्वं क्षितौ गुरौ ॥ ११॥

English
Pārvatī will not marry any other person except Śiva. The mountain will derive the benefit only after sometime if he gives the daughter in marriage without his full concurrence. Let the mountain stay on earth for the present. O preceptor, you shall make him stay on the earth as he is the support of many gems. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति देववचः श्रुत्वा प्रददौ कर्णयोः करम् ।
न स्वीचकार स गुरुः स्मरन्नाम शिवेति च ॥ १२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the gods, the preceptor of the gods, plugged his ears with his hands. He did not accept the proposal of the gods. He remembered the name "Śiva". (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ स्मृत्वा महादेवं बृहस्पतिरुदारधीः ।
उवाच देववर्यांश्च धिक्कृत्वा च पुनः पुनः ॥ १३॥

English
Then remembering lord Śiva, Bṛhaspati⁴⁶ of liberal mind rebuked the gods again and again and said. (13.)

[fn ⁴⁶. Bṛhaspati is designated as the family priest of divine community. He is the preceptor of the gods and intercedes on their behalf with the triad—Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva, seeking their support.]
---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।
सर्वे देवाः स्वार्थपराः परार्थध्वंसकारकाः ।
कृत्वा शङ्करनिन्दां हि यास्यामि नरकं ध्रुवम् ॥ १४॥

English
*Bṛhaspati said :—*  All of you gods seem to be selfish in nature. You want to destroy other's interests. Indeed I will go to hell by disparaging Śiva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्चिन्मध्ये च युष्माकं गच्छेच्छैलान्तिकं सुराः ।
सम्पादयेत्स्वाभिमतं शैलेन्द्रं प्रतिबोध्य च ॥ १५॥

English
O gods, one of you shall go to the mountain. Let him urge the lord of the mountains and achieve the desired object. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अनिच्छया सुतां दत्त्वा सुखं तिष्ठतु भारते ।
तस्मै भक्त्या सुतां दत्त्वा मोक्षं प्राप्स्यति निश्चितम् ॥ १६॥

English
Let him stay in Bhārata after giving his daughter without willingness. It is certain he will attain salvation if he gives his daughter with devotion. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चात्सप्तर्षयः सर्वे बोधयिष्यन्ति पर्वतम् ।
पिनाकिना विना दुर्गा वरं नान्यं वरिष्यति ॥ १७॥

English
Afterwards the seven celestial sages will properly persuade the mountain. Except Pināka-bearing deity, Pārvatī will not marry any other person. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा गच्छत सुरा ब्रह्मलोकं सवासवाः ।
वृत्तं कथयत स्वं तत्स वः कार्यं करिष्यति ॥ १८॥

English
Or, O gods, all of you go to Brahmā's region taking Indra with you. Tell Brahmā all your details. He will get your work done immediately. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा ते समालोच्याजग्मुर्मम सभां सुराः ।
सर्वे निवेदयाञ्चक्रुर्नत्वा तद्गतमादरात् ॥ १९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing it and consulting among themselves the gods came to my Assembly. After duly bowing to me they informed me about the details. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां तद्वचः श्रुत्वा शिवनिन्दाकरं तदा ।
वेदवक्ता विलप्याहं तानवोचं सुरान्मुने ॥ २०॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods about censuring Śiva, O sage, I the reciter of the Vedas spoke to them in an aggrieved tone. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
नाहं कर्तुं क्षमो वत्साः शिवनिन्दां सुदुस्सहाम् ।
सम्पद्विनाशरूपाञ्च विपदां बीजरूपिणीम् ॥ २१॥

English
"Dear children, I am incompetent to decry Śiva. It is unbearable. It destroys all riches. It is the seed of all adversities. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरा गच्छत कैलासं सन्तोषयत शङ्करम् ।
प्रस्थापयत तं शीघ्रं हिमालयगृहं प्रति ॥ २२॥

English
O gods, all of you go to Kailāsa and propitiate Śiva. Make Him go to Himavat's abode quickly. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स गच्छेदुपशैलेशमात्मनिन्दां करोतु वै ।
परनिन्दा विनाशाय स्वनिन्दा यशसे मता ॥ २३॥

English
Let him approach the lord of mountains and make disparaging remarks about Himself. Rebuking others is conducive to destruction. Rebuking oneself is conducive to fame". (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रुत्वेति मद्वचो देवा मां प्रणम्य मुदा च ते ।
कैलासं प्रययुः शीघ्रं शैलानामधिपं गिरिम् ॥ २४॥

English
On hearing my words, all the gods, joyously bowed to me and went to Kailāsa, the chief of mountains. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा शिवं दृष्ट्वा प्रणम्य नतमस्तकाः ।
सुकृताञ्जलयः सर्वे तुष्टुवुस्तं सुरा हरम् ॥ २५॥

English
Going there and seeing Śiva they bowed to Him with bent heads and palms joined in reverence. The gods eulogised Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणाकर शङ्कर ।
वयं त्वां शरणापन्नाः कृपां कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २६॥

English
*The gods said :—* O great lord, lord of gods, O Śiva, the merciful, we seek refuge in you. Be sympathetic. Obeisance be to you. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं भक्तवत्सलः स्वामिन्भक्तकार्यकरः सदा ।
दीनोद्धरः कृपासिन्धुर्भक्तापद्विनिमोचकः ॥ २७॥

English
O lord, you are favourably disposed to your devotees, always carrying out their tasks. You are the uplifter of the distressed, and an ocean of mercy. You save us from all our miseries and distresses. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा महेशानं सर्वे देवाः सवासवाः ।
सर्वं निवेदयाञ्चक्रुस्तद्वृत्तं तत आदरात् ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Eulogising lord Śiva thus, Indra and other gods respectfully submitted all the details. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा देववचनं स्वीचकार महेश्वरः ।
देवान् सुयापयामास तानाश्वास्य विहस्य सः ॥ २९॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods, lord Śiva agreed to the proposal. He made the gods return after assuring them smilingly. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा मुमुदिरे सर्वे शीघ्रं गत्वा स्वमन्दिरम् ।
सिद्धं मत्वा स्वकार्यं हि प्रशंसन्तः सदाशिवम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Hastening to their abodes, the gods rejoiced much considering their work fully fulfilled and praising Sadāśiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स भगवाञ्छम्भुर्महेशो भक्तवत्सलः ।
प्रययौ शैलभूपञ्च मायेशो निर्विकारवान् ॥ ३१॥

English
Then the lord Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees, the lord of magic and free from aberrations went to the lord of mountains. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा शैलः सभामध्ये समुवास मुदान्वितः ।
बन्धुवर्गैः परिवृतः पार्वतीसहितः स्वयम् ॥ ३२॥

एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र ह्याजगाम सदाशिवः ।
दण्डी छत्री दिव्यवासा बिभ्रत्तिलकमुज्ज्वलम् ॥ ३३॥

English
When the lord of the mountains was seated in his royal assembly along with Pārvatī and kinsmen, Sadāśiva came there, in the meantime bearing a staff and an umbrella. He was dressed in divine clothes and had a shining mark on the forehead. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
करे स्फटिकमालां च शालग्रामं गले दधत् ।
जपन्नाम हरेर्भक्त्या साधुवेषधरो द्विजः ॥ ३४॥

English
He was in the guise of a saintly brahmin. He was repeating the name of Viṣṇu with devotion. He had the garland of crystal beads in his hand and the Śālagrama stone round his neck. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तं च दृष्ट्वा समुत्तस्थौ सगणोऽपि हिमालयः ।
ननाम दण्डवद्भूमौ भक्त्यातिथिमपूर्वकम् ॥ ३५॥

English
On seeing that extraordinary guest, Himavat with his attendants stood up in reverence and prostrated before him with devotion. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ननाम पार्वती भक्त्या प्राणेशं विप्ररूपिणम् ।
ज्ञात्वा तं मनसा देवी तुष्टाव परया मुदा ॥ ३६॥

English
Pārvatī bowed with devotion to her dear lover in the guise of a brahmin. On realising him mentally the goddess eulogised him with great joy. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
आशिषं युयुजे विप्रः सर्वेषां प्रीतितश्शिवः ।
शिवाया अधिकं तात मनोऽभिलषितं हृदा ॥ ३७॥

English
With great pleasure Śiva bestowed his blessings on all. O dear, He bestowed on Pārvatī her cherished desire in addition. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
मधुपर्कादिकं सर्वं जग्राह ब्राह्मणो मुदा ।
दत्तं शैलाधिराजेन हिमागेन महादरात् ॥ ३८॥

English
The brahmin received with pleasure the articles of homage$^{47}$ etc. offered by Himavat, the lord of mountains. (38.)

[fn 47. Madhuparka is a mixture of honey, butter, sugar, curd and water offered to a guest when he first comes to the house.]
---

Sanskrit
पप्रच्छ कुशलं चास्य हिमाद्रिः पर्वतोत्तमः ।
तं द्विजेन्द्रं महाप्रीत्या सम्पूज्य विधिवन्मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, after duly worshipping the excellent brahmin with pleasure, the mountain Himavat enquired of his welfare. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः पप्रच्छ शैलेशस्तं ततः को भवानिति ।
उवाच शीघ्रं विप्रेन्द्रो गिरीन्द्रं सादरं वचः ॥ ४०॥

English
Again, the lord of mountains asked him “Who are you, please ?” Immediately the chief of brahmins, spoke to the lord of mountains thus. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्रेन्द्र उवाच ।
ब्राह्मणोऽहं गिरिश्रेष्ठ वैष्णवो बुधसत्तमः ।
घटिकीं वृत्तिमाश्रित्य भ्रमामि धरणीतले ॥ ४१॥

English
*The chief of brahmins said:—*  O foremost among mountains, I am a brahmin devotee of Viṣṇu, and a great scholar. My occupation is that of a match-maker. I roam about on the earth. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
मनोयायी सर्वगामी सर्वज्ञोऽहं गुरोर्बलात् ।
परोपकारी शुद्धात्मा दयासिन्धुर्विकारहा ॥ ४२॥

English
I go where I wish. I go everywhere. By the power of my preceptor I am omniscient. I am simple-minded and by nature I help others and I am sympathetic and quell aberrations. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
मया ज्ञातं हराय त्वं स्वसुतां दातुमिच्छसि ।
इमां पद्मसमां दिव्यां वररूपां सुलक्षणाम् ॥ ४३॥

English
I have come to know that you desire to give your daughter to Śiva, this daughter so tender like a lotus flower, of divinely excellent form and endowed with all accomplishments. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
निराश्रयायासङ्गाय कुरूपायागुणाय च ।
श्मशानवासिने व्यालग्राहिरूपाय योगिने ॥ ४४॥

दिग्वाससे कुगात्राय व्यालभूषणधारिणे ।
अज्ञातकुलनाम्ने च कुशीलायाविहारिणे ॥ ४५॥

विभूतिदिग्धदेहाय सङ्क्रुद्धायाविवेकिने ।
अज्ञातवयसेऽतीव कुजटाधारिणे सदा ॥ ४६॥

English
To Śiva—who has no support, who is devoid of associations, who is deformed, who is without attributes, who resides in the cremation-ground, who has the form of a snake-catcher, who is a Yogin, who is naked, who has deficient limbs, who wears snakes as his ornaments, whose name and pedigree are unknown, whose conduct is bad, who has no sport, whose body is smeared with ashes, who is furious, who lacks in discrimination, whose age is not known, whose matted hair is ill worn, who supports all who roam about, who has garland of snakes who is a mendicant, who is engaged in following wrong-paths and who tenaciously discards the Vedic path. (44-47.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाश्रयाय भ्रमिणे नागहाराय भिक्षवे
कुमार्गनिरतायाथ वेदाऽध्वत्यागिने हठात् । ४७॥

इयं ते बुद्धिरचल न हि मङ्गलदा खलु ।
विबोध ज्ञानिनां श्रेष्ठ नारायणकुलोद्भव ॥ ४८॥

English
O mountain, this inclination of yours is not at all conducive to auspiciousness. O foremost among the wise, born of Nārāyaṇa's family, learn sense. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
न ते पात्रानुरूपश्च पार्वतीदानकर्मणि ।
महाजनः स्मेरमुखः श्रुतमात्राद्भविष्यति ॥ ४९॥

English
For the marriage of Pārvatī, He is not at all a deserving person. On hearing of this, the general public will smile in derision. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्य शैलाधिप त्वं च न तस्यैकोऽस्ति बान्धवः ।
महारत्नाकरः त्वं च तस्य किञ्चिद्धनं न हि ॥ ५०॥

English
O lord of mountains, see for yourself. He has not a single kinsman. You are the storehouse of great gems and jewels. He has no assets at all. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
बान्धवान्मेनकां कुध्रपते शीघ्रं सुतांस्तथा ।
सर्वान्पृच्छ प्रयत्नेन पण्डितान्पार्वतीं विना ॥ ५१॥

English
O lord of mountains, you shall consult your kinsmen, sons, wife and wise counsellors, except Pārvatī. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
रोगिणो नौषधं शश्वद्रोचते गिरिसत्तम ।
कुपथ्यं रोचतेऽभीक्ष्णं महादोषकरं सदा ॥ ५२॥

English
O lord of mountains, the medicine does not appeal at all to the patient. Wrong diet that brings about great defects always appeals to him. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ब्राह्मणः शीघ्रं स वै भुक्त्वा मुदान्वितः ।
जगाम स्वालयं शान्तो नानालीलाकरश्शिवः ॥ ५३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Saying this, the brahmin stopped. He took food and left the place with pleasure for his abode. Śiva is one who quietly indulges in His divine sports. (53.)
