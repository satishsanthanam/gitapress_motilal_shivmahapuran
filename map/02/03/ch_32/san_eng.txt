### **CHAPTER THIRTYTWO - *(The seven celestial sages arrive)***

---

Sanskrit
३२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ब्राह्मणस्य वचः श्रुत्वा मेनोवाच हिमालयम् ।
शोकेनसाधुनयना हृदयेन विदूयता ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing the words of the brahmin, Menā spoke to Himavat with tears welling up in her eyes, due to grief and with the heart extremely dejected. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
श‍ृणु शैलेन्द्र मद्वाक्यं परिणामे सुखावहम् ।
पृच्छ शैववरान्सर्वान्किमुक्तं ब्राह्मणेन ह ॥ २॥

English
*Menā said:—* O lord of mountains, please listen to my words conducive to happiness. Please consult important devotees of Śiva regarding what has been mentioned by the brahmin. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
निन्दानेन कृता शम्भोर्वैष्णवेन द्विजन्मना ।
श्रुत्वा तां मे मनोऽतीव निर्विण्णं हि नगेश्वर ॥ ३॥

English
Many disparaging remarks about Śiva have been made by this brahminical devotee of Viṣṇu. O lord of mountains, on hearing these words, my mind is very much dejected. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मै रुद्राय शैलेश न दास्यामि सुतामहम् ।
कुरूपशीलनाम्ने हि सुलक्षणयुतां निजाम् ॥ ४॥

English
O lord of mountains, I shall not give my daughter endowed with all good accomplishments to Śiva with ugly features, ignoble conduct and defiled name. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
न मन्यसे वचो चेन्मे मरिष्यामि न संशयः ।
त्यक्ष्यामि च गृहं सद्यो भक्षयिष्यामि वा विषम् ॥ ५॥

English
If you do not accede to my request, I shall undoubtedly die. I will immediately leave this house or swallow poison. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गले बद्ध्वाम्बिकां रज्ज्वा यास्यामि गहनं वनम् ।
महाम्बुधौ मज्जयिष्ये तस्मै दास्यामि नो सुताम् ॥ ६॥

English
With a rope I shall tie Pārvatī round my neck and go to a thick forest. I would rather drown myself in the great ocean. I shall never give my daughter to him. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वाशु तथा गत्वा मेना कोपालयं शुचा ।
त्यक्त्वा हारं रुदन्ती सा चकार शयनं भुवि ॥ ७॥

English
Saying thus with great grief, Menā entered the chamber of anger. Casting off her necklaces she lay down on the ground sighing and sobbing. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तात शम्भुना सप्त एव ते ।
संस्मृता ऋषयः सद्यो विरहव्याकुलात्मना ॥ ८॥

English
O dear, in the meantime all those seven celestial sages were remembered by Śiva whose mind was agitated by the pangs of separation from Pārvatī. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषयश्चैव ते सर्वे शम्भुना संस्मृता यदा ।
तदाजग्मुः स्वयं सद्यः कल्पवृक्षा इवापरे ॥ ९॥

English
All those seven sages,⁴⁸ as soon as they were remembered by Śiva, came there in person as though they were another set of wish-yielding Kalpa trees. (9.)

[fn 48. On the seven sages, see Note 164 P. 163.]
---

Sanskrit
अरुन्धती तथायाता साक्षात्सिद्धिरिवापरा ।
तान्दृष्ट्वा सूर्यसङ्काशान्विजहौ स्वजपं हरः ॥ १०॥

English
Arundhati too came there as though she was an achievement personified. On seeing them resplendent like the sun, Śiva stopped his recitation of mantras. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थित्वाग्रे ऋषयः श्रेष्ठा नत्वा स्तुत्वा शिवं मुने ।
मेनिरे च तदात्मानं कृतार्थं ते तपस्विनः ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, standing in front of Śiva and bowing to and eulogising Him, the seven sages of great austerity considered themselves blessed. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विस्मयमापन्ना नमस्कृत्य स्थिताः पुनः ।
प्रोचुः प्राञ्जलयस्ते वै शिवं लोकनमस्कृतम् ॥ १२॥

English
Then, as they were struck with surprise, they joined their palms in reverence, bowed to and addressed Śiva adored by all the worlds:— (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सर्वोत्कृष्ट महाराज सार्वभौम दिवौकसाम् ।
स्वभाग्यं वर्ण्यतेऽस्माभिः किं पुनः सकलोत्तमम् ॥ १३॥

English
The sages said:—“O most excellent of all, O great ruler, O Emperor of the heaven-dwellers, how can our fortune which is very excellent be described by us ? (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्तप्तं त्रिधा पूर्वं वेदाध्ययनमुत्तमम् ।
अग्नयश्च हुताः पूर्वं तीर्थानि विविधानि च ॥ १४॥

वाङ्मनः कायजं किञ्चित्पुण्यं स्मरणसम्भवम् ।
तत्सर्वं सङ्गतं चाद्य स्मरणानुग्रहात्तव ॥ १५॥

English
Formerly we had performed three kinds of penance; we had studied the excellent Vedas ; we had made offerings in the fire, we had visited many holy centres; thus whatever merit we have acquired verbally, mentally and physically that entire merit has now accrued to us by your blessing in remembering us. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
यो वै भजति नित्यं त्वां कृतकृत्यो भवेन्नरः ।
किं पुण्यं वर्ण्यते तेषां येषां च स्मरणं तव ॥ १६॥

English
A man who worships you always shall be blessed. How can that merit be properly described, the merit of those whom you yourself remember ? (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वोत्कृष्टा वयं जाताः स्मरणात्ते सदाशिव ।
मनोरथपथं नैव गच्छसि त्वं कथञ्चन ॥ १७॥

English
O Sadāśiva, we have become the most excellent of all people by your remembering us. Usually you never even come across the path of ambitions and aspirations of ordinary people. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वामनस्य फलं यद्वज्जन्मान्धस्य दृशौ यथा ।
वाचालत्वञ्च मूकस्य रङ्कस्य निधिदर्शनम् ॥ १८॥

पङ्गोर्गिरिवराक्रान्तिर्वन्ध्यायाः प्रसवस्तथा ।
दर्शनं भवतस्तद्वज्जातं नो दुर्लभं प्रभो ॥ १९॥

English
O lord, your vision, very difficult to be acquired, is like the fruit stooping down within the reach of the dwarf, like sight to a man born blind, like eloquency acquired by a dumb man, like the indigent meeting with a treasure-trove, like the lame man reaching the top of a high mountain and like the barren woman bearing a child. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यप्रभृति लोकेषु मान्याः पूज्या मुनीश्वराः ।
जातास्ते दर्शनादेव स्वमुच्चैः पदमाश्रिताः ॥ २०॥

English
By seeing you today we have become the most respectable sages worthy of the worship of all the worlds. We have reached the highest position. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र किं बहुनोक्तेन सर्वथा मान्यतां गताः ।
दर्शनात्तव देवेश सर्वदेवेश्वरस्य हि ॥ २१॥

English
O lord of gods, by seeing you who are the lord of all gods we have become worthy of great respect. There is no necessity of talking more. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्णानां किञ्च कर्तव्यमस्ति चेत्परमा कृपा ।
सदृशं सेवकानां तु देयं कार्यं त्वया शुभम् ॥ २२॥

English
If any duty is assigned to us it will be a favour to us. An auspicious task befitting us, your utter slaves, shall be given to us. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा तेषां शम्भुर्महेश्वरः ।
लौकिकाचारमाश्रित्य रम्यं वाक्यमुपाददे ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* On hearing their words, Śiva the great lord, in conformity with the conventions of the world, spoke these pleasant words:— (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
ऋषयश्च सदा पूज्या भवन्तश्च विशेषतः ।
युष्माकं कारणाद्विप्राः स्मरणं च मया कृतम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  Sages are always to be adored and particularly you all. O brahmins, it was for a specific reason that you have been summoned here. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ममावस्था भवद्भिश्च ज्ञायते ह्युपकारिका ।
साधनीया विशेषेण लोकानां सिद्धिहेतवे ॥ २५॥

English
My attitude of being helpful is known to you. That must be achieved, especially in the interest of the fulfilment of the desires of the world. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां दुःखमुत्पन्नं तारकात्सुदुरात्मनः ।
ब्रह्मणा च वरो दत्तः किं करोमि दुरासदः ॥ २६॥

English
Cause for great misery has arisen for the gods at the hands of Tāraka the wicked. Boon has already been granted. He is invincible. What shall I do ? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मूर्तयोऽष्टौ च याः प्रोक्ता मदीयाः परमर्षयः ।
ताः सर्वा उपकाराय न तु स्वार्थाय तत्स्फुटम् ॥ २७॥

English
O great sages, all the eight cosmic bodies$^{49}$ that I possess are not for furthering my self-interest, they are for helping the wide world. (27.)

[fn 49. On the eightfold image of Śiva, see Note 89 P. 132.]
---

Sanskrit
तथा च कर्तुकामोऽहं विवाहं शिवया सह ।
तया वै सुतपस्तप्तं दुष्करं परमर्षिभिः ॥ २८॥

तस्यै परं फलं देयमभीष्टं तद्धितावहम् ।
एतादृशः पणो मे हि भक्तानन्दप्रदः स्फुटम् ॥ २९॥

English
A great penance has been performed by Pārvatī. That cannot be performed even by great sages. I have to give her the great fruit thereof. Indeed my vow is to render delight to my devotees. The fruit I bestow on her shall be conducive to her welfare. Hence I wish to marry her. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वतीवचनाद्भिक्षुरूपो यातो गिरेर्गृहम् ।
अहं पावितवान्कालीं यतो लीलाविशारदः ॥ ३०॥

English
At the request of Pārvatī I went to the abode of the mountain in the guise of a mendicant. Clever in divine sports I thereby sanctified her. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मां ज्ञात्वा तौ परं ब्रह्म दम्पती परभक्तितः ।
दातुकामावभूतां च स्वसुतां वेदरीतितः ॥ ३१॥

English
On hearing to know that I am the supreme Brahman, the couple were desirous of giving me their daughter with great devotion in accordance with the Vedic manner. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवप्रेरणयाहं वै कृतवानस्मि निन्दनम् ।
तदा स्वस्य च तद्भक्तिं विहन्तुं वैष्णवात्मना ॥ ३२॥

English
On the inducement of the gods, in order to reduce the quality of devotion (of Himavat and Menā) I took the guise of a devotee of Viṣṇu and rebuked myself. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा तौ सुनिर्विण्णौ तद्धीनौ सम्बभूवतुः ।
स्वकन्यां नेच्छतो दातुं मह्यं हि मुनयोऽधुना ॥ ३३॥

English
O sages, on hearing it they were dejected and have now lost interest in me and do not wish to give their daughter to me. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्भवन्तो गच्छन्तु हिमाचलगृहं ध्रुवम् ।
तत्र गत्वा गिरिवरं तत्पत्नीञ्च प्रबोधय ॥ ३४॥

English
Hence you all go to the abode of Himavat and urge the excellent mountain and his wife. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
कथनीयं प्रयत्नेन वचनं वेदसम्मितम् ।
सर्वथा करणीयं तद्यथा स्यात्कार्यमुत्तमम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Speak out the words as venerable as the Vedas. Do everything necessary to get the matter straightened out and settled. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्वाहं कर्तुमिच्छामि तत्पुत्र्या सह सत्तमाः ।
स्वीकृतस्तद्विवाहो मे वरो दत्तश्च तादृशः ॥ ३६॥

English
O excellent ones, I wish to marry their daughter. I have agreed to marry her and have already granted her that boon. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र किं बहुनोक्तेन बोधनीयो हिमालयः ।
तथा मेना च बोद्धव्या देवानां स्याद्धितं यथा ॥ ३७॥

English
What is the use of talking too much? Himavat must be convinced. Menā too must be convinced similarly, so that the purpose of the gods shall be served well. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
भवद्भिः कल्पितो यो वै विधिः स्यादधिकस्ततः ।
भवताञ्चैव कार्यं तु भवन्तः कार्यभागिनः ॥ ३८॥

English
Whatever mode is selected by you shall be more than necessary. The task is yours. You alone are the sharers of the credit. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा मुनयस्तेऽमलाशयाः ।
आनन्दं लेभिरे सर्वे प्रभुणानुग्रहीकृताः ॥ ३९॥

English
# Brahmā said :—  On hearing these words, the seven sages of pure mind became delighted and thought themselves blessed by the lord. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
वयं धन्या अभूवंश्च कृतकृत्याश्च सर्वथा ।
वन्द्या जाताश्च सर्वेषां पूजनीया विशेषतः ॥ ४०॥

English
"We have become blessed and contented in every respect. We have become venerable to every one, especially adorable. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणा विष्णुना यो वै वन्द्यः सर्वार्थसाधकः ।
सोऽस्मान्प्रेषयते प्रेष्यान्कार्ये लोकसुखावहे ॥ ४१॥

English
He who is worthy of being respected by Brahmā and Viṣṇu, he who secures everything accomplished is sending us, his emissaries on an errand that is conducive to the happiness of all the worlds. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं वै जगतां स्वामी पिता सा जननी मता ।
अयं युक्तश्च सम्बन्धो वर्द्धतां चन्द्रवत्सदा ॥ ४२॥

English
He is the master of the worlds and their father. She is considered the mother. Let this proper alliance increase for ever like the moon". (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ऋषयो दिव्या नमस्कृत्य शिवं तदा ।
गता आकाशमार्गेण यत्रास्ति हिमवत्पुरम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Saying thus the celestial sages bowed to Śiva and went by aerial path in the direction of the city of Himavat. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तां च पुरीं दिव्यां ऋषयस्तेऽतिविस्मिताः ।
वर्णयन्तश्च स्वं पुण्यमब्रुवन्वै परस्परम् ॥ ४४॥

English
On seeing that city of heavenly splendour, the sages were surprised. Expatiating on their good fortune they spoke to one another. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।

English
# The sages said :—  We are really blessed and meritorious in being able to see this city⁵⁰ because we have been engaged in a task like this. (45.)

[fn 50. Himavatpura is probably identical with Auṣadhiprastha, the capital of Himavat. See Note 10. P. 490.]


---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Slabs of solar and lunar stones are found in every house. Different kinds of celestial trees are also growing here. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The splendour of festoons is also seen in every house. They are of different colours and sorts with shapes of parrots and swans carved on the walls of the palaces. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The canopies with hanging festoons are of diverse character. There are many lakes and ponds. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The gardens and parks are of various kinds frequented by delighted people. Here men are like gods and the women are like the celestial damsels. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

English
In the land of activities (i.e. Bhārata), the sacrificial priests and the followers of Purāṇas perform holy rites with a desire to attain heaven. That is in vain because they have left off the city of Himavat. (52.)

Sanskrit
यावन्न दृष्टमेतच्च तावत्स्वर्गपरा नराः ।
दृष्ट्मेतद्यदा विप्राः किं स्वर्गेण प्रयोजनम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Men are eager to go to heaven only as long as this city is not seen. O brahmins, when this city is seen what is the use of heaven? (53.)

Sanskrit

English
Brahmā said :— Describing the city thus all those excellent sages went to the rich and well-furnished abode of Himavat. (54.)

Sanskrit

English
On seeing those seven sages, resplendent like the sun, coming along the aerial path from a distance, Himavat was surprised and said :— (55.)

Sanskrit

English
Himavat said :—  The seven venerable persons, resplendent like the sun, are approaching me. These sages shall be worshipped by me now. (56.)

Sanskrit

English
We householders are really blessed, to whom great men like these, bestowing happiness on all, pay their visit. (57.)

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  In the meantime they descended on the ground from the sky. On seeing them Himavat advanced to welcome them. (58.)

Sanskrit

English
With palms joined in reverence he bowed to them with stooping shoulders and worshipped them with due respect and honour. (59.)

English
Desiring welfare of others, the seven sages embraced Himavat, the lord of mountains and spoke words of auspicious blessings with pleasant faces. (60.)

Sanskrit

English
Keeping them ahead he said—“My household life is blessed”. With great devotion he got and offered them seats. (61.)

Sanskrit

English
When they were duly seated, he too sat with their permission. Then Himavat spoke to the refulgent sages :— (62.)

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
धन्यो हि कृतकृत्योऽहं सफलं जीवितं मम ।
लोकेषु दर्शनीयोऽहं बहुतीर्थसमो मतः ॥ ६३॥

यस्माद्भवन्तो मद्गेहमागता विष्णुरूपिणः ।

English
*Himavat said :—*  I am blessed. I am contented. My life is fruitful. I am the best person worthy of being seen in the three worlds. I am as pure as any of the holy centres. All this is because you, verily in lord Viṣṇu’s forms, have come to my abode. Perfect ones such as you, what special purpose can there be in visiting poor persons like me? (63-64.)

Sanskrit

English
Still I am your servant. Some task there may be to be entrusted to me. Mercifully may it be spoken out. May my life be fruitful. (65.)
