### **CHAPTER THIRTYTHREE - *(The appeasement of Himavat)***

---

Sanskrit
३३

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
जगत्पिता शिवः प्रोक्तो जगन्माता शिवा मता ।
तस्माद्देया त्वया कन्या शङ्कराय महात्मने ॥ १॥

English
*The sages said :—*  Śiva is the father of the universe. Pārvatī is the mother of the universe. Hence your daughter shall be given to Śiva, the supreme soul. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वा हिमगिरे सार्थकं ते भवेज्जनुः ।
जगद्गुरोर्गुरुस्त्वं हि भविष्यसि न संशयः ॥ २॥

English
O Himālaya, by this activity your life will be fruitful. You will become the venerable person of the most venerable in the universe. There is no doubt about it. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं वचनमाकर्ण्य सप्तर्षीणां मुनीश्वर ।
प्रणम्य तान्करौ बद्ध्वा गिरिराजोऽब्रवीदिदम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O great sage, on hearing these words of the seven sages, the lord of the mountains bowed to them with joined palms and spoke thus. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
सप्तर्षयो महाभागा भवद्भिर्यदुदीरितम् ।
तत्प्रमाणीकृतं मे हि पुरैव गिरिशेच्छया ॥ ४॥

English
*Himavat said :—*  O ye seven sages of great enlightenment, what you have just now said has been already cherished by me by the will of Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीमेक आगत्य विप्रो वैष्णवधर्मवान् ।
शिवमुद्दिश्य सुप्रीत्या विपरीतं वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥ ५॥

English
Now, a certain brahmin professing Vaiṣṇava cult came here and spoke very critically about Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तदारभ्य शिवामाता ज्ञानभ्रष्टा बभूव ह ।
सुताविवाहं रुद्रेण योगिना तेन नेच्छति ॥ ६॥

English
Ever since, the mother Pārvatī has gone out of sense. Hence she does not wish her daughter's marriage with Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कोपागारमगात्सा हि सुतप्ता मलिनाम्बरा ।
कृत्वा महाहठं विप्रा बोध्यमानापि नाबुधत् ॥ ७॥

English
She has entered the chamber of anger. She is aggrieved and her clothes have become dirty. O brahmins, her obduracy is so great that she does not pay heed to any advice. (7.)

---

English
I too am, you can say, out of sense. I am telling you the truth. I do not wish to give my daughter to Śiva who is apparently a mendicant. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं च ज्ञानविभ्रष्टो जातोऽहं सत्यमीर्यते ।
दातुं सुतां महेशाय नेच्छामि भिक्षुरूपिणे ॥

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा शैलराजस्तु शिवमायाविमोहितः ।
तूष्णीं बभूव तत्रस्थो मुनीनां मध्यतो मुने ॥ ९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O sage, after saying these words, the king of mountains deluded by Śiva's magic became silent and sat amidst the sages. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे सप्तर्षयस्ते हि शिवमायां प्रशस्य वै ।
प्रेषयामासुरथ तां मेनकां प्रत्यरुन्धतीम् ॥ १०॥

English
The seven celestial sages praised the magic of Śiva and sent Arundhatī$^{55}$ to Menakā. (10.)

[fn 55. She is the wife of Vasiṣṭha, one of the seven sages. In Hindu Mythology she is regarded as the highest pattern of conjugal excellence and wifely devotion.]
---

Sanskrit
अथ पत्युः समादाय निदेशं ज्ञानदा हि सा ।
जगामारुन्धती तूर्णं यत्र मेना च पार्वती ॥ ११॥

English
Then at the bidding of her husband Arundhatī, the bestower of knowledge, went quickly to the place where Menā and Pārvatī were sitting. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा ददर्श मेनां तां शयानां शोकमूर्च्छिताम् ।
उवाच मधुरं साध्वी सावधाना हितं वचः ॥ १२॥

English
After going in she saw Menā lying in her grief. The chaste lady spoke to her these carefully selected sweet and wholesome words :— (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं ददौ शिवायै स तपन्त्यै तात शङ्करः ।
न हीश्वरप्रतिज्ञातं विपरीताय कल्पते ॥ ५५॥

English
O dear one, Śiva has already granted Pārvatī the boon at the time of her penance. A promise of Śiva cannot be turned topsyturvy. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अरुन्धत्युवाच ।
उत्तिष्ठ मेनके साध्वि त्वद्गृहेऽहमरुन्धती ।
आगता मुनयश्चापि सप्तायाताः कृपालवः ॥ १३॥

English
*Arundhati said:—*  O Menakā, get up. O chaste lady, I, Arundhati, have come to your house. The seven sages of sympathetic nature have also come. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अरुन्धतीस्वरं श्रुत्वा शीघ्रमुत्थाय मेनका ।
उवाच शिरसा नत्वा तां पद्मामिव तेजसा ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing Arundhati’s voice, Menā got up quickly and bowed to her who was on a par with Lakṣmī in her brilliance. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
अहोऽद्य किमिदं पुण्यमस्माकं पुण्यजन्मनाम् ।
वधूर्जगद्विधेः पत्नी वसिष्ठस्यागतेह वै ॥ १५॥

English
*Menā said:—*  Ha, what a meritorious thing is this! We are blessed. Arundhati, the daughter-in-law of the Creator of the universe, the wife of Vasiṣṭha, has come here. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
किमर्थमागता देवि तन्मे ब्रूहि विशेषतः ।
अहं दासीसमा ते हि ससुता करुणां कुरु ॥ १६॥

English
O gentle lady, what for is your visit now? Please tell me specifically. My daughter and I are your slaves. Be merciful to us. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा मेनका साध्वी बोधयित्वा च तां बहु ।
तत्रागता च सुप्रीत्या यत्र सप्तर्षयोऽपि ते ॥ १७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Arundhati, the chaste lady thus addressed, advised her in various ways and returned to the place where the sages were seated. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शैलेश्वरं ते च बोधयामासुरादरात् ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदद्वन्द्वं सर्वे वाक्यविशारदाः ॥ १८॥

English
Then they began to advise the lord of the mountains, after thinking on the feet of Śiva. They were clever in speech and they spoke respectfully. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
शैलेन्द्र श्रूयतां वाक्यमस्माकं शुभकारणम् ।
शिवाय पार्वतीं देहि संहर्तुः श्वशुरो भव ॥ १९॥

English
*The sages said:—*  O lord of the mountains, may our words, the cause of everything auspicious, be heard. Give Pārvatī to Śiva. Become the father-in-law of the world-destroyer. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अयाचितारं सर्वेशं प्रार्थयामास यत्नतः ।
तारकस्य विनाशाय ब्रह्मा सम्बन्धकर्मणि ॥ २०॥

English
For the destruction of Tāraka, formerly Brahmā requested Śiva who is the lord of all and who does not beg of any one, to strive for this alliance. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नोत्सुको दारसंयोगे शङ्करो योगिनां वरः ।
विधेः प्रार्थनया देवस्तव कन्यां ग्रहीष्यति ॥ २१॥

English
Śiva, the foremost of Yogins was not eager to marry. But since requested by Brahmā, the lord agreed to take your daughter. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दुहितुस्ते तपस्तप्तं प्रतिज्ञानं चकार सा ।
हेतुद्वयेन योगीन्द्रो विवाहं च करिष्यति ॥ २२॥

English
Pārvatī performed a penance and the lord promised her. Thus for these two reasons the lord of Yogins wishes to marry her. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ऋषीणां वचनं श्रुत्वा प्रहस्य स हिमालयः ।
उवाच किञ्चिद्भीतस्तु परं विनयपूर्वकम् ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of the sages, Himavat laughed but he was a little frightened. He spoke with humility. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
शिवस्य राजसामग्रीं न हि पश्यामि काञ्चन ।
कञ्चिदाश्रयमैश्वर्यं कं वा स्वजनबान्धवम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Himavat said :—*  I do not see any royal paraphernalia with Śiva, He has none to support him. He has no assets. He has no kinsman. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नेच्छाम्यति विनिर्लिप्तयोगिने स्वां सुतामहम् ।
यूयं वेदविधातुश्च पुत्रा वदत निश्चितम् ॥ २५॥

English
I do not wish to give my daughter to a Yogin who is extremely detached. O ye sons of the Creator of the Vedas tell me decisively. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
वरायाननुरूपाय पिता कन्यां ददाति चेत् ।
कामान्मोहाद्भयाल्लोभात्स नष्टो नरकं व्रजेत् ॥ २६॥

English
If a father were to give his daughter in marriage to an unsuitable person, out of love, delusion, fear or covetousness, he is doomed. He will go to hell. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

यद्विधानं भवेद्योग्यमृषयस्तद्विधीयताम् ॥ २७॥

English
Out of my own free will, I will not give her to the trident-bearing Śiva. O sages, whatever arrangement is befitting here, may kindly be carried out. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य हिमागस्य मुनीश्वर ।
प्रत्युवाच वसिष्ठस्तं तेषां वाक्यविशारदः ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O excellent sages, on hearing these words of the mountain Himavat, Vasiṣṭha$^{56}$, the most eloquent among them replied :— (28.)

[fn 56. Vasiṣṭha was one of the seven great sages and one of the ten Prajāpatis. There are several accounts about his origin. He is declared to have been either a mind-born son of Brahmā or the son of Mitra and Varuṇa from Urvaśī.]
---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
श‍ृणु शैलेश मद्वाक्यं सर्वथा ते हितावहम् ।
धर्माविरुद्धं सत्यं च परत्रेह मुदावहम् ॥ २९॥

English
*Vasiṣṭha said :—*  O lord of mountains, listen to my words in every respect conducive to your welfare; they are not against virtue. They are true and shall bring about your joy here and hereafter. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

सर्वं जानाति शास्त्रज्ञो निर्मलज्ञानचक्षुषा ॥ ३०॥

English
Statements, in ordinary language and in the Vedas, are of three forms. A scholar knowing all lores understands them by means of his pure vision of knowledge. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

सुबुद्धिर्वक्ति शत्रुर्हि हितं नैव कदाचन ॥ ३१॥

English
It is only an enemy, though keen in intellect, who says what is pleasing to the ears now but what transpires to be untrue and unwholesome afterwards. He never speaks wholesome things. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
आदावप्रीतिजनकं परिणामे सुखावहम् ।
दयालुर्धर्मशीलो हि बोधयत्येव बान्धवः ॥ ३२॥

English
Only a virtuous and sympathetic friend will speak such words as are unpleasant in the beginning but conducive to happiness in the end. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
But the third variety of behaviour nectarlike to the ears, conducive to happiness on all occasions, essential and truthful is considered to be the most excellent. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

कथ्यतां त्रिषु मध्ये किं ब्रुवे वाक्यं त्वदीप्सितम् ॥ ३४॥

English
O mountain, these are the three types of behaviour as mentioned in the treatises on polity. Tell me which type of behaviour shall I adopt to please you. (34.)

---

English
Śiva, the lord of gods, is devoid of riches created by Brahmā. But His mind is engrossed in the ocean of true knowledge. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

तत्त्वज्ञानसमुद्रेषु सन्निमग्नैकमानसः । ३५॥

ज्ञानानन्दस्येश्वरस्य ब्राह्मवस्तुषु का स्पृहा ।
गृही ददाति स्वसुतां राज्यसम्पत्तिशालिने ॥ ३६॥

English
How can lord Śiva who is knowledge-Bliss Himself have any desire for articles created by Brahmā ? An ordinary householder gives his daughter to one who has a kingdom and riches in his possession ? (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
By offering his daughter to a miserable person, a father may be guilty of slaughtering his daughter. Who can think Śiva miserable whose servant is Kubera ? (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He is attributeless, supreme soul, great lord and greater than Prakṛti. He can create and annihilate things by a mere sportive touch of His eyebrows. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
His manifestations are threefold, He is the cause of creation sustenance and annihilation in the names of Brahmā Viṣṇu and Śiva. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Brahmā stays in Brahmaloka, Viṣṇu in the milk ocean, Śiva in Kailāsa, all these are the attributes of Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The primordial nature, born of Śiva, maintains threefold forms in the creative activity, partially out of sport with diverse digits. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Vāṇī, the deity presiding over the activity of speech, is born of his mouth ; Lakṣmī, in the form of riches, is born out of his chest. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Pārvatī manifested herself in the splendours of the gods. After killing all the demons she granted riches and glory to the gods. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
In another Kalpa she was born of the womb of Dakṣa's wife. Her name was Satī. She attained Śiva. Dakṣa gave her to Him. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
By her Yogic power she cast off her body on hearing about the insult to her husband. She is now born of you in the womb of Menā. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
This Pārvatī is the wife of Śiva in every birth. In every Kalpa she is the great cosmic intellect, mother of wise men. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English
After consoling her and granting her the boon He returned to His abode. It was for complying with her request that Śiva requested you for the hand of Śivā, O mountain. (50.)

English
Both of you had accepted the proposal as you were drawn by devotion to Śiva. O lord of mountains, how is it that your mind has taken a somersault now? Please tell me. (51.)

English
On being requested by the gods, the lord has sent us, the sages and Arundhatī to you. (52.)

Sanskrit

एवङ्कृते महानन्दो भविष्यति गिरे तव ॥ ५३॥

English
O mountain, we instruct you plainly. By giving Pārvatī to Śiva you will meet with great bliss. (53.)

Sanskrit
शिवां शिवाय शैलेन्द्र स्वेच्छया चेन्न दास्यसि ।
भविता तद्विवाहोऽत्र भवितव्यबलेन हि ॥ ५४॥

English
O lord of mountains, even if you do not give Pārvatī to Śiva out of your own free will, their marriage will take place as a result of the inexorable workings of fate. (54.)

Sanskrit
अहो प्रतिज्ञा दुर्लङ्घ्या साधूनामीशवर्तिनाम् ।
सर्वेषां जगतां मध्ये किमीशस्य पुनर्गिरे ॥ ५६॥

English
Oh! even the promise of ordinary good men acting under the guidance of Śiva cannot be transgressed in all the worlds. O mountain, what then about that of Śiva Himself. (56.)

Sanskrit

पार्वती लीलया मेरोः श‍ृङ्गभङ्गं चकार च ॥ ५७॥

English
Working singlehanded, Lord Indra chopped off the wings of mountains as though at play. Pārvatī too sportively broke the peak of Meru. (57.)

Sanskrit
एकार्थे नहि शैलेश नश्याः सर्वा हि सम्पदः ।
एकं त्यजेत्कुलस्यार्थे श्रुतिरेषा सनातनी ॥ ५८॥

English
All riches can be sacrificed, O lord of mountains, for the sake of a single entity, but the eternal Śruti has it that one should forsake a single entity for the sake of a unit. (58.)

Sanskrit

English
When danger was imminent at the hands of a brahmin, the chief of kings, Anaraṇya$^{57}$, saved his entire asset by giving his daughter to him. (59.)

[fn 57. According to ŚP. RS. Section III ch. 34, the King Anaraṇya belonged to the lineage of the fourteenth Manu called Indra-Sāvarni. He is said to have performed a hundred horse-sacrifices with Bhṛgu as his officiating priest but did not accept Indra-hood.]

Sanskrit

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

English

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
कस्य वंशोद्भवो ब्रह्मन् अनरण्यो नृपश्च सः ।
सुतां दत्त्वा स च कथं ररक्षाखिलसम्पदः ॥ ६३॥

English
*Himavat said :—*  O brahmin, what is the race to which the king Anaraṇya belonged ? How did he save his assets by giving his daughter ? (63.)

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of the mountain, Vasiṣṭha became glad and told him the details of the interesting story of the king. (64.)
