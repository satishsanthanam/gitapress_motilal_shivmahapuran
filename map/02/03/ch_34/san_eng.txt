### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - *(The Story of Anaraṇya)***

---

Sanskrit
३४

वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
मनोर्वंशोद्भवो राजा सोऽनरण्यो नृपेश्वरः ।
इन्द्रसावर्णिसंज्ञस्य चतुर्दशमितस्य हि ॥ १॥

English
*Vasiṣṭha said :—*  The king Anaraṇya hailed from the race of the fourteenth Manu Indrasāvarni. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अनरण्यो नृपश्रेष्ठः सप्तद्वीपमहीपतिः ।
शम्भुभक्तो विशेषेण मङ्गलारण्यजो बली ॥ २॥

English
The great king Anaraṇya, born of Maṅgalāraṇya* was very strong. He was a special devotee of Śiva and ruled over the seven continents⁶⁸. (2.)


[fn *. Contrast Pargiter AIHS PP. 143, 246. Anaraṇya was born of Sambhūta.]

[fn 58. It is a division of the terrestrial world. The number of these divisions varies according to different authorities. It is usually seven. These are situated round the mountain Meru like the petals of a lotus flower and each being separated from the other by a distinct ocean. The central one is Jambudvīpa in which is included Bharata Khaṇḍa (India).]
---

Sanskrit
भृगुं पुरोधसं कृत्वा शतं यज्ञांश्चकार सः ।
न स्वीचकार शक्रत्वं दीयमानं सुरैरपि ॥ ३॥

English
Having Bhrgu as his priest he performed a hundred sacrifices. He did not accept the position of Indra even when offered by the gods. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवुः शतपुत्राश्च राज्ञस्तस्य हिमालय ।
कन्यैका सुन्दरी नाम्ना पद्मा पद्मालया समा ॥ ४॥

English
O Himavat, hundred sons were born to him and a beautiful daughter Padmā who was equal to Lakṣmī. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यः स्नेहः पुत्रशतके कन्यायाञ्च ततोऽधिकः ।
नृपस्य तस्य तस्यां हि बभूव नगसत्तम ॥ ५॥

English
O excellent mountain, he was more fond of his daughter than of his hundred sons. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राणाधिका प्रियतमा महिष्यः सर्वयोषितः ।
नृपस्य पत्न्यः पञ्चासन् सर्वाः सौभाग्यसंयुताः ॥ ६॥

English
He had five queens who were endowed with great qualities and fortunes and were loved by him over and above his life. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सा कन्या यौवनस्था च बभूव स्वपितुर्गृहे ।
पत्रं प्रस्थापयामास सुवरानयनाय सः ॥ ७॥

English
The girl entered the prime of her youth in her father's palace. The king issued letters of invitation for the requisition of good bridegrooms. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा पिप्पलादर्षिर्गन्तुं स्वाश्रममुत्सुकः ।
तपःस्थाने निर्जने च गन्धर्वं स ददर्श ह ॥ ८॥

English
In the meantime the sage Pippalāda eagerly hastening back to his hermitage saw a certain Gandharva in an isolated place in the penance-grove. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीयुतं मग्नचित्तं च श‍ृङ्गारे रससागरे ।
विहरन्तं महाप्रेम्णा कामशास्त्रविशारदम् ॥ ९॥

English
The Gandharva was an expert in the science of erotics. He was in the company of a woman. He was therefore completely submerged in the ocean of pleasure, sexual dalliance and was lusty. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तं मुनिशार्दूलः सकामः सम्बभूव सः ।
तपःस्वदत्तचित्तश्चाचिन्तयद्दारसङ्ग्रहम् ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing him the great sage became very lustful. He lost interest in penance and began to think of acquiring a wife. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एवंवृत्तस्य तस्यैव पिप्पलादस्य सन्मुनेः ।
कियत्कालो गतस्तत्र कामोन्मथितचेतसः ॥ ११॥

English
Thus the good sage spent a long time with his mind utterly agitated by pangs of love. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा पुष्पभद्रायां स्नातुं गच्छन्मुनीश्वरः ।
ददर्श पद्मां युवतीं पद्मामिव मनोरमाम् ॥ १२॥

English
Once while the good sage was on his way to the river Puṣpabhadrā** for taking his bath he happened to see the young maiden Padmā who was as charming as goddess Lakṣmī. (12.)

[fn **. It has not been possible to identify this river.]

---

Sanskrit
केयं कन्येति पप्रच्छ समीपस्थाञ्जनान्मुनिः ।
जना निवेदयाञ्चक्रुर्नत्वा शापनियन्त्रिताः ॥ १३॥

English
The sage asked the persons standing by—"Who is this girl?" The people, afraid of the curse bowed to the sage and replied. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
जना ऊचुः ।
अनरण्यसुतेयं वै पद्मा नाम रमापरा ।
वरारोहा प्रार्थ्यमाना नृपश्रेष्ठैर्गुणालया ॥ १४॥

English
*The people said :—*  This excellent lady, the repository of all good qualities, is the daughter of Anaraṇya and is called Padmā. She is another Lakṣmī (goddess of fortune). She is being wooed by great kings. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा स मुनिर्वाक्यं जनानां तथ्यवादिनाम् ।
चुक्षोभातीव मनसि तल्लिप्सुरभवच्च सः ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of the people who spoke the truth, the sage became much agitated in the mind and was eager to possess her. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिः स्नात्वाभीष्टदेवं सम्पूज्य विधिवच्छिवम् ।
जगाम कामी भिक्षार्थमनरण्यसभां गिरे ॥ १६॥

English
O mountain, the sage took bath and worshipped his favourite deity Śiva duly. The lustful sage went to the council-chamber of Anaraṇya for the sake of alms. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा शीघ्रं मुनिं दृष्ट्वा प्रणनाम भयाकुलः ।
मधुपर्कादिकं दत्त्वा पूजयामास भक्तितः ॥ १७॥

English
Immediately after seeing the sage, the king was struck with awe and bowed to him. He offered him homage (Madhuparka)* and devoutly worshipped him. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
कामात्सर्वं गृहीत्वा च ययाचे कन्यकां मुनिः ।
मौनी बभूव नृपतिः किञ्चिन्निर्वक्तुमक्षमः ॥ १८॥

English
Out of love, the sage accepted everything and ultimately requested for the hand of his daughter. The king kept quiet, being unable to give any decisive reply. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिर्ययाचे कन्यां स तां देहीति नृपेश्वर ।
अन्यथा भस्मसात्सर्वं करिष्यामि क्षणेन च ॥ १९॥

English
The sage repeated his request saying—“O great king, give me your daughter. Otherwise in a trice I will reduce everything to ashes”. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे बभूवुराच्छन्ना गणास्तत्तेजसा मुने ।
रुदोद राजा सगणो दृष्ट्वा विप्रं जरातुरम् ॥ २०॥

English
The king and his attendants were overwhelmed by the splendour of the sage. Staring at the old emaciated brahmin, they began to cry. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
महिष्यो रुरुदुस्सर्वा इतिकर्तव्यताक्षमाः ।
मूर्च्छामाप महाराज्ञी कन्यामाता शुचाकुला ॥ २१॥

English
The queens, knowing not what shall be done, lamented. The chief queen, the mother of the girl, fell unconscious in the excess of her grief. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवुस्तनयाः सर्वे शोकाकुलितमानसाः ।
सर्वं शोकाकुलं जातं नृपसम्बन्धि शैलप ॥ २२॥

English
The brothers of the girl were agitated with sorrow. O lord of mountains, everything and every one connected with the king was overwhelmed with grief. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे प्राज्ञो द्विजो गुरुरनुत्तमः ।
पुरोहितश्च मतिमान् आगतो नृपसन्निधिम् ॥ २३॥

English
In the meantime the wise brahmin, the excellent preceptor of the king, as well as his intelligent priest came there. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा प्रणम्य सम्पूज्य रुदोद च तयोः पुरः ।
सर्वं निवेदयाञ्चक्रे पप्रच्छोचितमाशु तत् ॥ २४॥

English
The king bowed to them and paid homage. He cried before them. He explained to them everything and asked them what was the proper step to be taken immediately. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ राज्ञो गुरुर्विप्रः पण्डितश्च पुरोहितः ।
अपि द्वौ शास्त्रनीतिज्ञौ बोधयामासतुर्नृपम् ॥ २५॥

शोकाकुलांश्च महिषीर्नृपबालांश्च कन्यकाम् ।
उत्तमां नीतिमादृत्य सर्वेषां हितकारिणीम् ॥ २६॥

English
The brahmin, the preceptor of the king and the scholarly priest were experts in sacred lore and polity. They advised the king in that matter. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुपुरोधसावूचतुः
श‍ृणु राजन्महाप्राज्ञ वचो नौ सद्धितावहम् ।
मा शुचः सपरीवारः शास्त्रे कुरु मतिं सतीम् ॥ २७॥

English
*The preceptor and the priest said :—*  O wise king, listen to our beneficial words. Do not be anxious. In the company of your kinsmen turn your good attention to the sacred texts. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्य वाब्ददिनान्ते वा दातव्या कन्यका नृप ।
पात्राय विप्रायान्यस्मै कस्मैचिद्वा विशेषतः ॥ २८॥

English
O king, whether today or after a year, the princess is to be given to a deserving person, a brahmin or anyone else. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्पात्रं ब्राह्मणादन्यं न पश्यावो जगत्त्रये ।
सुतां दत्त्वा च मुनये रक्ष स्वां सर्वसम्पदम् ॥ २९॥

English
In the three worlds we do not see more deserving person than this brahmin. Give your daughter to this sage and save your riches. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
राजन्नेकनिमित्तेन सर्वसम्पद्विनश्यति ।
सर्वं रक्षति तं त्यक्त्वा विना तं शरणागतम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O king, if all riches face the danger of destruction due to one object or person, the wise man saves everything by abandoning that object or person unless it be that who has sought refuge. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
राजा प्राज्ञवचः श्रुत्वा विलप्य च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
कन्यां सालङ्कृतां कृत्वा मुनीन्द्राय ददौ किल ॥ ३१॥

English
*Vasisṭha said :—*  On hearing the words of the wise, the king lamented again and again but ultimately offered his daughter fully bedecked in ornaments to the excellent sage. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कान्तां गृहीत्वा स मुनिर्विवाह्य विधिवद्गिरे ।
पद्मां पद्मोपमां तां वै मुदितःस्वालयं ययौ ॥ ३२॥

English
O mountain, accepting and marrying the beautiful maiden Padmā, on a par with goddess Lakṣmī, in accordance with holy laws, the delighted sage returned to his abode. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा सर्वान्परित्यज्य दत्त्वा वृद्धाय चात्मजाम् ।
ग्लानिं चित्ते समाधाय जगाम तपसे वनम् ॥ ३३॥

English
After giving his daughter to an old man, the king was much dejected in mind. Abandoning everything he went to the forest for performing penance. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्भार्यापि वनं याते प्राणनाथे तदा गिरे ।
भर्तुश्च दुहितुः शोकात्प्राणांस्तत्याज सुन्दरी ॥ ३४॥

English
O mountain, when the king went to the forest, the queen, passed away, due to the pangs of separation from her husband and daughter. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पूज्याः पुत्राश्च भृत्याश्च मूर्च्छामापुर्नृपं विना ।
शुशुचुः श्वाससंयुक्ता ज्ञात्वा सर्वेऽपरे जनाः ॥ ३५॥

English
Without the king, the respectable sons and officers of the king became unconscious. The other people thinking that the king was dead lamented much. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अनरण्यो वनं गत्वा तपस्तप्त्वाति शङ्करम् ।
समाराध्य ययौ भक्त्या शिवलोकमनामयम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Anaraṇya went to the forest, performed great penance, and worshipped Śiva with devotion. In the end, he attained Śivaloka free from all ailments. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
नृपस्य कीर्तिमान्नाम्ना ज्येष्ठपुत्रोऽथ धार्मिकः ।
पुत्रवत्पालयामास प्रजा राज्यं चकार ह ॥ ३७॥

English
The eldest son of the king, Kīrtimān, virtuously ruled over the kingdom and tended the subjects like his own children. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कथितं शैलानरण्यचरितं शुभम् ।
कन्यां दत्त्वा यथारक्षद्वंशं चाप्यखिलं धनम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus, O mountain, I have narrated to you the auspicious story of Anaraṇya, how he saved his race and his wealth by offering his daughter to the sage. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलराज त्वमप्येवं सुतां दत्त्वा शिवाय च ।
रक्ष सर्वकुलं सर्वान्वशान्कुरु सुरानपि ॥ ३९॥

English
O king of mountains, you too, give your daughter to Śiva, save the entire family and keep even the gods under your control. (39.)
