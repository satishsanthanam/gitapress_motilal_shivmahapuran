### **CHAPTER THIRTYFIVE - *(The story of Padmā and Pippalāda)***

---

Sanskrit
३५

नारद उवाच ।
अनरण्यस्य चरितं सुतादानसमन्वितम् ।
श्रुत्वा गिरिवरस्तात किं चकार च तद्वद ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O dear, what did the excellent mountain do after hearing the anecdote of Anaraṇya and the marriage of his daughter? Please tell me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अनरण्यस्य चरितं कन्यादानसमन्वितम् ।
श्रुत्वा पप्रच्छ शैलेशो वसिष्ठं साञ्जलिः पुनः ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After hearing the story of Anaraṇya including the anecdote of the offer of his daughter, the lord of mountains again asked Vasiṣṭha with palms joined in reverence. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलेश उवाच ।
वसिष्ठ मुनिशार्दूल ब्रह्मपुत्र कृपानिधे ।
अनरण्यचरित्रन्ते कथितं परमाद्भुतम् ॥ ३॥

English
*The lord of mountains said:—*  O leading sage Vasiṣṭha, O son of Brahmā, O merciful one, you have narrated the wonderful story of Anaraṇya. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अनरण्यसुता यस्मात् पिप्पलादं मुनिं पतिम् ।
सम्प्राप्य किमकार्षीत्सा तच्चरित्रं मुदावहम् ॥ ४॥

English
What did Padmā, the daughter of Anaraṇya, do after marrying sage Pippalāda. Please mention her story fully.



5-6 *Vasiṣṭha said:—*  The very old and venerable sage Pippalāda returned to his hermitage along with his wife Padmā and passed time in pleasure. He was not too much sensuous. He continued to perform his penance and holy rites in the forest and on the mountain. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथानरण्यकन्या सा सिषेवे भक्तितो मुनिम् ।
कर्मणा मनसा वाचा लक्ष्मीर्नारायणं यथा ॥ ७॥

English
The daughter of Anaraṇya served the sage devoutly physically, mentally and verbally like Lakṣmī serving Viṣṇu. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा स्वर्णदीं स्नातुं गच्छन्तीं सुस्मितां च ताम् ।
ददर्श पथि धर्मश्च मायया नृपरूपधृक् ॥ ८॥

English
Once Dharma (Virtue) assumed the guise of a king by his magical power and happened to see on the way that lady of gentle smiles going to the celestial river for her holy dip.⁵⁹ (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चारुरत्नरथस्थश्च नानालङ्कारभूषितः ।
नवीनयौवनश्श्रीमान्कामदेवसमप्रभः ॥ ९॥

दृष्ट्वा तां सुन्दरीं पद्मामुवाच स वृषो विभुः ।
विज्ञातुं भावमन्तःस्थं तस्याश्च मुनियोषितः ॥ १०॥

English
The lord Dharma was seated in a beautiful chariot studded with gems. He was bedecked in many kinds of ornaments. He was in the prime of fresh youth, glorious and lustrous like the cupid. On seeing Padmā he spoke thus, in order to know the innermost feelings of the sage's wife. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्म उवाच ।
अयि सुन्दरि लक्ष्मीर्वै राजयोग्ये मनोहरे ।
अतीव यौवनस्थे च कामिनि स्थिरयौवने ॥ ११॥

English
*Dharma said :—*  O beautiful woman, you are Lakṣmī herself; you are charming, you are worthy of a king; you are in the very prime of youth; you will be ever young; you are a lovely sweet lady. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
जरातुरस्य वृद्धस्य पिप्पलादस्य वै मुनेः ।
सत्यं वदामि तन्वङ्गि समीपे नैव राजसे ॥ १२॥

English
I am telling you the truth, O slender-limbed lady. You lack lustre and colour in the presence of the sage Pippalāda who is old and weak. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्रं तपस्सु निरतं निर्घृणं मरणोन्मुखम् ।
त्यक्त्वा मां पश्य राजेन्द्रं रतिशूरं स्मरातुरम् ॥ १३॥

English
Cast off that ruthless old brahmin always engaged in penances. Look up to me a great king, heroic in sexual dalliance and agitated by Kāma. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्नोति सुन्दरी पुण्यात्सौन्दर्यं पूर्वजन्मनः ।
सफलं तद्भवेत्सर्वं रसिकालिङ्गनेन च ॥ १४॥

English
A beautiful woman acquires beauty as a result of the merit of a previous birth. The beauty becomes completely fruitful only after embracing a man of aesthetic taste. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रसुन्दरीकान्तं कामशास्त्रविशारदम् ।
किङ्करं कुरु मां कान्ते सम्परित्यज्य तं पतिम् ॥ १५॥

English
I am the lover of a thousand beautiful women. I am an expert in the erotic science and literature. Abandon that husband and make me your slave. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्जने कानने रम्ये शैले शैले नदीतटे ।
विहरस्व मया सार्धं जन्मेदं सफलं कुरु ॥ १६॥

English
You can indulge in sexual dalliance in the beautiful secluded forests, mountains and banks of rivers in my company. Make your life fruitful. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
इत्येवमुक्तवन्तं सा स्वरथादवरुह्य च ।
ग्रहीतुमुत्सुकं हस्ते तमुवाच पतिव्रता ॥ १७॥

English
*Vasiṣṭha said :—*  Saying this, he got down and was eager to catch her hands. The chaste lady then addressed him thus. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
पद्मोवाच ।
गच्छ दूरं गच्छ दूरं पापिष्ठस्त्वं नराधिप ।
मां चेत्पश्यसि कामेन सद्यो नष्टो भविष्यसि ॥ १८॥

English
*Padmā said :—*  Away, away, go away you sinful king. If you cast your lustful ogles at me you will be doomed in a trice. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पिप्पलादं मुनिश्रेष्ठं तपसा पूतविग्रहम् ।
त्यक्त्वा कथं भजेयं त्वां स्त्रीजितं रतिलम्पटम् ॥ १९॥

English
How can I resort to you, lecherous and mad after women, after forsaking the excellent sage Pippalāda whose body is sanctified by austerities? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीजितस्पर्शमात्रेण सर्वं पुण्यं प्रणश्यति ।
स्त्रीजितः परपापी च तद्दर्शनमघावहम् ॥ २०॥

English
By the very touch of a person under the influence of women all merits are destroyed. He is a great sinner. His very sight promotes sins. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्क्रियो ह्यशुचिर्नित्यं स पुमान् यः स्त्रिया जितः ।
निन्दन्ति पितरो देवा मानवाः सकलाश्च तम् ॥ २१॥

English
Even if he performs holy rites, a person succumbing to the viles of women is always impure. The manes, the gods and all men despise him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य किं ज्ञान सुतपोजपहोमप्रपूजनैः ।
विद्यया दानतः किं वा स्त्रीभिर्यस्य मनो हृतम् ॥ २२॥

English
Of what avail is knowledge, penance, repetition of sacred mantras, sacrifice, adoration, learning and charitable gift to him who is henpecked? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मातरं मां स्त्रियो भावं कृत्वा येन ब्रवीषि ह ।
भविष्यति क्षयस्तेन कालेन मम शापतः ॥ २३॥

English
Since you spoke to me viewing me with the feelings that I am your wife although I ought to have been viewed as your mother, you will have a gradual decline as a result of my curse. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
श्रुत्वा धर्मः सतीशापं नृपमूर्तिं विहाय च ।
धृत्वा स्वमूर्तिं देवेशः कम्पमान उवाच सः ॥ २४॥

English
*Vasisṭha said:—*  On hearing the curse of the chaste woman, O lord of mountains, Dharma cast off the guise of a king and assumed his real form. Tremblingly he spoke thus— (24.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्म उवाच ।
मातर्जानीहि मां धर्मं ज्ञानिनां च गुरोर्गुरुम् ।
परस्त्रीमातृबुद्धिं च कुर्वन्तं सततं सति ॥ २५॥

English
*Dharma said:—*  O mother, know me as Dharma elderly and venerable to men of wisdom and preceptors. O chaste lady, I always consider other's wives as mothers. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं तवान्तरं ज्ञातुमागतस्तव सन्निधिम् ।
तवाहञ्च मनो जाने तथापि विधिनोदितः ॥ २६॥

English
It was to know your innermost feelings that I approached you. I knew your mind, still I was urged by fate. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं मे दमनं साध्वि न विरुद्धं यथोचितम् ।
शास्तिः समुत्पथस्थानामीश्वरेण विनिर्मिता ॥ २७॥

English
Only proper suppression, not the contrary, is carried out by you. Chastisement of those who go astray from the right path is carried out by Śiva Himself. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

सम्पदं विपदं यो हि नमस्तस्मै शिवाय हि ॥ २८॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva who distributes happiness, misery, boons, prosperity or adversity on all. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

स्रष्टुं नष्टुं च यस्सृष्टिं नमस्तस्मै शिवाय हि ॥ २९॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva who can make people enemies or friends, create affection or quarrel, to generate or destroy things. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Obeisance to Śiva who has made milk white, who has bestowed chillness on water and heat on fire. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Obeisance to Śiva, by whom the primordial nature, the principles Mahat etc, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva and others are created. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

किञ्चिन्नोवाच चकितस्तत्पातिव्रत्यतोषितः ॥ ३२॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying thus Dharma, the most venerable god in the universe stood in front of her, stunned but delighted at her chastity. But he did not say anything. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Princess Padmā, the chaste beloved of Pippalāda, O mountain, was surprised on realising that it was Dharma and said. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Padmā said :—*  O Dharma, you are the ever present witness of all activities. O lord, why did you deign to deceive me to know my mind ? (34.)

---

Sanskrit

त्वं च शप्तो मयाज्ञानात्स्त्रीस्वभावाद् वृथा वृष ॥ ३५॥

English
O Dharma, what has been done already does not amount to any guilt on my part. You have been cursed in vain by me but it was due to my ignorance and innate nature of woman. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
I am now thinking as to what shall be done about it. May that idea strike me whereby I may get peace. (36.)

English
This sky, these quarters and the winds may get destroyed but the curse of a chaste lady will never be destroyed. (37.)

इत्येवमुक्त्वा सन्तस्थौ धर्मः स गिरिसत्तम ।

English
Vasisṭha said :— O excellent mountain, after saying thus, Dharma stood quiet there. She circumambulated him, bowed to him and returned to her house. (52.)

English
Bestowing blessings upon her, Dharma returned to his abode. He praised Padmā lovingly in every assembly he visited. (53.)

English
She sported about in secret with her husband who became a young man. She gave birth to sons who surpassed her husband in their good qualities. (54.)

Sanskrit
सप्ताहे समतीते तु दुर्लभेऽतिशुभे क्षणे ।
लग्नाधिपे च लग्नस्थे चन्द्रे स्वत्नयान्विते ॥ ५८॥

मुदिते रोहिणीयुक्ते विशुद्धे चन्द्रतारके ।
मार्गमासे चन्द्रवारे सर्वदोषविवर्जिते ॥ ५९॥

सर्वसद्ग्रहसंसृष्टेऽसद्ग्रहदृष्टिवर्जिते ।
सदपत्यप्रदे जीवे पतिसौभाग्यदायिनि ॥ ६०॥

जगदम्बां जगत्पित्रे मूलप्रकृतिमीश्वरीम् ।
कन्यां प्रदाय गिरिजां कृती त्वं भव पर्वत ॥ ६१॥

English
After a week there is a very auspicious hour very rare to meet with. The presiding planet of the lagna is in the lagna. The moon is in conjunction with his son, Mercury as well as the constellation Rohiṇī⁶³. The moon and the stars occupy pure positions. The month is Mārgaśīrṣa and the day is Monday free from all defects. All the planets are in auspicious conjunction. They are not aspected by the evil planets. The Jupiter is in a position that is conducive to the birth of a good child and all good fortune to the bridegroom. O lord of mountains, give your daughter Pārvatī, the mother of the universe, the primordial Being to Śiva, the father of the universe. You will then get quiet and contentment. (58-61.)
