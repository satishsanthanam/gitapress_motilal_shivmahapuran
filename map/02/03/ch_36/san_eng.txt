### **CHAPTER THIRTYSIX - *(The statements of the seven sages)***

---

Sanskrit
३६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
वसिष्ठस्य वचः श्रुत्वा सगणोऽपि हिमालयः ।
विस्मितो भार्यया शैलानुवाच स गिरीश्वरः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of the seven sages, Himācala, his wife and attendants were very much surprised. The lord of the mountains spoke to the other mountains. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालय उवाच ।
हे मेरो गिरिराट् सह्य गन्धमादन मन्दर ।
मैनाक विन्ध्य शैलेन्द्राः सर्वे श‍ृणुत मद्वचः ॥ २॥

English
*Himācala said:—*  O lord of mountains—Meru,$^{64}$ O Sahya$^{65}$ O Gandhamādana,$^{66}$ O Mandara,$^{67}$ O Maināka,$^{68}$ O Vindhya,$^{69}$ all of you listen to my words. (2.)

[fn 64. The mountain stands in the centre of the earth and is described as the paricarp of the earth-lotus with great islands or continents forming its petals. For details, see Note 217 P. 710.]

[fn 65. Sahya is the name applied to that part of the Western Ghats which lies to the north of the Travancore hills.]

[fn 66. Gandhamādana is that part of the Himālayas on which the Badarikāśrama is situated. See Note 309 P. 405.]

[fn 67. See Note 30 P. 561. For details see Note 36 P. 48.]

[fn 68. Maināka: See Note 12 P. 494.]

[fn 69. The name Vindhya is applied to the whole chain of hills running from Gujrat to the Gayā region and lying on both sides of the Narmadā river.]
---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठो हि वदत्येवं किं मे कार्यं विचार्यते ।
यथा तथा च शंसध्वं निर्णीय मनसाखिलम् ॥ ३॥

English
Vasiṣṭha says like this. It is to be considered what I shall do now. You consider well, decide and let me know. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छुत्वा वचनं तस्य सुमेरुप्रमुखाश्च ते ।
प्रोचुर्हिमालयं प्रीत्या सुनिर्णीय महीधराः ॥ ४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing his words, Sumeru and other mountains decided carefully and spoke to Himālaya lovingly. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शैला ऊचुः ।
अधुना किं विमर्शेन कृतं कार्यं तथैव हि ।
उत्पन्नेयं महाभाग देवकार्यार्थमेव हि ॥ ५॥

English
*The mountains said:—*  Of what avail is a long discussion and deliberation now? What should be done is only that. She is born only for the purpose of the gods. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रदातव्या शिवायेति शिवस्यार्थेऽवतारिणी ।
अनयाराधितो रुद्रो रुद्रेण यदि भाषिता ॥ ६॥

English
Incarnating for the sake of Śiva, she shall be given to Śiva. Śiva has been propitiated by her and Śiva has also spoken to her. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचस्तेषां मेर्वादीनां हिमाचलः ।
सुप्रसन्नतरोऽभूद् वै जहास गिरिजा हृदि ॥ ७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of Meru and others, Himācala was greatly pleased and Pārvatī laughed within herself. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अरुन्धती च तां मेनां बोधयामास कारणात् ।
नानावाक्यसमूहेनेतिहासैर्विविधैरपि ॥ ८॥

English
Arundhatī too convinced Menā with reasoned statements and examples from various mythological legends. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा मेनका शैलपत्नी बुद्ध्वा प्रसन्नधीः ।
मुनीनरुन्धतीं शैलं भोजयित्वा बुभोज च ॥ ९॥

English
Then the wife of the mountain too was delightedly convinced. She entertained Arundhatī, the sages and the mountain with a grand feast and then took food herself. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शैलवरो ज्ञानी सुसंसेव्य मुनींश्च तान् ।
उवाच साञ्जलिः प्रीत्या प्रसन्नात्मा गतभ्रमः ॥ १०॥

English
Then the chief of mountains, freed from wrong notions and grown wise, spoke with palms joined in reverence and mind extremely delighted. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचल उवाच ।
सप्तर्षयो महाभागा वचः श‍ृणुत मामकम् ।
विस्मयो मे गतः सर्वः शिवयोश्चरितं श्रुतम् ॥ ११॥

English
*Himācala said:—*  O fortunate sages, please listen to my words. All my bewilderment has vanished since I have heard the story of Śivā and Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
मदीयं च शरीरं वै पत्नी मेना सुता सुताः ।
ऋद्धिः सिद्धिश्च चान्यद्वै शिवस्यैव न चान्यथा ॥ १२॥

English
Everything that I possess, my body, wife, Menā, sons, daughter, assets and achievements and other things belong to Śiva and not otherwise. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स तदा पुत्रीं दृष्ट्वा तत्सादरं च ताम् ।
भूषयित्वा तदङ्गानि ऋष्युत्सङ्गे न्यवेशयत् ॥ १३॥

उवाच च पुनः प्रीत्या शैलराज ऋषींस्तदा ।
अयं भागो मया तस्मै दातव्य इति निश्चितम् ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying so, he bedecked his daughter with various ornaments. Then he took them all and placed them on the lap of the sage saying “These are the presents I have to give her.” (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
शङ्करो भिक्षुकस्तेऽथ स्वयं दाता भवान् गिरे ।
भैक्ष्यञ्च पार्वती देवी किमतः परमुत्तमम् ॥ १५॥

English
*The sages said:—*  O mountain, you are the donor, Śiva is the mendicant, and the alms goddess Pārvatī. What else can be better than this ? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमवन् शिखराणान्ते यद्धेतोः सदृशी गतिः ।
धन्यस्त्वं सर्वशैलानामधिपः सर्वतो वरः ॥ १६॥

English
Since the course of your summits is befitting, you are blessed, you are the chief of all mountains, you are great in every respect. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा तु कन्यायै मुनयो विमलाशयाः ।
आशिषं दत्तवन्तस्ते शिवाय सुखदा भव ॥ १७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying thus, the sages of pure mind offered their blessings to the girl—“Be pleasing to Śiva.” (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स्पृष्ट्वा करेण तां तत्र कल्याणं ते भविष्यति ।
शुक्लपक्षे यथा चन्द्रो वर्धन्तां त्वद्गुणास्तथा ॥ १८॥

English
They touched her with their hands and continued—“Everything will be well with you. As the moon in the bright half of the month, may your qualities increase.” (18.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा मुनयः सर्वे दत्त्वा ते गिरये मुदा ।
पुष्पाणि फलयुक्तानि प्रत्ययं चक्रिरे तदा ॥ १९॥

English
After saying thus and offering fruits and flowers to the lord of mountains, the sages made him believe that the alliance was a settled fact. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अरुन्धती तदा तत्र मेनां सा सुमुखी मुदा ।
गुणैश्च लोभयामास शिवस्य परमा सती ॥ २०॥

English
The great chaste lady Arundhati tempted Menā further with Śiva’s good qualities. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिद्राकुङ्कुमैः शैलश्मश्रूणि प्रत्यमार्जयत् ।
लौकिकाचारमाधाय मङ्गलायनमुत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
According to the worldly convention they smeared the moustache of the mountain with powdered turmeric and saffron as an auspicious custom. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च ते चतुर्थेऽह्नि सन्धार्य लग्नमुत्तमम् ।
परस्परं च सन्तुष्य सञ्जग्मुः शिवसन्निधिम् ॥ २२॥

English
After fixing the auspicious Lagna for the marriage and congratulating and complimenting one another the sages came to Śiva’s abode on the fourth day. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा शिवं नत्वा स्तुत्वा विविधसूक्तिभिः ।
ऊचुः सर्वे वसिष्ठाद्या मुनयः परमेश्वरम् ॥ २३॥

English
After reaching the place, Vasiṣṭha and other sages bowed to Śiva and eulogised Him with different hymns. They then spoke to lord Śiva. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव परमेश महाप्रभो ।
श‍ृण्वस्मद्वचनं प्रीत्या यत्कृतं सेवकैस्तव ॥ २४॥

English
*The sages said:—*  O lord Śiva, lord of the gods, O great lord Śiva, please listen lovingly to the narration of what we, your attendants, have done. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
बोधितो गिरिराजश्च मेना विविधसूक्तिभिः ।
सेतिहासं महेशान प्रबुद्धोऽसौ न संशयः ॥ २५॥

English
O great lord, the lord of mountains and Menā have been urged with different kinds of statements and examples from mythological legends. Undoubtedly he is enlightened. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
वाक्यदत्ता गिरीन्द्रेण पार्वती ते हि नान्यथा ।
उद्वाहाय प्रगच्छ त्वं गणैर्देवैश्च संयुतः ॥ २६॥

English
Pārvatī has been betrothed to you by the lord of mountains. It is not otherwise. Now please start for the marriage with your attendants and the gods. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
गच्छ शीघ्रं महादेव हिमाचलगृहं प्रभो ।
विवाहय यथा रीतिः पार्वतीमात्मजन्मने ॥ २७॥

English
O great god, O lord, go to the abode of Himācala and marry Pārvatī in accordance with the customs for the sake of a son. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तेषां लौकिकाचारतत्परः ।
प्रहृष्टात्मा महेशानः प्रहस्येदमुवाच सः ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing their words, lord Śiva who was delighted and who loved to follow worldly conventions laughed and said:— (28.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश उवाच ।
विवाहो हि महाभागा न दृष्टो न श्रुतो मया ।
यथा पुरा भवद्भिस्तद्विधिः प्रोच्यो विशेषतः ॥ २९॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  O fortunate one, a marriage ceremony has never been witnessed nor even heard of by me before. The details of the same shall be mentioned by you all, specifically. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य महेशस्य लौकिकं वचनं शुभम् ।
प्रत्यूचुः प्रहसन्तस्ते देवदेवं सदाशिवम् ॥ ३०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words in a worldly vein uttered by Śiva, they laughingly replied to Sadāśiva, lord of the gods. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
विष्णुमाहूय वै शीघ्रं ससमाजं विशेषतः ।
ब्रह्माणं ससुतं प्रीत्या तथा देवं शतक्रतुम् ॥ ३१॥

तथा ऋषिगणान्सर्वान् यक्षगन्धर्वकिन्नरान् ।
सिद्धान् विद्याधरांश्चैव तथा चैवाप्सरोगणान् ॥ ३२॥

एतांश्चान्यान्प्रभो सर्वानानयस्वेह सादरम् ।
सर्वं संसाधयिष्यन्ति त्वत्कार्यं ते न संशयः ॥ ३३॥

English
*The sages said:—*  Please invite and summon Viṣṇu with his retinue, Brahmā with his sons, lord Indra,⁷⁰ all the sages, Yakṣas, Gandharvas, Kinnaras, Siddhas, Vidyādharas, heavenly nymphs and others. All of them will jointly accomplish everything for you. There is no doubt about it. (31-33.)

[fn 70. Indra is called Śatakratu—'a God of hundred rites', for he achieves Indrahood—lordship of the gods—by performing one hundred horse-sacrifices.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा सप्त ऋषयस्तदाज्ञां प्राप्य ते मुदा ।
स्वधाम प्रययुः सर्वे शंसन्तः शङ्करीं गतिम् ॥ ३४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Saying this and taking His permission the seven sages joyfully returned to their abodes praising the way of Śiva. (34.)
