### **CHAPTER THIRTYSEVEN - *(The letter of betrothal is despatched, the requisites for the celebration are gathered and the mountain-invitees arrive)***

---

Sanskrit
३७

नारद उवाच ।
तात प्राज्ञ वदेदानीं सप्तर्षिषु गतेषु च ।
किमकार्षीद्धिमगिरिस्तन्मे कृत्वा कृपां प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  Dear wise father, when the seven sages returned what did Himācala do? Please tell me, O lord. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
गतेषु तेषु मुनिषु सप्तस्वपि मुनीश्वर ।
सारुन्धतीषु हिमवान् यदकार्षीद् ब्रवीमि ते ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O great sage, I shall tell you what Himācala did, when the seven sages and Arundhati left. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत आमन्त्र्य स्वभ्रातॄन् मेर्वादीन् ससुतप्रियः ।
महामनाः स मुमुदे हिमवान् पर्वतेश्वरः ॥ ३॥

English
Bidding farewell to his brothers, Meru and others Himācala, the lord of mountains, rejoiced in the company of his sons, daughter and wife. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाज्ञप्तस्ततः प्रीत्या हिमवान् लग्नपत्रिकाम् ।
लेखयामास सुप्रीत्या गर्गेण स्वपुरोधसा ॥ ४॥

English
Urged by them lovingly, Himācala caused the letter of betrothal to be written by Garga, his priest. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रस्थापयामास तां शिवाय स पत्रिकाम् ।
नानाविधास्तु सामग्र्यः स्वजनैर्मुदितात्मभिः ॥ ५॥

English
He despatched the letter of betrothal to Śiva along with articles of homage through his kinsmen. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ते जनास्तत्र गत्वा च कैलासे शिवसन्निधिम् ।
ददुः शिवाय तत्पत्रं तिलकं संविधाय च ॥ ६॥

English
Those people arrived at Kailāsa and handed over the letter to Śiva after applying the holy mark on his forehead. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्मानिता विशेषेण प्रभुणा च यथोचितम् ।
सर्वे ते प्रीतिमनस आजग्मुः शैलसन्निधिम् ॥ ७॥

English
After being duly honoured by the lord, they returned highly delighted to the penance of the mountain. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्मानितान्विशेषेण महेशेनागतान् जनान् ।
दृष्ट्वा सुहर्षितान् शैलो मुमोदातीव चेतसि ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing those people who had been highly honoured by lord Śiva and who had returned excessively delighted, the mountain rejoiced much. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निमन्त्रणं चक्रे स्वबन्धूनां प्रमोदितः ।
नानादेशस्थितानां च निखिलानां सुखास्पदम् ॥ ९॥

English
Then he extended his invitation highly pleasing to his kinsmen stationed in different places with great delight. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स कारयामास स्वन्नसङ्ग्रहमादरात् ।
नानाविधाश्च सामग्रीर्विवाहकरणोचिताः ॥ १०॥

English
Then he began collecting foodstuffs and other requisite articles intended for the performance of the marriage. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तण्डुलानां बहून् शैलान् पृथुकानां तथैव च ।
गुडानां शर्कराणां च लवणानां तथैव च ॥ ११॥

English
Mountainous masses of rice, beaten rice, jaggery, sugar candies and salt were heaped up. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षीराणां च घृतानां च दध्नां वापीश्चकार सः ।
यवादिधान्यपिष्टानां लड्डुकानां तथैव च ॥ १२॥

English
He caused huge tanks and receptacles built for milk, ghee and curds as well as for fried flour cakes of barley and other grains and ball-like sweets. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शष्कुलीनां स्वस्तिकानां शर्कराणां तथैव च ।
अमृतेक्षुरसानां च तत्र वापीश्चकार सः ॥ १३॥

English
Big tanks and receptacles were made for the nectar, sugarcane juice, baked cakes, and the sugar candies. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
बह्वीर्हैयङ्गवानां च ह्यासवानां तथैव च ।
नाना पक्वान्नसङ्घांश्च महास्वादुरसाँस्तथा ॥ १४॥

English
Tanks were built for butter, spirituous beverages, sweet juices of various kinds and rice preparations of various sorts. (14.)

---

English
Different kinds of pickles and side dishes were prepared that might appeal to Śiva's Gaṇas and the gods. Different kinds of valuable garments purified in fire were kept ready. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मणिरत्नप्रकाराणि सुवर्णरजतानि च ।
द्रव्याण्येतानि चान्यानि सङ्गृह्य विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ १६॥

English
Gems and jewels of different kinds, gold, silver and other articles were gathered duly. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मङ्गलं कर्तुमारेभे गिरिर्मङ्गलकृद्दिने ।
संस्कारं कारयामासुः पार्वत्याः पर्वतस्त्रियः ॥ १७॥

English
Auspicious rites were started by the mountain on an auspicious day. The womenfolk of the mountain performed the purificatory ceremony for Pārvatī. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ता मङ्गलं मुदा चक्रुर्भूषिता भूषणैः स्वयम् ।
पुरद्विजस्त्रियो हृष्टा लोकाचारं प्रचक्रिरे ॥ १८॥

English
Women bedecked in ornaments performed auspicious rites. The delighted brahmin women of the city did everything in accordance with the tradition and custom. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सोत्सवं विविधं तत्र सुमङ्गलपुरस्सरम् ।
हिमालयोऽपि हृष्टात्मा कृत्वाचारं सुमङ्गलम् ॥ १९॥

English
Great festivities and holy auspicious rites were performed by the delighted Himavat too. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वभावेन सुप्रीतो बन्धुवर्गागमोत्सुकः ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तस्य बान्धवाश्च निमन्त्रिताः ॥ २०॥

आजग्मुः सस्त्रियो हृष्टाः ससुताः सपरिच्छदाः ।
तदेव श‍ृणु देवर्षे गिर्यागमनमादृतः ॥ २१॥

English
Delighted in every respect and eagerly awaiting the arrival of his kinsmen he was excited with various emotions. The invitees came there along with their wives, children and attendants. O celestial sage, listen to a detailed narration of the arrival of those mountains. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्णयामि समासेन शिवप्रीतिविवृद्धये ।
देवालयगिरिर्यो हि दिव्यरूपधरो महान् ॥ २२॥

नानारत्नपरिभ्राजत्समाजः सपरिच्छदः ।
नानामणिमहारत्नसारमादाय यत्नतः ॥ २३॥

सुवेषालङ्कृतः श्रीमान् जगाम स हिमालयम् ।
मन्दरः सर्वशोभाढ्यः सनारीतनयो गिरिः ॥ २४॥

English
In order to increase the devotion to Śiva I shall explain in brief. Mandara, the chief mountain in heaven came to Himavat in a divine form. He was highly refulgent. He was accompanied by his wife and children. His company shone brilliantly. He had brought with him many gems and jewels. (22-24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूपायनानि सङ्गृह्य जगाम विविधानि च ।
अस्ताचलोऽपि दिव्यात्मा सोपायन उदारधीः ॥ २५॥

English
Bringing with him many articles of presentation, the liberal Western mountain$^{71}$ reached there in a divine form. (25.)

[fn 71. 'Asta' is a mythical sunset mountain in the West while 'udaya' is a mythical sunrise mountain in the East.]

---

Sanskrit
बहुशोभासमायुक्त आजगाम मुदान्वितः ।
उदयाचल आदाय सद्रत्नानि मणीनपि ॥ २६॥

English
The Eastern mountain came there with brilliant gems and jewels. He looked delighted and extremely brilliant. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्युत्कृष्टपरीवार आजगाम महासुखी ।
मलयो गिरिराजो हि सपरीवार आदृतः ॥ २७॥

English
The highly venerable lord of mountains, Malaya,$^{72}$ came there with his followers. He was happy with his excellent followers. (27.)

[fn 72. Malaya (derived from the Dravidian word malai meaning 'lill') was the name applied to the Travancore hills and the southernmost part of the Western ghats.]

---

Sanskrit
सुदिव्यरचनायुक्त आययौ बहुसद्बलः ।
सद्यो दर्दुरनामा च मुदितः सकलत्रकः ॥ २८॥

English
The mountain Dardura$^{73}$ came along with his wife. He was exquisitely dressed. He was delighted$^{74}$. He had many attendants with him. (28.)

[fn 73. Dardura, variously spelt as Darddara or Darddura is identified with the Deogarh peak in the eastern part of the Vindhyas. G. E. Part I P. 104.]

[fn 74. A mythic range of mountains lying south of Meru, but sometimes described as on the east. H. M P 24.]


---

Sanskrit
बहुशोभान्वितस्तात ययौ हिमगिरेर्गृहम् ।
निषदोऽपि प्रहृष्टात्मा सपरिच्छद आययौ ॥ २९॥

English
O dear, the delighted mountain Niṣadha came along with his attendants. He was very brilliant. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The fortunate mountain Gandhamādana came with great pleasure along with his children and womenfolk. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Mountains Karavīra$^{75}$ and Mahendra$^{76}$ of great wealth and prosperity also came there. (31.)

[fn 75. It has not been possible to identify this mountain.]

Sanskrit

---

English
Krauñca⁷⁸ the chief of mountains, came with a large army of attendants. He had articles of presentation with him. He was accompanied by his kinsmen and relatives. (33.)

[fn 78. Krauñch is the name of a mythical mountain said to be the grandson of Himālaya who was pierced by Kārtukeya and Paraśurāma.]

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
