### **CHAPTER THIRTYEIGHT - *(Description of the dais)***

---

Sanskrit
३८

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ शैलेश्वरः प्रीतो हिमवान्मुनिसत्तम ।
स्वपुरं रचयामास विचित्रं परमोत्सवम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Then the lord of mountains. O excellent sage, attended to the decoration of the entire city befitting the great festivities ahead. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सिक्तमार्गं संस्कृतं च शोभितं परमर्द्धिभिः ।
द्वारि द्वारि च रम्भादि मङ्गलं द्रव्यसंयुतम् ॥ २॥

English
The roads were watered and swept clean. At every door, stumps of plantain trees and other auspicious symbols were fixed. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राङ्गणं रचयामास रम्भास्तम्भसमन्वितम् ।
पट्टसूत्रैः सन्निबद्धं रसालपल्लवान्वितम् ॥ ३॥

English
The courtyard was embellished with plantain trees tied with silken cords. There were festoons of mango leaves. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मालतीमाल्यसंयुक्तं लसत्तोरणसुप्रभम् ।
शोभितं मङ्गलद्रव्यैश्चतुर्दिक्षु स्थितैः शुभैः ॥ ४॥

English
Festoons with garlands of jasmine flowers shone, everywhere. Other articles of auspicious portent were fixed in every quarter. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव सर्वं परया मुदान्वित-
श्चक्रे गिरीन्द्रः स्वसुतार्थमेव ।
गर्गं पुरस्कृत्य महाप्रभावं
प्रस्तावयोग्यं च सुमङ्गलं हि ॥ ५॥

English
These and other things were carried out by Himavat for the sake of his daughter. Every activity was supervised by Garga of great ability. Everything auspicious worth mentioning found a place there. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आहूय विश्वकर्माणं कारयामास सादरम् ।
मण्डपं च सुविस्तीर्णं वेदिकादिमनोहरम् ॥ ६॥

English
He called Viśvakarman$^{95}$ and requested him to erect a large and spacious dais beautiful with side rostrums, altars etc. (6.)

[fn 95. See Note 301 P. 401; Note 295 P. 389.]
---

Sanskrit
अयुतेन सुरर्षे तद्योजनानां च विस्तृतम् ।
अनेकलक्षणोपेतं नानाश्चर्यसमन्वितम् ॥ ७॥

English
The dais, O celestial sage, was ten thousand Yojanas wide. It was wonderfully constructed and had all the characteristic features. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थावरं जङ्गमं सर्वं सदृशं तैर्मनोहरम् ।
सर्वतोऽद्भुतसर्वस्वं नानावस्तुचमत्कृतम् ॥ ८॥

English
All the mobile and immobile objects of the world were represented there with realistic appearance. Everything was wonderfully portrayed. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जङ्गमं विजितं तत्र स्थावरेण विशेषतः ।
जङ्गमेन च तत्रासीज्जितं स्थावरमेव हि ॥ ९॥

English
The mobile objects presented there surpassed the immobile ones and the immobile ones surpassed the mobile ones in excellence. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पयसा च जिता तत्र स्थलभूमिर्न चान्यथा ।
जलं किं हि स्थलं किं हि न विदुः केऽपि कोविदाः ॥ १०॥

English
The watery places presented there excelled the solid grounds. Even experts could not distinguish what was water and what was solid ground. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
क्वचित्सिंहाः कृत्रिमाश्च क्वचित्सारसपङ्क्तयः ।
क्वचिच्छिखण्डिनस्तत्र कृत्रिमाश्च मनोहराः ॥ ११॥

English
There were artificial lions. There were rows of storks. There were artificial peacocks, but very beautiful in appearance. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
क्वचित्स्त्रियः कृत्रिमाश्च नृत्यन्त्यः पुरुषैस्सह ।
मोहयन्त्यो जनान्सर्वान्पश्यन्त्यः कृत्रिमास्तथा ॥ १२॥

English
Artificial women were represented as dancing with artificial men casting wistful glances at them and enchanting them. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा तेनैव विधिना द्वारपाला मनोहराः ।
हस्तैर्धनूंषि चोद्धृत्य स्थावरा जङ्गमोपमाः ॥ १३॥

English
Beautiful representations of gatekeepers with uplifted bows in their hands appeared like real originals. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारि स्थिता महालक्ष्मीः कृत्रिमा रचिताद्भुता ।
सर्वलक्षणसंयुक्तागता साक्षात्पयोर्णवात् ॥ १४॥

English
The statue of Mahālakṣmī at the main entrance appeared like the goddess just emerged from the milk-ocean. It was because all the characteristics were complete. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
गजाश्चालङ्कृता ह्यासन्कृत्रिमा अकृतोपमाः ।
तथाश्वाः सादिभिश्चैव गजाश्च गजसादिभिः ॥ १५॥

English
Elephants with their mahouts and horses with their riders were so natural that none would say that they were artificial. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रथा रथिभिराकृष्टा महाश्चर्यसमन्विताः ।
वाहनानि तथान्यानि पत्तयः कृत्रिमास्तथा ॥ १६॥

English
Chariots were driven by chariotcers, other vehicles by other drivers. There were foot-solidiers too. All of them were artificial. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विमोहनार्थं तु कृतं वै विश्वकर्मणा ।
देवानां च मुनीनां च तेन प्रीतात्मना मुने ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, Viśvakarman was so delighted that he made all these things to fascinate the visiting dignitaries, the gods and the sages. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
महाद्वारि स्थितो नन्दी कृत्रिमश्च कृतो मुने ।
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशो यथा नन्दी तथैव सः ॥ १८॥

English
O sage, the statue of Nandin, at the portals, of crystalline purity and brilliance, was a prototype of the real Nandin. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्योपरि महादिव्यं पुष्पकं रत्नभूषितम् ।
राजितं पल्लवैः शुभ्रैश्चामरैश्च सुशोभितम् ॥ १९॥

English
Above that there was the celestial chariot Puṣpaka decorated with sprouts. It shone with gods represented therein. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
वामपार्श्वे गजौ द्वौ च शुद्धकाश्मीरसन्निभौ ।
चतुर्दन्तौ षष्टिवर्षौ भेदमानौ महाप्रभौ ॥ २०॥

English
On the left side there were two huge saffron coloured elephants with four tusks and appearing to be of sixty years in age. They shone lustrously. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैवार्कनिभौ तेन कृतौ चाश्वौ महाप्रभौ ।
चामरालङ्कृतौ दिव्यौ दिव्यालङ्कारभूषितौ ॥ २१॥

English
There were two horses too, brilliant like the sun. They were bedecked in divine ornaments and other necessary embellishments. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दंशिता वररत्नाढ्या लोकपालास्तथैव च ।
सर्वे देवा यथार्थं वै कृता वै विश्वकर्मणा ॥ २२॥

English
The guardians of the quarters were shown as adorned with great gems. All the gods were portrayed by Viśvakarman realistically. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा हि ऋषयःसर्वे भृग्वाद्याश्च तपोधनाः ।
अन्ये ह्युपसुरास्तद्वत्सिद्धाश्चान्येऽपि वै कृताः ॥ २३॥

English
Bhṛgu⁹⁶ and other sages, secondary gods, Siddhas and others were represented by Viśvakarman. (23.)

[fn 96. See Note 299 P. 397.]
---

Sanskrit
विष्णुश्च पार्षदैः सर्वैर्गरुडाख्यैः समन्वितः ।
कृत्रिमो निर्मितस्तद्वत्परमाश्चर्यरूपवान् ॥ २४॥

English
A wonderful image of Viṣṇu with his attendants Garuḍa and others was created by him with wonderful features. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैवाहं सुतैर्वेदैस्सिद्धैश्च परिवारितः ।
कृत्रिमो निर्मितस्तद्वत्पठन्सूक्तानि नारद ॥ २५॥

English
I too was portrayed as surrounded by my sons, Vedas and Siddhas. O Nārada, I was represented as reciting the hymns. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ऐरावतगजारूढः शक्रः स्वदलसंयुतः ।
कृत्रिमो निर्मितस्तद्वत्परिपूर्णेन्दुसन्निभः ॥ २६॥

English
An artificial image of Indra seated on Airāvata and accompanied by his attendants was made by him looking as beautiful as the full moon. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन देवर्षे सर्वो वै विश्वकर्मणा ।
हिमागप्रेरितेनाशु क्लृप्तःसुरसमाजकः ॥ २७॥

English
O celestial sage, of what avail is a long-drawn description? The gods were drawn by Viśvakarman as desired by Himavat. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूतः कृतस्तेन मण्डपो दिव्यरूपवान् ।
अनेकाश्चर्यसम्भूतो महान्देवविमोहनः ॥ २८॥

English
The Altar was erected by him with wonderful features, fascinating the gods and exquisite in form. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अथाज्ञप्तो गिरीशेन विश्वकर्मा महामतिः ।
निवासार्थं सुरादीनां तत्तल्लोकान् हि यत्नतः ॥ २९॥

English
On being commanded by the lord of mountains, the intelligent Viśvakarman created different abodes for the residence of the gods and others. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रैव च महामञ्चाः सुप्रभाः परमाद्भुताः ।
रचिताःसुखदा दिव्यास्तेषां वै विश्वकर्मणा ॥ ३०॥

English
Great couches of wonderful brilliance very cosy and exquisite were made by Viśvakarman for their sake. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा स सत्यलोकं वै विरेचे क्षणतोऽद्भुतम् ।
दीप्त्या परमया युक्तं निवासार्थं स्वयम्भुवः ॥ ३१॥

English
For the residence of Brahmā, seven wonderful abodes were created in a trice. They had great brilliance. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव विष्णोस्त्वपरं वैकुण्ठाख्यं महोज्ज्वलम् ।
विरेचे क्षणतो दिव्यं नानाश्चर्यसमन्वितम् ॥ ३२॥

English
A brilliant abode of Viṣṇu called Vaikuṇṭha, with wonderful features, was created in a trice. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अमरेशगृहं दिव्यं तथैवाद्भुतमुत्तमम् ।
विरेचे विश्वकर्मासौ सर्वैश्वर्यसमन्वितम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Viśvakarman created a wonderfully divine palace for the lord of gods endowed with all riches. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहाणि लोकपालानां विरेचे सुन्दराणि च ।
तद्वत्स प्रीतितो दिव्यान्यद्भुतानि महान्ति च ॥ ३४॥

English
Wonderful mansions for the guardians of the quarters were erected by Viśvakarman. They were beautiful and large. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्येषाममराणां च सर्वेषां क्रमशस्तथा ।
सदनानि विचित्राणि रचितानि च तेन वै ॥ ३५॥

English
Mansions of various kinds were built by him for other gods too. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वकर्मा महाबुद्धिः प्राप्तशम्भुमहावरः ।
विरेचे क्षणतः सर्वं शिवतुष्ट्यर्थमेव च ॥ ३६॥

English
The highly intelligent Viśvakarman built everything very quickly for the propitiation of Śiva from whom he had secured great favours. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव चित्रं परमं महोज्ज्वलं
महाप्रभं देववरैः सुपूजितम् ।
गिरीशचिह्नं शिवलोकसंस्थितं
सुशोभितं शम्भुगृहं चकार ॥ ३७॥

English
Similarly he erected Śiva's mansion of various shapes and of great brilliance. Having the symbol of Śiva it was designated as Śivaloka. It was admired by all the gods. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूता कृता तेन रचना विश्वकर्मणा ।
विचित्रा शिवतुष्ट्यर्थं पराश्चर्या महोज्ज्वला ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus for propitiating Śiva, wonderful and very brilliant structures were erected by Viśvakarman. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वाखिलं चेदं व्यवहारं च लौकिकम् ।
पर्यैक्षिष्ट मुदा शम्भ्वागमनं स हिमाचलः ॥ ३९॥

English
Making all arrangements in accordance with worldly conventions, Himavat awaited eagerly for the arrival of Śiva. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रोक्तमशेषेण वृत्तान्तं प्रमुदावहम् ।
हिमालयस्य देवर्षे किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ४०॥

English
Thus, O divine sage, I have narrated the pleasing story of Himavat entirely. What else do you wish to hear? (40.)

---
