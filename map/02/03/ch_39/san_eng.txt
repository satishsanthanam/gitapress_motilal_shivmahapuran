### **CHAPTER THIRTYNINE - *(The gods arrive at Kailāsa on invitation and Śiva prepares to start)***

---

Sanskrit
३९

नारद उवाच ।
विधे तात महाप्राज्ञ विष्णुशिष्य नमोऽस्तु ते ।
अद्भुतेयं कथाश्रावि त्वत्तोऽस्माभिः कृपानिधे ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O dear father Brahmā, O intelligent disciple of Viṣṇu, obeisance be to you. O merciful one, this wonderful story has been heard by us from you. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं श्रोतुमिच्छामि चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
वैवाहिकं सुमाङ्गल्यं सर्वाघौघविनाशनम् ॥ २॥

English
Now I wish to hear the story of the auspicious marriage of the moon-crested lord that dispels all sins. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
किं चकार महादेवः प्राप्य मङ्गलपत्रिकाम् ।
तां श्रावय कथां दिव्यां शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ॥ ३॥

English
What did lord Śiva do on receiving the auspicious letter of betrothal. Please narrate that story of Śiva, the supreme soul. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु वत्स महाप्राज्ञ शाङ्करं परमं यशः ।
यच्चकार महादेवः प्राप्य मङ्गलपत्रिकाम् ॥ ४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Dear child of great intellect, listen to the glory of Śiva, what lord Śiva did on receiving the auspicious letter. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुर्गृहीत्वा तां मुदा मङ्गलपत्रिकाम् ।
विजहास प्रहृष्टात्मा मानं तेषां व्यधाद्विभुः ॥ ५॥

English
On reading the auspicious letter with joy, Śiva laughed in delight. The lord honoured them duly. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
वाचयित्वा च तां सम्यग्स्वीचकार विधानतः ।
तज्जनान्यापयामास बहुसम्मान्य चादृतः ॥ ६॥

English
Causing the letter to be read aloud, He duly accepted the proposal. Honouring the messengers He informed them. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच मुनिवर्गांस्तान्कार्यं सम्यक् कृतं शुभम् ।
आगन्तव्यं विवाहे मे विवाहः स्वीकृतो मया ॥ ७॥

English
He told the sages—“Every thing is auspicious and well done. All of you shall grace the celebration of my marriage. The marriage proposal has been accepted by me”. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भोः प्रहृष्टास्ते प्रणम्य तम् ।
परिक्रम्य ययुर्धाम शंसन्तः स्वं विधिं परम् ॥ ८॥

English
On hearing these words of Śiva, they were delighted. After bowing to and circumambulating Him they returned joyful of their great luck and grace. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवेश्वरः शम्भुः सामरस्तवां मुने द्रुतम् ।
लौकिकाचारमाश्रित्य महालीलाकरः प्रभुः ॥ ९॥

English
Then Śiva, the lord of the gods, the lord indulging in divine sports, remembered you, O sage, in accordance with worldly conventions. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमागतः परप्रीत्या प्रशंसंस्त्वं विधिं परम् ।
प्रणमंश्च नतस्कन्धो विनीतात्मा कृताञ्जलिः ॥ १०॥

अस्तौस्सुजयशब्दानि समुच्चार्य मुहुर्मुहुः ।
निदेशं प्रार्थयंस्तस्य प्रशंसंस्त्वं विधिम्मुने ॥ ११॥

English
You came there praising your good luck. Bowing to Him humbly with palms joined in reverence and with stooping shoulders you eulogised Him with the utterances of words "hail to Thee". O sage, you requested Him for his behest. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शम्भुः प्रहृष्टात्मा दर्शयँल्लौकिकीं गतिम् ।
उवाच मुनिवर्य त्वां प्रीणयन् शुभया गिरा ॥ १२॥

English
Then the delighted Śiva, heightening your pleasure with sweet speech and evincing interest in worldly conventions told you thus, O excellent sage. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
प्रीत्या श‍ृणु मुनिश्रेष्ठ ह्यस्मत्तोऽद्य वदामि ते ।
ब्रुवे तत्त्वां प्रियो मे यद्भक्तराजशिरोमणिः ॥ १३॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O excellent sage, listen to us lovingly. I am speaking to you because you are the crest-jewel of my devotees. (13.)

---

Sanskrit

तस्यै वरो मया दत्तः पतित्वे तोषितेन वै ॥ १४॥

English
At your bidding a great penance has been performed by Pārvatī. Propitiated by her I have granted her the boon of my being her husband. (14.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Being subservient to her by her devotion I shall marry her. The auspicious hour free of defects has been fixed by the seven sages. (15.)

---

Sanskrit

महोत्सवं करिष्यामि लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ॥ १६॥

English
O Nārada, the marriage will take place after seven days from today. Following the worldly conventions I shall make a grand festival of the same. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear one, on hearing these words of Śiva, the supreme soul, you were delighted and you spoke after bowing to the lord. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Nārada said :—*  This is your sacred rite. You have been considered subservient to your devotees. You have carried out the desire of Pārvatī. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O lord, a task befitting my capacity must be mentioned by you. Considering me your own servant please be kind to me. Obeisance to you. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O great sage, Śiva, favourably disposed to His devotees, on being thus requested by you replied very delightedly to you. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Śiva said :—*  O sage, on my behalf, invite all the gods beginning with Viṣṇu, and sages, Siddhas and others. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Giving due weightage to my bidding, may all of them come here enthusiastically, in all their splendour along with their women and children. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, those who do not take part in the celebration of my marriage, are not my people, even if they are the gods. (23.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Paying heed to this behest of Śiva, O sage, you, a great favourite of Śiva, invited all of them approaching everyone severally. (24.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Nārada, after carrying out your duties as his emissary you, the great sage, returned to Śiva and remained there with His permission. (25.)

Sanskrit

English
Śiva too waited there eagerly expecting their arrival while his attendants were celebrating great festivities by dance and songs. (26.)

Sanskrit

English

English

English

अगस्त्यश्च्यवनो गर्गश्शिलादोऽथ महामुने ॥ ५३॥

दधीचिरुपमन्युश्च भरद्वाजोऽकृतव्रणः ।
पिप्पलादोऽथ कुशिकः कौत्सो व्यासः सशिष्यकः ॥ ५४॥

एते चान्ये च बहव आगताश्शिवसन्निधिम् ।
मया सुनोदितास्तत्र चक्रुस्ते विधिवत्क्रियाम् ॥ ५५॥

English
The sages Kaśyapa, Atri, Vasiṣṭha, Gautama, Bhāguri, Bṛhaspati, Kaṇva, Śakti,⁹⁸ Jamadagni, Parāśara, Mārkaṇḍeya, Śilāpāka, Aruṇapāla, Akṛtaśrama, Agastya, Cyavana, Garga, Śilāda, Dadhīci, Upamanyu, Bharadvāja, Akṛtavraṇa, Pippalāda, Kuśika, Kautsa, Vyāsa, with his disciples, and other sages came to Śiva. Urged by me they performed the sacred rites duly. (52-55.)

[fn ⁹⁸. Śakti was the eldest son of Vasiṣṭha.]]

Sanskrit
वेदोक्तविधिना सर्वे वेदवेदाङ्गपारगाः ।
रक्षां चक्रुर्महेशस्य कृत्वा कौतुकमङ्गलम् ॥ ५६॥

English
All of them who had mastered the Vedas and Vedāṅgas performed the safety rites for Śiva and tied the auspicious thread round his wrist. (56.)

English
By reciting hymns from Ṛk, Yajus, and Sāman they performed the holy rites. All the sages were greatly pleased. (57.)

Sanskrit

English
In order to ward off obstacles they performed the adoration of the planets under my instruction. They worshipped the gods stationed in the altar. (58.)

English
After performing the social and Vedic rites in a fitting manner Śiva became pleased and bowed to the brahmins joyously. (59.)

Then the lord of all started from the excellent mountain Kailāsa keeping the brahmins and the gods ahead. (60.)
