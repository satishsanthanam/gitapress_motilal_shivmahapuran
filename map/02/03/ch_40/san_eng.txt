### **CHAPTER FORTY - *(The Marriage Procession of Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
४०

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ शम्भुः समाहूय नन्द्यादीन् सकलान्गणान् ।
आज्ञापयामास मुदा गन्तुं स्वेन च तत्र वै ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Then Śiva called Nandin and other Gaṇas and ordered them to accompany Him. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
अपि यूयं सह मया सङ्गच्छध्वं गिरेः पुरम् ।
कियद्गणानिहास्थाप्य महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ २॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  Station a few Gaṇas here and the rest of you accompany me to the city of the mountain in a jovial mood. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ ते समनुज्ञप्ता गणेशा निर्ययुर्मुदा ।
स्वं स्वं बलमुपादाय तान् कथञ्चिद्वदाम्यहम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Then the lords of Gaṇas thus ordered took their armies and started joyously. I shall explain it in general terms. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्यगाच्छङ्खकर्णश्च गणकोट्या गणेश्वरः ।
शिवेन सार्धं सङ्गन्तुं हिमाचलपुरं प्रति ॥ ४॥

English
The lord of Gaṇas, Śaṅkhakarṇa started with a crore of Gaṇas to the city of Himavat along with Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दशकोट्या केकराक्षो गणानां स महोत्सवः ।
अष्टकोट्या च विकृतो गणानां गणनायकः ॥ ५॥

English
Kekarākṣa took ten crores of Gaṇas with gaiety. Vikṛta, the leader of Gaṇas, took eight crores of Gaṇas. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुष्कोट्या विशाखश्च गणानां गणनायकः ।
पारिजातश्च नवभिः कोटिभिर्गणपुङ्गवः ॥ ६॥

English
Viśākha took four crores and Pārijāta took nine crores of Gaṇas. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
षष्टिः सर्वान्तकः श्रीमान् तथैव विकृताननः ।
गणानां दुन्दुभोऽष्टाभिः कोटिभिर्गणनायकः ॥ ७॥

English
The glorious Sarvāntaka and Vikṛtānana took sixty crores. Dundubha took eight crores. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चभिश्च कपालाख्यो गणेशः कोटिभिस्तथा ।
षड्भिस्सन्दारको वीरो गणानां कोटिभिर्मुने ॥ ८॥

English
O sage, Kapāla took five crores and the heroic Sandāraka took six crores of Gaṇas. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिकोटिभिरेवेह कन्दुकः कुण्डकस्तथा ।
विष्टम्भो गणपोऽष्टाभिर्गणानां कोटिभिस्तथा ॥ ९॥

English
Kanduka and Kuṇḍaka took a crore of the Gaṇas. Viṣṭambha took eight crores. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रकोट्या गणपः पिप्पलो मुदितो ययौ ।
तथा सनादको वीरो गणेशो मुनिसत्तम ॥ १०॥

English
The leader Pippala joyously went with a thousand crores. O excellent sage, Sanādaka the hero also took so many. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
आवेशनस्तथाष्टाभिः कोटिभिर्गणनायकः ।
महाकेशः सहस्रेण कोटीनां गणपो ययौ ॥ ११॥

English
Āveśana went with eight crores. Mahākeśa took a thousand crores. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कुण्डो द्वादशकोट्या हि तथा पर्वतको मुने ।
अष्टाभिः कोटिभिर्वीरः समगाच्चन्द्रतापनः ॥ १२॥

English
O sage, Kuṇḍa and Parvataka each took twelve crores of Gaṇas with him. The heroic Candratāpana went with eight crores. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
कालश्च कालकश्चैव महाकालः शतेन वै ।
कोटीनां गणनाथो हि तथैवाग्निकनामकः ॥ १३॥

English
Kāla, Kālaka and Mahākāla each went with hundred crores of Gaṇas. The leader of Gaṇas named Agnika went with a crore. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कोट्यग्निमुख एवागाद् गणानां गणनायकः ।
आदित्यमूर्धा कोट्या च तथा चैव घनावहः ॥ १४॥

English
Agnimukha, the leader of Gaṇas, went with a crore. Ādityamūrdhā and Ghanāvaha each went with a crore of Gaṇas. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्नाहः शतकोट्या हि कुमुदो गणपस्तथा ।
अमोघः कोकिलश्चैव शतकोट्या गणाधिपः ॥ १५॥

English
Sannāha and Kumuda went with hundred crores. So also Amogha and Kokila each went with hundred crores. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सुमन्त्रः कोटिकोट्या च गणानां गणानायकः ।
काकपादोदरः कोटिषष्ट्या सन्तानकस्तथा ॥ १६॥

English
Sumantra, the leader of Gaṇas, went with a crore of Gaṇas. Kākapādodara and Santānaka went each with six crores of Gaṇas. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
महाबलश्च नवभिर्मधुपिङ्गश्च कोकिलः ।
नीलो नवत्या कोटीनां पूर्णभद्रस्तथैव च ॥ १७॥

English
Mahābala, Madhupiṅga and Kokila each went with nine crores. Nila and Pūrṇabhadra each went with ninety crores of Gaṇas. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तकोट्या चतुर्वक्त्रः करणो विंशकोटिभिः ।
ययौ नवतिकोट्या तु गणेशानोऽहिरोमकः ॥ १८॥

English
Caturvaktra with seven crores, Karaṇa with twenty crores and the leader of Gaṇas Ahiromaka went with ninety crores. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्वाक्षः शतमन्युश्च मेघमन्युश्च नारद ।
तावत्कोट्या ययुः सर्वे गणेशा हि पृथक् पृथक् ॥ १९॥

English
O Nārada, Yajvākṣa, Śatamanyu and Meghamanyu each of these leaders too went with so many crores. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
काष्ठाङ्गुष्ठश्चतुः षष्ट्या कोटीनां गणनायकः ।
विरूपाक्षः सुकेशश्च वृषभश्च सनातनः ॥ २०॥

English
Kāṣṭhāgūḍha, the leader of Gaṇas, went with sixtyfour crores. So too Virūpākṣa, Sukeśa, Vṛṣabha and Sanātana. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तालकेतुः षडास्यश्च चञ्च्वास्यश्च सनातनः ।
संवर्तकस्तथा चैत्रो लकुलीशः स्वयम्प्रभुः ॥ २१॥

English
Similarly Tālaketu, Ṣaḍāsya, the eternal Cañcvasya Samvartaka, Caitra and the lord Lakuliśa himself. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकान्तकश्च दीप्तात्मा तथा दैत्यान्तको मुने ।
देवो भृङ्गिरिटिः श्रीमान्देवदेवप्रियस्तथा ॥ २२॥

अशनिर्भानुकश्चैव चतुःषष्ट्या सहस्रशः ।
ययुः शिवविवाहार्थं शिवेन सहसोत्सवाः ॥ २३॥

English
The brilliant Lokāntaka, Daityāntaka, lord Bhṛṅgiriṭi, the glorious Devapriya, Aśani and Bhānuka went with sixtyfour crores. Others in jovial mood went in thousands with Śiva to attend his marriage, O sage. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतकोटिसहस्रेण प्रमथाः कोटिभिस्त्रिभिः ।
वीरभद्रश्चतुःषष्ट्या रोमजानान्त्रिकोटिभिः ॥ २४॥

English
A thousand crores of Bhūtas and three crores of Pramathas went. Virabhadra went with sixtyfour crores of Gaṇas and three crores of Romajas. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिकोटिसहस्राणां शतैर्विंशतिभिर्वृताः ।
तत्र जग्मुश्च नन्द्याद्या गणपाः शङ्करोत्सवे ॥ २५॥

English
In the marriage procession of Śiva, Nandin and other leaders of Gaṇas went surrounded by hundreds and twenties of crores of Gaṇas. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेत्रपालो भैरवश्च कोटिकोटिगणैर्युतः ।
उद्वाहः शङ्करस्येत्याययौ प्रीत्या महोत्सवः ॥ २६॥

English
Knowing delightedly that it was Śiva's marriage, Bhairava the Kṣetrapāla went jovially with crores and crores of Gaṇas. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एते चान्ये च गणपा असङ्ख्याता महाबलाः ।
तत्र जग्मुर्महाप्रीत्या सोत्साहाः शङ्करोत्सवे ॥ २७॥

English
These and other leaders of Gaṇas of great strength and multitudinous in number joined the procession with joy and enthusiasm. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे सहस्रहस्ताश्च जटामुकुटधारिणः ।
चन्द्ररेखावतंसाश्च नीलकण्ठास्त्रिलोचनाः ॥ २८॥

English
They had a thousand hands. They wore matted hair and crowns. They were bedecked with streaks of the moon. They had three eyes and blue necks (like lord Śiva). (28.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्राक्षाभरणाः सर्वे तथा सद्भस्मधारिणः ।
हारकुण्डलकेयूरमुकुटाद्यैरलङ्कृताः ॥ २९॥

English
All of them wore garlands of Rudrākṣa beads. They had the holy ashes smeared over the body. They had the ornaments of necklaces, earrings, bracelets, crowns etc. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मविष्ण्विन्द्रसङ्काशा अणिमादिगुणैर्युताः ।
सूर्यकोटिप्रतीकाशास्तत्र रेजुर्गणेश्वराः ॥ ३०॥

English
The leaders were as refulgent as Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Indra, Aṇimā and other Energies.* They were as brilliant and lustrous as crores of suns. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिवीचारिणः केचित् केचित्पातालचारिणः ।
केचिद्व्योमचराः केचित्सप्तस्वर्गचरा मुने ॥ ३१॥

English
O sage, some of them belonged to this terrestrial world, some came through nether worlds, some came through the sky and some came through seven heavens. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन देवर्षे सर्वलोकनिवासिनः ।
आययुः स्वगणाः शम्भोः प्रीत्या वै शङ्करोत्सवे ॥ ३२॥

English
Of what avail is this talk ? O celestial sage, Śiva's own Gaṇas living in all the worlds came and joined the procession of Śiva, with pleasure. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं देवैर्गणैश्चान्यैः सहितः शङ्करः प्रभुः ।
ययौ हिमगिरिपुरं विवाहार्थं निजस्य वै ॥ ३३॥

English
Thus, lord Śiva, accompanied by his Gaṇas, gods and others, went to the city of Himagiri for the celebration of His marriage. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा जगाम सर्वेशो विवाहार्थं सुरादिभिः ।
तदा तत्र ह्यभूद्वृत्तं तच्छृणु त्वं मुनीश्वर ॥ ३४॥

English
O great sage, listen to another incident that happened when Śiva, the lord of all, went for his marriage along with the gods and others. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रस्य भगिनी भूत्वा चण्डी सूत्सवसंयुता ।
तत्राजगाम सुप्रीत्या परेषां सुभयावहा ॥ ३५॥

English
Rudra's sister Caṇḍī assuming a great festive mood came there with great pleasure but inspiring terror in others. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रेतासनसमारूढा सर्पाभरणभूषिता ।
पूर्णं कलशमादाय हैमं मूर्ध्नि महाप्रभम् ॥ ३६॥

English
She was riding on a ghost. She was bedecked in the ornaments of serpents. A gold pot filled (with water) shone over her head. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपरीवारसंयुक्ता दीप्तास्या दीप्तलोचना ।
कुतूहलं प्रकुर्वन्ती जातहर्षा महाबला ॥ ३७॥

English
She was accompanied by her attendants. Her face was beaming. Her eyes dazzled. She was enthusiastic and glad. She was strong. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र भूतगणा दिव्या विरूपाः कोटिशो मुने ।
विराजन्ते स्म बहुशः तथा नानाविधास्तदा ॥ ३८॥

English
The divine Bhūta attendants were crores and crores in number. They shone in diverse forms. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तैः समेताग्रतश्चण्डी जगाम विकृतानना ।
कुतूहलान्विता प्रीता प्रीत्युपद्रवकारिणी ॥ ३९॥

English
Accompanied by them Caṇḍī of deformed face went ahead gladly and enthusiastically. She was equally competent to please and to harass. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
चण्ड्या सर्वे रुद्रगणाः पृष्ठतश्च कृतास्तदा ।
कोट्येकादशसङ्ख्याका रौद्ररुद्रप्रियाश्च ते ॥ ४०॥

English
All the Gaṇas of Śiva numbering to eleven crores, terrible but favourites of Śiva were kept by her far behind. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा डमरुनिर्घोषैर्व्याप्तमासीज्जगत्त्रयम् ।
भेरीझङ्कारशब्देन शङ्खानां निनदेन च ॥ ४१॥

English
The loud sounds of Damarus, the Jhaṅkāra sound of the Bheris and the sound of the conches pervaded all the three worlds. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा दुन्दुभिनिर्घोषैः शब्दः कोलाहलोऽभवत् ।
कुर्वञ्जगन्मङ्गलं च नाशयेन्मङ्गलेतरत् ॥ ४२॥

English
The tumultuous sound of the Dundubhis rose up in the air blessing the universe auspiciously and destroying everything other than auspicious. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
गणानां पृष्ठतो भूत्वा सर्वे देवाः समुत्सुकाः ।
अन्वयुः सर्वसिद्धाश्च लोकपालादिका मुने ॥ ४३॥

English
O sage, behind the Gaṇas, the enthusiastic gods, the Siddhas, the guardians of the quarters and others followed. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
मध्ये व्रजन् रमेशोऽथ गरुडासनमाश्रितः ।
शुशुभे ध्रियमाणेन छत्रेण महता मुने ॥ ४४॥

English
O sage, Viṣṇu, seated on Garuḍa and going in the middle of the group shone with the great umbrella held aloft. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
चामरैर्वीज्यमानोऽसौ स्वगणैः परिवारितः ।
पार्षदैर्विलसद्भिश्च स्वभूषाविधिभूषितः ॥ ४५॥

English
He was surrounded by his attendants who fanned him with Cāmaras. His Pārṣadas too shone well. He was bedecked in all his ornaments. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तथाहमप्यशोभं वै व्रजन्मार्गे विराजितः ।
वेदैर्मूर्तिधरैः शास्त्रैः पुराणैरागमैस्तथा ॥ ४६॥

सनकादिमहासिद्धैः सप्रजापतिभिः सुतैः ।
परिवारैः संयुतो हि शिवसेवनतत्परः ॥ ४७॥

English
I too shone well on the way with the Vedas, Śāstras, Purāṇas and Āgamas personified and along with my sons, Prajāpatis, Sanaka and other Siddhas. I was eager in rendering service to Śiva. (46-47.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वसैन्यमध्यगः शक्र ऐरावतगजस्थितः ।
नानाविभूषितोऽत्यन्तं व्रजन् रेजे सुरेश्वरः ॥ ४८॥

English
Going along, seated on the elephant Airāvata in the midst of his armies, Indra, the lord of god, shone well fully decorated in various ways. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

विरेजुरतिसोत्कण्ठाः शिवस्योद्वाहनं प्रति ॥ ४९॥

English
Many other sages enthusiastic about the marriage of Śiva shone well on their way. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

Śākinīs, Yātudhānas Vetālas, Brahmarākṣasas, Bhūtas, Pretas and Pramathas, Tumburu, Nārada, Hāhā, Hūhū,⁹⁹ Gandharvas and Kinnaras went ahead playing on their musical instruments with great delight. (50-51.)

[fn 99. Tumbaru, Hāhā and Hūhū were the Gandharvas, attending on Kubera. They were expert musicians who played on Viṇā and other musical instruments and were generally invited at festivities]

उद्वाहः शङ्करस्येति जग्मुः सर्वा मुदान्विताः ॥ ५३॥

English
The Mothers of the universe, the celestial-virgins, Gāyatri, Sāvitri, Lakṣmī, the celestial maidens, the wives of the gods, the mothers of the worlds went ahead with great joy, only because it was the marriage of Śiva. (52-53.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशो वृषभः सर्वसुन्दरः ।
यो धर्म उच्यते वेदैः शास्त्रैः सिद्धैर्महर्षिभिः ॥ ५४॥

तमारूढो महादेवो वृषभं धर्मवत्सलः ।
शुशुभेऽतीव देवर्षिसेवितः सकलैर्व्रजन् ॥ ५५॥

English
Lord Śiva, favourably disposed to virtue, was (54-55.)

---

English
Tumbaru, Hāhā and Hūhū were the Gandharvas, attending on Kubera. They were expert musicians who played on Viṇā and other musical instruments and were generally invited at festivities seated on his bull of crystal purity and beauty—the bull who is called Dharma by the Vedas, Śāstras, Siddhas and sages. Śiva was being served by the gods and sages on his way. He shone well. (99.)

---

Sanskrit
एभिः समेतैः सफलैर्महर्षिभि-
र्बभौ महेशो बहुशोऽत्यलङ्कृतः ।
हिमालयाह्वस्य धरस्य संव्रजन्
पाणिग्रहार्थं सदनं शिवायाः ॥ ५६॥

English
Accompanied by all these sages and decorated in various ways, lord Śiva was going to the abode of the mountain Himālaya for the marriage with Pārvati. He shone well. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तं शम्भुचरितं गमनं परमोत्सवम् ।
हिमालयपुरोद्भूतं सद्वृत्तं श‍ृणु नारद ॥ ५७॥

English
Thus the story of Śiva’s grand procession has been narrated to you. O Nārada, listen to the incidents of good portent that happened in the city of Himavat. (57.)
