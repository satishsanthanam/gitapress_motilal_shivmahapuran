### **CHAPTER FORTYONE - *(Description of the Altar-Structure)***

---

Sanskrit
४१

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततः सम्मन्त्र्य च मिथः प्राप्याज्ञां शाङ्करीं हरिः ।
मुने त्वां प्रेषयामास प्रथमं कुधरालयम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Then after mutual consultation and getting Śiva’s permission, O sage, Viṣṇu sent you ahead to the abode of the mountain. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रणम्य सर्वेशं गतस्त्वं नारदाग्रतः ।
हरिणा नोदितः प्रीत्या हिमाचलगृहं प्रति ॥ २॥

English
Urged by Viṣṇu, O Nārada, you bowed to lord Śiva and went ahead of all to the abode of Himavat. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं मुनेऽपश्य आत्मानं गत्वा तद्व्रीडयान्वितम् ।
कृत्रिमं रचितं तत्र विस्मितो विश्वकर्मणा ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, after going there, you saw your own image made by Viśvakarman and were surprised. You were a bit ashamed too. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रान्तस्त्वमात्मना तेन कृत्रिमेण महामुने ।
अवलोकपरः सोऽभूच्चरितं विश्वकर्मणः ॥ ४॥

English
O great sage, tired of seeing the portrayal of yourself, you became engaged in seeing the other builds of Viśvakarman. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रविष्टो मण्डपं तस्य हिमाद्रे रत्नचित्रितम् ।
सुवर्णकलशैर्जुष्टं रम्भादिबहुशोभितम् ॥ ५॥

English
You entered the great altar of Himavat, studded with various gems and decorated with gold pots and stumps of plantain trees. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रस्तम्भसंयुक्तं विचित्रं परमाद्भुतम् ।
वेदिकां च तथा दृष्ट्वा विस्मयं त्वं मुने ह्ययाः ॥ ६॥

English
It had a thousand columns. It was wonderful. O sage, you were struck with surprise on seeing the altars. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तदावोचश्च स मुने नारद त्वं नगेश्वरम् ।
विस्मितोऽतीव मनसि नष्टज्ञानो विमूढधीः ॥ ७॥

English
Then you were a bit confused and greatly bewildered. You spoke to the lord of mountains thus. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतास्ते किमधुना देवा विष्णुपुरोगमाः ।
तथा महर्षयः सर्वे सिद्धा उपसुरास्तथा ॥ ८॥

महादेवो वृषारूढो गणैश्च परिवारितः ।
आगतः किं विवाहार्थं वद तथ्यं नगेश्वर ॥ ९॥

English
O lord of mountains, tell me the truth. Has lord Śiva seated on his bull and surrounded by his Gaṇas come already for the marriage? Have the gods with Viṣṇu and others at their head, the sages, the Siddhas and the secondary gods come already ?” (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा तव विस्मितचेतसः ।
उवाच त्वां मुने तथ्यं वाक्यं स हिमवान् गिरिः ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing your words full of surprise, O sage, the mountain Himavat told you the truth. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमवानुवाच ।
हे नारद महाप्राज्ञागतो नैवाधुना शिवः ।
विवाहार्थं च पार्वत्याः सगणः सवरातकः ॥ ११॥

English
*Himavat said :—*  O Nārada, O highly intelligent one, Śiva with the marriage party has not come till now for the purpose of marrying Pārvatī. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वकर्मकृतं चित्रं विद्धि नारद सद्धिया ।
विस्मयं त्यज देवर्षे स्वस्थो भव शिवं स्मर ॥ १२॥

English
O Nārada, know that all these things have been portrayed by Viśvakarman. O celestial sage, shake off your bewilderment. Be calm. Remember Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
भुक्त्वा विश्रम्य सुप्रीतः कृपां कृत्वा ममोपरि ।
मैनाकादिधरैः सार्धं गच्छ त्वं शङ्करान्तिकम् ॥ १३॥

English
Showing kindness to me you take your food and rest for a while. Then gladly accompany Maināka and others to Śiva’s presence. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एभिस्समेतो गिरिभिर्महामते
सम्प्रार्थ्य शीघ्रं शिवमत्र चानय ।
देवैः समेतं च महर्षिसङ्घैः
सुरासुरैरर्चितपादपल्लवम् ॥ १४॥

English
Accompanied by these mountains you request Śiva along with the gods, and the great sages, Śiva whose sprout-like feet are worshipped by gods and demons. Bring them here. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तथेति चोक्त्वागम आशु हि त्वं
तदैव तैः शैलसुतादिभिश्च ।
तत्रत्यकृत्यं सुविधाय भुक्त्वा
महामनास्त्वं शिवसन्निधानम् ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  You accepted the suggestion noble-heartedly and performed the duties there. Then accompanied by the sons of the mountain and others you went to Śiva’s presence. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र दृष्टो महादेवो देवादिपरिवारितः ।
नमस्कृतस्त्वया दीप्तः शैलैस्तैर्भक्तितश्च वै ॥ १६॥

English
There the brilliant god Śiva surrounded by the gods and others was seen and bowed to by you and the mountains with devotion. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा मया विष्णुना च सर्वे देवाः सवासवाः ।
पप्रच्छुस्त्वां मुने सर्वे रुद्रस्यानुचरास्तथा ॥ १७॥

English
Then all the gods including Indra, Viṣṇu and me and Śiva’s attendants asked you, O sage. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
विस्मिताः पर्वतान्दृष्ट्वा सन्देहाकुलमानसाः ।
मैनाकसह्यमेर्वाद्यान्नानालङ्कारसंयुतान् ॥ १८॥

English
They had been struck with surprise and suspicion on seeing the mountains Maināka, Sahya, Meru and others bedecked in all kinds of ornaments. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
हे नारद महाप्राज्ञ विस्मितस्त्वं हि दृश्यसे ।
सत्कृतोऽसि हिमागेन किं न वा वद विस्तरात् ॥ १९॥

English
*The gods said :—*  O Nārada, intelligent one, you appear to be bewildered. Have you been duly honoured by Himavat or not ? Tell us in detail. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
एते कस्मात्समायाताः पर्वता इह सत्तमाः ।
मैनाकसह्यमेर्वाद्याः सुप्रतापाः स्वलङ्कृताः ॥ २०॥

English
Why have these excellent mountains Maināka, Sahya, Meru and others highly bedecked and of great valour, come here ? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कन्यां दास्यति शैलोऽसौ शम्भवे वा न नारद ।
हिमालयगृहे तात किं भवत्यद्य तद्वद ॥ २१॥

English
O Nārada, does the mountain really intend to give his daughter to Śiva or not ? What is it that is taking place in the abode of Himavat now ? Please tell us. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सन्दिग्धमनसामस्माकं च दिवौकसाम् ।
वद त्वं पृच्छमानानां सन्देहं हर सुव्रत ॥ २२॥

English
We are having doubts in our minds. Hence we, the heaven-dwellers, ask you. Please say everything, O righteous one, and dispel our suspicions. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां विष्ण्वादीनान्दिवौकसाम् ।
अवोचस्तान्मुने त्वं हि विस्मितस्त्वाष्ट्रमायया ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing these words of Viṣṇu and other heaven-dwellers, O sage, you who had been fascinated by the magic of Tvaṣṭr (Viśvakarman) spoke to them. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एकान्तमाश्रित्य च मां हि विष्णु-
मभाषथा वाक्यमिदं मुने त्वम् ।
शचीपतिं सर्वसुरेश्वरं वै
पक्षच्छिदं पूर्वरिपुं धराणाम् ॥ २४॥

English
Going to an isolated place, O sage, you spoke these words to me, to Viṣṇu and also to Indra who is the lord of gods and a former enemy of the mountains, having cut off their wings.¹⁰⁰ (24.)

[fn 100. Indra is said to have clipped the wings of the mountains when they grew troublesome.]
---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
त्वष्ट्रा कृतं तद्विकृतं विचित्रं
विमोहनं सर्वदिवौकसां हि ।
येनैव सर्वान्स विमोहितुं सुरान्
समिच्छति प्रेमत एव युक्त्या ॥ २५॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  The distorted portrayal of heaven-dwellers is something enchanting. He desires to delude the gods in a loving but cunning manner. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा कृतं तस्य विमोहनं त्वया
सुविस्मृतं तत् सकलं शचीपते ।
तस्मादसौ त्वां विजिगीषुरेव
गृहे ध्रुवं तस्य गिरेर्महात्मनः ॥ २६॥

English
O lord of Śaci, have you forgotten everything ? Formerly you had deluded him. Hence he wishes to surpass you here in the abode of the mountain of noble heart. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विमोहितस्तेन प्रतिरूपेण भास्वता ।
तथा विष्णुः कृतस्तेन ब्रह्मा शक्रोऽपि तादृशः ॥ २७॥

English
I have been fascinated by my shining portrait. Viṣṇu, Brahmā and Indra have been realistically portrayed by him. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन देवेश सर्वदेवगणाः कृताः ।
कृत्रिमाश्चित्ररूपेण न किञ्चिदवशेषितम् ॥ २८॥

English
O lord of gods, why should I talk too much ? He has made artificial prototypes of all the gods. No one, not a single detail, has been left out. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
विमोहनार्थं सर्वेषां देवानां च विशेषतः ।
कृता माया चित्रमयी परिहासविकारिणी ॥ २९॥

English
It is for the purpose of particularly enchanting the gods that this spell has been employed by him through this caricature. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य देवेन्द्रो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ।
विष्णुम्प्रति तदा शीघ्रं भयाकुलतनुर्हरिम् ॥ ३०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing your words lord Indra who was frighttened from head to foot, immediately spoke to Viṣṇu. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
देवेन्द्र उवाच ।
देवदेव रमानाथ त्वष्टा मां निहनिष्यति ।
पुत्रशोकेन तप्तोऽसौ व्याजेनानेन नान्यथा ॥ ३१॥

English
*Lord Indra said :—*  O lord of Lakṣmī, O lord of gods, Tvaṣṭr who is agitated due to the grief over his son will surely kill me under this pretext and not otherwise. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा देवदेवो जनार्दनः ।
उवाच प्रहसन् वाक्यं शक्रमाश्वासयंस्तदा ॥ ३२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing his words Viṣṇu, the lord of gods laughingly consoled Indra by speaking thus. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
निवातकवचैः पूर्वं मोहितोऽसि शचीपते ।
महाविद्याबलेनैव दानवैः पूर्ववैरिभिः ॥ ३३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O lord of Śacī, formerly you had been enchanted by the demons Nivātakavacas, your previous enemies, by the power of their great spell. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वतो हिमवानेष तथान्येऽखिलपर्वताः ।
विपक्षा हि कृताः सर्वे मम वाक्याच्च वासव ॥ ३४॥

English
O Indra, at my instance, this mountain Himavat and others too were rendered wingless. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तेऽनुस्मृत्या तु वै दृष्ट्वा मायया गिरयो ह्यमी ।
जेतुमिच्छन्ति ये मूढा न भेतव्यमरावपि ॥ ३५॥

English
Let the mountains now create magic on remembering that and wish to surpass us foolishly. We are not to be afraid of our enemies. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वरो नो हि सर्वेषां शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
सर्वथा कुशलं शक्र करिष्यति न संशयः ॥ ३६॥

English
O Indra, Śiva favourably disposed to His devotees, will undoubtedly look to our welfare. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं संवदमानं तं शक्रं विकृतमानसम् ।
हरिणोक्तश्च गिरिशो लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ॥ ३७॥

English
While he was speaking this to Indra of agitated mind, Śiva spoke to Viṣṇu following the worldly custom. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
हे हरे हे सुरेशान किं ब्रूथोऽद्य परस्परम् ।
इत्युक्त्वा तौ महेशानो मुने त्वां प्रत्युवाच सः ॥ ३८॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  “O Viṣṇu, O lord of gods, what are you speaking to each other?” O sage, after speaking thus to them Śiva addressed you. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
किं नु वक्ति महाशैलो यथार्थं वद नारद ।
वृत्तान्तं सकलं ब्रूहि न गोप्यं कर्तुमर्हसि ॥ ३९॥

English
“O Nārada, what does the great mountain say ? Tell me the truth with details. You must not keep any secret. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ददाति वा नैव ददाति शैलः
सुतां स्वकीयां वद तच्च शीघ्रम् ।
किं ते दृष्टं किं कृतन्तत्र गत्वा
प्रीत्या सर्वं तद्वदाश्वद्य तात ॥ ४०॥

English
Does the mountain want to give the daughter or not ? Tell me that quickly. O dear one, on going there, what did you see ? What did you do ? Tell me that quickly. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
हत्युक्तः शम्भुना तत्र मुने त्वं देवदर्शनः ।
सर्वं रहस्यवोचो वै यद् दृष्टं तत्र मण्डपे ॥ ४१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Addressed thus by Śiva, O sage, you endowed with divine vision told him secretly what you saw in the altar. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव श‍ृणु मद्वचनं शुभम् ।
नास्ति विघ्नभयं नाथ विवाहे किञ्चिदेव हि ॥ ४२॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O great lord, lord of gods, listen to my auspicious words. O lord, there is no fear of any hindrance in the celebration of marriage. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
अवश्यमेव शैलेशस्तुभ्यं दास्यति कन्यकाम् ।
त्वामानयितुमायाता इमे शैला न संशयः ॥ ४३॥

English
The lord of mountains will surely give his daughter to you. It is certainly to take you there that these mountains have come here. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
किन्तु ह्यमरमोहार्थं माया विरचिताद्भुता ।
कुतूहलार्थं सर्वज्ञ न कश्चिद्विघ्नसम्भवः ॥ ४४॥

English
But to delude the gods a wonderful spell has been created. O omniscient, it is only to inspire curiosity. There is no possibility of any obstacle. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
विचित्रं मण्डपं गेहेऽकार्षीत्तस्य तदाज्ञया ।
विश्वकर्मा महामायी नानाश्चर्यमयं विभो ॥ ४५॥

English
O lord, Viśvakarman, a great expert in creating illusion has constructed a peculiar altar in his house at his instance. It is full of surprising things. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वदेवसमाजश्च कृतस्तत्र विमोहनः ।
तं दृष्ट्वा विस्मयं प्राप्तोऽहं तन्मायाविमोहितः ॥ ४६॥

English
A fascinating assembly of gods has been built there. On seeing it I was deluded by his skill and was struck with surprise. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा तद्वचस्तात लोकाचारकरः प्रभुः ।
हर्यादीन्प्रहसन् शम्भुरुवाच सकलान्सुरान् ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O dear, on hearing your words, the lord Śiva following the worldly convention, laughingly spoke to Viṣṇu and other gods. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
कन्यां दास्यति चेन्मह्यं पर्वतो हि हिमाचलः ।
मायया मम किं कार्यं वद विष्णो यथातथम् ॥ ४८॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O Viṣṇu, if the mountain Himavat gives his daughter to me, what have I to do with this spell? Speak to me what is true. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
हे ब्रह्मन् शक्र मुनयः सुरा ब्रूत यथार्थतः ।
मायया मम किं कार्यं कन्यां दास्यति चेद्गिरिः ॥ ४९॥

English
O Brahmā, O Indra, O sages, O gods, speak truly. What have I to do with the spell if the mountain gives his daughter ? (49.)

---

Sanskrit
केनाप्युपायेन फलं हि साध्य-
मित्युच्यते पण्डितैर्न्यायविद्भिः ।
तस्मात्सर्वैर्गम्यतां शीघ्रमेव
कार्यार्थिभिर्विष्णुपुरोगमैश्च ॥ ५०॥

English
It is held by scholars, knowing cogent reasons, that somehow or other the fruit should be achieved. Hence you, with Viṣṇu at the head, will hasten seeking only the task on hand. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं संवदमानोऽसौ देवैः शम्भुरभूत्तदा ।
कृतः स्मरेणेव वशी वशं वा प्राकृतो नरः ॥ ५१॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Discussing thus with gods, Śiva appeared to be completely overpowered by Kāma like an ordinary man. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भ्वाज्ञया सर्वे विष्ण्वाद्या निर्जरास्तदा ।
ऋषयश्च महात्मानो ययुर्मोहभ्रमावहम् ॥ ५२॥

पुरस्कृत्य मुने त्वां च पर्वतांस्तान्सविस्मयाः ।
हिमाद्रेश्च तदा जग्मुर्मन्दिरं परमाद्भुतम् ॥ ५३॥

अथ विष्ण्वादिसंयुक्तो मुदितैःस्वबलैर्युतः ।
आजगामोपहैमागपुरं प्रमुदितो हरः ॥ ५४॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva, Viṣṇu and other gods, the noble sages and others, O sage, kept you and mountains at the head and started for the abode of Himavat. They were surprised to see the wonderful abode. The delighted Śiva reached the outskirts of the city accompanied by Viṣṇu and others as well as his delighted Gaṇas. (52-54.)
