### **CHAPTER FORTYTWO - *(Description of the meeting of the lord and the mountain)***

---

Sanskrit
४२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथाकर्ण्य गिरीशश्च निजपुर्युपकण्ठतः ।
प्राप्तमीशं सर्वगं वै मुमुदेऽति हिमालयः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahma said :—*  On hearing that the all-pervading Śiva had come very near his city, the lord of mountains Himavat rejoiced much. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सम्भृतसम्भारः सम्भाषां कर्तुमीश्वरम् ।
शैलान्प्रस्थापयामास ब्राह्मणानपि सर्वशः ॥ २॥

English
Then, gathering all the requisite things he sent mountains and the brahmins to welcome Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयं जगाम सद्भक्त्या प्राणेप्सुं द्रष्टुमीश्वरम् ।
भक्त्युद्रुतमनाः शैलः प्रशंसन् स्वविधिं मुदा ॥ ३॥

English
With his mind melting with devotion and joyously praising his luck, the mountain personally went to see Śiva as dear as the very vital air. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
देवसेनां तदा दृष्ट्वा हिमवान्विस्मयं गतः ।
जगाम सम्मुखस्तत्र धन्योऽहमिति चिन्तयन् ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing the army of the gods Himavat was struck with wonder. Considering himself blessed he appeared in front of them. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा हि तद्बलं दृष्ट्वा विस्मयं परमं गताः ।
आनन्दं परमं प्रापुर्देवाश्च गिरयस्तथा ॥ ५॥

English
The gods too were struck with wonder on seeing his army. The gods and the mountains became delighted. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वतानां महासेना देवानां च तथा मुने ।
मिलित्वा विरराजेव पूर्वपश्चिमसागरौ ॥ ६॥

English
The vast army of the mountains and the gods, O sage, on coming together shone like the eastern and western oceans in juxtaposition. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
परस्परं मिलित्वा ते देवाश्च पर्वतास्तथा ।
कृतकृत्यं तथात्मानं मेनिरे परया मुदा ॥ ७॥

English
Meeting each other, the gods and the mountains considered themselves blessed. They were greatly delighted. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथेश्वरं पुरो दृष्ट्वा प्रणनाम हिमालयः ।
सर्वे प्रणेमुर्गिरयो ब्राह्मणाश्च सदाशिवम् ॥ ८॥

English
Seeing Śiva in front, Himavat bowed to Him. The mountains and the brahmins bowed to Sadāśiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषभस्थं प्रसन्नास्यं नानाभरणभूषितम् ।
दिव्यावयवलावण्यप्रकाशितदिगन्तरम् ॥ ९॥

सुसूक्ष्माहतसत्पट्टवस्त्रशोभितविग्रहम् ।
सद्रत्नविलसन्मौलिं विहसन्तं शुचिप्रभम् ॥ १०॥

भूषाभूताहियुक्ताङ्गमद्भुतावयवप्रभम् ।
दिव्यद्युतिं सुरेशैश्च सेवितं करचामरैः ॥ ११॥

वामस्थिताच्युतं दक्षभागस्थितविधिं प्रभुम् ।
पृष्ठस्थितहरिं पृष्ठपार्श्वस्थितसुरादिकम् ॥ १२॥

नानाविधसुराद्यैश्च संस्तुतं लोकशङ्करम् ।
स्वहेत्वात्ततनुं ब्रह्म सर्वेशं वरदायकम् ॥ १३॥

सगुणं निर्गुणं चापि भक्ताधीनं कृपाकरम् ।
प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्यापि परं सच्चित्सुखात्मकम् ॥ १४॥

English
He was seated on his bull, fully bedecked in ornaments and beaming in the face. The beauty of his divine person illuminated the quarters. His body shone in the delicate silken garments. His crown was lustrous with the gems set in it. He was smiling shedding pure brilliance everywhere. Serpents had transformed themselves into ornaments on his body. He had a wonderful lustre and a divine refulgence. Gods served him with chowries in their hands. Viṣṇu was standing to the left, Brahmā to the right, Indra at his back. Behind on either side, the gods were standing. He was being eulogised by the gods and others. He looked benevolent to the people. Being one He had assumed different physical bodies for his own reasons. He was Brahman itself, the lord of all and the bestower of boons. He was both with or without attributes, subservient to the devotees, merciful, greater than primordial Being and primordial nature, Existence, Knowledge and Bliss itself. (9-14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभोर्दक्षिणभागे तु ददर्श हरिमच्युतम् ।
विनतातनयारूढं नानाभूषणभूषितम् ॥ १५॥

English
The mountain saw Viṣṇu, bedecked in ornaments and seated on Garuḍa to the right of lord Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभोश्च वामभागे तु मुने मां सन्ददर्श ह ।
चतुर्मुखं महाशोभं स्वपरीवारसंयुतम् ॥ १६॥

English
O sage, to the left of the lord stood I, the four-faced deity, shining brilliantly and accompanied by my attendants. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एतौ सुरेश्वरौ दृष्ट्वा शिवस्यातिप्रियौ सदा ।
प्रणनाम गिरीशश्च सपरीवार आदरात् ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing us both, great favourites of Śiva for ever, the lord of mountains with all his retinue respectfully bowed to us. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा शिवस्य पृष्ठे च पार्श्वयोः सुविराजितान् ।
देवादीन्प्रणनामासौ दृष्ट्वा गिरिवरेश्वरः ॥ १८॥

English
Similarly, on seeing the gods and others brilliantly shining behind lord Śiva and at his sides, the lord of mountains bowed to them. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाज्ञया पुरो भूत्वा जगाम स्वपुरं गिरिः ।
शेषहर्यात्मभूः शीघ्रं मुनिभिर्निर्जरादिभिः ॥ १९॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva the mountain went ahead to his city. Behind him went Viṣṇu, Brahmā, the sages and the gods. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे मुनिसुराद्याश्च गच्छन्तः प्रभुणा सह ।
गिरेः पुरं समुदिताः शशंसुर्बहु नारद ॥ २०॥

English
O Nārada, the sages, the gods and others accompanying the lord, praised the city of Himavat with great delight. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
रचिते शिखरे रम्ये संस्थाप्य देवतादिकम् ।
जगाम हिमवाँस्तत्र यत्रास्ति विधिवेदिका ॥ २१॥

English
Stationing the gods and others on his beautiful summit specially arranged for them, Himavat went to the place where the altar for the rites had been erected. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कारयित्वा विशेषेण चतुष्कं तोरणैर्युतम् ।
स्नानदानादिकं कृत्वा परीक्षामकरोत्तदा ॥ २२॥

English
After causing squares and quadrangles to be made with festoons, he performed the ceremonial ablutions, gave charitable gifts and supervised everything. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपुत्रान्प्रेषयामास शिवस्य निकटे तथा ।
हिमो विष्ण्वादिसम्पूर्णवर्गयुक्तस्य शैलराट् ॥ २३॥

English
Then he sent his sons to Śiva accompanied by all his attendants and followers, Viṣṇu and others. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तुमैच्छद्वराचारं महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ।
महाहर्षयुतः सर्वबन्धुयुग्घिमशैलराट् ॥ २४॥

English
The extremely delighted lord of mountains in the company of his kinsmen desired to perform the reception to the bridegroom with great pomp and ceremony. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते गिरिपुत्राश्च तत्र गत्वा प्रणम्य तम् ।
सस्ववर्गं प्रार्थनान्तामूचुः शैलेश्वरस्य वै ॥ २५॥

English
The sons of the mountain accompanied by their relatives went to Śiva and acquainted him with the request of the mountain. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्ते स्वालयं जग्मुः शैलपुत्रास्तदाज्ञया ।
शैलराजाय सञ्चख्युः ते चायान्तीति हर्षिताः ॥ २६॥

English
The sons of the mountain came back to their abode with his permission and informed the king of mountains gladly that the bridegroom and the party were on their way there. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवाः प्रार्थनान्तां गिरेः श्रुत्वातिहर्षिताः ।
मुने विष्ण्वादयः सर्वे सेश्वरा मुमुदुर्भृशम् ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, on hearing the request thus made, Viṣṇu and other gods with the lord rejoiced much. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा सुवेषं सर्वेऽपि निर्जरा मुनयो गणाः ।
गमनं चक्रुरन्येऽपि प्रभुणा गिरिराड्गृहम् ॥ २८॥

English
Dressed richly and exquisitely the gods, the gaṇas, the sages and others started towards the abode of lord Himavat. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे मेना द्रष्टुकामाभवच्छिवम् ।
प्रभोराह्वाययामास मुने त्वां मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ २९॥

English
In the meantime, Menā desired to see Śiva. O sage, through her lord, you, the excellent sage, were requisitioned there. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अगमस्त्वं मुने तत्र प्रभुणा प्रेरितस्तदा ।
मनसा शिवहृद्धेतुं पूर्णं कर्तुं तमिच्छता ॥ ३०॥

English
O sage, urged by the lord who desired to fulfil the task of Śiva you went there. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां प्रणम्य मुने मेना प्राह विस्मितमानसा ।
द्रष्टुकामा प्रभो रूपं शङ्करस्य मदापहम् ॥ ३१॥

English
O sage, after bowing to you, Menā with her heart full of surprise told you that she wanted to see the real form of lord Śiva that dispels haughtiness. (31.)

---
