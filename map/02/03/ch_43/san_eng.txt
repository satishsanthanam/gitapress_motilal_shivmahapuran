### **CHAPTER FORTYTHREE - *(Description of Śiva's wonderful sport)***

---

Sanskrit
४३

मेनोवाच ।
निरीक्षिष्यामि प्रथमं मुने तं गिरिजापतिम् ।
कीदृशं शिवरूपं हि यदर्थे तप उत्तमम् ॥ १॥

English
*Menā said :—*  O sage, I shall first see the bridegroom of Pārvatī. Let me have an idea of the form and features of Śiva for which she performed the great penance. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्यज्ञानपरा सा च दर्शनार्थं शिवस्य च ।
त्वया मुने समं सद्यश्चन्द्रशालां समागता ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus, urged by ignorance, O sage, she went to the terrace along with you to see Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि च तदा तस्यां ज्ञात्वाहङ्कारमात्मनः ।
प्राह विष्णुं च मां तात लीलां कृत्वाद्भुतां प्रभुः ॥ ३॥

English
Then Śiva, realising her false pride in herself, spoke to Viṣṇu and me as a part of His wonderful sport. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
मदाज्ञया युवां तातौ सदेवौ च पृथक्पृथक् ।
गच्छतं हि गिरिद्वारं वयं पश्चाद् व्रजेमहि ॥ ४॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  "At my bidding, O dear ones, both of you go one by one accompanied by the gods to the threshold of the mountain. I shall follow afterwards." (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य हरिः सर्वानाहूयोवाच तन्मयाः ।
सुराः सर्वे तथैवाशु गमनं चक्रुरुत्सुकाः ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā said :— On hearing it Viṣṇu called all and told them of his suggestion. The gods then walked in accordance with that suggestion enthusiastically. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थितां शिरोगृहे मेनां मुने विश्वेश्वरस्त्वया ।
तथैव दर्शयामास हृद्विभ्रंशो यथा भवेत् ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, the lord of the universe, let Menā stand on the terrace and see the procession along with you in order to make her mind confused. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन् समये मेना सेनां च परमां शुभाम् ।
निरीक्षन्ती मुने दृष्ट्वा सामान्यं हर्षिताभवत् ॥ ७॥

English
In the meantime, seeing the splendid vast army, O sage, Menā became delighted as usual. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं चैव गन्धर्वाः सुन्दराः सुभगास्तदा ।
आयाताः शुभवस्त्राढ्या नानालङ्कारभूषिताः ॥ ८॥

English
At the head of procession came the beautiful fastidious Gandharvas, dressed in rich clothes and bedecked in fine ornaments. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नानावाहनसंयुक्ता नानावाद्यपरायणाः ।
पताकाभिर्विचित्राभिरप्सरोगणसंयुताः ॥ ९॥

English
They rode on different vehicles. They played on musical instruments. Flags and banners of various colours and sizes fluttered on their chariots. The heavenly nymphs accompanied them. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दृष्ट्वा वसुं तत्र तत्पतिं परमप्रभुम् ।
मेना प्रहर्षिता ह्यासीच्छिवोऽयमिति चाब्रवीत् ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing Vasu,¹⁰¹ the lord of Vasus, along with Vasus, Menā became delighted and exclaimed—“O this is Śiva”. (10.)

[fn 101. Vasu is the chief of the eight Vasus who in fact are personifications of natural phenomena, viz. water, pole-star, moon, earth, wind, fire, dawn and light. Cf. Note 163, P. 162.]

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्य गणका एते न शिवोऽयं शिवापतिः ।
इत्येवं त्वं ततस्तां वै अवोच ऋषिसत्तम ॥ ११॥

English
O excellent sage, you told her “These are only the attendants of Śiva. This is not Śiva, the bridegroom.” (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं श्रुत्वा तदा मेना विचारे तत्पराभवत् ।
इतश्चाभ्यधिको यो वै स च कीदृग्भविष्यति ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing this, Menā fell athinking “A person greater than this ! Hā, how will he be !” (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे यक्षा मणिग्रीवादयश्च ये ।
तेषां सेना तया दृष्टा शोभादि द्विगुणीकृता ॥ १३॥

English
In the meantime she saw Maṇigrīva,¹⁰² the other Yakṣas and their vast army with twice the splendour of Vasus. (13.)

[fn 102. Maṇigrīva is one of the sons of Kubera, distinct from Maṇibhadra who is also a Yakṣa.]
---

Sanskrit
तत्पतिं च मणिग्रीवं दृष्ट्वा शोभान्वितं हि सा ।
अयं रुद्रः शिवास्वामी मेना प्राहेति हर्षिता ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing the lustrous Maṇigrīva the lord of Yakṣas, Menā was delighted and said—“This is Śiva, the bridegroom of Pārvatī.” (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नायं रुद्रः शिवास्वामी सेवकोऽयं शिवस्य वै ।
इत्यवोचोऽगपत्न्यै त्वं तावद्वह्निः स आगतः ॥ १५॥

English
“This is not Śiva, the bridegroom of Pārvatī. He is only an attendant of Śiva” said you to Menā, the wife of the mountain. By that time the god of fire passed by. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽपि द्विगुणां शोभां दृष्ट्वा तस्य च साब्रवीत् ।
रुद्रोऽयं गिरिजास्वामी तदा नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ १६॥

English
On seeing his splendour twice that of Yakṣas, she said “This is Śiva, the bridegroom of Pārvatī” but you said “No”. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तावद्यमः समायातः ततोऽपि द्विगुणप्रभः ।
तं दृष्ट्वा प्राह सा मेना रुद्रोऽयमिति हर्षिता ॥ १७॥

English
By that time Yama passed by with twice the splendour of the previous one. On seeing him delighted Menā exclaimed “This is Śiva”. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
नेति त्वमब्रवीस्तां वै तावन्निरृतिरागतः ।
बिभ्राणो द्विगुणां शोभां शुभः पुण्यजनप्रभुः ॥ १८॥

English
“No” said you. By that time Nirṛti, the lord of Puṇyajanas, passed by having twice the splendour of Yama. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा प्राह सा मेना रुद्रोऽयमिति हर्षिता ।
नेति त्वमब्रवीस्तां वै तावद्वरुण आगतः ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing him the delighted Menā said “This is Śiva”. “No” said you to her. By that time Varuṇa passed that way. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽपि द्विगुणां शोभां दृष्ट्वा तस्य च साब्रवीत् ।
रुद्रोऽयं गिरिजास्वामी तदा नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing his splendour twice that of Nirṛti, she said “This is Śiva, the bridegroom of Pārvatī”. But you said “No”. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तावद्वायुः समायातः ततोऽपि द्विगुणप्रभः ।
तं दृष्ट्वा प्राह सा मेना रुद्रोऽयमिति हर्षिता ॥ २१॥

English
By that time Vāyu passed by with twice the splendour of Varuṇa. On seeing him the delighted Menā said “This is Śiva”. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नेति त्वमब्रवीस्तां वै तावद्धनद आगतः ।
ततोऽपि द्विगुणां शोभां बिभ्राणो गुह्यकाधिपः ॥ २२॥

English
“No” said you. By that time Kubera, the lord of Guhyakas, passed by with twice the splendour of Vāyu. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा प्राह सा मेना रुद्रोऽयमिति हर्षिता ।
नेति त्वमब्रवीस्तां वै तावदीशान आगतः ॥ २३॥

English
On seeing him the delighted Menā said “This is Śiva”. “No”, said you to her. By that time Īśāna passed by. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽपि द्विगुणां शोभां दृष्ट्वा तस्य च साब्रवीत् ।
रुद्रोऽयं गिरिजास्वामी तदा नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ २४॥

English
On seeing his splendour twice that of Kubera, she said “This is Rudra, the bridegroom of Pārvatī”. But you said “No”. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तावदिन्द्रः समायातः ततोऽपि द्विगुणप्रभः ।
सर्वामरवरो नानादिव्यभास्त्रिदिवेश्वरः ॥ २५॥

English
Then came Indra, the most important of all the gods, the lord of the three worlds, endowed with divine refulgence and who had twice the splendour of Īśāna. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा शङ्करः सोऽयमिति सा प्राह मेनका ।
शक्रः सुरपतिश्चायं स नेति त्वं तदाब्रवीः ॥ २६॥

English
On seeing him Menakā said—“This is Śiva”. “Not he”, said you then “This is Indra, the lord of gods”. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तावच्चन्द्रः समायातः शोभां तद्द्विगुणां दधत् ।
दृष्ट्वा तं प्राह रुद्रोऽयं तां तु नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ २७॥

English
By that time the moon passed by with twice the splendour of Indra. On seeing him she said “This is Śiva” and you denied it. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्सूर्यः समायातः शोभां तद्द्विगुणां दधत् ।
दृष्ट्वा तं प्राह सा सोऽयं तां तु नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ २८॥

English
By that time the Sun passed by with twice the splendour of the moon. On seeing him she said “It is he”. You said to her “No”. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्समागतास्तत्र भृग्वाद्याश्च मुनीश्वराः ।
तेजसो राशयः सर्वे स्वशिष्यगणसंयुताः ॥ २९॥

English
By that time Bhṛgu and other sages, all highly lustrous and accompanied by their disciples, passed by. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मध्ये चैव वागीशं दृष्ट्वा सा प्राह मेनका ।
रुद्रोऽयं गिरिजास्वामी तदा नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing Bṛhaspati in their midst Menakā said :—“This is Śiva the master of Pārvatī”. Then you said “No”. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तावद्ब्रह्मा समायातः तेजसां राशिरुत्तमः ।
सर्षिवर्यसुतः साक्षाद्धर्मपुञ्ज इव स्तुतः ॥ ३१॥

English
By that time Brahmā passed by. He was in an excellent form of lustre, praised by excellent sages and looking like an embodied Dharma itself. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा मां तं तदा मेना महाहर्षवती मुने ।
सोऽयं शिवापतिः प्राह तां तु नेति त्वमब्रवीः ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, seeing me the highly delighted Menā said—“This is Pārvatī’s husband”. You said to her “No”. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र विष्णुर्देवः समागतः ।
सर्वशोभान्वितः श्रीमान्मेघश्यामश्चतुर्भुजः ॥ ३३॥

कोटिकन्दर्पलावण्यः पीताम्बरधरः स्वराट् ।
राजीवलोचनः शान्तः पक्षीन्द्रवरवाहनः ॥ ३४॥

शङ्खादिलक्षणैर्युक्तो मुकुटादिविभूषितः ।
श्रीवत्सवक्षा लक्ष्मीशो ह्यप्रमेयप्रभान्वितः ॥ ३५॥

English
In the meantime lord Viṣṇu came that way. He looked glorious and splendid, dark-blue like the fresh cloud and having four arms. He had the handsome features of numberless cupids. He wore yellow garments. He was the king of heaven with eyes resembling the petals of a lotus, and looked very calm. He had Garuḍa as his vehicle. He possessed all the characteristic signs conch etc. He was bedecked in crown and other ornaments. He wore Śrīvatsa on his chest. He had an uncommon splendour that was incomprehensible (33-35.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा चकिताक्ष्यासीन्महाहर्षेण साब्रवीत् ।
सोऽयं शिवापतिः साक्षाच्छिवो वै नात्र संशयः ॥ ३६॥

English
On seeing him Menā’s eyes became dazed. With great delight she said—“This is Śiva himself the bridegroom of Pārvatī. There is no doubt about it”. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ त्वं मेनकावाक्यमाकर्ण्योवाच ऊतिकृत् ।
नायं शिवापतिरयं किन्त्वयं केशवो हरिः ॥ ३७॥

शङ्कराखिलकार्यस्य ह्यधिकारी च तत्प्रियः ।
अतोऽधिको वरो ज्ञेयः स शिवः पार्वतीपतिः ॥ ३८॥

तच्छोभां वर्णितुं मेने मया नैव हि शक्यते ।
स एवाखिलब्रह्माण्डपतिः सर्वेश्वरः स्वराट् ॥ ३९॥

English
On hearing Menakā’s words you said—“ No, this is not the lord, the cause of protection and enjoyment. This is not the bridegroom of Pārvatī. This is Viṣṇu, the officer-in-charge of the marriage-party of Śiva and a great favourite of Śiva. The bridegroom Śiva is better than him. O Menā, it is impossible for me to describe his beauty. He is the lord of the entire universe, the lord of all, the Self-Emperor”. (37-39.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य मेना मेने च तां शुभाम् ।
महाधनां भाग्यवतीं कुलत्रयसुखावहाम् ॥ ४०॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing your words, Menā thought her daughter auspicious, rich, fortunate and harbinger of happiness for the three families. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

स्वभाग्यमधिकं चापि वर्णयन्ती मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ४१॥

English
Her face was beaming with pleasure and her heart was delighted. Frequently congratulating herself on her good luck she said :— (41.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
धन्याहं सर्वथा जाता पार्वत्या जन्मनाधुना ।
धन्यो गिरीश्वरोऽप्यद्य सर्वं धन्यतमं मम ॥ ४२॥

English
*Menā said :—*  By the birth of Pārvatī, I have become blessed in every respect. The lord of mountains too is blessed. Every thing connected with me is blessed. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

एतेषां यः पतिः सोऽत्र पतिरस्या भविष्यति ॥ ४३॥

English
Her would-be-husband is the lord of these leaders of great lustre whom I have seen now. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्याः किं वर्ण्यते भाग्यमपि वर्षशतैरपि ।
वर्णितुं शक्यते नैव तत्प्रभुप्राप्तिदर्शनात् ॥ ४४॥

English
How can I describe her good luck even in hundred years? It is impossible to describe it when I see the lustre of these leaders. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्यवादीच्च सा मेना प्रेमनिर्भरमानसा ।
तावत्समागतो रुद्रोऽद्भुतोतिकारकः प्रभुः ॥ ४५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus spoke Menā with her mind full of love and hope. By that time Śiva, the wonderful source of enjoyment and protection, came that way. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

आत्मानं दर्शयन् मायानिर्लिप्तं निर्विकारकम् ॥ ४६॥

English
He showed himself in his real form free from change of illusion. O dear, the Gaṇas of wonderful forms proved to be the dispeller of Menā’s pride. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

मेनामवोचः सुप्रीत्या दर्शयंस्तं शिवापतिम् ॥ ४७॥

English
O sage Nārada, on seeing Him come, you lovingly pointed him out to her as the bridegroom of Śivā and spoke to her. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

अयं स शङ्करः साक्षाद् दृश्यतां सुन्दरि त्वया ।
यदर्थे शिवया तप्तं तपोऽति विपिने महत् ॥ ४८॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  This is Śiva Himself, O comely maiden, see. It was for him that Pārvatī performed a great penance in the forest. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा हर्षिता मेना तं ददर्श मुदा प्रभुम् ।
अद्भुताकृतिमीशानमद्भुतानुगमद्भुतम् ॥ ४९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Thus addressed by you the delighted Menā stared at the lord with joy; the lord Īśāna of wonderful features and of wonderful attendants. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

भूतप्रेतादिसंयुक्ता नानागणसमन्विता ॥ ५०॥

English
Immediately the army of Śiva came there consisting of wonderful arrays of Bhūtas, Pretas and Gaṇas. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
वात्यारूपधराः केचित्पताकामर्मरस्वनाः ।
वक्रतुण्डास्तत्र केचिद्विरूपाश्चापरे तथा ॥ ५१॥

English
Some were in the form of violent gusts of wind, producing hissing sounds with waving flags. Some had crooked faces. Others were deformed. (51.)

Some were awful with overgrown moustaches and beards. Some were lame. Some were blind. Some held staffs and nooses and some great iron clubs in their hands. (52.)

Sanskrit

English
Some had no faces. Some had distorted and deformed faces. Some had many faces. Some had no hands. Others had deformed hands. Some of them had many hands. (54.)

Sanskrit

English
Some had no eyes. Some had many eyes. Some had no head. Some had deformed heads. Some had no ears. Some had many ears. The Gaṇas had all types of dresses and features. (55.)

Sanskrit

English
Such and other innumerable deformed Gaṇas, heroic and terrible, strong and strenuous passed by, O dear. (56.)

Sanskrit

English
O sage, you pointed out the Gaṇas of Śiva to her with your finger and said—"O lovely lady, see the attendants of Śiva and Śiva Himself." (57.)

English
O sage, on seeing the innumerable Gaṇas, Bhūtas and Pretas, Menakā was terribly frightened instantaneously. (58.)

Sanskrit
तन्मध्ये शङ्करं चैव निर्गुणं गुणवत्तरम् ।
वृषभस्थं पञ्चवक्त्रं त्रिनेत्रं भूतिभूषितम् ॥ ५९॥

कपर्दिनं चन्द्रमौलिं दशहस्तं कपालिनम् ।
व्याघ्रचर्मोत्तरीयं च पिनाकवरपाणिनम् ॥ ६०॥

शूलयुक्तं विरूपाक्षं विकृताकारमाकुलम् ।
गजचर्म वसानं हि वीक्ष्य त्रेसे शिवाप्रसूः ॥ ६१॥

English
On seeing Śiva in their midst, the mother of Pārvatī trembled. She saw Śiva who though devoid of attributes was better than those who had all the attributes, He was seated on the Bull. He had five faces¹⁰³ and three eyes. He had ashes smeared over the body. He had matted hair with the crescent moon on His head. He had ten hands with the skull in one of them. His upper cloth was tiger's hide. He held the bow Pināka in one of his hands and the Trident in another. He had odd eyes, ugly features utterly dishevelled and untidy. He wore the hide of an elephant. (59-61.)
