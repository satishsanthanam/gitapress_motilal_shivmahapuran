### **CHAPTER FORTYFOUR - (*Menā regains consciousness*)**

---

Sanskrit
४४

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
संज्ञां लब्ध्वा ततः सा च मेना शैलप्रिया सती ।
विललापातिसङ्क्षुब्धा तिरस्कारमथाकरोत् ॥ १॥

तत्र तावत्स्वपुत्रांश्च निनिन्द स्खलिता मुहुः ।
प्रथमं सा ततः पुत्रीं कथयामास दुर्वचः ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  On regaining consciousness, the chaste beloved of the mountain lamented with great agitation and began to rebuke everyone. In faltering words she frequently censured her sons at first and then scolded her daughter. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
मुने पुरा त्वया प्रोक्तं वरिष्यति शिवा शिवम् ।
पश्चाद्धिमवतः कृत्यं पूजार्थं विनिवेशितम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Menā said : —*  O sage, formerly it was mentioned be you that Pārvatī would marry Śiva. Afterwards you assigned some activity of worship to Himavat. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दृष्टं फलं सत्यं विपरीतमनर्थकम् ।
मुनेऽधमाहं दुर्बुद्धे सर्वथा वञ्चिता त्वया ॥ ४॥

English
Its fruit is visible now, to be sure. But it is adverse and meaningless. O sage, O wicked minded one, I the innocent woman have been cheated by you by all means. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनस्तया तपस्तप्तं दुष्करं मुनिभिश्च यत् ।
तस्य लब्धं फलं ह्येतत्पश्यतां दुःखदायकम् ॥ ५॥

English
The fruit of penance which she performed and which is very difficult even for the sages to perform, has been this, painful to every onlooker. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि को मे दुःखं व्यपोहताम् ।
कुलादिकं विनष्टं मे विहितं जीवितं मम ॥ ६॥

English
What shall I do? Where shall I go? Who will dispel my sorrow? My family is wrecked. My life is doomed. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व गता ऋषयो दिव्याः श्मश्रूणि त्रोटयाम्यहम् ।
तपस्विनी च या पत्नी सा धूर्ता स्वयमागता ॥ ७॥

English
Where are those so called celestial sages? I shall pluck out their beards. Where is that mischievous woman who came here by herself in the guise of the wife of one of them? (7.)

---

Sanskrit
केषाञ्चैवापराधेन सर्वं नष्टं ममाधुना ।
इत्युक्त्वा वीक्ष्य च सुतामुवाच वचनं कटु ॥ ८॥

English
By whose guilt have I been ruined now?" Saying this she turned to her daughter and began to say harsh words. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कृतं ते सुते दुष्टे कर्म दुःखकरं मम ।
हेम दत्त्वा त्वयानीतः काचो वै दुष्टया स्वयम् ॥ ९॥

English
O wretched daughter, what is it that you have done? This is extremely painful to me. You have given gold and brought a glass piece, O wicked girl. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वा तु चन्दनं भूयो लेपितः कर्दमस्त्वया ।
हंसमुड्डीय काको वै गृहीतो हस्तपञ्जरे ॥ १०॥

English
You have cast away sandal paste and smeared yourself with mud. You have driven away the swan and have held a crow in your hands. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वा ब्रह्मजलं दूरे पीतं कूपोदकं त्वया ।
सूर्यं हित्वा तु खद्योतो गृहीतो यत्नतस्त्वया ॥ ११॥

English
Setting aside the sacred river water you have drunk the well-water. Losing the sun you have clung to the glowworm in all earnestness. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तण्डुलांश्च तथा हित्वा कृतं वै तुषभक्षणम् ।
प्रक्षिप्याज्यं तथा तैलं कारण्डं भुक्तमादरात् ॥ १२॥

English
Throwing away cooked rice you have eaten the husk. Spilling away the clarified butter you have eagerly swallowed castor oil. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहसेवां तथा मुक्त्वा श‍ृगालः सेवितस्त्वया ।
ब्रह्मविद्यां तथा मुक्त्वा कुगाथा च श्रुता त्वया ॥ १३॥

English
Setting the lion aside a jackal has been served by you. Without listening to the lore of Supreme Brahman$^{104}$ you have heard base ballads. (13.)

[fn 104. It refers to the spiritual knowledge as revealed in the Upaniṣads and other treatises, e.g. the identity of the individual soul with the universal soul, the reality of the spirit over the unreality of the matter and so on.]
---

Sanskrit
गृहे यज्ञविभूतिं हि दूरीकृत्य सुमङ्गलाम् ।
गृहीतश्च चिताभस्म त्वया पुत्रि ह्यमङ्गलम् ॥ १४॥

English
O daughter, casting off the holy sacrificial ashes at home you have taken the inauspicious ashes from the funeral pyre. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वान् देववरांस्त्यक्त्वा विष्ण्वादीन्परमेश्वरान् ।
कृतं त्वया कुबुद्ध्या वै शिवार्थं तप ईदृशम् ॥ १५॥

English
Abandoning the great lords, Viṣṇu and others you have performed penance for Śiva. Your intellect has really gone astray. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
धिक्त्वां च तव बुद्धिं च धिग्रूपं चरितं तव ।
धिक् चोपदेशकर्तारं धिक्सख्यावपि ते तथा ॥ १६॥

English
Fie on you. Fie on your intellect. Fie on your beauty and conduct. Fie on your adviser. Fie on your maids too. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
आवां च धिक्तथा पुत्रि यौ ते जन्मप्रवर्तकौ ।
धिक्ते नारद बुद्धिञ्च सप्तर्षींश्च कुबुद्धिदान् ॥ १७॥

English
Fie on us who brought you thus to the world, O daughter. O Nārada, fie on your intelligence and fie on those seven sages who gave us wrong advice. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
धिक्कुलं धिक् क्रियादाक्ष्यं सर्वं धिग्यत्कृतं त्वया ।
गृहं तु धुक्षितं त्वेतन्मरणं तु ममैव हि ॥ १८॥

English
Fie on the whole family. Fie on the efficiency in performing the rites. Fie on everything done by you. You have inflamed this household. Almost it has been a death blow to me. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वतानामयं राजा नायातु निकटे मम ।
सप्तर्षयः स्वयं नैव दर्शयन्तु मुखं मम ॥ १९॥

English
Let not the king of the mountains come near me. Let not the seven sages show their faces to me. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
साधितं किं च सर्वैस्तु मिलित्वा घातितं कुलम् ।
बन्ध्याहं न कथं जाता गर्भो न गलितः कथम् ॥ २०॥

English
Has anything been achieved? Our whole race is wrecked by all conspiring together. How is it that I have not remained a barren woman? How is it that a miscarriage did not take place when I conceived? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो न वा मृता चाहं पुत्रिका न मृता कथम् ।
राक्षसाद्यैः कथं नो वा भक्षिता गगने पुनः ॥ २१॥

English
How is it that I did not die? How is it that this girl did not die? Why is she not devoured by the demons and others from the sky? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
छेदयामि शिरस्तेऽद्य किं करोमि कलेवरैः ।
त्यक्त्वा त्वां च कुतो यायां हाहा मे जीवितं हतम् ॥ २२॥

English
I shall cut off your head. What shall I do with the bodies? Abandoning you where shall I go? Alas, my whole life is doomed. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा पतिता सा च मेना भूमौ विमूर्छिता ।
व्याकुला शोकरोषाद्यैर्न गता भर्तृसन्निधौ ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this Menā fell unconscious on the ground. Agitated by grief and anger she did not go near her husband. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारो महानासीत्तस्मिन्काले मुनीश्वर ।
सर्वे समागतास्तत्र क्रमात्तत्सन्निधौ सुराः ॥ २४॥

English
There was a great hue and cry at that time, O great sage. The gods came near her. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा देवमुने चाहमागतस्तु स्वयं तदा ।
मां दृष्ट्वा त्वं वचस्तां वै प्रावोच ऋषिसत्तम ॥ २५॥

English
O celestial sage, I too came myself. On seeing me, O excellent sage, you spoke to her. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
यथार्थं सुन्दरं रूपं न ज्ञातं ते शिवस्य वै ।
लीलयेदं धृतं रूपं न यथार्थं शिवेन च ॥ २६॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  The real handsome form of Śiva is not known by you. This form is assumed by Śiva in a sportive mood. It is not the real form. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्क्रोधं परित्यज्य स्वस्था भव पतिव्रते ।
कार्यं कुरु हठं त्यक्त्वा शिवां देहि शिवाय च ॥ २७॥

English
Hence, O chaste lady, cast off anger. Be calm. Leave off your obduracy. Do what is proper to be done. Give Pārvatī to Śiva. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

उत्तिष्ठेतो गच्छ दूरं दुष्टाधमवरो भवान् ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing your words, Menā spoke to you—“O wicked one, get up and go away. You are base”. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

समागत्य च दिक्पाला वचनं चेदमब्रुवन् ॥ २९॥

English

---

Sanskrit

English
*The gods said :—*  O Menā, O daughter of the Pīṭṛs, listen to our words joyously. This Śiva is the Supreme lord Himself, the bestower of the greatest happiness. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Menā cried aloud frequently and spoke to the gods—“My daughter will not be given to Śiva of fierce features. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Why have you all conspired together to render her beauty futile?” (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

(48-50.)
