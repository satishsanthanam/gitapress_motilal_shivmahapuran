### **CHAPTER FORTYFIVE - *(Śiva's comely form and the Jubilation of the Citizens)***

---

Sanskrit
४५

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे त्वं हि विष्णुना प्रेरितो द्रुतम् ।
अनुकूलयितुं शम्भुमयास्तन्निकटे मुने ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  In the meantime, O sage, urged by Viṣṇu you went immediately to Śiva to conciliate Him. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा स वै रुद्रो भवता सुप्रबोधितः ।
स्तोत्रैर्नानाविधैः स्तुत्वा देवकार्यचिकीर्षया ॥ २॥

English
After reaching there, with a desire to get the task of the gods fulfilled, you pleaded with Śiva after eulogising Him with different kinds of hymns. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा त्वद्वचनं प्रीत्या शम्भुना धृतमद्भुतम् ।
स्वरूपमुत्तमं दिव्यं कृपालुत्वं च दर्शितम् ॥ ३॥

English
On hearing your words Śiva joyously assumed a wonderfully excellent and divine form and showed His mercifulness. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा सुन्दरं शम्भुं स्वरूपं मन्मथाधिकम् ।
अत्यहृष्यो मुने त्वं हि लावण्यपरमायनम् ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, on seeing the comely form of Śiva, the receptacle of exquisite beauty, far better than that of the cupid, you were greatly delighted. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तोत्रैर्नानाविधैः स्तुत्वा परमानन्दसंयुतः ।
आगच्छस्त्वं मुने तत्र यत्र मेना स्थिताखिलैः ॥ ५॥

English
Highly delighted you eulogised Him again and again with different kinds of hymns and returned to the place where Menā was seated along with other gods. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य सुप्रसन्नो मुनेऽतिप्रेमसङ्कुलः ।
हर्षयंस्तां शैलपत्नीं मेनां त्वं वाक्यमब्रवीः ॥ ६॥

English
Reaching there, O sage, with great affection and delight, you spoke to the great pleasure of Menā, the wife of Himavat. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
मेने पश्य विशालाक्षि शिवरूपमनुत्तमम् ।
कृता शिवेन तेनैव सुकृपा करुणात्मना ॥ ७॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Menā of wide eyes, see the excellent features of Śiva. The merciful Śiva has taken great pity on us. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रुत्वा सा तद्वचो मेना विस्मिता शैलकामिनी ।
ददर्श शिवरूपं तत्परमानन्ददायकम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Extremely surprised on hearing your words, Menā the beloved wife of the mountain, saw Śiva's form that afforded great bliss. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिसूर्यप्रतीकाशं सर्वावयवसुन्दरम् ।
विचित्रवसनं चात्र नानाभूषणभूषितम् ॥ ९॥

सुप्रसन्नं सुहासं च सुलावण्यं मनोहरम् ।
गौराभं द्युतिसंयुक्तं चन्द्ररेखाविभूषितम् ॥ १०॥

सर्वैर्देवगणैः प्रीत्या विष्ण्वाद्यैः सेवितं तथा ।
सूर्येण च्छत्रितं मूर्ध्नि चन्द्रेण च विशोभितम् ॥ ११॥

सर्वथा रमणीयं च भूषितस्य विभूषणैः ।
वाहनस्य महाशोभा वर्णितुं नैव शक्यते ॥ १२॥

English
It was as refulgent as that of a thousand suns. Every part of the body was exquisite. The garments were of variegated colours. He was embellished with different ornaments. He was smiling with great delight. His comeliness was highly pleasing. He was fair-complexioned and lustrous. The crescent moon added to his beauty. Viṣṇu and other gods lovingly served Him. The sun acted as His royal umbrella. The moon embellished Him. In every way He was extremely handsome bedecked in ornaments. It was impossible to describe adequately the great beauty of His vehicle. (9-12.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गा च यमुना चैव विधत्तः स्म सुचामरे ।
सिद्धयोऽष्टौ पुरस्तस्य कुर्वन्ति स्म सुनर्तनम् ॥ १३॥

English
The Gaṅgā and the Yamunā were waving the Chowries. The eight Siddhis$^{105}$ danced in front of Him. (13.)

[fn 105. The eightfold Siddhis are personified here. For details see Note 203 P. 235]
---

Sanskrit
मया चैव तदा विष्णुरिन्द्राद्या ह्यमरास्तथा ।
स्वं स्वं वेषं सुसम्भूष्य गिरिशेनाचरन्युताः ॥ १४॥

English
Viṣṇu, I, Indra and the other gods bedecked their bodies and dress and accompanied Śiva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा जयेति भाषन्तो नानारूपा गणास्तदा ।
स्वलङ्कृतमहामोदा गिरीशपुरतोऽचरन् ॥ १५॥

English
The Gaṇas of various forms and features shouted cries of “Victory” “Victory” and walked in front of Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सिद्धाश्चोपसुराः सर्वे मुनयश्च महासुखाः ।
ययुः शिवेन सुप्रीताः सकलाश्चापरे तथा ॥ १६॥

English
The Siddhas, the secondary gods, the extremely delighted sages went in company of Śiva. The others too were equally delighted. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं देवादयः सर्वे कुतूहलसमन्विताः ।
परं ब्रह्म गृणन्तस्ते स्वपत्नीभिरलङ्कृताः ॥ १७॥

English
Thus the fully decorated gods, were very jubilant and in the company of their wives they eulogised Śiva, the Supreme Brahman. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वावसुमुखास्तत्र ह्यप्सरोगणसंयुताः ।
गायन्तोऽप्यग्रतस्तस्य परमं शाङ्करं यशः ॥ १८॥

English
Viśvāvasu$^{106}$ and others along with the celestial damsels sang songs of Śiva’s glory. (18.)

[fn 106. Viśvāvasu is the chief of the Gandharvas in Indra’s heaven. He is a famous musician and is said to possess all girls from the advent of their youth and transfer them to Agni from whom the bridegrooms obtain them for producing wealth and sons.]
---

Sanskrit
इत्थं महोत्सवस्तत्र बभूव मुनिसत्तम ।
नानाविधो महेशे हि शैलद्वारि च गच्छति ॥ १९॥

English
O excellent sage, when Śiva was nearing the threshold of the palace of Himavat, there was much jubilation there. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिंश्च समये तत्र सुषमा या परात्मनः ।
वर्णितुं तां विशेषेण कः शक्नोति मुनीश्वर ॥ २०॥

English
O excellent sage, who can describe the exquisite splendour of the supreme lord at that time. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तथाविधं च तं दृष्ट्वा मेना चित्रगता इव ।
क्षणमासीत्ततः प्रीत्या प्रोवाच वचनं मुने ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing Him in that form Menā stood stunned as though drawn in a picture for a moment, O sage, and spoke these words. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
धन्या पुत्री मदीया च यया तप्तं महत्तपः ।
यत्प्रभावान्महेशान त्वं प्राप्त इह मद्गृहे ॥ २२॥

English
*Menā said :—*  O great lord, my daughter is indeed blessed, she by whom the great penance was performed. It is by virtue of that penance that you have come to my threshold. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मया कृता पुरा या वै शिवनिन्दा दुरत्यया ।
तां क्षमस्व शिवास्वामिन्सुप्रसन्नो भवाधुना ॥ २३॥

English
O lord of Pārvatī, be pleased now. Pardon me for the heap of repulsive words I showered on Śiva. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थं सम्भाष्य सा मेना संस्तूयेन्दुललाटकम् ।
साञ्जलिः प्रणता शैलप्रिया लज्जापराभवत् ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying thus and eulogising the moon-crested lord, Menā, the beloved of the mountain, bowed to Him with palms joined in reverence and stood shy. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्स्त्रियः समाजग्मुर्हित्वा कामाननेकशः ।
वह्व्यस्ताः पुरवासिन्यः शिवदर्शनलालसाः ॥ २५॥

English
By that time the ladies of the town left the work they were engaged in, in their eagerness to see Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
मज्जनं कुर्वती काचित्तच्चूर्णसहिता ययौ ।
द्रष्टुं कुतूहलाढ्या च शङ्करं गिरिजावरम् ॥ २६॥

English
A certain lady in the midst of her bath and toilet was overwhelmed with the desire to see Śiva, the bridegroom of Pārvatī. She came out with the shampoo powder still held in her hands. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
काचित्तु स्वामिनःसेवां सखीयुक्ता विहाय च ।
सुचामरकरा प्रीत्यागाच्छम्भोर्दर्शनाय वै ॥ २७॥

English
A certain lady engaged in fanning her husband in the company of her maid left that job and came out to see Śiva with the fan still in her hands. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
काचित्तु बालकं हित्वा पिबन्तं स्तन्यमादरात् ।
अतृप्तं शङ्करं द्रष्टुं ययौ दर्शनलालसा ॥ २८॥

English
Another lady engaged in suckling her babe at her breast left him dissatisfied and came out eagerly to see the lord. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
रशनां बध्नती काचित्तयैव सहिता ययौ ।
वसनं विपरीतं वै धृत्वा काचिद्ययौ ततः ॥ २९॥

English
Another lady engaged in trying her waist girdle came out with it. Another lady came out with garments worn inside out (29.)

---

Sanskrit
भोजनार्थं स्थितं कान्तं हित्वा काचिद्ययौ प्रिया ।
द्रष्टुं शिवावरं प्रीत्या सतृष्णा सकुतूहला ॥ ३०॥

English
Another lady left her husband who had sat down to dine and came out athirsting and enthusiastic to see the bridegroom (30.)

---

Sanskrit
काचिद्धस्ते शलाकां च धृत्वाञ्जनकरा प्रिया ।
अञ्जित्वैकाक्षि सन्द्रष्टुं ययौ शैलसुतावरम् ॥ ३१॥

English
A certain lady holding the collyrium in her hand after applying it to one of her eyes came out to see the bridegroom of the daughter of the mountain with the salve stick still in her hand. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
काचित्तु कामिनी पादौ रञ्जयन्ती ह्यलक्तकैः ।
श्रुत्वा घोषं च तद्धित्वा दर्शनार्थमुपागता ॥ ३२॥

English
Another damsel engaged in applying the red lac juice to her feet heard the tumult outside and so left it in the middle and came out to see the procession. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यादि विविधं कार्यं हित्वा वासं स्त्रियो ययुः ।
दृष्ट्वा तु शाङ्करं रूपं मोहं प्राप्तास्तदाभवन् ॥ ३३॥

English
Thus the ladies forsook their activities, left their houses and came out. On seeing the exquisite form of Śiva they were greatly fascinated. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्ताः प्रेमसंविग्नाः शिवदर्शनहर्षिताः ।
निधाय हृदि तन्मूर्तिं वचनं चेदमब्रुवन् ॥ ३४॥

English
Delighted on seeing Śiva and overwhelmed by affection they cherished the comely form in their hearts and spoke as follows :— (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*The ladies said:—*  The eyes of the residents of this town have become fruitful. The life of the persons who have seen this comely form has become meaningful. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The life is fruitful and the rites are fruitful only of the person who has seen Śiva, the destroyer of all sins. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Pārvatī has accomplished everything in asmuch as she performed penance for Śiva. She is blessed, she is contented in securing Śiva as her husband. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If Brahmā had not joined this pair, Śiva and Śivā, his endeavour of creation would have entirely become fruitless. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
This is well done. The excellent pair has been united. Everything has become meaningful in every activity. (39.)
