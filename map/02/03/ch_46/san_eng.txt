### **CHAPTER FORTYSIX - *(The arrival of the bridegroom)***

---

Sanskrit
४६

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ शम्भुः प्रसन्नात्मा सदूतं स्वगणैःसुरैः ।
सर्वैरन्यैर्गिरेर्धाम जगाम सकुतूहलम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  The delighted Śiva accompanied by His Bhūtas, Gaṇas, gods and others went to the abode of the mountain zealously. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनापि स्त्रीगणैस्तैश्च हिमाचलवरप्रिया ।
तत उत्थाय स्वगृहाभ्यन्तरं सा जगाम ह ॥ २॥

English
Menā, the exquisite beloved of Himācala, got up from her seat and went into the harem along with the womenfolk. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नीराजनार्थं शम्भोश्च दीपपात्रकरा सती ।
सर्वर्षिस्त्रीगणैस्साकमगच्छद् द्वारमादरात् ॥ ३॥

English
For the customary Nirājana (waving of lights) rites of Śiva, the chaste lady came near the entrance with lights and vessels in her hands along with womenfolk of the sages. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागतं महेशानं शङ्करं गिरिजावरम् ।
ददर्श प्रीतितो मेना सेवितं सकलैः सुरैः ॥ ४॥

English
Menā saw with pleasure lord Śiva, the bridegroom of Pārvatī, served by all the gods and who by that time had come there. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
चारुचम्पकवर्णाभं पञ्चवक्त्रं त्रिलोचनम् ।
ईषद्धास्यप्रसन्नास्यं रत्नस्वर्णादिभूषितम् ॥ ५॥

मालतीमालया युक्तं सद्रत्नमुकुटोज्ज्वलम् ।
सत्कण्ठाभरणं चारुवलयाङ्गदभूषितम् ॥ ६॥

वह्निशौचेनातुलेन त्वतिसूक्ष्मेण चारुणा ।
अमूल्यवस्त्रयुग्मेन विचित्रेणातिराजितम् ॥ ७॥

चन्दनागरुकस्तूरीचारुकुङ्कुमभूषितम् ।
रत्नदर्पणहस्तं च कज्जलोज्ज्वललोचनम् ॥ ८॥

सर्वस्वप्रभयाच्छन्नमतीव सुमनोहरम् ।
अतीव तरुणं रम्यं भूषिताङ्गैश्च भूषितम् ॥ ९॥

कामिनीकान्तमव्यग्रं कोटिचन्द्राननाम्बुजम् ।
कोटिस्मराधिकतनुच्छविं सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरम् ॥ १०॥

ईदृग्विधं सुदेवं तं स्थितं स्वपुरतः प्रभुम् ।
दृष्ट्वा जामातरं मेना जहौ शोकं मुदान्विता ॥ ११॥

English
Śiva had the complexion of the colour of the Campaka flower. He had only one face but retained the three eyes. The face was beaming with a simple smile. He was bedecked in gems and gold and wore a garland of Mālatī flowers. The gemset crown was lustrous. He wore brilliant necklaces. He was bedecked in bangles and bracelets of fine workmanship. He was shining well with the two clothes of great value, fine texture and unrivalled beauty and purified in fire. Highly embellished in sandal paste, aguru, musk and fine saffron, he had a gemset mirror in his hand and his eyes were lustrous with the collyrium. He was shedding a halo around him enveloping everything. He was extremely beautiful. He appeared to be very young. His limbs had the full complement of their ornaments. He was very attractive to the ladies. He was not nervous or self-conscious. His lotuslike face had the brilliance of a thousand moons. His body shone with a refulgence more than that of a thousand cupids. He was beautiful in every limb. Seeing the lord thus as her son-in-law, Menā forgot all her grief. She was glad. (5-11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रशशंस स्वभाग्यं सा गिरिजां भूधरं कुलम् ।
मेने कृतार्थमात्मानं जहर्ष च पुनः पुनः ॥ १२॥

English
She praised her good luck. She congratulated Pārvatī, the mountain and his entire family. She congratulated herself. She rejoiced again and again. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नीराजनं चकारासौ प्रफुल्लवदना सती ।
अवलोकपरा तत्र मेना जामातरं मुदा ॥ १३॥

English
Gazing at her son-in-law joyously with beaming face, the chaste lady performed the Nirājana rite. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिजोक्तमनुस्मृत्य मेना विस्मयमागता ।
मनसैव ह्युवाचेदं हर्षफुल्लाननाम्बुजा ॥ १४॥

English
Remembering what Pārvatī had told her, Menā was agreeably surprised and with a beaming lotus-like face full of delight she muttered to herself. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्वै पुरोक्तं च तया पार्वत्या मम तत्र च ।
ततोऽधिकं प्रपश्यामि सौन्दर्यं परमेशितुः ॥ १५॥

English
"I see the beauty of the great lord far in excess of what Pārvatī had told me before. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
महेशस्य सुलावण्यमनिर्वाच्यं च सम्प्रति ।
एवं विस्मयमापन्ना मेना स्वगृहमाययौ ॥ १६॥

English
Śiva's loveliness cannot be expressed adequately now." In the same state of pleasant surprise she went in. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रशशंसुर्युवतयो धन्या धन्या गिरेः सुता ।
दुर्गा भगवतीत्येवमूचुः काश्चन कन्यकाः ॥ १७॥

English
The young ladies proclaimed that the daughter of the mountain was fortunate. Some girls said that she had become a goddess. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
न दृष्टो वर इत्येवमस्माभिर्दानगोचरः ।
धन्या हि गिरिजादेवीमूचुः काश्चन कन्यकाः ॥ १८॥

English
Some said—"Such a bridegroom has never been seen, not to our knowledge." Some girls said to Menā—"Pārvatī is really blessed." (18.)

---

Sanskrit
जगुर्गन्धर्वप्रवरा ननृतुश्चाप्सरोगणाः ।
दृष्ट्वा शङ्कररूपं च प्रहृष्टाः सर्वदेवताः ॥ १९॥

English
The chief of Gandharvas sang songs. The celestial damsels danced. On seeing Śiva's lovely form, the gods were delighted. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाप्रकारवाद्यानि वादका मधुराक्षरम् ।
नानाप्रकारशिल्पेन वादयामासुरादरात् ॥ २०॥

English
The instrument players played on musical instruments in sweet tones showing their diverse skill. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचलोऽपि मुदितो द्वाराचारमथाकरोत् ।
मेनापि सर्वनारीभिर्महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ २१॥

English
The delighted Himācala too carried out the customary rites of reception at the entrance. Menā also jubilantly took part in the same along with all the womenfolk. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
परपृच्छां चकारासौ मुदिता स्वगृहं ययौ ।
शिवो निवेदितं स्थानं जगाम गणनिर्जरैः ॥ २२॥

English
She made formal inquiries about the health of the bridegroom and gladly went into the house. Śiva went to the apartments assigned to Him along with the Gaṇas and the gods. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दुर्गां शैलान्तः पुरचारिकाः ।
बहिर्जग्मुः समादाय पूजितुं कुलदेवताम् ॥ २३॥

English
In the meantime the servant-maids in the harem of the mountain took Pārvatī out in order to worship the tutelar family deity. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तां ददृशुर्देवा निमेषरहिता मुदा ।
सुनीलाञ्जनवर्णाभां स्वाङ्गैश्च प्रतिभूषिताम् ॥ २४॥

त्रिनेत्रादृतनेत्रां तामन्यवारितलोचनाम् ।
ईषद्धास्यप्रसन्नास्यां सकटाक्षां मनोहराम् ॥ २५॥

सुचारुकबरीभारां चारुपत्रकशोभिताम् ।
कस्तूरीबिन्दुभिस्सार्धं सिन्दूरबिन्दुशोभिताम् ॥ २६॥

रत्नेन्द्रसारहारेण वक्षसा सुविराजिताम् ।
रत्नकेयूरवलयां रत्नकङ्कणमण्डिताम् ॥ २७॥

सद्रत्नकुण्डलाभ्यां च चारुगण्डस्थलोज्ज्वलाम् ।
मणिरत्नप्रभामुष्टिदन्तराजिविराजिताम् ॥ २८॥

मधुबिम्बाधरोष्ठां च रत्नयावकसंयुताम् ।
रत्नदर्पणहस्तां च क्रीडापद्मविभूषिताम् ॥ २९॥

चन्दनागुरुकस्तूरीकुङ्कुमेनातिचर्चिताम् ।
क्वणन्मञ्जीरपादां च रक्ताङ्घ्रितलराजिताम् ॥ ३०॥

English
There the gods saw joyously with winkless eyes the bride of dark complexion like the collyrium, and fully bedecked in ornaments in every limb. With a side glance she was respectfully looking at the three-eyed lord avoiding the eyes of others. With a gentle smile playing in her face she appeared very beautiful. Her plaited hair was thickly grown and looked beautiful. Decorative lines over her body were exquisite. She had the Tilaka with musk and saffron. Gemset necklace shone over her chest. Bracelets and bangles of gems and jewels shone brilliantly. With diamond earrings her cheeks appeared brilliant. Her rows of teeth sparkled like diamonds. Red lac applied over her lips which were naturally red like Bimba fruits was exquisite. She had a gemset mirror in her hand. A toy lotus also embellished her. Sandal paste, aguru musk and saffron were smeared over the body by her. Her feet and soles were naturally red. Tinkling anklets added to their beauty. (24-30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणेमुः शिरसा देवीं भक्तियुक्ताः समेनकाम् ।
सर्वे सुरादयो दृष्ट्वा जगदाद्यां जगत्प्रसूम् ॥ ३१॥

English
On seeing the primordial deity, the mother of the universe along with Menakā, the gods and others bowed down their heads with great devotion. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिनेत्रो नेत्रकोणेन तां ददर्श मुदान्वितः ।
शिवः सत्याकृतिं दृष्ट्वा विजहौ विरहज्वरम् ॥ ३२॥

English
The three-eyed deity saw her with the corner of an eye and was glad. On seeing the shapely body of Satī he forgot the pangs of separation. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः सर्वं विसस्मार शिवासंन्यस्तलोचनः ।
पुलकाञ्चितसर्वाङ्गो हर्षाद्गौरीविलोचनः ॥ ३३॥

English
With his eyes riveted to her, he forgot everything else. Hair stood on ends all over his body, as he continued seeing her with delight. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ काली बहिः पुर्या गत्वा पूज्य कुलाम्बिकाम् ।
विवेश भवनं रम्यं स्वपितुस्सद्विजाङ्गना ॥ ३४॥

English
Then Pārvatī went out of the city, worshipped the family goddess and returned to her parental abode along with the brahmin women. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करोऽपि सुरैः सार्धं हरिणा ब्रह्मणा तथा ।
हिमाचलसमुद्दिष्टं स्वस्थानमगमन्मुदा ॥ ३५॥

English
Śiva went to the apartments indicated by Himācala, joyously along with the gods, Viṣṇu and Brahmā. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र सर्वे सुखं तस्थुः सेवन्तः शङ्करं यथा ।
सम्मानिता गिरीशेन नानाविधसुसम्पदा ॥ ३६॥

English
All of them stayed there with joy, attending on Śiva. They were duly honoured by Himavat, the mountainous lord. (36.)
