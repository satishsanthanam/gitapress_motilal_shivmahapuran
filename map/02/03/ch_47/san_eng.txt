### **CHAPTER FORTYSEVEN - *(The ceremonious entry of Śiva into the inner apartments of the palace of Himavat)***

---

Sanskrit
४७

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततः शैलवरः सोऽपि प्रीत्या दुर्गोपवीतकम् ।
कारयामास सोत्साहं वेदमन्त्रैश्शिवस्य च ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Then the chief of mountains caused the investiture rite with the sacred thread for Pārvatī and Śiva with the Vedic hymns recited enthusiastically. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्ण्वादयो देवा मुनयः सकुतूहलम् ।
हिमाचलप्रार्थनया विवेशान्तर्गृहं गिरेः ॥ २॥

English
Then Viṣṇu, the other gods and the sages entered the inner apartments of the palace of the mountain enthusiastically at the request of Himācala. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्याचारं भवाचारं विधाय च यथार्थतः ।
शिवामलङ्कृतां चक्रुः शिवदत्तविभूषणैः ॥ ३॥

English
After performing the conventional rites in accordance with the Vedic injunctions and the social customs they decorated Pārvatī with the ornaments provided by Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं स्नापयित्वा तां भूषयित्वाथ सर्वशः ।
नीराजिता सखीभिश्च विप्रपत्नीभिरेव च ॥ ४॥

English
First of all she was bathed, then bedecked with the ornaments. The Nirājana rites too were also performed by the maids and brahmin women. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अहताम्बरयुग्मेन शोभिता वरवर्णिनी ।
विरराज महाशैलदुहिता शङ्करप्रिया ॥ ५॥

English
The daughter of the mountain and the beloved of Śiva, the lovely lady shone with the pair of fresh clothes. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कञ्चुकी परमा दिव्या नानारत्नान्विताद्भुता ।
विधृता च तया देव्या विलसन्त्यधिकं मुने ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, an exquisite divine jacket studded with various gems was worn by the goddess who shone all the more. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सा बभार तथा हारं दिव्यरत्नसमन्वितम् ।
वलयानि महार्हाणि शुद्धचामीकराणि च ॥ ७॥

English
She wore a necklace studded with divine gems. Costly banglès of pure gold were worn by her. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थिता तत्रैव सुभगा ध्यायन्ती मनसा शिवम् ।
शुशुभेऽति महाशैलकन्यका त्रिजगत्प्रसूः ॥ ८॥

English
The lovely lady, the daughter of the great mountain, the mother of the three worlds staying there itself meditated on Śiva and shone thereby. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोत्सवो महानासीदुभयत्र मुदावहः ।
दानं बभूव विविधं ब्राह्मणेभ्यो विवर्णितम् ॥ ९॥

English
Then there was great jubilation delighting both the sides. Different kinds of charitable gifts were distributed among the brahmins. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्येषां द्रव्यदानं च बभूव विविधं महत् ।
गीतवाद्यविनोदश्च तत्रोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ १०॥

English
Monetary gifts were distributed among others. They were diverse. Many songs were sung jubilantly. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुरहं धाता शक्राद्या अमरास्तथा ।
मुनयश्च महाप्रीत्या निखिलाः सोत्सवा मुदा ॥ ११॥

English
Then Viṣṇu, I the creator, Indra and other gods as well as the sages joined in jubilation with great pleasure. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रणम्य शिवां भक्त्या स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ।
सम्प्राप्य हिमगिर्याज्ञां स्वं स्वं स्थानं समाश्रिताः ॥ १२॥

English
Then after bowing humbly to Pārvatī with devotion and remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva they returned to their camps obtaining the permission of Himavat. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र ज्योतिः शास्त्रविशारदः ।
हिमवन्तं गिरीन्द्रं तं गर्गो वाक्यमभाषत ॥ १३॥

English
In the meantime Garga, a great expert in the science of astrology, spoke to Himavat, the lord of mountains. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्ग उवाच ।
हिमाचल धराधीश स्वामिन् कालीपतिः प्रभो ।
पाणिग्रहार्थं शम्भुं चानय त्वं निजमन्दिरम् ॥ १४॥

English
*Garga said: —*  O Himavat, O lord, O father of Pārvatī, now fetch Śiva to your palace for the marriage rites. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ तं समयं ज्ञात्वा कन्यादानोचितं गिरिः ।
निवेदितं च गर्गेण मुमुदेऽतीव चेतसि ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  On realising that the auspicious time for the marriage rites had been intimated by Garga, the mountain rejoiced much. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
महीधरान्द्विजांश्चैव परानपि मुदा गिरिः ।
प्रेषयामास सुप्रीत्या शिवानयनकाम्यया ॥ १६॥

English
With the desire to bring Śiva there, the mountain gladly sent mountains, brahmins and others. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ते पर्वता द्विजाश्चैव सर्वमङ्गलपाणयः ।
सञ्जग्मुः सोत्सवाः प्रीत्या यत्र देवो महेश्वरः ॥ १७॥

English
The mountains and brahmins with auspicious holy objects in their hands jubilantly went to the place where lord Śiva stood. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा वादित्रघोषेण ब्रह्मघोषेण भूयसा ।
महोत्साहोऽभवत्तत्र गीतनृत्यान्वितेन हि ॥ १८॥

English
Then the sound of the Vedic chants, musical instruments, songs and dances jubilantly arose there. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा वादित्रनिर्घोषं सर्वे शङ्करसेवकाः ।
उत्थितास्त्वैकपद्येन सदेवर्षिगणा मुदा ॥ १९॥

English
On hearing the loud sound of musical instruments trumpets etc. the attendants of Śiva simultaneously got up joyously along with the gods and sages. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
परस्परं समूचुस्ते हर्षनिर्भरमानसाः ।
अत्रागच्छन्ति गिरयः शिवानयनकाम्यया ॥ २०॥

English
With great joy in their minds they said to one another—"O here come the mountains to take Śiva over there ! (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पाणिग्रहणकालो हि नूनं सद्यः समागतः ।
महद्भाग्यं हि सर्वेषां सम्प्राप्तमिह मन्महे ॥ २१॥

English
The auspicious hour for marriage rites has come. We consider that our fortune is imminent. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या वयं विशेषेण विवाहं शिवयोर्ध्रुवम् ।
द्रक्ष्यामः परमप्रीत्या जगतां मङ्गलालयम् ॥ २२॥

English
Indeed we are highly blessed as to witness the marriage ceremony of Śiva and Pārvatī, highly portentous of the good fortune of all the worlds.” (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एवं यावदभूत्तेषां संवादस्तत्र चादरात् ।
तावत्सर्वे समायाताः पर्वतेन्द्रस्य मन्त्रिणः ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Even as these confabulations were going on, the ministers of the lord of mountains came there. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ते गत्वा प्रार्थयाञ्चक्रुः शिवं विष्ण्वादिकानपि ।
कन्यादानोचितः कालो वर्तते गम्यतामिति ॥ २४॥

English
They approached Śiva, Viṣṇu and others and made their submission that the time for the celebration of marriage had arrived and that they would please hasten to the palace. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ते तच्छ्रुत्वा सुराः सर्वे मुने विष्ण्वादयोऽखिलाः ।
मुमुदुश्चेतसातीव जयेत्यूचुर्गिरिं द्रुतम् ॥ २५॥

English
On hearing that, O sage, Viṣṇu and others rejoiced much and cried shouts of victory to the mountain. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि मुमुदेऽतीव कालीप्रापणलालसः ।
गुप्तं चकार तच्चिह्नं मनस्येवाद्भुताकृतिः ॥ २६॥

English
Śiva too rejoiced much eager that he was approaching Pārvatī but kept the signs of joy within his mind alone in a wonderfully serene manner. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ स्नानं कृतं तेन मङ्गलद्रव्यसंयुतम् ।
शूलिना सुप्रसन्नेन लोकानुग्रहकारिणा ॥ २७॥

English
Then the ceremonial ablution with the sacred articles of toilet, was performed by the delighted trident-bearing lord eager to bless the worlds. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नातः सुवाससा युक्तः सर्वैस्तैः परिवारितः ।
आरोपितो वृषस्कन्धे लोकपालैः सुसेवितः ॥ २८॥

English
The bath being over He wore fine clothes. He was attended upon by the guardians of the quarters and surrounded by several others. He was then seated on the shoulders of the Bull. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरस्कृत्य प्रभुं सर्वे जग्मुर्हिमगिरेर्गृहम् ।
वाद्यानि वादयन्तश्च कृतवन्तः कुतूहलम् ॥ २९॥

English
With the lord in front, all of them entered the palace of Himavat playing on various musical instruments and exhibiting their eagerness. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमागप्रेषिता विप्रास्तथा ते पर्वतोत्तमाः ।
शम्भोरग्रचरा ह्यासन्कुतूहलसमन्विताः ॥ ३०॥

English
The brahmins sent by Himavat and the excellent mountains enthusiastically went ahead of Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
बभौ छत्रेण महता ध्रियमाणो हि मूर्धनि ।
चामरैर्वीज्यमानोऽसौ सवितानो महेश्वरः ॥ ३१॥

English
The great royal umbrella was held aloft over the great lord. He was fanned by chowries and a canopy was spread over Him. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विष्णुस्तथा चेन्द्रो लोकपालास्तथैव च ।
अग्रगाः स्मातिशोभन्ते श्रिया परमया श्रिताः ॥ ३२॥

English
Viṣṇu, Indra, the other guardians of the quarters and I going ahead shone with great brilliance and splendour. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शङ्खाश्च भेर्यश्च पटहानकगोमुखाः ।
पुनः पुनरवाद्यन्त वादित्राणि महोत्सवे ॥ ३३॥

English
In that great festivity conches were blown, drums were beaten and the musical instruments, paṭaha, Ānaka and Gōmukha were played on, repeatedly. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव गायकाः सर्वे जगुः परममङ्गलम् ।
नर्तक्यो ननृतुः सर्वा नानातालसमन्विताः ॥ ३४॥

English
Musicians sang auspicious songs. Dancing girls danced to the tune. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एभिस्समेतो जगदेकबन्धु-
र्ययौ तदानीं परमेशवर्चसा ।
सुसेव्यमानः सकलैः सुरेश्वरै-
र्विकीर्यमाणः कुसुमैश्च हर्षितैः ॥ ३५॥

English
Accompanied by these, attended upon by all important gods and with flowers showered on Him delightedly, the sole kinsman of the universe walked ahead shedding lordly splendour. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पूजितस्तदा शम्भुः प्रविष्टो यज्ञमण्डपम् ।
संस्तूयमानो बह्वीभिः स्तुतिभिः परमेश्वरः ॥ ३६॥

English
Lord Śiva, eulogised with many hymns of praise, entered the sacrificial altar. He was duly worshipped. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषादुत्तारयामासुर्महेशं पर्वतोत्तमाः ।
निन्युर्गृहान्तरं प्रीत्या महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ ३७॥

English
The excellent mountains jubilantly made Śiva dismount the bull and lovingly took Him within. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमालयोऽपि सम्प्राप्तं सदेवगणमीश्वरम् ।
प्रणम्य विधिवद्भक्त्या नीराजनमथाकरोत् ॥ ३८॥

English
After duly bowing to Śiva who arrived there with the gods and Gaṇas, Himavat performed the Nirājana with great devotion. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वान्सुरान्मुनीनन्यान् प्रणम्य समहोत्सवः ।
सम्मानमकरोत्तेषां प्रशंसन्स्वविधिं मुदा ॥ ३९॥

English
Praising his own good luck and bowing to all the gods, sages and others jubilantly he honoured them suitably. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽगःसाच्युतमीशानं सुपाद्यार्घ्यपुरस्सरम् ।
सदेवमुख्यवर्गं च निनाय स्वालयान्तरम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The mountain, after offering Pādya and Arghya to them, took Śiva along with Viṣṇu and the important gods, within. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राङ्गणे स्थापयामास रत्नसिंहासनेषु तान् ।
सर्वान्विष्णुं च मामीशं विशिष्टांश्च विशेषतः ॥ ४१॥

English
In the quadrangle inside he made us, Viṣṇu, Śiva and other important persons sit on gemset thrones. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सखीभिर्मेनया प्रीत्या ब्राह्मणस्त्रीभिरेव च ।
अन्याभिश्च पुरन्ध्रीभिश्चक्रे नीराजनं मुदा ॥ ४२॥

English
The Nirājana rites was then performed by Menā, her maids and the brahmin women as well as other ladies of the city with joy. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरोधसा कृत्यविदा शङ्कराय महात्मने ।
मधुपर्कादिकं यद्यत्कृत्यं तत्तत्कृतं मुदा ॥ ४३॥

English
The necessary rites such as offering of Madhuparka etc. to Śiva, the supreme soul, were joyously performed by the priest who knew his duties. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
मया स नोदितस्तत्र पुरोधाः कृतवांस्तदा ।
सुमङ्गलं च यत्कर्म प्रस्तावसदृशं मुने ॥ ४४॥

अन्तर्वेद्यां महाप्रीत्या सम्प्रविश्य हिमाद्रिणा ।
यत्र सा पार्वती कन्या सर्वाभरणभूषिता ॥ ४५॥

English
O sage, urged by me, the priest carried out the auspicious rites relevant to the context after entering the enclosure where the altar had been built along with Himavat. Pārvatī bedecked in all her ornaments was seated as the bride. (44-45.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदिकोपरि तन्वङ्गी संस्थिता सुविराजिता ।
तत्र नीतो महादेवो विष्णुना च मया सह ॥ ४६॥

English
She was seated over the raised platform and Śiva was led along with Viṣṇu and me. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
लग्नं निरीक्षमाणास्ते वाचस्पतिपुरोगमाः ।
कन्यादानोचितं तत्र बभूवुः परमोत्सवाः ॥ ४७॥

English
Waiting for the auspicious Lagna befitting marriage, Bṛhaspati and others became jubilant. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रोपविष्टो गर्गश्च यत्रास्ति घटिकालयम् ।
यावच्छेषा घटी तावत्कृतं प्रणवभाषणम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Garga was seated in the place where the chronometer$^{107}$ had been kept. The Omkāra Mantra was repeated during the interval before the Lagna. (48.)

[fn 107. Ghaṭikā or a waterclock was not only useful for carrying the nuptial programme at proper times but was also symbolical of time that ruled over the entire universe.]
---

Sanskrit
पुण्याहं प्रवदन् गर्गः समादध्रेऽञ्जलिं मुदा ।
पार्वत्यक्षतपूर्णं च ववृषे च शिवोपरि ॥ ४९॥

English
Repeating the Punyāha mantras, Garga lifted the handful of rice-grains and handing them over to Pārvatī he made her shower it on Śiva. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तया सम्पूजितो रुद्रो दध्यक्षतकुशाम्बुभिः ।
परमोदारया तत्र पार्वत्या रुचिरास्यया ॥ ५०॥

English
Śiva was duly worshipped by the joyful and sweet-faced Pārvatī with the rice-grains mixed with curd and Darbha water. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
विलोकयन्ती तं शम्भुं यस्यार्थे परमं तपः ।
कृतं पुरा महाप्रीत्या विरराज शिवाति सा ॥ ५१॥

English
Gazing at Śiva for whom great penance had been performed by her formerly, Pārvatī shone beaming with pleasure. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
मया मुने तदोक्तस्तु गर्गादिमुनिभिश्च सः ।
समानर्च शिवां शम्भुः लौकिकाचारसंरतः ॥ ५२॥

English
Requested by me and the sages Garga and others, Śiva, following the worldly conventions worshipped her. (52.)

---

Sanskrit

अर्चयन्तौ तदानीं च शुशुभाते जगन्मयौ ॥ ५३॥

English
Thus, worshipping each other Śiva and Pārvatī identifying themselves with the universe, shone well. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रैलोक्यलक्ष्म्या संवीतौ निरीक्षन्तौ परस्परम् ।
तदा नीराजितौ लक्ष्म्यादिभिः स्त्रीभिर्विशेषतः ॥ ५४॥

English
Both of them, enveloped by the glory of the three worlds and gazing at each other, were offered the Nirājana by Lakṣmī and other ladies particularly. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापरा वै द्विजयोषितश्च
नीराजयामासुरथो पुरस्त्रियः ।
शिवां च शम्भुञ्च विलोकयन्त्योऽ-
वापुर्मुदं ताः सकला महोत्सवम् ॥ ५५॥

English
The brahmin ladies and the citizen ladies performed the Nirājana rites. All of them derived great pleasure and gaiety on seeing Śiva and Pārvatī. (55.)
