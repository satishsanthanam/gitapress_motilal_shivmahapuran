### **CHAPTER FORTYEIGHT - *(Description of Marriage)***

---

Sanskrit
४८

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र गर्गाचार्यप्रणोदितः ।
हिमवान्मेनया सार्धं कन्यां दातुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  In the meantime, urged by the priest Garga Himavat started the rite of marriage in the company of Menā. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हैमं कलशमादाय मेना चार्धाङ्गमाश्रिता ।
हिमाद्रेश्च महाभागा वस्त्राभरणभूषिता ॥ २॥

English
Himavat and Menā held the gold pot on either side. Himavat was bedecked in fine clothes and ornaments. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पाद्यादिभिस्ततः शैलः प्रहृष्टः स्वपुरोहितः ।
तं वरं वरयामास वस्त्रचन्दनभूषणैः ॥ ३॥

English
The joyous mountain with the assistance of his priest wooed the bridegroom after offering water, clothes, ornaments, sandal paste etc. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हिमाद्रिणा प्रोक्ता द्विजास्तिथ्यादिकीर्तने ।
प्रयोगो भण्यतां तावदस्मिन्समय आगते ॥ ४॥

English
Then the brahmins were requested by Himavat "May the rite be formally started after narrating the Tithi etc. The auspicious hour has come." (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तथेति चोक्ता ते सर्वे कालज्ञा द्विजसत्तमाः ।
तिथ्यादिकीर्तनं चक्रुः प्रीत्या परमनिर्वृताः ॥ ५॥

English
After saying "So be it", the excellent brahmins who knew the proper time proclaimed the Tithi etc. very delightedly. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हिमाचलः प्रीत्या शम्भुना प्रेरितो हृदा ।
सूतीकृतपरेशेन विहसन् शम्भुमब्रवीत् ॥ ६॥

English
Then Himācala mentally urged with pleasure by lord Śiva, the cause of great enjoyment, smilingly spoke to Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वगोत्रं कथ्यतां शम्भो प्रवरश्च कुलं तथा ।
नाम वेदं तथा शाखां माकार्षीः समयात्ययम् ॥ ७॥

English
"O Śiva, please do not delay. Please mention your genealogy, saintly lineage,¹⁰⁸ family, name and your Veda along with your branch of the Vedas." (7.)

[fn 108. Before the bride is given away to the bridegroom, the names of the ancestors of both the parties with Gotra and Pravara are announced loudly so that the people assembled should know that both, the bride and the bridegroom, come of good families, the pedigree of which can be traced to many generations. The ceremony is called Gotroccāra in the Gṛhyasūtras.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य हिमाद्रेः शङ्करस्तदा ।
सुमुखो विमुखः सद्योऽप्यशोच्यः शोच्यतां गतः ॥ ८॥

English
Brahmā said:—  On hearing these words of Himavat, Śiva of sweet face, turned His face away. He without sorrow attained a pitiable plight. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एवंविधः सुरवरैर्मुनिभिस्तदानीं
गन्धर्वयक्षगणसिद्धगणैस्तथैव ।
दृष्टो निरुत्तरमुखो भगवान्महेशोऽ-
कार्षीस्सुहास्यमथ तत्र स नारद त्वम् ॥ ९॥

English
When lord Śiva stood thus unable to say anything in reply and was seen so by the gods, sages, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, and Siddhas, O Nārada, you did something laughable. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
वीणामवादयंस्त्वं हि ब्रह्मविज्ञोऽथ नारद ।
शिवेन प्रेरितस्तत्र मनसा शम्भुमानसः ॥ १०॥

English
Urged by Śiva mentally O Nārada, you, the knower of Brahman with mind fixed in Śiva, played on your Viṇā. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा निवारितो धीमान् पर्वतेन्द्रेण वै हठात् ।
विष्णुना च मया देवैर्मुनिभिश्चाखिलैस्तथा ॥ ११॥

English
You were forbidden strictly by the lord of mountains, Viṣṇu, gods, sages and by me. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
न निवृत्तोऽभवस्त्वं हि स यदा शङ्करेच्छया ।
इति प्रोक्तोऽद्रिणा तर्हि वीणां मा वादयाधुना ॥ १२॥

English
When at the will of Śiva you did not desist from it, you were again spoken to thus by the mountain then—"Do not play on the Viṇā now." (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सुनिषिद्धो हठात्तेन देवर्षे त्वं यदा बुध ।
प्रत्यवोचो गिरीशं तं सुसंस्मृत्य महेश्वरम् ॥ १३॥

English
O celestial sage, O wise one, when you were thus strenuously forbidden, you remembered Śiva and spoke to the lord of the mountains. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
त्वं हि मूढत्वमापन्नो न जानासि च किञ्चन ।
वाच्ये महेशविषयेऽतीवासि त्वं बहिर्मुखः ॥ १४॥

English
Nārada said:—  You have been utterly deluded. You do not know anything about Śiva of whom you speak. You have no inner vision. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वया पृष्ठो हरः साक्षात्स्वगोत्रकथनं प्रति ।
समयेऽस्मिंस्तदत्यन्तमुपहासकरं वचः ॥ १५॥

English
Śiva was directly asked by you to mention His Gotra. On this occasion these words are utterly ridiculous and derisible. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य गोत्रं कुलं नाम नैव जानन्ति पर्वत ।
विष्णुब्रह्मादयोऽपीह परेषां का कथा स्मृता ॥ १६॥

English
O mountain, even Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods do not know His Gotra, family and name. What then can be said about others ? (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्यैकदिवसे शैल ब्रह्मकोटिर्लयं गता ।
स एव शङ्करस्तेद्य दृष्टः कालीतपोबलात् ॥ १७॥

English
It was a result of the severe penance of Pārvatī that Śiva was seen by you, O mountain, in one day according to whose calculation a crore of Brahmās become annihilated. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अरूपोऽयं परब्रह्म निर्गुणः प्रकृतेः परः ।
निराकारो निर्विकारी मायाधीशः परात्परः ॥ १८॥

English
He is the formless supreme Brahman. He is attributeless. He is greater than Primordial Nature. He has no shape, is free from aberrations He is the master of delusion. He is greater than the greatest. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अगोत्रकुलनामा हि स्वतन्त्रो भक्तवत्सलः ।
तदिच्छया हि सगुणः सुतनुर्बहुनामभृत् ॥ १९॥

English
He has no Gotra, family or name. He is independent. He is favourably disposed to His devotees. At His will He assumes bodies taking many names. He is full of attributes. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सुगोत्री गोत्रहीनश्च कुलहीनः कुलीनकः ।
पार्वतीतपसा सोऽद्य जामाता ते न संशयः ॥ २०॥

English
He is sugotrin (having good gotra) as well as devoid of gotra. He is of noble family as well as devoid of a family. Thanks to Pārvatī's penance. He has now become your son-in-law, There is no doubt about it. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
लीलाविहारिणा तेन मोहितं सचराचरम् ।
नो जानाति शिवं कोऽपि प्राज्ञोऽपि गिरिसत्तम ॥ २१॥

English
The whole world consisting of the mobile and immobile has been deluded by Him in His divine sport. O excellent mountain, even the wisest of men does not know Him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गाकृतेर्महेशस्य केन दृष्टं न मस्तकम् ।
विष्णुर्गत्वा हि पातालं तदैनं नाप विस्मितः ॥ २२॥

English
The head of lord Śiva of phallic image was not seen by Brahmā. Viṣṇu who went to the nether worlds did not see His foot. How surprised he was. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्त्या नगश्रेष्ठ शिवमाया दुरत्यया ।
तदधीनास्त्रयो लोका हरिब्रह्मादयोऽपि च ॥ २३॥

English
O excellent mountain, of what avail is this talk ? Śiva's magical power is inscrutable. The three worlds, Viṣṇu Brahmā and others too are subservient to Him. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वया शिवातात सुविचार्य प्रयत्नतः ।
न कर्तव्यो विमर्शोऽत्र त्वेवंविधवरे मनाक् ॥ २४॥

English
Hence, O father of Pārvatī, ponder over this deeply. No doubt need be entertained by you even slightly with respect to this bridegroom of your choice. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा त्वं मुने ज्ञानी शिवेच्छाकार्यकारकः ।
प्रत्यवोचः पुनस्तं वै शैलेद्रं हर्षयन् गिरा ॥ २५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, after saying this, you, of perfect wisdom, who carried out the will of Śiva replied again to the mountain after delighting him with your words. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात महाशैल शिवाजनक मद्वचः ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा तनयां देवीं देहि त्वं शङ्कराय हि ॥ २६॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O dear, O great mountain, O father of Pārvatī, listen to my words. After hearing them, give your daughter to Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सगुणस्य महेशस्य लीलया रूपधारिणः ।
गोत्रं कुलं विजानीहि नादमेव हि केवलम् ॥ २७॥

English
Know that the divine sound alone is the gotra, and family of Śiva in His divine form, who assumes forms in His divine sport. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो नादमयः सत्यं नादः शिवमयस्तथा ।
उभयोरन्तरं नास्ति नादस्य च शिवस्य च ॥ २८॥

English
Śiva is identical with Nāda.¹⁰⁹ And Nāda is identical with Śiva. There is no difference between the two—Nāda and Śiva. (28.)

[fn ¹⁰⁹. Nāda is a mystical sound identical with Śiva which symbolises his mystical origin.]
---

Sanskrit
सृष्टौ प्रथमजत्वाद्धि लीलासगुणरूपिणः ।
शिवान्नादस्य शैलेन्द्र सर्वोत्कृष्टस्ततः स हि ॥ २९॥

English
O lord of mountains, Nāda being prior to Śiva in His sportive, attributive form, Nāda is the most excellent of all. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो हि वादिता वीणा प्रेरितेन मयाद्य वै ।
सर्वेश्वरेण मनसा शङ्करेण हिमालय ॥ ३०॥

English
Hence, O Himācala, mentally urged by Śiva, the lord of all, I played upon my lute. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतच्छ्रुत्वा तव मुने वचस्तत्तु गिरिश्वरः ।
हिमाद्रिस्तोषमापन्नो गतविस्मयमानसः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, on hearing your words, Himavat, the lord of mountains was satisfied and the bewilderment in his mind vanished. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुप्रभृतयः सुराश्च मुनयस्तथा ।
साधु साध्विति ते सर्वे प्रोचुर्विगतविस्मयाः ॥ ३२॥

English
Then Viṣṇu, the other gods and the sages said “Well done, Well done”. They were freed of all bewilderment. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरस्य गाम्भीर्यं ज्ञात्वा सर्वे विचक्षणाः ।
सविस्मया महामोदान्विताः प्रोचुः परस्परम् ॥ ३३॥

English
The shrewd people realised the majesty of lord Śiva. They were pleasantly surprised and began to say to one another. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्याज्ञया जगदिदं च विशालमेव
जातं परात्परतरो निजबोधरूपः ।
शर्वः स्वतन्त्रगतिकृत्परभावगम्यः
सोऽसौ त्रिलोकपतिरद्य च नः सुदृष्टः ॥ ३४॥

English
“Śiva is of the form of knowledge. He is greater than the greatest. It is at His bidding that the vast universe is born. He is of independent movement. He can be realised by the greatest concentration. He, the lord of the three worlds, is now seen by us.” (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते पर्वतश्रेष्ठा मेर्वाद्या जातसम्भ्रमाः ।
ऊचुस्ते चैकपद्येन हिमवन्तं नगेश्वरम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Then Meru and the excellent mountains became agitated and simultaneously spoke to Himavat, the lord of mountains. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वता ऊचुः ।
कन्यादाने स्थीयतां चाद्य शैल-
नाथोक्त्या किं कार्यनाशस्तवैव ।
सत्यं ब्रूमो नात्र कार्यो विमर्शः
तस्मात्कन्या दीयतामीश्वराय ॥ ३६॥

English
*The mountains said:—*  O mountain, be firm and stand by your decision to give your daughter. If you say "No", you stand to lose. We speak the truth. Do not hesitate. Let the girl be given to Śiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तेषां सुहृदां स हिमालयः ।
स्वकन्यादानमकरोच्छिवाय विधिनोदितः ॥ ३७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of his friends, Himavat urged by Brahmā gave his daughter to Śiva. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

भार्यार्थं परिगृह्णीष्व प्रसीद सकलेश्वर ॥ ३८॥

English
"O lord Śiva, I am giving this girl, my daughter to you as your wife. O lord of all, be pleased to accept her." (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मै रुद्राय महते मन्त्रेणानेन दत्तवान् ।
हिमाचलो निजां कन्यां पार्वतीं त्रिजगत्प्रसूम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Himavat gave his daughter Pārvatī, the mother of the three worlds, to Śiva the great, repeating the mantra "Tasmai Rudrāya Mahate". (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं शिवाकरं शैलं शिवहस्ते निधाय च ।
मुमोदातीव मनसि तीर्णकाममहार्णवः ॥ ४०॥

English
Placing the hand of Pārvatī in the hand of Śiva the mountain rejoiced much mentally. He had the satisfaction of crossing the ocean of his ambition. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदमन्त्रेण गिरिशो गिरिजाकरपङ्कजम् ।
जग्राह स्वकरेणाशु प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ॥ ४१॥

English
Śiva grasped the lotus-like hand of Pārvatī in his hand repeating the Vedic mantras. Lord Śiva was greatly delighted. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षितिं संस्पृश्य कामस्य कोऽदादिति मनुं मुने ।
पपाठ शङ्करः प्रीत्या दर्शयँल्लौकिकीं गतिम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Touching the ground and showing the worldly course of action, O sage, Śiva recited the mantra "Kāmasya Kodāt".$^{110}$ (42.)

[fn 110. VS. 7-48. The mantra begins with कोऽवात् करमा अवात्]
---

Sanskrit
महोत्सवो महानासीत्सर्वत्र प्रमुदावहः ।
बभूव जयसंरावो दिवि भूम्यन्तरिक्षके ॥ ४३॥

English
There was a great jubilation everywhere that gladdened everyone. Cries of "Victory" rose up in the heaven, the earth and the sky. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
साधुशब्दं नमः शब्दं चक्रुः सर्वेऽतिहर्षिताः ।
गन्धर्वाः सुजगुः प्रीत्या ननृतुश्चाप्सरोगणाः ॥ ४४॥

English
The delighted people shouted "Well done" and "Obeisance to you". The Gandharvas sang sweetly with pleasure. The celestial damsels danced. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

मङ्गलं महदासीद्वै महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ ४५॥

English
The citizens, the subjects of Himavat rejoiced in their minds. There was great auspicious jubilation. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विष्णुश्च शक्रश्च निर्जरा मुनयोऽखिलाः ।
हर्षिता ह्यभवंश्चाति प्रफुल्लवदनाम्बुजाः ॥ ४६॥

English
Viṣṇu, Indra, I and the gods were delighted, with the faces beaming like full blown lotuses. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

शिवाय कन्यादानस्य साङ्गतां सुयथोचिताम् ॥ ४७॥

English
Then the gleeful lord of mountains gave the ancillary articles of present to Śiva in a fitting manner. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then his kinsmen worshipped Śiva with devotion and gave Pārvatī and monetary presents to Śiva in accordance with the various injunctions of the Śāstras. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

नानाविधानि द्रव्याणि ददौ तत्र मुनीश्वर ॥ ४९॥

English
O excellent sage, in order to please Śiva and Pārvatī, the delighted Himavat presented many gifts of articles. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He gave to Śiva some articles as dowry. Different kinds of gems and gemset vessels were given to him. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
