### **CHAPTER FIFTYONE - *(The resuscitation of Kāma)***

---

Sanskrit
५१

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तस्मिन्नवसरे ज्ञात्वानुकूलं समयं रतिः ।
सुप्रसन्ना च तं प्राह शङ्करं दीनवत्सलम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  At that time, thinking that the hour was favourable, Rati hopefully spoke to Śiva who is favourably disposed towards depressed people. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
रतिरुवाच ।
गृहीत्वा पार्वतीं प्राप्तं सौभाग्यमतिदुर्लभम् ।
किमर्थं प्राणनाथो मे निस्स्वार्थं भस्मसात्कृतः ॥ २॥

English
*Rati said:—*  Why did you reduce my beloved husband to ashes without gaining any interest when he had come near you with Pārvatī? He was my only fortunate possession very rare to get. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवयात्र पतिं मे हि कामव्यापारमात्मनि ।
कुरु दूरं च सन्तापं समविश्लेषहेतुकम् ॥ ३॥

English
Give me back my husband, the lord of my journey of life who used to work lovingly with me. Remove my distress caused by separation. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विवाहोत्सव एतस्मिन् सुखिनो निखिला जनाः ।
अहमेका महेशान दुःखिनी स्वपतिं विना ॥ ४॥

English
O lord Śiva, in the great festival of your marriage, all people are happy. I alone am unhappy without my husband. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सनाथां कुरु मां देव प्रसन्नो भव शङ्कर ।
स्वोक्तं सत्यं विधेहि त्वं दीनबन्धो पर प्रभो ॥ ५॥

English
O lord, make me possessed of my husband. O Śiva, be pleased. O lord, friend of the distressed, please make your words true. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां विना कः समर्थोऽत्र त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ।
नाशने मम दुःखस्य ज्ञात्वेति करुणां कुरु ॥ ६॥

English
Excepting you, who is there in the three worlds including the mobile and immobile creatures who can destroy my sorrow. Knowing this, be merciful. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सोत्सवे स्वविवाहेऽस्मिन्सर्वानन्दप्रदायिनी ।
सोत्सवामपि मां नाथ कुरु दीनकृपाकर ॥ ७॥

English
O lord, merciful to the depressed, make me jubilant at the jubilant celebration of your marriage that gives pleasure to everyone. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
जीविते मम नाथे हि पार्वत्या प्रियया सह ।
सुविहारः प्रपूर्णश्च भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ ८॥

English
There is no doubt in this, that only when my lord is resuscitated will your sportive dalliance with your beloved Pārvatī be complete and perfect. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं कर्तुं समर्थोऽसि यतस्त्वं परमेश्वरः ।
किं बहूक्त्यात्र सर्वेश जीवयाशु पतिं मम ॥ ९॥

English
You are competent to do everything because you are the supreme lord. O lord of all, of what avail is this talk. Please resuscitate my husband quickly. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तदित्युक्त्वा कामभस्म ददौ सग्रन्थिबन्धनम् ।
रुदोद पुरतः शम्भोर्नाथ नाथेत्युदीर्य च ॥ १०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying thus she gave him the ashes of the cupid along with the bag in which they had been contained. “O lord, O lord”, saying thus she lamented much in front of Śiva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
रतिरोदनमाकर्ण्य सरस्वत्यादयः स्त्रियः ।
रुरुदुः सकला देव्यः प्रोचुर्दीनतरं वचः ॥ ११॥

English
On hearing the lamentation of Rati, Sarasvati and other celestial ladies wept bitterly and spoke in piteous tones. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्य ऊचुः ।
भक्तवत्सलनामा त्वं दीनबन्धुर्दयानिधिः ॥

कामं जीवय सोत्साहां रतिं कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १२॥

English
*The celestial ladies said:—*  Obeisance to you, O lord, you are known as favourably disposed to your devotees. You are friend of the distressed, storehouse of mercy. Resuscitate the cupid. Make Rati jubilant. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा प्रसन्नोऽभून्महेश्वरः ।
कृपादृष्टिं चकाराशु करुणासागरः प्रभुः ॥ १३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing their words, lord Śiva was delighted. The lord, the ocean of mercy, glanced compassionately. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधादृष्ट्या शूलभृतो भस्मतो निर्गतः स्मरः ।
तद्रूपवेषचिह्नात्मा सुन्दरोऽद्भुतमूर्तिमान् ॥ १४॥

English
Thanks to the nectarine glance of the Trident-bearing lord, Kāma came out of the ashes, a comely wonder-inspiring body with splendid dress and features. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रूपश्च तदाकारं सस्मितं सधनुशरम् ।
दृष्ट्वा पतिं रतिस्तं च प्रणनाम महेश्वरम् ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing her husband in the same form as before, wielding the bow and the arrows and smiling, Rati bowed to lord Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतार्थाभूच्छिवं देवं तुष्टाव च कृताञ्जलिः ।
प्राणनाथप्रदं पत्या जीवितेन पुनः पुनः ॥ १६॥

English
She became contented. With her husband resuscitated and with palms joined in reverence she eulogised the lord, the bestower of her husband, frequently. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कामस्य स्तुतिमाकर्ण्य सनारीकस्य शङ्करः ।
प्रसन्नोऽभवदत्यन्तमुवाच करुणार्द्रधीः ॥ १७॥

English
On hearing the eulogy of Kāma and his wife, Śiva was delighted and he spoke with his heart melting with pity. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
प्रसन्नोऽहं तव स्तुत्या सनारीकस्य चित्तज ।
स्वयम्भव वरं ब्रूहि वाञ्छितं तद् ददामि ते ॥ १८॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  O Kāma, I am delighted by your eulogy in the company of your wife. O self-born, tell me the boon you desire. I shall grant it. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति शम्भुवचः श्रुत्वा महानन्दः स्मरस्ततः ।
उवाच साञ्जलिर्नम्रो गद्गदाक्षरया गिरा ॥ १९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Śiva, Kāma was highly delighted. Humbly and in faltering accents he spoke with palms joined in reverence. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
काम उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
यदि प्रसन्नः सर्वेश ममानन्दकरो भव ॥ २०॥

English
*Kāma said:—*  O lord of gods, O ocean of mercy, if you, the lord of all, are pleased with me please be delightful to me. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षमस्व मेऽपराधं हि यत्कृतश्च पुरा प्रभो ।
स्वजनेषु परां प्रीतिं भक्तिं देहि स्वपादयोः ॥ २१॥

English
O lord, please forgive my fault formerly perpetrated by me. Please grant me great affection towards my people and devotion to your feet. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य स्मरवचः प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ।
ओमित्युक्त्वाब्रवीत्तं वै विहसन्करुणानिधिः ॥ २२॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing the words of Kāma, lord Śiva was delighted. Giving consent, the lord of mercy laughingly said. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
हे कामाहं प्रसन्नोऽस्मि भयं त्यज महामते ।
गच्छ विष्णुसमीपं च बहिःस्थाने स्थितो भव ॥ २३॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  O Kāma, I am delighted. O intelligent one, do not fear. Go near Viṣṇu and wait outside. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा शिरसा नत्वा परिक्रम्य स्तुवन्विभुम् ।
बहिर्गत्वा हरिन्देवान् प्रणम्य समुपास्त सः ॥ २४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words he bowed to, circumambulated and eulogised the lord. Then he went out and bowed to Viṣṇu and gods. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कामं सम्भाष्य देवाश्च ददुस्तस्मै शुभाशिषम् ।
विष्ण्वादयः प्रसन्नास्ते प्रोचुः स्मृत्वा शिवं हृदि ॥ २५॥

English
Addressing Kāma, the gods congratulated him and offered him their auspicious blessings. Remembering Śiva, Viṣṇu and others spoke to him. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
धन्यस्त्वं स्मर सन्दग्धः शिवेनानुग्रहीकृतः ।
जीवयामास सत्त्वांशकृपादृष्ट्याखिलेश्वरः ॥ २६॥

English
*The gods said:—*  O Kāma, you are blessed. Burnt by Śiva you have been blessed by Him. The lord of all has resuscitated you by means of his sympathetic glance, the Sāttvika part. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखदुःखदो न चान्योऽस्ति यतः स्वकृतभुक् पुमान् ।
काले रक्षा विवाहश्च निषेकः केन वार्यते ॥ २७॥

English
No man causes happiness or sorrow to another man. Man experiences the fruits of what he does. Who can ward off the destined protection, marriage or consummation at the proper time? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ते च सम्मान्य तं सुखेनामरास्तदा ।
सन्तस्थुस्तत्र विष्ण्वाद्याः सर्वे लब्धमनोरथाः ॥ २८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying thus, the gods happily honoured him. Viṣṇu and other gods who had realised their desire stayed there with pleasure. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि प्रमुदितस्तत्र समुवास शिवाज्ञया ।
जयशब्दो नमः शब्दः साधुशब्दो बभूव ह ॥ २९॥

English
He too remained there, at the bidding of Śiva, with great delight. There were shouts of “Victory” “Obeisance” and “well-done”. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शम्भुर्वासगेहे वामे संस्थाप्य पार्वतीम् ।
मिष्टान्नं भोजयामास तं च सा च मुदान्विता ॥ ३०॥

English
At the bed-chamber Śiva placed Pārvatī on His left side and fed her with sweets. She too delightedly fed him with sweets in return. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुर्भवाचारी तत्र कृत्यं विधाय तत् ।
मेनामामन्त्र्य शैलं च जनवासं जगाम सः ॥ ३१॥

English
Śiva according to the conventions of the world performed the customary rites. Taking leave of Menā and the mountain He came to the audience hall. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
महोत्सवस्तदा चासीद् वेदध्वनिरभून्मुने ।
वाद्यानि वादयामासुर्जनाश्चतुर्विधानि च ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, there was great jubilation then. Sounds of Vedic chants rose up. People played on the four kinds of musical instruments. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरागत्य स्वस्थानं ववन्दे च मुनींस्तदा ।
हरिं च मां भवाचाराद्वन्दितोऽभूत्सुरादिभिः ॥ ३३॥

English
Coming back to His apartment, Śiva saluted the sages, Viṣṇu and me according to the worldly convention. He was duly saluted by the gods and others. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
जयशब्दो बभूवाथ नमश्शब्दस्तथैव च ।
वेदध्वनिश्च शुभदः सर्वविघ्नविदारणः ॥ ३४॥

English
Shouts of "Victory" and "Obeisance" rose up along with the sound of Vedic mantras which were auspicious and which removed all obstacles. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुरहं शक्रः सर्वे देवाश्च सर्षयः ।
सिद्धा उपसुरा नागास्तुष्टुवुस्ते पृथक्पृथक् ॥ ३५॥

English
Then Viṣṇu, I (Indra), gods, sages, Siddhas, secondary gods and the Nāgas eulogised Him severally. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
जय शम्भोऽखिलाधार जय नाम महेश्वर ।
जय रुद्र महादेव जय विश्वम्भर प्रभो ॥ ३६॥

English
The gods said:—  O Śiva, be victorious. O lord Śiva, the support of all, be victorious. O Rudra, O great lord, the supporter of the world, be victorious. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
जय कालीपते स्वामिन् जयानन्दप्रवर्धक ।
जय त्र्यम्बक सर्वेश जय मायापते विभो ॥ ३७॥

English
O Pārvatī's lord, O lord, accentuator of pleasure, O three-eyed one, O lord of all, the lord of illusion, be victorious, be victorious. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
जय निर्गुण निष्काम कारणातीत सर्वग ।
जय लीलाखिलाधार धृतरूप नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ३८॥

English
O lord, devoid of attributes, bereft of desires, O lord beyond all causes, O omnipresent, O playful support of all, O assumer of forms, Obeisance to you, be victorious. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
जय स्वभक्तसत्कामप्रदेश करुणाकर ।
जय सानन्दसद्रूप जय मायागुणाकृते ॥ ३९॥

English
O lord, bestower of good desires to your devotees, O merciful one, O bliss-formed, assuming forms through magic illusions, be victorious. Be victorious, O kind, O All-souled one, friend of the distressed, storehouse of mercy, O lord of illusion, free from aberrations, whose body is beyond the reach of speech and mind. (39-40.)

O dear one, Viṣṇu and others taking leave of the great lord delightedly returned to their respective places. They were duly honoured and their faces beamed with pleasure. (43.)
