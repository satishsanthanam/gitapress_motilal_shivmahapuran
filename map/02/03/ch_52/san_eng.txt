### **CHAPTER FIFTYTWO - *(The bridegroom's party is fed and Śiva retires to bed)***

---

Sanskrit
५२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ शैलवरस्तात हिमवान्भाग्यसत्तमः ।
प्राङ्गणं रचयामास भोजनार्थं विचक्षणः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O dear one, then the clever chief of mountains caused suitable arrangements to be made in the courtyard for feeding the visitors. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्जनं लेपनं सम्यक्कारयामास तस्य सः ।
स सुगन्धैरलञ्चक्रे नानावस्तुभिरादरात् ॥ २॥

English
He caused the ground to be swept clean and scrubbed well. Different kinds of fragrant stuffs were used to make the place attractive and pleasing. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शैलः सुरान् सर्वानन्यानपि च सेश्वरान् ।
भोजनायाह्वयामास पुत्रैः शैलैः परैरपि ॥ ३॥

English
Then the mountain invited all the gods and others along with the lord for taking food, through his sons and others. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलाह्वानमथाकर्ण्य स प्रभुः साच्युतो मुने ।
सर्वैःसुरादिभिस्तत्र भोजनाय ययौ मुदा ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, on hearing the invitation of the mountain, the lord accompanied by Viṣṇu, the gods and others went gladly to take His meal. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिः प्रभुं च सर्वांस्तान् सुसत्कृत्य यथाविधि ।
मुदोपवेशयामास सत्पीठेषु गृहान्तरे ॥ ५॥

English
The mountain received the lord and all those duly and made them sit in good seats in the inner apartment. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
नानासुभोज्यवस्तूनि परिवेष्य च तत्पुनः ।
साञ्चलिर्भोजनायाज्ञां चक्रे विज्ञप्तिमानतः ॥ ६॥

English
After serving sweet and well-cooked delicious food-stuffs, he requested them to take their food with palms joined in reverence and head bent down. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सम्मानितास्तत्र देवा विष्णुपुरोगमाः ।
सदाशिवं पुरस्कृत्य बुभुजुः सकलाश्च ते ॥ ७॥

English
Then duly honoured, Viṣṇu and other gods keeping Sadāśiva at the head took their food. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सर्वे हि मिलिता ऐकपद्येन सर्वशः ।
पङ्क्तिभूताश्च बुभुजुर्विहसन्तः पृथक्पृथक् ॥ ८॥

English
They sat in rows together, took their food simultaneously laughing (and talking). (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिभृङ्गिवीरभद्रवीरभद्रगणाः पृथक् ।
बुभुजुस्ते महाभागाः कुतूहलसमन्विताः ॥ ९॥

English
Nandin, Bhṛṅgi, Vīrabhadra and his Gaṇas took their meals separately. The fortunate people took food enthusiastically. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाः सेन्द्रा लोकपाला नानाशोभासमन्विताः ।
बुभुजुस्ते महाभागा नानाहास्यरसैस्सह ॥ १०॥

English
The gods, with Indra, the guardians of the quarters all fortunate and brilliant took their food cracking jokes and talking. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे च मुनयो विप्रा भृग्वाद्या ऋषयस्तथा ।
बुभुजुः प्रीतितः सर्वे पृथक् पङ्क्तिगतास्तदा ॥ ११॥

English
The sages and brahmins, Bhṛgu and other sages sat in separate rows and took their food with pleasure. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा चण्डीगणाः सर्वे बुभुजुः कृतभोजनाः ।
कुतूहलं प्रकुर्वन्तो नानाहास्यकरा मुदा ॥ १२॥

English
The Gaṇas of Caṇḍī took their meals and then cracked jokes and talked merrily. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं ते भुक्तवन्तश्चाचम्य सर्वे मुदान्विताः ।
विश्रामार्थं गताः प्रीत्या विष्ण्वाद्याः स्वं स्वमाश्रमम् ॥ १३॥

English
After taking meals and rinsing their mouths Viṣṇu and others went to their apartments for rest. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनाज्ञया स्त्रियः साध्व्यः शिवं सम्प्रार्थ्य भक्तितः ।
गेहे निवासयामासुर्वासाख्ये परमोत्सवे ॥ १४॥

English
At the bidding of Menā, the chaste ladies requested Śiva humbly and made Him stay in the bedchamber where great festivities were going on. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
रत्नसिंहासने शम्भुः मेनादत्ते मनोहरे ।
सन्निधाय मुदा युक्तो ददृशे वासमन्दिरम् ॥ १५॥

English
Seated on a gemset throne offered by Menā, Śiva surveyed the bedchamber with pleasure. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रत्नप्रदीपशतकैर्ज्वलद्भिर्ज्वलितं श्रिया ।
रत्नपात्रघटाकीर्णं मुक्तामणिविराजितम् ॥ १६॥

English
It was brightly illuminated with hundreds of gemset lamps. There were many gemset vessels. Pearls etc. were gorgeously displayed. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
रत्नदर्पणशोभाढ्यं मण्डितं श्वेतचामरैः ।
मुक्तामणिसुमालाभिर्वेष्टितं परमर्द्धिमत् ॥ १७॥

English
Gemset mirrors, white chowries, pearl necklaces and gorgeous things were richly displayed. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अनौपम्यं महादिव्यं विचित्रं सुमनोहरम् ।
चित्ताह्लादकरं नानारचनारचितस्थलम् ॥ १८॥

English
It was unequalled in its divine exquisiteness highly pleasing and richly decorated. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवदत्तवरस्यैव प्रभावमतुलं परम् ।
दर्शयन्तं समुल्लासि शिवलोकाभिधानकम् ॥ १९॥

English
It was evincing the powerful influence of the boon granted by Śiva. It appeared to be a replica of Śiva Loka itself. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नानासुगन्धसद्द्रव्यैर्वासितं सुप्रकाशकम् ।
चन्दनागरुसंयुक्तं पुष्पशय्यासमन्वितम् ॥ २०॥

English
It was richly rendered fragrant with various sweet smelling substances. It was very bright. There was sandal paste and aguru. Beds were richly strewn with flowers. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाचित्रविचित्राढ्यं निर्मितं विश्वकर्मणा ।
रत्नेन्द्रसाररचितैराचितं हारकैर्वरैः ॥ २१॥

English
Many wondrous things of variegated colours and shapes were displayed there. It had been constructed in gems by Viśvakarman* himself. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कुत्रचित्सुरनिर्माणं वैकुण्ठं सुमनोहरम् ।
कुत्रचिच्च ब्रह्मलोकं लोकपालपुरं क्वचित् ॥ २२॥

English
In some places replicas of Vaikuṇṭha, Brahmaloka and the cities of the guardians of the quarters were seen. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कैलासं कुत्रचिद्रम्यं कुत्रचिच्छक्रमन्दिरम् ।
कुत्रचिच्छिवलोकं च सर्वोपरि विराजितम् ॥ २३॥

English
In a certain place the beautiful Kailāsa was represented. In another place Indra's palace was depicted. Over all was represented the Śivaloka. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एतादृशं गृहं सर्वं दृष्ट्वाश्चर्यं महेश्वरः ।
प्रशंसन् हिमशैलेशं परितुष्टो बभूव ह ॥ २४॥

English
Seeing all these wonderful representations lord Śiva praised Himavat and was very glad. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रातिरमणीये च रत्नपर्यङ्क उत्तमे ।
अशयिष्ट मुदा युक्तो लीलया परमेश्वरः ॥ २५॥

English
In that bedchamber, in a beautiful gemset couch lord Śiva lay down with pleasure. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचलश्च स्वभ्रातॄन्भोजयामास कृत्स्नशः ।
सर्वानन्यांश्च सुप्रीत्या शेषकृत्यं चकार ह ॥ २६॥

English
Himavat fed all his brothers and others with pleasure and attended to the subsequent duties. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कुर्वति शैलेशे स्वपिति प्रेष्ठ ईश्वरे ।
व्यतीता रजनी सर्वा प्रातःकालो बभूव ह ॥ २७॥

English
While the supreme lord had his sleep and the lord of the mountains was engaged in these duties, the night passed away giving place to dawn. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रभातकाले च धृत्युत्साहपरायणाः ।
नानाप्रकारवाद्यानि वादयाञ्चक्रिरे जनाः ॥ २८॥

English
In the morning the enthusiastic people began to play on different kinds of musical instruments. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे सुराःसमुत्तस्थुर्विष्ण्वाद्याः सुमुदान्विताः ।
स्वेष्टं संस्मृत्य देवेशं सज्जीभूताः ससम्भ्रमाः ॥ २९॥

English
Viṣṇu and the other gods got up with joy, remembered the lord of gods and excitedly got ready. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
स्ववाहनानि सज्जानि कैलासं गन्तुमुत्सुकाः ।
कृत्वा सम्प्रेषयामासुर्धर्मं शिवसमीपतः ॥ ३०॥

English
They got their vehicles ready for the departure to Kailāsa and sent Dharma to Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वासगेहमथागत्य धर्मो नारायणाज्ञया ।
उवाच शङ्करं योगी योगीशं समयोचितम् ॥ ३१॥

English
At the bidding of Viṣṇu, Dharma went near the bed chamber. The Yogin Dharma addressed Śiva, the lord of Yogins, in a manner befitting the context. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्म उवाच ।
उत्तिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भद्रं ते भव नः प्रमथाधिप ।
जनावासं समागच्छ कृतार्थं कुरु तत्र तान् ॥ ३२॥

English
*Dharma said:—*  Get up, get up O Śiva, O lord of the Pramathas. Please come over to the audience hall. Make the assembled people gratified. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति धर्मवचः श्रुत्वा विजहास महेश्वरः ।
ददर्श कृपया दृष्ट्या तल्पमुज्झाञ्चकार ह ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Dharma lord Śiva laughed. He surveyed him with sympathetic looks and got up from the bed. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच विहसन् धर्मं त्वमग्रे गच्छ तत्र ह ।
अहमप्यागमिष्यामि द्रुतमेव न संशयः ॥ ३४॥

English
He laughingly said to Dharma—“You go ahead. I shall come there presently. There is no doubt in this matter. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्तः शङ्करेणाथ जनावासं जगाम सः ।
स्वयं गन्तुमना आसीत्तत्र शम्भुरपि प्रभुः ॥ ३५॥

English
Thus addressed by Śiva, he returned to the audience hall. The lord Śiva too wanted to go. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तज्ज्ञात्वा स्त्रीगणः सोऽसौ तत्रागच्छन्महोत्सवः ।
चक्रे मङ्गलगानं हि पश्यन् शम्भुपदद्वयम् ॥ ३६॥

English
On coming to know of it the ladies came enthusiastically. With their eyes fixed on the feet of Śiva, they sang auspicious songs. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुर्भवाचारी प्रातःकृत्यं विधाय च ।
मेनामामन्त्र्य कुध्रं च जनावासं जगाम सः ॥ ३७॥

English
Śiva then, in accordance with the worldly customs, went through his morning routine. He took leave of Menā and the mountain and went to the audience hall. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
महोत्सवस्तदा चासीद्वेदध्वनिरभून्मुने ।
वाद्यानि वादयामासुर्जनाश्चातुर्विधानि च ॥ ३८॥

English
There was great jubilation there, O sage. Vedic mantras were recited loudly. The people played on the four kinds of musical instruments. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरागत्य स्वस्थानं ववन्दे च मुनींस्तदा ।
हरिं च मां भवाचारात् वन्दितोऽभूत्सुरादिभिः ॥ ३९॥

English
Śiva came to His apartment and bowed to the sages, Viṣṇu and me in accordance with the worldly conventions and was saluted by the gods and others. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
जयशब्दो बभूवाथ नमः शब्दस्तथैव च ।
वेदध्वनिश्च शुभदो महाकोलाहलोऽभवत् ॥ ४०॥

English
Shouts of Victory and Obeisance rose up along with the auspicious sound of Vedic chants. There was great tumult. (40.)
