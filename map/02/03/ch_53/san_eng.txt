### **CHAPTER FIFTYTHREE - *(Description of Śiva's return journey)***

---

Sanskrit
५३

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ विष्ण्वादयो देवा मुनयश्च तपोधनाः ।
कृत्वावश्यककर्माणि यात्रां सन्तेनिरे गिरेः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Then Viṣṇu and other gods, the sages and ascetics sent message to the mountain about their intention to leave after finishing their immediate duties. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो गिरिवरः स्नात्वा स्वेष्टं सम्पूज्य यत्नतः ।
पौरबन्धून्समाहूय जनवासं ययौ मुदा ॥ २॥

English
Then the lord of mountains finished his ceremonial ablution and the worship of his favourite deity. Calling his kinsmen in the city, he came to the audience hall joyously. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र प्रभुं प्रपूज्याथ चक्रे सम्प्रार्थनां मुदा ।
कियद्दिनानि सन्तिष्ठ मद्गेहे सकलैः सह ॥ ३॥

English
There he worshipped the lord with pleasure and requested him to stay in his house for a few days more along with all the people. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विलोकनेन ते शम्भो कृतार्थोऽहं न संशयः ।
धन्यश्च यस्य मद्गेहे आयातोऽसि सुरैः सह ॥ ४॥

English
"O Śiva" he said "I am contented by your sight. I am blessed since you came here with the gods". (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा बहु शैलेशः करौ बद्ध्वा प्रणम्य च ।
प्रभुं निमन्त्रयामास सह विष्णुसुरादिभिः ॥ ५॥

English
Saying these words and many more, the lord of mountains pleaded with palms joined in reverence to the lord along with Viṣṇu and other gods. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते मनसा गत्वा शिव संयुतमादरात् ।
प्रत्यूचुर्मुनयो देवा हृष्टा विष्णुसुरादिभिः ॥ ६॥

English
Then the gods and sages remembered Śiva and spoke with delight. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
धन्यस्त्वं गिरिशार्दूल तव कीर्तिर्महीयसी ।
त्वत्समो न त्रिलोकेषु कोऽपि पुण्यतमो जनः ॥ ७॥

English
*The gods said:—*  O lord of the mountains, you are blessed. Your glory is great. Even in the three worlds, there is none equal to you in merit. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य द्वारि महेशानः परब्रह्म सतां गतिः ।
समागतः सदासैश्च कृपया भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ८॥

English
At your very door, lord Śiva, the supreme Brahman, the goal of the good and favourably disposed to His devotees, has deigned to come along with us, His slaves. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जनावासोऽतिरम्यश्च सम्मानो विविधः कृतः ।
भोजनानि त्वपूर्वाणि न वर्ण्यानि गिरीश्वर ॥ ९॥

English
O lord of mountains, this audience hall is very excellent. You have honoured us in diverse ways. The foodstuffs served to us were extraordinary. It is impossible to describe them suitably. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रं न खलु तत्रास्ति यत्र देवी शिवाम्बिका ।
परिपूर्णमशेषं च वयं धन्या यदागताः ॥ १०॥

English
It is no wonder that everything is perfect where the goddess Pārvatī is present. We too are blessed since we came. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्थं परस्परं तत्र प्रशंसाभवदुत्तमा ।
उत्सवो विविधो जातो वेदसाधुजयध्वनिः ॥ ११॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Thus there was mutual admiration and glorification of an enlightened nature. There was great jubilation. The sound of Vedic chant and shouts of victory were heard every where. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अभून्मङ्गलगानं च ननर्ताप्सरसाङ्गणः ।
नुतिं चक्रुर्मागधाद्या द्रव्यदानमभूद् बहु ॥ १२॥

English
There were auspicious songs. The celestial damsels danced. The bards sang songs of praise. There was a liberal exchange of monetary gifts. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तत आमन्त्रय देवेशं स्वगेहमगमद्गिरिः ।
भोजनोत्सवमारेभे नानाविधिविधानतः ॥ १३॥

English
Then the mountain took leave of the lord of gods and went home. He made arrangements for a joyous feast with all paraphernalia in accordance with the rules. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
भोजनार्थं प्रभुं प्रीत्यानयामास यथोचितम् ।
परिवारसमेतं च सकुतूहलमीश्वरम् ॥ १४॥

English
He brought the lord with all his attendants and followers for the feast. He was very enthusiastic. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रक्षाल्य चरणौ शम्भोर्विष्णोर्मम वरादरात् ।
सर्वेषाममराणां च मुनीनां च यथार्थतः ॥ १५॥

परेषां च गतानां च गिरीशो मण्डपान्तरे ।
आसयामास सुप्रीत्या तांस्तान्बन्धुभिरन्वितः ॥ १६॥

English
He washed the feet of Śiva, Viṣṇu and mine with reverence. He seated all of us, including the gods, the sages and others in the altar. The lord of mountains was assisted by his kinsmen. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरसैर्विविधान्नैश्च तर्पयामास तान् गिरिः ।
बुभुजुर्निखिलास्ते वै शम्भुना विष्णुना मया ॥ १७॥

English
The mountain satiated them with various kinds of juicy foodstuffs. All of them took food including Śiva, Viṣṇu and me. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तदानीं पुरनार्यश्च गालीदानं व्यधुर्मुदा ।
मृदुवाण्या हसन्त्यश्च पश्यन्त्यो यत्नतश्च तान् ॥ १८॥

English
Then the ladies of the city indulged in the customary utterance of foul abusive words laughing, jingling and peeping at all of them. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ते भुक्त्वाचम्य विधिवद्गिरिमामन्त्र्य नारद ।
स्वस्थानं प्रययुः सर्वे मुदितास्तृप्तिमागताः ॥ १९॥

English
O Nārada, they took their food and rinsed their mouths. Taking leave of the mountain they returned to their apartments fully satisfied and pleased. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं तृतीये घस्त्रेऽपि मानितास्तेऽभवन्मुने ।
गिरीश्वरेण विधिवद्दानमानादरादिभिः ॥ २०॥

English
O sage, on the third day similarly they were thus duly honoured by the lord of mountains with customary gifts. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्थे दिवसे प्राप्ते चतुर्थीकर्म शुद्धितः ।
बभूव विधिवद्येन विना खण्डित एव सः ॥ २१॥

English
On the fourth day, the rite of Caturthikarman$^{124}$ was performed with due observance. Without this the marriage rites would have been incomplete. (21.)

[fn 124. This rite is so named because it is performed on the fourth day after the wedding. It is performed at the house of the bride’s father before the marriage party leaves it. The purpose of this rite is to remove evil influence from the person of the bride which may cause harm to the family. For details see P.G.S. 1.11.19.]
---

Sanskrit
उत्सवो विविधश्चासीत्साधुवादजयध्वनिः ।
बहुदानं सुगानं च नर्तनं विविधं तथा ॥ २२॥

English
There was diverse jubilant festivity. Shouts of “well-done”, “Victory” etc were heard. There were exchanges of gifts, sweet music and different kinds of dances. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चमे दिवसे प्राप्ते सर्वे देवा मुदान्विताः ।
विज्ञप्तिं चक्रिरे शैलं यात्रार्थमतिप्रेमतः ॥ २३॥

English
On the fifth day the delighted gods lovingly intimated to the mountain about their desire to go back. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य गिरीशश्चोवाच देवान् कृताञ्जलिः ।
कियद्दिनानि तिष्ठन्तु कृपां कुर्वन्तु मां सुराः ॥ २४॥

English
On hearing that, the lord of mountains spoke to the gods with palms joined in reverence “O gods, please stay a few days more”. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा स्नेहतस्ताँश्च प्रभुं विष्णुं च मां परान् ।
वासयामास दिवसान् बहून्नित्यं समादरात् ॥ २५॥

English
Saying thus with great love he made all of us, the lord, Viṣṇu and others stay there for many days, honouring us duly every day. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं व्यतीयुर्दिवसा बहवो वसतां च तत् ।
सप्तर्षीन्प्रेषयामासुर्गिरीशान्ते ततः सुराः ॥ २६॥

English
Thus many days elapsed as the gods continued to stay there. Then the gods sent the seven sages to the lord of the mountains. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ते तं सम्बोधयामासुर्मेनां च समयोचितम् ।
शिवतत्त्वं परं प्रोचुः प्रशंसन्विधिवन्मुदा ॥ २७॥

English
They enlightened the mountain and Menā with what was relevant to the occasion. They told them about Śiva’s principles with due praise. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अङ्गीकृतं गिरीशेन तत्तद्बोधनतो मुने ।
यात्रार्थमगमच्छम्भुः शैलेशं सामरादिकः ॥ २८॥

English
O sage, the proposal was agreed to by the great lord. Then Śiva went to the mountain to tell him about the intended journey, along with the gods and others. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यात्रां कुर्वति देवेशे स्वशैलं सामरे शिवे ।
उच्चैः रुदोद सा मेना तमुवाच कृपानिधिम् ॥ २९॥

English
When the lord of gods started on his journey towards his mountain along with the gods, Menā cried aloud and told the merciful lord. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनोवाच ।
कृपानिधे कृपां कृत्वा शिवां सम्पालयिष्यसि ।
सहस्रदोषं पार्वत्या आशुतोषः क्षमिष्यसि ॥ ३०॥

English
*Menā said:—*  O merciful lord, do mercifully protect Pārvatī. You are quickly pleased. Hence you will please forgive even a thousand faults in her. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्पादाम्बुजभक्ता च मद्वत्सा जन्मजन्मनि ।
स्वप्ने ज्ञाने स्मृतिर्नास्ति महादेवं प्रभुं विना ॥ ३१॥

English
My dear daughter is devoted to your lotus-like feet in every birth. Even sleeping or awake she does not think about anything else. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वद्भक्तिश्रुतिमात्रेण हर्षाश्रुपुलकान्विता ।
त्वन्निन्दया भवेन्मौना मृत्युञ्जय मृता इव ॥ ३२॥

English
O conqueror of death, even on hearing about your devotion she is filled with tears of pleasure and horripilation. On hearing your censure she becomes silent as though dead. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा मेनका तस्मै समर्प्य स्वसुतां तदा ।
अत्युच्चै रोदनं कृत्वा मूर्च्छामाप तयोः पुरः ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Saying this, Menakā dedicated her daughter to Him and crying aloud became unconscious in front of them. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ मेनां बोधयित्वा तामामन्त्र्य गिरिं तथा ।
चकार यात्रां देवैश्च महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ॥ ३४॥

English
When she regained consciousness, Śiva took leave of her and the mountain and set on journey with the gods jubilantly. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते निर्जराः सर्वे प्रभुणा स्वगणैः सह ।
यात्रां प्रचक्रिरे तूष्णीं गिरिम्प्रति शिवं दधुः ॥ ३५॥

English
The gods with the lord and His Gaṇas started on their journey silently. They wished the mountain well. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचलपुरीबाह्योपवने हर्षिताः सुराः ।
सेश्वराः सोत्सवास्तस्थुः पर्यैषन्त शिवागमम् ॥ ३६॥

इत्युक्ता शिवसद्यात्रा देवैस्सह मुनीश्वर ।
आकर्णय शिवयात्रां विरहोत्सवसंयुताम् ॥ ३७॥

English
The lord and the gods waited in a part outside the city of Himavat for the arrival of Pārvatī there. O great sage, thus I have narrated the journey of Śiva. Now listen to the journey of Pārvatī and of her departure with festivities. (36-37.)
