### **CHAPTER FIFTYFOUR - *(Description of the duties of the chaste wife)***

---

Sanskrit
५४

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ सप्तर्षयस्ते च प्रोचुर्हिमगिरीश्वरम् ।
कारय स्वात्मजादेव्या यात्रामद्योचितां गिरे ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Then the seven sages spoke to the lord of the mountains—“O mountain, make arrangements for the journey of your daughter today itself.” (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा गिरीशो हि बुद्ध्वा तद्विरहं परम् ।
विषण्णोऽभून्महाप्रेम्णा कियत्कालं मुनीश्वर ॥ २॥

English
O great sage, on hearing these words and knowing her pangs of separation, the lord of mountains was greatly affected by his love towards her and remained silent for a short while. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कियत्कालेन सम्प्राप्य चेतनां शैलराट् ततः ।
तथास्त्विति गिरामुक्त्वा मेनां सन्देशमब्रवीत् ॥ ३॥

English
After some time, the lord of the mountains regained his consciousness and said—“Let it be so”. He then sent the message to Menā. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलसन्देशमाकर्ण्य हर्षशोकवशा मुने ।
मेना संयापयामास कर्तुमासीत्समुद्यता ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, on hearing the message of the mountain, Menā was both delighted and sorry. She immediately set about arranging for her journey. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुतिस्वकुलजाचारं चचार विधिवन्मुने ।
उत्सवं विविधं तत्र सा मेना क्षितिभृत्प्रिया ॥ ५॥

English
O sage, Menā, the beloved of the mountain, made arrangements for all kinds of festivities in accordance with the tradition of her family and the injunctions of the Vedas. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिजां भूषयामास नानारत्नांशुकैर्वरैः ।
द्वादशाभरणैश्चैव श‍ृङ्गारैर्नृपसम्मितैः ॥ ६॥

English
She bedecked Pārvatī with twelve kinds of ornaments and good silken garments of nice border. All kinds of embellishments befitting her royal state were made. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनामनोगतिं बुद्ध्वा साध्व्येका द्विजकामिनी ।
गिरिजां शिक्षयामास पातिव्रत्यव्रतं परम् ॥ ७॥

English
Realising Menā’s inclinations a chaste brahmin lady instructed Pārvatī in the duties of a chaste wife. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजपत्न्युवाच ।
गिरिजे श‍ृणु सुप्रीत्या मद्वचो धर्मवर्धनम् ।
इहामुत्रानन्दकरं श‍ृण्वतां च सुखप्रदम् ॥ ८॥

English
*The brahmin lady said:—*  O Pārvatī, listen to my words with love that accentuate righteousness, that increase the pleasure here and hereafter and afford happiness to those who pay heed to them. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्या पतिव्रता नारी नान्या पूज्या विशेषतः ।
पावनी सर्वलोकानां सर्वपापौघनाशिनी ॥ ९॥

English
A chaste lady sanctifies the worlds, destroys sins and is blessed. None else is so worthy of respect. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सेवते या पतिं प्रेम्णा परमेश्वरवच्छिवे ।
इह भुक्त्वाखिलान् भोगानन्ते पत्या शिवां गतिम् ॥ १०॥

English
O Pārvatī, she who serves her husband with love and considers him her sole lord, enjoys all pleasures here and obtains salvation hereafter along with her husband. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिव्रता च सावित्री लोपामुद्रा ह्यरुन्धती ।
शाण्डिल्या शतरूपानसूया लक्ष्मीः स्वधा सती ॥ ११॥

संज्ञा च सुमतिः श्रद्धा मेना स्वाहा तथैव च ।
अन्या बह्व्योऽपि साध्व्यो हि नोक्ता विस्तारजाद्भयात् ॥ १२॥

पातिव्रत्यवृषेणैव ता गताः सर्वपूज्यताम् ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुहरैश्चापि मान्या जाता मुनीश्वरैः ॥ १३॥

English
The chaste ladies—Sāvitri, Lopāmudrā, Arundhatī, Śāṇḍilyā, Śatarūpā, Anasūyā, Lakṣmī, Svadhā, Satī, Saṃjña, Sumati, Śraddhā, Menā, Svāhā and several others whose names are not mentioned lest the list should be too detailed have attained adoration from all people by their virtue of chastity. They have been honoured by Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva and great sages. (11-13.)

---

Sanskrit
सेव्यस्त्वया पतिस्तस्मात्सर्वदा शङ्करः प्रभुः ।
दीनानुग्रहकर्ता च सर्वसेव्यः सतां गतिः ॥ १४॥

English
Lord Śiva, benefactor of the depressed, worthy of worship and the goal of the good shall be served by you always. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
महान्पतिव्रताधर्मः श्रुतिस्मृतिषु नोदितः ।
यथैष वर्ण्यते श्रेष्ठो न तथान्योऽस्ति निश्चितम् ॥ १५॥

English
The duty of a chaste lady is very important and it has been mentioned in the Vedas and Smṛtis. No other duty is so admirable as this. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
भुञ्ज्याद्भुक्ते प्रिये पत्यौ पातिव्रत्यपरायणा ।
तिष्ठेत्तस्मिंञ्छिवे नारी सर्वथा सति तिष्ठति ॥ १६॥

English
A chaste lady shall take food only after her husband has taken it. O Śivā, if he stands, the woman too shall remain standing. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वप्यात्स्वपिति सा नित्यं बुध्येत्तु प्रथमं सुधीः ।
सर्वदा तद्धितं कुर्यादकैतवगतिः प्रिया ॥ १७॥

English
When he sleeps she can also sleep. But she must intelligently wake up before him. She shall do what is beneficial to him. She shall love him without any sort of deception. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अनलङ्कृतमात्मानं दर्शयेन्न क्वचिच्छिवे ।
कार्यार्थं प्रोषिते तस्मिन् भवेन्मण्डनवर्जिता ॥ १८॥

English
O Śivā, she shall never show herself unembellished to him. If for any important work he is on exile she shall never adorn herself. (18.)

---

English
A chaste lady shall never mention her husband's name. If the husband scolds or rebukes her she shall not abuse him in return. Even when beaten by him she shall remain glad and say "I may even be killed, O lord. Be kind to me." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्युर्नाम न गृह्णीयात् कदाचन पतिव्रता ।
आक्रुष्टापि न चाक्रोशेत्प्रसीदेत्ताडितापि च ॥

हन्यतामिति च ब्रूयात्स्वामिन्निति कृपां कुरु । १९॥

आहूता गृहकार्याणि त्यक्त्वा गच्छेत्तदन्तिकम् ।
सत्वरं साञ्जलिः प्रीत्या सुप्रणम्य वदेदिति ॥ २०॥

English
When called by him she shall leave the work she is engaged in and approach him immediately. With palms joined in reverence and love she shall bow to him and say as follows. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
किमर्थं व्याहृता नाथ स प्रसादो विधीयताम् ।
तदादिष्टा चरेत्कर्म सुप्रसन्नेन चेतसा ॥ २१॥

English
"O lord, be pleased to say what I have been called for." Whenever ordered by him to do any job she shall do it gladly. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
चिरं तिष्ठेन्न च द्वारे गच्छेन्नैव परालये ।
आदाय तत्त्वं यत्किञ्चित्कस्मैचिन्नार्पयेत्क्वचित् ॥ २२॥

English
She shall not stand near the entrance for a long time. She shall not go to other people's house. She shall not take his money, even though it be a little, and give it to others. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजोपकरणं सर्वमनुक्ता साधयेत्स्वयम् ।
प्रतीक्षमाणावसरं यथाकालोचितं हितम् ॥ २३॥

English
Without being told she shall arrange the necessary requisites for his daily worship. She shall wait for the opportunity to do him a timely service. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
न गच्छेत्तीर्थयात्रां वै पत्याज्ञां न विना क्वचित् ।
दूरतो वर्जयेत्सा हि समाजोत्सवदर्शनम् ॥ २४॥

English
Without the permission of her husband she shall not go even on pilgrimage. She shall eschew the desire to attend social festivities. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तीर्थार्थिनी तु या नारी पतिपादोदकं पिबेत् ।
तस्मिन्सर्वाणि तीर्थानि क्षेत्राणि च न संशयः ॥ २५॥

English
If a women wants holy water she shall drink the same with which her husband's feet have been washed. All holy rivers are present in that water. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भुञ्ज्यात्सा भर्तुरुच्छिष्टमिष्टमन्नादिकं च यत् ।
महाप्रसाद इत्युक्त्वा पतिदत्तं पतिव्रता ॥ २६॥

English
She shall partake of the leavings of her husband's food or whatever is given by him saying "This is thy great grace." (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अविभज्य न चाश्नीयाद्देवपित्रतिथिष्वपि ।
परिचारकवर्गेषु गोषु भिक्षुकुलेषु च ॥ २७॥

English
She shall never take food without first offering due share to the gods, the Pīṭṭs, the guests, the servants, cows and saintly mendicants. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
संयतोपस्करा दक्षा हृष्टा व्ययपराङ्मुखी ।
भवेत्सा सर्वदा देवी पतिव्रतपरायणा ॥ २८॥

English
A gentle lady of chaste rites shall always be clever to manage the household with limited requisites. She shall be averse to spend unnecessarily. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कुर्यात्पत्यननुज्ञाता नोपवासव्रतादिकम् ।
अन्यथा तत्फलं नास्ति परत्र नरकं व्रजेत् ॥ २९॥

English
Without being permitted by her husband she shall not observe fast and other rites. Should it be so, she will derive no benefit. She may fall into hell in other worlds. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखपूर्वं सुखासीनं रममाणं यदृच्छया ।
आन्तरेष्वपि कार्येषु पतिं नोत्थापयेत्क्वचित् ॥ ३०॥

English
While the husband is sportively engaged or seated comfortably she shall not worry him to get up under the pretext of attending to some household work. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
क्लीबं वा दुरवस्थं वा व्याधितं वृद्धमेव च ।
सुखितं दुःखितं वापि पतिमेकं न लङ्घयेत् ॥ ३१॥

English
Whether he is impotent, distressed, sick or senile, happy or unhappy, the husband shall never be transgressed. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीधर्मिणी त्रिरात्रं च स्वमुखं नैव दर्शयेत् ।
स्ववाक्यं श्रावयेन्नापि यावत्स्नानान्न शुध्यति ॥ ३२॥

English
During the three days of her monthly course she shall neither show her face nor speak to him. She shall not speak within his hearing till she becomes pure after her bath. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सुस्नाता भर्तृवदनमीक्षेतान्यस्य न क्वचित् ।
अथवा मनसि ध्यात्वा पतिं भानुं विलोकयेत् ॥ ३३॥

English
After her bath she shall see her husband's face and not that of anyone else. Or after thinking on her husband she shall then gaze at the sun. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिद्राकुङ्कुमं चैव सिन्दूरं कज्जलादिकम् ।
कूर्पासकं च ताम्बूलं माङ्गल्याभरणादिकम् ॥ ३४॥

केशसंस्कारकबरीकरकर्णादिभूषणम् ।
भर्तुरायुष्यमिच्छन्ती दूरयेन्न पतिव्रता ॥ ३५॥

English
If a chaste lady wishes for the longevity of her husband she shall not forsake turmeric, vermilion, saffron, collyrium, a blouse, the betel, the necklace, ornaments, brushing and plaiting the hair bangles and earrings. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
न रजक्या न बन्धक्या तथा श्रमणया न च ।
न च दुर्भगया क्वापि सखित्वं कारयेत् क्वचित् ॥ ३६॥

English
A chaste woman shall never associate intimately with a washerwoman, a harlot, a female ascetic or a fallen woman. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिविद्वेषिणीं नारीं न सा सम्भाषयेत् क्वचित् ।
नैकाकिनी क्वचित्तिष्ठेन्नग्ना स्नायान्न च क्वचित् ॥ ३७॥

English
She shall not talk to any woman who disparages or hates her husband. She shall not stand alone anywhere nor shall she take bath in the nude. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
नोलूखले न मुसले न वर्द्धन्यां दृषद्यपि ।
न यन्त्रके न देहल्यां सती च प्रवसेत् क्वचित् ॥ ३८॥

English
A chaste lady shall never sleep on a mortar threshing rod, a broom, a grinding stone, a machine or on the threshold. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
विना व्यवायसमयं प्रागल्भ्यं नाचरेत् क्वचित् ।
यत्र यत्र रुचिर्भर्तुस्तत्र प्रेमवती भवेत् ॥ ३९॥

English
Except at the time of sexual intercourse she shall never show her maturity and initiative. She shall like whatever her husband is interested in. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
हृष्टाहृष्टे विषण्णा स्याद्विषण्णास्ये प्रिये प्रिया ।
पतिव्रता भवेद्देवी सदा पतिहितैषिणी ॥ ४०॥

English
A chaste lady shall be delighted when her husband is delighted and dejected when he is dejected. She shall always wish for his benefit. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
एकरूपा भवेत्पुण्या सम्पत्सु च विपत्सु च ।
विकृतिं स्वात्मनः क्वापि न कुर्याद्धैर्यधारिणी ॥ ४१॥

English
She shall be virtuous and equanimous in affluence and adversity. She shall have fortitude and shall never go astray. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्पिर्लवणतैलादिक्षयेऽपि च पतिव्रता ।
पतिं नास्तीति न ब्रूयादायासेषु न योजयेत् ॥ ४२॥

English
Even when ghee, salt, oil or other things are exhausted she shall not tell her husband openly about it lest he should be subjected to undue strain. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
विधेर्विष्णोर्हराद्वापि पतिरेकोऽधिको मतः ।
पतिव्रताया देवेशि स्वपतिश्शिव एव च ॥ ४३॥

English
O Goddess, the husband is superior to Brahmā, Viṣṇu or Śiva, for a chaste lady her husband is on a par with Śiva. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
व्रतोपवासनियमं पतिमुल्लङ्घ्य याचरेत् ।
आयुष्यं हरते भर्तुर्मृता निरयमृच्छति ॥ ४४॥

English
She who transgresses her husband and observes fast and other rites wrecks the longevity of her husband and after death goes to hell. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
उक्ता प्रत्युत्तरं दद्याद्या नारी क्रोधतत्परा ।
सरमा जायते ग्रामे श‍ृगाली निर्जने वने ॥ ४५॥

English
If she furiously retorts to her husband she is born as a bitch in a village or as a vixen in a secluded place. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्चासनं न सेवेत न व्रजेद्दुष्टसन्निधौ ।
न च कातरवाक्यानि वदेन्नारी पतिं क्वचित् ॥ ४६॥

English
The chaste lady shall never take a higher seat never approach a defiled person, never speak to her husband in agitation. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अपवादं न च ब्रूयात्कलहं दूरतस्त्यजेत् ।
गुरूणां सन्निधौ क्वापि नोच्चैर्ब्रूयान्न वै हसेत् ॥ ४७॥

English
She should avoid slanderous words, shun quarrels and shall not speak aloud or laugh in the presence of elders. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
बाह्यादायान्तमालोक्य त्वरितान्नजलाशनैः ।
ताम्बूलैर्वसनैश्चापि पादसंवाहनादिभिः ॥ ४८॥

तथैव चाटुवचनैः खेदसन्नोदनैः परैः ।
या प्रियं प्रीणयेत्प्रीता त्रिलोकी प्रीणिता तया ॥ ४९॥

English
She who delights her husband delights all the worlds. When she sees her husband coming home she shall hasten to serve him food and water, hand him betel and change of garments, and serve him by massaging his feet. By pleasing words she shall fascinate him and dispel his gloom. (48-49.)

---

Sanskrit
मितं ददाति जनको मितं भ्राता मितं सुतः ।
अमितस्य हि दातारं भर्तारं पूजयेत्सदा ॥ ५०॥

English
What father gives is limited, what brother gives is limited and what the son gives is also limited. A chaste lady shall worship her husband who gives what has no limit. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
भर्ता देवो गुरुर्भर्ता धर्मतीर्थव्रतानि च ।
तस्मात्सर्वं परित्यज्य पतिमेकं समर्चयेत् ॥ ५१॥

English
To a wife the husband is god, preceptor, virtue, holy centre and sacred rite. She should cast off everything and adore him alone. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
या भर्तारं परित्यज्य रहश्चरति दुर्मतिः ।
उलूकी जायते क्रूरा वृक्षकोटरशायिनी ॥ ५२॥

English
She who forsakes her husband and secretly violates her fidelity is born as a she-owl of cruel nature wasting its days in the hollow of a tree. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ताडिता ताडितुं चेच्छेत्सा व्याघ्री वृषदंशिका ।
कटाक्षयति यान्यं वै केकराक्षी तु सा भवेत् ॥ ५३॥

English
If she desires to beat her husband in retaliation, she becomes a tiger or a wild cat. She who ogles at another man becomes squint-eyed. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
या भर्तारं परित्यज्य मिष्टमश्नाति केवलम् ।
ग्रामे वा सूकरी भूयाद्वल्गुर्वापि स्वविड्भुजा ॥ ५४॥

English
She who partakes of sweet dish denying the same to her husband becomes a pig in the village or a wild goat eating its own dung. (54.)

Sanskrit
या तुं कृत्य प्रियं ब्रूयान् मूका सा जायते खलु ।
या सपत्नीं सदेर्ष्येत दुर्भगा सा पुनः पुनः ॥ ५५॥

English
She who addresses her husband in singular becomes dumb. She who is jealous of a co-wife becomes ill-fated in matrimony again and again. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्टिं विलुप्य भर्त्तुर्या कश्चिदन्यं समीक्षते ।
काणा च विमुखी चापि कुरूपापि च जायते ॥ ५६॥

English
She who casts glance on another person hiding it from her husband becomes one-eyed, twisted-faced or ugly. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवहीनो यथा देहः क्षणादशुचितां व्रजेत् ।
भर्तृहीना तथा योषित्सुस्नाताप्यशुचिः सदा ॥ ५७॥

English
Just as a body bereft of the soul becomes unclean in a moment, similarly a woman without a husband is always unclean even though she may take a neat bath. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
सा धन्या जननी लोके स धन्यो जनकः पिता ।
धन्यःस च पतिर्यस्य गृहे देवी पतिव्रता ॥ ५८॥

English
The mother, the father and the husband are blessed if there is a chaste lady in the house. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृवंश्याः मातृवंश्याः पतिवंश्यास्त्रयस्त्रयः ।
पतिव्रतायाः पुण्येन स्वर्गे सौख्यानि भुञ्जते ॥ ५९॥

English
The three families—that of the father, that of the mother and that of the husband—enjoy the pleasures of heaven due to the merit of the chaste woman. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
शीलभङ्गेन दुर्वृत्ताः पातयन्ति कुलत्रयम् ।
पितुर्मातुस्तथा पत्युरिहामुत्रापि दुःखिता ॥ ६०॥

English
Disloyal women cause the downfall of the three families, that of the father, mother and husband and become distressed here and hereafter. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिव्रतायाश्चरणो यत्र यत्र स्पृशेद्भुवम् ।
तत्र तत्र भवेत्सा हि पापहन्त्री सुपावनी ॥ ६१॥

English
Wherever the chaste lady sets her foot, the sin is dispelled therefrom and the place is sanctified. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
विभुः पतिव्रतास्पर्शं कुरुते भानुमानपि ।
सोमो गन्धवहश्चापि स्वपावित्र्याय नान्यथा ॥ ६२॥

English
Even the sun, moon and wind touch the chaste woman to sanctify themselves and not otherwise. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
आपः पतिव्रतास्पर्शमभिलष्यन्ति सर्वदा ।
अद्य जाड्यविनाशो नो जातस्त्वद्यान्यपावनाः ॥ ६३॥

English
Waters desire the touch of the chaste lady thinking—"Now our sluggishness is gone. Now we are able to purify others". (63.)

---

Sanskrit
भार्या मूलं गृहस्थस्य भार्या मूलं सुखस्य च ।
भार्या धर्मफलावाप्त्यै भार्या सन्तानवृद्धये ॥ ६४॥

English
Wife is the root of the household, and of its happiness; she is the source of the fruit of virtue and for the flourishing of the family. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहे गृहे न किं नार्यो रूपलावण्यगर्विताः ।
परं विश्वेशभक्त्यैव लभ्यते स्त्री पतिव्रता ॥ ६५॥

English
In every house there are women proud of their exquisite beauty and comely appearance. But it is only due to the devotion of Śiva that a chaste lady is obtained. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
परलोकस्त्वयं लोको जीयते भार्यया द्वयम् ।
देवपित्रतिथीज्यादि नाभार्यः कर्म चार्हति ॥ ६६॥

English
The present and the next world can be won through her. A wifeless man is not authorized to perform the rites of gods, Pitṛs guests and sacrifices. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहस्थः स हि विज्ञेयो यस्य गेहे पतिव्रता ।
ग्रस्यतेऽन्यान्प्रतिदिनं राक्षस्या जरया यथा ॥ ६७॥

English
He alone is the true householder in whose house there is a chaste lady. The others are devoured by an ogress or old age. (67.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा गङ्गावगाहेन शरीरं पावनं भवेत् ।
तथा पतिव्रतां दृष्ट्वा सकलं पावनं भवेत् ॥ ६८॥

English
Just as the body is purified by a plunge in the Gaṅgā, so everything is sanctified on seeing a chaste woman. (68.)

---

Sanskrit
न गङ्गाया तया भेदो या नारी पतिदेवता ।
उमाशिवसमौ साक्षात्तस्मात्तौ पूजयेद्बुधः ॥ ६९॥

English
A chaste lady is not different from Gaṅgā. She and her husband are like Pārvatī and Śiva. Hence a sensible man shall worship them. (69.)

---

Sanskrit
तारः पतिः श्रुतिर्नारी क्षमा सा स स्वयं तपः ।
फलं पतिः सत्क्रिया सा धन्यौ तौ दम्पती शिवे ॥ ७०॥

English
The husband is the high tone and the wife is the quarter tone. The husband is austerity and the woman is forbearance. The husband is the fruit and the wife is a sacred rite. O Pārvatī, such a pair is blessed. (70.)

---

Sanskrit

तद्भेदान् श‍ृणु सुप्रीत्या सावधानतयाद्य मे ॥ ७१॥

English
O daughter of the lord of mountains, thus the duty of a chaste wife is described to you. Now, listen to their classifications with attention and love. (71.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O gentle lady, the chaste ladies can be divided into four classes. Even when they are remembered they dispel sins. The divisions comprise of the superior etc. (72.)

---

Sanskrit

English
They are superior, middling, inferior and very inferior. I shall explain their characteristics. Listen with attention. (73.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O gentle lady, she whose mind is not aware of any one else and who is conscious of her husband even in her dreams is the noblest of all. (74.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O daughter of the mountain, she who sees another man as her father, brother or son with a clean conscience is the middling among chaste ladies. (75.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Pārvatī, she who ponders over her duty mentally and desists from going astray is inferior among the chaste. Of course she is pure in conduct. (76.)

---

Sanskrit

English
She who remains chaste for fear of her husband or the family is very inferior among the chaste ladies, so say the ancient poets. (77.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Pārvatī, these four types of chaste ladies dispel sins. They sanctify all the worlds. They are delighted here and hereafter. (78.)

---

Sanskrit

English

English
