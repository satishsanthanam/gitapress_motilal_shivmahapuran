### **CHAPTER FIFTYFIVE - (*Śiva returns to Kailāsa*)**

---

Sanskrit
५५

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अथ सा ब्राह्मणी देव्यै शिक्षयित्वा व्रतं च तत् ।
प्रोवाच मेनामामन्त्र्य यात्रामस्याश्च कारय ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Thus instructing the goddess in the rites of a chaste lady, the brahmin lady told Menā while taking leave of her "Make arrangements for her journey". (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तथास्त्विति च सम्प्रोच्य प्रेमवश्या बभूव सा ।
धृतिं धृत्वाहूय कालीं विश्लेषविरहाकुला ॥ २॥

English
Saying "So be it" she became exasperated by her affection. Controlling herself a little she called Pārvatī to her when her agitation due to imminent separation became all the more unbearable. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्युच्चै रोदनं चक्रे संश्लिष्य च पुनः पुनः ।
पार्वत्यपि रुरोदोच्चैरुच्चरन्ती कृपावचः ॥ ३॥

English
Embracing her she cried loudly and frequently. Pārvatī too cried uttering piteous words. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलप्रिया शिवा चापि मूर्च्छामाप शुचार्दिता ।
मूर्च्छाम्प्रापुर्देवपत्न्यः पार्वत्या रोदनेन च ॥ ४॥

English
The beloved of the mountain as well as her daughter became unconscious due to grief. The wives of the gods too fainted on hearing Pārvatī cry. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाश्च रुरुदुर्नार्यः सर्वमासीदचेतनम् ।
स्वयं रुरोद योगीशो गच्छन्कोऽन्य परः प्रभुः ॥ ५॥

English
All the ladies cried. Everything became senseless. Who else, even the great lord, the leader of Yogins, cried at the time of departure. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शीघ्रमाजगाम हिमालयः ।
स सर्वतनयैस्तत्र सचिवैश्च द्विजैः परैः ॥ ६॥

English
In the meantime, Himavat came hurriedly along with his sons, ministers and brahmins. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयं रुरोद मोहेन वत्सां कृत्वा स्ववक्षसि ।
क्व यासीत्येवमुच्चार्य शून्यं कृत्वा मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ७॥

English
Holding his dear daughter to his bosom and saying "Where are you going?" with frequent vague vacant glances, he cried due to his fascination. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पुरोहितो विप्रैरध्यात्मविद्यया सुखम् ।
सर्वान्प्रबोधयामास कृपया ज्ञानवत्तरः ॥ ८॥

English
Then the chief priest in the company of other brahmins enlightened everyone. The wise priest by his spiritual discourse was able to convince them easily. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ननाम पार्वती भक्त्या मातरं पितरं गुरुम् ।
महामाया भवाचाराद्रुरोदोच्चैर्मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ९॥

English
With great devotion Pārvatī bowed to her parents and the preceptor. Following the worldly convention she cried aloud frequently. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्या रोदनेनैव रुरुदुः सर्वयोषितः ।
नितरां जननी मेना यामयो भ्रातरस्तथा ॥ १०॥

English
When Pārvatī cried the ladies cried too, particularly the mother Menā, sisters and brothers. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः पुनः शिवामाता यामयोऽन्याश्च योषितः ।
भ्रातरो जनकः प्रेम्णा रुरुदुर्बद्धसौहृदाः ॥ ११॥

English
Her mother, sister, brothers, father and the other ladies who were affectionately attached to her cried frequently. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा विप्राः समागत्य बोधयामासुरादरात् ।
लग्नं निवेदयामासुर्यात्रायाः सुखदं परम् ॥ १२॥

English
Then the brahmins respectfully intimated to them the auspicious hour for the starting of the journey and consoled them. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हिमालयो मेनां धृत्वा धैर्यं विवेकतः ।
शिबिकामानयामास शिवारोहणहेतवे ॥ १३॥

English
Then Himavat and Menā composed themselves and caused the palanquin to be brought for Pārvatī to get in. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवामारोहयामासुस्तत्र विप्राङ्गनाश्च ताम् ।
आशिषं प्रददुः सर्वाः पिता माता द्विजास्तथा ॥ १४॥

English
The brahmin ladies helped her to get into the palanquin. They gave their blessings. Her parents and the brahmins too offered their blessings. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
महाराज्ञ्युपचाराँश्च ददौ मेना गिरिस्तथा ।
नानाद्रव्यसमूहं च परेषां दुर्लभं शुभम् ॥ १५॥

English
Menā and the lord of mountains gave her a royal send-off with various auspicious rare presents not accessible to common people. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवा नत्वा गुरून्सर्वान् जनकं जननीं तथा ।
द्विजान्पुरोहितं यामीस्त्रीस्तथान्या ययौ मुने ॥ १६॥

English
O sage, Pārvatī started after bowing to the preceptors, elders, father, mother, the brahmins, the chief priest, sisters and the other women. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमाचलोऽपि ससुतोऽगच्छत्स्नेहवशी बुधः ।
प्राप्तस्तत्र प्रभुर्यत्र सामरः प्रीतिमावहन् ॥ १७॥

English
Himavat, the sensible affectionate father with his sons accompanied her as far as the place where the lord was waiting joyously along with the gods. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रीत्याभिरेभिरे सर्वे महोत्सवपुरस्सरम् ।
प्रभुं प्रणेमुस्ते भक्त्या प्रशंसन्तोऽविशन्पुरीम् ॥ १८॥

English
Everyone was jubilant and jolly with love. They bowed to the lord with devotion. Praising Him they returned to Kailāsa. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
जातिस्मरां स्मारयामि नित्यं स्मरसि चेद्वद ।
लीलया त्वां च देवेशि सदा प्राणप्रिया मम ॥ १९॥

English
Then Śiva told Pārvatī—"I am reminding you although you know the previous birth. If you remember, speak out. In my divine sport you are always my beloved." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य महेशस्य स्वनाथस्याथ पार्वती ।
शङ्करस्य प्रिया नित्यं सस्मितोवाच सा सती ॥ २०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of Śiva, Satī Pārvatī the beloved of Śiva replied smiling. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
सर्वं स्मरामि प्राणेश मौनीभूतो भवेति च ।
प्रस्तावोचितमद्याशु कार्यं कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २१॥

English
*Pārvatī said:—*  O dear lord, I remember everything as well as the fact that you became a silent ascetic. Obeisance to you. Please do everything necessary now befitting the occasion. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य प्रियावाक्यं सुधाधाराशतोपमम् ।
मुमुदेऽतीव विश्वेशो लौकिकाचारतत्परः ॥ २२॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing her words as pleasing as the steady flow of nectar, Śiva rejoiced much, eagerly devoted to the way of the world. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः सम्भृतसम्भारो नानावस्तुमनोहरम् ।
भोजयामास देवांश्च नारायणपुरोगमान् ॥ २३॥

English
Getting every requisite thing ready, he fed the gods including Viṣṇu and others with various pleasant things. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तथान्यान्निखिलान्प्रीत्या स्वविवाहसमागतान् ।
भोजयामास सुरसमन्नं बहुविधं प्रभुः ॥ २४॥

English
He fed all the others who had attended His marriage with juicy cooked food of various sorts. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो भुक्त्वा च ते देवा नानारत्नविभूषिताः ।
सस्त्रीकाः सगणाः सर्वे प्रणेमुश्चन्द्रशेखरम् ॥ २५॥

English
After taking food the gods and the Gaṇas, with their womenfolk fully bedecked in gems and jewels bowed to the moon-crested lord. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
संस्तुत्य वाग्भिरिष्टाभिः परिक्रम्य मुदान्विताः ।
प्रशंसन्तो विवाहं च स्वधामानि ययुस्ततः ॥ २६॥

English
After eulogising Him with pleasing words and circumambulating Him with joy they praised the marriage celebration and returned to their abodes. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणं मुने मां च प्रणनाम शिवःस्वयम् ।
लौकिकाचारमाश्रित्य यथा विष्णुश्च कश्यपम् ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, Śiva Himself bowed to me and to Viṣṇu following the worldly convention as Viṣṇu had bowed to Kaśyapa. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मयाश्लिष्याशिषं दत्त्वा शिवस्य पुनरग्रतः ।
मत्वा वै तं परं ब्रह्म चक्रे च स्तुतिरुत्तमा ॥ २८॥

English
Considering Him the supreme Brahman I eulogised him in the excellent manner after embracing him and offering him my benediction. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तमामन्त्र्य मया विष्णुः साञ्जलिः शिवयोर्मुदा ।
प्रशंसंस्तद्विवाहं च जगाम स्वालयं परम् ॥ २९॥

English
Viṣṇu and I with palms joined in reverence, took leave of them and praising the marriage of Śiva and Pārvatī went back to Viṣṇu's abode. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि स्वगिरौ तस्थौ पार्वत्या विहरन् मुदा ।
सर्वे गणाः सुखं प्रापुरतीव स्वभजन् शिवौ ॥ ३०॥

English
On the mountain, Śiva stayed with Pārvatī and continued his divine sports with joy. The Gaṇas too were happy and they worshipped the married couple. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं कथितस्तात शिवोद्वाहः सुमङ्गलः ।
शोकघ्नो हर्षजनक आयुष्यो धनवर्द्धनः ॥ ३१॥

English
O dear, I have thus narrated the auspicious story of the marriage of Śiva, that dispels sorrow, generates delight and increases wealth and longevity. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
य इमं श‍ृणुयान्नित्यं शुचिस्तद्गतमानसः ।
श्रावयेद्वाथ नियमाच्छिवलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥ ३२॥

English
He who hears this story with pure mind fixed on them or narrates the same, shall attain Śivaloka. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमाख्यातमद्भुतं मङ्गलायनम् ।
सर्वविघ्नप्रशमनं सर्वव्याधिविनाशनम् ॥ ३३॥

English
This narrative is said to be wondrous and the cause of everything auspicious. It quells all hindrances and ailments. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यशस्यं स्वर्ग्यमायुष्यं पुत्रपौत्रकरं परम् ।
सर्वकामप्रदं चेह भुक्तिदं मुक्तिदं सदा ॥ ३४॥

English
It is conducive to glory and the attainment of heaven. It bestows longevity, sons and grandsons, all cherished desires, worldly pleasures and salvation too. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अपमृत्युप्रशमनं महाशान्तिकरं शुभम् ।
सर्वदुःस्वप्नशमनं बुद्धिप्रज्ञादिसाधनम् ॥ ३५॥

English
It wards off premature death. It is auspicious and it causes peace. It makes bad dreams subside. It is an instrument for the acquisition of keen intellect. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोत्सवेषु सर्वेषु पठितव्यं प्रयत्नतः ।
शुभेप्सुभिर्जनैः प्रीत्या शिवसन्तोषकारणम् ॥ ३६॥

English
It shall be read on all occasions of Siva's festivals by the people who desire auspicious results. It gives satisfaction to Siva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पठेत्प्रतिष्ठाकाले तु देवादीनां विशेषतः ।
शिवस्य सर्वकार्यस्य प्रारम्भे च सुप्रीतितः ॥ ३७॥

English
At the installation of the idols of the deities this shall be particularly read. At the beginning of all auspicious rites it shall be read with pleasure. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणुयाद्वा शुचिर्भूत्वा चरितं शिवयोश्शिवम् ।
सिध्यन्ति सर्वकार्याणि सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ३८॥

English
With purity in mind and body it shall be heard. All affairs become fruitful thereby. This is true, really true. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे ब्रह्मनारदसंवादे द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां तृतीये पार्वतीखण्डे शिवकैलासगमनवर्णनं नाम पञ्चपञ्चाशत्तमोऽध्यायः ॥ २.३.५५॥

॥ इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे द्वितीयरुद्रसंहितायां तृतीयः पार्वतीखण्डः समाप्तः ॥ २.३॥
