### **CHAPTER ONE - *(The dalliance of Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
१

वन्दे वन्दनतुष्टमानसमतिप्रेमप्रियं प्रेमदं
पूर्णं पूर्णकरं प्रपूर्णनिखिलैश्वर्यैकवासं शिवम् ।
सत्यं सत्यमयं त्रिसत्यविभवं सत्यप्रियं सत्यदं
विष्णुब्रह्मनुतं स्वकीयकृपयोपात्ताकृतिं शङ्करम् ॥ १॥

English
I salute Śiva who is satisfied with salutation, who loves great devotion, who bestows affection, who makes others too perfect and complete and who is the abode of all fortune and achievements. I salute Śiva who is eulogised by Viṣṇu and Brahmā, who urged by His sympathy assumes visible forms, who bestows truth, who loves truth, whose assets are the threefold truths and who is identical with truth. (1.)

Sanskrit

English
*Nārada said :—*  O Brahmā, after marrying Pārvatī and returning to His mountain what did Śiva the benefactor of the worlds do? Please narrate it to me. (2.)

Sanskrit

Who was the son born to Śiva, the great soul, for which purpose the lord, though He rests and revels in Himself, married Pārvatī? (3.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

गुहजन्मकथां दिव्यां तारकासुरसद्वधम् ॥ ६॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O Nārada, listen to the tale of Śiva, the moon-crested lord. I shall narrate the story of Guha's¹²⁶ birth and the slaying of the demon Tāraka. (6.)

[fn 126. Guha, better known as Kārttikeya was the son of Śiva. Born in a thicket in a forest, out of the semen of Śiva, without the intervention of Pārvatī he was fostered by the Pleiades (Kṛttikās) whence he received the title Kāṭtikeya. When grown into youth, he became the Commander-in-Chief (senāpatī) of the army of Śiva, fought and slew the Daitya Tāraka. As he killed (māra) the evil (ku) Asura, he became known as kumāra.]

English
Making a wonderful bed conducive to good sexual pleasure, rendered smooth and fragrant with flowers and sandal paste and auspiciously supplemented with objects of enjoyment, lord 'Śiva' the bestower of honour, indulged in dalliance with Pārvatī for a thousand years of god.¹²⁷ (14-15.)

[fn 127. For different calculations of the time-durations see Sk. P. Ch. 142-145.]]
---

Sanskrit

English
In that divine sport at the mere contact with Pārvatī, Śiva lapsed in unconsciousness. She too lapsed into unconsciousness due to the contact with Śiva. She neither knew the day nor the night. (16.)

इत्युक्त्वा सकला देवा मया सह मुनीश्वर ।
दीनास्तस्थुः पुरो विष्णोर्मौनीभूताः सुदुःखिताः ॥ ४९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying this, O great sage, all the depressed gods, stood silent along with me in front of Viṣṇu with great misery. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य समादाय सुरान्नः सकलान् हरिः ।
जगाम पर्वतं शीघ्रं कैलासं शिववल्लभम् ॥ ५०॥

English
On hearing those words, Viṣṇu took us all immediately to the mountain Kailāsa, the favourite haunt of Śiva. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा हरिर्देवैर्मया च सुरवल्लभः ।
ययौ शिववरस्थानं शङ्करं द्रष्टुकाम्यया ॥ ५१॥

English
After going there in the company of the gods and me, the favourite deity of the gods went to the excellent resort of Śiva with a desire to see Śiva. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र दृष्ट्वा शिवं विष्णुर्न सुरैर्विस्मितोऽभवत् ।
तत्र स्थितान् शिवगणान् पप्रच्छ विनयान्वितः ॥ ५२॥

English
Unable to see Him there, Viṣṇu and the gods became surprised. With humility he asked the Gaṇas of Śiva who were there. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
हे शङ्कराः शिवः कुत्र गतः सर्वप्रभुर्गणाः ।
निवेदयत नः प्रीत्या दुःखितान्वै कृपालवः ॥ ५३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—*  O Gaṇas of Śiva, where has Śiva, the lord of all gone? Sympathetically intimate this to us who are depressed. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य सामरस्य हरेर्गणाः ।
प्रोचुः प्रीत्या गणास्ते हि शङ्करस्य रमापतिम् ॥ ५४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of Viṣṇu in the company of the gods, the Gaṇas of Śiva lovingly replied to Viṣṇu. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणा ऊचुः ।
हरे श‍ृणु शिवप्रीत्या यथार्थं ब्रूमहे वयम् ।
ब्रह्मणा निर्जरैः सार्द्धं वृत्तान्तमखिलं च यत् ॥ ५५॥

English
*The Gaṇas of Śiva said:—*  O Viṣṇu, please listen along with Brahmā and the gods, we shall tell you the truth and the details out of love for Śiva. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेश्वरो महादेवो जगाम गिरिजालयम् ।
संस्थाप्य नोऽत्र सुप्रीत्या नानालीलाविशारदः ॥ ५६॥

English
Śiva, the lord of all, had gone into the apartment of Pārvatī after stationing us here with love. He is an expert in indulging in divine sports. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्गुहाभ्यन्तरे शम्भुः किं करोति महेश्वरः ।
न जानीमो रमानाथ व्यतीयुर्बहवः समाः ॥ ५७॥

English
O lord of Lakṣmī, many years have gone by. We do not know what Śiva, the great lord, is doing within her apartment. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श्रुत्वेति वचनं तेषां स विष्णुः सामरो मया ।
विस्मितोऽति मुनिश्रेष्ठ शिवद्वारं जगाम ह ॥ ५८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing their words, O excellent sage, Viṣṇu, the gods and I were perplexed and went to the doorway of Śiva's apartment. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा मया देवैः स हरिर्देववल्लभः ।
आर्तवाण्या मुने प्रोचे तारस्वरतया तदा ॥ ५९॥

English
After going there along with me and the gods, Viṣṇu, the favourite deity of the gods, spoke in dejection but with joy in the heart. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुमस्तौन्महाप्रीत्या सामरो हि मया हरिः ।
तत्र स्थितो मुनिश्रेष्ठ सर्वलोकप्रभुं हरम् ॥ ६०॥

English
O excellent sage, standing there, along with me and the gods, he eulogised Śiva, the lord of all the worlds with great pleasure. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
किं करोषि महादेवाभ्यन्तरे परमेश्वर ।
तारकार्तान्सुरान्सर्वान् पाहि नः शरणागतान् ॥ ६१॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—*  O great lord, what are you doing there inside? Save us who are harassed by Tāraka and who have sought refuge in you. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यादि संस्तुवन् शम्भुं बहुधा सोऽमरैर्मया ।
रुरोदाति हरिस्तत्र तारकार्तैर्मुनीश्वर ॥ ६२॥

English
O great sage, praising and pleading like this to Śiva, Viṣṇu wept bitterly along with the gods harassed by Tāraka. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःखकोलाहलस्तत्र बभूव त्रिदिवौकसाम् ।
मिश्रितश्शिव संस्तुत्यासुरार्त्तानां मुनीश्वर ॥ ६३॥

English
O great sage, the tumultuous cry of the heaven-dwellers distressed by the demon got mingled with the sound of eulogy to Śiva. (63.)
