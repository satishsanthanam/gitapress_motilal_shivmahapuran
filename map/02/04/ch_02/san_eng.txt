### **CHAPTER TWO - *(The birth of Śiva's son)***

---

Sanskrit
२

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य महादेवो योगज्ञानविशारदः ।
त्यक्तकामो न तत्याज सम्भोगं पार्वतीभयात् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing that, the great lord, an expert in Yogic theory, though free from lust, did not emit the semen, fearing to offend Pārvatī. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
आजगाम गृहद्वारि सुराणां निकटं शिवः ।
दैत्येन पीडितानां च शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २॥

English
He came to the door, near the gods distressed by the demon. Śiva is the benefactor favourably disposed to His devotees. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाःसर्वे प्रभुं दृष्ट्वा हरिणा च मया शिवम् ।
बभूबुः सुखिनश्चाति तदा वै भक्तवत्सलम् ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing lord Śiva, favourably disposed to His devotees, the gods including me and Viṣṇu became extremely happy. (3.)

Sanskrit

English
O sage, bowing down with stooping shoulders the gods along with me and Viṣṇu eulogised Śiva with great pleasure. (4.)

---

Sanskrit

तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं सर्वे मया च हरिणा मुने ॥ ५॥

English
*The gods said:—*  "O great God, O lord of gods, O ocean of mercy, O Śiva, you are the immanent soul of all. You know everything. (5.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O lord, carry out the task of the gods. O great lord, save the gods. Slay Tāraka and other demons and take pity on us." (6.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On hearing these words of the gods, lord Śiva agitated in soul and dispirited in the mind, replied. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।

यद्भावि तद्भवत्येव कोऽपि नो तन्निवारकः ॥ ८॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  "O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O gods, you are the goal of everybody's mind. What should happen necessarily must happen. There is none to stop it. (8.)

---

Sanskrit

शिवरेतस्खलितं वीर्यं को ग्रहीष्यति मेऽधुना ॥ ९॥

स गृह्णीयादिति प्रोच्य पातयामास तद्भुवि ।
अग्निर्भूत्वा कपोतो हि प्रेरितःसर्वनिर्जरैः ॥ १०॥

अभक्षच्छाम्भवं वीर्यं चञ्च्वा तु निखिलं तदा ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्राजगाम गिरिजा मुने ॥ ११॥

English
What has happened has already happened. Now O gods, listen to what is relevant to the context. Let him who will, take up this discharged semen". After saying this He let it fall on the ground. Urged by the gods Agni became a dove and swallowed it with his beak. O sage, in the meantime Pārvatī came there. (9-11.)

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Cursing Viṣṇu and other gods, Pārvatī furiously told Agni who had swallowed Śiva’s semen. (19.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

*Nārada said:—*  “O Agni, listen to my words that will dispel your burning sensation. It will yield great pleasure and ward off your pains. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वोपायमिमं वह्ने सुखी भव विदाहकः ।
शिवेच्छया मया सम्यगुक्तं तातेदमादरात् ॥ ५३॥

English
O Agni, taking recourse to the following expedient you will be relieved of the burning sensation and be happy. O dear, this has been explained by me well at the will of Śiva. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
तपोमासस्नानकर्त्र्यः स्त्रियो याःस्युः प्रगे शुचे ।
तद्देहेषु स्थापय त्वं शिवरेतस्त्विदं महत् ॥ ५४॥

English
O Agni, you shall deposit this semen of Śiva in the bodies of the ladies who take their morning baths in the month of Māgha.” (54.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तस्मिन्नवसरे तत्रागताः सप्तमुनिस्त्रियः ।
तपोमासि स्नानकामाः प्रातः सन्नियमा मुने ॥ ५५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, meanwhile the wives of the seven celestial sages came there desirous of taking their early morning bath in the month of Māgha with other observances of rites. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नानं कृत्वा स्त्रियस्ता हि महाशीतार्द्दिताश्च षट् ।
गन्तुकामा मुने याता वह्निज्वालासमीपतः ॥ ५६॥

English
After the bath, six of them were distressed by the chillness and were desirous of going near the flame of fire. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
विमोहिताश्च ता दृष्ट्वारुन्धती गिरिशाज्ञया ।
निषिषेध विशेषेण सुचरित्रा सुबोधिनी ॥ ५७॥

English
Arundhati of good conduct and perfect knowledge saw them deluded and dissuaded them at the behest of Śiva. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
ताः षड् मुनिस्त्रियो मोहाद्धठात्तत्र गता मुने ।
स्वशीतविनिवृत्त्यर्थं मोहिताः शिवमायया ॥ ५८॥

English
O sage, the six ladies stubbornly insisted on going there to ward off their chillness because they were deluded by Śiva’s magical art. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रेतःकणिकाः सद्यस्तद्देहान् विविशुर्मुने ।
रोमद्वाराऽखिला वह्निरभूद्दाहविवर्जितः ॥ ५९॥

English
Immediately the particles of the semen entered their bodies through the pores of hairs, O sage. The fire was relieved of their burning sensation. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्धाय द्रुतं वह्निर्ज्वालारूपो जगाम ह ।
सुखी स्वलोकं मनसा स्मरंस्त्वां शङ्करं च तम् ॥ ६०॥

English
Vanishing immediately from the scene, Agni in the form of a flame, went back happily to his region, mentally remembering you and Śiva. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
सगर्भास्ताः स्त्रियः साधोऽभवन् दाहप्रपीडिताः ।
जग्मुःस्वभवनं तातारुन्धती दुःखिताग्निना ॥ ६१॥

English
O saintly one, the women became pregnant and were distressed by the burning sensation. They went home. O dear, Arundhati was displeased with fire. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा स्वस्त्रीगतिं तात नाथाः क्रोधाकुला द्रुतम् ।
तत्यजुस्ताः स्त्रियस्तात सुसम्मन्त्र्य परस्परम् ॥ ६२॥

English
O dear, the husbands on seeing the plight of their wives became furious. They consulted one another and discarded them. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ताः षट् स्त्रियःसर्वा दृष्ट्वा स्वव्यभिचारकम् ।
महादुःखान्वितास्ताताभवन्नाकुलमानसाः ॥ ६३॥

English
O dear, on seeing their own state the six ladies felt very miserable and distressed. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्यजुश्शिवरेतस्तद्गर्भरूपं मुनिस्त्रियः ।
ता हिमाचलपृष्ठेऽथाभवन् दाहविवर्जिताः ॥ ६४॥

English
The wives of the sages cast off their semen in the form of a foetus at the top of Himavat. They felt then relieved of their burning sensation. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
असहन् शिवरेतस्तद्धिमाद्रिः कम्पमुद्वहन् ।
गङ्गायां प्राक्षिपत्तूर्णमसह्यं दाहपीडितः ॥ ६५॥

English
Unable to bear that semen of Śiva and trembling much, Himavat became scorched by it and hurled it in the Gaṅgā. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गयाऽपि च तद्वीर्यं दुस्सहं परमात्मनः ।
निःक्षिप्तं हि शरस्तम्बे तरङ्गैः स्वैर्मुनीश्वर ॥ ६६॥

English
O great sage, the intolerable semen of lord Śiva was deposited by Gaṅgā in the forest of Śara grass by means of its waves. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
पतितं तत्र तद्रेतो द्रुतं बालो बभूव ह ।
सुन्दरः सुभगः श्रीमांस्तेजस्वी प्रीतिवर्द्धनः ॥ ६७॥

English
The semen that fell was turned in a handsome good-featured boy, full of glory and splendour. He increased everyone's pleasure. (67.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्गमासे सिते पक्षे तिथौ षष्ठ्यां मुनीश्वर ।
प्रादुर्भावोऽभवत्तस्य शिवपुत्रस्य भूतले ॥ ६८॥

English
O great sage, on the sixth day of the bright half of the lunar month of Mārgaśīrṣa, the son of Śiva was born in the world. (68.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे ब्रह्मन्नकस्माद्धिमशैलजा ।

English
At that time, O Brahmin, on their mountain, Pārvatī the daughter of Himavat and Śiva became very happy. (69.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Out of joy, milk exuded from the breasts of Pārvatī. On reaching the spot everyone felt very happy. (70.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O dear, there was auspiciousness in the three worlds, pleasing to the good. There occurred obstacles to the wicked and particularly to the demons. (71.)

English
