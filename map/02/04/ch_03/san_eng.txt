### **CHAPTER THREE - *(The boyhood sports of Kārttikeya)***

---

Sanskrit
३

नारद उवाच ।
देवदेव प्रजानाथ ब्रह्मन् सृष्टिकर प्रभो ।
ततः किमभवत्तत्र तद्वदाऽद्य कृपां कुरु ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  O lord of subjects, O Brahmin, O creator, what happened thereafter ? Please tell me the same. (1.)

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।

प्रेरितो विधिना तत्रागच्छत्प्रीतो यदृच्छया ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O dear, then the powerful sage Viśvāmitra, urged by Brahmā, came there casually and was delighted. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स दृष्ट्वालौकिकं धाम तत्सुतस्य सुतेजसः ।
अभवत्पूर्णकामस्तु सुप्रसन्नो ननाम च ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the unearthly splendour of that brilliant boy, he became very delighted. He bowed to the boy. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अकरोत्सुनुतिं तस्य सुप्रसन्नेन चेतसा ।
विधिप्रेरितवाग्भिश्च विश्वामित्रः प्रभाववित् ॥ ४॥

English
With a delighted mind he eulogised him with the words prompted by Brahmā. Viśvāmitra realised his power. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःसोऽभूत्सुतस्तत्र सुप्रसन्नो महोतिकृत् ।
सुप्रहस्याद्भुतमहो विश्वामित्रमुवाच च ॥ ५॥

English
The boy too was delighted and became the source of great enjoyment. Laughingly he spoke to Viśvāmitra. It was very surprising. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवसुत उवाच ।
शिवेच्छया महाज्ञानिन्नकस्मात्त्वमिहागतः ।
संस्कारं कुरु मे तात यथावद्वेदसम्मितम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Śiva's son said :—*  "O great one of perfect wisdom, it is due to the will of Śiva that you have come here by chance. O dear, perform my purificatory rites in accordance with Vedic injunctions. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यारभ्य पुरोधास्त्वं भव मे प्रीतिमावहन् ।
भविष्यसि सदा पूज्यः सर्वेषां नात्र संशयः ॥ ७॥

English
From now onwards you remain my priest conferring your love on me. It is certain that you will become the object of worship of all." (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य सुप्रसन्नो हि गाधिजः ।
तमुवाचानुदात्तेन स्वरेण च सुविस्मितः ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing his words, Gādhi's son¹³⁵ (Viśvāmitra) was highly delighted and surprised. He spoke to him in a tone, by no means highly accented. (8.)

[fn ¹³⁵. Viśvāmitra, the son of Gādhi or Gāthin, was a born Kṣatriya who by intense austerities attained Brāhmaṇahood. ŚP. attributes this change to the favour of Kumāra who raised him to Brāhmaṇahood in order to enable him to perform his purificatory rites.]
---

Sanskrit
विश्वामित्र उवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात न विप्रोऽहं गाधिक्षत्रियबालकः ।
विश्वामित्रेति विख्यातः क्षत्रियो विप्रसेवकः ॥ ९॥

English
*Viśvāmitra said :—*  Listen, O dear, I am not a brahmin. I am a Kṣatriya, son of Gādhi, famous as Viśvāmitra and a servant of brahmins. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इति स्वचरितं ख्यातं मया ते वरबालक ।
कस्त्वं स्वचरितं ब्रूहि विस्मितायाखिलं हि मे ॥ १०॥

English
O excellent boy, I have thus narrated my life to you. Who are you ? Now mention everything about your life to me who am surprised. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य तत्स्ववृत्तं जगाद ह ।
ततश्चोवाच सुप्रीत्या गाधिजं तं महोतिकृत् ॥ ११॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing his words the boy told him about his life. The divine boy, the cause of great enjoyment and protection, said to Gādhi's son with great pleasure. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवसुत उवाच ।
विश्वामित्र वरान्मे त्वं ब्रह्मर्षिर्नात्र संशयः ।
वसिष्ठाद्याश्च नित्यं त्वां प्रशंसिष्यन्ति चादरात् ॥ १२॥

English
*Śiva's son said :—*  O Viśvāmitra, thanks to my favour, you now become a brahminical sage. Vasiṣṭha and others will for ever regard you with respect. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अतस्त्वमाज्ञया मे हि संस्कारं कर्तुमर्हसि ।
इदं सर्वं सुगोप्यं ते कथनीयं न कुत्रचित् ॥ १३॥

English
Hence, at my behest you shall perform my purificatory rites. Keep this as a great secret. You shall not mention it anywhere. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ततोऽकार्षीत्स संस्कारं तस्य प्रीत्याऽखिलं यथा ।
शिवबालस्य देवर्षे वेदोक्तविधिना परम् ॥ १४॥

English
# *Brahmā said:—* O celestial sage, in the manner laid down in the Vedas he performed the purificatory rites for the son of Śiva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवबालोपि सुप्रीतो दिव्यज्ञानमदात्परम् ।
विश्वामित्राय मुनये महोतिकारकः प्रभुः ॥ १५॥

English
Śiva's son, the cause of great enjoyment and protection, was glad and conferred divine wisdom on the sage. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरोहितं चकारासौ विश्वामित्रं शुचेस्सुतः ।
तदारभ्य द्विजवरो नानालीलाविशारदः ॥ १६॥

English
The son of Agni made Viśvāmitra his priest. Form that time onwards he became a great brahmin and an expert in divine sports of various sorts. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं लीला कृता तेन कथिता सा मया मुने ।
तल्लीलामपरां तात श‍ृणु प्रीत्या वदाम्यहम् ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, the very first sport that he performed thus has been narrated to you by me. O dear, listen to another sport of his with wonder. I shall narrate it to you. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे तात श्वेतनामा च सम्प्रति ।
तत्राऽपश्यत्सुतं दिव्यं निजं परमपावनम् ॥ १८॥

ततस्तं पावको गत्वा दृष्ट्वालिङ्ग्य चुचुम्ब च ।
पुत्रेति चोक्त्वा तस्मै स शस्त्रं शक्तिं ददौ च सः ॥ १९॥

English
At that time he was known as white in colour. Agni went there and seeing his son who was divine and very holy called him "O dear son." Agni embraced and kissed him too. He gave him a miraculous weapon, spear. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
गुहस्तां शक्तिमादाय तच्छृङ्गं चारुरोह ह ।
तं जघान तया शक्त्या श‍ृङ्गो भुवि पपात सः ॥ २०॥

English
Guha took the spear and ascended the peak. He hit the peak with his spear and the peak fell down. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दशपद्ममिता वीरा राक्षसाः पूर्वमागताः ।
तद्वधार्थं द्रुतं नष्टा बभूवुस्तत्प्रहारतः ॥ २१॥

English
Ten thousand billions of heroic demons came there to attack him but were killed on being hit with the spear. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारो महानासीच्चकम्पे साचला मही ।
त्रैलोक्यं च सुरेशानःसदेवस्तत्र चागमत् ॥ २२॥

English
There was great hue and cry. The Earth, the mountains and the three worlds quaked. Indra the lord of gods came there. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणे तस्य पार्श्वे च वज्रेण स जघान च ।
शाखनामा ततो जातः पुमांश्चैको महाबलः ॥ २३॥

English
With his thunderbolt he hit on his right side. A person named "Śākha"$^{136}$ of great strength came out of that side. (23.)

[fn $^{136}$ Śākha, Viśākha and Naigama are the three brothers of Kumāra. According to ŚP. they are his very self. SP. RS. iv. 23-25.

V.S Agrawal gives quite a different interpretation. According to him Kumāra is the symbol of the life-principle manifesting in every individual. Kṛttikās are the six yogic *cakras* located in the human body in the golden reed. Kumāra who is born in this six-jointed single reed is called (i) Kārttikeya and (ii) Śākha. The life-principle then branches out in the form of five gross elements and the mind which together are called (iii) Viśākha. As the popular God of the merchant community Kumāra is called (iv) Naigama or Naigameya.]

Sanskrit

English
Śakra struck him again with his thunderbolt on his left side. Another strong person named Viśākha came out of that side. (24.)

Sanskrit

Then Indra struck his heart with his thunderbolt. Another person very powerful like him named Naigama came out. (25.)

English
Then the four of great heroic strength including Skanda rushed to attack Indra. I offered my protection to Indra. (26.)

Sanskrit

English
Afraid of Guha, Indra with all the gods went away to his region agitatedly. O sage, he did not know his secret. (27.)

Sanskrit

English
That boy remained there itself as fearless as before. O dear, he was highly pleased and continued his divine sports of various sorts. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Meanwhile the six ladies named Kṛttikās came there for bath and they saw the lordly boy. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
All of them desired to take and fondle him O sage, as a result of their simultaneous desire for taking and fondling the boy, a dispute arose. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वादशमनार्थं स षण्मुखानि चकार ह ।
पपौ दुग्धं च सर्वासां तुष्टास्ता अभवन्मुने ॥ ३१॥

English
In order to quell their mutual dispute, the boy assumed six faces and drank milk off their breasts. O sage, they were all satisfied. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

तमादाय ययुर्लोकं स्वकीयं मुदिता मुने ॥ ३२॥

English
Realising his desire, the Kṛttikās, O sage, took him to their region joyously. Feeding him with their breast milk they nursed and nurtured Śiva’s son who was more refulgent than the sun. (32-33.)

Sanskrit

English
They never let him go out of their sight. He became the object of their love, dearer to them than their own lives. Verily he who nurses and nurtures the child has the right of possession over the son.$^{137}$ (34.)
