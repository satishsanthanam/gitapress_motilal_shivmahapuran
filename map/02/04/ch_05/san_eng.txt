### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(Kārttikeya is crowned)***

---

Sanskrit
५

ब्रह्मोवाच
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र ददर्श रथमुत्तमम् ।
अद्भुतं शोभितं शश्वद्विश्वकर्मविनिर्मितम् ॥ १॥

शतचक्रं सुविस्तीर्णं मनोयायि मनोहरम् ।
प्रस्थापितं च पार्वत्या वेष्टितं पार्षदैर्वरैः ॥ २॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  In the meantime he saw an excellent, lustrous and wonderful chariot, made by Viśvakarman. It was a commodious with a hundred wheels. It was beautiful and had the quickness of the mind. It had been sent by Pārvatī and was surrounded by the excellent attendants of Śiva. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
समारोहत्ततोऽनन्तो हृदयेन विदूयता ।
कार्त्तिकः परमज्ञानी परमेशानवीर्यजः ॥ ३॥

English
With an aching heart, Kārttikeya, born of the semen of lord Śiva, the perfectly wise and endless being, got into it. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव कृत्तिकाः प्राप्य मुक्तकेश्यः शुचाऽऽतुराः ।
उन्मत्ता इव तत्रैव वक्तुमारेभिरे वचः ॥ ४॥

English
At the same time, the distressed grief-stricken Kṛttikās approached him with dishevelled hair and began to speak like mad women. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्तिका ऊचुः ।
विहायाऽस्मान् कृपासिन्धो गच्छसि त्वं हि निर्दयः ।
नायं धर्मो मातृवर्गान् पालितो यत् सुतस्त्यजेत् ॥ ५॥

English
*Kṛttikās said:—* O ocean of mercy, how is it that you ruthlessly leave us and go? This is not a virtuous thing for a fostered son to forsake his mothers. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नेहेन वर्द्धितोऽस्माभिः पुत्रोऽस्माकं च धर्मतः ।
किं कुर्मः क्व च यास्यामो वयं किं करवाम ह ॥ ६॥

English
You have been brought up by us affectionately. Hence you are our son in virtue of that. What shall we do? Where shall we go? What can we do? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा कृत्तिकाः सर्वाः कृत्वा वक्षसि कार्त्तिकम् ।
द्रुतं मूर्च्छामवापुस्ताःसुतविच्छेदकारणात् ॥ ७॥

English
After saying this and closely embracing Kārttikēya, the Kṛttikās fell into a swoon due to the imminent separation from their son. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ताः कुमारो बोधयित्वा अध्यात्मवचनेन वै ।
ताभिश्च पार्षदैः सार्द्धमारुरोह रथं मुने ॥ ८॥

English
Restoring them to consciousness and instructing them with spiritual utterances, O sage, he got into the chariot along with them and the Pārṣadas too. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा श्रुत्वा मङ्गलानि बहूनि सुखदानि वै ।
कुमारः पार्षदैः सार्द्धं जगाम पितृमन्दिरम् ॥ ९॥

English
Seeing and hearing various auspicious and pleasing things Kumāra went to the palace of his father along with the Pārṣadas. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षेण नन्दियुक्तश्च मनोयायिरथेन च ।
कुमारः प्राप कैलासं न्यग्रोधाऽक्षयमूलके ॥ १०॥

English
Kumāra reached the foot of a Nyagrodha tree at Kailāsa in the fast chariot along with Nandin seated to his right. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तस्थौ कृत्तिकाभिः पार्षदप्रवरैः सह ।
कुमारः शाङ्करिः प्रीतो नानालीलाविशारदः ॥ ११॥

English
There Kumāra, the son of Śiva, an expert in various divine sports, waited along with the Kṛttikās and the chief of Pārṣadas, in great delight. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सर्वे सुरगणा ऋषयः सिद्धचारणाः ।
विष्णुना ब्रह्मणा सार्द्धं समाचख्युस्तदागमम् ॥ १२॥

English
Then all the gods, sages, Siddhas, Cāraṇas, Viṣṇu and Brahmā announced his arrival. (12.)

---

English
Then in order to see him Śiva, along with Viṣṇu, Brahmā, the gods, sages and others went there. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा दृष्ट्वा च गाङ्गेयं ययौ प्रमुदितश्शिवः ।
अन्यैः समेतो हरिणा ब्रह्मणा च सुरर्षिभिः । १३॥

शङ्खाश्च बहवो नेदुर्भेरी तूर्याण्यनेकशः ।
उत्सवः सुमहानासीद्देवानां तुष्टचेतसाम् ॥ १४॥

English
Many conches, Bheris and Tūryas were sounded. There was great jubilation among the delighted gods. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तदानीमेव तं सर्वे वीरभद्रादयो गणाः ।
कुर्वन्तः स्वन्वयुः केलिं नानातालधरस्वराः ॥ १५॥

English
Virabhadra and other Gaṇas followed them with different chiming cymbals beating the time and sporting about. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तावकाः स्तूयमानाश्च चक्रुस्ते गुणकीर्त्तनम् ।
जयशब्दं नमश्शब्दं कुर्वाणाः प्रीतमानसाः ॥ १६॥

English
Eulogising and being eulogised they sang songs of praise. (16.)

---

English
Shouting cries of “Victory” and “Obeisance” the delighted people went to see the excellent son of Śiva born in the grove of Śara plants.¹⁴⁰ (17.)

[fn 140. Kumāra is said to be born of Śiva’s semen that was first swallowed by the Fire assuming the form of a pigeon, then by the Kṛttikās, the six wives of the sages, then by Gaṇḍā who deposited it in the reeds. Kumāra is thus called आग्नेय, कात्तिकेय, गांगेय and शरज।]
---

Sanskrit
द्रष्टुं ययुस्तं शरजं शिवात्मजमनुत्तमम् । १७॥

पार्वती मङ्गलं चक्रे राजमार्गं मनोहरम् ।
पद्मरागादिमणिभिः संस्कृतं परितः पुरम् ॥ १८॥

English
Pārvatī caused the entire outskirts of the city fully decorated with Padmarāga and other gems. The main highway was rendered beautiful and auspicious. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिपुत्रवतीभिश्च साध्वीभिः स्त्रीभिरन्विताः ।
लक्ष्म्यादित्रिंशद्देवीश्च पुरः कृत्वा समाययौ ॥ १९॥

English
The thirty goddesses Lakṣmī and others stood in front, along with chaste ladies whose husbands and sons were alive and Pārvatī stood ahead of them. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
रम्भाद्यप्सरसो दिव्याः सस्मिता वेषसंयुताः ।
सङ्गीतनर्तनपरा बभूवुश्च शिवाज्ञया ॥ २०॥

English
At the bidding of Pārvatī, the smiling celestial damsels, Rambhā and others, dressed gorgeously, were engaged in singing and dancing. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ये तं समीक्षयामासुर्गाङ्गेयं शङ्करोपमम् ।
ददृशुस्ते महत्तेजो व्याप्तमासीज्जगत्त्रये ॥ २१॥

English
Those who looked at Kumāra resembling Śiva saw a great halo pervading the three worlds. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्तेजसा वृतं बालं तप्तचामीकरप्रभम् ।
ववन्दिरे द्रुतं सर्वे कुमारं सूर्यवर्चसम् ॥ २२॥

English
Immediately they saluted Kumāra who was enveloped by the brilliant halo, the lustre of molten gold and the refulgence of the sun. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
जहृषुर्विनतस्कन्धा नमश्शब्दरतास्तदा ।
परिवार्योपतस्थुस्ते वामदक्षिणमागताः ॥ २३॥

English
With shoulders stooping down and eagerly engaged in shouting the cry of “Obeisance” they flanked him to the right and left and stood by. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं विष्णुश्च शक्रश्च तथा देवादयोऽखिलाः ।
दण्डवत्पतिता भूमौ परिवार्य कुमारकम् ॥ २४॥

English
Viṣṇu, Indra and I as well as the gods prostrated on the ground and went round Kumāra. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शम्भुर्गिरिजा च मुदान्विता ।
महोत्सवं समागम्य ददर्श तनयं मुदा ॥ २५॥

English
In the meantime Śiva, and Pārvatī highly delighted and jubilant came there and saw their son. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रं निरीक्ष्य च तदा जगदेकबन्धुः
प्रीत्यान्वितः परमया परया भवान्या ।
स्नेहान्वितो भुजगभोगयुतो हि साक्षात्
सर्वेश्वरः परिवृतः प्रमथैः परेशः ॥ २६॥

English
On seeing his son, the great lord Śiva, the sole kinsman of the universe along with the great goddess Pārvatī was filled with pleasure and love—the lord who wore snakes on his body and was surrounded by the Pramathas. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शक्तिधरः स्कन्दो दृष्ट्वा तौ पार्वतीशिवौ ।
अवरुह्य रथात्तूर्णं शिरसा प्रणनाम ह ॥ २७॥

English
On seeing Pārvatī and Śiva, Kārttikeya got down from the chariot immediately and saluted them. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
उपगुह्य शिवः प्रीत्या कुमारं मूर्ध्नि शङ्करः ।
जघ्रौ प्रेम्णा परेशानः प्रसन्नः स्नेहकर्तृकः ॥ २८॥

English
Embracing him with love, Śiva kissed Kumāra on the head. He, the cause of great affection, was highly delighted. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
उपगुह्य गुहं तत्र पार्वती जातसम्भ्रमा ।
प्रस्नुतं पाययामास स्तनं स्नेहपरिप्लुता ॥ २९॥

English
Embracing him in great excitement and melting with love, Pārvatī suckled him at her breasts. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा नीराजितो देवैःसकलत्रैर्मुदान्वितैः ।
जयशब्देन महता व्याप्तमासीन्नभस्तलम् ॥ ३०॥

English
The Nirājana rite was performed by the delighted gods in the company of their wives. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The sages adored Kumāra with the Vedic chants, the musicians by singing songs, and others by playing upon musical instruments. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

बभौ भवानीपतिरेव साक्षा-
च्छ्रियाऽन्वितः पुत्रवतां वरिष्ठः ॥ ३२॥

English
Placing Kumāra shining with brilliant lustre on his lap Pārvatī shone with glory as the greatest among women who carried sons. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

शिवाज्ञया महोत्साहैस्सह देवैर्महासुखी ॥ ३३॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva, Kumāra in the company of his Gaṇas came to Śiva's abode. He felt very happy in the company of jubilant gods. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

विवन्द्यमानावृषिभिरावृतौ सुरसत्तमैः ॥ ३४॥

English
The couple shone simultaneously being saluted by the sages and surrounded by the important gods. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Kumāra delightedly played about in the lap of Śiva. He teased Vāsuki$^{141}$ round Śiva's neck with his hands. (35.)

[fn 141. Vāsuki, the King of the Nāgas, is said to adorn Śiva's neck.]
---

Sanskrit

English
Seeing that sportive act with his merciful vision, lord Śiva spoke about it to Pārvatī laughingly. (36.)

Sanskrit

English

English

महामहोत्सवस्तत्र बभूव मुनिसत्तम ।
सर्वे प्रसन्नतां याता विशेषाच्च शिवाशिवौ ॥ ५३॥

English
O excellent sage, there was great jubilation there. Everyone was delighted, especially Pārvatī and Śiva. (53.)

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे काले प्रोवाच प्रहसन् मुदा ।
मुने ब्रह्मादिकान् देवान् रुद्रो भर्गः प्रतापवान् ॥ ५४॥

English
In the meantime, O sage, the powerful Śiva, spoke to Brahmā and to other gods laughingly and joyously. (54.)

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच
हे हरे हे विधे देवाः सर्वे श‍ृणुत मद्वचः ।
सर्वथाहं प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरान्वृणुत ऐच्छिकान् ॥ ५५॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  “O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O gods, you listen to my words. I am delighted in all respects. Please choose the boons you wish.” (55.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं शम्भोर्मुने विष्ण्वादयः सुराः ।
सर्वे प्रोचुः प्रसन्नास्या देवं पशुपतिं प्रभुम् ॥ ५६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, on hearing those words of Śiva, Viṣṇu and other gods spoke to Śiva with faces beaming with pleasure. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
कुमारेण हतो ह्येष तारको भविता प्रभो ।
तदर्थमेव सञ्जातमिदं चरितमुत्तमम् ॥ ५७॥

English
*The gods said:—* “O lord, Tāraka will certainly be killed by Kumāra. It is for that purpose that he is born. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादद्यैव यास्यामस्तारकं हन्तुमुद्यताः ।
आज्ञां देहि कुमाराय स तं हन्यात् सुखाय नः ॥ ५८॥

English
Hence in our effort to kill him we shall start this very day. Please give your directions to Kumāra. Let him slay Tāraka for our happiness. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तथेति मत्वा स विभुर्दत्तवांस्तनयं तदा ।
देवेभ्यस्तारकं हन्तुं कृपया परिभावितः ॥ ५९॥

English
Thinking that it shall be so, lord Śiva entrusted his son to the gods in order to kill Tāraka, urged by his mercy that he was. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाज्ञया सुराः सर्वे ब्रह्मविष्णुमुखास्तदा ।
पुरस्कृत्य गुहं सद्यो निर्जग्मुर्मिलिता गिरेः ॥ ६०॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva, Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other gods jointly started from the mountain keeping Kumāra in front. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
बहिर्निस्सृत्य कैलासात्त्वष्टा शासनतो हरेः ।
विरेचे नगरं रम्यमद्भुतं निकटे गिरेः ॥ ६१॥

English
After coming out of Kailāsa, at the behest of Viṣṇu, Tvaṣṭr built a wonderfully fine city very near the mountain. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र रम्यं गृहं दिव्यमद्भुतं परमोज्ज्वलम् ।
गुहार्थं निर्ममे त्वष्टा तत्र सिंहासनं वरम् ॥ ६२॥

English
There he built a divine, exquisite and wonderfully brilliant house for Kumāra. Tvaṣṭr set up an excellent throne there. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा हरिः सुधीर्भक्त्या कारयामास मङ्गलम् ।
कार्त्तिकस्याभिषेकं हि सर्वतीर्थजलैः सुरैः ॥ ६३॥

English
The intelligent Viṣṇu performed the auspicious ceremony of crowning Kārttikeya in the company of the gods by means of waters from all holy centres. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा समलङ्कृत्य वासयामास सङ्ग्रहम् ।
कार्त्तिकस्य विधिं प्रीत्या कारयामास चोत्सवम् ॥ ६४॥

English
He bedecked Kārttikeya in every manner and dressed him gorgeously. He went through the ceremony in brief and made everyone celebrate the event with pleasure. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माण्डाधिपतित्वं हि ददौ तस्मै मुदा हरिः ।
चकार तिलकं तस्य समानर्च सुरैस्सह ॥ ६५॥

English
Viṣṇu joyously gave him the suzerainty of the universe. He applied the Tilaka mark and worshipped him along with the gods. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य कार्त्तिकं प्रीत्या सर्वदेवर्षिभिः सह ।
तुष्टाव विविधैः स्तोत्रैः शिवरूपं सनातनम् ॥ ६६॥

English
Bowing to Karttikeya with pleasure along with the gods and sages he eulogised the eternal form of Śiva with various hymns. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
वरसिंहासनस्थो हि शुशुभेऽतीव कार्त्तिकः ।
स्वामिभावं समापन्नो ब्रह्माण्डस्यापि पालकः ॥ ६७॥

English
Karttikeya seated in the excellent throne and assuming the lordship and protectorate of the universe shone extremely well. (67.)
