### **CHAPTER SIX - *(The miraculous feat of Kārttikeya)***

---

Sanskrit
६

ब्रह्मोवाच
अथ तत्र स गाङ्गेयो दर्शयामास सूतिकाम् ।
तामेव श‍ृणु सुप्रीत्या नारद त्वं स्वभक्तिदाम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  There Kumāra showed a miraculous feat. O Nārada, listen to it that bestows devotion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विज एको नारदाख्य आजगाम तदैव हि ।
तत्राध्वरकरः श्रीमान् शरणार्थं गुहस्य वै ॥ २॥

English
Then a certain brahmin Nārada came there, seeking refuge in Kumāra. He was glorious and had been performing a sacrifice. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स विप्रः प्राप्य निकटं कार्त्तिकस्य प्रसन्नधीः ।
स्वाभिप्रायं समाचख्यौ सुप्रणम्य शुभैः स्तवैः ॥ ३॥

English
Approaching Kumāra, bowing to and eulogising him with auspicious hymns the delighted brahmin related his tale. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्र उवाच
श‍ृणु स्वामिन्वचो मेऽद्य कष्टं मे विनिवारय ।
सर्वब्रह्माण्डनाथस्त्वमतस्ते शरणं गतः ॥ ४॥

English
*The brahmin said:—*  O lord, listen to my words. Relieve my distress. You are the lord of the universe. I seek refuge in you. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अजमेधाध्वरं कर्तुमारम्भं कृतवानहम् ।
सोऽजो गतो गृहान्मे हि त्रोटयित्वा स्वबन्धनम् ॥ ५॥

English
I began a goat sacrifice. The goat got loosened and strayed away from my house. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
न जाने स गतः कुत्राऽन्वेषणं तत्कृतं बहु ।
न प्राप्तोऽतस्स बलवान् भङ्गो भवति मे क्रतोः ॥ ६॥

English
I do not know where it has gone. I have searched for it here and there but have not found it. Hence this will cause a serious default in my sacrifice. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयि नाथे सति विभो यज्ञभङ्गः कथं भवेत् ।
विचार्य्यैवाऽखिलेशान कामं पूर्णं कुरुष्व मे ॥ ७॥

English
While you are the lord, how can there be an obstacle to my sacrifice? O lord, after pondering over this matter please fulfil my task. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां विहाय शरण्यं कं यायां शिवसुत प्रभो ।
सर्वब्रह्माण्डनाथं हि सर्वामरसुसेवितम् ॥ ८॥

English
O lord, O son of Śiva, who else can I approach except you, who are worthy of being resorted to, who are the lord of the entire universe and are served by all the gods. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
दीनबन्धुर्दयासिन्धुः सुसेव्यो भक्तवत्सलः ।
हरिब्रह्मादिदेवैश्च सुस्तुतः परमेश्वरः ॥ ९॥

पार्वतीनन्दनस्स्कन्दः परमेकः परन्तपः ।
परमात्माऽत्मदः स्वामी सतां च शरणार्थिनाम् ॥ १०॥

English
You are the kinsman of the distressed. You are worthy of being served well. You are favourably disposed to your devotees. You are the great lord eulogised by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods. You are Skanda the delighter of Pārvatī, the sole destroyer of enemies, the great soul, the lord who bestows his own self upon the good seeking refuge in him. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
दीनानाथ महेश शङ्करसुत त्रैलोक्यनाथ प्रभो
मायाधीश समागतोऽस्मि शरणं मां पाहि विप्रप्रिय ।
त्वं सर्वप्रभुरानताऽखिलविदब्रह्मादिदेवैः स्तुतः
त्वं मायाकृतिरात्मभक्तसुखदो रक्षापरो मायिकः ॥ ११॥

English
O lord of the distressed, O great lord, O son of Śiva, O lord of the three worlds, O master of magical art, I have to seek refuge in you. O favourite of the brahmins, save me. You are the lord of all. You are eulogised by Brahmā and other gods who bow to you. You have assumed forms through magical art. You are the bestower of happiness to your devotees. You are eager to protect. You wield power of deluding others. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तप्राणगुणाकरस्त्रिगुणतो भिन्नोऽसि शम्भुप्रियः
शम्भुः शम्भुसुतः प्रसन्नसुखदः सच्चित्स्वरूपो महान् ।
सर्वज्ञस्त्रिपुरघ्नशङ्करसुतः सत्प्रेमवश्यःसदा
षड्वक्त्रः प्रियसाधुरानतप्रियः सर्वेश्वरः शङ्करः ।
साधुद्रोहकरघ्न शङ्करगुरो ब्रह्माण्डनाथो प्रभुः
सर्वेषाममरादिसेवितपदो मां पाहि सेवाप्रिय ॥ १२॥

English
You love devotees as your own vital air. You are the receptacle of all attributes. You are beyond three attributes. You are the beloved of Śiva. You are Śiva Himself. You confer welfare. You are the bestower of happiness with delight. You are the great Existent and cosmic consciousness. You are the son of Śiva, the omniscient who destroyed the three cities of Asuras. You are always subservient to great and pious love. You have six faces. You love the saintly persons who kneel to you. You are the lord of all people and their benefactor. You destroy those, who harass the good. You are the preceptor of even Śiva. You are the lord of the entire universe. Your feet are served by all the gods. O lover of service, save me. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वैरिभयङ्कर शङ्कर जनशरणस्य
वन्दे तव पदपद्मं सुखकरणस्य ।
विज्ञप्तिं मम कर्णे स्कन्द निधेहि
निजभक्तिं जनचेतसि सदा विधेहि ॥ १३॥

English
O Skanda, terrible to the enemies, the benefactor of the devotees, I bow to your lotus-like feet. You are the refuge of people and source of their happiness. Please hear my submission through your ears. Please instil into the heart of everyone the feelings of devotion to you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
करोति किं तस्य बली विपक्षो
दक्षोऽपि पक्षोभयपार्श्वगुप्तः ।
किं तक्षकोऽप्यामिषभक्षको वा
त्वं रक्षको यस्य सदक्षमानः ॥ १४॥

English
If you are the protector with efficient honour what harm can an opponent do even if he be strong and efficient and protected on either side? What harm can even Takṣaka¹⁴³ or even a carnivorous animal do unto him. (14.)

[fn 143. Takṣaka, the son of Kadru, is a venomous serpent chief.]
---

Sanskrit
विबुधगुरुरपि त्वां स्तोतुमीशो न हि स्यात्
कथय कथमहं स्यां मन्दबुद्धिर्वरार्च्यः ।
शुचिरशुचिरनार्यो यादृशस्तादृशो वा
पदकमलपरागं स्कन्द ते प्रार्थयामि ॥ १५॥

English
Even the preceptor of the gods cannot eulogise you adequately. Then tell me, how can I a foolish and wretched creature? O Skanda, pure or impure, noble or ignoble, of whatever nature I be, I pray unto the dust of your lotus-like feet. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
हे सर्वेश्वर भक्तवत्सल कृपा-सिन्धो त्वदीयोऽस्म्यहं
भृत्यः स्वस्य न सेवकस्य गणप-स्यागःशतं सत्प्रभो ।
भक्तिं क्वापि कृतां मनागपि विभो जानासि भृत्यार्तिहा
त्वत्तो नास्त्यपरोऽविता न भगवन् मत्तो नरः पामरः ॥ १६॥

English
O lord of all, ocean of mercy, favourably disposed to devotees, I am your own servant. May even a hundred sins of your own servant or a leader of the Gaṇas be forgiven. O lord, you know even the slightest act of devotion done anywhere. You are the destroyer of the distress of your servants. O lord, there is no other protector save you and no other wretched vulgar person than I. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्याणकर्त्ता कलिकल्मषघ्नः
कुबेरबन्धुः करुणार्द्रचित्तः ।
त्रिषट्कनेत्रो रसवक्त्रशोभी
यज्ञं प्रपूर्णं कुरु मे गुह त्वम् ॥ १७॥

English
O lord, you are the cause of welfare, the destroyer of the sins of Kali age and a friend of Kubera. Your heart melts with pity. You have twelve eyes and you shine with six faces. Please make my sacrifice complete and perfect. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षकस्त्वं त्रिलोकस्य शरणागतवत्सलः ।
यज्ञकर्त्ता यज्ञभर्त्ता हरसे विघ्नकारिणाम् ॥ १८॥

English
You are the protector of the three worlds, favourite of those who seek refuge in you. You are the performer and sustainer of sacrifices. You remove those who bring in obstacles. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विघ्नवारण साधूनां सर्ग कारण सर्वतः ।
पूर्णं कुरु ममेशान सुतयज्ञ नमोस्तु ते ॥ १९॥

English
O warder of obstacles, the cause of the creation of the good in every respect, O son of Iśāna, please make my sacrifices complete. Obeisance be to you, (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वत्राता स्कन्द हि त्वं सर्वज्ञाता त्वमेव हि ।
सर्वेश्वरस्त्वमीशानो निवेशसकलाऽवनः ॥ २०॥

English
O Skanda, you are the protector of all, the knower of all and the lord of all and Iśāna. By your penetration you protect all. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्गीतज्ञस्त्वमेवासि वेदविज्ञः परः प्रभुः ।
सर्वस्थाता विधाता त्वं देवदेवः सतां गतिः ॥ २१॥

English
You alone are the knower of music, the great lord and knower of the Vedas. You are all-in-all, the creator, the lord of the gods and the goal of the good. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
भवानीनन्दनः शम्भुतनयो वयुनः स्वराट् ।
ध्याता ध्येयः पितॄणां हि पिता योनिः सदात्मनाम् ॥ २२॥

English
You are the joy of Pārvatī, the son of Śiva. You are the perfect wisdom, the self-ruler, the meditator and the object of meditation. You are the father of the fathers and the source of origin of good souls. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य देवसम्राट् शिवात्मजः ।
स्वगणं वीरबाह्वाख्यं प्रेषयामास तत्कृते ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing his words, Śiva's son, the emperor of the gods, sent his attendant Virabāhu on that mission. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाज्ञया वीरबाहुस्तदन्वेषणहेतवे ।
प्रणम्य स्वामिनं भक्त्या महावीरो द्रुतं ययौ ॥ २४॥

English
At his bidding, the great hero Virabāhu who bowed to his master with devotion started in search of it. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्वेषणं चकारासौ सर्वब्रह्माण्डगोलके ।
न प्राप तमजं कुत्र शुश्राव तदुपद्रवम् ॥ २५॥

English
He searched throughout the universe but nowhere did he find the goat (although) he heard about the havoc done by it. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
जगामाऽथ स वैकुण्ठं तत्राऽजं प्रददर्श तम् ।
उपद्रवं प्रकुर्वन्तं गलयूपं महाबलम् ॥ २६॥

English
Then he went to Vaikuṇṭha where he saw the powerful goat working havoc with the sacrificial stake tied to its neck. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
धृत्वा तं श‍ृङ्गयोर्वीरो धर्षयित्वातिवेगतः ।
आनिनाय स्वामिपुरो विकुर्वन्तं रवं बहु ॥ २७॥

English
The hero dragged it catching hold of its horns and brought it quickly before his lord even as it was bleating loudly. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तं कार्त्तिकस्सोऽरमारुरोह स तं प्रभुः ।
धृतब्रह्माण्डगरिमा महासूतिकरो गुहः ॥ २८॥

English
On seeing it, lord Kārttikeya who could carry the weighty universe, and the worker of great miracles, quickly rode on it. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहूर्तमात्रतस्सोऽजो ब्रह्माण्डं सकलं मुने ।
बभ्राम श्रम एवाशु पुनस्तत्स्थानमागतः ॥ २९॥

English
Within a Muhūrta, O sage, the goat walked round the universe and without exhaustion returned to the same place. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तत उत्तीर्य स स्वामी समुवास स्वमासनम् ।
सोऽजः स्थितस्तु तत्रैव स नारद उवाच तम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Then the lord got down and resumed his seat. The goat stood there itself. Then the brahmin Nārada told the lord. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच
नमस्ते देव देवेश देहि मेऽजं कृपानिधे ।
कुर्यामध्वरमानन्दात्सखायं कुरु मामहो ॥ ३१॥

English
*Nārada said :—*  Obeisance to you, O lord of gods, O storehouse of mercy, give the goat to me. Let me perform the sacrifice with pleasure. Please assist me as my friend. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्त्तिक उवाच
वधयोग्यो न विप्राऽजः स्वगृहं गच्छ नारद ।
पूर्णोऽस्तु तेऽध्वरः सर्वः प्रसादादेव मे कृतः ॥ ३२॥

English
*Kārttikeya said :—*   O brahmin Nārada, this goat does not deserve to be killed. Return home. May your sacrifice be complete. It has been so ordained by my favour. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्याकर्ण्य द्विजःस्वामी वचनं प्रीतमानसः ।
जगाम स्वालयं दत्त्वा तस्मा आशिषमुत्तमाम् ॥ ३३॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the words of the lord, the brahmin was delighted. He returned home after bestowing his excellent blessings. (33.)
