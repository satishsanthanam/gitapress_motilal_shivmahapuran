### **CHAPTER SEVEN - *(Commencement of the War)***

---

Sanskrit
७

ब्रह्मोवाच
हर्य्यादयस्सुरास्ते च दृष्ट्वा तच्चरितं विभोः ।
सुप्रसन्ना बभूवुर्हि विश्वासासक्तमानसाः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On seeing that miraculous feat of Kumāra, Viṣṇu and other gods became delighted. They were convinced of his prowess. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वल्गन्तः कुर्वतो नादं भाविताश्शिवतेजसा ।
कुमारन्ते पुरस्कृत्य तारकं हन्तुमाययुः ॥ २॥

English
Keeping Kumāra at the head, shouting and roaring, purified by Śiva’s splendour they started to attack Tāraka. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानामुद्यमं श्रुत्वा तारकोऽपि महाबलः ।
सैन्येन महता सद्यो ययौ योद्धुं सुरान् प्रति ॥ ३॥

English
When he heard about the preparation of the gods, the powerful Tāraka rushed to fight back the gods with a great army. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा दृष्ट्वा समायान्तं तारकस्य महाबलम् ।
बलेन बहुकुर्वन्तः सिंहनादं विसिस्मियुः ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing the great army of Tāraka approaching, the gods were surprised but roared like lions. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा नभोऽङ्गना वाणीं जगादोपरि सत्वरम् ।
शङ्करप्रेरिता सद्यो हर्यादीनखिलान् सुरान् ॥ ५॥

English
Then a celestial voice, prompted by Śiva addressed Viṣṇu and all other gods. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
व्योमवाण्युवाच
कुमारं च पुरस्कृत्य सुरा यूयं समुद्यताः ।
दैत्यान्विजित्य सङ्ग्रामे जयिनोऽथ भविष्यथ ॥ ६॥

English
*The celestial Voice said ;—*  O gods, keeping Kumāra at the head you have entered the lists. Defeating the Asuras in the battle, you will be victorious. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
वाचं तु खेचरीं श्रुत्वा देवाः सर्वे समुत्सुकाः ।
वीरशब्दान् प्रकुर्वन्तो निर्भया ह्यभवंस्तदा ॥ ७॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  On hearing the celestial voice, the gods became enthusiastic. Fearlessly they roared like heroes. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
कुमारं च पुरस्कृत्य सर्वे ते गतसाध्वसाः ।
योद्धुकामाः सुरा जग्मुर्महीसागरसङ्गमम् ॥ ८॥

English
With their fear subsided, and keeping Kumāra ahead, the gods went to the confluence of the river Mahī and the ocean$^{144}$ desirous of fighting. (8.)

[fn 144. The scene of the battle between the gods and Asuras is placed in the Western India on the coast of the Arabian sea where the sacred river Mahī that issues from Sahyapāda hill falls into it. For details see Dr. Avasthi: Studies in Skandapurāṇa, pp. 128, 140, 160, 168.]
---

Sanskrit
आजगाम द्रुतं तत्र यत्र देवाः स तारकः ।
सैन्येन महता सार्द्धं असुरैर्बहुभिर्वृतः ॥ ९॥

English
Immediately Tāraka, along with a great army, came to the place where the gods stood and was surrounded by them in a body. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
रणदुन्दुभयो नेदुः प्रलयाम्बुदनिःस्वनाः ।
कर्कशानि च वाद्यानि पराणि च तदागमे ॥ १०॥

English
Battle drums were sounded as loud as the rumbling sound of the clouds at the dissolution of the world. The harsh musical instruments were also played when he came. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्जमानास्तदा दैत्यास्तारकेणासुरेण ह ।
कम्पयन्तो भुवं पादक्रमैर्वल्गुनकारकाः ॥ ११॥

English
The Asuras in the company of Tāraka roared and shook the ground with their thudding footsteps, leapings and bouncings. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा रवमत्युग्रं सर्वे देवा विनिर्भयाः ।
ऐकपद्येन चोत्तस्थुर्योद्धुकामाश्च तारकम् ॥ १२॥

English
Undaunted by that terrible noise, the gods simultaneously rose up to fight Tāraka. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
गजमारोप्य देवेन्द्रः कुमारं ह्यग्रतोऽभवत् ।
सुरसैन्येन महता लोकपालैः समावृतः ॥ १३॥

English
Accompanied by the great army of the gods and the guardians of the quarters, lord Indra seated Kumāra on an elephant and rushed forward. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा दुन्दुभयो नेदुर्भेरीतूर्याण्यनेकशः ।
वीणावेणुमृदङ्गानि तथा गन्धर्वनिस्स्वनाः ॥ १४॥

English
Great wardrums, Dundubhis, Bherīs and Tūryas, lutes, flutes and Mṛdaṅgas were sounded and the Gandharvas sang war songs. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
गजं दत्त्वा महेन्द्राय कुमारो यानमारुहत् ।
अनेकाश्चर्यसम्भूतं नानारत्नसमन्वितम् ॥ १५॥

English
Leaving the elephant to lord Indra, Kumāra got in an aerial chariot of wonderful build and studded with different sets of gems. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
विमानमारुह्य तदा महायशाः
स शाङ्करिःसर्वगुणैरुपेतः ।
श्रिया समेतः परया बभौ महान्
संवीज्यमानश्चमरैर्महाप्रभैः ॥ १६॥

English
Seated in the aerial chariot, the son of Śiva endowed with good qualities and of great renown shone with great splendour. He was being fanned with lustrous chowries. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राचेतसं छत्रमतीवसुप्रभं
रत्नैरुपेतं विविधैर्विराजितम् ।
धृतं तदा तच्च कुमारमूर्ध्नि वै
ह्यनन्तचान्द्रैः किरणैर्महाप्रभैः ॥ १७॥

English
The lustrous umbrella presented by Varuṇa, shining with various gems was held aloft over his head. Beams of light as though of infinite moons shed great lustre around. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मिलितास्ते तदा सर्वे देवाः शक्रपुरोगमाः ।
स्वैः स्वैर्बलैः परिवृता युद्धकामा महाबलाः ॥ १८॥

English
Indra and other gods of great strength, desirous of fighting, joined him with their own divisions of the army. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं देवाश्च दैत्याश्च योद्धुकामाः स्थिता भुवि ।
सैन्येन महता तेन व्यूहं कृत्वा पृथक् पृथक् ॥ १९॥

English
The gods and the demons stood in their arrays on the ground with a vast army ready to start the battle. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सेने सुरदैत्यानां शुशुभाते परस्परम् ।
हन्तुकामे तदान्योन्यं स्तूयमाने च बन्दिभिः ॥ २०॥

English
With the bards singing their songs of praise, the armies of the gods and the Asuras shone in their eagerness to pounce on and crush each other. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
उभे सेने तदा तेषामगर्जेतां वनोपमे ।
भयङ्करेऽत्यवीराणामितरेषां सुखावहे ॥ २१॥

English
The two armies as vast as a wild jungle roared. They were terrific to the coward and pleasing to the brave. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र बलोन्मत्ताः परस्परम् ।
दैत्या देवा महावीरा युयुधुः क्रोधविह्वलाः ॥ २२॥

English
In the meantime the rank and file of the Asuras and the gods, haughty of their strength and blazing with fury came together in a mutual clash. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आसीत्सुतुमुलं युद्धं देवदैत्यसमाकुलम् ।
रुण्डमुण्डाङ्कितं सर्वं क्षणेन समपद्यत ॥ २३॥

English
A terrific tumultuous fight between the gods and the Asuras ensued. Within a moment the place was littered with severed heads and headless trunks. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
भूमौ निपतितास्तत्र शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।
निकृत्ताङ्गा महाशस्त्रैर्निहता वीरसम्मताः ॥ २४॥

English
Wounded and killed by great weapons, hundreds and thousands of heroic soldiers fell on the ground. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
केषाञ्चिद्बाहवश्छिन्नाः खड्गपातैः सुदारुणैः ।
केषाञ्चिदूरवश्छिन्ना वीराणां मानिनां मृधे ॥ २५॥

English
The arms of some were cut off by terrible blows from swords. Others lost their thighs in the battle of those honourable, heroic people. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिन्मथितसर्वाङ्गा गदाभिर्मुद्गरैस्तथा ।
केचिन्निर्भिन्नहृदयाः पाशैर्भल्लैश्च पातिताः ॥ २६॥

English
The entire body of some was smashed by the maces; the chests and hearts of some were pounded by iron clubs; some were felled to the ground by spears and dragged with nooses. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिद्विदारिताः पृष्ठे कुन्तैरृष्टिभिरङ्कुशैः ।
छिन्नान्यपि शिरांस्येव पतितानि च भूतले ॥ २७॥

English
The backs of some were torn with javelins and goads. Several heads chopped off by double-edged swords fell on the ground. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
बहूनि च कबन्धानि नृत्यमानानि तत्र वै ।
वल्गमानानि शतशो उद्यतास्त्रकराणि च ॥ २८॥

English
Hundreds of headless, limbless trunks were seen dancing and bouncing with arrows sticking to their hands. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नद्यः प्रवर्तितास्तत्र शतशोऽसृङ्वहास्तदा ।
भूतप्रेतादयस्तत्र शतशश्च समागताः ॥ २९॥

English
Blood flowed like streams in hundreds of places. Hundreds of ghosts and goblins flocked there. (29.)

---

English
Jackals and vixens began eating the flesh. Numbers of vultures, kites, crows and carnivorous birds devoured the flesh of those falling down. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
गोमायवश्शिवाः तत्र भक्षयन्तः पलं बहु ।
तथा गृध्रवटा श्येना वायसा मांसभक्षकाः ॥

बुभुजुः पतितानां च पलानि सुबहूनि वै । ३०॥

एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र तारकाख्यो महाबलः ।
सैन्येन महता सद्यो ययौ योद्धुं सुरान् प्रति ॥ ३१॥

English
In the meantime Tāraka, the demon of great strength, came there with a huge army to fight with the gods. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा दृष्ट्वा समायान्तं तारकं युद्धदुर्मदम् ।
योद्धुकामं तदा सद्यो ययुः शक्रादयस्तदा ।
बभूवाथ महोन्नादःसेनयोरुभयोरपि ॥ ३२॥

English
On seeing the haughty warrior rushing on them, Indra and others, turned against him. Then a tumultuous sound arose from both the armies. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथाभूद् द्वन्द्वयुद्धं हि सुरासुरविमर्दनम् ।
यं दृष्ट्वा हर्षिता वीराः क्लीबाश्च भयमागताः ॥ ३३॥

English
Duels were fought by the gods and the Asuras crushing each other, on seeing which heroes were delighted and cowards were terrified. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तारको युयुधे युद्धे शक्रेण दितिजो बली ।
अग्निना सह संह्रादो जम्भेनैव यमः स्वयम् ॥ ३४॥

English
The Asura Tāraka of great strength fought with Indra, Saṃhrāda with Agni and Yama with Jambha. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
महाप्रभुर्नैरृतेन पाशी सह बलेन च ।
सुवीरो वायुना सार्धं पवमानेन गुह्यराट् ॥ ३५॥

English
Lord Varuṇa fought with Nairṛta and Bala. Suvīra, the king of Guhyas, fought with Vāyu. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशानेन समं शम्भुर्युयुधे रणवित्तमः ।
शुम्भः शेषेण युयुधे कुम्भश्चन्द्रेण दानवः ॥ ३६॥

English
Śambhu fought with Iśāna. Śumbha an expert in battle fought with Śeṣa. Kumbha the Asura fought with the Moon. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
कुञ्जरो मिहिरेणाजौ महाबलपराक्रमः ।
युयुधे परमास्त्रैश्च नानायुद्धविशारदः ॥ ३७॥

English
Kuñjara of great strength and exploit, an expert in different kinds of battles, fought with Mihira, using great weapons. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं द्वन्द्वेन युद्धेन महता च सुरासुराः ।
सङ्गरे युयुधुःसर्वे बलेन कृतनिश्चयाः ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus the gods and the Asuras, fought duels using their full strength with resolution. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्योन्यं स्पर्द्धमानास्तेऽमरा दैत्या महाबलाः ।
तस्मिन्देवासुरे युद्धे दुर्जया अभवन्मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, desiring to gain the upper hand and vying with each other, the powerful gods and the Asuras were equally invincible in the battle. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा च तेषां सुरदानवानां
बभूव युद्धं तुमुलं जयैषिणाम् ।
सुखावहं वीरमनस्विनां वै
भयावहं चैव तथेतरेषाम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The fight between the gods and the Asuras desirous of victory over each other was very tumultuous. It was pleasing to the brave and terrible to the others. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
मही महारौद्रतरा विनष्टकैः
सुरासुरैर्वै पतितैरनेकशः ।
तस्मिन्नगम्यातिभयानका तदा
जाता महासौख्यवहा मनस्विनाम् ॥ ४१॥

English
The battle ground became impassable and awful with the corpses of the gods and Asuras lying there in thousands but it was very pleasing to the brave. (41.)
