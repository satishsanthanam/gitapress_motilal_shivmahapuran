### **CHAPTER EIGHT - *(The battle between the gods and Asuras)***

---

Sanskrit
८

ब्रह्मोवाच
इति ते वर्णितस्तात देवदानव सेनयोः ।
सङ्ग्रामस्तुमुलोऽतीव तत्प्रभ्वोः श‍ृणु नारद ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada, O dear, thus I have described to you the fight between the rank and file of both the armies of the gods and Asuras. The fight was very tumultuous. Now listen to the fight between the two leaders on either side. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं युद्धेऽतितुमुले देवदानवसङ्क्षये ।
तारकेणैव देवेन्द्रः शक्त्या परमया सह ॥ २॥

सद्यः पपात नागाश्च धरण्यां मूर्च्छितोऽभवत् ।
परं कश्मलमापेदे वज्रधारी सुरेश्वरः ॥ ३॥

English
In the tumultuous fight that ensued reducing the numbers of the gods and the demons, lord Indra struck by the great spear fell from his elephant and became unconscious. The thunderbolt-bearing lord of gods attained great depression of spirits and swooned. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव लोकपाः सर्वेऽसुरैश्च बलवत्तरैः ।
पराजिता रणे तात महारणविशारदैः ॥ ४॥

English
In the same manner, O dear, the guardians of the quarters, though powerful, were defeated in battle by the Asuras, great experts in warfare. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्येऽपि निर्जरा दैत्यैर्युद्ध्यमानाः पराजिताः ।
असहन्तो हि तत्तेजः पलायनपरायणाः ॥ ५॥

English
The other gods too were fought and defeated by the Asuras. Unable to bear their ferocity they took to flight. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
जगर्जुरसुरास्तत्र जयिनः सुकृतोद्यमाः ।
सिंहनादं प्रकुर्वन्तः कोलाहलपरायणाः ॥ ६॥

English
The victorious Asuras, their effort having been successful, roared like lions and raised shouts of jubilation. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र वीरभद्रो रुषान्वितः ।
आससाद गणैर्वीरैस्तारकं वीरमानिनम् ॥ ७॥

English
In the meantime Vīrabhadra reached the place furiously along with his heroic Gaṇas and approached Tāraka who professed to be a great hero. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्जरान् पृष्ठतः कृत्वा शिवकोपोद्भवो बली ।
तत्सम्मुखो बभूवाथ योद्धुकामो गणाग्रणीः ॥ ८॥

English
The leader of the Gaṇas, the strong one born of the anger of Śiva, kept the gods in the rear and faced Tāraka desirous of fighting him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा ते प्रमथाः सर्वे दैत्याश्च परमोत्सवाः ।
युयुधुः संयुगेऽन्योन्यं प्रसक्ताश्च महारणे ॥ ९॥

English
Then the Pramathas and the jubilant Asuras, fond of great battle, fought one another. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिशूलैरृष्टिभिः पाशैः खड्गैः परशुपट्टिशैः ।
निजघ्नुःसमरेऽन्योऽन्यं रणे रणविशारदाः ॥ १०॥

English
Skilled adepts in warfare they hit and smashed one another with tridents, double-edged swords, nooses, axes and sharp-edged spikes. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तारको वीरभद्रेण स त्रिशूलाहतो भृशम् ।
पपात सहसा भूमौ क्षणं मूर्छापरिप्लुतः ॥ ११॥

English
Immediately after being hit hard with a trident by Vīrabhadra, Tāraka fell unconscious on the ground. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्थाय स द्रुतं वीरस्तारको दैत्यसत्तमः ।
लब्धसंज्ञो बलाच्छक्त्या वीरभद्रं जघान ह ॥ १२॥

English
Regaining consciousness quickly Tāraka the excellent Asura got up and forcefully hit Vīrabhadra with his spear. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रस्तथा वीरो महातेजा हि तारकम् ।
जघान त्रिशिखेनाशु घोरेण निशितेन तम् ॥ १३॥

English
In the same manner, the heroic Vīrabhadra of great brilliance hit Tāraka with his sharp terrible trident. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि शक्त्या वीरभद्रं जघान समरे ततः ।
तारको दितिजाधीशः प्रबलो वीरसम्मतः ॥ १४॥

English
The powerful king of the Asuras, the heroic Tāraka, hit Vīrabhadra$^{145}$ again with spear. (14.)

[fn 145. Vīrabhadra, the chief of the gaṇas of Śiva has become a mythical Being. In the scriptures in the caves of Elephanta and Ellora he is represented with eight hands. According to tradition, he was created by Śiva to destroy Dakṣa’s sacrifice, to harry away the Gods and sages who had assembled there. Dawson: H. M.]

Sanskrit

English
Fighting each other thus they hit each other with various weapons and missiles both being equally skilful in the art of warfare. (15.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even as others stood gazing, the two of great energy continued their duel causing hair to stand on ends, with tumultuous noise. (16.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then various military bands and drums like Bheris, Mṛdaṅgas, Paṭahas, Āṇakas and Gomukhas were sounded by the soldiers terrifying those who happened to hear. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

अन्योऽन्यमतिसंरब्धौ तौ बुधाङ्गारकाविव ॥ १८॥

English
Both of them were severely wounded by the mutual hits and thrusts but still they continued their fight with added vigour like Mercury and Mars. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

तत्र गत्वा वीरभद्रमवोचस्त्वं शिवप्रियः ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing the fight between him and Vīrabhadra, you, the favourite of Śiva went there and said to Vīrabhadra, (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच
वीरभद्र महावीर गणानामग्रणीर्भवान् ।
निवर्तस्व रणादस्माद्रोचते न वधस्त्वया ॥ २०॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  “O Vīrabhadra, of great heroism, you are the leader of the Gaṇas. Please desist from this fight. Your killing him does not fit in properly”. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं निशम्य त्वद्वाक्यं वीरभद्रो गणाग्रणीः ।
अवदत्स रुषाविष्टस्त्वां तदा तु कृताञ्जलिः ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing your words, the leader of the Gaṇas Vīrabhadra became furious but spoke to you with palms joined in reverence. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्र उवाच
मुनिवर्य महाप्राज्ञ श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।
तारकं च वधिष्यामि पश्य मेऽद्य पराक्रमम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Vīrabhadra said:—*  O excellent sage, of great wisdom, listen to my weighty words. I will kill Tāraka. See my exploit today. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
आनयन्ति च ये वीराःस्वामिनं रणसंसदि ।
ते पापिनो महाक्लीबा विनश्यन्ति रणं गताः ॥ २३॥

English
The soldiers who bring their masters to the battlefield are sinners. They are no better than eunuchs. They are doomed in the battle. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
असद्गतिं प्राप्नुवन्ति तेषां च निरयो ध्रुवम् ।
वीरभद्रो हि विज्ञेयो न वाच्यस्ते कदाचन ॥ २४॥

English
They will go the way of the wicked. Hell is definitely in store for them. Vīrabhadra should never be considered by you as such a despicable person. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शस्त्रास्त्रैर्भिन्नगात्रा ये रणं कुर्वन्ति निर्भयाः ।
इहामुत्र प्रशंस्यास्ते लभन्ते सुखमद्भुतम् ॥ २५॥

English
Those whose bodies are rent and split with weapons and missiles, but who still fight fearlessly shall be praised here and hereafter. They derive wonderful happiness. (25.)

---

Sanskrit

अतारकां महीमद्य करिष्ये स्वामिवर्जितः ॥ २६॥

English
Let Viṣṇu and other gods listen to my words—I shall make the earth freed of Tāraka today even without bringing my master here. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Saying thus and taking up his trident, Vīrabhadra mentally meditated on Śiva and fought with Tāraka, accompanied by Pramathas. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
With many heroic soldiers riding on bulls, wielding the tridents and possessing three eyes he shone well in the midst of that battle. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Keeping Vīrabhadra at their head, and shouting fearlessly jubilantly, hundreds of the Gaṇas fought with the Asuras. (29.)

Sanskrit

The Asuras too, the dependants of the demon Tāraka, all equally strong and heroic, began to smash the Gaṇas furiously. (30.)

The terrific mutual fights between the demons and the Gaṇas happened again and again. Ultimately the Gaṇas, experts in the use of great missiles, came out victorious and were jubilant. (31.)

Sanskrit

English
Defeated by the Gaṇas of great strength, the Asuras turned their faces and began to flee. They were distressed and agitated. (32.)

Sanskrit
