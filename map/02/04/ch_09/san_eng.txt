### **CHAPTER NINE - *(The boasting of Tāraka and the fight between him and Indra, Viṣṇu, Vīrabhadra)***

---

Sanskrit
९

ब्रह्मोवाच
देवदेव गुह स्वामिन् शाङ्करे पार्वतीसुत ।
न शोभते रणो विष्णुतारकासुरयोर्वृथा ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Guha, O lord of gods, O son of Śiva and Pārvatī, the fight between Viṣṇu and Tāraka is not proper. It is futile. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुना न हि वध्योऽसौ तारको बलवानति ।
मया दत्तवरस्तस्मात्सत्यं सत्यं वदाम्यहम् ॥ २॥

English
Tāraka the powerful cannot be killed by Viṣṇu. He has been granted such a boon by me. It is truth. I am telling you the truth. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नान्यो हन्तास्य पापस्य त्वां विना पार्वतीसुत ।
तस्मात्त्वया हि कर्तव्यं वचनं मे महाप्रभो ॥ ३॥

English
O son of Pārvatī, none except you can be the slayer of this sinner. O great lord, my words shall be carried out by you. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्नद्धो भव दैत्यस्य वधायाशु परन्तप ।
तद्वधार्थं समुत्पन्नः शङ्करात्त्वं शिवासुत ॥ ४॥

English
O scorcher of enemies, please get ready to slay him. O son of Pārvatī you are born of Śiva for killing that demon. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्ष रक्ष महावीर त्रिदशान् व्यथितान् रणे ।
न बालस्त्वं युवा नैव किं तु सर्वेश्वरः प्रभुः ॥ ५॥

English
O great hero, save the gods distressed in the battle. You are neither a boy nor a youth but the lord of all. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्रं पश्य तथा विष्णुं व्याकुलं च सुरान् गणान् ।
एनं जहि महादैत्यं त्रैलोक्यं सुखितं कुरु ॥ ६॥

English
See Indra and Viṣṇu. They are agitated and distressed, So also the gods and the Gaṇas. Slay this great demon. Make the three worlds happy. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेन विजितश्चेन्द्रो लोकपालैः पुरा सह ।
विष्णुश्चापि महावीरो तर्जितस्तपसो बलात् ॥ ७॥

English
Formerly Indra and the guardians of the quarters had been conquered by him. Due to the power of his penance, the heroic Viṣṇu too has been threatened by him. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रैलोक्यं निर्जितं सर्वमसुरेण दुरात्मना ।
इदानीं तव सान्निध्यात्पुनर्युद्धं कृतं च तैः ॥ ८॥

English
The entire universe of the three worlds has been defeated by this wicked Asura. Now, because of your presence, they have fought again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वया निहन्तव्यस्तारकः पापपूरुषः ।
अन्यवध्यो न चैवायं मद्वराच्छङ्करात्मज ॥ ९॥

English
Hence, O son of Śiva, this sinful being Tāraka shall be killed by you. Due to the boon granted by me he cannot be slain by any one else." (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा मम वचः कुमारः शङ्करात्मजः ।
विजहास प्रसन्नात्मा तथास्त्विति वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥ १०॥

English
— *Brahmā said:—*  On hearing these words of mine, Kumāra, son of Śiva, was delighted and he laughed. "So be it", said he. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विनिश्चित्यासुरवधं शाङ्करिःस महाप्रभुः ।
विमानादवतीर्याथ पदातिरभवत्तदा ॥ ११॥

English
Resolving to kill the Asura, the great lord, son of Śiva got down from the aerial chariot and stood on the ground. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पद्भ्यां तदासौ परिधावमानो
रेजेऽतिवीरः शिवजः कुमारः ।
करे समादाय महाप्रभां तां
शक्तिं महोल्कामिव दीप्तिदीप्ताम् ॥ १२॥

English
Running on foot, seizing his lustrous spear blazing like a meteor, the powerful warrior Kumāra born of Śiva shone well. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तमायान्तमतिप्रचण्ड-
मव्याकुलं षण्मुखमप्रमेयम् ।
दैत्यो बभाषे सुरसत्तमान्स
कुमार एष द्विषतां प्रहन्ता ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing the incomprehensible six-headed deity coming forward, fierce and unagitated, the Asura spoke to the gods derisively—“O this child indeed will slay the enemies !” (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेन साकं ह्यहमेकवीरो
योत्स्ये च सर्वानहमेव वीरान् ।
गणांश्च सर्वानपि घातयामि
सलोकपालान् हरिनायकांश्च ॥ १४॥

English
I will fight with him single-handed. I will kill the soldiers, the Gaṇas and the guardians of the quarters led by Viṣṇu. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवमुक्त्वा स तदा महाबलः
कुमारमुद्दिश्य ययौ च योद्धुम् ।
जग्राह शक्तिं परमाद्भुतां च
स तारको देववरान् बभाषे ॥ १५॥

English
Saying thus, the powerful Asura rushed at Kumāra to fight with him. Tāraka seized his wonderful spear and spoke to the gods. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तारक उवाच
कुमारो मेऽग्रतश्चाद्य भवद्भिश्च कथं कृतः ।
यूयं गतत्रपा देवा विशेषाच्छक्रकेशवौ ॥ १६॥

English
# *Tāraka said :—*  “How is it that you all kept Kumāra face to face with me? You gods are shameless especially Indra and Viṣṇu. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरैताभ्यां कृतं कर्म विरुद्धं वेदमार्गतः ।
तच्छृणुध्वं मया प्रोक्तं वर्णयामि विशेषतः ॥ १७॥

English
Formerly, both of them had acted in violation of the Vedic path. Listen. I shall describe the same. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र विष्णुश्छली दोषी ह्यविवेकी विशेषतः ।
बलिर्येन पुरा बद्धश्छलमाश्रित्य पापतः ॥ १८॥

English
Viṣṇu is deceptive, defective and indiscreet. It was by him that Bali$^{147}$ was formerly bound by taking recourse to deception with sinful intention. (18.)

[fn 147. Bali, the son of Virocana, was an Asura-chief. He was deceived by Viṣṇu in the form of a dwarfish Brahmin. The latter asked Bali to grant him three steps of ground and Bali consenting, the dwarf stepped and covered heaven and earth in two strides. However he desisted from taking the third step and left the nether region for Bali’s sake.]


---

Sanskrit
तेनैव यत्नतः पूर्वमसुरौ मधुकैटभौ ।
शिरोहीनौ कृतौ धौर्त्याद्वेदमार्गो विवर्जितः ॥ १९॥

English
The Asuras Madhu and Kaitabha$^{148}$ were beheaded by his roguishness. He forsook the Vedic path. (19.)

[fn 148. Madhu and Kaitabha were the two demons sprung from the ear of Viṣṇu while he was asleep at the end of a Kalpa. They were about to kill Brahmā but were treacherously killed by Viṣṇu and thrown into the sea.]


---

Sanskrit

English

[fn 149. In the guise of an enchantress, Viṣṇu deprived Asuras of their right of drinking nectar produced from churning the ocean.]

Sanskrit

English
Taking birth as Rāma he killed a woman (Tāḍakā). Bāli's death was brought about by him with a vile trick. A brahmin descendant of Viśravas was killed by him.¹⁵⁰ Thus he violated the injunction of the Śruti. (21.)

[fn 150. This refers to the slaying of the woman Tāḍakā and the monkey chief Bāli as well as the Brāhmaṇa King Rāvaṇa by Rāma, the son of Daśaratha, the seventh incarnation of Viṣṇu.]


---

Sanskrit

English

[fn 152. The event may refer to Paraśurāma who severed a tusk of Gaṇeśa, the son of his preceptor.]

Sanskrit

तत्त्रिशूलप्रहारेण स पपात क्षितौ तदा ।
पतितोऽपि महातेजास्तारकः पुनरुत्थितः ॥ ४८॥

English
Hit by the trident he fell on the ground. Though he fell down, Tāraka of mighty splendour got up again. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा क्रोधं महावीरःसकलासुरनायकः ।
जघान परया शक्त्या वीरभद्रं तदोरसि ॥ ४९॥

English
The great hero, the leader of the entire host of Asuras hit Vīrabhadra in his chest with his great spear. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रोऽपि पतितो भूतले मूर्छितः क्षणम् ।
तच्छक्त्या परया क्रोधान्निहतो वक्षसि ध्रुवम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Vīrabhadra, hit by the spear furiously in his chest, fell unconscious on the ground. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सगणाश्चैव देवास्ते गन्धर्वोरगराक्षसाः ।
हाहाकारेण महता चुक्रुशुश्च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ५१॥

English
The gods, the Gaṇas, Gandharvas, Serpents and Rākṣasas lamented frequently with cries of “Alas” “Alas.” (51.)

---

Sanskrit
निमेषमात्रात्सहसा महौजाः
स्स वीरभद्रो द्विषतां निहन्ता ।
त्रिशूलमुद्यम्य तडित्प्रकाशं
जाज्वल्यमानं प्रभया विरेजे ॥ ५२॥

English
Within a moment, the powerful Vīrabhadra, the slayer of enemies, got up lifting his trident aloft, that had the lustre of lightning and was blazing forth. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वरोचिषा भासितदिग्वितानं
सूर्येन्दुबिम्बाग्निसमानमण्डलम् ।
महाप्रभं वीरभयावहं परं
कालाख्यमत्यन्तकरं महोज्ज्वलम् ॥ ५३॥

English
The trident had a halo around, like that of the sun, the moon and the fire. It illuminated the quarters by means of its brilliance; caused terror even in the hearts of the brave. It had a deadly splendour and blazed well. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्त्रिशूलेन तदा हन्तुकामो महाबलः ।
वीरभद्रोऽसुरं तावत्कुमारेण निवारितः ॥ ५४॥

English
When the powerful Vīrabhadra was about to hit the Asura with his trident, he was prevented by Kumāra. (54.)
