### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - (The Victory of Kumāra and the death of Bāṇa and Pralamba)**

---

Sanskrit
११

ब्रह्मोवाच
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र क्रौञ्चनामाचलो मुने ।
आजगाम कुमारस्य शरणं बाणपीडितः ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O sage, in the meantime the mountain Krauñca, harassed by Bāṇa came there and sought refuge in Kumāra. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पलायमानो यो युद्धादसोढा तेज ऐश्वरम् ।
तुतोदातीव स क्रौञ्चं कोट्यायुतबलान्वितः ॥ २॥

English
This Bāṇa had been fleeing from the previous battle, unable to bear the brilliance of the lord. He with the army of ten thousand persons, inflicted pain on Krauñca with the tip of his missiles. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणिपत्य कुमारस्य स भक्त्या चरणाम्बुजम् ।
प्रेमनिर्भरया वाचा तुष्टाव गुहमादरात् ॥ ३॥

English
The mountain Krauñca devoutly bowed at the lotus-like feet of Kumāra and eulogised him with reverence with words full of love. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रौञ्च उवाच
कुमार स्कन्द देवेश तारकासुरनाशक ।
पाहि मां शरणापन्नं बाणासुरनिपीडितम् ॥ ४॥

English
Krauñca said:—  O Kumāra, O Skanda, O lord of gods, O slayer of the Asura Tāraka protect me who have sought refuge in you. I am harassed by the Asura Bāṇa. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्गरात्ते महासेन समुच्छिन्नः पलायितः ।
न्यपीडयच्च माऽऽगत्य हा नाथ करुणाकर ॥ ५॥

English
O Mahāsena, O lord, O merciful one, routed and uprooted from the battle with you he came and harassed me. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पीडितस्ते शरणमागतोऽहं सुदुःखितः ।
पलायमानो देवेश शरजन्मन् दयां कुरु ॥ ६॥

English
Afflicted by him I have run from him and sought refuge in you. O lord of gods, born amongst the reeds, be merciful. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यं तं नाशय विभो बाणाह्वं मां सुखीकुरु ।
दैत्यघ्नस्त्वं विशेषेण देवावनकरः स्वराट् ॥ ७॥

English
O lord, please slay the Asura Bāṇa. Make me happy. You are the slayer of Asuras and a special saviour of the gods. You are a self-ruler. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति क्रौञ्चस्तुतःस्कन्दः प्रसन्नो भक्तपालकः ।
गृहीत्वा शक्तिमतुलां स्वां सस्मार शिवो धिया ॥ ८॥

English
Brahmā said:—  Skanda who was thus eulogised by Krauñca became delighted. He, the saviour of the devotees, took up his matchless spear and remembered Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चिक्षेप तां समुद्दिश्य स बाणं शङ्करात्मजः ।
महाशब्दो बभूवाथ जज्वलुश्च दिशो नभः ॥ ९॥

English
The son of Śiva hurled the spear aiming at Bāṇa. It gave loud report, blazing forth the quarters and the sky. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सबलं भस्मसात्कृत्वासुरं तं क्षणमात्रतः ।
गुहोपकण्ठं शक्तिः सा जगाम परमा मुने ॥ १०॥

English
O sage, reducing the Asuras to ashes along with his army in a trice, the great spear returned to Kumāra. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कुमारः प्रोवाच क्रौञ्चं गिरिवरं प्रभुः ।
निर्भयः स्वगृहं गच्छ नष्टः स सबलोऽसुरः ॥ ११॥

English
The lord Kumāra told Krauñca, the chief of the mountains, “Go home fearlessly. That Asura has been slain along with his army.” (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा स्वामिवचनं मुदितो गिरिराट् तदा ।
स्तुत्वा गुहं तदारातिं स्वधाम प्रत्यपद्यत ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing the words of the lord, the delighted lord of the mountains eulogised Kumāra the slayer of his enemy and went back to his abode. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्कन्दो महेशस्य मुदा स्थापितवान्मुने ।
त्रीणि लिङ्गानि तत्रैव पापघ्नानि विधानतः ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, with great pleasure and observing the rules Skanda installed three phallic emblems of Śiva that quell all sins. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रतिज्ञेश्वरनामादौ कपालेश्वरमादरात् ।
कुमारेश्वरमेवाथ सर्वसिद्धिप्रदं त्रयम् ॥ १४॥

English
The first is called Pratijñesvara, the second Kapālesvara and the last Kumāresvara. The three are capable of conferring all the achievements. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनःसर्वेश्वरस्तत्र जयस्तम्भसमीपतः ।
स्तम्भेश्वराभिधं लिङ्गं गुहः स्थापितवान्मुदा ॥ १५॥

English
Thereafter Kumāra, the lord of all, joyously installed the phallic image Stambhesvara,¹⁵⁹ near the column of victory. (15.)

[fn ¹⁵⁹. The four phallic images of Śiva named Pratijñesvara, Kapālesvara, Kumāresvara and Stambhesvara were set up at Cambay, the scene of the battlefield, to commemorate the Victory of Guha over Tāraka, the Asura-chief.]


---

Sanskrit
ततः सर्वे सुरास्तत्र विष्णुप्रभृतयो मुदा ।
लिङ्गं स्थापितवन्तस्ते देवदेवस्य शूलिनः ॥ १६॥

English
Then Viṣṇu and other gods joyously installed the phallic emblem of Śiva, the lord of the gods. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां शिवलिङ्गानां महिमाभूत्तदाद्भुतः ।
सर्वकामप्रदश्चापि मुक्तिदो भक्तिकारिणाम् ॥ १७॥

English
The glory of the phallic emblems of Śiva was marvellous; conferring all cherished desires and salvation to the devotees. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सर्वे सुरा विष्णुप्रमुखाः प्रीतमानसाः ।
ऐच्छन्गिरिवरं गन्तुं पुरस्कृत्य गुहं मुदा ॥ १८॥

English
Then the delighted Viṣṇu and the gods desired to go to the chief of mountains joyously putting Bṛhaspati ahead. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे शेषपुत्रः कुमुदनामकः ।
आजगाम कुमारस्य शरणं दैत्यपीडितः ॥ १९॥

English
Then Kumuda¹⁶⁰ the son of Śeṣa who was harassed by the Asuras came and sought refuge in Kumāra. (19.)

[fn ¹⁶⁰. According to this account, Kumuda, the son of the serpent-chief Śeṣa, was troubled by the Asura Pralamba who was the ally of Tāraka. Kumuda slew Pralamba and relieved Kumuda of distress.

This Pralamba is distinct from the Asura of the same name whose destruction at the hands of Balarāma is recorded in the Mahābhārata.]
---

Sanskrit
प्रलम्बाख्योऽसुरो यो हि रणादस्मात्पलायितः ।
स तत्रोपद्रवं चक्रे प्रबलस्तारकानुगः ॥ २०॥

English
Another follower of Tāraka—Pralamba who had fled from the previous battle wrought great havoc with full force. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽथ शेषस्य तनयः कुमुदोऽहिपतेर्महान् ।
कुमारशरणं प्राप्तस्तुष्टाव गिरिजात्मजम् ॥ २१॥

English
Kumuda, the great son of Śeṣa the lord of serpents, sought refuge in Kumāra the son of Pārvatī and eulogised him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कुमुद उवाच
देवदेव महादेव वरतात महाप्रभो ।
पीडितोऽहं प्रलम्बेन त्वाऽहं शरणमागतः ॥ २२॥

English
*Kumuda said:—*  O excellent son of great lord, lord of the gods, O great chief, I am afflicted by Pralamba and am seeking refuge in you. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पाहि मां शरणापन्नं प्रलम्बासुरपीडितम् ।
कुमार स्कन्द देवेश तारकारे महाप्रभो ॥ २३॥

English
O Kumāra, O Skanda, O lord of the gods, O great lord, O slayer of Tāraka, save me harassed by the Asura Pralamba and seeking refuge in you. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं दीनबन्धुः करुणासिन्धुरानतवत्सलः ।
खलनिग्रहकर्ता हि शरण्यश्च सतां गतिः ॥ २४॥

English
You are the kinsman of the distressed, the ocean of mercy, favourably disposed to the devotees, the slayer of the wicked, worthy of refuge and the goal of the good. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कुमुदेनस्तुतश्चेत्थं विज्ञप्तस्तद्वधाय हि ।
स्वाश्च शक्तिं स जग्राह स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजौ ॥ २५॥

English
Eulogised thus by Kumuda and requested to slay the demon Pralamba, the lord took up his spear after remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
चिक्षेप तां समुद्दिश्य प्रलम्बं गिरिजासुतः ।
महाशब्दो बभूवाथ जज्वलुश्च दिशो नभः ॥ २६॥

English
The son of Pārvatī hurled the spear at Pralamba. It made a loud report. The quarters and the sky blazed. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तं सायुतबलं शक्तिर्द्रुतं कृत्वा च भस्मसात् ।
गुहोपकण्ठं सहसाजगामाक्लिष्टकारिणी ॥ २७॥

English
Reducing that powerful Asura to ashes in a trice the spear carried out the job without strain and returned to Kumāra. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कुमारः प्रोवाच कुमुदं नागबालकम् ।
निर्भयः स्वगृहं गच्छ नष्टः स सबलोऽसुरः ॥ २८॥

English
Then Kumāra told the Nāga child Kumuda—“Go home fearlessly. That Asura has been slain along with his army.” (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा गुहवाक्यं स कुमुदोऽहिपतेः सुतः ।
स्तुत्वा कुमारं नत्वा च पातालं मुदितो ययौ ॥ २९॥

English
On hearing the words of Guha, Kumuda, the son of the Nāga chief eulogised and bowed to Kumāra and went to Pātāla¹⁶¹ in great delight. (29.)

[fn 161. Pātāla is an island accessible through the searoute. It is an abode of the Nāgas with Bhogavatī as the capital. (cf M M K. Patala, XL P. 454) It is variously identified with Ceylon in the mid-ocean. G P. 1. 69. 24), Indo-China and old Annam. See Avasthi, Studies in Sk. P. P. 113.]

---

Sanskrit
एवं कुमारविजयं वर्णितं ते मुनीश्वर ।
चरितं तारकवधं परमाश्चर्यकारकम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Thus the story¹⁶² of the victory of Kumāra, including the wonderful way in which Tāraka was slain, has been narrated by me, O noble sage. (30.)

[fn 162. As ŚP states, the victory of Kumāra over the Asura Tāraka is a factual happening (vṛttam), while V. S. Agrawal insists on the symbolic interpretation of the legend. According to him Tāraka is the Āsuric form in the individual which remains in contact with the matter and is soiled by it. This form is suppressed and sublimated by Kumāra who is the symbol of Śakti quickened by Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
सर्वपापहरं दिव्यं सर्वकामप्रदं नृणाम् ।
धन्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं सताम् ॥ ३१॥

English
It is the divine story that removes all sins. It bestows all desires on men. It is conducive to the increase of wealth, glory and longevity. It confers worldly pleasures and salvation on the good. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ये कीर्तयन्ति सुयशोऽमितभाग्ययुता नराः ।
कुमारचरितं दिव्यं शिवलोकं प्रयान्ति ते ॥ ३२॥

English
Those who recite this divine story of Kumāra and glorify him are infinitely fortunate and attain Śivaloka. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रोष्यन्ति ये च तत्कीर्तिं भक्त्या श्रद्धान्विता जनाः ।
मुक्तिं प्राप्स्यन्ति ते दिव्यामिह भुक्त्वा परं सुखम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Those who listen to his glory with devotion and faith will attain divine salvation hereafter after enjoying great happiness here. (33.)
