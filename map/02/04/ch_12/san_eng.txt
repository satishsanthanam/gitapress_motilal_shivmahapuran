### **CHAPTER TWELVE - *(The story of Śiva and Pārvatī including that of Kārttikeya)***

---

Sanskrit
१२

ब्रह्मोवाच
निहतं तारकं दृष्ट्वा देवा विष्णुपुरोगमाः ।
तुष्टुवुः शाङ्करिं भक्त्या सर्वेऽन्ये मुदिताननाः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On seeing Tāraka killed, Viṣṇu and other gods, with faces refulgent with pleasure, eulogised Kārttikeya with devotion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
नमः कल्याणरूपाय नमस्ते विश्वमङ्गल ।
विश्वबन्धो नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्ते विश्वभावन ॥ २॥

English
*The gods said:—*  Obeisance to you of good features, obeisance to you who confer auspiciousness on the universe, O kinsman of the universe, obeisance be to you. Obeisance to you, O purifier of the universe. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नमोस्तु ते दानववर्यहन्त्रे
बाणासुरप्राणहराय देव ।
प्रलम्बनाशाय पवित्ररूपिणे
नमोनमः शङ्करतात तुभ्यम् ॥ ३॥

English
Obeisance to you, the slayer of the chief of the Asuras. O lord, obeisance to the slayer of the Asura Bāṇa. Obeisance to the destroyer of Pralamba. Obeisance to you of holy features. Obeisance to you, O son of Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव कर्त्ता जगतां च भर्त्ता
त्वमेव हर्त्ता शुचिज प्रसीद ।
प्रपञ्चभूतस्तव लोकबिम्बः
प्रसीद शम्भ्वात्मज दीनबन्धो ॥ ४॥

English
You alone are the creator, sustainer and annihilator of the universe. You, born of firegod, be pleased. Your cosmic image is identical with the universe. O son of Śiva, kinsman of the distressed, be pleased. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
देवरक्षाकर स्वामिन् रक्ष नः सर्वदा प्रभो ।
देवप्राणावनकर प्रसीद करुणाकर ॥ ५॥

English
O lord, protector of the gods, O lord, save us always. O merciful one, protector of gods, be pleased. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
हत्वा त्वं तारकं दैत्यं परिवारयुतं विभो ।
मोचिताः सकला देवा विपद्भ्यः परमेश्वर ॥ ६॥

English
After killing the Asura Tāraka along with his followers, O great lord, the gods have been freed from adversities by you. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच

वरान्ददावभिनवान्सर्वेभ्यः क्रमशो मुने ॥ ७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, thus eulogised by Viṣṇu and the other gods, lord Kumāra granted them fresh boons in order. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलान्निरीक्ष्य स्तुवतस्ततःस गिरिशात्मजः ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा प्रोवाच प्रददद्वरान् ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing the mountains eulogising, the son of Śiva became very glad and spoke to them after granting boons. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्कन्द उवाच
यूयं सर्वे पर्वता हि पूजनीयास्तपस्विभिः ।
कर्मिभिर्ज्ञानिभिश्चैव सेव्यमाना भविष्यथ ॥ ९॥

English
*Skanda said:—*  All of you mountains will become worthy of being worshipped by the sages and resorted to by persons following the paths of action and knowledge. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भोर्विशिष्टरूपाणि लिङ्गरूपाणि चैव हि ।
भविष्यथ न सन्देहः पर्वता वचनान्मम ॥ १०॥

English
O mountains, at my word you will be assuming the forms of phallic emblems, the special forms of Śiva. There is no doubt about it. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
योऽयं मातामहो मेऽद्य हिमवान्पर्वतोत्तमः ।
तपस्विनां महाभागः फलदो हि भविष्यति ॥ ११॥

English
My maternal grandfather, the excellent mountain Himavat, will become the fortunate bestower of fruits to ascetics. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
एवं दत्त्वा वरान् हत्वा तारकं चासुराधिपम् ।
त्वया कृताश्च सुखिनो वयं सर्वे चराचराः ॥ १२॥

English
*The gods said:—*  By killing Tāraka the lord of Asuras, and by granting bōons thus, all of us including the mobile and immobile beings have been made happy by you. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

जननी जनकौ द्रष्टुं शिवाशम्भू त्वमर्हसि ॥ १३॥

English
Now, it behoves you to go to Kailāsa with great pleasure, to the abode of Śiva in order to see your mother and father Śiva and Pārvatī. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा निखिला देवा विष्ण्वाद्याः प्राप्तशासनाः ।
कृत्वा महोत्सवं भूरि सकुमारा ययुर्गिरिम् ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  After saying thus and obtaining his permission, Viṣṇu and other gods went jubilantly to that mountain along with Kumāra. (14.)

---

Sanskrit

महामङ्गलमुत्तस्थौ जयशब्दो बभूव ह ॥ १५॥

English
When the lord Kumāra started to Kailāsa, the abode of Śiva, sounds of “Victory” arose indicating great auspiciousness. (15.)

---

Sanskrit

सर्वतोलङ्कृतं रम्यं सर्वोपरि विराजितम् ॥ १६॥

English
Kumāra got in the richly decorated aerial chariot that shone above all the things. (16.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, with great pleasure, Viṣṇu and I held the chowries aloft above the head of the lord with alertness. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

यथोचितं चतुर्दिक्षु जग्मुश्च प्रमुदास्तदा ॥ १८॥

English
Indra and other gods, rendering suitable service to Kumāra went ahead joyously flanking him on all sides. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

English
They reached Śiva’s mountain crying shouts of victory to Śiva. They entered the precincts with delight. Auspicious sounds arose. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing Śiva and Pārvatī, Viṣṇu and other gods bowed to Śiva with devotion and stood there humbly with palms joined in reverence. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Kumāra descended from the aerial chariot in all humility and bowed joyously to Śiva and Pārvatī seated on a throne. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Nārada, on seeing their beloved son Kumāra, the lordly couple Śiva and Pārvatī rejoiced much. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The great lord got up, kissed him on the head with joy, stroked him with the hand and placed him on his lap. (23.)

---

Sanskrit

English
With great affection, the highly delighted Śiva kissed the face of Kumāra, the great lord and the slayer of Tāraka. (24.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Pārvatī, too got up and placed him on her lap. Keeping him close to her head with great affection she kissed his lotus-like face. (25.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O dear Nārada, the joy of the couple—Śiva and Pārvatī who followed the worldly conventions, increased very much. (26.)

English

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्याज्ञप्तास्तदा देवा विष्ण्वाद्याः समुनीश्वराः ।
शिवां प्रणम्य सशिवं कुमारं च मुदान्विताः ॥ ५३॥

कथयन्तो यशो रम्यं शिवयोः शाङ्करेश्च तत् ।
आनन्दं परमं प्राप्य स्वधामानि ययुर्मुने ॥ ५४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O sage, thus ordered, Viṣṇu, the other gods and the sages bowed to Śiva, Pārvatī, and Kumāra joyously, and returned to their abodes in great delight singing the pleasant glory of Śiva, Pārvatī and their son. (53-54.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि शिवया सार्द्धं सगणः परमेश्वरः ।
कुमारेणयुतः प्रीत्योवास तस्मिन्गिरौ मुदा ॥ ५५॥

English
Śiva stayed on the mountain joyously along with Pārvatī, the Gaṇas and Kumāra. Lord Śiva was much pleased. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं कथितं सर्वं कौमारं चरितं मुने ।
शैवं च सुखदं दिव्यं किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ५६॥

English
Thus O sage, the divine and pleasant story of Kumāra and Śiva has been narrated to you. What else do you wish to hear? (56.)
