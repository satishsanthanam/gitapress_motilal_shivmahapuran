### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - *(The birth of Gaṇeśa)***

---

Sanskrit
१३

सूत उवाच
तारकारेरिति श्रुत्वा वृत्तमद्भुतमुत्तमम् ।
नारदः सुप्रसन्नोऽथ पप्रच्छ प्रीतितो विधिम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  On hearing the marvellously excellent story of the slayer of Tāraka thus, Nārada was highly delighted and he lovingly asked Brahmā. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच
देवदेव प्रजानाथ शिवज्ञाननिधे मया ।
श्रुतं कार्तिकसद्वृत्तममृतादपि चोत्तमम् ॥ २॥

English
Nārada said :—  O lord of gods and people, O storehouse of Śiva's cult, the excellent story of Kārttikeya, far better than nectar, has been heard by me. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अधुना श्रोतुमिच्छामि गाणेशं वृत्तमुत्तमम् ।
तज्जन्मचरितं दिव्यं सर्वमङ्गलमङ्गलम् ॥ ३॥

English
Now I wish to hear the excellent story of Gaṇeśa, the details of his divine nativity, auspicious of the auspicious. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य नारदस्य महामुनेः ।
प्रसन्नमानसो ब्रह्मा प्रत्युवाच शिवं स्मरन् ॥ ४॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing the words of Nārada the great sage, Brahmā became delighted and replied to him remembering Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
कल्पभेदाद्गणेशस्य जनिः प्रोक्ता विधेः परात् ।
शनिदृष्टं शिरश्छिन्नं सञ्चितं गाजमाननम् ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā said :—  Due to the difference of Kalpas, the story of the birth of Gaṇeśa is told in different ways. According to one account he is born of the great lord. His head looked at by Śani¹⁶³ was cut off and an elephant's head was put on him. (5.)

[fn 163. Śani (the planet Saturn) is called the evil-eyed (Krūradṛś), for his glance casts an evil effect. The present context refers to a legend of Pārvatī who proud of her son, Gaṇeśa, asked Śani to look at him. Śani looked and the child's head was burnt to ashes. Pārvatī felt greatly distressed and Brahmā offered consolation advising her to replace the head with the first she could find and that was an elephant's.]


---

Sanskrit
इदानीं श्वेतकल्पोक्ता गणेशोत्पत्तिरुच्यते ।
यत्र च्छिन्नं शिरस्तस्य शिवेन च कृपालुना ॥ ६॥

English
Now we narrate the story of the birth of Gaṇeśa in Śvetakalpa¹⁶⁴ when his head was cut off by the merciful Śiva. (6.)

[fn 164. Śvetakalpa, a short form of Śvetavārāha Kalpa, is one of the thirty Kalpas. According to the Purāṇas the legends of one Kalpa are repeated in the other. In this process some modifications in the accounts are bound to happen with the changes in the happenings. The legend of Gaṇeśa as recorded in this chapter belongs to Śvetavārāhakalpa. It is distinct from the one referred to above.]
---

Sanskrit
सन्देहो नात्र कर्तव्यः शङ्करस्सूतिकृन्मुने ।
स हि सर्वाधिपः शम्भुर्निर्गुणः सगुणोऽपि हि ॥ ७॥

English
No suspicion need be entertained, O sage. Śiva is certainly the cause of enjoyment and protection. He is the lord of all. Śiva is possessed as well as devoid of attributes. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्लीलयाखिलं विश्वं सृज्यते पाल्यते तथा ।
विनाश्यते मुनिश्रेष्ठ प्रस्तुतं श‍ृणु चादरात् ॥ ८॥

English
It is by His divine sport that the entire universe is created, sustained and annihilated. O excellent sage, listen to what is relevant to the context, with attention. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्वाहिते शिवे चात्र कैलासं च गते सति ।
कियता चैव कालेन जातो गणपतेर्भवः ॥ ९॥

English
A long time had lapsed after the marriage of Śiva and His return to Kailāsa that Gaṇeśa was born. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्नेव काले च जया च विजया सखी ।
पार्वत्या च मिलित्वा वै विचारे तत्पराभवत् ॥ १०॥

English
Once the friends Jayā and Vijayā conferred with Pārvatī and discussed. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रस्य च गणाःसर्वे शिवस्याज्ञापरायणाः ।
ते सर्वेऽप्यस्मदीयाश्च नन्दिभृङ्गिपुरस्सराः ॥ ११॥

English
All the Gaṇas of Rudra carry out the orders of Śiva. They all, Nandin, Bhṛṅgin and others are in a way our own. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमथास्ते ह्यसङ्ख्याता अस्मदीयो न कश्चन ।
द्वारि तिष्ठन्ति ते सर्वे शङ्कराज्ञापरायणाः ॥ १२॥

English
Pramathas are numerous. But none of them can be called our own. They all stand at the portals, subservient to Śiva's behests. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सर्वेप्यस्मदीयांश्च तथापि न मिलेन्मनः ।
एकश्चैवास्मदीयो हि रचनीयस्त्वयानघे ॥ १३॥

English
They also may be called our own but our mind is not in unison with them. Hence, O sinless lady, one, our own must be created. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा पार्वती देवी सखीभ्यां सुन्दरं वचः ।
हितं मेने तदा तच्च कर्तुं स्माप्यध्यवस्यति ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Goddess Pārvatī to whom this charming suggestion was made by the two friends considered it wholesome and resolved to carry it out. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कदाचिन्मज्जत्यां पार्वत्यां वै सदाशिवः ।
नन्दिनं परिभर्त्स्याथ ह्याजगाम गृहान्तरम् ॥ १५॥

English
Once when Pārvatī was taking her bath, Sadāśiva rebuked Nandin and came into the inner apartment. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आयान्तं शङ्करं दृष्ट्वाऽसमये जगदम्बिका ।
उत्तस्थौ मज्जती सा वै लज्जिता सुन्दरी तदा ॥ १६॥

English
The mother of the universe, seeing the untimely arrival of Śiva in the midst of her bath and toilet stood up. The beautiful lady was very shy then. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे देवी कौतुकेनातिसंयुता ।
तदीयं तद्वचश्चैव हितं मेने सुखावहम् ॥ १७॥

English
The goddess decided that her friend's suggestion would be conducive to her good and was so enthusiastic. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं जाते तदा काले कदाचित्पार्वती शिवा ।
विचिन्त्य मनसा चेति परमाया परेश्वरी ॥ १८॥

English
At the time when the incident occurred, Pārvatī, the great Māyā, the great goddess, thought as follows. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मदीयः सेवकः कश्चिद्भवेच्छुभतरः कृती ।
मदाज्ञया परं नान्यद्रेखामात्रं चलेदिह ॥ १९॥

English
"There must be a servant of my own who will be expert in his duties. He must not stray from my behest even a speck." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विचार्येति च सा देवी वपुषो मलसम्भवम् ।
पुरुषं निर्ममौ सा तु सर्वलक्षणसंयुतम् ॥ २०॥

English
Thinking thus the goddess created a person with all the characteristics, out of the dirt¹⁶⁵ from her body. (20.)

[fn 165. According to this account, Gaṇeśa was born of the scurf of Pārvatī's body, without the intervention of Śiva.]
---

Sanskrit
सर्वावयवनिर्दोषं सर्वावयव सुन्दरम् ।
विशालं सर्वशोभाढ्यं महाबलपराक्रमम् ॥ २१॥

English
He was spotless and handsome in every part of his body. He was huge in size and had all brilliance, strength and valour. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्त्राणि च तदा तस्मै दत्त्वा सा विविधानि हि ।
नानालङ्करणं चैव बह्वाशिषमनुत्तमाम् ॥ २२॥

मत्पुत्रस्त्वं मदीयोऽसि नान्यः कश्चिदिहास्ति मे ।
एवमुक्तः स पुरुषो नमस्कृत्य शिवां जगौ ॥ २३॥

English
She gave him various clothes and ornaments. She blessed him with benediction and said—"You are my son. You are my own. I have none else to call my own". Thus addressed the person bowed to her and said:— (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
किं कार्यं विद्यते तेऽद्य करवाणि तवोदितम् ।
इत्युक्ता सा तदा तेन प्रत्युवाच सुतं शिवा ॥ २४॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said :—*  "What is your order? I shall accomplish what you command." Thus addressed, Pārvatī replied to her son. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोवाच
हे तात श‍ृणु मद्वाक्यं द्वारपालो भवाद्य मे ।
मत्पुत्रस्त्वं मदीयोऽसि नान्यथा कश्चिदस्ति मे ॥ २५॥

English
# *Pārvatī said :—* “O dear, listen to my words. Work as my gatekeeper from today. You are my son. You are my own. It is not otherwise. There is none-else who belongs to me. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विना मदाज्ञां सत्पुत्र नैवायान्मद्गृहान्तरम् ।
कोऽपि क्वापि हठात्तात सत्यमेतन्मयोदितम् ॥ २६॥

English
O good son, without my permission, no one, by any means, shall intrude my apartment. I tell you the fact.” (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा च ददौ तस्मै यष्टिं चातिदृढां मुने ।
तदीयं रूपमालोक्य सुन्दरं हर्षमागता ॥ २७॥

English
# *Brahmā said:—* O sage, saying this, she gave him a hard stick. On seeing his handsome features she was delighted. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Out of love and mercy she embraced and kissed him. She placed him armed with a staff at her entrance as the gatekeeper. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then the son of the goddess, of great heroic power, stayed at the doorway armed with a staff with a desire to do what was good to her. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Thus placing her son at the doorway, Pārvatī began to take bath with her friends, unworried. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O excellent sage, at this very moment Śiva who is eagerly indulgent and an expert in various divine sports came near the door. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

मातुराज्ञां विना देव गम्यतां न त्वयाधुना ॥ ३२॥

English
Not knowing that he was lord Śiva, the consort of Pārvatī, Gaṇeśa said—“O sir, without my mother’s permission you shall not go in now. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
My mother has entered the bath. Where are you going now? Go away” saying thus, he took up his staff to ward him off. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing him Śiva said “O foolish fellow, whom are you forbidding? O wicked knave, don’t you know me? I am Śiva, none else”. (34.)

Sanskrit

English
Thereupon Gaṇeśa beat Śiva with the staff. Śiva expert in various sports became infuriated and spoke to his son thus. (35.)

Sanskrit

Sanskrit
