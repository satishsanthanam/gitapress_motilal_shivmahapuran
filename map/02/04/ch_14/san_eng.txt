### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - *(The Gaṇas argue and wrangle)***

---

Sanskrit
१४

ब्रह्मोवाच
गणास्ते क्रोधसम्पन्नास्तत्र गत्वा शिवाज्ञया ।
पप्रच्छुर्गिरिजापुत्रं तं तदा द्वारपालकम् ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  The infuriated Gaṇas of Śiva at his bidding went there and questioned the son of Pārvatī who stood at the gate. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणा ऊचुः
कोऽसि त्वं कुत आयातः किं वा त्वं च चिकीर्षसि ।
इतोऽद्य गच्छ दूरं वै यदि जीवितुमिच्छसि ॥ २॥

English
*Śiva’s Gaṇas said:—*  “Who are you? Whence do you come? What do you propose to do? If you have a desire to remain alive go away from here.” (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तदीयं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा गिरिजातनयः स वै ।
निर्भयो दण्डपाणिश्च द्वारपानब्रवीदिदम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing their words, the son of Pārvatī who was armed with the staff spoke to the Gaṇas as follows:— (3.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
यूयं के कुत आयाता भवन्तः सुन्दरा इमे ।
यात दूरं किमर्थं वै स्थिता अत्र विरोधिनः ॥ ४॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said:—*  O “handsome fellows, who are you? Whence have you come? Go away. Why have you come here and why do you stand in opposition to me?” (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
एवं श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य हास्यं कृत्वा परस्परम् ।
ऊचुःसर्वे शिवगणा महावीरा गतस्मयाः ॥ ५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing his words, Śiva’s Gaṇas of great heroism and arrogance laughingly spoke to one another. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
परस्परमिति प्रोच्य सर्वे ते शिवपार्षदाः ।
द्वारपालं गणेशं तं प्रत्यूचुः क्रुद्धमानसाः ॥ ६॥

English
After conferring with one another, the infuriated Pārṣadas of Śiva replied to Gaṇeśa, the doorkeeper. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणा ऊचुः
श्रूयतां द्वारपाला हि वयं शिवगणा वराः ।
त्वां निवारयितुं प्राप्ताः शङ्करस्याज्ञया विभोः ॥ ७॥

English
*Śiva's Gaṇas said:—*  "Listen. We are the excellent Gaṇas of Śiva. We are his doorkeepers. We have come here to throw you out at the bidding of lord Śiva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वामपीह गणं मत्वा न हन्यामोऽन्यथा हतः ।
तिष्ठ दूरे स्वतः त्वं च किमर्थं मृत्युमीहसे ॥ ८॥

English
Considering you too, as one of the Gaṇas, we are not going to kill you. Otherwise you would have been killed. Better stay away yourself. Why do you court death ?" (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्तोऽपि गणेशश्च गिरिजातनयोऽभयः ।
निर्भर्त्स्य शङ्करगणान्न द्वारं मुक्तवांस्तदा ॥ ९॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  Though warned thus, Gaṇeśa, the son of Pārvatī, stood fearless. He did not leave his post at the door. He rebuked Śiva's Gaṇas. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सर्वेऽपि गणाः शैवाः तत्रत्या वचनं तदा ।
श्रुत्वा तत्र शिवं गत्वा तद्वृत्तान्तमथाब्रुवन् ॥ १०॥

English
After hearing his words, the Gaṇas of Śiva went back and informed Śiva about his stand. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च तद्वचः श्रुत्वाद्भुतलीलो महेश्वरः ।
विनिर्भर्त्स्य गणानूचे निजाँल्लोकगतिर्मुने ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, on hearing their words, lord Śiva of wonderful divine sports, following the worldly conventions rebuked his Gaṇas. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच
कश्चायं वर्तते किं च ब्रवीत्यरिवदुच्छ्रितः ।
किं करिष्यत्यसद्बुद्धिः स्वमृत्युं वाञ्छति ध्रुवम् ॥ १२॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—*  "Who is this fellow ? What does he say ? He is standing there haughtily as though he is our enemy. What will that wicked knave do ? Certainly he wants to die. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
दूरतः क्रियतां ह्येष द्वारपालो नवीनकः ।
क्लीबा इव स्थितास्तस्य वृत्तं वदथ मे कथम् ॥ १३॥

English
Why ? Are you dastardly eunuchs to stand here helplessly and complain to me about him. Let this new doorkeeper be thrown out." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वामिनोक्ता गणास्ते चाद्भुतलीलेन शम्भुना ।
पुनरागत्य तत्रैव तमूचुर्द्वारपालकम् ॥ १४॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* Thus commanded by lord Śiva of wonderful sports the Gaṇas returned to the place and spoke to the doorkeeper. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणा ऊचुः ।
रे रे द्वारप कस्त्वं हि स्थितश्च स्थापितः कुतः ।
नैवास्मान्गणयस्येवं कथं जीवितुमिच्छसि ॥ १५॥

English
*Śiva's Gaṇas said:—*  O gatekeeper, who are you standing here ? Why have you been stationed here ? Why don't you care for us. How can you thus remain alive ? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारपाला वयं सर्वे स्थिताः किं परिभाषसे ।
सिंहासनगृहीतश्च श‍ृगालः शिवमीहते ॥ १६॥

English
We are here the duly appointed doorkeepers. What are you saying ? A jackal sitting on a lion's seat wishes for happiness. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तावद्गर्जसि मूर्ख त्वं यावद्गणपराक्रमः ।
नानुभूतस्त्वयात्रैव ह्यनुभूतः पतिष्यसि ॥ १७॥

English
O fool, you will roar only as long as you do not feel the brunt of our attack. Erelong you will fall by feeling the same.” (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तस्तैस्सुसङ्क्रुद्धो हस्ताभ्यां यष्टिकां तदा ।
गृहीत्वा ताडयामास गणांस्तान्परिभाषिणः ॥ १८॥

English
Thus taunted by them, Gaṇeśa became furious and took the staff with his hands and struck the Gaṇas even as they continued to speak harsh words. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाचाथ शिवापुत्रः परिभर्त्स्य गणेश्वरान् ।
शङ्करस्य महावीरान्निर्भयस्तान्गणेश्वरः ॥ १९॥

English
Then the fearless Gaṇeśa, son of Pārvatī rebuked the heroic Gaṇas of Śiva and spoke as follows:— (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवापुत्र उवाच
यात यात इतो दूरे नो चेद्वो दर्शयामि ह ।
स्वपराक्रममत्युग्रं यास्यथात्युपहास्यताम् ॥ २०॥

English
*The son of Pārvatī said:—*  “Get away. Get away. Or I shall give you a foretaste of my fierce valour. You will be the laughing-stock of all.” (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य गिरिजातनयस्य हि ।
परस्परमथोचुस्ते शङ्करस्य गणास्तदा ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing these words of Geṇeśa, the Gaṇas of Śiva spoke to one another. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणा ऊचुः ।
किं कर्तव्यं क्व गन्तव्यं क्रियते स न किं पुनः ।
मर्यादा रक्ष्यतेऽस्माभिरन्यथा किं ब्रवीति च ॥ २२॥

English
*Śiva’s Gaṇas said:—*  What shall be done? Where shall we go? Why shall we not act? Bounds of decency are observed by us. He would not have acted thus, otherwise. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
ततः शम्भुगणाःसर्वे शिवं दूरे व्यवस्थितम् ।
क्रोशमात्रं तु कैलासाद्गत्वा ते च तथाऽब्रुवन् ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Then the Gaṇas of Śiva went to Śiva who was standing at the distance of a Krośa from Kailāsa and spoke to him. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो विहस्य तान्सर्वांस्त्रिशूलकर उग्रधीः ।
उवाच परमेशो हि स्वगणान् वीरसम्मतान् ॥ २४॥

English
Śiva ridiculed them all. The trident-armed great lord of fierce temperament spoke to his Gaṇas who professed to be heroes. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच
रे रे गणाः क्लीबमता न वीरा वीरमानिनः ।
मदग्रे नोदितुं योग्या भर्त्सितः किं पुनर्वदेत् ॥ २५॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  “Hello, Gaṇas, impotent wretches, you profess to be heroic but are never so. You are unfit to stand before me and speak. If he is only taunted he will speak in similar tone again. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
गम्यतां ताड्यतां चैष यः कश्चित्प्रभवेदिह ।
बहुनोक्तेन किं चात्र दूरीकर्तव्य एव सः ॥ २६॥

English
Go and beat him. Some one among you may be competent to do so. Why should I speak more? He must be driven away.” (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति सर्वे महेशेन जग्मुस्तत्र मुनीश्वर ।
भर्त्सितास्तेन देवेन प्रोचुश्च गणसत्तमाः ॥ २७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O great sage, when rebuked thus by lord Śiva, the excellent Gaṇas went back and spoke to him. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवगणा ऊचुः ।
रेरे त्वं श‍ृणु वै बाल बलात्किं परिभाषसे ।
इतस्त्वं दूरतो याहि नो चेन्मृत्युर्भविष्यति ॥ २८॥

English
*Śiva's Gaṇas said:—*  Hello you boy there, listen. Why do you speak so arrogantly? You go away from here. If not, your death is certain. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तेषां शिवाज्ञाकारिणां ध्रुवम् ।
शिवासुतस्तदाभूत्स किं करोमीति दुःखितः ॥ २९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of Śiva's servants the son of Pārvatī became unhappy and thought "What shall I do?" (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवी तेषां तस्य च वै पुनः ।
श्रुत्वा तु कलहं द्वारि सखीं पश्येति साब्रवीत् ॥ ३०॥

English
In the meantime, the goddess heard the noise of this wrangle between the Gaṇas and the doorkeeper, then looked at her friend and spoke. "Go and see." (30.)

---

Sanskrit
समागत्य सखी तत्र वृत्तान्तं समबुध्यत ।
क्षणमात्रं तदा दृष्ट्वा गता हृष्टा शिवान्तिकम् ॥ ३१॥

English
The friend came to the door and saw them for a moment. She understood the whole matter. She was delighted and returned to Pārvatī. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा तु तत्सर्वं वृत्तं तद्यदभून्मुने ।
अशेषेण तया सख्या कथितं गिरिजाग्रतः ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, coming back she reported the matter to Pārvatī as it had occurred. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सख्युवाच
अस्मदीयो गणो यो हि स्थितो द्वारि महेश्वरि ।
निर्भर्त्सयन्ति तं वीराः शङ्करस्य गणा ध्रुवम् ॥ ३३॥

English
*The friend said:—*  O great Goddess, the heroic Gaṇas of Śiva are taunting and rebuking our own Gaṇa who is standing at the door. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवश्चैव गणाः सर्वे विना तेऽवसरं कथम् ।
प्रविशन्ति हठाद्गेहे नैतच्छुभतरं तव ॥ ३४॥

English
How do these Gaṇas and Śiva enter your apartment suddenly without looking to your convenience? This is not good for you. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्यक् कृतं ह्यनेनैव न हि कोऽपि प्रवेशितः ।
दुःखं चैवानुभूयात्र तिरस्कारादिकं तथा ॥ ३५॥

English
Even after undergoing the misery of rebuke etc. he, our Gaṇa, has done well in not allowing anyone in. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः परन्तु वाग्वादः क्रियते च परस्परम् ।
वाग्वादे च कृते नैव तर्ह्यायान्तु सुखेन वै ॥ ३६॥

English
What is more? They are arguing too. When the argument has started, they cannot come in happily. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतश्चैवात्र वाग्वादस्तं जित्वा विजयेन च ।
प्रविशन्तु तथा सर्वे नान्यथा कर्हिचित्प्रिये ॥ ३७॥

English
Now that they have started the argument let them conquer him and enter victoriously. Not otherwise, my dear friend. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मिन्नेवास्मदीये वै सर्वे सम्भर्त्सिता वयम् ।
तस्माद्देवि त्वया भद्रे न त्याज्यो मान उत्तमः ॥ ३८॥

English
When this man belonging to us is taunted, it amounts to our being taunted. Hence, O gentle lady, you shall not abandon your prestige of high order. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो मर्कटवत्तेऽद्य वर्तते सर्वदा सति ।
किं करिष्यत्यहङ्कारमानुकूल्यं भविष्यति ॥ ३९॥

English
Śiva always squeezes you like a crab, O Satī. What will he do now? His pride will take a favourable turn for us. (39.)

---

English
*Brahmā said:—*  Alas, being subservient to Śiva's wish, Pārvatī stood there for a moment. (40.)

---

English
Then taking up a haughty mood she spoke to herself. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
अहो क्षणं स्थिता तत्र शिवेच्छा वशतःसती । ४०॥

मनस्युवाच सा भूत्वा मानिनी पार्वती तदा । ४१॥

शिवोवाच
अहो क्षणं स्थितो नैव हठात्कारः कथं कृतः ।
कथं चैवात्र कर्त्तव्यं विनयेनाथ वा पुनः ॥ ४२॥

English
*Pārvatī said:—*   “Alas, he did not wait for a moment. Why should he force his way in? What shall be done now? Or shall I adopt a humble attitude. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
भविष्यति भवत्येव कृतं नैवान्यथा पुनः ।
इत्युक्त्वा तु सखी तत्र प्रेषिता प्रियया तदा ॥ ४३॥

English
What is to happen happens. What is done cannot be altered?” After saying this, Pārvatī sent her again lovingly. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
समागत्याऽब्रवीत्सा च प्रियया कथितं हि यत् ।
समाचष्ट गणेशं तं गिरिजातनयं तदा ॥ ४४॥

English
The friend came to the door and told Gaṇeśa what Pārvatī had said with affection. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सख्युवाच
सम्यक्कृतं त्वया भद्र बलात्ते प्रविशन्तु न ।
भवदग्रे गणा ह्येते किं जयन्तु भवादृशम् ॥ ४५॥

English
*The friend said:—*  O gentle sir, well done. Let them not enter forcibly. What are these Gaṇas before you? Can they win a person like you? (45.)

---

Sanskrit

जितो यस्तु पुनर्वापि न वैरमथ वा ध्रुवम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Whether good or bad let your duty be done. If you are conquered there will be no further enmity at all. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्या मातुश्चैव गणेश्वरः ।
आनन्दं परमं प्राप बलं भूरि महोन्नतिम् ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing the words of the friend and his mother Gaṇeśvara became highly delighted, strengthened and lifted up. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
बद्धकक्षस्तथोष्णीषं बद्ध्वा जङ्घोरु संस्पृशन् ।
उवाच तान् गणान् सर्वान् निर्भयं वचनं मुदा ॥ ४८॥

English
Girting up his loins, tying his turban firmly and clapping his calves and thighs, he spoke fearlessly to all the Gaṇas. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
अहं च गिरिजासूनुर्यूयं शिवगणास्तथा ।
उभये समतां प्राप्ताः कर्तव्यं क्रियतां पुनः ॥ ४९॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said:—*  I am the son of Pārvatī. You are the Gaṇas of Śiva. Both of us are thus equal. Let your duty be done, now. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
भवन्तो द्वारपालाश्च द्वारपोऽहं कथं न हि ।
भवन्तश्च स्थितास्तत्राऽहं स्थितोऽत्रेति निश्चितम् ॥ ५०॥

English
You are all doorkeepers. How is it that I am not? You are standing there and I am standing here. This is certain. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
भवद्भिश्च स्थितं ह्यत्र यदा भवति निश्चितम् ।
तदा भवद्भिः कर्त्तव्यं शिवाज्ञापरिपालनम् ॥ ५१॥

English
When it is certain that you are standing here, you must carry out the directions of Śiva. (51.)

English
O heroes, now I have to carry out the orders of Pārvatī faithfully. I have decided what is proper. (52.)

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छिवगणाःसर्वे वचनं श‍ृणुतादरात् ।
हठाद्वा विनयाद्वा न गन्तव्यं मन्दिरे पुनः ॥ ५३॥

English
Hence, O Gaṇas of Śiva, you shall listen with attention. You shall not enter the apartment either forcibly or humbly. (53.)

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्तास्ते गणेनैव सर्वे ते लज्जिता गणाः ।
ययुः शिवान्तिकं तं वै नमस्कृत्य पुरः स्थिताः ॥ ५४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  The Gaṇas when decisively told by Gaṇeśa became ashamed. They went to Śiva. After bowing to him they stood in front of him. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थित्वा न्यवेदयन्सर्वे वृत्तान्तं च तदद्भुतम् ।
करौ बद्ध्वा नतस्कन्धाः शिवं स्तुत्वा पुरः स्थिताः ॥ ५५॥

English
Then they acquainted him with that news of wonderful nature. They joined their palms, stooped their shoulders, eulogised Śiva and stood in front of him. (55.)

Sanskrit
तत्सर्वं तु तदा श्रुत्वा वृत्तं तत्स्वगणोदितम् ।
लौकिकीं वृत्तिमाश्रित्य शङ्करो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ५६॥

English
On hearing the detailed news mentioned by his Gaṇas, Śiva replied following the worldly conventions. (56.)

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच
श्रूयतां च गणाःसर्वे युद्धं योग्यं भवेन्न हि ।
यूयं चात्रास्मदीया वै स च गौरीगणस्तथा ॥ ५७॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  O Gaṇas, hear you all. A battle may not be a proper course. You are all my own. He is Pārvatī's Gaṇa. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
विनयः क्रियते चेद्वै वश्यः शम्भुः स्त्रिया सदा ।
इति ख्यातिर्भवेल्लोके गर्हिता मे गणा ध्रुवम् ॥ ५८॥

English
But if we are going to be humble, there is likely to be a rumour: "Śiva is subservient to his wife." O Gaṇas, this is certainly derogatory to me. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
कृते चैवात्र कर्तव्यमिति नीतिर्गरीयसी ।
एकाकी स गणो बालः किं करिष्यति विक्रमम् ॥ ५९॥

English
The policy of meeting an action with another (Tit for tat) is a weighty one. That single-handed Gaṇa is a mere boy. What valour can be expected of him ? (59.)

Sanskrit
भवन्तश्च गणा लोके युद्धे चाति विशारदाः ।
मदीयाश्च कथं युद्धं हित्वा यास्यथ लाघवम् ॥ ६०॥

English
O Gaṇas, you are all experts in warfare and reputed to be so in the world. You are my own men. How can you forsake war and demean yourselves ? (60.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियाऽऽग्रहः कथं कार्यो पत्युरग्रे विशेषतः ।
कृत्वा सा गिरिजा तस्य नूनं फलमवाप्स्यति ॥ ६१॥

English
How can a woman be obdurate especially with her husband ? Pārvatī will certainly derive the fruit of what she has done. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वे च मद्वीराः श‍ृणुतादरतो वचः ।
कर्त्तव्यं सर्वथा युद्धं भावि यत्तद्भवत्विति ॥ ६२॥

English
Hence, my heroic men, listen to my words with attention. This war has to be fought by all means. Let what is in store happen." (62.)

ब्रह्मोवाच

विरराम मुनिश्रेष्ठ दर्शयँल्लौकिकीं गतिम् ॥ ६३॥

English
