### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - (Gaṇeśa's battle)**

---

Sanskrit
१५

ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्ता विभुना तेन निश्चयं परमं गताः ।
सन्नद्धास्तु तदा तत्र जग्मुश्च शिवमन्दिरम् ॥ १॥

English
Brahmā said:—  When Śiva told them thus, they came to a decisive resolution. They got ready and went to Śiva's palace. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेशोऽपि तथा दृष्ट्वा ह्यायातान्गणसत्तमान् ।
युद्धाऽऽटोपं विधायैव स्थितांश्चैवाब्रवीदिदम् ॥ २॥

English
On seeing the excellent Gaṇas, fully equipped for war, coming, Gaṇeśa spoke thus to them. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
आयान्तु गणपाः सर्वे शिवाज्ञापरिपालकाः ।
अहमेकश्च बालश्च शिवाज्ञापरिपालकः ॥ ३॥

English
Gaṇeśa said:—  Welcome to the leaders of Gaṇas, carrying out the behests of Śiva. I am only one and that too a mere boy carrying out the directions of Pārvatī. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि पश्यतां देवी पार्वती सूनुजं बलम् ।
शिवश्च स्वगणानां तु बलं पश्यतु वै पुनः ॥ ४॥

English
Yet let the goddess see the strength of her son. Let Śiva see the strength of his Gaṇas too. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
बलवद्बालयुद्धं च भवानीशिवपक्षयोः ।
भवद्भिश्च कृतं युद्धं पूर्वं युद्धविशारदैः ॥ ५॥

English
The fight between the parties of Pārvatī and Śiva is the one between a strong army and a boy. You are all experts in warfare and have fought in many a battle. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मया पूर्वं कृतं नैव बालोऽस्मि क्रियतेऽधुना ।
तथापि भवतां लज्जा गिरिजाशिवयोरिह ॥ ६॥

English
I have never fought in a battle before. I am a mere boy. I am going to fight now. Still if you are put to shame, it will be shameful to Śiva and Pārvatī. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ममैवं तु भवेन्नैव वैपरीत्यं भविष्यति ।
ममैव भवतां लज्जा गिरिजाशिवयोरिह ॥ ७॥

English
But that will not happen to me. If I am put to shame, the contrary will happen to me. Pārvatī and Śiva will be put to shame but not I. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं ज्ञात्वा च कर्त्तव्यः समरश्च गणेश्वराः ।
भवद्भिः स्वामिनं दृष्ट्वा मया च मातरं तदा ॥ ८॥

English
O leader of the Gaṇas, the war shall be fought after realising this. You shall look up to your lord and I to my mother. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रियते कीदृशं युद्धं भवितव्यं भवत्विति ।
तस्य वै वारणे कोऽपि न समर्थस्त्रिलोकके ॥ ९॥

English
What sort of a fight shall be fought? Let what is destined to occur, occur. No one in the three worlds can ward it off. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्येवं भर्त्सितास्ते तु दण्डभूषितबाहवः ।
विविधान्यायुधान्येवं धृत्वा ते च समाययुः ॥ १०॥

English
Brahmā said:—  When thus taunted and rebuked they rushed towards him with big batons, decorating their arms and taking up different kinds of weapons. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
घर्षयन्तस्तथा दन्तान् हुङ्कृत्य च पुनः पुनः ।
पश्य पश्य ब्रुवन्तश्च गणास्ते समुपागताः ॥ ११॥

English
Gnashing their teeth, grunting and bellowing and calling out "See, See", the Gaṇas rushed at him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दी प्रथममागत्य धृत्वा पादं व्यकर्षयत् ।
धावन्भृङ्गी द्वितीयं च पादं धृत्वा गणस्य च ॥ १२॥

English
Nañdin came first and caught hold of his leg. He pulled at it. Bhṛṅgin then rushed at him and caught hold of his other leg. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्पादे विकर्षन्तौ तावद्धस्तेन वै गणः ।
आहत्य हस्तयोस्ताभ्यामुत्क्षिप्तौ पादकौ स्वयम् ॥ १३॥

English
Before the Gaṇas of Śiva had time to pull his legs Gaṇeśa struck a blow at their hands and got his legs free. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवीसुतो वीरस्सङ्गृह्य परिघं बृहत् ।
द्वारस्थितो गणपतिः सर्वानापोथयत्तदा ॥ १४॥

English
Then seizing a big iron club and standing at the doorway he smashed the gaṇas. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
केषाञ्चित्पाणयो भिन्नाः केषाञ्चित्पृष्ठकानि च ।
केषाञ्चिच्च शिरांस्येव केषाञ्चिन्मस्तकानि च ॥ १५॥

English
Some got their hands broken, others got their backs smothered. The heads of others were shattered and the foreheads of some were crushed. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
केषाञ्चिज्जानुनी तत्र केषाञ्चित्स्कन्धकास्तथा ।
केषाञ्चिज्जानुनी तत्र केषाञ्चित्स्कन्धास्तथा ।
सम्मुखे चागता ये वै ते सर्वे हृदये हताः ॥ १६॥

English
The knees of some were fractured, the shoulders of others were blasted. Those who came in front were hit in the chest. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिच्च पतिता भूमौ केचिच्च विदिशो गताः ।
केषाञ्चिच्चरणौ छिन्नौ केचिच्छर्वान्तिकं गताः ॥ १७॥

English
Some fell on the ground, some fled in various directions, some got their legs broken and some fled to Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां मध्ये तु कश्चिद्वै सङ्ग्रामे सम्मुखो न हि ।
सिंहं दृष्ट्वा यथा यान्ति मृगाश्चैव दिशो दश ॥ १८॥

तथा ते च गणाः सर्वे गताश्चैव सहस्रशः ।
परावृत्य तथा सोऽपि सुद्वारि समुपस्थितः ॥ १९॥

English
Now none among them stood face to face. Just as deer flee to any direction on seeing a lion, the Gaṇas, who were thousands in number fled in that manner. Then Gaṇeśa returned to doorway and stood there. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पान्तकरणे कालो दृश्यते च भयङ्करः ।
यथा तथैव दृष्टः स सर्वेषां प्रलयङ्करः ॥ २०॥

English
He was seen as the annihilator of all in the manner of Yama, the terrible god of death at the end of a Kalpa. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्समये चैव सरमेशसुरेश्वराः ।
प्रेरिता नारदेनेह देवाः सर्वे समागमन् ॥ २१॥

English
At this time, urged by Nārada, all the gods including Viṣṇu and Indra came there. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
समब्रुवंस्तदा सर्वे शिवस्य हितकाम्यया ।
पुरः स्थित्वा शिवं नत्वा ह्याज्ञां देहि प्रभो इति ॥ २२॥

English
Standing in front of Śiva and bowing to him with a desire to secure good for him they said—“O lord, be pleased to command us. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं परब्रह्म सर्वेशः सर्वे च तव सेवकाः ।
सृष्टेः कर्ता सदा भर्ता संहर्ता परमेश्वरः ॥ २३॥

English
You are the great Brahman, the lord of all, the creator, the sustainer and the annihilator of all created things. All are your servants. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
रजस्सत्त्वतमोरूपो लीलया निर्गुणः स्वतः ।
का लीला रचिता चाद्य तामिदानीं वद प्रभो ॥ २४॥

English
You are intrinsically devoid of attributes but by means of your sports you assume Rājasika, Sāttvika and Tāmasika forms. O lord, what sort of sport you are indulging in, now ?” (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां मुनिश्रेष्ठ महेश्वरः ।
गणान् भिन्नाँस्तदा दृष्ट्वा तेभ्यस्सर्वं न्यवेदयत् ॥ २५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—* O excellent sage, on hearing their words and seeing the Gaṇas completely shattered, lord Śiva told them everything. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सर्वेश्वरस्तत्र शङ्करो मुनिसत्तम ।
विहस्य गिरिजानाथो ब्रह्माणं मामुवाच ह ॥ २६॥

English
O excellent sage, Śiva, the lord of all, the consort of Pārvati, then laughingly told me, Brahmā. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच
ब्रह्मञ्छृणु मम द्वारि बाल एकः समास्थितः ।
महाबलो यष्टिपाणिर्गेहावेशनिवारकः ॥ २७॥

English
O Brahmā, listen. A boy is standing at the entrance to my house. He is very strong. He has a staff in his hand. He prevents me from entering the house. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
महाप्रहारकर्ताऽसौ मत्पार्षदविघातकः ।
पराजयः कृतस्तेन मद्गणानां बलादिह ॥ २८॥

English
He strikes very dexterously. He has destroyed many of my Pārṣadas. He has forcefully defeated my Gaṇas. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मन् त्वयैव गन्तव्यं प्रसाद्योऽयं महाबलः ।
यथा ब्रह्मन्नयः स्याद्वै तथा कार्यं त्वया विधे ॥ २९॥

English
O Brahmā, you alone should go there. This strong boy shall be propitiated. O Brahmā, you shall do everything to bring him under control. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्याकर्ण्य प्रभोर्वाक्यमज्ञात्वाऽज्ञानमोहितः ।
तदीयनिकटं तात सर्वैरृषिवरैरयाम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Brahmā said :—  On hearing the words of the lord and unable to know the reality, being deluded by ignorance, O dear, I went near Gaṇeśa accompanied by the sages. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
समायान्तं च मां दृष्ट्वा स गणेशो महाबली ।
क्रोधं कृत्वा समभ्येत्य मम श्मश्रूण्यवाकिरत् ॥ ३१॥

English
On seeing me approaching, the powerful Gaṇeśa came to me very furiously and plucked my moustache and beard. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षम्यतां क्षम्यतां देव न युद्धार्थं समागतः ।
ब्राह्मणोऽहमनुग्राह्यः शान्तिकर्तानुपद्रवः ॥ ३२॥

English
“Forgive me. Forgive me, O lord. I have not come for fighting. I am a brahmin and shall be blessed. I have come to make peace and I will cause no harm.” (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं ब्रुवति ब्रह्मंस्तावत्परिघमाददे ।
स गणेशो महावीरो बालोऽबालपराक्रमः ॥ ३३॥

English
While I said thus, O brahmin, the heroic Gaṇeśa, the boy of great valour uncommon to the boys took up the iron club. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीतपरिघं दृष्ट्वा तं गणेशं महाबलम् ।
पलायनपरो यातस्त्वहं द्रुततरं तदा ॥ ३४॥

English
On seeing the powerful Gaṇeśa seizing the iron club I began to run away immediately. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यात यात ब्रुवन्तस्ते परिघेन हतास्तदा ।
स्वयं च पतिताः केचित्केचित्तेन निपातिताः ॥ ३५॥

English
The others too who were shouting “Go, Go” were struck down with the iron club. Some fell themselves and some were felled by him. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिच्च शिवसामीप्यं गत्वा तत्क्षणमात्रतः ।
शिवं विज्ञापयाञ्चक्रुस्तद्वृत्तान्तमशेषतः ॥ ३६॥

English
Some of them fled to Śiva in a trice and intimated to him the details of the incident. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तथाविधांश्च तान् दृष्ट्वा तद्वृत्तान्तं निशम्य सः ।
अपारमादधे कोपं हरो लीलाविशारदः ॥ ३७॥

English
On seeing them in that plight and on hearing the news, Śiva, an adept in sports became very angry. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रादिकान्देवगणान् षण्मुखप्रवरान् गणान् ।
भूतप्रेतपिशाचांश्च सर्वानादेशयत्तदा ॥ ३८॥

English
He issued directives to Indra and other gods, to the Gaṇas led by the six-faced Kumāra and to goblins, ghosts and spirits. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सर्वे च यथायोग्यं गतास्ते सर्वतो दिशम् ।
तं गणं हन्तुकामा हि शिवाज्ञप्ता उदायुधाः ॥ ३९॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva they all desired to kill Gaṇeśa. Lifting up their weapons in a suitable manner they came there from all directions. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य यस्यायुधं यच्च तत्तत्सर्वं विशेषतः ।
तद् गणेशोपरि बलात्समागत्य विमोचितम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Whatever weapon they had was hurled on Gaṇeśa with force. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारो महानासीत् त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ।
त्रिलोकस्था जनाः सर्वे संशयं परमं गताः ॥ ४१॥

English
There was a great hue and cry in all the three worlds consisting of the mobile and immobile. The inhabitants of the worlds were in a great fix and uncertainty. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
न यातं ब्रह्मणोऽप्यायुर्ब्रह्माण्डं क्षयमेति हि ।
अकाले च तथा नूनं शिवेच्छावशतः स्वयम् ॥ ४२॥

English
“Brahmā’s life span is not over, but the whole universe is undergoing untimely destruction. Certainly it is due to Śiva’s wish. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सर्वे चागतास्तत्र षण्मुखाद्याश्च ये पुनः ।
देवा व्यर्थायुधा जाता आश्चर्यं परमं गताः ॥ ४३॥

English
The sixfaced deity and the other gods who came there failed to use their weapons effectively. They were very much surprised. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवी जगदम्बा विबोधना ।
ज्ञात्वा तच्चरितं सर्वमपारं क्रोधमादधे ॥ ४४॥

English
In the meantime, goddess, the mother of the universe, of special knowledge, came to know of the entire incident and was very furious. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिद्वयं तदा तत्र तया देव्या मुनीश्वर ।
निर्मितं स्वगणस्यैव सर्वसाहाय्यहेतवे ॥ ४५॥

English
O great sage, the goddess created two Śaktis¹⁶⁶ then and there for the assistance of her own Gaṇa. (45.)

[fn 166. One is the terrible aspect of the Goddess personified as Kālī, Caṇḍī and Bhairavī, the other a beautiful yellow form named Durgā with several hands and riding on a tiger in a fierce and minacing attitude.]


---

Sanskrit
एका प्रचण्डरूपं च धृत्वातिष्ठन्महामुने ।
श्यामपर्वतसङ्काशं विस्तीर्य मुखगह्वरम् ॥ ४६॥

English
O great sage, one Śakti assumed a very fierce form and stood there opening her mouth as wide as the cavern of a dark mountain. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
एका विद्युत्स्वरूपा च बहुहस्तसमन्विता ।
भयङ्करा महादेवी दुष्टदण्डविधायिनी ॥ ४७॥

English
The other assumed the form of lightning. She wore many arms. She was a huge and terrible goddess ready to punish the wicked. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
आयुधानि च सर्वाणि मोचितानि सुरैर्गणैः ।
गृहीत्वा स्वमुखे तानि ताभ्यां शीघ्रं च चिक्षिपे ॥ ४८॥

English
The weapons hurled by the gods and the Gaṇas were caught in the mouth and hurled back at them. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
देवायुधं न दृश्येत परिघः परितः पुनः ।
एवं ताभ्यां कृतं तत्र चरितं परमाद्भुतम् ॥ ४९॥

English
None of the weapons of the gods was seen anywhere around the iron club of Gaṇeśa. This wonderful feat was performed by them. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
एको बालोऽखिलं सैन्यं लोडयामास दुस्तरम् ।
यथा गिरिवरेणैव लोडितः सागरः पुरा ॥ ५०॥

English
A single boy stirred and churned the vast impassable army in the same manner as great mountain¹⁶⁷ churned the ocean of milk formerly. (50.)

[fn 167. The term ‘girivara’ the best of the mountain refers to the fabulous mountain Meru that was used for churning the ocean.]
---

Sanskrit
एकेन निहताः सर्वे शक्राद्या निर्जरास्तथा ।
शङ्करस्य गणाश्चैव व्याकुला अभवंस्तदा ॥ ५१॥

English
Indra and other gods were struck by him, single-handed. The Gaṇas of Śiva became agitated and distressed then. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सर्वे मिलित्वा ते निःश्वस्य च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
परस्परं समूचुस्ते तत्प्रहारसमाकुलाः ॥ ५२॥

English
Gasping frequently for their breath, being utterly shaken by his blows they gathered together and spoke to one another. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
देवगणा ऊचुः
किं कर्तव्यं क्व गन्तव्यं न ज्ञायन्ते दिशो दश ।
