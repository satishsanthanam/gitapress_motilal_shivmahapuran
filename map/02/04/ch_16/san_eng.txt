### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - *(The head of Gaṇeśa is chopped off during the battle)***

---

Sanskrit
१६

ब्रह्मोवाच
इति श्रुत्वा महेशानो भक्तानुग्रहकारकः ।
त्वद्वाचा युदकामोऽभूत्तेन बालेन नारद ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  O Nārada, on hearing your words, the great lord who grants benediction to his devotees became desirous of fighting with the boy. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुमाहूय सम्मन्त्र्य बलेन महता युतः ।
सामरः सम्मुखस्तस्याप्यभूद्देवस्त्रिलोचनः ॥ २॥

English
He called Viṣṇu and consulted him. Then with a great army and the gods, He, the three-eyed lord, stood face to face with him. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च युयुधुस्तेन स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ।
महाबला महोत्साहाश्शिवसद्दृष्टिलोकिताः ॥ ३॥

English
After remembering the lotuslike feet of Śiva, the gods possessing great strength, kindly glanced at by Śiva and highly jubilant, fought with him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
युयुधेऽथ हरिस्तेन महाबलपराक्रमः ।
महादेव्यायुधो वीरः प्रवणः शिवरूपकः ॥ ४॥

English
Viṣṇu of great strength, valour and skill and possessing great divine weapons and Śivā's form fought with him. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यष्ट्या गणाधिपः सोऽथ जघानामरपुङ्गवान् ।
हरिं च सहसा वीरः शक्तिदत्तमहाबलः ॥ ५॥

English
Gaṇeśa hit all the chief gods with his staff. He hit Viṣṇu too, all of a sudden. The hero had been conferred great strength by the Śaktis. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेऽमरगणास्तत्र विकुण्ठितबला मुने ।
अभूवन् विष्णुना तेन हता यष्ट्या पराङ्मुखाः ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, all the gods including Viṣṇu were hit by him with the stick. They were turned back with their strength sapped. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि सह सैन्येन युद्धं कृत्वा चिरं मुने ।
विकरालं च तं दृष्ट्वा विस्मयं परमं गतः ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, after fighting for a long time along with the army and seeing him terrific, even Śiva was greatly surprised. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
छलेनैव च हन्तव्यो नान्यथा हन्यते पुनः ।
इति बुद्धिं समास्थाय सैन्यमध्ये व्यवस्थितः ॥ ८॥

English
Thinking within himself "He has to be killed only by deception and not otherwise" he stayed in the midst of the army. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवे दृष्टे तदा देवे निर्गुणे गुणरूपिणि ।
विष्णौ चैवाथ सङ्ग्रामे आयाते सर्वदेवताः ॥ ९॥

गणाश्चैव महेशस्य महाहर्षं तदा ययुः ।
सर्वे परस्परं प्रीत्या मिलित्वा चक्रुरुत्सवम् ॥ १०॥

English
When lord Śiva who though devoid of attributes had assumed the attributive form was seen in the battle, when Viṣṇu too had come thither, the gods and Gaṇas of Śiva were highly delighted. They joined together and became jubilant. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शक्तिसुतो वीरो वीरगत्या स्वयष्टितः ।
प्रथमं पूजयामास विष्णुं सर्वसुखावहम् ॥ ११॥

English
Then Gaṇeśa the heroic son of Śakti following the course of heroes, at first worshipped (i.e struck) Viṣṇu with his staff, Viṣṇu who confers happiness to all. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं च मोहयिष्यामि हन्यतां च त्वया विभो ।
छलं विना न वध्योऽयं तामसोऽयं दुरासदः ॥ १२॥

इति कृत्वा मतिं तत्र सुसम्मन्त्र्य च शम्भुना ।
आज्ञां प्राप्याऽभवच्छैवीं विष्णुर्मोहपरायणः ॥ १३॥

English
"I shall cause him delusion. Then let him be killed by you, O lord. Without deception he cannot be killed. He is of Tāmasika nature and inaccessible." Thinking thus and consulting Śiva, Viṣṇu secured Śiva's permission and was engaged in the activities of delusion. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिद्वयं तथा लीनं हरिं दृष्ट्वा तथाविधम् ।
दत्त्वा शक्तिबलं तस्मै गणेशायाभवन्मुने ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, on seeing Viṣṇu in that manner, the two Śaktis handed over their power to Gaṇeśa and became submerged. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिद्वयेऽथ संलीने यत्र विष्णुः स्थितः स्वयम् ।
परिघं क्षिप्तवांस्तत्र गणेशो बलवत्तरः ॥ १५॥

English
When the two Śaktis became submerged, Gaṇeśa with more strength infused in him hurled the iron club in the place where Viṣṇu stood. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा यत्नं किमप्यत्र वञ्चयामास तद्गतिम् ।
शिवं स्मृत्वा महेशानं स्वप्रभुं भक्तवत्सलम् ॥ १६॥

English
Viṣṇu strenuously dodged the same after remembering Śiva, the great lord, favourably disposed towards His devotees. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एकतस्तन्मुखं दृष्ट्वा शङ्करोऽप्याजगाम ह ।
स्वत्रिशूलं समादाय सुक्रुद्धो युद्धकाम्यया ॥ १७॥

English
Seeing his face on a side, the infuriated Śiva took up his trident with a desire to fight and came there. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स ददर्शागतं शम्भुं शूलहस्तं महेश्वरम् ।
हन्तुकामं निजं वीरः शिवापुत्रो महाबलः ॥ १८॥

English
Pārvatī's son of great strength and heroism, saw Śiva arrived there with desire to fight him to a finish, the great lord with the trident in his hand. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्त्या जघान तं हस्ते स्मृत्वा मातृपदाम्बुजम् ।
स गणशो महावीरः शिवशक्तिप्रवर्द्धितः ॥ १९॥

English
Gaṇeśa, the great hero, who had been rendered more powerful by Pārvatī and the Śaktis remembered the lotuslike feet of his mother and struck him in his hand with his Śakti. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिशूलं पतितं हस्ताच्छिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
दृष्ट्वा सदूतिकस्तं वै पिनाकं धनुराददे ॥ २०॥

English
Thereupon the trident fell from the hand of Śiva of supreme soul. Seeing this, Śiva the source of great enjoyment and protection took up his bow Pināka. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तमप्यपातयद्भूमौ परिघेण गणेश्वरः ।
हताः पञ्च तथा हस्ताः पञ्चभिश्शूलमाददे ॥ २१॥

English
Gaṇeśa felled that to the ground by means of his iron club. Five of his hands too were struck. He took up the trident with the other five hands. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो दुःखतरं नूनं सञ्जातमधुना मम ।
भवेत्पुनर्गणानां किं भवाचारो जगाविति ॥ २२॥

English
"Alas, this has been more distressing even to me. What may not happen to the Gaṇas? Śiva who followed the worldly conventions cried out like this. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे वीरः परिघेण गणेश्वरः ।
जघान सगणान् देवान् शक्तिदत्तबलान्वितः ॥ २३॥

English
In the meantime the heroic Gaṇeśa endowed with the surplus power bestowed by the Śaktis struck the gods and the Gaṇas with his iron club. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
गता दशदिशो देवाः सगणाः परिघार्दिताः ।
न तस्थुःसमरे केऽपि तेनाद्भुतप्रहारिणा ॥ २४॥

English
The gods and the Gaṇas smothered by that wonderful striker with the iron club went away to the ten directions. None of them remained in the battlefield. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुस्तं च गणं दृष्ट्वा धन्योऽयमिति चाब्रवीत् ।
महाबलो महावीरो महाशूरो रणप्रियः ॥ २५॥

बहवो देवताश्चैव मया दृष्टास्तथा पुनः ।
दानवा बहवो दैत्या यक्षगन्धर्वराक्षसाः ॥ २६॥

नैतेन गणनाथेन समतां यान्ति केऽपि च ।
त्रैलोक्येऽप्यखिले तेजो रूपशौर्यगुणादिभिः ॥ २७॥

English
On seeing Gaṇeśa, Viṣṇu said—"He is blessed. He is a great hero of great strength. He is valorous and fond of battle. Many gods, Dānavas, Daityas, Yakṣas, Gandharvas, and Rākṣasas I have seen. In the entire extent of the three worlds, none of them can equal Gaṇeśa in regard to brilliance, form, features, valour and other qualities." (25-27.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सम्ब्रुवतेऽमुष्मै परिघं भ्रामयन् स च ।
चिक्षेप विष्णवे तत्र शक्तिपुत्रो गणेश्वरः ॥ २८॥

English
Gaṇeśa, son of the Śaktis whirled the iron club and hurled it at Viṣṇu even as he was saying so. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रं गृहीत्वा हरिणा स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ।
तेन चक्रेण परिघो द्रुतं खण्डीकृतस्तदा ॥ २९॥

English
After remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva, Viṣṇu took up his discus and split the iron club by means of discus. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
खण्डं तु परिघस्यापि हरये प्राक्षिपद्गणः ।
गृहीत्वा गरुडेनापि पक्षिणा विफलीकृतः ॥ ३०॥

English
Gaṇeśa hurled the piece of iron club at Viṣṇu which was caught by the bird Garuḍa and rendered futile. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विचरितं कालं महावीरावुभावपि ।
विष्णुश्चापि गणश्चैव युयुधाते परस्परम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Thus for a long time the two heroes Viṣṇu and Gaṇeśa fought with each other. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्वीरवरः शक्तिसुतःस्मृतशिवो बली ।
गृहीत्वा यष्टिमतुलां तया विष्णुं जघान ह ॥ ३२॥

English
Again the foremost among heroes, the son of Pārvatī took up his staff of unrivalled power remembering Śiva and struck Viṣṇu with it. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अविषह्य प्रहारं तं स भूमौ निपपात ह ।
द्रुतमुत्थाय युयुधे शिवापुत्रेण तेन वै ॥ ३३॥

English
Struck with that unbearable blow he fell on the ground. But he got up, quickly and fought with Pārvatī's son. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदन्तरमासाद्य शूलपाणिस्तथोत्तरे ।
आगत्य च त्रिशूलेन तच्छिरो निरकृन्तत ॥ ३४॥

English
Securing this opportunity, the Trident-bearing deity came there and cut off his head with his trident. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
छिन्ने शिरसि तस्यैव गणनाथस्य नारद ।
गणसैन्यं देवसैन्यमभवच्च सुनिश्चलम् ॥ ३५॥

English
O Nārada, when the head of Gaṇeśa was cut off, the armies of the gods and the Gaṇas stood still. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदेन त्वयाऽऽगत्य देव्यै सर्वं निवेदितम् ।
मानिनि श्रूयतां मानस्त्याज्यो नैव त्वयाधुना ॥ ३६॥

English
You, Nārada, then came and acquainted Pārvatī with the matter—“O proud woman, listen. You shall not cast off your pride and prestige.” (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वाऽन्तर्हितस्तत्र नारद त्वं कलिप्रियः ।
अविकारी सदा शम्भुर्मनोगतिकरो मुनिः ॥ ३७॥

English
O Nārada, saying this, you, fond of quarrels, vanished from there. You are the unchanging sage and a follower of the inclinations of Śiva. (37.)
