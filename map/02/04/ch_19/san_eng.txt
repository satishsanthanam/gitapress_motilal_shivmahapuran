### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - *(Gaṇapati's marriage)***

---

Sanskrit
१९

नारद उवाच
गणेशस्य श्रुता तात सम्यग्जनिरनुत्तमा ।
चरित्रमपि दिव्यं वै सुपराक्रमभूषितम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—* O dear father, the excellent story of the nativity and the divine conduct embellished by valour, of Gaṇeśa has been heard well. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः किमभवत्तात तत्त्वं वद सुरेश्वर ।
शिवाशिवयशस्स्फीतं महानन्दप्रदायकम् ॥ २॥

English
O dear father, O lord of gods, what happened thereafter? Please narrate it. The great glory of Pārvatī and Śiva confers great delight. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
साधु पृष्टं मुनिश्रेष्ठ भवता करुणात्मना ।
श्रूयतां दत्तकर्णं हि वक्ष्येऽहं ऋषिसत्तम ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* O excellent sage, you have asked well with a sympathetic mind. Listen attentively, O excellent sage, I shall narrate. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवा शिवश्च विप्रेन्द्र द्वयोश्च सुतयोः परम् ।
दर्शं दर्शं च तल्लीलां महत्प्रेम समावहत् ॥ ४॥

English
O excellent brahmin, seeing frequently the divine sports of both the sons, Pārvatī and Śiva had their love increased. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पित्रोर्लालयतोस्तत्र सुखं चाति व्यवर्द्धत ।
सदा प्रीत्या मुदा चातिखेलनं चक्रतुः सुतौ ॥ ५॥

English
The happiness of the parents knew no bounds. The son too used to sport in joy and love. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तावेव तनयौ तत्र मातापित्रोर्मुनीश्वर ।
महाभक्त्या यदा युक्तौ परिचर्यां प्रचक्रतुः ॥ ६॥

English
O great sage, the sons rendered great service to their parents with great devotion. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
षण्मुखे च गणेशे च पित्रोस्तदधिकं सदा ।
स्नेहो व्यवर्द्धत महान् शुक्लपक्षे यथा शशी ॥ ७॥

English
The love and affection of the parents towards the six-faced lord and Gaṇeśa increased to a great extent like the moon in the bright half of the month. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्तौ स्थितौ तत्र रहसि प्रेमसंयुतौ ।
शिवा शिवश्च देवर्षे सुविचारपरायणौ ॥ ८॥

English
O celestial sage, once the loving parents Pārvatī and Śiva held a secret talk and discussion. (8.)

---

English
They thought that the two sons had attained to marriageable age and how best their marriage should be celebrated now. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाशिवावूचतुः
विवाहयोग्यौ सञ्जातौ सुताविति च तावुभौ ॥

विवाहश्च कथं कार्यः पुत्रयोरुभयोः शुभम् । ९॥

षण्मुखश्च प्रियतमो गणेशश्च तथैव च ।
इति चिन्तासमुद्विग्नौ लीलानन्दौ बभूवतुः ॥ १०॥

English
The sixfaced lord Kārttikeya was their great beloved son. Gaṇeśa too was likewise. Thinking thus they were worried as well as delighted. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपित्रोर्मतमाज्ञाय तौ सुतावपि संस्पृहौ ।
तदिच्छया विवाहार्थं बभूवतुरथो मुने ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, coming to know of their parents’ opinion, the sons too were eager to get married.

“I shall marry, I shall marry” saying thus to each other they always quarrelled with each other. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा तद्वचनं तौ च दम्पती जगतां प्रभू ।
लौकिकाचारमाश्रित्य विस्मयं परमं गतौ ॥ १३॥

English
The couple, the rulers of the worlds, on hearing their words, were very much surprised, following the worldly conventions. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कर्तव्यं कथं कार्यो विवाहविधिरेतयोः ।
इति निश्चित्य ताभ्यां वै युक्तिश्च रचिताद्भुता ॥ १४॥

English
A wonderful expedient was devised by them after thinking about the course to be followed in the celebration of their marriage. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्समये स्थित्वा समाहूय स्वपुत्रकौ ।
कथयामासतुस्तत्र पुत्रयोः पितरौ तदा ॥ १५॥

English
Once they called the sons to them and spoke as follows. (15.)

---

English
*Śiva and Pārvatī said:—*  O good sons, we have framed the rules conducive to your happiness. Listen lovingly. We shall tell you the truth. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्माकं नियमः पूर्वं कृतश्च सुखदो हि वाम् ॥

श्रूयतां सुसुतौ प्रीत्या कथयावो यथार्थकम् । १६॥

समौ द्वावपि सत्पुत्रौ विशेषो नात्र लभ्यते ।
तस्मात्पणः कृतः शन्दः पुत्रयोरुभयोरपि ॥ १७॥

English
Both of you are good sons and equal in our eyes. There is no difference. Hence a condition that is beneficial to both of you has been made. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्चैव पृथिवीं सर्वां क्रान्त्वा पूर्वमुपाव्रजेत् ।
तस्यैव प्रथमं कार्यो विवाहः शुभलक्षणः ॥ १८॥

English
The auspicious marriage will be celebrated of that boy who comes here first after going round the entire earth. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तयोरेवं वचः श्रुत्वा शरजन्मा महाबलः ।
जगाम मन्दिरात्तूर्णं पृथिवीक्रमणाय वै ॥ १९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  On hearing their words, the powerful Kumāra started immediately from the fixed point in order to go round the earth. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
गणनाथश्च तत्रैव संस्थितो बुद्धिसत्तमः ।
सुबुद्ध्या संविचार्येति चित्त एव पुनः पुनः ॥ २०॥

English
Gaṇeśa of excellent intellect stood there itself after pondering in his mind frequently with his keen intellect. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कर्तव्यं क्व गन्तव्यं लङ्घितुं नैव शक्यते ।
क्रोशमात्रं गतः स्याद्वै गम्यते न मया पुनः ॥ २१॥

English
"What shall be done? Where am I to go? I cannot cross the earth. At best it may be possible to go a Krośa. I cannot go beyond it. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
किं पुनः पृथिवीमेतां क्रान्त्वा चोपार्जितं सुखम् ।
विचार्येति गणेशस्तु यच्चकार श‍ृणुष्व तत् ॥ २२॥

English
What avails that happiness which is achieved after going round the earth?" Please listen to what Gaṇeśa did after thinking thus. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नानं कृत्वा यथान्यायं समागत्य स्वयं गृहम् ।
उवाच पितरं तत्र मातरं पुनरेव सः ॥ २३॥

English
He performed the ceremonial ablution and returned home. He then spoke to his father and mother. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
आसने स्थापिते ह्यत्र पूजार्थं भवतोरिह ।
भवन्तौ संस्थितौ तातौ पूर्य्यतां मे मनोरथः ॥ २४॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said:—*  For your worship two seats have I placed here. Please be seated, dear parents. Let my desire be fulfilled.

*Brahmā said:—* (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य पार्वतीपरमेश्वरौ ।
अस्थातामासने तत्र तत्पूजाग्रहणाय वै ॥ २५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing his words, Pārvatī and Śiva sat on the seats for receiving worship. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तेनाथ पूजितौ तौ च प्रक्रान्तौ च पुनः पुनः ।
एवं च कृतवान् सप्त प्रणामांस्तु तथैव सः ॥ २६॥

English
They were worshipped by him and circumambulated seven times and bowed too seven times. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
बद्धाञ्जलिरथोवाच गणेशो बुद्धिसागरः ।
स्तुत्वा बहुतिथस्तात पितरौ प्रेमविह्वलौ ॥ २७॥

English
Joining his palms in reverence and eulogising his parents agitated by love and affection, many times, Gaṇeśa the ocean of intelligence, spoke thus. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
भो मातर्भो पितस्त्वं च श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।
शीघ्रं चैवात्र कर्तव्यो विवाहः शोभनो मम ॥ २८॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said:—*  "O mother, O father, you please listen to my weighty words. My auspicious marriage shall be celebrated quickly." (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा गणेशस्य महात्मनः ।
महाबुद्धिनिधिं तं तौ पितरावूचतुस्तदा ॥ २९॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing the words of the noble-minded Gaṇeśa, the parents spoke to him, the storehouse of great intellect. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाशिवावूचतुः
प्रक्रामेत भवान्सम्यक् पृथिवीं च सकाननाम् ।
कुमारो गतवांस्तत्र त्वं गच्छ पुर आव्रज ॥ ३०॥

English
*Śiva and Pārvatī said:—*  You shall circumambulate the earth with all its forests. Kumāra has already gone. You too start and return first. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा पित्रोर्गणपतिर्द्रुतम् ।
उवाच नियतस्तत्र वचनं क्रोधसंयुतः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing the words of his parents, Gaṇeśa spoke immediately and furiously but with some restraint. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
भो मातर्भो पितर्धर्मरूपौ प्राज्ञौ युवां मतौ ।
धर्मतः श्रूयतां सम्यक् वचनं मम सत्तमौ ॥ ३२॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said:—*  O mother, O father, you two are intelligent and embodied virtue. Hence O excellent ones, you may be pleased to hear my virtuous words. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
मया तु पृथिवी क्रान्ता सप्तवारं पुनः पुनः ।
एवं कथं ब्रुवाते वै पुनश्च पितराविह ॥ ३३॥

English
The earth has been circumambulated by me frequently, for seven times. Why then, my parents should say thus? (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तद्वचस्तु तदा श्रुत्वा लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितौ ।
महालीलाकरौ तत्र पितरावूचतुश्च तम् ॥ ३४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing his words, the sportively inclined parents, following the worldly conventions spoke to him thus— (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पितरावूचतुः
कदा क्रान्ता त्वया पुत्र पृथिवी सुमहत्तरा ।
सप्तद्वीपा समुद्रान्ता महद्भिर्गहनैर्युता ॥ ३५॥

English
*The parents said:—*  “O son, when was the great earth circumambulated by you, the earth consisting of seven continents$^{168}$ extending to the oceans and consisting of vast jungles? (35.)

[fn $^{168}$. On the seven continents, see S. M. Ali, Geography of the Purāṇas, Ch. II.]
---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
तयोरेवं वचः श्रुत्वा शिवाशङ्करयोर्मुने ।
महाबुद्धिनिधिः पुत्रो गणेशो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ३६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* O sage, on hearing the words of Pārvatī and Śiva, Gaṇeśa, the storehouse of great intellect spoke thus. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच
भवतोः पूजनं कृत्वा शिवाशङ्करयोरहम् ।
स्वबुद्ध्या हि समुद्रान्तपृथ्वीकृतपरिक्रमः ॥ ३७॥

English
*Gaṇeśa said:—*  By worshipping you, Pārvatī and Śiva, I have intelligently circumambulated the earth extending to the oceans. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं वचनं वेदे शास्त्रे वा धर्मसञ्चये ।
वर्त्तते किं च तत्तथ्यं नहि किं तथ्यमेव वा ॥ ३८॥

English
Is it not the verdict of the Vedas or the Śāstras or any other sacred code? Is it true or otherwise? (38.)

---

Sanskrit
पित्रोश्च पूजनं कृत्वा प्रक्रान्तिं च करोति यः ।
तस्य वै पृथिवीजन्यं फलं भवति निश्चितम् ॥ ३९॥

English
“He who worships his parents and circumambulates them, will certainly derive the fruit and merit of circumambulating the earth. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अपहाय गृहे यो वै पितरौ तीर्थमाव्रजेत् ।
तस्य पापं तथा प्रोक्तं हनने च तयोर्यथा ॥ ४०॥

English
He who leaves his parents at home and goes on a pilgrimage incurs the sin of their murder. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रस्य च महत्तीर्थं पित्रोश्चरणपङ्कजम् ।
अन्यतीर्थं तु दूरे वै गत्वा सम्प्राप्यते पुनः ॥ ४१॥

English
The holy centre of a son consists of the lotus-like feet of his parents. The other holy centres can be reached only after going a long distance. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं सन्निहितं तीर्थं सुलभं धर्मसाधनम् ।
पुत्रस्य च स्त्रियाश्चैव तीर्थं गेहे सुशोभनम् ॥ ४२॥

English
This holy centre is near at hand, easily accessible and a means of virtue. For a son and wife, the auspicious holy centre is in the house itself." (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इति शास्त्राणि वेदाश्च भाषन्ते यन्निरन्तरम् ।
भवद्भ्यां तत्प्रकर्त्तव्यमसत्यं पुनरेव च ॥ ४३॥

English
These things are mentioned frequently in the Śāstras and the Vedas. Now, are they going to be falsified by you? (43.)

---

Sanskrit
भवदीयं त्विदं रूपमसत्यं च भवेदिह ।
तदा वेदोऽप्यसत्यो वै भवेदिति न संशयः ॥ ४४॥

English
If so, your very forms will come false. Even the Vedas will become false. There is no doubt about it. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
शीघ्रं च भवितव्यो मे विवाहः क्रियतां शुभः ।
अथ वा वेदशास्त्रं च व्यलीकं कथ्यतामिति ॥ ४५॥

English
Let my auspicious marriage be celebrated and that too very quickly. Otherwise let the Vedas and Śāstras be declared false. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वयोः श्रेष्ठतमं मध्ये यत्स्यात्सम्यग्विचार्य तत् ।
कर्तव्यं च प्रयत्नेन पितरौ धर्मरूपिणौ ॥ ४६॥

English
Of the two alternatives whatever is excellent shall be followed, O parents, embodied virtues! (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा पार्वतीपुत्रः स गणेशः प्रकृष्टधीः ।
विरराम महाज्ञानी तदा बुद्धिमतां वरः ॥ ४७॥

English
*Brahmā said: —*  Saying thus, Gāṇeśa of excellent intellect, of great wisdom and foremost among intelligent persons assumed silence. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
तौ दम्पती च विश्वेशौ पार्वतीशङ्करौ तदा ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य विस्मयं परमं गतौ ॥ ४८॥

English
On hearing his words, Pārvatī and Śiva, the rulers of the universe, were very much surprised. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवा शिवश्चैव पुत्रं बुद्धिविचक्षणम् ।
सम्प्रशस्योचतुः प्रीत्या तौ यथार्थप्रभाषिणम् ॥ ४९॥

English
Then, Śiva and Pārvatī praised their son who was clever and intelligent and spoke to him who had spoken the truth. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाशिवावूचतुः
पुत्र ते विमला बुद्धिः समुत्पन्ना महात्मनः ॥

त्वयोक्तं यद्वचश्चैव ततश्चैव च नान्यथा ॥ ५०॥

English
*Śiva and Pārvatī said :—*  O son, you are a supreme soul and your thoughts are pure. What you have said is true and not otherwise. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
समुत्पन्ने च दुःखे च यस्य बुद्धिर्विशिष्यते ।
तस्य दुखं विनश्येत सूर्ये दृष्टे यथा तमः ॥ ५१॥

English
When misfortune comes, if a person is keenly intelligent, his misfortunes perish even as darkness perishes when the sun rises. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
बुद्धिर्यस्य बलं तस्य निर्बुद्धेस्तु कुतो बलम् ।
कूपे सिंहो मदोन्मत्तः शशकेन निपातितः ॥ ५२॥

English
He who has intelligence possesses strength as well. How can he who is devoid of intellect have strength? The proud lion was drowned in a well with a trick by a little hare.¹⁶⁹ (52.)

[fn 169. This verse introduces the story of the lion and the hare in the Pañcatantra. —]
---

Sanskrit
वेदशास्त्रपुराणेषु बालकस्य यथोदितम् ।
त्वया कृतं तु तत्सर्वं धर्मस्य परिपालनम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Whatever has been mentioned in the Vedas, Śāstras and Purāṇas for a boy, all that has been performed by you, namely, the observance of virtue. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्यक्कृतं त्वया यच्च तत्केनापि भवेदिह ।
आवाभ्यां मानितं तच्च नान्यथा क्रियतेऽधुना ॥ ५४॥

English
What has been executed by you shall be done by anyone. We have honoured it. It will not be altered now. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा तौ समाश्वास्य गणेशं बुद्धिसागरम् ।
विवाहकरणे चास्य मतिं चक्रतुरुत्तमाम् ॥ ५५॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  After saying this and appeasing Gaṇeśa, the ocean of intelligence, they resolved to perform his marriage. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं च परिणेष्यामि ह्यहं चैव पुनः पुनः ।
परस्परं च नित्यं वै विवादे तत्परावुभौ ॥ १२॥
