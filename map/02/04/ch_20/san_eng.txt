### **CHAPTER TWENTY - *(The celebration of Gaṇeśa's marriage)***

---

Sanskrit
२०

ब्रह्मोवाच
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र विश्वरूपः प्रजापतिः ।
तदुद्योगं संविचार्य सुखमाप प्रसन्नधीः ॥ १॥

English
*Brahmā said :—*  In the meantime Prajāpati Viśvarūpa became delighted and happy on knowing their intention. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वरूपप्रजेशस्य दिव्यरूपे सुते उभे ।
सिद्धिबुद्धिरिति ख्याते शुभे सर्वाङ्गशोभने ॥ २॥

English
Prajāpati Viśvarūpa had two daughters of divine features. They were famous as Siddhi and Buddhi.$^{170}$ They were exquisite in every part of their body. (2.)

[fn 170. Siddhi and Buddhi were the two daughters of the progenitor Viśvarūpa and these were married to Gaṇeśa. Siddhi gave birth to Kṣema and Lābha was born of Buddhi.]
---

Sanskrit
ताभ्यां चैव गणेशस्य गिरिजा शङ्करः प्रभू ।
महोत्सवं विवाहं च कारयामासतुर्मुदा ॥ ३॥

English
The lord Śiva and Pārvatī, jubilantly celebrated the marriage of Gaṇeśa with them. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तुष्टा देवताः सर्वास्तद्विवाहे समागमन् ।
यथा चैव शिवस्यैव गिरिजाया मनोरथः ॥ ४॥

English
The delighted gods attended their marriage as desired by Śiva and Pārvatī. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा च विश्वकर्माऽसौ विवाहं कृतवांस्तथा ।
तथा च ऋषयो देवा लेभिरे परमां मुदम् ॥ ५॥

English
Viśvakarman made all arrangements for the marriage. The sages and the gods were full of great joy. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेशोऽपि तदा ताभ्यां सुखं परमदुर्लभम् ।
प्राप्तवांश्च मुने तत्तु वर्णितुं नैव शक्यते ॥ ६॥

English
The happiness that Gaṇeśa derived by virtue of this marriage, O sage, cannot be adequately described. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कियता चैव कालेन गणेशस्य महात्मनः ।
द्वयोः पत्न्योश्च द्वौ दिव्यौ तस्य पुत्रौ बभूवतुः ॥ ७॥

English
After some time, the noble Gaṇeśa begot two sons, one each of his wives. They were endowed with divine features. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सिद्धेर्गणेशपत्न्यास्तु क्षेमनामा सुतोऽभवत् ।
बुद्धेर्लाभाभिधः पुत्रो ह्यासीत्परमशोभनः ॥ ८॥

English
The son Kṣema was born to Siddhi. The highly brilliant son Lābha was born to Buddhi. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सुखमचिन्त्यं वै भुञ्जाने हि गणेश्वरे ।
आजगाम द्वितीयश्च क्रान्त्वा पृथ्वीं सुतस्तदा ॥ ९॥

English
While Gaṇeśa was enjoying the inconceivable happiness, the second son returned after circumambulating the earth. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तावश्च नारदेनैव प्राप्तो गेहे महात्मना ।
यथार्थं वच्मि नोऽसत्यं न छलेन न मत्सरात् ॥ १०॥

English
Thereupon he was addressed by Nārada, the great soul. "I am speaking the truth, no lies. I am not actuated by deception or rivalry. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृभ्यां तु कृतं यच्च शिवया शङ्करेण ते ।
तन्न कुर्यात्परो लोके सत्यं सत्यं ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ११॥

English
What has been done by Śiva and Pārvatī your parents, no other person in the world will ever do. Truth. It is the truth I am speaking. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्कास्य त्वां कुक्रमणं मिषमुत्पाद्य यत्नतः ।
गणेशस्य वरोऽकारि विवाहः परशोभनः ॥ १२॥

English
After driving you out under the pretext of circum-ambulating the earth, they have celebrated the excellent and auspicious marriage of Gaṇeśa. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेशस्य कृतोद्वाहो लब्धवांस्त्रीद्वयं मुदा ।
विश्वरूपप्रजेशस्य कन्यारत्नं महोत्तमम् ॥ १३॥

English
By this marriage that was celebrated, Gaṇeśa has obtained two wives joyously. They are the excellent daughters of Prajāpati Viśvarūpa. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रद्वयं ललाभासौ द्वयोः पत्न्योः शुभाङ्गयोः ।
सिद्धेः क्षेमं तथा बुद्धेर्लाभं सर्वसुखप्रदम् ॥ १४॥

English
He has begot of his two wives of auspicious body two sons, Kṣema of Siddhi and Lābha of Buddhi. They bestow happiness on every one. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्न्योर्द्वयोर्गणेशोऽसौ लब्ध्वापुत्रद्वयं शुभम् ।
मातापित्रोर्मतेनैव सुखं भुङ्क्ते निरन्तरम् ॥ १५॥

English
Begetting two sons of auspicious features of his wives Gaṇeśa is continuously enjoying happiness as conceived by your parents. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
भवता पृथिवी क्रान्ता ससमुद्रा सकानना ।
तच्छलाज्ञावशात्तात तस्य जातं फलं त्विदम् ॥ १६॥

English
The entire earth consisting of oceans and jungles has been traversed by you due to their deceptive behest. O dear, this is the result of that. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृभ्यां हि कृतं यत्तु छलं तात विचार्यताम् ।
स्वस्वामिभ्यां विशेषेण ह्यन्यः किन्न करोति वै ॥ १७॥

English
O dear, consider. If parents begin to deceive or particularly if our masters begin to deceive, won't others also begin to deceive. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
असम्यक्च कृतं ताभ्यां त्वत्पितृभ्यां हि कर्म ह ।
विचार्यतां त्वयाऽपीह मच्चित्ते न शुभं मतम् ॥ १८॥

English
Your parents have not done well. Just ponder over it. I don't think their action has been good. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
दद्याद्यदि गरं माता विक्रीणीयात्पिता यदि ।
राजा हरति सर्वस्वं कस्मै किं च ब्रवीतु वै ॥ १९॥

English
If mother were to poison her son, if father were to sell his son, if the king were to confiscate the assets of his subjects what can be said and to whom ? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
येनैवेदं कृतं स्याद्वै कर्मानर्थकरं परम् ।
शान्तिकामस्सुधीस्तात तन्मुखं न विलोकयेत् ॥ २०॥

English
O dear, an intelligent peace-loving person shall never look at the face of the person who has committed this harmful deed. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इति नीतिः श्रुतौ प्रोक्ता स्मृतौ शास्त्रेषु सर्वतः ।
निवेदिता च सा तेऽद्य यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ २१॥

English
This policy has been mentioned in the Vedas, Smṛtis and sacred texts. It has been intimated to you now. Do as you wish." (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच
इत्युक्त्वा नारद त्वं तु महेश्वरमनोगतिः ।
तस्मै तथा कुमाराय वाक्यं मौनमुपागतः ॥ २२॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* O Nārada, following the mental process of lord Śiva, you spoke these words to Kumāra and then kept quiet. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स्कन्दोऽपि पितरं नत्वा कोपाग्निज्वलितस्तदा ।
जगाम पर्वतं क्रौञ्चं पितृभ्यां वारितोऽपि सन् ॥ २३॥

English
After bowing to his father, the infuriated Skanda went to the Krauñca mountain$^{171}$ though forbidden by his parents. (23.)

[fn 171. This famous hill represents part of the Kailāsa on which the Mānasarovara is situated. See Note 78 P. 629.]
---

Sanskrit
वारणे च कृते त्वद्य गम्यते च कथं त्वया ।
इत्येवं च निषिद्धोऽपि प्रोच्य नेति जगाम सः ॥ २४॥

English
"Even when forbidden by us why do you go?" Although he was prevented by saying this, he went away saying "No". (24.)

---

Sanskrit
न स्थातव्यं मया तातौ क्षणमप्यत्र किञ्चन ।
यद्येवं कपटं प्रीतिमपहाय कृतं मयि ॥ २५॥

English
"O parents, I shall not stay here even a moment when deception has been practised on me eschewing affection towards me." (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्त्वा गतस्तत्र मुने सोऽद्यापि वर्तते ।
दर्शनेनैव सर्वेषां लोकानां पापहारकः ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, he went away saying so. Even today he is staying there removing the sin of all by his very vision. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिनं हि समारभ्य कार्तिकेयस्य तस्य वै ।
शिवपुत्रस्य देवर्षे कुमारत्वं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ २७॥

English
Ever since that day, O celestial sage, the son of Śiva, Kārttikeya remains a bachelor. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्नाम शुभदं लोके प्रसिद्धं भुवनत्रये ।
सर्वपापहरं पुण्यं ब्रह्मचर्यप्रदं परम् ॥ २८॥

English
His name bestows auspiciousness in the world. It is famous in the three worlds. It dispels all sins, is meritorious and confers the sanctity of celibacy. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्तिक्यां च सदा देवा ऋषयश्च सतीर्थकाः ।
दर्शनार्थं कुमारस्य गच्छन्ति च मुनीश्वराः ॥ २९॥

English
In the month of Kārttika, the gods, the holy sages and great ascetics go there to see Kumāra. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्तिक्यां कृत्तिकासङ्गे कुर्याद्यः स्वामिदर्शनम् ।
तस्य पापं दहेत्सर्वं चित्तेप्सितफलं लभेत् ॥ ३०॥

English
He who has the vision of the lord in the Kṛttikā Nakṣatra in the month of Kārttika is divested of all sins. He derives all desired fruits. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
उमाऽपि दुःखमापन्ना स्कन्दस्य विरहे सति ।
उवाच स्वामिनं दीना तत्र गच्छ मया प्रभो ॥ ३१॥

English
Pārvatī became grief-stricken by separation from Skanda. She piteously told her lord. "O lord, let us go there." (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सुखार्थं स्वयं शम्भुर्गतः स्वांशेन पर्वते ।
मल्लिकार्जुननामासीज्ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं सुखावहम् ॥ ३२॥

English
Śiva went to that mountain partially for her happiness. He assumed the pleasing form of Jyotirlinga named Mallikārjuna.¹⁷² (32.)

[fn 172. According to the present context the Mallikārjuna Jyotirlinga was established on the Krauñca mountain. It is difficult to know how it came to be identified with the one on Śrīśaila overhanging the Kṛṣṇā river in the south.]
---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि दृश्यते तत्र शिवया सहितश्शिवः ।
सर्वेषां निजभक्तानां कामपूरः सतां गतिः ॥ ३३॥

English
Even now Śiva is seen there with Pārvatī satisfying the desires of his devotees. He is the goal of the good. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तमागतं स विज्ञाय कुमारः सशिवां शिवम् ।
स विरज्य ततोऽन्यत्र गन्तुमासीत्समुत्सुकः ॥ ३४॥

English
On coming to know that Śiva had come there with Pārvatī, Kumāra became unattached and was eager to go elsewhere. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
देवैश्च मुनिभिश्चैव प्रार्थितः सोऽपि दूरतः ।
योजनत्रयमुत्सृज्य स्थितः स्थाने च कार्तिकः ॥ ३५॥

English
On being requested by the gods and sages he stayed in a place three Yojanas away. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रस्नेहातुरौ तौ वै शिवौ पर्वणि पर्वणि ।
दर्शनार्थं कुमारस्य तस्य नारद गच्छतः ॥ ३६॥

English
O Nārada, on the full and new moon days, Pārvatī and Śiva are excited by love towards their son and they go there to see him. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अमावास्यादिने शम्भुः स्वयं गच्छति तत्र ह ।
पूर्णमासीदिने तत्र पार्वती गच्छति ध्रुवम् ॥ ३७॥

English
On new moon days, Śiva himself goes there. On full moon days, Pārvatī goes there certainly. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यत्तस्य च वृत्तान्तं भवत्पृष्टं मुनीश्वर ।
कार्तिकस्य गणेशस्य परमं कथितं मया ॥ ३८॥

English
O great sage, whatever you had asked in regard to Kārttikeya and Gaṇeśa has been narrated by me. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छ्रुत्वा नरो धीमान् सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ।
शोभनां लभते कामानीप्सितान्सकलान्सदा ॥ ३९॥

English
On hearing this, an intelligent man becomes free from all sins. He achieves all desired fruits of auspicious nature. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पठेत्पाठयेद्वापि श‍ृणुयाच्छ्रावयेत्तथा ।
सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ४०॥

English
Whoever reads, teaches, listens or narrates this story derives all desires. No doubt need be entertained in this respect. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणो ब्रह्मवर्चस्वी क्षत्रियो विजयी भवेत् ।
वैश्यो धनसमृद्धस्स्याच्छूद्रस्सत्तमतामियात् ॥ ४१॥

English
A brahmin derives brahminical splendour, a Kṣatriya becomes victorious, a Vaiśya prosperous and a Śūdra attains equality with the good. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
रोगी रोगात्प्रमुच्येत भयान्मुच्येत भीतियुक् ।
भूतप्रेतादिबाधाभ्यः पीडितो न भवेन्नरः ॥ ४२॥

English
A sick man becomes free from sickness; a frightened man becomes free from fear; no man is harassed by the visitation of goblins, ghosts etc. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदाख्यानमनघं यशस्यं सुखवर्द्धनम् ।
आयुष्यं स्वर्ग्यमतुलं पुत्रपौत्रादिकारकम् ॥ ४३॥

English
This narrative is sinless, conducive to glory and enhancer of happiness. It is conducive to longevity and attainment of heaven. It is unequalled and bestows sons and grandsons. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
अपवर्गप्रदं चापि शिवज्ञानप्रदं परम् ।
शिवाशिवप्रीतिकरं शिवभक्तिविवर्द्धनम् ॥ ४४॥

English
It confers salvation and reveals Śiva’s principles. It is pleasing to Pārvatī and Śiva and increases devotion to Śiva. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रवणीयं सदा भक्तैर्निष्कामैश्च मुमुक्षुभिः ।
शिवाद्वैतप्रदं चैतत्सदाशिवमयं शिवम् ॥ ४५॥

English
This shall always be heard by devotees and by those who seek liberation and are free from worldly desires. It confers identity with Śiva. It is conducive to welfare and is identical with Śiva himself. (45.)

॥ इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे द्वितीयरुद्रसंहितायां चतुर्थः कुमारखण्डः समाप्तः ॥ २.४॥
