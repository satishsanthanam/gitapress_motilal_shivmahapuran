### **CHAPTER THREE - *(The virtues of the Tripuras)***

---

Sanskrit
३

शिव उवाच ।
अयं वै त्रिपुराध्यक्षः पुण्यवान्वर्ततेऽधुना ।
यत्र पुण्यं प्रवर्तेत न हन्तव्यो बुधैः क्वचित् ॥ १॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  A meritorious person is the presiding ruler of the Tripuras now. He who practises meritorious deeds should not be killed by sensible persons. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
जानामि देवकष्टं च विबुधाः सकलं महत् ।
दैत्यास्ते प्रबला हन्तुमशक्यास्तु सुरासुरैः ॥ २॥

English
O gods, I know the misery of the gods completely. It is great. Those Asuras are very strong. They cannot be killed by the gods or demons. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पुण्यवन्तस्तु ते सर्वे समयास्तारकात्मजाः ।
दुस्साध्यस्तु वधस्तेषां सर्वेषां पुरवासिनाम् ॥ ३॥

English
The sons of Tāraka and Maya are equally meritorious. O sensible ones, they are invincible to all the citizens. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मित्रद्रोहं कथं जानन्करोमि रणकर्कशः ।
सुहृद्द्रोहे महत्पापं पूर्वमुक्तं स्वयम्भुवा ॥ ४॥

English
How can I knowingly commit malicious deeds to my friends though I am hardy and powerful in battles? Even Brahmā has said that there is a great sin attending on even casual malicious actions. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मघ्ने च सुरापे च स्तेये भग्नव्रते तथा ।
निष्कृतिर्विहिता सद्भिः कृतघ्ने नास्ति निष्कृतिः ॥ ५॥

English
There are ways of atonement and amends for a brahmin slayer, a wine addict, a thief or a person who violates sacred rites. But there is no expiation for ungrateful men (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मम भक्तास्तु ते दैत्या मया वध्याः कथं सुराः ।
विचार्यतां भवद्भिश्च धर्मज्ञैरेव धर्मतः ॥ ६॥

English
Those Asuras are my devotees. O Gods, how can they be slain by us? Let this aspect be thought over by you who know what is virtue. You must consider this virtuously. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्ते नैव हन्तव्या यावद्भक्तिकृतश्च मे ।
तथापि विष्णवे देवा निवेद्यं कारणं त्विदम् ॥ ७॥

English
They should not be slain as long as they continue their devotion to me. Yet, this reason may very well be intimated to Viṣṇu. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्येवं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा देवाः शक्रपुरोगमाः ।
न्यवेदयन् द्रुतं सर्वे ब्रह्मणे प्रथमं मुने ॥ ८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  O sage, thus when they heard these words Indra and other gods immediately intimated this to Brahmā in the first instance. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विधिं पुरस्कृत्य सर्वे देवाः सवासवाः ।
वैकुण्ठं प्रययुः शीघ्रं सर्वे शोभासमन्वितम् ॥ ९॥

English
Then, with Brahmā at their head, the gods including Indra quickly went to Vaikuṇṭha in all glory. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा हरिं दृष्ट्वा प्रणेमुर्जातसम्भ्रमाः ।
तुष्टुवुश्च महाभक्त्या कृताञ्जलिपुटाः सुराः ॥ १०॥

English
On going there and seeing Viṣṇu, the gods bowed to him in agitation, and with their palms joined in reverence they eulogised him with great devotion. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वदुःखकारणं सर्वं पूर्ववत्तदनन्तरम् ।
न्यवेदयन्द्रुतं तस्मै विष्णवे प्रभविष्णवे ॥ ११॥

English
They intimated to Viṣṇu the powerful, the reasons for their misery, the earlier ones as well as the later. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदुःखं ततः श्रुत्वा दत्तं च त्रिपुरालयैः ।
ज्ञात्वा व्रतं च तेषां तद्विष्णुर्वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing the distress of the gods as well as the vows observed by the Tripuras, Viṣṇu spoke as follows. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
इदं सत्यं वचश्चैव यत्र धर्मः सनातनः ।
तत्र दुःखं न जायेत सूर्ये दृष्टे यथा तमः ॥ १३॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* This is true that where the eternal virtue reigns supreme, no misery raises its head like darkness when the sun is seen. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्येतद्वचनं श्रुत्वा देवा दुःखमुपागताः ।
पुनरूचुस्तथा विष्णुं परिम्लानमुखाम्बुजाः ॥ १४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  On hearing these words the gods became miserable and dejected. They spoke to Viṣṇu with their lotus-like faces depressed. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
कथं चैव प्रकर्त्तव्यं कथं दुःखं निरस्यते ।
कथं भवेम सुखिनः कथं स्थास्यामहे वयम् ॥ १५॥

English
*The gods said:—* How are we to go about our activities. How can our misery be dispelled? How can we be happy? How are we to remain firm? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं धर्मा भविष्यन्ति त्रिपुरे जीविते सति ।
देवदुःखप्रदा नूनं सर्वे त्रिपुरवासिनः ॥ १६॥

English
As long as the Tripuras are alive, how can we observe virtuous activities? All the residents of the three cities give troubles to us. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
किं वा ते त्रिपुरस्येह वधश्चैव विधीयताम् ।
नो चेदकालिकी देवसंहतिः क्रियतां ध्रुवम् ॥ १७॥

English
What else can we say to you? Either the annihilation of the Tripuras shall be made or the untimely destruction of the gods shall be proceeded with. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ते तदा देवा दुःखं कृत्वा पुनः पुनः ।
स्थितिं नैव गतिं ते वै चक्रुर्देववरादिह ॥ १८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  After saying this and lamenting frequently, the gods were in a fix and could neither stay nor leave the proximity of Viṣṇu. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तान्वै तथाविधान्दृष्ट्वा हीनान्विनयसंयुतान् ।
सोऽपि नारायणः श्रीमांश्चिन्तयंश्चेतसा तथा ॥ १९॥

किं कार्यं देवकार्येषु मया देवसहायिना ।
शिवभक्तास्तु ते दैत्यास्तारकस्य सुता इति ॥ २०॥

इति सञ्चिन्त्य तत्काले विष्णुना प्रभविष्णुना ।
ततो यज्ञाः स्मृतास्तेन देवकार्यार्थमक्षयाः ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing them in that plight, distressed and humiliated, Viṣṇu thought within himself, “I am the benefactor of the gods. But what can I do in this affair? The sons of Tāraka are the devotees of Śiva.” After thinking like this, he thought upon the Supreme Viṣṇu, the lord of sacrifices, the primordial Puruṣa. (19-21.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्विष्णुस्मृतिमात्रेण यज्ञास्ते तत्क्षणं द्रुतम् ।
आगतास्तत्र यत्रास्ते श्रीपतिः पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ २२॥

English
Immediately on being thought upon by Viṣṇu all the sacrifices came where Viṣṇu was stationed. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विष्णुं यज्ञपतिं पुराणं पुरुषं हरिम् ।
प्रणम्य तुष्टुवुस्ते वै कृताञ्जलिपुटास्तदा ॥ २३॥

English
With palms joined in reverence they bowed to and eulogised Viṣṇu, the lord of sacrifices and the primordial Puruṣa. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
भगवानपि तान्दृष्ट्वा यज्ञान्प्राह सनातनान् ।
सनातनस्तदा सेन्द्रान्देवानालोक्य चाच्युतः ॥ २४॥

English
The eternal lord Viṣṇu saw the eternal sacrifices and told them looking at the gods too including Indra. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
अनेनैव सदा देवा यजध्वं परमेश्वरम् ।
पुरत्रयविनाशाय जगत्त्रयविभूतये ॥ २५॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* In order to destroy the three cities and to bring about prosperity in the three worlds, O gods perform the worship of lord Śiva along with the lord of sacrifices. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अच्युतस्य वचः श्रुत्वा देवदेवस्य धीमतः ।
प्रेम्णा ते प्रणतिं कृत्वा यज्ञेशं तेऽस्तुवन्सुराः ॥ २६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  On hearing the words of Viṣṇu, the intelligent lord of the gods and bowing to him lovingly, the gods eulogised the lord of sacrifices. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्तुत्वा ततो देवा अयजन्यज्ञपूरुषम् ।
यज्ञोक्तेन विधानेन सम्पूर्णविधयो मुने ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, eulogising thus, the gods worshipped the Sacrificial Being in accordance with the rules governing the same with the complete rites. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तस्माद्यज्ञकुण्डात्समुत्पेतुः सहस्रशः ।
भूतसङ्घा महाकायाः शूलशक्तिगदायुधाः ॥ २८॥

English
Then from the sacrificial pit rose up thousands of Bhūtas of huge size and armed with tridents, spears, iron clubs and other weapons. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ददृशुस्ते सुरास्तान् वै भूतसङ्घान् सहस्रशः ।
शूलशक्तिगदाहस्तान्दण्डचापशिलायुधान् ॥ २९॥

नानाप्रहरणोपेतान् नानावेषधराँस्तथा ।
कालाग्निरुद्रसदृशान्कालसूर्योपमाँस्तदा ॥ ३०॥

दृष्ट्वा तानब्रवीद्विष्णुः प्रणिपत्य पुरःस्थितान् ।
भूतान्यज्ञपतिः श्रीमान् रुद्राज्ञाप्रतिपालकः ॥ ३१॥

English
The gods saw thousands of Bhūtasaṅghas armed with tridents and spears and possessing various weapons such as staffs, bows, stones, etc. They had various missiles to strike with. They were in different guises. They resembled the destructive fire and Rudra. They were on a par with the destructive Sun. When they bowed to him and stood waiting in front, Viṣṇu saw them. The glorious lord of sacrifices who carried out the behests of Rudra, spoke to them. (29-31.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
भूताः श‍ृणुत मद्वाक्यं देवकार्यार्थमुद्यताः ।
गच्छन्तु त्रिपुरं सद्यः सर्वे हि बलवत्तराः ॥ ३२॥

English
Viṣṇu said—“O Bhūtas, listen to my statement. You are all very powerful and have risen up for fulfilling the task of the gods. All of you go immediately to the three cities. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा दग्ध्वा च भित्त्वा च भङ्क्त्वा दैत्यपुरत्रयम् ।
पुनर्यथागता भूतागन्तुमर्हथ भूतये ॥ ३३॥

English
Go there, strike at, break and burn the three cities of the Asuras. O Bhūtas, thereafter you can go away as you please for prosperity. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा भगवद्वाक्यं ततो भूतगणाश्च ते ।
प्रणम्य देवदेवं तं ययुर्दैत्यपुरत्रयम् ॥ ३४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  On hearing the words of the lord, the hosts of Bhūtas bowed to the lord of the gods and went to the three cities of the Asuras. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्प्रविशन्तश्च त्रिपुराधिपतेजसि ।
भस्मसादभवन्सद्यः शलभा इव पावके ॥ ३५॥

English
Immediately after their entry into the cities they were reduced to ashes like moths in the fire. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अवशिष्टाश्च ये केचित्पलायनपरायणाः ।
निस्सृत्यारं समायाता हरेर्निकटमाकुलाः ॥ ३६॥

English
Those who escaped fled out of the cities and came grief-stricken to Viṣṇu. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

चिन्तयामास भगवान्मनसा पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ ३७॥

English
On seeing them and hearing the incidents in detail, lord Viṣṇu pondered over this. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कृत्यमधुना कार्यमिति सन्तप्तमानसः ।
सन्तप्तानमरान्सर्वानाज्ञाय च सवासवान् ॥ ३८॥

English
On realising that all the gods were distressed and dejected in the mind, he too was extremely grieved. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

देवकार्यं करिष्यामीत्यासीच्चिन्तासमाकुलः ॥ ३९॥

English
He became much worried at the thought “How can I destroy the three cities of the Asuras, with force and carry out the task of the gods?” (39.)

---

Sanskrit

इति प्राह स्वयं चेशः श्रुत्याचारप्रमाणकृत् ॥ ४०॥

English
The lord who had authoritatively laid down the rules of conduct according to the Vedas, said “There is no doubt in this that the virtuous ones cannot be destroyed by black magic. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

तस्मादवध्यतां प्राप्ता नान्यथासुरपुङ्गवाः ॥ ४१॥

English
O good gods, these Asuras and the other residents of the three cities are virtuous. Hence they have become invincible. Not in any other manner. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
After perpetrating great sins they worship Śiva and so they are freed of all sins even as the leaves of the lotus from water. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O gods, thanks to the worship of Śiva, their cherished desires are realised. Different means of enjoyment in the world are brought under their control. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Hence these Asuras who are devoted to the phallic worship enjoy different kinds of pleasures and prosperity and salvation hereafter. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then putting obstacles in their activities of virtuous rites, by means of magic art I shall destroy the three cities of the Asuras quickly for accomplishing the task of the Asuras.” (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
After thinking thus, lord Viṣṇu set himself to the task of interfering with the sacred rites of the Asuras. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

यावच्च शुचिकृत्यानि तावन्नाशो भवेन्न हि ॥ ४७॥

English
As long as the worship of Śiva is continued and as long as the pure activities are strictly adhered to, there is no question of their ruin at all. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Hence such means shall be followed as will make Vedic Dharma disappear thence. Undoubtedly the Asuras will foresake their worship of the phallic emblem of Śiva. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Deciding thus, Viṣṇu started ridiculing the Vedas for putting up obstacles in the virtuous activities of the Asuras. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
At the bidding of Śiva, Viṣṇu who was commanded to protect the three worlds, and who renders help to the gods spoke to them. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

स्वभक्तिरहितान् ज्ञात्वा तान्करिष्यामि भस्मसात् ॥ ५२॥

English
Strenuously I shall make them averse to Śiva. Coming to know that they are devoid of devotion to him he will reduce them to ashes. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तदाज्ञां शिरसाधायश्वासितास्तेऽमरा मुने ।
स्वस्वधामानि विश्वस्ता ययुः परममोदिताः ॥ ५३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  O sage, accepting his behest with bent head, the gods and Brahmā felt satisfied and with faith in their hearts returned to their abodes. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चैवाकरोद्विष्णुर्देवार्थं हितमुत्तमम् ।
तदेव श्रूयतां सम्यक्सर्वपापप्रणाशनम् ॥ ५४॥

English
Thereafter Viṣṇu performed deeds conducive to the welfare of the gods. Listen to those excellent deeds destructive of all sins. (54.)
