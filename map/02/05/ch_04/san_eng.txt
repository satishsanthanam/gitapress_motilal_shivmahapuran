### **CHAPTER FOUR - *(The Tripuras are initiated)***

---

Sanskrit
४

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
असृजच्च महातेजाः पुरुषं स्वात्मसम्भवम् ।
एकं मायामयं तेषां धर्मविघ्नार्थमच्युतः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  For causing obstacles in their virtuous activities, Viṣṇu of great brilliance, created a Puruṣa$^{184}$ born of himself. (1.)

[fn 184. According to the present context Viṣṇu created a delusive teacher called Māyāmoha who created a Māyāśāstra of sixteen lakhs of ślokas in Apabhramśa, preaching Jina Dharma for misguiding the Asuras. Māyāmoha created four sorts of preachers for the propagation of Jina Dharma. He preached non-violence, forbade Śrauta and Smārta rituals, discarded Varṇāśrama system, created an order for women that resulted in leaving their home and leading the life of nuns. In some versions, the role is assigned to Bṛhaspati, the preceptor of the Gods who in the guise of their preceptor Śukra deludes the Asuras. For details, see Māyāmohaprakaraṇa in Padma, Viṣṇu, Linga, Matsya etc.]

तद्धर्मस्य महाराज ग्राह्या दीक्षा त्वया पुनः ॥ ५१॥

English
O great king, O excellent Asuras, if you have any interest in that cult, you shall get yourself initiated into it. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तदीयं स वचः श्रुत्वा महदर्थसुगर्भितम् ।
विस्मितो हृदि दैत्येशो जगौ तत्र विमोहितः ॥ ५२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  On hearing his words full of significance, the lord of the Asuras was deluded and exclaimed with surprise in his heart. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदो दीक्षितो यस्माद्वयं दीक्षामवाप्नुमः ।
इत्येवं च विदित्वा वै जगाम स्वयमेव ह ॥ ५३॥

English
“Since Nārada has been initiated we too shall be initiated.” Resolving thus, the Asura approached the sage. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रूपं च तदा दृष्ट्वा मोहितो मायया तथा ।
उवाच वचनं तस्मै नमस्कृत्य महात्मने ॥ ५४॥

English
On seeing his features, the Asura was deluded by his magic. After bowing to him he spoke thus. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिपुराधिप उवाच ।
दीक्षा देया त्वया मह्यं निर्मलाशय भो ऋषे ।
अहं शिष्यो भविष्यामि सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ५५॥

English
*The Tripura ruler said:—* O sage of pure mind, you shall perform my initiation. I shall become your disciple. True. It is undoubtedly true. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं तु वचः श्रुत्वा दैत्यराजस्य निर्मलम् ।
प्रत्युवाच सुयत्नेन ऋषिः स च सनातनः ॥ ५६॥

English
On hearing the frank words of the ruler of the Asuras the heretic sage, professing to be eternal, spoke emphatically. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
मदीया करणीया स्याद्यद्याज्ञा दैत्यसत्तम ।
तदा देया मया दीक्षा नान्यथा कोटियत्नतः ॥ ५७॥

English
O excellent Asura, if you are prepared to act according to my behests, I shall initiate you, otherwise not, even if you strive for a number of times. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं तु वचः श्रुत्वा राजा मायामयोऽभवत् ।
उवाच वचनं शीघ्रं यतिं तं हि कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ५८॥

English
On hearing these words the king was deluded by magic. With palms joined in reverence he immediately replied to the sage. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्य उवाच ।
यथाज्ञां दास्यसि त्वं च तत्तथैव न चान्यथा ।
त्वदाज्ञां नोल्लङ्घयिष्ये सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ५९॥

English
*The Asura said:—* I shall carry out whatever command you are pleased to give. I will not transgress your orders. True. It is certainly true. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य त्रिपुराधीशितुस्तदा ।
दूरीकृत्य मुखाद्वस्त्रमुवाच ऋषिसत्तमः ॥ ६०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  On hearing the words of the Tripura-ruler, the excellent sage removed the cloth from his mouth and said. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
दीक्षां गृह्णीष्व दैत्येन्द्र सर्वधर्मोत्तमोत्तमाम् ।
येन दीक्षाविधानेन प्राप्स्यसि त्वं कृतार्थताम् ॥ ६१॥

English
“O lord of Asuras, take initiation in this most excellent of all cults. By this initiation you will become contented.” (61.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा स तु मायावी दैत्यराजाय सत्वरम् ।
ददौ दीक्षां स्वधर्मोक्तां तस्मै विधिविधानतः ॥ ६२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  Saying thus, the deceptive sage immediately performed the initiation of the ruler of the Asuras, in accordance with his cult observing all rules. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यराजे दीक्षिते च तस्मिन्ससहजे मुने ।
सर्वे च दीक्षिता जातास्तत्र त्रिपुरवासिनः ॥ ६३॥

English
O sage, when the ruler of the Asuras was initiated along with his brothers, the residents of the three cities too got themselves initiated. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनेः शिष्यैः प्रशिष्यैश्च व्याप्तमासीद् द्रुतं तदा ।
महामायाविनस्तत्तु त्रिपुरं सकलं मुने ॥ ६४॥

English
O sage, the entire Tripuras were filled with the line of disciples of the sage, an expert in great art of illusion. (64.)

---
