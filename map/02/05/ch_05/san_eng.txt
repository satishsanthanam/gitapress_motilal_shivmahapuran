### **CHAPTER FIVE - *(The Tripuras are fascinated)***

---

Sanskrit
५

व्यास उवाच ।
दैत्यराजे दीक्षिते च मायिना तेन मोहिते ।
किमुवाच तदा मायी किं चकार स दैत्यपः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—* When the ruler of the Asuras was initiated after being deluded by the deceptive sage expert in the magic art what did the sage say? What did the ruler of the Asuras do? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
दीक्षां दत्त्वा यतिस्तस्मा अरिहन्नारदादिभिः ।
शिष्यैः सेवितपादाब्जो दैत्यराजानमब्रवीत् ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  After offering him initiation, the ascetic Arihan served by his disciples, Nārada and others, spoke to the ruler of the Asuras. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अरिहन्नुवाच ।
श‍ृणु दैत्यपते वाक्यं मम संज्ञानगर्भितम् ।
वेदान्तसारसर्वस्वं रहस्यं परमोत्तमम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Arihan said:—* O ruler of the Asuras, listen to my statement, pregnant with wisdom. It is the essence of the Vedānta and bears high esoteric importance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अनादिसिद्धः संसारः कर्तृकर्मविवर्जितः ।
स्वयं प्रादुर्भवत्येव स्वयमेव विलीयते ॥ ४॥

English
The entire universe is eternal. It has no creator nor it is an object of creation. It evolves itself and gets annihilated by itself. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मादिस्तम्बपर्यन्तं यावद्देहनिबन्धनम् ।
आत्मैवैकेश्वरस्तत्र न द्वितीयस्तदीशिता ॥ ५॥

English
There are many bodies from Brahmā down to a blade of grass. They themselves are the gods for them. There is no other God. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
यद् ब्रह्मविष्णुरुद्राख्यास्तदाख्या देहिनामिमाः ।
आख्या यथास्मदादीनामरिहन्नादिरुच्यते ॥ ६॥

English
What we mean by Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra are only the names of embodied beings just like my name Arihan etc. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
देहो यथास्मदादीनां स्वकालेन विलीयते ।
ब्रह्मादिमशकान्तानां स्वकालाल्लीयते तथा ॥ ७॥

English
Just as our bodies perish when their time arrives, so also the bodies of all beings from Brahmā to a mosquito perish when their time arrives. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
विचार्यमाणे देहेऽस्मिन्न किञ्चिदधिकं क्वचित् ।
आहारो मैथुनं निद्रा भयं सर्वत्र यत्समम् ॥ ८॥

English
When we consider, none of these bodies is superior to any other since in respect of taking food, copulation, sleep and fear these are invariably the same everywhere. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
निराहारपरीमाणं प्राप्य सर्वो हि देहभृत् ।
सदृशीमेव सन्तृप्तिं प्राप्नुयान्नाधिकेतराम् ॥ ९॥

English
Taking in water and foodstuffs to the required quantity, all living beings derive a kindred satisfaction, neither more nor less. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा वितृषिताः स्याम पीत्वा पेयं मुदा वयम् ।
तृषितास्तु तथान्येऽपि न विशेषोऽल्पकोऽधिकः ॥ १०॥

English
After drinking water we are gladly relieved of thirst. Others too are equally relieved. There is no deviation this way or that. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तु नार्यः सहस्राणि रूपलावण्यभूमयः ।
परं निधुवने काले ह्यैकेवेहोपयुज्यते ॥ ११॥

English
There may be a thousand damsels of exquisite beauty and comely features. But only one of them can be used at a time for the sexual intercourse. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वाः पराः शताःसन्तु सन्त्वनेकेऽप्यनेकधा ।
अधिरोहे तथाप्येको न द्वितीयस्तथात्मनः ॥ १२॥

English
Let there be hundreds of horses, of different varieties. But for the purpose of riding only one can be used on one occasion. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्यङ्कशायिनां स्वापे सुखं यदुपजायते ।
तदेव सौख्यं निद्राभिर्भूतभूशायिनामपि ॥ १३॥

English
The pleasure that one derives in that sleep on a cushioned couch is the same that one derives by sleeping on the bare ground. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यथैव मरणाद्भीतिरस्मदादिवपुष्मताम् ।
ब्रह्मादिकीटकान्तानां तथा मरणतो भयम् ॥ १४॥

English
Just as we, the embodied beings, are afraid of death so also the bodies from Brahmā to the worm are afraid of death. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे तनुभृतस्तुल्या यदि बुद्ध्या विचार्य्यते ।
इदं निश्चित्य केनापि नो हिंस्यः कोऽपि कुत्रचित् ॥ १५॥

English
If we analyse with a keen intellect, all the embodied being are equal. After coming to this conclusion it does not behove anyone to injure anyone else. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मो जीवदयातुल्यो न क्वापि जगतीतले ।
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन कार्या जीवदया नृभिः ॥ १६॥

English
There is no other virtue equal to the mercy shown to living beings. Hence all men shall strenuously practise acts of mercy to living beings. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन् रक्षिते जीवे त्रैलोक्यं रक्षितं भवेत् ।
घातिते घातितं तद्वत्तस्माद्रक्षेन्न घातयेत् ॥ १७॥

English
If a single living being is protected it amounts to the protection of the three worlds. If that is killed it amounts to the killing of all others. Hence it is our duty to protect and abstain from killing others. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अहिंसा परमो धर्मः पापमात्मप्रपीडनम् ।
अपराधीनता मुक्तिः स्वर्गोऽभिलषिताशनम् ॥ १८॥

English
Non-violence is the greatest virtue. Affliction of others is a great sin. Salvation is defined as non-dependence on others. Eating the food of our choice is heavenly bliss. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वसूरिभिरित्युक्तं सत्प्रमाणतया ध्रुवम् ।
तस्मान्न हिंसा कर्त्तव्या नरैर्नरकभीरुभिः ॥ १९॥

English
This has been mentioned by the earlier sages with good justification to be sure. Hence no violence should be indulged in by men who are afraid of hell. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
न हिंसासदृशं पापं त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ।
हिंसको नरकं गच्छेत्स्वर्गं गच्छेदहिंसकः ॥ २०॥

English
There is no sin equal to violence in the three worlds, consisting of the mobile and immobile. A person who afflicts others violently goes to hell. A non-violent man goes to heaven. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ति दानान्यनेकानि किं तैस्तुच्छफलप्रदैः ।
अभीतिसदृशं दानं परमेकमपीह न ॥ २१॥

English
There are many kinds of charitable gifts. Of what avail are these which give very insignificant results. There is no other gift equal to that of protection. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इह चत्वारि दानानि प्रोक्तानि परमर्षिभिः ।
विचार्य नानाशास्त्राणि शर्मणेऽत्र परत्र च ॥ २२॥

English
Four types of gifts have been mentioned by the great sages for the welfare of the people here and hereafter as a result of discussions and deliberations of various sacred texts. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
भीतेभ्यश्चाभयं देयं व्याधितेभ्यस्तथौषधम् ।
देया विद्यार्थिनां विद्या देयमन्नं क्षुधातुरे ॥ २३॥

English
Protection shall be granted to the frightened, medicine to the sick, learning to the student and food to the hungry. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यानि यानीह दानानि बहु मुन्युदितानि च ।
जीवाभयप्रदानस्य कलां नार्हन्ति षोडशीम् ॥ २४॥

English
All sorts of charitable gifts recommended by the sages do not merit even a sixteenth part of the gift of protection to a living being. (24.)

---

English
The strength that one derives by the use of gems mantras, and herbs is of inconceivable influence. But it is practised strenuously only for earning money. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्थानुपार्ज्य बहुशो द्वादशायतनानि वै ।
परितः परिपूज्यानि किमन्यैरिह पूजितैः ॥ २६॥

English
The hoarding and amassing of vast wealth is useful only for the propitiation of twelve organs of senses. Of what avail is the propitiation of other things? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चकर्मेन्द्रियग्रामाः पञ्च बुद्धीन्द्रियाणि च ।
मनो बुद्धिरिह प्रोक्तं द्वादशायतनं शुभम् ॥ २७॥

English
The twelve organs of senses are the five organs of activity and the five organs of knowledge together with the mind and intellect. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
इहैव स्वर्गनरकौ प्राणिनां नान्यतः क्वचित् ।
सुखं स्वर्गः समाख्यातो दुःखं नरकमेव हि ॥ २८॥

English
Living beings have heaven and hell here itself and not anywhere else. Happiness is heaven and misery is hell. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखेषु भुज्यमानेषु यत्स्याद्देहविसर्ज्जनम् ।
अयमेव परो मोक्षो विज्ञेयस्तत्त्वचिन्तकैः ॥ २९॥

English
If the body is cast off in the midst of enjoyment that is the greatest liberation conceived by the philosophers. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
वासनासहिते क्लेशसमुच्छेदे सति ध्रुवम् ।
अज्ञानोपरमो मोक्षो विज्ञेयस्तत्त्वचिन्तकैः ॥ ३०॥

English
When pain comes to an end along with its impressions, if ignorance too dies away, it is conceived as the greatest salvation by the philosophers. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रामाणिकी श्रुतिरियं प्रोच्यते वेदवादिभिः ।
न हिंस्यात्सर्वभूतानि नान्या हिंसाप्रवर्तिका ॥ ३१॥

English
Supporters and exponents of the Vedas accept this as an authoritative Vedic text that no living being shall be injured. Violence is not justifiable. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निष्टोमीयमिति या भ्रामिका साऽसतामिह ।
न सा प्रमाणं ज्ञातॄणां पश्वालम्भनकारिका ॥ ३२॥

English
The Vedic text encouraging slaughter of animals cannot be held authoritative by the learned. To say that violence is allowed in Agniṣṭoma is an erroneous view of the wicked. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वृक्षांश्छित्वा पशून् हत्वा कृत्वा रुधिरकर्द्दमम् ।
दग्ध्वा वह्नौ तिलाज्यादि चित्रं स्वर्गोऽभिलष्यते ॥ ३३॥

English
It is surprising that heaven is sought by cutting off trees, slaughtering animals, making a muddy mess with blood and by burning gingelly seeds and ghee. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं स्वमतं प्रोच्य यतिस्त्रिपुरनायकम् ।
श्रावयित्वाखिलान् पौरानुवाच पुनरादरात् ॥ ३४॥

English
Narrating his opinions to the leader of the Tripuras, the ascetic addressed the citizens with great zeal. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्टार्थप्रत्ययकरान्देहसौख्यैकसाधकान् ।
बौद्धागमविनिर्दिष्टान्धर्मान्वेदपरांस्ततः ॥ ३५॥

English
He referred to things which gave credence, being visible, which brought happiness to the body, which are indicated in Buddhistic theology and which are consistent with the Vedic passages. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
आनन्दं ब्रह्मणो रूपं श्रुत्यैवं यन्निगद्यते ।
तत्तथैवेह मन्तव्यं मिथ्या नानात्वकल्पना ॥ ३६॥

English
It is said in the Vedas that Bliss is an aspect of the Brahman. That shall be taken as it is. It is false to bring in various alternatives. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्स्वस्थमिदं वर्ष्म यावन्नेन्द्रियविक्लवः ।
यावज्जरा च दूरेऽस्ति तावत्सौख्यं प्रसाधयेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
One shall seek and enjoy happiness as long as the body is hale and hearty, as long as the sense-organs are not impaired and as long as the old age is far off. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्वास्थ्येन्द्रियवैकल्ये वार्द्धके तु कुतः सुखम् ।
शरीरमपि दातव्यमर्थिभ्योऽतः सुखेप्सुभिः ॥ ३८॥

English
When there is sickness, impairment of the sense-organs and old age how can one derive happiness? Hence those who seek happiness shall be prepared to give away even the body. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
याचमानमनोवृत्तिप्रीणने यस्य नो जनिः ।
तेन भूर्भारवत्येषा समुद्रागद्रुमैर्न हि ॥ ३९॥

English
The Earth is burdened by those who are not ready to please and satisfy the suppliant. It is not burdened by oceans, mountains and trees. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्वरं गत्वरो देहः सञ्चयाः सपरिक्षयाः ।
इति विज्ञाय विज्ञाता देहसौख्यं प्रसाधयेत् ॥ ४०॥

English
The body is ready to go in a trice, and hoarded things are attended with the risk of dwindling down. Realising this a sensible man shall see to the pleasure of his body. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
It is mentioned in the Vedas that this body is going to constitute the breakfast for dogs, crows and worms. The body has its ultimate end in being reduced to ashes. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
It is unnecessary to divide the people into different castes. When all are men who is superior and who is inferior? (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Kaśyapa, the son of Marīci married thirteen of the sweet-eyed daughters of Dakṣa, they say, in accordance with righteous path. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
But people of modern times whose intelligence and valour are but a modicum unnecessarily wrangle over the fact whether this is proper or improper. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

---

English

---

---

English

वेदधर्माश्च ये केचित्ते सर्वे दूरतः कृताः ॥ ५४॥

English
O foremost among brahmins, why shall I dilate upon this topic ? Suffice it to say that in the three cities every type of Vedic rites was completely stopped by that deceptive heretic sage. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
यतिधर्माश्रयाः सर्वा मोहितास्त्रिपुराङ्गनाः ।
भर्तृशुश्रूषणवतीं विजहुर्मतिमुत्तमाम् ॥ ५५॥

English
The women of the three cities who were hitherto devotedly attached to their husbands were deluded and misguided and they abandoned their noble inclinations to serve their husbands. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्यस्याकर्षणीं विद्यां वशीकृत्यमयीमपि ।
पुरुषाः सफलीचक्रुः परदारेषु मोहिताः ॥ ५६॥

English
The fascinated men practised rites of seduction and winning over and made their artifices fruitful in gaining other men's wives. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तः पुरचरा नार्यस्तथा राजकुमारकाः ।
पौराः पुराङ्गनाश्चापि सर्वे तैश्च विमोहिताः ॥ ५७॥

English
The attendant maids in the harems, the princes, the citizens and the ladies were perfectly enchanted by him. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पौरेषु सर्वेषु निजधर्मेषु सर्वथा ।
पराङ्मुखेषु जातेषु प्रोल्ललास वृषेतरः ॥ ५८॥

English
Thus when the citizens became averse to virtuous rites and actions, evil reigned supreme. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
माया च देवदेवस्य विष्णोस्तस्याज्ञया प्रभो ।
अलक्ष्मीश्च स्वयं तस्य नियोगात्त्रिपुरं गता ॥ ५९॥

English
At the bidding of lord Viṣṇu, his magic art and evil fortune visited the three cities. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
या लक्ष्मीस्तपसा तेषां लब्धा देवेश्वराद्वरात् ।
बहिर्गता परित्यज्य नियोगाद्ब्रह्मणः प्रभोः ॥ ६०॥

English
The glory that they had acquired by the boon of Brahmā, the lord of the gods, went out forsaking them, at the behest of Brahmā. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
बुद्धिमोहं तथाभूतं विष्णोर्मायाविनिर्मितम् ।
तेषां दत्त्वा क्षणादेव कृतार्थोऽभूत्स नारदः ॥ ६१॥

English
Blessing them with the utter delusion of their intellect, perpetrated by the illusion of Viṣṇu, Nārada became contented. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदोऽपि तथारूपो यथा मायी तथैव सः ।
तथापि विकृतो नाभूत्पारमेशादनुग्रहात् ॥ ६२॥

English
Though Nārada and the heretic sage had been in that guise for long, they were not defiled, thanks to the benediction of lord Śiva. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
आसीत्कुण्ठितसामर्थ्यो दैत्यराजोऽपि भो मुने ।
भ्रातृभ्यां सहितस्तत्र मयेन च शिवेच्छया ॥ ६३॥

English
As Śiva willed, O sage, the capacity of the ruler of the Asuras became stunted and thwarted as also of his brothers and Maya. (63.)
