### **CHAPTER SIX - *(Prayer to Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
६

व्यास उवाच ।
तस्मिन् दैत्याधिपे पौरे सभ्रातरि विमोहिते ।
सनत्कुमार किं चासीत्तदाचक्ष्वाखिलं विभो ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  When the ruler of the Asuras, his brothers and the citizens were thus deluded, O lord Sanatkumāra, what happened? Please mention everything. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
त्रिपुरे च तथाभूते दैत्ये त्यक्तशिवार्चने ।
स्त्रीधर्मे निखिले नष्टे दुराचारे व्यवस्थिते ॥ २॥

कृतार्थ इव लक्ष्मीशो देवैः सार्द्धमुमापतिम् ।
निवेदितुं तच्चरित्रं कैलासमगमद्धरिः ॥ ३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  When the Asuras had become so, when they had abandoned the worship of Śiva, when the virtuous rites of chaste women came to an end and evil conduct came to stay, Viṣṇu was apparently contented. Accompanied by the gods, Viṣṇu went to Kailāsa in order to intimate their activities to Śiva. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्योपकण्ठं स्थित्वाऽसौ देवैःसह रमापतिः ।
ततो भूरि स च ब्रह्मा परमेण समाधिना ॥ ४॥

मनसा प्राप्य सर्वज्ञं ब्रह्मणा स हरिस्तदा ।
तुष्टाव वाग्भिरिष्टाभिः शङ्करं पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ ५॥

English
Viṣṇu, the gods, Brahmā and others stood near him and with great concentration they meditated on him. Viṣṇu and Brahmā eulogised the omniscient Śiva with pleasing words. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
महेश्वराय देवाय नमस्ते परमात्मने ।
नारायणाय रुद्राय ब्रह्मणे ब्रह्मरूपिणे ॥ ६॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* “Obeisance to you, great lord, the great soul, Nārāyaṇa, Rudra and Brahmā, obeisance to you in the form of Brahman.” (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वा महादेवं दण्डवत्प्रणिपत्य ह ।
जजाप रुद्रमन्त्रं च दक्षिणामूर्तिसम्भवम् ॥ ७॥

English
After eulogising lord Śiva thus and prostrating at length, he repeated the mantra of Dakṣināmūrti Rudra. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
जले स्थित्वा सार्द्धकोटिप्रमितं तन्मनाः प्रभुः ।
संस्मरन् मनसा शम्भुं स्वप्रभुं परमेश्वरम् ॥ ८॥

तावद्देवास्तदा सर्वे तन्मनस्का महेश्वरम् ॥ ९॥

English
He repeated the mantra fifteen million times standing in water and concentrating his mind on him. Lord Viṣṇu meditated on the great lord Śiva. In the meantime, the gods too eulogised him with devotion. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
नमःसर्वात्मने तुभ्यं शङ्करायार्तिहारिणे ।
रुद्राय नीलकण्ठाय चिद्रूपाय प्रचेतसे ॥ १०॥

English
*The gods said:—* Obeisance to you, the soul of all, obeisance to Śiva the remover of distress, obeisance to the blue-necked Rudra, obeisance to the knowledge-formed Śiva of great mind. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गतिर्नः सर्वदा त्वं हि सर्वापद्विनिवारकः ।
त्वमेव सर्वदात्माभिर्वन्द्यो देवारिसूदन ॥ ११॥

English
You are our ultimate goal for ever. You are the remover of all adversities. O destroyer of the enemies of the gods, you alone are to be respected by us always. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमादिस्त्वमनादिश्च स्वानन्दश्चाक्षयः प्रभुः ।
प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्यापि साक्षात्स्रष्टा जगत्प्रभुः ॥ १२॥

English
You are the beginning. You are the primordial being. You are self-bliss. You are the everlasting lord. You are the lord of the universe, the direct creator of Prakṛti and Puruṣa. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव जगतां कर्ता भर्ता हर्ता त्वमेव हि ।
ब्रह्मा विष्णुर्हरो भूत्वा रजःसत्त्वतमोगुणैः ॥ १३॥

English
You alone are the creator, sustainer and the annihilator of the worlds. Assuming the Guṇas of Rajas, Sattva, and Tamas you are Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तारकोऽसि जगत्यस्मिन् सर्वेषामधिपोऽव्ययः ।
वरदो वाङ्मयो वाच्यो वाच्यवाचकवर्जितः ॥ १४॥

English
In this universe, you enable people to cross the ocean of Existence. You are the undecaying lord of all. You are the granter of boons. You are the subject and not the object of speech and contents. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
याच्यो मुक्त्यर्थमीशानो योगिभिर्योगवित्तमैः ।
हृत्पुण्डरीकविवरे योगिनां त्वं हि संस्थितः ॥ १५॥

English
You shall be requested for salvation by the Yogins, the formost among those who know the theory of Yoga. You are stationed inside the lotus like heart of the Yogins. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वदन्ति वेदास्त्वां सन्तः परब्रह्मस्वरूपिणम् ।
भवन्तं तत्त्वमित्यद्य तेजोराशिं परात्परम् ॥ १६॥

English
The Vedas and the saintly men speak of you as the supreme Brahman. You are a heaped mass of splendour and greater than the greatest. They call you the great principle. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
परमात्मानमित्याहुरस्मिन् जगति यद्विभो ।
त्वमेव शर्व सर्वात्मन् त्रिलोकाधिपते भव ॥ १७॥

English
What they call the great soul in the universe, O lord, are you yourself, O Śiva soul of all, ruler of the three worlds. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्टं श्रुतं स्तुतं सर्वं ज्ञायमानं जगद्गुरो ।
अणोरल्पतरं प्राहुर्महतोऽपि महत्तरम् ॥ १८॥

English
Whatever is seen, heard or eulogised, whatever is being realised, O preceptor of the universe, are you alone. They call you minuter than the atom and greater than the greatest. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतः पाणिपादं तत् सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखम् ।
सर्वतः श्रवणघ्राणं त्वां नमामि च सर्वतः ॥ १९॥

English
I bow to you everywhere, you who have hands, legs, eyes, heads, mouths, ears and noses everywhere. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वज्ञं सर्वतो व्यापिन् सर्वेश्वरमनावृतम् ।
विश्वरूपं विरूपाक्षं त्वां नमामि च सर्वतः ॥ २०॥

English
I bow to you everywhere, you who are omniscient who pervade everything, you who are unveiled as the lord of all, you who are omniformed and odd-eyed. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेश्वरं भवाध्यक्षं सत्यं शिवमनुत्तमम् ।
कोटि भास्करसङ्काशं त्वां नमामि च सर्वतः ॥ २१॥

English
I bow to you everywhere who are the lord of all, who preside over the worlds, who are the excellent Satya and Śiva and who have the refulgence of innumerable suns. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वदेवमनाद्यन्तं षट्त्रिंशत्कमनीश्वरम् ।
प्रवर्तकं च सर्वेषां त्वां नमामि च सर्वतः ॥ २२॥

English
I bow to you everywhere, you the lord of the universe devoid of beginning and end, the lord of the twenty six Tattvas$^{187}$ and the activiser of everything. (22.)

[fn 187. Substitute षड्विंशकमनीश्वरम् for षड्विंशकमनीश्वरम् । The latter reading which is found in most of the printed editions of Śivapurāṇa is incorrect. Cf. Lingapurāṇa : 1. 71. 109.

According to the present text, the intellect, ego, mind, 10 senses 5 gross and 5 subtle elements, the invisible primordial nature (Pradhāna) the individual soul (Jīva), the transcendent God (Īśvara) constitute a group of twenty-six categories wherein Jīva is the enjoyer of the fruits and Īśvara is the spectator of the working of Prakṛti. But there is another twenty-seventh category named Śiva or Sadāśiva, the highest divine being (परात्परतरः) who alone is capable of bestowing grace upon his devotees.]

---

Sanskrit
प्रवर्तकं च प्रकृतेः सर्वस्य प्रपितामहम् ।
सर्वविग्रहमीशं हि त्वां नमामि च सर्वतः ॥ २३॥

English
I bow to you everywhere you the activiser of the Prakṛti, the great grandfather of everyone, the lord, the body of everyone. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वदन्ति वरदं सर्वावासं स्वयम्भुवम् ।
श्रुतयः श्रुतिसारज्ञं श्रुतिसारविदश्च ये ॥ २४॥

English
The Śrutis and those who know the essence of Śrutis speak of you thus. You are the abode of all, the self-born and the knower of the essence of Śrutis. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अदृश्यमस्माभिरनेकभूतं
त्वया कृतं यद्भवताथ लोके ।
त्वामेव देवासुरभूसुराश्च
अन्ये च वै स्थावरजङ्गमाश्च ॥ २५॥

English
The various living beings created by you and to be created in future are invisible to us. The gods, the Asuras, the brahmins, nay, the mobile and immobile beings eulogise you alone. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पाह्यनन्यगतीन् शम्भो सुरान्नो देववल्लभ ।
नष्टप्रायांस्त्रिपुरतो विनिहत्यासुरान्क्षणात् ॥ २६॥

English
O Śiva, dear to the gods, save us, the gods who have no other go, by killing all the Asuras instantaneously. We are practically destroyed by the Tripuras. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मायया मोहितास्तेऽद्य भवतः परमेश्वर ।
विष्णुना प्रोक्तयुक्त्या त उज्झिता धर्मतः प्रभो ॥ २७॥

English
O lord Śiva, they are now deluded by your magic. O lord, they have gone astray from the virtuous path through the expedient taught by Viṣṇu. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्त्यक्तसर्वधर्माश्च बौद्धागमसमाश्रिताः ।
अस्मद्भाग्यवशाज्जाता दैत्यास्ते भक्तवत्सल ॥ २८॥

English
O lord, favourably disposed towards your devotees, those Asuras have resorted to Buddha's religion and philosophy, thanks to our good fortune and hence they have eschewed all Vedic sacred rites. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा त्वं कार्यकर्त्ता हि देवानां शरणप्रद ।
वयं ते शरणापन्ना यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ २९॥

English
You have always been the only one carrying out the task of the gods and the bestower of refuge. We have sought refuge in you. Please do as you desire. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा महेशानं देवास्तु पुरतः स्थिताः ।
कृताञ्जलिपुटा दीना आसन् सन्नतमूर्तयः ॥ ३०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After eulogising lord Śiva thus, the distressed gods stood in front of him with palms joined in reverence and kneeling low. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुतश्चैवं सुरेन्द्राद्यैर्विष्णोर्जाप्येन चेश्वरः ।
अगच्छत्तत्र सर्वेशो वृषमारुह्य हर्षितः ॥ ३१॥

English
Eulogised thus by Indra and others and by the repetition of Japas by Viṣṇu, the delighted lord came there seated on his bull. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुमालिङ्ग्य नन्दिशादवरुह्य प्रसन्नधीः ।
ददर्श सुदृशा तत्र नन्दीदत्तकरोऽखिलान् ॥ ३२॥

English
Getting down from Nandīśa and embracing Viṣṇu, lord Śiva delighted in his mind cast his benign look on all with his hand resting on Nandin. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवान् समालोक्य कृपादृष्ट्या हरिं हरः ।
प्राह गम्भीरया वाचा प्रसन्नः पार्वतीपतिः ॥ ३३॥

English
Casting a sympathetic glance on the gods, the delighted Śiva, lord of Pārvatī, spoke to Viṣṇu in a majestic tone. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
ज्ञातं मयेदमधुना देवकार्यं सुरेश्वर ।
विष्णोर्मायाबलं चैव नारदस्य च धीमतः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Śiva said:—* “O lord of gods, all the intentions of the gods have been understood by me now. The power of Viṣṇu’s magic and that of the intelligent Nārada has been known. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषामधर्मनिष्ठानां दैत्यानां देवसत्तम ।
पुरत्रयविनाशं च करिष्येऽहं न संशयः ॥ ३५॥

English
O most excellent of the gods, there is no doubt in this that I will destroy the three cities of the Asuras if they persist in indulging in evil activities. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
परन्तु ते महादैत्या मद्भक्ता दृढमानसाः ।
अथ वध्या मयैव स्युर्व्याजत्यक्तवृषोत्तमाः ॥ ३६॥

English
But the great Asuras are my firm devotees. They shall be killed only by me, for they have been forced to abandon their excellent virtue under false persuasion. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्हन्यात्परो वाथ यत्त्याजितवृषाः कृताः ।
दैत्या मद्भक्तिरहिताः सर्वे त्रिपुरवासिनः ॥ ३७॥

English
Let Viṣṇu or any one else slay them now that they have been made to abandon their Dharma. The Asuras of the three cities have become devoid of devotion to me.” (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति शम्भोस्तु वचनं श्रुत्वा सर्वे दिवौकसः ।
विमनस्का बभूवुस्ते हरिश्चापि मुनीश्वर ॥ ३८॥

English
O great sage, on hearing these words of Śiva, all the heaven-dwellers and Viṣṇu became dispirited. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
देवान् विष्णुमुदासीनान् दृष्ट्वा च भवकृद्विधिः ।
कृताञ्जलिपुरः शम्भुं ब्रह्मा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ३९॥

English
On seeing the gods and Viṣṇu sitting cheerless, Brahmā, the creator of the worlds spoke to Śiva with palms joined in reverence. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
न किञ्चिद्विद्यते पापं यस्मात्त्वं योगवित्तमः ।
परमेशः परब्रह्म सदा देवर्षिरक्षकः ॥ ४०॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* There is no sin in this, since you are the foremost among those who know the Yogic theory; you are the great lord, the great Brahman and the saviour of gods and sages always. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

त्यक्तस्वधर्मत्वत्पूजाः परवध्यास्तथापि न ॥ ४१॥

English
It is at your own bidding that they have been deluded. You induced them to be deluded. Although the Asuras have forsaken their duties and your worship, they cannot be killed by others. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English

[fn 188. Mlecchas are represented as violent, carnivorous, torturous, non-Aryan wild tribes who caused terror in the social life of the country.]
---

Sanskrit

English
As it is the duty of a king, you will not be sinning by their destruction. Hence, the good people, brahmins etc shall be saved and the thorns uprooted. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

---

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
हे ब्रह्मन् यद्यहं देवराजः सम्राट् प्रकीर्त्तितः ।
तत्प्रकारो न मे कश्चिद् गृह्णीयां यमिह प्रभुः ॥ ५०॥

English
*Śiva said:—* O Brahmā, if I am to be proclaimed the emperor of the gods, I do not have the paraphernalia characteristic of my lordship. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
रथो नास्ति महादिव्यस्तादृक् सारथिना सह ।
धनुर्बाणादिकं चापि सङ्ग्रामे जयकारकम् ॥ ५१॥

English
I do not have a divine chariot and a divine charioteer. I do not possess bows and arrows which accord victory in a battle. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
यमास्थाय धनुर्बाणान् गृहीत्वा योज्य वै मनः ।
निहनिष्याम्यहं दैत्यान् प्रबलानपि सङ्गरे ॥ ५२॥

English
If there had been a chariot I could have sat in it and with bow and arrows I could have killed even powerful Asuras, with a resolute determination. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अद्य सब्रह्मका देवाः सेन्द्रोपेन्द्राः प्रहर्षिताः ।
श्रुत्वा प्रभोस्तदा वाक्यं नत्वा प्रोचुर्महेश्वरम् ॥ ५३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  On hearing these words of the lord, the gods including Brahmā, Indra and Viṣṇu were delighted. After bowing to him they spoke to lord Śiva. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
वयं भवाम देवेश तत्प्रकारा महेश्वर ।
रथादिकाः तव स्वामिन्सन्नद्धाः सङ्गराय हि ॥ ५४॥

English
*The gods said:—* O lord of the gods, O great god, we shall constitute those paraphernalia—chariot etc. O lord, we are ready for the battle. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा संहताः सर्वे शिवेच्छामधिगम्य ह ।
पृथगूचुः प्रसन्नास्ते कृताञ्जलिपुटास्सुराः ॥ ५५॥

English
After saying so jointly after realising Śiva's wish they, the delighted gods, severally told him so, with palms joined in reverence. (55.)
