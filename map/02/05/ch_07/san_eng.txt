### **CHAPTER SEVEN - *(The gods pray)***

---

Sanskrit
७

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एतच्छुत्वा तु सर्वेषां देवादीनां वचो हरः ।
अङ्गीचकार सुप्रीत्या शरण्यो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of the gods and others, Śiva accepted the proposal. The lord is worthy of being sought refuge and is also favourably disposed to his devotees. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवी पुत्राभ्यां संयुता शिवा ।
आजगाम मुने तत्र यत्र देवान्वितो हरः ॥ २॥

English
O sage, in the meantime the goddess Pārvatī arrived there with the two sons where Śiva was in conversation with the gods. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथागतां शिवां दृष्ट्वा सर्वे विष्ण्वादयो द्रुतम् ।
प्रणेमुरतिनम्रास्ते विस्मिता गतसम्भ्रमाः ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing Pārvatī come there, Viṣṇu and others were surprised but without agitation they bowed to her in great humility. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रोचुर्जयेति सद्वाक्यं मुने सर्वे सुलक्षणम् ।
तूष्णीमासन्नजानन्तस्तदागमनकारणम् ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, they shouted cries of "Victory". But unable to know the cause of her arrival, they remained silent. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सर्वैः स्तुता देवैर्देव्यद्भुतकुतूहला ।
उवाच स्वामिनं प्रीत्या नानालीलाविशारदम् ॥ ५॥

English
On being eulogised by the gods, the goddess Pārvatī full of wonderful enthusiasm spoke lovingly to her lord, an expert in different kinds of sports. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
क्रीडमानं विभो पश्य षण्मुखं रविसन्निभम् ।
पुत्रं पुत्रवतां श्रेष्ठ भूषितं भूषणैर्वरैः ॥ ६॥

English
*The goddess said:—* "O lord, see the sportive six-faced¹⁸⁹ Kārttikeya, refulgent like the sun our excellent son embellished by excellent ornaments." (6.)

[fn 189. Kārttikeya is called a six-faced deity (षण्मुखः), for according to the legend Kārttikeya when born was fostered by the six Kṛttikās who offered their six breasts to him, so he became six-headed.]
---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच
इत्येवं लोकमात्रा च वाग्भिःसम्बोधितश्शिवः ।
न ययौ तृप्तिमीशानः पिबन् स्कन्दाननामृतम् ॥ ७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus addressed by the mother of the worlds with pleasing words, Lord Śiva was never satiated in drinking nectar of the beauty of Skanda's face. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
न सस्मारागतान् देवान् निजतेजोनिपीडितान् ।
स्कन्दमालिङ्ग्य चाघ्राय मुमोदाति महेश्वरः ॥ ८॥

English
He recollected the Asuras who had come (to fight and) pounded by his splendrous valour. Embracing and kissing Skanda on the head lord Śiva rejoiced much. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जगदम्बाथ तत्रैव सम्मन्त्र्य प्रभुणा च सा ।
स्थित्वा किञ्चित्समुत्तस्थौ नानालीलाविशारदा ॥ ९॥

English
Then the mother of the universe stayed there for a while and held conversation with the lord. Afterwards the goddess, an expert in different kinds of sports stood up. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स नन्दी सह षण्मुखेन
तया च सार्द्धं गिरिराजपुत्र्या ।
विवेश शम्भुर्भवनं सुलीलः
सुरैः समस्तैरभिवन्द्यमानः ॥ १०॥

English
Then Śiva of good sports entered his apartment accompanied by Nandin and Pārvatī. He was then saluted by all the gods. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वारस्य पार्श्वतः तस्थुर्देवदेवस्य धीमतः ।
तेऽथ देवा महाव्यग्रा विमनस्का मुनेऽखिलाः ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, all the gods dispirited and worried stood on either side of the doorway of mansion of Śiva, the intelligent lord of the gods. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कर्तव्यं क्व गन्तव्यं कः स्यादस्मत्सुखप्रदः ।
किं तु किन्त्विति सञ्जातं हा हताः स्मेति वादिनः ॥ १२॥

English
They began to mutter "What shall we do? Where shall we go? Who will make us happy? Everything has happened with a "but." We are doomed." (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्योन्यं प्रेक्ष्य शक्राद्या बभूवुश्चातिविह्वलाः ।
प्रोचुर्विकलवाक्यं ते धिक्कुर्वन्तो निजं विधिम् ॥ १३॥

English
Indra and others looked at one another's face. They were much agitated. They spoke in faltering words. They cursed their fate. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पापा वयमिहेत्यन्ये ह्यभाग्याश्चेति चापरे ।
ते भाग्यवन्तो दैत्येन्द्रा इति चान्येऽब्रुवन् सुराः ॥ १४॥

English
Some gods said "We are sinners." Others said "We are unfortunate." Still others said "The great Asuras are very fortunate." (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नेवान्तरे तेषां श्रुत्वा शब्दाननेकशः ।
कुम्भोदरो महातेजा दण्डेनाताडयत्सुरान् ॥ १५॥

English
In the meantime on hearing their multifarious voices, Kumbhodara¹⁹⁰ of excessive refulgence beat the gods with a baton. (15.)

[fn 190. Kālidāsa mentions Kumbhodara as an attendant of Śiva. Cf. Raghu. ii. 35.]
---

Sanskrit
दुद्रुवुस्ते भयाविष्टा देवा हाहेति वादिनः ।
अपतन्मुनयश्चान्ये विह्वलत्वं बभूव ह ॥ १६॥

English
The terrified gods shouting "Hā Hā" fled from there. The sages faltered and fell on the ground. There was excitement and great confusion. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रस्तु विकलोऽतीव जानुभ्यामवनीं गतः ।
अन्ये देवर्षयोऽतीव विकलाः पतिता भुवि ॥ १७॥

English
Unnerved and languid, Indra crawled on his knees. The celestial sages dropped to the ground. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे मिलित्वा मुनयः सुराश्च सममाकुलाः ।
सङ्गता विधिहर्योस्तु समीपं मित्रचेतसोः ॥ १८॥

English
The excessively agitated sages and gods gathered together and approached Brahmā and Viṣṇu of friendly tamperament. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो विधिबलं चैतन्मुनयः कश्यपादयः ।
वदन्ति स्म तदा सर्वे हरिं लोकभयापहम् ॥ १९॥

English
The sages Kaśyapa and others said to Viṣṇu who removes the fear of all the worlds “O this is due to our ill luck.” (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अभाग्यान्न समाप्तं तु कार्यमित्यपरे द्विजाः ।
कस्माद्विघ्नमिदं जातमित्यन्ये ह्यतिविस्मिताः ॥ २०॥

English
The other brahmins said—“Our task is not completed due to our ill luck.” Still others who were greatly surprised said—“How did this obstacle happen?” (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा कश्यपाद्युदितं मुने ।
आश्वासयन्मुनीन्देवान् हरिर्वाक्यमुपाददे ॥ २१॥

English
O sage, on hearing these words of Kaśyapa and others, Viṣṇu consoled the sages and the gods and spoke thus. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
हे देवा मुनयः सर्वे मद्वचः श‍ृणुतादरात् ।
किमर्थं दुःखमापन्ना दुखं तु त्यजताखिलम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* O gods, O sages, you listen to my words with attention. Why are you distressed? Eschew your sorrows. (22.)

---

English
O gods ponder over this. This propitiation of the great is not an easy task. It is heard that there is great difficulty at first in propitiating the great. The lord will certainly be pleased after coming to know of your resolute nature. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
महदाराधनं देवा न सुसाध्यं विचार्यताम् ।
महदाराधने पूर्वं भवेद्दुःखमिति श्रुतम् ॥

विज्ञाय दृढतां देवाः प्रसन्नो भवति ध्रुवम् । २३॥

शिवः सर्वगणाध्यक्षः सहसा परमेश्वरः ।
विचार्यतां हृदा सर्वैः कथं वश्यो भवेदिति ॥ २४॥

English
Let this be pondered over well by all of you, how can lord Śiva, the presiding deity of the Gaṇas be made favourable immediately. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणवं पूर्वमुच्चार्य नमः पश्चादुदाहरेत् ।
शिवायेति ततः पश्चाच्छुभद्वयमतः परम् ॥ २५॥

कुरुद्वयं ततः प्रोक्तं शिवाय च ततः पुनः ।
नमश्च प्रणवश्चैव मन्त्रमेवं सदा बुधाः ॥ २६॥

English
O scholars, the following mantra shall be repeated—Utter the syllable Oṅkāra first, then repeat the word Namaḥ (obeisance). Then say Śivāya (to Śiva). Then repeat Subham” twice and “Kuru” twice. Afterwards say “Śivāya Namaḥ Om.”$^{191}$ (25-26.)

[fn 191. The formula runs as follows : ओं नमः शिवाय शुभं शुभं कुरु कुरु शिवाय नमः ओं ।]
---

Sanskrit
आवर्तध्वं पुनर्यूयं यदि शम्भुकृते तदा ।
कोटिमेकं तथा जप्त्वा शिवः कार्यं करिष्यति ॥ २७॥

English
If you repeat this mantra a crore times thinking of Śiva, Śiva will carry out the task. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते च तदा तेन हरिणा प्रभविष्णुना ।
तथा देवाः पुनश्चक्रुर्हरस्याराधनं मुने ॥ २८॥

English
O sage, when this was mentioned by Viṣṇu the powerful, the gods began to propitiate Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सञ्जजाप हरिश्चापि सविधिश्शिवमानसः ।
देवानां कार्यसिद्ध्यर्थं मुनीनां च विशेषतः ॥ २९॥

English
For the fulfilment of the task of the gods and the sages, Viṣṇu and Brahmā, with minds fixed in Śiva performed the Japa. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहुः शिवेति भाषन्तो देवा धैर्यसमन्विताः ।
कोटिसङ्ख्यं तदा कृत्वा स्थितास्ते मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३०॥

English
O excellent sage, they stood there steady and repeated the mantra a crore times uttering the name “Śiva” several times. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे साक्षाच्छिवः प्रादुरभूत्स्वयम् ।
यथोक्तेन स्वरूपेण वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ ३१॥

English
In the meantime Śiva came into direct view assuming his real form and spoke. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशिव उवाच ।
हे हरे हे विधे देवा मुनयश्च शुभव्रताः ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूत जपेनानेन चेप्सितम् ॥ ३२॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—* O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O gods and O sages of auspicious rites, I am delighted by your Japa. Speak out the desired boon. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश जगदीश्वर शङ्कर ।
सुरान् विज्ञाय विकलान् हन्यन्तां त्रिपुराणि च ॥ ३३॥

English
*The gods said:—* O Śiva, lord of the gods, lord of the universe, if you are pleased, realising that the gods are unnerved, let the Tripuras be destroyed. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षास्मान्परमेशान दीनबन्धो कृपाकर ।
त्वयैव रक्षिता देवाः सदापद्भ्यो मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ३४॥

English
O lord Śiva, O merciful one, O kinsman of the distressed, save us. We, gods, have always been saved from adversities by you alone. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्तं वचनं तेषां श्रुत्वा सहरिवेधसाम् ।
विहस्यान्तस्तदा ब्रह्मन्महेशः पुनरब्रवीत् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O brahmin, on hearing these words uttered by them including Viṣṇu and Brahmā, lord Śiva laughed to himself and spoke again. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश उवाच ।
हे हरे हे विधे देवा मुनयश्चाखिला वचः ।
मदीयं श‍ृणुतादृत्य नष्टं मत्वा पुरत्रयम् ॥ ३६॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—* Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O gods, O sages all of you listen to my words with attention considering that the three cities have been already destroyed. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
रथं च सारथिं दिव्यं कार्मुकं शरमुत्तमम् ।
पूर्वमङ्गीकृतं सर्वमुपपादयताचिरम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Hence make arrangements for the chariot, charioteer, divine bow and excellent arrows as agreed to by you all. Do not delay. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
हे विष्णो हे विधे त्वं हि त्रिलोकाधिपतिर्ध्रुवम् ।
सर्वसंराट्प्रकारं मे कर्तुमर्हसि यत्नतः ॥ ३८॥

English
O Brahmā, O Viṣṇu, you are the lord of the three worlds, to be sure. Hence provide me with the paraphernalia of an emperor. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
नष्टं पुरत्रयं मत्वा देवसाहाय्यमित्युत ।
करिष्यथः प्रयत्नेनाधिकृतौ सर्गपालने ॥ ३९॥

English
You too had been entrusted with the tasks of creation and sustenance. You shall make all efforts, considering the destruction of the three cities an act of help to the gods. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं मन्त्रो महापुण्यो मत्प्रीतिजनकः शुभः ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदःसर्वकामदः शैवकावहः ॥ ४०॥

English
This mantra is highly meritorious and auspicious. It generates the pleasure of the gods. It yields both worlds by enjoyment and salvation, confers cherished desires and brings about the happiness of the devotees of Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्यो यशस्य आयुष्यः स्वर्गकामार्थिनां नृणाम् ।
अपवर्गो ह्यकामानां मुक्तानां भुक्तिमुक्तिदः ॥ ४१॥

English
It is conducive to blessedness, fame, longevity to those who seek heaven. Those who are free from desires derive the benefit of salvation. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
य इमं कीर्तयेन्मन्त्रं शुचिर्भूत्वा सदा नरः ।
श‍ृणुयाच्छ्रावयेद्वापि सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ ४२॥

English
The man who repeats this mantra in purity, hears or narrates this to anyone, shall attain all desires. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

सर्वे देवा मुदं प्रापुर्हरिर्ब्रह्माधिकं तथा ॥ ४३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Śiva, the great Ātman, the gods derived more pleasure than Viṣṇu and Brahmā. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
At his bidding, Viśvakarman made a splendid chariot of good features, consisting of all the gods, for the welfare of the people. (44.)
