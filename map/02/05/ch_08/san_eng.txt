### **CHAPTER EIGHT - *(The detailed description of the chariot etc)***

---

Sanskrit
८

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ शैवप्रवर सन्मते ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा तात श्राविता परमेशितुः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O Sanatkumāra, of good intellect, O omniscient one, O foremost among the devotees of Śiva, this wonderful story of lord Śiva has been narrated to us. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं रथनिर्माणं ब्रूहि देवमयं परम् ।
शिवार्थं यत्कृतं दिव्यं धीमता विश्वकर्मणा ॥ २॥

English
Now please mention the structure of the chariot$^{192}$ which consisted of all the gods and which had been made by the intelligent Viśvakarman. (2.)

[fn 192. The present account of the cosmic chariot made for Śiva tallies with that in the Matsya Purāṇa (chapter 133).]
---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य व्यासस्य स मुनीश्वरः ।
सनत्कुमारः प्रोवाच स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ३॥

English
On hearing these words of Vyāsa, Sanatkumāra the great sage remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and spoke thus. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाप्राज्ञ रथादेर्निर्मितिं मुने ।
यथामति प्रवक्ष्येऽहं स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O sage Vyāsa, of great intellect, listen to the description of the structure of the chariot etc which I shall give to the extent of my intellect after remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवस्य रुद्रस्य निर्मितो विश्वकर्मणा ।
सर्वलोकमयो दिव्यो रथो यत्नेन सादरम् ॥ ५॥

English
The divine chariot of lord Śiva consisting of all the worlds was built by Viśvakarman with devoted effort. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वभूतमयश्चैव सौवर्णः सर्वसम्मतः ।
रथाङ्गं दक्षिणं सूर्यस्तद्वामं सोम एव च ॥ ६॥

English
It was appreciated by all. It was golden in colour and all the elements had gone into its making. The right wheel was the sun and the left wheel was the moon. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शशिनः षोडशारास्तु कला वामस्य सुव्रत ।
ऋक्षाणि तु तथा तस्य वामस्यैव विभूषणम् ॥ ८॥

English
The right wheel had twelve spokes. O great brahmin, the twelve Ādityas presided over them. The left wheel had sixteen spokes. O you of excellent rites, the sixteen spokes of the left side wheel consisted of the sixteen digits of the moon. All the aṣṭerism; embellished the left side. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋतवो नेमयः षट् च तयोर्वै विप्रपुङ्गव ।
पुष्करं चान्तरिक्षं वै रथनीडश्च मन्दरः ॥ ९॥

English
The six seasons constituted the rims of the wheels of the chariot, O great Brahmin. The Puṣkara of the chariot was the sky. The inner side of the chariot was Mandara. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्ताद्रिरुदयाद्रिस्तु तावुभौ कूबरौ स्मृतौ ।
अधिष्ठानं महामेरुराश्रयाः केशराचलाः ॥ १०॥

English
The rising and the setting mountains constituted the poleshafts. Mahāmeru was the support and the Keśara mountains the sharp sides. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
वेगः संवत्सरास्तस्य अयने चक्रसङ्गमौ ।
मुहूर्ता बन्धुरास्तस्य शम्याश्चैव कलाः स्मृताः ॥ ११॥

English
The year constituted its velocity. The two Ayanas northern and southern constituted the junctions of the wheels and axles. The Muhūrtas constituted the joints and the Kalās the pins of the yoke. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य काष्ठाः स्मृता घोणाश्चाक्षदण्डाः क्षणाश्च वै ।
निमेषाश्चानुकर्षाश्च ईषाश्चानुलवाः स्मृताः ॥ १२॥

English
The division of time Kāṣṭhā constituted the nose of the chariot and the Kṣaṇas the axle-shaft. The Nimeṣas constituted the bottom of the carriage and the minutest divisions of time constituted the poles. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
द्यौर्वरूथं रथस्यास्य स्वर्गमोक्षावुभौ ध्वजौ ।
युगान्तकोटितौ तस्य भ्रमकामदुघौ स्मृतौ ॥ १३॥

English
The firmament constituted the fender of the chariot; Heaven and salvation the flag staffs; Abhramu and Kāmadhenu constituted its harrows at the end of the shafts. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ईषादण्डस्तथा व्यक्तं वृद्धिस्तस्यैव नड्वलः ।
कोणास्तस्याप्यहङ्कारो भूतानि च बलं स्मृतम् ॥ १४॥

English
The unmanifest principle formed their shaft and cosmic intellect the chariot's reeds. The cosmic Ego cosmic corners and elements its strength. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रियाणि च तस्यैव भूषणानि समन्ततः ।
श्रद्धा च गतिरस्यैव रथस्य मुनिसत्तम ॥ १५॥

English
O excellent sage, the cosmic sense-organs constituted the embellishments of this chariot on all sides. Faith was its movements. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तदानीं भूषणान्येव षडङ्गान्युपभूषणम् ।
पुराणन्यायमीमांसाधर्मशास्त्राणि सुव्रताः ॥ १६॥

English
The six Vedāṅgas were its ornaments. O great ones of good rites, the Purāṇas, Nyāya, Mīmāṃsā and Dharma Śāstras constituted the side trinkets. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
बलाशया वराश्चैव सर्वलक्षणसंयुताः ।
मन्त्रा घण्टाः स्मृतास्तेषां वर्णपादास्तदाश्रमाः ॥ १७॥

English
The forceful and excellent mantras with their syllables and feet, of all characteristic features and the stages in life constituted the tinkling bells. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो बन्धो ह्यनन्तस्तु सहस्रफणभूषितः ।
दिशः पादा रथस्यास्य तथा चोपदिशश्च ह ॥ १८॥

English
Ananta embellished with thousand hoods constituted its fittings and the main and subsidiary quarters, the pedestals of the chariot. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्कराद्याः पताकाश्च सौवर्णा रत्नभूषिताः ।
समुद्रास्तस्य चत्वारो रथकम्बलिनःस्मृताः ॥ १९॥

English
The clouds Puṣkara and others constituted the gem-studded banners of glowing colours. The four oceans are remembered as the bullocks of the chariot. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गाद्याः सरितश्रेष्ठाः सर्वाभरणभूषिताः ।
चामरासक्तहस्ताग्राः सर्वाः स्त्रीरूपशोभिताः ॥ २०॥

English
Gaṅgā and other rivers shining in excellent female forms and decorated in all ornaments held the Cāmaras in their hands. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तत्र कृतस्थानाः शोभयाञ्चक्रिरे रथम् ।
आवहाद्यास्तथा सप्त सोपानं हैममुत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
Taking up their places in the different parts of the chariot, they brightened it up. The seven courses of the wind Āvaha¹⁹³ etc. constituted the excellent steps of gold leading the chariot. (21.)

[fn 193. आवह, प्रवह, संवह, उहह, विवह, परिवह, परावह are the seven bands of air assigned to the atmospheric region between मूलोंक and स्वलोक।]

---

Sanskrit
लोकालोकाचलस्तस्योपसोपानाः समन्ततः ।
विषयश्च तथा बाह्यो मानसादिस्तु शोभनः ॥ २२॥

English
The Lokāloka mountain¹⁹⁴ formed its side steps. The lake mānasa etc. constituted its brilliant outer and oblique steps. (22.)

[fn 194. It is a fabulous belt of mountains bounding the outermost of the seven seas and dividing the visible world from the regions of darkness. H. M.]
---

Sanskrit
पाशाः समन्ततस्तस्य सर्वे वर्षाचलाः स्मृताः ।
तलास्तस्य रथस्याऽथ सर्वे तलनिवासिनः ॥ २३॥

English
The Varṣa mountains constituted the cords and chains all round the chariot. All the residents of the region Tala constituted the bottom surface of the chariot. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सारथिर्भगवान्ब्रह्मा देवा रश्मिधराः स्मृताः ।
प्रतोदो ब्रह्मणस्तस्य प्रणवो ब्रह्मदैवतम् ॥ २४॥

English
Lord Brahmā was the charioteer, the gods were holders of the bridle. Praṇava the Vedic divinity constituted the long whip of Brahmā. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अकारश्च महच्छत्रं मन्दरः पार्श्वदण्डभाक् ।
शैलेन्द्रः कार्मुकं तस्य ज्या भुजङ्गाधिपः स्वयम् ॥ २५॥

English
The syllable A constituted the great umbrella, Mandara the side staff. The lord of mountains became his bow and the lord of serpents the bowstring. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
घण्टा सरस्वती देवी धनुषः श्रुतिरूपिणी ।
इषुर्विष्णुर्महातेजास्त्वग्निः शल्यं प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ २६॥

English
Goddess Sarasvatī in the form of the Vedas constituted the bells of the bow. The brilliant Viṣṇu became the arrow and Agni the spear-head. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
हयास्तस्य तथा प्रोक्ताश्चत्वारो निगमा मुने ।
ज्योतींषि भूषणं तेषामवशिष्टान्यतः परम् ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, the four Vedas are said to be his horses. The remaining planets became their embellishments. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अनीकं विषसम्भूतं वायवो वादकाः स्मृताः ।
ऋषयो व्यासमुख्याश्च वाहवाहास्तथाभवन् ॥ २८॥

English
His army came up from water. The winds were his feathers, wings etc. Vyāsa and other sages were the drivers of the vehicle. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वल्पाक्षरैः सम्ब्रवीमि किं बहूक्त्या मुनीश्वर ।
ब्रह्माण्डे चैव यत्किञ्चिद्वस्तुतद्वै रथे स्मृतम् ॥ २९॥

English
O great sage, why should I dilate. I shall succinctly say. Everything in the world found a place in the chariot. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सम्यक्कृतस्तेन धीमता विश्वकर्मणा ।
सरथादिप्रकारो हि ब्रह्मविष्ण्वाज्ञया शुभः ॥ ३०॥

English
At the bidding of Brahmā and Viṣṇu the chariot and its adjuncts were created by the intelligent Viśvakarman.* (30.)

[fn *On the construction of the Cosmic Chariot compare Matsya P. Ch. 133]
