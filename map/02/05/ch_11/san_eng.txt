### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - *(The Gods' prayer)***

---

Sanskrit
११

व्यास उवाच ।
ब्रह्मपुत्र महाप्राज्ञ धन्यस्त्वं शैवसत्तम ।
किमकार्षुस्ततो देवा दग्धे च त्रिपुरेऽखिलाः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O son of Brahmā, of great intellect, O most excellent among the devotees of Śiva, you are blessed. When the three cities were burnt what did the gods do? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मयः कुत्र गतोऽदग्धो यतयः कुत्र ते गताः ।
तत्सर्वं मे समाचक्ष्व यदि शम्भुकथाश्रयम् ॥ २॥

English
Where did Maya who was spared go? Where did the ascetics go? Please narrate all, if it relates to Śiva's story. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
व्यासवाक्यं समाकर्ण्य भगवान्भवकृत्सुतः ।
सनत्कुमारः प्रोवाच शिवपादयुगं स्मरन् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—*  On hearing the words of Vyāsa, Sanatkumāra the holy son of the creator remembered the feet of Śiva and spoke. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाबुद्धे पाराशर्य्य महेशितुः ।
चरितं सर्वपापघ्नं लोकलीलानुसारिणः ॥ ४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Listen O Vyāsa, son of Parāśara, and of great intellect, to the sin-destroying story of the great lord, who follows worldly conventions. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरेण सर्वस्मिंस्त्रिपुरे दैत्यसङ्कुले ।
दग्धे विशेषतस्तत्र विस्मितास्तेऽभवन्सुराः ॥ ५॥

English
When the three cities of Asuras were utterly burnt, the Gods became surprised. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
न किञ्चिदब्रुवन्देवाः सेन्द्रोपेन्द्रादयस्तदा ।
महातेजस्विनं रुद्रं सर्वे वीक्ष्य ससम्भ्रमाः ॥ ६॥

English
The gods including Indra, Viṣṇu and others became silent and bewildered on seeing the excessively brilliant Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
महाभयङ्करं रौद्रं प्रज्वलन्तं दिशो दश ।
कोटिसूर्यप्रतीकाशं प्रलयानलसन्निभम् ॥ ७॥

भयाद्देवं निरीक्ष्यैव देवीं च हिमवत्सुताम् ।
बिभ्यिरे निखिला देवप्रमुखास्तस्थुरानताः ॥ ८॥

English
On merely seeing the terrible form of Śiva, dazzling the ten quarters, resembling countless suns in refulgence and on a par with the fire at the hour of dissolution, and also the goddess Pārvatī, the daughter of Himavat, the illustrious gods stood humbly in their fright. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वानीकं तदा भीतं देवानामृषिपुङ्गवाः ।
न किञ्चिदूचुः सन्तस्थुः प्रणेमुस्ते समन्ततः ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing the army of the gods terrified, the excellent sages did not say anything. They stood all round and bowed. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ब्रह्मापि सम्भीतो दृष्ट्वा रूपं च शाङ्करम् ।
तुष्टाव तुष्टहृदयो देवैः सह समाहितः ॥ १०॥

विष्णुना च सभीतेन देवदेवं भवं हरम् ।
त्रिपुरारिं सगिरिजं भक्ताधीनं महेश्वरम् ॥ ११॥

English
Then Brahmā too who was excessively afraid on seeing Śiva's terrible form, was delighted at heart and fervently prayed along with the gods. Viṣṇu who was also afraid prayed to Śiva the lord of the Gods, the slayer of the Tripuras, who was accompanied by his consort Pārvatī, the lord who is subservient to his devotees. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव भक्तानुग्रहकारक ।
प्रसीद परमेशान सर्वदेवहितप्रद ॥ १२॥

English
*Brahmā said: —* "O lord of the gods, O supreme lord, bestower of blessings to the devotees, be pleased, O bestower of wholesome blessings to all the gods. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसीद जगतां नाथ प्रसीदानन्ददायक ।
प्रसीद शङ्कर स्वामिन् प्रसीद परमेश्वर ॥ १३॥

English
Be pleased, O lord of the worlds, be pleased. O bestower of bliss. Be pleased, O lord Śiva. Be pleased, O supreme lord. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ओङ्काराय नमस्तुभ्यमाकारपरतारक ।
प्रसीद सर्वदेवेश त्रिपुरघ्न महेश्वर ॥ १४॥

English
Obeisance to you, of the form of Omkāra, O great lord who enable the devotees to cross the ocean of existence by your very form. Be pleased, O lord of gods, O destroyer of the Tripuras, O supreme lord. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नानावाच्याय देवाय प्रणतप्रिय शङ्कर ।
अगुणाय नमस्तुभ्यं प्रकृतेः पुरुषात्पर ॥ १५॥

English
O Śiva, O favourite of your devotees. Obeisance to you, the lord of many names. Obeisance to you, free from attributes, O you who are greater than Prakṛti and Puruṣa. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्विकाराय नित्याय नित्यतृप्ताय भास्वते ।
निरञ्जनाय दिव्याय त्रिगुणाय नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १६॥

English
Obeisance to you, free from aberrations, the eternal, the ever satiated, the resplendent, the unsullied, the divine one of three attributes. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सगुणाय नमस्तुभ्यं स्वर्गेशाय नमोस्तु ते ।
सदाशिवाय शान्ताय महेशाय पिनाकिने ॥ १७॥

English
Obeisance to you, possessed of attributes. Obeisance to you, the lord of heaven. Obeisance to the calm, trident-bearing Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वज्ञाय शरण्याय सद्योजाताय ते नमः ।
वामदेवाय रुद्राय तदाप्यपुरुषाय च ॥ १८॥

English
Obeisance to the omniscient, to one who is the refuge of all. Obeisance to you born in a trice. Obeisance to Vāmadeva, Rudra, the Puruṣa, accessible to the good. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अघोराय सुसेव्याय भक्ताधीनाय ते नमः ।
ईशानाय वरेण्याय भक्तानन्दप्रदायिने ॥ १९॥

English
Obeisance to Aghora, to one easily served. Obeisance to you, subservient to the devotees. Obeisance to Iśāna, the most excellent, the bestower of bliss to his devotees. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्ष रक्ष महादेव भीतान्नः सकलामरान् ।
दग्ध्वा च त्रिपुरं सर्वे कृतार्था अमराः कृताः ॥ २०॥

English
O great lord, save, save us all the frightened gods. By burning the three cities, the gods have been satisfied and contented.” (20.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुत्वैवं देवताःसर्वे नमस्कारं पृथक्पृथक् ।
चक्रुस्ते परमप्रीता ब्रह्माद्यास्तु सदाशिवम् ॥ २१॥

English
After eulogising thus, the gods severally bowed to him. The delighted gods, Brahmā and others, bowed to lord Śiva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ब्रह्मा स्वयं देवं त्रिपुरारिं महेश्वरम् ।
तुष्टाव प्रणतो भूत्वा नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ॥ २२॥

English
Then Brahmā himself eulogised lord Śiva the destroyer of the Tripuras after bowing to him with stooping shoulders and palms joined in reverence. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
भगवन्देवदेवेश त्रिपुरान्तक शङ्कर ।
त्वयि भक्तिः परा मेऽस्तु महादेवानपायिनी ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* “O holy lord, lord of the gods, O slayer of the Tripuras, O Śiva, O great lord, let my devotion to you remain eternal. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वदा मेऽस्तु सारथ्यं तव देवेश शङ्कर ।
अनुकूलो भव विभो सदा त्वं परमेश्वर ॥ २४॥

English
O Śiva, let me always remain your charioteer. O lord of the gods, O supreme lord, be favourable to me always.” (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा विधिः शम्भुं भक्तवत्सलमानतः ।
विरराम नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिरुदारधीः ॥ २५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After thus eulogising Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees, with humility, the liberal hearted Brahmā stopped and stood there with stooping shoulders and palms joined in reverence. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
जनार्दनोऽपि भगवान् नमस्कृत्य महेश्वरम् ।
कृताञ्जलिपुटो भूत्वा तुष्टाव च महेश्वरम् ॥ २६॥

English
Lord Viṣṇu too bowed to lord Śiva. With palms joined in reverence, he eulogised lord Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
देवाधीश महेशान दीनबन्धो कृपाकर ।
प्रसीद परमेशान कृपां कुरु नतप्रिय ॥ २७॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* O overlord of the Gods, O great lord, O merciful one, O kinsman of the distressed. Be pleased, O supreme lord. Be merciful, O favourite of those who bow to you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गुणाय नमस्तुभ्यं पुनश्च सगुणाय च ।
पुनः प्रकृतिरूपाय पुनश्च पुरुषाय च ॥ २८॥

English
Obeisance to you devoid of the attributes. Again obeisance to you possessed of attributes. Again obeisance to you of the form of Prakṛti and Puruṣa. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चाद् गुणस्वरूपाय नतो विश्वात्मने नमः ।
भक्तिप्रियाय शान्ताय शिवाय परमात्मने ॥ २९॥

English
Obeisance to you of the form of attributes. Obeisance to the soul of the universe. Obeisance to you who love devotion. Obeisance to Śiva the calm one, the great soul. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवाय रुद्राय जगतां पतये नमः ।
त्वयि भक्तिर्दृढा मेऽद्य वर्द्धमाना भवत्विति ॥ ३०॥

English
Obeisance to Sadāśiva. Obeisance to Śiva, the lord of the worlds. Let my devotion to you steadily increase. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा विररामासौ शैवप्रवरसत्तमः ।
सर्वे देवाः प्रणम्योचुस्ततस्तं परमेश्वरम् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this, lord Viṣṇu the most excellent of the great devotees of Śiva stopped. Then all the Gods bowed to him and spoke to lord Śiva. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवनाथ महादेव करुणाकर शङ्कर ।
प्रसीद जगतां नाथ प्रसीद परमेश्वर ॥ ३२॥

English
*The gods said:—* O lord of gods, O great god, O Śiva, the merciful. Be pleased O lord of the worlds. Be pleased O supreme lord. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसीद सर्वकर्ता त्वं नमामस्त्वां वयं मुदा ।
त्वयि भक्तिर्दृढास्माकं नित्यं स्यादनपायिनी ॥ ३३॥

English
Be pleased. You are the creator of every thing. We bow to you joyously. Let our devotion to you be steady and endless. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति स्तुतश्च देवेशो ब्रह्मणा हरिणामरैः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus eulogised by Brahmā, Viṣṇu and the Gods, Śiva the benefactor of the worlds, the delighted lord of the gods, replied. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
हे विधे हे हरे देवाः प्रसन्नोऽस्मि विशेषतः ।
मनोऽभिलषितं ब्रूत वरं सर्वे विचारतः ॥ ३५॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O Brahmā, O Viṣṇu, O gods, I am very much pleased with you all. All of you consider carefully and then let me know the boon you desire. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमारः उवाच ।
इत्युक्तं वचनं श्रुत्वा हरेण मुनिसत्तम ।
प्रत्यूचुः सर्वदेवाश्च प्रसन्नेनान्तरात्मना ॥ ३६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words mentioned by Śiva, O excellent sage, all the Gods replied delightedly. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे देवा ऊचुः ।
यदि प्रसन्नो भगवन्यदि देयो वरस्त्वया ।
देवदेवेश चास्मभ्यं ज्ञात्वा दासान्हि नः सुरान् ॥ ३७॥

यदा दुःखं तु देवानां सम्भवेद्देवसत्तम ।
तदा त्वं प्रकटो भूत्वा दुःखं नाशय सर्वदा ॥ ३८॥

English
*The gods said:—* O lord, if you are pleased, if the boon is to be granted by you to us, O lord of the master of gods, after knowing that we the gods are your slaves, then O most excellent deity, be pleased to appear always whenever misery befalls us and destroy the misery. (37-38.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो भगवान् रुद्रो ब्रह्मणा हरिणामरैः ।
युगपत्प्राह तुष्टात्मा तथेत्यस्तु निरन्तरम् ॥ ३९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus requested simultaneously by Brahmā, Viṣṇu and the gods, Rudra was pleased in his mind and he said “Let it be ever so. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तवैरेतैश्च तुष्टोऽस्मि दास्यामि सर्वदा ध्रुवम् ।
यदभीष्टतमं लोके पठतां श‍ृण्वतां सुराः ॥ ४०॥

English
I am delighted by these hymns. O gods, I shall confer on those who read, recite and hear these hymns whatever they crave for”. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा शङ्करः प्रीतो देवदुःखहरः सदा ।
सर्वदेवप्रियं यद्वै तत्सर्वं च प्रदत्तवान् ॥ ४१॥

English
Saying this, the delighted Śiva the remover of the distress of gods, gave them every thing that was highly delightful to all the gods. (41.)
