### **CHAPTER TWELVE - *(The Gods go back to their abodes)***

---

Sanskrit
१२

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शम्भुं प्रसन्नं वीक्ष्य दानवः ।
तत्राजगाम सुप्रीतो मयोऽदग्धः कृपाबलात् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* In the meantime the Asura Maya who was not burnt due to the strength of grace, came there on seeing Śiva delighted. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणनाम हरं प्रीत्या सुरानन्यानपि ध्रुवम् ।
कृताञ्जलिर्नतस्कन्धः प्रणनाम पुनस्शिवम् ॥ २॥

English
With great delight he bowed to Śiva and other gods. With palms joined in reverence and with stooping shoulders he bowed to Śiva again. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोत्थाय शिवं दृष्ट्वा प्रेम्णा गद्गदसुस्वरः ।
तुष्टाव भक्तिपूर्णात्मा स दानववरो मयः ॥ ३॥

English
Then he got up. Maya the foremost among the Asuras, with his mind full of devotion and voice choked with emotions of love he eulogised facing Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मय उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव भक्तवत्सल शङ्कर ।
कल्पवृक्षस्वरूपोऽसि सर्वपक्षविवर्जितः ॥ ४॥

English
*Maya said:—* O great lord, lord of the Gods and favourably disposed to your devotees, O Śiva, you are in the form of the wish-yielding Kalpa tree and devoid of special leaning to any side. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतीरूपो नमस्तेऽस्तु विश्वरूप नमोऽस्तु ते ।
नमः पूतात्मने तुभ्यं पावनाय नमो नमः ॥ ५॥

English
Obeisance to you O splendour-formed, obeisance to you omniformed; obeisance to you, O sanctified soul; obeisance to you, O holy one. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्ररूपाय नित्याय रूपातीताय ते नमः ।
दिव्यरूपाय दिव्याय सुदिव्याकृतये नमः ॥ ६॥

English
Obeisance to you of variegated-forms; to you, the eternal one; obeisance to you who extend beyond all forms. Obeisance to you of divine forms, shapes, and features. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः प्रणतसर्वार्तिनाशकाय शिवात्मने ।
कर्त्रे भर्त्रे च संहर्त्रे त्रिलोकानां नमो नमः ॥ ७॥

English
Obeisance to the destroyer of the distress of those who bow to you; obeisance to the welfare-hearted; to the creator, sustainer and annihilator of the three worlds. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तिगम्याय भक्तानां नमस्तुभ्यं कृपालवे ।
तपःसत्फलदात्रे ते शिवाकान्त शिवेश्वर ॥ ८॥

English
O Śiva, O consort of Pārvatī, obeisance to you who are accessible through devotion of the devotees; obeisance to the compassionate and the bestower of the good fruits of penance. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
न जानामि स्तुतिं कर्तुं स्तुतिप्रिय परेश्वर ।
प्रसन्नो भव सर्वेश पाहि मां शरणागतम् ॥ ९॥

English
O great lord, fond of eulogy, I know not how to eulogise you. O lord of all, be pleased. Save me who have sought refuge in you." (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य मयोक्तां हि संस्तुतिं परमेश्वरः ।
प्रसन्नोऽभूद् द्विजश्रेष्ठ मयं प्रोवाच चादरात् ॥ १०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing this eulogy of Maya, O excellent brahmin, lord Śiva, was delighted and he spoke to Maya eagerly. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
वरं ब्रूहि प्रसन्नोऽहं मय दानवसत्तम ।
मनोऽभिलषितं यत्ते तद्दास्यामि न संशयः ॥ ११॥

English
*Śiva said:—* O Maya, I am delighted. O excellent Asura speak out the boon you wish to have. There is no doubt. I shall grant you what you desire. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श्रुत्वा शिवं वचः शम्भोः स मयो दानवर्षभः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रभुं नत्वा नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ॥ १२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the auspicious words of Śiva, Maya the foremost among the Asuras spoke after bowing to the lord with stooping shoulders and palms joined in reverence. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
मय उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव प्रसन्नो यदि मे भवान् ।
वरयोग्योऽस्म्यहं चेद्धि स्वभक्तिं देहि शाश्वतीम् ॥ १३॥

English
*Maya said:—* "O great lord, lord of the Gods, if you are delighted and if I deserve the grant of a boon please grant me permanent devotion to you. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभक्तेषु सदा सख्यं दीनेषु च दयां सदा ।
उपेक्षामन्यजीवेषु खलेषु परमेश्वर ॥ १४॥

English
O supreme lord, grant me comradeship with your devotees for ever, compassion towards the distressed and indifference towards the wicked living beings. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कदापि नासुरो भावो भवेन्मम महेश्वर ।
निर्भयः स्यां सदा नाथ मग्नस्त्वद्भजने शुभे ॥ १५॥

English
O lord Śiva, let there be no demonaic instinct in me at any time. O lord, let me be fearless for ever engrossed in your auspicious worship." (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति सम्प्रार्थ्यमानस्तु शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ।
प्रत्युवाच मयं नाथः प्रसन्नो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On being thus requested, Śiva the great lord, who is favourably disposed to his devotees and was in a delightful mood replied to Maya. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
दानवर्षभ धन्यस्त्वं मद्भक्तो निर्विकारवान् ।
प्रदत्तास्ते वराः सर्वेऽभीप्सिता ये तवाधुना ॥ १७॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—* O excellent Asuras you are my devotees and are blessed. You are free from aberrations. All the boons desired by you are granted now. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
गच्छ त्वं वितलं लोकं रमणीयं दिवोऽपि हि ।
समेतः परिवारेण निजेन मम शासनात् ॥ १८॥

English
At my bidding, you go to the region Vīṭala,²⁰¹ more beautiful than heaven. Go in the company of your family and kinsmen. (18.)

[fn ²⁰¹. This is the second of the seven regions descending from the earth. Cf. Note 210 P. 247.]
---

Sanskrit
निर्भयस्तत्र सन्तिष्ठ संहृष्टो भक्तिमान्सदा ।
कदापि नासुरो भावो भविष्यति मदाज्ञया ॥ १९॥

English
You stay there without fear. Be devout always. At my bidding you will never have demonaic instinct. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याज्ञां शिरसाधाय शङ्करस्य महात्मनः ।
तं प्रणम्य सुरांश्चापि वितलं प्रजगाम सः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—* Receiving this behest of Śiva, the great soul with bowing head and paying homage to him and to the gods he went to Vīṭala. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे ते वै मुण्डिनश्च समागताः ।
प्रणम्योचुश्च तान्सर्वान्विष्णुब्रह्मादिकान् सुरान् ॥ २१॥

English
In the meantime those heretics of tonsured heads came there, knelt before Viṣṇu, Brahmā and others and spoke. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कुत्र याम वयं देवाः कर्म किं करवामहे ।
आज्ञापयत नः शीघ्रं भवदादेशकारकान् ॥ २२॥

English
O gods, where shall we go? What shall we do now? We are ready to carry out your behests. Please command us quickly. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं दुष्कर्म चास्माभिर्हे हरे हे विधे सुराः ।
दैत्यानां शिवभक्तानां शिवभक्तिर्विनाशिता ॥ २३॥

English
O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O gods, wicked deeds have been performed by us. We have destroyed the devotion to Śiva of all the Asuras who were great devotees of Śiva. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिकल्पानि नरके नो वासस्तु भविष्यति ।
नोद्धारो भविता नूनं शिवभक्तविरोधिनाम् ॥ २४॥

English
We will have to stay in hell for a countless Kalpas. Certainly there is no redemption for us that have offended devotees of Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
परन्तु भवदिच्छात इदं दुष्कर्म नः कृतम् ।
तच्छान्तिं कृपया ब्रूत वयं वः शरणागताः ॥ २५॥

English
But it was in accordance with your desire that this wicked deed was perpetrated. Please tell us the mode of atoning for the same. We have sought refuge in you. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

अब्रुवन्मुण्डिनस्तांस्ते स्थितानग्रे कृताञ्जलीन् ॥ २६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—* On hearing their words Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods spoke to the tonsured-heads who stood in front with joined palms. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Viṣṇu and others said :—* “O tonsured ones, you need not be afraid at all. These excellent activities have taken place at the bidding of Śiva. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Since you are the servants of Śiva and have carried out the activities conducive to the welfare of the gods and the sages, no mishap shall ever befall you bringing you to distress. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Śiva performs deeds conducive to the welfare of the gods and the sages. He is pleased with those who work for the welfare of the gods and sages. No mishap befalls those who work for the welfare of the gods and sages. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
From now onwards in the Kali age those who follow this cult will be faced with disastrous results. We tell you the truth. There is no doubt about it. (30.)

When the Kali age begins, you can propagate your cult. In the Kali age deluded fools will follow your cult. (32.)

Thus bidden by the great gods, O great sage, the tonsured heads bowed to them and went to their allotted abode. (33.)

Sanskrit
