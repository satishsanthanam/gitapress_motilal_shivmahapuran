### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - *(The Resuscitation of Indra in the context of the destruction of Jalandhara)***

---

Sanskrit
१३

व्यास उवाच ।
भो ब्रह्मन्भगवन्पूर्वं श्रुतं मे ब्रह्मपुत्रक ।
जलन्धरं महादैत्यमवधीच्छङ्करः प्रभुः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O holy lord, son of Brahmā, it has been heard by me before that the lord Śiva killed the great Asura Jalandhara. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्त्वं वद महाप्राज्ञ चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
विस्तारपूर्वकं श‍ृण्वन्कस्तृप्येत्तद्यशोऽमलम् ॥ २॥

English
O intelligent one, please narrate the story of the moon-crested lord in detail. Who can be satiated with listening to the spotless glory of the lord ? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं व्याससम्पृष्टो ब्रह्मपुत्रो महामुनिः ।
उवाचार्थवदव्यग्रं वाक्यं वाक्यविशारदः ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On being requested thus by Vyāsa, the great sage and son of Brahmā of eloquent speech spoke the following significant words without excitement. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एकदा जीवशक्रौ च भक्त्या परमया मुने ।
दर्शनं कर्तुमीशस्य कैलासं जग्मतुर्भृशम् ॥ ४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O sage, once Bṛhaspati and Indra went to Kailāsa with great devotion, to see lord Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ गुर्विन्द्रयोर्ज्ञात्वाऽऽगमनं शङ्करः प्रभुः ।
परीक्षितुं तयोर्ज्ञानं स्वदर्शनरतात्मनोः ॥ ५॥

महातेजस्विनं शान्तं जटासम्बद्धमस्तकम् ।
महाबाहुं महोरस्कं गौरं नयनभीषणम् ॥ ६॥

English
Coming to know of the arrival of Bṛhaspati and Indra eager to see him, lord Śiva wished to test their knowledge. Accordingly, the lord, the excellent goal of the good, stood blocking their path in the naked form with matted hair and beaming face. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तौ गुरुशक्रौ च कुर्वन्तौ गमनं मुदा ।
आलोक्य पुरुषं भीमं मार्गमध्येऽद्भुताकृतिम् ॥ ७॥

अथो पुरन्दरोऽपृच्छत्स्वाधिकारेण दुर्मदः ।
पुरुषं तं स्वमार्गान्तः स्थितमज्ञाय शङ्करम् ॥ ९॥

English
Bṛhaspati and Indra were walking on gleefully. On their way they saw this wonderful person of huge size. He was quiet and composed and very refulgent with matted hair on his head. He was fair-complexioned with long arms and wide chest. He was terrible to look at. Without realising that the person who stood there blocking their path was Śiva himself, Indra who was proud of his authority said to him. (7-9.)

Sanskrit
पुरन्दर उवाच ।

स्वस्थाने संस्थितः शम्भुः किं वान्यत्र गतः प्रभुः ॥ १०॥

English
Indra said :— O, who are you ? Where have you come from ? What is your name ? Tell me truly. Is the lord Śiva in his apartment or has he gone anywhere ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  O sage, on being asked by Indra thus, he did not say anything. Indra asked him again. But the naked person did not say anything. (11.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Indra, the supreme lord of the worlds, asked again. The lord the great Yagin who assumes forms variously kept quiet. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

नोवाच किञ्चिद्भगवान् शक्रज्ञानपरीक्षया ॥ १३॥

English
The naked lord, though asked repeatedly by Indra, did not say anything, for he wanted to test the knowledge of Indra. (13.)

---

Sanskrit

उवाच वचनं चैव तं निर्भर्त्स्य जटाधरम् ॥ १४॥

English
Then the lord of Gods, proud of the wealth of the three worlds, became enraged. Rebuking the lord with matted hair he spoke these words. (14.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Indra said :—  “O evil-minded one, though asked you did not reply to me. Hence I am going to kill you with my thunderbolt. Who can save you?” (15.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Sanatkumāra said :— After saying this and looking at him ferociously Indra raised his thunderbolt in order to kill him. (16.)

---

Sanskrit

चकार स्तम्भनं तस्य वज्रपातस्य शङ्करः ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing Indra lifting up his thunderbolt, Śiva prevented the fall of the thunderbolt by making his hand benumbed. (17.)

Sanskrit

द्रुतमेव प्रजज्वाल तेजसा प्रदहन्निव ॥ १८॥

English
Then Śiva became furious. His eyes became terrible. He blazed with his burning splendour. (18.)

Sanskrit

Then Indra burnt within himself by the benumbing of his arm like a serpent whose exploits had been curbed by pronouncing magical formulas.²⁰³ (19.)

[fn 203. For the similarity of idea and verbal expression, compare Kālidāsa’s Raghuvamśa II. 32.]

Sanskrit

English

English

गुरुशक्रौ भयान्मुक्तौ जग्मतुः सुखमुत्तमम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Then the lord Rudra of great divine sports vanished from the scene. Bṛhaspati and Indra were relieved of their fright and they became happy. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
यदर्थं गमनोद्युक्तौ दर्शनं प्राप्य तस्य वै ।
कृतार्थौ गुरुशक्रौ हि स्वस्थानं जग्मतुर्मुदा ॥ ५२॥

English
After having the immediate perception of Śiva for which they had come here, Bṛhaspati and Indra became contented and went away to their abodes joyously. (52.)
