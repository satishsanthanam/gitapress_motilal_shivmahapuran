### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - *(The birth of Jalandhara and his marriage)***

---

Sanskrit
१४

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ ब्रह्मपुत्र नमोऽस्तु ते ।
श्रुतेयमद्भुता मेऽद्य कथा शम्भोर्महात्मनः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said :—*  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, son of Brahmā, obeisance be to you. This wonderful story of Śiva, the great soul, has been heard. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षिप्ते स्वतेजसि ब्रह्मन्भालनेत्रसमुद्भवे ।
लवणाम्भसि किं ताताभवत्तत्र वदाशु तत् ॥ २॥

English
O sage, when the brilliance born of the eye in the forehead had been cast off into the briny ocean, O dear sir, what happened ? Please narrate it quickly. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात महाप्राज्ञ शिवलीलां महाद्भुताम् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा श्रद्धया भक्तो योगिनां गतिमाप्नुयात् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O dear one of great intellect, listen to the extremely wonderful sport of Śiva, on hearing which with faith a devotee attains the goal of Yogins. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो शिवस्य तत्तेजो भालनेत्रसमुद्भवम् ।
क्षिप्तं च लवणाम्भोधौ सद्यो बालत्वमाप ह ॥ ४॥

English
The brilliance of Śiva born of the eye in the forehead and cast off into the briny sea$^{207}$ immediately assumed the form of a boy. (4.)

[fn 207. The ocean of salt (lavanāmbhodhi) stands to the west of Bhāratavarṣa and is identical with the Arabian sea. The confluence of the Indus and this ocean (Sindhu-Sāgara-saṅgama) is the place where Jalandhara was born.]
---

Sanskrit
तत्र वै सिन्धुगङ्गायाः सागरस्य च सङ्गमे ।
रुरोदोच्चैः स वै बालः सर्वलोकभयङ्करः ॥ ५॥

English
At the confluence of the river Gaṅgā and the ocean, the boy of terrific features cried loudly. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
रुदतस्तस्य शब्देन प्राकम्पद्धरणी मुहुः ।
स्वर्गश्च सत्यलोकश्च तत्स्वनाद् बधिरीकृतः ॥ ६॥

English
At the sound of the crying boy, the earth quaked frequently. The heaven and the Satyaloka became deafened at the noise. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
बालस्य रोदनेनैव सर्वे लोकाश्च तत्रसुः ।
सर्वतो लोकपालाश्च विह्वलीकृतमानसाः ॥ ७॥

English
All the worlds were frightened. The guardians of the quarters became agitated in the mind (7.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन विप्रेन्द्र चचाल सचराचरम् ।
भुवनं निखिलं तात रोदनात्तच्छिशोर्विभो ॥ ८॥

English
O dear holy one, O great brahmin, the entire world including the mobile and immobile quaked at the cries of the boy. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते व्याकुलाः सर्वे देवाः समुनयो द्रुतम् ।
पितामहं लोकगुरुं ब्रह्माणं शरणं ययुः ॥ ९॥

English
Then the distressed gods and the sages immediately sought refuge in Brahmā the grandfather and preceptor of the worlds. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा च ते देवा मुनयश्च सवासवाः ।
प्रणम्य च सुसंस्तुत्य प्रोचुस्तं परमेष्ठिनम् ॥ १०॥

English
After going there, those sages and the gods including Indra bowed to and eulogised Brahmā and spoke these words. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
लोकाधीश सुराधीश भयं नः समुपस्थितम् ।
तन्नाशय महायोगिन् जातोऽयं ह्यद्भुतो रवः ॥ ११॥

English
*The gods said:—* “This mysterious sound has arisen. O lord of worlds, O lord of gods, we are frightened. O great Yogin please quell it.” (11.)

---

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—* On hearing their words, Brahmā the grandfather of the worlds wished to go there. He was perplexed as to what it was. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ॥

गन्तुमैच्छत्ततस्तत्र किमेतदिति विस्मितः । १२॥

ततो ब्रह्मा सुरैस्तातावतरत्सत्यलोकतः ।
रसां तज्ज्ञातुमिच्छन्स समुद्रमगमत्तदा ॥ १३॥

English
Then Brahmā descended from Satyaloka to the Earth along with the gods. Then he went to the ocean desirous of knowing what it was. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्तत्रागतो ब्रह्मा सर्वलोकपितामहः ।
तावत्समुद्रस्योत्सङ्गे तं बालं स ददर्श ह ॥ १४॥

English
When Brahmā the grandfather of the worlds came there, he saw the boy in the lap of the ocean. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतं विधिमालोक्य देवरूप्यथ सागरः ।
प्रणम्य शिरसा बालं तस्योत्सङ्गे न्यवेशयत् ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing Brahmā coming, the ocean assuming the form of a god bowed to him and placed the boy in his lap. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो ब्रह्माब्रवीद्वाक्यं सागरं विस्मयान्वितः ।
जलराशे द्रुतं ब्रूहि कस्यायं शिशुरद्भुतः ॥ १६॥

English
Then the surprised Brahmā spoke these words to the ocean—“O ocean, tell me quickly about the parentage of this boy.” (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
ब्रह्मणो वाक्यमाकर्ण्य मुदितः सागरस्तदा ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रजेशं स नत्वा स्तुत्वा कृताञ्जलिः ॥ १७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of Brahmā, the ocean was delighted. After bowing to and eulogising him with palms joined in reverence he replied to Prajāpati Brahmā. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्र उवाच ।
भो भो ब्रह्मन्मया प्राप्तो बालकोऽयमजानता ।
प्रभवं सिन्धुगङ्गायामकस्मात्सर्वलोकप ॥ १८॥

English
*The ocean said :—*  “O Brahmā, O lord of the worlds, this boy was suddenly seen in the confluence of the river Gaṅgā. I do not know about the origin of this boy. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
जातकर्मादिसंस्कारान्कुरुष्वास्य जगद्गुरो ।
जातकोक्तफलं सर्वं विधातर्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ १९॥

English
O preceptor of the universe, you perform the post-natal rites for this boy. O creator, let me know your predictions about his future according to his horoscope” (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवं वदति पाथोधौ स बालः सागरात्मजः ।
ब्रह्माणमग्रहीत्कण्ठे विधुन्वन्तं मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Even as the ocean said these words, the son of the ocean caught hold of the neck of Brahmā and shook it several times. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विधूननं च तस्यैवं सर्वलोककृतो विधेः ।
पीडितस्य च कालेय नेत्राभ्यामगमज्जलम् ॥ २१॥

English
In due course tears came out of the eyes of Brahmā, the creator of all the worlds, afflicted by the joggling and jolting. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कराभ्यामब्धिजातस्य तत्सुतस्य महौजसः ।
कथञ्चिन्मुक्तकण्ठस्तु ब्रह्मा प्रोवाच सादरम् ॥ २२॥

English
Brahmā somehow extricated himself from the grip of the son of the ocean by means of his hands and spoke to the ocean. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु सागर वक्ष्यामि तवास्य तनयस्य हि ।
जातकोक्तफलं सर्वं समाधानरतः खलु ॥ २३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* “O ocean, listen, I shall narrate the future as predicted from the horoscope, entirely. Be attentive please. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नेत्राभ्यां विधृतं यस्मादनेनैव जलं मम ।
तस्माज्जलन्धरेतीह ख्यातो नाम्ना भवत्वसौ ॥ २४॥

English
Since he was able to make my eyes water let him be famous in the name of Jalandhara. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अधुनैवैष तरुणः सर्वशास्त्रार्थपारगः ।
महापराक्रमो धीरो योद्धा च रणदुर्मदः ॥ २५॥

भविष्यति च गम्भीरः त्वं यथा समरे गुहः ।
सर्वजेता च सङ्ग्रामे सर्वसम्पद्विराजितः ॥ २६॥

English
He will become a youth now itself. He will become a master of all sacred lores, very valorous, courageous, heroic, invincible and majestic like you. Like Kārttikeya he will be the conqueror of all in battles. He will shine with all sorts of prosperity. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यानामधिपो बालः सर्वेषां च भविष्यति ।
विष्णोरपि भवेज्जेता न कुतश्चित्पराभवः ॥ २७॥

English
This boy will become the emperor of Asuras. He will conquer even Viṣṇu. He will face defeat from no quarter. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अवध्यः सर्वभूतानां विना रुद्रं भविष्यति ।
यत एष समुद्भूतस्तत्रेदानीं गमिष्यति ॥ २८॥

English
He cannot be slain by any one except Śiva. He will return to the place from where he sprang up. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिव्रतास्य भविता पत्नी सौभाग्यवर्द्धिनी ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरी रम्या प्रियवाक्छीलसागरा ॥ २९॥

English
His wife will be a chaste lady who will increase good fortune. She will be exquisitely beautiful in every limb. She will be an ocean of good conduct and will speak pleasing words. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा शुक्रमाहूय राज्ये तं चाभ्यषेचयत् ।
आमन्त्र्य सरितां नाथं ब्रह्मान्तर्द्धानमन्वगात् ॥ ३०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying so he called Śukra and performed his coronation. Brahmā then took leave of the ocean and disappeared. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तद्दर्शनोत्फुल्लनयनः सागरस्तदा ।
तमात्मजं समादाय स्वगेहमगमन्मुदा ॥ ३१॥

English
Thereafter the ocean with blooming eyes saw the son, took him to his abode joyously. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अपोषयन्महोपायैः स्वबालं मुदितात्मकः ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरं रम्यं महाद्भुतसुतेजसम् ॥ ३२॥

English
With a joyous heart he nurtured the boy with diverse great means. The boy grew into a beautiful youth of exquisite limbs and wonderful splendour. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथाम्बुधिः समाहूय कालनेमिं महासुरम् ।
वृन्दाभिधां सुतां तस्य तद्भार्यार्थमयाचत ॥ ३३॥

English
Then the ocean invited the great Asura Kālanemi and requested him to give his daughter named Vṛndā in marriage to his son. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
कालनेम्यसुरो वीरोऽसुराणां प्रवरः सुधीः ।
साधु मेनेम्बुधेर्याच्ञां स्वकर्मनिपुणो मुने ॥ ३४॥

English
O sage, the heroic Asura Kālanemi<sup>208</sup> foremost among the Asuras, intelligent and efficient in his activities, welcomed the request of the ocean. (34.)

[fn 208. Kālanemi, the great Asura, was the son of Virocana and the great-grandson of Hiraṇyakasipu. His daughter Vṛndā was married to Jalandhara.]
---

Sanskrit
जलन्धराय वीराय सागरप्रभवाय च ।
ददौ ब्रह्मविधानेन स्वसुतां प्राणवल्लभाम् ॥ ३५॥

English
He gave his beloved daughter to Jalandhara, the brave son of the ocean, in marriage performing the nuptial rites according to the Brāhma style.* (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोत्सवो महानासीद्विवाहे च तयोस्तदा ।
सुखं प्रापुर्नदा नद्योऽसुराश्चैवाखिला मुने ॥ ३६॥

English
O sage, great festivities were held in the marriage. The rivers and Asuras were happy. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्रोऽति सुखं प्राप सुतं दृष्ट्वा हि सस्त्रियम् ।
दानं ददौ द्विजातिभ्योऽप्यन्येभ्यश्च यथाविधि ॥ ३७॥

English
The ocean too became extremely happy seeing his son united to a bride. In accordance with the rules he made charitable gifts to the brahmins and others. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ये देवैर्निर्जिताः पूर्वं दैत्याः पातालसंस्थिताः ।
ते हि भूमण्डलं याता निर्भयाः तमुपाश्रिताः ॥ ३८॥

English
Those Asuras who had been formerly defeated by the gods and had sought shelter in Pātāla came fearlessly to the Earth and resorted to him. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ते कालनेमिप्रमुखास्ततोऽसुराः
तस्मै सुतां सिन्धुसुताय दत्त्वा ।
बभूवुरत्यन्तमुदान्विता हि
तमाश्रिता देवविनिर्जयाय ॥ ३९॥

English
Kālanemi and other Asuras were pleased after giving the daughter in marriage to the son of the ocean. In order to defeat the gods they resorted to him. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
स चापि वीरोऽम्बुधिबालकोऽसौ
जलन्धराख्योऽसुरवीरवीरः ।
सम्प्राप्य भार्यामतिसुन्दरीं वशी
चकार राज्यं हि कविप्रभावात् ॥ ४०॥

English
The heroic son of the ocean, Jalandhara, foremost among the Asura warriors, received a very beautiful lady as his wife and he ruled over the kingdom with the support of Śukrā. (40.)
