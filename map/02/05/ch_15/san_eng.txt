### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - *(The fight between the gods and Jalandhara)***

---

Sanskrit
१५

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एकदा वारिधिसुतो वृन्दापतिरुदारधीः ।
सभार्यः संस्थितो वीरोऽसुरैः सर्वैः समन्वितः ॥ १॥

English
Once the son of the ocean, the noble-hearted husband of Vṛndā, was seated along with his wife and the Asuras. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राजगाम सुप्रीतः सुवर्चास्त्वथ भार्गवः ।
तेजः पुञ्जो मूर्त इव भासयन्सकला दिशः ॥ २॥

English
The brilliant Bhārgava came there joyously illuminating the ten quarters as the embodied brilliance. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा गुरुमायान्तमसुरास्तेऽखिला द्रुतम् ।
प्रणेमुः प्रीतमनसः सिन्धुपुत्रोऽपि सादरम् ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the preceptor coming, the Asuras were delighted in their minds and bowed to him. The son of the ocean too respectfully bowed to him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दत्त्वाशीर्वचनं तेभ्यो भार्गवस्तेजसां निधिः ।
निषसादासने रम्ये सन्तस्थुस्तेऽपि पूर्ववत् ॥ ४॥

English
After bestowing his benediction on them, Bhārgava, the storehouse of splendour, sat on a beautiful seat. They too resumed their seats as before. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सिन्ध्वात्मजो वीरो दृष्ट्वा प्रीत्या निजां सभाम् ।
जलन्धरः प्रसन्नोऽभूदनष्टवरशासनः ॥ ५॥

English
Then the heroic son of the ocean, Jalandhara, saw his Assembly and was delighted to observe that his sway was unmitigated. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्स्थितं छिन्नशिरसं दृष्ट्वा राहुं स दैत्यराट् ।
पप्रच्छ भार्गवं शीघ्रमिदं सागरनन्दनः ॥ ६॥

English
Seeing the headless Rāhu$^{209}$ seated there, the son of the ocean, the emperor of the Asuras, immediately asked Bhārgava. (6.)

[fn 209. The Asura Rāhu, son of Vipracitti and Siṃbikā, is known as the guardian of the south-west quarter. It is said that when the gods produced the Amṛta by churning the ocean, he disguised himself as a god and drank some of it. But he was detected by the gods and produced before Viṣṇu who cut off his head.]

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
केनेदं विहितं राहोः शिरश्च्छेदनकं प्रभो ।
तद्ब्रूहि निखिलं वृत्तं यथावत्तत्त्वतो गुरो ॥ ७॥

English
*Jalandhara said:—*  O lord, by whom was this done to Rāhu? By whom was his head cut? Please tell me, O preceptor, everything in detail as it had happened. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं प्रत्युवाच यथार्थवत् ॥ ८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of the ocean's son, Bhārgava remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and replied exactly as it had happened. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्र उवाच ।
जलन्धर महावीर सर्वासुरसहायक ।
श‍ृणु वृत्तान्तमखिलं यथावत्कथयामि ते ॥ ९॥

English
*Bhārgava said:—*  O Jalandhara, O great hero, O benefactor of the Asuras, listen to the account. I shall relate everything exactly as it had happened. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराऽभवद् बलिर्वीरो विरोचनसुतो बली ।

English
Once there was a strong hero Bali, the son of Virocana and great-grandson of Hiraṇyakaśipu. He was foremost among the virtuous. (10.)

---

Sanskrit

सवासवाः स्ववृत्तान्तमाचख्युः स्वार्थसाधकाः ॥ ११॥

English
The gods including Indra being defeated by him sought refuge in Viṣṇu. Eager to gain their ends they told him all details. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाज्ञयाऽसुरैः सार्द्धं चक्रुः सन्धिमथो सुराः ।
स्वकार्यसिद्धये तात छलकर्मविचक्षणाः ॥ १२॥

English
O dear, at his bidding, the gods, very clever in deception, made an alliance with the Asuras, to further their own interest. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

विष्णोः सहायिनस्ते हि सुराः सर्वेऽसुरैस्सह ॥ १३॥

English
All those gods, the assistants of Viṣṇu churned the ocean eagerly for the gain of nectar, along with the Asuras. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो रत्नोपहरणमकार्षुर्दैत्यशत्रवः ।
जगृहुर्यत्नतो देवाः पपुरप्यमृतं छलात् ॥ १४॥

English
The enemies of the Asuras extracted jewels from the ocean. The gods seized the nectar and drank it deceitfully. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पराभवं चक्रुरसुराणां सहायतः ।
विष्णोः सुराः सशक्रास्तेऽमृतपानाद्बलान्विताः ॥ १५॥

English
Then the gods including Indra increased in strength and prowess by the drinking of the nectar and harassed the Asuras with the assistance of Viṣṇu. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरश्छेदं चकारासौ पिबतश्चामृतं हरिः ।
राहोर्देवसभायां वै पक्षपाती हरेस्सदा ॥ १६॥

English
This Viṣṇu who is always a partisan of Indra, cut off the head of Rāhu as he was drinking the nectar along with the gods. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवं कविस्तस्य शिरश्छेदं राहोः शशंस च ।
अमृतार्थे समुद्रस्य मन्थनं देवकारितम् ॥ १७॥

रत्नोपहरणं चैव दैत्यानां च पराभवम् ।
देवैरमृतपानं च कृतं सर्वं च विस्तरात् ॥ १८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus Bhārgava narrated in detail the story of the headless Rāhu, of the churning of the ocean pursued by the gods for the gain of nectar, of the removal of the jewels, of the drinking of the Amṛta by the gods and of the harassment to the Asuras. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit

चुक्रोध क्रोधरक्ताक्षः स्वपितुर्मन्थनं तदा ॥ १९॥

English
Then on hearing about the churning of his father, the heroic son of the ocean, the valorous Jalandhara became furious and his eyes turned red with anger. (19.)

Sanskrit

English
Then he called his excellent emissary Ghasmara and told him everything what the wise preceptor had said to him. (20.)

Sanskrit

English
He then lovingly honoured the clever emissary in various ways, assured him of protection and sent him to Indra as his messenger. (21.)

English
Ghasmara, the intelligent emissary of Jalandhara, hastened to heaven$^{210}$ where all the gods were present. (22.)

[fn 210. Triviṣṭapa or Tripiṣṭapa is the city of Indra supposed to be situated on Mount Meru.]

English

प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा गुरुः शुक्रो जलन्धरम् ॥ ५१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of the son of the ocean, the delighted Bhārgava, the preceptor, replied to Jalandhara. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्र उवाच ।
दिव्यौषधीः समानीय द्रोणाद्रेरङ्गिराः सुरान् ।
जीवयत्येष वै तात सत्यं जानीहि मे वचः ॥ ५२॥

English
*Bhārgava said:—*  “Aṅgiras is bringing divine herbs from the mountain Droṇa and enlivening the gods. O dear, know my words to be true. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
जयमिच्छसि चेत्तात श‍ृणु मे वचनं शुभम् ।
ततः सोऽरं भुजाभ्यां त्वं द्रोणमब्धावुपाहर ॥ ५३॥

English
O dear, if you wish for victory listen to my auspicious suggestion. Immediately you shall uproot the mountain Droṇa with your arms and hurl it into the ocean.” (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः स तु दैत्येन्द्रो गुरुणा भार्गवेण ह ।
द्रुतं जगाम यत्रासावास्ते चैवाद्रिराट् च सः ॥ ५४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus addressed by his preceptor Bhārgava, the lord of the Asuras, hastened to the lofty mountain. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
भुजाभ्यां तरसा दैत्यो नीत्वा द्रोणं च तं तदा ।
प्राक्षिपत्सागरे तूर्णं चित्रं न हरतेजसि ॥ ५५॥

English
With his powerful arms, the Asura brought the mountain Droṇa and hurled it immediately into the ocean. There is nothing wonderful and mysterious in regard to the splendour of Śiva. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनरायान्महावीरस्सिन्धुपुत्रो महाहवम् ।
जघानास्त्रैश्च विविधैः सुरान्कृत्वा बलं महत् ॥ ५६॥

English
The great hero, the son of the ocean, took a vast army with him, came to the battle ground and began to kill the gods with various weapons. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवान्हतान्दृष्ट्वा द्रोणाद्रिमगमद् गुरुः ।
तावत्तत्र गिरीद्रं तं न ददर्श सुरार्चितः ॥ ५७॥

English
On seeing the gods being killed Bṛhaspati went to the mountain Droṇa. Then he, the object of praise and worship by the gods, did not see the mountain there. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा दैत्यहृतं द्रोणं धिषणो भयविह्वलः ।
आगत्य देवान्प्रोवाच जीवो व्याकुलमानसः ॥ ५८॥

English
On realising that the mountain Droṇa had been removed by the Asuras, Bṛhaspati was terrified. He returned and said dejectedly. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुरुवाच ।
पलायध्वं सुराः सर्वे द्रोणो नास्ति गिरिर्महान् ।
ध्रुवं ध्वस्तश्च दैत्येन पाथोधितनयेन हि ॥ ५९॥

English
*Bṛhaspati said:—*  “O gods, run away, all of you. There is no trace of the great mountain Droṇa. Certainly it has been destroyed by the Asura, the son of the ocean. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरो महादैत्यो नायं जेतुं क्षमो यतः ।
रुद्रांशसम्भवो ह्येष सर्वामरविमर्दनः ॥ ६०॥

English
Jalandhara is a great Asura. He cannot be conquered since he is born of a part of Śiva. He will pound all the gods. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
मया ज्ञातः प्रभावोऽस्य यथोत्पन्नः स्वयं सुराः ।
शिवापमानकृच्छक्रचेष्टितं स्मरताखिलम् ॥ ६१॥

English
His power has been understood by me as he is self-born. O gods, all of you remember the act of offence to Śiva perpetrated by Indra. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श्रुत्वा तद्वचनं देवाःसुराचार्यप्रकीर्तितम् ।
जयाशां त्यक्तवन्तस्ते भयविह्वलितास्तथा ॥ ६२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words uttered by the preceptor of the gods, they abandoned all hopes of victory. They became excessively terrified. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यराजेन तेनातिहन्यमानाः समन्ततः ।
धैर्यं त्यक्त्वाऽपलायन्त दिशो दश सवासवाः ॥ ६३॥

English
All the gods including Indra, struck by the king of the Asuras all round, lost courage and fled in all directions. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
देवान्विद्रावितान्दृष्ट्वा दैत्यः सागरनन्दनः ।
शङ्खभेरीजयरवैः प्रविवेशामरावतीम् ॥ ६४॥

English
On seeing the gods routed, the Asura, Jalandhara, the son of the ocean, entered Amarāvatī$^{215}$ with sounds of victory from the conches and drums. (64.)

[fn 215. It is the mythical capital of Indra’s heaven, situated on the golden mount Meru.]
