### **CHAPTER SEVENTEEN - *(The fight between Viṣṇu and Jalandhara)***

---

Sanskrit
१७

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथ दैत्या महावीर्याः शूलैः परशुपट्टिशैः ।
निजघ्नुःसर्वदेवांश्च भयव्याकुलमानसान् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Then the heroic Asuras hit and struck the gods distressed and terrified, with the spears, axes and clubs. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यायुधैः समाविद्धदेहा देवाः सवासवाः ।
रणाद्विदुद्रुवुः सर्वे भयव्याकुलमानसाः ॥ २॥

English
With their bodies cut and pierced by the weapons of the Asuras, the gods including Indra became distressed in mind by fear and they fled from the battle. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पलायनपरान् दृष्ट्वा हृषीकेशः सुरानथ ।
विष्णुर्वै गरुडारूढो योद्धुमभ्याययौ द्रुतम् ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the gods fleeing, Viṣṇu hastened to the battle ground seated on his vehicle Garuḍa. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदर्शनेन चक्रेण सर्वतः प्रस्फुरन् रुचा ।
सुशोभितकराब्जश्च रेजे भक्ताभयङ्करः ॥ ४॥

English
By means of his discus Sudarśana he diffused his splendour all round. He shone with the brilliant lotus in his hand and offered fearlessness to his devotees. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खखड्गगदाशार्ङ्गधारी क्रोधसमन्वितः ।
कठोरास्त्रो महावीरः सर्वयुद्धविशारदः ॥ ५॥

English
Holding the conch, sword, mace and the bow, the heroic deity was very furious. He was efficient in the battle using fierce weapons. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
धनुषं शार्ङ्गनामानं विस्फूर्य्य विननाद ह ।
तस्य नादेन त्रैलोक्यं पूरितं महता मुने ॥ ६॥

English
He produced the twanging sound from his bow and roared aloud. O sage, all the three worlds were filled with its loud sound. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शार्ङ्गनिस्सृतबाणैश्च दितिजानां शिरांसि वै ।
चकर्त्त भगवान् विष्णुः कोटिशो रुट्समाकुलः ॥ ७॥

English
The lord Viṣṇu who was highly infuriated cut off the heads of countless Asuras by means of the arrows discharged from his bow. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथारुणानुजजवपक्षवातप्रपीडिताः ।
वात्याविवर्त्तिता दैत्या बभ्रमुः खे यथा घनाः ॥ ८॥

English
Then the Asuras afflicted by the gusts of wind set in motion by the wings of Garuḍa in his speedy flight were blown to and fro like the clouds in the sky tossed about in a stormy whirlwind. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जलन्धरो दृष्ट्वा दैत्यान्वात्याप्रपीडितान् ।
चुक्रोधाति महादैत्यो देववृन्दभयङ्करः ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing the Asuras afflicted by the gusts of wind Jalandhara the great Asura became furious and terrified all the gods. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
मर्द्दयन्तं च तं दृष्ट्वा दैत्यान् प्रस्फुरिताधरः ।
योद्धुमभ्याययौ वीरो वेगेन हरिणा सह ॥ १०॥

English
Seeing Viṣṇu suppressing and pounding the Asuras, the lips of the heroic Asura throbbed and he rushed at Viṣṇu to fight with him. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स चकार महानादं देवासुरभयङ्करम् ।
दैत्यानामधिपः कर्णा विदीर्णाः श्रवणात्ततः ॥ ११॥

English
The king of Asuras shouted and roared terrifying both the gods and the Asuras. On hearing it, the ears became pierced. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
भयंङ्करेण दैत्यस्य नादेनापूरितं तदा ।
जलन्धरस्य महता चकम्पे सकलं जगत् ॥ १२॥

English
The entire universe, filled with the terrible shouts of the Asura Jalandhara, quaked. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः समभवद्युद्धं विष्णुदैत्येन्द्रयोर्महत् ।
आकाशं कुर्वतोर्बाणैस्तदा निरवकाशवत् ॥ १३॥

English
Then a great battle ensued between Viṣṇu and Jalandhara, the ruler of Asuras, both filling up the sky with their arrows. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोश्च तेन युद्धेन परस्परमभून्मुने ।
देवासुरर्षिसिद्धानां भीकरेणातिविस्मयः ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, gods, Asuras, sages and the Siddhas were very much surprised at the terrible mutual clash between the two. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्दैत्यस्य बाणौघैर्ध्वजं छत्रं धनुः शरान् ।
चिच्छेद तं च हृदये बाणेनैकेन ताडयन् ॥ १५॥

English
Striking with a single arrow, Viṣṇu smote the heart of the Asura. With innumerable arrows he cut off the umbrella, banner, bow and arrows of the demon. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दैत्यः समुत्पत्य गदापाणिस्त्वरान्वितः ।
आहत्य गरुडं मूर्ध्नि पातयामास भूतले ॥ १६॥

English
Seizing the mace with his hand, the Asura jumped up quickly, hit Garuḍa on his head and felled him to the ground. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुं जघान शूलेन तीक्ष्णेन प्रस्फुरद्रुचा ।
हृदये क्रोधसंयुक्तो दैत्यः प्रस्फुरिताधरः ॥ १७॥

English
The infuriated Asura with throbbing lips hit Viṣṇu in his heart with his sharp spear diffusing its splendour. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्गदां च खड्गेन चिच्छेद प्रहसन्निव ।
तं विव्याध शरैस्तीक्ष्णैः शार्ङ्गं विस्फूर्य दैत्यहा ॥ १८॥

English
Viṣṇu laughingly split the mace with his sword. The destroyer of Asuras twanged his bow and split him with sharp arrows. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्जलन्धरं दैत्यं भयदेन शरेण ह ।
क्रोधाविष्टोऽतितीक्ष्णेन जघानाशु सुरारिहा ॥ १९॥

English
Viṣṇu the infuriated destroyer of the Asuras smote the Asura Jalandhara with a very sharp terrifying arrow. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतं तस्य तं बाणं दृष्ट्वा दैत्यो महाबलः ।
छित्त्वा बाणेन विष्णुं च जघान हृदये द्रुतम् ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing his arrow coming, the powerful Asura cut it off with another arrow and hit Viṣṇu in the chest. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
केशवोऽपि महाबाहुं विक्षिप्तमसुरेण तम् ।
शरं तिलप्रमाणेन च्छित्त्वा वीरो ननाद ह ॥ २१॥

English
The heroic Viṣṇu of long arms split the arrow discharged by the Asura to the size of gingelly seeds and roared. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्बाणं समाधत्त धनुषि क्रोधवेपितः ।
महाबलोऽथ बाणेन चिच्छेद स शिलीमुखम् ॥ २२॥

English
The infuriated great Asura fixed an arrow again to his bow and split the arrow of Viṣṇu. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वासुदेवः पुनर्बाणं नाशाय विबुधद्विषः ।
क्रोधेनाधत्त धनुषि सिंहवद्विननाद ह ॥ २३॥

English
Vāsudeva fixed another arrow to his bow for the destruction of the enemy of the gods angrily and roared like a lion. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरोऽथ दैत्येन्द्रः कोपाच्छिन्नाधरो बली ।
शरेण स्वेन शार्ङ्गाख्यं धनुश्चिच्छेद वैष्णवम् ॥ २४॥

English
Biting his lips with anger, Jalandhara the powerful king of Asuras split the bow of Viṣṇu with his arrow. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्बाणैः सुतीक्ष्णैश्च जघान मधुसूदनम् ।
उग्रवीर्यो महावीरो देवानां भयकारकः ॥ २५॥

English
The heroic Asura of fierce valour, terrible to the gods, hit Viṣṇu again with very sharp arrows. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
स च्छिन्नधन्वा भगवान् केशवो लोकरक्षकः ।
जलन्धरस्य नाशाय चिक्षेप स्वगदां पराम् ॥ २६॥

English
With his bow split, the lord Viṣṇu, protector of the worlds, hurled his great mace for the destruction of Jalandhara. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सा गदा हरिणा क्षिप्ता ज्वलज्ज्वलनसन्निभा ।
अमोघगतिका शीघ्रं तस्य देहे ललाग ह ॥ २७॥

English
That mace resembling a blazing flame when hurled by Viṣṇu moved with unerring aim and dashed against his body. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तया हतो महादैत्यो न चचालापि किञ्चन ।
जलन्धरो मदोन्मत्तः पुष्पमालाहतो यथा ॥ २८॥

English
Though hit by it, the great haughty Jalandhara did not move even slightly as though he was hit by a flower-garland. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जलन्धरः क्रोधी देवत्रासकरोऽक्षिपत् ।
त्रिशूलमनलाकारं हरये रणदुर्म्मदः ॥ २९॥

English
Then the infuriated Jalandhara, invincible in war, terrifying to the Asuras hurled a trident, resembling fire, at Viṣṇu. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुस्तत्त्रिशूलं चिच्छेद तरसा द्रुतम् ।
नन्दकाख्येन खड्गेन स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Immediately Viṣṇu remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and cut the trident with his sword Nandaka. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
छिन्ने त्रिशूले दैत्येन्द्र उत्प्लुत्य सहसा द्रुतम् ।
आगत्य हृदये विष्णुं जघान दृढमुष्टिना ॥ ३१॥

English
When the trident was split, the lord of the Asuras leapt and rushed against Viṣṇu and hit him in the chest with his fist. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि विष्णुर्महावीरोऽविगणय्य च तद्व्यथाम ।
जलन्धरं च हृदये जघान दृढमुष्टिना ॥ ३२॥

English
Without minding the pain in the least, the heroic Viṣṇu hit Jalandhara in the chest with his firm fist. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तौ बाहुयुद्धेन युयुधाते महाबलौ ।
बाहुभिर्मुष्टिभिश्चैव जानुभिर्नादयन्महीम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Then both of them equally powerful had a hand to hand fight hitting each other with arms, fists and knees. They filled the earth with reverberating sounds. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं हि सुचिरं युद्धं कृत्वा तेनासुरेण वै ।
विस्मितोऽभून्मुनिश्रेष्ठ हृदि ग्लानिमवाप ह ॥ ३४॥

English
Fighting with the Asura thus, for a long time, O excellent sage, Viṣṇu was surprised. He felt dejected in the heart. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रसन्नो भगवान्मायी मायाविदां वरः ।
उवाच दैत्यराजानं मेघगम्भीरया गिरा ॥ ३५॥

English
Then he the foremost among the magic-wielders assumed a delightful aspect. He addressed the king of Asuras in a thundering voice. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
भो भो दैत्यवरश्रेष्ठ धन्यस्त्वं रणदुर्मदः ।
महायुधवरैर्यत्त्वं न भीतो हि महाप्रभुः ॥ ३६॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* “O excellent Asura, you are blessed. You are invincible in war. Since you are a great lord you are not at all afraid of even great weapons. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एभिरेवायुधैरुग्रैर्दैत्या हि बहवो हताः ।
महाजौ दुर्मदा वीराश्छिन्नदेहा मृतिं गताः ॥ ३७॥

English
Many Asuras have been killed by these very same weapons in great battles. The wicked and haughty people have been pierced through their bodies and killed. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
युद्धेन ते महादैत्य प्रसन्नोऽस्मि महान्भवान् ।
न दृष्टस्त्वत्समो वीरस्त्रैलोक्ये सचराचरे ॥ ३८॥

English
O great Asura, I am delighted by this fight with you. You are really great. A hero like you has not been seen in the three worlds including the mobile and immobile beings. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं वरय दैत्येन्द्र प्रीतोऽस्मि तव विक्रमात् ।
अदेयमपि ते दद्मि यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ॥ ३९॥

English
O lord of Asuras, choose a boon. I am pleased at your valour. I shall give you anything even that which cannot be given, whatever is in your mind. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य विष्णोर्मायाविनो हरेः ।
प्रत्युवाच महाबुद्धिर्दैत्यराजो जलन्धरः ॥ ४०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Viṣṇu, skilled in magic, the intelligent king of the Asuras replied thus. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
यदि भावुक तुष्टोऽसि वरमेतं ददस्व मे ।
मद्भगिन्या मया सार्द्धं मद्गेहे सगणो वस ॥ ४१॥

English
O Brother-in-law, if you are pleased give me this boon. You stay in my house with all your followers, my sister and myself. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य महादैत्यस्य खिन्नधीः ।
तथास्त्विति च देवेशो जगाद भगवान् हरिः ॥ ४२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of the great Asura, lord Viṣṇu, the lord of gods, said distressingly—“So be it.” (42.)

---

Sanskrit
उवास स ततो विष्णुः सर्वदेवगणैस्सह ।
जलन्धरं नाम पुरमागत्य रमया सह ॥ ४३॥

English
Then Viṣṇu came to the city called Jalandhara$^{221}$ along with his followers, the gods and lakṣmī. (43.)

[fn 221. The town of the Daitya Jalandhara can be identified with that of the same name in the East Punjab. It was the capital of Jalandhara Deśa in the Uttarāpatha. For details see the Kumārikākhaṇḍa of Skandapurāṇa.]
---

Sanskrit
अथो जलन्धरो दैत्यः स्वभगिन्या च विष्णुना ।
उवास स्वालयं प्राप्तो हर्षाकुलितमानसः ॥ ४४॥

English
Then the Asura Jalandhara returned to his abode and stayed very delightedly in the company of his sister and Viṣṇu. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरोऽथ देवानामधिकारेषु दानवान् ।
स्थापयित्वा सहर्षः सन् पुनरागान्महीतलम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Thereafter Jalandhara appointed Asuras in the authoritative posts of the gods. Joyously he returned to the Earth. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
देवगन्धर्वसिद्धेषु यत्किञ्चिद्रत्नसञ्चितम् ।
तदात्मवशगं कृत्वाऽतिष्ठत्सागरनन्दनः ॥ ४६॥

English
The son of the ocean confiscated whatever gem or jewel the gods, Gandharvas or Siddhas had hoarded. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
पातालभवने दैत्यं निशुम्भं सुमहाबलम् ।
स्थापयित्वा स शेषादीनानयद्भूतलं बली ॥ ४७॥

English
After appointing the powerful Asura, Niśumbha, in the nether-worlds, the powerful ruler of the Asuras brought Śeṣa and others to the Earth. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
देवगन्धर्वसिद्धौघान् सर्पराक्षसमानुषान् ।
स्वपुरे नागरान्कृत्वा शशास भुवनत्रयम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Making gods, Gandharvas, Siddhas, Serpents, Rākṣasas and human beings, the denizens of his capital, he ruled over the three worlds. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं जलन्धरः कृत्वा देवान्स्ववशवर्तिनः ।
धर्मेण पालयामास प्रजाः पुत्रानिवौरसान् ॥ ४९॥

English
After making the gods thus subservient to himself, Jalandhara protected them all virtuously, like his own sons. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
न कश्चिद्व्याधितो नैव दुःखितो न कृशस्तथा ।
न दीनो दृश्यते तस्मिन्धर्माद्राज्यं प्रशासति ॥ ५०॥

English
When he was ruling the kingdom virtuously, none in his realm was sick or miserable or lean and emaciated or indigent. (50.)
