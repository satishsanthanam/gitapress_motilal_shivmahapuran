### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - *(The conversation between Nārada and Jalandhara)***

---

Sanskrit
१८

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवं शासति धर्मेण महीं तस्मिन्महासुरे ।
बभूवुर्दुःखिनो देवा भर्त्र्यभावान्मुनीश्वर ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* When the great Asura was ruling over the Earth virtuously, the gods were reduced to be mere slaves, O great sage. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःखितास्ते सुराः सर्वे शिवं शरणमाययुः ।
मनसा शङ्करं देवदेवं सर्वप्रभुं प्रभुम् ॥ २॥

English
The distressed gods mentally sought refuge in Śiva the benefactor, lord of gods and of everyone. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तुष्टुवुर्वाग्भिरिष्टाभिर्भगवन्तं महेश्वरम् ।
निवृत्तये स्वदुःखस्य सर्वदं भक्तवत्सलम् ॥ ३॥

English
They eulogised the great lord, the bestower of everything and favourably disposed to his devotees, by means of pleasant words. (3.)

---

English
The great lord, the bestower of all desires to his devotees called Nārada and commissioned him with a desire to carry out the task of the gods. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
आहूय स महादेवो भक्तानां सर्वकामदः ।
नारदं प्रेरयामास देवकार्यचिकीर्षया । ४॥

अथ देवमुनिर्ज्ञानी शम्भुभक्तः सतां गतिः ।
शिवाज्ञया ययौ दैत्यपुरे देवान्स नारदः ॥ ५॥

English
Then the celestial sage, the wise devotee of Śiva, the goal of the good, went to the gods in the city of the Asuras at the bidding of Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याकुलास्ते सुराः सर्वे वासवाद्या द्रुतं मुनिम् ।
आगच्छन्तं समालोक्य समुत्तस्थुर्हि नारदम् ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing the sage Nārada coming, the distressed gods, Indra and others, stood up. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ददुस्त आसनं नत्त्वा मुनये प्रीतिपूर्वकम् ।
नारदाय सुराः शक्रमुखा उत्कण्ठिताननाः ॥ ७॥

English
After bowing to the sage, Indra and other gods, their anxiety apparently manifest in their faces, offered a seat to Nārada. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखासीनं मुनिवरमासने सुप्रणम्य तम् ।
पुनः प्रोचुः सुरा दीना वासवाद्या मुनीश्वरम् ॥ ८॥

English
After bowing to Nārada the great sage who sat comfortably, the distressed gods, Indra and others spoke to him again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
भो भो मुनिवरश्रेष्ठ दुःखं श‍ृणु कृपाकर ।
श्रुत्वा तन्नाशय क्षिप्रं प्रभुस्त्वं शङ्करप्रियः ॥ ९॥

English
*The gods said:—* O excellent sage, listen to our misery. O merciful one, after listening to it, destroy it quickly. You are powerful and the favourite of Śiva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरेण दैत्येन सुरा विद्राविता भृशम् ।
स्वस्थानाद्भर्तृभावाच्च दुःखिता वयमाकुलाः ॥ १०॥

English
The gods have been routed by the Asura Jalandhara from their abodes and positions of controlling authority. Hence we are miserable and distressed. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्थानादुष्णरश्मिश्च चन्द्रो निस्सारितस्तथा ।
वह्निश्च धर्मराजश्च लोकपालास्तथेतरे ॥ ११॥

English
The hot-rayed sun and the moon have been ousted from their positions. The fire-god and the god of death and guardians of the quarters have been expelled. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सुबलिष्ठेन वै तेन सर्वे देवाः प्रपीडिताः ।
दुःखं प्राप्ता वयं चाति शरणं त्वां समागताः ॥ १२॥

English
The gods have been harassed by that powerful Asura. We who have been subjected to great grief now seek refuge in you. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्ग्रामे स हृषीकेशं स्ववशं कृतवान् बली ।
जलन्धरो महादैत्यः सर्वामरविमर्दकः ॥ १३॥

English
The great Asura Jalandhara who has suppressed the gods and who is very powerful has made Viṣṇu subservient to him in the battle. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य वश्यो वराधीनोऽवात्सीत्तत्सदने हरिः ।
स लक्ष्म्या सहितो विष्णुर्यो नः सर्वार्थसाधकः ॥ १४॥

English
Becoming subservient because of helplessness occasioned by the boon granted to him, Viṣṇu who carried out our tasks has now begun to stay in his palace along with Lakṣmī. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरविनाशाय यत्नं कुरु महामते ।
त्वं नो दैववशात्प्राप्तः सदा सर्वार्थसाधकः ॥ १५॥

English
O intelligent one, please exert yourself for the destruction of Jalandhara. You have fortunately come to us and you have always been the person who can achieve everything for us. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषाममराणां स नारदः ।
आश्वास्य मुनिशार्दूलस्तानुवाच कृपाकरः ॥ १६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of the gods, the great sage Nārada, the merciful, consoled them and said. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
जानेऽहं वै सुरा यूयं दैत्यराजपराजिताः ।
दुःख प्राप्ताः पीडिताश्च स्थानान्निस्सारिताः खलु ॥ १७॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O gods, I know that you have been defeated by the king of Asuras, that you are miserable and harassed and have been deposed. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वशक्त्या भवतां स्वार्थं करिष्ये नात्र संशयः ।
अनुकूलोऽहमिह वो दुःखं प्राप्ता यतोऽमराः ॥ १८॥

English
There is no doubt in this that I shall carry out your task according to my ability. O gods, since you are in misery I shall be favourable to you.” (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा मुनिश्रेष्ठो द्रष्टुं दानववल्लभम् ।
आश्वास्य सकलान्देवाञ्जलन्धरसभां ययौ ॥ १९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying so and consoling the gods, the excellent sage went to the assembly chamber of Jalandhara to see the favourite Asura. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अथागतं मुनिश्रेष्ठं दृष्ट्वा दैत्यो जलन्धरः ।
उत्थाय परया भक्त्या ददौ श्रेष्ठासनं वरम् ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing the excellent sage, the king Jalandhara stood up and offered him a splendid seat with great devotion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
स तं सम्पूज्य विधिवद्दानवेन्द्रोऽतिविस्मितः ।
सुप्रहस्य तदा वाक्यं जगाद मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
After worshipping him duly the surprised king of the Asuras laughed loudly and spoke to the excellent sage. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
कुत आगम्यते ब्रह्मन् किं च दृष्टं त्वया क्वचित् ।
यदर्थमिह आयातस्तदाज्ञापय मां मुने ॥ २२॥

English
*Jalandhara said:—*  O brahmin, whence do you come from? What did you see here? O sage, what is the aim of your present visit here? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य दैत्येन्द्रस्य महामुनिः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा नारदो हि जलन्धरम् ॥ २३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of king Jalandhara the delighted great sage Nārada replied to him. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
सर्वदानवदैत्येन्द्र जलन्धर महामते ।
धन्यस्त्वं सर्वलोकेश रत्नभोक्ता त्वमेव हि ॥ २४॥

English
*Nārada said:—*  O Jalandhara of great intellect, O lord of Dānavas and Daityas, O lord of all the worlds, you are blessed. You alone are the enjoyer of all jewels. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मदागमनहेतुं वै श‍ृणु दैत्येन्द्रसत्तम ।
यदर्थमिह चायातस्त्वहं वक्ष्येऽखिलं हि तत् ॥ २५॥

English
O excellent king of Daityas, listen to the purpose for which I have come here. I shall explain it to you. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
गतः कैलासशिखरं दैत्येन्द्राहं यदृच्छया ।
योजनायुतविस्तीर्णं कल्पद्रुममहावनम् ॥ २६॥

English
O lord of Daityas, I had been to the summit of Kailāsa casually. It is ten thousand Yojanas wide. It has a grove of Kalpa trees. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कामधेनुशताकीर्णं चिन्तामणिसुदीपितम् ।
सर्वरुक्ममयं दिव्यं सर्वत्राद्भुतशोभितम् ॥ २७॥

English
Hundreds of Kāmadhenus are found there. It is illuminated by Cintāmaṇi gems. It abounds in gold. It is divine and wonderfully brilliant. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रोमया सहासीनं दृष्टवानस्मि शङ्करम् ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरं गौरं त्रिनेत्रं चन्द्रशेखरम् ॥ २८॥

English
There I saw Śiva seated along with Pārvatī. He is fair-complexioned and exquisitely handsome. He has three eyes and the moon for his crest. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा महदाश्चर्यं वितर्को मेऽभवत्तदा ।
क्वापीदृशी भवेद् वृद्धिस्त्रैलोक्ये वा न वेति च ॥ २९॥

English
On seeing this wonderfully great thing, a doubt arose in my mind. Can there be anywhere in the three worlds such a splendour as this?" (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्तवापि दैत्येन्द्र समृद्धिः संस्मृता मया ।
तद्विलोकनकामोऽहं त्वत्सान्निध्यमिहागतः ॥ ३०॥

English
O lord of Daityas then the idea of your prosperity struck into my mind. Now I have come to you to see it personally. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति नारदतः श्रुत्वा स दैत्येन्द्रो जलन्धरः ।
स्वसमृद्धिं समग्रां वै दर्शयामास सादरम् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Nārada the lord of Daityas Jalandhara showed all his glory to Nārada. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा स नारदो ज्ञानी देवकार्यसुसाधकः ।
प्रभुप्रेरणया प्राह दैत्येन्द्रं तं जलन्धरम् ॥ ३२॥

English
On seeing it, the wise Nārada, eager to realise the interests of the gods, spoke to the king of Daityas, Jalandhara, induced by the lord. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद् उवाच ।
तवास्ति सुसमृद्धिर्हि वरवीराखिलाधुना ।
त्रैलोक्यस्य पतिस्त्वं हि चित्रं किं चात्र सम्भवम् ॥ ३३॥

English
*Nārada said:—* O foremost among heroes, you have everything conducive to prosperity. You are the lord of the three worlds. What wonder that you possess this wealth. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मणयो रत्नपुञ्जाश्च गजाद्याश्च समृद्धयः ।
ते गृहेऽद्य विभान्तीह यानि रत्नानि तान्यपि ॥ ३४॥

English
Big jewels, heaps of gems, elephants and other adjuncts to prosperity flourish in your mansion. Whatever valuable thing there is in the worlds finds a place here. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गजरत्नं त्वयानीतं शक्रस्यैरावतस्तथा ।
अश्वरत्नं महावीर सूर्यस्योच्चैःश्रवा हयः ॥ ३५॥

English
O great hero, the most excellent of all elephants, Airāvata of Indra has been brought by you. The most excellent of all horses, Uccaiḥśravas$^{222}$ of the sun has been brought by you. (35.)

[fn 222. Uccaiḥśravas, the horse of the sun, is distinct from that of the same name which was appropriated by Indra after it was produced at the churning of the ocean (Cp. V. 11 of the next chapter).]
---

Sanskrit
कल्पवृक्षस्त्वयानीतो निधयो धनदस्य च ।
हंसयुक्तविमानं च त्वयानीतं हि वेधसः ॥ ३६॥

English
The celestial Kalpa tree has been brought by you; the treasures of Kubera and the aerial chariot of Brahmā yoked to swan have been brought by you. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं वररत्नानि दिवि पृथ्व्यां रसातले ।
यानि दैत्येन्द्र ते भान्ति गृहे तानि समन्ततः ॥ ३७॥

English
Thus all excellent things available in heaven, earth and nether worlds, O great Daitya, flourish in your mansion in their entirety. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्समृद्धिमिमां पश्यन्सम्पूर्णां विविधामहम् ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि महावीर गजाश्वादिसुशोभिताम् ॥ ३८॥

English
O great hero, I am highly delighted on seeing your great affluence consisting of diverse objects—elephant horse etc. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
जायारत्नं महाश्रेष्ठं जलन्धर न ते गृहे ।
तदानेतुं विशेषेण स्त्रीरत्नं वै त्वमर्हसि ॥ ३९॥

English
But O Jalandhara, your mansion is deficient in the most excellent of all ladies. You deserve to bring that. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य गेहे सुरत्नानि सर्वाणि हि जलन्धर ।
जायारत्नं न चेत्तानि न शोभन्ते वृथा ध्रुवम् ॥ ४०॥

English
O Jalandhara, one who possesses all excellent things but does not possess the most excellent of women does not shine. His life is rendered waste. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा नारदस्य महात्मनः ।
उवाच दैत्यराजो हि मदनाकुलमानसः ॥ ४१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Nārada the noble soul, the king of Daityas, with his mind excited by passion, spoke as follows— (41.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
भो भो नारद देवर्षे नमस्तेऽस्तु महाप्रभो ।
जायारत्नवरं कुत्र वर्तते तद्वदाधुना ॥ ४२॥

English
Jalandhara said:—  “O celestial sage, O Nārada, obeisance be to you, O holy lord. Where is this most excellent of all ladies? Please tell me now. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माण्डे यत्र कुत्रापि तद्रत्नं यदि वर्त्तते ।
तदानेष्ये ततो ब्रह्मन्सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ४३॥

English
Wherever it may be in the whole of this universe, if such a lady exists anywhere, I will bring her here. Truth, it is certainly the truth.” (43.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
कैलासे ह्यतिरम्ये च सर्वर्द्धिसुसमाकुले ।
योगिरूपधरः शम्भुरस्ति तत्र दिगम्बरः ॥ ४४॥

English
*Nārada said:—* Kailāsa is very beautiful and it possesses all sorts of things conducive to prosperity. Śiva lives there assuming the form of a naked Yogin. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य भार्या सुरम्या हि सर्वलक्षणलक्षिता ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरीनाम्ना पार्वतीति मनोहरा ॥ ४५॥

English
His wife Pārvatī is exquisitely beautiful in every limb. She is charming and has all the characteristics of a beautiful lady. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तदीदृशं रूपमनन्यसङ्गतं
दृष्टं न कुत्रापि कुतूहलाढ्यम् ।
अत्यद्भुतं मोहनकृत्सुयोगिनां
सुदर्शनीयं परमर्द्धिकारि ॥ ४६॥

English
Such an exquisite beauteous form has never been seen anywhere. It incites the enthusiasm of everybody. It is highly wonderful. It fascinates even the Yogins. It is worthy of being seen. It is conducive to great prosperity. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वचित्ते कल्पयाम्यद्य शिवादन्यः समृद्धिमान् ।
जायारत्नान्विताद्वीर त्रिलोक्यां न जलन्धर ॥ ४७॥

English
This occurs to my mind, O valiant Jalandhara that there is none more prosperous in the three worlds than Śiva who possesses the most excellent of all ladies. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्या लावण्यजलधौ निमग्नश्चतुराननः ।
स्वधैर्य्यं मुमुचे पूर्वं तया कान्योपमीयते ॥ ४८॥

English
Even the four-faced²²³ lord Brahmā, immersed in her ocean of beauty, lost his mental steadiness formerly.²²⁴ Who can be compared to such a beautiful lady? (48.)

[fn 223. Brahmā is called four-faced (caturanana or caturmukha). Originally he had five heads but one was cut off by Śiva for telling lies. According to another version it was burnt off by the fire of Śiva’s central eye for speaking disrespectfully.]

[fn 224. Cp. ŚP. RS. Pārvatīkhaṇḍa 49. 8.]
---

Sanskrit
गतरागोऽपि हि यया मदनारिः स्वलीलया ।
निजतन्त्रोऽपि यतः स स्वात्मवशगः कृतः ॥ ४९॥

English
Even Śiva reputed to be free from infatuation has been won over by her womanly sports. Śiva who is independent has been subjugated by her. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा स्त्रीरत्नसम्भोक्तुः समृद्धिस्तस्य साभवत् ।
तथा न तव दैत्येन्द्र सर्वरत्नाधिपस्य च ॥ ५०॥

English
The prosperity that he enjoys inasmuch as he indulges in dalliance with the most excellent of all ladies has not come to you O lord of Daityas though you are the master of excellent gems and jewels. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स तु देवर्षिर्नारदो लोकविश्रुतः ।
ययौ विहायसा देवोपकारकरणोद्यतः ॥ ५१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this, the world-renowned celestial sage, Nārada, pursuing his attempt to help the gods departed from there by the aerial path. (51.)
