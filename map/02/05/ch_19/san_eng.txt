### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - *(Jalandhara's emissary to Śiva)***

---

Sanskrit
१९

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ नारदे हि गते दिवि ।
दैत्यराट् किमकार्षीत्स तन्मे वद सुविस्तरात् ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, what did the king of Daityas do after the departure of Nārada to heaven? Please narrate to me in detail. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तमामन्त्र्य गते दैत्यं नारदे दिवि दैत्यराट् ।
तद्रूपश्रवणादासीदनङ्गज्वरपीडितः ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* When Nārada departed to heaven after taking leave of the Daitya, the king of Daityas who had heard of the exquisite beauty of Pārvatī became harassed with pangs of love. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो जलन्धरो दैत्यः कालाधीनः प्रनष्टधीः ।
दूतमाह्वाययामास सैंहिकेयं विमोहितः ॥ ३॥

English
The deluded Daitya, Jalandhara, who had lost clear thinking, being swayed by Time (the annihilator) called his messenger Rāhu. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतं तं समालोक्य कामाक्रान्तमनाः स हि ।
सुसम्बोध्य समाचष्ट सिन्धुपुत्रो जलन्धरः ॥ ४॥

English
The infatuated son of the ocean, Jalandhara, addressed him politely with these words. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
भो भो दूतवरश्रेष्ठ सर्वकार्यप्रसाधक ।
सैंहिकेय महाप्राज्ञ कैलासं गच्छ पर्वतम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Jalandhara said:—*  O Rāhu of great intellect, most excellent of my emissaries, go to the mountain Kailāsa, O accomplisher of all activities. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रास्ति योगी शम्भ्वाख्यस्तपस्वी च जटाधरः ।
भस्मभूषितसर्वाङ्गो विरक्तो विजितेन्द्रियः ॥ ६॥

English
A sage and a Yogin named Śiva lives there. He has matted locks of hair. He is detached. He has controlled his senses. His body is smeared with ashes. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वेति वक्तव्यं योगिनं दूत शङ्करम् ।
जटाधरं विरक्तं तं निर्भयेन हृदा त्वया ॥ ७॥

English
O messenger, you shall go there and tell the detached Yogin Śiva with matted locks of hair, fearlessly. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हे योगिंस्ते दयासिन्धो जायारत्नेन किं भवेत् ।
भूतप्रेतपिशाचादिसेवितेन वनौकसा ॥ ८॥

English
'O Yogin, ocean of mercy, of what avail is an exquisitely beautiful wife to you who stay in the jungle attended by ghosts, goblins, spirits and other beings? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्नाथे भुवने योगिन्नोचिता गतिरीदृशी ।
जायारत्नमतस्त्वं मे देहि रत्नभुजे निजम् ॥ ९॥

English
O Yogin, this state of affairs is no good in a world with me as the Ruler: Hence you give up your wife, the most excellent lady, to me, the enjoyer of all excellent things. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यानि यानि सुरत्नानि त्रैलोक्ये तानि सन्ति मे ।
मदधीनं जगत्सर्वं विद्धि त्वं सचराचरम् ॥ १०॥

English
Know that the whole universe including the mobile and immobile beings is under my suzerainty. All the excellent things of the three worlds have come into my possession. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रस्य गजरत्नं चोच्चैःश्रवोरत्नमुत्तमम् ।
बलाद्गृहीतं सहसा पारिजातस्तरुस्तथा ॥ ११॥

English
I have forcibly seized the most excellent elephant of Indra, the most excellent horse, Uccaiḥśravas and the celestial tree pārijāta. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
विमानं हंससंयुक्तमङ्गणे मम तिष्ठति ।
रत्नभूतं महादिव्यमुत्तमं वेधसोद्भुतम् ॥ १२॥

English
The wonderfully excellent and the most divine aerial chariot fitted with the swan, belonging to Brahmā is now standing in my court-yard. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
महापद्मादिकं दिव्यं निधिरत्नं स्वदस्य च ।
छत्रं मे वारुणं गेहे काञ्चनस्रावि तिष्ठति ॥ १३॥

English
The divine and excellent treasure Mahāpadma etc. of Kubera is in my custody. The umbrella of Varuṇa stands in my house shedding its golden brilliance. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
किञ्जल्किनी महामाला सर्वदाऽम्लानपङ्कजा ।
मत्पितुः सा ममैवास्ति पाशश्च कपतेस्तथा ॥ १४॥

English
The great garland of never-fading lotuses of fine filaments belonging to my father is as good as mine. The noose of Varuṇa lord of waters is also mine. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मृत्योरुत्क्रान्तिदा शक्तिर्मया नीता बलाद्वरा ।
ददौ मह्यं शुचिर्दिव्ये शुचिशौचे च वाससी ॥ १५॥

English
The excellent Javelin of Mṛtyu has been seized by me with force. The god of fire has surrendered to me two clothes purified in fire. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं योगीन्द्र रत्नानि सर्वाणि विलसन्ति मे ।
अतस्त्वमपि मे देहि स्वस्त्रीरत्नं जटाधर ॥ १६॥

English
Thus, O great Yogin, all excellent things shine in my possession. Hence O ascetic (wearing matted hair) you too surrender your wife the most excellent of all ladies to me.²²⁵ (16.)

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य नन्दिना स प्रवेशितः ।
जगामोग्रसभां राहुर्विस्मयोद्भुतलोचनः ॥ १७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  On hearing his words Rāhu went to Kailāsa and was allowed to enter by Nandin. With surprise and mystery manifest in his eyes, he went to the assembly chamber of Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा शिवं साक्षाद्देवदेवं महाप्रभुम् ।
स्वतेजोध्वस्ततमसं भस्मलेपविराजितम् ॥ १८॥

महाराजोपचारेण विलसन्तं महाद्भुतम् ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरं दिव्यभूषणैर्भूषितं हरम् ॥ १९॥

प्रणनाम च तं गर्वात्तत्तेजः क्रान्तविग्रहः ।
निकटं गतवान् शम्भोः स दूतो राहुसंज्ञकः ॥ २०॥

English
On entering it, he saw Śiva, the lord of the gods, the great lord, quelling darkness with his refulgence, shining with ashes smeared (over his body), adorned with all Royal paraphernalia, of wonderful features, exquisite in every limb and embellished with divine ornaments. The emissary named Rāhu bowed to Śiva. His haughtiness subsided by the brilliance of his body. He went near Śiva. (18-20.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो तदग्र आसीनो वक्तुकामो हि सैंहिकः ।
त्र्यम्बकं स तदा संज्ञाप्रेरितो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ २१॥

English
Rāhu was desirous of speaking to him. He sat in front of Śiva. Urged by his gesture Rāhu spoke to the three-eyed god Śiva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
राहुरुवाच ।
दैत्यपन्नगसेव्यस्य त्रैलोक्याधिपतेः सदा ।
दूतोऽहं प्रेषितस्तेन त्वत्सकाशमिहागतः ॥ २२॥

English
*Rāhu said:—*  I am the messenger of the lord of the three worlds, worthy of being served for ever by Daityas and serpents. I have come here to you on being sent by him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरोऽब्धितनयः सर्वदैत्यजनेश्वरः ॥ ऽ
त्रैलोक्यस्येश्वरस्सोऽथाभवत्सर्वाधिनायकः ॥ २३॥

English
The son of the ocean Jalandhara became the lord of all Daityas and now he is the lord of the three worlds. He is the emperor of all. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
स दैत्यराजो बलवान्देवानामन्तकोपमः ।
योगिनं त्वां समुद्दिश्य स यदाह श‍ृणुष्व तत् ॥ २४॥

English
That powerful king of Daityas is like the god of death to the gods. Listen to what he says addressing you the Yogin. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
महादिव्यप्रभावस्य तस्य दैत्यपतेः प्रभोः ।
सर्वरत्नेश्वरस्य त्वमाज्ञां श‍ृणु वृषध्वज ॥ २५॥

English
O bull-bannered god, listen to the behest of the lord of Daityas who has divine power and who is the master of all excellent things. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
श्मशानवासिनो नित्यमस्थिमालाधरस्य च ।
दिगम्बरस्य ते भार्या कथं हैमवती शुभा ॥ २६॥

English
How can the auspicious daughter of Himavat be a wife unto you who habitually stay in the cremation ground wearing garlands of bones and assuming the form of a naked ascetic. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं रत्नाधिनाथोऽस्मि सा च स्त्रीरत्नसंज्ञिता ।
तस्मान्ममैव सा योग्या नैव भिक्षाशिनस्तव ॥ २७॥

English
I am the possessor of all excellent things. She is the most excellent of all ladies. She deserves me better than you who live on alms. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The three worlds are under my control. I partake of shares in sacrifices. The excellent things of the three worlds are found in my palace. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
We are the enjoyers of excellent things. You are a mere naked ascetic and a Yogin. Surrender your wife unto me. Subjects shall always keep their king happy. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
He was huge in size. He had long arms. His calves were as stout and huge as the palmyra tree. He was very terrible. He immediately rushed at Rāhu. (32.)

English
On seeing him rushing to devour, Rāhu was terrified. He ran out when he was caught by the terrible being. (33.)

Sanskrit
पूजनीयो विशेषेण स गणश्शिवपूजने ।
नार्चयन्तीह ये पूर्वं तेषामर्चा वृथा भवेत् ॥ ५१॥

English
This Gaṇa shall be specially worshipped in the course of the adoration of Śiva. Those who do not worship him at the outset will find their worship in vain. (51.)
