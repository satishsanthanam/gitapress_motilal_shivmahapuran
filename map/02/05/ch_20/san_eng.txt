### **CHAPTER TWENTY - (The fight between the rank and file of the Gaṇas and the Asuras)**

---

Sanskrit
२०

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ कथा ते श्राविताद्भुता ।
महाप्रभोः शङ्करस्य यत्र लीला च पावनी ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, a wonderful story has been narrated by you, wherein the sanctifying sports of Śiva the great lord are included. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं ब्रूहि सुप्रीत्या कृपां कृत्वा ममोपरि ।
राहुर्मुक्तः कुत्र गतः पुरुषेण महामुने ॥ २॥

English
Now take pity on me and tell me with pleasure. O great sage, when released by that being where did Rāhu go ? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य व्यासस्यामितमेधसः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा ब्रह्मपुत्रो महामुनिः ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the words of Vyāsa of immeasurable intelligence, the great sage, the delighted son of Brahmā, replied. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
राहुर्विमुक्तो यस्तेन सोऽपि तद्वर्वरस्थले ।
अतः स वर्वरो भूत इति भूमौ प्रथां गतः ॥ ४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Rāhu had been let off in the land of the outcastes.227 He too became an outcaste and came to be known in the world as such. (4.)

[fn 227. The country of Varbaras or Barbaras is identified with the Ābīr (Ābhīra) Deśa in the south-west in the Indus delta. Barbaras were the wild people associated with Yavanas and Khasas. There is a reference to the town Barbari or Barbarikā in Skanda Purāṇa (1.11.37.1) and Barbaricum or Barbaricon by Periplus and Ptolemy. For details see Awasthi: Studies in Skanda Purāṇa PP 100-101]
---

Sanskrit
ततः स मन्यमानः स्वं पुनर्जनिमथानतः ।
गतगर्वो जगामाथ जलन्धरपुरं शनैः ॥ ५॥

English
Considering that as his second birth he became humble. He became free from haughtiness. He slowly wended his way to the city of Jalandhara. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धराय सोऽभ्येत्य सर्वमीशविचेष्टितम् ।
कथयामास तद्व्यासाद्व्यास दैत्येश्वराय वै ॥ ६॥

English
After approaching Jalandhara the lord of Daityas, he explained everything concerning Śiva in detail, O Vyāsa. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरस्तु तच्छ्रुत्वा कोपाकुलितविग्रहः ।
बभूव बलवान्सिन्धुपुत्रो दैत्येन्द्रसत्तमः ॥ ७॥

English
On hearing it, the powerful son of the ocean, the excellent lord of Daityas, Jalandhara became furious from head to foot. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कोपपराधीनमानसो दैत्यसत्तमः ।
उद्योगं सर्वसैन्यानां दैत्यानामादिदेश ह ॥ ८॥

English
Then the infuriated excellent Daitya commanded the entire army of the Daityas to enter into the fray. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
निर्गच्छन्त्वखिला दैत्याः कालनेमिमुखाः खलु ।
तथा शुम्भनिशुम्भाद्या वीराः स्वबलसंयुताः ॥ ९॥

कोटिर्वीरकुलोत्पन्नाः कम्बुवंश्याश्च दौर्हृदाः ।
कालकाः कालकेयाश्च मौर्या धौम्रास्तथैव च ॥ १०॥

English
Jalandhara said:—  Let all the Asuras such as Kālanemi and others set out with their entire divisions; Sumbha, Niṣumbha and other heroes; the descendants of Koṭivīra, the scions of the family of Kambu. Daurhṛdas, Kālakas, Kālakeyas, Mauryas and Dhaumras—let all these start for the fight. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याज्ञाप्यासुरपतिः सिन्धुपुत्रः प्रतापवान् ।
निर्जगामाशु दैत्यानां कोटिभिः परिवारितः ॥ ११॥

English
After ordering thus, the lord of the Asuras the valorous son of the ocean set out quickly accompanied by crores of Daityas. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तस्याग्रतः शुक्रो राहुश्छिन्नशिरोऽभवत् ।
मुकुटश्चापतद्भूमौ वेगात्प्रस्खलितस्तदा ॥ १२॥

English
Then Śukra and Rāhu with his head severed went ahead of him. In his quick jerky movement, his crown became dislodged and fell on the ground. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यराजत नभः पूर्णं प्रावृषीव यथा घनैः ।
जाता अशकुना भूरि महानिद्राविसूचकाः ॥ १३॥

English
The sky was entirely enveloped by clouds as in the rainy season. Many ill omens occurred portending great slumber. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्योद्योगं तथा दृष्ट्वा गीर्वाणास्ते सवासवाः ।
अलक्षितास्तदा जग्मुः कैलासं शङ्करालयम् ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing his enterprise, the gods including Indra went to Kailāsa, the abode of Śiva without being observed. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा शिवं दृष्ट्वा सुप्रणम्य सवासवाः ।
देवाः सर्वे नतस्कन्धाः करौ बद्ध्वा च तुष्टुवुः ॥ १५॥

English
After going there and seeing Śiva, the gods including Indra, bowed to him with stooping shoulders. They joined their palms in reverence and eulogised. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणाकर शङ्कर ।
नमस्तेऽस्तु महेशा न पाहि नः शरणागतान् ॥ १६॥

English
*The gods said:—* O great lord, lord of the gods, O Śiva the merciful, obeisance be to you. Save us who have sought refuge in you. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विह्वलाः वयमत्युग्रं जलन्धरकृतात्प्रभो ।
उपद्रवात्सदेवेन्द्राः स्थानभ्रष्टाः क्षितिस्थिताः ॥ १७॥

English
O lord, we are very much distressed by this harassment. All including Indra are deposed and compelled to stay on the earth. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
न जानासि कथं स्वामिन्देवापत्तिमिमां प्रभो ।
तस्मान्नो रक्षणार्थाय जहि सागरनन्दनम् ॥ १८॥

English
O lord, how is it possible that you do not know this adversity of the gods? Hence in order to protect us please kill him. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्माकं रक्षणार्थाय यत्पूर्वं गरुडध्वजः ।
नियोजितस्त्वया नाथ न क्षमः सोऽद्य रक्षितुम् ॥ १९॥

English
O lord, Viṣṇu who was assigned by you the task of protection is now unable to protect us. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तदधीनो गृहे तस्य रमया सह तिष्ठति ।
वयं च तत्र तिष्ठामस्तदाज्ञावशगाः सुराः ॥ २०॥

English
He is also subservient to him and stays in his mansion along with Lakṣmī. All of us gods stay there obeying his behests. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अलक्षिता वयं चात्रागताः शम्भो त्वदन्तिकम् ।
स आयाति त्वया कर्त्तुं रणं सिन्धुसुतो बली ॥ २१॥

English
O Śiva, we have approached you unobserved by him. That powerful son of the ocean is coming hither to fight with you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः स्वामिन् रणे त्वं तमविलम्बं जलन्धरम् ।
हन्तुमर्हसि सर्वज्ञ पाहि नः शरणागतान् ॥ २२॥

English
O omniscient lord, you shall kill Jalandhara in the battle without delay. Save us who have sought refuge in you. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ते सुराः सर्वे प्रभुं नत्वा सवासवाः ।
पादौ निरीक्ष्य सन्तस्थुर्महेशस्य विनम्रकाः ॥ २३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this, the gods including Indra bowed to him and stood humbly glancing at the feet of lord Śiva. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
इति देववचः श्रुत्वा प्रहस्य वृषभध्वजः ।
द्रुतं विष्णुं समाहूय वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ २४॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods the bull-bannered deity laughed. He called Viṣṇu immediately and spoke these words. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।

जलन्धरकृतापीडाः शरणं मेऽतिविह्वलाः ॥ २५॥

English
*The Lord Śiva said:—* O great Viṣṇu, the distressed gods harassed by Jalandhara have sought refuge in me. (25.)

---

Sanskrit

तद् गृहं चापि यातोऽसि त्यक्त्वा वैकुण्ठमात्मनः ॥ २६॥

English
O Viṣṇu, how is it that Jalandhara was not killed in battle by you? Leaving off your own Vaikuṇṭha you have gone to his mansion? (26.)

---

Sanskrit

निग्रहाय खलानां च स्वतन्त्रेण विहारिणा ॥ २७॥

English
As I wanted to be free and sportful, I had appointed you for the protection of the good and the curbing of the wicked. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of lord Śiva, Viṣṇu replied humbly bowing down with palms joined in reverence. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

English
"O great Viṣṇu please stay in my mansion subservient to me along with my sister,²²⁸ the gods and myself." So I went to his mansion. (33.)

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

---

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

भयङ्करोऽतिरौद्रश्च बभूव प्रज्वलन्दिशः ॥ ५१॥

English
On hearing it, lord Śiva became terribly furious. He became terrific blazing the quarters as it were. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ रुद्रमुखात्कृत्या बभूवातीव भीषणा ।
तालजङ्घा दरीवक्त्रा स्तनापीडितभूरुहा ॥ ५२॥

English
A terrible Kṛtyā came out of Rudra's mouth. Her calves were as stout as Palmyra trees. Her mouth was huge and deep like mountain caverns. With her breasts she crushed huge trees. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
सा युद्धभूमिं तरसा ससाद मुनिसत्तम ।
विचचार महाभीमा भक्षयन्ती महासुरान् ॥ ५३॥

English
O excellent sage, she rushed immediately to the battle ground. The terrible Kṛtyā roamed the battle-ground devouring the great Asuras. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा रणमध्ये हि जगाम गतभीर्द्रुतम् ।
यत्रास्ते संवृतो दैत्यवरेन्द्रैस्स हि भार्गवः ॥ ५४॥

English
Fearlessly she rushed amid the battle-field where Bhārgava was stationed surrounded by the leading Daityas. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वतेजसा नभो व्याप्य भूमिं कृत्वा च सा मुने ।
भार्गवं स्वभगे धृत्वा जगामान्तर्हिता नभः ॥ ५५॥

English
O sage, she enveloped the whole sky with her terrible brilliance. She split the ground she trod; she stuffed Bhārgava into her vaginal passage and vanished in the sky. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
विधृतं भार्गवं दृष्ट्वा दैत्यसैन्यगणास्तथा ।
प्रम्लानवदना युद्धान्निर्जग्मुर्युद्धदुर्मदाः ॥ ५६॥

English
On seeing Bhārgava seized, the invincible armies of the Daityas became dejected and faded in their faces. They fled from the battle ground. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोऽभज्यत दैत्यानां सेना गणभयार्दिता ।
वायुवेगहता यद्वत्प्रकीर्णा तृणसंहतिः ॥ ५७॥

English
The army of the Daityas became scattered and split in their terrific fear of the Gaṇas like bundles of grass split and scattered when blown by the wind. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
भग्नां गणभयाद्दैत्यसेनां दृष्ट्वातिमर्षिताः ।
निशुम्भशुम्भौ सेनान्यौ कालनेमिश्च चुक्रुधुः ॥ ५८॥

English
On seeing the army of the Daityas thus dispersed and frightened of the Gaṇas, the leaders Śumbha and Niśumbha and Kālanemi became infuriated. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रयस्ते वारयामासुर्गणसेनां महाबलाः ।
मुञ्चन्तः शरवर्षाणि प्रावृषीव बलाहकाः ॥ ५९॥

English
-All the three powerful Daityas obstructed the army of the Gaṇas showering arrows like the destructive clouds in the rainy season. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दैत्यशरौघास्ते शलभानामिव व्रजाः ।
रुरुधुः खं दिशः सर्वा गणसेनामकम्पयन् ॥ ६०॥

English
The volleys of arrows discharged by the Daityas enveloped all the quarters and the atmosphere like huge swarms of locusts. They shook the hosts of Gaṇas. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
गणाः शरशतैर्भिन्ना रुधिरासारवर्षिणः ।
वसन्तकिंशुकाभासा न प्राजानन्हि किञ्चन ॥ ६१॥

English
Split by hundreds of arrows, the Gaṇas shed streams of blood. They resembled the red Kiṃśuka flowers of the spring season. They did not know what to do. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रभग्नं स्वबलं विलोक्य
नन्द्यादिलम्बोदरकार्त्तिकेयाः ।
त्वरान्विता दैत्यवरान्प्रसह्य
निवारयामासुरमर्षणास्ते ॥ ६२॥

English
On seeing their army thus shattered, the infuriated leaders Nandin, Gaṇeśa and Kārttikeya hurriedly checked the rushing Daityas. (62.)
