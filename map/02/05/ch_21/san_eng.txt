### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - *(Description of the Special War)***

---

Sanskrit
२१

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
ते गणाधिपतीन्दृष्ट्वा नन्दीभमुखषण्मुखान् ।
अमर्षादभ्यधावन्त द्वन्द्वयुद्धाय दानवाः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On seeing the leaders of the Gaṇas, Nandin, Gaṇeśa and Kārttikeya, the Dānavas rushed at them for a duel combat. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिनं कालनेमिश्च शुम्भो लम्बोदरं तथा ।
निशुम्भः षण्मुखं देवमभ्यधावत शङ्कितः ॥ २॥

English
Kālanemi clashed with Nandin; Śumbha fought Gaṇeśa and Niśumbha hesitatingly rushed at Kārttikeya. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
निशुम्भः कार्तिकेयस्य मयूरं पञ्चभिः शरैः ।
हृदि विव्याध वेगेन मूर्छितः स पपात ह ॥ ३॥

English
With five arrows Niśumbha hit the peacock of Kārttikeya in the chest and it fell unconscious. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शक्तिधरः क्रुद्धो बाणैः पञ्चभिरेव च ।
विव्याध स्यन्दने तस्य हयान्यन्तारमेव च ॥ ४॥

English
Then the infuriated Kārttikeya discharged five arrows at his chariot and pierced the horses and the charioteer. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शरेणान्येन तीक्ष्णेन निशुम्भं देववैरिणम् ।
जघान तरसा वीरो जगर्ज रणदुर्मदः ॥ ५॥

English
The invincible hero hit Niśumbha with another sharp arrow quickly and roared. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
असुरोऽपि निशुम्भाख्यो महावीरोऽतिवीर्यवान् ।
जघान कार्तिकेयं तं गर्जन्तं स्वेषुणा रणे ॥ ६॥

English
The Asura Niśumbha of great prowess and heroism hit Kārttikeya in the battle with his arrow as he roared. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शक्तिं कार्तिकेयो यावज्जग्राह रोषतः ।
तावन्निशुम्भो वेगेन स्वशक्त्या तमपातयत् ॥ ७॥

English
By the time the furious Kārttikeya seized his spear, Niśumbha struck him with it. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं बभूव तत्रैव कार्तिकेयनिशुम्भयोः ।
आहवो हि महान्व्यास वीरशब्दं प्रगर्जतोः ॥ ८॥

English
Thus, O Vyāsa, a great fight between Kārttikeya and Niśumbha ensued as they shouted heroically. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो नन्दीश्वरो बाणैः कालनेमिमविध्यत ।
सप्तभिश्च हयान्केतुं रथं सारथिमाच्छिनत् ॥ ९॥

English
Then Nandin hit Kālanemi with seven arrows and pierced his horses, banner, chariot and charioteer. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कालनेमिश्च सङ्क्रुद्धो धनुश्चिच्छेद नन्दिनः ।
स्वशरासननिर्मुक्तैर्महातीक्ष्णैः शिलीमुखैः ॥ १०॥

English
With very sharp shafts discharged from his bow, the infuriated Kālanemi cut the bow of Nandin. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ नन्दीश्वरो वीरः कालनेमिं महासुरम् ।
तमपास्य च शूलेन वक्षस्यभ्यहनद् दृढम् ॥ ११॥

English
Defying the great demon Kālanemi the heroic Nandīśvara hit him in the chest with his spear. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स शूलभिन्नहृदयो हताश्वो हतसारथिः ।
अद्रेः शिखरमुत्पाट्य नन्दिनं समताडयत् ॥ १२॥

English
With his horses and charioteer killed and himself wounded in the chest, he broke the top of a mountain and hit Nandin. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शुम्भो गणेशश्च रथमूषक वाहनौ ।
युध्यमानौ शरव्रातैः परस्परमविध्यताम् ॥ १३॥

English
Then Śumbha and Gaṇeśa seated respectively in a chariot and on a mouse fought each other with volleys of arrows. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेशस्तु तदा शुम्भं हृदि विव्याध पत्रिणा ।
सारथिं च त्रिभिर्बाणैः पातयामास भूतले ॥ १४॥

English
Gaṇeśa hit Śumbha in his chest with an arrow and felled his charioteer with three arrows on the ground. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽतिक्रुद्धः शुम्भोऽपि बाणवृष्ट्या गणाधिपम् ।
मूषकं च त्रिभिर्विध्वा ननाद जलदस्वनः ॥ १५॥

English
Then the infuriated Śumbha covered Gaṇeśa with a shower of arrows. Hitting the mouse with three arrows he roared like thunder. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मूषकः शरभिन्नाङ्गश्चचाल दृढवेदनः ।
लम्बोदरश्च पतितः पदातिरभवत्स हि ॥ १६॥

English
The mouse pierced by the arrows, shook with acute pain. Gaṇeśa was thrown off (his vehicle) and he became a foot soldier (as it were). (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो लम्बोदरः शुम्भं हत्वा परशुना हृदि ।
अपातयत्तदा भूमौ मूषकं चारुरोह सः ॥ १७॥

English
Then Gaṇeśa hit Śumbha in his chest with his axe and felled him to the ground. Thereafter he mounted his mouse again. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
समरायोद्यतश्चाभूत्पुनर्गजमुखो विभुः ।
प्रहस्य जघ्नतुः क्रोधात्तोत्रेणैव महाद्विपम् ॥ १८॥

English
Lord Gaṇeśa of elephantine face got ready for the fight. He hit him mockingly and angrily as if hitting a great elephant with a goad. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
कालनेमिर्निशुम्भश्च ह्युभौ लम्बोदरं शरैः ।
युगपच्चख्नतुः क्रोधादाशीविषसमैर्द्रुतम् ॥ १९॥

English
Kālanemi and Śumbha simultaneously attacked Gaṇeśa furiously with arrows as ruthless as serpents. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तं पीड्यमानमालोक्य वीरभद्रो महाबलः ।
अभ्यधावत वेगेन कोटिभूतयुतस्तथा ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing him afflicted, the powerful Vīrabhadra accompanied by a crore goblins rushed in. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कूष्माण्डा भैरवाश्चापि वेताला योगिनीगणाः ।
पिशाचा डाकिनी सङ्घा गणाश्चापि समं ययुः ॥ २१॥

English
The Kūṣmāṇḍas, Bhairavas, Vetālas, Yoginīs, Piśācas, Dākinīs and Gaṇas came there with him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः किलकिला शब्दैः सिंहनादैः सघर्घरैः ।
विनादिता डमरुकैः पृथिवी समकम्पत ॥ २२॥

English
The Earth, resonant with various kinds of noise, shouts of joy, leonine roars and the sounds of Damarukas, quaked. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो भूताः प्रधावन्तो भक्षयन्ति स्म दानवान् ।
उत्पत्य पातयन्ति स्म ननृतुश्च रणाङ्गणे ॥ २३॥

English
Then the Bhūtas ran here and there devouring the Dānavas. They jumped up and danced in the battle field and threw the Asura on the ground. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे व्यासाभूतां नन्दी गुहश्च तौ ।
उत्थितावाप्तसंज्ञौ हि जगर्जतुरलं रणे ॥ २४॥

English
In the meantime, O Vyāsa, Nandin and Guha regained their consciousness and got up. They roared in the battlefield again. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स नन्दी कार्तिकेयश्च समायातौ त्वरान्वितौ ।
जघ्नतुश्च रणे दैत्यान्निरन्तरशरव्रजैः ॥ २५॥

English
Nāndin and Kārttikeya came hurriedly and struck the Daityas in the battle ground with incessant volleys of arrows. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
छिन्नैर्भिन्नैर्हतैर्दैत्यैः पातितैर्भक्षितैस्तथा ।
व्याकुला साभवत्सेना विषण्णवदना तदा ॥ २६॥

English
Then the army of the Daityas became agitated and dejected with many Daityas wounded, split, killed, felled to the ground and devoured. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं नन्दी कार्तिकेयो विकटश्च प्रतापवान् ।
वीरभद्रो गणाश्चान्ये जगर्जुःसमरेऽधिकम् ॥ २७॥

English
Thus Nandin, Kārttikeya the formidable and valorous, Virabhadra and the other Gaṇas roared much in the battle. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
निशुम्भशुम्भौ सेनान्यौ सिन्धुपुत्रस्य तौ तथा ।
कालनेमिर्महादैत्योऽसुराश्चान्ये पराजिताः ॥ २८॥

English
Then those two generals of the son of the ocean, Nīsumbha and Śumbha, the great Daitya Kālanemi and the other Asuras were defeated. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रविध्वस्तां ततस्सेनां दृष्ट्वा सागरनन्दनः ।
रथेनातिपताकेन गणानभिययौ बली ॥ २९॥

English
On seeing the army destroyed, the powerful son of the ocean rushed at the Gaṇas in his chariot of waving and wafting colours. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पराजिता दैत्या अप्यभूवन्महोत्सवाः ।
जगर्जुरधिकं व्यास समरायोद्यतास्तदा ॥ ३०॥

English
Thereat even the defeated Daityas became jubilant. O Vyāsa, they roared much and got ready for the fray. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे रुद्रगणाश्चापि जगर्जुर्जयशालिनः ।
नन्दिकार्तिकदन्त्यास्यवीरभद्रादिका मुने ॥ ३१॥

English
The victorious Gaṇas of Śiva too roared, led by Nandin, Kārttikeya, Gaṇeśa and Virabhadra, O sage. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हस्त्यश्वरथसंह्रादः शङ्खभेरीरवस्तथा ।
अभवत्सिंहनादश्च सेनयोरुभयोस्तथा ॥ ३२॥

English
The trumpets of the elephants, the neighing of the horses, the rumbling of the chariots, the sounds of the conches and war-drums and the leonine roars of the armies rose up. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरशरव्रातैर्नीहारपटलैरिव ।
द्यावापृथिव्योराच्छन्नमन्तरं समपद्यत ॥ ३३॥

English
The space between heaven and the earth became enveloped by the many arrows discharged by Jalandhara as if by floating masses of mist. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलादिं पञ्चभिर्विध्वा गणेशं पञ्चभिश्शरैः ।
वीरभद्रं च विंशत्या ननाद जलदस्वनः ॥ ३४॥

English
Hitting Nandin and Gaṇeśa with five arrows each and Virabhadra with twenty he roared like thunder. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्तिकेयस्ततो दैत्यं शक्त्या विव्याध सत्वरम् ।
जलन्धरं महावीरो रुद्रपुत्रो ननाद च ॥ ३५॥

English
Kārttikeya the heroic son of Śiva then swiftly hit the Daitya Jalandhara with his spear and roared. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स घूर्णनयनो दैत्यः शक्तिनिर्भिन्नदेहकः ।
पपात भूमौ त्वरितमुदतिष्ठन्महाबलः ॥ ३६॥

English
With the body pierced through by the spear, the Daitya fell on the ground with eyes rolling. But the powerful Asura swiftly stood up. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्रोधपरीतात्मा कार्तिकेयं जलन्धरः ।
गदया ताडयामास हृदये दैत्यपुङ्गवः ॥ ३७॥

English
Then Jalandhara the infuriated leader of the Daityas hit Kārttikeya in his chest with his mace. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
गदाप्रभावं सफलं दर्शयन् शङ्करात्मजः ।
विधिदत्तवराद् व्यास स तूर्णं भूतलेऽपतत् ॥ ३८॥

English
O Vyāsa, plainly exhibiting the successful efficiency of the Mace secured as a favour from Brahmā Kārttikeya fell on the ground suddenly. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव नन्दी ह्यपतद्भूतले गदया हतः ।
महावीरोऽपि रिपुहा किञ्चिद्व्याकुलमानसः ॥ ३९॥

English
Similarly, struck by the mace Nandin too fell on the ground, He was distressed a little although he was a great hero and a destroyer of enemies. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो गणेश्वरः क्रुद्धः स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ।
सम्प्राप्यातिबलो दैत्यगदां परशुनाच्छिनत् ॥ ४०॥

English
Then the infuriated hero Gaṇeśa came there after remembering the lotus like feet of Śiva and split the mace of the Daitya with his axe. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रस्त्रिभिर्बाणैर्हृदि विव्याध दानवम् ।
सप्तभिश्च हयान्केतुं धनुश्छत्रं च चिच्छिदे ॥ ४१॥

English
Virabhadra then hit the Dānava in his chest with three arrows. He cut off the banner, umbrella, bow and the horses of the Daitya with seven arrows. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽतिक्रुद्धो दैत्येन्द्रः शक्तिमुद्यम्य दारुणाम् ।
गणेशं पातयामास रथमन्यं समारुहत् ॥ ४२॥

English
Then the infuriated leader of the Daityas lifted up his terrible Śakti and felled Gaṇeśa. He mounted another chariot then. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्यगादथ वेगेन स दैत्येन्द्रो महाबलः ।
विगणय्य हृदा तं वै वीरभद्रं रुषान्वितः ॥ ४३॥

English
The powerful leader of the Daityas did not mind Virabhadra at all. Angrily he rushed at him. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रं जघानाशु तीक्ष्णेनाशीविषेण तम् ।
ननाद च महावीरो दैत्यराजो जलन्धरः ॥ ४४॥

English
Jalandhara, the heroic king of Daityas, hit Virabhadra with a fierce arrow and roared. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रोऽपि सङ्क्रुद्धस्सितधारेण चेषुणा ।
चिच्छेद तच्छरं चैव तं विव्याध महेषुणा ॥ ४५॥

English
The infuriated Virabhadra split that arrow with a sharp-edged arrow. With another great arrow he hit him too. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तौ सूर्यसङ्काशौ युयुधाते परस्परम् ।
नानाशस्त्रैस्तथास्त्रैश्च चिरं वीरवरोत्तमौ ॥ ४६॥

English
Then both of them, the most excellent of heroes refulgent like the sun, fought each other with different kinds of weapons and missiles. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रस्ततस्तस्य हयान्बाणैरपातयत् ।
धनुश्चिच्छेद रथिनः पताकां चापि वेगतः ॥ ४७॥

English
Virabhadra then felled his horses with his arrows. He forcefully cut off him bow and flags too. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो स दैत्यराजो हि पुप्लुवे परिघायुधः ।
वीरभद्रोपकण्ठं स द्रुतमाप महाबलः ॥ ४८॥

English
Then the king of the Daityas leapt up to him with a great iron club. That powerful warrior reached very near Virabhadra very quickly. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
परिघेनातिमहता वीरभद्रं जघान ह ।
महाबलोऽब्धितनयो मूर्ध्नि वीरो जगर्ज च ॥ ४९॥

English
The heroic and powerful son of the ocean hit Virabhadra on his head with his great iron club. He then roared. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
परिघेनातिमहता भिन्नमूर्द्धा गणाधिपः ।
वीरभद्रः पपातोर्व्यां मुमोच रुधिरं बहु ॥ ५०॥

English
Virabhadra, the leader of the Gaṇas, fell on the ground with his head shattered by the iron club and shed much blood. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
पतितं वीरभद्रं तु दृष्ट्वा रुद्रगणा भयात् ।
अपागच्छन् रणं हित्वा क्रोशमाना महेश्वरम् ॥ ५१॥

English
On seeing Virabhadra fallen, the terrified Gaṇas abandoned the battle ground shrieking and fled to lord Śiva. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ कोलाहलं श्रुत्वा गणानां चन्द्रशेखरः ।
निजपार्श्वस्थितान् वीरानपृच्छद् गणसत्तमान् ॥ ५२॥

English
On hearing the tumultuous uproar of the Gaṇas, the moon-crested lord asked the excellent Gaṇas, the heroes standing near him. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
किमर्थं मद्गणानां हि महाकोलाहलोऽभवत् ।
विचार्यतां महावीराः शान्तिः कार्या मया ध्रुवम् ॥ ५३॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  How is this tumultuous uproar among my Gaṇas? O heroes, let this be enquired into. Peace shall be established by me, of course. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
इति यावत्स देवेशो गणान्पप्रच्छ सादरम् ।
तावद्गणवरास्ते हि समायाताः प्रभुं प्रति ॥ ५४॥

English
Even as the lord of the gods was conducting the enquiry, the leaders of the Gaṇas approached the lord. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
तान्दृष्ट्वा विकलान् रुद्रः पप्रच्छ कुशलं प्रभुः ।
यथावत्ते गणा वृत्तं समाचख्युश्च विस्तरात् ॥ ५५॥

English
On seeing them dejected, the lord enquired after their health. The Gaṇas then intimated to him everything in detail. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा भगवान् रुद्रो महालीलाकरः प्रभुः ।
अभयं दत्तवांस्तेभ्यो महोत्साहं प्रवर्द्धयन् ॥ ५६॥

English
On hearing it, lord Śiva, the expert in divine sports assured them of freedom from fear increasing their enthusiasm. (56.)
