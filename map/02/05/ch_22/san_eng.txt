### **CHAPTER TWENTYTWO - *(Description of Jalandhara's Battle)***

---

Sanskrit
२२

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथ वीरगणै रुद्रो रौद्ररूपो महाप्रभुः ।
अभ्यगाद् वृषभारूढः सङ्ग्रामं प्रहसन्निव ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  Then the great lord Śiva assuming a terrible form went laughingly to the battle-field and sat on his bull, accompanied by his heroic Gaṇas. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रमायान्तमालोक्य सिंहनादैर्गणाः पुनः ।
निवृत्ताः सङ्गरे रौद्रा ये हि पूर्वं पराजिताः ॥ २॥

English
On seeing Śiva coming, the Gaṇas who were formerly defeated returned to fight roaring like lions. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरशब्दं च कुर्वन्तस्तेऽप्यन्ये शाङ्करा गणाः ।
सोत्सवाःसायुधा दैत्यान्निजघ्नुः शरवृष्टिभिः ॥ ३॥

English
Other Gaṇas too shouted heroically and jubilantly. Well-equipped with their weapons they killed the Daityas with showers of arrows. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्या हि भीषणं रुद्रं सर्वे दृष्ट्वा विदुद्रुवुः ।
शाङ्करं पुरुषं दृष्ट्वा पातकानीव तद्भयात् ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing Śiva the terrible, all the Daityas fled for fear from the battle field as the sins on seeing a devotee of Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो जलन्धरो दैत्यान्निवृत्तान्प्रेक्ष्य सङ्गरे ।
अभ्यधावत्स चण्डीशं मुञ्चन्बाणान्सहस्रशः ॥ ५॥

English
On seeing the Daityas returning from the battle field, Jalandhara rushed at Śiva discharging thousands of arrows. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
निशुम्भशुम्भप्रमुखा दैत्येन्द्राश्च सहस्रशः ।
अभिजग्मुश्शिवं वेगाद्रोषात्सन्दष्टदच्छदाः ॥ ६॥

English
Thousands of leading Daityas, Niṣumbha, Śumbha and others rushed at Śiva, biting their lips. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कालनेमिस्तथा वीरः खड्गरोमा बलाहकः ।
घस्मरश्च प्रचण्डश्चापरे चापि शिवं ययुः ॥ ७॥

English
Similarly Kālanemi the hero, Khaḍgaromā, Balāhaka, Ghasmara, Pracaṇḍa and others rushed at Śiva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणैः सञ्छादयामासुर्द्रुतं रुद्रगणांश्च ते ।
अङ्गानि चिच्छिदुर्वीराः शुम्भाद्या निखिला मुने ॥ ८॥

English
O sage, the heroes Śumbha and others, covered the Gaṇas of Rudra with arrows and cut their limbs. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणान्धकारसञ्छन्नं दृष्ट्वा गणबलं हरः ।
तद्बाणजालमाच्छिद्य बाणैराववृते नभः ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing his army of Gaṇas enveloped in darkness by the volleys of arrows, Śiva split the net of their arrows and encompassed the sky with his own. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यांश्च बाणवात्याभिः पीडितानकरोत्तदा ।
प्रचण्डबाणजालौघैरपातयत भूतले ॥ १०॥

English
He afflicted the Daityas with the gusts of wind raised by the arrows. He felled them to the ground with fierce volleys of arrows. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
खड्गरोमशिरः कायात्तथा परशुनाच्छिनत् ।
बलाहकस्य च शिरः खट्वाङ्गेनाकरोद्द्विधा ॥ ११॥

English
He severed the head of Khaḍgaromā from his body with his axe. He shattered the head of Balāhaka with his club into two pieces. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स बध्वा घस्मरं दैत्यं पाशेनाभ्यहनद्भुवि ।
महावीरं प्रचण्डं च चकर्त्त त्रिशिखेन ह ॥ १२॥

English
He tied the Daitya Ghasmara with his noose and dashed him on the ground. With his trident, he chopped off the great hero Pracaṇḍa. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषभेण हताः केचित्केचिद्बाणैर्निपातिता ।
न शेकुरसुराः स्थातुं गजा सिंहार्दिता इव ॥ १३॥

English
Some of the Asuras were killed by the bull. Some were struck by the arrows. Like elephants harassed by lions, the Asuras were unable to stay there. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्रोधपरीतात्मा दैत्यान् धिक् कृतवान् रणे ।
शुम्भादिकान्महादैत्यः प्रहसन्प्राह धैर्यवान् ॥ १४॥

English
Then the great Asura Jalandhara became infuriated and rebuked the Daityas in the battle. The courageous Daitya mocked at Śumbha and others and spoke thus. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
किं व उच्चरितैर्मातुर्धावद्भिः पृष्ठतो हतैः ।
न हि भीतवधः श्लाघ्यः स्वर्गदः शूरमानिनाम् ॥ १५॥

English
# *Jalandhara said:—*  Of what avail is your boasting about the pedigree of your mother if you flee back on being attacked? To die cowardly while you profess to be heroes is not commendable, nor does it yield heaven. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि वः प्रधने श्रद्धा सारो वा क्षुल्लका हृदि ।
अग्रे तिष्ठत मात्रं मे न चेद्ग्राम्यसुखे स्पृहा ॥ १६॥

English
O trivial fellows, if you have faith in war or the essential strength in the heart or if you have no lurking pleasures for sexual indulgence then you come forward and stand before me. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
रणे मृत्युर्वरश्चास्ति सर्वकामफलप्रदः ।
यशःप्रदो विशेषेण मोक्षदोऽपि प्रकीर्त्तितः ॥ १७॥

English
Death in battle is preferable. It yields all cherished desires. It is especially conducive to fame. It has been proclaimed as the bestower of salvation too. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यस्य मण्डलं भित्त्वा यायाद्वै परमं पदम् ।
परिव्राट् परमज्ञानी रणे यः सम्मुखे हतः ॥ १८॥

English
The wandering recluse of supreme knowledge and wisdom as well as he who dies fighting face to face, attain the greatest region after breaking through the solar sphere. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मृत्योर्भयं न कर्तव्यं कदाचित्कुत्रचिद्बुधैः ।
अनिवार्यो यतो ह्येष उपायैर्निखिलैरपि ॥ १९॥

English
No sensible man should ever be afraid of death. Death is inevitable notwithstanding all the remedies employed to ward it off. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
मृत्युर्जन्मवतां वीरा देहेन सह जायते ।
अद्य वाब्दशतान्ते वा मृत्युर्वै प्राणिनां ध्रुवः ॥ २०॥

English
O heroes, death is congenital to any being born. Either today or at the end of a hundred years all living beings are sure to die. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मृत्युभयमुत्सार्य युध्यध्वं समरे मुदा ।
सर्वथा परमानन्द इहामुत्राप्यसंशयः ॥ २१॥

English
Hence, cast off all fear for death. Come and fight in war joyously. In every respect there is certainly a great bliss here and hereafter. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा बोधयामास स्ववीरान्बहुशः स हि ।
धैर्यं दधुर्न ते भीत्या पलायन्तो रणाद् रुतम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said: —* Saying this, he tried to encourage his heroes in several ways. But the frightened demons did not regain courage. They fled from the battle in a trice. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दृष्ट्वा स्वसैन्यं तत्पलायनपरायणम् ।
चुक्रोधाति महावीरः सिन्धुपुत्रो जलन्धरः ॥ २३॥

English
On seeing his army on the rout, the heroic son of the ocean Jalandhara became very furious. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्रोधपरीतात्मा क्रोधाद्रुद्रं जलन्धरः ।
आह्वापयामास रणे तीव्राशनिसमस्वनः ॥ २४॥

English
Then the infuriated Jalandhara challenged for a battle in a stentorian voice like the sound of fierce thunderbolt. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
युद्ध्यस्वाद्य मया सार्धं किमेभिर्निहतैस्तव ।
यच्च किञ्चिद्बलं तेऽस्ति तद्दर्शय जटाधर ॥ २५॥

English
*Jalandhara said: —*  O ascetic, fight with me now. What is the use of slaying these? Show me what little strength you have. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा बाण सप्तत्या जघान वृषभध्वजम् ।
जलन्धरो महादैत्यः शम्भुमक्लिष्टकारिणम् ॥ २६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said: —*  After saying this, Jalandhara the great Daitya hit the bull-bannered Śiva of indefatigable endeavour, with an incessant volley of arrows. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तानप्राप्तान्महादेवो जलन्धरशरान्द्रुतम् ।
निजैर्हि निशितैर्बाणैश्चिच्छेद प्रहसन्निव ॥ २७॥

English
Laughingly, lord Śiva split all the arrows of Jalandhara by discharging his own sharp arrows even before his arrows reached him. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हयान्ध्वजं छत्रं धनुश्चिच्छेद सप्तभिः ।
जलन्धरस्य दैत्यस्य न तच्चित्रं हरे मुने ॥ २८॥

English
Then with seven arrows he split the horses, banner, umbrella and the bow of Daitya Jalandhara. O sage, it is not surprising in the case of Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स च्छिन्नधन्वा विरथः पाथोधितनयोऽसुरः ।
अभ्यधावच्छिवं क्रुद्धो गदामुद्यम्य वेगवान् ॥ २९॥

English
The infuriated Asura the son of the ocean, devoid of a chariot and with bow split up rushed at Śiva lifting his mace vigorously. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभुर्गदां च तत्क्षिप्तां सहसैव महेश्वरः ।
पाराशर्य्य महालीलो द्रुतं बाणैर्द्विधाकरोत् ॥ ३०॥

English
O Vyāsa, lord Śiva of great sports immediately split asunder the mace hurled by him, by means of his arrows. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि मुष्टिमुद्यम्य महाक्रुद्धो महासुरः ।
अभ्युद्ययौ महावेगाद्रुद्रं तं तज्जिघांसया ॥ ३१॥

English
Yet the highly infuriated great Asura rushed at Śiva with the mailed fist lifted up, with a desire to kill him. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तावदेवेश्वरेणाशु बाणोघैः स जलन्धरः ।
अक्लिष्टकर्मकारेण क्रोशमात्रमपाकृतः ॥ ३२॥

English
By a volley of arrows Jalandhara was hurled back a Krośa by Śiva of indefatigable enterprise. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जलन्धरो दैत्यो रुद्रं मत्वा बलाधिकम् ।
ससर्ज मायां गान्धर्वीमद्भुतां रुद्रमोहिनीम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Then, considering Śiva more powerful, Jalandhara the Daitya, created the illusion of Gandharvas that mysteriously fascinated even Śiva. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य मायाप्रभावात्तु गन्धर्वाप्सरसां गणाः ।
आविर्भूता अनेके च रुद्रमोहनहेतवे ॥ ३४॥

English
By the power of his Māyā, hosts of Gandharvas and celestial damsels came into view for fascinating Śiva. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जगुश्च ननृतुर्गन्धर्वाप्सरसां गणाः ।
तालवेणुमृदङ्गांश्च वादयन्ति स्म चापरे ॥ ३५॥

English
The Gandharvas and celestial damsels sang and danced. Others played on flutes, mṛdangas and cymbals. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा महदाश्चर्यं गणै रुद्रो विमोहितः ।
पतितान्यपि शस्त्राणि करेभ्यो न विवेद सः ॥ ३६॥

English
On seeing that wonderful feat, Śiva was fascinated by the Gaṇas. He was not conscious of even the garments let down from the hands. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाग्रीभूतमालोक्य रुद्रं दैत्यो जलन्धरः ।
कामतस्स जगामाशु यत्र गौरी स्थिताऽभवत् ॥ ३७॥

English
On seeing Śiva concentrated in the dance Jalandhara urged by lust immediately went to the place where Gauri stood. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
युद्धे शुम्भनिशुम्भाख्यौ स्थापयित्वा महाबलौ ।
दशदोर्दण्डपञ्चास्यस्त्रिनेत्रश्च जटाधरः ॥ ३८॥

महावृषभमारूढस्सर्वथा रुद्रसन्निभः ।
आसुर्य्या मायया व्यास स बभूव जलन्धरः ॥ ३९॥

English
He entrusted the powerful Śumbha and Niśumbha with the conduct of war. With his demonaic Māyā he assumed the form of Śiva—with ten brawny arms, five faces, three eyes, and matted hair. He was seated on the great bull. In every respect, O Vyāsa, Jalandhara appeared like Śiva. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ रुद्रं समायान्तमालोक्य भववल्लभा ।
अभ्याययौ सखीमध्यात्तद्दर्शनपथेऽभवत् ॥ ४०॥

English
On seeing Śiva coming, the beloved of Śiva came out from the midst of her female friends within the range of his vision. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
यावद्ददर्श चार्वङ्गी पार्वतीं दनुजेश्वरः ।
तावत्स वीर्यं मुमुचे जडाङ्गश्चाभवत्तदा ॥ ४१॥

English
When the lord of Asuras saw the bauntiful Pārvatī, he let drops of semen fall and his limbs became benumbed. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ज्ञात्वा तदा गौरी दानवं भयविह्वला ।
जगामान्तर्हिता वेगात्सा तदोत्तरमानसम् ॥ ४२॥

English
On realising that he was the demon, the terrified Gauri vanished immediately to the northern shore of the Mānasa lake. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तामदृष्ट्वा ततो दैत्यः क्षणाद्विद्युल्लतामिव ।
जवेनागात्पुनर्योद्धुं यत्र देवो महेश्वरः ॥ ४३॥

English
Unable to see her who disappeared in a moment like lightning, the Daitya immediately went to the place where lord Śiva stood in order to fight him. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्यपि महाविष्णुं सस्मार मनसा तदा ।
तावद्ददर्श तं देवं सोपविष्टं समीपगम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Pārvatī remembered lord Viṣṇu mentally. Immediately she saw the lord seated near her. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा पार्वती विष्णुं जगन्माता शिवप्रिया ।
प्रसन्नमनसोवाच प्रणमन्तं कृताञ्जलिम् ॥ ४५॥

English
On seeing Viṣṇu bowing to her with palms joined in reverence, Pārvatī the beloved of Śiva, the mother of the universe, spoke delightedly. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
विष्णो जलन्धरो दैत्यः कृतवान्परमाद्भुतम् ।
तत्किं न विदितं तेऽस्ति चेष्टितं तस्य दुर्मतेः ॥ ४६॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  O Viṣṇu, is it not known to you that the wicked Daitya Jalandhara perpetrated a wonderfully base deed ?” (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा जगदम्बाया वचनं गरुडध्वजः ।
प्रत्युवाच शिवां नत्वा साञ्जलिर्नम्रकन्धरः ॥ ४७॥

English
On hearing the words of the mother of the universe, the Garuḍa-bannered lord bowed to Pārvatī bending his neck and joining his palms in reverence and spoke. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीभगवानुवाच ।
भवत्याः कृपया देवि तद्वृत्तं विदितं मया ।
यदाज्ञापय मां मातस्तत्कुर्य्यां त्वदनुज्ञया ॥ ४८॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* O mother, by your favour that incident is known to me. What you shall be pleased to commend I shall perform with your permission. (48.)

---

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of Viṣṇu, Pārvatī said again. The mother of the universe desired to teach Viṣṇu the policy based on Dharma. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उचाच
तच्छ्रुत्वा विष्णुवचनं पुनरप्याह पार्वती ॥

हृषीकेशं जगन्माता धर्मनीतिं सुशिक्षयन् । ४९॥

पार्वत्युवाच ।
तेनैव दर्शितः पन्था बुध्यस्व त्वं तथैव हि ।
तत्स्त्रीपातिव्रतं धर्मं भ्रष्टं कुरु मदाज्ञया ॥ ५०॥

English
*Pārvatī said :—*  He himself has shown the path. Know that to be the way in the same manner. At my bidding, make the chastity of his wife violated. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
नान्यथा स महादैत्यो भवेद्वध्यो रमेश्वर ।
पातिव्रतसमो नान्यो धर्मोऽस्ति पृथिवीतले ॥ ५१॥

English
O Viṣṇu, that great Daitya cannot be killed otherwise. In the earth there is no other virtue equal to chastity. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्यनुज्ञां समाकर्ण्य शिरसाधाय तां हरिः ।
छल कर्त्तुं जगामाशु पुनर्जालन्धरं पुरम् ॥ ५२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  On hearing this command and accepting it with lowered head, Viṣṇu immediately went to the city of Jalandhara for practising deception. (52.)
