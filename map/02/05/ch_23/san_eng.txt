### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - *(Outraging the modesty of Vṛndā)***

---

Sanskrit
२३

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ वद त्वं वदतां वर ।
किमकार्षीद्धरिस्तत्र धर्मं तत्याज सा कथम् ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said :—*  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, please narrate, O eloquent one, what did Viṣṇu do there ? How did she err from her virtue ? (1.)

---

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  After going to the city of Jalandhara, Viṣṇu thought of violating the chastity of Vṛndā. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
विष्णुर्जालन्धरं गत्वा दैत्यस्य पुटभेदनम् ॥

पातिव्रत्यस्य भङ्गाय वृन्दायाश्चाकरोन्मतिम् । २॥

वृन्दां स दर्शयामास स्वप्नं मायाविनां वरः ।
स्वयं तन्नगरोद्यानमास्थितोऽद्भुतविग्रहः ॥ ३॥

English
The foremost among those who wield illusion, he assumed a wonderful body and stationed himself in a park of the city. He made Vṛndā see a dream. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ वृन्दा तदा देवी तत्पत्नी निशि सुव्रता ।
हरेर्मायाप्रभावात्तु दुस्स्वप्नं सा ददर्श ह ॥ ४॥

English
The gentle lady Vṛndā, the wife of Jalandhara, though of pure rites, had a very bad dream at night on account of Viṣṇu's power of illusion. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वप्नमध्ये हि सा विष्णुमायया प्रददर्श ह ।
भर्त्तारं महिषारूढं तैलाभ्यक्तं दिगम्बरम् ॥ ५॥

English
In the dream as a result of Viṣṇu's power of illusion she saw the naked form of her husband anointed with oil and seated on a buffalo. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णप्रसूनभूषाढ्यं क्रव्यादगणसेवितम् ।
दक्षिणाशां गतं मुण्डं तमसा च वृतं तदा ॥ ६॥

English
He was proceeding in the southern direction. His head had been completely shaved. He was wearing black flowers to decorate himself. He was being served by a number of Asuras. He was completely encompassed by darkness. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपुरं सागरे मग्नं सहसैवात्मना सह ।
इत्यादि बहुदुःस्वप्नान्निशान्ते सा ददर्श ह ॥ ७॥

English
Later, towards the end of the night she had various bad dreams, such as the whole city was submerged in the sea, all of a sudden, along with herself. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रबुध्य सा बाला तं स्वप्नं स्वं विचिन्वती ।
ददर्शोदितमादित्यं सच्छिद्रं निष्प्रभं मुहुः ॥ ८॥

English
Then the lady woke up still thinking of the dream she had had. She saw the rising sun with a hole in the middle and fading repeatedly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तदनिष्टमिदं ज्ञात्वा रुदन्ती भयविह्वला ।
कुत्रचिन्नाप सा शर्म गोपुराट्टालभूमिषु ॥ ९॥

English
On realising that it was a bad portent, the terrified lady began to cry. She did not feel happy at all in the spacious terraces and towers of the palace. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःसखीद्वययुता नगरोद्यानमागमत् ।
तत्रापि सा गता बाला न प्राप कुत्रचित्सुखम् ॥ १०॥

English
With two of her friends she then went to the park in the city. Even there she did not find herself at ease. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जलन्धरस्त्री सा निर्विण्णोद्विग्नमानसा ।
वनाद्वनान्तरं याता नैव वेदात्मना तदा ॥ ११॥

English
Then she, the dejected gloomy wife of Jalandhara, wandered from forest to forest. She was not conscious of even herself. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रमती सा ततो बाला ददर्शातीव भीषणौ ।
राक्षसौ सिंहवदनौ दृष्ट्वा दशनभासुरौ ॥ १२॥

English
The wandering lady saw two demons of terrible leonine faces with shining curved fanglike teeth. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तौ दृष्ट्वा विह्वलातीव पलायनपरा तदा ।
ददर्श तापसं शान्तं सशिष्यं मौनमास्थितम् ॥ १३॥

English
Terrified much on seeing them, the lady fled from there and saw an ascetic of calm countenance observing silence and accompanied by his disciple. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तत्कण्ठमासाद्य निजां बाहुलतां भयात् ।
मुने मां रक्ष शरणमागतास्मीत्यभाषत ॥ १४॥

English
Putting her tender creeperlike hands round his neck due to fright she gasped out—"O sage, save me. I have sought refuge in you." (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिस्तां विह्वलां दृष्ट्वा राक्षसानुगतां तदा ।
हुङ्कारेणैव तौ घोरौ चकार विमुखौ द्रुतम् ॥ १५॥

English
Seeing the agitated lady followed by the demons the sage drove them back with a loud bellowing sound of "Hum". (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् हुङ्कारभयत्रस्तौ दृष्ट्वा तौ विमुखौ गतौ ।
विस्मितातीव दैत्येन्द्रपत्नी साभून्मुने हृदि ॥ १६॥

English
O sage, seeing them routed and terrified by the mere Humkāra, the wife of the king of Daityas was struck with a great wonder in her heart. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्सा मुनिनाथं तं भयान्मुक्ता कृताञ्जलिः ।
प्रणम्य दण्डवद्भूमौ वृन्दा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १७॥

English
Freed from the fear she bowed down to the great sage with palms joined in reverence and prostrated herself in front of him. Vṛndā then spoke. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वृन्दोवाच ।
मुनिनाथ दयासिन्धो परपीडानिवारक ।
रक्षिताहं त्वया घोराद्भयादस्मात्खलोद्भवात् ॥ १८॥

English
*Vṛndā said:—*  “O leader of sages, O ocean of mercy, O remover of harassment from others, I have been saved by you from this terrible danger from the wicked demons. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
समर्थः सर्वथा त्वं हि सर्वज्ञोऽपि कृपानिधे ।
किञ्चिद्विज्ञप्तुमिच्छामि कृपया तन्निशामय ॥ १९॥

English
You are competent in every respect. You are omniscient. Yet I wish to submit something. Be pleased to hear it. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरो हि मद्भर्त्ता रुद्रं योद्धुं गतः प्रभो ।
स तत्रास्ते कथं युद्धे तन्मे कथय सुव्रत ॥ २०॥

English
O lord, Jalandhara my husband has gone to fight Śiva. O holy one of good rites, how does he fare in the war? Please tell me.” (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
मुनिस्तद्वाक्यमाकर्ण्य मौनं कपटमास्थितः ।
कर्त्तुं स्वार्थं विधानज्ञः कृपयोर्ध्वमवैक्षत ॥ २१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing her words, the sage feigned a deceptive silence. Fully aware of the means of achieving his selfish ends he looked up sympathetically. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्कपीशावायातौ तं प्रणम्याग्रतः स्थितौ ।
ततस्तद्भ्रूलतासंज्ञानियुक्तौ गगनं गतौ ॥ २२॥

English
In the meantime two lordly monkeys came there and stood bowing down in front of him. At a significant gesture from his eyebrows, the monkeys rose into the sky again. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नीत्वा क्षणार्द्धमागत्य पुनस्तस्याग्रतः स्थितौ ।
तस्यैव कं कबन्धं च हस्तावास्तां मुनीश्वर ॥ २३॥

English
O great sage, within a trice, they came back taking with them his head, body and limbs and stood in front of the sage. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरः कबन्धं हस्तौ तौ दृष्ट्वाब्धितनयस्य सा ।
पपात मूर्च्छिता भूमौ भर्तृव्यसनदुःखिता ॥ २४॥

English
On seeing the head, body and limbs of her husband, Vṛndā fell unconscious, extremely pained at the misery of her lord. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
वृन्दोवाच ।
यः पुरा सुखसंवादैर्विनोदयसि मां प्रभो ।
स कथं न वदस्यद्य वल्लभां मामनागसम् ॥ २५॥

English
*Vṛndā said:—*  “O lord, formerly you used to humour me with pleasant chats. How is it that you do not speak to me now, to your pious beloved? (25.)

---

Sanskrit
येन देवाः सगन्धर्वा निर्जिता विष्णुना सह ।
कथं स तापसेनाद्य त्रैलोक्यविजयी हतः ॥ २६॥

English
How is it that you, by whom all the gods including the Gandharvas and Viṣṇu had been defeated, you who had conquered the three worlds, have now been killed by a poor sage? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नाङ्गीकृतं हि मे वाक्यं रुद्रतत्त्वमजानता ।
परं ब्रह्म शिवश्चेति वदन्त्या दैत्यसत्तम ॥ २७॥

English
O excellent Daitya, you did not know the reality of Śiva nor did you pay heed to my words 'Śiva is Supreme Brahman.' (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वं हि मया ज्ञातस्तव सेवाप्रभावतः ।
गर्वितेन त्वया नैव कुसङ्गवशगेन हि ॥ २८॥

English
Having served you I found that it was not due to haughtiness but due to your association with bad men that you did all this." (28.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं प्रभाष्य बहुधा स्वधर्मस्था च तत्प्रिया ।
विललाप विचित्रं सा हृदयेन विदूयता ॥ २९॥

English
Saying these and other words of lamentation, his beloved wife strictly adhering to virtue, cried in diverse ways with a pained heart. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःसा धैर्यमालम्ब्य दुःखोच्छ्वासान्विमुञ्चती ।
उवाच मुनिवर्यं तं सुप्रणम्य कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ३०॥

English
Then steadying herself a little, and heaving deep sighs of grief she bowed to the excellent sage with palms joined in reverence. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपानिधे मुनिश्रेष्ठ परोपकरणादर ।
मयि कृत्वा कृपां साधो जीवयैनं मम प्रभुम् ॥ ३१॥

English
"O excellent sage, storehouse of mercy, eager to help others, O gentle sir, take pity on me and resuscitate my lord. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्त्वमस्य पुनः शक्तो जीवनाय मतो मम ।
अतः सञ्जीवयैनं मे प्राणनाथं मुनीश्वर ॥ ३२॥

English
O great sage, I know that you are competent to enliven him again. Hence please resuscitate my beloved husband." (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा दैत्यपत्नी सा पतिव्रत्यपरायणा ।
पादयोः पतिता तस्य दुःखश्वासान् विमुञ्चती ॥ ३३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this, the chaste wife of the Daitya fell at his feet heaving sighs of grief. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिरुवाच ।
नायं जीवयितुं शक्तो रुद्रेण निहतो युधि ।
रुद्रेण निहता युद्धे न जीवन्ति कदाचन ॥ ३४॥

English
This Daitya cannot be enlivened because he has been killed by Śiva in the battle. Those killed in battle by Śiva never return to life. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि कृपयाविष्ट एनं सञ्जीवयाम्यहम् ।
रक्ष्याः शरणगाश्चेति जानन्धर्मं सनातनम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Still, knowing the eternal Dharma that those who seek refuge should be protected, I shall resuscitate him urged by pity. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स मुनिस्तस्या जीवयित्वा पतिं मुने ।
अन्तर्दधे ततो विष्णुः सर्वमायाविनां वरः ॥ ३६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this and restoring him to life, O sage, that sage who was Viṣṇu the foremost among those who wield illusion vanished from the scene. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
द्रुतं स जीवितस्तेनोत्थितः सागरनन्दनः ।
वृन्दामालिङ्ग्य तद्वक्त्रं चुचुम्ब प्रीतमानसः ॥ ३७॥

English
Jalandhara thus revived to life by him stood up. Delighted in mind he embraced Vṛndā and kissed her face. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ वृन्दापि भर्तारं दृष्ट्वा हर्षितमानसा ।
जहौ शोकं च निखिलं स्वप्नवद् हृद्यमन्यत ॥ ३८॥

English
On seeing her husband, Vṛndā too was delighted. She forgot her sorrow. She considered everything a dream. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रसन्नहृदया सा हि सञ्जातहृच्छया ।
रेमे तद्वनमध्यस्था तद्युक्ता बहुवासरान् ॥ ३९॥

English
Delighted in the heart and with all the dormant passions kindled up, she sported with him for many days in the middle of that forest. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्सुरतस्यान्ते दृष्ट्वा विष्णुं तमेव हि ।
निर्भर्त्स्य क्रोधसंयुक्ता वृन्दा वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ४०॥

English
Once at the end of the sexual intercourse she realised that it was Viṣṇu. Vṛndā rebuked him angrily and spoke thus. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
वृन्दोवाच ।
धिक् तदेवं हरे शीलं परदाराभिगामिनः ।
ज्ञातोऽसि त्वं मया सम्यङ्मायी प्रत्यक्षतापसः ॥ ४१॥

English
*Vṛndā said:—*  Fie on this misdeed of Viṣṇu in outraging the modesty of another man’s wife. I have now realised you as the wielder of illusion, appearing in the guise of an ascetic. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा क्रोधमापन्ना दर्शयन्ती स्वतेजसम् ।
शशाप केशवं व्यास पातिव्रत्यरता च सा ॥ ४२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O Vyāsa, saying thus in great anger she showed her brilliant powers as a staunch chaste lady by cursing Viṣṇu. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
रे महाधम दैत्यारे परधर्मविदूषक ।
गृह्णीष्व शठ मद्दत्तं शापं सर्वविषोल्बणम् ॥ ४३॥

English
“O base foe of the Daityas, defiler of other people’s virtue, O wicked one, take this curse from me, greater in force than all persons. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यौ त्वया मायया ख्यातौ स्वकीयौ दर्शितौ मम ।
तावेव राक्षसौ भूत्वा भार्यां तव हरिष्यतः ॥ ४४॥

English
The two persons whom you made to appear in front of me shall become Rākṣasas$^{229}$ and abduct your wife. (44.)

[fn 229. The two Rākṣasas referred to here were Mārīca and Rāvaṇa who abducted Sītā, wife of Rāma, the seventh incarnation of Viṣṇu.]
---

English
You will be distressed on account of separation from your wife roaming about with Śeṣa ‘lord of snakes’$^{230}$ who posed as your disciple here. You will seek the help of monkeys$^{231}$ in the forest. (45.)

[fn 230. The monkeys referred to here were Sugrīva, Hanūmān, Nala Nīla and others.]

[fn 231. The expression ‘lord of snakes’ signifies Lakṣamana, said to be the incarnation of Śeṣa.]
---

Sanskrit
त्वं चापि भार्यादुःखार्तो वने कपिसहायवान् ।
भ्रम सर्पेश्वरेणायं यस्ते शिष्यत्वमागतः४५॥

इत्युक्त्वा सा तदा वृन्दा प्रविशद्धव्यवाहनम् ।
विष्णुना वार्यमाणापि तत्स्थितासक्तचेतसा ॥ ४६॥

English
After saying this, Vṛndā entered fire though prevented by Viṣṇu who was fascinated by her charms. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे देवा ब्रह्माद्या निखिला मुने ।
आगताः खे समं दारैः सद्गतिं वै दिदृक्षवः ॥ ४७॥

English
O sage, then Brahmā and other gods, gathered in the sky accompanied by their wives in order to see the salvation of Vṛndā. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दैत्येन्द्रपत्न्यास्तु तज्ज्योतिः परमं महत् ।
पश्यतां सर्वदेवानामलोकमगमद्द्रुतम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Then the great brilliance of the wife of Jalandhara immediately went to Śivaloka even as the gods stood watching. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवातनौ विलीनं तद्वृन्दातेजो बभूव ह ।
आसीज्जयजयारावः खस्थितामरपङ्क्तिषु ॥ ४९॥

English
The refulgence of Vṛndā became merged in Pārvatī. There was a great shout of “Victory” in the rows of the gods standing in the sky. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वृन्दा महाराज्ञी कालनेमिसुतोत्तमा ।
पातिव्रत्यप्रभावाच्च मुक्तिं प्राप परां मुने ॥ ५०॥

English
O sage, thus the great queen Vṛndā the excellent daughter of Kālanemi attained great salvation, thanks to the power of her chastity. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हरिस्तामनुसंस्मन्मुहु-
र्वृन्दाचिताभस्मरजोऽवगुण्ठितः ।
तत्रैव तस्थौ सुरसिद्धसङ्घकैः
प्रबोध्यमानोऽपि ययौ न शान्तिम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Viṣṇu thought of Vṛndā remorsefully. The smoke and dust from her funeral pyre covered his face. He stood there itself without any peace of mind though urged and consoled by hosts of gods and Siddhas. (51.)

इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां पञ्चमे युद्धखण्डे जलन्धरवधोपाख्याने वृन्दापातिव्रतभङ्गदेहत्यागवर्णनं नाम त्रयोविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ २.५.२३॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥ 
द्वितीयायां रुद्रसंहितायां पञ्चमो युद्धखण्डे
