### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - *(Jalandhara is slain)***

---

Sanskrit
२४

व्यास उवाच ।
विधेः श्रेष्ठसुत प्राज्ञः कथेयं श्राविताद्भुता ।
ततश्च किमभूदाजौ कथं दैत्यो हतो वद ॥ १॥

English
O excellent son of Brahmā, O intelligent one, you have narrated a wonderful story. What happened thereafter in the battle? How was the Asura killed? Please narrate. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अदृश्य गिरिजां तत्र दैत्येन्द्रे रणमागते ।
गान्धर्वे च विलीने हि चैतन्योऽभूद्वृषध्वजः ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Unable to see Pārvatī, the king of Daityas returned to the battle ground. The groups of deceptive Gandharvas vanished. It was only then that the bull-bannered deity regained awareness of the surroundings. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्धानगतां मायां दृष्ट्वा बुद्धो हि शङ्करः ।
चुक्रोधातीव संहारी लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the illusion vanished, Śiva woke up. Following the way of the world, the annihilator became very furious. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवो विस्मितमानसः पुन-
र्जगाम युद्धाय जलन्धरं रुषा ।
स चापि दैत्यः पुनरागतं शिवं
दृष्ट्वा शरोघैःसमवाकिरद्रणे ॥ ४॥

English
Then Śiva was a bit surprised in the mind. He approached Jalandhara angrily in order to fight with him. On seeing Śiva approaching again, the Asura showered him with arrows. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षिप्तं प्रभुस्तं शरजालमुग्रं
जलन्धरेणातिबलीयसा हरः ।
द्रुतं प्रचिच्छेदशरैर्वरैर्निजै-
र्न चित्रमत्र त्रिभवप्रहन्तुः ॥ ५॥

English
Lord Śiva immediately split the cluster of arrows discharged by the powerful Jalandhara by means of his own excellent arrows. This was not surprising for the annihilator of the three worlds. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जलन्धरो दृष्ट्वा रुद्रमद्भुतविक्रमम् ।
चकार मायया गौरीं त्र्यम्बकं मोहयन्निव ॥ ६॥

English
Seeing Śiva exhibiting wonderful feats of valour, Jalandhara created Pārvatī by means of his illusion in order to delude Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
रथोपरि गतां बद्धां रुदन्तीं पार्वतीं शिवः ।
निशुम्भशुम्भदैत्यैश्च वध्यमानां ददर्श सः ॥ ७॥

English
Śiva saw Pārvatī tied to the chariot and crying. She was being harrassed by Niśumbha, Śumbha and other Daityas. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
गौरीं तथाविधां दृष्ट्वा लौकिकीं दर्शयन् गतिम् ।
बभूव प्राकृत इव शिवोऽप्युद्विग्नमानसः ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing that in her plight, Śiva became dispirited and dejected in the mind like an ordinary man pursuing the way of the world. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अवाङ्मुखस्थितस्तूष्णीं नानालीलाविशारदः ।
शिथिलाङ्गो विषण्णात्मा विस्मृत्य स्वपराक्रमम् ॥ ९॥

English
He, an expert in various kinds of sports, remained silent with face drooping down, utterly dejected, exhausted and forgetful of his own prowess. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जलन्धरो वेगात् त्रिभिर्विव्याध सायकैः ।
आपुङ्खमग्नैस्तं रुद्रं शिरस्युरसि चोदरे ॥ १०॥

English
Then Jalandhara hurriedly hit Śiva in his chest, belly and the head with three arrows that went deep down as far as their feathered tail. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो रुद्रो महालीलो ज्ञाततत्त्वः क्षणात्प्रभुः ।
रौद्ररूपधरो जातो ज्वालामालातिभीषणः ॥ ११॥

English
Then within a trice, lord Śiva, the principle of perfect wisdom, expert in great sports, assumed a terrific form, dreadfully blazing. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यातीव महारौद्ररूपं दृष्ट्वा महासुराः ।
न शेकुः प्रमुखे स्थातुं भेजिरे ते दिशो दश ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing his excessively terrible form, the Daityas fled to the ten different quarters. They were unable to stay facing him. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
निशुम्भशुम्भावपि यौ विख्यातौ वीरसत्तमौ ।
अपि तौ शेकतुर्नैव रणे स्थातुं मुनीश्वर ॥ १३॥

English
O great sage, even Śumbha and Niśumbha who were renowned for their prowess could not stand in the battle ground. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरकृता मायान्तर्हिताभूच्च तत्क्षणम् ।
हाहाकारो महानासीत्सङ्ग्रामे सर्वतोमुखे ॥ १४॥

English
The illusion created by Jalandhara had vanished in an instant. In that all out battle there was great hue and cry. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शापं ददौ रुद्रस्तयोः शुम्भनिशुम्भयोः ।
पलायमानौ तौ दृष्ट्वा धिक्कृत्य क्रोधसंयुतः ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing Śumbha and Niśumbha fleeing, the infuriated Śiva rebuked them and cursed as follows. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
युवां दुष्टावतिखलावपराधकरौ मम ।
पार्वतीदण्डदातारौ रणादस्मात्पराङ्मुखौ ॥ १६॥

English
*Śiva said:—* "You are wicked and excessively roguish. You have offended me by harassing Pārvatī. Now both of you have deserted the battle ground. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पराङ्मुखो न हन्तव्य इति वध्यौ न मे युवाम् ।
मम युद्धादतिक्रान्तौ गौर्य्या वध्यौ भविष्यतः ॥ १७॥

English
A person fleeing the battle ground shall not be killed. So I do not kill you. Since you have escaped from a fight with me you would be killed by Pārvatī."$^{232}$ (17.)

[fn 232. As cursed by Śiva, the Asuras Śumbha and Niśumbha were killed by Pārvatī later on. For the detailed description of their destruction, see Mārkaṇḍeya P.]
---

Sanskrit
एवं वदति गौरीशे सिन्धुपुत्रो जलन्धरः ।
चुक्रोधातीव रुद्राय ज्वलज्ज्वलनसन्निभः ॥ १८॥

English
Even as Śiva was saying, Jalandhara, son of the ocean, became very furious with Śiva like the blazing fire. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रे रणे महावेगाद्ववर्ष निशितान् शरान् ।
बाणान्धकारसञ्छन्नं तथा भूमितलं ह्यभूत् ॥ १९॥

English
One after the other, he showered many sharp arrows on Śiva in the battle. The whole of the Earth became enveloped in darkness by his arrows. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यावद् रुद्रः प्रचिच्छेद तस्य बाणगणान् द्रुतम् ।
तावत्स परिघेणाशु जघान वृषभं बली ॥ २०॥

English
Śiva split the arrows swiftly, the powerful Daitya hit the bull with an iron club. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषस्तेन प्रहारेण परवृत्तो रणाङ्गणात् ।
रुद्रेण कृश्यमाणोऽपि न तस्थौ रणभूमिषु ॥ २१॥

English
Due to that blow the bull turned away from the battle field. Even when dragged by Śiva it did not stand there. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ लोके महारुद्रः स्वीयं तेजोऽतिदुःसहम् ।
दर्शयामास सर्वस्मै सत्यमेतन्मुनीश्वर ॥ २२॥

English
Then the great Śiva put forth an unbearable splendour visible to all in the battle field. O great sage, this is true. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः परमसङ्क्रुद्धो रुद्रो रौद्रवपुर्धरः ।
प्रलयानलवद् घोरो बभूव सहसा प्रभुः ॥ २३॥

English
Then the infuriated Śiva assuming a terrible form, became as dreadful as the fire of dissolution, all of a sudden. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा पुरःस्थितं दैत्यं मेरुकूटमिव स्थितम् ।
अवध्यत्वमपि श्रुत्वाप्यन्यैरभ्युद्यतोऽभवत् ॥ २४॥

English
On seeing the Daitya standing in front like the lofty peak of Meru and hearing from others that he could not be killed, he stood ready for it. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणो वचनं रक्षन् रक्षको जगतां प्रभुः ।
हृदानुग्रहमातन्वंस्तद्वधाय मनो दधत् ॥ २५॥

English
As desired by Brahmā, the lord protector of the worlds decided to kill Jalandhara, blessing him in the heart of his heart. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
कोपं कृत्वा परं शूली पादाङ्गुष्ठेन लीलया ।
महाम्भसि चकाराशु रथाङ्गं रौद्रमद्भुतम् ॥ २६॥

English
Becoming excessively angry, the trident bearing deity made a mysteriously terrible wheel in the great waters by means of his big toe indulging in a divine sport. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वार्णवाम्भसि शितं भगवान् रथाङ्गं
स्मृत्वा जगत्त्रयमनेन हतं पुरारिः ।
दक्षान्धकान्तकपुरत्रययज्ञहन्ता
लोकत्रयान्तककरः प्रहसन्नुवाच ॥ २७॥

English
Creating a sharp wheel in the waters of the ocean and remembering that the three worlds had been harassed by Jalandhara, the lord Śiva who had slain Dakṣa, Andhaka Antaka and destroyed the three cities and the sacrifice of Dakṣa$^{233}$ and annihilated the three worlds said laughingly. (27.)

[fn 233. For the destruction of Dakṣa's sacrifice see SP. RS. Section 2. Chs 29-37. For the destruction of Andhaka, ibid. Section 5. Chs 42-49.]
---

Sanskrit
महारुद्र उवाच ।
पादेन निर्मितं चक्रं जलन्धर महाम्भसि ।
बलवान्यदि चोद्धर्त्तुं तिष्ठ योद्धुं न चान्यथा ॥ २८॥

English
*Śiva said:—* O Jalandhara, if you are powerful enough to lift the wheel created by me with the leg in the great waters, you will be competent to stand and fight with me, not otherwise. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा क्रोधेनादीप्तलोचनः ।
प्रदहन्निव चक्षुर्भ्यां प्राहालोक्य स शङ्करम् ॥ २९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing his words the Daitya's eyes gleamed fiercely with anger. He looked at Śiva as if burning him with his eyes and said:— (29.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धर उवाच ।
रेखामुद्धृत्य हत्वा च सगणं त्वां हि शङ्कर ।
हत्वा लोकान्सुरैः सार्धं स्वभागं गरुडो यथा ॥ ३०॥

हन्तुं चराचरं सर्वं समर्थोऽहं सवासवम् ।
को महेश्वर मद्बाणैरभेद्यो भुवनत्रये ॥ ३१॥

English
*Jalandhara said:—*  After uplifting the wheel, I shall be killing you with your Gaṇas. Like Garuḍa killing the serpents I shall kill all the people in the world along with the gods. I can destroy the mobile and immobile along with Indra. O lord Śiva, who is there in the three worlds that can escape being pierced by my arrows? (30-31.)

---

Sanskrit
बालभावेन भगवान्तपसैव विनिर्जितः ।
ब्रह्मा बलिष्ठः स्थाने मे मुनिभिः सुरपुङ्गवैः ॥ ३२॥

English
Even in my childhood, lord Brahmā had been defeated by my vigour. That powerful Brahmā is in my abode now along with the sages and leading gods. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्धं क्षणेन सकलं त्रैलोक्यं सचराचरम् ।
तपसा किं त्वया रुद्र निर्जितो भगवानपि ॥ ३३॥

English
Within a trice, the entire universe of the mobile and immobile has been burnt by me. O Śiva, what can be done by you or by your penance? Even lord Brahmā has been defeated. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्राग्नियमवित्तेशवायुवारीश्वरादयः ।
न सेहिरे यथा नागा गन्धं पक्षिपतेरिव ॥ ३४॥

English
Indra, Agni, Yama, Kubera, Vāyu, and Varuṇa and others were unable to endure my valour like the serpents unable to bear even the odour of the lord of birds. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
न लब्धं दिवि भूमौ च वाहनं मम शङ्कर ।
समस्तान्पर्वतान्प्राप्य धर्षिताश्च गणेश्वराः ॥ ३५॥

English
O Śiva, I have never been obstructed either in the heaven or on the earth. I have gone over all the mountains and crushed all the leading Gaṇas. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरीन्द्रो मन्दरः श्रीमान्नीलो मेरुः सुशोभनः ।
धर्षितो बाहुदण्डेन कण्ड्वा उत्सर्पणाय मे ॥ ३६॥

English
To remove the itching sensation in my arms I have hit the lofty mountain Mandara, the glorious mountain Nīla and the lustrous mountain Meru. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गा निरुद्धा बाहुभ्यां लीलार्थं हिमवद्गिरौ ।
अरीणां मम भृत्यैश्च जयो लब्धो दिवौकसाम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Just for the sport the river Gaṅgā was checked by me on the Himālaya mountain. Even my servants were victorious over the gods, my enemies. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
वडवाया मुखं बद्धं गृहीत्वा तां करेण तु ।
तत्क्षणादेव सकलमेकार्णवमभूत्तदा ॥ ३८॥

English
I seized the submarine fire$^{234}$ and closed its mouth when the entire ocean became one single unit instantaneously. (38.)

[fn 234. The submarine fire (Baḍavā) is a flame with the head of a horse that consumes the water of the rivers falling into the ocean. When it is incapacitated, the water overflows the shore and drowns the universe. The entire units of creation are then drawn together and remain invisible in the ocean.]


---

Sanskrit
ऐरावतादयो नागाः क्षिप्ताः सिन्धुजलोपरि ।
सरथो भगवानिन्द्रः क्षिप्तश्च शतयोजनम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Airāvata and other elephants have been hurled into the ocean. Lord Indra along with his chariot has been thrown by me a hundred Yojanas away. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even Guruḍa has been bound by me along with Viṣṇu by means of the serpent noose. Urvaśī$^{235}$ and other women have been imprisoned by me. (40.)

[fn 235. The celestial damsels, Urvaśī, Rambhā, Menakā, Tilottamā and others are the symbols of heavenly beauty.]
---

Sanskrit

जलन्धरं महादैत्यं सिन्धुपुत्रं महाबलम् ॥ ४१॥

English
O Śiva, you do not know me the conqueror of the three worlds, Jalandhara, the great Daitya and the powerful son of the ocean. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वाथ महादेवं तदा वारिधिनन्दनः ।

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this to lord Śiva, the son of the ocean did not move nor did he remember the Dānavas killed in the battle. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

तिरस्कृतो महादेवो वचनैः कटुकाक्षरैः ॥ ४३॥

English
Lord Śiva was slighted and insulted by means of harsh words by the haughty impudent Daitya after slapping each arm by the other forcibly. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

विजहास महादेवः परमं क्रोधमादधे ॥ ४४॥

English
On hearing the inauspicious words of the Daitya, lord Śiva laughed mockingly and became furious. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Śiva held in his hand the wheel Sudarśana which he had made with his toe and got ready to kill him. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Lord Śiva hurled the discus Sudarśana which resembled a crore suns and the fire of dissolution. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

जहार तच्छिरो वेगान्महदायतलोचनम् ॥ ४७॥

English
Blazing the heaven and the earth, the discus hit Jalandhara and severed his head with wide gaping eyes. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The body of the son of the ocean fell on the ground from the chariot making the earth resonant. The head too fell. There was a great hue and cry. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English
His body fell in two halves like the mountain of collyrium split by the thunderbolt and hurled in the ocean. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The whole universe was filled with his terrible blood O great sage, the entire earth became deformed. (50.)

Sanskrit
तद्रक्तमखिलं रुद्रनियोगान्मांसमेव च ।
महारौरवमासाद्य रक्तकुण्डमभूदिह ॥ ५१॥

English
His entire blood and flesh, at the bidding of Śiva was taken to the hell Mahāraurava$^{236}$ and became a big pit of blood there. (51.)

[fn 236. Mahāraurava is one of the many hells to which the souls of the wicked are sent. But as Jalandhara had emanated from Rudra, his soul was merged into Rudra’s soul but his flesh and blood went to Mahāraurava hell and were turned into a pool of blood.]
---

Sanskrit
तत्तेजो निर्गतं देहाद् रुद्रे च लयमागमत् ।
वृन्दादेहोद्भवं यद्वद्गौर्य्यां हि विलयं गतम् ॥ ५२॥

English
His splendour that came out of his body merged into Śiva just like the splendour that came out of the body of Vṛndā and merged into Pārvatī. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरं हतं दृष्ट्वा देवगन्धर्वपन्नगाः ।
अभवन्सुप्रसन्नाश्च साधु देवेति चाब्रुवन् ॥ ५३॥

English
On seeing Jalandhara killed, the gods, the Gandharvas and the serpents became highly delighted and said “Well done, O lord”. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे प्रसन्नतां याता देवसिद्धमुनीश्वराः ।
पुष्पवृष्टिं प्रकुर्वाणास्तद्यशो जगुरुच्चकैः ॥ ५४॥

English
The gods, Siddhas and great sages were delighted. Making showers of flowers they sang his glory loudly. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाङ्गना महामोदान्ननृतुः प्रेमविह्वलाः ।
कलस्वराः कलपदं किन्नरैः सह सञ्जगुः ॥ ५५॥

English
The celestial damsels excited by love and joy danced. In the company of Kinnaras they sang in harmonious sweet voice. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
दिशः प्रसेदुः सर्वाश्च हते वृन्दापतौ मुने ।
ववुः पुण्याः सुखस्पर्शा वायवस्त्रिविधा अपि ॥ ५६॥

English
O sage, the quarters became clear when Vṛndā's husband was killed. The three winds, gentle to the touch and sanctifying, blew. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रमाः शीततां यातो रविस्तेपे सुतेजसा ।
अग्नयो जज्वलुः शान्ता बभूवाविकृतं नभः ॥ ५७॥

English
The moon became cool. The sun blazed brilliantly. The fires blazed quietly. The sky became clear. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं त्रैलोक्यमखिलं स्वास्थ्यमापाधिकं मुने ।
हतेऽब्धितनये तस्मिन्हरेणानन्तमूर्तिना ॥ ५८॥

English
O sage, thus the entire universe of the three worlds regained their earlier health and normalcy much when the son of the ocean was killed by Śiva of infinite forms. (58.)
