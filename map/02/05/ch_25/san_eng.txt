### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - *(Prayer by the gods)***

---

Sanskrit
२५

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथ ब्रह्मादयो देवा मुनयश्चाखिलास्तथा ।
तुष्टुवुर्देवदेवेशं वाग्भिरिष्टाभिरानताः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Then Brahmā, other gods and the sages eulogised lord Śiva humbly by means of pleasing words. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
साधुसौख्यप्रदस्त्वं हि सर्वदा भक्तदुःखहा ॥ २॥

English
*The gods said:—* O great lord, lord of the gods favourably disposed to those who seek refuge, you always bestow happiness upon the saintly men and quell the misery of your devotees. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं महाद्भुतसल्लीलो भक्तिगम्यो दुरासदः ।
दुराराध्योऽसतां नाथ प्रसन्नः सर्वदा भव ॥ ३॥

English
O lord, you exhibit wonderfully good divine sports and are available by devotion. You are incapable of being attained or propitiated by the evil-minded. Be favourable to us always. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदोऽपि महिमानं ते न जानाति हि तत्त्वतः ।
यथामति महात्मानः सर्वे गायन्ति सद्यशः ॥ ४॥

English
Even the Veda does not know your greatness in reality. Noble men sing your great glory to the extent of their intellect. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
माहात्म्यमतिगूढं ते सहस्रवदनादयः ।
सदा गायन्ति सुप्रीत्या पुनन्ति स्वगिरं हि ते ॥ ५॥

English
Indra$^{237}$ and others sing your secret greatness always with pleasure and sanctify their own tongue. (5.)

[fn 237. Indra is called "the thousand-faced". In fact he is 'the thousand-eyed God'. According to the Puranic tradition, Indra seduced Ahalyā the wife of the sage Gautama, whereupon the sage cursed him to bear on his body a thousand marks resembling the female organ which were afterwards changed to eyes. He is therefore called Sahasrākṣa 'the thousand-eyed'.]
---

Sanskrit
कृपया तव देवेश ब्रह्मज्ञानी भवेज्जडः ।
भक्तिगम्यः सदा त्वं वा इति वेदा ब्रुवन्ति हि ॥ ६॥

English
O lord of gods, by your favour even a sluggish person realizes Brahman. The Vedas say that you are always attainable by devotion. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं वै दीनदयालुश्च सर्वत्र व्यापकः सदा ।
आविर्भवसि सद्भक्त्या निर्विकारःसतां गतिः ॥ ७॥

English
You are merciful to the distressed. You are all pervasive. You manifest yourself by good devotion. You are free from aberrations. You are the goal of the good. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्त्यैव ते महेशान बहवः सिद्धिमागताः ।
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा दुःखिता निर्विकारतः ॥ ८॥

English
O Lord Śiva, by devotion alone people have attained the power of miracles. They became indifferent to the pleasures they enjoy or the miseries they have to face. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा यदुपतिर्भक्तो दाशार्हः सिद्धिमागतः ।
कलावती च तत्पत्नी भक्त्यैव परमां प्रभो ॥ ९॥

English
O lord, it was by his devotion alone that the founder of the Yadu family, the devotee Dāśārha and his wife Kalāvatī attained great success. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा मित्रसहो राजा मदयन्ती च तत्प्रिया ।
भक्त्यैव तव देवेश कैवल्यं परमं ययौ ॥ १०॥

English
O lord of gods, the king Mitrasaha and his beloved queen Madayantī attained great salvation through devotion to you. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सौमिनी नाम तनया कैकेयाग्रभुवस्तथा ।
तव भक्त्या सुखं प्राप परं सद्योगिदुर्लभम् ॥ ११॥

English
The daughter of the elder brother of the king of Kekayas named Sauminī attained happiness inaccessible to even great Yogins, by his devotion to you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
विमर्षणो नृपवरः सप्तजन्मावधि प्रभो ।
भुक्त्वा भोगांश्च विविधांस्त्वद्भक्त्या प्राप सद्गतिम् ॥ १२॥

English
O lord, by devotion to you the excellent king Vimarṣaṇa enjoyed worldly pleasures for seven births in various ways and ultimately attained the goal of the good. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रसेनो नृपवरः त्वद्भक्त्या सर्वभोगभुक् ।
दुःखमुक्तः सुखं प्राप परमत्र परत्र च ॥ १३॥

English
The excellent king Candrasena enjoyed all pleasures, became free from misery and experienced great happiness here and hereafter by devotion to you. (13.)

---

English
Śrikara, the son of a cowherdess and the disciple of Mahāvīra enjoyed the goal of the good here and great happiness hereafter by his devotion to you. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
गोपीपुत्रः श्रीकरस्ते भक्त्या भुक्त्वेह सद्गतिम् ।
परं सुखं महावीरशिष्यः प्राप परत्र वै । १४॥

त्वं सत्यरथभूजानेर्दुःखहर्ता गतिप्रदः ।
धर्मगुप्तं राजपुत्रमतार्षीःसुखिनं त्विह ॥ १५॥

English
You removed the misery of the king Satyaratha and you conferred good goal on him. You enabled the prince Dharmagupta to cross the ocean of worldly existence and made him happy here. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा शुचिव्रतं विप्रमदरिद्रं महाप्रभो ।
त्वद्भक्तिवर्तिनं मात्रा ज्ञानिनं कृपयाऽकरोः ॥ १६॥

English
O great lord, mercifully you made the brahmin Śucivrata strictly adhering to devotion to you gain knowledge along with his mother and made him rich too. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रवर्मा नृपवरस्त्वद्भक्त्या प्राप सद्गतिम् ।
इह लोके सदा भुक्त्वा भोगानमरदुर्लभान् ॥ १७॥

English
By his devotion to you the excellent king Citravarman perpetually enjoyed in this world the pleasures inaccessible even to the gods and attained salvation, the goal of the good. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्राङ्गदो राजपुत्रः सीमन्तिन्या स्त्रिया सह ।
विहाय सकलं दुःखं सुखी प्राप महागतिम् ॥ १८॥

English
The prince Candrāṅgada along with his wife Simantini got rid of all miseries, enjoyed happiness and attained great goal. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजो मन्दरनामापि वेश्यागामी खलोऽधमः ।
त्वद्भक्तः शिव सम्पूज्य तया सह गतिं गतः ॥ १९॥

English
The brahmin named Mandara who became a base knave indulging in lecherous association with prostitutes, O Śiva, worshipped one of your women devotees and attained salvation along with her. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
भद्रायुस्ते नृपसुतः सुखमाप गतव्यथः ।
त्वद्भक्तिकृपया मात्रा गतिं च परमां प्रभो ॥ २०॥

English
O lord, thanks to the favour of a devotee of yours, the prince Bhadrāyu attained happiness free from pain and achieved great goal along with his mother. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्त्रीभोगनिरतो दुर्जनस्तव सेवया ।
विमुक्तोऽभूदपि सदाऽभक्ष्यभोजी महेश्वर ॥ २१॥

English
O lord Śiva, even wicked sinners eating forbidden foodstuffs and indulging in sexual dalliance with all sorts of women, have been liberated by their service to you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्बरः शङ्करे भक्तश्चिताभस्मधरः सदा ।
नियमाद्भस्मनः शम्भो स्वस्त्रिया ते पुरं गतः ॥ २२॥

English
O Śiva, Śambara a devotee of yours, smearing himself with the ashes of the funeral pyre, attained your region along with his wife, thanks to his regular adherence to Bhasma. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
भद्रसेनस्य तनयस्तथा मन्त्रिसुतः प्रभो ।
सुधर्मशुभकर्माणो सदा रुद्राक्षधारिणौ ॥ २३॥

त्वत्कृपातश्च तौ मुक्तावास्तां भुक्त्वेह सत्सुखम् ।
पूर्वजन्मनि यौ कीशकुक्कुटौ रुद्रभूषणौ ॥ २४॥

पिङ्गला च महानन्दा वेश्ये द्वे तव भक्तितः ।
सद्गतिं प्रापतुर्नाथ भक्तोद्धारपरायण ॥ २५॥

English
O lord, the son of Bhadrasena and the son of his minister both of virtuous and auspicious rites and regular wearers of Rudrākṣa beads, enjoyed good pleasures here and became liberated, thanks to your grace. The two devotees who had been monkey and a cock in a previous birth became the ornaments of Rudra. O lord, always engaged in uplifting the devotees, the two courtesans Piṅgalā and Mahānandā attained the goal of the good, thanks to their devotion to you. (23-25.)

---

Sanskrit
शारदा विप्रतनया बालवैधव्यमागता ।
तव भक्तेः प्रभावात्तु पुत्रसौभाग्यवत्यभूत् ॥ २६॥

English
The brahmin girl Śāradā who had become a widow in childhood, was fortunate to regain her lost husband and was blessed with sons, thanks to the power of devotion to you. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
बिन्दुगो द्विजमात्रो हि वेश्याभोगी च तत्प्रिया ।
वञ्चुका त्वद्यशः श्रुत्वा परमां गतिमाययौ ॥ २७॥

English
Binduga, a brahmin only in name, a harlot monger and his wife Cañculā$^{238}$ attained great salvation on hearing your glory. (27.)

[fn 238. For the narratives of Binduga and Cañculā see ŚP chs 3-5 'the glory of Śivapurāṇa'. Vañcukā in the printed text is a misprint for Cañculā.]
---

Sanskrit
इत्यादि बहवः सिद्धिं गता जीवास्तव प्रभो ।
भक्तिभावान्महेशान दीनबन्धो कृपालय ॥ २८॥

English
O lord Śiva, friend of the distressed, storehouse of mercy, many living beings have attained the goal in this way. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं परः प्रकृतेर्ब्रह्म पुरुषात्परमेश्वर ।
निर्गुणस्त्रिगुणाधारो ब्रह्मविष्णुहरात्मकः ॥ २९॥

English
O lord Śiva, you are greater than Prakṛti and Puruṣa. You are the Brahman. You are devoid of attributes as well as the support of attributes in the forms of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाकर्मकरो नित्यं निर्विकारोऽखिलेश्वरः ।
वयं ब्रह्मादयः सर्वे तव दासा महेश्वर ॥ ३०॥

English
You are free from aberrations, O lord of all, you perform different activities incessantly. O lord Śiva, we all, Brahmā and others are your slaves. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नो भव देवेश रक्षास्मान्सर्वदा शिव ।
त्वत्प्रजाश्च वयं नाथ सदा त्वच्छरणं गताः ॥ ३१॥

English
O lord of gods, be pleased. O Śiva, protect us ever. O lord, we are your subjects and we ever seek refuge in you.” (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा च ते देवा ब्रह्माद्याः समुनीश्वराः ।
तूष्णीं बभूवुर्हि तदा शिवाङ्घ्रिद्वन्द्वचेतसः ॥ ३२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After eulogising Brahmā, other gods and the great sages, the gods remained silent with their minds fixed on Śiva’s feet. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शम्भुर्महेशानः श्रुत्वा देवस्तुतिं शुभाम् ।
दत्त्वा वरान् वरान् सद्यः तत्रैवान्तर्दधे प्रभुः ॥ ३३॥

English
The great lord Śiva heard the auspicious prayer of the gods, conferred boons on them and then vanished immediately from the scene. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाःसर्वेऽपि मुदिता ब्रह्माद्या हतशत्रवः ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुः प्रीता गायन्तः शिवसद्यशः ॥ ३४॥

English
Brahmā and other gods were jubilant as the enemies had been killed. Delightfully singing the great glory of Śiva, they left for their own abodes. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं परममाख्यानं जलन्धरविमर्दनम् ।
महेशचरितं पुण्यं महाघौघविनाशनम् ॥ ३५॥

English
This great narrative describing the suppression of Jalandhara is a sanctifying story of lord Śiva that destroys all sins. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
देवस्तुतिरियं पुण्या सर्वपापप्रणाशिनी ।
सर्वसौख्यप्रदा नित्यं महेशानन्ददायिनी ॥ ३६॥

English
This prayer of the gods is holy and destructive of sins. It bestows happiness on the devotees and is delightful to Śiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पठेत्पाठयेद्वापि समाख्यानमिदं द्वयम् ।
स भुक्त्वेह परं सौख्यं गाणपत्यमवाप्नुयात् ॥ ३७॥

English
He who reads or teaches the two narratives, enjoys great happiness here and becomes the lord of Gaṇas hereafter. (37.)
