### **CHAPTER TWENTYSEVEN - *(The birth of Śaṅkhacūḍa)***

---

Sanskrit
२७

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथान्यच्छम्भुचरितं प्रेमतः श‍ृणु वै मुने ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण शिवभक्तिर्दृढा भवेत् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O sage, now listen to another story of Śiva lovingly, by listening to which the devotion to Śiva is stabilised. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडाभिधो वीरो दानवो देवकण्टकः ।
यथा शिवेन निहतो रणमूर्ध्नि त्रिशूलतः ॥ २॥

English
The story narrates how the heroic Dānava Śaṅkhacūḍa who harassed the gods was killed by Śiva in the battle by means of his trident. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छम्भुचरितं दिव्यं पवित्रं पापनाशनम् ।
श‍ृणु व्यास सुसम्प्रीत्या वच्मि सुस्नेहतस्तव ॥ ३॥

English
O Vyāsa, listen lovingly to the story of Śiva, divine, holy and destructive of sins. I shall narrate the same because of my affection to you. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मरीचेस्तनयो धातुः पुत्रो यः कश्यपो मुनिः ।
स धर्मिष्ठःसृष्टिकर्त्ता विद्याज्ञप्तः प्रजापतिः ॥ ४॥

English
The sage Kaśyapa son of Marīci and grandson of Brahmā was a virtuous Prajāpati engaged in creation. He possessed great learning. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षः प्रीत्या ददौ तस्मै निजकन्यास्त्रयोदश ।
तासां प्रसूतिः प्रसभं न कथ्या बहुविस्तृताः ॥ ५॥

English
Dakṣa gave him his thirteen daughters in marriage. The descendants of these women are many and they cannot be enumerated easily. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र देवादिनिखिलं चराचरमभूज्जगत् ।
विस्तरात्तत्प्रवक्तुं च कः क्षमोऽस्ति त्रिलोकके ॥ ६॥

English
The whole universe consisting of gods and others the mobile and immobile is born of them. Who in the three worlds can mention this in detail? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रस्तुतं श‍ृणु वृत्तान्तं शम्भुलीलान्वितं च यत् ।
तदेव कथयाम्यद्य श‍ृणु भक्तिप्रवर्द्धनम् ॥ ७॥

English
Listen to what is relevant to the context wherein the divine sports of Śiva too can be seen. It is conducive to the increase of devotion. I am narrating the same. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तासु कश्यपपत्नीषु दनुस्त्वेका वराङ्गना ।
महारूपवती साध्वी पतिसौभाग्यवर्द्धिता ॥ ८॥

English
Among the wives of Kaśyapa the excellent lady Danu was one. She was very beautiful, chaste and tenderly nurtured by her husband with all devotion and love. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
आसंस्तस्या दनोः पुत्रा बहवो बलवत्तराः ।
तेषां नामानि नोच्यन्ते विस्तारभयतो मुने ॥ ९॥

English
Many powerful sons were born to that lady Danu. Their names are not mentioned O sage, by the fear of dilation. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तेष्वेको विप्रचित्तिस्तु महाबलपराक्रमः ।
तत्पुत्रो धार्मिको दम्भो विष्णुभक्तो जितेन्द्रियः ॥ १०॥

English
One of them is Vipracitti who was very powerful and valorous. His virtuous son Dambha of self-control was a great devotee of Viṣṇu. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नासीत्तत्तनयो वीरस्ततश्चिन्तापरोऽभवत् ।
शुक्राचार्यं गुरुं कृत्वा कृष्णमन्त्रमवाप्य च ॥ ११॥

तपश्चकार परमं पुष्करे लक्षवर्षकम् ।
कृष्णमन्त्रं जजापैव दृढं बद्ध्वासनं चिरम् ॥ १२॥

English
No son was born to him. Hence the hero became worried. He made the preceptor Śukra his initiator and learnt the mantra of Kṛṣṇa. He performed a great penance in the holy centre Puṣkara$^{241}$ for a hundred thousand years. Seating himself in a stable pose he performed the Japa of Kṛṣṇa mantra for a long time. (11-12.)

[fn 241. Puṣkara is a sacred forest near Ajmer in Rajasthan. There is a celebrated lake where Brahmā set up a phallic image of Śiva. It is a famous place of pilgrimage in Rājaputānā.]
---

Sanskrit
तपः प्रकुर्वतस्तस्य मूर्ध्नो निःसृत्य प्रज्वलत् ।
विससार च सर्वत्र तत्तेजो हि सुदुःसहम् ॥ १३॥

English
While he was performing the penance, an unbearable refulgence sprang up blazing from his head and spread everywhere. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन तप्ताः सुराः सर्वे मुनयो मनवस्तथा ।
सुनासीरं पुरस्कृत्य ब्रह्माणं शरणं ययुः ॥ १४॥

English
All the gods, sages and Manus were scorched by that. With Indra ahead they sought refuge in Brahmā. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य च विधातारं दातारं सर्वसम्पदाम् ।
तुष्टुवुर्विकलाः प्रोचुः स्ववृत्तान्तं विशेषतः ॥ १५॥

English
Bowing to Brahmā, the bestower of riches, they eulogised him and narrated to him this event. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य विधातापि वैकुण्ठं तैर्ययौ सह ।
तदेव विज्ञापयितुं निखिलेन हि विष्णवे ॥ १६॥

English
On hearing that, Brahmā accompanied them to Vaikuṇṭha in order to tell the same to Viṣṇu in its entirety. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा त्रिलोकेशं विष्णुं रक्षाकरं परम् ।
प्रणम्य तुष्टुवुःसर्वे करौ बद्ध्वा विनम्रकाः ॥ १७॥

English
After going there they stood humbly joining their palms in reverence. After bowing to him they eulogised Viṣṇu the lord of the three worlds, the great saviour. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव न जानीमो जातं किं कारणं त्विह ।
सन्तप्ताः सकला जातास्तेजसा केन तद्वद ॥ १८॥

English
*The gods said:—* “O lord of gods we do not know how this happened to cause this. Please tell us. By what refulgence have all of us been scorched ? (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तप्तात्मनां त्वमविता दीनबन्धोऽनुजीविनाम् ।
रक्ष रक्ष रमानाथ शरण्यः शरणागतान् ॥ १९॥

English
O friend of the distressed, you are the protector of the distressed and dispirited servants. Save, O lord of Lakṣmī who are worthy of being sought refuge by us. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचो विष्णुर्ब्रह्मादीनां दिवौकसाम् ।
उवाच विहसन्प्रेम्णा शरणागतवत्सलः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Brahmā and other gods, Viṣṇu who is favourably disposed to those who seek refuge, said laughingly and lovingly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
सुस्वस्था भवताव्यग्रा न भयं कुरुतामराः ।
नोपप्लवा भविष्यन्ते लयकालो न विद्यते ॥ २१॥

English
*Viṣṇu said:—* “O gods, be calm and unperturbed, do not be afraid. No deluge will take place, this is not the time of dissolution. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दानवो दम्भनामा हि मद्भक्तः कुरुते तपः ।
पुत्रार्थी शमयिष्यामि तमहं वरदानतः ॥ २२॥

English
The Asura Dambha a devotee of mine is performing a penance seeking for a son. I shall bestow a boon and quieten him.” (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्तास्ते सुराःसर्वे धैर्यमालम्ब्य वै मुने ।
ययुर्ब्रह्मादयः सुस्थाः स्वस्वधामानि सर्वशः ॥ २३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O sage, on being consoled thus, Brahmā and other gods became encouraged and they returned to their respective abodes. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अच्युतोऽपि वरं दातुं पुष्करं सञ्जगाम ह ।
तपश्चरति यत्रासौ दम्भनामा हि दानवः ॥ २४॥

English
In order to grant the boon, Viṣṇu went to Puṣkara where Dambha was performing penance. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा वरं ब्रूहीत्युवाच परिसान्त्वयन् ।
गिरा सूनृतया भक्तं जपन्तं स्वमनुं हरिः ॥ २५॥

English
On reaching there Viṣṇu consoled Dambha who was repeating his name and told him the pleasing words—“Mention the boon you wish to be granted.” (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं विष्णोर्दृष्ट्वा तं च पुरः स्थितम् ।
प्रणनाम महाभक्त्या तुष्टाव च पुनः पुनः ॥ २६॥

English
On hearing his words and seeing Viṣṇu standing in front, the Dānava bowed with great devotion and eulogised him again and again. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
दम्भ उवाच ।
देवदेव नमस्तेऽस्तु पुण्डरीकविलोचन ।
रमानाथ त्रिलोकेश कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ २७॥

English
*Dambha said:—*  “O lord of gods, Obeisance be to you, O Lotus-eyed one, O lord of Lakṣmī, O lord of the three worlds, please take pity on me. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभक्तं तनयं देहि महाबल पराक्रमम् ।
त्रिलोकजयिनं वीरमजेयं च दिवौकसाम् ॥ २८॥

English
Please give me a powerful and valorous son who will be your devotee, who will be invincible to the gods and who will conquer the three worlds.” (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो दानवेन्द्रेण तं वरं प्रददौ हरिः ।
निवर्त्य चोग्रतपसः ततः सोऽन्तरधान्मुने ॥ २९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On being thus requested by the lord of Dānavas, Viṣṇu granted him the boon. O sage, making him desist from the penance he vanished from the place. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
गते हरौ दानवेन्द्रः कृत्वा तस्यै दिशे नमः ।
जगाम स्वगृहं सिद्धतपाः पूर्णमनोरथः ॥ ३०॥

English
When Viṣṇu went away, the lord of Dānavas performed obeisance to that direction and returned home, his penance having been fulfilled and his desires realised. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कालेनाल्पेन तत्पत्नी सगर्भा भाग्यवत्यभूत् ।
रराज तेजसात्यन्तं रोचयन्ती गृहान्तरम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Within a short time, his fortunate wife became pregnant. Illuminating the inner apartments of her abode by her brilliance she shone much. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदामा नाम गोपो यो कृष्णस्य पार्षदाग्रणीः ।
तस्या गर्भे विवेशासौ राधाशप्तश्च यन्मुने ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, it was Sudāmā a cowherd, one of the leading comrades of Kṛṣṇa who had been cursed by Rādhā, that entered her womb. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
असूत समये साध्वी सुप्रभं तनयं ततः ।
जातकं सुचकारासौ पिताऽऽहूय मुनीन्बहून् ॥ ३३॥

English
At the proper time the chaste lady gave birth to a brilliant son. The father invited sages and performed the post-natal rites. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्सवः सुमहानासीत्तस्मिञ्जाते द्विजोत्तम ।
नाम चक्रे पिता तस्य शङ्खचूडेति सद्दिने ॥ ३४॥

English
O excellent brahmin, when the boy was born there was great jubilation. On an auspicious day the father named him “Śaṅkhacūda.” (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुर्गेहे स ववृधे शुक्लपक्षे यथा शशी ।
शैशवेऽभ्यस्तविद्यस्तु स बभूव सुदीप्तिमान् ॥ ३५॥

English
In the abode of his father he grew up like the moon in the bright half. Learning all lores in childhood he became resplendent. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स बालक्रीडया नित्यं पित्रोर्हर्षं ततान ह ।
प्रियो बभूव सर्वेषां कुलजानां विशेषतः ॥ ३६॥

English
With his childish sports he increased the parents' delight. He became a special favourite of all the members of the family. (36.)
