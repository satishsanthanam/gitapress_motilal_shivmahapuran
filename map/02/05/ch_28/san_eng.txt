### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - *(The penance and marriage of Śaṅkhacūḍa)***

---

Sanskrit
२८

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
ततश्च शङ्खचूडोऽसौ जैगीषव्योपदेशतः ।
ततश्चकार सुप्रीत्या ब्रह्मणः पुष्करे चिरम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* As instructed by Jaigīśavya, Śaṅkhacūḍa performed a penance in Puṣkara for a long time in order to propitiate Brahmā with devotion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुदत्तां ब्रह्मविद्यां जजाप नियतेन्द्रियः ।
स एकाग्रमना भूत्वा करणानि निगृह्य च ॥ २॥

English
He concentrated his mind, controlled the senses and organs of activities, and muttered the mantra of Brahmā imparted by his preceptor. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तपन्तं पुष्करे तं वै शङ्खचूडं च दानवम् ।
वरं दातुं जगामाशु ब्रह्मा लोकगुरुर्विभुः ॥ ३॥

English
Lord Brahmā, the preceptor of the worlds, went to Śaṅkhacūḍa who was practising penance at Puṣkara in order to grant him the boon soon. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं ब्रूहीति प्रोवाच दानवेन्द्रं विधिस्तदा ।
स दृष्ट्वा तं ननामातिनम्रस्तुष्टाव सद्गिरा ॥ ४॥

English
Brahmā said to him : "Tell me the boon you wish to choose." On seeing Brahmā, the king of Dānavas bowed to him humbly and eulogised him with words of devotion. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं ययाचे ब्रह्माणमजेयत्वं दिवौकसाम् ।
तथेत्याह विधिस्तं वै सुप्रसन्नेन चेतसा ॥ ५॥

English
He requested Brahmā to grant him the power of being invincible to the gods. With a delighted mind, Brahmā said "Be it so." (5.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्णकवचं दिव्यं जगन्मङ्गलमङ्गलम् ।
दत्तवान् शङ्खचूडाय सर्वत्र विजयप्रदम् ॥ ६॥

English
He gave Śaṅkhacūḍa the divine amulet of Śrīkṛṣṇa the most auspicious of all auspicious things in the universe, that yielded victory everywhere. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
बदरीं सम्प्रयाहि त्वं तुलस्या सह तत्र वै ।
विवाहं कुरु तत्रैव सा तपस्यति कामतः ॥ ७॥

English
"You now go to Badari. There you marry Tulasi who is performing penance just at her own will. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मध्वजसुता सेति सन्दिदेश च तं विधिः ।
अन्तर्धानं जगामाशु पश्यतस्तस्य तत्क्षणात् ॥ ८॥

English
She is the daughter of Dharmadhvaja." Brahmā instructed him thus and vanished even as he was watching him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःस शङ्खचूडो हि तपःसिद्धोऽतिपुष्करे ।
गले बबन्ध कवचं जगन्मङ्गलमङ्गलम् ॥ ९॥

English
Then Śaṅkhacūḍa whose penance had been fruitful in the holy centre of Puṣkara tied the most auspicious amulet round his neck. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
आज्ञया ब्रह्मणः सोऽपि तपःसिद्धमनोरथः ।
समाययौ प्रहृष्टास्यस्तूर्णं बदरिकाश्रमम् ॥ १०॥

English
At the behest of Brahmā, the Dānava whose desire had been achieved through penance went to Badarikāśrama$^{242}$ with delight beaming in his face. (10.)

[fn 242. Badarikāśrama, known as Badari Nārāyaṇa or Badarinātha is situated on a peak of the Himalayas in Garhwal. It has a temple of Viṣṇu in his dual form of Nara-Nārāyaṇa. According to Varāha Purāṇa (140, 4-5) it is one of the three abodes of Viṣṇu on the Himalayas, the other two being Kokāmukha and Lohārgala. For details see Sircar GAMI. P. 219.]

---

Sanskrit
यदृच्छयाऽऽगतस्तत्र शङ्खचूडश्च दानवः ।
तपश्चरन्ती तुलसी यत्र धर्मध्वजात्मजा ॥ ११॥

English
The Dānava Śaṅkhacūḍa casually visited the place where the daughter of Dharmadhvaja, Tulasi was performing the penance. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरूपा सुस्मिता तन्वी शुभभूषणभूषिता ।
सकटाक्षं ददर्शासौ तमेव पुरुषं परम् ॥ १२॥

English
The smiling beautiful gentle woman fully bedecked in ornaments cast loving glances at the great man. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तां ललितां रम्यां सुशीलां सुन्दरीं सतीम् ।
उवास तत्समीपे तु मधुरं तामुवाच सः ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing that charming, tender, beautiful and chaste lady, he stopped near her and spoke to her sweetly. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूड उवाच ।
का त्वं कस्य सुता त्वं हि किं करोषि स्थितात्र किम् ।
मौनीभूता किङ्करं मां सम्भावितुमिहार्हसि ॥ १४॥

English
*Śaṅkhacūḍa said:—*  “Who are you, please? Whose daughter? What are you doing? Why do you stay here and observe silence. Consider me as your devoted slave.” (14.)

---

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words she spoke to him lovingly. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा सकामं तमुवाच सा । १५॥

तुलस्युवाच ।
धर्मध्वजसुताहं च तपस्यामि तपस्विनी ।
तपोवने च तिष्ठामि कस्त्वं गच्छ यथासुखम् ॥ १६॥

English
*Tulasi said:—*  I am the daughter of Dharmadhvaja. I am performing penance. I stay in this hermitage. Who are you? You can go as you please. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नारीजातिर्मोहिनी च ब्रह्मादीनां विषोपमा ।
निन्द्या दोषकरी माया श‍ृङ्खला ह्यनुशायिनाम् ॥ १७॥

English
The entire class of women is fascinating. It enchants even Brahmā, not to speak of others. It is censurable, poisonous and deceptive. It is illusion and a fetter to the devout and the faithful. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तुलसी तं च सरसं विरराम ह ।
दृष्ट्वा तां सस्मितां सोऽपि प्रवक्तुमुपचक्रमे ॥ १८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Tulasi thus spoke to the passionate Dambha and stopped. On seeing her smiling he began to say. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूड उवाच ।
त्वया यत्कथितं देवि न च सर्वमलीककम् ।
किञ्चित्सत्यमलीकं च किञ्चिन्मत्तो निशामय ॥ १९॥

English
*Saṅkhacūḍa said:—*  O gentle lady, what you said now is not entirely false. It is partially true also. Now listen to me. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिव्रताः स्त्रियो याश्च तासां मध्ये त्वमग्रणीः ।
न चाहं पापदृक्कामी तथा त्वं नेति धीर्मम ॥ २०॥

English
You are the foremost among chaste ladies. I am not a lusty person of sinful nature. I think you too are not like that. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
आगच्छामि त्वत्समीपमाज्ञया ब्रह्मणोऽधुना ।
गान्धर्वेण विवाहेन त्वां ग्रहीष्यामि शोभने ॥ २१॥

English
I come to you now at the behest of Brahmā. O gently lady, I shall take your hand by the Gāndharva rites of marriage. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडोऽहमेवास्मि देवविद्रावकारकः ।
मां न जानासि किं भद्रे न श्रुतोऽहं कदाचन ॥ २२॥

English
I am Śaṅkhacūḍa, the router of the gods. O gentle lady, don't you know me? Have I never been heard by you? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दनुवंश्यो विशेषेण दम्भपुत्रश्च दानवः ।
सुदामा नाम गोपोऽहं पार्षदश्च हरेः पुरा ॥ २३॥

English
I am a scion of the family of Danu. I am a Dānava, the son of Dambha. In the previous birth I was the cowherd Sudāmā, a comrade of Kṛṣṇa. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अधुना दानवेन्द्रोऽहं राधिकायाश्च शापतः ।
जातिस्मरोऽहं जानामि सर्वं कृष्णप्रभावतः ॥ २४॥

English
Due to the curse of Rādhā I have become a Dānava now. By the favour of Kṛṣṇa I remember events of previous birth. I know everything. (24.)

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा शङ्खचूडो विरराम च तत्पुरः ।
दानवेन्द्रेण सेत्युक्ता वचनं सत्यमादरात् ।
सस्मितं तुलसी तुष्टा प्रवक्तुमुपचक्रमे ॥ २५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying thus to her, Śaṅkhacūḍa stopped. Tulasi who was thus addressed truthfully and respectfully by the king of Dānavas, was delighted and she spoke smilingly. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तुलस्युवाच ।
त्वयाहमधुना सत्त्वविचारेण पराजिता ।

English
*Tulasi said:—*  I have now been overpowered by you who have Sāttvika thoughts. That man is blessed in the world who is not overwhelmed by a woman. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्क्रियोऽप्यशुचिर्नित्यं स पुमान्यः स्त्रिया जितः ।

English
Even though he may be the observer of sacred rites, if he is overpowered by a woman he becomes impure and unclean, so he remains for ever. The manes, gods and human beings censure him. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शुध्येद्विप्रो दशाहेन जातके मृतसूतके ।
क्षत्रियो द्वादशाहेन वैश्यः पञ्चदशाहतः ॥ २८॥

शूद्रो मासेन शुध्येत्तु हीति वेदानुशासनम् ।

English
A brahmin is purified from impurity arising from births or deaths in the family, after the tenth day. A Kṣatriya in twelve days, a Vaiśya in fifteen days and a Śūdra in a month. This is what the Vedas enjoin. But a henpecked man can never be purified till death. (28-29.)

Sanskrit

English
The manes do not receive willingly the balls of rice or holy waters offered by him. Nor do the gods accept his offering of fruits and flowers. (30.)

English
Of what avail are words of wisdom, penance, Japas, Homas, worships, learning or charitable gifts to that wretch whose mind is deadened by his thoughts of women? (31.)

Sanskrit

English
You have been tested by me in order to know your knowledge and power. A woman must test her bridegroom before wooing him. (32.)

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Even as Tulasi was saying so, Brahmā the creator came there and spoke these words. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
किं करोषि शङ्खचूड संवादमनया सह ।
गान्धर्वेण विवाहेन त्वमस्या ग्रहणं कुरु ॥ ३४॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* “O Śaṅkhacūḍa, why do you hold discussion with her? Marry her according to the Gāndharva²⁴³ form of marriage. (34.)

[fn 243. According to Manu (iii. 32) in the Gāndharva form of marriage, the bride and the bridegroom met each other of their own accord and their meeting consummated in copulation born of passion. It was called Gāndharva because it prevailed in a tribe called Gandharva which lived on the slopes of the Himalayas. It was considered the most natural form because the bride and the bridegroom attracted each other without any force or fraud. It became obsolete because it was performed without sacred rituals and originated from lust. See H.S. PP. 162-164.]
