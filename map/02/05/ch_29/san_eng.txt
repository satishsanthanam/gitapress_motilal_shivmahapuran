### **CHAPTER TWENTYNINE - *(The previous birth of Śaṅkhacūḍa)***

---

Sanskrit
२९

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
स्वगेहमागते तस्मिन् शङ्खचूडे विवाहिते ।
तपः कृत्वा वरं प्राप्य मुमुदुर्दानवादयः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* When Śaṅkhacūḍa returned home duly married, after performing the penance and receiving the boons, Dānavas and others rejoiced. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वलोकादाशु निर्गत्य गुरुणा स्वेन संयुताः ।
सर्वेऽसुराः सम्मिलिताः समाजग्मुस्तदन्तिकम् ॥ २॥

English
Leaving their world and accompanied by their preceptor, the Asuras assembled and approached the Dānava. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य तं सविनयं संस्तुत्य विविधादरात् ।
स्थितास्तत्रैव सुप्रीत्या मत्वा तेजस्विनं विभुम् ॥ ३॥

English
They bowed to that resplendent Dānava their lord, humbly and eulogised him with love and respect. They stayed with him alone. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि दम्भात्मजो दृष्ट्वाऽऽगतं कुलगुरुं च तम् ।
प्रणनाम महाभक्त्या साष्टाङ्गं परमादरात् ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing the family preceptor, Śaṅkhacūḍa, son of Dambha bowed to him with devotion and prostrated before him with respect. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शुक्रः कुलाचार्यो दृष्ट्वाशिषमनुत्तमम् ।
वृत्तान्तं कथयामास देवदानवयोस्तदा ॥ ५॥

English
After conferring his excellent benediction, Śukra, the family preceptor, narrated the tales of the gods and Dānavas. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा समुत्सवो जातोऽसुराणां मुदितात्मनाम् ।
उपायनानि सुप्रीत्या ददुस्तस्मै च तेऽखिलाः ॥ ६॥

English
He expatiated on the natural enmity of the two, the invariable defeat of the Asuras, the victory of the gods and the help rendered by Bṛhaspati. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स सम्मतं कृत्वाऽसुरैः सर्वैः समुत्सवम् ।
दानवाद्यसुराणां तमधिपं विदधे गुरुः ॥ ७॥

English
With the consent of the Asuras, the preceptor Śukra made him the emperor of Dānavas, Asuras and others with jubilant festivities. (7.)

---

English
The delighted Asuras were highly joyous. They offered him presents lovingly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दम्भात्मजो वीरः शङ्खचूडः प्रतापवान् ।
राज्याभिषेकमासाद्य स रेजेऽसुरराट् तदा ॥ ९॥

English
The son of Dambha, the heroic and valorous Śaṅkhacūḍa shone as the Emperor of Asuras. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स सेनां महतीं कर्षन्दैत्यदानवरक्षसाम् ।
रथमास्थाय तरसा जेतुं शक्रपुरीं ययौ ॥ १०॥

English
Taking a vast army of Daityas, Dānavas and Rākṣasas and seated in his chariot, he marched quickly to the city of Indra$^{244}$ with the intention to conquer it. (10.)

[fn 244. The capital city of Indra's dominions, also called Amarāvatī, is situated in the vicinity of Mount Meru. It is also called Devapura, city of the gods.]
---

Sanskrit
गच्छन्स दानवेन्द्रस्तु तेषां सेवनकुर्वताम् ।
विरेजे शशिवद्भानां ग्रहाणां ग्रहराडिव ॥ ११॥

English
The leader of the Dānavas going in the midst of his attendants shone as the moon in the midst of stars or as the sun in the midst of planets. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
आगच्छन्तं शङ्खचूडमाकर्ण्याखण्डलस्स्वराट् ।
निखिलैरमरैः सार्धं तेन योद्धुं समुद्यतः ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing that Śaṅkhacūḍa was coming, Indra the king of heaven, accompanied by the gods made preparations for a fight. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाऽसुरैःसुराणां च सङ्ग्रामस्तुमुलो ह्यभूत् ।
वीराऽऽनन्दकरः क्लीबभयदो रोमहर्षणः ॥ १३॥

English
Then a tremendous fight ensued between the Asuras and the gods delighting the heroic and terrifying the cowardly. It caused hairs to stand on end. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
महान्कोलाहलो जातो वीराणां गर्जतां रणे ।
वाद्यध्वनिस्तथा चाऽऽसीत्तत्र वीरत्ववर्द्धिनी ॥ १४॥

English
When the warriors roared in the battle, there was a tumultuous noise. The sound of drums and other instruments encouraged the warriors. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाः प्रकुप्य युयुधुरसुरैर्बलवत्तराः ।
पराजयं च सम्प्रापुरसुरा दुद्रुवुर्भयात् ॥ १५॥

English
The powerful gods fought with the Asuras ferociously and defeated them. They were afraid and fled. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पलायमानास्तान्दृष्ट्वा शङ्खचूडः स्वयं प्रभुः ।
युयुधे निर्जरैःसाकं सिंहनादं प्रगर्ज्य च ॥ १६॥

English
On seeing them fleeing, their leader Śaṅkhacūḍa roared like a lion and fought with the gods. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तरसा सहसा चक्रे कदनं त्रिदिवौकसाम् ।
प्रदुद्रुवुः सुराः सर्वे तत्सुतेजो न सेहिरे ॥ १७॥

English
With his power and force he distressed the gods. The gods could not endure his dazzling brilliance. They fled. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र तत्र स्थिता दीना गिरीणां कन्दरासु च ।
तदधीना न स्वतन्त्रा निष्प्रभाः सागरा यथा ॥ १८॥

English
The gods thus vanquished took shelter in the caves of the mountains. They lost their independence. They were subjugated. They lost their lustre like the frozen sea. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि दम्भात्मजः शूरो दानवेन्द्रः प्रतापवान् ।
सुराधिकारान्सञ्जह्रे सर्वाँल्लोकान्विजित्य च ॥ १९॥

English
Thus the son of Dambha, the valorous leader of the Dānavas, conquered all the worlds and took up the powers of the gods. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रैलोक्यं स्ववशं चक्रे यज्ञभागांश्च कृत्स्नशः ।
स्वयमिन्द्रो बभूवापि शासितं निखिलं जगत् ॥ २०॥

English
He kept the three worlds under his control. He partook of all the shares in sacrifices. He became Indra and ruled the universe. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कौबेरमैन्दवं सौर्यमाग्नेयं याम्यमेव च ।
कारयामास वायव्यमधिकारं स्वशक्तितः ॥ २१॥

English
He carried the tasks of Kubera, Moon, Sun, Fire, Yama and Vāyu, according to his ability. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानामसुराणां च दानवानां च रक्षसाम् ।
गन्धर्वाणां च नागानां किन्नराणां रसौकसाम् ॥ २२॥

त्रिलोकस्य परेषां च सकलानामधीश्वरः ।
स बभूव महावीरः शङ्खचूडो महाबली ॥ २३॥

English
The great hero, the powerful Śaṅkhacūḍa became overlord of the gods, Asuras, Dānavas, Rākṣasas, Gandharvas, serpents, Kinnaras, Nāgas and in fact of all the people of the three worlds. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स बुभुजे राज्यं राजराजेश्वरो महान् ।
सर्वेषां भुवनानां च शङ्खचूडश्चिरं समाः ॥ २४॥

English
Thus Śaṅkhacūḍa enjoyed the kingdom of the worlds for many years. He became a great Emperor. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य राज्ये न दुर्भिक्षं न मारी नाऽशुभग्रहाः ।
आधयो व्याधयो नैव सुखिन्यश्च प्रजाः सदा ॥ २५॥

English
There was no famine, plague or pestilence in his realm. The planets were not inauspicious. There was no worry or sickness among the people. The subjects were happy for ever. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अकृष्टपच्या पृथिवी ददौ सस्यान्यनेकशः ।
ओषध्यो विविधाश्चासन्सफलाःसरसाः सदा ॥ २६॥

English
Even without being tilled, the earth yielded plenty of plants and vegetation. Many medicinal herbs grew up. Plants remained always fruitful and juicy. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्याकराश्च नितरां रत्नखन्यश्च सागराः ।
सदा पुष्पफला वृक्षा नद्यः सुसलिलावहाः ॥ २७॥

English
The oceans yielded plenty of gems and jewels. Abundant flowers and fruits grew up on the Earth. Rivers flowed with pure crystal clear water. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
देवान् विनाखिला जीवाः सुखिनो निर्विकारकाः ।
स्वस्वधर्मास्थिताः सर्वे चतुर्वर्णाश्रमाः परे ॥ २८॥

English
Excepting the gods all living beings were happy and free from distress. The people of four castes and stages of life maintained their respective duties and activities. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन् शासति त्रैलोक्ये न कश्चिद् दुःखितोऽभवत् ।
भ्रातृवैरत्वमाश्रित्य केवलं दुःखिनोऽमराः ॥ २९॥

English
When he ruled, none was miserable in the three worlds. Only the gods were reduced to misery and that too by their fratricidal jealousy and enmity. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

कृष्णभक्तिरतः साधुः सदा गोलोकवासिनः ॥ ३०॥

English
Śaṅkhacūḍa was a close friend of Kṛṣṇa, the resident of Goloka. He was powerful, and always engaged in devotion to Kṛṣṇa. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वशापप्रभावेण दानवीं योनिमाश्रितः ।
न दानवमतिः सोऽभूद्दानवत्वेऽपि वै मुने ॥ ३१॥

English
O sage, although he was a Dānava, his nature was different. He was born as a Dānava due to a previous curse. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O dear, thereafter, the defeated gods, deprived of their kingdom, consulted among themselves and went to Brahmā’s assembly chamber along with the sages. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
They saw the creator and bowed to and eulogised him. With distress they explained to him everything in detail. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
After consoling the gods and the sages, Brahmā accompanied by them went to Vaikuṇṭha that yields happiness to the good. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Accompanied by the gods, Brahmā saw the lord of Lakṣmī decorated with a crown, earrings and a garland of wild flowers. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing Viṣṇu bearing Śaṅkha, Cakra, mace and the lotus, the lord with four arms, yellow garments, accompanied by Nandana, Siddhas, Brahmā and other gods bowed to the lord along with the great sages. They eulogised him with palms joined in reverence. (36-37.)
