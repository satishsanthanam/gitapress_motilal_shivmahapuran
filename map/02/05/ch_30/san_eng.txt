### **CHAPTER THIRTY - *(Prayer to the lord of gods)***

---

Sanskrit
३०

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
गत्वा तदैव सविधिस्तदा व्यास रमेश्वरः ।
शिवलोकं महादिव्यं निराधारमभौतिकम् ॥ १॥

साह्लादोऽभ्यन्तरं विष्णुर्जगाम मुदिताननः ।
नानारत्नपरिक्षिप्तं विलसन्तं महोज्ज्वलम् ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O Vyāsa, starting then itself along with Brahmā, Viṣṇu, the lord of Lakṣmī, went to Śivaloka, highly divine, propelless and unearthly. He was glad and his face beamed with pleasure. The region was strewn over with many gems. It was highly brilliant. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्प्राप्य प्रथमं द्वारं विचित्रं गणसेवितम् ।
शोभितं परया लक्ष्म्या महोच्चमतिसुन्दरम् ॥ ३॥

ददर्श द्वारपालांश्च रत्नसिंहासनस्थितान् ।
शोभितान् श्वेतवस्त्रैश्च रत्नभूषणभूषितान् ॥ ४॥

English
The first entrance was of variegated nature with many Gaṇas standing there. It was resplendent, lofty and beautiful. After reaching it he saw the gatekeepers seated on gem-set thrones. They had gem-set ornaments and white garments. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चवक्त्रत्रिनयनान्गौरसुन्दरविग्रहान् ।
त्रिशूलादिधरान्वीरान् भस्मरुद्राक्षशोभितान् ॥ ५॥

English
They had five faces, three eyes and fair handsome bodies. They were trident-bearing heroes shining with Bhasma and Rudrākṣa. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स ब्रह्मापि रमेशश्च तान् प्रणम्य विनम्रकः ।
कथयामास वृत्तान्तं प्रभुसन्दर्शनार्थकम् ॥ ६॥

English
Both Brahmā and Viṣṇu bowed to them humbly and told them that they wanted to see the lord. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाज्ञां च ददुस्तस्मै प्रविवेश तदाज्ञया ।
परं द्वारं महारम्यं विचित्रं परमप्रभम् ॥ ७॥

English
They permitted them to enter. They saw another door very beautiful, variegated and very brilliant. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभूपकण्ठगत्यर्थं वृत्तान्तं संन्यवेदयत् ।
तद् द्वारपाय चाज्ञप्तस्तेनान्यं प्रविवेश ह ॥ ८॥

English
They informed the gatekeeper of their desire to approach the lord. Permitted by them they entered and saw another door. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पञ्चदशद्वारान्प्रविश्य कमलोद्भवः ।
महाद्वारं गतस्तत्र नन्दिनं प्रददर्श ह ॥ ९॥

English
Thus Brahmā entered through fifteen doors and reached the main threshold. He saw Nandin. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्यङ्नत्वा च तं स्तुत्वा पूर्ववत्तेन नन्दिना ।
आज्ञप्तश्च शनैर्विष्णुर्विवेशाभ्यन्तरं मुदा ॥ १०॥

English
After bowing to and eulogising Nandin as Brahmā did before, Viṣṇu was permitted by Nandin and he entered joyously. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ददर्श गत्वा तत्रोच्चैः सभां शम्भोः समुत्प्रभाम् ।
तां पार्षदैः परिवृतां लसद्देहैः सुभूषिताम् ॥ ११॥

English
Going in, they saw the grand assembly chamber of Śiva, highly decorated and thronged by his attendants with lustrous bodies. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
माहेश्वरस्य रूपैश्च दिग्भुजैः शुभकान्तिभिः ।
पञ्चवक्त्रैस्त्रिनयनैः शितिकण्ठैर्महोज्ज्वलैः ॥ १२॥

English
The attendants had similar forms with lord Śiva. They had ten arms, five faces, three eyes and blue necks. They had auspicious lustre and were brilliant. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्रत्नयुक्तरुद्राक्षभस्माभरणभूषितैः ।
नवेन्दुमण्डलाकारां चतुरस्रां मनोहराम् ॥ १३॥

English
They were bedecked in ornaments set with gems. They wore Rudrākṣas. They had smeared themselves with the ashes. The chamber was square in shape and beautiful like the lunar sphere. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
मणीन्द्रहारनिर्माणहीरसारसुशोभिताम् ।
अमूल्यरत्नरचितां पद्मपत्रैश्च शोभिताम् ॥ १४॥

English
Gems, necklaces, diamonds, etc. increased its brilliance. Valuable precious stones were used to stud them. It was brightened by lotus petals. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
माणिक्यजालमालाभिर्नानाचित्रविचित्रिताम् ।
पद्मरागेन्द्ररचितामद्भुतां शङ्करेच्छया ॥ १५॥

English
Māṇikya, Padmarāga and other valuable gems were used in the same. It was very wonderful. It was laid according to the desire of Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सोपानशतकैर्युक्तां स्यमन्तकविनिर्मितैः ।
स्वर्णसूत्रग्रन्थियुक्तैश्चारुचन्दनपल्लवैः ॥ १६॥

English
It had hundreds of steps leading to it, each made of Syamantaka stone; knotted golden threads joined them. Beautiful sprouts of sandal beautified it. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रनीलमणिस्तम्भैर्वेष्टितां सुमनोहराम् ।
सुसंस्कृतां च सर्वत्र वासितां गन्धवायुना ॥ १७॥

English
Columns of sapphire supported it. It was richly decorated. The wind wafted fragrance everywhere. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रयोजनायामां सुपूर्णां बहुकिङ्करैः ।
ददर्श शङ्करं साम्बं तत्र विष्णुः सुरेश्वरः ॥ १८॥

English
The chamber was a thousand Yojanas wide. Many servants were in attendance. Viṣṇu the lord of gods saw Śiva seated along with Pārvatī. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
वसन्तं मध्यदेशे च येथेन्दुतारकावृतम् ।
अमूल्यरत्ननिर्माणचित्रसिंहासनस्थितम् ॥ १९॥

English
Śiva was in the midst of his attendants like the moon surrounded by stars. He was seated in a variegated throne set with valuable gems. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
किरीटिनं कुण्डलिनं रत्नमालाविभूषितम् ।
भस्मोद्धूलितसर्वाङ्गं बिभ्रतं केलिपङ्कजम् ॥ २०॥

English
He had a crown on his head, earrings in his ears. He was embellished with gem necklaces. Ashes were smeared all over his body. He held a toy lotus. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरतो गीतनृत्यञ्च पश्यन्तं सस्मितं मुदा ॥ २१॥

English
He was smilingly watching the song and dance going on in front of him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शान्तं प्रसन्नमनसमुमाकान्तं महोल्लसम् ।
देव्या प्रदत्तताम्बूलं भुक्तवन्तं सुवासितम् ॥ २२॥

English
He was calm and delighted in the mind. He was highly brilliant. He was chewing the fragrant betel leaves offered by the goddess. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
गणैश्च परया भक्त्या सेवितं श्वेतचामरैः ।
स्तूयमानं च सिद्धैश्च भक्तिनम्रात्मकन्धरैः ॥ २३॥

English
He was attended upon by Gaṇas with white chowries and eulogised by Siddhas with stooping shoulders with great devotion. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
गुणातीतं परेशानं त्रिदेवजनकं विभुम् ।
निर्विकल्पं निराकारं साकारं स्वेच्छया शिवम् ॥ २४॥

अमायमजमाद्यञ्च मायाधीशं परात्परम् ।
प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्यापि परमं स्वप्रभुं सदा ॥ २५॥

English
The great lord Śiva, the progenitor of the three deities, the lord beyond the reach of attributes, who assumes and discords his forms as he pleases and is invariable, who is free from illusion, unborn, the primordial being, the lord of illusion, greater than the greatest and greater than the Prakṛti and Puruṣa. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विशिष्टं तं दृष्ट्वा परिपूर्णतमं समम् ।
विष्णुर्ब्रह्मा तुष्टुवतुः प्रणम्य सुकृताञ्जली ॥ २६॥

English
On seeing Śiva of perfect features, Viṣṇu and Brahmā eulogised him together after bowing to him with palms joined in reverence. (26.)

---

English
*Viṣṇu and Brahmā said:—*  O lord Śiva, lord of the gods, O supreme Brahman, lord of all. O quiet one that is beyond the three attributes, O lord progenitor of the three deities. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुविधी ऊचतुः
देवदेव महादेव परब्रह्माखिलेश्वर ॥

त्रिगुणातीत निर्व्यग्र त्रिदेवजनक प्रभो । २७॥

वयं ते शरणापन्ना रक्षास्मान्दुःखितान्विभो ।
शङ्खचूडार्दितान्क्लिष्टान्सन्नाथान्परमेश्वर ॥ २८॥

English
We have sought refuge in you. O lord, save us who are distressed. O lord Śiva, we are harassed by Śaṅkhacūḍa and so dejected and well nigh exhausted. Save us. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं योऽधिष्ठितो लोको गोलोक इति स स्मृतः ।
अधिष्ठाता तस्य विभुः कृष्णोऽयं त्वदधिष्ठितः ॥ २९॥

English
The region that is adjacent to this place is called Goloka, Lord Kṛṣṇa is its presiding deity. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्षदप्रवरस्तस्य सुदामा दैवयन्त्रितः ।
राधाशप्तो बभूवाथ शङ्खचूडश्च दानवः ॥ ३०॥

English
One of his leading attendants and comrades, Sudāmā, cursed by Rādhā and led by fate, has become the Dānava Śaṅkhacūḍa. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन निःसारिताः शम्भो पीड्यमानाः समन्ततः ।
हृताधिकारास्त्रिदशा विचरन्ति महीतले ॥ ३१॥

English
O Śiva, the gods divested of all powers ousted and harassed by him roam over the Earth now. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वां विना न स वध्यश्च सर्वेषां त्रिदिवौकसाम् ।
तं घातय महेशान लोकानां सुखमावह ॥ ३२॥

English
Except by you he cannot be killed by any one of the gods. Please kill him and render the worlds happy. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव निर्गुणः सत्योऽनन्तोऽनन्तपराक्रमः ।
सगुणः सन्निवेशश्च प्रकृतेः पुरुषात्परः ॥ ३३॥

English
You alone are devoid as well as possessed of attributes, truthful, of infinite valour, embedded in the good and greater than Prakṛti and Puruṣa. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
रजसा सृष्टिसमये त्वं ब्रह्मा सृष्टिकृत्प्रभो ।
सत्त्वेन पालने विष्णुस्त्रिभुवावनकारकः ॥ ३४॥

English
At creation, O lord, you are Brahmā, the creator through Rajas. O protector of the three worlds, in the activity of protection through Sattva you are Viṣṇu. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तमसा प्रलये रुद्रो जगत्संहारकारकः ।
निस्त्रैगुण्ये शिवाख्यातस्तुर्य्यो ज्योतिःस्वरूपकः ॥ ३५॥

English
In dissolution through Tamas you are Rudra the annihilator of the universe. In the state free from the three attributes you are Śiva the fourth one, of the form of brilliance. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं दीक्षया च गोलोके त्वं गवां परिपालकः ।
त्वद्गोशालामध्यगश्च कृष्णः क्रीडत्यहर्निशम् ॥ ३६॥

English
At your behest, Kṛṣṇa the protector, goes to Goloka. Stationed in the middle of your cowshed he sports day and night. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं सर्वकारणं स्वामी विधिर्विष्ण्वीश्वरः परम् ।
निर्विकारी सदासाक्षी परमात्मा परेश्वरः ॥ ३७॥

English
You are the cause of all. You are the lord of all. You are Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. You are free from aberrations. You are the constant witness. You are the supreme soul, the great Iśvara. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
दीनानाथसहायी च दीनानां प्रतिपालकः ।
दीनबन्धुस्त्रिलोकेशः शरणागतवत्सलः ॥ ३८॥

English
You are the redeemer of the distressed and the poor, the protector and the kinsman of the distressed, the lord of the worlds. You are favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in you. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मानुद्धर गौरीश प्रसीद परमेश्वर ।
त्वदधीना वयं नाथ यदिच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ३९॥

English
O lord of Pārvatī, uplift us. O lord Śiva, be pleased. O lord, we are subservient to you. You do as you please, O lord. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तौ सुरौ व्यास हरिर्ब्रह्मा च वै तदा ।
विरेमतुः शिवं नत्वा करौ बद्ध्वा विनीतकौ ॥ ४०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this, O Vyāsa, those two deities, Viṣṇu and Brahmā bowed to Śiva and stopped. They joined their palms in reverence and stood humbly. (40.)
