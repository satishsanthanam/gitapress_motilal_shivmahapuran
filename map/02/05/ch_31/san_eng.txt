### **CHAPTER THIRTYONE - (*Śiva's advice*)**

---

Sanskrit
३१

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथाकर्ण्य वचः शम्भुर्हरिविध्योः सुदीनयोः ।
उवाच विहसन्वाण्या मेघनादगभीरया ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of the distressed Viṣṇu and Brahmā, Śiva laughingly spoke in the rumbling tone of the cloud. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
हे हरे वत्स हे ब्रह्मंस्त्यजतं सर्वशो भयम् ।
शङ्खचूडोद्भवं भद्रं सम्भविष्यत्यसंशयम् ॥ २॥

English
*Śiva said:—* "O dear Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, cast off your fear from all sides. Certainly something good will result from the activities of Śaṅkhacūḍa. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडस्य वृत्तान्तं सर्वं जानामि तत्त्वतः ।
कृष्णभक्तस्य गोपस्य सुदाम्नश्च पुरा प्रभो ॥ ३॥

English
I know all the details of his activities factually as well as those of Sudāmā the cowherd devotee of Kṛṣṇa. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मदाज्ञया हृषीकेशो कृष्णरूपं विधाय च ।
गोशालायां स्थितो रम्ये गोलोके मदधिष्ठिते ॥ ४॥

English
At my bidding Viṣṇu has assumed the form of Kṛṣṇa and is stationed in the cowshed in the beautiful Goloka presided over by me. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वतन्त्रोऽहमिति स्वं स मोहं मत्वा गतः पुरा ।
क्रीडाः समकरोद्बह्वीः स्वैरवर्तीव मोहितः ॥ ५॥

English
Considering himself independent under a delusion he indulged in many kinds of sportive dalliance like a deluded licentious person. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा मोहयत्युग्रं तस्याहं मायया स्वया ।
तेषां संहृत्य सद्बुद्धिं शापं दापितवान् किल ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing his excessive delusion as a result of my deceptive art I suppressed their virtuous intellect and made them suffer curse. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं कृत्वा स्वलीलां तां मायां संहृतवानहम् ।
ज्ञानयुक्तास्तदा ते तु मुक्तमोहाः सुबुद्धयः ॥ ७॥

English
Having thus performed my sport, I suppressed the illusion. Regaining knowledge they got rid of delusion and became well-intentioned. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
समीपमागतास्ते मे दीनीभूय प्रणम्य माम् ।
अकुर्वन्सुनुतिं भक्त्या करौ बध्वा विनम्रकाः ॥ ८॥

English
They came near me in a piteous plight. After bowing to me they eulogised me devoutly and humbly with palms joined in reverence. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
वृत्तान्तमवदन् सर्वं लज्जाकुलितमानसाः ।
ऊचुर्मत्पुरतो दीना रक्ष रक्षेति वै गिरः ॥ ९॥

English
Overwhelmed by shame they told me all the details. Dejected, they lamented before me saying the words "Save us, O save us." (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा त्वहं भवस्तेषां सन्तुष्टः प्रोक्तवान् वचः ।
भयं त्यजत हे कृष्ण यूयं सर्वे मदाज्ञया ॥ १०॥

English
Then I, becoming delighted, told them these words, Kṛṣṇa, you forget your fear at my behest. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षकोऽहं सदा प्रीत्या सुभद्रं वो भविष्यति ।
मदिच्छयाऽखिलं जातमिदं सर्वं न संशयः ॥ ११॥

English
I am the protector, always infused with love. Good will befall you. All this has happened at my will. There is no doubt in it. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्थानं गच्छ त्वं सार्द्धं राधया पार्षदेन च ।
दानवस्तु भवेत्सोऽयं भारतेऽत्र न संशयः ॥ १२॥

English
Go to your abode along with Rādhā and your comrade. He will become a Dānava here in Bhārata, certainly. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शापोद्धारं करिष्येऽहं युवयोः समये खलु ।
मदुक्तमिति सन्धार्य शिरसा राधया सह ॥ १३॥

श्रीकृष्णोऽमोददत्यन्तं स्वस्थानमगमत्सुधीः ।
न्यष्ठातां सभयं तत्र मदाराधनतत्परौ ॥ १४॥

English
At the proper time I shall redeem you from the curse”. What I told thus Śrīkṛṣṇa and Rādhā accepted readily. Śrīkṛṣṇa the intelligent rejoiced and returned to his abode. There they engaged themselves in propitiating me and bidding their time. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्वाखिलं मदधीनमस्वतन्त्रं निजं च वै ।
स सुदामाऽभवद्राधाशापतो दानवेश्वरः ॥ १५॥

English
Realising that everything is subject to my control and his will is not independent, Sudāmā became the lord of Dānavas as a result of the curse of Rādhā. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडाभिधो देवद्रोही धर्मविचक्षणः ।
क्लिश्नाति सुबलात्कृत्स्नं सदा देवगणं कुधीः ॥ १६॥

English
The virtuous demon Śaṅkhacūḍa distresses and harasses the gods always with his might. He is evil-minded to this extent. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्मायामोहितःसोऽतिदुष्टमन्त्रिसहायवान् ।
तद्भयं त्यजताश्वेव मयि शास्तरि वै सति ॥ १७॥

English
He has been deluded by my deception and hence he seeks the help of evil ministers. But myself being the chastiser of the wicked you can get rid of his fear quickly”. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्यूचिवान् शिवो यावद्धरिब्रह्मपुरः कथाम् ।
अभवत्तावदन्यच्च चरितं तन्मुने श‍ृणु ॥ १८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O sage, by the time Śiva completed this expatiation in front of Viṣṇu and Brahmā, another event happened there. Listen to it. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नेवान्तरे कृष्णो राधया पार्षदैः सह ।
सद्गोपैराययौ शम्भुमनुकूलयितुं प्रभुम् ॥ १९॥

English
In the meantime Kṛṣṇa came there along with Rādhā and his attendant cowherds in order to propitiate Lord Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभुं प्रणम्य सद्भक्त्या मिलित्वा हरिमादरात् ।
सम्मतो विधिना प्रीत्या सन्तस्थौ शिवशासनात् ॥ २०॥

English
Devoutly bowing to the lord, meeting Viṣṇu with respect and honoured by Brahmā with love he stood there awaiting Śiva’s behest. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शम्भुं पुनर्नत्वा तुष्टाव विहिताञ्जलिः ।
श्रीकृष्णो मोहनिर्मुक्तो ज्ञात्वा तत्त्वं शिवस्य हि ॥ २१॥

English
Then he bowed again to Śiva with palms joined in reverence. Realising the principle of Śiva and getting rid of his delusion Kṛṣṇa eulogised Śiva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्ण उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव परब्रह्म सताङ्गते ।
क्षमस्व चापराधं मे प्रसीद परमेश्वर ॥ २२॥

English
*Lord Kṛṣṇa saṭṭ:—*  O supreme God, lord of gods, Supreme Brahman and the goal of the good, forgive me my guilt. O supreme god, be pleased. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्तः शर्व च सर्वं च त्वयि सर्वं महेश्वर ।
सर्वं त्वं निखिलाधीश प्रसीद परमेश्वर ॥ २३॥

English
O Śiva, everything originates from you. O supreme lord, everything merges in you. O lord of all, you are everything. O supreme lord, be pleased. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं ज्योतिः परमं साक्षात्सर्वव्यापी सनातनः ।
त्वया नाथेन गौरीश सनाथाःसकला वयम् ॥ २४॥

English
You are the greatest splendour. You are the eternal being directly pervading everything. O lord of Gauri, with you as leader, we are well-guided. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वोपरि निजं मत्वा विहरन्मोहमाश्रितः ।
तत्फलं प्राप्तवानस्मि शापं प्राप्तः सवामकः ॥ २५॥

पार्षदप्रवरो यो मे सुदामा नाम गोपकः ।
स राधाशापतः स्वामिन्दानवीं योनिमाश्रितः ॥ २६॥

English
Considering myself above all, I sported about, under the delusion. I reaped the fruit thereof. He who went astray was cursed. O lord, my leading comrade Sudāmā the cowherd is born as a Dānava. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मानुद्धर दुर्गेश प्रसीद परमेश्वर ।
शापोद्धारं कुरुष्वाद्य पाहि नः शरणागतान् ॥ २७॥

English
O lord of Pārvatī, uplift us. O supreme lord, be pleased. Please redeem us from the curse. Save us who have sought refuge in you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रसन्नोऽभूच्छिवस्तत्र शरणागतवत्सलः ॥ २८॥

English
After saying this, Lord Kṛṣṇa, accompanied by Rādhā, stopped. Śiva was delighted thereat, Śiva who is favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in him. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Lord Śiva said:—* “O Kṛṣṇa, O lord of cowherdesses leave off your fear. Be happy. O dear, all this has been brought about by me with blessing in disguise. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Good will befall you. Go back to your excellent abode. You shall be cautious and guarded in your position of authority. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Sport about as you please after realising me the greater than the greatest. Accompanied by Rādhā and your comrades carry out your task unexasperated and unfluttered. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

English
In the excellent Vārāha Kalpa,²⁴⁹ you shall undergo the effect of the curse along with the young damsel Rādhā and then attain your region. (32.)

[fn 249. Vārāhakalpa, identical with Śveta-Vārāha or Śveta Kalpa, is one of the thirty Kalpas known to the Purāṇas. Each Kalpa lasts for a specified period and is repeated in order of succession.]
---

Sanskrit

दानवीं योनिमाश्रित्येदानीं क्लिश्नाति वै जगत् ॥ ३३॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, your comrade, the most beloved Sudāmā is born of a Dānava now and he harasses the universe. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He has become a Dānava, an enemy of the gods, named Śaṅkhacūḍa as a result of the power of Rādhā’s curse. He hates and belongs to the party of Daityas. (34.)

Sanskrit

English
Divested of their powers, ousted and harassed by him for ever, the demoralized gods including Indra have fled to the ten directions. (35.)

English

English

इति श्रुत्वा वचः शम्भुर्देवानां भक्तवत्सलः ।
उवाच विहसन् वाण्या मेघनादगभीरया ॥ ५२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* “On hearing the words of the gods, Śiva favourably disposed to his devotees spoke to them laughingly in the rumbling tone of the cloud. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीशङ्कर उवाच ।
हे हरे हे विधे देवाः स्वस्थानं गच्छत ध्रुवम् ।
शङ्खचूडं वधिष्यामि सगणं नात्र संशयः ॥ ५३॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—* O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O Gods, return to your own abodes by all means. I shall kill Śaṅkhacūḍa along with his followers and attendants. There is no doubt about it. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य महेशस्य वचः पीयूषसन्निभम् ।
ते सर्वे प्रमुदा ह्यासन्नष्टं मत्वा च दानवम् ॥ ५४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of lord Śiva sweet as nectar they were excessively delighted considering the Dānava already killed. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिर्जगाम वैकुण्ठं सत्यलोके विधिस्तदा ।
प्रणिपत्य महेशं च सुराद्याः स्वपदं ययुः ॥ ५५॥

English
After bowing to lord Śiva, Viṣṇu went to Vaikuṇṭha and Brahmā to Satyaloka. The god and others went to their own abodes. (55.)
