### **CHAPTER THIRTY TWO - *(The Emissary is sent)***

---

Sanskrit
३२

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथेशानो महारुद्रो दुष्टकालःसतां गतिः ।
शङ्खचूडवधं चित्ते निश्चिकाय सुरेच्छया ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—*  Then lord Śiva, Death to the wicked, goal of the good, decided in his mind to slay Śaṅkhacūḍa in accordance with the wishes of the gods. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दूतं कृत्वा चित्ररथं गन्धर्वेश्वरमीप्सितम् ।
शीघ्रं प्रस्थापयामास शङ्खचूडान्तिके मुदा ॥ २॥

English
He made his friend the lord of Gandharvas his messenger and sent him in a wonderful chariot$^{251}$ hurriedly to Śaṅkhacūḍa joyously. (2.)

[fn 251. Citraratha (lit. of wonderful chariot) seems to be an appellation of Puspadanta. Or Citraratha, lord of Gandharvas, may have assumed the name Puspadanta as an emissary.]
---

Sanskrit
सर्वेश्वराज्ञया दूतो ययौ तन्नगरं च सः ।
महेन्द्रनगरोत्कृष्टं कुबेरभवनाधिकम् ॥ ३॥

English
At the bidding of lord Śiva, the emissary went to the city of the Asura which was superior to Indra’s Amarāvatī and Kubera’s Palace. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा ददर्श तन्मध्ये शङ्खचूडालयं वरम् ।
राजितं द्वादशैर्द्वारैर्द्वारपालसमन्वितम् ॥ ४॥

English
Reaching there, he saw the excellent abode of Śaṅkhacūḍa in the middle; it shone with its twelve entrance doors with gatekeepers in each. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स दृष्ट्वा पुष्पदन्तस्तु वरं द्वारं ददर्श सः ।
कथयामास वृत्तान्तं द्वारपालाय निर्भयः ॥ ५॥

English
Puspadanta saw the main excellent entrance. Fearlessly he informed the gatekeeper. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अतिक्रम्य च तद् द्वारं जगामाभ्यन्तरे मुदा ।
अतीव सुन्दरं रम्यं विस्तीर्णं समलङ्कृतम् ॥ ६॥

English
Passing beyond that door he joyously went in. It was spacious, exquisitely fine and richly decorated. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स गत्वा शङ्खचूडं तं ददर्श दनुजाधिपम् ।
वीरमण्डलमध्यस्थं रत्नसिंहासनस्थितम् ॥ ७॥

English
Going in he saw Śaṅkhacūḍa, the ruler of Dānavas, seated on a gem-set throne in the midst of heroic warriors. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
दानवेन्द्रैः परिवृतं सेवितं च त्रिकोटिभिः ।
शतकोटिभिरन्यैश्च भ्रमद्भिः शस्त्रपाणिभिः ॥ ८॥

English
He was surrounded by leading Dānavas and served by three crores of attendants and guarded by another hundred crores of well armed soldiers moving to and fro. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूतं च तं दृष्ट्वा पुष्पदन्तः सविस्मयः ।
उवाच रणवृत्तान्तं यदुक्तं शङ्करेण च ॥ ९॥

English
Seeing him, Puspadanta was struck with wonder. He gave the message of war as conveyed by Śiva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पदन्त उवाच ।
राजेन्द्र शिवदूतोऽहं पुष्पदन्ताभिधः प्रभो ।
यदुक्तं शङ्करेणैव तच्छृणु त्वं ब्रवीमि ते ॥ १०॥

English
*Puspadanta said :—*  O great king, O lord, I am the Emissary of Śiva named Puṣpadanta. Please listen to what is mentioned by Śiva himself. I am telling you the same. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
राज्यं देहि च देवानामधिकारं हि साम्प्रतम् ।
नो चेत्कुरु रणं सार्द्धं परेण च मया सताम् ॥ ११॥

English
*Śiva said:—* Now, give back their kingdom to the gods and their authority. If not, fight with me, the greatest of the good warriors. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा मां शरणापन्ना देवेशं शङ्करं सताम् ।
अहं क्रुद्धो महारुद्रस्त्वां वधिष्याम्यसंशयम् ॥ १२॥

English
The gods have sought refuge in me, the lord of the gods and the benefactor of the good. I the infuriated will certainly slay you. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
हरोऽस्मि सर्वदेवेभ्यो ह्यभयं दत्तवानहम् ।
खलदण्डधरोऽहं वै शरणागतवत्सलः ॥ १३॥

English
I am Śiva, the destroyer. I have granted protection to all the gods. I am the holder of the chastising rod for the wicked and favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in me. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
राज्यं दास्यसि किं वा त्वं करिष्यसि रणं च किम् ।
तत्त्वं ब्रूहि द्वयोरेकं दानवेन्द्र विचार्य्य वै ॥ १४॥

English
O lord of Dānavas, consider and let me know one of the two alternatives specifically, whether you will return the kingdom or fight. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पदन्त उवाच ।
इत्युक्तं यन्महेशेन तुभ्यं तन्मे निवेदितम् ।
वितथं शम्भुवाक्यं न कदापि दनुजाधिप ॥ १५॥

English
*Puṣpadanta said:* O lord of Dānavas, what has been stated by Śiva has been conveyed to you. Śiva’s words have never gone in vain. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं स्वस्वामिनं गन्तुमिच्छामि त्वरितं हरम् ।
गत्वा वक्ष्यामि किं शम्भुं तथा त्वं वद मामिह ॥ १६॥

English
I wish to return to my lord Śiva immediately. After going back what shall I tell Śiva, you clearly let me know. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्थं च पुष्पदन्तस्य शिवदूतस्य सत्पतेः ।
आकर्ण्य वचनं राजा हसित्वा तमुवाच सः ॥ १७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Puṣpadanta who was the emissary of lord Śiva, the king laughed, then spoke to him. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूड उवाच ।
राज्यं दास्ये न देवेभ्यो वीरभोग्या वसुन्धरा ।
रणं दास्यामि ते रुद्र देवानां पक्षपातिने ॥ १८॥

English
*Śaṅkhacūḍa said:—*  I will never return the kingdom to the god. The earth shall be enjoyed by heroic warriors. O Śiva, I shall fight with you who are a partisan of the gods. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्योपरि प्रयायी स्यात्स वीरो भुवेनऽधमः ।
अतः पूर्वमहं रुद्र त्वां गमिष्याम्यसंशयम् ॥ १९॥

English
The hero who allows another to supercede him is the basest in the world. Hence O Śiva I shall certainly march towards you just now. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभात आगमिष्यामि वीरयात्रा विचारतः ।
त्वं गच्छाचक्ष्व रुद्राय हीदृशं वचनं मम ॥ २०॥

English
I reach there in the morning in the course of my victorious campaign. O messenger, go and tell all this to Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा शङ्खचूडवचनं सुप्रहस्य सः ।
उवाच दानवेन्द्रं स शम्भुदूतस्तु गर्वितम् ॥ २१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of Śaṅkhacūḍa, the emissary of Śiva laughed aloud and then spoke haughtily to the lord of the Asuras. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पदन्त उवाच
अन्येषामपि राजेन्द्र गणानां शङ्करस्य च ।
न स्थातुं सम्मुखे योग्यः किं पुनस्तस्य सम्मुखम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Puṣpadanta said:—*  O Great king, you cannot face the Gaṇas of Śiva. Then how can you face lord Śiva himself? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स त्वं देहि च देवानामधिकाराणि सर्वशः ।
त्वमरं गच्छ पातालं यदि जीवितुमिच्छसि ॥ २३॥

English
So return their positions of authority to the gods entirely. Move immediately to Pātāla if you wish to live. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सामान्यममरं तं नो विद्धि दानवसत्तम ।
शङ्करः परमात्मा हि सर्वेषामीश्वरेश्वरः ॥ २४॥

English
O excellent Dānava, do not regard Śiva an ordinary deity. He is indeed the great soul, the lord of the lord of all. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्राद्याः सकला देवा यस्याज्ञावर्तिनः सदा ।
सप्रजापतयः सिद्धा मुनयश्चाप्यहीश्वराः ॥ २५॥

English
Indra and other gods abide by his commands. The Siddhas, the patriarchs, the sages and the serpent lords all follow suit. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
हरेर्विधेश्च स स्वामी निर्गुणः सगुणः स हि ।
यस्य भ्रूभङ्गमात्रेण सर्वेषां प्रलयो भवेत् ॥ २६॥

English
He is the overlord of Viṣṇu and Brahmā. He is both possessed and devoid of attributes. By a mere twitch of his knitted eyebrow everything is dissolved. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्य पूर्णरूपश्च लोकसंहारकारकः ।
सतां गतिर्दुष्टहन्ता निर्विकारः परात्परः ॥ २७॥

English
Śiva is the perfect form of gods, the cause of the annihilation of the worlds, the goal of the good, the destroyer of the wicked. He is free from aberrations. He is greater than the greatest. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणोऽधिपतिः सोऽपि हरेरपि महेश्वरः ।
अवमान्यं न वै तस्य शासनं दानवर्षभ ॥ २८॥

English
He is the overlord of Brahmā. He is lord Śiva even into Viṣṇu. O excellent Dānava, his behest should never be slighted. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
किं बहूक्तेन राजेन्द्र मनसा संविचार्य च ।
रुद्रं विद्धि महेशानं परं ब्रह्म चिदात्मकम् ॥ २९॥

English
Of what avail is an unnecessary digression, O great king. Ponder deeply. Know him to be great lord, the great Brahman, the knowledge-formed. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
देहि राज्यं हि देवानामधिकारांश्च सर्वशः ।
एवं ते कुशलं तात भविष्यत्यन्यथा भयम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Return their kingdoms to the gods as well as their positions of authority. O dear, thus you will fare well. Otherwise, terror will strike you. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्त्कुमार उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा दानवेन्द्रः शङ्खचूडः प्रतापवान् ।
उवाच शिवदूतं तं भवितव्यविमोहितः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing this, the valorous king of the Dānavas, deluded by his fate spoke to the emissary of Śiva thus. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूड उवाच ।
स्वतो राज्यं न दास्यामि नाधिकारान् विनिश्चयात् ।
विना युद्धं महेशेन सत्यमेतद् ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ३२॥

English
*Śaṅkhacūḍa said:—*  I shall neither give up kingdom nor the positions of authority, without a fight with him. This is certain. I tell you the truth. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कालाधीनं जगत्सर्वं विज्ञेयं सचराचरम् ।
कालाद्भवति सर्वं हि विनश्यति च कालतः ॥ ३३॥

English
The entire universe whether mobile or immobile is subject to the vagaries of time. Everything originates in time and everything merges into time. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं गच्छ शङ्करं रुद्रं मयोक्तं वद तत्त्वतः ।
स च युक्तं करोत्वेवं बहुवार्तां कुरुष्व नो ॥ ३४॥

English
Go and tell Śiva exactly what I have said to you. Let him do what is proper. Do not talk much. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा शिवदूतोऽसौ जगाम स्वामिनं निजम् ।
यथार्थं कथयामास पुष्पदन्तश्च सन्मुने ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O good sage, Puṣpadanta the emissary of Śiva when thus addressed by the Asura returned to lord Śiva and told him everything duly. (35.)
