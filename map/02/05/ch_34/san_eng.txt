### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - (The March of Śaṅkhacūḍa)**

---

Sanskrit
३४

व्यास उवाच ।
विधितात महाबुद्धे मुने जीव चिरं समाः ।
कथितं सुमहच्चित्रं चरितं चन्द्रमौलिनः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—* O dear son of Brahmā, O sage of great intellect, live long for many years. You have narrated the great story of the mooncrested lord. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवदूते गते तत्र शङ्खचूडश्च दानवः ।
किं चकार प्रतापी स तत्त्वं वद सुविस्तरम् ॥ २॥

English
When Śiva's emissary had departed, what did the valorous Dānava, Śaṅkhacūḍa do? Please mention that in detail. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथ दूते गते तत्र शङ्खचूडः प्रतापवान् ।
उवाच तुलसीं वार्तां गत्वाभ्यन्तरमेव ताम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* When the messenger returned, the valorous Śaṅkhacūḍa went in and told his wife Tulasi all the details. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूड उवाच ।
शम्भुदूतमुखाद्देवि युद्धायाहं समुद्यतः ।
तेन गच्छाम्यहं योद्धुं शासनं कुरु मे ध्रुवम् ॥ ४॥

English
Śaṅkhacūḍa said:—  O dear lady, infuriated by the words of Śiva's messenger I have prepared for a war. Hence I am going to fight. You carry out my directions. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवमुक्त्वा स ज्ञानी नानाबोधनतः प्रियाम् ।
क्रीडां चकार हर्षेण तमनादृत्य शङ्करम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this and slighting Śiva, that demon professing to be wise advised his wife in various ways and sported with her with delight. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तौ दम्पती चिक्रीडाते निमग्नौ सुखसागरे ।
नानाकामकलाभिश्च निशि चाटुशतैरपि ॥ ६॥

English
Throughout that night, the couple indulged in sexual dalliance. Uttering coaxing and cajoling words, practising various erotic arts, they immersed themselves in the ocean of happiness. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मे मुहूर्ते चोत्थाय प्रातःकृत्यं विधाय च ।
नित्यकार्यं च कृत्वादौ ददौ दानमनन्तकम् ॥ ७॥

English
He got up in the Brāhma Muhūrta,²⁶⁰ and finished his daily routine in the morning. He then performed the offering of charitable gifts. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रं कृत्वा च राजेन्द्रं सर्वेषु दानवेषु च ।
पुत्रे समर्प्य भार्यां च स राज्यं सर्वसम्पदम् ॥ ८॥

प्रियामाश्वासयामास स राजा रुदतीं पुनः ।
निषेधन्तीं च गमनं नाना वार्तां प्रकथ्य च ॥ ९॥

English
He crowned his son as the lord of Dānavas. He entrusted his wife, his kingdom and his riches to the care of his son. When his wife cried and dissuaded him from going to the war he consoled her by various words of appeasement. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
निजसेनापतिं वीरं समाहूय समादृतः ।
आदिदेश स सनद्धः सङ्ग्रामं कर्तुमुद्यतः ॥ १०॥

English
He called his general and ordered him to be ready for the war. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूड उवाच ।
अद्य सेनापते वीराः सर्वे समरशालिनः ।
सन्नद्धाखिलकर्माणो निर्गच्छन्तु रणाय च ॥ ११॥

English
Śaṅkhacūḍa said:—  O general, let the heroic warriors start for the war. Let them be ready for action; they have been trained well for the war. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्याश्च दानवाः शूरा षडशीतिरुदायुधाः ।
कङ्कानां बलिनां शीघ्रं सेना निर्यान्तु निर्भयाः ॥ १२॥

English
Let the heroic Dānavas and Daityas, the armies of the powerful Kaṅkas of eightysix divisions well-equipped in arms set out fearlessly. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाशदसुराणां हि निर्गच्छन्तु कुलानि वै ।
कोटिवीर्याणि युद्धार्थं शम्भुना देवपक्षिणा ॥ १३॥

English
Let the fifty families of Asuras, having the heroism and prowess of a crore set out to fight with Śiva, the partisan of the gods. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्नद्धानि च धौम्राणां कुलानि च शतं द्रुतम् ।
निर्गच्छन्तु रणार्थं हि शम्भुना मम शासनात् ॥ १४॥

English
At my bidding, let the hundred armed families of Dhaumras speedily set out to fight with Śiva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कालकेयाश्च मौर्याश्च दौर्हृदाः कालकास्तथा ।
सज्जा निर्यान्तु युद्धाय रुद्रेण मम शासनात् ॥ १५॥

English
At my behest, let the Kālakeyas Mauryas, Dauhṛdas and the Kālakas set out ready for the fight with Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याज्ञाप्यासुरपतिर्दानवेन्द्रो महाबलः ।
निर्जगाम महासैन्यः सहस्रैर्बहुभिर्वृतः ॥ १६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After ordering thus, the powerful lord of Asuras and the Emperor of the Dānavas set out surrounded by thousands of warriors and great armies. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य सेनापतिश्चैव युद्धशास्त्रविशारदः ।
महारथो महावीरो रथिनां प्रवरो रणे ॥ १७॥

English
His general was an expert in the science and technique of warfare. He was the best of charioteers a great hero and skilled in warfare. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिलक्षाक्षौहिणीयुक्तो माण्डल्यं च चकार ह ।
बहिर्बभूव शिविराद्रणे वीरभयङ्करः ॥ १८॥

English
He had three hundred thousand Akṣauhiṇī²⁶¹ armies. He performed the rites of auspicious beginning and came out of the camp. He was terrible to the watching heroes. (18.)

[fn 261. A single Akṣauhiṇī consists of 21, 870 elephants, 21870 chariots, 65610 horses and 109, 350 foot.]
---

Sanskrit
रत्नेन्द्रसारनिर्माणं विमानमभिरुह्य सः ।
गुरुवर्गं पुरस्कृत्य रणार्थं प्रययौ किल ॥ १९॥

English
Mounting on an aerial chariot of exquisite build and inlaid with gems, and making obeisance to the elders and preceptors he set out for the battle. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पभद्रानदीतीरे यत्राक्षयवटः शुभः ।
सिद्धाश्रमे च सिद्धानां सिद्धिक्षेत्रं सुसिद्धिदम् ॥ २०॥

कपिलस्य ततःस्थानं पुण्यक्षेत्रे च भारते ।
पश्चिमोदधिपूर्वे च मलयस्य हि पश्चिमे ॥ २१॥

English
In the holy land of Bhārata, to the east of the western ocean and to the west of Malaya$^{262}$ mountain, on the banks of river Puṣpabhadrā,$^{263}$ there is a hermitage of Kapila$^{264}$ with an auspicious holy Banyan tree. It is called Siddhāśrama.$^{265}$ It is the place where holy men achieve the result of their action. (20-21.)

[fn 262. Malaya (Dravidian : malai) is identical with the Travancore hills and the southernmost part of the Western Ghats.]

[fn 263. It has not been possible to identify this river. According to the present context, Puṣpabhadrā issues from the Himalayas, rises along with the Sarasvatī and falls into the Western ocean. (Cp Verses 24-25 of this ch.)]

[fn 264. Kapila was an ancient sage who destroyed a hundred thousand sons of King Sagara.]

[fn 265. The wide tract of land, the Scene of Kapila's hermitage, lies to the East of the Western ocean to the West of Travancore hills to the north of Śrīśaila hills and to the south of Gandhamādana mountain.]


---

Sanskrit
श्रीशैलोत्तरभागे च गन्धमादनदक्षिणे ।
पञ्चयोजनविस्तीर्णे दैर्घ्ये शतगुणस्तथा ॥ २२॥

English
It is to the north of Śrīśaila$^{266}$ and to the south of Gandhamādana.$^{267}$ It is five Yojanas in width and a hundred times as much in length. (22.)

[fn 266. It is one of the most sacred and beautiful hills of the South overhanging the river kṛṣṇā.]

[fn 267. The location of Gandhamādana is highly controversial. See Note 309 P. 405 and Note 66 P. 623. Most probably this is the Himālayan Gandhamādana that is referred to here.]

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशा भारते च सुपुण्यदा ।
पुष्पभद्रा नदी रम्या जलपूर्णा सरस्वती ॥ २३॥

English
The river Puṣpabhadrā is very beautiful and full of transparent water. It confers merits an everyone in Bhārata, like the river Sarasvatī. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
लवणोदधिप्रिया भार्या शश्वत्सौभाग्यसंयुता ।
सरस्वतीसंश्रिता च निर्गता सा हिमालयात् ॥ २४॥

English
It starts from Himālaya, has its confluence with Sarasvatī. It is the beloved of the briny sea and blessess people with good fortune. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गोमन्तं वामतः कृत्वा प्रविष्टा पश्चिमोदधौ ।
तत्र गत्वा शङ्खचूडः शिवसेनां ददर्श ह ॥ २५॥

English
It enters the western ocean where Gomanta$^{268}$ is on its left. Śaṅkhacūḍa went there and saw the army of Śiva. (25.)

[fn 268. It is identical with Goa.]
