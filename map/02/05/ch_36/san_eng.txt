### **CHAPTER THIRTYSIX - *(Mutual fight)***

---

Sanskrit
३६

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
स दूतस्तत्र गत्वा च शिववाक्यं जगाद ह ।
सविस्तरं यथार्थं च निश्चयं तस्य तत्त्वतः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* The emissary returned and mentioned the words of Śiva, in detail and truthfully. He conveyed his decision as it was. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा शङ्खचूडोऽसौ दानवेन्द्रः प्रतापवान् ।
अङ्गीचकार सुप्रीत्या रणमेव स दानवः ॥ २॥

English
On hearing that, the valorous Dānava Śaṅkhacūḍa accepted lovingly the alternative of a fight. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
समारुरोह यानं च सहामात्यैश्च सत्वरः ।
आदिदेश स्वसैन्यं च युद्धार्थं शङ्करेण च ॥ ३॥

English
Hurriedly he got into his vehicle along with his ministers. He commanded his army against Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः स्वसैन्यं देवांश्च प्रेरयामास सत्वरः ।
स्वयमप्यखिलेशोऽपि सन्नद्धोऽभूच्च लीलया ॥ ४॥

English
Śiva too hastened to urge his army and the gods. The lord of all was ready himself with his sport. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
युद्धारम्भो बभूवाशु नेदुर्वाद्यानि भूरिशः ।
कोलाहलश्च सञ्जातो वीरशब्दस्तथैव च ॥ ५॥

English
The musical instruments formally announced the beginning of war. There was a great tumult along with the shouts of the heroes. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदानवयोर्युद्धं परस्परमभून्मुने ।
धर्मतो युयुधे तत्र देवदानवयोर्गणः ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, the mutual fight between the gods and the Dānavas ensued. Both the hosts of the gods and the Dānavas fought righteously. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयं महेन्द्रो युयुधे सार्धं च वृषपर्वणा ।
भास्करो युयुधे विप्रचित्तिना सह धर्मतः ॥ ७॥

English
Mahendra fought with Vṛṣaparvan. Bhāskara fought with Vipracitti. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
दम्भेन सह विष्णुश्च चकार परमं रणम् ।
कालासुरेण कालश्च गोकर्णेन हुताशनः ॥ ८॥

English
Viṣṇu fought a great battle with Dambha, Kāla with the Asura Kāla and the firegod fought with Gokarṇa. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
कुबेरः कालकेयेन विश्वकर्मा मयेन च ।
भयङ्करेण मृत्युश्च संहारेण यमस्तथा ॥ ९॥

English
Kubera fought with Kālakeya and Viśvakarman with Maya. Mṛtyu fought with Bhayāṅkara and Yama with Saṃhāra. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कालम्बिकेन वरुणश्चञ्चलेन समीरणः ।
बुधश्च घटपृष्ठेन रक्ताक्षेण शनैश्चरः ॥ १०॥

English
Varuṇa fought with Kālambika, the wind god with Cañcala. Mercury with Ghaṭapṛṣṭha and Śanaiścara with Raktākṣa. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
जयन्तो रत्नसारेण वसवो वर्चसां गणैः ।
अश्विनौ दीप्तिमद्भ्यां च धूम्रेण नलकूबरः ॥ ११॥

English
Jayanta fought with Ratnasāra; the Vasus with the groups of Varcas's; the Aśvins with the two Diptimants and Nalakūbara with Dhūmbra. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
धुरन्धरेण धर्मश्च गणकाक्षेण मङ्गलः ।
शोभाकरेण वैश्वानः पिपिटेन च मन्मथः ॥ १२॥

English
Dharma fought with Dhurandhara; Maṅgala with Gaṇakākṣa; Vaiśvana with Śobhākara and Manmatha with Pipiṭa. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
गोकामुखेन चूर्णेन खड्गनाम्नासुरेण च ।
धूम्रेण संहलेनापि विश्वेन च प्रतापिना ॥ १३॥

द्वादशार्का पलाशेन युयुधुर्धर्मतः परे ।
असुरैरमराः सार्द्धं शिवसाहाय्यशालिनः ॥ १४॥

English
The twelve sun gods fought with the Asuras—Gokāmukha, Cūrṇa, Khaḍga, Dhūmra, Samhala, the valorous Viśva and Palāśa. The other gods assisting Śiva fought righteously with the other Asuras. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादश महारुद्राश्चैकादशभयङ्करैः ।
असुरैर्युयुधुर्वीरैर्महाबलपराक्रमैः ॥ १५॥

English
The eleven Mahārudras$^{275}$ fought with the eleven terrible Asuras of great power and valour. (15.)

[fn 275. For eleven Rudras, see Note 127. P 138; 256 P 948.]
---

Sanskrit
महामणिश्च युयुधे चोग्रचण्डादिभिः सह ।
राहुणा सह चन्द्रश्च जीवः शुक्रेण धर्मतः ॥ १६॥

English
Mahāmaṇi fought with Ugrācaṇḍa and others. The god Moon fought with Rāhu and Jīva fought with Śukra. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वरादयः सर्वे दानवप्रवरैः सह ।
युयुधुश्च महायुद्धे नोक्ता विस्तरतः पृथक् ॥ १७॥

English
Nandiśvara and the rest fought with leading Dānavas in the great battle. This is not being explained separately. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वटमूले तदा शम्भुस्तस्थौ काल्याः सुतेन च ।
सर्वे च युयुधुः सैन्यसमूहाः सततं मुने ॥ १८॥

English
O sage, then Śiva stayed at the foot of the Banyan tree along with Kālī and his son. The hosts of the two armies fought continuously against each other. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रत्नसिंहासने रम्ये कोटिदानवसंयुते ।
उवास शङ्खचूडश्च रत्नभूषणभूषितः ॥ १९॥

English
Decorated with gemset ornaments, Śaṅkhcūda sat on his gemset throne of great beauty attended upon by a crore Dānavas. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
महायुद्धो बभूवाथ देवासुरविमर्दनः ।
नानायुधानि दिव्यानि चलन्ति स्म महामृधे ॥ २०॥

English
Then ensued a great war in which both gods and Asuras were crushed. In that great war many divine and miraculous weapons were hurled. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
गदर्ष्टिपट्टिशाश्चक्रभुशुण्डिप्रासमुद्गराः ।
निस्त्रिंशभल्लपरिघाः शक्त्युन्मुखपरश्वधाः ॥ २१॥

शरतोमरखड्गाश्च शतघ्न्यश्च सहस्रशः ।
भिन्दिपालादयश्चान्ये वीरहस्तेषु शोभिताः ॥ २२॥

English
Maces, long and short swords, Paṭṭiśas, Bhuśuṇḍis, Mudgaras (different kinds of iron clubs), javelins, spears, Parighas, Śaktis, axes, arrows, Tomaras, Śataghnīs, and other weapons shone in the hands of the heroes. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरांसि चिच्छिदुश्चैभिर्वीरास्तत्र महोत्सवाः ।
वीराणामुभयोश्चैव सैन्ययोर्गर्जतो रणे ॥ २३॥

English
Using these weapons, the heroes severed the heads of each other. It was a jubilant occasion for the roaring heroes of the armies. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
गजास्तुरङ्गा बहवः स्यन्दनाश्च पदातयः ।
सारोहवाहा विविधास्तत्रासन् सुविखण्डिताः ॥ २४॥

English
Elephants, horses, chariots and foot soldiers along with their drivers and riders were hit and split up. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
निकृत्तबाहूरुकरकटिकर्णयुगाङ्घ्रयः ।
सञ्छिन्नध्वजबाणासितनुत्रवरभूषणाः ॥ २५॥

English
The arms, thighs, hands, hips, ears and feet were cut off. The banners, arrows, swords, coats of mail and excellent ornaments were slit and split. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्धतकिरीटैश्च शिरोभिः सह कुण्डलैः ।
संरम्भनष्टैरास्तीर्णा बभौ भूः करभोरुभिः ॥ २६॥

English
The earth shone with heads divested of coronets but with earrings retained, strewn about and with thighs resembling trunks of elephants broken off during the tussle. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
महाभुजैः साभरणैः सञ्छिन्नैः सायुधैस्तथा ।
अङ्गैरन्यैश्च सहसा पटलैर्वा ससारघैः ॥ २७॥

English
Severed arms with the ornaments and weapons still retained and other limbs too were lying scattered about like honeycombs. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मृधे भटाः प्रधावन्तः कबन्धान् स्वशिरोक्षिभिः ।
पश्यन्तस्तत्र चोत्पेतुरुद्यतायुधसद्भुजैः ॥ २८॥

English
The soldiers running in the battle field saw several headless bodies that jumped with many weapons lifted in their hands. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
वल्गन्तोऽतितरां वीरा युयुधुश्च परस्परम् ।
शस्त्रास्त्रैर्विविधैस्तत्र महाबलपराक्रमाः ॥ २९॥

English
With different kinds of miraculous and ordinary weapons and missiles, the heroes of great strength and valour fought one another shouting and leaping. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
केचित्स्वर्णमुखैर्बाणैर्विनिहत्य भटान्मृधे ।
व्यनदन् वीरसन्नादं सतोया इव तोयदाः ॥ ३०॥

English
Some heroes killed the soldiers with their arrows fitted with goden tips and roared like water-laden rumbling clouds. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतः शरकूटेन वीरः सरथसारथिम् ।
वीरं सञ्छादयामास प्रावृट्सूर्यमिवाम्बुदः ॥ ३१॥

English
One hero fully encompassed another hero as well as his chariot and charioteer, by discharging heaps of arrows like the rainy season covering up the sun under the clouds. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्योऽन्यमभिसंसृत्य युयुधुर्द्वन्द्वयोधिनः ।
आह्वयन्तो विशन्तोऽग्रे क्षिपन्तो मर्मभिर्मिथः ॥ ३२॥

English
Fighters of duel rushed against one another, challenging, thrusting and diving in at the vulnerable points. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतो वीरसङ्घाश्च नानाबाहुध्वजायुधाः ।
व्यदृश्यन्त महासङ्ख्ये कुर्वन्तः सिंहसंरवम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Everywhere groups of heroes were seen in that terrible war roaring like lions with various weapons displayed in their hands. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
महारवान् स्वशङ्खांश्च विदध्मुर्वै पृथक् पृथक् ।
वल्गनं चक्रिरे तत्र महावीराः प्रहर्षिताः ॥ ३४॥

English
The heroes in their joy shouted and leapt blowing on their conches of loud sound severally. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं चिरतरं कालं देवदानवयोर्महत् ।
बभूव युद्धं विकटं करालं वीरहर्षदम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Thus for a long time the great combat between the gods and Dānavas continued, terrible and tumultuous but delightful to the heroes. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
महाप्रभोश्च लीलेयं शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ।
यया समोहितं सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Such was the divine sport of the great lord Śiva, the great soul. Everyone including the gods, Asuras and human beings was deluded by it. (36.)
