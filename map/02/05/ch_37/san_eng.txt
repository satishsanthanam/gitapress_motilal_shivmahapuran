### **CHAPTER THIRTYSEVEN - (*Śaṅkhacūḍa fights with the full contingent of his army*)**

---

Sanskrit
३७

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तदा देवगणाः सर्वे दानवैश्च पराजिताः ।
दुद्रुवुर्भयभीताश्च शस्त्रास्त्रक्षतविग्रहाः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Then the gods were defeated by the Dānavas. Their bodies were wounded by weapons and missiles. Terrified, they took to flight. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ते परावृत्य विश्वेशं शङ्करं शरणं ययुः ।
त्राहि त्राहीति सर्वेशेत्यूचुर्विह्वलया गिरा ॥ २॥

English
Returning to Śiva, the lord of the universe, they sought refuge in him. In agitated words they cried “O Lord of all, save, O save us.” (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा पराजयं तेषां देवादीनां स शङ्करः ।
सभयं वचनं श्रुत्वा कोपमुच्चैश्चकार ह ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the defeat of the gods and others and on hearing their cries of fear, Śiva was greatly infuriated. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
निरीक्ष्य स कृपादृष्ट्या देवेभ्यश्चाभयं ददौ ।
बलं च स्वगणानां वै वर्द्धयामास तेजसा ॥ ४॥

English
He glanced at the gods sympathetically and assured them of his protection. With his brilliance he enhanced the strength of his Gaṇas. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाज्ञप्तस्तदा स्कन्दो दानवानां गणैः सह ।
युयुधे निर्भयः सङ्ख्ये महावीरो हरात्मजः ॥ ५॥

English
Commanded by Śiva, the great hero Kārttikeya, son of Śiva fought fearlessly with the hosts of Dānavas in the battle. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा क्रोधं वीरशब्दं देवो यस्तारकान्तकः ।
अक्षौहिणीनां शतकं समरे स जघान ह ॥ ६॥

English
Shouting angrily and roaring like a hero, the lord, the slayer of Tāraka killed a hundred Akṣauhiṇīs$^{276}$ in the battle. (6.)

[fn 276. For Akṣauhiṇī see Note 261 P. 951.]
---

Sanskrit
रुधिरं पातयामास काली कमललोचना ।
तेषां शिरांसि सञ्छिद्य बभक्ष सहसा च सा ॥ ७॥

English
Clipping off their heads, Kālī with eyes like a red lotus, drank off the blood and devoured the flesh rapidly. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पपौ रक्तानि तेषां च दानवानां समन्ततः ।
युद्धं चकार विविधं सुरदानवभीषणम् ॥ ८॥

English
She fought in diverse ways terrifying both the gods and the Dānavas. She drank the blood of the Dānavas all round. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शतलक्षं गजेन्द्राणां दानवानां तथा रणे ।
समादायैकहस्तेन मुखे चिक्षेप लीलया ॥ ९॥

English
Seizing ten million elephants and an equal number of men with a single hand she playfully thrust them into her mouth. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कबन्धानां सहस्रं च सन्ननर्त रणे बहु ।
महान् कोलाहलो जातः क्लीबानां च भयङ्करः ॥ १०॥

English
Many thousands of headless bodies danced in the battle field. There was a great tumult that terrified the cowards. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः स्कन्दः प्रकुप्योच्चैः शरवर्षां चकार ह ।
पातयामास क्षणतः कोटिशोऽसुरनायकान् ॥ ११॥

English
Again Kārttikeya became furiously angry and showered volleys of arrows. He struck crores of leaders of the Asuras within a trice. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
दानवाः शरजालेन स्कन्दस्य क्षतविग्रहाः ।
भीताः प्रदुद्रुवुः सर्वे शेषा मरणतस्तदा ॥ १२॥

English
The Dānavas wounded in their bodies by the numerous arrows of Kārttikeya fled in fright. Those who remained were killed. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषपर्वा विप्रचित्तिर्दण्डश्चापि विकम्पनः ।
स्कन्देन युयुधुः सार्द्धं तेन सर्वे क्रमेण च ॥ १३॥

English
Vṛṣaparvan, Vipracitti, Daṇḍa, and Vikampana fought with Kārttikeya by turns. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
महामारी च युयुधे न बभूव पराङ्मुखी ।
बभूवुस्ते क्षताङ्गाश्च स्कन्दशक्तिप्रपीडिताः ॥ १४॥

English
Mahāmārī also fought. She was never routed. All of them afflicted by Kārttikeya’s spear were wounded. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
महामारीस्कन्दयोश्च विजयोऽभूत्तदा मुने ।
नेदुर्दुन्दुभयः स्वर्गे पुष्पवृष्टिः पपात ह ॥ १५॥

English
O sage, Mahāmārī and Skanda won the battle. Big wardrums were sounded in the heaven. Showers of flowers fell down. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्कन्दस्य समरं दृष्ट्वा महारौद्रं तमद्भुतम् ।
दानवानां क्षयकरं यथा प्रकृतिकल्पकम् ॥ १६॥

महामारीकृतं तच्चोपद्रवं क्षयहेतुकम् ।
चुकोपातीव सहसा सनद्धोऽभूत्स्वयं तदा ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing the wonderfully terrible fight of Kārttikeya that caused wastage in the rank and file of the Dānavas like natural disasters, as well as the harassment and havoc wrought by Mahāmārī, Śaṅkhacūḍa became furious and himself got ready for the battle. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं विमानमारुह्य नानाशस्त्रास्त्रसंयुतम् ।
अभयं सर्ववीराणां नानारत्नपरिच्छदम् ॥ १८॥

महावीरैः शङ्खचूडो जगाम रथमध्यतः ।
धनुर्विकृष्य कर्णान्तं चकार शरवर्षणम् ॥ १९॥

English
He got into his excellent aerial chariot that contained different weapons and missiles, that was set in diamond and that encouraged and emboldened the heroes. Śaṅkhacūḍa drew the string of the bow upto his ear and discharged volleys of arrows from his seat in the middle of the chariot. He was accompanied by many heroes. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य सा शरवृष्टिश्च दुर्निवार्या भयङ्करी ।
महाघोरान्धकारश्च वधस्थाने बभूव ह ॥ २०॥

English
His volley of arrows was terrifying. It could not be withstood. A terrible darkness spread in the battlefield. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाः प्रदुद्रुवुः सर्वे येऽन्ये नन्दीश्वरादयः ।
एक एव कार्त्तिकेयस्तस्थौ समरमूर्द्धनि ॥ २१॥

English
The gods Nandiśvara and others fled. Only Kārttikeya stayed behind in the battle field. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वतानां च सर्पाणां नागानां शाखिनां तथा ।
राजा चकार वृष्टिं च दुर्निवार्यां भयङ्करीम् ॥ २२॥

English
The king of Dānavas showered mountains, serpents, pythons and trees so terrifyingly that it could not be withstood. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दृष्ट्या प्रहतः स्कन्दो बभूव शिवनन्दनः ।
नीहारेण च सान्द्रेण संवृतो भास्करो यथा ॥ २३॥

English
Oppressed by that shower Kārttikeya, the son of Śiva, looked like the sun enveloped by thick sheets of frost. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाविधां स्वमायां च चकार मयदर्शिताम् ।
तां नाविदन् सुराः केऽपि गणाश्च मुनिसत्तम ॥ २४॥

English
He exhibited many types of illusions in the manner indicated by Maya. O excellent sage, none of the gods or Gaṇas understood it. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव शङ्खचूडश्च महामायी महाबलः ।
शरेणैकेन दिव्येन धनुश्चिच्छेद तस्य वै ॥ २५॥

English
At the same time, the powerful Śaṅkhacūḍa of great illusion split his bow with a divine arrow. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
बभञ्ज तद्रथं दिव्यं चिच्छेद रथरक्षकान् ।
मयूरं जर्जरीभूतं दिव्यास्त्रेण चकार सः ॥ २६॥

English
He split his divine chariot and the horses pulling it. With a divine missile he shattered the peacock too. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिं चिक्षेप सूर्याभां तस्य वक्षसि घातिनीम् ।
मूर्च्छामवाप सहसा तत्प्रहारेण स क्षणम् ॥ २७॥

English
The Dānava hurled his spear as refulgent as the sun fatally on his chest whereat he fell unconscious by the force of the blow. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्च चेतनां प्राप्य कार्तिकः परवीरहा ।
रत्नेन्द्रसारनिर्माणमारुरोह स्ववाहनम् ॥ २८॥

English
Regaining consciousness, Kārttikeya the destroyer of heroic enemies, mounted his vehicle of sturdy build, set with gems. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृत्वा पादौ महेशस्य साम्बिकस्य च षण्मुखः ।
शस्त्रास्त्राणि गृहीत्वैव चकार रणमुल्बणम् ॥ २९॥

English
Remembering the feat of lord Śiva accompanied by Pārvatī, and taking up weapons and missiles, the six-faced deity fought terrifically. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्पांश्च पर्वतांश्चैव वृक्षांश्च प्रस्तरांस्तथा ।
सर्वांश्चिच्छेद कोपेन दिव्यास्त्रेण शिवात्मजः ॥ ३०॥

English
With his divine missiles, the son of Śiva split the serpents, mountains, trees and rocks, everything furiously. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वह्निं निवारयामास पार्जन्येन शरेण ह ।
रथं धनुश्च चिच्छेद शङ्खचूडस्य लीलया ॥ ३१॥

English
He prevented a conflagration by the missile of cloud. He split the chariot and the bow of Śaṅkhacūḍa playfully. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्नाहं सर्ववाहांश्च किरीटं मुकुटोज्ज्वलम् ।
वीरशब्दं चकारासौ जगर्ज च पुनः पुनः ॥ ३२॥

English
He split his armour, coronet and the vehicles. He roared like a hero and shouted again and again. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
चिक्षेप शक्तिं सूर्याभां दानवेन्द्रस्य वक्षसि ।
तत्प्रहारेण सम्प्राप मूर्च्छां दीर्घतमेन च ॥ ३३॥

English
He hurled his spear refulgent like the sun at the chest of the lord of Dānavas. At the blow he fell unconscious. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहूर्तमात्रं तत्क्लेशं विनीय स महाबलः ।
चेतनां प्राप्य चोत्तस्थौ जगर्ज हरिवर्चसः ॥ ३४॥

English
That powerful Asura got rid of the affliction in a Muhūrta and regained consciousness. With a leonine vigour he got up and roared. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्त्या जघान तं चापि कार्तिकेयं महाबलम् ।
स पपात महीपृष्ठेऽमोघां कुर्वन् विधिप्रदाम् ॥ ३५॥

English
He bit Kārttikeya of great strength with his spear. Not making that spear, a gift of Brahmā, futile, Kārttikeya fell on the ground. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
काली गृहीत्वा तं क्रोडे निनाय शिवसन्निधौ ।
ज्ञानेन तं शिवश्चापि जीवयामास लीलया ॥ ३६॥

English
Taking him on her lap Kālī brought him near Śiva. By his divine sport and perfect wisdom Śiva enlivened him. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ददौ बलमनन्तं च समुत्तस्थौ प्रतापवान् ।
गमनाय मतिं चक्रे पुनस्तत्र शिवात्मजः ॥ ३७॥

English
Śiva gave him infinite strength. As a result of that the valorous Kārttikeya stood up and felt inclined to go to the battlefield. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे वीरो वीरभद्रो महाबलः ।
शङ्खचूडेन युयुधे समरे बलशालिना ॥ ३८॥

English
In the meantime the heroic Vīrabhadra of great strength fought with the powerful Śaṅkhacūḍa in the battle. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ववर्ष समरेऽस्त्राणि यानि यानि च दानवः ।
चिच्छेद लीलया वीरस्तानि तानि निजैः शरैः ॥ ३९॥

English
Whatever arrows were discharged by the Dānava in the battle were split playfully by Vīrabhadra by means of his own arrows. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यान्यस्त्राणि शतशो मुमुचे दानवेश्वरः ।
तानि चिच्छेद तं बाणैर्वीरभद्रः प्रतापवान् ॥ ४०॥

English
The lord of Dānavas discharged hundreds of divine missiles. The valorous Vīrabhadra split all of them by means of his arrows. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अथातीव चुकोपोच्चैः शङ्खचूडः प्रतापवान् ।
शक्त्या जघानोरसि तं स चकम्पे पपात कौ ॥ ४१॥

English
The valorous Śaṅkhacūḍa became infuriated and hit him on the grounds. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणेन चेतनां प्राप्य समुत्तस्थौ गणेश्वरः ।
जग्राह च धनुर्भूयो वीरभद्रो गणाग्रणीः ॥ ४२॥

English
Regaining consciousness in a trice the leader of the Gaṇas, Vīrabhadra caught hold of his bow again. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे काली जगाम समरं पुनः ।
भक्षितुं दानवान् स्वांश्च रक्षितुं कार्तिकेच्छया ॥ ४३॥

English
In the meantime Kālī went to the battle ground again at the request of Kārttikeya to devour the Dānavas and to protect her own people. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरास्तामनुजग्मुश्च ते च नन्दीश्वरादयः ।
सर्वे देवाश्च गन्धर्वा यक्षा रक्षांसि पन्नगाः ॥ ४४॥

English
Nandīśvara and other heroes, the gods, Gandharvas, Yakṣas, Rākṣasas and serpents followed her. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्यभाण्डाश्च बहुशः शतशो वाद्यवाहकाः ।
पुनः समुद्यताश्चासन् वीरा उभयतोऽखिलाः ॥ ४५॥

English
Drum-bearers and wine-carriers$^{277}$ accompanied them in hundreds. Heroic warriors on either side were active again. (45.)

[fn 277. The expression 'Maphuvāhaka' indicates that the custom of drinking wine among the fighting ranks in the battlefield prevailed even in ancient days.]
