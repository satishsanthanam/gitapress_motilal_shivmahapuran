### **CHAPTER THIRTYEIGHT - *(Kālī fights)***

---

Sanskrit
३८

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
सा च गत्वा हि सङ्ग्रामं सिंहनादं चकार ह ।
देव्याश्च तेन नादेन मूर्च्छामापुश्च दानवाः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Going to the battle ground, the goddess Kālī roared like a lion. On hearing that the Dānavas fainted. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अट्टाट्टहासमशिवं चकार च पुनः पुनः ।
तदा पपौ च माध्वीकं ननर्त रणमूर्द्धनि ॥ २॥

English
She laughed boisterously again and again boding ill to the Asuras. She drank the distilled grapewine and danced on the battle ground. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
उग्रदंष्ट्रा चोग्रदण्डा कोटवी च पपौ मधु ।
अन्याश्च देव्यस्तत्राजौ ननृतुर्मधु सम्पपुः ॥ ३॥

English
The manifestations of Durgā viz—Ugradaṃṣṭrā (one with fierce fangs) Ugradaṇḍā (one with fierce baton) and Kotavī (the naked) danced on the battle ground and drank wine. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
महान् कोलाहलो जातो गणदेवदले तदा ।
जहृषुर्बहुगर्जन्तः सर्वे सुरगणादयः ॥ ४॥

English
There was great tumult on the side of the Gaṇas and the gods. All the gods and the Gaṇas roared and rejoiced. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा कालीं शङ्खचूडः शीघ्रमाजौ समाययौ ।
दानवाश्च भयं प्राप्ता राजा तेभ्योऽभयं ददौ ॥ ५॥

English
On seeing Kāli, Śaṅkhacūḍa hastened to the battle ground. The Dānavas were frightened but the king Śaṅkhacūḍa assured them of protection. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
काली चिक्षेप वह्निं च प्रलयाग्निशिखोपमम् ।
राजा जघान तं शीघ्रं वैष्णवाङ्कितलीलया ॥ ६॥

English
Kāli hurled fire as fierce as the flame of dissolution which the king put out sportively by means of Vaiṣṇava missiles. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणास्त्रं सा देवी चिक्षेप तदुपर्यरम् ।
वृद्धिं जगाम तच्छस्त्रं दृष्ट्वा वामं च दानवम् ॥ ७॥

English
Immediately the goddess hurled the Nārāyaṇa missile at him. The missile developed its power on seeing the Dānavas Śaṅkhacūḍa. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा शङ्खचूडश्च प्रलयाग्निशिखोपमम् ।
पपात दण्डवद्भूमौ प्रणनाम पुनःपुनः ॥ ८॥

English
On realising it as fierce as the flame of fire of dissolution, the Dānavas Śaṅkhacūḍa fell flat on the ground and bowed again and again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्तिं प्राप तच्छ्स्त्रं दृष्ट्वा नम्रं च दानवम् ।
ब्रह्मास्त्रमथ सा देवी चिक्षेप मन्त्रपूर्वकम् ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing the Dānavas humbled the missile turned away. Then the goddess hurled the Brahmā missile with due invocation through the mantra.$^{278}$ (9.)

[fn 278. The reference to the missiles of unfailing effect discharged with the magic formulae indicates the heights that military science had attained in that age.]
---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा प्रज्ज्वलन्तं च प्रणम्य भुवि संस्थितः ।
ब्रह्मास्त्रेण दानवेन्द्रो विनिर्वारं चकार ह ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing the missile blazing he bowed and fell on the ground. The leader of the Dānavas thus prevented the Brahmā missile from attacking him. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ क्रुद्धो दानवेन्द्रो धनुराकृष्य रंहसा ।
चिक्षेप दिव्यान्यस्त्राणि देव्यै वै मन्त्रपूर्वकम् ॥ ११॥

English
Then the infuriated leader of the Dānavas drew the bow violently and discharged divine missiles at the goddess with due invocation through the mantras. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
आहारं समरे चक्रे प्रसार्य मुखमायतम् ।
जगर्ज साट्टहासं च दानवा भयमाययुः ॥ १२॥

English
Opening the mouth very wide she swallowed the missiles and roared with a boisterous laugh. The Dānavas were terrified. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
काल्यै चिक्षेप शक्तिं स शतयोजनमायताम् ।
देवी दिव्यास्त्रजालेन शतखण्डं चकार सा ॥ १३॥

English
He then hurled a Śakti, a hundred Yojanas long at Kāli. By means of divine missiles she broke it into a hundred pieces. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स च वैष्णवमस्त्रं च चिक्षेप चण्डिकोपरि ।
माहेश्वरेण काली च विनिर्वारं चकार सा ॥ १४॥

English
He hurled the Vaiṣṇava missile on Kāli. She blocked it with the Māheśvara missile. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं चिरतरं युद्धमन्योऽन्यं सम्बभूव ह ।
प्रेक्षका अभवन् सर्वे देवाश्च दानवा अपि ॥ १५॥

English
Thus the mutual combat went on for a long time. All the gods and Dānavas stood as mere onlookers. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ कुद्धा महादेवी काली कालसमा रणे ।
जग्राह मन्त्रपूतं च शरं पाशुपतं रुषा ॥ १६॥

English
Then the infuriated goddess Kāli, as fierce as the god of death on the battleground, took up angrily the Pāśupata arrow sanctified by mantras. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेपात्पूर्वं तन्निषेद्धुं वाग्बभूवाशरीरिणी ।
न क्षिपास्त्रमिदं देवि शङ्खचूडाय वै रुषा ॥ १७॥

English
In order to prevent it from being hurled, an un-embodied celestial voice said—“O goddess, do not hurl this missile angrily at Śaṅkhacūḍa.” (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मृत्युः पाशुपतान्नास्त्यमोघादपि च चण्डिके ।
शङ्खचूडस्य वीरस्योपायमन्यं विचारय ॥ १८॥

English
“O Caṇḍikā, death of this Dānava will not take place even through the never failing Pāśupata missile. Think of some other means for slaying this warrior Śaṅkhacūḍa.” (18.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य भद्रकाली न चिक्षेप तदस्त्रकम् ।
शतलक्षं दानवानां जघास लीलया क्षुधा ॥ १९॥

English
On hearing this, Bhadrakālī did not hurl the missile. Sportively she devoured ten million Dānavas as if in hunger. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्तुं जगाम वेगेन शङ्खचूडं भयङ्करी ।
दिव्यास्त्रेण च रौद्रेण वारयामास दानवः ॥ २०॥

English
The terrible goddess rushed at Śaṅkhacūḍa to devour him. The Dānava prevented her by means of the divine missile of Rudra. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ क्रुद्धो दानवेन्द्रः खड्गं चिक्षेप सत्वरम् ।
ग्रीष्मसूर्योपमं तीक्ष्णधारमत्यन्तभीकरम् ॥ २१॥

English
Then the infuriated leader of the Dānavas hurled a sword, as fierce as the summer sun, with sharp and terrific edge. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सा काली तं समालोक्यायान्तं प्रज्वलितं रुषा ।
प्रसार्य मुखमाहारं चक्रे तस्य च पश्यतः ॥ २२॥

English
On seeing the blazing sword approaching, Kālī furiously opened her mouth and swallowed it even as Śaṅkhacūḍa stood watching. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यान्यस्त्राणि चान्यानि चिच्छेद दानवेश्वरः ।
प्राप्तानि पूर्वतश्चक्रे शतखण्डानि तानि च ॥ २३॥

English
The lord of Dānavas hurled many divine missiles but before they reached her she broke them into hundreds of pieces. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनरत्तुं महादेवी वेगतस्तं जगाम ह ।
सर्वसिद्धेश्वरः श्रीमानन्तर्धानं चकार सः ॥ २४॥

English
Again the great goddess rushed at him in order to devour him. But that glorious Dānava, leader of all Siddhas vanished from sight. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
वेगेन मुष्टिना काली तमदृष्ट्वा च दानवम् ।
बभञ्ज च रथं तस्य जघान किल सारथिम् ॥ २५॥

English
Thus unable to see him, Kālī who rushed with great velocity crushed his chariot and killed the charioteer with her fist. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अथागत्य द्रुतं मायी चक्रं चिक्षेप वेगतः ।
भद्रकाल्यै शङ्खचूडः प्रलयाग्निशिखोपमम् ॥ २६॥

English
Then Śaṅkhacūḍa, an expert in using deception returned quickly and forcefully hurled the wheel blazing like the flame of fire of dissolution, at Bhadrakālī. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सा देवी तं तदा चक्रं वामहस्तेन लीलया ।
जग्राह स्वमुखेनैवाहारं चक्रे रुषा द्रुतम् ॥ २७॥

English
The goddess sportively caught hold of the wheel with her left hand and immediately swallowed it. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मुष्ट्या जघान तं देवी महाकोपेन वेगतः ।
बभ्राम दानवेन्द्रोऽपि क्षणं मूर्च्छामवाप सः ॥ २८॥

English
The goddess then hit him with her fist forcefully and angrily. The king of Dānavas whirled round and fainted for a short while. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणेन चेतनां प्राप्य स चोत्तस्थौ प्रतापवान् ।
न चक्रे बाहु युद्धं च मातृबुद्ध्या तया सह ॥ २९॥

English
Immediately the Dānava regained consciousness and got up valorously. He did not fight her with his arms by the thought that she was a woman like his mother. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीत्वा दानवं देवी भ्रामयित्वा पुनः पुनः ।
ऊर्ध्वं च प्रापयामास महाकोपेन वेगतः ॥ ३०॥

English
— The goddess seized the Dānava, whirled him again and again and tossed him up with great anger and velocity. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पपात च वेगेन शङ्खचूडः प्रतापवान् ।
निपत्य च समुत्तस्थो प्रणम्य भद्रकालिकाम् ॥ ३१॥

English
The valorous Śaṅkhacūḍa fell down after being tossed up very high. He got up and bowed down to Bhadrakālī. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
रत्नेन्द्रसारनिर्माणविमानं सुमनोहरम् ।
आरुरोह स हृष्टात्मा न भ्रान्तोऽपि महारणे ॥ ३२॥

English
Highly delighted thereafter, he got into a beautiful aerial chariot of exquisite workmanship set with gems and did not lose the balance of his mind in the battlefield. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
दानवानां हि क्षतजं सा पपौ कालिका क्षुधा ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र वाग्बभूवाशरीरिणी ॥ ३३॥

English
Hungrily Kālī drank the blood of the Dānavas. In the meantime an unembodied celestial voice said: (33.)

---

Sanskrit
लक्षं च दानवेन्द्राणामवशिष्टं रणेऽधुना ।
उद्धतं गुञ्जतां सार्द्धं ततस्त्वं भुङ्क्ष्व चेश्वरि ॥ ३४॥

English
O goddess, a hundred thousand haughty leading Dānavas have been left out in the battle still roaring. Devour them quickly. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्ग्रामे दानवेन्द्रं च हन्तुं न कुरु मानसम् ।
अवध्योऽयं शङ्खचूडस्तव देवीति निश्चयम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Do not think of slaying the king of Dānavas. O goddess, Śaṅkhacūḍa cannot be killed by you. It is certain. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा वचनं देवी निःसृतं व्योममण्डलात् ।
दानवानां बहूनां च मांसं च रुधिरं तथा ॥ ३६॥

भुक्त्वा पीत्वा भद्रकाली शङ्करान्तिकमाययौ ।
उवाच रणवृत्तान्तं पौर्वापर्येण सक्रमम् ॥ ३७॥

English
On hearing these words from the firmament, Bhadrakālī drank the blood and devoured the flesh of many Dānavas and went near Śiva. She then narrated to him the events of the war in the proper order. (36-37.)
