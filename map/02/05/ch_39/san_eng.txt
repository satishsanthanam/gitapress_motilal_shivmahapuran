### **CHAPTER THIRTYNINE - *(The annihilation of the army of Śaṅkhacūḍa)***

---

Sanskrit
३९

व्यास उवाच ।
श्रुत्वा काल्युक्तमीशानो किं चकार किमुक्तवान् ।
तत्त्वं वद महाप्राज्ञ परं कौतूहलं मम ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O intelligent one, on hearing the narrative of Kālī what did Śiva say? What did he do? Please narrate to me. I am eager to know it. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
काल्युक्तं वचनं श्रुत्वा शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ।
महालीलाकरः शम्भुर्जहासाश्वासयञ्च ताम् ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of Kālī, lord Śiva, the actor of great divine sports, laughed. Śiva consoled her. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
व्योमवाणीं समाकर्ण्य तत्त्वज्ञानविशारदः ।
ययौ स्वयं च समरे स्वगणैः सह शङ्करः ॥ ३॥

English
On hearing the celestial voice, Śiva, an expert in the knowledge of principles, went himself to the battle along with his Gaṇas. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
महावृषभमारूढो वीरभद्रादिसंयुतः ।
भैरवैः क्षेत्रपालैश्च स्वसमानैः समन्वितः ॥ ४॥

English
He was seated on his great bull and surrounded by Virabhadra and others, the Bhairavas and the Kṣetrapālas all equal in valour to him. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
रणं प्राप्तो महेशश्च वीररूपं विधाय च ।
विरराजाधिकं तत्र रुद्रो मूर्त इवान्तकः ॥ ५॥

English
Assuming a heroic form, lord Śiva entered the battle ground. There Śiva shone well as the embodied form of the annihilator. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडः शिवं दृष्ट्वा विमानादवरुह्य सः ।
ननाम परया भक्त्या शिरसा दण्डवद्भुवि ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing Śiva, Śaṅkhacūḍa got down from the aerial chariot, bowed with great devotion and fell flat on the ground. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तं प्रणम्य तु योगेन विमानमारुरोह सः ।
तूर्णं चकार सन्नाहं धनुर्जग्राह सेषुकम् ॥ ७॥

English
After bowing to him he immediately got into his chariot. He speedily prepared for the fight and seized the bow and the arrows. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवदानवयोर्युद्धं शतमब्दं बभूव ह ।
बाणवर्षमिवोग्रं तद्वर्षतोर्मोघयोस्तदा ॥ ८॥

English
The fight between Śiva and the Dānava went on for a hundred years and they showered arrows fiercely like clouds pouring down incessantly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडो महावीरः शरांश्चिक्षेप दारुणान् ।
चिच्छेद शङ्करस्तान्वै लीलया स्वशरोत्करैः ॥ ९॥

English
The heroic Śaṅkhacūḍa discharged terrible arrows playfully. Śiva split all of them by means of his arrows. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तदङ्गेषु च शस्त्रौघैस्ताडयामास कोपतः ।
महारुद्रो विरूपाक्षो दुष्टदण्डः सतां गतिः ॥ १०॥

English
Mahārudra, the odd-eyed Śiva, the punisher of the wicked and the goal of the good, angrily hit his limbs with various weapons. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दानवो निशितं खड्गं चर्म चादाय वेगवान् ।
वृषं जघान शिरसि शिवस्य वरवाहनम् ॥ ११॥

English
Taking up his sharp sword and the leather shield the Dānava rushed at the sacred bull of Śiva and hit it on its head. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ताडिते वाहने रुद्रस्तं क्षुरप्रेण लीलया ।
खड्गं चिच्छेद तस्याशु चर्म चापि महोज्ज्वलम् ॥ १२॥

English
When his bull was hit, Śiva sportively cut off the sword and the shining shield by means of his Kṣurapra. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
छिन्नेऽसौ चर्मणि तदा शक्तिं चिक्षेप सोऽसुरः ।
द्विधा चक्रे स्वबाणेन हरस्तां सम्मुखागताम् ॥ १३॥

English
When the shield was split, the Asura hurled his spear. Śiva split it into two with his arrow as it came before him. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कोपाध्मातः शङ्खचूडः चक्रं चिक्षेप दानवः ।
मुष्टिपातेन तच्चाप्यचूर्णयत्सहसा हरः ॥ १४॥

English
The infuriated Dānava, Śaṅkhacūḍa hurled a discus. Immediately Śiva smashed it into pieces with his fist. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
गदामाविध्य तरसा सञ्चिक्षेप हरं प्रति ।
शम्भुना सापि सहसा भिन्ना भस्मत्वमागता ॥ १५॥

English
He hurled his club with force at Śiva. Rapidly split by Śiva, the club was reduced to ashes. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः परशुमादाय हस्तेन दानवेश्वरः ।
धावति स्म हरं वेगाच्छङ्खचूडः क्रुधाकुलः ॥ १६॥

English
Then seizing an axe with his hand, the infuriated king of Dānavas, Śaṅkhacūḍa rushed at Śiva. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
समाहृत्य स्वबाणौघैरपातयत शङ्करः ।
द्रुतं परशुहस्तं तं भूतले लीलयासुरम् ॥ १७॥

English
By the volley of his arrows Śiva sportively struck the Asura with axe in his hand. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्षणेन सम्प्राप्य संज्ञामारुह्य सद्रथम् ।
धृतदिव्यायुधशरो बभौ व्याप्याखिलं नभः ॥ १८॥

English
The Dānava quickly regained consciousness and got into his excellent chariot. With divine weapons and arrows he encompassed the whole sky and shone. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
आयान्तं तं निरीक्ष्यैव डमरुध्वनिमादरात् ।
चकार ज्यारवं चापि धनुषो दुःसहं हरः ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing him coming on, Śiva sounded his Damaru enthusiastically and twanged the bowstring, the noise whereof was unbearable. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पूरयामास ककुभः श‍ृङ्गनादेन च प्रभुः ।
स्वयं जगर्ज गिरिशस्त्रासयन्नसुरांस्तदा ॥ २०॥

English
The lord filled all the quarters with the sound of his horn. Śiva himself roared then, frightening the Asuras. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
त्याजितेभमहागर्वैर्महानादैर्वृषेश्वरः ।
पूरयामास सहसा खं गां वसुदिशस्तथा ॥ २१॥

English
The lordly bull then bellowed putting the haughty trumpeting elephants to shame. The deep roar filled the sky, the earth and the eight quarters. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालः समुत्पत्य ताडयद् गां तथा नभः ।
कराभ्यां तन्निनादेन क्षिप्ता आसन्पुरा रवाः ॥ २२॥

English
With his hands the fierce lord Śiva clapped the earth and the sky. All the previous shouts and roars were surpassed by that sound. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अट्टाट्टहासमशिवं क्षेत्रपालश्चकार ह ।
भैरवोऽपि महानादं स चकार महाहवे ॥ २३॥

English
The Kṣetrapāla produced a boisterous laughing sound boding ill to the Asuras. In that great battle Bhairava too roared. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकोलाहलो जातो रणमध्ये भयङ्करः ।
वीरशब्दो बभूवाथ गणमध्ये समन्ततः ॥ २४॥

English
There was a terrific tumult in the midst of that battle. All round amongst the Gaṇas, the shouts of heroes rose up. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्त्रेसुर्दानवाःसर्वे तैः शब्दैर्भयदैः खरैः ।
चुकोपातीव तच्छ्रुत्वा दानवेन्द्रो महाबलः ॥ २५॥

English
The Dānavas were frightened by those harsh and terrible sounds. On hearing them the powerful king of Dānavas became very furious. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तिष्ठ तिष्ठेति दुष्टात्मन् व्याजहार यदा हरः ।
देवैर्गणैश्च तैः शीघ्रमुक्तं जय जयेति च ॥ २६॥

English
When Śiva shouted “O wicked one, stay by. Stay by”, the gods and the Gaṇas rapidly shouted “victory, Victory”. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथागत्य स दम्भस्य तनयः सुप्रतापवान् ।
शक्तिं चिक्षेप रुद्राय ज्वालामालातिभीषणाम् ॥ २७॥

English
Then coming again the valorous son of Dambha hurled at Rudra his spear terrible with shooting flames. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
वह्निकूटप्रभायान्ती क्षेत्रपालेन सत्वरम् ।
निरस्तागत्य साजौ वै मुखोत्पन्नमहोल्कया ॥ २८॥

English
While it came on, blazing brilliantly like a great conflagration in the battleground, it was immediately suppressed by Kṣetrapāla by means of the meteor springing from his mouth. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः प्रववृते युद्धं शिवदानवयोर्महत् ।
चकम्पे धरणी द्यौश्च सनगाब्धिजलाशया ॥ २९॥

English
Again the great battle between Śiva and the Dānavas was resumed. The heaven and the earth including all mountains, oceans and rivers shook and trembled. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
दाम्भिमुक्तान् शरान् शम्भुः शरांस्तत्प्रहितान्स च ।
सहस्रशः शरैरुग्रैश्चिच्छेद शतशस्तदा ॥ ३०॥

English
Śiva split up the arrows discharged by the son of Dambha by means of hundred and thousands of his fierce arrows. Similarly the arrows of Śiva were split up by the Dānavas. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शम्भुस्त्रिशूलेन सङ्क्रुद्धस्तं जघान ह ।
तत्प्रहारमसह्याशु कौ पपात स मूर्च्छितः ॥ ३१॥

English
Then the infuriated Śiva hit him with his trident. Unable to bear that blow he fell unconscious on the ground. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्षणेन सम्प्राप संज्ञां स च तदासुरः ।
आजघान शरै रुद्रं तान्सर्वानात्तकार्मुकः ॥ ३२॥

English
The Asura regained consciousness rapidly. He seized his bow and hit Rudra and all others by means of his arrows. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
बाहूनामयुतं कृत्वा छादयामास शङ्करम् ।
चक्रायुतेन सहसा शङ्खचूडः प्रतापवान् ॥ ३३॥

English
The valorous Śaṅkhacūḍa assumed ten thousand arms by means of magic and rapidly enveloped Śiva by means of ten thousand discuses. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दुर्गापतिः क्रुद्धो रुद्रो दुर्गार्तिनाशनः ।
तानि चक्राणि चिच्छेद स्वशरैरुत्तमैर्द्रुतम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Then Śiva, the infuriated consort of Durgā, the destroyer of all insurmountable distress split the discuses rapidly by means of his excellent arrows. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो वेगेन सहसा गदामादाय दानवः ।
अभ्यधावत वै हन्तुं बहुसेनावृतो हरम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Then the Dānava seized his mace and accompanied by a huge army rushed at Śiva with the intention to kill him. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गदां चिच्छेद तस्याश्वापततः सोऽसिना हरः ।
शितधारेण सङ्क्रुद्धो दुष्टगर्वापहारकः ॥ ३६॥

English
The infuriated Śiva, the destroyer of the pride of the wicked split the mace of the Dānava rushing headlong by means of a sharp-edged sword. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
छिन्नायां स्वगदायां च चुकोपातीव दानवः ।
शूलं जग्राह तेजस्वी परेषां दुःसहं ज्वलत् ॥ ३७॥

English
When the mace was split, the Dānava became very furious. The brilliant Dānava took up a spear that blazed unbearable to the enemies. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदर्शनं शूलहस्तमायान्तं दानवेश्वरम् ।
स्वत्रिशूलेन विव्याध हृदि तं वेगतो हरः ॥ ३८॥

English
By means of his trident Śiva hit the comely king of Dānavas rapidly in the chest even as he approached with the spear in his hand. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिशूलभिन्नहृदयान्निष्क्रान्तः पुरुषः परः ।
तिष्ठ तिष्ठेति चोवाच शङ्खचूडस्य वीर्यवान् ॥ ३९॥

English
From the chest of Śaṅkhacūḍa pierced by the trident, a valorous huge being came out and said “Stand by, Stand by”. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्क्रामतो हि तस्याशु प्रहस्य स्वनवत्ततः ।
चिच्छेद च शिरो भीममसिना सोऽपतद्भुवि ॥ ४०॥

English
Laughing noisily Śiva severed the terrible head of the being that was coming out, by means of a sword. He fell on the ground. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कालीं चखादोग्रं दंष्ट्राक्षुण्णशिरोधरान् ।
असुरांस्तान् बहून् क्रोधात् प्रसार्य स्वमुखं तदा ॥ ४१॥

English
Then spreading her mouth wide open Kāli furiously devoured innumerable Asuras whose heads were crushed by her fierce fangs. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेत्रपालश्चखादान्यान्बहून्दैत्यान्क्रुधाकुलः ।
केचिन्नेशुर्भैरवास्त्रच्छिन्ना भिन्नास्तथापरे ॥ ४२॥

English
The excited and infuriated Kṣetrapāla devoured many other Daityas. Some were killed struck down by Bhairava’s missiles. Others were wounded. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रोऽपरान् वीरान् बहून् क्रोधादनाशयत् ।
नन्दीश्वरो जघानान्यान् बहूनमरमर्दकान् ॥ ४३॥

English
Virabhadra furiously destroyed many other heroes. Nandiśvara killed many other demons. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं बहुगणा वीरास्तदा सन्नह्य कोपतः ।
व्यनाशयन्बहून्दैत्यानसुरान् देवमर्दकान् ॥ ४४॥

English
Thus the other Gaṇas, readily prepared and furiously heroic, destroyed many Daityas, Asuras and suppressors of the gods. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं बहुतरं तत्र तस्य सैन्यं ननाश तत् ।
विद्रुताश्चापरे वीरा बहवो भयकातराः ॥ ४५॥

English
Thus a major portion of his army was destroyed there. Many other soldiers, cowardly and terrified, fled. (45.)
