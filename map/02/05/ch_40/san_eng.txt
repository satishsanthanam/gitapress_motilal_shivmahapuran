### **CHAPTER FORTY - (*Śaṅkhacūḍa is slain*)**

---

Sanskrit
४०

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
स्वबलं निहतं दृष्ट्वा मुख्यं बहुतरं ततः ।
तथा वीरान् प्राणसमान् चुकोपातीव दानवः ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On seeing the important and major portion of his army killed, including heroes as dear to him as his life, the Dānava became very furious. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच वचनं शम्भुं तिष्ठाम्याजौ स्थिरो भव ।
किमेतैर्निहतैर्मेऽद्य सम्मुखे समरं कुरु ॥ २॥

English
He spoke to Śiva. “I am here standing ready. Be steady in the battle. What is it to me, if these are killed? Fight me standing face to face”. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा दानवेन्द्रोऽसौ सन्नद्धः समरे मुने ।
अगच्छन्निश्चयं कृत्वाऽभिमुखं शङ्करस्य च ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, after saying this and resolving resolutely the king of Dānavas stood ready facing Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यान्यस्त्राणि चिक्षेप महारुद्राय दानवः ।
चकार शरवृष्टिं च तोयवृष्टिं यथा घनः ॥ ४॥

English
The Dānava hurled divine missiles at him and showered arrows like the cloud pouring rain. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
मायाश्चकार विविधा अदृश्या भयदर्शिताः ।
अप्रतर्क्याः सुरगणैर्निखिलैरपिः सत्तमैः ॥ ५॥

English
He exhibited various kinds of deceptive measures invisible and inscrutable to all the excellent gods and Gaṇas and terrifying as well. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा शङ्करस्तत्र चिक्षेपास्त्रं च लीलया ।
माहेश्वरं महादिव्यं सर्वमायाविनाशनम् ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing that, Śiva sportively discharged thereat the excessively divine Māheśvara missiles that destroy all illusions. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तेजसा तस्य तन्माया नष्टाश्चासन् द्रुतं तदा ।
दिव्यान्यस्त्राणि तान्येव निस्तेजांस्यभवन्नपि ॥ ७॥

English
All the illusions, were quelled rapidly by its brilliance. Though they were divine missiles they became divested of their brilliance. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ युद्धे महेशानस्तद्वधाय महाबलः ।
शूलं जग्राह सहसा दुर्निवार्यं सुतेजसाम् ॥ ८॥

English
Then in the battle, the powerful lord Śiva suddenly seized his trident which could not be withstood even by brilliant persons, in order to slay him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव तन्निषेद्धुं च वाग्बभूवाशरीरिणी ।
क्षिप शूलं न चेदानीं प्रार्थनां श‍ृणु शङ्कर ॥ ९॥

English
In order to prevent him then, an unembodied celestial voice said—“O Śiva, do not hurl the trident now. Please listen to this request. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा त्वं समर्थो हि क्षणाद् ब्रह्माण्डनाशने ।
किमेकदानवस्येश शङ्खचूडस्य साम्प्रतम् ॥ १०॥

English
O Śiva, by all means, you are competent to destroy the entire universe in a trice. What doubt then in regard to a single Dānava Śaṅkhacūḍa ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि वेदमर्यादा न नाश्या स्वामिना त्वया ।
तां श‍ृणुष्व महादेव सफलं कुरु सत्यतः ॥ ११॥

English
Still, the limit imposed by the Vedas should not be disregarded by you, the lord. O great god, listen to that. Make it truthful and fruitful. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यावदस्य करेऽत्युग्रं कवचं परमं हरेः ।
यावत्सतीत्वमस्त्येव सत्या अस्यस्य योषितः ॥ १२॥

तावदस्य जरामृत्युः शङ्खचूडस्य शङ्कर ।
नास्तीत्यवितथं नाथ विधेहि ब्रह्मणो वचः ॥ १३॥

English
O lord Śiva, it has been mentioned by Brahmā, that, as long as he wears the armour of Viṣṇu and as long as his wife maintains the marital fidelity, Śaṅkhacūḍa has neither death nor old age. Please make those words truthful.’ (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य नभोवाणीं तथेत्युक्ते हरे तदा ।
हरेच्छयागतो विष्णुस्तं दिदेश सतां गतिः ॥ १४॥

English
On hearing this celestial voice, Śiva said “So be it”. Viṣṇu came there at the wish of Śiva. Śiva, who is the goal of the good, commanded him. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वृद्धब्राह्मणवेषेण विष्णुर्मायाविनां वरः ।
शङ्खचूडोपकण्ठं च गत्वोवाच स तं तदा ॥ १५॥

English
Then, in the guise of an old brahmin, Viṣṇu, the foremost of those who wield magic, approached Śaṅkhacūḍa and told him. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वृद्धब्राह्मण उवाच ।
देहि भिक्षां दानवेन्द्र मह्यं प्राप्ताय साम्प्रतम् ॥ १६॥

नेदानीं कथयिष्यामि प्रकटं दीनवत्सलम् ।
पश्चात्त्वां कथयिष्यामि पुनः सत्यं करिष्यसि ॥ १७॥

English
*The aged brahmin said:—*  “O lord of Dānavas, give me the alms for which I have come to you. I shall not say openly what I wish to have from you who are favourably disposed to the distressed. I shall tell you when you have promised me first. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
ओमित्युवाच राजेन्द्रः प्रसन्नवदनेक्षणः ।
कवचार्थी जनश्चाहमित्युवाचेति सच्छलात् ॥ १८॥

English
With face and eyes indicating pleasure the king replied affirmatively. Then the deceptive Viṣṇu in the form of a brahmin said—“I am the suppliant for your armour.” (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा दानवेन्द्रोऽसौ ब्रह्मण्यः सत्यवाग्विभुः ।
तद् ददौ कवचं दिव्यं विप्राय प्राणसम्मतम् ॥ १९॥

English
On hearing that, the lord of Dānavas, a well wisher of the brahmins and of truthful word handed over the divine armour, his vital breath, to the brahmin. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
माययेत्थं तु कवचं तस्माज्जग्राह वै हरिः ।
शङ्खचूडस्य रूपेण जगाम तुलसीं प्रति ॥ २०॥

English
Viṣṇu thus snatched off his armour by means of deception. Then in the guise of Śaṅkhacūḍa Viṣṇu approached Tulasi. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र हरिस्तस्या योनौ मायाविशारदः ।
वीर्याधानं चकाराशु देवकार्यार्थमीश्वरः ॥ २१॥

English
Lord Viṣṇu, an expert in wielding magic went there and deposited his semen in her vaginal passage for the protection of gods. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शम्भुमीरयन् स्ववचः प्रभुः ।
शङ्खचूडवधार्थाय शूलं जग्राह प्रज्वलत् ॥ २२॥

English
In the meantime the lord of Dānavas approached Śiva without the armour. He took up his trident that blazed to slay Śaṅkhacūḍa. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छूलं विजयं नाम शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ।
सञ्चकाशे दिशः सर्वा रोदसीं सम्प्रकाशयन् ॥ २३॥

English
That trident, named Vijaya, of Śiva, the great Ātman, shone illuminating heaven and earth. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिमध्याह्नमार्तण्डप्रलयाग्निशिखोपमम् ।
दुर्निवार्यं च दुर्द्धर्षमव्यर्थं वैरिघातकम् ॥ २४॥

English
It was as refulgent as a crore midday suns and as fierce as the shooting flame of fire at the time of dissolution. It could neither be prevented nor withstood. It was never ineffective in destroying enemies. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तेजसां चक्रमत्युग्रं सर्वशस्त्रास्त्रनायकम् ।
सुरासुराणां सर्वेषां दुःसहं च भयङ्करम् ॥ २५॥

English
It had a fierce halo all round. It was the best of all weapons and missiles. It was unbearable to gods and Asuras. It was terrible to all. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
संहर्तुं सर्वब्रह्माडमवलम्ब्य च लीलया ।
संस्थितं परमं तत्र एकत्रीभूय विज्वलत् ॥ २६॥

English
In order to annihilate the whole cosmos sportively all brilliance had converged into it. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
धनुः सहस्रं दीर्घेण प्रस्थेन शतहस्तकम् ।
जीवब्रह्मस्वरूपं च नित्यरूपमनिर्मितम् ॥ २७॥

English
It was a thousand Dhanus in length and a hundred Hastas in width. It was in the form of individual as well as universal soul. It was eternal and uncreated. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विभ्रमद् व्योम्नि तच्छूलं शङ्खचूडोपरि क्षणात् ।
चकार भस्म तच्छीघ्रं निपत्य शिवशासनात् ॥ २८॥

English
That trident whirling round over the head of Śaṅkhacūḍa for a while fell on the head of the Dānava at the behest of Śiva and reduced him to ashes. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शूलं महेशस्य द्रुतमावृत्य शङ्करम् ।
ययौ विहायसा विप्र मनोयायि स्वकार्यकृत् ॥ २९॥

English
O brahmin, then it rapidly returned to Śiva and having finished its work went away by the aerial path with the speed of the mind. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नेदुर्दुन्दुभयः स्वर्गे जगुर्गन्धर्वकिन्नराः ।
तुष्टुवुर्मुनयो देवा ननृतुश्चाप्सरोगणाः ॥ ३०॥

English
The Dundubhis were sounded in the heaven. Gandharvas and Kinnaras sang. The sages and the gods eulogised and the celestial damsels danced. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूव पुष्पवृष्टिश्च शिवस्योपरि सन्ततम् ।
प्रशशंस हरिर्ब्रह्मा शक्राद्या मुनयस्तथा ॥ ३१॥

English
A continuous shower of flowers fell over Śiva. Viṣṇu, Brahmā, Indra, other gods and sages praised him. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खचूडो दानवेन्द्रः शिवस्य कृपया तदा ।
शापमुक्तो बभूवाथ पूर्वरूपमवाप ह ॥ ३२॥

English
Śaṅkhacūḍa the king of Dānavas was released from his curse by the favour of Śiva. He regained his original form. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्थिभिः शङ्खचूडस्य शङ्खजातिर्बभूव ह ।
प्रशस्तं शङ्खतोयं च सर्वेषां शङ्करं विना ॥ ३३॥

English
All the conches in the world are formed of the bones of Śaṅkhacūḍa. Except for Śiva, the holy water from the conch is sacred for every one. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
विशेषेण हरेर्लक्ष्म्याः शङ्खतोयं महाप्रियम् ।
सम्बन्धिनां च तस्यापि न हरस्य महामुने ॥ ३४॥

English
O great sage, particularly to Viṣṇu and Lakṣmī the water from the conch is pleasant. To all persons connected with Viṣṇu it is so but not to Śiva. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तमित्थं शङ्करो हत्वा शिवलोकं जगाम सः ।
सुप्रहृष्टो वृषारूढः सोमस्कन्दगणैर्वृतः ॥ ३५॥

English
After slaying him thus, Śiva went to Śivaloka seated on his bull, joyously, accompanied by Pārvatī, Kārttikeya and the Gaṇas. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिर्जगाम वैकुण्ठं कृष्णः स्वस्थो बभूव ह ।
सुराः स्वविषयं प्रापुः परमानन्दसंयुताः ॥ ३६॥

English
Viṣṇu went to Vaikuṇṭha. Kṛṣṇa became complacent. The gods went to their abodes with great delight. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्स्वास्थ्यमतीवाप सर्वं निर्विघ्नमाप कम् ।
निर्मलं चाभवद्व्योम क्षितिः सर्वा सुमङ्गला ॥ ३७॥

English
The universe regained normalcy. The whole earth was freed of obstacles. The sky was pure. The whole world became auspicious. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रोक्तं महेशस्य चरितं प्रमुदावहम् ।
सर्वदुःखहरं श्रीदं सर्वकामप्रपूरकम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus I have narrated to you the delightful story of lord Śiva that removes all misery, yields wealth and fulfils cherished desires. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं सर्वविघ्ननिवारणम् ।
भुक्तिदं मुक्तिदं चैव सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ ३९॥

English
It is conducive to prosperity and longevity. It prevents all obstacles. It yields worldly pleasure and salvation. It confers the fruits of all cherished desires. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयान्नित्यं चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
श्रावयेद्वा पठेद्वापि पाठयेद्वा सुधीर्नरः ॥ ४०॥

धनं धान्यं सुतं सौख्यं लभेतात्र न संशयः ।
सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति शिवभक्तिं विशेषतः ॥ ४१॥

English
The intelligent man who hears or narrates the story of the moon-crested lord, or reads or teaches it shall undoubtedly derive wealth, grains, progeny, happiness, all desires and particularly devotion to Śiva. (40-41.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमतुलं सर्वोपद्रवनाशनम् ।
परमज्ञानजननं शिवभक्तिविवर्द्धनम् ॥ ४२॥

English
This narrative is unequalled. It destroys all torments. It generates great knowledge. It increases devotion to Śiva. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणो ब्रह्मवर्चस्वी क्षत्रियो विजयी भवेत् ।
धनाढ्यो वैश्यजः शूद्रः श‍ृण्वन् सत्तमतामियात् ॥ ४३॥

English
The brahmin listener attains brahminical splendour; the Kṣatriya becomes a conqueror; the Vaiśya rich and the Śūdra the most excellent of men. (43.)
