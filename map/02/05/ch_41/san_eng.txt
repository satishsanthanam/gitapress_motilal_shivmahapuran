### **CHAPTER FORTYONE - *(The curse of Tulasi)***

---

Sanskrit
४१

व्यास उवाच ।
नारायणश्च भगवान् वीर्याधानं चकार ह ।
तुलस्याः केन यत्नेन योनौ तद्वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  How did the lord Nārāyaṇa manage to deposit his semen in the vaginal passage of Tulasi? Please narrate the same. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।

शङ्खचूडस्य रूपेण रेमे तद्रामया सह ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Nārāyaṇa is the person who carries on the task of the gods. He is the goal of the good. It was in the guise of Śaṅkhacūḍa that he indulged in sexual dalliance with his wife. (2.)

Sanskrit

English
Listen to the story of Viṣṇu that causes delight, the story of Viṣṇu who acts at the behest of Śiva and Pārvatī, the mother of the worlds. (3.)

English
On hearing the aerial voice in the course of the war and urged by lord Śiva, Viṣṇu carried off the excellent armour of Śaṅkhacūḍa assuming the guise of a brahmin, rapidly, by having recourse to his Māyā. He then assumed the guise of Śaṅkhacūḍa and went to Tulasi’s palace. (4-5.)

विष्णुब्रह्मादयोऽतीता बहवः क्षणमात्रतः ॥ १७॥

कर्तुं सार्द्धं च तेनैव समरं त्वं गतः प्रभो ।
कथं बभूव सङ्ग्रामस्तेन देवसहायिना ॥ १८॥

English
*Tulasi said:—*  "O lord, how did you fare in the battle with Śiva who renders help to the gods? You had gone to fight Śiva who is the foremost of the gods, who is the annihilator of innumerable universes, whose behests are strictly adhered to and carried out by Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods always, who is the progenitor of the three deities, who is the soul of three attributes, who being devoid of attributes puts on the form of attributes at the request and wish of the devotees; who makes Viṣṇu and Brahmā work, who assumed the form of Śiva the resident of Kailāsa at the request of Kubera, who is the lord of Gaṇas, the supreme Brahman, the goal of the good; in a single moment according to whose calculation a crore cosmic worlds undergo destruction; and in whose minutest time many Viṣṇus, Brahmās and others pass away. It is with such a Śiva that you had gone to fight. (14-18.)

O Viṣṇu, Śālagrāma stone shall signify your contact with Tulasi. Similar in appearance they shall increase merit. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
तुलसीपत्रविच्छेदं शालग्रामे करोति यः ।
तस्य जन्मान्तरे भद्रे स्त्रीविच्छेदो भविष्यति ॥ ५३॥

English
O gentle lady, If anyone plucks the leaves of Tulasi lying on Śālagrāma he will be separated from his wife in the next birth. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
तुलसीपत्रविच्छेदं शङ्खं हित्वा करोति यः ।
भार्याहीनो भवेत्सोऽपि रोगी स्यात्सप्तजन्मसु ॥ ५४॥

English
If any one plucks Tulasi leaves without using conch he will become a widower and a chronic patient for seven births. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
शालग्रामश्च तुलसी शङ्खं चैकत्र एव हि ।
यो रक्षति महाज्ञानी स भवेच्छ्रीहरिप्रियः ॥ ५५॥

English
He who keeps Śālagrāma, Tulasi and Śaṅkha in one place shall become wise and a favourite of Viṣṇu. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं प्रियाः शङ्खचूडस्य चैकमन्वन्तरावधि ।
शङ्खेन सार्धं त्वद्भेदः केवलं दुःखदस्तव ॥ ५६॥

English
You have been the beloved of Śaṅkhacūḍa for the period of a Manvantara. Your separation from Śaṅkha cūḍa is really painful to you. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा शङ्करस्तत्र माहात्म्यमूचिवांस्तदा ।
शालग्रामशिलायाश्च तुलस्या बहुपुण्यदम् ॥ ५७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying so, Śiva narrated the greatness of Śālagrāma stone and Tulasi, that is highly meritorious. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चान्तर्हितो भूत्वा मोदयित्वा हरिं च ताम् ।
जगाम स्वालयं शम्भुः शर्मदो हि सदा सताम् ॥ ५८॥

English
After delighting Viṣṇu and Tulasi, Śiva the benefactor of the good vanished from there and went to his abode. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा वचः शम्भोः प्रसन्ना तु तुलस्यभूत् ।
तद्देहं च परित्यज्य दिव्यरूपा बभूव ह ॥ ५९॥

English
On hearing the words of Śiva, Tulasi was delighted. She cast off that body and assumed a divine form. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजगाम तया सार्द्धं वैकुण्ठं कमलापतिः ।
सद्यस्तद्देहजाता च बभूव गण्डकी नदी ॥ ६०॥

English
The lord of Lakṣmī went to Vaikuṇṭha with her. Immediately the river Gaṇḍaki took its origin from her cast off body. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलोऽभूदच्युतः सोऽपि तत्तीरे पुण्यदो नृणाम् ।
कुर्वन्ति तत्र कीटाश्च छिद्रं बहुविधं मुने ॥ ६१॥

English
On its banks Viṣṇu became a mountain conferring merit on men. O sage, germs make different kinds of holes therein. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
जले पतन्ति यास्तत्र शिलास्तास्त्वतिपुण्यदाः ।
स्थलस्था पिङ्गला ज्ञेयाश्चोपतापाय चैव हि ॥ ६२॥

English
The pieces that fall into the water are highly meritorious. Those that remain on the dry land are known as Piṅgalas. They are harmful. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं कथितं सर्वं तव प्रश्नानुसारतः ।
चरितं पुण्यदं शम्भोः सर्वकामप्रदं नृणाम् ॥ ६३॥

English
Thus I have narrated everything in accordance with your enquiry. The story of Śiva is meritorious and bestows all cherished desires on men. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
आख्यानमिदमाख्यातं विष्णुमाहात्म्यमिश्रितम् ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं पुण्यं किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ६४॥

English
This narrative has been made in detail intermingled with the greatness of Viṣṇu. It confers worldly pleasures and salvation. What is it that you wish to hear further? (64.)
