### **CHAPTER FORTYTHREE - (*Hiranyakasipu is slain*)**

---

Sanskrit
४३

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ हते तस्मिन्सुरद्रुहि ।
किमकार्षीत्ततस्तस्य ज्येष्ठभ्राता महासुरः ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O Santakumāra of great intellect, when that Asura was killed what did his elder brother, the great Asura, do? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कुतूहलमिति श्रोतुं ममास्तीह मुनीश्वर ।
तच्छ्रावय कृपां कृत्वा ब्रह्मपुत्र नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २॥

English
O great sage, I am eager to hear this. O son of Brahmā, please narrate the same. Obeisance be to you. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य व्यासस्य स मुनीश्वरः ।
सनत्कुमारः प्रोवाच स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing these words of Vyāsa, that great sage, Sanatkumāra spoke after remembering the lotus like feet of Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
भ्रातर्येवं विनिहते हरिणा क्रोडमूर्तिना ।
हिरण्यकशिपुर्व्यास पर्यतप्यद्रुषा शुचा ॥ ४॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  When his brother was thus killed by Viṣṇu in the form of a Boar, O Vyāsa, Hiraṇyakaśipu was distressed with grief and excited by anger. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रजानां कदनं विधातुं कदनप्रियान् ।
निर्दिदेशाऽसुरान्वीरान्हरिवैरप्रियो हि सः ॥ ५॥

English
Always fond of enmity with Viṣṇu that he was, he instigated heroic Asuras, fond of havoc, to work havoc among the people. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते भर्तृसन्देशमादाय शिरसाऽसुराः ।
देवप्रजानां कदनं विदधुः कदनप्रियाः ॥ ६॥

English
Receiving the command of their lord with bowed heads, the Asura fond of havoc worked havoc among the gods and the people. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विप्रकृते लोकेऽसुरैस्तैर्दुष्टमानसैः ।
दिवं देवाः परित्यज्य भुवि चेरुरलक्षिताः ॥ ७॥

English
Thus when the universe was utterly disturbed by the evil-minded Asuras, the gods abandoned heaven and roamed on the Earth unobserved. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यकशिपुर्भ्रातुः सम्परेतस्य दुःखितः ।
कृत्वा करोदकादीनि तत्कलत्राद्यसान्त्वयत् ॥ ८॥

English
After performing the obsequies and water obla-²⁹¹ tions to his departed brother, the distressed Hiraṇyakaśipu consoled his wife and others. (8.)

[fn 291. A handful of water mixed with sesamum is offered to the departed soul.]
---

Sanskrit
ततःस दैत्यराजेन्द्रो ह्यजेयमजरामरम् ।
आत्मानमप्रतिद्वन्द्वमेकराज्यं व्यधित्सत ॥ ९॥

English
Then the emperor of the Daityas desired to make himself invincible, undying, unageing, unrivalled and sole ruler. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स तेपे मन्दरद्रोण्यां तपः परमदारुणम् ।
ऊर्ध्वबाहुर्नभोदृष्टिः षादाङ्गुष्ठाश्रितावनिः ॥ १०॥

English
He performed a severe penance in the ravine of the Mandara mountain. Keeping his arms lifted up he fixed his eyes on the sky. He stood on the Earth on his big toes alone. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिंस्तपस्तप्यमाने देवाःसर्वे बलान्विताः ।
दैत्यान्सर्वान्विनिर्जित्य स्वानि स्थानानि भेजिरे ॥ ११॥

English
When he was performing penance, the gods accompanied by their forces defeated the Daityas and regained their lost seats. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य मूर्ध्नः समुद्भूतः सधूमोऽग्निस्तपोमयः ।
तिर्यगूर्ध्वमधोलोकानतपद्विष्वगीरितः ॥ १२॥

English
The smoking fire of penance springing from his head, spreading all round scorched the worlds all round, above and below. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन तप्ता दिवं त्यक्त्वा ब्रह्मलोकं ययुः सुराः ।
धात्रे विज्ञापयामासुस्तत्तपोविकृताननाः ॥ १३॥

English
The gods scorched by that, abandoned heaven and went to Brahmā's region. With their faces turned pale and deformed by his penance they informed the creator of every thing. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विज्ञापितो देवैर्व्यास तैरात्मभूर्विधिः ।
परीतो भृगुदक्षाद्यैर्ययौ दैत्येश्वराश्रमम् ॥ १४॥

English
O Vyāsa, thus informed by the gods, the self-born Brahmā went to the hermitage of the Daitya accompanied by Bhr̥gu, Dakṣa and others. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रताप्य लोकानखिलांस्ततोऽसौ
समागतं पद्मभवं ददर्श ।
वरं हि दातुं तमुवाच धाता
वरं वृणीष्वेति पितामहोऽपि ॥

निशम्य वाचं मधुरां विधातु-
र्वचोऽब्रवीदेवममूढबुद्धिः ॥ १५॥

English
The Asura who had already scorched the worlds saw that the lotus-born deity had arrived. In order to grant him the boon Dhāṭr the grandfather of the worlds said—“Choose a boon.” On hearing the sweet words of the creator, the Asura of undismayed intellect spoke thus. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यकशिपुरुवाच ।
मृत्योर्भयं मे भगवन्प्रजेश
पितामहाभून्न कदापि देव ।
शस्त्रास्त्रपाशाशनिशुष्कवृक्ष-
गिरीन्द्रतोयाग्निरिपुप्रहारैः ॥ १६॥

देवैश्च दैत्यैर्मुनिभिश्च सिद्धैः
त्वत्सृष्टजीवैर्बहुवाक्यतः किम् ।
स्वर्गे धरण्यां दिवसे निशायां
नैवोर्ध्वतो नाप्यधतः प्रजेश ॥ १७॥

English
*Hiranyakaśipu said:—*  “O creator, O lord of subjects, never may I have the fear of death from weapons, missiles, thunderbolts, dry trees, mountains, water, fire and onslaught of enemies—gods, Daityas, sages, Siddhas or in fact from any living being created by you. Why should I expatiate on it? Let there be no death for me in heaven, on earth, in the day time, at night, from above or below, O lord of subjects!” (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तस्यैतदीदृग्वचनं निशम्य
दैत्येन्द्र तुष्टोऽस्मि लभस्व सर्वम् ।
प्रणम्य विष्णुं मनसा तमाह
दयान्वितोऽसाविति पद्मयोनिः ॥ १८॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing these words of the Asura, the merciful lotus-born deity bowed to Viṣṇu mentally and spoke—“O lord of Daityas, I am delighted. Attain everything. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अलं तपस्ते परिपूर्णकामः
समाः सहस्राणि च षण्णवत्यः ।
उत्तिष्ठ राज्यं कुरु दानवानां
श्रुत्वा गिरं तत्सुमुखो बभूव ॥ १९॥

English
Stop your penance which has already run on to ninetysix thousand years. You have realised your desires entirely. Stand up. Rule over the kingdom of the Dānavas.” On hearing these words, the Asura was pleased and beaming in his face. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
राज्याभिषिक्तः प्रपितामहेन
त्रैलोक्यनाशाय मतिं चकार ।
उत्साद्य धर्मान् सकलान्प्रमत्तो
जित्वाहवे सोऽपि सुरान्समस्तान् ॥ २०॥

English
He was coronated by Brahmā the great grandfather of the worlds. He became inclined to destroy the three worlds. The highly elated Asuras disturbed all righteous activities and defeated all the gods in battle. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो भयाद् इन्द्रमुखाश्च देवाः
पितामहाज्ञां समवाप्य सर्वे ।
उपद्रुता दैत्यवरेण जाताः
क्षीरोदधिं यत्र हरिस्तु शेते ॥ २१॥

English
Then the terrified Indra and other gods harassed by him got the permission of Brahmā and went to the milk ocean$^{292}$ where Viṣṇu was lying. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
आराधयामासुरतीव विष्णुं
स्तुत्वा वचोभिः सुखदं हि मत्वा ।
निवेदयामासुरथो प्रसन्नं
दुःखं स्वकीयं सकलं हि ते ते ॥ २२॥

English
Considering him the bestower of happiness, they propitiated and eulogised Viṣṇu with various hymns. When he was pleased they told him their woeful tale. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा तदीयं सकलं हि दुःखं
तुष्टो रमेशः प्रददौ वरांस्तु ।
उत्थाय तस्माच्छयनादुपेन्द्रो
निजानुरूपैर्विविधैर्वचोभिः ॥ २३॥

आश्वास्य देवानखिलान्मुनीन्वा
उवाच वैश्वानरतुल्यतेजाः ।
दैत्यं हनिष्ये प्रसभं सुरेशाः
प्रयात धामानि निजानि तुष्टाः ॥ २४॥

English
On learning their misery in entirety, Viṣṇu, the delighted lord of Lakṣmī granted them boons. Getting up from his couch, Viṣṇu consoled the gods and the sages by means of different words befitting himself. The lord as refulgent as the fire said—“O leading gods, I shall kill the Daitya with force. Return to your own abodes fully assured, all of you”. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा रमेशस्य वचः सुरेशाः
शक्रादिकास्ते निखिलाः सुतुष्टाः ।
ययुः स्वधामानि हिरण्यनेत्रा-
नुजं च मत्वा निहतं मुनीश ॥ २५॥

English
O great sage, on hearing the words of Viṣṇu, Indra and other leading gods, fully assured and satisfied, went to their abodes thinking that the younger brother Hiraṇyākṣa was already killed. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
आश्रित्य रूपं जटिलं करालं
दंष्ट्रायुधं तीक्ष्णनखं सुनासम् ।
सैंहं च नारं सुविदारितास्यं
मार्तण्डकोटिप्रतिमं सुघोरम् ॥ २६॥

युगान्तकालाग्निसमप्रभावं
जगन्मयं किं बहुभिर्वचोभिः ।
अस्ते रवौ सोऽपि हि गच्छतीशो
गतोऽसुराणां नगरीं महात्मा ॥ २७॥

English
The noble soul Viṣṇu assumed the form partly of lion and partly of man. His head was matted and full of manes. Sharp fangs were his weapons. The claws were keen and pointed. The snout was finely shaped. The mouth was wide open. The body was terrible and refulgent like a crore of suns, blazing and powerful like the fire at the time of dissolution. He was identical with the universe. More words need not be used to describe him. When the sun was about to set, the lord went to the city of the Asuras. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा च युद्धं प्रबलैःस दैत्यै-
र्हत्वाथ तान्दैत्यगणान्गृहीत्वा ।
बभ्राम तत्राद्भुतविक्रमश्च

English
The Man-lion fought with the powerful Daityas. He killed many of them. He held them up and whirled. Exhibiting wonderful prowess he smashed and crushed the various Asuras. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

सिंहं च तं सर्वमयं निरीक्ष्य
प्रह्लादनामा दितिजेन्द्रपुत्रः ।
उवाच राजानमयं मृगेन्द्रो
जगन्मयः किं समुपागतश्च ॥ २९॥

English
On seeing that omniformed lion, the son of the lord of Daityas, Prahlāda,²⁹³ said to the king, his father. *Prahlāda said:—* “Is it the universe-formed lord who has come as the majestic lion? (29.)

[fn ²⁹³. Though son of the Asura Hiraṇyakaśipu, Prahlāda was an ardent devotee of Viṣṇu. He had to suffer much at the hands of his cruel father for devotion to Viṣṇu. It was to avenge Prahlāda that Viṣṇu incarnated as the man-lion and killed Hiraṇyakaśipu.]

Sanskrit
प्रह्लाद उवाच ।

नृसिंहमात्रो नगरं त्वदन्तः ।

English

[fn ²⁹⁴. The story relates to Hiraṇyakaśipu the younger brother of Hiraṇyākṣa. Hiraṇyakaśipu was proud of his prowess and as blessed by Śiva, could not be killed in an ordinary way. He struck terror in the hearts of the gods whom he had ousted from their vantage positions. He was killed by Viṣṇu assuming the form of Man-lion.]

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

---

कृतस्वकार्यं भगवन्तमीड्यम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Then the gods were delighted. They bowed to lord Viṣṇu who had finished their task and who deserved worship, O Brahmin. Thereafter Brahmā and others returned to their abodes. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवर्णितं त्वन्धकजन्म रुद्राद्
हिरण्यनेत्रस्य मृतिर्वराहात् ।
नृसिंहतस्तत्सहजस्य नाशः
प्रह्लादराज्याप्तिरिति प्रसङ्गात् ॥ ४२॥

English
Thus incidentally, I have narrated to you the story of Andhaka's birth from Rudra, the death of Hiraṇyākṣa at the hands of the Boar, the annihilation of his brother Hiraṇyakaśipu by the Man-lion and the coronation of Prahlāda. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु त्विदानीं द्विजवर्य मत्तोऽ-
न्धकप्रभावं भवकृत् प्रलब्धम् ।
हरेण युद्धं खलु तस्य पश्चाद्
गणाधिपत्यं गिरिशस्य तस्य ॥ ४३॥

English
O foremost of brahmins, now listen to the prowess of Andhaka secured from the creator, his fight with Śiva and his acquisition afterwards of the leadership of the Gaṇas. (43.)
