### **CHAPTER FORTYSEVEN - *(Description of swallowing Śukra)***

---

Sanskrit
४७

व्यास उवाच ।
तस्मिन्महति सङ्ग्रामे दारुणे लोमहर्षणे ।
शुक्रो दैत्यपतिर्विद्वान् भक्षितस्त्रिपुरारिणा ॥ १॥

इति श्रुतं समासान्मे तत्पुनर्ब्रूहि विस्तरात् ।
किं चकार महायोगी जठरस्थः पिनाकिनः ॥ २॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  This has been succinctly heard by me that in the great terrible battle that caused horripilation, Śukra the wise lord of the Daityas was swallowed by Śiva, the enemy of the Tripuras. Please narrate in detail what that great Yogin, stationed in the stomach of the trident-bearing lord Śiva did. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
न ददाह कथं शम्भोः शुक्रं तं जठरानलः ।
कल्पान्तदहनः कालो दीप्ततेजाश्च भार्गवः ॥ ३॥

विनिष्क्रान्तः कथं धीमान् शम्भोर्जठरपञ्जरात् ।
कथमाराधयामास कियत्कालं स भार्गवः ॥ ४॥

English
How was it that the gastric fire of Śiva, that terrible fire of dissolution at the end of Kalpa did not burn Śukra? How did the intelligent Śukra of bright refulgence, come out of the stomach of Śiva. How long and in what manner did Śukra propitiate him? (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं च लब्धवान्विद्यां तां मृत्युशमनीं पराम् ।
का सा विद्या परा तात यया मृत्युर्हि वार्यते ॥ ५॥

English
How did he obtain that great knowledge that suppresses death? O dear one, what is that knowledge which prevents death. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
लेभेऽन्धको गाणपत्यं कथं शूलाद्विनिर्गतः ।
देवदेवस्य वै शम्भोर्मुनेर्लीलाविहारिणः ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, how did Andhaka get the position of the chieftain of Gaṇas after being released from the trident of Śiva, the lord of the gods, indulging in divine sports. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वमशेषेण महाधीमन् कृपां कुरु ।
शिवलीलामृतं तात श‍ृण्वतः कथयस्व मे ॥ ७॥

English
O highly intelligent one, please be merciful. Mention all these sweet sports entirely to me who listen with attention. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा व्यासस्यामिततेजसः ।
सनत्कुमारः प्रोवाच स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ८॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* On hearing these words of Vyāsa of immeasurable refulgence, Sanatkumāra remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and said. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाबुद्धे शिवलीलामृतं परम् ।
धन्यस्त्वं शैवमुख्योऽसि ममानन्दकरः स्वतः ॥ ९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O Vyāsa of great intellect, listen to the nectar-like sport of Śiva. You are the foremost of devotees of Śiva and you delight me. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवर्तमाने समरे शङ्करान्धकयोस्तयोः ।
अनिर्भेद्यपविव्यूहगिरिव्यूहाधिनाथयोः ॥ १०॥

पुरा जयो बभूवापि दैत्यानां बलशालिनाम् ।
शिवप्रभावादभवत्प्रमथानां मुने जयः ॥ ११॥

English
When the battle began between Śiva and Andhaka, the leaders who had formed their armies in the unpiercing arrays of thunderbolt and the mountain, the powerful Daityas became victorious at first but O sage, by the power of Śiva, the Pramathas became victorious afterwards. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वासीद्विषण्णो हि महादैत्योऽन्धकासुरः ।
कथं स्यान्मे जय इति विचारणपरोऽभवत् ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing that the Asura Andhaka was dejected. He began to think "How can I be victorious?" (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अपसृत्य ततो युद्धादन्धकः परबुद्धिमान् ।
द्रुतमभ्यगमद्वीर एकलः शुक्रसन्निधिम् ॥ १३॥

English
Going away from the battle ground the heroic and intelligent Andhaka went immediately to Śukra, unattended by anyone else. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य स्वगुरुं काव्यमवरुह्य रथाच्च सः ।
बभाषेदं विचार्याथ साञ्जलिर्नीतिवित्तमः ॥ १४॥

English
Getting down from his chariot, he bowed to his preceptor. He was foremost among the politicians, pondered well and spoke with palms joined in reverence. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्धक उवाच ।
भगवंस्त्वामुपाश्रित्य गुरोर्भावं वहामहे ।
पराजिता भवामो नो सर्वदा जयशालिनः ॥ १५॥

English
*Andhaka said:—*  O holy lord, after resorting to you we feel the respect due to a preceptor towards you. We are never vanquished. We are always victorious. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्प्रभावात्सदा देवान्समस्तान्सानुगान्वयम् ।
मन्यामहे हरोपेन्द्रमुखानपि हि कत्तृणान् ॥ १६॥

English
Due to our power we consider all the gods and their followers including Śiva and Viṣṇu as insignificant as the useless blades of grass. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मत्तो बिभ्यति सुराः तदा भवदनुग्रहात् ।
गजा इव हरिभ्यश्च तार्क्ष्येभ्य इव पन्नगाः ॥ १७॥

English
Due to your blessings, the gods are afraid of us as the elephants of the lions and the serpents of Garuḍas. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अनिर्भेद्यं पविव्यूहं विविशुर्दैत्यदानवाः ।
प्रमथानीकमखिलं विधूय त्वदनुग्रहात् ॥ १८॥

English
By smashing the entire host of the Pramathas, by your grace, the Daityas and the Dānavas have entered the impenetrable thunderbolt Vyūha. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
वयं त्वच्छरणा भूत्वा सदा गा इव निश्चलाः ।
स्थित्वा चरामो निःशङ्कमाजावपि हि भार्गव ॥ १९॥

English
O Bhārgava, by seeking refuge in you we roam about unhesitatingly in the battle ground, like the cows grazing in the field fearlessly. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षरक्षाभितो विप्र प्रव्रज्य शरणागतान् ।
असुरान् शत्रुभिर्वीरैरर्दितांश्च मृतानपि ॥ २०॥

English
But now the Asuras are harassed by the heroic enemy. They are killed. Save, save us O, Brahmin, who have sought refuge in you. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमैर्भीमविक्रान्तैः क्रान्तान्मृत्युप्रमाथिभिः ।
सूदितान्पतितान्पश्य हुण्डादीन्मद्गणान्वरान् ॥ २१॥

English
See Huṇḍa and others, leading lieutenants of mine who have been slain or who have fallen. They have been attacked by Pramathas of terrible valour who can destroy even death. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पीत्वा कणधूमं वै सहस्रं शरदां पुरा ।
त्वया प्राप्ता वरा विद्या तस्याः कालोऽयमागतः ॥ २२॥

English
Formerly you did a great penance drinking the smoke of husks or eating bits of grain$^{308}$ for a thousand years and secured a great lore. Now the opportunity has arrived to put it to a practical use. (22.)

[fn 308. Kaṇadhūma is a kind of penance wherein the performer sustains himself on the grains of corn or balls of iron-ash (lohagulika).]
---

Sanskrit
अद्य विद्याफलं तत्ते सर्वे पश्यन्तु भार्गव ।
प्रमथा असुरान्सर्वान् कृपया जीवयिष्यतः ॥ २३॥

English
O Bhārgava, let all the Pramathas see the fruit of your lore as you will kindly resuscitate the Asuras by that lore. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्थमन्धकवाक्यं स श्रुत्वा धीरो हि भार्गवः ।
तदा विचारयामास दूयमानेन चेतसा ॥ २४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing the words of Andhaka, the perplexed Bhārgava began to reflect sadly. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कर्तव्यं मयाद्यापि क्षेमं मे स्यात्कथं त्विति ।
सन्निपातविधिर्जीवः सर्वथानुचितो मम ॥ २५॥

English
“What shall I do? What will benefit me? Any living being has various activities to be performed. It may seem improper to me. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्येयं शङ्करात्प्राप्ता तद्गुणान् प्रति योजये ।
तद्रणे मर्दितान्वीरैः प्रमथैः शङ्करानुगैः ॥ २६॥

English
This Vidyā has been derived from Śiva and I am going to use it on the heroes suppressed by the heroic Pramathas, the followers of Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शरणागतधर्मोऽथ प्रवरः सर्वतो हृदा ।
विचार्य शुक्रेण धिया तद्वाणी स्वीकृता तदा ॥ २७॥

English
It is my duty to protect those who seek refuge in me.” After thinking like this, the proposal of Andhaka was accepted by Śukra. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
किञ्चित्स्मितं तदा कृत्वा सोऽब्रवीद्दानवाधिपम् ।
भार्गवः शिवपादाब्जं स्मृत्वा स्वस्थेन चेतसा ॥ २८॥

English
Smiling slightly and thinking upon the lotus like feet of Śiva with a peaceful mind, Bhārgava spoke to the ruler of Dānavas. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्र उवाच ।
यत्त्वया भाषितं तात तत्सर्वं तथ्यमेव हि ।
एतद्विद्योपार्जनं हि दानवार्थं कृतं मया ॥ २९॥

English
*Śukra said:—*  O dear, what has been mentioned by you is entirely true. I have acquired this lore just for the welfare of the Dānavas. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःसहं कणधूमं वै पीत्वा वर्षसहस्रकम् ।
विद्येयमीश्वरात्प्राप्ता बन्धूनां सुखदा सदा ॥ ३०॥

English
Drinking the unbearable smoke of husks or eating bits of grains for a thousand years, this lore has been acquired from Śiva. It is pleasing and beneficent to you my kinsmen. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमथैर्मथितान्दैत्यान् रणेऽहं विद्ययानया ।
उत्थापयिष्ये म्लानानि शस्यानि जलभुग्यथा ॥ ३१॥

English
With this lore, I shall revive the Daityas destroyed in the battle by the Pramathas just like the cloud that revives scorched plants. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्व्रणान्नीरुजः स्वस्थान् सुप्त्वेव पुनरुत्थितान् ।
मुहूर्तेऽस्मिंश्च द्रष्टासि दैत्यांस्तानुत्थितान्निजान् ॥ ३२॥

English
Within a Muhūrta you will see these Daityas as if waking from sleep, healed from wounds, devoid of pain and very healthy. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा सोऽधकं शुक्रो विद्यामावर्तयत्कविः ।
एकैकं दैत्यमुद्दिश्य स्मृत्वा विद्येशमादरात् ॥ ३३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this to Andhaka, Śukra repeated the mantra once for each of the Daityas after thinking upon lord Śiva. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्यावर्तनमात्रेण ते सर्वे दैत्यदानवाः ।
उत्तस्थुर्युगपद्वीराः सुप्ता इव धृतायुधाः ॥ ३४॥

English
As soon as the mantra was repeated, the Daityas and Dānavas rose up simultaneously as if from sleep, with the weapons lifted in their hands. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाभ्यस्ता यथा वेदाः समये वा यथाम्बुदाः ।
श्रद्धयार्थास्तथा दत्ता ब्राह्मणेभ्यो यथापदि ॥ ३५॥

English
They rose up like the merit of men who give water to the thirsty during the fight or like charity given to brahmins at the time of distress in the spirit of faith. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
उज्जीवितांस्तु तान्दृष्ट्वा हुण्डादींश्च महासुरान् ।
विनेदुरसुराः सर्वे जलपूर्णा इवाम्बुदाः ॥ ३६॥

English
On seeing Huṇḍa and other Asuras revived to life, the Asuras shouted loudly like clouds laden with water. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
रणोद्यताः पुनश्चासन्गर्जन्तो विकटान् रवान् ।
प्रमथैः सह निर्भीता महाबलपराक्रमाः ॥ ३७॥

English
Roaring with awful sounds the fearless valorous Asuras got ready to fight with Pramathas. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्रेणोज्जीवितान्दृष्ट्वा प्रमथा दैत्यदानवान् ।
विसिष्मिरे ततः सर्वे नन्द्याद्या युद्धदुर्मदाः ॥ ३८॥

English
On seeing the Daityas and Dānavas resuscitated to life by Śukra, Nandin and other Pramathas, very haughty and invincible were surprised. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञाप्यमेवं कर्मैतद्देवेशे शङ्करेऽखिलम् ।
विचार्य बुद्धिमन्तश्च ह्येवं तेऽन्योन्यमब्रुवन् ॥ ३९॥

English
After pondering intelligently they consulted one another—“This activity shall be mentioned to Śiva the lord of gods”. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
आश्चर्यरूपे प्रमथेश्वराणां
तस्मिंस्तथा वर्तति युद्धयज्ञे ।
अमर्षितो भार्गवकर्म दृष्ट्वा
शिलादपुत्रोऽभ्यगमन्महेशम् ॥ ४०॥

English
When the sacrifice of war was going on like that, kindling the surprise of the leading Pramathas, Nandin, the son of Śilāda who was infuriated on seeing the work of Bhārgava approached lord Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
जयेति चोक्त्वा जययोनिमुग्र-
मुवाच नन्दी कनकावदातम् ।
गणेश्वराणां रणकर्म देव
देवैश्च सेन्द्रैरपि दुष्करं यत् ॥ ४१॥

तद्भार्गवेणाद्य कृतं वृथा नः
सञ्जीवितांस्तान्हि मृतान्विपक्षान् ।
आवर्त्य विद्यां मृतजीवदात्री-
मेकैकमुद्दिश्य सहेलमीश ॥ ४२॥

English
After saying “Be victorious” to Śiva, the cause of victory and golden-complexioned, Nandin said—“O lord, the warlike activities of leading Gaṇas, which are difficult even for the gods including Indra, to perform, have been rendered futile by Bhārgava. O lord, after repeating the lore that revives the dead to life, one for each, all the dead enemies have been playfully resuscitated. (41-42.)

---

Sanskrit
तुहुण्डहुण्डादिककुम्भजम्भ-
विपाकपाकादिमहासुरेन्द्राः ।
यमालयादद्य पुनर्निवृत्ता
विद्रावयन्तः प्रमथांश्चरन्ति ॥ ४३॥

English
The great Asuras, viz., Tuhuṇḍa, Huṇḍa, Kumbha, Jambha, Vipāka, Pāka and others have returned from Yama’s abode. They are routing the Pramathas and are roaming about. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि ह्यसौ दैत्यवरान्निरस्तान्
सञ्जीवयेदत्र पुनः पुनस्तान् ।
जयः कुतो नो भविता महेश
गणेश्वराणां कुत एव शान्तिः ॥ ४४॥

English
O lord Śiva, how can we be victorious if he were to revive to life all the Daityas killed by us, again and again? How can there be peace to the leading Gaṇas? (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्येवमुक्तः प्रमथेश्वरेण
स नन्दिना वै प्रमथेश्वरेशः ।
उवाच देवः प्रहसंस्तदानीं
तं नन्दिनं सर्वगणेशराजम् ॥ ४५॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On being thus addressed by the leading Pramatha, Nandin, the lord of leading Pramathas laughingly spoke to Nandin, the chief of all lords of Gaṇas. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
नन्दिन्प्रयाहि त्वरितोऽति मात्रं
द्विजेन्द्रवर्यं दितिनन्दनानाम् ।
मध्यात्समुद्धृत्य तथानयाशु
श्येनो यथा लावकमण्डजातम् ॥ ४६॥

English
*Śiva said:—* O Nandin, go very quickly and seize that great brahmin from the midst of the Daityas and bring him here like a vulture bringing the bird quail. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
स एवमुक्तो वृषभध्वजेन
ननाद नन्दी वृषसिंहनादः ।
जगाम तूर्णं च विगाह्य सेनां
यत्राभवद्भार्गववंशदीपः ॥ ४७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus commanded by the bull-bannered deity Nandin bellowed like a bull and roared like a lion. Moving fast through the army he reached the place where the bright scion of the family of Bhārgavas was sitting. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
तं रक्ष्यमाणं दितिजैः समस्तैः
पाशासिवृक्षोपलशैलहस्तैः ।
विक्षोभ्य दैत्यान् बलवान् जहार
काव्यं स नन्दी शरभो यथेभम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Harassing and exciting the Daityas, Nandin snatched off Śukra well guarded by all the Daityas who had in their hands, nooses, swords trees, boulders and mountains, like the mythological animal Śarabha snatching off an elephant. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
स्रस्ताम्बरं विच्युतभूषणं च
विमुक्तकेशं बलिना गृहीतम् ।
विमोचयिष्यन्त इवानुजग्मुः
सुरारयस्सिंहरवांस्त्यजन्तः ॥ ४९॥

English
The demons followed him roaring like lions to get him released even as his cloth became loose, his ornaments fell off and his tuft of hair got untied as he was caught tightly by the strong Nandin. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
दम्भोलिशूलासिपरश्वधाना-
मुद्दण्डचक्रोपलकम्पनानाम् ।
नन्दीश्वरस्योपरि दानवेन्द्रा
वर्षं ववर्षुर्जलदा इवोग्रम् ॥ ५०॥

English
The leading Dānavas showered on Nandiśvara, thunderbolts, spears, swords, axes, staffs, discus and other missiles like clouds making a fierce downpour. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
तं भार्गवं प्राप्य गणाधिराजो
मुखाग्निना शस्त्रशतानि दग्ध्वा ।
आयात्प्रवृद्धेऽसुरदेवयुद्धे
भवस्य पार्श्वे व्यथितारिपक्षः ॥ ५१॥

English
When the combat between the Asuras and gods deepened, the chief of Gaṇas burnt hundreds of the weapons of the enemy by the fire originating from his mouth and reached Śiva taking Bhārgava with him. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं स शुक्रो भगवन्नितीदं
निवेदयामास भवाय शीघ्रम् ।
जग्राह शुक्रं स च देवदेवो
यथोपहारं शुचिना प्रदत्तम् ॥ ५२॥

English
“O lord, here is Bhārgava”, saying this he handed him over to Śiva immediately. The lord of gods caught hold of Bhārgava like a present offered by a devotee. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
न किञ्चिदुक्त्वा स हि भूतगोप्ता
चिक्षेप वक्त्रे फलवत्कवीन्द्रम् ।
हाहारवस्तैरसुरैःसमस्तै-
रुच्चैर्विमुक्तो हहहेति भूरि ॥ ५३॥

English
Without saying anything, the protector of the Bhūtas, thrust Bhārgava into his mouth like a fruit. The Asuras shouted loudly “Alas ! Alas.” (53.)
