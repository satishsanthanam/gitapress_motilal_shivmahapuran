### **CHAPTER FIFTYONE - *(The story of Ūṣā)***

---

Sanskrit
५१

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ श्राविता सुकथाद्भुता ।
भवतानुग्रहात्प्रीत्या शम्भ्वनुग्रहनिर्भरा ॥ १॥

इदानीं श्रोतुमिच्छामि चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
गाणपत्यं ददौ प्रीत्या यथा बाणासुराय वै ॥ २॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, a wonderfully excellent story has been narrated with love and blessings by you. It is full of Śiva’s benediction. I wish to know more of the story of the moon-crested lord wherein he gave the Asura Bāṇa the position of the chieftain of his Gaṇas. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यासादरात्तां वै कथां शम्भोः परात्मनः ।
गाणपत्यं यथा प्रीत्या ददौ बाणासुराय हि ॥ ३॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O Vyāsa, listen with reverence to the story of Śiva, the great Ātman wherein it is explained how he bestowed the chieftainship of his Gaṇas on the Asura Bāṇa. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रैव सुचरित्रं च शङ्करस्य महाप्रभोः ।
कृष्णेन समरोप्यत्र शम्भोर्बाणानुगृह्णतः ॥ ४॥

English
Here is the good story of Śiva, the great lord. Here too is the story of Śiva’s fight with Kṛṣṇa when the former blessed Bāṇa. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रानुरूपं श‍ृणु मे शिवलीलान्वितं परम् ।
इतिहासं महापुण्यं मनः श्रोत्रसुखावहम् ॥ ५॥

English
Listen from me the most befitting and the highly meritorious legend of the sports of Śiva. It is pleasing to the mind and to the ears. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मपुत्रो मरीचिर्यो मुनिरासीन्महामतिः ।
मानसः सर्वपुत्रेषु ज्येष्ठः श्रेष्ठः प्रजापतिः ॥ ६॥

English
Marīci, the sage of great intellect, was the mentally created eldest son of Brahmā. He was a Prajāpati too. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रो महात्मासीत्कश्यपो मुनिसत्तमः ।
सृष्टिप्रवृद्धकोऽत्यन्तं पितुर्भक्तो विधेरपि ॥ ७॥

English
His son Kaśyapa was a noble soul. He was the most excellent of all sages. He made the creation flourish well. He was devoted to his father and to Brahmā. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य त्रयोदशमिता दक्षकन्याः सुशीलिकाः ।
कश्यपस्य मुनेर्व्यास पत्न्यश्चासन्पतिव्रताः ॥ ८॥

English
O Vyāsa, thirteen daughters of Dakṣa were his wives. They were of good conduct and very faithful to their husband, the sage Kaśyapa. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र ज्येष्ठा दितिश्चासीद्दैत्यास्तत्तनयाः स्मृताः ।
अन्यासां च सुता जाता देवाद्याः सचराचराः ॥ ९॥

English
The eldest of the wives was Diti. The Daityas were her sons. The gods and others including the mobile and immobile beings were born to others. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्येष्ठायाः प्रथमौ पुत्रौ दितेश्चास्तां महाबलौ ।
हिरण्यकशिपुर्ज्येष्ठो हिरण्याक्षोऽनुजस्ततः ॥ १०॥

English
The eldest Diti had the heroic sons Hiraṇyakaśipu the elder and Hiraṇyākṣa the younger. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यकशिपोः पुत्राश्चत्वारो दैत्यसत्तमाः ।
ह्रादानुह्रादसंह्रादप्रह्रादश्चेत्यनुक्रमात् ॥ ११॥

English
Hiraṇyakaśipu had four sons. They were in order Hrāda, Anuhrāda, Samhrāda and Prahlāda. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रह्रादस्तत्र हि महान्विष्णुभक्तो जितेन्द्रियः ।
यं नाशितुं न शक्तास्तेऽभवन्दैत्याश्च केऽपि ह ॥ १२॥

English
Prahlāda was a great devotee of Viṣṇu. He had full control of his sense-organs. The Daityas were unable to destroy him. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विरोचनः सुतस्तस्य महादातृवरोऽभवत् ।
शक्राय स्वशिरो योऽदाद्याचमानाय विप्रतः ॥ १३॥

English
His son Virocana was the most excellent of donors. He gave even his head to Indra who requested for the same in the guise of a brahmin. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रो बलिश्चासीन्महादानी शिवप्रियः ।
येन वामनरूपाय हरयेऽदायि मेदिनी ॥ १४॥

English
His son was Bali who was a favourite of Śiva and a liberal donor. The earth was given by him to Viṣṇu who assumed the form of a dwarf. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यौरसः सुतो बाणः शिवभक्तो बभूव ह ।
मान्यो वदान्यो धीमांश्च सत्यसन्धः सहस्रदः ॥ १५॥

English
His son Bāṇa became a devotee of Śiva. He was highly respected and intelligent. He was truthful and a liberal donor making thousands of charitable gifts. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शोणिताख्ये पुरे स्थित्वा स राज्यमकरोत्पुरा ।
त्रैलोक्यं च बलाज्जित्वा तन्नाथानसुरेश्वरः ॥ १६॥

English
Staying in the Śoṇita town$^{318}$ he ruled over the three worlds after defeating several rulers forcefully. (16.)

[fn 318. Śoṇitapura was the capital of Bāṇāsura, the ruler of Tripura. Dey identifies it with the town of that name on the bank of the river Kedāra Gaṅgā (See Note 23 P. 532). Avasthi identifies it with Bānagarh in the Dinajapura district of East Bengal. The above identifications are merely tentative, for Bāṇa is said to have ruled in Tripuri (Mod. Tewar) on the Narmadā river in Madhya Pradeśa, far away from the locus suggested by the scholars.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्य बाणासुरस्यैव शिवभक्तस्य चामराः ।
शङ्करस्य प्रसादेन किङ्करा इव तेऽभवन् ॥ १७॥

English
As a result of the grace of Śiva, the gods became the virtual servants of Bāṇa, the devotee of Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

सापत्न्याद् दुःखितास्ते हि परधर्मप्रवर्तिनः ॥ १८॥

सहस्रबाहुवाद्येन स कदाचिन्महासुरः ।
ताण्डवेन हि नृत्येनातोषयत्तं महेश्वरम् ॥ १९॥

English
They were distressed by his enmity although he practised high virtues. In accompaniment of the instrumental music played by his thousand arms, by means of the Tāṇḍava dance he propitiated Śiva. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन नृत्येन सन्तुष्टः सुप्रसन्नो बभूव ह ।
ददर्श कृपया दृष्ट्या शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २०॥

English
Śiva favourably disposed to his devotees was highly delighted and satisfied by his dance and he glanced at him with sympathetic eyes. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The lord of the worlds, worthy of being sought refuge in, the bestower of the desires of the devotees asked the great demon, the son of Bali, to choose a boon he liked. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रणम्य शङ्करं भक्त्या नुनाव परमेश्वरम् ॥ २२॥

English
*Śiva said:—* The great Daitya Bāṇa, son of Bali, the foremost among the devotees and highly intelligent, bowed to lord Śiva with devotion and eulogised him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
सन्तुष्टोऽसि महेशान ममोपरि विभो यदि ॥ २३॥

मद्रक्षको भव सदा मदुपस्थः पुराधिपः ।

English

Be present with me as the lord of my city along with your sons and Gaṇas. O lord, be delightful to me in every respect. (23-24.)

Sanskrit

English

Śiva, who is favourably disposed to his devotees, granted boons to him and stayed there lovingly along with his sons and his Gaṇas. (25-26.)

English
On seeing the change of form of Urvaśī, Ghṛtācī adopted the form of Kālī and Viśvācī that of Caṇḍikā. (50.)

English
Rambhā assumed the form of Sāvitri, Menakā that of Gāyatri; Sahajanyā that of Jayā and Puñjikasthali that of Vijayā. (51.)

Sanskrit

English
The unnamed celestial damsels assumed the forms of unnamed mothers with effort by employing their art. (52.)

Sanskrit

English
On seeing their forms, the daughter of Kumbhāṇḍa, taking recourse to the Vaiṣṇava and her own Yoga, knew everything and emulated the same. (53.)

Sanskrit

English
Ūṣā, daughter of the Asura Bāṇa efficient in divine Yoga, assumed the wonderfully auspicious and divine form of Pārvatī. (54.)

Sanskrit

English
Her feet were of excellent lustre. They shone like the great red lotus. They had all the divine characteristics bestowing every desired object. (55.)

Sanskrit

English
Knowing that she wanted to indulge in love-sport with Śiva, the omniscient and omnipresent Pārvatī spoke. (56.)

Sanskrit
गिरिजोवाच ।
यतो मम स्वरूपं वै धृतमूषे सखि त्वया ।
सकामत्वेन समये सम्प्राप्ते सति मानिनि ॥ ५७॥

अस्मिंस्तु कार्तिके मासि ऋतुधर्मास्तु माधवे ।
द्वादश्यां शुक्लपक्षे तु यस्तु घोरे निशागमे ॥ ५८॥

कृतोपवासां त्वां भोक्ता सुप्तामन्तःपुरे नरः ।
स ते भर्त्ता कृतो देवैस्तेन सार्द्धं रमिष्यसि ॥ ५९॥

English
### *Pārvatī said—*  O friend Ūṣā, chaste and honourable lady since you have adopted my form out of passion, so you will have the monthly course in the appropriate time in the Kārttika month. On the twelfth day in the bright half of Vaiśākha, you will undertake a fast. During the night while you are asleep in the harem, a man will come there and enjoy you. He has been made your husband by the gods. You will sport with him. (57-59.)
