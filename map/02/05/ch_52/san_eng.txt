### **CHAPTER FIFTYTWO - *(The story of Ūśā)***

---

Sanskrit
५२

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणुष्वान्यच्चरित्रं च शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
भक्तवात्सल्यसङ्गर्भि परमानन्ददायकम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Listen to another story of Śiva the great soul which bestows the highest pleasure and wherein his endearment to his devotees can be seen. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा बाणासुरो नाम दैवदोषाच्च गर्वितः ।
कृत्वा ताण्डवनृत्यं च तोषयामास शङ्करम् ॥ २॥

ज्ञात्वा सन्तुष्टमनसं पार्वतीवल्लभं शिवम् ।
उवाच चासुरो बाणो नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ३॥

English
Formerly the Asura Bāṇa had pleased Śiva by performing the Tāṇḍava dance. By adverse fate he became haughty. On realising that Śiva, the lover of Pārvatī, was delighted in mind, the Asura Bāṇa joined his palms in reverence, bent his shoulders and spoke. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
बाण उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव सर्वदेवशिरोमणे ।
त्वत्प्रसादाद् बली चाहं श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ॥ ४॥

English
*Bāṇa said:—*  O great god, lord of the gods and crest-jewel of all the gods, I am very strong, thanks to your favour. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दोःसहस्रं त्वया दत्तं परं भाराय मेऽभवत् ।
त्रिलोक्यां प्रतियोद्धारं न लभे त्वदृते समम् ॥ ५॥

English
A thousand hands have been given to me by you. They are only a burden to me, since except you I do not find any match to oppose me having an equal strength. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
हे देव किमनेनापि सहस्रेण करोम्यहम् ।
बाहूनां गिरितुल्यानां विना युद्धं वृषध्वज ॥ ६॥

English
O bull-bannered lord, what can I do with these thousand mountain-like hands without a fight. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कण्डूत्या निभृतैर्दोर्भिर्युयुत्सुर्दिग्गजानहम् ।
पुराण्याचूर्णयन्नद्रीन्भीतास्तेऽपि प्रदुद्रुवुः ॥ ७॥

English
When my hands itch for war I desire to fight the elephants of the quarters. By hitting the cities and mountains I reduced them to powder. Being frightened they fled away. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मया यमः कृतो योद्धा वह्निश्च कृतको महान् ।
वरुणश्चापि गोपालो गवां पालयिता तथा ॥ ८॥

English
Yama has been made a combatant with me and the great fire-god too, similarly. Varuṇa has been made a cowherd looking after my cows. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
गजाध्यक्षः कुबेरस्तु सैरन्ध्री चापि निरृतिः ।
जितश्चाखण्डलो लोके करदायी सदा कृतः ॥ ९॥

English
Kubera has been made the in-charge of couches, Nirṛti a chaperon. Indra has been defeated and forced to pay tribute. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
युद्धस्यागमनं ब्रूहि यत्रैते बाहवो मम ।
शत्रुहस्तप्रयुक्तैश्च शस्त्रास्त्रैर्जर्जरीकृताः ॥ १०॥

पतन्तु शत्रुहस्ताद्वा पातयन्तु सहस्रधा ।
एतन्मनोरथं मे हि पूर्णं कुरु महेश्वर ॥ ११॥

English
Please suggest a fight unto me wherein my hands may fall shattered by the weapons hurled by the enemy or cause him to fall in thousand pieces. O lord Śiva, please fulfil this desire of mine. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा कुपितो रुद्रस्त्वट्टहासं महाद्भुतम् ।
कृत्वाऽब्रवीन्महामन्युर्भक्तबाधाऽपहारकः ॥ १२॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Becoming furious on hearing that, Śiva laughed boisterously and in a wonderful manner; Śiva the remover of the distress of devotees became very angry and said. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
धिग्धिक् त्वां सर्वतो गर्विन्सर्वदैत्यकुलाधम ।
बलिपुत्रस्य भक्तस्य नोचितं वच ईदृशम् ॥ १३॥

English
*Śiva said—* Fie upon you, O haughty base Daitya, such a talk as this does not behove the son of Bali and a devotee. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दर्पस्यास्य प्रशमनं लप्स्यसे चाशु दारुणम् ।
महायुद्धमकस्माद्वै बलिना मत्समेन हि ॥ १४॥

English
Very soon, you will meet a terrible challenge to your bluff by fighting a great battle against person equal to me in strength. The battle will be sudden. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र ते गिरिसङ्काशा बाहवोऽनलकाष्ठवत् ।
छिन्ना भूमौ पतिष्यन्ति शस्त्रास्त्रैः कदलीकृताः ॥ १५॥

English
Therein your mountain-like hands will be cut off by weapons and missiles. They will fall off like reeds or stumps of plantain trees. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यदेष मानुषशिरो मयूरसहितो ध्वजः ।
विद्यते तव दुष्टात्मंस्तस्य स्यात्पतनं यदा ॥ १६॥

स्थापितस्यायुधागारे विना वातकृतं भयम् ।
तदा युद्धं महाघोरं सम्प्राप्तमिति चेतसि ॥ १७॥

निधाय घोरं सङ्ग्रामं गच्छेथाः सर्वसैन्यवान् ।
साम्प्रतं गच्छ तद्वेश्म यतस्तद्विद्यते शिवः ॥ १८॥

English
“O wicked soul, when this flagstaff of yours, with the emblem of a peacock with a human head, which is installed in your armoury, falls without being blown off by a gust of wind, you can decide within your mind that a terrible battle is at hand. Go to that terrible war accompanied by all your generals. Now return to your abode where Śiva is present. (16-18.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा तान्स्वमहोत्पातांस्तत्र द्रष्टासि दुर्मते ।
इत्युक्त्वा विररामाथ गर्वहृद्भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १९॥

English
O wicked one, you will see the great evil portents.” After saying this, the lord who is favourably disposed to the devotees and is the dispeller of pride stopped. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा रुद्रमभ्यर्च्य दिव्यैरञ्जलिकुड्मलैः ।
प्रणम्य च महादेवं बाणश्च स्वगृहं गतः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After hearing that, Bāṇa worshiped Śiva with palms joined in reverence in the form of buds and bowed to lord Śiva. He then went to his abode. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भाण्डाय यथावृत्तं पृष्टः प्रोवाच हर्षितः ।
पर्यैक्षिष्टासुरो बाणस्तं योगं ह्युत्सुकः सदा ॥ २१॥

English
On being asked, the delighted Asura mentioned everything to Kumbhāṇḍa in the manner it had happened. The Asura Bāṇa awaited the particular conjunction of circumstances eagerly. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ दैवात्कदाचित्स स्वयं भग्नं ध्वजं च तम् ।
दृष्ट्वा तत्रासुरो बाणो हृष्टो युद्धाय निर्ययौ ॥ २२॥

English
Once, by chance he saw the flagstaff broken and fallen. On seeing it, he was delighted and he set out for war. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स स्वसैन्यं समाहूय संयुक्तः साष्टभिर्गणैः ।
इष्टिं साङ्ग्रामिकां कृत्वा दृष्ट्वा साङ्ग्रामिकं मधु ॥ २३॥

ककुभां मङ्गलं सर्वं सम्प्रेक्ष्य प्रस्थितोऽभवत् ।
महोत्साहो महावीरो बलिपुत्रो महारथः ॥ २४॥

English
He called together his entire army. He was accompanied by his eight lieutenants. He performed the sacrifice for success in war. He surveyed the wine$^{322}$ prepared for use in the course of war. He observed the auspicious signs in the quarters and set off. He, the son of Bali,$^{323}$ the heroic warrior capable of fighting with ten thousand persons, was very enthusiastic. (23-24.)

[fn 322. Cp. Note 277 P. 963.]

[fn 323. Bāṇa was the son of Bali also called Mahābali. He ruled at Śoṇitapura while his father’s capital was Mahābalipura. We can construct the ancestry of Bāṇa from Śivapurāṇa : Hiraṇyakasipu—Prahlāda—Virocana—Bali—Bāṇa.]


---

Sanskrit
इति हृत्कमले कृत्वा कः कस्मादागमिष्यति ।
योद्धा रणप्रियो यस्तु नानाशस्त्रास्त्रपारगः ॥ २५॥

यस्तु बाहुसहस्रं मे छिनत्त्वनलकाष्ठवत् ।
तथा शस्त्रैर्महातीक्ष्णैश्च्छिनद्मि शतशस्त्विह ॥ २६॥

English
He thought within himself—“Who can be that warrior fond of battle, a master of the art of using weapons and missiles who will cut off my thousand hands like reeds or whom I shall cut into hundred pieces?” (25-26.)

Sanskrit

यत्र सा बाणदुहिता सुजाता कृतमङ्गला ॥ २७॥

English

---

Sanskrit

कृष्णात्मजात्मजेनाथ रुदन्ती सा ह्यनाथवत् ॥ २९॥

English

[fn 324. Aniruddha was the son of Pradyumna and grandson of Kṛṣṇa.]

[fn 325. There were two Dvārakās connected with Kṛṣṇa. The one was situated near Kodinar on the sea-shore between the mouths of the rivers : Somat and Singāvara. Kṛṣṇa is said to have resided here and later on transferred himself to Dvārakā in Okhā maṇḍala in Kathiawar.]

Sanskrit

English

एकेन क्षणमात्रेण नभसा दिव्ययोगिनी ।
ततश्चान्तःपुरोद्याने प्राद्युम्निर्ददृशे तया ॥ ५२॥

क्रीडन्नारीजनैः सार्द्धं प्रपिबन्माधवी मधु ।
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरः श्यामः सुस्मितो नवयौवनः ॥ ५३॥

English
When the third day after the fourteenth day in the dark half of the month of Jyeṣṭha elapsed, a Muhūrta before the dawn, she reached the city of Dvārakā within a moment by the aerial path because she was a Yogini. Then in the park of the harem the son of Pradyumna was seen by her playing with women and drinking wine. He was dark-complexioned but beautiful in every limb, smiling and in the prime of youth. (51-53.)
