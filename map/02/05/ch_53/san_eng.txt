### **CHAPTER FIFTYTHREE - *(The dalliance of Uṣā and Aniruddha)***

---

Sanskrit
५३

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अथ बाणासुरः क्रुद्धस्तत्र गत्वा ददर्श तम् ।
दिव्यलीलात्तवपुषं प्रथमे वयसि स्थितम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said :—* The infuriated Asura Bāṇa went there and saw Aniruddha who was in the fresh years of youth and who seemed to have been born especially for divine sports. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा विस्मितं वाक्यं किं कारणमथाब्रवीत् ।
बाणः क्रोधपरीतात्मा युधि शौण्डो हसन्निव ॥ २॥

English
The infuriated Bāṇa, very efficient in war, was a bit surprised on seeing him and wondered why he had done like that and therefore said mockingly. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो मनुष्यो रूपाढ्यःसाहसी धैर्यवानिति ।
कोऽयमागतकालश्च दुष्टभाग्यो विमूढधीः ॥ ३॥

English
“Oh this man is really handsome, bold and daring. Who can this unfortunate deluded person be? His death is imminent. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
येन मे कुलचारित्रं दूषितं दुहिता हिता ।
तं मारयध्वं कुपिताः शीघ्रं शस्त्रैः सुदारुणैः ॥ ४॥

English
O angry ones, with terrible weapons immediately kill the fellow who has outraged the traditional purity of my family and defiled my dear daughter. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दुराचारं च तं बद्ध्वा घोरे कारागृहे ततः ।
रक्षध्वं विकटे वीरा बहुकालं विशेषतः ॥ ५॥

English
O heroic ones, bind the terrible fellow of evil conduct. Put him in a frightful prison for a long time”. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
न जाने कोऽयमभयः को वा घोरपराक्रमः ।
विचार्येति महाबुद्धिः सन्दिग्धोऽभूच्छरासुरः ॥ ६॥

English
After saying this, the Asura Bāṇa thought within himself intelligently. “It is not known who this fearless fellow is. Certainly he is a man of terrible exploit”. Thinking thus Bāṇa hesitated to act. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दैत्येन सैन्यं तु दशसाहस्रकं शनैः ।
वधाय तस्य वीरस्य व्यादिष्टं पापबुद्धिना ॥ ७॥

English
Then gradually the evil-minded Daitya ordered ten thousand men from his army for slaying him. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तदादिष्टास्तु ते वीराः सर्वतोऽन्तःपुरं द्रुतम् ।
छादयामासुरत्युग्राश्छिन्धि भिन्दीति वादिनः ॥ ८॥

English
Commanded by him those heroic terrible fellows encircled the harem saying, "Cut him, pierce him". (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शत्रुसैन्यं ततो दृष्ट्वा गर्जमानः स यादवः ।
अन्तःपुरं द्वारगतं परिघं गृह्य चातुलम् ॥ ९॥

निष्क्रान्तो भवनात्तस्माद्वज्रहस्त इवान्तकः ।
तेन तान्किङ्करान् हत्वा पुनश्चान्तःपुरं ययौ ॥ १०॥

English
On seeing the army of the enemy, Aniruddha the scion of the Yādava family, roared. He seized the big iron club from the harem-gate and came out of the apartment like the god of death armed with thunderbolt. With that iron club he killed the servants and returned to the harem. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं दशसहस्राणि सैन्यानि मुनिसत्तम ।
जघान रोषरक्ताक्षो वर्द्धितः शिवतेजसा ॥ ११॥

English
O excellent sage, thus Aniruddha strengthened by Śiva's splendour, with eyes turned red by anger, killed all the ten thousand men of the army. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
लक्षे हतेऽथ योधानां ततो बाणासुरो रुषा ।
कुम्भाण्डं स गृहीत्वा तु युद्धे शौण्डं समाह्वयत् ॥ १२॥

अनिरुद्धं महाबुद्धिं द्वन्द्वयुद्धे महाहवे ।
प्राद्युम्निं रक्षितं शैवतेजसा प्रज्वलत्तनुम् ॥ १३॥

ततो दशसहस्राणि तुरङ्गाणां रथोत्तमान् ।
युद्धप्राप्तेन खड्गेन दैत्येन्द्रस्य जघान सः ॥ १४॥

English
With the sword captured in the course of the battle he killed ten thousands of horses and charioteers of the lord of Daityas. When a hundred thousand soldiers had been killed, the Asura Bāṇa furiously entered the fray taking with him Kumbhāṇḍa, expert in war. He then challenged Aniruddha for a duel in the course of that war—Aniruddha the highly intelligent son of Pradyumna, of refulgent body and protected by Śiva's brilliance. (12-14.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वधाय ततः शक्तिं कालवैश्वानरोपमाम् ।
अनिरुद्धो गृहीत्वा तां तया तं निजघान हि ॥ १५॥

English
Aniruddha then seized a spear blazing like the fire of death, for killing him and hit him with that. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रथोपस्थे ततो बाणस्तया शक्त्याहतो दृढम् ।
स साश्वस्तत्क्षणं वीरस्तत्रैवान्तरधीयत ॥ १६॥

English
Bāṇa was hit with the spear even as he was seated in a chariot. In a trice the heroic demon vanished along with his horse. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिंस्त्वदर्शनं प्राप्ते प्राद्युम्निरपराजितम् ।
आलोक्य ककुभः सर्वाः तस्थौ गिरिरिवाचलः ॥ १७॥

English
When he vanished, Aniruddha, the unvanquished son of Pradyumna, stood steady like a mountain observing all the quarters. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अदृश्यमानस्तु तदा कूटयोधः स दानवः ।
नानाशस्त्रसहस्रैस्तं जघान हि पुनः पुनः ॥ १८॥

English
Remaining invisible, that Dānava Bāṇa, practising deceptive fight, hit him again and again with thousands of weapons. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
छद्मना नागपाशैस्तं बबन्ध स महाबलः ।
बलिपुत्रो महावीरः शिवभक्तः शरासुरः ॥ १९॥

English
That powerful son of Bali, the heroic devotee of Śiva, the Asura Bāṇa deceitfully bound him with Serpent-nooses.326 (19.)

[fn 326. Nāgapāśa was a sort of magical noose used in battle to entangle an enemy. Formerly it was the exclusive weapon of Asura Varuṇa.]
---

Sanskrit
तं बद्ध्वा पञ्जरान्तःस्थं कृत्वा युद्धादुपारमत् ।
उवाच बाणः सङ्क्रुद्धः सूतपुत्रं महाबलम् ॥ २०॥

English
After binding him and putting him in a cage he stopped the battle. The infuriated Bāṇa then spoke to the very powerful son of the charioteer. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणासुर उवाच ।
सूतपुत्र शिरश्छिन्धि पुरुषस्यास्य वै लघु ।
येन मे दूषितं पूतं बलाद्दुष्टेन सत्कुलम् ॥ २१॥

English
*The Asura Bāṇa said:—*  O son of the charioteer, cut off the head of this wicked fellow who has defiled my family. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
छित्वा तु सर्वगात्राणि राक्षसेभ्यः प्रयच्छ भोः ।
अथास्य रक्तमांसानि क्रव्यादा अपि भुञ्जताम् ॥ २२॥

English
After chopping off the limbs, give them to the Rākṣasas. Or let the beasts of prey swallow his flesh and blood. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अगाधे तृणसङ्कीर्णे कूपे पातकिनं जहि ।
किं बहूक्त्या सूतपुत्र मारणीयो हि सर्वथा ॥ २३॥

English
Or kill this sinner and put him in a grassy well. O son of the charioteer, what more shall I say? By all means he must be slain. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा धर्मबुद्धिर्निशाचरः ।
कुम्भाण्डस्त्वब्रवीद्वाक्यं बाणं सन्मन्त्रिसत्तमम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* On hearing his words, the Asura Kumbhāṇḍa, the most excellent of the ministers and righteous in thought, spoke to Bāṇa. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भाण्ड उवाच ।
नैतत्कर्तुं समुचितं कर्म देव विचार्यताम् ।
अस्मिन् हते हतो ह्यात्मा भवेदिति मतिर्मम ॥ २५॥

English
*Kumbhāṇḍa said:—*  O lord, this is not a proper thing to do. Please consider. I think by killing him we will be killing ourselves. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं तु दृश्यते देव तुल्यो विष्णोः पराक्रमैः ।
वर्धितश्चन्द्रचूडस्य त्वदिष्टस्य सुतेजसा ॥ २६॥

English
O lord, he seems to be equal to Viṣṇu in exploits. His strength has been increased by the brilliance of the moon-crested lord, your favourite. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ चन्द्रललाटस्य साहसेन समत्स्वयम् ।
इमामवस्थां प्राप्तोऽस्ति पौरुषे संव्यवस्थितः ॥ २७॥

English
Moreover, in daring he is equal to the moon-crested lord; though he is reduced to this plight he maintains his manliness. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं शिवप्रसादाद्वै कृष्णपौत्रो महाबलः ।
अस्मांस्तृणोपमान् वेत्ति दष्टोऽपि भुजगैर्बलात् ॥ २८॥

English
It is by the grace of Śiva that he, the grandson of Kṛṣṇa, considers us insignificant as the blades of grass. Although he is bitten by serpents cruelly he is still very strong. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एतद्वाक्यं तु बाणाय कथयित्वा स दानवः ।
अनिरुद्धमुवाचेदं राजनीतिविदुत्तमः ॥ २९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying thus to Bāṇa, the Dānava, the most excellent among statesmen, spoke to Aniruddha. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भाण्ड उवाच ।
कोऽसि कस्यासि रे वीर सत्यं वद ममाग्रतः ।
केन वा त्वमिहानीतो दुराचार नराधम ॥ ३०॥

English
*Kumbhāṇḍa said:—* “O hero, who are you? Whose son are you? Tell us the truth. O meanest of men, of evil conduct, by whom have you been brought here? (30.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्येन्द्रं स्तुहि वीरं त्वं नमस्कुरु कृताञ्जलिः ।
जितोऽस्मीति वचो दीनं कथयित्वा पुनः पुनः ॥ ३१॥

English
Repeat piteously “I am vanquished”. Eulogise the heroic lord of the Daityas. Join your palms in reverence and bow to him. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृते तु मोक्षःस्यादन्यथा बन्धनादि च ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य प्रतिवाक्यमुवाच सः ॥ ३२॥

English
If you do like this, you may get release. Otherwise this captivity and tortures will continue”. On hearing this Aniruddha replied. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अनिरुद्ध उवाच ।
रे रे दैत्याऽधमसखे करपिण्डोपजीवक ।
निशाचर दुराचार शत्रुधर्मं न वेत्सि भोः ॥ ३३॥

English
*Aniruddha said:—*  O friend of the basest of Daityas, O you that sustain yourself with the balls of rice offered in the hands, O demon of evil conduct, you do not know the laws of adversaries. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
दैन्यं पलायनं चाथ शूरस्य मरणाधिकम् ।
विरुद्धं चोपशल्यं च भवेदिति मतिर्मम ॥ ३४॥

English
I think that for the valorous person humble supplications and running away from the battlefield are worse than death. These are adverse and painful to him like a dart. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षत्रियस्य रणे श्रेयो मरणं सम्मुखे सदा ।
न वीरमानिनो भूमौ दीनस्येव कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ३५॥

English
For a Kṣatriya, death while fighting face to face with the enemy is commendable rather than joining the palms in reverence like a man in humble condition professing to be valorous. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्यादि वीरवाक्यानि बहूनि स जगाद तम् ।
तदाकर्ण्य स बाणोऽसौ विस्मितोऽभूच्चुकोप च ॥ ३६॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* These and many other heroic words he uttered, on hearing which Bāṇa was surprised and angry too. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोवाच नभोवाणी बाणस्याश्वासनाय हि ।
श‍ृण्वतां सर्ववीराणामनिरुद्धस्य मन्त्रिणः ॥ ३७॥

English
Then a celestial voice was heard for the pacification of Bāṇa which all the heroes, Aniruddha and the minister, stood listening to. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
व्योमवाण्युवाच ।
भो भो बाण महावीर न क्रोधं कर्तुमर्हसि ।
बलिपुत्रोऽसि सुमते शिवभक्त विचार्यताम् ॥ ३८॥

English
*The celestial voice said:—*  “O Bāṇa, O great hero, you shall not be angry. “O devotee of Śiva, O intelligent one, you are the son of Bali. Ponder over this. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः सर्वेश्वरः साक्षी कर्मणां परमेश्वरः ।
तदधीनमिदं सर्वं जगद्वै सचराचरम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Śiva, the lord of all, the supreme lord, is the witness of all activities. This entire universe including the mobile and immobile beings is subservient to him. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव कर्ता भर्ता च संहर्ता जगतां सदा ।
रजः सत्त्वतमोधारी विधिविष्णुहरात्मकः ॥ ४०॥

English
He alone is the creator, maintainer and the destroyer of the worlds, always taking up the attributes of Rajas, Sattva and Tamas in the form of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्यान्तर्गतः स्वामी प्रेरकः सर्वतः परः ।
निर्विकार्यव्ययो नित्यो मायाधीशोऽपि निर्गुणः ॥ ४१॥

English
The lord is omnipresent. He is the inducer, greater than all. He is free from aberrations, unchanging, eternal, the lord of illusion and devoid of attributes. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्येच्छयाऽबलो ज्ञेयो बली बलिवरात्मज ।
इति विज्ञाय मनसि स्वस्थो भव महामते ॥ ४२॥

English
Even a weak person becomes strong, thanks to his will, O excellent son of Bali. O intelligent one, realise this in your mind, be normal and complacent. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्वापहारी भगवान्नानालीलाविशारदः ।
नाशयिष्यति ते गर्वमिदानीं भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ४३॥

English
The lord who quells pride, who is an expert in various sports and who is favourably disposed to his devotees will destroy your arrogance.” (43.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याभाष्य नभोवाणी विरराम महामुने ।
बाणासुरस्तद्वचनादनिरुद्धं न जघ्निवान् ॥ ४४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O great sage, having spoken thus, the celestial voice stopped. On hearing these words the Asura Bāṇa did not kill Aniruddha. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

तद्वाक्यं च विसस्मार विजहार विरुद्धधीः ॥ ४५॥

English
Then he went to his harem and drank excellent beverages. His intellect was adversely affected, he forgot those words and began to sport. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽनिरुद्धो बद्धस्तु नागभोगैर्विषोल्बणैः ।

English
Aniruddha was bound by serpentine bodies emitting poison powerfully. His passion for his beloved had not been satiated fully. He remembered Durgā then. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अनिरुद्ध उवाच ।
शरण्ये देवि बद्धोऽस्मि दह्यमानस्तु पन्नगैः ।
आगच्छ मे कुरु त्राणं यशोदे चण्डरोषिणि ॥ ४७॥

English
*Aniruddha said:—*  O goddess, you are worthy of being resorted to. I have been bound by serpents. O goddess bestowing fame, O goddess of fierce fury, come and save me. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवभक्ते महादेवि सृष्टिस्थित्यन्तकारिणी ।
त्वां विना रक्षको नान्यस्तस्माद्रक्ष शिवे हि माम् ॥ ४८॥

English
O great goddess, devotee of Śiva, O cause of creation, sustenance and dissolution, there is no other saviour except you. O Śivā, save me. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तेनेत्थं तोषिता तत्र काली भिन्नाञ्जनप्रभा ।
ज्येष्ठकृष्णचतुर्दश्यां सम्प्राप्तासीन्महानिशि ॥ ४९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Propitiated by him, Kālī, lustrous like the split collyrium, arrived there in the dark night of the fourteenth day in the dark half of the month Jyeṣṭha. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुभिर्मुष्टिनिर्घातैर्दारयामास पञ्जरम् ।
शरांस्तान्भस्मसात्कृत्वा सर्परूपान् भयानकान् ॥ ५०॥

मोचयित्वानिरुद्धं तु ततश्चान्तःपुरं ततः ।
प्रवेशयित्वा दुर्गा तु तत्रैवादर्शनं गता ॥ ५१॥

English
With the heavy blows of her fists she broke the cage. She reduced the serpentine arrows to ashes. She released Aniruddha and let him enter the harem and then vanished from the scene. (50-51.)

Sanskrit
इत्थं देव्याः प्रसादात्तु शिवशक्तेर्मुनीश्वर ।
कृच्छ्रमुक्तोऽनिरुद्धोऽभूत्सुखी चैव गतव्यथः ॥ ५२॥

English
O great sage, thus, thanks to the grace of the goddess—the Energy of Śiva, Aniruddha got rid of the difficulty, became free from pain and obtained happiness. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ लब्धजयो भूत्वानिरुद्धः शिवशक्तितः ।
प्राद्युम्निर्बाणतनयां प्रियां प्राप्य मुमोद च ॥ ५३॥

English
Securing success by means of Śiva’s Energy, Aniruddha the son of Pradyumna gained access to his beloved, the daughter of Bāṇa and rejoiced. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्ववद्विजहारासौ तया स्वप्रियया सुखी ।
पीतपानः सुरक्ताक्षः स बाणसुतया ततः ॥ ५४॥

English
In the company of his beloved—the daughter of Bāṇa, he carried on dalliance and was happy drinking the beverages till his eyes became red. (54.)
