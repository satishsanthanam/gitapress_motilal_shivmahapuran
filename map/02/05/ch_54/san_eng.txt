### **CHAPTER FIFTYFOUR - *(The fight among Bāṇa, Śiva, Kṛṣṇa and others)***

---

Sanskrit
५४

व्यास उवाच ।
अनिरुद्धे हृते पौत्रे कृष्णस्य मुनिसत्तम ।
कुम्भाण्डसुतया कृष्णः किमकार्षीद्धि तद्वद ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O excellent sage, when Aniruddha the grandson of Kṛṣṇa was abducted by the daughter of Kumbhāṇḍa what did Kṛṣṇa do? Please narrate it to me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
ततो गतेऽनिरुद्धे तु तत्स्त्रीणां रोदनस्वनम् ।
श्रुत्वा च व्यथितः कृष्णो बभूव मुनिसत्तम ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O excellent sage, on hearing the woeful cries of his women when Aniruddha had gone off suddenly, Kṛṣṇa too became vexed. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अपश्यतां चानिरुद्धं तद्बन्धूनां हरेस्तथा ।
चत्वारो वार्षिका मासा व्यतीयुरनुशोचताम् ॥ ३॥

English
The four months of the rainy season thus went by when his relatives and Viṣṇu could not see Aniruddha and so they bewailed. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदात्तदुपाकर्ण्य वार्तां बद्धस्य कर्म च ।
आसन्सुव्यथिताः सर्वे वृष्णयः कृष्णदेवताः ॥ ४॥

English
On hearing from Nārada about the imprisonment and activities of Aniruddha the Vṛṣṇis, followers of Kṛṣṇa, became dejected. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णस्तद्वृत्तमखिलं श्रुत्वा युद्धाय चादरात् ।
जगाम शोणितपुरं तार्क्ष्यमाहूय तत्क्षणात् ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing everything, Kṛṣṇa immediately called Garuḍa and went to the city of Śoṇita eagerly for fighting. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रद्युम्नो युयुधानश्च गतःसाम्बोऽथ सारणः ।
नन्दोपनन्दभद्राद्या रामकृष्णानुवर्तिनः ॥ ६॥

English
Pradyumna, Yuyudhāna, Sāmba, Sāraṇa, Nanda, Upananda, Bhadra and others following Rāma and Kṛṣṇa went there. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षौहिणीभिर्द्वादशभिः समेता सर्वतो दिशम् ।
रुरुधुर्बाणनगरं समन्तात्सात्वतर्षभाः ॥ ७॥

English
They were accompanied by twelve Akṣauhiṇīs. They, the chief of Sātvatas$^{327}$ laid a siege all round the city of Bāna. (7.)

[fn 327. Sātvatas were a family of Yādava race whose founder was Yadu, son of King Yayāti. King Sātvata founded a branch of the Yādavas after his name. He had four sons : (1) Bhajin Bhajamāna (2) Devavṛdha (3) Andhaka mahābhoja and (4) Vṛṣṇi Kṛṣṇa, the celebrated hero of the Mahābhārata was born in the Sātvata-Vṛṣṇi family.]
---

Sanskrit
भज्यमानपुरोद्यानप्राकाराट्टालगोपुरम् ।
वीक्ष्यमाणो रुषाविष्टस्तुल्यसैन्योऽभिनिर्ययौ ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing the parks, fortresses, ceilings and minarets of the city thus broken, Bāna became infuriated and set forth with an equal number of armies. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणार्थे भगवान् रुद्रः ससुतः प्रमथैर्वृतः ।
आरुह्य नन्दिवृषभं युद्धं कर्त्तुं समाययौ ॥ ९॥

English
In order to help Bāna, lord Rudra accompanied by his son and the Pramathas rode on the bull Nandin and arrived there to fight. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
आसीत्सुतुमुलं युद्धमद्भुतं लोमहर्षणम् ।
कृष्णादिकानां तैस्तत्र रुद्राद्यैर्बाणरक्षकैः ॥ १०॥

English
A tumultuous fight, wonderfully causing horripilation ensued between Kṛṣṇa and his followers on the one hand and the supporters of Bāna, Rudra etc. on the other. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णशङ्करयोरासीत्प्रद्युम्नगुहयोरपि ।
कूष्माण्डकूपकर्णाभ्यां बलेन सह संयुगः ॥ ११॥

English
The fight was between Kṛṣṇa and Śiva; Pradyumna and Kārttikeya; Kumbhāṇḍaka and Kūpakarṇa, Bala and Saṃyuga. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
साम्बस्य बाणपुत्रेण बाणेन सह सात्यकेः ।
नन्दिना गरुडस्यापि परेषां च परैरपि ॥ १२॥

English
Sāmba fought with Bāna’s son; Sātyaki with Bāna; Garuḍa with Nandin and groups of one side fought with the groups of the other. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मादयः सुराधीशा मुनयः सिद्धचारणाः ।
गन्धर्वाऽप्सरसो यानैर्विमानैर्द्रष्टुमागमन् ॥ १३॥

English
Brahmā and other gods, sages, Siddhas, Cāraṇas, Gandharvas and celestial damsels came there in aerial chariots to witness the affray. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमथैर्विविधाकारै रेवत्यन्तैः सुदारुणम् ।
युद्धं बभूव विप्रेन्द्र तेषां च यदुवंशिनाम् ॥ १४॥

English
O best of brahmins, a terrible fight ensued between the members of the Yadu family with the groups of Pramathas ending with “Revatis”. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रात्रा रामेण सहितः प्रद्युम्नेन च धीमता ।
कृष्णश्चकार समरमतुलं प्रमथैः सह ॥ १५॥

English
Kṛṣṇa, his brother Rāma and the intelligent Pradyumna, fought an unequalled fight with the Pramathas. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राग्निनाऽभवद्युद्धं यमेन वरुणेन च ।
विमुखेन त्रिपादेन ज्वरेण च गुहेन च ॥ १६॥

प्रमथैर्विविधाकारैस्तेषामत्यन्तदारुणम् ।
युद्धं बभूव विकटं वृष्णीनां रोमहर्षणम् ॥ १७॥

English
The fight continued with Agni, Yama, Varuṇa, Vimukha, Tripāda, Jvara and Kārttikeya and groups of Pramathas with the Vṛṣṇis. It was terrible and frightful causing horripilation. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
बिभीषिकाभिर्बह्वीभिः कोटरीभिः पदे पदे ।
निर्लज्जाभिश्च नारीभिः प्रबलाभिरदूरतः ॥ १८॥

English
There was fight with the shameless women too, several groups of terrifying Koṭaris at every step, not far from one another. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्करानुचरान् शौरिर्भूतप्रमथगुह्यकान् ।
द्रावयामास तीक्ष्णाग्रैः शरैः शार्ङ्गधनुश्च्युतैः ॥ १९॥

English
Kṛṣṇa routed the Bhūtas, Pramathas and Guhyakas, the followers of Śiva with sharp-pointed arrows discharged from his bow. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं प्रद्युम्नप्रमुखा वीरा युद्धमहोत्सवाः ।
चक्रुर्युद्धं महाघोरं शत्रुसैन्यं विनाशयन् ॥ २०॥

English
The heroes Pradyumna and others jubilant over the war destroyed the armies of the enemies and fought terribly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विशीर्यमाणं स्वबलं दृष्ट्वा रुद्रोऽत्यमर्षणः ।
क्रोधं चकार सुमहन्ननाद च महोल्बणम् ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing his army being scattered, Śiva became highly infuriated and roared terribly. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा शङ्करगणा विनेदुर्युयुधुश्च ते ।
मर्दयन्प्रतियोद्धारं वर्द्धिताः शम्भुतेजसा ॥ २२॥

English
On hearing that, Śiva's Gaṇas too shouted and fought. They suppressed the opponents with their strength increased by Śiva's brilliance. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथग्विधानि चायुङ्क्त शार्ङ्गास्त्राणि पिनाकिने ।
प्रत्यक्षैः शमयामास शूलपाणिरविस्मितः ॥ २३॥

English
Kṛṣṇa discharged separate types of missiles from his bow towards the trident-bearing lord Śiva who without showing any dismay quelled them directly. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मास्त्रस्य च ब्रह्मास्त्रं वायव्यस्य च पार्वतम् ।
आग्नेयस्य च पार्जन्यं नैजं नारायणस्य च ॥ २४॥

English
They discharged Brahmā-missile in counter to Brahmā missile; the mountain-missile to the wind-missile; the cloud-missile to the fire-missile and Śiva-missile to the Nārāyaṇa missile. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णसैन्यं विदुद्राव प्रतिवीरेण निर्जितम् ।
न तस्थौ समरे व्यास पूर्णरुद्रसुतेजसा ॥ २५॥

English
Defeated by the opponents, the army of Kṛṣṇa fled, O Vyāsa; it could not face the full refulgence of Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्राविते स्वसैन्ये तु श्रीकृष्णश्च परन्तपः ।
स्वं ज्वरं शीतलाख्यं हि व्यसृजद् वारुणं मुने ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, when his army was routed, lord Kṛṣṇa, the scorcher of enemies, discharged terrible fever missile named 'cold'.$^{328}$ (26.)

[fn 328. The mention of the missile of cold fever and of the counter missile to ward it off indicates the heights that India had attained in military science.]
---

Sanskrit
विद्राविते कृष्णसैन्ये कृष्णस्य शीतलज्वरः ।
अभ्यपद्यत तं रुद्रं मुने दशदिशो दहन् ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, when the army of Kṛṣṇa was routed, the cold fever missile of Kṛṣṇa rushed at Rudra blazing the ten quarters. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरोऽथ तं दृष्ट्वायान्तं स्वं विसृजज्ज्वरम् ।
माहेश्वरो वैष्णवश्च युयुधाते ज्वरावुभौ ॥ २८॥

English
On seeing that coming, lord Śiva discharged his own fever missile. The two fever missiles fought each other. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
वैष्णवोऽथ समाक्रन्दन्माहेश्वरबलार्दितः ।
अलब्ध्वाऽभयमन्यत्र तुष्टाव वृषभध्वजम् ॥ २९॥

English
Oppressed by the fever missile of lord Śiva, the fever missile of Śiva cried aloud. Unable to secure succour elsewhere, it eulogised the bull-bannered lord. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रसन्नो भगवान्विष्णुज्वरनुतो हरः ।
विष्णुशीतज्वरं प्राह शरणागतवत्सलः ॥ ३०॥

English
The delighted lord Śiva, favourably disposed to those who seek refuge, eulogised by Viṣṇu's fever missile, spoke to the cold fever missile of Viṣṇu. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

शीतज्वर प्रसन्नोऽहं व्येतु ते मज्ज्वराद्भयम् ।
यो नौ स्मरति संवादं तस्य न स्याज्ज्वराद्भयम् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—* “O cold fever, I am delighted. Leave off your fear from my fever. There is no fear from fever to him who remembers this anecdote. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो रुद्रमानम्य गतो नारायणज्वरः ।

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus advised, the fever missile of Viṣṇu went away after bowing to Śiva. On seeing that activity, Kṛṣṇa was surprised and dismayed. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
स्कन्दः प्रद्युम्नबाणौघैरर्द्यमानोऽथ कोपितः ।
जघान शक्त्या प्रद्युम्नं दैत्यसङ्घात्यमर्षणः ॥ ३३॥

English
When attacked by the arrows of Pradyumna, the infuriated Kārttikeya, the slayer of Daityas, hit Pradyumna with his spear. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

असृग्विमुञ्चन्गात्रेभ्यो बलेनापाक्रमद्रणात् ॥ ३४॥

English
When hit by Kārttikeya’s spear, Pradyumna, though very strong, shed blood from his limbs and fled from the battle ground. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Hit with various missiles by Kumbhāṇḍa and Kūpakarṇa, Balabhadra though strong did not stay there. He fled from the battle-field. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Garuḍa, took up a thousand bodies and drank up the water from the great sea. He then began to work havoc by showering the sea-waters through Āvarta clouds. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then the infuriated bull, the powerful vehicle of lord Śiva, hit him with great force by means of his horns. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
When his limbs were shattered by the blows of his horns, Garuḍa was dismayed. He forsook Viṣṇu and fled from the battle ground immediately. (38.)

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

---

Sanskrit

English
