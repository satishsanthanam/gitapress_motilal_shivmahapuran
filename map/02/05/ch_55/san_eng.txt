### **CHAPTER FIFTYFIVE - *(The chopping of Bāṇa's arms and his humiliation)***

---

Sanskrit
५५

व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ ब्रह्मपुत्र नमोऽस्तु ते ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा तात श्राविता मे त्वया मुने ॥ १॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O dear sage, Sanatkumāra, O omniscient son of Brahmā, obeisance be to you. A wonderful story has been narrated to me by you. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
जृम्भिते जृम्भणास्त्रेण हरिणा समरे हरे ।
हते बाणबले बाणः किमकार्षीच्च तद्वद ॥ २॥

English
When Śiva was made to yawn and lie flat by Viṣṇu through the Jṛmbhaṇa missile, in the course of the war and when the army of Bāṇa was slain, what did Bāṇa do? Please narrate. (2.)

---

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words of Vyāsa of immeasurable refulgence, the great sage, the delighted son of Brahmā, replied, (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाप्राज्ञ कथां च परमाद्भुताम् ।
कृष्णशङ्करयोस्तात लोकलीलानुसारिणोः ॥ ४॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O Vyāsa of great intellect, listen to the highly wonderful story of Kṛṣṇa and Śiva who indulge in sports in accordance with the worldly conventions. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शयिते लीलया रुद्रे सपुत्रे सगणे सति ।
बाणो विनिर्गतो युद्धं कर्तुं कृष्णेन दैत्यराट् ॥ ५॥

English
When Śiva sportively lay flat on the ground along with his sons and Gaṇas, Bāṇa the king of Daityas came out to fight with Kṛṣṇa. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भाण्डसङ्गृहीताश्वो नानाशस्त्रास्त्रधृक् ततः ।
चकार युद्धमतुलं बलिपुत्रो महाबलः ॥ ६॥

English
With the horses mobilised by Kumbhāṇḍa, and holding various weapons and missiles, the powerful son of Bali performed an incomparable fight. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा निजबलं नष्टं स दैत्येन्द्रोऽत्यमर्षितः ।
चकार युद्धमतुलं बलिपुत्रो महाबलः ॥ ७॥

English
On seeing his army destroyed, the lord of the Daityas became infuriated. The powerful son of Bali fought an incomparable battle. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्णोऽपि महावीरो गिरिशाप्तमहाबलः ।
उच्चैर्जगर्ज तत्राजौ बाणं मत्वा तृणोपमम् ॥ ८॥

English
Lord Kṛṣṇa the great hero with additional strength derived from Śiva roared loudly in the battlefield considering Bāṇa as insignificant as a blade of grass. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
धनुष्टङ्कारयामास शार्ङ्गाख्यं निजमद्भुतम् ।
त्रासयन्बाणसैन्यं तदवशिष्टं मुनीश्वर ॥ ९॥

English
O great sage, he made the twanging sound on his wonderful bow called Śārṅga and frightened what little remained of Bāṇa's army. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन नादेन महता धनुष्टङ्कारजेन हि ।
द्यावाभूम्योरन्तरं वै व्याप्तमासीदनन्तरम् ॥ १०॥

English
The intervening space between heaven and Earth was filled with the great sound originating from the twang on his bow. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
चिक्षेप विविधान्बाणान्बाणाय कुपितो हरिः ।
कर्णान्तं तद्विकृष्याथ तीक्ष्णानाशीविषोपमान् ॥ ११॥

English
Drawing the string of his bow upto the ear, the infuriated Viṣṇu discharged sharp arrows, as furious as serpents, on Bāṇa. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
आयातस्तान्निरीक्ष्याऽथ स बाणो बलिनन्दनः ।
अप्राप्तानेव चिच्छेद स्वशरैः स्वधनुश्च्युतैः ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing the arrows coming, Bāṇa the son of Bali, split them even before they reached him, by means of arrows discharged from his bow. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्जगर्ज स विभुर्बाणो वैरिगणार्दनः ।
तत्रसुर्वृष्णयः सर्वे कृष्णात्मानो विचेतसः ॥ १३॥

English
Lord Bāṇa, suppressor of enemies roared again. The Vṛṣṇis thinking of Kṛṣṇa were afraid and dejected. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं चिक्षेप निजसायकान् ।
स कृष्णायातिशूराय महागर्वो बलेः सुतः ॥ १४॥

English
Thinking upon the lotus-like feet of Śiva, the haughty son of Bali discharged his arrows at Kṛṣṇa the most valorous. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णोऽपि तानसम्प्राप्तानच्छिनत्स्वशरैर्द्रुतम् ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजममरारिर्महाबलः ॥ १५॥

English
Thinking upon the lotus-like feet of Śiva, the powerful destroyer of Asuras, Kṛṣṇa split them before they reached him by means of his own arrows. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
रामादयो वृष्णयश्च स्वं स्वं योद्धारमाहवे ।
निजघ्नुर्बलिनःसर्वे कृत्वा क्रोधं समाकुलाः ॥ १६॥

English
Rāma and other Vṛṣṇis, the powerful ones agitated by anger, slew their respective opponents. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं चिरतरं तत्र बलिनोश्च द्वयोरपि ।
बभूव तुमुलं युद्धं श‍ृण्वतां विस्मयावहम् ॥ १७॥

English
Thus the tumultuous fight between the two strong armies went on for a long time aggravating the wonder of the spectators. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे तत्र क्रोधं कृत्वाऽति पक्षिराट् ।
बाणासुरबलं सर्वं पक्षाघातैरमर्दयत् ॥ १८॥

English
In the meantime the infuriated king of birds suppressed the army of Bāṇa striking all with his wings. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मर्दितं स्वबलं दृष्ट्वा मर्दयन्तं च तं बली ।
चुकोपाति बलेः पुत्रः शैवराड् दितिजेश्वरः ॥ १९॥

English
Seeing his army routed and himself oppressed, Bāṇa, the powerful lord of Daityas, son of Bali, foremost among Śiva's devotees, was infuriated. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं सहस्रभुजवान्द्रुतम् ।
महत्पराक्रमं चक्रे वैरिणां दुःसहं स वै ॥ २०॥

English
Thinking upon the lotus-like feet of Śiva, the thousand-armed Bāṇa displayed his valour unbearable to his enemies. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
चिक्षेप युगपद्बाणानमितांस्तत्र वीरहा ।
कृष्णादिसर्वयदुषु गरुडे च पृथक् पृथक् ॥ २१॥

English
The destroyer of heroes discharged simultaneously an unlimited number of arrows on Garuḍa, Kṛṣṇa and Yadus separately. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
जघानैकेन गरुडं कृष्णमेकेन पत्त्रिणा ।
बलमेकेन च मुने परानपि तथा बली ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, he hit Garuḍa with an arrow, Kṛṣṇa with another, Bala with a third. The powerful hero hit others too. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कृष्णो महावीर्यो विष्णुरूपः सुरारिहा ।
चुकोपातिरणे तस्मिञ्जगर्ज च महेश्वरः ॥ २३॥

English
Then Kṛṣṇa the great lord, of great valour, of the form of Viṣṇu, the destroyer of demons, became angry and roared in the course of the battle. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
जघान बाणं तरसा शार्ङ्गनिःसृतसच्छरैः ।
अति तद्बलमत्युग्रं युगपत्स्मृतशङ्करः ॥ २४॥

English
Thinking upon Śiva he hit Bāṇa and his terrible army simultaneously with the good arrows discharged with force from his bow. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
चिच्छेद तद्धनुः शीघ्रं छत्रादिकमनाकुलः ।
हयांश्च पातयामास हत्वा तान्स्वशरैर्हरिः ॥ २५॥

English
Viṣṇu split his bow and umbrella as well as other things. Without being excited he killed and felled his horses by means of his arrows. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणोऽपि च महावीरो जगर्जाति प्रकुप्य ह ।
कृष्णं जघान गदया सोऽपतद्धरणीतले ॥ २६॥

English
Bāṇa the great hero roared furiously. He hit Kṛṣṇa with his iron club and Kṛṣṇa fell on the ground. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्थायारं ततः कृष्णो युयुधे तेन शत्रुणा ।
शिवभक्तेन देवर्षे लोकलीलाऽनुसारतः ॥ २७॥

English
O celestial sage, Kṛṣṇa got up immediately and fought with Bāṇa, the great devotee of Śiva. It was to keep up the worldly sport that he fought thus. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं द्वयोश्चिरं कालं बभूव सुमहान् रणः ।
शिवरूपो हरिः कृष्णः स च शैवोत्तमो बली ॥ २८॥

English
A great battle went on for a long time between Kṛṣṇa who was Viṣṇu or Śiva himself and that strong Asura who was the most excellent devotee of Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णोऽथ कृत्वा समरं चिरं बाणेन वीर्यवान् ।
शिवाऽऽज्ञया प्राप्तबलश्चुकोपाति मुनीश्वर ॥ २९॥

English
O great sage, the powerful Kṛṣṇa fought for a long time with Bāṇa. Deriving strength at the instance of Śiva he became furious. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःसुदर्शनेनाशु कृष्णो बाणभुजान्बहून् ।
चिच्छेद भगवान् शम्भुः शासनात्परवीरहा ॥ ३०॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva, lord Kṛṣṇa, the destroyer of heroic enemies, chopped off several arms of Bāṇa by means of Sudarśana. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अवशिष्टा भुजास्तस्य चत्वारोऽतीव सुन्दराः ।
गतव्यथो बभूवाशु शङ्करस्य प्रसादतः ॥ ३१॥

English
Only his four beautiful arms were left. Thanks to the grace of Śiva, the demon too was freed from pain. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
गतस्मृतिर्यदा बाणः शिरश्छेत्तुं समुद्यतः ।
कृष्णो वीरत्वमापन्नस्तदा रुद्रः समुत्थितः ॥ ३२॥

English
Forgetting himself, Kṛṣṇa who assumed a great prowess, attempted to cut off the head of Bāṇa. Then Śiva got up. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
भगवन्देवकीपुत्र यदाज्ञप्तं मया पुरा ।
तत्कृतं च त्वया विष्णो मदाज्ञाकारिणा सदा ॥ ३३॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—* O lord, son of Devakī, O Viṣṇu, what was ordained by me formerly has been accomplished by you who always follow my dictates. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मा बाणस्य शिरश्छिन्धि संहरस्व सुदर्शनम् ।
मदाज्ञया चक्रमिमममोघं मज्जने सदा ॥ ३४॥

English
Do not cut off the head of Bāṇa. Withdraw your weapon Sudarśana. At my bidding the discus shall always be rendered ineffective with regard to my people. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
दत्तं मया पुरा तुभ्यमनिवार्यं रणे तव ।
चक्रं जयं च गोविन्द निवर्तस्व रणात्ततः ॥ ३५॥

English
O Viṣṇu, this unfailing discus and victory in battle were formerly bestowed on you by me. Hence you withdraw from the battleground. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीचे रावणे वीरे तारकादिपुरेष्वपि ।

English
O lord of Lakṣmī, you did not hurl this discus at Dadhīca<sup>331</sup> Rāvaṇa, Tāraka, Tripuras and others without my consent. (36.)

[fn 331. It refers to the incident of the battle between Viṣṇu and Dadhīca. Cp. ŚP. RS II Ch 39.]

Sanskrit

English
You are a great Yogin, the supreme soul and the exciter of men. Hence you ponder over this yourself. You are engaged in the welfare of all living beings. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
I have granted him a boon that he will not fear death. These words of mine shall remain true for ever. I am pleased with you. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Viṣṇu, sometime back he became haughty enough to say “Give me battle” while he scratched his arms and forgot his goal. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then I cursed him—“Ere long, the person will arrive and cut off your arms. You shall be cured of your haughtiness”. (40.)

Sanskrit

English
(Turning to Bāṇa) At my bidding Viṣṇu has cut off your arms. Now withdraw from the battlefield. Go back to your abode along with the married couple. (41.)

Sanskrit

English
