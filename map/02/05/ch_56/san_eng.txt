### **CHAPTER FIFTYSIX - *(Bāṇāsura attains the position of Śiva's Gaṇa)***

---

Sanskrit
५६

नारद उवाच ।
कृष्णे गते द्वारकायामनिरुद्धेन भार्यया ।
अकार्षीत्किं ततो बाणस्तत्त्वं वद महामुने ॥ १॥

English
*Nārada said:—* O great sage, when Kṛṣṇa left for Dvārakā along with Aniruddha and his wife, what did Bāṇa do? Please narrate the same to me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
कृष्णे गते द्वारकायामनिरुद्धेन भार्यया ।
दुःखितोऽभूत्ततो बाणः स्वाज्ञानं संस्मरन्हृदा ॥ २॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* When Kṛṣṇa left for Dvārakā with Aniruddha and his wife, Bāṇa was distressed thinking on his previous ignorance. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो नन्दी शिवगणो बाणं प्रोवाच दुःखितम् ।
दैत्यं शोणितदिग्धाङ्गमनुतापसमन्वितम् ॥ ३॥

English
Then Nandin, a gaṇa of Śiva, spoke to the grief-stricken Daitya Bāṇa whose limbs were smeared with blood and who repented repeatedly. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
बाण शङ्करसद्भक्त मानुतापं कुरुष्व भोः ।
भक्तानुकम्पी शम्भुर्वै भक्तवत्सलनामधृक् ॥ ४॥

English
*Nandīśvara said:—*  O Bāṇa, devotee of Śiva, do not repent. Śiva is compassionate towards his devotees. Hence he is called Bhaktavatsala (i.e. favourably disposed to the devotees). (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिच्छया च यज्जातं तज्जातमिति चेतसा ।
मन्यस्व भक्तशार्दूल शिवं स्मर पुनःपुनः ॥ ५॥

English
O foremost among devotees, whatever has happened, has happened at his will. Consider this and remember Śiva again and again. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मन आद्ये समाधाय कुरु नित्यं महोत्सवम् ।
भक्तानुकम्पनश्चाऽस्य शङ्करस्य पुनःपुनः ॥ ६॥

English
Fixing your mind in the primordial being, Śiva who is compassionate towards the devotees, you celebrate his festival again and again. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिवाक्यात्ततो बाणो द्विषा शीर्षकमात्रकः ।
शिवस्थानं जगामाशु धृत्वा धैर्यं महामनाः ॥ ७॥

English
At the suggestion of Nandin, Bāṇa who had been like Rāhu unto his enemies immediately went to Śiva's temple with lofty mind and great courage. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र प्रभुं नत्वा रुरोदातीव विह्वलः ।
गतगर्वव्रजो बाणः प्रेमाकुलितमानसः ॥ ८॥

English
After going there he bowed to the lord and lamented in great agitation. Bāṇa who had been divested of his haughtiness became overwhelmed with love and devotion. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
संस्तुवन्विविधैः स्तोत्रैः सन्नमन्नुतितस्तथा ।
यथोचितं पादघातं कुर्वन्विक्षेपयन्करान् ॥ ९॥

ननर्त ताण्डवं मुख्यं प्रत्यालीढादिशोभितम् ।
स्थानकैर्विविधाकारैरालीढप्रमुखैरपि ॥ १०॥

English
He eulogised him with various hymns. He bowed to the lord in the course of his worship. With proper steps and gestures of hands he performed the Tāṇḍava dance assuming various poses and postures—Pratyālidha, Sthānaka, Āliḍha$^{332}$ being the chief among them. (9-10.)

[fn 332. Āliḍha is a kind of pose in dance, with the right foot advanced and the left retracted. The opposite of this is the pose of Pratyālidha in which the left foot is advanced and the right is drawn back. Sthānaka is a particular attitude of the body in dance.]
---

Sanskrit
मुखवादसहस्राणि भ्रूक्षेपसहितान्यपि ।
शिरःकम्पसहस्राणि प्राप्तानीकः सहस्रशः ॥ ११॥

वारीश्च विविधाकारा दर्शयित्वा शनैः शनैः ।
तथा शोणितधाराभिः सिञ्चयित्वा महीतलम् ॥ १२॥

रुद्रं प्रसादयामास शूलिनं चन्द्रशेखरम् ।
बाणासुरो महाभक्तो विस्मृतात्मगतिर्नतः ॥ १३॥

ततो नृत्यं महत्कृत्वा भगवान्भक्तवत्सलः ।
उवाच बाणं संहृष्टो नृत्य गीतप्रियो हरः ॥ १४॥

English
He produced thousands of gestures through his mouth. He knit and bent his brows and shook his head in various ways. He kept thousands of attendants in rows. He showed various gestures gradually. Much of his blood was shed on the ground. By all these means be propitiated the trident-bearing moon-crested lord. Bāṇāsura the great devotee of Śiva forgot himself and his activities in the course of his worship. Śiva who is fond of dance and music and is favourably disposed to his devotees spoke to Bāṇa. (11-14.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
बाण तात बलेः पुत्र सन्तुष्टो नर्तनेन ते ।
वरं गृहाण दैत्येन्द्र यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ॥ १५॥

English
*Śiva said:—* “Dear Bāṇa, son of Bali, I am delighted by your dance. O lord of Daityas, you choose the boon whatever be in your mind”. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भोर्दैत्येन्द्रेण तदा मुने ।
बाणेन संवृणीतोऽभूद्वरस्तु व्रणरोपणे ॥ १६॥

बाहुयुद्धस्य चोद्धत्तिर्गाणपत्यमथाक्षयम् ।
उषापुत्रस्य राज्यं तु तस्मिन् शोणितकाह्वये ॥ १७॥

निर्वैरता च विबुधैर्विष्णुना च विशेषतः ।
न पुनर्दैत्यता दुष्टा रजसा तमसा युता ॥ १८॥

शम्भुभक्तिर्विशेषेण निर्विकारा सदा मुने ।
शिवभक्तेषु च स्नेहो दया सर्वेषु जन्तुषु ॥ १९॥

इति कृत्वा वरान् शम्भोर्बलिपुत्रो महाऽसुरः ।
प्रेम्णाऽश्रुनयनो रुद्रं तुष्टाव सुकृताञ्जलिः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O sage, on hearing the words of Śiva, Bāṇa the lord of Daityas asked for the healing of wounds, the skill in duels, everlasting position of Gaṇahood, the kingship for Uṣā’s son at the city of Śoṇita, absence of enmity with the gods and Viṣṇu in particular, absence of rebirth as a Daitya defiled by the attributes Rajas and Tamas, special devotion to Śiva without any aberration for ever, friendship with the devotees of Śiva and kindness to all living beings. After requesting for these boons, the son of Bali, the great Asura joined his palms in reverence and eulogised Śiva with tears of love in his eyes. (16-20.)

---

Sanskrit
बाण उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
त्वां नमामि महेशान दीनबन्धो दयानिधे ॥ २१॥

English
*Bāṇa said:—*  O great lord, lord of the gods, favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in you, O great lord, I bow to you, O kinsman of the distressed, O storehouse of mercy. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कृता मयि कृपातीव कृपासागर शङ्कर ।
गर्वोपहारितः सर्वः प्रसन्नेन मम प्रभो ॥ २२॥

English
O Śiva, O ocean of sympathy, you have taken pity on me. O lord, being delighted with me you have removed my arrogance. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं ब्रह्म परमात्मा हि सर्वव्याप्यखिलेश्वरः ।
ब्रह्माण्डतनुरुग्रेशो विराट् सर्वान्वितः परः ॥ २३॥

English
You are Brahman, the great soul, the all-pervading lord. Your body is the whole cosmos. You are Ugra, Iśa, Virāṭ, the great, accompanied by everything. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नाभिर्नभोऽग्निर्वदनमम्बु रेतो दिशः श्रुतिः ।
द्यौः शीर्षमङ्घ्रिरुर्वी ते मनश्चन्द्रस्तव प्रभो ॥ २४॥

English
O lord, your navel is the sky, mouth is the fire, semen is the water, ear the quarters, head the heaven, foot the earth and mind the moon. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
दृगर्को जठरं वार्द्धिर्भुजेन्द्रो धिषणा विधिः ।
प्रजापतिर्विसर्गश्च धर्मो हि हृदयं तव ॥ २५॥

English
Your eye is the sun, the stomach the ocean, the arm Indra, and the intellect Brahmā. Your excretion is Prajāpati and your heart is Dharma. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
रोमाण्यौषधयो नाथ केशा जलमुचस्तव ।
गुणास्त्रयस्त्रिनेत्राणि सर्वात्मा पुरुषो भवान् ॥ २६॥

English
O lord, your hairs are the herbs and plants, your tresses the clouds, your eyes the three attributes. You are the Puruṣa, the soul of all. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणं ते मुखं प्राहुर्बाहुं क्षत्रियमेव च ।
ऊरुजं वैश्यमाहुस्ते पादजं शूद्रमेव च ॥ २७॥

English
They call Brāhmaṇa your mouth, Kṣatriya your arms, Vaiśya your thighs and Śūdra your feet.$^{333}$ (27.)

[fn 333. It refers to the fourfold classification of ancient Indian society as mentioned in the Puruṣasūkta of the Ṛgveda.]
---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव सर्वदोपास्यःसर्वैर्जीवैर्महेश्वर ।
त्वां भजन्परमां मुक्तिं लभते पुरुषो ध्रुवम् ॥ २८॥

English
O lord Śiva, you alone deserve to be adored by all living beings. A person worshipping you certainly derives liberation. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्त्वां विसृजते मर्त्य आत्मानं प्रियमीश्वरम् ।
विपर्ययेन्द्रियार्थार्थं विषमत्त्यमृतं त्यजन् ॥ २९॥

English
O lord, the man who forsakes you, the favourite Ātman, for the adverse objects of sense, swallows poison forsaking nectar. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्ब्रह्माऽथ विबुधा मुनयश्चामलाशयाः ।
सर्वात्मना प्रपन्नास्त्वां शङ्करं प्रियमीश्वरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Viṣṇu, Brahmā, the gods and the sages of pure mind, in every respect resort to you, the favourite lord. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा बलिपुत्रस्तु विरराम शरासुरः ।
प्रेमप्रफुल्लिताङ्गश्च प्रणम्य स महेश्वरम् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* After saying this, the Asura Bāṇa, son of Bali stopped with all the limbs blooming with love and bowed to lord Śiva. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा स्वभक्तस्य बाणस्य भगवान्भवः ।
सर्वं लभिष्यसीत्युक्त्वा तत्रैवान्तरधीयत ॥ ३२॥

English
On hearing the request of his devotee Bāṇa, lord Śiva said “You will get everything” and vanished there itself. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शम्भोः प्रसादेन महाकालत्वमागतः ।
रुद्रस्यानुचरो बाणो महाप्रमुदितोऽभवत् ॥ ३३॥

English
Then through the grace of Śiva, Bāṇa attained the immortality of the eternal time and becoming one of the attendants of Śiva he rejoiced much. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इति किल शरनाम्ना शङ्करस्यापि वृत्तं
सकलगुरुजनानां सद्गुरोः शूलपाणेः ।
कथितमिह वरिष्ठं श्रोत्ररम्यैर्वचोभिः
सकलभुवनमध्ये क्रीडमानस्य नित्यम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Thus I have narrated by means of words pleasing to the ears, the excellent story of the trident-bearing lord Śiva, who is the preceptor of the preceptors and who sports about always in the middle of the worlds. His story includes his activities in relation to Bāṇa. (34.)
