### **CHAPTER FIFTYSEVEN - *(Gajāsura is slain)***

---

Sanskrit
५७

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाप्रेम्णा चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
यथाऽवधीत् त्रिशूलेन दानवेन्द्रं गजासुरम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* O Vyāsa, listen with great devotion to the story of moon-crested lord, how he killed Gajāsura, the lord of Dānavas, by means of his trident. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दानवे निहते देव्या समरे महिषासुरे ।
देवानां च हितार्थाय पुरा देवाः सुखं ययुः ॥ २॥

English
Formerly when the Asura Mahiṣa$^{334}$ was killed in battle by the goddess for the welfare of the gods, they became very happy. (2.)

[fn 334. The Asura Mahiṣa, father of Gaja, was slain in battle by Caṇḍikā. He should be distinguished from the Asura Mahiṣa who was killed by kārttikeya.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रो महावीरो मुनीश्वर गजासुरः ।
पितुर्वधं हि संस्मृत्य कृतं देव्या सुरार्थनात् ॥ ३॥

स तद्वैरमनुस्मृत्य तपोऽर्थं गतवान् वने ।
समुद्दिश्य विधिं प्रीत्या तताप परमं तपः ॥ ४॥

English
O great sage, his son the great hero Gajāsura could not forget the slaying of his father by the goddess at the request of the gods and hence remembered that enmity. He therefore went to the forest for undertaking penance. Interestingly he performed penance meditating on Brahmā. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
अवध्योऽहं भविष्यामि स्त्रीपुंसैः कामनिर्जितैः ।
संविचार्येति मनसाऽभूत्तपोरतमानसः ॥ ५॥

English
"I shall not be killed by men or women overwhelmed by lust." Thinking thus in his mind he directed his attention to austerities. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स तेपे हिमवद् द्रोण्यां तपः परमदारुणम् ।
ऊर्ध्वबाहुर्नभोदृष्टिः पादाङ्गुष्ठाश्रितावनिः ॥ ६॥

English
He performed a severe penance in a valley on the Himālaya mountain. He kept his arms lifted. He fixed his eyes at the sky. He stood on the ground on the two big toes. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
जटाभारैः स वै रेजे प्रलयार्क इवांशुभिः ।
महिषासुरपुत्रोऽसौ गजासुर उदारधीः ॥ ७॥

English
With plenty of matted tresses of hair the benevolent Gajāsura, the son of Mahiṣa shone with his refulgence like the sun at the time of dissolution. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य मूर्ध्नः समुद्भूतः सधूमोऽग्निस्तपोमयः ।
तिर्यगूर्ध्वमधोलोकांस्तापयन्विष्वगीरितः ॥ ८॥

English
The fire in the form of penance originating from his head filled with smoke spread all round to the worlds above, below and on the sides scorching them. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चुक्षुभुर्नद्युदन्वन्तश्चाग्नेर्मूर्द्धसमुद्भवात् ।
निपेतुः सग्रहास्तारा जज्ज्वलुश्च दिशो दश ॥ ९॥

English
The rivers and the seas were agitated by the fire originating from his head. The stars fell along with the planets. The ten quarters blazed. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन तप्ताः सुराः सर्वे दिवं त्यक्त्वा सवासवाः ।
ब्रह्मलोकं ययुर्विज्ञापयामासुश्चचाल भूः ॥ १०॥

English
The gods scorched by the fire left heaven and went to Brahma's world along with Indra and submitted to him. The earth quaked. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
विधे गजासुरतपस्तप्ता वयमथाकुलाः ।
न शक्नुमो दिवि स्थातुमतस्ते शरणं गताः ॥ ११॥

English
*The gods said:—* O Brahmā, we are agitated on being scorched by the penance of Gajāsura. We are unable to stay in heaven. Hence we seek refuge in you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
विधे ह्युपशमं तस्य चान्याञ्जीवयितुं कृथा ।
लोका नङ्क्ष्यत्यन्यथा हि सत्यं सत्यं ब्रुवामहे ॥ १२॥

English
Make it subside and find out a merciful remedy to enliven others. Otherwise the worlds will perish. Truth. It is the truth that we speak. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इति विज्ञापितो देवैर्वासवाद्यैः स आत्मभूः ।
भृगुदक्षादिभिर्ब्रह्मा ययौ दैत्यवराश्रमम् ॥ १३॥

English
Thus informed by the gods including Indra and others the selfborn deity Brahmā went to the hermitage of the excellent Daitya along with Bhṛgu,³³⁵ Dakṣa³³⁶ and others. (13.)

[fn ³³⁵. Bhṛgu was a Prajāpati and a great sage who pounded the race of the Bhṛgus in which Jamadagni and Paraśurāma were born. According to the present Purāṇa he was the son of Brahmā, born out of his heart. (See RS I. 16.4).]

[fn ³³⁶. Dakṣa, son of Brahmā, born out of his breath (Ibid 1.16.5) was a Prajāpati. His daughter Sati was married to Śiva. For details see Ibid. II section.]


---

Sanskrit
तपन्तं तपसा लोकान् यथाऽभ्रापिहितं दिवि ।
विलक्ष्य विस्मितः प्राह विहसन्सृष्टिकारकः ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing him scorching the heaven and the worlds by his penance, the surprised creator laughed and said. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
उत्तिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ दैत्येन्द्र तपः सिद्धोऽसि माहिषे ।
प्राप्तोऽहं वरदस्तात वरं वृणु यथेप्सितम् ॥ १५॥

English
*Brahmā said:—* “O lord of Daityas, stand up, stand up. O son of Mahiṣa, you have achieved perfection in penance. O dear one, I, the granter of boons, have come. Choose your boon as you wish.” (15.)

---

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Getting up in a hurry, the son of Mahiṣa, the lord of Daityas glanced at the lord and praised him lovingly with choking words. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
गजासुर उवाच ।
नमस्ते देवदेवेश यदि दास्यसि मे वरम् ।
अवध्योऽहं भवेयं वै स्त्रीपुंसैः कामनिर्जितैः ॥ १७॥

महाबलो महावीर्योऽजेयो देवादिभिः सदा ।
सर्वेषां लोकपालानां निखिलर्द्धिसुभुग्विभो ॥ १८॥

English
Gajāsura said:—  O lord, O lord of the gods, if you are going to grant me a boon let me be immune from death by men or women overwhelmed by lust. Let me be very powerful, valorous and invincible to the gods, the guardians of the worlds³³⁷ and others for ever. Let me enjoy all prosperities. (17-18.)

[fn ³³⁷. Lokapālas are the regents of the four cardinal and four intermediate points of the world. They are:

इन्द्रो वह्निः पितृपतिनै श्रुतो वरुणो मरुत् ।
कुबेर ईशः पतयः पूर्वादीनां दिशां कमात् ॥]
---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवं वृतः शतधृतिर्दानवेन स तेन वै ।
प्रादात्तत्तपसा प्रीतो वरं तस्य सुदुर्लभम् ॥ १९॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus requested by the Dānava, Brahmā$^{338}$ who was delighted by his penance granted him the rare boon. (19.)

[fn 338. Brahmā assumed the name Śatadhṛti after completing a hundred sacrifices. This name he shares with Indra, the performer of a hundred horse-sacrifices.]


---

Sanskrit
एवं लब्धवरो दैत्यो माहिषिश्च गजासुरः ।
सुप्रसन्नमनाः सोऽथ स्वधाम प्रत्यपद्यत ॥ २०॥

English
Securing the boons thus, the Daitya Gajāsura the son of Mahiṣa, returned to his abode with a happy mind. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
स विजित्य दिशः सर्वा लोकांश्च त्रीन्महासुरः ।
देवासुरमनुष्येन्द्रान् गन्धर्वगरुडोरगान् ॥ २१॥

इत्यादीन्निखिलाञ्जित्वा वशमानीय विश्वजित् ।
जहार लोकपालानां स्थानानि सह तेजसा ॥ २२॥

English
The great Asura conquered all the quarters, the three worlds, the gods, Asuras, human beings, kings, Gandharvas, Garuḍa, serpents and others. He made them subservient to him. He became the conqueror of the universe. He usurped the places of the guardians of the worlds and took away their glory. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
देवोद्यानश्रियाजुष्टमध्यास्ते स्म त्रिविष्टपम् ।
महेन्द्रभवनं साक्षान्निर्मितं विश्वकर्मणा ॥ २३॥

English
He occupied heaven$^{339}$ possessing the glory of the celestial garden and the palace of lord Indra built by Viśvakarman.$^{340}$ (23.)

[fn 339. See Note 298 P. 992.]

[fn 340. Viśvakarman was the founder of the science of architecture and mechanics among the gods. He is credited with the construction of the royal palace for lord Indra.]
---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्महेन्द्रस्य गृहे महाबलो
महामना निर्जितलोक एकराट् ।
रेमेऽभिवन्द्याङ्घ्रियुगः सुरादिभिः
प्रतापितैरूर्जितचण्डशासनः ॥ २४॥

English
The powerful Daitya of a lofty mind who reigned as the sole ruler after conquering all the worlds sported in the palace of lord Indra. The gods and others who were overwhelmed by his power worshipped the pair of his feet. He exercised a stern and fierce authority. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स इत्थं निर्जितककुबेकराड् विषयान्प्रियान् ।
यथोपजोषं भुञ्जानो नातृप्यदजितेन्द्रियः ॥ २५॥

English
Thus conquering the quarters and reigning as the supreme overlord he enjoyed pleasures to the maximum extent. Since he had not conquered his senses he was never satiated in his enjoyment of pleasures. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमैश्वर्यमत्तस्य दृप्तस्योच्छास्त्रवर्तिनः ।
काले व्यतीते महति पापबुद्धिरभूत्ततः ॥ २६॥

English
He was haughty, puffed up with prosperity. He slighted and transgressed the injunctions of the sacred scriptures. After the lapse of some time he became evil-minded. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
महिषासुरपुत्रोऽसौ सञ्चिक्लेश द्विजान्वरान् ।
तापसान्नितरां पृथ्व्यां दानवः सुरमर्दनः ॥ २७॥

English
The Dānava, the suppressor of the gods, the son of Mahiṣāsura harassed the excellent brahmins and the sages on the earth very much. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरान्नरांश्च प्रमथान्सर्वाञ्चिक्लेशदुर्मतिः ।
धर्मान्वितान्विशेषेण पूर्ववैरमनुस्मरन् ॥ २८॥

English
The wicked Daitya harassed the gods, the human beings, and all the Pramathas. He tortured all righteous people particularly recollecting the previous enmity. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्समये तात दानवोऽसौ महाबलः ।
अगच्छद्राजधानीं व शङ्करस्य गजासुरः ॥ २९॥

English
O dear, once this Dānava of great strength, Gajāśura came to the capital city of Śiva. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
समागतेऽसुरेन्द्रे हि महान्कलकलो मुने ।
त्रात त्रातेति तत्रासीदानन्दनवासिनाम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O sage, when the lord of Asuras came there, there was a great tumult among the residents of Ānandavana, They shouted “Protect, Protect.” (30.)

---

Sanskrit
महिषाऽसुरपुत्रोऽसौ यदा पुर्यां समागतः ।
प्रमथन्प्रमथान्सर्वान्निजवीर्यमदोद्धतः ॥ ३१॥

तस्मिन्नवसरे देवाः शक्राद्यास्तत्पराजिताः ।
शिवस्य शरणं जग्मुर्नत्वा तुष्टुवुरादरात् ॥ ३२॥

English
When this son of Mahiṣāsura haughty of his valour entered the city suppressing the Pramathas, Indra and other gods previously vanquished by him sought refuge in Śiva. After bowing to him they eulogised him with a great respect. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
न्यवेदयन्दानवस्य तस्य काश्यां समागमम् ।
क्लेशाधिक्यं तत्रत्यानां तन्नाथानां विशेषतः ॥ ३३॥

English
They mentioned to him the arrival of the Dānava at Kāśī, the height of distress of the people there, especially of the rulers. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव तव पुर्य्यां गतोऽसुरः ।
कष्टं दत्ते त्वज्जनानां तं जहि त्वं कृपानिधे ॥ ३४॥

English
*The gods said:—* O great gods, O lord of gods, the Asura has gone to your city. He is inflicting pain on your people. O storehouse of benignity, please slay him. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

अचलां सचलां तत्र करोति निज भारतः ॥ ३५॥

English
Wherever he sets foot on the earth he shakes the ground there by his great weight. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ऊरुवेगेन तरवः पतन्ति शिखरैः सह ।
यस्य दोर्दण्डघातेन चूर्णाः स्युश्च शिलोच्चयाः ॥ ३६॥

English
By his great velocity trees fall down with roots and branches. Hit by his brawny arms, big mountains are reduced to powder. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्य मौलिजसङ्घर्षाद् घना व्योम त्यजन्त्यपि ।

English
The clouds leave the sky hit by his head. Still they do not lose their blueness due to the contact with his hair. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

नद्योऽप्यमन्दकल्लोला भवन्ति तिमिभिः सह ॥ ३८॥

English
When he breathes out, the mighty oceans surge up with their billows. Even the rivers are filled with waves as though with whales. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
His height is nine thousand Yojanas. The girth of this Asura who wields Māyā is also of that proportion. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The tawny colour and the tremulousness of his eyes is not borne even by the lightning. Thus he has come there all of a sudden. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Whichever quarter he approaches, the Dānava is unbearably oppressive. “I am not to be killed by men or women overwhelmed by lust” he shouts. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O lord of the gods, we have thus mentioned humbly the activities of that Dānava. Please protect your devotees, O lord, desirous of protecting Kāśī. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—* Thus requested by the gods, Śiva desirous of protecting the devotees came there quickly with the desire of slaying him. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing that Śiva, favourably disposed to his devotees, had come roaring with the trident in his hand, Gajāsura too roared. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
A wonderfully terrible and great battle was fought between them roaring heroically and hitting with various weapons and missiles. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The brilliant Gajāsura of great strength and valour pierced Śiva, the slayer of the Dānavas, with sharp arrows. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, Śiva who assumed a terrible body, split with his terrible arrows, the arrows of the Daitya to small pieces like gingelly seeds, even before they reached him. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English
