### **CHAPTER ONE - (The five incarnations of the supreme Brahman)**


---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
व्यासशिष्य महाभाग सूत ज्ञानदयानिधे ।
वद शम्भ्ववतारांश्च यैरकार्षीत्सतां शिवम् ॥ १॥

English

I bow to lord Śiva of great bliss and of endless divine sports, who is great and omnipresent, who is the beloved consort of Pārvatī, who is the progenitor of Kārttikeya and Gaṇeśa, and who is the primordial lord of the universe.

*Śaunaka said :—* O Sūta, O great fortunate disciple of Vyāsa, O storehouse of wisdom and mercy, please narrate the incarnations of lord Śiva by means of which the lord enhanced the welfare of the good. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
मुने शौनक सद्भक्त्या दत्तचित्तो जितेन्द्रियः ।
अवताराञ्छिवस्याहं वच्मि ते मुनये श‍ृणु ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O sage Śaunaka, with great devotion I shall narrate the incarnations¹ of Śiva to you a sage. Listen to it with attention and control over the senses. (2.)

[fn 1. The concept of Rudrasṛṣṭi can be traced as far back as the Vedic Literature. According to Yv 16: 4 one Rudra created many Rudras out of his own body. The Purāṇas too have recorded the tradition of many Rudras evolved out of a single Rudra. The present Saṃhitā describes one hundred incarnations of Śiva and is therefore called Śatarudra.

But according to ŚB (9.1.1.7), Śatarudriya means a single hundred-headed Śiva (शतशीर्षणं रुद्रं शतरुद्रियमित्याचक्षते). This statement of Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa abrogates the concept of many Rudras evolved out of a single Rudra. But it is not corroborated by the evidence of the present Saṃhitā which enumerates the countless forms of Rudra.]
---

Sanskrit
एतत्पृष्टः पुरा नन्दी शिवमूर्तिस्सतां गतिः ।
सनत्कुमारेण मुने तमुवाच शिवं स्मरन् ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, formerly, Nandin, a form of Śiva and goal of the good, when asked by Sanatkumāra, told him the narrative with devotion to Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
असङ्ख्याता हि कल्पेषु विभोः सर्वेश्वरस्य वै ।
अवतारास्तथापीह वच्म्यहं तान्यथामति ॥ ४॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  The incarnations of the omnipresent lord Śiva are countless in the different aeons.² Yet I shall narrate them as far as I know. (4.)

[fn 2. A kalpa is a day of Brahmā or one thousand yugas equal to a period of four hundred thirty two million years of mortals. Each kalpa is presided over by a particular form of Rudra. For instance, the Śvetalohita kalpa has Sadyojāta as the presiding deity, Rakta has Vāmadeva, Pitavāsas has Tatpuruṣa, Śiva has Aghora and Viśva-rūpa has Iṣāna.]
---

Sanskrit
एकोनविंशकः कल्पो विज्ञेयः श्वेतलोहितः ।
सद्योजातावतारस्तु प्रथमः परिकीर्तितः ॥ ५॥

English
The nineteenth Kalpa is known as Śvetalohita. The first incarnation is glorified as the incarnation of Sadyojāta. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिंस्तत्परमं ब्रह्म ध्यायतो ब्रह्मणस्तथा ।
उत्पन्नस्तु शिखायुक्तः कुमारः श्वेतलोहितः ॥ ६॥

English
In that Kalpa, Śvetalohita, endowed with a tuft, was born as the son of Brahmā as he meditated upon the supreme Brahman. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा पुरुषं ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मरूपिणमीश्वरम् ।
ज्ञात्वा ध्यात्वा स हृदये ववन्दे प्रयताञ्जलिः ॥ ७॥

English
On seeing that Being in the form of Brahman and realising that to be Īśvara, Brahmā meditated on him in his heart and saluted him with palms joined in reverence. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्योजातं शिवं बुद्ध्वा जहर्ष भुवनेश्वरः ।
मुहुर्मुहुश्च सद्बुद्ध्या परं तं समचिन्तयत् ॥ ८॥

English
Knowing that Sadyojāta to be Śiva, Brahmā, the lord of the worlds became delighted. With pure intelligence he pondered over that great Being again and again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽस्य ध्यायतः श्वेताः प्रादुर्भूता यशस्विनः ।
कुमाराः परविज्ञानपरब्रह्मस्वरूपिणः ॥ ९॥

English
As he thus meditated, sons white in colour and famous for valour, were born to him. They were of great wisdom and had the form of the Supreme Brahman. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सुनन्दो नन्दनश्चैव विश्वनन्दोपनन्दनौ ।
शिष्यास्तस्य महात्मानो यैस्तद्ब्रह्म समावृतम् ॥ १०॥

English
They were Sunanda, Nandana, Viśvananda and Upanandana. They were the disciples of that noble soul. By them the Brahman was encircled. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्योजातश्च वै शम्भुर्ददौ ज्ञानं च वेधसे ।
सर्गशक्तिमपि प्रीत्या प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ॥ ११॥

English
The delighted great lord, the Sadyojāta incarnation of Śiva, lovingly bestowed on Brahmā the perfect wisdom and the power of creation. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विंशतिमः कल्पो रक्तो नाम प्रकीर्तितः ।
ब्रह्मा यत्र महातेजा रक्तवर्णमधारयत् ॥ १२॥

English
The next Kalpa, the twentieth one is named and glorified as Rakta (red) wherein Brahmā assumed a great splendour red in hue. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायतः पुत्रकामस्य प्रादुर्भूतो विधेः सुतः ।
रक्तमाल्याम्बरधरो रक्ताक्षो रक्तभूषणः ॥ १३॥

English
As Brahmā desirous of sons meditated, a son appeared in front of him wearing red garments and garlands. His eyes were red and his ornaments too were red. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स तं दृष्ट्वा महात्मानं कुमारं ध्यानमाश्रितः ।
वामदेवं शिवं ज्ञात्वा प्रणनाम कृताञ्जलिः ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing that noble-souled son, he resorting to meditation knew him as the Vāmadeva incarnation of Śiva and so bowed to him with palms joined in reverence. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तस्य सुता ह्यासंश्चत्वारो रक्तवाससः ।
विरजाश्च विवाहश्च विशोको विश्वभावनः ॥ १५॥

English
Your sons wearing red garments were born of him Viz. Virajas, Vivāha, Viśoka and Viśvabhāvana. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वामदेवः स वै शम्भुर्ददौ ज्ञानं च वेधसे ।
सर्गशक्तिमपि प्रीत्या प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ॥ १६॥

English
The delighted Parameśvara, the Vāmadeva incarnation of Śiva bestowed on Brahmā lovingly, the power of creation and perfect wisdom. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एकविंशतिमः कल्पः पीतवासा इति स्मृतः ।
ब्रह्मा यत्र महाभागः पीतवासा बभूव ह ॥ १७॥

English
The twenty-first Kalpa is said to be Pitavāsas wherein Brahmā of great achievement became yellow-robed. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायतः पुत्रकामस्य विधेर्जातः कुमारकः ।
पीतवस्त्रादिकप्रौढो महातेजा महाभुजः ॥ १८॥

English
As Brahmā desirous of sons meditated, a son was born unto him mature, of great arms and great splendour and wearing yellow garments. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा ध्यानसंयुक्तं ज्ञात्वा तत्पुरुषं शिवम् ।
प्रणनाम ततो बुद्ध्या गायत्रीं शाङ्करीं विधिः ॥ १९॥

जपित्वा तु महादेवीं सर्वलोकनमस्कृताम् ।
प्रसन्नस्तु महादेवो ध्यानयुक्तेन चेतसा ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing him engrossed in meditation and realising that Being as Śiva, the Creator bowed to him after performing the Japa of Śiva-Gāyatrī, who is the great goddess, bowed to by all the worlds. The great god was delighted with the mind in contemplation. (19-20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽस्य पार्श्वतो दिव्याः प्रादुर्भूताः कुमारकाः ।
पीतवस्त्रा हि सकला योगमार्गप्रवर्तकाः ॥ २१॥

English
Then from his sides sons of divine features issued forth. All of them had yellow garments and they were the instigators of the Yogic path. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तस्मिन्गते कल्पे पीतवर्णे स्वयम्भुवः ।
पुनरन्यः प्रवृत्तस्तु कल्पो नाम्ना शिवस्तु स ॥ २२॥

English
When that Kalpa of yellow colour of the selfborn lord elapsed, another Kalpa set in and it was named Śiva. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एकार्णवे संव्यतीते दिव्यवर्षसहस्रके ।
स्रष्टुकामः प्रजा ब्रह्मा चिन्तयामास दुःखितः ॥ २३॥

English
After a thousand divine years had elapsed and the entire universe had become one vast ocean,³ Brahmā desirous of creating subjects thought in sorrow. (23.)

[fn 3. Ekārṇava symbolises the state of the universe during the period of dissolution when the divided units are drawn together forming a single watery mass. For details see MP.—A Study Pp 9-10.]

Sanskrit
ततोऽपश्यन्महातेजाः प्रादुर्भूतं कुमारकम् ।
कृष्णवर्णं महावीर्यं दीप्यमानं स्वतेजसा ॥ २४॥

English
That lord of great splendour saw a son coming into view. He was black in colour, possessed of great strength. He was shining in his own splendour. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
धृतकृष्णाम्बरोष्णीषं कृष्णयज्ञोपवीतिनम् ।
कृष्णेन मौलिनायुक्तं कृष्णस्नानानुलेपनम् ॥ २५॥

English
He was clad in black clothes and a black turban. He wore a black sacred thread. He had a black coronet, black toilet and unguent articles. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
स तं दृष्ट्वा महात्मानमघोरं घोरविक्रमम् ।
ववन्दे देवदेवेशमद्भुतं कृष्णपिङ्गलम् ॥ २६॥

English
On seeing that noble soul of terrible exploits, (named) Aghora (non-terrible), he worshipped him—the lord of god, of wonderful black and tawny colour. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अघोरं तु ततो ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मरूपं व्यचिन्तयत् ।
तुष्टाव वाग्भिरिष्टाभिर्भक्तवत्सलमव्ययम् ॥ २७॥

English
Brahmā then contemplated on Aghora in the form of Brahman. He eulogised that unperishing Being favourably disposed towards his devotees, by means of pleasing words. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथास्य पार्श्वतः कृष्णाः कृष्णस्नानानुलेपनाः ।
चत्वारस्तु महात्मानः सम्बभूवुः कुमारकाः ॥ २८॥

English
Then from his sides sprang up four noble-souled sons all of whom were black in colour and had black toilet and unguent articles. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्ण कृष्णशिखश्चैव कृष्णास्यः कृष्णकण्ठधृक् ।
इति तेऽव्यक्तनामानः शिवरूपाः सुतेजसः ॥ २९॥

English
These brilliant beings in the form of Śiva had the clear names Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇaśikha, Kṛṣṇāsya and Kṛṣṇakaṇṭha-dhṛk (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एवम्भूता महात्मानो ब्रह्मणः सृष्टिहेतवे ।

English
The noble-souled beings of this nature initiated the great and wonderful Yoga called Ghora for the purpose of Brahmā's creation. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O great sages, then another extremely wonderful Kalpa of Brahmā, named Viśvarūpa began to function. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

English
As Brahmā, desirous of sons, meditated on Śiva mentally, Sarasvatī (Goddess of speech) of great sound manifested herself. She was Viś pā (Universe-formed). (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Iśāna of such renowned features, supreme lord of crystal-like pure brilliance and bedecked in all ornaments manifested himself. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा प्रणनामासौ ब्रह्मेशानमजं विभुम् ।

English
On seeing Iś the unborn lord, all pervading, all-bestowing, the all-in-all, of good forms as well as formless, Brahmā bowed to him. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Lord Iśāna after instructing the path of the good to Brahmā, created four auspicious sons in collaboration with his Energy. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit
वपुषश्च रसस्यापि रूपस्याग्नेस्तथैव च ।
अघोराख्यमधिष्ठानं रूपं प्राहुर्मनीषिणः ॥ ४६॥

English
Intelligent persons call the form Aghora, the support of the physical body, the sense of taste, of colour and of fire as well. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
रशनायाश्च पायोश्च रसस्यापां तथैव च ।
ईश्वरं वामदेवाख्यं स्वरूपं शाङ्करं स्मृतम् ॥ ४७॥

English
Vāmadeva form of Śiva is remembered as the lord of the organ of taste, of the organ of excretion, of taste itself and of waters as well. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
घ्राणस्य चैवोपस्थस्य गन्धस्य च भुवस्तथा ।
सद्योजाताह्वयं रूपमीश्वरं शाङ्करं विदुः ॥ ४८॥

English
They know the form of Sadyojata as the lord of the organ of smell, of smell itself, of the earth and of the generative organ. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
इमे स्वरूपाः शम्भोर्हि वन्दनीयाः प्रयत्नतः ।
श्रेयोऽर्थिभिर्नरैर्नित्यं श्रेयसामेकहेतवः ॥ ४९॥

English
These forms of Siva, the source of all glory shall be honoured and bowed to strenuously by those who seek glory. (49.)

---

English
He who reads and listens to the origin of Sadyojata and others, enjoys all desires and attains the ultimate goal. (50.)
