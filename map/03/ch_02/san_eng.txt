### **CHAPTER TWO - (The eight forms of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात महेशस्यावतारान्परमान्प्रभो ।
सर्वकार्यकराँल्लोके सर्वस्य सुखदां मुने ॥ १॥

English
Nanditvara said :— O dear sage, listen to the great incarnations of lord Śiva that carry out all worldly activities and bestow happiness on all. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य शम्भोः परेशस्य मूर्त्यष्टकमयं जगत् ।
तस्मिन्व्याप्य स्थितं विश्वं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव ॥ २॥

English
The universe consists of the eight forms of lord Śiva.<sup>5</sup> Pervading that the universe subsists like the pearls in the string. (2.)

[fn 5. The eight forms of lord Śiva constitute the five gross material elements, the soul, the sun and the moon. Each form stands is relation to its constituents as follows :—

(i) Śarva—earth (ii) Bhava—water (iii) Rudra—fire (iv) Ugra—wind (v) Bhīma—ether (vi) Paśupati—soul (vii) Iśāna—sun (viii) Mahādeva—moon.]
---

Sanskrit
शर्वो भवस्तथा रुद्र उग्रो भीमः पशोः पतिः ।
ईशानश्च महादेवो मूर्तयश्चाष्ट विश्रुताः ॥ ३॥

English
The eight presiding deities are Śarva, Bhava, Rudra, Ugra, Bhīma, Paśupati, Iśāna and Mahādeva. They are well known. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भूम्यम्भोऽग्निमरुद्व्योमक्षेत्रज्ञार्कनिशाकराः ।
अधिष्ठिताश्च शर्वाद्यैरष्टरूपैः शिवस्य हि ॥ ४॥

English
By these eight forms of Śiva, viz. Śarva etc, the earth, the waters, the fire, the wind, the sky, the individual soul, the sun and the moon are presided over. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
धत्ते चराचरं विश्वं रूपं विश्वम्भरात्मकम् ।
शङ्करस्य महेशस्य शास्त्रस्यैवेति निश्चयः ॥ ५॥

English
The conclusion of all sacred lore is that the form of lord Śiva consisting of the earth that holds the entire universe of the mobile and immobile beings is called Śarva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सञ्जीवनं समस्तस्य जगतः सलिलात्मकम् ।
भव इत्युच्यते रूपं भवस्य परमात्मनः ॥ ६॥

English
The form of the Supreme Soul, consisting of water that enlivens the entire world, is called Bhava. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
बहिरन्तर्जगद्विश्वं बिभर्ति स्पन्दते स्वयम् ।
उग्र इत्युच्यते सद्भी रूपमुग्रस्य सत्प्रभोः ॥ ७॥

English
The form of the great lord Śiva that holds the outer and inner universe and throbs by itself is called Ugra by the noble. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वावकाशदं सर्वव्यापकं गगनात्मकम् ।
रूपं भीमस्य भीमाख्यं भूपवृन्दस्व भेदकम् ॥ ८॥

English
That form of lord Śiva consisting of the firmament that pervades everything and affords space to all is called Bhīma and it splits and differentiates the group of elements. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वात्मनामधिष्ठानं सर्वक्षेत्रनिवासकम् ।
रूपं पशुपतेर्ज्ञेयं पशुपाशनिकृन्तनम् ॥ ९॥

English
That form of Śiva shall be known as Paśupati which is the support of individual souls, and abiding therein splits the bondage that encircles them. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्दीपयञ्जगत्सर्वं दिवाकरसमाह्वयम् ।
ईशानाख्यं महेशस्य रूपं दिवि विसर्पति ॥ १०॥

English
Brightening the entire universe in the form of the sun, the form of Śiva, Iśāna, moves about in the heaven. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
आप्याययति यो विश्वममृतांशुर्निशाकरः ।
महादेवस्य तद्रूपं महादेवस्य चाह्वयम् ॥ ११॥

English
The moon of nectarlike rays that strengthens and fattens the universe to satiety, is the form of lord Śiva called Mahadeva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मा तस्याष्टमं रूपं शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
व्यापिकेतरमूर्तीनां विश्वं तस्माच्छिवात्मकम् ॥ १२॥

English
The eighth form of Siva, the great soul, is the Atman, more pervasive than the other forms. The universe is pervaded by that form. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शाखाः पुष्यन्ति वृक्षस्य वृक्षमूलस्य सेचनात् ।
तद्वदस्य वपुर्विश्वं पुष्यते च शिवार्चनात् ॥ १३॥

English
By watering the root of a tree the branches of the tree blossom. Similarly the body of the universe develops by means of worshipping Siva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यथेह पुत्रपौत्रादेः प्रीत्या प्रीतो भवेत्पिता ।
तथा विश्वस्य सम्प्रीत्या प्रीतो भवति शङ्करः ॥ १४॥

English
Just as a father is delighted at the pleasures of sons, grandsons and others, so also Siva is delighted at the pleasures of the universe. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रियते यस्य कस्यापि देहिनो यदि निग्रहः ।
अष्टमूर्तेरनिष्टं तत्कृतमेव न संशयः ॥ १५॥

English
Should the slaughter or captivity of a person be carried out, undoubtedly it is an offence committed against the eight-formed lord Siva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टमूर्त्यात्मना विश्वमधिष्ठायास्थितं शिवम् ।
भजस्व सर्वभावेन रुद्रं परमकारणम् ॥ १६॥

English
Worship Siva who presides over the universe in the eightfold forms with all devotion. Worship Rudra, the great cause. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रोक्ताः स्वरूपास्ते विधिपुत्राष्टविश्रुताः ।
सर्वोपकारनिरताः सेव्याः श्रेयोऽर्थिभिर्नरैः ॥ १७॥

English
O son of Brahma, thus the well known eight forms have been explained to you. These forms engaged in helping everyone shall be resorted to by those who seek glory. (17.)
