### **CHAPTER THREE - (The half-female incarnation of Lord Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात महाप्राज्ञ विधिकामप्रपूरकम् ।
अर्द्धनारीनराख्यं हि शिवरूपमनुत्तमम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said :—  O dear one of great intellect, please listen to the description of Siva's form half-male and half-female<sup>6</sup>, than which there is nothing more excellent and which completes the work of the Creator. (1.)

[fn 6. The half-male and half-female (ardhanārisvara) form of Siva is symbolical of the process of creation by copulation. The concept is comparable to the Sāṃkhya doctrine of Puruṣa (cosmic soul) and Prakṛti (cosmic nature) whose union produces the different units of the universe.]
---

Sanskrit
यदा सृष्टाः प्रजाः सर्वाः न व्यवर्द्धन्त वेधसा ।
तदा चिन्ताकुलोऽभूत्स तेन दुःखेन दुखितः ॥ २॥

English
When the subjects created by the Creator did not multiply, he was very much distressed and worried. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नभोवाणी तदाभूद्वै सृष्टिं मिथुनजां कुरु ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा मैथुनीं सृष्टिं ब्रह्मा कर्तुममन्यत ॥ ३॥

English
Then a celestial voice said—“Carry on the creation produced by couples.” On hearing it, Brahmā pondered over the creation produced by couples in order to carry it out. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नारीणां कुलमीशानान्निर्गतं न पुरा यतः ।
ततो मैथुनजां सृष्टिं कर्तुं शेके न पद्मभूः ॥ ४॥

English
Since no generation of women had formerly come out of Iśāna, the lotus-born deity (Brahmā) was unable to produce the creation by means of copulation. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभावेण विना शम्भोर्न जायेरन्निमाः प्रजाः ।
एवं सञ्चिन्तयन्ब्रह्मा तपः कर्त्तुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā then began to perform penance thinking “Without the power of Śiva, the subjects will not be born.” (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवया परया शक्त्या संयुक्तं परमेश्वरम् ।
सञ्चिन्त्य हृदये प्रीत्या तेपे स परमं तपः ॥ ६॥

English
Within his heart he pondered lovingly over lord Śiva united with the great Śakti called Śivā. He then performed great penance. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तीव्रेण तपसा तस्य संयुक्तस्य स्वयम्भुवः ।
अचिरेणैव कालेन तुतोष स शिवो द्रुतम् ॥ ७॥

English
Ere long Śiva became satisfied with the severe penance of the self-born lord Brahmā. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पूर्णचिदीशस्य मूर्तिमाविश्य कामदाम् ।
अर्द्धनारीनरो भूत्वा ततो ब्रह्मान्तिकं हरः ॥ ८॥

English
Then assuming the wish-yielding form of Iśāna—the perfect consciousness, and in the guise of half-male and half-female form Śiva approached Brahmā. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा शङ्करं देवं शक्त्या परमयान्वितम् ।
प्रणम्य दण्डवद्ब्रह्मा स तुष्टाव कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing lord Śiva united with great Śakti and bowing to him prostrate, Brahmā eulogised him with palms joined in reverence. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवो महादेवो वाचा मेघगभीरया ।
सम्भवाय सुसम्प्रीतो विश्वकर्ता महेश्वरः ॥ १०॥

English
Then the delighted lord Śiva, the creator of the universe, spoke in words majestic in sound like that of rumbling cloud. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
वत्स वत्स महाभाग मम पुत्र पितामह ।
ज्ञातवानस्मि सर्वं तत्तत्त्वतस्ते मनोरथम् ॥ ११॥

English
Lord Śiva said :—  “O dear son of great fortune, O grandfather, I have understood correctly all that you have cherished in your mind. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजानामेव वृद्ध्यर्थं तपस्तप्तं त्वयाधुना ।
तपसा तेन तुष्टोऽस्मि ददामि च तवेप्सितम् ॥ १२॥

English
It is for the increase of the subjects that this penance has been performed by you now. I am delighted by that penance. I shall confer on you what you desire.” (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा परमोदारं स्वभावमधुरं वचः ।
पृथक्चकार वपुषो भागाद्देवीं शिवां शिवः ॥ १३॥

English
After saying these naturally sweet and magnanimous words, lord Śiva detached Śivā from his body. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा परमां शक्तिं पृथग्भूतां शिवागताम् ।
प्रणिपत्य विनीतात्मा प्रार्थयामास तां विधिः ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing that great Śakti, detached separately from Śiva, Brahmā bowed to her politely and requested her. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
देवदेवेन सृष्टोऽहमादौ त्वत्पतिना शिवे ।
प्रजाः सर्वा नियुक्ताश्च शम्भुना परमात्मना ॥ १५॥

English
Brahmā said :—  “O Śivā, I was created at first by your husband the lord of gods. I was ordered by Śiva the great soul to create the subjects. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसा निर्मिताः सर्वे शिवे देवादयो मया ।
न वृद्धिमुपगच्छन्ति सृज्यमानाः पुनःपुनः ॥ १६॥

English
O Śivā, the gods and others were mentally created by me. But they do not multiply themselves. Hence they are being created again and again. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मिथुनप्रभवामेव कृत्वा सृष्टिमतः परम् ।
संवर्द्धयितुमिच्छामि सर्वा एव मम प्रजाः ॥ १७॥

English
Hereafter I wish to make all my subjects flourish by making the creation originate from pairs. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
न निर्गतं पुरा त्वत्तो नारीणां कुलमव्ययम् ।
तेन नारीकुलं स्रष्टुं मम शक्तिर्न विद्यते ॥ १८॥

English
The unchanging generation of women has not come out of you before. Hence I do not have the power to create women. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वासामेव शक्तीनां त्वत्तः खलु समुद्भवः ।
तस्मात्त्वां परमां शक्तिं प्रार्थयाम्यखिलेश्वरीम् ॥ १९॥

English
All Śaktis spring up from you. Hence I request you the great Śakti, the goddess of all. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवे नारीकुलं स्रष्टुं शक्तिं देहि नमोऽस्तु ते ।
चराचरजगद्वृद्धिहेतोर्मातः शिवप्रिये ॥ २०॥

English
O Śivā, O mother, O beloved of Śiva, for the increase of the mobile and immobile beings of the universe, give me the power to create women. Obeisance be to you. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यं त्वत्तः प्रार्थयामि वरं च वरदेश्वरि ।
देहि मे तं कृपां कृत्वा जगन्मातर्नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २१॥

English
O goddess bestowing boons, I request for another boon from you. O mother of the universe, take pity on me and grant that too unto me. Obeisance be to you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
चराचरविवृद्ध्यर्थमीशेनैकेन सर्वगे ।
दक्षस्य मम पुत्रस्य पुत्री भव भवाम्बिके ॥ २२॥

English
O omnipresent mother, be born as the daughter of Dakṣa, my son, for the increase of the mobile and immobile beings through only Iśa.” (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं संयाचिता देवी ब्रह्मणा परमेश्वरी ।
तथास्त्विति वचः प्रोच्य तच्छक्तिं विधये ददौ ॥ २३॥

English
Thus implored by the Creator, the Supreme goddess consented and bestowed that power upon the Creator. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्धि सा शिवा देवी शिवशक्तिर्जगन्मयी ।
शक्तिमेकां भ्रुवोर्मध्यात्ससर्जात्मसमप्रभाम् ॥ २४॥

English
Hence that goddess Śivā, the Śakti of Śiva constituting the universe, created a Śakti equal in lustre unto her self, from the middle of her eyebrows. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तामाह प्रहसन्प्रेक्ष्य शक्तिं देववरो हरः ।
कृपासिन्धुर्महेशानो लीलाकारी भवाम्बिकाम् ॥ २५॥

English
Śiva, the foremost among gods, the ocean of mercy, the great Iśāna indulging in divine sports saw that Śakti and laughingly spoke to her. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
तपसाराधिता देवि ब्रह्मणा परमेष्ठिना ।
प्रसन्ना भव सुप्रीत्या कुरु तस्याखिलेप्सितम् ॥ २६॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—* “O goddess, you have been propitiated by Brahmā, through his penance. Lovingly become pleased with him and fulfil his desires.” (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तामाज्ञां परमेशस्य शिरसा प्रतिगृह्य सा ।
ब्रह्मणो वचनाद्देवी दक्षस्य दुहिताऽभवत् ॥ २७॥

English
Receiving that behest of lord Śiva with her bent head, the goddess became the daughter of Dakṣa on the importunity of Brahmā. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
दत्त्वैवमतुलां शक्तिं ब्रह्मणे सा शिवा मुने ।`
विवेश देहं शम्भोर्हि शम्भुश्चान्तर्दधे प्रभुः ॥ २८॥

English
O sage, after giving the unequalled power to Brahmā, the goddess Śivā entered the body of Śiva. And lord Śiva vanished from the scene. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाप्रभृति लोकेऽस्मिन्स्त्रिया भागः प्रकल्पितः ।
आनन्दं प्राप स विधिः सृष्टिर्जाता च मैथुनी ॥ २९॥

English
Ever since then, the section of women was created in the world, Brahma attained bliss. Creation became copulatory. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्ते कथितं तात शिवरूपं महोत्तमम् ।
अर्द्धनारीनरार्द्धं हि महामङ्गलदं सताम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Thus O dear, the excellent form of Siva, halfmale and half-female that bestows auspiciousness upon the good has been told. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदाख्यानमनघं यः पठेच्छृणुयादपि ।
स भुक्त्वा सकलान्भोगान्प्रयाति परमां गतिम् ॥ ३१॥

English
He who reads or listens to this pious narrative enjoys all pleasures and attains to the greatest goal. (31.)

---
