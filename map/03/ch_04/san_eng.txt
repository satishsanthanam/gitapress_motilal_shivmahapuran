### **CHAPTER FOUR - (The story of Ṛṣabha)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ चरितं शाङ्करं मुदा ।
रुद्रेण कथितं प्रीत्या ब्रह्मणे सुखदं सदा ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— O omniscient Sanatkumāra, the story of Śiva that ever confers happiness, was gladly and lovingly narrated by Śiva to Brahmā. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
सप्तमे चैव वाराहे कल्पे मन्वन्तराभिधे ।
कल्पेश्वरोऽथ भगवान्सर्वलोकप्रकाशनः ॥ २॥

मनोर्वैवस्वतस्यैव ते प्रपुत्रो भविष्यति ।
तदा चतुर्युगाश्चैव तस्मिन्मन्वन्तरे विधे ॥ ३॥

English
Lord Śiva said :—  In the seventh Vārāha Kalpa, in the Vaivasvata Manvantara, the lord Kalpeśvara who illuminates all the worlds will be born as your great grandson and son of Vaivasvata Manu. O Creator, in that Manvantara all the four Yugas will take place. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहार्थं लोकानां ब्राह्मणानां हिताय च ।
उत्पत्स्यामि विधे ब्रह्मन्द्वापराख्ययुगान्तिके ॥ ४॥

English
For the welfare of the worlds and for the benefit of the brahmins O Creator, whatever will take place in the Yuga called Dvāpara, I foresee now. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
युगप्रवृत्त्या च तदा तस्मिंश्च प्रथमे युगे ।
द्वापरे प्रथमे ब्रह्मन्यदा व्यासः स्वयम्प्रभुः ॥ ५॥

तदाहं ब्राह्मणार्थाय कलौ तस्मिन्युगान्तिके ।
भविष्यामि शिवायुक्तः श्वेतो नाम महामुनिः ॥ ६॥

English
In that first Yuga, towards the close of Dvāpara and the beginning of Kali when Vyāsa is Svayamprabhu I will be born as the great sage named Śveta, with a tuft, for the welfare of Brahmins. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमवच्छिखरे रम्ये छागले पर्वतोत्तमे ।
तदा शिष्याः शिखायुक्ता भविष्यन्ति विधे मम ॥ ७॥

English
Then O Brahmā, on the mountain Chāgala⁷—the beautiful summit of Himavat, will be born my disciples endowed with tuft. (7.)

[fn 7. Chāgala, one of the peaks of the Himālayas, has not been identified so far.]
---

Sanskrit
श्वेतः श्वेतशिखश्चैव श्वेताश्वः श्वेतलोहितः ।
चत्वारो ध्यानयोगात्ते गमिष्यन्ति पुरं मम ॥ ८॥

English
Those four disciples Sveta, Svetaśikha, Svetāśva, and Svetalohita will come to my city by the Yoga of meditation. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो भक्ता भविष्यन्ति ज्ञात्वा मां तत्त्वतोऽव्ययम् ।
जन्ममृत्युजराहीनाः परब्रह्मसमाधयः ॥ ९॥

English
Realising my unchanging real form, the devotees will become devoid of birth, death and old age and will meditate on the Supreme Brahman. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
द्रष्टुं शक्यो नरैर्नाहमृते ध्यानात्पितामह ।
दानधर्मादिभिर्वत्स साधनैः कर्महेतुभिः ॥ १०॥

English
O dear Pitamaha, except through meditation I cannot be seen by men by resorting to charitable gifts and sacred rites that bring about the bondage of Karman. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वितीये द्वापरे व्यासः सत्यो नाम प्रजापतिः ।
यदा तदा भविष्यामि सुतारो नामतः कलौ ॥ ११॥

English
In the second aeon of Dvāpara when the patriarch Satya becomes Vyasa I will be born as Sutāra and continue to live in Kali age. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि मे भविष्यन्ति शिष्या वेदविदो द्विजाः ।
दुन्दुभिः शतरूपश्च हृषीकः केतुमांस्तथा ॥ १२॥

English
There too my disciples will be the four Brahmins well-versed in the Vedic lore viz. Dundubhi, Satarūpa, Hrṣika and Ketumān. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
चत्वारो ध्यानयोगात्ते गमिष्यन्ति पुरं मम ।
ततो मुक्ता भविष्यन्ति ज्ञात्वा मां तत्त्वतोऽव्ययम् ॥ १३॥

English
By resorting to the Yoga of meditation all the four will go to my city and then realising my unchanging real form they will become liberated. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तृतीये द्वापरे चैव यदा व्यासस्तु भार्गवः ।
तदाप्यहं भविष्यामि दमनस्तु युगान्तिके ॥ १४॥

English
In the third aeon of Dvāpara in the age of Bhārgava Vyasa I will be born as Damana at the outskirts of the city. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि च भविष्यन्ति चत्वारो मम पुत्रकाः ।
विशोकश्च विशेषश्च विपापः पापनाशनः ॥ १५॥

English
Four sons will be born of me there viz—Visoka, Visesa, Vipapa and Papanāśana. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शिष्यैः साहायं व्यासस्य करिष्ये चतुरानन ।
निवृत्तिमार्गं सुदृढं वर्त्तयिष्ये कलाविह ॥ १६॥

English
O four-faced deity, I will help Vyasa and will propagate firmly the path of renunciation in the Kali age. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्थे द्वापरे चैव यदा व्यासोंऽगिरा स्मृतः ।
तदाप्यहं भविष्यामि सुहोत्रो नाम नामतः ॥ १७॥

English
In the fourth aeon of Dvāpara in the age of Angiras Vyasa, I shall be born as Suhotra. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि मम ते पुत्राश्चत्वारो योगसाधकाः ।
भविष्यन्ति महात्मानस्तन्नामानि ब्रुवे विधे ॥ १८॥

English
There also four sons will be born of me all aspiring after the Yogic path and noble-souled. O Brahma, I shall mention their names. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सुमुखो दुर्मुखश्चैव दुर्दमो दुरतिक्रमः ।
शिष्यैः साहायं व्यासस्य करिष्येऽहं तदा विधे ॥ १९॥

English
They are Sumukha, Durmukha, Durdarbha and Duratikrama. O Brahma, I shall then render help to Vyasa through the disciples. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चमे द्वापरे चैव व्यासस्तु सविता स्मृतः ।
तदा योगी भविष्यामि कङ्को नाम महातपाः ॥ २०॥

English
In the fifth aeon of Dvāpara there will be Savitr Vyasa. I shall be born as a Yogin of great penance and called Kaṅka. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि मम ते पुत्राश्चत्वारो योगसाधकाः ।
भविष्यन्ति महात्मानस्तन्नामानि श‍ृणुष्व मे ॥ २१॥

English
There also four sons will be born of me, all aspiring after the Yogic path and noble-souled. Please hear their names from me. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सनकः सनातनश्चैव प्रभुर्यश्च सनन्दनः ।
विभुः सनत्कुमारश्च निर्मलो निरहङ्कृतिः ॥ २२॥

English
They are Sanaka, Sanātana, Sanandana and Sanat-kumāra freed from sin and egotism. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि कङ्कनामाहं साहाय्यं सवितुर्विधे ।
व्यासस्य हि करिष्यामि निवृत्तिपथवर्द्धकः ॥ २३॥

English
There also O Brahmā, I, named Kaṅka, enhancing the path of renunciation shall render help to Vyāsa in the form of Savitr. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
परिवर्त्ते पुनः षष्ठे द्वापरे लोककारकः ।
कर्ता वेदविभागस्य मृत्युर्व्यासो भविष्यति ॥ २४॥

English
When the sixth aeon of Dvāpara takes place there will be Mṛtyu Vyāsa who will classify the Vedas. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाऽप्यहं भविष्यामि लोकाक्षिर्नाम नामतः ।
व्यासस्य सुसहायार्थं निवृत्तिपथवर्द्धनः ॥ २५॥

English
Then I will be born as Lokākṣi, making the path of renunciation flourish and helping Vyāsa. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि शिष्याश्चत्वारो भविष्यन्ति दृढव्रताः ।
सुधामा विरजाश्चैव सञ्जयो विजयस्तथा ॥ २६॥

English
Four disciples of steady rites viz. Sudhāmā, Virajas, Sañjaya and Vijaya will be born then. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तमे परिवर्त्ते तु यदा व्यासः शतक्रतुः ।
तदाप्यहं भविष्यामि जैगीषव्यो विभुर्विधे ॥ २७॥

English
O Brahmā, in the seventh aeon of Śatakratu Vyāsa I shall be born as the lord Jaigīṣavya. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
योगं सन्द्रढयिष्यामि महायोगविचक्षणः ।
काश्यां गुहान्तरे संस्थो दिव्यदेशे कुशास्तरिः ॥ २८॥

English
Seated on a Kuśa mat in a divine spot in the middle of a mountain-cavern in Kāśī, I, an expert in the great Yoga, will stabilise the path of Yoga. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
साहाय्यं च करिष्यामि व्यासस्य हि शतक्रतोः ।
उद्धरिष्यामि भक्तांश्च संसारभयतो विधे ॥ २९॥

English
O Brahmā, I shall render help to Śatakratu Vyāsa I shall lift up the devotees from the fear of worldly existence. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि मम चत्वारो भविष्यन्ति सुता युगे ।
सारस्वतश्च योगीशो मेघवाहः सुवाहनः ॥ ३०॥

English
There also in that aeon, four sons will be born of me viz. Sārasvata, Yogīśa, Meghavāha and Suvāhana. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टमे परिवर्त्ते हि वसिष्ठो मुनिसत्तमः ।
कर्त्ता वेदविभागस्य वेदव्यासो भविष्यति ॥ ३१॥

English
In the eighth aeon there will be Vasiṣṭha Veda-vyāsa—the excellent sage who will classify the Vedas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राप्यहं भविष्यामि नामतो दधिवाहनः ।
व्यासस्य हि करिष्यामि साहाय्यं योगवित्तम ॥ ३२॥

English
There I will be born as Dadhivāhana by name. As the foremost among those who know Yoga I will render help to Vyāsa. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कपिलश्चासुरिः पञ्चशिखः शाल्वलपूर्वकः ।
चत्वारो योगिनः पुत्रा भविष्यन्ति समा मम ॥ ३३॥

English
Four sons, all Yogins on a par with me, viz—Kapila, Āsuri, Śālvala and Pañcaśikha will be born of me. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
नवमे परिवर्त्ते तु तस्मिन्नेव युगे विधे ।
भविष्यति मुनिश्रेष्ठो व्यासः सारस्वताह्वयः ॥ ३४॥

English
O Brahmā, in the ninth aeon of the same Yuga, there will be Sārasvata Vyāsa, the excellent sage. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यासस्य ध्यायतस्तस्य निवृत्तिपथवृद्धये ।
तदाप्यहं भविष्यामि ऋषभो नामतः स्मृतः ॥ ३५॥

English
As Vyāsa sits in meditation for the flourish of the path of renunciation, I will be born as Ṛṣabha. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पराशरश्च गर्गश्च भार्गवो गिरिशस्तथा ।
चत्वारस्तत्र शिष्या मे भविष्यन्ति सुयोगिनः ॥ ३६॥

English
There, Parāśara, Garga, Bhārgava and Giriśa, all good Yogins, will be my four disciples. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तैः साकं द्रढयिष्यामि योगमार्गं प्रजापते ।
करिष्यामि सहायं वै वेदव्यासस्य सन्मुने ॥ ३७॥

English
O Patriarch, with them I will stabilise the path of Yoga. O good sage, I will render help to Vedavyāsa. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन रूपेण भक्तानां बहूनां दुःखिनां विधे ।
उद्धारं भवतोऽहं वै करिष्यामि दयाकरः ॥ ३८॥

English
O Brahmā, in that form, I, the merciful, will cause the uplift of many distressed devotees, from the worldly existence. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽवतारो विधे मे हि ऋषभाख्यः सुयोगकृत् ।
सारस्वतव्यासमनःकर्त्ता नानोतिकारकः ॥ ३९॥

English
O Brahmā, such is my incarnation named Ṛṣabha. It causes Yogic practice; it fulfils the desire of Vyāsa in the guise of Sārasvata and it consists of many divine sports. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अवतारेण मे येन भद्रायुर्नृपबालकः ।
जीवितो हि मृतः क्ष्वेडदोषतो जनकोज्झितः ॥ ४०॥

English
Bhadrāyu a prince who had been abandoned by his father and who had died of poison infection, was resuscitated by me in that incarnation. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्तेऽथ षोडशे वर्षे तस्य राजशिशोः पुनः ।
ययौ तद्वेश्म सहसा ऋषभः स मदात्मकः ॥ ४१॥

English
When that prince reached his sixteenth year I as Rṣabha, went to his mansion suddenly. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजितस्तेन स मुनिः सद्रूपश्च कृपानिधिः ।
उपादिदेश तद्धर्मान् राजयोगान्प्रजापते ॥ ४२॥

English
O patriarch, being duly worshipped by him, that sage, of good form, the store-house of mercy instructed him in virtues in connection with the administration of his kingdom. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स कवचं दिव्यं शङ्खं खङ्गं च भास्वरम् ।
ददौ तस्मै प्रसन्नात्मा सर्वशत्रुविनाशनम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Becoming excessively delighted, he gave him a divine conch and a lustruous sword that could destroy his enemies. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तदङ्ग भस्मनामृश्य कृपया दीनवत्सलः ।
स द्वादशसहस्रस्य गजानां च बलं ददौ ॥ ४४॥

English
The lord, favourably disposed to the indigent, smeared his body with the ashes from his own body and gave him the strength of twelve thousand elephants. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
इति भद्रायुषं सम्यगनुश्वास्य समातृकम् ।
ययौ स्वैरगतिस्ताभ्यां पूजितस्त्वृषभः प्रभुः ॥ ४५॥

English
After consoling Bhadrāyus and his mother very well, lord Rṣabha after being duly worshipped by them went as he pleased. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
भद्रायुरपि राजर्षिर्जित्वा रिपुगणान्विधे ।
राज्यं चकार धर्मेण विवाह्य कीर्त्तिमालिनीम् ॥ ४६॥

English
O Brahmā, the saintly king Bhadrāyus conquered all his enemies, married Kīrtimālini and virtuously ruled the kingdom. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थम्प्रभावं ऋषभोऽवतारः शङ्करस्य मे ।
सतां गतिर्दीनबन्धुर्नवमः कथितस्तव ॥ ४७॥

English
Thus the ninth Rṣabha incarnation of Śiva has been narrated to you. He is the goal of the good and kinsman of the indigent. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषभस्य चरित्रं हि परमं पावनं महत् ।
स्वर्ग्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं श्रोतव्यं च प्रयत्नतः ॥ ४८॥

English
The story of Rṣabha is great, highly sacred, and conducive to heaven, fame and longevity. It shall be listened to with devoted attention. (48.)

---
