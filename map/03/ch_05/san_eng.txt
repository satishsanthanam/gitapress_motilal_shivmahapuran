### **CHAPTER FIVE - (The nineteen incarnations of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
दशमे द्वापरे व्यासस्त्रिधामा नामतो मुनिः ।
हिमवच्छिखरे रम्ये भृगुतुङ्गे नगोत्तमे ॥ १॥

English
Śiva said :— In the tenth aeon of Dvāpara there will be Tridhāmā Vyāsa on the excellent Bhṛgutuṅga⁸ peak of Himavat. (1.)

[fn 8. Bhṛgutuṅga is one of the peaks of the Himalayas. According to Varāḥapurāṇa (ch. 146, 45-46) it is a mountain in Nepal on the eastern bank of the Gandaka where the sage Bhṛgu had a hermitage. The Vāmanapurāṇa (81: 33) locates it near the Vitastā and Himavat. See GEAMI Part I P. 70.]
---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि मम पुत्राश्च भृग्वाद्याः श्रुतिसम्मिताः ।
बलबन्धुर्नरामित्रः केतुश‍ृङ्गस्तपोधनः ॥ २॥

English
There my sons well-versed in the Vedic lore are Bhṛṅga and others i.e. Balabandhu, Nara, Mitra and the sage Ketuśṛṅga. (2.)

Sanskrit

English

[fn 9. Gaṅgādvāra represents modern Haradvāra, It is also known by various names like Mokṣadvāra, Haridvāra and Māyāpura. Cp Sk iv. 1. 7. 114.

केचिद्रूपुर्हरिद्वारं मोक्षद्वारं ततः परे ।

Sanskrit

English
There also the four sons of steady rites viz. Lambodara, Lambākṣa, Keśalamba and Pralambaka will be born. (4.)

Sanskrit

हेमकञ्चुकमासाद्य नाम्ना ह्यत्रिः परिप्लुतः ।

English
In the twelfth aeon, there will be Śatatejas Vyāsa. I will be born in the Kali age at the end of Dvāpara adopting golden bodice. I shall be wandering for rendering help to Vyāsa as the reviver of the path of renunciation. (5-6.)

Sanskrit

English
O great sage, these four sons of good yogic skill will be born as my sons viz. Sarvajña, Samabuddhi, Sādhya and Śarva. (7.)

Sanskrit

English
In the thirteenth aeon, there will be Nārāyaṇa Vyāsa. Then I shall be born as the great sage Bali. (8.)

---

Sanskrit

English
In the hermitage of Bālakhilyas on the excellent mountain of Gandhamādana,¹⁰ the auspicious sons will be Sudhāmā, Kāśyapa, Varṣiṣṭha and Virajas. (9.)

[fn 10. The location of Gandhamādana is highly controversial. See PP. 405, 623. Most probably it refers to that part of the Himālayas on which the Badarikāśrama is situated.]

Sanskrit

English
In the fourteenth aeon, there will be Rakṣa Vyāsa. I shall be born as Gautama, in the family of Aṅgiras. (10.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
In the fifteenth aeon of Dvāpara, there will be Trayyāruṇi Vyāsa. I shall be born as Vedaśiras with a powerful weapon. Vedaśīrṣa is a mountain on the top of Himavat on the northern bank of Sarasvatī.¹¹ (12-13.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
In the sixteenth aeon, there will be Deva Vyāsa; I shall be born as Gokarṇa for imparting instructions in Yoga. (15.)

---

Sanskrit

तत्रापि योगिनः पुत्र भविष्यन्त्यम्बुसम्मिताः ॥ १६॥

English
It is there itself that the holy forest Gokarṇa¹² exists. There also four Yogin sons will be born of me. (16.)

[fn 12. Gokarṇa, 'cow's ear' is a town sacred to Śiva in Nepal on the Bāgamati river. There is also a sacred hill of this name on the western ghat neaGoa. It cannot be ascertained which of the two is meant here]

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English

[fn 13. This peak of the Himālayas has not been identified so far.]

There too my disciples will be the sages Kuśika, Garga, Mitra and Taurusya. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
योगिनो ब्राह्मणा वेदपारगा ऊर्द्ध्वरेतसः ।
प्राप्य माहेश्वरं योगं गमिष्यन्ति शिवं पुरम् ॥ ५०॥

English
The Yogic brahmins well-versed in the Vedas, and of sublimated power will attain the Yoga of Maheśvara and reach the auspicious city. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
वैवस्वतेऽन्तरे सम्यक् प्रोक्ता हि परमात्मना ।
योगेश्वरावताराश्च सर्वावर्तेषु सुव्रताः ॥ ५१॥

English
In the Vaivasvata manvantara, during all its recurring aeons, the Yogeśvara incarnations of the great Ātman of virtuous deeds have been explained to you. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यासाश्चैवाष्टविंशत्का द्वापरे द्वापरे विभो ।
योगेश्वरावताराश्च प्रारम्भे च कलौ कलौ ॥ ५२॥

English
O great lord, the Vyāsas in every recurring Dvāpara are twenty-eight in number. The Yogeśvara incarnations at the beginning of every recurring Kali age are also so many. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
योगेश्वरावताराणां योगमार्गप्रवर्द्धकाः ।
महाशैवाश्च चत्वारः शिष्याः प्रत्येकमव्ययाः ॥ ५३॥

English
In each of the incarnations of Yogeśvara, there are four disciples who make the path of Yoga flourish and who are eternal as well as great devotees of Śiva. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
एते पाशुपताः शिष्या भस्मोद्धूलितविग्रहाः ।
रुद्राक्षमालाभरणास्त्रिपुण्ड्राङ्कितमस्तकाः ॥ ५४॥

English
These disciples of Śiva smear their bodies with holy ashes, use the necklaces of Rudrākṣa beads as their ornaments and mark their foreheads with Tripuṇḍra. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
शिष्या धर्मरताः सर्वे वेदवेदाङ्गपारगाः ।
लिङ्गार्चनरता नित्यं बाह्याभ्यन्तरतः स्थिताः ॥ ५५॥

English
All the disciples are pious and well-versed in the Vedas and Vedāṅgas; they are devoted both externally and internally to the worship of the phallic image of Śiva. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्त्या मयि च योगेन ध्याननिष्ठा जितेन्द्रियाः ।
सङ्ख्यया द्वादशाधिक्यशतं च गणिता बुधैः ॥ ५६॥

English
By their devotion to me and by means of Yoga they are engaged in meditation; they have perfect control over the sense-organs. They are considered one hundred and twelve in number by the learned. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येतद्वै मया प्रोक्तमवतारेषु लक्षणम् ।
मन्वादिकृष्णपर्यन्तमष्टाविंशद्युगक्रमात् ॥ ५७॥

English
Thus I have mentioned the characteristic features of the incarnations from Manu to Kṛṣṇa in the twenty-eighth recurring cycles of the Yoga in order. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र श्रुतिसमूहानां विधानं ब्रह्मलक्षणम् ।
भविष्यति तदा कल्पे कृष्णद्वैपायनो यदा ॥ ५८॥

English
The classification of the Vedas was the characteristic sign of the Kalpa when Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana became the chief redactor. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवमुक्त्वा ब्रह्माणमनुगृह्य महेश्वरः ।
पुनः सम्प्रेक्ष्य देवेशस्तत्रैवान्तरधीयत ॥ ५९॥

English
After saying this and blessing Brahma, the great lord of the gods glanced at him again and vanished there itself. (59.)
