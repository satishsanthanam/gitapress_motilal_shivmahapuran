### **CHAPTER SIX - (The incarnation of Nandīśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
अथ नन्दीश्वरावतारमाह ।
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
भवान्कथमनुप्राप्तो महादेवांशजः शिवम् ।
श्रोतुमिच्छामि तत्सर्वं वक्तुमर्हसि मे प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  O lord, how did you happen to be born of Mahā-deva and gain access to Śiva I wish to hear. Please narrate everything in detail. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ सावधानतया श‍ृणु ।
यथाऽहं च शिवं प्राप्तो महादेवांशजो मुने ॥ २॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— O sage Sanatkumāra, O omniscient one, listen with attention as to how I was born of Mahādeva and how I gained access to Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजाकामः शिलादोऽभूदुक्तः पितृभिरादरात् ।
तदुद्धर्तुमना भक्त्या समुद्धारमभीप्सुभिः ॥ ३॥

अधोदृष्टिः सुधर्मात्मा शिलादो नाम वीर्यवान् ।
तस्यासीन्मुनिकैर्वृत्तिः शिवलोके च सोऽगमत् ॥ ४॥

English
Urged eagerly by the manes who desired their uplift Śilāda piously thought of raising them up. He was therefore desirous of a progeny. For that he of great potentiality performed a penance with his drooping eyes. He had the same activities as the sages. He went to Śivaloka. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्रमुद्दिश्य स मुनिस्तपस्तेपे सुदुःसहम् ।
निश्चलात्मा शिलादाख्यो बहुकालं दृढव्रतः ॥ ५॥

English
That sage Śilāda performed a severe penance with Indra in view, for a long time. His mind was steady and he was stead-fast in his activities. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तपतस्तस्य तपसा सन्तुष्टोऽभूच्छतक्रतुः ।
जगाम च वरं दातुं सर्वदेवप्रभुस्तदा ॥ ६॥

English
Indra was delighted at his penance. He, the lord of gods, went to him in order to grant him the boon. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शिलादमाह सुप्रीत्या शक्रस्तुष्टोऽस्मि तेऽनघ ।
तेन त्वं मुनिशार्दूल वरयस्व वरानिति ॥ ७॥

English
Indra lovingly spoke to Śilāda—"O excellent sage, O sinless one, I am delighted with you. Therefore choose your boons." (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रणम्य देवेशं स्तुत्वा स्तुतिभिरादरात् ।
शिलादो मुनिशार्दूलस्तमाह सुकृताञ्जलिः ॥ ८॥

English
Bowing to the lord of the gods and eulogising him with holy hymns, Śilāda, the most excellent sage, told him with palms joined in reverence. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शिलाद उवाच ।
शतक्रतो सुरेशान सन्तुष्टो यदि मे प्रभो ।
अयोनिजं मुत्युहीनं पुत्रमिच्छामि सुव्रतम् ॥ ९॥

English
Śilāda said :— O lord of gods, O Indra, if you are pleased with me, I wish to have a son of holy rites free of death and not born of a womb. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्र उवाच ।
पुत्रं दास्यामि पुत्रार्थिन्योनिजं मृत्युसंयुतम् ।
अन्यथा ते न दास्यामि मृत्युहीना न सन्ति वै ॥ १०॥

English
Indra said :— O desirer of a son, I can give you only a son born of a womb and sure to die. I cannot give it to you otherwise. There are no persons who can escape death. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
न दास्यामि सुतं तेऽहं मृत्युहीनमयोनिजम् ।
हरिर्विधिश्च भगवान्किमुतान्यो महामुने ॥ ११॥

English
I shall not give you a son devoid of death and not born of a womb. O great sage, even lord Vişnu and Brahma die. Not to speak of others then? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तावपि त्रिपुरार्यङ्गसम्भवौ मरणान्वितौ ।
तयोरप्यायुषां मानं कथितं निगमे पृथक् ॥ १२॥

English
Even these two, born of Siva, the destroyer of the Tripuras, die. The extent of their longevity has been mentioned separately in the sacred lore. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादयोनिजे पुत्रे मृत्युहीने प्रयत्नतः ।
परित्यजाशां विप्रेन्द्र गृहाणात्मक्षमं सुतम् ॥ १३॥

English
Hence O great brahmin, abandon your desire of deathless son who is not born of a womb. Accept a son befitting your mortal nature. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
किन्तु देवेश्वरो रुद्रः प्रसीदति महेश्वरः ।
सुदुर्लभो मृत्युहीनस्तव पुत्रो ह्ययोनिजः ॥ १४॥

English
But if Siva, the lord of the gods, becomes delighted, even an immortal son not born of a womb, though hard to get, may be obtained. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं च विष्णुर्भगवान् द्रुहिणश्च महामुने ।
अयोनिजं मृत्युहीनं पुत्रं दातुं न शक्नुमः ॥ १५॥

English
Neither I, nor lord Vişnu, nor Brahma, O, great sage, can give an immortal son not born of a womb. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आराधय महादेवं तत्पुत्रविनिकाम्यया ।
सर्वेश्वरो महाशक्तः स ते पुत्रं प्रदास्यति ॥ १६॥

English
Propitiate lord Siva therefore, for the fulfilment of your desire for such a son. The great lord of all is very powerful. He will give you a son. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवं व्याहृत्य विप्रेन्द्रमनुगृह्य च तं घृणी ।
देवैर्वृतः सुरेशानः स्वलोकं समगान्मुने ॥ १७॥

English
Nandāvara said :—  O sage, after speaking thus to the great brahmin and blessing him, the compassionate lord of the gods returned to his region surrounded by the gods. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
गते तस्मिंश्च वरदे सहस्राक्षे शिलाशनः ।
आराधयन्महादेवं तपसाऽतोषयद्भवम् ॥ १८॥

English
When the granter of boons, the thousand-eyed lord Indra had gone away, Silada propitiated lord Siva. He delighted Siva by means of his penance. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्यैवमनिशं तत्परस्य द्विजस्त वै ।
दिव्यं वर्षसहस्रं तु गतं क्षणमिवाद्भुतम् ॥ १९॥

English
As the brahmin was engrossed in penance day and night, a thousand divine years elapsed like a moment. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
वल्मीकेन वृताङ्गश्च लक्षकोटगणैर्मुनिः ।
वज्रसूचीमुखैश्चान्यै रक्तभुग्भिश्च सर्वतः ॥ २०॥

निर्मांसरुधिरत्वग्वै बिले तस्मिन्नवस्थितः ।
अस्थिशेषोऽभवत्पश्चाच्छिलादो मुनिसत्तमः ॥ २१॥

English
His body was covered with anthill. He was surrounded by a hundred thousand worms, with pointed beaks, as hard as adamant and other types of blood-sucking insects all round. He remained in the cavity with his flesh and blood drained up. He was left with a bare skeleton. The excellent sage Śilāda was reduced to such a state. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
तुष्टः प्रभुस्तदा तस्मै दर्शयामास स्वां तनुम् ।
दिव्यां दिव्यगुणैर्युक्तामलभ्यां वामबुद्धिभिः ॥ २२॥

English
The delighted lord showed him his divine body endowed with all divine qualities and inaccessible to people of crooked intellect. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यवर्षसहस्रेण तप्यमानाय शूलधृक् ।
सर्वदेवाधिपस्तस्मै वरदोऽस्मीत्यभाषत ॥ २३॥

English
As he continued the penance for a thousand divine years, the trident-bearing lord of all the gods spoke to him "I am here, the granter of boons." (23.)

---

Sanskrit
महासमाधिसंलीनः स शिलादो महामुनिः ।
नाश‍ृणोत्तद्गिरं शम्भोर्भक्त्यधीनतरस्य वै ॥ २४॥

English
The great sage Šilāda engrossed in the great trance did not hear those words of Šiva who is subservient to the devotees. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा स्पृष्टो मुनिस्तेन करेण त्रिपुरारिणा ।
तदैव मुनिशार्दूल उत्ससर्ज तपःक्रमम् ॥ २५॥

English
Only when the great sage was touched by the slayer of the Tripuras did he leave off his penance. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोन्मील्य मुनिर्नेत्रे सोमं शम्भुं विलोकयन् ।
द्रुतं प्रणम्य समुदा पादयोर्न्यपतन्मुने ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, on seeing Siva accompanied by Pārvati after opening his eyes, he immediately bowed to him and fell at his feet with joy. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
हर्षगद्गदया वाचा नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ।
प्रसन्नात्मा शिलादः स तुष्टाव परमेश्वरम् ॥ २७॥

English
The delighted Šilāda, drooping down his shoulders, with palms joined in reverence eulogised lord Šiva with words choked with pleasure. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नो भगवान्देवदेवस्त्रिलोचनः ।
वरदोऽस्मीति तं प्राह शिलादं मुनिपुङ्गवम् ॥ २८॥

English
Then the delighted lord of gods, the three-eyed deity told the great sage "I am the granter of boons." (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसानेन किं कार्यं भवते हि महामते ।
ददामि पुत्रं सर्वज्ञं सर्वशास्त्रार्थपारगम् ॥ २९॥

English
O intelligent one, what shall be done with this penance? I shall give you a son who will be omniscient and a master of all sacred lore. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रणम्य देवेशं तच्छ्रुत्वा च शिलाशनः ।
हर्षगद्गदया वाचोवाच सोमविभूषणम् ॥ ३०॥

English
On hearing that, the sage Šilāda bowed to the moon-crested lord and spoke in words choked with pleasure. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शिलाद उवाच ।
महेश यदि तुष्टोऽसि यदि वा वरदश्च मे ।
इच्छामि त्वत्समं पुत्रं मृत्युहीनमयोनिजम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Śilāda said :— O lord Śiva, if you are delighted, if you are ready to grant boons, I wish for a son equal to you, devoid of death and not born of a womb. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्तस्ततो देवस्त्र्यम्बकस्तेन शङ्करः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा शिलादं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ ३२॥

English
Nandīśvara said :—  When thus requested by him, the three-eyed lord Śiva delightedly replied to the excellent sage Śilāda. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
॥ शिव उवाच ।
पूर्वमाराधितो विप्र ब्रह्मणाऽहं तपोधन ।
तपसा चावतारार्थं मुनिभिश्च सुरोत्तमैः ॥ ३३॥

English
Śiva said :— O brahmin, O sage, formerly I have been propitiated by means of penance by Brahma, by sages and by leading gods and urged to take an incarnation. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तव पुत्रो भविष्यामि नन्दी नाम्ना त्वयोनिजः ।
पिता भविष्यसि मम पितुर्वै जगतां मुने ॥ ३४॥

English
O sage, I shall become your son by the name of Nandin. I shall not be born of a womb. Thus you shall be my father, of me who am the father of the worlds. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा मुनिं प्रेक्ष्य प्रणिपत्यास्थितं घृणी ।
सोमस्तूर्णं तमादिश्य तत्रैवान्तर्दधे हरः ॥ ३५॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— After addressing these words to the sage who stood waiting after bowing, the merciful lord Siva gave him his directions and vanished there itself along with Parvati. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गते तस्मिन्महादेवे स शिलादो महामुनिः ।
स्वमाश्रममुपागम्य ऋषिभ्योऽकथयत्ततः ॥ ३६॥

English
After lord Siva had disappeared, the great sage Silada returned to his hermitage and mentioned the tidings to the sages. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
कियता चैव कालेन तदासौ जनकः स मे ।
यज्ञाङ्गणं चकर्षाशु यज्ञार्थं यज्ञवित्तमः ॥ ३७॥

English
After a short while, my father, the foremost among those who knew the sacrificial rites, ordered for the construction of the sacrificial altar for the performance of sacrifice. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्षणादहं शम्भोस्तनुजस्तस्य चाज्ञया ।
स जातः पूर्वमेवाहं युगान्ताग्निसमप्रभः ॥ ३८॥

English
But before the beginning of sacrifice I was born as Siva's son at his bidding, with a lustre as brilliant as the fire at the end of a Yuga. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अवर्षंस्तदा पुष्करावर्तकाद्या
जगुः खेचराः किन्नराः सिद्धसाध्याः ।
शिलादात्मजत्वं गते मय्यृषीन्द्राः
समन्ताच्च वृष्टिं व्यधुः कौसुमीं ते ॥ ३९॥

English
Then Puṣkarāvartaka$^{21}$ and other clouds showered. When I was born as the son of Silāda, the Kinnaras, Siddhas, Sādhyas and other beings walked through the sky and the great sages made showers of flowers all round. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ब्रह्मादयो देवा देवपत्न्यश्च सर्वशः ।
तत्राजग्मुश्च सुप्रीत्या हरिश्चैव शिवोऽम्बिका ॥ ४०॥

English
Then Brahma and other gods together with their wives, Visnu, Siva and Parvati came there lovingly. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोत्सवो महानासीन्ननृतुश्चाप्सरोगणाः ।
आदृत्य मां तथालिङ्ग्य तुष्टुवुर्हर्षिताश्च ते ॥ ४१॥

English
There was great jubilation. The celestial damsels danced. After honouring me all of them delightedly eulogised my phallic image. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रशस्य शिलादं तं स्तुत्वा च सुस्तवैः शिवौ ।
सर्वे जग्मुश्च धामानि शिवावप्यखिलेश्वरौ ॥ ४२॥

English
After praising Silada and eulogising Siva and Parvati by reciting good hymns all of them returned to their respective abodes. Siva and Siva, the lord and goddess of all, went away too. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
शिलादोऽपि च मां दृष्ट्वा कालसूर्यानलप्रभम् ।
त्र्यक्षं चतुर्भुजं बालं जटामुकुटधारिणम् ॥ ४३॥

त्रिशूलाद्यायुधं दीप्तं सर्वथा रुद्ररूपिणम् ।
महानन्दभरः प्रीत्या प्रणम्यं प्रणनाम च ॥ ४४॥

English
On seeing me the boy with three eyes, four arms and lustre like that of Yama, sun and fire and clad in matted hair and coronet, Silada was highly delighted. I was in the form of Rudra in every respect with the trident and other weapons. He bowed to me lovingly. (43-44.)

---

Sanskrit
शिलाद उवाच ।
त्वयाहं नन्दितो यस्मान्नन्दी नाम्ना सुरेश्वर ।
तस्मात्त्वां देवमानन्दं नमामि जगदीश्वरम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Śilāda said :— O lord of gods, since I have been delighted by you, you will be named Nandin. Hence I bow to you, bliss (incarnate), the lord of the universe. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
मया सह पिता हृष्टः सुप्रणम्य महेश्वरम् ।
उटजं स्वं जगामाशु निधिं लब्ध्वेव निर्धनः ॥ ४६॥

English
Nandīvara said :— My delighted father after bowing to the great lord returned to his hut with me, as pleased as an indigent person who had struck a treasure-trove. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा गतोऽहमुटजं शिलादस्य महामुने ।
तदाहं तादृशं रूपं त्यक्त्वा मानुष्यमास्थितः ॥ ४७॥

English
O great sage, when I reached the hut I cast off my divine form and assumed the human one. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
मानुष्यमास्थितं दृष्ट्वा पिता मे लोकपूजितः ।
विललापातिदुःखार्त्तः स्वजनैश्च समावृतः ॥ ४८॥

English
After seeing me assuming human form, my father, honoured by the world, was distressed and so lamented surrounded by his own people. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
जातकर्मादिकान्येव सर्वाण्यपि चकार मे ।
शालङ्कायनपुत्रो वै शिलादः पुत्रवत्सलः ॥ ४९॥

English
The son of Sālaṅkāyana Śilāda fond of his son performed all the post-natal²² rites for me. (49.)

[fn 22. Jātakarma etc are sacraments essential for the material and spiritual growth of the child. For details see Hindu Saṃskāra.]
---

Sanskrit
वेदानध्यापयामास साङ्गोपाङ्गानशेषतः ।
शास्त्राण्यन्यान्यपि तथा पञ्चवर्षे पिता च माम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Within five years my father taught me all the Vedas with their Aṅgas and Upāṅgas as well as other sacred texts. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पूर्णे सप्तमे वर्षे मित्रावरुणसंज्ञकौ ।
मुनी तस्याश्रमं प्राप्तौ द्रष्टुं मां चाज्ञया विभोः ॥ ५१॥

English
When I completed the age of seven, the two sages Mitra and Varuṇa came to the hermitage at the bidding of the lord in order to see me. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
मित्रावरुणावूचतुः ।
तात नन्दी तवाल्पायुः सर्वशास्त्रार्थपारगः ।
न दृष्टमेव चापश्यं ह्यायुर्वर्षादतः परम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Mitra and Varuṇa said :— O dear sage, although he has mastered the sacred lore, your son Nandin will be short-lived. I do not see his life for more than a year from now. (53.)

English
When the brahmins said this, Silaida fond of his son was much distressed. He lamented loudly after embracing me. (54.)

Sanskrit

English
On seeing my father and grand-father fallen like a dead body I spoke after remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva with a delightful mind. (55.)

Sanskrit
केन त्वं तात दुःखेन वेपमानश्च रोदिषि ।
दुःखं ते कुत उत्पन्नं ज्ञातुमिच्छामि तत्त्वतः ॥ ५६॥

English
"O father, what is your distress for which you tremble and cry? I wish to know correctly whence this misery has come to you?" (56.)

---

Sanskrit
पितोवाच ।
तवाल्पमृत्युदुःखेन दुःखितोऽतीव पुत्रक ।
को मे दुःखं हरतु वै शरणं तं प्रयामि हि ॥ ५७॥

English
The father said :—  O son, I am much distressed at the thought of your early death. Who can remove my misery? I shall indeed seek refuge in him. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्र उवाच ।
देवो वा दानवो वापि यमः कालोऽथवापि हि ।
ऋध्येयुर्यद्यपि ह्येते मामन्येऽपि जनास्तथा ॥ ५८॥

अथापि चाल्पमृत्युर्मे न भविष्यति मा तुदः ।
सत्यं ब्रवीमि जनक शपथं ते करोम्यहम् ॥ ५९॥

English
The son said :—  Even if the gods, demons, Yama or Kāla were to be angry with me or even if it be any other person, I will not be short-lived. Do not be grieved. O father, I speak the truth, I take a vow unto you. (58-59.)

Sanskrit
पितोवाच ।
किं तपः किं परिज्ञानं को योगश्च प्रभुश्च ते ।
येन त्वं दारुणं दुःखं वञ्चयिष्यसि पुत्र मे ॥ ६०॥

English
The father said :— What is your penance? What is your knowledge? What is your Yoga? Who is your lord? Whereby O son, you propose to ward off my terrible misery? (60.)

---

Sanskrit
॥ पुत्र उवाच ।
न तात तपसा मृत्युं वञ्चयिष्ये न विद्यया ।
महादेवस्य भजनान्मृत्युं जेष्यामि नान्यथा ॥ ६१॥

English
The son said :— O father, I shall deceive death by means of my penance, not learning. By worshipping great god, I will conquer death, not otherwise. (61.)

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वाहं पितुः पादौ प्रणम्य शिरसा मुने ।
प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य च तमगच्छं वनमुत्तमम् ॥ ६२॥

English
Nandāvara said :—  O sage, after saying this and bowing at his feet and circumambulating my father I went to the excellent forest. (62.)
