### **CHAPTER SEVEN - (The coronation and the nuptials of Nandīśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
॥ नन्दिकेश्वर उवाच ।
तत्र गत्वा मुनेऽहं वै स्थित्वैकान्तस्थले सुधीः ।
अतपं तप उग्रं सन्मुनीनामपि दुष्करम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— O sage, after going there and staying in an isolated place with a steady mind I performed a severe penance very difficult even for good sages. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हृत्पुण्डरीकसुषिरे ध्यात्वा देवं त्रियम्बकम् ।
त्र्यक्षं दशभुजं शान्तं पञ्चवक्त्रं सदाशिवम् ॥ २॥

रुद्रजाप्यमकार्षं वै परमध्यानमास्थितः ।
सरितश्चोत्तरे पुण्ये ह्येकचित्तः समाहितः ॥ ३॥

English
Meditating in the lotus-like cavity of the heart the lord Tryambaka, Sadasiva with five faces,[23] ten arms and three eyes and quiet in features I performed the repetition of Rudra Japa. I was in great meditation on the northern bank of the river. My mind was concentrated and I had pure thoughts. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिञ्जाप्येऽथ सम्प्रीतः स्थितं मां परमेश्वरः ।
तुष्टोऽब्रवीन्महादेवः सोमः सोमार्द्धभूषणः ॥ ४॥

English
Delighted with that Japa, lord Siva, accompanied by Pārvatī and bedecked with the crescent moon, said :— (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
शैलादे वरदोऽहं ते तपसानेन तोषितः ।
साधु तप्तं त्वया धीमन् ब्रूहि यत्ते मनोगतम् ॥ ५॥

English
Śiva said :— "O Sailadi (son of Silada) I have been pleased by this penance of yours. O intelligent one, you have performed the penance very well. Tell me what you desire. I am here the granter of boons." (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स एवमुक्तो देवेन शिरसा पादयोर्नतः ।
अस्तवं परमेशानं जराशोकविनाशनम् ॥ ६॥

English
When thus addressed by the lord I bowed to his feet with my head and eulogised lord Siva, the destroyer of the sorrow of old age. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ मां नन्दिनं शम्भुर्भक्त्या परामया युतम् ।
अश्रुपूर्णेक्षणं सम्यक् पादयोः शिरसा नतम् ॥ ७॥

उत्थाप्य परमेशानः पस्पर्श परमार्तिहा ।
कराभ्यां सम्मुखाभ्यां तु सङ्गृह्य वृषभध्वजः ॥ ८॥

English
I was endowed with great devotion. My eyes were filled with tears. I fell prostrate at his feet with my head resting there. Lord Siva, the destroyer of great distress, lifted me up and stroked me with his hands. The bull-bannered lord clasped me with his hands and stood face to face with me. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
निरीक्ष्य गणपांश्चैव देवीं हिमवतः सुताम् ।
उवाच मां कृपादृष्ट्या समीक्ष्य जगतां पतिः ॥ ९॥

English
Glancing at the leaders of the Ganas and the goddess, the daughter of Himavat, the lord of the worlds, looked at me with sympathy and spoke. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
वत्स नन्दिन् महाप्राज्ञ मृत्योर्भीतिः कुतस्तव ।
मयैव प्रेषितौ विप्रौ मत्समस्त्वं न संशयः ॥ १०॥

English
"Dear Nandin, O intelligent one, wherefore can you be afraid of death? The two brahmins were sent by me alone. There is no doubt. You are equal to me. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अमरो जरया त्यक्तोऽदुःखी गणपतिः सदा ।
अव्ययश्चाक्षयश्चेष्टः सपिता ससुहृज्जनः ॥ ११॥

English
You will be free from death and old age. You will not be unhappy. You will for ever be the leader of the Gaṇas. You will be unchanging and unwasting. You will be loved by all in the company of your father and friends. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्बलः पार्श्वगो नित्यं ममेष्टो भवितानिशम् ।
न जरा जन्म मृत्युर्वै मत्प्रसादाद्भविष्यति ॥ १२॥

English
You will gain strength from me. You will always be at my sides. You will be for ever my favourite. Due to my favour you will have neither birth, nor death, nor old age." (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा शिरोमालां कुशेशयमयीं निजाम् ।
समुन्मुच्य बबन्धाशु मम कण्ठे कृपानिधिः ॥ १३॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— After saying thus, the store-house of mercy took off his own lotus-garland round his head and tied it round my neck immediately. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तयाहं मालया विप्र शुभया कण्ठसक्तया ।
त्र्यक्षो दशभुजश्चासं द्वितीय इव शङ्करः ॥ १४॥

English
O brahmin, as soon as that garland adorned my neck I became three-eyed and ten-armed like a second Śiva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तत एव समादाय हस्तेन परमेश्वरः ।
उवाच ब्रूहि किं तेऽद्य ददामि वरमुत्तमम् ॥ १५॥

English
Then, taking me by the hand, lord Śiva said— "Tell me, what excellent boon shall I grant you?" (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जटाश्रितं वारि गृहीत्वा हारनिर्मलम् ।
उक्त्वा नदी भवेतीह विससर्ज वृषध्वजः ॥ १६॥

English
Then he took the water from his matted hair, water as pure as necklace, and said "Be Nandin." Then the bull-bannered lord let off the water. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पञ्चमिता नद्यः प्रावर्तन्त शुभावहाः ।
सुतोयाश्च महावेगा दिव्यरूपा च सुन्दरीः ॥ १७॥

English
Then five auspicious rivers flowed. They had pure water. They were very rapid. They were beautiful and divine in form. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
जटोदका त्रिस्रोताश्च वृषध्वनिरितीव हि ।
स्वर्णोदका जम्बुनदी पञ्च नद्यः प्रकीर्तिताः ॥ १८॥

English
The five rivers²⁴ are glorified thus—Jaṭodakā, Trisrotas, Vṛṣadhvani, Svarṇodakā and Jambunadi. (18.)

[fn 24. This group of five rivers is mythical. It is not appropriate to identify the Trisrotas of this group with the Teesta in North Bengal and the Jambu with the Jambu of mount Abu. For the Purāṇa speaks of the group of five rivers forming a confluence near Japeśvara. The Teesta and the Jambu meet nowhere and cannot be held identical with the Trisrotas and the Jambu that have entered into this group.]
---

Sanskrit
एतत्पञ्चनदं नाम शिवपृष्ठतमं शुभम् ।
जपेश्वरसमीपे तु पवित्रं परमं मुने ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, this basin called Pañcanada is a very auspicious spot of Śiva. It is near Japeśvara and is very holy. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पञ्चनदमासाद्य स्नात्वा जप्त्वेश्वरेश्वरम् ।
पूजयेच्छिवसायुज्यं प्रयात्येव न संशयः ॥ २०॥

English
He who goes to the holy centre of Pañcanada, takes his bath there, performs the Japa of the lord of lords and worships him, certainly attains the Sāyujya of Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
उमोवाच ।
दातुमर्हसि देवेश नन्दिने परमेश्वर ।
महाप्रियतमो नाथ शैलादिस्तनयो मम ॥ २२॥

English
Pārvatī said :— O lord of gods, it behoves you to give that to Nandin. O lord, Śailādi is my most beloved son. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
ततः स शङ्करः स्वीयान्सस्मार गणपान्वरान् ।
स्वतन्त्रः परमेशानः सर्वदो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २३॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— Then Śiva the independent lord, the Supreme deity, the bestower of everything and favourably disposed towards his devotees remembered his excellent leaders of Gañas. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मरणादेव रुद्रस्य सम्प्राप्ताश्च गणेश्वराः ।
असङ्ख्याता महामोदाः शङ्कराकृतयोऽखिलाः ॥ २४॥

English
Even as they were remembered, all the leading Gañas of Rudra came there. They were countless. They had the shape of Śiva. They were highly delighted. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ते गणेशाः शिवं देवीं प्रणम्याहुः शुभं वचः ।
ते प्रणम्य करौ बद्ध्वा नतस्कन्धा महाबलाः ॥ २५॥

English
Those leading Gañas bowed to lord Śiva and the goddess Pārvatī with joined palms and drooping shoulders. The powerful Gañas spoke. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेशा ऊचुः ।
किमर्थं च स्मृता देव ह्याज्ञापय महाप्रभो ।
किङ्करान्नः समायातांस्त्रिपुरार्दन कामद ॥ २६॥

English
The leading Gaṇas said :— O great lord, the destroyer of Tripuras, why are we called? Please command us, O bestower of wishes. We are your servants and we have come here. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
किं सागरान् शोषयामो यमं वा सह किङ्करैः ।
हन्मो मृत्युं महामृत्युं विशेषं वृद्धपद्मजम् ॥ २७॥

English
Shall we dry up the oceans? Shall we kill Yama with his servants? Shall we slay great death or the aged Brahma especially? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
बद्ध्वेन्द्रं सह देवैश्च विष्णुं वा पार्षदैः सह ।
आनयामः सुसङ्क्रुद्धान्दैत्यान्वा दानवैः सह ॥ २८॥

English
Shall we bind Indra with all the gods or Visnu with all his Parsadas and bring them here? Or the Daityas and Danavas? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्याद्य व्यसनं घोरं करिष्यामस्तवाज्ञया ।
कस्य वाद्योत्सवो देव सर्वकामसमृद्धये ॥ २९॥

English
To whom shall we cause great and terrible distress at your behest? O lord, who is to be extremely jubilant to-day for the increase of all cherished desires? (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां गणानां वीरवादिनाम् ।
उवाच तान्स प्रशंस्य गणेशान्परमेश्वरः ॥ ३०॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— On hearing these heroic words of the Gaṇas the great lord spoke after praising them. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
नन्दीश्वरोऽयं पुत्रो मे सर्वेषामीश्वरेश्वरः ।
प्रियो गणाग्रणीः सर्वैः क्रियतां वचनं मम ॥ ३१॥

English
Śiva said :— Nandiśvara is my son. He is the lord of lords. He is the favourite leader of my Gañas. Let my behest be carried out by you. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे प्रीत्याभिषिञ्चध्वं मद्गणानां गतिं पतिम् ।
अद्यप्रभृति युष्माकमयं नन्दीश्वरः प्रभुः ॥ ३२॥

English
All of you, crown him lovingly as your leader and resort of all my Gañas. From today onwards, Nandiśvara is your lord. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्ताः शङ्करेण गणपाः सर्व एव ते ।
एवमस्त्विति सम्प्रोच्य सम्भारानाहरँस्ततः ॥ ३३॥

English
# Nandīśvara said :— Thus ordered by Siva, the leading Ganas said "Let it be so"; they brought all the requisite things (for his consecration) (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो देवाश्च सेन्द्राश्च नारायणमुखास्तथा ।
मुनयः सर्वतो लोका आजग्मुर्मुदिताननाः ॥ ३४॥

English
Then Nārāyaṇa and other gods including Indra, and the sages came from all the worlds with pleased faces. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पितामहोऽपि भगवन्नियोगाच्छङ्करस्य वै ।
चकार नन्दिनः सर्वमभिषेकं समाहितः ॥ ३५॥

English
At the behest of Siva, the holy Brahma performed the coronation of Nandin with care and attention. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विष्णुस्ततः शक्रो लोकपालास्तथैव च ।
ऋषयस्तुष्टुवुश्चैव पितामहपुरोगमाः ॥ ३६॥

English
Then Visnu, Indra, the guardians of the quarters, Brahma and the sages eulogised me. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुतिमत्सु ततस्तेषु विष्णुः सर्वजगत्पतिः ।
शिरस्यञ्जलिमाधाय तुष्टाव च समाहितः ॥ ३७॥

English
While they were eulogising, Visnu, the lord of the universe, eulogised me with devotion with palms joined together over the head. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राञ्जलिः प्रणतो भूत्वा जयशब्दं चकार च ।
ततो गणाधिपाः सर्व ततो देवास्ततोऽसुराः ॥ ३८॥

English
With palms joined in reverence he bowed to me and cried shouts of victory. Then all the leading Ganas, the gods and the Asuras did likewise in order. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्तुतश्चाभिषिक्तो देवैः सब्रह्मकैस्तदा ।
नन्दीश्वरोहं विप्रेन्द्र नियोगात्प रमेशितुः ॥ ३९॥

English
O great brahmin, thus at the instance of the great lord, I, Nandiśvara was coronated and eulogised by the gods including Brahma. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्वाहश्च कृतस्तत्र नियोगात्परमेष्ठिनः ।
महोत्सवयुतः प्रीत्या विष्णुब्रह्मादिभिर्मम ॥ ४०॥

English
My nuptial ceremony was also conducted at the instance of Brahma with great jubilation and pleasure by Visnu, Brahma and others. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
मरुतां च सुता देवी सुयशास्तु मनोहरा ।
पत्नी सा मेऽभवद्दिव्या मनोनयननन्दिनी ॥ ४१॥

English
The daughter of the Maruts, the gentle and beautiful lady Suyasa became my divine wife pleasing to my eyes and mind. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

चामरैश्चामरासक्तहस्ताग्रैः स्त्रीगणैर्युतम् ॥ ४२॥

English
A well-decorated umbrella shedding moonlike lustre was held above her in the company of women holding the chowries in their hands. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
A fine throne was occupied by me along with her. I was bedecked by the illustrious Laksm with various ornaments like crown and others. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
A fine necklace worn by the goddess round her neck was given to me. I got a fine bull, a white elephant, a lion, the lion-banner, a chariot, gold necklace, like the disc of the moon and many other auspicious articles, O sage. (44-45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O great sage, the marriage having been celebrated thus I, in the company of my wife, saluted the feet of Siva, Brahma and Visnu for the sake of welfare. (46.)

---

English
Siva, lord of the three worlds, favourably disposed towards his devotees spoke these words with great love and pleasure to me accompanied by my wife. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।

English
Lord Śiva said :— O dear son, listen. This Suyasā is your beloved wife : Whatever is desired by you in your mind I shall grant you that boon. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Nandīśa, O lord of Gaṇas, I am always pleased with you in the company of the goddess. O dear boy, listen to my weighty words. (49.)

---

English

You will be all-victorious and worthy of worship. You will be endowed with great strength. Wherever I am, you shall also be and wherever you are I shall also be. (50-51.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O son, your father will be endowed with all prosperity. He will become a presiding officer of the Gaṇas, a great devotee of mine and very powerful. (52.)

Sanskrit
