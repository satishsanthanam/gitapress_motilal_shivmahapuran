### **CHAPTER EIGHT - (Bhairava incarnation)**

---

Sanskrit
अथ भैरवावतारमाह ।
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ श‍ृणु त्वं भैरवीं कथाम् ।
यस्याः श्रवणमात्रेण शैवी भक्तिर्दृढा भवेत् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— O Sanatkumāra, O omniscient one, listen to the story of Bhairava by listening to which alone, devotion to Śiva becomes firm and steady. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
भैरवः पूर्णरूपो हि शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ।
मूढास्तं वै न जानन्ति मोहिताः शिवमायया ॥ २॥

English
Bhairava is the prior form of Siva, the great soul. Fools who are deluded by Śiva's Māyā do not know about it. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार नो वेत्ति महिमानं महेशितुः ।
चतुर्भुजोऽपि विष्णुर्वै चतुर्वक्त्रोऽपि वै विधिः ॥ ३॥

English
O Sanatkumāra, neither Vişnu of four arms nor Brahmā of four faces knows the greatness of lord Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रमत्र न किञ्चिद्वै दुर्ज्ञेया खलु शाम्भवी ।
तया सम्मोहिताः सर्वे नार्चयन्त्यपि तं परम् ॥ ४॥

English
There is nothing to be surprised at in this. Siva's Māyā is inscrutable. Deluded by it people do not even worship him, the great lord. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदयेद्यदि वात्मानं स एव परमेश्वरः ।
तदा विदन्ति ते सर्वे स्वेच्छया न हि केऽपि तम् ॥ ५॥

English
If any one knows him the supreme soul, he alone is lord Siva. But then, none of these people know him out of their own will. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वगोऽपि महेशानो नेक्ष्यते मूढबुद्धिभिः ।
देववद् बुध्यते लोके योऽतीतो मनसां गिराम् ॥ ६॥

English
Although lord Siva is omnipresent he is not seen by the people of deluded intellect. He is known as a mere lord, he who is beyond the reach of minds and words. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रेतिहासं वक्ष्येऽहं परमर्षे पुरातनम् ।
श‍ृणु तं श्रद्धया तात परमं ज्ञानकारणम् ॥ ७॥

English
O great sage, in this connection I shall narrate an ancient legend. O dear one, listen to it with faith. It is the great cause of perfect knowledge. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मेरुश‍ृङ्गेऽद्भुते रम्ये स्थितं ब्रह्माणमीश्वरम् ।
जग्मुर्देवर्षयः सर्वे सुतत्त्वं ज्ञातुमिच्छया ॥ ८॥

English
As Brahma was stationed on the wonderful and beautiful summit of Meru, all the celestial sages approached him with a desire to know the real truth. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य विधिं नत्वा पप्रच्छुस्ते महादरात् ।
कृताञ्जलिपुटाः सर्वे नतस्कन्धा मुनीश्वराः ॥ ९॥

English
All the great sages with palms joined in reverence and shoulders drooping down came there and bowed to Brahma. They asked him respectfully. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवर्षय ऊचुः ।
देवदेव प्रजानाथ सृष्टिकृल्लोकनायक ।
तत्त्वतो वद चास्मभ्यं किमेकं तत्त्वमव्ययम् ॥ १०॥

English
The celestial sages said:—  O lord of gods, O lord of subjects, O Creator, O leader of the worlds, tell us the real fact. What is that sole unchanging entity ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
स मायया महेशस्य मोहितः पद्मसम्भवः ।
अविज्ञाय परं भावं सम्भावं प्रत्युवाच ह ॥ ११॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  Deluded by the Māyā of great lord and not knowing the great fact the lotus-born Brahmā replied haughtily. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
हे सुरा ऋषयः सर्वे सुमत्या श‍ृणुतादरात् ।
वच्म्यहं परमं तत्त्वमव्ययं वै यथार्थतः ॥ १२॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O gods, O sages, all of you listen with attention and intelligence I shall describe the great unchanging entity as it really is. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
जगद्योनिरहं धाता स्वयम्भूरज ईश्वरः ।
अनादिभागहं ब्रह्म ह्येक आत्मा निरञ्जनः ॥ १३॥

English
I am the womb (material cause) of the universe, the creator, the self-born, the unborn, the lord, the primordial being, the Brahman, the sole unsullied soul. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवर्तको हि जगतामहमेव निवर्त्तकः ।
संवर्तको मदधिको नान्यः कश्चित्सुरोत्तमाः ॥ १४॥

English
O excellent gods, I am the instigator of the worlds, and the deterrent too. There is none greater than I or any other who causes their revolution. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तस्यैवं वदतो धातुर्विष्णुस्तत्र स्थितो मुने ।
प्रोवाच प्रहसन्वाक्यं सङ्क्रुद्धो मोहितोऽजया ॥ १५॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  While the Creator was saying thus, O sage, Visnu who was there became angry as he too was deluded by Maya. He spoke these words derisively. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
न चैतदुचिता ब्रह्मन्योगयुक्तस्य मूर्खता ।
अविज्ञाय परं तत्त्वं वृथैतत्ते निगद्यते ॥ १६॥

English
O Brahma, this is not proper. It is sheer foolishness on the part of a practiser of Yoga. Without knowing the great reality, this is being mentioned in vain. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्ताऽहं सर्वलोकानां परमात्मा परः पुमान् ।
यज्ञो नारायणो देवो मायाधीशः परागतिः ॥ १७॥

English
I am the Creator of all the worlds, the great Atman, the greatest Purusa, the sacrifice, the lord Narayana, the master of Maya and the greatest goal. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ममाज्ञया त्वया ब्रह्मन् सृष्टिरेषा विधीयते ।
जगतां जीवनं नैव मामनादृत्य चेश्वरम् ॥ १८॥

English
O Brahma, it is at my behest that creation is carried on by you. Without regard for me, the lord, no life can exist in the worlds. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विप्रकृतौ मोहात्परस्परजयैषिणौ ।
प्रोचतुर्निगमांश्चात्र प्रमाणे सर्वथा तनौ ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, thus starting a mutual clash due to delusion and desiring to conquer each other they asked the Vedas about truth. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रष्टव्यास्ते विशेषेण स्थिता मूर्तिधराश्च ते ।
पप्रच्छतुः प्रमाणज्ञानित्युक्त्वा चतुरोऽपि तान् ॥ २०॥

English
The Vedas were there in embodied form. They shall be specifically asked. With this idea in mind they asked the four Vedas about the truth. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विधिविष्णू ऊचतुः ।
वेदाः प्रमाणं सर्वत्र प्रतिष्ठां परमामिताः ।
यूयं वदत विश्रब्धं किमेकं तत्त्वमव्ययम् ॥ २१॥

English
Brahmā and Viṣṇu said:— Vedas are the authorities everywhere. They have been honoured everywhere. Hence you please say unreservedly. What is the sole unchanging entity? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य तयोर्वाचं पुनस्ते हि ऋगादयः ।
अवदंस्तत्त्वतः सर्वे परेशं संस्मरतो प्रभुम् ॥ २२॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— On hearing their words, the Vedas spoke in reality after remembering the lord. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि मान्या वयं देवौ सृष्टिस्थितिकरौ विभू ।
तदा प्रमाणं वक्ष्यामो भवत्सन्देहभेदकम् ॥ २३॥

English
"If we are honoured, O deities as the causes of creation and maintenance, we shall cite authorities that will dispel your doubts. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श्रुत्युक्तविधिमाकर्ण्य प्रोचतुस्तौ सुरौ श्रुतीः ।
युष्मदुक्तं प्रमाणं नौ किं तत्त्वं सम्यगुच्यताम् ॥ २४॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing the conditions mentioned by the Vedas the two deities told the Vedas—“What is mentioned by you is acceptable to us as authority. What is that entity? Please tell us that. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋग्वेद उवाच ।
यदन्तः स्थानि भूतानि यतः सर्वं प्रवर्त्तते ।
यदाहुः परमं तत्त्वं स रुद्रस्त्वेक एव हि ॥ २५॥

English
Ṛgveda said:— That in which the Bhūtas stay, that from which everything comes out, what they call as the greatest entity—that is Rudra alone. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यजुर्वेद उवाच ।
यो यज्ञैरखिलैरीशो योगेन च समिज्यते ।
येन प्रमाणं खलु नः स एकः सर्वदृक् शिवः ॥ २६॥

English
Yajurveda said:— The lord who is worshipped by means of all sacrifices and meditation, whereby we have an authority—that sole seer of all is Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सामवेद उवाच ।
येनेदं भ्रम्यते विश्वं योगिभिर्यो विचिन्त्यते ।
यद्भासा भासते विश्वं स एकस्त्र्यम्बकः परः ॥ २७॥

English
Sāmaveda said:—  That by which this universe whirls, that which is meditated upon by Yogins, the light whereby the universe is illuminated—that Tryambaka alone is the greatest. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथर्वणवेद उवाच ।
यं प्रपश्यन्ति देवेशं भक्त्यनुग्रहिणो जनाः ।
तमाहुरेकं कैवल्यं शङ्करं दुःखतः परम् ॥ २८॥

English
Atharvaveda said:— They call that sole being Śiva who is beyond all misery who is the lord of gods whom the people who have the blessing of devotion see. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श्रुत्युक्तमिदमाकर्ण्यातीव मायाविमोहितौ ।
स्मित्वाहतुर्विधिहरी निगमांस्तान्विचेतनौ ॥ २९॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— After hearing what was thus said by the Vedas, Brahmā and Viṣṇu, deluded very much by the Māyā (and hence) crooked in consciousness smiled and said to the Vedas. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विधिहरी ऊचतुः ।
हे वेदाः किमिदं यूयं भाषन्ते गतचेतनाः ।
किं जातं वोऽद्य सर्वं हि नष्टं सुवयुनं परम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Brahmā and Viṣṇu said:—  O Vedas, what is this that you all speak without sense? What has happened to you? All wisdom is utterly lost now. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं प्रमथनाथोऽसौ रममाणो निरन्तरम् ।
दिगम्बरः पीतवर्णो शिवया धूलिधूसरः ॥ ३१॥

विरूपवेषो जटिलो वृषगो व्यालभूषणः ।

English
The lord of Pramathas incessantly sports with Śivā. He is naked and of yellow colour and tawny due to dust. His dress and features are ugly. He has matted hair and moves about on his bull. He wears serpents as his ornaments. How can he be the supreme Brahman? Where is that Brahman free from any attachment?" (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit

अमूर्तो मूर्तिमान्प्रीत्या जृम्भमाण उवाच तौ ॥ ३३॥

English
On hearing their words, the all-pervasive Praṇava who though unembodied assumed an embodied, extensive form told them thus. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Praṇava said: Lord Iśa, Rudra, Śiva indulging in sports and assuming various forms never sports about with the Śakti different from him. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The great lord, is self-luminous and eternal. Śivā is his Śakti in the form of Bliss, not a chance occurrence. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

नाज्ञानमगमन्नाशं श्रीकण्ठस्यैव मायया ॥ ३६॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— Even after this explanation the ignorance of Brahmā and Viṣṇu was not quelled. It was due to the Māyā of Śiva alone. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
The fifth head of Brahmā burnt with rage (as though saying) “who is this bearing a man’s outline and features in our midst?” (39.)

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---

---

English

---

English

---

नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एतान्वरान्प्रगृह्याथ तत्क्षणात्कालभैरवः ।
वामाङ्गुलिनखाग्रेण चकर्त च विधेश्शिरः ॥ ५२॥

English
# Nandīśvara said:— After receiving all these boons, Kāla-Bhairava, in a trice, cut off Brahma's head with the tip of the nails of the fingers of his left hand.[25] (52.)

[fn 25. Vs(Ch 8) speaks of Bhairava's attempt to cut off the fifth head of Brahmā at the instance of Śiva and also of the cancellation of that attempt by the intervention of Śiva at the behest of Viṣṇu. The present account of Śatarudra Saṃhitā is somewhat different from the above account. Here Kālabhairava is mentioned to have severed the fifth head of Brahmā with the nails of his left finger. This account is substantiated by similar accounts in the other Purāṇas.]
