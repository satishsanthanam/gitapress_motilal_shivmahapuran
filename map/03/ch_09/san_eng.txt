### **CHAPTER NINE - (The sports of Bhairava)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ भैरवीमपरां कथाम् ।
श‍ृणु प्रीत्या महादोषसंहर्त्रीं भक्तिवर्द्धिनीम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said :—  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, listen lovingly to another story of Bhairava that destroys all defects and enhances devotion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्सान्निध्यं भैरवोऽपि कालोऽभूत्कालकालनः ।
स देवदेववाक्येन बिभ्रत्कापालिकं व्रतम् ॥ २॥

English
Due to Brahmahatyā’s presence Bhairava began to calculate the time for release. At the words of the lord of gods, he kept on the rite of Kāpālika. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालपाणिर्विश्वात्मा चचार भुवनत्रयम् ।
नात्याक्षीच्चापि तं देवं ब्रह्महत्यापि दारुणा ॥ ३॥

English
He, the soul of the universe, roamed over the three worlds with the skull in his hand. The terrible Brahmahatyā never left Bhairava. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रतितीर्थं भ्रमन् नापि विमुक्तो ब्रह्महत्यया ।
अतः कामारिमहिमा सर्वोऽपि ह्यवगम्यताम् ॥ ४॥

English
Although he went to every holy centre, he was not left off by Brahmahatyā. Let the greatness of Śiva, the enemy of Kāma, be understood thereby. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमथैः सेव्यमानोऽपि ह्येकदा विहरन्हरः ।
कापालिको ययौ स्वैरी नारायणनिकेतनम् ॥ ५॥

English
Once, while sporting about, being attended upon by Pramathas, Bhairava went as he pleased, to the abode of Viṣṇu. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथायान्तं महाकालं त्रिनेत्रं सर्पकुण्डलम् ।
महादेवांशसम्भूतं पूर्णाकारं च भैरवम् ॥ ६॥

पपात दण्डवद्भूमौ तं दृष्ट्वा गरुडध्वजः ।
देवाश्च मुनयश्चैव देवनार्य्यः समन्ततः ॥ ७॥

English
On seeing the three-eyed Bhairava of full size, born partially of lord Śiva, with serpents for his ear-rings the Garuḍa-bannered Viṣṇu fell prostrate on the ground like a staff. The gods, the sages and the womenfolk of the gods too did the same. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विष्णुः प्रणम्यैनं प्रयातः कमलापतिः ।
शिरस्यञ्जलिमाधाय तुष्टाव विविधैः स्तवैः ॥ ८॥

English
After bowing to him, pure in mind, and keeping the joined palms on his head, Viṣṇu, the consort of Lakṣmī, eulogised him with various hymns. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सानन्दोऽथ हरिः प्राह प्रसन्नात्मा महामुने ।
क्षीरोदमथनोद्भूतां पद्मां पद्मालयां मुदा ॥ ९॥

English
The delighted Viṣṇu being highly pleased in his mind, O great sage, joyously spoke to Lakṣmī, born at the time of the churning of the milky ocean²⁶ and residing in the lotus. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
प्रिये पश्याब्जनयने धन्यासि सुभगेऽनघे ।
धन्योऽहं देवि सुश्रोणि यत्पश्यावो जगत्पतिम् ॥ १०॥

English
Viṣṇu said :—  “O lotus-eyed beloved, you are blessed, O comely sinless lady. O gentle lady of beautiful hips, I am blessed, since we both see the lord of the universe. (10.)

---

Sanskrit

अनादिः शरणः शान्तः पुरः षड्विंशसम्मितः ॥ ११॥

English

Sanskrit
सर्वज्ञः सर्वयोगीशः सर्वभूतैकनायकः ।
सर्वभूतान्तरात्माऽयं सर्वेषां सर्वदः सदा ॥ १२॥

English
He is omniscient, the lord of all Yogins, the sole leader of all Beings, the inner Ātman of all living creatures and the bestower of everything to everyone always. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ये विनिद्रा विनिःश्वासाः शान्ताः ध्यानपरायणाः ।
धिया पश्यन्ति हृदये सोऽयं पद्मे समीक्षताम् ॥ १३॥

English
O Lakṣmī, see, he is that lord whom the quiet wakeful people, engrossed in breathless meditation, see in their heart of hearts through their mind. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यं विदुर्वेदतत्त्वज्ञा योगिनो यतमानसाः ।
अरूपो रूपवान्भूत्वा सोऽयमायाति सर्वगः ॥ १४॥

English
Here he comes, the all-pervasive who is without form but has assumed a form (now) and whom only the Yogins of restrained mind, the knowers of the principles of the Vedas, understand. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो विचित्रं देवस्य चेष्टितं परमेष्ठिनः ।
यस्याख्यां ब्रुवतो नित्यं न देहः सोऽपि देहभृत् ॥ १५॥

English
Oh, wonderful indeed is the activity of the lord Śiva, for he who repeats his names is freed from the bondage of the body that he has taken then. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा न पुनर्जन्म लभ्यते मानवैर्भुवि ।
सोऽयमायाति भगवांस्त्र्यम्बकः शशिभूषणः ॥ १६॥

English
On seeing him men do not obtain a rebirth in the world. Such a lord the moon-crested Tryambaka comes here. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पुण्डरीकदलायामे धन्ये मेऽद्य विलोचने ।
यद् दृश्यते महादेवो ह्याभ्यां लक्ष्मि महेश्वरः ॥ १७॥

English
O Lakṣmī, blessed indeed are my eyes as wide as the petals of a lotus, since by them the great god lord Śiva is seen. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
धिग्धिक्पदं तु देवानां परं दृष्ट्वा न शङ्करम् ।
लभ्यते यत्र निर्वाणं सर्वदुःखान्तकृत्तु यत् ॥ १८॥

English
Fie upon the position of the gods where emancipation is not obtained, but on seeing Śiva it is obtained and it puts an end to all miseries. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
देवत्वादशुभं किञ्चिद्देवलोके न विद्यते ।
दृष्ट्वापि सर्वे देवेशं यन्मुक्तिं न लभामहे ॥ १९॥

English
There is nothing more inauspicious than the state of the gods in the heaven that here even after seeing the lord of the gods all of us do not obtain salvation. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा हृषीकेशः सम्प्रहृष्टतनूरुहः ।
प्रणिपत्य महादेवमिदमाह वृषध्वजम् ॥ २०॥

English
Nandīśvara said :—  After saying this, Viṣṇu bowed to the great lord with the thrill of delight through pleasure and spoke this to the bull-bannered deity ? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
किमिदं देवदेवेन सर्वज्ञेन त्वया विभो ।
क्रियते जगतां धात्रा सर्वपापहराव्यय ॥ २१॥

English
Viṣṇu said :— O lord, O unchanging one, O queller of all sins, what is this being done by you the omniscient lord of the gods and the creator of the worlds ? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रीडेयं तव देवेश त्रिलोचन महामते ।
किं कारणं विरूपाक्ष चेष्टितं ते स्मरार्दन ॥ २२॥

English
O lord of the gods, O three-eyed intelligent one this is only your sport. O Virūpākṣa, O suppresser of Kāma, what is this activity of yours motivated by ? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
किमर्थं भगवञ्छम्भो भिक्षां चरसि शक्तिप ।
संशयो मे जगन्नाथ एष त्रैलोक्यराज्यद ॥ २३॥

English
O lord Śiva, O lord of Śakti, why do you go a-begging? O lord of the universe, this is my doubt, O bestower of the kingdom of the three worlds ! (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्तस्ततः शम्भुर्विष्णुना भैरवो हरः ।
प्रत्युवाचाद्भुतोतिः स विष्णुं हि विहसन्प्रभुः ॥ २४॥

English
Nandīśvara said :—  Thus addressed by Viṣṇu, lord Śiva, the destroyer of the worlds of ferocious form of wonderful sports, laughingly said to Viṣṇu. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
भैरव उवाच ।
ब्रह्मणस्तु शिरश्छिन्नमङ्गुल्याग्रनखेन ह ।
तदघं प्रतिहन्तुं हि चराम्येतद् व्रतं शुभम् ॥ २५॥

English
Bhairava said :—  Brahmā's head was cut off with the top of the nail of my fingers. It is to remove that sin that I am observing this auspicious sacred rite. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्तो महेशेन भैरवेण रमापतिः ।
स्मृत्वा किञ्चिन्नतशिराः पुनरेवमजिज्ञपत् ॥ २६॥

English
Nandīvara said :—  Thus addressed by Śiva, Bhairava, the lord of Lakṣmī remembered something and bent down his head. He spoke again. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
यथेच्छसि तथा क्रीड सर्वविघ्नापनोदक ।
मायया मां महादेव नाच्छादयितुमर्हसि ॥ २७॥

English
Viṣṇu said :—  "O great god, O remover of all obstacles, sport about as you please. But it does not behove you to envelop me with your Māyā. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नाभीकमलकोशात्तु कोटिशः कमलासनाः ।
कल्पे कल्पे पुरा ह्यान्सस्त्वन्नियोगबलाद्विभो ॥ २८॥

English
O lord, it is true that from within the lotus from my navel, crores of lotus-seated Brahmās have come out in the different Kalpas through the power of Yoga. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
त्यज मायामिमां देव दुस्तरामकृतात्मभिः ।
ब्रह्मादयो महादेव मायया तव मोहिताः ॥ २९॥

English
O lord, please eschew this Māyā very difficult to be crossed by those who have no control over themselves. Brahmā and others are deluded by your Māyā. (29.)

---

Sanskrit

तवैवानुग्रहाच्छम्भो सर्वेश्वर सताङ्गते ॥ ३०॥

English
O Śiva, O lord of all, O goal of the good, O lord of Śivā, it is due to your own blessing that I am able to follow your activity. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

लोकान्वर्णाश्रमवतो हरिष्यसि यदा हर ॥ ३१॥

English
When the time of dissolution comes, O destroyer of the world, you annihilate all the gods, sages and people of all castes and stages of life. O great god, then, the sin of slaying brahmins and others does not affect you. You are not bound by these and are quite independent. You sport as you please. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Śiva, the garland of the bones of Brahmā shines around your neck. Similarly, O Śiva, O sinless one, the primordial Brahmahatyā has followed you. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even after committing a great sin, if a person, remembers you who are the lord and support of the worlds, his sin is quelled. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Just as darkness does not stand in the presence of the sun, similarly the sin of your devotee does not stand but is wasted away. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If a pious soul contemplates on the lotus-like pair of your feet, even his sin of brahmin-slaughter becomes wasted away. (36.)

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English
Viṣṇu said :—  O lord of gods, this itself is a great favour that I see you who being the lord of the gods are inconceivable by mind and speech. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
अदभ्रेयं सुधादृष्टिरनया मे महोत्सवः ।
अयत्त्ननिधिलाभोऽयं वीक्षणं हर ते सताम् ॥ ५१॥

English
O Siva, this is like a shower of nectar without clouds. I am jubilant over this; thy benign glance to the good is a treasure easily acquired by them. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अवियोगोऽस्तु मे देव त्वदङ्घ्रियुगलेन वै ।
एष एव वरः शम्भो नान्यं कश्चिद् वृणे वरम् ॥ ५२॥

English
O lord Siva, let me never be detached from your feet; this is the favour I seek, nothing else. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीभैरव उवाच ।
एवं भवतु ते तात यत्त्वयोक्तं महामते ।
सर्वेषामपि देवानां वरदस्त्वं भविष्यसि ॥ ५३॥

English
Lord Bhairava said :—  “O dear, O intelligent one, let what you have said occur. You will be the granter of boons to all the gods.” (53.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अनुगृह्येति दैत्यारिं केन्द्राद्रिभुवने चरन् ।
भेजेऽविमुक्तनगरीं नाम्ना वाराणसीं पुरीम् ॥ ५४॥

English
Nandīśvara said :—  Blessing Visnu moving in the central mountain Meru<sup>28</sup> and the world, the lord went to the city of the liberated, Varanasi. (54.)

[fn 28. The mount Meru, situated in the centre of the earth, is the common resort of Viṣṇu, Indra and other gods.]
---

Sanskrit
क्षेत्रे प्रविष्टमात्रेऽथ भैरवे भीषणाकृतौ ।
हाहेत्युक्त्वा ब्रह्महत्या पातालं चाविशत्तदा ॥ ५५॥

English
As soon as Bhairava of terrible features entered the holy centre, Brahmahatya felt aggrieved and entered the nether worlds. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालं ब्राह्मणः सद्यो भैरवस्य कराम्बुजात् ।
पपात भुवि तत्तीर्थमभूत्कापालमोचनम् ॥ ५६॥

English
The skull of Brahma fell on the ground suddenly from the lotus-like hand of Bhairava. The holy centre then became Kapalamocana. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालं ब्रह्मणो रुद्रः सर्वेषामेव पश्यताम् ।
हस्तात्पतन्तमालोक्य ननर्त परया मुदा ॥ ५७॥

English
On seeing the skull of Brahma falling from his hand, even as everyone was watching it, Rudra danced with great joy. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
विधेः कपालं नामुञ्चत्करमत्यन्तदुस्सहम् ।
परस्य भ्रमतः क्वापि तत्काश्यां क्षणतोऽपतत् ॥ ५८॥

English
The unbearable skull of Brahma did not leave his hand as he was roaming elsewhere but it fell down in a trice at Kasi. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
शूलिनो ब्रह्मणो हत्या नापैति स्म च या क्वचित् ।
सा काश्यां क्षणतो नष्टा तस्मात्सेव्या हि काशिका ॥ ५९॥

English
Brahmahatyā which did not leave Śiva at any other place vanished in a moment at Kāśī. Hence Kāśī shall be resorted to for shedding off evil. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालमोचनं काश्यां यः स्मरेत्तीर्थमुत्तमम् ।
इहान्यत्रापि यत्पापं क्षिप्रं तस्य प्रणश्यति ॥ ६०॥

English
If a person remembers the excellent holy centre Kapālamocana at Kāśī, his sin committed here and elsewhere becomes quelled immediately. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्य तीर्थप्रवरे स्नानं कृत्वा विधानतः ।
तर्पयित्वा पितॄन्देवान्मुच्यते ब्रह्महत्यया ॥ ६१॥

English
After coming to the foremost of holy centres, performing the ablution in accordance with the rules and by making the water-offering to the manes one is rid of Brahmahatyā. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
कपालमोचनं तीर्थं पुरस्कृत्वा तु भैरवः ।
तत्रैव तस्थौ भक्तानां भक्षयन्नघसन्ततिम् ॥ ६२॥

English
Bhairava stood facing the holy centre of Kapālamocana, devouring the series of sins of devotees. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णाष्टम्यां तु मार्गस्य मासस्य परमेश्वरः ।
आविर्बभूव सल्लीलो भैरवात्मा सतां प्रियः ॥ ६३॥

English
It was on the eighth day in the dark half of the month of Mārgaśīrṣa that lord Śiva, the favourite of the good and indulging in auspicious sports, manifested himself in the form of Bhairava. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्गशीर्षासिताष्टम्यां कालभैरवसन्निधौ ।
उपोष्य जागरं कुर्वन्महापापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ६४॥

English
The person who observes fast on the eighth day of the dark half of the month of Mārgaśīrṣa in front of Kālabhairava and also keeps awake at night is freed from great sins. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यत्रापि नरो भक्त्या तद् व्रतं यः करिष्यति ।
सजागरं महापापैर्मुक्तो यास्यति सद्गतिम् ॥ ६५॥

English
He who performs that rite with devotion even elsewhere and also keeps awake is rid of great sins and attains the goal of the good. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेकजन्मनियुतैर्यत्कृतं जन्तुभिस्त्वघम् ।
तत्सर्वं विलयं याति कालभैरवदर्शनात् ॥ ६६॥

English
The sin committed by persons in the course of countless millions of births is quelled entirely on seeing Kālabhairava. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
कालभैरवभक्तानां पातकानि करोति यः ।

English
He who commits offence against the devotees of Kālabhairava is deluded and becomes miserable. He will attain the distress of the wicked people. (67.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेश्वरेऽपि ये भक्ता नो भक्ताः कालभैरवे ।

English
The devotees of lord Śiva, who do not show devotion for Kālabhairava, attain great sorrow and especially so at Kāśī. (68.)

---

Sanskrit
वाराणस्यामुषित्वा यो भैरवं न भजेन्नरः ।

English
The man who, while staying at Vārāṇasī, neglects the worship of Bhairava acquires sins that multiply like the moon in the bright half. (69.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If a person neglects the worship of Kālarāja at Kāśī on every Tuesday and Bhūtāṣṭamī day, his merit decreases like the moon in the dark half. (70.)

Sanskrit

English
A person is rid of all sins on hearing this narrative of "Bhairava's nativity" which can quell even Brahmahatyā. (71.)

English
Even a person put in a prison, or involved in adversity is freed from distress on hearing of the narrative of Bhairava. (72.)
