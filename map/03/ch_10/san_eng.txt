### **CHAPTER TEN - (The narrative of Man-lion)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
विध्वंसी दक्षयज्ञस्य वीरभद्राह्वयः प्रभोः ।
अवतारश्च विज्ञेयः शिवस्य परमात्मनः ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— The destroyer of Dakṣa's sacrifice Virabhadra shall be known as an incarnation of lord Śiva, the great soul. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सतीचरित्रे कथितं चरितं तस्य कृत्स्नशः ।
श्रुतं त्वयापि बहुधा नातः प्रोक्तं सुविस्तरात् ॥ २॥

English
His narrative has been mentioned in the episode of Sati. You too have heard it several times. Hence it is not mentioned in detail. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः परं मुनिश्रेष्ठ भवत्स्नेहाद् ब्रवीमि तत् ।
शार्दूलाख्यावतारं च शङ्करस्य प्रभोः श‍ृणु ॥ ३॥

English
O excellent sage, now by my affection to you I shall narrate the Śārdūla incarnation of lord Śiva, Listen. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवेन देवानां हितार्थं रूपमद्भुतम् ।
शारभं च धृतं दिव्यं ज्वलज्ज्वालासमप्रभम् ॥ ४॥

English
For the benefit of the gods, the wonderfully divine form of a Sarabha (the mythological animal with eight legs and capacity of killing lions) of blazing fire-like lustre was assumed by lord Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवावतारा अमिताः सद्भक्तहितकारकाः ।
सङ्ख्या न शक्यते कर्तुं तेषां च मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ ५॥

English
O excellent sages, Śiva's incarnations are countless and conducive to the benefit of good devotees. Their number cannot be counted or specified. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशस्य च ताराणां रेणुकानां क्षितेस्तथा ।
आसाराणां च वृद्धेन बहुकल्पैः कदापि हि ॥ ६॥

सङ्ख्या विशक्यते कर्तुं सुप्राज्ञैर्बहुजन्मभिः ।
न वै शिवावताराणां सत्यं जानीहि मद्वचः ॥ ७॥

English
The number of the stars in the sky, of the sand particles on the earth and of the bursting showers of heavy rain can be specified by intelligent persons at some time in the course of many births over many Kalpas; but not of the incarnations of Śiva; know my words to be true. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि च यथाबुद्ध्या कथयामि यथाश्रुतम् ।
चरित्रं शारभं दिव्यं परमैश्वर्यसूचकम् ॥ ८॥

English
Still in accordance with my intelligence I shall mention, exactly as I had heard, the divine story of the Sarabha, portending great prosperity. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जयश्च विजयश्चैव भवद्भिः शापितौ यदा ।
तदा दितिसुतौ द्वौ तावभूतां कश्यपान्मुने ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, when Jaya and Vijaya were cursed by you, they became the sons of Diti and Kaśyapa. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यकशिपुश्चाद्यो हिरण्याक्षोऽनुजो बली ।
देवर्षिपार्षदौ जातौ तौ द्वावपि दितेः सुतौ ॥ १०॥

English
Those two attendants; the celestial sages were born as Diti's sons. The elder was Hiranyakaśipu and the younger was Hiranyākṣa the very powerful. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथ्व्युद्धारे विधात्रा व प्रार्थितो हि पुरा प्रभुः ।
हिरण्याक्षं जघानासौ विष्णुर्वाराहरूपधृक् ॥ ११॥

English
Formerly lord Viṣṇu was requested by Brahmā to uplift the Earth. He assumed the form of a boar and slew Hiranyākṣa. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तं श्रुत्वा भ्रातरं वीरं निहतं प्राणसन्निभम् ।
चुकोप हरयेऽतीव हिरण्यकशिपुर्मुने ॥ १२॥

English
O sage, on hearing that his heroic brother, as dear to him as his very vital breath, was killed, Hiranyakaśipu became furious with Viṣṇu. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्षाणामयुतं तप्त्वा ब्रह्मणो वरमाप सः ।
न कश्चिन्मारयेन्मां वै त्वत्सृष्टाविति तुष्टतः ॥ १३॥

English
He performed penance for ten thousand years and propitiated Brahmā from whom he obtained the desired boon. "None of your creations shall kill me." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शोणिताख्यपुरं गत्वा देवानाहूय सर्वतः ।
त्रिलोकीं स्ववशे कृत्वा चक्रे राज्यमकण्टकम् ॥ १४॥

English
He left for Śoṇita.²⁹ He challenged the gods everywhere. He put the three worlds in his control and had an unrivalled rule over it. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
देवर्षिकदनं चक्रे सर्वधर्मविलोपकः ।
द्विजपीडाकरः पापी हिरण्यकशिपुर्मुने ॥ १५॥

English
O sage, Hiranyakaśipu harassed the gods and the sages. He violated all virtuous rites. He oppressed brahmins and became a sinner. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रह्लादेन स्वपुत्रेण हरिभक्तेन दैत्यराट् ।
यदा विद्वेषमकरोद्धरिर्वैरी विशेषतः ॥ १६॥

English
When the king of the Daityas hated his own son Prahlāda, Viṣṇu became his particular enemy. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सभास्तम्भात्तदा विष्णुरभूदाविर्द्रुतं मने ।
सन्ध्यायां क्रोधमापन्नो नृसिंहवपुषा ततः ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, Viṣṇu then came out from a pillar in the hall, at dusk in the body of a man-lion, with great fury. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा मुनिशार्दूल करालं नृहरेर्वपुः ।
प्रजज्वालातिभयदं त्रासयन्दैत्यसत्तमान् ॥ १८॥

English
O great sage, the body of the man-lion was very terrible in every respect. It blazed frighteningly and terrified the leading Daityas. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नृसिंहेन तदा दैत्या निहताश्चैव तत्क्षणम् ।
हिरण्यकशिपुश्चाथ युद्धं चक्रे सुदारुणम् ॥ १९॥

English
The Daityas were killed in a trice by the man-lion. Hiranyakaśipu fought a terrible battle. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
महायुद्धं तयोरासीन्मुहूर्त्तं मुनिसत्तमाः ।
विकरालं च भयदं सर्वेषां रोमहर्षणम् ॥ २०॥

English
O excellent sages, a great fight ensued between them for some time. It was terrifying, frightening and caused horripilation to every one. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सायं चकर्ष देवेशो देहल्यां दैत्यपुङ्गवम् ।
व्योम्नि देवेषु पश्यत्सु नृसिंहश्च रमेश्वरः ॥ २१॥

English
The lord of gods, lord of Lakṣmī, the man-lion dragged the leading Daitya to the threshold even as the gods were watching in the sky. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोत्सङ्गे च तं कृत्वा नखैस्तदुदरं द्रुतम् ।
विदार्य मारयामास पश्यतां त्रिदिवौकसाम् ॥ २२॥

English
While the gods were watching he placed the daitya on his lap and immediately tore open his stomach with his claws and killed him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
हते हिरण्यकशिपौ नृसिंहेनैव विष्णुना ।
जगत्स्वास्थ्यं तदा लेभे न वै देवा विशेषतः ॥ २३॥

English
When Hiranyakaśipu was killed by the man-lion Viṣṇu, the whole universe attained normalcy and peace. But the gods did not derive any special comfort. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदुन्दुभयो नेदुः प्रह्लादो विस्मयं गतः ।
लक्ष्मीश्च विस्मयं प्राप्ता रूपं दृष्ट्वाऽद्भुतं हरेः ॥ २४॥

English
The celestial drums were sounded. On seeing the wonderful form of Viṣṇu, Prahlāda was surprised, Lakṣmī was in a state of suspense. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
हतो यद्यपि दैत्येन्द्रस्तथापि न परं सुखम् ।
ययुर्देवा नृसिंहस्य ज्वाला सा न निवर्तिता ॥ २५॥

English
Though the lord of daityas was killed, yet the gods did not derive any pleasure. The fiery fury of the man-lion did not subside. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तया च व्याकुलं जातं सर्वं चैव जगत्पुनः ।
देवाश्च दुःखमापन्नाः किं भविष्यति वा पुनः ॥ २६॥

इत्येवं च वदन्तस्ते भयादू दूरमुपस्थिताः ।
नृसिंहक्रोधजज्वालाव्याकुलाः पद्मभूमुखाः ॥ २७॥

English
The entire universe was again excited by that fiery splendour. The gods were miserable. Saying "What will happen now?" they kept themselves at a safe distance from fear. The lotus-born deity and others were excited by the fiery fury of the man-lion. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रह्रादं प्रेषयामासुस्तच्छान्त्यै निकटं हरेः ।
सर्वान्मिलित्वा प्रह्लादः प्रार्थितो गतवांस्तदा ॥ २८॥

English
They sent Prahlāda near Viṣṇu in order to subside his anger. At the behest of all in a body Prahlāda approached the man-lion. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
उरसाऽऽलिङ्गयामास तं नृसिंहः कृपानिधिः ।
हृदयं शीतलं जातं रुड्ज्वाला न निवर्त्तिता ॥ २९॥

English
The man-lion, the store-house of mercy, embraced him. The heart became cool, still the flame of fury did not subside. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि न निवृता रुड्ज्वाला नरहरेर्यदा ।
दुःखं प्राप्तास्ततो देवाः शङ्कर शरणं ययुः ॥ ३०॥

English
When it did not subside, the afflicted and miserable gods sought refuge in Śiva. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा सुराः सर्वे ब्रह्माद्या मुनयस्तथा ।
शङ्करं स्तवयामासुर्लोकानां सुखहेतवे ॥ ३१॥

English
Going there, Brahmā, other gods and the sages eulogised Śiva for the happiness of all the worlds. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
पाहि नः शरणापन्नान्सर्वान्देवाञ्जगन्ति च ॥ ३२॥

English
The gods said :—  O great lord of gods, favourably disposed towards those who seek refuge, save us the gods who have sought refuge in you as well as all the worlds. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्तेऽस्तु सदाशिव ।
पूर्वं दुःखं यदा जातं तदा ते रक्षिता वयम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Obeisance be to you, O Sadāsiva. Whenever there had been misery formerly, it was you that saved us. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्रो मथितश्चैव रत्नानां च विभागशः ।
कृते देवैस्तदा शम्भो गृहीतं गरलं त्वया ॥ ३४॥

English
The ocean was churned and the gems were shared by the gods. Then O Śiva, poison was taken by you.³⁰ (34.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षिताः स्म तदा नाथ नीलकण्ठ इति श्रुतः ।
विषं पास्यसि नो चेत्त्वं भस्मीभूतास्तदाखिलाः ॥ ३५॥

English
We were saved by you, O lord and you became famous as Nīlakaṇṭha (Blue-necked). If you had not drunk the poison then, every thing would have been reduced to ashes. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसिद्धं च यदा यस्य दुःखं च जायते प्रभो ।
तदा त्वन्नाममात्रेण सर्वं दुःखं विलीयते ॥ ३६॥

English
This is well known, O lord, that whenever a person is in misery, by repeating your very name the misery is quelled. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं नृहरिज्वालापीडितान्नः सदाशिव ।
तां त्वं शमयितुं देव शक्तोऽसीति सुनिश्चितम् ॥ ३७॥

English
O Sadāsiva, we are now afflicted by the fiery fury of the man-lion; O lord, it is certain that you are competent to quell it. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति स्तुतस्तदा देवैः शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्माऽभयं दत्त्वा परं प्रभुः ॥ ३८॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— Thus eulogised by the gods, lord Siva, favourably disposed to the devotees, assured them of protection and spoke delightedly. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
स्वस्थानं गच्छत सुराः सर्वे ब्रह्मादयोऽभयाः ।
शमयिष्यामि यद्दुःखं सर्वथा हि व्रतं मम ॥ ३९॥

English
Siva said :—O ye Brahma and other gods, Return to your abodes fearlessly. My rite quells misery in every respect: (39.)

---

Sanskrit
गतो मच्छरणं यस्तु तस्य दुःखं क्षयं गतम् ।
मत्प्रियः शरणापन्नः प्राणेभ्योऽपि न संशयः ॥ ४०॥

English
The misery of the person who seeks refuge in me vanishes. Undoubtedly the seeker of refuge is dearer to me than life. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा तदा देवा ह्यानन्दम्परमं गताः ।
यथागतं तथा जग्मुः स्मरन्तः शङ्करं मुदा ॥ ४१॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— On hearing this, the gods were delighted much. Remembering Śiva joyously they returned. (41.)
