### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - (Prologue to Śarabha incarnation)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमभ्यर्थितो देवैर्मतिं चक्रे कृपालयः ।
महातेजो नृसिंहाख्यं संहर्त्तुं परमेश्वरः ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— Thus implored by the gods, lord Siva, the abode of mercy, decided to quell that great fiery fury of man-lion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तदूर्ध्वं स्मृतवान् रुद्रो वीरभद्रं महाबलम् ।
आत्मनो भैरवं रूपं प्राह प्रलयकारकम् ॥ २॥

English
Thereafter Rudra remembered the powerful Virabhadra, the cause of dissolution and addressed Bhairava, his own form. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
आजगाम ततः सद्यो गणानामग्रणीर्हसन् ।
साट्टहासैर्गणवरैरुत्पतद्भिरितस्ततः ॥ ३॥

नृसिंहरूपैरत्युग्रैः कोटिभिः परिवारितः ।
माद्यद्भिरभितो वीरैर्नृत्यद्भिश्च मुदान्वितैः ॥ ४॥

क्रीडद्भिश्च महावीरैर्ब्रह्माद्यैः कन्दुकैरिव ।
अदृष्टपूर्वैरन्यैश्च वेष्टितो वीरवन्दितः ॥ ५॥

English
Then suddenly there came the leader of the Ganas laughing loudly and accompanied by the leading Ganas laughing boisterously and jumping about here and there. He was surrounded by crores of fierce manlion-formed Ganas, madly dancing heroes joyously leaping about. The warlike heroes Brahma and others were playing about as if with balls; the leader of the Ganas was surrounded by others too who had remained unseen. He was respected by the heroes. (3-5.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पान्तज्वलनज्वालो विलसल्लोचनत्रयः ।
अशस्त्रो हि जटाजूटी ज्वलद् बालेन्दुमण्डितः ॥ ६॥

English
He had a fiery splendour as fierce as the fire at the end of Kalpa. His three eyes shone splendidly. He had no weapon. He had matted hair. He was bedecked by the refulgent crescent moon. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
बालेन्दुवलयाकारतीक्ष्णदंष्ट्राङ्कुरद्वयः ।
आखण्डलधनुःखण्डसन्निभभ्रूलतान्वितः ॥ ७॥

English
He had two sharp fangs curved like the crescent moon. His eyebrows were like the split pieces of Indra's bow. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
महाप्रचण्डहुङ्कारबधिरीकृतदिङ्मुखः ।
नीलमेघाञ्जनश्यामो भीषणः श्मश्रुलोऽद्भुतः ॥ ८॥

English
With his fierce hissing whooping sound be deafened the faces of the quarters. He was as dark as the blue cloud and collyrium He was terrible and wondrous with full grown moustaches and beards. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
वाद्यखण्डमखण्डाभ्यां भ्रामयंस्त्रिशिखं मुहुः ।
वीरभद्रोऽपि भगवान्वीरशक्तिविजृम्भितः ॥ ९॥

English
He was whirling his trident of unsplit lustre like a piece of musical instrument over and over again. The lord Virabhadra arrived with his heroic strength fully expanded. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयं विज्ञापयामास किमत्र स्मृतिकारणम् ।
आज्ञापय जगत्स्वामिन् प्रसादः क्रियतां मयि ॥ १०॥

English
He said to Siva—"O lord of the universe, why I have been remembered by you? Please do me a favour and command me." (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य महेशानो वीरभद्रोक्तमादरात् ।
विलोक्य वचनं प्रीत्या प्रोवाच खलदण्डधृक् ॥ ११॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— On hearing the words of Vīrabhadra, and glancing at his face respectfully lord Śiva, the chastiser of the wicked, said lovingly. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
अकाले भयमुत्पन्नं देवानामपि भैरवम् ।
ज्वलितः स नृसिंहाग्निः शमयैनं दुरासदम् ॥ १२॥

English
Śiva said:— An untimely terrible fear has cropped up for the gods. The fire of that man-lion is blazing You shall quell this inaccessible one. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सान्त्वयन् बोधयादौ तं तेन किन्नोपशाम्यति ।
ततो मत्परमं भावं भैरवं सम्प्रदर्शय ॥ १३॥

English
At the outset convince him in a consoling manner. If he does not subside thereby, show my great terrible form. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सूक्ष्मं संहृत्य सूक्ष्मेण स्थूलं स्थूलेन तेजसा ।
वशमानय वन्हिं त्वं वीरभद्र ममाज्ञया ॥ १४॥

English
O Virabhadra, at my behest, bring me his head and hide after quelling his subtle fury with your subtle one and his gross splendour with your gross one. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्यादिष्टो गणाध्यक्षो प्रशान्तं वपुरास्थितः ।
जगाम रंहसा तत्र यत्रास्ते नरकेसरी ॥ १५॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— Urged thus, the leader of the Ganas, assumed a calm body and features and hastened to the place where the man-lion was sitting. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सम्बोधयामास वीरभद्रो हरो हरिम् ।
उवाच वाक्यमीशानः पितापुत्रमिवौरसम् ॥ १६॥

English
Then Virabhadra, the destroyer addressed Visnu. Siva, then spoke to him as a father speaks to his own son. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्र उवाच ।
जगत्सुखाय भगवन्नवतीर्णोऽसि माधव ।
स्थित्यर्थं त्वं प्रयुक्तोऽसि परेशः परमेष्ठिना ॥ १७॥

English
Vīrabhadra said:—  O lord Viṣṇu, you have taken the incarnation for the happiness of the universe. You, the great lord, have been entrusted with the task of maintaining the universe by Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
जन्तुचक्रं भगवता प्रच्छन्नं मत्स्यरूपिणा ।
पुच्छेनैव समाबध्य भ्रमन्नेकार्णवे पुरा ॥ १८॥

English
Formerly, multitudes of creatures moving about in the one vast ocean have been split by your lordship in the form of a fish by binding them with your tail. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
बिभर्षि कूर्मरूपेण वाराहेणोद्धृता मही ।
अनेन हरिरूपेण हिरण्यकशिपुर्हतः ॥ १९॥

English
You support the earth in the form of a tortoise. You lifted it up in the form of a boar. In the form of a lion Hiranyakasipu has been killed. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
वामनेन बलिर्बद्धस्त्वया विक्रमता पुनः ।
त्वमेव सर्वभूतानां प्रभवः प्रभुरव्ययः ॥ २०॥

English
Again in the form of a dwarf, you had bound Bali. You are the origin of all living beings. You are the eternal lord. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा यदा हि लोकस्य दुःखं किञ्चित्प्रजायते ।
तदा तदावतीर्णस्त्वं करिष्यसि निरामयम् ॥ २१॥

English
Whenever any misery torments the world you incarnate yourself and make it free from distress and ailment. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नाधिकस्त्वत्समोऽप्यस्ति हरे शिवपरायणः ।
त्वया वेदाश्च धर्माश्च शुभमार्गे प्रतिष्ठिताः ॥ २२॥

English
O Visnu, there is none else devoted to Siva more than you or even equal to you. The Vedas and sacred rites have been established by you towards an auspicious goal. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यदर्थमवतारोऽयं निहतः स हि दानवः ।
हिरण्यकशिपुश्चैव प्रह्लादोऽपि सुरक्षितः ॥ २३॥

English
That Dānava Hiranyakasipu has been killed for which purpose you have taken this incarnation. Prahlāda too has been saved. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीव घोरं भगवन्नरसिंहवपुस्तव ।
उपसंहर विश्वात्मंस्त्वमेव मम सन्निधौ ॥ २४॥

English
O lord Narasimha, O soul of the universe, subdue this extremely terrible body yourself in my presence. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो वीरभद्रेण नृसिंहः शान्तया गिरा ।
ततोऽधिकं महाघोरं कोपं चक्रे महामदः ॥ २५॥

English
# Nandīśvara said:— Urged thus with quiet peaceful words of Virabhadra, the arrogant man-lion assumed a more terrible anger. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच च महाघोरं कठिनं वचनं तदा ।
वीरभद्रं महावीरं दंष्ट्राभिर्भीषयन्मुने ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, threatening the heroic Virabhadra with his curved fangs he spoke harsh and terrible words. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नृसिंह उवाच ।
आगतोऽसि यतस्तत्र गच्छ त्वं मा हितं वद ।
इदानीं संहरिष्यामि जगदेतच्चराचरम् ॥ २७॥

English
# The Man-lion said:— Go back the way you have come from. Do not speak offending words. I am now going to annihilate the universe of the mobile and immobile beings. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
संहर्तुर्न हि संहारः स्वतो वा परतोऽपि वा ।
शासिनं मम सर्वत्र शास्ता कोऽपि न विद्यते ॥ २८॥

English
The annihilation is not the withdrawal of myself by myself or through others. I have my sway everywhere. There is none to rule over me. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्प्रसादेन सकलमभयं हि प्रवर्तते ।
अहं हि सर्वशक्तीनां प्रवर्तकनिवर्तकः ॥ २९॥

English
Everything functions fearlessly by my favour. I am the instigator as well as the restrainer of all powers. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा ।
तत्तद्विद्धि गणाध्यक्ष मम तेजोविजृम्भितम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O leader of the Ganas, whatever is endowed with magnificence, whatever is glorious and powerful, know that to be my expanded splendour. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
देवताः परमार्थज्ञं मामेव परमं विदुः ।
मदंशाः शक्तिसम्पन्ना ब्रह्मशक्रादयः सुराः ॥ ३१॥

English
They know me, the knower of the reality of the divinity, as the greatest. Brahmā, Indra and other gods are powerful in as much as they are my own parts. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्नाभिकमलाज्जातः पुरा ब्रह्मा जगत्करः ।
सर्वाधिकः स्वतन्त्रश्च कर्ता हर्ताखिलेश्वरः ॥ ३२॥

English
Brahmā, the creator of the universe who was formerly born of my umbilical lotus³¹ is greater than all, being independent, maker, remover and the lord of all. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं तु मत्परं तेजः किं पुनः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ।
अतो मां शरणं प्राप्य गच्छ त्वं विगतज्वरः ॥ ३३॥

English
This is my greatest glory. What else do you wish to hear again? Hence seek refuge in me and return to your abodes being freed from feverish ailment. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अवेहि परमं भावमिदम्भूतं गणेश्वर ।
मामकं सकलं विश्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥ ३४॥

English
O lord of Gaṇas, know that the entire universe of this nature, is my own; this universe including the gods, Asuras and human beings is the manifestation of my greatness. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
कालोऽस्म्यहं लोकविनाशहेतु-
र्लोकान्समाहर्तुमहं प्रवृत्तः ।
मृत्योर्मृत्युं विद्धि मां वीरभद्र
जीवन्त्येते मत्प्रसादेन देवाः ॥ ३५॥

English
I am Kāla, the cause of destroying the worlds. I am engaged in annihilating the worlds. O Vīrabhadra, know me to be the Death of Death. These gods are alive by my very favour. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
साहङ्कारं वचः श्रुत्वा हरेरमितविक्रमः ।
विहस्योवाच सावज्ञं ततो विस्फुरिताधरः ॥ ३६॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing the arrogant words of Viṣṇu, Vīrabhadra of immeasurable exploits laughed and said contemptuously with throbbing lips. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्र उवाच ।
किन्न जानासि विश्वेशं संहर्तारं पिनाकिनम् ।
असद्वादो विवादश्च विनाशस्त्वयि केवलः ॥ ३७॥

English
Vīrabhadra said:—  Don't you know the Pināka-bearing lord of the universe as the annihilator? Baseless argument and disputation will end in death for you. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तवान्योन्यावताराणि कानि शेषाणि साम्प्रतम् ।
कृतानि येन केनैव कथाशेषो भविष्यति ॥ ३८॥

English
What other incarnation of yours is left still to be taken³² with that whatever it may be you will remain alive only in the story. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
दोषं तं वद येन त्वमवस्थामीदृशीं गतः ।
तेन संहारदक्षेण दक्षिणाशेषमेष्यसि ॥ ३९॥

English
Tell me the defect which has led you to this plight. With that lord capable of annihilation you will get the balance of Dakṣiṇā. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृतिस्त्वं पुमान्रुद्रस्त्वयि वीर्यं समाहितम् ।
त्वन्नाभिपङ्कजाज्जातः पञ्चवक्त्रः पितामहः ॥ ४०॥

English
You are Prakṛti and Rudra is Puruṣa. The semen is deposited in you. The five-faced Brahmā was born of your umbilical lotus. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्त्रयीसर्जनार्थं शङ्करं नीललोहितम् ।
ललाटेऽचिन्तयत्सोऽयं तपस्युग्रे च संस्थितः ॥ ४१॥

English
Remaining in a severe penance, he contemplated on Śiva, the blue-necked, in his forehead for the creation of the three worlds. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्ललाटादभूच्छम्भुः सृष्ट्यर्थे तेन भूषणम् ।
अतोऽहं देवदेवस्य तस्य भैरवरूपिणः ॥ ४२॥

English
Śiva came out of his forehead. I am the ornament of the lord of gods in the form of Bhairava in the matter of creation. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्संहारे नियुक्तोऽस्मि विनयेन बलेन च ।
देवदेवेन रुद्रेण सकलप्रभुणा हरे ॥ ४३॥

English
O Viṣṇu, I have been engaged in subduing you either through humility or through force, by Rudra the lord of gods, the lord of all. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एकं रक्षो विदार्यैव तच्छक्तिकलया युतः ।
अहङ्कारावलेपेन गर्जसि त्वमतन्द्रितः ॥ ४४॥

English
After tearing to pieces a single demon you roar by your arrogance. Actually you are free from slumber only because you are endowed with a digit of his power. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
उपकारो हि साधूनां सुखाय किल सम्मतः ।
उपकारो ह्यसाधूनामपकाराय केवलम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Help rendered to good men is accepted as conducive to happiness. Help rendered to bad men is conducive to offence. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
यन्नृसिंह महेशानं पुनर्भूतं तु मन्यसे ।
तर्ह्यज्ञानी महागर्वी विकारी सर्वथा भवान् ॥ ४६॥

English
O Nṛsiṃha, if you consider lord Śiva as a mere living being you are ignorant, very arrogant and full of aberrations in every respect. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
न त्वं स्रष्टा न संहर्ता भर्तापि न नृसिंहक ।
परतन्त्रो विमूढात्मा न स्वतन्त्रो हि कुत्रचित् ॥ ४७॥

English
O base Nṛsiṃha, you are neither creator, nor sustainer, nor annihilator. You are subservient and deluded in mind. You are not independent anywhere. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
कुलालचक्रवच्छक्त्या प्रेरितोऽसि पिनाकिना ।
नानावतारकर्ता त्वं तदधीनः सदा हरे ॥ ४८॥

English
O Viṣṇu, like the potter's wheel you are forcibly induced by Śiva when you take the different incarnations. You are always dependent on him. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि तव निक्षिप्तं कपालं कूर्मरूपिणः ।
हरहारलतामध्ये दग्धं कश्चिन्न बध्यते ॥ ४९॥

विस्मृतिः किं तदंशेन दंष्ट्रोत्पातनपीडितम् ।
वाराहविघ्नहस्तेऽद्य याक्रोशन्तारकारिणा ॥ ५०॥

English
Your skull, left by you in the midst of the necklace of Śiva, while you had assumed the form of a tortoise, is still there. None who is burnt is bound. Is it your partial forgetfulness that in your body as a Boar your fangs were felled by the slayer of Tāraka?$^{33}$ (49-50.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्धोऽसि पश्य शूलाग्नेर्विष्वक्सेनच्छलाद्भवान् ।
दक्षयज्ञे शिरश्छिन्नं मया तेजःस्वरूपिणा ॥ ५१॥

English
You were burnt by the fire of the Trident, O Viṣvaksena. At the time of Dakṣa's sacrifice your head was cut off by me brilliant in form. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि तव पुत्रस्य ब्रह्मणः पञ्चमं शिरः ।
छिन्नं न सज्जितं भूयो हरे तद्विस्मृतं त्वया ॥ ५२॥

English
O Viṣṇu, the fifth head of Brahmā your son, which was cut, has not been set right even now. It is forgotten by you. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्जितस्त्वं दधीचेन सङ्ग्रामे समरुद्गणः ।
कण्डूयमाने शिरसि कथं तद्विस्मृतं त्वया ॥ ५३॥

English
In a battle you were defeated by Dadhīca along with the gods.³⁴ How can it be forgotten by you while scratching your head? (53.)

Sanskrit
चक्रं विक्रमतो यस्य चक्रपाणे तव प्रियम् ।
कुतः प्राप्तं कृतं केन त्वया तदपि विस्मृतम् ॥ ५४॥

English
O discus-armed one, you are fond of your discus but you have forgotten whence you got it and by whom it was made. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
ये मया सकला लोका गृहीतास्त्वं पयोनिधौ ।
निद्रापरवशः शेषे स कथं सात्त्विको भवान् ॥ ५५॥

English
Whatever worlds I have seized, in them, you lie down in the ocean, overcome by sleep. How can you then be a Sāttvika? (55.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वदादिस्तम्बपर्यन्तं रुद्रशक्तिविजृम्भितम् ।
शक्तिमानभितस्त्वं च ह्यनलात्त्वं विमोहितः ॥ ५६॥

English
From you to a blade of grass, every thing expands and develops only through Rudra's power. Professing to possess power you have been deluded by the fire all round. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्तेजसो हि माहात्म्यं पुमान्द्रष्टुं न हि क्षमः ।
अस्थूला ये प्रपश्यन्ति तद्विष्णोः परमं पदम् ॥ ५७॥

English
Ordinary man is incapable of seeing the greatness of his splendour. That is the greatest of the regions of Viṣṇu which only the people of subtle intellect can perceive. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
द्यावापृथिव्या इन्द्राग्नेर्यमस्य वरुणस्य च ।
ध्वान्तोदरे शशाङ्के च जनित्वा परमेश्वरः ॥ ५८॥

English
Lord Śiva manifests himself in between heaven and earth, Indra and Agni, Yama and Varuṇa, in the heart of darkness and in the light of the moon. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
कालोऽसि त्वं महाकालः कालकालो महेश्वरः ।
अतस्त्वमुग्रकलया मृत्योर्मृत्युर्भविष्यसि ॥ ५९॥

English
You are Kāla but lord Śiva is Mahākāla and Kālakāla. Hence you will be death of death only through Śiva's digit. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थिरोऽद्य त्वक्षरो वीरो वीरो विश्वावकः प्रभुः ।
उपहन्ता ज्वरं भीमो मृगः पक्षी हिरण्मयः ॥ ६०॥

English
But the steady one today is the imperishable hero, the lord protector of the universe. He is the destroyer of fever, a dreadful beast, a golden bird. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
शास्ता शेषस्य जगतस्तत्त्वं नैव चतुर्मुखः ।
नान्ये च केवलं शम्भुः सर्वशास्ता न संशयः ॥ ६१॥

English
You and the four-faced Brahmā are not the rulers of the world. Nor any one else is the ruler. Śiva is certainly the ruler of all. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं सर्वं समालोक्य संहारात्मानमात्मना ।
न विनष्टं त्वमात्मानं कुरु हे नृहरेऽबुध ॥ ६२॥

English
Thus taking everything in consideration you subdue yourself by yourself. O Man-lion, O wise one, keep yourself safe. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
नो चेदिदानीं क्रोधस्य महाभैरवरूपिणः ।
वज्राशनिरिव स्थाणौ त्वयि मृत्युः पतिष्यति ॥ ६३॥

English
Otherwise death will befall you like a thunderbolt on a stump due to the fury of the lord in the form of Bhairava. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा वीरभद्रोऽपि विररामाकुतोभयः ।
दृष्ट्वा नृसिंहाभिप्रायं क्रोधमूर्त्तिश्शिवस्य सः ॥ ६४॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  After saying this and observing the view of the Man-lion, Vīrabhadra the furious form of Śiva, stopped. He was not afraid of anyone. (64.)
