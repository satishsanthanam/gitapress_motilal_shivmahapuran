### **CHAPTER TWELVE - (The incarnation of Śarabha)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
नन्दीश्वर महाप्राज्ञ विज्ञातं तदनन्तरम् ।
ममोपरि कृपां कृत्वा प्रीत्या त्वं तद्वदाधुना ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O Nandīśvara, O intelligent one, this is understood. Take pity on me and tell me now lovingly what happened thereafter. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो वीरभद्रेण नृसिंहः क्रोधविह्वलः ।
निनदन्ननुवेगेन तं ग्रहीतुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ २॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  Thus admonished by Vīrabhadra, the Man-lion became furious. He roared and began to seize him with force. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रान्तरे महाघोरं प्रत्यक्षभयकारणम् ।
गगनव्यापि दुर्धर्षं शैवतेजः समुद्भवम् ॥ ३॥

English
In the meantime, the splendour of Śiva rose up unbearably enveloping the sky all around. It was terrible and cause for imminent fear. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्रस्य तद्रूमदृश्यं तु ततः क्षणात् ।
तद्वै हिरण्मयं सौम्यं न सौरं नाग्निसम्भवम् ॥ ४॥

न तडिच्चन्द्रसदृशमनौपम्यं महेश्वरम् ।
तदा तेजांसि सर्वाणि तस्मिंल्लीनानि शङ्करे ॥ ५॥

English
Then in a trice the form of Vīrabhadra became invisible. The splendour of Lord Śiva was neither golden nor fiery, neither lunar nor solar. It resembled neither the lightning nor the moon. It was beyond comparison. The forms of brilliance were merged in Śiva. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
न तद्व्योमं महत्तेजो व्यक्तान्तश्चाभवत्ततः ।
रुद्रसाधारणं चैव चिह्नितं विकृताकृति ॥ ६॥

English
That great splendour was not of the sky too. Thereafter its middle became clearly manifested in the form characteristic of Rudra of deformed shape. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्संहाररूपेण सुव्यक्तं परमेश्वरः ।
पश्यतां सर्वदेवानां जयशब्दादिमङ्गलैः ॥ ७॥

English
Even as all the gods stood watching with the auspicious shouts of victory, Lord Śiva became manifest in the form the Annihilator. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रबाहुर्जटिलश्चन्द्रार्द्धकृतशेखरः ।
समृद्धोग्रशरीरेण पक्षाभ्यां चञ्चुना द्विजः ॥ ८॥

English
He had a thousand hands and wore matted hair. His head was adorned by the crescent moon. He appeared like a bird with wings and beak. His body was fierce and fully developed. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अतितीक्ष्णो महादंष्ट्रो वज्रतुल्यनखायुधः ।
कण्ठे कालो महाबाहुश्चतुष्पाद्वह्निसन्निभः ॥ ९॥

English
His fangs were very sharp. Adamantine claws were his weapons. His neck was black in colour. He had huge arms and four legs. He was blazing like fire. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
युगान्तोद्यतजीमूतभीमगम्भीरनिःस्वनः ।
महाकुपितकृत्याग्निव्यावृत्तनयनत्रयः ॥ १०॥

English
His voice was resonant and terrible like the rumbling sound of the clouds that gather at the end of a Yuga. His three eyes were as wide and blazing as the fire of the evil spirit of great fury. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स्पष्टदंष्ट्राधरोष्ठश्च हुङ्कारैः संयुतो हरः ।
ईदृग्विधस्वरूपश्च ह्युग्र आविर्बभूव ह ॥ ११॥

English
His fangs and lips were clearly visible. He was producing a hissing sound of Huṃkāra. Śiva of such a form appeared in sight. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिस्तद्दर्शनादेव विनष्टबलविक्रमः ।
बिभ्रद्धामसहस्रांशोरधः खद्योतविभ्रमम् ॥ १२॥

English
At his very vision Viṣṇu lost his strength and exploit. He assumed a lustre of the flickering glow-worm beneath the sun. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विभ्रम्य पक्षाभ्यां नाभिपादान्विदारयन् ।
पादान्बबन्ध पुच्छेन बाहुभ्यां बाहुमण्डलम् ॥ १३॥

भिन्दन्नुरसि बाहुभ्यां निजग्राह हरो हरिम् ।
ततो जगाम गगनं देवैस्सह महर्षिभिः ॥ १४॥

English
Fluttering with his wings, and tearing the navel and legs he bound the legs of the Man-lion with his tail and his hands with his own hands. He struck his chest, caught hold of Viṣṇu. It expanded in the sky along with the gods and the sages. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
सहसैवाभयाद्विष्णुं स हि श्येन इवोरगम् ।
उत्क्षिप्योत्क्षिप्य सङ्गृह्य निपात्य च निपात्य च ॥ १५॥

उड्डीयोड्डीय भगवान्पक्षघातविमोहितम् ।
हरीं हरस्तं वृषभं विवेशानन्त ईश्वरः ॥ १६॥

English
Like a vulture seizing a serpent he fearlessly caught hold of Viṣṇu, lifted him up and fell him to the ground. At the time of flight he was excessively oppressed, being hit with the wings. Then Lord Śiva took Viṣṇu and flew up. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुयान्तं सुराः सर्वे नमोवाक्येन तुष्टुवुः ।
प्रणेमुः सादरं प्रीत्या ब्रह्माद्याश्च मुनीश्वराः ॥ १७॥

English
All the gods followed him eulogising him with the words of obeisance. Brahmā and other sages bowed to him with reverence and love. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
नीयमानः परवशो दीनवक्त्रः कृताञ्जलिः ।
तुष्टाव परमेशानं हरिस्तं ललिताक्षरैः ॥ १८॥

English
Being led thus Viṣṇu was helpless. His face was pallid and sorrowful. With palms joined in reverence he eulogised Lord Śiva with prayers of simple words. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नाम्नामष्टशतेनैव स्तुत्वा तं मृडमेव च ।
पुनश्च प्रार्थयामास नृसिंहः शरभेश्वरम् ॥ १९॥

English
After eulogising Mṛḍa with the hundred and eight names of the lord, Nṛsiṁha requested lord Śarabha again. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा यदा ममाज्ञेयं मतिः स्याद्गर्वदूषिता ।
तदा तदाऽपनेतव्या त्वयैव परमेश्वर ॥ २०॥

English
"O great lord, whenever my ignorant mind becomes defiled by arrogance, it shall be removed by you alone."



21 Nandiśvara said:—  Thus saying with love to Śiva, the Man-lion turned submissive and bowed to him. Viṣṇu was utterly defeated and came to the end of his life. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वक्त्रं शेषगात्रान्तं कृत्वा सर्वस्वविग्रहम् ।
शक्तियुक्तं तदीयाङ्गं वीरभद्रः क्षणात्ततः ॥ २२॥

English
Within a trice Virabhadra made his powerful body powerless and dead. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अथ ब्रह्मादयो देवाः शारभं रूपमास्थितम् ।
तुष्टुवुः शङ्करं देवं सर्वलोकैकशङ्करम् ॥ २३॥

English
Nandiśvara said:—  Then Brahmā and other gods eulogised lord Śiva who had assumed the form of a Śarabha and who was the sole benefactor of all the worlds. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
॥ देवा ऊचुः ।
ब्रह्मविष्ण्विन्द्रचन्द्रादिसुराः सर्वे महर्षयः ।
दितिजाद्याः सम्प्रसूताः त्वत्तः सर्वे महेश्वर ॥ २४॥

English
The gods said:— O lord Śiva, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Indra, the Moon, the gods, the sages, and the Daityas are born of you. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेन्द्रांश्च सूर्याद्यानसुरान्सुरान् ।
त्वं वै सृजसि पास्यत्सि त्वमेव सकलेश्वरः ॥ २५॥

English
O lord of all, you alone create and protect Brahmā Viṣṇu and Indra, the sun and others, the gods and Asuras and you alone devour them. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यतो हरसि संसारं हर इत्युच्यते बुधैः ।
निगृहीतो हरिर्यस्माद्धर इत्युच्यते बुधैः ॥ २६॥

English
Since you annihilate the world, you are called Hara by the learned. Since Vişnu was curbed and caught hold of by you, you are called Hara by the learned. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यतो बिभर्षि सकलं विभज्य तनुमष्टधा ।
अतोऽस्मान्पाहि भगवन् सुरान् दानैरभीप्सितैः ॥ २७॥

English
Since you support and hold every thing after dividing your body into eight, O lord, protect us, the gods, by means of the desired gifts. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं महापुरुषः शम्भुः सर्वेशः सुरनायकः ।
निःस्वात्मा निर्विकारात्मा परब्रह्म सतां गतिः ॥ २८॥

English
You are the great Puruṣa, Śiva, lord of all, leader of the gods, the soul of the indigent, of a mind without aberrations. You are the great Brahman, the goal of the good. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
दीनबन्धुर्दयासिन्धुरद्भुतोतिः परात्मदृक् ।
प्राज्ञो विराट् विभुः सत्यः सच्चिदानन्दलक्षणः ॥ २९॥

English
You are the kinsman of the distressed, the ocean of mercy, of wonderful sports, the vision of the great soul, the intelligent, the Immense Cosmic Being, the all-pervasive, the truth, the Existence, knowledge and Bliss as the characteristics. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः शम्भुर्देवानां परमेश्वरः ।
उवाच तान् सुरान्देवामहर्षींश्च पुरातनान् ॥ ३०॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  On hearing these words of the gods, Siva, the great God, spoke to the gods and the great ancient sages. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Just as water poured into water, or milk poured into milk, or ghee poured into ghee becomes one with those things, so also Vişnu is merged into Šiva, not otherwise. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

जगत्संहारकरणे प्रवृत्तो नरकेसरी ॥ ३२॥

English
It was Vişnu alone in the form of the Man-lion, haughty and strong, engaged in the activity of annihilating the universe. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

मद्भक्तप्रवरश्चैव मद्भक्तवरदायकः ॥ ३३॥

English
He shall be prayed and bowed by my devotees aspiring achievements. He is the foremost of my devotees and the granter of boons. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एतावदुक्त्वा भगवान् पक्षिराजो महाबलः ।

English
Nandiśvara said :— After saying this, the lordly king of birds of great strength, vanished there itself even as all the gods were watching. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Lord Virabhadra, the leader of the Ganas, of great strength, peeled off the hide of the Man-lion and went to the mountain taking it with him. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
From that time Siva began to wear the hide of the Man-lion. His head was the leading bead in his necklace of skulls. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then the gods freed from their distress began to sing the glory of this story. With their eyes blooming with wonder they returned the way they came. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He who reads or listens to the great narrative, which being sacred has the flavour of the Vedas, will realise all his desires (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
This narrative is conducive to wealth, fame, longevity and health. It increases prosperity, subdues obstacles and quells ailments. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
This great form of Siva in the form of Sarabha shall be made public only among the leading devotees of Siva. (41.)

Sanskrit

(44-45.)
