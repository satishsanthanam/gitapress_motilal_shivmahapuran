### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - (The incarnation of Gṛhapati)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु ब्रह्मसुत प्रीत्या चरितं शशिमौलिनः ।
सोऽवतीर्णो यथा प्रीत्या विश्वानरगृहे शिवः ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O son of Brahmā, listen lovingly to the narrative of the moon-crested lord Śiva how he was born willingly in the house of Viśvānara. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नाम्ना गृहपतिः सोऽभूदग्निलोकपतिर्मुने ।
अग्निरूपस्तैजसश्च सर्वात्मा परमः प्रभुः ॥ २॥

English
He was Gṛhapati by name. O sage, he became the lord of Agniloka. The great lord, the soul of all, was fiery in form and brilliant too. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
नर्मदायास्तटे रम्ये पुरे नर्मपुरे पुरा ।
पुरारिभक्तः पुण्यात्माभवद्विश्वानरो मुनिः ॥ ३॥

English
On the beautiful banks of Narmadā in the city of Dharmapura,³⁵ formerly there lived a devotee of Śiva, a soul, the sage Viśvānara. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचर्याश्रमे निष्ठो ब्रह्मयज्ञरतः सदा ।
शाण्डिल्यगोत्रः शुचिमान्ब्रह्मतेजोनिधिर्वशी ॥ ४॥

English
While in the stage of celibacy, he was devoted to Brahmayajña rites. He belonged to the Śāṇḍilya lineage. He was pure, the storehouse of Brahminical splendour and had self-control. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञाताखिलशास्त्रार्थः सदाचाररतः सदा ।
शैवाचारप्रवीणोऽति लौकिकाचारविद्वरः ॥ ५॥

English
He understood the real meaning of sacred lord. He followed the conduct of the virtuous. He was highly skilled in Śiva ethics. He was the foremost among the knowers of worldly conventions. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्ते विचार्य गृहिणीगुणान्विश्वानरः शुभान् ।
उदुवाह विधानेन स्वोचितां कुलकन्यकाम् ॥ ६॥

English
After pondering over the good qualities of an ideal housewife, Viśvānara married with due rites the daughter of Kāla who was really worthy of him. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निशुश्रूषणरतः पञ्चयज्ञपरायणः ।
षट्कर्मनिरतो नित्यं देवपित्रतिथिप्रियः ॥ ७॥

English
He maintained the sacrificial fires. He was devoted to the performance of the five daily sacrifices.³⁶ He practised the six sacred rites every day.³⁷ He was fond of serving gods, manes and guests. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं बहुतिथे काले गते तस्याग्रजन्मनः ।
भार्या शुचिष्मती नाम भर्तारं प्राह सुव्रता ॥ ८॥

English
When a long time elapsed in that manner Śuciṣmati the wife of that Brāhmaṇa who observed all rites spoke to her husband. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नाथ भोगा मया सर्वे भुक्ता वै त्वत्प्रसादतः ।
स्त्रीणां समुचिता ये स्युः त्वां समेत्य मुदावहाः ॥ ९॥

English
"O Lord, by your favour, all pleasures which women could expect have been enjoyed by me in your company. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं मे प्रार्थितं नाथ चिराय हृदि संस्थितम् ।
गृहस्थानां समुचितं त्वमेतद्दातुमर्हसि ॥ १०॥

English
O Lord, still a desire has been lurking in my heart for a long time. It is proper to all householders. You shall grant it to me. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वानर उवाच ।
किमदेयं हि सुश्रोणि तव प्रियहितैषिणी ।
तत्प्रार्थय महाभागे प्रयच्छाम्यविलम्बितम् ॥ ११॥

English
Viśvānara said:—  O gentle lady of beautiful hips ever desirous of my welfare, what is there that cannot be granted to you? O lucky one, you may ask for it. I shall grant it to you without lelay. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
महेशितुः प्रसादेन मम किञ्चिन्न दुर्लभम् ।
इहामुत्र च कल्याणि सर्वकल्याणकारिणः ॥ १२॥

English
O good woman, by the favour of Lord Śiva, nothing is inaccessible to me either here or hereafter. Lord Śiva is the cause of all good. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचः पत्युस्तस्य सा पतिदेवता ।
उवाच हृष्टवदना करौ बद्ध्वा विनीतिका ॥ १३॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— On hearing these words of her husband she who thought her husband to be a deity was delighted. Joining her palms in reverence she humbly said. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शुचिष्मत्युवाच ।
वरयोग्यास्मि चेन्नाथ यदि देयो वरो मम ।
महेशसदृशं पुत्रं देहि नान्यं वरं वृणे ॥ १४॥

English
Śuciṣmati said:— O Lord, if I deserve a boon, if a boon is to be granted to me, please give me a son equal to Lord Śiva. I am not choosing any other boon. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति तस्या वचः श्रुत्वा ब्राह्मणः स शुचिव्रतः ।
क्षणं समाधिमाधाय हृद्येतत्समचिन्तयत् ॥ १५॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing her words that Brahmin of pure rites entered into mystic trance for a short while and thought in his heart thus.

16 "What is this? This lady has wished for an extremely inaccessible thing. It is beyond the reach of wishes. Let it be. Only He does everything. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तेनैवास्या मुखे स्थित्वा वाक्स्वरूपेण शम्भुना ।
व्याहृतं कोऽन्यथा कर्तुमुत्सहेत भवेदिदम् ॥ १७॥

English
It is by Śiva himself stationing himself in her mouth in the form of speech that this has been said. This alone is possible. Who else could have endeavoured to say so?" (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सञ्चिन्त्य स मुनिर्विश्वानर उदारधीः ।
ततः प्रोवाच तां पत्नीमेकपत्नीव्रते स्थितः ॥ १८॥

English
After thinking like this, the sage Viśvānara of liberal heart who strictly adhered to the principle of monogamy spoke to her. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्थमाश्वास्य तां पत्नीं जगाम तपसे मुनिः ।
यत्र विश्वेश्वरः साक्षात्काशीनाथोऽधितिष्ठति ॥ १९॥

English
After consoling his wife, the sage set out for penance to the city of Vārāṇa, where Lord Śiva himself is stationed. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्य वाराणसीं तूर्णं दृष्ट्वा तां मणिकर्णिकाम् ।
तत्याज तापत्रितयमपि जन्मशतार्जितम् ॥ २०॥

English
Reaching Vārāṇasi immediately and seeing Manikarnikā$^{38}$, he was freed from three distresses acquired in the course of hundreds of births. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा सर्वाणि लिङ्गानि विश्वेशप्रमुखानि च ।
स्नात्वा सर्वेषु कुण्डेषु वापीकूपसरस्सु च ॥ २१॥

English
He saw Viśveśvara and other phallic images. He performed ablutions in all holy ditches, wells, tanks and lakes. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नत्वा विनायकान्सर्वान्गौरीं शर्वां प्रणम्य च ।
सम्पूज्य कालराजं च भैरवं पापभक्षणम् ॥ २२॥

English
He bowed to all Vinayakas and Sarva Gauri. He worshipped Kalaraja Bhairava who devours sins. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दण्डनायकमुख्यांश्च गणान्स्तुत्वा प्रयत्नतः ।
आदिकेशवमुख्यांश्च केशवं परितोष्य च ॥ २३॥

English
Strenuously he eulogised Daṇḍanāyaka and other Gaṇas, Adikeśava and others. He propitiated Keśava. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
लोलार्कमुखसूर्यांश्च प्रणम्य स पुनः पुनः ।
कृत्वा च पिण्डदानानि सर्वतीर्थेष्वतन्द्रितः ॥ २४॥

English
He bowed to Lolarka and other suns again and again. Without lethargy he offered balls of rice in all the holy centres. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रभोजनाद्यैश्च मुनीन्विप्रान्प्रतर्प्य च ।
महापूजोपचारैश्च लिङ्गान्यभ्यर्च्य भक्तितः ॥ २५॥

English
He satiated sages and brahmins by means of "feeding thousand" and other rites. With devotion he propitiated the phallic images of Siva by means of great worship and service. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
असकृच्चिन्तयामास किं लिङ्गं क्षिप्रसिद्धिदम् ।
यत्र निश्चलतामेति तपस्तनयकाम्यया ॥ २६॥

English
He thought frequently—"What is that phallic image that yields the result quickly? Where can the penance due to the longing for a son find ultimate rest." (26.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षणं विचार्य स मुनिरिति विश्वानरः सुधीः ।
क्षिप्रं पुत्रप्रदं लिङ्गं वीरेशं प्रशशंस ह ॥ २७॥

English
Thinking thus for a moment, the intelligent sage Viśvānara praised the Vireśa form of Śiva which confers the blessings of a son immediately. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
असङ्ख्याताः सहस्राणि सिद्धाः सिद्धिं गतास्ततः ।
सिद्धलिङ्गमिति ख्यातं तस्माद्वीरेश्वरं परम् ॥ २८॥

English
Innumerable thousands of Siddhas have derived the fulfilment of their desire from the worship of Siva. Hence Vireśa is famous as the fruit-yielding phallic image. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरेश्वरं महालिङ्गमब्दमभ्यर्च्य भक्तितः ।
आयुर्मनोरथं सर्वं पुत्रादिकमनेकशः ॥ २९॥

English
If any one worships the Vireśvara phallic image for a year with great devotion he obtains longevity, cherished desires, sons and other things. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अहमप्यत्र वीरेशं समाराध्य त्रिकालतः ।
आशु पुत्रमवाप्स्यामि यथाभिलषितं स्त्रिया ॥ ३०॥

English
I too shall worship Vireśa here thrice a day and shall ere long acquire a son as desired by my wife. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति कृत्वा मतिं धिरो विप्रो विश्वानरः कृती ।
चन्द्रकूपजले स्नात्वा जग्राह नियमं व्रती ॥ ३१॥

English
After thinking like this, the intelligent brahmin, the contented Viśvānara took a bath in the well Candrakūpa and took the vow of the sacred rite. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाहारोऽभवन्मासं मासं नक्ताशनोऽभवत् ।
अयाचिताशनो मासं मासं त्यक्ताशनः पुनः ॥ ३२॥

पयोव्रतोऽभवन्मासं मासं शाकफलाशनः ।
मासं मुष्टितिलाहारो मासं पानीयभोजनः ॥ ३३॥

पञ्चगव्याशनो मासं मासं चान्द्रायणव्रती ।
मासं कुशाग्रजलभुग्मासं श्वसनभक्षणः ॥ ३४॥

English
For a month he lived on a single meal a day. For another month he took only the night meal. He spent another month taking only the food he got without begging. He spent another month without any food. For another month he took only milk. For another month he took only fruits and vegetables. For another month he ate only a handful of gingelly seeds. For another month he took liquid diet. For another month he took only Pañcagavya. For another month he performed the Cāndrāyaṇa rite. For another month he drank water from the tip of Kuśa grass and for another month he had air alone as his food. (32-34.)

---

Sanskrit

त्रिकालमर्चयद्भक्त्या वीरेशं लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Thus for a full year he performed the wonderful penance. He worshipped the excellent Vīreśa thrice a day. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रत्यूष एव वीरेशं यावदायाति स द्विजः ॥ ३६॥

English
On the thirteenth month while he was going to Vīreśa after taking bath in the Gaṅgā river very early in the morning, the sage saw a child of eight years smeared with ashes all over the body, in the middle of the phallic image. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit

चारुपिङ्गजटामौलिं नग्न प्रहसिताननम् ॥ ३८॥

English
The child had eyes as long as his ears. He had ruddy lips. He had matted hair of beautiful tawny colour. He was naked and had a smiling face. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He had all the dress and features proper to childhood. He seemed intelligent. He was reciting hymns from the Vedas. He was laughing sportively. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing him he was joyous. He was thrilled all over the body. He uttered the formula to you over and over again. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The contented Vaiśvānara eulogised Śiva, the great bliss in the form of child by means of eight verses that gave expression to his desire. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वानर उवाच ।
एकं ब्रह्मैवाद्वितीयं समस्तं
सत्यं सत्यं नेह नानास्ति किञ्चित् ।
एको रुद्रो न द्वितीयोऽवतस्थे

English
Viśvānara said :—  The Brahman is alone without a second. It is true. Indeed there is no multitude of different things. There is only one Rudra. There is none second to him.39 Hence I seek refuge in you, the sole great lord. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्ता हर्त्ता त्वं हि सर्वस्य शम्भो
नानारूपेष्वेकरूपोऽप्यरूपः ।
यद्वत्प्रत्यग्धर्म एकोऽप्यनेक-
स्तस्मान्नान्यं त्वां विनेशं प्रपद्ये ॥ ४३॥

English
O Śiva, you alone are the creator and annihilator of every thing. In the different forms you are of single form and formless. Just as the inner virtue is only one though it may seem to be many. Hence I do not resort to any one except you. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
रज्जौ सर्पः शुक्तिकायां च रौप्यं
नैरः पूरस्तन्मृगाख्ये मरीचौ ।
यद्यत्सद्वद्विष्वगेव प्रपञ्चो
यस्मिन् ज्ञाते तं प्रपद्ये महेशम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The snake in the rope, the silver in the oyster shell, the water in the mirage—like that when he is known, the whole world vanishes and Sat alone remains. I resort to that great lord. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तोये शैत्यं दाहकत्वं च वह्नौ
तापो भानौ शीतभानौ प्रसादः ।
पुष्पे गन्धो दुग्धमध्येऽपि सर्पि-

English
Coolness in the water, heat in the fire, the scorching nature in the sun and the pleasing gentleness in the moon, fragrance in the flower and the ghee in the milk—just, in that manner, O Śiva, you are the essense in the world. Hence I resort to you. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
शब्दं गृह्णास्यश्रवास्त्वं हि जिघ्र-
स्यघ्राणस्त्वं व्यङ्घ्रिरायासि दूरात् ।
व्यक्षः पश्येस्त्वं रसज्ञोऽप्यजिह्वः
कस्त्वां सम्यग्वेत्त्यतस्त्वां प्रपद्ये ॥ ४६॥

English
You the earless perceive sound; you the noseless perceive fragrance; you the footless come from far; you the eyeless see; you the tongueless perceive the taste. Who knows you really? Hence I seek refuge in you. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

नो वा विष्णुर्नो विधाताखिलस्य ।
नो योगीन्द्रा नेन्द्रमुख्याश्च देवा
भक्तो वेद त्वामतस्त्वां प्रपद्ये ॥ ४७॥

English
O lord, even the Vedas do not know you directly, nor Viṣṇu, nor the Creator of every thing, nor the leading Yogins, nor Indra and other gods. Only a devotee knows. Hence I seek refuge in you. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

नो वा रूपं नैव शीलं न देशः ।
इत्थम्भूतोऽपीश्वरस्त्वं त्रिलोक्याः
सर्वान्कामान्पूरयेस्त्वं भजे त्वाम् ॥ ४८॥

English
You have no spiritual lineage; you are unborn; you are immortal, formless without any prescribed conduct or native land. Still you are the lord of three worlds and you fulfil all our desires. I worship you. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
