### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - (The incarnation of Gṛhapati)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
विश्वानरः सपत्नीकः तच्छ्रुत्वा नारदेरितम् ।
तदेवं मन्यमानोऽभूद्वज्रपातं सुदारुणम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  On hearing the revelation of Nārada, Viśvānara and his wife considered it a terrible bolt from the blue. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हा हतोऽस्मीति वचसा हृदयं समताडयत् ।
मूर्च्छामवाप महतीं पुत्रशोकसमाकुलः ॥ २॥

English
He shouted "O I am doomed!" He beat his chest. Excited by the grief about his son he fell into a great swoon. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शुचिष्मत्यपि दुःखार्ता रुरोदातीव दुस्सहम् ।
अतिस्वरेण हारावैरत्यन्तं व्याकुलेन्द्रिया ॥ ३॥

English
The grief-stricken Suciṣmati too lamented much. Her senses were benumbed and she lamented aloud. (3.)

---

Sanskrit

हित्वोत्थितः किमिति किं त्विति किं किमेतत् ।
उच्चैर्वदन् गृहपतिः क्व स मे बहिस्थः
प्राणोऽन्तरात्मनिलयस्सकलेन्द्रियेशः ॥ ४॥

English
On hearing her loud lamentation, Visvanara woke up from his swoon. He cried aloud "What is this? What is this? Where is Grhapati my vital breath who stands outside and who is the lord of all the senses lodged within me. (4.)

---

Sanskrit

स्मित्वोवाच गृहपतिः सबालः शङ्करांशजः ॥ ५॥

English
On seeing his parents excessively grieved, the boy Gṛhapati, a part of Śiva, smiled and said. (5.)

---

Sanskrit

हे मातस्तात किं जातं कारणं तद्वदाधुना ।

English
Gṛhapati said :— O Mother, O Father, what is the reason? Please tell me now. Why do both of you cry? What and whence is such a fright for you? (6.)

---

Sanskrit

कालः कलयितुं शक्तो वराकी चञ्चलाल्पिका ॥ ७॥

English
Time and pitiable fickle existence cannot affect me, whose body is guarded by the dust-particles of your feet. (7.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O my parents, please listen to my vow. If I am your true son, I will do that whereby death itself will be terrified. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मृत्युञ्जयं समाराध्य सर्वज्ञं सर्वदं सताम् ।
जपिष्यामि महाकालं सत्यं तातौ वदाम्यहम् ॥ ९॥

English
I shall propitiate lord Śiva, the conqueror of death, the omniscient and the bestower of all to the good. I shall perform the Japa of Mahākāla. O parents, I am telling you the truth. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य जारितौ द्विजदम्पती ।

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing his words the aged brahmin couple were freed of their distress by the untimely showers of nectar. They then spoke. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजदम्पती ऊचतुः ।
पुनर्ब्रूहि पुनर्ब्रूहि कीदृक् कीदृक् पुनर्वद ।
कालः कलयितुं नालं वराकी चञ्चलास्ति का ॥ ११॥

English
The brahmin couple said :—  Say again. Say again. What is it? What is it? Speak again. Time and pitiable fickle existence will not affect you? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
आवयोस्तापनाशाय महोपायस्त्वयेरितः ।
मृत्युञ्जयाख्यदेवस्य समाराधनलक्षणः ॥ १२॥

English
A great means has been suggested by you to dispel our distress, that of the propitiation of lord Śiva, the conqueror of death. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The same is the result for those who seek refuge in Śiva who dispels sins and does every thing beyond the path of our desire. There is nothing better than this. (13.)

Sanskrit

English
O dear, haven't you heard that the slayer of the Tripuras protected, in days of yore, Śvetaketu who was bound by the noose of Kāla? (14.)

Sanskrit

English
When Śilāda's son was threatened by death, when he was only eight, Śiva saved him and made him his own attendant as Nandin, the delighter of the whole universe. (15.)

Sanskrit

पीत्वा हालाहलं घोरमरक्षद्भुवनत्रयम् ॥ १६॥

English
He protected the three worlds by drinking off the terrible poison as fearful as the fire at the hour of dissolution, when it was produced at the churning of the milk-ocean. (16.)

---

Sanskrit

English
With the wheel raised up by the line drawn with his beautiful toe he killed the haughty Jalandhara who had deprived the three worlds of their pleasure. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He burnt the Tripuras, who were haughty and deluded by their lordship of the three worlds, by means of the blazing fires emanating from the fall of a single arrow. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even as Brahma and others were watching, he reduced Kama to ashes by a single glance, Kama whose strength had been enhanced by his conquest of the three worlds. (19.)

Sanskrit

English
O son, seek refuge in that Siva, the amulet of protection of the universe, the sole creator of Indra, Acyuta, Brahma and others. (20.)

Sanskrit

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Thus he secured the permission of his parents. He bowed at their feet and circumambulated them. He then pacified them and started. (21.)

Sanskrit
सम्प्राप्य काशीं दुष्प्रापां ब्रह्मनारायणादिभिः ।
महासंवर्त्तसन्तापहन्त्रीं विश्वेशपालिताम् ॥ २२॥

स्वर्धुन्या हारयष्ट्येव राजितां कण्ठभूमिषु ।
विचित्रगुणशालिन्या हरपत्न्या विराजिताम् ॥ २३॥

तत्र प्राप्य स विप्रेशः प्राग्ययौ मणिकर्णिकाम् ।
तत्र स्नात्वा विधानेन दृष्ट्वा विश्वेश्वरं प्रभुम् ॥ २४॥

साञ्जलिर्नतशीर्षोऽसौ महानन्दान्वितः सुधीः ।
त्रैलोक्यप्राणसन्त्राणकारिणं प्रणनाम ह ॥ २५॥

English
He reached Kasi inaccessible even to Brahma, Visnu and others. He reached Kasi that dispels all recurring distress, that is protected by Visvea, that shines with the celestial river Gangā as with a necklace round its neck, that shines splendidly by the presence of Parvati, the consort of Siva and of wonderful qualities. After reaching Kasi he went to Manikarnika at the outset. After taking bath in accordance with the injunctions of the Sāstras he saw Lord Visveśvara$^{51}$ with palms joined and head bent in reverence. The intelligent brahmin was greatly delighted. He bowed to lord Siva who instils life into and protects the three worlds. (22-25.)

Sanskrit

English

Every day he bathed the phallic image with hundred and eight potfulls of water. Then he put a garland of blue lotus round the deity. (29-31.)

He was delighted and he evinced the thrill of hair. For a moment he stood motionless like the mountain Citrakūta.* He forgot himself like a man suddenly becoming rich. He was unable to eulogise or bow down or plead for anything. Then Śiva smiled and said. (45-49.)

Sanskrit
वरं ददामि ते भद्र त्वमग्निपदभाग्भव ।
सर्वेषामेव देवानां वरदस्त्वं भविष्यसि ॥ ५२॥

English
O gentle one, I shall grant you the boon. I confer the title of Agni on you. I authorise you to grant boons to whatever deity you think fit. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषामेव भूतानां त्वमग्नेऽन्तश्चरो भव ।
धर्मराजेन्द्रयोर्मध्ये दिगीशो राज्यमाप्नुहि ॥ ५३॥

English
O Agni, you will be moving in the midst of all mortals. Acquire kingship as the guardian of the quarter in the south-east. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयेदं स्थापितं लिङ्गं तव नाम्ना भविष्यति ।
अग्नीश्वर इति ख्यातं सर्वतेजोविबृंहणम् ॥ ५४॥

English
The phallic image installed by you will be known by your name. It will be known as Agniśvara and it will be conducive to the enhancement of all splendour. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्नीश्वरस्य भक्तानां न भयं विद्युदग्निभिः ।
अग्निमान्द्यभयं नैव नाकालमरणं क्वचित् ॥ ५५॥

English
The devotees of Agniśvara need not fear lightning and fire. They will never suffer from impaired digestion or premature death. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्नीश्वरं समभ्यर्च्य काश्यां सर्वसमृद्धिदम् ।
अन्यत्रापि मृतो दैवाद्वह्निलोके महीयते ॥ ५६॥

English
A person who worships Agniśvara, the bestower of prosperity, at Varāṇasi, is honoured in the region of fire even if, by chance, he dies elsewhere. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वानीय तद्बन्धून्पित्रोश्च परिपश्यतोः ।
दिक्पतित्वेऽभिषिच्याग्निं तत्र लिङ्गे शिवोऽविशत् ॥ ५७॥

English
Nandīśvara said :— After saying this and fetching his kinsmen Siva crowned him as the guardian of the quarter. Even as the parents watched it, Siva entered his phallic image (57.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थमग्न्यवतारस्ते वर्णितो मे जनार्दनः ।
नाम्ना गृहपतिस्तात शङ्करस्य परात्मनः ॥ ५८॥

English
Thus, O dear, the incarnation of Siva as fire has been explained to you. This incarnation of Siva, the great soul, is famous as Grhapati also. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रहोत्रपुरी रम्या सुखदार्चिष्मती वरा ।
जातवेदसि ये भक्ता ते तत्र निवसन्ति वै ॥ ५९॥

English
The city of Citrahotra is beautiful, pleasant and finely lustrous. Those who are devotees of fire stay there. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निप्रवेशं ये कुर्युर्दृढसत्त्वा जितेन्द्रियाः ।
स्त्रियो वा सत्त्वसम्पन्नास्ते सर्वेऽप्यग्नितेजसः ॥ ६०॥

English
People of steady Sattvika quality and control over their sense-organs, who enter fire, or women endowed with Sattva quality become as lustrous as fire. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निहोत्ररता विप्राः स्थापिता ब्रह्मचारिणः ।
पश्चाग्निवर्त्तिनोऽप्येवमग्निलोकेऽग्निवर्चसः ॥ ६१॥

English
Brahmins performing Agnihotra regularly, Brahma-carins maintaining sacrificial fires, and those who stay amidst five fires become as splendours as fire in the region of fire. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
शीते शीतापनुत्त्यै यस्त्वेधोभारान्प्रयच्छति ।
कुर्यादग्नीष्टिकां वाथ स वसेदग्निसन्निधौ ॥ ६२॥

English
He who makes gifts of bundles of fuel during winter for alleviating chillness or he who makes fire-bricks (of cow-dung) stays with fire. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
अनाथस्याग्निसंस्कारं यः कुर्याच्छ्रद्धयान्वितः ।
अशक्तः प्रेरयेदन्यं सोऽग्निलोके महीयते ॥ ६३॥

English
He who faithfully performs the rites of cremation for the unclaimed bodies or he who urges others to do so, himself being incapable, is honoured in the region of fire. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निरेको द्विजातीनां निःश्रेयसकरः परः ।
गुरुर्देवो व्रतं तीर्थं सर्वमग्निर्विनिश्चितम् ॥ ६४॥

English
Fire alone is the greatest means of salvation for the brahmins. He is the preceptor, the lord, the sacred rite, the holy centre. Certainly everything is fire. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
अपावनानि सर्वाणि वह्निसंसर्गतः क्षणात् ।
पावनानि भवन्त्येव तस्माद्यः पावकः स्मृतः ॥ ६५॥

English
All unholy things become pure the moment they come into contact with fire. Therefore fire is called Purifier. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तरात्मा ह्ययं साक्षान्निश्चयो ह्याशुशुक्षणिः ।
मांसग्रासान्पचेत्कुक्षौ स्त्रीणां नो मांसपेशिकाम् ॥ ६६॥

English
The fire is certainly the immanent soul of man. Even morsels of flesh it will digest in the stomach, but not the muscles of women. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
तैजसी शाम्भवी मूर्तिः प्रत्यक्षा दहनात्मिका ।
कर्त्री हर्त्री पालयित्री विनैतां किं विलोक्यते ॥ ६७॥

English
The fiery form of Śiva is in the form of the fire visible. Excepting this what else is seen as creative, protective and destructive? (67.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रभानुरयं साक्षान्नेत्रं त्रिभुवनेशितुः ।
अन्धे तमोमये लोके विनैनं कः प्रकाशनः ॥ ६८॥

English
Fire is the eye of the lord of the three worlds. In this world of pitch darkness what else than this becomes the illuminator? (68.)

---

Sanskrit
धूपप्रदीपनैवेद्यपयोदधिघृतैक्षवम् ।
एतद्भुक्तं निषेवन्ते सर्वे दिवि दिवौकसः ॥ ६९॥

English
The incense, the lights, the food offerings, milk, curd, ghee and sugarcane juice—all these go unto the dwellers of heaven when offered into the fire here. (69.)
