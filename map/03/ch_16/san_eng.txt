### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - (Śiva's Incarnation as Yakṣeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
यक्षेश्वरावतारं च श‍ृणु शम्भोर्मुनीश्वर ।
गर्विणं गर्वहन्तारं सतां भक्तिविवर्द्धनम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandṭīvara said:—  O great sage, listen to the Yakṣeśvara incarnation of Śiva, that dispels the arrogance of the arrogant and lets the devotion of the good flourish. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा देवाश्च दैत्याश्च पीयूषार्थं महाबलाः ।
क्षीरोदधिं ममन्थुस्ते सुकृतस्वार्थसन्धयः ॥ २॥

English
Formerly, the powerful gods and the Daityas motivated by the desire to gain their selfish ends churned the milk ocean for achieving nectar. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
मथ्यमानेऽमृते पूर्वं क्षीराब्धेः सुरदानवैः ।
अग्नेः समुत्थितं तस्माद्विषं कालानलप्रभम् ॥ ३॥

English
When the churning of the milk ocean started as the gods and demons wanted nectar, poison blazing as the fire of dissolution came out at first. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा निखिला देवा दैत्याश्च भयविह्वलाः ।
विद्रुत्य तरसा तात शम्भोस्ते शरणं ययुः ॥ ४॥

English
O dear, at the sight of it, the gods and the daityas were highly agitated and frightened. They fled from there immediately and sought refuge in Siva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तं शङ्करं सर्वे सर्वदेवशिखामणिम् ।
प्रणम्य तुष्टुवुर्भक्त्या साच्युता नतमस्तकाः ॥ ५॥

English
After seeing Siva, the crestjewel of all the gods, all of them including Visnu devoutly bent their heads and bowed to Visnu with devotion. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नो भगवान् शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
पपौ विषं महाघोरं सुरासुरगणार्दनम् ॥ ६॥

English
Then the delighted lord Siva favourably disposed to the devotees, quaffed off the terrible poison afflicting gods and Asuras. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिं तं विषमं कण्ठे निदधे विषमुल्बणम् ।
रेजे तेनाति स विभुर्नीलकण्ठो बभूव ह ॥ ७॥

English
The lord retained obliquely the acute poison that he drank, in his neck. He shone much thereby and became Blue-necked. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सुरासुरगणा ममन्थुः पुनरेव तम् ।
विषदाहविनिर्मुक्ताः शिवानुग्रहतोऽखिलाः ॥ ८॥

English
Released from the burning sensation of the poison thanks to the blessings of Siva, the gods and the Asuras began to churn again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो बहूनि रत्नानि निस्सृतानि ततो मुने ।
अमृतं च पदार्थं हि सुरदानवयोर्मुने ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, many gems came out of it, but O sage, it was the nectar that the gods and the Dānavas prized much. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तं पपुः केवलं देवा नासुराः कृपया हरेः ।
ततो बभूव सुमहद्रत्नं तेषां मिथोऽकदम् ॥ १०॥

English
Not the Asuras, but the gods drank the nectar, thanks to the favour of Visnu. Then a great battle ensued between them inflicting pain on both.[53] (10.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वन्द्वयुद्धं बभूवाथ देवदानवयोर्मुने ।
तत्र राहुभयाच्चन्द्रो विदुद्राव तदर्दितः ॥ ११॥

English
A mutual clash occurred between the gods and the Dānavas, O sage. The moon was eclipsed by Rāhu and fled in terror of Rāhu. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
जगाम सदनं शम्भोः शरण भयविह्वलः ।
सुप्रणम्य च तुष्टाव पाहि पाहीति संवदन् ॥ १२॥

English
He went to the abode of Siva extremely agitated by fear and sought Siva's protection. Saying "Protect me O Lord, protect me" he bowed to the lord and eulogised him. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सतामभयदः शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
दध्रे शिरसि चन्द्रं स विभुः शरणमागतम् ॥ १३॥

English
Lord Siva, the bestower of protection to the good, and favourably disposed to his devotees, wore the moon on his head as the latter had sought refuge. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथागतस्तदा राहुस्तुष्टाव सुप्रणम्य तम् ।
शङ्करं सकलाधीशं वाग्भिरिष्टाभिरादरात् ॥ १४॥

English
Then Rahu too, came there. He bowed to and eulogised lord Siva with reverence by means of sweet words. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुस्तन्मतमाज्ञाय तच्छिरांस्यच्युतेन ह ।
पुरा छिन्नानि वै केतुसंज्ञानि निदधे गले ॥ १५॥

English
Realising his desire, Siva fixed on to his neck his head that had been formerly cut off by Visnu, and that was known as Ketu. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो युद्धेऽसुराः सर्वे देवैश्चैव पराजिताः ।
पीत्वामृतं सुराः सर्वे जय प्रापुर्महाबलाः ॥ १६॥

English
Then the Asuras were defeated by the gods in the battle. By drinking nectar the gods had become powerful and gained victory. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुप्रभृतयः सर्वे बभूवुश्चातिगर्विताः ।
बलानि चाङ्कुरन्तोऽन्तः शिवमायाविमोहिताः ॥ १७॥

English
Deluded by Śiva's Māyā, Viṣṇu and others became very haughty. Their strength began to take root and sprout within them. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स शङ्करो देवः सर्वाधीशोऽथ गर्वहा ।
यक्षो भूत्वा जगामाशु यत्र देवाः स्थिता मुने ॥ १८॥

English
O sage, then Lord Śiva, the suppressor of arrogance, became a Yakṣa. He hastened to the place, O sage, where the gods were stationed. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वान् दृष्ट्वाच्युतमुखान्देवान्यक्षपतिः स वै ।
महागर्वाढ्यमनसा महेशः प्राह गर्वहा ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing Viṣṇu and all other gods arrogant in their minds, lord Śiva, the lord of Yakṣas and the suppressor of arrogance spoke. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यक्षेश्वर उवाच ।
किमर्थं संस्थिता यूयमत्र सर्वे सुरा मिथः ।
किमु काष्ठाखिलं ब्रुत कारणं मेऽनु पृच्छते ॥ २०॥

English
Yakṣeśvara said:—  O ye gods, why have you all assembled here. What is your goal? Tell me the reason as I ask unto you. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
अभूदत्र महान्देव रणः परमदारुणः ।
असुरा नाशिताः सर्वेऽवशिष्टा विद्रुता गताः ॥ २१॥

English
The gods said:—  O lord, a terrible battle took place here wherein all the Asuras were killed. The few that remained fled away. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
वयं सर्वे महावीरा दैत्यघ्ना बलवत्तराः ।
अग्रेऽस्माकं कियन्तस्ते दैत्याः क्षुद्रबलाः सदा ॥ २२॥

English
We are great heroes and powerful slayers of the Daityas. How insignificant in strength are those Daityas in front of us! (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तेषां सुराणां गर्वगर्भितम् ।
गर्वहासौ महादेवो यक्षरूपो वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २३॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing the arrogant words of those gods, lord Śiva, the suppressor of arrogance, in the form of Yakṣa said. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यक्षेश्वर उवाच ।
हे सुरा निखिला यूयं मद्वचः श‍ृणुतादरात् ।
यथार्थं वच्मि नासत्यं सर्वगर्वापहारकम् ॥ २४॥

English
Yakṣeśvara said:—  O ye gods, you listen to my words with attention. I shall tell you the truth that will dispel arrogance. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्वमेनं न कुरुत कर्ता हर्तापरः प्रभुः ।
विस्मृताश्च महेशानं कथयध्वं वृथा बलाः ॥ २५॥

English
Do not be arrogant. There is another lord who is the creator and annihilator. You have forgotten the great lord. You boast too much but you are devoid of strength. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
युष्माकं चेत्स हि मदो जानतां स्वबलं महत् ।
मत्स्थापितं तृणमिदं छिन्त स्वास्त्रैश्च तैः सुराः ॥ २६॥

English
If you are proud and think that you know your strength to be great, O gods, split this blade of grass placed by me, with your own weapons. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वैकं तृणं तेषां निचिक्षेप पुरस्ततः ।
जह्रे सर्वमदं यक्षरूप ईशः सतां गतिः ॥ २७॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  Saying this, Śiva in the form of Yakṣa, the goal of the good, placed a blade of grass before them and dispelled their arrogance. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सर्वे सुरा विष्णुप्रमुखा वीरमानिनः ।
कृत्वा स्वपौरुषं तत्र स्वायुधानि विचिक्षिपुः ॥ २८॥

English
Then Viṣṇu and other gods professing to be heroic exerted their manliness and hurled their respective weapons. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रासन् विफलान्याशु तान्यस्त्राणि दिवौकसाम् ।
शिवप्रभावतस्तेषां मूढगर्वापहारिणः ॥ २९॥

English
But the weapons of the gods, were rendered fruitless before the power of Śiva, the destroyer of the arrogance of the deluded. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथासीत्तु नभोवाणी देवविस्मयहारिणी ।
यक्षोऽयं शङ्करो देवाः सर्वगर्वापहारकः ॥ ३०॥

English
Then a celestial voice arose that dispelled the surprise of the gods—"O gods this Yakṣa is Śiva who removes the arrogance of the arrogant. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्ता हर्ता तथा भर्तायमेव परमेश्वरः ।
एतद्बलेन बलिनो जीवाः सर्वेऽन्यथा न हि ॥ ३१॥

English
The lord himself is the creator, sustainer and annihilator. The living beings are powerful thanks only to his power. Otherwise they are not powerful. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य मायाप्रभावाद् वै मोहिताः स्वप्रभुं शिवम् ।
मदतो बुबुधुर्नैवाद्यापि बोधतनुं प्रभुम् ॥ ३२॥

English
Deluded by the power of his illusion you have become arrogant and have not realised lord Śiva, who is the very knowledge embodied. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा नभोवाणीं बुबुधुस्ते गतस्मयाः ।
यक्षेश्वरं प्रणेमुश्च तुष्टुवुश्च तमीश्वरम् ॥ ३३॥

English
O hearing the celestial voice they became divested of their arrogance. They had realised Yakṣeśvara. They bowed to and eulogised him. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव सर्वगर्वापहारक ।
यक्षेश्वर महालील माया तेऽत्यद्भुता प्रभो ॥ ३४॥

English
The gods said:—  O lord of the gods, the dispeller of arrogance, O Yakṣeśvara of great sports, O lord, your Māyā is wonderful. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
मोहिता माययाद्यापि तव यक्षस्वरूपिणः ।
सगर्वमभिभाषन्तस्त्वत्पुरो हि पृथङ्मयाः ॥ ३५॥

English
Even now we are deluded by this Māyā of yours in the form of Yakṣa. Hence, separated from you we began to speak arrogantly before you. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं ज्ञानमायातं तवैव कृपया प्रभो ।
कर्ता हर्ता च भर्ता च त्वमेवान्यो न शङ्कर ॥ ३६॥

English
O lord, thanks to your grace, alone, knowledge has dawned on us. O Śiva, you alone are the creator, sustainer and annihilator, none else. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव सर्वशक्तीनां सर्वेषां हि प्रवर्तकः ।
निवर्तकश्च सर्वेशः परमात्माव्ययोऽद्वयः ॥ ३७॥

English
You alone are the initiator and the withdrawer of all powers. You are lord of all, the great soul, the unchanging, the unrivalled. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यक्षेश्वरस्वरूपेण सर्वेषां नो मदो हृतः ।
कृतो मन्यामहे तत्तेऽनुग्रहो हि कृपालुना ॥ ३८॥

English
In the form of Yakṣeśvara you have removed our arrogance. With sympathy and kindness we think we have been blessed by you. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो स यक्षनाथोऽनुगृह्य वै सकलान् सुरान् ।
विबोध्य विविधैर्वाक्यैस्तत्रैवान्तरधीयत ॥ ३९॥

English
Then the Yakṣanātha blessed the gods enlightened them with various instructions and vanished there itself. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं स वर्णितः शम्भोरवतारः सुखावहः ।
यक्षेश्वराख्यः सुखदः सतां तुष्टोऽभयङ्करः ॥ ४०॥

English
Thus has been described the pleasant incarnation of Śiva called Yakṣeśvara that bestows pleasure, satisfaction and protection of the good. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानममलं सर्वगर्वापहारकम् ।
सतां सुशान्तिदं नित्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं नृणाम् ॥ ४१॥

English
The narrative is sacred; it dispels all arrogance; it bestows peace, worldly pleasure and salvation upon all good men. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या श्रावयेद्वा सुधीः पुमान् ।
सर्वकामानवाप्नोति ततश्च लभते गतिम् ॥ ४२॥

English
The intelligent man who listens to or narrates this with devotion attains all desires. Then he attains salvation. (42.)
