### **CHAPTER SEVENTEEN - (The incarnations of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वथो गिरिशस्याद्यावतारान् दशसङ्ख्यकान् ।
महाकालमुखान् भक्त्योपासनाकाण्डसेवितान् ॥ १॥

English
Nandikeśvara said:—  Now listen to the first ten incarnations of Śiva beginning with that of Mahākāla. They are resorted to with devotion and explained in Upāsanākāṇḍa. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राद्यो हि महाकालो भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदः सताम् ।
शक्तिस्तत्र महाकाली भक्तेप्सितफलप्रदा ॥ २॥

English
The first incarnation is that of Mahākāla, the bestower of worldly pleasures and liberation to the good. Śakti there, is Mahākāli⁵⁴ who bestows the desired fruits on the devotees. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तारनामा द्वितीयश्च ताराशक्तिस्तथैव सा ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदौ चोभौ स्वसेवकसुखप्रदौ ॥ ३॥

English
The second incarnation is called Tāra. Śakti there, is Tārā. Both of them confer worldly pleasures and salvation on their attendants. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भुवनेशो हि बालाह्वस्तृतीयः परिकीर्तितः ।
भुवनेशी शिवा तत्र बालाह्वा सुखदा सताम् ॥ ४॥

English
The lord of worlds called Bāla is glorified as the third incarnation. Śakti there, is Śivā that bestows pleasure on the good. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीविद्येशः षोडशाह्वः श्रीर्विद्या षोडशी शिवा ।
चतुर्थो भक्तसुखदो भुक्तिमुक्तिफलप्रदः ॥ ५॥

English
Śrīvidyeśa Śoḍaśa is the fourth incarnation and Śrīvidyā, Śivā Śoḍaśī is the Śakti. They confer pleasure on the devotees and yield worldly pleasures as well as salvation. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चमो भैरवः ख्यातः सर्वदा भक्तकामदः ।
भैरवी गिरिजा तत्र सदुपासककामदा ॥ ६॥

English
The fifth incarnation is famous as Bhairava, who bestows desires on the devotees. Śakti there is Bhairavī who bestows desires on good worshippers. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
छिन्नमस्तकनामासौ शिवः षष्ठः प्रकीर्तितः ।
भक्तकामप्रदा चैव गिरिजा छिन्नमस्तका ॥ ७॥

English
The sixth incarnation of Śiva is glorified as Chinnamastaka who confers desires. Śakti there, is Dhūmāvatī who bestows all desires on the devotees. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
धूमवान् सप्तमः शम्भुः सर्वकामफलप्रदः ।
धूमवती शिवा तत्र सदुपासककामदा ॥ ८॥

English
The seventh incarnation of Siva is Dhūmavan, the bestower of the fruits of all desires. Saktit here, is Dhūmavatī who bestows all desires on the good worshippers. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवावतारः सुखदो ह्यष्टमो बगलामुखः ।
शक्तिस्तत्र महानन्दा विख्याता बगलामुखी ॥ ९॥

English
The eighth pleasant incarnation of Siva is Bagalamukha. Sakti there, is Bagalamukhi who is also famous as Mahānanda. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवावतारो मातङ्गो नवमः परिकीर्तितः ।
मातङ्गी तत्र शर्वाणी सर्वकामफलप्रदा ॥ १०॥

English
The ninth incarnation of Siva is glorified as Mataṅga. Sakti there, is Mataṅgi who bestows the fruits of all desires. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दशमः कमलः शम्भुर्भुक्तिमुक्तिफलप्रदः ।
कमला गिरिजा तत्र स्वभक्तपरिपालिनी ॥ ११॥

English
The tenth incarnation of Siva is Kamala, the bestower of fruits of worldly pleasures and salvation. Sakti there, is Kamala, the protectress of her own devotees. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एते दशमिताः शैवा अवताराः सुखप्रदाः ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदाश्चैव भक्तानां सर्वदा सताम् ॥ १२॥

English
The ten incarnations of Siva are pleasing as they confer worldly pleasures and salvation. They bestow everything on good devotees. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एते दशावतारा हि शङ्करस्य महात्मनः ।
नानासुखप्रदा नित्यं सेवतां निर्विकारतः ॥ १३॥

English
These ten incarnations of Siva the great soul bestow different kinds of pleasures on those who always resort to them without aberrations. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद्दशावताराणां माहात्म्यं वर्णितं मुने ।
सर्वकामप्रदं ज्ञेयं तन्त्रशास्त्रादिगर्भितम् ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, the glory of the ten incarnations of Siva has thus been described. It bestows all desires as explained in the Tantraśāstra and other treatises. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एतासामादिशक्तीनामद्भुतो महिमा मुने ।
सर्वकामप्रदो ज्ञेयस्तत्रंशास्त्रादिगर्भितः ॥ १५॥

English
O sage, the greatness of these Saktis too is wonderful. It bestows all desires and shall be known as explained in Tantraśāstra and other treatises. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शत्रुमारणकार्यादौ तत्तच्छक्तिः परा मता ।
खलदण्डकरी नित्यं ब्रह्मतेजोविवर्द्धिनी ॥ १६॥

English
The different Saktis are considered in such activities as destroying enemies etc. They punish the wicked and enhance brahminical splendour. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तास्ते मया ब्रह्मन्नवतारा महेशितुः ।
सशक्तिका दशमिता महाकालमुखाः शुभाः ॥ १७॥

English
O Brahmin, thus I have explained to you the ten incarnations of Siva along with his Sakti, beginning with Mahakala. They are all auspicious. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवपर्वेसु सर्वेषु योऽधीते भक्तितत्परः ।
एतदाख्यानममलं सोऽतिशम्भुप्रियो भवेत् ॥ १८॥

English
He who reads this sacred narrative during the festivals of Siva, with great devotion becomes a great favourite of Siva. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणो ब्रह्मवर्चस्वी क्षत्रियो विजयी भवेत् ।
धनाधिपो हि वैश्यः स्याच्छूद्रः सुखमवाप्नुयात् ॥ १९॥

English
A brahmin attains the sacred splendour of Brahman; Kṣatriya becomes victorious; a Vaiśya becomes lord of wealth and a Sūdra attains happiness. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शाङ्करा निजधर्मस्थाः श‍ृण्वन्तश्चरितं त्विदम् ।
सुखिनः स्युर्विशेषेण शिवभक्ता भवन्तु च ॥ २०॥

English
The devotees of Siva maintaining their respective duties and listening to this narrative become especially happy and devoted to Siva. (20.)
