### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - (*Śiva's Eleven Incarnations*)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एकादशवतारान्वै श‍ृण्वथो शाङ्करान्वरान् ।
यान् श्रुत्वा न हि बाध्येत बाधासत्यादिसम्भवा ॥ १॥

English
*Śiva said* :—  Listen to the eleven excellent incarnations of Śiva on hearing which obstacles due to intimate contact with worldly objects etc. do not torment. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा सर्वे सुराः शक्रमुखा दैत्यपराजिताः ।
त्यक्त्वामरावतीं भीत्यापलायन्त निजां पुरीम् ॥ २॥

English
Formerly Indra and other gods defeated by the Asuras left their capital Amarāvatī⁵⁵ due to fear. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यप्रपीडिता देवा जग्मुस्ते कश्यपान्तिकम् ।
बध्वा करान्नतस्कन्धाः प्रणेमुस्तं सुविह्वलम् ॥ ३॥

English
The gods harrassed by the Daityas went to Kaśyapa. Highly agitated, they bowed down to him with palms joined and shoulders drooping. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सुनुत्वा तं सुराः सर्वे कृत्वा विज्ञप्तिमादरात् ।
सर्वं निवेदयामासुः स्वदुःखं तत्पराजयम् ॥ ४॥

English
After eulogising well, the gods made the submission respectfully. They intimated to him their misery due to that defeat. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स कश्यपस्तात तत्पिता शिवसक्तधीः ।
तदाकर्ण्यामराकं वै दुःखितोऽभून्न चाधिकम् ॥ ५॥

English
O dear, on hearing the misery of the gods their father Kaśyapa whose mind was attached to Śiva, was much distressed. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तानाश्वास्य मुनिः सोऽथ धैर्यमाधाय शान्तधीः ।
काशीं जगाम सुप्रीत्या विश्वेश्वरपुरीं मुने ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, the sage consoled and encouraged them. With his mind regaining calmness he joyously went to Kāśī presided over by lord Viśveśvara. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गाम्भसि ततः स्नात्वा कृत्वा तं विधिमादरात् ।
विश्वेश्वरं समानर्च साम्बं सर्वेश्वरं प्रभुम् ॥ ७॥

English
Taking his ceremonial bath in the waters of the Gaṅgā and performing due rites, he worshipped Lord Viśveśvara, lord of the universe, accompanied by Pārvatī. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवलिङ्गं सुसंस्थाप्य चकार विपुलं तपः ।
शम्भुमुद्दिश्य सुप्रीत्या देवानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ८॥

English
With the desire for the benefit of the gods he joyously installed Śivaliṅga and performed an elaborate penance with Śiva as the object of pursuit. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
महान्कालो व्यतीयाय तपतस्तस्य वै मुने ।
शिवपादाम्बुजासक्तमनसो धैर्यशालिनः ॥ ९॥

English
A long time elapsed, O sage, as he boldly performed penance with his mind attached to the lotus-like feet of Śiva (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रादुरभूच्छम्भुर्वरं दातुं तदर्षये ।
स्वपदासक्तमनसे दीनबन्धुः सतां गतिः ॥ १०॥

English
In order to grant the boon to that sage whose mind was devoted to his feet, Śiva, kinsman of the distressed and goal of the good, appeared in front of him. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं ब्रूहीति चोवाच सुप्रसन्नो महेश्वरः ।
कश्यपं मुनिशार्दूलं स्वभक्तं भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ११॥

English
The delighted Śiva who is favourably disposed towards his devotees said to Kaśyapa, the best of his devotees, to ask for the boon. (11.)

---

English
It is the capital of Indra's heaven, famous for its greatness and splendour. It is called Devapura, 'city of gods' and Pūṣābhāsā, 'sun-splendour' and by various other names. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वाथ तं महेशानं स प्रणम्य कृताञ्जलिः ।
तुष्टाव कश्यपो हृष्टो देवतातः प्रसन्नधीः ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing lord Śiva, the delighted Kaśyapa bowed to him with palms joined in reverence. Kaśyapa, the progenitor of the gods, eulogised him with contentment in his mind. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्यप उवाच ।
देवदेव महेशान शरणागतवत्सल ।
सर्वेश्वः परमात्मा त्वं ध्यानगम्योऽद्वयोऽव्ययः ॥ १३॥

English
# Kaśyapa said:- O lord Siva, lord of the gods, favourably disposed towards those who seek refuge in you, you are the lord of all, the great soul, achievable only through meditation, the unequalled and unchanging. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
बलनिग्रहकर्ता त्वं महेश्वर सतां गतिः ।
दीनबन्धुर्दयासिन्धुर्भक्तरक्षणदक्षधीः ॥ १४॥

English
O lord Siva, you are the restrainer of evil forces. You are the goal of the good, kinsman of the distressed, ocean of mercy and intellectually efficient in protecting your devotees. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एते सुरास्त्वदीया हि त्वद्भक्ताश्च विशेषतः ।
दैत्यैः पराजिताश्चाथ पाहि तान्दुःखितान् प्रभो ॥ १५॥

English
O lord, these gods are your own. They are especially devoted to you. Now they are defeated by the Asuras. Save them from distress. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
असमर्थो रमेशोऽपि दुःखदस्ते मुहुर्मुहुः ।
अतः सुरा मच्छरणा वेदयन्तोऽसुखं च तत् ॥ १६॥

English
Even Visnu cannot move in this matter. He has frequently pressed you for this job. The gods have sought refuge in me intimating their misfortune. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तदर्थं देवदेवेश देवदुःखविनाशक ।
तत्पूरितुं तपोनिष्ठां प्रसन्नार्थं तवासदम् ॥ १७॥

English
O lord of the chiefs of the gods, O destroyer of the miseries of the gods, it is for this that I have performed this penance and resorted to you. My purpose has now been fulfilled by you since you are pleased. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शरणं ते प्रपन्नोऽस्मि सर्वथाहं महेश्वर ।
कामं मे पूरय स्वामिन्देवदुःखं विनाशय ॥ १८॥

English
O lord, I have sought refuge in you by all means O lord, please fulfill my desire. Please dispel the misery of the gods. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रदुःखैश्च देवेश दुःखितोऽहं विशेषतः ।
सुखिनं कुरु मामीश सहायस्त्वं दिवौकसाम् ॥ १९॥

English
O lord of gods, I too am much distressed at the misfortune of my sons. O lord, make me happy. You are the source of succour to the heaven-dwellers.
20 O lord Siva, the gods and the Yaksas, born as my sons, have been defeated by the powerful Asuras. Be the bestower of bliss on the gods. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सदैवास्तु महेशान सर्वलेखसहायकः ।
यथा दैत्यकृता बाधा न बाधेत सुरान्प्रभो ॥ २१॥

English
O lord Siva, do help the gods always lest the harassment by the Asuras should afflict them. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्य तु सर्वेशस्तथेति प्रोच्य शङ्करः ।
पश्यतस्तस्य भगवांस्तत्रैवान्तर्दधे हरः ॥ २२॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On being thus requested, lord, Siva said "So be it" and even as he was watching he vanished there. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्यपोऽपि महाहृष्टः स्वस्थानमगमद् द्रुतम् ।
देवेभ्यः कथयामास सर्ववृत्तान्तमादरात् ॥ २३॥

English
Kasyapa too was delighted. Immediately he returned to his abode. He eagerly intimated to the gods all the details. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स शङ्करः शर्वः सत्यं कर्तुं स्वकं वचः ।
सुरभ्यां कश्यपाज्जज्ञे एकादशस्वरूपवान् ॥ २४॥

English
Then, in order to keep his promise Siva was born of Surabhi assuming eleven forms. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
महोत्सवस्तदासीद् वै सर्वं शिवमयं त्वभूत् ।
आसन्हृष्टाः सुराश्चाथ मुनिना कश्यपेन च ॥ २५॥

English
There was great jubilation then. Everything became auspicious. The gods and the sage Kasyapa became delighted. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
कपाली पिङ्गलो भीमो विरूपाक्षो विलोहितः ।
शास्ताजपादहिर्बुध्न्यश्शम्भुश्चण्डो भवस्तथा ॥ २६॥

एकादशैते रुद्रास्तु सुरभीतनयाः स्मृताः ।
देवकार्यार्थमुत्पन्नाः शिवरूपाः सुखास्पदम् ॥ २७॥

English
These are the eleven Rudras born of Surabhi (1) Kapalin (2) Pingala (3) Bhima (4) Virupaksa (5) Vlohita (6) Sastr (7) Ajapad (8) Ahirbudhnya (9) Siva (10) Canada and (11) Bhava. They were born for the aid to the gods. They are identical with Siva and the cause of happiness. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
ते रुद्राः काश्यपा वीरा महाबलपराक्रमाः ।
दैत्यान् जघ्नुश्च सङ्ग्रामे देवसाहाय्यकारिणः ॥ २८॥

English
Those Rudras, born of Kasyapa, rendering help to the gods, slew the Daityas in the battle. They were heroes of great strength and exploit. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रुद्रकृपया देवा दैत्यान् जित्वा च निर्भयाः ।
चक्रुः स्वराज्यं सर्वे ते शक्राद्याः स्वस्थमानसाः ॥ २९॥

English
By the grace of those Rudras, the gods conquered the Asuras and were rid of fear. Indra and other gods had peace of mind and ruled over their own kingdom.
30 Even today the great Rudras, identical with Siva, shine always in the sky for the protection of the gods. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ऐशान्यां पुरि ते वासं चक्रिरे भक्तवत्सलाः ।
विरमन्ते सदा तत्र नानालीलाविशारदाः ॥ ३१॥

English
Those Rudras favourably disposed towards their devotees took up their residence in the north-eastern city of Isana. Experts in different kinds of sports they sport about there always. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषामनुचरा रुद्राः कोटिशः परिकीर्तिताः ।
सर्वत्र संस्थितास्तत्र त्रिलोकेष्वभिभागशः ॥ ३२॥

English
The Rudras glorified as their followers and retainers are crores in number. They are stationed everywhere in the three worlds, undividedly. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते वर्णितास्तातावताराः शङ्करस्य वै ।
एकादशमिता रुद्राः सर्वलोकसुखावहाः ॥ ३३॥

English
O dear, thus the eleven Rudra incarnations of Siva, pleasing to all the worlds have been described to you. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानममलं सर्वपापप्रणाशकम् ।
धन्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं सर्वकामप्रदायकम् ॥ ३४॥

English
This narrative is pure, it destroys all sins. It is conducive to wealth, fame and longevity. It yields all desires. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयात्तात श्रावयेद्वै समाहितः ।
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा ततो मुक्तिं लभेत सः ॥ ३५॥

English
He who listens to or reads this with pious heart enjoys all pleasures here and attains liberation hereafter. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
भूत्वा मम सुतो नाथ देवा यक्षाः पराजिताः ।
दैत्यैर्महाबलैः शम्भो सुरानन्दप्रदो भव ॥ २०॥

---

Sanskrit
अद्यापि ते महारुद्राः सर्वे शिवस्वरूपकाः ।
देवानां रक्षणार्थाय विराजन्ते सदा दिवि ॥ ३०॥
