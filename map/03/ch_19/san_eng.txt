### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - # (The Narrative of Durvāsas) - Nandīśvara said :—**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अथान्यच्चरितं शम्भोः श‍ृणु प्रीत्या महामुने ।
यथा बभूव दुर्वासाः शङ्करो धर्महेतवे ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O great sage, now listen lovingly to another story of Śiva, how Śiva was born as Durvāsas for the maintenance of virtue. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मपुत्रो बभूवातितपस्वी ब्रह्मवित्प्रभुः ।
अनसूयापतिर्धीमान्ब्रह्माज्ञाप्रतिपालकः ॥ २॥

English
Atri, son of Brahma, was a great sage. He was holy and a knower of Brahman. He was the husband of Anasūyā. He was intelligent and he strictly followed the orders of Brahma. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सुनिर्देशाद् ब्रह्मणो हि सस्त्रीकः पुत्रकाम्यया ।
स त्र्यक्षकुलनामानं ययौ च तपसे गिरिम् ॥ ३॥

English
At the direction of Brahma he went to the mountain Rkṣakula57 accompanied by his wife in order to perform penance there, with the desire to get son. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राणानायम्य विधिवन्निर्विन्ध्यातटिनीतटे ।
तपश्चचार सुमहद् अद्वन्द्वोऽब्दशतं मुनिः ॥ ४॥

English
On the banks of the river Nirvindhyā,58 the sage who had controlled his breath and had freed himself from mutually clashing emotions performed a great penance for a hundred years. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
य एक ईश्वरः कश्चिदविकारी महाप्रभुः ।
स मे पुत्रवरं दद्यादिति निश्चितमानसः ॥ ५॥

English
He decided in his mind thus, "let that sole great lord, whoever he is, who is free from aberrations, grant me the boon of a son." (5.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुकालो व्यतीयाय तस्मिंस्तपति सत्तपः ।
आविर्बभूव तस्मात्तु शुचिर्ज्वाला महीयसी ॥ ६॥

English
A long time elapsed while he performed the holy penance. A holy flame of fire came out of him. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तयासन्निखिला लोका दग्धप्राया मुनीश्वराः ।
तथा सुरर्षयः सर्वे पीडिता वासवादयः ॥ ७॥

English
O great sage, the worlds were almost burnt by that flame. Indra and other gods and the celestial sages too were afflicted by it. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सर्वे वासवाद्या सुराश्च मुनयो मुने ।
ब्रह्मस्थानं ययुः शीघ्रं तज्ज्वालातिप्रपीडिताः ॥ ८॥

English
Then, highly distressed by that flame, O sage, Indra and other gods and the sages hastened to the abode of Brahma. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नत्वा नुत्वा विधिं देवाः तत्स्वदुःखं न्यवेदयन् ।
ब्रह्मा सह सुरैस्तात विष्णुलोकं ययावरम् ॥ ९॥

English
After bowing to and eulogising Brahma the gods mentioned to him their grief. O dear, Brahma immediately went to Visnu's region along with the god. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा रमानाथं नत्वा नुत्वा विधिः सुरैः ।
स्वदुःखं तत्समाचख्यौ विष्णवेऽनन्तकं मुने ॥ १०॥

English
O sage, after reaching there along with the gods Brahma bowed to and eulogised Visnu and mentioned his endless grief to him. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुश्च विधिना देवै रुद्रस्थानं ययौ द्रुतम् ।
हरं प्रणम्य तत्रैत्य तुष्टाव परमेश्वरम् ॥ ११॥

English
Visnu too, accompanied by Brahma and the gods hastened to Rudra's abode. After reaching it he bowed to and eulogised lord Siva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुत्वा बहुतया विष्णुः स्वदुःखं च न्यवेदयत् ।
शर्वं ज्वालासमुद्भूतमत्रेश्च तपसः परम् ॥ १२॥

English
After eulogising him Visnu mentioned to Siva his grief caused by the flame due to the penance of Atri. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तत्र समेतास्तु ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेश्वराः ।
मुने सम्मन्त्रयाञ्चक्रुरन्योऽन्यं जगतां हितम् ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, thus gathering there, together, Brahma, Visnu and Siva consulted one another about the welfare of the worlds. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा ब्रह्मादयो देवास्त्रयस्ते वरदर्षभाः ।
जग्मुस्तदाश्रमं शीघ्रं वरं दातुं तदर्षये ॥ १४॥

English
The three deities Brahma and others, foremost among the bestowers of boons, went to the hermitage of Atri immediately to grant him the boon. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वचिह्नचिह्नितांस्तान्स दृष्ट्वात्रिर्मुनिसत्तमः ।
प्रणनाम च तुष्टाव वाग्भिरिष्टाभिरादरात् ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing them marked by their respective characteristic signs, the excellent sage Atri bowed to and eulogised them respectfully by uttering pleasing words. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स विस्मितो विप्रस्तानुवाच कृताञ्जलिः ।
ब्रह्मपुत्रो विनीतात्मा ब्रह्मविष्णुहराभिधान् ॥ १६॥

English
The surprised brahmin, the humble son of Brahma spoke to those deities Brahma, Visnu and Siva, with palms joined in reverence. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रिरुवाच ।
हे ब्रह्मन् हे हरे रुद्र पूज्यास्त्रिजगतां मताः ।
प्रभवश्चेश्वराः सृष्टिरक्षासंहारकारकाः ॥ १७॥

English
Atri said :— O Brahma, O Visnu, O Siva, you are worthy of being worshipped by the three worlds. You are lords and masters and the cause of creation, maintenance and annihilation. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
एक एव मया ध्यात ईश्वरः पुत्रहेतवे ।
यः कश्चिदीश्वरः ख्यातो जगतां स्वस्त्रिया सह ॥ १८॥

English
Accompanied by my wife, I meditated only on a single lord for obtaining a son, that lord whoever he may be, who is famous as Isvara. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यूयं त्रयस्सुराः कस्मादागता वरदर्षभाः ।
एतन्मे संशयं छित्त्वा ततो दत्तेप्सितं वरम् ॥ १९॥

English
How is it that you three deities, the foremost among the bestowers of boons have come here? Please dispel my doubt and then grant me the desired boon. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य प्रत्यूचुस्ते सुरास्त्रयः ।
यादृक्कृतस्ते सङ्कल्पस्तथैवाभून्मुनीश्वर ॥ २०॥

English
On hearing his words, the three deities replied "O great sage, what has occurred is in accordance with what you have conceived. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
वयं त्रयो भवेशानाः समाना वरदर्षभाः ।
अस्मदंशभवास्तस्माद्भविष्यन्ति सुतास्त्रयः ॥ २१॥

English
We three are great lords and equally leading among the grantors of boons. Hence three sons originating from our parts will be born to you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
विदिता भुवने सर्वे पित्रोः कीर्तिविवर्द्धनाः ।
इत्युक्त्वा त्रयो देवाः स्वधामानि ययुर्मुदा ॥ २२॥

English
They will be known in the world as enhancers of their parent's reputation." After saying this, the three deities returned to their abodes joyously. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वरं लब्ध्वा मुनिः सोऽथ जगाम स्वाश्रमं मुदा ।
युतोऽनुसूयया प्रीतो ब्रह्मानन्दप्रदो मुने ॥ २३॥

English
After securing the boon, the sage joyously returned to his hermitage accompanied by Anasūyā. O sage, he was delighted and he experienced the bliss of the supreme Brahman. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ब्रह्मा हरिः शम्भुरवतेरुः स्त्रियां ततः ।
पुत्ररूपैः प्रसन्नास्ते नानालीलाप्रकाशकाः ॥ २४॥

English
Then Brahma, Visnu and Siva, the delighted exponents of different kinds of sports, took their incarnations as the sons of his wife. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
विधेरंशाद्विधुर्जज्ञेऽनसूयायां मुनीश्वरात् ।
आविर्बभूवोदधितः क्षिप्तो देवैः स एव हि ॥ २५॥

English
The part of Brahma was born as the moon in Anasuyā from the great sage. Hurled by the gods into the ocean he alone could come out of it. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोरंशात् स्त्रियां तस्यामत्रेर्दत्तो व्यजायत ।
संन्यासपद्धतिर्येन वर्द्धिता परमा मुने ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, the part of Visnu was born of that lady, wife of Atri, as Datta by whom the great path of renunciation was expanded. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्वासा मुनिशार्दूलः शिवांशान्मुनिसत्तम ।
जज्ञे तस्यां स्त्रियामत्रेर्वरधर्मप्रवर्तकः ॥ २७॥

English
The part of Siva was born of that wife of Atri as the excellent and leading sage Durvasas who propagated excellent virtue. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
भूत्वा रुद्रश्च दुर्वासा ब्रह्मतेजोविवर्द्धनः ।
चक्रे धर्मपरीक्षां च बहूनां स दयापरः ॥ २८॥

English
Siva by becoming Durvasas, the enhancer of Brahminical splendour, tested the piety of many people. He was merciful. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यवंशे समुत्पन्नो योऽम्बरीषो नृपोऽभवत् ।
तत्परीक्षामकार्षीत्स तां श‍ृणु त्वं मुनीश्वर ॥ २९॥

English
The king Ambarisa, a scion of the solar dynasty, was tested by him. O great sage, listen to that story. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽम्बरीषो नृपवरः सप्तद्वीपरसापतिः ।
नियमं हि चकारासावेकादश्यां व्रते दृढम् ॥ ३०॥

English
The king Ambarisa was the lord of the earth consisting of seven continents.[59] He observed the rite of Ekadai regularly with steady restraint. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादश्या व्रतं कृत्वा द्वादश्यां चैव पारणाम् ।
करिष्यामीति सुदृढसङ्कल्पस्तु नराधिपः ॥ ३१॥

English
The king had the firm resolve that he would perform the rite of breaking fast on the Dvadasi day. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा तन्नियमं तस्य दुर्वासा मुनिसत्तमः ।
तदन्तिकं गतः शिष्यैर्बहुभिः शङ्करांशजः ॥ ३२॥

English
Coming to know of his regular observance the excellent sage Durvasas, born of Siva's part approached him with many disciples. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पारणे द्वादशीं स्वल्पां ज्ञात्वा यावत्स भोजनम् ।
कर्त्तुं व्यवसितस्तावदागतं स न्यमन्त्रयत् ॥ ३३॥

English
The king was about to take his breakfast realising that very little of Dvadasi was left on that day when the sage came there. The king invited him for food. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्नानार्थमगमद्दुर्वासाः शिष्यसंयुतः ।
विलम्बं कृतवांस्तत्र परीक्षार्थं मुनिर्बहु ॥ ३४॥

English
Then the sage Durvāsas went away for his ceremonial ablution, accompanied by his disciples. In order to test the king, the sage purposely delayed there. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मविघ्नं तदा ज्ञात्वा स नृपः शास्त्रशासनात् ।
जलं प्राश्यास्थितस्तत्र तदागमनकाङ्क्षया ॥ ३५॥

English
Realising that there would be violation of sacred injunction otherwise, the king took in a small quantity of water and waited for the arrival of the sage. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र दुर्वासा मुनिरागतः ।
कृताशनं नृपं ज्ञात्वा परीक्षार्थं धृताकृतिः ॥ ३६॥

चुक्रोधाति नृपे तस्मिन्परीक्षार्थं वृषस्य सः ।
प्रोवाच वचनं तूग्रं स मुनिः शङ्करांशजः ॥ ३७॥

English
In the meantime the sage returned. Knowing that the king had already had his breakfast, the sage, born of Śiva's part, who had assumed that form for the testing of piety, became very furious with the king. He spoke very harsh words for testing his piety. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्वासा उवाच ।
मां निमन्त्र्य नृपाभोज्य जलं पीतं त्वयाधम ।
दर्शयामि फलं तस्य दुष्टदण्डधरो ह्यहम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Durvāsas said:—  O base king, after inviting but without feeding me you have taken in water. I shall show you the fruit thereof. For I am the chastiser of the wicked. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा क्रोधताम्राक्षो नृपं दग्धुं समुद्यतः ।
समुत्तस्थौ द्रुतं चक्रं तत्स्थं रक्षार्थमैश्वरम् ॥ ३९॥

प्रजज्वालाति तं चक्रं मुनिं दग्धुं सुदर्शनम् ।
शिवरूपं तमज्ञात्वा शिवमायाविमोहितम् ॥ ४०॥

English
After saying this he stood up, his eyes turned red due to fury and attempted to burn him. Immediately, Āudarśana the discus of the lord blazed forth to protect the king and burn the sage unmindful of the fact that he was the part of Śiva. It had been deluded by the Māyā of Śiva. (39-40.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे व्योमवाण्युवाचाशरीरिणी ।
अम्बरीषं महात्मानं ब्रह्मभक्तं च वैष्णवम् ॥ ४१॥

English
In the meantime an unembodied celestial voice spoke to Ambariṣa, the noble soul, the devotee of Brahmā and Viṣṇu. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
व्योमवाण्युवाच ।
सुदर्शनमिदं चक्रं हरये शम्भुनार्पितम् ।
शान्तं कुरु प्रज्वलितमद्य दुर्वाससे नृप ॥ ४२॥

English
The celestial voice said:—  O king, this discus had been given to Viṣṇu by Śiva. It is now blazing against Durvāsas. Quieten it. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्वासायं शिवः साक्षात्स चक्रं हरयेऽर्पितम् ।
एवं साधारणमुनिं न जानीहि नृपोत्तम ॥ ४३॥

English
This sage Durvāsas is Śiva himself whose discus had been given to Viṣṇu. O excellen king, do not take him for an ordinary sage. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तव धर्मपरीक्षार्थमागतोऽयं मुनीश्वरः ।
शरणं याहि तस्याशु भविष्यत्यन्यथा लयः ॥ ४४॥

English
It was to test your piety that this great sage came. Seek refuge in him. Otherwise there will be a great destruction. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा च नभोवाणी विरराम मुनीश्वर ।
अस्तावीत्स हरांशं तमम्बरीषोऽपि चादरात् ॥ ४५॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  O great sage, after saying thus, the celestial voice stopped. Ambariṣa too eulogised the sage who was the part of Śiva himself. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
अम्बरीष उवाच ।
यद्यस्ति दत्तमिष्टं च स्वधर्मो वा स्वनुष्ठितः ।
कुलं नो विप्रदैवं चेद्धरेरस्त्रं प्रशाम्यतु ॥ ४६॥

English
Ambarīṣa said :—  If I possess the merit of charity and sacrifice, if my spiritual duty has been well performed by me, and if our family considers brahmins godly, let the weapon of Vişnu turn calm. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि नो भगवान्प्रीतो मद्भक्तो भक्तवत्सलः ।
सुदर्शनमिदं चास्त्रं प्रशाम्यतु विशेषतः ॥ ४७॥

English
If our lord, favourably disposed to his devotees, is delighted with my devotion let this missile Sudarsana become quite calm. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति स्तुवति रुद्राग्रे शैवं चक्रं सुदर्शनम् ।
अशाम्यत्सर्वथा ज्ञात्वा तं शिवांशं सुलब्धधीः ॥ ४८॥

अथाम्बरीषः स नृपः प्रणनाम च तं मुनिम् ।
शिवावतारं संज्ञाय स्वपरीक्षार्थमागतम् ॥ ४९॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  While be eulogised in front of Durvasas, the discus Sudarsana that originally belonged to Siva became calm in all respects. Knowing him to be Siva's part, the king Ambariṣa, who had regained the composure of mind bowed to the sage whom he realised as Siva's incarnation come there to test his vow. (48-49.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रसन्नो बभूवाथ स मुनिः शङ्करांशजः ।
भुक्त्वा तस्मै वरं दत्त्वा स्वाभीष्टं स्वालयं ययौ ॥ ५०॥

English
The sage, born of Siva's part, was much delighted. He took his meals and granted him the desired boon. Then he returned to his abode. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, I have narrated the story of Durvasas in the course of his test of Ambariṣa. O great sage, listen to another story of his. (51.)

Sanskrit
