### **CHAPTER TWENTY - *(The Incarnation of Hanūmat and his story)***

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अतः परं श‍ृणु प्रीत्या हनुमच्चरितं मुने ।
यथा चकाराशु हरो लीलास्तद्रूपतो वराः ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O sage, henceforth listen lovingly to the story of Hanūmat, how Śiva performed excellent sports in that form. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
चकार सुहितं प्रीत्या रामस्य परमेश्वरः ।
तत्सर्वं चरितं विप्र श‍ृणु सर्वसुखावहम् ॥ २॥

English
Out of love, Lord Śiva rendered great help to Rāma. O brahmin, listen to the whole of that story which is pleasing to all. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्समये शम्भुरद्भुतोतिकरः प्रभुः ।
ददर्श मोहिनीरूपं विष्णोः स हि वसेद्गुणः ॥ ३॥

English
Once lord Śiva of wonderful sports saw Viṣṇu in the form of an enchantress with glittering qualities. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रे स्वं क्षुभितं शम्भुः कामबाणहतो यथा ।
स्वं वीर्यं पातयामास रामकार्यार्थमीश्वरः ॥ ४॥

English
As if hit by the arrows of Cupid, Śiva let fall his semen dislodged from its seat, for Rāma’s work. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वीर्यं स्थापयामासुः पत्रे सप्तर्षयश्च ते ।
प्रेरिता मनसा तेन रामकार्यार्थमादरात् ॥ ५॥

English
Eagerly urged by him mentally for Rāma’s work, the seven celestial sages retained that semen in a leaf. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तैर्गौतमसुतायां तद्वीर्यं शम्भोर्महर्षिभिः ।
कर्णद्वारा तथाञ्जन्यां रामकार्यार्थमाहितम् ॥ ६॥

English
For Rāma’s work that semen was poured through the ears of Añjanī, the daughter of Gautama, by those sages. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च समये तस्माद्धनूमानिति नामभाक् ।
शम्भुर्जज्ञे कपितनुर्महाबलपराक्रमः ॥ ७॥

English
In due course Śiva was born of it in the form of a monkey named Hanūmat. He had great strength and exploit. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हनूमान्स कपीशानः शिशुरेव महाबलः ।
रविबिम्बं बभक्षाशु ज्ञात्वा लघुफलं प्रगे ॥ ८॥

English
Even as a child the lord of monkeys, Hanūmat was very powerful. Early in the morning he took the disc of the sun for a small fruit and wanted to eat it. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवप्रार्थनया तं सोऽत्यजज्ज्ञात्वा महाबलम् ।
शिवावतारं च प्राप वरान्दत्तान् सुरर्षभिः ॥ ९॥

English
At the request of the gods he left it. Knowing him to be an incarnation of Śiva of great power the gods and the sages granted him boons. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वजनन्यन्तिकं प्रागादथ सोऽतिप्रहर्षितः ।
हनूमान्सर्वमाचख्यौ तस्यै तद् वृत्तमादरात् ॥ १०॥

English
Highly delighted he approached his mother and eagerly told her everything in detail. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाज्ञया ततो धीरः सर्वविद्यामयत्नतः ।
सूर्यात्पपाठ स कपिर्गत्वा नित्यं तदन्तिकम् ॥ ११॥

English
At her behest the bold monkey went to the sun every day and learnt all lore from the sun without any strain. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्याज्ञया तदंशत्य सुग्रीवस्यान्तिकं ययौ ।
मातुराज्ञामनुप्राप्य रुद्रांशः कपिसत्तमः ॥ १२॥

English
The excellent monkey, a part of Śiva approached Sugrīva, a part of the sun, at his behest, after getting the permission of his mother. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्येष्ठभ्रात्रा वालिना हि स्वस्त्रीभोक्त्रा तिरस्कृतः ।
ऋष्यमूकगिरौ तेन न्यवसत्स हनूमता ॥ १३॥

English
Sugrīva who had been exiled by his elder brother Bālī who enjoyed his (Sugrīva's) wife, stayed on the mountain Rṣyamūka⁶¹ along with Hanūmat. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽभूत्स सुकण्ठस्य मन्त्री कपिवरः सुधीः ।
सर्वथा सुहितं चक्रे सुग्रीवस्य हरांशजः ॥ १४॥

English
The intelligent monkey born of a part of Śiva became Sugrīva's minister and did everything beneficial to him in every respect. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागतेन सभ्रात्रा हृतभार्येण दुःखिना ।
कारयामास रामेण तस्य सख्यं सुखावहम् ॥ १५॥

English
He made him enter into a pleasing alliance with Rāma who came there along with his brother and who was sad because his wife had been abducted by Rāvaṇa. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
घातयामास रामश्च वालिनं कपिकुञ्जरम् ।
भ्रातृपत्न्याश्च भोक्तारं पापिनं वीरमानिनम् ॥ १६॥

English
Rāma slew the powerful monkey Bālī who was a sinner as he enjoyed his brother's wife and who professed to be a great hero. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो रामाज्ञया तात हनूमान्वानरेश्वरः ।
स सीतान्वेषणं चक्रे बहुभिर्वानरैः सुधीः ॥ १७॥

English
Then at the behest of Rāma, O dear, the intelligent and leading monkey Hanūmat went out in search of Sītā along with many monkeys. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा लङ्कागतां सीतां गतस्तत्र कपीश्वरः ।
द्रुतमुल्लङ्घ्य सिन्धुं तमनिस्तीर्यं परैः स वै ॥ १८॥

English
On knowing that she was in Laṅkā, the leading monkey quickly jumped across the ocean, never before crossed by any and reached Laṅkā.⁶² (18.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रेऽद्भुतचरित्रं स तत्र विक्रमसंयुतम् ।
अभिज्ञानं ददौ प्रीत्या सीतायै स्वप्रभोर्वरम् ॥ १९॥

English
There he performed wonderful exploits and handed over the excellent token of recognition of his lord Rāma to Sītā. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सीताशोकं जहाराशु स वीरः कपिनायकः ।
श्रावयित्वा रामवृत्तं तत्प्राणावनकारकम् ॥ २०॥

English
Narrating the endeavours of Rāma for the protection of her life, the heroic leader of monkeys dispelled the sorrow of Sītā. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तदभिज्ञानमादाय निवृत्तो रामसन्निधिम् ।
रावणाराममाहत्य जघान बहुराक्षसान् ॥ २१॥

English
Taking a token from her he turned back to go towards Rāma. On the way he broke Rāvaṇa's park and killed many Rākṣasas. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव रावणसुतं हत्वा सबहुराक्षसम् ।
स महोपद्रवं चक्रे महोतिस्तत्र निर्भयः ॥ २२॥

English
At the same time he killed Rāvaṇa's son also along with many Rākṣasas. That fearless monkey of great exploits wrought a havoc there. (22.)

---

English
Rṣyamūkāgiri, associated with the life of Rāma, is placed in the south about three miles from Vijayanagara. (61.)

---

English
The identification of Laṅkā is controversial. According to the present context, Laṅkā, the capital of Rāvaṇa, lay to the south of Rṣyamūka in the island of Ceylon surrounded by the sea. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा दग्धो रावणेनावगुण्ठ्य वसनानि च ।
तैलाभ्यक्तानि सुदृढं महाबलवता मुने ॥ २३॥

उत्प्लुत्योत्प्लुत्य च तदा महादेवांशजः कपिः ।
ददाह लङ्कां निखिलां कृत्वा व्याजं तमेव हि ॥ २४॥

English
O sage, when the powerful Rāvaṇa bound him with oil-dripping cloths and set fire to them, the monkey, a part of Śiva himself, jumped about and making that a pretext burnt the city of Laṅkā. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्ध्वा लङ्कां वञ्चयित्वा विभीषणगृहं ततः ।
अपतद्वारिधौ वीरस्ततः स कपिकुञ्जरः ॥ २५॥

English
After burning Laṅkā except the mansion of Bībhīṣaṇa, the heroic leader of monkeys jumped into the ocean. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपुच्छं तत्र निर्वाप्य प्राप तस्य परन्तटम् ।
अखिन्नः स ययौ रामसन्निधिं गिरिशांशजः ॥ २६॥

English
Thereby extinguishing the fire from his tail he reached the other shore. Not at all tired or grieved the monkey born of a part of Śiva approached Rāma. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अविलम्बेन सुजवो हनूमान् कपिसत्तमः ।
रामोपकण्ठमागत्य ददौ सीताशिरोमणिम् ॥ २७॥

English
The rapid and excellent monkey Hanūmat reached Rāma's side without delay and handed over the crest-jewel of Sītā. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तदाज्ञया वीरः सिन्धौ सेतुमबन्धयत् ।
वानरैः स समानीय बहून् गिरिवरान् बली ॥ २८॥

English
At his behest, the powerful hero, gathered many mountains through the hosts of monkeys and built a bridge in the ocean. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र ततो रामस्तर्तुकामो यथा ततः ।
शिवलिङ्गं समानर्च प्रतिष्ठाप्य जयेप्सया ॥ २९॥

English
At the time when he was about to cross the ocean, Rāma installed a phallic image of Śiva and worshipped it with a desire to be victorious. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वरात्स जयं प्राप्य वरं तीर्त्वोदधिं ततः ।
लङ्कामावृत्य कपिभी रणं चक्रे स राक्षसैः ॥ ३०॥

English
Securing his boon of victory, he crossed the ocean, surrounded Laṅkā with the monkeys and fought against the Rākṣasas. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
जघानाथासुरान्वीरो रामसैन्यं ररक्ष सः ।
शक्तिक्षतं लक्ष्मणं च सञ्जीविन्या ह्यजीवयत् ॥ ३१॥

English
The heroic Hanūmat slew many Asuras and protected Rāma's army. He enlivened Lakṣmaṇa by the medicinal herb Sañjīvanī when he was wounded by Meghanāda's javelin. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा सुखिनं चक्रे सरामं लक्ष्मणं हि सः ।
सर्वसैन्यं ररक्षासौ महादेवात्मजः प्रभुः ॥ ३२॥

English
The lordly son of Śiva made Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa happy in every respect. He protected the entire army. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
रावणं परिवाराढ्यं नाशयामास विश्रमः ।
सुखीचकार देवान्स महाबलग्रहः कपिः ॥ ३३॥

English
The untiring monkey destroyed Rāvaṇa, his family and his followers. The monkey, the storehouse of great strength made the gods happy. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
महीरावणसंज्ञं स हत्वा रामं सलक्ष्मणम् ।
तत्स्थानादानयामास स्वस्थानं परिपाल्य च ॥ ३४॥

English
He slew the demon Mahīrāvaṇa and brought Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa from his place to their own after guarding them well. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
रामकार्यं चकाराशु सर्वथा कपिपुङ्गवः ।
असुरान्नमयामास नानालीलां चकार च ॥ ३५॥

English
The leading monkey quickly carried out the tasks of Rāma by all means, made the Asuras bow down and submit and performed various sports. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थापयामास भूलोके रामभक्तिं कपीश्वरः ।
स्वयं भक्तवरो भूत्वा सीतारामसुखप्रदः ॥ ३६॥

English
Himself a leader of devotees, he made Sītā and Rāma happy. He, the lord of monkeys, established the cult of devotion to Rāma in the world. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
लक्ष्मणप्राणदाता च सर्वदेवमदापहः ।
रुद्रावतारो भगवान्भक्तोद्धारकरः स वै ॥ ३७॥

English
Indeed he was the Incarnation of Śiva the lord and uplifter of devotees. He was the life-saviour of Lakṣmaṇa and the destroyer of the arrogance of all Daityas. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
हनुमान्स महावीरो रामकार्यकरः सदा ।
रामदूताभिधो लोके दैत्यघ्नो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ३८॥

English
He was the heroic Hanūmat who carried the tasks of Rāma always. In the world he is called Rāma's messenger. He is favourably disposed to his devotees. He is the slayer of Daityas. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कथितं तात हनुमच्चरितं वरम् ।
धन्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Thus O dear, the excellent story of Hanumat has been narrated to you. It is conducive to wealth, fame and longevity. It yields the fruits of all desires. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या श्रावयेद्वा समाहितः ।
स भुक्त्वेहाखिलान्कामान् अन्ते मोक्षं लभेत्परम् ॥ ४०॥

English
He who listens to this or narrates this with devotion and purity of mind enjoys all desires here and attains liberation hereafter. (40.)
