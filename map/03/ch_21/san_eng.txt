### **CHAPTER TWENEYONE - (The Incarnation and the story of Maheśa)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अथ प्रीत्या श‍ृणु मुनेऽवतारं परमं प्रभोः ।
शङ्करस्यात्मभूपुत्र श‍ृण्वतां सर्वकामदम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:- O sage, O son of the self-born Brahmā, listen to the great incarnation of lord Śiva, which yields all desires to the hearer. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा मुनिशार्दूल गिरिजाशङ्करावुभौ ।
विहर्तुकामौ सञ्जातौ स्वेच्छया परमेश्वरौ ॥ २॥

English
O great sage, once the supreme god Śiva and the great goddess Pārvatī became desirous of indulging in sports. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
भैरवं द्वारपालं च कृत्वाभ्यन्तरमागतौ ।
नानासखिगणैः प्रीत्या सेवितौ नरशीलितौ ॥ ३॥

English
Keeping Bhairava as the watch-man they went inside. Acting like human beings they were lovingly served by many attendants. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
चिरं विहृत्य तत्र द्वौ स्वतन्त्रौ परमेश्वरौ ।
बभूवतुः प्रसन्नौ तौ नानालीलाकरौ मुने ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, indulging in different sports of their choice for a long time, the lordly deities were highly delighted. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोन्मत्ताकृतिर्देवी स्वतन्त्रा लीलया शिवा ।
आगता द्वारि तद्रूपा प्रभोराज्ञामवाप सा ॥ ५॥

English
The independent goddess Pārvatī sportively assumed the form of a mad woman and with the permission of her lord came near to the door in that form itself. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तां देवीं भैरवः सोऽथ नारीदृष्ट्या विलोक्य च ।
निषिषेध बहिर्गन्तुं तद्रूपेण विमोहितः ॥ ६॥

English
Fascinated by her charming form Bhairava glanced at her as at a woman and prevented her from going out. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नारीदृष्ट्या सुदृष्टा सा भैरवेण यदा मुने ।
क्रुद्धाभवच्छिवा देवी तं शशाप तदाऽम्बिका ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, viewed at very closely by Bhairava, the goddess Pārvatī became furious and cursed him. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोवाच ।
नारीदृष्ट्या पश्यसि त्वं यतो मां पुरुषाधम ।
अतो भव धरायां हि मानुषस्त्वं च भैरव ॥ ८॥

English
Śiva said:— O base man, since you glanced at me as at a woman, O Bhairava, be born as a human being on the earth. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्थं यदाभवच्छप्तो भैरवः शिवया मुने ।
हाहाकारो महानासीद्दुःखमाप स लीलया ॥ ९॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— O sage, when Bhairava was thus cursed by her there was a hue and cry. He became very miserable due to the sport of the goddess. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च शङ्करः शीघ्रं तमागत्य मुनीश्वर ।
आश्वासयद्भैरवं हि नानानुनयकोविदः ॥ १०॥

English
O great sage, hastening to that place, Śiva consoled Bhairava. Indeed, the lord is very expert in the various forms of consolation. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छापाद्भैरवः सोऽथ क्षिताववतरन्मुने ।
मनुष्ययोन्यां वैतालसंज्ञकः शङ्ककरेच्छया ॥ ११॥

English
Due to her curse, and as a result of Śiva's will, O sage, Bhairava descended to the earth in human birth under the name of Vaitāla. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्स्नेहतः शिवः सोऽपि क्षिताववतरद्विभुः ।
शिवया सह सल्लीलो लौकिकीं गतिमाश्रितः ॥ १२॥

English
Urged by his friendship lord Śiva also incarnated on the earth along with Śivā. He indulged in good sports and followed worldly conventions. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
महेशाह्वः शिवश्चासीच्छारदा गिरिजा मुने ।
सुलीलां चक्रतुः प्रीत्या नानालीलाविशारदौ ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, Śiva was known as Maheśa and Pārvatī as Śāradā. Both of them experts in different sports indulged in fine sports. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कथितं तात महेशचरितं वरम् ।
धन्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ १४॥

English
O dear, thus the story of Maheśa has been narrated to you. It is excellent, conducive to wealth, fame and longevity and yields all desires. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या श्रावयेद्वा समाहितः ।
स भुक्त्वेहाखिलान्भोगानन्ते मोक्षमवाप्नुयात् ॥ १५॥

English
He who listens to and narrates this story with devotion and purity of mind enjoys all pleasures here and attains salvation hereafter. (15.)
