### **CHAPTER TWENTYTWO - (Harassment by Viṣṇu's sons and the story of Śiva's incarnation of the Bull)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु ब्रह्मसुत प्राज्ञ वृषेशाख्यं मुनीश्वर ।
शिवावतारं सल्लीलं हरिगर्वहरं वरम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:— O great sage, O intelligent son of Brahma, listen to the excellent Vṛṣeśa incarnation of Śiva that performed good sports and that destroyed the arrogance of Viṣṇu. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा देवासुराः सर्वे जरामृत्युभयार्दिताः ।
परस्परं च सन्धाय रत्नान्यादित्सवोऽभवन् ॥ २॥

English
Formerly all the gods and Asuras, distressed by the fear of old age and death made alliance with each other and became desirous of taking gems from the ocean. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सुरासुराः सर्वे क्षीरोदं सागरोत्तमम् ।
उद्यता मथितुं तं च बभूवुर्मुनिनन्दन ॥ ३॥

English
O son of the sage, then the gods and the Asuras attempted to churn the excellent milky ocean.64 (3.)

---

Sanskrit
आसन् शुचिस्मिताः सर्वे केनेदं मन्थनं भवेत् ।
स्वकार्यसिद्धये तस्य ब्रह्मन्निति सुरासुराः ॥ ४॥

English
"With what shall the churning be carried out for the attainment of our object?" thus the gods and the Asuras were quite dismayed. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा नभोगता वाणी मेघगम्भीरनिःस्वना ।
उवाच देवान्दैत्यांश्चश्वासयन्तीश्वराज्ञया ॥ ५॥

English
Then a celestial voice with the rumbling sound of clouds spoke to the gods and the daityas as a result of Siva's wish and consoled them. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
नभोवाण्युवाच ।
हे देवा असुराश्चैव मन्थध्वं क्षीरसागरम् ।
भवतां बलबुद्धिर्हि भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ ६॥

English
The celestial voice said :— O gods, O Asuras, churn the milk-ocean. Undoubtedly a factual test of your strength will take place. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्दरं चैव मन्थानं रज्जुं कुरुत वासुकिम् ।
मिथः सर्वे मिलित्वा तु मन्थनं कुरुतादरात् ॥ ७॥

English
Make Mandara your churning rod and Vāsuki your churning rope. Unitedly you carry on the churning with firm devotion. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
नभोगतां तदा वाणीं निशम्याथ सुरासुराः ।
उद्योगं चक्रिरे सर्वे तत्कर्तुं मुनिसत्तम ॥ ८॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O excellent sage, on hearing the celestial voice the gods and the Asuras exerted themselves in carrying out the plan. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सुसन्धायाखिलास्ते वै मन्दरं पर्वतोत्तमम् ।
कनकाभं च सरलं नानाशोभार्चितं ययुः ॥ ९॥

English
Allying together they went straight to the excellent mountain Mandara of golden splendour and of diverse lustre. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रसाद्य गिरीशं तं तदाज्ञप्ताः सुरासुराः ।
बलादुत्पाटयामासुर्नेतुकामाः पयोऽर्णवम् ॥ १०॥

English
After propitiating Siva and after obtaining his consent they tried to uproot the Mandara in their desire to take it to the milk-ocean. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
भुजैरुत्पाट्य ते सर्वे जग्मुः क्षीरार्णवं मुने ।
अशक्ता अभवंस्तत्र तमानेतुं हतौजसः ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, uprooting and lifting it by means of their arms they went to the milk-ocean. Their strength failing they became incapable of taking it up to the sea. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्भुजैः स परिभ्रष्टः पतितो मन्दरो गिरिः ।
सहसातिगुरुः सद्यो देवदैत्योपरि ध्रुवम् ॥ १२॥

English
Slipping from their arms the Mandara became very heavy suddenly and fell over the gods and the Asuras. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं भग्नोद्यमा भग्नाः सम्बभूवुः सुरासुराः ।
चेतनाः प्राप्य च ततस्तुष्टुवुर्जगदीश्वरम् ॥ १३॥

English
Failing in their effort the gods and the Asuras broke down. After regaining consciousness they eulogised the lord of the universe. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिच्छयोद्यताः सर्वे पुनरुत्थाप्य तं गिरिम् ।
निचिक्षिपुर्जले नीत्वा क्षीरोदस्योत्तरे तटे ॥ १४॥

English
At his wish in their next attempt they lifted up the mountain. They took it to the northern shore of the ocean and hurled it into the waters. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सुरासुरगणा रज्जुं कृत्वा च वासुकिम् ।
रत्नान्यादातुकामास्ते ममन्थुः क्षीरसागरम् ॥ १५॥

English
Then both the gods and the Asuras made Vāsuki the churning rope. Desirous of taking the excellent articles they churned the milk-ocean. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षीरोदे मथ्यमाने तु श्रीः स्वर्लोकमहेश्वरी ।
समुद्भूता समुद्राच्च भृगुपुत्री हरिप्रिया ॥ १६॥

English
When the milk-ocean was churned the goddess of heaven sprang up from the ocean as the daughter of Bhṛgu who later became Viṣṇu's beloved. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्वन्तरिः शशाङ्कश्च पारिजातो महाद्रुमः ।
उच्चैःश्रवाश्च तुरगो गज ऐरावतस्तथा ॥ १७॥

सुरा हरिधनुः शङ्खो गावः कामदुघास्ततः ।
कौस्तुभाख्यो मणिश्चैव तथा पीयूषमेव च ॥ १८॥

English
There also emerged—Dhanvantari, the moon, the Pārijāta tree, the horse Uccaiśśravas, the elephant Airāvata, wine, the bow of Viṣṇu, the conch, the cow Kāmadhenu, the jewel Kaustubha and the nectar. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्च मथ्यमाने तु कालकूटं महाविषम् ।
युगान्तानलभं जातं सुरासुरभयावहम् ॥ १९॥

English
When it was churned again, the great poison Kālakūṭa blazing like the fire at the dissolution of the Yugas and terrifying the gods and the Asuras, came out. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पीयूषजन्मकाले तु बिन्दवो ये बहिर्गताः ।
तेभ्यः कान्ताः समुद्भूता बह्वयो ह्यद्भुतदर्शनाः ॥ २०॥

English
From the sprays and drops that sprang up at the outcome of nectar, many damsels of wonderful beauty were born. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
शरत्पूर्णेन्दुवदनास्तडित्सूर्यानलप्रभाः ।
हारकेयूरकटकैर्दिव्यरत्नैरलङ्कृताः ॥ २१॥

English
Their faces resembled the full moon in the Autumn; their lustre was as glittering and dazzling as fire, lightning or the sun and they were bedecked in divine jewels, necklaces, bracelets and bangles. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
लावण्यामृततोयेन ताः सिञ्चन्त्यो दिशो दश ।
जगदुन्मादयन्त्येव भ्रूभङ्गायतवीक्षणाः ॥ २२॥

English
Sprinkling the ten quarters by the water of nectarine beauty they seemed to madden the world by their beauteous glances. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिशस्ताः समुत्पन्नास्त्वमृतात्कामनिस्सृताः ।
ततोऽमृतं समुत्पन्नं जरामृत्युनिवारणम् ॥ २३॥

English
They emerged in crores from the nectar sprays. Then the nectar that wards off old age and death came out. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
लक्ष्मीं शङ्खं कौस्तुभं च खड्गं जग्राह केशवः ।
जग्राहार्को हयं दिव्यमुच्चैःश्रवसमादरात् ॥ २४॥

English
Viṣṇu kept for himself Lakṣmī, the conch, Kaustubha and the sword; the Sun chose the divine horse Uccaiśśravas for himself. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पारिजातं तरुवरमैरावतमिभेश्वरम् ।
शचीपतिश्च जग्राह निर्जरेशो महादरात् ॥ २५॥

English
With great eagerness the husband of Śaci, the lord of the gods, took Pārijāta the excellent tree and Airāvata the lordly elephant. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
कालकूटं शशाङ्कं च देवत्राणाय शङ्करः ।
स्वकण्ठे धृतवान् शम्भुः स्वेच्छया भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २६॥

English
For the protection of the gods, Śiva, favourably disposed towards his devotees, voluntarily retained the Kāla-kūṭa in his neck and held the moon on his forehead. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्याः सुराख्यां रमणीमीश्वराजविमोहिताः ।
जगृहुः सकला व्यास सर्वे धन्वन्तरिं जनाः ॥ २७॥

English
O Vyāsa, deluded by the illusion of Śiva, the Daityas accepted Surā (wine) and the common people took Dhanvantari (the physician). (27.)

---

Sanskrit
जगृहुर्मुनयः सर्वे कामधेनुं मुनीश्वराः ।
सामान्यतस्त्रियस्ताश्च स्थिता आसन्विमोहिकाः ॥ २८॥

English
The great sages took the cow Kāmadhenu. The damsels, the enchantresses became common to all. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अमृतार्थं महायुद्धं सम्बभूव जयैषिणाम् ।
सुराणामसुराणां च मिथः सङ्क्षुब्धचेतसाम् ॥ २९॥

English
A great battle for the possession of Amṛta ensued between the gods and the Asuras who desired to conquer each other and who were agitated in their minds. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
हृतं सोमं च दैतेयैर्बलाद्देवान्विजित्य च ।
बलिप्रभृतिभिर्व्यास युगान्ताग्न्यर्कसुप्रभैः ॥ ३०॥

English
The Amṛta was forcibly taken by the Daityas Bali and others, O Vyāsa, of fine lustre resembling that of the fire and the sun at the dissolution of the Yugas. They defeated the gods. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाः शङ्करमापन्ना विह्वलाः शिवमायया ।
सर्वे शक्रादयस्तात दैतेयैरर्दिता बलात् ॥ ३१॥

English
Indra and other gods, O dear, overwhelmed by Śiva's illusion and harassed by the Daityas sought refuge in Śiva. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तदमृतं यत्नात्स्त्रीस्वरूपेण मायया ।
शिवाज्ञया रमेशेन दैत्येभ्यश्च हृतं मुने ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, then the Amṛta was strenuously snatched away from the Daityas by Viṣṇu at the behest of Śiva, after assuming the form of a woman. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अपाययत्सुरांस्तांश्च मोहिनी स्त्रीस्वरूपधृक् ।
मोहयित्वासुरान्सर्वान्हरिर्मायाविनां वरः ॥ ३३॥

English
Viṣṇu, the most excellent of those who wield Māyā assuming the form of a woman Mohinī deluded the Asuras and made the gods drink it. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा निकटमेतस्या ऊचिरे दैत्यपुङ्गवाः ।
पाययस्व सुधामेतां माभूद्भेदोऽत्र पङ्क्तिषु ॥ ३४॥

English
The leading Daityas approached her and said— “Make us drink this nectar. Let there be no break in the lines.” (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदुक्त्वा ददुस्तस्मै विष्णवे छलरूपिणे ।
ते दैत्या दानवाः सर्वे शिवमायाविमोहिताः ॥ ३५॥

English
All those Daityas and Dānavas deluded by Śiva's Māyā said thus and gave the nectar to Viṣṇu in disguise. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दृष्ट्वा स्त्रियो दानवपुङ्गवाः ।
अनयन्नमृतोद्भूता यथास्थानं यथासुखम् ॥ ३६॥

English
In the meantime on seeing the damsels born of Amṛta, the leading Dānavas took them to their abodes according to their convenience. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तासां पुराणि दिव्यानि स्वर्गाच्छतगुणान्यपि ।
घोरैर्यन्त्रैः सुगुप्तानि मयमायाकृतानि च ॥ ३७॥

English
The cities built by the architect Maya for those women were divine, hundred times more beautiful than heaven. They were well fortified by terrible machines. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरक्षितानि सर्वाणि कृत्वा युद्धाय निर्ययुः ।
अस्पृष्टवक्षसो दैत्याः कृत्वा समयमेव हि ॥ ३८॥

English
The daityas made those cities well guarded and set off for fighting after taking this pledge touching their chests.* (38.)

---

Sanskrit
न स्पृशामः स्त्रियश्चेमा यदि देवैर्विनिर्जिताः ।
इत्युक्त्वा ते महावीरा दैत्याः सर्वे युयुत्सवः ॥ ३९॥

English
"We will never touch these women if we were to be defeated by the gods." After saying this, the heroic Daityas became eager to fight. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहनादं ततश्चक्रुः शङ्खान्दध्मुः पृथक्पृथक् ।
पूरयन्त इवाकाशं तर्पयन्तो बलाहकान् ॥ ४०॥

English
They roared like lions. Severally they blew their conches as if filling the sky and propitiating the clouds. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
A terrible fight ensued between the gods and the Asuras. The unrivalled battle between the gods and Asuras is famous in the three worlds. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Protected by Vişnu, the gods gained victory. The Daityas fled and those who remained were killed by Vişnu and his associate gods. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The Daityas were deluded by the gods and Vişnu the great soul. Those that survived entered the nether regions. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
In the meantime Vişnu saw those damsels born of nectar sprays who were haughty due to their divine beauty and whose faces resembled the full moon. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Fascinated by the Cupid's arrows Vişnu attained highest pleasure only there. He began to indulge in sexual dalliance with those women of exquisite beauty. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Vişnu begot of them sons of great exploits and valour, experts in various kinds of warfare, shaking the entire earth. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

Sanskrit
