### **CHAPTER TWENTY-THREE - (The bull incarnation of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
ततो वृषभरूपेण गर्जमानः पिनाकधृक् ।
प्रविष्टो विवरं तत्र निनदन्भैरवान् रवान् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Then bellowing in the form of a Bull, the Pinaka-bearing lord entered the crevice and shouted terribly. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
निपेतुस्तस्य निनदैः पुराणि नगराणि च ।
प्रकम्पो हि बभूवाथ सर्वेषां पुरवासिनाम् ॥ २॥

English
At his bellowing sound, cities and towns crumbled down and all the citizens trembled. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो वृषो हरेः पुत्रान्सङ्ग्रामोद्यतकार्मुकान् ।
शिवमायाविमूढात्ममहाबलपराक्रमान् ॥ ३॥

English
Then the bull attacked Visnu's sons wielding their bows for fighting, deluded by Siva's Maya and proud of their great strength and exploit. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिपुत्रास्ततस्तेऽथ प्राकुप्यन्मुनिसत्तम ।
प्रदुद्रुवुः प्रगर्ज्योच्चैर्वीराः शङ्करसम्मुखम् ॥ ४॥

English
O excellent sage, the sons of Visnu became furious. Those heroes roared loudly and rushed against Siva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
आयातांस्तान्हरेः पुत्रान् रुद्रो वृषभरूपधृक् ।
प्राकुप्यद्विष्णुपुत्रांश्च खुरैः श‍ृङ्गैर्व्यदारयत् ॥ ५॥

English
Rudra in that form of a bull was furious at those sons of Visnu who rushed at him. He kicked them with his hoofs and tore them with his horns. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
विदारिताङ्गा रुद्रेण सर्वे हरिसुताश्च ते ।
नष्टा द्रुतं सन्बभूवुर्गतप्राणा विचेतसः ॥ ६॥

English
When their limbs were split by Rudra, they swooned and lost their lives. They were immediately destroyed. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
हतेषु तेषु पुत्रेषु विष्णुर्बलवतां वरः ।
निष्क्रम्याथ प्रगर्ज्योच्चैर्ययौ शीघ्रं हरान्तिकम् ॥ ७॥

English
When they were killed Visnu the foremost of the strong came out shouting loudly and hastened towards Siva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा रुद्रं प्रव्रजन्तं हतविष्णुसुतं वृषम् ।
शरैः सन्ताडयामास दिव्यैरस्त्रैश्च केशवः ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing Śiva in the form of the bull going along after killing his sons, Viṣṇu hit him with divine arrows and missiles. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्रुद्धो महादेवो वृषरूपी मदाबलः ।
अस्त्राणि तानि विष्णोश्च जग्रास गिरिगोचरः ॥ ९॥

English
Then the infuriated Śiva, the highly powerful one in the form of a bull stood like a mountain and caught hold of all those missiles of Viṣṇu. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ कृत्वा महाकोपं वृषात्मा स महेश्वरः ।
विननाद महाघोरं कम्पयंस्त्रिजगन्मुने ॥ १०॥

English
O sage, shouting furiously, lord Śiva in the form of a bull shook the three worlds terribly. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत उत्प्लुत्य तरसा खुरैः श‍ृङ्गैर्व्यदारयत् ।
विष्णुं क्रोधाकुलं मूढमजानन्तं निजं हरिम् ॥ ११॥

English
The lord leaped up suddenly and kicked the infuriated Viṣṇu who was deluded and could not realise him. The lord then tore him with his horns. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स शिथिलात्मा हि व्यथिताङ्गो बभूव ह ।
तत्प्रहारमसह्याशु हरिर्मायाविमोहितः ॥ १२॥

English
Unable to bear the onslaught, Viṣṇu deluded by Māyā was exhausted and utterly shattered. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
गतगर्वो हरिश्चैव विचेता गतचेतनः ।
ज्ञातवान् परमेशानं विहरन्तं वृषात्मना ॥ १३॥

English
When his pride fell off, Viṣṇu was dejected, and dispirited. He then realised that Śiva was sporting about in the form of a bull. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ विज्ञाय गौरीशमागतं वृषरूपतः ।
प्राह गम्भीरया वाचा नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ॥ १४॥

English
Then on realising that Śiva himself had come in the form of a Bull, Viṣṇu spoke in a shrill voice with drooping shoulders and palms joined in reverence. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिरुवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणासागर प्रभो ।
मायया ते महेशान मोहितोऽहं विमूढधीः ॥ १५॥

English
Viṣṇu said :— O lord of the gods, O ocean of mercy, O lord Śiva, I had been deluded by your Māyā and my mind had been confused. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं युद्धं त्वयेशेन स्वनाथेन मया प्रभो ।
कृपां कृत्वा मयि स्वामिन्सोऽपराधो हि सह्यताम् ॥ १६॥

English
O lord, I fought with you, Śiva. Taking pity on me, O lord, let this offence be excused. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा हरेर्दीनतया मुने ।
भगवान् शङ्करः प्राह रमेशं भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १७॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O sage, on hearing those piteous words of Viṣṇu, lord Śiva favourably disposed to his devotees spoke to Viṣṇu. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
हे विष्णो हे महाबुद्धे कथं मां ज्ञातवान्न हि ।
युद्धं कृतं कृतस्तेऽद्य ज्ञानं सर्वं च विस्मृतम् ॥ १८॥

English
Śiva said :—  O Viṣṇu, O intelligent one, how is that you did not know me? Why did you fight? Your intellect has completely been depraved. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मानं किन्न जानासि मदधीनपराक्रमम् ।
त्वया नात्र रतिः कार्या निवर्तस्व कुचारतः ॥ १९॥

English
Why don't you realise yourself as one whose exploit is dependent on me. You should not have indulged in sexual dalliance there. You should have refrained from all misdeeds. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
कामाधीनं कथं ज्ञानं स्त्रीषु सक्तो विहारकृत् ।
नोचितं तव देवेश स्मरणं विश्वतारणम् ॥ २०॥

English
Why should your intellect be subservient to lust? That you should indulge in dalliance with women does not behove you, O lord of gods. Remember the task of uplifting the universe. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा शम्भुवचनं विज्ञानप्रदमादरात् ।
व्रीड्यन्स्वमनसा विष्णुः प्राह वाचं महेश्वरम् ॥ २१॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing those words of Śiva that imparted knowledge Viṣṇu was ashamed of himself and then he spoke to lord Śiva respectfully. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
ममात्र विद्यते चक्रं तद् गृहीत्वेतदादरात् ।
गमिष्यामि स्वलोकं तं त्वदाज्ञापरिपालकः ॥ २२॥

English
Viṣṇu said :—  My discus is here. I shall take the discus and return to my region abiding by your order with reverence. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य महेशानो वचनं वैष्णवं हरः ।
प्रत्युवाच वृषात्मा हि वृषरक्षः पुनर्हरिम् ॥ २३॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing the words of Viṣṇu, lord Śiva the protector of virtue in the guise of a bull, replied to Viṣṇu again. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
न विलम्बः प्रकर्त्तव्यो गन्तव्यमित आशु ते ।
मच्छासनाद्धरे लोके चक्रमत्रैव तिष्ठताम् ॥ २४॥

English
You shall go away quickly from this place. You shall not delay, O Viṣṇu, this is my behest. Let your discus stay in this world itself. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तानादित्यसंस्थानाच्छिवत्ववचनादपि ।
अहं घोरतरं तस्माच्चक्रमन्यद्ददामि ते ॥ २५॥

English
I shall give you a discus more terrible than the sun at the time of dissolution and more efficacious than the words of Śiva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदुक्त्वा हरोऽलेखीद्दिव्यं कालानलप्रभम् ।
परं चक्रं प्रदीप्तं हि सर्वदुष्टविनाशनम् ॥ २६॥

English
After saying this Śiva created another blazing discus, as lustrous as the Kāla fire, for the destruction of the wicked. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णवे प्रददौ चक्रं घोरार्कायुतसुप्रभम् ।
सर्वामरमुनीन्द्राणां रक्षकाय महात्मने ॥ २७॥

English
He gave the discus, as lustrous as ten thousand fierce suns, to Viṣṇu, the great Ātman, the protector of the gods and the leading sages. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
लब्ध्वा सुदर्शनं चान्यञ्चक्रं परमदीप्तिमत् ।
उवाच विबुधांस्तत्र विष्णुर्बुद्धिमतां वरः ॥ २८॥

English
After securing another Sudarśana discus of more brilliance, Viṣṇu the foremost of intelligent gods spoke to the gods. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वदेववरा यूयं मद्वाक्यं श‍ृणुतादरात् ।
कर्तव्यं तत्तथा शीघ्रं ततः शं वो भविष्यति ॥ २९॥

English
“O ye gods, you listen to my statement eagerly. You must act accordingly and immediately. It will be to your advantage. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्या वराङ्गनास्सन्ति पाताले यौवनान्विताः ।
ताभिः सार्द्धं महाक्रीडां यः करोतु करोतु सः ॥ ३०॥

English
There are many youthful maidens in the nether regions exquisitely dressed. Any one of you can go and indulge in sexual dalliance with them.” (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा केशवाद्वाक्यं शूरास्त्रिदशयोनयः ।
प्रवेष्टुकामाः पातालं बभूवुर्विष्णुना सह ॥ ३१॥

English
On hearing these words of Viṣṇu the valorous gods became desirous of entering the nether regions along with Viṣṇu. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
विचारमथ विज्ञाय तं तदा भगवान्हरः ।
क्रोधाच्छापं ददौ घोरं देवयोन्यष्टकस्य च ॥ ३२॥

English
Knowing their intention then, lord Śiva angrily cursed all the eight⁶⁶ types of the gods. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
हर उवाच ।
वर्जयित्वा मुनिं शान्तं दानवान्वा मदंशजम् ।
इदं यः प्रविशेत्स्थानं तस्य स्यान्निधनं क्षणात् ॥ ३३॥

English
Śiva said :—  “Excepting a quiescent sage and the Dānavas born of my parts whoever enters this place shall die in a trice.” (33.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा वाक्यमिदं घोरं मनुष्यहितवर्धनम् ।
प्रत्याख्यातास्तु रुद्रेण देवाः स्वगृहमाययुः ॥ ३४॥

English
After hearing these terrible words which are conducive to the increase of the benefit of human beings, the gods thus restrained by Rudra returned to their own abodes. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्त्रीलः परो विष्णुः शिवेन प्रतिशासितः ।
स्वर्लोकमगमद् व्यास स्वास्थ्यं प्राप जगच्च तत् ॥ ३५॥

English
Thus Viṣṇu who was excessively indulgent in women was chastised by Śiva. O Vyāsa, he returned to heaven. The world attained normalcy. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषेश्वरोऽपि भगवान् शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
इत्थं कृत्वा देवकार्यं जगाम स्वगिरीश्वरम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Lord Śiva, favourably disposed towards his devotees, returned to his mountain after carrying out the task of the gods in the guise of a bull. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषेश्वरावतारस्तु वर्णितः शङ्करस्य च ।
विष्णुमोहहरः शर्वः त्रैलोक्यसुखकारकः ॥ ३७॥

English
Thus the bull incarnation of Śiva that dispelled Viṣṇu’s delusion and caused happiness to the three worlds, has been narrated. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
पवित्रमिदमाख्यानं शत्रुबाधाहरं परम् ।
स्वर्ग्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं सताम् ॥ ३८॥

English
This narrative is sacred. It dispels the harassment from enemies. It is conducive to the attainment of heaven, fame and longevity. It confers worldly pleasures and salvation to the good. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या श्रावयेद्वै समाहितः ।
तथा पठिति यो हीदं पाठयेत् सुधियो नरान् ।
स भुक्त्वा सकलान् कामान् अन्ते मोक्षमवाप्नुयात् ॥ ३९॥

English
He who listens to this with devotion and narrates this with purity enjoys all pleasures here and attains salvation hereafter. So also is the case with him who reads or teaches this to intelligent men. (39.)

---

English
Amara mentions ten semi-divine beings viz. विद्याधर, अप्सरस्, यक्ष, रक्षस्, गन्धर्व, किन्नर, पिशाच, गुह्यक, सिद्ध and भूत। ŚP. mentions eight, without giving their names. It is not clear which two shall be excluded from the above list. (66.)
