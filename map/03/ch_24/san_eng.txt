### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - (Pippalāda incarnation of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
पिप्पलादाख्यमपरमवतारं महेशितुः ।
श‍ृणु प्राज्ञ महाप्रीत्या भक्तिवर्धनमुत्तमम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O intelligent one, listen with very great pleasure to the Pippalāda incarnation of Śiva that is excellent and that inspires devotion. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पुरा गदितो विप्रो दधीचिर्मुनिसत्तमः ।
महाशैवः सुप्रतापी च्यावनिर्भृगुवंशजः ॥ २॥

English
Formerly there was a great brahmin and an excellent sage Dadhici. He was born of Cyavana of Bhṛgu family. He was a great devotee of Śiva and very powerful. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुवेण सह सङ्ग्रामे येन विष्णुः पराजितः ।
सनिर्जरोऽथ संशप्तो महेश्वरसहायिना ॥ ३॥

English
In the fight with Kṣuva, Viṣṇu and other gods were defeated by this sage who had the support of Śiva and they were cursed. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पत्नी महाभागा सुवर्चा नाम नामतः ।
महापतिव्रता साध्वी यया शप्ता दिवौकसः ॥ ४॥

English
He had a fortunate wife Suvarca, who was very noble and chaste. The gods were cursed by her. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्तस्यां महादेवो नानालीलाविशारदः ।
प्रादुर्बभूव तेजस्वी पिप्पलादेति नामतः ॥ ५॥

English
The great lord, an expert in different kinds of sports and extremely brilliant was born of her in the name of Pippalāda. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य मुनिश्रेष्ठो नन्दीश्वरवचोऽद्भुतम् ।
सनत्कुमारः प्रोवाच नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ६॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing these wonderful words of Nandīśvara, the excellent sage Sanatkumāra spoke with palms joined in reverence and shoulders drooping down. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
नन्दीश्वर महाप्राज्ञ साक्षाद् रुद्रस्वरूपधृक् ।
धन्यस्त्वं सद्गुरुस्तात श्रावितेयं कथाद्भुता ॥ ७॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  O Nandiśvara, O intelligent one, you are blessed in that you have assumed the form of Rudra himself. O dear, you are my excellent preceptor. This wonderful story has been narrated by you. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुवेण सह सङ्ग्रामे श्रुतो विष्णुपराजयः ।
ब्रह्मणा मे पुरा तात तच्छापश्च शिलादज ॥ ८॥

English
O son of Silāda, from my father Brahma, the account of the battle with Kṣuva and the defeat of Viṣṇu has already been heard by me. So also the curse has been heard by me. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अधुना श्रोतुमिच्छामि देवशापं सुवर्चया ।
दत्तं पश्चात् पिप्पलादचरितं मङ्गलायनम् ॥ ९॥

English
Now, I was to hear about the curse that Suvarca pronounced on the gods. Afterwards, I wish to hear the auspicious story of Pippalāda. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वाथ शैलादिर्विधिपुत्रवचः शुभम् ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ॥ १०॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing these auspicious words of the son of Brahmā the delighted son of Śilāda remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and replied. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एकदा निर्जराः सर्वे वासवाद्या मुनीश्वर ।
वृत्रासुरसहायैश्च दैत्यैरासन्पराजिताः ॥ ११॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O great sage, once Indra and other gods were defeated by the Daityas who had the support of the demon Vṛtra. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वानि स्वानि वरास्त्राणि दधीचस्याश्रमेऽखिलाः ।
निःक्षिप्य सहसा सद्योऽभवन् देवाः पराजिताः ॥ १२॥

English
All the gods had formerly kept their respective weapons in the hermitage of Dadhīca. Hence they were immediately defeated. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सर्वे सुराः सेन्द्रा वध्यमानास्तथर्षयः ।
ब्रह्मलोकं गताः शीघ्रं प्रोचुः स्वं व्यसनं च तत् ॥ १३॥

English
Then the harassed gods and the sages along with Indra immediately went to Brahmā's region and narrated their distress to him. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा देववचनं ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ।
सर्वं शशंस तत्त्वेन त्वष्टुश्चैव चिकीर्षितम् ॥ १४॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods Brahmā, the grandfather of the worlds, mentioned the details of what Tvaṣṭṛ was desirous of doing. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रयत्नः क्रियतां भवेदस्य वधो यथा ।
तत्रोपायं श‍ृणु प्राज्ञ धर्महेतोर्वदामि ते ॥ १६॥

English
You shall make such efforts as would ensure his death. O intelligent one, listen to the means thereof. For the sake of virtue I shall tell you the same. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
महामुनिर्दधीचिर्यः स तपस्वी जितेन्द्रियः ।
लेभे शिवं समाराध्य वज्रास्थित्ववरं पुरा ॥ १७॥

English
There is a great sage Dadhīci of great penance and of full control of his sense-organs. After propitiating Śiva he secured the boon of adamantine bones formerly. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यास्थीन्येव याचध्वं स दास्यति न संशयः ।
निर्माय तैर्दण्डवज्रं वृत्रं जहि न संशयः ॥ १८॥

English
You all request for his bones. He will undoubtedly give them to you. Make the thunderbolt staff therewith and kill Vṛtra without hesitation. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा ब्रह्मवचनं शक्रो गुरुसमन्वितः ।
आगच्छत्सामरः सद्यो दधीच्याश्रममुत्तमम् ॥ १९॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing the words of Brahmā, Indra accompanied by his preceptor and other gods went immediately to the excellent hermitage of Dadhīci. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तत्र मुनिं शक्रः सुवर्चान्वितमादरात् ।
ननाम साञ्जलिर्नम्रः सगुरुः सामरश्च तम् ॥ २०॥

English
There Indra saw the sage in the company of Suvarcā. Along with the preceptor and other gods he humbly and respectfully bowed to him with palms joined in reverence. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तदभिप्रायमाज्ञाय स मुनिर्बुधसत्तमः ।
स्वपत्नीं प्रेषयामास सुवर्चां स्वाश्रमान्तरम् ॥ २१॥

English
On realising his intention the sage, the most excellent of scholars sent his wife Suvarcā to the inner apartments of the hermitage. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्स देवराजश्च सामरः स्वार्थसाधकः ।
अर्थशास्त्रपरो भूत्वा मुनीशं वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ २२॥

English
Then the king of gods, accompanied by the gods bent upon achieving his own object, and most efficient in polity spoke to the great sage. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्र उवाच ।
त्वष्ट्रा विप्रकृताः सर्वे वयं देवास्तथर्षयः ।
शरण्यं त्वां महाशैवं दातारं शरणं गताः ॥ २३॥

English
Indra said :  All of us, the gods and the sages are oppressed by Tvaštr. We seek refuge in you, worthy of refuge, a great devotee of Šiva and a liberal donor. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वास्थीनि देहि नो विप्र महावज्रमयानि हि ।
अस्थ्ना ते स्वपविं कृत्वा हनिष्यामि सुरद्रुहम् ॥ २४॥

English
O brahmin, give us your bones of adamantine strength. I shall make my thunderbolt with your love and kill the demon. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तस्तेन स मुनिः परोपकरणे रतः ।
ध्यात्वा शिवं स्वनाथं हि विससर्ज कलेवरम् ॥ २५॥

English
Thus requested, the sage fond of helping others meditated on lord Siva and abandoned his body. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मलोकं गतः सद्यः स मुनिर्ध्वस्तबन्धनः ।
पुष्पवृष्टिरभूत्तत्र सर्वे विस्मयमागताः ॥ २६॥

English
Immediately he reached Brahma's region with his bondage of actions quashed. There was a shower of flowers and the people were agreeably surprised. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ गां सुरभिं शक्र आहूयाशु ह्यलेहयत् ।
अस्त्रनिर्मितये त्वाष्ट्रं निर्दिदेश तदस्थिभिः ॥ २७॥

English
Indra then called the divine cow Surabhi to lick up the marrow etc. He directed Visvakarman to make the weapon with the bones. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वकर्मा तदाज्ञप्तश्चक्लृपेऽस्त्राणि कृत्स्नशः ।
तदस्थिभिर्वज्रमयैः सुदृढैः शिववर्चसा ॥ २८॥

English
At his behest Visvakarman made all the weapons with the sage's bones—very firm and adamantine—that had the brilliance of Siva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य वंशोद्भवैर्वज्रं शरो ब्रह्मशिरस्तथा ।
अन्यास्थिभिर्बहूनि स्वपराण्यस्त्राणि निर्ममे ॥ २९॥

English
With his backbone he made the thunderbolt, arrow and the missile called Brahmasiras. With the other bones he made many other weapons. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तमिन्द्रो वज्रमुद्यम्य वर्द्धितः शिववर्चसा ।
वृत्रमभ्यद्रवत्क्रुद्धो मुने रुद्र इवान्तकम् ॥ ३०॥

English
O sage, flourishing with Siva's brilliance, the infuriated Indra lifted the thunderbolt and rushed against Vrtra like Rudra rushing against the god of death. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शक्रः सुसन्नद्धस्तेन वज्रेण स द्रुतम् ।
उच्चकर्त शिरो वार्त्रं गिरिश‍ृङ्गमिवौजसा ॥ ३१॥

English
Indra who was well prepared, cut off the peak-like head of Vrtra with the thunderbolt forcefully. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा समुत्सवस्तात बभूव त्रिदिवौकसाम् ।
तुष्टुवुर्निर्जराः शक्रं पेतुः कुसुमवृष्टयः ॥ ३२॥

English
There was great jubilation among the heaven-dwellers. O dear, the gods eulogised Indra. Showers of flowers fell on him. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कथितं तात प्रसङ्गाच्चरितं त्विदम् ।
पिप्पलादावतारं मे श‍ृणु शम्भोर्महादरात् ॥ ३३॥

English
O dear, thus this story of Vrtra's slaughter is related to you as arising from the context. Now listen respectfully to the incarnation of Siva as Pippalāda. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्चा सा मुनेः पत्नी दधीचस्य महात्मनः ।
ययौ स्वमाश्रमाभ्यन्तस्तदाज्ञप्ता पतिव्रता ॥ ३४॥

English
At the behest of her lord Suvarca the chaste wife of the sage Dadhici, the noble soul went to the inner apartments of the hermitage. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्य तत्र सा दृष्ट्वा न पतिं स्वं तपस्विनी ।
गृहकार्यं च सा कृत्वाखिलं पतिनिदेशतः ॥ ३५॥

English
At the behest of her husband she finished her household duties and came there. The pitiable lady could not see her lord there. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
आजगाम पुनस्तत्र पश्यन्ती बह्वशोभनम् ।
देवांश्च तान्मुनिश्रेष्ठ सुवर्चा विस्मिताभवत् ॥ ३६॥

English
After coming there and seeing the most inauspicious occurrence there, O excellent sage, the surprised Suvarca spoke to the gods. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा च तत्सर्वमिदं सुराणां
कृत्यं तदानीं च चुकोप साध्वी ।
ददौ तदा शापमतीव रुष्टा
तेषां सुवर्चा ऋषिवर्यभार्या ॥ ३७॥

English
The chaste lady, on realising that all this was the action of the gods, became angry. The infuriated wife of the excellent sage, Suvarca, cursed them. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्चोवाच ।
अहो सुरा द्रुष्टतराश्च सर्वे
स्वकार्यदक्षा ह्यबुधाश्च लुब्धाः ।
तस्माच्च सर्वे पशवो भवन्तु
सेन्द्राश्च मेऽद्यप्रभृतीत्युवाच ॥ ३८॥

English
Suvarcā said :—  "Ah! the gods are very wicked. They are covetous, stupid but very skilful in manipulating their own affairs. Let them all, inclusive of Indra, become beasts from today." (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शापं ददौ तेषां सुराणां सा तपस्विनी ।
सशक्राणां च सर्वेषां सुवर्चा मुनिकामिनी ॥ ३९॥

English
Suvarca, a woman saint and the beloved wife of the sage cursed the gods inclusive of Indra thus. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुगन्तुं पतेर्लोकमथेच्छत्सा पतिव्रता ।
चितां चक्रे समेधोभिः सुपवित्रैर्मनस्विनी ॥ ४०॥

English
That chaste lady desired to go to the worlds of her husband. With the sacred twigs, the noble lady prepared the funeral pyre. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो नभोगिरा प्राह सुवर्चां तां मुनिप्रियाम् ।
आश्वासयन्ती गिरिशप्रेरिता सुखदायिनी ॥ ४१॥

English
Then a pleasant celestial voice urged by Siva spoke to Suvarca, consoling her. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशवाण्युवाच ।
साहसं न कुरु प्राज्ञे श‍ृणु मे परमं वचः ।
मुनितेजस्त्वदुदरे तदुत्पादय यत्नतः ॥ ४२॥

English
The Celestial Voice said :—  O intelligent lady, do not carry out this calamitous task. Listen to my weighty words. The semen of the sage is in your womb. Develop it carefully. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्वाभीष्टचरणं देवि कर्तुं त्वमर्हसि ।
सगर्भा न दहेद् गात्रमिति ब्रह्मनिदेशनम् ॥ ४३॥

English
O gentle lady, later on, you may do as you please. It is the command of Brahma that a pregnant woman should not burn herself. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा सा नभोवाणी विरराम मुनीश्वर ।
तां श्रुत्वा सा मुनेः पत्नी विस्मिताभूत्क्षणं च सा ॥ ४४॥

English
Nandisvara said:—  O great sage, after saying this the celestial voice stopped. On hearing that voice the wife of the sage was surprised for a moment. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्चा सा महासाध्वी पतिलोकमभीप्सती ।
उपविश्याश्मना भूयः सोदरं विददार ह ॥ ४५॥

English
That great chaste lady, Suvarca, desirous of attaining the world of her husband sat down and broke open her belly with a stone. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गतो जठरात्तस्या गर्भो मुनिवरस्य सः ।
महादिव्यतनुर्दीप्तो भासयंश्च दिशो दश ॥ ४६॥

English
The foetus of the child of the great sage, of brilliant divine body, came out of her belly illuminating all the ten quarters. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
साक्षाद् रुद्रावतारोऽसौ दधीचिवरतेजसः ।
प्रादुर्भूतः स्वयं तात स्वलीलाकरणे क्षमः ॥ ४७॥

English
O dear, it was the incarnation of Rudra himself born of the sage Dadhīci. He was competent to perform his divine sports. He therefore manifested himself. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा स्वसुतं दिव्यं स्वरूपं मुनिकामिनी ।
सुवर्चाज्ञाय मनसा साक्षाद् रुद्रावतारकम् ॥ ४८॥

प्रहृष्टाभून्महासाध्वी प्रणम्याशु नुनाव सा ।
स्वहृदि स्थापयामास तत्स्वरूपं मुनीश्वर ॥ ४९॥

English
On seeing her son of divine form, the beloved wife of the sage Suvarcā realised in her mind that it was the incarnation of Rudra himself. The great chaste lady became delighted, bowed to him and eulogised him. O great sage, that form of the child was impressed on her heart. (48-49.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्चा तनयं तं च प्रहस्य विमलेक्षणा ।
जननी प्राह सुप्रीत्या पतिलोकमभीप्सती ॥ ५०॥

English
Suvarcā of pure vision, the mother, desirous of going to her husband's region, smilingly and lovingly spoke to her son. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्चोवाच ।
हे तात परमेशान चिरं तिष्ठास्य सन्निधौ ।
अश्वत्थस्य महाभाग सर्वेषां सुखदो भवेः ॥ ५१॥

English
Suvarcā said:—  O dear, O lord Śiva, O fortunate one, stay near this holy fig tree for a long time. Be the bestower of happiness to all. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
मामाज्ञापय सुप्रीत्या पतिलोकाय चाधुना ।
तत्रस्थाहं च पतिना त्वां ध्याये रुद्ररूपिणम् ॥ ५२॥

English
Out of affection allow me to go to the region of my husband. Staying there along with my husband I shall meditate on you in the form of Rudra. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्येवं सा बभाषेऽथ सुवर्चा तनयं प्रति ।
पतिमन्वगमत्साध्वी परमेण समाधिना ॥ ५३॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  After speaking thus to her son that chaste lady Suvarcā followed her husband by means of the great transcendental meditation. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं दधीचपत्नी सा पतिना सङ्गता मुने ।
शिवलोकं समासाद्य सिषेवे शङ्करं मुदा ॥ ५४॥

English
O sage, thus the wife of Dadhīci was re-united with her husband. After reaching Śiva's region she served Śiva devotedly. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवाः सेन्द्राश्च मुनिभिः सह ।
तत्राजग्मुस्त्वरा तात आहूता इव हर्षिताः ॥ ५५॥

English
In the meantime the delighted gods inclusive of Indra, hurriedly came there along with the sages as though invited there. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिर्ब्रह्मा च सुप्रीत्यावतीर्णं शङ्करं भुवि ।
सुवर्चायां दधीचाद्वा ययतुः स्वगणैः सह ॥ ५६॥

English
Viṣṇu and Brahmā too, accompanied by their attendants came over there to see Śiva who had taken incarnation in the world through Suvarcā and Dadhīci. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र दृष्ट्वावतीर्णं तं मुनिपुत्रत्वमागतम् ।
रुद्रं सर्वे प्रणेमुश्च तुष्टुवुर्बद्धपाणयः ॥ ५७॥

English
On seeing him there, incarnated as the sage's son, they bowed to him with palms joined in reverence and made obeisances. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोत्सवो महानासीद्देवानां मुनिसत्तम ।
नेदुर्दुन्दुभयस्तत्र नर्तक्यो ननृतुर्मुदा ॥ ५८॥

English
O excellent sage, there was great jubilation among the gods. The Dundubhis were sounded and dancing girls danced joyously. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
जगुर्गन्धर्वपुत्राश्च किन्नरा वाद्यवादकाः ।
वादयामासुरमराः पुष्पवृष्टिं च चक्रिरे ॥ ५९॥

English
The sons of Gandharvas sang. Kinnaras and other players played on instruments. The gods showered flowers. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीचेः पिप्पलपितुर्विलसन्तं सुतं च तम् ।
संस्कृत्य विधिवत्सर्वे विष्ण्वाद्यास्तुष्टुवुः पुनः ॥ ६०॥

English
After consecrating the refulgent child and the fig tree who became the father of Rudra, Visnu and other gods eulogised him again. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
पिप्पलादेति तन्नाम चक्रे ब्रह्मा प्रसन्नधीः ।
प्रसन्नो भव देवेश इत्यूचे हरिणा सुरैः ॥ ६१॥

English
The delighted Brahma named him Pippalāda. Visnu and the gods said "O lord of the gods be pleased". (61.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा तमनुज्ञाय ब्रह्मा विष्णुः सुरास्तथा ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुः सर्वे विधाय च महोत्सवम् ॥ ६२॥

English
After saying this and making great jubilation, Brahma, Visnu and the gods went to their respective abodes. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ रुद्रः पिप्पलादोऽश्वत्थमूले महाप्रभुः ।
तताप सुचिरं कालं लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ६३॥

English
Then Rudra, the great lord in the form of Pippalāda, performed a penance for a long time at the foot of the fig tree, with a desire to bestow benefit on the worlds. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं सुतपतस्तस्य पिप्पलादस्य सम्मुखे ।
महाकालो व्यतीयाय लोकचर्यानुसारिणः ॥ ६४॥

English
As Pippalāda, following the convention of the world, performed the penance, a long time passed by. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
भवद्वधार्थं जनितस्त्वष्ट्रायं तपसा सुराः ।
वृत्रो नाम महातेजाः सर्वदैत्याधिपो महान् ॥ १५॥
