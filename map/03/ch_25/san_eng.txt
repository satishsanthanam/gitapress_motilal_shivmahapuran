### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - (The story of Pippalāda incarnation)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अथ लोके व्यवस्थाय धर्मस्य स्थापनेच्छया ।
महालीलां चकारेशस्तामहो सन्मुने श‍ृणु ॥ १॥

English
Nandisvara said :—  After arranging and establishing Dharma with a desire for its good foundation, lord Siva performed great sports. O good sage, listen to that. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा पुष्पभद्रायां स्नातुं गच्छन्मुनीश्वरः ।
ददर्श पद्मां युवतीं शिवांशां सुमनोहराम् ॥ २॥

English
Once while going to the river Puspabhadrā$^{67}$ for his ablution, the great sage saw the beautiful youthful maiden Padma, a part of Siva herself. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्लिप्सुस्तत्पितुः स्थानमनरण्यस्य भूपतेः ।
जगाम भुवनाचारी लोकतत्त्वविचक्षणः ॥ ३॥

English
Desirous of getting her, the sage traversing the world and an expert in following the established conventions went to the abode of king Anaranya, her father. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा नराणां तं दृष्ट्वा प्रणम्य च भयाकुलः ।
मधुपर्कादिकं दत्त्वा पूजयामास भक्तितः ॥ ४॥

English
On seeing him, the awe-struck king of men bowed to him. Offering him Madhuparka he worshipped him devotedly. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नेहात्सर्वं गृहीत्वा स ययाचे कन्यकां मुनिः ।
मौनी बभूव नृपतिः किञ्चिन्निर्वक्तुमक्षमः ॥ ५॥

English
The sage accepted everything with regard and requested for the hand of his daughter. Thereupon the king was unable to say anything. He was silent. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनिः प्रोवाच नृपतिं कन्यां मे देहि भक्तितः ।
अन्यथा भस्मसात्सर्वं करिष्येऽहं त्वया सह ॥ ६॥

English
The sage repeated the request, saying "Give me your daughter devoutly. Otherwise I will reduce everything to ashes along with you." (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो बभूवुराच्छन्नाः सर्वे राजजनास्तदा ।
तेजसा पिप्पलादस्य दाधीचस्य महामुने ॥ ७॥

English
O great sage, then all the people of the king were enveloped by the brilliance of Pippalāda, son of Dadhici. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ राजा महाभीतो विलप्य च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
कन्यामलङ्कृतां पद्मां वृद्धाय मुनये ददौ ॥ ८॥

English
Then the king was much frightened and he lamented again and again. He gave his daughter Padma, fully bedecked in ornaments to the old sage. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
पद्मां विवाह्य स मुनिः शिवांशाम्भूपतेः सुताम् ।
पिप्पलादो गृहीत्वा तां मुदितः स्वाश्रमं ययौ ॥ ९॥

English
After marrying Padma, the daughter of the king and a part of Siva, the sage Pippalada joyously took her to his hermitage. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा मुनिवरो वयसा जर्जरोऽधिकः ।
उवास नार्या स तया तपस्वी नातिलम्पटः ॥ १०॥

English
After going to the hermitage the aged excellent sage stayed there with that woman. The sage was not too indulgent. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोऽनरण्यकन्या सा सिषेवे भक्तितो मुनिम् ।
कर्मणा मनसा वाचा लक्ष्मीर्नारायणं यथा ॥ ११॥

English
Then the daughter of Anaranya served the sage with devotion, in mind, word and deed, like Laksmī serving Visnu. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं स पिप्पलादो हि शिवांशो मुनिसत्तमः ।
रेमे तया युवत्या च युवाभूय स्वलीलया ॥ १२॥

English
Then the excellent sage Pippalāda, a part of Śiva, became a young man by means of his divine sport and sported with that young lady. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
दश पुत्रा महात्मानो बभूवुः सुतपस्विनः ।
मुनेः पितुः समाः सर्वे पद्मायाः सुखवर्द्धनाः ॥ १३॥

English
Ten noble sons, all of them great sons, were born to the sage. They were equal to their father and increased the pleasure of Padma. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं लीलावतारो हि शङ्करस्य महाप्रभोः ।
पिप्पलादो मुनिवरो नानालीलाकरः प्रभुः ॥ १४॥

English
This is the sportive incarnation of Siva the great lord. The excellent sage Pippalada was the lord indulgent in different kinds of divine sports. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
येन दत्तो वरः प्रीत्या लोकेभ्यो हि दयालुना ।
दृष्ट्वा लोके शनेः पीडां सर्वेषामनिवारिणीम् ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing the harassment by the planet Saturn of every one irremediably, the sympathetic lord lovingly granted a boon to all the world. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
षोडशाब्दावधि नृणां जन्मतो न भवेच्च सा ।
तथा च शिवभक्तानां सत्यमेतद्धि मे वचः ॥ १६॥

English
"For sixteen years from birth no man will have that affliction. Similarly to the devotees of Siva too. This word of mine is true. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अथानादृत्य मद्वाक्यं कुर्यात्पीडां शनिः क्वचित् ।
तेषां नृणां तदा स स्याद्भस्मसान्न हि संशयः ॥ १७॥

English
If Saturn were to disregard my instruction and harass men, he will undoubtedly be reduced to ashes." (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इति तद्भयतस्तात विकृतोऽपि शनैश्चरः ।
तेषां न कुरुते पीडां कदाचिद् ग्रहसत्तमः ॥ १८॥

English
O dear, thus, afraid of him the excellent planet Saturn, though in aberration, does not afflict men at anytime. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
इति लीलामनुष्यस्य पिप्पलादस्य सन्मुने ।
कथितं सुचरित्रं ते सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ १९॥

English
Thus I have narrated to you the good story of the great sage Pippalāda who had assumed the form of a human being in the course of his divine sports. The story yields the fruit of all desires. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
गाधिश्च कौशिकश्चैव पिप्पलादो महामुनिः ।
शनैश्चरकृतां पीडां नाशयन्ति स्मृतास्त्रयः ॥ २०॥

English
These three—Gādhi, Kauśika and the great sage Pippalāda quell the affliction of Śani on being remembered. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पिप्पलादस्य चरितं पद्माचरितसंयुतम् ।
यः पठेच्छृणुयाद्वापि सुभक्त्या भुवि मानवः ॥ २१॥

शनिपीडाविनाशार्थमेतच्चरितमुत्तमम् ।
यः पठेच्छृणुयाद्वापि सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ २२॥

English
For the dissolution of the affliction of Saturn, the man who reads or listens devoutly to the story of Pippalāda along with that of Padmā attains all desires in this world. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्यो मुनिवरो ज्ञानी महाशैवः सतां प्रियः ।
अस्य पुत्रो महेशानः पिप्पलादाख्य आत्मवान् ॥ २३॥

English
Blessed indeed is the great sage, who was wise and a great devotee of Śiva. He was a favourite of good men. Lord Śiva named Pippalāda was his noble son. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं स्वर्ग्यं कुग्रहदोषहृत् ।
सर्वकामप्रदं तात शिवभक्तिविवर्द्धनम् ॥ २४॥

English
This pious narrative is conducive to the attainment of heaven. It quells the adverse effects of the evil planets. O dear, it yields all desires and enhances devotion to Śiva. (24.)

---
