### **CHAPTER TWENTYSIX - (*Śiva's incarnation as Vaiśyanātha*)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु तात प्रवक्ष्यामि शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अवतारं परानन्दं वैश्यनाथाह्वयं मुने ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said:*—  O dear sage, listen to me. I shall tell you about Vaiśyanātha, the incarnation of Śiva, the supreme soul that yields great bliss. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दिग्रामे पुरा काचिन्महानन्देति विश्रुता ।
बभूव वारवनिता शिवभक्ता सुसुन्दरी ॥ २॥

English
Formerly, in Nandigrāma there was a prostitute known as Mahānandā. She was very beautiful and a devotee of Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
महाविभवसम्पन्ना सुधनाढ्या महोज्ज्वला ।
नानारत्नपरिच्छिन्नश‍ृङ्गाररसनिर्भरा ॥ ३॥

English
She was very prosperous, rich and brilliant. She was bedecked in gems of different kinds. She was voluptuous in love. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वसङ्गीतविद्यासु निपुणातिमनोहरा ।
तस्या गेयेन हृष्यन्ति राज्ञ्यो राजान एव च ॥ ४॥

English
She was an adept in all lores of music. She was delightful to all. Queens and kings were delighted by her songs. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
समानर्च सदा साम्बं सा वेश्या शङ्करं मुदा ।
शिवनामजपासक्ता भस्मरुद्राक्षभूषणा ॥ ५॥

English
She always worshipped Siva and Parvati with great joy. She was interested in recounting the names of Siva. She used to wear ashes and Rudraksa. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवं सम्पूज्य सा नित्यं सेवन्ती जगदीश्वरम् ।
ननर्त परया भक्त्या गायन्ती शिवसद्यशः ॥ ६॥

English
After worshipping Siva, the lord of the universe she used to dance with great devotion and sing the great glory of Siva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्राक्षैर्भूषयित्वैकं मर्कटं चैव कुक्कुटम् ।
करतालैश्च गीतैश्च सदा नर्तयति स्म सा ॥ ७॥

English
She kept a monkey and a cock which she decorated with Rudrakas. She made them dance singing herself and clapping her hands. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
नृत्यमानौ च तौ दृष्ट्वा शिवभक्तिरता च सा ।
वेश्या स्म विहसत्युच्चैः प्रेम्णा सर्वसखीयुता ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing them dance, she, engrossed in devotion to Siva, used to laugh lovingly along with her friends and attendants. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्राक्षैः कृतकेयूरकर्णाभरणमण्डना ।
मर्कटः शिक्षया तस्याः पुरो नृत्यति बालवत् ॥ ९॥

English
The monkey, as a result of her instruction, danced before her, like a boy bedecked in Rudrakas as ear-rings, and shoulderlets. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिखासम्बद्धरुद्राक्षः कुक्कुटः कपिना सह ।
नित्यं ननर्त नृत्यज्ञः पश्यतां हितमावहन् ॥ १०॥

English
The cock too was bedecked in Rudraksa tied round its tuft. It too danced along with the monkey delighting the onlookers. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सा कुर्वती वेश्या कौतुकं परमादरात् ।
शिवभक्तिरता नित्यं महानन्दभराभवत् ॥ ११॥

English
Thus that prostitute indulging in these delightful pranks and pursuing devotional service to Siva was very happy always. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवभक्तिं प्रकुर्वन्त्या वेश्याया मुनिसत्तम ।
बहुकालो व्यतीयाय तस्याः परमसौख्यतः ॥ १२॥

English
O excellent sage, a long time thus elapsed when she was quite happy performing her devotional service to Siva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा च गृहे तस्या वैश्यो भूत्वा शिवः स्वयम् ।
परीक्षितुं च तद्भावमाजगाम शुभो व्रती ॥ १३॥

English
Once Siva assumed the form of a merchant and visited her house to test her. He had assumed the auspicious form of a devotee. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिपुण्ड्रविलसद्भालो रुद्राक्षाभरणः कृती ।
शिवनामजपासक्तो जटिलः शैववेषभृत् ॥ १४॥

English
His forehead was marked with Tripundra. He wore Rudraksa as ornaments. He was interested in recounting the names of Siva. He was clad as a devotee of Siva. He wore matted locks of hair. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स बिभ्रद्भस्मनिचयं प्रकोष्ठे वरकङ्कणम् ।
महारत्नपरिस्तीर्णं राजते परकौतुकी ॥ १५॥

English
He had smeared ashes all over his body. Round his wrist was tied an excellent bangle set with gems. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तमागतं सुसम्पूज्य सा वेश्या परया मुदा ।
स्वस्थाने सादरं वैश्यं सुन्दरी हि न्यवेशयत् ॥ १६॥

English
The beautiful prostitute worshipped and welcomed the visitor with great joy. She made him sit with great respect. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्रकोष्ठे वरं वीक्ष्य कङ्कणं सुमनोहरम् ।
तस्मिञ्जातस्पृहा सा च तं प्रोवाच सुविस्मिता ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing the excellent bangle round his wrist she coveted the same. In great surprise she said. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
महानन्दोवाच ।
महारत्नमयश्चायं कङ्कणस्त्वत्करे स्थितः ।
मनो हरति मे सद्यो दिव्यस्त्रीभूषणोचितः ॥ १८॥

English
Mahānandā said :—  This bangle set with great gems around your arm captivates my mind. It is worthy of being an ornament of a celestial damsel. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति तां नवरत्नाढ्ये सस्पृहां करभूषणे ।
वीक्ष्योदारमतिर्वैश्यः सस्मितं समभाषत ॥ १९॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On seeing that she wished for the bangle set with nine gems, the liberal-minded merchant spoke smilingly. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
वैश्यनाथ उवाच ।
अस्मिन् रत्नवरे दिव्ये सस्पृहं यदि ते मनः ।
त्वमेवाधत्स्व सुप्रीत्या मौल्यमस्य ददासि किम् ॥ २०॥

English
Vaiśyanātha said :—  If your mind wishes for this excellent gemset ornament you can have it with pleasure. But what is the price you are willing to pay? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
वेश्योवाच ।
वयं हि स्वैरचारिण्यो वेश्यास्तु न पतिव्रताः ।
अस्मत्कुलोचितो धर्मो व्यभिचारो न संशयः ॥ २१॥

English
The prostitute said :—  We are prostitutes flirting here and there and not chaste ladies. Undoubtedly the regular practice observed in our family is harlotry. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्येतदखिलं चित्तं गृह्णाति करभूषणम् ।
दिनत्रयमहोरात्रं पत्नी तव भवाम्यहम् ॥ २२॥

English
If I take the bangle from you I shall be your wife for three full days and nights. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वैश्य उवाच ।
तथास्तु यदि ते सत्यं वचनं वीरवल्लभे ।
ददामि रत्नवलयं त्रिरात्रं भव मे वधूः ॥ २३॥

English
The merchant said :—  O beloved harlot, if your words are true, let it be as you say. I shall give you the gemset bangle. Be my wife for three days and nights. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्व्यवहारे तु प्रमाणं शशिभास्करौ ।
त्रिवारं सत्यमित्युक्त्वा हृदयं मे स्पृश प्रिये ॥ २४॥

English
In this deal the witnesses are the moon and the sun. Repeat three times "True" and touch my heart, O beloved. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तम्भेन सह निर्दग्धं तल्लिङ्गं शकलीकृतम् ।
दृष्ट्वा वेश्या स वैश्यश्च दुरन्तं दुःखमापतुः ॥ ३५॥

English
On seeing the phallic image burnt along with the pillar and smashed utterly, the prostitute and the merchant felt endless distress. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा हि महानन्दा त्रिवारं शशिभास्करौ ।
प्रमाणीकृत्य सुप्रीत्या सा तद् हृदयमस्पृशत् ॥ २६॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After repeating this for three times, with the moon and the sun as witnesses, Mahānanda touched his heart with pleasure. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्यै स वैश्यस्तु प्रदत्त्वा रत्नकङ्कणम् ।
लिङ्गं रत्नमयं तस्य हस्ते दत्त्वेदमब्रवीत् ॥ २७॥

English
After giving her the gemset bangle that merchant handed to her a gemset phallic image and spoke thus. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
वैश्यनाथ उवाच ।
इदं रत्नमयं लिङ्गं शैवं मत्प्राणदवल्लभम् ।
रक्षणीयं त्वया कान्ते गोपनीयं प्रयत्नतः ॥ २८॥

English
The merchant said :—  O my beloved, this gemset phallic image is very dear to me. It shall be well-preserved strenuously and in secret by you. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमस्त्विति सा प्रोच्य लिङ्गमादाय रत्नजम् ।
नाट्यमण्डपिकामध्ये निधाय प्राविशद् गृहम् ॥ २९॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  She said “Let it be so” and took the gemset phallic image. She kept it in the middle of the dancing platform and entered the house. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सा तेन सङ्गता रात्रौ वैश्येन विटधर्मिणा ।
सुखं सुष्वाप पर्यङ्के मृदुतल्पोपशोभिते ॥ ३०॥

English
During the night she had her sexual union with the merchant who behaved like a libertine. She then went to sleep on the couch brilliant with a soft bed. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निशीथसमये मुने वैश्यपतीच्छया ।
अकस्मादुत्थिता वाणी नृत्यमण्डपिकान्तरे ॥ ३१॥

English
O sage, then at midnight, at the will of the lordly merchant, suddenly there arose a fire$^{70}$ in the middle of the dancing platform. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
महाप्रज्वलितो वह्निः सुसमीरसहायवान् ।
नाट्यमण्डपिकां तात तामेव सहसावृणोत् ॥ ३२॥

English
The blazing fire helped by a forceful wind enveloped the dancing platform all of a sudden. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्डपे दह्यमाने तु सहसोत्थाय सम्भ्रमात् ।
मर्कटं मोचयामास सा वेश्या तत्र बन्धनात् ॥ ३३॥

English
When the platform was burning the prostitute got up suddenly in her bewilderment and let the monkey free untying it. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
स मर्कटो मुक्तबन्धः कुक्कुटेन सहामुना ।
भिया दूरं हि दुद्राव विधूयाग्निकणान्बहून् ॥ ३४॥

English
Freed from the bondage the monkey in its fear ran here and there along with the cock and scattered sparks of fire. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा ह्यात्मसमं लिङ्गं दग्धं वैश्यपतिस्तदा ।
ज्ञातुं तद्भावमन्तःस्थं मरणाय मतिं दधे ॥ ३६॥

English
On seeing the phallic image as it were his very soul burnt, the lordly merchant desired to end his life in order to know her mentality. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
निविश्येऽतितरां खेदाद्वैश्यस्तामाह दुःखिताम् ।
नानालीलो महेशानः कौतुकान्नरदेहवान् ॥ ३७॥

English
In great despair and sorrow the merchant told her in grief. Lord Śiva who had assumed a human body just for pleasure indulged in different sports. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
वैश्यपतिरुवाच ।
शिवलिङ्गे तु निर्भिन्ने दग्धे महत्प्राणवल्लभे ।
सत्यं वच्मि न सन्देहो नाहं जीवितुमुत्सहे ॥ ३८॥

English
“O my beloved, when the phallic image is smashed and burnt I do not desire to live. I am telling you the truth. There is no doubt in this. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
चितां कारय मे भद्रे स्वभृत्यैस्त्वं वरैर्लघु ।
शिवे मनः समावेश्य प्रवेक्ष्यामि हुताशनम् ॥ ३९॥

English
O gentle lady, prepare a pyre for me immediately through your servants. With my mind fixed in Śiva, I shall enter the fire. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि ब्रह्मेन्द्रविष्ण्वाद्या वारयेयुः समेत्य माम् ।
तथाप्यस्मिन् क्षणे भद्रे प्रविशामि त्यजाम्यसून् ॥ ४०॥

English
Even if Brahmā, Indra, Viṣṇu and others were to prevent me, O gentle lady, I shall enter the fire this moment and shall forsake my life." (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तमेवं दृढनिर्बन्धं सा विज्ञाय सुदुःखिता ।
स्वभृत्यैः कारयामास चितां स्वभवनाद् बहिः ॥ ४१॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On realising his tenacious decision the harlot was much distressed. Outside her house she caused the pyre to be made through her servants. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स वैश्यः शिव एक एव
प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य समिद्धमग्निम् ।
विवेश पश्यत्सु नरेषु धीरः
सुकौतुकी सङ्गतिभावमिच्छुः ॥ ४२॥

English
Then the merchant who was only Siva himself went round the blazing fire. Desirous of knowing her intentions he entered the fire firmly. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा सा तद्गतिं वेश्या महानन्दातिविस्मिता ।
अनुतापं च युवती प्रपेदे मुनिसत्तम ॥ ४३॥

English
O excellent sage, on seeing him enter the pyre, the harlot was dismayed. The youthful maiden sympathised with him. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा दुःखिता वेश्या स्मृत्वा धर्म सुनिर्मलम् ।
सर्वान्बन्धुजनान्वीक्ष्य बभाषे करुणं वचः ॥ ४४॥

English
The miserable harlot remembered the sacred vow and said piteously glancing at all her kinsmen. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
महानन्दोवाच ।
रत्नकङ्कणमादाय मया सत्यमुदाहृतम् ।
दिनत्रयमहं पत्नी वैश्यस्यामुष्य सम्मता ॥ ४५॥

English
Mahānandā said :— Taking the gemset bangle I had taken the pledge that I shall be the avowed wife of this merchant for three days.
46 This merchant, the avowed Saivite, died of my activities. Hence I shall enter fire along with him. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वधर्मचारिणीत्युक्तमाचार्यैः सत्यवादिभिः ।
एवं कृते मम प्रीत्या सत्यं मयि न नश्यतु ॥ ४७॥

English
Truthful preceptors have ordained that a wife shall observe her duty. If I do likewise my truthfulness will not be marred. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्याश्रयः परो धर्मः सत्येन परमा गतिः ।
सत्येन स्वर्गमोक्षौ च सत्ये सर्वं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Abiding by truth is a great virtue. Great goal can be attained through truth. Heaven and liberation can be achieved through truth. Everything is founded on truth. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति सा दृढनिर्बन्धा वार्यमाणापि बन्धुभिः ।
सत्यलोपभिया नारी प्राणांस्त्यक्तुं मनो दधे ॥ ४९॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Although prevented by her kinsmen the harlot was obstinate in her resolve. The woman eager in the attainment of Satyaloka decided on giving up her life. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्वं द्विजमुख्येभ्यो दत्त्वा ध्यात्वा सदाशिवम् ।
तमग्निं त्रिःपरिक्रम्य प्रवेशाभिमुखी ह्यभूत् ॥ ५०॥

English
She gifted away her property to leading brahmins. She meditated on Sadasiva. She circumambulated the fire thrice and stood ready to enter it. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

वारयामास विश्वात्मा प्रादुर्भूतः स वै शिवः ॥ ५१॥

English
As she was going to jump in the fire with her mind fixed in his feet, Śiva, the soul of the universe, appeared in front and stopped her. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

त्रिलोचनं चन्द्रकलावतंसम् ।
शशाङ्कसूर्यानलकोटिभासं
स्तब्धेव भीतेव तथैव तस्थौ ॥ ५२॥

English
On seeing the three-eyed lord of the gods, decorated by the moon's digit, and as brilliant as crores of moons, suns and fires she stood as if stunned and frightened. (52.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The lord caught hold of the hands of the frightened and agitated lady, stunned, trembling and shedding tears. He consoled her and spoke. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।

परीक्षितुं त्वत्सकाशं वैश्यो भूत्वाहमागतः ॥ ५४॥

English
Śiva said :—  It is to test your truthfulness, piety, courage and steady devotion to me that I came near you assuming the form of a merchant. (54.)

Sanskrit
माययाग्निं समुद्दीप्य दग्धं ते नाट्यमण्डपम् ।
दग्धं कृत्वा रत्नलिङ्गं प्रविष्टोऽहं हुताशनम् ॥ ५५॥

English
With my magic I produced the blazing fire and burnt your dancing platform. After burning the gemset phallic image I entered fire myself. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
सा त्वं सत्यमनुस्मृत्य प्रविष्टाग्निं मया सहा ।
अतस्ते सम्प्रदास्यामि भोगांस्त्रिदशदुर्लभान् ॥ ५६॥

English
But you remembered the promise and entered the fire along with me. Hence I shall give you such pleasures as are inaccessible to gods. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यदिच्छसि सुश्रोणि तदेव हि ददामि ते ।
त्वद्भक्त्याहं प्रसन्नोऽस्मि तवादेयं न विद्यते ॥ ५७॥

English
O beautiful lady of good hips, whatever you wish I shall give you. I am delighted by your devotion. There is nothing which cannot be given to you. (57.)

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति ब्रुवति गौरीशे शङ्करे भक्तवत्सले ।
महानन्दा च सा वेश्या शङ्करं प्रत्यभाषत ॥ ५८॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  When Śiva, lord of Parvatī, favourably disposed to his devotees said thus, the harlot Mahānandā replied to Śiva. (58.)

Sanskrit
वेश्योवाच ।
न मे वाञ्छास्ति भोगेषु भूमौ स्वर्गे रसातले ।
तव पादाम्बुजस्पर्शादन्यत्किञ्चिन्न कामये ॥ ५९॥

English
The prostitute said :—  I have no desire for enjoyment on the earth, in the heaven or in the nether worlds. I do not desire anything except the touch of your lotus-like feet. (59.)

Sanskrit
ये मे भृत्याश्च दास्यश्च ये चान्ये मम बान्धवाः ।
सर्वे त्वद्दर्शनपरास्त्वयि सन्न्यस्तवृत्तयः ॥ ६०॥

English
My servants, my maids and my kinsmen are interested in seeing you. They have dedicated everything to you. (60.)

Sanskrit
सर्वानेतान्मया सार्धं निनीयात्मपरं पदम् ।
पुनर्जन्मभयं घोरं विमोचय नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ६१॥

English
Take them all along with me to your great region and quash the terrible fear of rebirth. Obeisance be to you. (61.)

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
ततः स तस्या वचनं प्रतिनन्द्य महेश्वरः ।
ताः सर्वाश्च तया सार्धं निनाय स्वं परं पदम् ॥ ६२॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Appreciating her words, the great Śiva took all of them to his great region. (62.)

Sanskrit
वैश्यनाथावतारस्ते वर्णितः परमो मया ।
महानन्दासुखकरो भक्तानन्दप्रदः सदा ॥ ६३॥

English
Thus I have described to you the great incarnation of Vaiśyanatha. It was pleasing to Mahānanda. It yields great bliss to devotees. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं चरित्रं परमं पवित्रं
सतां च सर्वप्रदमाशु दिव्यम् ।
शिवावतारस्य विशाम्पतेर्महा-
नन्दामहासौख्यकरं विचित्रम् ॥ ६४॥

English
This narrative is great holy and divine. It grants desires to good men immediately. This story of the incarnation of Siva as the lordly merchant is the source of great happiness. It is wonderful. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं यः श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या श्रावयेद्वा समाहितः ।
च्यवते न स्वधर्मात्स परत्र लभते गतिम् ॥ ६५॥

English
He who listens to this story with devotion and narrates it with purity never swerves from his duties. He attains salvation hereafter. (65.)
