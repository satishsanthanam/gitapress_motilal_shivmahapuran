### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - (*Śiva's incarnation as Yatinātha haṃsa*)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु प्राज्ञ प्रवक्ष्यामि शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अवतारं परानन्दं यतिनाथाह्वयं मुने ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O intelligent sage, listen. I shall explain the incarnation of great bliss named Yatinatha, of Siva the supreme soul. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्बुदाचलसंज्ञे तु पर्वते भिल्लवंशजः ।
आहुकश्च तदभ्याशे वसति स्म मुनीश्वर ॥ २॥

English
O great sage, on the mountain Arbuda$^{74}$ there lived a hunter Ahuka of Bhilla tribe. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पत्नी ह्याहुका नाम बभूव किल सुव्रता ।
उभावपि महाशैवावास्तां तौ शिवपूजकौ ॥ ३॥

English
His wife Ahuka observed some good rites. Both of them were great devotees of Siva and worshipped Siva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्मिंश्चित्समये भिल्लः शिवभक्तिरतः सदा ।
आहारार्थं स्वपत्न्याश्च सुदूरं स गतो मुने ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, once this Bhilla who was always devoted to Siva went far into the forest for fetching food for his wife. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र गेहे भिल्लस्य शङ्करः ।
भूत्वा यतिवपुः सायं परीक्षार्थं समाययौ ॥ ५॥

English
In the meantime towards the evening, Siva assumed the guise of an ascetic and came to his house to test him (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे तत्राजगाम स गृहाधिपः ।
पूजनं च यतीशस्य चकार प्रेमतः सुधीः ॥ ६॥

English
The lord of the house that intelligent man too came back then and lovingly worshipped the leading ascetic. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्भावस्य परीक्षार्थं यतिरूपः स शङ्करः ।
महालीलातरः प्रीत्या भीतः प्रोवाच दीनगीः ॥ ७॥

English
In order to test his feelings, Siva in the guise of an ascetic, indulging in great sports lovingly said to him in a piteous tone. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यतिनाथ उवाच ।
अद्य स्थलं निवासार्थं देहि मे प्रातरेव हि ।
यास्यामि सर्वथा भिल्ल स्वस्ति स्यात्तव सर्वदा ॥ ८॥

English
Yatinātha said :—  O Bhilla, give me some place here to stay today. I shall by all means leave in the next morning, you will be blessed for ever. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
भिल्ल उवाच ।
सत्यं प्रोक्तं त्वया स्वामिन् श‍ृणु मद्वचनं च ते ।
अति स्वल्पं स्थलं मे हि स्यान्निवासः कथं तव ॥ ९॥

English
Bhilla said :—  O lord, what you say is true. But please listen to me. The room is not spacious enough to hold us all together. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः स यतिस्तेन गमनाय मतिं दधे ।
तावद्भिल्ल्या वचः प्रोक्तं स्वामिनं संविचार्य वै ॥ १०॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Thus addressed, the ascetic wanted to leave. By that time, considering the trend of thought of her husband the Bhilli said. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
भिल्ल्युवाच ।
स्वामिन्देहि यतेः स्थानं विमुखं कुरु मातिथिम् ।
गृहधर्मं विचार्य त्वमन्यथा धर्मसङ्क्षयः ॥ ११॥

English
O lord, give him room to stay. Do not turn out a guest. Consider the duty of a householder or virtue will be affected. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थीयतां ते गृहाभ्यन्तः सुखेन यतिना सह ।
अहं बहिः स्थितिं कुर्यामायुधानि बृहन्त्यपि ॥ १२॥

English
You stay in the house along with the ascetic. I shall remain outside with immense weapons. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तस्यास्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा भिल्ल्या धर्मान्वितं शिवम् ।
स्वपत्न्या मनसा तेन भिल्लेन च विचारितम् ॥ १३॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing the virtuous and auspicious words of his wife, the Bhilla thought thus. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियं बहिश्च निष्कास्य कथं स्थेयं मया गृहे ।
यतेरन्यत्र गमनमधर्मकरमात्मनः ॥ १४॥

English
"How can I stay in the house after keeping my wife out. It will be sinful on my part if the ascetic goes elsewhere. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वयमप्युचितं नैव सर्वथा गृहमेधिनः ।
यद्भावि तद्भवेदेव मया स्थेयं गृहाद् बहिः ॥ १५॥

English
Neither of the two courses is proper for a householder. What is destined to happen will surely happen. I shall stay outside." (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याग्रहं तदा कृत्वा गृहान्तः स्थाप्य तौ मुदा ।
स्वायुधानि च संस्थाप्य भिल्लोऽतिष्ठद् गृहाद् बहिः ॥ १६॥

English
After thinking thus and insisting on it, he kept both of them within the house joyously, himself stayed outside the house keeping all the weapons beside him. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
रात्रौ तं पशवः क्रूराः हिंसकाः समपीडयन् ।
तेनापि च यथाशक्ति कृतो यत्नो महांस्तदा ॥ १७॥

English
During the night the cruel beasts of prey tormented him. He too strenuously withstood them to the extent of his ability. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं यत्नं प्रकुर्वाणः स भिल्लो बलवानपि ।
प्रारब्धात्प्रेरितैर्हिंस्रैर्बलादासीच्च भक्षितः ॥ १८॥

English
Thus fighting strenuously and although very strong, the Bhilla was devoured by the beasts of prey which seized him forcibly, as though incited by his own adverse fate. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रातरुत्थाय स यतिर्दृष्ट्वा हिंस्रैश्च भक्षितम् ।
भिल्लं वनेचरं तं वै दुःखितोऽभूदतीव हि ॥ १९॥

English
Getting up in the morning the ascetic saw the Bhilla of the forest devoured by the beasts of prey. He felt much dejected at it. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दुखितं तं यतिं दृष्ट्वा भिल्ली सा दुःखितापि हि ।
धैर्यात्स्वदुःखं संहृत्य वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ २०॥

English
Although the Bhilli too was distressed, she suppressed her sorrow on seeing the ascetic miserable and spoke thus. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
भिल्ल्युवाच ।
किमर्थं क्रियते दुःखं भद्रं जातं यतेऽधुना ।
धन्योऽयं कृतकृत्यश्च यज्जातो मृत्युरीदृशः ॥ २१॥

English
Bhilli said :—  O ascetic, why are you sorry? What has happened is for good. Blessed and contented is he since the death has happened in this manner. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं चैनं गमिष्यामि भस्म भूत्वानले यते ।
चितां कारय सुप्रीत्या स्त्रीणां धर्मः सनातनः ॥ २२॥

English
O ascetic, reducing myself to ashes, I too shall follow him. Be pleased to arrange for the pyre. This is the eternal duty of women. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
इति तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा हितं मत्वा स्वयं यतिः ।
चितां व्यरचयत्सा हि प्रविवेश स्वधर्मतः ॥ २३॥

English
On hearing her words which he thought to be proper, the ascetic arranged the pyre. She entered the pyre dutifully. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे साक्षात्पुरः प्रादुरभूच्छिवः ।
धन्ये धन्ये इति प्रीत्या प्रशंसस्तां हरोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २४॥

English
In the meantime Siva himself appeared before her and spoke praising her lovingly "O blessed lady, O blessed lady." (24.)

---

Sanskrit
हर उवाच ।
वरं ब्रूहि प्रसन्नोऽस्मि त्वदाचरणतोऽनघे ।
तवादेयं न वै किञ्चिद् वश्योऽहं ते विशेषतः ॥ २५॥

English
Śiva said :—  O sinless lady, mention the boon you wish to have. I am pleased with your conduct. There is nothing which cannot be granted to you. I am utterly subservient to you. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तच्छुत्वा शम्भुवचनं परमानन्ददायकम् ।
सुखं प्राप्तं विशेषेण न किञ्चित्स्मरणं ययौ ॥ २६॥

English
Nandāvara said :—  On hearing the words of Siva yielding the greatest bliss she felt the highest pleasure. She did not remember anything. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यास्तद्गतिमालक्ष्य सुप्रसन्नो हरोऽभवत् ।
उवाच च पुनः शम्भुर्वरं ब्रूहीति तां प्रभुः ॥ २७॥

English
On seeing her in that state, Siva was much delighted. He asked her again to mention the boon she wished to have. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
अयं यतिश्च मद्रूपो हंसरूपो भविष्यति ।
परजन्मनि वां प्रीत्या संयोगं कारयिष्यति ॥ २८॥

English
Śiva said :—  This ascetic who is in my own form will assume the form of a swan in the next birth. He will lovingly unite both of you. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
भिल्लश्च वीरसेनस्य नैषधे नगरे वरे ।
महान्पुत्रो नलो नाम भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ २९॥

English
This Bhilla will be born as Nala, the son of Virasena, in the excellent city of Nişadha.[75] There is no doubt in this. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं सुता भीमराजस्य वैदर्भे नगरेऽनघे ।
दमयन्ती च विख्याता भविष्यसि गुणान्विता ॥ ३०॥

English
You will be born in the illustrious city of Vidarbha as the daughter of king Bhima. You will be famous as Damayanti endowed with all good qualities. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
युवां चोभौ मिलित्वा च राजभोगं सुविस्तरम् ।
भुक्त्वा मुक्तिं च योगीन्द्रैर्लप्स्येथे दुर्लभां ध्रुवम् ॥ ३१॥

English
After coming together you will enjoy royal pleasures. You will attain salvation too, inaccessible even to leading Yogins. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा च स्वयं शम्भुर्लिङ्गरूपोऽभवत्तदा ।
तस्मान्न चलितो धर्मादचलेश इति स्मृतः ॥ ३२॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  After saying this, Siva assumed the form of phallic image. He became stationary. He is thereafter remembered as Acaleśa. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
स भिल्ल आहुकश्चापि वीरसेनसुतोऽभवत् ।
नैषधे नगरे तात नलनामा महानृपः ॥ ३३॥

English
O dear, the Bhilla Ahuka was born as the son of Virasena in the city of Nişadha. He was the great king Nala. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
आहुका सा महाभिल्ली भीमस्य तनयाभवत् ।
वैदर्भे नगरे राज्ञो दमयन्तीति विश्रुता ॥ ३४॥

English
That great Bhilli, Ahukā was born as the daughter of king Bhima in the city of Vidarbha. She became famous as Damayanti. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यतिनाथाह्वयः सोऽपि हंसरूपोऽभवच्छिवः ।
विवाहं कारयामास दमयन्त्या नलेन वै ॥ ३५॥

English
Siva in the form of Yatinatha was born as a swan. He managed the marriage of Damayanti with Nala. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वसत्काररूपेण महापुण्येन शङ्करः ।
हंसरूपं विधायैव ताभ्यां सुखमदात्प्रभुः ॥ ३६॥

English
It was due to the merit accruing from hospitality that lord Siva gave them pleasure after assuming the form of a swan. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो हंसावतारो हि नानावार्ताविचक्षणः ।
दमयन्त्या नलस्यापि परमानन्ददायकः ॥ ३७॥

English
The incarnation of Siva as the swan adept in the art of intelligence, was the bestower of great bliss to Damayanti as well as to Nala. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं चरितं परमं पवित्रं शिवावतारस्य पवित्रकीर्तेः ।
यतीशसंज्ञस्य महाद्भुतं हि हंसाह्वयस्यापि विमुक्तिदं हि ॥ ३८॥

English
This great and holy narrative of Siva's incarnation of illustrious fame as swan is wonderful and indeed the bestower of salvation (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यतीशब्रह्महंसाख्यावतारचरितं शुभम् ।
श‍ृणुयाच्छ्रावयेद्यो हि स लभेत परां गतिम् ॥ ३९॥

English
He who narrates or listens to the auspicious story of incarnation of Yatinatha hamsa, attains the greatest goal. (39.)

---

English
The city of Nişadha or Naisadha associated with King Nala can be identified with Nalapura, modern Narwar situated on the river Kalisindhu to the south-west of Gwalior. (75.)

---

English
The capital of Vidarbha is placed in Mahārāṣṭra. It is the same as Kuṇḍinpur modern Beder to the east of Amarāvatī in Berar. (76.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ।
स्वर्ग्यं यशस्यमायुष्यं भक्तिवर्धनमुत्तमम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The story is faultless, bestower of the fruits of all desires and very excellent. It is conducive to the attainment of heaven, fame and longevity. It enhances devotion.* (40.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वैतच्चरितं शम्भोर्यतिहंसस्वरूपयोः ।
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा सोऽन्ते शिवपुरं व्रजेत् ॥ ४१॥

English
After hearing this narrative of Śiva in the forms of an ascetic and a swan, the hearer enjoys all pleasures here and goes to Śiva's abode hereafter. (41.)
