### **CHAPTER TWENTYNINE - (The incarnation of Śiva as Kṛṣṇadarśana)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार शम्भोस्त्ववतारं परमं श‍ृणु ।
नभगज्ञानदं कृष्णदर्शनाह्वयमुत्तमम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O Sanatkumāra, listen to the description of the great incarnation of Śiva, the excellent Kṛṣṇadarśana who gave perfect wisdom to Nabhaga. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इक्ष्वाकुप्रमुखा आसन् श्राद्धदेवसुताश्च ये ।
नभगस्तत्र नवमो नाभागस्तत्सुतः स्मृतः ॥ २॥

English
Ikṣvāku and others were the sons of Śrāddhadeva⁷⁷ of whom Nabhaga, was the ninth. His son was Nābhaga. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अम्बरीषः सुतस्तस्य विष्णुभक्तो बभूव सः ।
यस्योपरि प्रसन्नोऽभूद्दुर्वासा ब्रह्मभक्तितः ॥ ३॥

English
Ambariṣa was his son who was a devotee of Viṣṇu with whom the sage Durvāsas was much pleased due to his devotion for Brahmins. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पितामहोऽम्बरीषस्य नभगो यः प्रकीर्तितः ।
तच्चरितं श‍ृणु मुने यस्मै ज्ञानमदाच्छिवः ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, the grandfather of Ambariṣa was Nabhaga to whom Śiva imparted perfect knowledge. Listen to his story. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
नभगो मनुपुत्रस्तु पठनार्थं सुबुद्धिमान् ।
चक्रे गुरुकुले वासं बहुकालं जितेन्द्रियः ॥ ५॥

English
Nabhaga the son of Manu, highly intelligent, and with perfect control of his sense-organs stayed in the abode of his preceptors for studies for a long time. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्समये ते वै इक्ष्वाकुप्रमुखाः सुताः ।
तस्मै भागमकल्प्यैव भेजुर्भागान्निजान्क्रमात् ॥ ६॥

English
During this interval Ikṣvāku and other sons had their due shares in the property without leaving any share for Nabhaga. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वं स्वं भागं गृहीत्वा ते बुभुजू राज्यमुत्तमम् ।
अविषादं महाभागाः पित्रादेशात्सुबुद्धयः ॥ ७॥

English
At the behest of their father, the shrewd sons took their shares and enjoyed the kingdom with out any misery. They were fortunate. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स पश्चादागतस्तत्र ब्रह्मचारी गुरुस्थलात् ।
नभगोऽधीत्य सर्वाश्च साङ्गोपाङ्गाः श्रुतीः क्रमात् ॥ ८॥

English
After learning the Vedas with their sections and sub-sections, in due course, the Brahmacārin Nabhga returned. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रातृन्विलोक्य नभगो विभक्तान्सकलान्निजान् ।
दायार्थी प्राह तान्स्नेहादिक्ष्वाकुप्रमुखान्मुने ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, on seeing his brothers Ikṣvāku and others having taken their shares, Nabhaga desirous of his share told them affectionately. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नभग उवाच ।
भ्रातरोऽभक्तकं मह्यं दायं कृत्वा यथातथम् ।
सर्वे विभक्ताः सुप्रीत्या स्वदायार्थागताय च ॥ १०॥

English
Nabhaga said:—  O my brothers you have taken your shares duly without leaving anything for me. I have now come for my share. Please give me mine. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा विस्मृतमस्माभिरिदानीं पितरं तव ।
विभजामो वयं भागं तं गृहाण न संशयः ॥ ११॥

English
Then we had forgotten to allow your share. We shall certainly do it now. Our father is your share. Take him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छुत्वा भ्रातृवचनं नभगः परविस्मृतः ।
तदोपकण्ठमागत्य पितरं समभाषत ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing the words of his brothers Nabhaga was surprised. He approached his father and said. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नभग उवाच ।
हे तात भ्रातरः सर्वे त्यक्त्वा मां न्यभजंश्च ते ।
पठनार्थं गतश्चाहं ब्रह्मचारी गुरोः कुले ॥ १३॥

English
Nabhaga said:—  O father, my brothers have taken their shares leaving me out. I had been to the preceptor's abode for study as a student. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तत आगत्य मे पृष्टा दायदानार्थमादरात् ।
ते त्वामूचुर्विभागं मे तदर्थमहमागतः ॥ १४॥

English
When I returned I asked them to allot my share; they replied that you are my share. I have now come for that purpose. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य पिता तं प्राह विस्मितः ।
आश्वास्य श्राद्धदेवः स सत्यधर्मरतं मुने ॥ १५॥

English
Nanditvara said:—  O sage, on hearing his words, his father Śrāddhadeva was surprised. He consoled his son who was devoted to truth and virtue. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मनुरुवाच ।
तदुक्तं मादृथास्तात प्रतारणकरं हि तत् ।
न ह्यहं परमं दायं सर्वथा भोगसाधनम् ॥ १६॥

English
Manu said:—  O dear, do not pay heed to what they said. It was only to deceive you. I am not a share by any means, an instrument of enjoyment. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि दायभावेन दत्तोऽहं तैः प्रतारिभिः ।
तव वै जीवनोपायं वदामि श‍ृणु तत्त्वतः ॥ १७॥

English
Still since I have been given as a share by the deceitful persons I shall tell you the means of livelihood. Hear attentively. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्रमाङ्गिरसा विप्राः कुर्वन्त्यद्य सुमेधसः ।
तत्र कर्मणि मुह्यन्ति षष्ठं षष्ठमहः प्रति ॥ १८॥

English
The intelligent Āṅgirasa brahmins are performing a sacrifice. Every sixth day the rites are marred. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र त्वं गच्छ नभग तान् सुशंस महाकवे ।
सूक्ते द्वे वैश्वदेवे हि सत्रं शुद्धं हि तद्भवेत् ॥ १९॥

English
O wise Nabhaga, you go there and praise them. Recite two Vaiśvadeva sūkatas there. That sacrifice will then become perfect. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्कर्मणि समाप्ते हि स्वर्यान्तो ब्राह्मणाश्च ते ।
धनं दास्यन्ति ते तुष्टाः स्वसत्रपरिशेषितम् ॥ २०॥

English
As soon as the sacrifice is concluded those Brāhmins going heavenwards will give you delightedly the wealth left over in the sacrifice. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य पितुर्वाक्यं नभगः सत्यसारवान् ।
जगाम तत्र सुप्रीत्या यत्र तत्सत्रमुत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing the words of his father, Nabhaga who considered truth as virtue went eagerly where the excellent sacrifice was on. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाहः कर्मणि मुने सत्रे तस्मिन्स मानवः ।
सूक्ते द्वे वैश्वदेवे हि प्रोवाच स्पष्टतः सुधीः ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, in that sacrifice during the diurnal rites the intelligent son of Manu recited two Vaiśvadeva suktas clearly. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
समाप्ते कर्मणि ततो विप्रा आङ्गिरसाश्च ते ।
तस्मै दत्त्वा ययुः स्वर्गं स्वं स्वं सत्रावशेषितम् ॥ २३॥

English
When the sacrifice was concluded the Āṅgirasa brahmins gave him the wealth left after the sacrifice. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्तदा स्वीकरिष्यन्तं सुसत्रपरिशेषितम् ।
विज्ञाय गिरिशः सद्य आविर्भूतः सदूतिकृत् ॥ २४॥

English
Realising that he was taking the wealth left after the sacrifice, Śiva of divine sports manifested himself all of a sudden. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरः श्रीमान्पुरुषः कृष्णदर्शनः ।
भावं समीक्षितुं भागं दातुं ज्ञानं परं च तत् ॥ २५॥

English
He was beautiful in every limb, a glorious Puruṣa Kṛṣṇadarśana. It was to survey his emotional reaction and to bestow on him the perfect knowledge as his share that the lord manifested himself. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो स शङ्करः शम्भुः परीक्षाकर ईश्वरः ।
उवाचोत्तरतोऽभ्येत्य नभगं तं हि मानवम् ॥ २६॥

English
Lord Śiva desirous of testing Nabhaga the son of Manu approached from north and said. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
कस्त्वं गृह्णासि पुरुष ममेदं वास्तुकं वसु ।
प्रेषितः केन तत्सर्वं सत्यं वद ममाग्रतः ॥ २७॥

English
Śiva said :—  O man, who are you that take away my assets and wealth? Who has sent you here? Tell me the whole truth. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा तद्वचस्तात मानवो नभगः कविः ।
प्रत्युवाच विनीतात्मा पुरुषं कृष्णदर्शनम् ॥ २८॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On heaving his words, O dear, the poet Nabhaga, son of Manu humbly replied to the Puruṣa Kṛṣṇadarśana. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नभग उवाच ।
ममेदमृषिभिर्दत्तं वसु यज्ञगतं खलु ।
कथं वारयसे मां त्वं गृह्णतं कृष्णदर्शन ॥ २९॥

English
Nabhaga said:—  This wealth of the sacrifice has been given to me by the sages. Why do you, O Kṛṣṇadarśana prevent me from taking it. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
आकर्ण्य नाभगं वाक्यमिदं सत्यमुदीरितम् ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा पुरुषः कृष्णदर्शनः ॥ ३०॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing these truthful words uttered by Nabhaga, the delighted puruṣa Kṛṣṇadarśana spoke. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णदर्शन उवाच ।
विवादेऽस्मिन्हि नौ तात प्रमाणं जनकस्तव ।
याहि तं पृच्छ स ब्रूयात्तत्प्रमाणं तु सत्यतः ॥ ३१॥

English
Kṛṣṇadarśana said:—  O dear, in our dispute the ultimate authority is your father. Go to him and ask him. What he says will be true and final. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य नभगो मानवः कविः ।
आगच्छत्पितरं प्रीत्या तदुक्तं पृष्टवान्मुने ॥ ३२॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing his words, the wise Nabhaga son of Manu, approached his father anxiously, O sage, and asked him about what he said. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रोदितं समाकर्ण्य श्राद्धदेवः स वै मनुः ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं प्राप्तस्मृतिरुवाच तम् ॥ ३३॥

English
On hearing what his son said, Manu Śrāddhadeva remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and after re-collecting spoke to him. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मनुरुवाच ।
हे तात श‍ृणु मद्वाक्यं स देवः पुरुषः शिवः ।
तस्यैव सकलं वस्तु यज्ञप्राप्तं विशेषतः ॥ ३४॥

English
Manu said:—  O dear, listen to my words : that Puruṣa is lord Śiva. Every object is his and particularly what is obtained from the sacrifice. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अध्वरोर्वरितं वस्तु रुद्रभागः प्रकीर्तितः ।
इत्यपि प्राज्ञवादो हि क्वचिज्जातस्तदिच्छया ॥ ३५॥

English
That what is left over after the sacrifice is Śiva's share. This argument is current among some intelligent people due to his will. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स देव ईश्वरः सर्वं वस्त्वर्हति न संशयः ।
यज्ञावशिष्टं किमुत परे तस्येच्छया विभोः ॥ ३६॥

English
Undoubtedly lord Śiva deserves everything. What then of the wealth left over in a sacrifice ? More depends on the lord's wish. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहार्थमायातस्तव तद्रूपतः प्रभुः ।
तत्र त्वं गच्छ नभग प्रसन्नं कुरु सत्यतः ॥ ३७॥

English
O Nabhaga, the lord has come in that form in order to bless you. Go there. Propitiate him properly. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षमापय स्वापराधं सुप्रणम्य स्तुतिं कुरु ।
सर्वप्रभुः स एवेशो यज्ञाधीशोऽखिलेश्वरः ॥ ३८॥

English
Request him to forgive your fault. Bow to him well and eulogise him. That Śiva alone is the lord of all, the lord of sacrifice, the lord of everything. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुब्रह्मादयो देवाः सिद्धाः सर्वर्षयोऽपि हि ।
तदनुग्रहतस्तात समर्थाः सर्वकर्मणि ॥ ३९॥

English
Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods, Siddhas and the sages, O dear, are competent in their respective duties only due to his blessings. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
किम्बहूक्त्यात्मजश्रेष्ठ गच्छ तत्राशु माचिरम् ।
प्रसादय महादेवं सर्वथा सकलेश्वरम् ॥ ४०॥

English
O foremost of my sons, go there quickly. Do not delay. Propitiate the great god, the lord of all by every means. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स मनुः श्राद्धदेवश्च तनयं द्रुतम् ।
प्रेषयामास निकटं शम्भोः सोऽपि समेत्य तम् ॥ ४१॥

नभगश्च प्रणम्याशु साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तकः ।
प्रोवाच सुप्रसन्नात्मा विनयेन महामतिः ॥ ४२॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After saying this, Manu Śrāddhadeva, sent his son immediately to Śiva. After approaching him and bowing to him with palms joined in reverence and head bent down, the delighted Nabhaga of great intellect spoke to him humbly. (41-42.)

---

Sanskrit
नभग उवाच ।
इदं तवेश सर्वं हि वस्तु त्रिभुवने हि यत् ।
इत्याह मे पिता नूनं किमुताध्वरशेषितम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Nabhaga said:—  O lord, my father says that whatever there is in the three worlds is yours to be sure. What then about the wealth left over in a sacrifice? (43.)

---

Sanskrit
अजानता मया नाथ यदुक्तं तद्वचो भ्रमात् ।
अपराधं त्वं क्षमस्व शिरसा त्वां प्रसादये ॥ ४४॥

English
O lord, what I said before was done in error and ignorance. Please forgive the same. I propitiate you with my head bent down. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा नभगः सोऽतिदीनधीस्तु कृताञ्जलिः ।
तुष्टाव तं महेशानं कृष्णदर्शनमानतः ॥ ४५॥

English
After saying this, Nabhaga of dejected mind, eulogised lord Śiva Kṛṣṇadarśana with palms joined in reverence and with his head bent down. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
श्राद्धदेवोऽपि शुद्धात्मा नतकः साञ्जलिः सुधीः ।
तुष्टाव तं प्रभुं नत्वा स्वापराधं क्षमापयन् ॥ ४६॥

English
The pure-souled Śrāddhadeva too with head bent and palms joined eulogised the lord and requested him to forgive his fault. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र विष्णुब्रह्मादयः सुराः ।
वासवाद्याः समाजग्मुः सिद्धाश्च मुनयोऽपि हि ॥ ४७॥

English
In the meantime, Viṣṇu, Brahmā, Indra and the gods, Siddhas and the sages came there. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
महोत्सवं प्रकुर्वन्तः सुकृताञ्जलयोऽखिलाः ।
तुष्टुवुर्नतका भक्त्या सुप्रणम्य पृथक्पृथक् ॥ ४८॥

English
They were jubilant. They joined their palms in reverence. With great devotion they severally bowed down and eulogised. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ रुद्रः प्रसन्नात्मा कृपादृष्ट्या विलोक्य तान् ।
उवाच नभगं प्रीत्या सस्मितं कृष्णदर्शनः ॥ ४९॥

English
Then the delighted Rudra, Kṛṣṇadarśana glanced at them with sympathetic eyes and smilingly spoke to Nabhaga. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णदर्शन उवाच ।
यत्ते पितावदद्धर्म्यं वाक्यं तत्तु तथैव हि ।
त्वयापि सत्यमुक्तं तत् साधुस्त्वं नात्र संशयः ॥ ५०॥

English
Kṛṣṇadarśana said :—  What your father said is virtuous and true. You too spoke the truth. Hence you are good. There is no doubt in this. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
अतोऽहं सुप्रसन्नोऽस्मि सर्वथा सुव्रतेन ते ।
ददामि कृपया ते हि ज्ञानं ब्रह्म सनातनम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Hence I am delighted with your holy rites. Sympathetically I am conferring on you the eternal Brahman, the perfect knowledge. (51.)

Sanskrit
महाज्ञानी भव त्वं हि सविप्रो नभगं द्रुतम् ।
गृहाण वस्त्विदं सर्वं मद्दत्तं कृपयाधुना ॥ ५२॥

English
Be great and wise, O Nabhaga, along with the brahmins immediately. Take all this wealth granted by me out of compassion. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
इह सर्वसुखं भुङ्क्ष्व निर्विकारं महामते ।
सुगतिं प्राप्स्यसि त्वं हि सविप्रः कृपया मम ॥ ५३॥

English
O intelligent one, enjoy all pleasures without any aberration. By my grace you will attain the good goal along with the brahmin sacrificers. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तात भगवान्स रुद्रः सत्यवत्सलः ।
सर्वेषां पश्यतां तेषां तत्रैवान्तर्दधे हरः ॥ ५४॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O dear, after saying this, lord Śiva, favourably disposed to truth, vanished there itself even as all were watching. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुर्ब्रह्मापि देवाद्याः सर्वे ते मुनिसत्तम ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुः प्रीत्या तस्यै नत्वा दिशे मुदा ॥ ५५॥

English
Vişnu, Brahma, the gods and all others, O excellent sage, went to their respective abodes after joyously bowing to that direction. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
सपुत्रः श्राद्धदेवोऽपि स्वस्थानमगमन्मुदा ।
भुक्त्वा भोगान्सुविपुलान्सोऽन्ते शिवपुरं ययौ ॥ ५६॥

English
Sraddhadeva too, accompanied by his son, went joyously to his abode. After enjoying extensive pleasures he went to Siva's city in the end. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं ते कीर्तितो ब्रह्मन्नवतारः शिवस्य हि ।
कृष्णदर्शननामा वै नभगानन्ददायकः ॥ ५७॥

English
Thus, O Brahmin, the incarnation of Siva, named Krsnadarśana, bestower of bliss on Nabhaga, has been narrated to you. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं सताम् ।
पठतां श‍ृण्वतां वापि सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ ५८॥

English
This narrative is holy and conducive to worldly enjoyment. It grants salvation to the good. It yields the fruits of desires to those who read and listen to it. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
य एतच्चरितं प्रातः सायं च स्मरते सुधीः ।
कविर्भवति मन्त्रज्ञो गतिमन्ते लभेत्पराम् ॥ ५९॥

English
The intelligent man who remembers this narrative both in the morning and in the evening becomes wise and knower of mantras. He attains the greatest goal in the end. (59.)
