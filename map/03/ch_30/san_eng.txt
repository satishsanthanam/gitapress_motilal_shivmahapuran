### **CHAPTER THIRTY - (The incarnation of Śiva as Avadhūteśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु त्वं ब्रह्मपुत्राद्यावतारं परमेशितुः ।
अवधूतेश्वराह्वं वै शक्रगर्वापहारकम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O son of Brahma, listen to the first incarnation of the great lord, named Avadhuteśvara, who dispelled the arrogance of Indra. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्रः पुरा हि सगुरुः सर्वदेवसमन्वितः ।
दर्शनं कर्तुमीशस्य कैलासमगमन्मुने ॥ २॥

English
O sage, formerly Indra accompanied by Bhaspati and the other gods went to Kailasa in order to see Siva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ गुर्विन्द्रयोर्ज्ञात्वागमनं शङ्करस्तयोः ।
परीक्षितुं च तद्भावं स्वदर्शनरतात्मनोः ॥ ३॥

अवधूतस्वरूपोऽभून्नानालीलाकरः प्रभुः ।
दिगम्बरो महाभीमो ज्वलदग्निसमप्रभः ॥ ४॥

English
On knowing that Bhaspati and Indra were coming and in order to test their mind in their eagerness to see him, the lord indulging in different kinds of sports assumed the form of an Avadhuta. He was naked. He looked fierce and had the lustre of blazing fire. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽवधूतस्वरूपो हि मार्गमारुद्ध्य सद्गतिः ।
लम्बमानपटः शम्भुरतिष्ठच्छोभिताकृतिः ॥ ५॥

English
The Avadhuta, the goal of the good, blocked their way. He stood there with matted hair hanging loosely and the whole feature shining brilliantly. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तौ गुरुशक्रौ च गच्छन्तौ शिव सन्निधिम् ।
अद्राष्टां पुरुषं भीमं मार्गमध्येऽद्भुताकृतिम् ॥ ६॥

English
Passing that way towards Siva, Bhaspati and Indra saw the gigantic figure of wonderful features on the way. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ शक्रो मुनेऽपृच्छत्स्वाधिकारेण दुर्मदः ।
पुरुषं तं स्वमार्गान्तः स्थितमज्ञाय शङ्करम् ॥ ७॥

English
Without knowing that the Puruṣa was Śiva himself, O sage, Indra who was arrogant due to his authority asked him as he stood in his way. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्र उवाच ।
कस्त्वं दिगम्बराकारावधूतः कुत आगतः ।
किन्नाम तव विख्यातं तत्त्वतो वद मेऽचिरम् ॥ ८॥

English
Indra said :—  Who are you? Whence have you come in this form of a naked Avadhūta? What is your true name? Tell me quickly and factually. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्थाने संस्थितः शम्भुः किं वान्यत्र गतोऽधुना ।
दर्शनार्थं हि तस्याहं गच्छामि सगुरुः सुरैः ॥ ९॥

English
Is Śiva in his abode? Or has he gone out? I am now going to see him along with my preceptor and the gods. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
शक्रेणेत्थं स पृष्टश्च किञ्चिन्नोवाच पूरुषः ।
लीलागृहीतदेहः स शङ्करो मदहा प्रभुः ॥ १०॥

English
Nandīvara said :— When asked by Indra that Puruṣa who was lord Śiva himself who had taken that body sportively in order to quell his arrogance did not say anything. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्रः पुनरपृच्छत्तं नोवाच स दिगम्बरः ।
अविज्ञातगतिः शम्भुर्महाकौतुककारकः ॥ ११॥

English
Indra asked him again; but Śiva whose ways are unknown, who was in the naked form and who was the creator of fanciful things did not say anything. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः पुरन्दरोऽपृच्छ्त् त्रैलोक्याधिपतिः स्वराट् ।
तूष्णीमास महायोगी महालीलाकरः स वै ॥ १२॥

English
Again Indra, the lord of heaven and the master of the three worlds, asked him. The great Yogin of great sports remained silent. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं पुनः पुनः पृष्टः शक्रेण स दिगम्बरः ।
नोवाच किञ्चिद्भगवान् शक्रदर्पजिघांसया ॥ १३॥

English
Though asked by Indra again and again thus, lord Śiva remained silent desirous of quelling the arrogance of Indra. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ चुक्रोध देवेशस्त्रैलोक्यैश्वर्यगर्वितः ।
उवाच वचनं क्रोधात्तं निर्भर्त्स्य जटाधरम् ॥ १४॥

English
Then the lord of the gods proud of his suzerainty over the three worlds became furious and said rebuking the lord having matted hair. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्र उवाच ।
पृच्छमानोऽपि रे मूढ नोत्तरं दत्तवानसि ।
अतस्त्वां हन्मि वज्रेण कस्ते त्रातास्ति दुर्मते ॥ १५॥

English
Indra said :—  O you fool, even though I am asking, you are not giving me any answer. Hence I am going so kill you with my thunderbolt. O wicked fellow, who is there to protect you? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युदीर्य ततो वज्री सन्निरीक्ष्य क्रुधा हि तम् ।
हन्तुं दिगम्बरं वज्रमुद्यतं स चकार ह ॥ १६॥

English
After saying this and staring at him in fury Indra raised his thunderbolt in order to kill the naked person. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वज्रहस्तं च तं दृष्ट्वा शक्रं शीघ्रं सदाशिवः ।
चकार स्तम्भनं तस्य वज्रपातस्य शङ्करः ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing Indra lifting up the thunderbolt, Śiva made his hand stunned. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स पुरुषः क्रुद्धः करालाक्षो भयङ्करः ।
द्रुतमेव प्रजज्वाल तेजसा प्रदहन्निव ॥ १८॥

English
Then that Puruṣa, furious and terrific, with terrible eyes, blazed with his brilliance as though he would burn everything. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
बाहुप्रतिष्टम्भभुवा मन्युनान्तः शचीपतिः ।
समदह्यत भोगीव मन्त्ररुद्धपराक्रमः ॥ १९॥

English
The lord of Śacī burnt within himself due to the anger resulting from the benumbed state of his hand like a serpent whose fury had been curbed by a magical formula. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा बृहस्पतिस्त्वेनं प्रज्वलन्तं स्वतेजसा ।
पुरुषं तं धियामास प्रणनाम हरं द्रुतम् ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing the Puruṣa blazing thus in his own brilliance Bṛhaspati knew him to be Śiva and bowed to him soon. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
कृताञ्जलिपुटो भूत्वा ततो गुरुरुदारधीः ।
दण्डवत्कौ पुनर्नत्वा प्रभुं तुष्टाव भक्तितः ॥ २१॥

English
Bṛhaspati of liberal mind kept his palms joined together and prostrated on the ground like a staff and eulogised him with devotion. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुरुवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
प्रसन्नो भव गौरीश सर्वेश्वर नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ २२॥

English
Bṛhaspati said : —  O great god, lord of the gods, favourably disposed towards those who seek refuge in you, O lord of Gaurī, be pleased. O lord of all, obeisance be to you. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मायया मोहिताः सर्वे ब्रह्मविष्ण्वादयोऽपि ते ।
त्वां न जानन्ति तत्त्वेन जानन्ति त्वदनुग्रहात् ॥ २३॥

English
Even Brahmā, Viṣṇu and others are all deluded by your Māyā. They do not know you really. If at all they know, it is only by your blessings. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
बृहस्पतिरिति स्तुत्वा स तदा शङ्करं प्रभुम् ।
पादयोः पातयामास तस्येशस्य पुरन्दरम् ॥ २४॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After eulogising lord Śiva, Bṛhaspati made Indra fall at his feet. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तात सुराचार्यः कृताञ्जलिरुदारधीः ।
बृहस्पतिरुवाचेदं प्रश्रयावनतः सुधीः ॥ २५॥

English
O dear, then the liberal-minded preceptor of the gods, Bṛhaspati, the intelligent one, bowed humbly and spoke thus. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।
दीनानाथ महादेव प्रणतं तव पादयोः ।
समुद्धर च मां तत्त्वं क्रोधं न प्रणयं कुरु ॥ २६॥

English
Bṛhaspati said :—  O great god, lord of the distressed, please raise me up, who have fallen at your feet. Do not be furious. Be kind and loving. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तुष्टो भव महादेव पाहीन्द्रं शरणागतम् ।
वह्निरेष समायाति भालनेत्रसमुद्भवः ॥ २७॥

English
O great god, be satisfied. Save Indra who has sought refuge in you. Fire originating from your eye in the forehead is coming out. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य गुरोर्वाक्यमवधूताकृतिः प्रभुः ।
उवाच करुणासिन्धुर्विहसन् स सदूतिकृत् ॥ २८॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing the words of Bṛhaspati, the lord in the form of Avadhūta, the ocean of mercy, indulging in good sports, laughingly said. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अवधूत उवाच ।
क्रोधाच्च निस्सृतं तेजो धारयामि स्वनेत्रतः ।
कथं हि कञ्चुकीं सर्पः सन्धत्ते चोज्झितां पुनः ॥ २९॥

English
Avadhūta said :—  How can I withhold the fire that has come out of my eye due to anger? How can a serpent take up a slough that has been cast off. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य शङ्करस्य बृहस्पतिः ।
उवाच साञ्जलिर्भूयो भयव्याकुलमानसः ॥ ३०॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing these words of Śiva, Bṛhaspati with his mind agitated due to fright spoke again with palms joined in reverence. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।
हे देव भगवन्भक्ता अनुकम्प्याः सदैव हि ।
भक्तवत्सलनामेति स्वं सत्यं कुरु शङ्कर ॥ ३१॥

English
Bṛhaspati said :—  O lord, devotees are always to be pitied. O Śiva, thus make your name Bhaktavatsala true. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षेप्तुमन्यत्र देवेश स्वतेजोऽत्युग्रमर्हसि ।
उद्धर्ता सर्वभक्तानां समुद्धर पुरन्दरम् ॥ ३२॥

English
O lord of the gods, it behoves you to cast off this fire elsewhere. Please lift up Indra since you are the up-lifter of all devotees. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो गुरुणा रुद्रो भक्तवत्सलनामभाक् ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा सुरेज्यं प्रणतार्तिहा ॥ ३३॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Thus requested by Bṛhaspati, Śiva who bore the name of Bhaktavatsala, the delighted soul and the dispeller of the distress of those who bow down, replied. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
प्रीतस्तेऽहं सुराचार्य ददामि वरमुत्तमम् ।
इन्द्रस्य जीवदानेन जीवेति त्वं प्रथां व्रज ॥ ३४॥

English
Śiva said :—  O preceptor of the gods, I am pleased with you. I shall grant you excellent boon. You will be famous as 'Jīva' because you have gifted life to Indra. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्भूतोऽनलो योऽयं भालनेत्रात्सुरासहः ।
एनं त्यक्ष्याम्यहं दूरे यथेन्द्रं नैव पीडयेत् ॥ ३५॥

English
I shall cast off this fire which has come out of the eye in my forehead, and which is unbearable to the gods, so that it will not afflict Indra. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स करे धृत्वा स्वतेजोऽनलमद्भुतम् ।
भालनेत्रसमुद्भूतं प्राक्षिपल्लवणाम्भसि ॥ ३६॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After saying this he took the wonderful fire, originating from his eye in the forehead in his hand and cast it off into the briny sea.⁷⁸ (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो शिवस्य तत्तेजो भालनेत्रसमुद्भवम् ।
क्षिप्तं च लवणाम्भोधौ सद्यो बालो बभूव ह ॥ ३७॥

English
Then that fire originating from the eye in the forehead of Śiva and cast off into the briny sea became a boy immediately. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
स जलन्धरनामाभूत्सिन्धुपुत्रोऽसुरेश्वरः ।
तं जघान महेशानो देवप्रार्थनया प्रभुः ॥ ३८॥

English
He became the lord of Asuras known as Jalandhara and Sindhuputra (Son of the Sea). At the request of the gods, lord Śiva slew him. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं कृत्वा सुचरितं शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ।
अवधूतस्वरूपेण ततश्चान्तर्हितोऽभवत् ॥ ३९॥

English
After carrying out this task Śiva, the benefactor of the worlds in the form of Avadhūta, vanished from there. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवुः सकला देवाः सुखिनश्चातिनिर्भयाः ।
गुरुशक्रौ भयान्मुक्तौ जग्मतुः सुखमुत्तमम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The gods became happy and fearless. Bṛhaspati and Indra became free from terror and attained supreme happiness. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
यदर्थे गमनोद्युक्तौ दर्शनं प्राप्य तस्य तौ ।
कृतार्थौ गुरुशक्रौ हि स्वस्थानं जग्मतुर्मुदा ॥ ४१॥

English
After seeing the lord for which they had come there, Bṛhaspati and Indra returned to their respective abodes joyous and contented. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अवधूतेश्वराह्वोऽवतारस्ते कथितो मया ।
परमेशस्य परमानन्ददः खलदण्डदः ॥ ४२॥

English
Thus the incarnation Avadhūteśvara of lord Śiva has been narrated by me. It yields the highest bliss and punishes the wicked. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं यशस्यं स्वर्ग्यमेव च ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं दिव्यं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ ४३॥

English
This narrative is faultless and conducive to heaven and fame. It yields worldly pleasures and salvation. It is divine and it gives the fruits of all desires. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयान्नित्यं श्रावयेद्वा समाहितः ।
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा सोऽन्ते शिवगतिं लभेत् ॥ ४४॥

English
He who listens to this or narrates this with attentive mind enjoys all pleasures and attains the goal of Śiva in the end. (44.)
