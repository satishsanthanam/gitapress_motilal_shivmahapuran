### **CHAPTER THIRTYONE - (The incarnation of Śiva as Bhikṣuvarya)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अथ वक्ष्ये मुनिश्रेष्ठ शम्भोः श‍ृण्ववतारकम् ।
स्वभक्तदयया विप्र नारीसन्देहभञ्जकम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O excellent sage, I shall now narrate the incarnation of Śiva in which he dispelled the suspicion of a brahmin woman. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
आसीत्सत्यरथो नाम्ना विदर्भविषये नृपः ।
धर्मात्मा सत्यशीलश्च महाशैवजनप्रियः ॥ २॥

English
There was a king in the land of Vidarbha, named Satyaratha. He was virtuous, regularly observing truthfulness and a favourite of great devotees of Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य राज्ञः सुधर्मेण महीं पालयतो मुने ।
महान्कालो व्यतीयाय सुखेन शिवधर्मतः ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, as the king ruled over the kingdom virtuously observing piety to Śiva, a long time elapsed very happily. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्तस्य राज्ञस्तु शाल्वैश्च पुररोधिभिः ।
महान् रणो बभूवाथ बहुसैन्यैर्बलोद्धतैः ॥ ४॥

English
Once a great fight ensued between that king and the Śālvas who laid siege to his city with many armies puffed up with their strength. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स विदर्भनृपः कृत्वा सार्धं तैर्दारुणं रणम् ।
प्रनष्टोरुबलः शाल्वैर्निहतो दैवयोगतः ॥ ५॥

English
The king of Vidarbha had a terrible fight with them. But his armies were destroyed and he was killed by Śālvas due to adverse fate. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नृपे हते युद्धे शाल्वैस्तु भयविह्वलाः ।
सैनिका हतशेषाश्च मन्त्रिभिः सह दुद्रुवुः ॥ ६॥

English
When the king was killed in the battle by the Sālas, the terrified soldiers who survived death fled along with the ministers. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्य महाराज्ञी रात्रौ स्वपुरतो मुने ।
संरुद्धा रिपुभिर्यत्नादन्तर्वत्नी बहिर्ययौ ॥ ७॥

English
His crowned queen who was pregnant and who had been imprisoned by the enemies escaped from the city, O sage, during the night. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गता शोकसन्तप्ता सा राजमहिषी शनैः ।
प्राचीं दिशं ययौ दूरं स्मरन्तीशपदाम्बुजम् ॥ ८॥

English
Coming out of the city the grief-stricken crowned queen slowly went very far in the eastern direction with her heart set on the lotus-like feet of Siva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रभाते सा राज्ञी ददर्श विमलं सरः ।
अतीता दूरमध्वानं दयया शङ्करस्य हि ॥ ९॥

English
Early in the morning next day after she had traversed a long way, the queen saw a pure lake, thanks to the mercy of Siva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य प्रिया राज्ञः सन्तप्ता सुकुमारिणी ।
निवासार्थं सरस्तीरे छायावृक्षमुपाश्रयत् ॥ १०॥

English
After reaching the banks of the lake the distressed beloved of the king, of tender body, resorted to a shady tree for rest and stay. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र दैववशाद्राज्ञी मुहूर्त्ते सद्गुणान्विते ।
असूत तनयं दिव्यं सर्वलक्षणलक्षितम् ॥ ११॥

English
Fortunately in an auspicious and meritorious hour the queen gave birth to a son endowed with all divine characteristics. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तज्जननी दैवात्तृषिताति नृपाङ्गना ।
सरोऽवतीर्णा पानार्थं ग्रस्ता ग्राहेण पाथसि ॥ १२॥

English
The thirsty mother, the wife of the king, entered the lake for drinking water but as willed by fate she was devoured by a crocodile in the water. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
स सुतो जातमात्रस्तु क्षुत्पिपासार्दितो भृशम् ।
रुरोद च सरस्तीरे विनष्टपितृमातृकः ॥ १३॥

English
The son bereft of father and mother, distressed by hunger and thirst at the time of birth, cried out. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्वने क्रन्दमाने जातमात्रे सुते मुने ।
कृपान्वितो महेशोऽभूदन्तर्यामी स रक्षकः ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, when the new born babe was weeping and wailing in the forest, lord Siva, the immanent soul and protector, was moved to pity. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रेरिता मनसा काचिदीशेन त्रासहारिणा ।
अकस्मादागता तत्र भ्रमन्ती भैक्ष्यजीविनी ॥ १५॥

English
A wandering beggar woman came there accidentally, urged mentally by Siva, the dispeller of fear. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सा त्वेकहायनं बालं वहन्ती विधवा निजम् ।
अनाथमेकं क्रन्दन्तं शिशुं तत्र ददर्श ह ॥ १६॥

English
She was a widow and was herself carrying her one year old son. She saw an orphaned child weeping there. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सा दृष्ट्वा तत्र तं बालं वने निर्मनुजे मुने ।
विस्मिताति द्विजस्त्री सा चिचिन्त हृदये बहु ॥ १७॥

English
O sage, on seeing an infant boy in the desolate forest that brahmin woman was surprised and pondered over the situation in diverse ways. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो सुमहदाश्चर्यमिदं दृष्टं मयाधुना ।
असम्भाव्यमकथ्यं च सर्वथा मनसा गिरा ॥ १८॥

English
"An extremely wonderful thing has been seen by me now. It cannot be conceived by the mind or explained in words. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अच्छिन्ननाभिनालोऽयं रसायां केवलं शिशुः ।
शेते मातृविहीनश्च क्रन्दंस्तेजस्विनां वरः ॥ १९॥

English
This infant whose umbilical cord has not been cut lies on the bare ground. He has no mother. He is weeping but he is very brilliant (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य पित्रादयः केऽपि न सन्तीह सहायिनः ।
कारणं किं बभूवाथ ह्यहो दैवबलं महत् ॥ २०॥

English
He has no parents or persons to help him. What might have caused this? Alas, how strong is power of fate! (20.)

---

Sanskrit
न जाने कस्य पुत्रोऽयमस्य ज्ञातात्र कोऽपि न ।
यतः पृच्छाम्यस्य जन्म जाता च करुणा मयि ॥ २१॥

English
I do not know his parentage. There is none who knows him. Whom can I enquire about the details of his birth? But I feel drawn towards him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इच्छाम्येनं पोषितुं हि बालमौरसपुत्रवत् ।
संस्प्रष्टुं नोत्सहेऽज्ञात्वा कुलजन्मादि चास्य वै ॥ २२॥

English
Without knowing his birth and parentage I shall not touch him$^{79}$ although I wish to nourish him and bring him up as a child born of me.” (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति सञ्चिन्त्यमानायां तस्यां विप्रवरस्त्रियाम् ।
कृपां चकार महतीं शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २३॥

English
*Nandisvara said:—*  While the excellent brahmin lady was musing thus, Śiva favourably disposed to his devotees, took pity. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
दध्रे भिक्षुस्वरूपं हि महालीलो महेश्वरः ।
सर्वथा भक्तसुखदो निरुपाधिः स्वयं सदा ॥ २४॥

English
Lord Śiva of great sports, bestower of happiness on devotees in every respect, unconditionally for ever, by himself assumed the form of a mendicant. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राजगाम सहसा स भिक्षुः परमेश्वरः ।
यत्रास्ति सन्देहवती द्विजस्त्री ज्ञातुमिच्छती ॥ २५॥

English
The mendicant who was lord Śiva himself suddenly came to the place where the doubting brahmin lady, desirous of knowing the details, was standing. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भिक्षुवर्यस्वरूपोऽसावविज्ञातगतिः प्रभुः ।
तामाह विप्रवनितां विहस्य करुणानिधिः ॥ २६॥

English
The lord whose ways are unscrutable, who is the storehouse of mercy and who assumed the form of the leading mendicant, laughed and told the brahmin lady. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
भिक्षुवर्य उवाच ।
सन्देहं कुरु नो चित्ते विप्रभामिनि मा खिद ।
रक्षैनं बालकं प्रीत्या सुपवित्रं स्वपुत्रकम् ॥ २७॥

English
*The mendicant said:—*  O brahmin lady, do not entertain any doubt in your mind. Do not be sorry. Protect this pure boy, your adopted son, lovingly. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेन शिशुना श्रेयः प्राप्स्यसे न चिरात्परम् ।
पुष्णीहि सर्वथा ह्येनं महातेजस्विनं शिशुम् ॥ २८॥

English
Ere long you will attain a great glory through this infant. Nourish this extremely brilliant infant by all means. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तवन्तं तं भिक्षुस्वरूपं करुणानिधिम् ।
सा विप्रवनिता शम्भुं प्रीत्या पप्रच्छ सादरम् ॥ २९॥

English
*Nandisvara said:—*  That brahmin lady respectfully and lovingly asked Śiva, the storehouse of mercy, in the form of a mendicant who spoke thus. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्रवनितोवाच ।
त्वदाज्ञयैनं बालं हि रक्षिष्यामि स्वपुत्रवत् ।
पोक्ष्यामि नात्र सन्देहो मद्भाग्यात्त्वमिहागतः ॥ ३०॥

English
*The brahmin lady said:—*  At your behest I shall nourish and bring up this boy as though he is my own son. There shall be no doubt in this. You have come here by my good fortune. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि ज्ञातुमिच्छामि विशेषेण तु तत्त्वतः ।
कोऽयं कस्य सुतश्चायं कस्त्वमत्र समागतः ॥ ३१॥

English
Still I wish to know the particular details and facts about him. Whose son is he ? Who are you, that have come here ? (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मुहुर्मम समायाति ज्ञानं भिक्षुवर प्रभो ।
त्वं शिवः करुणासिन्धुस्त्वद्भक्तोऽयं शिशुः पुरा ॥ ३२॥

English
O lord, O excellent mendicant, again and again the thought occurs to me that you are Śiva, the ocean of mercy and this infant had been your devotee formerly. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
केनचित्कर्मदोषेण सम्प्राप्तोऽयं दशामिमाम् ।
तद्भुक्त्वा परमं श्रेयः प्राप्स्यते त्वदनुग्रहात् ॥ ३३॥

English
Through some defect in his past actions he has attained this plight. After experiencing it he will surely attain glory, thanks to your bliss. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वन्माययैव साहं वै मार्गभ्रष्टा विमोहिता ।
आगता प्रेषिता त्वत्तो ह्यस्य रक्षणहेतुतः ॥ ३४॥

English
It is by your Māyā that I have been deluded. I had lost my way. In order to protect him I have been urged by you to come over here. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति तद्दर्शनप्राप्तविज्ञानां विप्रकामिनीम् ।
ज्ञातुकामां विशेषेण प्रोचे भिक्षुतनुः शिवः ॥ ३५॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  Śiva in the guise of a mendicant spoke to the brahmin lady who had attained perfect knowledge by seeing him and who evinced the desire to know the details. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
भिक्षुवर्य उवाच ।
श‍ृणु प्रीत्या विप्रपत्नि बालस्यास्य पुरेहितम् ।
सर्वमन्यस्य सुप्रीत्या वक्ष्यते तत्त्वतोऽनघे ॥ ३६॥

English
The mendicant said :—  O brahmin lady, listen with pleasure to the antecedents of this boy. O sinless one, I shall mention all the details to you. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतो विदर्भराजस्य शिवभक्तस्य धीमतः ।
अयं सत्यरथस्यैव स्वधर्मनिरतस्य हि ॥ ३७॥

English
This boy is the son of Satyaratha, king of Vidarbha a great devotee of Śiva and devoted to his duty. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु सत्यरथो राजा हतः शाल्वे रणे परैः ।
तत्पत्नी निशि सुव्यग्रा निर्ययौ स्वगृहाद् द्रुतम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Listen, the king Satyaratha was killed by his enemies in the battle. His excited and distressed wife set out from the palace at night. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
असूत तनयं चैनं समायाता प्रगेऽत्र हि ।
सरोऽवतीर्णा तृषया ग्रस्ता ग्राहेण दैवतः ॥ ३९॥

English
She came here in the morning and gave birth to this boy. Due to thirst she got into the lake and as fate would have it, was seized by the crocodile. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति तस्य समुत्पत्तिं तत्पितुः सङ्गरे मृतिम् ।
तन्मातृमरणं ग्राहात्सर्वं तस्यै न्यवेदयत् ॥ ४०॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Thus he narrated to her all the details, the manner of his birth, the death of his father in the battle and that of his mother due to the crocodile. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा ब्राह्मणी चैव विस्मिताति मुनीश्वर ।
पुनः पप्रच्छ तं भिक्षुं ज्ञानिनं सिद्धरूपकम् ॥ ४१॥

English
O great sage, then the brahmin lady who was surprised asked the mendicant of perfect knowledge in the form of a Siddha, again. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मण्युवाच ।
स राजोऽस्य पिता भिक्षो वरभोगान्तरैव हि ।
कस्माच्छाल्वैः स्वरिपुभिः स्वल्पेहैश्च विघातितः ॥ ४२॥

English
*The brahmin lady said :—*  O mendicant, how was his father, the king, killed in the midst of his enjoyment of excellent pleasures, by his enemies the Śālvas in a few days ? (42.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्मादस्य शिशोर्माता ग्राहेणाशु सुभक्षिता ।
यस्मादनाथोऽयं जातो विबन्धुश्चैव जन्मतः ॥ ४३॥

English
How was the mother of this infant devoured by the crocodile so suddenly ? How did he happen to be orphaned and helpless ever since his birth ? (43.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्मात्सुतो ममापीह सुदरिद्रो हि भिक्षुकः ।
भवेत्कथं सुखं भिक्षो पुत्रयोरनयोर्वद ॥ ४४॥

English
How is it that my son too is very poor and a beggar ? O mendicant, how will these two sons attain happiness ? Please tell me. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति तस्या वचः श्रुत्वा स भिक्षुः परमेश्वरः ।
विप्रपत्न्याः प्रसन्नात्मा प्रोवाच विहसँश्च ताम् ॥ ४५॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing these words of the brahmin wife, the delighted mendicant, lord Śiva himself, said laughingly. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
भिक्षुवर्य उवाच ।
विप्रपत्नि विशेषेण सर्वप्रश्नान्वदामि ते ।
श‍ृणु त्वं सावधानेन चरित्रमिदमुत्तमम् ॥ ४६॥

English
*The mendicant said :—* O brahmin lady, I shall answer all questions in detail. Listen with attention to this excellent narrative. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अमुष्य बालस्य पिता स विदर्भमहीपतिः ।
पूर्वजन्मनि पाण्ड्योऽसौ बभूव नृपसत्तमः ॥ ४७॥

English
The father of this boy, the king of Vidarbha, was the excellent king Pāṇḍya⁸⁰ in his previous birth. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
स शैवनृपतिर्धर्मात्पालयन्निखिलां महीम् ।
स्वप्रजां रञ्जयामास सर्वोपद्रवनाशनः ॥ ४८॥

English
That king was a devotee of Śiva. He ruled over the entire earth virtuously. Quelling all disturbances he made all the subjects happy and contented. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्स हि सर्वेशं प्रदोषे पर्यपूजयत् ।
त्रयोदश्यां निराहारो दिवानक्तव्रती शिवम् ॥ ४९॥

English
Observing fast and other rites in the day and night on a Trayodaśī (thirteenth) day, once he worshipped Śiva, the lord of all, at dusk. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पूजयतः शम्भुं प्रदोषे गिरिशं रते ।
महान् शब्दो बभूवाथ विकटः सर्वथा पुरे ॥ ५०॥

English
As he was worshipping Śiva at dusk in the course of his holy rites there arose a great noise, in the city, hideous in every respect. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
तमाकर्ण्य रवं सोऽथ राजा त्यक्तशिवार्चनः ।
रिप्वागमनशङ्कातो निर्ययौ भवनाद् बहिः ॥ ५१॥

English
On hearing that noise, the king left the worship of Śiva unfinished and went out of his palace suspecting the arrival of enemies. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नेव काले तु तस्यामात्यो महाबली ।
गृहीतशत्रुसामन्तो राजान्तिकमुपाययौ ॥ ५२॥

English
In the meantime his powerful minister came near the king catching hold of the enemy king.⁸¹ (52.)
