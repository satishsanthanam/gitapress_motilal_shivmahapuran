### **CHAPTER THIRTYTWO - (The incarnation of Śiva named Sureśvara) •**

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु तात प्रवक्ष्यामि शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
सुरेश्वरावतारस्ते धौम्याग्रजहितावहम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O dear, listen, I shall narrate the incarnation of Śiva, the great soul, as Indra in which he was beneficial to Dhaumya's elder brother. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याघ्रपादसुतो धीमानुपमन्युः सतां प्रियः ।
जन्मान्तरेण संसिद्धः प्राप्तो मुनिकुमारताम् ॥ २॥

English
The intelligent son of Vyāghrapāda, Upamanyu, the beloved of the good, became the son of a sage in another birth. He achieved great attainments then. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
उवास मातुलगृहे स मात्रा शिशुरेव हि ।
उपमन्युर्व्याघ्रपादिः स्याद्दरिद्रश्च दैवतः ॥ ३॥

English
Upamanyu, the son of Vyāghrapāda, while yet a small child stayed in his uncle's house along with his mother. Due to adverse fate he was very poor. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्क्षीरमत्यल्पं पीतवान्मातुलाश्रमे ।
ययाचे मातरं प्रीत्या बहुशो दुग्धलालसः ॥ ४॥

English
Once he tasted a little milk in his uncle's hermitage. Approaching his mother he implored for milk, yearning for it. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा पुत्रवचनं तन्माता च तपस्विनी ।
सान्तः प्रविश्याथ तदा शुभोपायमरीरचत् ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing the words of her son the pitiable mother went in and thought of a proper means to satisfy the child. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
उञ्छवृत्त्यर्जितान्बीजान्पिष्ट्वालोड्य जलेन तान् ।
उपलाल्य सुतं तस्मै सा ददौ कृत्रिमं पयः ॥ ६॥

English
She ground some seeds and grains she got by gleaning, into a paste with water. She gave this artificial milk to her son after fondling and coaxing him. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
पीत्वा च कृत्रिमं दुग्धं मात्रा दत्तं स बालकः ।
नैतत्क्षीरमिति प्राह मातरं चारुदत्पुनः ॥ ७॥

English
After drinking the artificial milk given by his mother the boy said "O, this is not milk" and cried again. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा सुतस्य रुदितं प्राह सा दुःखिता सुतम् ।
सम्मार्ज्य नेत्रे पुत्रस्य कराभ्यां कमलाकृतिः ॥ ८॥

English
On hearing the cry of her son, she wiped off his lotus-like eyes and said in grief. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मातोवाच ।
क्षीरमत्र कुतोऽस्माकं वने निवसतां सदा ।
प्रसादेन विना शम्भोः पयः प्राप्तिर्भवेन्न हि ॥ ९॥

English
The mother said :—  How can we the dwellers of the forest for ever have milk? Without the grace of Śiva we can never have milk. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वजन्मनि यत्कृत्यं शिवमुद्दिश्य हे सुत ।
तदेव लभ्यते नूनं नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १०॥

English
O son, only that can be attained what we had dedicated to Śiva in our previous birth. We need not worry in this matter. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
इति मातृवचः श्रुत्वा व्याघ्रपादिः स बालकः ।
प्रत्युवाच विशोकात्मा मातरं मातृवत्सलः ॥ ११॥

English
On hearing the words of his mother, the son of Vyāghrapāda excessively fond of his mother, freed of his grief, replied to her. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शोकेनालमिमं मातः शम्भुर्यद्यस्ति शङ्करः ।
त्यज शोकं महाभागे सर्वं भद्रं भविष्यति ॥ १२॥

English
"O blessed mother, do not grieve. Cast off this sorrow. If Śiva is the benefactor, everything will result in welfare. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु मातर्वचो मेऽद्य महादेवोऽस्ति चेत्क्वचित् ।
चिराद्वा ह्यचिराद्वापि क्षीरोदं साधयाम्यहम् ॥ १३॥

English
O mother, listen to me. If there is lord Śiva anywhere, sooner or later I shall achieve the ocean of milk". (13.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स शिशुः प्रीत्या शिवं मेऽस्त्वित्युदीर्य च ।
विसृज्य तां सुप्रणम्य तपः कर्तुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ १४॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  After saying thus with pleasure and repeating "Let there be a welfare", he bowed to his mother and left her. He began to perform penance. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमवत्पर्वतगतः वायुभक्षः समाहितः ।
अष्टेष्टकाभिः प्रासादं कृत्वा लिङ्गं च मृन्मयम् ॥ १५॥

तत्रावाह्य शिवं साम्बं भक्त्या पञ्चाक्षरेण ह ।
पत्रपुष्पादिभिर्वन्यैः समानर्च शिशुः स वै ॥ १६॥

English
It was on the mountain Himavat that he went to perform his penance with pure mind taking nothing in except air. He installed Siva's phallic image of clay after making a pedestal for it with eight bricks. He invoked Siva along with Parvati into the phallic image with devotion. He repeated the five-syllabled mantra.83 The boy worshipped Siva with leaves, flowers and other things. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यात्वा शिवं च तं साम्बं जपन्पञ्चाक्षरं मनुम् ।
समभ्यर्च्य चिरं कालं चचार परमं तपः ॥ १७॥

English
He meditated on Siva along with Parvati repeating the five-syllabled mantra. Worshipping thus he performed great penance for a long time. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा तस्य बालस्य ह्युपमन्योर्महात्मनः ।
चराचरं च भुवनं प्रदीपितमभून्मुने ॥ १८॥

English
O sage, due to the penance of the boy Upamanyu, the great soul, the whole world consisting of the mobile and immobile beings blazed as it were. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शम्भुर्विष्ण्वाद्यैः प्रार्थितः प्रभुः ।
परीक्षितुं च तद्भक्तिं शक्ररूपोऽभवत्तदा ॥ १९॥

English
In the meantime, requested by Visnu aud others, Siva assumed the form of Indra in order to test his devotion. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवा शचीस्वरूपाभूद्गणाः सर्वेऽभवन्सुराः ।
ऐरावतगजो नन्दी सर्वमेव च तन्मयम् ॥ २०॥

English
Parvati took the form of Saci. The Ganas became the gods, Nandin became the elephant Airavata. Everything belonging to Siva became that of Indra. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः साम्बः शिवः शक्रस्वरूपः सगणो द्रुतम् ।
जगामानुग्रहं कर्तुमुपमन्योस्तदाश्रमम् ॥ २१॥

English
Then Siva accompanied by Parvati and the Ganas, and assuming the form of Indra went to the hermitage of Upamanyu in order to bless him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
परीक्षितुं च तद्भक्तिं शक्ररूपधरो हरः ।
प्राह गम्भीरया वाचा बालकं तं मुनीश्वर ॥ २२॥

English
O great sage, in order to test his devotion, Siva in the form of Indra, spoke to the boy in a grave tone. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरेश्वर उवाच ।
तुष्टोऽस्मि ते वरं ब्रूहि तपसानेन सुव्रत ।
ददामि चेच्छितान्कामान् सर्वान्नात्रास्ति संशयः ॥ २३॥

English
Indra said: —⁸⁴  "O boy of good rites, I am pleased with you and your penance. Mention the boon you wish to have. I shall give you whatever you desire. There is no doubt about this." (23.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्तः स वै तेन शक्ररूपेण शम्भुना ।
वरयामि शिवे भक्तिमित्युवाच कृताञ्जलिः ॥ २४॥

English
Thus addressed by Siva in the form of Indra, the boy replied with palms joined in reverence, "I choose devotion into Siva." (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्निशम्य हरिः प्राह मां न जानासि लेखपम् ।
त्रैलोक्याधिपतिं शक्रं सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ॥ २५॥

English
On hearing it Indra said—“You do not know me the lord of the gods, the lord of the three worlds, Indra, bowed to by all the gods. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्भक्तो भव विप्रर्षे मामेवार्चय सर्वदा ।
ददामि सर्वं भद्रं ते त्यज रुद्रं च निर्गुणम् ॥ २६॥

English
O brahmin sage, be my devotee. Worship me alone on every occasion. I shall grant you every benefit. Leave off Rudra devoid of attributes. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रेण निर्गुणेनालं किं ते कार्यं भविष्यति ।
देवजातिबहिर्भूतो यः पिशाचत्वमागतः ॥ २७॥

English
Enough of Rudra devoid of attributes. What purpose of yours can be achieved by his worship? He is out of the caste of the gods. He has become a Piśāca. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा स मुनेः पुत्रो जपन्पञ्चाक्षरं मनुम् ।
मन्यमानो धर्मविघ्नं प्राह तं कर्तुमागतम् ॥ २८॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  On hearing these words, the son of the sage considered them an obstacle to his austerities. Repeating the five-sillabled mantra he told him who had come to create obstacles. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
त्वयैवं कथितं सर्वं भवनिन्दारतेन वै ।
प्रसङ्गाद्देवदेवस्य निर्गुणत्वं पिशाचता ॥ २९॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  All this has been mentioned by you indulging in insult of Śiva. Incidentally you have mentioned that Śiva, the lord of the gods, is attributeless as well as Piśāca. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं न जानासि वै रुद्रं सर्वदेवेश्वरेश्वरम् ।
ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेशानां जनकं प्रकृतेः परम् ॥ ३०॥

English
You do not know Śiva, lord of the chiefs of gods, the father and progenitor of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Maheśa and greater than Prakṛti. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सदसद् वयक्तमव्यक्तं यमाहुर्ब्रह्मवादिनः ।
नित्यमेकमनेकं च वरं तस्माद् वृणोम्यहम् ॥ ३१॥

English
I choose the boon from that deity, whom the philosophers call eternal, the single, the multitude, separate from the existent and non-existent and the unmanifest. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हेतुवादविनिर्मुक्तं साङ्ख्ययोगार्थदं परम् ।
यमुशन्ति हि तत्त्वज्ञा वरं तस्माद् वृणोम्यहम् ॥ ३२॥

English
I choose the boon from that deity whom the knowers of reality consider free from causations, disputes and the bestower of the principles of Sāṅkhya and Yoga. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्ति शम्भोः परं तत्त्वं सर्वकारणकारणत् ।
ब्रह्मविष्ण्वादिदेवानां श्रेष्ठो गुणपराद्विभोः ॥ ३३॥

English
There is no greater entity than lord Śiva who is beyond the attributes. He is the cause of all causes. He is the most excellent of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other gods. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
नाहं वृणे वरं त्वत्तो न विष्णोर्ब्रह्मणोऽपि वा ।
नान्यस्मादमराद्वापि शङ्करो वरदोऽस्तु मे ॥ ३४॥

English
I do not wish to take a boon from you or Brahmā or Viṣṇu. Nor do I want any boon from any other god. Let Śiva be the bestower of boons on me. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुनात्र किमुक्तेन वच्मि तत्त्वं मतं स्वकम् ।
न प्रार्थये पशुपतेरन्यं देवादिकं स्फुटम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Of what avail is speaking much ? I tell you the truth and my decision. I do not request any other god except Śiva. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्भावं श‍ृणु गोत्रारे मयाद्यानुमितं त्विदम् ।
भवान्तरे कृतं पापं श्रुता निन्दा भवस्य चेत् ॥ ३६॥

श्रुत्वा निन्दां भवस्याथ तत्क्षणादेव सन्त्यजेत् ।
स्वदेहं तन्निहत्याशु शिवलोकं स गच्छति ॥ ३७॥

English
O enemy of mountains, listen to my feeling in this matter. I infer that I have committed sins in the previous birth since I am forced to hear the insult to Śiva, on hearing which one shall shed his own mortal body at the very same moment. He goes to Śiva's region thereby. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
आस्तां तावन्ममेच्छेयं क्षीरं प्रति सुराधम ।
निहत्य त्वां शिवास्त्रेण त्यजाम्येतत्कलेवरम् ॥ ३८॥

English
O base god, let it be that I had yearned for milk. I shall kill you by Śiva's missile and then leave off my mortal frame. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वोपमन्युस्तं मर्तुं व्यवसितः स्वयम् ।
क्षीरे वाच्छामपि त्यक्त्वा निहन्तुं शक्रमुद्यतः ॥ ३९॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  After saying thus, Upamanyu who was prepared to die, cast off his desire for milk and got ready to kill Indra. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
भस्मादाय तदाधारादघोस्त्राभिमन्त्रितम् ।
विसृज्य शक्रमुद्दिश्य ननाद स मुनिस्तदा ॥ ४०॥

English
He took some ashes from the receptacle and invoked it with the mantra Aghorāstra. The sage, thereafter discharged it against Indra and roared. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृत्वा स्वेष्टपदद्वन्द्वं स्वदेहं दग्धुमुद्यतः ।
आग्नेयीं धारणां बिभ्रदुपमन्युरवस्थितः ॥ ४१॥

English
Remembering the feet of Siva, his favourite deity, he got ready to burn himself. He stood there bearing the fiery pose. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं व्यवसिते विप्रे भगवान् शक्ररूपवान् ।
वारयामास सौम्येन धारणां तस्य योगिनः ॥ ४२॥

English
When the brahmin was engaged thus, the lord in the form of Indra warded off the fiery pose of the Yogin by gentle means. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

जगृहे मध्यतः क्षिप्तं नन्दी शङ्करवल्लभम् ॥ ४३॥

English
At the behest of Siva, Nandin the favourite of Siva delightedly caught hold of the Aghorastra discharged by him, in the middle. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

दर्शयामास विप्राय बालेन्दुकृतशेखरम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Assuming his own form with the moon for his crest, lord Siva revealed it to the brahmin. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

भक्ष्यभोज्यार्णवं तस्मै दर्शयामास स प्रभुः ॥ ४५॥

English
The lord then showed him a thousand milk oceans, the ocean of curds and the ocean of different foodstuffs. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Thus Siva was seen seated on his bull accompanied by the goddess. He was surrounded by the leading Ganas and the divine weapons, the trident and others. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The drums were sounded in the heaven. There was a shower of flowers. All the ten quarters were covered by Brahma, Visnu and other gods. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then Upamanyu was swept by the waves of the ocean of delight and he prostrated on the ground like a staff, with his mind humble with devotion. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Śiva said :— “Dear Upamanyu, I am pleased with your excellent behaviour. O brahmin sage, you are very firm in your devotion. I just desired to know you thoroughly. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Enjoy the pleasure of eating sumptuously forever along with your kinsmen. Be ever happy and free from misery. Be devoted to me. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O fortunate Upamanyu, you have been made eternally my son today. Pārvatī, here, is your mother. (52.)

---

तुभ्यं दत्ता मया प्रीत्या त्वं गृह्णीष्व महामुने ।

English
O great sage, thousands of oceans of milk, curd, ghee and honey, and of different foodstuffs I grant you lovingly. Accept them. I confer on you the state of a god and the eternal chieftainship of Gaṇas. (53-54.)
