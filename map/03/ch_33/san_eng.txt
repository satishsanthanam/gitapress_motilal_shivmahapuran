### **CHAPTER THIRTYTHREE - (Śiva's incarnation as the student-ascetic)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सुप्रीत्या शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अवतारं श‍ृणु विभोर्जटिलाह्वं सुपावनम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O Sanatkumāra, listen to the incarnation of Śiva the great soul. It is named Jaṭila and is very sanctifying. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा सती दक्षकन्या त्यक्त्वा देहं पितुर्मखे ।
स्वपित्रानादृता जज्ञे मेनायां हिमभूधरात् ॥ २॥

English
After abandoning her body when she was insulted by her own father in the sacrificial hall, Sati, the daughter of Dakṣa, was born of Menā and the mountain Himavat. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सा गत्वा गहनेऽरण्ये तेपे सुविमलं तपः ।
शङ्करं पतिमिच्छन्ती सखीभ्यां संयुता शिवा ॥ ३॥

English
Desiring to attain Śiva as her husband, she went to the dense forest and performed a pure penance. She was attended upon by two of her friends. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्तपः सुपरीक्षार्थं सप्तर्षीन्प्रैषयच्छिवः ।
तपःस्थानं तु पार्वत्या नानालीलाविशारदः ॥ ४॥

English
In order to test her penance, Śiva an expert in various kinds of sports sent the seven sages⁸⁵ to the penance grove of Pārvatī. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ते गत्वा तत्र मुनयः परीक्षां चक्रुरादरात् ।
तस्याः सुयत्नतो नैव समर्था ह्यभवंश्च ते ॥ ५॥

English
The sages went there and thoroughly tested her. As she was strenuous in her effort they could not dissuade her from her penance. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य शिवं नत्वा वृत्तान्तं च निवेद्य तत् ।
तदाज्ञां समनुप्राप्य स्वर्लोकं जग्मुरादरात् ॥ ६॥

English
They returned and bowed to Śiva. After mentioning all the details to him and obtaining his permission they returned to heaven eagerly. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
गतेषु तेषु मुनिषु स्वस्थानं शङ्करः स्वयम् ।
परीक्षितुं शिवावृत्तमैच्छत्सूतिकरः प्रभुः ॥ ७॥

English
When they had gone to their abodes, lord Śiva, the cause of excellent sports, desired to test Śiva's activity himself. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रसन्नस्तपस्वीच्छाशमनादयमीश्वरः ।
ब्रह्मचर्यस्वरूपोऽभूत्तदाद्भुततरः प्रभुः ॥ ८॥

English
Then the lord assumed the pleasant, self-controlled and wonderful form of a student-ascetic. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीव स्थविरो विप्रदेहधारी स्वतेजसा ।
प्रज्वलन्मनसा हृष्टो दण्डी छत्री महोज्जलः ॥ ९॥

English
He looked very old and had assumed the body of a brahmin. He was shining with brilliance. He had a staff and an umbrella. He was mentally delighted. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
धृत्वैवं जाटिलं रूपं जगाम गिरिजावनम् ।
अतिप्रीतियुतः शम्भुः शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १०॥

English
Assuming this form of a Jaṭila (one with matted hair), the delighted Śiva, favourably disposed to his devotees went to the penance-grove of Pārvatī. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापश्यत् स्थितां देवीं सखीभिः परिवारिताम् ।
वेदिकोपरि शुद्धान्तां शिवामिव विधोः कलाम् ॥ ११॥

English
There he saw the goddess Pārvatī, seated on the dais surrounded by her friends. She appeared like the pure digit of the moon. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुर्निरीक्ष्य तां देवीं ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपवान् ।
उपकण्ठं ययौ प्रीत्या चोत्सुकी भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing the goddess, Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees and who had assumed the form of a student-ascetic approached her eagerly and lovingly. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
आगतं सा तदा दृष्ट्वा ब्राह्मणं तेजसाद्भुतम् ।
अङ्गेषु लोमशं शान्तं दण्डचर्मसमन्वितम् ॥ १३॥

ब्रह्मचर्यधरं वृद्धं जटिलं सकमण्डलुम् ।
अपूजयत्परप्रीत्या सर्वपूजोपहारकैः ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing the brahmin visitor of wonderful splendour, quiet with hairy limbs, bearing the staff and hide of deer, the aged bachelor with matted hair and the water-pot, she welcomed and worshipped him with all the articles of worship. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सा पार्वती देवी पूजितं परया मुदा ।
कुशलं पर्यपृच्छत्तं ब्रह्मचारिणमादरात् ॥ १५॥

English
The goddess Pārvatī enquired after the health of that aged bachelor respectfully after worshipping him with great joy. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपेण कस्त्वं हि कुत आगतः ।
इद्ं वनं भासयसि वद वेदविदां वर ॥ १६॥

English
"O most excellent one among the knowers of the Vedas, who are you, please, in the guise of a student-ascetic and whence have you come illuminating this forest? (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति पृष्टस्तु पार्वत्या ब्रह्मचारी स वै द्विजः ।
प्रत्युवाच द्रुतं प्रीत्या शिवाभावपरीक्षया ॥ १७॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On being asked thus by Pārvatī, that brahmin replied immediately with great pleasure in order to test the feelings of Śivā. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचार्युवाच ।
अहमिच्छाभिगामी च ब्रह्मचारी द्विजोऽस्मि वै ।
तपस्वी सुखदोऽन्येषामुपकारी न संशयः ॥ १८॥

English
The student-ascetic said :  I am a brahmin bachelor going about here and there as I please. I am a saint pleasing and helping others undoubtedly. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ब्रह्मचारी स शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
तस्थिवानुपकण्ठं स गोपायन् रूपमात्मनः ॥ १९॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After saying this, the bachelor Śiva, who is favourably disposed to his devotees, stood near her concealing his real form. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचार्युवाच ।
किं ब्रवीमि महादेवि कथनीयं न विद्यते ।
महानर्थकरं वृत्तं दृश्यते विकृतं महत् ॥ २०॥

English
The student-ascetic said :- What shall I say, O great lady? There is nothing to be mentioned. A great calamity seems to have happened, extremely confusing.[86] (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नवे वयसि सद्भोगसाधने सुखकारणे ।
महोपचारसद्भोगैर्वृथैव त्वं तपस्यसि ॥ २१॥

English
In your fresh youth, the means of good enjoyment and the cause of happiness through all requisite articles of pleasure, you are performing this penance in vain. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
का त्वं कस्यासि तनया किमर्थं विजने वने ।
तपश्चरसि दुर्धर्षं मुनिभिः प्रयतात्मभिः ॥ २२॥

English
Who are you? Whose daughter are you? Why are you performing a penance in this desolate forest? Your penance is such as cannot be outdone even by sages of elevated souls. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा प्रहस्य परमेश्वरी ।
उवाच वचनं प्रीत्या ब्रह्मचारिणमुत्तमम् ॥ २३॥

English
Nandisvara said :—  On hearing his words, Pārvatī laughed and spoke to the excellent student ascetic with reverence. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
श‍ृणु विप्र ब्रह्मचारिन् मद्वृत्तमखिलं मुने ।
जन्म मे भारते वर्षे साम्प्रतं हिमवद्गृहे ॥ २४॥

English
Pārvatī said :—  O sage, O brahmin bachelor, listen to the details of my wife. I am born in Bhārata in the house of Himavat. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वं दक्षगृहे जन्म सती शङ्करकामिनी ।
योगेन त्यक्तदेहाहं तातेन पतिनिन्दिना ॥ २५॥

English
Formerly my birth was in the house of Dakṣa. I was then Sati, the wife of Siva. On account of my father's insulting my husband I cast off my body by means of Yoga. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र जन्मनि सम्प्राप्तः सुपुण्येन शिवो द्विज ।
मां त्यक्त्वा भस्मसात्कृत्वा मन्मथं स जगाम ह ॥ २६॥

English
O brahmin, in this birth too, Śiva came my way due to merit but he reduced Kāma to ashes, abandoned me and went away. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रयाते शङ्करे तापाद् व्रीडिताहं पितुर्गृहात् ।
आगच्छमत्र तपसे गुरुवाक्येन संयता ॥ २७॥

English
When Śiva went away I was overwhelmed with grief. A bit ashamed, I came away from my house to this place for performing penance after being initiated by the statement of my preceptor. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसा वचसा साक्षात्कर्मणा पतिभावतः ।
सत्यं ब्रवीमि नोऽसत्यं संवृतः शङ्करो मया ॥ २८॥

English
Mentally, verbally and through activities too, Śiva has been chosen by me as my husband. I am telling you the truth and not falsehood. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
जानामि दुर्लभं वस्तु कथं प्राप्यं मया भवेत् ।
तथापि मनसौत्सुक्यात्तप्यते मे तपोऽधुना ॥ २९॥

English
I know that this is an extremely inaccessible object. How can I achieve it ? Still, thanks to the great zeal, this penance is being pursued by me now. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
हित्वेन्द्रप्रमुखान्देवान्विष्णुं ब्रह्माणमप्यहम् ।
पतिं पिनाकपाणिं वै प्राप्तुमिच्छामि सत्यतः ॥ ३०॥

English
Leaving off Indra and other gods, Viṣṇu and Brahmā I wish to obtain Śiva as my husband really. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा पार्वत्या हि सुनिश्चितम् ।
मुने स जटिलो रुद्रो विहसन्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ३१॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O sage, on hearing these firm words of Pārvatī that Jaṭila Rudra laughingly spoke again. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
जटिल उवाच ।
हिमाचलसुते देवि का बुद्धिः स्वीकृता त्वया ।
रुद्रार्थं विबुधान्हित्वा करोषि विपुलं तपः ॥ ३२॥

English
Jaṭila said :—  O gentle lady, O daughter of Himavat, what is this idea that has struck you? You are performing this great penance for obtaining Śiva after leaving off all the gods. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
जानाम्यहं च तं रुद्रं श‍ृणु त्वं प्रवदामि ते ।
वृषध्वजः स रुद्रो हि विकृतात्मा जटाधरः ॥ ३३॥

English
I know that Śiva. Listen. I shall tell you that bull-bannered Śiva wears matted hair and is ill-mannered. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाकी च सदा नित्यं विरागी च विशेषतः ।
तस्मात्त्वं तेन रुद्रेण मनो योक्तुं न चार्हसि ॥ ३४॥

English
He is an isolationist for ever. Particularly he is a detached fellow. Hence it does not behove you to get mentally entangled with him. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं विरुद्धं रूपादि तव देवि हरस्य च ।
मह्यं न रोचते ह्येतद्यदीच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ३५॥

English
O gentle lady, your features etc. and those of Śiva are ill-matched. I do not like this. But do as you please. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा च पुना रुद्रो ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपवान् ।
निनिन्द बहुधात्मानं तदग्रे तां परीक्षितुम् ॥ ३६॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After saying this, Śiva in the guise of a student-ascetic rebuked himself in her presence in order to test her. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा पार्वती देवी विप्रवाक्यं दुरासदम् ।
प्रत्युवाच महाक्रुद्धा शिवनिन्दापरं च तम् ॥ ३७॥

English
On hearing his intolerable words, Pārvatī replied extremely infuriated at him who was engrossed in insulting Śiva. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एतावद्धि मया ज्ञातं कश्चिद् धन्यो भविष्यति ।
परन्तु सकलं ज्ञातमवध्यो दृश्यतेऽधुना ॥ ३८॥

English
Till now I thought that you would be some one fit to be killed. But now it is quite clear that you are not to be killed. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपेण कश्चित्त्वं धूर्त आगतः ।
शिवनिन्दा कृता मूढ त्वया मन्युरभून्मम ॥ ३९॥

English
You are some foolish knave in the guise of a student-ascetic. O fool, Śiva has been insulted by you. Hence, I am infuriated. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवं त्वं च न जानासि शिवात्त्वं हि बहिर्मुखः ।
त्वत्पूजा च कृता यन्मे तस्मात्तापयुताभवम् ॥ ४०॥

English
You do not know Śiva. You are averse to Śiva. Since I had already welcomed and honoured you I am now distressed. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनिन्दां करोतीह तत्त्वमज्ञाय यः पुमान् ।
आजन्मसञ्चितं पुण्यं तस्य भस्मीभवत्युत ॥ ४१॥

English
The accumulated merits of the person who insults Śiva without knowing reality are reduced to ashes. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवविद्वेषिणं स्पृष्ट्वा प्रायश्चित्तं समाचरेत् ॥ ४२॥

English
One should perform expiation after touching a Śiva-hater. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
रे रे दुष्ट त्वया प्रोक्तमहं जानामि शङ्करम् ।
निश्चयेन न विज्ञातः शिव एव परः प्रभुः ॥ ४३॥

English
O wicked fellow, you said I know Śiva. Certainly he is not known to you. Śiva alone is the great lord. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा तथा भवेद्रुद्रो मायया बहुरूपवान् ।
ममाभीष्टप्रदोऽत्यन्तं निर्विकारः सतां प्रियः ॥ ४४॥

English
Assuming many guises through his Māyā, Rudra may appear in any form. But the beloved of the good, having no aberrations, lavishly bestows all desired things on me. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तं शिवा देवी शिवतत्त्वं जगाद सा ।
यत्र ब्रह्मतया रुद्रः कथ्यते निर्गुणोऽव्ययः ॥ ४५॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After saying this, Pārvatī expatiated on Śiva's principle, where Śiva was explained as attributeless and unchanging performing the penance of Brahman. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाकर्ण्य वचो देव्या ब्रह्मचारी स वै द्विजः ।
पुनर्वचनमादातुं यावदेव प्रचक्रमे ॥ ४६॥

प्रोवाच गिरिजा तावत्स्वसखीं विजयां द्रुतम् ।
शिवासक्तमनोवृत्तिः शिवनिन्दापराङ्मुखी ॥ ४७॥

English
On hearing her words, the brahmin began to say something; but by that time, Pārvatī whose mind was attached to Śiva and who was averse to Śiva being insulted, immediately spoke to her friend Vijayā. (46-47.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिजोवाच ।
वारणीयः प्रयत्नेन सख्ययं हि द्विजाधमः ।
पुनर्वक्तुमनाश्चायं शिवनिन्दां करिष्यति ॥ ४८॥

English
Pārvatī said:—  Friend, this base brahmin must be prevented. He is going to speak again. He is sure to insult Śiva. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
न केवलं भवेत्पापं निन्दाकर्तुः शिवस्य हि ।
यो वै श‍ृणोति तन्निन्दां पापभाक्स भवेदिह ॥ ४९॥

English
It is not only the censurer of Śiva who becomes sinful but also he who listens to such insults. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनिन्दाकरो वध्यः सर्वथा शिवकिङ्करैः ।
ब्राह्मणश्चेत्स वै त्याज्यो गन्तव्यं तत्स्थलाद्द्रुतम् ॥ ५०॥

English
A rebuker of Śiva shall be slain by all means by Śiva's attendants. If he happens to be a brahmin he shall be abandoned. He shall go away from that place immediately. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं दुष्टः पुनर्निन्दां करिष्यति शिवस्य हि ।
ब्राह्मणत्वादवध्यश्च त्याज्योऽदृश्यश्च सर्वथा ॥ ५१॥

English
This wicked fellow will insult Śiva again. Being a brahmin he cannot be killed. He shall be abandoned. He should not even be looked at. (51.)

---

English
Let us leave this place and go elsewhere. Do not delay, lest there should be any further talk with this ignorant man. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा चोमया यावत्पदमुत्क्षिप्यते मुने ।
असौ तावच्छिवः साक्षादाललम्बे पटं स्वयम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O sage, after saying this Pārvatī was just to walk away when Śiva revealed himself and caught hold of her cloth. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा स्वरूपं दिव्यं च शिवाध्यानं यथा तथा ।
दर्शयित्वा शिवायै तामुवाचावाङ्मुखीं शिवः ॥ ५४॥

English
After assuming that divine form which Pārvatī was meditating upon, Śiva spoke to her as she stood face downwards. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
कुत्र त्वं यासि मां हित्वा न त्वं त्याज्या मया शिवे ।
मया परीक्षितासि त्वं दृढभक्तासि मेऽनघे ॥ ५५॥

English
Śiva said :—  “O Pārvatī, where are you going, leaving me ? You cannot be forsaken by me. You have been tested by me O faultless one, you are really steady in your devotion to me. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मचारिस्वरूपेण भावमिच्छुस्त्वदीयकम् ।
तवोपकण्ठमागत्य प्रावोचं विविधं वचः ॥ ५६॥

English
Desirous of knowing your feelings I have approached you in the guise of a student-ascetic and spoke these diverse words. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि दृढं भक्त्या शिवे तव विशेषतः ।
चित्तेप्सितं वरं ब्रूहि नादेयं विद्यते तव ॥ ५७॥

English
O Pārvatī, I am delighted at your steady devotion. Mention the boon that you have cherished in your mind. There is nothing that cannot be given to you. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यप्रभृति ते दासस्तपोभिः प्रेमनिर्भरे ।
कृतोऽस्मि तव सौन्दर्यात्क्षण एको युगायते ॥ ५८॥

English
O loving lady, I have been made your slave from now onwards by means of your penance. Due to your beauty, even a moment seems to expand into a Yoga. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
त्यज्यतां च त्वया लज्जा मम पत्नी सनातनी ।
एहि प्रिये त्वया साकं द्रुतं यामि स्वकं गिरिम् ॥ ५९॥

English
Leave off your bashfulness. You are my eternal wife. O beloved, come on, I shall go to my hilly abode along with you.” (59.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तवति देवेशे शिवातिमुदमाप सा ।
तपोदुःखं तु यत्सर्वं तज्जहौ द्रुतमेव हि ॥ ६०॥

English
When the lord of gods said thus, Pārvatī rejoiced much. Whatever misery she experienced in performing the penance, she cast off immediately. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रहृष्टा सा दृष्ट्वा दिव्यरूपं शिवस्य तत् ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रभुं प्रीत्या लज्जयाधोमुखी शिवा ॥ ६१॥

English
On seeing that divine form of Śiva, Pārvatī replied to the lord with face lowered due to bashfulness. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश करोषि च कृपां मयि ।
पतिर्मे भव देवेश इत्युक्तः शिवया शिवः ॥ ६२॥

नन्दीश्वर उवाच
गृहीत्वा विधिवत्पाणिं कैलासं स तया ययौ ।
पतिं तं गिरिजा प्राप्य देवकार्यं चकार सा ॥ ६३॥

English
Pārvatī said :—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if you have pity on me, be my lord and husband.” Nandīśvara said :— Thus requested by Pārvatī Śiva took her hand duly and went to Kailāsa along with her. On getting him as her lord, she carried out the task of the gods. (62-63.)

English
This narrative explained by me is faultless and magnificent. He who listens to this with pleasure becomes happy and attains salvation. (64.)
