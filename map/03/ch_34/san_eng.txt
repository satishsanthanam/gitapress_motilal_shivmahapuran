### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - (The incarnation of Śiva as Sunartaka naṭa) .**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अवतारं श‍ृणु विभोः सुनर्तकनटाह्वयम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O omniscient Sanatkumāra, listen to the incarnation of lord Śiva, the great soul, as Sunartaka naṭa. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा हि कालिका देवी पार्वती हिमवत्सुता ।
तेपे तपः सुविमलं वनं गत्वा शिवाप्तये ॥ २॥

तदा शिवः प्रसन्नोऽभूत्तस्याः सुतपसो मुने ।
तद्वृत्तसुपरीक्षार्थं वरं दातुं मुदा ययौ ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, when goddess Pārvatī, daughter of Himavat, performed the pious penance in order to attain Śiva, after going to the forest, Śiva was much delighted at her austerity. He approached to test her conduct and out of pleasure to grant her the desired boon. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वरूपं दर्शयामास तस्यै सुप्रीतमानसः ।
वरं ब्रूहीति चोवाच तां शिवां शङ्करो मुने ॥ ४॥

English
O sage, the delighted Śiva revealed his real form to her and told Pārvatī, “Mention the boon you wish to have.” (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा शम्भुवचनं दृष्ट्वा तद्रूपमुत्तमम् ।
सुजहर्ष शिवातीव प्राह तं सुप्रणम्य सा ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing the words of Śiva and on seeing his excellent form, Pārvatī was delighted. She spoke to him after paying her homage. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश मह्यं देयो वरो यदि ।
पतिर्भव ममेशान कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ ६॥

English
Pārvatī said :—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if a boon shall be granted to me, O Śiva, you be my lord and husband. Please take pity on me. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुर्गृहे मया सम्यग्गम्यते त्वदनुज्ञया ।
गन्तव्यं भवता नाथ मत्पितुः पार्श्वतः प्रभो ॥ ७॥

English
With your permission I shall go to my father's abode. O lord, you shall approach my father. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
याचस्व मां ततो भिक्षुः ख्यापयंश्च यशः शुभम् ।
पितुर्मे सफलं सर्वं कुरु प्रीत्या गृहाश्रमम् ॥ ८॥

English
In the form of a mendicant and making your spotless fame known, beg me of my father and make the householder's life fruitful. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो यथोक्तविधिना कर्तुमर्हसि भो प्रभो ।
विवाहं त्वं महेशान देवानां कार्यसिद्धये ॥ ९॥

English
O lord Śiva, it behoves to marry me with due rites for the fulfilment of the task of the gods. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कामं मे पूरय विभो निर्विकारो भवान्सदा ।
भक्तवत्सलनामा हि तव भक्तास्म्यहं सदा ॥ १०॥

English
O lord, you are without aberrations. Please fulfil my desires. You are favourably disposed to the devotees. I am always your devotee. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः स तया शम्भुर्महेशो भक्तवत्सलः ।
तथास्त्विति वचः प्रोच्यान्तर्हितः स्वगिरिं ययौ ॥ ११॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  Thus requested by her, Śiva who is favourably disposed towards his devotees said “So be it” and vanished from there. He returned to his mountainous abode. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्यपि ततः प्रीत्या स्वसखीभ्यां समन्विता ।
जगाम स्वपितुर्गेहं रूपं कृत्वा तु सार्थकम् ॥ १२॥

English
After making her beauty fruitful, Pārvatī returned to her father's abode jealously in the company of her female friends. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्यागमनं श्रुत्वा मेनया स हिमाचलः ।
परिवारयुतो द्रष्टुं स्वसुतां तां ययौ मुदा ॥ १३॥

English
On hearing Pārvatī's return, Himavat accompanied by his attendants and his wife Menā joyously came to see his daughter. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तां सुप्रसन्नास्यामानयामासतुर्गृहम् ।
कारयामासतुः प्रीत्या महानन्दौ महोत्सवम् ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing her delighted, both of them received her suitably within the house and glad at heart celebrated the event with pleasure. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
धनं ददौ द्विजादिभ्यो मेना गिरिवरस्तथा ।
मङ्गलं कारयामास सवेदध्वनिमादरात् ॥ १५॥

English
Menā and the lord of mountains made gifts to brahmins and others. The auspicious mantras of the Vedas were chanted. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्वकन्यया सार्धमुवास प्राङ्गणे मुदा ।
मेना च हिमवाञ्छैलः स्नातुं गङ्गां जगाम सः ॥ १६॥

English
Then Menā joyously stayed for a while in the yard with her daughter and Himavat went to the Ganges for a bath. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शम्भुः सुलीलो भक्तवत्सलः ।
सुनर्तकनटो भूत्वा मेनकासन्निधिं ययौ ॥ १७॥

English
In the meantime Śiva, favourably disposed towards his devotees and indulging in good sports, assumed the form of a dancer and approached Menaka. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृङ्गं वामे करे धृत्वा दक्षिणे डमरुं तथा ।
पृष्ठे कन्थां रक्तवासा नृत्यगानविशारदः ॥ १८॥

English
He held the horn in his left hand and the small drum in his right. He carried a bundle of patched garments on his back. He wore a red cloth. He appeared to be an expert in dance and music. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तु नटरूपोऽसौ मेनकाप्राङ्गणे मुदा ।
चक्रे स नृत्यं विविधं गानं चाति मनोहरम् ॥ १९॥

English
Then the lord in the form of a dancer performed various dances joyously in the yard of Menakā and sang sweetly. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृङ्गञ्च डमरुं तत्र वादयामास सुध्वनिम् ।
महोतिं विविधां प्रीत्या स चकार मनोहराम् ॥ २०॥

English
He blew on the horn and played on Damaru producing sweet notes. With great pleasure he exhibited a variety of splendid sports. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तं द्रष्टुं नागराः सर्वे पुरुषाश्च स्त्रियस्तथा ।
आजग्मुः सहसा तत्र बाला वृद्धा अपि ध्रुवम् ॥ २१॥

English
The citizens including men, women, children and old women gathered there suddenly to see him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा सुगीतं तं दृष्ट्वा सुनृत्यं च मनोहरम् ।
सहसा मुमुहुः सर्वे मेनापि च तदा मुने ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, hearing the sweet song and witnessing the splendid dance, the people were fascinated and Menā too was highly delighted. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो मेनाशु रत्नानि स्वर्णपात्रस्थितानि च ।
तस्मै दातुं ययौ प्रीत्या तदूतिप्रीतमानसा ॥ २३॥

English
Immediately Menā whose mind was in exultation, thanks to his clever feats, went to give him potfuls of gems and jewels. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तानि न स्वीचकारासौ भिक्षां काङ्क्षे शिवां च ताम् ।
पुनः सुनृत्यं गानं च कौतुकात्कर्तुमुद्यतः ॥ २४॥

English
He did not accept those gems. He wished to have Pārvatī for his alms. Eagerly he began to dance and sing again. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मेना तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा चुकोपाति सुविस्मिता ।
भिक्षुकं भर्त्सयामास बहिष्कर्तुमियेष सा ॥ २५॥

English
On hearing his words Menā was surprised. She became furious as well. She rebuked the mendicant and wanted to drive him out. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र गङ्गातो गिरिराड् ययौ ।
ददर्श पुरतो भिक्षुं प्राङ्गणस्थं नराकृतिम् ॥ २६॥

English
In the meantime the lord of the mountains returned from the Ganges. He saw the mendicant in the form of a man standing before him in the yard. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा मेनामुखाद् वृत्तं तत्सर्वं सुचुकोप सः ।
आज्ञां चकारानुचरान्बहिः कर्तुं च भिक्षुकम् ॥ २७॥

English
On hearing from Menā all the details he too was infuriated. He ordered his attendants to drive out the mendicant. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
महाग्निमिव दुःस्पर्शं प्रज्वलन्तं सुतेजसम् ।
न शशाक बहिः कर्तुं कोऽपि तं मुनिसत्तम ॥ २८॥

English
O excellent sage, no one could drive him out. He was blazing and brilliant like a big fire and could not be touched. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स भिक्षुकस्तात नानालीलाविशारदः ।
दर्शयामास शैलाय स्वप्रभावमनन्तकम् ॥ २९॥

English
O dear, then that mendicant, an expert in different sports, exhibited his infinite power to the mountain. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलो ददर्श तं तत्र विष्णुरूपधरं द्रुतम् ।
ततो ब्रह्मस्वरूपं च सूर्यरूपं ततः क्षणात् ॥ ३०॥

English
Immediately the mountain saw him assuming the form of Viṣṇu, then the form of Brahmā and in a trice that of the sun. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो ददर्श तं तात रुद्ररूपं महाद्भुतम् ।
पार्वतीसहितं रम्यं विहसन्तं सुतेजसम् ॥ ३१॥

English
O dear, then he saw him in the excessively wonderful form of Śiva accompanied by Pārvatī. He was exquisitely handsome and brilliant. He was laughing. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सुबहुरूपाणि तस्य तत्र ददर्श सः ।
सुविस्मितो बभूवाशु परमानन्दसम्प्लुतः ॥ ३२॥

English
Thus he saw him in various forms. He was much surprised and extremely delighted. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथासौ भिक्षुवर्यो हि तस्मात्तस्याश्च सूतिकृत् ।
भिक्षां ययाचे दुर्गां तां नान्यज्जग्राह किञ्चन ॥ ३३॥

English
Then the leading mendicant of good sports begged for Durgā as his alms, of her (Menā) and of him (the mountain). He did not accept anything else. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चान्तर्दधे भिक्षुस्वरूपः परमेश्वरः ।
स्वालयं स जगामाशु दुर्गावाक्यप्रणोदितः ॥ ३४॥

English
Then lord Śiva in the form of the mendicant, urged by Durgā's words vanished and went away to his abode suddenly. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा बभूव सुज्ञानं मेनाशैलेशयोरपि ।
आवां शिवो वञ्चयित्वा गतवान्स्वालयं विभुः ॥ ३५॥

English
Perfect knowledge then dawned on Menā and the lord of mountains. They thought: "The lord Śiva has deceived us and returned to his abode. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मै देया स्वकन्येयं पार्वती सुतपस्विनी ।
एवं विचार्य च तयोः शिवे भक्तिरभूत्परा ॥ ३६॥

English
Our Pārvatī who has performed a hard penance shall be given to him", thinking thus they became extremely devoted to Śiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो रुद्रो महोतीश्च कृत्वा भक्तमुदावहम् ।
विवाहं कृतवान्प्रीत्या पार्वत्या स विधानतः ॥ ३७॥

English
Thence after performing great sports, Śiva performed the marriage rites duly with Pārvatī delighting all devotees. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रोक्तस्तु ते तात सुनर्तकनटाह्वयः ।
शिवावतारो हि मया शिवावाक्यप्रपूरकः ॥ ३८॥

English
O dear, thus the incarnation of Śiva as Sunartaka naṭa in which the desire of Pārvatī was fulfilled has been explained to you by me. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं परमं व्याहृतं मया ।
य एतच्छृणुयात्प्रीत्या स सुखी गतिमाप्नुयात् ॥ ३९॥

English
Thus the pious narrative of great value has been explained by me. He who listens to this with pleasures becomes happy and attains salvation. (39.)
