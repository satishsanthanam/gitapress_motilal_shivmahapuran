### **CHAPTER THIRTYFIVE - (The incarnation of Śiva as a saintly brahmin)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अवतारं श‍ृणु विभोः साधुवेषद्विजाह्वयम् ॥ १॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* O omniscient Sanatkumāra, listen to the incarnation of Śiva, the supreme soul, as Brahmin in the garb of a saint. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मेनाहिमालयोर्भक्तिं शिवे ज्ञात्वा महोत्तमाम् ।
चिन्तामापुः सुराः सर्वे मन्त्रयामासुरादरात् ॥ २॥

English
On realising the excellent devotion of Menā and Himavat towards Śiva, the gods became worried and they consulted one another eagerly. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
एकान्तभक्त्या शैलश्चेत्कन्यां दास्यति शम्भवे ।
ध्रुवं निर्वाणतां सद्यः सम्प्राप्स्यति शिवस्य वै ॥ ३॥

English
If the mountain were to give his daughter to Śiva with undiluted devotion, certainly he will attain salvation immediately. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्तरत्नाधारोऽसौ चेत्प्रयास्यति मोक्षताम् ।
रत्नगर्भाभिधा भूमिर्मिथ्यैव भविता ध्रुवम् ॥ ४॥

English
He is the receptacle of infinite gems. If he attains liberation, the name of the earth, Ratnagarbhā (having gems within) will certainly be meaningless. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थावरत्वं परित्यज्य दिव्यरूपं विधाय सः ।
कन्यां शूलभृते दत्त्वा शिवलोकं गमिष्यति ॥ ५॥

English
He will cast off the stationary state of a mountain and assume a divine form. After giving his daughter in marriage to the trident-bearing lord he will go to Śiva's region. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेवस्य सारूप्यं प्राप्य शम्भोरनुग्रहात् ।
तत्र भुक्त्वा महाभोगांस्ततो मोक्षमवाप्स्यति ॥ ६॥

English
With the blessings of Śiva he will attain identity in form with the great god. He will enjoy all great pleasures there and then attain liberation. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यालोच्य सुराः सर्वे जग्मुर्गुरुगृहं मुने ।
चक्रुर्निवेदनं गत्वा गुरवे स्वार्थसाधकाः ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, after discussing like this, the gods went to the abode of their preceptor. In order to achieve their self-interest they submitted to the preceptor thus. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
गुरो हिमालयगृहं गच्छास्मत्कार्यसिद्धये ।
कृत्वा निन्दां महेशस्य गिरिभक्तिं निवारय ॥ ८॥

English
The gods said :—  O preceptor, please go to the abode of Himavat for the accomplishment of our task. Reproach lord Śiva and try to prevent the mountain's excessive devotion to him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वश्रद्धया सुतां दत्त्वा शिवाय स गिरिर्गुरो ।
लभेत मुक्तिमत्रैव धरण्यां स हि तिष्ठतु ॥ ९॥

English
O preceptor, if the mountain gives his daughter to Śiva with great faith he will attain salvation. Let him stay on the Earth itself. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इति देववचः श्रुत्वा प्रोवाच च विचार्य तान् ॥ १०॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods, he thought over that and spoke to them. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुरुवाच ।
कश्चिन्मध्ये च युष्माकं गच्छेच्छैलान्तिकं सुराः ।
सम्पादयेत्स्वाभिमतमहं तत्कर्तुमक्षमः ॥ ११॥

English
*The preceptor said :—*  O gods, one of you shall go to the mountain for achieving the end. I am incapable of doing it. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा गच्छत सुरा ब्रह्मलोकं सवासवाः ।
तस्मै वृत्तं कथय स्वं स वः कार्यं करिष्यति ॥ १२॥

English
Or you all, including Indra, go to Brahmā’s region. Tell him all your details. He will carry out your work. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा ते समालोच्य जग्मुर्विधिसभां सुराः ।
सर्वं निवेदयामासुस्तद्वृत्तं पुरतो विधेः ॥ १३॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing these words and reflecting thereon, the gods went to Brahmā’s assembly. They submitted all the details to Brahmā. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अवोचत्तान्विधिः श्रुत्वा तद्वचः सुविचिन्त्य वै ।
नाहं करिष्ये तन्निन्दां दुःखदां कहरां सदा ॥ १४॥

English
On hearing their words and reflecting deeply Brahmā told them—“I shall not censure him, for that will yield misery to us and dispel out happiness for ever. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरा गच्छत कैलासं सन्तोषयत शङ्करम् ।
प्रस्थापयत तं देवं हिमालयगृहं प्रति ॥ १५॥

English
O gods, you go to Kailāsa and propitiate Śiva. Request that lord to go to the abode of Himavat. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स गच्छेदथ शैलेशमात्मनिन्दां करोतु वै ।
परनिन्दा विनाशाय स्वनिन्दा यशसे मता ॥ १६॥

English
Let him approach the lord of mountains and censure himself. Censuring others is conducive to destruction and censuring oneself is conducive to glory. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
ततस्ते प्रययुः शीघ्रं कैलासं निखिलाः सुराः ।
सुप्रणम्य शिवं भक्त्या तद्वृत्तं निखिला जगुः ॥ १७॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Then the gods hastened to Kailāsa⁸⁷ and bowed to Śiva with great devotion. They mentioned everything in detail to him. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा देववचनं स्वीचकार महेश्वरः ।
देवान्सुयापयामास तानाश्वास्य विहस्य सः ॥ १८॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods, lord Śiva accepted their proposal. He laughingly consoled the gods and made them return without worry. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स भगवाञ्छम्भुर्महेशो भक्तवत्सलः ।
गन्तुमैच्छच्छैलमूलं मायेशो न विकारवान् ॥ १९॥

English
Then lord Śiva, who is favourably disposed towards his devotees, who is the wielder of Māyā and who is free from aberrations, desired to approach Himavat. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दण्डी छत्री दिव्यवासा बिभ्रत्तिलकमुज्ज्वलम् ।
करे स्फटिकमालां च शालग्रामं गले दधत् ॥ २०॥

जपन्नाम हरेर्भक्त्या साधुवेषधरो द्विजः ।
हिमाचलं जगामाशु बन्धुवर्गैः सर्मन्वितम् ॥ २१॥

English
He assumed the form of a brahmin Saint. He held a staff and an umbrella. He wore divine garments. A brilliant caste mark shone on his forehead. He had tied a Śālagrāma stone round his neck and held a string of crystal beads in his hand. He frequently repeated the names of Viṣṇu with devotion. He approached Himavat who was accompanied by his kinsmen. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
तं च दृष्ट्वा समुत्तस्थौ सगणोऽपि हिमालयः ।
ननाम दण्डवद्भूमौ साष्टाङ्गं विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ २२॥

English
On seeing him, Himavat and his attendants stood up and then prostrated before him. They performed Aṣṭāṅga salvation on the ground. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पप्रच्छ शैलेशस्तं द्विजं को भवानिति ।
उवाच शीघ्रं विप्रेन्द्रः स योग्यद्रिं महादरात् ॥ २३॥

English
Then the lord of mountains asked him "Who are you please?" The brahmin Yogin replied immediately with reverence. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
साधुद्विज उवाच ।
साधुद्विजाह्वः शैलाहं वैष्णवः परमार्थदृक् ।
परोपकारी सर्वज्ञः सर्वगामी गुरोर्बलात् ॥ २४॥

English
The Saintly brahmin said :—  O mountain, I am a devotee of Viṣṇu known as Sādhudvija. I possess true vision. I am omniscient and I assist others. By the power of the preceptor I can go everywhere. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मया ज्ञातं स्वविज्ञानात्स्वस्थाने शैलसत्तम ।
तच्छृणु प्रीतितो वच्मि हित्वा दम्भं तवान्तिकम् ॥ २५॥

English
O excellent mountain, by my divine knowledge, I have known something while I was in my abode. Listen. I shall mention the same with pleasure to you without any arrogance. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कराय सुतां दातुं त्वमिच्छसि निजोद्भवाम् ।
इमां पद्मासमां रम्यामज्ञातकुलशीलिने ॥ २६॥

English
You wish to give your daughter who is as comely as Lakṣmi to Śiva whose family and conduct are unknown. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं मतिस्ते शैलेन्द्र न युक्ता मङ्गलप्रदा ।
निबोध ज्ञानिनां श्रेष्ठ नारायणकुलोद्भव ॥ २७॥

English
O lord of mountains, this idea of yours is not conducive to auspiciousness. O most excellent one among the wise, born of Viṣṇu's race, you should know this. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्य शैलाधिपत्वं च न तस्यैकोऽस्ति बान्धवः ।
बान्धवान्स्वान्प्रयत्नेन पृच्छ मेनां च स्वप्रियाम् ॥ २८॥

English
O lord of mountains, see for yourself. He has no single relative. You can verify your relatives and your beloved. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वान्सम्पृच्छ यत्नेन मेनादीन्पार्वतीं विना ।
रोगिणे नौषधं शैल कुपथ्यं रोचते सदा ॥ २९॥

English
Ask Menā and others excluding Pārvatī. O mountain, the patient never likes the medicine but relishes undesirable diet. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
न ते पात्रानुरूपश्च पार्वतीदानकर्मणि ।
महाजनः स्मेरमुखः श्रुतिमात्राद्भविष्यति ॥ ३०॥

English
In the matter of gifting away Pārvatī, he is not the deserving person. As soon as this alliance is announced, the public will smile derisively. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
निराश्रयः सदासङ्गो विरूपो निर्गुणोऽव्ययः ।
स्मशानवासी विकटो व्यालग्राही दिगम्बरः ॥ ३१॥

विभूतिभूषणो व्यालवरावेष्टितमस्तकः ।
सर्वाश्रमपरिभ्रष्टस्त्वविज्ञातगतिस्सदा ॥ ३२॥

English
He is without support. He is unattached. He is ugly, devoid of attributes and unchanging. He is hideous. He resides in the cremation ground. He is naked and handles serpents He smears ashes as decoration. Serpents twine round his head. He has fallen from all stages of life. His ways are ever unknown. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
इत्याद्युक्त्वा वचस्तथ्यं शिवनिन्दापरं स हि ।
जगाम स्वालयं शीघ्रं नानालीलाकरः शिवः ॥ ३३॥

English
Brahmā said :—  After saying these factual statements insulting to Siva, Siva of diverse sports, quickly returned to his abode. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा विप्रवचनमभूतां च तनू तयोः ।
विपरीतानर्थपरे किं करिष्यावहे ध्रुवम् ॥ ३४॥

English
On hearing the words of the brahmin, they thought thus—"What can we do with regard to him who is engrossed in adverse things?" (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो रुद्रो महोतिं च कृत्वा भक्तमुदावहाम् ।
विवाहयित्वा गिरिजां देवकार्यं चकार सः ॥ ३५॥

English
Then Siva performed great sports delighting the devotees. He married Parvati aud carried out the task of the gods. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रोक्तस्तु ते तात साधुवेषो द्विजाह्वयः ।
शिवावतारो हि मया देवकार्यकरः प्रभो ॥ ३६॥

English
O dear, thus the incarnation of lord Siva as Sādhu-vesadvija, that carried out the task of the gods has been narrated to you. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमाख्यानमनघं स्वर्ग्यमायुष्यमुत्तमम् ।
यः पठेच्छृणुयाद्वापि स सुखी गतिमाप्नुयात् ॥ ३७॥

English
He who reads or listens to this pious narrative, conducive to heaven and longevity, becomes happy and attains salvation. (37.)
