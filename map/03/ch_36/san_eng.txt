### **CHAPTER THIRTYSIX - (Śiva's incarnation as Aśvatthāman)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अवतारं श‍ृणु विभोरश्वत्थामाह्वयं परम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, listen to the incarnation of lord Siva, the great soul Aśvatthāman. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतेर्महाबुद्धेर्देवर्षेरंशतो मुने ।
भरद्वाजात्समुत्पन्नो द्रोणोऽयोनिज आत्मवान् ॥ २॥

English
O sage, though a part of the celestial sage of great intellect Bhaspati, the self-possessed and prudent Drona was born of Bharadvaja but not of any womb. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
धनुर्भृतां वरः शूरो विप्रर्षिः सर्वशास्त्रवित् ।
बृहत्कीर्तिर्महातेजा यः सर्वास्त्रविदुत्तमः ॥ ३॥

English
He was the foremost among the archers, heroes, a brahminical sage, well-versed in all lore, of great fame and brilliance and the most excellent of all the wielders of weapons and missiles. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
धनुर्वेदे च वेदे च निष्णातं यं विदुर्बुधाः ।
वरिष्ठं चित्रकर्माणं द्रोणं स्वकुलवर्धनम् ॥ ४॥

English
Learned men knew Drona as the most excellent, skilful in the lore of archery as well as the Vedas, of variegated activities, and as one who made his race flourish. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कौरवाणां स आचर्य आसीत्स्वबलतो द्विज ।
महारथिषु विख्यातः षट्सु कौरवमध्यतः ॥ ५॥

English
O brahmin, thanks to his strength, he was the instructor of the Kauravas. He was the most well known among the six Mahārathas among the Kauravas. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
साहाय्यार्थं कौरवाणां स तेपे विपुलं तपः ।
शिवमुद्दिश्य पुत्रार्थं द्रोणाचार्यो द्विजोत्तमः ॥ ६॥

English
The most excellent brahmin Drona performed an elaborate penance with Siva in view for obtaining a son in order to help the Kauravas. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नो भगवाच्छङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
आविर्बभूव पुरतो द्रोणस्य मुनिसत्तम ॥ ७॥

English
O excellent sage, then the delighted lord Siva, favourably disposed to his devotees, appeared in front of Drona. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा स द्विजो द्रोणस्तुष्टावाशु प्रणम्य तम् ।
महाप्रसन्नहृदयो नतकः सुकृताञ्जलिः ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing him, the brahmin Drona whose heart was highly delighted bowed to him with palms joined in reverence and eulogised him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य स्तुत्या च तपसा सन्तुष्टः शङ्करः प्रभुः ।
वरं ब्रूहीति चोवाच द्रोणं तं भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ९॥

English
Satisfied and pleased with his eulogy and penance, lord Siva, favourably disposed towards his devotees told Drona—"Mention the boon you wish to choose". (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा शम्भुवचनं द्रोणः प्राहाथ सन्नतः ।
स्वांशजं तनयं देहि सर्वाजेयं महाबलम् ॥ १०॥

English
On hearing the words of Siva, Drona bowed low and said—"Please give me a son born of your part who will be invincible to every one and who will be very powerful. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा द्रोणवचनं शम्भुः प्रोचे तथास्त्विति ।
अभूदन्तर्हितस्तात कौतुकी सुखकृन्मुने ॥ ११॥

English
On hearing the words of Drona, Siva said, "So be it." O dear sage, the lord who bestows happiness, then vanished. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
द्रोणोऽपगच्छत्स्वं धाम महाहृष्टो गतभ्रमः ।
स्वपत्न्यै कथयामास तद्वृतं सकलं मुदा ॥ १२॥

English
Drona returned to his abode highly delighted and free from illusion. He joyously narrated all the details to his wife. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथावसरमासाद्य रुद्रः सर्वान्तकः प्रभुः ।
स्वांशेन तनयो जज्ञे द्रोणस्य स महाबलः ॥ १३॥

English
Taking the due opportunity, lord Siva, the annihilator of all and very powerful was born as Drona's son through a part of himself. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वत्थामेति विख्यातः स बभूव क्षितौ मुने ।
प्रवीरः कञ्जपत्राक्षः शत्रुपक्षक्षयङ्करः ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, he became well known as Aśvatthāman on the earth. He was heroic, had lotus-like eyes and he could reduce the strength of his enemy. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यो भारते रणे ख्यातः पितुराज्ञामवाप्य च ।
सहायकृद् बभूवात कौरवाणां महाबलः ॥ १५॥

English
Securing the permission and behest of his father that famous hero helped the Kauravas in the Mahābhārata war. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यमाश्रित्य महावीरं कौरवाः सुमहाबलाः ।
भीष्मादयो बभूवुस्तेऽजेया अपि दिवौकसाम् ॥ १६॥

English
By resorting to that powerful hero, Bhisma and other Kauravas became invincible even to the gods. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्भयात्पाण्डवाः सर्वे कौरवाञ्जेतुमक्षमाः ।
आसन्नष्टा महावीरा अपि सर्वे च कोविदाः ॥ १७॥

English
Fearing him, the Pāṇḍavas, though they were great heroes and very skilful, became incapable of conquering the Kauravas. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णोपदेशतः शम्भोस्तपः कृत्वातिदारुणम् ।
प्राप्य चास्त्रं शम्भुवराज्जिग्ये तानर्जुनस्ततः ॥ १८॥

English
At the instance of Kṛṣṇa, Arjuna performed the severe penance of Śiva and obtained a divine missile due to Śiva's boon. He then conquered the Kauravas. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वत्थामा महावीरो महादेवांशजो मुने ।
तथापि तद्भक्तिवशः स्वप्रतापमदर्शयत् ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, although the great hero Aśvatthāman was born of a part of lord Śiva. Still due to his devotion to the Kauravas, he exhibited his full vigour. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विनाश्य पाण्डवसुतान् शिक्षितानपि यत्नतः ।
कृष्णादिभिर्महावीरैरनिवार्यबलः परैः ॥ २०॥

English
He destroyed the sons of Pāṇḍavas though they were trained in warfare by Kṛṣṇa and other great heroes. He had the strength that could not be withstood by the enemy. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रशोकेन विकलमापतन्तं तमर्जुनम् ।
रथेनाच्युतवन्तं हि दृष्ट्वा स च पराद्रवत् ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing Arjuna dejected by the death of his son but rushing on him along with Kṛṣṇa he fled away from the battle-field. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्त्रं ब्रह्मशिरो नाम तदुपर्यसृजत्स हि ।
ततः प्रादुरभूत्तेजः प्रचण्डं सर्वतो दिशम् ॥ २२॥

English
He discharged the divine missile Brahmaśiras on Arjuna. Then a fierce splendour enveloping all the quarters manifested itself. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राणापदमभिप्रेक्ष्य सोऽर्जुनः क्लेशसंयुतः ।
उवाच कृष्णं विक्लान्तो नष्टतेजा महाभयः ॥ २३॥

English
Sensing danger to his own life Arjuna was much distressed. Losing all his brilliance and highly frightened he spoke to Kṛṣṇa becoming utterly exhausted. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
किमिदं स्वित्कुतो वेति कृष्ण कृष्ण न वेद्म्यहम् ।
सर्वतोमुखमायाति तेजश्चेदं सुदुस्सहम् ॥ २४॥

English
Arjuna said:—  O Kṛṣṇa, O Kṛṣṇa, what is this? Whence it has arisen I do not know. This unbearable fiery splendour comes from all sides.



25, Nandiśvara said:—  On hearing the words of Arjuna, Kṛṣṇa the most excellent among the devotees of Śiva meditated on Śiva and Pārvatī and then spoke to Arjuna. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्ण उवाच ।
वेत्थेदं द्रोणपुत्रस्य ब्राह्ममस्त्रं महोल्बणम् ।
न ह्यस्यान्यतमं किञ्चिदस्त्रं प्रत्यवकर्शनम् ॥ २६॥

English
Kṛṣṇa said:—  Know this to be the most powerful Brāhma missile of Asvatthāman. There is no other missile to counteract this. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवं स्मर द्रुतं शम्भुं स्वप्रभुं भक्तरक्षकम् ।
येन दत्तं हि ते स्वास्त्रं सर्वकार्यकरं परम् ॥ २७॥

English
Immediately think of Śiva, your lord, the protector of the devotees who had given you a weapon capable of every action. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
जह्यस्त्रतेज उन्नद्धं त्वं तच्छैवास्त्रतेजसा ।
इत्युक्त्वा च स्वयं कृष्णः शिवं दध्यौ तदर्थकः ॥ २८॥

English
Counteract the fiery splendour of the Brāhma missile with your Śaiva missile. After saying this Kṛṣṇa himself meditated on Śiva for that purpose. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा कृष्णवचनं पार्थः स्मृत्वा शिवं हृदि ।
स्पृष्ट्वापस्तं प्रणम्याशु चिक्षेपास्त्रं ततो मुने ॥ २९॥

English
O sage, on hearing the words of Kṛṣṇa. Arjuna remembered Śiva, touched water, bowed to Śiva and discharged the Śaiva missile. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यप्यस्त्रं ब्रह्मशिरस्त्वमोघं चाप्रतिक्रियम् ।
शैवास्त्रतेजसा सद्यः समशाम्यन्महामुने ॥ ३०॥

English
O great sage, although the Brahmaśiras missile never goes futile and cannot be counteracted, it subsided before the splendour of the Śaiva missile. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मा मंस्था ह्येतदाश्चर्यं सर्वचित्रमये शिवे ।
यः स्वशक्त्याखिलं विश्वं सृजत्यवति हन्त्यजः ॥ ३१॥

English
Do not consider this very surprising in Śiva who is full of wondrous things and who, the unborn, creates, protects and annihilates the entire universe by his own power. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वत्थामा ततो ज्ञात्वा वृत्तमेतच्छिवांशजः ।
शैवं न विव्यथे किञ्चिच्छिवेच्छातुष्टधीर्मुने ॥ ३२॥

English
O sage, knowing that this happened by the influence of Siva, Asvatthaman, born of Siva's part and delighted in heart at Siva's wish, was not at all sorry. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ द्रौणिरिदं विश्वं कृत्स्नं कर्तुमपाण्डवम् ।
उत्तरागर्भगं बालं नाशितुं मन आदधे ॥ ३३॥

English
Then the son of Drona wishing the world to be rid of Pāṇḍava lineage desired to kill the child in the womb of Uttarā. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मास्त्रमनिवार्यं तदन्यैरस्त्रैर्महाप्रभम् ।
उत्तरागर्भमुद्दिश्य चिक्षेप स महाप्रभुः ॥ ३४॥

English
The powerful Asvatthaman hurled the excessively lustrous Brahma-missile that could not be countermanded by any other missile, aiming it at the womb of Uttarā. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च सोत्तरा जिष्णुवधूर्विकलमानसा ।
कृष्णं तुष्टाव लक्ष्मीशं दह्यमाना तदस्त्रतः ॥ ३५॥

English
Then Uttarā the daughter-in-law of Arjuna, burning within due to that missile and agitated in the mind eulogised Kṛṣṇa, the lord of Lakṣmi. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कृष्णः शिवं ध्यात्वा हृदा नुत्वा प्रणम्य च ।
अपाण्डवमिदं कर्तुं द्रौणेरस्त्रमबुध्यत ॥ ३६॥

English
Then meditating on Siva mentally, bowing to and eulogising him, Kṛṣṇa understood that to be the missile of the son of Drona. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वरक्षार्थेन्द्रदत्तेन तदस्त्रेण सुवर्चसा ।
सुदर्शनेन तस्याश्च व्यधाद्रक्षां शिवाज्ञया ॥ ३७॥

English
By means of his missile—the brilliant Sudarsana, bestowed on him by Siva for his protection, he saved her at the behest of Siva. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वरूपं शङ्करादेशात्कृतं शैववरेण ह ।
कृष्णेन चरितं ज्ञात्वा विमनस्कः शनैरभूत् ॥ ३८॥

English
Asvatthaman then knew that Krsna had assumed his form at the behest of Siva and did it as he was the foremost among the devotees of Siva, slowly refrained from the thought of destroying Uttarā. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स कृष्णः प्रीतात्मा पाण्डवान्सकलानपि ।
अपातयत्तदङ्घ्र्योस्तु तुष्टये तस्य शैवराट् ॥ ३९॥

English
Then the leading devotee of Siva, the delighted Krsna, made all the Pandavas fall at the feet of Asvatthaman for pleasing him. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ द्रौणिः प्रसन्नात्मा पाण्डवान्कृष्णमेव च ।
नानावरान्ददौ प्रीत्या सोऽश्वत्थामानुगृह्य च ॥ ४०॥

English
Then Asvatthaman, son of Drona, highly delighted in the mind, blessed the Pandavas and Krsna with various boons. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं महेश्वरस्तात चक्रे लीलां परां प्रभुः ।
अवतीर्य क्षितौ द्रौणिरूपेण मुनिसत्तम ॥ ४१॥

English
O excellent sage, incarnating thus on the earth in the form of Drona's son, lord Siva performed various sports. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवावतारोऽश्वत्थामा महाबलपराक्रमः ।
त्रैलोक्यसुखदोऽद्यापि वर्तते जाह्नवीतटे ॥ ४२॥

English
The incarnation of Siva Asvatthaman of great strength and exploit is even now alive on the banks of the river Gaṅgā bestowing happiness on the three worlds. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वत्थामावतारस्ते वर्णितः शङ्करप्रभोः ।
सर्वसिद्धिकरश्चापि भक्ताभीष्टफलप्रदः ॥ ४३॥

English
The incarnation in the form of Aśvatthāman of lord Śiva has been explained to you. It is the cause of all Siddhis and it fulfils all desires of the devotees. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या कीर्तयेद्वा समाहितः ।
स सिद्धिं प्राप्नुयादिष्टामन्ते शिवपुरं व्रजेत् ॥ ४४॥

English
He who listens to this with devotion or narrates it with pure mind attains all Siddhis here and attains Śiva's abode hereafter. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
श्रुत्वार्जुनवचश्चेदं स कृष्णः शैवसत्तमः ।
दध्यौ शिवं सदारं च प्रत्याहार्जुनमादरात् ॥ २५॥
