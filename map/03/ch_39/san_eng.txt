### **CHAPTER THIRTYNINE - (The demon Mūka is killed in the context of the incarnation of Kirāta)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
स्नानं स विधिवत्कृत्वा न्यासादि विधिवत्तथा ।
ध्यानं शिवस्य सद्भक्त्या व्यासोक्तं यत्तथाकरोत् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  He performed the ablution duly, and the Nyasa (the mystic placing of fingers etc.) in accordance with the Sastric injunctions. He performed the meditation of Siva with great devotion in the manner explained by Vyasa. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एकपादतलेनैव तिष्ठन्मुनिवरो यथा ।
सूर्ये दृष्टिं निबध्यैकां मन्त्रमावर्तयन् स्थितः ॥ २॥

English
Like a great sage, he stood on the sole of a single foot. He fixed one of his eyes at the sun and stood repeating the mantra. (2.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Repeating the five-syllabled mantra of Siva the most excellent of all the mantras and mentally remembering Siva with pleasure he performed the penance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The splendour of the penance was such that the gods were surprised. They went to Siva again and told him sincerely. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
नरेणैकेन सर्वेश त्वदर्थे तप आहितम् ।

English
The gods said:—  O lord of all, a penance is being performed by a man for your propiation. O lord, why don't you grant him what he desires. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।

English
Nandīśvara said:— After saying this the gods eulogised him in different ways. Fixing their eyes to his feet they stood there in agony. (6.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On hearing their words the lord of benevolent mind, the delighted Siva laughingly spoke thus to the gods. (7.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Śiva said:—  O gods, you go to your abodes. Undoubtedly I shall carry out your task in every respect. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On hearing those words of Śiva, they became assured. They therefore returned to their abodes. (9.)

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दैत्यो मूकनामागतस्तदा ।
सौकरं रूपमास्थाय प्रेषितश्च दुरात्मना ॥ १०॥

यत्रार्जुनः स्थितश्चासीत्तेन मार्गेण वै तदा ॥ ११॥

श‍ृङ्गाणि पर्वतस्यैव च्छिन्दन्वृक्षाननेकशः ।
शब्दं च विविधं कुर्वन्नतिवेगेन संयुतः ॥ १२॥

अर्जुनोऽपि च तं दृष्ट्वा मूकनामासुरं तदा ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं विचारे तत्परोऽभवत् ॥ १३॥

English
In the meantime there came the demon Mūka, instigated by the wicked Duryodhana, wielding Māyā against Arjuna. O great brahmin, he had assumed the form of a boar.⁹⁶ He came along the path where Arjuna was staying. He was digging up the peaks of mountains and trees in plenty. He was roaring and grunting in diverse ways and was rushing at a great speed. On seeing the demon Mūka, Arjuna remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and began to think. (10-13.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Verily he is a benefactor on seeing whom the mind becomes pleased. If on seeing anyone the mind is agitated he is surely an enemy. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
The concealed mind is revealed by the general mien, the mode of walking, the activities, the speech, the features of the eyes and the face.⁹⁷ (19.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The eye is of four sorts—the resplendent, the impassioned the squinted and the ruddy. The features are likewise different, O scholars. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
