### **CHAPTER FORTY - (The Kirāta-Arjuna dialogue)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ श‍ृणु लीलां परात्मनः ।
भक्तवात्सल्यसंयुक्तां तद् दृढत्वविदर्भिताम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, listen to the sport of the great soul Śiva full of affection for the devotee and to the test of its stability. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽप्यथ स्वभृत्यं वै प्रेषयामास स द्रुतम् ।
बाणार्थं च तदा तत्रार्जुनोऽपि समगात्ततः ॥ २॥

English
Śiva immediately sent his attendant to fetch the arrow. The Arjuna too came there. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन् समये प्राप्तौ बाणार्थं तद्गणार्जुनौ ।
अर्जुनस्तं पराभर्त्स्य स्वबाणं चाग्रहीत्तदा ॥ ३॥

English
The Gaṇa and Arjuna reached the place simultaneously for the arrow. Arjuna rebuked him and took up his arrow. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
गणः प्रोवाच तं तत्र किमर्थं गृह्यते शरः ।
बाणश्चैवास्मदीयो वै मुच्यतां ऋषिसत्तम ॥ ४॥

English
The Gaṇa said—"O excellent sage, why do you take this arrow? The arrow is mine. Leave it off." (4.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तस्तेन भिल्लस्य गणेन मुनिसत्तम ।
सोऽर्जुनः शङ्करं स्मृत्वा वचनं च तमब्रवीत् ॥ ५॥

English
O excellent sage, thus addressed by the Gaṇa of the Bhilla, Arjuna remembered Śiva and spoke. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
अज्ञात्वा किं च वदसि मूर्खोऽसि त्वं वनेचर ।
बाणश्च मोचितो मेऽद्य त्वदीयश्च कथं पुनः ॥ ६॥

English
Arjuna said :—  O forest-roamer, what do you say without understanding? You are a fool. This arrow had been discharged by me just now. How can it be yours? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
रेखारूपं च पिच्छानि मन्नामाङ्कित एव च ।
त्वदीयश्च कथं जातः स्वभावो दुस्त्यजस्तव ॥ ७॥

English
It is engraved with my name in many lines. The feathers are mine. How does it become yours? You insist according to your wild nature. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्येवं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा विहस्य स गणेश्वरः ।
अर्जुनं ऋषिरूपं तं भिल्लो वाक्यमुपाददे ॥ ८॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* On hearing his words, the leader of the Gaṇas laughed and spoke to Arjuna who was in the garb of a sage. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तापस श्रूयतां रे त्वं न तपः क्रियते त्वया ।
वेषतश्च तपस्वी त्वं न यथार्थं छलायते ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, listen. You are a sage in disguise. You are not a true sage. You do not perform penance. People are being deceived by you. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्वी च कथं मिथ्या भाषणं कुरुते नरः ।
नैकाकिनं च मां त्वं च जानीहि वाहिनीपतिम् ॥ १०॥

English
How can a saint utter a lie? Do not take me to be single-handed. Know me to be the commander-in-chief. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुभिर्वनभिल्लैश्च युक्तः स्वामी स आसते ।
समर्थः सर्वथा कर्तुं विग्रहानुग्रहौ पुनः ॥ ११॥

English
My master is sitting there with many hunters. In every respect, he is capable of destroying or blessing you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्तते तस्य बाणोऽयं यो नीतश्च त्वयाधुना ।
अयं बाणश्च ते पार्श्वे न स्थास्यति कदाचन ॥ १२॥

English
It is his arrow that has been taken by you now. This arrow will never remain with you. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तपःफलं कथं त्वं च हातुमिच्छसि तापस ।
चौर्याच्छलादर्द्यमानाद् विस्मयात्सत्यभञ्जनात् ॥ १३॥

तपसा क्षीयते सत्यमेतदेव मया श्रुतम् ।
तस्माच्च तपसस्तेऽद्य भविष्यति फलं कुतः ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, why do you wish to lose the fruit of your penance? Truly have I heard that penance is marred and wasted by theft, pride afflicted by deceit and the breaking of promise. Hence O sage, whence will you derive the fruit of penance ? (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादमुक्तबाणो हि कृतघ्नस्त्वं भविष्यसि ।
ममैव स्वामिना बाणस्तवार्थे मोचितो ध्रुवम् ॥ १५॥

English
If you do not give up this arrow you will be an ungrateful fellow. It was certainly to save you that my master discharged this arrow. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शत्रुश्च मारितस्तेन पुनर्बाणश्च रक्षितः ।
अत्यन्तं च कृतघ्नोऽसि तपोऽशुभकरस्तथा ॥ १६॥

English
Your enemy has been killed by him and you have kept the arrow to yourself. Very ungrateful indeed are you. You are making your own penance fruitless. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यं न भाषसे त्वं च किमतः सिद्धिमिच्छसि ।
प्रयोजनं चेद् बाणेन स्वामी च याच्यतां मम ॥ १७॥

English
You do not speak the truth. What you wish to gain thereby? If you have any need for the arrow, you may approach my master and request him. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशांश्च बहून्बाणांस्तदा दातुं क्षमः स्वयम् ।
राजा च वर्तते मेऽद्य किं त्वेवं याच्यते त्वया ॥ १८॥

English
He can give you many similar arrows. My master is here. Why don't you request him? (18.)

---

Sanskrit
उपकारं परित्यज्य ह्यपकारं समीहसे ।
नैतद्युक्तं त्वयाद्यैव क्रियते त्यज चापलम् ॥ १९॥

English
Unmindful of his help to you, you wish him harm. This does not behove you. Eschew this fickleness: (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं तस्य श्रुत्वा पार्थोऽर्जुनस्तदा ।
क्रोधं कृत्वा शिवं स्मृत्वा मितं वाक्यमथाब्रवीत् ॥ २०॥

English
On hearing his words, Arjuna, son of Pṛthā, was angry. After remembering Śiva, he spoke these measured words. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
श‍ृणु भिल्ल प्रवक्ष्यामि न सत्यं तव भाषणम् ।
यथा जातिस्तथा त्वां च जानामि हि वनेचर ॥ २१॥

English
Arjuna said :—  O hunter, listen. I shall explain. Your words are not true. O forest-wanderer, I know you. You are true to the caste in which you are born. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं राजा भवान् चौरः कथं युद्धप्रयुक्तता ।
युद्धं मे सबलैः कार्यं नाधमैर्हि कदाचन ॥ २२॥

English
I am a king. You are a thief. How can a war be pursued? I must fight with people equal in strength to me, never with base people. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्ते च तथा स्वामी भविष्यति भवादृशः ।
दातारश्च वयं प्रोक्ताश्चौरा यूयं वनेचराः ॥ २३॥

English
Your master will be only like you. We are the benevolent donors and you the forest-dwellers, thieves. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं याच्यो मया भिल्लराज एवं च साम्प्रतम् ।
त्वमेव याचसे नैव बाणं मां किं वनेचर ॥ २४॥

English
How can the king of foresters be requested by me now? O forest-wanderer, why don't you beg the arrow of me? (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ददामि ते तथा बाणान् सन्ति मे बहवो ध्रुवम् ।
राजा च ग्रहणं चैव न दास्यति तथा भवेत् ॥ २५॥

English
I shall readily give you the arrows. Certainly I have many. If a king does not give what is taken, your objection may come up.
26 Then O forester, how can I simply give away the arrow, if what I am desirous of doing is not being understood? (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यथागच्छत्तु ते भर्ता किमर्थं भाषतेऽधुना ।
आगत्य च मया सार्धं जित्वा युद्धे च मां पुनः ॥ २७॥

नीत्वा बाणमिमं भिल्लस्वामी ते वाहिनीपतिः ।
निजालयं सुखं यातु विलम्बः क्रियते कथम् ॥ २८॥

English
Why should there be a long talk? Let your master come and fight with me. Defeating me in the battle, let him take the arrow and return to his abode. Why should there be any delay? (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
महेश्वरकृपाप्राप्तसद्बलस्यार्जुनस्य हि ।
इत्येतद्वचनं श्रुत्वा भिल्लो वाक्यमथाब्रवीत् ॥ २९॥

English
Nandisvara said :—  On hearing these words of Arjuna who derived his strength from the mercy of lord Śiva, the forester spoke these words. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
भिल्ल उवाच ।
अज्ञोऽसि त्वं ऋषिर्नासि मरणं त्वीहसे कथम् ।
देहि बाणं सुखं तिष्ठ त्वन्यथा क्लेशभाग्भवेः ॥ ३०॥

English
The forester said :—  You are ignorant. You are not a sage. Why do you invite death ? Give up the arrow and stay here happily or you will come to grief. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तेन भिल्लेन शिवसच्छक्तिशोभिना ।
गणेन पाण्डवस्तं च प्राह स्मृत्वा च शङ्करम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Nandisvara said :—  Thus challenged by the forester who derived his strength from Śiva and who was a Gaṇa, Arjuna, son of Pāṇḍu remembered Śiva and said. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
मद्वाक्यं तत्त्वतो भिल्ल श‍ृणु त्वं च वनेचर ।
आगमिष्यति ते स्वामी दर्शयिष्ये फलं तदा ॥ ३२॥

English
Arjuna said :—  O forester, listen attentively to my words. If your master comes here I shall show my strength. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
न शोभते त्वया युद्धं करिष्ये स्वामिना तव ।
उपहासकरं ज्ञेयं युद्धं सिंहश‍ृगालयोः ॥ ३३॥

English
It does not behove me to fight with you. I shall fight with your master. The fight between a lion and a jackal is very ridiculous. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुतं च मद्वचस्तेऽद्य द्रक्ष्यसि त्वं महाबलम् ।
गच्छस्व स्वामिनं भिल्ल यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ ३४॥

English
You have heard my words. You shall see my strength now. O Bhilla, return to your master. Do as you please. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तु गतस्तत्र भिल्लः पार्थेन वै मुने ।
शिवावतारो यत्रास्ते किरातो वाहिनीपतिः ॥ ३५॥

English
Nandisvara said :—  O sage, thus addressed by Arjuna, the Bhilla returned to the place where the general Kirata, the incarnation of Siva, stood. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अथार्जुनस्य वचनं भिल्लनाथाय विस्तरात् ।
सर्वं निवेदयामास तस्यै भिल्लपरात्मने ॥ ३६॥

English
He mentioned Arjuna's talk in detail to lord Siva, the great soul in the form of Bhilla. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
स किरातेश्वरः श्रुत्वा तद्वचो हर्षमागतः ।
आजगाम स्वसैन्येन शङ्करो भिल्लरूपधृक् ॥ ३७॥

English
On hearing his words, lord Kirata was much delighted. It was Siva dressed as a Bhilla who had come there with his army. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुनश्च तदा सेनां किरातस्य च पाण्डवः ।
दृष्ट्वा गृहीत्वा सशरं धनुः सम्मुख आययौ ॥ ३८॥

English
On seeing Kirata with his army, Arjuna, son of Pandu, took up his bow and arrows and faced him. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो किरातश्च पुनः प्रेषयामास तं चरम् ।
तन्मुखेन जगौ वाक्यं भारताय महात्मने ॥ ३९॥

English
Then Kirata sent the messenger again and through him delivered the message to Arjuna, the great soul. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
किरात उवाच ।
पश्य सैन्यं तपस्विस्त्वं मुञ्च बाणं व्रजाधुना ।
मरणं स्वल्पकार्यार्थं कथमिच्छसि साम्प्रतम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Kirāta said:—  O sage, see this army coming. Give up the arrow and go away. Why do you seek death for a trifle. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रातरस्तव दुःखार्ताः कलत्रं च ततः परम् ।
पृथिवी हस्ततस्तेऽद्य यास्यतीति मतिर्मम ॥ ४१॥

English
Your brothers are in distress. Your wife is grief-stricken. I think your kingdom will slip off your hands, I am sure. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तं परमेशेन पार्थदार्ढ्यपरीक्षया ।
सर्वथार्जुनरक्षार्थं धृतरूपेण शम्भुना ॥ ४२॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* It was just to test the firmness of Arjuna that the great lord said this, the great lord Śiva who had assumed that form to protect Arjuna by all means. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तस्तु तदागत्य स गणः शाङ्करश्च तत् ।
विस्तराद् वृत्तमखिलमर्जुनाय न्यवेदयत् ॥ ४३॥

English
What the lord said, the Gana of Siva mentioned that to Arjuna in detail after approaching him. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

वाहिनीपतये वाच्यं विपरीतं भविष्यति ॥ ४४॥

English
On hearing that, Arjuna replied to the messenger "You shall tell your general that the reverse of what he says will happen to him. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यहं चैव ते बाणं यच्छामि च मदीयकम् ।
कुलस्य दूषणं चाहं भविष्यामि न संशयः ॥ ४५॥

English
If I were to give up the arrow that belongs to me I shall be the cause of ignominy to my family. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रातरश्चैव दुःखार्ताः भवन्तु च तथा ध्रुवम् ।

English
Well may my brothers be distressed. Let all my knowledge be fruitless. So, come on to fight with me. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहश्चैव श‍ृगालाद्वा भीतो नैव मया श्रुतः ।
तथा वनेचराद्राजा न बिभेति कदाचन ॥ ४७॥

English
No lion is ever frightened of a jackal. A king is never afraid of a forester." (47.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तं पुनर्गत्वा स्वामिनं पाण्डवेन सः ।
सर्वं निवेदयामास तदुक्तं हि विशेषतः ॥ ४८॥

English
Thus replied by Arjuna the Bhilla returned to his master and conveyed all that Arjuna had said. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

तच्छ्रुत्वा सैन्यसंयुक्तो ह्यर्जुनं चागमत्तदा ॥ ४९॥

English
Lord Siva, a Kirata, heard the message and approached Arjuna along with his army. (49.)
