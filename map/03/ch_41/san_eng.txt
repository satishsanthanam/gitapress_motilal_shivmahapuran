### **CHAPTER FORTYONE - (The incarnation of lord Śiva as Kirāta)**

---

Sanskrit
तमागतं ततो दृष्ट्वा ध्यानं कृत्वा शिवस्य सः ।
गत्वा तत्रार्जुनस्तेन युद्धं चक्रे सुदारुणम् ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  On seeing him come, Arjuna meditated on Śiva and fought with him a terrible battle. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
गणैश्च विविधैस्तीक्ष्णैरायुधैस्तं न्यपीडयत् ।
तैस्तदा पीडितः पार्थः सस्मार स्वामिनं शिवम् ॥ २॥

English
He was severely afflicted by the Gaṇas with many sharp weapons. Thus tortured by them Arjuna remembered lord Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुनश्च तदा तेषां बाणावलिमथाच्छिनत् ।
यदायुद्धं च तैः क्षिप्तं ततः शर्वं परामृशत् ॥ ३॥

English
Arjuna split the arrows discharged by Kirāta and his gaṇas. Then he thought upon Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पीडितास्ते गणास्तेन ययुश्चैव दिशो दश ।
गणेशा वारितास्ते च नाजग्मुः स्वामिनं प्रति ॥ ४॥

English
When distressed by him, the Gaṇas fled to the quarters. Then Śiva summoned the chief Gaṇas. But they did not respond to his call. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवश्चैवार्जुनश्चैव युयुधाते परस्परम् ।
नानाविधैश्चायुधैर्हि महाबलपराक्रमौ ॥ ५॥

English
Śiva and Arjuna of great strength and exploit fought each other with various weapons of war. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि मनसा नूनं दयां कृत्वार्जुनं ह्यगात् ।
अर्जुनश्च दृढं तत्र प्रहारं कृतवांस्तदा ॥ ६॥

English
Taking pity on him Śiva approached Arjuna, but Arjuna hit him heavily. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
आयुधानि शिवः सो वै ह्यर्जुनस्याच्छिनत्तदा ।
कवचानि च सर्वाणि शरीरं केवलं स्थितम् ॥ ७॥

English
Śiva split the weapons of Arjuna as well as his armour. Arjuna stood in his bare body. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तदार्जुनः शिवं स्मृत्वा मल्लयुद्धं चकार सः ।
वाहिनीपतिना तेन भयात्क्लिष्टोऽपि धैर्यवान् ॥ ८॥

English
Although afflicted by fear, Arjuna remembered Śiva and fought a duel with the general spiritedly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्युद्धेन मही सर्वा चकम्पे ससमुद्रका ।
देवा दुःखं समापन्नाः किं भविष्यति वा पुनः ॥ ९॥

English
The earth along with the oceans shook due to their fight. “What will happen now ?” Thinking thus, the gods were distressed. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मित्रन्तरे देवः शिवो गगनमास्थितः ।
युद्धं चकार तत्रस्थः सोऽर्जुनश्च तथाकरोत् ॥ १०॥

English
In the meantime, lord Śiva stood in mid-air and fought. Arjuna too did likewise. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
उड्डीयोड्डीय तौ युद्धं चक्रतुर्देवपार्थिवौ ।
देवाश्च विस्मयं प्रापू रणं दृष्ट्वा तदाद्भुतम् ॥ ११॥

English
Flying at each other, lord Śiva and the earthly king Arjuna fought with each other. On seeing that wonderful battle the gods were surprised. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथार्जुनोत्तरं ज्ञात्वा स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्बुजम् ।
दधार पादयोस्तं वै तद्ध्यानादाप्तसद्बलः ॥ १२॥

English
Then Arjuna came to the ground, remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and caught hold of his feet deriving sufficient strength, thanks to his meditation on Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
धृत्वा पादौ तदा तस्य भ्रामयामास सोऽर्जुनः ।
विजहास महादेवो भक्तवत्सल ऊतिकृत् ॥ १३॥

English
Catching hold of his feet, Arjuna whirled him. Lord Śiva, favourably disposed towards his disciples, laughed indulging in divine sports. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दातुं स्वदासतां तस्मै भक्तवश्यतया मुने ।
शिवेनैव कृतं ह्येतच्चरितं नान्यथा भवेत् ॥ १४॥

English
O sage, it was surely done by Śiva himself who is subservient to his devotees. It could not be otherwise as this was just to grant him his devotion. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चाद्विहस्य तत्रैव शङ्करो रूपमद्भुतम् ।
दर्शयामास सहसा भक्तवश्यतया शुभम् ॥ १५॥

English
Then Śiva smiled and revealed his wonderful auspicious form as he is always well disposed to his devotees. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यथोक्तं वेदशास्त्रेषु पुराणे पुरुषोत्तम ।
व्यासोपदिष्टं ध्यानाय तस्य यत्सर्वसिद्धिदम् ॥ १६॥

तद्दृष्ट्वा सुन्दरं रूपं ध्यानप्राप्तं शिवस्य तु ।
बभूव विस्मितोऽतीव ह्यर्जुनो लज्जितः स्वयम् ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing that beautiful form, the form of the excellent supreme being as mentioned in the Vedas, Śāstras and Purāṇas, the form which Vyāsa had advised him to meditate upon, the form which bestows all Siddhis, the form of Śiva, Arjuna was abashed and surprised much. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो शिवश्शिवः सोऽयं यो मे प्रभुतया वृतः ।
त्रिलोकेशः स्वयं साक्षाद् हा कृतं किं मयाधुना ॥ १८॥

English
"Hā, this is Śiva, this is Śiva who has been chosen by me as my lord, the lord of the three worlds himself. O what a thing have I committed now! (18.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभोर्बलवती माया मायिनामपि मोहिनी ।
किं कृतं रूपमाच्छाद्य प्रभुणा छलितो ह्यहम् ॥ १९॥

English
Powerful indeed is the Māyā of the lord. It deludes even the wielders of Māyā. Why have I been deceived by the lord who concealed his own form." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
धियेति संविचार्यैवं साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तकः ।
प्रणनाम प्रभुं प्रीत्या तदोवाच स खिन्नधीः ॥ २०॥

English
After thinking thus within himself he joined his palms in reverence and bending his head he bowed to the lord with pleasure. Then he spoke in a delighted manner. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव करुणाकर शङ्कर ।
ममापराधंः सर्वेश क्षन्तव्यश्च त्वया पुनः ॥ २१॥

English
Arjuna said:—  O great god, O lord of gods, O Śiva the merciful, O lord of all, my fault shall be forgiven by you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कृतं रूपमाच्छाद्य च्छलितोऽस्मि त्वयाधुना ।
धिङ् मां समरकर्तारं स्वामिना भवता प्रभो ॥ २२॥

English
What you have done? By concealing your own form you have deceived me now. Fie on me who fought with you, my master, O lord. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्येवं पाण्डवः सोऽथ पश्चात्तापमवाप सः ।
पादयोर्निपपाताशु शङ्करस्य महाप्रभोः ॥ २३॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  Thus Arjuna, son of Pāṇḍu, repented. He fell at the feet of lord Śiva. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अथेश्वरः प्रसन्नात्मा प्रत्युवाचार्जुनं च तम् ।
समाश्वास्येति बहुशो महेशो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २४॥

English
Cheering up in various ways the delighted lord Śiva, favourably disposed to his devotees, replied to Arjuna. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
न खिद्य पार्थ भक्तोऽसि मम त्वं हि विशेषतः ।
परीक्षार्थं मया तेऽद्य कृतमेवं शुचं जहि ॥ २५॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Arjuna, do not worry yourself. You are a great devotee of mine. I have done this just to test you. Give up your sorrow. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तं स्वहस्ताभ्यामुत्थाप्य प्रभुरर्जुनम् ।
विलज्जं कारयामास गणैश्च स्वामिनो गुणैः ॥ २६॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  After saying this and lifting Arjuna with his own hands, the lord rid him of his bashfulness through his own lordly qualities. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः शिवोऽर्जुनं प्राह पाण्डवं वीरसम्मतम् ।
हर्षयन् सर्वथा प्रीत्या शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २७॥

English
Delighting Arjuna, son of Pāṇḍu, honoured by heroes, Śiva, favourably disposed towards his devotees, spoke to him with pleasure. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
हे पार्थ पाण्डवश्रेष्ठ प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं वृणु ।
प्रहारैस्ताडनैस्तेऽद्य पूजनम्मानितम्मया ॥ २८॥

English
Śiva said :—  O Arjuna, foremost among the sons of Pāṇḍu, I am delighted. Choose your boon. By your hits and blows I have been worshipped by you. I consider it so. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
इच्छया च कृतं मेऽद्य नापराधस्तवाधुना ।
नादेयं विद्यते तुभ्यं यदिच्छसि वृणीष्व तत् ॥ २९॥

English
This has been done by my own will. The fault is not yours. There is nothing which cannot be given to you. Choose whatever you desire. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ते शत्रुषु यशोराज्यस्थापनाय शुभं कृतम् ।
एतद्दुःखं न कर्तव्यं वैक्लव्यं च त्यजाखिलम् ॥ ३०॥

English
In order to establish the rule of your fame among your enemies what you have done is auspicious. You shall not be struck with grief. Eschew all your feebleness. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्त्वर्जुनस्तेन प्रभुणा शङ्करेण सः ।
उवाच शङ्करं भक्त्या सावधानतया स्थितः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* Thus addressed by lord Śiva, Arjuna stood carefully and spoke to Śiva with devotion. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
भक्तप्रियस्य शम्भोस्ते सुप्रभो किं समीहितम् ।
वर्णनीयं मया देव कृपालुस्त्वं सदाशिव ॥ ३२॥

English
Arjuna said :— O good lord, O Sadāśiva, is your desire, is your compassion to be described by me? You are the favourite of your devotees, you are Śiva, the bestower of welfare. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा संस्तुतिं तस्य शङ्करस्य महाप्रभोः ।
चकार पाण्डवः सोऽथ सद्भक्तिं वेदसम्मताम् ॥ ३३॥

English
After saying this, the son of Pāṇḍu eulogised Śiva the great lord, devotedly, in the manner approved of by the Vedas. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
नमस्ते देवदेवाय नमः कैलासवासिने ।
सदाशिव नमस्तुभ्यं पञ्चवक्त्राय ते नमः ॥ ३४॥

English
"Obeisance to you the lord of gods. Obeisance to you the resident of Kailasa. O Sadāśiva, obeisance to you. Obeisance to you, the five-faced98 one. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
कपर्दिने नमस्तुभ्यन्त्रिनेत्राय नमोऽस्तु ते ।
मनः प्रसन्नरूपाय सहस्रवदनाय च ॥ ३५॥

English
Obeisance to Kapardin (one with matted hair), obeisance to you, the three-eyed. Obeisance to the delighted one, obeisance to the thousand-faced. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नीलकण्ठ नमस्तेऽस्तु सद्योजाताय वै नमः ।
वृषध्वज नमस्तेऽस्तु वामाङ्गगिरिजाय च ॥ ३६॥

English
O blue-necked one, obeisance to you. Obeisance to Sadyojata. O bull-bannered one, obeisance to you with Pārvati as your left half. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
दशदोष नमस्तुभ्यं नमस्ते परमात्मने ।
डमरूकपालहस्ताय नमस्ते मुण्डमालिने ॥ ३७॥

English
O ten-armed one, Obeisance to you the great soul. Obeisance to you holding the drum and the skull in your hands. Obeisance to you, the one wearing a garland of skulls. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशशुद्धकर्पूरवर्ष्मणे ।
पिनाकपाणये तुभ्यं त्रिशूलवरधारिणे ॥ ३८॥

English
Obeisance to you with the body refulgent like pure crystal and pure camphor, obeisance to you with the Pināka in your hand, obeisance to the bearer of the excellent Trident. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याघ्रचर्मोत्तरीयाय गजाम्बरविधारिणे ।
नागाङ्गाय नमस्तुभ्यं गङ्गाधर नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ३९॥

English
Obeisance to the one with the tiger hide⁹⁹ as the upper cloth; obeisance to the wearer of elephant's hide.¹⁰⁰ Obeisance to you with your body encircled by the serpents. Obeisance to you the supporter of Gaṅgā. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सुपादाय नमस्तेऽस्तु आरक्तचरणाय च ।
नन्द्यादिगणसेव्याय गणेशाय च ते नमः ॥ ४०॥

English
Obeisance to you of splendid, reddish feet, worthy to be served by Nandin and other Gaṇas. Obeisance to you, the chief of Gaṇas. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नमो गणेशरूपाय कार्तिकेयानुगाय च ।
भक्तिदाय च भक्तानां मुक्तिदाय नमो नमः ॥ ४१॥

English
Obeisance to one in the form of Gaṇeśa, followed by Kārttikeya. Obeisance to the bestower of devotion and salvation to the devotees. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अगुणाय नमस्तेऽस्तु सगुणाय नमो नमः ।
अरूपाय सरूपाय सकलायाकलाय च ॥ ४२॥

English
Obeisance to you devoid of attributes. Obeisance to you possessed of attributes. Obeisance to one with and without forms, having digits and devoid of digits. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः किरातरूपाय मदनुग्रहकारिणे ।
युद्धप्रियाय वीराणां नानालीलानुकारिणे ॥ ४३॥

English
Obeisance to the one who blessed me in the form of Kirāta. Obeisance to the one who is fond of fighting with heroes, obeisance to the one performing various sports. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्किञ्चित् दृश्यते रूपं तत्तेजस्तावकं स्मृतम् ।
चिद्रूपस्त्वं त्रिलोकेषु रमसेऽन्वयभेदतः ॥ ४४॥

English
Whatever splendid form is seen, its splendour is yours. You are in the form of pure consciousness. You sport about in the three worlds in different guises. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
गुणानां ते न सङ्ख्यास्ति यथा भूरजसामिह ।
आकाशे तारकाणां हि कणानां वृष्ट्यपामपि ॥ ४५॥

English
There is no limit to your excellences as that of the dust particles, or stars in the sky or rain-drops. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
न ते गुणांस्तु सङ्ख्यातुं वेदा वै सम्भवन्ति हि ।
मन्दबुद्धिरहं नाथ वर्णयामि कथम्पुनः ॥ ४६॥

English
Even the Vedas cannot enumerate your good qualities. O lord, how can I, deficient in intellect, describe any ? (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽसि योऽसि नमस्तेऽस्तु कृपां कर्तुमिहार्हसि ।
दासोऽहं ते महेशान स्वामी त्वं मे महेश्वर ॥ ४७॥

English
Whatever be your nature, obeisance be to you. It behoves you to take pity. O lord, I am your slave. O lord Śiva, you are my master. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य पुनः प्रोवाच शङ्करः ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा विहसन्प्रभुरर्जुनम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  On hearing his words, lord Śiva, who was highly delighted spoke to Arjuna delightedly. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
वचसा किम्बहूक्तेन श‍ृणुष्व वचनं मम ।
शीघ्रं वृणु वरं पुत्र सर्वं तच्च ददामि ते ॥ ४९॥

English
Śiva said :—  Of what avail is a long talk? Listen to my words. O son, choose your boon quickly. I shall give you everything. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
इत्युक्तश्चार्जुनस्तेन प्रणिपत्य सदाशिवम् ।
साञ्जलिर्नतकः प्रेम्णा प्रोवाच गद्गदाक्षरम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Nandiśvara said :—  Thus urged by him, Arjuna bowed to Sadāśiva with palms joined in reverence and with head bending down. He spoke with words choked with devotion (50.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्जुन उवाच ।
किं ब्रूयां त्वं च सर्वेषामन्तर्यामितया स्थितः ।
तथापि वर्णितं मेऽद्य श्रूयतां च त्वया विभो ॥ ५१॥

English
Arjuna said :—  What shall I say? You are the immanent Soul of all. Still let my detailed explanation be heard by you, O lord. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
शत्रूणां सङ्कटं यच्च तद्गतन्दर्शनात्तव ।

English
Whatever danger I had from enemies has vanished at your sight. Please do that which may confer worldly Siddhis on me. (52.)

Sanskrit

English
Nandiśvara said :—  After saying this and bowing to Śiva who is favourably disposed to his devotees, Arjuna stood by with shoulder drooping down and palms joined in reverence. (53.)

English
Seeing Arjuna, son of Pāṇḍu, the foremost of his devotees, lord Śiva was extremely glad and satisfied. (54.)

English
Lord Śiva gave him Pāśupata missile that could not be thwarted by anyone at any time and spoke thus. (55.)

Sanskrit

English
Śiva said :—  I have given you my own great missile. You will become invincible thereby. You will reap success over your enemies through this. (56.)

English
I shall tell Kṛṣṇa also. He will help you. He is my own soul, my devotee and he carries out my task. (57.)

English
O Bhārata, due to my power, you will rule over your kingdom unhindered. You shall perform righteous deeds of various kinds always along with your brother. (58.)

English
*Nandīśvara said :—* After saying this and placing his hand over his head, Siva vanished after being duly worshipped by Arjuna. (59.)

The delighted Arjuna, after obtaining the excellent missile from the lord, returned to his hermitage remembering devotedly his chief preceptor and Siva. (60.)

When he arrived, his brother felt fully revived as the body with vital airs. They felt happy at his sight. Draupadi of good rites was also delighted. (61.)

The delighted Pāṇḍavas were not satiated by hearing the details from Arjuna and knowing that Śiva was satisfied. (62.)
