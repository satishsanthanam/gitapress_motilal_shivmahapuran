### **CHAPTER FORTYTWO - (The Twelve Jyotirlinga incarnations)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीश्वर उवाच ।
अवतारान् श‍ृणु विभोर्द्वादशप्रमितान्परान् ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपान्वै नानोतिकारकान्मुने ॥ १॥

English
Nandīśvara said:—  O sage, now listen to the twelve incarnations of the lord in the form of jyotirlingas, the cause of diverse sports. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सौराष्ट्रे सोमनाथश्च श्रीशैले मल्लिकार्जुनः ।
उज्जयिन्यां महाकाल ओङ्कारे चामरेश्वरः ॥ २॥

केदारो हिमवत्पृष्टे डाकिन्याम्भीमशङ्करः ।
वाराणस्यां च विश्वेशस्त्र्यम्बको गौतमीतटे ॥ ३॥

वैद्यनाथश्चिताभूमौ नागेशो दारुकावने ।
सेतुबन्धे च रामेशो घुश्मेशश्च शिवालये ॥ ४॥

English
The jyotirlingas¹⁰¹ are—Somanātha in the Saurāṣṭra¹⁰² territory; Mallikārjuna on the mountain Śrīśaila¹⁰³; Mahākāla in Ujjayinī¹⁰⁴; Amareśvara in Omkāra¹⁰⁵ Māndhatā; Kedāra¹⁰⁶ on the Himalaya; Bhīmaśaṅkara in the city of Dākinī;¹⁰⁷ Viśveśvara in the city of Vārāṇasī¹⁰⁸; Tryambaka on the banks of Gautamī¹⁰⁹; Vaidyanātha in the cremation ground¹¹⁰; Nāgeśa in the forest of Dārukā¹¹¹; Rāmeśvara in the Setubandha¹¹² Ghuśmeśvara in the Śiva temple. (2-4.)

---

Sanskrit
अवतारद्वादशकमेतच्छम्भोः परात्मनः ।
सर्वानन्दकरं पुंसां दर्शनात्स्पर्शनान्मुने ॥ ५॥

English
O sage, the twelve incarnations of the supreme soul are wholly delightful to all men for seeing and touching. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राद्यः सोमनाथो हि चन्द्रदुःखक्षयङ्करः ।
क्षयकुष्ठादिरोगाणां नाशकः पूजनान्मुने ॥ ६॥

English
There, the first one, Somanātha, annihilated the distress of the moon. O sage, by worshipping, it dispels the ailments of consumption, leprosy etc. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवावतारः सोमेशो लिङ्गरूपेण संस्थितः ।
सौराष्ट्रे शुभदेशे च शशिना पूजितः पुरा ॥ ७॥

English
This incarnation of Śiva, Someśa, is stationed in the auspicious land of Saurāṣṭra, in the form of a phallic image. It was worshipped by the moon formerly. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रकुण्डं च तत्रैव सर्वपापविनाशकम् ।
तत्र स्नात्वा नरो धीमान्सर्वरोगैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ८॥

English
Candarakuṇḍa (the pond of the Moon) is there itself and it destroys all sins. By bathing there a man becomes intelligent and gets rid of his ailments. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सोमेश्वरं महालिङ्गं शिवस्य परमात्मकम् ।
दृष्ट्वा प्रमुच्यते पापाद्भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च विन्दति ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing Someśvara, the great phallic image of Śiva, people are freed from sin. They attain worldly pleasures and salvation. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
मल्लिकार्जुनसंज्ञश्चावतारः शकरस्य वै ।
द्वितीयः श्रीगिरौ तात भक्ताभीष्टफलप्रदः ॥ १०॥

English
O dear, the second incarnation of Śiva Mallikārjuna, on the mountain, Śrīśaila, is the bestower of the desires of devotees. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
संस्तुतो लिङ्गरूपेण सुतदर्शनहेतुतः ।
गतस्तत्र महाप्रीत्या स शिवः स्वगिरेर्मुने ॥ ११॥

English
O sage, Śiva went there from his mountain, with great pleasure in order to see his son Kārttikeya. It is stationed there in the form of the phallic image. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं द्वितीयं तद्दर्शनात्पूजनान्मुने ।
महासुखकरं चान्ते मुक्तिदं नात्र संशयः ॥ १२॥

English
It is the second Jyotirlinga. O sage, it, is very pleasing. By seeing and worshipping it one derives salvation hereafter. There is no doubt in this. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालाभिधस्तातावतारः शङ्करस्य वै ।
उज्जयिन्यां नगर्यां च बभूव स्वजनावनः ॥ १३॥

English
O dear, Śiva incarnated himself as Mahākāla in the city of Ujjayinī for the protection of his devotees. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
दूषणाख्यासुरं यस्तु वेदधर्मप्रमर्दकम् ।
उज्जयिन्यां गतं विप्रद्वेषिणं सर्वनाशनम् ॥ १४॥

वेदविप्रसुतध्यातो हुङ्कारेणैव स द्रुतम् ।
भस्मसात्कृतवांस्तं च रत्नमाल निवासिनम् ॥ १५॥

English
The demon Dūṣaṇa, a resident of Ratnamāla,¹¹³ was a defiler of Vedic rites, a hater of brahmins and destroyer of everyone. When he reached Ujjayinī, the lord was meditated upon by the sons of the Vedic brahmins. He reduced the demon to ashes by a mere Huṃkāra. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
तं हत्वा स महाकालो ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपतः ।
देवैः स प्रार्थितोऽतिष्ठत्स्वभक्तपरिपालकः ॥ १६॥

English
After killing him, on being requested by the gods; lord Mahākāla stayed there in the form of a Jyotirlinga. He continues to protect his devotees. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालाह्वयं लिङ्गं दृष्ट्वाभ्यर्च्य प्रयत्नतः ।
सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति लभते परतो गतिम् ॥ १७॥

English
By seeing and strenuously worshipping the phallic image of Mahākāla, the devotee attains his desires and the greatest goal. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ओङ्कारः परमेशानो धृतः शम्भोः परात्मनः ।
अवतारश्चतुर्थो हि भक्ताभीष्टफलप्रदः ॥ १८॥

English
The fourth incarnation of Śiva the supreme soul, the great lord in Oṃkāra Māndhātā is a bestower of the desires of his devotees. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विधिना स्थापितो भक्त्या स्वलिङ्गात्पार्थिवान्मुने ।
प्रादुर्भूतो महादेवो विन्ध्यकामप्रपूरकः ॥ १९॥

English
O sage, lord Śiva manifested himself from his earthen phallic image devoutly installed by Brahmā and he fulfilled the desires of the mountain. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
देवैः सम्प्रार्थितस्तत्र द्विधारूपेण संस्थितः ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदो लिङ्गरूपो वै भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २०॥

English
On being requested by the gods the lord in the form of his phallic image stayed there two-fold, bestowing worldly pleasures and salvation and being favourably disposed towards his devotees. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणवे चैव चोङ्कारनामासील्लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ।
परमेश्वरनामासीत्पार्थिवश्च मुनीश्वर ॥ २१॥

English
The excellent phallic image abides in the syllable Oṃkāra. O great sage, it also abides in the earthen phallic image in Oṃkāra Māndhātā. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्ताभीष्टप्रदो ज्ञेयो योऽपि दृष्टोऽर्चितो मुने ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गे महादिव्ये वर्णिते ते महामुने ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, O great sage, whatever is seen or worshipped in the extremely divine phallic image described to you is the bestower of the desires of all devotees. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
केदारेशोऽवतारस्तु पञ्चमः परमश्शिवः ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपेण केदारे संस्थितः स च ॥ २३॥

English
The fifth great incarnation of Siva is the Kedaresa. He is stationed in Kedara in the form of Jyotirlinga. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नरनारायणाख्यौ याववतारौ हरेर्मुने ।
तत्प्रार्थितः शिवस्तत्स्थैः केदारे हिमभूधरे ॥ २४॥

English
It was at the request of the twofold incarnations of Visnu, named Nara and Narayana that Siva stayed in Kedara on the mountain Himavat, O sage. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ताभ्यां च पूजितो नित्यं केदारेश्वरसंज्ञकः ।
भक्ताभीष्टप्रदः शम्भुर्दर्शनादर्चनादपि ॥ २५॥

English
Siva named Kedareśvara was worshipped everyday by them. He is the bestower of the desires of the devotees, by his sight and worship. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य खण्डस्य स स्वामी सर्वेशोऽपि विशेषतः ।
सर्वकामप्रदस्तात सोऽवतारः शिवस्य वै ॥ २६॥

English
Although he is the lord of all, he is the particular reigning deity of this territory. O dear that incarnation of Siva is the bestower of all desires. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमशङ्करसंज्ञस्तु षष्ठः शम्भोर्महाप्रभोः ।
अवतारो महालीलो भीमासुरविनाशनः ॥ २७॥

English
The sixth incarnation of Siva, the great lord named Bhimaśaṅkara, performed great sports. He destroyed the demon Bhima. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदक्षिणाभिधं भक्तं कामरूपेश्वरं नृपम् ।
यो ररक्षाद्भुतं हत्वासुरं तं भक्तदुःखदम् ॥ २८॥

English
After killing this wonderfully lusty demon who caused misery to the devotees, the lord saved the devotee Sudakṣiṇa who was the king of Kāmarūpa. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमशङ्करनामा स डाकिन्यां संस्थितः स्वयम् ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपेण प्रार्थितस्तेन शङ्करः ॥ २९॥

English
Requested by him Siva himself in the name of Bhimaśaṅkara stayed in Dākinī in the form of Jyotirlinga. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेश्वरावतारस्तु काश्यां जातो हि सप्तमः ।
सर्वब्रह्माण्डरूपश्च भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदो मुने ॥ ३०॥

English
O sage, the seventh incarnation as Visvesvara at Kasi is in the form of the entire cosmic egg. It yields worldly pleasures and salvation. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजितः सर्वदेवैश्च भक्त्या विष्ण्वादिभिः सदा ।
कैलासपतिना चापि भैरवेणापि नित्यशः ॥ ३१॥

English
He is always worshipped by Visnu and all other gods as well as Kubera and Bhairava. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपेण संस्थितस्तत्र मुक्तिदः ।
स्वयं सिद्धस्वरूपो हि तथा स्वपुरि स प्रभुः ॥ ३२॥

English
He is stationed there in the form of Jyotirlinga. In his own city the lord is in the form of Siddha and he confers salvation. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
काशीविश्वेशयोर्भक्त्या तन्नामजपकारकाः ।
निर्लिप्ताः कर्मभिर्नित्यं केवल्यपदभागिनः ॥ ३३॥

English
Those who repeat the names of Kasi and Visvesa and perform their Japa with devotion become unaffected by actions and reach the state of Kaivalya. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
त्र्यम्बकाख्योऽवतारो यः सोऽष्टमो गौतमीतटे ।
प्रार्थितो गौतमेनाविर्बभूव शशिमौलिनः ॥ ३४॥

English
The eighth incarnation Tryambaka of the mooncrested lord Siva made its appearance on the banks of the river Gautami on being requested by the sage Gautama. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमस्य प्रार्थनया ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपतः ।
स्थितस्तत्राचलः प्रीत्या तन्मुनेः प्रीतिकाम्यया ॥ ३५॥

English
At the request of Gautama and with a desire to delight that sage, the lord stood there motionless in the form of Jyotirlinga. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य सन्दर्शनात्स्पर्शादर्चनाच्च महेशितुः ।
सर्वे कामाः प्रसिध्यन्ति ततो मुक्तिर्भवेदहो ॥ ३६॥

English
All desires fructify at the sight and touch of the great lord. Thereafter salvation too is attained. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवानुग्रहतस्तत्र गङ्गा नाम्ना तु गौतमी ।
संस्थिता गौतमप्रीत्या पावनी शङ्करप्रिया ॥ ३७॥

English
With the blessings of Siva, and pleased with Gautama, the holy river Gaṅgā, the beloved of Siva, stayed there in the name of Gautami. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
वैद्यनाथावतारो हि नवमस्तत्र कीर्तितः ।
आविर्भूतो रावणार्थं बहुलीलाकरः प्रभुः ॥ ३८॥

English
The ninth incarnation is glorified as Vaidyanatha. The lord indulging in many sports manifested himself for the sake of Rāvana. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तदानयनरूपं हि व्याजं कृत्वा महेश्वरः ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपेण चिताभूमौ प्रतिष्ठितः ॥ ३९॥

English
Under the pretext of fetching him, lord Siva was installed in the cremation ground in the form of Jyotirlinga. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
वैद्यनाथेश्वरो नाम्ना प्रसिद्धोऽभूज्जगत्त्रये ।
दर्शनात्पूजनाद्भक्त्या भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदः स हि ॥ ४०॥

English
The lord in the name of Vaidyanatha became famous in the three worlds. Seeing and worshipping him with devotion yields worldly pleasures and salvation. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
वैद्यनाथेश्वरशिवमाहात्म्यमनुशासनम् ।
पठतां श‍ृण्वतां चापि भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं मुने ॥ ४१॥

English
O sage, this benedictory passage referring to the greatness of Siva in the name of Vaidyanathesvara yields worldly pleasures and salvation to those who read and listen. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
नागेश्वरावतारस्तु दशमः परिकीर्तितः ।
आविर्भूतः स्वभक्तार्थं दुष्टानां दण्डदः सदा ॥ ४२॥

English
The incarnation of Nagesvara is glorified as the tenth one. The lord manifested himself as the chastiser of the wicked for his devotees. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
हत्वा दारुकनामानं राक्षसं धर्मघातकम् ।
स्वभक्तं वैश्यनाथं च प्रारक्षत्सुप्रियाभिधम् ॥ ४३॥

English
He killed the Raksasa Daruka, a violator of virtue. He saved his devotee Supriya who was a merchant king. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकानामुपकारार्थं ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपधृक् ।
सन्तस्थौ साम्बिकः शम्भुर्बहुलीलाकरः परः ॥ ४४॥

English
In order to help all the worlds, Siva, the great indulgent in sports, remained there assuming the form of Jyotirlinga, accompanied by Parvati. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा शिवलिङ्गं तु मुने नागेश्वराभिधम् ।
विनश्यन्ति द्रुतं चार्च्य महापातकराशयः ॥ ४५॥

English
O sage, at the sight of Siva's phallic image named Nagesvara and thanks to its worship, heaps of great sins perish immediately. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
रामेश्वरावतारस्तु शिवस्यैकादशः स्मृतः ।
रामचन्द्रप्रियकरो रामसंस्थापितो मुने ॥ ४६॥

English
O sage, the incarnation of Siva is the eleventh one. It was pleasing to Rama and it was installed by him. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Duly propitiated by Rama, Siva who is favourably disposed towards his devotees manifested himself in the phallic image and granted the boon of victory to him. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

सन्तस्थौ सेतुबन्धे च रामसंसेवितो मुने ॥ ४८॥

English
O sage, the lord, implored ardently by Rama and propitiated by Rama stayed at Setubandha in the form of Jyotirlinga. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदश्चैव सर्वदा भक्तकामदः ॥ ४९॥

English
The grandeur of Rame'sara in the world was wonderful and incomparable, yielding worldly pleasures and salvation and bestowing the desires of the devotees ever. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

रामेश्वरं च सद्भक्त्या स जीवन्मुक्त एव हि ॥ ५०॥

English
The man who performs the ablution of Rāmeśvara is really a living liberated soul. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
इह भुक्त्वाखिलान्भोगान्देवतादुर्ल्लभानपि ।
अतः प्राप्य परं ज्ञानं कैवल्यं मोक्षमाप्नुयात् ॥ ५१॥

English
After enjoying all worldly pleasures here, and then getting perfect knowledge, he shall obtain salvation hereafter. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The incarnation of Siva as Ghusmeśvara is the twelfth wherein the lord indulged in different sports and conferred bliss on Ghusma. He is favourably disposed towards his devotees. (52.)

Sanskrit
दक्षिणस्यां दिशि मुने देवशैलसमीपतः ।
आविर्बभूव सरसि घुश्माप्रियकरः प्रभुः ॥ ५३॥

English
O sage, the lord manifested himself in a lake near the Devaśaila$^{114}$ in the southern quarter acting favourably to Ghusma. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदेहामारितं घुश्मापुत्रं साकल्यतो मुने ।
तुष्टस्तद्भक्तितः शम्भुर्योऽरक्षद्भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ५४॥

English
O sage, Siva who is favourably disposed towards his devotees entirely revived the son of Ghusma who was killed by Sudehya. He was so pleased with her devotion. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्रार्थितः स वै शम्भुस्तडागे तत्र कामदाः ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपेण तस्थौ घुश्मेश्वराभिधः ॥ ५५॥

English
At her request, Siva stayed in the lake in the form of Jyotirlinga Ghusmeśvara conferring desires to the devotees. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा शिवलिङ्गं तु समभ्यर्च्य च भक्तितः ।
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा ततो मुक्तिं च विन्दति ॥ ५६॥

English
The man who sees that phallic image and worships it with devotion enjoys happiness here and attains salvation hereafter. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते हि समाख्याता ज्योतिर्लिङ्गावली मया ।
द्वादशप्रमिता दिव्या भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायिनी ॥ ५७॥

English
Thus I have explained to you the series of divine Jyotirlingas twelve in number yielding both worldly pleasures and salvation. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
एतां ज्योतिर्लिङ्गकथां यः पठेच्छृणुयादपि ।
मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यो भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च विन्दति ॥ ५८॥

English
He who reads or listens to this narrative of Jyotirlingas becomes freed of all sins and derives worldly pleasures and salvation. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
शतरुद्राभिधा चेयं वर्णिता संहिता मया ।
शतावतारसत्कीर्तिः सर्वकामफलप्रदा ॥ ५९॥

English
Thus I have described the compendium Satarudra by name which glorifies the hundred incarnations of Siva and yields the fruits of desires. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
इमां यः पठते नित्यं श‍ृणुयाद्वा समाहितः ।
सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति ततो मुक्तिं लभेद् ध्रुवम् ॥ ६०॥

English
He who reads this always or listens to it devotedly attains all desires and then becomes liberated, to be sure. (60.)
