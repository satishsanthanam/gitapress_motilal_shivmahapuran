### **CHAPTER ONE - (The greatness of Jyotirlingas and their Upalingas)**

---

Sanskrit
यो धत्ते निजमाययैव भुवनाकारं विकारोज्झितो ।
यस्याहुः करुणाकटाक्षविभवौ स्वर्गापवर्गाभिधौ ।
प्रत्यग्बोधसुखाद्वयं हृदि सदा पश्यन्ति यं योगिन-
स्तस्मै शैलसुताञ्चितार्धवपुषे शश्वन्नमस्तेजसे ॥ १॥

English
Obeisance for ever to that brilliance, half of whose body has been beautified by the daughter of the mountain; who, rid of all aberrations, assumes the shape of the worlds by means of his own Māyā; whose sympathetic glance, they say, brings about the wealth and glory in the form of heaven and salvation and whom the Yogins see in their hearts as the one without a second and as the inner understanding and bliss. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपाललितवीक्षणं स्मितमनोज्ञवक्त्राम्बुजं
शशाङ्ककलयोज्ज्वलं शमितघोरतापत्रयम् ।
करोतु किमपि स्फुरत्परमसौख्यसच्चिद्वपु-
र्धराधरसुताभुजोद्वलयितं महो मङ्गलम् ॥ २॥

English
May that mass of splendour, whatever it be, be auspicious, the splendour that has eyes gentle due to compassion; which has lotus-like face smiling charmly; which is brilliant with the digit of the moon; which subdues the terrible three-fold distresses; whose body of excessive bliss, existence and knowledge sheds brightness; and which is encircled by the arms of the daughter of the mountain, Pārvati. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः
सम्यगुक्तं त्वया सूत लोकानां हितकाम्यया ।
शिवावतारमाहात्म्यं नानाख्यानसमन्वितम् ॥ ३॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, the greatness of the incarnations of Śiva inclusive of their narratives, has been well explained by you with a desire for the welfare of the world. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्च कथ्यतां तात शिवमाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ।
लिङ्गसम्बन्धि सुप्रीत्या धन्यस्त्वं शैवसत्तम ॥ ४॥

English
O dear, may the excellent grandeur of Siva be narrated with pleasure in connection with his phallic forms. You are blessed and the most excellent among the devotees of Siva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वन्तस्त्वन्मुखाम्भोजान्न तृप्ताः स्मो वयं प्रभो ।
शैवं यशोऽमृतं रम्यं तदेव पुनरुच्यताम् ॥ ५॥

English
Listening from your lotus-like mouth, O holy lord, to the nectar-like glory of Siva, we are never satiated. Let the same sweet narrative be repeated. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यां यानि लिङ्गानि तीर्थे तीर्थे शुभानि हि ।
अन्यत्र वा स्थले यानि प्रसिद्धानि स्थितानि वै ॥ ६॥

तानि तानि च दिव्यानि लिङ्गानि परमेशितुः ।
व्यासशिष्य समाचक्ष्व लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ७॥

English
O disciple of Vyasa, with a desire for the welfare of the worlds, please narrate in detail those divine phallic images of the great lord whether they be on the earth in the different holy centres or in other places glorified by men. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टमृषिश्रेष्ठा लोकानां हितकाम्यया ।
कथयामि भवत्स्नेहात्तानि सङ्क्षेपतो द्विजाः ॥ ८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, O excellent sages, you have asked well. I shall explain them in brief with a desire for the welfare of the worlds and due to my affection for you. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां शिवलिङ्गानां मुने सङ्ख्या न विद्यते ।
सर्वा लिङ्गमयी भूमिः सर्वं लिङ्गमयं जगत् ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, there is no limit to the number of phallic images. The entire earth, the entire universe is in the form of a phallus. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गयुक्तानि तीर्थानि सर्वं लिङ्गे प्रतिष्ठितम् ।
सङ्ख्या न विद्यते तेषां तानि किञ्चिद्ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ १०॥

English
The holy centres are equipped with phallic images. Everything is founded on these. There is no limit to their number. I shall mention a few of them. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्किञ्चिद् दृश्यते दृश्यं वर्ण्यते स्मर्यते च यत् ।
तत्सर्वं शिवरूपं हि नान्यदस्तीति किञ्चन ॥ ११॥

English
Whatever is seen, described or remembered is Śiva's form. Nothing other than that has real existence. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि श्रूयतां प्रीत्या कथयामि यथाश्रुतम् ।
लिङ्गानि च ऋषिश्रेष्ठाः पृथिव्यां यानि तानि ह ॥ १२॥

पाताले चापि वर्तन्ते स्वर्गे चापि तथा भुवि ।
सर्वत्र पूज्यते शम्भुः स देवासुरमानुषैः ॥ १३॥

English
Still I shall explain in the manner I have heard. Let it be heard with pleasure O excellent sages, whatever images are there on the earth in the Pātāla or in heaven, everywhere Śiva is worshipped by the gods, Asuras and human beings. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिजगच्छम्भुना व्याप्तं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ।
अनुग्रहाय लोकानां लिङ्गरूपेण सत्तमाः ॥ १४॥

English
O excellent ones, the three worlds consisting of the gods, Asuras and human beings are pervaded by Śiva in the form of phallic image for the welfare of the worlds. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहाय लोकानां लिङ्गानि च महेश्वरः ।
दधाति विविधान्यत्र तीर्थे चान्यस्थले तथा ॥ १५॥

English
In order to bless the worlds, lord Śiva assumes different phallic forms here in the holy centres as well as in other places. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र यत्र यदा शम्भुर्भक्त्या भक्तैश्च संस्मृतः ।
तत्र तत्रावतीर्याथ कार्यं कृत्वा स्थितस्तदा ॥ १६॥

English
Śiva incarnates in those places wherever he is devoutly remembered by the devotees. After carrying out their task he stations himself there. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकानामुपकारार्थं स्वलिङ्गं चाप्यकल्पयत् ।
तल्लिङ्गं पूजयित्वा तु सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥ १७॥

English
He has assigned his phallic form for the benefit of the worlds. The devotee attains perfection by worshipping that form. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यां यानि लिङ्गानि तेषां सङ्ख्या न विद्यते ।
तथापि च प्रधानानि कथ्यन्ते च मया द्विजाः ॥ १८॥

English
O noble ascetics, there is no limit to the number of his phallic images on the earth. Still I narrate the important ones. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधानेषु च यानीह मुख्यानि प्रवदाम्यहम् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते मानवः क्षणात् ॥ १९॥

English
Even among the important ones, I shall enumerate the principal ones, on hearing which a man instantly gets rid of his sins. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गानि यानीह मुख्यमुख्यानि सत्तम ।
तान्यहं कथयाम्यद्य श्रुत्वा पापं व्यपोहति ॥ २०॥

English
O excellent one, I shall mention the most important of the Jyotirlingas on hearing which a person becomes sinless. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सौराष्ट्रे सोमनाथं च श्रीशैले मल्लिकार्जुनम् ।
उज्जयिन्यां महाकालमोङ्कारे परमेश्वरम् ॥ २१॥

केदारं हिमवत्पृष्ठे डाकिन्यां भीमशङ्करम् ।
वाराणस्यां च विश्वेशं त्र्यम्बकं गौतमीतटे ॥ २२॥

वैद्यनाथं चिताभूमौ नागेशं दारुकावने ।
सेतुबन्धे च रामेशं घुश्मेशं च शिवालये ॥ २३॥

द्वादशैतानि नामानि प्रातरुत्थाय यः पठेत् ।
सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः सर्वसिद्धिफलं लभेत् ॥ २४॥

English
He who reads or recites the twelve names early in the morning is freed from all sins and attains the fruits of all Siddhis. The twelve Jyotirlingas are—Somanātha in Saurāṣṭra, Mallikārjuna in Śrīśaila, Mahākāla in Ujjayinī Parameśvara in Omkāra, Kedāra on the Himavat, Bhīma-Śaṅkara in Dākinī, Viśveśa in Vārāṇasī, Tryambaka on the bank of the Gautamī, Vaidyanātha in the cremation ground, Nāgeśa in the Dārukā forest, Rāmeśa at Setubandha and Ghuśmeśa at Śivālaya. (21-24.)

---

Sanskrit
यं यं काममपेक्ष्यैव पठिष्यन्ति नरोत्तमाः ।
प्राप्स्यन्ति कामं तं तं हि परत्रेह मुनीश्वराः ॥ २५॥

English
O great sages, such excellent men will attain those desires for which they recite the names. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ये निष्कामतया तानि पठिष्यन्ति शुभाशयाः ।
तेषां च जननीगर्भे वासो नैव भविष्यति ॥ २६॥

English
Those who recite them without any desire but with purity of mind will never have rebirth involving residence in the mother's womb. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एतेषां पूजनेनैव वर्णानां दुःखनाशनम् ।
इहलोके परत्रापि मुक्तिर्भवति निश्चितम् ॥ २७॥

English
By worshipping these, people of all castes get their miseries destroyed in this world and become liberated hereafter, to be sure. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ग्राह्यमेषां च नैवेद्यं भोजनीयं प्रयत्नतः ।
तत्कर्तुः सर्वपापानि भस्मसाद्यान्ति वै क्षणात् ॥ २८॥

English
The food offerings of these Jyotirlingas shall be taken and others shall be fed with the same. Such acts reduce the sins to ashes instantaneously. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिषां चैव लिङ्गानां ब्रह्मादिभिरलं द्विजाः ।
विशेषतः फलं वक्तुं शक्यते न परैस्तथा ॥ २९॥

English
O brahmins, the results of the worship of Jyotirlingas cannot be described adequately by Brahmā and others. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एकं च पूजितं येन षण्मासं तन्निरन्तरम् ।
तस्य दुःखं न जायेत मातृकुक्षिसमुद्भवम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Even if one of them is worshipped continuously for six months, the misery of rebirth in the mother's womb will never recur. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
हीनयोनौ यदा जातो ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं च पश्यति ।
तस्य जन्म भवेत्तत्र विमले सत्कुले पुनः ॥ ३१॥

English
Even if a person of base birth happens to see Jyotirlinga, he will be born in a pure and good family. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्कुले जन्म सम्प्राप्य धनाढ्यो वेदपारगः ।
शुभकर्म तदा कृत्वा मुक्तिं यात्यनपायिनीम् ॥ ३२॥

English
Being born in a good family he will become rich and master all the Vedas. Then by performing auspicious rites, he shall attain liberation without any obstacle. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
म्लेच्छो वाप्यन्त्यजो वापि षण्ढो वापि मुनीश्वराः ।
द्विजो भूत्वा भवेन्मुक्तस्तस्मात्तद्दर्शनं चरेत् ॥ ३३॥

English
O great sages, whoever he may be, an outcaste, a slave or an eunuch, he will be born as a brahmin and become liberated. Hence one shall view the Jyotirlinga. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिषां चैव लिङ्गानां किञ्चित्प्रोक्तं फलं मया ।
ज्योतिषां चोपलिङ्गानि श्रूयन्तामृषिसत्तमाः ॥ ३४॥

English
O excellent sages, a few of the fruits of the Jyotirlingas have been narrated by me. Let the Upalingas (ancillary phallic forms) of these Jyotirlingas be listened to. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सोमेश्वरस्य यल्लिङ्गमन्तकेशमुदाहृतम् ।
मह्याः सागरसंयोगे तल्लिङ्गमुपलिङ्गकम् ॥ ३५॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Someśvara is called Antakeśa and it is installed at the confluence of the river Mahī and the ocean.$^{115}$ (35.)

---

Sanskrit
मल्लिकार्जुनसम्भूतमुपलिङ्गमुदाहृतम् ।
रुद्रेश्वरमिति ख्यातं भृगुकक्षे सुखावहम् ॥ ३६॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Mallikārjuna is glorified as Rudreśvara. It is pleasing and is stationed in Bhṛgukakṣa.$^{116}$ (36.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालभवं लिङ्गं दुग्धेशमिति विश्रुतम् ।
नर्मदायां प्रसिद्धं तत्सर्वपापहरं स्मृतम् ॥ ३७॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Mahākāla is well known as Dugdheśa. It is famous in the region of Narmadā and is remembered as destructive of all sins. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ऊँकारजं च यल्लिङ्गं कर्दमेशमिति श्रुतम् ।
प्रसिद्धं बिन्दुसरसि सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ ३८॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Omkāra is famous as Kardameśa in the Bindu lake$^{117}$ and yields the fruits of all desires. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
केदारेश्वरसञ्जातं भूतेशं यमुनातटे ।
महापापहरं प्रोक्तं पश्यतामर्चतां तथा ॥ ३९॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Kedāreśvara is Bhūteśa on the banks of the Yamunā. It is destructive of sins of the visiting devotees. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमशङ्करसम्भूतं भीमेश्वरमिति स्मृतम् ।
सह्याचले प्रसिद्धं तन्महाबलविवर्द्धनम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Bhīmaśaṅkara is Bhīmeśvara. It is famous in the Sahya mountain and it causes the increase of great strength. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेश्वराच्च यज्जातं शरण्येश्वर विश्रुतम् ।
त्र्यम्बकस्य च यत्प्रोक्तं सिद्धेश्वर इति श्रुतम् ॥ ४१॥

English
The Upaliṅga of Nāgeśvara is Bhūteśvara. Installed on the banks of Mallikāsarasvatī,$^{118}$ it quells sins by the very sight. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
वैद्यनाथाच्च यज्जातं वैजनाथ इति स्मृतम् ।
नागेश्वरसमुद्भूतं भूतेश्वरमुदाहृतम् ॥ ४२॥

English
The Upaliṅga attached to Rāmeśvara is Gupteśvara. The Upaliṅga attached to Ghuśmeśa is Vyāghreśvara. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
मल्लिकासरस्वतीतीरे दर्शनात्पापहारकम् ।
रामेश्वराच्च यज्जातं गुप्तेश्वरमिति स्मृतम् ॥ ४३॥

English
O brahmins, thus I have mentioned the Upaliṅgas and the Jyotirlingas. They dispel sins by their sight and yield the fruit of all desires. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
घुश्मेशाच्चैव यज्जातं व्याघ्रेश्वरमिति स्मृतम्
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गोपलिङ्गानि प्रोक्तानीह मया द्विजाः ॥ ४४॥

English
These are the most important ones. O excellent sages, let the other important ones also be heard. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
दर्शनात्पापहारीणि सर्वकामप्रदानि च ।
एतानि सुप्रधानानि मुख्यतां हि गतानि च ।
अन्यानि चापि मुख्यानि श्रूयन्तामृषिसत्तमाः ॥ ४५॥
