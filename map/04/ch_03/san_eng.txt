### **CHAPTER THREE - (The penance of Anasūyā and Atri)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
ब्रह्मपुर्यां चित्रकूटे लिङ्गं मत्तगजेन्द्रकम् ।
ब्रह्मणा स्थापितं पूर्वं सर्वकामसमृद्धिदम् ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said :—  The Linga Mattagajendraka in Brahmapuri$^{126}$ at Citrakūṭa had been installed formerly by Brahma. It bestows all desires lavishly. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पूर्वदिशि कोटीशं लिङ्गं सर्ववरप्रदम् ।
गोदावर्याः पश्चिमे तल्लिङ्गं पशुपतिनामकम् ॥ २॥

English
To the east of it is the phallic image Kotiśa bestowing all boons. It is installed to the west of Godavari and is famous as Pasupati. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणस्यां दिशि कश्चिदत्रीश्वर इति स्वयम् ।
लोकानामुपकारार्थमनसूयासुखाय च ॥ ३॥

प्रादुर्भूतः स्वयं देवो ह्यनावृष्ट्यामजीवयत् ।
स एव शङ्करः साक्षादंशेन स्वयमेव हि ॥ ४॥

English
The lord himself appeared in the southern quarter in the name of Atriśvara for helping the people and for the happiness of Anasūyā. He enlivened people at the time of draught. He is himself Śiva partially. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग कथमत्रीश्वरो हरः ।
उत्पन्नः परमो दिव्यस्तत्त्वं कथय सुव्रत ॥ ५॥

English
The sages said :—  O Sūta, O fortunate Sūta, how was the excessively divine Śiva born as Atriśvara ? O one of excellent rites, please narrate it. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टमृषिश्रेष्ठाः कथयामि कथां शुभाम् ।
यां कथां सततं श्रुत्वा पातकैर्मुच्यते ध्रुवम् ॥ ६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, you have put the question well. I shall narrate the story on hearing which constantly one is relieved of sins. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणस्यां दिशि महत् कामदं नाम यद्वनम् ।
चित्रकूटसमीपेऽस्ति तपसां हितदं सताम् ॥ ७॥

तत्र च ब्रह्मणः पुत्रो ह्यत्रिनामा ऋषिः स्वयम् ।
तपस्तेपेऽतिकठिनमनसूयासमन्वितः ॥ ८॥

English
There is a great forest Kāmada$^{127}$ in the south near Citrakūṭa. It is beneficent to the good in the performance of penance. There the son of Brahma, the sage Atri, performed a severe penance accompanied by Anasūyā. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वं कदाचित्तत्रैव ह्यनावृष्टिरभून्मुने ।
दुःखदा प्राणिनां दैवाद्विकटा शतवार्षिकी ॥ ९॥

English
O sage, formerly once, there was a great draught there, extending to a hundred years which put all living beings to distress. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
वृक्षाः शुष्कास्तदा सर्वे पल्लवानि फलानि च ।
नित्यार्थं न जलं क्वापि दृष्टमासीन्मुनीश्वराः ॥ १०॥

English
O great sages, the trees became dry then without sprouts or fruits. No water was seen anywhere even for daily routine work. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्द्रीभावो न लभ्येत खरा वाता दिशो दश ।
हाहाकारो महानासीत्पृथिव्यां दुःखदोऽति हि ॥ ११॥

English
There was no greenery anywhere. The winds blew dry and rough. There was a painful hue and cry all over the earth. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
संवर्तं चैव भूतानां दृष्ट्वात्रिगृहिणी प्रिया ।
साध्वी चैवाब्रवीदत्रिं मया दुःखं न सह्यते ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing the dissolution of all living beings, the chaste lady, the beloved wife of Atri said to her husband. "I cannot bear this distress." (12.)

---

Sanskrit
समाधौ च विलीनोऽभूदासने संस्थितः स्वयम् ।
प्राणायामं त्रिरावृत्त्या कृत्वा मुनिवरस्तदा ॥ १३॥

English
The sage then sat in the yogic pose, repeated the Pranayama three times and sank into the yogic trance. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायति स्म परं ज्योतिरात्मस्थमात्मना च सः ।
अत्रिर्मुनिवरो ज्ञानी शङ्करं निर्विकारकम् ॥ १४॥

English
Atri, the great sage of perfect knowledge, meditated on Siva devoid of aberrations, the great splendour stationed in the soul, through his own soul. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वामिनि ध्यानलीने च शिष्यास्ते दूरतो गताः ।
अन्नं विना तदा ते तु मुक्त्वा तं स्वगुरुं मुनिम् ॥ १५॥

English
When the preceptor and into meditation his disciples unable to get food deserted the sage, their preceptor, and went away. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
एकाकिनी तदा जाता सानसूया पतिव्रता ।
सिषेवे सा च सततं तं मुदा मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ १६॥

English
Anasuyā the chaste lady then was left all alone. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवं सुन्दरं कृत्वा मन्त्रेण विधिपूर्वकम् ।
मानसैरुपचारैश्च पूजयामास शङ्करम् ॥ १७॥

तुष्टाव शङ्करं भक्त्या संसेवित्वा मुहुर्मुहुः ।
बद्धाञ्जलिपुटा भूत्वा प्रक्रम्य स्वामिनं शिवम् ॥ १८॥

English
She joyously served the sage constantly. Making a beautiful clay image through mantras in accordance with the rules, she worshipped Siva through mental modes of service. After serving Śiva repeatedly she eulogised him with devotion. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
दण्डवत्प्रणिपातेन प्रतिप्रक्रमणं तदा ।
चकार सुचरित्रा सानसूया मुनिकामिनी ॥ १९॥

दैत्याश्च दानवाः सर्वे दृष्ट्वा तु सुन्दरीं तदा ।
विह्वलाश्चाभवँस्तत्र तेजसा दूरतः स्थिताः ॥ २०॥

अग्निं दृष्ट्वा यथा दूरे वर्तन्ते तद्वदेव हि ।
तथैनां च तदा दृष्ट्वा नायान्तीह समीपगाः ॥ २१॥

English
Anasūyā the well behaved wife of the sage circumambulated her lord and Śiva. After prostration she went round anticlockwise too. On seeing that beautiful lady the Daityas and Dānavas were excessively agitated by her brilliance. They kept themselves away as people do from a blazing fire. (19-21.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रेश्च तपसश्चैवानसूयाशिवसेवनम् ।
विशिष्यते स्म विप्रेन्द्रा मनोवाक्कायसंस्कृतम् ॥ २२॥

English
O leading brahmins, Anasūyā's service to Śiva performed sacredly through mind, speech and body surpassed even the penance of Atri. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्कालं तु सा देवी परिचर्यां चकार ह ।
यावत्कालं मुनिवरः प्राणायामपरायणः ॥ २३॥

English
As long as the sage was engrossed in Prāṇāyāma, the gentle lady continued her service. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तौ दम्पती तदा तत्र स्वस्वकार्यपरायणौ ।
संस्थितौ मुनिशार्दूल नान्यः कश्चित्परः स्थितः ॥ २४॥

English
O excellent sage, the couple thus engaged in their activities remained there, alone. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं जाते तदा काले ह्यत्रिश्च ऋषिसत्तमः ।
ध्याने च परमे लीनो न व्यबुध्यत किञ्चन ॥ २५॥

English
Thus a long time elapsed. The excellent sage, sunk in great meditation did not wake up at all. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अनसूयापि सा साध्वी स्वामिनं वै शिवं तथा ।
भेजे नान्यत्परं किञ्चिज्जानीते स्म च सा सती ॥ २६॥

English
Anasūyā the chaste lady worshipped only her lord and Śiva. That chaste lady did not care to know anything else. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यैव तपसा सर्वे तस्याश्च भजनेन च ।
देवाश्च ऋषयश्चैव गङ्गाद्याः सरितस्तथा ॥ २७॥

दर्शनार्थं तयोः सर्वाः परप्रीत्या समाययुः ।
दृष्ट्वा च तत्तपः सेवां विस्मयं परमं ययुः ॥ २८॥

English
On account of his penance and her worship the gods and the sages, Gaṅgā and the rivers came there in order to see them with pleasure. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोस्तदद्भुतं दृष्ट्वा समूचुर्भजनं वरम् ।
उभयोः किं विशिष्टं च तपसो भजनस्य च ॥ २९॥

अत्रेश्चैव तपःप्रोक्तमनसूयानुसेवनम् ।
तत्सर्वमुभयोर्दृष्ट्वा समूचुर्भजनं वरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing his penance and her service they were surprised. On seeing their wonderful activity they asked one another. "Which of the two—the penance or the service, is the greater?" They themselves replied—"Arti's penance is good but Anasūyā's service is better". (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वैश्च ऋषिभिश्चैव दुष्करं तु तपः कृतम् ।
एतादृशं तु केनापि क्व कृतं नैतदब्रुवन् ॥ ३१॥

English
On seeing their performance some said "Her service is better." Others said "Difficult penances had been performed by sages of yore but nothing like Dadhīci's penance was ever performed by any". (31.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्योऽयं च मुनिर्धन्या तथेयमनसूयिका ।
यदैताभ्यां परं प्रीत्या क्रियते सुतपः पुनः ॥ ३२॥

एतादृशं शुभं चैतत्तपो दुष्करमुत्तमम् ।
त्रिलोक्यां क्रियते केन साम्प्रतं ज्ञायते न हि ॥ ३३॥

English
The sage is blessed. Anasūyā is blessed, since great penance is pursued by these with great pleasure. If there be a person performing such an auspicious, difficult and excellent penance anywhere in the three worlds, we do not know. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोरेव प्रशंसां च कृत्वा ते तु यथागतम् ।
गतास्ते च तदा तत्र गङ्गाञ्च गिरिशं विना ॥ ३४॥

English
After praising them thus, they went away but only Gaṅgā and Śiva were left. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गा मद्भजनप्रीता साध्वी धर्मविमोहिता ।
कृत्वोपकारमेतस्यागमिष्यामीत्युवाच सा ॥ ३५॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  “Delighted in worshipping us the chaste lady is too much engrossed in virtuous rites. I shall help her and then go.” (35.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि ध्यानसम्बद्धो मुनेरत्रेर्मुनीश्वराः ।
पूर्णांशेन स्थितस्तत्र कैलासं न जगाम ह ॥ ३६॥

English
O great sages, Śiva too, drawn by the meditation of Atri, stationed himself there in full digits. He did not go to Kailāsa. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाशच्च तथा चात्र चत्वारि ऋषिसत्तमाः ।
वर्षाणि च गतान्यासन् वृष्टिर्नैवाभवत्तदा ॥ ३७॥

English
O excellent sages, fifty-four years had elapsed. There was no rain at all. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यावच्चाप्यत्रिणा ह्येवं तपसा ध्यानमाश्रितम् ।
अनसूया तदा नैव गृह्णामीतीषणा कृता ॥ ३८॥

एवं च क्रियमाणे हि मुनिना तपसि स्थिते ।
अनसूयासुभजने यज्जातं श्रूयतामिति ॥ ३९॥

English
As long as the sage was in meditation and penance, Anasūyā decided not to eat anything. Now listen to what happened when the sage was performing penance and Anasūyā continued her service. (38-39.)
