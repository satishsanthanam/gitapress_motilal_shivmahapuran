### **CHAPTER FOUR - (The greatness of Atriśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
कदाचित्स ऋषिश्रेष्ठो ह्यत्रिर्ब्रह्मविदां वरः ।
जागृतश्च जलं देहि प्रत्युवाच प्रियामिति ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Once that great sage Atri, the foremost among the knowers of Brahman woke up and spoke to his wife “Give me water.” (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सापि साध्वी त्ववश्यं च गृहीत्वाथ कमण्डलुम् ।
जगाम विपिने तत्र जलं मे नीयते कुतः ॥ २॥

किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि कुतो नीयेत वै जलम् ।
इति विस्मयमापन्ना तां गङ्गां हि ददर्श सा ॥ ३॥

English
Of course the chaste lady took the water pot and went into the forest. (But she began to muse) “Whence can I fetch water? What shall I do? Where shall I go? How can water be fetched?” Wondering thus she saw Gaṅgā. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तामनुव्रजती यावत् साब्रवीच्च तदा हि ताम् ।
गङ्गा सरिद्वरा देवी बिभ्रती सुन्दरां तनुम् ॥ ४॥

English
By the time she went over to her, Gaṅgā, the most excellent river, assumed a beautiful form and spoke to her. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
प्रसन्नास्मि च ते देवि कुत्र यासि वदाधुना ।
धन्या त्वं सुभगे सत्यं तवाज्ञां च करोम्यहम् ॥ ५॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  O gentle lady, I am delighted with you. Where are you going? Tell me now. O fortunate lady, you are blessed. I shall truly carry out your behest. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तद्वचश्च तदा श्रुत्वा ऋषिपत्नी तपस्विनी ।
प्रत्युवाच वचः प्रीत्या स्वयं सुचकिता द्विजाः ॥ ६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing her words the saintly woman, the wife of the sage, was greatly surprised O brahmins. She replied with great pleasure. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं वचस्तदा श्रुत्वा मुनिर्विस्मयमानसः ।
प्रियामुवाच सुप्रीत्या शङ्करं मनसा स्मरन् ॥ ७॥

English
Anasūyā said:—  O beautiful lady with eyes like lotus-petals, whence have you come? Please tell me the truth. A chaste lady speaks good words. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ते च तया तत्र मुनिपत्न्या मुनीश्वराः ।
सरिद्वरा दिव्यरूपा गङ्गा वाक्यमथाब्रवीत् ॥ ८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, when the wife of the sage spoke thus, the divine formed Gaṅgā, the foremost of the rivers, said. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
स्वामिनः सेवनं दृष्ट्वा शिवस्य च परात्मनः ।
साध्वि धर्मं च ते दृष्ट्वा स्थितास्मि तव सन्निधौ ॥ ९॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  O chaste lady, on seeing the service rendered by you unto your lord and to Siva the great soul and on taking view of your holy rites, I am staying near you. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं गङ्गा समायाता भजनात्ते शुचिस्मिते ।
वशीभूता ह्यहं जाता यदिच्छसि वृणीष्व तत् ॥ १०॥

English
O lady of pure smiles, I am Gaṅgā come here attracted by your worship. Choose whatever you wish. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ते गङ्गया साध्वी नमस्कृत्य पुरः स्थिता ।
उवाचेति जलं देहि चेत्प्रसन्ना ममाधुना ॥ ११॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing what Gaṅgā said, the chaste lady bowed to her and said—"If you are pleased madam, give me water now." (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येतद्वचनं श्रुत्वा गर्तं कुर्विति साब्रवीत् ।
शीघ्रं चायाच्च तत्कृत्वा स्थिता तत्क्षणमात्रतः ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing her words Gaṅgā said—"Dig a pit." She then made a pit in a trice, came and stood before her. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र सा च प्रविष्टा च जलरूपमभूत्तदा ।
आश्चर्यं परमं गत्वा गृहीतं च जलं तया ॥ १३॥

English
Gaṅgā entered the pit which became full of water In surprise, Anasūyā took the water. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच वचनं चैतल्लोकानां सुखहेतवे ।
अनसूया मुनेः पत्नी दिव्यरूपां सरिद्वराम् ॥ १४॥

English
Anasūyā the wife of the sage spoke for the welfare of the people to Gaṅgā, the excellent river of divine form. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अनसूयोवाच ।
यदि त्वं सुप्रसन्ना मे वर्तसे च कृपा मयि ।
स्थातव्यं च त्वया तावत् मत्स्वामी यावदाव्रजेत् ॥ १५॥

English
Anasūyā said:—  If you are delighted, if you have sympathy for me you will please stay here till my husband comes. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वानसूयाया वचनं सुखदं सताम् ।
गङ्गोवाच प्रसन्नाति ह्यत्रेर्दास्यसि मेऽनघे ॥ १६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the words of Anasūyā pleasing to the good, the delighted Gaṅgā said—"O sinless lady, you will give my water to Atri." (16.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते च तया तत्र ह्यनयापि कृतं तथा ।
स्वामिने तज्जलं दिव्यं दत्त्वा तत्पुरतः स्थिता ॥ १७॥

English
When Gaṅgā said thus, Anasūyā acted accordingly. She gave the divine water to her lord and stood before him. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स ऋषिश्चापि सुप्रीत्या स्वाचम्य विधिपूर्वकम् ।
पपौ दिव्यं जलं तच्च पीत्वा सुखमवाप ह ॥ १८॥

English
The sage performed the Ācamana duly with great pleasure and drank the divine water. On drinking it he derived great happiness. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो नित्यं जलं यच्च पीयते तज्जलं न हि ।
विचार्येति च तेनाशु परितश्चावलोकितम् ॥ १९॥

English
"O this is not the water usually drunk by me." After thinking thus he suddenly looked all round. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शुष्कान्वृक्षान्समालोक्य दिशो रूक्षतरास्तथा ।
उवाच तामृषिश्रेष्ठो न जातं वर्षणं पुनः ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing the trees dry and withered and the quarters rough and arid, the excellent sage spoke to her "No rain has fallen?" (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तदुक्तं तत् समाकर्ण्य नेति नेति प्रियां तदा ।
तामुवाच पुनः सोऽपि जलं नीतं कुतस्त्वया ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing what he said, the beloved lady replied in the negative. He then asked her whence she brought that water for him. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते तु तदा तेन विस्मयं परमं गता ।
अनसूया स्वमनसि सचिन्ता तु मुनीश्वराः ॥ २२॥

English
When he said this, O great sages, Anasūyā was much surprised. She then thought within herself. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
निवेद्यते मया चेद्वै तदोत्कर्षो भवेन्मम ।
निवेद्यते यदा नैव व्रतभङ्गो भवेन्मम ॥ २३॥

English
"If I tell him the truth it will be the exhibition of my greatness. If I do not tell him, it will be a violation of my rites. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
नोभयं च तथा स्याद्वै निवेद्यं तत्तथा मम ।
इति यावद्विचार्येत तावत्पृष्टा पुनः पुनः ॥ २४॥

English
I shall tell him in a manner to avoid the contingencies. Even as she was musing thus, she was asked again and again. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अथानुग्रहतः शम्भोः प्राप्तबुद्धिः पतिव्रता ।
उवाच श्रूयतां स्वामिन्यज्जातं कथयामि ते ॥ २५॥

English
Then, thanks to the blessings of Śiva, an intelligent idea struck her mind. "O lord, please hear me. I shall tell you what has happened." (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अनसूयोवाच ।
शङ्करस्य प्रतापाच्च तवैव सुकृतैस्तथा ।
गङ्गा समागतात्रैव तदीयं सलिलं त्विदम् ॥ २६॥

English
Anasūyā said:—  "Thanks to the power of Śiva and your merits, Gaṅgā has come here herself. This is her water, to be sure." (26.)

---

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing this, the sage was surprised in his mind. Remembering Śiva mentally he spoke to his beloved with great pleasure. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रिरुवाच ।
प्रिये सुन्दरि त्वं सत्यमथ वाचं व्यलीककाम् ।
ब्रवीषि च यथार्थं त्वं न मन्ये दुर्लभं त्विदम् ॥ २८॥

English
Atri said:—  O beloved, O beautiful lady, whether you speak truth or lie, I cannot believe it. This is something rare. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
असाध्यं योगिभिर्यच्च देवैरपि सदा शुभे ।
तच्चैवाद्य कथं जातं विस्मयः परमो मम ॥ २९॥

English
O auspicious lady, how did that happen which is impossible even to the yogins and the gods. I am extremely surprised. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्येवं दृश्यते चेद्वै तन्मन्येऽहं न चान्यथा ।
इति तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा प्रत्युवाच पतिं प्रिया ॥ ३०॥

English
If I see that myself I shall believe it, not otherwise. On hearing his words the beloved wife spoke to the husband :— (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अनसूयोवाच ।
आगम्यतां मया सार्धं त्वया नाथ महामुने ।
सरिद्वराया गङ्गाया द्रष्टुमिच्छा भवेद्यदि ॥ ३१॥

English
Anasūyā said:—  O lord, O great sage, please come with me if you wish to see Gaṅgā, the foremost among the rivers. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तु समादाय पतिं तं सा पतिव्रता ।
गता द्रुतं शिवं स्मृत्वा यत्र गङ्गा सरिद्वरा ॥ ३२॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this and remembering Śiva, the chaste lady took her husband to the place where Gaṅgā, the foremost among the rivers was present. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
दर्शयामास तां तत्र गङ्गां पत्ये पतिव्रता ।
गर्ते च संस्थितां तत्र स्वयं दिव्यस्वरूपिणीम् ॥ ३३॥

English
There the chaste lady showed to her husband Gaṅgā of divine form stationed within the pit. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा ऋषिश्रेष्ठो गर्तं च जलपूरितम् ।
आकण्ठं सुन्दरं दृष्ट्वा धन्येयमिति चाब्रवीत् ॥ ३४॥

English
Going there and seeing the beautiful pit full to the brim with water, the excellent sage said—“She is blessed.” (34.)

---

Sanskrit
किं मदीयं तपश्चैव किमन्येषां पुनस्तदा ।
इत्युक्त्वा मुनिशार्दूलो भक्त्या तुष्टाव तां तदा ॥ ३५॥

English
“Is this the fruit of my own penance or of somebody else?” After saying this the leading sage eulogised her with devotion. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हि स मुनिस्तत्र सुस्नातः सुभगे जले ।
आचम्य पुनरेवात्र स्तुतिं चक्रे पुनः पुनः ॥ ३६॥

English
Then the sage took a clean bath in that excellent water. After performing the Ācamana he eulogised her again and again. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अनसूयापि संस्नाता सुन्दरे तज्जले तदा ।
नित्यं चक्रे मुनिः कर्म सानसूयापि सुव्रता ॥ ३७॥

English
Anasūya too took her bath in that beautiful, clear water. The sage performed the routine rites. Anasūyā too, of good rites, performed likewise. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सोवाच तां गङ्गा गम्यते स्वस्थलं मया ।
इत्युक्ते च पुनः साध्वी तामुवाच सरिद्वराम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Then Gaṅgā told her “I am returning to my place.” When this was said, the chaste lady spoke to the leading river again. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अनसूयोवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्ना देवेशि यद्यस्ति च कृपा मयि ।
त्वया स्थेयं निश्चलत्वादस्मिन्देवि तपोवने ॥ ३९॥

English
Anasūyā said:—  O goddess if you are delighted, if you are kind to me, you will stay in this penance-grove for ever. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
महतां च स्वभावश्च नाङ्गीकृत्य परित्यजेत् ।
इत्युक्त्वा च करौ बद्ध्वा तां तुष्टाव पुनः पुनः ॥ ४०॥

English
This is the nature of the great that once they accept a request they will never forego it. After expressing her desire with joined palms, she eulogised her again and again. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिश्चापि तथोवाच त्वया स्थेयं सरिद्वरे ।
सानुकूला भव त्वं हि सनाथान्देवि नः कुरु ॥ ४१॥

English
The sage said likewise—“O leading river, you shall stay. O goddess, be favourable to us and bless us with your support.” (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तदीयं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा रम्यं गङ्गा सरिद्वरा ।
प्रसन्नमानसा गङ्गानसूयां वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ४२॥

English
On hearing his pleasing words, the leading river Gaṅgā was delighted in her mind and spoke to Anasūyā. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
शङ्करार्चनसम्भूतफलं वर्षस्य यच्छसि ।
स्वामिनश्च तदा स्थास्ये देवानामुपकारणात् ॥ ४३॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  I shall stay back for helping the gods if you pass on to me a year's merit accruing to you by worshipping lord Śiva. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा दानैर्न मे तुष्टिस्तीर्थस्नानैस्तथा च वै ।
यज्ञैस्तथाथवा योगैर्यथा पातिव्रतेन च ॥ ४४॥

English
I am not so much satisfied with charitable gifts, or ceremonial ablutions in the holy centres or sacrifices or the practice of Yogic rites as I am satisfied with chastity. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिव्रतां यथा दृष्ट्वा मनसः प्रीणनं भवेत् ।
तथा नान्यैरुपायैश्च सत्यं मे व्याहृतं सति ॥ ४५॥

English
O chaste lady, this is the truth I speak that the mind is not so pleased by any other means as it is pleased at the sight of a chaste lady. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिव्रतां स्त्रियं दृष्ट्वा पापनाशो भवेन्मम ।
शुद्धा जाता विशेषेण गौरीतुल्या पतिव्रता ॥ ४६॥

English
On seeing a chaste lady, all my sins have perished. I have become purified and like Pārvatī have become chaste. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्च यदि लोकस्य हिताय तत्प्रयच्छसि ।
तर्ह्यहं स्थिरतां यास्ये यदि कल्याणमिच्छसि ॥ ४७॥

English
If you seek for welfare, you give up that merit for the benefit of the world, then I shall become steady and stable here. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वानसूया सा पतिव्रता ।
गङ्गायै प्रददौ पुण्यं सर्वं तद्वर्षसम्भवम् ॥ ४८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words, the chaste Anasūyā passed on a year's merit to Gaṅgā. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
महतां च स्वभावो हि परेषां हितमावहेत् ।
सुवर्णं चन्दनं चेक्षुरसस्तत्र निदर्शनम् ॥ ४९॥

English
It is the nature of the great that they do what is beneficial to others. The instance of gold-sandal and sugarcane juice may be cited. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद् दृष्ट्वानसूयं तत्कर्म पातिव्रतं महत् ।
प्रसन्नोऽभून्महादेवः पार्थिवादाविराशु वै ॥ ५०॥

English
On seeing this performance of Anasūya, a chaste woman, Lord Śiva was delighted and appeared out of his earthen phallic-form. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरुवाच ।
दृष्ट्वा ते कर्म साध्व्येतत् प्रसन्नोऽस्मि पतिव्रते ।
वरं ब्रूहि प्रिये मत्तो यतः प्रियतरासि मे ॥ ५१॥

English
Śiva said:—  O chaste lady, on seeing this activity of yours I am delighted. Mention the boon you wish to choose, since I am very fond of you. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

पञ्चवक्त्रादिसंयुक्तं हरं प्रेक्ष्य सुविस्मितौ ॥ ५२॥

English
Then the couple glanced at the wonderfully beautiful form of Siva. On seeing five-faced Siva they were much surprised. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
नत्वा स्तुत्वा करौ बद्ध्वा महाभक्तिसमन्वितौ ।
अवोचेतां समभ्यर्च्य शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Bowing to and eulogising Siva, the benefactor of the worlds, the couple endowed with great devotion spoke to him after worshipping him. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
दम्पती ऊचतुः ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश प्रसन्ना जगदम्बिका ।
अस्मिंस्तपोवने तिष्ठ लोकानां सुखदो भव ॥ ५४॥

English
The couple said:—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if the mother of the universe is also delighted, please stay in this penance-grove and be the bestower of happiness to the worlds. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्ना च तदा गङ्गा प्रसन्नश्च शिवस्तदा ।
उभौ तौ च स्थितौ तत्र यत्रासीदृषिसत्तमः ॥ ५५॥

English
Then the river Gangā was delighted. Śiva too was pleased. They stayed at the hermitage of the excellent sage. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रीश्वरश्च नाम्नासीदीश्वरः परदुःखहा ।
गङ्गा सापि स्थिता तत्र तदा गर्तेऽथ मायया ॥ ५६॥

English
Lord Siva, the destroyer of miseries was known as Atriśvara. Gangā too stayed in the pit by her magical power. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिनं हि समारभ्य तत्राक्षय्यजलं सदा ।
हस्तमात्रे हि तद्गर्ते गङ्गा मन्दाकिनी ह्यभूत् ॥ ५७॥

English
Thence onwards there is a perennial supply of water in that pit deep only by a hand's length. Gangā flows gently there. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रैव ऋषयो दिव्याः समाजग्मुः सहाङ्गनाः ।
तीर्थात्तीर्थाच्च ते सर्वे ये पुरा निर्गता द्विजाः ॥ ५८॥

English
Divine sages came there with their womenfolk and the sages who had formerly gone out of the various holy centres returned. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
यवाश्च व्रीहयश्चैव यज्ञयागपरायणाः ।
युक्ता ऋषिवरैस्तैश्च होमं चक्रुश्च ते जनाः ॥ ५९॥

English
Barleys and food-grains were plenty. People interested in sacrifices performed these accompanied by the sages. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मभिस्तैश्च सन्तुष्टा वृष्टिं चक्रुर्घनास्तदा ।
आनन्दः परमो लोके बभूवाति मुनीश्वराः ॥ ६०॥

English
O great sages, delighted with these rites, clouds showered in plenty. There was great bliss in the world. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रीश्वरस्य माहात्म्यमित्युक्तं वः सुखावहम् ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं सर्वकामदं भक्तिवर्धनम् ॥ ६१॥

English
Thus the greatness of Atriśvara has been explained to you. It is pleasing and conducive to worldly pleasures and salvation. It yields all desires and enhances devotion. (61.)
