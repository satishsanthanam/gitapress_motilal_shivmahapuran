### **CHAPTER FIVE - (The death of the Brahmin lady and the greatness of Nandikeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
नन्दीकेशं च यश्चैव पूजयेत्परया मुदा ।
नित्यं तस्याखिला सिद्धिर्भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said :—  Nilakantha, the great god, stands in the phallic form on the divine mountain, Kālañjara.[128] He is the bestower of bliss to the devotees always (1.)

---

Sanskrit
महिमा तस्य दिव्योऽस्ति श्रुतिस्मृतिप्रकीर्तितः ।
तीर्थं तदाख्यया तत्र स्नानात्पातकनाशकृत् ॥ २॥

English
His greatness is divine as glorified in Srutis and Smrtis. The holy centre there is of that name. By taking ceremonial ablution there, sins are quelled. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याश्चैव तु यत्कृत्यं तत्सर्वं संविधाय सः ।
मासिकं कर्म कृत्वा तु गमनाय प्रचक्रमे ॥ ३॥

English
O great ones of good rites, there is no limit to the phallic forms of Siva on the bank of Reva[129] that bestow all sorts of happiness. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सा च रुद्रस्वरूपा हि दर्शनात्पापहारिका ।
तस्यां स्थिताश्च ये केचित्पाषाणाः शिवरूपिणः ॥ ४॥

English
The river itself is in the form of Rudra that quells sins by mere sight. Any stone or pebble within it is in the form of Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वोक्तं तया तत्र न्यूनं मे विद्यते बहु ।
तदेव क्रियते चेद्वै सुखेन मरणं भवेत् ॥ ५॥

English
O great sages, still I shall enumerate and explain the important phallic forms that yield worldly pleasures here and salvation hereafter. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्येष्ठपुत्रश्च यस्तस्यास्तेनोक्तं कथ्यतां त्वया ।
करिष्यामि तदेतद्धि तया च कथितं तदा ॥ ६॥

English
One with the good name of Arteśvara is the dispeller of sins. What is famous as Parameśvara and what is remembered as Simheśvara (are good shrines). (6.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजपत्न्युवाच ।
श‍ृणु पुत्र वचः प्रीत्या पुरासीन्मे मनःस्पृहा ।
काश्यां गन्तुं तथा नासीदिदानीं म्रियते पुनः ॥ ७॥

English
Sarmeśa, Kumāreśvara, Puṇḍarikeśvara and Maṇḍapeśvara are all well known. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ममास्थीनि त्वया पुत्र क्षेपणीयान्यतन्द्रितम् ।
गङ्गाजले शुभं तेऽद्य भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ ८॥

English
Tiksneśa is there which quells sins at the very sight. What is called Dhundhureśvara is on the banks of the Narmadā river. It quells sins. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शूलेश्वर इति ख्यातस्तथा कुम्भेश्वरः स्मृतः ।
कुबेरेश्वरनामापि तथा सोमेश्वरः स्मृतः ॥ ९॥

English
Suleśvara, Kumbheśvara, Kubereśvara and Someśvara are very famous. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नीलकण्ठो मङ्गलेशो मङ्गलायतनो महान् ।
महाकपीश्वरो देवः स्थापितो हि हनूमता ॥ १०॥

English
Maṅgaleśa is blue-necked and is a great bestower of auspiciousness. Lord Mahākapīśvara, was installed by Hanūmat. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च नन्दिको देवो हत्याकोटिनिवारकः ।
सर्वकामार्थदश्चैव मोक्षदो हि प्रकीर्तितः ॥ ११॥

English
Lord Nandika prevents the sins of a crore of murders. He is glorified as the bestower of the fruits of desire and salvation too. (11.)

---

English
If one worships Nandikeśa with great devotion one will certainly achieve all Siddhis. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तीरे च यः स्नाति रेवायां मुनिसत्तमाः ।
तस्य कामाश्च सिध्यन्ति सर्वं पापं विनश्यति ॥ १३॥

English
O excellent sages, he who takes bath in the Revā or on its banks, realises all desires and gets rid of sins. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
एवं तस्य च माहात्म्यं कथं तत्र महामते ।
नन्दिकेशस्य कृपया कथ्यतां च त्वयाधुना ॥ १४॥

English
The sages said:—  O highly intelligent one, how is the greatness of Nandikeśa there. Let it be kindly mentioned by you now. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सम्यक् पृष्टं भवद्भिश्च कथयामि यथाश्रुतम् ।
शौनकाद्याश्च मुनयः सर्वे हि श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ १५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O sages, Saunaka and others, you have well put the question. I shall narrate in the manner I have heard. Listen respectfully. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा युधिष्ठिरेणैवं पृष्टश्च ऋषिसत्तमः ।
यथोवाच तथा वच्मि भवत्स्नेहानुसारतः ॥ १६॥

English
Formerly the excellent sage was asked by Yudhiṣṭhira. Out of affection for you, I shall tell you as he had mentioned then. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
रेवायाः पश्चिमे तीरे कर्णिकी नाम वै पुरी ।
विराजते सुशोभाढ्या चतुर्वर्णसमाकुला ॥ १७॥

English
The city named Karṇiki$^{130}$ of great splendour shines on the western bank of the Revā. People of four castes reside there in plenty. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र द्विजवरः कश्चिदुतथ्यकुलसम्भवः ।
काश्यां गतश्च पुत्राभ्यामर्पयित्वा स्वपत्निकाम् ॥ १८॥

English
A certain noble brahmin there, born of the family of Uta, entrusted his wife to the care of his two sons and went to Kasi. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रैव स मृतो विप्रः पुत्राभ्यां च श्रुतं तदा ।
तदीयं चैव तत्कृत्यं चक्राते पुत्रकावुभौ ॥ १९॥

English
The brahmin died there itself. On getting the news the two sons performed the obsequies. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्नी च पालयामास पुत्रौ पुत्रहितैषिणी ।
किञ्चिच्च वर्जयित्वा च विभक्तं वै धनं तया ॥ २०॥

English
The wife looked after the sons, seeking their welfare. After bringing up the sons she divided the assets. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वीयं च रक्षितं किञ्चिद्धनं मरणहेतवे ।
ततश्च द्विजपत्नी हि कियत्काले गते च सा ॥ २१॥

कदाचिन्म्रियमाणा सा विविधं पुण्यमाचरत् ।
न मृता दैवयोगेन द्विजपत्नी च सा द्विजाः ॥ २२॥

English
She reserved some amount for her funeral expenses. After sometime when she was going to die, she made pious gifts of various sorts. O brahmins, providentially she did not die. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा प्राणान्न मुमुचे माता दैवात्तयोश्च सा ।
तद् द्रुष्टवा जननीकष्टं पुत्रकावूचतुस्तदा ॥ २३॥

English
When she did not die providentially, the son observing her ailments said. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रावूचतुः ।
किं न्यूनं विद्यते मातः कष्टं यद्विद्यते महत् ।
व्रियतां तद् द्रुतं प्रीत्या तदावां करवावहे ॥ २४॥

English
The sons said:—  O mother, what is amiss that you suffer so much. Please tell us so that we shall carry it out with pleasure. (24.)

---

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words she said—"There is much that is amiss. If you do the same I shall die happily." (25.)

---

English
The elder son replied—"Please do mention. I shall do the same." She then replied. (26.)

---

English
The brahmin lady said:—  O son, hear with attention. I had a great desire to go to Kasi. That was not to be. Now I am dying here. (27.)

---

English
Dear son, my bones shall be cast off into the waters of the Gangā assiduously. That pious act will bless you, to be sure. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ते च तया मात्रा स ज्येष्ठतनयोऽब्रवीत् ।
मातरं मातृभक्त्तस्तु सुव्रतां मरणोन्मुखीम् ॥ २९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* When she of good rites and on the verge of death, said thus, the affectionate elder son replied to her. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्र उवाच ।
मातस्त्वया सुखेनैव प्राणास्त्याज्या न संशयः ।
तव कार्यं पुरा कृत्वा पश्चात्कार्यं मदीयकम् ॥ ३०॥

English
The son said:—  “O mother, you shall die peacefully and with confidence. I shall carry out your task first and then attend to mine.” (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इति हस्ते जलं दत्त्वा यावत्पुत्रो गृहं गतः ।
तावत्सा च मृता तत्र हरस्मरणतत्परा ॥ ३१॥

English
After saying this, he handed water to her and went to his house. Remembering Śiva, she died in the meantime. (31.)

---

English
After performing her obsequies and the monthly Śrāddha he started on his journey. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वयोः श्रेष्ठतरो यो वै सुवादो नाम विश्रुतः ।
तदस्थीनि समादाय निःसृतस्तीर्थकाम्यया ॥ ३३॥

English
The elder son who was famous as Suvāda took her bones and started on his pilgrimage. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्गृह्य सेवकं कञ्चित्तेनैव सहितस्तदा ।
आश्वास्य भार्यां पुत्राँश्च मातुः प्रियचिकीर्षया ॥ ३४॥

श्राद्धदानादिकं भोज्यं कृत्वा विधिमनुत्तमम् ।
मङ्गलस्मरणं कृत्वा निर्जगाम गृहाद् द्विजः ॥ ३५॥

English
He engaged a servant to accompany him. He consoled his wife and sons. He performed Śrāddha, Dāna and other pious rites. He then set out from his house after Maṅgala Smaraṇa accompanied by the servant. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिने योजनं गत्वा विंशति ग्रामके शुभे ।
उवासास्तं गते भानौ गृहे विप्रस्य कस्यचित् ॥ ३६॥

English
He walked about twelve kilometres that day and when the sun set, he halted at the house of a certain Brahmin in the auspicious village of Viṁśatigrāma.¹³¹ (36.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रे सन्ध्यादि सत्कर्म स द्विजो विधिपूर्वकम् ।
स्तवादि कृतवांस्तत्र शम्भोरद्भुतकर्मणः ॥ ३७॥

English
He performed Sandhyā and other rites duly. He recited the hymns of prayer to Śiva of wonderful activities. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सेवकेन तदा युक्तो ब्राह्मणः संस्थितस्तदा ।
यामिनी च गता तत्र मुहूर्तद्वयसम्मिता ॥ ३८॥

एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्रैकमाश्चर्यमभूत्तदा ।
श‍ृणुतादरतस्तच्च मुनयो वो वदाम्यहम् ॥ ३९॥

English
The brahmin stayed there accompanied by the servant. The night had passed by two muhūrtas when something surprising happened. O sages, listen attentively. I shall tell you everything. (38-39.)
