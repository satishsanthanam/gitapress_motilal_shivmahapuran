### **CHAPTER SEVEN - (The greatness of Nandikeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
कथं गङ्गा समायाता वैशाखे सप्तमीदिने ।
नर्मदायां विशेषेण सूतैतद्वर्णय प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O holy lord, Sūta, narrate in detail how Gaṅgā came to Narmadā on the seventh day in Vaiśākha. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वरश्च कथं जातो नन्दिकेशो हि नामतः ।
वृत्तं तदपि सुप्रीत्या कथय त्वं महामते ॥ २॥

English
How did the lord come to be known by the name Nandikeśa? O intelligent one, narrate that too with pleasure. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टमृषिश्रेष्ठा नन्दिकेशाश्रितं वचः ।
तदहं कथयाम्यद्य श्रवणात्पुण्यवर्धनम् ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said :—  O excellent sages, you have put a very good question concerning Nandikeśa. I shall narrate it as it enhances merit. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणी ऋषिका नाम्नी कस्यचिच्च द्विजन्मनः ।
सुता विवाहिता कस्मैचिद् द्विजाय विधानतः ॥ ४॥

English
A brahmin lady Rsika, the daughter of a certain brahmin, was duly married to a certain brahmin youth. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वकर्मप्रभावेण पत्नी सा हि द्विजन्मनः ।
सुव्रतापि च विप्रेन्द्रा बालवैधव्यमागता ॥ ५॥

English
O leading brahmins, although she observed holy rites, the brahmin lady became a widow at an early age as a result of previous actions. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा द्विजपत्नी हि ब्रह्मचर्यव्रतान्विता ।
पार्थिवार्चनपूर्वं हि तपस्तेपे सुदारुणम् ॥ ६॥

English
Then the brahmin widow, observing the rite of celibacy performed a severe penance by worshipping Siva's earthen phallic image. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे दुष्टो मूढनामासुरो बली ।
ययौ तत्र महामायी कामबाणेन ताडितः ॥ ७॥

English
In the meantime, a wicked and very powerful Asura named Mūdha came there. He, the wielder of great Māyā, was struck by the arrows of the cupid. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तपन्तीं तां समालोक्य सुन्दरीमतिकामिनीम् ।
तया भोगं ययाचे स नानालोभं प्रदर्शयन् ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing that charming beautiful lady performing penance, he offered her many temptations and requested for sexual pleasure with her. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा सुव्रता नारी शिवध्यानपरायणा ।
तस्मिन्दृष्टिं दधौ नैव कामदृष्ट्या मुनीश्वराः ॥ ९॥

English
O great sages, the lady observing holy rites and engrossed in Siva's meditation did not cast a loving glance at him. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
न मानितवती तं च ब्राह्मणी सा तपोरता ।
अतीव हि तपोनिष्ठासीच्छिवध्यानमाश्रिता ॥ १०॥

English
She did not honour him. Engaged in penance she remained meditating upon Siva and strictly continued her penance. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ मूढः स दैत्येन्द्रः तया तन्व्या तिरस्कृतः ।
चुक्रोध विकटं तस्यै पश्चाद्रूपमदर्शयत् ॥ ११॥

English
That leading Daitya, Mūdha, when slighted by that lady, became angry and showed his hideous form. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रोवाच दुष्टात्मा दुर्वचो भयकारकम् ।
त्रासयामास बहुशस्तां च पत्नीं द्विजन्मनः ॥ १२॥

English
The wicked-minded demon spoke harsh words terrifying her. He threatened the brahmin lady in many ways. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सा भयसन्त्रस्ता बहुवारं शिवेति च ।
बभाषे स्नेहतस्तन्वी द्विजपत्नी शिवाश्रया ॥ १३॥

English
Then the frightened brahmin lady, lovingly resorting to Siva, repeated the name Siva many times. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
विह्वलातीव सा नारी शिवनामप्रभाषिणी ।
जगाम शरणं शम्भोः स्वधर्मावनहेतवे ॥ १४॥

English
The agitated lady repeated the names of Siva, sought refuge in him for the protection of her sacred virtue. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शरणागतरक्षार्थं कर्तुं सद्वृत्तमाहितम् ।
आनन्दार्थं हि तस्यास्तु शिव आविर्बभूव ह ॥ १५॥

English
To protect one who sought refuge in him and to uphold good conduct, Siva manifested himself, as well as to delight her. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तं मूढनामानं दैत्येन्द्रं कामविह्वलम् ।
चकार भस्मसात्सद्यः शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १६॥

English
Siva, favourably disposed towards his devotees, immediately reduced to ashes that lusty leading demon named Mūdha. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च परमेशानो कृपादृष्ट्या विलोक्य ताम् ।
वरं ब्रूहीति चोवाच भक्तरक्षणदक्षधीः ॥ १७॥

English
Looking at her with a compassionate look, lord Siva efficient in and mindful of protecting the devotees told her, "Mention the boon you wish to have". (17.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा महेशवचनं सा साध्वी द्विजकामिनी ।
ददर्श शाङ्करं रूपमानन्दजनकं शुभम् ॥ १८॥

English
On hearing the words of lord Siva that chaste lady the wife of the brahmin, saw the auspicious and delightful form of Siva. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रणम्य तं शम्भुं परमेशं सुखावहम् ।
तुष्टाव साञ्जलिः साध्वी नतस्कन्धा शुभाशया ॥ १९॥

English
Then bowing to Siva the beautiful supreme lord, the chaste lady of pure mind eulogised him with palms joined together and shoulders drooping down. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिकोवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
दीनबन्धुस्त्वमीशानो भक्तरक्षाकरः सदा ॥ २०॥

English
Rṣikā said :— O great God, lord of the gods, favourably disposed towards those who seek refuge, you are the kinsman of the distressed. You are lord Siva, protecting your devotees always. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वया मे रक्षितो धर्मो मूढनाम्नोऽसुरादिह ।
यदयं निहतो दुष्टो जगद्रक्षा कृता त्वया ॥ २१॥

English
My virtue has been saved by you from the Asura Mūdha. By killing that wicked person the whole universe has been saved by you. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपादयोः परां भक्तिं देहि मे ह्यनपायिनीम् ।
अयमेव वरो नाथ किमन्यदधिकं ह्यतः ॥ २२॥

English
O lord, grant me the greatest devotion to your feet which will never decay. This alone is the boon I ask of you. Nothing more than this. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यदाकर्णय विभो प्रार्थनां मे महेश्वर ।
लोकानामुपकारार्थमिह त्वं संस्थितो भव ॥ २३॥

English
O great lord, please listen to another request of mine. Please stay here permanently for the benefit of the people. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा महादेवमृषिका सा शुभव्रता ।
तूष्णीमासाथ गिरिशः प्रोवाच करुणाकरः ॥ २४॥

English
Sūta said :—  After eulogising lord Śiva thus, Rṣikā of auspicious rites remained silent. Then the merciful lord Śiva spoke. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिश उवाच ।
ऋषिके सुचरित्रा त्वं मम भक्ता विशेषतः ।
दत्ता वराश्च ते सर्वे तुभ्यं ये ये हि याचिताः ॥ २५॥

English
Lord Śiva said:—  O Rṣikā, you are well-behaved. You are a special devotee of mine. Whatever boons you have asked for have been granted to you. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र हरिब्रह्मादयः सुराः ।
शिवाविर्भावमाज्ञाय ययुर्हर्षसमन्विताः ॥ २६॥

English
In the meantime, coming to know of the manifestation of Śiva, Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods came there with great delight. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवं प्रणम्य सुप्रीत्या समानर्चुश्च तेऽखिलाः ।
तुष्टुवुर्नतका विप्राः करौ बद्ध्वा सुचेतसः ॥ २७॥

English
O brahmins, bowing down with great pleasure to Śiva they all worshipped him. Joining their palms and bending down they eulogised him attentively. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्समये गङ्गा साध्वीं तां स्वर्धुनी जगौ ।
ऋषिकां सुप्रसन्नात्मा प्रशंसन्ती च तद्विधिम् ॥ २८॥

English
In the meantime, Gaṅgā the celestial river, with delighted mind, praised her rites and spoke to that chaste lady Ṛṣikā. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
ममार्थं चैव वैशाखे मासि देयं त्वया वचः ।
स्थित्यर्थं दिनमेकं मे सामीप्यं कार्यमेव हि ॥ २९॥

English
Gaṅgā said :—  For my sake you should make a verbal promise You should stay with me for a day in the month of Vaiśākha. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
गङ्गावचनमाकर्ण्य सा साध्वी प्राह सुव्रता ।
तथास्त्विति वचः प्रीत्या लोकानां हितहेतवे ॥ ३०॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing the words of Gaṅgā, the chaste lady of good rites consented with pleasure for the benefit of the world. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
आनन्दार्थं शिवस्तस्याः सुप्रसन्नश्च पार्थिवे ।
तस्मिँल्लिङ्गे लयं यातः पूर्णांशेन तया हरः ॥ ३१॥

English
For her bliss, the delighted Śiva, vanished into that earthen phallic image along with her. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाः सर्वे सुप्रसन्नाः प्रशंसन्ति शिवं च ताम् ।
स्वं स्वं धाम ययुर्विष्णुर्ब्रह्माद्या अपि स्वर्णदी ॥ ३२॥

English
Praising Śiva and the brahmin lady, the delighted Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other gods and the celestial river Gaṅgā returned to their abodes. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिनात्पावनं तीर्थमासीदीदृशमुत्तमम् ।
नन्दिकेशः शिवः ख्यातः सर्वपापविनाशनः ॥ ३३॥

English
From that day onwards this became a holy and excellent centre. Śiva became famous as Nandikeśa, the destroyer of all sins. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गापि प्रतिवर्षं तद्दिने याति शुभेच्छया ।
क्षालनार्थं स्वपापस्य यद् गृहीतं नृणां द्विजाः ॥ ३४॥

English
O brahmin, in order to wipe off her sins which she takes from others, Gaṅgā comes here on that day every year out of her own good will. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र स्नातो नरः सम्यङ् नन्दिकेशं समर्च्य च ।
ब्रह्महत्यादिभिः पापैर्मुच्यते ह्यखिलैरपि ॥ ३५॥

English
The man who takes bath there and worships Nandikeśa with devotion becomes freed from all sins such as the slaughter of a Brahmin etc. (35.)

---
