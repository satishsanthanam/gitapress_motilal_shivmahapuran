### **CHAPTER NINE - *(The attainment of good goal by the outcaste woman)***

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं शैवसत्तमः ।
चाण्डाली का समाख्याता तत्कथां कथय प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
*The sages said :*  O Sūta, O fortunate Sūta, you are blessed, you are the most excellent among the devotees of Śiva. O holy lord, please narrate the story of the outcaste woman you just referred to. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
द्विजाः श‍ृणुत सद्भक्त्या तां कथां परमाद्भुताम् ।
शिवप्रभावसम्मिश्रां श‍ृण्वतां भक्तिवर्धिनीम् ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said :—* O brahmins, listen with great devotion to that wonderful story illustrative of Śiva’s power. It enhances the devotional feelings of the listeners. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
चाण्डाली सा पूर्वभवेऽभवद् ब्राह्मणकन्यका ।
सौमिनी नाम चन्द्रास्या सर्वलक्षणसंयुता ॥ ३॥

English
The outcaste woman was a brahmin girl in her previous birth. She was named Saumini. She was endowed with all characteristic signs. Her face was bright like the moon. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा समये कन्या युवतिः सौमिनी द्विजाः ।
पित्रा दत्ता च कस्मैचिद्विधिना द्विजसूनवे ॥ ४॥

English
O brahmins, that youthful maiden Saumini was married to a certain brahmin boy duly by her father. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सा भर्तारमनुप्राप्य किञ्चित्कालं शुभव्रता ।
रेमे तेन द्विजश्रेष्ठा नवयौवनशालिनी ॥ ५॥

English
After getting a husband, O great brahmins, she in her fresh blooming youth indulged in sexual dalliance with him and maintained her auspicious rites. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्याः पतिर्विप्रस्तरुणः सुरुजार्दितः ।
सौमिन्याः कालयोगात्तु पञ्चत्वमगमद् द्विजाः ॥ ६॥

English
O brahmins, then her youthful husband, the brahmin boy became distressed with a great ailment. Due to the misfortune of Saumini he died. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मृते भर्तरि सा नारी दुःखितातिविषण्णधीः ।
किञ्चित्कालं शुभाचारा सुशीलोवास सद्मनि ॥ ७॥

English
When the husband passed away, the woman was much distressed, dejected and despondent. For some time she maintained her pure conduct. She remained in the house, a well-behaved lady. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सा मन्मथाविष्टहृदया विधवापि च ।
युवावस्थाविशेषेण बभूव व्यभिचारिणी ॥ ८॥

English
Thereafter, though a widow, her heart became defiled by lustful feelings because she was still in her prime of youth. She then transgressed the limits of decency. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तज्ज्ञात्वा गोत्रिणस्तस्या दुष्कर्म कुलदूषणम् ।
समेतास्तत्यजुर्दूरं नीत्वा तां सकचग्रहाम् ॥ ९॥

English
Coming to know of her misdeeds defiling the family her kinsmen gathered together, caught hold of her hair, took her far off and abandoned her. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्चिच्छूद्रवरस्तां वै विचरन्तीं निजेच्छया ।
दृष्ट्वा वने स्त्रियं चक्रे निनाय स्वगृहं ततः ॥ १०॥

English
A leading Sūdra saw her roaming about in the forest, took her to his house and made her his wife. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा पिशिताहारा नित्यमापीतवारुणी ।
अजीजनत्सुतां तेन शूद्रेण सुरतप्रिया ॥ ११॥

English
She then became very fond of sexual intercourse, began to take in meat and indulged in drinking wine. She bore that Sūdra a daughter too. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिद्भर्तरि क्वापि याते पीतसुराथ सा ।
इयेष पिशिताहारं सौमिनी व्यभिचारिणी ॥ १२॥

English
Once while the husband had gone somewhere, Saumini the erring woman drank wine and badly wanted meat. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो मेषेषु बद्धेषु गोभिः सह बहिर्व्रजे ।
निशामुखे तमोऽन्धे हि खड्गमादाय सा ययौ ॥ १३॥

English
In her cattleshed outside, goats and sheep had been tethered along with cows. It was dusk when nothing could be visible due to darkness. She then took a sword and went out. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अविमृश्य मदावेशान्मेषबुद्ध्यामिषप्रिया ।
एकं जघान गोवत्सं क्रोशन्तमतिदुर्भगा ॥ १४॥

English
Inebriated that she was, the foul woman fond of flesh diet killed a calf taking it to be a goat without much consideration. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
हतं तं गृहमानीय ज्ञात्वा गोवत्समङ्गना ।
भीता शिव शिवेत्याह केनचित्पुण्यकर्मणा ॥ १५॥

English
After taking it within she realised that it was a calf. The terrified woman blurted out "Siva, Siva", thanks to some previous merit. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सा मुहूर्तं शिवं ध्यात्वामिषभोजनलालसा ।
छित्त्वा तमेव गोवत्सं चकाराहारमीप्सितम् ॥ १६॥

English
She meditated on Siva for a short while. But the desire for meat overwhelmed her. She minced the meat of the calf and ate it up with great relish. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं बहुतिथे काले गते सा सौमिनी द्विजाः ।
कालस्य वशमापन्ना जगाम यमसङ्क्षयम् ॥ १७॥

English
O brahmins, when a long time elapsed thus, Saumini died and went to Yama's abode. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यमोऽपि धर्ममालोक्य तस्याः कर्म च पौर्विकम् ।
निवर्त्य निरयावासाच्चक्रे चाण्डालजातिकाम् ॥ १८॥

English
Yama went through her antecedents and taking into consideration her merits and demerits he sent her back from hell to be born in an outcaste family. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
साथ भ्रष्टा यमपुराच्चाण्डालीगर्भमाश्रिता ।
ततो बभूव जन्मान्धा प्रशान्ताङ्गारमेचका ॥ १९॥

English
Fallen from Yama's city she was born as a congenitally blind girl out of the womb of a Cāṇḍāla woman. Her complexion was as dark as the dying embers. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
जन्मान्धा साथ बाल्येऽपि विध्वस्तपितृमातृका ।
ऊढा न केनचिद्दुष्टा महाकुष्ठरुजार्दिता ॥ २०॥

English
She was born blind. Even during her infancy her father and mother died. She was not married to anyone. She became infected with leprosy and was utterly defiled. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्षुधार्दिता दीना यष्टिपाणिर्गतेक्षणा ।
चाण्डालोच्छिष्टपिण्डेन जठराग्निमतर्पयत् ॥ २१॥

English
Emaciated and distressed with hunger, blind and moving about with a staff in her hand, she somehow subdued her gastric fire by the leavings of food of the Cāṇḍalas nearby. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृच्छ्रेण महता नीत्वा स्वविपुलं वयः ।
जरया ग्रस्तसर्वाङ्गी दुःखमाप दुरत्ययम् ॥ २२॥

English
With great difficulty and sufferings she passed a major portion of her life. When old age affected her limbs she was subjected to unending grief and distress. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्साथ चाण्डाली गोकर्णं तं महाजनान् ।
आयास्यन्त्यां शिवतिथौ गच्छतो बुबुधेऽध्वगान् ॥ २३॥

English
Once she came to know of travellers going to visit Mahābala and Gokarna on the Śiva festival day. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अथासावपि चाण्डाली वसनाशनतृष्णया ।
महाजनान् याचयितुं सञ्चचार शनैः शनैः ॥ २४॥

English
Then prompted by a desire for clothes and meals she walked slowly to beg of the general public. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्राथ चाण्डाली प्रार्थयन्ती महाजनान् ।
यत्र तत्र चचारासौ दीनवाक्प्रसृताञ्जलिः ॥ २५॥

English
She moved about here and there begging of the people imploring them with piteous words and outstretched hands after arriving at that place. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमभ्यर्थयन्त्यास्तु चाण्डाल्याः प्रसृताञ्जलौ ।
एकः पुण्यतमः पान्थः प्राक्षिपद्विल्वमञ्जरीम् ॥ २६॥

English
In the outstretched hands of the suppliant woman, a meritorious traveller hurled a bunch of Bilva leaves. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तामञ्जलौ निपतितां सा विमृश्य पुनः पुनः ।
अभक्ष्यमिति मत्वाथ दूरे प्राक्षिपदातुरा ॥ २७॥

English
When that bunch fell into her hands she felt it again and again and realising that it was not edible she cast it off dejectedly. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याः कराद्विनिर्मुक्ता रात्रौ सा बिल्वमञ्जरी ।
पपात कस्यचिद्दिष्ट्या शिवलिङ्गस्य मस्तके ॥ २८॥

English
That bunch of Bilva leaves cast off from her hands during the night, fortunately fell on the top of a Siva's phallic image. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सैवं शिवचतुर्दश्यां रात्रौ पान्थजनान् मुहुः ।
याचमानापि यत्किञ्चिन्न लेभे दैवयोगतः ॥ २९॥

English
Thus on the Siva Caturda'si night though she begged of the travellers again and again she did not get anything through the intercession of fate. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शिवचतुर्दश्या व्रतं जातं च निर्मलम् ।
अज्ञानतो जागरणं परमानन्ददायकम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Thus, unwittingly she observed the rites of Sivacaturda'si and kept awake during the night and obtained great bliss. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रभाते सा नारी शोकेन महतावृता ।
शनैर्निववृते दीना स्वदेशायैव केवलम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Then in the next morning, overcome with great grief the dejected woman returned to her native place alone slowly. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रान्ता चिरोपवासेन निपतन्ती पदे पदे ।
अतीत्य तावतीं भूमिं निपपात विचेतना ॥ ३२॥

English
Exhausted by long starvation, and faltering and staggering at every step she somehow covered the long distance but fell down unconscious. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सा शम्भुकृपया जगाम परमं पदम् ।
आरुह्य सुविमानं च नीतं शिवगणैर्द्रुतम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Seated in an aerial chariot brought immediately by Siva's Ganas, thanks to Siva's mercy, she went to Siva's region. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
आदौ यदेषा शिवनाम नारी
प्रमादतो वाप्यसती जगाद ।
तेनेह भूयः सुकृतेन विप्रा
महाबलस्थानमवाप दिव्यम् ॥ ३४॥

English
O brahmins, since at the outset the unchaste woman had cried out Śiva's name unwittingly, she acquired a great merit whereby she reached the divine abode of Mahābala. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीगोकर्णे शिवतिथावुपोष्य शिवमस्तके ।
कृत्वा जागरणं सा हि चक्रे बिल्वार्चनं निशि ॥ ३५॥

English
At Gokarna on Siva's Tithi during the night she had kept awake and observed fast. She had performed the worship of Siva's image with Bilva bunch. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अकामतः कृतस्यास्य पुण्यस्यैव च तत्फलम् ।
भुनक्त्यद्यापि सा चैव महाबलप्रसादतः ॥ ३६॥

English
That was the fruit of a meritorious action performed unconsciously. She was liberated thanks to the grace of Mahābala. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एवंविधं महालिङ्गं शङ्करस्य महाबलम् ।
सर्वपापहरं सद्यः परमानन्ददायकम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Thus is the great image of Mahābala, destructive of sins and the bestower of the highest bliss instantaneously. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वः कथितं विप्रा माहात्म्यं परमं मया ।
महाबलाभिधानस्य शिवलिङ्गवरस्य हि ॥ ३८॥

English
O brahmins thus the greatness of the excellent Siva image Mahabala has been mentioned to you by me. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अथान्यदपि वक्ष्यामि माहात्म्यं तस्य चाद्भुतम् ।
श्रुतमात्रेण येनाशु शिवे भक्तिः प्रजायते ॥ ३९॥

English
I shall now mention another wonderful glory of the same. By merely listening to it, the devotion for Siva is generated. (39.)

---
