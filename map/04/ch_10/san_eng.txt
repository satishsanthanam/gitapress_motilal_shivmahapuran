### **CHAPTER TEN - (The greatness and glory of Mahābala)**

---

Sanskrit
स राजानुचितं शापं विज्ञाय क्रोधमूर्छितः ।
जलाञ्जलिं समादाय गुरुं शप्तुं समुद्यतः ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said :—  There was a highly virtuous king Mitrasaha in the glorious family of Ikṣvāku. He was the greatest of skilled archers. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य राज्ञः सुधर्मिष्ठा मदयन्ती प्रिया शुभा ।
दमयन्ती नलस्येव बभूव विदिता सती ॥ २॥

English
The virtuous and auspicious-natured lady Madayanti was his beloved wife, like Damayanti of Nala. She was known as a chaste lady. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अभिसृत्य स राजेन्द्रो गौतमं विमलाशयम् ।
तद्दर्शनाप्तकिञ्चित्कः प्रणनाम मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ३॥

English
Fond of hunting, once that king Mitrasaha went to a thick forest accompanied by a huge army. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
विहरंस्तत्र स नृपः कमठाह्वं निशाचरम् ।
निजघान महादुष्टं साधुपीडाकरं खलम् ॥ ४॥

English
Sporting about there, the king killed a wicked demon Kamatha who used to harass good men. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्यानुजः पापी जयेयं छद्मनैव तम् ।
मत्वा जगाम नृपतेरन्तिकं छद्मकारकः ॥ ५॥

English
The demon's younger brother, a deceitful sinner thought "I shall conquer him by deceitful means" and with this evil intention approached the king. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तं विनम्राकृतिं दृष्ट्वा भृत्यतां कर्तुमागतम् ।
चक्रे महानसाध्यक्षमज्ञानात्स महीपतिः ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing him humble in behaviour in seeking service under him, the king made him the chief cook without knowing his real nature. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्मिन्वने राजा कियत्कालं विहृत्य सः ।
निवृत्तो मृगयां हित्वा स्वपुरीमाययौ मुदा ॥ ७॥

English
After indulging in hunting sports for sometime in the forest, the king abandoned the game and joyously returned to his capital. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुः क्षयाहे सम्प्राप्ते निमन्त्र्य स्वगुरुं नृपः ।
वसिष्ठं गृहमानिन्ये भोजयामास भक्तितः ॥ ८॥

English
On the Sraddha day of his father the king invited his preceptor Vasiṣṭha to his palace and fed him devoutly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षसा सूदरूपेण सम्मिश्रितनरामिषम् ।
शाकामिषं पुरः क्षिप्तं दृष्ट्वा गुरुरथाब्रवीत् ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing the vegetarian and non-vegetarian dish in which the demon in the guise of a cook had mixed human flesh, the preceptor said. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुरुवाच ।
धिक् त्वां नरामिषं राजंस्त्वयैतच्छद्मकारिणा ।
खलेनोपहृतं मह्यं ततो रक्षो भविष्यसि ॥ १०॥

English
The preceptor said :—  O wicked king, fie upon you. Human flesh has been deceitfully offered to me by you. Hence you will become a demon. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षःकृतं च विज्ञाय तदैवं स गुरुस्तदा ।
पुनर्विमृश्य तं शापं चकार द्वादशाब्दिकम् ॥ ११॥

English
On realising that it had been perpetrated by the demon, the preceptor pondered over and prescribed a time-limit of twelve years for the curse. (11.)

---

English
Thinking that the curse was unjustified, the infuriated king was about to return the curse with a handful of water. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा च तत्प्रिया साध्वी मदयन्ती सुधर्मिणी ।
पतित्वा पादयोस्तस्य शापं तं हि न्यवारयत् ॥ १३॥

English
Then the chaste and virtuous queen Madayanti fell at his feet and entreated him to spare the curse. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निवृत्तशापस्तु तस्या वचनगौरवात् ।
तत्याज पादयोरम्भः पादौ कल्मषतां गतौ ॥ १४॥

English
Out of deference for her entreaties the king desisted from cursing, but dropped the handful of water on his own feet which became deformed. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःप्रभृति राजा भूत्स लोकेऽस्मिन्मुनीश्वराः ।
कल्मषाङ्घ्रिरिति ख्यातः प्रभावात्तज्जलस्य हि ॥ १५॥

English
O great sages, from that day onwards the king became famous in the world as Kalmasanghri by the virtue of that water. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा मित्रसहः शापाद् गुरोरॄषिवरस्य हि ।
बभूव राक्षसो घोरो हिंसको वनगोचरः ॥ १६॥

English
By the curse of his preceptor the leading sage, the king Mitrasaha became a terrible violent Raksasa, roaming in the forest. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
स बिभ्रद्राक्षसं रूपं कालान्तकयमोपमम् ।
चखाद विविधाञ्जन्तून् मानुषादीन्वनेचरः ॥ १७॥

English
Transformed as Raksasa and resembling Yama, the destroyer of the world, he roamed about in the forest devouring various living beings, men and others. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स कदाचिद्वने क्वापि रममाणौ किशोरकौ ।
अपश्यदन्तकाकारो नवोढौ मुनिदम्पती ॥ १८॥

English
Once, somewhere in the forest, the king who was as hideous as the god of death saw a newly married couple—a young sage and his young wife indulging in sexual dalliance. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
राक्षसः स नराहारः किशोरं मुनिनन्दनम् ।
जग्धुं जग्राह शापार्त्तो व्याघ्रो मृगशिशुं यथा ॥ १९॥

English
The demon habituated to eat human flesh in his distressed state due to the curse caught hold of the young sage in order to eat him like a tiger seizing a fawn. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
कक्षे गृहीतं भर्तारं दृष्ट्वा भीता च तत्प्रिया ।
सा चक्रे प्रार्थनां तस्मै वदती करुणं वचः ॥ २०॥

English
The young woman, on seeing her husband seized by the demon, was much frightened and implored him with piteous cries. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रार्थ्यमानोऽपि बहुशः पुरुषादः स निर्घृणः ।
चखाद शिर उत्कृत्य विप्रसूनोर्दुराशयः ॥ २१॥

English
In spite of repeated importunities the man-eating ruthless wicked demon cut off the head of the brahmin sage and devoured it. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ साध्वी च सा दीना विलप्य भृशदुःखिता ।
आहृत्य भर्तुरस्थीनि चितां चक्रे किलोल्बणाम् ॥ २२॥

English
The distressed, grief-stricker, chaste lady lamented much. She gathered the bones of her husband and lighted a funeral pyre. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
भर्तारमनुगच्छन्ती संविशन्ती हुताशनम् ।
राजानं राक्षसाकारं सा शशाप द्विजाङ्गना ॥ २३॥

English
The brahmin lady desirous of entering the pyre in order to follow her husband cursed the Rākṣasa king. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यप्रभृति नारीषु यदा त्वं सङ्गतो भवेः ।
तदा मृतिस्तवेत्युक्त्वा विवेश ज्वलनं सती ॥ २४॥

English
The chaste lady entered fire after proclaiming "From now onwards if you become united with any woman in sexual embrace you will die." (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि राजा गुरोः शापमनुभूय कृतावधिम् ।
पुनः स्वरूपमास्थाय स्वगृहं मुदितो ययौ ॥ २५॥

English
After undergoing the results of his preceptor's curse to which there was a time-limit, the king regained his original form and joyously returned to his palace. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा विप्रसतीशापं मदयन्ती रतिप्रियम् ।
पतिं निवारयामास वैधव्यादतिबिभ्यती ॥ २६॥

English
Madayanti who knew of the curse of the chaste brahmin lady prevented her husband who evinced a desire for sexual embrace. She was much frightened of widowhood. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अनपत्यो विनिर्विण्णो राज्यभोगेषु पार्थिवः ।
विसृज्य सकलां लक्ष्मीं वनमेव जगाम ह ॥ २७॥

English
The issueless king became disgusted with kingly pleasures. Eschewing all riches he went to the forest. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वपृष्ठतः समायान्तीं ब्रह्महत्यां सुदुःखदाम् ।
ददर्श विकटाकारां तर्जयन्तीं मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २८॥

English
He saw the hideous form of Brahmahatyā closely following him, threatening him again and again and tormenting him. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्या निर्भद्रमन्विच्छन् राजा निर्विण्णमानसः ।
चकार नानोपायान्स जपव्रतमखादिकान् ॥ २९॥

English
Dejected in mind the king wanted to get rid of Brahmahatyā. He tried japas, holy rites, sacrifices and various other means. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नानोपायैर्यदा राज्ञस्तीर्थस्नानादिभिर्द्विजाः ।
न निवृत्ता ब्रह्महत्या मिथिलां स ययौ तदा ॥ ३०॥

English
O brahmins, when Brahmahatyā did not leave him inspite of the means like the ceremonial ablution in holy centres, the king went to Mithilā. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
बाह्योद्यानगतस्तस्याश्चिन्तया परयार्दितः ।
ददर्श मुनिमायान्तं गौतमं पार्थिवश्च सः ॥ ३१॥

English
Distressed and worried by anxiety the king reached a park at the outskirts of the city. There he saw the sage Gautama approaching him. (31.)

---

English
The king advanced towards that sage of pure mind. He felt satiated on seeing him. He bowed to him again and again. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तत्पृष्टकुशलो दीर्घमुष्णं च निःश्वसन् ।
तत्कृपादृष्टिसम्प्राप्तसुखः प्रोवाच तं नृपः ॥ ३३॥

English
When the sage enquired of his welfare, the king heaved a sigh of grief and distress. Viewed mercifully by him the king felt pleased and contented. He said thus. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ।
मुने मां बाधते ह्येषा ब्रह्महत्या दुरत्यया ।
अलक्षिता परैस्तात तर्जयन्ती पदे पदे ॥ ३४॥

English
The king said :—  O sage, this endless Brahmahatyā harasses me. O dear, it is imperceptible to others but it threatens me at every step. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यन्मया शापदग्धेन विप्रपुत्रश्च भक्षितः ।
तत्पापस्य न शान्तिर्हि प्रायश्चित्तसहस्रकैः ॥ ३५॥

English
A brahmin boy had been devoured by me in the clutches of a curse. That sin cannot be suppressed even by thousands of expiatory rites. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नानोपायाः कृता मे हि तच्छान्त्यै भ्रमता मुने ।
न निवृत्ता ब्रह्महत्या मम पापात्मनः किमु ॥ ३६॥

English
O sage, I roamed here and there and tried various means to quell it. But it did not leave me off. Is it not due to my being a sinning soul ? (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्य मे जन्मसाफल्यं सम्प्राप्तमिव लक्षये ।
यतस्त्वद्दर्शनादेव ममानन्दभरोऽभवत् ॥ ३७॥

English
Now it seems to me that I have attained the fruits of my birth because merely by your sight my heart is filled with bliss. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्य मे तव पादाब्जशरणस्य कृतैनसः ।
शान्तिं कुरु महाभाग येनाहं सुखमाप्नुयाम् ॥ ३८॥

English
O fortunate one, I, the sinner, seek refuge in your lotus-like feet. Grant me peace, whereby I can be happy. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति राज्ञा समादिष्टो गौतमः करुणार्द्रधीः ।
समादिदेश घोराणामघानां साधु निष्कृतिम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Sūta said :— Implored thus by the king, the kind-hearted Gautama instructed him in the expiatory means for terrible sins. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
साधु राजेन्द्र धन्योऽसि महाघेभ्यो भयं त्यज ।
शिवे शास्तरि भक्तानां क्व भयं शरणैषिणाम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Gautama said :—  O excellent king, well done You are blessed. Eschew all fear from sins. As long as Śiva is the ruler, there can be no terror to devotees who seek refuge in him. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु राजन्महाभाग क्षेत्रमन्यत्प्रतिष्ठितम् ।
महापातकसंहारि गोकर्णाख्यं शिवालयम् ॥ ४१॥

English
O fortunate king, listen. There is another shrine well consecrated. There is a Śiva's temple named Gokarṇa. It destroys all great sins. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र स्थितिर्न पापानां महद्भ्यो महतामपि ।
महाबलाभिधानेन शिवः सन्निहितः स्वयम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Sins greater than the greatest cannot stay there. Śiva himself is present there in the name of Mahābala. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां शिवलिङ्गानां सार्वभौमो महाबलः ।
चतुर्युगे चतुर्वर्णः सर्वपापापहारकः ॥ ४३॥

English
Mahābala is the emperor of all phallic images. It assumes four different colours in the four Yugas. It dispels all sins. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चिमाम्बुधितीरस्थं गोकर्णं तीर्थमुत्तमम् ।
तत्रास्ति शिवलिङ्गं तन्महापातकनाशकम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The excellent holy centre of Gokarṇa is on the shore of western ocean. There is a phallic image of Śiva, destructive of great sins. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा महापापाः स्नात्वा तीर्थेषु भूरिशः ।
महाबलं च सम्पूज्य प्रयाताः शाङ्करं पदम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Great sinners go there, take their ceremonial ablutions many times in the sacred waters and worship Mahābala. They have all attained Śiva's region. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा त्वमपि राजेन्द्र गोकर्णं गिरिशालयम् ।
गत्वा सम्पूज्य तल्लिङ्गं कृतकृत्यत्वमाप्नुयाः ॥ ४६॥

English
O king, you also go to Gokarṇa, the temple of Śiva. After going there and worshipping Śiva attain contentment. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र सर्वेषु तीर्थेषु स्नात्वाभ्यर्च्य महाबलम् ।
सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तः शिवलोकं त्वमाप्नुयाः ॥ ४७॥

English
Take your holy dip in the sacred waters there. Worship Mahābala. Thus you will be rid of sins and attain Śivaloka. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्यादिष्टः स मुनिना गौतमेन महात्मना ।
महाहृष्टमना राजा गोकर्णं प्रत्यपद्यत ॥ ४८॥

English
Sūta said :—  Advised thus by the noble-souled Gautama, the king highly delighted in the mind reached Gokarṇa. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तीर्थेषु सुस्नात्वा समभ्यर्च्य महाबलम् ।
निर्धूताशेषपापौघोऽलभच्छम्भोः परं पदम् ॥ ४९॥

English
He took his dip in the holy ponds and worshipped Mahābala. His sins were wiped off without the least vestige. He attained the great region of Śiva. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
य इमां श‍ृणुयान्नित्यं महाबलकथां प्रियाम् ।
त्रिसप्तकुलजैः सार्धं शिवलोके व्रजत्यसौ ॥ ५०॥

English
He who listens to this pleasing narrative of Mahābala goes to Śivaloka along with the members of his family upto twentyone generations. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्यातं माहात्म्यं परमाद्भुतम् ।
महाबलस्य गिरिशलिङ्गस्य निखिलाघहृत् ॥ ५१॥

English
Thus the extremely wonderful glory of Mahābala, the phallic image of Śiva, has been narrated to you. It dispels all sins. (51.)

---
