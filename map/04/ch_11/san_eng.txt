### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - (The greatness of the moon-crested Paśupatinātha)**

---

Sanskrit
दधीचमूर्तिस्तत्रैव समर्च्या विधिपूर्वकम् ।
शिवप्रीत्यर्थमेवाशु तीर्थयात्राफलार्थिभिः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said :—  O Sūta, O fortunate Sūta, you are blessed with your mind attached to Śiva. This wonderful story of Mahābala has been narrated to you. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तरस्यां दिशायां च शिवलिङ्गानि यानि च ।
तेषां माहात्म्यमनघं वद त्वं पापनाशकम् ॥ २॥

English
Please narrate the sacred glory of the sin-destroying phallic images that are in the northern quarter. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श‍ृणुतादरतो विप्रा औत्तराणां विशेषतः ।
माहात्म्यं शिवलिङ्गानां प्रवदामि समासतः ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said :—  O brahmins, listen with respect. I shall succinctly explain the particular glory of the northern phallic images of Siva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
गोकर्णं क्षेत्रमपरं महापातकनाशनम् ।
महावनं च तत्रास्ति पवित्रमतिविस्तरम् ॥ ४॥

English
There is another shrine Gokarna destructive of great sins. There is an extensive holy forest there. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रास्ति चन्द्रभालाख्यं शिवलिङ्गमनुत्तमम् ।
रावणेन समानीतं सद्भक्त्या सर्वसिद्धिदम् ॥ ५॥

English
The moon-crested Siva's phallic image, excellent of all, is situated there. It was installed by Ravaṇa with great devotion and it confers all accomplishments. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य तत्र स्थितिर्वैद्यनाथस्येव मुनीश्वराः ।
सर्वलोकहितार्थाय करुणासागरस्य च ॥ ६॥

English
O great sages, its situation there, is for the benefit of the people as that of Vaidyanatha, the ocean of mercy. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नानं कृत्वा तु गोकर्णे चन्द्रभालं समर्च्य च ।
शिवलोकमवाप्नोति सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ ७॥

English
Taking the ceremonial ablution in Gokarna and worshipping the moon-crested Siva, the devotee attains Sivaloka. True. It is true undoubtedly. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रभालस्य लिङ्गस्य महिमा परमाद्भुतः ।
न शक्यो वर्णितुं व्यासाद्भक्तिस्नेहरतस्य हि ॥ ८॥

English
It is impossible to describe in detail the excessively wonderful glory of the moon-crested lord Siva who has a great affection for his devotees. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रभालमहादेवलिङ्गस्य महिमा महान् ।
यथाकथञ्चित्सम्प्रोक्तः परलिङ्गस्य वै श‍ृणु ॥ ९॥

English
Somehow the extreme grandeur of the phallic Siva has been explained to you. Now listen to that of the other phallic image. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दाधीचं शिवलिङ्गं तु मिश्रर्षिवरतीर्थके ।
दधीचिना मुनीशेन सुप्रीत्या च प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १०॥

English
In the excellent holy centre$^{134}$ of the sage Misra, the phallic image of Siva had been installed by the great sage Dadhici. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा च तत्तीर्थे स्नात्वा सम्यग्विधानतः ।
शिवलिङ्गं समर्चेद्वै दाधीचेश्वरमादरात् ॥ ११॥

English
After going there and taking the due ceremonial ablution in the sacred pond, the devotee shall worship this image reverentially. (11.)

---

English
The idol of Dadhica should be duly worshipped for the propitiation of Siva by those who seek the fruit of the pilgrimage. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृते मुनिश्रेष्ठाः कृतकृत्यो भवेन्नरः ।
इह सर्वसुखं भुक्त्वा परत्र गतिमाप्नुयात् ॥ १३॥

English
O excellent sages, if this is duly performed, the devotee will feel contented. After enjoying all pleasure here he will attain the supreme goal hereafter. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
नैमिषारण्यतीर्थे तु निखिलर्षिप्रतिष्ठितम् ।
ऋषीश्वरमिति ख्यातं शिवलिङ्गं सुखप्रदम् ॥ १४॥

English
The phallic image of Siva known as Rsiśvara installed by the sages in the holy centre of Naimisa$^{135}$ forest, yields happiness. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दर्शनात्पूजनाच्च जनानां पापिनामपि ।
भुक्तिर्मुक्तिश्च तेषां तु परत्रेह मुनीश्वराः ॥ १५॥

English
O great sages, enjoyment of worldly pleasures and salvation here and hereafter will fall to the lot of even sinful persons who visit and worship it. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
हत्याहरणतीर्थे तु शिवलिङ्गमघापहम् ।
पूजनीयं विशेषेण हत्याकोटिविनाशनम् ॥ १६॥

English
The phallic image destructive of sins, in the holy centre of Hatyāharaṇa¹³⁶ should be particularly worshipped. It destroys even the sins of several slaughters. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
देवप्रयागतीर्थे तु ललितेश्वरनामकम् ।
शिवलिङ्गं सदा पूज्यं नरैः सर्वाघनाशनम् ॥ १७॥

English
Laliteśvara, the phallic image of Śiva in the holy centre Devaprayāga should be worshipped by men. It is destructive of all sins. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
नयपालाख्यपुर्यां तु प्रसिद्धायां महीतले ।
लिङ्गं पशुपतीशाख्यं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ १८॥

English
The phallic image Paśupatiśa in the Nayapāla¹³⁷ town famous on the earth is the bestower of the fruits of all desires. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरोभागस्वरूपेण शिवलिङ्गं तदस्ति हि ।
तत्कथां वर्णयिष्यामि केदारेश्वरवर्णने ॥ १९॥

English
That phallic image is in the form of the crest. I shall narrate the story in the description of Kedāreśvara. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तदारान्मुक्तिनाथाख्यं शिवलिङ्गं महाद्भुतम् ।
दर्शनादर्चनात्तस्य भुक्तिर्मुक्तिश्च लभ्यते ॥ २०॥

English
Close to it lies the wonderful phallic image Mukti-nātha. Its very sight and worship bestows worldly enjoyment here and liberation hereafter. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्यातं लिङ्गवर्णनमुत्तमम् ।
चतुर्दिक्षु मुनिश्रेष्ठाः किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छथ ॥ २१॥

English
O great sages, the excellent phallic images in the four quarters have been thus described to you all. What else do you wish to hear. (21.)

---
