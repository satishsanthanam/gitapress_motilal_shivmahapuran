### **CHAPTER TWELVE - (The reason for Śiva's assuming the phallic form)**

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा ऋषिपत्न्यस्ताः परं त्रासमुपागताः ।
विह्वला विस्मिताश्चान्याः समाजग्मुस्तथा पुनः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said :—  O Sūta, you know every thing by the grace of Vyāsa. There is nothing not known to you. Hence we approach you with a query. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गं च पूज्यते लोके तत्त्वया कथितं च यत् ।
तत्तथैव न चान्यद्वा कारणं विद्यते त्विह ॥ २॥

English
The phallic form of Śiva is worshipped throughout the world. You have said so. Is there any special reason for the same ? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणरूपा श्रुता लोके पार्वती शिववल्लभा ।
एतत्किं कारणं सूत कथय त्वं यथाश्रुतम् ॥ ३॥

English
Pārvatī, the beloved of Śiva, is heard in the world in the form of an arrow. O Sūta, what is the reason for this ? Please satisfy our curiosity in this respect by giving the account you have heard. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
कल्पभेदकथा चैव श्रुता व्यासान्मया द्विजाः ।
तामेव कथयाम्यद्य श्रूयतामृषिसत्तमाः ॥ ४॥

English
Sūta said :—  O brahmins, the story of a different Kalpa was heard from Vyāsa by me. O excellent sages, I shall narrate the same. Please listen. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा दारुवने जातं यद् वृत्तं तु द्विजन्मनाम् ।
तदेव श्रूयतां सम्यक् कथयामि यथाश्रुतम् ॥ ५॥

English
What happened formerly among the brahmins in the Dāruvana¹³⁸ forest may kindly be heard. I shall narrate the same in the manner I have heard. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
दारुनाम वनं श्रेष्ठं तत्रासन् ऋषिसत्तमाः ।
शिवभक्ताः सदा नित्यं शिवध्यानपरायणाः ॥ ६॥

English
There is an excellent forest Dāruvana. There were many excellent sages there, great devotees of Śiva who were always engrossed in meditation on Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O great sages, they performed the worship of Śiva incessantly thrice a day. They eulogised Śiva with different devotional hymns. (7.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Once the leading brahmin devotees of Śiva engrossed in the meditation of Śiva went into the forest for bringing sacrificial twigs. (8.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
It was with a mind to do something pleasing to the forest-dwellers that Śiva, favourite of the devotees, came to the forest at his will. (11.)

Sanskrit

Sanskrit
इह सर्वसमृद्धिः स्यान्नानासुखवहाधिका ।
परत्र परमा मुक्तिर्नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ५४॥

English
Here everything will flourish in plenty conferring supreme happiness and bliss. Hereafter it will bestow final liberation. No doubt need be entertained in this respect. (54.)
