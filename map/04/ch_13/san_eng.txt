### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - (The origin of Vāṭuka)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
यथाभवल्लिङ्गरूपः सम्पूज्यस्त्रिभवे शिवः ।
तथोक्तं वा द्विजाः प्रीत्या किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छथ ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said :—  O brahmins, it has been narrated to you how Śiva came to be worshipped in the three worlds in the form of a phallic image. What else do you wish to hear? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
अन्धकेश्वरलिङ्गस्य महिमानं वद प्रभो ।
तथान्यच्छिवलिङ्गानां प्रीत्या वक्तुमिहार्हसि ॥ २॥

English
The sages said :—  O holy lord, please narrate the glory of the phallic image known as Andhakeśvara. Similarly it behoves you to mention the glory of other phallic images willingly. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
पुराब्धिगर्तमाश्रित्य वसन् दैत्योऽन्धकासुरः ।
स्ववशं कारयामास त्रैलोक्यं सुरसूदनः ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said :—  Formerly the Asura Andhaka resorted to the deep ditch of the ocean and kept the three worlds under his control. He harrassed the gods. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्गर्ताच्च निःसृत्य पीडयित्वा पुनः प्रजाः ।
प्राविशच्च तदा दैत्यस्तं गर्तं सुपराक्रमः ॥ ४॥

English
He used to come out of the ditch and harrass the people. Then he of great exploit re-entered the ditch. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च दुःखिताः सर्वे शिवं प्रार्थ्य पुनःपुनः ।
सर्वं निवेदयामासुः स्वदुःखं च मुनीश्वराः ॥ ५॥

English
O great sages, the gods were grief-stricken. Imploring Siva again and again, they mentioned their miseries to him. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तदाकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां देवानां परमेश्वरः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा दुष्टहन्ता सतां गतिः ॥ ६॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing the words of the gods, the delighted lord Śiva, the slayer of the wicked and the goal of the good replied. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
घातयिष्यामि तं दैत्यमन्धकं सुरसूदनम् ।
सैन्यं च नीयतान्देवा ह्यायामि च गणैः सह ॥ ७॥

English
Śiva said :—  I shall slay that Asura Andhaka, the murderer of the gods. O gods, proceed with the army. I too shall come along with the Gaṇas. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्गर्तादन्धके हि देवर्षिद्रुहि भीकरे ।
निःसृते च तदा तस्मिन्देवा गर्तमुपाश्रिताः ॥ ८॥

English
When that terrible antagonist of the gods and the sages came out of the ditch, the gods went and occupied it. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्याश्च देवताश्चैव युद्धं चक्रुः सुदारुणम् ।
शिवानुग्रहतो देवाः प्रबलाश्चाभवँस्तदा ॥ ९॥

English
The Asuras and the gods fought a terrible battle. By the blessings of Śiva, the gods became very strong. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवैश्च पीडितः सोऽपि यावद्गर्तमुपागतः ।
तावच्छूलेन सम्प्रोतः शिवेन परमात्मना ॥ १०॥

English
Attacked and tormented by the gods, the Asura returned to the ditch when he was pierced through with the trident by Śiva, the supreme soul. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रत्यश्च तदा शम्भुं ध्यात्वा सम्प्रार्थयत्तदा ।
अन्तकाले च त्वां दृष्ट्वा तादृशो भवति क्षणात् ॥ ११॥

English
Remaining there he meditated on Śiva and prayed —“On seeing you at the hour of death, one becomes identical with you instantaneously.” (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं संस्तुतः सोऽपि प्रसन्नः शङ्करस्तदा ।
उवाच वचनं तत्र वरं ब्रूहि ददामि ते ॥ १२॥

English
Thus eulogised, the delighted Śiva spoke to him—"Mention the boon you wish to have. I shall grant it to you." (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा स दैत्यः पुनरब्रवीत् ।
सुप्रणम्य शिवं स्तुत्वा सत्त्वभावमुपाश्रितः ॥ १३॥

English
On hearing these words, the Asura said again after bowing to and eulogising Siva with pious feelings. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्धक उवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश स्वभक्तिं देहि मे शुभाम् ।
कृपां कृत्वा विशेषेण संस्थितो भव चेह वै ॥ १४॥

English
Andhaka said:—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted grant me the auspicious devotion to you. Taking unusual compassion on me, be stationed here. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तेन दैत्यं तं तद्गर्ते चाक्षिपद्धरः ।
स्वयं तत्र स्थितो लिङ्गरूपोऽसौ लोककाम्यया ॥ १५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Requested thus, Siva hurled that Asura into that ditch. He himself stood there in the form of a phallic image with a desire to help the world. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्धकेशं च तल्लिङ्गं नित्यं यः पूजयेन्नरः ।
षण्मासाज्जायते तस्य वाञ्छासिद्धिर्न संशयः ॥ १६॥

English
If a man worships Andhakeśa every day, his desires are fulfilled within six months. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वृत्त्यर्थं पूजयेल्लिङ्गं लोकस्य हितकारकम् ।
षण्मासं यो द्विजश्चैव स वै देवलकः स्मृतः ॥ १७॥

यथा देवलकश्चैव स भवेदिह वै तदा ।
देवलकश्च यः प्रोक्तो नाधिकारो द्विजस्य हि ॥ १८॥

॥ ऋषय ऊचुः ॥

देवलकश्च कः प्रोक्तः किं कार्यं तस्य विद्यते ।
तत्त्वं वद महाप्राज्ञ लोकानां हितहेतवे ॥ १९॥

English
The brahmin who worships the beneficent phallic image for his livelihood for six months becomes a Devalaka, the worshipper of idol for subsistence. He is not authorised in ordinary rites of brahmins. The sages said:— Who is a Devalaka? What is his function? O intelligent one, mention that for the guidance of the people. (17-19.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
दधीचिर्नाम विप्रो यो धर्मिष्ठो वेदपारगः ।
शिवभक्तिरतो नित्यं शिवशास्त्रपरायणः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* There was a brahmin Dadhici who was well-versed in the Vedas and highly virtuous. He was engrossed in his devotion to Siva and was interested in Siva's lore. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रस्तथा ह्यासीत्स्मृतो नाम्ना सुदर्शनः ।
तस्य भार्या दुकूला च नाम्ना दुष्टकुलोद्भवा ॥ २१॥

English
His son was known as Sudarsana whose wife was Dukula. She was born of a defiled family. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वशे स च भर्तासीत्तस्य पुत्रचतुष्टयम् ।
सोऽपि नित्यं शिवस्यैव पूजां च स्म करोत्यसौ ॥ २२॥

English
The husband was always under her control. He had four sons. Of course he used to worship Siva every day. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दधीचेस्तु तदा ह्यासीद् ग्रामान्तरनिवेशनम् ।
ज्ञातिसंयोगतश्चैव ज्ञातिभिर्न स मोचितः ॥ २३॥

English
Then it happened that Dadhici had to go and live in another village along with his kinsmen. The kinsmen did not let him go back. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कथयित्वा च पुत्रं स शिवभक्तिरतो भव ।
इत्युक्त्वा स गतो मुक्तो दधीचिः शैवसत्तमः ॥ २४॥

English
While leaving home, Dadhici, the excellent devotee, had already instructed his son, "Be devoted to Siva." (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदर्शनस्तत्पुत्रोऽपि शिवपूजां चकार ह ।
एवं चिरतरः कालो व्यतीयाय मुनीश्वराः ॥ २५॥

English
Sudarsana's son also worshipped Siva. O great sages, thus a long time elapsed. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं च शिवरात्रिश्च समायाता कदाचन ।
तस्यां चोपोषिताः सर्वे स्वयं संयोगतस्तदा ॥ २६॥

पूजां कृत्वा गतः सोऽपि सुदर्शन इति स्मृतः ।
स्त्रीसङ्गं शिवरात्रौ तु कृत्वा पुनरिहागतः ॥ २७॥

न स्नानं तेन च कृतं तद्रात्र्यां शिवपूजनम् ।
तेन तत्कर्मपाकेन क्रुद्धः प्रोवाच शङ्करः ॥ २८॥

English
So, once it happened that on a Sivaratri occasion when all observed fast, Sudarsana had his sexual intercourse with his wife and then performed the worship. But before he performed the worship he abstained from taking bath. For this misdeed Siva was infuriated and said. (26-28.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वर उवाच ।
शिवरात्र्यां त्वया दुष्ट सेवनं च स्त्रियाः कृतम् ।
अस्नातेन मदीया च कृता पूजाविवेकिना ॥ २९॥

English
Lord Śiva said:—  O knave, you had sexual intercourse with your wife on the occasion of Sivaratri. Without taking bath you performed the worship. You are an indiscreet fellow. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा चैवं कृतं यस्मात्तस्मात्त्वं जडतां व्रज ।
ममास्पृश्यो भव त्वं च दूरतो दर्शनं कुरु ॥ ३०॥

English
Because you have done this consciously, be sluggish and insensible. You are an untouchable person for me. Avoid touching me. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति शप्तो महेशेन दाधीचिः स सुदर्शनः ।
जडत्वं प्राप्तवान्सद्यः शिवमायाविमोहितः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Cursed thus by lord Siva, Sudarsana, the son of Dadhici, attained insensibility immediately, deluded by Siva's. Maya. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्समये विप्रा दधीचिः शैवसत्तमः ।
ग्रामान्तरात्समायातो वृत्तान्तं श्रुतवाँश्च सः ॥ ३२॥

English
O brahmins, meanwhile Dadhici, the excellent devotee of Siva, returned from the other village and heard the details. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेन भर्त्सितः सोऽपि दुःखितोऽभूदतीव हि ।
रुरोद हा हतोऽस्मीति दुःखेन सुतकर्मणा ॥ ३३॥

English
He too was rebuked by Siva. Hence he felt much distressed. He cried—"Oh I am doomed by this foul deed of my son." (33.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनःपुनरुवाचेति स दधीचिः सतां मतः ।
अनेनेदं कुपुत्रेण हतं मे कुलमुत्तमम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Dadhici, honoured amongst the good, grumbled again and again—"My excellent family is damned by the misbehaviour of my vicious son. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
स पुत्रोऽपि हतो भार्यां पुंश्चलीमुक्तवान्द्रुतम् ।
पश्चात्तापमनुप्राप्य स्वपित्रा परिभर्त्सितः ॥ ३५॥

English
That son too is doomed. He has taken a vicious slut to his wife." Thus the son was rebuked by his father who repented. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पित्रा गिरिजा तत्र पूजिता विधिभिर्वरैः ।
सुयत्नतो महाभक्त्या स्वपुत्रसुखहेतवे ॥ ३६॥

English
The father then worshipped Pārvatī with excellent rites, sincere devotion and perseverance for the happiness of his son. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदर्शनोऽपि गिरिजां पूजयामास च स्वयम् ।
चण्डीपूजनमार्गेण महाभक्त्या शुभैः स्तवैः ॥ ३७॥

English
Sudarśana himself performed the worship of Pārvatī with great devotion adopting the path of Caṇḍī worship and with auspicious hymns. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं तौ पितृपुत्रौ हि नानोपायैः सुभक्तितः ।
प्रसन्नां चक्रतुर्देवीं गिरिजां भक्तवत्सलाम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus by various means and with sincere devotion the father and the son propitiated the goddess Pārvatī who is favourably disposed towards her devotees. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोः सेवाप्रभावेण प्रसन्ना चण्डिका तदा ।
सुदर्शनं च पुत्रत्वे चकार गिरिजा मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, by the devotion of their service Caṇḍikā was delighted. She adopted Sudarśana as her son. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवं प्रसादयामास पुत्रार्थे चण्डिका स्वयम् ।
क्रुद्धाक्रुद्धा पुनश्चण्डी तत्पुत्रस्य प्रसन्नधीः ॥ ४०॥

English
For the sake of her adopted son she herself implored Śiva. Śiva who was angry had become quite calm. The son also was delighted. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अथाज्ञाय प्रसन्नं तं महेशं वृषभध्वजम् ॥

नमस्कृत्य स्वयं तस्य ह्युत्सङ्गे तं न्यवेशयत् ॥ ४१॥

English
Then realising that lord Śiva the bull-bannered deity was delighted, she bowed to him herself and placed the son on his lap. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
घृतस्नानं तश्चक्रे पुत्रस्य गिरिजा स्वयम् ।
त्रिरावृत्तोपवीतं च ग्रन्थिनैकेन संयुतम् ॥ ४२॥

सुदर्शनाय पुत्राय ददौ प्रीत्या तदाम्बिका ।
उद्दिश्य शिवगायत्रीं षोडशाक्षरसंयुताम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Making the ablution of the son with ghee, Pārvatī handed over to her son Sudarśana the three-stringed sacred thread with a single knot and imparted instruction in Śivagāyatri¹⁴⁰ consisting of sixteen syllables. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
तदों नमः शिवायेति श्रीशब्दपूर्वकाय च ।
वारान्षोडश सङ्कल्पपूजां कुर्यादयं बटुः ॥ ४४॥

English
Then the brahmin boy performed the Saṃkal-papūjā (worship by mental conception) sixteen times with the five-syllabled mantra with the word Śrī prefixed to Śiva. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
आस्नानादिप्रणामान्तं पूजयन्वृषभध्वजम् ।
मन्त्रवादित्रपूजाभिः सर्षीणां सन्निधौ तथा ॥ ४५॥

नाममन्त्राननेकांश्च पाठयामास वै तदा ।
उवाच सुप्रसन्नात्मा चण्डिका च शिवस्तथा ॥ ४६॥

English
Beginning with ceremonial ablution and ending with the concluding obeisance he worshipped the bull-bannered deity by means of devotional mantras, musical instruments and adorations in the presence of the sages. He recited various names of Śiva. Then the delighted Śiva and (45-46.)

---

Sanskrit
मदर्पितं च यत्किञ्चिद्धनधान्यादिकं तथा ।
तत्सर्वं च त्वया ग्राह्यं न दोषाय भविष्यति ॥ ४७॥

English
Caṇḍikā said.  "Wealth, foodgrain etc. whatever is offered to me shall be taken by you. There is no sin in this action. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
मम कृत्ये भवान्मुख्यो देवीकृत्ये विशेषतः ।
घृततैलादिकं सर्वं त्वया ग्राह्यं मदर्पितम् ॥ ४८॥

English
In the rites concerning me you will officiate as the chief, especially so in the rites concerning the goddess. Ghee, oil etc., everything offered to me shall be taken by you. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राजापत्यं भवेद्यर्हिं तर्ह्येको हि भवान्भवेत् ।
तदा पूजा च सम्पूर्णान्यथा सर्वा च निष्फला ॥ ४९॥

English
When the Prajāpatya rite is performed only one of you shall be there. Then alone is the worship complete. Or everything becomes fruitless. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तिलकं वर्तुलं कार्यं स्नानं कार्यं सदा त्वया ।
शिवसन्ध्या च कर्तव्या गायत्री च तदीयका ॥ ५०॥

English
The Tilaka must be made round. Then you shall take bath. Sandhyā prayers to Śiva shall be offered and his Gāyatri repeated. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्सेवां प्रथमं कृत्वा कार्यमन्यत्कुलोचितम् ।
एवं कृतेऽखिले भद्रं दोषाः क्षान्ता मया तव ॥ ५१॥

English
After rendering service to me at the outset, the other rites shall be performed according to the family tradition. When everything is thus performed there shall be welfare. Your blemishes have been excused by me. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तस्य पुत्राश्च चत्वारो बटुकास्तदा ।
अभिषिक्ताश्चतुर्दिक्षु शिवेन परमात्मना ॥ ५२॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this, the supreme soul Siva consecrated his four sons as Vataukas in the four quarters. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
चण्डी चैवात्मनिकटे पुत्रं स्थाप्य सुदर्शनम् ।
तत्पुत्रान्प्रेरयामास वरान्दत्त्वा ह्यनेकशः ॥ ५३॥

English
After keeping Sudarsana as her son with herself she granted boons to his sons and blessed them in several ways. (53.)

उभयोर्व्यूहयोर्मध्ये बटुको यो भवेन्मम ।
तस्य स्याद्विजयो नित्यं नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ५४॥

English
The Goddess said:—  Between you two whoever becomes my Vatauka shall be victorious. No doubt need be entertained in this respect. (54.)

Sanskrit

English
He who worships you has worshipped me too. You shall perform your duties always, O son. (55.)

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं तस्मै वरा दत्ताः सपुत्राय महात्मने ।
सुदर्शनाय कृपया शिवाभ्यां जगतां कृते ॥ ५६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus boons were granted to the noble Sudarsana and his sons by Siva and Siva out of pity for the welfare of the worlds. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाभ्यां स्थापिता यस्मात्तस्मात्ते बटुकाः स्मृताः ।
तपोभ्रष्टा यतो जाताः स्मृतास्तस्मात्तपोऽधमाः ॥ ५७॥

English
Because they had been established by Siva and Siva they are known as Vataukas. Those who neglect penance are known s Tapodhamas. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवयोः कृपया सर्वे विस्तारं बहुधा गताः ।
तेषां च प्रथमा पूजा महापूजा महात्मनः ॥ ५८॥

English
Thanks to the mercy of Siva and Siva they expanded in various ways. Their worship at the outset is the great worship of Śiva, the supreme soul. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन यावत्कृता नैव पूजा वै शङ्करस्य च ।
तावत्पूजा न कर्त्तव्या कृता चेन्न शुभापि सा ॥ ५९॥

English
No worship shall be performed by any person as long as he has not performed Śiva's worship. If it is performed it does not turn out to be auspicious. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
शुभं वाप्यशुभं वापि बटुकं न परित्यजेत् ।
प्राजापत्ये च भोज्ये वै बटुरेको विशिष्यते ॥ ६०॥

English
Whether auspicious or inauspicious, the Vaṭuka is not to be eschewed. In the Prājāpatya rite and at the feast a single Vaṭu is considered excellent. (60.)

Sanskrit

English
In the rites of Śiva and Śivā this difference is marked. O intelligent one, hear the same. O faultless one, I shall explain it now. (61.)

Sanskrit
तस्यैव नगरे राज्ञो भद्रस्य नित्यभोजने ।
प्राजापत्यस्य नियमे ह्यन्धकेशसमीपतः ॥ ६२॥

यज्जातमद्भुतं वृत्तं शिवानुग्रहकारणात् ।

English
I shall explain in the manner I have heard, the event that happened in the city of king Bhadra near the shrine of Andhakeśa during the daily rite of feasting in the Prājāpatya rite. (62-63.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्वज एकश्च तद्राज्ञे दत्तस्तुष्टेन शम्भुना ।
प्रोक्तश्च कृपया राजा देवदेवेन तेन सः ॥ ६४॥

English
A certain banner was given to that king by Śiva who was satisfied with his devotion. The king was addressed too by the lord mercifully. (64.)

Sanskrit
प्रातश्च बध्यतां राजन्ध्वजो रात्रौ पतिष्यति ।
मम त्वेवं च सम्पूर्णे प्राजापत्ये तथा पुनः ॥ ६५॥

अन्यथायं ध्वजो मे हि रात्रावपि स्थिरो भवेत् ।
इत्युक्त्वान्तर्हितः शम्भू राज्ञे तुष्टः कृपानिधिः ॥ ६६॥

English
The banner will be raised and bound in the morning. It will fall down in the night. This will happen when the Prājāpatya rite is full and complete. Otherwise this banner will remain steady even during the night. After saying this to the king, the delighted Śiva, the storehouse of mercy, vanished. (65-66.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O great sage, the king adopted the procedure accordingly. The Prājāpatya rite was performed every day in accordance with the rules of Śiva-worship. (67.)

Sanskrit

English
If the rites became full and complete, the banner rose up itself in the morning and fell down during the night. (68.)

Sanskrit

On one occasion the function of the Vaṭu took place in the beginning. The banner then fell even before the feast. (69.)

Sanskrit

English
On seeing it there, the learned men were asked by the king. The brahmins are at their feast. Why is the banner not flying? (70.)

How did it fall down? O brahmins, tell me the truth. Thus asked the brahmins, the excellent scholars, said. (71.)
