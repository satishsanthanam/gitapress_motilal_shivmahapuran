### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - (The origin of the Jyotirlinga Somanātha)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
ज्योतिषां चैव लिङ्गानां माहात्म्यं कथयाधुना ।
उत्पत्तिं च तथा तेषां ब्रूहि सर्वं यथाश्रुतम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  Please narrate the greatness of the Jyotirlingas. Please mention their origin and everything else in the manner you have heard. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श‍ृण्वन्तु विप्रा वक्ष्यामि तन्माहात्म्यं जनिं तथा ।
सङ्क्षेपतो यथाबुद्धि सद्गुरोश्च मया श्रुतम् ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, please listen, I shall narrate its greatness and origin succinctly in the manner I have heard from my good preceptor, to the extent of my intellect. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
एतेषां चैव माहात्म्यं वक्तुं वर्षशतैरपि ।
शक्यते न मुनिश्रेष्ठास्तथापि कथयामि वः ॥ ३॥

English
The first among them is glorified as Somanatha. O sage, at the outset, listen to its greatness with attention. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सोमनाथश्च तेषां वै प्रथमः परिकीर्तितः ।
तन्माहात्म्यं श‍ृणु मुने प्रथमं सावधानतः ॥ ४॥

English
O great sages, the greatness of Jyotirlingas cannot be adequately described even in hundreds of years. Still I shall tell you. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तविंशन्मिताः कन्या दक्षेण च महात्मना ।
तेन चन्द्रमसे दत्ता अश्विन्याद्या मुनीश्वराः ॥ ५॥

English
O great sages, his daughters, twenty-seven in number, Asvini and others, were given in marriage to the moon by Daksa, the great soul. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रं च स्वामिनं प्राप्य शोभमाना विशेषतः ।
चन्द्रोऽपि चैव ताः प्राप्य शोभते स्म निरन्तरम् ॥ ६॥

English
On securing the moon as their lord, those daughters shone extraordinarily. The moon too, on securing them shone incessantly. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
हेम्ना चैव मणिर्भाति मणिना हेम चैव हि ।
एवं च समये तस्य यज्जातं श्रूयतामिति ॥ ७॥

English
The jewel shines with gold and the gold shines with the jewel. Now listen to what happened in due course. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वास्वपि च पत्नीषु रोहिणी नाम या स्मृता ।
यथैका सा प्रिया चासीत्तथान्या न कदाचन ॥ ८॥

English
Out of his twenty-seven wives he did not love any one so much at anytime as he loved Rohiṇī. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्याश्च दुःखमापन्नाः पितरं शरणं ययुः ।
गत्वा तस्मै च यद्दुःखं तथा ताभिर्निवेदितम् ॥ ९॥

English
The others became distressed and sought refuge in their father. After going to him they mentioned their misery to him. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षः स च तथा श्रुत्वा दुःखं च प्राप्तावांस्तदा ।
समागत्य द्विजाश्चन्द्रं शान्त्यावोचद् वचस्तदा ॥ १०॥

English
On hearing it, Dakṣa too was struck with grief. O brahmins, approaching the moon he calmly spoke to him thus. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
विमले च कुले त्वं हि समुत्पन्नः कलानिधे ।
आश्रितेषु च सर्वेषु न्यूनाधिक्यं कथं तव ॥ ११॥

English
Dakṣa said:—  O storehouse of digits, you are born of a noble family. How can you cherish oddity in your affection towards your dependents ? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं चेत्तत्कृतं तच्च न कर्तव्यं त्वया पुनः ।
वर्तनं विषमत्वेन नरकप्रदमीरितम् ॥ १२॥

English
If you have done so, stop with it. This is enough. You shall not repeat it. Oddity in behaviour is said to be conducive to hell. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
दक्षश्चैवं च सम्प्रार्थ्य चन्द्रं जामातरं स्वयम् ।
जगाम मन्दिरं स्वं वै निश्चयं परमं गतः ॥ १३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After requesting the moon, his son-in-law, thus, Dakṣa returned to his abode fully assured. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रोऽपि वचनं तस्य न चकार विमोहितः ।
शिवमायाप्रभावेण यया सम्मोहितं जगत् ॥ १४॥

English
The moon did not act according to his advice being deluded by Śiva's Māyā which deludes the universe. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शुभं भावि यदा यस्य शुभं भवति तस्य वै ।
अशुभं च यदा भावि कथं तस्य शुभं भवेत् ॥ १५॥

English
Welfare befalls him who is destined to have it. How can one destined to court woe have a weal ? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रोऽपि बलवद्भाविवशान्मेने न तद्वचः ।
रोहिण्यां च समासक्तो नान्यां मेने कदाचन ॥ १६॥

English
Being subjected to the force of destiny the moon did not accept his advice. Infatuated of Rohiṇī he neglected others. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा पुनरागत्य स्वयं दुःखसमन्वितः ।
प्रार्थयामास चन्द्रं स दक्षो दक्षः सुनीतितः ॥ १७॥

English
On hearing it the skilful Dakṣa returned to the moon excessively distressed. He politely asked the moon to mend his ways. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
श्रूयतां चन्द्र यत्पूर्वं प्रार्थितो बहुधा मया ।
न मानितं त्वया यस्मात्तस्मात्त्वं च क्षयी भव ॥ १८॥

English
Dakṣa said:—  O moon, listen. Although requested repeatedly you did not pay heed to my entreaties. Hence, fall a prey to the wasting disease. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ते तेन चन्द्रो वै क्षयी जातः क्षणादिह ।
हाहाकारो महानासीत्तदेन्दौ क्षीणतां गते ॥ १९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Just as he cursed him, the moon contracted consumption within a trice. When he was wasted away there was a great hue and cry. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
देवर्षयस्तदा सर्वे किं कार्यं हा कथं भवेत् ।
इति दुःखं समापन्ना विह्वला ह्यभवन्मुने ॥ २०॥

English
O sage, the gods and the sages became extremely agitated and felt miserable—"What shall be done? What will happen now?" (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञापिताश्च चन्द्रेण सर्वे शक्रादयः सुराः ।
ऋषयश्च वसिष्ठाद्या ब्रह्माणं शरणं ययुः ॥ २१॥

English
Informed and implored by the moon, Indra and other gods, Vasiṣṭha and other sages sought refuge in Brahmā. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वापि तु तदा प्रोचुस्तद् वृत्तं निखिलं मुने ।
ब्रह्मणे ऋषयो देवा नत्वा नुत्वातिविह्वलाः ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, after approaching him in their great agitation they bowed to and eulogised Brahmā and mentioned everything to him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मापि तद्वचः श्रुत्वा विस्मयं परमं ययौ ।
शिवमायां सुप्रशस्य श्रावयंस्तानुवाच ह ॥ २३॥

English
On hearing their words Brahmā was much surprised. Praising the Māyā of Śiva within their hearing he said. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अहो कष्टं महज्जातं सर्वलोकस्य दुःखदम् ।
चन्द्रस्तु सर्वदा दुष्टो दक्षश्च शप्तवानमुम् ॥ २४॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O what a pity? This is distressing to the entire world. The moon is always wicked and defiled. Now Dakṣa has cursed him. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं दुष्टेन चन्द्रेण कृतं कर्माप्यनेकशः ।
श्रूयतामृषयो देवाश्चन्द्रकृत्यं पुरातनम् ॥ २५॥

English
Many unholy deeds have been committed by the wicked moon. O sages, O gods, may the early misdeeds of the moon be heard. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतेर्गृहं गत्वा तारा दुष्टेन वै हृता ।
तस्य भार्या पुनश्चैव स दैत्यान्समुपस्थितः ॥ २६॥

English
The wicked fellow went to Bṛhaspati's house and abducted his wife Tārā. He then allied himself with the Daityas. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
समाश्रितस्तदा दैत्यान्युद्धं देवैश्चकार ह ।
मयात्रिणा निषिद्धश्च तस्मै तारां ददौ शशी ॥ २७॥

English
Having sought refuge in the Daityas he fought against the god though forbidden by me and Atri. Then the moon restored Tārā to Bṛhaspati. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तां च गर्भवतीं दृष्ट्वा न गृह्णामीति सोऽब्रवीत् ।
अस्माभिर्वारितो जीवः कृच्छ्राज्जग्राह तां तदा ॥ २८॥

English
Seeing her pregnant, Bhaspati refused to take her back. Induced by us he accepted her unwillingly. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि गर्भं जहातीह गृह्णामीत्यब्रवीत्पुनः ।
गर्भे मया पुनस्तत्र त्याजिते मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ २९॥

कस्यायं च पुनर्गर्भः सोमस्येति च साब्रवीत् ।
पश्चात्तेन गृहीता सा मया च वारितेन वै ॥ ३०॥

English
But he said, "If she casts off the foetus I shall accept her." When they asked her whose child she bore in the womb, she replied that the foetus grew out of the seed of the moon. O excellent sages, I then removed the foetus and urged by me, she was accepted by him. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवंविधानि चन्द्रस्य दुश्चरित्राण्यनेकशः ।
वर्ण्यन्ते किं पुनस्तानि सोऽद्यापि कुरुते कथम् ॥ ३१॥

English
What avails the narration of his previous countless vicious deeds? He still continues to do similar things. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्जातं तत् सुसञ्जातं नान्यथा भवति ध्रुवम् ।
अतः परमुपायं वो वक्ष्यामि श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ ३२॥

English
What has happened cannot be reversed. I shall tell you the means to be adopted now. Listen attentively. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभासके शुभे क्षेत्रे व्रजेश्चन्द्रः सदैवतैः ।
शिवमाराधयेत्तत्र मृत्युञ्जयविधानतः ॥ ३३॥

English
Let the moon go to the auspicious shrine at Prabhasa along with the gods. Let him propitiate Siva there according to Mrtyunjaya rites. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
निधायेशं पुरस्तत्र चन्द्रस्तपतु नित्यशः ।
प्रसन्नश्च शिवः पश्चादक्षयं तं करिष्यति ॥ ३४॥

English
Let him perform the penance incessantly sitting before the lord. The delighted Siva will cure him from consumption afterwards. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य ब्रह्मणस्ते सुरर्षयः ।
सन्निवृत्याययुः सर्वे यत्र दक्षविधू ततः ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the words of Brahma, the gods and sages returned to the place where Daksa and the moon were present. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीत्वा ते ततश्चन्द्रं दक्षं चाश्वास्य निर्जराः ।
प्रभासे ऋषयश्चक्रुस्तत्र गत्वाखिलाश्च वै ॥ ३६॥

आवाह्य तीर्थवर्याणि सरस्वत्यादिकानि च ।
पार्थिवेन तदा पूजां मृत्युञ्जयविधानतः ॥ ३७॥

English
The gods and the sages consoled Daksa and took the moon to Prabhasa.[141] They invoked the sacred waters of Sarasvat and other rivers and performed the worship of the earthen phallic image in accordance with the Mrtyunjaya rites. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
ते देवाश्च तदा सर्वे ऋषयो निर्मलाशयाः ।
स्थाप्य चन्द्रं प्रभासे च स्वं स्वं धाम ययुर्मुदा ॥ ३८॥

English
The gods and the sages of pious mind left the moon at Prabhasa and joyously returned to their abodes. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रेण च तपस्तप्तं षण्मासं च निरन्तरम् ।
मृत्युञ्जयेन मन्त्रेण पूजितो वृषभध्वजः ॥ ३९॥

English
The moon performed the penance incessantly for six months. He worshipped the bull-bannered deity with the Mrtyunjaya mantra. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
दशकोटिमितं मन्त्रं समावृत्य शशी च तम् ।
ध्यात्वा मृत्युञ्जयं मन्त्रं तस्थौ निश्चलमानसः ॥ ४०॥

English
The moon repeated the Mrtyunjaya$^{142}$ mantra a hundred million times. He meditated on the lord Mrtyunjaya with his mind unruffled. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा शङ्करो देवः प्रसन्नोऽभूत्ततः प्रभुः ।
आविर्भूय विधुं प्राह स्वभक्तं भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ४१॥

English
Lord Siva, who is favourably disposed towards his devotees, was pleased by his devotion and manifesting himself there spoke to him thus. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
वरं वृणीष्व भद्रं ते मनसा यत्समीप्सितम् ।
प्रसन्नोऽहं शशिन्सर्वं दास्ये वरमनुत्तमम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Śiva said:—  O moon, may there be welfare to you. Choose your boon, whatever you desire in your mind. I am pleased with you. I shall grant you an excellent boon. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्र उवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश किमसाध्यं भवेन्मम ।
तथापि मे शरीरस्य क्षयं वारय शङ्कर ॥ ४३॥

English
The moon said:—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, what is not attainable to me? Still, O lord, let not my body decay by consumption. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षन्तव्यो मेऽपराधश्च कल्याणं कुरु सर्वदा ।
इत्युक्ते च तदा तेन शिवो वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ४४॥

English
Pardon my faults. Remain benevolent to me always. When he had said thus Śiva spoke to him again. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
पक्षे च क्षीयतां चन्द्र कला ते च दिने दिने ।
पुनश्च वर्धतां पक्षे सा कला च निरन्तरम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Śiva said:—  O moon, let your digit decline day by day in one fortnight and increase steadily in another. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं सति तदा देवा हर्षनिर्भरमानसाः ।
ऋषयश्च तथा सर्वे समाजग्मुर्द्रुतं द्विजाः ॥ ४६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, when this happened the delighted gods and the sages came there quickly. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्य च तदा सर्वे चन्द्रायाशिषमब्रुवन् ।
शिवं नत्वा करौ बद्ध्वा प्रार्थयामासुरादरात् ॥ ४७॥

English
After coming there they offered benedictory blessings to the moon. They joined their palms in reverence and bowed to Siva. They prayed to him devotionally. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाः ऊचुः ।
देवदेव महादेव परमेश नमोऽस्तु ते ।
उमया सहितः शम्भो स्वामिन्नत्र स्थिरो भव ॥ ४८॥

English
The gods said:—  O great god, O lord of gods, obeisance be to you. O Śiva, O lord, be stable here along with Umā. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
ततश्चन्द्रेण सद्भक्त्या संस्तुतः शङ्करः पुरा ।
निराकारश्च साकारः पुनश्चैवाभवत्प्रभुः ॥ ४९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Then the moon eulogised the lord with good devotion first in the Nirguna and then in the Saguṇa form. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नश्च स देवानां क्षेत्रमाहात्म्यहेतवे ।
चन्द्रस्य यशसे तत्र नाम्ना चन्द्रस्य शङ्करः ॥ ५०॥

सोमेश्वरश्च नाम्नासीद्विख्यातो भुवनत्रये ।
क्षयकुष्ठादिरोगाणां नाशकः पूजनाद् द्विजाः ॥ ५१॥

English
Siva was delighted with the gods. For increasing the glory and greatness of the region and the moon, Siva stayed there in the name of Someśvara. He became famous in the three worlds. O brahmins, by worship Siva becomes the destroyer of ailments such as consumption, leprosy etc. (50-51.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्योऽयं कृतकृत्योऽयं यन्नाम्ना शङ्करः स्वयम् ।
स्थितश्च जगतां नाथः पावयञ्जगतीतलम् ॥ ५२॥

English
Blessed indeed and contented is he in whose name Śiva the lord of the worlds, himself stayed there sanctifying the entire world. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्कुण्डं तैश्च तत्रैव सर्वैर्देवैः प्रतिष्ठितम् ।
शिवेन ब्रह्मणा तत्र ह्यविभक्तं तु तत्पुनः ॥ ५३॥

English
A pond was established there itself by all the gods. That was shared by Śiva and Brahmā together. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रकुण्डं प्रसिद्धं च पृथिव्यां पापनाशनम् ।
तत्र स्नाति नरो यः स सर्वैः पापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ५४॥

English
It is famous as the moon's pond on the earth. It is destructive of sins. The man who takes bath there gets rid of sins here. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
रोगाः सर्वे क्षयाद्याश्च ह्यसाध्या ये भवन्ति वै ।
ते सर्वे च क्षयं यान्ति षण्मासं स्नानमात्रतः ॥ ५५॥

English
Incurable diseases like consumption are entirely eradicated if a person takes ablutions there for six months. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रभासं च परिक्रम्य पृथिवीक्रमसम्भवम् ।
फलं प्राप्नोति शुद्धात्मा मृतः स्वर्गे महीयते ॥ ५६॥

English
A pious man attains the fruit of circumambulating the earth, if he circumambulates Prabhāsa. After death he is honoured in heaven by the gods. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
सोमलिङ्गं नरो दृष्ट्वा सर्वपापात्प्रमुच्यते ।
लब्ध्वा फलं मनोऽभीष्टं मृतः स्वर्गं समीहते ॥ ५७॥

English
On seeing this Somalunga a person is relieved of all sins. After enjoying the fruits as desired, he attains heaven after death. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यत्फलं समुद्दिश्य कुरुते तीर्थमुत्तमम् ।
तत्तत्फलमवाप्नोति सर्वथा नात्र संशयः ॥ ५८॥

English
He who makes pilgrimage to this place attains the fruits of whatever he might have had in view. There is no doubt about this. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते ऋषयो देवाः फलं दृष्ट्वा तथाविधम् ।
मुदा शिवं नमस्कृत्य गृहीत्वा चन्द्रमक्षयम् ॥ ५९॥

परिक्रम्य च तत्तीर्थं प्रशंसन्तश्च ते ययुः ।
चन्द्रश्चापि स्वकीयं च कार्यं चक्रे पुरातनम् ॥ ६०॥

English
Thus seeing the fruit of that high order, the sages and the gods joyously bowed to Siva and took the moon now freed from wastage. Circumambulating and praising that holy centre they returned to their abodes. The moon resumed his former activity. (59-60.)

Sanskrit
इति सर्वः समाख्यातः सोमेशस्य समुद्भवः ।
एवं सोमेश्वरं लिङ्गं समुत्पन्नं मुनीश्वराः ॥ ६१॥

English
O great sages, thus the details of the origin of Somesa have been narrated to you. Somesvara has originated thus. (61.)

Sanskrit
यः श‍ृणोति तदुत्पत्तिं श्रावयेद्वा परान्नरः ।
सर्वान्कामानवाप्नोति सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ६२॥

English
He who listens to this origin or narrates it to others attains all desires and becomes rid of all sins. (62.)
