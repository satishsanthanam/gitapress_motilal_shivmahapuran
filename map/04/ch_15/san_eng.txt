### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - (The origin of the Second Mallikārjuna Jyotirlinga)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि मल्लिकार्जुनसम्भवम् ।
यं श्रुत्वा भक्तिमान्धीमान्सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Hereafter I shall explain the origin of Mallikarjuna on hearing which the devotee becomes intelligent and rid of all sins. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वं च कथितं यच्च तत्पुनः कथयाम्यहम् ।
कुमारचरितं दिव्यं सर्वपापविनाशनम् ॥ २॥

English
The story of Kumāra has already been mentioned by me. I shall explain it again. It is divine and destructive of all sins. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा पृथ्वीं समाक्रम्य कैलासं पुनरागतः ।
कुमारः स शिवापुत्रस्तारकारिर्महाबलः ॥ ३॥

तदा सुरर्षिरागत्य सर्वं वृत्तं जगाद ह ।
गणेश्वरविवाहादि भ्रामयंस्तं स्वबुद्धितः ॥ ४॥

English
When Kumara, son of Siva and the powerful enemy of Taraka returned to Kailasa, the celestial sage Narada came there and told him all the news such as the marriage of Ganesvara and corrupted his mind. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा स कुमारो हि प्रणम्य पितरौ च तौ ।
जगाम पर्वतं क्रौञ्चं पितृभ्यां वारितोऽपि हि ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing it, Kumāra bowed to his parents and went to the mountain Krauñca¹⁴³ though forbidden by his parents. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कुमारस्य वियोगेन तन्माता गिरिजा यदा ।
दुःखितासीत्तदा शम्भुस्तामुवाच सुबोधकृत् ॥ ६॥

English
Due to separation from her son, Pārvatī was distressed. Then Śiva enlightened her. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं प्रिये दुःखितासि न दुःखं कुरु पार्वति ।
आयास्यति सुतः सुभ्रूस्त्यज्यतां दुःखमुत्कटम् ॥ ७॥

English
O beloved, why are you distressed? O Pārvatī, do not be grieved. O beautiful lady, our son will certainly return. Let not your excessive grief overpower you. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सा यदा च न तं मेने पार्वती दुःखिता भृशम् ।
तदा च प्रेषितास्तत्र शङ्करेण सुरर्षयः ॥ ८॥

English
When the distressed Pārvatī did not pay heed to his advice, the celestial sages were sent to Kārttika by Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च ऋषयः सर्वे सगणा हि मुदान्विताः ।
कुमारानयनार्थं वै तत्र जग्मुः सुबुद्धयः ॥ ९॥

English
The gods and the sages, accompanied by their attendants went there joyously to bring back Kumāra. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र गत्वा च ते सर्वे कुमारं सुप्रणम्य च ।
विज्ञाप्य बहुधाप्येनं प्रार्थनां चक्रुरादरात् ॥ १०॥

English
They went there and bowed to Kumāra, implored him in many ways and requested him humbly and respectfully. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
देवादिप्रार्थनां तां च शिवाज्ञासङ्कुलां गुरुः ।
न मेने स कुमारो हि महाहङ्कारविह्वलः ॥ ११॥

English
Agitated by great pride Kumāra did not care for the importunities of the gods and sages, intermingled with Śiva's behest. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च पुनरावृत्य सर्वे ते हि शिवान्तिकम् ।
स्वं स्वं स्थानं गता नत्वा प्राप्य शङ्करशासनम् ॥ १२॥

English
Then they returned again to Śiva. After bowing to him and taking leave of him they returned to their abodes. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा च गिरिजा देवी विरहं पुत्रसम्भवम् ।
शम्भुश्च परमं दुःखं प्राप तस्मिन्ननागते ॥ १३॥

English
When he did not return, goddess Pārvatī felt severely the pangs of separation from her son. Śiva too was extremely miserable. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो सुदुःखितौ दीनौ लोकाचारकरौ तदा ।
जग्मतुस्तत्र सुस्नेहात्स्वपुत्रो यत्र संस्थितः ॥ १४॥

English
Grief-stricken and distressed following the conventions of the world, out of affection they went to the place where their son was. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स पुत्रश्च कुमाराख्यः पित्रोरागमनं गिरेः ।
ज्ञात्वा दूरं गतोऽस्नेहाद्योजनत्रयमेव च ॥ १५॥

English
Coming to know of the arrival of his parents, from the top of the mountain Kumāra went thirtysix kilometres further out of disregard for their love. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रौञ्चे च पर्वते दूरं गते तस्मिन्स्वपुत्रके ।
तौ च तत्र समासीनौ ज्योतीरूपं समाश्रितौ ॥ १६॥

English
When he went further on the Krauñca hill, Śiva and Pārvatī stayed behind assuming the fiery form. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रस्नेहातुरौ तौ वै शिवौ पर्वणि पर्वणि ।
दर्शनार्थं कुमारस्य स्वपुत्रस्य हि गच्छतः ॥ १७॥

English
Out of filial affection they used to visit Kumāra on the concluding day of every fortnight. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अमावास्यादिने शम्भुः स्वयं गच्छति तत्र ह ।
पौर्णमासीदिने तत्र पार्वती गच्छति ध्रुवम् ॥ १८॥

English
Śiva goes there on Amāvasyā day and Pārvatī on the Full Moon day. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिनं हि समारभ्य मल्लिकार्जुनसम्भवम् ।
लिङ्गं चैव शिवस्यैकं प्रसिद्धं भुवनत्रये ॥ १९॥

English
From that day onwards that phallic image of Śiva born of Mallikārjuna became famous in the three worlds. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तल्लिङ्गं यः समीक्षेत स सर्वैः किल्बिषैरपि ।
मुच्यते नात्र सन्देहः सर्वान्कामानवाप्नुयात् ॥ २०॥

English
He who sees that phallic image becomes rid of all sins and attains all desires. There is no doubt about it. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःखं च दूरतो याति सुखमात्यन्तिकं लभेत् ।
जननीगर्भसम्भूतं कष्टं नाप्नोति वै पुनः ॥ २१॥

English
His misery disappears. He obtains supreme happiness. He never suffers from the distress of rebirth in the mother's womb. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
धनधान्यसमृद्धिश्च प्रतिष्ठारोग्यमेव च ।
अभीष्टफलसिद्धिश्च जायते नात्र संशयः ॥ २२॥

English
There is no doubt that he obtains flourishing wealth, foodgrains, fame, health and the fruits of cherished desires. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं द्वितीयं च प्रोक्तं मल्लिकसंज्ञितम् ।
दर्शनात्सर्वसुखदं कथितं लोकहेतवे ॥ २३॥

English
Thus the second Jyotirlinga Mallika has been narrated. It is the bestower of happiness and the welfare to the world. (23.)

---
