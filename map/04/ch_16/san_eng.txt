### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - (The greatness of the Jyotirlinga Mahākāla)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सर्वं विजानासि वस्तु व्यासप्रसादतः ।
ज्योतिषां च कथां श्रुत्वा तृप्तिर्नैव प्रजायते ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, you know everything by Vyāsa's favour. We are never satiated by hearing about the Jyotirlingas. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्त्वं हि विशेषेण कृपां कृत्वातुलां प्रभो ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं तृतीयं च कथय त्वं हि नोऽधुना ॥ २॥

English
Hence O holy lord, be kind to us and narrate the third Jyotirlinga to us now. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
धन्योऽहं कृतकृत्योऽहं श्रीमतां भवतां यदि ।
गतश्च सङ्गमं विप्रा धन्या वै साधुसङ्गतिः ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, if I remain in the company of you all, the glorious ones, I am blessed and contented. Blessed indeed is the association of saintly men. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो मत्वा स्वभाग्यं हि कथयिष्यामि पावनीम् ।
पापप्रणाशिनीं दिव्यां कथां च श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ ४॥

English
Hence, considering it my good fortune, I shall narrate the sanctifying sin-destroying divine story. Please listen to this story respectfully. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अवन्ती नगरी रम्या मुक्तिदा सर्वदेहिनाम् ।
शिवप्रिया महापुण्या वर्तते लोकपावनी ॥ ५॥

English
There is a beautiful city Avanti¹⁴⁴ yielding liberation to all souls. It is a favourite of Śiva, highly meritorious and sanctifying. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रासीद् ब्राह्मणश्रेष्ठः शुभकर्मपरायणः ।
वेदाध्ययनकर्ता च वेदकर्मरतः सदा ॥ ६॥

English
An excellent brahmin engrossed in the performance of auspicious rites lived there. He studied the Vedas and performed the Vedic rites. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्न्याधानसमायुक्तः शिवपूजारतः सदा ।
पार्थिवीं प्रत्यहं मूर्तिं पूजयामास वै द्विजः ॥ ७॥

English
The brahmin maintained sacrificial fires. He was the devotee of Siva. Everyday he worshipped the idol of Siva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वकर्मफलं प्राप्य द्विजो वेदप्रियः सदा ।
सतां गतिं समालेभे सम्यज्ज्ञानपरायणः ॥ ८॥

English
This brahmin Vedapriya was always interested in acquiring perfect knowledge. After securing the fruits of holy rites he attained the goal of the good. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पुत्रास्तादृशाश्चासंश्चत्वारो मुनिसत्तमाः ।
शिवपूजारता नित्यं पित्रोरनवमाः सदा ॥ ९॥

English
O excellent sages, he had four sons like him. They were not inferior to their parents and they were ever engaged in the worship of Siva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवप्रियश्च तज्ज्येष्ठः प्रियमेधास्ततः परम् ।
तृतीयः सुकृतो नाम धर्मवाही च सुव्रतः ॥ १०॥

English
The eldest was Devapriya. The next was Priyamedhas. The third was Sukrta and the fourth was Dharmavahin of good rites. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां पुण्यप्रतापाच्च पृथिव्यां सुखमैधत ।
शुक्लपक्षे यथा चन्द्रो वर्धते च निरन्तरम् ॥ ११॥

English
Thanks to the power of their merit, happiness increased on the earth even as the moon waxes incessantly in the bright half of the month. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा तेषां गुणास्तत्र वर्धन्ते स्म सुखावहाः ।
ब्रह्मतेजोमयी सा वै नगरी चाभवत्तदा ॥ १२॥

English
Their good qualities too flourished delighting and pleasing all. That city was enveloped in the brahminical splendour. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र यज्जातं वृत्तमुत्तमम् ।
श्रूयतां तद् द्विजश्रेष्ठाः कथयामि यथाश्रुतम् ॥ १३॥

English
O excellent brahmins, let the excellent event that happened in the meantime be heard. I shall narrate it in the manner I have heard. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वते रत्नमाले च दूषणाख्यो महासुरः ।
बलवान्दैत्यराजश्च धर्मद्वेषी निरन्तरम् ॥ १४॥

English
There was a great Asura Dūṣaṇa on the Ratnamāla$^{145}$ hill. He was very powerful. He was the king of the Daityas incessantly engaged in hating virtue and virtuous rites. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणो वरदानाच्च जगत्तुच्छीचकार ह ।
देवाः पराजितास्तेन स्थानान्निःसारितास्तथा ॥ १५॥

English
Thanks to the grant of boons by Brahma he considered the whole universe insignificant. The gods were defeated by him and ousted from their places and positions. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यां वेदधर्माश्च स्मृतिधर्माश्च सर्वशः ।
स्फोटितास्तेन दुष्टेन सिंहेनेव शशाः खलु ॥ १६॥

English
Vedic rites and rites according to Smrtis all over the earth were smothered by that wicked fellow like rabbits by a lion. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यावन्तो वेदधर्माश्च तावन्तो दूरतः कृताः ।
तीर्थे तीर्थे तथा क्षेत्रे धर्मो नीतश्च दूरतः ॥ १७॥

English
Whatever Vedic rites were there in the shrines and holy centres were stopped by him. Dharma was exiled to a distant place. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अवन्ती नगरी रम्या तत्रैका दृश्यते पुनः ।
इत्थं विचार्य तेनैव यत्कृतं श्रूयतां हि तत् ॥ १८॥

English
"There is a beautiful city Avanti." Thinking that what the Asura did, hear. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुसैन्यसमायुक्तो दूषणः स महासुरः ।
तत्रस्थान्ब्राह्मणान्सर्वानुद्दिश्य समुपाययौ ॥ १९॥

English
Accompanied by a vast army, the great Asura Dūṣaṇa went there desiring to harm the brahmins staying in the city. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य स दैत्येन्द्रश्चतुरो दैत्यसत्तमान् ।
प्रोवाचाहूय वचनं विप्रद्रोही महाखलः ॥ २०॥

English
After reaching the place, that great knave, that hater of brahmins called four hefty Daityas to him and said these words. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्य उवाच ।
किमेते ब्राह्मणा दुष्टा न कुर्वन्ति वचो मम ।
वेदधर्मरता एते सर्वे दण्ड्या मते मम ॥ २१॥

English
The Daitya said:—  Why don't the wicked brahmins do as I say ? All these, engaged in Vedic rites and rituals are worthy of being punished by me. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे देवा मया लोके राजानश्च पराजिताः ।
वशे किं ब्राह्मणाः शक्या न कर्तुं दैत्यसत्तमाः ॥ २२॥

English
O excellent Daityas, the gods and the kings in the world have been defeated by me. Why should not the brahmins be kept in my control ? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि जीवितुमिच्छा स्यात्तदा धर्मं शिवस्य च ।
वेदानां परमं धर्मं त्यक्त्वा सुखसुभागिनः ॥ २३॥

English
If they wish to live happily let them abandon the rites of Śiva and the rituals of the Vedas. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा जीवने तेषां संशयश्च भविष्यति ।
इति सत्यं मया प्रोक्तं तत्कुरुध्वं विशङ्किताः ॥ २४॥

English
Otherwise it is doubtful that they will continue to live. I have said this truthfully. Hence, do that unhesitatingly. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति निश्चित्य ते दैत्याश्चत्वारः पावका इव ।
चतुर्दिक्षु तदा जाताः प्रलये च यथा पुरा ॥ २५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing this and receiving the instruction the four Daityas besieged the four quarters of the city like the fires of dissolution. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ते ब्राह्मणास्तदा श्रुत्वा दैत्यानामुद्यमं तदा ।
न दुःखं लेभिरे तत्र शिवध्यानपरायणाः ॥ २६॥

English
On coming to know of the intent of the Daityas, the brahmins were not at all distressed because they were engaged in meditating on Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
धैर्यं समाश्रितास्ते च रेखामात्रं तदा द्विजाः ।
न चेलुः परमध्यानाद्वराकाः के शिवाग्रतः ॥ २७॥

English
Resorting to courage, the brahmins did not move even a line's breadth from meditation. With Śiva to help who can remain in distress ? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तैस्तु व्याप्तासीन्नगरी शुभा ।
लोकाश्च पीडितास्तैस्तु ब्राह्मणान्समुपाययुः ॥ २८॥

English
In the meantime, the whole of that auspicious city was overrun by them. The people were harassed and they rushed against the brahmins. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
लोका ऊचुः ।
स्वामिनः किं च कर्तव्यं दुष्टाश्च समुपागताः ।
हिंसिता बहवो लोका आगताश्च समीपतः ॥ २९॥

English
The people said:—  O holy lords, what shall be done ? The wicked Asuras have come. People are tortured. They have come very near. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तेषामिति वचः श्रुत्वा वेदप्रियसुताश्च ते ।
समूचुर्ब्राह्मणाँस्तान्वै विश्वस्ताः शङ्करे सदा ॥ ३०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing their words, the brahmins, the sons of Vedapriya, placing their faith in Śiva for ever, spoke to them. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणा ऊचुः ।
श्रूयतां विद्यते नैव बलं दुष्टभयावहम् ।
न शस्त्राणि तथा सन्ति यच्च ते विमुखाः पुनः ॥ ३१॥

English
The Brahmins said:—  Listen, we do not have an army to frighten the wicked Asuras. We do not have weapons to drive them back. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सामान्यस्यापमानो नो ह्याश्रयस्य भवेदिह ।
पुनश्च किं समर्थस्य शिवस्येह भविष्यति ॥ ३२॥

English
The general dishonour and insult reflects on him who supports us. Moreover, what can happen to Siva who is omnipotent? (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो रक्षां करोत्वद्यासुराणां भयतः प्रभुः ।
नान्यथा शरणं लोके भक्तवत्सलतः शिवात् ॥ ३३॥

English
Let lord Siva protect us from the terror of the Asuras. Except Siva, favourably disposed to his devotees there is no other refuge. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति धैर्यं समास्थाय समर्चां पार्थिवस्य च ।
कृत्वा ते च द्विजाः सम्यक् स्थिता ध्यानपरायणाः ।
दृष्टा दैत्येन तावच्च ते विप्राः सबलेन हि ॥ ३४॥

दूषणेन वचः प्रोक्तं हन्यतां वध्यतामिति ।
तच्छ्रुतं तैस्तदा नैव दैत्यप्रोक्तं वचो द्विजैः ।
वेदप्रियसुतैः शम्भोर्ध्यानमार्गपरायणैः ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Emboldened thus they performed the worship of Siva's earthen idol. They sat firmly engaged in meditation. On seeing them thus the mighty Dūṣaṇa shouted "Let them be killed. Let them be bound." But the brahmins, the sons of Vedapriya engrossed in meditation on Siva did not hear the shouts. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ यावत्स दुष्टात्मा हन्तुमैच्छद् द्विजांश्च तान् ।
तावच्च प्रार्थिवस्थाने गर्त आसीत्सशब्दकः ॥ ३६॥

English
When the wicked Daitya desired to kill the brahmins, the spot near the Pārthiva idol caved in with a loud report. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्तात्ततः समुत्पन्नः शिवो विकटरूपधृक् ।
महाकाल इति ख्यातो दुष्टहन्ता सतां गतिः ॥ ३७॥

English
From the chasm thus created, Siva rose up assuming a hideous form. He became famous as Mahākāla, the slayer of the wicked and the goal of the good. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालः समुत्पन्नो दुष्टानां त्वादृशामहम् ।
खल त्वं ब्राह्मणानां हि समीपाद् दूरतो व्रज ॥ ३८॥

English
"I am Mahākāla (the great god of death) risen up against the wicked like you. O sinful one, go away, leave the presence of these brahmins." (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा हुङ्कृतेनैव भस्मसात्कृतवांस्तदा ।
दूषणं च महाकालः शङ्करः सबलं द्रुतम् ॥ ३९॥

English
After saying this Siva reduced Dūṣaṇa and his army, to ashes immediately with a mere sound of Hum. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
कियत्सैन्यं हतं तेन किञ्चित्सैन्यं पलायितम् ।
दूषणश्च हतस्तेन शिवेनेह परात्मना ॥ ४०॥

English
Dūṣaṇa and a portion of his army too were killed by Siva the great soul. A part of the army fled. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यं दृष्ट्वा यथा याति सङ्क्षयं सर्वशस्तमः ।
तथैव च शिवं दृष्ट्वा तत्सैन्यं विननाश ह ॥ ४१॥

English
Just as darkness is dispelled on seeing the sun, so also that army was destroyed on seeing Siva. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदुन्दुभयो नेदुः पुष्पवृष्टिः पपात ह ।
देवः समाययुः सर्वे हरिब्रह्मादयस्तथा ॥ ४२॥

English
Divine drums were sounded. Shower of flowers fell. Vişnu, Brahma and other gods came there. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्त्या प्रणम्य तं देवं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम् ।
तुष्टुवुर्विविधैः स्तोत्रैः कृताञ्जलिपुटा द्विजाः ॥ ४३॥

English
After bowing to the benefactor of the worlds, the brahmins joined their palms in reverence and eulogised him with different hymns (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणांश्च समाश्वास्य सुप्रसन्नः शिवः स्वयम् ।
वरं ब्रूतेति चोवाच महाकालो महेश्वरः ॥ ४४॥

English
After consoling the brahmins the delighted Śiva asked the Brahmins to choose the boon they wished to have. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा ते द्विजाः सर्वे कृताञ्जलिपुटास्तदा ।
सुप्रणम्य शिवं भक्त्या प्रोचुः सन्नतमस्तकाः ॥ ४५॥

English
On hearing it, the devout brahmins with palms joined in reverence and heads bent down bowed to Śiva and said. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजा ऊचुः ।
महाकाल महादेव दुष्टदण्डकर प्रभो ।
मुक्तिं प्रयच्छ नः शम्भो संसाराम्बुधितः शिव ॥ ४६॥

English
The brahmins said:—  O lord, the chastiser of the wicked, O Siva, grant us liberation from the ocean of worldly existence. O Siva. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रैव लोकरक्षार्थं स्थातव्यं हि त्वया शिव ।
स्वदर्शकान्नरान् शम्भो तारय त्वं सदा प्रभो ॥ ४७॥

English
O Siva, you shall stay here itself for the protection of the people. O lord Siva, save those who see you. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तैः शिवस्तत्र तस्थौ गर्ते सुशोभने ।
भक्तानां चैव रक्षार्थं दत्त्वा तेभ्यश्च सद्गतिम् ॥ ४८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Requested thus, Siva remained in that auspicious chasm for the protection of his devotees after granting them the goal of the good. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजास्ते मुक्तिमापन्नाश्चतुर्द्दिक्षु शिवास्पदम् ।
क्रोशमात्रं तदा जातं लिङ्गरूपिण एव च ॥ ४९॥

English
The brahmins attained salvation. The supporting base of Siva in the form of Linga extended to 3 kilometres in each of the four directions. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालेश्वरो नाम शिवः ख्यातश्च भूतले ।
तं दृष्ट्वा न भवेत्स्वप्ने किञ्चिद्दुःखमपि द्विजाः ॥ ५०॥

English
O brahmins, Siva became famous as Mahakaleśvara. On seeing this image no one will have misery even in dreams. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
यं यं काममपेक्ष्यैव तल्लिङ्गं भजते तु यः ।
तं तं काममवाप्नोति लभेन्मोक्षं परत्र च ॥ ५१॥

English
A man worshipping this image with a cherished desire in his mind will attain the same here and salvation hereafter. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वं समाख्यातं महाकालस्य सुव्रताः ।
समुद्भवश्च माहात्म्यं किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छथ ॥ ५२॥

English
O great ones with good rites, the origin and the greatness of Mahakala has thus been narrated to you. What else do you wish to hear? (52.)
