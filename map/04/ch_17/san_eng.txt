### **CHAPTER SEVENTEEN - (The greatness of Jyotirlinga Mahākāla)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
महाकालसमाह्वस्थज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्य रक्षिणः ।
भक्तानां महिमानं च पुनर्ब्रूहि महामते ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O most intelligent one, please mention again the greatness of the Jyotirlinga Mahakala, the protector of his devotees. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श‍ृणुतादरतो विप्रा भक्तरक्षाविधायिनः ।
महाकालस्य लिङ्गस्य माहात्म्यं भक्तिवर्धनम् ॥ २॥

English
Suta said:- O brahmins, listen with interest to the devotion enhancing greatness of Mahakala, the protector of the devotees. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
उज्जयिन्यामभूद्राजा चन्द्रसेनाह्वयो महान् ।
सर्वशास्त्रार्थतत्त्वज्ञः शिवभक्तो जितेन्द्रियः ॥ ३॥

English
In Ujjayinī there was a king Candrasena, a devotee of Śiva, who had conquered his sense-organs and who knew the principles of all Śāstras. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याभवत्सखा राज्ञो मणिभद्रो गणो द्विजाः ।
गिरीशगणमुख्यश्च सर्वलोकनमस्कृतः ॥ ४॥

English
O brahmins, the Gana Manibhadra, honoured by the people, the chief of the Ganas of Siva, was a friend of that king. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा स गणेन्द्रो हि प्रसन्नास्यो महामणिम् ।
मणिभद्रो ददौ तस्मै चिन्तामणिमुदारधीः ॥ ५॥

English
Once the liberal-minded Gana-chief Manibhadra, gave him the great jewel Cintamani, out of pleasure. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स वै मणिः कौस्तुभवद् द्योतमानोऽर्कसन्निभः ।
ध्यातो दृष्टः श्रुतो वापि मङ्गलं यच्छति ध्रुवम् ॥ ६॥

English
The jewel was as brilliant as the sun. It shone like Kaustubha. It blessed auspiciously when meditated upon, heard of or sighted. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य कान्तितलस्पृष्टं कांस्यं ताम्रमयस्त्रपु ।
पाषाणादिकमन्यद्वा द्रुतं भवति हाटकम् ॥ ७॥

English
Any thing made of bell-metal, copper, tin or stone touched with its brilliant surface turned into gold. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स तु चिन्तामणिं कण्ठे बिभ्रद् राजा शिवाश्रयः ।
चन्द्रसेनो रराजाति देवमध्येव भानुमान् ॥ ८॥

English
Wearing that gem round his neck the king Candrasena, dependent on Siva shone well like the sun in the midst of gods. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा चिन्तामणिग्रीवं चन्द्रसेनं नृपोत्तमम् ।
निखिलाः क्षितिराजानस्तृष्णाक्षुब्धहृदोऽभवन् ॥ ९॥

English
On hearing that the excellent king Candrasena had the Cintamani round his neck, the kings on the earth were agitated in their hearts due to greed. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नृपा मत्सरिणः सर्वे तं मणिं चन्द्रसेनतः ।
नानोपायैरयाचन्त देवलब्धमबुद्धयः ॥ १०॥

English
The kings ignorantly trying to rival with him, begged of Candrasena, that jewel secured from the god. They used various means to get the jewel. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां भूभृतां याञ्चा चन्द्रसेनेन तेन वै ।
व्यर्थीकृता महाकालदृढभक्तेन भूसुराः ॥ ११॥

English
O brahmins, the importunity of the kings was rendered futile by Cindrasena, a staunch devotee of Siva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ते कदर्थीकृताः सर्वे चन्द्रसेनेन भूभृता ।
राजानः सर्वदेशानां संरम्भं चक्रिरे तदा ॥ १२॥

English
Disappointed and offended thus by him the kings of all the lands became wrathful against him. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ते सर्वराजानश्चतुरङ्गबलान्विताः ।
चन्द्रसेनं रणे जेतुं सम्बभूवुः किलोद्यताः ॥ १३॥

English
Then the kings fully equipped with the four types of armies¹⁴⁶ attempted to conquer Candrasena in the battle. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ते तु सर्वे समेता वै कृतसङ्केतसंविदः ।
उज्जयिन्याश्चतुर्द्वारं रुरुधुर्बहुसैनिकाः ॥ १४॥

English
They joined together, consulted and conspired with one another. With a vast army they laid siege to the four main gates of Ujjayinī. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
संरुध्यमानां स्वपुरीं दृष्ट्वा निखिलराजभिः ।
तमेव शरणं राजा महाकालेश्वरं ययौ ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing his city thus attacked by the kings, the king Candrasena sought refuge in Mahākāleśvara. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्विकल्पो निराहारः स नृपो दृढनिश्चयः ।
समानर्च महाकालं दिवानक्तमनन्यधीः ॥ १६॥

English
Without doubts and hesitations, without taking in any food that king of steady resolve worshipped Mahākāla day and night without turning his mind to anything else. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स भगवाञ्छम्भुर्महाकालः प्रसन्नधीः ।
तं रक्षितुमुपायं वै चक्रे तं श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ १७॥

English
Then the lord Śiva, delighted in his mind, concocted a means in order to save him. Listen to that with attention. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव समये गोपी काचित्तत्र पुरोत्तमे ।
चरन्ती सशिशुर्विप्रा महाकालान्तिकं ययौ ॥ १८॥

English
O brahmins, at that very time a certain cowherdess roaming here and there in that excellent city accompanied by her child came near Mahākāla. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाब्दवयसं बालं वहन्ती गतभर्तृका ।
राज्ञा कृतां महाकालपूजां सापश्यदादरात् ॥ १९॥

English
She had lost her husband. She carried her babe five years old. With great devotion she watched the Mahākāla worship performed by the monarch. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सा दृष्ट्वा सुमहाश्चर्यां शिवपूजां च तत्कृताम् ।
प्रणिपत्य स्वशिविरं पुनरेवाभ्यपद्यत ॥ २०॥

English
After witnessing the wonderful Śiva-worship performed by him and bowing down she returned to her camp. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वमशेषेण स दृष्ट्वा वल्लवीसुतः ।
कुतूहलेन तां कर्तुं शिवपूजां मनो दधे ॥ २१॥

English
The son of that cowherdess who had watched everything out of curiosity, thought of performing Śiva's worship similarly. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
आनीय हृद्यं पाषाणं शून्ये तु शिविरान्तरे ।
अविदूरे स्वशिविराच्छिवलिङ्गं स भक्तितः ॥ २२॥

गन्धालङ्कारवासोभिर्धूपदीपाक्षतादिभिः ।
विधाय कृत्रिमैर्द्रव्यैर्नैवेद्यं चाप्यकल्पयत् ॥ २३॥

English
He brought a fine pebble from somewhere and considered it his Śivaliṅga. He placed it in a vacant place not far from his camp. Various articles were conceived by him as sweet scents, ornaments, cloth, incense, lamp, rice grains and foodgrains in the course of his worship. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
भूयो भूयः समभ्यर्च्य पत्रैः पुष्पैर्मनोरमैः ।
नृत्यं च विविधं कृत्वा प्रणनाम पुनः पुनः ॥ २४॥

English
Worshipping again and again with delightful leaves and flowers he danced in various ways and made obeisance again and again. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्समये पुत्रं शिवासक्तसुचेतसम् ।
प्रणयाद्गोपिका सा तं भोजनाय समाह्वयत् ॥ २५॥

English
As his mind was engrossed in the worship of Śiva the cowherdess, his mother called him to take his meals. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यदाहूतोऽपि बहुश; शिवपूजाक्तमानसः ।
बालश्च भोजनं नैच्छत्तदा तत्र ययौ प्रसूः ॥ २६॥

English
When the son engrossed in the worship did not like to take meals though called many times, the mother went there. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तं विलोक्य शिवस्याग्रे निषण्णं मीलितेक्षणम् ।
चकर्ष पाणिं सङ्गृह्य कोपेन समताडयत् ॥ २७॥

English
On seeing him seated in front of Śiva with eyes closed she angrily caught hold of his hand, dragged him and beat him. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
आकृष्टस्ताडितश्चापि नागच्छत् स्वसुतो यदा ।
तां पूजां नाशयामास क्षिप्त्वा लिङ्गं च दूरतः ॥ २८॥

English
When the son did not come away even after being dragged and struck she threw the image far off and spoiled his worship. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहेति दूयमानं तं निर्भर्त्स्य स्वसुतं च सा ।
पुनर्विवेश स्वगृहं गोपी क्रोधसमन्विता ॥ २९॥

English
Rebuking her son who was lamenting piteously the infuriated cowherdess entered her house again. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मात्रा विनाशितां पूजां दृष्ट्वा देवस्य शूलिनः ।
देवदेवेति चुक्रोश निपपात स बालकः ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing his worship spoilt by his mother, the boy fell down and lamented, "O lord, O lord." (30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रनष्टसंज्ञः सहसा स बभूव शुचाकुलः ।
लब्धसंज्ञो मुहूर्तेन चक्षुषी उदमीलयत् ॥ ३१॥

English
In his excessive grief he became unconscious suddenly. Regaining consciousness after a while he opened his eyes. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तदैव जातं शिविरं महाकालस्य सुन्दरम् ।
ददर्श स शिशुस्तत्र शिवानुग्रहतोऽचिरात् ॥ ३२॥

English
Immediately the camp became a beautiful temple of Mahākāla. Thanks to the blessings of Śiva, that child saw all these. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्मयबृहद् द्वारं कपाटवरतोरणम् ।
महार्हनीलविमलवज्रवेदीविराजितम् ॥ ३३॥

English
The doorway was made of gold. There were excellent festoons at the doorstep. The temple had a shining dais set with costly and pure blue diamonds. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तप्तहेमकलशैर्विचित्रैर्बहुभिर्युतम् ।
प्रोद्भासितमणिस्तम्भैर्बद्धस्फटिकभूतलैः ॥ ३४॥

English
The temple was equipped with many golden pot-like domes, shining jewel-bedecked columns and the floor-paved with crystal bricks. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मध्ये रत्नलिङ्गं हि शङ्करस्य कृपानिधेः ।
स्वकृतार्चनसंयुक्तमपश्यद्गोपिकासुतः ॥ ३५॥

English
In the midst, the cowherd's son saw a jewel-bedecked linga of Śiva, the storehouse of mercy, along with the articles he used for worshipping. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स दृष्ट्वा सहसोत्थाय शिशुर्विस्मितमानसः ।
सन्निमग्न इवासीद्वै परमानन्दसागरे ॥ ३६॥

English
On seeing these, the boy was much surprised in his mind. He got up immediately. It seemed as if he was immersed in the ocean of great bliss. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्तुत्वा स गिरिशं भूयो भूयः प्रणम्य च ।
सूर्ये चास्तङ्गते बालो निर्जगाम शिवालयात् ॥ ३७॥

English
He eulogised and bowed to Śiva again and again. When the sun set the boy came out of the Śiva temple. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अथापश्यत्स्वशिविरं पुरन्दरपुरोपमम् ।
सद्यो हिरण्मयीभूतं विचित्रं परमोज्ज्वलम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Then he saw his own camp as beautiful as the city of Indra. It had been suddenly transformed into one of gold, of variegated nature and shining much. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽन्तर्विवेश भवनं सर्वशोभासमन्वितम् ।
मणिहेमगणाकीर्णं मोदमानो निशामुखे ॥ ३९॥

English
He entered the house equipped with everything brilliant and glittering in the night. Jewels and gold pieces were scattered everywhere. He was joyous. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापश्यत्स्वजननीं स्वपन्तीं दिव्यलक्षणाम् ।
रत्नालङ्कारदीप्ताङ्गीं साक्षात्सुरवधूमिव ॥ ४०॥

English
There he saw his mother sleeping. She was like a celestial lady with all divine characteristics. Her limbs shone and glittered with ornamental jewelry. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अथो स तनयो विप्राः शिवानुग्रहभाजनम् ।
जवेनोत्थापयामास मातरं सुखविह्वलः ॥ ४१॥

English
O brahmins, then that son, the special object of Śiva's blessings, excited due to happiness awakened his mother immediately. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सोत्थिताद्भुतमालक्ष्यापूर्वं सर्वमिवाभवत् ।
महानन्दसुमग्ना हि सस्वजे स्वसुतं च तम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Getting up and seeing everything unprecedentedly wonderful, she was as it were immersed in great bliss. She embraced her son. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा पुत्रमुखात्सर्वं प्रसादं गिरिजापतेः ।
प्रभुं विज्ञापयामास यो भजत्यनिशं शिवम् ॥ ४३॥

English
Hearing everything of the benign favour of the lord of Pārvatī from her son she sent word of it to the monarch who was worshipping Śiva constantly. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
स राजा सहसागत्य समाप्तनियमो निशि ।
ददर्श गोपिकासूनोः प्रभावं शिवतोषणम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The king who had concluded the observance of rites during the night came there immediately and saw the refulgence of the cowherd's son in propitiating Śiva. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा महीपतिः सर्वं तत्सामात्यपुरोहितः ।
आसीन्निमग्नो विधृतिः परमानन्दसागरे ॥ ४५॥

English
On seeing everything in the company of his ministers and chief priest the king was immersed in the ocean of great bliss and emboldened. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रेम्णा बाष्पजलं मुञ्चन् चन्द्रसेनो नृपो हि सः ।
शिवनामोच्चरन्प्रीत्या परिरेभे तमर्भकम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Shedding tears of love and repeating the names of Siva with pleasure, the king Candrasena embraced the boy. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
महामहोत्सवस्तत्र प्रबभूवाद्भुतो द्विजाः ।
महेशकीर्तनं चक्रुः सर्वे च सुखविह्वलाः ॥ ४७॥

English
O brahmins, there was a great and wonderful jubilation. Excited with happiness they sang the glorious songs of lord Siva. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमत्यद्भुताचाराच्छिवमाहात्म्यदर्शनात् ।
पौराणां सम्भ्रमाच्चैव सा रात्रिः क्षणतामगात् ॥ ४८॥

English
On account of this wonderful event, this manifestation of the greatness of Siva and the flutter in the midst of the citizens, the night elapsed as though it was only a moment. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रभाते युद्धाय पुरं संरुध्य संस्थिताः ।
राजानश्चारवक्त्रेभ्यः शुश्रुवुश्चरितं च तत् ॥ ४९॥

English
The kings who had besieged the city for an attack heard of this event in the morning through their spies. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
ते समेताश्च राजानः सर्वे ये ये समागताः ।
परस्परमिति प्रोचुस्तच्छ्रुत्वा चकित अति ॥ ५०॥

English
Extremely surprised on hearing it the kings who had come there met together and consulted one another. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
राजान ऊचुः ।
अयं राजा चन्द्रसेनः शिवभक्तोऽतिदुर्जयः ।
उज्जयिन्या महाकालपुर्याः पतिरनाकुलः ॥ ५१॥

English
# The kings said:—  This king Candrasena is a devotee of Siva and hence invincible. The king of Ujjayinī, the city of Mahākāla, is never distressed. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशाः शिशवो यस्य पुर्यां सन्ति शिवव्रताः ।
स राजा चन्द्रसेनस्तु महाशङ्करसेवकः ॥ ५२॥

English
The king Candrasena is a great devotee of Siva inasmuch as even children in his city observe Siva's rites. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
नूनमस्य विरोधेन शिवः क्रोधं करिष्यति ।
तत्क्रोधाद्धि वयं सर्वे भविष्यामो विनष्टकाः ॥ ५३॥

English
Certainly Siva will be furious if we offend him. We will be doomed if Siva is furious. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादनेन राज्ञा वै मिलापः कार्य एव हि ।
एवं सति महेशानः करिष्यति कृपां पराम् ॥ ५४॥

English
Hence we shall make an alliance with him. In that case lord Siva will be compassionate to us. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति निश्चित्य ते भूपास्त्यक्तवैराः सदाशयाः ।
सर्वे बभूवुः सुप्रीता न्यस्तशस्त्रास्त्रपाणयः ॥ ५५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus deciding, the kings abandoned their enmity. They regained purity of mind. They were pleased. They abandoned their weapons and missiles. (55.)
