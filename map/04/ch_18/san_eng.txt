### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - (The greatness of the Jyotirlinga Omkāreśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग श्राविता ह्यद्भुता कथा ।
महाकालाख्यलिङ्गस्य निजभक्तसुरक्षिणः ॥ १॥

English
The sage said:—  O Sūta, O fortunate Sūta, a wonderful story has been narrated of Mahākāla, the phallic image of Śiva that protects his devotees. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं चतुर्थं च कृपया वद वित्तम ।
ओंङ्कारे परमेशस्य सर्वपातकहारिणः ॥ २॥

English
O foremost of the wise, please describe the fourth Jyotirlinga of the great lord, the destroyer of all sins, in Omkāra. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
ओंङ्कारे परमेशाख्यं लिङ्गमासीद्यथा द्विजाः ।
तथा वक्ष्यामि वः प्रीत्या श्रूयतां परमर्षयः ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, O great sages, I shall describe to you with pleasure how the phallic image of lord Siva named Paramesa manifested from Omkāra. Let it be heard. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्मिंश्चित्समये चात्र नारदो भगवान्मुनिः ।
गोकर्णाख्यं शिवं गत्वा सिषेवे परभक्तिमान् ॥ ४॥

English
Once, the holy sage Nārada served with great devotion the phallic form of Śiva named Gokarna after going there. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स आगतो विन्ध्यं नगेशं मुनिसत्तमः ।
तत्रैव पूजितस्तेन बहुमानपुरःसरम् ॥ ५॥

English
From there the excellent sage came to Vindhya,\(^{148}\) the lord of mountains. He was duly worshipped by the mountain with great honour. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मयि सर्वं विद्यते च न न्यूनं हि कदाचन ।
इति भावं समास्थाय संस्थितो नारदाग्रतः ॥ ६॥

English
"Every thing is present in me. I am not deficient in anything." It was with this obsession that he sat in front of Nārada. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मानं तत्तदा श्रुत्वा नारदो मानहा ततः ।
निःश्वस्य संस्थितस्तत्र श्रुत्वा विन्ध्योऽब्रवीदिदम् ॥ ७॥

English
On knowing his arrogance, Nārada the dispeller of pride heaved a sigh and stayed there. On hearing that Vindhya spoke again. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
विन्ध्य उवाच ।
किं न्यूनं च त्वया दृष्टं मयि निः श्वासकारणम् ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा नारदो वाक्यमब्रवीत्स महामुनिः ॥ ८॥

English
Vindhya said:—  "What deficiency do you observe in me that you heave this deep breath?" On hearing these words, the great sage Nārada spoke again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
विद्यते त्वयि सर्वं हि मेरुरुच्चतरः पुनः ।
देवेष्वपि विभागोऽस्य न तवास्ति कदाचन ॥ ९॥

English
Nārada said:—  Everything is present in you. But Meru is loftier. He is classified among gods. But you, never. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा नारदस्तस्माज्जगाम च यथागतम् ।
विन्ध्यश्च परितप्तो वै धिग्वै मे जीवितादिकम् ॥ १०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this Nārada left the place and went in the manner he had come. Vindhya was distressed. "O fie upon my life", he exclaimed. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेश्वरं तथा शम्भुमाराध्य च तपाम्यहम् ।
इति निश्चित्य मनसा शङ्करं शरणं गतः ॥ ११॥

English
"I shall propitiate Śiva, the lord of the universe and perform penance." Deciding thus in his mind he sought refuge in Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
जगाम तत्र सुप्रीत्या ह्योङ्कारो यत्र वै स्वयम् ।
चकार च पुनस्तत्र शिवमूर्तिं च पार्थिवीम् ॥ १२॥

English
With great pleasure he went to the place where Omkāra himself was present. There he made an earthen idol of Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
आराध्य च तदा शम्भुं षण्मासं स निरन्तरम् ।
न चचाल तपःस्थानाच्छिवध्यानपरायणः ॥ १३॥

English
Continuously for six months he propitiated Śiva. Engaged in meditation on Śiva he never moved from the place of penance. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विन्ध्यतपो दृष्ट्वा प्रसन्नः पार्वतीपतिः ।
स्वरूपं दर्शयामास दुर्लभं योगिनामपि ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing the penance of Vindhya, Śiva, the delighted consort of Pārvati, revealed to him his real form inaccessible even to the Yogins. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नः स तदोवाच ब्रूहि त्वं मनसेप्सितम् ।
तपसा ते प्रसन्नोऽस्मि भक्तानामीप्सितप्रदः ॥ १५॥

English
Delightedly he said—"Tell me what you wish in your mind. I am delighted with your penance. I fulfil the desires of my devotees." (15.)

---

Sanskrit
विन्ध्य उवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश बुद्धिं देहि यथेप्सिताम् ।
स्वकार्यसाधिनीं शम्भो त्वं सदा भक्तवत्सलः ॥ १६॥

English
Vindhya said:—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, give me such intellect as I may achieve my object. O Śiva, you are always favourably disposed towards your devotees. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा भगवान् शम्भुश्चिचेत हृदये चिरम् ।
परोपतापदं विन्ध्यो वरमिच्छति मूढधीः ॥ १७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing that, lord Śiva thought within himself—"This Vindhya of deluded intellect desires for a boon capable of harassing others. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
किं करोमि यदेतस्मै वरदानं भवेच्छुभम् ।
मद्दत्तं परदुःखाय वरदानं यथा न हि ॥ १८॥

English
What shall I do? I shall grant him that boor which being auspicious for him may not harass others?" (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तथापि दत्तवान् शम्भुस्तस्मै तद्वरमुत्तमम् ।
विन्ध्य पर्वतराज त्वं यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ १९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Still Śiva granted him that excellent boon "O Vindhya, king of mountains, do as you please." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं च समये देवा ऋषयश्चामलाशयाः ।
सम्पूज्य शङ्करं तत्र स्थातव्यमिति चाब्रुवन् ॥ २०॥

English
At this time the gods and the sages worshipped Śiva and said—"You must stay here." (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा देववचनं प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ।
तथैव कृतवान्प्रीत्या लोकानां सुखहेतवे ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing the words of the gods, the delighted lord Śiva did so with pleasure for the happiness of the worlds. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ओङ्कारं चैव यल्लिङ्गमेकं तच्च द्विधा गतम् ।
प्रणवे चैव ओङ्कारनामासीत्स सदाशिवः ॥ २२॥

English
Tha phallus image of Siva known as Omkāra is twofold. In the Praṇava, Sadāśiva came to be known as Omkāra. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवे चैव यज्जातं तदासीत्परमेश्वरः ।
भक्ताभीष्टप्रदौ चोभौ भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदौ द्विजाः ॥ २३॥

English
What is born of the earthen idol became Parameśvara. Both of them yield the desires of the devotees. O brahmins, they yield worldly pleasures and salvation. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पूजां च तदा चक्रुर्देवाश्च ऋषयस्तथा ।
प्रापुर्वराननेकांश्च सन्तोष्य वृषभध्वजम् ॥ २४॥

English
The gods and the sages performed his worship then. After propitiating the bull-bannered deity they attained many boons. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्वस्थानं ययुर्देवा विन्ध्योऽपि मुदितोऽधिकम् ।
कार्यं साधितवान्स्वीयं परितापं जहौ द्विजाः ॥ २५॥

English
O brahmins, the gods returned to their abodes. Vindhya was much delighted. He realised his object and eschewed his despair. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
य एवं पूजयेच्छम्भुं मातृगर्भं वसेन्न हि ।
यदभीष्टं फलं तच्च प्राप्नुयान्नात्र संशयः ॥ २६॥

English
He who worships Siva in this manner will not stay in the mother's womb. He will obtain all the fruits desired. There is no doubt in this. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एतत्ते सर्वमाख्यातमोङ्कारप्रभवे फलम् ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि केदारं लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ॥ २७॥

English
Thus I have narrated to you the fruit of worshipping Omkāra. Hereafter I shall mention the glory of Kedāra, the excellent phallic form of Siva. (27.)

---
