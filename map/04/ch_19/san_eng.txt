### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - (The greatness of the Jyotirlinga Kedareśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
नरनारायणाख्यौ याववतारौ हरेर्द्विजाः ।
तेपाते भारते खण्डे बदर्याश्रम एव हि ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, the incarnations of Viṣṇu—Nara and Nārāyaṇa performed their penance in the Bhārata Khaṇḍa in the hermitage of Badarikāśrama.¹⁴⁹ (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ताभ्यां सम्प्रार्थितः शम्भुः पार्थिवे पूजनाय वै ।
आयाति नित्यं तल्लिङ्गे भक्ताधीनतया शिवः ॥ २॥

English
Requested by them for their worship, Śiva, being subservient to the devotees comes every day to that earthen phallic image of Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पूजयतोः शम्भुं तयोर्विष्ण्ववतारयोः ।
चिरकालो व्यतीयाय शैवयोर्धर्मपुत्रयोः ॥ ३॥

English
A long time elapsed while these two incarnations of Viṣṇu, the spiritual sons of Śiva, performed the worship of Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्समये तत्र प्रसन्नः परमेश्वरः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरो मे व्रियतामिति ॥ ४॥

English
On one occasion the delighted Śiva told them—"I am delighted. Choose your boon from me." (4.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते च तदा तेन नरो नारायणः स्वयम् ।
ऊचतुर्वचनं तत्र लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ५॥

English
When he said this, Nara and Nārāyaṇa spoke these words keeping in mind the welfare of the world. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
नरनारायणावूचतुः ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश यदि देयो वरस्त्वया ।
स्थीयतां स्वेन रूपेण पूजार्थं शङ्कर स्वयम् ॥ ६॥

English
Nara and Nārāyaṇa said:—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if the boon is to be granted by you, O Śiva, stay here in your own form and accept the devotion of your devotees. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तु तदा ताभ्यां केदारे हिमसंश्रये ।
स्वयं च शङ्करस्तस्थौ ज्योतीरूपो महेश्वरः ॥ ७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus requested, lord Śiva himself stayed in Kedāra on the Himavat in the form of Jyotirlinga. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ताभ्यां च पूजितश्चैव सर्वदुःखभयापहः ।
लोकानामुपकारार्थं भक्तानां दर्शनाय वै ॥ ८॥

English
He was worshipped by them for helping the worlds and for appearing in the presence of the devotees. He destroys their miseries and terrors. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयं स्थितस्तदा शम्भुः केदारेश्वरसंज्ञकः ।
भक्ताभीष्टप्रदो नित्यं दर्शनादर्चनादपि ॥ ९॥

English
Then Śiva himself stayed there in the name of Kedāreśvara. By his sight and worship he bestows the desires of the devotees. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च पूजयन्तीह ऋषयश्च पुरातनाः ।
मनोऽभीष्टफलं ते ते सुप्रसन्नान्महेश्वरात् ॥ १०॥

English
The gods and the sages of yore worship here. They get the fruit of their desire from the delighted lord Śiva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
भवस्य पूजनान्नित्यं बदर्याश्रमवासिनः ।
प्राप्नुवन्ति यतः सो हि भक्ताभीष्टप्रदः सदा ॥ ११॥

English
Since the residents of Badarikāśrama derive their desires every day by worshipping him, he is called the bestower of the desires of his devotees ever. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिनं हि समारभ्य केदारेश्वर एव च ।
पूजितो येन भक्त्या वै दुःखं स्वप्नेऽपि दुर्लभम् ॥ १२॥

English
From that day onwards, if anyone worships Kedāreśvara with devotion he will not suffer distress even in dreams. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यो वै हि पाण्डवान्दृष्ट्वा माहिषं रूपमास्थितः ।
मायामास्थाय तत्रैव पलायनपरोऽभवत् ॥ १३॥

English
It was he who on seeing Pāṇḍavas assumed the form of a buffalo, having recourse to his magical skill and began to run away. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
धृतश्च पाण्डवैस्तत्र ह्यवाङ्मुखतया स्थितः ।
पुच्छं चैव धृतं तैस्तु प्रार्थितश्च पुनःपुनः ॥ १४॥

English
When he was caught by the Pāṇḍavas he stood with his face bent down. They held his tail and implored him again and again. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रूपेण स्थितस्तत्र भक्तवत्सलनामभाक् ।
नयपाले शिरोभागो गतस्तद्रूपतः स्थितः ॥ १५॥

English
He remained in that form in the name of Bhaktavatsala. His head portion went and remained fixed in the city of Nayapāla.¹⁵⁰ The lord stood in that form there. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव पूजनान्नित्यमाज्ञां चैवाप्यदात्तथा ।
पूजितश्च स्वयं शम्भुस्तत्र तस्थौ वरानदात् ॥ १६॥

English
He asked them to worship him in that trunkless form. Worshipped by them, Śiva remained there and granted boons. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजयित्वा गतास्ते तु पाण्डवा मुदितास्तदा ।
लब्ध्वा चित्तेप्सितं सर्वं विमुक्ताः सर्वदुःखतः ॥ १७॥

English
The Pāṇḍavas went away with joy after worship- ping him. After obtaining what they desired in their minds, they were rid of all their miseries. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र नित्यं हरः साक्षात्क्षेत्रे केदारसंज्ञके ।
भारतीभिः प्रजाभिश्च तथैव परिपूज्यते ॥ १८॥

English
There in the shrine of Kedāra, Śiva is directly worshipped by the Indian people. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रत्यं वलयं यो वै ददाति हरवल्लभः ।
हररूपान्तिकं तच्च हररूपसमन्वितम् ॥ १९॥

तथैव रूपं दृष्ट्वा च सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ।
जीवन्मुक्तो भवेत्सोऽपि यो गतो बदरीवने ॥ २०॥

English
He who makes a gift of a ring or a bracelet after going there becomes a beloved of Siva. He comes very near the form of Siva. He is endowed with the form of Siva. On seeing that form of Siva, a person gets rid of sins. By going to Badari forest he becomes a living liberated soul. (19-20.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा रूपं नरस्यैव तथा नारायणस्य हि ।
केदारेश्वरशम्भोश्च मुक्तिभागी न संशयः ॥ २१॥

English
On seeing the forms of Nara, Narayana and Kedareśvara, undoubtedly he can achieve liberation. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
केदारेशस्य भक्ता ये मार्गस्थास्तस्य वै मृताः ।
तेऽपि मुक्ता भवन्त्येव नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ २२॥

English
The devotees of Kedareśa who die on the way are released from rebirth. No doubt need be entertained in this respect. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा तत्र प्रीतियुक्तः केदारेशं प्रपूज्य च ।
तत्रत्यमुदकं पीत्वा पुनर्जन्म न विन्दति ॥ २३॥

English
Going there, with pleasure, worshipping Kedareśa and drinking the water there a person is released from re-birth. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
खण्डेऽस्मिन् भारते विप्रा नरनारायणेश्वरः ।
केदारेशः प्रपूज्यश्च सर्वैर्जीवैः सुभक्तितः ॥ २४॥

English
O brahmins, in this Bharata country people should worship with devotion Nara-Narayaneśvara and Kedareśa. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य खण्डस्य स स्वामी सर्वेशोऽपि विशेषतः ।
सर्वकामप्रदः शम्भुः केदाराख्यो न संशयः ॥ २५॥

English
Although he is the lord of the universe still he is particularly the lord of Bharata. There is no doubt that Siva Kedara is the bestower of all desires. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद्वचः समाख्यातं यत्पृष्टमृषिसत्तमाः ।
श्रुत्वा पापं हरेत्सर्वं नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ २६॥

English
O excellent sages, I have narrated to you what you have asked for. On hearing this narrative the sins disappear at once. No doubt need be entertained in this regard. (26.)

---
