### **CHAPTER TWENTY - (The greatness of the Jyotirlinga—Bhimeśvara and the havoc perpetrated by Bhimāsura)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि माहात्म्यं भैमशङ्करम् ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण सर्वाभीष्टं लभेन्नरः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Hereafter I shall explain the greatness of Bhimeśvara on hearing which alone a man obtains all desires. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कामरूपाभिधे देशे शङ्करो लोककाम्यया ।
अवतीर्णः स्वयं साक्षात्कल्याणसुखभाजनम् ॥ २॥

English
With a desire to bless the worlds Śiva himself, the receptacle of welfare and happiness, took his incarnation in Kāmarūpa.[151] (2.)

---

Sanskrit
यदर्थमवतीर्णोऽसौ शङ्करो लोकशङ्करः ।
श‍ृणुतादरतस्तच्च कथयामि मुनीश्वराः ॥ ३॥

English
O great sages, I shall tell you why Siva, the benefactor of the people, took the incarnation. Please listen with respect. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमो नाम महोवीर्यो राक्षसोऽभूत्पुरा द्विजाः ।
दुःखदः सर्वभूतानां धर्मध्वंसकरः सदा ॥ ४॥

English
O brahmins, formerly there was a Raksasa of great virility named Bhima who harassed all living beings and who spoiled virtuous rites always. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भकर्णात्समुत्पन्नः कर्कट्यां सुमहाबलः ।
सह्ये च पर्वते सोऽपि मात्रावासं चकार ह ॥ ५॥

English
This powerful demon was born of Karkati from Kumbhakarna. He took up residence on the Sahya[152] mountain along with his mother. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भकर्णे च रामेण हते लोकभयङ्करे ।
राक्षसी पुत्रसंयुक्ता सह्येऽतिष्ठत्स्वयं तदा ॥ ६॥

English
When Kumbhakarna, the terror of the worlds, was killed by Rama, the ogress remained on the Sahya alone in the company of her son. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स बाल एकदा भीमः कर्कटीं मातरं द्विजाः ।
पप्रच्छ च खलो लोकदुःखदो भीमविक्रमः ॥ ७॥

English
O brahmins, once Bhima, the wicked tormentor of the worlds, of terrible exploits, while yet a boy asked his mother Karkati. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
भीम उवाच ।
मातर्मे कः पिता कुत्र कथं वैकाकिनी स्थिता ।
ज्ञातुमिच्छामि तत्सर्वं यथार्थं त्वं वदाधुना ॥ ८॥

English
Bhima said:—  O mother, who is my father? Where is he? How is it that you are living alone? I wish to know everything. Please tell me the truth. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं पृष्टा तदा तेन पुत्रेण राक्षसी च सा ।
उवाच पुत्रं सा दुष्टा श्रूयतां कथयाम्यहम् ॥ ९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Asked thus by her son the defiled ogress spoke to her son. Listen, I shall tell you. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्कट्युवाच ।
पिता ते कुम्भकर्णश्च रावणानुज एव च ।
रामेण मारितः सोऽयं भ्रात्रा सह महाबलः ॥ १०॥

English
Karkati said:— Your father was Kumbhakarna the younger brother of Ravaṇa. That powerful demon was killed by Rama along with his brother. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रागतः कदाचिद्वै कुम्भकर्णः स राक्षसः ।
मद्भोगं कृतवांस्तात प्रसह्य बलवान्पुरा ॥ ११॥

English
Once that powerful demon Kumbhakarna had been here. He caught me forcefully and enjoyed me. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
लङ्कां स गतवान् मां च त्यक्त्वात्रैव महाबलः ।
मया न दृष्टा सा लङ्का ह्यत्रैव निवसाम्यहम् ॥ १२॥

English
That powerful demon left me here itself and returned to Laṅkā. That Laṅkā was not visited by me. I have been staying here alone. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पिता मे कर्कटो नाम माता मे पुष्कसी मता ।
भर्ता मम विराधो हि रामेण निहतः पुरा ॥ १३॥

English
My father was Karkaṭa and my mother Puṣkasī. My husband Virādha was killed by Rāma. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पित्रोः पार्श्वे स्थिता चाहं निहते स्वामिनि प्रिये ।
पितरौ मे मृतौ चात्र ऋषिणा भस्मसात्कृतौ ॥ १४॥

English
When my husband was killed I stayed with my parents. My parents are dead now, reduced to ashes by a certain sage. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्षणार्थं गतौ तत्र क्रुद्धेन सुमहात्मना ।
सुतीक्ष्णेन सुतपसागस्त्यशिष्येण वै तदा ॥ १५॥

English
When they had been there for food, the infuriated disciple of Agastya, the noble Sutikṣṇa of good penance reduced them to ashes. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
साहमेकाकिनी जाता दुःखिता पर्वते पुरा ।
निवसामि स्म दुःखार्ता निरालम्बा निराश्रया ॥ १६॥

English
That was why I happened to be alone on this mountain, miserable and helpless. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वं च समुत्पन्नो महाबलपराक्रमः ।
अवलम्ब्य पुनस्त्वां च कालक्षेपं करोम्यहम् ॥ १७॥

English
It was during this period that Kumbhakarṇa the younger brother of Rāvaṇa came here and had his sexual intercourse with me. He left me alone and went away. (17.)

---

English
You were born after that. You have great strength and exploit. Depending on you I spend my days here. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्या भीमो भीमपराक्रमः ।
क्रुद्धश्च चिन्तयामास किं करोमि हरिं प्रति ॥ १९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing this, Bhīma of terrible exploits was angry and thought “What am I to do against Viṣṇu ? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पितानेन हतो मे हि तथा मातामहा अपि ।
विराधश्च हतोऽनेन दुःखं बहुतरं कृतम् ॥ २०॥

English
My father was killed by him and so my grand parents. Virādha too was killed by him. Many miseries have been inflicted on us. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पुत्रोऽहं भवेयं चेद्धरिं तं पीडयाम्यहम् ।
इति कृत्वा मतिं भीमस्तपस्तप्तुं महद्ययौ ॥ २१॥

English
If I am the true son of my mother I shall surely harass Viṣṇu. Thinking thus Bhīma went to perform a great penance. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माणं च समुद्दिश्य वर्षाणां च सहस्रकम् ।
मनसा ध्यानमाश्रित्य तपश्चक्रे महत्तदा ॥ २२॥

English
With Brahmā as the goal of his meditation he performed a great penance for a thousand years. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ऊर्ध्वबाहुश्चैकपादः सूर्ये दृष्टिं दधत्पुरा ।
संस्थितः स बभूवाथ भीमो राक्षसपुत्रकः ॥ २३॥

English
He kept his arms lifted up; he stood on a single foot. He fixed his vision at the sun. Bhīma the son of Kumbhakarṇa remained like this. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरसस्तस्य सञ्जातं तेजः परमदारुणम् ।
तेन दग्धास्तदा देवा ब्रह्माणं शरणं ययुः ॥ २४॥

English
A terrible fiery splendour arose from his head. Scorched by it the gods sought refuge in Brahmā. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य वेधसं भक्त्या तुष्टुवुर्विविधैः स्तवैः ।
दुःखं निवेदयाञ्चक्रुर्ब्रह्मणे ते सवासवाः ॥ २५॥

English
The gods including Indra bowed to Brahmā with devotion and eulogised him with different hymns. They mentioned their miserable plight to him. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
ब्रह्मन्वै रक्षसस्तेजो लोकान्पीडितुमुद्यतम् ।
यत्प्रार्थ्यते च दुष्टेन तत्त्वं देहि वरं विधे ॥ २६॥

English
The gods said:—  O Brahmā, the fiery splendour of the demon has risen to harass the worlds. O Brahmā, grant the boon to this wicked Rākṣasa. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नो चेदद्य वयं दग्धास्तीव्रतत्तेजसा पुनः ।
यास्यामः सङ्क्षयं सर्वे तस्मात्तं देहि प्रार्थितम् ॥ २७॥

English
If not, we shall be scorched by his terrible fiery splendour and court ruin. Hence grant him his prayer. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति तेषां वचः श्रुत्वा ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ।
जगाम च वरं दातुं वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ २८॥

English
Sūta said:-  On hearing their words, Brahmā the grandfather of the universe went there to grant him the boon and spoke thus. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूहि यत्ते मनसि वर्तते ।
इति श्रुत्वा विधेर्वाक्यमब्रवीद्राक्षसो हि सः ॥ २९॥

English
Brahmā said:—  "I am delighted with you. Mention the boon you desire in your mind." On hearing the words of Brahmā the Rākṣasa said. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
भीम उवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश यदि देयो वरस्त्वया ।
अतुलं च बलं मेऽद्य देहि त्वं कमलासन ॥ ३०॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if a boon is to be granted by you, O Lotus-seated one, grant me unequalled strength. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तु नमश्चक्रे ब्रह्मणे स हि राक्षसः ।
ब्रह्मा चापि तदा तस्मै वरं दत्त्वा गृहं ययौ ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this, the Rākṣasa made obeisance to Brahmā. After granting him the boon, Brahmā returned to his abode. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
राक्षसो गृहमागत्य ब्रह्माप्तातिबलस्तदा ।
मातरं प्रणिपत्याशु स भीमः प्राह गर्ववान् ॥ ३२॥

English
The Rākṣasa Bhīma who acquired great strength from Brahmā returned to his house puffed up with pride. After bowing to his mother he said. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
भीम उवाच ।
पश्य मातर्बलं मेऽद्य करोमि प्रलयं महत् ।
देवानां शक्रमुख्यानां हरेर्वै तत्सहायिनः ॥ ३३॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O mother, observe my strength. I shall work up a havoc among the gods including their chief Indra and also Viṣṇu who goes to help them. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा प्रथमं भीमो जिग्ये देवान्सवासवान् ।
स्थानान्निःसारयामास स्वात्स्वात्तान्भीमविक्रमः ॥ ३४॥

English
After saying this, Bhīma of terrible exploit conquered the gods including Indra at the outset and ousted them from their respective positions. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो जिग्ये हरिं युद्धे प्रार्थितं निर्जरैरपि ।
ततो जेतुं रसां दैत्यः प्रारम्भं कृतवान्मुदा ॥ ३५॥

English
Then in a battle he conquered Viṣṇu respected even by the gods. Then joyously the Rākṣasa began to conquer the earth. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा सुदक्षिणं तत्र कामरूपेश्वरं प्रभुम् ।
जेतुं गतस्ततस्तेन युद्धमासीद्भयङ्करम् ॥ ३६॥

English
At first he went to conquer Sudakṣiṇa, the king of Kāmarūpa. Then he fought a terrible battle with him. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमोऽथ तं महाराजं प्रभावाद् ब्रह्मणोऽसुरः ।
जिग्ये वरप्रभावेण महावीरं शिवाश्रयम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Thanks to the powers of Brahmā and that of the boon, the Asura Bhima conquered that great king though he was a great hero and had the support of Siva. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
स हि जित्वा ततस्तं च कामरूपेश्वरं प्रभुम् ।
बबन्ध ताडयामास भीमो भीमपराक्रमः ॥ ३८॥

English
After conquering the lord of Kāmarūpa, Bhima of terrible exploit bound him and struck him. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीतं तस्य सर्वस्वं राज्यं सोपस्करं द्विजाः ।
तेन भीमेन दुष्टेन शिवदासस्य भूपतेः ॥ ३९॥

English
O brahmins, all that belonged to that king who was a servant of Siva, including his kingdom and its adjuncts was seized by that wicked Bhima. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा चापि सुधर्मिष्ठः प्रियधर्मो हरप्रियः ।
गृहीतो निगडैस्तेन ह्येकान्ते स्थापितश्च सः ॥ ४०॥

English
The virtuous king, fond of sacred rites, a favourite of Siva, was bound with fetters and imprisoned in an isolated cell. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तेन तदा कृत्वा पार्थिवीं मूर्तिमुत्तमाम् ।
भजनं च शिवस्यैव प्रारब्धं प्रियकाम्यया ॥ ४१॥

English
There the captive monarch made an excellent earthen idol of Siva and started its worship with a desire for pleasant results. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गायाः स्तवनं तेन बहुधा च तदा कृतम् ।
मानसं स्नानकर्मादि कृत्वा शङ्करपूजनम् ॥ ४२॥

English
He eulogised Gaṅgā in various ways. He performed the rites of ablutions and the worship of Siva. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवेन विधानेन चकार नृपसत्तमः ।
तद्ध्यानं च यथा स्याद्वै कृत्वा च विधिपूर्वकम् ॥ ४३॥

English
He performed meditation duly in accordance with the rules prescribed for the worship of earthen idols. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणिपातैस्तथा स्तोत्रैर्मुद्रासनपुरःसरम् ।
कृत्वा हि सकलं तच्च स भेजे शङ्करं मुदा ॥ ४४॥

English
He joyously worshipped Siva after performing ob- esances, prayers and the like with the mystic signs and poses. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाक्षरमयीं विद्यां जजाप प्रणवान्विताम् ।
नान्यत्कार्यं स वै कर्तुं लब्धवानन्तरं तदा ॥ ४५॥

English
He repeated the japas of the five-syllabled mantra with the Pranava prefixed. He did not get any leisure to do any thing else. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पत्नी च तदा साध्वी दक्षिणानाम विश्रुता ।
विधानं पार्थिवं प्रीत्या चकार नृपवल्लभा ॥ ४६॥

English
The king's beloved wife, the chaste lady, famous as Dakṣiṇā performed the worship of the earthen idol with pleasure. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
दम्पती त्वेकभावेन शङ्करं भक्तशङ्करम् ।
भेजाते तत्र तौ नित्यं शिवाराधनतत्परौ ॥ ४७॥

English
The couple, devoutly engaged in the propitiation of Siva worshipped the deity, the benefactor of the devotees with single-mindedness. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
राक्षसो यज्ञकर्मादि वरदर्पविमोहितः ।
लोपयामास तत्सर्वं मह्यं वै दीयतामिति ॥ ४८॥

English
But the Raksasa deluded by the arrogance due to the boon spoiled all sacrificial rites saying, "Let the offerings be made to me." (48.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुसैन्यसमायुक्तो राक्षसानां दुरात्मनाम् ।
चकार वसुधां सर्वां स्ववशे चर्षिसत्तमाः ॥ ४९॥

English
O excellent sages, with a huge army of wicked Raksasas he put the entire earth under his control. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदधर्मं शास्त्रधर्मं स्मृतिधर्मं पुराणजम् ।
लोपयित्वा च तत्सर्वं बुभुजे स्वयमूर्जितः ॥ ५०॥

English
Defiling the Vedic rites and the sacred rituals of Sāstras, Smṛtis and Purānas, the powerful demon enjoyed everything himself. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च पीडितास्तेन सशक्रा ऋषयस्तथा ।
अत्यन्तं दुःखमापन्ना लोकान्निःसारिता द्विजाः ॥ ५१॥

English
The gods including Indra were harassed by him. The sages were subjected to misery, Brahmins were ousted from the world. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
ते ततो विकलाः सर्वे सवासवसुरर्षयः ।
ब्रह्मविष्णू पुरोधाय शङ्करं शरणं ययुः ॥ ५२॥

English
Then the dejected gods and sages including Indra, with Brahmā and Viṣṇu at their head, sought refuge in Śiva. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुत्वा स्तोत्रैरनेकैश्च शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम् ।
प्रसन्नं कृतवन्तस्ते महाकोश्यास्तटे शुभे ॥ ५३॥

English
On the auspicious banks of the Mahākosi they delighted Śiva, the benefactor of the worlds after eulogising him with various prayers. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा च पार्थिवीं मूर्तिं पूजयित्वा विधानतः ।
तुष्टुवुर्विविधैः स्तोत्रैर्नमस्कारादिभिः क्रमात् ॥ ५४॥

English
They made the earthen idol and performed worship in accordance with the rules and eulogised him with different prayers and obeisances duly. (54.)

Sanskrit
एवं स्तुतस्तदा शम्भुर्देवानां स्तवनादिभिः ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा तान्सुरानिदमब्रवीत् ॥ ५५॥

English
Thus eulogised by means of prayers, by the gods, Śiva was much delighted and spoke to the gods. (55.)

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
हे हरे हे विधे देवा ऋषयश्चाखिला अहम् ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूत किं कार्यं करवाणि वः ॥ ५६॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Viṣṇu, O Brahmā, O gods, O sages, I am delighted, mention the boon you wish to have. What shall I do for you? (56.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ते च तदा तेन शिवेन वचने द्विजाः ।
सुप्रणम्य करौ बध्वा देवा ऊचुः शिवं तदा ॥ ५७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, when these words were mentioned by Śiva, the gods spoke to Śiva after bowing to him with palms joined in reverence. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
सर्वं जानासि देवेश सर्वेषां मनसि स्थितम् ।
अन्तर्यामी च सर्वस्य नाज्ञातं विद्यते तव ॥ ५८॥

English
The gods said:—  O lord of gods, you know everything in the mind of others. You are the immanent soul. There is nothing unknown to you. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तथापि श्रूयतां नाथ स्वदुःखं ब्रूमहे वयम् ।
त्वदाज्ञया महादेव कृपादृष्ट्या विलोकय ॥ ५९॥

English
Still at your behest, O lord, we shall mention our grief. Be pleased to hear. O lord, look at us with your merciful eye. (59.)

Sanskrit
राक्षसः कर्कटीपुत्रः कुम्भकर्णोद्भवो बली ।
पीडयत्यनिशं देवान्ब्रह्मदत्तवरोर्जितः ॥ ६०॥

English
The Rākṣasa, the powerful son of Karkaṭī, begot by Kumbhakarṇa, harasses the gods always, with his strength enhanced by the boon granted by Brahmā. (60.)

Sanskrit
तमिमं जहि भीमाह्वं राक्षसं दुःखदायकम् ।
कृपां कुरु महेशान विलम्बं न कुरु प्रभो ॥ ६१॥

English
Please kill this Rākṣasa who causes much misery. O great lord, be merciful. O lord, do not delay. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तु सुरैः सर्वैः शम्भुर्वै भक्तवत्सलः ।
वधं तस्य करिष्यामीत्युक्त्वा देवांस्ततोऽब्रवीत् ॥ ६२॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus requested by the gods, Śiva favourably disposed to his devotees said—"I shall slay him." He then spoke to the gods. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भुरुवाच ।
कामरूपेश्वरो राजा मदीयो भक्त उत्तमः ।
तस्मै ब्रूतेति वै देवाः कार्यं शीघ्रं भविष्यति ॥ ६३॥

English
Śiva said:—  The king of Kāmarūpa is my excellent devotee. O gods, tell him that his task will soon be accomplished by me. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदक्षिण महाराज कामरूपेश्वर प्रभो ।
मद्भक्तस्त्वं विशेषेण कुरु मद्भजनं रतेः ॥ ६४॥

English
The great king Sudakṣiṇa, lord of Kāmarūpa, is a special devotee of mine. Let him continue my worship interestedly. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यं भीमाह्वयं दुष्टं ब्रह्मप्राप्तवरोर्जितम् ।
हनिष्यामि न सन्देहस्त्वत्तिरस्कारकारिणम् ॥ ६५॥

English
I shall kill Bhima who is wicked, whose power is enhanced by the boon granted by Brahma and who has insulted you. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अथ ते निर्जराः सर्वे तत्र गत्वा मुदान्विताः ।
तस्मै महानृपायोचुर्यदुक्तं शम्भुना च तत् ॥ ६६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Then the gods went there. The delighted gods informed the great king what lord Siva had said. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
तमित्युक्त्वा च वै देवा आनन्दं परमं गताः ।
महर्षयश्च ते सर्वे ययुः शीघ्रं निजाश्रमान् ॥ ६७॥

English
After informing him thus, the gods were highly delighted. The sages then returned to their respective abodes. (67.)
