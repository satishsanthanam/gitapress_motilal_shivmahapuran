### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - (The origin of the Jyotirlinga Bhīmeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
शिवोऽपि च गणैः सार्धं जगाम हितकाम्यया ।
स्वभक्तनिकटं गुप्तस्तस्थौ रक्षार्थमादरात् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* With a desire to accord benefit to him Siva stayed with his devotee along with his Ganas. In order to protect him he remained concealed there. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र कामरूपेश्वरेण च ।
अत्यन्तं ध्यानमारब्धं पार्थिवस्य पुरस्तदा ॥ २॥

English
In the meantime a great meditation was started by the lord of Kāmarūpa in front of the earthen idol of Siva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
केनचित्तत्र गत्वा च राक्षसाय निवेदितम् ।
राजा किञ्चित्करोत्येवं त्वदर्थं ह्याभिचारिकम् ॥ ३॥

English
Then somebody informed the Raksasa, "The king is performing a black magic against you." (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
राक्षसः स च तच्छ्रुत्वा क्रुद्धस्तद् हननेच्छया ।
गृहीत्वा करवालं च जगाम नृपतिं प्रति ॥ ४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing it the Raksasa was infuriated. With a desire to kill him he seized a sword and rushed at the king. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा राक्षसस्तत्र पार्थिवादि स्थितं च यत् ।
तदर्थं तत्स्वरूपं च दृष्ट्वा किञ्चित्करोत्यसौ ॥ ५॥

अत एनं बलादद्य हन्मि सोपस्करं नृपम् ।
विचार्येति महाक्रुद्धो राक्षसः प्राह तं नृपम् ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing the earthen idol of Siva, the Raksasa thought “Contemplating on this idol he is doing something. Hence I shall kill him along with his adjuncts.” Thinking thus the extremely infuriated Rākṣasa spoke thus. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
भीम उवाच ।
रे रे पार्थिव दुष्टात्मन् क्रियते किं त्वयाधुना ।
सत्यं वद न हन्यां त्वामन्यथा हन्मि निश्चितम् ॥ ७॥

English
Bhīma said:—  O wicked king, what are you doing here ? Tell me the truth. I shall not kill you then. Otherwise I will certainly kill you. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य कामरूपेश्वरश्च सः ।
मनसीति चिचिन्ताशु शिवविश्वासपूरितः ॥ ८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing his words, the lord of Kāmarūpa full of faith in Śiva thought within his mind thus. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
भविष्यं यद्भवत्येव नास्ति तस्य निवर्तकः ।
प्रारब्धाधीनमेवात्र प्रारब्धः स शिवः स्मृतः ॥ ९॥

English
What is destined to happen will happen. There is nothing to prevent it. Everything is determined by our own previous action and Śiva is the dispenser of the result. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपालुः शङ्करश्चात्र पार्थिवे वर्तते ध्रुवम् ।
मदर्थं न करोतीह कुतः कोऽयं च राक्षसः ॥ १०॥

English
The merciful lord is certainly present in this earthen idol. He is carrying out my task. Who is this Rākṣasa ? Whence has he come ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वानुरूपां प्रतिज्ञां स सत्यं चैव करिष्यति ।
सत्यप्रतिज्ञो भगवान् शिवश्चेति श्रुतौ श्रुतः ॥ ११॥

English
Lord Śiva is reputed in the Vedas as true to his promise. He carries out his promises in the interest of his devotees. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
मम भक्तं यदा कश्चित्पीडयत्यतिदारुणः ।
तदाहं तस्य रक्षार्थं दुष्टं हन्मि न संशयः ॥ १२॥

English
The promise is “If and when any terrible person harasses my devotee I shall kill that wicked person in order to save him. There is no doubt in this.” (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं धैर्यं समालम्ब्य ध्यात्वा देवं च शङ्करम् ।
प्रार्थयामास सद्भक्त्या मनसैव रसेश्वरः ॥ १३॥

English
Thus emboldened and meditating on lord Śiva the king reminded him of the promise, mentally and devotedly. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वदीयोऽस्मि महाराज यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ।
सत्यं च वचनं ह्यत्र ब्रवीमि कुरु मे हितम् ॥ १४॥

English
“O lord, I am yours. Do as you wish. I will tell him the truth. Do what is beneficial to me,” (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं मनसि स ध्यात्वा सत्यपाशेन यन्त्रितः ।
प्राह सत्यं वचो राजा राक्षसं चावमानयन् ॥ १५॥

English
Thus meditating in his mind, the king bound by the noose of truth spoke the truth slighting the Rākṣasa. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
नृप उवाच ।
भजामि शङ्करं देवं स्वभक्तपरिपालकम् ।
चराचराणां सर्वेषामीश्वरं निर्विकारकम् ॥ १६॥

English
The king said:—  I am worshipping lord Śiva who protects his devotees, who is the lord of all beings—mobile or immobile and who is free from aberrations. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति तस्य वचः श्रुत्वा कामरूपेश्वरस्य सः ।
क्रोधेन प्रचलद्गात्रो भीमो वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १७॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing these words of the king of Kāmarūpa, Bhīma spoke thunderingly, his body shaking with rage. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
भीम उवाच ।
शङ्करस्ते मया ज्ञातः किं करिष्यति वै मम ।
यो मे पितृव्यकेनैव स्थापितः किङ्करो यथा ॥ १८॥

English
Bhīma said:—  Your Śiva is known to me? What harm can he do to me? He was kept as a servant by my uncle. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्बलं हि समाश्रित्य विजेतुं त्वं समीहसे ।
तर्हि त्वया जितं सर्वं नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १९॥

English
You wish to conquer me depending on his strength. Then you have conquered everything. indeed. No need for any hesitation. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यावन्मया न दृष्टो हि शङ्करस्त्वत्प्रपालकः ।
तावत्त्वं स्वामिनं मत्वा सेवसे नान्यथा क्वचित् ॥ २०॥

English
As long as Siva your protector is not seen by me you can serve him as your lord and not otherwise. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मया दृष्टे च तत्सर्वं स्फुटं स्यात्सर्वथा नृप ।
तस्मात्त्वं वै शिवस्येदं रूपं दूरतरं कुरु ॥ २१॥

English
If he is seen by me, O king, everything will be clear in every respect. Hence you remove all these idols of Siva. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा हि भयं तेऽद्य भविष्यति न संशयः ।
स्वामिनस्ते करं तीक्ष्णं दास्येऽहं भीमविक्रमः ॥ २२॥

English
Otherwise great terror will befall you. There is no doubt about it. I am of terrible exploit and I shall lay my hands heavily on your lord. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा कामरूपेश्वरो नृपः ।
दृढं शङ्करविश्वासो द्रुतं वाक्यमुवाच तम् ॥ २३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing his words, the king of Kāmarūpa who had steady faith in Śiva immediately spoke to the Rākṣasa. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ।
अहं च पामरो दुष्टो न मोक्ष्ये शङ्करं पुनः ।
सर्वोत्कृष्टश्च मे स्वामी न मां मुञ्चति कर्हिचित् ॥ २४॥

English
The king said:—  I am a wicked ignorant brute in your eyes but I shall never forsake Śiva. My lord who is the most excellent of all will never forsake me. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं वचस्तदा श्रुत्वा तस्य राज्ञः शिवात्मनः ।
तं प्रहस्य द्रुतं भीमो भूपतिं राक्षसोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २५॥

English
Sūta said :—  On hearing the words of the king with his soul dedicated to Śiva, the Rākṣasa laughed and spoke to the king immediately. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भीम उवाच ।
मत्तो भिक्षयते नित्यं स किं जानाति स्वाकृतिम् ।
योगिनां का च निष्ठा वै भक्तानां प्रतिपालने ॥ २६॥

इति कृत्वा मतिं त्वं च दूरतो भव सर्वथा ।
अहं च तव स स्वामी युद्धं वै करवावहे ॥ २७॥

English
Bhīma said:—  “He is intoxicated. He begs everyday. What does he know about his features? What steadfastness can a Yogi have in affording protection to his devotees?” Think thus and keep aloof in every respect. Your lord and I shall proceed with our fight. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः स नृपश्रेष्ठः शम्भुभक्तो दृढव्रतः ।
प्रत्युवाचाभयो भीमं दुःखदं जगतां सदा ॥ २८॥

English
Sūta said :—  Thus ridiculed, the excellent king, a devotee of Śiva, steadfast in his rites, spoke fearlessly to Bhīma who caused misery to the people. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ।
श‍ृणु राक्षस दुष्टात्मन्मया कर्तुं न शक्यते ।
त्वया विक्रियते तर्हि कुतस्त्वं शक्तिमानसि ॥ २९॥

English
The king said:—  O wicked Rākṣasa, listen. I shall not comply with your threats. If you go astray wherefore are you powerful? (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः सैन्यमादाय राजानं परिभर्त्स्य तम् ।
करालं करवालं च पार्थिवे प्राक्षिपत्तदा ॥ ३०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus addressed by the king, the demon rebuked him and hurled his terrible sword at the earthen idol. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्य त्वं स्वामिनोऽद्यैव बलं भक्तसुखावहम् ।
इत्युवाच विहस्यैव राक्षसैः स महाबलः ॥ ३१॥

English
“See now itself your lord’s strength pleasing to the devotees.” Saying this, the powerful demon laughed in the company of the Rākṣasas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
करवालः पार्थिवं च यावत्स्पृशति नो द्विजाः ।
तावच्च पार्थिवात्तस्मादाविरासीत्स्वयं हरः ॥ ३२॥

English
O brahmins, no sooner did the sword touch the earthen idol than Śiva manifested himself from the earthen idol. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्य भीमेश्वरोऽहं च रक्षार्थं प्रकटोऽभवम् ।
मम पूर्वव्रतं ह्येतद्रक्ष्यो भक्तो मया सदा ॥ ३३॥

English
“See, I am Bhīmeśvara. I have revealed myself to protect my devotee. This is my old rite that the devotee should be protected by me always. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मात्पश्य मे शीघ्रं बलं भक्तसुखावहम् ।
इत्युक्त्वा स पिनाकेन करवालो द्विधा कृतः ॥ ३४॥

English
From this alone see soon my strength that is pleasing to the devotees.” After saying this he split the sword into two with his Pināka. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्चैव त्रिशूलं स्वं चिक्षिपे तेन रक्षसा ।
तच्छूलं शतधा नीतमपि दुष्टस्य शम्भुना ॥ ३५॥

English
His spear was then hurled by the Rākṣasa. That spear too of the wicked Rākṣasa was split into a hundred pieces by Śiva. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः शक्तिश्च निःक्षिप्ता तेन शम्भूपरि द्विजाः ।
शम्भुना सापि बाणैः स्वैर्लक्षधा च कृता द्रुतम् ॥ ३६॥

English
O brahmins, then a lance was hurled at Śiva by him. It too was split into a hundred-thousand pieces with his arrows by Śiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पट्टिशश्च ततस्तेन निःक्षिप्तो हि शिवोपरि ।
शिवेन स त्रिशूलेन तिलशश्च कृतं क्षणात् ॥ ३७॥

English
Then a huge iron club was hurled at Śiva by him. Within a trice, it too was reduced to small pieces like gingelly seeds by Śiva with his trident. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवगणानां च राक्षसानां परस्परम् ।
युद्धमासीत्तदा घोरं पश्यतां दुःखकावहम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Then a terrible fight ensued between the attendants of Śiva and the Rākṣasa. It frightened all the onlookers with distress. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च पृथिवी सर्वा व्याकुला चाभवत्क्षणात् ।
समुद्राश्च तदा सर्वे चुक्षुभुः समहीधराः ॥ ३९॥

English
Within a trice, the entire earth became agitated. The oceans and the mountains became excited. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च ऋषयः सर्वे बभूवुर्विकला अति ।
ऊचुः परस्परं चेति व्यर्थं वै प्रार्थितः शिवः ॥ ४०॥

English
The god and the sages were much distressed. They said to one another. “In vain did we beseech Śiva.” (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदश्च समागत्य शङ्करं दुःखदाहकम् ।
प्रार्थयामास तत्रैव साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तकः ॥ ४१॥

English
Nārada came there and requested Śiva, the destroyer of miseries with palms joined in reverence and head bent down. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
क्षम्यतां क्षम्यतां नाथ त्वया विभ्रमकारक ।
तृणे कश्च कुठारो वै हन्यतां शीघ्रमेव हि ॥ ४२॥

English
Nārada said:—  O lord, forgive, O cause of fierce agitation. Why shall a dagger be thrust to cut a blade of grass, Let the demon be killed soon. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सम्प्रार्थितः शम्भुः सर्वान् रक्षोगणान्प्रभुः ।
हुङ्कारेणैव चास्त्रेण भस्मसात्कृतवांस्तदा ॥ ४३॥

English
Thus requested, lord Siva reduced the Raksasa to ashes with only a Hum sound for his weapon. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे ते राक्षसा दग्धाः शङ्करेण क्षणं मुने ।
बभूवुस्तत्र सर्वेषां देवानां पश्यतां द्रुतम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Even as all the gods were watching, O sage, the Raksasas were burnt down by Siva in a trice. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
दावानलगतो वह्निर्यथा च वनमादहेत् ।
तथा शिवेन क्रुद्धेन राक्षसानां बलं क्षणात् ॥ ४५॥

English
Just as the conflagration consumes the forest so did the fury of Siva destroy the army of the Raksasas in a trice. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमस्यैव च किं भस्म न ज्ञातं केनचित्तदा ।
परिवारयुतो दग्धो नाम न श्रूयते क्वचित् ॥ ४६॥

English
None could see even the ashes of Bhima. He was burnt down along with his followers. There was no trace of him to be seen. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवस्य कृपया शान्तिं प्राप्ता मुनीश्वराः ।
देवाः सर्वे च शक्राद्याः स्वास्थ्यं प्रापाखिलं जगत् ॥ ४७॥

English
The flame of the fury of Siva spread from forest to forest. The ashes of the Raksasas spread everywhere in the forest. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रोधज्वाला महेशस्य निःससार वनाद्वनम् ।
राक्षसानां च तद्भस्म सर्वं व्याप्तं वनेऽखिलम् ॥ ४८॥

English
The fiery flame of the anger of the great lord Siva came out and spread from forest to forest. The ashes of the Raksasas spread throughout the forest. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चौषधयो जाता नानाकार्यकरास्तथा ।
रूपान्तरं ततो नॄणां भवेद्वेषान्तरं तथा ॥ ४९॥

English
From the ashes different herbs cropped up effectively in various ways. Men could assume different forms and features through the ashes. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतप्रेतपिशाचादि दूरतश्च ततो व्रजेत् ।
तन्न कार्यं च यच्चैव ततो न भवति द्विजाः ॥ ५०॥

English
Evil spirits, ghosts are removed far by the ashes O brahmins, there is no task which cannot be carried out through it. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
तैर्देवैः प्रार्थितः शम्भुर्मुनिभिश्च विशेषतः ।
स्थातव्यं स्वामिना ह्यत्र लोकानां सुखहेतवे ॥ ५१॥

English
Then Siva was implored by the gods and sages "O lord, you should stay here itself for the happiness of the people. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
अयं वै कुत्सितो देश अयोध्यालोकदुःखदः ।
भवन्तं च तदा दृष्ट्वा कल्याणं सम्भविष्यति ॥ ५२॥

English
This despicable land causing distress to the people of Ayodhyā will yield welfare to the people at your very sight. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
भीमशङ्करनामा त्वं भविता सर्वसाधकः ।
एतल्लिङ्गं सदा पूज्यं सर्वापद्विनिवारकम् ॥ ५३॥

English
You will be known as Bhimaśaṅkara—a phallic image which shall be worshipped always. It will avert mishaps and be conducive to the attainment of all desires. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं प्रार्थितः शम्भुर्लोकानां हितकारकः ।
तत्रैवास्थितवान्प्रीत्या स्वतन्त्रो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ५४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus requested, Śiva the benefactor of the worlds, the lord favourably disposed towards his devotees remained there itself. (54.)
