### **CHAPTER TWENTY-TWO - (The greatness of Viśveśvara, the arrival of Rudra at Kāśī)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि श्रूयतामृषिसत्तमाः ।
विश्वेश्वरस्य माहात्म्यं महापातकनाशनम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O excellent sages, hereafter I shall narrate the greatness of Viśveśvara, destructive of great sins. May it be heard. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
यदिदं दृश्यते किञ्चिज्जगत्यां वस्तुमात्रकम् ।
चिदानन्दस्वरूपं च निर्विकारं सनातनम् ॥ २॥

तस्यैव कैवल्यरतेर्द्वितीयेच्छा ततोऽभवत् ।
स एव सगुणो जातः शिव इत्यभिधीयते ॥ ३॥

English
That which is seen as the substantive, as an entity in the form of knowledge and bliss, eternal and free from aberration, desired for a second. That second, of the tree of salvation, was possessed of attributes. It is named Śiva. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव हि द्विधा जातः पुंस्त्रीरूपप्रभेदतः ।
यः पुमान्स शिवः ख्यातः स्त्री शक्तिः सा हि कथ्यते ॥ ४॥

English
That split itself into two in the male and the female forms. The male is famous as Śiva and the female is known as Śakti. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
चिदानन्दस्वरूपाभ्यां पुरुषावपि निर्मितौ ॥ ५॥

अदृष्टाभ्यां तदा ताभ्यां स्वभावान्मुनिसत्तमाः ।
तावदृष्ट्वा तदा तौ च स्वमातृपितरौ द्विजाः ॥ ६॥

English
O great sages, the unseen Cit and Ānanda created Prakṛti and Puruṣa. O brahmins, on seeing their parents, Prakṛti and Puruṣa were plunged in doubt. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
महासंशयमापन्नौ प्रकृतिः पुरुषश्च तौ ।
तदा वाणी समुत्पन्ना निर्गुणात्परमात्मनः ।
तपश्चैव प्रकर्तव्यं ततः सृष्टिरनुत्तमा ॥ ७॥

English
Then a voice arose from the great attributeless Ātman—"Penance shall be pursued by you for the generation of excellent creation." (7.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृतिपुरुषावूचतुः ।
तपसस्तु स्थलं नास्ति कुत्रावाभ्यां प्रभोऽधुना ।
स्थित्वा तपः प्रकर्तव्यं तव शासनतः शिव ॥ ८॥

English
Prakṛti and Puruṣa said:—  O lord Śiva, there is no place for penance. Where shall we sit and perform this penance at your behest? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च तेजसः सारं पञ्चक्रोशात्मकं शुभम् ।
सर्वोपकरणैर्युक्तं सुन्दरं नगरं तथा ॥ ९॥

निर्माय प्रेषितं तत्स्वं निर्गुणेन शिवेन च ।
अन्तरिक्षे स्थितं तच्च पुरुषस्य समीपतः ॥ १०॥

English
Then a beautiful city endowed with the requisite articles, the auspicious essence of brilliance extending to five Krośas was created and established by Śiva devoid of attributes, in the firmament near the Puruṣa. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
तदधिष्ठाय हरिणा सृष्टिकामनया ततः ।
बहुकालं तपस्तप्तं तद्ध्यानमवलम्ब्य च ॥ ११॥

English
Occupying it with a desire for creation and meditating, Viṣṇu performed penance for a long time. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रमेण जलधाराश्च विविधाश्चाभवंस्तदा ।
ताभिर्व्याप्तं च तच्छून्यं नान्यत्किञ्चिददृश्यत ॥ १२॥

English
Due to his exertion various currents of water began to flow. The void was pervaded by the water-currents. Nothing else was seen. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च विष्णुना दृष्टं किमेतद् दृश्यतेऽद्भुतम् ।
इत्याश्चर्यं तदा दृष्ट्वा शिरसः कम्पनं कृतम् ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing it and thinking "What is this wonderful thing in sight", Viṣṇu shook his head. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च पतितः कर्णान्मणिश्च पुरतः प्रभोः ।
तद् बभूव महत्तीर्थं नामतो मणिकर्णिका ॥ १४॥

English
Then a jewel fell in front from the ear of the lord. It became the great holy centre named Maṇikarṇikā. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
जलौघे प्लाव्यमाना सा पञ्चक्रोशी यदाभवत् ।
निर्गुणेन शिवेनाशु त्रिशूलेन धृता तदा ॥ १५॥

English
When the holy centre extending to five Krośas began to float on the waters, it was supported on Trident by Śiva devoid of attributes. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुस्तत्रैव सुष्वाप प्रकृत्या स्वस्त्रिया सह ।
तन्नाभिकमलाज्जातो ब्रह्मा शङ्करशासनात् ॥ १६॥

English
Viṣṇu slept there itself along with his consort Prakṛti. At the behest of Śiva, Brahmā was born of his umbilical lotus. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाज्ञां स समासाद्य सृष्टिं चक्रेऽद्भुतां तदा ।
चतुर्द्दशमिता लोका ब्रह्माण्डे यत्र निर्मिताः ॥ १७॥

English
At the behest of Śiva, he evolved a wonderful creation. The fourteen worlds were created in the Cosmic Egg. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
योजनानां च पञ्चाशत्कोटिसङ्ख्याप्रमाणतः ।
ब्रह्माण्डस्यैव विस्तारो मुनिभिः परिकीर्तितः ॥ १८॥

English
The extent of the Cosmic Egg is fifty crores of Yojanas as glorified by the sages. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माण्डे कर्मणा बद्धाः प्राणिनो मां कथं पुनः ।
प्राप्स्यन्तीति विचिन्त्यैतत्पञ्चक्रोशी विमोचिता ॥ १९॥

English
"How will the beings in the Cosmic Egg bound by action attain me otherwise?", the holy centre of Pañcakrośī was released by him after thinking like this. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं च शुभदा लोके कर्मनाशकरी मता ।
मोक्षप्रकाशिका काशी ज्ञानदा मम सुप्रिया ॥ २०॥

English
"This Kāśī is the bestower of auspiciousness in the world. It is destructive of action. It illuminates salvation. It is the bestower of knowledge. It is my great favourite. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अविमुक्तं स्वयं लिङ्गं स्थापितं परमात्मना ।
न कदापि त्वया त्याज्यमिदं क्षेत्रं ममांशक ॥ २१॥

English
The phallic image Avimukta is installed by the great Śiva himself. O you born of my parts, this holy centre should never be abandoned by you." (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा च त्रिशूलात्स्वादवतार्य हरः स्वयम् ।
मोचयामास भुवने मर्त्यलोके हि काशिकाम् ॥ २२॥

English
After saying this, Śiva himself brought Kāśikā from his Trident and released it in the mortal world. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणश्च दिने सा हि न विनश्यति निश्चितम् ।
तदा शिवस्त्रिशूलेन दधाति मुनयश्च ताम् ॥ २३॥

English
At the close of the day of Brahmā (when all other objects perish) it certainly does not perish. Then, O sages, Śiva upholds it by means of his Trident. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्च ब्रह्मणा सृष्टौ कृतायां स्थाप्यते द्विजाः ।
कर्मणां कर्षणाच्चैव काशीति परिपठ्यते ॥ २४॥

English
O brahmins, when the creation is resumed again by Brahmā it is replaced. It is called Kāśī because it pulls out or destroys our actions. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अविमुक्तेश्वरं लिङ्गं काश्यां तिष्ठति सर्वदा ।
मुक्तिदातृ च लोकानां महापातकिनामपि ॥ २५॥

English
The phallic image Avimukteśvara stays in Kāśī always, yielding salvation to the people including even the great sinners. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यत्र प्राप्यते मुक्तिः सारूप्यादिर्मुनीश्वराः ।
अत्रैव प्राप्यते जीवैः सायुज्या मुक्तिरुत्तमा ॥ २६॥

English
O great sages, the Sārūpya$^{152}$ and other types of salvation are attained elsewhere. The excellent type of salvation Sāyujya is attained here alone. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
येषां क्वापि गतिर्नास्ति तेषां वाराणसी पुरी ।
पञ्चक्रोशी महापुण्या हत्याकोटिविनाशनी ॥ २७॥

English
Of those who do not attain salvation elsewhere, the city of Vāranasi is the resort. The meritorious holy centre Pañcakrosi is destructive of sins of crores of slaughter. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अमरा मरणं सर्वे वाञ्छन्तीह परे च के ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदा चैषा सर्वदा शङ्करप्रिया ॥ २८॥

English
The immortal gods too desire death here. What then of others? Thus holy centre yields worldly pleasures and salvation always. It is a favourite resort of Siva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा च श्लाघते चामुं विष्णुः सिद्धाश्च योगिनः ।
मुनयश्च तथैवान्ये त्रिलोकस्था जनाः सदा ॥ २९॥

English
Brahma praises this city. So also do the Siddhas, Yogins, sages and other people in the three worlds and Visnu too. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
काश्याश्च महिमानं वै वक्तुं वर्षशतैरपि ।
शक्नोम्यहं न सर्वं हि यथाशक्ति ब्रुवे ततः ॥ ३०॥

English
I cannot describe adequately and entirely the greatness of Kasi even in hundreds of years. I shall explain to the extent I can. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कैलासस्य पतिर्यो वै ह्यन्तः सत्त्वो बहिस्तमाः ।
कालाग्निर्नामतः ख्यातो निर्गुणो गुणवान्भवः ।
प्रणिपातैरनेकैश्च वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ ३१॥

English
The lord of Kailasa, who is Sattvaic within and Tamasaic without, who is famous in the name of Kalagni and who is the supreme Being with and without attributes spoke after making many obeisances. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्र उवाच ।
विश्वेश्वर महेशान त्वदीयोऽस्मि न संशयः ।
कृपां कुरु महादेव मयि त्वं साम्ब आत्मजे ॥ ३२॥

English
Rudra said:—  O lord of the universe, O great lord, I belong to you undoubtedly. O great god, be merciful to me, your son, O lord, accompanied by mother Parvati. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थातव्यं च सदात्रैव लोकानां हितकाम्यया ।
तारयस्व जगन्नाथ प्रार्थयामि जगत्पते ॥ ३३॥

English
O lord of the universe, you shall stay here for the benefit of the worlds. I request you, O ruler of the universe to save the universe. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अविमुक्तोऽपि दान्तात्मा तं सम्प्रार्थ्य पुनः पुनः ।
नेत्राश्रूणि प्रमुच्यैव प्रीतः प्रोवाच शङ्करम् ॥ ३४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Avimukta, of suppressed and controlled soul, shed tears from his eyes and imploring him again and again spoke to Śiva. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अविमुक्त उवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव कालामयसुभेषज ।
त्वं त्रिलोकपतिः सत्यं सेव्यो ब्रह्माच्युतादिभिः ॥ ३५॥

English
Avimukta said:—  O great lord, O lord of gods, O good panacea for all the evils of time, you are in truth the lord of the three worlds. You are worthy of being served by Brahma, Visnu and others. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
काश्यां पुर्यां त्वया देव राजधानी प्रगृह्यताम् ।
मया ध्यानितया स्थेयमचिन्त्यसुखहेतवे ॥ ३६॥

English
O lord, may you establish your capital in the city of Kasi. For the sake of inconceivable pleasure I shall stay in meditation. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्तिदाता भवानेह कामदश्च न चापरः ।
तस्मात्त्वमुपकाराय तिष्ठोमासहितः सदा ॥ ३७॥

English
You alone are the bestower of liberation and worldly desires. For rendering help to the people you stay here always along with Uma. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवान्भवाब्धेरखिलांस्तारय त्वं सदाशिव ।
भक्तकार्यं कुरु हर प्रार्थयामि पुनःपुनः ॥ ३८॥

English
O Sadasiva, save the living beings from the ocean of worldly existence. O Siva, carry out the task of your devotees. I request you again and again. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं प्रार्थितस्तेन विश्वनाथेन शङ्करः ।
लोकानामुपकारार्थं तस्थौ तत्रापि सर्वराट् ॥ ३९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus requested by Visvanatha, Siva the sovereign ruler of all, stayed there for rendering help to the people. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्दिनं हि समारभ्य हरः काश्यामुपागतः ।
तदारभ्य च सा काशी सर्वश्रेष्ठतराभवत् ॥ ४०॥

English
Kasi became the most excellent centre for all since Siva came to Kasi. (40.)
