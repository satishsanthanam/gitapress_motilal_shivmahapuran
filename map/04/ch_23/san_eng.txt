### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - (The greatness of the Jyotirlinga Kāsi-Viśveśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
एवं वाराणसी पुण्या यदि सूत महापुरी ।
तत्प्रभावं वदास्माकमविमुक्तस्य च प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, if the great city of Vārāṇasī is so meritorious please narrate its greatness to us as well as that of lord Avimukta. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
वक्ष्ये सङ्क्षेपतः सम्यग्वाराणस्याः सुशोभनम् ।
विश्वेश्वरस्य माहात्म्यं श्रूयतां च मुनीश्वराः ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, I shall explain neatly and succinctly the splendid greatness of Vāranasi and Visvesvara. May it be listened to. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्पार्वती देवी शङ्करं परया मुदा ।
लोककामनयापृच्छन्माहात्म्यमविमुक्तयोः ॥ ३॥

English
Once goddess Pārvati asked Śiva with great joy about the greatness of the two Avimuktas—Kasi and Visvesvara, out of desire for the welfare of the worlds. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
अस्य क्षेत्रस्य माहात्म्यं वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषतः ।
ममोपरि कृपां कृत्वा लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ ४॥

English
Pārvatī said:—  It behoves you to explain the greatness of this shrine entirely, taking pity on me and desiring the benefit of the worlds. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
देव्यास्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा देवदेवो जगत्प्रभुः ।
प्रत्युवाच भवानीं तां जीवानां प्रियहेतवे ॥ ५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the words of the goddess, the lord of the gods, the lord of the universe, replied to her for the pleasure of all living beings. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
परमेश्वर उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टं त्वया भद्रे लोकानां सुखदं शुभम् ।
कथयामि यथार्थं वै माहात्म्यमविमुक्तयोः ॥ ६॥

English
Lord Śiva said:—  What, gentle lady, has been asked by you is splendid. It is auspicious and pleasing to the people. I shall explain as a matter of fact the greatness of the two Avimuktas. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं गुह्यतमं क्षेत्रं सदा वाराणसी मम ।
सर्वेषामेव जन्तूना हेतुर्मोक्षस्य सर्वथा ॥ ७॥

English
Vārāṇasi is my mysterious shrine. It is the cause of salvation for the people in every respect. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मिन्सिद्धाः सदा क्षेत्रे मदीयं व्रतमाश्रिताः ।
नानालिङ्गधरा नित्यं मम लोकाभिकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ ८॥

English
In this holy centre, the Siddhas have always taken to my holy rites. They hold different phallic images. They yearn to attain my world. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्यस्यन्ति महायोगं जितात्मानो जितेन्द्रियाः ।
परं पाशुपतं श्रौतं भुक्तिमुक्तिफलप्रदम् ॥ ९॥

English
Those who have conquered themselves and controlled their sense-organs pursue the great Pāśupata Yoga which is explained in the Vedas, yielding worldly pleasures and salvation. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
रोचते मे सदा वासो वाराणस्यां महेश्वरि ।
हेतुना येन सर्वाणि विहाय श‍ृणु तद् ध्रुवम् ॥ १०॥

English
O great goddess, the residence in Vārāṇasi is always appealing to me. Forsaking everything listen to the cause of its greatness. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यो मे भक्तश्च विज्ञानी तावुभौ मुक्तिभागिनौ ।
तीर्थापेक्षा च न तयोर्विहिताविहिते समौ ॥ ११॥

English
The two deserve liberation—he who is my devotee and he who has perfect knowledge. Their is not the dependence on holy centres. They are equanimous in regard to what is prescribed and what is forbidden. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवन्मुक्तौ तु तौ ज्ञेयौ यत्र कुत्रापि वै मृतौ ।
प्राप्नुतो मोक्षमाश्वेव मयोक्तं निश्चितं वचः ॥ १२॥

English
They must be known as liberated souls, no matter where they die. They are sure to attain salvation. This decisive word has been uttered by me. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र तीर्थे विशेषोऽस्त्यविमुक्ताख्ये परोत्तमे ।
श्रूयतां तत्त्वया देवि परशक्ते सुचित्तया ॥ १३॥

English
Here in this excellent holy centre Avimukta, there is one special thing to be noted. O goddess, O great Sakti, please listen attentively. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे वर्णा आश्रमाश्च बालयौवनवार्धकाः ।
अस्यां पुर्यां म्रुताश्चेत्स्युर्मुक्ता एव न संशयः ॥ १४॥

English
People of all castes, of all stages of life, whether children, youths or the aged, if they die in this city, are undoubtedly liberated. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अशुचिश्च शुचिर्वापि कन्या परिणता तथा ।
विधवा वाथ वा वन्ध्या रजोदोषयुतापि वा ॥ १५॥

प्रसूतासंस्कृता कापि यादृशी तादृशी द्विजाः ।
अत्र क्षेत्रे मृता चेत्स्यान्मोक्षभाङ्नात्र संशयः ॥ १६॥

English
O brahmins, whether pure or impure, whether virgin or married, whether widow or barren, whether suffering from menstrual disorders or defects or whether recently delivered of a child, of whatever nature she may be, if she dies at this holy centre she attains salvation. There is no doubt in this. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वेदजश्चाण्डजो वापि ह्युद्भिज्जोऽथ जरायुजः ।
मृतो मोक्षमवाप्नोति यथात्र न तथा क्वचित् ॥ १७॥

English
The sweat-born, (lice, bugs etc.) the egg-born (birds), the germinating ones (plants and trees) and the embryo-born (mammals) these living beings do not attain salvation elsewhere as they do at this place. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानापेक्षा न चात्रैव भक्त्यपेक्षा न वै पुनः ।
कर्मापेक्षा न देव्यत्र दानापेक्षा न चैव हि ॥ १८॥

English
O goddess, there is no dependence on perfect knowledge here, nor that on devotion, nor that on holy rites, nor that on charitable gifts. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
संस्कृत्यपेक्षा नैवात्र ध्यानापेक्षा न कर्हिचित् ।
नामापेक्षार्चनापेक्षा सुजातीनां तथात्र न ॥ १९॥

English
There is no dependence on culture, nor that on meditation at anytime, nor that on repetition of names, nor that on adoration, nor that even on noble nativity. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
मम क्षेत्रे मोक्षदे हि यो वा वसति मानवः ।
यथा तथा मृतः स्याच्चेन्मोक्षमाप्नोति निश्चितम् ॥ २०॥

English
Whoever may be the man staying in my holy centre, whatever may be the manner of his death, if he dies here, he certainly attains salvation. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतन्मम पुरं दिव्यं गुह्याद् गुह्यतरं प्रिये ।
ब्रह्मादयोऽपि जानन्ति माहात्म्यं नास्य पार्वति ॥ २१॥

English
O beloved, this divine city of mine is most mysterious one. O Párvati, even Brahma and others do not know its greatness. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
महत्क्षेत्रमिदं तस्मादविमुक्तमिति स्मृतम् ।
सर्वेभ्यो नैमिषादिभ्यः परं मोक्षप्रदं मृते ॥ २२॥

English
Hence this holy centre is known as Avimukta. It is greater than Naimisa and all other holy centres in yielding salvation to the deceased. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मस्योपनिषत्सत्यं मोक्षस्योपनिषत्समम् ।
क्षेत्रतीर्थोपनिषदमविमुक्तं विदुर्बुधाः ॥ २३॥

English
Truth is the esoteric doctrine of Dharmas. Equanimity is the esoteric doctrine of salvation. Learned men know Avimukta as the esoteric doctrine of all shrines and holy centres. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
कामं भुञ्जन्स्वपन्क्रीडन्कुर्वन्हि विविधाः क्रियाः ।
अविमुक्ते त्यजन्प्राणाञ्जन्तुर्मोक्षाय कल्पते ॥ २४॥

English
Any being, enjoying pleasures at his will, sleeping, sporting or performing different activities, attains salvation if he casts off his life at Avimukta. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा पापसहस्राणि पिशाचत्वं वरं नृणाम् ।
न च क्रतुसहस्रत्वं स्वर्गे काशीं पुरीं विना ॥ २५॥

English
It is far better for men to become ghost after committing thousands of sins and stay at Kasi than to attain heaven after performing a thousand sacrifices but without going to the city of Kasi. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन सेव्यते काशिका पुरी ।
अव्यक्तलिङ्गं मुनिभिर्ध्यायते च सदाशिवः ॥ २६॥

English
Hence the people resort to Kasi with every strenuous effort. The sages meditate on Sadasiva in the form of his phallic image. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यत्फलं समुद्दिश्य तपन्त्यत्र नराः प्रिये ।
तेभ्यश्चाहं प्रयच्छामि सम्यक्तत्तत्फलं ध्रुवम् ॥ २७॥

English
O beloved, I distribute the different fruits among different men who perform penance with those ends in view. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सायुज्यमात्मनः पश्चादीप्सितं स्थानमेव च ।
न कुतश्चित्कर्मबन्धस्त्यजतामत्र वै तनुम् ॥ २८॥

English
No entanglement of actions binds those who cast off their bodies here. Afterwards they realise complete identity with the godhead as their goal. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा देवर्षिभिः सार्धं विष्णुर्वापि दिवाकरः ।
उपासते महात्मानः सर्वे मामिह चापरे ॥ २९॥

English
Brahmā along with the gods and the sages, Viṣṇu, the sun, and all other noble souls perform my worship. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विषयासक्तचित्तोऽपि त्यक्तधर्मरुचिर्नरः ।
इह क्षेत्रे मृतो यो वै संसारं न पुनर्विशेत् ॥ ३०॥

English
He who dies in this holy centre does not get rebirth even if he happens to be a person whose mind is drawn towards sensual pleasures or who has eschewed interest in virtue. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
किं पुनर्निर्ममा धीराः सत्त्वस्था दम्भवर्जिताः ।
कृतिनश्च निरारम्भाः सर्वे ते मयि भाविताः ॥ ३१॥

English
What then, if they are free from attachment, devoid of arrogance, possessd of Sāttvaic nature, blessed ones devoted to me and not initiating evil activities. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
जन्मान्तरसहस्रेषु जन्म योगी समाप्नुयात् ।
तदिहैव परं मोक्षं मरणादधिगच्छति ॥ ३२॥

English
Among thousands of rebirths, a Yogin is born here. Then, dying here, he attains the highest salvation. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र लिङ्गान्यनेकानि भक्तैः संस्थापितानि हि ।
सर्वकामप्रदानीह मोक्षदानि च पार्वति ॥ ३३॥

English
O Pārvatī, there are many phallic forms installed by my devotees. They yield all desires and bestow salvation too. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चक्रोशं चतुर्द्दिक्षु क्षेत्रमेतत्प्रकीर्तितम् ।
समन्ताच्च तथा जन्तोर्मृतिकालेऽमृतप्रदम् ॥ ३४॥

English
This holy centre is glorified as extending to five Kṛṣas in every one of the four directions all round. At the time of death it yields immortal beatitude. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
अपापश्च मृतो यो वै सद्यो मोक्षं समश्नुते ।
सपापश्च मृतो यः स्यात्कायव्यूहान्समश्नुते ॥ ३५॥

English
If a sinless man dies, he attains salvation immediately. If a sinner dies here he assumes multitudes of births. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
यातनां सोऽनुभूयैव पश्चान्मोक्षमवाप्नुयात् ।
पातकं योऽविमुक्ताख्ये क्षेत्रेऽस्मिन्कुरुते ध्रुवम् ॥ ३६॥

भैरवीं यातनां प्राप्य वर्षाणामयुते पुनः ।
ततो मोक्षमवाप्नोति भुक्त्वा पापं च सुन्दरि ॥ ३७॥

English
He attains salvation only after experiencing tortures. He who commits sins in the holy centre of Avimukta, experiences tortures at the hands of Bhairava for ten thousand years and then attains salvation, O beautiful lady. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते च समाख्याता पापाचारे च या गतिः ।
एवं ज्ञात्वा नरः सम्यक्सेवयेदविमुक्तकम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus the course of events in regard to one who commits sins has been narrated to you. A man should realise this and worship Avimukta properly. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतकर्मक्षयो नास्ति कल्पकोटिशतैरपि ।
अवश्यमेव भोक्तव्यं कृतं कर्म शुभाशुभम् ॥ ३९॥

English
An action once performed is never wasted even in hundreds of crores of Kalpas. Good or bad, one must of necessity enjoy the fruit of one's action. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
केवलं चाशुभं कर्म नरकाय भवेदिह ।
शुभं स्वर्गाय जायेत द्वाभ्यां मानुष्यमीरितम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Exclusively inauspicious action is contributory to hellish distress. Exclusively auspicious action is conducive to the attainment of heaven. A mixture of the two is said to be resulting in human birth. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
जन्म सम्यगसम्यक् च न्यूनाधिक्ये भवेदिह ।
उभयोश्च क्षयो मुक्तिर्भवेत्सत्यं हि पार्वति ॥ ४१॥

English
The birth will be good or evil in accordance with the predominance or deficiency of the one or the other. The annihilation of good and evil is liberation. This is the truth, O Pārvatī. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्म च त्रिविधं प्रोक्तं कर्मकाण्डे महेश्वरि ।
सञ्चितं क्रियमाणं च प्रारब्धं चेति बन्धकृत् ॥ ४२॥

English
O great goddess, the action that causes bondage is said to be threefold as mentioned in the Karmakāṇḍa section of the Vedas. It is (1) hoarded (Sañcita) (2) the current (Kriyamāna) and (3) that which has started yielding results (Prārabdha). (42.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वजन्मसमुद्भूतं सञ्चितं समुदाहृतम् ।
भुज्यते च शरीरेण प्रारब्धं परिकीर्तितम् ॥ ४३॥

अनेन जन्मना यच्च क्रियते कर्म साम्प्रतम् ।
शुभाशुभं च देवेशि क्रियमाणं विदुर्बुधाः ॥ ४४॥

English
The actions of the previous births are classified as hoarded. The action the fruit of which is being experienced in the present birth is Prārabdha. O goddess, whether good or bad the action performed in this birth is known as Kriyamāna.$^{153}$ (43-44.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रारब्धकर्मणो भोगात्क्षयश्चैव न चान्यथा ।
उपायेन द्वयोर्नाशः कर्मणोः पूजनादिना ॥ ४५॥

English
The annihilation of the Prārabdha Karman is only through enjoyment and not otherwise. The destruction of the other two types of action is possible through worship alone. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां कर्मणां नाशो नास्ति काशीं पुरीं विना ।
सर्वं च सुलभं तीर्थं दुर्लभा काशिका पुरी ॥ ४६॥

English
Except in Kāśī in no other place can all the actions be wiped off. The other holy centres are easily accessible but the city of Kāśī is difficult of access. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वजन्मकृतं चेद्वै काशीदर्शनमादरात् ।
तदा काशीं च सम्प्राप्य लभेन्मृत्युं न चान्यथा ॥ ४७॥

English
If Kāśī had been visited with devotion in the previous birth, the person in the present birth reaches Kāśī and dies there and not otherwise. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
काशीं प्राप्य नरो यस्तु गङ्गायां स्नानमाचरेत् ।
तदा च क्रियमाणस्य सञ्चितस्यापि सङ्क्षयः ॥ ४८॥

English
If after reaching Kāśī a man takes his ceremonial bath in the Gaṅges, the two types of actions viz. the current and the hoarded are destroyed. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रारब्धं न विना भोगं नश्यतीति सुनिश्चितम् ।
मृतिश्च तस्य सञ्जाता तदा तस्य क्षयो भवेत् ॥ ४९॥

English
It is certain that the Prārabdha is not destroyed except through enjoyment. When that is enjoyed it is also destroyed.

50 If a man visits Kāśī first and commits sin afterwards, the seed of that action takes him again to Kāśī. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सर्वाणि पापानि भस्मसाच्च भवन्ति हि ।
तस्मात्काशीं नरः सेवेत्कर्मनिर्मूलनीं ध्रुवम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Then his sins are reduced to ashes. Hence a man shall resort to Kāśī. That certainly debars Karmans to fructify. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
एकोऽपि ब्राह्मणो येन काश्यां संवासितः प्रिये ।
काशीवासमवाप्यैव ततो मुक्तिं स विन्दति ॥ ५२॥

English
O beloved, if even a single brahmin is afforded shelter and residence at Kāsi, the man who affords shelter and residence, gets the benefit of residence in Kāsi himself and thence he attains salvation. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
काश्यां यो वै मृतश्चैव तस्य जन्म पुनर्न हि ।
समुद्दिश्य प्रयागे च मृतस्य कामनाफलम् ॥ ५३॥

English
If a person dies in Kasi, he is freed from rebirth. If a man dies at Prayaga, his desires for worldly enjoyment and salvation are fulfilled. (53.)
