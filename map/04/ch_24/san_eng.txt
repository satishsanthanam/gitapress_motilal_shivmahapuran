### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - (The greatness of Gautama in the context of the greatness of Trymbakeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रूयतामृषयः श्रेष्ठाः कथां पापप्रणाशिनीम् ।
कथयामि यथा व्यासात्सद्गुरोश्च श्रुता मया ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O excellent sages, listen. I shall tell you the story that dispels sins in the manner I have heard it from Vyāsa, the good preceptor. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा ऋषिवरश्चासीद्गौतमो नाम विश्रुतः ।
अहल्या नाम तस्यासीत्पत्नी परमधार्मिकी ॥ २॥

English
Formerly there was an excellent sage named Gautama. His wife Ahalyā was extremely virtuous. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणस्यां दिशि हि यो गिरिर्ब्रह्मेति संज्ञकः ।
तत्र तेन तपस्तप्तं वर्षाणामयुतं तथा ॥ ३॥

English
A penance extending to ten thousand years was performed by him on the Brahmagiri¹⁵⁵ mountain in the southern quarter. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिच्च ह्यनावृष्टिरभवत्तत्र सुव्रताः ।
वर्षाणां च शतं रौद्री लोका दुःखमुपागताः ॥ ४॥

English
O great ones of good rites, once a terrible drought lasting for a hundred years occurred there. All the people were put to great distress. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्द्रं च पल्लवं न स्म दृश्यते पृथिवीतले ।
कुतो जलं विदृश्येत जीवानां प्राणधारकम् ॥ ५॥

English
Not a single juicy tender sprout was seen on the face of the earth. How could there be a water, the sustainer of living beings. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा ते मुनयश्चैव मनुष्याः पशवस्तथा ।
पक्षिणश्च मृगास्तत्र गताश्चैव दिशो दश ॥ ६॥

English
Then the sages and other men, the beasts, birds and the deer of the forest went to the different quarters. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा चर्षयो विप्राः प्राणायामपरायणाः ।
ध्यानेन च तदा केचित्कालं निन्युः सुदारुणम् ॥ ७॥

English
On seeing the drought, the sages and the brahmins spent the horrible time in meditation and performing Prāṇāyāmas. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमोऽपि स्वयं तत्र वरुणार्थे तपः शुभम् ।
चकार चैव षण्मासं प्राणायामपरायणः ॥ ८॥

English
Gautama himself performed an auspicious penance of Varuṇa for six months engrossed in Prāṇāyāma. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च वरुणस्तस्मै वरं दातुं समागतः ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूहि ददामि च वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥ ९॥

English
Then Varuṇa came there to grant him the boon. He said —“I am delighted. Mention the boon you wish to have. I shall grant it.” (9.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च गौतमस्तं वै वृष्टिं च प्रार्थयत्तदा ।
ततः स वरुणस्तं वै प्रत्युवाच मुनिं द्विजाः ॥ १०॥

English
O brahmins, then Gautama requested him for rain. Then Varuṇa replied to the sage. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
वरुण उवाच ।
देवाज्ञां च समुल्लङ्घ्य कथं कुर्यामहं च ताम् ।
अन्यत्प्रार्थय सुज्ञोऽसि यदहं करवाणि ते ॥ ११॥

English
Varuṇa said:—  How can I countermand the behest of the gods? Please ask for something else. You are a wise man. I shall grant it. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येतद्वचनं तस्य वरुणस्य महात्मनः ।
परोपकारी तच्छ्रुत्वा गौतमो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ १२॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words of Varuṇa the great Ātman, Gautama, eager to help others spoke thus. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश यदि देयो वरो मम ।
यदहं प्रार्थयाम्यद्य कर्तव्यं हि त्वया तथा ॥ १३॥

English
Gautama said :—  O lord of gods, if you are delighted, if a boon is to be granted to me, you shall grant it according to my desire. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यतस्त्वं जलराशीशस्तस्माद्देयं जलं मम ।
अक्षयं सर्वदेवेश दिव्यं नित्यफलप्रदम् ॥ १४॥

English
Since you are the lord of waters, you shall give me water. O lord of gods, you shall give me divine, everlasting water yielding permanent results. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति सम्प्रार्थितस्तेन वरुणो गौतमेन वै ।
उवाच वचनं तस्मै गर्तश्च क्रियतां त्वया ॥ १५॥

English
Sūta said :—  Requested thus by Gautama, Varuna said—"Please dig a ditch here". (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते च कृतस्तेन गर्तो हस्तप्रमाणतः ।
जलेन पूरितस्तेन दिव्येन वरुणेन सः ॥ १६॥

English
When this was said, a ditch of a hand's depth was dug by him. It was filled with divine water by Varuna. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोवाच मुनिं देवो वरुणो हि जलाधिपः ।
गौतमं मुनिशार्दूलं परोपकृतिशालिनम् ॥ १७॥

English
Then lord Varuna the king of waters spoke to Gautama, the excellent sage, eager to help others. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वरुण उवाच ।
अक्षयं च जलं तेऽस्तु तीर्थभूतं महामुने ।
तव नाम्ना च विख्यातं क्षितावेतद्भविष्यति ॥ १८॥

English
Varuṇa said:—  O great sage, let there be a perennial supply of water in this ditch that has become sacred. This will become famous on the earth by your name. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र दत्तं हुतं तप्तं सुराणां यजनं कृतम् ।
पितॄणां च कृतं श्राद्धं सर्वमेवाक्षयं भवेत् ॥ १९॥

English
Charitable gifts made here, rites performed here, penance pursued here, the sacrifices done here for the gods and the Sraddha offered to the manes, everything will be imperishable. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तान्तर्दधे देवः स्तुतस्तेन महर्षिणा ।
गौतमोऽपि सुखं प्राप कृत्वान्योपकृतिं मुनिः ॥ २०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this, the lord eulogised by that sage vanished. After rendering help to others, the sage Gautama too was happy. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
महतो ह्याश्रयः पुंसां महत्त्वायोपजायते ।
महान्तस्तत्स्वरूपं च पश्यन्ति नेतरेऽशुभाः ॥ २१॥

English
Seeking the support of great men is conducive to greatness. Only great men see its real nature, not others, the inauspicious ones. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यादृङ्नरं च सेवेत तादृशं फलमश्नुते ।
महतः सेवयोच्चत्वं क्षुद्रस्य क्षुद्रतां तथा ॥ २२॥

English
Man attains fruits in accordance with the nature of the man one serves. Loftiness is achieved by serving the great and meanness by serving the mean. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहस्य मन्दिरे सेवा मुक्ताफलकरी मता ।
श‍ृगालमन्दिरे सेवा त्वस्थिलाभकरी स्मृता ॥ २३॥

English
Service in the abode of a lion is sure to yield pearls. Service in the hutment of a jackal is profitable only in yielding bones. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तमानां स्वभावोऽयं परदुःखासहिष्णुता ।
स्वयं दुःखं च सम्प्राप्तं मन्यतेऽन्यस्य वार्यते ॥ २४॥

English
This is the nature of the excellent people that they cannot bear others' misery. They consider others' misery their own and try to eradicate it. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
वृक्षाश्च हाटकं चैव चन्दनं चेक्षुकस्तथा ।
एते भुवि परार्थे च दक्षा एवं न केचन ॥ २५॥

English
Trees, gold, sandal paste and the sugarcane exist for the enjoyment of the people. Men similarly competent are very rare in the world. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
दयालुरमदस्पर्श उपकारी जितेन्द्रियः ।
एतैश्च पुण्यस्तम्भैस्तु चतुर्भिर्धार्यते मही ॥ २६॥

English
A kind man, a man unpuffed by arrogance a person habitually helping others and a person with perfect control over his sense-organs, these are the four holy pillars that support the earth. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च गौतमस्तत्र जलं प्राप्य सुदुर्लभम् ।
नित्यनैमित्तिकं कर्म चकार विधिवत्तदा ॥ २७॥

English
Having secured rare water thus, Gautama performed his routine and ceremonial rites duly. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो व्रीहीन् यवांश्चैव नीवारानप्यनेकधा ।
वापयामास तत्रैव हवनार्थं मुनीश्वरः ॥ २८॥

English
Thereafter he sowed different food-grains and bar-leys there itself for the performance of his sacrifice. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
धान्यानि विविधानीह वृक्षाश्च विविधास्तथा ।
पुष्पाणि च फलान्येव ह्यासंस्तत्राप्यनेकशः ॥ २९॥

English
Different kinds of food-grains, trees, flowers and fruits were produced in plenty there. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा ऋषयश्चान्ये तत्रायाताः सहस्रशः ।
पशवः पक्षिणश्चान्ये जीवाश्च बहवोऽगमन् ॥ ३०॥

English
On hearing of it, the sages came there in thousands. Many beasts, birds and other living beings flocked there. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वनं सुन्दरं ह्यासीत्पृथिव्यां मण्डले परम् ।
तदक्षयजलायोगादनावृष्टिर्न दुःखदा ॥ ३१॥

English
The forest became very beautiful, the most excellent on the surface of the world. In view of this imperishable contact the drought was not distressing. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषयोऽपि वने तत्र शुभकर्मपरायणाः ।
वासं चक्रुरनेके च शिष्यभार्यासुतान्विताः ॥ ३२॥

English
The sages in the forest were engaged in auspicious rites. They stayed there along with their disciples, children and womenfolk. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
धान्यानि वापयामासुः कालक्रमणहेतवे ।
आनन्दस्तद्वने ह्यासीत्प्रभावाद्गौतमस्य च ॥ ३३॥

English
They sowed food-grains for their livelihood. There was great joy and bliss in that forest, thanks to the power of Gautama. (33.)
