### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - (The planned arrangement of Gautama)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
कदाचिद्गौतमेनैव जलार्थं प्रेषिता निजाः ।
शिष्यास्तत्र गता भक्त्या कमण्डलुकरा द्विजाः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, once Gautama sent his disciples there for water. Carrying out his orders they reached the spot with the water-pots in their hands. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिष्यान् जलसमीपे तु गतान्दृष्ट्वा न्यषेधयन् ।
जलार्थमागतास्तत्र चर्षिपत्न्योऽप्यनेकशः ॥ २॥

English
Many of the womenfolk who had assembled there for water saw the disciples of Gautama who had come for water. They prevented them from taking water. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिपत्न्यो वयं पूर्वं ग्रहीष्यामो विदूरतः ।
पश्चाच्चैव जलं ग्राह्यमित्येवं पर्यभर्त्सयन् ॥ ३॥

English
"We are the wives of the sages. We shall take water first. You stand far off. You can take water afterwards." Thus they rebuked them. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
परावृत्य तदा तैश्च ऋषिपत्न्यै निवेदितम् ।
सा चापि तान्समादाय समाश्वास्य च तैः स्वयम् ॥ ४॥

जलं नीत्वा ददौ तस्मै गौतमाय तपस्विनी ।
नित्यं निर्वाहयामास जलेन ऋषिसत्तमः ॥ ५॥

English
The disciples returned and informed the wife of the sage Gautama. The wife of the sage took them with her, pacified those women, herself took water and gave it to Gautama. The excellent sage performed the roufine rites with that water. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
ताश्चैवमृषिपत्न्यस्तु क्रुद्धास्तां पर्यभर्त्सयन् ।
परावृत्य गताः सर्वास्तूटजान्कुटिलाशयाः ॥ ६॥

English
The other women, the wives of the sages, became infuriated and rebuked her. They returned to their huts with evil intentions lurking in their minds. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वाम्यग्रे विपरीतं च तद्वृत्तं निखिलं ततः ।
दुष्टाशयाभिः स्त्रीभिश्च ताभिर्वै विनिवेदितम् ॥ ७॥

English
The entire incident was distorted and intimated to their husbands by those evil-minded women. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तासां वचः श्रुत्वा भाविकर्मवशात्तदा ।
गौतमाय च सङ्क्रुद्धाश्चासंस्ते परमर्षयः ॥ ८॥

English
On hearing their words, the great sages became furious with Gautama as it was destined to be. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
विघ्नार्थं गौतमस्यैव नानापूजोपहारकैः ।
गणेशं पूजयामासुः सङ्क्रुद्धास्ते कुबुद्धयः ॥ ९॥

English
The infuriated sages of crooked intellect worshipped Ganeisa with various articles of worship in order to put obstacle in the activity of Gautama. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
आविर्बभूव च तदा प्रसन्नो हि गणेश्वरः ।
उवाच वचनं तत्र भक्ताधीनः फलप्रदः ॥ १०॥

English
The delighted Ganeśvara who is subservient to the devotees and who is the bestower of benefits appeared before them and said. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूत यूयं किं करवाण्यहम् ।
तदीयं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा ऋषयस्तेऽब्रुवंस्तदा ॥ ११॥

English
Ganeśa said:—  “I am pleased with you. Mention the boon you wish to choose. What shall I do?” On hearing his words the sages said then. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
त्वया यदि वरो देयो गौतमः स्वाश्रमाद बहिः ।
निष्काश्यमानो ऋषिभिः परिभर्त्स्य तथा कुरु ॥ १२॥

English
The sages said:—  If the boon is to be given by you, let Gautama be turned out of his hermitage after being rebuked by the sages. Please do so. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
स एवं प्रार्थितस्तैस्तु विहस्य वचनं पुनः ।
प्रोवाचेभमुखः प्रीत्या बोधयंस्तान्सतां गतिः ॥ १३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On being requested thus, the elephant-faced deity,¹⁵⁶ the goal of the good, laughed and said gently enlightening them. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच ।
श्रूयतामृषयः सर्वे युक्तं न क्रियतेऽधुना ।
अपराधं विना तस्मै क्रुध्यतां हानिरेव च ॥ १४॥

English
Ganeśa said:—  May this be heard, O sages. This is not proper. Those who become furious without any offence are sure to incur loss. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
उपस्कृतं पुरा यैस्तु तेभ्यो दुःखं हितं न हि ।
यदा च दीयते दुःखं तदा नाशो भवेदिह ॥ १५॥

English
According misery to the people who had rendered help before is not beneficial. If it is carried out, ruin will surely follow. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशं च तपः कृत्वा साध्यते फलमुत्तमम् ।
शुभं फलं स्वयं हित्वा साध्यते नाहितं पुनः ॥ १६॥

English
Excellent results are sought after performing penance. No harm is sought after voluntarily eschewing auspicious results. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा तस्य ते मुनिसत्तमाः ।
बुद्धिमोहं तदा प्राप्ता इदमेव वचोऽब्रुवन् ॥ १७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Even after hearing these words, the excellent sages, still deluded in their intellect, spoke these words. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ॥

कर्तव्यं हि त्वया स्वामिन्निदमेव न चान्यथा ।
इत्युक्तस्तु तदा देवो गणेशो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ १८॥

English
The sages said:—  “O lord, this must be done by you. Not otherwise.” Gaṇeśa who was thus implored spoke. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच ।
असाधुः साधुतां चैव साधुश्चासाधुतां तथा ।
कदाचिदपि नाप्नोति ब्रह्मोक्तमिति निश्चितम् ॥ १९॥

English
Gaṇeśa said:—  This has been decisively said by Brahma—"A wicked man never attains saintly nature nor does a saintly man assume perversion at any time." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा च भवतां दुःखं जातं चानशनात्पुरा ।
तदा सुखं प्रदत्तं वै गौतमेन महर्षिणा ॥ २०॥

English
Formerly when you were distressed by starvation, the sage Gautama afforded you happiness. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं वै भवद्भिश्च तस्मै दुःखं प्रदीयते ।
नैतद्युक्ततमं लोके सर्वथा सुविचार्यताम् ॥ २१॥

English
Now you intend to inflict misery on him. This is not proper. Please think over this in all aspects. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीबलान्मोहिता यूयं न मे वाक्यं करिष्यथ ।
एतद्धिततमं तस्य भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ २२॥

English
Deluded by your women-folk you never cared to act by my advice. There is no doubt that my advice will be beneficial to him at the end. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्चायमृषिश्रेष्ठो दास्यते वः सुखं ध्रुवम् ।
तारणं न च युक्तं स्याद्वरमन्यं वृणीत वै ॥ २३॥

English
Certainly the excellent sage will render happiness unto you. Deception is not proper. Please choose another boon. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं तेन गणेशेन महात्मना ।
यद्यप्युक्तमृषिभ्यश्च तदप्येते न मेनिरे ॥ २४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Although this advice was given by Ganeisa, the great Atman, still the sage did not pay heed. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्ताधीनतया सोऽथ शिवपुत्रोऽब्रवीत्तदा ।
उदासीनेन मनसा तानृषीन्दुष्टशेमुषीन् ॥ २५॥

English
Then Ganeisa, who is subservient to his devotees, spoke indifferently to those sages of evil intent. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
गणेश उवाच ।
भवद्भिः प्रार्थ्यते यच्च करिष्येऽहं तथा खलु ।
पश्चाद्भावि भवेदेव इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे पुनः ॥ २६॥

English
Gaṇeśa said:—  I shall do what is being requested by you all. What is destined to happen will surely happen. After saying this he vanished. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमः स न जानाति मुनीनां वै दुराशयम् ।
आनन्दमनसा नित्यं पत्न्या कर्म चकार तत् ॥ २७॥

English
Gautama did not know the evil intention of the sages. He went on performing his routine along with his wife with delighted mind. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तदन्तरे च यज्जातं चरित्रं वरयोगतः ।
तद्दुष्टर्षिप्रभावात्तु श्रूयतां तन्मुनीश्वराः ॥ २८॥

English
O great sages, hear what happened in the meantime as a result of the influence of those wicked sages by the granting of the boon. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमस्य च केदारे तत्रासन्व्रीहयो यवाः ।
गणेशस्तत्र गौर्भूत्वा जगाम किल दुर्बला ॥ २९॥

English
There were food-grains and barleys in Gautama's field. Ganesa assumed the form of a feeble cow and went there. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कम्पमाना च सा गत्वा तत्र तद्वरयोगतः ।
व्रीहीन्सम्भक्षयामास यवांश्च मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ ३०॥

English
O excellent sages, staggering and faltering, the cow, as a result of the boon began to eat the food-grains and barleys. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे दैवाद् गौतमस्तत्र चागतः ।
स दयालुस्तृणस्तम्बैर्वारयामास तां तदा ॥ ३१॥

English
In the meantime Gautama happened to come there casually. Compassionate by nature, he warded off the cow with a bunch of grass blades. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तृणस्तम्बेन सा स्पृष्टा पपात पृथिवीतले ।
मृता च तत्क्षणादेव तदृषेः पश्यतस्तदा ॥ ३२॥

English
No sooner was it touched by the blades of grass than the cow fell down dead even as the sage was watching it. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषयश्छन्नरूपास्ते ऋषिपत्न्यस्तथाशुभाः ।
ऊचुस्तत्र तदा सर्वे किं कृतं गौतमेन च ॥ ३३॥

English
The sages and their wicked wives who were hiding there began to shout, "O see, what is done by Gautama." (33.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमोऽपि तथाहल्यामाहूयासीत्सुविस्मितः ।
उवाच दुःखतो विप्रा दूयमानेन चेतसा ॥ ३४॥

English
O brahmins, the bewildered Gautama called Ahalya and spoke with a dejected, distressed mind. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
किं जातं च कथं देवि कुपितः परमेश्वरः ।
किं कर्तव्यं क्व गन्तव्यं हत्या च समुपस्थिता ॥ ३५॥

English
Gautama said:—  O gentle lady, what has happened? How did it happen? Certainly lord Śiva is angry. What should be done? Where shall we go? The sin of slaughter has befallen me. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे विप्रा गौतमं पर्यभर्त्सयन् ।
विप्रपत्न्यस्तथाहल्यां दुर्वचोभिर्व्यथां ददुः ॥ ३६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* In the meantime the brahmins reproached Gautama. The wives of the brahmins tormented Ahalya with threats. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्बुद्धयश्च तच्छिष्याः सुतास्तेषां तथैव च ।
गौतमं परिभर्त्स्यैव प्रत्यूचुर्धिग्वचो मुहुः ॥ ३७॥

English
The evil-minded disciples and sons of the brahmins reviled at Gautama repeating the words, "Fie on you." (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
मुखं न दर्शनीयं ते गम्यतां गम्यतामिति ।
दृष्ट्वा गोघ्नमुखं सद्यः सचैलं स्नानमाचरेत् ॥ ३८॥

English
The sages said:—  Your face is not fit to be seen. Go hence. If a person sees the face of a cow-killer, he shall take bath in full dress. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यावदाश्रममध्ये त्वं तावदेव हविर्भुजः ।
पितरश्च न गृह्णन्ति ह्यस्मद्दत्तं हि किञ्चन ॥ ३९॥

English
As long as you stay in the midst of the hermitage, the sacrificial fires and the manes will not accept our gifts. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्गच्छान्यतस्त्वं च परिवारसमन्वितः ।
विलम्बं कुरु नैव त्वं धेनुहन्पापकारक ॥ ४०॥

English
Hence, O sinner, O slayer of a cow, go somewhere else along with your followers. Do not delay. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा ते च तं सर्वे पाषाणैः समताडयन् ।
व्यथां ददुरतीवास्मै त्वहल्यां च दुरुक्तिभिः ॥ ४१॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Saying this, they pelted him with stones. They inflicted pain on him and Ahalya with insulting words. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ताडितो भर्त्सितो दुष्टैर्गौतमो गिरमब्रवीत् ।
इतो गच्छामि मुनयो ह्यन्यत्र निवसाम्यहम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Beaten and reproachad by the wicked persons Gautama spoke these words—"O sages, I am going away from here. I shall stay somewhere else. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा गौतमस्तस्मात्स्थानाच्च निर्गतस्तदा ।
गत्वा क्रोशं तदा चक्रे ह्याश्रमं तदनुज्ञया ॥ ४३॥

English
After saying this, Gautama went out of that place. Going a krosa he made his hermitage there with their permission. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यावच्चैवाभिशापो वै तावत्कार्यं न किञ्चन ।
न कर्मण्यधिकारोऽस्ति दैवे पित्र्येऽथ वैदिके ॥ ४४॥

English
As long as there is this curse, no rite shall be performed. You are not authorised in any of the rites of god, manes or the Vedic rituals.* (44.)

---

Sanskrit
मासार्धं च ततो नीत्वा मुनीन्सम्प्रार्थयत्तदा ।
गौतमो मुनिवर्यः स तेन दुःखेन दुखितः ॥ ४५॥

English
The excellent sage Gautama excessively distressed by this regrettable incident spent half of month thus and then requested. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
अनुकम्प्यो भवद्भिश्च कथ्यतां क्रियते मया ।
यथा मदीयं पापं च गच्छत्विति निवेद्यताम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Gautama said:—  I am to be pitied by you. Please tell me how my sin can be wiped off. I shall do as you bid me. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तास्ते तदा विप्रा नोचुश्चैव परस्परम् ।
अत्यन्तं सेवया पृष्टा मिलिता ह्येकतः स्थिताः ॥ ४७॥

English
Sūta said :—  Thus requested the brahmins did not say anything. When they were implored humbly they met separately and remained aloof. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमो दूरतः स्थित्वा नत्वा तानृषिसत्तमान् ।
पप्रच्छ विनयाविष्टः किं कार्यं हि मयाधुना ॥ ४८॥

English
Gautama stood far off. Bowing down to the sages he humbly asked them what he should do. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते मुनिना तेन गौतमेन महात्मना ।
मिलिताः सकलास्ते वै मुनयो वाक्यमब्रुवन् ॥ ४९॥

English
When this was said by Gautama of noble soul, the sages spoke these words. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
निष्कृतिं हि विना शुद्धिर्जायते न कदाचन ।
तस्मात्त्वं देहशुद्ध्यर्थं प्रायश्चित्तं समाचर ॥ ५०॥

English
The sages said :—  "Without expiation purity cannot be attained. Hence, for the sake of purification you shall perform the rites of atonement. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिवारं पृथिवीं सर्वां क्रम पापं प्रकाशयन् ।
पुनरागत्य चात्रैव चर मासव्रतं तथा ॥ ५१॥

English
Go round the earth three times announcing your sin. After returning to this place perform the holy Vrata for a month. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
शतमेकोत्तरं चैव ब्रह्मणोऽस्य गिरेस्तथा ।
प्रक्रमणं विधायैवं शुद्धिस्ते च भविष्यति ॥ ५२॥

English
If you go round this mountain Brahmagiri$^{157}$ hundred and one times you can attain purity. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा त्वं समानीय गङ्गास्नानं समाचर ।
पार्थिवानां तथा कोटिं कृत्वा देवं निषेवय ॥ ५३॥

English
Or you fetch water from Gangā and perform the ceremonial ablution. Propitiate lord Śiva making a crore of his phallic images. (53.)

Sanskrit
गङ्गायां च ततः स्नात्वा पुतश्चैव भविष्यसि ।
पुरा दश तथा चैकं गिरेस्त्वं क्रमणं कुरु ॥ ५४॥

English
At the outset, you circumambulate this mountain eleven times. Taking bath in Gangā you will become pure thereafter. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
शतकुम्म्भैस्तथा स्नात्वा पार्थिवं निष्कृतिर्भवेत् ।
इति तैरॄषिभिः प्रोक्तस्तथेत्योमिति तद्वचः ॥ ५५॥

English
Performing the ablution of the earthen phallic image of Siva with a hundred potful of water, the expiatory rite will be completed." When he was thus urged by the sages he said in the affirmative. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवानां तथा पूजां गिरेः प्रक्रमणं तथा ।
करिष्यामि मुनिश्रेष्ठा आज्ञया श्रीमतामिह ॥ ५६॥

English
O excellent sages, at the behest of your honours I shall worship the earthen phallic image of Siva and also circumambulate the mountain. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा सर्षिवर्यश्च कृत्वा प्रक्रमणं गिरेः ।
पूजयामास निर्माय पार्थिवान्मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ५७॥

English
After saying this and circumambulating the mountain, the excellent sage made earthen phallic images of Siva and worshipped them. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
अहल्या च ततः साध्वी तच्च सर्वं चकार सा ।
शिष्याश्च प्रतिशिष्याश्च चक्रुः सेवां तयोस्तदा ॥ ५८॥

English
The chaste lady Ahalya too acted accordingly. Their disciples and the disciples of their disciples rendered service to them. (58.)
