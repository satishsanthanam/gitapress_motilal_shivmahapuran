### **CHAPTER TWENTYSIX - *(The greatness of Tryambakeśvara)***

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं कृते तु ऋषिणा सस्त्रीकेन द्विजाः शिवः ।
आविर्बभूव सशिवः प्रसन्नः सगणस्तदा ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, when all this was performed thus by the sage along with his wife, the delighted Siva accompanied by his Ganas appeared before him. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रसन्नः सशिवो वरं ब्रूहि महामुने ।
प्रसन्नोऽहं सुभक्त्या त इत्युवाच कृपानिधिः ॥ २॥

English
Then the gladdened Siva, the storehouse of mercy said—"O great sage, mention the boon you wish to choose. I am delighted with your devotion." (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा तत्सुन्दरं रूपं दृष्ट्वा शम्भोर्महात्मनः ।
प्रणम्य शङ्करं भक्त्या स्तुतिं चक्रे मुदान्वितः ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the beautiful form of Siva, the supreme soul, the sage bowed to Siva with devotion and eulogised him joyously. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुत्वा बहु प्रणम्येशं बद्धाञ्जलिपुटः स्थितः ।
निष्पापं कुरु मां देवाब्रवीदिति स गौतमः ॥ ४॥

English
After eulogising and bowing to him many times, Gautama stood there with palms joined in reverence and said—"O lord, make me sinless." (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तस्य गौतमस्य महात्मनः ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा शिवो वाक्यमुपाददे ॥ ५॥

English
On hearing these words of the noble soul Gautama, Siva became much delighted and spoke. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
धन्योऽसि कृतकृत्योऽसि निष्पापोऽसि सदा मुने ।
एतैर्दुष्टैः किल त्वं च छलितोऽसि खलात्मभिः ॥ ६॥

English
Śiva said:—  O sage, you are blessed. You shall be contented. You are already sinless. You have been deceived by these wicked knaves. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वदीयदर्शनाल्लोका निष्पापाश्च भवन्ति हि ।
किं पुनस्त्वं सपापोऽसि मद्भक्तिनिरतः सदा ॥ ७॥

English
Indeed, on seeing you, people become sinless. How can you be a sinner? You are always engaged in devotion to me. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
उपद्रवस्त्वयि मुने यैः कृतस्तु दुरात्मभिः ।
ते पापाश्च दुराचारा हत्यावन्तस्त एव हि ॥ ८॥

English
O sage, indeed, all the wicked persons, by whom you have been harassed, are sinners, evil-minded and murder-stained. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
एतेषां दर्शनादन्ये पापिष्ठाः सम्भवन्तु च ।
कृतघ्नाश्च तथा जाता नैतेषां निष्कृतिः क्वचित् ॥ ९॥

English
Others too are likely to be sinners on seeing these. They have become already ungrateful. There is no way of atonement for them. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा शङ्करस्तस्मै तेषां दुश्चरितं तदा ।
बहूवाच प्रभुर्विप्राः सत्कदोऽसत्सु दण्डदः ॥ १०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this, O brahmins, lord Siva, the bestower of happiness to the good and chastiser of the wicked told him their evil actions in detail. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शर्वोक्तमिति स श्रुत्वा सुविस्मितमना ऋषिः ।
सुप्रणम्य शिवं भक्त्या साञ्जलिः पुनरब्रवीत् ॥ ११॥

English
On hearing the words of Siva, the sage was greatly surprised. After bowing to him with devotion, he spoke again to him with palms joined in reverence. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
ऋषिभिस्तैर्महेशान ह्युपकारः कृतो महान् ।
यद्येवं न कृतं तैस्तु दर्शनं ते कुतो भवेत् ॥ १२॥

English
Gautama said:—  O lord Siva, a great help has been rendered by these sages. If they had not done like this, how could I have had your vision? (12.)

---

Sanskrit
धन्यास्ते ऋषयो यैस्तु मह्यं शुभतरं कृतम् ।
तद्दुराचरणादेव मम स्वार्थो महानभूत् ॥ १३॥

English
Blessed indeed are the sages who have pursued this auspicious activity towards me. It is by their so-called evil action that my great self-interest is made possible: (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा सुप्रसन्नो महेश्वरः ।
गौतमं प्रत्युवाचाशु कृपादृष्ट्या विलोक्य च ॥ १४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing his words, lord Śiva became all the more delighted. Looking at Gautama mercifully he replied, (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
ऋषिर्धन्योऽसि विप्रेन्द्र ऋषिश्रेष्ठतरोऽसि वै ।
ज्ञात्वा मां सुप्रसन्नं हि वृणु त्वं वरमुत्तमम् ॥ १५॥

English
Śiva said:—  O leading brahmin, you are a blessed sage. You are the most excellent among sages. Realising that I am highly delighted, you choose the excellent boon. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
गौतमोऽपि विचार्यैवं लोके विश्रुतमित्युत ।
अन्यथा न भवेदेव तस्मादुक्तं समाचरेत् ॥ १६॥

निश्चित्यैवं मुनिश्रेष्ठो गौतमः शिवभक्तिमान् ।
साञ्जलिर्नतशीर्षो हि शङ्करं वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ १७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Gautama thought—“It is thus that the scandal was spread in the world. Otherwise it would not have been so. Hence I must do what is proper”. After thinking like this, the excellent sage Gautama, devoted to Śiva, spoke to Śiva with palms joined in reverence and head bent down. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
सत्यं नाथ ब्रवीषि त्वं तथापि पञ्चभिः कृतम् ।
नान्यथा भवतीत्यत्र यज्जातं जायतां तु तत् ॥ १८॥

English
Gautama said:—  O lord, what you say is true. Still what is done by the five leading persons cannot be otherwise. So, let what has happened happen. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश गङ्गा च दीयतां मम ।
कुरु लोकोपकारं हि नमस्तेऽस्तु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १९॥

English
O lord of gods, if you are delighted, may Gaṅgā be given to me. Render help to the worlds. Obeisance be to you. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा वचनं तस्य धृत्वा वै पादपङ्कजम् ।
नमश्चकार देवेशं गौतमो लोककाम्यया ॥ २०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying these words and grasping his lotus-like feet, Gautama made obeisance to the lord of gods with a desire for the welfare of the worlds. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तु शङ्करो देवः पृथिव्याश्च दिवश्च सः ।
सारं चैव समुद्धृत्य रक्षितं पूर्वमेव तत् ॥ २१॥

विवाहे ब्रह्मणा दत्तमवशिष्टं च किञ्चन ।
तत्तस्मै दत्तवान् शम्भुर्मुनये भक्तवत्सलः ॥ २२॥

English
The essence of the earth and heaven had been extracted of yore, a part of this had been given to the sage by Brahmā at his marriage. What had been held over, lord Śiva favourably disposed towards his devotees, now gave to the sage. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गाजलं तदा तत्र स्त्रीरूपमभवत्परम् ।
तस्याश्चैव ऋषिश्रेष्ठः स्तुतिं कृत्वा नतिं व्यधात् ॥ २३॥

English
Then the waters of Gaṅgā assumed the form of a lady and stood there. The excellent sage eulogised her and made obeisance. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
धन्यासि कृतकृत्यासि पावितं भुवनं त्वया ।
मां च पावय गङ्गे त्वं पतन्तं निरये ध्रुवम् ॥ २४॥

English
Gautama said:—  O Gaṅgā, you are blessed. You are contented. The whole world has been sanctified by you. Sanctify me too. I am likely to fall into hell. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
शम्भुश्चापि तदोवाच सर्वेषां हितकृच्छृणु ।
गङ्गे गौतममेनं त्वं पावयस्व मदाज्ञया ॥ २५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Šiva too then spoke—"O Gangā, listen. You are beneficial to all. At my behest, sanctify Gautama also." (25.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य शम्भोश्च गौतमस्य च ।
उवाचैव शिवं गङ्गा शिवशक्तिर्हि पावनी ॥ २६॥

English
On hearing these words of Siva and Gautama, Gangā, the holy Sakti of Siva, spoke to Siva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
ऋषिं तु पावयित्वाहं परिवारयुतं प्रभो ।
गमिष्यामि निजस्थानं वचः सत्यं ब्रवीमि ह ॥ २७॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  O lord, after sanctifying the sage along with his followers, I shall return to my abode. I am telling you the truth. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तं गङ्गया तत्र महेशो भक्तवत्सलः ।
लोकोपकरणार्थाय पुनर्गङ्गां वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* When this was mentioned by Gaṅgā, lord Śiva, favourably disposed towards his devotees, spoke again to Gaṅgā keeping in mind the welfare of the worlds. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
त्वया स्थातव्यमत्रैवाव्रजेद्यावत्कलिर्युगः ।
वैवस्वतो मनुर्देवि ह्यष्टाविंशत्तमो भवेत् ॥ २९॥

English
Śiva said:—  O goddess, till the advent of the Kali Yuga, when the son of Vivasvat shall be the twenty-eighth Manu, you shall stay here alone. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य स्वामिनः शङ्करस्य तत् ।
प्रत्युवाच पुनर्गङ्गा पावनी सा सरिद्वरा ॥ ३०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words of lord Śiva, Gaṅgā the sanctifying leading river replied. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
माहात्म्यमधिकं चेत्स्यान्मम स्वामिन्महेश्वर ।
सर्वेभ्यश्च तदा स्थास्ये धरायां त्रिपुरान्तक ॥ ३१॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  O lord Siva, O slayer of the Tripuras, if my greatness were to surpass that of all, I shall stay on the earth. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
किञ्चान्यच्च श‍ृणु स्वामिन्वपुषा सुन्दरेण ह ।
तिष्ठ त्वं मत्समीपे वै सगणः साम्बिकः प्रभो ॥ ३२॥

English
O lord, please listen to something more. O lord, please abide with me with this beautiful body of yours along with your consort and the Ganas. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं तस्या वचः श्रुत्वा शङ्करो भक्तवत्सलः ।
लोकोपकरणार्थाय पुनर्गङ्गां वचोऽब्रवीत् ॥ ३३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing her words, Śiva favourably disposed towards his devotees, spoke again to Gaṅgā for rendering help to the worlds. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
धन्यासि श्रूयतां गङ्गे ह्यहं भिन्नस्त्वया न हि ।
तथापि स्थीयते ह्यत्र स्थीयतां च त्वयापि हि ॥ ३४॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Gaṅgā you are blessed. Listen. I am not distinct from you. Still I stay here. You too must stay. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा स्वामिनः परमेशितुः ।
प्रसन्नमानसा भूत्वा गङ्गा च प्रत्यपूजयत् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words of lord Śiva, Gaṅgā was delighted in the mind and she accepted his advice. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवा ऋषयश्च पुरातनाः ।
सुतीर्थान्यप्यनेकानि क्षेत्राणि विविधानि च ॥ ३६॥

आगत्य गौतमं सर्वे गङ्गां च गिरिशं तथा ।
जय जयेति भाषन्तः पूजयामासुरादरात् ॥ ३७॥

English
In the meantime, the gods and sages, several holy centres and holy rivers came there. Proclaiming "Be victorious, be victorious", they respectfully worshipped Gautama, Gaṅgā and Pārvatī. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्ते निर्जराः सर्वे तेषां चक्रुः स्तुतिं मुदा ।
करान् बध्वा नतस्कन्धा हरिब्रह्मादयस्तदा ॥ ३८॥

English
Thereafter Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods eulogised them joyously joining their palms and drooping down their shoulders. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गा प्रसन्ना तेभ्यश्च गिरिशश्चोचतुस्तदा ।
वरं ब्रूत सुरश्रेष्ठा दद्वो वः प्रियकाम्यया ॥ ३९॥

English
Gaṅgā and Pārvatī were delighted and they said "O excellent gods, mention the boon you desire to choose. We shall grant the same with a desire to do what is pleasing to you." (39.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
यदि प्रसन्नो देवेश प्रसन्ना त्वं सरिद्वरे ।
स्थातव्यमत्र कृपया नः प्रियार्थं तथा नृणाम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The gods said:—  "O lord of gods, O leading, river, if you are delighted, you shall stay here taking pity on us and for the benefit of men." (40.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गोवाच ।
यूयं सर्वप्रियार्थं च तिष्ठथात्र न किं पुनः ।
गौतमं क्षालयित्वाहं गमिष्यामि यथागतम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Gaṅgā said:—  Why don't you stay here for the pleasure and welfare of all? After washing off the sin of Gautama I shall go back the way I have come. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
भवत्सु मे विशेषोऽत्र ज्ञेयश्चैव कथं सुराः ।
तत्प्रमाणं कृतं चेत्स्यात्तदा तिष्ठाम्यसंशयम् ॥ ४२॥

English
O gods, how may my speciality be known apart from yours? If you can prove the same, I shall undoubtedly stay here (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे ऊचुः ।
सिंहराशौ यदा स्याद्वै गुरुः सर्वसुहृत्तमः ।
तदा वयं च सर्वे त्वागमिष्यामो न संशयः ॥ ४३॥

English
The gods said:—  When Jupiter, the most friendly to all, comes to the zodiac of Leo all of us will come. There is no doubt about it. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादश च वर्षाणि लोकानां पातकं त्विह ।
क्षालितं यद्भवेदेवं मलिनाः स्मः सरिद्वरे ॥ ४४॥

तस्यैव क्षालनाय त्वायास्यामः सर्वथा प्रिये ।
त्वत्सकाशं महादेवि प्रोच्यते सत्यमादरात् ॥ ४५॥

English
O leading river, we will become unclean ourselves by cleaning off the sins of the people for eleven years. O beloved great goddess, we will come to you for washing that off in every respect. With respect we reveal this truth to you. (44-45.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहाय लोकानामस्माकं प्रियकाम्यया ।
स्थातव्यं शङ्करेणापि त्वया चैव सरिद्वरे ॥ ४६॥

English
To bless the worlds, to do what is pleasing to us Śiva shall stay here and you too, O leading river. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्सिंहे गुरुश्चैव स्थास्यामस्तावदेव हि ।
त्वयि स्नानं त्रिकालं च शङ्करस्य च दर्शनम् ॥ ४७॥

कृत्वा स्वपापं निखिलं विमोक्ष्यामो न संशयः ।
स्वदेशांश्च गमिष्यामो भवच्छासनतो वयम् ॥ ४८॥

English
We shall stay here as long as Jupiter is in Leo. We shall take our bath thrice a day and visit Śiva. Then we shall get rid of our sins and with your permission return to our native lands. (47-48.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं प्रार्थितस्तैस्तु गौतमेन महर्षिणा ।
स्थितोऽसौ शङ्करः प्रीत्या स्थिता सा च सरिद्वरा ॥ ४९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus requested by them as well as by the sage Gautama, Siva stayed there with pleasure. The excellent river Gangā too stayed. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

ख्याताख्यातं बभूवाथ महापातकनाशनम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Gangā there became famous as Gautami. The phallic image became famous by the name Trymbaka. Both are destructive of great sins. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

आयान्ति सर्वतीर्थानि क्षेत्राणि दैवतानि च ॥ ५१॥

English
From that time onwards whenever Jupiter enters Leo, the holy rivers, sacred centres and the gods come there. (51.)

Sanskrit
सरांसि पुष्करादीनि गङ्गाद्याः सरितस्तथा ।
वासुदेवादयो देवाः सन्ति वै गौतमीतटे ॥ ५२॥

English
The holy lakes Puśkara, and others, the sacred river Gangā and others, Vāsudeva and other gods stay on the banks of the Gautami river. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्तत्र स्थितानीह तावत्तेषां फलं न हि ।
स्वप्रदेशे समायातास्तर्ह्येतेषां फलं भवेत् ॥ ५३॥

English
While they are here they lose sacredness in their original places. They will have it back when they return there. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गमिदं प्रोक्तं त्र्यम्बकं नाम विश्रुतम् ।
स्थितं तटे हि गौतम्या महापातकनाशनम् ॥ ५४॥

English
This is a Jyotirlinga famous as Tryambaka. It is stationed on the banks of the Gautami destructive of great sins. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
यः पश्येद्भक्तितो ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं त्र्यम्बकनामकम् ।
पूजयेत्प्रणमेत्स्तुत्वा सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ५५॥

English
He who sees the Jyotirlinga Tryambaka with devotion and worships with obeisance after eulogising it is freed from all sins. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गं त्र्यम्बकं हि पूजितं गौतमेन ह ।
सर्वकामप्रदं चात्र परत्र परमुक्तिदम् ॥ ५६॥

English
This very Jyotirlinga Tryambaka was worshipped by Gautama. Being worshipped it yields all desires here and bestows liberation hereafter. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्यातं यत्पृष्टोऽहं मुनीश्वराः ।
किमन्यदिच्छथ श्रोतुं तद् ब्रूयां वो न संशयः ॥ ५७॥

English
O great sages, thus what I have been asked has been narrated to you all. What else do you wish to hear? I shall explain it to you. No doubt need be entertained in this respect. (57.)
