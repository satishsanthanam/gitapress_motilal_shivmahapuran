### **CHAPTER TWENTYSEVEN - (The greatness of the Jyotirlinga Tryambakesvara)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
गङ्गा च जलरूपेण कुतो जाता वद प्रभो ।
तन्माहात्म्यं विशेषेण कुतो जातं वद प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O holy lord, whence did Gangā originate in the form of water? How could it attain its greatness? Please narrate. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
यैर्विप्रैर्गौतमायैव दुःखं दत्तं दुरात्मभिः ।
तेषां किं च ततो जातमुच्यतां व्याससद्गुरो ॥ २॥

English
What happened to those wicked brahmins who tormented Gautama? O sage with Vyasa as your good preceptor, please narrate. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं सम्प्रार्थिता गङ्गा गौतमेन तदा स्वयम् ।
ब्रह्मणश्च गिरेर्विप्रा द्रुतं तस्मादवातरत् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, Gangā who was thus requested by Gautama immediately descended from the mountain Brahmagiri. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
औदुम्बरस्य शाखायास्तत्प्रवाहो विनिःसृतः ।
तत्र स्नानं मुदा चक्रे गौतमो विश्रुतो मुनिः ॥ ४॥

English
Its current flowed from the branches of an Udumbara tree. Gautama, the famous sage, took his bath joyously. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमस्य च ये शिष्या अन्ये चैव महर्षयः ।
समागताश्च ते तत्र स्नानं चक्रुर्मुदान्विताः ॥ ५॥

English
Gautama's disciples and other sages too came there and joyously took their bath. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गाद्वारं च तन्नाम प्रसिद्धमभवत्तदा ।
सर्वपापहरं रम्यं दर्शनान्मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ ६॥

English
O excellent sages, the place became well known as Gangadvara. It is beautiful and its sight dispels all sins. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमस्पर्द्धिनस्ते च ऋषयस्तत्र चागताः ।
स्नानार्थं तांश्च सा दृष्ट्वा ह्यन्तर्धानं गता द्रुतम् ॥ ७॥

English
The sages who were malicious towards Gautama came there to take their bath; but on seeing them Gangā vanished immediately. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मा मेति गौतमस्तत्र व्याजहार वचो द्रुतम् ।
मुहुर्मुहुः स्तुवन् गङ्गां साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तकः ॥ ८॥

English
Then Gautama hastened to that place and eulogised Gangā again and again with palms joined in reverence and head bent down. He then spoke. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
इमे च श्रीमदान्धाश्च साधवो वाप्यसाधवः ।
एतत्पुण्यप्रभावेण दर्शनं दीयतां त्वया ॥ ९॥

English
Gautama said:—  Whether these are blind in their arrogance due to their affluence, whether they are good or bad, you shall grant them your vision by the virtue of this holy region. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
ततो वाणी समुत्पन्ना गङ्गाया व्योममण्डलात् ।
तच्छृणुध्वमृषिश्रेष्ठा गङ्गावचनमुत्तमम् ॥ १०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O excellent sages, then a voice arose from the firmament. Please listen to the excellent words that Gaṅgā spoke. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एते दुष्टतमाश्चैव कृतघ्नाः स्वामिद्रोहिणः ।
जाल्माः पाखण्डिनश्चैव द्रष्टुं वर्ज्याश्च सर्वदा ॥ ११॥

English
"These are the most wicked sages, most ungrateful and traitors to their lord. They are knaves and blasphemous heretics. They are unworthy of sight." (11.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
मातश्च श्रूयतामेतन्महतां गिर एव च ।
तस्मात्त्वया च कर्त्तव्यं सत्यं च भगवद् वचः ॥ १२॥

English
Gautama said:—  "Mother please hear the words of the great lord which you shall make truthful. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अपकारिषु यो लोक उपकारं करोति वै ।
तेन पूतो भवाम्यत्र भगवद्वचनं त्विदम् ॥ १३॥

English
The lord has said, "I become sanctified by the person who in this world helps those who harm him." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा मुनेर्वाक्यं गौतमस्य महात्मनः ।
पुनर्वाणी समुत्पन्ना गङ्गाया व्योममण्डलात् ॥ १४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the words of the sage Gautama the great Atman, the voice rose again from the firmament above Gangā. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कथ्यते हि त्वया सत्यं गौतमर्षे शिवं वचः ।
तथापि सङ्ग्रहार्थं च प्रायश्चित्तं चरन्तु वै ॥ १५॥

English
"O sage Gautama, what is said by you is true. These are auspicious words. Still let them perform expiatory rites in brief. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शतमेकोत्तरं चात्र कार्यं प्रक्रमणं गिरेः ।
भवच्छासनतस्त्वेतैस्त्वदधीनैर्विशेषतः ॥ १६॥

English
At your behest and particularly under your guidance, they shall circumambulate this mountain. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चैवाधिकारश्च जायते दुष्टकारिणाम् ।
मद्दर्शने विशेषेण सत्यमुक्तं मया मुने ॥ १७॥

English
Then these wicked persons will be authorised in having a vision of mine. O sage, this truth I mention to you. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्याश्चक्रुर्वै ते तथाखिलाः ।
सम्प्रार्थ्य गौतमं दीनाः क्षन्तव्यो नोऽपराधकः ॥ १८॥

English
On hearing these words, the sages did so duly after dejectedly requesting Gautama, "Our offence shall be excused." (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृते तदा तेन गौतमेन तदाज्ञया ।
कुशावर्तं नाम चक्रे गङ्गाद्वारादधोगतम् ॥ १९॥

English
When they did so, a ditch named Kusāvarta just beneath Gaṅgādvāra was made by Gautama with her permission. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रादुरभूत्तत्र सा तस्य प्रीतये पुनः ।
कुशावर्तं च विख्यातं तीर्थमासीदनुत्तमम् ॥ २०॥

English
The sacred river flowed thence for the pleasure of the sage. Kusāvarta became a famous and excellent place of pilgrimage. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र स्नातो नरो यस्तु मोक्षाय परिकल्पते ।
त्यक्त्वा सर्वानघान्सद्यो विज्ञानं प्राप्य दुर्लभम् ॥ २१॥

English
A man who takes bath there becomes worthy of attaining salvation after immediately eschewing the sins and securing perfect knowledge which is difficult of access. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमो ऋषयश्चान्ये मिलिताश्च परस्परम् ।
लज्जितास्ते तदा ये च कृतघ्ना ह्यभवन्पुरा ॥ २२॥

English
Gautama and the other sages mixed with one another. Those who had been ungrateful before were ashamed of their wickedness. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ॥

अस्माभिरन्यथा सूत श्रुतं तद्वर्णयामहे ।
गौतमस्तान्द्विजान् क्रुद्धः शशापेति प्रबुध्यताम् ॥ २३॥

English
O Sūta, this narrative has been heard by us in another manner.¹⁶⁰ We shall mention it. According to that the infuriated Gautama cursed the brahmins. May this be known to you. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
द्विजास्तदपि सत्यं वै कल्पभेदसमाश्रयात् ।
वर्णयामि विशेषेण तां कथामपि सुव्रताः ॥ २४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, that is also true, for that happened in a different Kalpa. O great ones of good rites, I shall describe the particular points of that story also. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमोऽपि ऋषीन्दृष्ट्वा तदा दुर्भिक्षपीडितान् ।
तपश्चकार सुमहद्वरुणस्य महात्मनः ॥ २५॥

English
On seeing the sages distressed by the famine, Gautama perfomed a great penance with god Varuṇa as the object of his worship. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षय्यं कल्पयामास जलं वरुणदायया ।
ततो व्रीहीन् यवांश्चैव वापयामास भूरिशः ॥ २६॥

English
Through Varuṇa's Māyā he caused a perennial supply of water. Then he made them sow food-grains and barleys in plenty. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं परोपकारी स गौतमो मुनिसत्तमः ।
आहारं कल्पयामास तेभ्यः स्वतपसो बलात् ॥ २७॥

English
Thus the excellent sage Gautama eager to help others afforded them sufficient food, thanks to the power of his penance. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्तत्स्त्रियो दुष्टा जलार्थमपमानिताः ।
ऊचुः पतिभ्यस्ताः क्रुद्धा गौतमेर्ष्याकरं वचः ॥ २८॥

English
Once the wicked wives of those sages feeling insulted in an incident over taking water became furious and complained to their husbands slandering Gautama. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्ते भिन्नमतयो गां कृत्वा कृत्रिमां द्विजाः ।
तद्धान्यभक्षणासक्तां चक्रुस्तां कुटिलाशयाः ॥ २९॥

English
With their minds thus prejudiced the evil-minded brahmins created an artificial cow grazing in his grain-field. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वधान्यभक्षणासक्तां गां दृष्ट्वा गौतमस्तदा ।
तृणेन ताडयामास शनैस्तां सन्निवारयन् ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing a cow eating his food-grains, Gautama struck it mildly with a blade of grass trying to ward it off. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तृणसंस्पर्शमात्रेण सा भूमौ पतिता च गौः ।
मृता ह्यभूत्क्षणं विप्रा भाविकर्मवशात्तदा ॥ ३१॥

English
The cow fell down immediately, being touched by the blade of grass. O brahmins, it died in an instant due to the forces of unavoidable destiny. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
गौर्हता गौतमेनेति तदा ते कुटिलाशयाः ।
एकत्रीभूय तत्रत्याः सकला ऋषयोऽवदन् ॥ ३२॥

English
Then the wicked sages held a gathering and said "O a cow has been killed by Gautama". (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स गौतमो भीतो गौर्हतेति बभूव ह ।
चकार विस्मयं नार्यहल्याशिष्यैः शिवानुगः ॥ ३३॥

English
Then Gautama was frightened thinking that a cow had been killed. Along with his wife Ahalyā, his disciples, the devotees of Śiva, he began to wonder. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स गौतमो ज्ञात्वा तां गां क्रोधसमाकुलः ।
शशाप तानृषीन् सर्वान् गौतमो मुनिसत्तमः ॥ ३४॥

English
Then the excellent sage Gautama realised the truth and was angry. He cursed the sages. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतम उवाच ।
यूयं सर्वे दुरात्मानो दुःखदा मे विशेषतः ।
शिवभक्तस्य सततं स्युर्वेदविमुखाः सदा ॥ ३५॥

English
Gautama said:—  You are evil-minded desiring to put me in distress as I am a devotee of Siva. Hence you shall be averse to the Vedas always. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यप्रभृति वेदोक्ते सत्कर्मणि विशेषतः ।
मा भूयाद्भवतां श्रद्धा शैवमार्गे विमुक्तिदे ॥ ३६॥

English
Henceforth your faith in the rites of the Vedas, particularly with reference to the path of Siva yielding salvation shall cease to exist. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यप्रभृति दुर्मार्गे तत्र श्रद्धा भवेत्तु वः ।
मोक्षमार्गविहीने हि सदा श्रुतिबहिर्मुखे ॥ ३७॥

English
Henceforth your attention, strayed from the path of salvation, will be diverted to evil paths extraneous to the tenets of the Vedas. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्यप्रभृति भालानि मृल्लिप्तानि भवन्तु वः ।
स्रंसध्वं नरके यूयं भालमृल्लेपना द्विजाः ॥ ३८॥

English
From today let your foreheads be smeared with clay. Fall, ye all, deep down into the hell, O brahmins with foreheads smeared with clay. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
भवन्तो मा भविष्यन्तु शिवैकपरदैवताः ।
अन्यदेव समत्वेन जानन्तु शिवमद्वयम् ॥ ३९॥

English
You will not have Siva as your sole deity. You will be considering the matchless Siva on a par with other gods. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
मा भूयाद्भवतां प्रीतिः शिवपूजादिकर्मणि ।
शिवनिष्ठेषु भक्तेषु शिवपर्वसु सर्वदा ॥ ४०॥

English
You will never have any interest in Siva's worship and other rites, in devotees staunchly faithful to Siva and in the festivals of Siva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्य दत्ता मया शापा यावन्तो दुःखदायकाः ।
तावन्तः सन्तु भवतां सन्ततावपि सर्वदा ॥ ४१॥

English
Let all these curses causing misery, descend on your progeny as well. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अशैवाः सन्तु भवतां पुत्रपौत्रादयो द्विजाः ।
पुत्रैः सहैव तिष्ठन्तु भवन्तो नरके ध्रुवम् ॥ ४२॥

English
All your descendants—sons, grandsons and others, O brahmins, shall be non-Saivites. You shall stay certainly in hell along with your sons. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो भवन्तु चाण्डाला दुःखदारिद्र्यपीडिताः ।
शठा निन्दाकराः सर्वे तप्तमुद्राङ्किताः सदा ॥ ४३॥

English
Afterwards you shall be born as Cāṇḍalas harassed by miseries and poverty. You shall be rogues, censuring others and marked with red-hot seals. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति शप्त्वा मुनीन् सर्वान् गौतमः स्वाश्रमं ययौ ।
शिवभक्तिं चकाराति स बभूव सुपावनः ॥ ४४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After cursing the sages, Gautama returned to his hermitage. He performed the devotional rites of Siva and became very holy. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तैः खिन्नहृदया ऋषयस्तेऽखिला द्विजाः ।
काञ्च्यां चक्रुर्निवासं हि शैवधर्मबहिष्कृताः ॥ ४५॥

English
Then the brahmins and sages distressed by the curses of Gautama put up their residence in Kānci. They were excluded from the rites of Siva. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पुत्राश्चाभवन्सर्वे शैवधर्मबहिष्कृताः ।
अग्रे तद्वद्भविष्यन्ति कलौ बहुजनाः खलाः ॥ ४६॥

English
Their sons too remained excluded from the sacred rites of Siva. In future, in the Kali age too, they will be born as rogues and illiterate ordinary people. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
इति प्रोक्तमशेषेण तद्वृत्तं मुनिसत्तमाः ।
पूर्ववृत्तमपि प्राज्ञाः श्रुतं सर्वैस्तु चादरात् ॥ ४७॥

English
O excellent sages, thus I have narrated their stories. O intelligent ones, their previous story too has been heard by all. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्यातो गौतम्याश्च समुद्भवः ।
माहात्म्यमुत्तमं चैव सर्वपापहरं परम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Thus the origin of Gautami river has been narrated to you. So also its glory destructive of all sins. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
त्र्यम्बकस्य च माहात्म्यं ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्य कीर्तितम् ।
यच्छ्रुत्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ४९॥

English
The glory of the Jyotirlinga Tryambaka is also narrated to you. On hearing this, one is released from all sins. There is no doubt about this. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि वैद्यनाथेश्वरस्य हि ।
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्य माहात्म्यं श्रूयतां पापहारकम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Hereafter I shall narrate the glory of the Jyotirlinga Vaidyanathesvara which dispels all sins. May it be heard. (50.)

---
