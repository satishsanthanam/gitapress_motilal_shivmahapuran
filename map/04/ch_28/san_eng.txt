### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - (The glory of the Jyotirlinga Vaidyanāthesvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
रावणो राक्षसश्रेष्ठो मानी मानपरायणः ।
आरराध हरं भक्त्या कैलासे पर्वतोत्तमे ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Rāvana the arrogant and prestige-minded leader of Rākṣasas propitiated Śiva with devotion on the excellent mountain Kailāsa. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
आराधितः कियत्कालं न प्रसन्नो हरो यदा ।
तदा चान्यत्तपश्चक्रे प्रसादार्थं शिवस्य सः ॥ २॥

English
Though the worship continued for a long time, Siva was not delighted. Then Rāvana performed another penance to delight Siva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चायं हिमवतः सिद्धिस्थानस्य वै गिरेः ।
पौलस्त्यो रावणः श्रीमान् दक्षिणे वृक्षखण्डके ॥ ३॥

भूमौ गर्तं वरं कृत्वा तत्राग्निं स्थाप्य स द्विजाः ।
तत्सन्निधौ शिवं स्थाप्य हवनं स चकार ह ॥ ४॥

English
The glorious Rāvana, scion of the family of Pulastya dug a deep ditch among the groves of trees on the southern side of Himavat, the mountain that is usually the spot of Siddhi. O brahmins, he then kindled fire within it. Near it he installed the idol of Siva and performed a sacrifice. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
ग्रीष्मे पञ्चाग्निमध्यस्थो वर्षासु स्थण्डिलेशयः ।
शीते जलान्तरस्थो हि त्रिधा चक्रे तपश्च सः ॥ ५॥

English
He performed three types of penance. During the summer he stationed himself in the midst of five fires, during rainy days he lay on the bare ground and in the winter he stood within water and performed his penance. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
चकारैवं बहु तपो न प्रसन्नस्तदापि हि ।
परमात्मा महेशानो दुराराध्यो दुरात्मभिः ॥ ६॥

English
Thus he performed penance a great deal. Even then Siva the supreme soul, difficult to be propitiated by the wicked was not delighted. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेव उवाच ।
रे रे रावण दुर्भक्त मा गर्वं वह दुर्मते ।
शीघ्रं च तव हस्तानां दर्पघ्नश्च भवेदिह ॥ ७॥

English
Then the ambitious Ravaṇa, lord of Daityas, began to worship Siva by cutting off his heads. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एकैकं च शिरश्छिन्नं विधिना शिवपूजने ।
एवं सत्क्रमतस्तेन छिन्नानि नव वै यदा ॥ ८॥

एकस्मिन्नवशिष्टे तु प्रसन्नः शङ्करस्तदा ।
आविर्बभूव तत्रैव सन्तुष्टो भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ९॥

English
In the due performance of the worship he cut off his heads one by one. Thus when he had cut off his nine heads and one head remained, the delighted Siva favourably disposed towards his devotees, appeared in front of him. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरांसि पूर्ववत्कृत्वा नीरुजानि तथा प्रभुः ।
मनोरथं ददौ तस्मै चातुलं बलमुत्तमम् ॥ १०॥

English
The lord reinstated the severed heads without causing pain and conferred on him his desire as well as unequalled excellent strength. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादं तस्य सम्प्राप्य रावणः स च राक्षसः ।
प्रत्युवाच शिवं शम्भुं नतस्कन्धः कृताञ्जलिः ॥ ११॥

English
Securing his favour, the Raksasa Ravaṇa replied to Siva, with palms joined in reverence and shoulders drooping down. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
रावण उवाच ।
प्रसन्नो भव देवेश लङ्कां च त्वां नयाम्यहम् ।
सफलं कुरु मे कामं त्वामहं शरणं गतः ॥ १२॥

English
Rāvaṇa said:—  O lord of gods, be pleased. I am taking your image to Laṅkā.¹⁶² Please make my desire fruitful. I seek refuge in you. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तश्च तदा तेन शम्भुर्वै रावणेन सः ।
प्रत्युवाच विचेतस्कः सङ्कटं परमं गतः ॥ १३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus addressed by Rāvaṇa, Śiva was in a dilemma and a bit disinclined. He replied. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
श्रूयतां राक्षसश्रेष्ठ वचो मे सारवत्तया ।
नीयतां स्वगृहे मे हि सद्भक्त्या लिङ्गमुत्तमम् ॥ १४॥

English
Śiva said:—  O excellent Raksasa, hear. My words are significant. Let my excellent phallic image be taken to your abode. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भूमौ लिङ्गं यदा त्वं च स्थापयिष्यसि यत्र वै ।
स्थास्यत्यत्र न सन्देहो यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ १५॥

English
Wherever this phallic image is placed on the ground it will become stationary. There is no doubt about it. Do as you please. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तः शम्भुना तेन रावणो राक्षसेश्वरः ।
तथेति तत्समादाय जगाम भवनं निजम् ॥ १६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus warned by Śiva, Rāvaṇa, the king of Rākṣasas, took it saying “So be it” and went to his abode. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
आसीन्मूत्रोत्सर्गकामो मार्गे हि शिवमायया ।
तत्स्तम्भितुं न शक्तोऽभूत्पौलस्त्यो रावणः प्रभुः ॥ १७॥

English
Thanks to Śiva's Māyā, he became desirous of passing urine while yet on his way. Lord Rāvaṇa of the family of Pulastya could not stop this urge. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वैकं तत्र वै गोपं प्रार्थ्य लिङ्गं ददौ च तत् ।
मुहूर्तके ह्यतिक्रान्ते गोपोऽभूद्विकलस्तदा ॥ १८॥

English
He saw a cowherd there and requested him to hold the image. About an hour elapsed, when Rāvaṇa did not return, the cowherd became nervous. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
भूमौ संस्थापयामास तद्भारेणातिपीडितः ।
तत्रैव तत्स्थितं लिङ्गं वज्रसारसमुद्भवम् ।
सर्वकामप्रदं चैव दर्शनात्पापहारकम् ॥ १९॥

वैद्यनाथेश्वरं नाम्ना तल्लिङ्गमभवन्मुने ।
प्रसिद्धं त्रिषु लोकेषु भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं सताम् ॥ २०॥

ज्योतिर्लिङ्गमिदं श्रेष्ठं दर्शनात्पूजनादपि ।
सर्वपापहरं दिव्यं मुक्तिवर्धनमुत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
He was distressed by its heavy weight. He placed it on the ground. The phallic image made of the essence of diamond remained fixed there. O sage, that phallic image is known as Vaidyanātheśvara. It yields all desires and dispels sins at its very sight. It is famous in the three worlds. It grants worldly pleasures here and salvation hereafter. (19-21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिँलिङ्गे स्थिते तत्र सर्वलोकहिताय वै ।
रावणः स्वगृहं गत्वा वरं प्राप्य महोत्तमम् ।
प्रियायै सर्वमाचख्यौ सुखेनाति महासुरः ॥ २२॥

English
It stood there for the benefit of the worlds. Rāvaṇa returned to his abode after securing the blissful boon. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा सकला देवाः शक्राद्या मुनयस्तथा ।
परस्परं समामन्त्र्य शिवासक्तधियोऽमलाः ॥ २३॥

English
On hearing of it Indra and other gods and the pious sages with minds fixed in Śiva consulted one another. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्काले सुराः सर्वे हरिब्रह्मादयो मुने ।
आजग्मुस्तत्र सुप्रीत्या पूजां चक्रुर्विशेषतः ॥ २४॥

English
O sage, Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods arrived at the spot and devotedly performed the worship of Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्यक्षं तं तदा दृष्ट्वा प्रतिष्ठाप्य च ते सुराः ।
वैद्यनाथेति सम्प्रोच्य नत्वा नुत्वा दिवं ययुः ॥ २५॥

English
The gods saw the deity personally and after consecrating the image they called Vaidyanātha. They then bowed to the image and eulogised it. Then they returned to heaven. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
तस्मिँल्लिङ्गे स्थिते तत्र रावणे च गृहं गते ।
किं चरित्रमभूत्तात ततस्तद्वद विस्तरात् ॥ २६॥

English
The sages said:—  O dear, when the image was installed and Rāvaṇa had returned to his abode, what happened? Please narrate it in detail. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
रावणोऽपि गृहं गत्वा वरं प्राप्य महोत्तमम् ।
प्रियायै सर्वमाचख्यौ मुमोदाति महासुरः ॥ २७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After securing the great and excellent boon, the great Asura Rāvaṇa returned to his abode and mentioned everything to his beloved wife. He rejoiced much. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा सकलं देवाः शक्राद्या मुनयस्तथा ।
परस्परं समूचुस्ते समुद्विग्ना मुनीश्वराः ॥ २८॥

English
O great sages, on hearing everything Indra and other gods and sages too became extremely dejected. They spoke to one another. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
देवादय ऊचुः ॥

रावणोऽयं दुरात्मा हि देवद्रोही खलः कुधीः ।
शिवाद् वरं च सम्प्राप्य दुःखं दास्यति नोऽति सः ॥ २९॥

English
The gods and sages said:—  "Rāvana is a wicked knave. He is evil-minded and hater of the gods. After securing the boon from Śiva, he will make us more miserable. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कुर्मः क्व च गच्छामः किं भविष्यति वा पुनः ।
दुष्टश्च दक्षतां प्राप्तः किं किं नो साधयिष्यति ॥ ३०॥

English
What shall we do? Where shall we go? What will happen now? What evil deeds this wicked fellow will not do after becoming more efficient?" (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इति दुःखं समापन्नाः शक्राद्या मुनयः सुराः ।
नारदं च समाहूय पप्रच्छुर्विकलास्तदा ॥ ३१॥

English
Thus distressed, Indra and other gods, and the sages too invited Nārada and asked him dejectedly. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
सर्वं कार्यं समर्थोऽसि कर्तुं त्वं मुनिसत्तम ।
उपायं कुरु देवर्षे देवानां दुःखनाशने ॥ ३२॥

English
The gods said:—  O excellent sage, you can do everything. O celestial sage, find out some means to dispel the grief of the gods. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
रावणोऽयं महादुष्टः किं किं नैव करिष्यति ।
क्व यास्यामो वयं चात्र दुष्टेनापीडिता वयम् ॥ ३३॥

English
What evil deeds the extremely wicked Rāvana will not do? Where shall we go, being harassed by the wicked fellow? (33.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
दुःखं त्यजत भो देवा युक्तिं कृत्वा च याम्यहम् ।
देवकार्यं करिष्यामि कृपया शङ्करस्य वै ॥ ३४॥

English
Nārada said:—  O gods, give up your sorrow. I shall plan out and go. By the mercy of Śiva, I shall carry out the task of the gods. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा स तु देवर्षिरगमद्रावणालयम् ।
सत्कारं समनुप्राप्य प्रीत्योवाचाखिलं च तत् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus saying, the celestial sage went to Rāvana's abode. After receiving the formal welcome he spoke with great pleasure. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
राक्षसोत्तम धन्यस्त्वं शैववर्यस्तपोधन ।
त्वां दृष्ट्वा च मनो मेऽद्य प्रसन्नमति रावण ॥ ३६॥

English
Nārada said:—  "O excellent Rākṣasa, you are a blessed sage, a great devotee of Śiva. At your sight today my mind is much delighted. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
स्ववृत्तं ब्रूह्यशेषेण शिवाराधनसम्भवम् ।
इति पृष्टस्तदा तेन रावणो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ ३७॥

English
Please narrate the details how you propitiate Śiva. Thus asked by him Rāvana replied. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
रावण उवाच ।
गत्वा मया तु कैलासे तपोऽर्थं च महामुने ।
तत्रैव बहुकालं वै तपस्तप्तं सुदारुणम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Rāvana said:—  O great sage, after going to Kailasa for penance, I performed a severe penance for a long time. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा न शङ्करस्तुष्टस्ततश्च परिवर्तितम् ।
आगत्य वृक्षखण्डे वै पुनस्तप्तं मया मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, when Siva was not pleased, I returned from there to the grove and resumed penance. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
ग्रीष्मे पञ्चाग्निमध्ये तु वर्षासु स्थण्डिलेशयः ।
शीते जलान्तरस्थो हि कृतं चैव त्रिधा तपः ॥ ४०॥

English
Standing in the midst of five fires during the summer, lying on the bare ground during rains and staying inside water during the winter I performed three-fold penance. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं मया कृतं तत्र तपोऽत्युग्रं मुनीश्वर ।
तथापि शङ्करो मह्यं न प्रसन्नोऽभवन्मनाक् ॥ ४१॥

English
O great sage, thus I performed a severe penance there. Still Siva was not even slightly pleased with me. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा मया तु क्रुद्धेन भूमौ गर्तं विधाय च ।
तत्राग्निं च समाधाय पार्थिवं च प्रकल्प्य च ॥ ४२॥

गन्धैश्च चन्दनैश्चैव धूपैश्च विविधैस्तदा ।
नैवेद्यैः पूजितः शम्भुरारार्तिकविधानतः ॥ ४३॥

English
I then became angry. I dug a ditch in the ground and kindled fire. I made earthen idols. By waving lights in the course of worship I worshipped Siva with scents, sandal paste, incense and food-offerings. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणिपातैः स्तवैः पुण्यैस्तोषितः शङ्करो मया ।
गीतैर्नृत्यैश्च वाद्यैश्च मुखाङ्गुलिसमर्पणैः ॥ ४४॥

English
Siva was propitiated by me by obeisances, eulogies, songs, dances, musical instruments and mystic gestures of faces and fingers. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
एतैश्च विविधैश्चान्यैरुपायैर्भूरिभिर्मुने ।
शास्त्रोक्तेन विधानेन पूजितो भगवान् हरः ॥ ४५॥

English
By such different modes and various other means lord Siva was worshipped by me according to the rules laid down in the Sāstras. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
न तुष्टः सम्मुखो जातो यदा च भगवान् हरः ।
तदाहं दुःखितोऽभूवं तपसोऽप्राप्य सत्फलम् ॥ ४६॥

English
When lord Siva was not pleased and did not appear in my presence I was distressed as I could not obtain the fruits of penance. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
धिक् शरीरं बलं चैव धिक् तपःकरणं मम ।
इत्युक्त्वा तु मया तत्र स्थापितेऽग्नौ हुतं बहु ॥ ४७॥

English
"Fie upon my body. Fie upon my strength. Fie upon my penance". Saying this I performed several sacrifices in the fire that had been kept there. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्चेति विचार्यैव त्यक्षाम्यग्नौ निजां तनुम् ।
सञ्छिन्नानि शिरांस्येव तस्मिन् प्रज्वलिते शुचौ ॥ ४८॥

सुच्छित्वैकैकशस्तानि कृत्वा शुद्धानि सर्वशः ।
शङ्करायार्पितान्येव नवसङ्ख्यानि वै मया ॥ ४९॥

English
Then I thought—"I will cast off my body into the fire". Thereafter I cut off my heads one by one, purified them and offered them to the fire. Thus nine heads were offered by me. (48-49.)

---

Sanskrit

तावदाविरभूत्तत्र ज्योतीरूपो हरः स्वयम् ॥ ५०॥

English
O excellent sage, when I was about to cut off my tenth head, Siva appeared before me as a mass of splendour. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रसन्नश्च वरं ब्रूहि ददामि मनसेप्सितम् ॥ ५१॥

English
The lord favourably disposed towards his devotees immediately spoke to me, "I am delighted. Mention the boon you wish to have. I shall grant you what you desire in your mind." (51.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ते च तदा तेन मया दृष्टो महेश्वरः ।
प्रणतः संस्तुतश्चैव करौ बध्वा सुभक्तितः ॥ ५२॥

English
When lord Siva said this I eulogised and bowed to him with palms joined in great devotion. (52.)
