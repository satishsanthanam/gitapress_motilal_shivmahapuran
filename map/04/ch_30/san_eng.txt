### **CHAPTER THIRTY - *(The greatness of the Jyotirlinga Nāgeśvara)***

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
कदाचित्सेवकस्तस्य राक्षसस्य दुरात्मनः ।
तदग्रे सुन्दरं रूपं शङ्करस्य ददर्श ह ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:*  Once an attendant of the wicked Rākṣasa saw the beautiful form of Śiva in front of him. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मै निवेदितं राज्ञे राक्षसानां यथार्थकम् ।
सर्वं तच्चरितं तेन सकौतुकमथाद्भुतम् ॥ २॥

English
That wonderful incident was truthfully intimated to the king of Rākṣasas. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
राजापि तत्र चागत्य राक्षसानां स दारुकः ।
विह्वलः सबलः शीघ्रं पर्यपृच्छच्च तं शिवम् ॥ ३॥

English
The king of the Rākṣasas, Dāruka, though very powerful was excited much. He hastened to the Vaiśya and asked him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
दारुक उवाच ।
किं ध्यायसि हि वैश्य त्वं सत्यं वद ममाग्रतः ।
एवं सति न मृत्युस्ते मम वाक्यं च नान्यथा ॥ ४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O Vaiśya, what is it that you meditate upon? Tell me the truth. Then you will not die. My words cannot be otherwise. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तेनोक्तं च न जानामि तच्छ्रुत्वा कुपितः स वै ।
राक्षसान्प्रेरयामास हन्यतां राक्षसा अयम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Śiva said:—*  He replied “I do not know.” On hearing it the Rākṣasa was angry. He incited the Rākṣasas—“O Rākṣasas, let him be killed.” (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तदुक्तास्ते तदा हन्तुं नानायुधधरा गताः ।
द्रुतं तं वैश्यशार्दूलं शङ्करासक्तचेतसम् ॥ ६॥

English
Urged by him, they seized various weapons and rushed at the leading Vaiśya whose mind was attached to Śiva, with desire to kill him. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तानागतांस्तदा दृष्ट्वा भयवित्रस्तलोचनः ।
शिवं सस्मार सुप्रीत्या तन्नामानि जगौ मुहुः ॥ ७॥

English
On seeing them come, the Vaiśya remembered Śiva with devotion and repeated his names though his eyes indicated that he was extremely frightened. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
वैश्यपतिरुवाच ।
पाहि शङ्कर देवेश पाहि शम्भो शिवेति च ।
दुष्टादस्मात्त्रिलोकेश खलहन् भक्तवत्सल ॥ ८॥

English
The leading Vaiśya said:—  O Śiva, save me. O lord of gods, save me. O Śiva, save me now from this wicked Rākṣasa. O lord of the three worlds, O slayer of the wicked, O one favourably disposed towards his devotees. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्वं च भवानद्य मम देव त्वमेव हि ।
त्वदधीनस्त्वदीयोऽहं त्वत्प्राणः सर्वदा प्रभो ॥ ९॥

English
O lord, you alone are my entire possession. I belong to you. I am subservient to you. O lord, my very life is always in you. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति सम्प्रार्थितः शम्भुर्विवरान्निर्गतस्तदा ।
भवनेनोत्तमेनाथ चतुर्द्वारयुतेन च ॥ १०॥

English
Śūta said:—  Thus implored, Śiva appeared out of the crevice with an excellent temple with four doors. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
मध्य ज्योतिःस्वरूपं च शिवरूपं तदद्भुतम् ।
परिवारसमायुक्तं दृष्ट्वा चापूजयत्स वै ॥ ११॥

English
Seeing in its midst the wonderfully brilliant form of Śiva along with his attendants he worshipped him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजितश्च तदा शम्भुः प्रसन्नो ह्यभवत्स्वयम् ।
अस्त्रं पाशुपतं नाम दत्त्वा राक्षसपुङ्गवान् ॥ १२॥

जघान सोपकरणांस्तान्सर्वान्सगणान्द्रुतम् ।
अरक्षच्च स्वभक्तं वै दुष्टहा स हि शङ्करः ॥ १३॥

English
On being worshipped, Śiva became delighted. He gave him the Pāśupata missile. He immediately slew all the Rākṣasas with all their attendants and adjuncts. He saved thus his devotee. Indeed Śiva is the slayer of the wicked. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वांस्तांश्च तदा हत्वा वरं प्रादाद्वनस्य च ।
अत्यद्भुतकरः शम्भुः स्वलीलात्तसुविग्रहः ॥ १४॥

English
After killing all of them, Siva the creator of his Oracles and who assumes bodies in the course of sports granted a boon to the forest. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मिन्वने सदा वर्णधर्मा वै सम्भवन्तु च ।
ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियविशां शूद्राणां हि तथैव च ॥ १५॥

English
May the rites and activities of the four castes, Brahmins, Kṣatriyas, Vaiśyas and Śūdras flourish in this forest always. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
भवन्त्वत्र मुनिश्रेष्ठास्तामसा न कदाचन ।
शिवधर्मप्रवक्तारः शिवधर्मप्रवर्तकाः ॥ १६॥

English
Let there never be people of the Tāmasaic qualities. Let there be excellent sages, the propounders and the followers of the Śaiva cult. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एतस्मिन्समये सा वै राक्षसी दारुकाह्वया ।
देव्याः स्तुतिं चकारासौ पार्वत्या दीनमानसा ॥ १७॥

English
In the meantime the Rākṣasi Dārukā dejected in her mind, eulogised Pārvatī. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्ना च तदा देवी किं करोमीत्युवाच हि ।
साप्युवाच पुनस्तत्र वंशो मे रक्ष्यतां त्वया ॥ १८॥

English
The goddess being pleased said "What shall I do?" She replied - "Let my race be protected by you". (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षयिष्यामि ते वंशं सत्यं च कथ्यते मया ।
इत्युक्त्वा च शिवेनैव विग्रहं सा चकार ह ॥ १९॥

English
"I shall protect your race. I say this truth to you." Saying this the Goddess quarrelled with Siva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवोऽपि कुपितां देवीं दृष्ट्वा वरवशः प्रभुः ।
प्रत्युवाचेति सुप्रीत्या यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु ॥ २०॥

English
On seeing the goddess furious, the lord, bound by the boon granted by him, replied with pleasure. "Do as you please". (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य स्वपतेः शङ्करस्य वै ।
सुप्रसन्ना विहस्याशु पार्वती वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing the words of her husband Siva, the delighted Parvati laughed and spoke these words. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्युवाच ।
भवदीयं वचस्तथ्यं युगान्ते सम्भविष्यति ।
तावच्च तामसी सृष्टिर्भवत्विति मतं मम ॥ २२॥

English
Pārvatī said :—  "Your words will be true at the end of this Yuga. Till then, I think, there can be the Tāmasaic creations as well. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा प्रलयः स्याद्वै सत्यं मे व्याहृतं शिव ।
प्रमाणीक्रियतां नाथ त्वदीयास्मि त्वदाश्रया ॥ २३॥

English
Otherwise the dissolution will take place even now. O Siva, let the words uttered by me be proved true. O lord, I belong to you. I depend on you. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं च दारुका देवी राक्षसी शक्तिका मम ।
बलिष्ठा राक्षसीनां च रक्षोराज्यं प्रशास्तु च ॥ २४॥

English
This Raksasi Darukā is my own Saktī. She is the most powerful among the Raksasis. Let her rule over the kingdom of the Raksasas. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इमा राक्षसपत्न्यस्तु प्रसविष्यन्ति पुत्रकान् ।
ते सर्वे मिलिताश्चैव वने वासाय मे मताः ॥ २५॥

English
These wives of the Raksasas will give birth to sons. I have permitted all of them to live jointly in the forest.


26 *Sūta said:—* On hearing these words of Pārvatī, lord Śiva was delighted in his mind and spoke these words. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
इति ब्रवीषि त्वं वै चेच्छृणु मद्वचनं प्रिये ।
स्थास्याम्यस्मिन्वने प्रीत्या भक्तानां पालनाय च ॥ २७॥

English
Śiva said:—  O beloved, if you say so, please listen to my words. I will stay in this forest with pleasure for protecting the devotees. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र मे वर्णधर्मस्थो दर्शनं प्रीतिसंयुतम् ।
करिष्यति च यो वै स चक्रवर्ती भविष्यति ॥ २८॥

English
He who maintains the discipline of rites and activities prescribed for different castes and has a sight of me with pleasure here will become an emperor. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा कलिपर्याये सत्यस्यादौ नृपेश्वरः ।
महासेनयुतो यो वै वीरसेनेति विश्रुतः ॥ २९॥

स मे भक्त्यातिविक्रान्तो दर्शनं मे करिष्यति ।
दर्शनं मे स कृत्वैव चक्रवर्ती भविष्यति ॥ ३०॥

English
Or, at the end of the recurring cycle of Kali or in the beginning of Kṛta Age, the leading king equipped with a vast army, famous in the name of Virasena son of Mahāsena, will have a sight of me. He will be a very valorous devotee of mine. After viewing me he will become an emperor. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं दम्पती तौ च कृत्वा हास्यं परस्परम् ।
स्थितौ तत्र स्वयं साक्षान्महोतीकारकौ द्विजाः ॥ ३१॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Exchanging pleasantries thus, the couple, O brahmins, indulging in great sports remained there. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्योतिर्लिङ्गस्वरूपो हि नाम्ना नागेश्वरः शिवः ।
नागेश्वरी शिवा देवी बभूव च सतां प्रियौ ॥ ३२॥

English
The Jyotirlinga form of Śiva came to be known as Nāgeśvara. Goddess Śivā became Nāgeśvari. Both of them are favourites of the devotees. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
वीरसेनः कथं तत्र यास्यते दारुकावने ।
कथमर्चिष्यति शिवं त्वं तद्वद महामते ॥ ३३॥

English
The sages said:—  O intelligent one, how will Virasena go to Dārukā vana and worship Śiva ? Please narrate it. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
निषधे सुन्दरे देशे क्षत्रियाणां कुले च सः ।
महासेनसुतो वीरसेनश्चैव शिवप्रियः ॥ ३४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Virasena, son of Mahāsena, was a great favourite of Śiva. He was born of a Kṣatriya family in the beautiful land of Niṣadha.¹⁶⁹ (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवेशार्चनं कृत्वा तपः परमदुष्करम् ।
चकार वीरसेनो वै वर्षाणां द्वादशावधिः ॥ ३५॥

English
Worshipping the earthen image of the lord, Virasena performed a severe penance, lasting for twelve years. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नो देवेशः प्रत्यक्षं प्राह शङ्करः ।
काष्ठस्य मत्स्यिकां कृत्वा त्रपुधातुविलेपनाम् ॥ ३६॥

विधाय योगमायां च दास्यामि वीरसेनक ।
तां गृहीत्वा प्रविश्यैनं नौभिः सह व्रजाधुना ॥ ३७॥

ततस्त्वं तत्र गत्वा च विवरे च कृते मया ।
प्रविश्य च तदा पूजां कृत्वा नागेश्वरस्य च ॥ ३८॥

ततः पाशुपतं प्राप्य हत्वा च राक्षसीमुखान् ।
मयि दृष्टे तदा किञ्चिन्न्यूनं ते न भविष्यति ॥ ३९॥

English
Then Śiva, the delighted lord of the gods, became visible to him and said :—Make a fish-like canoe of wood. Coat it with tin and other metals. O Virasena, I shall create Yogamāyā and give it to you. Enter the canoe accompanied by Yogamāyā and your men. Proceed to the island in the Western ocean.¹⁷⁰ Enter the crevice made by me. Perform the worship of Nāgeśvara. Thereafter secure the missile Pāśupata and slay the Rākṣasī and others. When I appear before you; you will not remain deficient in anything. (36-39.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्वत्याश्च बलं चैव सम्पूर्णं वै भविष्यति ।
अन्ये च म्लेच्छरूपा ये भविष्यन्ति वने शुभाः ॥ ४०॥

English
Pārvatī's strength will grow to be complete. The Mlecchas of the forest will become pious and auspicious. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा शङ्करस्तत्र वीरसेनं हि दुःखहा ।
कृत्वा कृपां च महतीं तत्रैवान्तर्दधे प्रभुः ॥ ४१॥

English
After saying this to Virasena and bestowing his mercy on everyone lord Śiva, the destroyer of miseries, vanished there itself. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इति दत्तवरः सोऽपि शिवेन परमात्मना ।
शक्तः सर्वं तदा कर्तुं सम्बभूव न संशयः ॥ ४२॥

English
Having received the boon form Śiva the supreme soul, Virasena became competent to do everything, of course. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं नागेश्वरो देव उत्पन्नो ज्योतिषां पतिः ।
लिङ्गरूपस्त्रिलोकस्य सर्वकामप्रदः सदा ॥ ४३॥

English
Thus is the manifestation of Nāgeśvara, the lord in the form of a Jyotirlinga conferring the attainment of desires to the three worlds. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद्यः श‍ृणुयान्नित्यं नागेशोद्भवमादरात् ।
सर्वान्कामानियाद्धीमान्महापातकनाशनान् ॥ ४४॥

English
He who listens to the origin of Nāgeśa respectfully attains all desires. He becomes intelligent. And his sins are destroyed. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा पार्वत्याः स्वस्त्रियाः प्रभुः ।
प्रसन्नमानसो भूत्वा शङ्करो वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ २६॥
