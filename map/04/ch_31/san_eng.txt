### **CHAPTER THIRTYONE - (The greatness of Rāmeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि लिङ्गं रामेश्वराभिधम् ।
उत्पन्नं च यथा पूर्वमृषयः श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Hereafter I shall explain how the phallic image Rāmeśvara originated formerly. O sages, listen to it respectfully. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा विष्णुः पृथिव्यां चावततार सतां प्रियः ॥ २॥

English
Formerly, Viṣṇu the favourite of the good, took his incarnation as Rāma on the earth. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र सीता हृता विप्रा रावणेनोरुमायिना ।
प्रापिता स्वगृहं सा हि लङ्कायां जनकात्मजा ॥ ३॥

English
O brahmins, Rāma's wife Sītā, the daughter of Janaka, was kidnapped by Rāvaṇa the wielder of great Māyā and taken to his abode in Laṅkā. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्वेषणपरस्तस्याः किष्किन्धाख्यां पुरीमगात् ।
सुग्रीवहितकृद्भूत्वा वालिनं सञ्जघान ह ॥ ४॥

English
Searching for her, Rāma went to the city called Kīskindhā.[171] Having alliance with Sugriva for his benefit he killed Bāli. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र स्थित्वा कियत्कालं तदन्वेषणतत्परः ।
सुग्रीवाद्यैर्लक्ष्मणेन विचारं कृतवान्स वै ॥ ५॥

English
After staying there for sometime, he held consultations with Lakṣmaṇa, Sugriva and others eager to find her out. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
कपीन् सम्प्रेषयामास चतुर्दिक्षु नृपात्मजः ।
हनुमत्प्रमुखान् रामस्तदन्वेषणहेतवे ॥ ६॥

English
The prince Rāma sent the monkeys, the chief of whom was Hanūmat, to various directions in order to find her out. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ ज्ञात्वा गतां लङ्कां सीतां कपिवराननात् ।
सीताचूडामणिं प्राप्य मुमुदे सोऽति राघवः ॥ ७॥

English
On getting the crest-jewel of Sītā and on knowing from the excellent monkey that she was in Laṅkā, Rāma rejoiced much. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सकपीशस्तदा रामो लक्ष्मणेन युतो द्विजाः ।
सुग्रीवप्रमुखैः पुण्यैर्वानरैर्बलवत्तरैः ॥ ८॥

पद्मैरष्टादशाख्यैश्च ययौ तीरं पयोनिधेः ।
दक्षिणे सागरे यो वै दृश्यते लवणाकरः ॥ ९॥

English
O brahmins, accompanied by Hanuman the chief of monkeys, Lakṣmaṇa, Sugriva and other monkey chiefs of great might and a vast army of monkeys eighteen thousand billions in number Rāma reached the shores of the Southern salt ocean. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रागत्य स्वयं रामो वेलायां संस्थितो हि सः ।
वानरैः सेव्यमानस्तु लक्ष्मणेन शिवप्रियः ॥ १०॥

English
Reaching there, Rāma the favourite of Śiva stationed himself on the shore, being served by the monkeys and his brother Lakṣmaṇa. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हा जानकि कुतो याता कदा चेयं मिलिष्यति ।
अगाधः सागरश्चैवातार्या सेना च वानरी ॥ ११॥

राक्षसो गिरिधर्ता च महाबलपराक्रमः ।
लङ्काख्यो दुर्गमो दुर्ग इन्द्रजित्तनयोऽस्य वै ॥ १२॥

इत्येवं स विचार्यैव तटे स्थित्वा सलक्ष्मणः ।
आश्वासितो वनौकोभिरङ्गदादिपुरः सरैः ॥ १३॥

English
"Alas where has Jānakī gone? When will she meet me. The ocean is very deep. The army of the monkeys is to be taken across. The Rākṣasa of great strength and valour, the uplifter of the mountain Kailāsa has resorted to his stronghold Laṅkā. His son is Indrajit, the conqueror of Indra. Thus thought Rāma in the company of Lakṣmaṇa standing on the shore itself. He was appeased by Aṅgada and other monkeys. (11-13.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र राघवः शैवसत्तमः ।
उवाच भ्रातरं प्रीत्या जलार्थी लक्ष्मणाभिधम् ॥ १४॥

English
Then Rāma the excellent devotee of Śiva, desirous of water, lovingly spoke to his brother Lakṣmaṇa. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीराम उवाच ।
भ्रातर्लक्ष्मण वीरेशाहं जलार्थी पिपासितः ।
तदानय द्रुतं पाथो वानरैः कैश्चिदेव हि ॥ १५॥

English
Rāma said:—  O brother Lakṣmaṇa, lord of heroes, I am thirsty. I desire water. You get some water quickly through some monkey. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वानरास्तत्र ह्यधावन्त दिशो दश ।
नीत्वा जलं च ते प्रोचुः प्रणिपत्य पुरः स्थिताः ॥ १६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing it the monkeys ran in all directions. They brought water, bowed to Rāma and standing before him said. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वानरा ऊचुः ।
जलं च गृह्यतां स्वामिन्नानीतं तत्त्वदाज्ञया ।
महोत्तमं च सुस्वादु शीतलं प्राणतर्पणम् ॥ १७॥

English
The monkeys said:—  O lord, at your behest we have brought excellent water, sweet, cool and refreshing. Please take it. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो भूत्वा कृपादृष्ट्या विलोक्य तान् ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा रामचन्द्रोऽसौ स्वयं जग्राह तज्जलम् ॥ १८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing it Rāma was much delighted. With a benign glance at them he accepted the water offered. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
स शैवस्तज्जलं नीत्वा पातुमारब्धवान्यदा ।
तदा च स्मरणं जातमित्थमस्य शिवेच्छया ॥ १९॥

English
When Rāma, the devotee of Śiva, took the water and was about to drink it, it occurred to him at the will of Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
न कृतं दर्शनं शम्भोर्गृह्यते च जलं कथम् ।
स्वस्वामिनः परेशस्य सर्वानन्दप्रदस्य वै ॥ २०॥

English
"I have not yet had the sight of Śiva, the great lord, the bestower of all bliss. How can the water be taken in?" (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा च जलं मुक्तं तदा रघुवरेण च ।
पश्चाच्च पार्थिवीं पूजां चकार रघुनन्दनः ॥ २१॥

English
After saying this, he drank water and then performed the worship of Siva's earthen image. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
आवाहनादिकांश्चैव ह्युपचारान्प्रकल्प्य वै ।
विधिवत्षोडशं प्रीत्या देवमानर्च शङ्करम् ॥ २२॥

English
He worshipped Siva performing all the sixteen ancillary services like invocation etc. with devotion according to the rules obtaining. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणिपातैः स्तवैर्दिव्यैः शिवं सन्तोष्य यत्नतः ।
प्रार्थयामास सद्भक्त्या स रामः शङ्करं मुदा ॥ २३॥

English
After propitiating him with obeisances, divine eulogies etc. strenuously, Rama joyously prayed to Siva with great devotion. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीराम उवाच ।
स्वामिन् शम्भो महादेव सर्वदा भक्तवत्सल ।
पाहि मां शरणापन्नं त्वद्भक्तं दीनमानसम् ॥ २४॥

English
# Rāma said:—  O lord Siva, O great lord, always favourably disposed towards your devotees, save me your devotee, dejected in the mind and seeking refuge in you. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
एतज्जलमगाधं च वारिधेर्भवतारण ।
रावणाख्यो महावीरो राक्षसो बलवत्तरः ॥ २५॥

English
O Bhavatāraṇa (one who takes devotees across the ocean of worldly existence), this ocean is very deep. The Rākṣasa Rāvaṇa is heroic and mighty. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
वानराणां बलं ह्येतच्चञ्चलं युद्धसाधनम् ।
मम कार्यं कथं सिद्धं भविष्यति प्रियाप्तये ॥ २६॥

English
The army of monkeys is a very fickle instrument of war. How can my task be achieved resulting in pleasing acquisition. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्देव त्वया कार्यं साहाय्यं मम सुव्रत ।
साहाय्यं ते विना नाथ मम कार्यं हि दुर्लभम् ॥ २७॥

English
In that, O lord of good rites, help must be rendered to me. O lord, without your help my purpose is difficult to be achieved. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वदीयो रावणोऽपीह दुर्जयः सर्वथाखिलैः ।
त्वद्दत्तवरदृप्तश्च महावीरस्त्रिलोकजित् ॥ २८॥

English
Rāvaṇa is your devotee. He is invincible in every respect to everyone. He is haughty due to the boons granted by you. He is a great hero who has conquered the three worlds. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अप्यहं तव दासोऽस्मि त्वदधीनश्च सर्वथा ।
विचार्येति त्वया कार्यः पक्षपातः सदाशिव ॥ २९॥

English
O Sadasiva, I too am your slave, in every respect subservient to you. Considering this, you shall be partial to me. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं स च सम्प्रार्थ्य नमस्कृत्य पुनः पुनः ।
तदा जय जयेत्युच्चैरुद्घोषैः शङ्करेति च ॥ ३०॥

इति स्तुत्वा शिवं तत्र मन्त्रध्यानपरायणः ।
पुनः पूजां ततः कृत्वा स्वाम्यग्रे स ननर्त ह ॥ ३१॥

English
# *Sūta said:—* After praying and bowing to the lord again and again, he repeated loudly, "O Siva, be victorious, be victorious" and eulogised Siva. Repeating the mantras and meditating, he performed the worship again and danced before the lord. (30-31.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रेमविक्लिन्नहृदयो गल्लनादं यदाकरोत् ।
तदा च शङ्करो देवः सुप्रसन्नो बभूव ह ॥ ३२॥

English
With the heart drenched with devotional love he produced the throat-sound. Lord Siva was much delighted. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
साङ्गः सपरिवारश्च ज्योतीरूपो महेश्वरः ।
यथोक्तरूपममलं कृत्वाविरभवद् द्रुतम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Assuming the pure form as mentioned and accompanied by all his attendants, lord Śiva of brilliant form appeared before him immediately. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सन्तुष्टहृदयो रामभक्त्या महेश्वरः ।
शिवमस्तु वरं ब्रूहि रामेति स तदाब्रवीत् ॥ ३४॥

English
Then, satisfied in his heart with Rāma's devotion lord Śiva said—"O Rāma, may there be welfare to you. Mention the boon you wish to have." (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रूपं च तदा दृष्टवा सर्वे पूतास्ततः स्वयम् ।
कृतवान् राघवः पूजां शिवधर्मपरायणः ॥ ३५॥

English
On seeing that form, they became sanctified. Rāma himself, interested in Śaiva cult, performed the worship. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तुतिं च विविधां कृत्वा प्रणिपत्य शिवं मुदा ।
जयं च प्रार्थयामास रावणाजौ तदात्मनः ॥ ३६॥

English
Eulogising in diverse ways and bowing to Śiva joyously he requested for his victory in the imminent fight with Rāvaṇa. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नहृदयो रामभक्त्या महेश्वरः ।
जयोऽस्तु ते महाराज प्रीत्या स पुनरब्रवीत् ॥ ३७॥

English
Delighted in his heart at Rām's devotion, lord Śiva said lovingly—"O great king, may you be victorious". (37.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवदत्तं जयं प्राप्य ह्यनुज्ञां समवाप्य च ।
पुनश्च प्रार्थयामास साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तकः ॥ ३८॥

English
Securing the blessing of victory granted by Śiva and receiving the permission of the lord he prayed again with palms joined in reverence and head bent down. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीराम उवाच ।
त्वया स्थेयमिह स्वामिंल्लोकानां पावनाय च ।
परेषामुपकारार्थं यदि तुष्टोऽसि शङ्कर ॥ ३९॥

English
Rāma said:—  O Śiva, if you are pleased, you shall stay here, O lord, to sanctify the worlds and to render help to the people. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तु शिवस्तत्र लिङ्गरूपोऽभवत्तदा ।
रामेश्वरश्च नाम्ना वै प्रसिद्धो जगतीतले ॥ ४०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus implored Śiva took up the phallic image named Rāmeśvara. It is now famous on the surface of the world. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
रामस्तु तत्प्रभावाद्वै सिन्धुमुत्तीर्य चाञ्जसा ।
रावणादीन्निहत्याशु राक्षसान्प्राप तां प्रियाम् ॥ ४१॥

English
It was by Śiva's favour that Rāma crossed the ocean soon, killed Rāvaṇa and other Rākṣasas and regained his beloved. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
रामेश्वरस्य महिमाद्भुतोऽभूद्भुवि चातुलः ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदश्चैव सर्वदा भक्तकामदः ॥ ४२॥

English
The greatness of Rāmeśvara became wonderful and unequalled in the world. It yields worldly pleasures and salvation and bestows all desires on the devotees always. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यगङ्गाजलेनैव स्नापयिष्यति यः शिवम् ।
रामेश्वरं च सद्भक्त्या स जीवन्मुक्त एव हि ॥ ४३॥

English
He who performs the ablution of Śiva with the waters of the divine Gaṅgā and worships Rāmeśvara with great devotion becomes a living liberated soul indeed. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
इह भुक्त्वाखिलान्भोगान्देवानां दुर्लभानपि ।
अन्ते प्राप्य परं ज्ञानं कैवल्यं प्राप्नुयाद् ध्रुवम् ॥ ४४॥

English
After enjoying all pleasures here, rare even to the gods and deriving perfect knowledge in the end he will certainly attain salvation. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्यातं ज्योतिर्लिगं शिवस्य तु ।
रामेश्वराभिधं दिव्यं श‍ृण्वतां पापहारकम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Thus has been narrated to you the story of Rāmeśvara, the Jyotirlinga of Śiva. It is divine. It destroys the sins of the devotees who listen to it. (45.)
