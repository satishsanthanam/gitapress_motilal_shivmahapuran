### **CHAPTER THIRTYTWO - (The narrative of Sudehā and Sudharmā)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
अतः परं च घुश्मेशं ज्योतिर्लिङ्गमुदाहृतम् ।
तस्यैव च सुमाहात्म्यं श्रूयतामृषिसत्तमाः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O excellent sages, the next Jyotirlinga to be mentioned is Ghuśmeśa. May its greatness be listened to. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणस्यां दिशि श्रेष्ठो गिरिर्देवेति संज्ञकः ।
महाशोभान्वितो नित्यं राजतेऽद्भुतदर्शनः ॥ २॥

English
There is the excellent mountain in the Southern region named Deva.¹⁷² It shines with great splendour and is very wonderful to view. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यैव निकटे कश्चिद्भारद्वाजकुलोद्भवः ।
सुधर्मा नाम विप्रश्च न्यवसद् ब्रह्मवित्तमः ॥ ३॥

English
A certain brahmin Sudharmā who was the most excellent among the knowers of Brahman and who was born of Bhāradvāja family lived near it. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य प्रिया सुदेहा च शिवधर्मपरायणा ।
पतिसेवापरा नित्यं गृहकर्मविचक्षणा ॥ ४॥

English
His beloved wife Sudehā was interested in Śaiva cult and sacred rites. She was skilful in all household activities and she devoutly served her husband. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मा च द्विजश्रेष्ठो देवतातिथिपूजकः ।
वेदमार्गपरो नित्यमग्निसेवापरायणः ॥ ५॥

English
The excellent brahmin Sudharmā worshipped gods and guests. He was devotedly attached to the Vedic path and scrupulously maintained the sacrificial fires. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिकालसन्ध्यया युक्तः सूर्यरूपसमद्युतिः ।
शिष्याणां पाठकश्चैव वेदशास्त्रविचक्षणः ॥ ६॥

English
He had the brilliance of the Sun. He performed Sandhyā prayers thrice a day. He taught disciples and was an expert in the Vedas and Śāstras both. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
धनवांश्च परो दाता सौजन्यगुणभाजनः ।
शिवकर्मरतो नित्यं शैवः शैवजनप्रियः ॥ ७॥

English
He was affluent and had also the qualities of gentlemanliness. He was a liberal donor, a devotee of Śiva, a favourite of his followers and he engaged himself in the sacred rites of Śiva-cult. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
आयुर्बहु व्यतीयाय तस्य धर्मं प्रकुर्वतः ।
पुत्रश्च नाभवत्तस्य ऋतुः स्यादफलः स्त्रियाः ॥ ८॥

English
A long period in his life thus passed when he continued his religious rites but no son was born to him. His wife's menstrual periods were fruitless. She did not conceive. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन दुःखं कृतं नैव वस्तुज्ञानपरेण हि ।
आत्मनस्तारकश्चात्मा ह्यात्मनः पावनश्च सः ॥ ९॥

English
He did not grieve over this because he realised the reality of different things. He knew that the soul is the uplifter of the soul and the soul is the sanctifier of the soul. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं मानसं धृत्वा दुःखं न कृतवान्स्तदा ।
सुदेहा च तदा दुःखं चकारापुत्रसम्भवम् ॥ १०॥

English
Thinking thus he did not feel aggrieved even mentally. But Sudehā was distressed for not having a son. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
नित्यं च स्वामिनं सा वै प्रार्थयद्यत्नसाधने ।
पुत्रोत्पादनहेतोश्च सर्वविद्याविशारदम् ॥ ११॥

English
Everyday she implored her learned husband to strive for the acquisition of a son. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि स्त्रियं तदाभर्त्स्य किं पुत्रश्च करिष्यति ।
का माता कः पिता पुत्रः को बन्धुश्च प्रियश्च कः ॥ १२॥

सर्वं स्वार्थपरं देवि त्रिलोक्यां नात्र संशयः ।
जानीहि त्वं विशेषेण बुद्ध्या शोकं न वै कुरु ॥ १३॥

तस्माद्देवि त्वया दुःखं त्यजनीयं सुनिश्चितम् ।
नित्यं मह्यं त्वया नैव कथनीयं शुभव्रते ॥ १४॥

एवं तां सन्निवार्यैव भगवद्धर्मतत्परः ।
आसीत्परमसन्तुष्टो द्वन्द्वदुःखं समत्यजत् ॥ १५॥

English
He used to reproach her saying, “O gentle lady, there is no son, no mother, no father, no lover. Every one is selfishly inclined in the world, there is no doubt about this. Know this particularly with a keen intellect. Do not be aggrieved. O gentle lady, misery shall be eschewed by you, certainly. O one of auspicious rites, you shall not pester me like this everyday.” After enlightening her, he became interested in the worship of the lord. He was perfectly contented. He did not feel the misery or joy of mutually contrary interests. (12-15.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिच्च सुदेहा वै गेहे च सहवासिनः ।
जगाम प्रियगोष्ठ्यर्थं विवादस्तत्र सङ्गतः ॥ १६॥

English
Once Sudehā went to the neighbour’s house for a friendly chat but a quarrel arose there. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पत्नी स्त्रीस्वभावाच्च भर्त्सिता सा तया तदा ।
उक्ता चेति दुरुक्त्या वै सुदेहा विप्रकामिनी ॥ १७॥

English
She was reproached by the neighbour’s wife as natural to women. Sudehā was aggrieved with her harsh words. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्न्युवाच ।
अपुत्रिणि कथं गर्वं कुरुषे पुत्रिणी ह्यहम् ।
मद्धनं भोक्ष्यते पुत्रो धनं ते कश्च भोक्ष्यते ॥ १८॥

English
*The neighbourly woman said:—*  O sonless woman, why are you arrogant? I have a son who will inherit my wealth. But there is none to inherit your property. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नूनं हरिष्यते राजा त्वद्धनं नात्र संशयः ।
धिग्धिक्त्वां ते धनं धिक्च धिक् ते मानं हि वन्ध्यके ॥ १९॥

English
Undoubtedly the king will confiscate your estate. O barren woman, fie upon you, upon your wealth, upon your honour and prestige. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
भर्त्सिता ताभिरिति सा गृहमागत्य दुःखिता ।
स्वामिने कथयामास तदुक्तं सर्वमादरात् ॥ २०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus reproached by her she returned home depressed. She narrated to her husband all what her neighbour had said to her. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणोऽपि तदा दुःखं न चकार सुबुद्धिमान् ।
कथितं कथ्यतामेव यद्भावि तद्भवेत्प्रिये ॥ २१॥

English
The intelligent brahmin was not grieved by hearing it. He said “Let her say what she wishes, O beloved, what is destined to happen will happen.” (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं च तदा तेन ह्याश्वस्तापि पुनः पुनः ।
न तदा सात्यजद्दुःखं ह्याग्रहं कृतवत्यसौ ॥ २२॥

English
Thus pacified and consoled again and again by her husband she did not stop worrying. She again pleaded. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदेहोवाच ॥

यथा तथा त्वया पुत्रः समुत्पाद्यः प्रियोऽसि मे ।
त्यक्षयामि ह्यन्यथाहं च देहं देहभृतां वर ॥ २३॥

English
*Sudehā said:—*  You are my husband. Somehow you shall beget a son. O excellent among men, otherwise I shall cast off this body. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवमुक्तं तया श्रुत्वा सुधर्मा ब्राह्मणोत्तमः ।
शिवं सस्मार मनसा तदाग्रहनिपीडितः ॥ २४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing what she said, Sudharma the excellent brahmin, harassed by her importunities, mentally remembered Siva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्नेरग्रेऽक्षिपत्पुष्पद्वयं विप्रो ह्यतन्द्रितः ।
मनसा दक्षिणं पुष्पं तन्मेने पुत्रकामदम् ॥ २५॥

English
The alert brahmin offered two flowers to the sacrificial fire. He thought in his mind that the right side flower would yield a son. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं कृत्वा पणं पत्नीमुवाच ब्राह्मणः स च ।
अनयोर्ग्राह्यमेकं ते पुष्पं पुत्रफलाप्तये ॥ २६॥

English
Making this as a condition the brahmin told his wife. "You shall take one of these' two flowers to decide about the acquisition of a son" (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तया च मनसा धृत्वा पुत्रश्चैव भवेन्मम ।
तदा च स्वामिना यच्च धृतं पुष्पं समेतु माम् ॥ २७॥

इत्युक्त्वा च तया तत्र नमस्कृत्य शिवं तदा ।
नत्वा चाग्निं पुनः प्रार्थ्य गृहीतं पुष्पमेककम् ॥ २८॥

English
She thought "I will surely have a son. Let the insignia flower come to me." Then she bowed to Siva and the sacrificial fire." After praying again she picked up one of the flowers. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वामिना चिन्तितं यच्च तद् गृहीतं तया न हि ।
सुदेहया विमोहेन शिवेच्छासम्भवेन वै ॥ २९॥

English
The insignia flower was missed by her due to delusion caused by Siva's desire. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् दृष्ट्वा पुरुषश्चैव निःश्वासं पर्यमोचयत् ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजमुवाच निजकामिनीम् ॥ ३०॥

English
On seeing it the husband sighed deeply. Remembering the lotus-like feet of Siva he spoke to his beloved. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मोवाच ।
निर्मितं चेश्वरेणैव कथं चैवान्यथा भवेत् ।
आशां त्यज प्रिये त्वं च परिचर्यां कुरु प्रभोः ॥ ३१॥

English
Sudharmā said:—  O beloved, how can what is ordained by the lord be otherwise. Cast off your vain hopes. Render service to the lord. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा तु स्वयं विप्र आशां परिविहाय च ।
धर्मकार्यरतः सोऽभूच्छङ्करध्यानतत्परः ॥ ३२॥

English
After saying so and giving up hope himself, the brahmin became engaged in sacred rites and meditation on Siva. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सा सुदेहाग्रहं नैव मुमोचात्मजकाम्यया ।
प्रत्युवाच पतिं प्रेम्णा साञ्जलिर्नतमस्तका ॥ ३३॥

English
Yearning for a son Sudeha did not leave off her pleadings. With palms joined in reverence and bending her head she lovingly said to her husband. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदेहोवाच ।
मयि पुत्रो न चास्त्वन्यां पत्नीं कुरु मदाज्ञया ।
तस्यां नूनं सुतश्चैव भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ ३४॥

English
Sudehā said:—  Let there be no son born of me. With my consent you shall take another wife. Undoubtedly she will bear you a son. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तदैव प्रर्थितो वै स ब्रह्मणः शैवसत्तमः ।
उवाच स्वप्रियां तां च सुदेहां धर्मतत्परः ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Requested thus by his beloved wife, that brahmin the most excellent of the devotees of Śiva and fond of sacred rites spoke to her. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मोवाच ।
त्वदीयं च मदीयं च सर्वं दुःखं गतं ध्रुवम् ।
तस्मात्त्वं धर्मविघ्नं च प्रिये मा कुरु साम्प्रतम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Sudharmā said:—  Our miseries have gone off, O beloved, do not put obstacles in our holy rites now. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वारिता सा च स्वमातुः पुत्रिकां तदा ।
गृहमानीय भर्तारं वृणु त्वेनामिदं जगौ ॥ ३७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Although prevented thus, she brought her mother's younger daughter to her house and said to her husband "Take her." (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मोवाच ।
इदानीं वदसि त्वं च मत्प्रियेयं ततः पुनः ।
पुत्रसूश्च यदा स्याद्वै तदा स्पर्धां करिष्यसि ॥ ३८॥

English
Sudharmā said:—  Now you say that she must be my beloved wife. But should she give birth to a son you will begin to be jealous of her. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ता तेन पतिना सा सुदेहा च तत्प्रिया ।
पुनः प्राह करौ बद्ध्वा सुधर्माणं पतिं द्विजाः ॥ ३९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, when thus warned by her husband, Sudehā joined her palms in reverence and spoke to him. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
नाहं स्पर्धां भगिन्या वै करिष्ये द्विजसत्तम ।
उपयच्छस्व पुत्रार्थमिमामाज्ञापयामि च ॥ ४०॥

English
"O excellent brahmin, I will not be jealous of my own sister. Please marry her and beget a son. I urge upon you." (40.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं प्रार्थितः सोऽपि सुधर्मा प्रियया तया ।
घुश्मां तां समुपायंस्त विवाहविधिना द्विजः ॥ ४१॥

English
Thus implored by her, Sudharmā married that lady Ghuśmā with due ceremonies. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तां परिणीयाथ प्रार्थयामास तां द्विजः ।
त्वदीयेयं कनिष्ठा हि सदा पोष्याऽनघे प्रिये ॥ ४२॥

English
After marrying her he requested Sudehā, "O my pious beloved, this is your younger sister. You must bring her up." (42.)

---

Sanskrit
उक्त्वैवं स च धर्मात्मा सुधर्मा शैवसत्तमः ।
यथायोग्यं चकाराशु धर्मसङ्ग्रहमात्मनः ॥ ४३॥

English
After saying this, Sudharmā, the most excellent devotee of Śiva, pursued his sacred rites to the utmost with his mind leaning towards virtue. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
सा चापि मातृपुत्रीं तां सखीवत्पर्यवर्त्तत ।
परित्यज्य विरोधं हि पुपोषाहर्निशं प्रिया ॥ ४४॥

English
Sudehā served her sister like a hired wench eschewing all opposition and lovingly nurtured her. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
कनिष्ठा चैव या पत्नी स्वस्रनुज्ञामवाप्य च ।
पार्थिवान्सा चकाराशु नित्यमेकोत्तरं शतम् ॥ ४५॥

English
The junior wife with the consent of her sister, made hundred and one earthen idols every day. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
विधानपूर्वकं घुश्मा सोपचारसमन्वितम् ।
कृत्वा तान्प्राक्षिपत्तत्र तडागे निकटस्थिते ॥ ४६॥

English
According to the sacred rules Ghuśmā rendered devotional services to these images and threw them in the neighbouring lake. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं नित्यं सा चकार शिवपूजां स्वकामदाम् ।
विसृज्य पुनरावाह्य तत्सपर्य्याविधानतः ॥ ४७॥

English
Thus she performed the wish-yielding worship of Śiva every day leaving them off and invoking them again in accordance with the rules of devotional service. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
कुर्वन्त्या नित्यमेवं हि तस्याः शङ्करपूजनम् ।
लक्षसङ्ख्याभवत्पूर्णा सर्वकामफलप्रदा ॥ ४८॥

English
Performing the worship of Śiva thus regularly she completed a hundred-thousand images and their worship which would yield the benefits of all desires. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपया शङ्करस्यैव तस्याः पुत्रो व्यजायत ।
सुन्दरः सुभगश्चैव कल्याणगुणभाजनः ॥ ४९॥

English
By the mercy of Siva a son was born to her. He was beautiful, fortunate and endowed with all auspicious qualities. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा परमप्रीतः स विप्रो धर्मवित्तमः ।
अनासक्तः सुखं भेजे ज्ञानधर्मपरायणः ॥ ५०॥

English
On seeing him the brahmin was much delighted. Being the most excellent of all who knew virtue and engrossed in the virtues of knowledge he enjoyed the pleasure with due detachment. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदेहा तावदस्यास्तु स्पर्धामुग्रां चकार सा ।
प्रथमं शीतलं तस्या हृदयं ह्यसिवत्पुनः ॥ ५१॥

English
Then Sudeha grew fiercely jealous of her. Her heart which was cool and quiet before began to blaze like fire now. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

सावधानेन मनसा श्रूयतां तन्मुनीश्वराः ॥ ५२॥

English
O great sages, may the despicable event that followed, causing misery, be heard with attention. (52.)
