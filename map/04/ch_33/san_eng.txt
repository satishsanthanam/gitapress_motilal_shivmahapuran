### **CHAPTER THIRTYTHREE - (Origin and glory of the Jyotirlinga Ghuśmeśvara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
पुत्रं दृष्ट्वा कनिष्ठाया ज्येष्ठा दुःखमुपागता ।
विरोधं सा चकाराशु न सहन्ती च तत्सुखम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On seeing the son of the younger sister, the elder sister was much distressed. Unable to see her pleasure she became inimical. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे पुत्रप्रसूतिं तां प्रशसंसुर्निरन्तरम् ।
तया तत्सह्यते न स्म शिशो रूपादिकं तथा ॥ २॥

English
Her people celebrated the birth of a son continually. But she could not bear the sight of the beautiful child. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सुप्रियं तनयं तं च पित्रोः सद्गुणभाजनम् ।
दृष्ट्वाभवत्तदा तस्या हृदयं तप्तमग्निवत् ॥ ३॥

English
On seeing the son, a great favourite of the parents and endowed with all good qualities, her heart began to blaze like fire. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे विप्राः कन्यां दातुं समागताः ।
विवाहं तस्य तत्रैव चकार विधिवच्च सः ॥ ४॥

English
In the meantime many brahmins came there offering their daughters in marriage. Sudharma celebrated the marriage of his son-duly. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मा घुश्मया सार्धमानन्दं परं गतः ।
सर्वे सम्बन्धिनस्तस्यां घुश्मायां मानमादधुः ॥ ५॥

English
Sudharma was extremely delighted in the company of Ghusma. All the relatives honoured Ghusma much. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा सा सुदेहा हि मनसि ज्वलिता तदा ।
अत्यन्तं दुःखमापन्ना हा हतास्मीति वादिनी ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing that, Sudeha blazed furiously in her mind. Extremely distressed she began to lament, "O I am doomed." (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मा गृहमागत्य वधूं पुत्रं विवाहितम् ।
उत्साहं दर्शयामास प्रियाभ्यां हर्षयन्निव ॥ ७॥

English
When the daughter-in-law came to the house and the son was duly married, Sudharmā evinced his enthusiasm to both the wives with an intention of delighting them equally. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अभवद् हर्षिता घुश्मा सुदेहा दुःखमागता ।
न सहन्ती सुखं तच्च दुःखं कृत्वापतद्भुवि ॥ ८॥

English
But Ghuśmā was delighted and Sudehā was distressed. Unable to bear her happiness, she fell on the ground. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
घुश्मावदद्वधूपुत्रौ त्वदीयौ न मदीयकौ ।
वधूः पुत्रश्च तां प्रीत्या प्रसूं श्वश्रूममन्यत ॥ ९॥

English
Ghuśmā said:—  “These are your own son and daughter-in-law not mine.” But the daughter-in-law considered Sudehā her mother-in-law and the son considered her his mother. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
भर्ता प्रियां तां ज्येष्ठां च मेने नैव कनिष्ठिकाम् ।
तथापि सा तदा ज्येष्ठा स्वान्तर्मलवती ह्यभूत् ॥ १०॥

English
The husband too loved the elder more than he loved the younger. Still the elder wife retained dirt in her mind. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्दिवसे ज्येष्ठा सा सुदेहा च दुःखिनी ।
हृदये सञ्चिचिन्तेति दुःखशान्तिः कथं भवेत् ॥ ११॥

English
One day the senior wife Sudehā, extremely distressed in her heart, thought how she could subdue her misery. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदेहोवाच ।
मदीयो हृदयाग्निश्च घुश्मानेत्रजलेन वै ।
भविष्यति ध्रुवं शान्तो नान्यथा दुःखजेन हि ॥ १२॥

English
Sudehā said:—  Certainly the fire in my heart can be tranquillised only through the tears of Ghuśmā caused by her misery and not otherwise. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अतोऽहं मारयाम्यद्य तत्पुत्रं प्रियवादिनम् ।
अग्रे भावि भवेदेवं निश्चयः परमो मम ॥ १३॥

English
So I shall kill her son today, the son that speaks pleasing words to her. What is destined to happen shall happen. This is my final decision. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
कदर्याणां विचारश्च कृत्याकृत्ये भवेन्नहि ।
कठोरः प्रायशो विप्राः सापत्नो भाव आत्महा ॥ १४॥

English
Śūta said:—  O brahmins, the inimical nature among co-wives is extremely ruthless and suicidal. The wicked and the mean do not ponder what shall be done or not done. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एकस्मिन्दिवसे ज्येष्ठा सुप्तं पुत्रं वधूयुतम् ।
चिच्छिदे निशि चाङ्गेषु गृहीत्वा छुरिकां च सा ॥ १५॥

English
One day during the night as the son was sleeping with his wife she took a big knife and cut off his limbs. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाङ्गं खण्डयामास रात्रौ घुश्मासुतस्य सा ।
नीत्वा सरसि तत्रैवाक्षिपद् दृप्ता महाबला ॥ १६॥

यत्र क्षिप्तानि लिङ्गानि घुश्मया नित्यमेव हि ।
तत्र क्षिप्त्वा समायाता सुष्वाप सुखमागता ॥ १७॥

English
The senior wife cut off the limbs of Ghuśmā’s son at night. Strong that she was she took them to the lake where the earthen images of Śiva were usually hurled by Ghuśmā. After casting off the limbs into the lake she returned and slept. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रातश्चैव समुत्थाय घुश्मा नित्यं तथाकरोत् ।
सुधर्मा च स्वयं श्रेष्ठो नित्यकर्म समाचरत् ॥ १८॥

English
In the morning Ghuśmā got up and performed her daily household duties. Sudharmā too performed his daily sacred rites. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे सा च ज्येष्ठा कार्यं गृहस्य वै ।
चकारानन्दसंयुक्ता सुशान्तहृदयानला ॥ १९॥

English
In the meantime the senior wife too got up and performed her duties with great pleasure, now that the fire in her heart had been tranquillised. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रातःकाले समुत्थाय वधूः शय्यां विलोक्य सा ।
रुधिरार्द्रां देहखण्डैर्युक्तां दुःखमुपागता ॥ २०॥

English
Getting up in the morning, the daughter-in-law saw to her horror the bed wet with blood and a few scattered pieces of the body. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
श्वश्रूं निवेदयामास पुत्रस्ते च कुतो गतः ।
शय्या च रुधिरार्द्रा वै दृश्यन्ते देहखण्डकाः ॥ २१॥

English
She told her mother-in-law, "where has your son gone? The bed is wet with blood. Pieces of the body are also seen." (21.)

---

Sanskrit
हा हतास्मि कृतं केन दुष्टं कर्म शुचिव्रते ।
इत्युच्चार्य रुरोदाति विविधं तत्प्रिया च सा ॥ २२॥

English
The daughter-in-law fond of her husband began to lament—"O I am doomed. O my mother of holy rites, who has perpetrated this wicked deed?" (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्येष्ठा दुःखं तदापन्ना हा हतास्मि किलेति च ।
बहिर्दुःखं चकारासौ मनसा हर्षसंयुता ॥ २३॥

English
The senior wife, though delighted inwardly, showed great distress outwardly saying "O I am doomed!" (23.)

---

Sanskrit
घुश्मा चापि तदा तस्या वध्वा दुःखं निशम्य सा ।
न चचाल व्रतात्तस्मान्नित्यपार्थिवपूजनात् ॥ २४॥

English
Ghuśmā heard the pitiable lamentation of her daughter-in-law but did not leave off her sacred rite, the daily worship of Śiva's earthen image. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मनश्चैवोत्सुकं नैव जातं तस्या मनागपि ।
भर्तापि च तथैवासीद्यावद् व्रतविधिर्भवेत् ॥ २५॥

English
Her mind did not exhibit any agitation. The husband too remained calm till the completion of the sacred rite. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
मध्याह्ने पूजनान्ते च दृष्ट्वा शय्यां भयावहाम् ।
तथापि न तदा किञ्चित्कृतं दुःखं हि घुश्मया ॥ २६॥

English
At noon when the worship was concluded she looked at the terrible bed. Still Ghuśmā did not show signs of grief. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
येनैव चार्पितश्चायं स वै रक्षां करिष्यति ।
भक्तप्रियः स विख्यातः कालकालः सतां गतिः ॥ २७॥

यदि नो रक्षिता शम्भुरीश्वरः प्रभुरेकलः ।
मालाकार इवासौ यान्युङ्क्ते तान्वियुनक्ति च ॥ २८॥

अद्य मे चिन्तया किं स्यादिति तत्त्वं विचार्य सा ।
न चकार तदा दुःखं शिवे धैर्यं समागता ॥ २९॥

English
"He who has given this boy will protect him too. The annihilator of Kāla, the goal of the good, is famous as a favourite of his devotee. Our protector, the sole lord, Śiva is like a gardener. He separates those whom he has united. What can I gain by worrying over it?" Realising this truth, being emboldened in dedicating herself to Śiva she did not grieve much. (27-29.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवांश्च गृहीत्वा सा पूर्ववत्स्वस्थमानसा ।
शम्भोर्नामान्युच्चरन्ती जगाम सरसस्तटे ॥ ३०॥

English
Taking the day's earthly phallic images she went towards the bank of the lake repeating the names of Śiva, maintaining the normalcy of her mind as afore. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षिप्त्वा च पार्थिवांस्तत्र परावर्तत सा यदा ।
तदा पुत्रस्तडागस्थो दृश्यते स्म तटे तया ॥ ३१॥

English
When she cast off the earthen phallic images and returned she saw her son standing on the bank of the lake. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्र उवाच ।
मातरेहि मिलिष्यामि मृतोऽहं जीवितोऽधुना ।
तव पुण्यप्रभावाद्धि कृपया शङ्करस्य वै ॥ ३२॥

English
The son said:—  I shall meet my mother. After dying I have been resuscitated, thanks to the power of her virtue and the mercy of Śiva. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
जीवितं तं सुतं दृष्ट्वा घुश्मा सा तत्प्रसूर्द्विजाः ।
प्रहृष्टा नाभवत्तत्र दुःखिता न यथा पुरा ॥ ३३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, on seeing her son restored to life the mother was not delighted as she was not distressed before. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्समये तत्र स्वाविरासीच्छिवो द्रुतम् ।
ज्योतीरूपो महेशश्च सन्तुष्टः प्रत्युवाच ह ॥ ३४॥

English
At that time, lord Siva, appeared before her immediately in the form of a mass of splendour. He said delightedly. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि वरं ब्रूहि दुष्टया मारितो ह्ययम् ।
एनां च मारयिष्यामि त्रिशूलेन वरानने ॥ ३५॥

English
Śiva said:—  O excellent-faced lady, I am delighted. Mention the boon you wish to have This boy had been killed by that wicked lady. I shall kill her with my trident. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
घुश्मा तदा वरं वव्रे सुप्रणम्य शिवं नता ।
रक्षणीया त्वया नाथ सुदेहेयं स्वसा मम ॥ ३६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Bowing down to Śiva she requested for the boon "O lord, Sudchā, my sister, must be protected by you." (36.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
अपकारः कृतस्तस्यामुपकारः कथं त्वया ।
क्रियते हननीया च सुदेहा दुष्टकारिणी ॥ ३७॥

English
Śiva said:—  Harm has been done by her. Why should you render help to her? Sudchā the perpetrator of wicked deeds must be killed. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
घुश्मोवाच ॥

तव दर्शनमात्रेण पातकं नैव तिष्ठति ।
इदानीं त्वां च वै दृष्ट्वा तत्पापं भस्मतां व्रजेत् ॥ ३८॥

English
Ghuśmā said:—  No sin stays, the moment you are seen. Now, on seeing you her sin will be reduced to ashes. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अपकारेषु यश्चैव ह्युपकारं करोति च ।
तस्य दर्शनमात्रेण पापं दूरतरं व्रजेत् ॥ ३९॥

English
'Sin is far removed at the mere sight of the person who renders help to those who harm him. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रुतं मया देव भगवद्वाक्यमद्भुतम् ।
तस्माच्चैवं कृतं येन क्रियतां च सदाशिव ॥ ४०॥

English
These wonderful words of the lord have been heard by me O lord. Hence, O Sadasiva let it not be done otherwise. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तस्तु तया तत्र प्रसन्नोऽत्यभवत्पुनः ।
महेश्वरः कृपासिन्धुः तामूचे भक्तवत्सलः ॥ ४१॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus requested by her, lord Śiva the ocean of mercy, favourably disposed towards his devotees was delighted and spoke thus. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
अन्यद्वरं ब्रूहि घुश्मे ददामि च हितं तव ।
त्वद्भक्त्या सुप्रसन्नोऽस्मि निर्विकारस्वभावतः ॥ ४२॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Ghuśmā, request for another boon. I shall grant you what is beneficial to you. I am delighted at your devotion and your nature of being free from aberrations. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सोवाच तद्वचः श्रुत्वा यदि देवो वरस्त्वया ।
लोकानां चैव रक्षार्थमत्र स्थेयं मदाख्यया ॥ ४३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* She said after hearing his words "If a boon is to be given by you, you shall stay here in my name for protecting the worlds." (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोवाच शिवस्तत्र सुप्रसन्नो महेश्वरः ।
स्थास्येऽत्र तव नाम्नाहं घुश्मेशाख्यः सुखप्रदः ॥ ४४॥

English
Then the delighted lord Siva said—"I shall stay here in your name. I shall be named Ghusmesa and I shall confer happiness. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
घुश्मेशाख्यं सुप्रसिद्धं लिङ्गं मे जायतां शुभम् ।
इदं सरस्तु लिङ्गानामालयं जायतां सदा ॥ ४५॥

English
May my phallic image named Ghusmesa be auspicious and famous. Let this lake be the storehouse of phallic images always. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छिवालयं नाम प्रसिद्धं भुवनत्रये ।
सर्वकामप्रदं ह्येतद्दर्शनात्स्यात्सदा सरः ॥ ४६॥

English
Hence it will be famous in the three worlds in the name of "Sivalaya."273 This lake shall always yield desires by its mere sight. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तव वंशे शतं चैकं पुरुषावधि सुव्रते ।
ईदृशाः पुत्रकाः श्रेष्ठा भविष्यन्ति न संशयः ॥ ४७॥

English
O lady of good rites, upto a hundred and one generations in your family sons of great excellence like this will be born undoubtedly. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
सुस्त्रीकाः सुधनाश्चैव स्वायुष्याश्च विचक्षणाः ।
विद्यावन्तो ह्युदाराश्च भुक्तिमुक्तिफलाप्तये ॥ ४८॥

English
They will have good wives, plenty of wealth, longevity and skill in everything. They will be learned and liberal. They will attain worldly pleasures and salvation. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
शतमेकोत्तरं चैव भविष्यन्ति गुणाधिकाः ।
ईदृशो वंशविस्तारो भविष्यति सुशोभनः ॥ ४९॥

English
They will be hundred and one endowed with many qualities. Your family will expand like this splendidly. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा च शिवस्तत्र लिङ्गरूपोऽभवत्तदा ।
घुश्मेशो नाम विख्यातः सरश्चैव शिवालयम् ॥ ५०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this Siva assumed the form of a phallic image named Ghusmesa. He became famous. The lake was called Sivalaya.[273] (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सुधर्मा स च घुश्मा च सुदेहा च समागताः ।
प्रदक्षिणं शिवस्याशु शतमेकोत्तरं दधुः ॥ ५१॥

English
Sudharma, Ghusma and Sudeha came there and circumambulated Siva hundred and one times. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजां कृत्वा महेशस्य मिलित्वा च परस्परम् ।
हित्वा चान्तर्मलं तत्र लेभिरे परमं सुखम् ॥ ५२॥

English
After performing the worship of lord Siva, fondly meeting one another and eschewing the dirt of the mind they attained great happiness. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रं दृष्ट्वा सुदेहा सा जीवितं लज्जिताभवत् ।
तौ क्षमाप्याचरद्विप्रा निजपापापहं व्रतम् ॥ ५३॥

English
On seeing the son regained to life, Sudeha was ashamed. O brahmins, she performed the expiatory rite for her sins and requested for forgiveness. (53.)

Sanskrit
घुश्मेशाख्यमिदं लिङ्गमित्थं जातं मुनीश्वराः ।
तद् दृष्ट्वा पूजयित्वा हि सुखं संवर्धते सदा ॥ ५४॥

English
O great sages, the phallic image named Ghuśmeśa originated thus. Its very sight and worship renders happiness always. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्याता ज्योतिर्लिङ्गावली मया ।
द्वादशप्रमिता सर्वकामदा भुक्तिमुक्तिदा ॥ ५५॥

English
Thus I have narrated to you the twelve Jyotirlingas which yield all desires, worldly pleasures and salvation. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
एतज्ज्योतिर्लिङ्गकथां यः पठेच्छृणुयादपि ।
मुच्यते सर्वपापेभ्यो भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च विन्दति ॥ ५६॥

English
He who reads or listens to this narrative of Jyotirlingas gets rid of sins and attains worldly pleasures and salvation. (56.)
