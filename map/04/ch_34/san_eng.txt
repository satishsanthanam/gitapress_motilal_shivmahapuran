### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - (Acquisition of Sudarśana by Viṣṇu)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य सूतस्य च मुनीश्वराः ।
समूचुस्तं सुप्रशस्य लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O great sages, on hearing the words of Sūta the sages praised him and spoke desiring the welfare of the worlds. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सर्वं विजानासि ततः पृच्छामहे वयम् ।
हरीश्वरस्य लिङ्गस्य महिमानं वद प्रभो ॥ २॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, you know everything. Hence we ask you. O holy lord, please narrate the greatness of the phallic image Hariśvara. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रं सुदर्शनं प्राप्तं विष्णुनेति श्रुतं पुरा ।
तदाराधनतस्तात तत्कथां च विशेषतः ॥ ३॥

English
O dear, this was heard by us formerly that by propitiating Hariśvara, Viṣṇu acquired his discus Sudarśana. Please narrate that story particularly. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रूयतां च ऋषिश्रेष्ठा हरीश्वरकथा शुभा ।
यतः सुदर्शनं लब्धं विष्णुना शङ्करात्पुरा ॥ ४॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, may the auspicious story of Hariśvara be heard, how Sudarśana was acquired by Viṣṇu formerly from Śiva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्मिंश्चित्समये दैत्याः सञ्जाता बलवत्तराः ।
लोकांस्ते पीडयामासुर्धर्मलोपं च चक्रिरे ॥ ५॥

English
On a certain occasion the Daityas became very powerful. They harassed the worlds and violated sacred rites. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ते देवाः पीडिता दैत्यैर्महाबलपराक्रमैः ।
स्वं दुःखं कथयामासुर्विष्णुं निर्जररक्षकम् ॥ ६॥

English
Tormented by the Daityas of great strength and exploit, the gods narrated their misery to Viṣṇu, their saviour. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
कृपां कुरु प्रभो त्वं च दैत्यैः सम्पीडिता भृशम् ।
कुत्र यामश्च किं कुर्मः शरण्यं त्वां समाश्रिताः ॥ ७॥

English
The gods said:—  O lord, be merciful. We are very much afflicted by the Daityas. Where shall we go? What shall we do? We have resorted to you, for you are worthy of seeking refuge by us. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्येवं वचनं श्रुत्वा देवानां दुःखितात्मनाम् ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं विष्णुर्वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words of the gods distressed in mind, Viṣṇu remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and said. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
करिष्यामि च वः कार्यमाराध्य गिरिशं सुराः ।
बलिष्ठाः शत्रवो ह्येते विजेतव्याः प्रयत्नतः ॥ ९॥

English
Viṣṇu said:—  O gods, I shall carry out your task after propitiating Śiva. These powerful enemies shall be conquered with great effort. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तास्ते सुराः सर्वे विष्णुना प्रभविष्णुना ।
मत्वा दैत्यान्हतान्दुष्टान्ययुर्धाम स्वकं स्वकम् ॥ १०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Thus consoled by lord Viṣṇu, the gods thought that the Daityas would be surely killed. They then went to their respective abodes. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरप्यमराणां तु जयार्थमभजच्छिवम् ।
सर्वामराणामधिपं सर्वसाक्षिणमव्ययम् ॥ ११॥

English
For the victory of the gods, Viṣṇu worshipped Śiva, the lord of all the gods, the imperishable and the cosmic witness of all. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
गत्वा कैलासनिकटे तपस्तेपे हरिः स्वयम् ।
कृत्वा कुण्डं च संस्थाप्य जातवेदसमग्रतः ॥ १२॥

English
He went to Kailāsa and dug a pit there wherein he kindled fire and performed penance. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पार्थिवेन विधानेन मन्त्रैर्नानाविधैरपि ।
स्तोत्रैश्चैवाप्यनेकैश्च गिरिशं चाभजन्मुदा ॥ १३॥

English
He worshipped Śiva joyously with various mantras and hymns prescribed for the worship of earthen image. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कमलैः सरसो जातैर्मानसाख्यान्मुनीश्वराः ।
बद्ध्वा चैवासनं तत्र न चचाल हरिः स्वयम् ॥ १४॥

English
O great sages, he worshipped with the lotus-flowers growing in the Mānasa lake. He sat firmly in the Yogic posture and did not move at all. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादावधि चैवात्र स्थेयं वै सर्वथा मया ।
इत्येवं निश्चयं कृत्वा समानर्च शिवं हरिः ॥ १५॥

English
Thinking decisively—“By all means I shall stay here till the lord is pleased” Viṣṇu worshipped Śiva. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा नैव हरस्तुष्टो बभूव हरये द्विजाः ।
तदा स भगवान्विष्णुर्विचारे तत्परोऽभवत् ॥ १६॥

English
O brahmins, when Śiva was not pleased with Viṣṇu, the latter began to think anxiously. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विचार्यैवं स्वमनसि सेवनं बहुधा कृतम् ।
तथापि न हरस्तुष्टो बभूवोतिकरः प्रभुः ॥ १७॥

English
After thinking thus he rendered him various services. Still, lord Śiva, indulging in divine sports was not satisfied. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सुविस्मितो विष्णुर्भक्त्या परमयान्वितः ।
सहस्रैर्नामभिः प्रीत्या तुष्टाव परमेश्वरम् ॥ १८॥

English
Surprised much at this, Viṣṇu, endowed with great devotion, eulogised lord Śiva by reciting his thousand names with pleasure. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्येकं कमलं तस्मै नाममन्त्रमुदीर्य च ।
पूजयामास वै शम्भुं शरणागतवत्सलम् ॥ १९॥

English
Repeating every name, he worshipped Siva, favourably disposed towards those who seek refuge in him; with a lotus flower. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
परीक्षार्थं विष्णुभक्तेस्तदा वै शङ्करेण ह ।
कमलानां सहस्रात्तु हृतमेकं च नीरजम् ॥ २०॥

English
In order to test the piety of Visnu, one of the thousand lotus-flowers was taken away by Siva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
न ज्ञातं विष्णुना तच्च मायाकारणमद्भुतम् ।
न्यूनं तच्चापि संज्ञाय तदन्वेषणतत्परः ॥ २१॥

English
That wonderful Maya of Siva was not noticed by Visnu. But when he came to know of the shortage he started to search. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
बभ्राम सकलां पृथ्वीं तत्प्रीत्यै सुदृढव्रतः ।
तदप्राप्य विशुद्धात्मा नेत्रमेकमुदाहरत् ॥ २२॥

English
In order to find it out he roamed over the world, steadily maintaining his sacred rite. But not finding the same, the pious Visnu offered one of his eyes (instead of the lotus). (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा स प्रसन्नोऽभूच्छङ्करः सर्वदुःखहा ।
आविर्बभूव तत्रैव जगाद वचनं हरिम् ॥ २३॥

English
On seeing it Siva who destroys all miseries was delighted and appeared before him. He spoke these words to Visnu. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
प्रसन्नोऽस्मि हरे तुभ्यं वरं ब्रूहि यथेप्सितम् ।
मनोऽभिलषितं दद्मि नादेयं विद्यते तव ॥ २४॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Viṣṇu, I am delighted with you. Mention the boon you wish to have. I shall grant you what you desire. There is nothing which cannot be given to you. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा शम्भुवचनं केशवः प्रीतमानसः ।
महाहर्षसमापन्नो ह्यब्रवीत्साञ्जलिः शिवम् ॥ २५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the words of Śiva, Viṣṇu was pleased in his mind. Extremely delighted he spoke to Śiva with palms joined in reverence. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
वाच्यं किं मे त्वदग्रे वै ह्यन्तर्यामी त्वमास्थितः ।
तथापि कथ्यते नाथ तव शासनगौरवात् ॥ २६॥

English
Viṣṇu said:—  What is there to be mentioned to you? You are the immanent soul. Still, O Lord, I mention the same with due deference to your behest. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यैश्च पीडितं विश्वं सुखं नो नः सदाशिव ।
दैत्यान् हन्तुं मम स्वामिन्स्वायुधं न प्रवर्तते ॥ २७॥

English
O Sadasiva, the entire universe is afflicted by the Daityas. We have no pleasure at all. Our weapons are ineffectual in killing the Daityas. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि नान्यो मे रक्षकः परः ।
अतोऽहं परमेशान शरणं त्वां समागतः ॥ २८॥

English
What shall I do? Where shall I go? I have no greater saviour than you. O lord, I seek refuge in you. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा च नमस्कृत्य शिवाय परमात्मने ।
स्थितश्चैवाग्रतः शम्भोः स्वयं च पुरुपीडितः ॥ २९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this he stood in affliction and bowed to Śiva, the supreme soul. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचो विष्णोर्देवदेवो महेश्वरः ।
ददौ तस्मै स्वकं चक्रं तेजोराशिं सुदर्शनम् ॥ ३०॥

English
On hearing these words of Viṣṇu, Śiva, the lord of gods, gave him his discus Sudarśana shining with the mass of splendour. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्राप्य भगवान्विष्णुर्दैत्यांस्तान्बलवत्तरान् ।
जघान तेन चक्रेण द्रुतं सर्वान्विना श्रमम् ॥ ३१॥

English
On receiving it Viṣṇu slew the powerful demons immediately with the discus without any strain. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्स्वास्थ्यं परं लेभे बभूवुः सुखिनः सुराः ।
सुप्रीतः स्वायुधं प्राप्य हरिरासीन्महासुखी ॥ ३२॥

English
Thereupon the universe derived normalcy, the gods became happy. After receiving the Sudarśana missile Viṣṇu was greatly delighted. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ॥

किं तन्नामसहस्रं वै कथय त्वं हि शाङ्करम् ।
येन तुष्टो ददौ चक्रं हरये स महेश्वरः ॥ ३३॥

English
*The sages said:—*  What is that hymn of Śiva that consists of his thousand names? What is that hymn whereby Śiva was so much delighted that he gave Viṣṇu the discus. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्माहात्म्यं मम ब्रूहि शिवसंवादपूर्वकम् ।
कृपालुत्वं च शम्भोर्हि विष्णूपरि यथातथम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Please expound the glory of this hymn to us and narrate the dialogue between Śiva and Viṣṇu. The merciful nature of Śiva towards Viṣṇu may also be narrated to us. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
इति तेषां वचः श्रुत्वा मुनीनां भावितात्मनाम् ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं सूतो वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ३५॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  On hearing the words of the pious sages, Sūta spoke after remembering the lotus-like feet of Śiva. (35.)
