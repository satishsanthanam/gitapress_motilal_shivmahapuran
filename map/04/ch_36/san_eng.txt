### **CHAPTER THIRTYSIX - (Efficacy of the thousand names of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रुत्वा विष्णुकृतं दिव्यं परनामविभूषितम् ।
सहस्रनाम स्वस्तोत्रं प्रसन्नोऽभून्महेश्वरः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing the eulogy made by Visnu consisting of the hymn of his thousand divine names lord Siva became delighted. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
परीक्षार्थं हरेरीशः कमलेषु महेश्वरः ।
गोपयामास कमलं तदैकं भुवनेश्वरः ॥ २॥

English
In order to test Visnu, Siva, the lord of the worlds concealed one of the lotus-flowers. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पङ्कजेषु तदा तेषु सहस्रेषु बभूव च ।
न्यूनमेकं तदा विष्णुर्विह्वलः शिवपूजने ॥ ३॥

English
There was a shortage of one lotus-flower from among the thousand lotuses. Hence Visnu was distressed in the course of his worship of Siva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हृदा विचारितं तेन कुतो वै कमलं गतम् ।
यातं यातु सुखेनैव मन्नेत्रं कमलं न किम् ॥ ४॥

English
He thought within himself "Where could the lotusflower have gone? If it has gone, let it go for good. Is not my own eye a lotus? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वेति नेत्रमुद्धृत्य सर्वसत्त्वावलम्बनात् ।
पूजयामास भावेन स्तवयामास तेन च ॥ ५॥

English
After thinking thus, he plucked out his eye and repeated the last name Sarvasattvāvalambana. Full of emotion he worshipped and eulogised Siva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तुतमथो दृष्ट्वा तथाभूतं हरो हरिम् ।
मा मेति व्याहरन्नेव प्रादुरासीज्जगद्गुरुः ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing Visnu in that attitude, Visnu the preceptor of the universe appeared before him and forbade him do that. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादवतताराशु मण्डलात्पार्थिवस्य च ।
प्रतिष्ठितस्य हरिणा स्वलिङ्गस्य महेश्वरः ॥ ७॥

English
Lord Siva descended from the pedestal of that earthen phallic image installed by Visnu. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यथोक्तरूपिणं शम्भुं तेजोराशिसमुत्थितम् ।
नमस्कृत्य पुरः स्थित्वा स तुष्टाव विशेषतः ॥ ८॥

English
Bowing to Siva, rising up in the form of a mass of splendour as mentioned before, Visnu stood in front of him and eulogised him particularly. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा प्राह महादेवः प्रसन्नः प्रहसन्निव ।
सम्प्रेक्ष्य कृपया विष्णुं कृताञ्जलिपुटं स्थितम् ॥ ९॥

English
Looking mercifully at Visnu, who stood before him with palms joined in reverence, the delighted lord Siva laughingly said then. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
ज्ञातं मयेदं सकलं तव चित्तेप्सितं हरे ।
देवकार्यं विशेषेण देवकार्यरतात्मनः ॥ १०॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Viṣṇu, all the desires in your mind, particularly the task of the gods have been understood by me. Indeed you are engaged in the task of the gods. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
देवकार्यस्य सिद्ध्यर्थं दैत्यनाशाय चाश्रमम् ।
सुदर्शनाख्यं चक्रं च ददामि तव शोभनम् ॥ ११॥

English
For the achievement of the task of the gods and for the destruction of the Daityas without strain, I shall give the auspicious discus Sudarśana to you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्रूपं भवता दृष्टं सर्वलोकसुखावहम् ।
हिताय तव देवेश धृतं भावय तद्ध्रुवम् ॥ १२॥

English
O lord of the gods, for your benefit ponder over this form, pleasing to all the worlds, which has seen by you (now). (12.)

---

Sanskrit
रणाजिरे स्मृतं तद्वै देवानां दुःखनाशनम् ।
इदं चक्रमिदं रूपमिदं नामसहस्रकम् ॥ १३॥

English
If this discus, this form and this hymn of thousand names is remembered in the battlefield it will quell the misery of the gods. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ये श‍ृण्वन्ति सदा भक्त्या सिद्धिः स्यादनपायिनी ।
कामानां सकलानां च प्रसादान्मम सुव्रत ॥ १४॥

English
O one of good rites, thanks to my blessings, all those who listen to this will achieve their desires without serious dangers. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा ददौ चक्रं सूर्यायुतसमप्रभम् ।
सुदर्शनं स्वपादोत्थं सर्वशत्रुविनाशनम् ॥ १५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After saying this, the lord gave the discus of the brilliance of ten thousand suns, born of his own feet and destructive of all enemies, to Viṣṇu. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुश्चापि सुसंस्कृत्य जग्राहोदङ्मुखस्तदा ।
नमस्कृत्य महादेवं विष्णुर्वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ १६॥

English
Viṣṇu purified himself and accepted it facing the north. After making obeisance to lord Śiva, Viṣṇu spoke these words to him. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
श‍ृणु देव मया ध्येयं पठनीयं च किं प्रभो ।
दुःखानां नाशनार्थं हि वद त्वं लोकशङ्कर ॥ १७॥

English
Viṣṇu said:—  O lord, O benefactor of the worlds, listen. Please tell me what I shall meditate upon and what I shall recite for the destruction of the distress. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति पृष्टस्तदा तेन सन्तुष्टस्तु शिवोऽब्रवीत् ।
प्रसन्नमानसो भूत्वा विष्णुं देवसहायकम् ॥ १८॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On being thus asked by him, Śiva becoming satisfied and delighted in his mind spoke to Viṣṇu who helped the gods. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
रूपं ध्येयं हरे मे हि सर्वानर्थप्रशान्तये ।
अनेकदुःखनाशार्थं पठ नामसहस्रकम् ॥ १९॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Viṣṇu, it is my form that is to be meditated upon for subduing all mishaps. Recite my thousand names for destroying the distress that troubles you. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
धार्यं चक्रं सदा मे हि सर्वाभीष्टस्य सिद्धये ।
त्वया विष्णो प्रयत्नेन सर्वचक्रवरं त्विदम् ॥ २०॥

English
For the attainment of your desires you shall hold this discus always. O Viṣṇu, this is the best of all discuses. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्ये च ये पठिष्यन्ति पाठयिष्यन्ति नित्यशः ।
तेषां दुःखं न स्वप्नेऽपि जायते नात्र संशयः ॥ २१॥

English
There is no doubt that those who recite and teach this hymn will have no misery at all even in dreams. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
राज्ञां च सङ्कटे प्राप्ते शतावृत्तिं चरेद्यदा ।
साङ्गं च विधिसंयुक्तं कल्याणं लभते नरः ॥ २२॥

English
When kings are in distress this worship with thousand names shall be repeated hundred times in accordance with the rules laid down and after due performance of the ancillary services people will have welfare. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
रोगनाशकरं ह्येतद्विद्यावित्तदमुत्तमम् ।
सर्वकामप्रदं पुण्यं शिवभक्तिप्रदं सदा ॥ २३॥

English
This is destructive of distress. It yields learning and wealth. It is excellent and meritorious and bestows all desires and devotion to Śiva always. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यदुद्दिश्य फलं श्रेष्ठं पठिष्यन्ति नरास्त्विह ।
लप्स्यन्ते नात्र सन्देहः फलं तत्सत्यमुत्तमम् ॥ २४॥

English
People undoubtedly derive excellent benefits which they will have in view in reciting this. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्च प्रातः समुत्थाय पूजां कृत्वा मदीयिकाम् ।
पठते मत्समक्षं वै नित्यं सिद्धिर्न दूरतः ॥ २५॥

English
Achievement is not far off if a person rising in the morning, performs my worship and recites this in my presence everyday. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ऐहिकीं सिद्धिमाप्नोति निखिलां सर्वकामिकाम् ।
अन्ते सायुज्यमुक्तिं वै प्राप्नोत्यत्र न संशयः ॥ २६॥

English
There is no doubt in this that he will attain all the worldly pleasures and desires and in the end Sāyujya¹⁸⁶ type of salvation. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा तदा विष्णुं शङ्करः प्रीतमानसः ।
उपस्पृश्य कराभ्यां तमुवाच गिरिशः पुनः ॥ २७॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Delighted in his mind, Śiva spoke thus to Viṣṇu and touched him with his hands. Śiva then told him again. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
वरदोऽस्मि सुरश्रेष्ठ वरान्वृणु यथेप्सितान् ।
भक्त्या वशीकृतो नूनं स्तवेनानेन सुव्रत ॥ २८॥

English
Śiva said:—  O most excellent of gods, I am the granter of the boons. Choose the boons you desire to have. O one of good rites, I am attracted by your devotion and by this hymn as well. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तो देवदेवेन देवदेवं प्रणम्य तम् ।
सुप्रसन्नतरो विष्णुः साञ्जलिर्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ॥ २९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Urged thus the delighted Viṣṇu bowed to the lord of gods and spoke with palms joined in reverence. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
यथेदानीं कृपा नाथ क्रियते चान्यतः परा ।
कार्या चैव विशेषेण कृपालुत्वात्त्वया प्रभो ॥ ३०॥

English
Viṣṇu said:—  O lord, you shall be equally merciful to others as well just as you are merciful to me now. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयि भक्तिर्महादेव प्रसीद वरमुत्तमम् ।
नान्यमिच्छामि भक्तानामार्त्तयो नैव यत्प्रभो ॥ ३१॥

English
O great lord, devotion to you is the excellent boon I wish to have. Be pleased. I do not wish for anything else, because, O lord, devotees are never distressed. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य दयावान्सुतरां भवः ।
पस्पर्श च तदङ्गं वै प्राह शीतांशुशेखरः ॥ ३२॥

English
*Sūta said:—* On hearing these words, the extremely sympathetic lord touched him. Then the moon-crested deity spoke. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
मयि भक्तिः सदा ते तु हरे स्यादनपायिनी ।
सदा वन्द्यश्च पूज्यश्च लोके भव सुरैरपि ॥ ३३॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Vişnu, your devotion to me will always remain firm. Be worthy of the reverence and worship of all gods in the world. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वम्भरेति ते नाम सर्वपापहरं परम् ।
भविष्यति न सन्देहो मत्प्रसादात्सुरोत्तम ॥ ३४॥

English
O most excellent god, there is no doubt in this that, thanks to my favour, your name—Viśvambhara (bearer of the universe) will be destructive of all sins. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वान्तर्दधे रुद्रः सर्वदेवेश्वरः प्रभुः ।
पश्यतस्तस्य विष्णोस्तु तत्रैव च मुनीश्वराः ॥ ३५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, after saying this, lord Siva, the lord of the gods, vanished there itself even as Vişnu was watching. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
जनार्दनोऽपि भगवान् वचनाच्छङ्करस्य च ।
प्राप्य चक्रं शुभं तद्वै जहर्षाति स्वचेतसि ॥ ३६॥

English
After securing the auspicious discus and hearing the words of Siva, lord Vişnu was extremely delighted in his mind. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा ध्यानं च तच्छम्भोः स्तोत्रमेतन्निरन्तरम् ।
पपाठाध्यापयामास भक्तेभ्यस्तदुपादिशत् ॥ ३७॥

English
Meditating on Siva, he repeated the hymn incessantly and instructed the devotees too in this. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति पृष्टं मयाख्यातं श‍ृण्वतां पापहारकम् ।
अतः परं च किं श्रेष्ठाः प्रष्टुमिच्छथ वै पुनः ॥ ३८॥

English
O excellent ones, thus I have narrated the story in accordance with your enquiry. It destroys the sins of those who listen to it. What then do you wish to ask? (38.)
