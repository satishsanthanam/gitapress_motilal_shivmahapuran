### **CHAPTER THIRTYSEVEN - (Devotion to lord Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत सूत महाभाग ज्ञानवानसि सुव्रत ।
पुनरेव शिवस्यैव चरितं ब्रूहि विस्तरात् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, O fortunate Sūta, you are endowed with knowledge, O one of good rites. Please narrate the story of Śiva again in detail. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरातनाश्च राजान ऋषयो देवतास्तथा ।
आराधनं च तस्यैव चक्रुर्देववरस्य हि ॥ २॥

English
The ancient kings, sages and gods propitiated that excellent deity Siva alone. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टमृषिश्रेष्ठाः श्रूयतां कथयामि वः ।
चरित्रं शाङ्करं रम्यं श‍ृण्वतां भुक्तिमुक्तिदम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, you have put the question properly. I shall narrate the charming story of Śiva. It is conducive to the attainment of worldly pleasures and salvation by those who listen to it. May it be heard. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदेव पुरा पृष्टो नारदेन पितामहः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मा नारदं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ ४॥

English
Brahmā was asked by Nārada this alone. Delighted in his mind, he replied to Nārada, the excellent sage. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
श‍ृणु नारद सुप्रीत्या शाङ्करं चरितं वरम् ।
प्रवक्ष्यामि भवत्स्नेहान्महापातकनाशनम् ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O Nārada, listen. I shall narrate the excellent story of Śiva with pleasure out of affection to you. It destroys great sins (5.)

---

Sanskrit
रमया सहितो विष्णुः शिवपूजां चकार ह ।
कृपया परमेशस्य सर्वान्कामानवाप हि ॥ ६॥

English
Vişnu, accompanied by Lakşmi, performed the worship of Śiva. Thanks to the mercy of lord Śiva, he attained all desires. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अहं पितामहश्चापि शिवपूजनकारकः ।
तस्यैव कृपया तात विश्वसृष्टिकरः सदा ॥ ७॥

English
I, the grandfather of the worlds, am a worshipper of Śiva. Thanks to his mercy that I create the worlds always. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवपूजाकरा नित्यं मत्पुत्राः परमर्षयः ।
अन्ये च ऋषयो ये ते शिवपूजनकारकाः ॥ ८॥

English
My sons, the great sages, always worship Śiva. Many other sages also do so. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद त्वं विशेषेण शिवपूजनकारकः ।
सप्तर्षयो वसिष्ठाद्याः शिवपूजनकारकाः ॥ ९॥

English
O Nārada, particularly you are a worshipper of Śiva. The seven sages,\* Vasiṣṭha and others too are the worshippers of Śiva. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अरुन्धती महासाध्वी लोपामुद्रा तथैव च ।
अहल्या गौतमस्त्री च शिवपूजनकारिकाः ॥ १०॥

English
The great chaste lady Arundhati, Lopamudra, and Ahalya the wife of Gautama, do also worship Siva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्वासाः कौशिकः शक्तिर्दधीचो गौतमस्तथा ।
कणादो भार्गवो जीवो वैशम्पायन एव च ॥ ११॥

एते च मुनयः सर्वे शिवपूजाकरा मताः ।
तथा पराशरो व्यासः शिवपूजारतः सदा ॥ १२॥

English
These sages, viz.-Durvasas, Kausika, Sakti, Dadhici, Gautama, Kanada, Jiva, Bhargava, Vaisampayana, Parasara and Vyasa are the worshippers of Siva. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युर्महाभक्तः शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
याज्ञवल्क्यो महाशैवो जैमिनिर्गर्ग एव च ॥ १३॥

English
Upamanyu is a great devotee of Siva, the great soul. Yajnavalkya, Jaimini and Garga are great followers of Siva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शुकश्च शौनकाद्याश्च शङ्करस्य प्रपूजकाः ।
अन्येऽपि बहवः सन्ति मुनयो मुनिसत्तमाः ॥ १४॥

English
Sukra, Saunaka and others are the worshippers of Siva. There are many other sages, excellent sages, who worship Siva. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अदितिर्देवमाता च नित्यं प्रीत्या चकार ह ।
पार्थिवीं शैवपूजां वै सवधूः प्रेमतत्परा ॥ १५॥

English
Aditi, the mother of the gods, performed the worship of Siva in earthen phallic idols every day. She is a lady engaged in Siva's devotion. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्रादयो लोकपाला वसवश्च सुरास्तथा ।
महाराजिकदेवाश्च साध्याश्च शिवपूजकाः ॥ १६॥

English
Indra and other guardians of the quarters,¹⁸⁷ Vasus,¹⁸⁸ Mahārājika gods, Sādhyas are the worshippers of Śiva. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
गन्धर्वाः किन्नराद्याश्चोपसुराः शिवपूजकाः ।
तथासुरा महात्मानः शिवपूजाकरा मताः ॥ १७॥

English
The subordinate gods, Gandharvas, Kinnaras and others are worshippers of Śiva. Similarly the Asuras of noble minds are the worshippers of Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यकशिपुर्दैत्यः सानुजः ससुतो मुने ।
शिवपूजाकरो नित्यं विरोचनबली तथा ॥ १८॥

English
O sage, the Daitya Hiraṇyakaśipu, his sons and younger brother, as well as Virocana and Bali worship Śiva every day. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
महाशैवः स्मृतो बाणो हिरण्याक्षसुतास्तथा ।
वृषपर्वा दनुस्तात दानवाः शिवपूजकाः ॥ १९॥

English
Bāṇa is a great follower of Śiva. So also the sons of Hiraṇyākṣa. O dear one, Vṛṣaparvan and other Dāna- vas are the worshippers of Śiva.

20 Śeṣa, Vāsuki, Takṣaka and many other great serpents are devotees of Śiva. Garuḍa and other birds too are so. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यचन्द्रावुभौ देवौ पृथ्व्यां वंशप्रवर्तकौ ।
शिवसेवारतौ नित्यं सवश्यौ तौ मुनीश्वर ॥ २१॥

English
O great sage, the two gods, the Sun and the Moon who started two lines of kings on the earth are devoted to the service of Śiva always along with their descendants. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
मनवश्च तथा चक्रुः स्वायम्भुवपुरः सराः ।
शिवपूजां विशेषेण शिववेषधरा मुने ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, the self-born Brahmā and other Manus performed the worship of Śiva and they were in the guise of Śiva too. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रियव्रतश्च तत्पुत्रास्तथा चोत्तानपात्सुतः ।
तद्वंशाश्चैव राजानः शिवपूजनकारकाः ॥ २३॥

English
Priyavrata, his son Uttānapāda, and his son and successors, were kings worshipping Śiva. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्रुवश्च ऋषभश्चैव भरतो नवयोगिनः ।
तद्भ्रातरः परे चापि शिवपूजनकारकाः ॥ २४॥

English
Dhruva, Ṛṣabha, Bharata, his brothers the nine Yogins and others too were worshippers of Śiva. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
वैवस्वतसुतास्तार्क्ष्य इक्ष्वाकुप्रमुखा नृपाः ।
शिवपूजारतात्मानः सर्वदा सुखभोगिनः ॥ २५॥

English
Vaivasvata's sons, Tārkṣya, Ikṣvāku and other kings, were always devoted to the worship of Śiva and enjoyed happiness. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ककुत्स्थश्चापि मान्धाता सगरः शैवसत्तमः ।
मुचुकुन्दो हरिश्चन्द्रः कल्माषाङ्घ्रिस्तथैव च ॥ २६॥

English
Kakutstha, Māndhātā, Sagara, Mucukunda, Hariścandra and Kalmāṣāṅghri were all excellent devotees of Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
भगीरथादयो भूपा बहवो नृपसत्तमाः ।
शिवपूजाकरा ज्ञेयाः शिववेषविधायिनः ॥ २७॥

English
Bhagiratha and other kings, many excellent kings are known as worshippers of Śiva assuming the guise of Śiva. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
खट्वाङ्गश्च महाराजो देवसाहाय्यकारकः ।
विधितः पार्थिवीं मूर्तिं शिवस्यापूजयत्सदा ॥ २८॥

English
The great king Khaṭvāṅga, who helped the gods, worshipped the earthen idol of Śiva always. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्पुत्रो हि दिलीपश्च शिवपूजनकृत् सदा ।
रघुस्तत्तनयः शैवः सुप्रीत्या शिवपूजकः ॥ २९॥

English
His son Dilīpa was a perpetual worshipper of Śiva. Raghu, his son, was a great devotee of Śiva. He worshipped Śiva with pleasure. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अजः शिवार्चकस्तस्य तनयो धर्मयुद्धकृत् ।
जातो दशरथो भूयो महाराजो विशेषतः ॥ ३०॥

English
Aja, his son, who waged a virtuous war was a worshipper of Śiva. Daśaratha who was born of him became a great king. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रार्थे पार्थिवीं मूर्त्तिं शैवीं दशरथो हि सः ।
समानर्च विशेषेण वसिष्ठस्याज्ञया मुनेः ॥ ३१॥

English
At the behest of the sage Vasiṣṭha, Daśaratha particularly worshipped the earthen idol of Śiva for obtaining sons. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पुत्रेष्टिं च चकारासौ पार्थिवो भवभक्तिमान् ।
ऋष्यश‍ृङ्गमुनेराज्ञां सम्प्राप्य नृपसत्तमः ॥ ३२॥

English
As directed by Ṛṣyaśṛṅga this excellent king, devoted to Śiva performed the sacrifice called Putreṣṭi. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कौसल्या तत्प्रिया मूर्त्तिं पार्थिवीं शाङ्करीं मुदा ।
ऋष्यश‍ृङ्गसमादिष्टा समानर्च सुताप्तये ॥ ३३॥

English
Urged by Ṛṣyaśṛṅga, Kausalyā, the beloved queen of Daśaratha joyously performed the worship of the earthen idol of Śiva for the acquisition of a son. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सुमित्रा च शिवं प्रीत्या कैकेयी नृपवल्लभा ।
पूजयामास सत्पुत्रप्राप्तये मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३४॥

English
O excellent sage, Sumitrā, and Kaikeyi the beloved queens, performed the worship of Śiva with pleasure for the acquisition of good sons. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवप्रसादतस्ता वै पुत्रान्प्रापुः शुभङ्करान् ।
महाप्रतापिनो वीरान् सन्मार्गनिरतान्मुने ॥ ३५॥

English
O sage, they obtained auspicious sons of great valour and prowess who followed the path of the good. It was due to the favour of Śiva. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवाज्ञया तस्मात्तासु राज्ञः स्वयं हरिः ।
चतुर्भिश्चैव रूपैश्चाविर्बभूव नृपात्मजः ॥ ३६॥

English
Then at the behest of Śiva, Viṣṇu himself was born of the ladies in four different forms. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
कौसल्यायाः सुतो रामः सुमित्रायाश्च लक्ष्मणः ।
शत्रुघ्नश्चैव कैकेय्या भरतश्चेति सुव्रताः ॥ ३७॥

English
Kausalyā's son was Rāma, Sumitrā's Lakṣmaṇa and Śatrughna and Kaikeyī's Bharata. All of them performed Śiva's rites. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
रामः स सहजो नित्यं पार्थिवं समपूजयत् ।
भस्मरुद्राक्षधारी च विरजागममास्थितः ॥ ३८॥

English
Rāma performed the worship of the earthen idol every day in the company of his brothers. He wore Bhasma and Rudrākṣa and followed the Śiva-cult.¹⁸⁴ (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वंशे ये समुत्पन्ना राजानः सानुगा मुने ।
ते सर्वे पार्थिवं लिङ्गं शिवस्य समपूजयन् ॥ ३९॥

English
O sage, the kings born of his family worshipped the earthen idol of Śiva along with their followers. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सुद्युम्नश्च महाराजः शैवो मनुसुतो मुने ।
शिवशापात्प्रियाहेतोरभून्नारी ससवेकः ॥ ४०॥

English
O sage, the great king Sudyumna, the son of a sage, became a woman along with his attendants as a result of Śiva's curse for the sake of his beloved. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Thanks to the worship of the earthen image of Śiva, he became an excellent man again. By remaining a man for a month and a woman for a month, his womanhood disappeared. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then he forsook the kingdom and engaged himself in the Siva-cult assuming the guise of Siva. By virtue of devotion he attained salvation inaccessible to others. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English
His son Pururavas, a great king, was a worshipper of lord Siva. His son Bharata performed the great worship of Siva always. Nahuṣa, a great devotee was interested in the worship of Siva. (43-44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Yayati realised his desires due to Siva's worship. He begot five sons all interested in Siva cult. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Yadu and others, the five sons of Yayati were the worshippers of Siva. Thanks to the power of the worship of Siva, they realised all their desires. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
O dear, his son Pradyumna was a worshipper of Siva always. Samba and other excellent descendants of Krsna were also great worshippers of Siva. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
In the presence of Siva a sage (who was Siva himself) was saved by him. He was himself devoured by tigers and other beasts at night. (53.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit
इति ते कथितं तात यत्पृष्टं भवतानघ ।
शाङ्करं चरितं दिव्यं किमन्यत्प्रष्टुमिच्छसि ॥ ५५॥

English
O dear, thus I have narrated the divine story of Siva as asked by you. What else do you wish to hear? (55.)
