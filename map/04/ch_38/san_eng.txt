### **CHAPTER THIRTYEIGHT - (The greatness of Śivarātri)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
धन्योऽसि कृतकृत्योऽसि जीवितं सफलं तव ।
यच्छ्रावयसि नस्तात महेश्वरकथां शुभाम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O dear, you are blessed and contented. Your life is fruitful since you narrate to us the auspicious narrative of lord Siva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुभिश्चर्षिभिः सूत श्रुतं यद्यपि वस्तु सत् ।
सन्देहो न गतोऽस्माकं तदेतत्कथयामि ते ॥ २॥

English
O Sūta, although this matter has been heard from many sages, our doubt is not set at rest. Hence we ask you. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
केन व्रतेन सन्तुष्टः शिवो यच्छति सत्सुखम् ।
कुशलः शिवकृत्ये त्वं तस्मात्पृच्छामहे वयम् ॥ ३॥

English
By what sacred rite is Siva satisfied. By what sacred rite does he impart happiness to the good. You are expert in the knowledge of Siva's legends and so we ask you. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भुक्तिर्मुक्तिश्च लभ्येत भक्तैर्येन व्रतेन वै ।
तद्वद त्वं विशेषेण व्यासशिष्य नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ४॥

English
O disciple of Vyasa, obeisance be to you. Please explain clearly the rite by which the devotee can secure both worldly pleasures and salvation. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सम्यक्पृष्टमृषिश्रेष्ठा भवद्भिः करुणात्मभिः ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं कथयामि यथाश्रुतम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, the question is well put by you. There is sympathy brimming in your hearts. After remembering the lotus-like feet of Siva I shall mention it in the manner I have heard. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा भवद्भिः पृच्छ्येत तथा पृष्टं हि वेधसा ।
हरिणा शिवया चैव तथा वै शङ्करं प्रति ॥ ६॥

English
The same question had been put by Brahma, Visnu and Siva before Siva in the manner in which you are asking me now. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्मिंश्चित्समये तैस्तु पृष्टं च परमात्मने ।
केन व्रतेन सन्तुष्टो भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च यच्छसि ॥ ७॥

English
On a certain occasion they asked Siva, the great soul. "What rite satisfies you to bestow worldly pleasures and salvation?" (7.)

---

Sanskrit
इति पृष्टस्तदा तैस्तु हरिणा तेन वै तदा ।
तदहं कथयाम्यद्य श‍ृण्वतां पापहारकम् ॥ ८॥

English
When he was asked thus, Siva replied thus. I shall mention it now. It quells the sins of those who listen to it. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
। शिव उवाच ॥

भूरि व्रतानि मे सन्ति भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदानि च ।
मुख्यानि तत्र ज्ञेयानि दशसङ्ख्यानि तानि वै ॥ ९॥

English
Śiva said:—  There are many rites yielding worldly pleasures and salvation. The most important among them are ten in number. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दश शैवव्रतान्याहुर्जाबालश्रुतिपारगाः ।
तानि व्रतानि यत्नेन कार्याण्येव द्विजैः सदा ॥ १०॥

English
Those who have mastered the text "Jābālaśruti" have mentioned ten sacred rites of Śiva. These rites shall be performed by the brahmins always strenuously with great devotion. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्यष्टम्यां प्रयत्नेन कर्तव्यं नक्तभोजनम् ।
कालाष्टम्यां विशेषेण हरे त्याज्यंऽह्निभोजनम् ॥ ११॥

English
On Aṣṭami days (the eighth day in the lunar fortnight) meals shall be taken only at night. O Viṣṇu, on Kālaṣṭami day food shall be eschewed altogether. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादश्यां सितायां तु त्याज्यं विष्णोऽह्निभोजनम् ।
असितायां तु भोक्तव्यं नक्तमभ्यर्च्य मां हरे ॥ १२॥

English
O Viṣṇu, on the eleventh day in the bright lunar fortnight, food during the day is forbidden. O Viṣṇu, on the eleventh day in the dark half food shall be taken at night after my worship. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रयोदश्यां सितायां तु कर्तव्यं निशि भोजनम् ।
असितायां तु भूतायां तन्न कार्यं शिवव्रतैः ॥ १३॥

English
On the thirteenth day in the bright half, food shall be taken at night. In the dark half, it is forbidden to those who follow Śiva's rites. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
निशि यत्नेन कर्तव्यं भोजनं सोमवासरे ।
उभयोः पक्षयोर्विष्णो सर्वस्मिञ्छिवतत्परैः ॥ १४॥

English
O Viṣṇu, in both the halves on every Monday, food shall be taken only during the night by the followers of Śiva-cult. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
व्रतेष्वेतेषु सर्वेषु शैवा भोज्याः प्रयत्नतः ।
यथाशक्ति द्विजश्रेष्ठा व्रतसम्पूर्तिहेतवे ॥ १५॥

English
In all these rites, excellent brahmin devotees of Śiva shall be fed according to one's ability for the completion of the sacred rites. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
व्रतान्येतानि नियमात्कर्तव्यानि द्विजन्मभिः ।
व्रतान्येतानि तु त्यक्त्वा जायन्ते तस्करा द्विजाः ॥ १६॥

English
The sacred rites shall always be performed regularly by the brahmins. By abandoning these Vratas, brahmins become robbers. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्तिमार्गप्रवीणैश्च कर्तव्यं नियमादिति ।
मुक्तेस्तु प्रापकं चैव चतुष्टयमुदाहृतम् ॥ १७॥

शिवार्चनं रुद्रजप उपवासः शिवालये ।
वाराणस्यां च मरणं मुक्तिरेषा सनातनी ॥ १८॥

English
The rites shall be performed regularly by the persons who are adepts in the path of salvation. The four vratas that bring about salvation are the worship of Śiva, the repetition of Rudra Mantra by way of Japa, observance of fast in Śiva's temple and death in Vārāṇasi. That salvation is eternal. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टमी सोमवारे च कृष्णपक्षे चतुर्दशी ।
शिवतुष्टिकरं चैतन्नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १९॥

English
Aṣṭami that falls on Monday, and Caturdaṭi in the dark half are sure to bring about the propitiation of Śiva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्ष्वपि बलिष्ठं हि शिवरात्रिव्रतं हरे ।
तस्मात्तदेव कर्तव्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिफलेप्सुभिः ॥ २०॥

English
O Viṣṇu, among the four the most powerful is the rite of Śivarātri.¹⁹¹ Hence that alone shall be performed by those who desire the fruits of worldly pleasures and salvation. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्माच्च व्रतादन्यन्नास्ति नृणां हितावहम् ।
एतद् व्रतं तु सर्वेषां धर्मसाधनमुत्तमम् ॥ २१॥

English
There is no other rite more beneficial to men. This rite is the most excellent means of virtue for all. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्कामानां सकामानां सर्वेषां च नृणां तथा ।
वर्णानामाश्रमाणां च स्त्रीबालानां तथा हरे ॥ २२॥

English
To those without desire, to those with specific desires, to all men of all castes and stages of life, even to women and to children, this rite is very beneficial. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
दासानां दासिकानां च देवादीनां तथैव च ।
शरीरिणां च सर्वेषां हितमेतद् व्रतं वरम् ॥ २३॥

English
To men and women slaves, to gods and others and to all embodied souls this excellent rite is most beneficial. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
माघस्य ह्यसिते पक्षे विशिष्टा साति कीर्तिता ।
निशीथव्यापिनी ग्राह्या हत्याकोटिविनाशिनी ॥ २४॥

English
In the dark half of the month of Māgha, Śivarātri is specifically glorified. The rite of Śivarātri is performed when the Caturdaśī extends up-to mid-night. The observance of the rite on that Tithi quells the sin of even a crore of human slaughters. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दिने चैव यत्कार्यं प्रातरारभ्य केशव ।
श्रूयतां तन्मनो दत्त्वा सुप्रीत्या कथयामि ते ॥ २५॥

English
O Viṣṇu, what shall be done on that day from morning onwards you hear with attention. I shall explain it to you with pleasure. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रातरुत्थाय मेधावी परमानन्दसंयुतः ।
समाचरेन्नित्यकृत्यं स्नानादिकमतन्द्रितः ॥ २६॥

English
Rising in the morning, the intelligent man with great delight, shall perform his daily routine of bath with care. (26.)

---

Sanskrit

English
He shall then go to the temple of Śiva for worship. After making obeisance to Śiva, he shall observe the Ritualistic affirmation of his performance according to the prescribed course. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
"O lord of gods, "O blue-necked, obeisance be to you. O lord, I wish to perform the Śivarātri rite sacred to you. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

रात्रौ तत्र स्वयं गत्वा सम्पाद्य विधिमुत्तमम् ॥

शिवस्य दक्षिणे भागे पश्चिमे वा स्थले शुभे ॥ ३१॥

निधाय चैव तद द्रव्यं पूजार्थं शिवसन्निधौ ।

English

The phallic image shall be that which is prescribed in the Āgamas. The materials of worship shall be placed on a good spot near Śiva either to the South or to the West. Then the devotee shall take bath again duly. (30-32.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The undercloth and the usual cloth shall be pure. The devotee shall perform the Ācamana thrice and begin worship. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The worship shall be performed in due concordance with the material and the mantras. Śiva's worship shall always be performed in accompaniment with the mantras. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The intelligent devotee shall repeat the mantras after performing worship in the first 3-hour period with music, songs, dances and devotional services. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If he knows the sacred verses, he shall make earthen images of excellent type. After performing the daily routine he shall worship the earthen image. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
After making the earthen image, he shall install it thereafter. The devotee shall propitiate the bull-bannered lord, with several hymns. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The glory of the Vrata shall be proclaimed by the intelligent devotee. It shall be heard by the devotee desiring the completion of the Vrata. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

He shall keep awake with pleasure and jubilation. In the morning he shall take bath again. After installing the idol, he shall perform the worship. (39-40.)

---

Sanskrit

English
After concluding the rites and bowing to Siva again and again he shall pray to him with palms joined in devotion and shoulders drooping down. (41.)

---

---

Sanskrit

---

---

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English

---

---

English
