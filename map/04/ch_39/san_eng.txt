### **CHAPTER THIRTYNINE - (The conclusion of the rite of Śivarātri)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय उचुः ।
उद्यापनविधिं ब्रूहि शिवरात्रिव्रतस्य च ।
यत्कृत्वा शङ्करः साक्षात्प्रसन्नो भवति ध्रुवम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  Please narrate the Udyāpana (formal concluding) of the rite of Śivarātri as a result of which Śiva himself becomes delighted. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रूयतामृषयो भक्त्या तदुद्यापनमादरात् ।
यस्यानुष्ठानतः पूर्णं व्रतं भवति तद् ध्रुवम् ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O sages, may the concluding rite be heard with devotion and respect. By performing it, the Vrata certainly becomes complete. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्दशाब्दं कर्तव्यं शिवरात्रिव्रतं शुभम् ।
एकभक्तं त्रयोदश्यां चतुर्दश्यामुपोषणम् ॥ ३॥

English
The auspicious Sivarātrivrata shall be performed for fourteen years. On the Trayodasi day only one meal shall be taken and a complete fast shall be observed on the Caturdasi day. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवरात्रिदिने प्राप्ते नित्यं सम्पाद्य वै विधिम् ।
शिवालयं ततो गत्वा पूजां कृत्वा यथाविधि ॥ ४॥

English
On the Sivaratri day after performing the routine, the devotee shall go to the temple of Siva and perform worship. He shall make the mystical diagram famous in the three worlds by the name of Gauritilaka.[193] (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च कारयेद्दिव्यं मण्डलं तत्र यत्नतः ।
गौरीतिलकनाम्ना वै प्रसिद्धं भुवनत्रये।५॥

तन्मध्ये लेखयेद्दिव्यं लिङ्गतोभद्रमण्डलम् ।
अथवा सर्वतोभद्रं मण्डपान्तः प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ६॥

English
In the middle of the mystical diagram Lingatobhadra and Sarvatobhadra shall be delineated. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भास्तत्र प्रकर्तव्याः प्राजापत्यविसंज्ञया ।
सवस्त्राः सफलास्तत्र दक्षिणासहिताः शुभाः ॥ ७॥

English
As in the Prajāpatya rite, auspicious water-pots shall be placed wrapped in cloth, with a cocoanut above and the Dakṣiṇā in. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्डलस्य च पार्श्वे वै स्थापनीयाः प्रयत्नतः ।
मध्ये चैकश्च संस्थाप्यः सौवर्णो वापरो घटः ॥ ८॥

English
The pots shall be carefully placed at the sides of the altar. In middle either a discus or a gold-pot shall be placed. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रोमासहितां शम्भुमूर्तिं निर्माय हाटकीम् ।
पलेन वा तदर्धेन यथाशक्त्याथवा व्रती ॥ ९॥

निधाय वामभागे तु शिवामूर्तिमतन्द्रितः ।
मदीयां दक्षिणे भागे कृत्वा रात्रौ प्रपूजयेत् ॥ १०॥

English
The devotee shall make an idol of Siva accompanied by Uma, of gold a pala in weight. According to his capacity the weight may be reduced to half of a pala. Siva shall be on the left side and Siva on the right. The devotee shall worship the idol at night. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
आचार्यं वरयेत्तत्र चर्त्विग्भिः सहितं शुचिम् ।
अनुज्ञातश्च तैर्भक्त्या शिवपूजां समाचरेत् ॥ ११॥

English
He shall woo a pious preceptor accompanied by Rtviks. With their formal permission he shall begin the worship of Siva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
रात्रौ जागरणं कुर्यात्पूजां यामोद्भवां चरन् ।
रात्रिमाक्रमयेत्सर्वां गीतनृत्यादिना व्रती ॥ १२॥

English
He shall keep awake during the night performing worship in the different 3-hour periods and singing songs of prayer or devoutly dancing. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सम्पूज्य विधिवत्सन्तोष्य प्रातरेव च ।
पुनः पूजां ततः कृत्वा होमं कुर्याद्यथाविधि ॥ १३॥

English
After performing the worship thus in accordance with the injunctions he shall propitiate the lord. He shall worship again in the morning and perform Homa duly. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाशक्ति विधानं च प्राजापत्यं समाचरेत् ।
ब्राह्मणान्भोजयेत्प्रीत्या दद्याद्दानानि भक्तितः ॥ १४॥

English
He shall perform the Prajāpatya rites according to his ability. With pleasure he shall feed brahmins and with devotion he shall make charitable gifts. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋत्विजश्च सपत्नीकान्वस्त्रालङ्कारभूषणैः ।
अलङ्कृत्य विधानेन दद्याद्दानं पृथक्पृथक् ॥ १५॥

English
He shall give the Rtviks and their wives good clothes and ornaments for bedecking themselves and make gifts to them separately. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
गां सवत्सां विधानेन यथोपस्करसंयुताम् ।
उक्त्वाचार्याय वै दद्याच्छिवो मे प्रीयतामिति ॥ १६॥

English
Saying "May Siva be pleased", he shall make the gift of a cow and a calf together with the essential requisites to the preceptor. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सकुम्भां तन्मूर्तिं सवस्त्रां वृषभे स्थिताम् ।
सर्वालङ्कारसहितामाचार्याय निवेदयेत् ॥ १७॥

English
He shall offer to him the waterpot and the Siva idol with all ornaments, placing them on a bullock. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सम्प्रार्थयेद्देवं महेशानं महाप्रभुम् ।
कृताञ्जलिर्नतस्कन्धः सुप्रीत्या गद्गदाक्षरः ॥ १८॥

English
He shall pray to lord Siva the great lord with great pleasure, with palms joined in reverence, with shoulders drooping and words choked with emotion. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
व्रतेनानेन देवेश कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ १९॥

English
"O great lord, lord of the gods, favourably disposed to the devotees, be merciful to me, thanks to this Vrata. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
मया भक्त्यनुसारेण व्रतमेतत्कृतं शिव ।
न्यूनं सम्पूर्णतां यातु प्रसादात्तव शङ्कर ॥ २०॥

English
O Siva, this Vrata is performed by me according to my ability. O lord, if it is deficient in any respect let it become perfect by your favour. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अज्ञानाद्यदि वा ज्ञानाज्जपपूजादिकं मया ।
कृतं तदस्तु कृपया सफलं तव शङ्कर ॥ २१॥

English
Japa and worship and other things have been performed partly in ignorance and partly with knowledge by me. May these be fruitful, O Siva, by your mercy." (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पुष्पाञ्जलिं दत्त्वा शिवाय परमात्मने ।
नमस्कारं ततः कुर्यात्प्रार्थनां पुनरेव च ॥ २२॥

English
After saying this the flower-offering shall be made to Siva the great soul. He shall make obeisance and pray again. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं व्रतं कृतं येन न्यूनं तस्य न विद्यते ।
मनोभीष्टां ततः सिद्धिं लभते नात्र संशयः ॥ २३॥

English
If the Vrata is performed thus it will be complete and no deficiency will remain. The devotee will attain Siddhi desired by him. There is no doubt in it. (23.)

---
