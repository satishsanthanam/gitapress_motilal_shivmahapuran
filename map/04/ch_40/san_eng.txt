### **CHAPTER FORTY - (The glory of Sivarātri)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
सूत ते वचनं श्रुत्वा परानन्दं वयं गताः ।
विस्तरात्कथय प्रीत्या तदेव व्रतमुत्तमम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, we are extremely delighted on hearing your words. Please narrate the excellent Vrata in detail. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं पुरा च केनेह सूतैतद् व्रतमुत्तमम् ।
कृत्वाप्यज्ञानतश्चैव प्राप्तं किं फलमुत्तमम् ॥ २॥

English
O Suta, by whom was this excellent Vrata performed formerly? Was an excellent benefit derived by any by performing it without full knowledge? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रूयतामृषयः सर्वे कथयामि पुरातनम् ।
इतिहासं निषादस्य सर्वपापप्रणाशनम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* May this be heard, O sages. I shall narrate the ancient story of the hunter$^{194}$ which destroys all sins. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरा कश्चिद्वने भिल्लो नाम्ना ह्यासीद् गुरुद्रुहः ।
कुटुम्बी बलवान्क्रूरः क्रूरकर्मपरायणः ॥ ४॥

English
Formerly there was a hunter in a forest, Gurudruha by name. He had a large family. He was strong, ruthless and engaged in cruel activities. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
निरन्तरं वने गत्वा मृगान् हन्ति स्म नित्यशः ।
चौर्यं च विविधं तत्र करोति स्म वने वसन् ॥ ५॥

English
Everyday he used to go to the forest incessantly and kill deer. There in the forest he committed thefts also in various ways. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
बाल्यादारभ्य तेनेह कृतं किञ्चिच्छुभं न हि ।
महान्कालो व्यतीयाय वने तस्य दुरात्मनः ॥ ६॥

English
No auspicious action was performed by him from childhood onwards. Thus the wicked fellow passed a long time in the forest. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिच्छिवरात्रिश्च प्राप्तासीत्तत्र शोभना ।
न दुरात्मा स्म जानाति महद्वननिवासकृत् ॥ ७॥

English
Once it was a Śivarātri day. But the wicked hunter, staying in the great forest did not know that. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन् समये भिल्लो मात्रा पित्रा स्त्रिया तथा ।
प्रार्थितश्च क्षुधाविष्टैर्भक्ष्यं देहि वनेचर ॥ ८॥

English
On this occasion he was requested by his parents and wife who were oppressed by starvation, "O forest-roamer give us food." (8.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सम्प्रार्थितः सोऽपि धनुरादाय सत्वरम् ।
जगाम मृगहिंसार्थं बभ्राम सकलं वनम् ॥ ९॥

English
Thus requested he took up his bow and started immediately for hunting deer. He roamed here and there in the forest. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दैवयोगात्तदा तेन न प्राप्तं किञ्चिदेव हि ।
अस्तं प्राप्तस्तदा सूर्यः स वै दुःखमुपागतः ॥ १०॥

English
Due to adverse fate no game was secured by him. The sun too had set and he was greatly distressed. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
किं कर्तव्यं क्व गन्तव्यं न प्राप्तं मेऽद्य किञ्चन ।
बालाश्च ये गृहे तेषां किं पित्रोश्च भविष्यति ॥ ११॥

मदीयं वै कलत्रं च तस्याः किञ्चिद्भविष्यति ।
किञ्चिद् गृहीत्वा हि मया गन्तव्यं नान्यथा भवेत् ॥ १२॥

English
"What shall I do? Where shall I go? Nothing has been obtained by me. What will happen to my parents and my children at home? Then there is my wife too. What will happen to her? I must go home only with something in my hand. It is impossible to face them empty-handed." (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं विचार्य स व्याधो जलाशयसमीपगः ।
जलावतरणं यत्र तत्र गत्वा स्वयं स्थितः ॥ १३॥

English
Thinking thus, the hunter went near a pond. He stood near the path leading to water. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अवश्यमत्र कश्चिद्वै जीवश्चैवागमिष्यति ।
तं हत्वा स्वगृहं प्रीत्या यास्यामि कृतकार्यकः ॥ १४॥

English
"Some animal is sure to come here. I shall kill it and go home with pleasure. My purpose will be served fully. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इति मत्वा स वै वृक्षमेकं बिल्वेति संज्ञकम् ।
समारुह्य स्थितस्तत्र जलमादाय भिल्लकः ॥ १५॥

English
Thinking thus the hunter climbed up a Bilva tree, taking some water with him. He sat on its branch. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कदायास्यति कश्चिद्वै कदा हन्यामहं पुनः ।
इति बुद्धिं समास्थाय स्थितोऽसौ क्षुत्तृषान्वितः ॥ १६॥

English
With hunger and thirst oppressing him he waited and thought. "When will some animal come here? When shall I kill it?" (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्रात्रौ प्रथमे यामे मृगी त्वेका समागता ।
तृषार्ता चकिता सा च प्रोत्फालं कुर्वती तदा ॥ १७॥

English
During the first part of the night a thirsty hind came there springing and jumping timidly. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा च तदा तेन तद्वधार्थमथो शरः ।
संहृष्टेन द्रुतं बाणं धनुषि स्वे हि सन्दधे ॥ १८॥

English
On seeing it he was much delighted. He fixed an arrow to his bow immediately in order to kill it. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवं कुर्वतस्तस्य जलं बिल्वदलानि च ।
पतितानि ह्यधस्तत्र शिवलिङ्गमभूत्ततः ॥ १९॥

यामस्य प्रथमस्यैव पूजा जाता शिवस्य च ।
तन्महिम्ना हि तस्यैव पातकं गलितं तदा ॥ २०॥

English
When he did this, some water (that he had with him) got spilt and a few leaves of the Bilva tree fell down. There was a phallic idol of Śiva beneath the tree. Hence this became his worship for the first part of the night. As a result of this his sin was dissolved. (19-20.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रत्यं चैव तच्छब्दं श्रुत्वा सा हरिणी भिया ।
व्याधं दृष्ट्वा व्याकुला हि वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing the noise, the hind became frightened. It was much distressed on seeing the hunter. It spoke thus. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
मृग्युवाच ।
किं कर्तुमिच्छसि व्याध सत्यं वद ममाग्रतः ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा हरिणीवाक्यं व्याधो वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ २२॥

English
The hind said :—  "O hunter, what is it that you propose to do?  Please speak truth before me." On hearing the words of the hind, the hunter said. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याध उवाच ।
कुटुम्बं क्षुधितं मेऽद्य हत्वा त्वां तर्पयाम्यहम् ।
दारुणं तद्वचः श्रुत्वा दृष्ट्वा तं दुर्धरं खलम् ॥ २३॥

किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि ह्युपायं रचयाम्यहम् ।
इत्थं विचार्यं सा तत्र वचनं चेदमब्रवीत् ॥ २४॥

English
The hunter said :—  "My family is starving today. I shall satisfy their hunger by killing you." On hearing these terrible words and seeing that ruthless knave, the hind thought "What shall I do ? Where shall I go ? Well, I shall use a trick, " thinking thus the hind spoke. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
मृग्युवाच ।
मन्मांसेन सुखं ते स्याद्देहस्यानर्थकारिणः ।
अधिकं किं महत्पुण्यं धन्याहं नात्र संशयः ॥ २५॥

English
The Hind said :—  There is no doubt that I am blessed. You will be happy with my flesh. What greater merit can I have through this body which is in every respect harmful? (25.)

---

Sanskrit
उपकारकरस्यैव यत्पुण्यं जायते त्विह ।
तत्पुण्यं शक्यते नैव वक्तुं वर्षशतैरपि ॥ २६॥

English
It is impossible to expatiate on the merit of a person who helps others even if we take a hundred years. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
परं तु शिशवो मेऽद्य वर्तन्ते स्वाश्रमेऽखिलाः ।
भगिन्यै तान्समर्प्यैव प्रायास्ये स्वामिनेऽथवा ॥ २७॥

English
But all my infant fawns are in the hermitage. I shall intrust them to the care of my sister or husband and return. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
न मे मिथ्यावचस्त्वं हि विजानीहि वनेचर ।
आयास्येऽहं पुनश्चेह समीपं ते न संशयः ॥ २८॥

English
O forest-roamer, do not take my words to be lies. Undoubtedly I will come again to you. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थिता सत्येन धरणी सत्येनैव च वारिधिः ।
सत्येन जलधाराश्च सत्ये सर्वं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ २९॥

English
The earth stays steady through truth. The ocean is steady through truth. The water flows steadily through truth. Everything is founded on truth. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्तोऽपि तया व्याधो न मेने तद्वचो यदा ।
तदा सुविस्मिता भीता वचनं साब्रवीत्पुनः ॥ ३०॥

English
*Sūta said:—* When the hunter did not accept its proposal even after being implored thus, the bewildered and frightened hind spoke again:— (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मृग्युवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्याध प्रवक्ष्यामि शपथं हि करोम्यहम् ।
आगच्छेयं यथा ते न समीपं स्वगृहाद्गता ॥ ३१॥

ब्राह्मणो वेदविक्रेता सन्ध्याहीनस्त्रिकालकम् ।
स्त्रियः स्वस्वामिनो ह्याज्ञां समुल्लङ्घ्य क्रियान्विताः ॥ ३२॥

कृतघ्ने चैव यत्पापं यत्पापं विमुखे हरे ।
द्रोहिणश्चैव यत्पापं यत्पापं धर्मलङ्घने ॥ ३३॥

विश्वासघातके यच्च तथा वै छलकर्तरि ।
तेन पापेन लिम्पामि यद्यहं नागमे पुनः ॥ ३४॥

English
The Hind said:—  O hunter, listen. I shall explain. I take this vow. After going home if I do not return to you let me be smeared with the sin that these people have viz—A brahmin who sells the Vedas, one who does not offer Sandhyā prayers, women who transgress the commands of their husbands and do various rites, an undgrateful person, a person who is averse to Śiva, a person who illtreats others, who violates Virtuē, a person committing breach of trust, and a person who deceives others. (31-34.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याद्यनेकशपथान्मृगी कृत्वा स्थिता यदा ।
तदा व्याधः स विश्वस्य गच्छेति गृहमब्रवीत् ॥ ३५॥

English
When the hind made these promises the hunter believed it and said "Go home". (35.)

---

Sanskrit

तावच्च प्रथमो यामस्तस्य निद्रां विना गतः ॥ ३६॥

English
The delighted hind drank water and went to its hermitage. By that time the first part of the night came to a close and the hunter did not have a wink of sleep. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

तस्या मार्गं विचिन्वन्ती ह्याजगाम जलार्थिनी ॥ ३७॥

English
The sister of the hind, not able to see it and so distressed came there in search of that hind. It too was thirsty. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing the hind, the Bhilla drew the bow and was about to shoot the arrow. As before, water and Bilva leaves fell on Śiva. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

पूजा जाता प्रसङ्गेन व्याधस्य सुखदायिनी ॥ ३९॥

English
Incidentally it became the worship of the second part of the night. It was pleasing to the hunter. (39.)

---

Sanskrit

पूर्ववत् कथितं तेन तच्छ्रुत्वाह मृगी पुनः ॥ ४०॥

English
The hind asked "O forest-roamer what are you doing?" After seeing him, the hunter replied as before. On hearing it, the hind said again. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
मृग्युवाच ।

अनित्येन शरीरेण ह्युपकारो भविष्यति ॥ ४१॥

English

---

Sanskrit
व्याध उवाच ।

तच्छ्रुत्वा हरिणी प्राह शपथं कुर्वती हरेः ॥ ४३॥

English
The hunter said:—  "I do not agree to what you say. Undoubtedly I am going to kill you." On hearing that the hind said on the oath of Śiva. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
मृग्युवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्याध प्रवक्ष्यामि नागच्छेयं पुनर्यदि ।
वाचा विचलितो यस्तु सुकृतं तेन हारितम् ॥ ४४॥

परिणीतां स्त्रियं हित्वा गच्छत्यन्यां च यः पुमान् ।
वेदधर्मं समुल्लङ्घ्य कल्पितेन च यो व्रजेत् ॥ ४५॥

विष्णुभक्तिसमायुक्तः शिवनिन्दां करोति यः ।
पित्रोः क्षयाहमासाद्य शून्यं चैवाक्रमेदिह ॥ ४६॥

कृत्वा च परितापं हि करोति वचनं पुनः ।
तेन पापेन लिम्पामि नागच्छेयं पुनर्यदि ॥ ४७॥

English

If I do not return let me be smeared with the sin that accrues to such people who forsake their legally wedded wife and cohabit with another, who transgress the Vedic virtue and follow an imaginary and fictitious cult; who profess to be devoted to Viṣṇu and censure Śiva, who perform the Kṣayāha rites of their parents on a Sūnyatithi day and who add insult to injury." (44-47.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit
