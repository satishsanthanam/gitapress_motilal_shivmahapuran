### **CHAPTER FORTYONE - (Review of salvation)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
मुक्तिर्नाम त्वया प्रोक्ता तस्यां किं नु भवेदिह ।
अवस्था कीदृशी तत्र भवेदिति वदस्व नः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said.—  What is liberation has been explained by you. What will happen in that state? What is the nature of that state? Please explain to us. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
मुक्तिश्चतुर्विधा प्रोक्ता श्रूयतां कथयामि वः ।
संसारक्लेशसंहर्त्री परमानन्ददायिनी ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Liberation is of four types. May this be heard. I shall tell you. It quells the pain of worldly existence. It bestows the greatest bliss. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सारूप्या चैव सालोक्या सान्निध्या च तथा परा ।
सायुज्या च चतुर्थी सा व्रतेनानेन या भवेत् ॥ ३॥

English
It is fourfold:—Sārūpya (identity in form), Sālokya (being within the view of the lord or in the same world), Sānnidhya (proximity with the lord), and Sāyujya (complete identity with him). The fourth arises out of this Vrata. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्तेर्दाता मुनिश्रेष्ठाः केवलं शिव उच्यते ।
ब्रह्माद्या न हि ते ज्ञेयाः केवलं च त्रिवर्गदाः ॥ ४॥

English
O great sages, only Śiva can bestow liberation. Brahmā and others cannot do that. They shall be known as bestowers of virtue, wealth and love. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माद्यास्त्रिगुणाधीशाः शिवस्त्रिगुणतः परः ।
निर्विकारी परब्रह्म तुर्यः प्रकृतितः परः ॥ ५॥

English
Brahmā and others are dependent on the attributes. Śiva is beyond the attributes. He is free from aberrations. He is the great Brahman, the fourth deity, greater than Prakṛti. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानरूपोऽव्ययः साक्षी ज्ञानगम्योऽद्वयः स्वयम् ।
कैवल्यमुक्तिदः सोऽत्र त्रिवर्गस्य प्रदोऽपि हि ॥ ६॥

English
He is in the form of knowledge. He is imperishable and unchangeable. He is the cosmic witness, attainable through knowledge. He is without a second. He is the bestower of Trivarga (Dharma, Artha, Kāma) and the Kaivalya¹⁹⁸ liberation. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कैवल्याख्या पञ्चमी च दुर्लभा सर्वथा नृणाम् ।
तल्लक्षणं प्रवक्ष्यामि श्रूयतामृषिसत्तमाः ॥ ७॥

English
A fifth type of salvation Kaivalya is inaccessible to men in every respect. O excellent sages, I shall explain its characteristics. May that be heard. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पद्यते यतः सर्वं येनैतत्पाल्यते जगत् ।
यस्मिंश्च लीयते तद्धि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥ ८॥

तदेव शिवरूपं हि पठ्यते च मुनीश्वराः ।
सकलं निष्कलं चेति द्विविधं वेदवर्णितम् ॥ ९॥

English
O great sages, Śiva's form is twofold: Saguṇa and Nirguṇa as explained in the Vedas. It is that from which the entire universe originates, by which it is protected and in which it merges and by whom all this has been pervaded. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुना तच्च न ज्ञातं ब्रह्मणा न च तत्तथा ।
कुमाराद्यैश्च न ज्ञातं न ज्ञातं नारदेन वै ॥ १०॥

English
It is not known to Viṣṇu nor to Bhaḥmā. It is not known to Kumāra and others nor to Nārada. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शुकेन व्यासपुत्रेण व्यासेन च मुनीश्वरैः ।
तत्पूर्वैश्चाखिलैर्देवैर्वेदैः शास्त्रैस्तथा न हि ॥ ११॥

English
It is not known to Śuka, son of Vyāsa nor to Vyāsa or other great sages. It is not known to the earlier beings, all the gods, all the Vedas and all the Śāstras. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यं ज्ञानमनन्तं च सच्चिदानन्दसंज्ञितम् ।
निर्गुणो निरुपाधिश्चाव्ययः शुद्धो निरञ्जनः ॥ १२॥

English
It is truth, knowledge, the infinity. It is named Saccidānanda (existence, knowledge, bliss). It is Nirguṇa, Nirvyādhi (free from limitations and conditions). It is imperishable. It is pure and unsullied. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
न रक्तो नैव पीतश्च न श्वेतो नील एव च ।
न ह्रस्वो न च दीर्घश्च न स्थूलः सूक्ष्म एव च ॥ १३॥

English
It is neither red nor yellow, neither white nor blue, neither short nor long, neither gross nor subtle. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यतो वाचो निवर्तन्ते अप्राप्य मनसा सह ।
तदेव परमं प्रोक्तं ब्रह्मैव शिवसंज्ञकम् ॥ १४॥

English
That great Brahman Śiva is said to be that from which all words return along with the mind, unable to attain it. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशं व्यापकं यद्वत्तथैव व्यापकं त्विदम् ।
मायातीतं परात्मानं द्वन्द्वातीतं विमत्सरम् ॥ १५॥

English
This is all pervasive like the expansive firmament. It is the great soul beyond Māyā. It is beyond Dvandvas-mutually clashing opposites and free from rivalry. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्राप्तिश्च भवेदत्र शिवज्ञानोदयाद् ध्रुवम् ।
भजनाद्वा शिवस्यैव सूक्ष्ममत्या सतां द्विजाः ॥ १६॥

English
O brahmins, good people attain it when knowledge of Śiva rises up or when they worship him with deep devotion. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानं तु दुष्करं लोके भजनं सुकरं मतम् ।
तस्माच्छिवं च भजत मुक्त्यर्थमपि सत्तमाः ॥ १७॥

English
The knowledge is difficult to attain but worship can easily be performed. Hence, O excellent sages, worship Śiva for the attainment of salvation too. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो हि भजनाधीनो ज्ञानात्मा मोक्षदः परः ।
भक्त्यैव बहवः सिद्धा मुक्तिं प्रापुः परां मुदा ॥ १८॥

English
Śiva is subservient to worship. He is knowledge-souled and the greatest bestower of salvation. Many Siddhas have joyously attained the great salvation through devotion alone. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानमाता शम्भुभक्तिर्मुक्तिभुक्तिप्रदा सदा ।
सुलभा यत्प्रसादाद्धि सत्प्रेमाङ्कुरलक्षणा ॥ १९॥

English
Devotion to Śiva, characterised by sprouting pure love, is the mother of perfect knowledge. It is easy of access by means of his benevolence. It yields worldly pleasures and salvation too. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सा भक्तिर्विविधा ज्ञेया सगुणा निर्गुणा द्विजाः ।
वैधी स्वाभाविकी या या वरा सा सा स्मृता परा ॥ २०॥

नैष्ठिक्यनैष्ठिकीभेदाद् द्विविधैव हि कीर्तिता ।
षड्विधा नैष्ठिकी ज्ञेया द्वितीयैकविधा स्मृता ॥ २१॥

English
Devotion is of various types. O brahmins, it is both Saguṇa and Nirguṇa. Whatever is legitimate and natural is the most excellent and the greatest. It is again two-fold: Naiṣṭhika (permanent and attended with ritualistic observances) and Anaiṣṭhika (non-Naiṣṭhika). The Naiṣṭhika is of six varieties and the other is only of one type. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
विहिताविहिताभेदात्तामनेकां विदुर्बुधाः ।
तयोर्बहुविधत्वाच्च विस्तारो न हि वर्ण्यते ॥ २२॥

English
Learned men consider devotion to be of different sorts, such as ordained and non-ordained. Since they are of various types they are not explained in detail. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ते नवाङ्गे उभे ज्ञेये श्रवणादिकभेदतः ।
सुदुष्करे तत्प्रसादं विना च सुकरे ततः ॥ २३॥

English
Differentiated by the differences in those who observe Sravana (listening) etc. both these are of nine subdivisions each. Without his favour they are difficult of performance and with His favour they can easily be performed. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तिज्ञाने न भिन्ने हि शम्भुना वर्णिते द्विजाः ।
तस्माद्भेदो न कर्तव्यः तत्कर्तुः सर्वदा सुखम् ॥ २४॥

English
O brahmins, devotion and knowledge$^{199}$ are essentially non-different from each other as explained by Siva. Hence, they should not be differentiated. Happiness befalls him who observes them always. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञानं न भवत्येव द्विजा भक्तिविरोधिनः ।
शम्भुभक्तिकरस्यैव भवेज्ज्ञानोदयो द्रुतम् ॥ २५॥

English
O brahmins, a person who is against devotion will never attain perfect knowledge. Knowledge rises up immediately only in him who observes devotion to Siva. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्भक्तिर्महेशस्य साधनीया मुनीश्वराः ।
तयैव निखिलं सिद्धं भविष्यति न संशयः ॥ २६॥

English
Hence, O great sages, devotion to lord Siva shall be practised. There is no doubt in this that everything is realisable solely through devotion. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
इति पृष्टं भवद्भिर्यत्तदेव कथितं मया ।
तच्छुत्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ २७॥

English
Thus, what has been asked by you has been explained. There is no doubt in this that on hearing this, one is rid of his sin completely. (27.)
