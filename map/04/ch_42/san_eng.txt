### **CHAPTER FORTYTWO - (The difference between Saguṇa and Nirguṇa)**

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
शिवः को वा हरिः को वा रुद्रः को वा विधिश्च कः ।
एतेषु निर्गुणः को वा ह्येतं नश्छिन्धि संशयम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  Who is Śiva? Who is Viṣṇu? Who is Rudra? Who is Brahmā? Of these who is Nirguṇa? Please clear this doubt of ours. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
यच्चादौ हि समुत्पन्नं निर्गुणात्परमात्मनः ।
तदेव शिवसंज्ञं हि वेदवेदान्तिनो विदुः ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—* Masters of the Vedas and Vedānta know that which originates from the Nirguṇa great soul as the one termed Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्प्रकृतिरुत्पन्ना पुरुषेण समन्विता ।
ताभ्यां तपः कृतं तत्र मूलस्थे च जले सुधीः ॥ ३॥

पञ्चक्रोशीति विख्याता काशी सर्वातिवल्लभा ।
व्याप्तं च सकलं ह्येतत्तज्जलं विश्वतो गतम् ॥ ४॥

English
Prakṛti accompanied by Puruṣa originated from him. Penance was performed by them both. The famous city Kāśi known as Pañcakrośi, dear to all, originated in the water there at the base. There was water everywhere in the universe. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्भाव्य मायया युक्तस्तत्र सुप्तो हरिः स वै ।
नारायणेति विख्यातो माया नारायणी मता ॥ ५॥

English
Viṣṇu famous as Nārāyaṇa slept there enveloped by Māyā. The Prakṛti is known as Nārāyaṇī (belonging to Nārāyaṇa.) (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्नाभिकमले यो वै जातः स च पितामहः ।
तेनैव तपसा दृष्टः स वै विष्णुरुदाहृतः ॥ ६॥

English
He who was born of his umbilical lotus was Pitāmaha. He who was seen by him through penance is said to be Viṣṇu. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
उभयोर्वादशमने यद्रूपं स दर्शितं बुधाः ।
महादेवेति विख्यातं निर्गुणेन शिवेन हि ॥ ७॥

English
The form that was revealed by Nirguṇa Śiva at the time of subduing the dispute between the two is, O learned ones, famous as Mahādeva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन प्रोक्तमहं शम्भुर्भविष्यामि कभालतः ।
रुद्रो नाम स विख्यातो लोकानुग्रहकारकः ॥ ८॥

English
This was said by him, "I will be Śiva with fire in my forehead." He became famous by the name Rudra. He is the cause of blessing the worlds. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यानार्थं चैव सर्वेषामरूपो रूपवानभूत् ।
स एव च शिवः साक्षाद्भक्तवात्सल्यकारकः ॥ ९॥

English
The formless one became one with forms in order to facilitate meditation. He alone is Śiva himself, favourably disposed towards his devotees. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवे त्रिगुणसम्भिन्ने रुद्रे तु गुणधामनि ।
वस्तुतो न हि भेदोऽस्ति स्वर्णे तद्भूषणे यथा ॥ १०॥

English
Essentially there is no difference between Śiva who is different from the three Guṇas and Rudra the abode of Guṇas, as in the case of gold and an ornament made thereof. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
समानरूपकर्माणौ समभक्तगतिप्रदौ ।
समानाखिलसंसेव्यौ नानालीलाविहारिणौ ॥ ११॥

English
Their forms and activities are the same; they bestow good goal on the devotees equally; they are equally worthy of being served by all and they indulge in various divine sports. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वथा शिवरूपो हि रुद्रो रौद्रपराक्रमः ।
उत्पन्नो भक्तकार्यार्थं हरिब्रह्मसहायकृत् ॥ १२॥

English
Rudra of terrible exploits has the form of Śiva in every respect. He was born for carrying out the tasks of the devotees. He helps Viṣṇu and Brahmā. (12.)

---

English
Other beings and gods who are born get dissolved in due order but not so Rudra. Rudra gets merged in Śiva. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ते वै रुद्रं मिलित्वा तु प्रयान्ति प्रकृता इमे ।
इमान् रुद्रो मिलित्वा तु न याति श्रुतिशासनम् ॥ १४॥

English
This is the directive in the Vedas that all these Prakṛtas (born of Prakṛti) meet Rudra and go but Rudra does not meet these and go. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे रुद्रं भजन्त्येव रुद्रः कञ्चिद्भजेन्न हि ।
स्वात्मना भक्तवात्सल्याद्भजत्येव कदाचन ॥ १५॥

English
All worship Rudra but Rudra does not worship any one. Yet due to his favourable attitude to his devotees sometimes he worships himself. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यं भजन्ति ये नित्यं तस्मिंस्ते लीनतां गताः ।
तेनैव रुद्रं ते प्राप्ताः कालेन महता बुधाः ॥ १६॥

English
Those who worship others get dissolved in them. That is why they attain Rudra in due course, O learned ones. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रभक्तास्तु ये केचित्तत्क्षणं शिवतां गताः ।
अन्यापेक्षा न वै तेषां श्रुतिरेषा सनातनी ॥ १७॥

English
Those who are Rudra's devotees attain the state of Śiva in a trice. They need not depend on any one else. This is the eternal statement in the Vedas. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अज्ञानं विविधं ह्येतद्विज्ञानं विविधं न हि ।
तत्प्रकारमहं वक्ष्ये श‍ृणुतादरतो द्विजाः ॥ १८॥

English
O brahmins, ignorance is of various types. Perfect knowledge is not so. I shall explain their modes. Listen respectively. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मादितृणपर्यन्तं यत्किञ्चिद् दृश्यते त्विह ।
तत्सर्वं शिव एवास्ति मिथ्या नानात्वकल्पना ॥ १९॥

English
Whatever is seen here beginning with Brahmā is Śiva himself. The concept of manifoldness is unreal. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टेः पूर्वं शिवः प्रोक्तः सृष्टेर्मध्ये शिवस्तथा ।
सृष्टेरन्ते शिवः प्रोक्तः सर्वशून्ये सदाशिवः ॥ २०॥

English
Śiva is said to be before creation. Śiva is there in the midst of creation. Śiva is said to be at the end of creation. When everything is a void, Sadāśiva exists. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्चतुर्गुणः प्रोक्तः शिव एव मुनीश्वराः ।
स एव सगुणो ज्ञेयः शक्तिमत्त्वाद् द्विधापि सः ॥ २१॥

English
O great sages, thus Śiva is fourfold. He alone shall be known as Saguṇa. In view of the possession of Śakti he is twofold. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
येनैव विष्णवे दत्ताः सर्वे वेदाः सनातनाः ।
वर्णा मात्रा ह्यनेकाश्च ध्यानं स्वस्य च पूजनम् ॥ २२॥

English
It is by him that the eternal Vedas, syllables, syllabic instants and his meditation and worship were given to Viṣṇu. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशानः सर्वविद्यानां श्रुतिरेषा सनातनी ।
वेदकर्ता वेदपतिस्तस्माच्छम्भुरुदाहृतः ॥ २३॥

English
The eternal Śruti says—"Iśānaḥ Sarvavidyānām". Hence Śiva is the creator of the Vedas and the lord of the Vedas. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव शङ्करः साक्षात्सर्वानुग्रहकारकः ।
कर्ता भर्ता च हर्ता च साक्षी निर्गुण एव सः ॥ २४॥

English
He alone is Śiva himself who blesses all. He is the creator, sustainer and annihilator. He is the cosmic witness and Nirguṇa. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्येषां कालमानं च कालस्य कलना न हि ।
महाकालः स्वयं साक्षान्महाकालीसमाश्रितः ॥ २५॥

English
Restriction of time is for others and not for Rudra. He is Mahākāla himself associating with Mahākāli. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा च ब्राह्मणा रुद्रं तथा कालीं प्रचक्षते ।
सर्वं ताभ्यां ततः प्राप्तमिच्छया सत्यलीलया ॥ २६॥

English
It is so that Brahmins mention Rudra and Kāli. Thanks to their wish and truthful sports everything is obtained by them. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
न तस्योत्पादकः कश्चिद्भर्ता हर्ता न तस्य हि ।
स्वयं सर्वस्य हेतुस्ते कार्यभूताच्युतादयः ॥ २७॥

English
He has no progenitor, no sustainer and no annihilator. He himself is the cause of all Viṣṇu and others are his creations. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वयं च कारणं कार्यं स्वस्य नैव कदाचन ।
एकोऽप्यनेकतां यातोऽप्यनेकोऽप्येकतां व्रजेत् ॥ २८॥

English
He is not the creation of any one. Though single he assumes manifold shapes. Seeming to be many he becomes unified into one. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एकं बीजं बहिर्भूत्वा पुनर्बीजं च जायते ।
बहुत्वे च स्वयं सर्वं शिवरूपी महेश्वरः ॥ २९॥

English
A single seed grows into a tree and produces many seeds. Even in their multiplicity they are of one tree. Mahesvara in the form of Siva is verily like this. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्परं शिवज्ञानं तत्त्वतस्तदुदाहृतम् ।
जानाति ज्ञानवानेव नान्यः कश्चिदृषीश्वराः ॥ ३०॥

English
This is the great knowledge of Siva explained factually. O great sages, only the wise know this, none else. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
ज्ञानं सलक्षणं ब्रूहि यज्ज्ञात्वा शिवतां व्रजेत् ।
कथं शिवश्च तत्सर्वं सर्वं वा शिव एव च ॥ ३१॥

English
The sages said :—  Please explain that knowledge with all its characteristics realising which one attains the state of Śiva. How is Śiva all and how is all Śiva? (31.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
एतदाकर्ण्य वचनं सूतः पौराणिकोत्तमः ।
स्मृत्वा शिवपदाम्भोजं मुनींस्तानब्रवीद्वचः ॥ ३२॥

English
Vyāsa said :—  On hearing these words, Sūta the excellent propounder of the Purāṇas remembered the lotus-like feet of Śiva and spoke these words to the sages. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्ये च ये समुत्पन्ना यथानुक्रमतो लयम् ।
यान्ति नैव तथा रुद्रः शिवे रुद्रो विलीयते ॥ ४३॥

---
