### **CHAPTER FORTYTHREE - (Review of Knowledge)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रूयतामृषयः सर्वे शिवज्ञानं यथा श्रुतम् ।
कथयामि महागुह्यं परमुक्तिस्वरूपकम् ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O ye sages, may this be heard. I shall explain the perfect knowledge of Siva in the manner I have heard. It is a great secret as it is the form of the highest salvation. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कनारदकुमाराणां व्यासस्य कपिलस्य च ।
एतेषां च समाजे तैर्निश्चित्य समुदाहृतम् ॥ २॥

English
In the assembly of Brahma, Narada, Kumara, Vyasa and Kapila they had discussed this and come to this conclusion. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ज्ञानं सदा ज्ञेयं सर्वं शिवमयं जगत् ।
शिवः सर्वमयो ज्ञेयः सर्वज्ञेन विपश्चिता ॥ ३॥

English
It shall be known that the entire universe is wholly Siva. That Siva is in everything must be known by the learned scholar. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
आब्रह्मतृणपर्यन्तं यत्किञ्चिद् दृश्यते जगत् ।
तत्सर्वं शिव एवास्ति स देवः शिव उच्यते ॥ ४॥

English
Beginning with Brahma and ending with a blade of grass whatever is seen as constituting the universe is Siva himself. That deity is called Siva. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यदेच्छा तस्य जायेत तदा च क्रियते त्विदम् ।
सर्वं स एव जानाति तं न जानाति कश्चन ॥ ५॥

English
When he wishes, this is created. He alone knows all. No one knows him. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
रचयित्वा स्वयं तच्च प्रविश्य दूरतः स्थितः ।
न तत्र च प्रविष्टोऽसौ निर्लिप्तश्चित्स्वरूपवान् ॥ ६॥

English
He himself creates it and enters it but stands far off. The Citsvarūpa (knowledge-formed) being who is pure does not really enter it. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा च ज्योतिषश्चैव जलादौ प्रतिबिम्बता ।
वस्तुतो न प्रवेशो वै तथैव च शिवः स्वयम् ॥ ७॥

English
Luminary beings are reflected in water etc. They do not actually enter them. Similarly Siva too appears entering other things. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्तुतस्तु स्वयं सर्वं क्रमो हि भासते शुभः ।
अज्ञानं च मतेर्भेदो नास्त्यन्यच्च द्वयं पुनः ॥ ८॥

English
Really Siva alone, the auspicious being, manifests himself. The ignorance of the same is a defect of the mind. In fact there is no second entity. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
दर्शनेषु च सर्वेषु मतिभेदः प्रदर्श्यते ।
परं वेदान्तिनो नित्यमद्वैतं प्रतिचक्षते ॥ ९॥

English
In all philosophical systems the concept of duality is evident. But the Vedantins call him eternal and non-dualistic. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्याप्यंशस्य जीवांऽशो ह्यविद्यामोहितोऽवशः ।
अन्योऽहमिति जानाति तया मुक्तो भवेच्छिवः ॥ १०॥

English
The individual soul, though it is a part of his becomes deluded by Avidyā. He then thinks that he is different. If he is released from Avidyā he becomes Siva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं व्याप्य शिवः साक्षाद् व्यापकः सर्वजन्तुषु ।
चेतनाचेतनेशोऽपि सर्वत्र शङ्करः स्वयम् ॥ ११॥

English
Siva pervades all creatures. He is the lord of the sentient and the insentient. He is the benefactor. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
उपायं यः करोत्यस्य दर्शनार्थं विचक्षणः ।
वेदान्तमार्गमाश्रित्य तद्दर्शनफलं लभेत् ॥ १२॥

English
He who cleverly tries means of realising him after resorting to the Vedantic path attains the fruit of his sight. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाग्निर्व्यापकश्चैव काष्ठे काष्ठे च तिष्ठति ।
यो वै मन्थति तत्काष्ठं स वै पश्यत्यसंशयम् ॥ १३॥

English
Just as the pervading fire is latent in every block of wood but only he who churns it sees it manifested, to be sure. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्त्यादिसाधनानीह यः करोति विचक्षणः ।
स वै पश्यत्यवश्यं हि तं शिवं नात्र संशयः ॥ १४॥

English
So also, the clever devotee who makes use of the expedients of devotion etc. certainly reaches Siva. This is undoubtedly true. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवःशिवःशिवश्चैव नान्यदस्तीति किञ्चन ।
भ्रान्त्या नानास्वरूपो हि भासते शङ्करः सदा ॥ १५॥

English
Lord Siva is everywhere. There is nothing else. Siva appears in different forms always due to our illusion. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा समुद्रो मृच्चैव सुवर्णमथवा पुनः ।
उपाधितो हि नानात्वं लभते शङ्करस्तथा ॥ १६॥

English
The ocean or the lump of clay or the piece of gold attains different shapes due to delimiting conditions. Siva too is so. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कार्यकारणयोर्भेदो वस्तुतो न प्रवर्तते ।
केवलं भ्रान्तिबुद्ध्यैव तदभावे स नश्यति ॥ १७॥

English
There is one essential difference between the material cause and its effect. The difference is due to illusory perception. If the one ceases to exist, the other is quelled. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा बीजात्प्ररोहश्च नानात्वं हि प्रकाशयेत् ।
अन्ते च बीजमेव स्यात्तत्प्ररोहश्च नश्यति ॥ १८॥

English
The shooting sprout from a seed may exhibit multiplicity but ultimately it becomes the seed and the shoot perishes. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानी च बीजमेव स्यात्प्ररोहो विकृतीर्मता ।
तन्निवृत्तौ पुनर्ज्ञानी नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १९॥

English
The perfectly wise is the seed. Deformity is the sprout. When the deformity disappears he becomes the perfectly wise again. There is nothing to doubt in this regard. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं शिवः शिवः सर्वो नास्ति भेदश्च कश्चन ।
कथं च विविधं पश्यत्येकत्वं च कथं पुनः ॥ २०॥

English
Everything is Siva. Siva is everything. There is no difference at all. How is this manifoldness seen? How is the unity regained? (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यथैकं चैव सूर्याख्यं ज्योतिर्नानाविधं जनैः ।
जलादौ च विशेषेण दृश्यते तत्तथैव सः ॥ २१॥

English
Just as the luminary called the sun is seen differently in water etc. so also is the case with it. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वत्र व्यापकश्चैव स्पर्शत्वं न विबध्यते ।
तथैव व्यापको देवो बध्यते न क्वचित्स वै ॥ २२॥

English
The all-pervading sky is not bound or fettered anywhere. So also the all-pervading lord is not bound anywhere. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
साहङ्कारस्तथा जीवस्तन्मुक्तः शङ्करः स्वयम् ।
जीवस्तुच्छः कर्मभोगी निर्लिप्तः शङ्करो महान् ॥ २३॥

English
The individual soul is contaminated by the ego. Siva is free from it. The individual soul is insignificant and it experiences the fruits of actions. But the great Siva is uncontaminated. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यथैकं च सुवर्णादि मिलितं रजतादिना ।
अल्पमूल्यं प्रजायेत तथा जीवोऽप्यहंयुतः ॥ २४॥

English
Gold mixed with silver or other base metal depreciates in value. So also is the individual soul in its association with the ego. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
यथैव हि सुवर्णादि क्षारादेः शोधितं शुभम् ।
पूर्ववन्मूल्यतां याति तथा जीवोऽपि संस्कृतेः ॥ २५॥

English
When a gold alloy is purified with chemicals it regains its original value. Similarly the consecrated soul too attains purity. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं सद्गुरुं प्राप्य भक्तिभावसमन्वितः ।
शिवबुद्ध्या करोत्युच्चैः पूजनं स्मरणादिकम् ॥ २६॥

English
The devotee at the outset shall go to a competent preceptor with devout and reverential feelings. He shall worship and serve him considering him Siva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्बुध्या देहतो याति सर्वपापादिको मलः ।
तदाज्ञानं च नश्येत ज्ञानवाञ्जायते यदा ॥ २७॥

English
Thanks to this conception, all sins and dirts are removed from the body. When he gains knowledge his ignorance disappears. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाहङ्कारनिर्मुक्तो जीवो निर्मलबुद्धिमान् ।
शङ्करस्य प्रसादेन याति शङ्करतां पुनः ॥ २८॥

English
Freed from the ego, the individual soul attains pure intellect. Thanks to Siva's grace he attains the state of Siva again. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यथादर्शस्वरूपे च स्वीयरूपं प्रदृश्यते ।
तथा सर्वत्रगं शम्भुं पश्यतीति सुनिश्चितम् ॥ २९॥

English
Just as one sees one's own form in the mirror so also the pure soul sees the all-pervading Siva, certainly. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवन्मुक्तः स एवासौ देहः शिर्णः शिवे मिलेत् ।
प्रारब्धवशगो देहस्तद्भिन्नो ज्ञानवान् मतः ॥ ३०॥

English
He becomes the living liberated soul. When the body perishes he merges into Siva. The body is begot by the Prarabdha karman. The perfectly wise is considered different from it. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शुभं लब्ध्वा न हृष्येत कुप्येल्लब्ध्वाशुभं न हि ।
द्वन्द्वेषु समता यस्य ज्ञानवानुच्यते हि सः ॥ ३१॥

English
If a person is not elated on acquiring something good and is not annoyed on acquiring something bad and if he has equanimity, he is said to be perfectly wise. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मयोगेन तत्त्वानामथवा च विवेकतः ।
यथा शरीरतो यायाच्छरीरं मुक्तिमिच्छता ॥ ३२॥

सदाशिवो विलीयेत मुक्तो विरहमेव च ।
ज्ञानमूलं तथाध्यात्म्यं तस्य भक्तिः शिवस्य च ॥ ३३॥

English
By the practice of Yoga, discrimination between the different principles is generated. Then there is a desire to get released from the body. The aspirant then is blessed with devotion to Śiva.²⁰⁰ (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तेश्च प्रेम सम्प्रोक्तं प्रेम्णश्च श्रवणं तथा ।
श्रवणाच्चापि सत्सङ्गः सत्सङ्गाच्च गुरुर्बुधः ॥ ३४॥

English
From devotion there arises love; from love, the desire to hear about the lord; from this desire, association with the good and from this association a competent preceptor is attained. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पन्ने च तथा ज्ञाने मुक्तो भवति निश्चितम् ।
इति चेज्ज्ञानवान्यो वै शम्भुमेव सदा भजेत् ॥ ३५॥

English
If knowledge is attained he certainly becomes liberated. Hence if one desires to be perfectly wise one should worship Śiva alone always. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्यया च भक्त्या वै युक्तः शम्भुं भजेत्पुनः ।
अन्ते च मुक्तिमायाति नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ३६॥

English
He shall worship Śiva with unflinching and exclusive devotion. Salvation is the result. There is nothing to be doubtful about this. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अतोऽधिको न देवोऽस्ति मुक्तिप्राप्त्यै च शङ्करात् ।
शरणं प्राप्य यं चैव संसाराद्विनिवर्तते ॥ ३७॥

English
There is no other deity greater than Śiva for the attainment of salvation. After seeking refuge in him one withdraws from worldly existence. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति मे विविधं वाक्यमृषीणां च समागतैः ।
निश्चित्य कथितं विप्रा धिया धार्यं प्रयत्नतः ॥ ३८॥

English
O brahmins, these words have been uttered by me after considering the statement of sages. You shall retain these strenuously in your minds. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं विष्णवे दत्तं शम्भुना लिङ्गसम्मुखे ।
विष्णुना ब्रह्मणे दत्तं ब्रह्मणा सनकादिषु ॥ ३९॥

English
At the outset this instruction was given to Viṣṇu by Śiva in front of his phallic image. It was then given to Brahmā by Viṣṇu and to Sanaka²⁰¹ and others by Brahmā. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
नारदाय ततः प्रोक्तं तज्ज्ञानं सनकादिभिः ।
व्यासाय नारदेनोक्तं तेन मह्यं कृपालुना ॥ ४०॥

English
That knowledge was then imparted to Nārada by Sanaka and others. Nārada communicated it to Vyāsa and the merciful Vyāsa communicated it to me. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
मया चैव भवद्भ्यश्च भवद्भिर्लोकहेतवे ।
स्थापनीयं प्रयत्नेन शिवप्राप्तिकरं च तत् ॥ ४१॥

English
I have now communicated it to you. For the welfare of the world you shall establish this in the world strenuously. It is conducive to the attainment of Śiva. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वश्च समाख्यातं यत्पृष्टोऽहं मुनीश्वराः ।
गोपनीयं प्रयत्नेन किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छथ ॥ ४२॥

English
O great sages; thus, what I have been asked has been narrated by me. You shall preserve this carefully. What else do you wish to hear? (42.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
एतचछ्रुत्वा तु ऋषय आनन्दं परमं गताः ।
हर्षगद्गदया वाचा नत्वा ते तुष्टुवुर्मुहुः ॥ ४३॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  On hearing this, the sages attained great bliss. After bowing to him they eulogised him again and again with words choked with pleasure. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
व्यासशिष्य नमस्तेऽस्तु धन्यस्त्वं शैवसत्तम ।
श्रावितं नः परं वस्तु शैवं ज्ञानमनुत्तमम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The sages said:—  O disciple of Vyasa, obeisance be to you. You are blessed. You are the most excellent of the devotees of Siva. The highest thing, the excellent knowledge of Siva has been imparted to us. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्माकं चेतसो भ्रान्तिर्गता हि कृपया तव ।
सन्तुष्टाः शिवसज्ज्ञानं प्राप्य त्वत्तो विमुक्तिदम् ॥ ४५॥

English
Thanks to your mercy, our mental delusion has vanished. Securing the excellent knowledge of Siva from you we are satisfied. This knowledge yields salvation. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
नास्तिकाय न वक्तव्यमश्रद्धाय शठाय च ।
अभक्ताय महेशस्य न चाशुश्रुषवे द्विजाः ॥ ४६॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O brahmins, this shall not be mentioned to an atheist or an unfaithful man or a rogue. Nor shall it be mentioned to one who has no devotion to Siva or who does not wish to hear. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
इतिहासपुराणानि वेदाच्छास्त्राणि चासकृत् ।
विचार्य्योद्धृत्य तत्सारं मह्यं व्यासेन भाषितम् ॥ ४७॥

English
After poring through Itihasas, Puranas, Vedas and Sāstras many a time, Vyasa extracted this essence and imparted it to me. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छ्रुत्वा ह्येकवारं भवेत्पापं हि भस्मसात् ।
अभक्तो भक्तिमाप्नोति भक्तस्य भक्तिवर्धनम् ॥ ४८॥

English
On hearing this once, all sins are reduced to ashes. A non-devotee becomes a devotee. This enhances the devotion of the devotee. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः श्रुते च सद्भक्तिर्मुक्तिः स्याच्च श्रुतेः पुनः ।
तस्मात्पुनः पुनः श्राव्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिफलेप्सुभिः ॥ ४९॥

English
If it is heard again one attains good devotion. If it is heard again liberation is attained. Hence this must be heard again and again by the devotees who desire to get the benefit of worldly pleasures and salvation. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
आवृत्तयः पञ्च कार्याः समुद्दिश्य फलं परम् ।
तत्प्राप्नोति न सन्देहो व्यासस्य वचनं त्विदम् ॥ ५०॥

English
With the expectation of the greatest benefit it shall be repeated five times. There is no doubt. He will attain it. Vyasa has said so. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
न दुर्लभं हि तस्यैव येनेदं श्रुतमुत्तमम् ।
पञ्चकृत्वस्तदावृत्त्या लभ्यते शिवदर्शनम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Nothing is difficult to get for a man who hears this excellent thing. By repeating it five times Siva's vision is attained. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरातनाश्च राजानो विप्रा वैश्याश्च सत्तमाः ।
इदं श्रुत्वा पञ्चकृत्वो धिया सिद्धिं परां गताः ॥ ५२॥

English
Ancient kings, brahmins and excellent Vaiśyas have attained great Siddhis by listening to this with attention, five times. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रोष्यत्यद्यापि यश्चेदं मानवो भक्तितत्परः ।
विज्ञानं शिवसंज्ञं वै भुक्तिं मुक्तिं लभेच्च सः ॥ ५३॥

English
Even now if a man listens to this with devotion he will attain perfect knowledge of Siva, worldly pleasures and salvation. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
इति तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा परमानन्दमागताः ।
समानर्चुश्च ते सूतं नानावस्तुभिरादरात् ॥ ५४॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  On hearing his words the sages were extremely delighted. They worshipped Sūta with reverence offering him various articles. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्कारैः स्तवैश्चैव स्वस्तिवाचनपूर्वकम् ।
आशीर्भिर्वर्धयामासुः सन्तुष्टाश्छिन्नसंशयाः ॥ ५५॥

English
Satisfied and cleared of their doubts they bowed to him, eulogised him, hailed him. and blessed him. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
परस्परं च सन्तुष्टाः सूतस्ते च सुबुद्धयः ।
शम्भुं देवं परं मत्वा नमन्ति स्म भजन्ति च ॥ ५६॥

English
Considering Sūta to be the great lord Śiva they worshipped him and bowed to him. With good intellect they praised him speaking to one another. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
एतच्छिवसुविज्ञानं शिवस्यातिप्रियं महत् ।
भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदं दिव्यं शिवभक्तिविवर्धनम् ॥ ५७॥

English
The excellent knowledge of Siva is pleasing to Siva; it yields worldly pleasures and salvation; it enhances devotion to Siva. It is divine. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं हि संहिता पुण्या कोटिरुद्राह्वया परा ।
चतुर्थी शिवपुराणस्य कथिता मे मुदावहा ॥ ५८॥

English
Thus the fourth meritorious and pleasing compendium of Sivapurāna named Koṭirudra has been narrated. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
एतां यः श‍ृणुयाद्भक्त्या श्रावयेद्वा समाहितः ।
स भुक्त्वेहाखिलान्भोगानन्ते परगतिं लभेत् ॥ ५९॥

English
He who listens to this with devotion and narrates this with pure mind enjoys all pleasures here and attains salvation hereafter. (59.)
