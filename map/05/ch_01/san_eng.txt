### **CHAPTER ONE - (*Kṛṣṇa meets Upamanyu*)**

---

Sanskrit
यो धत्ते भुवनानि सत्त्वगुणवान्स्रष्टा रजःसंश्रयः
संहर्त्ता तमसान्वितो गुणवतीं मायामतीत्य स्थितः ।
सत्यानन्दमनन्तबोधममलं ब्रह्मादिसंज्ञास्पदं
नित्यं सत्त्वसमन्वयादधिगतं पूर्णं शिवं धीमहि ॥ १॥

English
We meditate upon Śiva, the perfect deity, who supports the seven$^{203}$ worlds with the Sattva Guṇa as his attribute, who creates them resorting to Rajas and who annihilates them when endowed with Tamas; who is beyond Māyā consisting of the Guṇas and who stands firm; who is truth, bliss, and the infinite pure consciousness; who assumes the names of Brahmā etc. and who is attained always by the application of Sattva Guṇa. (1.)

Sanskrit

चतुर्थी कोटिरुद्राख्या श्राविता संहिता त्वया ॥ २॥

English
*The sages said:—*  O Sūta of great intelligence, O Sūta, disciple of Vyāsa, obeisance be to you. The fourth compendium Koṭirudra saṁhitā has been narrated by you. (2.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
He who decisively annihilates the universe at the end of a kalpa and for whom there was none to be spared from death in the three worlds consisting of the mobile and immobile beings which being cast away from his hands burnt instantaneously and entirely, certainly within half a moment. (16-17.)

Sanskrit
