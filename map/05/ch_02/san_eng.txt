### **CHAPTER TWO - (Upamanyu's instruction)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य मुनेर्वाक्यमुपमन्योर्महात्मनः ।
जातभक्तिर्महादेवे कृष्णः प्रोवाच तं मुनिम् ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  On hearing the words of sage Upamanyu the great soul, devotion to lord Śiva was generated in Kṛṣṇa and he spoke to the sage. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्ण उवाच ।
उपमन्यो मुने तात कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ।
ये ये शिवं समाराध्य कामानापुश्च तान्वद ॥ २॥

English
Lord Kṛṣṇa said :—  O dear, O Upamanyu, be merciful to me. Please mention the details of the persons who have attained their desires by propitiating Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्योपमन्युः स मुनिः शैववरो महान् ।
कृष्णवाक्यं सुप्रशस्य प्रत्युवाच कृपानिधिः ॥ ३॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  On hearing the words of Kṛṣṇa, that sage Upamanyu, the great devotee of Śiva, the storehouse of mercy praised him and said. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
यैर्यैर्भवाराधनतः प्राप्तो हृत्काम एव हि ।
तांस्तान् भक्तान् प्रवक्ष्यामि श‍ृणु त्वं वै यदूद्वह ॥ ४॥

English
Upamanyu said:—  O leading scion of the family of Yadus, I shall enumerate the various devotees, by whom the cherished desires of their hearts have been realised through the propitiation of Siva. Please listen. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शर्वात्सर्वामरैश्वर्यं हिरण्यकशिपुः पुरा ।
वर्षाणां दशलक्षाणि सोऽलभच्चन्द्रशेखरात् ॥ ५॥

English
Formerly Hiranyakasipu attained the glory of the suzerainty over all the gods for a period of a million years from the moon-crested lord Siva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याथ पुत्रप्रवरो नन्दनो नाम विश्रुतः ।
स च शर्ववरादिन्द्रं वर्षायुतमयोधयत् ॥ ६॥

English
His excellent son Nandana was very famous. Thanks to Siva's boon he kept Indra under him for ten thousand years. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुचक्रं च तद् घोरं वज्रमाखण्डलस्य च ।
शीर्णं पुराभवत्कृष्ण तदङ्गेषु महाहवे ॥ ७॥

English
O Krsna, the terrible discus of Visnu and the thunderbolt of Indra became shattered against his person during a great battle. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
न शस्त्राणि वहन्त्यङ्गे धर्मतस्तस्य धीमतः ।
ग्रहस्यातिबलस्याजौ चक्रवज्रमुखान्यपि ॥ ८॥

English
The weapons, missiles, discus, thunderbolt never became effective in the body of Graha of great might during battles, thanks to the Dharma of that intelligent one. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्द्यमानाश्च विबुधा ग्रहेण सुबलीयसा ।
देवदत्तवरा जघ्नुरसुरेन्द्रान् सुरा भृशम् ॥ ९॥

English
The gods were harassed by Graha the mighty one. Leading Asuras to whom the lord had granted boons killed gods with impunity. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तुष्टो विद्युत्प्रभस्यापि त्रैलोक्येश्वरतामदात् ।
शतवर्षसहस्राणि सर्वलोकेश्वरो भवः ॥ १०॥

English
Siva, the lord of all worlds, when satisfied granted the lordship over the three worlds to Vidyutprabha for a period of a hundred thousand years. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा पुत्रसहस्राणामयुतं च ददौ शिवः ।
मम चानुचरो नित्यं भविष्यस्यब्रवीदिति ॥ ११॥

English
Siva blessed him with ten million sons also and said "You will become my follower for ever." (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कुशद्वीपे शुभं राज्यमददाद्भगवान् भवः ।
स तस्मै शङ्करः प्रीत्या वासुदेव प्रहृष्टधीः ॥ १२॥

English
O Krsna, the delighted lord Siva, lovingly gave him an auspicious kingdom in Kusa Dvipa.[215] (12.)

---

Sanskrit
धात्रा सृष्टः शतमखो दैत्यो वर्षशतं पुरा ।
तपः कृत्वा सहस्रं तु पुत्राणामलभद्भवात् ॥ १३॥

English
The Daitya Satamukha created by Brahma, performed the penance of Śiva formerly for a hundred years and obtained a thousand sons. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
याज्ञवल्क्य इति ख्यातो गीतो वेदेषु वै मुनिः ।
आराध्य स महादेवं प्राप्तवान् ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ॥ १४॥

English
The famous sage Yājñavalkya glorified in the Vedas, propitiated him and acquired excellent knowledge. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदव्यासस्तु यो नाम्ना प्राप्तवानतुलं यशः ।
सोऽपि शङ्करमाराध्य त्रिकालज्ञानमाप्तवान् ॥ १५॥

English
The sage Vedavyasa who had acquired unequalled glory propitiated him and attained knowledge of the three times—past, present and future. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रेण वालखिल्यास्ते परिभूतास्तु शङ्करात् ।
लेभिरे सोमहर्तारं गरुडं सर्वदुर्जयम् ॥ १६॥

English
The Bālakhilyas harassed by Indra secured from Śiva the invincible Garuḍa who brought them Soma juice. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
आपः प्रनष्टाः सर्वाश्च पूर्वरोषात्कपर्दिनः ।
शर्वं समकपालेन देवैरिष्ट्वाः प्रवर्तिताः ॥ १७॥

English
When all the waters became dried up and lost by the prior fury of Śiva they were worshipped and made to flow again by the gods through the skull. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रेर्भार्या चानसूया त्रीणि वर्षशतानि च ।
मुशलेषु निराहारा सुप्त्वा शर्वात्ततः सुतान् ॥ १८॥

दत्तात्रेयं मुनिं लेभे चन्द्रं दुर्वाससं तथा ।
गङ्गां प्रवर्तयामास चित्रकूटे पतिव्रता ॥ १९॥

English
Anasūyā, the chaste wife of Atri observed fast for three hundred years sleeping only on the threshing rod. She secured the three sons—the sage Dattātreya, Durvāsas and the moon, thanks to Śiva's favour. She made Gaṅgā flow in Citrakūṭa.[216] (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
विकर्णश्च महादेवं तथा भक्तसुखावहम् ।
प्रसाद्य महतीं सिद्धिमाप्तवान् मधुसूदन ॥ २०॥

English
O Madhusūdana, after pleasing Mahadeva the bestower of happiness to the devotees, Vikarna obtained great Siddhi. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्रसेनो नृपः शम्भुं प्रसाद्य दृढभक्तिमान् ।
समस्तनृपभीतिभ्योऽभयं प्रापातुलं च कम् ॥ २१॥

English
King Citrasena of steady devotion delighted Siva and attained freedom from the terror of all other kings and unequalled pleasure as well. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकरो गोपिकासूनुर्नृपपूजाविलोकनात् ।
जातभक्तिर्महादेवे परमां सिद्धिमाप्तवान् ॥ २२॥

English
Srikara, the son of a cowherdess, became his devotee on seeing the worship of Siva performed by the king and attained great Siddhi. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
चित्राङ्गदो नृपसुतः सीमन्तिन्याः पतिर्हरे ।
शिवानुग्रहतो मग्नो यमुनायां मृतो न हि ॥ २३॥

English
O Krsna, prince Citrangada, the husband of Simantini, was drowned in Yamuna but was saved by Siva's blessings (23.)

---

Sanskrit
स च तक्षालयं गत्वा तन्मैत्रीं प्राप्य सुव्रतः ।
आयातः स्वगृहं प्रीतो नानाधनसमन्वितः ॥ २४॥

English
He went to the abode of Taksa 217 and gained his friendship. The delighted prince of good rites then returned to his house with diverse riches. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सीमन्तिनी प्रिया तस्य सोमव्रतपरायणा ।
शिवानुग्रहतः कृष्ण लेभे सौभाग्यमुत्तमम् ॥ २५॥

English
O Krsna, his beloved wife Simantini who regularly performed Somavrata acquired excellent blessedness and fortune, thanks to Śiva's grace. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्रभावाद् व्रते तस्मिन्नेको द्विजसुतः पुरा ।
कश्चित्स्त्रीत्वं गतो लोभात्कृतदाराकृतिश्छलात् ॥ २६॥

English
While she was performing this Vrata a certain brahmin boy, out of greed, had assumed the guise of a woman fraudulently. Due to her power he became a woman permanently. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
चञ्चुका पुंश्चली दुष्टा गोकर्णे द्विजतः पुरा ।
श्रुत्वा धर्मकथां शम्भोर्भक्त्या प्राप परां गतिम् ॥ २७॥

English
Cañculā, a vicious harlot, heard a virtuous story from a brahmin at Gokarṇa and became a devotee of Śiva. She attained the great goal. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वस्त्र्यनुग्रहतः पापी बिन्दुगो चञ्चुकापतिः ।
श्रुत्वा शिवपुराणं स सद्गतिं प्राप शाङ्करीम् ॥ २८॥

English
Binduga²¹⁸ the sinning husband of Cañculā heard Śivapurāṇa through the good offices of his wife and attained the good goal of Śiva. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पिङ्गला गणिका ख्याता मदराह्वो द्विजाधमः ।
शैवमृषभमभ्यर्च्य लेभाते सद्गतिं च तौ ॥ २९॥

English
Piṅgalā a notorious prostitute and the base brahmin Mandara worshipped the bull of Śiva and attained the supreme goal. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
महानन्दाभिधा कश्चिद्वेश्या शिवपदारता ।
दृढात्पणात्सुप्रसाद्य शिवं लेभे च सद्गतिम् ॥ ३०॥

English
A certain prostitute Mahānandā devoted to Śiva, dedicating herself at the feet of Śiva delighted him and attained good goal. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कैकेयी द्विजबाला च सादराह्वा शिवव्रता ।
परमं हि सुखं प्राप शिवेशव्रतधारणात् ॥ ३१॥

English
Kaikeyī, a brahmin girl, respectfully performing Śiva's Vratas attained great happiness, thanks to the regular performance of Śiva's vrata. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
विमर्षणश्च नृपतिः शिवभक्तिं विधाय वै ।
गतिं लेभे परां कृष्ण शिवानुग्रहतः पुरा ॥ ३२॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, king Vimarṣaṇa adopting Śiva's devotion attained great goal formerly, thanks to Śiva's blessings. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्जनश्च नृपः पापी बहुस्त्रीलम्पटः खलः ।
शिवभक्त्या शिवं प्राप निर्लिप्तः सर्वकर्मसु ॥ ३३॥

English
Durjana, a sinful knavish king running after women attained Śiva, thanks to devotion to Śiva. He was unaffected by all actions. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सस्त्रीकः शम्बरो नाम्ना शङ्करश्च शिवव्रती ।
चिताभस्मरतो भक्त्या लेभे तद्गतिमुत्तमाम् ॥ ३४॥

English
A hunter Śaṅkara performing Śivavrata along with his wife and attached to the Bhasma from the funeral pyre attained the great goal by his devotion. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सौमिनी नाम चाण्डाली सम्पूज्याज्ञानतो हि सा ।
लेभे शैवीं गतिं कृष्ण शङ्करानुग्रहात्परात् ॥ ३५॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, a cāṇḍāla woman Saumini performed the worship by ignorance and attained the goal of Śiva by the blessings of the lord. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
महाकालाभिधो व्याधः किरातः परहिंसकः ।
समभ्यर्च्य शिवं भक्त्या लेभे सद्गतिमुत्तमाम् ॥ ३६॥

English
A hunter Mahākāla, a wild Kirāta habitually injuring others, worshipped Śiva with devotion and attained the goal of the good. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्वासा मुनिशार्दूलः शिवानुग्रहतः पुरा ।
तस्तार स्वमतं लोके शिवभक्तिं विमुक्तिदाम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Durvāsas a leading sage popularised his cult in the world, thanks to the blessings of Śiva. He spread Śiva's cult of devotion that yields salvation to the devotee. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
कौशिकश्च समाराध्य शङ्करं लोक शङ्करम् ।
ब्राह्मणोऽभूत्क्षत्रियश्च द्वितीय इव पद्मभूः ॥ ३८॥

English
Propitiating Śiva, the benefactor of the worlds, Viśvāmitra a Kṣatriya became a brahmin. He was as though a second Brahmā since he created a new heaven. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवमभ्यर्च्य सद्भक्त्या विरञ्चिः शैवसत्तमः ।
अभूत्सर्गकरः कृष्ण सर्वलोकपितामहः ॥ ३९॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, by worshipping Śiva with devotion Brahmā, the most excellent devotee of Śiva, became the creator and grandfather of the worlds. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्कण्डेयो मुनिवरश्चिरञ्जीवी महाप्रभुः ।
शिवभक्तवरः श्रीमान् शिवानुग्रहतो हरे ॥ ४०॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, it was due to Śiva's blessings that the excellent sage Mārkaṇḍeya, the holy lord and foremost among Śiva's devotees, became long-lived. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
देवेन्द्रो हि महाशैवस्त्रैलोक्यं बुभुजे पुरा ।
शिवानुग्रहतः कृष्ण सर्वदेवाधिपः प्रभुः ॥ ४१॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, thanks to Śiva's blessings, Devendra, a great devotee of Śiva, enjoyed the three worlds formerly. He became the lord of all the gods. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
बलिपुत्रो महाशैवः शिवानुग्रहतो वशी ।
बाणो बभूव ब्रह्माण्डनायकः सकलेश्वरः ॥ ४२॥

English
Bāṇa, the son of Bali, was a great devotee of Śiva and had great control over senses. Thanks to Śiva's blessings he became the leader of the universe and lord of all. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

शिवानुग्रहतोऽभूवंस्तथा रामो हि शाङ्करः ॥ ४३॥

कणादो भार्गवश्चैव गुरुर्गौतम एव च ।
शिवभक्त्या बभूवुस्ते महाप्रभव ईश्वराः ॥ ४४॥

English
Śakti became Viṣṇu due to great devotion, Dadhīca became a great Īśvara, Rāma became a devotee of Śaṅkara. Kāṇāda, Bhārgava, Guru and Gautama became great masters and lords due to devotion to Śiva. (43-44.)

---

Sanskrit

भवमाराधयामास मनोयज्ञेन माधव ॥ ४५॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, Śākalya, the praiseworthy soul propitiated Śiva for nine hundred years by means of mental sacrifice. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
O descendant of Yadu, thus the great sage secured boons from Śiva and became well known and honoured in the three worlds. (48.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे पञ्चम्यामुमासंहितायां सनत्कुमारव्याससंवादे उपमन्यूपदेशो नाम द्वितीयोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥
