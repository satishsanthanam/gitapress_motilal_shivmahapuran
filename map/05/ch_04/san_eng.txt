### **CHAPTER FOUR - (The exhibition of Śiva's spell)**

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
तात तात महाभाग धन्यस्त्वं हि महामते ।
अद्भुतेयं कथा शम्भोः श्राविता परभक्तिदा ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O dear, O fortunate one, you are blessed, O dear one of great intellect. This wonderful narrative of Śiva that bestows great devotion has been narrated. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्ब्रूहि कथां शम्भोर्व्यासप्रश्नानुसारतः ।
सर्वज्ञस्त्वं व्यासशिष्यः शिवतत्त्वविचक्षणः ॥ २॥

English
Mention again the story of Śiva in accordance with Vyāsa's enquiry. You are omniscient. You are the disciple of Vyāsa and an expert in Śiva's principles. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवमेव गुरुर्व्यासः पृष्टवान् मेऽजसम्भवम् ।
सनत्कुमारं सर्वज्ञं शिवभक्तं मुनीश्वरम् ॥ ३॥

English
*Sūta said:—* It was in this very same manner that my preceptor Vyāsa asked Sanatkumāra, son of Brahmā, who was an omniscient great sage and a devotee of Śiva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ श्रावितेयं शुभा कथा ।
शङ्करस्य महेशस्य नानालीलाविहारिणः ॥ ४॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, this auspicious story of Śiva, the great lord indulging in different sports has been narrated to you. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्ब्रूहि महादेवमहिमानं विशेषतः ।
श्रद्धा च महती श्रोतुं मम तात प्रवर्धते ॥ ५॥

English
Speak again particularly of the greatness of lord Siva. O dear, my faith and eagerness to hear the same is great and it increases. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
महिम्ना येन शम्भोस्तु ये ये लोके विमोहिताः ।
मायया ज्ञानमाहृत्य नानालीलाविहारिणः ॥ ६॥

English
Who are the people deluded by the Māyā of Śiva who indulges in great sports? What is that greatness by which their knowledge has been deluded? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाबुद्धे शाङ्करीं सुखदां कथाम् ।
यस्याः श्रवणमात्रेण शिवे भक्तिः प्रजायते ॥ ७॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O Vyasa, of great intellect, listen to the pleasing story of Siva, the mere hearing of which generates devotion to Siva. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः सर्वेश्वरो देवः सर्वात्मा सर्वदर्शनः ।
महिम्ना तस्य सर्वं हि व्याप्तं च सकलं जगत् ॥ ८॥

English
Lord Siva is the lord of all, soul of all and the vision of all. The entire universe has been pervaded by his greatness. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्यैव परा मूर्तिर्ब्रह्मविष्ण्वीश्वरात्मिका ।
सर्वभूतात्मभूताख्या त्रिलिङ्गालिङ्गरूपिणी ॥ ९॥

English
It is the great image of Siva that manifests as Brahma, Visnu and Siva. It is that which has become the soul of all living beings. It is both three-symbolled and symbolless. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां योनयश्चाष्टौ मानुषी नवमी च या ।
तिरश्चां योनयः पञ्च भवन्त्येवं चतुर्दश ॥ १०॥

English
There are eight classes of gods. Human beings constitute the ninth. There are five types of lower beings. Thus there are fourteen types of living beings. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
भूता वा वर्तमाना वा भविष्याश्चैव सर्वशः ।
शिवात्सर्वे प्रवर्तन्ते लीयन्ते वृद्धिमागताः ॥ ११॥

English
All living beings past, present and future originate from Siva, flourish in him and finally merge into him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मेन्द्रोपेन्द्रचन्द्राणां देवदानवभोगिनाम् ।
गन्धर्वाणां मनुष्याणामन्येषां वापि सर्वशः ॥ १२॥

बन्धुर्मित्रमथाचार्यो रक्षन्नेतार्थवान् गुरुः ।
कल्पद्रुमोऽथ वा भ्राता पिता माता शिवो मतः ॥ १३॥

English
Siva is the kinsman, friend, guide, protector, leader, purposeful preceptor, wish-yielding Kalpa tree, brother, father or mother of Brahma, Indra, Visnu, moon, the gods, danavas, serpents, Gandharvas, human beings and all others. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः सर्वमयः पुंसां स्वयं वेद्यः परात्परः ।
वक्तुं न शक्यते यश्च परं चानुपरं च यत् ॥ १४॥

English
Siva is identical with all; self-manifest to all men, greater than the greatest. Than him it is impossible to predicate any other greater thing. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्माया परमा दिव्या सर्वत्र व्यापिनी मुने ।
तदधीनं जगत्सर्वं सदेवासुरमानुषम् ॥ १५॥

English
O sage, his Māyā is divine and great pervading everything. The entire universe is subservient to it, inclusive of the gods, Asuras and human beings. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
कामेन स्वसहायेन प्रबलेन मनोभुवा ।
सर्वः प्रधर्षितो वीरो विष्ण्वादिप्रबलोऽपि हि ॥ १६॥

English
All heroic beings, even Visnu and mighty people, have been overwhelmed by the mighty Kama, born of the mind who has no other helper. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवमायाप्रभावेणाभूद्धरिः काममोहितः ।
परस्त्रीधर्षणं चक्रे बहुवारं मुनीश्वर ॥ १७॥

English
O great sage, Visnu was deluded by Kama by the power of Siva's Maya. He outraged the modesty of other men's wives many times. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रस्त्रिदशपो भूत्वा गौतमस्त्रीविमोहितः ।
पापं चकार दुष्टात्मा शापं प्राप मुनेस्तदा ॥ १८॥

English
Indra, the lord of the gods, became fascinated by Gautama's wife. That vicious one committed sin and so was cursed by the sage. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पावकोऽपि जगच्छ्रेष्ठो मोहितः शिवमायया ।
कामाधीनः कृतो गर्वात्ततस्तेनैव चोद्धृतः ॥ १९॥

English
Even the firegod, the most excellent in the universe was deluded by Siva's Maya. Due to his pride he became subservient to lust and was ultimately saved by Siva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्प्राणोऽपि गर्वेण मोहितः शिवमायया ।
कामेन निर्जितो व्यास चक्रेऽन्यस्त्रीरतिं पुरा ॥ २०॥

English
The wind-god, the vital air of the universe was deluded by Siva's Maya due to his arrogance. Overwhelmed by Kama, Vyasa too, had sexual intercourse with other men's wives. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
चण्डरश्मिस्तु मार्तण्डो मोहितः शिवमायया ।
कामाकुलो बभूवाशु दृष्ट्वाश्वीं हयरूपधृक् ॥ २१॥

English
The sun of fierce rays, deluded by Siva's Maya became lustful on seeing a mare and assumed the form of a horse. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रश्च मोहितः शम्भोर्मायया कामसङ्कुलः ।
गुरुपत्नीं जहाराथ युतस्तेनैव चोद्धृतः ॥ २२॥

English
The moon deluded by Siva's Maya became lustful and abducted the wife of Bhaspati and had his sexual union with her. But he was finally saved by Bhaspati himself. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वं तु मित्रावरुणौ घोरे तपसि संस्थितौ ।
मोहितौ तावपि मुनी शिवमायाविमोहितौ ॥ २३॥

English
Formerly the two gods Mitra and Varuṇa, while performing a severe penance, were fascinated and deluded by Śiva's Māyā. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
उर्वशीं तरुणीं दृष्ट्वा कामुकौ सम्बभूवतुः ।
मित्रः कुम्भे जहौ रेतो वरुणोऽपि तथा जले ॥ २४॥

English
On seeing the youthful maiden Ūrvasi, both of them had the emission of their semen. Mitra deposited his semen in a pot and Varuṇa in water. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कुम्भात्समुत्पन्नो वसिष्ठो मित्रसम्भवः ।
अगस्त्यो वरुणाज्जातो वडवाग्निसमद्युतिः ॥ २५॥

English
Vasiṣṭha was born out of the pot and is called Mitra's son. Agastya born of Varuṇa has the lustre of the submarine fire. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षश्च मोहितः शम्भोर्मायया ब्रह्मणः सुतः ।
भ्रातृभिः स भगिन्यां वै भोक्तुकामोऽभवत्पुरा ॥ २६॥

English
Dakṣa, son of Brahmā deluded by Śiva's Māyā, had a desire for sexual intercourse with his sister along with his other brothers. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा च बहुवारं हि मोहितः शिवमायया ।
अभवद्भोक्तुकामश्च स्वसुतायां परासु च ॥ २७॥

English
Many times, Brahmā, deluded by Śiva's Māyā, had the desire for sexual union with his own daughter and other women. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
च्यवनोऽपि महायोगी मोहितः शिवमायया ।
सुकन्यया विजह्रे स कामासक्तो बभूव ह ॥ २८॥

English
Gyavana the great Yogin, deluded by Śiva's Māyā, became lustful and had sexual dalliance with Sukanyā. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्यपः शिवमायातो मोहितः कामसङ्कुलः ।
ययाचे कन्यकां मोहाद्धन्वनो नृपतेः पुरा ॥ २९॥

English
Kaśyapa became passionate on being deluded by Śiva's Māyā. Formerly, out of delusion, he requested king Dhanvan to give his daughter to him. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
गरुडः शाण्डिलीं कन्यां नेतुकामः सुमोहितः ।
विज्ञातस्तु तया सद्यो दग्धपक्षो बभूव ह ॥ ३०॥

English
The deluded Garuḍa while abducting the virgin Śāṇḍili was found out by her and got his wings burnt. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
विभाण्डको मुनिर्नारीं दृष्ट्वा कामवशं गतः ।
ऋष्यश‍ृङ्गसुतस्तस्य मृग्यां जातः शिवाज्ञया ॥ ३१॥

English
The sage Vibhāṇḍaka became lustful on seeing a woman. At the behest of Śiva, Ṛṣyaśṛṅga$^{225}$ was his son born of a hind. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
गौतमश्च मुनिः शम्भोर्मायामोहितमानसः ।
दृष्ट्वा शारद्वतीं नग्नां रराम क्षुभितस्तया ॥ ३२॥

रेतः स्कन्नं दधार स्वं द्रोण्यां चैव स तापसः ।
तस्माच्च कलशाज्जातो द्रोणः शस्त्रभृतां वरः ॥ ३३॥

English
The sage Gautama had his mind deluded by Śiva's Māyā. On seeing Śāṛadvati in the nude he was excited and he indulged in sexual intercourse with her. He collected the semen emitted in a wooden bowl. Droṇa the foremost among marksmen was born of that bowl. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
पराशरो महायोगी मोहितः शिवमायया ।
मत्स्योदर्या च चिक्रीडे कुमार्या दाशकन्यया ॥ ३४॥

English
The great yogin Parāśara, deluded by Śiva's Māyā, indulged in sexual intercourse with Matsyodari the virgin daughter of a fisherman. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वामित्रो बभूवाथ मोहितः शिवमायया ।
रेमे मेनकया व्यास वने कामवशं गतः ॥ ३५॥

English
O Vyāsa, Viśvāmitra became deluded by Śiva's Māyā and lustfully indulged in sexual dalliance with Menakā. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठेन विरोधं तु कृतवान्नष्टचेतनः ।
पुनः शिवप्रसादाच्च ब्राह्मणोऽभूत्स एव वै ॥ ३६॥

English
Becoming confounded in thought he rivalled with Vasiṣṭha. But thanks to Śiva's grace he became a brahmin. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
रावणो वैश्रवाः कामी बभूव शिवमायया ।
सीतां जह्रे कुबुद्धिस्तु मोहितो मृत्युमाप च ॥ ३७॥

English
Rāvaṇa, the son of Viśravas, became lustful due to Śiva's Māyā. The wicked-minded one abducted Sītā, became deluded and courted death. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतिर्मुनिवरो मोहितः शिवमायया ।
भ्रातृपत्न्या वशी रेमे भरद्वाजस्ततोऽभवत् ॥ ३८॥

English
The excellent sage Bṛhaspati deluded by Śiva's Māyā had sexual intercourse with his brother's wife and Bharadvāja was born. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इति मायाप्रभावो हि शङ्करस्य महात्मनः ।
वर्णितस्ते मया व्यास किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ३९॥

English
O Vyāsa, thus the power of the Māyā of Śiva, the great soul has been narrated to you. What else do you wish to hear? (39.)
