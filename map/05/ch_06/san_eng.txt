### **CHAPTER SIX - (Different types of sins) 229**

Sanskrit

English

English
Debauchery, miserliness, malice towards good men illicit approach to another man's wife, defiling the virgins of good men.
3 Parivitti (the elder brother who is not married but who has allowed the younger brother to marry); Parivett (the younger brother who marries thus); Giving daughters to these two or allowing them to officiate in sacrifices. (2.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
Selling of sacrificial parks and ponds, wives and children, pilgrimages and fasts, sacred rites and investiture with sacred thread. (6.)

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English
Using bullock constantly as a vehicle through wild jungles, Uccatana (exorcising) and Abhicara (black magic) acceptance of grains as gift, working as a physician. (9.)

Sanskrit

English
Engagement in sacred rites to satisfy the palate or the sexual urge, teaching only the text of the Vedas (not the meaning). (10.)

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English
Those who have ceased to perform sacrifices to the gods and the manes, those who have abandoned their duties and rites, persons of evil conduct, atheists, sinners, and habitual liars. (13.)

Sanskrit

English
He who indulges in sexual intercourse during new moon and full moon days during day time, or in the vaginal passages of animals, or through other passages or emits semen in water, or cohabits with a woman in her monthly course. (14.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
The foregoing are some of the minor sins. Men or women guilty of the same are minor sinners, I shall mention some others also. Listen. (17.)

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Those who undergo agony due to another man's wife; those who have an eye on another man's wealth; those who steal that and those who make use of false weights. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Those who cause misery to brahmins by means of kicks and blows; the brahmins who serve Śūdra women and drink wine due to passion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Those who are cruel and engaged in sinful acts; those who are fond of violence and those who perform Dāna, Yajña and other rites as a profession for livelihood. (21.)

English

Sanskrit

(46-49.)

Sanskrit
नियमाचारयुक्तानां प्रमादात्स्खलितात्मनाम् ।
प्रायश्चित्तैर्गुरुः शास्ता न बुधैरिष्यते यमः ॥ ५५॥

English
For those who regularly observe restraints and disciplined life but who err slightly due to oversight, the preceptor is the chastiser through expiatory rites and not Yama as explained by learned men. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
पारदारिकचौराणामन्यायव्यवहारिणाम् ।
नृपतिः शासकः प्रोक्तः प्रच्छन्नानां स धर्म्मराट् ॥ ५६॥

English
The king is the chastiser for outragers of others' wives and thieves and unjust persons. But for those who are hidden Yama is the chastiser. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात् कृतस्य पापस्य प्रायश्चित्तं समाचरेत् ।
नाभुक्तस्यान्यथा नाशः कल्पकोटिशतैरपि ॥ ५७॥

English
Hence one should perform expiatory rites for the commission of sins. If the sin is not consumed it will not perish even in hundreds of crores of Kalpas. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
यः करोति स्वयं कर्म कारयेच्चानुमोदयेत् ।
कायेन मनसा वाचा तस्य पापगतिः फलम् ॥ ५८॥

English
If the action is by oneself or through one's incitement or instigation or if it is applauded by one later, physically, mentally or verbally he reaps the fruit of the sin. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
परिवित्तिः परिवेत्ता यया च परिविद्यते ।
तयोर्दानं च कन्यायास्तयोरेव च याजनम् ॥ ३॥
