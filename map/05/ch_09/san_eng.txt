### **CHAPTER NINE - (Pangs of hell)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एषु पापाः प्रपच्यन्ते शोष्यन्ते नरकाग्निषु ।
यातनाभिर्विचित्राभिरास्वकर्मक्षयाद् भृशम् ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  In the hellish fires, the sinners are roasted and dried up by diverse tortures till their action is exhausted completely. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वमलप्रक्षयाद्यद्वदग्नौ धास्यन्ति धातवः ।
तत्र पापक्षयात्पापा नराः कर्मानुरूपतः ॥ २॥

English
Just as metals are melted in fire to remove their impurities so also sinners are put in hells in order to remove their sins. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सुगाढं हस्तयोर्बद्ध्वा ततः श‍ृङ्खलया नराः ।
महावृक्षाग्रशाखासु लम्ब्यन्ते यमकिङ्करैः ॥ ३॥

English
The hands are tightly tied and the men are battered. Then they are suspended from the branches of tall trees by the attendants of Yama. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्ते सर्वयत्नेन क्षिप्ता दोलन्ति किङ्करैः ।
दोम्यन्तश्चातिवेगेन विसंज्ञा यान्ति योजनम् ॥ ४॥

English
Then they are pushed by the servants till they begin to oscillate a Yojana unconscious by their rapidity. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तरिक्षस्थितानां च लोहभारशतं पुनः ।
पादयोर्बध्यते तेषां यमदूतैर्महाबलैः ॥ ५॥

English
Even as they are suspended in mid air, iron hundred Bhāras in weight is tied to their feet by the emissaries of Yama. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन भारेण महता प्रभृशं ताडिता नराः ।
ध्यायन्ति स्वानि कर्माणि तूष्णीं तिष्ठन्ति निश्चलाः ॥ ६॥

English
Afflicted by such a heavy weight the sinners begin to think about their evil actions and keep quiet and motionless. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽङ्कुशैरग्निवर्णैर्लोहदण्डैश्च दारुणैः ।
हन्यन्ते किङ्करैघोरैः समन्तात्पापकर्मिणः ॥ ७॥

English
Then the sinners are beaten with goads of fiery colour and terrible iron rods by the terrible servants of Yama. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः क्षारेण दीप्तेन वह्नेरपि विशेषतः ।
समन्ततः प्रलिप्यन्ते तीव्रेण तु पुनः पुनः ॥ ८॥

English
Then they are again smeared with glowing acid more unbearable than fire. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
द्रुतेनात्यन्तलिप्तेन कृत्ताङ्गा जर्जरीकृताः ।
पुनर्विदार्य चाङ्गानि शिरसः प्रभृति क्रमात् ॥ ९॥

वृन्ताकवत्प्रपच्यन्ते तप्तलोहकटाहकैः ।
विष्ठापूर्णे तथा कूपे कृमीणां निचये पुनः ॥ १०॥

मेदोऽसृक्पूयपूर्णायां वाप्यां क्षिप्यन्ति ते पुनः ।
भक्ष्यन्ते कृमिभिस्तीक्ष्णैर्लोहतुण्डैश्च वायसैः ॥ ११॥

English
Their limbs are cut and smothered, gradually torn and severed and smeared with molten metal. They are then roasted like brinjal in red-hot iron cauldrons. They are then cast into wells full of filth, swarms of worms or in tanks full of putrid fat and blood. They are eaten by worm and crows with beaks strong as iron. (9-11.)

---

Sanskrit
श्वभिर्देंशैर्वृकैर्व्याघ्रै रौद्रैश्च विकृताननैः ।
पच्यन्ते मत्स्यवच्चापि प्रदीप्ताङ्गारराशिषु ॥ १२॥

English
Dogs, mosquitoes, wolves and tigers of terrible and hideous faces too devour them. They are roasted like fish over glowing heaps of coal. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
भिन्नाः शूलैः सुतीक्ष्णैश्च नराः पापेन कर्मणा ।
तैलयन्त्रेषु चाक्रम्य घोरैः कर्मभिरात्मनः ॥ १३॥

तिला इव प्रपीड्यन्ते चक्राख्ये जनपिण्डकाः ।
भ्रज्यन्ते चातपे तप्तलोहभाण्डेष्वनेकधा ॥ १४॥

English
Due to their sinful actions men are pierced through with sharp spears. They are put in oil extracting machines called Cakra and crushed like gingelly seeds and beaten into pulp. They are fried in iron cauldrons red hot in the blazing sunlight. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
तैलपूर्णकटाहेषु सुतप्तेषु पुनःपुनः ।
बहुधा पच्यते जिह्वा प्रपीड्योरसि पादयोः ॥ १५॥

English
They are fried in boiling oil in cauldrons again and again. Their tongues, chests and feet are struck in many ways. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
यातनाश्च महत्योऽत्र शरीरस्याति सर्वतः ।
निःशेषनरकेष्वेवं क्रमन्ति क्रमशो नराः ॥ १६॥

English
Here the tortures to the body are very severe. Men thus go from one hell to another and are tortured in all the hells. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नरकेषु च सर्वेषु विचित्रा यमयातनाः ।
याम्यैश्च दीयते व्यास सर्वाङ्गेषु सुकष्टदाः ॥ १७॥

English
O vyasa, terrible tortures are inflicted in diverse ways in all the hells by Yama's emissaries. They are grievous and painful to every part of the body. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्वलदङ्गारमादाय मुखमापूर्य ताड्यते ।
ततः क्षारेण दीप्तेन ताम्रेण च पुनःपुनः ॥ १८॥

घृतेनात्यन्ततप्तेन तदा तैलेन तन्मुखम् ।
इतस्ततः पीडयित्वा भृशमापूर्य हन्यते ॥ १९॥

English
Their mouths are filled with the burning coals and again with acid red hot, copper hot clarified butter and oil and they are beaten afterwards. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्ठाभिः कृमिभिश्चापि पूर्यमाणाः क्वचित् क्वचित् ।
परिष्वजन्ति चात्युग्रां प्रदीप्तां लोहशाल्मलीम् ॥ २०॥

English
They are filled with faeces and worms and are forced to embrace the red hot fierce iron-silk cotton tree. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
हन्यन्ते पृष्ठदेशे च पुनर्दीप्तैर्महाघनैः ।
दन्तुरेणादिकुण्ठेन क्रकचेन बलीसया ॥ २१॥

शिरःप्रभृति पीड्यन्ते घोरैः कर्मभिरात्मजैः ।
खाद्यन्ते च स्वमांसानि पीयते शोणितं स्वकम् ॥ २२॥

English
They are then beaten on the back with heated hammer. Their heads and their limbs are crushed with too blunt tooth-edged strong scissors. They are thus tortured by their own actions. Their flesh is eaten and their blood is drunk by them. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नं पानं न दत्तं यैः सर्वदा स्वात्मपोषकैः ।
इक्षुवत्ते प्रपीड्यन्ते जर्जरीकृत्य मुद्गरैः ॥ २३॥

English
Those who fed and nurtured only themselves and never made any gift of food or drink are crushed with iron rods like sugarcane. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
असितालवने घोरे छिद्यन्ते खण्डशस्ततः ।
सूचीभिर्भिन्नसर्वाङ्गाः तप्तशूलाग्ररोपिताः ॥ २४॥

English
In the terrible hell Asitalavana they are cut into pieces. Their limbs are pierced through with needles. They are staked on to heated spearheads. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सञ्चाल्यमाना बहुशः क्लिश्यन्ते न म्रियन्ति च ।
तथा च तच्छरीराणि सुखदुःखसहानि च ॥ २५॥

English
Tossed about many times their bodies do not perish but are severely pained. They become used to bear the pleasure or pain. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
देहादुत्पाट्य मांसानि भिद्यन्ते स्वैश्च मुद्गरैः ।
दन्तुराकृतिभिर्घोरैर्यमदूतैर्बलोत्कटैः ॥ २६॥

English
Flesh is torn off their bodies and they are pounded with iron clubs of tooth-edged shapes by terribly mighty emissaries of Yama. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
निरुच्छ्वासे निरुच्छ्वासास्तिष्ठन्ति नरके चिरम् ।
उत्ताड्यन्ते तथोच्छ्वासे वालुकासदने नराः ॥ २७॥

English
In the hell Nirucchvasa they are forced to stand without breathing, for a long time. In the hell Ucchvasa they are hit and thrashed in a house of sand. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
रौरवे रोदमानाश्च पीड्यन्ते विविधैर्वधैः ।
महारौरवपीडाभिर्महान्तोऽपि रुदन्ति च ॥ २८॥

English
In the hell Raurava they are made to cry and inflicted pain by various tortures. Due to the tortures in the hell, Maharaurava, even great men cry. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्सु वक्त्रे गुदे मुण्डे नेत्रयोश्चैव मस्तके ।
निहन्यन्ते घनैस्तीक्ष्णैः सुतप्तैर्लोहशङ्कुभिः ॥ २९॥

English
They are beaten on the faces, feet anus, skull, eyes and forehead with blocks of iron and sharp-edged redhot iron spikes. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतप्तवालुकायां तु प्रयोज्यन्ते मुहुर्मुहुः ।
जन्तुपङ्के भृशं तप्ते क्षिप्ताः क्रन्दन्ति विस्वरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
They are again and again rolled on hot sand. Cast into boiling marsh of creatures they make harsh shrieking sounds. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
कुम्भीपाकेषु पच्यन्ते तप्ततैलेषु वै मुने ।
पापिनः क्रूरकर्माणोऽसह्येषु सर्वथा पुनः ॥ ३१॥

English
O sage, in the hell kumbhipaka sinners of ruthless misdeeds are fried in unbearable boiling oil. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
लालाभक्षेषु पापास्ते पात्यन्ते दुःखदेषु वै ।
नानास्थानेषु च तथा नरकेषु पुनःपुनः ॥ ३२॥

English
The sinners are felled and dragged along various torturing places again and again in the hell named Lalabhaksa. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सूचीमुखे महाक्लेशे नरके पात्यते नरः ।
पापी पुण्यविहीनश्च ताड्यते यमकिङ्करैः ॥ ३३॥

English
Sinners devoid of merit are beaten by Yama's servants. They are hurled into the torturous hell Sucimukha. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
लोहकुम्भे विनिःक्षिप्ताः श्वसन्तश्च शनैः शनैः ।
महाग्निना प्रपच्यन्ते स्वपापैरेव मानवाः ॥ ३४॥

English
They are put into iron pots. They breathe hard and slow. They are scorched by great fire, as if by their own sins. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
दृढं रज्ज्वादिभिर्बद्ध्वा प्रपीड्यन्ते शिलासु च ।
क्षिप्यन्ते चान्धकूपेषु दश्यन्ते भ्रमरैर्भृशम् ॥ ३५॥

English
They are tied tightly with ropes and are rolled on rocks and harassed. They are cast into deep gloomy wells. They are subjected to bee-bites. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
कृमिभिर्भिन्नसर्वाङ्गाः शतशो जर्जरीकृताः ।
सुतीक्ष्णक्षारकूपेषु क्षिप्यन्ते तदनन्तरम् ॥ ३६॥

English
When their bodies are cut, bitten and gnawed by worms in a hundred places, they are hurled into wells of pungent acid. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
महाज्वालेऽत्र नरके पापाः क्रन्दन्ति दुःखिताः ।
इतश्चेतश्च धावन्ति दह्यमानास्तदर्चिषा ॥ ३७॥

English
In the blazing hell the distressed sinners, shriek and run about here and there scorched by its flames. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
पृष्ठे चानीय तुण्डाभ्यां विन्यस्तस्कन्धयोजिते ।
तयोर्मध्येन वाकृष्य बाहुपृष्ठेन गाढतः ॥ ३८॥

बद्धाः परस्परं सर्वे सुभृशं पाशरज्जुभिः ।
बद्धपिण्डास्तु दृश्यन्ते महाज्वाले तु यातनाः ॥ ३९॥

English
People are clubbed and tied together with mouths resting on shoulders and hands brought through the hollow and tied with nooses and ropes to the back. Such bundles of bodies are seen in the Mahajvāla undergoing tortures. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
रज्जुभिर्वेष्टिताश्चैव प्रलिप्ताः कर्दमेन च ।
करीषतुषवह्नौ च पच्यन्ते न म्रियन्ति च ॥ ४०॥

English
They are tied with ropes, smeared with mud and scorched in husk and cowdung fire but do not perish. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सुतीक्ष्णचरितास्ते हि कर्कशासु शिलासु च ।
आस्फाल्य शतशः पापाः पच्यन्ते तृणवत्ततः ॥ ४१॥

English
Dragged and rolled through rough and coarse rocks, beaten and burnt like straw, the sinners of ruthless couduct, are tortured. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शरीराभ्यन्तरगतैः प्रभूतैः कृमिभिर्नराः ।
भक्ष्यन्ते तीक्ष्णवदनैरात्मदेहक्षयाद् भृशम् ॥ ४२॥

कृमीणां निचये क्षिप्ताः पूयमांसास्थिराशिषु ।
तिष्ठन्त्युद्विग्नहृदयाः पर्वताभ्यां निपीडिताः ॥ ४३॥

English
The worms eat through their bodies with their sharp mouths and teeth. With their bodies gradually hurled over swarms of worms, heaps of putrid flesh and bones they stay there distressed, dejected and crushed between two mountains (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
तप्तेन वज्रलेपेन शरीरमनुलिप्यते ।
अधोमुखोर्ध्वपादश्च तातप्यन्ते स्म वह्निना ॥ ४४॥

English
They are kept with face down and legs up and scorched in fire. Their bodies are smeared with hot adamantine solution. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
वदनान्तःप्रविन्यस्तां सुप्रतप्तामयोगदाम् ।
ते खादन्ति पराधीनास्तैस्ताड्यन्ते च मुद्गरैः ॥ ४५॥

English
Red hot iron clubs are thrust into their mouths. They are helplessly forced to gnaw at it. They are then beaten with iron threshing rods. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं व्यास कुकर्माणो नरकेषु पचन्ति हि ।
वर्णयामि विवर्णत्वं तेषां तत्त्वाय कर्मिणाम् ॥ ४६॥

English
O Vyasa, thus the sinners of misdeeds are cooked and scorched in hells. I shall now describe their various modes of undergoing the tortures of hell. (46.)

---
