### **CHAPTER ELEVEN - (The glory of the gift of food)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
कृतपापा नरा यान्ति दुःखेन महतान्विताः ।
यममार्गे सुखं यैश्च तान्धर्मान्वद मे प्रभो ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  Those who commit sins go along the path to Yama with great misery. O holy lord, now narrate the righteous deeds which render pleasure in the path of Yama. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अवश्यं हि कृतं कर्म भोक्तव्यमविचारतः ।
शुभाशुभमथो वक्ष्ये तान्धर्मान् सुखदायकान् ॥ २॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  Auspicious or inauspicious, one has of necessity to reap the fruit of actions committed by him without thinking. I shall now mention those sacred rites that yield happiness. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्र ये शुभकर्माणः सौम्यचित्ता दयान्विताः ।
सुखेन ते नरा यान्ति यममार्गं भयावहम् ॥ ३॥

English
Men who perform auspicious rites here, whose minds are gentle and who are merciful pass through the terrible path of Yama happily. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यः प्रदद्याद् द्विजेन्द्राणामुपानत्काष्ठपादुके ।
स नरोऽश्वेन महता सुखं याति यमालयम् ॥ ४॥

English
He who makes gifts of sandals of leather or wood to leading brahmins goes happily to the abode of Yama on a great horse. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
छत्रदानेन गच्छन्ति यथा छत्रेण देहिनः ।
शिबिकायाः प्रदानेन तद्रथेन सुखं व्रजेत् ॥ ५॥

English
By making gifts of umbrella they go holding an umbrella. By making gifts of palanquin he goes by means of a chariot. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
शय्यासनप्रदानेन सुखं याति सुविश्रमम् ।
आरामच्छायाकर्तारो मार्गे वा वृक्षरोपकाः ।
व्रजन्ति यमलोकं च आतपेऽति गतक्लमाः ॥ ६॥

English
By making gifts of beds and seats he goes with ample rest. Those who make shady parks or those who plant trees on the wayside go without weariness even in the hottest region. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
यान्ति पुष्पकयानेन पुष्पारामकरा नराः ।
देवायतनकर्तारः क्रीडन्ति च गृहोदरे ॥ ७॥

English
Men making flower gardens go by the aerial chariot Puspaka. Makers of temples feel at home on the way. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्तारश्च तथा ये च यतीनामाश्रमस्य च ।
अनाथमण्डपानां तु क्रीडन्ति च गृहोदरे ॥ ८॥

English
The founders of hermitages of ascetics and Rest houses for the orphans feel as if they play in the house. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाग्निगुरुविप्राणां मातापित्रोश्च पूजकाः ।
पूज्यमाना नरा यान्ति कामुकेन यथासुखम् ॥ ९॥

English
Worshippers of the gods, fire-god brahmins and their parents go along that path as they please. They are honoured on the way. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
द्योतयन्तो दिशः सर्वा यान्ति दीपप्रदायिनः ।
प्रतिश्रयप्रदानेन सुखं यान्ति निरामयाः ॥ १०॥

English
Those who gift lamps, go brightly illuminating all the ten quarters. By giving dwellings and asylums they go without ailments on the way. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्राम्यमाणा गच्छन्ति गुरुशुश्रूषका नराः ।
आतोद्यविप्रदातारः सुखं यान्ति स्वके गृहे ॥ ११॥

English
Those who habitually serve their preceptors go without difficulty. They get ample rest on the way. Those who gift musical instruments to brahmins go happily feeling at home on the way. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वकामसमृद्धेन यथा गच्छन्ति गोप्रदाः ।
अत्र दत्तान्नपानानि तान्याप्नोति नरः पथि ॥ १२॥

English
Givers of cows go along the path flourishing with all their desires. They get those food-stuffs and drinks which they give away here, on that path also. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पादशौचप्रदानेन सजलेन पथा व्रजेत् ।
पादाभ्यङ्गं च यः कुर्यादश्वपृष्ठेन गच्छति ॥ १३॥

English
By giving water for washing the feet one goes along the path full of water. He who gifts oils for massaging the feet goes on horseback along that way. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पादशौचं तथाभ्यङ्गं दीपमन्नं प्रतिश्रयम् ।
यो ददाति सदा व्यास नोपसर्पति तं यमः ॥ १४॥

English
O Vyasa, Yama never comes near the man who always makes gifts of water for washing the feet, oil for massaging, lamps, foodstuffs and dwellings. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
हेमरत्नप्रदानेन याति दुर्गाणि निस्तरन् ।
रौप्यानडुत्प्रदानेन यमलोकं सुखेन सः ॥ १५॥

English
By making gifts of gold and gems he crosses all impassable hurdles. By gifting silver and oxen he goes to Yamaloka happily. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवमादिभिर्दानैः सुखं यान्ति यमालयम् ।
स्वर्गे तु विविधान्भोगान् प्राप्नुवन्ति सदा नराः ॥ १६॥

English
By these and similar charitable gifts people go happily to Yama's abode. They attain different pleasures in the heaven. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषामेव दानानामन्नदानं परं स्मृतम् ।
सद्यः प्रीतिकरं हृद्यं बलबुद्धिविवर्धनम् ॥ १७॥

English
Of all charitable gifts the gift of food is the greatest. It is pleasing. It gives pleasure immediately. It enhances strength and intellect. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
नान्नदानसमं दानं विद्यते मुनिसत्तम ।
अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि तदभावे म्रियन्ति च ॥ १८॥

English
O excellent sage, there is no other charitable gift on a par with the gift of food. All living beings originate from food. In its absence they die. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्तं मांसं वसा शुक्रं क्रमादन्नात्प्रवर्धते ।
शुक्राद्भवन्ति भूतानि तस्मादन्नमयं जगत् ॥ १९॥

English
Food gives blood, flesh, fat and semen and out of semen living beings are born. Hence the universe is identical with food. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
हेमरत्नाश्वनागेन्द्रैर्नारीस्रक्चन्दनादिभिः ।
समस्तैरपि सम्प्राप्तैर्न रमन्ति बुभुक्षिताः ॥ २०॥

English
Hungry men are not delighted even if they possess these things viz—gold, gems, horses, elephants, women, garlands, sandal paste and the like. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भस्था जायमानाश्च बालवृद्धाश्च मध्यमाः ।
आहारमभिकाङ्क्षन्ति देवदानवराक्षसाः ॥ २१॥

English
All persons, the child in the womb, the new born babe, the child, the middle aged man, the old man, the gods, the dānavas or Rākṣasas desire food. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुधा निःशेषरोगाणां व्याधिः श्रेष्ठतमः स्मृतः ।
स चान्नौषधिलेपेन नश्यतीह न संशयः ॥ २२॥

English
Hunger is the acutest of all ailments. There is no doubt that it perishes on the application of the medicinal ointment viz food. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्ति क्षुधासमं दुःखं नास्ति रोगः क्षुधासमः ।
नास्त्यरोगसमं सौख्यं नास्ति क्रोधसमो रिपुः ॥ २३॥

English
There is no misery equal to hunger; no sickness equal to hunger; no happiness on a par with being free from sickness and no enemy equal to anger. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्त एव महत्पुण्यमन्नदाने प्रकीर्तितम् ।
तथा क्षुधाग्निना तप्ता म्रियन्ते सर्वदेहिनः ॥ २४॥

English
All embodied beings die when scorched by the fire of hunger. That is why it is glorified that there is great merit in the charitable gift of food. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नदः प्राणदः प्रोक्तः प्राणदश्चापि सर्वदः ।
तस्मादन्नप्रदानेन सर्वदानफलं लभेत् ॥ २५॥

English
The giver of food is the giver of life. The giver of life is the giver of all. Hence one attains the benefit of gifting everything by means of the charitable gift of food. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्यान्नपानपुष्टाङ्गः कुरुते पुण्यसञ्चयम् ।
अन्नप्रदातुस्तस्यार्धं कर्तुश्चार्धं न संशयः ॥ २६॥

English
If a man performs holy and sacred rites after being nourished with the food gifted by someone, the merit of the sacred rites is shared half and half by the two—namely by the giver of food and the performer of the rites. There is no doubt in this. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रैलोक्ये यानि रत्नानि भोगस्त्रीवाहनानि च ।
अन्नदानप्रदः सर्वमिहामुत्र च तल्लभेत् ॥ २७॥

English
The giver of charitable gift of food obtains here and hereafter whatever means of pleasure there are in the three worlds such as gems, women and vehicles. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मार्थकाममोक्षाणां देहः परमसाधनम् ।
तस्मादन्नेन पानेन पालयेद्देहमात्मनः ॥ २८॥

English
This body is the greatest of the means of achievement of virtue, wealth, love and salvation. Hence one should preserve one's body with food and drink. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नमेव प्रशंसन्ति सर्वमन्ने प्रतिष्ठितम् ।
अन्नेन सदृशं दानं न भूतं न भविष्यति ॥ २९॥

English
They praise only food. Everything is founded on food. There has never been a charitable gift like that of food, nor will there ever be any. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नेन धार्यते सर्वं विश्वं जगदिदं मुने ।
अन्नमूर्जस्करं लोके प्राणा ह्यन्ने प्रतिष्ठिताः ॥ ३०॥

English
O sage, the entire universe is sustained by food. It is food that infuses virility in the people. The vital airs are founded on food. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
दातव्यं भिक्षवे चान्नं ब्राह्मणाय महात्मने ।
कुटुम्बं पीडयित्वापि ह्यात्मनो भूतिमिच्छता ॥ ३१॥

English
Even by putting one's family to inconvenience and trouble one should give food to a mendicant brahmin of noble soul, if one wishes for one's prosperity. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
विददाति निधिश्रेष्ठं यो दद्यादन्नमर्थिने ।
ब्राह्मणायार्तरूपाय पारलौकिकमात्मनः ॥ ३२॥

English
He who gives food to a suppliant brahmin in distress actually makes an excellent deposit facilitating his benefit in the other world. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्चयेद्भूतिमन्विच्छन्काले द्विजमुपस्थितम् ।
श्रान्तमध्वनि वृत्त्यर्थं गृहस्थो गृहमागतम् ॥ ३३॥

English
A householder, desirous of prosperity shall worship a brahmin who has approached him at the proper time for his livelihood, or who is travel-weary and comes to his house as a guest. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नदः पूजयेद् व्यास सुशीलस्तु विमत्सरः ।
क्रोधमुत्पतितं हित्वा दिवि चेह महत्सुखम् ॥ ३४॥

नाभिनिन्देदधिगतं न प्रणुद्यात्कथञ्चन ।
अपि श्वपाके शुनि वा नान्नदानं प्रणश्यति ॥ ३५॥

English
O Vyāsa, a giver of food, of good conduct and free from malice, is honoured. A gift of food never goes in vain be it to a dog or a person who cooks dogs. If a person eschews anger that surges up he derives happiness here and hereafter. He should not encourage it even if it is natural. One shall try to eschew it somehow. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रान्तायादृष्टपूर्वाय ह्यन्नमध्वनि वर्तते ।
यो दद्यादपरिक्लिष्टः स समृद्धिमवाप्नुयात ॥ ३६॥

English
He who gives food to a weary, unknown traveller, without feeling distressed for the same will derive prosperity. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पितॄन् देवांस्तथा विप्रानतिथींश्च महामुने ।
यो नरः प्रीणयत्यन्नैः तस्य पुण्यफलं महत् ॥ ३७॥

English
The meritorious benefit of the man, O great sage, who delights Pīṭṭs, gods, brahmins and guests with food-stuffs, in really immense. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नं पानं च शूद्रेऽपि ब्राह्मणे च विशिष्यते ।
न पृच्छेद्गोत्रचरणं स्वाध्यायं देशमेव च ॥ ३८॥

English
Gift of food and drink whether to a Śūdra or to a brahmin is equally excellent. One should not ask the spiritual lineage,²³⁶ branch of the Vedas²³⁷ or the country of the recipient. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
भिक्षितो ब्राह्मणेनेह दद्यादन्नं च यः पुमान् ।
स याति परमं स्वर्गं यावदाभूतसम्प्लवम् ॥ ३९॥

English
He who gives food when begged by a brahmin, goes to the highest heaven and remains there till the final dissolution when all living beings are destroyed. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नदस्य च वृक्षाश्च सर्वकामफलान्विताः ।
भवन्तीह यथा विप्रा हर्षयुक्तास्त्रिविष्टपे ॥ ४०॥

English
The garden of a food-giver acquires the fruits of his cherished desire as the brahmins do in the highest heaven. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नदानेन ये लोकाः स्वर्गे विरचिता मुने ।
अन्नदातुर्महादिव्यास्तान् श‍ृणुष्व महामुने ॥ ४१॥

English
O sage, O great sage, listen to the divine regions which are created in the heaven by the gifts of food, for the giver of food. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
भवनानि प्रकाशन्ते दिवि तेषां महात्मनाम् ।
नानासंस्थानरूपाणि नानाकामान्वितानि च ॥ ४२॥

English
The abodes of those noble souls in the heaven shine in various shapes and are equipped with every desirable object. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वकामफलाश्चापि वृक्षा भवनसंस्थिताः ।
हेमवाप्यः शुभाः कूपा दीर्घिकाश्चैव सर्वशः ॥ ४३॥

English
The trees bear the fruits of all desires. The golden lakes in the mansions, the auspicious wells and tanks are all excellent. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
घोषयन्ति च पानानि शुभान्यथ सहस्रशः ।
भक्ष्यभोज्यमयाः शैला वासांस्याभरणानि च ॥ ४४॥

English
The auspicious drinks of various varieties are proclaimed. There are huge mountains of food-stuffs, garments and ornaments. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षीरं स्रवन्त्यः सरितस्तथैवाज्यस्य पर्वताः ।
प्रासादाः पाण्डुराभासाः शय्याश्च कनकोज्ज्वलाः ॥ ४५॥

English
Milk flows in rivers there. There are huge mountains of ghee. The palaces are white in colour. Beds are brilliant like gold. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तानन्नदाश्च गच्छन्ति तस्मादन्नप्रदो भवेत् ।
यदीच्छेदात्मनो भव्यमिह लोके परत्र च ॥ ४६॥

English
The giver of food goes to these regions. One should be a giver of food if one wishes for one's own good in this or in the other world. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
एते लोकाः पुण्यकृतामन्नदानां महाप्रभाः ।
तस्मादन्नं विशेषेण दातव्यं मानवैर्ध्रुवम् ॥ ४७॥

English
These regions of the meritorious givers of food are very brilliant. Hence food shall be given as gift by the people. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नं प्रजापतिः साक्षादन्नं विष्णुः स्वयं हरः ।
तस्मादन्नसमं दानं न भूतं न भविष्यति ॥ ४८॥

English
Food is Brahma himself. Food is Visnu himself. Food is Siva. There has never been nor will there ever be a gift on a par with that of food. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वापि सुमहत्पापं यः पश्चादन्नदो भवेत् ।
विमुक्तः सर्वपापेभ्यः स्वर्गलोकं स गच्छति ॥ ४९॥

English
Even after committing a great sin if a man gives food to the needy he becomes rid of all sins. He goes to heaven. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्नपानाश्वगोवस्त्रशय्याच्छत्रासनानि च ।
प्रेतलोके प्रशस्तानि दानान्यष्टौ विशेषतः ॥ ५०॥

English
The eight gifts are honoured most in the land of the dead viz food, drink, horse, cow, garments, bed, umbrella and seat. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं दानविशेषेण धर्मराजपुरं नरः ।
यस्माद्याति विमानेन तस्माद्दानं समाचरेत् ॥ ५१॥

English
If a man wishes to go to the city of Yama in the aerial chariot, he shall necessarily make these special gifts. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदाख्यानमनघमन्नदानप्रभावतः ।
यः पठेत्पाठयेदन्यान्स समृद्धः प्रजायते ॥ ५२॥

English
This sacred narrative gives instances of the virtue of gifts of foodstuffs; whoever reads this or teaches this to others flourishes indeed. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणुयाच्छ्रावयेच्छ्राद्धे ब्राह्मणान्यो महामुने ।
अक्षय्यमन्नदानं च पितॄणामुपतिष्ठति ॥ ५३॥

English
O great sage, if a person listens to this or narrates this at the performance of Śrāddha to brahmins, it results in a never-ending gift of food to the Pīṭṭs. (53.)

इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे पञ्चम्यामुमासंहितायां अन्नदानमाहात्म्यवर्णनं नामैकादशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.११॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥
