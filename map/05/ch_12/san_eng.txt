### **CHAPTER TWELVE - (The glory of penance)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
पानीयदानं परमं दानानामुत्तमं सदा ।
सर्वेषां जीवपुञ्जानां तर्पणं जीवनं स्मृतम् ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  The gift of drink is the greatest and the most excellent of all gifts. Water the enlivener is the most satisfying to all living beings. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रपादानमतः कुर्यात् सुस्नेहादनिवारितम् ।
जलाश्रयविनिर्माणं महानन्दकरं भवेत् ॥ २॥

English
Hence one should make gifts of drinking watersheds lovingly and unrestrictedly. Making of water bases, tanks etc. is the cause of great bliss. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
इह लोके परे वापि सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ।
तस्माद्वापीश्च कूपांश्च तडागान् कारयेन्नरः ॥ ३॥

English
It is truthful, really truthful. There is no doubt in this, here and hereafter. Men should cause tanks, wells and lakes to be dug therefore. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्धं पापस्य हरति पुरुषस्य विकर्मणः ।
कूपः प्रवृत्तपानीयः सुप्रवृत्तस्य नित्यशः ॥ ४॥

English
A well that abounds in water removes half of the sin of the man who causes it to be dug. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं तारयते वंशं यस्य खाते जलाशये ।
गावः पिबन्ति विप्राश्च साधवश्च नराः सदा ॥ ५॥

English
If cows drink and good men and brahmins make use of the water in a water-base dug by a man, the whole family is redeemed. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
निदाघकाले पानीयं यस्य तिष्ठत्यवारितम् ।
सुदुर्गं विषमं कृच्छ्रं न कदाचिदवाप्यते ॥ ६॥

English
If there is plenty of water in the water-base dug by a man, during summer, he never suffers distress or mishaps at any time. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तडागानां च वक्ष्यामि कृतानां ये गुणाः स्मृता ।
त्रिषु लोकेषु सर्वत्र पूजितो यस्तडागवान् ॥ ७॥

English
I shall mention the benefits of lakes dug by men. He who has a lake to his credit is honoured in all the three worlds. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा मित्रसदने मैत्रं मित्रार्तिवर्जितम् ।
कीर्तिसञ्जननं श्रेष्ठं तडागानां निवेशनम् ॥ ८॥

English
Or the digging of lakes is conducive to the advent of fame and attainment of friendship in the abode of the sun but without distressing heat. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मस्यार्थस्य कामस्य फलमाहुर्मनीषिणः ।
तडागः सुकृतो येन तस्य पुण्यमनन्तकम् ॥ ९॥

English
He who digs a lake attains an endless merit. The wise say that it is the fruit of virtue, wealth and love. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्विधानां भूतानां तडागः परमाश्रयः ।
तडागादीनि सर्वाणि दिशन्ति श्रियमुत्तमाम् ॥ १०॥

English
The lake is the greatest resort of the four classes of living beings. The construction of the lake etc. indicates excellent glory. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा मनुष्या गन्धर्वाः पितरो नागराक्षसाः ।
स्थावराणि च भूतानि संश्रयन्ति जलाशयम् ॥ ११॥

English
The gods, human beings, Gandharvas, Pīṭṭs, serpents, Rākṣasas and the immobile beings depend upon water bases. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रावृडृतौ तडागे तु सलिलं यस्य तिष्ठति ।
अग्निहोत्रफलं तस्य भवतीत्याह चात्मभूः ॥ १२॥

English
Brahmā says that the benefit of the sacred rite Agmhotra is derived by a man in the lake dug by whom there is plenty of water in the rainy season. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शरत्काले तु सलिलं तडागे यस्य तिष्ठति ।
गोसहस्रफलं तस्य भवेन्नैवात्र संशयः ॥ १३॥

English
There is no doubt at all in this that the benefit of gifting away a thousand cows is derived by a man, in the lake dug by whom there is plenty of water in the Autumn. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
हेमन्ते शिशिरे चैव सलिलं यस्य तिष्ठति ।
स वै बहुसुवर्णस्य यज्ञस्य लभते फलम् ॥ १४॥

English
If there is plenty of water in the lake dug during the seasons of early winter and late winter, he derives the benefit of that sacrifice in which plenty of gold is given as a sacrificial gift. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वसन्ते च तथा ग्रीष्मे सलिलं यस्य तिष्ठति ।
अतिरात्राश्वमेधानां फलमाहुर्मनीषिणः ॥ १५॥

English
If there is plenty of water in the lake dug during the seasons of Spring and Summer, the wise say that he derives the benefit of the holy rites of Atirātra+ and Aśva-medha* sacrifices. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मुने व्यासाथ वृक्षाणां रोपणे च गुणान् श‍ृणु ।
प्रोक्तं जलाशयफलं जीवप्रीणनमुत्तमम् ॥ १६॥

English
O sage Vyāsa, the merit accruing from the digging of water bases, excellent and pleasing to all living beings, has been narrated to you. Now listen to the merits of planting trees. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीतानागतान् सर्वान् पितृवंशांस्तु तारयेत् ।
कान्तारे वृक्षरोपी यस्तस्माद् वृक्षांस्तु रोपयेत् ॥ १७॥

English
If a man plants trees in the forests, he enables his ancestors and the successors of his family, to gain redemption. Hence one should plant trees. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र पुत्रा भवन्त्येते पादपा नात्र संशयः ।
परं लोकं गतः सोऽपि लोकानाप्नोति चाक्षयान् ॥ १८॥

English
There is no doubt that these trees become his sons. When he goes to the other world he attains the undying regions. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पैः सुरगणान् सर्वान् फलैश्चापि तथा पितॄन् ।
छायया चातिथीन् सर्वान् पूजयन्ति महीरुहाः ॥ १९॥

English
Trees honour and worship the gods through flowers, the Pīṭṭs through fruits, and the guests through shade. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
किन्नरोरगरक्षांसि देवगन्धर्वमानवाः ।
तथैवर्षिगणाश्चैव संश्रयन्ति महीरुहान् ॥ २०॥

English
The Kinnaras, the serpents, the Rākṣasas, the gods, the Gandharvas, the human beings and the sages depend upon trees. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पुष्पिताः फलवन्तश्च तर्पयन्तीह मानवान् ।
इह लोके परे चैव पुत्रास्ते धर्मतः स्मृताः ॥ २१॥

English
While in bloom or when they bear fruits, the trees delight human beings. They are as sons by way of virtue in this world and hereafter. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तडागकृद् वृक्षरोपी चेष्टयज्ञश्च यो द्विजः ।
एते स्वर्गान्न हीयन्ते ये चान्ये सत्यवादिनः ॥ २२॥

English
These people never fail to attain heaven—viz. the digger of lakes, the planter of trees, the brahmin who performs sacrifices and those who speak truth. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यमेव परं ब्रह्म सत्यमेव परं तपः ।
सत्यमेव परो यज्ञः सत्यमेव परं श्रुतम् ॥ २३॥

English
Truth alone is the great Brahman, the greatest penance, the greatest sacrifice and the greatest learning. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यं सुप्तेषु जागर्ति सत्यं च परमं पदम् ।
सत्येनैव धृता पृथ्वी सत्ये सर्वं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ २४॥

English
When others sleep, truth is wakeful. Truth is the greatest base, it is truth alone by which the earth is sustained, everything is founded in truth. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तपो यज्ञश्च पुण्यं च देवर्षिपितृपूजने ।
आपो विद्या च ते सर्वे सर्वं सत्ये प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ २५॥

English
Penance, sacrifice, merit, the worship of the gods, sages and the Pīṛs, the waters, the Vidyās—all these things are established in truth. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यं यज्ञस्तपो दानं मन्त्रा देवी सरस्वती ।
ब्रह्मचर्यं तथा सत्यमोङ्कारः सत्यमेव च ॥ २६॥

English
Truth is sacrifice, penance, charitable gift, mantras, goddess Sarasvatī, celibacy and Oṅkāra. These are really trut¹. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्येन वायुरभ्येति सत्येन तपते रविः ।
सत्येनाग्निर्निर्दहति स्वर्गः सत्येन तिष्ठति ॥ २७॥

English
It is truth whereby the wind blows, the sun blazes, the fire burns and the heaven is upheld. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पालनं सर्ववेदानां सर्वतीर्थावगाहनम् ।
सत्येन वहते लोके सर्वमाप्नोत्यसंशयम् ॥ २८॥

English
By upholding truth the people can attain the benefits of keeping up the traditions of all the Vedas and taking the ceremonial ablutions in all holy centres. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वमेधसहस्रं च सत्यं च तुलया धृतम् ।
लक्षाणि क्रतवश्चैव सत्यमेव विशिष्यते ॥ २९॥

English
When weighed against truth in a balance, a thousand hōrse-sacrifices and a hundred thousand other sacrifices do not equal it. Truth alone excels. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्येन देवाः पितरो मानवोरगराक्षसाः ।
प्रीयन्ते सत्यतः सर्वे लोकाश्च सचराचराः ॥ ३०॥

English
The gods, the Pīṛs, the human beings, the serpents, the Rākṣasas and the worlds including the mobile and immobile beings are delighted by Truth. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यमाहुः परं धर्मं सत्यमाहुः परं पदम् ।
सत्यमाहुः परं ब्रह्म तस्मात्सत्यं सदा वदेत् ॥ ३१॥

English
They say that Truth is the greatest virtue, the greatest region, the greatest Brahman; hence one shall tell the Truth always. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनयः सत्यनिरतास्तपस्तप्त्वा सुदुश्चरम् ।
सत्यधर्मरताः सिद्धाः ततः स्वर्गं च ते गताः ॥ ३२॥

English
After performing very difficult penance, it is by depending solely on Truth, by maintaining truthful virtue that the sages have attained Siddhis and reached heaven. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अप्सरोगणसंविष्टैर्विमानैः परिमातृभिः ।
वक्तव्यं च सदा सत्यं न सत्याद्विद्यते परम् ॥ ३३॥

English
The sages have attained heaven travelling in aerial chariots along with the celestial damsels. Truth shall be ever spoken, for there is nothing greater than Truth. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अगाधे विपुले सिद्धे सत्यतीर्थे शुचिह्रदे ।
स्नातव्यं मनसा युक्तं स्थानं तत्परमं स्मृतम् ॥ ३४॥

English
In the deep, extensive and pure eddy, the holy centre of Truth, one shall mentally take one's ceremonial ablution. That is said to be the highest bliss. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मार्थे वा परार्थे वा पुत्रार्थे वापि मानवाः ।
अनृतं ये न भाषन्ते ते नराः स्वर्गगामिनः ॥ ३५॥

English
Men who never utter a lie whether for their own sake, or for other's sake, or even for their own son's sake, do go to heaven. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदा यज्ञास्तथा मन्त्राः सन्ति विप्रेषु नित्यशः ।
नौ भान्त्यपि ह्यसत्येषु तस्मात्सत्यं समाचरेत् ॥ ३६॥

English
The Vedas, the sacrifices and the Mantras exist in brahmins for ever. They are never visible to the untruthful. Hence one shall always speak truth. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
तपसो मे फलं ब्रूहि पुनरेव विशेषतः ।
सर्वेषां चैव वर्णानां ब्राह्मणानां तपोधन ॥ ३७॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O ascetic, please explain again with special emphasis on the fruit of penance for the brahmins as well as other castes. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
प्रवक्ष्यामि तपोऽध्यायं सर्वकामार्थसाधकम् ।
सुदुश्चरं द्विजातीनां तन्मे निगदतः श‍ृणु ॥ ३८॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  I shall explain the section on penance which is the means of achieving all the objects of desire, very difficult to be performed even by brahmins. Please listen even as I explain. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तपो हि परमं प्रोक्तं तपसा विद्यते फलम् ।
तपोरता हि ये नित्यं मोदन्ते सह दैवतैः ॥ ३९॥

English
Penance is the greatest rite. Fruits are achieved by penance. Those who are engaged in penance incessantly rejoice along with the gods. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा प्राप्यते स्वर्गस्तपसा प्राप्यते यशः ।
तपसा प्राप्यते कामस्तपः सर्वार्थसाधनम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Heaven, fame and love are achieved through penance. Penance is the means of achievement for all objects. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा मोक्षमाप्नोति तपसा विन्दते महत् ।
ज्ञानविज्ञानसम्पत्तिः सौभाग्यं रूपमेव च ॥ ४१॥

English
One achieves great things through penance, One attains salvation, knowledge, perfect wisdom, good fortune and beauty by penance. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाविधानि वस्तूनि तपसा लभते नरः ।
तपसा लभते सर्वं मनसा यद्यदिच्छति ॥ ४२॥

English
Man obtains different things through penance; he gets everything, whatever he wishes in his mind through penance. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
नातप्ततपसो यान्ति ब्रह्मलोकं कदाचन ।
नातप्ततपसां प्राप्यः शङ्करः परमेश्वरः ॥ ४३॥

English
Those who have never performed penance do not attain Brahmaloka; lord Siva is not accessible to those who have never performed penance. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्कार्यं किञ्चिदास्थाय पुरुषस्तपते तपः ।
तत्सर्वं समवाप्नोति परत्रेह च मानवः ॥ ४४॥

English
After deciding on a particular thing, if a man performs penance he attains it here as well as hereafter. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरापः परदारी च ब्रह्महा गुरुतल्पगः ।
तपसा तरते सर्वं सर्वतश्च विमुञ्चति ॥ ४५॥

English
The wine-addict, the defiler of other women, the brahmin-slayer, the defiler of teacher's bed overcome all sins through penance and get rid of them. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
अपि सर्वेश्वरः स्थाणुर्विष्णुश्चैव सनातनः ।
ब्रह्मा हुताशनः शक्रो ये चान्ये तपसान्विताः ॥ ४६॥

अष्टाशीतिसहस्राणि मुनीनामूर्ध्वरेतसाम् ।
तपसा दिवि मोदन्ते समेता दैवतैः सह ॥ ४७॥

English
Siva, the lord of all, the eternal Visnu, Brahma, fire god, Indra and others are endowed with penance. The eighty-eight thousand sages of sublimated semen rejoice in heaven through penance, along with the gods. (46-47.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा लभ्यते राज्यं स च शक्रः सुरेश्वरः ।
तपसापालयत्सर्वमहन्यहनि वृत्रहा ॥ ४८॥

English
Kingdoms are achieved through penance. Indra, the lord of gods and the slayer of Vṛtra, protects everything day by day through penance. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्याचन्द्रमसौ देवौ सर्वलोकहिते रतौ ।
तपसैव प्रकाशन्ते नक्षत्राणि ग्रहास्तथा ॥ ४९॥

English
Due to penance, the lords Sun and the Moon are engaged in the welfare of the worlds. The stars and the planets too shine by virtue of their penance. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
न चास्ति तत्सुखं लोके यद्विना तपसा किल ।
तपसैव सुखं सर्वमिति वेदविदो विदुः ॥ ५०॥

English
There is no happiness in the world that can be gained without penance. The knowers of the Vedas understand that all happiness is attained through penance alone. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमारोग्यं रूपवत्त्वं तथैव च ।
सौभाग्यं चैव तपसा प्राप्यते सर्वदा सुखम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Knowledge, perfect wisdom, health, beauty, good fortune and perpetual happiness are the offshoots of penance. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा सृज्यते विश्वं ब्रह्मा विश्वं विना श्रमम् ।
पाति विष्णुर्हरोऽप्यत्ति धत्ते शेषोऽखिलां महीम् ॥ ५२॥

English
It is by penance that Brahma creates the universe; Visnu protects it without any strain; Siva annihilates and Sesa upholds the entire earth. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वामित्रो गाधिसुतस्तपसैव महामुने ।
क्षत्रियोऽथाभवद्विप्रः प्रसिद्धं त्रिभवे त्विदम् ॥ ५३॥

English
O great sage, the Kṣatriya king Viśvāmitra,\(^{238}\) the son of Gādhi, became a brahmin through penance. This is well known in the three worlds. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तं ते महाप्राज्ञ तपोमाहात्म्यमुत्तमम् ।
श‍ृण्वध्ययनमाहात्म्यं तपसोऽधिकमुत्तमम् ॥ ५४॥

English
O intelligent one, thus the excellent glory of penance has been mentioned to you. Now listen to the glory of the study of the Vedas which is far more excellent than penance itself. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
