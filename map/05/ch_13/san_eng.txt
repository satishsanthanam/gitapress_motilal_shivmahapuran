### **CHAPTER THIRTEEN - ( The glory of the Purāṇas)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तपस्तपति योऽरण्ये वन्यमूलफलाशनः ।
योऽधीते ऋचमेकां हि फलं स्यात्तत्समं मुने ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  O sage, the fruit is the same whether one performs penance in a forest restricting his diet to roots and fruits there or studies a single Vedic verse. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुतेरध्ययनात्पुण्यं यदाप्नोति द्विजोत्तमः ।
तदध्यापनतश्चापि द्विगुणं फलमश्नुते ॥ २॥

English
By teaching the Veda, an excellent brahmin attains twice the merit that he attains by studying it. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
जगद्यथा निरालोकं जायतेऽशशिभास्करम् ।
विना तथा पुराणं ह्यध्येयमस्मान्मुने सदा ॥ ३॥

English
The universe will go without light if the moon and the sun were not present. The same will happen if the Puranas too, O sage, were not. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तप्यमानं सदाज्ञानान्निरये योऽपि शास्त्रतः ।
सम्बोधयति लोकं तं तस्मात् पूज्यः पुराणगः ॥ ४॥

English
A Purāṇist enlightens the people through Śāstras, the people who are in distress in the hell due to ignorance. Hence he shall be worshipped. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां चैव पात्राणां मध्ये श्रेष्ठः पुराणवित् ।
पतनात् त्रायते यस्मात् तस्मात्पात्रमुदाहृतम् ॥ ५॥

English
Among all the deserving men the knower of Purāṇa is the most excellent. He is considered worthy because he saves people from fall (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मर्त्यबुद्धिर्न कर्तव्या पुराणज्ञे कदाचन ।
पुराणज्ञः सर्ववेत्ता ब्रह्मा विष्णुर्हरो गुरुः ॥ ६॥

English
Never shall a Purāṇist be considered an ordinary man. A preceptor proficient in Purāṇa is omniscient. He is Brahma, Vişnu and Śiva. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
धनं धान्यं हिरण्यं च वासांसि विविधानि च ।
देयं पुराणविज्ञाय परत्रेह च शर्मणे ॥ ७॥

English
For the welfare here and hereafter, wealth food-grain, gold, different clothes etc. shall be given to a Purāṇist as gift. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यो ददाति महाप्रीत्या पुराणज्ञाय सज्जनः ।
पात्राय शुभवस्तूनि स याति परमां गतिम् ॥ ८॥

English
The good man who out of pleasure gives auspicious things to a Purāṇist who is a deserving person, attains the greatest salvation. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
महीं गां वा स्यन्दनांश्च गजानश्वांश्च शोभनान् ।
यः प्रयच्छति पात्राय तस्य पुण्यफलं श‍ृणु ॥ ९॥

English
Listen to the merit that a person derives from lands, cows, chariots, elephants and good horses to a deserving person. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षयान्सर्वकामांश्च परत्रेह च जन्मनि ।
अश्वमेधमखस्यापि स फलं लभते पुमान् ॥ १०॥

English
That man derives the fruit of horse-sacrifice, and realises all his cherished desires without wastage, both here and hereafter. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
महीं ददाति यस्तस्मै कृष्टां फलवतीं शुभाम् ।
स तारयति वै वंश्यान्दश पूर्वान् दशापरान् ॥ ११॥

English
He who gives him a good fertile and well ploughed land facilitates the redemption of the members of his family upto ten generations before him and after him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इह भुक्त्वाखिलान् कामानन्ते दिव्यशरीरवान् ।
विमानेन च दिव्येन शिवलोकं स गच्छति ॥ १२॥

English
After enjoying all the pleasures here, he becomes endowed with a divine body on death and goes to Sivaloka, by a divine aerial chariot. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
न यज्ञैस्तुष्टिमायान्ति देवाः प्रोक्षणकैरपि ।
बलिभिः पुष्पपूजाभिर्यथा पुस्तकवाचनैः ॥ १३॥

English
The gods are not satisfied so much with sacrifices, Prokṣaṇakas (immolations of animals at the sacrifices), oblations and floral worships as with the recitation of books. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भोरायतने यस्तु कारयेद्धर्मपुस्तकम् ।
विष्णोरर्कस्य कस्यापि श‍ृणु तस्यापि तत्फलम् ॥ १४॥

English
Listen to the meritorious benefits of that man who makes arrangement for the Sacred Text book in the temple of Śiva, Viṣṇu, the Sun or any one else. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
राजसूयाश्वमेधानां फलमाप्नोति मानवः ।
सूर्यलोकं च भित्त्वाशु ब्रह्मलोकं स गच्छति ॥ १५॥

English
The man derives the benefit of Rājasūya$^{239}$ and Aśvamedhas. Piercing through the sun's sphere he goes to the Brahmaloka (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थित्वा कल्पशतान्यत्र राजा भवति भूतले ।
भुङ्क्ते निष्कण्टकं भोगान्नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ १६॥

English
After remaining there for hundreds of Kalpas he becomes a king on the earth. He enjoys the pleasures without pinpricks. No hesitation need be entertained in this regard. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वमेधसहस्रस्य यत्फलं समुदाहृतम् ।
तत्फलं समावाप्नोति देवाग्रे यो जपं चरेत् ॥ १७॥

English
He who performs Japas in front of the deity obtains that benefit which is mentioned as the fruit of a thousand horse-sacrifices. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इतिहासपुराणाभ्यां शम्भोरायतने शुभे ।
नान्यत्प्रीतिकरं शम्भोस्तथान्येषां दिवौकसाम् ॥ १८॥

English
Nothing else is delightful to Śiva and the heaven-dwellers more than the recitation of Itihāsas and Purāṇas in the auspicious temples of Śiva. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन कार्यं पुस्तकवाचनम् ।
तथास्य श्रवणं प्रेम्णा सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ॥ १९॥

English
Hence arrangements shall be assiduously made for the reciting of the book. Listening to it with devotion and love yields the fruit of all cherished desires. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराणश्रवणात् शम्भोर्निष्पापो जायते नरः ।
भुक्त्वा भोगान् सुविपुलान् शिवलोकमवाप्नुयात् ॥ २०॥

English
Man becomes free from sins on listening to Śiva-purāṇa. After enjoying immense pleasures he attains the world of Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
राजसूयेन यत्पुण्यमग्निष्टोमशतेन च ।
तत्पुण्यं लभते शम्भोः कथाश्रवणमात्रतः ॥ २१॥

English
Merely by listening to the story of Śiva one derives the merit similar to that resulting from Rājasūya and a hundred Agniṣṭoma rites. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतीर्थावगाहेन गवां कोटिप्रदानतः ।
तत् फलं लभते शम्भोः कथाश्रवणतो मुने ॥ २२॥

English
O sage, by listening to the story of Śiva one derives the benefit and merit derived from ablution in the sacred rivers and from gifting away a crore of cows. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ये श‍ृण्वन्ति कथां शम्भोः सदा भुवनपावनीम् ।
ते मनुष्या न मन्तव्या रुद्रा एव न संशयः ॥ २३॥

English
Those who constantly listen to Śiva's story that sanctifies the worlds are not mere human beings, they are Rudras. There is no doubt in this. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वतां शिवसत्कीर्तिं सतां कीर्तयतां च ताम् ।
पादाम्बुजरजांस्येव तीर्थानि मुनयो विदुः ॥ २४॥

English
Sages consider the dust in the lotus-feet of those who listen to the holy glory of Śiva and those who constantly glorify it, to be as holy as the holy centres. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
गन्तुं निःश्रेयसं स्थानं येऽभिवाञ्छन्ति देहिनः ।
कथां पौराणिकीं शैवीं भक्त्या श‍ृण्वन्तु ते सदा ॥ २५॥

English
Let those embodied beings who desire to attain salvation listen always the Puranic story of Siva with devotion. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
कथां पौराणिकीं श्रोतुं यद्यशक्तः सदा भवेत् ।
नियतात्मा प्रतिदिनं श‍ृणुयाद्वा मुहूर्तकम् ॥ २६॥

English
If any one is unable to listen to the Puranic story let him listen at least for a Muhurta every day with pure mind. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि प्रतिदिनं श्रोतुमशक्तो मानवो भवेत् ।
पुण्यमासादिषु मुने श‍ृणुयाच्छाङ्करीं कथाम् ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, if any man, is unable to listen to the story of Siva every day let him listen to it in the course of holy months, days etc. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवीं कथां हि श‍ृण्वानः पुरुषो हि मुनीश्वर ।
स निस्तरति संसारं दग्ध्वा कर्ममहाटवीम् ॥ २८॥

English
O great sage, he who regularly listens to Siva's story crosses the ocean of worldly existence after burning away the great forest of Karman. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
कथां शैवीं मुहूर्तं वा तदर्धं वा क्षणं च वा ।
ये श‍ृण्वन्ति नरा भक्त्या न तेषां दुर्गतिर्भवेत् ॥ २९॥

English
No mishap befalls those men who listen to Siva's story even for a Muhurta, or half that time or even for a moment with devotion. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्पुण्यं सर्वदानेषु सर्वयज्ञेषु वा मुने ।
शम्भोः पुराणश्रवणात्तत्फलं निश्चलं भवेत् ॥ ३०॥

English
O sage, certainly the fruit derived from listening to Sivapurana is the same as that derived from making all charitable gifts or from performing all sacrifices. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
विशेषतः कलौ व्यास पुराणश्रवणादृते ।
परो धर्मो न पुंसां हि मुक्तिध्यानपरः स्मृतः ॥ ३१॥

English
O Vyasa, especially in the age of Kali there is no greater virtue aimed at salvation and meditation than listening to the Puranas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराणश्रवणं शम्भोर्नामसङ्कीर्तनं तथा ।
कल्पद्रुमफलं रम्यं मनुष्याणां न संशयः ॥ ३२॥

English
There is no doubt that the listening to Siva-Purana and the holy recital of his names yield fruits readily like the Kalpa tree to human beings. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कलौ दुर्मेधसां पुंसां धर्माचारोज्झितात्मनाम् ।
हिताय विदधे शम्भुः पुराणाख्यं सुधारसम् ॥ ३३॥

English
Siva has created the nectarine juice in the form of Purana for the benefit of men of poor intellect in the Kali age who have eschewed virtue and good conduct. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एकोऽजरामरः स्याद्वै पिबन्नेवामृतं पुमान् ।
शम्भोः कथामृतापानात् कुलमेवाजरामरम् ॥ ३४॥

English
Only one person becomes free from old age and death by drinking the nectar Amrta. But by drinking the nectarine story of Siva the entire family becomes free from death and old age. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
या गतिः पुण्यशीलानां यज्विनां च तपस्विनाम् ।
सा गतिः सहसा तात पुराणश्रवणात्खलु ॥ ३५॥

English
O dear, one instantaneously attains by listening to the Puranas that salvation which meritorious ascetics and performers of sacrifices derive. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानावाप्तिर्यदा न स्याद्योगशास्त्राणि यत्नतः ।
अध्येतव्यानि पौराणं शास्त्रं श्रोतव्यमेव च ॥ ३६॥

English
As long as perfect knowledge is not attained, Yogasastras shall be learnt assiduously and the Puranic text be listened to. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पापं सङ्क्षीयते नित्यं धर्मश्चैव विवर्धते ।
पुराणश्रवणाज्ज्ञानी न संसारं प्रपद्यते ॥ ३७॥

English
By listening to the Puranas, sin is reduced and eliminated; virtue is enhanced; and the wise man does not re-enter worldly life. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अतएव पुराणानि श्रोतव्यानि प्रयत्नतः ।
धर्मार्थकामलाभाय मोक्षमार्गाप्तये तथा ॥ ३८॥

English
Hence indeed, the Purāṇas shall be assiduously listened to for the achievement of virtue, wealth, love and salvation. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्ञैर्दानैस्तपोभिस्तु यत्फलं तीर्थसेवया ।
तत्फलं समवाप्नोति पुराणश्रवणान्नरः ॥ ३९॥

English
Man attains, thanks to listening to the Purāṇas, that benefit which is attained by sacrifices, charitable gifts, penances and pilgrimages. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
न भवेयुः पुराणानि धर्ममार्गेक्षणानि तु ।
यद्यत्र यद् व्रती स्थाता कोऽत्र पारत्रिकीं कथाम् ॥ ४०॥

English
If there be no purāṇa showing the path of Dharma, there will be no person observing Vrata or interested in the life hereafter. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
षड्विंशति पुराणानां मध्येऽप्येकं श‍ृणोति यः ।
पठेद्वा भक्तियुक्तस्तु स मुक्तो नात्र संशयः ॥ ४१॥

English
He who listens to at least one among the thirty six Purāṇas$^{240}$ or reads it with devotion is liberated. There is no doubt in this. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यो न दृष्टः सुखदो हि मार्गः
पुराणमार्गो हि सदा वरिष्ठः ।
शास्त्रं विना सर्वमिदं न भाति
सूर्येण हीना इव जीवलोकाः ॥ ४२॥

English
No other path so pleasing is there. The path of the Purāṇas is the most excellent. Nothing in the world shines without the Śāstra as the worlds of living beings without the sun. (42.)
