### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - *(General charitable gifts)***

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
शस्तानि घोरदानानि महादानानि नित्यशः ।
पात्रेभ्यस्तु प्रदेयानि आत्मानं तारयन्ति च ॥ १॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  Ten charitable gifts are called great gifts and are praiseworthy. They shall be given to deserving persons every day. They enable the soul to cross the ocean of worldly existence. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यदानं गोदानं भूमिदानं द्विजोत्तम ।
गृह्णन्तो वै पवित्राणि तारयन्ति स्वमेव तम् ॥ २॥

English
The charitable gifts of gold, cow and land, O excellent brahmin, are very holy. Those who make them redeem themselves. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्णदानं गोदानं पृथिवीदानमेव च ।
एतानि श्रेष्ठदानानि कृत्वा पापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३॥

English
There are gifts of gold, cows and land, by making these excellent gifts one is liberated from sins. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तुलादानानि शस्तानि गावः पृथ्वी सरस्वती ।
द्वे तु तुल्यबले शस्ते ह्यधिका च सरस्वती ॥ ४॥

English
Tulādānas are highly praised like the gifts of cows. Gifts of lands and of speech are of equal potency and deserve praise. But gifts of speech shall be greater. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
नित्य ह्यनुडुहो गावच्छत्रं वस्त्रमुपानहौ ।
देयानि याचमानेभ्यः पानमन्नं तथैव च ॥ ५॥

English
These things shall be given everyday to those who beg for them—viz. oxen, cows, umbrella, cloth, pairs of shoes, drink and food. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्कल्पविहितो योऽर्थो ब्राह्मणेभ्यः प्रदीयते ।
अर्थिभ्योऽपीडितेभ्यश्च मनस्वी तेन जायते ॥ ६॥

English
An object which is given to brahmins, suppliers and distressed people after performing the Samkalpa rites enables the donee to become greatly intelligent. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कनकं च तिला नागाः कन्या दासी गृहं रथः ।
मणयः कपिला गावो महादानानि वै दश ॥ ७॥

English
There are ten great gifts—viz. gold, gingelly seeds, elephants, virgins, servant-maid, house, chariot, jewels, the tawny coloured cow and ordinary cows. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीत्वैतानि सर्वाणि ब्राह्मणो ज्ञानवित्सदा ।
वदान्यांस्तारयेत्सद्यो ह्यात्मानं च न संशयः ॥ ८॥

English
After taking all these a brahmin of great wisdom enables the donee to cross the ocean of worldly existence as well as himself. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्णं ये प्रयच्छन्ति नराः शुद्धेन चेतसा ।
देवतास्तं प्रयच्छन्ति समन्तादिति मे श्रुतम् ॥ ९॥

English
If men make gifts of gold with pious mind, the gods bestow the same on him. Thus I have heard. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निर्हि देवताः सर्वाः सुवर्णं च हुताशनः ।
तस्मात् सुवर्णं दत्त्वा च दत्ताः स्युः सर्वदेवताः ॥ १०॥

English
Gold is fire. Indeed fire represents all gods. Hence by making the gift of gold one makes the gift of all gods. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथ्वीदानं महाश्रेष्ठं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ।
सौवर्णं च विशेषेण यत्कृतं पृथुना पुरा ॥ ११॥

English
Gift of lands is very excellent and it yields the fruits of all desires. The gift of gold is also excellent and it was made by king Prthu formerly. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
दीयमानां प्रपश्यन्ति पृथ्वीं रुक्मसमन्विताम् ।
सर्वपापविनिर्मुक्तास्ते यान्ति परमां गतिम् ॥ १२॥

English
If plots of lands are given along with gold it is praiseworthy. They become liberated from all sins and attain the greatest goal. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथान्यच्च प्रवक्ष्यामि दानं सर्वोत्तमं मुने ।
कान्तारं यन्न पश्यन्ति यमस्य बहुदुःखदम् ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, I shall mention another gift which is the most excellent and whereby people do not see the forest of Yama, the cause of many miseries. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कुर्यात् कान्तारदानं हि विधिना शुद्धमानसः ।
न्यायार्जितेन द्रव्येण वित्तशाठ्यविवर्जितः ॥ १४॥

English
One shall make the charitable gift of forest with due observance of rules and pious mind. The wealth shall be earned by justiciable means. The donee shall be devoid of stinginess associated with affluence. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तिलप्रस्थमयीं कृत्वा धेनुं सर्वगुणान्विताम् ।
धेनुवत्सं सुवर्णं च सुदिव्यं सर्वलक्षणम् ॥ १५॥

पद्ममष्टदलं कृत्वा कुङ्कुमाक्ताक्षतैः शुभैः ।
पूजयेत्तत्र रुद्रादीन्सर्वान्देवान्सुभक्तितः ॥ १६॥

एवं सम्पूज्य तां दद्याद् ब्राह्मणाय स्वशक्तितः ।
सरत्नां सहिरण्यां च सर्वाभरणभूषिताम् ॥ १७॥

ततो नक्तं समश्नीयाद्दीपान् दद्यात्तु विस्तरात् ।
कार्तिक्यामिति कर्तव्यं पूर्णिमायां प्रयत्नतः ॥ १८॥

एवं यः कुरुते सम्यग्विधानेन स्वशक्तितः ।
यममार्गभयं घोरं नरकं च न पश्यति ॥ १९॥

English
With a Prastha measure of gingelly seeds an image of a cow endowed with all qualities shall be made. The calf shall be made of gold. It shall be divine in shape and it shall possess all characteristics. The mystic diagram of the eight-petalled lotus shall be made with auspicious vermillion and raw rice grains. The devotee shall worship Rudra and all other gods there with great devotion. After the worship the cow and the calf shall be given to a brahmin along with gems and gold according to ability and bedecked too in all ornaments. The devotee shall take food only in the night. He shall then make elaborate gifts of lamps. This shall be done on the full moon day in the month of Kārttika *strenuously. He who does thus according to his ability but strictly observing the rules does not see the terrible hell or the path of Yama. (15-19.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा पापान्यशेषाणि सबन्धुः ससुहृज्जनः ।
दिवि सङ्क्रीडते व्यास यावदिन्द्राश्चतुर्दश ॥ २०॥

English
O Vyāsa, even after committing sins, the man enjoys in the heaven along with his kinsmen and friends as long as fourteen Indras rule over it (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विधितो गोश्च दानं वै सर्वोत्तममिह स्मृतम् ।
न तेन सदृशं व्यास परं दानं प्रकीर्तितम् ॥ २१॥

English
The gift of the cow duly performed is the most excellent. O Vyāsa, no other gift is glorified so much as this. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रयच्छते यः कपिलां सवत्सां स्वर्णश‍ृङ्गिकाम् ।
कांस्यपात्रां रौप्यखुरां सर्वलक्षणलक्षिताम् ॥ २२॥

तैस्तैर्गुणैः कामदुघा भूत्वा सा गौरुपैति तम् ।
प्रदातारं नरं व्यास परत्रेह च जन्मनि ॥ २३॥

English
If any one makes the gift of a tawny cow along with its calf adorning the horns with gold, the hoofs with silver and endowed with other characteristics and also gives a bell metal vessel, that cow returns to him in the form of the wish-yielding celestial cow. O Vyāsa, the donee is thus blessed both here and hereafter. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यदिष्टतमं लोके तदस्ति दयितं गृहे ।
तत्तद् गुणवते देयं तदेवाक्षयमिच्छता ॥ २४॥

English
One shall make gifts of such things to a man of good qualities, such thing as he likes most in the world, as are very dear to him in the house and as are wished for by him to be his permanent possessions. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तुलापुरुषदानं हि दानानां दानमुत्तमम् ।
तुलासंरोहणं कार्यं यदीच्छेच्छ्रेय आत्मनः ॥ २५॥

English
Gift of anything equal in weight to the giver is the most excellent of all gifts. If he wishes for his prosperity he shall ascend the weighing balance. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्कृत्वा मुच्यते पापैर्वधबन्धकृतोद्भवैः ।
तुलादानं महत्पुण्यं सर्वपापक्षयङ्करम् ॥ २६॥

English
The weighing balance is highly meritorious. It eliminates all sins. On making the gift of it one gets rid of sins arising from slaughter and captivity. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा पापान्यशेषाणि तुलादानं करोति यः ।
सर्वैस्तु पातकैर्मुक्तः स दिवं यात्यसंशयम् ॥ २७॥

English
Even after committing sins if a man makes the gift of weighing balance he becomes free from all sins and goes to heaven. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पापं कृतं यद्दिवसे निशायां
द्विसन्ध्ययोर्मध्यदिने निशान्ते ।
कालत्रये कायमनोवचोभिः
तुलापुमान्वै तदपाकरोति ॥ २८॥

बालेन वृद्धेन मया हि यूना
विजानता ज्ञानपरेण पापम् ।
तत्सर्वमेवाशु कृतं मदीयं
तुलापुमान् वै हरतु स्मरारिः ॥ २९॥

पात्रे प्रयुक्तं द्रविणं मयाद्य
प्रमाणपूर्णं निहितं तुलायाम् ।
तेनैव सार्धं तु ममावशेषं
कृताकृतं यत्सुकृतं समेतु ॥ ३०॥

English
(The following shall be recited before making the gift). "May the presiding deity of the weighing balance remove the sin committed by me physically, mentally or verbally during the day, night, the twilights, midday, the close of the night or in any of the three times. May the presiding deity of the balance, lord Śiva, enemy of the cupid, remove all the sins done by me, whether as a boy or a youth, or an old man wakefully and consciously. Wealth has been consigned by me to a deserving person. It has been deposited in the balance according to my weight. May merit accrue to me along with this." (28-30.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एवमुच्चार्य तं दद्यात् द्विजेभ्यः सर्वदा हितः ।
नैकस्यापि प्रदातव्यं न निस्तारस्ततो भवेत् ॥ ३१॥

English
The presiding deity shall be made after reciting the above formula It shall not be given to a single person. There is no benefit in it. It shall be distributed among many brahmins. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ददात्येवं तु यो व्यास तुलापुरुषमुत्तमम् ।
हत्वा पापं दिव्यं तिष्ठेद्यावदिन्द्राश्चतुर्दश ॥ ३२॥

English
O Vyasa, he who makes this excellent gift of the presiding deity of the balance destroys his sins and attains heaven He stays there as long as the fourteen Indras rule over it. (32.)
