### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - (Description of the Nether worlds)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
येनैकेन हि दत्तेन सर्वेषां प्राप्यते फलम् ।
दानानां तन्ममाख्याहि मानुषाणां हितार्थतः ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  For the benefit of human beings tell me that which when given as gift makes it possible to derive the benefit of all gifts. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु काले प्रदत्ताद्वै फलं विन्दन्ति मानवाः ।
एकस्मादपि सर्वेषां दानानां तद्वदामि ते ॥ २॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  Listen. I shall mention that gift on giving which, at the proper time, men obtain the benefit of all the gifts (2.)

---

Sanskrit
दानानामुत्तमं दानं ब्रह्माण्डं खलु मानवैः ।
दातव्यं मुक्तिकामैस्तु संसारोत्तारणाय वै ॥ ३॥

English
This is the most excellent of all gifts. The whole universe shall be given as gift by those who desire salvation for crossing the ocean of worldly existence. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माण्डे सकले दत्ते यत्फलं लभते नरः ।
तदेकभावादाप्नोति सप्तलोकाधिपो भवेत् ॥ ४॥

English
A man becomes the lord of the seven worlds, with a fraction of the benefit that he obtains when the whole universe is given as a gift. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यावच्चन्द्रदिवाकरौ नभसि वा यावत् स्थिरा मेदिनी
तावत्सोऽपि नरः स्वबान्धवयुतः स्वर्गौकसामोकसि ।
सर्वेष्वेव मनोनुगेषु ककुभिर्ब्रह्माण्डदः क्रीडते
पश्चाद्याति पदं सुदुर्लभतरं देवैर्मुदे माधवम् ॥ ५॥

English
The man who gifts away the universe sports in the abode of the gods along with his kinsmen as long as the moon and the sun are in the sky or the earth is steady or every one in the abode of the heaven-dwellers is favourable. Afterwards, for rejoicing, he attains the region of Vişnu which is difficult of access even to the gods. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
भगवन् ब्रहि ब्रह्माण्डं यत्प्रमाणं यदात्मकम् ।
यदाधारं यथाभूतं येन मे प्रत्ययो भवेत् ॥ ६॥

English
*Vyāsa said:—*  O holy lord, please explain the universe, its extent, its main soul, its support and its structure so that I can have a full idea. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
मुने श‍ृणु प्रवक्ष्यामि यदुत्सेधं तु विस्तरम् ।
ब्रह्माण्डं तत्तु सङ्क्षेपाच्छ्रुत्वा पापात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ७॥

English
*Sanatkumāra said:—*  O sage, listen. I shall explain how high and how extensive is the universe. On hearing it succinctly one gets rid of sins. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्तत्कारणमव्यक्तं व्यक्तं शिवमनामयम् ।
तस्मात्सञ्जायते ब्रह्मा द्विधाभूताद्धि कालतः ॥ ८॥

English
That which is the unmanifest ultimate cause becomes manifest as Śiva, free from ailment. When that becomes dichotomised, Brahmā is born of that in due course. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माण्डं सृजति ब्रह्मा चतुर्दशभवात्मकम् ।
तद्वच्मि क्रमतस्तात समासाच्छृणु यत्नतः ॥ ९॥

English
Brahmā creates the universe consisting of the fourteen$^{241}$ worlds. O dear, I shall succinctly explain that in proper order. Listen to it assiduously. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पातालानि तु सप्तैव भुवनानि तथोर्ध्वतः ।
उच्छ्रायो द्विगुणस्तस्य जलमध्ये स्थितस्य च ॥ १०॥

English
The nether worlds are seven only. The upper worlds are also seven. The height of the universe is twice as such and it stands in the middle of water. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याधारः स्थितो नागः स च विष्णुः प्रकीर्तितः ।
ब्रह्मणो वचसां हेतोर्बिभर्ति सकलं त्विदम् ॥ ११॥

English
Its support is the serpent Śeṣa. He is glorified as Viṣṇu. At the instance of Brahmā he supports all this. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शेषस्यास्य गुणान् वक्तुं न शक्ता देवदानवाः ।
योऽनन्तः पठ्यते सिद्धैर्देवर्षिगणपूजितः ॥ १२॥

English
Even the gods and the Asuras are incompetent to describe the attributes of Śeṣa who is worshipped by the gods, sages and the Gaṇas and who is called as Ananta by the Siddhas. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरः सहस्रयुक्तः स सर्वा विद्योतयन्दिशः ।
फणामणिसहस्रेण स्वस्तिकामलभूषणः ॥ १३॥

English
He has a thousand hoods. By means of the thousand jewels on his hoods, he illuminates all the quarters. He has the brilliant ornament Svastika. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
मदाघूर्णितनेत्रोऽसौ साग्निः श्वेत इवाचलः ।
स्रग्वी किरीटी ह्याभाति यः सदैवैककुण्डलः ॥ १४॥

English
He has only a single ear-ring. He has a crown and wears garlands. His eyes are rolling due to inebriation. He shines like a white fiery mountain. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
साभ्रगङ्गाप्रवाहेण श्वेतशैलोपशोभितः ।
नीलवासा मदोद्रिक्तः कैलासाद्रिरिवापरः ॥ १५॥

English
He is like a Kailāsa mountain which shines white due to the flow of the Gaṅgā. But he wears a blue cloth and is inebriated. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
लाङ्गलासक्तहस्ताग्रो बिभ्रन्मुसलमुत्तमम् ।
योऽर्च्यते नागकन्याभिः स्वर्णवर्णाभिरादरात् ॥ १६॥

सङ्कर्षणात्मको रुद्रो विषानलशिखोज्ज्वलः ।
कल्पान्ते निष्क्रमन्ते यद्वक्त्रेभ्योऽग्निशिखा मुहुः ।
दग्ध्वा जगत्त्रयं शान्ता भवन्तीत्यनुशुश्रुमः ॥ १७॥

English
He is worshipped by the gold-complexioned Nāgavirgins in this aspect—viz. the tips of his hands are in contact with the ploughshare. He is bearing an excellent threshing rod. He is Rudra in the form of Saṅkarṣaṇa. He is brilliant with the flame of the poisonous fire. At the end of the Kalpa, flames of fire come out of his mouths. After consuming the three worlds they subside. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
आस्ते पातालमूलस्थः सशेषः क्षितिमण्डलम् ।
बिभ्रत्स्वपृष्ठे भूतेशः शेषोऽशेषगुणार्चितः ॥ १८॥

English
Holding the sphere of the earth on his back, Sesa, the lord of Bhutas stands at the root of Patala. He is worshipped for his endless attributes. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य वीर्यप्रभावश्च साकाङ्क्षैस्त्रिदशैरपि ।
न हि वर्णयितुं शक्यः स्वरूपं ज्ञातुमेव वा ॥ १९॥

English
The power of his virility cannot be adequately described even by the ambitious gods nor can his form be known. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
आस्ते कुसुममालेव फणामणिशिखारुणा ।
यस्यैषा सकला पृथ्वी कस्तद्वीर्यं वदिष्यति ॥ २०॥

English
Who will adequately express his prowess? The series of his hoods, pink with the jewels go round the entire earth like a garland of flowers. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा विजृम्भतेऽनन्तो मदाघूर्णितलोचनः ।
तदा चलति भूरेषा साद्रितोयाधिकानना ॥ २१॥

English
With his eyes rolling due to inebriation when Sesa stretches himself, the earth quakes along with all its mountains, oceans and forests. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दशसाहस्रमेकैकं पातालं मुनिसत्तम ।
अतलं वितलं चैव सुतलं च रसातलम् ॥ २२॥

तलं तलातलं चाग्र्यं पातालं सप्तमं मतम् ।
भूमेरधः सप्तलोका इमे ज्ञेया विचक्षणैः ॥ २३॥

English
O excellent sage, each of the nether worlds extends to ten thousand Yojanas. The seven nether worlds are Atala, Vitala, Sutala, Rasatala, Tala, Talatala and the Patala. The seven worlds are beneath the earth. So say the learned people. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्छ्रायो द्विगुणश्चैषां सर्वेषां रत्नभूमयः ।
रत्नवन्तोऽथ प्रासादा भूमयो हेमसम्भवाः ॥ २४॥

English
The height of each of these is twice its extent. The surfaces of all are grounds of gems. The palaces are full of gems and their terraces are made of gold. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषु दानवदैतेया नागानां जातयस्तथा ।
निवसन्ति महानागा राक्षसा दैत्यसम्भवाः ॥ २५॥

English
Dānavas, Daiteyas, Serpents, and Rākṣasas of the Daitya origin reside there. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राह स्वर्गसदोमध्ये पातालानीति नारदः ।
स्वर्लोकादति रम्याणि तेभ्योऽसावागतो दिवि ॥ २६॥

English
Nārada who came to heaven from the nether region announced in the middle of the celestial assembly that the nether worlds are more beautiful than heaven. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नानाभूषणभूषाश्च मणयो यत्र सुप्रभाः ।
आह्लादकारिणः शुभ्राः पातालं केन तत्समम् ॥ २७॥

English
There, in all sorts of ornaments, lustrous jewels are present. They are white and delightful. What is there equal to it? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पाताले कस्य न प्रीतिरितश्चेतश्च शोभिते ।
देत्यदानवकन्याभिर्विमुक्तस्याभिजायते ॥ २८॥

English
The nether region is here and there brightened up by the daughters of Daityas and Dānavas. Even to the liberated soul, the nether region is pleasing. Which liberated soul does not like it? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
दिवार्करश्मयो यत्र न भवन्ति विधोर्निशि ।
न शीतमातपो यत्र मणितेजोऽत्र केवलम् ॥ २९॥

English
There during the daytime there are no sun's rays, nor the moon's rays during the night. There is neither chillness nor bright sunlight. There is only the lustre of the jewel. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्ष्यभोज्यान्नपानानि भुज्यन्ते मुदितैर्भृशम् ।
यत्र न ज्ञायते कालो गतोऽपि मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३०॥

English
O excellent sage, all sorts of foodstuffs and drinks are consumed there by extremely joyous persons. The time that passes by is not known there at all. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पुंस्कोकिलरुतं यत्र पद्मानि कमलाकराः ।
नद्यः सरांसि रम्याणि मनोज्ञान्यम्बराणि च ॥ ३१॥

English
The cooings of the male cuckoo are there. Lotuses are there. There are lotus-ponds. There are rivers and lakes. Each of them is more excellent than the other. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
भूषणान्यतिशुभ्राणि गन्धाढ्यं चानुलेपनम् ।
वीणावेणुमृदङ्गानां स्वना गेयानि च द्विज ॥ ३२॥

English
There the ornaments are very brilliant. The unguents are sweet-smelling. O brahmin, the sound of Vinās, flutes and Mṛdaṅgas can be heard there. There are songs everywhere. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्योरगैश्च भुज्यन्ते पाताले वै सुखानि च ।
तपसा समवाप्नोति दानवैः सिद्धमानवैः ॥ ३३॥

English
There the Daityas and serpents rejoice and enjoy pleasures. It is by performing penances that Dānavas and Siddha beings attain the same. (33.)
