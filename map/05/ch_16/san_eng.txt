### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - (Uplift from the hell)**

---

Sanskrit
५.१६. षोडशोऽध्यायः । नरकोद्धारवर्णनम् ।

सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
तेषां मूर्धोपरिष्टाद्वै नरकांस्तान् श्रुणुष्व च ।
मत्तो मुनिवरश्रेष्ठ पच्यन्ते यत्र पापिनः ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O excellent sage, above the nether regions, are the hells where the sinners are scorched. Listen to their description from me. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
रौरवः शूकरो रोधस्तालो विवसनस्तथा ।
महाज्वालस्तप्तकुम्भो लवणोऽपि विलोहितः ॥ २॥

English
They are—Raurava, Sukara, Radha, Tala, Vivasvat, Mahajvala, Taptakumbha, Lavana, Vilohita. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वैतरणी पूयवहा कृमिशः कृमिभोजनः ।
असिपत्रवनं घोरं लालाभक्षश्च दारुणः ॥ ३॥

English
The river Vaitarani consists of putrid stuff flowing in it, Krmina. Krmibhojana, Asipatravana and Lalabhaksa are the terrible hells: (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा पूयवहः प्रायो वन्हिज्वालो ह्यधःशिराः ।
सन्दंशः कालसूत्रश्च तमश्चावीचिरोधनः ॥ ४॥

English
Puyavaha has usually flames of fire outside and is topsyturvy. Sandamsa, Kalasutra, Tamas, Avicirodhana (4.)

---

Sanskrit
श्वभोजनोऽथ रुष्टश्च महारौरवशाल्मली ।
इत्याद्या बहवस्तत्र नरका दुःखदायकाः ॥ ५॥

English
Svabhojana, Ruṣṭa, Mahāraurava and Sālmali—these and other hells are there; all of these are very grievous. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पच्यन्ते तेषु पुरुषाः पापकर्मरतास्तु ये ।
क्रमाद्वक्ष्ये तु तान् व्यास सावधानतया श‍ृणु ॥ ६॥

English
Men who are indulgent in sins are scorched there. O Vyāsa, I shall mention them in order. Listen attentively. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कूटसाक्ष्यं तु यो वक्ति विना विप्रान् सुरांश्च गाः ।
सदानृतं वदेद्यस्तु स नरो याति रौरवम् ॥ ७॥

English
He who commits perjury except for the brahmins, gods and cows and he who utters a lie always goes to Raurava. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रूणहा स्वर्णहर्ता च गोरोधी विश्वघातकः ।
सुरापो ब्रह्महन्ता च परद्रव्यापहारकः ॥ ८॥

यस्तत्सङ्गी स वै याति मृतो व्यास गुरोर्वधात् ।
तप्तकुम्भे स्वसुर्मातुर्गोश्चैव दुहितुस्तथा ॥ ९॥

साध्व्या विक्रयकृच्चाथ वार्द्धकी केशविक्रयी ।
तप्तलोहेषु पच्यन्ते यश्च भक्तं परित्यजेत् ॥ १०॥

English
The following sinners viz :—the destroyer of the child in the womb, the stealer of gold, of cows, one who commits the breach of trust, the wine-addict, the brahmin slayer, the stealer of other's wealth and he who associates with these—go to the hell Kumbha, O Vyāsa. He who kills his preceptor, sister, mother, daughter or a cow too goes there. He who sells his chaste wife, he who is addicted to usury, he who sells tresses of hair and he who forsakes a devotee—all these are scorched in redhot iron. (8-10.)

---

Sanskrit
अवमन्ता गुरूणां यः पश्चाद्भोक्ता नराधमः ।
देवदूषयिता चैव देवविक्रयिकश्च यः ॥ ११॥

अगम्यगामी यश्चान्ते याति सप्तबलं द्विज ।
चौरो गोघ्नो हि पतितो मर्यादादूषकस्तथा ॥ १२॥

देवद्विजपितृद्वेष्टा रत्नदूषयिता च यः ।
स याति कृमिभक्षं वै कृमीनत्ति दुरिष्टिकृत् ॥ १३॥

English
He who insults preceptors, he who dismisses visitors and then dines, he who commits blasphemy, he who sells idols and he who cohabits with forbidden women—all these, O brahmin, go to Saptabala hell. A thief, a slayer of cows, a fallen man, a defiler of boundaries, the hater of gods, brahmins and Pīṭṭs and the defiler of gems go to Kṛmibhakṣa hell. They eat worms and other displeasing things. (11-13.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृदेवसुरान् यस्तु पर्यश्नाति नराधमः ।
लालाभक्षं स यात्यज्ञो यः शास्त्रकूटकृन्नरः ॥ १४॥

यश्चान्त्यजेन संसेव्यो ह्यसद्ग्राही तु यो द्विजः ।
अयाज्ययाजकश्चैव तथैवाभक्ष्यभक्षकः ॥ १५॥

रुधिरौघे पतन्त्येते सोमविक्रयिणश्च ये ।
मधुहा ग्रामहा याति क्रूरां वैतरणीं नदीम् ॥ १६॥

English
The base man who eats before Pīṭṭs, deities and gods and he who ignorantly or deceitfully misquotes sacred texts—these go to Lālābhakṣa hell. The brahmin who associates with evil men and is surrounded by outcasts, the brahmin who officiates as priest in the sacrifice of undeserving persons, and eats forbidden food, and he who sells Soma juice—these fall into Rudhiraugha hell. He who spoils honey and creates disturbances in the village falls in the ruthless river Vaitaraṇī. (14-16.)

---

Sanskrit
नवयौवनमत्ताश्च मर्यादाभेदिनश्च ये ।
ते कृम्यं यान्त्यशौचाश्च कुलटाजीविनश्च ये ॥ १७॥

English
Those who are arrogant in the freshness of youth, who transgress the bounds of decency, who are unclean and who maintain themselves on the earnings of unchaste women go to Kṛmya hell. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
असिपत्रवनं याति वृक्षच्छेदी वृथैव यः ।
क्षुरभ्रका मृगव्याधा वह्निज्वाले पतन्ति ते ॥ १८॥

English
He who cuts off trees without purpose goes to Asipatravana hell. Those who hunt deer with Kṣuraprakas (arrows with horse-shoe-shaped heads) fall into Vahnijvāla hell. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रष्टाचारो हि यो विप्रः क्षत्रियो वैश्य एव च ।
यात्यन्ते द्विज तत्रैव यः श्वपाकेषु वह्निदः ॥ १९॥

English
O brahmin, the brahmin, the Kṣatriya or the Vaiśya who swerves from the path of good conduct ultimately fall into the hell prescribed for dog-cooking cāṇḍālas. (19.)

---

English
Those who drop sacred rites in the middle and those who are fallen off from their duties in accordance with the stages of their life fall into the hell Sandamsa where the tortures are very terrible. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
व्रतस्य लोपका ये च स्वाश्रमाद्विच्युताश्च ये ।
सन्दंशयातनामध्ये पतन्ति भृशदारुणे ॥ ५.१६.२०॥

वीर्यं स्वप्नेषु स्कन्देयुर्ये नरा ब्रह्मचारिणः ।
पुत्रा नाध्यापिता यैश्च ते पतन्ति श्वभोजने ॥ २१॥

English
The students who are guilty of nocturnal emission of their semen and the fathers who do not properly educate their sons fall into the Svabhojana hell. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
एते चान्ये च नरकाः शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।
येषु दुष्कृतकर्माणः पच्यन्ते यातनागताः ॥ २२॥

English
There are hundreds and thousands of these and other hells where thousands of sinners undergo tortures and are scorched. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव पापान्येतानि तथान्यानि सहस्रशः ।
भुज्यन्ते यानि पुरुषैर्नरकान्तरगोचरैः ॥ २३॥

English
These sins and others are thousands in number similarly, which the beings in the hells experience and try to wipe off. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्णाश्रमविरुद्धं च कर्म कुर्वन्ति ये नराः ।
कर्मणा मनसा वाचा निरये तु पतन्ति ते ॥ २४॥

English
Those who perpetrate misdeeds contrary to the injunctions of caste and stages of life whether physically, mentally or verbally fall into hell. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अधःशिरोभिर्दृश्यन्ते नारका दिवि दैवतैः ।
देवानधोमुखान् सर्वानधः पश्यन्ति नारकाः ॥ २५॥

English
The beings in the hells are seen standing upside down by the gods in the heaven. These beings see the gods also similarly with heads down, beneath them. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थावराः कृमयोऽब्जाश्च पक्षिणः पशवो नराः ।
धार्मिकास्त्रिदशास्तद्वन्मोक्षिणश्च यथाक्रमम् ॥ २६॥

यावन्तो जन्तवः स्वर्गे तावन्तो नरकौकसः ।
पापकृद्याति नरकं प्रायश्चित्तपराङ्मुखः ॥ २७॥

English
Immovable beings—plants etc. and movable beings worms, insects, birds, beasts, righteous men, gods and liberated beings, all these are equal in number in heaven as also in hell. A sinner who is averse to the performance of expiatory rites goes to hell. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरूणि गुरुभिश्चैव लघूनि लघुभिस्तथा ।
प्रायश्चित्तानि कालेय मनुः स्वायम्भुवोऽब्रवीत् ॥ २८॥

English
Svāyambhuva Manu has ordained expiatory rites for great sins and small expiatory rites for small sins in the Kali age (28.)

---

Sanskrit
यानि तेषामशेषाणां कर्माण्युक्तानि तेषु वै ।
प्रायश्चित्तमशेषेण हरानुस्मरणं परम् ॥ २९॥

English
Of the many rites mentioned by way of expiation the remembrance of Siva is the greatest. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रायश्चित्तं तु तस्यैकं यस्य पुंसः प्रजायते ।
कृते पापेऽनुतापोऽपि शिवसंस्मरणं परम् ॥ ३०॥

English
If a person commits a sin but repents after committing it, it is also an expiation. There also the remembrance of Siva is the greatest expiation. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
माहेश्वरमवाप्नोति मध्याह्नादिषु संस्मरन् ।
प्रातर्निशि च सन्ध्यायां क्षीणपापो भवेन्नरः ॥ ३१॥

English
A man remembering lord Siva in the midday or other occasions attains lord Siva. By remembering the lord in the morning, night or dusk, he gets his sins eliminated. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्तिं प्रयाति स्वर्गं वा समस्तक्लेशसङ्क्षयम् ।
शिवस्य स्मरणादेव तस्य शम्भोरुमापतेः ॥ ३२॥

English
It is only by remembering Siva, the lord of Uma that a man attains salvation, the annihilation of all pains or heaven. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पापन्तरायो विप्रेन्द्र जपहोमार्चनादि च ।
भवत्येव न कुत्रापि त्रैलोक्ये मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३३॥

English
O leading brahmin, Japas, Homas, worship etc. are obstacles in the path of sins. O excellent sage, they do not occur anywhere in the three worlds. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरे मतिर्यस्य जपहोमार्चनादिषु ।
यत्पुण्यं तत्कृतं तेन देवेन्द्रत्वादिकं फलम् ॥ ३४॥

English
The attainment of the status of Indra, lord of the gods, is the fruit of the merit attained in Japa, Homa, worship etc. performed by the man whose mind is in lord Siva. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
पुमान्न नरकं याति यः स्मरेद् भक्तितो मुने ।
अहर्निशं शिवं तस्मात् स क्षीणाशेषपातकः ॥ ३५॥

English
O sage, he who devoutly remembers Siva day and night never goes to hell since his sins are eliminated without any vestige. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
नरकस्वर्गसंज्ञे वै पापपुण्ये द्विजोत्तम ।
ययोस्त्वेकं तु दुःखायान्यत्सुखायोद्भवाय च ॥ ३६॥

English
O excellent brahmin, sin and merit indicate hell and heaven. One is conducive to misery and the other to pleasure and rebirth. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तदेव प्रीतये भूत्वा पुनर्दुःखाय जायते ।
तत्स्माद्दुःखात्मकं नास्ति न च किञ्चित्सुखात्मकम् ॥ ३७॥

English
That at the outset is conducive to happiness but later brings in misery. Hence ultimately everything is of the nature of misery. There is nothing in fact of an invariably pleasurable nature. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसः परिणामोऽयं सुखदुःखोपलक्षणः ।
ज्ञानमेव परं ब्रह्म ज्ञानं तत्त्वाय कल्पते ॥ ३८॥

English
Happiness and misery constitute only a temporary transformation of the mind. But knowledge is the greatest Brahman. Knowledge is conducive to the understanding of reality. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानात्मकमिदं विश्वं सकलं सचराचरम् ।
परविज्ञानतः किञ्चित् विद्यते न परं मुने ॥ ३९॥

English
The entire universe consisting of the mobile and immobile beings has knowledge as its soul. O sage, there is nothing greater than the perfect knowledge of the greatest being. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमेतन्मयाख्यातं सर्वं नरकमण्डलम् ।
अत ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि साम्प्रतं मण्डलं भुवः ॥ ४०॥

English
Thus the entire sphere of the hell has been explained by me. Hereafter I shall explain the sphere of the earth. (40.)

---

--- Colophon ---

Sanskrit
इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे पञ्चम्यामुमासंहितायां ब्रह्माण्डवर्णने नरकोद्धारवर्णनं नाम षोडशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.१६॥
