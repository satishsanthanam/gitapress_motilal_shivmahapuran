### **CHAPTER TWENTY - (The statement of a special Mantra)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ तत्प्राप्तिं वद सत्तम ।
यद् गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते शिवभक्तियुता नराः ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, O the most excellent of the good, explain the acquisition of that by attaining which men endowed with devotion to Śiva do not return again. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
पराशरसुत व्यास श‍ृणु प्रीत्या शुभां गतिम् ।
व्रतं हि शुद्धभक्तानां तथा शुद्धं तपस्विनाम् ॥ २॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O Vyasa, son of Parasara, listen with pleasure to the auspicious salvation and the holy rite of pious devotees and the sacred rite of ascetics. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ये शिवं शुद्धकर्माणः सुशुद्धतपसान्विताः ।
समर्चयन्ति तं नित्यं वन्द्यास्ते सर्वथान्वहम् ॥ ३॥

English
Those who perform holy rites, who are endowed with very pious penance and who always worship Siva should be honoured in every way always. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
नातप्ततपसो यान्ति शिवलोकमनामयम् ।
शिवानुग्रहसद्धेतुस्तप एव महामुने ॥ ४॥

English
Those who have not performed austerities do not go to Siva's region which is free from ailments. O great sage, penance alone leads to Siva's blessings. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा दिवि मोदन्ते प्रत्यक्षं देवतागणाः ।
ऋषयो मुनयश्चैव सत्यं जानीह मद्वचः ॥ ५॥

English
The gods and Ganas rejoice in heaven due to penance. So also the sages and the ascetics. Know my statement to be true. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदुर्द्धरं दुराराध्यं सुदूरं दुरतिक्रमम् ।
तत्सर्वं तपसा साध्यं तपो हि दुरतिक्रमम् ॥ ६॥

English
Every thing can be achieved through penance, whether it is difficult to be sustained, or propitiated or is burdensome or difficult to be transgressed. Penance cannot be transgressed. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सुस्थितस्तपसि ब्रह्मा नित्यं विष्णुर्हरस्तथा ।
देवा देव्योऽखिलाः प्राप्तास्तपसा दुर्लभं फलम् ॥ ७॥

English
Brahma stays steady in his penance. So also Visnu and Siva. All the gods and the goddesses have attained rare benefits through penance. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
येन येन हि भावेन स्थित्वा यत्क्रियते तपः ।
ततः सम्प्राप्यतेऽसौ तैरिह लोके न संशयः ॥ ८॥

English
There is no doubt that in this world the particular goal is realised through penance by those who aspire for it. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सात्त्विकं राजसं चैव तामसं त्रिविधं स्मृतम् ।
विज्ञेयं हि तपो व्यास सर्वसाधनसाधनम् ॥ ९॥

English
O Vyasa, penance is the means for achieving everything. It is of three kinds viz. Sattvika, Rajasa and Tamasa. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सात्त्विकं दैवतानां हि यतीनामूर्ध्वरेतसाम् ।
राजसं दानवानां हि मनुष्याणां तथैव च ।
तामसं राक्षसानां हि नराणां क्रूरकर्मणाम् ॥ १०॥

English
The Sattvika penance is performed by the gods and the ascetics of sublimated prowess, ; the Rajasa penance is by the Dānavas and human beings. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिविधं तत्फलं प्रोक्तं मुनिभिस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः ।
जपो ध्यानं तु देवानामर्चनं भक्तितः शुभम् ॥ ११॥

सात्त्विकं तद्धि निर्दिष्टमशेषफलसाधकम् ।
इह लोके परे चैव मनोऽभिप्रेतसाधनम् ॥ १२॥

English
Their result too is threefold, achievable by the sages who have truthful vision. The Sāttvika penance consists of Japa, meditation and auspicious worship of the deities with devotion. It is the means of achieving all benefits and all desires here and hereafter. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
कामनाफलमुद्दिश्य राजसं तप उच्यते ।
निजदेहं सुसम्पीड्य देहशोषकदुःसहैः ॥ १३॥

English
The Rājasa penance is performed with a specific desire as the fruit. Here the body is subjected to tortures and unbearable whithering and macerating processes. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्तामसमुद्दिष्टं मनोऽभिप्रेतसाधनम् ॥ १४॥

English
That is Tāmasa penance which has a passionate end in view. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तमं सात्त्विकं विद्याद्धर्मबुद्धिश्च निश्चला ।
स्नानं पूजा जपो होमः शुद्धशौचमहिंसनम् ॥ १५॥

व्रतोपवासचर्या च मौनमिन्द्रियनिग्रहः ।
धीर्विद्या सत्यमक्रोधो दानं क्षान्तिर्दमो दया ॥ १६॥

वापीकूपतडागादेः प्रासादस्य च कल्पना ।
कृच्छ्रं चान्द्रायणं यज्ञः सुतीर्थान्याश्रमाः पुनः ॥ १७॥

धर्मस्थानानि चैतानि सुखदानि मनीषिणाम् ।
सुधर्मः परमो व्यास शिवभक्तेश्च कारणम् ॥ १८॥

सङ्क्रान्तिविषुवद्योगो नादमुक्ते नियुज्यताम् ।
ध्यानं त्रिकालिकं ज्योतिरुन्मनीभावधारणा ॥ १९॥

रेचकः पूरकः कुम्भः प्राणायामस्त्रिधा स्मृतः ।
नाडीसञ्चारविज्ञानं प्रत्याहारनिरोधनम् ॥ २०॥

तुरीयं तदधो बुद्धिरणिमाद्यष्टसंयुतम् ।
पूर्वोत्तमं समुद्दिष्टं परज्ञानप्रसाधनम् ॥ २१॥

English
The Sattvika penance is the most excellent. It makes righteous intellect firm and steady. It has following characteristics—performance of ceremonial ablution, worship, Japa, sacrifice, scrupulous purity and cleanliness, non-violence, sacred rites, observance of fasts, silence, control of the sense-organs, pure intelligence, learning, truthfulness, refraining from anger, charitable gifts, forgiveness, suppression of the sense-organs, mercifulness, digging of wells, tanks and lakes, building of mansions for public cause observing Krcchra, Candrayaṇa and other rites, Yajnas, pilgrimages, hermitages, holy places and other acts pleasing to sensible persons. O Vyasa, good virtue is the cause of Siva's devotion. Celebration of the day of sun's transit, the conjunction of equinoxes the Nadamukta, Dhyana or meditation shall be in all the three occasions. It is the retention of the mind in the brilliance. Pranayama is three-fold viz. Recaka, Puraika and Kumbhaka. This along with the knowledge of the course of the veins, withdrawal or obstruction of senses are the means of perfect knowledge and the achievement of the eight siddhis, Animā, etc. (15-21.)

---

Sanskrit
काष्ठावस्था मृतावस्था हरिता वेति कीर्तिताः ।
नानोपलब्धयो ह्येताः सर्वपापप्रणाशनाः ॥ २२॥

English
In the state of trance there are various stages such as the stage of the log of wood, the stage of the dead and the stage of Harita. All these are destructive of all sins. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
नारी शय्या तथा पानं वस्त्रधूपविलेपनम् ।
ताम्बूलभक्षणं पञ्च राजैश्वर्यविभूतयः ॥ २३॥

English
These five are the prosperities of royal glory-women, bed, drink, good garments unguents and incense, and the chewing of betel leaves. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
हेमभारस्तथा ताम्रं गृहाश्च रत्नधेनवः ।
पाण्डित्यं वेदशास्त्राणां गीतनृत्यविभूषणम् ॥ २४॥

शङ्खवीणामृदङ्गाश्च गजेन्द्रः छ्त्रचामरे ।
भोगरूपाणि चैतानि एभिः सक्तोऽनुरज्यते ॥ २५॥

English
The forms of various pleasures are—blocks of gold, copper, houses, gems, cows, scholarship in the Vedas and Sāstras, ornaments, ability to sing and dance, conches, Vinās, Mrdaṅgas, lordly elephants, umbrellas and chowries. Man yearning for these takes delight in these. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
आदर्शवन्मुने स्नेहैस्तिलवत्स निपीड्यते ।
अरं गच्छेति चाप्येनं कुरुते ज्ञानमोहितः ॥ २६॥

English
O sage, but he does not know that these are only reflections in a mirror, not real substances. Just as the gingelly seeds are crushed to yield oil he gets himself crushed to yield things of pleasure. He madly rushes after them deluded by false knowledge. Some one seems to tell him, "Go on quickly." (26.)

---

Sanskrit
जानन्नपीह संसारे भ्रमते घटियन्त्रवत् ।
सर्वयोनिषु दुःखार्तः स्थावरेषु चरेषु च ॥ २७॥

English
In spite of realising this he moves up and down in the world like the water-raising wheel. He takes various births in the mobile and immobile forms of life. He is distressed throughout. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं योनिषु सर्वासु प्रतिक्रम्य भ्रमेण त ।
कालान्तरवशाद्याति मानुष्यमतिदुर्लभम् ॥ २८॥

English
After the transmigration through various forms of life ultimately he attains the rare human birth. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
व्युत्क्रमेणापि मानुष्यं प्राप्यते पुण्यगौरवात् ।
विचित्रा गतयः प्रोक्ताः कर्मणां गुरुलाघवात् ॥ २९॥

English
Human birth is attained even out of turn if the merit is weighty enough. As a result of the weightiness or otherwise of the actions, the goals achieved are of diverse nature. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
मानुष्यं च समासाद्य स्वर्गमोक्षप्रसाधनम् ।
नाचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयः स मृतः शोचते चिरम् ॥ ३०॥

English
If anyone does not strive for salvation even after obtaining human birth which is capable of achieving heaven and salvation, surely he bemoans his lot for a long time, when dead. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
देवासुराणां सर्वेषां मानुष्यं चातिदुर्लभम् ।
तत्सम्प्राप्य तथा कुर्यान्न गच्छेन्नरकं यथा ॥ ३१॥

English
Even to the god and the Asuras the birth as human being is very rare to be achieved. After attaining the same he should do such things as would prevent his fall in the hell. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वर्गापवर्गलाभाय यदि नास्ति समुद्यमः ।
दुर्लभं प्राप्य मानुष्यं वृथा तज्जन्म कीर्तितम् ॥ ३२॥

English
If there is no endeavour for the acquisition of heaven and salvation even after attaining the rare human form that birth is to be dubbed as useless. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्य मूलं मानुष्यं चतुर्वर्गस्य कीर्तितम् ।
सम्प्राप्य धर्मतो व्यास तद्यत्नादनुपालयेत् ॥ ३३॥

English
Human birth is glorified as the root-cause for the four aims of life. O Vyasa, after getting it, one shall assiduously and virtuously maintain it. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्ममूलं हि मानुष्यं लब्ध्वा सर्वार्थसाधकम् ।
यदि लाभाय यत्नः स्यान्मूलं रक्षेत्स्वयं ततः ॥ ३४॥

English
Human birth has virtue as its root. It shall achieve all objects. Even if there is no endeavour to achieve any special aim after getting human birth at least one should preserve the root. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
मानुष्येऽपि च विप्रत्वं यः प्राप्य खलु दुर्लभम् ।
नाचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयः कोऽन्यस्तस्मादचेतनः ॥ ३५॥

English
Who can be a more senseless man than one who does not strive for the welfare of the soul after attaining the rare birth as a brahmin in the course of human birth? (35.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वीपानामेव सर्वेषां कर्मभूरियमुच्यते ।
इतः स्वर्गश्च मोक्षश्च प्राप्यते समुपार्जितः ॥ ३६॥

English
Of all the continents, this continent is called the land of sacred rites. It is from here that heaven and salvation are attained. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
देशेऽस्मिन् भारते वर्षे प्राप्य मानुष्यमध्रुवम् ।
न कुर्यादात्मनः श्रेयस्तेनात्मा खलु वञ्चितः ॥ ३७॥

English
Indeed the soul is deceived by that man who does not strive for welfare despite attaining human form in this land of Bhārata Varṣa. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मभूमिरियं विप्र फलभूमिरसौ स्मृता ।
इह यत्क्रियते कर्म स्वर्गे तदनुभुज्यते ॥ ३८॥

English
O brahmin, this is the land of sacred rites and that is the land of enjoyment. The fruit of the sacred rite performed is enjoyed in heaven. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यावत्स्वास्थ्यं शरीरस्य तावद्धर्मं समाचरेत् ।
अस्वस्थश्चोदितोऽप्यन्यैर्न किञ्चित्कर्तुमुत्सहेत् ॥ ३९॥

English
One should continue to perform virtuous rites as long as the health and normalcy of the body is maintained. A sick man is not eager to perform anything even when urged by others. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अध्रुवेण शरीरेण ध्रुवं यो न प्रसाधयेत् ।
ध्रुवं तस्य परिभ्रष्टमध्रुवं नष्टमेव च ॥ ४०॥

English
If anyone does not try to achieve the permanent through the non-permanent body, the permanent is lost for him and the non-permanent has already perished.²⁸⁵ (40.)

---

Sanskrit
आयुषः खण्डखण्डानि निपतन्ति तदग्रतः ।
अहोरात्रोपदेशेन किमर्थं नावबुध्यते ॥ ४१॥

English
Under the pretext of passing days and nights pieces of life fall off. Why is it not realised? (41.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा न ज्ञायते मृत्युः कदा कस्य भविष्यति ।
आकस्मिके हि मरणे धृतिं विन्दति कस्तथा ॥ ४२॥

English
When it is not known who will die and when, when the death is so sudden and unexpected who can have self-confidence? (42.)

---

Sanskrit
परित्यज्य यदा सर्वमेकाकी यास्यति ध्रुवम् ।
न ददाति कदा कस्मात्पाथेयार्थमिदं धनम् ॥ ४३॥

English
It is certain that one will ultimately go away leaving off everything. Why then does he not give off in charity, all the wealth, so that it can be the packet of fooding on his way to Yama's abode? (43.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीतदानपाथेयः सुखं याति यमालयम् ।
अन्यथा क्लिश्यते जन्तुः पाथेयरहिते पथि ॥ ४४॥

English
A man who has taken the packet of fooding with him by virtue of giving charitable gifts, goes to Yama's abode without difficulty. Otherwise the creature suffers on the way without the packet of food. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
येषां कालेय पुण्यानि परिपूर्णानि सर्वतः ।
गच्छतां स्वर्गदेशं हि तेषां लाभः पदे पदे ॥ ४५॥

English
O Kāleya, they gain at every step whose merits are full and perfect and who go to heaven. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ज्ञात्वा नरः पुण्यं कुर्यात्पापं विवर्जयेत् ।
पुण्येन याति देवत्वमपुण्यो नरकं व्रजेत् ॥ ४६॥

English
Realising this, man should perform meritorious rites and avoid sinful activities. Thanks to the merit one attains godhood. A person devoid of merit goes to hell. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ये मनागपि देवेशं प्रपन्नाः शरणं शिवम् ।
तेऽपि घोरं न पश्यन्ति यमं न नरकं तथा ॥ ४७॥

English
They who take refuge in Śiva, lord of the gods, even slightly, do not see the terrible Yama nor the terrible hell. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
किन्तु पापैर्महामोहैः किञ्चित्काले शिवाज्ञया ।
वसन्ति तत्र मानुष्यास्ततो यान्ति शिवास्पदम् ॥ ४८॥

English
But at the behest of Śiva, due to sins and great delusions, those men stay there for sometime and then go to the region of Śiva. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
ये पुनः सर्वभावेन प्रतिपन्ना महेश्वरम् ।
न ते लिम्पन्ति पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा ॥ ४९॥

English
But those who have taken refuge in lord Siva wholly are not smeared with sin like the leaf of the lotus with water. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
उक्तं शिवेति यैर्नाम तथा हर हरेति च ।
न तेषां नरकाद्भीतिर्यमाद्धि मुनिसत्तम ॥ ५०॥

English
O excellent sage, they who repeat the names of Siva need not be afraid of hell or Yama. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
परलोकस्य पाथेयं मोक्षोपायमनामयम् ।
पुण्यसङ्घैकनिलयं शिव इत्यक्षरद्वयम् ॥ ५१॥

English
The two syllables "Siva" constitute the packet of fooding for the journey to the other world; it is the means of salvation, free from worries and is the abode of all merits. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनामैव संसारमहारोगैकशामकम् ।
नान्यत्संसाररोगस्य शामकं दृश्यते मया ॥ ५२॥

English
It is only the name of Siva that subdues and quells the great ailment of worldly existence. Nothing else does the same, as I see. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्महत्यासहस्राणि पुरा कृत्वा तु पुल्कसः ।
शिवेति नाम विमलं श्रुत्वा मोक्षं गतः पुरा ॥ ५३॥

English
Formerly Pulkasa committed thousands of murders of Brahmins but on hearing the sacred name Siva, he attained salvation. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्विवर्धयेद्भक्तिमीश्वरे सततं बुधः ।
शिवभक्त्या महाप्राज्ञ भुक्तिं मुक्तिं च विन्दति ॥ ५४॥

English
Hence a sensible man shall enhance his devotion to Siva incessantly. O intelligent one, one attains worldly pleasures and salvation due to the devotion to Siva. (54.)

Sanskrit
इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे पञ्चम्यामुमासंहितायां मनुविशेषकथनं नाम विंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२०॥

॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीगौरीशङ्कराभ्यां नमः ॥ 
॥ श्रीशिवमहापुराणम् ॥
