### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - (The fruits of righteous war)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
ब्राह्मणत्वं हि दुष्प्राप्यं निसर्गाद् ब्राह्मणो भवेत् ।
ईश्वरस्य मुखात्क्षत्रं बाहुभ्यामूरुतो विशः ॥ १॥

पद्भ्यां शूद्रः समुत्पन्न इति तस्य मुखात् श्रुतिः ।
किमु स्थितिमधःस्थानादाप्नुवन्ति ह्यतो वद ॥ २॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  Brahminhood is difficult to be achieved. Brahmin is born of the face of Śiva.; Kṣatra, of the arms; Vaiśya of the thighs and Śūdra of the feet of the lord—so says the Veda.²⁸⁶ But do people attain salvation from the lower position? Please tell me. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
दुष्कृतेन तु कालेय स्थानाद् भ्रश्यन्ति मानवाः ।
श्रेष्ठं स्थानं समासाद्य तस्माद्रक्षेत पण्डितः ॥ ३॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O Kāleya, men fall off their status due to evil deeds. Hence, after attaining the high status, the learned man shall preserve it. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तु विप्रत्वमुत्सृज्य क्षत्रयोन्यां प्रसूयते ।
ब्राह्मण्यात्स परिभ्रष्टः क्षत्रियत्वं निषेवते ॥ ४॥

English
He who forsakes brahminhood and is born as a Kṣatriya falls off the status of a brahmin and assumes the status of a Kṣatriya. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अधर्मसेवनान्मूढस्तथैव परिवर्तते ।
जन्मान्तरसहस्राणि तमस्याविशते यतः ॥ ५॥

English
Due to his resorting to evil, the foolish fellow goes on changing thus. He takes thousands of births and plunges himself into gloom. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्प्राप्य परं स्थानं प्रमाद्येन्न तु नाशयेत् ।
स्वस्थानं सर्वदा रक्षेत्प्राप्यापि विपदो नरः ॥ ६॥

English
Hence after attaining the highest status one should not err or destroy it. Even in the face of adversity one shall always preserve his status. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणत्वं शुभं प्राप्य ब्राह्मण्यं योऽवमन्यते ।
भोज्याभोज्यं न जानाति स पुमान् क्षत्रियो भवेत् ॥ ७॥

English
After attaining the auspicious brahminhood if a person disrespects brahminhood and does not differentiate between what could be eaten and what could not be eaten he becomes a Kṣatriya. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मणा येन मेधावी शूद्रो वैश्यो हि जायते ।
तत्ते वक्ष्यामि निखिलं येन वर्णोत्तमो भवेत् ॥ ८॥

English
I shall explain to you entirely that which gives a higher caste whereby an intelligent Sūdra becomes a Vaiśya. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शूद्रकर्म यथोद्दिष्टं शूद्रो भूत्वा समाचरेत् ।
यथावत्परिचर्यां तु त्रिषु वर्णेषु नित्यदा ॥ ९॥

English
Being born a Sūdra, one shall take up the duties of Sūdra as ordained, viz, the due service to the people of the other three castes. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कुरुते कामयानस्तु शूद्रोऽपि वैश्यतां व्रजेत् ।
यो योजयेद्धनैर्वैश्यो जुह्वानश्च यथाविधि ॥ १०॥

अग्निहोत्रमुपादाय शेषान्नकृतभोजनः ।
स वैश्यः क्षत्रियकुले जायते नात्र संशयः ॥ ११॥

क्षत्रियो जायते यज्ञैः संस्कृतैरात्तदक्षिणैः ।
अधीते स्वर्गमन्विच्छंस्त्रेताग्निशरणं सदा ॥ १२॥

आर्द्रहस्तपदो नित्यं क्षितिं धर्मेण पालयेत् ।
ऋतुकालाभिगामी च स्वभार्यां धर्मतत्परः ॥ १३॥

सर्वातिथ्यं त्रिवर्गस्य भूतेभ्यो दीयतामिति ।
गोब्राह्मणात्मनोऽर्थं हि सङ्ग्रामाभिहतो भवेत् ॥ १४॥

तेनाग्निमन्त्रपूतात्मा क्षत्रियो ब्राह्मणो भवेत् ।
विधितो ब्राह्मणो भूत्वा याजकस्तु प्रजायते ॥ १५॥

English
By doing this regularly and aspiring for Vaiśya-hood, even a Śūdra attains it. A Vaiśya who uses his wealth for sacred rites, who performs sacrifices duly, who performs the Agnihotra and partakes of the remnants of food therefrom, is reborn in a Kṣatriya family. There is no doubt in this. He is born as a Kṣatriya by means of consecrated sacrifices with proper gifts. A Kṣatriya shall do like this. He shall study the Vedas desirous of heaven. He shall maintain the three sacrificial fires. He shall wash his hands and feet before entering shrines. He shall protect the earth virtuously. He shall cohabit with his wife during the prescribed period after the monthly menses. He shall remain devout and pious. He shall be hospitable to all. He shall fight and get killed in battle for acquiring facilities to achieve the three aims of life (virtue, wealth and love) to all living beings or for the sake of cows and brahmins. Thus when his soul is purified by fires and mantras he becomes a brahmin. After becoming a brahmin, if he follows the rules he becomes a priest in a sacrifice. (10-15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वकर्मनिरतो नित्यं सत्यवादी जितेन्द्रियः ।
प्राप्यते विपुलः स्वर्गो देवानामपि वल्लभः ॥ १६॥

English
Strictly observing his rites, speaking truth and controlling the sense-organs he derives vast pleasures of heaven. He becomes a favourite of the gods. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणत्वं हि दुष्प्राप्यं कृच्छ्रेण साध्यते नरैः ।
ब्राह्मण्यात्सकलं प्राप्य मोक्षं चापि मुनीश्वर ॥ १७॥

तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन ब्राह्मणो धर्मतत्परः ।
साधनं सर्ववर्गस्य रक्षेद् ब्राह्मण्यमुत्तमम् ॥ १८॥

English
O great sage, brahminhood is very difficult to achieve. It is attained by men who undergo hardships. Salvation is achieved after attaining brahminhood entirely. Hence a brahmin shall be virtuous. With great effort he shall preserve his excellent brahminhood which is a good means of achieving all the aims of life. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
सङ्ग्रामस्येह माहात्म्यं त्वयोक्तं मुनिसत्तम ।
एतदिच्छाम्यहं श्रोतुं ब्रूहि त्वं वदतां वर ॥ १९॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O excellent sage, you have mentioned about the glory. O foremost among the eloquent, I wish to hear this. Please mention. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
अग्निष्टोमादिभिर्यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा विपुलदक्षिणैः ।
न तत्फलमवाप्नोति सङ्ग्रामे यदवाप्नुयात् ॥ २०॥

इति तत्त्वविदः प्राहुर्यज्ञकर्मविदः सदा ।
तस्मात्तत्ते प्रवक्ष्यामि यत्फलं शस्त्रजीविनाम् ॥ २१॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  Those who know the principles, who understand sacrifices and sacred rites say thus—“By performing the Agniṣṭoma and other sacrifices with profuse gifts one does not attain that benefit which is attained through battle.” Hence I shall explain to you the fruit attained by those who maintain themselves by means of weapons. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मलाभोऽर्थलाभश्च यशोलाभस्तथैव च ।
यः शूरो वाञ्छते युद्धं विमृदन् परवाहिनीम् ॥ २२॥

तस्य धर्मार्थकामाश्च यज्ञश्चैव सदक्षिणः ।
परं ह्यभिमुखं दत्त्वा तद्यानं योऽधिरोहति ॥ २३॥

विष्णुलोके स जायेत यश्च युद्धेऽपराजितः ।
अश्वमेधानवाप्नोति चतुरो न मृतः स चेत् ॥ २४॥

English
The hero who offers battle subduing the army of the enemy attains virtue, wealth and fame. He achieves the fruit of a sacrifice with due gifts and the three aims of life, virtue, wealth and love. He who faces the enemy, kills him and gets on to the enemy’s chariot goes to Viṣṇuloka. He who is not defeated in war, nor killed in it attains the fruit of four horse-sacrifices. (22-24.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तु शस्त्रमनुत्सृज्य म्रियते वाहिनीमुखे ।
सम्मुखो वर्तते शूरः स स्वर्गान्न निवर्तते ॥ २५॥

English
He who dies in the war-front without surrendering his weapons attains heaven from which there is no return. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा वा राजपुत्रो वा सेनापतिरथापि वा ।
हतः क्षात्रेण यः शूरस्तस्य लोकोऽक्षयो भवेत् ॥ २६॥

English
The king, or prince or the general who heroically fights and is killed by Kṣatriya attains everlasting regions. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यावन्ति तस्य रोमाणि भिद्यन्तेऽस्त्रैर्महाहवे ।
तावतो लभते लोकान् सर्वकामदुघाऽक्षयान् ॥ २७॥

English
He attains as many worlds as the number of his hairs split by arrows in the battle. All of these will be wish-yielding and eternal. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरासनं वीरशय्या वीरस्थानस्थितिः स्थिरा ।
सर्वदा भवति व्यास इह लोके परत्र च ॥ २८॥

English
O Vyāsa, both here and hereafter he will have heroic seat, heroic bed, heroic status and heroic stability always. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
गवार्थे ब्राह्मणार्थे च स्थानस्वाम्यर्थमेव च ।
ये मृतास्ते सुखं यान्ति यथा सुकृतिनस्तथा ॥ २९॥

English
Those who die for the protection of cows, brahmins, status and the master go happily to the other world like virtuous persons.²⁸⁷ (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यः कश्चिद् ब्राह्मणं हत्वा पश्चात्प्राणान् परित्यजेत् ।
तत्रासौ स्वपतेर्युद्धे स स्वर्गान्न निवर्तते ॥ ३०॥

English
Even a slayer of a brahmin fighting for his master in a battle and dying there attains heaven which becomes his permanent abode. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रव्यादैर्दन्तिभिश्चैव हतस्य गतिरुत्तमा ।
द्विजगोस्वामिनामर्थे भवेद्विपुलदाक्षया ॥ ३१॥

English
The goal realised by the person who dies for the brahmins, cows and his master, being killed by carnivorous beasts or tusked beasts is excellent. It yields him extensive and never-ending benefits. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्नोत्विह समर्थश्च यष्टुं क्रतुशतैरपि ।
आत्मदेहपरित्यागः कर्तुं युधि सुदुष्करः ॥ ३२॥

English
A man may be competent to perform hundreds of sacrifices, but it is very difficult to renounce his own body in a righteous war. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
युद्धं पुण्यतमं स्वर्ग्यं रूपज्ञं सर्वतोमुखम् ।
सर्वेषामेव वर्णानां क्षत्रियस्य विशेषतः ॥ ३३॥

English
A righteous war is conducive to the attainment of heaven. It is sanctifying. It makes known the multi-faced beauty of a man. This is the case with the people of all castes,[288] particularly so in the case of a Kṣatriya. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
भृशं चैव प्रवक्ष्यामि युद्धधर्मं सनातनम् ।
यादृशाय प्रहर्तव्यं यादृशं परिवर्जयेत् ॥ ३४॥

English
Now I shall explain clearly the eternal virtue concerning war, as to who can be attacked and hit and who shall be spared. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
आततायिनमायान्तमपि वेदान्तङ्गं द्विजम् ।
जिघांसन्तं जिघांसेत्तु न तेन ब्रह्महा भवेत् ॥ ३५॥

English
Even if a brahmin well-versed in Vedanta doctrine were to attack one violently with lethal weapons one can fight him in return. He will not become guilty of brahminicide thereby.[289] (35.)

---

Sanskrit
हन्तव्योऽपि न हन्तव्यः पानीयं यश्च याचते ।
रणे हत्वातुरा व्यास स नरो ब्रह्महा भवेत् ॥ ३६॥

English
Even a murderer shall not be attacked and killed if he is thirsty and begs for water. O Vyasa, by killing the sick and the distressed even in battle a man becomes guilty of brahminicide. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याधितं दुर्बलं बालं स्त्र्यनाथौ कृपणं ध्रुवम् ।
धनुर्भग्नं छिन्नगुणं हत्वा वै ब्रह्महा भवेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
One becomes a murderer of a brahmin by killing the sick, the feeble, the child, the hen-pecked, the miserly and one whose bow or bowstring is broken. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विचार्य सद्धीमान् भवेत्प्रीत्याः रणप्रियः ।
स जन्मनः फलं प्राप्य परत्रेह प्रमोदते ॥ ३८॥

English
After considering all this, an intelligent man shall strenuously indulge in battles. He will achieve the purpose of his life and rejoice here and hereafter. (38.)
