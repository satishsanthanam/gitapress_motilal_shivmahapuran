### **CHAPTER TWENTYTWO - (The origin and development of the body)**

---

Sanskrit
५.२२. द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः । देहोत्पत्तिवर्णनम् ।

व्यास उवाच ।
विधिं तात वदेदानीं जीवजन्म विधानतः ।
गर्भे स्थितिं च तस्यापि वैराग्यार्थं मुनीश्वर ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O dear, O great sage, for realizing the worldly detachment please mention the detailed mode of the birth of living beings and their stay in the womb. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास समासेन शास्त्रसारमशेषतः ।
वदिष्यामि सुवैराग्यं मुमुक्षोर्भवबन्धकृत् ॥ २॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O Vyāsa, listen succinctly to the essence of the sacred lore. I shall explain the excellent mode of worldly detachment which removes the bondage with the world, of a man desirous of liberation. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
पाकपात्रस्य मध्ये तु पृथगन्नं पृथग्जलम् ।
अग्नेरूर्ध्वं जलं स्थाप्यं तदन्नं च जलोपरि ॥ ३॥

जलस्याधः स चाग्निर्हि स्थितोऽग्निं धमते शनैः ।
वायुना धम्यमानोऽग्निरत्युष्णं कुरुते जलम् ॥ ४॥

English
When we cook rice the process is as follows. In the cooking vessel the rice and water remain separate. Water is above fire. The rice is above water. The wind slowly blows against the fire beneath the water. The fire kindled by the wind makes the water boil. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदन्नमुष्णतोयेन समन्तात्पच्यते पुनः ।
द्विधा भवति तत्पक्वं पृथक्किट्टं पृथग्रसः ॥ ५॥

English
The rice with hot-boiling water all round it becomes cooked. When cooked it becomes separated into sediment and juice. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
मलैर्द्वादशभिः किट्टं भिन्नं देहाद्बहिर्भवेत् ।
रसस्तु देहे सरति स पुष्टस्तेन जायते ॥ ६॥

English
(More or less a similar process takes place in the body.) The sediment in the body consists of twelve forms of waste matter. These are split and sent out of the body. The juice matter circulates in the body. Man gets nourished through it. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्णाक्षिनासिका जिह्वा दन्ताः शिश्नो गुदं नखाः ।
मलाश्रयः कफः स्वेदो विण्मूत्रं द्वादश स्मृताः ॥ ७॥

English
The ears, the eyes, the nose, the tongue, the teeth, the penis, the anus, and the nails are the receptacles of dirt secreted by them. The phlegm, the sweat, the faeces and the urine—these together make up the twelve. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हृत्पद्मे प्रतिबद्धाश्च सर्वनाड्यः समन्ततः ।
ज्ञेया रसप्रवाहिन्यस्तत्प्रकारं ब्रुवे मुने ॥ ८॥

English
All the vessels scattered all over the body are joined centrally at the heart. These vessels shall be understood to convey Rasa. O sage, I shall mention their process of working. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तासां मुखेषु तं सूक्ष्मं प्राणः स्थापयेत् रसम् ।
रसेन तेन नाडीस्ताः प्राणं पूरयते पुनः ॥ ९॥

English
Prāṇa (the vital air) places the subtle lymph at the mouths of the vessels. Then it fills the entire vessels with the lymph. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः प्रयान्ति सम्पूर्णाः ताश्च देहं समन्ततः ।
ततः स नाडीमध्यस्थः शरीरेणात्मना रसः ॥ १०॥

पच्यते पच्यमानाच्च भवेत्पाकद्वयं पुनः ।
त्वक् तया वेष्ट्यते पूर्वं रुधिरं च प्रजायते ॥ ११॥

English
Thus filled, the vessels circulate all over the body. Then the lymph inside the vessels gets cooked by the fire in the body. There are two modes of cooking. [Probably the separation of sediment and watery part]. The skin is covered thereby. [The cells constituting the skin are generated]. Blood is produced. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्ताल्लोमानि मांसं च केशाः स्नायुश्च मांसतः ।
स्नायुतश्च तथास्थीनि नखा मज्जास्थिसम्भवाः ॥ १२॥

English
The hair of the body and flesh are produced from the blood. The hair on the head and the sinews are produced from the flesh. From the sinews the bones are produced. The nails and marrow are produced from the bones. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
मज्जाकारणवैकल्यं शुक्रं हि प्रसवात्मकम् ।
इति द्वादशधान्नस्य परिणामः प्रकीर्तिताः ॥ १३॥

English
An alternative cause of marrow, the semen itself becomes progeny. Thus the twelvefold metabolism of bood is explained. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्रोऽन्नाज्जायते शुक्राद्दिव्यदेहस्य सम्भवः ।
ऋतुकाले यदा शुक्रं निर्दोषं योनिसंस्थितम् ॥ १४॥

तदा तद् वायुसंस्पृष्टं स्त्रीरक्तेनैकतां व्रजेत् ।
विसर्गकाले शुक्रस्य जीवः कारणसंयुतः ॥ १५॥

संवृतः प्रविशेद्योनिं कर्मभिः स्वैर्नियोजितः ।
तच्छुक्ररक्तमेकस्थमेकाहात्कललं भवेत् ॥ १६॥

English
The semen is secreted from the food eaten From the semen, the birth of another body is made possible. When the semen unalloyed is deposited in the vaginal passage during the prescribed period of cohabitation after the monthly menses, then the semen blown by the vital wind mingles with the blood of the woman. At the time when the semen is discharged the individual soul with the causal body or unit of sense-organs etc. enters the vaginal passage fully covered and urged by its past actions. The semen and blood in the unified state become foetus in a day. (14-16.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चरात्रेण कललं बुद्बुदाकारतां व्रजेत् ।
बुद्बुदः सप्तरात्रेण मांसपेशी भवेत् पुनः ॥ १७॥

English
In five nights the foetus assumes the shape of bubbles and the bubbly mass becomes muscular mass in seven nights. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ग्रीवा शिरश्च स्कन्धौ च पृष्ठवंशस्तथोदरम् ।
पाणिपादं तथा पार्श्वे कटिर्गात्रं तथैव च ॥ १८॥

द्विमासाभ्यन्तरेणैव क्रमशः शम्भवेदिह ।
त्रिभिर्मासैः प्रजायन्ते सर्वे ह्यङ्कुरसन्धयः ॥ १९॥

English
Within two months the following parts of the body are formulated in order Viz. the neck, the head the shoulders, the backbone, the belly, hands and feet, the sides the lips etc. Within three months all the joints are completed. (18-19.)

---

English
Within the fourth month the fingers are duly formed. The mouth, nose, and ears are ready within the fifth month. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मासैश्चतुर्भिरङ्गुल्यः प्रजायन्ते यथाक्रमम् ।
मुखं नासा च कर्णौ मासैः पञ्चभिरेव च ॥ ५.२२.२०॥

दन्तपङ्क्तिस्तथा गुह्यं जायन्ते च नखाः पुनः ।
कर्णयोस्तु भवेच्छिद्रं षण्मासाभ्यन्तरेण तु ॥ २१॥

English
Within six months the row of teeth, the private parts and the nails and the orifice of the ears take shape. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पायुर्मेहमुपस्थं च नाभिश्चाभ्युपजायते ।
सन्धयो ये च गात्रेषु मासैर्जायन्ति सप्तभिः ॥ २२॥

English
The rectum, or the penis of the vaginal passage and the navel too are formulated. Within the seventh month all the limb-joints are completed. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अङ्गप्रत्यङ्गसम्पूर्णः परिपक्वः स तिष्ठति ।
उदरे मातुराच्छन्नो जरायौ मुनिसत्तम ॥ २३॥

English
O excellent sage, thus the complete child with all the limbs stays in the womb covered by the embryonic skin. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
मातुराहारचौर्येण षड्विधेन रसेन तु ।
नाभिनालनिबद्धेन वर्द्धते स दिने दिने ॥ २४॥

English
The child increases and develops day by day, stealthily taking in the food of the mother, of all the six tastes, through the umbilical cord. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्मृतिं लभेज्जीवः सम्पूर्णेऽस्मिन् शरीरके ।
सुखं दुःखं विजानाति निद्रास्वप्नं पुराकृतम् ॥ २५॥

English
Then the soul complete in this body recollects all the pleasures and sorrows and every thing done by it formerly as if in a dream. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
मृतश्चाहं पुनर्जातो जातश्चाहं पुनर्मृतः ।
नानायोनिसहस्राणि मया दृष्टानि जायता ॥ २६॥

English
"I am dead and born many times In these different births I have seen many types of wombs. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अधुना जातमात्रोऽहं प्राप्तसंस्कार एव च ।
श्रेयोऽमुना करिष्यामि येन गर्भे न सम्भवः ॥ २७॥

English
This time when I am born and consecrated I shall strive for welfare by means of this body so that I may not have to stay in another womb in future. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भस्थश्चिन्तयन्नेवमहं गर्भाद्विनिःसृतः ।
अन्वेष्यामि शिवज्ञानं संसारविनिवर्तकम् ॥ २८॥

English
So thinks the child in the womb "As soon as I am released from this womb I shall seek Siva's perfect knowledge that repels all worldly existence." (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स गर्भदुःखेन महता परिपीडितः ।
जीवः कर्मवशादास्ते मोक्षोपायं विचिन्तयन् ॥ २९॥

English
Troubled and tormented by the distress in the womb as a result of his past actions the individual soul stays there pondering over the means of liberation. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा गिरिवराक्रान्तः कश्चिद्दुःखेन तिष्ठति ।
तथा जरायुणा देही दुःखं तिष्ठति वेष्टितः ॥ ३०॥

English
Just as a traveller surrounded by huge impassable mountains on all sides stands helplessly in his distress, so also the creature in the womb enveloped by the embryonic skin stays miserably. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
पतितः सागरे यद्वद्दुःखमास्ते समाकुलः ।
गर्भोदकेन सिक्ताङ्गः सर्वदाकुलितस्तदा ॥ ३१॥

English
Just as a man drowning in the sea is very much distressed, so also the child in the womb completely drenched by the liquid secretion in the womb is always distressed excessively. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
लोहकुम्भे यथा न्यस्तः पच्यते कश्चिदग्निना ।
गर्भकुम्भे तथा क्षिप्तः पच्यते जठराग्निना ॥ ३२॥

English
Just as a person is cooked and boiled in an iron cauldron by means of fire, so also the child hurled into the pot of womb is cooked by the gastric fire. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सूचीभिरग्निवर्णाभिर्निर्भिन्नस्य निरन्तरम् ।
यद्दुःखं जायते तस्य तत्र संस्थस्य चाधिकम् ॥ ३३॥

English
For him staying inside the womb the misery is equal to, nay, more than the misery of a person incessantly pierced by means of redhot needles. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भावासात्परं दुःखं कष्टं नैवास्ति कुत्रचित् ।
देहिनां दुःखबहुलं सुघोरमतिसङ्कटम् ॥ ३४॥

English
There is nowhere a greater misery than staying within the womb. To all embodied souls it is full of misery, very terrible and grievous. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येतत्सुमहद्दुःखं पापिनां परिकीर्तितम् ।
केवलं धर्मबुध्दीनां सप्तमासैर्भवः सदा ॥ ३५॥

English
This type of misery is for the sinners. But those of virtuous intellect take birth within seven months. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भात्सुदुर्लभं दुःखं योनियन्त्रनिपीडनात् ।
भवेत्पापात्मनां व्यास न हि धर्मयुतात्मनाम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Getting crushed in the vaginal passage as if within a machine is a misery more grievous than the stay within the womb. O Vyasa, this takes place in regard to the sinners and not the virtuous. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इक्षुवत्पीड्यमानस्य यन्त्रेणैव समन्ततः ।
शिरसा ताड्यमानस्य पापमुद्गरकेण च ॥ ३७॥

English
Just as the sugarcane is crushed in the machine, the sinner is crushed and beaten on the head by the ironclub of sin. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यन्त्रेण पीडिता यद्वन्निःसाराः स्स्युस्तिलाः क्षणात् ।
तथा शरीरं निस्सारं योनियन्त्रनिपीडनात् ॥ ३८॥

English
Just as the gingelly seeds pressed in the oil machine become a worthless residue, so also the body becomes a waste being crushed by the muscular walls of the vaginal passage. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्थिपादतुलास्तम्भं स्नायुबन्धेन यन्त्रितम् ।
रक्तमांसमृदालिप्तं विण्मूत्रद्रव्यभाजनम् ॥ ३९॥

केशरोमनखच्छन्नं रोगायतनमातुरम् ।
वदनैकमहाद्वारं गवाक्षाष्टकभूषितम् ॥ ४०॥

ओष्ठद्वयकपाटं च तथा जिह्वार्गलान्वितम् ।
भोगतृष्णातुरं मूढं रागद्वेषवशानुगम् ॥ ४१॥

संवर्तिताङ्गप्रत्यङ्गं जरायुपरिवेष्टितम् ।
सङ्कटेनाविविक्तेन योनिमार्गेण निर्गतम् ॥ ४२॥

विण्मूत्ररक्तसिक्ताङ्गं विकोशिकसमुद्भवम् ।
अस्थिपञ्जरविख्यातमस्मिन् ज्ञेयं कलेवरम् ॥ ४३॥

English
This body in the womb is in an unenviable position. The hands and legs are like suspended pillars. Sinews tie them up in various ways. The whole body is smeared with dirty blood and flesh. It is the receptacle of faeces and urine. It is covered with hairs and nails. It is sickly and an abode of ailments. The only great opening is the mouth. It has eight small apertures like windows. The two lips are like two doors. The tongue is the bolt for them. The body is sick due to its thirst for pleasures. It is subservient to passion and hatred and utterly deluded. The various limbs overlap one another. It is surrounded by the embryonic membrane. With very great pain and difficulty it has to come out of the vaginal passage. It is drenched with urine, blood and faeces. It comes out as from a sheath. It is a mass of skeleton. (39-43.)
