### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - (The description of infancy and other stages and the impurity of the body)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
श‍ृणु व्यास महाबुद्धे देहस्याशुचितां मुने ।
महत्त्वं च स्वभावस्य समासात्कथयाम्यहम् ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O Vyasa, O highly intelligent sage, listen to the impurity of the body and the greatness of good feelings. I shall explain briefly. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्रशोणितसंयोगाद्देहः सञ्जायते यतः ।
नित्यं विण्मूत्रसम्पूर्णस्तेनायमशुचिः स्मृतः ॥ २॥

English
The body is considered impure because it is produced by the mingling of semen and blood and is always full of faeces and urine. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
यथान्तर्विष्ठया पूर्णः शुचिमान्न बहिर्घटः ।
शोध्यमानो हि देहोऽयं तेनायमशुचिस्ततः ॥ ३॥

English
Just as a pot, full of faeces cannot become pure even when washed outside, so also the body washed outside cannot become pure. Hence it is impure. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्प्राप्याति पवित्राणि पञ्चगव्यं हवींषि च ।
अशुचित्वं क्षणाद्यान्ति किमन्यदशुचिस्ततः ॥ ४॥

English
Even after taking in the sanctifying Pañcagavya, Havis (Ghee oblation) etc. the body becomes impure the next moment. What else is more impure than this body? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
हृद्यान्यप्यन्नपानानि यं प्राप्य सुरभीणि च ।
अशुचित्वं प्रयान्त्याशु किमन्यदशुचिस्ततः ॥ ५॥

English
Even after taking in sweet, fragrant and delicious foodstuffs and drinks the body becomes impure quickly. What else is more impure than this. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
हे जनाः किन्न पश्यन्ति यन्निर्याति दिने दिने ।
स्वदेहात्कश्मलं पूतिस्तदाधारः कथं शुचिः ॥ ६॥

English
O men, don't you see dirty and foetal matter coming out of your bodies day by day. How can the receptacle of such matter be pure. ? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
देहः सशोध्यमानोऽपि पञ्चगव्यकुशाम्बुभिः ।
घृष्यमाण इवाङ्गारो निर्मलत्वं न गच्छति ॥ ७॥

English
Even if the body is sprinkled with the waters of the holy Kuśa grass and smeared with Pañcagavya,20 it will not become free from dirt like charcoal even when it is rubbed and polished. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स्रोतांसि यस्य सततं प्रभवन्ति गिरेरिव ।
कफमूत्रपुरीषाद्यैः स देहः शुध्यते कथम् ॥ ८॥

English
Like the perennial flow of the springs of a mountain the vessels of the body discharge phlegm, urine, faeces and other types of filth. How can that body become clean? (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाशुचिनिधानस्य शरीरस्य न विद्यते ।
शुचिरेकः प्रदेशोऽपि विण्मूत्रस्य दृतेरिव ॥ ९॥

English
There is not even a single clean spot on the body—the receptacle of all types of filth and a leather bag of faeces and urine. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्ट्वात्मदेहस्रोतांसि मृत्तोयैः शोध्यते करः ।
तथाप्यशुचिभाण्डस्य न विभ्रश्यति किं करः ॥ १०॥

English
After touching the tips of the vessels of one's own body the hand is washed with sand and water. Still the persons who are slaves to this filthy body do not feel. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
कायः सुगन्धधूपाद्यैर्यत्नेनापि सुसंस्कृतः ।
न जहाति स्वभावं स श्वपुच्छमिव नामितम् ॥ ११॥

English
Inspite of being smeared and sanctified assiduously by sweet scents, incense and other things the body does not eschew its nature like the curved tail of a dog. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा जात्यैव कृष्णोर्थः शुक्लः स्स्यान्नह्युपायतः ।
संशोद्ध्यमानापि तथा भवेन्मूर्तिर्न निर्मला ॥ १२॥

English
Just as an object, naturally black, cannot become white by any means, so also the physical body does not become clean though it is scrubbed and cleaned. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
जिघ्रन्नपि स्वदुर्गन्धं पश्यन्नपि स्वकं मलम् ।
न विरज्येत लोकोऽयं पीडयन्नपि नासिकाम् ॥ १३॥

English
Though he smells his own noxious odour, though he sees his own dirt, no man becomes detached and keeps his hand on his nose. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो मोहस्य माहात्म्यं येनेदं छादितं जगत् ।
शीघ्रं पश्यन् स्वकं दोषं कायस्य न विरज्यते ॥ १४॥

English
Wonderful indeed is this great delusion whereby the whole universe is enveloped. He does not observe the impurities of his body and become disgusted with it. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वदेहस्य विगन्धेन न विरज्येत यो नरः ।
विरागकारणं तस्य किमेतदुपदिश्यते ॥ १५॥

English
If a man is not detached from the world due to the foetal smell of his own what other cause of detachment can be imparted to him? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्यैव जगन्मध्ये देह एवाशुचिर्भवेत् ।
तन्मलावयवस्पर्शाच्छुचिरप्यशुचिर्भवेत् ॥ १६॥

English
In the midst of the entire universe, the body alone is the most unclean. By touching its filthy limbs even the pure becomes impure. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
गन्धलेपापनोदार्थ शौचं देहस्य कीर्तितम् ।
द्वयस्यापगमाच्छुद्धिः शुद्धस्पर्शाद्विशुध्यति ॥ १७॥

English
The purification of the body is for removing bad odour and sliminess. If both are removed there is purity. The touch of a pure thing also sanctifies it. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गातोयेन सर्वेण मृद्भारैः पर्वतोपमैः ।
आमृत्योराचरेच्छौचं भावदुष्टो न शुध्यति ॥ १८॥

English
But let him be cleaned with all the waters of the Gaṅgā, with heaps of sand resembling a mountain or let him observe purificatory rites till death, if a man has wicked feelings and emotions he can never be purified. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तीर्थस्नानैस्तपोभिर्वा दुष्टात्मा नैव शुध्यति ।
श्वदृतिः क्षालिता तीर्थे किं शुद्धिमधिगच्छति ॥ १९॥

English
A vicious soul is not sanctified by the waters in the pilgrim spots nor by austerities. Does the canine hide become pure when washed in a holy river? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्भावप्रदुष्टस्य विशतोऽपि हुताशनम् ।
न स्वर्गो नापवर्गश्च देहनिर्दहनं परम् ॥ २०॥

English
Neither heaven nor salvation can be attained by a man of vicious emotion even if he immolates himself in the holy fire. The only result will be that his body is entirely burnt off. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेण गाङ्गेन जलेन सम्यङ्
मृत्पर्वतेनाप्यथ भावदुष्टः ।
आजन्मनः स्नानपरो मनुष्यो
न शुध्यतीत्येव वयं वदामः ॥ २१॥

English
We assert that a man of vicious instinct and feeling never becomes pure though he may indulge in ablutions throughout his life with all the waters of the Gangā and a mountain of sand. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रज्वाल्य वह्निं घृततैलसिक्तं
प्रदक्षिणावर्तशिखं महान्तम् ।
प्रविश्य दग्धस्त्वपि भावदुष्टो
न धर्ममाप्नोति फलं न चान्यत् ॥ २२॥

English
If a man of defiled emotion gets himself burnt after entering a huge blazing fire kindled by sprinkling ghee and oil and with flames of circular motion he does not become pure. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गादितीर्थेषु वसन्ति मत्स्या
देवालये पक्षिगणाश्च नित्यम् ।
भावोज्झितास्ते न फलं लभन्ते
तीर्थावगाहाच्च तथैव दानात् ॥ २३॥

English
Fishes stay in the holy Gangā and other sacred rivers. Flocks of birds stay in the temple. They do not attain any special benefit from the ceremonial ablutions and charitable gifts since they are devoid of holy feelings. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
भावशुद्धिः परं शौचं प्रमाणे सर्वकर्मसु ।
अन्यथालिङ्ग्यते कान्ता भावेन दुहितान्यथा ॥ २४॥

English
It is the purity of the feelings that is the criterion for the sanctity of rites. The wife is embraced with one feeling and the daughter is embraced with another. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसो भिद्यते वृत्तिरभिन्नेष्वपि वस्तुषु ।
अन्यथैव सुतं नारी चिन्तयत्यन्यथा पतिम् ॥ २५॥

English
Feelings are different in the mind even if the objects are not different. The same woman is looked at in one way by the son and in another way by the husband. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्यध्वमस्य भावस्य महाभाग्यमशेषतः ।
परिष्वक्तोऽपि यन्नार्या भावहीनं न कामयेत् ॥ २६॥

English
O observe the great fortune in the difference of emotions. The man, though embraced, does not love the woman who is devoid of the feelings of love. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नाद्याद्विविधमन्नाद्यं भक्ष्याणि सुरभीणि च ।
यदि चिन्तां समाधत्ते चित्ते कामादिषु त्रिषु ॥ २७॥

English
If the three emotions, passion etc. were to create worries in the mind no one would relish different delicious or fragrant stuffs. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
गृह्यते तेन भावेन नरो भावाद्विमुच्यते ।
भावतः शुचि शुद्धात्मा स्वर्गं मोक्षं च विन्दति ॥ २८॥

English
Man is caught hold of by emotions and man can rid himself of emotions. A man purified by pure emotions attains heaven and salvation. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
भावेनैकात्मशुद्धात्मा दहन् जुह्वन् स्तुवन् मृतः ।
ज्ञानावाप्तेरवाप्याशु लोकान् सुबहुयाजिनाम् ॥ २९॥

English
Performing Japas, sacrifices and eulogies after purifying the soul with pure emotions a man acquires perfect knowledge and after death he attains the worlds of persons who perform many sacrifices. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानामलाम्भसा पुंसां सद्वैराग्यमृदा पुनः ।
अविद्यारागविण्मूत्रलेपगन्धविशोधनम् ॥ ३०॥

English
The purification and removal of the sliminess and bad odour of the faeces and urine of ignorance and passion of men is effected by the pure water of knowledge and the clay of detachment from the world. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमेतच्छरीरं हि निसर्गादशुचि स्मृतम् ।
त्वङ्मात्रसारं निःसारं कदलीसारसन्निभम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Thus as the body is naturally unclean it is worthless. Like the inner stump of the plantain tree it has only the outer skin as substantial. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वैवं दोषवद्देहं यः प्राज्ञः शिथिलो भवेत् ।
देहभोगोद्भवाद्भावाच्छमचित्तः प्रसन्नधीः ॥ ३२॥

सोऽतिक्रामति संसारं जीवन्मुक्तः प्रजायते ।
संसारङ्कदलीसारदृढग्राह्यवतिष्ठते ॥ ३३॥

English
An intelligent man shall understand that the body is defective and faulty. He shall be disinterested in such emotions as revolve round the pleasures of the body. With the mind in restraint and intellect delighted he surmounts the worldly existence and becomes a living liberated soul. He who firmly grasps the inner stump of the plantain tree of worldly existence is not liberated. (32-33.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमेतन्महाकष्टं जन्मदुःखं प्रकीर्तितम् ।
पुंसामज्ञानदोषेण नानाकर्मवशेन च ॥ ३४॥

English
Thus the misery of life is proclaimed to be grievous and painful to men due to the fault of ignorance and the influence of different activities. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
श्लोकार्धेन तु वक्ष्यामि यदुक्तं ग्रन्थकोटिभिः ।
ममेति परमं दुःखं न ममेति परं सुखम् ॥ ३५॥

English
I shall explain in half a stanza what is mentioned in crores of books. The feeling of "My-ness" is the greatest affliction. The feeling of "not-my-ness" is the greatest joy. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
बहवोऽपीह राजानः परं लोकमितो गताः ।
निर्ममत्वसमेतास्तु बद्धाः शतसहस्रशः ॥ ३६॥

English
Hundreds and thousands of monarchs who had been in bondage had attained the greatest world on reaching the Nirmamatva (not-my-ness) state. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भस्थस्य स्मृतिर्यासीत्सा च तस्य प्रणश्यति ।
सम्मूर्छितेन दुःखेन योनियन्त्रनिपीडनात् ॥ ३७॥

English
What little recollection the child had while within the womb perishes when his misery develops further on being crushed by the muscular walls of the vaginal passage as if within a machine. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
बाह्येन वायुना वास्य मोहसङ्गेन देहिनः ।
स्पृष्टमात्रेण घोरेण ज्वरः समुपजायते ॥ ३८॥

English
A great fever overtakes him as soon as the external air touches him or delusion overwhelms him. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन ज्वरेण महता सम्मोहश्च प्रजायते ।
सम्मूढस्य स्मृतिभ्रंशः शीघ्रं सञ्जायते पुनः ॥ ३९॥

English
Due to that fever a great fascination overpowers him and deludes him. After being deluded he soon suffers from the loss of memory. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृतिभ्रंशात्ततस्तस्य स्मृतिर्न्नो पूर्वकर्मणः ।
रतिः सञ्जायते तूर्णं जन्तोस्तत्रैव जन्मनि ॥ ४०॥

English
Loss of memory leads to forgetfulness of all previous activities. The creature soon begins to love the present birth. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्तो मूढश्च लोकोऽयं न कार्ये सम्प्रवर्तते ।
न चात्मानं विजानाति न परं न च दैवतम् ॥ ४१॥

English
The passionate and deluded world does not engage in activities beneficial to itself. It does not realise itself nor others nor even the deity. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
न श‍ृणोति परं श्रेयः सति कर्णेऽपि सन्मुने ।
न पश्यति परं श्रेयः सति चक्षुषि तत्क्षमे ॥ ४२॥

English
O good sage, though he has ears man does not listen to what is beneficial to him. Even when he has competent eyes he does not see what is good. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
समे पथि शनैर्गच्छन् स्खलतीव पदे पदे ।
सत्यां बुद्धौ न जानाति बोध्यमानो बुधैरपि ॥ ४३॥

English
Walking slowly on the level, even path he falters at every step. Even when he has intellect, even when he is enlightened by scholars he does not understand. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
संसारे क्लिश्यते तेन गर्भलोभवशानुगः ।
गर्भस्मृतेन पापेन समुज्झितमतिः पुमान् ॥ ४४॥

English
Thereby, urged and prompted by great greed he suffers pain and distress in the world. His intellect no longer remembers the sins he committed which he remembered in the womb. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं महत्परं दिव्यं शास्त्रमुक्तं शिवेन तु ।
तपसः कथनार्थाय स्वर्गमोक्षप्रसाधनम् ॥ ४५॥

English
This great divine Śāstra has been narrated by Siva in the context of penance as a means to achieving heaven and salvation. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
ये सत्यस्मिन् शिवे ज्ञाने सर्वकामार्थसाधने ।
न कुर्वन्त्यात्मनः श्रेयस्तदेव महदद्भुतम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Even when the perfect knowledge of Siva that leads to the achievement of desire is available, it is really a great surprise if people do not strive for its attainment in their own interest. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अव्यक्तेन्द्रियवृत्तित्वाद् बाल्ये दुःखं महत्पुनः ।
इच्छन्नपि न शक्नोति वक्तुं कर्तुं प्रतिक्रियाम् ॥ ४७॥

English
There is again this great misery in infancy because the operative power of the sense-organs is not clearly manifest or developed. Even if he wishes he is unable to express or take remedial or preventive measures. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
दन्तोत्थाने महद्दुःखमल्पेन व्याधिना तथा ।
बालरोगैश्च विविधैः पीडा बालग्रहैरपि ॥ ४८॥

English
There is a great trouble when the child cuts the teeth. There is pain due to minor childhood ailments, different kinds of children's diseases and adverse evil planets and other evils. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
क्वचित्क्षुत्तृट्परीताङ्गः क्वचित्तिष्ठति संरटन् ।
विण्मूत्रभक्षणाद्यं च मोहाद् बालः समाचरेत् ॥ ४९॥

English
The infant boy may remain somewhere very hungry and thirsty, or lying somewhere he may be crying or he may be eating or drinking faeces and the urine due to delusion. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
कौमारे कर्णपीडायां मातापित्रोश्च साधनैः ।
अक्षराध्ययनाद्यैश्च नानादुःखं प्रवर्तते ॥ ५०॥

बाल्ये दुःखमतीत्यैव पश्यन्नपि विमूढधीः ।
न कुर्वीतात्मनः श्रेयस्तदत्र महदद्भुतम् ॥ ५१॥

English
During childhood when the rite of earboring is performed or when the parents beat him or when he feels difficulty in learning the alphabet it is surprising that the stupid fellow does not strive for his welfare, even after seeing and somehow surmounting the miseries of boyhood. (50-51.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवृत्तेन्द्रियवृत्तित्वात्कामरोगप्रपीडनात् ।
तदप्राप्ते तु सततं कुतः सौख्यं तु यौवने ॥ ५२॥

English
Where is happiness during youth, when all the sense-organs are developed fully and begin to function, and there is an attack from the sickness of lust that remains unrealised ever? (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ईर्ष्यया च महद्दुःखं मोहाद्रक्तस्य तस्य च ।
नेत्रस्य कुपितस्येव त्यागी दुःखाय केवलम् ॥ ५३॥

English
Then he feels misery from malice and rivalry.  By delusion his eyes become red like those of an infuriated man. Its abandonment is only conducive to misery. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
न रात्रौ विन्दते निद्रां कामाग्निपरिवेदितः ।
दिवापि च कुतः सौख्यमर्थोपार्जनचिन्तया ॥ ५४॥

English
During nights he does not get proper sleep because he is scorched by the fire of lust. Where is pleasure even during the day because he is worried over the ways and means of earning wealth? (54.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीष्वध्यासितचित्तस्य ये पुंसः शुक्रबिन्दवः ।
ते सुखाय न मन्यन्ते स्वेदजा इव ते तथा ॥ ५५॥

English
The drops of semen of a man whose mind is dwelling on women do not bring him pleasure. They are like his own sweat drops, mere dirt. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
कृमिभिस्तुद्यमानस्य कुष्ठिनो वानरस्य च ।
कण्डूयनाभितापेन यद्भवेत् स्त्रीषु तद्विदः ॥ ५६॥

English
What happens in women is exactly what happens to a leprous monkey when it scratches itself on being afflicted by worms. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
यादृशं मन्यते सौख्यं गण्डे पूतिविनिर्गमात् ।
तादृशं स्त्रीषु मन्तव्यं नाधिकं तासु विद्यते ॥ ५७॥

English
The pleasure in women is similar to the pleasure that is felt when the pus comes out of a swollen tumour. It is nothing more than that. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
विण्मूत्रस्य समुत्सर्गात्सुखं भवति यादृशम् ।
तादृशं स्त्रीषु विज्ञेयं मूढैः कल्पितमन्यथा ॥ ५८॥

English
The pleasure in women is similar to the pleasure one feels when faeces and urine are discharged. But it has been considered otherwise by the stupid. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
नारीष्ववस्तुभूतासु सर्वदोषाश्रयासु च ।
नाणुमात्रं सुखं तासु कथितं पञ्चचूडया ॥ ५९॥

English
It has been mentioned by Pañcacūda that there is not even a particle of pleasure in women who are the storehouses of all blemishes and who are creatures unsubstantial. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्माननावमानाभ्यां वियोगेनेष्टसङ्गमात् ।
यौवनं जरया ग्रस्तं क्व सौख्यमनुपद्रवम् ॥ ६०॥

English
Due to honour and disrespect, due to union with and separation from the beloved people youth is soon devoured by old age. Where is pleasure without torment? (60.)

---

Sanskrit
वलीपलितखालित्यैः शिथिलिकृतविग्रहम् ।
सर्वक्रियास्वशक्तिं च जरया जर्जरीकृतम् ॥ ६१॥

स्त्रीपुंसयौवनं हृद्यमन्योऽन्यस्य प्रियं पुरा ।
तदेव जरया ग्रस्तमनयोरपि न प्रियम् ॥ ६२॥

English
The youth of women and men which had been mutually pleasurable formerly is not so now when it is overwhelmed by old age, when the body is rendered shattered and rickety by wrinkles, grey hairs and baldness and when the man is incompetent to perform any serious task. (61-62.)

---

Sanskrit
अपूर्ववत्स्वमात्मानं जरया परिवर्तितम् ।
यः पश्यन्नपि रज्येत कोऽन्यस्तस्मादचेतनः ॥ ६३॥

English
Who can be a more senseless person than the man who feels attracted to worldly pleasures even after seeing himself completely transformed due to old age as if he were a new person not existing before? (63.)

---

Sanskrit
जराभिभूतः पुरुषः पुत्रीपुत्रादिबान्धवैः ।
असक्तत्वाद्दुराधर्षैर्भृत्यैश्च परिभूयते ॥ ६४॥

English
A man overwhelmed by old age is tormented by daughters, sons and other kinsmen and even by servants who cannot be kept under control because they are not attached to him. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्ममर्थं च कामं वा मोक्षं वातिजरातुरः ।
अशक्तः साधितुं तस्माद्युवा धर्मं समाचरेत् ॥ ६५॥

English
A sick and an aged man is incompetent to strive for virtue, wealth, love and salvation. Hence a young man shall strive for these virtues. (65.)
