### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - (Women's nature)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
कुत्सितं योषिदर्थं यत्सम्प्रोक्तं पञ्चचूडया ।
तन्मे ब्रूहि समासेन यदि तुष्टोऽसि मे मुने ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said:—  O sage, if you are pleased with me you shall narrate succinctly what was mentioned by Pañcacūḍā viz., that women are despicable. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
स्त्रीणां स्वभावं वक्ष्यामि श‍ृणु विप्र यथातथम् ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण भवेद्वैराग्यमुत्तमम् ॥ २॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  I shall explain the nature of women as it is. O dear, listen to it, merely by hearing which excellent detachment results. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियो मूलं हि दोषाणां लघुचित्ताः सदा मुने ।
तदासक्तिर्न कर्तव्या मोक्षेप्सुभिरतन्द्रितैः ॥ ३॥

English
O sage, women are light-minded. They are at the root of all troubles. Attachment towards them should not be pursued by wakeful persons who desire liberation. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्राप्युदाहरन्तीममितिहासं पुरातनम् ।
नारदस्य च संवादं पुंश्चल्या पञ्चचूडया ॥ ४॥

English
In this respect they quote an ancient tradition, the conversation of Nārada with the unchaste woman Pañcacūda. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकान् परिचरन् धीमान् देवर्षिर्नारदः पुरा ।
ददर्शाप्सरसं बालां पञ्चचूडामनुत्तमाम् ॥ ५॥

English
Formerly, while the intelligent celestial sage Nārada was wandering in the worlds he saw the beautiful celestial damsel Pañcacūda. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पप्रच्छाप्सरसं सुभ्रूं नारदो मुनिसत्तमः ।
संशयो हृदि मे कश्चित्तन्मे ब्रूहि सुमध्यमे ॥ ६॥

English
The excellent sage Nārada asked the beautiful woman, the Apsaras—"O lady of beautiful waist, I have a certain doubt. Please explain it to me." (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्ता तु सा विप्रं प्रत्युवाच वराप्सरा ।
विषये सति वक्ष्यामि समर्थां मन्यसेऽथ माम् ॥ ७॥

English
O brahmin, thus addressed the excellent celestial damsel replied—"If you consider me competent and if there is a proper subject, I shall explain." (7.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
न त्वामविषये भद्रे नियोक्ष्यामि कथञ्चन ।
स्त्रीणां स्वभावमिच्छामि त्वत्तः श्रोतुं सुमध्यमे ॥ ८॥

English
Nārada said:—  O gentle lady, I shall never engage you in a subject beyond your scope. O lady of slender waist, I wish to hear from you the nature of women. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य देवर्षेरप्सरोत्तमा ।
प्रत्युवाच मुनीशं तं देवर्षिं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ ९॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  On hearing these words of that celestial sage the excellent Apsaras replied to that lord of sages, Nārada the excellent sage. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चचूडोवाच ।
मुने श‍ृणु न शक्या स्त्री सती वै निन्दितुं स्त्रिया ।
विदितास्ते स्त्रियो याश्च यादृश्यश्च स्वभावतः ॥ १०॥

English
Pañcacūḍā said:—  "O sage listen, being a woman I cannot censure women. You already know what women are and what their nature is. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
न मामर्हसि देवर्षे नियोक्तुं प्रश्नमीदृशम् ।
इत्युक्त्वा साभवत्तूष्णीं पञ्चचूडाप्सरोवरा ॥ ११॥

English
O celestial sage, it does not behove you to urge me in such a question." After saying this the excellent Apsaras Pañcacūḍā kept quiet. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ देवर्षिवर्यो हि श्रुत्वा तद्वाक्यमुत्तमम् ।
प्रत्युवाच पुनस्तां वै लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ १२॥

English
On hearing her excellent statement the most excellent of the celestial sages replied to her with a desire for the benefit of the worlds. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
मृषावादे भवेद्दोषः सत्ये दोषो न विद्यते ।
इति जानीहि सत्यं त्वं वदातस्तत्सुमध्यमे ॥ १३॥

English
Nārada said:—  It may be wrong to make a false statement. There is no defect in speaking the truth. Know this, O lady of good waist and speak the truth. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इत्युक्ता सा कृतमती रभसा चारुहासिनी ।
स्त्रीदोषान् शाश्वतान्सत्यान् भाषितुं सम्प्रचक्रमे ॥ १४॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  Thus prompted, the sweet-smiling lady, resolved and immediately began to explain truthfully the permanent defects of women. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चचूडोवाच ।
कुलीना नाथवन्त्यश्च रूपवन्त्यश्च योषितः ।
मर्यादाषु न तिष्ठन्ति स दोषः स्त्रीषु नारद ॥ १५॥

English
Pañcacūḍā said:—  O Nārada, this is the defect in women. Even women of noble families, women with husbands and women endowed with beauty do not stand within the limits of decency. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
न स्त्रीभ्यः किञ्चिदन्यद्वै पापीयस्तरमस्ति हि ।
स्त्रियो मूलं हि पापानां तथा त्वमपि वेत्थ ह ॥ १६॥

English
There is none more sinning and more sinful than women. Women are at the root of all sins. This you know already. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
समाज्ञातानर्थवतः प्रतिरूपान् यथेप्सितान् ।
पतीनन्तरमासाद्य नालं नार्यः प्रतीक्षितुम् ॥ १७॥

English
They might have husbands of good knowledge, of ample wealth, of great comeliness and pleasing to them. But when they get opportunities for erring, they do not wait. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
असद्धर्मस्त्वयं स्त्रीणामस्माकं भवति प्रभो ।
पापीयसो नरान् यद्वै लज्जां त्यक्त्वा भजामहे ॥ १८॥

English
O holy lord, this is the evil practice of all of us, women, that we resort to sinful men casting off all shame and shyness. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रियं च यः प्रार्थयते सन्निकर्षं च गच्छति ।
ईषच्च कुरुते सेवां तमेवेच्छन्ति योषितः ॥ १९॥

English
Women love only those persons who solicit their company, who approach them intimately and who render them a little bit of service.[291] (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अनर्थित्वान्मनुष्याणां भयात्पतिजनस्य च ।
मर्यादायाममर्यादाः स्त्रियस्तिष्ठन्ति भर्तृषु ॥ २०॥

English
Women usually do not observe the limitations of conventional decency. If at all they stand by them with their husbands it is because no man makes advances to them or because they are afraid of their husbands.$^{292}$ (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नासां कश्चिदमान्योऽस्ति नासां वयसि निश्चयः ।
सुरूपं वा कुरूपं वा पुमांसमुपभुञ्जते ॥ २१॥

English
There is no man not worthy of honour to women. They are not mindful of the age of the man. They carry on their dalliances with any man ugly or beautiful.$^{293}$ (21.)

---

Sanskrit
न भयादथ वाक्रोशान्नार्थहेतोः कथञ्चन ।
न ज्ञातिकुलसम्बन्धात् स्त्रियस्तिष्ठन्ति भर्तृषु ॥ २२॥

English
It is not due to fear, taunts or affection for their husbands or regard for their lineage that women remain loyal to their husbands. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
यौवने वर्तमानानामिष्टाभरणवाससाम् ।
नारीणां स्वैरवृत्तीनां स्पृहयन्ति कुलस्त्रियः ॥ २३॥

English
Even women of noble families aspire for the life of lascivious women who in their prime of youth adorned with lovable ornaments and beautiful wearing garments move about frivolously. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
या हि शश्वद् बहुमता रक्ष्यन्ते दयिताः स्त्रियः ।
अपि ताः सम्प्रसज्जन्ते कुब्जान्धजडवामने ॥ २४॥

English
Even the women who are honoured well, loved intimately and looked after with care become attached to hunchbacks, blind men, imbeciles and dwarfs. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पङ्गुष्वपि च देवर्षे ये चान्ये कुत्सिता नराः ।
स्त्रीणामगम्यो लोकेषु नास्ति कश्चिन्महामुने ॥ २५॥

English
O celestial sage, they become attached to lame and even despicable persons. O great sage, there is none in the world who cannot be approached by women with solicitations of lust. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
यदि पुंसां गतिर्ब्रह्मन् कथञ्चिन्नोपपद्यते ।
अप्यन्योऽन्यं प्रवर्तन्ते न च तिष्ठन्ति भर्तृषु ॥ २६॥

English
O brahmin, if women do not get men for their dalliance they begin to indulge in abnormal sexual activity with one another. They do not stand by their husbands. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अलाभात्पुरुषाणां च भयात्परिजनस्य च ।
वधबन्धभयाच्चैव ता भग्नाशा हि योषितः ॥ २७॥

English
Women become desperate when they do not get men, when they are afraid of servants, when they are frightened of being killed or imprisoned. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
चलस्वभाव दुश्चेष्टा दुर्गाह्या भावतस्तथा ।
प्राज्ञस्य पुरुषस्येह यथा रतिपरिग्रहात् ॥ २८॥

English
Because they indulge in sexual intercourse as they please they are fickleminded, of evil deeds and emotionally incomprehensible even to an intelligent man. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नाग्निस्तुष्यति काष्ठानां नापगानां महोदधि ।
नान्तकः सर्वभूतानां न पुंसां वामलोचनाः ॥ २९॥

English
Fire is not satiated with the logs of wood it consumes; the ocean is not satiated with the rivers that flow into it. The god of death is not satiated with the living beings he kills and women are not satiated with the number of men they cohabit with.$^{294}$ (29.)

---

Sanskrit
इदमन्यच्च देवर्षे रहस्यं सर्वयोषिताम् ।
दृष्ट्वैव पुरुषं सद्यो योनिः प्रक्लिद्यते स्त्रियाः ॥ ३०॥

English
O excellent sage, there is another secret of all women that immediately on seeing a man their vaginal passage begins to exude slimy secretions. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
सुस्नातं पुरुषं दृष्ट्वा सुगन्धं मलवर्जितम् ।
योनिः प्रक्लिद्यते स्त्रीणां दृतेः पात्रादिवोदकम् ॥ ३१॥

English
On seeing a man fresh and clean from his bath with his body perfumed with sweet scents, the vaginal passage of women begins to exude like water dripping from a leather bag. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कायानामपि दातारं कर्त्तारं मानसान्त्वयोः ।
रक्षितारं न मृष्यन्ति भर्तारं परमं स्त्रियः ॥ ३२॥

English
Women do not brook their husbands who may give all that they love, who may honour and console them and who may look after them well. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
न कामभोगात्परमान्नालङ्कारार्थसञ्चयात् ।
तथा हितं न मन्यन्ते यथा रतिपरिग्रहात् ॥ ३३॥

English
Women do not remain satisfied and contented so much with simple loves and pleasures and with ornaments and money as with illicit love pursued by them with other men. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तकः शमनो मृत्युः पातालं वडवामुखम् ।
क्षुरधारा विषं सर्पो वह्निरित्येकतः स्त्रियः ॥ ३४॥

English
Women can be kept equally balanced against all these put together viz., god of death, Yama, Antaka, Pātāla, the submarine fire, the sharp edge of razor, poison, serpent and fire. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
यतश्च भूतानि महान्ति पञ्च
यतश्च लोको विहितो विधात्रा ।
यतः पुमांसः प्रमदाश्च निर्मिताः
सदैव दोषः प्रमदासु नारद ॥ ३५॥

English
Ever since the five elements, the world, the men and women were created by Brahmā, O Nārada, the defect lies in women always. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्या नारदस्तुष्टमानसः ।
तथ्यं मत्वा ततस्तद्वै विरक्तोऽभूद्धि तासु च ॥ ३६॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:— On hearing her words Nārada was satisfied in his mind. Considering it to be the truth he became disinterested in them. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तः स्त्रीस्वभावस्ते पञ्चचूडोक्त आदरात् ।
वैराग्यकारणं व्यास किमन्यच्छ्रोतुमर्हसि ॥ ३७॥

English
O Vyāsa, thus the nature of women as mentioned by Pañcacūḍā has been narrated to you. What other cause of detachment do you wish to hear? (37.)
