### **CHAPTER TWENTYSIX - (The deception or dodging of Kāla)**

---

Sanskrit
षड्विंशोऽध्यायः । कालवञ्चनवर्णनम् ।

देव्युवाच ।
कथितं तु त्वया देव कालज्ञानं यथार्थतः ।
कालस्य वञ्चनं ब्रूहि यथा तत्त्वेन योगिनः ॥ १॥

English
The goddess said:—  O lord, the perfect knowledge of Kāla has been mentioned by you. Now please mention the dodging of Kāla by a Yogin, factually. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कालस्तु सन्निकृष्टो हि वर्तते सर्वजन्तुषु ।
यथा चास्य न मृत्युश्च वञ्चते कालमागतम् ॥ २॥

तथा कथय मे देव प्रीतिं कृत्वा ममोपरि ।
योगिनां च हिताय त्वं ब्रूहि सर्वसुखप्रद ॥ ३॥

English
Kāla follows upon the track of all creatures. O lord, now taking pity on me, please mention how the Kāla that has come does not deceive. O bestower of happiness, please mention it, for the benefit of Yogins. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि प्रवक्ष्यामि पृष्टोऽहं यत्त्वया शिवे ।
समासेन च सर्वेषां मानुषाणां हितार्थतः ॥ ४॥

English
Śiva said:—  O Goddess Śiva listen. I shall explain briefly what I have been asked by you for the benefit of all men. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यापस्तथा तेजो वायुराकाशमेव च ।
एतेषां हि समायोगः शरीरं पाञ्चभौतिकम् ॥ ५॥

English
The body is a conglomeration of earth, water, fire, wind and ether. It consists of five elements. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशस्तु ततो व्यापी सर्वेषां सर्वगः स्थितः ।
आकाशे तु विलीयन्ते सम्भवन्ति पुनस्ततः ॥ ६॥

English
The ether is all-pervasive. The elements merge in ether and are reborn therefrom. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
वियोगे तु सदा कस्य स्वं धाम प्रतिपेदिरे ।
तस्या न स्थिरता चास्ति सन्निपातस्य सुन्दरि ॥ ७॥

English
When the ether is disintegrated the elements resort to their causes. O beautiful woman, there is no stability for this conglomeration. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानिनोऽपि तथा तत्र तपोमन्त्रबलादपि ।
ते सर्वे सुविजानन्ति सर्वमेतन्न संशयः ॥ ८॥

English
There is no doubt that the wise know all this perfectly through the power of austerity and mantras. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
खं तेन यन्नश्यति घोररूपः
कालः करालस्त्रिदिवैकनाथः ।
दग्धस्त्वया त्वं पुनरेव तुष्टः
स्तोत्रैः स्तुतः स्वां प्रकृतिं स लेभे ॥ ९॥

English
The goddess said:—  Kāla of terrible form, the awful sole lord of the gods, perishes while taking them to the Ether. He was burnt by you. When eulogised you were pleased and then you revived him to life. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वया स चोक्तः कथया जनाना-
मदृष्टरूपः प्रचरिष्यसीति ।
दृष्टस्त्वया तत्र महाप्रभावः
प्रभोर्वरात्ते पुनरुत्थितश्च ॥ १०॥

English
Kāla was addressed by you. "In the talk of the people you will be moving about in the invisible form." He of great power was seen by you there. By means of the boon granted by you he was reborn. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तदद्य भोः काल इहास्थि किञ्चित्
निहन्यते येन वदस्व तन्मे ।
त्वं योगिवर्यः प्रभुरात्मतन्त्रः
परोपकारात्ततनुर्महेश ॥ ११॥

English
Hence there is Kāla here. Something is killed by him. Please mention that to me. You are the foremost among Yogins. You are self-controlled lord. O lord Śiva, you take up physical bodies for helping others. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
न हन्यते देववरैस्तु दैत्यैः
सयक्षरक्षोरगमानुषैश्च ।
ये योगिनो ध्यानपराः सदेहा
भवन्ति ते घ्नन्ति सुखेन कालम् ॥ १२॥

English
Śiva said:—  Kāla is not killed by the leading gods, Daityas, Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, serpents and human beings. But those embodied beings who are Yogins engrossed in meditation can easily kill Kāla. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
एतच्छ्रुत्वा त्रिभुवनगुरोः प्राह गौरी विहस्य
सत्यं त्वं मे वद कथमसौ हन्यते येन कालः ।
शम्भुस्तामाह सद्यो हिमकरवदने योगिनो ये क्षिपन्ति
कालव्यालं सकलमनघास्तच्छृणुष्वैकचित्ताः ॥ १३॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  On hearing this from Śiva, the preceptor of the three worlds, Gauri laughed and said "Please tell me the truth. How is this Kāla killed? By whom is it killed? Śiva told her immediately "O moon-faced lady, those who are sinless Yogins hurl the serpent Kāla entirely. Listen to that with concentration." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
पञ्चभूतात्मको देहः सदा युक्तस्तु तद्गुणैः ।
उत्पाद्यते वरारोहे तद्विलीनो हि पार्थिवः ॥ १४॥

English
Śiva said:—  O beautiful lady, the physical body consists of five elements. It arises from them and merges in them. It is always endowed with their attributes. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशाज्जायते वायुर्वायोस्तेजश्च जायते ।
तेजसोऽम्बु विनिर्द्दिष्टं तस्माद्धि पृथिवी भवेत् ॥ १५॥

English
From the ether the wind is produced. From the wind, fire is generated. Water is said to be produced from fire and from it the earth comes out. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यादीनि भूतानि गच्छन्ति क्रमशः परम् ।
धरा पञ्चगुणा प्रोक्ता ह्यापश्चैव चतुर्गुणाः ॥ १६॥

त्रिगुणं च तथा तेजो वायुर्द्विगुण एव च ।
शब्दैकगुणमाकाशं पृथिव्यादिषु कीर्तितम् ॥ १७॥

English
Each of the elements merges into the other in order. Earth has five attributes. Water has four. Fire has three. Wind has only two. Ether has only one. The attributes of the earth and other elements can be mentioned thus. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
शब्दः स्पर्शश्च रूपं च रसो गन्धश्च पञ्चमः ।
विजहाति गुणं स्वं स्वं तदा भूतं विपद्यते ॥ १८॥

English
The five attributes are sound, touch, colour, taste and smell. When it is relieved of its attributes the element perishes. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा गुणं विगृह्णाति प्रादुर्भूतं तदुच्यते ।
एवं जानीहि देवेशि पञ्चभूतानि तत्त्वतः ॥ १९॥

English
When it takes up the attributes the element manifests itself. O goddess, know thus all the elements factually. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्धि योगिना नित्यं स्वस्वकालेंऽशजा गुणाः ।
चिन्तनीयाः प्रयत्नेन देवि कालजिगीषुणा ॥ २०॥

English
Hence O goddess, the partial attributes shall be thought of by the Yogin assiduously if he is desirous of conquering Kāla. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
कथं जेजीयते कालो योगिभिर्योगवित्प्रभो ।
ध्यानेन चाथ मन्त्रेण तत्सर्वं कथयस्व मे ॥ २१॥

English
The goddess said:—  O lord of the knowers of Yoga, how is Kāla wished to be conquered by the Yogins? Is it by meditation or incantations? Please mention everything to me. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि प्रवक्ष्यामि योगिनां हितकाम्यया ।
परज्ञानप्रकथनं न देयं यस्य कस्यचित् ॥ २२॥

English
Śiva said:—  O goddess, listen. I shall explain it for the benefit of the Yogins. The perfect knowledge shall not be imparted to anyone and everyone. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रद्दधानाय दातव्यं भक्तियुक्ताय धीमते ।
अनास्तिकाय शुद्धाय धर्मनित्याय भामिनि ॥ २३॥

English
O fair lady, it is to be imparted to the faithful, to the intelligent, to one who is endowed with devotion, to the pious who is not an atheist and to one who is always virtuous. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सुश्वासेन सुशय्यायां योगं युञ्जीत योगवित् ।
दीपं विनान्धकारे तु प्रजाः सुप्तेषु धारयेत् ॥ २४॥

English
The knower of Yoga shall practise it taking breath normally sitting on a good bed. If he practises in darkness he shall not keep any lamp. He shall practise when people are asleep. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तर्जन्या पिहितौ कर्णौ पीडयित्वा मुहूर्त्तकम् ।
तस्मात्संश्रूयते शब्दस्तुदन्वह्निसमुद्भवः ॥ २५॥

English
When the ears are closed with the index finger for a short while a kind of sound is heard as that of a blazing fire. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्ध्यातो भुक्तमेवं हि चावसन्नं क्षणादपि ।
सर्वरोगान्निहत्याशु ज्वरोपद्रवकान्बहून् ॥ २६॥

English
The practice of Yoga quells all ailments, fevers and many other distressing ills.²⁹⁶ (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्चोपलक्षयेन्नित्यमाकारं घटिकाद्वयम् ।
जित्वा मृत्युं तथा कामं स्वेच्छया पर्यटेदिह ॥ २७॥

English
He who visualises the form of death for two Ghaṭikās conquers death and lust and can roam about as he pleases.²⁹⁷ (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वज्ञः सर्वदर्शी च सर्वसिद्धिमवाप्नुयात् ।
यथा नदति खेऽब्दो हि प्रावृडद्भिः सुसंयतः ॥ २८॥

तं श्रुत्वा मुच्यते योगी सद्यः संसारबन्धनात् ।
ततः स योगिभिर्न्नित्यं सूक्ष्मात्सूक्ष्मतरो भवेत् ॥ २९॥

English
He becomes omniscient and omniseer. He attains all Siddhis. If the Yogin hears the sound resembling that of the rain-bearing cloud he becomes rid of worldly bondage immediately. He then becomes the subtlest of all Yogins. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
एष ते कथितो देवि शब्दब्रह्मविधिक्रमः ।
पलालमिव धान्यार्थी त्यजेद्बन्धमशेषतः ॥ ३०॥

English
O Goddess, thus the order and procedure of the meditation of Śabdabrahman has been narrated to you. Just as the seeker of grains eschews husks, he shall eschew bondage. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
शब्दब्रह्म त्विदं प्राप्य ये केचिदन्यकाङ्क्षिणः ।
घ्नन्ति ते मुष्टिनाकाशं कामयन्ते क्षुधां तृषाम् ॥ ३१॥

English
If after attaining the Sabdabrahman he were to aspire for other things he will be hitting the sky with his fist, he will be loving hunger and thirst. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञात्वा परमिदं ब्रह्म सुखदं मुक्तिकारणम् ।
अबाह्यमक्षरं चैव सर्वोपाधिविवर्जितम् ॥ ३२॥

English
After knowing the great Brahman, the cause of salvation, the bestower of happiness, the non-external, the imperishable, freed from all limitations, nothing else need be aspired after. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
मोहिताः कालपाशेन मृत्युपाशवशङ्गताः ।
शब्दब्रह्म न जानन्ति पापिनस्ते कुबुद्धयः ॥ ३३॥

English
Sinners of crooked intellect deluded by the noose of Kāla, fastened into death's bondage do not realise Sabda-brahman. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तावद् भ्रमन्ति संसारे यावद्धाम न विन्दते ।
विदिते तु परे तत्त्वे मुच्यते जन्मबन्धनात् ॥ ३४॥

English
One remains in the world as long as he has not obtained the supreme abode. When the great reality is understood he is released from the bondage of life. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
निद्रालस्यं महाविघ्नं जित्वा शत्रुं प्रयत्नतः ।
सुखासने स्थितो नित्यं शब्दब्रह्माभ्यसन्निति ॥ ३५॥

शतवृद्धः पुमांल्लब्ध्वा यावदायुः समभ्यसेत् ।
मृत्युञ्जयवपुस्तम्भ आरोग्यं वायुवर्द्धनम् ॥ ३६॥

English
After conquering sleep and idleness—the great enemies and the great obstacles and sitting in a comfortable posture one should practise Sabdabrahman for ever. Even if he is an old man of hundred years he will gain health and virility and the power of stopping the decay of the body and of conquering death. He shall then continue the practice as long as he is alive. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्ययो दृश्यते वृद्धे किं पुनस्तरुणे जने ।
न चोङ्कारो न मन्त्रोऽपि नैव बीजं न चाक्षरम् ॥ ३७॥

अनाहतमनुच्चार्यं शब्दब्रह्म शिवं परम् ।
ध्यायन्ते देवि सततं सुधियो यत्नतः प्रिये ॥ ३८॥

English
Proof of belief is visible even in an old man. Why not then in a youthful man? O beloved goddess, intelligent persons meditate on the great Siva assiduously forever. The Sabdabrahman cannot be uttered, cannot be struck at. It is neither Omkāra nor any mantra nor any Bija nor any syllable. (37-38.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छब्दा नव प्रोक्ताः प्राणविद्भिस्तु लक्षिताः ।
तान् प्रवक्ष्यामि यत्नेन नादसिद्धिमनुक्रमात् ॥ ३९॥

English
Hence the Sabdas are ninefold as explained by those who know the science of breath. I shall explain them assiduously in the proper order according to the Siddhi of Nada. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
घोषं कांस्यं तथा श‍ृङ्गं घण्टां वीणादिवंशजान् ।
दुन्दुभिं शङ्खशब्दं तु नवमं मेघगर्जितम् ॥ ४०॥

English
They are Ghoṣa (sound of the soft consonant) Kāmsya (sound of bell metal) Śṛṅga (sound of horn) Ghaṇṭā (bell) Vinā (lute) Vamsaja (flute) Dundubhi (drum) Sāṅkha (conch) and Meghagarjita (the rumbling thunder). (40.)

---

Sanskrit
नव शब्दान् परित्यज्य तुङ्कारं तु समभ्यसेत् ।
ध्यायन्नेवं सदा योगी पुण्यैः पापैर्न लिप्यते ॥ ४१॥

English
After eschewing all these sounds, one shall practise Tumkāra? Meditating thus for ever the Yogin is not affected by either virtues or sins. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
न श‍ृणोति यदा श‍ृण्वन्योगाभ्यासेन देविके ।
म्रियतेऽभ्यसमानस्तु योगी तिष्ठेद्दिवानिशम् ॥ ४२॥

English
O goddess, what others do not hear the Yogin hears by the practice of Yoga. While practising, if he dies, he may stay in that posture for days and nights. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादुत्पद्यते शब्दो मृत्युजित्सप्तभिर्दिनैः ।
स वै नवविधो देवि तं ब्रवीमि यथार्थतः ॥ ४३॥

English
From him the sound of conquest of death rises up in seven days. O goddess, it is of nine varieties. I shall mention it factually. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रथमं नदते घोषमात्मशुद्धिकरं परम् ।
सर्वव्याधिहरं नादं वश्याकर्षणमुत्तमम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The first sound is Ghoṣa. It purifies the soul. It dispels sickness. It is excellent. It attracts and controls. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वितीयं नदते कांस्यं स्तम्भयेत् प्राणिनां गतिम् ।
विषं भूतग्रहान् सर्वान् बध्नीयान्नात्र संशयः ॥ ४५॥

English
The second sound is Kāṁsya. It stops the movement of living beings. There is no doubt that it stops the effect of poison, evil spirits and evil planets. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तृतीयं नदते श‍ृङ्गमभिचारि नियोजयेत् ।
विद्विडुच्चाटने शत्रोर्मारणे च प्रयोजयेत् ॥ ४६॥

English
The third sound is Śṛṅga. It is employed in black magic for exorcizing enemies and killing them. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
घण्टानादं चतुर्थं तु वदते परमेश्वरः ।
आकर्षः सर्वदेवानां किं पुनर्मानुषा भुवि ॥ ४७॥

English
The fourth is Ghaṇṭānāda, so says lord Śiva. It is one that attracts the gods even. What then of human beings on this earth? (47.)

---

Sanskrit
यक्षगन्धर्वकन्याश्च तस्याकृष्टा ददन्ति हि ।
यथेप्सितां महासिद्धिं योगिने कामतोऽपि वा ॥ ४८॥

English
Attracted by him the virgins of Yakṣas and Gandharvas give him great Siddhis as desired by him or out of their own accord. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
वीणा तु पञ्चमो नादः श्रूयते योगिभिः सदा ।
तस्मादुत्पद्यते देवि दूरादर्शनमेव हि ॥ ४९॥

English
Viṇā is the fifth sound. It is heard by Yogins always. O goddess, from it arises the power of vision from a great distance. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायतो वंशनादं तु सर्वतत्त्वं प्रजायते ।
दुन्दुभिं ध्यायमानस्तु जरामृत्युविवर्जितः ॥ ५०॥

English
All principles are understood by one who meditates on Vaṁśa Nāda. One who meditates on Dundubhi is devoid of old age and death. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्खशब्देन देवेशि कामरूपं प्रपद्यते ।
योगिनो मेघनादेन न विपत्सङ्गमो भवेत् ॥ ५१॥

English
O goddess, through the conch sound he assumes any form as he wishes. By means of Megha Nāda the Yogin wards off all adversities. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्चैकमनसा नित्यं तुङ्कारं ब्रह्मरूपिणम् ।
किमसाध्यं न तस्यास्ति यथामति वरानने ॥ ५२॥

सर्वज्ञः सर्वदर्शी च कामरूपी व्रजत्यसौ ।
न विकारैः प्रयुज्येत शिव एव न संशयः ॥ ५३॥

English
O pretty lady, is there anything impossible for one who meditates with single-minded devotion the Tumkāra form of Brahman? He becomes omniscient and visualiser of all. He moves about assuming any form he wishes. He is not affected by aberrations. He is Śiva himself. There is no doubt in this. (52-53.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्ते परमेशानि शब्दब्रह्मस्वरूपकम् ।
नवधा सर्वमाख्यातं किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ५४॥

English
O goddess, thus the form of Śabdabrahman with its nine aspects has been wholly narrated to you. What else do you wish to hear? (54.)
