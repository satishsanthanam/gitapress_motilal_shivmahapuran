### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - The Chāyāpuruṣa**

---

Sanskrit
अष्टाविंशोऽध्यायः । छायापुरुषदर्शनवर्णनम् ।

देव्युवाच ।
देवदेव महादेव कथितं कालवञ्चनम् ।
शब्दब्रह्मस्वरूपं च योगलक्षणमुत्तमम् ॥ १॥

कथितं ते समासेन छायिकं ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।
विस्तरेण समाख्याहि योगिनां हितकाम्यया ॥ २॥

English
The goddess said:—  O great lord of the gods, the way of dodging Kāla has been narrated; the form of Śabdabrahman and the excellent characteristics of Yoga have been mentioned. The reference to the shadow-person has been made by you in brief. Please explain in detail with a desire for the welfare of Yogins. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
शङ्कर उवाच ।
श‍ृणु देवि प्रवक्ष्यामि छायापुरुषलक्षणम् ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा पुरुषः सम्यक्सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३॥

English
Śiva said:—  O goddess, listen. I shall explain the characteristics of the Chayapuruṣa, on knowing which man becomes released from all sins. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्यं हि पृष्ठतः कृत्वा सोमं वा वरवर्णिनि ।
शुक्लाम्बरधरः स्त्रग्वी गन्धधूपादिवासितः ॥ ४॥

संस्मरेन्मे महामन्त्रं सर्वकामफलप्रदम् ।
नवात्मकं पिण्डभूतं स्वां छायां सन्निरीक्षयेत् ॥ ५॥

English
The aspirant wears garlands and white cloth. He makes himself fragrant white scents, incense and other things. O fair lady, he then sits with his back to the sun or the moon remembering the great mantra that bestows the benefits of all desires and that consists of the nine units solidified into one. He then glances at his own shadow. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तां पुनराकाशे श्वेतवर्णस्वरूपिणीम् ।
स पश्यत्येकभावस्तु शिवं परमकारणम् ॥ ६॥

ब्रह्मप्राप्तिर्भवेत्तस्य कालविद्भिरितीरितम् ।
ब्रह्महत्यादिकैः पापैर्मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ७॥

English
If he can see the shadow in the sky in a white-coloured form and begins to meditate on Siva, the great cause with single-minded attention, he attains Brahman then. This has been mentioned by the experts. In this state he is rid of Brahminicide and other sins. (6-7.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरोहीनं यदा पश्येत्षड्भिर्मासैर्भवेत्क्षयः ।
समस्तं वाङ्मयं तस्य योगिनस्तु यथा तथा ॥ ८॥

English
If he finds the shadow-person headless, the entire power of oral expression of practising Yogin becomes eliminated within six months. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शुक्ले धर्मं विजानीयात्कृष्णे पापं विनिर्दिशेत् ।
रक्ते बन्धं विजानीयात्पीते विद्विषमादिशेत् ॥ ९॥

English
If the shadow is white, know that it is an indication of virtue; if it is black, it indicates sin; if it is red, it indicates bondage and if it is yellow it indicates an enemy. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
विवाहो बन्धुनाशः स्याद्द्वितुण्डे चैव क्षुद्भयम् ।
विकटौ नश्यते भार्या विजङ्घे धनमेव हि ॥ १०॥

English
If the shadow is devoid of arms it indicates death or ruin of kinsmen; if it is without snout it indicates the fear of hunger; if it is devoid of hips, wife dies; if it is devoid of calves it indicates wealth. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पादाभावे विदेशः स्यादित्येतत्कथितं मया ।
तद्विचार्यं प्रयत्त्नेन पुरुषेण महेश्वरि ॥ ११॥

English
If there is no foot, exile to a foreign country takes place. This has been mentioned by me. O great goddess, this shall be carefully considered by the people. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्यक्तं पुरुषं दृष्ट्वा सन्निवेश्यात्मनात्मनि ।
जपेन्नवात्मकं मन्त्रं हृदयं मे महेश्वरि ॥ १२॥

English
(Practising still further and) seeing the shadow-person perfectly and keeping it within the Atman through the Atman, O goddess, the aspirant shall repeat the nine-syllabled Mantra in the heart. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
वत्सरे विगते मन्त्री तन्नास्ति यन्न साधयेत् ।
अणिमादिगुणानष्टौ खेचरत्वं प्रपद्यते ॥ १३॥

English
If a year passes by there will be nothing that the repeater of the mantra cannot achieve. He attains the eight Siddhis, Anima and others and the ability to walk through the sky. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनरन्यत्प्रवक्ष्यामि शक्तिं ज्ञातुं दुरासदाम् ।
प्रत्यक्षं दृश्यते लोके ज्ञानिनामग्रतः स्थितम् ॥ १४॥

English
I shall mention another power which is inscrutable but is known to those possessed of perfect knowledge. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अज्ञेया लिख्यते लोके या सर्पीकृतकुण्डली ।
सा मात्रा यानसंस्थापि दृश्यते न च पठ्यते ॥ १५॥

ब्रह्माण्डमूर्ध्निगा या च स्तुता वेदैस्तु नित्यशः ।
जननी सर्वविद्यानां गुप्तविद्येति गीयते ॥ १६॥

English
An unknowable figure in the form of a curling serpent is drawn and that figure placed in the vehicle, nowhere mentioned, is seen there. That which stands uppermost in the universe and that which is eulogised by the Vedas for ever is sung as a secret Vidyā and is the mother of all Vidyās. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
खेचरा सा विनिर्दिष्टा सर्वप्राणिषु संस्थिता ।
दृश्यादृश्याचला नित्या व्यक्ताव्यक्ता सनातनी ॥ १७॥

English
It is the lore of traversing the sky. It is stationed in all living beings. It is visible and invisible. It is immovable, eternal, manifest and unmanifest. It is permanent. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अवर्णा वर्णसंयुक्ता प्रोच्यते बिन्दुमालिनी ।
तां पश्यन्सर्वदा योगी कृतकृत्योऽभिजायते ॥ १८॥

English
It is devoid of colour yet it possesses colour. It is called Bindumalini. The Yogin who sees it always remains contented. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतीर्थकृतस्नानाद्भवेद्दानस्य यत्फलम् ।
सर्वयज्ञफलं यच्च मालिन्या दर्शनात्तदा ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing Mālinī one attains the fruit of all sacrifices, the benefit of charitable gifts and the ceremonial ablutions in all holy centres. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राप्नोत्यत्र न सन्देहः सत्यं वै कथितं मया ।
सर्वतीर्थेषु यत्स्नात्वा दत्त्वा दानानि सर्वशः ॥ २०॥

सर्वेषां देवि यज्ञानां यत्फलं तल्लभेत्पुमान् ।
किं बहूक्त्या महेशानि सर्वान्कामान्समश्नुते ॥ २१॥

English
There is no doubt in this. I mention the truth to you. The man attains the fruit derivable from all sacrifices, O goddess, as well as the benefit of making charitable gifts and taking the ceremonial ablutions in all the holy centres. O goddess, of what avail is speaking much? He attains all cherished desires. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माज्ज्ञानं यथायोगमभ्यसेत्सततं बुधः ।
अभ्यासाज्जायते सिद्धिर्योगोऽभ्यासात्प्रवर्धते ॥ २२॥

English
Hence a sensible person shall cultivate this knowledge as well as Yoga. Siddhi is attained by practice. Yoga is enhanced by practice. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
संवित्तिर्लभ्यतेऽभ्यासादभ्यासान्मोक्षमश्नुते ।
अभ्यासः सततं कार्यो धीमता मोक्षकारणम् ॥ २३॥

English
Perfect realization is attained by practice. Liberation is attained by practice. An intelligent man shall always maintain practice. Practice is the cause of salvation. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येतत्कथितं देवि भुक्तिमुक्तिफलप्रदम् ।
किमन्यत्पृच्छ्यते तत्त्वं वद सत्यं ब्रवीमि ते ॥ २४॥

English
O goddess, I have mentioned that all which bestows the benefit of worldly pleasures and salvation. What other tenet do you propose? Tell me, I shall tell you the truth. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा ब्रह्मपुत्रवचनं परमार्थदम् ।
प्रसन्नोऽभूदति व्यासः पाराशर्यो मुनीश्वराः ॥ २५॥

English
*Sūta said:—* O great sages, on hearing these words of Sanatkumāra, son of Brahma, the words bestowing the knowledge of truth, Vyasa, son of Parasara, was much delighted. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमारं सर्वज्ञं ब्रह्मपुत्रं कृपानिधिम् ।
व्यासः परमसन्तुष्टः प्रणनाम मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २६॥

English
The extremely delighted Vyasa, bowed again and again to the omniscient son of Brahma, Sanatkumara, the storehouse of mercy. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तुष्टाव तं व्यासः कालेयः स मुनीश्वरः ।
सनत्कुमारं मुनयः सुरविज्ञानसागरम् ॥ २७॥

English
O sages, then the great sage Vyasa, eulogised Sanatkumāra, the ocean of the perfect knowledge of the deities. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
कृतार्थोऽहं मुनिश्रेष्ठ ब्रह्मत्वं मे त्वया कृतम् ।
नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्तेऽस्तु धन्यस्त्वं ब्रह्मवित्तमः ॥ २८॥

English
Vyāsa said:— O great sage, I am satisfied. The position of Brahmā has been assigned to me. Obeisance be to you. You are blessed. You are the most excellent of all knowers of Brahman. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इति स्तुत्वा स कालेयो ब्रह्मपुत्रं महामुनिम् ।
तूष्णीं बभूव सुप्रीतः परमानन्दनिर्भरः ॥ २९॥

English
*Sūta said:—* After eulogising the great sage the son of Brahma, Vyasa, the grandson of Brahma, kept silent. He was extremely delighted. He was filled with great bliss. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मपुत्रस्तमामन्त्र्य पूजितस्तेन शौनकः ।
ययौ स्वधाम सुप्रीतो व्यासोऽपि प्रीतमानसः ॥ ३०॥

English
O Saunaka, Sanatkumara, son of Brahma, took leave of him. Worshipped by Vyasa he returned to his abode. Vyasa too left for his abode with his mind full of delight. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
इति मे वर्णितो विप्राः सुखदः परमार्थयुक् ।
सनत्कुमारकालेयसंवादो ज्ञानवर्धनः ॥ ३१॥

English
O brahmins, thus I have described to you the conversation between Sanatkumara and Vyasa. It deals with truth. It is pleasing. It enhances knowledge. (31.)

इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे पञ्चम्यामुमासंहितायां छायापुरुषदर्शनवर्णनं नामाष्टाविंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ५.२८॥
