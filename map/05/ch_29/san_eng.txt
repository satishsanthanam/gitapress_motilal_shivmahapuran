### **CHAPTER TWENTYNINE - (The primeval creation)**

---

Sanskrit
एकोनत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । आदिसर्गवर्णनम् ।

शौनक उवाच ।
श्रुतं मे महदाख्यानं यत्त्वया परिकीर्तितम् ।
सनत्कुमारकालेयसंवादं परमार्थदम् ॥ १॥

English
Saunaka said:—  The great narrative of the conversation of Sanatkumāra and Vyāsa has been heard by me as narrated by you. It has bestowed the knowledge of truth. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अतोऽहं श्रोतुमिच्छामि यथा सर्गस्तु ब्रह्मणः ।
समुत्पन्नं तु मे ब्रूहि यथा व्यासाच्च ते श्रुतम् ॥ २॥

English
Now I wish to hear about the creation of Brahmā, the manner of its origin and the way in which you heard it from Vyāsa. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
मुने श‍ृणु कथां दिव्यां सर्वपापप्रणाशिनीम् ।
कथ्यमानां मया चित्रां बह्वर्थां श्रुतविस्तराम् ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said :—  O sage, listen to the divine story that destroys all sins; the variegated story that is being described by me; the story full of meaning and well known details. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्चैनां पाठयेत्तां च श‍ृणुयाद्वाप्यभीक्ष्णशः ।
स्ववंशधारणं कृत्वा स्वर्गलोके महीयते ॥ ४॥

English
He who teaches or hears this story repeatedly sustains his family and is honoured in the world of heaven. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधानं पुरुषो यत्तन्नित्यं सदसदात्मकम् ।
प्रधानपुरुषो भूत्वा निर्ममे लोकभावनः ॥ ५॥

स्रष्टारं सर्वभूतानां नारायणपरायणम् ।
तं वै विद्धि मुनिश्रेष्ठ ब्रह्माणममितौजसम् ॥ ६॥

English
Puruṣa is eternal and he is of the nature of Sat and Asat as Pradhāna and Puruṣa. The creator of the worlds created Pradhāna after becoming Puruṣa. He created the Creator of all living beings devoted to Nārāyaṇa. O great sage, understand him to be Brahmā of immeasurable strength and prowess. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मादकल्पयत्कल्पाः समग्राः शुचयो यतः ।
भवन्ति मुनिशार्दूल नमस्तस्मै स्वयम्भुवे ॥ ७॥

English
O leading sage, obeisance to that Self-born being from whom the Kalpas and all beings originate. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मै हिरण्यगर्भाय पुरुषायेश्वराय च ।
नमस्कृत्य प्रवक्ष्यामि भूयः सर्गमनुत्तमम् ॥ ८॥

English
After bowing to that Puruṣa, lord Hiranyagarbha, I shall explain the excellent mode of creation again. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मा स्रष्टा हरिः पाता संहर्ता च महेश्वरः ।
तस्य सर्गस्य नान्योऽस्ति काले काले तथा गते ॥ ९॥

English
Brahmā is the creator; Viṣṇu is the protector and Śiva is the annihilator. Even when the time passes there is no other cause of that creation. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि स्वयम्भूर्भगवान् सिसृक्षुर्विविधाः प्रजाः ।
अप एव ससर्जादौ तासु वीर्यमवासृजत् ॥ १०॥

English
Being self-born himself the lord, desirous of creating various subjects, created only the waters first. He then instilled virility into them.¹ (10.)

---

Sanskrit
आपो नारा इति प्रोक्ता आपो वै नरसूनवः ।
अयनं तस्य ताः पूर्वं तेन नारायणः स्मृतः ॥ ११॥

English
The waters are called Nāra (belonging to Nara).  The waters are the progenies of Nara. Since waters had been his abode he is called Nārāyaṇa.² (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यवर्णमभवत्तदण्डमुदकेशयम् ।
तत्र जज्ञे स्वयं ब्रह्मा स्वयम्भूरिति विश्रुतः ॥ १२॥

English
The Egg floating in the water assumed golden colour. Brahma himself famous as the self-born was born there.3 (12.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यगर्भो भगवानुषित्वा परिवत्सरम् ।
तदण्डमकरोद् द्वैधं दिवं भूमिं च निर्ममे ॥ १३॥

English
After staying there for a year, lord Hiranyagarbha, dichotomised that egg and created heaven and earth.4 (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अधोऽधोर्ध्वं प्रयुक्तानि भुवनानि चतुर्द्दश ।
तयोः शकलयोर्मध्य आकाशममृजत्प्रभुः ॥ १४॥

English
Fourteen worlds were created including those above and below. With the intervening space between those two pieces the lord created the ether.5 (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अप्सु परिप्लवां पृथ्वीं दिशश्च दशधा दधे ।
तत्र काले मनो वाचं कामक्रोधावथो रतिम् ॥ १५॥

English
He created the earth floating in the waters and the ten quarters in the firmament. Then he created the mind, the speech, love and anger and the sexual delight. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मरीचिमत्र्यङ्गिरसौ पुलस्त्यं पुलहं क्रतुम् ।
वसिष्ठं तु महातेजाः सोऽसृजत्सप्त मानसान् ॥ १६॥

English
The excessively brilliant creator created seven mental sons, the sages, Marici, Atri, Angiras, Pulastya, Pulaha, Kratu and Vasiṣṭha.6 (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्त बह्माण इत्येते पुराणे निश्चयं गताः ।
ततोऽसृजत्पुनर्ब्रह्मा रुद्रान् क्रोधसमुद्भवान् ॥ १७॥

English
They have been decisively termed the seven Brahmās in the Purānas. Brahma again created the Rudras arising from anger. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमारं च ऋषिं सर्वेषामपि पूर्वजम् ।
सप्त चैते प्रजायन्ते पश्चाद्रुद्राश्च सर्वतः ॥ १८॥

English
He created the sage Sanatkumāra too, the eldest of all. They are thus seven born. The Rudras were born afterwards. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः सनत्कुमारस्तु तेजः सङ्क्षिप्य तिष्ठति ।
तेषां सप्त महावंशा दिव्या देवर्षिपूजिताः ॥ १९॥

English
Sanatkumara stays with controlled brilliance. From them seven great families originated, all being divine and worshipped by the celestial sages. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजायन्ते क्रियावन्तो महर्षिभिरलङ्कृताः ।
विद्युतोऽशनिमेघांश्च रोहितेन्द्रधनूंषि च ॥ २०॥

English
Endowed with sacred rites they were born along with the other Sages. The lightning, the thunderbolt, the red clouds and the rainbow were created. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पयांसि च ससर्जादौ पर्जन्यं च ससर्ज ह ।
ऋचो यजूंषि सामानि निर्ममे यज्ञसिद्धये ॥ २१॥

English
He created water at the outset. He created the rain-bearing cloud. He created the Rks, the Yajus and the Samans for facilitating Yajnas.7 (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पूज्यांस्तैरयजन्देवानित्येवमनुशुश्रुम ।
मुखाद्देवानजनयत्पितॄंश्चैवाथ वक्षसः ।
प्रजनाच्च मनुष्यान्वै जघनान्निर्ममेऽसुरान् ॥ २२॥

English
With these he worshipped the gods worthy of being adored. This is what we had heard. He created the gods from his mouth, the manes from the chest, men through impregnation and the Asuras from his loins. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
उच्चावचानि भूतानि गात्रेभ्यस्तस्य जज्ञिरे ।
आपवस्य प्रजासर्गं सृजतो हि प्रजापतेः ॥ २३॥

English
Living beings high and low were born of his limbs as the patriarch Apava generated the creation of the aquatic beings. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सृज्यमानाः प्रजाश्चैव नावर्धन्त यदा तदा ।
द्विधा कृत्वात्मनो देहं स्त्री चैव पुरुषोऽभवत् ॥ २४॥

English
When the beings created did not increase he dichotomised his body and became man and woman.
25 With his greatness he persuaded the universe and created the people. Vişnu created the Virat (the cosmic being). Thus the Puruşa Virat was created. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वितीयं तं मनुं विद्धि मनोरन्तरमेव च ।
स वैराजः प्रजाः सर्वाः ससर्ज पुरुषः प्रभुः ॥ २६॥

English
Know that he was another Manu that began the age. That lord, Puruşa, created all the subjects out of Vairaja. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणविसर्गस्य प्रजास्तस्याप्ययोनिजः ।
आयुष्मान् कीर्तिमान्धन्यः प्रजावांश्चाभवत्ततः ॥ २७॥

English
The creations of Vişnu were not born of womb. They were long-lived, famous, blessed and possessed of progeny. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्येवमादिसर्गस्ते वर्णितो मुनिसत्तम ।
आदिसर्गं विदित्वैवं यथेष्टां प्राप्नुयाद्गतिम् ॥ २८॥

English
O excellent sage, thus the premeval creation has been narrated to you on knowing which one attains his cherished goal. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
ससृजेऽथ प्रजाः सर्वा महिम्ना व्याप्य विश्वतः ।
विराजमसृजद्विष्णुः स सृष्टः पुरुषं विराट् ॥ २५॥

---
