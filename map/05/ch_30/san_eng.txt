### **CHAPTER THIRTY - (Description of Creation)**

---

Sanskrit
त्रिंशोऽध्यायः । सर्गवर्णनम् १ ।

सूत उवाच ।
संसृष्टासु प्रजास्वेव आपवोऽथ प्रजापतिः ।
लेभे वै पुरुषः पत्नीं शतरूपामयोनिजाम् ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said:—  When the subjects were created the patriarch Apava as Puruşa acquired as his wife Satarūpā who was not born of womb.[10] (1.)

---

Sanskrit
आपवस्य महिम्ना तु दिवमावृत्य तिष्ठतः ।
धर्मेणैव महात्मा स शतरूपाप्यजायत ॥ २॥

English
With his greatness Apava enveloped the heaven and stood virtuously. He the noble soul became Satarūpā thus. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सा तु वर्षशतं तप्त्वा तपः परमदुश्चरम् ।
भर्तारं दीप्ततपसं पुरुषं प्रत्यपद्यत ॥ ३॥

English
She performed a penance for a hundred years and attained the Puruşa of illumined penance as her husband. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स वै स्वायम्भुवो जज्ञे पुरुषो मनुरुच्यते ।
तस्यैकसप्ततियुगं मन्वन्तरमिहोच्यते ॥ ४॥

English
The Puruşa who was born is called Svāyambhuva Manu. His age is said to be seventyone Yugas. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वैराजात्पुरुषाद्वीरा शतरूपा व्यजायत ।
प्रियव्रतोत्तानपादौ वीरकायामजायताम् ॥ ५॥

English
The intelligent Śatarūpā bore the Vairāja Puruṣa two sons Priyavrata and Uttānapāda of heroic prowess. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
काम्या नाम महाभागा कर्दमस्य प्रजापतेः ।
काम्यापुत्रास्त्रयस्त्वासन्संराट्साक्षिरविट्प्रभुः ॥ ६॥

English
The blessed lady Kāmyā, wife of the patriarch Kardama, gave birth to three sons Samrāṭ, Sākṣi, and Avitprabhu. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तानपादोऽजनयत्पुत्रान् शक्रसमान् प्रभुः ।
ध्रुवं च तनयं दिव्यमात्मानन्दसुवर्चसम् ॥ ७॥

English
Lord Uttānapāda begot sons equal to Indra. He begot another son Dhruva of divine nature who had the excellent lustre and the inner bliss. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मस्य कन्या सुश्रोणी सुनीतिर्नाम विश्रुता ।
उत्पन्ना चापि धर्मेण ध्रुवस्य जननी तथा ॥ ८॥

English
Dharma’s daughter, the virtuously born beautiful woman named Sunīti was the mother of Dhruva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्रुवो वर्षसहस्राणि त्रीणि दिव्यानि कानने ।
तपस्तेपे स बालस्तु प्रार्थयन्स्थानमव्ययम् ॥ ९॥

English
Even as a child Dhruva performed penance for three thousand divine years wishing for an imperishable status. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मै ब्रह्मा ददौ प्रीतः स्थानमात्मसमं प्रभुः ।
अचलं चैव पुरतः सप्तर्षीणां प्रजापतिः ॥ १०॥

English
Lord Brahmā, being delighted gave him a stable position as his own, facing the seven sages. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात् पुष्टिश्च धान्यश्च ध्रुवात्पुत्रौ व्यजायताम् ।
पुष्टिरेवं समुत्थायाः पञ्चपुत्रानकल्मषान् ॥ ११॥

रिपुं रिपुञ्जयं विप्रं वृकलं वृषतेजसम् ।
रिपोरेवं च महिषी चाक्षुषं सर्वतोदिशम् ॥ १२॥

English
Two sons, Puṣṭi and Dhānya were born of Dhruva. Puṣṭi begot of Samutthā five pious sons viz Ripu, Ripuñjaya, Vipra, Vṛkala and Vṛṣatejas. The queen of Ripu gave birth to Cākṣuṣa Manu. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
अजीजनत्पुष्करिण्यां वरुणं चाक्षुषो मनुः ।
मनोरजायन्त दश नड्वलायां महौजसः ॥ १३॥

कन्यायां हि मुनिश्रेष्ठ वैराजस्य प्रजापतेः ।
पुरुर्मासः शतद्युम्नस्तपस्वी सत्यवित्कविः ॥ १४॥

अग्निष्टोमोऽतिरात्रश्चातिमन्युः सुयशा दश ।
पूरोरजनयत्पुत्रान् षडाग्नेयी महाप्रभान् ॥ १५॥

अङ्गं सुमनसं ख्यातिं सृतिमङ्गिरसं गयम् ।
अङ्गात्सुनीथा भार्या वै वेनमेकमसूयत ॥ १६॥

English
Cākṣuṣa Manu begot Varuṇa of Puṣkariṇī. O excellent sage, from Manu of great prowess, in Naḍalā the daughter of the patriarch Vaisyajanma (?) were born ten sons viz Puru, Māsa, Satadyumna, Tapasvin, Satyavit, Kavi, Agniṣṭoma, Atirātra, Atimanyu and Suyasas. To Puru, Āgneyi bore six sons of great lustre viz Aṅga, Sumanas, Khyāti, Sṛti, Aṅgiras and Gaya. Sunithā, wife of Aṅga, bore him a son Vena. (13-16.)

---

Sanskrit
अपचारेण वेनस्य कोपस्तेषां महानभूत् ।
हुङ्कारेणैव तं जघ्नुर्मुनयो धर्मतत्पराः ॥ १७॥

English
Due to the misbehaviour of Vena the pious sages became very furious and killed him by very huṅkāra.$^{11}$ (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ प्रजार्थमृषयः प्रार्थिताश्च सुनीथया ।
सारस्वतास्तदा तस्य ममन्थुर्दक्षिणं करम् ॥ १८॥

English
Sārasvata Sages were requested by Sunithā for the birth of a son. They then churned and pressed his right hand. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
वेनस्य पाणौ मथिते सम्बभूव ततः पृथुः ।
स धन्वी कवची जातस्तेजसादित्यसन्निभः ॥ १९॥

English
When the hand of Vena was churned Pṛthu was born. He had a bow and a coat of mail even as he was born and was equal to the sun in brilliance. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अवतारः स विष्णोर्हि प्रजापालनहेतवे ।
धर्मसंरक्षणार्थाय दुष्टानां दण्डहेतवे ॥ २०॥

English
He was indeed an incarnation of Viṣṇu, for protecting the subjects, for the maintenance of virtue and for punishing the wicked. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथुर्वैन्यस्तदा पृथ्वीमरक्षत्क्षत्रपूर्वजः ।
राजसूयाभिषिक्तानामाद्यः स वसुधापतिः ॥ २१॥

English
Pṛthu, son of Vena, the ancestor of all Kṣatriyas protected the earth. He was the first lord of Earth, the first among those anointed in the Rājasūya sacrifice. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्चैव समुत्पन्नौ निपुणौ सूतमागधौ ।
तेनेयं गौर्मुनिश्रेष्ठ दुग्धा सर्वहिताय वै ॥ २२॥

English
The two efficient persons Sūta and Māgadha were born of him. O great Sage, by him the earth in the form of cow was milked for the common welfare. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषां वृत्तिदश्चाभूद्देवर्षिसुररक्षसाम् ।
मनुष्याणां विशेषेण शतयज्ञकरो नृपः ॥ २३॥

English
Performing a hundred sacrifices he as a king become the provider of livelihood to all the gods, sages and Rākṣasas and to men in particular. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथोः पुत्रौ तु जज्ञाते धर्मज्ञौ भुवि पार्थिवौ ।
विजिताश्वश्च हर्यक्षो महावीरौ सुविश्रुतौ ॥ २४॥

English
Two virtuous princes, Vivitāsva and Haryakṣa were born to Pṛthu. They were great heroes who had become very famous. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शिखण्डिनी चाजनयत्पुत्रं प्राचीनबर्हिषम् ।
प्राचीनाग्राः कुशास्तस्य पृथिवीतलचारिणः ॥ २५॥

English
Śikhaṇḍini gave birth to a son Prācinabarhis. While he roamed on the earth Kuśa grasses had their tips towards the East. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्रतनया तेन धर्मतः सुविवाहिता ।
रेजेऽधिकतरं राजा कृतदारो महाप्रभुः ॥ २६॥

English
The daughter of the lord of the Ocean was duly married by him. The great lord, shone all the more when he had a wife. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्रतनयायास्तु दश प्राचीनबर्हिषः ।
बभूवुस्तनया दिव्या बहुयज्ञकरस्य वै ॥ २७॥

English
Ten sons were born to Prācinabarhis the great sacrificer, of his wife Samudratanayā. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वे प्राचेतसा नाम्ना धनुर्वेदस्य पारगाः ।
अपृथग्धर्माचरणास्तेऽतप्यन्त महत्तपः ॥ २८॥

English
They were Prācetas by name. They had mastered the science of archery. Together they practised virtue and performed penance. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

रुद्रगीतं जपन्तश्च शिवध्यानपरायणाः ॥ २९॥

English
They lay sunk in the waters of the ocean for ten thousand years, repeating the Japa of Rudragita and meditating on Śiva. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तपश्चरत्सु पृथ्व्यामभवंश्च महीरुहाः ।
अरक्ष्यमाणायां पृथ्व्यां बभूवाथ प्रजाक्षयः ॥ ३०॥

English
While they were performing penance trees grew on the earth. When the Earth stood unprotected there was a great destruction of subjects. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तान् दृष्ट्वा तु निवृत्तास्ते तपसो लब्धसद्वराः ।
चुक्रुधुर्मुनिशार्दूल दग्धुकामास्तपोबलाः ॥ ३१॥

English
O great king, after attaining boons when they returned they saw the trees. They were furious and wanted to burn them by the power of penance. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

वृक्षानुन्मूल्य वायुस्तानदहद्धव्यवाहनः ॥ ३२॥

English
The Prācetasas created fire and wind from their mouths. The wind uprooted the trees and the fire burnt them. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

उपगम्याब्रवीदेतान् राजा सोमः प्रतापवान् ॥ ३३॥

English
On seeing the destruction of trees and observing that only a few trees were left, the valorous king Soma approached them and said. (33.)

---

Sanskrit

कोपं यच्छत राजानः सर्वे प्राचीनबर्हिषः ।
अनुभूतानुकन्येयं वृक्षाणां वरवर्णिनी ॥ ३४॥

English
Soma said :—  O Prācinabarhiṣas, O kings, subdue your anger. This daughter of the trees, Anubhūtā, is a woman of fair complexion. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

भार्या वोऽस्तु महाभागाः सोमवंशविवर्धिनी ॥ ३५॥

English
O fortunate ones, she was conceived by me in my womb as I knew the future. Let her be your wife and make the race of the moon flourish. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The patriarch Dakṣa will be born of her. He, the ancient son of Brahmā, will become a creator of great brilliance. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
With half your brilliance, and with half my brilliance this king full of Brahmā's brilliance will make the subjects flourish. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Then at the instance of Soma the Pracetasas lovingly took the fair-complexioned lady, daughter of trees, as their wife. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
इमां विसृष्टिं दक्षस्य यो विद्यात्सचराचराम् ।
प्रजावानायुषा पूर्णः स्वर्गलोके महीयते ॥ ५४॥

English
He who knows this mode of creation of Dakṣa of the mobile and immobile beings becomes endowed with progeny and longevity. After death he is honoured in the heavenly region. (54.)
