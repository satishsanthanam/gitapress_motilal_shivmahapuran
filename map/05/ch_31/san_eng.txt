### **CHAPTER THIRTYONE - (Description of the Creation)**

---

Sanskrit
एकत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । सर्गवर्णनम् २ ।

शौनक उवाच ।
देवानां दानवानां च गन्धर्वोरगरक्षसाम् ।
सृष्टिं तु विस्तरेणेमां सूतपुत्र वदाशु मे ॥ १॥

English
Śaunaka said:—  O son of Sūta, tell me quickly about the details of the creation of the gods, Dānavas, Gandharvas, serpents and Rākṣasas. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
यदा न ववृधे सा तु वीरणस्य प्रजापतिः ।
सुतां सुतपसा युक्तामाह्वयत्सर्गकारणात् ॥ २॥

English
Sūta said:—  When the creation did not multiply, the patriarch asked the daughter of Vīraṇa, endowed with penance, for the purpose of creation (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स मैथुनेन धर्मेण ससर्ज विविधाः प्रजाः ।
ताः श‍ृणु त्वं महाप्राज्ञ कथयामि समासतः ॥ ३॥

English
He created different people by the process of copulation. O intelligent one, listen to them. I shall explain in brief. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यां पुत्रसहस्राणि वीरिण्यां पञ्च वीर्यवान् ।
आश्रित्य जनयामास दक्ष एव प्रजापतिः ॥ ४॥

English
Uniting with her, the powerful patriarch begot of Vīriṇī five thousand sons. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एतान् सृष्टांस्तु तान्दृष्ट्वा नारदः प्राह वै मुनिः ।
सत्रे स तु समुत्पन्नो नारदः परमेष्ठिनः ॥ ५॥

श्रुतवान्वा कश्यपाद्वै पुंसां सृष्टिर्भविष्यति ।
दक्षस्यैव दुहितृषु तस्मात्तानब्रवीत्तु सः ॥ ६॥

English
Nārada, son of Brahmā, had heard at the Satra that the creation can take place only through Kaśyapa in the daughters of Dakṣa. So, on seeing the created sons, the sage Nārada addressed them thus. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
अजानन्तः कथं सृष्टिं बालिशा वै करिष्यथ ।
दिशं काञ्चिदजानन्तस्तस्माद्विज्ञाय तां भुवम् ॥ ७॥

English
“You do not know any direction in the world. Without knowing that what can you create ? You are very childish. Proceed only after knowing the world.” (7.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ताः प्रययुः सर्वे आशां विज्ञातुमोजसा ।
तदन्तं न हि सम्प्राप्य न निवृत्ताः पितुर्गृहम् ॥ ८॥

English
Thus addressed by him they all proceeded to know the direction by means of their power. Without being able to reach their end they never returned to their father’s house. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तज्ज्ञात्वा जनयामास पुनः पञ्चशतान् सुतान् ।
तानुवाच पुनः सोऽपि नारदः सर्वदर्शनः ॥ ९॥

English
On knowing that he created five hundred sons, Nārada, the visualiser of all, spoke to them also. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
नारद उवाच ।
भुवो मानमजानन्तः कथं सृष्टिं करिष्यथ ।
सर्वे हि बालिशाः किं हि सृष्टिकर्तुं समुद्यताः ॥ १०॥

English
Nārada said:—  How can you create without knowing the extent of the world ? All of you are childish. What creation do you propose to make ? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तेऽपि तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा निर्याताः सर्वतोदिशम् ।
सुबलाश्वा दक्षसुता हर्यश्वा इव ते पुरा ॥ ११॥

English
Sūta said:—  On hearing his words, Subalāśvas, sons of Dakṣa, as did the Haryaśvas formerly, started in quest of all directions. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अनन्तं पुष्करं प्राप्य गतास्तेऽपि पराभवम् ।
अद्यापि न निवर्तन्ते समुद्रेभ्य इवापगाः ॥ १२॥

English
After reaching Puṣkara and attaining despair due to the endlessness of the world they do not return even now at the rivers from the ocean. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाप्रभृति वै भ्राता भ्रातुरन्वेषणे रतः ।
प्रयातो नश्यति मुने तन्न कार्यं विपश्चिता ॥ १३॥

English
Ever since, O sage, if a brother goes in quest of a brother he perishes. That should not be done by any learned man. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तांश्चापि नष्टान् विज्ञाय पुत्रान्दक्षः प्रजापतिः ।
स च क्रोधाद्ददौ शापं नारदाय महात्मने ॥ १४॥

English
Realising that his sons had perished, the patriarch Dakṣa cursed the noble soul Nārada in anger. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कुत्रचिन्न लभस्वेति संस्थितिं कलहप्रिय ।
तव सान्निध्यतो लोके भवेच्च कलहः सदा ॥ १५॥

English
"O wretch, fond of quarrels, you will never stay anywhere permanently. Quarrel will always occur among the people where you happen to be present." (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सान्त्वितोऽथ विधात्रा हि स दक्षस्तु प्रजापतिः ।
कन्याः षष्ट्यसृजत्पश्चाद्वीरिण्यामिति नः श्रुतम् ॥ १६॥

English
We have heard that Dakṣa the patriarch was consoled by Brahmā afterwards and that he begot of Vīriṇī sixty daughters.¹³ (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ददौ स दश धर्माय कश्यपाय त्रयोदश ।
सप्तविंशति सोमाय चतस्रोऽरिष्टनेमिने ॥ १७॥

English
He gave ten of them to Dharma; thirteen to Kaśyapa; twentyseven to the moon and four to Ariṣṭanemi. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वे चैवं ब्रह्मपुत्राय द्वे चैवाङ्गिरसे तदा ।
द्वे कृशाश्वाय विदुषे तासां नामानि मे श‍ृणु ॥ १८॥

English
Two to the son of Brahmā, two to Aṅgiras and two to the learned Kṛśāśva. Now listen to their names. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अरुन्धती वसुर्यामिर्लम्बा भानुर्मरुत्वती ।
सङ्कल्पा च मुहूर्ता च सन्ध्या विश्वा च वै मुने ॥ १९॥

धर्मपत्न्यो मुने त्वेतास्तास्वपत्यानि मे श‍ृणु ।
विश्वेदेवास्तु विश्वायाः साध्यान्साध्या व्यजायत ॥ २०॥

English
O sage, the wives of Dharma are Arundhaṭī, Vasu, Yāmi, Lambā, Bhānu, Marutvatī, Saṅkalpā, Muhūrtā, Sādhyā and Viśvā. Now listen to their children. Viśvedevas are the sons of Viśvā, and Sādhyā gave birth to Sādhyas. (19-20.)

---

Sanskrit
मरुत्वत्यां मरुत्वन्तो वसोस्तु वसवस्तथा ।
भानोस्तु भानवः सर्वे मुहूर्तायां मुहूर्तजाः ॥ २१॥

English
Marutvats were born of Marutvatī, Vasus were born of Vasu; the suns were born of Bhānu and the Muhūrtajas were born of Muhūrtā. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
लम्बायाश्चैव घोषोऽथ नागवीथी च यामिजा ।
पृथिवीविषमस्तस्यामरुन्धत्यामजायत ॥ २२॥

English
Ghoṣa was born of Lambā; Nāgavīthī was born of Yāmi and Pṛthivīviṣama was born of Arundhaṭī. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्कल्पायास्तु सत्यात्मा जज्ञे सङ्कल्प एव हि ।
अयादयो वसोः पुत्रा अष्टौ तान् श‍ृणु शौनक ॥ २३॥

English
Saṅkalpa of the nature of Truth was born of Saṅkalpā. Aya and others were born of Vasu. They are eight. O Śaunaka, listen to them. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अयो धुवश्च सोमश्च धरश्चैवानिलोऽनलः ।
प्रत्यूषश्च प्रभासश्च वसवाऽष्टौ च नामतः ॥ २४॥

English
Aya, Dhruva, Soma, Dhara, Anila, Anala, Pratyūṣa and Prabhāsa are the eight Vasus by name. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अयस्य पुत्रो वैतण्डः श्रमः शान्तो मुनिस्तथा ।
ध्रुवस्य पुत्रो भगवान् कालो लोकभावनः ॥ २५॥

English
Aya's sons are Vaitanda, Śrama, Śānta and Muni. Dhruva's son was lord Kāla influencing the worlds. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सोमस्य भगवान् वर्चा वर्चस्वी येन जायते ।
धरस्य पुत्रो द्रविणो हुतहव्यवहस्तथा ॥ २६॥

English
Soma's son was lord Varcas imparting lustre to others. Dhara's sons were Draviṇa and Hutahavyavaha. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मनोहरायाः शिशिरः प्राणोऽथ रमणस्तथा ।
अनिलस्य शिवा भार्या यस्याः पुत्राः पुरोजवः ॥ २७॥

अविज्ञातगतिश्चैव द्वौ पुत्रावनिलस्य तु ।
अग्निपुत्रः कुमारस्तु शरस्तम्बे श्रियावृते ॥ २८॥

English
Manoharā's sons were Śaśira, Prāṇa and Ramaṇa. Anila's wife was Śivā whose sons were Purojava and Avijñātagati. Anila had only two sons. Kumāra was the son of Agni in the grove of Śara grass surrounded by glory. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य शाखो विशाखश्च नैगमेयश्च पृष्ठतः ।
अपत्यं कृत्तिकानां तु कार्तिकेय इति स्मृतः ॥ २९॥

English
His sons were Sākha, Viśākha and Naigameya. The son of Kṛttikās was called Kārttikeya.¹⁶ (29.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्यूषस्य त्वभूत्पुत्र ऋषिर्नाम्ना तु देवलः ।
द्वौ पुत्रौ देवलस्यापि प्रजावन्तौ मनीषिणौ ॥ ३०॥

English
Pratyūṣa's son was Devala a sage. Devala had two sons who too had children and who were intelligent. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
बृहस्पतेस्तु भगिनी वरस्त्री ब्रह्मचारिणी ।
योगसिद्धा जगत्कृत्स्नं समन्ताद्व्यचरत्तदा ॥ ३१॥

प्रभासस्य तु सा भार्या वसूनामष्टमस्य च ।
विश्वकर्मा महाभाग तस्य जज्ञे प्रजापतिः ॥ ३२॥

English
Bṛhaspati's sister was a woman of excellence, well-versed in Yoga and she traversed the entire world maintaining celibacy. She became the wife of Prabhāsa the eighth of Vasus. O fortunate one, Viśvakarman the patriarch was born of him.¹⁷ (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्ता शिल्पसहस्राणां त्रिदशानां च वार्धकिः ।
भूषणानां च सर्वेषां कर्ता शिल्पवतां वरः ॥ ३३॥

English
He was the creator of thousands of skilled arts. He is the architect of the gods. He was maker of all sorts of ornaments. He was the foremost among artisans and craftsmen. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यः सर्वासां विमानानि देवतानां चकार ह ।
मनुष्याश्चोपजीवन्ति यस्य शिल्पं महात्मनः ॥ ३४॥

English
He built the aerial chariots of all gods. He is a great Ātman whose craft is emulated and made use of by men too. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
रैवतोऽजो भवो भीमो वाम उग्रो वृषाकपिः ।
अजैकपादहिर्बुध्न्यो बहुरूपो महानिति ॥ ३५॥

सरूपायां प्रसूतस्य स्त्रियां रुद्रश्च कोटिशः ।
तत्रैकादशमुख्यास्तु तन्नामानि मुने श‍ृणु ॥ ३६॥

English
According to another version his wife Sarūpā gave birth to crores of Rudras such as Raivata, Aja, Bhava, Bhima, Vāma, Ugra, Vṛṣākapi, Ajaikapād, Ahirbudhnya, Bahurūpa, Mahat etc. Eleven of them are important. O sage, listen to their names. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
अजैकपादहिर्बुध्न्यस्त्वष्टा रुद्रश्च वीर्यवान् ।
हरश्च बहुरूपश्च त्र्यम्बकश्चापराजितः ॥ ३७॥

वृषाकपिश्च शम्भुश्च कपर्दी रैवतस्तथा ।
एकादशैते कथिता रुद्रास्त्रिभुवनेश्वराः ॥ ३८॥

English
These eleven are the Rudras, lords of the three worlds viz:—Ajaikapād, Ahirbudhnya, Tvaṣṭr, the powerful Rudrahara of many forms, Tryambaka, Aparājita, Vṛṣākapi, Śambhu, Kapardin and Raivata.$^{18}$ (37-38.)

---

Sanskrit
शतं त्वेवं समाख्यातं रुद्राणाममितौजसाम् ।
श‍ृणु कश्यपपत्नीनां नामानि मुनिसत्तम ॥ ३९॥

English
Thus a hundred Rudras of immense prowess have been mentioned to you. Now listen to the names of Kaśyapa’s wives, O excellent sage. (39.)
