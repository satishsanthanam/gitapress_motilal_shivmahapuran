### **CHAPTER THIRTY TWO - *(The family of Kaśyapa)***

---

Sanskrit
५.३२. द्वात्रिंशोऽध्यायः । कश्यपवंशवर्णनम् १ ।

सूत उवाच ।
अदितिर्दितिश्च सुरसारिष्टेला दनुरेव च ।
सुरभिर्विनता चैव ताम्रा क्रोधवशा खशा ॥ १॥

कद्रूर्मुनिश्च विप्रेन्द्र तास्वपत्यानि मे श‍ृणु ।
पूर्वमन्वन्तरे श्रेष्ठे द्वादशासन्सुरोत्तमाः ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O brahmin, they are Aditi, Surasā, Ariṣṭelā, Danu, Surabhi, Vinatā, Ilā, Tāmrā, Krodhavasā, Kadru and Muni. Now listen to the names of their sons. In the previous excellent Manvantara twelve excellent gods were born. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
तुषिता नाम तेऽन्योन्यमूचुर्वैवस्वतेऽन्तरे ।
उपस्थिते सुयशसश्चाक्षुषस्यान्तरे मनोः ॥ ३॥

हिताय सर्वलोकानां समागम्य परस्परम् ।
आगच्छत द्रुतं देवा अदितिं च प्रविश्य वै ॥ ४॥

मन्वन्तरे प्रसूयामः सतां श्रेयो भविष्यति ।
एवमुक्तास्तु ते सर्वे चाक्षुषस्यान्तरे मनोः ॥ ५॥

मारीचात्कश्यपाज्जातास्तेऽदित्यां दक्षकन्यया ।
तत्र विष्णुश्च शक्रश्च जज्ञाते पुनरेव हि ॥ ६॥

English
They are Tuṣitas. During the intervening period of the Vaivasvata and Cākṣuṣa Manvantaras they consulted one another for the welfare of the worlds. After meeting together they spoke—“We will enter Aditi and be born. This will happen for the welfare of the gods.” After conferring they were born of Aditi daughter of Dakṣa and Kaśyapa son of Marīci. Viṣṇu and Indra were born again. (3-6.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्यमा चैव धाता च त्वष्टा पूषा तथैव च ।
विवस्वान् सविता चैव मित्रावरुण एव च ॥ ७॥

अंशो भगश्चातितेजा आदित्या द्वादश स्मृताः ।
चाक्षुषस्यान्तरे पूर्वमासन्ये तुषिताः सुराः ॥ ८॥

पुरैव तस्यान्तरे तु आदित्या द्वादश स्मृताः ।
इति प्रोक्तानि क्रमशोऽदित्यपत्यानि शौनक ॥ ९॥

English
They along with Aryaman, Dhātr, Tvaṣṭr, Pūṣan, Vivasvat, Savitr, Mitrāvaruṇa, Amśa, Bhaga and Atitejas are the twelve Ādityas. Those who were born before as Tuṣitas were born again as the twelve Ādityas in the Cākṣuṣa Manvantara, O Śaunaka thus the names of Aditi’s sons have been mentioned to you. (7-9.)

---

English
Diptis of immeasurable splendour became the children of the twentyseven women of sacred rites and the wives of Soma. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तविंशतिर्याः प्रोक्ताः सोमपत्न्योऽथ सुव्रताः ।
तासामपत्यान्यभवन्दीप्तयोऽमिततेजसः ॥ ५.३२.१०॥

अरिष्टनेमिपत्नीनामपत्यानीह षोडश ।
बहुपुत्रस्य विदुषश्चतस्रो विद्युतः स्मृताः ॥ ११॥

कृशाश्वस्य तु देवर्षे देवप्रहरणाः स्मृताः ।
भार्म्यायामर्चिषि मुने धूम्रकेशस्तथैव च ॥ १२॥

English
The children of Ariṣṭanemi’s wives were sixteen in number. “O celestial sage, the learned Kṛśāśva had many sons. Four of them are known as Devapraharaṇa. O sage, in his wife Arcis, Dhūmrakeśa too was born. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वधा सती च द्वे पत्न्यौ स्वधा ज्येष्ठा सती परा ।
स्वधासूत पितॄन् वेदमथर्वाङ्गिरसं सती ॥ १३॥

English
Two wives were Svadhā and Satī of whom Svadhā was the elder and Satī the younger. Svadhā gave birth to the Pīṭṭs and Satī to the Atharvāṅgirasaveda. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एते युगसहस्रान्ते जायन्ते पुनरेव हि ।
सर्वदेवनिकायाश्च त्रयस्त्रिंशत्तु कामजाः ॥ १४॥

English
At the end of a thousand Yugas these will be born again. All the groups of the gods are thirtythree. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा सूर्यस्य नित्यं हि उदयास्तमयाविह ।
एवं देवानिकास्ते च सम्भवन्ति युगे युगे ॥ १५॥

English
Just as the sun rises and sets every day so also the groups of gods are born in every Yuga. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
दित्यां बभूवतुः पुत्रौ कश्यपादिति नः श्रुतम् ।
हिरण्यकशिपुश्चैव हिरण्याक्षश्च वीर्यवान् ॥ १६॥

English
We have heard that Hiraṇyakaśipu and Hiraṇyākṣa were born as the sons of Diti and Kaśyapa. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सिंहिका ह्यभवत्कन्या विप्रचित्तेः परिग्रहः ।
हिरण्यकशिपोः पुत्राश्चत्वारः प्रथितौजसः ॥ १७॥

English
Siṃhikā their daughter became the wife of Vipracitti. The four sons of Hiraṇyakaśipu were famous for their strength and prowess. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुह्रादश्च ह्रादश्च संह्रादश्चैव वीर्यवान् ।
प्रह्रादश्चानुजस्तत्र विष्णुभक्तिविचारधीः ॥ १८॥

English
They are Anuhrāda, Hrāda, Saṃhrāda and Prahlāda. The youngest Prahlāda was a devotee of Viṣṇu. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुह्रादस्य सूर्यायां पुलोमा महिषस्तथा ।
ह्रादस्य धमनिर्भार्यासूत वातापिमिल्वलम् ॥ १९॥

English
Pūloman and Mahiṣa were the sons of Anuhrāda born of Sūryā. Hrāda’s wife Dhamani gave birth to Vātāpi and Ilvala. (19.)

---

English
Saṃhrāda’s wife Kṛti gave birth to Pañcajana. Virocana was the son of Prahlāda. Bali was born of his queen Devī. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
संह्रादस्य कृतिर्भार्यासूत पञ्चजनं ततः ।
विरोचनस्तु प्राह्रादिर्देव्यां तस्याभवद् बलिः ॥ ५.३२.२०॥

बलेः पुत्रशतं त्वासीदशनायां मुनीश्वर ।
बलिरासीन्महाशैवः शिवभक्तिपरायणः ॥ २१॥

दानशील उदारश्च पुण्यकीर्तितपाः स्मृतः ।
तत्पुत्रो बाणनामा यः सोऽपि शैववरः सुधीः ।
यः सन्तोष्य शिवं सम्यग्गाणपत्यमवाप ह ॥ २२॥

English
O great sage, Bali begot hundred sons of his wife Aśanā. He was a great devotee of Śiva. He engaged himself in the activities of devotion to Śiva. He was charitably inclined and liberal-minded. He attained meritorious fame and austerity. His son Bāṇa was foremost among the devotees of Śiva. By propititing Śiva he attained the leadership of Gaṇas. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
सा कथा श्रुतपूर्वा ते बाणस्य हि महात्मनः ।
कृष्णं यः समरे वीरः सुप्रसन्नं चकार ह ॥ २३॥

English
The story of Bāṇa has already been heard by you. In the battle Kṛṣṇa was delighted with him.

24-25 Hiraṇyāksa’s five sons were strong and learned. They were Kukura, Śakuni, Bhūta Santāpana, Mahānāda and Kālanābha. Thus the sons of Diti have been mentioned. O sage, now listen to the names of the sons of Danu. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अभवन्दनुपुत्राश्च शतं तीव्रपराक्रमाः ।
अयोमुखः शम्बरश्च कपोलो वामनस्तथा ॥ २६॥

वैश्वानरः पुलोमा च विद्रावणमहाशिरौ ।
स्वर्भानुर्वृषपर्वा च विप्रचित्तिश्च वीर्यवान् ॥ २७॥

एते सर्वे दनोः पुत्राः कश्यपादनुजज्ञिरे ।
एषां पुत्रीः श‍ृणु मुने प्रसङ्गाद्वच्मि तेऽनघ ॥ २८॥

English
Danu's sons were hundred in number and were of severe exploits. Ayomukha, Śambara, Kapola, Vāmana, Vaiśvānara, Puloman, Vidrāvaṇa, Mahāśira, Svarbhānu, Vṛṣaparvan and the powerful Vipracitti—these were the sons of Danu begot by Kaśyapa. O sage, now listen to the names of their sons. O sinless one, incidentally I shall mention a few of them. (26-28.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभार्नोस्तु प्रभा कन्या पुलोम्नस्तु शची सुता ।
उपदानवी हयशिरा शर्मिष्ठा वार्षपर्वणी ॥ २९॥

English
Prabhā was the daughter of Svarbhānu. Śacī was the daughter of Puloman. So also Upadānavī, Hayaśirā and Śarmiṣṭhā were daughters of Vṛṣaparvan. Pulomā and Pulomikā were the daughters of Vaiśvānara. They were the wives of Kaśyapa son of Marīci and had many heroic sons. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
पुलोमिका पुलोमा च वैश्वानरसुते उभे ।
बह्वपत्ये महावीर्ये मारीचेस्तु परिग्रहा त् ॥ ५.३२.३०॥

तयोः पुत्रसहस्राणि षष्टिर्दानवनन्दनाः ।
मरीचिर्जनयामास महता तपसान्विताः ॥ ३१॥

English
Kaśyapa, son of Marīci, endowed with great penance, begot of them sixty thousand sons delighting the Dānavas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पौलोमाः कालखञ्जाश्च दानवानां महाबलाः ।
अवध्या देवतानां च हिरण्यपुरवासिनः ॥ ३२॥

English
Kālakhañjas, the powerful sons of Pulomā, lived in Hiranyapura.¹⁹ They could not be killed either by the Dānavas or by the gods. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पितामहप्रसादेन ये हताः सव्यसाचिना ।
सिंहिकायामथोत्पन्ना विप्रचित्तेः सुतास्तथा ॥ ३३॥

English
Vipracitti's sons, born of Siṃhikā, were later killed by Arjuna by the grace of Brahmā. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
दैत्यदानवसंयोगाज्जातास्तीव्रपराक्रमाः ।
सैंहिकेया इति ख्यातास्त्रयोदश महाबलाः ॥ ३४॥

English
Due to the intermixture of Daityas and Dānavas these sons became valorous and famous as Saiṃhikeyas. They are thirteen in number. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
राहुः शल्यो सुबलिनो बलश्चैव महाबलः ।
वातापिर्नमुचिश्चैवाथेल्वलः स्वसृपस्तथा ॥ ३५॥

अजिको नरकश्चैव कालनाभस्तथैव च ।
शरमाणः शरः कल्प एते वंशविवर्धनाः ॥ ३६॥

English
They were the very powerful Rāhu and Śalya, Bala, Mahābala, Vātāpi, Namuci, Ilvala, Svasṛpa, Ajika, Naraka, Kālanābha, Śaramāṇa and Śarakalpa. These increased their races. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit

बहवश्च समुद्भूता विस्तरत्वान्न वर्णिताः ॥ ३७॥

English
Many sons and grandsons were born of them increasing the race of Danu. Being innumerable they are not mentioned in detail. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

उत्पन्ना मरुतस्तस्मिंस्तपसा भावितात्मनः ॥ ३८॥

English
In the family of Saṃhrāda the Daiteyas Nivātakavacas and Maruts were born and they were purified by penance. (38.)

---

Sanskrit

काकी श्येनी च भासी च सुग्रीवी च शुकी तथा ॥ ३९॥

English
Ṣaṇmukha and others are glorified as the sons of Tāmrā. Kākī, Śyenī, Bhāsī, Sugrīvī, Śukī, Gṛdhṛikā, Aśvī, and Ulūkī were the daughters of Tāmrā. Kākī gave birth to crows and Ulūkī to owls. (39-40.)

---

Sanskrit

काकी काकानजनयदुलूकी प्रत्युलूककान् ॥ ५.३२.४०॥

श्येनी श्येनांस्तथा भासी भासान्गृद्धी तु गृध्रकान् ।
शुकी शुकानजनयत्सुग्रीवी शुभपक्षिणः ॥ ४१॥

English
Śyeni gave birth to vultures, Bhāsī to Bhāsas Gṛdhrī to Gṛdhrakas. Śukī gave birth to parrots and Sugrīvī to auspicious birds. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Tāmrā the beloved of Kaśyapa gave birth to horses, camels and donkeys. Thus the family of Tāmrā has been described to you. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

The sons of Ailā were Padma, Karkoṭaka, Dhanañjaya, Mahānila, Mahākarṇa, Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Balāhaka, Kuhara, Puṣpadanta, Durmukha, Sumukha, Khararoman, Kharapāṇi and many others. (44-47.)
