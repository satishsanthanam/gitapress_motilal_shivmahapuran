### **CHAPTER THIRTYTHREE - (Description of the creation)**

---

Sanskrit
त्रयस्त्रिंशत्तमोऽध्यायः । कश्यपवंशवर्णनम् २ ।

सूत उवाच ।
एष मन्वन्तरे तात सर्गः स्वारोचिषे स्मृतः ।
वैवस्वते तु महति वारुणे वित ते क्रतौ ॥ १॥

जुह्वानस्य ब्रह्मणो वै प्रजासर्ग इहोच्यते ।
पूर्वं यानथ ब्रह्मर्षीनुत्पन्नान् सप्त मानसान् ॥ २॥

पुत्रान्वै कल्पयामास स्वयमेव पितामहः ।
तेषां विरोधो देवानां दानवानां महाॠषे ॥ ३॥

दितिर्विनष्टपुत्रा तु कश्यपं समुपस्थिता ।
स कश्यपः प्रसन्नात्मा सम्गयगाराधितस्तया ॥ ४॥

वरेणच्छन्दयामास सा च वव्रे वरं तदा ।
पुत्रमिन्द्रवधार्थाय समर्थममितौजसम् ॥ ५॥

English
Sūta said:—  Such is the creation as mentioned in the Manvantara, Svārocişa, O dear. Now I shall describe the creation as evolved by Brahman while offering oblations in the elaborate Vāruṇa sacrifice in the great Vaivasvata Manvantara. All the Brahmarṣis born in the beginning, Pitāmaha made his mental sons. When a great fight ensued between the gods and the Dānavas, Diti lost all her sons. She approached Kaśyapa and propitiated him. The delighted Kaśyapa asked her to choose a boon as she pleased. She chose the boon that she be favoured with a son of immeasurable power capable of slaying Indra. (1-5.)

---

Sanskrit
स तस्यै च वरं प्रादात्प्रार्थितं सुमहातपाः ।
ब्रह्मचर्य्यादिनियमं प्राह चैव शतं समाः ॥ ६॥

English
He granted her the boon of her choice. The sage of great penance advised her celibacy and other restraints for a hundred years. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
धारयामास गर्भं तु शुचिः सा वरवर्णिनी ।
ब्रह्मचर्य्यादिनियमं दितिर्दध्रे तथैव वै ॥ ७॥

English
The pious fair-complexioned lady conceived. She began to observe celibacy and other restraints. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वाधाय सो दित्यां गर्भं तं शंसितव्रतः ।
जगाम कश्यपस्तप्तुं तपः संहृष्टमानसः ॥ ८॥

English
After impregnating Diti, Kaśyapa of noble sacred rites went away satisfied for the performance of penance. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याश्चैवान्तरं प्रेप्सुः सोऽभवत्पाकशासनः ।
ऊनवर्षे शते चास्या ददर्शान्तरमेव सः ॥ ९॥

English
Indra waited for finding out a weak point in her. He found one such when the period of hundred years was short by a year. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अकृत्वा पादयोः शौचं दितिरर्वाक्षिरास्तदा ।
निद्रामाहारयामास भाविनोऽर्थस्य गौरवात् ॥ १०॥

English
Due to the gravity of the event destined to happen, once Diti did not wash her feet. She went to sleep with head hanging down. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे शक्रस्तस्याः कुक्षिं प्रविश्य सः ।
वज्रपाणिस्तु तं गर्भं सप्तधा हि न्यकृन्तत ॥ ११॥

English
In the meantime Indra entered her belly with the thunderbolt. He cut off the foetus into seven pieces. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स पाट्यमानो गर्भोऽथ वज्रेण प्ररुरोद ह ।
रुदन्तं सप्तधैकैकं मा रोदीरिति तान्पुनः ।
चकर्त वज्रपाणिस्तान्नेव मम्रुस्तथापि ते ॥ १२॥

English
While being split into seven the foetus began to cry in seven different tones. Indra told the foetus "Do not cry, Do not cry" repeatedly. He cut them again but they did not die. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ते तमूचुः पाट्यमानाः सर्वे प्राञ्जलयो मुने ।
नो जिघांससि किं शक्र भ्रातरो मरुतस्तव ॥ १३॥

English
O sage, being felled down they told him with palms joined in reverence. "O Indra, why do you wish to kill us? We are your brothers, the Maruts." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रेण स्वीकृतास्ते हि भ्रातृत्वे सर्व एव च ।
तत्यजुर्द्दैत्यभावं ते विप्रर्षे शङ्करेच्छया ॥ १४॥

English
O brahmin sage, at the will of Siva, they eschewed their Daitya nature. They were accepted by Indra with due fraternity. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मरुतो नाम ते देवा बभूवुः सुमहाबलाः ।
खगा एकोनपञ्चाशत्सहाया वज्रपाणिनः ॥ १५॥

English
In the name of Maruts they became gods of great prowess. Thus traversing the sky the fortynine Maruts became assistants of Indra. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषामेव प्रवृद्धानां हरिः प्रादात्प्रजापतिः ।
क्रमशस्तानि राज्यानि पृथुपूर्वं श‍ृणुष्व तत् ॥ १६॥

English
When they flourished, Vişnu the patriarch, offered them kingdoms beginning with Pṛthu. Listen to that. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अरिष्टः पुरुषो वीरः कृष्णो जिष्णुः प्रजापतिः ।
पर्जन्यस्तु धनाध्यक्षस्तस्य सर्वमिदं जगत् ॥ १७॥

English
Ariṣṭa was the heroic person. Kṛṣṇa the patriarch was ever victorious. Parjanya was the presiding deity of clouds. This entire universe belongs to him. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतसर्गमिमं सम्यगवोचं ते महामुने ।
विभागं श‍ृणु राज्यानां क्रमशस्तं ब्रुवेऽधुना ॥ १८॥

English
O great sage, I have explained the creation of living beings to you. Listen to the division of kingdoms. I shall explain that duly now. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अभिषिच्याधिराज्ये तु पृथुं वैन्यं पितामहः ।
ततः क्रमेण राज्यानि व्यादेष्टुमुपचक्रमे ॥ १९॥

English
After crowning Pṛthu the son of Vena in the emperorship, Brahmā began to assign the kingdoms in due order. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजानां वीरुधां चैव नक्षत्रग्रहयोस्तथा ।
यज्ञानां तपसां चैव सोमं राज्येऽभ्यषेचयत् ॥ २०॥

English
He crowned Soma in the kingdom of birds, creepers, stars, planets, sacrifices and austerities. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अपां तु वरुणं राज्ये राज्ञां वैश्रवणं प्रभुम् ।
आदित्यानां तथा विष्णुं वसूनामथ पावकम् ॥ २१॥

English
He crowned Varuṇa in the kingdom of waters; Vaiśravaṇa as the lord of kings; Viṣṇu as the lord of Āditvas and Pāvaka as the lord of Vasus. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजापतीनां दक्षं तु मरुतामथ वासवम् ।
दैत्यानां दानवानां च प्रह्लादममितौजसम् ॥ २२॥

English
He crowned Dakṣa as the lord of patriarchs, Indra as the lord of Maruts, and Prahlāda of great strength as the lord of Daityas and Dānavas. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
वैवस्वतं पितॄणां च यमं राज्येऽभ्यषेचयत् ।
मातॄणां च व्रतानां च मन्त्राणां च तथा गवाम् ॥ २३॥

English
He crowned Vaivasvata Yama in the kingdom of Pītrs, Mātrs, Vratas, Mantras and cows. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यक्षाणां राक्षसानां च पार्थिवानां तथैव च ।
सर्वभूतपिशाचानां गिरिशं शूलपाणिनम् ॥ २४॥

English
He crowned the trident-bearing Śiva, in the kingdom of Yakṣas, Rākṣasas, kings, goblins and ghosts. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शैलानां हिमवन्तं च नदीनामथ सागरम् ।
मृगाणामथ शार्दूलं गोवृषं तु गवामपि ॥ २५॥

English
He crowned Himavat as the lord of mountains, the ocean of rivers, the tiger of deer and Bull as the lord of cows. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
वनस्पतीनां वृक्षाणां वटं राज्येऽभ्यषेचयत् ।
इति दत्तं प्रजेशेन राज्यं सर्वत्र वै क्रमात् ॥ २६॥

English
He crowned the banyan tree in the kingdom of plants and trees. Thus the kingdom was assigned everywhere by the lord of subjects. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वस्यां दिशि पुत्रं तु वैराजस्य प्रजापतेः ।
स्थापयामास सर्वात्मा राज्ये विश्वपतिर्विभुः ॥ २७॥

English
The lord of the universe, the soul of all, established the son of patriarch Vairāja in the kingdom in the east. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव मुनिशार्दूल कर्दमस्य प्रजापतेः ।
दक्षिणस्यां तथा पुत्रं सुधन्वानमचीक्लृपत् ॥ २८॥

English
Similarly, O great king, he established Sudhanvan, son of Kardama, the patriarch, in the kingdom in the south. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चिमायां दिशि तथा रजसः पुत्रमच्युतम् ।
केतुमन्तं महात्मानं राजानं व्यादिशत्प्रभुः ॥ २९॥

English
The lord directed and assigned the unfailing noble-minded son of Rajas, Ketumat as the king in the kingdom in the west. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा हिरण्यरोमाणं पर्जन्यस्य प्रजापतेः ।
उदीच्यां दिशि राजानं दुर्धर्षं सोऽभ्यषेचयत् ॥ ३०॥

English
He crowned the indefatigable son of Parjanya the patriarch, Hiranyaroman, in the kingdom in the north. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य विस्तारमाख्यातं पृथोर्वैन्यस्य शौनक ।
महर्ध्यै तदधिष्ठानं पुराणं परिकीर्तितम् ॥ ३१॥

English
O Saunaka, the details of Pṛthu the son of Vena have been narrated to you. This base is glorified as the most ancient spot of great prosperity. (31.)
