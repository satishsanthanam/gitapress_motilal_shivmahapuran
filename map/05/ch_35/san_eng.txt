### **CHAPTER THIRTYFIVE - (The description of Vaivasvata)**

---

Sanskrit
पचत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । मन्वन्तरकीर्तने वैवस्वतवर्णनम् ।

सूत उवाच ।
विवस्वान् कश्यपाज्जज्ञे दाक्षायण्यां महाऋषेः ।
तस्य भार्याभवत्संज्ञा त्वाष्ट्री देवी सुरेणुका ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said:—  Vivasvat was born of Dakṣa's daughter and Kaśyapa the great sage. His wife was Saṃjñā, daughter of Tvaṣṭr and also known as Sureṇukā. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनेऽसहिष्णुना तेन तेजसा दुस्सहेन च ।
भर्तृरूपेण नातुष्यद्रूपयौवनशालिनी ॥ २॥

English
Due to the unbearable heat of her husband she in her prime of youth was dissatisfied. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
आदित्यस्य हि तद्रूपमसहिष्णुः सुतेजसः ।
दह्यमाना तदोद्वेगमकरोद्वरवर्णिनी ॥ ३॥

English
Being scorched and not being able to hear the brilliance of the lustrous sun, the fair-complexioned lady was excited and sorrowful. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषेऽस्यां त्रीण्यपत्यानि जनयामास भास्करः ।
संज्ञायां तु मनुः पूर्वं श्राद्धदेवः प्रजापतिः ॥ ४॥

यमश्च यमुना चैव यमलौ सम्बभूवतुः ।
एवं हि त्रीण्यपत्यानि तस्यां जातानि सूर्यतः ॥ ५॥

English
O sage, the sun begot three offsprings of her: the patriarch Manu Śrāddhadeva, Yama and Yamunā born as twins. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
संवर्तुलं तु तद्रूपं दृष्ट्वा संज्ञा विवस्वतः ।
असहन्ती ततश्छायामात्मनः सासृजच्छुभाम् ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing the brilliance of the sun as unbearable as the fire at the final dissolution she created Chāyā of herself. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मायामयी तु सा संज्ञामवोचद्भक्तितः सुभे ।
किं करोमीह कार्यं ते कथयस्व शुचिस्मिते ॥ ७॥

English
She of illusory form told Saṃjñā : "O sweet-smiled auspicious lady, what shall I do for you, please tell me". (7.)

---

Sanskrit
संज्ञोवाच
अहं यास्यामि भद्रं ते ममैव भवनं पितुः ।
त्वयैतद्भवने सत्यं वस्तव्यं निर्विकारतः ॥ ८॥

English
Saṃjñā said:—  "Welfare unto you. I go to my father's house alone. Indeed you have to stay in this house without aberration. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
इमौ मे बालकौ साधू कन्या चेयं सुमध्यमा ।
पालनीयाः सुखेनैव मम चेदिच्छसि प्रियम् ॥ ९॥

English
These two well-behaved sons and this pretty girl should be happily protected if you wish to please me." (9.)

---

Sanskrit
छायोवाच ।
आकेशग्रहणाद्देवि सहिष्येऽहं सुदुष्कृतम् ।
नाख्यास्यामि मतं तुभ्यं गच्छ देवि यथासुखम् ॥ १०॥

English
Chāyā said :—  "O lady, I shall brook their misdeeds even as much as pulling out my hair. I shall make no complaint to you. O goddess, you can go away happily." (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्ता साऽगमद्देवी व्रीडिता सन्निधौ पितुः ।
पित्रा निर्भर्त्सिता तत्र नियुक्ता सा पुनः पुनः ॥ ११॥

English
Sūta said:—  Ashamed on being addressed thus, the lady went to her father's house but was rebuked by him. He urged her again and again to return to her husband. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अगच्छद्वडवा भूत्वाच्छाद्य रूपं ततः स्वकम् ।
कुरुंस्तदोत्तरान् प्राप्य नृणान्येव चचार ह ॥ १२॥

English
She assumed the form of a mare. Going to Northern Kurus she wandered among the people. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
संज्ञां तां तु रविर्मत्वा छायायां सुसुतं तदा ।
जनयामास सावर्णिं मनुं वै सविता किल ॥ १३॥

English
The sun took her for Saṃjñā and begot of her a son the Manu Sāvarṇi. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
संज्ञानु प्रार्थिता छाया सा स्वपुत्रेऽपि नित्यशः ।
चकाराभ्यधिकं स्नेहं न तथा पूर्वजे सुते ॥ १४॥

English
Though requested by Saṃjñā, Chāyā did not love the elder children. She loved her own son more and fondled him always. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मनुस्तस्याक्षमत्तं यमस्तं नैव चक्षमे ।
स सरोषस्तु बाल्याच्च भाविनोऽर्थस्य गौरवात् ॥ १५॥

English
The younger brother Yama could not hear that. He did not forgive. Ever since childhood he was furious and ill-tempered due to the gravity of what was destined to happen later. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
छायां सन्तर्जयामास पदा वैवस्वतो यमः ।
तं शशाप ततः क्रोधाच्छाया तु कलुषीकृता ॥ १६॥

English
When Yama, son of Vivasvat threatened Chāyā she became infuriated and cursed him angrily. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
चरणः पततामेष तवेति भृशरोषतः ।
यमस्ततः पितुः सर्वं प्राञ्जलिः प्रत्यवेदयत् ॥ १७॥

English
Due to excessive anger she cursed—“Let your leg fall off”. Yama approached his father with palms joined in reverence and said. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
भृशं शापभयोद्विग्नः संज्ञावाक्यैर्विचेष्टितः ।
मात्रा स्नेहेन सर्वेषु वर्तितव्यं सुतेषु वै ॥ १८॥

स्नेहमस्मास्वपाकृत्य कनीयांसं बिभर्ति सा ।
तस्मान्मयोद्यतः पादस्तद्भवान् क्षन्तुमर्हति ॥ १९॥

शप्तोऽहमस्मि देवेश जनन्या तपतांवर ।
तव प्रसादाच्चरणो न पतेन्मम गोपते ॥ २०॥

English
“I was agitated due to Chāyā’s words. I am sorry and frightened of the curse. I had only said that a mother shall have impartial and equal love for all her children. She has lost affection for us. She nurtures only the youngest one. Hence I raised my foot. It behoves you to forgive me. O lord of gods, O foremost among the refulgent ones, I have been cursed by my mother. O lord of rays, let not my leg fall off by your grace.” (18-20.)

---

Sanskrit
सवितोवाच ।
असंशयं पुत्र महद्भविष्यत्यत्र कारणम् ।
येन त्वामाविशत्क्रोधो धर्मज्ञं सत्यवादिनम् ॥ २१॥

English
*The sun said:—*  O son, there shall be a great cause for this. That was why you too who know virtue and speak the truth had been infuriated. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
न शक्यते तन्मिथ्या वै कर्तुं मातृवचस्तव ।
कृमयो मांसमादाय गमिष्यन्ति महीतले ॥ २२॥

English
It is not possible to make your mother’s words false. Worms will take away the flesh from your leg and go to the earth. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वाक्यं भविता सत्यं त्वं च त्रातौ भविष्यसि ।
कुरु तात न सन्देहं मनश्चाश्वास्य स्वं प्रभो ॥ २३॥

English
Her words will come up true and you will also be saved. Have no doubt, O deer, cheer up your mind, O lord. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्युक्त्वा तनयं सूर्यो यमसंज्ञं मुनीश्वर ।
आदित्यश्चाब्रवीत्तान्त्तु छायां क्रोधसमन्वितः ॥ २४॥

English
*Sūta said:—*  O great sage, after saying thus to his son Yama, the sun angrily spoke to Chāyā. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सूर्य उवाच ।
हे प्रिये कुमते चण्डि किं त्वयाचरितं किल ।
किन्तु मेऽभ्यधिकः स्नेह एतदाख्यातुमर्हसि ॥ २५॥

English
*The sun said :—*  O beloved wife, O evil-minded angry lady, what it is that you have done ? Why do you love one son more ? You shall tell me. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सा रवेर्वचनं श्रुत्वा यथातथ्यं न्यवेदयत् ।
निर्दग्धा कामरविणा सान्त्वयामास वै तदा ॥ २६॥

English
Sūta said:—  On hearing the words of the sun she told him the truth. Scorched by him she consoled him thus. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यास्तद् वचनं श्रुत्वा सूर्याऽगात्तवष्टुरन्तिकम्
पप्रच्छ तं क्व संज्ञेति त्वष्टा सूर्यमथाब्रवीत् ॥ २७॥

English
Chāyā said:—  This fierce form of yours was not pleasant to Saṃjñā. She was scorched by your excessive brilliance. She could not bear it. She now resides on the grassy plain in a forest. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वष्टोवाच ।
तवातितेजसा दग्धा इदं रूपं न शोभते ।
असहन्ती च तत्संज्ञा वने वसति शाद्वले ॥ २८॥

English
O lord of rays, she is endowed with the power of Yoga. She has resorted to yogic practice. She is praiseworthy. O lord of gods, be favourable to her by sending the message of your opinion. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
श्लाघ्या योगबलोपेता योगमासाद्य गोपते ।
अनुकूलस्तु देवेश सन्दिश्यात्ममयं मतम् ॥ २९॥

English
I shall change your form. I shall make it pleasingly brilliant. Sūta said:— On hearing this, the sun was appeased. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
रूपं निवर्तयाम्यद्य तव कान्तं करोम्यहम् ।
सूत उवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वापगतः क्रोधो मार्तण्डस्य विवस्वतः ॥ ३०॥

English
The sage Tvaṣṭṛ put him on the turner's lathe and sharpened him further. His blazing form was slightly reduced in brilliance. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रमिमारोप्य तत्तेजः शातयामास वै मुनिः ।
ततो विभ्राजितं रूपं तेजसा संवृतेन च ॥ ३१॥

English
When the form was made more pleasingly brilliant by Tvaṣṭṛ it shone splendidly. Then resorting to Yogic practice he saw his wife Saṃjñā. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतं कान्ततरं रूपं त्वष्ट्रा तच्छुशुभे तदा ।
ततोऽधियोगमास्थाय स्वां भार्यां हि ददर्श ह ॥ ३२॥

English
He assumed the form of a horse and approached her for sexual indulgence with her who could not be overwhelmed by any living being due to her lustre and observance of restraint. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अधृष्यां सर्वभूतानां तेजसा नियमेन च ।
सोऽश्वरूपं समास्थाय गत्वा तां मैथुनेच्छया ॥ ३३॥

English
O sage, in the course of the sexual activity she suspected him to be another man. Hence she received the semen through the mouth into the nostril. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
मैथुनाय विचेष्टन्तीं परपुंसोऽभिशङ्कया ।
मुखतो नासिकायां तु शुक्रं तद्व्यदधान्मुने ॥ ३४॥

English
Thence were born the twin gods Aśvins, the foremost among physicians. They are known as Nāsatyas and Dasras. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
देवौ ततः प्रजायेतामश्विनौ भिषजां वरौ ।
नासत्यौ तौ च दस्रौ च स्मृतौ द्वावश्विनावपि ॥ ३५॥

English
The sun showed his pleasingly splendid form to them. On seeing her husband she was extremely delighted. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तां तु कान्तेन रूपेण दर्शयामास भास्करः ।
आत्मानं सा तु तं दृष्ट्वा प्रहृष्टा पतिमादरात् ॥ ३६॥

English
The chaste lady returned to the house with her husband with the face beaming with pleasure. The pair rejoiced more than before. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्या तेन गृहं प्रागात्स्वं सती मुदितानना ।
मुमुदातेऽथ तौ प्रीत्या दम्पती पूर्वतोधिकम् ॥ ३७॥

English
This incident distressed the mind of Yama. As pious king he gladdened the subjects virtuously. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
यमस्तु कर्मणा तेन भृशं पीडितमानसः ।
धर्मेण रञ्जयामास धर्मराज इमा प्रजाः ॥ ३८॥

English
Yama of great lustre attained the lordship of the names and the guardianship of the quarters. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
लेभे स कर्मणा तेन धर्मराजो महाद्युतिः ।
पितॄणामाधिपत्यं च लोकपालत्वमेव च ॥ ३९॥

English
Sāvarṇi Manu became the patriarch. In the Sāvarṇika Manvantara he will become the future Manu by virtue of his action. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
मनुः प्रजापतिस्त्वासीत्सावर्णिः स तपोधनः ।
भाव्यः स कर्मणा तेन मनोः सावर्णिकेऽन्तरे ॥ ४०॥

मेरुपृष्ठे तपो घोरमद्यापि चरते प्रभुः ।
यवीयसी तयोर्या तु यमी कन्या यशस्विनी ॥ ४१॥

अभवत्सा सरिच्छ्रेष्ठा यमुना लोकपा वनी ।
मनुरित्युच्यते लोके सावर्णिरिति चोच्यते ॥ ४२॥

English
The lord is performing penance even today on the top of Meru$^{21}$. Their younger sister, the famous Yamī, became the most excellent river Yamunā, the sanctifier of all the worlds. He is called Sāvarṇi Manu in the world. He who listens to or retains in memory this origin of the gods attains great fame. Should he suffer from any adversity he will be rid of it. (40-42.)

Sanskrit

आपदं प्राप्य मुच्येत प्राप्नुयात्सुमहद्यशः ॥ ४३॥
