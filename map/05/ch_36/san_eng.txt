### **CHAPTER THIRTYSIX - *(The description of the nine sons and the race of Manu)***

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
मनोर्वैवस्वतस्यासन्पुत्रा वै नव तत्समाः ।
पश्चान्महोन्नता धीराः क्षत्रधर्मपरायणाः ॥ १॥

English
*Sūta said:—*  Nine sons all equal to him were born of Vaivasvata Manu. Later they became very lofty, bold and devoted to the virtues of Kṣatriyas. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इक्ष्वाकुः शिबिनाभागौ धृष्टः शर्यातिरेव च ।
नरिष्यन्तोऽथ नाभागः करूषश्च प्रियव्रतः ॥ २॥

English
They were Ikṣvāku, Śibi, Nābhāga, Dhṛṣṭa, Śaryāti, Nariṣyanta, Nabhaga, Karūṣa and Priyavrata.$^{22}$ (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अकरोत्पुत्रकामस्तु मनुरिष्टिं प्रजापतिः ।
अनुत्पन्नेषु पुत्रेषु तत्रेष्ट्यां मुनिपुङ्गवः ॥ ३॥

सा हि दिव्याम्बरधरा दिव्याभरणभूषिता ।
दिव्यसंहनना चैवमिला जज्ञे हि विश्रुता ॥ ४॥

तामिडेत्येव होवाच मनुर्दण्डधरस्तथा ।
अनुगच्छस्व मामेति तमिडा प्रत्युवाच ह ॥ ५॥

English
Desirous of sons, Manu the patriarch performed a sacrifice. O great sage, when no sons were born as a result of the sacrifice, the famous Ilā was born, wearing divine garments, bedecked in divine ornaments and with a divine body. Manu the chastiser addressed her as Ilā and said “Follow me”. Ilā replied to him. (3-5.)

---

Sanskrit

इडोवाच ।
मित्रावरुणयोरंशैर्जातास्मि वदतां वर ॥ ६॥

English
Ilā said:—  She spoke these words to the patriarch desirous of a son—“O foremost among the eloquent, I am born of the parts of Mitra and Varuṇa. (6.)

---

Sanskrit

एवमुक्त्वा सती सा तु मित्रावरुणयोस्ततः ॥ ७॥

गत्वान्तिकं वरारोहा प्राञ्जलिर्वाक्यमब्रवीत् ।
अंशैस्तु युवयोर्जाता मनुयज्ञे महामुनी ॥ ८॥

आगता भवतोरन्ति ब्रूतं किं करवाणि वाम् ।
अन्यान् पुत्रान् सृज विभो तैर्वंशस्ते भविष्यति ॥ ९॥

English
I am approaching them. I shall never be interested in sinful things.” After saying this, the chaste lady approached Mitra and Varuṇa and spoke with palms joined in reverence “O great sages, I am born in the sacrifice of Manu through your parts. I have come to you. Please tell me. What I can do for you. O lord, create other sons so that your race may flourish.” (7-9.)

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।

English
Sūta said:—  When the chaste lady born in the sacrifice of Manu said, the sages Mitra and Varuṇa spoke thus respectfully. (10.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Sudyumna had three very virtuous heirs Utkala, Gaya and the powerful Vinatāśva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

तत्पुरूरवसे प्रादाद् राज्यं प्राप्य महायशाः ॥ २१॥

आनर्तस्य हि दायादो रैभ्यो नाम स रैवतः ॥ २४॥

English

O great sages, Sudyumna had the characteristics of both man and woman. The Śakas were the sons of Nariṣyanta. Nabhaga’s son Ambariṣa got the region Bāhlaka$^{25}$ and so was known as Bāhleya. Śaryāti had a son and a daughter. The son was known as Ānarta and the daughter as Sukanyā who later became the wife of Cyavana. Ānarta’s son was Raibhya known also as Raivata. (20-24.)

English
Once due to an error in his charitable gift of a cow, prompted by his own sins and crooked intellect, he became a chameleon and was redeemed by Śrīkṛṣṇa. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यैकोऽभूत्सुतः श्रेष्ठः प्रयातिर्धर्मवित्तथा ।
इति श्रुतं मया व्यासात्तत्प्रोक्तं हि समासतः ॥ ५२॥

English
An excellent son was born to him named Prayāti, the knower of virtues. This I have heard from Vyāsa and have now briefly mentioned to you. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
वृषघ्नस्तु मनोः पुत्रो गोपालो गुरुणा कृतः ।
पालयामास गा यत्तो रात्र्यां वीरासनव्रतः ॥ ५३॥

English
Pṛṣadhra, a son of Manu was made the keeper of cows by his preceptor. During the nights, observing the posture of heroes, he diligently looked after the cows. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
स एकदागतं गोष्ठे व्याघ्रं गा हिंसितुं बली ।
श्रुत्वा गोक्रन्दनं बुद्धो हन्तुं तं खड्गधृग्ययौ ॥ ५४॥

English
Once a tiger entered the cowshed to attack the cows. On hearing the shrieks of the cows he woke up. Strong that he was he seized a sword to kill it and started. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
अजानन्नहनद्बभ्रोः शिरः शार्दूलशङ्कया ।
निश्चक्राम सभीर्व्याघ्रो दृष्ट्वा तं खड्गिनं प्रभुम् ॥ ५५॥

English
On seeing the lord armed with a sword the frightened tiger slipped away. Not knowing it and mistaking a reddish brown cow as the tiger he hit it with his sword on its head. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्यमानो हतं व्याघ्रं स्वस्थानं स जगाम ह ।
रात्र्यां तस्यां भ्रमापन्नो वर्षवातविनष्टधीः ॥ ५६॥

English
Due to rain and gust of wind in the night he was deluded. Thinking that the tiger had been killed he returned to his place. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
व्युष्टायां निशि चोत्थाय प्रगे तत्र गतो हि सः ।
अद्राक्षीत्स हतां बभ्रुं न व्याघ्रं दुःखितोऽभवत् ॥ ५७॥

English
When the night dawned into day he got up and went there. He saw only the cow killed and not the tiger. He felt dejected. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा तद्वृत्तमाज्ञाय तं शशाप कृतागसम् ।
अकामतो विचार्येति शूद्रो भव न क्षत्रियः ॥ ५८॥

English
On hearing about the incident and realising that he had committed a sin unconsciously and not wilfully, the preceptor cursed him—"Become a Sūdra, not a Kṣatriya." (58.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शप्तस्तु गुरुणा कुलाचार्येण कोपतः ।
निःसृतश्च पृषध्रस्तु जगाम विपिनं महत् ॥ ५९॥

English
On being cursed by his preceptor, the family priest out of anger, Prśadhra started from there and went to the great forest. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्विण्णः स तु कष्टेन विरक्तोऽभूत्स योगवान् ।
वनाग्नौ दग्धदेहश्च जगाम परमां गतिम् ॥ ६०॥

English
Dejected by sufferings he became detached and practised Yoga. He burnt himself in conflagration and attained the greatest goal. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
कविः पुत्रो मनोः प्राज्ञः शिवानुग्रहतोऽभवत् ।
इह भुक्त्वा सुखं दिव्यं मुक्तिं प्राप सुदुर्लभाम् ॥ ६१॥

English
Kavi, son of Manu, was highly intelligent due to Siva's blessings. After enjoying divine pleasures here he attained the rare salvation hereafter. (61.)
