### **CHAPTER THIRTYSEVEN - (The race of Manu)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
पूर्वतस्तु मनोर्जज्ञे इक्ष्वाकुर्घ्राणतः सुतः ।
तस्य पुत्रशतं त्वासीदिक्ष्वाकोर्भूरिदक्षिणम् ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said:—  Formerly Ikṣvāku was born as the son of Manu through the nose. He had a hundred sons who were liberal in bestowing gifts. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां पुरस्तादभवन्नार्यावर्ते नृपा द्विजाः ।
तेषां विकुक्षिर्ज्येष्ठस्तु सोऽयोध्यायां नृपोऽभवत् ॥ २॥

English
Before them O brahmins, there were no kings in Āryāvarta. Of them the eldest was Vikukṣi. He became the king of Ayodhyā. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्कर्म श‍ृणु तत्प्रीत्या यज्जातं वंशतो विधेः ।
श्राद्धकर्मणि चोद्दिष्टो ह्यकृते श्राद्धकर्मणि ॥ ३॥

भक्षयित्वा शशं शीघ्रं शशादत्वमतो गतः ।
इक्ष्वाकुणा परित्यक्तः शशादो वनमाविशत् ॥ ४॥

English
Listen to one of his misdeeds. He wanted to perform Śrāddha but before performing the same he ate a rabbit and came to be known as Śaśāda. Abandoned by Ikṣvāku, he entered a forest. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
इक्ष्वाकौ संस्थिते राजा वसिष्ठवचनादभूत् ।
शकुनिप्रमुखास्तस्य पुत्राः पञ्चदश स्मृताः ॥ ५॥

English
When Ikṣvāku passed away he was installed a king at the instance of Vasiṣṭha. He had fifteen sons of whom the chief was Śakuni. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तरापथदेशस्य रक्षितारो महीक्षितः ।
अयोधस्य तु दायादः ककुत्स्थो नाम वीर्यवान् ॥ ६॥

English
They all became kings and protectors of the northern country. Ayodha's son was the powerful king Kakutstha. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अरिनाभः ककुत्स्थस्य पृथुरेतस्य वै सुतः ।
विष्टराश्वः पृथोः पुत्रस्तस्मादिन्द्रः प्रजापतिः ॥ ७॥

English
Kakutstha's son was Arinābha. His son was Pṛthu. Pṛthu's son was Viṣṭarāśva. From him was born Indra the lord of people. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
इन्द्रस्य युवनाश्वस्तु श्रावस्तस्य प्रजापतिः ।
जज्ञे श्रावस्तकः प्राज्ञः श्रावस्ती येन निर्मिता ।
श्रावस्तस्य तु दायादो बृहदश्वो महायशाः ॥ ८॥

English
Indra's son was Yuvanāśva, the ruler of Śrāvasta. His son was Śrāvastaka who built Śrāvastī.²⁸ Śrāvastaka's son was Bṛhadaśva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
युवनाश्वः सुतस्तस्य कुवलाश्वश्च तत्सुतः ।
स हि धुन्धुवधाद्भूतो धुन्धुमारो नृपोत्तमः ॥ ९॥

English
His son was Yuvanāśva and Kuvalāśva was his son. Since he slew Dhundhu he became the excellent king Dhundhumāra. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कुवलाश्वस्य पुत्राणां शतमुत्तमधन्विनाम् ।
बभूवाथ पिता राज्ये कुवलाश्वं न्ययोजयत् ॥ १०॥

पुत्रसङ्क्रामितश्रीको वनं राजा समाविशत् ।
तमुत्तङ्कोऽथ राजर्षिः प्रयान्तं प्रत्यवारयत् ॥ ११॥

English
Kuvalāśva had hundred sons who were excellent archers. He was entrusted with the kingdom by his father. After transferring the royal glory to the son the king entered the forest. But Uttāṅka prevented him. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तङ्क उवाच ।
भवता रक्षणं कार्यं पृथिव्या धर्मतः श‍ृणु ।
त्वया हि पृथिवी राजन् रक्ष्यमाणा महात्मना ॥ १२॥

भविष्यति निरुद्विग्ना नारण्यं गन्तुमर्हसि ।
ममाश्रमसमीपे तु समेषु मरुधन्वसु ॥ १३॥

समुद्रवालुकापूर्णे दानवो बलदर्पितः ।
देवतानामवध्यो हि महाकायो महाबलः ॥ १४॥

English
*Uttāṅka said:—*  Listen. You shall protect the earth virtuously. O king, only when protected by you can the earth be relieved of excitement and sorrow. It does not behove you to go to the forest. There is a Dānava, proud of his strength near my hermitage, in the snow-covered wilderness, full of sea-sand. He is indestructible even to the gods. He has a huge body and is very strong. (12-14.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तर्भूमिगतस्तत्र वालुकान्तर्हितः स्थितः ।
राक्षसस्य मधोः पुत्रो धुन्धुनामा सुदारुणः ॥ १५॥

English
He stays underground concealed by the sand. The terrible Rākṣasa son of the demon Madhu is Dhundhu by name. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
शेते लोकविनाशाय तप आस्थाय दारुणम् ।
संवत्सरस्य पर्यन्ते स निश्वासं विमुञ्चति ॥ १६॥

English
He stays there performing a terrible penance for the destruction of the worlds. At the end of every year he exhales terribly. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा तदा भूश्चलति सशैलवनकानना ।
सविस्फुलिङ्गं साङ्गारं सधूममतिदारुणम् ॥ १७॥

English
When he exhales, the whole world including mountains, forests and wilderness, quakes. Blazing flames with pink smoke smoulder everywhere. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन रायन्न शक्नोमि तस्मिन्न् स्थातुं स्व आश्रमे ।
तं मारय महाबाहो लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ १८॥

English
Hence, O king, I cannot stay in my hermitage. O strong one with huge arms ward him off desiring the welfare of the worlds. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकाः स्वस्था भवन्त्वद्य तस्मिन् विनिहते त्वया ।
त्वं हि तस्य वधायैव समर्थः पृथिवीपते ॥ १९॥

English
Let the words become happy and peaceful after he had been killed by you. O lord of earth, you alone can slay him. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुना च वरो दत्तो मह्य पूर्वयुगेऽनघ ।
तेजसा स्वेन ते विष्णुस्तेज आप्याययिष्यति ॥ २०॥

English
O sinless one, a great boon has been granted to you. Vişnu will heighten your splendour by his own splendour. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
पालने हि महान् धर्मः प्रजानामिह दृश्यते ।
न तथा दृश्यतेऽरण्ये मा तेऽभूद् बुद्धिरीदृशी ॥ २१॥

English
Great virtue accrues from the protection of the subjects. A similar opportunity is wanting in the forest. Let not your mind be directed that way. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ईदृशो न हि राजेन्द्र क्वचिद्धर्मः प्रविद्यते ।
प्रजानां पालने यादृक् पुरा राजर्षिभिः कृतः ॥ २२॥

English
O leading king, nowhere such a virtue exists as it does in the protection of the subjects. This has been pursued by the saintly kings of yore. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स एवमुक्तो राजर्षिरुत्तङ्केन महात्मना ।
कुवलाश्वं सुतं प्रादात्तस्मै धुन्धुनिवारणे ॥ २३॥

English
Thus requested the saintly king entrusted his son Kuvalasva with the task of thwarting Dhundhu. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
भगवन्न्यस्तशस्त्रोऽहमयं तु तनयो मम ।
भविष्यति द्विजश्रेष्ठ धुन्धुमारो न संशयः ॥ २४॥

English
"O holy lord, O excellent brahmin, I have already laid aside my weapons. Here is my son who will surely destroy Dhundhu." (24.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा पुत्रमादिश्य ययौ स तपसे नृपः ।
कुवलाश्वश्च सोत्तङ्को ययौ धुन्धुविनिग्रहे ॥ २५॥

English
Having said thus and instructed his son the king proceeded with penance. Kuvalasva accompanied by Uttanka went to fight with Dhundhu. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तमाविशत्तदा विष्णुर्भगवांस्तेजसा प्रभुः ।
उत्तङ्कस्य नियोगाद्वै लोकानां हितकाम्यया ॥ २६॥

English
At the approach of Uttanka and for the benefit of the worlds, lord Vişnu entered him with his splendour. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्प्रयाते दुर्धर्षे दिवि शब्दो महानभूत् ।
एष श्रीमान्नृपसुतो धुन्धुमारो भविष्यति ॥ २७॥

English
When the invincible Kuvalasva started there was a loud shout in the heaven. "This glorious prince will slay Dhundhu." (27.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यैर्माल्यैश्च तं देवाः समन्तात्समवारयन् ।
प्रशंसां चक्रिरे तस्य जय जीवेति वादिनः ॥ २८॥

English
The gods surrounded him with garlands of flowers. They praised him saying "Be victorious, Be long-lived." (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स गत्वा जयतां श्रेष्ठस्तनयैः सह पार्थिवः ।
समुद्रं खनयामास वालुकार्णवमध्यतः ॥ २९॥

English
The most excellent among the victorious, the king went there accompanied by his sons. He caused an ocean to be dug in the midst of that vast expanse of sand. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणस्य विप्रर्षेस्तेजसाप्यायितस्तु सः ।
बभूव सुमहातेजा भूयो बलसमन्वितः ॥ ३०॥

English
O brahminical sage, heightened in strength by the splendour of Viṣṇu he became very brilliant and stronger. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रैः खनद्भिस्तु वालुकान्तर्गतस्तु सः ।
धुन्धुरासादितो ब्रह्मन् दिशमाश्रित्य पश्चि माम् ॥ ३१॥

English
O brahmin, the demon Dhundhu was found out, concealed beneath the sand towards the western quarter as the sons of the king dug up the place. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मुखजेनाग्निना क्रोधाल्लोकान् संवर्तयन्निव ।
वारि सुस्राव वेगेन विधोः कधिरिवोदये ॥ ३२॥

English
He appeared to consume all the worlds out of fury in the fire emerging from his mouth. Water too gushed out from him as from the moon-stone at the moonrise. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽनलैरभिहतं दग्धं पुत्रशतं हि तत् ।
त्रय एवावशिष्टाश्च तेषु मध्ये मुनीश्वर ॥ ३३॥

English
The hundred sons were scorched and burnt in the fire. O great sage, among them only three survived. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स राजा विप्रेन्द्र राक्षसं तं महाबलम् ।
आससाद महातेजा धुन्धुं विप्रविनाशनम् ॥ ३४॥

English
O leading brahmin, then the king of great splendour rushed at the very powerful Rākṣasa, the brahmin-slayer Dhundhu. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य वारिमयं वेगमापीय स नराधिपः ।
वह्निबाणेन वह्निं तु शमयामास वारिणा ॥ ३५॥

English
The king quaffed off the gushing water through fiery arrows and quelled the fire through water. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तं निहत्य महाकायं बलेनोदकराक्षसम् ।
उत्तङ्कस्येक्षयामास कृतं कर्म नराधिपः ॥ ३६॥

English
After killing the aquatic demon of huge body with his strength, the king requested Uttaṅka to survey his work. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तङ्कस्तु वरं प्रादात्तस्मै राज्ञे महामुने ।
अददच्चाक्षयं वित्तं शत्रुभिश्चापराजयम् ॥ ३७॥

English
O great sage, Uttaṅka granted him boons. He gave him never-ending wealth and invincibility to enemies. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्मे मतिं च सततं स्वर्गे वासं तथाक्षयम् ।
पुत्राणां चाक्षयं लोकं रक्षसा ये तु संहताः ॥ ३८॥

English
He blessed him with interest in virtue, perpetual residence in the heaven and the imperishable world to his sons who were killed in the battle. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रास्त्रयः शिष्टाः दृढाश्वः श्रेष्ठ उच्यते ।
हंसाश्वकपिलाश्वौ च कुमारौ तत्कनीयसौ ॥ ३९॥

English
Three of his sons survived. The eldest of them was Dr̥dhāśva. Haṁsāśva and Kapilāśva were younger princes. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
धौन्धुमारिर्दृढाश्वो यो हर्यश्वस्तस्य चात्मजः ।
हर्यश्वस्य निकुम्भोऽभूत्पुत्रो धर्मरतः सदा ॥ ४०॥

English
Haryaśva was the son of Dr̥dhāśva who was the son of Dhundhumāra. Nikumbha who was always engaged in sacred rites was the son of Haryaśva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
संहताश्वो निकुम्भस्य पुत्रो रणविशारदः ।
अक्षाश्वश्च कृताश्वश्च संहताश्वसुतौ द्विजाः ॥ ४१॥

English
Saṁhatāśva an expert in war was the son of Nikumbha. Akṣāśva and Kṛtāśva were the sons of Saṁhatāśva. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य हैमवती भार्या सतां मान्या दृषद्वती ।
विख्याता त्रिषु लोकेषु पुत्रस्तस्याः प्रसेनजित् ॥ ४२॥

English
He had two daughters Haimavatī and Dr̥ṣadvatī honoured by the good. She was famous in the three worlds. Her son was Prasenajit. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
लेभे प्रसेनजिद्भार्यां गौरीं नाम पतिव्रताम् ।
अभिशप्ता तु सा भर्त्रा नदी सा बाहुदा कृता ॥ ४३॥

English
Prasenajit had a chaste wife Gauri. She was cursed by her husband and turned into the river Bahuda. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पुत्रो महानासीद्युवनाश्वो महीपतिः ।
मान्धाता युवनाश्वस्य त्रिषु लोकेषु विश्रुतः ॥ ४४॥

English
His great son was Yuvanasva, the lord of the earth. Mandhata famous in the three worlds was Yuvanasva's son. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य चैत्ररथी भार्या शशबिन्दुसुताऽभवत् ।
पतिव्रता च ज्येष्ठा च भ्रातॄणामयुतस्य सा ॥ ४५॥

तस्यामुत्पादयामास मान्धाता द्वौ सुतौ तदा ।
पुरुकुत्सं च धर्मज्ञं मुचुकुन्दं च धार्मिकम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Sasabindu's daughter the chaste Caitrarathi was his wife. She was the eldest sister of ten thousand brothers. Mandhata begot of her two sons, Purukutsa, the knower of sacred rites and Mucukunda the righteous. (45-46.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरुकुत्ससुतस्त्वासीद्विद्वांस्त्रय्यारुणिः कविः ।
तस्य सत्यव्रतो नाम कुमारोऽभून्महाबली ॥ ४७॥

English
Purukutsa's son was Trayyaruṇi. His son was Satyavrata. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
पाणिग्रहणमन्त्राणां विघ्रं चक्रे महात्मभिः ।
येन भार्या हृता पूर्वं कृतोद्वाहः परस्य वै ॥ ४८॥

बलात्कामाच्च मोहाच्च संहर्षाच्च यदोत्कटात् ।
जहार कन्यां कामाच्च कस्यचित्पुरवासिनः ॥ ४९॥

अधर्मसङ्गिनं तं तु राजा त्रय्यारुणिस्त्यजन् ।
अपध्वंसेति बहुशोऽवदत्क्रोधसमन्वितः ॥ ५०॥

English
He was evil-minded. Whenever sacred mantras were recited he put obstacles. After the marriage was celebrated he abducted the brides of others with force, out of lust, delusion, fun or arrogance. He abducted the virgins to satisfy his lust. The king Trayyaruṇi forsook him for such evil practices. Infuriated he called him a disgraceful wretch. (48-50.)

---

Sanskrit
पितरं सोऽब्रवीन्द् दुष्टः क्व गच्छामीति वै तदा ।
वस श्वपाकनिकटे राजा प्राहेति तं तदा ॥ ५१॥

English
When cast-off he asked his father where to go. The king asked him to stay with the outcasts. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

पित्रा त्यक्तोऽवसद्वीरो धर्मपालेन भूभुजा ॥ ५२॥

English
Cast off by his father the righteous king and protector, the heroic Satyavrata lived with the outcasts. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्रय्यारुणी राजा विरक्तः पुत्रकर्मणा ।
स शङ्करतपः कर्तुं सर्वं त्यक्त्वा वनं ययौ ॥ ५३॥

English
Becoming detached due to the activities of his son, the king Trayyaruṇi forsook everything and went to the forest in order to perform penance to propitiate Siva. (53.)

---

Sanskrit

समा द्वादश विप्रर्षे तेनाधर्मेण वै तदा ॥ ५४॥

English
O brahmin sage, due to that sinful misdeed Indra did not rain in his kingdom for twelve years. (54.)

Sanskrit
दारांस्तस्य तु विषये विश्वामित्रो महातपाः ।
सन्त्यज्य सागरानूपे चचार विपुलं तपः ॥ ५५॥

English
Viśvāmitra of great penance abandoned his wife in that land and performed extensive penance in the marshy foreshore of the ocean. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य पत्नी गले बद्ध्वा मध्यमं पुत्रमौरसम् ।
शेषस्य भरणार्थाय व्यक्रीणाद्गोशतेन च ॥ ५६॥

English
His wife tied her middle son round her neck and offered him for sale in exchange for a hundred cows in order to sustain the other children. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तां तु दृष्ट्वा गले बद्धं विक्रीणन्तीं स्वमात्मजम् ।
महर्षिपुत्रं धर्मात्मा मोचयामास तं तदा ॥ ५७॥

English
On seeing her offering her own son, tied round her neck, for sale, Satyavrata released him. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यव्रतो महाबाहुर्भरणं तस्य चाकरोत् ।
विश्वामित्रस्य तुष्ट्यर्थमनुक्रोशार्थमेव च ॥ ५८॥

English
The mighty Satyabrata sustained him just to satisfy Visvamitra and out of human sympathy. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तदारभ्य स पुत्रस्तु विश्वामित्रस्य वै मुनेः ।
अभवद्गालवो नाम गलबन्धान्महातपाः ॥ ५९॥

English
Ever since then, that son of sage Viśvāmitra came to be called Gālava because he was tied round the neck. He too performed great penance. (59.)
