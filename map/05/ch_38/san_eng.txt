### **CHAPTER THIRTYEIGHT - (From Satyavrata to Sagara)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
सत्यव्रतस्तु तद्भक्त्या कृपया च प्रतिज्ञया ।
विश्वामित्रकलत्रं च पोषयामास वै तदा ॥ १॥

English
By his devotion to Viśvāmitra his compassionate nature and his vow, Satyavrata nurtured and looked after Viśvāmitra's wife. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
हत्वा मृगान्वराहांश्च महिषांश्च वनेचरान् ।
विश्वामित्राश्रमाभ्याशे तन्मांसं चाक्षिपन्मुने ॥ २॥

English
O sage, he killed deer, boars and buffaloes of the forest and he cast off their flesh near the hermitage of Viśvāmitra. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तीर्थं गां चैव गात्रं च तथैवान्तःपुरं मुनिः ।
याज्योपाध्यायसंयोगाद्वसिष्ठः पर्यरक्षत ॥ ३॥

English
In virtue of his being the priest and the teacher the sage Vasiṣṭha looked after the holy centres, cows, clans and the harem. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्यव्रतस्य वाक्याद्वा भाविनोऽर्थस्य वै बलात् ।
वसिष्ठोऽभ्यधिकं मन्युं धारयामास नित्यशः ॥ ४॥

English
Vasiṣṭha nursed more and more grudge against Satyavrata forced by the gravity of what was destined to happen. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
पित्रा तु तं तदा राष्ट्रात्परित्यक्तं स्वमात्मजम् ।
न वारयामास मुनिर्वसिष्ठः कारणेन च ॥ ५॥

English
Vasiṣṭha had not prevented the father from exiling the son from the kingdom because there was sufficient cause for the same. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पाणिग्रहणमन्त्राणां निष्ठा स्यात्सप्तमे पदे ।
न च सत्यव्रतस्तस्य तमुपांशुमबुद्ध्यत ॥ ६॥

English
When the seventh step is taken (round the fire in the altar) the marriage becomes valid. But Satyavrata did not understand this secret. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्स परितोषाय पितुरासीन्महात्मनः ।
कुलस्य निष्कृतिर्विप्र कृता सा वै भवेदिति ॥ ७॥

English
Only for the satisfaction of his father did he do the same thinking that the family customs should remain unviolated. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
न तं वसिष्ठो भगवान्पित्रा त्यक्तं न्यवारयत् ।
अभिषेक्ष्याम्यहं पुत्रमस्य नैवाब्रवीन्मुनिः ॥ ८॥

English
When he was abandoned by his father Vasiṣṭha did not interfere on his behalf. The sage proclaimed that he would never install him in this kingdom. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स तु द्वादश वर्षाणि दीक्षां तामुद्वहद् बली ।
अविद्यामाने मांसे तु वसिष्ठस्य महात्मनः ॥ ९॥

सर्वकामदुहां दोग्ध्रीं ददर्श स नृपात्मजः ।
तां वै क्रोधाच्च लोभाच्च श्रमाद्वै च क्षुधान्वितः ॥ १०॥

दाशधर्मगतो राजा तां जघान स वै मुने ।
स तं मांसं स्वयं चैव विश्वामित्रस्य चात्मजम् ॥ ११॥

भोजयामास तच्छ्रुत्वा वसिष्ठो ह्यस्य चुक्रुधे ।
उवाच च मुनिश्रेष्ठस्तं तदा क्रोधसंयुतः ॥ १२॥

English
During the twelve years of famine and drought Satyavrata observed his vow. When there was no meat available the prince saw the wish-yielding cow of Vasiṣṭha. O sage, the king who observed the ten tenets³⁰ of Dharma killed the cow either due to anger or greed or exhaustion or hunger. The meat of the same he utilised to feed Viśvāmitra's son. On hearing of it the sage Vasiṣṭha became angry and said. (9-12.)

---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठ उवाच ।
पातयेयमहं क्रूर तव शङ्कुमशंसयम् ।
यदि ते द्वाविमौ शङ्कू नस्यातां वै कृतौ पुरा ॥ १३॥

English
# Vasiṣṭha said  If the two iron stakes thrust by me fail I shall fix another cruel one. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पितुश्चापरितोषेण गुरोर्दोग्ध्रीवधेन च ।
अप्रोक्षितोपयोगाच्च त्रिविधस्ते व्यतिक्रमः ॥ १४॥

English
Your transgression is threefold. You have displeased your father, killed the cow of your preceptor and used things unsprinkled with holy water. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिशङ्कुरिति होवाच त्रिशङ्कुरिति स स्मृतः ।
विश्वामित्रस्तु दाराणामागतो भरणे कृते ॥ १५॥

तेन तस्मै वरं प्रादान्मुनिः प्रीतस्त्रिशङ्कवे ।
छन्द्यमानो वरेणाथ वरं वव्रे नृपात्मजः ॥ १६॥

English
He called him Triśaṅku and ever since he is known as Triśaṅku. When Viśvāmitra returned he was pleased with Triśaṅku for having maintained his family during his absence. When pressed to choose a boon the prince chose it. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
अनावृष्टिभये चास्मिञ्जाते द्वादशवार्षिके ।
अभिषिच्य पितू राज्ये याजयामास तं मुनिः ॥ १७॥

English
When there was drought for twelve years he had helped his family, therefore the sage anointed him in the kingdom of his father and officiated as priest in his coronation. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मिषतां देवतानां च वसिष्ठस्य च कौशिकः ।
सशरीरं तदा तं तु दिवमारोहयत्प्रभुः ॥ १८॥

English
Ever as the gods and Visiṣṭha were watching, the saintly lord Viśvāmitra made him ascend heaven in his physical body. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य सत्यरथा नाम भार्या केकयवंशजा ।
कुमारं जनयामास हरिश्चन्द्रमकल्मषम् ॥ १९॥

English
His wife Satyarathā, hailing from the family of Kekayas³¹ bore him a son who was named Hariścandra. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स वै राजा हरिश्चन्द्रो त्रैशङ्कव इति स्मृतः ।
आहर्ता राजसूयस्य सम्राडिति ह विश्रुतः ॥ २०॥

English
That king Hariścandra is known as Traiśaṅkava. He is famous as the performer of the Rājasūya sacrifice and as an Emperor. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिश्चन्द्रस्य हि सुतो रोहितो नाम विश्रुतः ।
रोहितस्य वृकः पुत्रो वृकाद् बाहुस्तु जज्ञिवान् ॥ २१॥

English
Hariśchandra's son Rohita was famous. Rohita's son was Vṛka and Bāhu was born of Tṛka. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
हैहयास्तालजङ्घाश्च निरस्यन्ति स्म तं नृपम् ।
नात्यर्थे धार्मिको विप्राः स हि धर्मयुगेऽभवत् ॥ २२॥

English
Haihayas and Tālajaṅghas removed that king. O brahmin, he was very virtuous. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सगरं स सुतं बाहुर्जज्ञे सह गरेण वै ।
और्वस्याश्रममासाद्य भार्गवेणाभिरक्षितः ॥ २३॥

English
Bāhu begot a son. Sagara was born with poison. Reaching the hermitage of Aurva, he was saved by Bhārgava. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
आग्नेयमस्त्रं लब्ध्वा च भार्गवात्सगरो नृपः ।
जिगाय पृथिवीं हत्वा तालजङ्घान्स हैहयान ॥ २४॥

English
Securing fiery missiles from Bhārgava king Sagara conquered the earth after killing Tālajaṅghas$^{32}$ and Haihayas.$^{33}$ (24.)

---

Sanskrit
शकान्बहूदकांश्चैव पारदान्सगणान्खशान् ।
सुधर्मं स्थापयामास शशास वृषतः क्षितिम् ॥ २५॥

English
He defeated Śakas,$^{34}$ Bahūdakas, Pāradas$^{35}$ tagaṇas$^{36}$ and Khaśas.$^{37}$ He established a good religious cult and ruled over the earth virtuously. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
स वै गरेण सहितः कथं जातस्तु क्षत्रियात् ।
जितवानेतदाचक्ष्व विस्तरेण हि सूतज ॥ २६॥

English
*Śaunaka Said :—*  How was he born with poison ? How did he conquer the Kṣatriyas ? O son of sūta, please narrate this in detail. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
पारीक्षितेन सम्पृष्टो वैशम्पायन एव च ।
यदाचष्ट स्म तद्वक्ष्ये श‍ृणुष्वैकमना मुने ॥ २७॥

English
*Sūta said :—*  O sage, listen with attention. I shall narrate what Vaiśampāyana said on being asked by Janamejaya, son of Pārikṣita. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
परीक्षितोवाच ।
कथं स सगरो राजा गरेण सहितो मुने ।
जातः स जघ्निवान्भूपानेतदाख्यातुमर्हसि ॥ २८॥

English
*Pārikṣita said :—*  O sage, how was the king born with poison ? How did he kill the kings ? Please narrate this. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
वैशम्पायन उवाच ।
बाहोर्व्यसनिनस्तातहृतं राज्यमभूत्किल ।
हैहयैस्तालजङ्घैश्च शकैः सार्धं विशाम्पते ॥ २९॥

English
*Vaiśampāyana said :—*  O dear, O lord of the subjects, the kingdom of Bāhu who indulged in vices was captured by Haihayas and Tālajaṅghas and the Śakas. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यवनाः पारदाश्चैव काम्बोजाः पाह्लवास्तथा ।
बहूदकाश्च पञ्चैव गणाः प्रोक्ताश्च रक्षसाम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Five groups of Rākṣasas are mentioned, viz.—Yavanas,$^{38}$ Pāradas,$^{39}$ Kāmbojas,$^{40}$ Pahlavas$^{41}$ and Bahūdakas.$^{42}$ (30.)

---

Sanskrit
एते पञ्च गणा राजन् हैहयार्थेषु रक्षसाम् ।
कृत्वा पराक्रमान् बाहो राज्यं तेभ्यो ददुर्बलात् ॥ ३१॥

English
O king, these five groups of Rākṣasas pursuing activities of exploit on behalf of the Haihayas seized the kingdom of Bāhu and gave it to the Haihayas. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हतराज्यस्ततो राजा स वै बाहुर्वनं ययौ ।
पत्न्या चानुगतो दुःखी स वै प्राणानवासृजत् ॥ ३२॥

English
Having lost the kingdom, Bāhu went to the forest along with his wife. Distressed that he was he abandoned his life. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पत्नी या यादवी तस्य सगर्भा पृष्ठतो गता ।
सपत्न्या च गरस्तस्यै दत्तः पूर्वसुतेर्ष्यया ॥ ३३॥

English
One of his wives belonging to the house of Yadu followed him in pregnancy. Due to jealousy as a result of her expected son before her, the co-wife administered poison to her. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सा तु भर्तुश्चितां कृत्वा ज्वलनं चावरोहत ।
और्वस्तां भार्गवो राजन्कारुण्यात्समवारयत् ॥ ३४॥

English
She made the funeral pyre of her husband ready and was about to enter the fire, Aurva Bhārgava mercifully prevented her. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याश्रमे स्थिता राज्ञी गर्भरक्षणहेतवे ।
सिषेवे मुनिवर्यं तं स्मरन्ती शङ्करं हृदा ॥ ३५॥

English
The queen stayed in his hermitage for the sake of her child in the womb. She served the sage, mentally remembering Śiva. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा खलु तद् गर्भो गरेणैव सह च्युतः ।
सुमुहूर्त्ते सुलग्ने च पञ्चोच्चग्रहसंयुते ॥ ३६॥

English
Once when the Muhūrta and the Lagna were good when the five planets were ascendant the child was born along with the poison administered to the queen. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिँल्लग्ने च बलिनि सर्वथा मुनिसत्तम ।
व्यजायत महाबाहुः सगरो नाम पार्थिवः ॥ ३७॥

English
In that auspicious Lagna, O excellent sage, the king Sagara, of mighty arms, was born. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
और्वस्तु जातकर्मादि तस्य कृत्वा महात्मनः ।
अध्याप्य वेदशास्त्राणि ततोऽस्त्रं प्रत्यपादयत् ॥ ३८॥

English
Aurva performed the postnatal rites of that prince. He taught him Vedas and Śāstras and instructed him in the use of missiles. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
आग्नेयं तं महाभागो ह्यमरैरपि दुःसहम् ।
जग्राह विधिना प्रीत्या सगरोऽसौ नृपोत्तमः ॥ ३९॥

English
The blessed Sagara, earnestly learnt the lore of the fiery missile, in accordance with the rules of procedure, the missile that is unbearable even to the gods. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
स तेनास्त्रबलेनैव बलेन च समन्वितः ।
हैहयान्विजघानाशु सङ्क्रुद्धोऽस्त्रबलेन च ॥ ४०॥

English
The infuriated Sagaras, equipped with this miraculous and other weapons and with his own natural strength, killed the Haihayas. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
आजहार च लोकेषु कीर्तिं कीर्तिमतां वरः ।
धर्मं संस्थापयामास सगरोऽसौ महीतले ॥ ४१॥

English
This Sagara became foremost among the famous, earned fame in all the worlds and established piety on the earth. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शकाः सयवनाः काम्बोजाः पाह्लवास्तथा ।
हन्यमानास्तदा ते तु वसिष्ठं शरणं ययुः ॥ ४२॥

English
Then the Śakas, Yavanas, Kāmbojas and Pahlavas, being destroyed sought refuge in Vasiṣṭha. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
वसिष्ठो वञ्चनां कृत्वा समयेन महाद्युतिः ।
सगरं वारयामास तेषां दत्वाभयं नृपम् ॥ ४३॥

English
After deceitfully compelling them to enter into an agreement, Vasiṣṭha of great brilliance offered them freedom from fear and brought them to king Sagara. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
सगरः स्वां प्रतिज्ञां तु गुरोर्वाक्यं निशम्य च ।
धर्मं जघान तेषां वै केशान्यत्वं चकार ह ॥ ४४॥

English
At the instance of his priest Sagara maintained his vow by destroying their mode of worship and effecting alterations in their hair style. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्धं शकानां शिरसो मुण्डं कृत्वा व्यसर्जयत् ।
यवनानां शिरः सर्वं काम्बोजानां तथैव च ॥ ४५॥

English
He released the Śakas after shaving off half of their heads. Complete tonsure was assigned to Yavanas and Kambojas. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
पारदा मुण्डकेशाश्च पाह्नवाश्श्मश्रुधारिणः ।
निःस्वाध्यायवषट्काराः कृतास्तेन महात्मना ॥ ४६॥

English
Pāradas were given close hair-cut and Pahlavas were asked to grow beard and moustache. All of them were deprived of the right of the Vedic study and the use of Vaṣaṭkāra. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
जिता च सकला पृथ्वी धर्मतस्तेन भूभुजा ।
सर्वे ते क्षत्रियास्तात धर्महीनाः कृताः पुरा ॥ ४७॥

English
All those Kṣatriyas who had been deprived of virtue were re-instated in piety. The entire earth was conquered by him virtuously. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
स धर्मविजयी राजा विजित्वेमां वसुन्धराम् ।
अश्वं संस्कारयामास वाजिमेधाय पार्थिवः ॥ ४८॥

English
Thus conquering the earth virtuously the king instituted a horse-sacrifice. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य चारयतः सोऽश्वः समुद्रे पूर्वदक्षिणे ।
गतः षष्टिसहस्रैस्तु तत्पुत्रैरन्वितो मुने ॥ ४९॥

English
O sage, the sacrificial horse was let loose, followed by his sixty thousand sons. It reached the shore of the ocean in the south-eastern region. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
देवराजेन शक्रेण सोऽश्वो हि स्वार्थसाधिना ।
वेलासमीपेऽपहृतो भूमिं चैव प्रवेशितः ॥ ५०॥

English
It was stealthily removed by Indra, king of the gods, for his selfish ends near the sea-shore and taken underground. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

स तं देशं तदा पुत्रैः खानयामास सर्वतः ॥ ५१॥

English
In order to search out the horse, king Sagara caused the country around dug up through his sons. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
आसेदुस्ते ततस्तत्र खन्यमाने महार्णवे ।

English
While it was being dug near the ocean, they met the sage Kapila, the primordial Puruṣa of cosmic form. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य चक्षुःसमुत्थेन वह्निना प्रतिबुध्यतः ।
दग्धाः षष्टिसहस्राणि चत्वारस्त्ववशेषिताः ॥ ५३॥

English
As he woke up from trance all but four of the sixty thousand sons were burnt by the fire from his eyes. (53.)

---

Sanskrit

शूरः पञ्चजनश्चैव तस्य वंशकरा नृपाः ॥ ५४॥

English
The four who were spared were Harṣaketu, Suketu, Dharmaratha and Pañcajana. They became the kings establishing his line. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रादाच्च तस्मै भगवान् हरिः पञ्च वरान्स्वयम् ।
वंशं मेधां च कीर्तिं च समुद्रं तनयं धनम् ॥ ५५॥

English
Lord Viṣṇu granted him five boons, viz. flourishing family, intelligence, fame, the ocean as son and wealth. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
सागरत्वं च लेभे स कर्मणा तस्य तेन वै ।
तं चाश्वमेधिकं सोऽश्वं समुद्रादुपलब्धवान् ॥ ५६॥

English
By that virtue he attained the fatherhood of ocean. He regained the sacrificial horse from the ocean. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
आजहाराश्वमेधानां शतं स तु महायशाः ।
ईजे शम्भुविभूतीश्च देवतास्तत्र सुव्रताः ॥ ५७॥

English
He performed a hundred horse-sacrifices and became famous. He acquired wealth bestowed by Śiva. He propitiated the deities by performing the sacred rites. (57.)
