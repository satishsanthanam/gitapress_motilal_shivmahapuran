### **CHAPTER FORTYONE - (The attainment of the seven hunters)**

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
सप्त ते तपतां श्रेष्ठ स्वर्गे पितृगणाः स्मृताः ।
चत्वारो मूर्त्तिमन्तो वै त्रयश्चैव ह्यमूर्तयः ॥ १॥

English
Sanatkumāra said:—  O foremost among the performers of penance, the Pitṛs in the heaven are seven in number. Four of them are embodied and three bodiless. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तान्यजन्ते देवगणा आद्या विप्रादयस्तथा ।
आप्याययन्ति ते पूर्वं सोमं योगबलेन वै ॥ २॥

English
The primordial groups of gods, brahmins and others worship them. With the power of their Yoga they strengthen and gladden Soma. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छ्राद्धानि देयानि योगिनां तु विशेषतः ।
सर्वेषां राजतं पात्रमथवा रजतान्वितम् ॥ ३॥

दत्तं स्वधां पुरोधाय श्राद्धं प्रीणाति वै पितृन् ।
वह्नेराप्यायनं कृत्वा सोमस्य तु यमस्य वै ॥ ४॥

उदगायनमप्यग्नावग्न्यभावेऽप्सु वा पुनः ।
पितॄन्प्रीणाति यो भक्त्या पितरः प्रीणयन्ति तम् ॥ ५॥

यच्छन्ति पितरः पुष्टिं प्रजाश्च विपुलास्तथा ।
स्वर्गमारोग्यवृद्धिं च यदन्यदपि चेप्सितम् ॥ ६॥

English
Hence people shall offer Śrāddhas especially to the Yogins. A silver vessel or a vessel with silver when offered with Svadhā, delights the Pīṭṛs. When the sun is in the northern transit he shall make offerings in the fire or in its absence in water. He shall propitiate fire-god, Soma or Yama. Those who delight the Pīṭṛs with devotion are delighted by Pīṭṛs. The Pīṭṛs bestow nourishment, offspring, heaven, health and other desired objects. (3-6.)

---

Sanskrit
देवकार्यादपि मुने पितृकार्यं विशिष्यते ।
पितृभक्तोऽसि विप्रर्षे तेन त्वमजरामरः ॥ ७॥

English
O sage, the rites of Pīṭṛs are better than the rites of the gods. O brahmin sage, since you are a devotee of the Pīṭṛs you shall be free from old age and death. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
न योगेन गतिः सा तु पितृभक्तस्य या मुने ।
पितृभक्तिर्विशेषेण तस्मात्कार्या महामुने ॥ ८॥

English
O sage, the goal attained by devotion to the Pīṭṛs is not attained by the practices of Yoga. O great sage, so devotion to the Pīṭṛs shall be pursued with care. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्कण्डेय उवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वाशु देवेशो देवानामपि दुर्लभम् ।
चक्षुर्दत्त्वा सविज्ञानं जगाम यौगिकीं गतिम् ॥ ९॥

English
# Mārkaṇḍeya said :—  After saying thus and immediately bestowing the vision of perfect knowledge the lord of the gods disappeared. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु भीष्म पुरा भूयो भारद्वाजात्मजा द्विजाः ।
योगधर्ममनुप्राप्य भ्रष्टा दुश्चरितेन वै ॥ १०॥

English
O Bhīṣma, listen again. Formerly, even after learning the code of Yoga, the brahmin sons of Bhāradvāja were degraded due to their evil course. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
वाग्दुष्टः क्रोधनो हिंस्रः पिशुनः कविरेव च ।
खसृषः पितृवर्ती च नामभिः कर्मभिस्तथा ॥ ११॥

English
Their names which indicate their activities are Vāgduṣṭa (defiled in speech), Krodhana (angry), Himsra (violent), Piśuna (backbiter), Kavi (poet), Svasṛṣa (self-creating) and Pitṛvartin (worshipping the pīṭṛs) (11.)

---

Sanskrit
कौशिकस्य सुतास्तात शिष्या गर्गस्य चाभवन् ।
पितर्युपरते सर्वे प्रवसन्तस्तदाभवन् ॥ १२॥

English
O dear, the sons of Kauśika became the disciples of Garga. When their father had expired they went in exile. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
विनियोगाद् गुरोस्तस्य गां दोग्ध्रीं समकालयन् ।
समानवत्सां कपिलां सर्वेऽन्यायागतास्तदा ॥ १३॥

English
At the instance of their preceptor they looked after his cow and her calf. All of them were unjust in their actions. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां पथि क्षुधार्तानां बाल्यान्मोहाच्च भारत ।
क्रूरा बुद्धिः समुत्पन्ना तां गां वै हिंसितुं तदा ॥ १४॥

English
O descendant of Bharata, once in their course of wandering in the forest, they were oppressed by hunger. They had the cruel intention of injuring the cow then. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तां कविः खसृपश्चैव याचेते नैति वै तदा ।
न चाशक्यास्तु ताभ्यां वा तदा वारयितुं निजाः ॥ १५॥

English
Kavi and Svasṛṣa forbade them do so. But they could not be prevented from that act. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृवर्ती तु यस्तेषां नित्यं श्राद्धाह्निको द्विजः ।
स सर्वानब्रवीत्कोपात् पितृभक्तिसमन्वितः ॥ १६॥

English
The brahmin Pitṛvartin who performed Śrāddha everyday with devotion to the Pīṭṛs spoke to them angrily. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यशक्यं प्रकर्तव्यं पितृनुद्दिश्य साध्यताम् ।
प्रकुर्वन्तो हि श्राद्धं तु सर्व एव समाहिताः ॥ १७॥

English
If it is not possible to stop this do it with the Pīṭṛs in view. Perform the Śrāddha, ye all cautiously. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमेषा च गौर्धर्मं प्राप्स्यते नात्र संशयः ।
पितॄनभ्यर्च्य धर्मेण नाधर्मो नो भविष्यति ॥ १८॥

English
If performed thus, the cow will attain piety undoubtedly. By worshipping Pīṭṛs we shall not be affected by sin. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्ताश्च ते सर्वे प्रोक्षयित्वा च गां तदा ।
पितृभ्यः कल्पयित्वा तु ह्युपायुञ्जत भारत ॥ १९॥

English
O descendant of Bharata, on being advised thus, they all sprinkled the cow with sacred water, dedicated it to the Pīṭṛs and used it as their food. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
उपयुज्य च गां सर्वे गुरोस्तस्य न्यवेदयन् ।
शार्दूलेन हता धेनुर्वत्सा वै गृह्यतामिति ॥ २०॥

English
After eating the cow they said to the preceptor saying—"The cow is killed by a tiger. Let the calf be accepted." (20.)

---

Sanskrit
आर्जवात्स तु तं वत्सं प्रतिजग्राह वै द्विजः ।
मिथ्योपचारतः पापमभूत्तेषां च गोघ्नताम् ॥ २१॥

English
The sage accepted the calf with great distress. The killers of the cow became sinful by their false reverence and service. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कालेन कियता कालधर्ममुपागताः ।
ते सप्त भ्रातरस्तात बभूवुः स्वायुषः क्षये ॥ २२॥

English
O dear, in due course, when their term of life expired the seven brothers passed away. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ते वै क्रूरतया हैंस्त्र्यात्स्वानार्यत्वाद् गुरोस्तथा ।
उग्रहिंसाविहाराश्च जाताः सप्त सहोदराः ॥ २३॥

लुब्धकस्य सुतास्तावद् बलवन्तो मनस्विनः ।
जाता व्याधा दशार्णेषु सप्त धर्मविचक्षणाः ॥ २४॥

English
They were reborn as the sons of a hunter as a result of their cruelty, violence, being ignoble towards the preceptor and their over-indulgence in fierce violence. They were reborn in the country of Daśārṇas.⁴⁴ They were strong, intelligent and experts in piety. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वधर्मनिरताः सर्वे मृगा मोहविवर्जिताः ।
आसन्नुद्वेगसंविग्ना रम्ये कालञ्जरे गिरौ ॥ २५॥

English
They were engaged in the practice of sacred rites. They were free from the delusion of hunting animals. On the beautiful mountain Kālañjara they passed their time with distress. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तमेवार्थमनुध्याय ज्ञातिस्मरणसम्भवम् ।
आसन्वनचराः क्षान्ता निर्द्वन्द्वा निष्परिग्रहाः ॥ २६॥

English
Recollecting the event of their death the forest-roamers became forbearers, free from Dvandvas and averse to taking gifts. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ते सर्वे शुभकर्माणः सद्धर्माणो वनेचराः ।
विधर्माचरणैर्हीना जातिस्मरणसिद्धयः ॥ २७॥

English
The hunters performed auspicious rites, and holy deeds, disassociating themselves from the wicked. They had the power of the memory of the previous birth. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वजातिषु यो धर्मः श्रुतो गुरुकुलेषु वै ।
तथैव चास्थिता बुद्धिः संसारेऽपि निवर्तने ॥ २८॥

English
Whatever sacred rites they had heard in the preceptor's hermitage in the previous births were retained in their minds. So also the goal of non-return to this world. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
गिरिमध्ये जहुः प्राणाँल्लब्धाहारास्तपस्विनः ।
तेषां तु पतितानां च यानि स्थानानि भारत ॥ २९॥

तथैवाद्यापि दृश्यन्ते गिरौ कालञ्जरे नृप ।
कर्मणा तेन ते जाताः शुभाशुभविवर्जकाः ॥ ३०॥

English
They performed their penance, had their food and finally cast off their lives on that mountain. O descendant of Bharata, O king, the different places where they fell dead are still seen in the same manner on the mount Kālañjara.⁴⁵ Thanks to their activities neither auspicious nor inauspicious, they were reborn in a life neither auspicious nor inauspicious. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
शुभाच्छुभतरां योनिं चक्रवाकत्वमागताः ।
शुभे देशे शरद्वीपे सप्तैवासञ्जलौकसः ॥ ३१॥

English
On an island in an auspicious spot the seven became aquatic birds. They were reborn as Cakravāka birds in a life that is neither auspicious nor inauspicious. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
त्यक्त्वा सहचरीधर्मं मुनयो धर्मधारिणः ।
निःसङ्गो निर्ममश्शान्तो निर्द्वन्द्वो निष्परिग्रहः ॥ ३२॥

English
They abandoned the contact with their mates. They were like sages practising sacred rites, free from associations and Egotistic feelings. They remained calm. They did not accept gifts. They were free from Dvandvas. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्तिनिर्वृतश्चैव शकुना नामतः स्मृताः ।
ते ब्रह्मचारिणः सर्वे शकुना धर्मचारिणः ॥ ३३॥

English
They were birds only in name. They were holy bachelors delighted in renunciation. They were birds practising sacred rites. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
जातिस्मराः सुसंवृद्धाः सप्तैव ब्रह्मचारिणः ।
स्थिता एकत्र सद्धर्मा विकाररहिताः सदा ॥ ३४॥

English
They could remember their previous births. They grew old even as they were bachelors. They remained together free from aberrations and performed good rites. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
विप्रयोनौ तु यन्मोहान्मिथ्यापचरितं गुरौ ।
तिर्यग्योनौ तथा जन्म श्राद्धाज्ज्ञानं च लेभिरे ॥ ३५॥

English
When they were born as brahmins they acted falsely to their preceptor. Still in their birth as birds they attained knowledge as a result of the Śrāddha they had performed. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा तु पितृकार्यार्थं कृतं श्राद्धं व्यवस्थितैः ।
तदा ज्ञानं च जातिं च क्रमात्प्राप्तं गुणोत्तरम् ॥ ३६॥

English
They had performed the Śrāddha for the Pīṭṭs with due rituals. They retained memory of previous noble birth. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वजातिषु यद्ब्रह्म श्रुतं गुरुकुलेषु वै ।
तथैव संस्थित ज्ञानं तस्माज्ज्ञानं समभ्यसेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
The knowledge of Brahma practised by the ancients or found in the preceptors' families stands as of yore even today. One shall practise that knowledge therefore. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सुमनाश्च सुवाक्छुद्धः पञ्चमश्छिद्रदर्शकः ।
स्वतन्त्रश्च सुयज्ञश्च कुलीङ्गा नामतः स्मृताः ॥ ३८॥

English
They were of noble birth and were named Svatantra. Suyajña, Sumanas, Suvākṣuddha and the fifth one Chidradarśaka. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां तत्र विहङ्गानां चरतां धर्मचारिणाम् ।
सुवृत्तमभवत्तत्र तच्छृणुष्व महामुने ॥ ३९॥

English
While they were practising sacred rites an auspicious event happened there. O great sage, please listen to that. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
नीपानामीश्वरो राजा प्रभावेण समन्वितः ।
श्रीमानन्तःपुरवृतो वनं तत्राविवेश ह ॥ ४०॥

English
The prosperous king of Nīpas,⁴⁶ endowed with strength, and accompanied by his harem entered that forest. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वतन्त्रश्चक्रवाकः स स्पृहयामास तं नृपम् ।
दृष्ट्वा यान्तं सुखोपेतं राज्यशोभासमन्वितम् ॥ ४१॥

English
The Cakravaka Svatantra yearned much, on seeing the happy king endowed with the glory of the kingdom pass along. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मात्कर्म ब्रुवाणस्त्वं योगधर्ममवाप्य तम् ।
एवं वरं प्रार्थयसे तस्माद्वाक्यं निबोध मे ॥ ४६॥

English
After renouncing action by the Yogic practices why do you long for such a boon? Hence hear my statement. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यस्ति सुकृतं किञ्चित्तपो वा नियमोऽपि वा ।
खिन्नोऽहमुपवासेन तपसा निश्चलेन च ॥ ४२॥

तस्य सर्वस्य पूर्णेन फलेनापि कृतेन हि ।
सर्वसौभाग्यपात्रश्च भवेयमहमीदृशः ॥ ४३॥

English
I have become weary with the observance of fasts and steady penance. If there is a merit accruing from penance or the observance of checks and restraints let me become like him the abode of fortune and bliss. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्कण्डेय उवाच ।
ततस्तु चक्रवाकौ द्वावासतुः सहचारिणौ ।
आवां वै सचिवौ स्याव तव प्रियहितैषिणौ ॥ ४४॥

English
*Mārkaṇḍeya said:—*  Then two of his comrades said. “We shall be your ministers, your delighters and well-wishers.” (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तथेत्युक्त्वा तु तस्यासीत्तदा योगात्मनो गतिः ।
एवं तौ चक्रवाकौ च स्ववाक्यं प्रत्यभाषताम् ॥ ४५॥

English
After saying “So be it”, the Yogic soul attained his goal. The two Cakravākas replied to him. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
राजा त्वं भविता तात काम्पिल्ये नगरोत्तमे ।
एतौ ते सचिवौ स्यातां व्यभिचारप्रधर्षितौ ॥ ४७॥

English
O dear, you will become a king in the excellent city of Kāmpilya.⁴⁷ These too will be your ministers who will not go astray. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
न तानूचुस्त्रयो राज्यं चतुरः सहचारिणः ।
स प्रसादं पुनश्चक्रे तन्मध्ये सुमनाब्रवीत् ॥ ४८॥

English
The three did not speak about the kingdom to their four comrades. Being delighted Sumanas said again. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्तवान् भविता शापः पुनर्योगमवाप्स्यथ ।
सर्वसत्त्वरुतज्ञश्च स्वतन्त्रोऽयं भविष्यति ॥ ४९॥

English
When the curse is over you will attain Yoga. Sarvasattva, Suyajña and Svatantra too will attain yogic powers. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृप्रसादाद्युष्माभिः सम्प्राप्तं सुकृतं भवेत् ।
गां प्रोक्षयित्वा धर्मेण पितृभ्यश्चोपकल्पिताः ॥ ५०॥

English
Due to their favour you will attain merit. You have sprinkled the cow and offered it to the Pīṭṭs. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्माकं ज्ञानसंयोगः सर्वेषां योगसाधनम् ।
इदं च वाक्यं संरब्धं श्लोकमेकमुदाहृतम् ॥ ५१॥

English
We shall acquire knowledge which shall work as the means of Yoga for all. This statement is bold and spirited and is quoted as a verse. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरुषान्तरितं श्रुत्वा ततो योगमवाप्स्यथ ।
इत्युक्त्वा स तु मौनोऽभूद्विहङ्गः सुमना बुधः ॥ ५२॥

English
After attaining human life you will attain “Yoga”. After saying this the learned bird Sumanas became silent. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
मार्कण्डेय उवाच ।
लोकानां स्वस्तये तात शन्तनुप्रवरात्मज ।
इत्युक्तं तच्चरित्रं मे किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ५३॥

English
*Mārkaṇḍeya said :—*  Thus I have mentioned their story to you. What more do you wish to hear? (53.)
