### **CHAPTER FORTYTHREE - *(The mode of worshipping Vyāsa)***

---

Sanskrit
शौनक उवाच ।
आचार्यपूजनं ब्रूहि सूत व्यासगुरोऽधुना ।
ग्रन्थस्य श्रवणान्ते हि किं कर्तव्यं तदप्यहो ॥ १॥

English
*Śaunaka said:—*  O Sūta having Vyāsa as preceptor, please tell me now the mode of worshipping the preceptor. What shall be done at the end of listening to the holy book? Please mention that also. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
पूजयेद्विधिवद्भक्त्याचार्यं श्रुत्वा कथां पराम् ।
ग्रन्थान्ते विधिवद्दद्यादाचार्याय प्रसन्नधीः ॥ २॥

English
*Sūta said:—*  After hearing the story the devotee shall worship the preceptor duly with devotion. Delighted in mind he shall make liberal gifts duly to the preceptor at the conclusion of the holy book. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो वक्तारमानम्य सम्पूज्य च यथाविधि ।
भूषणैर्हस्तकर्णानां वस्त्रैः सौम्यादिभिः सुधीः ॥ ३॥

English
The intelligent devotee shall bow to the propounder and worship him duly. Gifts of ornaments for hands and ears and fine garments shall be made. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवपूजासमाप्तौ तु दद्याद्धेनुं सवत्सिकाम् ।
कृत्वासनं सुवर्णस्य पलमानस्य साम्बरम् ॥ ४॥

तत्रास्थाप्य शुभं ग्रन्थं लिखितं ललिताक्षरैः ।
आचार्याय सुधीर्दद्यान्मुक्तः स्याद्भवबन्धनैः ॥ ५॥

English
After the worship of Śiva he shall present to the preceptor a cow and its calf. He shall make a book-seat with gold, a Pala in weight and cover it with a good cloth. The manuscript of the holy book written in beautiful hand shall be placed on it and given to the preceptor. The intelligent devotee will be liberated from worldly bondage. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
ग्रामो गजो हयश्चापि यथाशक्त्यपराणि च ।
मुने सर्वाणि देयानि वाचकाय महात्मने ॥ ६॥

English
O sage, a village, an elephant or a horse or other things shall be given as possible to the noble preceptor who reads and propounds the story. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
विधानसहितं सम्यक् श्रुतं हि सफलं स्मृतम् ।
पुराणं शौनकमुने सत्यमेवोदितं मया ॥ ७॥

English
O sage Śaunaka, Purāṇa is efficacious if it is listened to in accordance with the rules. I speak truth to you. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्विधानयुक्तं तु श‍ृणुयाद्भक्तितो मुने ।
पुराणं निगमार्थाढ्यं पुण्यदं हृदयं श्रुतेः ॥ ८॥

English
O sage, the Purāṇa full of the meanings of the Vedas, the heart of the Vedas, the meritorious holy book shall be listened to with devotion and in accordance with the rules. (8.)
