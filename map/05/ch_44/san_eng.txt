### **CHAPTER FORTYFOUR - (The birth of Vyāsa)**

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
व्यासोत्पत्तिं महाबुद्धे ब्रूहि सूत दयानिधे ।
कृपया परया स्वामिन् कृतार्थान्नः कुरु प्रभो ॥ १॥

व्यासस्य जननी प्रोक्ता नाम्ना सत्यवती शुभा ।
विवाहिता तु सा देवी राज्ञा शन्तनुना किल ॥ २॥

तस्यां जातो महायोगी कथं व्यासः पराशरात् ।
सन्देहोऽत्र महाञ्जातस्तं भवाञ्छेत्तुमर्हति ॥ ३॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta of great intellect, O store-house of mercy, please narrate the birth of Vyāsa. O holy lord, please satisfy our curiosity. Vyāsa's mother is known as Satyavatī. She was married to king Śantanu. How was Vyāsa the great Yogin born of her from Parāsara? There is a great doubt in regard to that. It behoves you to remove that doubt. (1-3.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एकदा तीर्थयात्रायां व्रजन्योगी पराशरः ।
यदृच्छयागतो रम्यं यमुनायास्तटं शुभम् ॥ ४॥

English
Suta said:—  Once, while he was making his pilgrimage, Parasara the yogin casually came to the auspicious and beautiful banks of the Yamuna. (4.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The righteous soul spoke to the ferryman who was taking his meals "Take me across the Yamuna quickly in your boat". (5.)

---

Sanskrit

English
When the ferryman was thus urged by the sage he spoke to his daughter Matsyagandha: "Girl, quickly take this sage to the other bank in the boat. (6.)

---

Sanskrit

English
This sage, O fortunate girl, is born of the womb of Drsyana. He is the ocean of sacred rites. He has mastered the four Vedas. Now he desires to cross this river. (7.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Thus directed by her father, Matsyagandha began to take the great sage of solar splendour seated in the boat across the river. (8.)

Sanskrit

English
That great Yogin who had never been fascinated even on seeing the beauty of the celestial damsels became overwhelmed by lust towards her, in the circumstances. (9.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Desirous of clasping the beautiful daughter of the fisherman, the sage touched her in the right hand with his right hand. (10.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The wide-eyed lass spoke to him smilingly—"O sage of controlled speech, why do you intend to perpetrate this despicable deed? (11.)

Sanskrit

English
O most intelligent one, you are born of the beautiful family of Vasiṣṭha. O brahmin, I am the daughter of a Nīṣāda. How can our union be proper? (12.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O excellent sage, birth as a man is very difficult to obtain; especially that as a brahmin. Even there the state of an ascetic is very rarely achieved. (13.)

Sanskrit

English
It is extremely surprising that you endowed with learning, good physique, speech, noble birth and good conduct have become subservient to the arrows of Kāma!" (14.)

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
On hearing her words the king of Yogins Parāśara left off her hand. He reached the other shore. (17.)

Sanskrit

English
Afflicted with lust the sage caught hold of the lass. Tremblingly the lass spoke to the merciful sage. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

English
"O great sage, I have foul smell and black complexion. I am the daughter of a Niṣāda. You are the most excellent of Yogins of extremely liberal thoughts. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The union between us is not proper as of a piece of worthless glass with gold. The sexual union of two persons of the same class and features may be conducive to bliss." (20.)

---

Sanskrit

कृता योजनगन्धा तु रम्यरूपा मनोरमा ॥ २१॥

English
As the sage addressed her thus, the damsel was instantly converted into Yojanagandhā, (one whose fragrant odours spread to a Yojana), one of fine features and beauty. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Afflicted by lust the sage caught hold of her again. On seeing him bent upon seizing her she said again. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

दिवासङ्गे महान्दोषो निन्दा चापि दुरासदा ॥ २३॥

English
"It is mentioned in the Vedas that sexual intercourse shall be indulged in at night; not during the day. There is great harm in having sexual intercourse during the day-time. It is censured. (23.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit
