### **CHAPTER FORTYFIVE⁷⁷ - (The incarnation of Mahākālikā)**

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
श्रुता शम्भोः कथा रम्या नानाख्यानसमन्विता ।
नानावतारसंयुक्ता भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदा नृणाम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said :—  The beautiful episode of Siva with various incidental anecdotes and narratives of various incarnations, is heard. It yields both worldly pleasures and salvation to men. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
इदानीं श्रोतुमिच्छामस्त्वत्तो ब्रह्मविदांवर ।
चरित्रं जगदम्बाया भगवत्या मनोहरम् ॥ २॥

English
O foremost among those who know Brahman, we wish to hear from you the beautiful story of the mother of the universe, the goddess. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
परब्रह्ममहेशस्य शक्तिराद्या सनातनी ।
उमा या समभिख्याता त्रैलोक्यजननी परा ॥ ३॥

English
The first primordial Saktī of Siva the great Brahman, who is called Uma, is the great mother of the three worlds. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सती हैमवती तस्या अवतारद्वयं श्रुतम् ।
अपरानवतारांस्त्वं ब्रूहि सूत महामते ॥ ४॥

English
Her two incarnations—Sati and Haimavati, have been heard, O intelligent Sūta. Please mention her other incarnations too. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
को विरज्येत मतिमान् गुणश्रवणकर्मणि ।
श्रीमातुर्ज्ञानिनो यानि न त्यजन्ति कदाचन ॥ ५॥

English
Which intelligent man will be reluctant to hear the good attributes of the glorious mother? Wise men never abandon them. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
धन्या यूयं महात्मानः कृतकृत्याः स्थ सर्वदा ।
यत्पृच्छथ पराम्बाया उमायाश्चरितं महत् ॥ ६॥

English
Sūta said :—  You are all blessed and contented noble souls in as much as you inquire about the great story of Umā, the great mother. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वतां पृच्छतां चैव तथा वाचयतां च तत् ।
पादाम्बुजरजांस्येव तीर्थानि मुनयो विदुः ॥ ७॥

English
Sages consider the dust-particles of the feet of those who listen, narrate and inquire about it, on a par with holy centres. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ते धन्या कृतकृत्याः स्युर्धन्या तेषां प्रसूः कुलम् ।
येषां चित्तं भवेल्लीनं श्रीदेव्यां परसंविदि ॥ ८॥

English
Blessed and contented are they, their parents and their family, whose mind is merged in the goddess, the great Knowledge. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ये न स्तुवन्ति देवेशीं सर्वकारणकारणाम् ।
मायागुणैर्मोहिताः स्युर्हतभाग्या न संशयः ॥ ९॥

English
Those who do not eulogise the goddess of the gods, the cause of all causes are deluded by the attributes of Māyā. They are unfortunate. There is no doubt in this. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
न भजन्ति महादेवीं करुणारससागराम् ।
अन्धकूपे पतन्त्येते घोरे संसाररूपिणि ॥ १०॥

English
Those who do not worship the great goddess, the ocean of the juice of mercy, fall in the blind well in the form of the cruel worldly existence. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गङ्गां विहाय तृप्त्यर्थं मरुवारि यथा व्रजेत् ।
विहाय देवीं तद्भिन्नं तथा देवान्तरं व्रजेत् ॥ ११॥

English
Abandoning the goddess and resorting to another deity is as bad as abandoning the Gaṅgā and resorting to water in the desert for the sake of satisfaction. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्याः स्मरणमात्रेण पुरुषार्थचतुष्टयम् ।
अनायासेन लभते कस्त्यजेत्तां नरोत्तमः ॥ १२॥

English
Which excellent man will abandon her whose very remembrance bestows all the four aims of life without any strain? (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्पृष्टः पुरा मेधाः सुरथेन महात्मना ।
यदुक्तं मेधसा पूर्वं तच्छृणुष्व वदामि ते ॥ १३॥

English
This same question was put to Medhas by the noble Suratha. Please listen. I shall narrate what was mentioned by Medhas. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वारोचिषेऽन्तरे पूर्वं विरथो नाम पार्थिवः ।
सुरथस्तस्य पुत्रोऽभून्महाबलपराक्रमः ॥ १४॥

English
Formerly in the Svārociṣa Manvantara there was a king Virathā. Suratha his son was of great strength and exploit. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
दानशौण्डः सत्यवादी स्वधर्मकुशलः कृती ।
देवीभक्तो दयासिन्धुः प्रजानां परिपालकः ॥ १५॥

English
He was very munificent, truthful, efficient in duties, a devotee of the goddess, an ocean of kindness and the protector of his subjects. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिवीं शासतस्तस्य पाकशासनतेजसः ।
बभूवुर्नव ये भूपाः पृथ्वीग्रहणतत्पराः ॥ १६॥

English
While he was thus ruling over the earth, with the brilliance of Indra, nine other kings became desirous of seizing his land. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कोलानाम्नीं राजधानीं रुरुधुस्तस्य भूपतेः ।
तैः समं तुमुलं युद्धं समपद्यत दारुणम् ॥ १७॥

English
They laid siege to his capital Kolā.⁷⁸ The king had a terrible war with them. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
युद्धे स निर्जितो भूपः प्रबलैस्तैर्द्विषद्गणैः ।
उज्जासितश्च कोलाया हृत्वा राज्यमशेषतः ॥ १८॥

English
That king was defeated by his powerful enemies in the war. His kingdom was seized and he was banished from his city Kolā. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
स राजा स्वपुरीमेत्याकरोद्राज्यं स्वमन्त्रिभिः ।
तत्रापि च महापक्षैर्विपक्षैः स पराजितः ॥ १९॥

English
The king went to another city of his along with his ministers. There too he was defeated by his enemies who had a large following. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
दैवाच्छत्रुत्वमापन्नैरमात्यप्रमुखैर्गणैः ।
कोशस्थितं च यद्वित्तं तत्सर्वं चात्मसात्कृतम् ॥ २०॥

English
His ministers and officers became inimical to him due to adverse fate. They took away all the wealth from the treasury. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स निर्गतो राजा नगरान्मृगयाछलात् ।
असहायोऽश्वमारुह्य जगाम गहनं वनम् ॥ २१॥

English
The king left his city pretending to go out for hunting. Alone he got up on a horse and went to the dense forest. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
इतस्ततस्तत्र गच्छन्राजा मुनिवराश्रमम् ।
ददर्श कुसुमारामभ्राजितं सर्वतोदिशम् ॥ २२॥

वेदध्वनिसमाकीर्णं शान्तजन्तुसमाश्रितम् ।
शिष्यैः प्रशिष्यैस्तच्छिष्यैः समन्तात्परिवेष्टितम् ॥ २३॥

English
Going about here and there, the king saw the hermitage of a great sage. It was shining all round with flower gardens. Everywhere the sound of Vedic hymns was heard. All the animals there were quiet and peaceful. The disciples and the disciples of their disciples moved here and there. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याघ्रादयो महावीर्या अल्पवीर्यान्महामते ।
तदाश्रमे न बाधन्ते द्विजवर्यप्रभावतः ॥ २४॥

English
O intelligent one, tigers and other ferocious and powerful animals did not harass the animals of less strength in that hermitage, thanks to the power of the leading brahmin. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
उवास तत्र नृपतिर्महाकारुणिको बुधः ।
सत्कृतो मुनिनाथेन सुवचो भोजनासनैः ॥ २५॥

English
The king who was very kind and learned was welcomed and honoured by the leading sage with pleasing words, accommodation and food and stayed there. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा स महाराजश्चिन्तामाप दुरत्ययाम् ।
अहो मे हीनभाग्यस्य दुर्बुद्धेर्हीनतेजसः ॥ २६॥

हृतं राज्यमशेषेण शत्रुवर्गैर्मदोद्धतैः ।
मत्पूर्वै रक्षितं राज्यं शत्रुभिर्भुज्यतेऽधुना ॥ २७॥

English
Once the king began to think anxiously—“Alas, my kingdom has been seized by my haughty enemies. I am unfortunate. My intellect is confounded. I have lost my splendour. The kingdom which had been well guarded by my ancestors is now enjoyed by my enemies. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
मादृशश्चैत्रवंशेऽस्मिन्न कोऽप्यासीन्महीपतिः ।
किं करोमि क्व गच्छामि कथं राज्यं लभेय हि ॥ २८॥

English
There was no king in this family as weak as I. What shall I do? Where shall I go? How shall I get the kingdom back? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अमात्या मन्त्रिणश्चैव मामका ये सनातनाः ।
न जाने कं च नृपतिं समासाद्याधुनासते ॥ २९॥

English
My hereditary ministers and counsellors have left me. I do not know which king they serve now. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विनाश्य राज्यमधुना न जाने कां गतिं गताः ।
रणभूमिमहोत्साहा अरिवर्गनिकर्तनाः ॥ ३०॥

मामका ये महाशूरा नृपमन्यं भजन्ति ते ।
पर्वताभा गजा अश्वा वातवद्वेगगामिनः ॥ ३१॥

पूर्वपूर्वार्जितः कोशः पाल्यते तैर्न वाधुना ।
एवं मोहवशं यातो राजा परमधार्मिकः ॥ ३२॥

English
I do not know what plight they are in, after the ruination of the kingdom. My heroic soldiers, very enthusiastic in the battle, slayers of enemies, now serve another king. Huge elephants like mountains, horses fast as wind have gone away. Is the traditional treasury accumulated by my ancestors well guarded or not? Thus the very virtuous king became much deluded. (30-32.)

---

Sanskrit

राजा पप्रच्छ कस्त्वं भोः किमर्थमिह चागतः ॥ ३३॥

दुर्मना लक्ष्यसे कस्मादेतन्मे ब्रूहि साम्प्रतम् ।

दृग्भ्यां विमुञ्चन्नश्रूणि समाधिर्वैश्यपुङ्गवः ।
प्रत्युवाच महीपालं प्रणयावनतां गिरम् ॥ ३५॥

English

Please tell me this, now.” On hearing the sweet words of the king, the leading Vaiśya Samādhi shed tears. He spoke to the king in words full of humility and friendship. (33-35.)

---

Sanskrit

भ्रातृणां भ्रातृपुत्राणां परेषां सुहृदां तथा ।

English

O king, I have come here to the forest. I am worried about my sons, grandsons, wife, brothers, their sons and friends. O lord, ocean of mercy, I am in dark about their welfare. (36-38.)

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ।

English
*The king said:—*  How is it that you love, even as senseless animals do, those sons and others who are of evil conduct and covetous for wealth and by whom you have been ousted? (39.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*The Vaiśya said:—*  O king, words pregnant with substantial sense have been uttered by you. Still my mind is deluded by the tie of love. (40.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
With palms joined in reverence the king spoke to the sage:—“O holy lord, it behoves you now to dispel our delusion. (43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Abandoned by the royal glory I have resorted to this dense forest. Still I am not content and happy since my kingdom has been taken away. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English
This Vaiśya has been expelled from his house by his people, wife and others. Still his sense of affinity and kinship with them does not go off. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
What is the reason hereof? Please say. Wise though we are, our minds are afflicted and agitated by delusion. This is great foolishness. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिरुवाच ।
महामाया जगद्धात्री शक्तिरूपा सनातनी ।
सा मोहयति सर्वेषां समाकृष्य मनांसि वै ॥ ४७॥

English
The sage said:—  The great Māyā in the form of the eternal Śakti is the material cause of the universe. It is this that drags the minds of all and makes them deluded. (47.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O lord, Brahmā and other gods deluded by this Māyā do not realise the truth. What then is the story of men? (48.)

---

Sanskrit

सैव संहरते काले त्रिगुणा परमेश्वरी ॥ ४९॥

English
That alone, Parameśvari of three attributes, creates the universe; she alone sustains it and she alone destroys it at the proper time. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O excellent king, only he surmounts this delusion on whom the favourite goddess who assumes forms as she pleases becomes delighted. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ।

English
The king said:—  O sage, who is that Goddess? Who is that great Māyā who fascinates all? How was that goddess born? Please tell me. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

जगत्येकार्णवे जाते शेषमास्तीर्य योगराट् ।

They became notorious on the surface of the world in the names of Madhu and Kaiṭabha. (52-53.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing the lotus-seated deity in the umbilical lotus⁸⁰ of the lord, the two Asuras⁸¹ shouted “Hey, who are you?” and attempted to kill him. (55.)
