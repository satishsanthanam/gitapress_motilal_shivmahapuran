### **CHAPTER FORTYEIGHT - (The manifestation of Sarasvatī)**

---

Sanskrit
राजोवाच ॥

धूम्राक्षं चण्डमुण्डं च रक्तबीजासुरं तथा ।
भगवन्निहतं देव्या श्रुत्वा शुम्भः सुरार्दनः ॥ १॥

किमकार्षीत्ततो ब्रह्मन्नेतन्मे ब्रूहि साम्प्रतम् ।
शुश्रूषवे जगद्योनेश्चरित्रं पापनाशनम् ॥ २॥

English
The king said:—  O holy lord, O brahmin, what did Śumbha, the suppressor of the gods, do on hearing that Dhūmrākṣa, Caṇḍa and Raktabīja were killed by the goddess? Please tell me this now. I desire to hear the purifying tale of the goddess, the cause of the universe. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिरुवाच ।
हतानिमान्दैत्यवरान्महासुरो
निशम्य राजन्महनीयविक्रमः ।
अजिज्ञपत्स्वीयगणान्दुरासदान्
रणाभिधोच्चारणजातसम्मदान् ॥ ३॥

English
The sage said:—  O king, on hearing that the leading Daityas had been killed, the great Asura of commendable valour ordered his invincible followers who were thrilled at the very mention of the war. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
बलान्विताः सम्मिलिता ममाज्ञया
जयाशया कालकवंशसम्भवाः ।
सकालकेयासुरमौर्यदौर्हृदाः
तथा परेऽप्याशु प्रयाणयन्तु ते ॥ ४॥

English
At my behest the Daityas born of the race of Kālaka⁸⁷ have all assembled here with their armies and along with Kālakeyas,⁸⁸ Mauryas,⁸⁹ Daurhṛdas⁹⁰ and others. Let them march on with hopes of victory. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
निशुम्भशुम्भौ दितिजान्निदेश्य तान्
रथाधिरूढौ निरयाम्बभूवतुः ।
बलान्यनूकाबलिनोस्तयोर्धराद्
विनाशवन्तः शलभा इवोत्थिताः ॥ ५॥

English
After ordering the demons, Śumbha and Niśumbha mounted on their chariots and set out. Their armies too followed them like swarms of moths rising up from a mountain definitely for their destruction. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवादयामास मृदङ्गमर्दलं
सभेरिकाडिण्डिमझर्झरानकम् ।
रणस्थले सञ्जहृषू रणप्रिया
असुप्रियाः सङ्गरतः पराययुः ॥ ६॥

English
Mṛdaṅgas, Mardalas, Bherikās Diṇḍimas, Jharjharas and Ānakas were sounded. The warmongers rejoiced in the battle-ground. Those who were afraid fled away for their life. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
भटाश्च ते युद्धपटावृतास्तदा
रणस्थलीमापुरपापविग्रहाः ।
गृहीतशस्त्रास्त्रचया जिगीषया
परस्परं विग्रहयन्त उल्बणम् ॥ ७॥

English
Clad in their martial dress the soldiers came in their healthy spirits to the battleground. Holding various weapons and missiles they teased one another each eager for his victory. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
गजाधिरूढास्तुरगाधिरोहिणो
रथाधिरूढाश्च तथापरेऽसुराः ।
अलक्षयन्तः स्वपराञ्जनान्मुदाऽ-
सुरेशसङ्गे समरेऽभिरेभिरे ॥ ८॥

English
Soldiers on elephants, looked at the enemy with indifference. In the company of the lord of Asuras they rejoiced in the battle. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्वनिः शतघ्नीजनितो मुहुर्मुहु-
र्बभूव तेन त्रिदशाः समेजिताः ।
महान्धकारः समपद्यताम्बरे
विलोक्यते नो रथमण्डलं रवेः ॥ ९॥

English
The sound arising from the guns rose up repeatedly making the gods tremble. A great darkness enveloped the sky. Even the chariot of the sun was not visible. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पदातयो निर्व वजुर्हि कोटिशः
प्रभूतमाना विजयाभिलाषिणः ।
रथाश्वगा वारणगा अथापरेऽ-
सुरा निरीयुः कति कोटिशो मुदा ॥ १०॥

English
Foot-soldiers set out in excessive numbers desirous of victory. Soldiers in chariots, on horsebacks, elephants and others set out joyously in crores and crores. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अशुक्लशैला एव मत्तवारणा
अतानिषुश्चीत्कृतिशब्दमाहवे ।
क्रमेलकाश्चापि गलद्गलध्वनिं
वितन्वते क्षुद्रमहीधरोपमाः ॥ ११॥

English
The rutting elephants like massive black mountains spread their trumpeting sound in the battle-field. Camels resembling small hills produced hoarse sounds from their throats. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हयाश्च ह्रेषन्त उदग्रभूमिजा
विशालकण्ठाभरणा गतेर्विदः ।
पदानि दन्तावलमूर्ध्नि बिभ्रतः
सुडिड्यिरे व्योमपथा यथावयः ॥ १२॥

English
Neighing horses hailing from exalted lands, with big ornaments round their necks, expert in the knowledge of their gaits planted their legs on the heads of elephants and flew like birds. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
समीक्ष्य शत्रोर्बलमित्थमापतत्
चकार सज्यं धनुरम्बिका तदा ।
ननाद घण्टां रिपुसाददायिनीं
जगर्ज सिंहोऽपि सटां विधूनयन् ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing the army of the enemy advancing thus, Ambikā kept her bow well-strung. She sounded her bell that distressed the enemy. The lion too shook his manes and roared. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निशुम्भस्तुहिनाचलस्थितां
विलोक्य रम्याभरणायुधां शिवाम् ।
गिरं बभाषे रसनिर्भरां परां
विलासनीभावविचक्षणो यथा ॥ १४॥

English
Seeing her bedecked in fine ornaments, holding weapons and stationed on the Himālaya mountain Niśumbha spoke words full of sentiments like a man clever in understanding the emotions of beautiful women. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भवादृशीनां रमणीयविग्रहे
दुनोति कीर्णं खलु मालतीदलम् ।
कथं करालाहवमातनोष्यसे
महेशि तेनैव मनोज्ञवर्ष्मणा ॥ १५॥

English
"Even a petal of the Mālatī (Jasmine) flower thrown on the beautiful body of women like you may distress you. O goddess, how will you carry on a terrible war with the self-same handsome body of yours?" (15.)

---

Sanskrit
इतीरयित्वा वचनं महासुरो
बभूव मौनी तमुवाच चण्डिका ।
वृथा किमात्थासुर मूढ सङ्गरं
कुरुष्व नागालयमन्यथा व्रज ॥ १६॥

English
After saying thus the great Asura became silent. Caṇḍikā spoke to him—"O foolish Asura, why do you prattle in vain? Either fight or else return to Pātāla." (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽतिरुष्टः समरे महारथ-
श्चकार बाणावलिवृष्टिमद्भुताम् ।
घनाघनाः संववृषुर्यथोदकं
रणस्थले प्रावृडिवागतास्तदा ॥ १७॥

English
The heroic demon, becoming infuriated, made a wonderful shower of arrows on the battle-field just as masses of clouds shower water during rains. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शरैश्शितैः शूलपरश्वधायुधैः
सभिन्दिपालैः परिघैः शरासनैः ।
भुशुण्डिकाप्रासक्षुरप्रसंज्ञकै-
र्महासिभिः संयुयुधे मदोद्धतैः ॥ १८॥

English
Along with his arrogant followers the demon fought with sharp weapons as spears, axes, iron clubs, parighas, bows, Bhuśuṇḍikās, javelins, horseshoe-edged arrows and great swords. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
विबभ्रमुस्तत्समरे महागजा
विभिन्नकुम्भा असिताद्रिसन्निभाः ।
चलद्बलाकाधवला विकेतवो
विसेतवः शुम्भनिशुम्भकेतवः ॥ १९॥

English
In that war great elephants looking like black mountains with foreheads pierced ran here and there. The banners of Śumbha and Niśumbha white like flying cranes fluttered here and there. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विभिन्नदेहा दितिजा झषोपमा
विकन्धरा वाजिगणा भयङ्कराः ।
परासवः कालिकया कृता रणे
मृगारिणा चामिषतां परेऽसुराः ॥ २०॥

English
The demons were shattered by Kālikā like fishes. The dreadful horses were beheaded and killed in the battle. The other demons were devoured by the lion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
विसुस्रुवू रक्तवहास्तदन्तरे
सरिच्चयास्तत्र विपुप्लुवे हतैः ।
कचा भटानां जलनीलिकोपमा-
स्तदुत्तरीयं सितफेनसन्निभम् ॥ २१॥

English
In the battlefield streams of blood flowed. The dead soldiers floated. Their tresses of hair resembled the moss. Their upper cloths resembled the white foam. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तुरङ्गसादी तुरगाधिरोहिणं
गजस्थितानभ्यपतन्गजारुहः ।
रथी रथेशं खलु पत्तिरङ्घ्रिगान्
समप्रतिद्वन्द्विकलिर्महानभूत् ॥ २२॥

English
A great fight ensued where soldiers of equal rank fought with one another. The cavalrymen fought their counterpart; the elephant-riders with those on elephants: the charioteers with those on chariots and the footsoldiers with footsoldiers. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निशुम्भो हृदये व्यचिन्तयत्
करालकालोऽयमुपागतोऽधुना ।
भवेद्दरिद्रोऽपि महाधनो महा-
धनो दरिद्रो विपरीतकालतः ॥ २३॥

English
Then Niśumbha thought to himself—"A terrible period has set in now. Even a poor man may become rich and rich man poor if the time is adverse. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
जडो भवेत्स्फीतमतिर्महामति-
र्जडो नृशंसो बहुमन्तुसंस्तुतः ।
पराजयं यान्ति रणे महाबला
जयन्ति सङ्ग्राममुखे च दुर्बलाः ॥ २४॥

English
A senseless fellow may become intelligent and an intelligent man dull. A wicked man may be praised by the noble. The great and the powerful may be vanquished. The weak may come out victorious in the war. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
जयोऽजयो वा परमेश्वरेच्छया
भवत्यनायासत एव देहिनाम् ।
न कालमुल्लङ्घ्य शशाक जीवितुं
महेश्वरः पद्मजनी रमापतिः ॥ २५॥

English
Victory or defeat come naturally at the stance of the lord. Neither Śiva nor Brahmā nor Viṣṇu has been able to transgress his law. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
उपेत्य सङ्ग्राममुखं पलायनं
न साधु वीरा हृदयेऽनुमन्वते ।
परन्तु युद्धे कथमेतया जयो
विनाशितं मे सकलं बलं यथा ॥ २६॥

English
Heroes do not come to the battlefield for turning back. But how can I attain victory in a battle with this lady who has destroyed my entire host? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
इयं हि नूनं सुरकर्म साधितुं
समागता दैत्यबलं च बाधितुम् ।
पुराणमूर्तिः प्रकृतिः परा शिवा
न लौकिकीयं वनिता कदापि वा ॥ २७॥

English
Indeed this lady is the ancient Prakṛti, the great Śivā come here to achieve the task of the gods and harass the army of the Daityas. She can never be an ordinary woman (27.)

---

Sanskrit
वधोऽपि नारीविहितोऽयशस्करः
प्रगीयते युद्धरसं लिलिक्षुभिः ।
तथाप्यकृत्वा समरं कथं मुखं
प्रदर्शयामोऽसुरराजसन्निधौ ॥ २८॥

English
It is inglorious to be killed by a woman or to kill a woman for those who desire to taste the pleasures of war. Still how shall we show our faces to the king of Asuras without fighting? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
विचारयित्वेति महारथो रथं
महान्तमध्यास्य नियन्तृचोदितम् ।
ययौ द्रुतं यत्र महेश्वराङ्गना
सुराङ्गनाप्रार्थितयौवनोद्गमा ॥ २९॥

English
After thinking thus and sitting in a great chariot driven by a charioteer he hastened to the spot where the lady consort of Śiva was present; the goddess whose youth was sought after by the celestial damsels. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अवोचदेनां स महेशि किं भवे-
देभिर्हतैर्वेतनजीविभिर्भटैः ।
तवास्ति काङ्क्षा यदि योद्धुमावयो-
स्तदा रणः स्याद्धृतयुद्धसत्पटैः ॥ ३०॥

English
He addressed her thus—"O goddess, of what avail is it if the mercenary soldiers are killed? If you desire to fight, let both of us clad in martial dress fight with each other. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
उवाच कालीं प्रति कौशिकी तदा
समीक्ष्यतामेष दुराग्रहोऽनयोः ।
करोति कालो विपदागमे मतिं
विभिन्नवृत्तिं सदसत्प्रवर्तकः ॥ ३१॥

English
The goddess addressed Kāli then—"See the foolish ambition of the two Asuras. Time the instigator of good and bad actions renders the mind work in a different way when adversity is imminent." (31.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निशुम्भोऽभिजघान चण्डिकां
शरैः सहस्रैश्च तथैव कालिकाम् ।
बिभेद बाणानसुरप्रचोदितान्
सहस्रखण्डं स्वशरोत्करैः शिवा ॥ ३२॥

English
Then Niśumbha attacked Caṇḍikā as well as Kāli with thousands of arrows. With the volleys of her arrows Śivā split into a thousand pieces the arrows discharged by the Asura. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः समुत्थाप्य कृपाणमुज्ज्वलं
सचर्म कण्ठीरवमूर्ध्न्यताडयत् ।
बिभेद तं चापि महासिनाम्बिका
यथा कुठारेण तरुं तरुश्छिदः ॥ ३३॥

English
He then lifted up his lustrous sword along with the shield and struck the lion on its head. With her great sword she split it too as the woodcutters do to a tree with the axe. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
स भिन्नखड्गो निचखान मार्गणं
पराम्बिकावक्षसि सोऽपि चिच्छिदे ।
पुनस्त्रिशूलं हृदयेऽक्षिपत्तद-
प्यचूर्णयन्मुष्टिनिपातनेन सा ॥ ३४॥

English
When the sword was split he thrust an arrow into her chest. The arrow too was cut. He then hurled the trident which powdered it with her fist. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
गदां समादाय पुनर्महारथो-
ऽभ्यधावदम्बां मरणोन्मुखोऽसुरः ।
अचूर्णयत्तामपि शूलधारया
पुनस्त्रिशूलं विददार सोऽन्यया ॥ ३५॥

English
Prepared to die the heroic Asura seized a mace and rushed at her. She reduced the mace into powder with the edge of her trident. The demon shattered the trident with another mace. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽम्बिका भीमभुजङ्गमोपमैः
सुरद्विषां शोणितचूषणोचितैः ।
निशुम्भमात्मीयशिलीमुखैः शितै-
र्निहत्य भूमीमनयद्विषोक्षितैः ॥ ३६॥

English
Then she struck Niśumbha with her sharp, terrible serpentine poisonous arrows that were accustomed to drink the blood of Asuras and brought him down to the earth. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
निपातितेऽमानबलेऽसुरप्रभुः
कनीयसि भ्रातरि रोषपूरितः ।
रथस्थितो बाहुभिरष्टभिर्वृतो
जगाम यत्र प्रमदा महेशितुः ॥ ३७॥

English
When his younger brother of great honour and strength fell Śumbha was furious. The eight-armed demon seated himself in a chariot and came to the place where Śivā was present. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
अवादयच्छङ्खमरिन्दमं तदा
धनुःस्वनं चापि चकार दुःसहम् ।
ननाद सिंहोऽपि सटां विधूनयन्
बभूव नादत्रयनादितं नभः ॥ ३८॥

English
She blew the conch Arindama, produced unbearable bow-twang; the lion shaking its manes roared. The whole sky reverberated with the threefold round. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽट्टहासं जगदम्बिकाऽकरो-
द्वितत्रसुस्तेन सुरारयोऽखिलाः ।
जयेति शब्दं जगदुस्तदा सुरा
यदाम्बिकोवाच रणे स्थिरो भव ॥ ३९॥

English
Then the mother of the universe laughed boisterously making all the demons tremble with fear. The gods shouted cries of "Victory" when she challenged the Asuras in the battle. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
स दैत्यराजो महतीं ज्वलच्छिखां
मुमोच शक्तिं निहता च सोल्कया ।
बिभेद शुम्भप्रहितान् शरान् शिवा
शिवेरितान्सोऽपि सहस्रधा शरान् ॥ ४०॥

English
The king of Daityas hurled a spear of shining flames which was struck down by a meteor. Śivā shattered the arrows discharged by Śumbha. He too split the arrows discharged by Śivā into a thousand pieces. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिशूलमुत्क्षिप्य जघान चण्डिका
महासुरं तं स पपात मूर्च्छितः ।
विभिन्नपक्षो हरिणा यथा नगः
प्रकम्पयन् द्यां वसुधां सवारिधिम् ॥ ४१॥

English
She lifted up her trident and struck the great Asura. He fell down unconscious shaking heaven and earth along with the ocean like a mountain whose wings had been chopped off by Indra.⁹² (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो मृषित्वा त्रिशिखोद्भवां व्यथां
विधाय बाहूनयुतं महाबलः ।
स कालिकां सिंहयुतां महेश्वरीं
जघान चक्रैरमरक्षयङ्करैः ॥ ४२॥

English
Suffering the pain arising from the trident, the powerful demon, the suppressor of the gods, created ten thousand hands and struck Kālī and Caṇḍikā along with the lion, by means of his discus. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तदस्य चक्राणि विभिद्य लीलया
त्रिशूलमुद्गूर्य जघान सासुरम् ।
शिवाजगत्पावनपाणिपङ्कजा-
दुपात्तमृत्यू परमं पदं गतौ ॥ ४३॥

English
Splitting sportively the discus hurled by him, she hurt and struck the Asura with the trident. Thus both of them met their death at the lotus-hands of Śivā that sanctify the universe and attained the great region. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
हते तस्मिन्महावीर्ये निशुम्भे भीमविक्रमे ।
शुम्भे च सकला दैत्या विविशुर्बलिसद्मनि ॥ ४४॥

English
When the powerful Niṣumbha, and Śumbha of terrible exploit were killed the Daityas entered Pātāla.⁹³ (44.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्षिता अपरे कालीसिंहाद्यैरमरद्विषः ।
पलायितास्तथान्ये च दशदिक्षु भयाकुलाः ॥ ४५॥

English
Others were devoured by Kālī's lion. The remaining Asuras being excessively frightened fled in the quarters. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
बभूवुर्मार्गवाहिन्यः सरितः स्वच्छपाथसः ।
ववुर्वाताः सुखस्पर्शा निर्मलत्वं ययौ नभः ॥ ४६॥

English
The rivers with clear water flowed along their paths; the winds blew very gentle to the touch; the sky became clear. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्यागः समारेभे देवैर्ब्रह्मर्षिभिस्तथा ।
सुखिनश्चाभवन्सर्वे महेन्द्राद्या दिवौकसः ॥ ४७॥

English
Sacrifices were revived by the gods and the sages, Lord Indra and the gods felt blissful again. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
पवित्रं परमं पुण्यमुमायाश्चरितं प्रभो ।
दैत्यराजवधोपेतं श्रद्धया यः समभ्यसेत् ॥ ४८॥

स भुक्त्वेहाखिलान्भोगांस्त्रिदशैरपि दुर्लभान् ।
परत्रोमालयं गच्छेन्महामायाप्रसादतः ॥ ४९॥

English
O lord, this story of Umā is holy and meritorious. It describes the destruction of the king of Daityas. He who regularly reads this with faith enjoys all worldly pleasures inaccessible even to the gods and attains the abode of Umā hereafter by the very grace of the goddess. (48-49.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिरुवाच ।
एवं देवी समुत्पन्ना शुम्भासुरनिबर्हिणी ।
प्रोक्ता सरस्वती साक्षादुमांशाविर्भवा नृप ॥ ५०॥

English
Thus was the goddess, slayer of Śumbha born. She is said to be Sarasvatī. O king, she has manifested herself as a part of Umā. (50.)
