### **CHAPTER FORTYNINE - (The manifestation of Umā)**

---

Sanskrit
एकोनपञ्चाशत्तमोऽध्यायः । उमाप्रादुर्भाववर्णनम् ।

मुनय ऊचुः ।
उमाया भुवनेशान्याः सूत सर्वार्थवित्तम ।
अवतारं समाचक्ष्व यतो जाता सरस्वती ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O Sūta, the most excellent among the omniscients please mention the incarnation of Umā, the goddess of all the worlds and the mother of Sarasvatī. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
या गीयते परब्रह्ममूलप्रकृतिरीश्वरी ।
निराकारापि साकारा नित्या नन्दमयी सती ॥ २॥

English
She is sung as the primordial Prakṛti of the supreme Brahman. She is both possessed and devoid of forms. She is of the form of eternal bliss. She is Satī. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
तापसाः श‍ृणुत प्रेम्णा चरित्रं परमं महत् ।
यस्य विज्ञानमात्रेण नरो याति परां गतिम् ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said:—  O sages, listen lovingly to the great story, the perfect knowledge of which alone helps man to attain the great goal. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदानवयोर्युद्धमेकदासीत्परस्परम् ।
महामायाप्रभावेणामराणां विजयोऽभवत् ॥ ४॥

English
Once there was a clash between the gods and the Asuras. Thanks to the power of the goddess the gods became victorious. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽवलिप्ता अमराः स्वप्रशंसां वितेनिरे ।
वयं धन्या वयं धन्याः किं करिष्यन्ति नोऽसुराः ॥ ५॥

English
Then the gods were proud and haughty. They boasted—"We are blessed. What can the Asuras do to us?" (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ये प्रभावं समालोक्यास्माकं परमदुःसहम् ।
भीता नागालयं याता यात यातेति वादिनः ॥ ६॥

English
Of our unbearable prowess, the Asuras are afraid. They have gone to Pātāla saying, "Run, run". (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अहो बलमहो तेजो दैत्यवंशक्षयङ्करम् ।
अहो भाग्यं सुमनसामेवं सर्वेऽभ्यवर्णयन् ॥ ७॥

English
Then all of them proclaimed thus: "Wonderful is the strength, splendour and good luck of the gods that has reduced the race of the Daityas. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तत आविरभूत्तेजः कूटरूपं तदैव हि ।
अदृष्टपूर्वं तद् दृष्ट्वा विस्मिता अभवन्सुराः ॥ ८॥

English
Then a mass of splendour appeared, at the unprecedented sight of which the gods were surprised. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
किमिदं किमिदं चेति रुद्धकण्ठाः समब्रुवन ।
अजानन्तः परं श्यामानुभावं मानभञ्जनम् ॥ ९॥

English
Ignorant of the real glory and the intention of the goddess to quell their arrogance, the Asuras said with choking throats "What is this? What is this?" (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तत आज्ञापयद्देवान् देवानामधिनायकः ।
यात यूयं परीक्षध्वं याथातथ्येन किन्विति ॥ १०॥

English
Then the lord Indra ordered the gods—"Go ye all and examine it and ascertain what it is." (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सुरेन्द्रप्रेरितो वायुर्महसः सन्निधिं गतः ।
कस्त्वं भोरिति सम्बोध्यावोचदेनं च तन्महः ॥ ११॥

English
Urged by Indra, the wind-god approached splendour and addressed it, "Who are you?" The splendour too put the same question to him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इति पृष्टस्तदा वायुर्महसातिगरीयसा ।
वायुरस्मि जगत्प्राणः साभिमानोऽब्रवीदिदम् ॥ १२॥

English
Thus asked by the big mass of splendour the wind-god replied arrogantly.—"I am Vāyu the vital breath of the universe." (12.)

---

Sanskrit
जङ्गमाजङ्गमं सर्वमोतप्रोतमिदं जगत् ।
मय्येव निखिलाधारे चालयाम्यखिलं जगत् ॥ १३॥

English
Everything in the universe, mobile or immobile, is woven like warp and woof into me. I am the support of everything. I move the entire universe." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तदोवाच महातेजः शक्तोऽसि यदि चालने ।
धृतमेतत्तृणं वायो चालयस्व निजेच्छया ॥ १४॥

English
Then the great splendour said:—"If you are competent to move, O wind-god, please move this blade of grass I have set before you. Move it as you please." (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सर्वप्रयत्नेनाकरोद्यत्नं सदागतिः ।
न चचाल यदा स्थानात्तदासौ लज्जितोऽभवत ॥ १५॥

English
Then wind-god put forth all his efforts. But the blade of grass did not stir from its position. Then he was put to shame. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तूष्णीं भूत्वा ततो वायुर्जगामेन्द्रंसभां प्रति ।
कथयामास तद् वृत्तं स्वकीयाभिभवान्वितम् ॥ १६॥

English
The wind-god went silently to the assembly of Indra. There he narrated the details of his discomfiture. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेशत्वं वयं सर्वे मृषैवात्मनि मन्महे ।
न पारयामहे किञ्चिद्विधातुं क्षुद्रवस्त्वपि ॥ १७॥

English
"False is our pride that we are the lords. In fact we are helpless, insignificant creatures." (17.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्च प्रेषयामास मरुत्वान्सकलान्सुरान् ।
न शेकुस्ते यदा ज्ञातुं तदेन्द्रः स्वयमभ्यगात् ॥ १८॥

English
Then Indra sent all the gods. When they could not realise anything Indra himself went there. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मघवन्तमथायान्तं दृष्ट्वा तेजोऽतिदुःसहम् ।
बभूवान्तर्हितं सद्यो विस्मितोऽभूच्च वासवः ॥ १९॥

English
On seeing Indra come, the unbearable splendour vanished immediately. Indra was surprised. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
चरित्रमीदृशं यस्य तमेव शरणं श्रये ।

इति सञ्चिन्तयामास सहस्राक्षः पुनः पुनः ॥ २०॥

English
Then Indra thought to himself again and again—"I seek refuge in him alone whose conduct is such as this." (20.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तत्र निर्व्याजकरुणातनुः ।
तेषामनुग्रहं कर्तुं हर्तुं गर्वं शिवाङ्गना ॥ २१॥

चैत्रशुक्लनवम्यां तु मध्याह्नस्थे दिवाकरे ।
प्रादुरासीदुमा देवी सच्चिदानन्दरूपिणी ॥ २२॥

English
In the meantime, in order to bless them and remove their arrogance, Śivā, the embodied form of undeceitful mercy, existence, knowledge and bliss, manifested herself on the ninth day of the bright half of the month Caitra when the sun was in the middle of the day. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
महोमध्ये विराजन्ती भासयन्ती दिशो रुचा ।
बोधयन्ती सुरान्सर्वान् ब्रह्मैवाहमिति स्फुटम् ॥ २३॥

English
Shining among the mass of splendour she brightened the quarters with her brilliance. She enlightened the gods, saying "I alone am Brahman." (23.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्भिर्दधती हस्तैर्वरपाशाङ्कुशाभयान् ।
श्रुतिभिः सेविता रम्या नवयौवनगर्विता ॥ २४॥

English
In her four hands she held boons, noose, goad and the mystic gesture of protection. She was served by the Vedas and looked beautiful and proud of her blooming youth. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्ताम्बरपरीधाना रक्तमाल्यानुलेपना ।
कोटिकन्दर्पसङ्काशा चन्द्रकोटिसमप्रभा ॥ २५॥

English
She wore red garments and red garlands. Red sandal paste was smeared over her body. She was as dazzling as a crore of cupids. Her lustre was that of a crore moons. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याजहार महामाया सर्वान्तर्यामिरूपिणी ।
साक्षिणी सर्वभूतानां परब्रह्मस्वरूपिणी ॥ २६॥

English
The great Māyā, in the form of the immanent soul of all, the cosmic witness of all living beings, and the great Brahman, spoke. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
उमोवाच ।
न ब्रह्मा न सुरारातिर्न पुरारातिरीश्वरः ।
मदग्रे गर्वितुं किञ्चित्का कथान्यसुपर्वणाम् ॥ २७॥

English
Umā said:—  Neither Brahmā nor Viṣṇu nor Śiva the slayer of the Tripura demon can bluff before me. What about the other gods ? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
परं ब्रह्म परं ज्योतिः प्रणवद्वन्द्वरूपिणी ।
अहमेवास्मि सकलं मदन्यो नास्ति कश्चन ॥ २८॥

English
The great Brahman, the great light in the form of the two Praṇavas, I alone am. I am all. There is none other beside me. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
निराकारापि साकारा सर्वतत्त्वस्वरूपिणी ।
अप्रतर्क्यगुणा नित्या कार्यकारणरूपिणी ॥ २९॥

English
Though devoid of form I possess forms. I constitute all principles. I am eternal and my attributes cannot be disputed. I am both the cause and the effect. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिद्दयिताकारा कदाचित्पुरुषाकृतिः ।
कदाचिदुभयाकारा सर्वाकाराहमीश्वरी ॥ ३०॥

English
Sometimes I have the form of a woman, sometimes of a man. Sometimes both. I am goddess assuming all forms. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
विरञ्चिः सृष्टिकर्ताहं जगत्पाताहमच्युतः ।
रुद्रः संहारकर्ताहं सर्वविश्वविमोहिनी ॥ ३१॥

English
I am the creator, the protector and the destroyer of the world. I am the enchantress of the universe. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
कालिकाकमलावाणीमुखाः सर्वा हि शक्तयः ।
मदंशादेव सञ्जातास्तथेमाः सकलाः कलाः ॥ ३२॥

English
All the Śaktis—Kālī, Lakṣmī and Sarasvatī as well as others are born of my parts. So also the arts originate from me. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
मत्प्रभावाज्जिताः सर्वे युष्माभिर्दितिनन्दनाः ।
तामविज्ञाय मां यूयं वृथा सर्वेशमानिनः ॥ ३३॥

English
Due to my power alone the demons are conquered by you. Without knowing me of this nature you bluff as the lords of all. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा दारुमयीं योषां नर्तयत्यैन्द्रजालिकः ।
तथैव सर्वभूतानि नर्तयाम्यहमीश्वरी ॥ ३४॥

English
Just as a magician makes the wooden doll of a woman dance so also I the goddess make all living beings dance. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
मद्भयाद्वाति पवनः सर्वं दहति हव्यभुक् ।
लोकपालाः प्रकुर्वन्ति स्वस्वकर्माण्यनारतम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Being afraid of me the wind blows, the fire-god burns and the guardians of the quarters carry on their duties. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचिद्देववर्गाणां कदाचिद्दितिजन्मनाम् ।
करोमि विजयं सम्यक् स्वतन्त्रा निजलीलया ॥ ३६॥

English
Independent that I am, I sportively bestow victory on the gods sometimes and on the Daityas sometimes. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अविनाशि परं धाम मायातीतं परात्परम् ।
श्रुतयो वर्णयन्ते यत्तद्रूपं तु ममैव हि ॥ ३७॥

English
It is my form that is described in the Vedas as indestructible, the great abode, the one beyond Māyā and that which is greater than the greatest. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सगुणं निर्गुणं चेति मद्रूपं द्विविधं मतम् ।
मायाशबलितं चैकं द्वितीयं तदनाश्रितम् ॥ ३८॥

English
My form is two-fold—Saguṇa and Nirguṇa. One is mixed with Māyā and the other is free from it. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विज्ञाय मां देवाः स्वं स्वं गर्वं विहाय च ।
भजत प्रणयोपेताः प्रकृतिं मां सनातनीम् ॥ ३९॥

English
O gods, endowed with devotion, realise this and eschew your arrogance, worship me, the eternal Prakṛti. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इति देव्या वचः श्रुत्वा करुणागर्भितं सुराः ।
तुष्टुवुः परमेशानीं भक्तिसन्नतकन्धराः ॥ ४०॥

English
On hearing the merciful words of the goddess, the gods eulogised her, with their shoulders drooping with devotion. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षमस्व जगदीशानि प्रसीद परमेश्वरि ।
मैवं भूयात्कदाचिन्नो गर्वो मातर्दयां कुरु ॥ ४१॥

English
O goddess of the universe, forgive us. O goddess, be pleased. O mother, let us not be arrogant again. Please be merciful. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
ततःप्रभृति ते देवा हित्वा गर्वं समाहिताः ।
उमामाराधयामासुर्यथापूर्वं यथाविधि ॥ ४२॥

English
Since then the gods began to propitiate her as before. They eschewed their arrogance and became pure in mind. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
इति वः कथितो विप्रा उमाप्रादुर्भवो मया ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण परमं पदमश्नुते ॥ ४३॥

English
O Brahmins, thus I have narrated to you the manifestation of Umā. By only hearing this men attain the great region. (43.)

---
