### **CHAPTER FIFTY - (The incarnation of Śatākṣī etc.)**

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चाशत्तमोऽध्यायः । शताक्ष्याद्यवतारवर्णनम् ।

मुनय ऊचुः ।
श्रोतुकामा वयं सर्वे दुर्गाचरितमन्वहम् ।
अपरं च महाप्राज्ञ तत्त्वं वर्णय नोऽद्भुतम् ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  O intelligent one, all of us are anxious to hear the episode of Durgā every day. Please tell us another wonderful tale of the goddess. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृण्वतां त्वन्मुखाम्भोजात् कथा नाना सुधोपमाः ।
न तृप्यति मनोऽस्माकं सूत सर्वार्थवित्तम ॥ २॥

English
O Sūta, the most excellent of the story-tellers, we are not satiated even as we hear different nectarine stories narrated by you. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
दुर्गमः प्रथितो नाम्ना रुरुपुत्रो महाबलः ।
ब्रह्मणो वरदानेन चतस्रोऽलभत श्रुतीः ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said:—  The most powerful son of Ruru, famous as Durgama secured the four Vedas as a result of the boon granted by Brahma. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाजेयबलं चापि सम्प्राप्य जगतीतले ।
करोति स्म बहूत्पातान्दिवि देवाश्चकम्पिरे ॥ ४॥

English
Securing this strength that cannot be thwarted even by the gods he wrought havoc on the earth, making the gods tremble in heaven. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वा नष्टेषु वेदेषु क्रिया नष्टा बभूव ह ।
ब्राह्मणाश्च दुराचारा बभूवुः ससुरास्तदा ॥ ५॥

English
When the Vedas were destroyed the sacred rites were ruined. Brahmins and the gods fell off from their right conduct. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
न दानं न तपोऽत्युग्रं न यागो हवनं न हि ।
अनावृष्टिस्ततो जाता पृथिव्यां शतवार्षिकी ॥ ६॥

English
None offered charitable gifts; none performed penances; there was neither worship nor sacrifice. Then a great drought befell the earth extending to a period of a hundred years. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
हाहाकारो महानासीत् त्रिषु लोकेषु दुःखिताः ।
अभवंश्च जनाःसर्वे क्षुत्तृड्भ्यां पीडिता भृशम् ॥ ७॥

English
There was a great hue and cry in all the three worlds. The people felt miserable being afflicted by hunger and thirst. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सरितः सागराश्चैव वापीकूपसरांसि च ।
निर्जला अभवन्सर्वे संशुष्का वृक्षवीरुधः ॥ ८॥

English
Rivers, oceans, tanks, wells and lakes dried. Trees and plants withered. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दृष्ट्वा महादुःखं प्रजानां दीनचेतसाम् ।
त्रिदशाः शरणं याता योगमायां महेश्वरीम् ॥ ९॥

English
On seeing the misery of the distressed subjects the gods sought refuge in the Yogic Māyā, Maheśvari. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
रक्ष रक्ष महामाये स्वकीयाः सकलाः प्रजाः ।
कोपं संहर नूनं त्वं लोका नङ्क्ष्यन्ति चान्यथा ॥ १०॥

English
The gods said—  O Mahāmāyā, save all your people. Check your fury or the worlds will be ruined. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा शुम्भो हतो दैत्यो निशुम्भश्च महाबलः ।
धूम्राक्षश्चण्डमुण्डौ च रक्तबीजो महाबलः ॥ ११॥

समधुः कैटभो दैत्यो महिषासुर एव च ।
तथैवामुं कृपासिन्धो दीनबन्धो जहि द्रुतम् ॥ १२॥

English
O ocean of mercy, O supportress of the distressed, kill this demon too just as you have killed Śumbha, Niśumbha. Dhūmrākṣa, Caṇḍa, Muṇḍa, Raktabīja, Madhu, Kaiṭabha and Mahiṣa. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
अपराधो भवत्येव बालकानां पदे पदे ।
सहते को जनो लोके केवलं मातरं विना ॥ १३॥

English
Boys are guilty of offence at every step but who on earth brooks it except the mother ? (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा यदाभवद्दुःखं देवानां ब्रह्मणां तथा ।
तदा तदावतीर्याशु कुरुषे सुखिनो जनान् ॥ १४॥

English
When miseries afflict the gods and the brahmins you incarnate for the welfare of the people. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इति विक्लवितं तेषां समाकर्ण्य कृपामयी ।
अनन्ताक्षमयं रूपं दर्शयामास साम्प्रतम् ॥ १५॥

English
On hearing the distress of the gods the merciful goddess revealed to them her form possessed of many eyes. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
धनुर्बाणौ तथा पद्मं नानामूलफलानि च ।
चतुर्भिर्दधती हस्तैः प्रसन्नमुखपङ्कजा ॥ १६॥

English
The goddess with her lotus-face beaming with delight held in her four hands the bow, the arrows, the lotus and different fruits and roots. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दृष्ट्वा प्रजास्तप्ताः करुणापूरितेक्षणा ।
रुरोद नव घस्राणि नव रात्रीः समाकुला ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing her people scorched and distressed, the goddess was dejected. With her eyes full of mercy she cried for nine days and nights. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मोचयामास दृष्टिभ्यो वारिधाराः सहस्रशः ।
ताभिः प्रतर्पिता लोका औषध्यः सकला अपि ॥ १८॥

English
The tears took the forms of thousands of water-currents released from her eyes. The worlds, the plants and medicinal herbs were delighted by them. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अगाधतोयाः सरितो बभूवुः सागरा अपि ।
रुरुहुर्धरणीपृष्ठे शाकमूलफलानि च ॥ १९॥

English
They turned into the rivers and oceans of deep waters. Vegetables, roots and fruits grew on the earth. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विततार करस्थानि सुमनोभ्यः फलानि च ।
गोभ्यस्तृणानि रम्याणि तथान्येभ्यो यथार्हतः ॥ २०॥

English
To the learned and the gods she distributed the fruits in her hands. To the cows she gave the tender blades of grass and to others the things they deserved. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्तुष्टा अभवन्सर्वे सदेवद्विजमानुषाः ।
ततो जगाद सा देवी किमन्यत्करवाणि वः ॥ २१॥

English
All including the gods, brahmins and men were satisfied. Then the goddess said—“What else shall I do for you ?” (21.)

---

Sanskrit
समेत्योचुस्तदा देवा भवत्या तोषिता जनाः ।
वेदान्देहि कृपां कृत्वा दुर्गमेण समाहृताम् ॥ २२॥

English
The gods then gathered together and said, “The people have been rendered happy and satisfied. O goddess, be pleased and redeem the Vedas taken by Durgama.” (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तथास्त्विति प्रभाष्याह यात यात निजालयम् ।
वितरिष्यामि वो वेदानचिरेणैव कालतः ॥ २३॥

English
After saying “So be it” she said. “Go ye all to your abodes. Ere long I shall give you the Vedas.” (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रमुदिता देवाः स्वं स्वं धाम समाययुः ।
सुप्रणम्य जगद्योनिं फुल्लेन्दीवरलोचनाम् ॥ २४॥

English
Then the joyous gods returned to their abodes after bowing to the goddess the cause of the universe with her eyes that resembled full-blown blue lotus. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः कोलाहलो जातो दिवि भूम्यन्तरिक्षके ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा रौरवः सद्यो न्यरुणत्सर्वतः पुरीम् ॥ २५॥

English
Then a great bustle arose in the three regions heaven, earth and sky. On hearing it the son of Ruru besieged the city immediately. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तेजोमयं चक्रं विधाय परितः शिवा ।
रक्षणार्थं देवतानां स्वयं तस्माद् बहिर्गता ॥ २६॥

English
Then after creating a big circle of splendour all round for the protection of the gods, Śivā herself came out of it. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः समभवद्युद्धं देव्या दैत्यस्य चोभयोः ।
ववृषुः समरे बाणान्निशितान्कङ्कटच्छिदः ॥ २७॥

English
A great battle ensued between the goddess and the Daitya. Both showered arrows in the battle, piercing the armour of each other. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे तस्याः शरीराद् रम्यमूर्तयः ।
काली तारा छिन्नमस्ता श्रीविद्या भुवनेश्वरी ॥ २८॥

भैरवी बगला धूम्रा श्रीमत्त्रिपुरसुन्दरी ।
मातङ्गी च महाविद्या निर्गता दश सायुधाः ॥ २९॥

English
In the meantime ten beautiful forms came out of Kālī Viz Tārā, Chinnamastā, Śrīvidyā, Bhuvanesvarī, Bhairavī, Bagalā, Dhūmrā, Śrīmatripurasundarī, Mātaṅgī and Mahāvidyā with weapons in their hands. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
असङ्ख्यातास्ततो जाता मातरो दिव्यमूर्त्तयः ।
चन्द्रलेखाधराः सर्वाः सर्वा विद्युत्समप्रभाः ॥ ३०॥

English
Then appeared innumerable mothers of divine forms, wearing the digit of the moon and with a lustre resembling the splendour of lightning. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो मातृगणैर्युद्धं प्रावर्तत भयङ्करम् ।
रौरवीयं हतं ताभिर्दलमक्षौहिणीशतम् ॥ ३१॥

English
Then a terrible combat ensued between the Asuras and the mothers. The army of the son of Ruru, consisting of a hundred Akṣauhiṇīs was killed in the battle. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
जघान सा तदा दैत्यं दुर्गमं शूलधारया ।
पपात धरणीपृष्ठे खातमूलद्रुमो यथा ॥ ३२॥

English
Then the goddess struck Durgama with the edge of her trident. He fell on the earth like an uprooted tree. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं हत्वा तदा दैत्यं दुर्गमासुरनामकम् ।
आदाय चतुरो वेदान्ददौ देवेभ्य ईश्वरी ॥ ३३॥

English
Thus after killing him the goddess redeemed the four Vedas and gave them to the gods. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
अस्मदर्थं त्वया रूपमनन्ताक्षिमयं धृतम् ।
मुनयः कीर्तयिष्यन्ति शताक्षीं त्वामतोऽम्बिके ॥ ३४॥

English
# The gods said:—  O Ambikā, for our welfare that form of infinite eyes was assumed by you. Hence sages will glorify you as Śatākṣī. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मदेहसमुद्भूतैः शाकैर्लोका मृता यतः ।
शाकम्भरीति विख्यातं तत्ते नाम भविष्यति ॥ ३५॥

English
Since the worlds were sustained by you with vegetables sprouting from your body, you will be famous as Śākambharī. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्गमाख्यो महादैत्यो हतो यस्मात्ततः शिवे ।
दुर्गां भगवतीं भद्रां व्याहरिष्यन्ति मानवाः ॥ ३६॥

English
O Śivā, since you killed the Daitya Durgama you will be known as Durgā. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
योगनिद्रे नमस्तुभ्यं नमस्तेऽस्तु महाबले ।
नमो ज्ञानप्रदे तुभ्यं विश्वमात्रे नमो नमः ॥ ३७॥

English
O Yogic slumber, O Mahābalā, O bestower of knowledge, O the mother of the universe, Obeisance be to you. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्त्वमस्यादिवाक्यैर्या बोध्यते परमेश्वरी ।
अनन्तकोटिब्रह्माण्डनायिकायै नमो नमः ॥ ३८॥

English
Obeisance to the heroine of infinite universes known as the supreme goddess in the Mahāvākyas as Tat-vamasi. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
वाङ्मनःकायदुष्प्रापां सूर्यचन्द्राग्निलोचनाम् ।
स्तोतुं न शक्नुमो मातस्त्वत्प्रभावाबुधा वयम् ॥ ३९॥

English
O Mother, we who are unaware of your power and greatness, cannot adequately eulogise you who are inaccessible through words, mind or body and whose eyes are the sun and the moon. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
मादृशानमरान्दृष्ट्वा कः कुर्यादीदृशीं दयाम् ।
वर्जयित्वा सुरेशानीं शताक्षीं मातरं विना ॥ ४०॥

English
Who else will bestow mercy on seeing us the gods except the great goddess Śatākṣī the mother? (40.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिलोकी नाभिभूयेत बाधाभिश्च निरन्तरम् ।
एवं कार्यस्त्वया यत्नोऽस्माकं वैरिविनाशनम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Effort shall be made by you so that the three worlds will not be pestered continuously with obstacles and our enemies shall be destroyed. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
वत्सान्दृष्ट्वा यथा गावो व्यग्रा धावन्ति सत्वरम् ।
तथैव भवतो दृष्ट्वा धावामि व्याकुला सती ॥ ४२॥

English
*The gods said:—*  Just as the cows run about in excitement quickly on seeing their calves in distress, so also I, Satī, am excited on seeing you in distress and run about. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
मम युष्मानपश्यन्त्याः पश्यन्त्या बालकानिव ।
अपि प्राणान्प्रयच्छन्त्याः क्षण एको युगायते ॥ ४३॥

English
Even a moment appears to be a Yuga if I do not see you since I look at you as my own children for whom I am ready even to lay down my life. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
कापि चिन्ता न कर्तव्या युष्माभिर्भक्तिशालिभिः ।
भवत्यां मयि तिष्ठन्त्यां संहरन्त्यां निजापदः ॥ ४४॥

English
No worry or anxious thought need be entertained by you endowed with devotion as long as I stand by you destroying your distress. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा पूर्वं हता दैत्या हनिष्यामि तथाऽसुरान् ।
संशयो नात्र कर्त्तव्यः सत्यं सत्यं ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥ ४५॥

English
In the manner I have killed the Daityas I shall kill the Asuras. You need not entertain any doubt in this regard. Truth, I am speaking the truth. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा शुम्भो निशुम्भश्चापरौ दैत्यौ भविष्यतः ।
तदाहं नन्दभार्यायां यशोदायां यशोमयी ॥ ४६॥

योनिजं रूपमास्थाय जनिष्ये गोपगोकुले ।
हनिष्याम्यसुरौ तन्मां व्याहरिष्यन्ति नन्दजाम् ॥ ४७॥

English
I shall be born of the womb in the family of cowherds as the daughter of Yaśodā wife of Nandā, when Śumbha and Niśumbha will be born as Daityas. I shall kill the demons and become famous. Men will therefore call me Nandajā. (46-47.)

---

Sanskrit
भ्रामरं रूपमास्थाय वधिष्याम्यरुणं यतः ।
भ्रामरीति च मां लोके कीर्तयिष्यन्ति मानवाः ॥ ४८॥

English
Since I shall be taking the form of a bee and killing the demon Aruṇa, men will glorify me as Bhrāmari (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा भीमं पुना रूपं रक्षांस्यत्स्याम्यहं यदा ।
भीमा देवीति विख्यातं तन्मे नाम भविष्यति ॥ ४९॥

English
Again I shall take a terrible form and devour the Rākṣasas. I shall then be famous as Bhimā Devi. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा यदासुरोत्थैवं बाधा भुवि भविष्यति ।
तदा तदावतीर्याहं शं करिष्याम्यसंशयम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Whenever there is harassment caused by the Asuras I shall surely incarnate and work for your welfare. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
या शताक्षी स्मृता देवी सैव शाकम्भरी मता ।
सैव प्रकीर्तिता दुर्गा व्यक्तिरेकैव त्रिष्वपि ॥ ५१॥

English
The goddess Śatākṣī, Śākambhari and Durgā are identical. In all the three the individual is the same. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
न शताक्षीसमा काचिद्दयालुर्भुवि देवता ।
दृष्ट्वारुदत्प्रजास्तप्ता या नवाहं महेश्वरी ॥ ५२॥

English
There is no deity so compassionate as Śatākṣī the great goddess who cried for nine days on seeing her people scorched and distressed. (52.)
