### **CHAPTER FIFTYONE - (Review of holy rites)**

---

Sanskrit
एकपञ्चाशत्तमोऽध्यायः । क्रियायोगनिरूपणम् ।

मुनय ऊचुः ।
व्यासशिष्य महाभाग सूत पौराणिकोत्तम ।
अपरं श्रोतुमिच्छामः किमप्याख्यानमीशितुः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said :—  O blessed disciple of Vyāsa, O Sūta the most excellent among the knowers of the Purāṇas, we wish to hear some other story of the lord. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
उमाया जगदम्बायाः क्रियायोगमनुत्तमम् ।
प्रोक्तं सनत्कुमारेण व्यासाय च महात्मने ॥ २॥

English
We wish to hear the most excellent sacred rites of Umā, the mother of the universe, those which were narrated to Vyāsa the great Ātman by Sanatkumāra (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
धन्या यूयं महात्मानो देवीभक्तिदृढव्रताः ।
पराशक्तेः परं गुप्तं रहस्यं श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said :—  All of you are blessed great souls; steady in your devotional rites to the goddess. Now listen devotedly to the well-guarded secret of the great Śakti. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
सनत्कुमार सर्वज्ञ ब्रह्मपुत्र महामते ।
उमायाः श्रोतुमिच्छामि क्रियायोगं महाद्भुतम् ॥ ४॥

English
Vyāsa said :—  O omniscient Sanatkumāra, O intelligent son of Brahmā, I wish to hear the wonderful holy rites of Umā. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
कीदृक्च लक्षणं तस्य किं कृते च फलं भवेत् ।
प्रियं यच्च पराम्बायास्तदशेषं वदस्व मे ॥ ५॥

English
Please tell me what is pleasing to the great mother. What are its characteristics and on doing what can it be efficacious? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सनत्कुमार उवाच ।
द्वैपायन यदेतत्त्वं रहस्यं परिपृच्छसि ।
तच्छृणुष्व महाबुद्धे सर्वं मे वर्णयिष्यतः ॥ ६॥

English
Sanatkumāra said :—  O Dvaipāyana of great intellect, listen to the full description I am going to give of the secret that you ask. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानयोगः क्रियायोगो भक्तियोगस्तथैव च ।
त्रयो मार्गाः समाख्याताः श्रीमातुर्भुक्तिमुक्तिदाः ॥ ७॥

English
Three paths of the glorious mother have been narrated which yield both worldly pleasures and salvation. They are paths of knowledge, holy rites and devotion. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानयोगस्तु संयोगश्चित्तस्यैवात्मना तु यः ।
यस्तु बाह्यार्थसंयोगः क्रियायोगः स उच्यते ॥ ८॥

English
Jñānayoga is the union of the mind with the Ātman. The union with the external objects is called Kriyāyoga. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
भक्तियोगो मतो देव्या आत्मनश्चैक्यभावनम् ।
त्रयाणामपि योगानां क्रियायोगः स उच्यते ॥ ९॥

English
Bhaktiyoga is the concept of the unity of the Ātman with the goddess. Of the three I mention Kriyāyoga in detail. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मणा जायते भक्तिर्भक्त्या ज्ञानं प्रजायते ।
ज्ञानात्प्रजायते मुक्तिरिति शास्त्रेषु निश्चयः ॥ १०॥

English
It is concluded in the sacred scriptures that devotion is generated by activities, knowledge is born of devotion, liberation is the result of knowledge. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधानं कारणं योगो विमुक्तेर्मुनिसत्तम ।
क्रियायोगस्तु योगस्य परमं ध्येयसाधनम् ॥ ११॥

English
O excellent sage, the chief reason of liberation is the Yoga. Kriyāyoga is the greatest means of liberation. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
मायां तु प्रकृतिं विद्यान्मायावी ब्रह्म शाश्वतम् ।
अभिन्नं तद्वपुर्ज्ञात्वा मुच्यते भवबन्धनात् ॥ १२॥

English
One shall know the Māyā as Prakṛti and the wielder of Māyā as the eternal Brahman. After realising the identity of the two one is released from the world by bondage. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तु देव्यालयं कुर्यात्पाषाणं दारवं तथा ।
मृन्मयं वाथ कालेय तस्य पुण्यफलं श‍ृणु ।
अहन्यहनि योगेन यजतो यन्महाफलम् ॥ १३॥

प्राप्नोति तत्फलं देव्या यः कारयति मन्दिरम् ।
सहस्रकुलमागामि व्यतीतं च सहस्रकम् ।
स तारयति धर्मात्मा श्रीमातुर्धाम कारयन् ॥ १४॥

English
O Vyāsa, listen to the merit of the person who builds a temple to the goddess either of stone or of wood or of mud. He who builds a temple to the goddess attains the benefit which a person who worships every day through Yoga attains in plenty. That virtuous soul who builds the temple for the glorious mother enables a thousand past and a thousand future members of his family to attain liberation. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
कोटिजन्मकृतं पापं स्वल्पं वा यदि वा बहु ।
श्रीमातुर्मन्दिरारम्भक्षणादेव प्रणश्यति ॥ १५॥

English
The sins committed by a person in a crore of births, whether they be small or big, are quelled at the very moment when the foundation for temple of the glorious mother is laid. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
नदीषु च यथा गङ्गा शोणः सर्वनदेषु च ।
क्षमायां च यथा पृथ्वी गाम्भीर्ये च यथोदधिः ॥ १६॥

ग्रहाणां च समस्तानां यथा सूर्यो विशिष्यते ।
तथा सर्वेषु देवेषु श्रीपराम्बा विशिष्यते ॥ १७॥

English
The glorious great Ambā is the most excellent among all the deities just as Gaṅgā and Śoṇa among the rivers, the earth in forbearance, the ocean in majesty and the sun among the planets. (16-17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वदेवेषु सा मुख्या यस्तस्याः कारयेद् गृहम् ।
प्रतिष्ठां समवाप्नोति स च जन्मनि जन्मनि ॥ १८॥

English
She is the most important among all the deities. He who causes her temple to be built attains established power in every birth. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
वाराणस्यां कुरुक्षेत्रे प्रयागे पुष्करे तथा ।
गङ्गासमुद्रतीरे च नैमिषेऽमरकण्टके ॥ १९॥

श्रीपर्वते महापुण्ये गोकर्णे ज्ञानपर्वते ।
मथुरायामयोध्यायां द्वारावत्यां तथैव च ॥ २०॥

इत्यादिपुण्यदेशेषु यत्र कुत्र स्थलेऽपि वा ।
कारयन्मातुरावासं मुक्तो भवति बन्धनात् ॥ २१॥

English
One who makes the temple for the mother in any of these holy centres becomes free from bondage—viz. Vārāṇasī, Kurukṣetra, Prayāga, Puṣkara, the banks of the Gaṅgā or the shores of the ocean, Naimiṣa, amarakaṇṭaka, the highly meritorious Śrīparvata, Gokarṇa, Jñānaparvata,⁹⁴ Mathurā, Ayodhyā, Dvāravatī etc. (19-21.)

---

Sanskrit
इष्टकानां तु विन्यासो यावद्वर्षाणि तिष्ठति ।
तावद्वर्षसहस्राणि मणिद्वीपे महीयते ॥ २२॥

English
He is honoured in the Maṇidvīpa for as many thousand years as the number of years the brickwork holds good. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रतिमाः कारयेद्यस्तु सर्वलक्षणलक्षिताः ।
स उमायाः परं लोकं निर्भयो व्रजति धुवम् ॥ २३॥

English
He who makes idols with the full complement of characteristics fearlessly attains indeed the great region of Umā. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
देवीमूर्तिं प्रतिष्ठाप्य शुभर्तुग्रहतारके ।
कृतकृत्यो भवेन्मर्त्यो योगमायाप्रसादतः ॥ २४॥

English
After installing the image of the goddess, in the auspicious stars, planets and the season, man becomes contented and blessed by the favour of the Yogamāyā. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ये भविष्यन्ति येऽतीता आकल्पात्पुरुषाः कुले ।
तांस्तांस्तारयते देव्या मूर्तिं संस्थाप्य शोभनाम् ॥ २५॥

English
After installing an auspicious image of the goddess one can redeem the men of one's family both of the past and the future. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिलोकीस्थापनात्पुण्यं यद्भवेन्मुनिपुङ्गव ।
तत्कोटिगुणितं पुण्यं श्रीदेवीस्थापनाद्भवेत् ॥ २६॥

English
O leading sage, by installing the goddess the merit derived is a crore times more than that derived by establishing the three worlds. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
मध्ये देवीं स्थापयित्वा पञ्चायतनदेवताः ।
चतुर्दिक्षु स्थापयेद्यस्तस्य पुण्यं न गण्यते ॥ २७॥

English
The merit cannot be calculated of the man who installs the Pañcāyatana⁹⁵ deities with Ambikā in the centre and the other four in the four quarters. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोर्नाम्नां कोटिजपाद् ग्रहणे चन्द्रसूर्ययोः ।
यत्फलं लभ्यते तस्माच्छतकोटिगुणोत्तरम् ॥ २८॥

शिवनाम्नो जपादेव तस्मात्कोटिगुणोत्तरम् ।
श्रीदेवीनामजापात्तु ततः कोटिगुणोत्तरम् ॥ २९॥

देव्याः प्रासादकरणात्पुण्यं तु समवाप्यते ।
स्थापिता येन सा देवी जगन्माता त्रयीमयी ॥ ३०॥

न तस्य दुर्लभं किञ्चित् श्रीमातुः करुणावशात् ।
वर्धन्ते पुत्रपौत्राद्या नश्यत्यखिलकश्मलम् ॥ ३१॥

English
One attains great merit by repeating the names of Viṣṇu a crore times during the solar and lunar eclipses, a hundred crore times by repeating the names of Śiva, a crore times by repeating the names of Śrīdevī, a crore times by building the palace for the goddess. To the man who installs the image of the goddess, mother of the universe, identical with the three Vedas there is nothing inaccessible, thanks to the merit of the glorious mother. His sons, grandsons and others flourish. All his sins perish. (28-31.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसा ये चिकीर्षन्ति मूर्तिस्थापनमुत्तमम् ।
तेऽत्युमायाः परं लोकं प्रयान्ति मुनिदुर्लभम् ॥ ३२॥

English
Even those who desire mentally the installation of the excellent image attain the great region of Umā inaccessible even to the sages. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रियमाणं तु यः प्रेक्ष्य चेतसा ह्यनुचिन्तयेत् ।
कारयिष्याम्यहं यर्हि सम्पन्मे सम्भविष्यति ॥ ३३॥

एवं तस्य कुलं सद्यो याति स्वर्गं न संशयः ।
महामायाप्रभावेण दुर्लभं किं जगत्त्रये ॥ ३४॥

English
If a man, on seeing the image being made or the temple being built thinks mentally: "If I get sufficient wealth I too will make", his family attains heaven undoubtedly. What is there in the three worlds which cannot be secured by the power of Mahāmāyā? (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीपराम्बां जगद्योनिं केवलं ये समाश्रिताः ।
ते मनुष्या न मन्तव्याः साक्षाद्देवीगणाश्च ते ॥ ३५॥

English
Those who have resorted only to the goddess the cause of the universe, are not ordinary men. They are the Gaṇas of the Goddess herself. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ये व्रजन्तः स्वपन्तश्च तिष्ठन्तो वाप्यहर्निशम् ।
उमेति द्व्यक्षरं नाम ब्रुवते ते शिवागणाः ॥ ३६॥

English
Those who repeat the two syllables "U-mā" day and night, sitting, sleeping or moving become Śivā's Gaṇas. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
नित्ये नैमित्तिके देवीं ये यजन्ति परां शिवाम् ।
पुष्पैर्धूपैस्तथा दीपैस्ते प्रयास्यन्त्युमालयम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Those who worship the goddess Śivā, with flowers, incense and lamps whether as daily routine or on special occasions due to certain reasons will attain Umā's abode. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ये देवीमण्डपं नित्यं गोमयेन मृदाथवा ।
उपलिम्पन्ति मार्जन्ति ते प्रयास्यन्त्युमालयम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Those who clean, scrub and smear the altar of the goddess with cowdung or clay will attain Umā's abode. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यैर्देव्या मन्दिरं रम्यं निर्मापितमनुत्तमम् ।
तत्कुलीनाञ्जनान्माता ह्याशिषः सम्प्रयच्छति ॥ ३९॥

English
The goddess mother bestows her blessings on the family of the person who builds a beautiful temple for the goddess. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
मदीयाः शतवर्षाणि जीवन्तु प्रेमभाजनाः ।
नापदामयनानीत्थं श्रीमाता वक्त्यहर्निशम् ॥ ४०॥

English
The glorious mother repeats day and night: "May my devotees live for a hundred years. Let them not be the victims of adversities." (40.)

---

Sanskrit
येन मूर्तिर्महादेब्या उमायाः कारिता शुभा ।
नरायुतं तत्कुलजं मणिद्वीपे महीयते ॥ ४१॥

English
Ten thousand people of the person who has caused the image of the goddess Umā to be made, are honoured in the Maṇidvīpa.⁹⁶ (41.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थापयित्वा महामायामूर्तिं सम्यक्प्रपूज्य च ।
यं यं प्रार्थयते कामं तं तं प्राप्नोति साधकः ॥ ४२॥

English
Whatever an aspirant solicits after installing and worshipping the image of the goddess he attains that. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
यः स्नापयति श्रीमातुः स्थापितां मूर्तिमुत्तमाम् ।
घृतेन मधुनाक्तेन तत्फलं गणयेत्तु कः ॥ ४३॥

English
Who can calculate the merit of a person who performs the ablution of the installed image of the glorious mother, with clarified butter after smearing it with honey? (43.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्दनागुरुकर्पूरमांसीमुस्तादियुग्जलैः ।
एकवर्णगवां क्षीरैः स्नापयेत्परमेश्वरीम् ॥ ४४॥

English
The devotee shall perform the ablution of the goddess either with water scented with sandal, aguru, camphor māṃsī, mustā etc. or with the milk of single-coloured cows. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
धूपेनाष्टादशाङ्गेन दद्यादाहुतिमुत्तमाम् ।
नीराजनं चरेद्देव्याः साज्यकर्पूरवर्तिभिः ॥ ४५॥

English
The excellent offering shall be made with the incense of eighteen ingredients. The waving of the light for the goddess shall consist of wicks soaked in ghee or camphor. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
कृष्णाष्टम्यां नवम्यां वामायां वा पञ्चदिक्तिथौ ।
पूजयेज्जगतां धात्रीं गन्धपुष्पैर्विशेषतः ॥ ४६॥

English
On the fifth, eighth, ninth and tenth days of the dark lunar half and on new moon days the devotee shall worship the mother of the worlds with fragrant flowers. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पठञ्जननीसूक्तं श्रीसूक्तमथवा पठन् ।
देवीसूक्तमथो वापि मूलमन्त्रमथापि वा ॥ ४७॥

English
Jananīsūkta, Srīsūkta or Devīsūkta shall be recited or Mūlamantra shall be chanted. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुक्रान्तां च तुलसीं वर्जयित्वाखिलं सुमम् ।
देवीप्रीतिकरं ज्ञेयं कमलं तु विशेषतः ॥ ४८॥

English
All flowers except Viṣṇukrāntā and Tulasi are pleasing to the goddess particularly the lotus. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्पयेत्स्वर्णपुष्पं यो देव्यै राजतमेव वा ।
स याति परमं धाम सिद्धकोटिभिरन्वितम् ॥ ४९॥

English
He who offers golden or silver flowers to the goddess goes to the greatest abode where crores of Siddhas live. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजनान्ते सदा कार्यं दासैरेनः क्षमापनम् ।
प्रसीद परमेशानि जगदानन्ददायिनि ॥ ५०॥

इति वाक्यैः ‍स्तुवन्मन्त्री देवीभक्तिपरायणः ।
ध्यायेत्कण्ठीरवारूढां वरदाभयपाणिकाम् ॥ ५१॥

English
After the worship the rite of forgiveness shall be performed by the devotees for the sins committed :—“O goddess, O bestower of happiness and bliss be pleased.” He shall eulogise her with these words and meditate on the goddess as seated on a lion with boons and the majestic gesture of protection. (50-51.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं ध्यात्वा महेशानीं भक्ताभीष्टफलप्रदाम् ।
नानाफलानि पक्वानि नैवेद्यत्वे प्रकल्पयेत् ॥ ५२॥

English
After meditating on the goddess, the bestower of desires on the devotees he shall offer various ripe fruits as Naivedya. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
नैवेद्यं भक्षयेद्यस्तु शम्भुशक्तेः परात्मनः ।
स निर्धूयाखिलं पङ्कं निर्मलो मानवो भवेत् ॥ ५३॥

English
The man who partakes of Naivedya of Siva Sakti shakes off all dirt and becomes pure. (53.)

Sanskrit
