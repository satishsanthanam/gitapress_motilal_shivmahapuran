### **CHAPTER ONE - (The discussion among Vyāsa, Śaunaka and others)**

---

Sanskrit
अथ षष्ठी कैलाससंहिता प्रारभ्यते ॥

नमः शिवाय साम्बाय सगणाय ससूनवे ।
प्रधानपुरुषेशाय सर्गस्थितत्यन्तहेतवे ॥ १॥

English
Obeisance to Śiva accompanied by Ambā, his sons and the Gaṇas, obeisance to the lord of Pradhāna and Puruṣa, obeisance to him who is the cause of creation, sustenance and dissolution. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषय ऊचुः ।
श्रुतोमासंहिता रम्या नानाख्यानसमन्विता ।
कैलाससंहितां ब्रूहि शिवतत्त्वविवर्धिनीम् ॥ २॥

English
The sages said :—  The beautiful Umāsaṃhitā full of various anecdotes we have heard. Now narrate the Kailāsasaṃhitā that propagates Śiva's tenets. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
श‍ृणुत प्रीतितो वत्साः कैलासाख्यां हि संहिताम् ।
शिवतत्त्वपरां दिव्यां वक्ष्ये वः स्नेहतः पराम् ॥ ३॥

English
Vyāsa said :—  O dear, now listen with pleasure. Out of affection for you I shall narrate the divine Saṃhitā Kailāsa expounding Śiva's tenets. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
हिमवच्छिखरे पूर्वं तपस्यन्तो महौजसः ।
वाराणसीं गन्तुकामा मुनयः कृतसंविदः ॥ ४॥

English
Formerly the sages of great power and splendour performing penances on the summit of Himavat⁹⁷ desired to go to Vārāṇasī⁹⁸ and decided accordingly. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्गत्य तस्मात्सम्प्राप्य गिरेः काशीं समाहिताः ।
स्नातव्यमेवेति तदा ददृशुर्मणिकर्णिकाम् ॥ ५॥

English
They started from the mountain and reached Kāśī. They decided to bathe there and saw Maṇikarṇikā.⁹⁹ (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र स्नात्वा सुसन्तर्प्य देवादीनथ जाह्नवीम् ।
दृष्ट्वा स्नात्वा मुनीशास्ते विश्वेशं त्रिदशेश्वरम् ॥ ६॥

नमस्कृत्याथ सम्पूज्य भक्त्या परमयान्विताः ।
शतरुद्रादिभिः स्तुत्वा स्तुतिभिर्वेदपारगाः ॥ ७॥

आत्मानं मेनिरे सर्वे कृतार्था वयमित्युत ।
शिवप्रीत्या सुपूर्णार्थाः शिवभक्तिरताः सदा ॥ ८॥

English
The lordly sages bathed there and performed the Tarpaṇa rites to the gods and others and to Gaṅgā. They visited Viśveśa the lord of the gods, bowed and worshipped him with devotion. The sages, the masters of the Vedas eulogised him with Śatarudra and other mantras and thought "O, we are blessed. We are content." Due to Śiva's pleasure they realised everything. They remained ever engaged in devotion to Śiva. (6-8.)

Sanskrit

English
In the meantime Sūta who had left his hermitage to visit Pañcakrośa$^{100}$ reached there. On seeing him the joyous sages paid obeisance to him. (9.)

Sanskrit

English
After bowing to Viśveśvara, the lord of gods, the consort of Uma, Sūta entered the Muktimandapa along with them. (10.)

Sanskrit

English
When Sūta the great soul, the excellent of the Paurāṇikas took his seat, the sages paid respects to him with Arghya and other ingredients of worship. (11.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The delighted Suta saw the sages of auspicious rites and enquired after their health and welfare. They replied suitably. (12.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रणवार्थावगत्यर्थमूचुः प्रास्ताविकं वचः ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing him pleased at heart the sages spoke in relevance to the context, in order to know the meaning of Praṇava. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
व्यासशिष्य महाभाग सूत पौराणिकोत्तम ।
धन्यस्त्वं शिवभक्तो हि सर्वविज्ञानसागरः ॥ १४॥

English
# The sages said:—  O blessed disciple of Vyasa, O Suta the best among the Paurāṇikas, you are a great devotee of Siva, the ocean of perfect knowledge. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
भवन्तमेव भगवान्व्यासः सर्वजगद्गुरुः ।
अभिषिच्य पुराणानां गुरुत्वे समयोजयत् ॥ १५॥

English
Lord Vyasa, the preceptor of the universe has crowned you as the chief of the Paurāṇikas and made you the propounder of the Purānas. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्पौराणिकी विद्या भवतो हृदि संस्थिता ।
पुराणानि च सर्वाणि वेदार्थं प्रवदन्ति हि ॥ १६॥

English
The Purānic lore is firmly set in your heart. Indeed the Purānas explain the meaning of the Vedas. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदाः प्रणवसम्भूताः प्रणवार्थो महेश्वरः ।
अतो महेश्वरस्थानं त्वयि धिष्ण्यं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १७॥

English
Vedas arise from the Praṇava. The Praṇava is lord Śiva. Hence you are the permanent abode of lord Śiva's stay. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वन्मुखाब्जपरिस्यन्दन्मकरन्देमनोहरम् ।
प्रणवार्थामृतं पीत्वा भविष्यामो गतज्वराः ॥ १८॥

English
We shall be free from illness after we have drunk the nectar of Praṇava which is as sweet and pleasing as the honey oozing out of your lotus-mouth. (18.)

---

Sanskrit

परं भावं महेशस्य परया कृपया वद ॥ १९॥

English
O intelligent one, you alone are our preceptor, none else. Out of compassion please narrate the nature of lord Siva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit

शिलादतनयं देवं नन्दीशं सुयशापतिम् ।
सनत्कुमारं व्यासं च प्रणिपत्येदमब्रवीत् ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing their words, the intelligent Suta, the beloved disciple of Vyasa, made obeisance to Ganeisa, six-faced Kārttika, Maheśvari, lord Nandīśa-son of Śilāda and husband of Suyasā, Sanatkumāra and Vyāsa and said. (20-21.)

Sanskrit

साधु साधु महाभागा मुनयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।

English
Sūta said :—  Well done, well done, O blessed sages, your sins have been quelled. Your minds are steadied. It is rare in persons of evil deeds. (22.)

Sanskrit

यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण शिवभक्तिर्भवेन्नृणाम् ।

English
O leading sages, I shall narrate to you what was mentioned by my preceptor Vyāsa to the sages in the Naimiṣa forest. On hearing this alone men are blessed with devotion to Śiva. You shall listen with attention and joy. (23-24.)

Sanskrit
स्वारोचिषेऽन्तरे पूर्वं तपस्यन्तो दृढव्रताः ।
ऋषयो नैमिषारण्ये सर्वसिद्धनिषेविते ॥ २५॥

दीर्घसत्रं वितन्वन्तो रुद्रमध्वरनायकम् ।
प्रीणयन्तः परं भावमैश्वर्यं ज्ञातुमिच्छवः ॥ २६॥

निवसन्ति स्म ते सर्वे व्यासदर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ।
शिवभक्तिरता नित्यं भस्मरुद्राक्षधारिणः ॥ २७॥

English
Formerly in the Svārociṣa Manvantara, sages of steady rites performed penance in the Naimiṣa¹⁰² forest where all the Siddhas live. They conducted a sacrifice of long duration, propitiated Rudra the leader of sacrifices. They desired to know the supreme nature of the lord. They waited for Vyāsa. They were engaged in devotion to Śiva and wore Rudrākṣa after smearing their bodies with the ashes. (25-27.)

English
Observing their eagerness, lord Bādarāyaṇa, the soul of all, the fruit of the penance of Parāśara appeared before them. (28.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

English
Your activity seems to me to be the outcome of your desire to hear more of the great nature of lord Śiva, the cause of salvation. (33.)

English
Thus urged by the sage Vyāsa of great brilliance the great sages of the Naimiṣa forest bowed to him, the noble soul whose mind was delighted by his devotion to Śiva. (34-35.)

Sanskrit
