### **CHAPTER TWO - (The dialogus between the god and the goddess)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
साधु पृष्टमिदं विप्रा भवद्भिर्भाग्यवत्तमैः ।
दुर्लभं हि शिवज्ञानं प्रणवार्थप्रकाशकम् ॥ १॥

येषां प्रसन्नो भगवान्साक्षाच्छूलवरायुधः ।
तेषामेव शिवज्ञानं प्रणवार्थप्रकाशकम् ॥ २॥

जायते न हि सन्देहो नेतरेषामिति श्रुतिः ।
शिवभक्तिविहीनानामिति तत्त्वार्थनिश्चयः ॥ ३॥

English
Vyāsa said :—  O blessed brahmins, the question has been well put by you. The knowledge of Śiva's principle that illuminates the meaning of Pṛaṇava's incomprehensible. It can be obtained surely by those with whom the trident-bearing lord is pleased and not by others who are undoubtedly devoid of Śiva's devotion. This is true. (1-3.)

---

Sanskrit
दीर्घसत्रेण युष्माभिर्भगवानम्बिकापतिः ।
उपासित इतीदं मे दृष्टमद्य विनिश्चितम् ॥ ४॥

English
Lord Śiva, consort of Ambā, has been adored by you by long sacrifices. This I see actually. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्वक्ष्यामि युष्माकमितिहासं पुरातनम् ।
उमामहेशसंवादरूपमद्भुतमास्तिकाः ॥ ५॥

English
O devotees, I shall narrate to you an ancient story containing a wonderful dialogue between Umā and Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पुराखिलजगन्माता सती दाक्षायणी तनुम् ।
शिवनिन्दाप्रसङ्गेन त्यक्त्वा च जनकाध्वरे ॥ ६॥

ततः प्रभावात्सा देवी सुताभूद्धिमवद्गिरेः ।
शिवार्थमतपत्सा वै नारदस्योपदेशतः ॥ ७॥

तस्मिन्भूधरवर्ये तु स्वयंवरविधानतः ।
देवेशे च कृतोद्वाहे पार्वती सुखमाप सा ॥ ८॥

English
Formerly Satī, daughter of Dakṣa, the mother of the universe abandoned her body in the sacrifice of her father in view of the insult offered to Śiva. As a result of the penance the goddess became the daughter of the Himavat. On the advice of Nārada she performed a penance for Śiva on that mountain. When lord Śiva married her with Svayamvara rites Pārvatī attained happiness. (6-8.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैकस्मिन्महादेवी समये पतिना सह ।
सूपविष्टा महाशैले गौरी देवमभाषत ॥ ९॥

English
Once she was cosily seated with lord Śiva on that great mountain. She spoke to the lord. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेव्युवाच ।
भगवन्परमेशान पञ्चकृत्यविधायक ।
सर्वज्ञ भक्तिसुलभ परमामृतविग्रह ॥ १०॥

दाक्षायणीं तनुं त्यक्त्वा तव निन्दाप्रसङ्गतः ।
आसमद्य महेशान पुत्री हिमवतो गिरेः ॥ ११॥

English
O omniscient lord who hath ordained to himself the five-fold duties, O lord easily accessible through devotion, O lord of nectarine body, I was the daughter of Dakṣa in my former birth. I abandoned my body because of the insult offered to you. I have now become the daughter of the mountain Himavat. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
कृपया परमेशान मन्त्रदीक्षाविधानतः ।
मां विशुद्धात्मतत्त्वस्थां कुरु नित्यं महेश्वर ॥ १२॥

English
O lord, please make me merged in the principle of pure Ātman by duly initiating me in the Mantras. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सम्प्रार्थितो देव्या देवः शीतांशुभूषणः ।
प्रत्युवाच ततो देवीं प्रहृष्टेनान्तरात्मना ॥ १३॥

English
Thus requested by the goddess the moon-crested lord replied to the goddess with a delighted mind. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेव उवाच ।
धन्या त्वं देवदेवशि यदि जातेदृशी मतिः ।
कैलासशिखरं गत्वा करिष्ये त्वां च तादृशीम् ॥ १४॥

English
# Lord Śiva said :—  If your mind is thus diverted, O goddess, you are blessed indeed. I shall initiate you so after going to the summit of Kailāsa. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो हिमवतो गत्वा कैलासं भूधरेश्वरम् ।
जगौ दीक्षाविधानेन प्रणवादीन्मनून् क्रमात् ॥ १५॥

English
Then after leaving Himavat and reaching Kailāsa, the lord of mountains, Śiva, recited mantras Praṇava and others in the proper order after duly initiating her. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
उक्त्वा मन्त्रांश्च तान्देवीं कृत्वा शुद्धात्मनि स्थिताम् ।
सार्धं देव्या महादेवो देवोद्यानं गतोऽभवत् ॥ १६॥

English
Then making the goddess merged in the pure Ātman, lord Śiva went to his garden accompanied by the goddess. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सुमालिनीमुख्यैर्देव्याः प्रियसखीजनैः ।
समाहृतैः प्रफुल्लैस्तैः पुष्पैः कल्पतरूद्भवैः ॥ १७॥

अलङ्कृत्य महादेवीं स्वाङ्कमारोप्य शङ्करः ।
प्रहृष्टवदनस्तस्थौ विलोक्य च तदाननम् ॥ १८॥

English
Śiva bedecked the goddess with the full-blown kalpa tree¹⁰⁴ flowers brought by the dearest friends of the goddess the chief of whom was Sumālinī. He made her sit on his lap. Looking at her face he sat there with his face beaming with delight. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रियकथा जाताः पार्वतीपरमेशयोः ।
हिताय सर्वलोकानां साक्षाच्छ्रुत्यर्थसम्मिताः ॥ १९॥

English
Then ensued the delightful discourse conformable to the Vedas between Pārvatī and lord Śiva. It was meant for the welfare of the world. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा सर्वजगन्माता भर्तुरङ्कं समाश्रिता ।
विलोक्य वदनं भर्तुरिदमाहः तपोधनाः ॥ २०॥

English
O ascetics, then the mother of the universe seated on the lap of her husband looked at the lord's face and said. (20.)

---

English
# The goddess said :—  O lord, you have discoursed on the Mantras including the Praṇava. There at the outset I wish to know the meaning of the Praṇava. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं प्रणव उत्पन्नः कथं प्रणव उच्यते ।
मात्राः कति समाख्याताः कथं वेदादिरुच्यते ॥ २२॥

English
How did Praṇava originate ? How is Praṇava mentioned ? How many Mātrās have been mentioned ? How is it that it is called the initial mantra of the Vedas ? (22.)

---

Sanskrit
देवताः कति च प्रोक्ताः कथं देवादिभावना ।
क्रियाः कतिविधाः प्रोक्ता व्याप्यव्यापकता कथम् ॥ २३॥

English
How many deities are mentioned ? What is the concept of the Vedas thereto ? What types of rites are mentioned ? What is their cause and what is their result ? (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माणि पञ्च मन्त्रेऽस्मिन्कथं तिष्ठन्त्यनुक्रमात् ।
कलाः कति समाख्याताः प्रपञ्चात्मकता कथम् ॥ २४॥

English
How do the five Brahmans stay in this mantra in their proper order ? How many Kalās are enumerated ? How is it the Ātman of the universe ? (24.)

---

Sanskrit
वाच्यवाचकसम्बन्धस्थानानि च कथं शिव ।
कोऽत्राधिकारी विज्ञेयो विषयः क उदाहृतः ॥ २५॥

English
O Śiva, what is the link between the syllables and their meaning ? What are the places of their articulation ? Who is authorised in this ? What is the theme ? (25.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्बन्धः कोऽत्र विज्ञेयः किं प्रयोजनमुच्यते ।
उपासकस्तु किंरूपः किं वा स्थानमुपासनम् ॥ २६॥

English
What is the link between the knower and the known and what is the purpose? What shall be the nature of the practising devotee? What shall be the place for the practice of meditation? (26.)

---

Sanskrit
उपास्यं वस्तु किंरूपं किं वा फलमुपासितुः ।
अनुष्ठानविधिः कोवा पूजास्थानं च किं प्रभो ॥ २७॥

English
Of what form is the object of devotion? What is the benefit derived from this? What is the procedure? What is the place of worship? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजायां मण्डलं किं वा किं वा ऋष्यादिकं हर ।
न्यासजापविधिः को वा को वा पूजाविधिक्रमः ॥ २८॥

English
What is the mystic diagram of the worship? O Siva, what are the sages and the rules regarding Nyasa, Japa etc. What is the order of procedure in this worship? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वं महेशान समाचक्ष्व विशेषतः ।
श्रोतुमिच्छामि तत्त्वेन यद्यस्ति मयि ते कृपा ॥ २९॥

English
O lord Siva, please mention these all particularly, if you have mercy on me. I wish to hear everything factually. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
इति देव्या समापृष्टो भगवानिन्दुभूषणः ।
सम्प्रशस्य महेशानीं वक्तुं समुपचक्रमे ॥ ३०॥

English
Thus implored, the moon-crested lord praised the goddess and began to explain. (30.)
