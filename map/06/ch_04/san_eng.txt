### **CHAPTER FOUR - *(The daily conduct of a Sannyāsin)***

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि संन्यासाह्निककर्म च ।
तव स्नेहान्महादेवि सम्प्रदायानुरोधतः ॥ १॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—*  O goddess, after this I shall mention the daily routine during the Sannyāsa according to the convention. It is out of my affection for you that I am narrating the same. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मे मुहूर्त्त उत्थाय शिरसि श्वेतपङ्कजे ।
सहस्रारे समासीनं गुरुं सञ्चिन्तयेद्यतिः ॥ २॥

English
The ascetic shall get up in the Brāhma Muhūrta and think about the preceptor Śiva seated in the thousand-petalled lotus on the head. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशं द्विनेत्रं वरदाभये ।
दधानं शिवसद्भावमेवात्मनि मनोहरम् ॥ ३॥

English
The preceptor resembling pure crystal, with two eyes and holding the mystic gestures of boon and protection in the hand is conceived as Śiva of beautiful form in the soul. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भावोपनीतैः सम्पूज्य गन्धादिभिरनुक्रमात् ।
बद्धाञ्जलिपुटो भूत्वा नमस्कुर्याद् गुरुं ततः ॥ ४॥

English
He shall bow to the preceptor with palms joined in reverence and worship him with scents, fragrant flowers etc. created by his imagination. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रातःप्रभृति सायान्ते सायादि प्रातरन्ततः ।
यत्करोमि महादेव तदस्तु तव पूजनम् ॥ ५॥

English
He shall pray thus—“O lord, let whatever I do from morning till sunset and from sunset till daybreak be your worship.” (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रतिविज्ञाप्य गुरवे लब्धानुज्ञस्ततो गुरोः ।
निरुद्धप्राण आसीनो विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ॥ ६॥

English
After intimating to the preceptor thus and securing the permission he shall retain the breath and sit down, with his mind and the sense-organs under full control. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
मूलादिब्रह्मरन्ध्रान्तं षट्चक्रं परिचिन्तयेत् ।
विद्युत्कोटिसमप्रख्यं सर्वतेजोमयं परम् ॥ ७॥

तन्मध्ये चिन्तयेन्मां च सच्चिदानन्दविग्रहम् ।
निर्गुणं परमं ब्रह्म सदाशिवमनामयम् ॥ ८॥

English
He shall then meditate on the six-fold wheel$^{109}$ from the root to the Brahma Randhra. In the middle he shall think of me, the Nirguṇa Sadāśiva, free from ailment, the great Brahman, with the lustre of crores of lightning, identical with splendour, with the body of existence, knowledge and bliss. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽहमस्मीति मतिमान्मदैक्यमनुभूय च ।
बहिर्निर्गत्य च ततो दूरं गच्छेद्यथासुखम् ॥ ९॥

English
He shall realize identity with me in the form "I am he". The intelligent ascetic then shall go out far according to convenience. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
वस्त्रेणाच्छाद्य मतिमाञ्छिरो नासिकया सह ।
विशोध्य देहं विधिवत्तृणमाधाय भूतले ॥ १०॥

English
The intelligent devotee shall cover the head along with the nose with a piece of cloth. He shall spread some grass on the ground and evacuate his bowels there duly. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
गृहीतशिश्न उत्थाय ततो गच्छेज्जलाशयम् ।
उद्धृत्य वार्यथान्यायं शौचं कुर्यादतन्द्रितः ॥ ११॥

English
He shall stand up holding the penis and go to the water-pond. He shall take out water and use it for cleansing carefully. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
हस्तौ पादौ च संशोध्य द्विराचम्योमिति स्मरन् ।
उत्तराभिमुखो मौनी दन्तधावनमाचरेत् ॥ १२॥

तृणपर्णैः सदा कुर्यादमामेकादशीं विना ।
अपां द्वादशगण्डूषैर्मुखं संशोधयेत्ततः ॥ १३॥

English
He shall wash hands, feet and perform Ācamana twice remembering Om. He shall face the north and clean his teeth always with leaves or grass except on the eleventh and the New-moon day. He shall rinse the mouth by gargling twelve times. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विराचम्य मृदा तोयैः कटिशौचं विधाय च ।
अरुणोदयकाले तु स्नानं कुर्यान्मृदा सह ॥ १४॥

English
After performing Ācamana twice he shall clean the hips with water and clay. At the time of dawn he shall take his bath using clay. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुं संस्मृत्य मां चैव स्नानसन्ध्याद्यमाचरेत् ।
विस्तारभयतो नोक्तमत्र द्रष्टव्यमन्यतः ॥ १५॥

English
The bath and the Sandhyā prayers shall be performed with thoughts on the preceptor and me. The procedure of bath is not detailed here for fear of prolixity. That shall be seen elsewhere. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
आबध्य शङ्खमुद्रां च प्रणवेनाभिषेचयेत् ।
शिरसि द्वादशावृत्त्या तदर्धं वा तदर्धकम् ॥ १६॥

English
Joining the palms so as to from the Śaṅkhamudrā water shall be poured over the head repeating the Praṇava, twelve times, six times or three times. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तीरमागत्य कौपीनं प्रक्षाल्याचम्य च द्विधा ।
प्रोक्षयेत्प्रणवेनैव वस्त्रमङ्गोपमार्जनम् ॥ १७॥

English
He shall go to the bank and wash the loin-cloth. He shall perform the Ācamana twice. Repeating the Praṇava he shall sprinkle water over the towel and wipe off the body. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मुखं प्रथमतो मृज्य शिर आरभ्य सर्वतः ।
तेनैव मार्जयेद्देहं स्थित्वा च गुरुसन्निधौ ॥ १८॥

English
He shall wipe off his face first and then the head and other parts of the body standing by the side of the preceptor. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
आबध्याद् वामतः शुद्धं कौपीनं च सडोरकम् ।
ततः सन्धारयेद्भस्म तद्विधिः प्रोच्यतेऽद्रिजे ॥ १९॥

English
A pure loin-cloth shall be tied with its string with the knot on the left side. Then he shall smear his body with the ashes. O daughter of the mountain, now I tell you the procedure thereof. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विराचम्य समादाय भस्म सद्यादिमन्त्रतः ।
अग्निरित्यादिभिर्मन्त्रैरभिमन्त्र्य स्पृशेत्तनुम् ॥ २०॥

English
After performing the Ācamana twice he shall take the ashes repeating the Sadyādi mantra¹¹⁰. Then repeating the mantras “Agniriti”¹¹¹ he shall touch the body. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
आपोवेत्यभिमन्त्र्याथ जलं तेनैव सेचयेत् ।
ओमापोज्योतिरित्युक्त्वा मानस्तोकेति मन्त्रतः ॥ २१॥

सम्मर्द्य कवलद्वन्द्वं कुर्यादेकं तु पञ्चधा ।
शिरोवदनहृद्गुह्यपादेषु परमेश्वरि ॥ २२॥

English
Repeating the mantra “Āpo vai—¹¹²” he shall mix the ashes with water. He shall make two balls of the pasted ashes with the mantras “Om Āpo Jyoti”¹¹³ and “Mā Nastoke”¹¹⁴—He shall divide one of the balls into five, O goddess, and apply each respectively to the head, face, chest, private parts and the feet. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशानादि समारभ्य सद्यान्तं पञ्चभिः क्रमात् ।
उद्धूल्य कवलं पश्चात्प्रणवेनाभिषेचयेत् ॥ २३॥

सर्वाङ्गं च ततो हस्तौ प्रक्षाल्यान्यत्समाहरेत्
सम्मर्द्य पूर्ववत्तत्तु त्रिपुण्ड्रांस्तेन धारयेत् ॥ २४॥

English
He shall repeat the mantra beginning with “Īśāna¹¹⁵—” and ending with “Sadya.” After applying the ashes he shall repeat Om touching all the parts of the body. He shall wash his hands, feet and take the other ball. Adoring as before he shall apply three parallel lines on the forehead repeating the mantra ‘Tryāyuṣa’¹¹⁶ and the ‘Tryambaka’.¹¹⁷ He shall apply the same on the chest with the Praṇava and on the shoulders with “Om Namaḥ Śivāya.” (23-25.)

---

Sanskrit
नाभौ बाह्वोः सन्धिषु च पृष्ठे चैव यथाक्रमम् ।
प्रक्षाल्य हस्तौ च ततो द्विराचम्य यथाविधि ॥ २६॥

English
Uttering Pañcīkaraṇa mantra, the scholarly ascetic shall think of his preceptor. In the manner as explained hereafter he shall perform six Prāṇāyāmas. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चीकरणमुच्चार्य भावयेत्स्वगुरुं बुधः ।
वक्ष्यमाणप्रकारेण प्राणायामान्षडाचरेत् ॥ २७॥

दक्षहस्तेन सङ्गृह्य जलं वामेन पाणिना ।
समाच्छाद्य द्विषड्वारं प्रणवेनाभिमन्त्रयेत् ॥ २८॥

English
Touching the navel, the joints of the arms and the back in order he shall wash both the hands duly and perform Ācamana twice. He shall take some water in the right hand and cover it with the left hand. Praṇava is then repeated twelve times. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं त्रिवारं सम्प्रोक्ष्य शिरसि त्रिः पिबेत्ततः ।
समाहितेन मनसा ध्यायन्नोङ्कारमीश्वरम् ॥ २९॥

सौरमण्डलमध्यस्थं सर्वतेजोमयं परम् ।
अष्टबाहुं चतुर्वक्त्रमर्द्धनारीकमद्भुतम् ॥ ३०॥

सर्वाश्चर्यगुणोपेतं सर्वालङ्कारशोभितम् ।
एवं ध्यात्वाथ विधिवद्दद्यादर्घ्यत्रयं ततः ॥ ३१॥

English
Sprinkling this water thrice on the head he shall drink water thrice meditating on Oṅkāra—Śiva with pure mind—Śiva who is stationed in the middle of the solar disc, the god consisting of all splendour, possessing eight arms, four faces, the wonderful form half-female, endowed with wonderful qualities and bedecked in ornaments. After meditating thus duly he shall offer three Arghyas. (29-31.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टोत्तरशतं जप्त्वा द्विषड्वारं तु तर्पयेत् ।
पुनराचम्य विधिवत्प्राणायामत्रयं चरेत् ॥ ३२॥

English
After performing the Japa of Gayatri hundred and eight times, he shall offer Tarpaṇa twelve times. After performing the Ācamana he shall perform Prāṇāyāma thrice. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
पूजासदनमागच्छेन्मनसा संस्मरन् शिवम् ।
द्वारमासाद्य प्रक्षाल्य पादौ मौनी द्विराचमेत् ॥ ३३॥

English
Then he shall go to the hall of worship thinking on Siva. After reaching the door he shall wash his feet silently and perform Ācamana twice. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रविशेद्विधिना तत्र दक्षपादपुरःसरम् ।
मण्डपान्तः सुधीस्तत्र मण्डलं रचयेत्क्रमात् ॥ ३४॥

English
He shall enter it duly placing the right foot at first. Inside the Maṇḍapa there, he, the intelligent ascetic, shall draw the mystic diagram in due order. (34.)
