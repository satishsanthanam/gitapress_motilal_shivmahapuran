### **CHAPTER TEN - (Sūta's instruction)**

---

Sanskrit
व्यास उवाच ।
गतेऽथ सूते मुनयः सुविस्मिता
विचिन्त्य चान्योऽन्यमिदं तु विस्मृतम् ।
यद्वामदेवस्य मतं मुनीश्वर
प्रसूचितं तत्खलु कष्टमद्य नः ॥ १॥

English
Vyāsa said :—  When Sūta had left, the wondering sages thought and spoke to one another—"What the great sage said about Vāmadeva's opinion has been forgotten. This is indeed distressing to us. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कदानुभूयान्मुनिवर्यदर्शनं
भवाब्धिदुःखौघहरं परं हि तत् ।
महेश्वराराधनपुण्यतोऽधुना
मुनीश्वरः सत्वरमाविरस्तु नः ॥ २॥

English
When shall we see the excellent sage again? His sight dispels the vast miseries of worldly existence. May the great sage appear again as a result of the merit accruing from the worship of lord Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
इति चिन्तासमाविष्टा मुनयो मुनिपुङ्गवम् ।
व्यासं सम्पूज्य हृत्पद्मे तस्थुस्तद्दर्शनोत्सुकाः ॥ ३॥

English
The sages who were worried with these thoughts worshipped Sūta in the lotus of their heart and stayed there being eager to see him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
संवत्सरान्ते स पुनः काशीं प्राप महामुनिः ।
शिवभक्तिरतो ज्ञानी पुराणार्थप्रकाशकः ॥ ४॥

English
At the end of a year, the sage, engaged in devotional activities of Śiva, the wise propounder of the meaning to the Purāṇas, came to Kāśi again. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा सूतमायान्तं मुनयो हृष्टचेतसः ।
अभ्युत्थानासनार्घ्यादिपूजया समपूजयन् ॥ ५॥

English
On seeing Sūta coming, the sages delighted in their minds, worshipped him duly rising up and offering seat and Arghya. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि तान्मुनिशार्दूलानभिनन्द्य स्मितोदरम् ।
प्रीत्या स्नात्वा जाह्नवीये जले परमपावने ॥ ६॥

English
He too congratulated the sages with a smiling face. With pleasure he took the bath in the holy waters of the Gaṅgā. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषीन्सन्तर्प्य च सुरान् पितॄंश्च तिलतण्डुलैः ।
तीरमागत्य सम्प्रोक्ष्य वाससी परिधाय च ॥ ७॥

English
He propitiated the sages, the gods and the Pīṭṭs with gingelly seeds and rice grains. He came to the bank and put on his dress after sprinkling it with water. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विराचम्य समादाय भस्म सद्यादिमन्त्रतः ।
उद्धूलनादिक्रमतो विधार्याथ मुनीश्वरः ॥ ८॥

रुद्राक्षमालाभरणः कृतनित्यक्रियः सुधी ।
यथोक्ताङ्गेषु विधिना त्रिपुण्ड्रं रचति स्म ह ॥ ९॥

English
He performed the Ācamana twice and took the Bhasma repeating the Sadyādi mantras. Wearing the garland of Rudrākṣa beads he applied the Bhasma to his body and made the Tripuṇḍra marks on the limbs. He then performed his routine rites. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेश्वरमुमाकान्तं ससुतं सगणाधिपम् ।
पूजयामास सद्भक्त्या ह्यस्तौ नत्वा मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ १०॥

English
With great devotion he eulogised, bowed to and worshipped lord Śiva and Umā along with his sons and chief Gaṇas. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
कालभैरवनाथं च सम्पूज्याथ विधानतः ।
प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य पुनस्त्रेधा नत्वा च पञ्चधा ॥ ११॥

पुनः प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य प्रणम्य भुवि दण्डवत् ।
तुष्टाव परया स्तुत्या संस्मरंस्तत्पदाम्बुजम् ॥ १२॥

English
He worshipped Kālabhairava in accordance with the rules after circumambulating him thrice. He prostrated five times and circumambutaled again. Thinking upon his lotus-feet he eulogised him again with the great hymn. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमत्पञ्चाक्षरीं विद्यामष्टोत्तरसहस्रकम् ।
सञ्जप्य पुरतः स्थित्वा क्षमापय्य महेश्वरम् ॥ १३॥

चण्डीशं सम्प्रपूज्याऽथ मुक्तिमण्डपमध्यतः ।
निर्दिष्टमासनं भेजे मुनिभिर्वेदपारगैः ॥ १४॥

English
He performed the Japa of the sacred Pañcākṣarī lore one thousand and eight times. Standing in front of lord Śiva's image he begged for forgiveness. After worshipping him he took his seat in the the middle of Muktimandapa offered by the sages who had mastered the Vedas. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं स्थितेषु सर्वेषु नमस्कृत्य समन्त्रकम् ।
अथ प्राह मुनीन्द्राणां भाववृद्धिकरं वच ॥ १५॥

English
When all had taken their seats after due obeisance with the recital of the mantras he spoke in accents that increased the piety of the sages. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
धन्या यूयं महाप्राज्ञा मुनयः शंसितव्रताः ।
भवदर्थमिह प्राप्तोऽहं तद्वृत्तमिदं श‍ृणु ॥ १६॥

English
Sūta said:—  O intelligent sages, you all who have fulfilled your vows are blessed. I have come for your sake. Please listen in details. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
यदाहमुपदिश्याथ भवतः प्रणवार्थकम् ।
गतस्तीर्थाटनार्थाय तद्वृत्तान्तं ब्रवीमि वः ॥ १७॥

English
After telling you the meaning of the Praṇava I went on a pilgrimage. Now hear the details. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इतो निर्गत्य सम्प्राप्य तीरं दक्षपयोनिधेः ।
स्नात्वा सम्पूज्य विधिवद्देवीं कन्यामयीं शिवाम् ।
पुनरागत्य विप्रेन्द्राः सुवर्णमुखरीतटम् ॥ १८॥

English
O leading brahmans, after starting from here I went to the southern ocean. I took bath therein and worshipped the goddess Śivā, Kanyākumāri. On my return I came to the river Suvarṇamukharī¹³⁵. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकालहस्तिशैलाख्यनगरे परमाद्भुते ।
सुवर्णमुखरीतोये स्नात्वा देवानृषीनपि ॥ १९॥

सन्तर्प्य विधिवद्भक्त्या समुद्रं गिरिशं स्मरन् ।
समर्च्य कालहस्तीशं चन्द्रकान्तसमप्रभम् ॥ २०॥

पश्चिमाभिमुखं पञ्चशिरसं परमाद्भुतम् ।
सकृद्दर्शनमात्रेण सर्वाघक्षयकारणम् ॥ २१॥

सर्वसिद्धिप्रदं भुक्तिमुक्तिदं त्रिगुणेश्वरम् ।
ततश्च परया भक्त्या तस्य दक्षिणगां शिवाम् ॥ २२॥

ज्ञानप्रसूनकलिकां समर्च्य हि जगत्प्रसूम् ।
श्रीमत्पञ्चाक्षरीं विद्यामष्टोत्तरसहस्रकम् ॥ २३॥

जप्त्वा प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य स्तुत्वा नत्वा मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ २४॥

ततः प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य गिरिं प्रत्यहमादरात् ।
आमोदतीव मनसि प्रत्यहं नियमास्थितः ॥ २५॥

English
I reached the wonderful city on the Kālahasti Śaila. I took my bath in the waters of Suvarṇamukharī¹³⁶ and performed Tarpaṇa to the gods and the sages. Remembering Śiva with the mystic signs I worshipped Kālahastīśa with the lustre of the lunar stone, the lord with the face directed to the west, the wonderful lord of five faces, the lord who dispels all sins at his very sight, the lord Triguṇeśvara who bestows Siddhis, worldly pleasures and salvation. Then with great devotion I worshipped Śivā stationed to his right, the goddess who is the bud of the flower of knowledge and the mother of the universe. I performed the Japa of the holy Pañcākṣarī Vidyā one thousand eight times. I circumambulated, eulogised and bowed again. Thus I went round the hill every day with great devotion. I observed all restraints and performed rites every day with rejoicing mind. (19-25.)

---

Sanskrit
अनयन् चतुरो मासानेवं तत्र मुनीश्वराः ।
ज्ञानप्रसूनकलिकामहादेव्याः प्रसादतः ॥ २६॥

English
O great sages, I spent four months there by the favour of the goddess—the bud of the flower of knowledge. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एकदा तु समास्तीर्य चैलाजिनकुशोत्तरम् ।
आसनं परमं तस्मिन् स्थित्वा रुद्धेन्द्रियो मुनिः ॥ २७॥

समाधिमास्थाय सदा परमानन्दचिद्घनः ।
परिपूर्णः शिवोऽस्मीति निर्व्यग्रहृदयोऽभवम् ॥ २८॥

English
Once I spread the cloth, deerskin and the Kuśa grass on the seat. Seated on it silently with restrained mind I entered into the mystic trance. “I am Śiva the solidified knowledge and bliss, the perfect one for ever.” This realisation dawned in my heart and I was free from agitation. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नेव समये मद्गुरुः करुणानिधिः ।
नीलजीमूतसङ्काशो विद्युत्पिङ्गजटाधरः ॥ २९॥

प्रांशुः कमण्डलूदण्डकृष्णाजिनधरः स्वयम् ।
भस्मावदातसर्वाङ्गः सर्वलक्षणलक्षितः ॥ ३०॥

त्रिपुण्ड्रविलसद्भालो रुद्राक्षालङ्कृताकृतिः ।
पद्मपत्रारुणायामविस्तीर्णनयनद्वयः ॥ ३१॥

प्रादुर्भूय हृदम्भोजे तदानीमेव सत्वरम् ।
विमोहितस्तदैवासमेतदद् भुतमास्तिकाः ॥ ३२॥

English
Then my preceptor, the ocean of mercy, resembling the dark cloud, with matted tawny hair resembling the lightning, the tall sage bearing the water-pot and staff and wearing the antelope’s skin, with the body turned white with the ashes having all auspicious marks with the Tripuṇḍra shining on the forehead, decorated with Rudrākṣa garlands and the two lotus-like wide eyes gleaming, appeared in the lotus of my heart. Thereafter I swooned soon, O theists, this was the wonderful incident indeed. (29-32.)

---

Sanskrit
तत उन्मील्य नयने विलापं कृतवानहम् ।
आसीन्ममाश्रुपातश्च गिरिनिर्झरसन्निभः ॥ ३३॥

English
Then I opened my eyes and cried. The tears I shed quashed out like a mountain stream. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नेव समये श्रुता वागशरीरिणी ।
व्योम्नो महाद्भुता विप्रास्तामेव श‍ृणुतादरात् ॥ ३४॥

English
Then a celestial unimbodied voice was heard from the sky. It was wonderful. O brahmins, listen to that devotedly. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सूतपुत्र महाभाग गच्छ वाराणसीं पुरीम् ।
तत्रासन्मुनयः पूर्वमुपदिष्टास्त्वयाधुना ॥ ३५॥

त्वदुपागमकल्याणं काङ्क्षन्ते विवशा भृशम् ।
तिष्ठन्ति ते निराहारा इत्युक्त्वा विरराम सा ॥ ३६॥

English
“O blessed son of Sūta, go to Vārāṇasī; the sages whom you had instructed before, are desirous of the pleasure of your arrival. They are on fast theretoo.” After saying this the voice stopped. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
तत उत्थाय तरसा देवं देवीं च भक्तितः ।
प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य पुनः प्रणम्य भुवि दण्डवत् ॥ ३७॥

द्विषड्वारं गुरोराज्ञां विज्ञाय शिवयोरथ ।
क्षेत्रान्निर्गत्य तरसा चत्वारिंशद्दिनान्तरे ॥ ३८॥

आगतोऽस्मि मुनिश्रेष्ठा अनुगृह्णन्तु मामिह ।
मया किमद्य वक्तव्यं भवन्तस्तद् ब्रुवन्तु मे ॥ ३९॥

English
I got up immediately and circumambulated the lord and the goddess with devotion. I bowed and prostrated for twelve times. Considering it the order of my preceptor and of Śiva and Śivā I set out from the temple. O excellent sages, I have come here in forty days. Please bless me. Please tell me what I shall say now. (37-39.)

Sanskrit

अवोचन्मुनिशार्दूलं व्यासं नत्वा मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ४०॥

English
On hearing the words of Sūta, the sages were delighted in their minds. They bowed to the sage Vyāsa again and again and said. (40.)
