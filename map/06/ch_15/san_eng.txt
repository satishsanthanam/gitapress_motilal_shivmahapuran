### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - (The idol of Śiva for worship)**

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चदशोऽध्यायः । उपासनामूर्त्तिवर्णनम् ।

ईश्वर उवाच ।
ततः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि सृष्टिपद्धतिमुत्तमाम् ।
सदाशिवान्महेशादिचतुष्कस्य वरानने ॥ १॥

English
Ṣubrahmanya said :—  Henceforth I shall explain, O good-faced lady, the mode of creation for the set of four, Maheśa etc. from Sadāśiva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवः समष्टिः स्यादाकाशधिपतिः प्रभुः ।
अस्यैव व्यष्टितापन्नं महेशादिचतुष्टयम् ॥ २॥

English
Sadāśiva the lord of ether is the composite. The set of four, Maheśa etc. is the individualistic form. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवसहस्रांशान्महेशस्य समुद्भवः ।
पुरुषानन्तरूपत्वाद्वायोरधिपतिश्च सः ॥ ३॥

English
Maheśa is a thousandth part of Sadāśiva. It has the form of Puruṣa for its face. It is the lord of wind too. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
मायाशक्तियुतो वामे सकलश्च क्रियाधिकः ।
अस्यैव व्यष्टिरूपं स्यादीश्वरादिचतुष्टयम् ॥ ४॥

English
He is associated with Māyā Śakti on his left. He is Saguṇa having much activity. The set of four Īśvara etc. is his own Vyaṣṭi. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशो विश्वेश्वरः पश्चात्परमेशस्ततः परम् ।
सर्वेश्वर इतीदं तु तिरोधाचक्रमुत्तमम् ॥ ५॥

English
This set of four comprising Īśvara, Viśveśvara, Parameśvara and Sarveśvara is the excellent Tirobhāvacakra. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तिरोभावो द्विधा भिन्न एको रुद्रादिगोचरः ।
अन्यश्च देहभावेन पशुवर्गस्य सन्ततेः ॥ ६॥

English
Tirobhāva is twofold. One concerns Rudra, the other the individual souls in view of their physical bodies. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
भोगानुरञ्जनपरः कर्मसाम्यक्षणावधि ।
कर्मसाम्ये स एकः स्यादनुग्रहमयो विभुः ॥ ७॥

English
The latter remains till equality in activity is achieved. When this is achieved, there remains only the lord of the form of blessing. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र सर्वेश्वरा यास्ते देवताः परिकीर्तिताः ।
परब्रह्मात्मकाः साक्षान्निर्विकल्पा निरामयाः ॥ ८॥

English
There all the Īśvaras who are glorified as the deities, are identical with the great Brahman itself, free from alternatives and ailments. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तिरोभावात्मकं चक्रं भवेच्छान्तिकलामयम् ।
महेश्वराधिष्ठितं च पदमेतदनुत्तमम् ॥ ९॥

English
The Tirobhāva Cakra consists of the Śāntikalā. This excellent region is presided over by Maheśvara. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदेव पदं प्राप्यं महेशपदसेविनाम् ।
माहेश्वराणां सालोक्यक्रमादेव विमुक्तिदम् ॥ १०॥

English
This region is attainable by those who serve Maheśa etc. It yields Sālokya liberation to the devotees of Maheśvara. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरसहस्रांशाद् रुद्रमूर्तिरजायत ।
अघोरवदनाकारस्तेजस्तत्त्वाधिपश्च सः ॥ ११॥

English
The image Rudra is born of a thousandth part of Maheśvara. He has Aghora for his face. He is the lord of the principle of fire. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
गौरीशक्तियुतो वामे सर्वसंहारकृत्प्रभुः ।
अस्यैव व्यष्टिरूपं स्याच्छिवाद्यथ चतुष्टयम् ॥ १२॥

English
He is associated with Gaurīśakti on his left. He is the cause of dissolution of the universe; of him alone is the Vyaṣṭi born—the set of four, Śiva etc. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो हरो मृडभवौ विदितं चक्रमद्भुतम् ।
संहाराख्यं महादिव्यं परमं हि मुनीश्वर ॥ १३॥

English
This set of four consists of Śiva, Hara, Mrda and Bhava. This wheel of dissolution, O sage, is wonderful and divine. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स संहारस्त्रिधा प्रोक्तो बुधैर्नित्यादिभेदतः ।
नित्यो जीवसुषुप्त्याख्यो विधेर्नैमित्तिकः स्मृतः ॥ १४॥

विलयस्तस्य तु महानिति वेदनिदर्शितः ।
जीवानां जन्मदुःखादिशान्तानामुषितात्मनाम् ॥ १५॥

विश्रान्त्यर्थं मुनिश्रेष्ठ कर्मणां पाकहेतवे ।
संहारः कल्पितस्त्रेधा रुद्रेणामिततेजसा ॥ १६॥

English
Dissolution has been classified into three. The first Nitya is the daily slumber of living beings. The second is the conditioned dissolution of Brahmā. The third is the final dissolution. The three have been indicated by the Vedic texts. O excellent sage, the dissolution has been classified into three by the illustrious Rudra for the maturing of the activities of individual souls and for their rest. The souls released from their recurring births are ultimately placed in the great Ātman itself. (14-16.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रस्यैव तु कृत्यानां त्रयमेतदुदाहृतम् ।
संहृतवपि सृष्ट्यादिकृत्यानां पञ्चकं विभोः ॥ १७॥

English
Thus I have mentioned the three activities of Rudra in respect of dissolution. The five activities of the lord in respect of creation shall also be explained to you. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मुने तत्र भवाद्यास्ते देवताः परिकीर्तिताः ।
परब्रह्मस्वरूपाश्च लोकानुग्रहकारकाः ॥ १८॥

English
O sage, the deities Bhava etc. are identical with the great Brahman, being the cause of bliss. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
संहाराख्यमिदं चक्रं विद्यारूपकलामयम् ।
अधिष्ठितं च रुद्रेण पदमेतन्निरामयम् ॥ १९॥

English
This wheel of dissolution has Vidyārūpa Kalā. This healthy region is presided over by Rudra himself. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदेव पदं प्राप्यं रुद्राराधनकाङ्क्षिणाम् ।
रुद्राणां तद्धि सालोक्यक्रमात्सायुज्यदं मुने ॥ २०॥

English
This region is attainable by those who desire the propitiation of Rudras. O sage, it yields the Sāyujya of Rudras gradually through Sālokya. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
रुद्रमूर्तेः सहस्रांशाद्विष्णोश्चैवाभवज्जनिः ।
स वामदेवचक्रात्मा वारितत्त्वैकनायकः ॥ २१॥

English
Viṣṇu was born from a thousandth part of Rudra. In the form of Vāmadeva wheel he is the presiding deity of the principle of water. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
रमाशक्तियुतो वामे सर्व्वरक्षाकरो महान् ।
चतुर्भुजोऽरविन्दाक्षः श्यामः शङ्खादिचिह्नभृत् ॥ २२॥

English
He is associated with Lakṣmī Śakti on the left. He is the protector of all. He has four arms. He has eyes as splendid as the lotus. He is dark-complexioned and bears the characteristics of Śaṅkha etc. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्यैव वासुदेवादिचतुष्कं व्यष्टितां गतम् ।
उपासनरतानां वै वैष्णवानां विमुक्तिदम् ॥ २३॥

English
Of him alone is the set of four Vāsudevas in the Vyaṣṭi form. It bestows liberation on the devotees of Viṣṇu engaged in worship through meditation. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
वासुदेवोऽनिरुद्धश्च ततः सङ्कर्षणः परः ।
प्रद्युम्नश्चेति विख्यातं स्थितिचक्रमनुत्तमम् ॥ २४॥

English
This set of four consists of Vāsudeva, Aniruddha, Saṅkarṣaṇa and Pradyumna. This excellent wheel of sustenance is well known. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थितिः सृष्टस्य जगतस्तत्कर्त्रा सह पालनम् ।
आरब्धकर्मभोगान्तं जीवानां फलभोगिनाम् ॥ २५॥

English
Sustenance means the protection of the existing universe along with its creator until the completion of the pleasures of the souls, the reapers of the fruit. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोरेवेदमाख्यातं कृत्यं रक्षाविधायिनः ।
स्थितावपि तु सृष्ट्यादिकृत्यानां पञ्चकं विभोः ॥ २६॥

English
Protection is the activity of Viṣṇu. In the sustenance also the lord has five activities, viz; creation etc. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र प्रद्युम्नमुख्यास्ते देवताः परिकीर्तिताः ।
निर्विकल्पा निरातङ्का मुक्तानन्दकराः सदा ॥ २७॥

English
The deities of whom Pradyumna is the chief are absolute and without distress. They cause bliss to liberated souls. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थितिचक्रमिदं ब्रह्मन् प्रतिष्ठारूपमुत्तमम् ।
जनार्दनाधिष्ठितं च परमं पदमुच्यते ॥ २८॥

English
O Brahmin, this wheel of sustenance which is permanent is presided over by Viṣṇu. It is the highest abode. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एवदेव पदं प्राप्यं विष्णुपादाब्जसेविनाम् ।
वैष्णवानां चक्रमिदं सालोक्यादिपदप्रदम् ॥ २९॥

English
This region is attainable by those who serve the lotus-feet of Viṣṇu. This wheel yields Sālokya etc. to the devotees of Viṣṇu. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोरेव सहस्रांशात्सम्बभूव पितामहः ।
सद्योजातमुखात्मा यः पृथिवीतत्त्वनायकः ॥ ३०॥

English
Brahmā was born of a thousandth part of Viṣṇu. He has the face of Sadyojāta and is the presiding deity of the principle of earth. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वाग्देवीसहितो वामे सृष्टिकर्त्ता जगत्प्रभुः ।
चतुर्मुखो रक्तवर्णो रजोरूपस्वरूपवान् ॥ ३१॥

English
He is accompanied by the goddess of speech on his left. He is the creator and lord of the universe. He has four faces. He is red in complexion and his form is possessed of Rajas attribute. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हिण्यगर्भाद्यस्यैव व्यष्टिरूपं चतुष्टयम् ।
हिरण्यगर्भोऽथ विराट् पुरुषः काल एव च ॥ ३२॥

English
Hiranyagarbha, Virat, Puruṣa and Kāla, the four constitute his personal form. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिचक्रमिदं ब्रह्म पुत्रादिऋषिसेवितम् ।
सर्वकामार्थदं ब्रह्मन्परिवारसुखप्रदम् ॥ ३३॥

English
O Brahmin, this wheel of creation is the cause of Brahma's sons and other sages. It bestows desires and happiness to the devotee. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्टिस्तु संहृतस्यास्य जीवस्य प्रकृतौ बहिः ।
आनीय कर्मभोगार्थं साधनाङ्गफलैस्सह ॥ ३४॥

संयोजनमितीदं तु कृत्यं पैतामहं विदुः ।
जगत्सृष्टिक्रियाविज्ञा यावद् व्यूहं सुखावहम् ॥ ३५॥

English
Those who have the knowledge of creation know this to be the function of Brahman. Creation is the process of re-unification of the soul with the body previously annihilated—the body which along with the means, ancillaries and results comes out of Prakrti for gradual enjoyment. It is pleasing as long as it holds good. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्सृष्टावपि मुने कृत्यानां च पञ्चकं विभोः ।
अस्ति कालोदयस्तत्र देवताः परिकीर्तिताः ॥ ३६॥

English
O sage, in the creation of the universe there are five functions of the lord. The present time etc. are the deities there. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्तिरूपमाख्यातं सृष्टिचक्रमिदं बुधैः ।
पितामहाधिष्ठितं च पदमेतद्धि शोभनम् ॥ ३७॥

English
This wheel of creation is in the form of turning round. This charming region is presided over by Brahman. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदेव प्रदं प्राप्यं ब्रह्मार्पितधियां नृणाम् ।
पैतामहानामेतद्धि सालोक्यादिविमुक्तिदम् ॥ ३८॥

English
This region is attainable by those who have dedicated their minds to Brahma. This indeed bestows Salokya and other liberations on the devotees of Brahma. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मिन्नपि चतुष्के तु चक्राणां प्रणवो भवेत् ।
महेशादिक्रमादेव गौण्या वृत्त्या स वाचकः ॥ ३९॥

English
Even in regard to the set of four wheels the Pranava indicates Mahefa etc. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं खलु जगच्चक्रं श्रुतिविश्रुतवैभवम् ।
पञ्चारं चक्रमिति ह स्तौति श्रुतिरिदं मुने ॥ ४०॥

English
O sage, this wheel of universe is glorified by the Srutis. The Vedas eulogise it as the wheel with five spokes. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
एकमेव जगच्चक्रं शम्भोः शक्तिविजृम्भितम् ।
सृष्ट्यादिपञ्चावयवं पञ्चारमिति कथ्यते ॥ ४१॥

English
Although the wheel of the universe with its five constituents: creation etc. has five spokes, it develops and increases by Siva's energy. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
अलातचक्रभ्रमिवदविच्छिन्नलयोदयम् ।
परितो वर्तते यस्मात्तस्माच्चक्रमितीरितम् ॥ ४२॥

English
It is called a "wheel" because it moves round and round with incessant dissolution and recreation like the whirling fire-brand. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सृष्ट्यादिपृथुसृष्टित्वात्पृथुत्वेनोपदृश्यते ।
हिरण्मयस्य देवस्य शम्भोरमिततेजसः ॥ ४३॥

शक्तिकार्यमिदं चक्रं हिरण्यज्योतिराश्रितम् ।
सलिलेनावृतमिदं सलिलं वह्निनावृतम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Because of the immensity of creation it is called Pṛthu, the resultant of the Śakṭi of lord Śiva of immense splendour and of golden features. The wheel is dependent on golden splendour and is surrounded by water encompassed by fire. (43-44.)

---

Sanskrit
आवृतो वायुना वह्निराकाशेनावृतं महत् ।
भूतादिना तथाकाशो भूतादिर्महतावृतः ॥ ४५॥

English
Fire is surrounded by wind; wind by ether; ether by the primordial element and that by intellect. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
अव्यक्तेनावृतस्तद्वन्महानित्येवमास्तिकैः ।
ब्रह्माण्डमिति सम्प्रोक्तमाचार्यैर्मुनिसत्तम ॥ ४६॥

English
Intellect is encompassed by the unmanifest. O sage, the universe is thus explained by the learned preceptors. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
उक्तानि सप्तावरणान्यस्य विश्वस्य गुप्तये ।
चक्राद्दशगुणाधिक्यं सलिलस्य विधीयते ॥ ४७॥

English
Such are the seven sheaths which protect the universe. The expanse of water is ten times more massive than the wheel. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
उपर्युपरि चान्योऽन्यमेवं दशगुणाधिकम् ।
ब्रह्माण्डमिति विज्ञेयं तद् विजैर्मुनिनायक ॥ ४८॥

English
The world above is ten times more than the one below. O leading sage, the universe shall be known thus by those who desire to know that. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
इममर्थमुरीकृत्य चक्रसामीप्यवर्त्तनात् ।
सलिलस्य च तन्मध्ये इति प्राह श्रुतिः स्वयम् ॥ ४९॥

English
It is by accepting this sense that the Śruti says "In the middle of water". Of course the earth rotates as the wheel. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहतिरोभावसंहृतिस्थितिसृष्टिभिः ।
करोत्यविरतं लीलामेकः शक्तियुतः शिवः ॥ ५०॥

English
That Śiva alone accompanied by Śakti performs incessant sports through blessing, disappearance, dissolution, sustenance and creation. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुनेह किमुक्तेन मुने सारं वदामि ते ।
शिव एवेदमखिलं शक्तिमानिति निश्चितम् ॥ ५१॥

English
O sage, of what avail is much talk? I shall tell you the essence. Śiva endowed with Śakti is all this. It is definite conclusion. (51.)

---
