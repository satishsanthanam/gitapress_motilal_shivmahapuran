### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - (Śiva's principle)**

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
श्रुत्वोपदिष्टं गुरुणा वेदार्थं मुनिपुङ्गवः ।
परमात्मनि सन्दिग्धं परिपप्रच्छ सादरम् ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said:—  On hearing this truthful purport enunciated, by the preceptor the leading sage asked him about his doubt concerning the great Ātman. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वामदेव उवाच ।
ज्ञानशक्तिधर स्वामिन्परमानन्दविग्रह ।
प्रणवार्थामृतं पीतं श्रीमुखाब्जात्परिस्रुतम् ॥ २॥

दृढप्रज्ञश्च जातोऽस्मि सन्देहो विगतो मम ।
किञ्चिदन्यन्महासेन पृच्छामि त्वां श‍ृणु प्रभो ॥ ३॥

English
Vāmadeva said:—  O lord endowed with the power of knowledge and of the form of great bliss, I have heard the sweet meaning of Praṇava from your glorious lotus-like mouth and nourished intellect. My doubts have gone. O lord, Mahā-sena, I ask you something else. Please listen. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
सदाशिवादिकीटान्तरूपस्य जगतः स्थितिः ।
स्त्रीपुंरूपेण सर्वत्र दृश्यते न हि संशयः ॥ ४॥

English
Surely this universe from Sadāśiva to a worm is reflected in male and female forms everywhere. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं रूपस्य जगतः कारणं यत्सनातनम् ।
स्त्रीरूपं तत्किमाहोस्वित्पुरुषो वा नपुंसकम् ॥ ५॥

English
Is the eternal cause of the universe in the form of a woman, or of a man or a eunuch? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
उत मिश्रं किमन्यद्वा न जातस्तत्र निर्णयः ।
बहुधा विवदन्तीह विद्वांसः शास्त्रमोहिताः ॥ ६॥

English
Or is it a mixture of both or something else? There has been no decision so far. Deluded by sacred scriptures scholars argue on this point in various ways. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
जगत्सृष्टिविधायिन्यः श्रुतयो जगता सह ।
विष्णुब्रह्मादयो देवाः सिद्धाश्च न विदन्ति हि ॥ ७॥

English
The Vedas expatiating on the creation of the universe itself, Viṣṇu, Brahmā and other gods and the Siddhas do not know it. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यथैक्यभावं गच्छेयुरेतदन्यच्च वेदय ।
जानामीति करोमीति व्यवहारः प्रदृश्यते ॥ ८॥

स हि सर्वात्मसंसिद्धो विवादो नात्र कस्यचित् ।
सर्वदेहेन्द्रियमनोबुद्ध्यहङ्कारसम्भवः ॥ ९॥

English
Please explain so that the conflicting views may be resolved into one truthful interpretation. We say—“I know,” “I do”. This is accepted by all. There is no dispute about this. But I have some doubts. “Is there a form of the Ātman or is the Ātman identical with the body, sense-organs, mind, intellect and ego? The two are the points of dispute among many. (8-10.)

---

Sanskrit
आहोस्विदात्मनो रूपं महानत्रापि संशयः ।
द्वयमेतद्धि सर्वेषां विवादास्पदमद्भुतम् ॥ ६।१६।१०॥

उत्पाट्याज्ञानसम्भूतं संशयाख्यं विषद्रुमम् ।
शिवाद्वैतमहाकल्पवृक्षभूमिर्यथा भवेत् ॥ ११॥

चित्तं मम यथा देव बोध्योऽस्मि कृपया तव ।
कृपातस्तव देवेश दृढज्ञानी भवाम्यहम् ॥ १२॥

English
O lord, uproot this poisonous tree of doubt arising from ignorance. I shall be enlightened by you so that my mind may become the fertile ground for the growth of monistic knowledge of the Śaivite cult. O lord of gods, by your benign favour I shall become wise. (11-12.)

---

English
*Sūta said:—*  On hearing the enquiry of the sage full of Vedāntic topics, the lord smiled a little and unfolded the secret. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
सुब्रह्मण्य उवाच ।
एतदेव मुने गुह्यं शिवेन परिभाषितम् ।
अम्बायाः श‍ृण्वतो देव्या वामदेव ममापि हि ॥ १४॥

English
*Subrahmanya said:—*  O sage, this very secret was narrated by Śiva while goddess Ambā and I listened. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्याः स्तन्यं तदा पीत्वा सन्तृप्तोऽस्मि मुहुर्मुहुः ।
श्रुतवान्निश्चलं तद्वै निश्चितं मे विचारितम् ॥ १५॥

English
Then I was fully satiated with the drink of her breast-milk. I listened to it and I came to a decisive thought. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्ते वदामि दयया वामदेव महामुने ।
महद्गुह्यं च परमं सुत त्वं श‍ृणु साम्प्रतम् ॥ १६॥

English
O sage, I shall sympathetically explain the same to you. O son, it is the greatest secret. Please listen to it now. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मास्ति तत्त्वादारभ्य शास्त्रवादः सुविस्तरः ।
यथाविवेकं श्रोतव्यो ज्ञानिना ज्ञानदो मुने ॥ १७॥

English
The arguments in the philosophical text books are extensive beginning with those on Karmans and the principle of existence. But O sage, those arguments shall be listened to with discrimination by a wise person. Then it may yield wisdom. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयोपदिष्टा ये शिष्यास्तत्र को वा भवत्समः ।
कपिलादिषु शास्त्रेषु भ्रमन्त्यद्यापि तेऽधमाः ॥ १८॥

English
You have taught many disciples. But among them who is like you? Those base persons are rotting in delusion even now, wrangling in the philosophical texts propounded by Kapila and others. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ते शप्ता मुनिभिः षड्भिः शिवनिन्दापराः पुरा ।
न श्रोतव्या हि तद्वार्त्ता तेऽन्यथावादिनो यतः ॥ १९॥

English
They indulged in insulting Śiva and hence were cursed by the seven sages. Their views should not be heard because they explain false notions. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुमानप्रयोगस्याप्यवकाशोऽत्रन विद्यते ।
पञ्चावयवयुक्तस्य स तु धूमस्य दर्शनात् ॥ ६।१६।२०॥

पर्वतस्याग्निमद्भावं वदन्त्यत्रापि सुव्रत ।
प्रत्यक्षस्य प्रपञ्चस्य दर्शनालम्बनं त्वतः ॥ २१॥

ज्ञातव्यः परमेशानः परमात्मा न संशयः ।
स्त्रीपुंरूपमयं विश्वं प्रत्यक्षेणैव दृश्यते ॥ २२॥

English
There is no scope for inference that consists of the five syllogisms. On seeing smoke they say that there is fire too. But here, O sage, the world is the object of perception. Its cause and support, the great Ātman Śiva must be known directly. The universe in the form of male and female is directly perceived. (20-22.)

---

Sanskrit
षट्कोशरूपः पिण्डो हि तत्र चाद्यत्रयं भवेत् ।
मात्रंशजं पुनश्चान्यत्पित्रंशजमिति श्रुतिः ॥ २३॥

एवं सर्वशरीरेषु स्त्रीपुम्भावविदो जनाः ।
परमात्मन्यपि मुने स्त्रीपुम्भावं विदुर्बुधाः ॥ २४॥

English
The physical body consists of six sheaths. The first three are born of the parts of the mother and the other three of the parts of the father. So says the Śruti. (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
सच्चिदानदरूपत्वं वदति ब्रह्मणः श्रुतिः ।
असन्निवर्तकः शब्दः सदात्मेति निगद्यते ॥ २५॥

English
The Śruti speaks of the Brahman as having the form of existence, knowledge and bliss. The word 'existence' is used in order to exclude the 'non-existent'. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
निवर्तनं जडत्वस्य चिच्छब्देन विधीयते ।
त्रिलिङ्गवर्ती सच्छब्दः पुरुषोऽत्र विधीयताम् ॥ २६॥

प्रकाशवाची स भवेत्सत्प्रकाश इति स्फुटम् ।
ज्ञानशब्दस्य पर्यायश्चिच्छब्दः स्त्रीत्वमागतः ॥ २७॥

English
The exclusion of the insentient is effected by the word sentient. Of course the word Cit is used in all the three genders but it signifies here a male being. The word Cit in the feminine gender is a synonym of the word knowledge. If it is taken to mean 'Light', it is clear that it illuminates. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकाशश्चिच्च मिथुनं जगत्कारणतां गतम् ।
सच्चिदात्मन्यपि तथा जगत्कारणतां गते ॥ २८॥

English
The pair Light and Cit is the cause of the universe. Similarly the Sat and the Cit are the causes of the universe. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एकत्रैव शिवः शक्तिरिति भावो विधीयते ।
तैलवर्त्त्यादिमालिन्यात्प्रकाशस्यापि वर्तते ॥ २९॥

English
In the self-same individual the feeling of Śiva and Śakti exists. If there is dirt in the oil or the wick there may be dirt in the light too. (29.)

---

English
Thus dirt and inauspiciousness is seen in the funeral fire also. Śivatva is due to illusion there. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
मालिन्यमशिवत्वं च चिताग्न्यादिषु दृश्यते ।
एवं निवर्तकत्वेन शिवत्वं श्रुतिचोदितम् ॥ ६।१६।३०॥

जीवाश्रितायाश्चिच्छक्तेर्दौर्बल्यं विद्यते सदा ।

English
There is weakness in the power of Cit in the individual soul. It is to exclude it that the conception of Śakti is ever present. (31.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O sage, people say in the world: he is strong, he is powerful and in the Vedas too there is a similar expression. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
Hence, O sage, sinless ascetics aiming at bliss fix their minds in Śiva and attain untainted auspiciousness. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The word Brahman in the Upaniṣads signifies Ātman collectively. It can be traced to the root Bṛh to increase or grow. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Growing in size and massiveness is ever present in Śiva constituting the five Brahmans. The word Brahman denotes the universe. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O Vāmadeva, due to my affection for you I shall explain the origin of the Praṇava when the word Haṃsaḥ is reverted. Listen attentively. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
It shall be known as the great Mantra by the expert sages. This great Mantra is subtle. I shall tell you its analysis. (39.)

---

English
The great Mantra is formed with स and the sixteenth vowel (:)=स: and अ+इ with the fifteenth vowel (m)=अइस्=(सोऽइस्) (40.)

---

Sanskrit
आद्ये त्रिपञ्चरूपे च स्वरे षोडशके त्रिषु ।
महामन्त्रो भवेदादौ ससकारो भवेद्यदा ॥ ६।१६।४०॥

शक्त्यात्मको महामन्त्रवाच्यः स्यादिति निर्णयः ॥ ४१॥

English
It is the inverted form of Haṃsa. The meaning of the letter 'Sa' is Śiva. The conclusion is, the Śakti form is expressed by the great Mantra. (41.)

---

Sanskrit

English
At the instruction of the preceptor, the great mantra is Śiva in the form of Śakti. Hence the great mantra is the individual soul too. The individual soul is in the form of Śakti and is a part of Śiva. Due to the identity with Śiva it attains equality with Śiva. In the passage (of the Vedas) "Prajñānam Brahma" the meaning of "Prajñānam" is seen. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Undoubtedly the word "Prajñānam" is a synonym of consciousness. O sage, there is the aphorism. (44.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English
When these reaching the centre of the mind become the object of perception on the part of sense-organs the individual soul enters these, then knows and acts. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
