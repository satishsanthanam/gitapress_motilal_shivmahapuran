### **CHAPTER TWENTY - (Rules for hair-cutting and ablution)**

---

Sanskrit
विंशोऽध्यायः । क्षौरस्नानविधिवर्णनम् ।

सुब्रह्मण्य उवाच ।
क्षौरस्नानविधिं वक्ष्ये वामदेव महामुने ।
यस्य सद्यो विधानेन शुद्धिः स्याद्यतिनः परा ॥ १॥

English
Subrahmanya said:-  O sage Vāmadeva I shall now explain the rules of hair-cutting and ablution. These if performed immediately are conducive to great purity of the ascetic. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
योगपट्टप्रकारस्य विधिं प्राप्य मुनीश्वर ।
स शिष्यः स्याद् व्रती पूर्णः क्षौरकर्मोद्यतो भवेत् ॥ २॥

English
O sage, after the consecration as a disciple he shall observe the other rites and get ready for the tonsorial ceremony. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
गुरुं नत्वा विशेषेण लब्धानुज्ञस्ततो गुरोः ।
शिरः सङ्क्षाल्य चाचम्य सवासाः क्षौरमाचरेत् ॥ ३॥

English
He shall bow to the preceptor and secure permission from him. After washing the head and performing the Ācamana he shall have the tonsure while putting on cloth. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षालयेद्वसनं पश्चान्मृदम्भोभिः क्षुरादिकम् ।
तद्धस्तौ च मृदालिप्य क्षालयेति मृदं ददेत् ॥ ४॥

English
After that he shall wash the cloth, the razor etc. with water and clay. The barber also shall be given clay and asked to wash his hands. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थापितं प्रोक्षितं तोयैः शिवं शिवमितीरयन् ।
स्वनेत्रे पिहिते चैवानामाङ्गुष्ठाभिमन्त्रिते ॥ ५॥

अस्त्रेणोन्मील्य सन्दृश्य क्षुरादिक्षौरसाधनम् ।
अभिमन्त्र्य द्वादशाथ प्रोक्षयेदस्त्रमन्त्रतः ॥ ६॥

English
Repeating "Śivam" "Śivam" he shall sprinkle every object with water. He shall then close his eyes with the ring-finger and the thumb. Repeating the Astra mantra he shall open his eyes and look at the razor and other implements of tonsorial rite. Repeating the mantra twelve times he shall sprinkle them with the Astra mantra. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षुरं गृहीत्वा तारेण दक्षभागे निकृन्तयेत् ।
केशांश्च कांश्चिदग्रेषु वप्त्वा सर्वं च वापयेत् ॥ ७॥

English
He shall take the razor and cut off a patch of hair on the right side. Then a few hair on the front, then the entire hair shall be shaved off. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथिव्यां पर्णमादाय विक्षिपेन्न भुवः स्थले ।
श्मश्रूणि हस्तपादस्थनखानि च निकृन्तयेत् ॥ ८॥

English
The hair shaven off shall be put on a leaf and not allowed to fall on the ground. The beards, the moustache also shall be shaven. Nails of the hands and feet shall be pared. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
बिल्वाश्वत्थतुलस्यादिस्थाने सङ्गृह्य मृत्तिकाम् ।
द्विषड्वारं निमज्याप्सु तीरं गत्वोपविश्य च ॥ ९॥

शुद्धे देशे तु संस्थाप्य मृदं त्रेधा विभज्य च ।
एवं पुनस्त्रिधा कृत्वा प्रोक्ष्यास्त्रेणाभिमन्त्रयेत् ॥ १०॥

English
He shall take clay from the root of Bilva, Asvattha or Tulasi. He shall plunge into water twelve times and come to the bank. He shall place the clay on a pure spot of ground and divide it into three parts. Each part shall be divided into three. It shall be sprinkled with water with the Astra mantra. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रैकां मृदमादाय दापयित्वान्यपाणिना ।
करौ द्वादशधालिप्य प्रत्येकं केन क्षालयेत् ॥ ११॥

English
Taking a lump of clay he shall wipe off his hands with it twelve times. Each of the hands shall be separately washed with water. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनरेकां पादयोश्च मुखे चान्यां करे क्रमात् ।
संलिप्याक्षाल्य चाम्भोभिः पुनश्च जलमाविशेत् ॥ १२॥

English
Another lump of clay shall be used similarly for the feet, another lump for the face, another for the arms and so on. After smearing and washing he shall enter water again. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यां मृदं भागयित्वा शिरसि द्वादश क्रमात् ।
आलिप्य मृदमास्यान्तं निमज्य च पुनः पुनः ॥ १३॥

English
Another lump of clay shall be divided into twelve and applied over the beard. He shall then plunge into the water again. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तीरं गत्वा तु गण्डूषान् षोडशाचमनं द्विधा ।
प्राणानायम्य च पुनः प्रणवं द्व्यष्टसङ्ख्यया ॥ १४॥

English
He shall come to the bank, gargle sixteen times, perform Ācamana twice and Prāṇāyāma sixteen times repeating the Praṇava. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मृदमन्यां पुनस्त्रेधा विभज्य च तदेकया ।
कटिशौचं पादशौचं विधायाचम्य च द्विधा ॥ १५॥

English
He shall divide another lump of clay into three. With one part he shall purify the hips and feet and perform Ācamana twice. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणवेनाथ षोडश प्राणानायम्य वाग्यतः ।
पुनरन्यां स्वोरुदेशे त्रिधा विन्यस्य चोमिति ॥ १६॥

English
Repeating the Praṇava he shall perform Prāṇāyāma silently sixteen times. He shall take another part of the clay lump and apply it on his thighs thrice repeating "Om". (16.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रोक्ष्याभिमन्त्रयेत्सप्त स्वपाण्योस्तलमेकधा ।
त्रिधालिप्याथ सम्पश्येत्सूर्यमूर्तिं च पावनीम् ॥ १७॥

English
He shall sprinkle water over it repeating Om seven times. He shall apply the clay on the palms once in the beginning and thrice afterwards. Then he shall glance at the holy disc of the sun. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वकक्षयोः समालिप्य व्यत्यस्ताभ्यामथान्यया ।
पाणिभ्यां च मृदा शिष्यः सुमतिर्दृढमानसः ॥ १८॥

गृहीत्वान्यां मृदं शुद्धां तथासौ गुरुभक्तिमान् ।
शिर आरभ्य पादान्तं विलिप्यादित्यदृष्टया ॥ १९॥

English
With another lump of clay he shall purify the armpits by means of the hands on the opposite sides. With steady and pure mind the disciple shall take another lump of pure clay and smear his body fromhead to foot with devotion to the preceptor and eyes fixed in the sun. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
समुत्थाय ततोऽसौ वै दण्डमादाय भूतले ।
स्वगुरुं मन्त्रदं भक्त्या संस्मरेज्ज्ञाननिष्ठया ॥ २०॥

English
Fixing the staff on the ground he shall stand up and remember his preceptor who has taught him the mantra with devotion strengthened by wisdom. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः साम्बं महेशानं शङ्करं चन्द्रशेखरम् ।
संस्मरेद्भक्तितः शिष्य सर्वैश्वर्यपतिं शिवम् ॥ २१॥

English
He shall devoutly remember Sāmba, Maheśāna, Śaṅkara, the moon-crested Śiva, the lord of prosperity and glory. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिवारं प्रणमेत्प्रीत्या साष्टाङ्गं च गुरुं शिवम् ।
पञ्चाङ्गेनैकवारं च समुत्थाय च वन्दयेत् ॥ २२॥

English
He shall prostrate before the preceptor Śiva thrice with eight limbs and once with five limbs touching the ground and stand up making obeisance. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तीर्थं प्रविश्य तन्मध्ये निमज्योन्मज्य तां मृदम् ।
स्कन्धे संस्थाप्य पूर्वोक्तप्रकारेण विलेपयेत् ॥ २३॥

English
He shall enter the river again and plunge once. Coming up he shall apply the clay on the shoulders in the manner mentioned before. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रावशिष्टं सङ्गृह्य जलमध्ये प्रविश्य च ।
विलोड्य सम्यक् तां तत्र सर्वाङ्गेषु विलिप्य च ॥ २४॥

English
The remaining clay he shall take up and dissolve it in the water and then apply it all over his body. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिवारमोमिति प्रोच्य शिवपादाम्बुजं स्मरन् ।
संसाराम्बुधिसन्तारं सदा यद्विधितो हि सः ॥ २५॥

English
He shall repeat 'Om' thrice and remember the lotus-like feet of Śiva which enable him to cross the ocean of worldly existence.¹⁶⁸ (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अभिषिच्योमिति जलं विरजाभस्मलोलितम् ।
अङ्गोपमार्ज्जनं कृत्वा सुस्नायाद्भस्मना ततः ॥ २६॥

English
Repeating Om he shall pour water over his body besmeared with the Bhasma of the Virajā Homa. He shall wipe the body, take his bath and apply Bhasma. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिपुण्ड्रं च विधायाथ यथोक्तविधिना शुभम् ।
यथोक्ताङ्गेषु सर्वेषु सावधानतया मुने ॥ २७॥

English
O sage, then he shall carefully apply Tripuṇḍra in accordance with the rules. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शुद्धमना भूत्वा कुर्यान्मध्यदिनक्रियाः ।
महेश्वरं नमस्कृत्य गुरूंस्तीर्थादिकानि च ॥ २८॥

English
After paying obeisance to Maheśvara, the preceptors, holy centres and others he shall perform the midday rites. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्पूजयेन्महेशानं भक्त्या परमया मुने ।
साम्बिकं ज्ञानदातारं पातारं त्रिभवस्य वै ॥ २९॥

English
O sage, with great devotion he shall worship Śiva accompanied by Śivā and the bestower of perfect knowledge, the protector of the three worlds. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततोऽसौ दृढचेतस्को यतिः स्ववृषसंस्थितः ।
भिक्षार्थं प्रव्रजेच्छुद्धो विप्रवर्गेषु साधुषु ॥ ३०॥

English
With steady mind, abiding by his virtuous duties and pure in body, he shall go to good brahmin householders for alms. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तत्र च शुद्धात्मा पञ्चधा परिकल्पितम् ।
भैक्ष्यं यथोचितं कुर्याद् दूषितान्नं विवर्जयेत् ॥ ३१॥

English
He shall divide the alms into five parts and do with them as proper. He shall avoid defiled food. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
शौचं स्नानं तथा भिक्षां नित्यमेकान्तसेवनम् ।
भिक्षौश्चत्वारि कर्माणि पञ्चमं नैव विद्यते ॥ ३२॥

English
A mendicant shall perform four activities : Purificatory rites, ablution, begging for alms and resorting to isolated places for meditation. He has no fifth rite. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अलाबुं वेणुपात्रं च दारवं मृण्मयं तथा ।
भिक्षोश्चत्वारि पात्राणि पञ्चमं नैव विद्यते ॥ ३३॥

English
He shall use only four types of begging bowls, that made of dried gourd, or of bamboo, or of wood or of clay. He shall not take any other type of bowl. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ताम्बूलं तैजसं पात्रं रेतस्सेकं सिताम्बरम् ।
दिवास्वापो हिनक्तान्नं यतीनां षड्विवर्जिताः ॥ ३४॥

English
Six things are forbidden to the ascetics: chewing the betel, using the metallic vessel, emission of semen, white cloth, sleeping during the day and taking meal at night. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
साक्षरा विपरीताश्च राक्षसास्त इति स्मृताः ।
तस्माद्वै विपरीतं च कर्म नैवाचरेद् यतिः ॥ ३५॥

English
If they observe the rules they are wise. If they do not they are Rākṣasas. The ascetic shall never indulge in activities contrary to the rules. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
यतिः प्रयत्नतः कुर्यात्क्षौरस्नानं च शुद्धये ।
संस्मरन्मनसा शुद्धं परं ब्रह्म सदाशिवम् ॥ ३६॥

English
He shall perform the post-shaving ablution assiduously for purity, remembering mentally the supreme Brahman Sadāśiva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यैव मुनिशार्दूल तव स्नेहान्मयाखिलः ।
क्षौरस्नानविधिः प्रोक्तः किम्भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ३७॥

English
O sage, thus out of affection for you, the rites of post-shaving ablution I have explained to you. What else do you wish to hear? (37.)

---

Sanskrit
इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे षष्ठ्यां कैलाससंहितायां क्षौरस्नानविधिवर्णनं नाम विंशोऽध्यायः ॥ ६.२०॥
