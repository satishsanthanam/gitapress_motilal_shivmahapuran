### **CHAPTER TWENTY TWO - (The rites on the eleventh day for the ascetics) - Subrahmanya said:—**

---

Sanskrit
द्वाविंशोऽध्यायः । यतीनामेकादशाहकृत्यवर्णनम् ।

सुब्रह्मण्य उवाच ।
एकादशेऽह्नि सम्प्राप्ते यो विधिः समुदाहृतः ।
तं वक्ष्ये मुनिशार्दूल यतीनां स्नेहतस्तव ॥ १॥

English
O sage, out of affection for you I shall explain the rite for ascetics on the eleventh day after death. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सम्मार्ज्य वेदीमालिप्य कृत्वा पुण्याहवाचनम् ।
प्रोक्ष्य पश्चिममारभ्य पूर्वान्तं पञ्च च क्रमात् ॥ २॥

मण्डलान्युत्तराशास्यः कुर्यात्स्वयमवस्थितः ।
प्रादेशमात्रं सङ्कल्प्य चतुरस्रं च मध्यतः ॥ ३॥

English
The performer of obsequies shall sweep the platform and smear it with cowdung. He shall sprinkle water with Puṇyāhavācana mantras five times from the west to the east. He shall sit down facing the north and draw the mystic diagrams himself, the middle square of 18 cms in length. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
बिन्दुत्रिकोणषट्कोणवृत्ताकाराणि च क्रमात् ।
शङ्खं च पुरतः स्थाप्य पूजोक्तक्रममार्गतः ॥ ४॥

English
The Bindu, triangle, hexagon and circle shall be drawn in order. The conch shall be placed in front in accordance with the rules of worship. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राणानायम्य सङ्कल्प्य पूजयित्वा सुरेश्वरीः ।
देवताः पञ्च पूर्वोक्ता अतिवाहिकरूपिणीः ॥ ५॥

English
He shall perform the Prāṇāyāma and after due Saṃkalpa rite, he shall worship the five deities mentioned as messengers before. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्त्यज्योत्तरतो दर्भानयश्च संस्पृशेत् ततः ।
पश्चिमादि समारभ्य षडुत्थासनमार्गतः ॥ ६॥

मण्डलानि च तेष्वन्तः पुष्पाण्याधाय पीठवत् ।
ॐ ह्रीमित्युक्त्वाग्निरूपां तामतिवाहिकदेवताम् ॥ ७॥

आवाहयामि नम इत्यन्तं सर्वत्र भावयेत् ।
दर्शयेत्स्थापनाद्यास्तु मुद्राः प्रत्येकमादरात् ॥ ८॥

English
He shall cast off Darbha grass to the north and not touch it again. Beginning with the west the Maṇḍalas shall be worshipped according to the rules for the rite of "Sadutthāpana." Flowers shall be stacked within them like a pedestal with "Om Hrīm. I invoke the messenger goddess in the form of fire, obeisance." This latter portion is common to the five. The gestures of installation shall be shown severally to each. (6-8.)

---

Sanskrit
ह्रांह्रीमित्यादिना कुर्यादासामङ्गानि च क्रमात् ।
पाशाङ्कुशाभयाभीष्टपाणिचन्द्रोपलप्रभाः ॥ ९॥

English
The Aṅganyāsas for the deities shall be performed with Hrām, Hrīm etc. The messenger goddesses shall have the noose, goad, gestures of fearlessness and wish in their hands. They are as lustrous as the lunar stone. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्ताङ्गुलीयकच्छायरञ्जिताखिलदिङ्मुखाः ।
रक्ताम्बरधराः हस्तपदपङ्कजशोभिताः ॥ १०॥

English
They have changed (or enhanced) the hue of the quarters by the lustre of their gemset rings. They wear red garments and shine with lotus-like hands and feet. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रिनेत्रोल्लासिवदनपूर्णचन्द्रमनोहराः ।
माणिक्यमुकुटोद्भासिचन्द्रलेखावतंसिताः ॥ ११॥

English
They look charming with their faces resembling the full moon and brilliant with three eyes. They are decorated with the crescent moon shining above their ruby-set coronets.

12 Their swinging ear-rings tickle their cheeks. They have plump and elevated breasts. They are charming with their necklaces, shoulderlets, bangles and girdles. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तनुमध्याः पृथुश्रोण्यो रक्तदिव्याम्बरावृताः ।
माणिक्यमयमञ्जीरसिञ्जत्पदसरोरुहाः ।
पादाङ्गुलीयकश्रोणीर्मञ्जुलातिमनोहराः ॥ १३॥

English
Their waists are slender and buttocks large. They wear divine red garments. The ruby-set anklets jingle and tinkle in their lotus-like feet. The rings in their toes are very charming. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहेण मूर्तेन शिववत् किं नु साध्यते ।
तस्माच्छक्त्यात्ममूर्तेन सर्वं साध्यं महेशवत् ॥ १४॥

English
What blessing can be secured from a departed soul? Only from a powerful soul can everything be achieved as from Maheśa. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वानुग्रहकर्त्रैव स्वीकृताः पञ्चमूर्तयः ।
सर्वकार्यकरा दिव्याः परानुग्रहतत्पराः ॥ १५॥

English
The five deities capable of all activities and eager to bless others are accepted by the lord as the bestowers of bliss. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं ध्यात्वा तु ताः सर्वा अनुग्रहपराः शिवाः ।
पादयोः पाद्यमेतासां दद्याच्छङ्खोदबिन्दुभिः ॥ १६॥

English
After meditating on the auspicious deities eager to bless he shall offer Pādyā at their feet with drops of water from the conch. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
हस्तेष्वाचमनीयं च मौलिष्वर्घ्यं प्रदापयेत् ।
शङ्खोदबिन्दुभिस्तासां स्नानकर्म च भावयेत् ॥ १७॥

English
The water for Ācamana shall be offered in their hands and Arghya on their heads. He shall conceive of their ritualistic ablution with the drops of water from the conch. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्ताम्बराणि दिव्यानि सोत्तरीयाणि दापयेत् ।
मुकुटादीन्यनर्घ्याणि दद्यादाभरणानि च ॥ १८॥

English
Beautiful garments red in colour shall be offered along with upper cloths. Coronets and ornaments shall also be offered. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवासितं च श्रीखण्डमक्षतांश्चातिशोभनान् ।
सुरभीणि मनोज्ञानि कुसुमानि च दापयेत् ॥ १९॥

English
Fragrant sandal wood, auspicious Akṣatas and charming fragrant flowers shall also be offered. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
धूपं च परमामोदं साज्यवर्तिं च दीपकम् ।
सर्वं समर्पयामीति प्रणवं ह्रीमुपक्रमात् ॥ २०॥

नमोऽन्तं च ततो दद्यात्पायसं मधुनाप्लुतम् ।
साज्यशर्करयापूपकदलीगुडपूरितम् ॥ २१॥

English
Fragrant incense and lamp with wicks soaked in ghee shall be offered saying "I dedicate everything." Om should be uttered in the beginning and Namah at the end. Then milk pudding with honey shall be offered along with ghee, sugar, sweet pie and plantain fruits stuffed with jaggery. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रत्येकं कदलीपत्रे भरितं च सुवासितम् ।
भूर्भुवः स्वरिति प्रोच्य प्रोक्ष्णादीनि कारयेत् ॥ २२॥

English
Different articles shall be heaped on separate plantain leaves and rendered fragrant. They shall be sprinkled with water uttering "Bhuḥ Bhuvaḥ Svah." (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ॐ ह्रीमिति समुच्चार्य नैवेद्यं वह्निजायया ।
पानीयं नम इत्युक्त्वा परं प्रेम्णा समर्पयेत् ॥ २३॥

English
Naivedya shall be offered with; "Om Hrim Svāhā". With "Namah" water shall be offered with great devotion. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तत उद्वासयेत् प्रीत्या पूर्वतो मुनिसत्तम ।
स्थलं विशोध्य गण्डूषाचमनार्घ्याणि दापयेत् ॥ २४॥

English
O excellent sage, thereafter the rite of Udvasana shall be performed with devotion. The place shall be mopped clean beginning with the east. Then water for gargling and Ācamana shall be offered with Arghya. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ताम्बूलं धूपदीपौ च प्रदक्षिणनमस्कृती ।
विधाय प्रार्थयेदेताः शिरस्यञ्जलिमादधत् ॥ २५॥

English
After offering Tāmbūla, incense and lights, the circumambulation and obeisance shall be performed. The deities shall be prayed with the joined palms placed on the head reverentially. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीमातरः सुप्रसन्ना यतिं शिवपदैषिणम् ।
रक्षणीयं प्रब्रुवन्तु परमेशपदाब्जयोः ॥ २६॥

English
"May the glorious mothers be delighted. At the lotus-like feet of Parameśa let them recommend the protection of the deceased ascetic who is desirous of attaining the region of Śiva. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सम्प्रार्थ्य ताः सर्वा विसृज्य च यथागतम् ।
तासां प्रसादमुद्धृत्य कन्यकाभ्यः प्रदापयेत् ॥ २७॥

English
After ritualistically dismissing them along the way they had come, the remanents of the Naivedya shall be distributed among the virgins. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
गोभ्यो वा जलमध्ये वा निक्षिपेन्नान्यथा क्वचित् ।
अत्रैव पार्वणं कुर्यान्नैकोद्दिष्टं यतेः क्वचित् ॥ २८॥

English
It shall be given to the cows or cast off into water: There is no other way of disposal. Pārvaṇa and not Ekoddiṣṭa Śrāddha shall be performed for an ascetic. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रायं पार्वणश्राद्धे नियमः प्रोच्यते मया ।
तं श‍ृणुष्व मुनिश्रेष्ठ येन श्रेयो भवेत्ततः ॥ २९॥

English
I shall now tell you the rules for the Pārvaṇa for your welfare. O sage, listen. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्ता स्नात्वा धृतप्राण उपवीती समाहितः ।
सपवित्रकरस्त्वस्यां पुण्यतिथ्यामिति ब्रुवन् ॥ ३०॥

करिष्ये पार्वणं श्राद्धमिति सङ्कल्प्य चोत्तरे ।
दद्याद्दर्भानुत्तमांश्च ह्यासनार्थं जलं स्पृशेत् ॥ ३१॥

तत्रोपवेशयेद्भक्त्या साभ्यङ्गं कृतमज्जनान् ।
आहूय चतुरो विप्रान् शिवभक्तान्दृढव्रतान् ॥ ३२॥

English
The performer of the Pārvaṇa shall take his bath, perform Prāṇāyāma with pious mind wearing the sacred thread as usual. Wearing the Darbha ring he shall say "On this auspicious Tithi I shall perform the Pārvaṇa Śrāddha." In the place of Āsana Darbha grass shall be placed. After Saṃkalpa Darbhas shall be cast off to the north. Water shall be touched. He shall invite four Śaiva brahmins who are steady in their rites. The brahmins shall take oil bath after which they shall be made to sit. (30-32.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वेदेवार्थं भवता प्रसादः क्रियतामिति ।
आत्मने भवता पश्चादन्तरात्मन इत्यपि ॥ ३३॥

परमात्मन इत्येवं प्रोच्य प्रार्थ्य च तान्यतिः ।
श्रद्धया वरणं तेषां कुर्याद्याथार्थ्यमादरात् ॥ ३४॥

English
"Ye all shall represent the Viśvedevas." After saying this he shall say "obcisance to Ātman, Antarātman and Paramātman." Then he shall touch their feet with devotion. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
पादौ प्रक्षाल्य तेषां तु प्राङ्मुखानुपवेश्य च ।
गन्धादिभिरलङ्कृत्य भोजयेच्च शिवाग्रतः ॥ ३५॥

English
The feet of the brahmins shall be washed and they shall be made to sit facing the east. After decorating them with scents, sandal paste etc., they shall be fed in front of Śiva (35.)

---

Sanskrit
गोमयेनोपलिप्यात्र दर्भान्प्रागग्रकल्पितान् ।
आस्तीर्य संयतप्राणः पिण्डानां च प्रदानकम् ॥ ३६॥

करिष्य इति सङ्कल्प्य मण्डलत्रयमर्च्य च ।
आत्मानमन्तरात्मानं परमात्मनमप्यतः ॥ ३७॥

चतुर्थ्यन्तं वदन् पश्चादिमं पिण्डमितीरयन् ।
ददामीति च सम्प्रोच्य दद्यात्पिण्डान् स्वभक्तितः ॥ ३८॥

कुशोदकं ततो दद्याद्यथाविधि विधानतः ।
तत उत्थाय वै कुर्यात् प्रदक्षिणनमस्कृती ॥ ३९॥

English
The ground shall be smeared with cowdung. Darbha grass shall be spread with their tips to the east. He shall perform Prāṇāyāma and the rite of saṃkalpa "I shall offer the Piṇḍa." He shall then worship the Maṇḍala Ātman, Antarātman and the Paramātman turning the face back. "I am giving these Piṇḍas," saying this he shall offer the Piṇḍas with devotion. Water from the Darbha grass shall be offered. Getting up he shall perform circumambulation and the Namaskāra. (36-39.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दत्त्वा ब्राह्मणेभ्यो दक्षिणां च यथाविधि ।
नारायणबलिं कुर्यात् तस्मिन्नेव स्थले दिने ॥ ४०॥

English
After giving gifts to the Brahmins according to the rules he shall offer Nārāyaṇa Bali at the same spot on the same day. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्षार्थमेव सर्वत्र विष्णोः पूजाविधिः स्मृतः ।
कुर्याद्विष्णोर्महापूजां पायसान्नं निवेदयेत् ॥ ४१॥

English
Everywhere Viṣṇu shall be worshipped for protection. He shall perform the worship of Viṣṇu and offer milk pudding as Naivedya. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वादशाथ समाहूय ब्राह्मणान्वेदपारगान् ।
केशवादिभिरभ्यर्च्य गन्धपुष्पाक्षतादिभिः ॥ ४२॥

उपानच्छत्रवस्त्रादि दत्त्वा तेभ्यो यथाविधि ।
सन्तोषयेन्महाभक्त्या विविधैर्वचनैः शुभैः ॥ ४३॥

English
He shall invite twelve brahmins who are masters of the Vedas. He shall worship them in the names of Keśava etc. with scents, flowers and Akṣatas. He shall propitiate them and offer them shoes, umbrella, cloth, according to the rules. He shall speak to them sweet, auspicious words. (42-43.)

---

Sanskrit
आस्तीर्य दर्भान्पूर्वाग्रान्भूः स्वाहा च भुवः भुवः ।
प्रणवादि प्रोच्य भूमौ पायसान्नं बलिं हरेत् ॥ ४४॥

English
He shall spread Darbha grass on the ground with their tips to the east. After saying “Bhūḥ Svāhā, Bhuvaḥ Svāhā, Suvaḥ Svāhā, Om.” He shall offer milk pudding as oblation. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
एकादशाहसुविधिर्मया प्रोक्तो मुनीश्वर ।
द्वादशाहविधिं वक्ष्ये श‍ृणुष्वादरतो द्विज ॥ ४५॥

English
O great sage, the rite of the eleventh day has been mentioned by me. O brahmin, I shall now explain the twelfth day rites. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
कुण्डलामृष्टगण्डाश्च पीनोन्नतपयोधराः ।
हारकेयूरकटककाञ्चीदाममनोहराः ॥ १२॥
