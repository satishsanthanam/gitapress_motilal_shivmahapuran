व्यासाय वक्ता स च शैववर्यः शुकाय वक्ता भविता च पूर्णः ॥ २७॥

Hindi

--- Verse 28-29 ---

--- Verse 30 ---

Hindi

--- Verse 31-32 ---

--- Verse 33 ---

Sanskrit

Hindi

--- Verse 34 ---

Sanskrit

Hindi

--- Verse 35-36 ---

Sanskrit

Hindi

--- Verse 37-38 ---

--- Verse 41-42 ---

--- Verse 43-45 ---
