### **CHAPTER TWO - (The problem of the sages)**

---

Sanskrit
२

सूत उवाच ।
पुरा कालेन महता कल्पेऽतीते पुनः पुनः ।
अस्मिन्नुपस्थिते कल्पे प्रवृत्ते सृष्टिकर्मणि ॥ १॥

प्रतिष्ठितायां वार्तायां प्रबुद्धासु प्रजासु च ।
मुनीनां षट्कुलीयानां ब्रुवतामितरेतरम् ॥ २॥

इदं परमिदं नेति विवादः सुमहानभूत् ।
परस्य दुर्निरूपत्वान्न जातस्तत्र निश्चयः ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said:—  When a long time had elapsed, many kalpas had come and gone, the present kalpa had started, the activity of creation had begun, and the customs among the enlightened subjects had become established, a dispute arose among the sages of six clans. One said “This is the greatest being.” Another said, "It is not." Because the greatest being could not be reviewed or defined no conclusion was arrived at. (1-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तेऽभिजग्मुर्विधातारं द्रष्टुं ब्रह्माणमव्ययम् ।
यत्रास्ते भगवान् ब्रह्मा स्तूयमानः सुरासुरैः ॥ ४॥

English
In order to see the eternal creator they went where the lord was staying eulogised by gods and Asuras. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
मेरुश‍ृङ्गे शुभे रम्ये देवदानवसङ्कुले ।
सिद्धचारणसम्बादे यक्षगन्धर्वसेविते ॥ ५॥

विहङ्गसङ्घसङ्घुष्टेमणिविद्रुमभूषिते ।
निकुञ्जकन्दरदरीगृहानिर्झरशोभिते ॥ ६॥

English
It was on the auspicious and charming peak of Meru,¹⁸⁴ where there were plenty of gods and Asuras where Siddhas and Cāraṇas thronged, where Yakṣas and Gandharvas frequented, where flocks of birds chirped and crew, which was embellished by jewels and corals and which shone due to streams and rivulets, caves and crevices, bushes and hedges. (5-6.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र ब्रह्मवनं नाम नानामृगसमाकुलम् ।
दशयोजनविस्तीर्णं शतयोजनमायतम् ॥ ७॥

सुरसामलपानीयपूर्णरम्यसरोवरम् ।
मत्तभ्रमरसञ्छन्नरम्यपुष्पितपादपम् ॥ ८॥

English
There was a forest Brahmavana¹⁸⁵ which abounded in different species of deer. It was ten Yojanas wide and hundred Yojanas long. There were beautiful lakes full of sweet and clean water. It had plenty of trees in full bloom where swarms of bees hummed and hovered. (7-8.)

---

Sanskrit
तरुणादित्यसङ्काशं तत्र चारु महत्पुरम् ।
दुर्द्धर्षबलदृप्तानां दैत्यदानवरक्षसाम् ॥ ९॥

English
This was a great city as beautiful and brilliant as the midday sun. It could not be attacked by the haughty Daityas, Dānavas and Rākṣasas. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तप्तजाम्बूनदमयं प्रांशुप्राकारतोरणम् ।
निर्यूहवलभीकूटप्रतोलीशतमण्डितम् ॥ १०॥

English
It had lofty ramparts and portals made of molten gold. It was embellished by hundreds of main streets, turrets and wooden ceilings. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
महार्हमणिचित्राभिर्लेलिहानमिवाम्बरम् ।
महाभवनकोटीभिरनेकाभिरलङ्कृतम् ॥ ११॥

English
It appeared to lick the sky with crores of huge mansions set with precious gems of variegated colours. It was decorated with many such mansions. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्निवसति ब्रह्मा सभ्यैः सार्द्धं प्रजापतिः ।
तत्र गत्वा महात्मानं साक्षाल्लोकपितामहम् ॥ १२॥

ददृशुर्मुनयो देवा देवर्षिगणसेवितम् ।
शुद्धचामीकरप्रख्यं सर्वाभरणभूषितम् ॥ १३॥

English
Lord Brahmā resides there along with his councillors. Going there the sages saw the lord, the noble Ātman served by the groups of gods and sages. He was shining like pure gold. He was bedecked in all ornaments. (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नवदनं सौम्यं पद्मपत्रायतेक्षणम् ।
दिव्यकान्तिसमायुक्तं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् ॥ १४॥

English
He was gentle with delightful face. His eyes were as large as the petals of a lotus. He was endowed with a divine lustre. He had smeared divine unguents and scents. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
दिव्यशुक्लाम्बरधरं दिव्यमालाविभूषितम् ।
सुरासुरेन्द्रयोगीन्द्रवन्द्यमानपदाम्बुजम् ॥ १५॥

English
He was clad in brilliant white garments. He was bedecked in divine garlands. His lotus-like feet were saluted by the gods, Asuras and leading yogins. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वलक्षणयुक्ताङ्ग्या लब्धचामरहस्तया ।
भ्राजमानं सरस्वत्या प्रभयेव दिवाकरम् ॥ १६॥

English
Accompanied by Sarasvatī 'whose body had all auspicious marks who held the chowries in her hands, he shone as the sun with his dazzling light. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा मुनयः सर्वे प्रसन्नवदनेक्षणाः ।
शिरस्यञ्जलिमाधाय तुष्टुवुः सुरपुङ्गवम् ॥ १७॥

English
On seeing him the sages were delighted and their eyes and faces shone. With palms joined in reverence and held over their heads they eulogised the leading god. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
नमस्त्रिमूर्तये तुभ्यं सर्गस्थित्यन्तहेतवे ।
पुरुषाय पुराणाय ब्रह्मणे परमात्मने ॥ १८॥

English
The sages said:—  Obeisance to you in the form of Trinity, the cause of creation, sustenance and annihilation, the ancient Puruṣa, Brahmā the great Ātman. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नमः प्रधानदेहाय प्रधानक्षोभकारिणे ।
त्रयोविंशतिभेदेन विकृतायाविकारिणे ॥ १९॥

English
Obeisance to the deity who has Prakṛti for his body, who is the cause of quickening Pradhāna, who though transformed into twentythree principles is yet free from aberration. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
नमो ब्रह्माण्डदेहाय ब्रह्माण्डोदरवर्तिने ।
तत्र संसिद्धकार्याय संसिद्धकरणाय च ॥ २०॥

English
Obeisance to the deity who has universe for his body, who is stationed within the belly of the universe, who has accomplished his task and whose body is perfectly accomplished. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नमोऽस्तु सर्वलोकाय सर्वलोकविधायिने ।
सर्वात्मदेहसंयोगवियोगविधिहेतवे ॥ २१॥

English
Obeisance to the deity identical with the world, the creator, the sustainer and the annihilator of the world. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयैव निखिलं सृष्टं संहृतं पालितं जगत् ।
तथापि मायया नाथ न विद्मस्त्वां पितामह ॥ २२॥

English
O Brahmā, it is by you alone that the entire universe is created, sustained and annihilated. Still, due to illusion, O lord, we do not know you. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
एवं ब्रह्मा महाभागैर्महर्षिभिरभिष्टुतः ।
प्राह गम्भीरया वाचा मुनीन् प्रह्लादयन्निव ॥ २३॥

English
Sūta said:—  Thus eulogised by the fortunate sages, Brahmā spoke to the sages in a majestic tone delighting them. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
ऋषयो हे महाभागा महासत्त्वा महौजसः ।
किमर्थं सहिताः सर्वे यूयमत्र समागताः ॥ २४॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O blessed sages of great splendour and strength, why have you come here all together? (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तमेवंवादिनं देवं ब्रह्माणं ब्रह्मवित्तमाः ।
वाग्भिर्विनयगर्भाभिः सर्वे प्राञ्जलयोऽब्रुवन् ॥ २५॥

English
The sages foremost of those who know Brahman spoke with palms joined in reverence, with words couched in humility to lord Brahmā who spoke thus. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
भगवन्नन्धकारेण महता वयमावृताः ।
खिन्ना विवदमानाश्च न पश्यामोऽत्र यत्परम् ॥ २६॥

English
The sages said:—  O lord, we have been encompassed in deep darkness. We are distressed. Unable to understand the greatest being we are arguing among ourselves. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं हि सर्वजगद्धाता सर्वकारणकारणम् ।
त्वया ह्यविदितं नाथ नेह किञ्चन विद्यते ॥ २७॥

English
Indeed you are the creator of the universe. You are the cause of all causes. O lord, there is nothing here not known to you. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
कः पुमान् सर्वसत्त्वेभ्यः पुराणः पुरुषः परः ।
विशुद्धः परिपूर्णश्च शाश्वतः परमेश्वरः ॥ २८॥

English
Who is that being more ancient than all living beings? Who is the greatest Puruṣa? Who is the purest, perfect, and eternal Being? (28.)

---

Sanskrit
केनैव चित्रकृत्येन प्रथमं सृज्यते जगत् ।
तत्त्वं वद महाप्राज्ञ स्वसन्देहापनुत्तये ॥ २९॥

English
Who by indulging in wonderful activities has created the universe? O intelligent lord, please mention it and quell our doubts. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं पृष्टस्तदा ब्रह्मा विस्मयस्मेरवीक्षणः ।
देवानां दानवानां च मुनीनामपि सन्निधौ ॥ ३०॥

उत्थाय सुचिरं ध्यात्वा रुद्र इत्युच्चरन् गिरिम् ।
आनन्दक्लिन्नसर्वाङ्गः कृताञ्जलिरभाषत ॥ ३१॥

English
Thus questioned, Brahma, with smiling eyes, stood up and meditated for long in the presence of the gods, Dānavas and sages. Uttering the words "Rudra" he joined his palms in reverence, with a blissful sensation moistening his body and spoke. (30-31.)
