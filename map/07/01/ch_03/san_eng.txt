### **CHAPTER THREE - (The Naimīṣa episode)**

---

Sanskrit
३

ब्रह्मोवाच ।
यतो वाचो निवर्तन्ते अप्राप्य मनसा सह ।
आनन्दं यस्य वै विद्वान्न बिभेति कुतश्चन ॥ १॥

यस्मात्सर्वमिदं ब्रह्मविष्णुरुद्रेन्द्रपूर्वकम् ।
सह भूतेन्द्रियैः सर्वैः प्रथमं सम्प्रसूयते ॥ २॥

कारणानां च यो धाता ध्याता परमकारणम् ।
न सम्प्रसूयतेऽन्यस्मात्कुतश्चन कदाचन ॥ ३॥

सर्वैश्वर्येण सम्पन्नो नाम्ना सर्वेश्वरः स्वयम् ।
सर्वैर्मुमुक्षुभिर्ध्येयः शम्भुराकाशमध्यगः ॥ ४॥

English
Brahmā said:—  Siva stationed in the middle of the ether shall be meditated upon by those desirous of salvation; Siva from whom the words recede not approaching him along with the mind; knowing and realising whose bliss-form one has no fear from anywhere; from whom every thing inclusive of Brahma, Visnu, Rudra, Indra, the elements, the sense-organs, is born at first, who is the creator, meditator and ultimate cause of all causes; who is not born from anything else at any time; who is endowed with glory and prosperity and who is known as Sarveśvara. (1-4.)

---

Sanskrit
योऽग्रे मां विदधे पुत्रं ज्ञानं च प्रहिणोति मे ।
तत्प्रसादान्मया लब्धं प्राजापत्यमिदं पदम् ॥ ५॥

English
It was he who created me at the outset as his son and gave me perfect knowledge. It was due to his grace that the lordship of the people was attained by me. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशो वृक्ष इव स्तब्धो य एको दिवि तिष्ठति ।
येनेदमखिलं पूर्णं पुरुषेण महात्मना ॥ ६॥

English
He is the lord who stands in heaven like the lofty stump of a tree; and by whom, by the Puruṣa, the great Ātman, all this is filled up. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एको बहूनां जन्तूनां निष्क्रियाणां च सक्रियः ।
य एको बहुधा बीजं करोति स महेश्वरः ॥ ७॥

English
He is active among the inactive creatures; he who being single himself creates many is Maheśvara. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
जीवैरेभिरिमांल्लोकान्सर्वानीशो य ईशते ।
य एको भागवान् रुद्रो न द्वितीयोऽस्ति कश्चन ॥ ८॥

English
He rules over all the worlds through the Jīvas. He is the only lord Rudra. There is none second to him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सदा जनानां हृदये सन्निविष्टोऽपि यः परैः ।
अलक्ष्यो लक्षयन्विश्वमधितिष्ठति सर्वदा ॥ ९॥

English
Though he has entered and is ever present in the hearts of the people he is invisible to others; he occupies and views at the universe always. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तु कालात्प्रमुक्तानि कारणान्यखिलान्यपि ।
अनन्तशक्तिरेवैको भगवानधितिष्ठति ॥ १०॥

English
He is the sole lord of infinite Śakti who presides over all causes and is released from Time—Death. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
न यस्य दिवसो रात्रिर्न समानो न चाधिकः ।
स्वाभाविकी पराशक्तिर्नित्या ज्ञानक्रिये अपि ॥ ११॥

English
He has neither day nor night; neither equals nor superiors. The great knowledge and activity is innate in him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
यदिदं क्षरमव्यक्तं यदप्यमृतमक्षरम् ।
तावुभावक्षरात्मानावेको देवः स्वयं हरः ॥ १२॥

English
That which is perishable and unmanifest, that which is imperishable and immortal—both these have the imperishable as their Ātman. The sole lord is Śiva himself. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशते तदभिध्यानाद् योजनात्तत्वभावनः ।
भूयो ह्यस्य पशोरन्ते विश्वमाया निवर्तते ॥ १३॥

English
By concentrating on him, the person of Sāttvaic feelings becomes the lord himself. At the end the universal illusion recedes from him. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मिन्न भासते विद्युन्न सूर्यो न च चन्द्रमाः ।
यस्य भासा विभातीदमित्येषा शाश्वती श्रुतिः ॥ १४॥

English
The eternal Śruti says "In whom neither the lightning flashes nor the sun blazes nor the moon sheds light and by whose brilliance this universe comes in the limelight." (14.)

---

Sanskrit
एको देवो महादेवो विज्ञेयस्तु महेश्वरः ।
न तस्य परमं किञ्चित्पदं समधिगम्यते ॥ १५॥

English
That lord Śiva shall be realised. There is no other region greater than his worthy of attainment. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अयमादिरनाद्यन्तः स्वभावादेव निर्मलः ।
स्वतन्त्रः परिपूर्णश्च स्वेच्छाधीनश्चराचरः ॥ १६॥

English
He is the cause. He has no beginning, no end. He is naturally pure, independent and perfect. The mobile and immobile beings are subservient to his will. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अप्राकृतवपुः श्रीमांल्लक्ष्यलक्षणवर्जितः ।
अयं मुक्तो मोचकश्च ह्यकालः कालचोदकः ॥ १७॥

English
His body is not the creation of Prakṛti. He is glorious, devoid of examples and definitions. He is both the liberated and liberator. He is not influenced by Time. He is the activiser of time. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वोपरिकृतावासः सर्वावासश्च सर्ववित् ।
षड्विधाध्वमयस्यास्य सर्वस्य जगतः पतिः ॥ १८॥

English
He has taken up residence above all. He is the repository of all. He is the knower of all. He is the lord of the universe comprising the sixfold path. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्तरोत्तरभूतानामुत्तरश्च निरुत्तरः ।
अनन्तानन्तसन्दोहमकरन्दमध्रुव्रतः ॥ १९॥

English
He is the Being above all beings, one above the other. There is no being above him. He is the bee imbibing the honey of endless bliss. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
अखण्डजगदण्डानां पिण्डीकरणपण्डितः ।
औदार्यवीर्यगाम्भीर्यमाधुर्यमकरालयः ॥ २०॥

English
He is an expert in solidifying the unsevered cosmic eggs. He is the ocean of benevolence, prowess, majesty and sweetness. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
नैवास्य सदृशं वस्तु नाधिकं चापि किञ्चन ।
अतुलः सर्वभूतानां राजराजश्च तिष्ठति ॥ २१॥

English
There is no object equal to him or excelling him. He stands as the unparalleled Emperor of all living beings. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेन चित्रकृत्येन प्रथमं सृज्यते जगत् ।
अन्तकाले पुनश्चेदं तस्मिन्प्रलयमेष्यति ॥ २२॥

English
The universe is created by him indulging in wonderful activities. At the time of dissolution this gets dissolved in him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य भूतानि वश्यानि अयं सर्वनियोजकः ।
अयं तु परया भक्त्या दृश्यते नान्यथा क्वचित् ॥ २३॥

English
The living beings are under his control. He is the employer of all. He is seen by great devotion and not otherwise. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
व्रतानि सर्वदानानि तपांसि नियमास्तथा ।
कथितानि पुरा सद्भिर्भावार्थं नात्र संशयः ॥ २४॥

English
Sacred rites, charitable gifts, penances and observances, these are advocated by people for the purification of emotions. There is no doubt in this. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
हरिश्चाहं च रुद्रश्च तथान्ये च सुरासुराः ।
तपोभिरुग्रैरद्यापि तस्य दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ २५॥

English
Vişnu, I, Rudra, gods and Asuras are desirous of seeing him even today by performing great penances. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अदृश्यः पतितैर्मूढैर्दुर्जनैरपि कुत्सितैः ।
भक्तैरन्तर्बहिश्चापि पूज्यः सम्भाष्य एव च ॥ २६॥

English
He is invisible to fallen wicked men, the despicable and the stupid. He is worshipped within and without by devotees and can be spoken to. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिदं त्रिविधं रूपं स्थूलं सूक्ष्मं ततः परम् ।
अस्मदाद्यमरैर्दृश्यं स्थूलं सूक्ष्मं तु योगिभिः ॥ २७॥

ततः परं तु यन्नित्यं ज्ञानमानन्दमव्ययम् ।
तन्निष्ठैस्तत्परैर्भक्तैर्दृश्यं तद् व्रतमाश्रितैः ॥ २८॥

English
Forms in the universe are threefold—the gross, subtle and that which is beyond. The gross is seen by us, the gods and others. The subtle is seen by the Yogins. That which is beyond the two, is eternal, knowledge, bliss and the unchanging can be seen by those who dwell in it, are devoted to it and are engaged in the rites concerning it. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
बहुनाऽत्र किमुक्तेन गुह्याद्गुह्यतरं परम् ।
शिवे भक्तिर्न सन्देहस्तया युक्तो विमुच्यते ॥ २९॥

English
Of what avail is much talk? Devotion to Siva is the secret of secrets. He who is endowed with it becomes liberated. There is no doubt in this. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादादेव सा भक्तिः प्रसादो भक्तिसम्भवः ।
यथा चाङ्कुरतो बीजं बीजतो वा यथाङ्कुरः ॥ ३०॥

English
Devotion is the result of grace and grace is the result of devotion just as the sprout comes out of the seed and produces the seed. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादपूर्विका एव पशोः सर्वत्र सिद्धयः ।
स एव साधनैरन्ते सर्वैरपि च साध्यते ॥ ३१॥

English
The achievements of the individual are due to the grace of God. Lord alone is achieved in the end through all the means. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादसाधनं धर्मः स च वेदेन दर्शितः ।
तदभ्यासवशात्साम्यं पूर्वयोः पुण्यपापयोः ॥ ३२॥

English
Virtue and holy rites constitute the means for the attainment of grace. That is indicated by the Vedas. By practising Dharma the sins and merits are levelled to equality. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
साम्यात्प्रसादसम्पर्को धर्मस्यातिशयस्ततः ।
धर्मातिशयमासाद्य पशोः पापपरिक्षयः ॥ ३३॥

English
Contact with grace yields excellence of Dharma. After attaining this excellence the sins of the individual decline. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं प्रक्षीणपापस्य बहुभिर्जन्मभिः क्रमात् ।
साम्बे सर्वेश्वरे भक्तिर्ज्ञानपूर्वा प्रजायते ॥ ३४॥

English
When his sins decline through succession of births the devotion to Sarvesvara and Amba is generated along with knowledge. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
भावानुगुणमीशस्य प्रसादो व्यतिरिच्यते ।
प्रसादात्कर्मसन्त्यागः फलतो न स्वरूपतः ॥ ३५॥

English
The grace of the lord varies in accordance with the purity of emotions. Due to grace, eschewment of rites results. Here the eschewment is of the fruits of rites and not the rites themselves. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्कर्मफलत्यागाच्छिवधर्मान्वयः शुभः ।
स च गुर्वनपेक्षश्च तदपेक्ष इति द्विधा ॥ ३६॥

English
As a result of the abandonment of the desire for the fruits of holy rites, the auspicious Śaivite virtues are attained. This is twofold : that dependent on the preceptor and that which is not. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रानपेक्षात्सापेक्षो मुख्यः शतगुणाधिकः ।
शिवधर्मान्वयस्यास्य शिवज्ञानसमन्वयः ॥ ३७॥

English
That which is dependent on the preceptor is more important and hundred times more efficacious since in the Śaivite creed there is an association of Śaivite knowledge. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानान्वयवशात्पुंसः संसारे दोषदर्शनम् ।
ततो विषयवैराग्यं वैराग्याद्भावसाधनम् ॥ ३८॥

English
In view of the knowledge the man sees the defects in the worldly existence. Thereafter arises non-attachment to the sensual objects and thence Bhāva is attained. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
भावसिद्ध्युपपन्नस्य ध्याने निष्ठा न कर्मणि ।
ज्ञानध्यानाभियुक्तस्य पुंसो योगः प्रवर्तते ॥ ३९॥

English
When Bhāva is attained the man becomes more inclined towards meditation than towards rituals. A man endowed with knowledge and meditation becomes engaged in Yoga. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
योगेन तु परा भक्तिः प्रसादस्तदनन्तरम् ।
प्रसादान्मुच्यते जन्तुर्मुक्तः शिवसमो भवेत् ॥ ४०॥

English
Through Yoga arises the great devotion and then grace of God. By means of the grace the creature is liberated and becomes equal to Śiva. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्रहप्रकारस्य क्रमोऽयमविवक्षितः ।
यादृशी योग्यता पुंसस्तस्य तादृगनुग्रहः ॥ ४१॥

English
The different modes of blessings may not be in the order mentioned. The blessing is based on the ability of the man. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भस्थो मुच्यते कश्चिज्जायमानस्तथापरः ।
बालो वा तरुणो वाथ वृद्धो वा मुच्यते परः ॥ ४२॥

English
Some soul is liberated even while in the womb; another even while being born ; a third whether he be a boy or a youth or an old man. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तिर्यग्योनिगतः कश्चिन्मुच्यते नारकोऽपरः ।
अपरस्तु पदं प्राप्तो मुच्यते स्वपदक्षये ॥ ४३॥

English
A soul born as a lower species, a soul undergoing torture in hell, a soul achieving a heavenly region may be liberated when the tenure is over. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
कश्चित्क्षीणपदो भूत्वा पुनरावर्त्य मुच्यते ।
कश्चिदध्वगतस्तस्मिन् स्थित्वा स्थित्वा विमुच्यते ॥ ४४॥

English
Some soul may return after the enjoyment of heavenly region and then liberated. Still another soul may be liberated on its way. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मान्नैकप्रकारेण नराणां मुक्तिरिष्यते ।
ज्ञानभावानुरूपेण प्रसादेनैव निर्वृतिः ॥ ४५॥

English
Hence there is no stipulated mode or order in the attainment of liberation. The supreme bliss is in accordance with the knowledge and emotion and attained by the grace of the lord. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादस्य प्रसादार्थं वाङ्मनोदोषवर्जिताः ।
ध्यायन्तश्शिवमेवैकं सदारतनयाग्नयः ॥ ४६॥

तन्निष्ठास्तत्पराः सर्वे तद्युक्तास्तदुपाश्रयाः ।
सर्वक्रियाः प्रकुर्वाणास्तमेव मनसा गताः ॥ ४७॥

दीर्घसूत्रसमारब्धं दिव्यवर्षसहस्रकम् ।
सत्रान्ते मन्त्रयोगेन वायुस्तत्र गमिष्यति ॥ ४८॥

English
Hence, in order to win his grace you shall avoid verbal and mental defects; meditate on Śiva alone along with your wives, sons and others ; abide by him; be attached to him. You shall associate with him, resort to him, do holy rites with your minds fixed in him, continue a long sacrifice for a thousand divine years. At the end of the sacrifice due to the efficacy of the mantras Vāyu will come there. (46-48.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव भवतां श्रेयः सोपायं कथयिष्यति ।
ततो वाराणसी पुण्या पुरी परमशोभना ॥ ४९॥

गन्तव्या यत्र विश्वेशो देव्या सह पिनाकधृक् ।
सदा विहरति श्रीमान् भक्तानुग्रहकारणात् ॥ ५०॥

English
He will tell you what is good for you along with the means. Then you shall go to the holy and auspicious city of Vārāṇasī where the glorious trident-bearing lord sports about with the goddess for blessing his devotees. (49-50.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्राश्चर्यं महद् दृष्ट्वा मत्समीपं गमिष्यथ ।
ततो वः कथयिष्यामि मोक्षोपायं द्विजोत्तमाः ॥ ५१॥

English
O excellent brahmins, after seeing the great wonder therein you come to me again. Then I shall tell you the means of salvation. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
येनैकजन्मना मुक्तिर्युष्मत्करतले स्थिता ।
अनेकजन्मसंसारबन्धनिर्मोक्षकारिणी ॥ ५२॥

English
Thereby in a single birth, salvation comes within your reach, salvation which sets you free from the bondage of births and transmigrations. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
एतन्मनोमयं चक्रं मया सृष्टं विसृज्यते ।
यत्रास्य शीर्यते नेमिः स देशस्तपसः शुभः ॥ ५३॥

English
Now I am releasing this mentally created wheel. The place where its rim gets shattered is auspicious for your penance. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा सूर्यसङ्काशं चक्रं दृष्ट्वा मनोमयम् ।
प्रणिपत्य महादेवं विससर्ज पितामहः ॥ ५४॥

English
After saying this Brahmā created a mental wheel as bright as the sun. After bowing to lord Śiva he released it. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
तेऽपि हृष्टतरा विप्राः प्रणम्य जगतां प्रभुम् ।
प्रययुस्तस्य चक्रस्य यत्र नेमिरशीर्यत ॥ ५५॥

English
Those delighted brahmins bowed to the lord and pursued the wheel to the place where its rim got shattered. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रं तदपि सङ्क्षिप्तं श्लक्ष्णं चारुशिलातले ।
विमलस्वादुपानीये निपपात वने क्वचित् ॥ ५६॥

English
The wheel thus hurled fell on a smooth charming rock in a certain forest where the water was sweet and clear. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्वनं तेन विख्यातं नैमिषं मुनिपूजितम् ।
अनेकयक्षगन्धर्वविद्याधरसमाकुलम् ॥ ५७॥

English
That forest became famous as Naimiṣa. It is worshipped by the sages. It abounds in Yakṣas, Gandharvas, and Vidyādharas. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टादश समुद्रस्य द्वीपानश्नन्पुरूरवाः ।
विलासवशमुर्वश्या यातो दैवेन चोदितः ॥ ५८॥

अक्रमेणाहरन्मोहाद्यज्ञवाटं हिरण्मयम् ।
मुनिभिर्यत्र सङ्क्रुद्धैः कुशवज्रैर्निपातितः ॥ ५९॥

English
Indulging in sexual dalliance with Urvaśī, Purūravas was moving about in the eighteen islands in the ocean. Urged by fate he came to this place and foolishly spoiled the golden sacrificial altar here. The infuriated sages hurled Kuśa grass at him which had the power of thunderbolt and felled him to the ground. (58-59.)

Sanskrit
विश्वं सिसृक्षमाणा वै यत्र विश्वसृजः पुरा ।
सत्रमारेभिरे दिव्यं ब्रह्मज्ञा गार्हपत्यगाः ॥ ६०॥

English
Formerly the Viśvasrja Brahmeśa householders desirous of creating the universe began a divine sacrifice here. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिभिर्यत्र विद्वद्भिः शब्दार्थन्यायकोविदैः ।
शक्तिप्रज्ञाक्रियायोगैर्विधिरासीदनुष्ठितः ॥ ६१॥

English
There had been many scholarly sages here experts in words, meanings and logical arguments. They performed rites by their power of intellect and yogic practice. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र वेदविदो नित्यं वेदवादबहिष्कृतान् ।
वादजल्पबलैर्घ्नन्ति वचोभिरतिवादिनः ॥ ६२॥

English
Here persons well-versed in the Vedas refute those who are excluded from Vedic path by their power of arguments. They speak eloquently. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
स्फटिकमयमहीभृत्पादजाभ्यश्शिलाभ्यः
प्रसरदमृतकल्पस्वच्छपानीयरम्यम् ।
अतिरसफलवृक्षप्रायमव्यालसत्त्वं
तपस उचितमासीन्नैमिषं तन्मुनीनाम् ॥ ६३॥

English
This Naimisa forest was the fitting place for the penance of the sages. It was beautiful with nectarine clear water flowing from the bottom rocks of the crystalline mountain. It abounded in fruit-bearing trees and was free from beasts of prey. (63.)
