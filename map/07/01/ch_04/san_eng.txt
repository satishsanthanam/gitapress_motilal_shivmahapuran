### **CHAPTER FOUR - (The advent of Vāyu)**

---

Sanskrit
४

सूत उवाच ।
तस्मिन्देशे महाभागा मुनयः शंसितव्रताः ।
अर्चयन्तो महादेवं सत्रमारेभिरे तदा ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said :—  The fortunate sages of devout rites worshipped lord Siva and began their sacrifice there. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्च सत्रं प्रववृते सर्वाश्चर्यं महर्षिणाम् ।
विश्वं सिसृक्षमाणानां पुरा विश्वसृजामिव ॥ २॥

English
The sacrifice of the sages functioned evoking wonder in everyone like that of the Visvasrjas formerly who were desirous of creating the universe. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ काले गते सत्रे समाप्ते भूरिदक्षिणे ।
पितामहनियोगेन वायुस्तत्रागमत्स्वयम् ॥ ३॥

English
After sometime when the Satra had concluded with the distribution of manifold gifts Vāyu himself came there at the instance of Brahma. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिष्यः स्वयम्भुवो देवः सर्वप्रत्यक्षदृग्वशी ।
आज्ञायां मरुतो यस्य संस्थिताः सप्तसप्तकाः ॥ ४॥

प्रेरयञ्छश्वदङ्गानि प्राणाद्याभिः स्ववृत्तिभिः ।
सर्वभूतशरीराणां कुरुते यश्च धारणम् ॥ ५॥

अणिमादिभिरष्टाभिरैश्वर्यैश्च समन्वितः ।
तिर्यक् करादिभिर्मेध्यैर्भुवनानि बिभर्ति यः ॥ ६॥

आकाशयोनिर्द्विगुणः स्पर्शशब्दसमन्वयात् ।
तेजसां प्रकृतिश्चेति यमाहुस्तत्त्वचिन्तकाः ॥ ७॥

English
Vāyu the disciple of Brahma, the self-controlled lord who perceives everything directly; in whose bidding stay the fortynine Maruts$^{187}$ always; who sustains the bodies of all living beings urging them perpetually by his own functionaries Pāṇa and others; who is endowed with the eightfold glories; who supports the worlds with his holy hands; who is born of Ākāsa; who possesses the two qualities of touch and sound and whom the philosophers call the material cause of fiery principle. (4-7.)

---

Sanskrit
तमाश्रमगतं दृष्ट्वा मुनयो दीर्घसत्रिणः ।
पितामहवचः स्मृत्वा प्रहर्षमतुलं ययुः ॥ ८॥

English
On seeing him at the hermitage the sages who were busy in a long Satra remembered the words of Brahma and felt unequalled pleasure. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्युत्थाय ततः सर्वे प्रणम्याम्बरसम्भवम् ।
चामीकरमयं तस्मै विष्टरं समकल्पयन् ॥ ९॥

English
Standing up they welcomed and bowed to him. They offered him a golden seat. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि तत्र समासीनो मुनिभिः सम्यगर्चितः ।
प्रतिनन्द्य च तान् सर्वान् पप्रच्छ कुशलं ततः ॥ १०॥

English
He seated himself there and was worshipped by the sages. Congratulating them he enquired after their health. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
अत्र वः कुशलं विप्राः कच्चिद्वृत्ते महाक्रतौ ।
कच्चिद्यज्ञहनो दैत्या न बाधेरन्सुरद्विषः ॥ ११॥

English
*Vāyu said:—*  O brahmins, hope you are all quite well, now that this great sacrifice has concluded. Hope that the Asuras, the enemies of the gods, the destroyers of sacrifices do not harass you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रायश्चित्तं दुरिष्टं वा न कश्चित्समजायत ।
स्तोत्रमन्त्रजपैर्देवान् पितृन् पित्र्यैश्च कर्मभिः ॥ १२॥

कच्चिदभ्यर्च्य युष्माभिर्विधिरासीत्स्वनुष्ठितः ।
निवृत्ते च महासत्रे पश्चात्किं वश्चिकीर्षितम् ॥ १३॥

English
Hope that imprecations and expiatory rites do not take place. Hope that the rites are duly performed by you after worshipping the gods, with Stotra and Śastra hymns and the ancestors with the rites due to them. What do you propose to do afterwards, now that the great Satra has been concluded? (12-13.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्ता मुनयः सर्वे वायुना शिवभाविना ।
प्रहृष्टमनसः पूताः प्रत्यूचुर्विनयान्विताः ॥ १४॥

English
When addressed thus by Vāyu, the meditator on Śiva, the sages were delighted in their minds. They considered themselves sanctified. They replied thus humbly. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
अद्य नः कुशलं सर्वमद्य साधु भवेत्तपः ।
अस्मच्छ्रेयोऽभिवृद्ध्यर्थं भवानत्रागतो यतः ॥ १५॥

English
Today we have attained bliss; our penances are rendered fruitful since you have come for the increase of our welfare. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु चेदं पुरावृत्तं तमसाक्रान्तमानसैः ।
उपासितः पुरास्माभिर्विज्ञानार्थं प्रजापतिः ॥ १६॥

English
Listen to an old anecdote. Formerly we meditated on Prajāpati when we were overwhelmed by darkness. We wanted to acquire perfect knowledge. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽप्यस्माननुगृह्याह शरण्यः शरणागतान् ।
सर्वस्मादधिको रुद्रो विप्राः परमकारणम् ॥ १७॥

English
The lord Brahmā worthy of being sought refuge in, blessed us as we sought refuge in him and said, “O brahmins, Lord Rudra is superior to all. He is the ultimate cause. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तमप्रतर्क्यं याथात्म्यं भक्तिमानेव पश्यति ।
भक्तिश्चास्य प्रसादेन प्रसादादेव निर्वृतिः ॥ १८॥

English
Only the devotee sees the lord whose real nature cannot be reflected upon or argued over. Devotion is acquired through grace and through grace is bliss attained. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादस्य प्रसादार्थं नैमिषे सत्रयोगतः ।
यजध्वं दीर्घसत्रेण रुद्रं परमकारणम् ॥ १९॥

English
Hence, in order to propitiate him perform the Satra. Worship him who is the ultimate cause, perform Satra in Naimiṣa extending over a long period. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्रसादेन सत्रान्ते वायुस्तत्रागमिष्यति ।
तन्मुखाज्ज्ञानलाभो वस्तत्र श्रेयो भविष्यति ॥ २०॥

English
At the end of the Satra, by means of his grace Vāyu will come there. You will acquire perfect knowledge from his mouth and attain welfare. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यादिश्य वयं सर्वे प्रेषिताः परमेष्ठिना ।
अस्मिन्देशे महाभाग तवागमनकाङ्क्षिणः ॥ २१॥

English
Commanding thus Brahmā sent us all to this place. O fortunate one, we were eagerly awaiting your arrival. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दीर्घसत्रं समासीना दिव्यवर्षसहस्रकम् ।
अतस्तवागमादन्यत्प्रार्थ्यं नो नास्ति किञ्चन ॥ २२॥

English
We sat and performed the Satra for a thousand divine years. We had nothing to wish for except your advent. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्याकर्ण्य पुरावृत्तमृषीणां दीर्घसत्रिणाम् ।
वायुः प्रीतमना भूत्वा तत्रासीन्मुनिसंवृतः ॥ २३॥

English
On hearing thus this story of the sages who had performed the satra for a long time Vāyu was delighted in his mind and stayed there surrounded by the sages. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तैर्मुनिभिः पृष्टस्तेषां भावविवृद्धये ।
सर्गादि शार्वमैश्वर्यं समासादवदद् विभुः ॥ २४॥

English
On being implored by the sages, the lord succinctly narrated the glory of Siva beginning with creation in order to enhance their piety. (24.)
