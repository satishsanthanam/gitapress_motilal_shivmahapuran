### **CHAPTER FIVE - # (The Principles of Śiva cult)**

---

Sanskrit
५

सूत उवाच ।
तत्र पूर्वं महाभागा नैमिषारण्यवासिनः ।
प्रणिपत्य यथान्यायं पप्रच्छुः पवनं प्रभुम् ॥ १॥

English
Sūta said:-  There at the outset the fortunate residents of the Naimisa bowed duly to lord Vāyu and asked him. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नैमिषीया ऊचुः ।
भवान् कथमनुप्राप्तो ज्ञानमीश्वरगोचरम् ।
कथं च शिष्यभावस्ते ब्रह्मणोऽव्यक्तजन्मनः ॥ २॥

English
The sages said:-  How did you, sir, acquire the knowledge of Śiva? Wherefore is your devotion to Śiva, the Brahman whose birth is unmanifest. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
एकोनविंशतिः कल्पो विज्ञेयः श्वेतलोहितः ।
तस्मिन्कल्पे चतुर्वक्त्रः स्रष्टुकामोऽतपत्तपः ॥ ३॥

English
Vāyu said:-  The twenty-first kalpa shall be known as Svetalohita. In that kalpa the four-faced lord, desirous of creative activity performed a penance. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तपसा तेन तीव्रेण तुष्टस्तस्य पिता स्वयम् ।
दिव्यं कौमारमास्थाय रूपं रूपवतां वरः ॥ ४॥

श्वेतो नाम मुनिर्भूत्वा दिव्यां वाचमुदीरयन् ।
दर्शनं प्रददौ तस्मै देवदेवो महेश्वरः ॥ ५॥

English
Delighted by his austere penance his father lord Siva, foremost among the handsome assumed the divine form of a bechelor. He became a sage Sveta and uttering divine words appeared in front of him. (4-5.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा पितरं ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मणोऽधिपतिं पतिम् ।
प्रणम्य परमज्ञानं गायत्र्या सह लब्धवान् ॥ ६॥

English
On seeing his father, Brahma bowed to him and secured perfect knowledge along with Gayatri. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स लब्धविज्ञानो विश्वकर्मा चतुर्मुखः ।
असृजत्सर्वभूतानि स्थावराणि चराणि च ॥ ७॥

English
Having acquired knowledge, the four-faced lord created all living beings—the mobile and immobile. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यतः श्रुत्वाऽमृतं लब्धं ब्रह्मणा परमेश्वरात् ।
ततस्तद्वदनादेव मया लब्धं तपोबलात् ॥ ८॥

English
That immortal knowledge was acquired by me through the power of penance from that face through which it was acquired by Brahmā. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
किं तज्ज्ञानं त्वया लब्धं तथ्यात्तथ्यतरं शुभम् ।
यत्र कृत्वा परां निष्ठां पुरुषः सुखमृच्छति ॥ ९॥

English
The sages said:—  What is that knowledge attained by you—the knowledge that is auspicious, more truthful than the most truthful, abiding by which a man attains happiness. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
पशुपाशपतिज्ञानं यल्लब्धं तु मया पुरा ।
तत्र निष्ठा परा कार्या पुरुषेण सुखार्थिना ॥ १०॥

English
Vāyu said:—  Great and steady devotion shall be applied by a man who wishes for happiness, for the perfect knowledge of the individual soul, illusion and the lord who releases from the bondage. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अज्ञानप्रभवं दुःखं ज्ञानेनैव निवर्त्तते ।
ज्ञानं वस्तुपरिच्छेदो वस्तु च त्रिविधं स्मृतम् ॥ ११॥

अजडं च जडं चैव नियन्तृ च तयोरपि ।
पशुः पाशः पतिश्चेति कथ्यते तत्त्रयं क्रमात् ॥ १२॥

English
Misery arises from ignorance. It is removed by knowledge that is a correct and precise comprehension of objects. Object is twofold: sentient and non-sentient. There is a controller of the two. The three are called Paśu, Pāśa and Pati. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षरं च क्षरं चैव क्षराक्षरपरं तथा ।
तदेतत्त्रितयं भूम्ना कथ्यते तत्त्ववेदिभिः ॥ १३॥

English
On many occasions knowers of reality call the three by Akṣara, Kṣara and Kṣarākṣarapara. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षरं पशुरित्युक्तः क्षरं पाश उदाहृतः ।
क्षराक्षरपरं यत्तत् पतिरित्यभिधीयते ॥ १४॥

English
The Paśu is called Akṣara. The Pāśa is called Kṣara. Pati is called Kṣarākṣarapara. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
किं तच्च क्षरमित्युक्तं किं चाक्षरमुदाहृतम् ।
तयोश्च परमं किं वा तदेतद् ब्रूहि मारुत ॥ १५॥

English
The sages said:—  O Māruta, please mention What is Kṣara? What is Akṣara? What is that which is greater and beyond these two? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
प्रकृतिः क्षरमित्युक्तं पुरुषोऽक्षर उच्यते ।
ताविमौ प्रेरयत्यन्यः स परः परमेश्वरः ॥ १६॥

English
Vāyu said:—  Prakṛti is Kṣara. Puruṣa is Akṣara. The one who urges these two is the greatest Parameśvara. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
कैषा प्रकृतिरित्युक्ता क एष पुरुषो मतः ।
अनयोः केन सम्बन्धः कोऽयं प्रेरक ईश्वरः ॥ १७॥

English
The sages said:—  What is this Prakṛti? Who is Puruṣa? What is the relationship between the two? Who is this instigator Īśvara? (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
मायाप्रकृतिरुद्दिष्टा पुरुषो माययाऽऽवृतः ।
सम्बन्धो मलकर्मभ्यां शिवः प्रेरक ईश्वरः ॥ १८॥

English
Vāyu said:—  Māyā is the Prakṛti. Puruṣa is enveloped by Māyā. The relationship is through the root and Karman. Lord Śiva is the instigator. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
केयं माया समाख्याता किंरूपो मायया वृतः ।
मलं कीदृक् कुतो वास्य किं शिवत्वं कुतश्शिवः ॥ १९॥

English
The sages said:—  What is this Māyā that is mentioned? Of what form is he who is enveloped by Māyā? Of what nature is the root? Whence is it ? What is Śivahood ? Whence is Śiva ? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
माया माहेश्वरी शक्तिश्चिद्रूपो मायया वृतः ।
मलश्चिच्छादको नैजो विशुद्धिः शिवता स्वतः ॥ २०॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Māyā is the Śakti of Maheśvara. The form of Cit is enveloped by Māyā. The dirt is that which covers the Cit. Śivatva is his own innate purity. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
आवृणोति कथं माया व्यापिनं केन हेतुना ।
किमर्थं चावृतिः पुंसः केन वा विनिवर्तते ॥ २१॥

English
The sages said :—  How does the Māyā cover up the pervading one? What for? Why is this covering up of the Purusa ? By what is it made to recede ? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
आवृतिर्व्यापिनोऽपि स्याद्व्यापि यस्मात्कलाद्यपि ।
हेतुः कर्मैव भोगार्थं निवर्तेत मलक्षयात् ॥ २२॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Even a pervasive can be covered just as Kalā etc. that are pervasive. The cause is Karman alone. The purpose is enjoyment. It recedes when dirt is quelled. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
कलादि कथ्यते किं तत्कर्म वा किमुदाहृतम् ।
तत्किमादि किमन्तं वा किम्फलं वा किमाश्रयम् ॥ २३॥

कस्मै भोगेश्च किं भोग्यं किं वा तद्भोगसाधनम् ।
मलक्षयस्य को हेतुः कीदृक् क्षीणमलः पुमान् ॥ २४॥

English
The sages said :—  What are these Kalā etc. ? What is Karman ? What its beginning and what its end ? What the fruit ? What the support ? Whose enjoyment ? What is enjoyed What is the means of enjoyment, the cause of the diminution of dirt ? Of what nature is the Puruṣa devoid of dirt ? (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
कला विद्या च रागश्च कालो नियतिरेव च ।
कलादयः समाख्याता यो भोक्ता पुरुषो भवेत् ॥ २५॥

पुण्यपापात्मकं कर्म सुखदुःखफलं तु यत् ।
अनादिमलभोगान्तमज्ञानात्मसमाश्रयम् ॥ २६॥

भोगः कर्मविनाशाय भोग्यमव्यक्तमुच्यते ।
बाह्यान्तःकरणद्वारं शरीरं भोगसाधनम् ॥ २७॥

भावातिशयलब्धेन प्रसादेन मलक्षयः ।
क्षीणे चात्ममले तस्मिन् शिवसमो भवेत् ॥ २८॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Kalā, Vidyā, Rāga, Kāla and Niyati, these are certain entities The enjoyer is Purusa, Karman is merit and sin. The fruits are happiness and misery. The beginningless dirt till the enjoyment rests in the ignorant soul. Enjoyment is for quelling Karman. The unmanifest is what is enjoyed. The means of enjoyment is the body with external and the internal senses as doors. Dirt is quelled by grace acquired by piety. When the dirt is quelled the Pūrusa becomes equal to Śiva. (25-28.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
कलादिपञ्चतत्त्वानां किं कर्म पृथगुच्यते ।
भोक्तेति पुरुषश्चेति येनात्मा व्यपदिश्यते ॥ २९॥

English
The sages said :—  What are the several functions of the five principles Kalā etc. ? Why is Ātman called the enjoyer and Puruṣa ? (29.)

---

Sanskrit
किमात्मकं तदव्यक्तं केनाकारेण भुज्यते ।
किं तस्य शरणं भुक्तौ शरीरं च किमुच्यते ॥ ३०॥

English
Of what nature is that unmanifest ? In what form is it enjoyed ? What is its instrument in the act of enjoyment ? What is it called body ? (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
दिक्क्रियाव्यञ्जिका विद्याकलो रागः प्रवर्तकः ।
कालोऽवच्छेदकस्तत्र नियतिस्तु नियामिका ॥ ३१॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Vidyā (learning) that reveals the place and activity, time and passion these are the inducing agents. Time is the conditioning factor there ; destiny is the controlling factor. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
अव्यक्तं कारणं यत्तत्त्रिगुणप्रभवाप्ययम् ।
प्रधानं प्रकृतिश्चेति यदाहुस्तत्त्वचिन्तकाः ॥ ३२॥

English
The unmanifest is the cause. It consists of three Guṇas. It is the source of origin and the place of merger. It is called Pradhāna and Prakṛti by philosophers. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
कलातस्तदभिव्यक्तमनभिव्यक्तलक्षणम् ।
सुखदुःखविमोहात्मा भुज्यते गुणवांस्त्रिधा ॥ ३३॥

English
Characteristically unmanifest it becomes manifest through Kalās. It is of the form and content of happiness, misery and delusion while being enjoyed. It has the three Guṇas. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसम्भवाः ।
प्रकृतौ सूक्ष्मरूपेण तिले तैलमिव स्थिताः ॥ ३४॥

English
The Guṇas Sattva, Rājas and Tāmas, originate from Prakṛti. Like oil in the gingelly seed they are present in the latest form in Prakṛti. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सुखं च सुखहेतुश्च समासात्सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ।
राजसं तद्विपर्यासात् स्तम्भमोहौ तु तामसौ ॥ ३५॥

सात्त्विक्यूर्ध्वगतिः प्रोक्ता तामसी स्यादधोगतिः ।
मध्यमा तु गतिर्या सा राजसी परिपठ्यते ॥ ३६॥

English
Happiness and its cause constitute briefly the Sātttvaic trait. The Rājasaic trait is its opposite. Stunned state and delusion are Tāmasic traits. The Sāttvaic movement is upward progress; the Tāmasic one is downfall ; the middle course is cited as Rājasaic. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्मात्रापञ्चकं चैव भूतपञ्चकमेव च ।
ज्ञानेन्द्रियाणि पञ्चैव पञ्चकर्मेन्द्रियाणि च ॥ ३७॥

प्रधानबुद्ध्यहङ्कारमनांसि च चतुष्टयम् ।
समासादेवमव्यक्तं सविकारमुदाहृतम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Briefly the unmanifest with its ramifications is as follows :—five subtle and primary elements, five gross elements, five sense-organs of activity and the set of four, viz. Pradhāna, intellect, ego and mind. (37-38.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्कारणदशापन्नमव्यक्तमिति कथ्यते ।
व्यक्तं कार्यदशापन्नं शरीरादिघटादिवत् ॥ ३९॥

English
When it is in the state of a cause, it is unmanifest ; when it is in the state of an effect such as the body, pot etc, it is manifest. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा घटादिकं कार्यं मृदादेर्नातिभिद्यते ।
शरीरादि तथा व्यक्तमव्यक्तान्नातिभिद्यते ॥ ४०॥

English
Just as the pot is not materially different from clay, so the manifest, body etc. is not materially different from the unmanifest. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादव्यक्तमेवैकं कारणं करणानि च ।
शरीरं च तदाधारं तद्भोग्यं चापि नेतरत् ॥ ४१॥

English
Hence the unmanifest alone is the cause of the composite viz. organs, body but their 'support, the object of enjoyment is unmanifest. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
मुनय ऊचुः ।
बुद्धीन्द्रियशरीरेभ्यो व्यतिरेकस्य कस्यचित् ।
आत्मशब्दाभिधेयस्य वस्तुतोऽपि कुतः स्थितिः ॥ ४२॥

English
The sages said :—  How can Ātman which is separate from the sense-organs and the body exist ? (42.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
बुद्धीन्द्रियशरीरेभ्यो व्यतिरेको विभोर्ध्रुवम् ।
अस्त्येव कश्चिदात्मेति हेतुस्तत्र सुदुर्गमः ॥ ४३॥

English
Vāyu said :—  The separateness of all-pervading lord from the intellect, sense-organs and the body is definite. There is something called Ātman. A reason thereof is difficult to understand. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
बुद्धीन्द्रियशरीराणां नात्मता सद्भिरिष्यते ।
स्मृतेरनियतज्ञानादयावद्देहवेदनात् ॥ ४४॥

English
The intellect, the sense-organs and the bodies are not the Ātman due to indefinite knowledge and non-eternality. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः स्मर्तानुभूतानामशेषज्ञेयगोचरः ।
अन्तर्यामीति वेदेषु वेदान्तेषु च गीयते ॥ ४५॥

English
Hence the one who has the cognition of experienced objects and comprehends the entire knowables, is sung about in the Vedas and Upaniṣads as the immanent soul. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं तत्र स सर्वत्र व्याप्य तिष्ठति शाश्वतः ।
तथापि क्वापि केनापि व्यक्तमेष न दृश्यते ॥ ४६॥

English
It is eternal. It incorporates and pervades everything. It stands everywhere. Still it is not perceived by any one clearly anywhere. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
नैवायं चक्षुषा ग्राह्यो नापरैरिन्द्रियैरपि ।
मनसैव प्रदीप्तेन महानात्मावसीयते ॥ ४७॥

English
This Ātman cannot be perceived by the eye nor by any other sense-organ. The great Ātman is comprehended only by the enlightened mind. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
न च स्त्री न पुमानेष नैव चापि नपुंसकः ।
नैवोर्ध्वं नापि तिर्यक् च नाधस्तान्न कुतश्चन ॥ ४८॥

English
It is neither woman nor man nor an eunuch. It is neither above nor below nor in the sides. It is nowhere. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अशरीरं शरीरेषु चलेषु स्थाणुमव्ययम् ।
सदा पश्यति तं धीरो नरः प्रत्यवमर्शनात् ॥ ४९॥

English
It remains unembodied in the unsteady bodies. It is stable and steady like a stump. It is unchanging. Only the sober, self-possessed and bold can perceive it by means of reflection. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
किमत्र बहुनोक्तेन पुरुषो देहतः पृथक् ।
अपृथग्ये तु पश्यन्ति ह्यसम्यक् तेषु दर्शनम् ॥ ५०॥

English
Of what avail is much talk? The Puruṣa is separate from the body. Unwarranted is the vision of those who do not see it separate. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
यच्छरीरमिदं प्रोक्तं पुरुषस्य ततः परम् ।
अशुद्धमवशं दुःखमध्रुवं न च विद्यते ॥ ५१॥

English
There is nothing more impure, uncontrolled, miserable and uncertain than the body of the Puruṣa. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
विपदां बीजभूतेन पुरुषस्तेन संयुतः ।
सुखी दुःखी च मूढश्च भवति स्वेन कर्मणा ॥ ५२॥

English
The Puruṣa becomes happy, miserable or delud on becoming associated with the body—the seed of all adversities caused by his own activities. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्भिराप्लावितं क्षेत्रं जनयत्यङ्कुरं यथा ।
अज्ञानात्प्लावितं कर्म देहं जनयते तथा ॥ ५३॥

English
As the field flooded with water generates sprouts so, also the action flooded with ignorance generates the man. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्यन्तमसुखावासाः स्मृताश्चैकान्तमृत्यवः ।
अनागता अतीताश्च तनवोऽस्य सहस्रशः ॥ ५४॥

English
Thousands in number are his bodies both of the past and the future. These are mortals, abodes of excessive distress. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
आगत्यागत्य शीर्णेषु शरीरेषु शरीरिणः ।
अत्यन्तवसतिः क्वापि न केनापि च लभ्यते ॥ ५५॥

English
A perpetual stay in the bodies that successively come to the embodied soul and get shattered, is never attained by any one. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
छादितश्च वियुक्तश्च शरीरैरेषु लक्ष्यते ।
चन्द्रबिम्बवदाकाशे तरलैरभ्रसञ्चयैः ॥ ५६॥

English
Covered by and separated from these bodies this soul resembles the disc of the moon in the sky that is covered by and separated from the passing clouds. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेकदेहभेदेन भिन्ना वृत्तिरिहात्मनः ।
अष्टापदपरिक्षेपे ह्यक्षमुद्रेव लक्ष्यते ॥ ५७॥

English
The different activities of the soul in the different bodies resemble the motion of the die in the different squares in the chess-board. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
नैवास्य भविता कश्चिन्नासौ भवति कस्यचित् ।
पथि सङ्गम एवायं दारैः पुत्रैश्च बन्धुभिः ॥ ५८॥

English
None belongs to him. He does not belong to anyone. The contact with wives, sons and kinsmen is but a chance meeting on the way. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा काष्ठं च काष्ठं च समेयातां महोदधौ ।
समेत्य च व्यपेयातां तद्वद् भूतसमागमः ॥ ५९॥

English
The mutual contact of living beings is like that of a piece of log with another log in the great ocean. The two meet together and get separated.[188] (59.)

---

Sanskrit
स पश्यति शरीरं तच्छरीरं तन्न पश्यति ।
तौ पश्यति परः कश्चित्तावुभौ तं न पश्यतः ॥ ६०॥

English
He sees the body but the body does not see him. A certain other being, the great Ātman sees both but both of them do not see him. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माद्याः स्थावरान्ताश्च पशवः परिकीर्तिताः ।
पशूनामेव सर्वेषां प्रोक्तमेतन्निदर्शनम् ॥ ६१॥

English
All living beings beginning with Brahma and ending with the immobile are termed Paus. Examples cited relate to these Paus. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
स एष बध्यते पाशैः सुखदुःखाशनः पशुः ।
लीलासाधनभूतो य ईश्वरस्येति सूरयः ॥ ६२॥

English
These Paus are bound with Pasa (noose) and are fed upon joy and misery as their fodder. The wise say that these are but instruments of games and sports of lord Siva. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
अज्ञो जन्तुरनीशोऽयमात्मनः सुखदुःखयोः ।
ईश्वरप्रेरितो गच्छेत्स्वर्गं वा श्वभ्रमेव वा ॥ ६३॥

English
The ignorant creature cannot effect and prevent his happiness and misery. Induced by the lord he goes up to the heaven or falls into the deep ditch. (63.)
