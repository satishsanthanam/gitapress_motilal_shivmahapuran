### **CHAPTER SIX - (The Principle of Śiva cult)**

---

Sanskrit
६

मुनय ऊचुः ।
योऽयं पशुरिति प्रोक्तो यश्च पाश उदाहृतः ।
आभ्यां विलक्षणः कश्चित्कोऽयमस्ति तयोः पतिः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  You have explained what is Paśu and what is Pāśa. Now mention what is their lord different in characteristics from the two? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
अस्ति कश्चिदपर्यन्तरमणीयगुणाश्रयः ।
पतिर्विश्वस्य निर्माता पशुपाशविमोचनः ॥ २॥

English
Vāyu said:—  There is a certain releaser of Paśu from Pāsa. He is Pati, the creator of the universe. He is the abode of endless charming attributes. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अभावे तस्य विश्वस्य सृष्टिरेषा कथं भवेत् ।
अचेतनत्वादज्ञानादनयोः पशुपाशयोः ॥ ३॥

English
In his absence how could the universe have been created, since Pasa is insentient and the Pasa is ignorant? (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधानपरमाण्वादि यावत्किञ्चिदचेतनम् ।
तत्कर्तृकं स्वयं दृष्टं बुद्धिमत्कारणं विना ॥ ४॥

English
Without an intelligent cause nothing has ever been seen anywhere created by Pradhana, Paramanu or any such insentient being. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
जगच्च कर्तृसापेक्षं कार्यं सावयवं यतः ।
तस्मात्कार्यस्य कर्तृत्वं पत्युर्न पशुपाशयोः ॥ ५॥

English
Since the universe is an effect possessing parts it is dependent on a creator. And creativity can be only in Pati and not in Paśu or Pāsa. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पशोरपि च कर्तृत्वं पत्युः प्रेरणपूर्वकम् ।
अयथाकरणज्ञानमन्धस्य गमनं यथा ॥ ६॥

English
Creativity of Paśu without the knowledge of creation like the going of a blind man is due to the inducement of his lord. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मानं च पृथङ्मत्वा प्रेरितारं ततः पृथक् ।
असौ जुष्टस्ततस्तेन ह्यमृतत्वाय कल्पते ॥ ७॥

English
Realising distinction between him and himself and being gratified after resorting to him Paśu can attain immortality. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
पशोः पाशस्य पत्युश्च तत्त्वतोऽस्ति पदं परम् ।
ब्रह्मवित् तद्विदित्वैव योनिमुक्तो भविष्यति ॥ ८॥

English
The sphere of Pati is really greater than and beyond that of Paśu and Pāsa. The knower of Brahman becomes free from births by knowing him alone. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
संयुक्तमेतद् द्वितयं क्षरमक्षरमेव च ।
व्यक्ताव्यक्तं बिभर्तीशो विश्वं विश्वविमोचकः ॥ ९॥

English
Lord the redeemer of the universe sustains the universe of Kṣara and Akṣara, the two in unison, the manifest and the unmanifest. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
भोक्ता भोग्यं प्रेरयिता मन्तव्यं त्रिविधं स्मृतम् ।
नातः परं विजानद्भिर्वेदितव्यं हि किञ्चन ॥ १०॥

English
The enjoyer, the enjoyed and the instigator—the three alone shall be distinguished. There is nothing else to be known by the seekers of knowledge. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
तिलेषु वा यथा तैलं दध्नि वा सर्पिरर्पितम् ।
यथापः स्रोतसि व्याप्ता यथारण्यां हुताशनः ॥ ११॥

एवमेव महात्मानमात्मन्यात्मविलक्षणम् ।
सत्येन तपसा चैव नित्ययुक्तोऽनुपश्यति ॥ १२॥

English
The man endowed with truth and penance sees in his Ātman the great Ātman like the oil in the gingelly seed or the ghee in the curd or water in the current or the fire in the Araṇi. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
य एको जालवानीश ईशानीभिः स्वशक्तिभिः ।
सर्वांल्लोकानिमान् कृत्वा एक एव स ईशते ॥ १३॥

English
He is the lord born alone. Associated with Isānis, his Saktis he creates the worlds and rules over them. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
एक एव सदा रुद्रो न द्वितीयोऽस्ति कश्चन ।
संसृज्य विश्वभुवनं गोप्ता ते सञ्चुकोच यः ॥ १४॥

English
He is always alone. There is none second to him.[189] After creating the worlds he protects them and withdraws them. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वतश्चक्षुरेवायमुतायं विश्वतोमुखः ।
तथैव विश्वतोबाहुर्विश्वतः पादसंयुतः ॥ १५॥

English
He has eyes, faces and feet all round.[190] (15.)

---

Sanskrit
द्यावाभूमी च जनयन् देव एको महेश्वरः ।
स एव सर्वदेवानां प्रभवश्चोद्भवस्तथा ॥ १६॥

English
The sole lord and creator of the heaven and earth is Mahesvara. He is the origin and the place of merger of all the gods.[191] (16.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्यगर्भं देवानां प्रथमं जनयेदयम् ।
विश्वस्मादधिको रुद्रो महर्षिरिति हि श्रुतिः ॥ १७॥

English
The Sruti says that Rudra the great sage is superior to all; he creates Hiranyagarbha the first among the deities.[192] (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदाहमेतं पुरुषं महान्तममृतं ध्रुवम् ।
आदित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात्संस्थितं प्रभुम् ॥ १८॥

English
I know this great Puruṣa, the undying, steady, having the colour of the sun, the lord stationed beyond darkness.[193] (18.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्मान्नास्ति परं किञ्चिदपरं परमात्मनः ।
नाणीयोऽस्ति न च ज्यायस्तेन पूर्णमिदं जगत् ॥ १९॥

English
There is nothing greater, subtler and grosser than this. The universe is filled by him. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वाननशिरोग्रीवः सर्वभूतगुहाशयः ।
सर्वव्यापी च भगवांस्तस्मात्सर्वगतश्शिवः ॥ २०॥

English
He has all faces, heads and necks. He lies in the cavity of the heart of all. He is all-pervading and present everywhere. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतः पाणिपादोऽयं सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखः ।
सर्वतः श्रुतिमांल्लोके सर्वमावृत्य तिष्ठति ॥ २१॥

English
He has hands, feet, eyes, heads, ears and faces all around. He stands enveloping everything in the world. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेन्द्रियगुणाभासः सर्वेन्द्रियविवर्जितः ।
सर्वस्य प्रभुरीशानः सर्वस्य शरणं सुहृत् ॥ २२॥

English
He has all apparent attributes but is devoid of the sense-organs. The lord is the friend and refuge of all. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
अचक्षुरपि यः पश्यत्यकर्णोऽपि श‍ृणोति यः ।
सर्वं वेत्ति न वेत्तास्य तमाहुः पुरुषं परम् ॥ २३॥

English
They call him the great Puruṣa—who sees without eyes, hears without ears and comprehends all and whom no one knows. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अणोरणीयान्महतो महीयानयमव्ययः ।
गुहायां निहितश्चापि जन्तोरस्य महेश्वरः ॥ २४॥

English
He is subtler than the atom, greater than the greatest.[194] He is the unchanging. He lies in the cavity of the heart of this Paśu. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तमक्रतुं क्रतुप्रायं महिमातिशयान्वितम् ।
धातुः प्रसादादीशानं वीतशोकः प्रपश्यति ॥ २५॥

English
By the grace of the creator, one devoid of sorrows perceives him who is endowed with the excellence of greatness and who does not possess intelligence but is intelligence itself. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
वेदाहमेनमजरं पुराणं सर्वगं विभुम् ।
निरोधं जन्मनो यस्य वदन्ति ब्रह्मवादिनः ॥ २६॥

English
I know the unaging ancient omnipresent lord whose birth is denied by those who can expound the knowledge of the Brahman.¹⁹⁵ (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एकोऽपि त्रीनिमांल्लोकान् बहुधा शक्तियोगतः ।
विदधाति विचेत्यन्ते विश्वमादौ महेश्वरः ॥ २७॥

English
After annihilating in the end, the lord creates the universe again from the beginning in association with his manifold Śaktis. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वधात्रीत्यजाख्या च शैवी चित्राकृतिः परा ।
तामजां लोहितां शुक्लां कृष्णामेकां त्वजः प्रजाम् ॥ २८॥

जनित्रीमनुशेतेऽन्योजुषमाणः स्वरूपिणीम् ।
तामेवाजामजोऽन्यस्तु भक्तभोगां जहाति च ॥ २९॥

English
The Māyā of Śiva is the unborn mother of the universe and has variegated colours and shapes. It is white, red and black. She, the unborn, gives birth to man. He, the unborn, resorts to it and gets involved in distress Another Aja, the liberated soul, eschews her after she has been enjoyed.¹⁹⁶ (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वौ सुपर्णौ च सयुजौ समानं वृक्षमास्थितौ ।
एकोऽत्ति पिप्पलं स्वादु परोऽनश्नन् प्रपश्यति ॥ ३०॥

English
Two birds resort to the same tree. One eats the sweet fruit, the other simply watches without eating.¹⁹⁷ (30.)

---

Sanskrit
वृक्षेऽस्मिन् पुरुषो मग्नो गुह्यमानश्च शोचति ।
जुष्टमन्यं यदा पश्येदीशं परमकारणम् ॥ ३१॥

तदास्य महिमानं च वीतशोकः सुखी भवेत् ।
छन्दांसि यज्ञाः क्रतवो यद्भूतं भव्यमेव च ॥ ३२॥

English
Sitting on this tree the deluded Puruṣa bewails. When he sees the lord, the ultimate cause, and realises his greatness he is freed from sorrow and becomes happy. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
मायी विश्वं सृजत्यस्मिन्निविष्टो मायया परः ।
मायां तु प्रकृतिं विद्यान्मायिनं तु महेश्वरम् ॥ ३३॥

English
The great Ātman, the Māyin entering this, creates the universe by his Māyā. Māyā shall be known as Prakṛti and Maheśvara as Māyin. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यास्त्ववयवैरेव व्याप्तं सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
सूक्ष्मातिसूक्ष्ममीशानं कललस्यापि मध्यतः ॥ ३४॥

स्रष्टारमपि विश्वस्य चेष्टितारं च तस्य तु ।
शिवमेवेश्वरं ज्ञात्वा शान्तिमत्यन्तमृच्छति ॥ ३५॥

English
The universe is permeated by his parts. The Puruṣa attains perpetual peace and calmness on realising lord Śiva the creator and enveloper of the universe and the subtler of the subtlest, from his very inception in the foetus. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव कालो गोप्ता च विश्वस्याधिपतिः प्रभुः ।
तं विश्वाधिपतिं ज्ञात्वा मृत्युपाशात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३६॥

English
He alone is Kāla, the protector, the lord of universe. On realising him one is released from the noose of death. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
घृतात्परं मण्डमिव सूक्ष्मं ज्ञात्वा स्थितं प्रभुम् ।
सर्वभूतेषु गूढं च सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३७॥

English
On realising the lord pervading all living beings like the subtle cream over the ghee, one is freed from sins. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
एष एव परो देवो विश्वकर्मा महेश्वरः ।
हृदये सन्निविष्टं तं ज्ञात्वैवामृतमश्नुते ॥ ३८॥

English
He alone is the great god Śiva, the creator of the universe. Only on realising him one attains immortality. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा समस्तं न दिवा न रात्रिर्न सदप्यसत् ।
केवलश्शिव एवैको यतः प्रज्ञा पुरातनी ॥ ३९॥

English
When everything was neither existent nor non- existent, when it was neither day nor night, Śiva alone existed from whom the ancient wisdom emanated. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
नैनमूर्ध्वं न तिर्यक्च न मध्यं पर्यजिग्रहत् ।
न तस्य प्रतिमा चास्ति यस्य नाम महद्यशः ॥ ४०॥

English
None can grasp his top, sides or middle. There is nothing resembling him. His is the great fame.[198] (40.)

---

Sanskrit
अजातमिममेवैकं बुद्ध्वा जन्मनि भीरवः ।
रुद्रस्यास्य प्रपद्यन्ते रक्षार्थं दक्षिणं सुखम् ॥ ४१॥

English
Those who are frightened of rebirths meditate on the unborn. For the sake of succour they resort to the front face of Rudra. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वे अक्षरे ब्रह्मपरे त्वनन्ते समुदाहृते ।
विद्याविद्ये समाख्याते निहिते यत्र गूढवत् ॥ ४२॥

English
The two Vidya and Avidya lie hidden in the imperishable, infinite great Brahman. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षरं त्वविद्या ह्यमृतं विद्येति परिगीयते ।
ते उभे ईशते यस्तु सोऽन्यः खलु महेश्वरः ॥ ४३॥

English
Avidya is perishable while Vidya is imperishable. He who rules over the two is lord Siva. He is other than the two. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एकैकं बहुधा जालं विकुर्वन्नेकवच्च यः ।
सर्वाधिपत्यं कुरुते सृष्ट्वा सर्वान् प्रतापवान् ॥ ४४॥

English
It is he who spreads his net in various ways, creates all and rules all. He possesses such exploits. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
दिश ऊर्ध्वमधस्तिर्यक् भासयन् भ्राजते स्वयम् ।
यो निःस्वभावादप्येको वरेण्यस्त्वधितिष्ठति ॥ ४५॥

English
He illuminates the quarters above, below and to the sides, shining himself. He is innately alone, foremost of all who presides over all. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभावं वाचकान् सर्वान् वाच्यांश्च परिणामयन् ।
गुणांश्च भोग्यभोक्तृत्वे तद्विश्वमधितिष्ठति ॥ ४६॥

English
Transforming the attributes, the expressions of nature and expressed ones into what is enjoyed and the one that enjoys, he occupies the universe. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
तं वै गुह्योपनिषदि गूढं ब्रह्म परात्परम् ।
ब्रह्मयोनिं जगत्पूर्वं विदुर्देवा महर्षयः ॥ ४७॥

English
The gods and the sages know him as hidden in the secret Upanisad, who is Brahman, greater than the greatest, the progenitor of Brahma and the cause of the universe. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
भावग्राह्यमनीहाख्यं भावाभावकरं शिवम् ।
कलासर्गकरं देवं ये विदुस्ते जहुस्तनुम् ॥ ४८॥

English
Those who knew the lord eschewed their bodies. The lord can be realised only through piety. He is wishless, the cause of positive and negative substances and the creator of Kalas. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभावमेके मन्यन्ते कालमेके विमोहिताः ।
देवस्य महिमा ह्येष येनेदं भ्राम्यते जगत् ॥ ४९॥

English
It is the greatness of the lord whereby this universe revolves but the deluded people call it a natural occurrence or the power of time. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
येनेदमावृतं नित्यं कालकालात्मना यतः ।
तेनेरितमिदं कर्म भूतैः सह विवर्तते ॥ ५०॥

English
It is by him in the form of the annihilator of death that these are enveloped perpetually, the activities urged by him transform into worldly existence along with the elements. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्कर्म भूयशः कृत्वा विनिवर्त्य च भूयशः ।
तत्त्वस्य सह सत्त्वेन योगं चापि समेत्य वै ॥ ५१॥

अष्टाभिश्च त्रिभिश्चैव द्वाभ्यां चैकेन वा पुनः ।
कालेनात्मगुणैश्चापि कृत्स्नमेव जगत् स्वयम् ॥ ५२॥

गुणैरारभ्य कर्माणि स्वभावादीनि योजयेत् ।
तेषामभावे नाशः स्यात्कृतस्यापि च कर्मणः ॥ ५३॥

English
The individual indulges in activities more and more and returns to the earth. He gets entangled in the tattvas. By means of the fourteen attributes of the Atman he pursues worldly activities. When these activities cease even the results are quelled. (51-53.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्मक्षये पुनश्चान्यत्ततो याति स तत्त्वतः ।
स एवादिः स्वयं योगनिमित्तं भोक्तृभोगयोः ॥ ५४॥

English
When these are quelled he goes to another world. This is the cause of union of the two—the enjoyer and the enjoyed. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
परस्त्रिकालादकलः स एव परमेश्वरः ।
सर्ववित् त्रिगुणाधीशो ब्रह्म साक्षात् परात्परः ॥ ५५॥

English
Lord alone is beyond the three times. He is attributeless, the knower of all, the lord of the three attributes. He is brahman itself greater than the greatest. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
तं विश्वरूपमभवं भवमीड्यं प्रजापतिम् ।

English
We worship and meditate upon the lord of manifold forms, the unborn, lord of people, object of worship and meditation by the entire universe. He is stationed in our minds. (56.)

Sanskrit

English

English

Sanskrit

English
He has neither cause nor effect. Nowhere in the universe there is anyone equal or superior to him. (59.)

Sanskrit

English
It is mentioned in the Vedas that his threefold power is great, manifold and innate, comprising knowledge, strength and activity. It is by these activities that the universe is created. (60.)

Sanskrit

English
He has no lord, no sex and no ruler. He is the cause of causes and the lord of lords. (61.)

Sanskrit

English
He has no progenitor nor does he take birth from anywhere. He has no causes of birth such as Dirt, Māyā etc. (62.)

Sanskrit

English
He is the solitary lord hidden in all living beings.[199] Spread all round he is the innate soul of all living beings. He is mentioned as the presiding deity of all sacred rites. (63.)

English
He is the abode of all living beings, the cosmic witness, the provoker of thoughts, devoid of attributes and the sole lord with self-control, devoid of activities and helpless. (64.)

English
He is the most permanent among the permanent. He is the most sentient among the sentient. (65.)

English
On realising the lord of the universe, comprehensible through Sāṅkhya and Yoga, the cause of the universe the person gets released from all illusions. (66.)

Sanskrit
