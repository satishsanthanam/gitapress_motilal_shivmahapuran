### **CHAPTER NINE - (The creation and sustenance)**

---

Sanskrit
९

मुनय ऊचुः ।
कथं जगदिदं कृत्स्नं विधाय च निधाय च ।
आज्ञया परमां क्रीडां करोति परमेश्वरः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  How does lord Śiva perform his great sport commanding all, creating the universe and placing it in position? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
किं तत्प्रथमसम्भूतं केनेदमखिलं ततम् ।
केन वा पुनरेवेदं ग्रस्यते पृथुकुक्षिणा ॥ २॥

English
What is it that came into being at the outset? By what are all these pervaded? By which being of huge belly is this swallowed? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
शक्तिः प्रथमसम्भूता शान्त्यतीतपदोत्तरा ।
ततो माया ततोऽव्यक्तं शिवाच्छक्तिमतः प्रभोः ॥ ३॥

English
Vāyu said:—  Śakti came into being at the outset; with Śāntya-titapadā following. From Śiva in association with Śakti Māyā came into being and then the unmanifest from it. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शान्त्यतीतपदं शक्तेस्ततः शान्तिपदं क्रमात् ।
ततो विद्यापदं तस्मात्प्रतिष्ठापदसम्भवः ॥ ४॥

English
Śāntyatitapada originated from Śakti and from it Śāntipada; then the Vidyāpada; from it the Pratiṣṭhāpada came into being. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्तिपदमुत्पन्नं प्रतिष्ठापदतः क्रमात् ।
एवमुक्ता समासेन सृष्टिरीश्वरचोदिता ॥ ५॥

English
Nivṛttipada originated from Pratiṣṭhāpada. Thus has been briefly described the creation induced by the lord. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
आनुलोम्यात्तथैतेषां प्रातिलोम्येन संहृतिः ।
अस्मात्पञ्चपदोद्दिष्टात्परः स्रष्टा समिष्यते ॥ ६॥

English
The creation of these is in the inverse order; the anni- hilation is in the reverse order. There is a creator apart from and greater than what is indicated by these five Padas. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
कलाभिः पञ्चभिर्व्याप्तं तस्माद्विश्वमिदं जगत् ।
अव्यक्तं कारणं यत्तदात्मना समनुष्ठितम् ॥ ७॥

English
Hence the entire universe is pervaded by the five Kalās. Avyakta is the cause there but activitised by the Ātman. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
महदादिविशेषान्तं सृजतीत्यपि सम्मतम् ।
किं तु तत्रापि कर्तृत्वं नाव्यक्तस्य न चात्मनः ॥ ८॥

अचेतनत्वात्प्रकृतेरज्ञत्वात्पुरुषस्य च ।
प्रधानपरमाण्वादि यावत्किञ्चिदचेतनम् ॥ ९॥

तत्कर्तृकं स्वयं दृष्टं बुद्धिमत्कारणं विना ।
जगच्च कर्तृसापेक्षं कार्यं सावयवं यतः ॥ १०॥

English
It is agreed that everything begins with intellect and ends with Viśeṣa but the makership is neither of the Avyakta nor of the Puruṣa since Prakṛti is insentient and Puruṣa ignorant. Without an intelligent cause nothing is created by the insentient beings—Pradhāna, Paramāṇu etc. But the universe is dependent on a maker since it is an effect composed of parts. (8-10.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छक्तः स्वतन्त्रो यः सर्वशक्तिश्च सर्ववित् ।
अनादिनिधनश्चायं महदैश्वर्यसंयुतः ॥ ११॥

स एव जगतः कर्ता महादेवो महेश्वरः ।
पाता हर्ता च सर्वस्य ततः पृथगनन्वयः ॥ १२॥

English
Hence the creator must be powerful, independent, omnipotent and omniscient with no beginning, no end and endowed with lordly qualities. He is the maker of the universe, Mahādeva, Mahesvara, the protector and annihilator of everything. He is separate from it. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
परिणामः प्रधानस्य प्रवृत्तिः पुरुषस्य च ।
सर्वं सत्यव्रतस्यैव शासनेन प्रवर्तते ॥ १३॥

English
The transformation of Pradhāna and the activities of the Puruṣa function at the bidding of the lord of truthful vow. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
इतीयं शाश्वती निष्ठा सतां मनसि वर्तते ।
न चैनं पक्षमाश्रित्य वर्तते स्वल्पचेतनः ॥ १४॥

English
It is in the minds of the noble that this firm and permanent conviction finds a place. A being of meagre consciousness does not resort to this side. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यावदादिसमारम्भो यावद्यः प्रलयो महान् ।
तावदप्येति सकलं ब्रह्मणः शारदां शतम् ॥ १५॥

परमित्यायुषो नाम ब्रह्मणोऽव्यक्तजन्मनः ।
तत्पराख्यं तदर्द्धं च परार्धमभिधीयते ॥ १६॥

परार्द्धद्वयकालान्ते प्रलये समुपस्थिते ।
अव्यक्तमात्मनः कार्यमादायात्मनि तिष्ठति ॥ १७॥

English
Hundred Brahmā years constitute the span of Brahmā born of unmanifest It is termed Para. Its half is Parardha. At the advent of Dissolution the Avyakta withdraws to itself all its effects and abides in Ātman. (15-17.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मन्यवस्थितेऽव्यक्ते विकारे प्रतिसंहृते ।
साधर्म्येणाधितिष्ठेते प्रधानपुरुषावुभौ ॥ १८॥

English
When the Avyakta abides in Ātman, when the effects have been withdrawn, Pradhana and Puruṣa stand on an equal footing. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
तमः सत्त्वगुणावेतौ समत्वेन व्यवस्थितौ ।
अनुद्रिक्तावनूनौ तावोतप्रोतौ परस्परम् ॥ १९॥

English
The two have the Guṇas of Tamas and Sattva, remaining on a par with each other. The two without the Guṇas being predominant are mutually interwoven. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
गुणसाम्ये तदा तस्मिन्नविभागे तमोदये ।
शान्तवातैकनीरे च न प्राज्ञायत किञ्चन ॥ २०॥

English
In the equal and undivided state of Guṇas in the rise of darkness, when the wind was in lull and the water was calm, there was nothing to be seen. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
अप्रज्ञाते जगत्यस्मिन्नेक एव महेश्वरः ।
उपास्य रजनीं कृत्स्नां परां माहेश्वरीं ततः ॥ २१॥

प्रभातायां तु शर्वर्यां प्रधानपुरुषावुभौ ।
प्रविश्य क्षोभयामास मायायोगान्महेश्वरः ॥ २२॥

English
When the universe was unmanifest the lord meditated upon the great Śakti throughout the night. When it dawned the lord in contact with Māya entered Pradhāna and Puruṣa and set them to motion. (21-22.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः पुनरशेषाणां भूतानां प्रभवाप्ययात् ।
अव्यक्तादभवत्सृष्टिराज्ञया परमेष्ठिनः ॥ २३॥

English
Then at the bidding of Brahma, the creation evolved out of the unmanifest that is the source of origin and the spot of merger for all living creatures. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वोत्तरोत्तरविचित्रमनोरथस्य
यस्यैकशक्तिशकले सकलः समाप्तः ।
आत्मानमध्वपतिमध्वविदो वदन्ति
तस्मै नमः सकललोकविलक्षणाय ॥ २४॥

English
Obeisance to him who is different from the worlds, in a fraction of whose Sakti everything terminates, whose will is ever variegated and wonderful far above that of the universe and whom those who know the path call the lord of the path—the Ātman. (24.)
