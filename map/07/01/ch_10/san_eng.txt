### **CHAPTER TEN - (The description of creation)**

---

Sanskrit
१०

वायुरुवाच ।
पुरुषाधिष्ठितात्पूर्वमव्यक्तादीश्वराज्ञया ।
बुद्ध्यादयो विशेषान्ता विकाराश्चाभवन् क्रमात् ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said:—  At the outset, at the bidding of Iśvara the secondary elements beginning with the cosmic intellect and ending with Viseşa came into being from the Avyakta presided over by Puruşa. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तेभ्यो विकारेभ्यो रुद्रो विष्णुः पितामहः ।
कारणत्वेन सर्वेषां त्रयो देवाः प्रजज्ञिरे ॥ २॥

English
Then from these, the three deities Rudra, Vişnu and Brahma were born as the cause of all. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वतो भुवनव्याप्तिशक्तिमव्याहतां क्वचित् ।
ज्ञानमप्रतिमं शश्वदैश्वर्यं चाणिमादिकम् ॥ ३॥

सृष्टिस्थितिलयाख्येषु कर्मसु त्रिषु हेतुताम् ।
प्रभुत्वेन सहैतेषां प्रसीदति महेश्वरः ॥ ४॥

English
Mahesvara bestows on these the power to pervade all the worlds unobstructed anywhere, unequalled perfect wisdom, the perpetual Siddhis of Anima etc; and the state of being the cause of activities of creation, sustenance and dissolution. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पान्तरे पुनस्तेषामस्पर्द्धाबुद्धिमोहिनाम् ।
सर्गरक्षालयाचारं प्रत्येकं प्रददौ च सः ॥ ५॥

English
In another Kalpa he conferred on each the activities of creation, sustenance and dissolution in order that they may not have any mutual rivalry due to delusion of intellect. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
एते परस्परोत्पन्ना धारयन्ति परस्परम् ।
परस्परेण वर्द्धन्ते परस्परमनुव्रताः ॥ ६॥

English
These three are born from one another, sustain one another, flourish one another, respect and follow one another. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
क्वचिद्ब्रह्मा क्वचिद्विष्णुः क्वचिद् रुद्रः प्रशस्यते ।
नानेन तेषामाधिक्यमैश्वर्यं चातिरिच्यते ॥ ७॥

English
Somewhere Brahma, somewhere Vişnu and somewhere Rudra are praised. However their glory does not diminish. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मूर्खा निन्दन्ति तान्वाग्भिः संरम्भाभिनिवेशिनः ।
यातुधाना भवन्त्येव पिशाचाश्च न संशयः ॥ ८॥

English
Only impetuous fools censure them. Undoubtedly they become demons and ghosts. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवो गुणत्रयातीतश्चतुर्व्यूहो महेश्वरः ।
सकलः सकलाधारः शक्तेरुत्पत्तिकारणम् ॥ ९॥

English
Lord Siva who is beyond the three Gunas, who is divided into four units, who is all-in-all and the support of all is the cause of creation. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽयमात्मा त्रयस्यास्य प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्य च ।
लीलाकृतजगत्सृष्टिरीश्वरत्वे व्यवस्थितः ॥ १०॥

English
The Atman who has created the universe sportively remains as the lord of three deities as well as of Prakrti and Purusa. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यः सर्वस्मात्परो नित्यो निष्कलः परमेश्वरः ।
स एव च तदाधारस्तदात्मा तदधिष्ठितः ॥ ११॥

English
He alone is their support, he who is greater than all, who is supreme, eternal, devoid of attributes and is their Atman and their presiding deity. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मान्महेश्वरश्चैव प्रकृतिः पुरुषस्तथा ।
सदाशिवो भवो विष्णुर्ब्रह्मा सर्वं शिवात्मकम् ॥ १२॥

English
Hence he alone is Prakrti, Purusa, Sadasiva, Bhava, Visnu and Brahma. Everything is identical with Siva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रधानात्प्रथमं जज्ञे बुद्धिः ख्यातिर्मतिर्महान् ।
महत्तत्त्वस्य सङ्क्षोभादहङ्कारस्त्रिधाऽभवत् ॥ १३॥

English
In the beginning were born the cosmic intellect, the great principle. When it was agitated the ego was born. The ego was split into three.[200] (13.)

---

Sanskrit
अहङ्कारश्च भूतानि तन्मात्राणीन्द्रियाणि च ।
वैकारिकादहङ्कारात्सत्त्वोद्रिक्तात्तु सात्त्विकः ॥ १४॥

English
The ego split into the gross and subtle elements and the sense-organs. When the ego had the Sattva predominant the Sattvika creation took place. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
वैकारिकः स सर्गस्तु युगपत्सम्प्रवर्तते ।
बुद्धीन्द्रियाणि पञ्चैव पञ्चकर्मेन्द्रियाणि च ॥ १५॥

एकादशं मनस्तत्र स्वगुणेनोभयात्मकम् ।
तमोयुक्तादहङ्काराद्भूततन्मात्रसम्भवः ॥ १६॥

English
The secondary creation took place simultaneously. The five sense-organs, the five organs of activity and the eleventh the mind, originated. The mind is both an organ of knowledge and of activity. When the ego becomes associated with Tamas, the Bhutatanmatras are born. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतानामादिभूतत्वाद् भूतादिः कथ्यते तु सः ।
भूतादेः शब्दमात्रं स्यात्तत्र चाकाशसम्भवः ॥ १७॥

English
Since it is the cause of Bhutas it is called Bhutadi. From this is born the Sabdatanmatra, from this the ether. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
आकाशात्स्पर्श उत्पन्नः स्पर्शाद्वायुसमुद्भवः ।
वायो रूपं ततस्तेजस्तेजसो रससम्भवः ॥ १८॥

English
From the ether the Sparsatanmatra, from it Vayu, from Vayu the Rupatanmatra, from this the Tejas, from Tejas the Rasa tanmatra is born. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
रसादापः समुत्पन्नास्ताभ्यो गन्धसमुद्भवः ।
गन्धाच्च पृथिवी जाता भूतेभ्योऽन्यच्चराचरम् ॥ १९॥

English
From Rasa the water; from this the Gandha Tanmatra is born, from this the Prthivi, from these elements the mobile and immobile beings are created. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरुषाधिष्ठितत्वाच्च अव्यक्तानुग्रहेण च ।
महदादिविशेषान्ता ह्यण्डमुत्पादयन्ति ते ॥ २०॥

English
Since they are presided over by Purusa and blessed by Avyakta the principles beginning with Mahat and ending with Visea generate the cosmic egg.[201] (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र कार्यं च करणं संसिद्धं ब्रह्मणो यदा ।
तदण्डे सुप्रवृद्धोऽभूत् क्षेत्रज्ञो ब्रह्मसंज्ञितः ॥ २१॥

English
When thus the activities of Brahma are fulfilled Brahma becomes flourished in that Egg. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
स वै शरीरी प्रथमः स वै पुरुष उच्यते ।
आदिकर्ता स भूतानां ब्रह्माग्रे समवर्तत ॥ २२॥

English
He is the first embodied soul, called Purusa. He is the first creator of living beings. Brahma thus existed in the beginning. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्येश्वरस्याप्रतिमा ज्ञानवैराग्यलक्षणा ।
धर्मैश्वर्यकरी बुद्धिर्ब्राह्मी यज्ञेऽभिमानिनः ॥ २३॥

English
From him was born the intellect, the cause of virtue and prosperity, with the characteristics of wisdom and detachment. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
अव्यक्ताज्जायते तस्य मनसा यद्यदीप्सितम् ।
वशीकृतत्वात्त्रैगुण्यात्सापेक्षत्वात्स्वभावतः ॥ २४॥

त्रिधा विभज्य चात्मानं त्रैलोक्ये सम्प्रवर्त्तते ।
सृजते ग्रसते चैव वीक्षते च त्रिभिः स्वयम् ॥ २५॥

English
Whatever is wished for by the mind is born of Avyakta. He functions in the three worlds, dividing himself into three because he has all in his control, possesses all the attributes and is dependent on Avyakta. In these three forms he creates, supports and annihilates. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्मुखस्तु ब्रह्मत्वे कालत्वे चान्तकः स्मृतः ।
सहस्रमूर्द्धा पुरुषस्तिस्रोऽवस्थास्स्वयम्भुवः ॥ २६॥

English
He is four-faced as creator; as destroyer he is Kāla and as Puruṣa he has thousand heads. The self-born has thus three states. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
सत्त्वं रजश्च ब्रह्मत्वे च कालत्वे च तमो रजः ।
विष्णुत्वे केवलं सत्त्वं गुणवृद्धिस्त्रिधा विभोः ॥ २७॥

English
As Brahma he has Sattva and Rajas, as Kāla Tamas and Rajas, as Visnu he has Sattva alone. Thus the increase of Guṇas in the lord is threefold. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मत्वे सृजते लोकान् कालत्वे सङ्क्षिपत्यपि ।
पुरुषत्वेऽत्युदासीनः कर्म च त्रिविधं विभोः ॥ २८॥

English
As Brahma he creates the worlds, as Kāla he condenses; as Puruṣa he sustains; he is indifferent. Thus the activity of the lord is threefold. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं त्रिधा विभिन्नत्वाद् ब्रह्मा त्रिगुण उच्यते ।
चतुर्द्धा प्रविभक्तत्वाच्चातुर्व्यूहः प्रकीर्तितः ॥ २९॥

English
Since he is divided into three he is called Triguna. Since he has split himself into four he is glorified as Caturvyūha. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
आदित्वादादिदेवोऽसावजातत्वादजः स्मृतः ।
पाति यस्मात्प्रजाः सर्वाः प्रजापतिरिति स्मृतः ॥ ३०॥

English
Since he is the beginning he is called Adideva; since he is unborn he is called Aja; since he protects the subjects he is called Prajapati. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
हिरण्मयस्तु यो मेरुस्तस्योल्बं सुमहात्मनः ।
गर्भोदकं समुद्राश्च जरायुश्चाऽपि पर्वताः ॥ ३१॥

English
The golden Meru is the foetus of that great Atman; the oceans constitute the water of the womb and the mountains constitute the outer skin of the womb. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नण्डे त्विमे लोका अन्तर्विश्वमिदं जगत् ।
चन्द्रादित्यौ सनक्षत्रौ सग्रहौ सह वायुना ॥ ३२॥

English
All these worlds in this universe, the moon, sun, stars, planets and Vāyu are in this cosmic egg. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्भिर्दशगुणाभिस्तु बाह्यतोऽण्डं समावृतम् ।
आपो दशगुणेनैव तेजसा बहिरावृताः ॥ ३३॥

English
The egg is enveloped with waters ten times bigger than it. The waters are enveloped by Tejas ten times bigger. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
तेजो दशगुणेनैव वायुना बहिरावृतम् ।
आकाशेनावृतो वायुः खं च भूतादिनाऽऽवृतम् ॥ ३४॥

English
The Tejas is enveloped by Vāyu ten times bigger. Vāyu is enveloped by Ākāsa and Ākāsa is enveloped by Bhūtādi. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतादिर्महता तद्वदव्यक्तेनावृतो महान् ।
एतैरावरणैरण्डं सप्तभिर्बहिरावृतम् ॥ ३५॥

English
The Bhūtādi is enveloped by the Mahat. The Mahat is enveloped by Avyakta. Thus the egg is enveloped by even coverings. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
एतदावृत्य चान्योऽन्यमष्टौ प्रकृतयः स्थिताः ।
सृष्टिपालनविध्वंसकर्मकर्त्र्यो द्विजोत्तमाः ॥ ३६॥

English
O excellent brahmins, eight Prakrtis, the causes of creation, sustenance and destruction are stationed covering each of these. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं परस्परोत्पन्ना धारयन्ति परस्परम् ।
आधाराधेयभावेन विकारास्तु विकारिषु ॥ ३७॥

English
Thus born of one another, the effects and their causes sustain one another supporting and being supported. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
कूर्मोऽङ्गानि यथा पूर्वं प्रसार्य्य विनियच्छति ।
विकारांश्च तथाऽव्यक्तं सृष्ट्वा भूयो नियच्छति ॥ ३८॥

English
Just as the tortoise spreads its limbs first and then withdraws them, so the Avyakta creates the beings and then withdraws them. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
अव्यक्तप्रभवं सर्वमानुलोम्येन जायते ।
प्राप्ते प्रलयकाले तु प्रातिलोम्येऽनुलीयते ॥ ३९॥

English
Everything originating from Avyakta is born in this order. When the time of dissolution arrives it gets dissolved in the reverse order. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
गुणाः कालवशादेव भवन्ति विषमाः समाः ।
गुणसाम्ये लयो ज्ञेयो वैषम्ये सृष्टिरुच्यते ॥ ४०॥

English
The Guṇas become equal and unequal due to Kāla. Dissolution takes place when there is equilibrium of the Guṇas. When this is upset, creation takes place. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिदं ब्रह्मणो योनिरेतदण्डं घनं महत् ।
ब्रह्मणः क्षेत्रमुद्दिष्टं ब्रह्मा क्षेत्रज्ञ उच्यते ॥ ४१॥

English
Thus this egg, large and thick, is the source of origin of Brahmā. It is called Brahmā’s sphere ; Brahmā is the lord of this. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
इतीदृशानामण्डानां कोट्यो ज्ञेयाः सहस्रशः ।
सर्वगत्वात्प्रधानस्य तिर्य्यगूर्ध्वमधः स्थिताः ॥ ४२॥

English
There are thousands and crores of such eggs stationed at the sides, above and below since Pradhāna is present everywhere. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र तत्र चतुर्वक्त्रा ब्रह्माणो हरयो भवाः ।
सृष्टा प्रधानेन तथा लब्ध्वा शम्भोस्तु सन्निधिम् ॥ ४३॥

English
In the different places there are four-faced Brahmās, Viṣṇus and Rudras created by Pradhāna with the presence of Śiva. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरः परोव्यक्तादण्डमव्यक्तसम्भवम् ।
अण्डाज्जज्ञे विभुर्ब्रह्मा लोकास्तेन कृतास्त्विमे ॥ ४४॥

English
The lord is beyond the manifest. The egg is born of the unmanifest. From that egg is born lord Brahmā and the worlds are created by him. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
अबुद्धिपूर्वः कथितो मयैषः
प्रधानसर्गः प्रथमः प्रवृत्तः ।
आत्यन्तिकश्च प्रलयोऽन्तकाले
लीलाकृतः केवलमीश्वरस्य ॥ ४५॥

English
The primary creation of Pradhāna is without the intervention of the cosmic intellect. The ultimate dissolution is effected sportively by the lord alone at the end. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्तत्स्मृतं कारणमप्रमेयं
ब्रह्म प्रधानं प्रकृतेः प्रसूतिः ।
अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्यं
शुक्लं सुरक्तं पुरुषेण युक्तम् ॥ ४६॥

English
What is called as the unmanifest cause is Pradhāna. Brahmā is the progeny of Prakṛti. The Pradhāna has neither beginning nor middle nor end. Its prowess is infinite. It is white and deep red. It is associated with Puruṣa. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पादकत्वाद्रजसोऽतिरेका
ल्लोकस्य सन्तानविवृद्धिहेतून् ।
अष्टौ विकारानपि चादिकाले
सृष्ट्वा समश्नाति तथान्तकाले ॥ ४७॥

English
When the Rajas predominates it becomes generative. It creates the eight secondary tattvas in the beginning, which cause flourishing of the worlds. In the end it devours them. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृत्यवस्थापितकारणानां
या च स्थितिर्या च पुनः प्रवृत्तिः ।
तत्सर्वमप्राकृतवैभवस्य
सङ्कल्पमात्रेण महेश्वरस्य ॥ ४८॥

English
The stability and the subsequent operation of the causes projected by Prakṛti take place by the thought of supreme lord whose prowess is not effected by Prakṛti. (48.)

---
