### **CHAPTER FOURTEEN - (The manifestation of Rudras)**

---

Sanskrit
१४

वायुरुवाच ।
प्रतिकल्पं प्रवक्ष्यामि रुद्राविर्भावकारणम् ।
यतोऽविच्छिन्नसन्ताना ब्रह्मसृष्टिः प्रवर्तते ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said:—  I shall explain the reasons for the manifestation of Rudra in every Kalpa whereby the broken series of Brahma's creation is resumed. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पे कल्पे प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा ब्रह्मा ब्रह्माण्डसम्भवः ।
अवृद्धिहेतोर्भूतानां मुमोह भृशदुःखितः ॥ २॥

English
After creating the subjects in every Kalpa, Brahma born of the cosmic egg felt excessively distressed. He was deluded. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य दुःखप्रशान्त्यर्थं प्रजानां च विवृद्धये ।
तत्तत्कल्पेषु कालात्मा रुद्रो रुद्रगणाधिपः ॥ ३॥

निर्दिष्टः परमेशेन महेशो नीललोहितः ।
पुत्रो भूत्वानुगृह्णाति ब्रह्माणं ब्रह्मणोऽनुजः ॥ ४॥

English
In order to pacify his misery and to multiply his subjects, in the various Kalpas, Rudra, Brahma's younger brother, becomes the son of Brahma and blesses him. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव भगवानीशस्तेजोराशिरनामयः ।
अनादिनिधनो धाता भूतसङ्कोचको विभुः ॥ ५॥

English
He alone is lord Isa, a mass of splendour, free from ailment, devoid of beginning and end, the creator and the lord making all living beings shrink. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
परमैश्वर्यसंयुक्तः परमेश्वरभावितः ।
तच्छक्त्याधिष्ठितः शश्वत्तच्चिह्नैरपि चिह्नितः ॥ ६॥

English
Endowed with lordly qualities, he is purified by Paramesvara. He is presided over by his Sakti and marked by his symbols. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्नामनामा तद्रूपस्तत्कार्यकरणक्षमः ।
तत्तुल्यव्यवहारश्च तदाज्ञापरिपालकः ॥ ७॥

English
He has the same name, the same form; he can perform his task; he has same function as he maintains and abides by his commands. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
सहस्रादित्यसङ्काशश्चन्द्रावयवभूषणः ।
भुजङ्गहारकेयूरवलयो मुञ्जमेखलः ॥ ८॥

English
He resembles a thousand suns, he is bedecked in crescent moon. Serpents constitute his necklaces, shoulderlets and bangles. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरविरिञ्चेन्द्रकपालशकलोज्ज्वलः ।
गङ्गातुङ्गतरङ्गार्द्रपिङ्गलाननमूर्द्धजः ॥ ९॥

English
He is brilliant holding the skulls of asura Jalandhara, Brahma, and Indra, his tawny hair, moustache and beard are drenched in the surging waves of Gangā. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
भग्नदंष्ट्राङ्कुराक्रान्तप्रान्तकान्तधराधरः ।
सव्यश्रवणपार्श्वान्तमण्डलीकृतकुण्डलः ॥ १०॥

English
His lips shine with the tips being hit by the sharp-pointed curved fangs; his ear-rings make a halo around his left ear. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
महावृषभनिर्याणो महाजलदनिःस्वनः ।
महानलसमप्रख्यो महाबलपराक्रमः ॥ ११॥

English
He is seated on a great bull; his voice sounds like thunder. He is lustrous like fire, his strength and exploits are great. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं घोरमहारूपो ब्रह्मपुत्रो महेश्वरः ।
विज्ञानं ब्रह्मणे दत्त्वा सर्गं सह करोति च ॥ १२॥

English
He whose features are hideous in this manner and who is born as the son of Brahma bestows wisdom on him and co-operates with him in the activity of creation. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद् रुद्रप्रसादेन प्रतिकल्पं प्रजापतेः ।
प्रवाहरूपतो नित्या प्रजासृष्टिः प्रवर्तते ॥ १३॥

English
It is due to his grace that the creation in every Kalpa functions as a perpetual series. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्प्रार्थितः स्रष्टुं ब्रह्मणा नीललोहितः ।
स्वात्मना सदृशान् सर्वान् ससर्ज मनसा विभुः ॥ १४॥

English
Once, when requested by Brahma to create, he mentally created all beings exactly like himself. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
कपर्दिनो निरातङ्कान्नीलग्रीवाँस्त्रिलोचनान् ।
जरामरणनिर्मुक्तान् दीप्तशूलवरायुधान् ॥ १५॥

English
All of them had matted hair; they were free from fear and distress, had blue necks and three eyes, were free from decaying age and death; they had shining tridents as their excellent weapons. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तैस्तु सञ्छादितं सर्वं चतुर्दशविधं जगत् ।
तान्दृष्ट्वा विविधान् रुद्रान् रुद्रमाह पितामहः ॥ १६॥

English
The fourteen worlds of the universe were filled up by them. On seeing these various Rudras, Brahma spoke to Siva. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
नमस्ते देवदेवेश मास्राक्षीरीदृशीः प्रजाः ।
अन्याः सृज त्वं भद्रं ते प्रजा मृत्युसमन्विताः ॥ १७॥

English
"Obeisance to you, O lord of gods, do not create such subjects. Welfare to you. Create other subjects that may be endowed with death." (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तः प्रहसन्प्राह ब्रह्माणं परमेश्वरः ।
नास्ति मे तादृशः सर्गः सृज त्वमशुभाः प्रजाः ॥ १८॥

English
When told thus, the lord replied to Brahma, "Such a creation is not my task. You alone create inauspicious beings. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
ये त्विमे मनसा सृष्टा महात्मानो महाबलाः ।
चरिष्यन्ति मया सार्द्धं सर्व एव हि याज्ञिकाः ॥ १९॥

English
The noble beings of great strength created by me mentally will walk about in my company. They will regulate the sacrifices." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा विश्वकर्माणं विश्वभूतेश्वरो हरः ।
सह रुद्रैः प्रजासर्गान्निवृत्तात्माध्यतिष्ठत ॥ २०॥

English
After saying thus to the creator, Siva the lord of living beings desisted from creation and remained in the company of Rudras. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रभृति देवोऽसौ न प्रसूते प्रजाः शुभाः ।
ऊर्ध्वरेताः स्थितः स्थाणुर्यावदाभूतसम्प्लवम् ॥ २१॥

English
From that time onwards the lord does not procreate auspicious beings. He stands in Yogic trance till the dissolution of the universe. (21.)

---
