### **CHAPTER FIFTEEN - (Song of Prayer addressed to Śiva and Śivā)**

---

Sanskrit
१५

वायुरुवाच ।
यदा पुनः प्रजाः सृष्टा न व्यवर्द्धन्त वेधसः ।
तदा मैथुनजां सृष्टिं ब्रह्मा कर्तुममन्यत ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said:—  When the creation of Brahma did not multiply Brahma thought of creation by the process of sexual intercourse. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
न निर्गतं पुरा यस्मान्नारीणां कुलमीश्वरात् ।
तेन मैथुनजां सृष्टिं न शशाक पितामहः ॥ २॥

English
Since the race of women had not emanated from the lord formerly, Brahma could not produce creation through sexual intercourse. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स विदधे बुद्धिमर्थनिश्चयगामिनीम् ।
प्रजानमेव वृद्ध्यर्थं प्रष्टव्यः परमेश्वर ॥ ३॥

English
Then he finally resolved that the lord should be asked in respect of multiplying the subjects. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादेन विना तस्य न वर्द्धेरन्निमाः प्रजाः ।
एवं सञ्चिन्त्य विश्वात्मा तपः कर्तुं प्रचक्रमे ॥ ४॥

English
Thinking that without his grace the subjects will not increase, Brahmā began to perform penance. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदाद्या परमा शक्तिरनन्ता लोकभाविनी ।
आद्या सूक्ष्मतरा शुद्धा भावगम्या मनोहरा ॥ ५॥

निर्गुणा निष्प्रपञ्चा च निष्कला निरुपप्लवा ।
निरन्तररता नित्या नित्यमीश्वरपार्श्वगा ॥ ६॥

तया परमया शक्त्या भगवन्तं त्रियम्बकम् ।
सञ्चिन्त्य हृदये ब्रह्मा तताप परमं तपः ॥ ७॥

English
His primordial eternal Śakti is subtle, pure, pleasant and intelligible only through piety; it is devoid of attributes, unsullied, unramified unruffled, perpetual and it ever remains with Īśvara. Thus Brahmā pondered over the lord and his great Śakti and performed penance. (5-7.)

---

Sanskrit
तीव्रेण तपसा तस्य युक्तस्य परमेष्ठिनः ।
अचिरेणैव कालेन पिता सम्प्रतुतोष ह ॥ ८॥

English
Ere long the lord was pleased with Brahmā who was endowed with severe penance. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः केनचिदंशेन मूर्तिमाविश्य कामपि ।
अर्द्धनारीश्वरो भूत्वा ययौ देवः स्वयं हरः ॥ ९॥

English
Becoming half-female$^{205}$ after introducing certain parts into a certain image, the lord himself came there. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा परमं देवं तमसः परमव्ययम् ।
अद्वितीयमनिर्देश्यमदृश्यमकृतात्मभिः ॥ १०॥

सर्वलोकविधातारं सर्वलोकेश्वरेश्वरम् ।
सर्वलोकविधायिन्या शक्त्या परमया युतम् ॥ ११॥

अप्रतर्क्यमनाभासममेयमजरं ध्रुवम् ।
अचलं निर्गुणं शान्तमनन्तमहिमास्पदम् ॥ १२॥

सर्वगं सर्वदं सर्वसदसद्व्यक्तिवर्जितम् ।
सर्वोपमाननिर्मुक्तं शरण्यं शाश्वतं शिवम् ॥ १३॥

प्रणम्य दण्डवद् ब्रह्मा समुत्थाय कृताञ्जलिः ।
श्रद्धाविनयसम्पन्नैः श्राव्यैः संस्कारसंयुतैः ॥ १४॥

यथार्थयुक्तसर्वार्थैर्वेदार्थपरिबृंहितैः ।
तुष्टाव देवं देवीं च सूक्तैः सूक्ष्मार्थगोचरैः ॥ १५॥

English
On seeing the lord, Brahmā eulogised him as well as the goddess, with hymns full of subtle meaning—the lord who is beyond darkness, is eternal, without a second, incomprehensible, invisible to the libertine, the creator, overlord, endowed with the creative power, who is beyond arguments, has no fallacious appearance, is immeasurable, unaging, stable, unmoving, devoid of attributes, quiescent, possessed of infinity, omnipresent, bestower of boons, devoid of difference between the Existent and the non-Existent, incomparable, worthy of being sought refuge and permanent. He prostrated before him and then stood up with palms joined with reverence. He eulogised him with faith and humility by means of hymns of subtle, sweet, refined and pious meanings that explained the Vedic texts. (10-15.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
जय देव महादेव जयेश्वर महेश्वर ।
जय सर्वगुणश्रेष्ठ जय सर्वसुराधिप ॥ १६॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O lord, be victorious. O Īśvara, O Maheśvara, splendid with attributes, be victorious. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
जय प्रकृतिकल्याणि जय प्रकृतिनायिके ।
जय प्रकृतिदूरे त्वं जय प्रकृतिसुन्दरि ॥ १७॥

English
O auspicious goddess, O heroine of Prakṛti and the one beyond Prakṛti and naturally beautiful, be victorious. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
जयामोघमहामाय जयामोघमनोरथ ।
जयामोघमहालील जयामोघमहाबल ॥ १८॥

English
O lord of fruitful Māyā, of fruitful will, of great sports and of fruitful strength, be victorious. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
जय विश्वजगन्मातर्जय विश्वजगन्मयि ।
जय विश्वजगद्धात्रि जय विश्वजगत्सखि ॥ १९॥

English
O mother of the universe, O goddess identical with the universe, O creator and companion of the universe, be victorious. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
जय शाश्वतिकैश्वर्य जय शाश्वतिकालय ।
जय शाश्वतिकाकार जय शाश्वतिकानुग ॥ २०॥

English
Be victorious, O lord of eternal prosperity and permanent abode. Be victorious, O lord of eternal form. Be victorious, O lord of permanent devotees. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
जयात्मत्रयनिर्मात्रि जयात्मत्रयपालिनि ।
जयात्मत्रयसंहर्त्रि जयात्मत्रयनायिके ॥ २१॥

English
Be victorious O creatress, protectress, annihilatress and the heroine of the three Ātmans. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
जयावलोकनायत्तजगत्कारणबृंहण ।
जयोपेक्षाकटाक्षोत्थहुतभुग्भुक्तभौतिक ॥ २२॥

English
Be victorious, O lord ready to survey, enlarge the universe. Be victorious, O lord from whose terrible glance rises the fire that consumes all the worlds. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
जय देवाद्यविज्ञेये स्वात्मसूक्ष्मदृशोज्ज्वले ।
जय स्थूलात्मशक्त्येशे जय व्याप्तचराचरे ॥ २३॥

English
Be victorious, O goddess incomprehensible to the gods and others, O goddess that shinest brilliantly with your subtle vision. Be victorious, O goddess the part of the gross Śakti of the Ātman. Be victorious, O goddess pervading the mobile and immobile beings. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
जय नानैकविन्यस्तविश्वतत्त्वसमुच्चय ।
जयासुरशिरोनिष्ठश्रेष्ठानुगकदम्बक ॥ २४॥

English
Be victorious, O lord merging various principles of the universe into one. Be victorious, O lord the groups of whose followers have stepped on the heads of Asuras. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
जयोपाश्रितसंरक्षासंविधानपटीयसि ।
जयोन्मूलितसंसारविषवृक्षाङ्कुरोद्गमे ॥ २५॥

English
Be victorious, O goddess affording protection to those who resort to you. Be victorious, O goddess who uproot the spirits of the poisonous tree of worldly existence. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
जय प्रादेशिकैश्वर्यवीर्यशौर्यविजृम्भण ।
जय विश्वबहिर्भूत निरस्तपरवैभव ॥ २६॥

English
Be victorious, O lord enhancing prosperity, prowess and heroism. Be victorious, O lord, stationed beyond the universe, O lord who hast quelled the might of the enemies. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
जय प्रणीतपञ्चार्थप्रयोगपरमामृत ।
जय पञ्चार्थविज्ञानसुधास्रोतःस्वरूपिणि ॥ २७॥

English
Be victorious, O immortal lord who hast laid the rite of "five objects." Be victorious, O goddess having the form of the hymn with the knowledge of the "five objects." (27.)

---

Sanskrit
जयातिघोरसंसारमहारोगभिषग्वर ।
जयानादिमलाज्ञानतमःपटलचन्द्रिके ॥ २८॥

English
Be victorious, O lord, the physician par excellence for the great suffering of the terrible worldly existence. Be victorious, O goddess the moon-light for the layers of darkness of ignorance, the primordial dirt. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
जय त्रिपुरकालाग्ने जय त्रिपुरभैरवि ।
जय त्रिगुणनिर्मुक्ते जय त्रिगुणमर्दिनि ॥ २९॥

English
Be victorious, O black fire that consumed the Tripuras. Be victorious, O goddess Tripurabhairavī. Be victorious, O lord devoid of three attributes. Be victorious O goddess, the possessor of three attributes. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
जय प्रथमसर्वज्ञ जय सर्वप्रबोधिके ।
जय प्रचुरदिव्याङ्ग जय प्रार्थितदायिनि ॥ ३०॥

English
Be victorious, O omniscient one. Be victorious, O goddess enlightening every one. Be victorious O lord having prolific divine limbs. Be victorious, O goddess bestowing all desires. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
क्व देव ते परं धाम क्व च तुच्छं च नो वचः ।
तथापि भगवन् भक्त्या प्रलपन्तं क्षमस्व माम् ॥ ३१॥

English
O lord, where is your great splendour and where the worthless words of ours. Still O lord, forgive me that babble but with devotion. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
विज्ञाप्यैवंविधैः सूक्तैर्विश्वकर्मा चतुर्मुखः ।
नमश्चकार रुद्राय रद्राण्यै च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ३२॥

English
After eulogising with these and similar hymns the four-faced lord, the creator of the universe bowed again and again to Rudra and Rudrānī. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं स्तोत्रवरं पुण्यं ब्रह्मणा समुदीरितम् ।
अर्द्धनारीश्वरं नाम शिवयोर्हर्षवर्द्धनम् ॥ ३३॥

English
This holy and excellent hymn "Ardhanārīśvara Stotra" uttered by Brahma enhances the delight of Śiva and Śiva. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं कीर्त्तयेद्भक्त्या यस्य कस्यापि काङ्क्षया ।
स तत्फलमवाप्नोति शिवयोः प्रीतिकारणात् ॥ ३४॥

English
He who recites this with devotion or teaches to anyone whatsoever reaps the fruit thereof as a result of the pleasure of Siva and Siva. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
सकलभुवनभूतभावनाभ्यां
जननविनाशविहीनविग्रहाभ्याम् ।
नरवरयुवतीवपुर्द्धराभ्यां
सततमहं प्रणतोऽस्मि शङ्कराभ्याम् ॥ ३५॥

English
I bow to Siva and Siva the sanctifiers of living beings in the entire universe; the couple whose bodies are devoid of birth and death and who have taken the bodies of an excellent man and a youthful maiden. (35.)
