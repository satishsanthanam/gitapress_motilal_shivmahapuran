### **CHAPTER SIXTEEN - (The manifestation of divine (akti)**

---

Sanskrit
१६

वायुरुवाच ।
अथ देवो महादेवो महाजलदनादया ।
वाचा मधुरगम्भीरशिवदश्लक्ष्णवर्णया ॥ १॥

अर्थसम्पन्नपदया राजलक्षणयुक्तया ।
अशेषविषयारम्भरक्षाविमलयदक्षया ॥ २॥

मनोहरतरोदारमधुरस्मितपूर्वया ।
सम्बभाषेसुसम्प्रीतो विश्वकर्माणमीश्वरः ॥ ३॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Then the delighted god Śiva spoke to Brahmā with a benign and sweet smile. His voice sounded like thunder. All the letters were sweet, majestic, soft, clear, full and rich in meanings. They had royal qualities being pure and efficient in the protection of all subjects. (1-3.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
वत्स वत्स महाभाग मम पुत्र पितामह ।
ज्ञातमेव मया सर्वं तव वाक्यस्य गौरवम् ॥ ४॥

English
Lord Śiva said :—  Dear child, O fortunate one, the gravity of your statements has been understood by me. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजानामेव वृद्ध्यर्थं तपस्तप्तं त्वयाधुना ।
तपसानेन तुष्टोऽस्मि ददामि च तवेप्सितम् ॥ ५॥

English
It is for the increase in the number of the subjects that this penance has been performed by you. I am delighted with your penance. I shall grant you what you desire. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा परमोदारं स्वभावमधुरं वचः ।
ससर्ज वपुषो भागाद्देवीं देववरो हरः ॥ ६॥

English
After this sweet and liberal speech the excellent lord created the goddess from a part of his body. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
यामाहुर्ब्रह्मविद्वांसो देवीं दिव्यगुणान्विताम् ।
परस्य परमां शक्तिं भवस्य परमात्मनः ॥ ७॥

English
It is the goddess whom scholars of the Vedas call the greatest Śakti endowed with the divine qualities of Śiva the great soul. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्यां न खलु विद्यन्ते जन्ममृत्युजरादयः ।
या भवानी भवस्याङ्गात्समाविरभवत्किल ॥ ८॥

English
It was the goddess in whom there is neither birth, nor death nor old age nor other similar things. It was the goddess who manifested herself from the body of Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्या वाचो निवर्तन्ते मनसा चेन्द्रियैः सह ।
सा भर्तुर्वपुषो भागाज्जातेव समदृश्यत ॥ ९॥

English
She from whom words recede along with the mind and the sense-organs,²⁰⁶ appeared to be born of a part of the body of her husband. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
या सा जगदिदं कृत्स्नं महिम्ना व्याप्य तिष्ठति ।
शरीरिणीव स देवी विचित्रं समलक्ष्यत ॥ १०॥

English
The goddess who pervades everything through her greatness was seen like a wonderfully embodied being. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं जगदिदं चैषा सम्मोहयति मायया ।
ईश्वरात्सैव जाताऽभूदजाता परमार्थतः ॥ ११॥

English
It is she who fascinates the entire universe with her Māyā. Really she is unborn but she appeared to be born of Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
न यस्याः परमो भावः सुराणामपि गोचरः ।
विश्वामरेश्वरी चैव विभक्ता भर्तुरङ्गतः ॥ १२॥

English
Her great form is not visible even to the gods. Such a great goddess of all the gods was divided in the body of her lord. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा परमेशानीं सर्वलोकमहेश्वरीम् ।
सर्वज्ञां सर्वगां सूक्ष्मां सदसद्व्यक्तिवर्जिताम् ॥ १३॥

परमां निखिलं भासा भासयन्तीमिदं जगत् ।
प्रणिपत्य महादेवीं प्रार्थयामास वै विराट् ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing the great omniscient, omnipresent subtle goddess, devoid of the difference between Sat and Asat, the goddess who illuminates the universe with her brilliance, Brahmā bowed and prayed. (13-14.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
देवि देवेन सृष्टोऽहमादौ सर्वजगन्मयि ।
प्रजासर्गे नियुक्तश्च सृजामि सकलं जगत् ॥ १५॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O Goddess in the beginning I was created by the lord and employed in the activity of the creation. I create the universe. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मनसा निर्मिताः सर्वे देवि देवादयो मया ।
न वृद्धिमुपगच्छन्ति सृज्यमानाः पुनः पुनः ॥ १६॥

English
O Goddess, the gods and others are mentally created by me. Though they are created again and again they do not increase in number. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
मिथुनप्रभवामेव कृत्वा सृष्टिमतः परम् ।
संवर्धयितुमिच्छामि सर्वा एव मम प्रजाः ॥ १७॥

English
Hereafter I wish to increase the number through copulation. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
न निर्गतं पुरा त्वत्तो नारीणां कुलमव्ययम् ।
तेन नारीकुलं स्रष्टुं शक्तिर्मम न विद्यते ॥ १८॥

English
The race of women has not emerged from you. Hence I cannot create an everlasting race of women.²⁰⁷ (18.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वासामेव शक्तीनां त्वत्तः खलु समुद्भवः ।
तस्मात्सर्वत्र सर्वेषां सर्वशक्तिप्रदायिनीम् ॥ १९॥

त्वामेव वरदां मायां प्रार्थयामि सुरेश्वरीम् ।
चराचरविवृद्ध्यर्थमंशेनैकेन सर्वगे ॥ २०॥

दक्षस्य मम पुत्रस्य पुत्री भव भवार्दिनि ।
एवं सा याचिता देवी ब्रह्मणा ब्रह्मयोनिना ॥ २१॥

शक्तिमेकां भ्रुवोर्मध्यात् ससर्जात्मसमप्रभाम् ।
तामाह प्रहसन् प्रेक्ष्य देवदेववरो हरः ॥ २२॥

English
Śaktis originate from you. Hence I worship you alone who bestow Śaktis upon everyone everywhere, who grant boons and who are Māyā and the goddess of the gods. O omnipresent goddess for the increase of the mobile and immobile beings, please be born with a part of yours, as the daughter of Dakṣa my son. Thus requested by Brahmā the goddess created a Śakti equal in splendour to herself from the middle of her eyebrows. Lord Śiva looked at her laughingly and spoke. (19-22.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माणं तपसाराध्य कुरु तस्य यथेप्सितम् ।
तामाज्ञां परमेशस्य शिरसा प्रतिगृह्य सा ॥ २३॥

ब्रह्मणो वचनाद्देवी दक्षस्य दुहिताऽभवत् ।
दत्त्वैवमतुलां शक्तिं ब्रह्मणे ब्रह्मरूपिणीम् ॥ २४॥

विवेश देहं देवस्य देवश्चान्तरधीयत ।
तदाप्रभृति लोकेऽस्मिन् स्त्रियां भोगः प्रतिष्ठितः ॥ २५॥

English
"After propitiating Brahmā with penance you shall carry out what he desires". The Śakti obeyed the lord. At the instance of Brahmā she became the daughter of Dakṣa. After giving the incomparable Śakti in the form of Brahman to Brahmā, the goddess re-entered the body of the lord. The lord vanished. Since that time the enjoyment of sexual pleasures in women was established. (23-25.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजासृष्टिश्च विप्रेन्द्रा मैथुनेन प्रवर्तते ।
ब्रह्मापि प्राप सानन्दं सन्तोषं मुनिपुङ्गवाः ॥ २६॥

English
O leading brahmins, procreation functions through copulation. O leading sages, Brahmā too was satisfied. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
एतद्वः सर्वमाख्यातं देव्याः शक्तिसमुद्भवम् ।
पुण्यवृद्धिकरं श्राव्यं भूतसर्गानुषङ्गतः ॥ २७॥

English
Thus the manifestation of Śakti has been narrated to you. It enhances merit. It shall be narrated with the story of creation. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
य इदं कीर्तयेन्नित्यं देव्याः शक्तिसमुद्भवम् ।
पुण्यं सर्वमवाप्नोति पुत्रांश्च लभते शुभान् ॥ २८॥

English
He who recites this every day attains merits and obtains worthy sons. (28.)
