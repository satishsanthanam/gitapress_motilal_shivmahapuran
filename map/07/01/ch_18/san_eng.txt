### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - *(The abandonment of the body by Sati)***

---

Sanskrit
१८

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
देवी दक्षस्य तनया त्यक्त्वा दाक्षायणीं तनुम् ।
कथं हिमवतः पुत्री मेनायामभवत्पुरा ॥ १॥

English
*The sages said :—*  How did the goddess, daughter of Dakṣa become the daughter of Himavat and Menā after abandoning the body born of Dakṣa? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं च निन्दितो रुद्रो दक्षेण च महात्मना ।
निमित्तमपि किं तत्र येन स्यान्निन्दितो भवः ॥ २॥

English
How was Rudra censured by Dakṣa the noble soul? What could be the cause whereby Śiva was censured? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पन्नश्च कथं दक्षो ह्यभिशापाद्भवस्य तु ।
चाक्षुषस्यान्तरे पूर्वं मनोः प्रब्रूहि मारुत ॥ ३॥

English
O wind-god, formerly in the Cākṣuṣa Manvantara how was Dakṣa born due to the curse of Śiva? Please narrate. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाव
श‍ृण्वन्तु कथयिष्यामि दक्षस्य लघुचेतसः ।
वृत्तं पापात् प्रमादाच्च विश्वामरविदूषणम् ॥ ४॥

English
Vayā said :—  Please listen. I shall narrate the activity of the mean Dakṣa resulting in his insult to all gods due to his sinful blunder. (4.)

Sanskrit

English
Once formerly, all the gods, Asuras, Siddhas and the sages went to the summit of Himavat in order to see the lord. (5.)

Sanskrit

English
O Brahmins, the lord and the goddess were seated on a divine seat as they granted interview to the gods and others. (6.)

Sanskrit

Sanskrit

English
In view of their dignity, the lord and the Goddess did not give any special preferential treatment to Dakṣa. (8.)

Sanskrit

English
But deluded that he was, Dakṣa did not realise the greatness of the lord and the goddess. Thinking her to be his daughter he began to nurse a grudge and hatred against her. (9.)

Sanskrit

English

English
He invited the other sons-in-law and honoured them severally. (11.)

Sanskrit

वज्रकल्पितसोपानं विद्रुमस्तम्भतोरणम् ॥ १५॥

पुष्पपट्टपरिस्तीर्णं चित्ररत्नमहासनम् ।
वज्रजालकिरच्छिद्रमच्छिद्रमणिकुट्टिमम् ॥ १६॥

अधिष्ठितमहाद्वारमप्रधृष्यैर्गणेश्वरैः ॥ १८॥

मृदङ्गतालगीतादिवेणुवीणाविशारदैः ।
विदग्धवेषभूषैश्च बहुभिः स्त्रीजनैर्वृतम् ॥ १९॥

English
She stepped into the aeroplane along with her dear friends. It was a divine plane with doors on all sides; had good conditions; could be easily mounted. It was very charming. It shed golden lustre. It was studded with jewels of various sorts and had a canopy covered with pearls. It was decorated with stringed flower garlands. It had been wrought in molten gold; hundreds of be-jewelled pillars supported it all round. The adamantine steps were neatly built. The columns and festoons were decorated with corals. The chief seat therein was studded with gems and covered with a silken cloth with flower-designs. Through every aperture rays of diamonds were diffused; a good gemset platform without dents shone. A flagstaff as pure as cloud decorated its front with the sign of the great Bull. It was studded with jewels. The main door was guarded by invincible Gaṇeśvaras wearing jewel-studded bodices and wielding canes in their hands. Many women, experts in playing on Mṛdaṅgas, flutes, Viṇās and in music were seated there richly dressed and bedecked in ornaments. (13-19.)

Two Rudra virgins held the auspicious chowries and fans beautiful in their handles set with diamonds, and fanned the goddess. In the middle of the chowries the face of the goddess shone like a lotus in the midst of two fighting swans. (20-21.)

Sanskrit

धर्मार्थकामयुक्तेषु कर्मस्वपि पुनः पुनः ॥ ५८॥

English
O wicked one, there also I shall put up obstacles before you in all activities regarding virtue, wealth and love. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तेनैवं व्याहृतो दक्षो रुद्रेणामिततेजसा ।
स्वायम्भुवीं तनुं त्यक्त्वा पपात भुवि दुःखितः ॥ ५९॥

English
Thus addressed by Rudra of unmeasured splendour, Dakṣa abandoned the body and fell on the ground. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्राचेतसो दक्षो जज्ञे वै चाक्षुषेऽन्तरे ।
प्राचीनबर्हिषः पौत्रः पुत्रश्चैव प्रचेतसाम् ॥ ६०॥

English
Then in the Cākṣuṣa Manvantara Dakṣa was born as the grandson of Prācinabarhiṣ and the son of Pracetas. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
भृग्वादयोऽपि जाता वै मनोर्वैवस्वतस्य तु ।
अन्तरे ब्रह्मणो यज्ञे वारुणीं बिभ्रतस्तनुम् ॥ ६१॥

English
Bhṛgu and others were born in the Vaivasvata Manvantara in the sacrifice of Brahmā bearing the bodies of Varuṇa. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा दक्षस्य धर्मार्थं प्रवृत्तस्य दुरात्मनः ।
महेशः कृतवान् विघ्नं मनौ वैवस्वते सति ॥ ६२॥

English
Then in the Vaivasvata Manvantara in the virtuous sacrifice of the wicked Dakṣa the lord created obstacles (62.)
