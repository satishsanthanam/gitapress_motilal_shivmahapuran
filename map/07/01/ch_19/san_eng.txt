### **CHAPTER NINETEEN - (The origin of Vīrabhadra)**

---

Sanskrit
१९

ऋषय ऊचुः ।

English
The sages said:—  How did the lord create obstacles before the wicked Dakṣa who performed a sacrifice for virtue's sake? We wish to know this. (1.)

Sanskrit

English

(2-4.)

Sanskrit

ज्वालामालापरिक्षिप्तं दीप्तमौक्तिकभूषणम् ।

English
He had a thousand faces, a thousand lotus-like eyes. He was holding a thousand iron clubs, thousands of arrows, spear, axe, mace, bow, discus and the thunderbolt. He looked fierce and terrible. The crescent moon adorned his crest; the thunderbolt illuminated his hands. His hair shone like lightning. He had a huge mouth terrible with the curved fangs and a huge belly. His tongue was like lightnings. His lips hung down. His voice sounded like the cloud and the ocean. He wore the tiger's hide dripping with blood. He ear-rings formed circles close to his cheeks. He had adorned his crest with the garlands from the heads of leading gods. He was bedecked in golden shoulderlets and jingling anklets. He was shining with heaps of gems. His chest was covered by necklaces. He was equal in exploit to the great fabulous animal Śarabha, tigers and lions. His gait was gentle and majestic as that of a stately elephant in rut. His lustre was white like conch, chowrie, Kunda flower, moon and the lotus-stalk. He was like the snow-capped lord of mountains who had become mobile. Flames surrounded him. Wearing pearl ornaments he shone brilliantly as the fire at the dissolution of the world. (25-33.)

कालानलशतैर्युक्तो यथान्ते कालभैरवः ॥ ५६॥

English
Lord Bhadra surrounded by the Bhadras shone like Kālabhairava surrounded by hundreds of deadly fires at the time of dissolution. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां मध्ये समारुह्य वृषेन्द्रं वृषभध्वजः ।
जगाम भगवान् भद्रः शुभ्रमभ्रं यथा भवः ॥ ५७॥

English
In their midst, mounted on a lordly bull and with a bull-emblem, lord Bhadra went ahead like Siva seated on Nandin. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्वृषभमारूढे भद्रे तु भसितप्रभः ।
बभार मौक्तिकं छत्रं गृहीतसितचामरः ॥ ५८॥

English
When Bhadra was seated on the bull, Bhasitaprabha held the pearl umbrella and the white chowries. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
स तदा शुशुभे पार्श्वे भद्रस्य भसितप्रभः ।
भगवानिव शैलेन्द्रः पार्श्वे विश्वजगद्गुरोः ॥ ५९॥

English
Bhasita shone by the side of Bhadra like the lord of mountains by the side of Siva. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि तेन बभौ भद्रः श्वेतचामरपाणिना ।
बालसोमेन सौम्येन यथा शूलवरायुधः ॥ ६०॥

English
Bhadra too shone in his company as he held the white chowries like the lord with the trident weapon and the crescent moon. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
दध्मौ शङ्खं सितं भद्रं भद्रस्य पुरतः शुभम् ।
भानुकम्पो महातेजा हेमरत्नैरलङ्कृतः ॥ ६१॥

English
Bhanukampa of great splendour bedecked in gold and jewels blew on the auspicious white conch Bhadra in front of Bhadra. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदुन्दुभयो नेदुर्दिव्यसङ्कुलनिःस्वनाः ।
ववृषुः शतशो मूर्ध्नि पुष्पवर्षं बलाहकाः ॥ ६२॥

English
The divine drums were sounded with divine tumultuous tone. The clouds in hundreds made showers of flowers on his head. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
फुल्लानां मधुगर्भाणां पुष्पाणां गन्धबन्धवः ।
मार्गानुकूलसंवाहा ववुश्च पथि मारुताः ॥ ६३॥

English
Winds taking up the fragrance of full blown flowers pregnant with honey blew favourably on their way. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो गणेश्वराः सर्वे मत्ता युद्धबलोद्धताः ।
ननृतुर्मुमुदुर्नेदुर्जहसुर्जगदुर्जगुः ॥ ६४॥

English
Then the elated chiefs proud of their strength danced, rejoiced, cried, laughed, spoke and sang. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा भद्रगणान्तःस्थो बभौ भद्रः सभद्रया ।
यथा रुद्रगणान्तः स्थस्त्र्यम्बकोऽम्बिकया सह ॥ ६५॥

English
Then Bhadra stationed in the midst of the Bhadra-ganas in the company of Bhadra shone like Siva in the midst of Rudraganas in the company of Parvati. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्क्षणादेव दक्षस्य यज्ञवाटं हिरण्मयम् ।
प्रविवेश महाबाहुर्वीरभद्रो महानुगः ॥ ६६॥

English
In a moment the strong Virabhadra with his followers entered the golden gate of the sacrificial altar. (66.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्तु दक्षप्रतिपादितस्य
क्रतुप्रधानस्य गणप्रधानः ।
प्रयोगभूमिं प्रविवेश भद्रो
रुद्रो यथान्ते भुवनं दिधक्षुः ॥ ६७॥

English
Then the chief of Gaṇas Bhadra entered like Rudra desirous of burning the worlds at the time of dissolution. (67.)
