### **CHAPTER TWENTY - (The destruction of Dakṣa's sacrifice)**

---

Sanskrit
२०

वायुरुवाच ।
ततो विष्णुप्रधानानां सुराणाममितौजसाम् ।
ददर्श च महत्सत्रं चित्रध्वजपरिच्छदम् ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Then he saw the great Satra of the gods of great splendour the chief of whom was Visnu. The satra had all the paraphernalia—the flags of various colours etc. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सुदर्भऋतुसंस्तीर्णं सुसमिद्धहुताशनम् ।
काञ्चनैर्यज्ञभाण्डैश्च भ्राजिष्णुभिरलङ्कृतम् ॥ २॥

English
Good grass was scattered in straight lines. The sacrificial fire was well-kindled. It was embellished with glittering sacrificial vessels of gold. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ऋषिभिर्यज्ञपटुभिर्यथावत्कर्मकर्तृभिः ।
विधिना वेददृष्टेन स्वनुष्ठितबहुक्रमम् ॥ ३॥

English
The rites were performed neatly in accordance with the rules found in the Vedas by the sages who were experts in sacrifices and who performed the rites properly. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाङ्गनासहस्राढ्यमप्सरोगणसेवितम् ।
वेणुवीणारवैर्जुष्टं वेदघोषैश्च बृंहितम् ॥ ४॥

English
Thousands of celestial damsels were there. The Satra was attended by the Apsaras. The sweet sound of flutes and lutes spread and was enhanced by the sound of Vedic hymns. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा दक्षाध्वरं वीरो वीरभद्रः प्रतापवान् ।
सिंहनादं तदा चक्रे गम्भीरो जलदो यथा ॥ ५॥

English
On seeing all this, the heroic Virabhadra of great exploits roared like a lion or a majestic rumbling cloud. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः किलकिलाशब्द आकाशं पूरयन्निव ।
गणेश्वरैः कृतो जज्ञे महान्न्यक्कृतसागरः ॥ ६॥

English
Then a hurrah of joy, filling the sky and superseding the sound of the ocean was produced by the lords of gaṇas. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन शब्देन महता ग्रस्ताः सर्वे दिवौकसः ।
दुद्रुवुः परितो भीताः स्रस्तवस्त्रविभूषणाः ॥ ७॥

English
Overwhelmed by the great sound the frightened heaven-dwellers ran helter-skelter dropping their garments and ornaments. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
किंस्विद्भग्नो महामेरुः किंस्वित्सन्दीर्यते मही ।
किमिदं किमिदं वेति जजल्पुस्त्रिदशा भृशम् ॥ ८॥

English
"Has the great Meru been burst ? Is the earth pierced through ? What is this?" the gods began to scream and shout. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मृगेन्द्राणां यथा नादं गजेन्द्रा गहने वने ।
श्रुत्वा तथाविधं केचित्तत्यजुर्जीवितं भयात् ॥ ९॥

English
Some abandoned their lives due to fear like lordly elephants in a thick forest on hearing the roar of the lion. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
पर्वताश्च व्यशीर्यन्त चकम्पे च वसुन्धरा ।
मरुतश्च व्यघूर्णन्त चुक्षुभे मकरालयः ॥ १०॥

English
Mountains crumbled. The earth quaked. Winds rolled and reeled. The ocean was agitated. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्नयो नैव दीप्यन्ते न च दीप्यति भास्करः ।
ग्रहाश्च न प्रकाशन्ते नक्षत्राणि च तारकाः ॥ ११॥

English
Fires did not blaze. The sun did not shine. Neither the planets nor the stars nor the other luminary bodies shone. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नेव काले तु यज्ञवाटं तदुज्ज्वलम् ।
सम्प्राप भगवान्भद्रो भद्रैश्च सह भद्रया ॥ १२॥

English
In the meantime lord Bhadra in the company of Bhadras and the goddess Bhadrā reached the brilliant sacrificial altar. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा भीतभीतोऽपि दक्षो दृढ इव स्थितः ।
क्रुद्धवद् वचनं प्राह को भवान् किमिहेच्छसि ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing him, Dakṣa stood steady and firm though he was frightened. He spoke angrily "Who are you? What do you wish?" (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा दक्षस्य च दुरात्मनः ।
वीरभद्रो महातेजा मेघगम्भीरनिःस्वनः ॥ १४॥

स्मयन्निव तमालोक्य दक्षं देवांश्च ऋत्विजः ।
अर्थगर्भमसम्भ्रान्तमवोचदुचितं वचः ॥ १५॥

English
On hearing the words of the wicked Dakṣa, Vīrabhadra of great splendour and of majestically rumbling sound like the cloud smilingly looked at Dakṣa, the gods and the Ṛtiviks. He spoke the relevent words full of meaning without the least excitement. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरभद्र उवाच ।
वयं ह्यनुचराः सर्वे शर्वस्यामिततेजसः ।
भागाभिलिप्सया प्राप्ता भागो नः सम्प्रदीयताम् ॥ १६॥

English
Vīrabhadra said :—  We are the followers of lord Śiva of great splendour. We have come for our share. May that be given to us. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ चेदध्वरेऽस्माकं न भागः परिकल्पितः ।
कथ्यतां कारणं तत्र युध्यतां वा मयामरैः ॥ १७॥

English
If the share has not been allotted to us in the sacrifice let the reason be mentioned or let the gods fight with me. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तास्ते गणेन्द्रेण देवा दक्षपुरोगमाः ।
ऊचुर्मन्त्राः प्रमाणं नो न वयं प्रभवस्त्विति ॥ १८॥

English
When they were told thus by the chief of Gaṇas the gods headed by Dakṣa said—"The mantras are our authorities. We are not the lords." (18.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्रा ऊचुस्सुरा यूयं मोहोपहतचेतसः ।
येन प्रथमभागार्हं न यजध्वं महेश्वरम् ॥ १९॥

English
The Mantras said—"O gods, your minds are oppressed by Tamas. Hence you do not deserve the first share. Worship lord Śiva." (19.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्रोक्ता अपि ते देवाः सर्वे सम्मूढचेतसः ।
भद्राय न ददुर्भागं तत्प्रहाणमभीप्सवः ॥ २०॥

English
Though advised by the Mantras the gods with deluded minds did not offer the share to Bhadra. They desired to drop him out. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा तथ्यं च पथ्यं च स्ववाक्यं तद्वृथाऽभवत् ।
तदा ततो ययुर्मन्त्रा ब्रह्मलोकं सनातनम् ॥ २१॥

English
When their truthful and wholesome words went in vain the Mantras turned away from that place and went to the eternal Brahmaloka. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अथोवाच गणाध्यक्षो देवान् विष्णुपुरोगमान् ।
मन्त्राः प्रमाणं न कृता युष्माभिर्बलगर्वितैः ॥ २२॥

यस्मादस्मिन् मखे देवैरित्थं वयमसत्कृताः ।
तस्माद्वो जीवितैस्सार्द्धमपनेष्यामि गर्वितम् ॥ २३॥

English
Then the chief of Gaṇas spoke to the gods headed by Viṣṇu, "You have not accepted the mantras being proud of your strength. Since we have been dishonoured by the gods thus in this sacrifice, I will drive out your arrogance along with your lives." (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा भगवान् क्रुद्धो व्यदहन्नेत्रवह्निना ।
यज्ञवाटं महाकूटं यथा तिस्रः पुरो हरः ॥ २४॥

English
After saying thus the infuriated lord burnt the sacrificial altar with the fire of his eyes as Śiva had burnt the three Puras.217 (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो गणेश्वराः सर्वे पर्वतोदग्रविग्रहाः ।
यूपानुत्पाट्य होतॄणां कण्ठेष्वाबध्य रज्जुभिः ॥ २५॥

यज्ञपात्राणि चित्राणि भित्त्वा सञ्चूर्ण्य वारिणि ।
गृहीत्वा चैव यज्ञाङ्गं गङ्गास्रोतसि चिक्षिपुः ॥ २६॥

English
Then the lords of gaṇas of mountain-like huge bodies uprooted the sacrificial posts and tied them to the necks of the Hoṭṛ priests by means of ropes. They broke and powdered the sacrificial vessels of various shapes and sizes dissolved them in water and hurled all the appendages of the sacrifice in the currents of the Gaṅgā river. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र दिव्यान्नपानानां राशयः पर्वतोपमाः ।
क्षीरनद्योऽमृतस्रावाः सुस्निग्धदधिकर्दमाः ॥ २७॥

उच्चावचानि मांसानि भक्ष्याणि सुरभीणि च ।
रसवन्ति च पानानि लेह्यचोष्याणि तानि वै ॥ २८॥

English
There were heaps of foodstuffs and beverages; milk flowed like rivers exuding nectar; curds formed smooth slimy slush. Sweet smelling meat and foodstuffs lay in heaps high and low. There were juicy beverages, and foodstuffs to be lapped up. The heroes ate, chewed and scattered them. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
वीरास्तद्भुञ्जते वक्त्रैर्विलुम्पन्ति क्षिपन्ति च ।
वज्रैश्चक्रैर्महाशूलैः शक्तिभिः पाशपट्टिशैः ॥ २९॥

मुसलैरसिभिष्टङ्कैर्भिन्दिपालैः परश्वधैः ।
उद्धतांस्त्रिदशान्सर्वांल्लोकपालपुरस्सरान् ॥ ३०॥

English
The heroic Bhadras born of Vīrabhadra's body hit and split the haughty gods including the guardians of the quarters by means of discus, thunderbolts, spears, Śikitis, nooses, iron clubs, iron rods, swords, axes, Bhindipalas and huge battle-axes (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
बिभिदुर्बलिनो वीरा वीरभद्राङ्गसम्भवाः ।
छिन्धि भिन्धि क्षिप क्षिप्रं मार्यतां दार्यतामिति ॥ ३१॥

हरस्व प्रहरस्वेति पाटयोत्पाटयेति च ।
संरम्भप्रभवाः क्रूराः शब्दाः श्रवणशङ्कवः ॥ ३२॥

English
"Chop off and split" "Hurl quickly" "Let him be killed" "Take away and strike" "Peel and tear off" such ruthless words full of excitement, usual in wars and jarring to the ears arose from the lords of Gaṇas. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्र तत्र गणेशानां जज्ञिरे समरोचिताः ।
विवृत्तनयनाः केचिद् दष्टदंष्ट्रोष्ठतालवः ॥ ३३॥

आश्रमस्थान् समाकृष्य मारयन्ति तपोधनान् ।
स्रुवानपहरन्तश्च क्षिपन्तोऽग्निं जलेषु च ॥ ३४॥

कलशानपि भिन्दन्तश्छिन्दन्तो मणिवेदिकाः ।
गायन्तश्च नदन्तश्च हसन्तश्च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
रक्तासवं पिबन्तश्च ननृतुर्गणपुङ्गवाः ॥ ३५॥

निर्मथ्य सेन्द्रानमरान् गणेन्द्रा
वृषेन्द्रनागेन्द्रमृगेन्द्रसाराः ।
चक्रुर्बहून्यप्रतिमभावाः
सहर्षरोमाणि विचेष्टितानि ॥ ३६॥

English
Some rolled their eyes, gnashed their fanglike teeth and bit their lips and palates. They pulled out the sages in the hermitages and killed them. They took away sacrificial ladles and spoons and hurled them into fire and water. They broke domes, gemset platforms. They sang, shouted and laughed again and again drinking blood-like wine. The leading Gaṇas danced. (33-36.)

---

Sanskrit
नन्दन्ति केचित्प्रहरन्ति केचित्
धावन्ति केचित्प्रलपन्ति केचित् ॥ ३७॥

English
Pounding up the gods including Indra, the leading Gaṇas, strong like lordly bulls, elephants and lions, of unrivalled glory, perpetrated hair-raising deeds of destruction. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
नृत्यन्ति केचिद्विहसन्ति केचित्
वल्गन्ति केचित्प्रमथा बलेन ॥ ३८॥

English
The Pramathas rejoiced, struck, ran, prattled, danced, laughed and jumped about. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
केचिज्जिघृक्षन्ति घनान्स तोयान्
केचिद्ग्रहीतुं रविमुत्पतन्ति ।
केचित्प्रसर्तुं पवनेन सार्द्ध-
मिच्छन्ति भीमाः प्रमथा वियत्स्थाः ॥ ३९॥

English
Some of them evinced a desire to seize the watery clouds, some jumped up to seize the sun; others wished to blow along with the wind. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
आक्षिप्य केचिच्च वरायुधानि
महाभुजङ्गानिव वैनतेयाः ।
भ्रमन्ति देवानपि विद्रवन्तः
खमण्डले पर्वतकूटकल्पाः ॥ ४०॥

English
Some wielded weapons and rushed through the sky like peaks of mountains; tossed the gods like the Garuḍa tossing huge pythons. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
उत्पाट्य चोत्पाट्य गृहाणि केचित्
सजालवातायनवेदिकानि ।
विक्षिप्य विक्षिप्य जलस्य मध्ये
कालाम्बुदाभाः प्रमथा निनेदुः ॥ ४१॥

English
Some looking like the black clouds uprooted the houses, windows, daises, hurled them into water and roared. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्वर्तितद्वारकपाटकुड्यं
विध्वस्तशालावलभीगवाक्षम् ।
अहो बताभज्यत यज्ञवाट-
मनाप्तवद्वाक्यमिवायथार्थम् ॥ ४२॥

English
The doors, door-frames and walls of the sacrificial mansion were upset; the windows, ceiling frames, the halls were destroyed utterly. The complete edifice of sacrifice crumbled helplessly like a statement without foundation. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
हा नाथ तातेति पितुः सुतेति
भ्रातर्ममाम्बेति च मातुलेति ।
उत्पाट्यमानेषु गृहेषु नार्यो ह्यनाथशब्दान्बहुशः प्रचक्रुः ॥ ४३॥

English
While houses were being dismantled, the women shrieked and cried helplessly "O husband" "O child" "O father" "O brother" "O mother" "O uncle." (43.)
