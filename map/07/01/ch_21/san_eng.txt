### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - (The punishment of the gods)**

---

Sanskrit
२१

वायुरुवाच ।
ततस्त्रिदशमुख्यास्ते विष्णुशक्रपुरोगमाः ।
सर्वे भयपरित्रस्ता दुद्रुवुर्भयविह्वलाः ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said :—  The chiefs of gods headed by Vīṇu and Indra were terribly afraid. They fled in excessive fright. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
निजैरदूषितैरङ्गैर्दृष्ट्वा देवानुपद्रुतान् ।
दण्ड्यानदण्डितान् मत्वा चुकोप गणपुङ्गवः ॥ २॥

English
Seeing that the gods had fled uninjured and thinking that those who deserved to be punished remained unpunished the leading Gana became angry. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्रिशूलमादाय शर्वशक्तिनिबर्हणम् ।
ऊर्ध्वदृष्टिर्महाबाहुर्मुखाज्ज्वालाः समुत्सृजन् ॥ ३॥

अमरानपि दुद्राव द्विरदानिव केसरी ।
तानभिद्रवतस्तस्य गमनं सुमनोहरम् ॥ ४॥

English
He took up the all-destroying trident and looked up. He vomitted flames from his mouth, chased the gods as the lion chases the elephants. His gait as he pursued them resembled that of an elephant in its rut. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
वारणस्येव मत्तस्य जगाम प्रेक्षणीयताम् ।
ततस्तत्क्षोभयामास महत्सुरबलं बली ॥ ५॥

महासरोवरं यद्वन्मत्तो वारणयूथपः ।
विकुर्वन्बहुधा वर्णान्नीलपाण्डुरलोहितान् ॥ ६॥

बिभ्रद् व्याघ्राजिनं वासो हेमप्रवरतारकम् ।
छिन्दन् भिन्दन्नुदन् क्लिन्दन्दारयन्प्रमथन्नपि ॥ ७॥

English
Then he agitated the army of the gods as the lord of elephants in rut stirs up a lake, shedding different hues blue, grey and red. He wore a tiger's skin as his cloth which was inlaid with excellent gold and pearls. Cutting, piercing, moistening, tearing and pounding, Bhadra moved among the hosts of the gods like fire consuming dry grass. (5-7.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यचरद्देवसङ्घेषु भद्रोऽग्निरिव कक्षगः ।
यत्र तत्र महावेगाच्चरन्तं शूलधारिणम् ॥ ८॥

तमेकं त्रिदशाः सर्वे सहस्रमिव मेनिरे ।
भद्रकाली च सङ्क्रुद्धा युद्धवृद्धमदोद्धता ॥ ९॥

English
As he walked about speedily armed with a spear all alone, the gods thought him to be numbering a thousand. The infuriated Bhadrakali excited by the fight pierced through the gods in the war holding spears shedding flames. (8-9.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्तज्वालेन शूलेन निर्बिभेद रणे सुरान् ।
स तया रुरुचे भद्रो रुद्रकोपसमुद्भवः ॥ १०॥

प्रभयेव युगान्ताग्निश्चलया धूमधूम्रया ।
भद्रकाली तदा युद्धे विद्रुतत्रिदशा बभौ ॥ ११॥

English
Bhadra born of Rudra's anger shone in her company as the fire at the time of dissolution with a lustre pale and smoky due to smoke. Pursuing the gods in the course of war, Bhadrakali shone as the flame of fire at the end of the Kalpa that burns the universe. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
कल्पे शेषानलज्वाला दग्धाविश्वजगद्यथा ।
तदा सवाजिनं सूर्यं रुद्रान् रुद्रगणाग्रणीः ॥ १२॥

भद्रो मूर्ध्नि जघानाशु वामपादेन लीलया ।
असिभिः पावकं भद्रः पट्टिशैस्तु यमं यमीम् ॥ १३॥

रुद्रान् दृढेन शूलेन मुद्गरैर्वरुणं दृढैः ।
परिघैर्निरृतिं वायुं टङ्कैष्टङ्कधरः स्वयम् ॥ १४॥

निर्बिभेद रणे वीरो लीलयैव गणेश्वरः ।
सर्वान्देवगणान्सद्यो मुनीन् शम्भोर्विरोधिनः ॥ १५॥

English
Bhadra the leader of Rudraganas angrily kicked the sun and his horse on the head sportively with his left leg. The heroic Ganeśvara, Bhadra with self-control hit Pavaka with his swords, Yama with iron-clubs, Rudras with his spear and Varuna with iron clubs. Holding the axe he hit Vayu with it and Nirṭṭi with big clubs. The war was a sportive game for him. He attacked the gods and the sages antagonistic to Śiva. (12-15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो देवः सरस्वत्या नासिकाग्रं सुशोभनम् ।
चिच्छेद करजाग्रेण देवमातुस्तथैव च ॥ १६॥

English
Then the lord cut off the tip of the nose of Sarasvati as well as of the mother of the gods with the tip of his nail. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
चिच्छेद च कुठारेण बाहुदण्डं विभावसोः ।
अग्रतो द्व्यङ्गुलां जिह्वामात्तहव्यां लुलाव च ॥ १७॥

English
With a dagger he chopped off an arm of Vibhāvasu, and the tongue up to two inches from the tip, of the mother of the gods. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वाहादेव्यास्तथा देवो दक्षिणं नासिकापुटम् ।
चकर्त करजाग्रेण वामं च स्तनचूचुकम् ॥ १८॥

English
The lord nipped off the right nostril and the nipple of the left breast of Svāhā with his nail-end. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
भगस्य विपुले नेत्रे शतपत्रसमप्रभे ।
प्रसह्योत्पाटयामास भद्रः परमवेगवान् ॥ १९॥

English
The impetuous Bhadra uprooted the eyes of Bhaga large and lustrous like the lotus. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
पूष्णो दशनरेखां च दीप्तां मुक्तावलीमिव ।
जघान धनुषः कोट्या स तेनास्पष्टवागभूत् ॥ २०॥

English
With the end of his bow he hit Pusan's pearl like row of teeth. Thereafter Pusan could not utter words clearly. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततश्चन्द्रमसं देवः पादाङ्गुष्ठेन लीलया ।
क्षणं कृमिवदाक्रम्य घर्षयामास भूतले ॥ २१॥

English
Then the lord stamped the moon with his toe, as though he was only a worm and ground him on the ground. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
शिरश्चिच्छेद दक्षस्य भद्रः परमकोपतः ।
क्रोशन्त्यामेव वैरिण्यां भद्रकाल्यै ददौ च तत् ॥ २२॥

English
The infuriated Bhadra cut off the head of Dakṣa and handed it over to Bhadrakālī even as Vīrīnī, wife of Daksa was lamenting. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्रहृष्टा समादाय शिरस्तालफलोपमम् ।
सा देवी कन्दुकक्रीडां चकार समराङ्गणे ॥ २३॥

English
Taking up the head resembling the fruit of a palmyra the goddess played with it like a ball in the battlefield. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो दक्षस्य यज्ञस्त्री कुशीला भर्तृभिर्यथा ।
पादाभ्यां चैव हस्ताभ्यां हन्यते स्म गणेश्वरैः ॥ २४॥

English
Then the sacrifice itself was hit and smashed with feet and hands like fallen women hit by their husbands. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अरिष्टनेमिनं सोमं धर्मं चैव प्रजापतिम् ।
बहुपुत्रं चाङ्गिरसं कृशाश्वं काश्यपं तथा ॥ २५॥

गले प्रगृह्य बलिनो गणपाः सिंहविक्रमाः ।
भर्त्सयन्तो भृशं वाग्भिर्निजघ्नुर्मूर्ध्नि मुष्टिभिः ॥ २६॥

English
Catching hold of by the neck, Ariṣṭanemi, Soma, Dharma, Prajāpati Aṅgiras, father of many sons, Kṛśāśva and Kaśyapa, the powerful chiefs of Gaṇas of leonine exploits rebuked and hit them on the heads with fists. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
धर्षिता भूतवेतालैर्दाराः सुतपरिग्रहाः ।
यथा कलियुगे जारैर्बलेन कुलयोषितः ॥ २७॥

English
Just as in Kaliyuga women of noble families are molested and defiled by paramours forcibly so also the women and the daughters-in-law were molested by Bhūtas and Vetālas. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्च विध्वस्तकलशं भग्नयूपं गतोत्सवम् ।
प्रदीपितमहाशालं प्रभिन्नद्वारतोरणम् ॥ २८॥

उत्पाटितसुरानीकं हन्यमानतपोधनम् ।
प्रशान्तब्रह्मनिर्घोषं प्रक्षीणजनसञ्चयम् ॥ २९॥

क्रन्दमानातुरस्त्रीकं हताशेषपरिच्छदम् ।
शून्यारण्यनिभं जज्ञे यज्ञवाटं तदार्दितम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Thus the sacrificial spot appeared like a desolate forest. The domes were smashed. The posts were broken. The festivities ended. The hall was burnt. The portals and festoons had crumbled. The army of the gods was uprooted. The sages were killed. The Vedic chant had subsided. The population had dwindled. The distressed women shrieked. The appendages were spoiled. (28-30.)

---

Sanskrit
शूलवेगप्ररुग्णाश्च भिन्नबाहूरुवक्षसः ।
विनिकृत्तोत्तमाङ्गाश्च पेतुरुर्व्यां सुरोत्तमाः ॥ ३१॥

English
The excellent gods fell on the ground with their arms, thighs and chests pierced, with the heads cut off with the trident. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हतेषु तेषु देवेषु पतितेषु सहस्रशः ।
प्रविवेश गणेशानः क्षणादाहवनीयकम् ॥ ३२॥

English
When thousands of gods were killed and their bodies lay sprawled on the ground, the chief of Ganas entered the place of sacrificial fires. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रविष्टमथ तं दृष्ट्वा भद्रं कालाग्निसन्निभम् ।
दुद्राव मरणाद्भीतो यज्ञो मृगवपुर्धरः ॥ ३३॥

English
On seeing that Bhadra resembling the deadly fire had come the sacrifice was afraid of death and fled assuming the form of a deer. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
स विस्फार्य महच्चापं दृढज्याघोषणभीषणम् ।
भद्रस्तमभिदुद्राव विक्षिपन्नेव सायकान् ॥ ३४॥

English
Bhadra pursued him drawing his great bow, terrible due to the twanging sound of the firm bow-string, and discharging the arrows. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
आकर्णपूर्णमाकृष्टं धनुरम्बुदसन्निभम् ।
नादयामास च ज्यां द्यां खं च भूमिं च सर्वशः ॥ ३५॥

English
He sounded the bow by drawing the string to the ears like the thundering cloud. The bowstring, heaven, sky and earth reverberated. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
तमुपश्रुत्य सन्नादं हतोऽस्मीत्येव विह्वलम् ।
शरेणार्धेन वक्रेण स वीरोऽध्वरपूरुषम् ॥ ३६॥

महाभयस्खलत्पादं वेपन्तं विगतत्विषम् ।
मृगरूपेण धावन्तं विशिरस्कं तदाकरोत् ॥ ३७॥

English
The sacrifice was terrified on hearing the sound. 'O I am doomed', he thought. He was trembling with the legs shaking. His lustre disappeared. He ran in the form of a deer. The heroic Bhadra, with a half curved arrow, decapitated him. (36-37.)

---

Sanskrit
तमीदृशमवज्ञातं दृष्ट्वा वै सूर्यसम्भवम् ।
विष्णुः परमसङ्क्रुद्धो युद्धायाभवदुद्यतः ॥ ३८॥

English
On seeing the sacrifice thus insulted, Viṣṇu was infuriated and got ready for a fight. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तमुवाह महावेगात्स्कन्धेन नतसन्धिना ।
सर्वेषां वयसां राजा गरुडः पन्नगाशनः ॥ ३९॥

देवाश्च हतशिष्टा ये देवराजपुरोगमाः ।
प्रचक्रुस्तस्य साहाय्यं प्राणांस्त्यक्तुमिवोद्यताः ॥ ४०॥

विष्णुना सहितान् देवान् मृगेन्द्रः क्रोष्टुकानिव ।
दृष्ट्वा जहास भूतेन्द्रो मृगेन्द्र इव विव्यथः ॥ ४१॥

English
Garuḍa the devourer of serpents and the king of birds bore him speedily over his shoulders. The god Indra and others who had escaped helped him as if ready to abandon their lives. Seeing them along with Viṣṇu like the lion viewing the jackals the lord of Bhūtas laughed. He looked like a lion without distress. (39-41.)
