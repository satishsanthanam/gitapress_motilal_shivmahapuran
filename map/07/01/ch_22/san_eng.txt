### **CHAPTER TWENTYTWO - (The destruction of Dakṣa's sacrifice)**

---

Sanskrit
२२

वायुरुवाच
तस्मिन्नवसरे व्योम्नि समाविरभवद्रथः ।
सहस्रसूर्यसङ्काशश्चारुचीरवृषध्वजः ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said :—  At that time a chariot resembling a thousand suns and with the emblem of a bull on the banner cloth appeared in the sky. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वरत्नद्वयोदारो रथचक्रचतुष्टयः ।
सञ्चितानेकदिव्यास्त्रशस्त्ररत्नपरिष्कृतः ॥ २॥

English
The splendid chariot had two horses and four wheels. Several divine weapons and missiles had been kept ready therein. It was embellished with jewels. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्यापि रथवर्यस्यासीत्स एव हि सारथिः ।
यश्चैव त्रैपुरे युद्धे पूर्वं शार्वरथे स्थितः ॥ ३॥

English
It was driven by the same charioteer who drove Śiva's chariot in the latter's war with the Tripuras. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स तं रथवरं ब्रह्मा शासनादेव शूलिनः ।
हरेः समीपमानीय कृताञ्जलिरभाषत ॥ ४॥

English
At the bidding of Śiva, Brahmā brought the excellent chariot to Vīrabhadra and spoke with palms joined in reverence. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
भगवन् भद्र भद्राङ्ग भगवानिन्दुभूषणः ।
आज्ञापयति वीरस्त्वां रथमारोढुमव्ययः ॥ ५॥

English
"O lord Vīrabhadra the eternal moon-crested lord commands you to mount the chariot. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
रैभ्याश्रमसमीपस्थस्त्र्यम्बकोऽम्बिकया सह ।
सम्पश्यते महाबाहो दुस्सहं ते पराक्रमम् ॥ ६॥

English
O great-armed one, Śiva, stationed near the hermitage of Raibhya along with Śivā, is witnessing your unbearable exploit. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा स वीरो गणकुञ्जरः ।
आरुरोह रथं दिव्यमनुगृह्य पितामहम् ॥ ७॥

English
On hearing his words and Blessing Brahmā, the heroic leading Gaṇa mounted the divine chariot. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा रथवरे तस्मिन् स्थिते ब्रह्मणि सारथौ ।
भद्रस्य ववृधे लक्ष्मी रुद्रस्येव पुरद्विषः ॥ ८॥

English
In the excellent chariot where Brahmā sat as the charioteer the splendour of Bhadra increased like that of Rudra, the enemy of the Tripuras. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शङ्खवरं दीप्तं पूर्णचन्द्रसमप्रभम् ।
प्रदध्मौ वदने कृत्वा भानुकम्पो महाबलः ॥ ९॥

English
Then keeping an excellent conch shining like the full moon, in his mouth, the powerful Bhānukampa blew on it. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य शङ्खस्य तं नादं भिन्नसागरसन्निभम् ।
श्रुत्वा भयेन देवानां जज्वाल जठरानलः ॥ १०॥

English
At the sound of the conch resembling that of the swan, the fire in the belly of the gods blazed with fear. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यक्षविद्याधराहीन्द्रैः सिद्धैर्युद्धदिदृक्षुभिः ।
क्षणेन निबिडीभूताः साकाशविवरा दिशः ॥ ११॥

English
In an instant the quarters including open spaces and crevices were filled up and thronged with leading Yakjas Vidyādharas, serpents and Siddhas who desired to witness the battle. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शार्ङ्गेण चापाङ्कात्स नारायणनीरदः ।
महता बाणवर्षेण तुतोद गणगोवृषम् ॥ १२॥

English
Then Nārāyaṇa as the cloud inflicted pain on the Gaṇas as the cattle, through a great shower of arrows from his bow. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा विष्णुमायान्तं शतधा बाणवर्षिणम् ।
स चाददे धनुर्जैत्रं भद्रो बाणसहस्रमुक् ॥ १३॥

English
On seeing Visnu coming and discharging arrows Bhadra took up his bow and showered thousands of arrows. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
समादाय च तद्दिव्यं धनुः समरभैरवम् ।
शनैर्विस्फारयामास मेरुं धनुरिवेश्वरः ॥ १४॥

English
He took up the divine bow and slowly drew it like Siva who drew the bow Meru. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य विस्फार्य्यमाणस्य धनुषोऽभून्महास्वनः ।
तेन स्वनेन महता पृथिवीं समकम्पयत् ॥ १५॥

English
As the bow was drawn a loud report was produced shaking the earth. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शरवरं घोरं दीप्तमाशीविषोपमम् ।
जग्राह गणपः श्रीमान्स्वयमुग्रपराक्रमः ॥ १६॥

English
Then the chief of Ganas, of fierce valour and glory took an excellent arrow blazing like a serpent. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
बाणोद्धारे भुजो ह्यस्य तूणीवदनसङ्गतः ।
प्रत्यदृश्यत वल्मीकं विवेक्षुरिव पन्नगः ॥ १७॥

English
His hand in contact with the opening of the quiver for taking up the arrows appeared like a serpent wishing to enter an anthill. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
समुद्धृतः करे तस्य तत्क्षणं रुरुचे शरः ।
महाभुजङ्गसन्दष्टो यथा बालभुजङ्गमः ॥ १८॥

English
The arrow lifted and held in the hand shone like the young one of a serpent held within the mouth of a great serpent. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शरेण घनतीव्रेण भद्रो रुद्रपराक्रमः ।
विव्याध कुपितो गाढं ललाटे विष्णुमव्ययम् ॥ १९॥

English
With his stout and sharp arrow, the infuriated Bhadra who resembled Rudra, hit the unchanging Visnu on his forehead. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
ललाटेऽभिहतो विष्णुः पूर्वमेवावमानितः ।
चुकोप गणपेन्द्राय मृगेन्द्रायेव गोवृषः ॥ २०॥

English
Visnu thus insulted and hit on the forehead became angry with the chief of Ganas like a cow or bull becoming angry with the lion. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वशनिकल्पेन क्रूरास्येन महेषुणा ।
विव्याध गणराजस्य भुजे भुजगसन्निभे ॥ २१॥

English
Then he hit the serpent like arm of the chief of Ganas, with a cruel, pointed great arrow resembling the thunderbolt. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि तस्य भुजे भूयः सूर्यायुतसमप्रभम् ।
विससर्ज शरं वेगाद्वीरभद्रो महाबलः ॥ २२॥

English
The powerful Virabhadra too, with a great velocity discharged an arrow brilliant like ten thousand suns again at his arm. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
स च विष्णुः पुनर्भद्रं भद्रो विष्णुं तथा पुनः ।
स च तं च स तं विप्राः शरैस्तावनुजघ्नतुः ॥ २३॥

English
Visnu hit Bhadra. Bhadra hit Visnu. O brahmins both of them hit each other. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोः परस्परं वेगाच्छरानाशु विमुञ्चतोः ।
द्वयोः समभवद्युद्धं तुमुलं रोमहर्षणम् ॥ २४॥

English
The tumultuous hair-raising fight between the two discharging the arrows on each other in quick succession then ensued. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तद्दृष्ट्वा तुमुलं युद्धं तयोरेव परस्परम् ।
हाहाकारो महानासीदाकाशे खेचरेरितः ॥ २५॥

English
On seeing their mutual noisy battle the sounds of 'Ha-Ha' raised by the aerial wanderers rose in the sky. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ततस्त्वनलतुण्डेन शरेणादित्यवर्चसा ।
विव्याध सुदृढं भद्रं विष्णोर्महति वक्षसि ॥ २६॥

English
Then Bhadra hit in the broad chest of Vişnu with an arrow that shone like the sun and the tip of which blazed like fire. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
स तु तीव्रप्रपातेन शरेण दृढमाहतः ।
महतीं रुजमासाद्य निपपात विमोहितः ॥ २७॥

English
Acutely hit by the arrow that fell sharply, Vişnu suffered great pain and fell into swoon. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः क्षणादिवोत्थाय लब्धसंज्ञस्तदा हरिः ।
सर्वाण्यपि च दिव्यास्त्राण्यथैनं प्रत्यवासृजत् ॥ २८॥

English
He regained consciousness instantaneously and got up. He discharged his weapons against Bhadra. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
स च विष्णुर्धनुर्मुक्तान्सर्वान् शर्वचमूपतिः ।
सहसा वारयामास घोरैः प्रतिशरैः शरान् ॥ २९॥

English
The chief of the army of Siva stopped all the missiles discharged from Vişnu's bow by means of terrible anti-missiles. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विष्णुः स्वनामाङ्कं बाणमव्याहतं क्वचित् ।
ससर्ज क्रोधरक्ताक्षः तमुद्दिश्य गणेश्वरम् ।
तं बाणं बाणवर्येण भद्रो भद्राह्वयेण तु ॥ ३०॥

English
Then Vişnu with eyes reddened by anger discharged an arrow in which his name had been engraved and which had never been obstructed anywhere, against the Gana chief. Showering arrows, lord Bhadra split the arrow into pieces on the way before it reached him. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
अप्राप्तमेव भगवान् चिच्छेद शतधा पथि ।
अथैकेनेषुणा शार्ङ्गं द्वाभ्यां पक्षौ गरुत्मतः ॥ ३१॥

निमेषादेव चिच्छेद तदद्भुतमिवाभवत् ।
ततो योगबलाद्विष्णुर्देहाद्देवान् सुदारुणान् ॥ ३२॥

शङ्खचक्रगदाहस्तान् विससर्ज सहस्रशः ।
सर्वांस्तान्क्षणमात्रेण त्रैपुरानिव शङ्करः ॥ ३३॥

English
Then within an instant he split the bow with a single arrow and the wings of Garuda with the two arrows. This was a wonderful feat. Then by his Yogic powers Vişnu let loose from his body thousands of terrific gods holding conch, discus and the club. But the mighty Bhadra burnt all of them instantaneously by means of the fire of his eyes like Siva who burnt the Tripuras. (31-33.)

---

Sanskrit
निर्ददाह महाबाहुर्नेत्रसृष्टेन वह्निना ।
ततः क्रुद्धतरो विष्णुश्चक्रमुद्यम्य सत्वरः ॥ ३४॥

तस्मिन्वीरे समुत्स्रष्टुं तदानीमुद्यतोऽभवत् ।
तं दृष्ट्वा चक्रमुद्यम्य पुरतः समुपस्थितम् ॥ ३५॥

स्मयन्निव गणेशानो व्यष्टम्भयदयत्नतः ।
स्तम्भिताङ्गस्तु तच्चक्रं घोरमप्रतिमं क्वचित् ॥ ३६॥

English
Thus infuriated Vişnu raised his discus in a hurry and attempted to hurl it on the hero. On seeing him in front with the discus lifted up, the chief of the Ganas smiled and without any strain made his hand stiff and benumbed. With his limbs turned numb, Vişnu became incapable though he was desirous of hurling the unequalled and terrible discus. (34-36.)

---

Sanskrit
इच्छन्नपि समुत्स्रष्टुं न विष्णुरभवत्क्षमः ।
श्वसन्निवैकमुद्धृत्य बाहुं चक्रसमन्वितम् ॥ ३७॥

अतिष्ठदलसो भूत्वा पाषाणामिव निश्चलः ।
विशरीरो यथा जीवो विश‍ृङ्गो वा यथा वृषः ॥ ३८॥

विदंष्ट्रश्च यथा सिंहस्तथा विष्णुरवस्थितः ।
तं दृष्ट्वा दुर्दशापन्नं विष्णुमिन्द्रादयः सुराः ।
समुन्नद्धा गणेन्द्रेण मृगेन्द्रेणेव गोवृषाः ॥ ३९॥

English
Gasping for breath with a hand holding the discus he stood idly, immobile like a stone, like the soul without a body, like a bull without a horn or like a lion without its fangs. So stood Vişnu. On seeing Vişnu in a miserable plight the infuriated Indra and other gods took up their weapons. They hastened to fight with the chief of Gaṇas like cows or bulls attempting to fight with a lion. (37-39.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रगृहीतायुधा यौद्धुं क्रुद्धाः समुपतस्थिरे ।
तान्दृष्ट्वा समरे भद्रः क्षुद्रानिव हरिर्मृगान् ॥ ४०॥

साक्षाद् रुद्रतनुर्वीरो वरवीरगणावृतः ।
अट्टहासेन घोरेण व्यष्टम्भयदनिन्दितः ॥ ४१॥

English
On glancing at them as a lion glances at the deer, Bhadra of Rudra's body, the hero surrounded by excellent heroes, benumbed them with a boisterous laugh. (40-41.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा शतमखस्यापि सवज्रो दक्षिणः करः ।
सिसृक्षोरेव उद्वज्रश्चित्रीकृत इवाभवत् ॥ ४२॥

English
The right hand of Indra who desired to release the thunderbolt it held remained steady as painted in a picture. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्येषामपि सर्वेषां सरक्ता अपि बाहवः ।
अलसानामिवारम्भास्तादृशाः प्रतियान्त्युत ॥ ४३॥

English
The hands of others too became fixed, as the activities of idle men at the start remain unprogressive. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं भगवता तेन व्याहताशेषवैभवात् ।
अमराः समरे तस्य पुरतः स्थातुमक्षमाः ॥ ४४॥

English
Thus the gods with their efficacy thwarted in the battle were rendered incapable of standing in front of him. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तब्धैरवयवैरेव दुद्रुवुर्भयविह्वलाः ।
स्थितिं च चक्रिरे युद्धे वीरतेजोऽभयाकुलाः ॥ ४५॥

English
Frightened by the splendour of Virabhadra they could not stay further in the battle. In that fierce battle they fled even with their benumbed limbs. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्रुतांस्त्रिदशान्वीरान्वीरभद्रो महाभुजः ।
विव्याध निशितैर्बाणैएर्मेघो वर्षैरिवाचलान् ॥ ४६॥

English
The mighty Virabhadra hit the fleeing warriors, with sharp arrows like the cloud hitting the mountains with sharp showers. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
बहवस्तस्य वीरस्य बाहवः परिघोपमाः ।
शस्त्रैश्चकाशिरे दीप्तैः साग्निज्वाला इवोरगाः ॥ ४७॥

English
The multitudinous arms of Virabhadra, resembling iron clubs, shone with their various shining weapons like serpents with fiery flames. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्त्रशस्त्राण्यनेकानि स वीरो विसृजन्बभौ ।
विसृजन्सर्वभूतानि यथादौ विश्वसम्भवः ॥ ४८॥

English
The hero discharging weapons and missiles shone like Brahma discharging (creating) all living beings. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा रश्मिभिरादित्यः प्रच्छादयति मेदिनीम् ।
तथा वीरः क्षणादेव शरैः प्राच्छादयद्दिशः ॥ ४९॥

English
Just as the sun covers the earth by his rays so the hero enveloped the quarters by means of his arrows. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
खमण्डले गणेन्द्रस्य शराः कनकभूषिताः ।
उत्पतन्तस्तडिद्रूपैरुपमानपदं ययुः ॥ ५०॥

English
The arrows of the Gana chieftains embellished with gold and flying in the firmament like lightning became the standard of comparison. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
महान्तस्ते सुरगणान् मण्डूकानिव डुण्डुभाः ।
प्राणैर्वियोजयामासुः पपुश्च रुधिरासवम् ॥ ५१॥

English
The great arrows took away the lives of the gods even as the water snakes squeeze the lives out of the frogs. They drank their blood as though that were wine. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
निकृत्तबाहवः केचित्केचिल्लूनवराननाः ।
पार्श्वे विदारिताः केचिन्निपेतुरमरा भुवि ॥ ५२॥

English
The hands of some had been cut off. The faces of some had been split. Some gods fell on the ground with their sides lacerated and bruised. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
विशिखोन्मथितैर्गात्रैर्बहुभिश्छिन्नसन्धिभिः ।
विवृत्तनयनाः केचिन्निपेतुर्भूतले मृताः ।
गां प्रवेष्टुमिवेच्छन्तः खं गन्तुमिव लिप्सवः ॥ ५३॥

अलब्धात्मनिरोधानां व्यलीयन्तः परस्परम् ।
भूमौ केचित्प्रविविशुः पर्वतानां गुहाः परे ॥ ५४॥

English
With their limbs shattered by the arrows, joints severed and eyes dislocated they fell dead on the ground. Some desired to enter the earth and some wished to go up to the sky. Being unobstructed they merged into one another. Some entered the earth. Others entered the caves of mountains. Others went up to the sky. Still others entered the water. (53-54.)

---

Sanskrit
अपरे जग्मुराकाशं परे च विविशुर्जलम् ।
तथा सञ्छिन्नसर्वाङ्गैः स वीरस्त्रिदशैर्बभौ ॥ ५५॥

परिग्रस्तः प्रजावर्गो भगवानिव भैरवः ।
