### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - (Śiva's Fury)**

---

Sanskrit
२३

वायुरुवाच ।
इति सञ्छिन्नभिन्नाङ्गा देवा विष्णुपुरोगमाः ।
क्षणात्कष्टां दशामेत्य त्रेसुः स्तोकावशेषिताः ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said :— Thus the gods headed by Vişnu with their bodies split and mutilated attained a miserable plight and trembled. Only a few of them survived. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रस्तांस्तान्समरे वीरान् देवानन्यांश्च वै गणाः ।
प्रमथाः परमक्रुद्धा वीरभद्रप्रणोदिताः ॥ २॥

प्रगृह्य च तथा दोषं निगडैरायसैर्दृढैः ।
बबन्धुः पाणिपादेषु कन्धरेषूदरेषु च ॥ ३॥

English
The excessively infuriated Pramathas and Gañas urged by Virabhadra seized the warriors frightened of the battle tying their hands, feet, shoulders and bellies by means of strong iron fetters. (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मिन्नवसरे ब्रह्मा भद्रमद्रीन्द्रजानुतम् ।
सारथ्याल्लब्धवात्सल्यः प्रार्थयन् प्रणतोऽब्रवीत् ॥ ४॥

English
In the meantime Brahma who had secured his favour due to his charioteership, implored Bhadra, the devotee of the daughter of Himavat. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
अलं क्रोधेन भगवन्नष्टाश्चैते दिवौकसः ।
प्रसीद क्षम्यतां सर्वं रोमजैः सह सुव्रत ॥ ५॥

English
"O lord, enough of this fury. The gods are ruined. Please forgive their faults. O you of auspicious rites." (5.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विज्ञापितस्तेन ब्रह्मणा परमेष्ठिना ।
शमं जगाम सम्प्रीतो गणपस्तस्य गौरवात् ॥ ६॥

English
Implored thus by Brahma the delighted chief of Gañas became calm. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
देवाश्च लब्धावसरा देवदेवस्य मन्त्रिणः ।
धारयन्तोऽञ्जलीन्मूर्ध्नि तुष्टुवुर्विविधैः स्तवैः ॥ ७॥

English
Getting the opportunity through Brahma the gods joined their palms in reverence above their heads and eulogised him by various hymns. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
नमः शिवाय शान्ताय यज्ञहन्त्रे त्रिशूलिने ।
रुद्रभद्राय रुद्राणां पतये रुद्रभूतये ॥ ८॥

English
The gods said :—  Obeisance to Śiva the quiescent. Obeisance to the trident-bearing destroyer of sacrifice. Obeisance to Bhadra, the lord of Rudras, the wealth and prosperity of Rudras. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
कालाग्निरुद्ररूपाय कालकामाङ्गहारिणे ।
देवतानां शिरोहन्त्रे दक्षस्य च दुरात्मनः ॥ ९॥

English
Obeisance to Rudra the deity of deadly fire; to the slayer of death and Kāma; the remover of the heads of the gods and of the wicked-souled Dakṣa. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
संसर्गादस्य पापस्य दक्षस्य क्लिष्टकर्मणः ।
शासिताः समरे वीर त्वया वयमनिन्दिताः ॥ १०॥

English
O hero, incensurable though we are we have been chastised by you in the war due to our association with this sinner Dakṣa. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
दग्धाश्चामी वयं सर्वे त्वत्तो भीताश्च भो प्रभो ।
त्वमेव गतिरस्माकं त्राहि नः शरणागतान् ॥ ११॥

English
We have been burnt. O lord, we are afraid of you. You alone are our goal. Save us who have sought refuge in you. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
तुष्टस्त्वेवं स्तुतो देवान् विसृज्य निगडात्प्रभुः ।
आनयद्देवदेवस्य समीपममरानिह ॥ १२॥

English
*Vāyu said :—*  Thus eulogised the lord was satisfied. He set gods free from fetters. He took them to the presence of lord Śiva. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
देवोऽपि तत्र भगवानन्तरिक्षे स्थितः प्रभुः ।
सगणः सर्वगः शर्वः सर्वलोकमहेश्वरः ॥ १३॥

English
Śiva, the omnipresent lord of the worlds, was standing in the firmament along with the Gaṇas. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वा परमेशानं देवा विष्णुपुरोगमाः ।
प्रीता अपि च भीताश्च नमश्चक्रुर्महेश्वरम् ॥ १४॥

English
On seeing him the gods headed by Viṣṇu were pleased and awe-struck. They bowed to him. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्ट्वा तानमरान्भीतान्प्रणतार्तिहरो हरः ।
इदमाह महादेवः प्रहसन् प्रेक्ष्य पार्वतीम् ॥ १५॥

English
Seeing them frightened, Śiva the destroyer of the distress of his devotees said laughingly after glancing at Pārvatī. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेव उवाच ।
मा भैष्ट त्रिदशाः सर्वे यूयं वै मामिकाः प्रजाः ।
अनुग्रहार्थमेवेह धृतो दण्डः कृपालुना ॥ १६॥

English
*Lord Śiva said :—*  O gods, do not be afraid. You are my own subjects. It is only to bless you that the punishment has been meted out to you by the merciful lord. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
भवतां निर्ज्जराणां हि क्षान्तोऽस्माभिर्व्यतिक्रमः ।
क्रुद्धेष्वस्मासु युष्माकं न स्थितिर्न च जीवितम् ॥ १७॥

English
The transgression of you the gods has been forgiven by us. When we are infuriated you have neither sustenance nor life. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
इत्युक्तास्त्रिदशाः सर्वे शर्वेणामिततेजसा ।
सद्यो विगतसन्देहा ननृतुर्विबुधा मुदा ॥ १८॥

English
*Vāyu said :—*  Thus spoken to by Śiva of unmeasured splendour the gods immediately had their doubts cleared. The gods danced joyously. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसन्नमनसो भूत्वानन्दविह्वलमानसाः ।
स्तुतिमारेभिरे कर्तुं शङ्करस्य दिवौकसः ॥ १९॥

English
Becoming delighted and excited in their minds with bliss, the gods began to eulogise Śiva.' (19.)

---

Sanskrit
देवा ऊचुः ।
त्वमेव देवाखिललोककर्ता
पाता च हर्ता परमेश्वरोऽसि ।
कविष्णुरुद्राख्यस्वरूपभेदै
रजस्तमः सत्त्वधृतात्ममूर्त्ते ॥ २०॥

English
*The gods said :—*  O lord assuming the forms of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra through Rajas, Tamas and Sattva you are the creator, protector and the annihilator of the worlds. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वमूर्त्ते नमस्तेऽस्तु विश्वभावन पावन ।
अमूर्त्ते भक्तहेतोर्हि गृहीताकृतिसौख्यद ॥ २१॥

English
O omniformed, O conceiver of the universe, O sanctifier. O formless, O bestower of happiness you take up forms only for the sake of devotees. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
चन्द्रोऽगदो हि देवेश कृपातस्तव शङ्कर ।
निमज्जनान्मृतः प्राप सुखं मिहिरजाजलिः ॥ २२॥

English
O Siva, O lord of gods, it is due to your favour that the moon became free from ailment, when dead she plunged into Yamuna and regained life and happiness. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सीमन्तिनी हतधवा तव पूजनतः प्रभो ।
सौभाग्यमतुलं प्राप सोमवारव्रतात्सुतान् ॥ २३॥

English
Simantini whose husband had died, O lord, attained unequalled marital bliss by worshipping you. By performing the holy rites on Mondays she bore sons. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकराय ददौ देवः स्वीयं पदमनुत्तमम् ।
सुदर्शनमरक्षस्त्वं नृपमण्डलभीतितः ॥ २४॥

English
The lord gave excellent region to Srikara. You protected Sudarsana from the fear of danger from the hosts of kings. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
मेदुरं तारयामास सदारं च घृणानिधिः ।
शारदां विधवां चक्रे सधवां क्रियया भवान् ॥ २५॥

English
The storehouse of mercy enabled Medura to cross the ocean of worldly existence. By your miraculous activity you enlivened the husband of Sarada. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
भद्रायुषो विपत्तिं च विच्छिद्य त्वमदाः सुखम् ।
सौमिनी भवबन्धाद्वै मुक्ताभूत्तव सेवनात् ॥ २६॥

English
Destroying the calamity of Bhadrāyus you conferred happiness on him. By serving you Saumini became free from worldly bondage. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
त्वं शम्भो कहरीशाश्च रजस्सत्त्वतमोगुणैः ।
कर्ता पाता तथा हर्ता जनानुग्रहकाङ्क्षया ॥ २७॥

English
Viṣṇu said :—  O Siva you are Brahma, Visnu and Siva by means of the Gunas Rajas, Sattva and Tamas. With the desire to bless the people you became the creator, sustainer and the annihilator. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वगर्वापहारी च सर्वतेजोविलासकः ।
सर्वविद्यादिगूढश्च सर्वानुग्रहकारकः ॥ २८॥

English
You are the destroyer of the arrogance and the brilliance of everyone. You are the hidden secret of all lores, the cause of blessings for all. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वत्तः सर्वं च त्वं सर्वं त्वयि सर्वं गिरीश्वर ।
त्राहि त्राहि पुनस्त्राहि कृपां कुरु ममोपरि ॥ २९॥

English
O lord, everything originates from you. You are all. Everything is in you. Save us again. Please be merciful on me. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अथास्मिन्नन्तरे ब्रह्मा प्रणिपत्य कृताञ्जलिः ।
एवं त्ववसरं प्राप्य व्यज्ञापयत शूलिने ॥ ३०॥

English
In the meantime, getting the opportunity Brahma bowed to him with palms joined in reverence and informed the trident-bearing lord. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
जय देव महादेव प्रणतार्तिविभञ्जन ।
ईदृशेष्वपराधेषु कोऽन्यस्त्वत्तः प्रसीदति ॥ ३१॥

English
Brahmā said:—  O lord, be victorious. O destroyer of the distress of your devotees, who else other than you becomes pleased when offences are of this nature ? (31.)

---

Sanskrit
लब्धात्मानो भविष्यन्ति ये पुरा निहता मृधे ।
प्रत्यापत्तिर्न कस्य स्यात्प्रसन्ने परमेश्वरे ॥ ३२॥

English
Those who are killed in battle will regain their souls. When the lord is delighted who will not revive ? (32.)

---

Sanskrit
यदिदं देवदेवानां कृतमन्तुषु दूषणम् ।
तदिदं भूषणं मन्ये त अङ्गीकारगौरवात् ॥ ३३॥

English
The fault in the offences committed by the gods, O lord, is actually a blessing because of the weightiness of your acceptance. I consider so. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
इति विज्ञाप्यमानस्तु ब्रह्मणा परमेष्ठिना ।
विलोक्य वदनं देव्या देवदेवः स्मयन्निव ॥ ३४॥

पुत्रभूतस्य वात्सल्याद् ब्रह्मणः पद्मजन्मनः ।
देवादीनां यथापूर्वमङ्गानि प्रददौ प्रभुः ॥ ३५॥

English
When Brahmā implored thus, the lord smiled and glanced at the face of the goddess. As a result of his affection to Brahmā who was like a son unto him, the lord revived the gods to life. (34-35.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमथाद्यैश्च या देव्यो दण्डिता देवमातरः ।
तासामपि यथापूर्वाण्यङ्गानि गिरिशो ददौ ॥ ३६॥

English
He restored the goddesses—mothers of the gods who had been punished by the Pramathas and others. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षस्य भगवानेव स्वयं ब्रह्मा पितामहः ।
तत्पापानुगुणं चक्रे जरच्छागमुखं मुखम् ॥ ३७॥

English
Lord Brahmā made the face of Dakṣa look like the the face of an old goat as a befitting punishment for his sins. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि संज्ञां ततो लब्ध्वा स दृष्ट्वा जीवितः सुधीः ।
भीतः कृताञ्जलिः शम्भुं तुष्टाव प्रलपन्बहु ॥ ३८॥

English
He was resuscitated and endowed with good intellect. On seeing Śiva he was afraid. With palms joined in reverence he eulogised Śiva. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्ष उवाच ।
जय देव जगन्नाथ लोकानुग्रहकारक ।
कृपां कुरु महेशानापराधं मे क्षमस्व ह ॥ ३९॥

English
*Dakṣa said:—*  O lord of the universe, O cause of the blessing for the worlds, O lord, be merciful. Forgive my guilt. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
कर्त्ता भर्त्ता च हर्ता च त्वमेव जगतां प्रभुः ।
मया ज्ञातं विशेषेण विष्ण्वादिसकलेश्वरः ॥ ४०॥

English
You are the creator, protector and the annihilator of the worlds. This has been known by me particularly. You are the lord of Viṣṇu and all others. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयैव विततं सर्वं व्याप्तं सृष्टं च नाशितम् ।
न हि त्वदधिकाः केचिदीशास्तेऽच्युतकादयः ॥ ४१॥

English
Everything is spread and pervaded by you alone, created and destroyed by you alone. Viṣṇu and other lords are not superior to you. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
तं तथा व्याकुलं भीतं प्रलपन्तं कृतागसम् ।
स्मयन्निवावदत्प्रेक्ष्य मा भैरिति घृणानिधिः ॥ ४२॥

English
*Vāyu said :—*  The lord, the storehouse of mercy glanced at the guilty one who was emotionally distressed and smilingly said “Do not fear”. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
तथोक्त्वा ब्रह्मणस्तस्य पितुः प्रियचिकीर्षया ।
गाणपत्यं ददौ तस्मै दक्षायाक्षयमीश्वरः ॥ ४३॥

English
Saying so and with a desire to please his father Brahmā, the lord bestowed on Dakṣa the imperishable chief-tancy of the Gaṇas. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो ब्रह्मादयो देवा अभिवन्द्य कृताञ्जलिः ।
तुष्टुवुः प्रश्रया वाचा शङ्करं गिरिजाधिपम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Then Brahmā and other gods saluted the lord with palms joined in reverence. In words of humility they eulogised the lord. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मादय ऊचुः ।
जय शङ्कर देवेश दीनानाथ महाप्रभो ।
कृपां कुरु महेशानापराधं नो क्षमस्व वै ॥ ४५॥

English
Brahmā and others said :—  O Siva, O lord of the gods of the distressed and the helpless, O great lord, be merciful, forgive our guilt. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
मखपाल मखाधीश मखविध्वंसकारक ।
कृपां कुरु महेशानापराधं नः क्षमस्व वै ॥ ४६॥

English
O protector of sacrifices, O lord of sacrifices, O destroyer of sacrifices, O Mahesana, forgive our guilt. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव परेशान भक्तप्राणप्रपोषक ।
दुष्टदण्डप्रदः स्वामिन्कृपां कुरु नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ ४७॥

English
O lord of the gods, O great lord, O nourisher of the vital breath of your devotees, O lord, bestower of punishment on the wicked, be merciful. Obeisance be to you. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं प्रभो गर्वहर्ता वै दुष्टानां त्वामजानताम् ।
रक्षको हि विशेषेण सतां त्वत्सक्तचेतसाम् ॥ ४८॥

English
O lord, you remove the arrogance of the wicked who do not know you. You are the protector of the good whose minds are devoted to you. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
अद्भुतं चरितं ते हि निश्चितं कृपया तव ।
सर्वापराधः क्षन्तव्यो विभवो दीनवत्सलाः ॥ ४९॥

English
Certainly due to your kindness your conduct is wonderful. O lord, forgive us for our fault. Lords are favourably disposed towards the distressed. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
इति स्तुतो महादेवो ब्रह्माद्यैरमरैः प्रभुः ।

English
Vāyu said :—  Thus eulogised by Brahma and other gods lord Siva, the ocean of mercy, favourably disposed towards the devotees was pleased. (50.)

---

Sanskrit
चकारानुग्रहं तेषां ब्रह्मादीनां दिवौकसाम् ।
ददौ वरांश्च सुप्रीत्या शङ्करो दीनवत्सलः ॥ ५१॥

English
Siva favourably disposed to the distressed blessed Brahma and other gods and delightedly granted them boons. (51.)

---

Sanskrit

परमकारुणिकः परमेश्वरः ।
अनुगतस्मितलक्षणया गिरा
शमितसर्वभयः समभाषत ॥ ५२॥

English
Then the merciful lord who made their fear subside addressed the gods who had sought refuge in him, with words characterised by smile. (52.)

---

Sanskrit

र्विधिनियोगवशादिव यन्त्रितैः ।
शरणमेव गतानवलोक्य व-
स्तदखिलं किल विस्मृतमेव नः ॥ ५३॥

English
Śiva said :—  On seeing you all seeking refuge in me your entire guilt and sin committed as if urged by fate, has been excused by us. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
तदिह यूयमपि प्रकृतं मन-
स्यविगणय्य विमर्दमपत्रपाः ।
हरिविरिञ्चिसुरेन्द्रमुखाः
सुखं व्रजत देवपुरं प्रति सम्प्रति ॥ ५४॥

English
O gods headed by Visnu, Brahma and Indra return happily to Devapura$^{218}$ unmindful of the present suppression and without the sense of shame in your minds. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सुरानभिधाय सुरेश्वरो
निकृतदक्षकृतक्रतुरक्रतुः ।
स गिरिजानुचरः सपरिच्छदः
स्थित इवाम्बरतोन्तरधाद्धरः ॥ ५५॥

English
Saying this to the gods lord Siva the destroyer of the sacrifice performed by Daksa, along with Siva, his attendants and followers disappeared even as he stood in the firmament. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ सुरा अपि ते विगतव्यथाः
कथितभद्रसुभद्रपराक्रमाः ।
सपदि खेन सुखेन यथासुखं
ययुरनेकमुखाः मघवन्मुखाः ॥ ५६॥

English
The gods too returned through the aerial path, free from worries. Discussing about the heroic exploits of Bhadra, Indra and others went in different directions. (56.)
