### **CHAPTER TWENTYFOUR - (*Śiva's sports on the Mandara mountain*)**

---

Sanskrit
२४

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
अन्तर्धानगतो देव्या सह सानुचरो हरः ।
क्व यातः कुत्र वा वासः किं कृत्वा विरराम ह ॥ १॥

English
*The sages said :—*  Where did Śiva who vanished along with the goddess and his attendants go ? Where did he stay ? What did he do and not do ? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
महीधरवरः श्रीमान् मन्दरश्चित्रकन्दरः ।
दयितो देवदेवस्य निवासस्तपसोऽभवत् ॥ २॥

English
*Vāyu said :—*  The excellent mountain Mandara$^{219}$ with different caves became the pleasing spot of penance for lord Śiva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तपो महत्कृतं तेन वोढुं स्वशिरसा शिवौ ।
चिरेण लब्धं तत्पादपङ्कजस्पर्शजं सुखम् ॥ ३॥

English
He had performed a great penance for the privilege to support Śiva and Śivā on his head. After a long time he felt the pleasure of contact with their lotus like feet. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य शैलस्य सौन्दर्यं सहस्रवदनैरपि ।
न शक्यं विस्तराद्वक्तुं वर्षकोटिशतैरपि ॥ ४॥

English
The beauty of the mountain cannot be mentioned in detail by persons with thousand faces even in hundreds of crores of years. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्यमप्यस्य सौन्दर्यं न वर्णयितुमुत्सहे ।
पर्वतान्तरसौन्दर्यं साधारणविधारणात् ॥ ५॥

English
Even if it is possible to describe I do not attempt to describe its beauty lest it should stand comparison with the other beautiful mountains. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
इदं तु शक्यते वक्तुमस्मिन्पर्वतसुन्दरे ।
ऋद्ध्या कयापि सौन्दर्यमीश्वरावासयोग्यता ॥ ६॥

English
This is possible to mention that it has the beauty of being qualified to be the residence of the lord through some supernatural magnificence. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अत एव हि देवेन देव्याः प्रियचिकीर्षया ।
अतीव रमणीयोऽयं गिरिरन्तःपुरीकृतः ॥ ७॥

English
That was why the lord made this mountain as his harem with a desire to do what was pleasing to the goddess. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मेखलाभूमयस्तस्य विमलोपलपादपाः ।
शिवयोर्नित्यसान्निध्यान्न्यक्कुर्वन्त्यखिलं जगत् ॥ ८॥

English
The slopes of the mountain abounding in rocks and trees humiliate the universe due to the perpetual presence of Śiva and Śivā. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
पितृभ्यां जगतो नित्यं स्नानपानोपयोगतः ।
अवाप्तपुण्यसंस्कारः प्रसरद्भिरितस्ततः ॥ ९॥

लघुशीतलसंस्पर्शैरच्छाच्छैर्निर्झराम्बुभिः ।
अधिराज्येन चाद्रीणामद्रीरेषोऽभिषिच्यते ॥ १०॥

English
The mountain renders help to the parents of the worlds with light and clean waters of the streams cool to the touch for bath as well as drinking. Due to these merits he is coronated as the king of mountains with the very waters spreading here and there. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
निशासु शिखरप्रान्तर्वर्तिना स शिलोच्चयः ।
चन्द्रेणाचलसाम्राज्यच्छत्रेणेव विराजते ॥ ११॥

English
During the nights when the moon remains at the outskirts of its peaks the mountain shines with the imperial umbrella in the form of the moon. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स शैलश्चञ्चलीभूतैर्बालैश्चामरयोषिताम् ।
सर्वपर्वतसाम्राज्यचामरैरिव वीज्यते ॥ १२॥

English
When the tresses of the womenfolk of the gods shake, he appears as being fanned by the chowries signifying his Emperorship. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रातरभ्युदिते भानौ भूधरो रत्नभूषितः ।
दर्पणे देहसौभाग्यं द्रष्टुकाम इव स्थितः ॥ १३॥

English
In the morning when the sun rises, the mountain embellished with jewels and gems appears to be eager for observing the beauty of his body in a mirror. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
कूजद्विहङ्गवाचालैर्वातोद्धृतलताभुजैः ।
विमुक्तपुष्पैः सततं व्यालम्बिमृदुपल्लवैः ॥ १४॥

लताप्रतानजटिलैस्तरुभिस्तापसैरिव ।
जयाशिषा सहाभ्यर्च्य निषेव्यत इवाद्रिराट् ॥ १५॥

English
This king of mountains appears to be honoured with benedictions of victory by the trees that seem to be sages with the twining growths of creepers for the matted hair; through the chirping of vociferous birds, by extending their arms in the form of creepers shaken by the breeze and shedding flowers and tender sprouts. (14-15.)

---

Sanskrit
अधोमुखैरूर्ध्वमुखैः श‍ृङ्गैस्तिर्यङ्मुखैस्तथा ।
प्रपतन्निव पाताले भूपृष्ठादुत्पतन्निव ॥ १६॥

परीतः सर्वतो दिक्षु भ्रमन्निव विहायसि ।
पश्यन्निव जगत्सर्वं नृत्यन्निव निरन्तरम् ॥ १७॥

गुहामुखैः प्रतिदिनं व्यात्तास्यो विपुलोदरैः ।
अजीर्णलावण्यतया जृम्भमाण इवाचलः ॥ १८॥

ग्रसन्निव जगत्सर्वं पिबन्निव पयोनिधिम् ।
वमन्निव तमोऽन्तस्थं माद्यन्निव खमम्बुदैः ॥ १९॥

English
With the various peaks some facing down, some shooting up and some projecting sideways he appears to jump into the nether worlds, jump up from the ground, fly through the sky in all directions. Through the vast caves he appears to have kept his mouth gaping. He appears to see the universe and dance continuously. His beauty never fades nor wears out. He appears to stretch himself to devour the universe, to drink the ocean; to vomit the darkness within and to cover up the sky with clouds. (16-19.)

---

Sanskrit
निवासभूमयस्तास्ता दर्पणप्रतिमोदराः ।
तिरस्कृतातपाः स्निग्धाश्रमच्छायामहीरुहाः ॥ २०॥

English
The different abodes have mirror-like surface. The tall shady trees in the hermitages set the blazing sunshine at nought. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
सरित्सरस्तडागादिसम्पर्कशिशिरानिलाः ।
तत्र तत्र निषण्णाभ्यां शिवाभ्यां सफलीकृताः ॥ २१॥

English
The breezes made cool by their contact with the rivers, tanks and lakes are rendered fruitful by Śiva and Śivā who roam here and there and enjoy them. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
तमिमं सर्वतः श्रेष्ठं स्मृत्वा साम्बस्त्रियम्बकः ।
रैभ्याश्रमसमीपस्थश्चान्तर्धानं गतो ययौ ॥ २२॥

English
It was to this excellent mountain that Śiva accompanied by Śivā while staying in the hermitage of Raibhya²²⁰ was attracted and proceeded. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रोद्यानमनुप्राप्य देव्या सह महेश्वरः ।
रराम रमणीयासु देव्यान्तःपुरभूमिषु ॥ २३॥

English
Reaching the garden there along with the goddess, lord Śiva sported about in the divine harem grounds. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा गतेषु कालेषु प्रवृद्धासु प्रजासु च ।
दैत्यौ शुम्भनिशुम्भाख्यौ भ्रातरौ सम्बभूवतुः ॥ २४॥

English
As time elapsed and population increased, two demons were born. They were two brothers Śumbha and Niśumbha. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
ताभ्यां तपोबलाद्दत्तं ब्रह्मणा परमेष्ठिना ।
अवध्यत्वं जगत्यस्मिन्पुरुषैरखिलैरपि ॥ २५॥

English
As a result of the power of their penance Brahmā granted them the boon that they would not be killed by any man in the universe. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अयोनिजा तु या कन्या ह्यम्बिकांशसमुद्भवा ।
अजातपुंस्पर्शरतिरविलङ्घ्यपराक्रमा ॥ २६॥

तया तु नौ वधः सङ्ख्ये तस्यां कामाभिभूतयोः ।
इति चाभ्यर्थितो ब्रह्मा ताभ्यां प्राह तथास्त्विति ॥ २७॥

English
Brahmā was requested by them thus—“Our death may take place in a battle with the lady with whom we fall passionately in love. She shall be a virgin born of Śiva’s part, not from any womb. She should not have experienced the sexual contact with any man. Her exploit shall be unthwartable.” Brahmā had consented to their request. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रभृति शक्रादीन्विजित्य समरे सुरान् ।
निःस्वाध्यायवषट्कारं जगच्चक्रतुरक्रमात् ॥ २८॥

English
Ever since, the two demons wrought havoc in the universe. They defeated Indra and other gods in war. They abolished the study of the Vedas and offering to the gods. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोर्वधाय देवेशं ब्रह्माभ्यर्थितवान्पुनः ।
विनिन्द्यापि रहस्यम्बां क्रोधयित्वा यथा तथा ॥ २९॥

तद्वर्णकोशजां शक्तिमकामां कन्यकात्मिकाम् ।
निशुम्भशुम्भयोर्हन्त्रीं सुरेभ्यो दातुमर्हसि ॥ ३०॥

English
Then Brahmā requested Śiva, the lord of the gods for killing them—“It behoves you to bestow on the gods the goddess who will kill Śumbha and Niśumbha. She shall be the Śakti born of the sheath of Śiva’s colour. She shall be in the form of a virgin devoid of passion. By rebuking her secretly or by infuriating her this shall be arranged. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमभ्यर्थितो धात्रा भगवान्नीललोहितः ।
कालीत्याह रहस्यं वां निन्दयन्निव सस्मितः ॥ ३१॥

ततः क्रुद्धा तदा देवी सुवर्णा वर्णकारणात् ।
स्मयन्ती चाह भर्तारमसमाधेयया गिरा ॥ ३२॥

English
Thus requested by Brahmā, the lord Nilalohita called Kālī in secret outwardly rebuking her while smiling within. The goddess Kālī was angry by her allusion to the colour. Derisively and forcibly she spoke to her husband thus. (31-32.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
ईदृशे मम वर्णेऽस्मिन्न रतिर्भवतोऽस्ति चेत् ।
एवावन्तं चिरं कालं कथमेषा नियम्यते ॥ ३३॥

English
The goddess said :—  “If my lord has no pleasure in my complexion how is it that I have been held up here so long. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अरत्या वर्तमानोऽपि कथं च रमसे मया ।
न ह्यशक्यं जगत्यस्मिन्नीश्वरस्य जगत्प्रभोः ॥ ३४॥

English
If you are displeased with me why do you sport with me? There is nothing in this universe which is unachievable for my lord, the lord of the universe. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वात्मारामस्य भवतो रतिर्न सुखसाधनम् ।
इति हेतोः स्मरो यस्मात्प्रसभं भस्मसात्कृतः ॥ ३५॥

English
To my lord taking delight in the soul, happiness is not derivable from sexual indulgence; for the very reason Kāma was reduced to ashes. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
या च नाभिरता भर्तुरपि सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरी ।
सा वृथैव हि जायेत सर्वैरपि गुणान्तरैः ॥ ३६॥

English
The life of the lady most beautiful in limbs is in vain though she may possess all other qualities if her husband is not delighted with her. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
भर्तुर्भोगैकशेषो हि सर्ग एवैष योषिताम् ।
तथासत्यन्यथा भूता नारी कुत्रोपयुज्यते ॥ ३७॥

English
The creation of women rests solely in the enjoyment of her husband. That being the case, where can a woman who is contrary to that be used? (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्वर्णमिमं त्यक्त्वा त्वया रहसि निन्दितम् ।
वर्णान्तरं भजिष्ये वा न भजिष्यामि वा स्वयम् ॥ ३८॥

English
Hence, eschewing this colour censured by you in secret I shall attain another colour or I shall cease to exist myself." (38.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वोत्थाय शयनाद्देवी साचष्ट गद्गदम् ।
ययाचेऽनुमतिं भर्तुस्तपसे कृतनिश्चया ॥ ३९॥

English
After saying this she stood up from the bed. Bent upon penance she requested for the permission of her lord in words choked with emotion. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा प्रणयभङ्गेन भीतो भूतपतिः स्वयम् ।
पादयोः प्रणमन्नेव भवानीं प्रत्यभाषत ॥ ४०॥

English
Then lord Śiva, afraid of a rupture in love, replied to her falling at her feet. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
अजानती च क्रीडोक्तिं प्रिये किं कुपितासि मे ।
रतिः कुतो वा जायेत त्वत्तश्चेदरतिर्मम ॥ ४१॥

English
Lord Śiva said :— "O beloved, why are you angry with me, not knowing that this was only a jocular remark of mine. If I am not delighted with you, where else can I seek delight? (41.)

---

Sanskrit
माता त्वमस्य जगतः पिताहमधिपस्तथा ।
कथं तदुपपद्येत त्वत्तो नाभिरतिर्मम ॥ ४२॥

English
If I am not delighted with you how can it be proper since you are mother of this universe and I am its father and lord ? (42.)

---

Sanskrit
आवयोरभिकामोऽपि किमसौ कामकारितः ।
यतः कामसमुत्तेः प्रागेव जगदुद्भवः ॥ ४३॥

English
Is our mutual love generated by Kāma? Even before the birth of Kāma the universe existed. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथग्जनानां रतये कामात्मा कल्पितो मया ।
ततः कथमुपालब्धः कामदाहादहं त्वया ॥ ४४॥

English
The deity Kāma was formulated by me for the sexual pleasure of men and women. Then why did you taunt me for having burnt Kāma ? (44.)

---

Sanskrit
मां वै त्रिदशसामान्यं मन्यमानो मनोभवः ।
मनाक्परिभवं कुर्वन्मया वै भस्मसात्कृतः ॥ ४५॥

English
The mind-born Kāma considered me on a par with the gods. While he attempted to tease me I reduced him to ashes. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
विहारोऽप्यावयोरस्य जगतस्त्राणकारणात् ।
ततस्तदर्थं त्वय्यद्य क्रीडोक्तिं कृतवानहम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Even our sport and pastime is meant for the universe. For that very purpose I have made this jocular remark. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
स चायमचिरादर्थस्तवैवाविष्करिष्यते ।
क्रोधस्य जनकं वाक्यं हृदि कृत्वेदमब्रवीत् ॥ ४७॥

English
That purpose ere long will be revealed to you." On hearing this the goddess, remembered the remark that caused her annoyance and said. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
श्रुतपूर्वं हि भगवंस्तव चाटुवचो मया ।
येनैवमतिधीराहमपि प्रागभिवञ्चिता ॥ ४८॥

English
The goddess said :—  O lord, your jocular remarks I have heard before whereby I have been deceived though I am very bold. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राणानप्यप्रिया भर्तुर्नारी या न परित्यजेत् ।
कुलाङ्गना शुभा सद्भिः कुत्सितैव हि गम्यते ॥ ४९॥

English
Even an auspicious lady of a noble family who does not abandon her life incurs the displeasure of her husband and is held despicable by good men. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
भूयसी च तवाप्रीतिरगौरमिति मे वपुः ।
क्रीडोक्तिरपि कालीति घटते कथमन्यथा ॥ ५०॥

English
Your displeasure is very great because I am not fair. How is the remark “Kālī” relevant otherwise though it be a jocular remark? (50.)

---

Sanskrit
सद्भिर्विगर्हितं तस्मात्तव कार्ष्ण्यमसम्मतम् ।
अनुत्सृज्य तपोयोगात्स्थातुमेवेह नोत्सहे ॥ ५१॥

English
Dark complexion is hated by good men. You too disapprove of it. Without wiping it off by dint of penance I am not inclined to stay here. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
यद्येवंविधतापस्ते तपसा किं प्रयोजनम् ।
ममेच्छया स्वेच्छया वा वर्णान्तरवती भव ॥ ५२॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  If your distress is such, of what avail is penance? You can assume any other colour at my will or at your choice. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
नेच्छामि भवतो वर्णं स्वयं वा कर्तुमन्यथा ।
ब्रह्माणं तपसाराध्य क्षिप्रं गौरी भवाम्यहम् ॥ ५३॥

English
*The goddess said :—*  I do not wish to have any other colour either at your will or at my choice. I shall propitiate Brahmā by penance and become Gaurī. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
मत्प्रसादात्पुरा ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मत्वं प्राप्तवान्पुरा ।
तमाहूय महादेवि तपसा किं करिष्यसि ॥ ५४॥

English
*Śiva said :—*  O goddess, Brahmā attained his status formerly by my grace. What will you do by invoking him through your penance? (54.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
त्वत्तो लब्धपदा एव सर्वे ब्रह्मादयः सुराः ।
तथाप्याराध्य तपसा ब्रह्माणं त्वन्नियोगतः ॥ ५५॥

पुरा किल सती नाम्ना दक्षस्य दुहिताऽभवम् ।
जगतां पतिमेव त्वां पतिं प्राप्तवती तथा ॥ ५६॥

English
*The goddess said :—*  True that Brahmā and other gods acquired their status only from you. Still at your bidding I propitiated Brahmā formerly and in the name of Satī I became Dakṣa’s daughter and attained you, the lord of the worlds, as my husband. (55-56.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमद्यापि तपसा तोषयित्वा द्विजं विधिम् ।
गौरी भवितुमिच्छामि को दोषः कथ्यतामिह ॥ ५७॥

English
So now also by propitiating him by penance I wish to become Gaurī. What is the harm therein? (57.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्तो महादेव्या वामदेवः स्मयन्निव ।
न तां निर्बन्धयामास देवकार्यचिकीर्षया ॥ ५८॥

English
Thus replied to by the goddess, Śiva did not insist on his point as he desired to carry out the task of the gods. (58.)
