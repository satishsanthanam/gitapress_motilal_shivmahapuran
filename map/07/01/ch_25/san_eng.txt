### **CHAPTER TWENTYFIVE - (The goddess attains fair complexion)**

---

Sanskrit
२५

वायुरुवाच ।
ततः प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य पतिमम्बा पतिव्रता ।
नियम्य च वियोगार्तिं जगाम हिमवद्गिरिम् ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said:—  Then circumambulating the lord and restraining the pangs of separation she went to the mountain Himavat. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
तपः कृतवती पूर्वं देशे यस्मिन्सखीजनैः ।
तमेव देशमवृणोत्तपसे प्रणयात्पुनः ॥ २॥

English
Out of love she selected the same spot for her penance where she had formerly performed the same along with her friends. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्वपितरं दृष्ट्वा मातरं च तयोर्गृहे ।
प्रणम्य वृत्तं विज्ञाप्य ताभ्यां चानुमता सती ॥ ३॥

पुनस्तपोवनं गत्वा भूषणानि विसृज्य च ।
स्नात्वा तपस्विनो वेषं कृत्वा परमपावनम् ॥ ४॥

सङ्कल्प्य च महातीव्रं तपः परमदुश्चरम् ।
सदा मनसि सन्धाय भर्तुश्चरणपङ्कजम् ॥ ५॥

तमेव क्षणिके लिङ्गे ध्यात्वा बाह्यविधानतः ।
त्रिसन्ध्यमभ्यर्चयन्ती वन्यैः पुष्पैः फलादिभिः ॥ ६॥

स एव ब्रह्मणो मूर्तिमास्थाय तपसः फलम् ।
प्रदास्यति ममेत्येवं नित्यं कृत्वाऽकरोत्तपः ॥ ७॥

English
She visited her parents at their house and bowed to them. Informing them of her intention and securing their permission she went to the penance-grove and discarded her ornaments. She took her bath and assumed the holy dress and features of ascetics. She performed the very difficult penance. She meditated upon the lotus-like feet of her lord. She conceived the same in Śiva's external phallic emblem in accordance with the injunctions. In the three junctions of time every day she worshipped the same with the flowers and fruits of the forest. Thinking thus—"He alone, after assuming the form of Brahmā, will grant me the fruit of this penance", she conducted the penance. (3-7.)

---

Sanskrit
तथा तपश्चरन्तीं तां काले बहुतिथे गते ।
दृष्टः कश्चिन्महाव्याघ्रो दुष्टभावादुपागमत् ॥ ८॥

English
After some time had elapsed, seeing her performing the penance a huge tiger approached her with wicked intention. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैवोपगतस्यापि तस्यातीव दुरात्मनः ।
गात्रं चित्रार्पितमिव स्तब्धं तस्याः सकाशतः ॥ ९॥

English
The body of that animal of wicked soul became stiff and benumbed when he approached her. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तं दृष्ट्वापि तथा व्याघ्रं दुष्टभावादुपागतम् ।
न पृथग्जनवद्देवी स्वभावेन विविच्यते ॥ १०॥

English
Even after seeing the tiger that approached her with wicked intention the goddess did not turn away from her pious thoughts like ordinary people. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
स तु विष्टब्धसर्वाङ्गो बुभुक्षापरिपीडितः ।
ममामिषं ततो नान्यदिति मत्वा निरन्तरम् ॥ ११॥

निरीक्ष्यमाणः सततं देवीमेव तदाऽनिशम् ।
अतिष्ठदग्रतस्तस्या उपासनमिवाचरत् ॥ १२॥

English
The animal with his body stunned and overwhelmed with hunger, stood there in front of her glancing perpetually at the goddess and thought, "My prey is nothing else." Virtually this became his cherished goal. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्याश्च हृदये नित्यं ममैवायमुपासकः ।
त्राता च दुष्टसत्त्वेभ्य इति प्रववृते कृपा ॥ १३॥

English
Mercy was generated in the heart of the goddess who thought, "He is the perpetual performer of contemplation on me and my protector from the wicked animals." (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्या एव कृपायोगात्सद्यो नष्टमलत्रयः ।
बभूव सहसा व्याघ्रो देवीं च बुबुधे तदा ॥ १४॥

English
By this feeling of mercy the threefold dirt of the tiger perished and he realised the goddess. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
न्यवर्तत बुभुक्षा च तस्याङ्गस्तम्भनं तथा ।
दौरात्म्यं जन्मसिद्धं च तृप्तिश्च समजायत ॥ १५॥

English
His hunger receded. The benumbed stiffness subsided. His congenital wickedness disappeared. Contentment set in. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा परमभावेन ज्ञात्वा कार्तार्थ्यमात्मनः ।
सद्योपासक एवैष सिषेवे परमेश्वरीम् ॥ १६॥

English
Realising his contentment with great piety he waited upon the goddess as a sudden devotee. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
दुष्टानामपि सत्त्वानां तथान्येषां दुरात्मनाम् ।
स एव द्रावको भूत्वा विचचार तपोवने ॥ १७॥

English
He roamed about the penance-grove as a router of wicked animals and wicked souls. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तपश्च ववृधे देव्यास्तीव्रं तीव्रतरात्मकम् ।
देवाश्च दैत्यनिर्बन्धाद् ब्रह्माणं शरणं गताः ॥ १८॥

English
The penance of the goddess increased and became gradually acute. Meanwhile the gods sought refuge in Brahmā due to the pressure of the Daityas. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
चक्रुर्निवेदनं देवाः स्वदुःखस्यारिपीडनात् ।
यथा च ददतुः शुम्भनिशुम्भौ वरसम्मदात् ॥ १९॥

English
The gods submitted to him how Śumbha and Niśumbha inflicted pain on them due to their strength derived from the boons. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
सोऽपि श्रुत्वा विधिर्दुःखं सुराणां कृपयान्वितः ।
आसीद्दैत्यवधायैव स्मृत्वा हेत्वाश्रयां कथाम् ॥ २०॥

सामरः प्रार्थितो ब्रह्मा ययौ देव्यास्तपोवनम् ।
संस्मरन्मनसा देवदुःखमोक्षं स्वयत्नतः ॥ २१॥

English
On hearing the distress of the gods Brahmā grew merciful. He knew how Daityas could be destroyed. On being requested he went to the penance-grove of the goddess along with the gods reflecting in his mind that the liberation of the gods from misery could be achieved through his effort. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
ददर्श च सुरश्रेष्ठः श्रेष्ठे तपसि निष्ठिताम् ।
प्रतिष्ठामिव विश्वस्य भवानीं परमेश्वरीम् ॥ २२॥

ननाम चास्य जगतो मातरं स्वस्य वै हरेः ।
रुद्रस्य च पितुर्भार्यामार्यामद्रीश्वरात्मजाम् ॥ २३॥

English
Brahmā saw the goddess, the mother of this universe, the mother of Trinity, the daughter of the lord of mountains, the foundation of the universe, firmly engaged in penance. On seeing her he was delighted. (22-23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माणमागतं दृष्ट्वा देवी देवगणैः सहः ।
अर्घ्यं तदर्हं दत्त्वाऽस्मै स्वागताद्यैरुपाचरत् ॥ २४॥

English
On seeing that Brahmā had come along with the gods, the goddess offered him Arghya and received him with words of welcome. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तां च प्रत्युपचारोक्तिं पुरस्कृत्याभिनन्द्य च ।
पप्रच्छ तपसो हेतुमजानन्निव पद्मजः ॥ २५॥

English
Replying suitably and congratulating her the lotus-born Brahmā asked her the purpose of her penance as though he did not know. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
तीव्रेण तपसानेन देव्या किमिह साध्यते ।
तपःफलानां सर्वेषां त्वदधीना हि सिद्धयः ॥ २६॥

English
*Brahmā Said :—*  What is being striven for, O goddess, by means of this penance ? The benefits of penances are under your control. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्चैव जगतां भर्ता तमेव परमेश्वरम् ।
भर्तारमात्मना प्राप्य प्राप्तं च तपसः फलम् ॥ २७॥

English
The fruit of the penance has been obtained by you in having obtained lord Śiva as your husband, who alone is the lord of all worlds. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा सर्वमेवैतत्क्रीडाविलसितं तव ।
इदं तु चित्रं देवस्य विरहं सहसे कथम् ॥ २८॥

English
Or all this is only a form of your divine sport. But this is surprising how you can bear separation from the lord. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
सर्गादौ भवतो देवादुत्पत्तिः श्रूयते यदा ।
तदा प्रजानां प्रथमस्त्वं मे प्रथमजः सुतः ॥ २९॥

English
The Goddess said :—  When at the beginning of creation as mentioned in the Vedas you are born of lord Śiva you are the first of my creation, my first-born son. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
यदा पुनः प्रजावृद्ध्यै ललाटाद्भवतो भवः ।
उत्पन्नोऽभूत्तदा त्वं मे गुरुः श्वशुरभावतः ॥ ३०॥

English
When for multiplying the subjects, Śiva was born of your forehead you became my father-in-law and so elder to me. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यदाभवद्गिरीन्द्रस्ते पुत्रो मम पिता स्वयम् ।
तदा पितामहस्त्वं मे जातो लोकपितामह ॥ ३१॥

English
When the lord of mountains, my father became your son you became my grandfather, O grandfather of the worlds! (31.)

---

Sanskrit
तदीदृशस्य भवतो लोकयात्राविधायिनः ।
वृत्तमन्तःपुरे भर्त्रा कथयिष्ये कथं पुनः ॥ ३२॥

English
How can I inform you, the arranger of worldly existence what happened at the harem with my husband? (32.)

---

Sanskrit
किमत्र बहुना देहे यश्चायं मम कालिमा ।
त्यक्त्वा सत्त्वविधानेन गौरी भवितुमुत्सहे ॥ ३३॥

English
Of what avail is this talk? I wish to get rid of my dark complexion through legitimate remedies and obtain white colour. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
एतावता किमर्थेन तीव्रं देवि तपः कृतम् ।
स्वेच्छैव किमपर्याप्ता क्रीडेयं हि तवेदृशी ॥ ३४॥

English
Brahmā said :—  O goddess, why did you perform a severe penance for this purpose? Was not your wish alone sufficient for that? Indeed this is only your sport (34.)

---

Sanskrit
क्रीडाऽपि च जगन्मातस्तव लोकहिताय वै ।
अतो ममेष्टमनया फलं किमपि साध्यताम् ॥ ३५॥

English
O mother of the universe, your play too benefits the worlds. Hence some benefit pleasing to me may be sought through it. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
निशुम्भशुम्भनामानौ दैत्यौ दत्तवरौ मया ।
दृप्तौ देवान्प्रबाधेते त्वत्तो लब्धस्तयोर्वधः ॥ ३६॥

English
Two Daityas, Sumbha and Nisumbha to whom I had granted boons have turned arrogant. They are harassing the gods. Their destruction is only through you. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
अलं विलम्बनेनात्र त्वं क्षणेन स्थिरा भव ।
शक्तिर्विसृज्यमानाद्य तयोर्मृत्युर्भविष्यति ॥ ३७॥

English
Do not delay. For a short while be steady. The Sakti that is to be released now shall be their death. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्राह्मणाऽभ्यर्थिता चैव देवी गिरिवरात्मजा ।
त्वक्कोशं सहसोत्सृज्य गौरी सा समजायत ॥ ३८॥

English
Thus requested by Brahma, the goddess, daughter of the mountain, cast off her outer skin and became white. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सा त्वक्कोशात्मनोत्सृष्टा कौशिकी नाम नामतः ।
काली कालाम्बुदप्रख्या कन्यका समपद्यत ॥ ३९॥

English
The outer sheath thus cast off became Kausiki who is known as Kali, the virgin with the lustre of the black cloud. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
सा तु मायात्मिका शक्तिर्योगनिद्रा च वैष्णवी ।
शङ्खचक्रत्रिशूलादिसायुधाष्टमहाभुजा ॥ ४०॥

English
She is the Sakti in the form of Maya, the Yogic slumber of Visnu. She held the weapons, conch, discus, trident in her eight great arms. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सौम्या घोरा च मिश्रा च त्रिनेत्रा चन्द्रशेखरा ।
अजातपुंस्पर्शरतिरधृष्या चातिसुन्दरी ॥ ४१॥

English
She had three traits: gentle, terrible and a mixture of the two. She had three eyes. She was moon-crested. She had not experienced the sexual contact of any man. She was invincible and beautiful. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
दत्ता च ब्रह्मणे देव्या शक्तिरेषा सनातनी ।
निशुम्भस्य च शुम्भस्य निहन्त्री दैत्यसिंहयोः ॥ ४२॥

English
This eternal Śakti was given to Brahmā by the goddess as the would-be destroyer of Śumbha and Niśumbha, the leonine Daityas. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणाऽपि प्रहृष्टेन तस्यै परमशक्तये ।
प्रबलः केसरी दत्तो वाहनत्वे समागतः ॥ ४३॥

English
A very powerful lion that came there, was given to her as vehicle by the delighted Brahmā. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
विन्ध्ये च वसतिं तस्याः पूजामासवपूर्वकैः ।
मांसैर्मत्स्यैरपूपैश्च निर्वर्त्यासौ समादिशत् ॥ ४४॥

English
He fixed her abode on the Vindhya mountain. He ordained her worship by the gift of meat, fish, pies and wine. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सा चैव सम्मता शक्तिर्ब्रह्मणो विश्वकर्मणः ।
प्रणम्य मातरं गौरीं ब्रह्माणं चानुपूर्वशः ॥ ४५॥

शक्तिभिश्चापि तुल्याभिः स्वात्मजाभिरनेकशः ।
परीता प्रययौ विन्ध्यं दैत्येन्द्रौ हन्तुमुद्यता ॥ ४६॥

English
That Śakti, approved of by Brahmā, the creator of the universe, bowed to her mother Gauri and Brahmā in due order. Surrounded by many Śaktis equal to her and born of her she proceeded to the Vindhya$^{221}$ ready to slay the leading Daityas. (45-46.)

---

Sanskrit
निहतौ च तया तत्र समरे दैत्यपुङ्गवौ ।
तद्बाणैः कामबाणैश्च च्छिन्नभिन्नाङ्गमानसौ ॥ ४७॥

तद्युद्धविस्तरश्चात्र न कृतोऽन्यत्र वर्णनात् ।
ऊहनीयं परस्माच्च प्रस्तुतं वर्णयामि वः ॥ ४८॥

English
The leading Daityas were killed by her in battle, the Daityas whom the arrows of Kāma pierced in the minds and her own arrows split and pierced in the bodies. That battle is not described here as it has been described elsewhere. It can be guessed through other sources as well. I shall resume the present description. (47-48.)
