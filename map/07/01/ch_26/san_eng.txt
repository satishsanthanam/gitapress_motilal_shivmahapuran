### **CHAPTER TWENTYSIX - *(The attainment of higher status by the tiger)***

---

Sanskrit
२६

वायुरुवाच ।
उत्पाद्य कौशिकीं गौरी ब्रह्मणे प्रतिपाद्य ताम् ।
तस्य प्रत्युपकाराय पितामहमथाब्रवीत् ॥ १॥

English
*Vāyu said:—*  After creating Kauśikī and handing her over to Brahmā, Gauri spoke to Brahmā demanding the recompense. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
दृष्टः किमेष भवता शार्दूलो मदुपाश्रयः ।
अनेन दुष्टसत्त्वेभ्यो रक्षितं मत्तपोवनम् ॥ २॥

English
*The goddess said:—*  Is this tiger that has resorted to me seen by you? He has guarded my penance grove from wicked animals. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
मय्यर्पितमना एष भजते मामनन्यधीः ।
अस्य संरक्षणादन्यत्प्रियं मम न विद्यते ॥ ३॥

English
Dedicating his mind to me he worships me without thinking of anything else. There is nothing more pleasing to me than his protection. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
भवितव्यमनेनातो ममान्तःपुरचारिणा ।
गणेश्वरपदं चास्मै प्रीत्या दास्यति शङ्करः ॥ ४॥

English
He shall be appointed as an official in my harem. Out of pleasure Śiva will grant him the post of Ganeśvara. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
एनमग्रेसरं कृत्वा सखीभिर्गन्तुमुत्सहे ।
प्रदीयतामनुज्ञा मे प्रजानां पतिना त्वया ॥ ५॥

English
I wish to return with my friends keeping him ahead. I seek permission from you the lord of the subjects. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्तः प्रहसन्ब्रह्मा देवीं मुग्धामिव स्मयन् ।
तस्य तीव्रैः पुरावृत्तैर्दौरात्म्यं समवर्णयत् ॥ ६॥

English
Thus addressed, Brahmā smiled and told the goddess describing the lion's wicked antecedents as though the goddess was unaware. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
पशौ देवि मृगाः क्रूराः क्व च तेऽनुग्रहः शुभः ।
आशीविषमुखे साक्षादमृतं किं निषिच्यते ॥ ७॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  O Goddess, animals are cruel. How can your blissful thought be directed to these? Why do you sprinkle nectar in the mouth of the serpent? (7.)

---

Sanskrit
व्याघ्रमात्रेण सन्नेष दुष्टः कोऽपि निशाचरः ।
अनेन भक्षिता गावो ब्राह्मणाश्च तपोधनाः ॥ ८॥

English
This is a certain night-prowling wicked demon in the garb of a tiger. Cows, Brahmins and saints have been devoured by him. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
तर्पयंस्तान् यथाकामं कामरूपी चरत्यसौ ।
अवश्यं खलु भोक्तव्यं फलं पापस्य कर्मणः ॥ ९॥

English
He is roaming about assuming forms as he pleases, pleasing and propitiating them. The fruit of a sinful action must of necessity be reaped by him. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः किं कृपया कृत्यमीदृशेषु दुरात्मसु ।
अनेन देव्याः किं कृत्यं प्रकृत्या कलुषात्मना ॥ १०॥

English
Why shall mercy be shown to wicked souls like this? What is the purpose served by one innately sinful? (10.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
यदुक्तं भवता सर्वं तथ्यमस्त्वयमीदृशः ।
तथापि मां प्रपन्नोऽभून्न त्याज्यो मामुपाश्रितः ॥ ११॥

English
*The goddess said:—*  What you have said is entirely true. Let him be like this. Still he has resorted to me. One who has sought refuge in me shall not be abandoned. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मोवाच ।
अस्य भक्तिमविज्ञाय प्राग्वृत्तं ते निवेदितम् ।
भक्तिश्चेदस्य किं पापैर्न ते भक्तः प्रणश्यति ॥ १२॥

English
*Brahmā said:—*  I narrated his antecedents without realising his devotion. If there be devotion, sins are not effective? Your devotee never perishes. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
पुण्यकर्मापि किं कुर्य्यात्त्वदीयाज्ञानपेक्षया ।
अजा प्रज्ञा पुराणी च त्वमेव परमेश्वरी ॥ १३॥

English
What can a man of virtuous rites achieve without depending on your behest? You alone are the unborn, intelligent, ancient goddess. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वदधीना हि सर्वेषां बन्धमोक्षव्यवस्थितिः ।
त्वदृते परमा शक्तिः संसिद्धिः कस्य कर्मणा ॥ १४॥

English
Bondage and liberation depend on you. There is no greater Śakti than you. Without you rites cannot achieve results. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वमेव विविधा शक्तिः भवानामथ वा स्वयम् ।
अशक्तः कर्मकरणे कर्ता वा किं करिष्यति ॥ १५॥

English
You alone constitute Śakti of living beings. Himself incompetent to do anything what will a mere agent do? (15.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोश्च मम चान्येषां देवदानवरक्षसाम् ।
तत्तदैश्वर्यसम्प्राप्त्यै तवैवाज्ञा हि कारणम् ॥ १६॥

English
It is only your behest that is the cause of acquisition of prosperity and glory by Viṣṇu, by me or by any of these—gods, Dānavas or Rākṣasas. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीताः खल्वसङ्ख्याता ब्रह्माणो हरयो भवाः ।
अनागतास्त्वसङ्ख्यातास्त्वदाज्ञानुविधायिनः ॥ १७॥

English
Innumerable Brahmās, Viṣṇus and Śivas have passed by. Such innumerable ones are yet to be born, these carry out your behests. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वामनाराध्य देवेशि पुरुषार्थचतुष्टयम् ।
लब्धुं न शक्यमस्माभिरपि सर्वैः सुरोत्तमैः ॥ १८॥

English
O goddess of the gods without propitiating you the fourfold arms of life cannot be acquired by all of us. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यत्यासोऽपि भवेत्सद्यो ब्रह्मत्वस्थावरत्वयोः ।
सुकृतं दुष्कृतं चापि त्वयैव स्थापितं यतः ॥ १९॥

English
Since merit and evil have been established by you the mobile and immobile may even be inter-changed. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं हि सर्वजगद्भर्तुः शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
अनादिमध्यनिधना शक्तिराद्या सनातनी ॥ २०॥

English
You are the primordial and eternal Śakti of Śiva the great soul, the lord of the universe, the Śakti without beginning, middle or death. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
समस्तलोकयात्रार्थं मूर्तिमाविश्य कामपि ।
क्रीडसे विविधैर्भावैः कस्त्वां जानाति तत्त्वतः ॥ २१॥

English
For the functioning of the universe you assume some form or other and play about in different aspects. Who knows you factually ? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो दुष्कृतकर्मापि व्याघ्रोऽयं त्वदनुग्रहात् ।
प्राप्नोतु परमां सिद्धिमत्र कः प्रतिबन्धकः ॥ २२॥

English
Hence let this wicked tiger too attain the great Siddhi by your blessings. Nothing can prevent it ?” (22.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यात्मनः परं भावं स्मारयित्वानुरूपतः ।
ब्रह्मणाभ्यर्थिता गौरी तपसोऽपि न्यवर्तत ॥ २३॥

English
Thus requested by Brahmā after duly reminding her of her great aspect the goddess ceased from her penance. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो देवीमनुज्ञाप्य ब्रह्मण्यन्तर्हिते सति ।
देवीं च मातरं दृष्ट्वा मेनां हिमवता सह ॥ २४॥

प्रणम्याश्वास्य बहुधा पितरौ विरहासहौ ।
तपः प्रणयिनो देवी तपोवनमहीरुहान् ॥ २५॥

विप्रयोगशुचेवाग्रे पुष्पबाष्पं विमुञ्चतः ।
तत्तच्छाखासमारूढविहगोदीरितै रुतैः ॥ २६॥

व्याकुलं बहुधा दीनं विलापमिव कुर्वतः ।
सखीभ्यः कथयन्त्वेवं सत्त्वरा भर्तृदर्शने ॥ २७॥

पुरस्कृत्य च तं व्याघ्रं स्नेहात्पुत्रमिवौरसम् ।
देहस्य प्रभया चैव दीपयन्ती दिशो दश ॥ २८॥

प्रययौ मन्दरं गौरी यत्र भर्ता महेश्वरः ।
सर्वेषां जगतां धाता कर्ता पाता विनाशकृत् ॥ २९॥

English
Then taking leave of the goddess Brahmā vanished. The goddess visited her parents Menā, Himavat. She bowed to them and alleviated the pangs of her parents due to her absence. The goddess then proceeded to Mandara talking to her friends about the trees in the penance-grove who had been her loving companions. “They are shedding tears due to their grief at separation, by their scattering flowers, they are crying in distress by the chirping sound of birds seated on their boughs.” The goddess was impatient to see her lord. She kept the tiger ahead thinking him as son born of herself, out of affection. With the brilliant lustre of her body she illuminated the quarters. Thus Gaurī reached Mandara where lord Śiva was staying—the lord who is the creator, protector and annihilator of the worlds. (24-29.)
