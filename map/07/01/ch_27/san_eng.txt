### **CHAPTER TWENTYSEVEN - (Gauri's embellishment)**

---

Sanskrit
२७

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
कृत्वा गौरं वपुर्दिव्यं देवी गिरिवरात्मजा ।
कथं ददर्श भर्तारं प्रविष्टा मन्दिरं सती ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  After assuming fair complexion how did the goddess, the daughter of the excellent mountain look at her husband on entering the mansion? (1.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवेशसमये तस्या भवनद्वारगोचरैः ।
गणेशैः किं कृतं देवस्तां दृष्ट्वा किं तदाकरोत् ॥ २॥

English
What was done by the Gaṇeśas at the portals at her entry? What did the lord do? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
प्रवक्तुमञ्जसाशक्यः तादृशः परमो रसः ।
येन प्रणयगर्भेण भावो भाववतां हृतः ॥ ३॥

English
Vāyu said:—  It is impossible to narrate adequately such an exquisite sentiment couched in love whereby the imagination of all sentimental people has been captured. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
द्वाःस्थैः ससम्भ्रमैरेव देवो देव्यागमोत्सुकः ।
शङ्कमाना प्रविष्टान्तस्तं च सा समपश्यत ॥ ४॥

English
She was received by the doorkeepers in full flutter and agitation. The lord was eagerly awaiting her arrival. Entering within the mansion she hesitatingly glanced at the lord. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तैस्तैः प्रणयभावैश्च भवनान्तरवर्तिभिः ।
गणेन्द्रैर्वन्दिता वाचा प्रणनाम त्रियम्बकम् ॥ ५॥

English
She was welcomed by the inmates of the mansion with loving feelings. She was saluted by the Gana chiefs with words of welcome. She bowed to lord Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणम्य नोत्थिता यावत्तावत्तां परमेश्वरः ।
प्रगृह्य दोर्भ्यामाश्लिष्य परितः परया मुदा ॥ ६॥

स्वाङ्के धर्तुं प्रवृत्तोऽपि सा पर्यङ्के न्यषीदत ।
पर्यङ्कतो बलाद्देवीं सोऽङ्कमारोप्य सुस्मिताम् ॥ ७॥

सस्मितो विवृतैर्नेत्रैस्तद्वक्त्रं प्रपिबन्निव ।
तया सम्भाषणायेशः पूर्वभाषितमब्रवीत् ॥ ८॥

English
After bowing no sooner did she get up than the lord seized her with his hands, embraced her and joyously walked her round. Even when he attempted to make her sit on his lap she sat on the couch. Smilingly he seized her from the couch and made her sit on his lap. Smilingly gazing at her with his wide open eyes as if he would drink her in, the lord initiated the conversation with her by speaking himself first. (6-8.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव उवाच ।
सा दशा च व्यतीता किं तव सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरि ।
यस्यामनुनयोपायः कोऽपि कोपान्न लभ्यते ॥ ९॥

English
The lord of the gods said :—  O lady, exquisitely beautiful in every limb, has that condition of yours passed wherein no means of pacification is fruitful due to your anger? (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वेच्छयापि न कालीति नान्यवर्णवतीति च ।
त्वत्स्वभावहृतं चित्तं सुभ्रु चिन्तावहं मम ॥ १०॥

विस्मृतः परमो भावः कथं स्वेच्छाङ्गयोगतः ।
न सम्भवन्ति ये तत्र चित्तकालुष्यहेतवः ॥ ११॥

English
O lovely lady, I do not mind whether you are Kālī or of any other complexion. My mind is attracted by your innermost feeling. How did this concept escape your memory ? Out of our will we have accepted physical bodies. Causes dirtying up the mind do not crop up there. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
पृथग्जनवदन्योऽन्यं विप्रियस्यापि कारणम् ।
आवयोरपि यद्यस्ति नास्त्येवैतच्चराचरम् ॥ १२॥

English
Should there be any cause for our mutual displeasure as in the case of ignorant fellows, the whole world consisting of the mobile and immobile beings would cease to exist. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अहमग्निशिरोनिष्ठस्त्वं सोमशिरसि स्थिता ।
अग्नीषोमात्मकं विश्वमावाभ्यां समधिष्ठितम् ॥ १३॥

English
I am stationed on the head of Agni. You are stationed on the head of Soma. This universe in the form of “Agnīṣoma” is presided over by us both. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
जगद्धिताय चरतोः स्वेच्छाधृतशरीरयोः ।
आवयोर्विप्रयोगे हि स्यान्निरालम्बनं जगत् ॥ १४॥

English
We move about for the welfare of the universe. We have taken up physical bodies out of our free will. At our separation the universe is left without support. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्ति हेत्वन्तरं चात्र शास्त्रयुक्तिविनिश्चितम् ।
वागर्थमिव चैवैतज्जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् ॥ १५॥

त्वं हि वागमृतं साक्षादहमर्थामृतं परम् ।
द्वयमप्यमृतं कस्माद्वियुक्तमुपपद्यते ॥ १६॥

English
There is another reason based on Scriptural texts and independent arguments. The universe comprising the mobile and immobile beings is integrated as words to their meaning. You are the nectarine word. I am the nectarine meaning. How can the two nectars become disjointed? (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्याप्रत्यायिका त्वं मे वेद्योऽहं प्रत्ययात्तव ।
विद्यावेद्यात्मनोरेव विश्लेषः कथमावयोः ॥ १७॥

English
You are the lore that makes my conviction possible. I am the object of knowledge on the basis of your conviction. How can we separate in as much as we are in the forms of knowledge and object of knowledge ? (17.)

---

Sanskrit
न कर्मणा सृजामीदं जगत्प्रतिसृजामि च ।
सर्वस्याज्ञैकलभ्यत्वादाज्ञात्वं हि गरीयसी ॥ १८॥

English
I am not creating and re-creating this universe merely by my activity since everything gets evolved through command or behest. You are the great command. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
आज्ञैकसारमैश्वर्यं यस्मात्स्वातन्त्र्यलक्षणम् ।
आज्ञया विप्रयुक्तस्य चैश्वर्यं मम कीदृशम् ॥ १९॥

English
Lordship has behest as its core because it is the symbol of freedom. How can there be lordship in me if I am deprived of command ? (19.)

---

Sanskrit
न कदाचिदवस्थानमावयोर्विप्रयुक्तयोः ।
देवानां कार्यमुद्दिश्य लीलोक्तिं कृतवानहम् ॥ २०॥

English
We can never stay apart from each other. It was for accomplishing the task of the gods that I made that joke. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वयाप्यविदितं नास्ति कथं कुपितवत्यसि ।
ततस्त्रिलोकरक्षार्थे कोपो मय्यपि ते कृतः ॥ २१॥

English
It was not unknown to you that you got angry ? It was for the protection of the worlds that you feigned anger towards me. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
यदनर्थाय भूतानां न तदस्ति खलु त्वयि ।
इति प्रियंवदे साक्षादीश्वरे परमेश्वरे ॥ २२॥

श‍ृङ्गारभावसाराणां जन्मभूमिरकृत्रिमा ।
स्वभर्त्रा ललितं तथ्यमुक्तं मत्वा स्मितोत्तरम् ॥ २३॥

लज्जया न किमप्यूचे कौशिकी वर्णनात्परम् ।
तदेव वर्णयाम्यद्य श‍ृणु देव्याश्च वर्णनम् ॥ २४॥

English
What is harmful to the living beings does not find room in you. When lord himself spoke thus the goddess, the natural source of love, smiled but did not reply to her husband. Out of bashfulness she did not say anything but gave an accormt of Kaučiki. (22-24.)

---

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
किं देवेन न सा दृष्टा या सृष्टा कौशिकी मया ।
तादृशी कन्यका लोके न भूता न भविष्यति ॥ २५॥

English
The goddess said:—  Was not Kauśiki, created by me seen by my lord? Such a girl has never been before in the world nor will ever be. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्या वीर्यं बलं विन्ध्यनिलयं विजयं तथा ।
शुम्भस्य च निशुम्भस्य मारणे च रणे तयोः ॥ २६॥

प्रत्यक्षफलदानं च लोकाय भजते सदा ।
लोकानां रक्षणं शश्वद् ब्रह्मा विज्ञापयिष्यति ॥ २७॥

English
Brahmā will tell you about her prowess, strength, residence on the Vindhya, her victory in the battle with Śumbha and Niśumbha, their death, her blessing to the devotees and her protection of the worlds. (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सम्भाषमाणाया देव्या एवाज्ञया तदा ।
व्याघ्रः सख्या समानीय पुरोऽवस्थापितस्तदा ॥ २८॥

English
At the behest of the goddess who spoke thus her friends brought the tiger in their presence. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तं प्रेक्ष्याह पुनर्देवी देवानीतमुपायतम् ।
व्याघ्रं पश्य न चानेन सदृशो मदुपासकः ॥ २९॥

English
On seeing him the goddess spoke again—“O lord, see this tiger. There is no other devotee of mine like him. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अनेन दुष्टसङ्घेभ्यो रक्षितं मत्तपोवनम् ।
अतीव मम भक्तश्च विश्रब्धश्च स्वरक्षणात् ॥ ३०॥

स्वदेशं च परित्यज्य प्रसादार्थं समागतः ।
यदि प्रीतिरभून्मत्तः परां प्रीतिं करोषि मे ॥ ३१॥

नित्यमन्तःपुरद्वारि नियोगान्नन्दिनः स्वयम् ।
रक्षिभिः सह तच्चिह्नैर्वर्ततामयमीश्वर ॥ ३२॥

English
My penance-grove was guarded by him from wicked hosts. He is greatly devoted to me. In view of his protecting me he is trustworthy. He has left his native place and come here for your favour. If you are pleased with him and if you love me, O god, let him stay at the door of the harem along with the other guards and under the charge of Nandin himself.” (30-32.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
मधुरं प्रणयोदर्कं श्रुत्वा देव्याः शुभं वचः ।
प्रीतोऽस्मीत्याह तं देवः स चादृश्यत तत्क्षणात् ॥ ३३॥

बिभ्रद्वेत्रलतां हैमीं रत्नचित्रं च कञ्चुकम् ।
छुरिकामुरगप्रख्यां गणेशो रक्षवेषधृक् ॥ ३४॥

English
Vāyu said:—  On hearing the auspicious, loving and sweet words of the goddess the lord said to him “I am pleased”. Immediately he was seen like Gaṇeśa wearing the dress and features of a watchman, holding the cane made of gold and a dagger of serpentine lustre and wearing a bodice set with various gems. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मात्सोमो महादेवो नन्दी चानेन नन्दितः ।
सोमनन्दीति विख्यातस्तस्मादेष समाख्यया ॥ ३५॥

English
He was named Somanandin because Soma means Śiva and this tiger pleased Soma as well as Nandin. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थं देव्याः प्रियं कृत्वा देवश्चार्द्धेन्दुभूषणः ।
भूषयामास तं दिव्यैर्भूषणै रत्नभूषितैः ॥ ३६॥

English
After carrying out this task pleasing to the goddess the lord with the circular moon on his crest embellished him with divine ornaments set with gems. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स गौरीं गिरिशो गिरीन्द्रजां
सगौरवां सर्वमनोहरां हरः ।
पर्यङ्कमारोप्य वराङ्गभूषणै-
र्विभूषयामास शशाङ्कभूषणः ॥ ३७॥

English
Then lord Śiva with the moon as his ornament made the majestic beautiful Gauri the daughter of the lord of Himavat sit on the conch and embellished her with ornaments. (37.)
