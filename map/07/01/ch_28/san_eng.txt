### **CHAPTER TWENTYEIGHT - (The glory of Bhasma)**

---

Sanskrit
२८

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
देवीं समादधानेन देवेनेदं किमीरितम् ।
अग्निषोमात्मकं विश्वं वागर्थात्मकमित्यपि ॥ १॥

आज्ञैकसारमैश्वर्यमाज्ञात्वमिति चोदितम् ।
तदिदं श्रोतुमिच्छामो यथावदनुपूर्वशः ॥ २॥

English
The sages said:—  We wish to hear these things in the proper order. What was it mentioned by the lord while pacifying the goddess? He had said that the universe is in the form of Agniṣoma. He had said that it was in the form of words and their meanings. He also mentioned lordship has command as its core. You are that command. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
अग्निरित्युच्यते रौद्री घोरा या तैजसी तनुः ।
सोमः शाक्तोऽमृतमयः शक्तेः शान्तिकरी तनुः ॥ ३॥

English
Vāyu said:—  The body of Rudra which is fiery and terrible is called fire. The moon pertains to Śakti. It is nectarine and is the body of Śakti that causes calmness. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अमृतं यत्प्रतिष्ठा सा तेजो विद्या कला स्वयम् ।
भूतसूक्ष्मेषु सर्वेषु त एव रसतेजसी ॥ ४॥

English
What is called Amṛta is Pratiṣṭhā. Tejas is Vidyā and Kalā itself. In all the subtle elements the two are predominant, viz, Rasa and Tejas. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विविधा तेजसो वृत्तिः सूर्यात्मा चानलात्मिका ।
तथैव रसवृत्तिश्च सोमात्मा च जलात्मिका ॥ ५॥

English
Tejas functions in two ways, in the form of the sun and of fire. Rasa too functions in the form of the moon and of water. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
विद्युदादिमयं तेजो मधुरादिमयो रसः ।
तेजोरसविभेदैस्तु धृतमेतच्चराचरम् ॥ ६॥

English
Tejas is lightning etc. Rasa is sweet etc. The universe of the mobile and immobile beings, is sustained by Tejas and Rasa. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्नेरमृतनिष्पत्तिरमृतेनाग्निरेधते ।
अत एव हि विक्रान्तमग्नीषोमं जगद्धितम् ॥ ७॥

English
Amṛta issues from Agni. Agni increases through Amṛta. The powerful Agniṣoma is beneficent to the universe. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
हविषे सस्यसम्पत्तिर्वृष्टिः सस्याभिवृद्धये ।
वृष्टेरेव हविस्तस्मादग्नीषोमधृतं जगत् ॥ ८॥

English
The luxuriant growth of Vegetation is for the Havis. The rain contributes to the flourishing growth of vegetation. So Havis is the outcome of rain, the universe is sustained by Agniṣoma. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्निरूर्ध्वं ज्वलत्येष यावत्सौम्यं परामृतम् ।
यावदग्न्यास्पदं सौम्यममृतं च स्रवत्यधः ॥ ९॥

English
The Agni blazes upwards as far as the great Amṛta of Soma. The Amṛta of Soma flows as far as the support of Agni. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अत एव हि कालाग्निरधस्ताच्छक्तिरूर्ध्वतः ।
यावदादहनं चोर्ध्वमधश्चाप्लावनं भवेत् ॥ १०॥

English
That is why the Kālāgni 'stays below and the Śakti upwards. The upward blazing is till burning and the downward flow is till complete drenching. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
आधारशक्त्यैव धृतः कालाग्निरयमूर्ध्वगः ।
तथैव निम्नगः सोमः शिवशक्तिपदास्पदः ॥ ११॥

English
The Kālāgni supported by Ādhāra Śakti proceeds upwards. Similarly Soma having its region in Śiva-Śakti flows downwards. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवश्चोर्ध्वमधः शक्तिरूर्ध्वं शक्तिरधः शिवः ।
तदित्थं शिवशक्तिभ्यां नाव्याप्तमिह किञ्चन ॥ १२॥

English
Sivā sustains Śakti from below and Śiva is Śakti-dhara (upholder of Śakti) above. Thus there is nothing not pervaded by Śiva and Śakti. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
असकृच्चाग्निना दग्धं जगद्यद्भस्मसात्कृतम् ।
अग्नेर्वीर्यमिदं चाहुस्तद्वीर्यं भस्म यत्ततः ॥ १३॥

English
Frequently the world consumed by fire is reduced to ashes. They call it Agnivirya because Bhasma constitutes the powerful part of the same. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यश्चेत्थं भस्मसद्भावं ज्ञात्वा स्नाति च भस्मना ।
अग्निरित्यादिभिर्मन्त्रैर्बद्धः पाशात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ १४॥

English
He who dusts himself with Bhasma repeating the mantra "Agniriti"²²² etc., if bound becomes free from bondage. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
अग्नेर्वीर्यं तु यद्भस्म सोमेनाप्लावितं पुनः ।
अयोगयुक्त्या प्रकृतेरधिकाराय कल्पते ॥ १५॥

English
Bhasma which is Agnivirya when drenched with Soma is conducive to the lordship of Prakṛti without having recourse to Yogic union. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
योगयुक्त्या तु तद्भस्म प्लाव्यमानं समन्ततः ।
शाक्तेनामृतवर्षेण चाधिकारान्निवर्तयेत् ॥ १६॥

English
But being drenched all round by having recourse to Yogic union the Bhasma may turn one back from lordship by the shower of Amṛta belonging to Śakti. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अतो मृत्युञ्जयायेत्थममृतप्लावनं सदा ।
शिवशक्त्यमृतस्पर्शे लब्धं येन कुतो मृतिः ॥ १७॥

English
Hence for the conquest of death the drenching with Amṛta shall be perpetual. How and wherefore shall one die if the nectarine contact of Śiva and Śakti is acquired? (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यो वेद दहनं गुह्यं प्लावनं च यथोदितम् ।
अग्नीषोमपदं हित्वा न स भूयोऽभिजायते ॥ १८॥

English
He who knows the secret of burning and drenching as mentioned is never reborn after leaving the world, the region of Agniṣoma. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवाग्निना तनुं दग्ध्वा शक्तिसौम्यामृतेन यः ।
प्लावयेद्योगमार्गेण सोऽमृतत्वाय कल्पते ॥ १९॥

English
He who burns his body with Śaivite fire and drenches it with the nectar of Śakti and Soma attains immortality. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
हृदि कृत्वेममर्थं वै देवेन समुदाहृतम् ।
अग्नीषोमात्मकं विश्वं जगदित्यनुरूपतः ॥ २०॥

English
It was after keeping this in mind that the lord had said—"The universe is in the form of Agniṣoma." (20.)
