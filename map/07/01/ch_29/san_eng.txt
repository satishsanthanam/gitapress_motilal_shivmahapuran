### **CHAPTER TWENTYNINE - (The analysis of Vāg-Artha)**

---

Sanskrit
२९

वायुरुवाच ।
निवेदयामि जगतो वागर्थात्म्यं कृतं यथा ।
षडध्ववेदनं सम्यक् समासान्न तु विस्तरात् ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said:—  I shall mention briefly how the universe in the form of 'Vāg-Artha' can be known through the six pathways. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्ति कश्चिदशब्दार्थो नापि शब्दो निरर्थकः ।
ततो हि समये शब्दः सर्वः सर्वार्थबोधकः ॥ २॥

English
There is nothing that is not the meaning of a word.  There is no word without meaning. Therefore all words make all meanings known in their proper time. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रकृतेः परिणामोऽयं द्विधा शब्दार्थभावना ।
तामाहुः प्राकृतीं मूर्तिं शिवयोः परमात्मनोः ॥ ३॥

English
The two words and their meanings are the transformations of Prakṛti which is form of Śiva and Śivā. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शब्दात्मिका विभूतिर्या सा त्रिधा कथ्यते बुधैः ।
स्थूला सूक्ष्मा परा चेति स्थूला या श्रुतिगोचरा ॥ ४॥

English
The magnificence in the form of word is mentioned as threefold by scholars—gross, subtle and the great. The gross one is what we hear with our ears. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
सूक्ष्मा चिन्तामयी प्रोक्ता चिन्तया रहिता परा ।
या शक्तिः सा परा शक्तिः शिवतत्त्वसमाश्रया ॥ ५॥

ज्ञानशक्तिसमायोगादिच्छोपोद्बलिका तथा ।
सर्वशक्तिसमष्ट्यात्मा शक्तितत्त्वसमाख्यया ॥ ६॥

समस्तकार्यजातस्य मूलप्रकृतितां गता ।
सैव कुण्डलिनी माया शुद्धाध्वपरमा सती ॥ ७॥

सा विभागस्वरूपैव षडध्वात्मा विजृम्भते ।
तत्र शब्दास्त्रयोऽध्वानस्त्रयश्चार्थाः समीरिताः ॥ ८॥

English
The subtle one is in the form of thought, the one beyond is devoid of thought. The Śakti based on the principle of Śiva is Parāśakti. In the name of “Śakti Tattva” it is the root cause of all effects. It is the composite unit (Samaṣṭi) of all Śaktis because it is in association with Jñāna Śakti and is supported by Icchāśakti. The same is called Kuṇḍalinī Māyā and Śuddhādhvaparamā. This Śakti is ramified into six paths. The three paths pertain to words and the three pertain to their meanings. (5-8.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वेषामपि वै पुंसां नैजशुद्ध्यनुरूपतः ।
लयभोगाधिकाराः स्युः सर्वतत्त्वविभागतः ॥ ९॥

कलाभिस्तानि तत्त्वानि व्याप्तान्येव यथातथम् ।
परस्याः प्रकृतेरादौ पञ्चधा परिणामतः ॥ १०॥

English
The capacity for merging and enjoyment is possessed by all men in accordance with their purity based on the divisions of all Principles. The principles are pervaded by Kalās suitably as in the beginning the great Prakṛti is transformed into five. They are Kalā, Nivṛtti and others. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
कलाश्च ता निवृत्त्याद्याः पर्य्याप्ता इति निश्चयः ।
मन्त्राध्वा च पदाध्वा च वर्णाध्वा चेति शब्दतः ॥ ११॥

भुवनाध्वा च तत्त्वाध्वा कलाध्वा चार्थतः क्रमात् ।
अत्रान्योऽन्यं च सर्वेषां व्याप्यव्यापकतोच्यते ॥ १२॥

English
The three paths based on words are : the path of mantras, the path of words, the path of letters. The three paths based on meanings are the path of universe, the path of principles, the path of Kalās. These are mutually pervaded and pervading. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्राः सर्वे पदैर्व्याप्ता वाक्यभावात्पदानि च ।
वर्णैर्वर्णसमूहं हि पदमाहुर्विपश्चितः ॥ १३॥

English
The mantras are pervaded by the words constituting the statement. The words are pervaded by letters. Learned men call the group of letters a word. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्णास्तु भुवनैर्व्याप्तास्तेषां तेषूपलम्भनात् ।
भुवनान्यपि तत्त्वौघैरुत्पत्त्यान्तर्बहिष्क्रमात् ॥ १४॥

व्याप्तानि कारणैस्तत्त्वैरारब्धत्वादनेकशः ।
अन्तरादुत्थितानीह भुवनानि तु कानिचित् ॥ १५॥

पौराणिकानि चान्यानि विज्ञेयानि शिवागमे ।
साङ्ख्ययोगप्रसिद्धानि तत्त्वान्यपि च कानिचित् ॥ १६॥

शिवशास्त्रप्रसिद्धानि ततोऽन्यान्यपि कृत्स्नशः ।
कलाभिस्तानि तत्त्वानि व्याप्तान्येव यथातथम् ॥ १७॥

English
The letters are pervaded by the words because they are obtained there. The words are pervaded by the Tattvas because of their origin from within and without. They are effected by the Tattvas in various ways. Some of the words have cropped up from the middle, some from the Śaiva Āgamas. Some of Tattvas are those famous in the Sāṅkhya and Yoga systems of Philosophy. Others are famous in the sacred texts of Śiva. The Tattvas are duly pervaded by Kalās. (14-17.)

---

Sanskrit
परस्याः प्रकृतेरादौ पञ्चधा परिणामतः ।
कलाश्च ता निवृत्त्याद्या व्याप्ताः पञ्च यथोत्तरम् ॥ १८॥

English
Since in the beginning the primordial Prakṛti is transformed into five Kalās, Nivṛtti and others pervaded by each other. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
व्यापिकातः पराशक्तिरविभक्ता षडध्वनाम् ।
परप्रकृतिभावस्य तत्सत्त्वाच्छिवतत्त्वतः ॥ १९॥

English
The undivided Sakti pervades the six paths. She is pervaded by Sivatattva. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्त्यादि च पृथिव्यन्तं शिवतत्त्वसमुद्भवम् ।
व्याप्तमेकेन तेनैव मृदा कुम्भादिकं यथा ॥ २०॥

English
Everything from Sakti to Earth is born of Sivatattva and pervaded by it alone just as pot etc. are pervaded by clay. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवं तत्परमं धाम यत्प्राप्यं षड्भिरध्वभिः ।
व्यापिकाऽव्यापिका शक्तिः पञ्चतत्त्वविशोधनात् ॥ २१॥

English
The great abode of Siva is attainable through six paths. The non-pervading Sakti becomes pervasive by the purification by five Tattvas. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्त्या रुद्रपर्यन्तं स्थितिरण्डस्य शोध्यते ।
प्रतिष्ठया तदूर्ध्वं तु यावदव्यक्तगोचरम् ॥ २२॥

English
The situation of the cosmic egg upto Rudra is purified by Nivrtti. Above that upto the sphere of Avyakta It is purified by Pratiṣṭha. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तदूर्ध्वं विद्यया मध्ये यावद्विश्वेश्वरावधि ।
शान्त्या तदूर्ध्वम्मध्वान्ते विशुद्धिः शान्त्यतीतया ॥ २३॥

English
Above that upto Visvesvara it is purified by Vidya in the middle. Above that it is purified by Santi. At the end of the path the purification is through Santyatita. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
यामाहुः परमं व्योम परप्रकृतियोगतः ।
एतानि पञ्चतत्त्वानि यैर्व्याप्तमखिलं जगत् ॥ २४॥

English
It is this they call the greatest firmament in view of the contact with Paraprakrti. These are the five Tattvas whereby the entire universe is pervaded. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्रैव सर्वमेवेदं द्रष्टव्यं खलु साधकैः ।
अध्वव्याप्तिमविज्ञाय शुद्धिं यः कर्तुमिच्छति ॥ २५॥

स विप्रलम्भकः शुद्धेर्नालं प्रापयितुं फलम् ।
वृथा परिश्रमस्तस्य निरयायैव केवलम् ॥ २६॥

English
All this could be seen only there by the aspirants. He who wishes to purify without realising the mode of pervasion of the paths is a deceiver of purity. He cannot achieve the fruit. His effort is wasted and leads him to hell. (25-26.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिपातसमायोगादृते तत्त्वानि तत्त्वतः ।
तद्व्याप्तिस्तद्विवृद्धिश्च ज्ञातुमेवं न शक्यते ॥ २७॥

English
It is not possible to know the Tattvas factually nor their pervasiveness nor increase without the union of Sakti and the individual soul. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्तिराज्ञा परा शैवी चिद्रूपा परमेश्वरी ।
शिवोऽधितिष्ठत्यखिलं यया कारणभूतया ॥ २८॥

English
Sakti is the order of Siva in the form of cit. With that as the cause Siva presides over everything. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
नात्मनो नैव मायैषा न विकारो विचारतः ।
न बन्धो नापि मुक्तिश्च बन्धमुक्तिविधायिनी ॥ २९॥

English
It is not of the nature of the Atman nor of Maya nor of secondary nature. It is neither bondage nor liberation but it causes bondage and liberation. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वैश्वर्यपराकाष्ठा शिवस्याव्यभिचारिणी ।
समानधर्मिणी तस्य तैस्तैर्भावैर्विशेषतः ॥ ३०॥

English
She is the acme of Siva's lordship never swerving. By means of sensations she is of the same traits as he. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
स तयैव गृही सापि तेनैव गृहिणी सदा ।
तयोरपत्यं यत्कार्यं परप्रकृतिजं जगत् ॥ ३१॥

English
With her alone he is a househr' der and with him alone she is a housewife always. As a result of their union the universe is the offspring. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
स कर्ता कारणं सेति तयोर्भेदो व्यवस्थितः ।
एक एव शिवः साक्षाद् द्विधाऽसौ समवस्थितः ॥ ३२॥

English
Their difference is specified in this that he is the maker and she is the cause. The single Śiva himself stands two-fold.²²³ (32.)

---

Sanskrit
स्त्रीपुंसभावेन तयोर्भेद इत्यपि केचन ।
अपरे तु परा शक्तिः शिवस्य समवायिनी ॥ ३३॥

प्रभेव भानोश्चिद्रूपा भिन्नैवेति व्यवस्थितः ।
तस्माच्छिवः परो हेतुस्तस्याज्ञा परमेश्वरी ॥ ३४॥

English
Some declare that their difference consists in their genders: man and woman. Others say that she is undetachably associated with him as the light of the sun. But in the form of cit she is different. The arrangement is that Śiva is the great cause and his behest is the great goddess. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
तयैव प्रेरिता शैवी मूलप्रकृतिरव्यया ।
महामाया च माया च प्रकृतिस्त्रिगुणेति च ॥ ३५॥

त्रिविधा कार्यभेदेन सा प्रसूते षडध्वनः ।
स वागर्थमयश्चाध्वा षड्विधो निखिलं जगत् ॥ ३६॥

English
Urged by his behest the original unchanging Prakṛti becomes threefold in accordance with the three types of effects. It is called Mahāmāyā, Māyā and Triguṇā Prakṛti. It produces the six paths in the form of words and their meanings. The entire universe is thus sixfold. The whole collection of scriptural text is only an extension of this. (35-36.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्यैव विस्तरं प्राहुः शास्त्रजातमशेषतः ॥ ३७॥
