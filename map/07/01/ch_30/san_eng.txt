### **CHAPTER THIRTY - (The principle of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
३०

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
चरितानि विचित्राणि गृह्याणि गहनानि च ।
दुर्विज्ञेयानि देवैश्च मोहयन्ति मनांसि नः ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  The activities of deities of domestic nature are curious, dense and inscrutable, even to the gods. They delude our minds. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवयोस्तत्त्वसम्बन्धे न दोष उपलभ्यते ।
चरितैः प्राकृतो भावस्तयोरपि विभाव्यते ॥ २॥

English
In the union of the principles of Śiva and Śivā no defect is noticeable. However in their activities the Prakṛta feeling is rather prominent. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मादयोऽपि लोकानां सृष्टिस्थित्यन्तहेतवः ।
निग्रहानुग्रहौ प्राप्य शिवस्य वशवर्तिनः ॥ ३॥

English
Brahmā and others the causes of creation, sustenance and annihilation attain restraint and blessing from Śiva and are subservient to him. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवः पुनर्न कस्यापि निग्रहानुग्रहास्पदम् ।
अतोऽनायत्तमैश्वर्यं तस्यैवेति विनिश्चितम् ॥ ४॥

English
Śiva is not subject to anyone's Nigraha and Anugraha. Surely his lordship is not dependent on any one else. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्येवमीदृशैश्वर्यं तत्तु स्वातन्त्र्यलक्षणम् ।
स्वभावसिद्धं चैतस्य मूर्तिमत्तास्पदं भवेत् ॥ ५॥

English
If his lordship is like this, characterised by freedom, it can be either innate or subject to his embodied state (5.)

---

Sanskrit
न मूर्तिश्च स्वतन्त्रस्य घटते मूलहेतुना ।
मूर्तेरपि च कार्यत्वात्तत्सिद्धिः स्यादहैतुकी ॥ ६॥

English
But a physical form does not fit in with a free being for this basic reason. But the form is an effect of some cause. Lordship has no cause for its being. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वत्र परमो भावोऽपरमश्चान्य उच्यते ।
परमापरमौ भावौ कथमेकत्र सङ्गतौ ॥ ७॥

English
Everywhere the greater and the lower Bhāvas are mentioned. How can these two be relevant in one place? (7.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्फलो हि स्वभावोऽस्य परमः परमात्मनः ।
स एव सकलः कस्मात्स्वभावो ह्यविपर्ययः ॥ ८॥

English
Indeed the great Ātman is devoid of attributes. How can he become possessed of attributes. Svabhāva is unrevertible. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभावो विपरीतश्चेत्स्वतन्त्रः स्वेच्छया यदि ।
न करोति किमीशानो नित्यानित्यविपर्ययम् ॥ ९॥

English
If you say that his innate nature is reverted by his own wish he being free, why does not Īśāna make an interchange in permanent and non-permanent things? (9.)

---

Sanskrit
मूर्तात्मा सकलः कश्चित्स चान्यो निष्फलः शिवः ।
शिवेनाधिष्ठितश्चेति सर्वत्र लघु कथ्यते ॥ १०॥

English
The wise have said that the embodied Ātman is Sakala; the unembodied Śiva is Niṣkala. The embodied Ātman is presided over by Śiva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
मूर्त्यात्मैव तदा मूर्तिः शिवस्यास्य भवेदिति ।
तस्यां मूर्तौ मूर्तिमतोः पारतन्त्र्यं हि निश्चितम् ॥ ११॥

English
If it is contended that the embodied Ātman is the form of Śiva, the dependence of the embodied on the form becomes certain. How can we explain that? (11.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा निरपेक्षेण मूर्तिः स्वीक्रियते कथम् ।
मूर्तिस्वीकरणं तस्मान्मूर्तौ साध्यफलेप्सया ॥ १२॥

English
Otherwise how is the form adopted by the non-dependant? The adoption of form is with a purpose to get the fruit of his desire. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
न हि स्वेच्छाशरीरत्वं स्वातन्त्र्यायोपपद्यते ।
स्वेच्छैव तादृशी पुंसां यस्मात्कर्मानुसारिणी ॥ १३॥

English
The assuming of physical bodies out of wish is not consistent with freedom. Such a wish too follows the activities of men. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वीकर्तुं स्वेच्छया देहं हातुं च प्रभवन्त्युत ।
ब्रह्मादयः पिशाचान्ताः किं ते कर्मातिवर्तिनः ॥ १४॥

English
Even beings including Brahmā and Piśācas can assume or cast off their bodies out of their wish. But don't they come under the scope of Karmans? (14.)

---

Sanskrit
इच्छया देहनिर्माणमिन्द्रजालोपमं विदुः ।
अणिमादिगुणैश्वर्यवशीकारानतिक्रमात् ॥ १५॥

English
They consider the assumption of bodies at one's choice on a par with jugglery. It is not beyond the achievements derived from Aṇimā and other powers. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
विश्वरूपं दधद्विष्णुर्दधीचेन महर्षिणा ।
युध्यता समुपालब्धस्तद्रूपं दधता स्वयम् ॥ १६॥

English
While Viṣṇu who had assumed the cosmic form was fighting with Dadhīca the latter assumed the cosmic form of Viṣṇu.²²⁴ (16.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्मादधिकस्यापि शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
शरीरवत्तयान्यात्मसाधर्म्यं प्रतिभाति नः ॥ १७॥

English
Although Śiva is the great Ātman and superior to all, his similarity with other beings is observed by us because he has a body. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वानुग्राहकं प्राहुः शिवं परमकारणम् ।
स निर्गृह्णाति देवानां सर्वानुग्राहकः कथम् ॥ १८॥

English
They say that Śiva the supreme cause blesses us all. He curbs and kills the gods. How can he be a blesser of all? (18.)

---

Sanskrit
चिच्छेद बहुशो देवो ब्रह्मणः पञ्चमं शिरः ।
शिवनिन्दां प्रकुर्वन्तं पुत्रेति कुमतेर्हठात् ॥ १९॥

English
The lord cut off the fifth head$^{225}$ of Brahmā who stubbornly and repeatedly censured Śiva calling him “O son.” (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोरपि नृसिंहस्य रभसा शरभाकृतिः ।
बिभेद पद्भ्यामाक्रम्य हृदयं नखरैः खरैः ॥ २०॥

English
In the form of a Śarabha he forcefully attacked Viṣṇu the man-lion. Pressing him with his feet he tore off his heart by his sharp claws. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
देवस्त्रीषु च देवेषु दक्षस्याध्वरकारणात् ।
वीरेण वीरभद्रेण न हि कश्चिददण्डितः ॥ २१॥

English
With the sacrifice of Dakṣa for a pretext none among the gods or their womenfolk was spared from punishment by the heroic Vīrabhadra. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरत्रयं च सस्त्रीकं सदैत्यं सह बालकैः ।
क्षणेनैकेन देवेन नेत्राग्नेरिन्धनीकृतम् ॥ २२॥

English
The three cities$^{226}$ along with the Daityas, their womenfolk and children were made the fuel for the fire from his eyes in an instant by him. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रजानां रतिहेतुश्च कामो रतिपतिः स्वयम् ।
क्रोशतामेव देवानां हुतो नेत्रहुताशने ॥ २३॥

English
Kāma the husband of Rati, the cause of the sexual pleasure of the people was consigned to the fire from his eyes even as the gods were lamenting. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
गावश्च कश्चिद्दुग्धौघं स्रवन्त्यो मूर्ध्नि खेचराः ।
सरुषा प्रेक्ष्य देवेन तत्क्षणे भस्मसात्कृताः ॥ २४॥

English
Angrily glancing at some cows walking along the sky and shedding milk over his head he reduced them to ashes immediately. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
जलन्धरासुरो दीर्णश्चक्रीकृत्य जलं पदा ।
बद्ध्वानन्तेन यो विष्णुं चिक्षेप शतयोजनम् ॥ २५॥

English
The demon Jalandhara who bound Viṣṇu and his Serpent and hurled them a hundred Yojanas, was pierced after evolving a discus by making a circle in water with the foot$^{227}$ (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तमेव जलसन्धायी शूलेनैव जघान सः ।
तच्चक्रं तपसा लब्ध्वा लब्धवीर्यो हरिः सदा ॥ २६॥

English
He then killed him with his trident in the water (?). By performing penance Viṣṇu secured the discus and became powerful. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
जिघांसतां सुरारीणां कुलं निर्घृणचेतसाम् ।
त्रिशूलेनान्धकस्योरः शिखिनैवोपतापितम् ॥ २७॥

English
The family of cruel demons who desired to kill him was scorched in fire. The chest of Andhaka was pierced with the trident. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
कण्ठात्कालाङ्गनां सृष्ट्वा दारकोऽपि निपातितः ।
कौशिकीं जनयित्वा तु गौर्य्यास्त्वक्कोशगोचराम् ॥ २८॥

शुम्भः सह निशुम्भेन प्रापितो मरणं रणे ।
श्रुतं च महदाख्यानं स्कान्दे स्कन्दसमाश्रयम् ॥ २९॥

English
After creating a dark woman from his neck he killed Dāraka. After creating Kauśikī out of the outer skin of Gaurī, Śumbha and Niśumbha were killed.$^{228}$ The narrative on Skanda is found in the Skanda Purāṇa. (28-29.)

---

Sanskrit
वधार्थे तारकाख्यस्य दैत्येन्द्रस्येन्द्रविद्विषः ।
ब्रह्मणाभ्यर्थितो देवो मन्दरान्तःपुरं गतः ॥ ३०॥

English
The lord, requested by Brahmā for the destruction of the leading Daitya Tāraka²²⁹ the enemy of Indra, went to his harem on the Mandara mounton. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
विहृत्य सुचिरं देव्या विहारातिप्रसङ्गतः ।
रसां रसातलं नीतामिव कृत्वाभिधां ततः ॥ ३१॥

देवीं च वञ्चयंस्तस्यां स्ववीर्यमतिदुर्वहम् ।
अविसृज्य विसृज्याग्नौ हविः पूतमिवामृतम् ॥ ३२॥

English
The lord indulged in sexual dalliance with the goddess for a long time. Due to the excessive sport the earth appeared to sink into the nether worlds. He deceived his own name and the goddess by withholding the discharge of semen in her. The unbearable semen was discharged into the fire like nectar or the sanctified Havis. (31-32.)

Sanskrit
गङ्गादिष्वपि निक्षिप्य वह्निद्वारा तदंशतः ।
तत्समाहृत्य शनकैस्तोकं स्तोकमितस्ततः ॥ ३३॥

स्वाहया कृत्तिकारूपात्स्वभर्त्रा रममाणया ।

English
The fire cast it off into Gaṅgā and other places. Svāhā who assumed the form of the Kṛttikās and sported with her husband gathered the parts and deposited them in the Śara plants somewhere on the Meru. Svāhā was turned into gold in the process. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्दीपयित्वा कालेन तस्य भासा दिशो दश ।

English
After some time her lustre illuminated quarters and the mountains. Meru itself was turned into gold. (35.)

---

Sanskrit

English
After a long time the splendour grew into a boy of beautiful and tender limbs, a model for all boys. (36.)

---

Sanskrit

English
On seeing him of charming features, the world of the gods, Asuras and mankind was surprised and fascinated. (37.)

Sanskrit
देवोऽपि स्वयमायातः पुत्रदर्शनलालसः ।

पीतामृतमिव स्नेहविवशेनान्तरात्मना ।

English

The lord made him play on his chest and enjoyed the fun. The lord and the goddess congratulated each other. (38-40.)

Sanskrit

तवावतारो जगतां हितायेत्यनुशास्य च ॥ ४१॥

Tāraka's head along with Indra's fear was chopped off by his trident in battle, the trident that resembled the deadly fire and pierced the Asura Krauñca. Viṣṇu Brahmā and other gods particularly eulogised him. (41-45.)
