### **CHAPTER THIRTYONE - (Instruction in perfect wisdom)**

---

Sanskrit
३१

वायुरुवाच ।
स्थने संशयितं विप्रा भवद्भिर्हेतुचोदितैः ।
जिज्ञासा हि न नास्तिक्यं साधयेत्साधुबुद्धिषु ॥ १॥

English
Vāyu said:—  O brahmins, it is but proper that you have had your doubts prompted by sufficient reasons. Mere desire to know does not bring about atheism in those with good intellects. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमणमत्र वक्ष्यामि सतां मोहनिवर्तकम् ।
असतां त्वन्यथाभावः प्रसादेन विना प्रभोः ॥ २॥

English
I shall mention authoritative testimonies in this context quelling your delusions. The wickedness of the wicked is due to the absence of the grace of the lord. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवस्य परिपूर्णस्य परानुग्रहमन्तरा ।
न किञ्चिदपि कर्तव्यमिति साधु विनिश्चितम् ॥ ३॥

English
It is clear and certain that nothing can be done without the grace of Śiva, the perfect and supreme soul. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वभाव एव पर्याप्तः परानुग्रहकर्मणि ।
अन्यथा निःस्वभावेन न किमप्यनुगृह्यते ॥ ४॥

English
Innate good nature alone is the sufficient ground for the lord's benediction; nothing can be blessed without innate good nature. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
परं सर्वमनुग्राह्यं पशुपाशात्मकं जगत् ।
परस्यानुग्रहार्थं तु पत्युराज्ञासमन्वयः ॥ ५॥

English
The universe in the form of Paśu and Pāśa, has to be blessed. The lord endowed with the authority blesses the universe. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
पतिराज्ञापकः सर्वमनुगृह्णाति सर्वदा ।
तदर्थमर्थस्वीकारे परतन्त्रः कथं शिवः ॥ ६॥

English
The lord, the commander blesses everything always. If a means is adopted for that, how does Śiva become dependant? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुग्राह्यानपेक्षोऽस्ति न हि कश्चिदनुग्रहः ।
अतः स्वातन्त्र्यशब्दार्थाननपेक्षत्वलक्षणः ॥ ७॥

English
No blessing can be independent of one that is blessed. Hence the meaning of the word freedom is not characterised by 'not depending on another'. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्पुनरनुग्राह्यं परतन्त्रं तदिष्यते ।
अनुग्रहाद् ऋते तस्य भुक्तिमुक्त्योरनन्वयात् ॥ ८॥

English
That which is to be blessed is accepted as 'depending on another'. Without blessing, the enjoyment of pleasures or liberation cannot take place. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
मूर्तात्मनोऽप्यनुग्राह्या शिवाज्ञाननिवर्तनात् ।
अज्ञानाधिष्ठितं शम्भोर्न किञ्चिदिह विद्यते ॥ ९॥

English
Embodied Ātmans too stand in the category of those who are to be blessed. Śiva's blessing is construed in the form of driving out their ignorance. There is nothing in Śiva influenced by ignorance. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
येनोपलभ्यतेऽस्माभिः सकलेनापि निष्कलः ।
स मूर्त्यात्मा शिवः शैवमूर्तिरित्युपचर्यते ॥ १०॥

English
Śiva in the form of the idol, whereby despite its being Sāguṇa the Niṣkala is realised, is called Śaivamūrti only in a figurative use. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
न ह्यसौ निष्कलः साक्षाच्छिवः परमकारणम् ।
साकारेणानुभावेन केनाप्यनुपलक्षितः ॥ ११॥

English
Actually in the Niṣkala form Śiva is not the cause of the universe. In the Sakala form too he has not been seen by any. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रमाणगम्यतामात्रं तत्स्वभावोपपादकम् ।
न तावतात्रोपेक्षाधीरुपलक्षणमन्तरा ॥ १२॥

English
The state of being understood through the testimonies alone indicates his nature. Merely on account of this it should not be treated with indifference without the implication. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मोपमोल्बणं साक्षान्मूर्तिरेव हि काचन ।
शिवस्य मूर्तिर्मूर्त्यात्मा परस्तस्योपलक्षणम् ॥ १३॥

English
Some form, intense with the similarity of Ātman, is Śiva's idol. The other, the great one, the Ātman of the Mūrti is its implication. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा काष्ठेष्वनारूढो न वह्निरुपलभ्यते ।
एवं शिवोऽपि मूर्त्यात्मन्यनारूढ इति स्थितिः ॥ १४॥

English
Just as fire cannot be obtained without its being mounted in the twigs so also Śiva is not known or understood without its being superimposed in the idol. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यथाग्निमानयेत्युक्ते ज्वलत्काष्ठादृते स्वयम् ।
नाग्निरानीयते तद्वत्पूज्यो मूर्त्यात्मना शिवः ॥ १५॥

English
If some one says "Bring fire," only the burning twig is brought. The fire by itself is not brought. In the same way Śiva is to be worshipped in the form of the idol. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अत एव हि पूजादौ मूर्त्यात्मपरिकल्पनम् ।
मूर्त्यात्मनि कृतं साक्षाच्छिव एव कृतं यतः ॥ १६॥

English
That is why an idol is used in the worship of Śiva, for what is done unto the idol is what is done unto Śiva himself. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
लिङ्गादावपि तत्कृत्यमर्चायां च विशेषतः ।
तत्तन्मूर्त्यात्मभावेन शिवोऽस्माभिरुपास्यते ॥ १७॥

English
In the form of different idols, phallic etc. and particularly in Arcā (offerings of flowers etc.) Śiva is worshipped by us. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
यथानुगृह्यते सोऽपि मूर्त्यात्मा पारमेष्ठिना ।
तथा मूर्त्यात्मनिष्ठेन शिवेन पशवो वयम् ॥ १८॥

English
Just as the Ātman of the idol is blessed by the great Ātman so also we, the paśus, are blessed by Śiva stationed in the Mūrtyātman. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
लोकानुग्रहणायैव शिवेन परमेष्ठिना ।
सदाशिवादयः सर्वे मूर्त्यात्मनोऽप्यधिष्ठिताः ॥ १९॥

English
The Mūrtyātmans, Sadāśiva and others are presided over by Śiva for blessing the worlds. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
आत्मनामेव भोगाय मोक्षाय च विशेषतः ।
तत्त्वातत्त्वस्वरूपेषु मूर्त्यात्मसु शिवान्वयः ॥ २०॥

English
It is for the enjoyment of pleasures and for salvation in particular, that Śiva’s connection with the Mūrtyātmans in the form of Tattva and Atattva is accepted. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
भोगः कर्मविपाकात्मा सुखदुःखात्मको मतः ।
न च कर्म शिवेऽस्तीति तस्य भोगः किमात्मकः ॥ २१॥

English
Enjoyment of pleasures is in the nature of the ultimate transformation of the Karmans either by way of happiness or by way of misery. There is no Karman in Śiva and hence of what nature can his enjoyment be? (21.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं शिवोऽनुगृह्णाति न निगृह्णाति किञ्चन ।
निगृह्णतां तु ये दोषाश्शिवे तेषामसम्भवात् ॥ २२॥

English
Śiva blessess every one. He does not curb any. It is impossible to attribute to Śiva those faults which are usually present in those who kill. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ये पुनर्निग्रहाः केचिद् ब्रह्मादिषु निदर्शिताः ।
तेऽपि लोकहितायैव कृताः श्रीकण्ठमूर्तिना ॥ २३॥

English
The instances of killing and curbing pointed out with regard to Brahmā and others are those of the activities of Śrikaṇṭhamūrti performed for the welfare of the worlds. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्माण्डस्याधिपत्यं हि श्रीकण्ठस्य न संशयः ।
श्रीकण्ठाख्यां शिवो मूर्तिं क्रीडतीमधितिष्ठति ॥ २४॥

English
Surely Śrikaṇṭha has the overlordship of the universe. Śiva presides over the Mūrti Śrikaṇṭha in the course of his divine sport. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
सदोषा एव देवाद्या निगृहीता यथोदितम् ।
ततस्तेऽपि विपाप्मानः प्रजाश्चापि गतज्वराः ॥ २५॥

English
Only the gods and others who were faulty were restrained or slain by him as described above. Thereby the gods became sinners and the people free from ailments. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
निग्रहोऽपि स्वरूपेण विदुषां न जुगुप्सितः ।
अत एव हि दण्ड्येषु दण्डो राज्ञां प्रशस्यते ॥ २६॥

English
Restraining or killing as such is not declared despicable by the learned. That is why the punishment meted out by kings to those who deserve it, is commended. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
यत्सिद्धिरीश्वरत्वेन कार्यवर्गस्य कृत्स्नशः ।
न स चेदीशतां कुर्याज्जगतः कथमीश्वरः ॥ २७॥

English
If he does not have that which is achieved through the suzerainty of the whole class of effects how can he rule over the universe? (27.)

---

Sanskrit
ईशेच्छा च विधातृत्वं विधेराज्ञापनं परम् ।
आज्ञावश्यमिदं कुर्यान्न कुर्यादिति शासनम् ॥ २८॥

English
The wish of the lord comprises the establishment of rules and conditions. Brahmā is the commandment. His order is the mode of direction such as, “This shall be done. This shall not be done.” (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छासनानुवर्तित्वं साधुभावस्य लक्षणम् ।
विपरीतमसाधोः स्यान्न सर्वं तत्तु दृश्यते ॥ २९॥

English
The characteristic of a man of good nature is the strict adherence to his directives; the opposite thereof is that of the non-saintly one. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
साधु संरक्षणीयं चेद्विनिवर्त्यमसाधु यत् ।
निवर्तते च सामादेरन्ते दण्डो हि साधनम् ॥ ३०॥

English
If good nature is to be preserved the evil one is to be eschewed. It is quelled by the expedients of Sāma etc. If other means fail punishment alone is the means. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
हितार्थलक्षणं चेदं दण्डान्तमनुशासनम् ।
अतो यद्विपरीतं तदहितं सम्प्रचक्षते ॥ ३१॥

English
This is the characteristic of what is beneficent viz. chastisement ending with disciplinary measures. What is contrary to this is called maleficent. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
हिते सदा निषण्णानामीश्वरस्य निदर्शनम् ।
स कथं दुष्यते सद्भिरसतामेव निग्रहात् ॥ ३२॥

English
The lord is the standard example of those who perpetually abide by what is beneficent. How can he be condemned by good men for curbing and killing the evil ones alone. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
अयुक्तकारिणो लोके गर्हणीया विवेकिता ।
यदुद्वेजयते लोकं तदयुक्तं प्रचक्षते ॥ ३३॥

English
Perpetrators of improper actions are to be decried and despised by a judicious person. Improper action is that which afflicts and harasses the world. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वोऽपि निग्रहो लोके न च विद्वेषपूर्वकः ।
न हि द्वेष्टि पिता पुत्रं यो निगृह्याति शिक्षयेत् ॥ ३४॥

English
Every act of curbing and restraining is not attended with hatred. A father who trains his son even by curbing and restraining him does not hate him. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
माध्यस्थेनापि निग्राह्यान्यो निगृह्णाति मार्गतः ।
तस्याप्यवश्यं यत्किञ्चिन्नैर्घृण्यमनुवर्तते ॥ ३५॥

English
There is bound to be some ruthlessness in him who curbs or slays those worthy of it even by standing detached. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा न हिनस्त्येव सदोषानप्यसौ परान् ।
हिनस्ति चायमप्यज्ञान्परं माध्यस्थ्यमाचरन् ॥ ३६॥

English
The lord does not injure others though guilty, otherwise. Of course, he injures the ignorant by adopting neutrality. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद्दुःखात्मिकां हिंसां कुर्वाणो यः स निर्घृणः ।
इति निर्बन्धयन्त्येके नियमो नेति चापरे ॥ ३७॥

English
Hence we shall say that he who inflicts injury ultimately painful is ruthless. Thus a few insist upon this condition. Others do not. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
निदानज्ञस्य भिषजो रुग्णे हिंसां प्रयुञ्जतः ।
न किञ्चिदपि नैर्घृण्यं घृणैवात्र प्रयोजिका ॥ ३८॥

English
Ruthlessness cannot be attributed to the surgeon who operates upon the patient. The inducing factor is kindness alone. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
घृणापि न गुणायैव हिंस्रेषु प्रतियोगिषु ।
तादृशेषु घृणी भ्रान्त्या घृणान्तरितनिर्घृणः ॥ ३९॥

English
Even kindness to violent enemies is not conducive to ultimate good. He who is kind to such persons is ruthless though his ruthlessness is concealed by an illusory kindness. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
उपेक्षापीह दोषाय रक्ष्येषु प्रतियोगिषु ।
शक्तौ सत्यामुपेक्षातो रक्ष्यः सद्यो विपद्यते ॥ ४०॥

English
Even neglect and indifference for the opponents who ought to be protected, results in default. He who ought to be protected perishes immediately if neglected despite the capacity of the protector. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्पस्यास्यगतं पश्यन् यस्तु रक्ष्यमुपेक्षते ।
दोषाभासान्समुत्प्रेक्ष्य फलतः सोऽपि निर्घृणः ॥ ४१॥

English
He who neglects the man worthy of being protected on considering the apparent defaults though he observes his plight is in effect ruthless. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माद् घृणा गुणायैव सर्वथेति न सम्मतम् ।
सम्मतं प्राप्तकामित्वं सर्वं त्वन्यदसम्मतम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Hence it is not generally agreed that kindness is conducive to good in every respect. What is admitted is the performance of what is befitting. Everything else is unapproved. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
मूर्त्यात्मस्वपि रागाद्या दोषाः सन्त्येव वस्तुतः ।
तथापि तेषामेवैते न शिवस्य तु सर्वथा ॥ ४३॥

अग्नावपि समाविष्टं ताम्रं खलु सकालिकम् ।
इति नाग्निरसौ दुष्येत्ताम्रसंसर्गकारणात् ॥ ४४॥

नाग्नेरशुचिसंसर्गादशुचित्वमपेक्षते ।
अशुचेस्त्वग्निसंयोगाच्छुचित्वमपि जायते ॥ ४५॥

English
In reality there are the defects of passion, etc., in the Murtyatmans (Sadasiva, Brahma and others). Still the defects belong to them alone and not to Siva. There may be flaw in the copper put in fire but due to its contact fire does not fade. If impure things are consigned to fire they do not make the fire impure. Some impure things become pure due to their contact with fire. Similarly Siva does not become impure due to the contact with the Atmans that are to be purified. (43-45.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं शोध्यात्मसंसर्गान्न ह्यशुद्धः शिवो भवेत् ।
शिवसंसर्गतस्त्वेष शोध्यात्मैव हि शुद्ध्यति ॥ ४६॥

English
The Atman alone is purified through the contact with Siva. If the iron-rod is put in fire and heated, the burning is that of the fire, not of the iron-rod. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अयस्यग्नौ समाविष्टे दाहोऽग्नेरेव नायसः ।
मूर्त्तात्मन्येवमैश्वर्यमीश्वरस्यैव नात्मनाम् ॥ ४७॥

English
The prosperity, glory etc. of the Murtyatmans is really that of lord Siva and not of the Atmans. It is the fire, not the fuel, that blazes upwards. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
न हि काष्ठं ज्वलत्यूर्ध्वमग्निरेव ज्वलत्यसौ ।
काष्ठस्याङ्गारता नाग्नेरेवमत्रापि योज्यताम् ।
अत एव जगत्यस्मिन्काष्ठपाषाणमृत्स्वपि ॥ ४८॥

शिवावेशवशादेव शिवत्वमुपचर्यते ।
मैत्र्यादयो गुणा गौणास्तस्मात्ते भिन्नवृत्तयः ।
तैर्गुणैरुपरक्तानां दोषाय च गुणाय च ॥ ४९॥

यत्तु गौणमगौणं च तत्सर्वमनुगृह्णतः ।
न गुणाय न दोषाय शिवस्य गुणवृत्तयः ॥ ५०॥

English
The state of being the coal belongs to the wood and not to the fire. Similarly Sivatva is imposed on wood, stone and clay. But the attributes Maitri, etc., are secondary and they act differently. They are conducive to both good and evil to those who are endowed with qualities. What is both secondary and non-secondary is not wholly conducive to good or evil. (48-50.)

---

Sanskrit
न चानुग्रहशब्दार्थं गौणमाहुर्विपश्चितः ।
संसारमोचनं किं तु शैवमाज्ञामयं हितम् ॥ ५१॥

English
Learned men do not say that the meaning of Anugraha is secondary. But the liberation from worldly existence is in the form of Ajna and is beneficent. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
हितं तदाज्ञाकरणं यद्धितं तदनुग्रहः ।
सर्वं हिते नियुञ्जानः सर्वानुग्रहकारकः ॥ ५२॥

English
Carrying out his Ajna is beneficent. What is beneficent is Blessing. He who employs everything in what is beneficent is the cause of blessing to all. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्तूपकारशब्दार्थस्तमप्याहुरनुग्रहम् ।
तस्यापि हितरूपत्वाच्छिवः सर्वोपकारकः ॥ ५३॥

English
