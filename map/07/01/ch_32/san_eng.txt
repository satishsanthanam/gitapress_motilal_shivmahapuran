### **CHAPTER THIRTY TWO - (The description of excellent practice)**

---

Sanskrit
३२

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
किं तच्छ्रेष्ठमनुष्ठानं मोक्षो येनापरोक्षितः ।
तत्तस्य साधनं चाद्य वक्तुमर्हसि मारुत ॥ १॥

English
The sages said :—  What is that excellent practice of holy rites, whereby salvation is directly acquired? O Māruta, it behoves you to explain it as well as its means. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
शैवो हि परमो धर्मः श्रेष्ठानुष्ठानशब्दितः ।
यत्रापरोक्षो लक्ष्येत साक्षान्मोक्षप्रदः शिवः ॥ २॥

English
Vāyu said:—  What is termed excellent practice is the great cult of Śiva wherein Śiva the bestower of salvation is directly perceived. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
स तु पञ्चविधो ज्ञेयः पञ्चभिः पर्वभिः क्रमात् ।
क्रियातपोजपध्यानज्ञानात्मभिरनुत्तरैः ॥ ३॥

English
It is fivefold divided into five sections, holy rites, penance, japa, meditation and knowledge (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तैरेव सोत्तरैः सिद्धो धर्मस्तु परमो मतः ।
परोक्षमपरोक्षं च ज्ञानं यत्र च मोक्षदम् ॥ ४॥

English
The five activities along with attendant virtuous rites constitute the greatest Dharma. Thereby one attains the direct and indirect knowledge that bestows salvation. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
परमोऽपरमश्चोभौ धर्मौ हि श्रुतिचोदितौ ।
धर्मशब्दाभिधेयेऽर्थे प्रमाणं श्रुतिरेव नः ॥ ५॥

English
The two Dharmas the higher and secondary are mentioned in the Vedas. In the matter of Dharma the Vedas constitute the final authority for us. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
परमो योगपर्यन्तो धर्मः श्रुतिशिरोगतः ।
धर्मस्त्वपरमस्तद्वदधः श्रुतिमुखोत्थितः ॥ ६॥

English
The higher dharma upto the practice of Yoga is mentioned in the Vedantic section of the Vedas. The secondary Dharma is mentioned in the Karmakāṇḍa section of the same. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अपश्वात्माधिकारत्वाद्यो धर्मः परमो मतः ।
साधारणस्ततोऽन्यस्तु सर्वेषामधिकारतः ॥ ७॥

English
The Ātmans freed from Pāśa are authorised in the higher Dharma and in the other one, every one is authorised. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
स चायं परमो धर्मः परधर्मस्य साधनम् ।
धर्मशास्त्रादिभिः सम्यक् साङ्ग एवोपबृंहितः ॥ ८॥

English
This higher Dharma is the means for achieving the greatest virtue of Śiva. It shall be supplemented in all parts by Dhasmaśāstras and other holy treatises. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवो यः परमो धर्मः श्रेष्ठानुष्ठानशब्दितः ।
इतिहासपुराणाभ्यां कथञ्चिदुपबृंहितः ॥ ९॥

English
The greatest Dharma of Śiva termed the excellent practice is explained in the Itihāsas and the Purāṇas. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवागमैस्तु सम्पन्नः सहाङ्गोपाङ्गविस्तरः ।
तत्संस्काराधिकारैश्च सम्यगेवोपबृंहितः ॥ १०॥

English
In the Śaiva Āgamas a detailed explanation of every thing including the consecratory rites is given. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शैवागमो हि द्विविधः श्रौतोऽश्रौतश्च संस्कृतः ।
श्रुतिसारमयः श्रौतः स्वतन्त्र इतरो मतः ॥ ११॥

English
The Śaiva āgama is of two varieties : Śrauta and Aśrauta. The Śrauta consists of condensed Vedic texts; the other one consists of independent texts but well consecrated. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वतन्त्रो दशधा पूर्वं तथाष्टादशधा पुनः ।
कामिकादिसमाख्याभिः सिद्धः सिद्धान्तसंज्ञितः ॥ १२॥

English
The independent texts were originally ten but supplemented subsequently by eight more so as to constitute eighteen texts. They are called Kāmika etc., and the entire literature is called "Śaiva Siddhānta" (12.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुतिसारमयो यस्तु शतकोटिप्रविस्तरः ।
परं पाशुपतं यत्र व्रतं ज्ञानं च कथ्यते ॥ १३॥

English
The Śrauta literature consists of a billion verses. In it the Pāśupata Vrata and Jñāna are explained. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
युगावर्तेषु शिष्येत योगाचार्यस्वरूपिणा ।
तत्र तत्रावतीर्णेन शिवेनैव प्रवर्त्यते ॥ १४॥

English
In every circle of Yugas, Śiva incarnates as Yogācārya in different places and propagates yoga. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
सङ्क्षिप्यास्य प्रवक्तारश्चत्वारः परमर्षयः ।
रुरुर्दधीचोऽगस्त्यश्च उपमन्युर्महायशाः ॥ १५॥

English
The four great sages Ruru, Dadhīca, Agastya and the renowned Upamanyu have condensed these principles and propagated them. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
ते च पाशुपता ज्ञेयाः संहितानां प्रवर्तकाः ।
तत्सन्ततीया गुरवः शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ॥ १६॥

English
They are all Pāśupatas and exponents of the Saṃhitās. Hundreds and thousands of their descendants have been the preceptors of their principles. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रोक्तः परमो धर्मश्चर्याद्यात्मा चतुर्विधः ।
तेषु पाशुपतो योगः शिवं प्रत्यक्षयेद् दृढम् ॥ १७॥

English
The great virtue of Śiva mentioned before is four-fold with regular performance and observance as the basic, Ātman for them. Among them the Pāśupata yoga facilitates the direct perception of Śiva. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छ्रेष्ठमनुष्ठानं योगः पाशुपतो मतः ।
तत्राप्युपायको युक्तो ब्रह्मणा स तु कथ्यते ॥ १८॥

English
Hence the excellent practice is this Pāśupata Yoga. The mode of it as practised by Brahmā shall now be mentioned. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
नामाष्टकमयो योगः शिवेन परिकल्पितः ।
तेन योगेन सहसा शैवी प्रज्ञा प्रजायते ॥ १९॥

English
This is Nāmāṣṭakayoga prescribed by Śiva himself. By means of this Yoga the discernment of Śiva is generated. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रज्ञया परमं ज्ञानमचिराल्लभते स्थिरम् ।
प्रसीदति शिवस्तस्य यस्य ज्ञानं प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ २०॥

English
Through this discernment the stable and perfect knowledge is attained ere long. Śiva is delighted with him whose knowledge is well-founded. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रसादात्परमो योगो यः शिवं चापरोक्षयेत् ।
शिवापरोक्षात्संसारकारणेन वियुज्यते ॥ २१॥

English
Thanks to his grace the great Yoga is attained which facilitates the direct perception of Śiva. By perceiving Śiva directly the cause of worldly existence is quelled. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्यान्मुक्तसंसारो मुक्तः शिवसमो भवेत् ।
ब्रह्मप्रोक्त इत्युपायः स एव पृथगुच्यते ॥ २२॥

English
Then the devotee is liberated from worldly existence and being liberated he becomes identical with Śiva. The means mentioned by Brahmā is now separately mentioned here. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवो महेश्वरश्चैव रुद्रो विष्णुः पितामहः ।
संसारवैद्यः सर्वज्ञः परमात्मेति मुख्यतः ॥ २३॥

नामाष्टकमिदं मुख्यं शिवस्य प्रतिपादकम् ।
आद्यं तु पञ्चकं ज्ञेयं शान्त्यतीताद्यनुक्रमात् ॥ २४॥

English
Śiva, Maheśvara, Rudra, Viṣṇu, Pitāmaha, Samsāravaidya, Sarvajña and Paramātman—these eight names mainly indicate Śiva. The first five are the names of the deities presiding over the Kalās, Śāntyatīta etc.²¹⁰ (23-24.)

---

Sanskrit
संज्ञा सदाशिवादीनां पञ्चोपाधिपरिग्रहात् ।
उपाधिविनिवृत्तौ तु यथास्वं विनिवर्तते ॥ २५॥

पदमेव हि तं नित्यमनित्याः पदिनः स्मृताः ।
पदानां परिवृत्तौ तु मुच्यन्ते पदिनो यतः ॥ २६॥

परिवृत्त्यन्तरे भूयस्तत्पदप्राप्तिरुच्यते ।
आत्मान्तराभिधानं स्याद्यदाद्यं नामपञ्चकम् ॥ २७॥

अन्यत्तु त्रितयं नाम्नामुपादानादियोगतः ।
त्रिविधोपाधिवचनाच्छिव एवानुवर्तते ॥ २८॥

English
The five names of Sadāśiva originate from the conditioning factors. When the conditioning factors cease to exist they too recede. The region is eternal and the Ātmans who occupy them are non-eternal. When the Padas are changed the Padins are released. In another evolution they attain the same region. But the first five Ātmans undergo the change of names. The last three names are due to the adoption of the three conditioning factors. They indicate only Śiva. (25-28.)

---

Sanskrit
अनादिमलसंश्लेषः प्रागभावात्स्वभावतः ।
अत्यन्तं परिशुद्धात्मेत्यतोऽयं शिव उच्यते ॥ २९॥

अथवाशेषकल्याणगुणैकधन ईश्वरः ।
शिव इत्युच्यते सद्भिः शिवतत्त्वार्थवादिभिः ॥ ३०॥

English
He who is naturally pure is called Śiva. He has the antecedent non-existence of the contact of the primordial dirt. Or, he who is full of good attributes and is Iśvara is called Śiva by good men who propound the Śaivite principles. (29-30.)

---

Sanskrit
त्रयोविंशतितत्त्वेभ्यः प्रकृतिर्हि परा मता ।
प्रकृतेस्तु परं प्राहुः पुरुषं पञ्चविंशकम् ॥ ३१॥

यं वेदादौ स्वरं प्राहुर्वाच्यवाचकभावतः ।
वेदैकवेद्ययाथात्म्याद्वेदान्ते च प्रतिष्ठितः ॥ ३२॥

तस्य प्रकृतिलीनस्य यः परः स महेश्वरः ।
तदधीनप्रवृत्तित्वात्प्रकृतेः पुरुषस्य च ॥ ३३॥

अथवा त्रिगुणं तत्त्वमुपेयमिदमव्ययम् ।
मायां तु प्रकृतिं विद्यान्मायिनं तु महेश्वरम् ॥ ३४॥

मायाविक्षोभकोऽनन्तो महेश्वरसमन्वयात् ।
कालात्मा परमात्मादिः स्थूलः सूक्ष्मः प्रकीर्तितः ॥ ३५॥

English
The name Maheśvara is explained thus: Prakṛti is greater than the twenty-three principles. Puruṣa the twenty fifth principle is greater than Prakṛti.²³¹ Puruṣa is Praṇava the first Svara in the Vedas. Since his real nature is comprehensible only through the Vedas he is established in the Vedānta. He who is beyond this Puruṣa, who is associated with Prakṛti is Maheśvara because both the Prakṛti and Puruṣa function in subservience to him. Or, Maheśvara is the wielder of Māyā. Māyā is Prakṛti the principle with the three Guṇas. It is unchanging. He who makes this Māyā energetic is Maheśvara. He is glorified as Kālātman, Paramātman, the primordial, the gross and the subtle. (31-35.)

Sanskrit

रुद्र इत्युच्यते सद्भिः शिवः परमकारणम् ॥ ३६॥

English
The explanation of the word Rudra:— 'Rud' means misery and 'Drāvayati' means 'routs'. Since the lord quells our misery he is called Rudra.²³² He is Śiva, the great cause. (36.)

Sanskrit
तत्त्वादिभूतपर्यन्तं शरीरादिष्वतन्द्रितः ।
व्याप्याधितिष्ठति शिवस्ततो रुद्र इतस्ततः ॥ ३७॥

English
Śiva pervades all living beings, the principles and elements. He is wakefully present in the bodies and presides over them. Hence he is called Viṣṇu. (37.)

---

Sanskrit

पितृभावेन सर्वेषां पितामह उदीरितः ॥ ३८॥

English
Śiva is the progenitor of the souls that have attained the status of fathers. He is therefore called the grandfather. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
निदानज्ञो यथा वैद्यो रोगस्य विनिवर्तकः ।
उपायैर्भेषजैस्तद्वल्लयभोगाधिकारतः ॥ ३९॥

संसारस्येश्वरो नित्यं समूलस्य निवर्तकः ।

English
Śiva is called the physician of the universe. Just as the physician who is conversant with the pathology diagnoses and cures the ailment with medicines so also the lord annihilates the worldly existence along with its roots. He is so called by all those who understand the nature of principles. (39-40.)

Sanskrit
दशार्थज्ञानसिद्ध्यर्थमिन्द्रियेष्वेषु सत्स्वपि ।
त्रिकालभाविनो भावान्स्थूलान्सूक्ष्मानशेषतः ॥ ४१॥

अणवो नैव जानन्ति माययैव मलावृताः ।
असत्स्वपि च सर्वेषु सर्वार्थज्ञानहेतुषु ॥ ४२॥

यद्यथावस्थितं वस्तु तत्तथैव सदाशिवः ।
अयत्नेनैव जानाति तस्मात्सर्वज्ञ उच्यते ॥ ४३॥

English
Even when they have the sense-organs for comprehending the ten objects of sensual perception, the atoms do not know the beings gross and subtle, present in the three periods of time, in their entirety because they are hidden by the particles of dirt in the form of Māyā, whereas Sadāśiva has not these causes of perception. Even when they are not present, he knows without any strain every object as it is. Hence, he is Sarvajña (omniscient). (41-43.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वात्मा परमैरेभिर्गुणैर्नित्यसमन्वयात् ।
स्वस्मात्परात्मविरहात्परमात्मा शिवः स्वयम् ॥ ४४॥

English
Śiva is the Ātman of all. He perpetually possesses all these qualities. There is no greater Ātman than Śiva. Śiva is Paramātman. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
नामाष्टकमिदं चैव लब्ध्वाचार्यप्रसादतः ।
निवृत्त्यादिकलाग्रन्थिं शिवाद्यैः पञ्चनामभिः ॥ ४५॥

यथास्वं क्रमशश्छित्वा शोधयित्वा यथागुणम् ।

हृत्कण्ठतालुभ्रूमध्यब्रह्मरन्ध्रसमन्विताम् ।
छित्त्वा पर्यष्टकाकारं स्वात्मानं च सुषुम्णया ॥ ४७॥

English

The knots of the Kalā, Nivṛtti and others shall be cut off with the five names of Śiva and purified by repetitions, strokes and non-restraints. By means of the Suṣumṇā, the Puryāṣṭaka alone with the chest, neck, palate, middle of the eyebrows and the hole on the top of the head, shall be cut off. (45-47.)

Sanskrit
द्वादशान्तः स्थितस्येन्दोर्नीत्वोपरि शिवौजसि ।
संहृत्य वदनं पश्चाद्यथासंस्करणं लयात् ॥ ४८॥

शाक्तेनामृतवर्षेण संसिक्तायां तनौ पुनः ।

English
The Ātman shall be taken above to the splendour of Śiva beyond the moon stationed in the twelve-petalled lotus of the heart. The mouth is shrunk in size. The body is drenched with the shower of the nectar of the Śakti and merged in their reasons. The Ātman is then let down into the heart. (48-49.)

Sanskrit
द्वादशान्तःस्थितस्येन्दोः परस्ताच्छ्वेतपङ्कजे ।
समासीनं महादेवं शङ्करं भक्तवत्सलम् ॥ ५०॥

अर्धनारीश्वरं देवं निर्मलं मधुराकृतिम् ।
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशं प्रसन्नं शीतलद्युतिम् ॥ ५१॥

ध्यात्वा हि मानसे देवं स्वस्थचित्तोऽथ मानवः ।
शिवनामाष्टकेनैव भावपुष्पैः समर्चयेत् ॥ ५२॥

English
The devotee then meditates on the great god Śiva, favourably disposed to his devotees who is conceived as sitting in the white twelve-petalled lotus beyond the moon, who in the sweet crystal-pure, delighted, cool, lustrous form of Arḍhanārīśvara, shall be meditated upon. The devotee shall have the mind in normal state. He shall then worship the lord with the eight names of Śiva and the sacred flowers. (50-52.)

---

Sanskrit
अभ्यर्च्चनान्ते तु पुनः प्राणानायम्य मानवः ।
सम्यक् चित्तं समाधाय शार्वं नामाष्टकं जपेत् ॥ ५३॥

English
At the end of the worship the devotee shall perform Prāṇāyāma and concentrate the mind well. He shall perform the japa of the eight names of Śiva. (53.)

Sanskrit
नाभौ चाष्टाहुतीर्हुत्वा पूर्णाहुत्या नमस्ततः ।
अष्टपुष्पप्रदानेन कृत्वाभ्यर्च्चनमन्तिमम् ॥ ५४॥

निवेदयेत्स्वमात्मानं चुलुकोदकवर्त्मना ।
एवं कृत्वाचिरादेव ज्ञानं पाशुपतं शुभम् ॥ ५५॥

लभते तत्प्रतिष्ठां च वृत्तं चानुत्तमं तथा ।
योगं च परमं लब्ध्वा मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ५६॥

English
He shall perform eight Āhutis in the navel and repeat “Namah” alone with the Pūrṇāhūti, offering eight flowers and conclude worship. With a palmful of water he shall dedicate his Ātman. By doing this, ere long, the auspicious knowledge of Pāśupata cult is obtained. He acquires its magnificent status and the excellent conduct. Then securing the great Yoga he is liberated. There is no doubt in this. (54-56.)
