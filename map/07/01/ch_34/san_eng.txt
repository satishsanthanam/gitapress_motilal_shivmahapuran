### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - (The penance of Upamanyu)²³⁷**

---

Sanskrit
३४

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
धौम्याग्रजेन शिशुना क्षीरार्थं हि तपः कृतम् ।
तस्मात् क्षीरार्णवो दत्तस्तस्मै देवेन शूलिना ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  It was for obtaining milk that penance was performed by the boy Upamanyu, the elder brother of Dhaumya. So an ocean of milk was granted to him by lord Siva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
स कथं शिशुको लेभे शिवशास्त्रप्रवक्तृताम् ।
कथं वा शिवसद्भावं ज्ञात्वा तपसि निष्ठितः ॥ २॥

English
How could that infant attain power to propound the sacred doctrine of Siva? How could he realise the existence of Siva and perform the penance? (2.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं च लब्धविज्ञानस्तपश्चरणपर्वणि ।
रुद्राग्नेर्यत्परं वीर्यं लभे भस्म स्वरक्षकम् ॥ ३॥

English
How could he attain the perfect knowledge in the course of his observance of penance? How did he secure the protective ashes the virile element of the Rudra fire. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
न ह्येष शिशुकः कश्चित्प्राकृतः कृतवांस्तपः ।
मुनिवर्यस्य तनयो व्याघ्रपादस्य धीमतः ॥ ४॥

English
Vāyu said :—  It was not an ignorant common infant who performed the penance. He was the son of a great, wise and the learned sage Vyāghrapāda.[236] (4.)

---

Sanskrit
जन्मान्तरेण संसिद्धः केनापि खलु हेतुना ।
स्वपदप्रच्युतो दिष्ट्या प्राप्तो मुनिकुमारताम् ॥ ५॥

English
He was a great Siddha in his former birth who had fallen from his position for some reason. Fortunately he was born as the son of a sage. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
महादेवप्रसादस्य भाग्यापन्नस्य भाविनः ।
दुग्धाभिलाषप्रभवद्वारतामगमत्तपः ॥ ६॥

English
His penance for the sake of obtaining milk became the gateway to the future grace of lord Siva obtained due to good luck. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अतः सर्वगणेशत्वं कुमारत्वं च शाश्वतम् ।
सह दुग्धाब्धिना तस्मै प्रददौ शङ्करः स्वयम् ॥ ७॥

English
Śiva granted him perpetual bachelorhood, lord-ship of all the Gaṇas along with an ocean of milk. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य ज्ञानागमोऽप्यस्य प्रसादादेव शाङ्करात् ।
कौमारं हि परं साक्षाज्ज्ञानं शक्तिमयं विदुः ॥ ८॥

English
The acquisition of Śiva's knowledge is due to Śiva's grace alone. He acquired the knowledge of Śakti even as a boy, (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवशास्त्रप्रवक्तृत्वमपि तस्य हि तत्कृतम् ।
कुमारोमुखतो लब्धज्ञानाब्धेरिव नन्दिनः ॥ ९॥

English
His ability to propound the Śivaśāstra was also derived from the lord. He rejoiced in attaining the ocean of knowledge from the sage. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
दृष्टं तु कारणं तस्य शिवज्ञानसमन्वये ।
स्वमातृवचनं साक्षाच्छोकजं क्षीरकारणात् ॥ १०॥

English
There was an apparent reason for his acquisition of Śiva-jñāna. These were his mother's words full of sorrow for the milk. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
कदाचित्क्षीरमत्यल्पं पीतवान्मातुलाश्रमे ।
ईर्ष्यया मातुलसुतं सन्तृप्तक्षीरमुत्तमम् ॥ ११॥

पीत्वा स्थितं यथाकामं दृष्ट्वा वै मातुलात्मजम् ।
उपमन्युर्व्याघ्रपादिः प्रीत्या प्रोवाच मातरम् ॥ १२॥

English
Once the boy had tasted some quantity of milk in the hermitage of his uncle. His uncle's son had drunk ample milk to his satiety. The jealous Upamanyu affectionately told his mother thus. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
मातर्मातर्महाभागे मम देहि तपस्विनि ।
गव्यं क्षीरमतिस्वादु नाल्पमुष्णं पिबाम्यहम् ॥ १३॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  O mother, my blessed mother, O saintly woman, please give me sweet cow's milk. I shall drink it very hot. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
तच्छ्रुत्वा पुत्रवचनं तन्माता च तपस्विनी ।
व्याघ्रपादस्य महिषी दुःखमापत्तदा च सा ॥ १४॥

English
Vāyu said:—  On hearing the words of her son, the saintly mother, the wife of Vyāghrapāda, was very much distressed. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
उपलाल्याथ सुप्रीत्या पुत्रमालिङ्ग्य सादरम् ।
दुःखिता विललापाथ स्मृत्वा नैर्धन्यमात्मनः ॥ १५॥

English
With love and affection she embraced the son and fondled him. Considering her poverty she became dejected and lamented. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
स्मृत्वा स्मृत्वा पुनः क्षीरमुपमन्युः स बालकः ।
देहि देहीति तामाह रुद्रन्भूयो महाद्युतिः ॥ १६॥

English
The boy Upamanyu could not forget the milk he wanted. He the innocent child of great splendour cried frequently pressing his mother to give him milk. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तद् हठं सा परिज्ञाय द्विजपत्नी तपस्विनी ।
शान्तये तद् हठस्याथ शुभोपायमरीरचत् ॥ १७॥

English
Realising that the boy was tenacious the saintly brahmin lady thought of a plan to quieten his stubbornness (17.)

---

Sanskrit
उञ्छवृत्त्यार्जितान्बीजान्स्वयं दृष्ट्वा च सा तदा ।
बीजपिष्टमथालोड्य तोयेन कलभाषिणी ॥ १८॥

एह्येहि मम पुत्रेति सामपूर्वं ततः सुतम् ।
आलिङ्ग्यादाय दुःखार्ता प्रददौ कृत्रिमं पयः ॥ १९॥

English
She had gleaned a few grains which the sweet voiced lady ground into a paste with water. "Come on, so dear," she addressed her son and though she was distressed at heart she gave him the improvised milk. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
पीत्वा च कृत्रिमं क्षीरं मात्रां दत्तं स बालकः ।
नैतत्क्षीरमिति प्राह मातरं चातिविह्वलः ॥ २०॥

English
On drinking that watery stuff offered as milk, the excited boy shouted. 'O mother, this is no milk.' (20.)

---

Sanskrit
दुःखिता सा तदा प्राह सम्प्रेक्ष्याघ्राय मूर्द्धनि ।
सम्मार्ज्य नेत्र पुत्रस्य कराभ्यां कमलायते ॥ २१॥

English
The distressed mother when she saw the boy thus in misery caught hold of him, kissed on his head, wiped his lotus-like eyes with her hand and said:— (21.)

---

Sanskrit
जनन्युवाच ।
तटिनी रत्नपूर्णास्ताः स्वर्गपातालगोचराः ।
भाग्यहीना न पश्यन्ति भक्तिहीनाश्च ये शिवे ॥ २२॥

English
*The mother said:—*  Rivers full of gems whether in heaven or in the nether worlds are never seen by unlucky people and those devoid of devotion to Śiva. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
राज्यं स्वर्गं च मोक्षं च भोजनं क्षीरसम्भवम् ।
न लभन्ते प्रियाण्येषां न तुष्यति यदा शिवः ॥ २३॥

English
If Śiva is not pleased with them, they do not get those pleasing things viz. kingdom, heaven, salvation or a milk diet. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
भवप्रसादजं सर्वं नान्यद्देवप्रसादजम् ।
अन्यदेवेषु निरता दुःखार्ता विभ्रमन्ति च ॥ २४॥

English
Everything is the result of Śiva’s grace and not that of any other lord. Those who are devoted to other lords wander in distress. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
क्षीरं तत्र कुतोऽस्माकं वने निवसतां सदा ।
क्व दुग्धसाधनं वत्स क्व वयं वनवासिनः ॥ २५॥

English
Whence can we have milk, we who stay in the forest for ever? O dear, where the sources of milk and where we the forest-dwellers? (25.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्स्नाभावेन दारिद्र्यान्मया ते भाग्यहीनया ।
मिथ्यादुग्धमिदं दत्तं पिष्टमालोड्य वारिणा ॥ २६॥

English
Wanting everything and overwhelmed by poverty I, your unfortunate mother, have given you this improvised milk grinding the fried grains into paste with water. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
त्वं मातुलगृहे स्वल्पं पीत्वा स्वादु पयः श्रुतम् ।
ज्ञात्वा स्वादु त्वया पीतं तज्जातीयमनुस्मरन् ॥ २७॥

दत्तं न पय इत्युक्त्वा रुदन् दुःखीकरोषि माम् ।
प्रसादेन विना शम्भोः पयस्तव न विद्यते ॥ २८॥

English
You tasted the boiled milk at your uncle’s. So when you drank this ‘sour stuff you found out on comparison that what I gave you was not milk. When you cry out saying so you make me miserable. Without the grace of Lord Śiva you cannot have milk. (27-28.)

---

Sanskrit
पादपङ्कजयोस्तस्य साम्बस्य सगणस्य च ।
भक्त्या समर्पितं यत्तत्कारणं सर्वसम्पदाम् ॥ २९॥

English
What is dedicated with devotion at the lotus-like feet of the lord accompanied by Śivā and his Gaṇas is the cause of all riches. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
अधुना वसुदोऽस्माभिर्महादेवो न पूजितः ।
सकामानां यथाकामं यथोक्तफलदायकः ॥ ३०॥

English
The great lord who is the bestower of wealth and befitting benefits to those who desire them, has not been worshipped by us now. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
धनान्युद्दिश्य नास्माभिरितः प्रागर्चितः शिवः ।
अतो दरिद्राः सञ्जाता वयं तस्मान्न ते पयः ॥ ३१॥

English
Aiming at riches we have not worshipped Śiva hitherto. So we are poor. Hence you do not get milk. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्वजन्मनि यद्दत्तं शिवमुद्दिश्य वै सुतः ।
तदेव लभ्यते नान्यद् विष्णुमुद्दिश्य वा प्रभुम् ॥ ३२॥

English
O son, nothing else is obtained by us except what has been given by us in respect of lord Śiva or Viṣṇu. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
इति मातृवचः श्रुत्वा तथ्यं शोकादिसूचकम् ।
बालोऽप्यनुतपन्नन्तः प्रगल्भमिदमब्रवीत् ॥ ३३॥

English
*Vāyu said:—*  On hearing the words of his mother, true but expressive of her grief, the boy, though distressed within spoke thus in a mature way. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
शोकेनालमितो मतः साम्बो यद्यस्ति शङ्करः ।
त्यज शोकं महाभागे सर्वं भद्रं भविष्यति ॥ ३४॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  O mother, do not feel unhappy ; if there is Śiva and Śivā everything good will result. O blessed lady, eschew your grief. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
श‍ृणु मातर्वचो मेऽद्य महादेवोऽस्ति चेत् क्वचित् ।
चिराद्वा ह्यचिराद्वापि क्षीरोदं साधयाम्यहम् ॥ ३५॥

English
O mother, listen now to my words. If there is lord Śiva anywhere sooner or later I shall get an ocean of milk. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य बालकस्य महामतेः ।
प्रत्युवाच तदा माता सुप्रसन्ना मनस्विनी ॥ ३६॥

English
Vāyu said:—  On hearing the words of her highly intelligent son the delighted noble mother replied. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
मातोवाच ।
शुभं विचारितं तात त्वया मत्प्रीतिवर्द्धनम् ।
विलम्बं मा कथास्त्वं हि भज साम्बं सदाशिवम् ॥ ३७॥

English
The mother said:—  O dear, what you have thought of is auspicious. It increases my pleasure. Do not delay. Worship Sadāśiva and the goddess Śivā. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वस्मादधिकोऽस्त्येव शिवः परमकारणम् ।
तत्कृतं हि जगत्सर्वं ब्रह्माद्यास्तस्य किङ्कराः ॥ ३८॥

English
Definitely there is Śiva superior to all, the great cause. The entire universe in created by him. Brahmā and and others are his servants. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्प्रसादकृतैश्वर्या दासास्तस्य वयं प्रभोः ।
तं विनान्यं न जानीमः शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम् ॥ ३९॥

English
If we have any prosperity it is created by his grace. We are only the slaves of the lord. Except Śiva, the benefactor of the worlds we do not know anyone else. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यान् देवान्न् परित्यज्य कर्मणा मनसा गिरा ।
तमेव साम्बं सगणं भज भावपुरस्सरम् ॥ ४०॥

English
Eschewing other gods physically, mentally and verbally, worship him alone, with Śivā with due devotion. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य देवाधिदेवस्य शिवस्य वरदायिनः ।
साक्षान्नमः शिवायेति मन्त्रोऽयं वाचकः स्मृतः ॥ ४१॥

English
“Namah Śivāya”, this mantra is directly expressive of Śiva, the overlord of the gods, the bestower of boons. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्तकोटिमहामन्त्राः सर्वे सप्रणवाः परे ।
तस्मिन्नेव विलीयन्ते पुनस्तस्माद्विनिर्गताः ॥ ४२॥

English
The seven crores of mantras including the Praṇava Om merge into this mantra and come out again. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
सप्रसादाश्च ते मन्त्राः स्वाधिकाराद्यपेक्षया ।
सर्वाधिकारस्त्वेकोऽयं मन्त्र एवेश्वराज्ञया ॥ ४३॥

English
Those Mantras are also beneficent with regard to those who are authorized therein. At the behest of the lord every one is authorized in this Mantra. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा निकृष्टानुत्कृष्टान्सर्वानप्यात्मनः शिवः ।
क्षमते रक्षितुं तद्वन्मन्त्रोऽयमपि सर्वदा ॥ ४४॥

English
Just as Śiva, this mantra too is capable of protecting all Ātmans base or excellent. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रबलश्च तथा ह्येष मन्त्रो मन्त्रान्तरादपि ।
सर्वरक्षाक्षमोऽप्येष नापरः कश्चिदिष्यते ॥ ४५॥

English
This mantra is stronger than any other mantra. Only this and no other mantra is capable of protecting all. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मान्मन्त्रान्तरांस्त्यक्त्वा पञ्चाक्षरपरो भव ।
तस्मिन् जिह्वान्तरगते न किञ्चिदिह दुर्लभम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Hence eschew all the other Mantras and devote yourself to this five-syllabled mantra. When that is in the mouth nothing is inaccessible here. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
अघोरास्त्रं च शैवानां रक्षाहेतुरनुत्तमम् ।
तच्च तत्प्रभवं मत्वा तत्परो भव नान्यथा ॥ ४७॥

English
The excellent Aghora missile affording protection to the devotees of Śiva originates from this mantra. Considering this, be devoted to it and not otherwise. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
भस्मेदं तु मया लब्धं पितुरेव तवोत्तमम् ।
विरजानलसंसिद्धं महाव्यापन्निवारणम् ॥ ४८॥

English
This Bhasma produced in the Viraja fire was obtained by me from your father. It is excellent and it averts great mishaps. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
मन्त्रं च ते मया दत्तं गृहाण मदनुज्ञया ।
अनेनैवाशु जप्तेन रक्षा तव भविष्यति ॥ ४९॥

English
Accept the Mantra bestowed on you by me. If the Japa of this mantra is performed your protection will be assured. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
एवं मात्रा समादिश्य शिवमस्त्वित्युदीर्य च ।
विसृष्टस्तद्वचो मूर्ध्नि कुर्वन्नेव तदा मुनिः ॥ ५०॥

तां प्रणम्यैवमुक्त्वा च तपः कर्तुं प्रचक्रमे ।

English
*Vāyu said:—*  The mother directed him thus saying, “May it be auspicious” and allowed him to go. The sage accepted her words with his bent head. Bowing to her he made preparations for performing penance. Then the mother said to him, “May the gods do everything auspicious for yo .” (50-51.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुज्ञातस्तया तत्र तपस्तेपे स दुश्चरम् ।
हिमवत्पर्वतं प्राप्य वायुभक्षः समाहितः ॥ ५२॥

English
Permitted by her he went to the mountain Himavat and performed penance with purity of minds. He took in only wind (no food). (52.)

---

Sanskrit
अष्टेष्टकाभिः प्रासादं कृत्वा लिङ्गं च मृण्मयम् ।
तत्रावाह्य महादेवं साम्बं सगणमव्ययम् ॥ ५३॥

भक्त्या पञ्चाक्षरेणैव पुत्रैः पुष्पैर्वनोद्भवैः ।
समभ्यर्च्य चिरं कालं चचार परमं तपः ॥ ५४॥

English
With eight bricks he built an altar and installed Śiva’s phallic image of clay. He invoked the unchanging lord Śiva accompanied by the Gaṇas and Pārvatī. He worshipped him with the leaves and flowers available in the forest repeating the five-syllabled Mantra with devotion. He performed penance for a long time. (53-54.)

Sanskrit
ततस्तपश्चरत्तं तं बालमेकाकिनं कृशम् ।
उपमन्युं द्विजवरं शिवसंसक्तमानसम् ॥ ५५॥

पुरा मरीचिना शप्ताः केचिन्मुनिपिशाचकाः ।
सम्पीड्य राक्षसैर्भावैस्तपसो विघ्नमाचरन् ॥ ५६॥

English
Assuming the forms of Rākṣasa certain ghosts of sages cursed formerly by Marīci harassed the lonely, lean boy Upamanyu performing penance though he was an excellent brahmin devotee of Śiva. Thus they caused obstacles to his penance. (55-56.)

Sanskrit
स च तैः पीड्यमानोऽपि तपः कुर्वन्कथञ्चन ।
सदा नमः शिवायेति क्रोशति स्मार्तनादवत् ॥ ५७॥

English
Though harassed by them he somehow maintained his penance. He uttered “Namaḥ Śivāya” like one in great distress. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्नादश्रवणादेव तपसो विघ्नकारिणः ।
ते तं बालं समुत्सृज्य मुनयः समुपाचरन् ॥ ५८॥

English
At the very hearing of that sound the sages who hindered the penance left off that boy and began to serve him. (58.)

Sanskrit
तपसा तस्य विप्रस्य चोपमन्योर्महात्मनः ।
चराचरं च मुनयः प्रदीपितमभूज्जगत् ॥ ५९॥

English
O sages, the whole universe including the mobile and immobile beings became ignited due to the penance of that brahmin, the noble Upamanyu. (59.)
