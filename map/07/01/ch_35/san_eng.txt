### **CHAPTER THIRTYFIVE - *(The story of Upamanyu)***

---

Sanskrit
३५

वायुरुवाच ।
अथ सर्वे प्रदीप्ताङ्गा वैकुण्ठं प्रययुर्द्रुतम् ।
प्रणम्याहुश्च तत्सर्वं हरये देवसत्तमाः ॥ १॥

English
*Vāyu said:—*  Then the excellent gods with their bodies illuminated hastened to Vaikuṇṭha. After bowing they mentioned everything to Viṣṇu. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रुत्वा तेषां तदा वाक्यं भगवान्पुरुषोत्तमः ।
किमिदं त्विति सञ्चिन्त्य ज्ञात्वा तत्कारणं च सः ॥ २॥

जगाम मन्दरं तूर्णं महेश्वरदिदृक्षया ।
दृष्ट्वा देवं प्रणम्यैवं प्रोवाच सुकृताञ्जलिः ॥ ३॥

English
On hearing them, lord Viṣṇu thought “What is this?” After understanding the reason he immediately went to the Mandara mountain with a desire to see lord Śiva. On seeing the lord and bowing to him with palms joined in reverence he said:— (2-3.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णुरुवाच ।
भगवन्ब्राह्मणः कश्चिदुपमन्युरिति श्रुतः ।
क्षीरार्थमदहत्सर्वं तपसा तन्निवारय ॥ ४॥

English
*Viṣṇu said :—*  O lord, a certain brahmin well known as Upamanyu has burnt everything by his penance for obtaining the milk. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
इति श्रुत्वा वचो विष्णोः प्राह देवो महेश्वरः ।
शिशुं निवारयिष्यामि तत्त्वं गच्छ स्वमाश्रमम् ॥ ५॥

English
*Vāyu said :—*  On hearing the words of Viṣṇu, lord Śiva said:—“I shall prevent the boy. You may please go back to your abodes”. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तच्छ्रुत्वा शम्भुवचनं स विष्णुर्देववल्लभः ।
जगामाश्वास्य तान्सर्वान् स्वलोकममरादिकान् ॥ ६॥

English
On hearing the words of lord Śiva, Viṣṇu the favourite of the gods went to his abode after consoling the gods and others. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे देवः पिनाकी परमेश्वरः ।
शक्रस्य रूपमास्थाय गन्तुं चक्रे मतिं ततः ॥ ७॥

English
In the meantime, the trident-bearing lord Śiva resolved to go there assuming the form of Indra. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ जगाम मुनेस्तु तपोवनं
गजवरेण सितेन सदाशिवः ।
सह सुरासुरसिद्धमहोरगै-
रमरराजतनुं स्वयमास्थितः ॥ ८॥

English
Taking up the physical form as the king of gods, accompanied by gods, Asuras, Siddhas and serpents, and seated on a white elephant Sadāsiva went to the hermitage of the sage. (8.)

---

Sanskrit

निवीज्य वालव्यजनेन दिव्यम् ।
दधार शच्या सहितं सुरेन्द्रं
करेण वामेन सितातपत्रम् ॥ ९॥

English
The elephant fanned the lord with the chowrie and held the white umbrella with the left hand while bearing the lord of gods accompanied by Śaci. (9.)

---

Sanskrit

तेनातपत्रेण यथा चन्द्रबिम्बेन मन्दरः ॥ १०॥

English
Lord Sadāśiva accompanied by Umā and assuming the form of Indra, shone with that umbrella like the Mandara mountain with the disc of the moon. (10.)

---

Sanskrit

जगामानुग्रहं कर्तुमुपमन्योस्तदाश्रमम् ॥ ११॥

English
Assuming the form of Indra, lord Śiva went to the hermitage of Upamanyu in order to bless him. (11.)

---

Sanskrit

प्रणम्य शिरसा प्राह महामुनिवरः स्वयम् ॥ १२॥

English
On seeing lord Śiva, in the form of Indra, the sage bowed his head and spoke. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
पावितश्चाश्रमः सोऽयं मम देवेश्वर स्वयम् ।
प्राप्तो यत्त्वं जगन्नाथ भगवन्देवसत्तम ॥ १३॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  O lord of gods, O lord of the universe, O excellent god, my hermitage is sanctified since you have come here. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
एवमुक्त्वा स्थितं प्रेक्ष्य कृताञ्जलिपुटं द्विजम् ।
प्राह गम्भीरया वाचा शक्ररूपधरो हरः ॥ १४॥

English
Vāyu said :—  Glancing at the brahmin who stood steady after saying thus, with palms joined in reverence, Śiva in the guise of Indra spoke majestically. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
शक्र उवाच ।
तुष्टोऽस्मि ते वरं ब्रूहि तपसाऽनेन सुव्रत ।
ददामि चेप्सितान्सर्वान् धौम्याग्रज महामुने ॥ १५॥

English
Indra said :—  O sage of good rites, O elder brother of Dhaumya, I am delighted with your penance. Mention the boon you wish to have. I shall grant you whatever you desire. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
एवमुक्तस्तदा तेन शक्रेण मुनिपुङ्गवः ।
वरयामि शिवे भक्तिमित्युवाच कृताञ्जलिः ॥ १६॥

English
Vāyu said :—  The leading sage when urged thus by Indra spoke with palms joined in reverence—‘I request you to grant me the boon of devotion to Śiva’. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
तन्निशम्य हरिः प्राह मां न जानासि लेखपम् ।
त्रैलोक्याधिपतिं शक्रं सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ॥ १७॥

मद्भक्तो भव विप्रर्षे मामेवार्चय सर्वदा ।
ददामि सर्वं भद्रं ते त्यज रुद्रं च निर्गुणम् ॥ १८॥

रुद्रेण निर्गुणेनापि किं ते कार्यं भविष्यति ।
देवपङ्क्तिबहिर्भूतो यः पिशाचत्वमागतः ॥ १९॥

English
On hearing that Indra said:—“you do not know me the lord of gods the overlord of the three worlds, Indra, saluted by the gods. O brahminical sage, be my devotee. Always worship only me. Welfare be to you. I shall give you everything. Abandon Rudra who is devoid of attributes. What purpose of yours can be served by, Rudra who without attributes has become a ghost ostracised from the midst of the gods.” (17-19.)

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।

मन्यमानो धर्मविघ्नं प्राह तं कर्तुमागतम् ॥ २०॥

English
Vāyu said :—  On hearing that, the sage began to repeat the five-syllabled Mantra. He thought that Indra had come there to put obstacles in his holy rites and so said. (20.)

---

Sanskrit

English
*Upamanyu said :—*  You have said all these things in your eagerness to disparage Śiva. In that context you have mentioned the Nirguṇatva of the great lord. (21.)

---

Sanskrit

English
You do not know Rudra the lord of the chiefs of the gods, the progenitor of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva and who is greater than Prakṛti. (22.)

---

Sanskrit

English
I desire to get my boon granted by the lord who is separate from Sat and Asat, who is the unmanifest, as mentioned by the propounders of Brahman and who is eternal, single and multifarious (23.)

---

Sanskrit

English
I desire to secure the boon from that lord whom the knowers of truth meditate upon, who bestows the goal of Sāṃkhya and Yoga, viz. release. (24.)

---

Sanskrit

English
There is no higher truth than Śiva who is the cause of all causes, the creator of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and other gods and who is the lord beyond attributes. (25.)

Sanskrit

English

Sanskrit

Sanskrit
सोऽपि लब्ध्वा वरान्दिव्यान् कुमारत्वं च सर्वदा ।
तस्माच्छिवाच्च तस्याश्च शिवाया मुदितोऽभवत् ॥ ५३॥

English
Obtaining the divine boons and the perpetual bachelorhood from Śivā and Śiva he became joyous. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रसन्नचेतस्कः सुप्रणम्य कृताञ्जलिः ।
ययाचे स वरं विप्रो देवदेवान्महेश्वरात् ॥ ५४॥

English
Delighted in his mind he bowed with palms joined in reverence. The brahmin then requested lord Śiva for a boon and said. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
प्रसीद देवदेवेश प्रसीद परमेश्वर ।
स्वभक्तिं देहि परमां दिव्यामव्यभिचारिणीम् ॥ ५५॥

English
“O lord of the chiefs of the gods, be pleased. Please grant me devotion to you, great, divine and unflinching. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रद्धां देहि महादेव स्वसम्बन्धिषु मे सदा ।
स्वदास्यं परमं स्नेहं सान्निध्यं चैव सर्वदा ॥ ५६॥

English
O great lord, grant me an abiding faith in persons devoted to you. Grant me the great slavery and the perpetual proximity to you. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
मुक्त्वा प्रसन्नात्मा हर्षगद्गदया गिरा ।
स तुष्टाव महादेवमुपमन्युर्द्विजोत्तमः ॥ ५७॥

English
After saying this, Upamanyu the excellent brahmin eulogised the lord in the words choked with delight. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
देवदेव महादेव शरणागतवत्सल ।
प्रसीद करुणासिन्धो साम्ब शङ्कर सर्वदा ॥ ५८॥

English
“O great lord, O lord of gods, favourably disposed to those who seek refuge in you. O ocean of mercy, be pleased always, O Śiva, accompanied by Pārvatī. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
एवमुक्तो महादेवः सर्वेषां च वरप्रदः ।
प्रत्युवाच प्रसन्नात्मोपमन्युं मुनिसत्तमम् ॥ ५९॥

English
Vāyu said:—  Thus requested lord Śiva, the granter of boons replied with delighted mind to Upamanyu the excellent sage. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
शिव उवाच ।
वत्सोपमन्यो तुष्टोऽस्मि सर्वं दत्तं मया हि ते ।
दृढभक्तोऽसि विप्रर्षे मया विज्ञासितो ह्यसि ॥ ६०॥

English
Śiva said:—  O dear Upamanyu, I am delighted. Indeed everything has been granted by me to you. O brahminical sage, you are of steady devotion. I wanted to test you. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
अजरश्चामरश्चैव भव त्वं दुःखवर्जितः ।
यशस्वी तेजसा युक्तो दिव्यज्ञानसमन्वितः ॥ ६१॥

English
Be free from old age, death and misery. Be glorious and endowed with splendour and divine knowledge. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
अक्षया बान्धवाश्चैव कुलं गोत्रं च ते सदा ।
भविष्यति द्विजश्रेष्ठ मयि भक्तिश्च शाश्वती ॥ ६२॥

English
Your kinsmen, your family and spiritual lineage shall be everlasting. O excellent brahmin, your devotion to me shall be permanent. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
सान्निध्यं चाश्रमे नित्यं करिष्यामि द्विजोत्तम ।
उपकण्ठं मम त्वं वै सानन्दं विहरिष्यसि ॥ ६३॥

English
O excellent brahmin, I shall always be present in your hermitage. Near me you shall always blissfully sport about. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमुक्त्वा स भगवान् सूर्यकोटिसमप्रभः ।
ईशानः स वरान्दत्त्वा तत्रैवान्तर्दधे हरः ॥ ६४॥

English
Thus granting him the boons lord Siva who had the lustre of a crore suns, vanished there itself. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युः प्रसन्नात्मा प्राप्य तस्माद्वराद्वरान् ।
जगाम जननीस्थानं सुखं प्रापाधिकं च सः ॥ ६५॥

English
Securing the boons from the lord, Upamanyu, delighted in his mind, went to his mother's abode and attained the supreme bliss. (65.)
