### **CHAPTER TWO - (The glory of lord Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
२

ऋषय ऊचुः ।
किं तत्पाशुपतं ज्ञानं कथं पशुपतिः शिवः ।
कथं धौम्याग्रजः पृष्टः कृष्णेनाक्लिष्टकर्मणा ॥ १॥

English
The sages said:—  What is the Pāśupata knowledge? How is Śiva the lord of Paśus? Why was Dhaumya's elder brother asked by Kṛṣṇa of unstrained activities. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
एतत्सर्वं समाचक्ष्व वायो शङ्करविग्रह ।
त्वत्समो न हि वक्तास्ति त्रैलोक्येष्वपरः प्रभुः ॥ २॥

English
O lord Vāyu, of the form of Śiva, please explain all these things. In the whole of the three worlds there is no other person competent to explain it like you. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत उवाच ।
इत्याकर्ण्य वचस्तेषां महर्षीणां प्रभञ्जनः ।
संस्मृत्य शिवमीशानं प्रवक्तुमुपचक्रमे ॥ ३॥

English
Sūta said:—  On hearing the words of the sages, Vāyu thought upon Śiva, and began to explain. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
वायुरुवाच ।
पुरा साक्षात् महेशेन श्रीकण्ठाख्येन मन्दरे ।
देव्यै देवेन कथितं ज्ञानं पाशुपतं परम् ॥ ४॥

English
Vāyu said:—  This excellent and exalted Pāśupata knowledge was mentioned to the goddess by lord Śiva on the mountain Mandara. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तदेव पृष्टं कृष्णेन विष्णुना विश्वयोनिना ।
पशुत्वं च सुरादीनां पतित्वं च शिवस्य च ॥ ५॥

English
The same question was asked by Kṛṣṇa, the cause of the universe about the animal nature of the gods and others and the overlordship of Śiva. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
यथोपदिष्टं कृष्णाय मुनिना ह्युपमन्युना ।
तथा समासतो वक्ष्ये तच्छृणुध्वमतन्द्रिताः ॥ ६॥

English
I shall explain it succinctly as imparted to Kṛṣṇa by Upamanyu. Please hear attentively. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
पुरोपमन्युमासीनं विष्णुःकृष्णवपुर्धरः ।
प्रणिपत्य यथान्यायमिदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥ ७॥

English
Formerly Viṣṇu in the form of Kṛṣṇa bowed to Upamanyu sitting in his hermitage and spoke courteously. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्ण उवाच ।
भगवञ्छ्रोतुमिच्छामि देव्यै देवेन भाषितम् ।
दिव्यं पाशुपतं ज्ञानं विभूतिं वास्य कृत्स्नशः ॥ ८॥

English
*Lord Kṛṣṇa said:—*  O lord, I wish to hear about the divine Pāśupata knowledge as mentioned to the goddess by the lord. I wish to hear about his prosperity and glory entirely. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
कथं पशुपतिर्देवः पशवः के प्रकीर्तिताः ।
कैः पाशैस्ते निबध्यन्ते विमुच्यन्ते च ते कथम् ॥ ९॥

English
How is the lord known as Paśupati, lord of Paśus? Who are the Paśus? What are those Pāśas (strings) with which they are bound? How are they released? (9.)

---

Sanskrit
इति सञ्चोदितः श्रीमानुपमन्युर्महात्मना ।
प्रणम्य देवं देवीं च प्राह पुष्टो यथा तथा ॥ १०॥

English
Thus implored by the noble Kṛṣṇa the glorious Upamanyu bowed to the lord and the goddess and spoke as follows. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
ब्रह्माद्याः स्थावरान्ताश्च देवदेवस्य शूलिनः ।
पशवः परिकीर्त्यन्ते संसारवशवर्तिनः ॥ ११॥

English
*Upamanyu said:—*  Every being beginning with Brahmā and ending with immobile beings is called Paśu in relation to the lord of gods. Every being is subject to worldly existence. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तेषां पतित्वाद्देवेशः शिवः पशुपतिः स्मृतः ।
मलमायादिभिः पाशैः स बध्नाति पशून् पतिः ॥ १२॥

English
Śiva, lord of the gods, is known as Paśupati in view of his being the lord of the Paśus. The Pati binds the Paśus through the Pāśas (strings) of dirt. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
स एव मोचकस्तेषां भक्त्या सम्यगुपासितः ।
चतुर्विंशतितत्त्वानि मायाकर्मगुणा अमी ॥ १३॥

विषया इति कथ्यन्ते पाशा जीवनिबन्धनाः ।
ब्रह्मादिस्तम्बपर्यन्तान् पशून् बद्ध्वा महेश्वरः ॥ १४॥

English
He alone releases when he is meditated upon and worshipped with devotion. The twentyfour principles, Māyā, Karman and the three Guṇas are called Viṣayas. These are the Pāśas that bind the Paśus. After binding the Paśus, from Brahmā to the grass, by means of these Pāśas, lord Śiva makes them do their respective duties. (13-14.)

Sanskrit
पाशैरेतैः पतिर्देवः कार्यं कारयति स्वकम् ।
तस्याज्ञया महेशस्य प्रकृतिः पुरुषोचिताम् ॥ १५॥

बुद्धिं प्रसूते सा बुद्धिरहङ्कारमहङ्कृतिः ।
इन्द्रियाणि दशैकं च तन्मात्रापञ्चकं तथा ॥ १६॥

शासनाद्देवदेवस्य शिवस्य शिवदायिनः ।
तन्मात्राण्यपि तस्यैव शासनेन महीयसा ॥ १७॥

महाभूतान्यशेषाणि भावयन्त्यनुपूर्वशः ।
ब्रह्मादीनां तृणान्तानां देहिनां देहसङ्गतिम् ॥ १८॥

महाभूतान्यशेषाणि जनयन्ति शिवाज्ञया ।
अध्यवस्यति वै बुद्धिरहङ्कारोभिमन्यते ॥ १९॥

चित्तं चेतयते चापि मनः सङ्कल्पयत्यपि ।
श्रोत्रादीनि च गृह्णन्ति शब्दादीन्विषयान् पृथक् ॥ २०॥

English
At his behest Prakṛti generates Buddhi befitting the Puruṣa. Buddhi (cosmic intellect) generates Ahaṅkāra (Ego). The ego generates the eleven sense-organs and five Tanmātras. At his bidding the Tanmātras too, create the great Bhūtas entirely in their order; the great Bhūtas create the physical bodies of all beings from Brahmā to the grass. Intellect determines and resolves. Ego feels pride in and identifies with what is possessed. Consciousness observes and becomes conscious of things. Mind conceives and imagines. The organs of knowledge apprehend the objects separately. (15-20.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वानेव नान्यान् देवस्य दिव्येनाज्ञाबलेन वै ।
वागादीन्यपि यान्यासंस्तानि कर्मेन्द्रियाणि च ॥ २१॥

यथास्वं कर्म कुर्वन्ति नान्यत्किञ्चिद् शिवाज्ञया ।
शब्दादयोऽपि गृह्यन्ते क्रियन्ते वचनादयः ॥ २२॥

English
The sense-organs grasp their objects and not others and this is due to the divine command of the lord. The organs of activity too do their own duties. Nothing else. It is due to Śiva's order that sound, etc. are grasped, speech, etc. are made. Śiva's weighty command is not to be transgressed by any. (21-22.)

Sanskrit
अविलङ्घ्या हि सर्वेषामाज्ञा शम्भोर्गरीयसी ।
अवकाशमशेषाणां भूतानां सम्प्रयच्छति ॥ २३॥

आकाशः परमेशस्य शासनादेव सर्वगः ।
प्राणाद्यैश्च तथा नामभेदैरन्तर्बहिर्जगत् ॥ २४॥

बिभर्ति सर्वं शर्वस्य शासनेन प्रभञ्जनः ।
हव्यं वहति देवानां कव्यं कव्याशिनामपि ॥ २५॥

पाकाद्यं च करोत्यग्निः परमेश्वरशासनात् ।
सञ्जीवनाद्यं सर्वस्य कुर्वत्यापस्तदाज्ञया ॥ २६॥

English
It is only at the behest of the lord that ether pervades all, yields space to the elements. At the bidding of Śiva the wind sustains the entire universe, internal and external, under different names of Prāṇa, etc. At the bidding of the lord the firegod bears offerings to the gods and oblations to the Pīṛs. It facilitates cooking etc. At his behest the waters enliven all. At the bidding of the lord the earth holds up the universe for ever. (23-26.)

Sanskrit
विश्वम्भरा जगन्नित्यं धत्ते विश्वेश्वराज्ञया ।
देवान् यात्यसुरान् हन्ति त्रिलोकमभिरक्षति ॥ २७॥

आज्ञया तस्य देवेन्द्रः सर्वैर्देवैरलङ्घ्यया ।
आधिपत्यमपां नित्यं कुरुते वरुणः सदा ॥ २८॥

पाशैर्बध्नाति च यथा दण्ड्यांस्तस्यैव शासनात् ।
ददाति नित्यं यक्षेन्द्रो द्रविणं द्रविणेश्वरः ॥ २९॥

पुण्यानुरूपं भूतेभ्यः पुरुषस्यानुशासनात् ।
करोति सम्पदः शश्वत् ज्ञानं चापि सुमेधसाम् ॥ ३०॥

English
In respect of Śiva's inviolable command lord Indra protects the gods, kills the Asuras and guards the worlds. By Śiva's command lord Varuṇa rules over the waters and binds those who are to be punished, by means of his noose. At the behest of Śiva the lord of wealth, the lord of the Yakṣas, distributes wealth to the living beings in accordance with their merit. At the bidding of Śiva, Īśāna bestows knowledge on the intelligent ones and affords them riches, and curbs the evil-doers. (27-30.)

Sanskrit
निग्रहं चाप्यसाधूनामीशानः शिवशासनात् ।
धत्ते तु धरणीं मूर्ध्ना शेषः शिवनियोगतः ॥ ३१॥

यामाहुस्तामसीं रौद्रीं मूर्तिमन्तकरीं हरेः ।

अन्याभिर्मूर्तिभिः स्वाभिः पाति चान्ते निहन्ति च ।
विष्णुः पालयते विश्वं कालकालस्य शासनात् ॥ ३३॥

सृजते त्रसते चापि स्वकाभिस्तनुभिस्त्रिभिः ।

English

At his behest, through other forms of his own, he protects the universe and annihilates it in the end. He protects, creates and devours the universe through his own three bodies. At his bidding alone Rudra annihilates the universe in the end. (31-34.)

Sanskrit

At his bidding with the three parts of his splendour, the sun supports the universe, commands the shower and rains in the heaven. At the behest of the moon-crested lord, the moon nourishes the plants, delights the living beings and is imbibed by the gods. (35-37.)

(38-43.)

तदन्तरे हैमवती देवी दिव्यविभूषणा ।
आविरासीन्नभोरङ्गे शोभमाना शुचिस्मिता ॥ ५६॥

English
In the meantime the goddess Haimavatī bedecked in divine ornaments, appeared in the sky smiling and shining brilliantly. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
तां दृष्ट्वा विस्मयाविष्टा देवाः शक्रपुरोगमाः ।
प्रणम्य यक्षं पप्रच्छुः कोऽसौ यक्षो विलक्षणः ॥ ५७॥

English
On seeing her the wonderstruck gods, Indra and others bowed to her humbly and asked, "Who is this uncommon Yakṣa?" (57.)

---

Sanskrit
साऽब्रवीत्सस्मितं देवी स युष्माकमगोचरः ।
तेनेदं भ्रम्यते चक्रं संसाराख्यं चराचरम् ॥ ५८॥

English
The goddess smiled and said—"He is invisible to you. He is the lord by whom this wheel of the world including the mobile and immobile beings, revolves. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
तेनादौ क्रियते विश्वं तेन संह्रियते पुनः ।
न तन्नियन्ता कश्चित् स्यात्तेन सर्वं नियम्यते ॥ ५९॥

English
In the beginning the universe is created by him, and it is annihilated again by him. There is none to control him. Everything is controlled by him". (59.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्युक्त्वा सा महादेवी तत्रैवान्तरधत्त वै ।
देवाश्च विस्मिताः सर्वे तां प्रणम्य दिवं ययुः ॥ ६०॥

English
After saying this the great goddess vanished there itself. The surprised gods bowed to her and went to heaven. (60.)
