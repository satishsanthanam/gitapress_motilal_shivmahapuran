### **CHAPTER SIX - (The Principle of Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
६

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
न शिवस्याणवो बन्धः कार्यो मायेय एव वा ।
प्राकृतो वाथ बोद्धा वा ह्यहङ्कारात्मकस्तथा ॥ १॥

नैवास्य मानसो बन्धो न चैत्तो नेन्द्रियात्मकः ।
न च तन्मात्रबन्धोऽपि भूतबन्धो न कश्चन ॥ २॥

English
Upamanyu said:—  Siva has no bondage due to the atoms, effects, Māyā, Prakrti, cosmic intellect, ego, mind, sens-eorgans, Tanmātras or the elements. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
न च कालः कला चैव नाविद्या नियतिस्तथा ।
न रागो न च विद्वेषः शम्भोरमिततेजसः ॥ ३॥

English
To Siva of unmeasured brilliance there is no Kāla or Kalā; no Vidyā or Niyati; neither lust nor hatred. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
न चास्त्यभिनिवेशोऽस्य कुशलाकुशलान्यपि ।
कर्माणि तद्विपाकश्च सुखदुःखे च तत्फले ॥ ४॥

English
He has no keen desire. He has neither happiness nor unhappiness; neither Karmans nor their after-effects; neither, pleasure nor misery as a result of those Karmans. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
आशयैर्नापि सम्बन्धः संस्कारैः कर्मणामपि ।
भोगैश्च भोगसंस्कारैः कालत्रितयगोचरैः ॥ ५॥

English
He has no connection with the seats of feelings nor with the impressions of actions. He has no contact with enjoyments of pleasures nor with their impressions of the three units of time—past, present, future (5.)

---

Sanskrit
न तस्य कारणं कर्ता नादिरन्तस्तथान्तरम् ।
न कर्म करणं वापि नाकार्यं कार्यमेव च ॥ ६॥

English
He has no cause or maker; he has no beginning no end nor the intervening space. He has no activity or instrument. He has neither Akārya nor Kārya. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्य बन्धुरबन्धुर्वा नियन्ता प्रेरकोऽपि वा ।
न पतिर्न गुरुस्त्राता नाधिको न समस्तथा ॥ ७॥

English
He has neither kinsman nor non-kinsman; he has no one to check or to urge. He has no lord, preceptor or protector. He has no superior or equal. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
न जन्ममरणे तस्य न काङ्क्षितमकाङ्क्षितम् ।
न विधिर्न निषेधश्च न मुक्तिर्न च बन्धनम् ॥ ८॥

English
He has neither birth nor death; neither expectation nor disinclination. He is not subject to injunctions or prohibitions; he has neither liberation nor bondage. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्ति यद्यदकल्याणं तत्तदस्य कदाचन ।
कल्याणं सकलं चास्ति परमात्मा शिवो यतः ॥ ९॥

English
He never has anything not conducive to welfare or inauspicious. He has everything auspicious since Siva is the great Atman. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
स शिवः सर्वमेवेदमधिष्ठाय स्वशक्तिभिः ।
अप्रच्युतः स्वतो भावः स्थितः स्थाणुरतः स्मृतः ॥ १०॥

English
Presiding over everything by means of his Saktis, he is stationed without dropping his intrinsic nature. Hence he is known as Siva. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेनाधिष्ठितं यस्मात् जगत्स्थावरजङ्गमम् ।
सर्वरूपः स्मृतः शर्वस्तथा ज्ञात्वा न मुह्यति ॥ ११॥

English
Since the universe consisting of the mobile and immobile beings is presided over by Siva, he is known as omniformed. One who knows him as such, is never deluded. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
शर्वो रुद्रो नमस्तस्मै पुरुषः सत्परो महान् ।
हिरण्यबाहुर्भगवान् हिरण्यपतिरीश्वरः ॥ १२॥

English
Siva is Rudra. Obeisance to him. He is the great Puruṣa beyond the Sat. He has golden arms, he is the lord of gold. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अम्बिकापतिरीशानः पिनाकी वृषवाहनः ।
एको रुद्रः परं ब्रह्म पुरुषः कृष्णपिङ्गलः ॥ १३॥

English
Isana is the consort of Siva. He is the trident-bearing, and bull-vehicled lord. The sole Rudra is the great Brahman. He is the black and tawny Puruṣa. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
बालाग्रमात्रो हृन्मध्ये विचिन्त्यो दहरान्तरे ।
हिरण्यकेशः पद्माक्षो ह्यरुणस्ताम्र एव च ॥ १४॥

English
He shall be meditated upon in the cavity within the heart as minute as the tip of a hair, with golden hair, and lotus eyes. He is pink and copper-coloured. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
योऽवसर्पत्यसौ देवो नीलग्रीवो हिरण्मयः ।
सौम्योऽघोरस्तथा मिश्रश्चाक्षारश्चामृतोऽव्ययः ॥ १५॥

स पुंविशेषः परमो भगवानन्तकान्तकः ।
चेतनाचेतनोन्मुक्तः प्रपञ्चाच्च परात्परः ॥ १६॥

English
He moves about golden in colour, blue-necked, both gentle and terrible and a mixture of the both, imperishable, deathless and unchangeable. Such is the great lord, the slayer of the god of death. He is liberated from the sentient and non-sentient. He is greater than the great universe. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेनातिशयत्वेन ज्ञानैश्वर्ये विलोकिते ।
लोकेशातिशयत्वेन स्थितं प्राहुर्मनीषिणः ॥ १७॥

English
Since the knowledge and supremacy of Siva excels those of others he excels all the lords of the worlds so say the intelligent persons. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रतिसर्गप्रसूतानां ब्रह्मणां शास्त्रविस्तरम् ।
उपदेष्टा स एवादौ कालावच्छेदवर्तिनाम् ॥ १८॥

English
In the beginning he was the instructor of the scriptural texts to the Brahmans born during the period of re-creation. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
कालावच्छेदयुक्तानां गुरूणामप्यसौ गुरुः ।
सर्वेषामेव सर्वेशः कालावच्छेदवर्जितः ॥ १९॥

English
He is not conditioned by time. He is the lord of all. He is the preceptor of all the preceptors, subject to the influence of Time. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धा स्वाभाविकी तस्य शक्तिः सर्वातिशायिनी ।
ज्ञानमप्रतिमं नित्यं वपुरत्यन्तनिर्मिलम् ॥ २०॥

English
His Sakti is pure, natural and all-excelling. His knowledge is unparalleled. His physical body is eternal and built to defy death. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
ऐश्वर्यमप्रतिद्वन्द्वं सुखमात्यन्तिकं बलम् ।
तेजःप्रभावो वीर्यं च क्षमा कारुण्यमेव च ॥ २१॥

English
His lordship is unrivalled, so also his happiness, undying strength, power of brilliance, virility, forbearance and mercifulness. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
परिपूर्णस्य सर्गाद्यैर्नात्मनोऽस्ति प्रयोजनम् ।
परानुग्रह एवास्य फलं सर्वस्य कर्मणः ॥ २२॥

English
Since he is full and perfect he has no selfish end to be served by creation etc. The fruit of his activities is only the blessing of others. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रणवो वाचकस्तस्य शिवस्य परमात्मनः ।
शिवरुद्रादिशब्दानां प्रणवो हि परः स्मृतः ॥ २३॥

English
Praṇava is the word expressive of lord Śiva. Praṇava is the greatest symbol of Śiva, Rudra and other words. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
शम्भोः प्रणववाच्यस्य भवनात्तज्जपादपि ।
या सिद्धिः सा परा प्राप्या भवत्येव न संशयः ॥ २४॥

English
Undoubtedly the great Siddhi can be acquired by meditation on Praṇava that is expressive of Śiva and by the performance of its Japa. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मादेकाक्षरं देवमाहुरागमपारगाः ।
वाच्यवाचकयोरैक्यं मन्यमाना मनस्विनः ॥ २५॥

English
Hence intelligent men well-versed in the Āgamas considering identity between the word and its meaning call the lord as single-syllabled. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अस्य मात्राः समाख्याताश्चतस्रो वेदमूर्द्धनि ।
अकारश्चाप्युकारश्च मकारो नाद इत्यपि ॥ २६॥

English
In the Upaniṣads its Mātrās are four A,U, M and nāda. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
अकारं बह्वृचं प्राहुरुकारो यजुरुच्यते ।
मकारः सामनादोऽस्य श्रुतिराथर्वणी स्मृता ॥ २७॥

English
The letter “A” is the Ṛgveda; “U” is the Yajurveda; “M” is the Sāmaveda and the Nāda is the Atharvaveda. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
अकारश्च महाबीजं रजः स्रष्टा चतुर्मुखः ।
उकारः प्रकृतिर्योनिः सत्त्वं पालयिता हरिः ॥ २८॥

English
The letter “A” is the great Bija, Rajas and the creator, the four-faced lord. The letter “U” is Prakṛti, the womb, Sattva and the protector Viṣṇu. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
मकारः पुरुषो बीजं तमः संहारको हरः ।
नादः परः पुमानीशो निर्गुणो निष्क्रियः शिवः ॥ २९॥

English
The letter “M” is Puruṣa, the seed, the Tamas and the annihilator Rudra. Nāda is the great Puruṣa, Iśa, Śiva, devoid of Guṇas and activities. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वं तिसृभिरेवेदं मात्राभिर्निखिलं त्रिधा ।
अभिधाय शिवात्मानं बोधयत्यर्धमात्रया ॥ ३०॥

English
Praṇava expresses everything through the three Mātrās in three ways and indicates Śivātman through half a Mātrā. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मात्परं नापरमस्ति किञ्चिद्-
यस्मान्नाणीयो न ज्यायोऽस्ति किञ्चित् ।
वृक्ष इव स्तब्धो दिवि तिष्ठत्येक-
स्तेनेदं पूर्णं पुरुषेण सर्वम् ॥ ३१॥

English
All this is filled by that Puruṣa than whom there is nothing else greater; than whom there is nothing smaller nor bigger and who like a tree stands steady and stiff in the heaven.²⁴⁸ (31.)
