### **CHAPTER TEN - (Devotion to Śiva)**

---

Sanskrit
१०

कृष्ण उवाच ।
भगवन् सर्वयोगीन्द्र गणेश्वर मुनीश्वर ।
षडाननसमप्रख्य सर्वज्ञाननिधे गुरो ॥ १॥

प्रायस्त्वमवतीर्योर्व्यां पाशविच्छित्तये नृणाम् ।
महर्षिवपुरास्थाय स्थितोऽसि परमेश्वर ॥ २॥

English
Śrī Kṛṣṇa said:—  O lord, foremost of all Yogins, O leader of the Ganas, O excellent sage, O preceptor having the lustre equal to that of the six-faced deity, O storehouse of knowledge, you have assumed the form of a sage but are really the supreme lord. You have incarnated on the earth for breaking the binding cords of men. (1-2.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्यथा हि जगत्यस्मिन् देवो वा दानवोऽपि वा ।
त्वत्तोऽन्यः परमं भावं को जानीयात् शिवात्मकम् ॥ ३॥

English
There is none else but you, among the gods and Danavas in this universe who knows the true nature of Siva. (3.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Hence my mind is not satiated by drinking in the nectarine lore of the lord coming out of your mouth as if from that of the trident-bearing lord himself. (4.)

Sanskrit

भगवन्किन्नु पप्रच्छ भर्तारं परमेश्वरी ॥ ५॥

English
O holy lord, what was it that the goddess seated in the lap of her husband, the creator of the universe, had asked him? (5.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।

भवभक्तस्य युक्तस्य तव कल्याणचेतसः ॥ ६॥

English
*Upamanyu said:—*  You have very pertinently put the question O Kṛṣṇa. I shall explain it precisely to you of auspicious mind. You as the devotee of Śiva are the person fit to hear it. (6.)

Sanskrit

English
Accompanied by the goddess, the lord went to his beautiful garden on the beautiful mountain Mandara abounding in beautiful caves. (7.)

Sanskrit

English
Then Śubhāvatī, the beloved friend of the goddess with a smiling face, brought many full-blown flowers of great beauty. (8.)

Sanskrit

English
Thereupon Śiva, the chief of the gods, seated the goddess on his lap and bedecked her with the flowers. He was much pleased with himself. (9.)

Sanskrit

English
The gentle ladies bedecked in glittering ornaments working in the harem and the confidential attendants, the chiefs of Gaṇas served the goddess and the lord, with chowries in their hands. (10-11.)

Sanskrit

त्राणाय च नृणां लोके ये शिवं शरणं गताः ॥ १२॥

English
Then pleasing discourses ensued for the diversion of the lord and the goddess and for the succour of the devotees who sought refuge in him. (12.)

Sanskrit

English
Then seeing an opportune moment the goddess asked Śiva, the lord of the worlds. (13.)

Sanskrit
देव्युवाच ।
केन वश्यो महादेवो मर्त्यानां मन्दचेतसाम् ।
आत्मतत्त्वाद्यशक्तानामात्मनामकृतात्मनाम् ॥ १४॥

English
*The goddess said:—*  How can the lord be brought under control by slow-witted men who are not interested in spiritual principles and who do not have self-control? (14.)

---

Sanskrit
ईश्वर उवाच ।
न कर्मणा न तपसा न जपैर्नासनादिभिः ।
न ज्ञानेन न चान्येन वश्योऽहं श्रद्धया विना ॥ १५॥

English
*Lord Śiva said:—*  I cannot be enticed either by holy rites or penance or Japas or postures or knowledge or by any other means except faith.$^{253}$ (15.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रद्धा मय्यस्ति चेत्पुंसां येन केनापि हेतुना ।
वश्यः स्पृश्यश्च दृश्यश्च पूज्यस्सम्भाष्य एव च ॥ १६॥

English
If men have faith in me I can be brought under control, be touched, seen, worshipped and spoken to by some means or other. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
साध्या तस्मान् मयि शद्धा मां वशीकर्तुमिच्छता ।
श्रद्धा हेतुः स्वधर्मस्य रक्षणं वर्णिनामिह ॥ १७॥

स्ववर्णाश्रमधर्मेऽथ वर्तते यस्तु मानवः ।
तस्यैव भवति श्रद्धा मयि नान्यस्य कस्यचित् ॥ १८॥

English
Hence faith shall be sought and acquired by him who desires to subject me to his control. Faith supports the duties of the different castes and is generated in him alone who abides by all duties of the castes and stages of life. No one else has faith in me. (17-18.)

---

Sanskrit
आम्नायसिद्धमखिलं धर्ममाश्रमिणामिह ।
ब्रह्मणा कथितं पूर्वं ममैवाज्ञापुरः सरम् ॥ १९॥

English
At my bidding, the duties as derived from the Vedas of the various stages in life were mentioned formerly by Brahmā. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
स तु पैतामहो धर्मो बहुवित्तक्रियान्वितः ।
नात्यन्तफलभूयिष्ठः क्लेशायाससमन्वितः ।
तेन धर्मेण महता श्रद्धां प्राप्य सुदुर्ल्लभाम् ॥ २०॥

वर्णिनो ये प्रपद्यन्ते मामनन्यसमाश्रयाः ।
तेषां सुखेन मार्गेण धर्मकामार्थमुक्तयः ॥ २१॥

English
That compendium of duties is called Brahmā's Dharma. It consists of expensive rites, and is attended with great stress and strain. The fruits are not comparatively plentiful. By resorting to this great Dharma it is possible for those persons of different castes who resort to me without seeking the support of others, and who have attained the rare faith, to attain virtue, love, wealth and salvation. (20-21.)

Sanskrit
वर्णाश्रमसमाचारो मया भूयः प्रकल्पितः ।
तस्मिन् भक्तिमतामेव मदीयानां तु वर्णिनाम् ॥ २२॥

अधिकारो न चान्येषामित्याज्ञा नैष्ठिकी मम ।
तदाज्ञप्तेन मार्गेण वर्णिनो मदुपाश्रयाः ॥ २३॥

मलमायादिपाशेभ्यो विमुक्ता मत्प्रसादतः ।
परं मदीयमासाद्य पुनरावृत्तिदुर्लभम् ।
परमं मम साधर्म्यं प्राप्य निर्वृतिमाययुः ॥ २४॥

English
The duties of various castes and stages of life have been re-organised by me. This is my permanent injunction that only those who have devotion to me are authorised in those duties and not others. Hence those who resort to me along the path directed, become liberated from the binding cords of dirt, Māyā, etc. due to my grace. They attain my city²⁵⁴ whence there is no return. Then they become identical with me and secure bliss. (22-24.)

Sanskrit
तस्मात् लब्ध्वाप्यलब्ध्वा वा वर्णधर्मं मयेरितम् ।
आश्रित्य मम भक्तश्चेत्स्वात्मनात्मानमुद्धरेत् ॥ २५॥

अलब्धलाभ एवैष कोटिकोटिगुणाधिकः ।
तस्मान्मे मुखतो लब्धं वर्णधर्मं समाचरेत् ॥ २६॥

English
Hence, after securing or not securing the duties mentioned by me if my devotee lifts himself up by his efforts, it is an acquisition of what is not acquired superior in qualities crores and crores of times. Hence, one shall practise the duties mentioned by me. (25-26.)

Sanskrit

सर्वान्तरेषु सन्त्यार्ये सन्ततिश्च सहस्रशः ॥ २७॥

English
O noble lady, these are my incarnations as Yogācārya²⁵⁵ in all the Manvantaras and thousands of generations. (27.)

---

Sanskrit

English
O goddess, the knowledge of generations is inaccessible to those who are not my devotees, who are devoid of intellect and who do not practise Yoga. Hence one shall assiduously resort to these. (28.)

---

Sanskrit

English
That is a great loss, a great blemish, a delusion, blindness and muteness, if excluded from the path of salvation one shall exert oneself elsewhere. (29.)

Sanskrit

English
O goddess, my eternal Dharma is fourfold : Jñāna, Kriyā, Caryā and Yoga. (30.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Jñāna is the knowledge of Paśu, Pāśa and Pati. Kriyā is the purificatory rite in regard to the six paths under the instructions of the preceptor. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
वर्णाश्रमप्रयुक्तस्य मयैव विहितस्य च ।
ममार्चनादिधर्मस्य चर्या चर्येति कथ्यते ॥ ३२॥

English
Caryā is the practical application of the holy rites such as my worship and the duties of the different castes and stages in life as prescribed by me. (32.)

---

Sanskrit

वृत्त्यन्तरनिरोधो यो योग इत्यभिधीयते ॥ ३३॥

English
Yoga is the fixation of the mind in me, along the path indicated by me, restraining other activities. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अश्वमेधगणात् श्रेष्ठं देवि चित्तप्रसाधनम् ।
मुक्तिदं च तथा ह्येतद्दुष्प्राप्यं विषयैषिणाम् ॥ ३४॥

English
O goddess, disciplining of the mind is far more excellent than many a horse-sacrifice. It is conducive to salvation. It is inaccessible to those who adhere to sensual objects. (34.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The Yoga that removes previous sins, belongs only to the unattached who has conquered the group of sense-organs by restraints and observances. (35.)

Sanskrit

English
Detachment gives birth to knowledge. Knowledge facilitates the functioning of Yoga. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
योगज्ञः पतितो वापि मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ।
दया कार्याथ सततमहिंसा ज्ञानसङ्ग्रहः ॥ ३७॥

सत्यमस्तेयमास्तिक्यं श्रद्धा चेन्द्रियनिग्रहः ।
अध्यापनं चाध्ययनं यजनं याजनं तथा ॥ ३८॥

ध्यानमीश्वरभावश्च सततं ज्ञानशीलता ।
य एवं वर्तते विप्रो ज्ञानयोगस्य सिद्धये ॥ ३९॥

अचिरादेव विज्ञानं लब्ध्वा योगं च विन्दति ।
दग्ध्वा देहमिमं ज्ञानी क्षणात् ज्ञानाग्निना प्रिये ।
प्रसादान्मम योगज्ञः कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यति ॥ ४०॥

पुण्यःपुण्यात्मकं कर्म मुक्तेस्तत्प्रतिबन्धकम् ।
तस्मान्नियोगतो योगी पुण्यापुण्यं विवर्जयेत् ॥ ४१॥

English
One conversant with Yoga, though fallen, is surely liberated. The following shall be carefully maintained. Mercy shall be practised along with non-violence. Knowledge shall be acquired. Truth, non-stealing, faith in scriptures and God, self-restraint, teaching, studying, performing sacrifice, presiding over sacrifice, meditation, piety towards God and practice of knowledge. A brahmin who follows all these for the acquisition of the path of knowledge, attains perfect knowledge ere long and secures Yoga too. O beloved, the wise devotee burns the physical body in a trice with the fire of knowledge. Due to my grace, the devotee conversant with Yoga eschews the bondage of the Karmans. Karman is in the nature of both merit and demerit. Both these are hindrances to liberation. The Yogin shall eschew both the merit and demerit. (37-41.)

Sanskrit
फलकामनया कर्मकरणात्प्रतिबध्यते ।

English
One is not subjected to bondage merely by the performance of rites. If the rites are performed with desire for the fruits thereof it results in bondage. Hence one shall abandon the fruits of actions. (42.)

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

---

Sanskrit

English
A Yogin engaged in the path of knowledge, an excellent sage permanently engaged in Yoga, my devotee enriched by purity of mind, shall attain my Sayujya. (45.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Those who have not had sufficient detachment in their minds but have resorted to me are authorised only in Jnana, Caryā and Kriyā according to their deserts. (46.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English

---

Sanskrit

English
My external worship is performed in the view of other persons. The same thing known and knowable to oneself alone is the internal worship. (49.)

---

Sanskrit

English
The mind that is devoted to me is the true mind and not any mind as it is. The speech that pertains to my name is true speech and not anything else. (50.)

---

Sanskrit

English

---

English

English

---

Sanskrit

ममागमार्थविज्ञानं ज्ञानं नान्यार्थवेदनम् ॥ ५४॥

English
Meditation is the pondering over my form and not the trances of the soul. Jñāna is the knowledge of my Āgamas and not the understanding of other topics. (54.)

Sanskrit
बाह्ये वाभ्यन्तरे वाथ यत्र स्यान्मनसो रतिः ।
प्राग्वासनावशाद्देवि तत्त्वनिष्ठां समाचरेत् ॥ ५५॥

English
O gentle lady, stability in the Tattvas shall be practised beginning with an external or internal object where the mind feels interested urged by previous impressions. (55.)

---

Sanskrit
बाह्यादाभ्यन्तरं श्रेष्ठं भवेच्छतगुणाधिकम् ।
असङ्करत्वाद्दोषाणां दृष्टानामप्यसम्भवात् ॥ ५६॥

English
The internal worship is hundred times more excellent than the external one in view of the absence of flaws seen and their intermixture avoided. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
शौचमाभ्यन्तरं विद्यान्न बाह्यं शौचमुच्यते ।
अन्तः शौचविमुक्तात्मा शुचिरप्यशुचिर्यतः ॥ ५७॥

English
Purity too is the internal one. External purity is not enough. A man though physically pure is not pure if he is devoid of inner purity. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
बाह्यमाभ्यंर्तरं चैव भजनं भवपूर्वकम् ।
न भावरहितं देवि विप्रलम्भैककारणम् ॥ ५८॥

English
O gentle lady, the adorative service whether external or internal shall be attended with devotion. If devoid of devotion, it is the cause of deceit. (58.)

---

Sanskrit
कृतकृत्यस्य पूतस्य मम किं क्रियते नरैः ।
बहिर्वाभ्यन्तरं वाथ मया भावो हि गृह्यते ॥ ५९॥

English
I am content and pure. What shall be done unto me by men? Externally or internally only devotion is taken into consideration by me. (59.)

---

Sanskrit
भावैकात्मा क्रिया देवि मम धर्मः सनातनः ।
मनसा कर्मणा वाचा ह्यनपेक्ष्य फलं क्वचित् ॥ ६०॥

English
That activity the soul of which is devotion, O gentle lady, is my eternal Dharma. It shall be performed without yearning for the fruit thereof mentally, verbally and physically. (60.)

---

Sanskrit
फलोद्देशेन देवेशि लघुर्मम समाश्रयः ।
फलार्थी तदभावे मां परित्यक्तुं क्षमो यतः ॥ ६१॥

English
O goddess, resorting to me with the fruit in view is easily feasible since the seeker of the fruit can abandon me in case there is no desired fruit. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
फलार्थिनोऽपि यस्यैव मयि चित्तं प्रतिष्ठितम् ।
भावानुरूपफलदस्तस्याप्यहमनिन्दिते ॥ ६२॥

English
O pious lady, I am the bestower of fruits in accordance with the extent of devotion even to him who, though seeking fruits, keeps his mind well established in me. (62.)

---

Sanskrit
फलानपेक्षया येषां मनो मत्प्रवणं भवेत् ।
प्रार्थयेयुः फलं पश्चाद्भक्तास्तेऽपि मम प्रियाः ॥ ६३॥

English
Those devotees are dearer to me whose minds are attached to me without reference to fruits but who may later on solicit for favours. (63.)

---

Sanskrit
प्राक् संस्कारवशादेव ये विचिन्त्य फलाफले ।
विवशा मां प्रपद्यन्ते मम प्रियतमा मताः ॥ ६४॥

English
Dearer unto me are those who resort to me with abject helplessness, unmindful of fruition or otherwise due to the impressions of previous actions. (64.)

---

Sanskrit
मल्लाभान्न परो लाभस्तेषामस्ति यथातथम् ।
ममापि लाभस्तल्लाभान्नापरः परमेश्वरि ॥ ६५॥

English
Verily, they do not acquire any further gain than acquiring me. O goddess, my gain too is nothing else but acquiring them into my fold. (65.)

---

Sanskrit
मदनुग्रहतस्तेषां भावो मयि समर्पितः ।
फलं परमनिर्वाणं प्रयच्छति बलादिव ॥ ६६॥

English
Their piety dedicated unto me due to my blessing is compelled to bestow the fruit on them, the fruit being the great beatitude. (66.)

Sanskrit
महात्मनामनन्यानां मयि संन्यस्तचेतसाम् ।
अष्टधा लक्षणं प्राहुर्मम धर्माधिकारिणाम् ॥ ६७॥

मद्भक्तजनवात्सल्यं पूजायां चानुमोदनम् ।
स्वयमभ्यर्चनं चैव मदर्थे चाङ्गचेष्टितम् ॥ ६८॥

मत्कथाश्रवणे भक्तिः स्वरनेत्राङ्गविक्रियाः ।
ममानुस्मरणं नित्यं यश्च मामुपजीवति ॥ ६९॥

English
They mention about eight traits for those authorised in Dharma, the noble souls who have dedicated their minds unto me without having recourse to anyone else, those who have favourable disposition to my devotees, who encourage my worship, or offer worship to me themselves or perform all activities of the body for my sake or have interest in listening to my stories, flutter in tones, eyes and limbs, remember me perpetually or surrender to me completely. (67-69.)

Sanskrit
एवमष्टविधं चिह्नं यस्मिन् म्लेच्छेऽपि वर्तते ।
स विप्रेन्द्रो मुनिः श्रीमान् स यतिः स च पण्डितः ॥ ७०॥

न मे प्रियश्चतुर्वेदी मद्भक्तो श्वपचोऽपि यः ।
तस्मै देयं ततो ग्राह्यं स च पूज्यो यथा ह्यहम् ॥ ७१॥

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति ॥ ७२॥

English
If these eight traits are present even in an out-caste he is equal to a leading brahmin, a sage, a glorious ascetic and a learned scholar. A master of four Vedas is not dearer to me than a Cāṇḍāla²⁵⁶ devoted to me. Things can be given to and taken from him. He shall be worshipped like me. I am not lost to him nor is he lost to me who offers unto me with devotion, even a leaf, a flower, a fruit or mere water. (70-72.)
