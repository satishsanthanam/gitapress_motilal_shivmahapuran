### **CHAPTER EIGHTEEN - (The purification of the six paths)**

---

Sanskrit
१८

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
ततः स्नानादिकं सर्वं समाप्याचार्यचोदितः ।
गच्छेद्बद्धाञ्जलिर्ध्यायन् शिवमण्डलपार्श्वतः ॥ १॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  After completing ablution and other rites the disciple shall, at the bidding of the preceptor, go near the Maṇḍala of Śiva meditating on Śiva, with palms joined in reverence. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ पूजां विना सर्वं कृत्वा पूर्वदिने यथा ।
नेत्रबन्धनपर्यन्तं दर्शयेत् मण्डलं गुरुः ॥ २॥

English
All rites upto the binding of the eyes shall be performed as on the previous day, except the worship. Then the preceptor shall show him the Maṇḍala. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
बद्धनेत्रेण शिष्येण पुष्पावकिरणे कृते ।
यत्रापतन्ति पुष्पाणि तस्य नामाऽस्य सन्दिशेत् ॥ ३॥

English
When flowers are strewn by the disciple whose eyes are tied, the preceptor shall tell him the places where the flowers fell. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
तं चोपनीय निर्माल्यमण्डलेऽस्मिन्यथा पुरा ।
पूजयेद्देवमीशानं जुहुयाच्च शिवानले ॥ ४॥

English
The preceptor shall take him to the Maṇḍala where worship had already been performed. As before he shall worship lord Iśāna and perform homa in the fire. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
शिष्येण यदि दुःस्वप्नो दृष्टस्तद्दोषशान्तये ।
शतमर्द्धं तदर्द्धं वा जुहुयान्मूलविद्यया ॥ ५॥

English
If any bad dream had been seen by the disciple, the preceptor shall perform hundred, or fifty, or twentyfive homas with the basic mantra for the removal of that defect. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सूत्रं शिखाबद्धं लम्बयित्वा यथा पुरा ।
आधारपूजाप्रभृति यन्निवृत्तिकलाश्रयम् ॥ ६॥

वागीश्वरीपूजनान्तं कुर्याद्धोमपुरःसरम् ।
अथ प्रणम्य वागीशं निवृत्तेर्व्यापिकां सतीम् ॥ ७॥

मण्डले देवमभ्यर्च्य हुत्वा चैवाहुतित्रयम् ।
प्रापयेच्च शिशोः प्राप्तिं युगपत्सर्वयोनिषु ॥ ८॥

English
The thread tied to the tuft shall be loosened to hang down as before, He shall perform the worship of Adhara' based on Nivrttikalā and conclude with the worship of Vāgisvari with due homas. He shall bow to Vāgisa and Sati who pervade Nivrtti. He shall worship the lord in the.. Mandala and make three Ahutis Then the mystical rite of making the simultaneous entry into all types of living beings, shall be performed. (6-8.)

---

Sanskrit
सूत्रदेहेऽथ शिष्यस्य ताडनप्रोक्षणादिकम् ।
कृत्वात्मानं समादाय द्वादशान्ते निवेद्य च ॥ ९॥

ततोऽप्यादाय मूलेन मुद्रया शास्त्रदृष्टया ।
योजयेन्मनसाचार्यो युगपत्सर्वयोनिषु ॥ १०॥

English
The rites of striking and sprinkling shall be performed in the gross body of the disciple and then the Atman shall be taken and placed in the twelve-petalled lotus. It shall be taken again from there through the repetition of the basic mantra and the gestures as prescribed in the scripture. The preceptor shall unite it mentally with all types of living beings simultaneously. (9-10.)

---

Sanskrit
देवानां जातयश्चाष्टौ तिरश्चां पञ्च जातयः ।
जात्यैकया च मानुष्या योनयश्च चतुर्दश ॥ ११॥

English
The living beings are fourteen in number: the eight types of the gods, five types of the lower animals and birds, and human beings forming one type. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
तासु सर्वासु युगपत्प्रवेशाय शिशोर्द्धिया ।
वागीशान्यां यथान्यायं शिष्यात्मानं निवेशयेत् ॥ १२॥

English
In order to facilitate the simultaneous entry into all of them, he shall deposit the Atman of the disciple along with his mind into Vagisanl duly. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
गर्भनिष्पत्तये देवं सम्पूज्य प्रणिपत्य च ।
हुत्वा चैव यथान्यायं निष्पन्नं तदनुस्मरेत् ॥ १३॥

English
For the accomplishment of the spiritual knowledge he shall worship and bow to the lord. After performing the homa he shall remember it as developed. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्पन्नस्यैवमुत्पत्तिमनुवृत्तिं च कर्मणा ।
आर्जवं भोगनिष्पत्तिः कुर्यात् प्रीतिं परां तथा ॥ १४॥

English
He shall duly perform the mystical rites of nativity, the pursuit of previous actions, straightforwardness and the great pleasure of enjoyment. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
निष्कृत्यर्थं च जात्यायुर्भोगसंस्कारसिद्धये ।
हुत्वाहुतित्रयं देवं प्रार्थयेद्देशिकोत्तमः ॥ १५॥

English
For the sake of redemption and the achievement of birth, longevity, enjoyment and purificatory impressions, the preceptor shall perform three Ahutis and pray to the lord. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
भोक्तृत्वविषयासङ्गमलं तत्कायशोधनम् ।
कृत्वैवमेव शिष्यस्य छिन्द्यात्पाशत्रयं ततः ॥ १६॥

English
Thus he shall purify his body that contains the dirt of enjoyment of pleasures and contact with sensual objects. He shall cut off the three bondages of the disciple. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
निकृत्या परिबद्धस्य पाशस्यात्यन्तभेदतः ।
कृत्वा शिष्यस्य चैतन्यं स्वच्छं मन्येत केवलम् ॥ १७॥

English
By the complete splitting of the bondage he shall make the disciple clean. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
हुत्वा पूर्णाहुतिं वह्नौ ब्रह्माणं पूजयेत्ततः ।
हुत्वाहुतित्रयं तस्मै शिवाज्ञामनुसन्दिशेत् ॥ १८॥

English
After making the Pūrṇāhuti in the fire he shall worship Brahmā. After performing three Āhutis for him he shall submit to him the behest of Śiva. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
पितामह त्वया नास्य यातुः शैवं परं पदम् ।
प्रतिबन्धो विधातव्यः शैवाज्ञैषा गरीयसी ॥ १९॥

English
'O Pitāmaha, no hindrance shall be placed for this disciple who is going to the great region of Śiva. This is the behest of Śiva'. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्यादिश्य तमभ्यर्च्य विसृज च विधानतः ।
समभ्यर्च्य महादेवं जुहुयादाहुतित्रयम् ॥ २०॥

English
Thus imploring him, he shall worship and ritualistically dismiss him. He shall worship Mahādeva and perform three Āhutis. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्त्या शुद्धमुद्धृत्य शिष्यात्मानं यथा पुरा ।
निवेश्यात्मनि सूत्रे च वागीशं पूजयेत्ततः ॥ २१॥

English
Uplifting the Ātman of the disciple, as before, when it has been purified by Nivṛttikalā he shall deposit it in his own Ātman. He shall then worship Vāgiśa. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
हुत्वाहुतित्रयं तस्मै प्रणम्य च विसृज्य ताम् ।
कुर्यान्निवृत्तः सन्धानं प्रतिष्ठां कलया सह ॥ २२॥

English
He shall perform three Āhutis now and ritualistically dismiss him. Returning he shall make it united with Pratiṣṭhākalā. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
सन्धाने युगपत्पूजां कृत्वा हुत्वाहुतित्रयम् ।
शिष्यात्मनः प्रतिष्ठायां प्रवेशं त्वथ भावयेत् ॥ २३॥

English
Performing the worship and the three Āhutis simultaneously he shall imagine the entry of the Ātman of the disciple into the Pratiṣṭhā Kalā. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः प्रतिष्ठामावाह्य कृत्वाशेषं पुरोदितम् ।
तद्व्याप्तिं व्यापिकां तस्य वागीशानीं च भावयेत् ॥ २४॥

English
Then after invoking Pratiṣṭhā and performing everything as previously mentioned he shall conceive its pervasion and the pervading Vāgiśāni. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
पूर्णेन्दुमण्डलप्रख्यां कृत्वा शेषं च पूर्ववत् ।
विष्णवे संविशेदाज्ञां शिवस्य परमात्मनः ॥ २५॥

English
He shall imagine Vāgiśāni as lustrous as the disc of the full moon. After doing everything as before he shall submit to Viṣṇu the behest of Śiva the great Ātman. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
विष्णोर्विसर्जनाद्यं च कृत्वा शेषं च विद्यया ।
प्रतिष्ठामनुसन्धाय तस्यां चापि यथा पुरा ॥ २६॥

कृत्वानुचिन्त्य तद्व्याप्तिं वागीशां च यथाक्रमम् ।
दीप्ताग्नौ पूर्णहोमान्तं कृत्वा शेषं च पूर्ववत् ॥ २७॥

नीलरुद्रमुपस्थाप्य तस्मै पूजादिकं तथा ।
कृत्वा कर्म शिवाज्ञां च दद्यात्पूर्वोक्तवर्त्मना ॥ २८॥

English
Performing everything like the ritualistic dismissal of Viṣṇu by means of Vidyā he shall think of Pratiṣṭhā and its pervasion and Vāgiśā too in order. He shall then perform everything concluding with the Pūrṇāhuti in the blazing fire as before. Nilarudra shall be invoked and its worship performed. As mentioned before, the behest of the lord shall be carried out. (26-28.)

---

Sanskrit
तपस्तमपि चोद्वास्य कृत्वा तस्याथ शान्तये ।
विद्याकलां समाधाय तद्व्याप्तिं चावलोकयेत् ॥ २९॥

English
Then the lord shall be ritualistically dismissed. For the purpose of quietening him, Vidyākalā shall be conceived and its pervasion surveyed. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वात्मनो व्यापिकां तद्वद्वागीशीं च यथा पुरा ।
बालार्कसदृशाकारां भासयन्तीं दिशो दश ॥ ३०॥

English
He shall then as before invoke Vāgiśī that pervades his Ātman, that has the form and features of the early morning sun and that illuminates the ten quarters. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शेषं यथापूर्वं कृत्वा देवं महेश्वरम् ।
आवाह्याराध्य हुत्वास्मै शिवाज्ञां मनसा दिशेत् ॥ ३१॥

English
He shall perform the remaining rites as before. He shall invoke lord Śiva and propitiate him. After performing homa he shall mentally submit to the behest of Śiva. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
महेश्वरं तथोत्सृज्य कृत्वान्यां च कलामिमाम् ।
शान्त्यतीतां कलां नीत्वा तद्व्याप्तिमवलोकयेत् ॥ ३२॥

English
After ritualistically dismissing the lord he shall lead another Kalā to Śāntyatītakalā and survey its pervasion. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वात्मनो व्यापिकां तद्वद्वागीशीं च विचिन्तयेत् ।
नभोमण्डलसङ्काशां पूर्णान्तं चापि पूर्ववत् ॥ ३३॥

English
As before he shall meditate on Vāgiśā that pervades the Ātman as resembling the permanent. Upto the final offering (Pūrṇāhuti) everything is as before. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा शेषविधानेन समभ्यर्च्य सदाशिवम् ।
तस्मै समादिशेदाज्ञां शम्भोरमितकर्मणः ॥ ३४॥

English
After doing everything in accordance with injunctions and worshipping Sadāśiva he shall submit to him the behest of Śiva of unmeasured activity. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रापि च यथापूर्वं शिवं शिरसि पूर्ववत् ।
समभ्यर्च्य च वागीशं प्रणम्य च विसर्जयेत् ॥ ३५॥

English
There too, after worshipping Śiva as before on the head and bowing to Vāgiśa he shall ritualistically dismiss him. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः शिवेन सम्प्रोक्ष्य शिष्यं शिरसि पूर्ववत् ।
विलयं शान्त्यतीतायाः शक्तितत्त्वेऽथ चिन्तयेत् ॥ ३६॥

English
Sprinkling then the disciple on the head as before with Śiva mantra he shall think of the merging of Śāntyatītakalā in the Śakti Tattva. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
षडध्वनः परे पारे सर्वाध्वव्यापिनीं पराम् ।
कोटिसूर्यप्रतीकाशां शैवीं शक्तिं च चिन्तयेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
On the other side beyond the six pathways he shall think of the greatest Śakti of Śiva that pervades the pathways and that equals a crore of suns. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
तदग्रे शिष्यमानीय शुद्धस्फटिकनिर्मलम् ।
प्रक्षाल्य कर्त्तरीं पश्चात् शिवशास्त्रोक्तमार्गतः ॥ ३८॥

कुर्यात्तस्य शिखाच्छेदं सह सूत्रेण देशिकः ।
ततस्तां गोमये न्यस्य शिवाग्नौ जुहुयाच्छिखाम् ॥ ३९॥

English
He shall bring the disciple as pure as the crystal in front of it. After washing the scissors, the preceptor shall cut off the tuft of the disciple as well as his thread in the manner mentioned in the Śaivite Scriptures. He shall place it in cowdung and consign it to Śiva fire. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
वौषडन्तेन मूलेन पुनः प्रक्षाल्य कर्तरीम् ।
हस्ते शिष्यस्य चैतन्यं तद्देहे विनिवर्तयेत् ॥ ४०॥

English
Then with the basic mantra ending with Vauṣaṭ he shall wash the scissors and hands. He shall restore the consciousness of the disciple to his body. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः स्नातं समाचान्तं कृतस्वस्त्ययनं शिशुम् ।
प्रवेश्य मण्डलाभ्यासं प्रणिपत्य च दण्डवत् ॥ ४१॥

पूजां कृत्वा यथान्यायं क्रियावैकल्यशुद्धये ।
वाचकेनैव मन्त्रेण जुहुयादाहुतित्रयम् ॥ ४२॥

English
The disciple shall take bath and perform Ācamana. Good wishes and blessings shall be conferred on him. He shall be brought to the Maṇḍala and made to prostrate. In order to expiate for the shortcomings in the rites due worship shall be performed. The preceptor shall pour three Āhutis by reciting the mantra. (41-42.)

---

Sanskrit
उपांशूच्चारयोगेन जुहुयादाहुतित्रयम् ।
पुनः सम्पूज्य देवेशं मन्त्रवैकल्यशुद्धये ॥ ४३॥

English
In order to expiate for the shortcomings in the Mantra he shall worship the lord of the gods and pour three Āhutis by muttering the mantra. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
मानसोच्चारयोगेन जुहुयादाहुतित्रयम् ।
तत्र शम्भुं समाराध्य मण्डलस्थं सहाम्बया ।
हुत्वाहुतित्रयं पश्चात्प्रार्थयेत्प्राञ्जलिर्गुरुः ॥ ४४॥

English
He shall pour three Āhutis by repeating the mantra mentally. After propitiating Śiva there, in the Maṇḍala and Pārvatī, he shall pour three Āhutis. With palms joined in reverence the preceptor shall pray thereafter. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
भगवंस्त्वत्प्रसादेन शुद्धिरस्य षडध्वनः ।
कृता तस्मात्परं धाम गमयैनं तवाव्ययम् ॥ ४५॥

English
'O lord, due to your grace the purity of the six pathways of the disciple has been completed. Now, O lord, lead him unto your imperishable abode.' (45.)

---

Sanskrit
इति विज्ञाप्य देवाय नाडीसन्धानपूर्वकम् ।
पूर्णान्तं पूर्ववत्कृत्वा ततो भूतानि शोधयेत् ॥ ४६॥

English
After submitting thus to the lord he shall perform every thing as before, from Nāḍisandhāna to the Pūrṇāhuti. Then he shall purify the elements (46.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थिरास्थिरे ततः शुद्ध्यै शीतोष्णे च ततः पदे ।
ध्यायेद्व्याप्त्यैकताकारे भूतशोधनकर्मणि ॥ ४७॥

English
Then, for conducting purity he shall meditate on the regions stable or unstable, hot or cold in the purificatory rite of the Bhūtas in the form of unity that is pervaded. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतानां ग्रन्थिविच्छेदं कृत्वा त्यक्त्वा सहाधिपैः ।
भूतानि स्थितियोगेन यो जपेत्परमे शिवे ॥ ४८॥

English
After cutting off the knot of the Bhūtas he shall join the Bhūtas on to the great lord Śiva by the path of steady concentration. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
विशोध्यास्यं तनुं दग्ध्वा प्लावयित्वा सुधाकणैः ।
स्थाप्यात्मानं ततः कुर्याद्विशुद्धाध्वमयं वपुः ॥ ४९॥

English
He shall purify his body, burn it, and drench it with drops of nectar. Steadying the Ātman he shall make his body in consonance with the pure pathways. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्रादौ शान्त्यतीतां तु व्यापिकां स्वाध्वनः कलाम् ।
शुद्धामेव शिशोर्मूर्ध्नि न्यसेच्छान्तिमुखे तथा ॥ ५०॥

विद्यां गलादिनाभ्यन्तं प्रतिष्ठां तदधः क्रमात् ।
जान्वन्तं तदधो न्यस्येन्निवृत्तिं चानुचिन्तयेत् ॥ ५१॥

English

Thereafter he shall meditate on it. (50-51.)
