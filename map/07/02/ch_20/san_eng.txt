### **CHAPTER TWENTY - *(Special consecration)***

---

Sanskrit
२०

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
अथैवं संस्कृतं शिष्यं कृतपाशुपतव्रतम् ।
आचार्यत्वेऽभिषिञ्चेत तद्योगत्वेन चान्यथा ॥ १॥

English
*Upamanyu said:—*  The preceptor shall crown consecrated disciple as a preceptor after he has performed the Pāśupata rite if he has the requisite qualities and not otherwise. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्डलं पूर्ववत्कृत्त्वा सम्पूज्य परमेश्वरम् ।
स्थापयत्पञ्चकलशान्दिक्षु मध्ये च पूर्ववत् ॥ २॥

English
The Maṇḍala shall be made as before and after worshipping the lord he shall, as before, place five water-jars one in each of the four quarters and one in the middle. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
निवृत्तिं पुरतो न्यस्य प्रतिष्ठां पश्चिमे घटे ।
विद्यां दक्षिणतः शान्तिमुत्तरे मध्यतः पराम् ॥ ३॥

English
Nivṛtti Kalā shall be installed in the vessel in the east, Pratiṣṭhā Kalā in the vessel in the west; Vidyā Kalā in the south. Śānti Kalā in the north and the Parā Kalā in the middle. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वा रक्षादिकं तत्र बद्ध्वा मुद्रां च धैनवीम् ।
अभिमन्त्र्य घटान्हुत्वा पूर्णान्तं च यथा पुरा ॥ ४॥

English
The following rites shall be performed as before, viz. those of Rakṣā, the Mudrābandha or the gesture of the cow, repetition of mantra over the vessels and the homa till the Pūrṇāhuti. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रवेश्य मण्डले शिष्यमनुष्णीषं च देशिकः ।
तर्पणाद्यं तु मन्त्राणां कुर्यात्पूर्वावसानकम् ॥ ५॥

English
The preceptor shall make the disciple enter the Maṇḍala. He shall not wear the head-dress. The Tarpaṇa and other rites too shall be performed as before. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
ततः सम्पूज्य देवेशमनुज्ञाप्य च पूर्ववत् ।
अभिषेकाय तं शिष्यमासनं त्वधिरोहयेत् ॥ ६॥

English
Then the lord shall be worshipped and his permission obtained ritualistically. He shall make the disciple sit on the seat for the pouring of water. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सकलीकृत्य तं पश्चात्कलापञ्चकरूपिणम् ।
न्यस्तमन्त्रतनुं बद्ध्वा शिवं शिष्यं समर्पयेत् ॥ ७॥

English
The disciple imagined as the five-fold offering of fruits shall be united with the Kalās and conceived as Śiva inasmuch as the mantra has been installed on his body; he shall be worshipped then. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो निवृत्तिकुम्भादिघटानुद्धृत्य वै क्रमात् ।
मध्यमान्ताच्छिवेनैव शिष्यं तमभिषेचयत् ॥ ८॥

English
Then the water from the vessels beginning with Nivṛtti Kumbha and ending with the middle shall be poured over the disciple repeating the names of Śiva. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवहस्तं समर्प्याथ शिशोः शिरसि देशिकः ।
शिवभावसमापन्नः शिवाचार्यं तमादिशेत् ॥ ९॥

English
Then the preceptor shall place his hand, repeating the Śiva mantra on the head of the disciple. The preceptor equipped with the piety towards Śiva shall call him Śivācārya. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथालङ्कृत्य तं देवमाराध्य शिवमण्डले ।
शतमष्टोत्तरं हुत्वा दद्यात्पूर्णाहुतिं ततः ॥ १०॥

English
He shall be adorned. Then the preceptor shall propitiate him in Śivamaṇḍala. After performing hundred and eight Āhūtis he shall offer the Pūrṇāhūti. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः सम्पूज्य देवेशं प्रणम्य भुवि दण्डवत् ।
शिरस्यञ्जलिमाधाय शिवं विज्ञापयेद्गुरुः ॥ ११॥

English
The preceptor shall worship the lord and prostrate on the ground with palms joined in reverence over the head. He shall submit to the lord Śiva. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
भगवंस्त्वत्प्रसादेन देशिकोऽयं मया कृतः ।
अनुगृह्य त्वया देव दिव्याज्ञास्मै प्रदीयताम् ॥ १२॥

English
"O lord, by your grace this disciple has been made a preceptor by me. O lord, after blessing him, may the divine behest be bestowed on him. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं विज्ञाप्य शिष्येण सह भूयः प्रणम्य च ।
शिवं शिवागमं दिव्यं पूजयेत् शिववद्गुरुः ॥ १३॥

English
After submitting thus, the preceptor shall bow to Śiva again along with the disciple. He shall worship the divine Śiva-Āgama in the same manner as Śiva himself. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनः शिवमनुज्ञाप्य शिवज्ञानस्य पुस्तकम् ।
उभाभ्यामथ पाणिभ्यां दद्याच्छिष्याय देशिकः ॥ १४॥

English
Then he shall take the ritualistic permission from Śiva. The preceptor shall take the book "Śivajñāna" with both the hands and hand it over to the disciple. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स तां मूर्ध्नि समाधाय विद्यां विद्यासनोपरि ।
अधिरोप्य यथान्यायमभिवन्द्य समर्चयेत् ॥ १५॥

English
The disciple shall accept it with his bent head and place the Vidyā over the seat. He shall then respectfully adore it. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ तस्मै गुरुर्दद्याद्राजोपकरणान्यपि ।
आचार्यपदवीं प्राप्तो राज्यं चापि यतोऽर्हति ॥ १६॥

English
The preceptor shall give him royal paraphernalia since one who has attained the status of Ācārya deserves even a kingdom. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अथानुशासनं कुर्यात्पूर्वैराचरितं यथा ।
यथा च शिवशास्त्रोक्तं यथा लोकेषु पूज्यते ॥ १७॥

English
Then he shall impart to him the modes of discipline followed by elders as mentioned in Śaivite sacred literature and as honoured amongst the people. (17.)

---

Sanskrit
शिष्यान् परीक्ष्य यत्नेन शिवशास्त्रोक्तलक्षणैः ।
संस्कृत्य च शिवज्ञानं तेभ्यो दद्याच्च देशिकः ॥ १८॥

English
The preceptor shall test the disciples assiduously through the characteristics mentioned in Śaivite scriptures. He shall consecrate them and impart to them the Śaivite knowledge. (18.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं सर्वमनायासं शौचं क्षान्तिं दयां तथा ।
अस्पृहामप्यसूयां च यत्नेन च विभावयेत् ॥ १९॥

English
He shall infuse in him all these qualities without difficulty, viz. purity, forbearance, mercifulness, non-covetousness and absence of jealousy even strenuously. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थमादिश्य तं शिष्यं शिवमुद्वास्य मण्डलात् ।
शिवकुम्भानलादींश्च सदस्यानपि पूजयेत् ॥ २०॥

English
After thus urging the disciple he shall ritualistically dismiss Śiva from the Maṇḍala. He shall worship the Śiva-kumbha, fire and others who had been invited there. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
युगपद्वाथ संस्कारान् कुर्वीत सगणो गुरुः ।
तत्र त्रयं द्वयं वापि प्रयोगस्योपदिश्यते ॥ २१॥

तदादावेव कलशान्कल्पयेदध्वशुद्धिवत् ।
कृत्वा समयसंस्कारमभिषेकं विनाऽखिलम् ॥ २२॥

समभ्यर्च्य शिवं भूयः कृत्वा चाध्वविशोधनम् ।
तस्मिन् परिसमाप्ते तु पुनर्देवं प्रपूजयेत् ॥ २३॥

English
Or the preceptor accompanied by his attendants shall simultaneously perform all the consecratory rites; the rites shall be repeated twice. The water-vessels shall be installed in the beginning itself as in the rite of the purification of the paths. After performing the Samaya consecration, wholly without ablution Śiva shall be worshipped again after the purification of the path. When that is concluded he shall worship the lord again. (21-23.)

---

Sanskrit
हुत्वा मन्त्रं तु सन्तर्प्य सन्दीप्याशास्य चेश्वरम् ।
समर्प्य मन्त्रं शिष्यस्य पाणौ शेषं समापयेत् ॥ २४॥

English
After homa the Tarpaṇa rite of the mantra shall be performed. After the blessing of the lord the mantra shall be placed in the hand of the disciple and the remaining rites concluded. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा मन्त्रसंस्कारमनुचिन्त्याखिलं क्रमात् ।
अध्वशुद्धिं गुरुः कुर्यादभिषेकावसानिकम् ॥ २५॥

English
Or beginning with the consecration of the Mantra and concluding with the rite of Abhiṣeka the rites of the purification of the path shall be performed by the preceptor. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र यः शान्त्यतीतादिकलासु विहितो विधिः ।
स सर्वोऽपि विधातव्यस्तत्त्वत्रयविशोधने ॥ २६॥

English
In the purificatory rite of the three Tattvas the rites mentioned for the Kalās Śāntyatīta and others shall be performed. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवविद्यात्मतत्त्वाख्यं तत्त्वत्रयमुदाहृतम् ।
शक्तौ शिवस्ततो विद्यात्तस्यास्त्वात्मा समुद्बभौ ॥ २७॥

English
Śiva, Vidyā and Ātmatattva—these are the three Tattvas. Śiva came out of Śakti and from Śiva the Vidyā and from Vidyā the Ātman. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवेन शान्त्यतीताध्वा व्याप्तस्तदपरः परः ।
विद्यया परिशिष्टोऽध्वा ह्यात्मना निखिलः क्रमात् ॥ २८॥

English
The Śāntyatīta path is pervaded by Śiva. The next path is pervaded by Vidyā and the remaining paths are pervaded by Ātman. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
दुर्लभं शाम्भवं मत्वा मन्त्रमूलं मनीषिणः ।
शाक्तं शंसीत संस्कारं शिवशास्त्रार्थपारगाः ॥ २९॥

English
Intelligent persons who have mastered Śaivite Scriptures laud the consecration through Śakti after considering that the consecration through Śiva is very difficult of access. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
इति ते सर्वमाख्यातं संस्काराख्यस्य कर्मणः ।
चातुर्विध्यमिदं कृष्ण किं भूयः श्रोतुमिच्छसि ॥ ३०॥

English
O Kṛṣṇa, thus the four types of consecratory rites have been narrated to you. What else do you wish to hear? (30.)
