### **CHAPTER TWENTYONE - (Nitya and Naimittika rites)**

---

Sanskrit
२१

कृष्ण उवाच ।
भगवन् श्रोतुमिच्छामि शिवाश्रमनिषेविणाम् ।
शिवशास्त्रोदितं कर्म नित्यनैमित्तिकं तथा ॥ १॥

English
Sri Kṛṣṇa said:—  O holy lord, I wish to hear the compulsory and optional rites of those who follow Śaivite conduct of life, as mentioned in Śaivite scriptures. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
प्रातरुत्थाय शयनाद्ध्यात्वा देवं सहाम्बया ।
विचार्य कार्यं निर्गच्छेद् गृहादभ्युदितेऽरुणे ॥ २॥

English
Upamanyu said:—  He shall rise early in the morning and meditate on the lord and the goddess For performing necessaries he shall go out of the house when the sun has risen. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
अबाधे विजने देशे कुर्यादावश्यकं ततः ।
कृत्वा शौचं विधानेन दन्तधावनमाचरेत् ॥ ३॥

English
In a secluded spot where there is no hindrance he shall evacuate his bowels. After washing in accordance with the rules he shall clean the teeth. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
अलाभे दन्तकाष्ठानामष्टम्यादिदिनेषु च ।
अपां द्वादशगण्डूषैः कुर्यादास्यविशोधनम् ॥ ४॥

English
On the eighth and other days whenever tooth-brush twig is not available he shall gargle twelve times and clean the teeth. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
आचम्य विधिवत्पश्चाद्वारुणं स्नानमाचरेत् ।
नद्यां वा देवखाते वा ह्रदे वाथ गृहेऽपि वा ॥ ५॥

English
After performing Ācamana he shall take bath either in a river or in a pond or in an eddy or in the house itself. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नानद्रव्याणि तत्तीरे स्थापयित्वा बहिर्मलम् ।
व्यापोह्य मृदमालिप्य स्नात्वा गोमयमालिपेत् ॥ ६॥

English
The toilet articles shall be kept on the bank. The dirt of the body shall be washed off. The clay shall be applied over the body. After taking bath he shall apply cow-dung over the body. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
स्नात्वा पुनः पुनर्वस्त्रं त्यक्त्वा वाथ विशोध्य च ।
सुस्नातो नृपवद्भूयः शुद्धं वासो वसीत च ॥ ७॥

English
He shall take bath again and abandon the old cloth. Like a king he shall put on new dress. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
मलस्नानं सुगन्धाद्यैः स्नानं दन्तविशोधनम् ।
न कुर्याद् ब्रह्मचारी च तपस्वी विधवा तथा ॥ ८॥

English
Neither a Brahmacārin nor an ascetic nor a widow shall take bath with the scented soaps. They shall not use tooth-brush twig for cleaning the teeth. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
सोपवीतः शिखां बद्ध्वा प्रविश्य च जलान्तरम् ।
अवगाह्य समाचान्तो जले न्यस्येत् त्रिमण्डलम् ॥ ९॥

English
He shall tie his tuft and have the sacred thread in the normal way. He shall enter water and plunge therein. After performing the Ācamana he shall take plunge thrice in the water. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
तोये मग्नः पुनर्मन्त्रं जपेच्छक्त्या शिवं स्मरेत् ।
उत्थायाचम्य तेनैव स्वात्मानमभिषेचयेत् ॥ १०॥

गोश‍ृङ्गेण सदर्भेण पालाशेन दलेन वा ।
पाद्मेन वाथ पाणिभ्यां पञ्चकृत्वस्त्रिरेव वा ॥ ११॥

English
After plunging in, he shall repeat the mantra and think on Śiva and Śakti. He shall get up and perform Ācamana. Then he shall pour water over the body with the cow-horn along with Darbha, or with the leaf of a Palāśa tree, or the lotus leaf or with both the hands, five or three times. (10-11.)

---

Sanskrit
उद्यानादौ गृहे चैव वर्द्धन्या कलशेन वा ।
अवगाहनकालेऽद्भिर्मन्त्रितैरभिषेचयेत् ॥ १२॥

English
If he is taking bath in a garden or in the house he shall pour water with Vardhanī or a water-jar. When taking bath he shall pour water with the mantras. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ चेद्वारुणं कर्तुमशक्तः शुद्धवाससा ।
आर्द्रेण शोधयेद्देहमापादतलमस्तकम् ॥ १३॥

English
If one is unable to take water-bath, one shall take a wet cloth and wipe the body from foot to head. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
आग्नेयं वाथ वा मान्त्रं कुर्यात् स्नानं शिवेन वा ।
शिवचिन्तापरं स्नानं युक्तस्यात्मीयमुच्यते ॥ १४॥

English
Or he shall take the self-bath. That is, one shall repeat the name Śiva. That bath wherein Śiva is remembered is called the self-bath of a Yogin. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वसूत्रोक्तविधानेन मन्त्राचमनपूर्वकम् ।
आचरेद् ब्रह्मयज्ञान्तं कृत्वा देवादितर्पणम् ॥ १५॥

English
In accordance with the rules prescribed in one's code he shall perform the rites of Tarpaṇa for the gods beginning with Ācamana and ending with Brahmayajña. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
मण्डलस्थं महादेवं ध्यात्वाभ्यर्च्य यथाविधि ।
दद्यादर्घ्यं ततस्तस्मै शिवायादित्यरूपिणे ॥ १६॥

English
Meditating on lord Śiva stationed in the Maṇḍala and worshipping him duly he shall offer Arghya to Śiva in the form of the sun. (16.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवैतत्स्वसूत्रोक्तं कृत्वा हस्तौ विशोधयेत् ।
करन्यासं ततः कृत्वा सकलीकृतविग्रहः ॥ १७॥

वामहस्तगताम्भोभिर्गन्धसिद्धार्थकान्वितैः ।
कुशपुञ्जेन वाऽभ्युक्ष्य मूलमन्त्रसमन्वितैः ॥ १८॥

आपोहिष्ठादिभिर्मन्त्रैः शेषमाघ्राय वै जलम् ।
वामनासापुटेनैव देवं सम्भावयेत्सितम् ॥ १९॥

English
Or after performing what is prescribed in one's own Sūtra he shall wash his hands and perform the rite of Karanyāsa. He shall make his body united with the Kalās through water taken in the left palm and containing scents and white mustard. He shall then sprinkle water over him through the bunch of Kuśa grass repeating the mantras 'Āpo hi ṣṭhā'²⁷¹ etc. accompanied by the basic mantra. Sniffing at the water remaining in the left nostril he shall think on lord Śiva again. (17-19.)

---

Sanskrit
अर्घमादाय देहस्थं सव्यनासापुटेन च ।
कृष्णवर्णेन बाह्यस्थं भावयेच्च शिलागतम् ॥ २०॥

English
Taking off the dark-complexioned sin of the body through the left nostril and that outside, he shall conceive it as gone to the rock. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
तर्पयेदथ देवेभ्य ऋषिभ्यश्च विशेषतः ।
भूतेभ्यश्च पितृभ्यश्च दद्यादर्घ्यं यथाविधि ॥ २१॥

English
He shall perform the Tarpaṇa rite to the gods, to the sages in particular, to the Bhūtas and to the Pīṭṭs. He shall offer Arghya duly. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
रक्तचन्दनतोयेन हस्तमात्रेण मण्डलम् ।
सुवृत्तं कल्पयेद्भूमौ रक्तचूर्णाद्यलङ्कृतम् ॥ २२॥

English
With a handful of red sandal water he shall make a circular Maṇḍala on the ground and embellish it with red powder. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र सम्पूजयेद्भानुं स्वकीयावरणैः सह ।
खखोल्कायेति मन्त्रेण साङ्गतः सुखसिद्धये ॥ २३॥

English
There he shall worship the sun with the covers along with his ancillaries repeating the mantra "Khakholkāya" for securing happiness.²⁷² (23.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनश्च मण्डलं कृत्वा तदङ्गैः परिपूज्य च ।
तत्र स्थाप्य हेमपात्रं मागधप्रस्थसम्मितम् ॥ २४॥

पूरयेत् गन्धतोयेन रक्तचन्दनयोगिना ।
रक्तपुष्पैस्तिलैश्चैव कुशाक्षतसमन्वितैः ॥ २५॥

English
He shall make another Maṇḍala and worship it with its ancillaries. He shall place there a gold-vessel of the size of the Prastha²⁷³ current in the Magadha land. He shall fill it with scented water and red sandal along with red flowers, gingelly seeds, Akṣatas and Kuśa grass. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
दूर्वापामार्गगव्यैश्च केवलेन जलेन वा ।
जानुभ्यां धरणीं गत्वा नत्वा देवं च मण्डले ॥ २६॥

कृत्वा शिरसि तत्पात्रं दद्यादर्घ्यं शिवाय तत् ।
अथवाञ्जलिना तोयं सदर्भं मूलविद्यया ॥ २७॥

उत्क्षिपेदम्बरस्थाय शिवायादित्यमूर्तये ।
कृत्वा पुनः करन्यासं करशोधनपूर्वकम् ॥ २८॥

बुद्ध्वेशानादि सद्यान्तं पञ्चब्रह्ममयं शिवम् ।
गृहीत्वा भसितं मन्त्रैर्विमृज्याङ्गानि संस्पृशेत् ॥ २९॥

English
Or it can contain Dūrvā, Apāmārga and other articles or mere water. He shall keep that pot over his head. Kneeling down he shall bow to the lord in the Maṇḍala and offer it as Arghya to Śiva. Or he shall take water in the palms joined together and throw it up as an offering to Śiva stationed in the sky in the form of the sun. The water shall contain Darbha grass and the basic Mantra shall be repeated. Again he shall wash his hands and perform the Karanyāsa realising Śiva in the form of the five Brahmans²⁷⁴ from Īśāna to Sadyojāta. He shall take Bhasma, in the hand and repeating mantra yā dināntaiḥ²⁷⁵ he shall touch limbs in order, viz—the face, chest, private parts, feet. (26-29.)

---

Sanskrit
आपादान्तैः शिरोवक्त्रहृद्गुह्यचरणान् क्रमात् ।
ततो मूलेन सर्वाङ्गमालभ्य वसनान्तरम् ॥ ३०॥

परिधाय द्विराचम्य प्रोक्ष्यैकादशमन्त्रितैः ।
जलैराच्छाद्य वासोऽयद् द्विराचम्य शिवं स्मरेत् ॥ ३१॥

English
Then repeating the basic mantra he shall touch all parts of the body and wear another cloth. After performing Ācamana twice he shall sprinkle water over himself eleven times repeating the mantra. He shall wear another cloth, perform Ācamana twice and remember Śiva. (30-31.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्न्यस्तकरो मन्त्री त्रिपुण्ड्रं भस्मना लिखेत् ।
अवक्रमाय तं व्यक्तं ललाटे गन्धवारिणा ॥ ३२॥

English
He shall perform Karanyāsa again and repeating the mantra he shall apply the Tripuṇḍra marks. On the forehead the marks shall be clear, broad and straight. Scented water shall be used to paste the Bhasma. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
वृत्तं वा चतुरस्रं वा बिन्दुमर्द्धेन्दुमेव वा ।
ललाटे यादृशं पुण्ड्रं लिखितं भस्मना पुनः ॥ ३३॥

तादृशं भुजयोर्मूर्ध्नि स्तनयोरन्तरे लिखेत् ।
सर्वाङ्गोद्धूलनं चैव न समानं त्रिपुण्ड्रकैः ॥ ३४॥

English
It may also be circular or square in shape. It may be a single dot or a semicircular figure. But he shall apply the marks in the same way on the arms, head and chest as on the forehead. Dusting with the Bhasma is not on a par with Tripuṇḍra. (33-34.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात् त्रिपुण्ड्रमेवैकं लिखेदुद्धूलनं विना ।
रुद्राक्षान्धारयेन्मूर्ध्नि कण्ठे श्रोते करे तथा ॥ ३५॥

English
Hence it is better to have Tripuṇḍras above without dusting. He shall wear Rudrākṣa beads on the head, round the neck, in the ears and hands. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्णवर्णमच्छिन्नं शुभं नान्यैर्धृतं शुभम् ।
विप्रादीनां क्रमात् श्रेष्ठं पीतं रक्तमथासितम् ॥ ३६॥

English
The Rudrākṣa bead with the lustre of gold is excellent. It shall not be cut. What is not worn by others is the most auspicious. To the three castes the colours yellow, red and black are respectively excellent. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
तदलाभे यथालाभं धारणीयमदूषितम् ।
तत्रापि नोत्तरं नीचैर्धार्यं नीचमथोत्तरैः ॥ ३७॥

English
If the excellent bead is not available whatever is available shall be used for wearing. But it shall not be defective. The lower caste men shall not wear the bead intended for the higher caste. The higher caste men can wear the other types too. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
नाशुचिर्द्धारयेदक्षं सदा कालेषु धारयेत् ।
इत्थं त्रिसन्ध्यमथवा द्विसन्ध्यं सकृदेव वा ॥ ३८॥

कृत्वा स्नानादिकं शक्त्या पूजयेत्परमेश्वरम् ।
पूजास्थानं समासाद्य बद्ध्वा रुचिरमासनम् ॥ ३९॥

English
Impure men shall not wear the Rudrākṣa bead. Everyone shall take bath twice, twice or once a day according to his strength, and worship the lord seated in a charming pose in the pedestal. (38-39.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायेद्देवं च देवीं च प्राङ्मुखो वाप्युदङ्मुखः ।
श्वेतादीन्नकुलीशान्तांस्तच्छिष्यान्प्रणमेद्गुरुम् ॥ ४०॥

English
He shall face the east or the north while meditating on the lord and the goddess. He shall worship the preceptor and the disciples from Śveta to Nakuliśa.²⁷⁶ (40.)

---

Sanskrit
पुनर्देवं शिवं नत्वा ततो नामाष्टकं जपेत् ।
शिवो महेश्वरश्चैव रुद्रो विष्णुः पितामहः ॥ ४१॥

संसारवैद्यः सर्वज्ञः परमात्मेति चाष्टकम् ।
अथवा शिवमेवैकं जपित्वैकादशाधिकम् ॥ ४२॥

English
Again he shall bow to the lord and repeat his eight²⁷⁷ names, viz. Śiva, Maheśvara, Rudra, Viṣṇu, Pitāmaha Saṃsāravaidya, Sarvajña and Paramātman. Or he shall repeat the name Śiva eleven times or more. (41-42.)

---

Sanskrit
जिह्वाग्रे तेजसो राशिं ध्यात्वा व्याध्यादिशान्तये ।
प्रक्षाल्य चरणौ कृत्वा करौ चन्दनचर्चितौ ।
प्रकुर्वीत करन्यासं करशोधनपूर्वकम् ॥ ४३॥

English
For the removal of sickness he shall meditate on the mass of splendour at the tip of his tongue. He shall wash his feet and apply sandal paste on his hands. He shall perform Karanyāsa after wiping off his hands. (43.)
