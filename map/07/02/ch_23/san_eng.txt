### **CHAPTER TWENTYTHREE - (A gloss on the rules governing worship)**

---

Sanskrit
२३

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
व्याख्यां पूजाविधानस्य प्रवदामि समासतः ।
शिवशास्त्रे शिवेनैव शिवायै कथितस्य तु ॥ १॥

English
Upamanyu said:—  I shall briefly give a commentary to the rules governing worships mentioned by Śivā to Śivā in the Śaivite scriptures. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अङ्गमभ्यन्तरं यागमग्निकार्यावसानकम् ।
विधाय वा न वा पश्चाद् बहिर्यागं समाचरेत् ॥ २॥

English
With or without performing the internal worship which is its ancillary and which concludes with the rites in fire, one shall perform the external worship. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र द्रव्याणि मनसा कल्पयित्वा विशोध्य च ।
ध्यात्वा विनायकं देवं पूजयित्वा विधानतः ॥ ३॥

दक्षिणे चोत्तरे चैव नन्दीशं सुयशां तथा ।
आराध्य मनसा सम्यगासनं कल्पयेद्बुधः ॥ ४॥

English
The materials are mentally conceived and purified. After meditating on Vināyaka and worshipping the lord in accordance with the injunctions, the learned devotee shall propitiate Nandīśa and Suyasā in the south and the north and offer mentally well-conceived seat. (3-4.)

---

Sanskrit
आराधनादिकैर्युक्तः सिंहयोगासनादिकम् ।
पद्मासनं वा विमलं तत्त्वत्रयसमन्वितम् ॥ ५॥

English
It may be a throne or a yogic seat or lotus-seat equipped with the three principles.²⁷⁹ (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्योपरि शिवं ध्यायेत् साम्बं सर्वमनोहरम् ।
सर्वलक्षणसम्पन्नं सर्वावयवशोभनम् ॥ ६॥

English
Śiva shall be meditated on it. He is accompanied by Ambā. He is charming, endowed with all characteristics, auspicious in every limb. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वातिशयसंयुक्तं सर्वाभरणभूषितम् ।
रक्तास्यपाणिचरणं कुन्दचन्द्रस्मिताननम् ॥ ७॥

English
He possesses all excellences. He is bedecked in all ornaments. His face, hands and feet, are red. His face beams with smiles resembling the Kunda flower and the moon. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
शुद्धस्फटिकसङ्काशं फुल्लपद्मत्रिलोचनम् ।
चतुर्भुजमुदाराङ्गं चारुचन्द्रकलाधरम् ॥ ८॥

English
He is as pure as the pure 'crystal. He has three eyes resembling full blown lotus. He has four arms and splendid body. He wears the charming digit of the moon. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
वरदाभयहस्तं च मृगटङ्कधरं हरम् ।
भुजङ्गहारवलयं चारुनीलगलान्तरम् ॥ ९॥

English
His hands show the gestures of granting the boon and protection. He holds the deer and an axe. He has serpents for necklaces and bangles. His neck is charmingly blue. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वोपमानरहितं सानुगं सपरिच्छदम् ।
ततः सञ्चिन्तयेत्तस्य वामभागे महेश्वरीम् ॥ १०॥

English
He has none to compare with. He is accompanied by his followers and attendants. Then the devotee shall think of him to his left. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रफुल्लोत्पलपत्राभां विस्तीर्णायतलोचनाम् ।
पूर्णचन्द्राभवदनां नीलकुञ्चितमूर्धजाम् ॥ ११॥

English
She is as tender as the petals of full blown-lotus. Her eyes are large and broad. Her face has the lustre of the full moon. Her tresses are dark-blue and curly. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
नीलोत्पलदलप्रख्यां चन्द्रार्द्धकृतशेखराम् ।
अतिवृत्तघनोत्तुङ्गस्निग्धपीनपयोधराम् ॥ १२॥

English
Her complexion has the lustre of the petals of a blue lotus. She has the half moon for her coronet. Her breasts are round, weighty, lifted up, smooth and plump. (12.)

---

Sanskrit
तनुमध्यां पृथुश्रोणीं पीतसूक्ष्मवराम्बराम् ।
सर्वाभरणसम्पन्नां ललाटतिलकोज्ज्वलाम् ॥ १३॥

English
Her middle is slender. Her buttocks are large. She wears an exquisitely fine yellow garment. She is richly bedecked in all ornaments. The Tilaka mark on her forehead is dazzlingly brilliant. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
विचित्रपुष्पसङ्कीर्णकेशपाशोपशोभिताम् ।
सर्वतोऽनुगुणाकारां किञ्चिल्लज्जानताननाम् ॥ १४॥

English
She shines with flowers of various colours embellishing her braid of hair. In every respect her features are in consonance with her qualities. Due to bashfulness her face is slightly lowered. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
हेमारविन्दं विलसद्दधानां दक्षिणे करे ।
दण्डवच्चापरं हस्तं न्यस्यासीनां महासने ॥ १५॥

English
In her right hand she bears a shining golden lotus. She is seated on a splendid seat with the other hand placed on it like a supporting staff. (15.)

---

Sanskrit
पाशविच्छेदिकां साक्षात्सच्चिदानन्दरूपिणीम् ।
एवं देवं च देवीं च ध्यात्वासनवरे शुभे ॥ १६॥

सर्वोपचारवद् भक्त्या भावपुष्पैः समर्चयेत् ।
अथवा परिकल्प्यैवं मूर्तिमन्यतमां विभोः ॥ १७॥

शैवीं सदाशिवाख्यां वा तथा माहेश्वरीं पराम् ।
षड्विंशकाभिधानां वा श्रीकण्ठाख्यामथापि वा ॥ १८॥

मन्त्रन्यासादिकांश्चापि कृत्वा स्वस्यां तनौ यथा ।
अस्यां मूर्तौ मूर्तिमन्तं शिवं सदसतः परम् ॥ १९॥

English
She splits the Pāśa. She is in the form of existence, knowledge and bliss. After conceiving the lord and the goddess thus and meditating on them in an excellent auspicious seat, the devotee shall worship with piety as flowers along with all types of service. Or he can conceive the form of the lord in any of the following ways:—as Śiva, Sadāśiva, Maheśvara, or as one having the name of twenty-six principles or as Śrīkaṇṭha. Just as Mantra Nyāsa is performed in the body so also in this form the devotee can meditate on embodied Śiva who is beyond Sat and Asat as in the manner of external worship. He shall complete the worship mentally. (16-19.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यात्वा बाह्यक्रमेणैव पूजां निर्वर्त्तयेद्धिया ।
समिदाज्यादिभिः पश्चान्नाभौ होमं च भावयेत् ॥ २०॥

भ्रूमध्ये च शिवं ध्यायेच्छुद्धदीपशिखाकृतिम् ।
इत्थमङ्गे स्वतन्त्रे वा योगे ध्यानमये शुभे ॥ २१॥

अग्निकार्यावसानं च सर्वत्रैव समो विधिः ।
अथ चिन्तामयं सर्वं समाप्याराधनक्रमम् ॥ २२॥

लिङ्गे च पूजयेद्देवं स्थण्डिले वानलेऽपि वा ॥ २३॥

English
He shall then mentally imagine the Homa in the navel with sacrificial twig, ghee etc. He shall meditate on Śiva in the middle of the eye-brows in the form of the flame of the pure lamp. Thus in this mentally conceived auspicious worship whether in the body or independently, the rules until the concluding rites in fire are the same. Or after concluding the imaginary procedure of propitiation the devotee shall worship the lord in the phallic image or on the ground or in the fire. (20-23.)
