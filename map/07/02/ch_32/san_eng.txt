### **CHAPTER THIRTY TWO - *(The rites for achieving worldly benefits)***

---

Sanskrit
३२

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
एतत्ते कथितं कृष्ण कर्मेहामुत्र सिद्धिदम् ।
क्रियातपोजपध्यानसमुच्चयमयं परम् ॥ १॥

English
*Upamanyu said :—*  O Kṛṣṇa, what has been mentioned to you is the site which yields benefit here and hereafter. It is a great synthesis of physical rites, penance, Japas and meditation. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
अथ वक्ष्यामि शैवानामिहैव फलदं नृणाम् ।
पूजाहोमजपध्यानतपोदानमयं महत् ॥ २॥

English
Now I shall mention that great rite of worship, Homa, Japa, meditation, penance and charitable gifts, which yields benefits here itself to men who follow Siva. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र संसाधयेत्पूर्वं मन्त्रं मन्त्रार्थवित्तमः ।
दृष्टसिद्धिकरं कर्म नान्यथा फलदं यतः ॥ ३॥

English
The devotee expert in the meaning of mantras shall at the outset practise mantras since the rites of visible benefits here itself are not fruitful otherwise. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
सिद्धमन्त्रोऽप्यदृष्टेन प्रबलेन तु केनचित् ।
प्रतिबन्धफलं कर्म न कुर्यात्सहसा बुधः ॥ ४॥

English
A learned and sensible devotee, even after the mantra has been achieved, shall not haphazardly perform any rite the fruit whereof has been thwarted by some unseen powerful obstacle. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्य तु प्रतिबन्धस्य कर्तुं शक्येह निष्कृतिः ।
परीक्ष्य शकुनाद्यैस्तदादौ निष्कृतिमाचरेत् ॥ ५॥

English
Atonement is possible for that obstacle. Hence he shall perform that atonement after testing it at the outset by means of omens. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
योऽन्यथा कुरुते मोहात्कर्मैहिकफलं नरः ।
न तेन फलभाक्स स्यात्प्राप्नुयाच्चोपहास्यताम् ॥ ६॥

English
He who due to delusion performs the rite yielding the benefit here itself does not attain the fruit thereof and becomes the laughing stock. (6.)

---

Sanskrit
अविस्रब्धो न कुर्वीत कर्म दृष्टफलं क्वचित् ।
स खल्वश्रद्धधानः स्यान्नाश्रद्धः फलमृच्छति ॥ ७॥

English
Without faith and devotion no one shall perform the rite intended to yield direct benefit. He becomes an unbeliever and an unbeliever does not attain fruit. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
नापराधोऽस्ति देवस्य कर्मण्यपि तु निष्फले ।
यथोक्तकारिणां पुंसामिहैव फलदर्शनात् ॥ ८॥

English
It is not the fault of the lord if the rite does not yield the fruit since it is found to yield fruit here itself for those who perform the rite exactly as ordained. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
साधकः सिद्धमन्त्रश्च निरस्तप्रतिबन्धकः ।
विश्वस्तः श्रद्धधानश्च कुर्वन्नाप्नोति तत्फलम् ॥ ९॥

English
An aspirant who has mastered the mantras overcoming the obstacles and who performs the rite with full confidence and conviction attains the fruit. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा तत्फलावाप्त्यै ब्रह्मचर्यरतो भवेत् ।
रात्रौ हविष्यमश्नीयात्पायसं वा फलानि वा ॥ १०॥

English
Or, for the attainment of the benefit let him be scrupulously celibate, eating only the Haviṣya, milk-pudding or fruits. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
हिंसादि यन्निषिद्धं स्यान्न कुर्यान्मनसापि तत् ।
सदा भस्मानुलिप्ताङ्गः सुवेषश्च शुचिर्भवेत् ॥ ११॥

English
He shall not even think of, much less physically do, such prohibited actions as violence. He shall be pure always with clear dress and smear the body with Bhasma always. (11.)

---

Sanskrit
इत्थमाचारवान्भूत्वा स्वानुकूले शुभेऽहनि ।
पूर्वोक्तलक्षणे देशे पुष्पदामाद्यलङ्कृते ॥ १२॥

आलिप्य शकृता भूमिं हस्तमानावरां यथा ।
विलिखेत्कमले भद्रे दीप्यमानं स्वतेजसा ॥ १३॥

तप्तजाम्बूनदमयमष्टपत्रं सकेसरम् ।
मध्ये कर्णिकया युक्तं सर्वरत्नैरलङ्कृतम् ॥ १४॥

स्वाकारसदृशेनैव नालेन च समन्वितम् ।
तादृशे स्वर्णनिर्माणे कन्दे सम्यग्विधानतः ॥ १५॥

तत्राणिमादिकं सर्वं सङ्कल्प्य मनसा पुनः ।
रत्नजं वाथ सौवर्णं स्फटिकं वा सलक्षणम् ।
लिङ्गं सवेदिकं चैव स्थापयित्वा विधानतः ॥ १६॥

तत्रावाह्य यजेद्देवं साम्बं सगणमव्ययम् ।
तत्र माहेश्वरी कल्प्या मूर्तिर्मूर्तिमतः प्रभोः ॥ १७॥

English
After observing the rules of conduct, the devotee shall on an auspicious and favourable day with the characteristics mentioned before, smear the ground with cowdung in a spot bedecked with garlands of flowers and draw the auspicious lotus shining with its own refulgence. It shall be of molten gold with eight petals and filaments, with the pericarp in the middle set with all gems. It shall be not less than a Hasta in width and must have a stalk befitting its size. He shall conceive Animā etc. in the bulbous root made of gold, in accordance with the injunctions. He shall instal the phallic idol with its pedestal, made of gems, gold or crystal with the requisite characteristics. He shall invoke the eternal lord accompanied by Ambā and the Gaṇas. Śiva's form as Maheśvara shall also be conceived in the idol (12-17.)

---

Sanskrit
चतुर्भुजा चतुर्वक्त्रा सर्वाभरणभूषिता ।
शार्दूलचर्मवसना किञ्चिद्विहसितानना ॥ १८॥

वरदाभयहस्ता च मृगटङ्कधरा तथा ।
अथवाष्टभुजा चिन्त्या चिन्तकस्य यथारुचि ॥ १९॥

English
It shall have four arms and four faces. It shall be bedecked in all ornaments. It shall wear the tiger's hide with the smiling face and the gestures of granting boons and of protection to the devotee. Other hands shall hold the deer and the axe. Or if the conceiver desires he can conceive of the Mūrti as having eight arms. (18-19.)

---

Sanskrit
तदा त्रिशूलपरशुखड्गवज्राणि दक्षिणे ।
वामे पाशाङ्कुशौ तद्वत्खेटं नागं च बिभ्रती ॥ २०॥

English
Then the right hand holds the trident, axe, sword and the thunderbolt. The left hands hold the noose, goad, iron club and the serpent. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
बालार्कसदृशप्रख्या प्रतिवक्त्रं त्रिलोचना ।
तस्याः पूर्वमुखं सौम्यं स्वाकारसदृशप्रभम् ॥ २१॥

English
The Mūrti shall have the lustre of the rising sun. It shall have three eyes in every face. The face towards the east is gentle and has the refulgence befitting its size and features. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
दक्षिणं नीलजीमूतसदृशं घोरदर्शनम् ।
उत्तरं विद्रुमप्रख्यं नीलालकविभूषितम् ॥ २२॥

English
The southern face resembles the blue cloud and has a terrific appearance. The northern face has the lustre of coral and is bedecked with dark forelocks. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
पश्चिमं पूर्णचन्द्राभं सौम्यमिन्दुकलाधरम् ।
तदङ्कमण्डलारूढा शक्तिर्माहेश्वरी परा ॥ २३॥

English
The western face is gentle, with the lustre of the full moon. Seated on his lap is the great Śakti of Maheśvara (23.)

---

Sanskrit
महालक्ष्मीरिति ख्याता श्यामा सर्वमनोहरा ।
मूर्तिं कृत्वैवमाकारां सकलीकृत्य च क्रमात् ॥ २४॥

मूर्तिमन्तमथावाह्य यजेत्परमकारणम् ।
स्नानार्थे कल्पयेत्तत्र पञ्चगव्यं तु कापिलम् ॥ २५॥

English
She is famous as Mahālakṣmī dark coloured and wholly charming. After conceiving the Mūrti thus and unifying it into a whole he shall invoke the embodied lord and worship him. For the purpose of ablution he shall arrange for Pañcagavya prepared from a tawny cow. (24-25.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चामृतं च पूर्णानि बीजानि च विशेषतः ।
पुरस्तान्मण्डलं कृत्वा रत्नचूर्णाद्यलङ्कृतम् ॥ २६॥

कर्णिकायां प्रविन्यस्येदीशानकलशं पुनः ।
सद्यादिकलशान्पश्चात्परितस्तस्य कल्पयेत् ॥ २७॥

English
There shall be Pañcāmṛta and particularly the full seeds. He shall prepare the Maṇḍala ahead decorated with gem-dust. The water-pot of Īśāna shall be put in the pericarp. The water-pots of Sadyojāta shall be placed around, afterwards.²⁹⁹ (26-27.)

---

Sanskrit
ततो विद्येशकलशानष्टौ पूर्वादिवत्क्रमात् ।
तीर्थाम्बुपूरितान्कृत्वा सूत्रेणावेष्ट्य पूर्ववत् ॥ २८॥

पुण्यद्रव्याणि निक्षिप्य समन्त्रं सविधानकम् ।
दुकूलाद्येन वस्त्रेण समाच्छाद्य समन्ततः ॥ २९॥

सर्वत्र मन्त्रं विन्यस्य तत्तन्मन्त्रपुरः सरम् ।
स्नानकाले तु सम्प्राप्ते सर्वमङ्गलनिःस्वनैः ॥ ३०॥

English
Eight pots of Vidyeśa shall be filled with the waters of holy centres and threads shall be tied round them. Sacred articles of worship shall be dropped therein with due incantations in accordance with the injunctions. They shall be completely covered into the silken cloth with the mantras. When the proper time arrives he shall perform the ablution of the lord with Pañcagavya. (28-30.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चगव्यादिभिश्चैव स्नापयेत्परमेश्वरम् ।
ततः कुशोदकाद्यानि स्वर्णरत्नोदकान्यपि ॥ ३१॥

गन्धपुष्पादिसिद्धानि मन्त्रसिद्धानि च क्रमात् ।
उद्धृत्योद्धृत्य मन्त्रेण तैस्तैः स्नाप्य महेश्वरम् ॥ ३२॥

गन्धपुष्पादिदीपांश्च पूजाकर्म समाचरेत् ।
पलावरः स्यादालेप एकादशपलोत्तरः ॥ ३३॥

English
Waters dropped from the Darbha grass, golden and gemset vessels, scented and flowery waters purified with the mantras shall be taken from the vessels and Maheśvara shall be bathed. Scents and flowers shall be offered, lights shown and the worship performed. The unguent shall be not less than a Pala by weight and the maximum shall be eleven Palas. (31-33.)

---

Sanskrit
सुवर्णरत्नपुष्पाणि शुभानि सुरभीणि च ।
नीलोत्पलाद्युत्पलानि बिल्वपत्राण्यनेकशः ॥ ३४॥

कमलानि च रक्तानि श्वेतान्यपि च शम्भवे ।
कृष्णागुरूद्भवो धूपः सकर्पूराज्यगुग्गुलः ॥ ३५॥

कपिलाघृतसंसिद्धा दीपाः कर्पूरवर्तिजाः ।
पञ्च ब्रह्मषडङ्गानि पूज्यान्यावरणानि च ॥ ३६॥

English
Auspicious and charming flowers of good colour and fragrance blue and other lilies, plenty of Bilva leaves, red lotuses and white lotuses if available shall be used. The incense shall be offered with the black Aguru. The gum-resin shall be used with the camphor and ghee. The ghee used for lamps shall be of the milk of tawny cows. The five Brahmans, the six Aṅgas and the Āvaraṇas shall be worshipped. (34-36.)

---

Sanskrit
नैवेद्यः पयसा सिद्धः स गुडाज्यो महाचरुः ।
पाटलोत्पलपद्माद्यैः पानीयं च सुगन्धितम् ॥ ३७॥

English
The Naivedya shall be made of Caru prepared in ghee and milk with jaggery. The water shall be rendered fragrant with the Pāṭala flowers, lilies and lotuses. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
पञ्चसौगन्धिकोपेतं ताम्बूलं च सुसंस्कृतम् ।
सुवर्णरत्नसिद्धानि भूषणानि विशेषतः ॥ ३८॥

English
Well-consecrated Tāmbūla along with five fragrant spices shall be offered.³⁰⁰ The ornaments offered shall be made in gold and set with gems. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
वासांसि च विचित्राणि सूक्ष्माणि च नवानि च ।
दर्शनीयानि देयानि गानवाद्यादिभिः सह ॥ ३९॥

English
Freshly woven cloths of various colours and of fine fabric attractive to the sight shall be offered with songs and instrumental music. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
जपश्च मूलमन्त्रस्य लक्षः परमसङ्ख्यया ।
एकावरा त्र्युत्तरा च पूजा फलवशादिह ॥ ४०॥

English
The maximum number of times for Japa is hundred thousand. According to the benefit desired the number of worship varies from one to three. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
दशसङ्ख्यावरो होमः प्रतिद्रव्यं शतोत्तरः ।
घोररूपश्शिवश्चिन्त्यो मारणोच्चाटनादिषु ॥ ४१॥

English
The number of sacrifices is not less than ten for every article and the maximum is hundred. In such rites as Māraṇa and Uccāṭana Śiva shall be conceived terrific in form. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवलिङ्गे शिवाग्नौ च ह्यन्यासु प्रतिमासु च ।
चिन्त्यः सौम्यतनुः शम्भुः कार्ये शान्तिकपौष्टिके ॥ ४२॥

English
When quiescent or nourishing rites are being performed, Śiva shall be thought of as gentle in the phallicimage in the Śiva-fire and in other idols as well. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
आयसौ स्रुक्स्रुवौ कार्यौ मारणादिसु कर्मसु ।
तदन्यत्र तु सौवर्णौ शान्तिकाद्येषु कृत्स्नशः ॥ ४३॥

English
In Māraṇa and other rites the Śruk and Śruva shall be made of iron. In all other rites such as quiescent these shall be made of gold. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
दूर्वया घृतगोक्षीरमिश्रया मधुना तथा ।
चरुणा सघृतेनैव केवलं पयसापि वा ॥ ४४॥

जुहुयान्मृत्युविजये तिलै रोगोपशान्तये ।
घृतेन पयसा चैव कमलैर्वाथ केवलैः ॥ ४५॥

समृद्धिकामो जुहुयान्महादारिद्र्यशान्तये ।
जातीपुष्पेण वश्यार्थी जुहुयात्सघृतेन तु ॥ ४६॥

English
In the rite of Mṛtyuñjaya the Homa is performed with Dūrvā mixed with ghee and cow's milk, or honey or with Caru along with ghee, or milk alone. For the rites intended to quell ailments to attain prosperity and subdue poverty, gingelly seeds, ghee, milk or lotus-flowers shall be used. Persons seeking to bring persons under control shall perform Homa with Jātī flowers and ghee. (44-46.)

---

Sanskrit
घृतेन करवीरैश्च कुर्यादाकर्षणं द्विजः ।
तैलेनोच्चाटनं कुर्यात्स्तम्भनं मधुना पुनः ॥ ४७॥

English
A Brahmin shall perform the rite of Ākarṣaṇa with ghee and Karavīra flowers, Uccāṭana with oil and Stambhana with honey. (47.)

---

Sanskrit
स्तम्भनं सर्षपेणापि लशुनेन तु पातनम् ।
ताडनं रुधिरेण स्यात्खरस्योष्ट्रस्य चोभयोः ॥ ४८॥

English
Stambhana rite can be performed with mustard also; Pātana with garlic; Tāḍana with the blood of mule, camel or of both. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
मारणोच्चाटने कुर्याद्रोहिबीजैस्तिलान्वितैः ।
विद्वेषणं च तैलेन कुर्याल्लाङ्गलकस्य तु ॥ ४९॥

English
Māraṇa and Uccāṭana shall be performed with Rohi and gingelly seeds. Vidveṣaṇa rite shall be performed with the oil of Lāṅgala. (49.)

---

Sanskrit
बन्धनं रोहिबीजेन सेनास्तम्भनमेव च ।
रक्तसर्षपसम्मिश्रैर्होमद्रव्यैरशेषतः ॥ ५०॥

हस्तयन्त्रोद्भवैस्तैलैर्जुहुयादाभिचारिके ।
कटुकीतुषसंयुक्तैः कार्पासास्थिभिरेव च ॥ ५१॥

सर्षपैस्तैलसम्मिश्रैर्जुहुयादाभिचारिके ।
ज्वरोपशान्तिदं क्षीरं सौभाग्यफलदं तथा ॥ ५२॥

English
Bandhana rites and the rite of paralysing of a vast army shall be performed with the seeds of Rohi. In Ābhicārika rites the articles of Homa shall be used with the red mustard and the Homa shall be performed with oils extracted from manually operated machines. It can also be performed with seeds of cotton in combination with Kaṭukī and husk. In Ābhicārika rites the devotee shall perform Homa with mustard seeds mixed with oil. Milk yields the subdual of fevers and is conducive to good fortune. (50-52.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वसिद्धिकरो होमः क्षौद्राज्यदधिभिर्युतैः ।
क्षीरेण तन्दुलैश्चैव चरुणा केवलेन वा ॥ ५३॥

English
Homa offered with honey, ghee, curds, milk and rice-grains or with Caru grants all desires. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
शान्तिकं पौष्टिकं वापि सप्तभिः समिदादिभिः ।
द्रव्यैर्विशेषतो होमे वश्यमाकर्षणं तथा ॥ ५४॥

English
Quiescent, nourishing, Vaśya and Ākarṣaṇa rites shall be performed with the seven articles of worship sacrificial twigs etc. (54.)

English
Vaśya, Ākarṣaṇa and Śrīpada (glorious position) rites shall be performed with the leaves of Bilva for Havana. It yields conquest over the enemies. (55.)

Sanskrit
समिधः शान्तिकार्येषु पालाशखदिरादिकाः ।
करवीरार्कजाः क्रौर्ये कण्टकिन्यश्च विग्रहे ॥ ५६॥

English
In quiescent rites the twigs of Palāśa, Khadira etc. shall be used. In rites of cruelty the twigs of Karavīra and Arka shall be used. Twigs of thorny trees shall be used in the rite of war. (56.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रशान्तः शान्तिकं कुर्यात्पौष्टिकं च विशेषतः ।

English
A quiet man shall perform the quiescent and nourishing rites particularly. A ruthless man with angry mind shall perform Abhicarika rites. (57.)

---

Sanskrit
अतीव दुरवस्थायां प्रतीकारान्तरं न चेत् ।
आततायिनमुद्दिश्य प्रकुर्यादाभिचारिकम् ॥ ५८॥

English
When the harassment is unbearable and condition is pitiable, when there is no other remedy, only then shall one perform the Abhicārika rite against desperadoes. (58.)

---

Sanskrit

English
No virtuous man, occupying honourable position or otherwise shall perform the Abhicārika rite against the ruler of his country. (59.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Even if he happens to be a desperado one shall not perform Abhicārika rite against a person who has sought refuge in Śiva, mentally, verbally or physically. (60.)

---

Sanskrit

कृत्वाभिचारिकं कर्म सद्यो विनिपतेन्नरः ॥ ६१॥

English
A man performing Abhicārika rite against the ruler of his country whether he be a devotee of Śiva or not, shall have a fall immediately. (61.)

---

Sanskrit
स्वराष्ट्रपालकं तस्माच्छिवभक्तं च कञ्चन ।

English
If he wishes for his own happiness he shall not perform Abhicārika rite against the protector of his country or against any devotee of Śiva. (62.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If he performs the rite of Māraṇa etc. against any other person he shall perform expiatory rites after due repentance. (63.)

---

Sanskrit

English
Whether the devotee is rich or poor he shall worship the lord in a Bāṇa or selfborn or Ārṣaka or a Vaidika liṅga. (64.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If liṅgas of gold and gems are not available or if he is unable to possess them he shall perform the worship mentally or with substitute articles. (65.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If a devotee can perform worship in some part he too derives the benefit of that part in accordance with his ability. (66.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If the benefit is not seen even if the rite is performed, it shall be repeated twice or thrice. By all means the benefit will be received. (67.)

Sanskrit

English
Whatever material is used in worship, gold, gem and other articles shall be handed to the preceptor apart from the Daksiṇā offered separately. (68.)

---

Sanskrit

English
If the preceptor does not wish to receive, the entire gift shall be given to Śiva or the devotees of Śiva. It shall not be given to any other. (69.)

Sanskrit

English
If any one performs the rite himself without the help of a preceptor the procedure shall be the same. He shall not appropriate the gift to himself. If he appropriates the articles of worship for himself out of greed under delusion he will not attain his desire. (70-71.)

Sanskrit

English
The liṅga used for worship may be taken by himself or given to others. If he takes that himself he shall worship it everyday or cause it to be worshipped on his behalf. (72.)

Sanskrit

(77-79.)
