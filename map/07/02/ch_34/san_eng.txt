### **CHAPTER THIRTYFOUR - (Delusion of Viṣṇu and Brahmā)**

---

Sanskrit
३४

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
नित्यनैमित्तिकात्काम्याद्या सिद्धिरिह कीर्तिता ।
सा सर्वा लभ्येत सद्यो लिङ्गबेरप्रतिष्ठया ॥ १॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  The achievement of benefit that has been mentioned here from the Nitya, Naimittika and Kāmya rites can entirely be secured immediately by installing the phallic and the bodily image of Śiva. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
सर्वो लिङ्गमयो लोकः सर्वं लिङ्गे प्रतिष्ठितम् ।
तस्मात्प्रतिष्ठिते लिङ्गे भवेत्सर्वं पतिष्ठितम् ॥ २॥

English
The world is in the form of Liṅga. Everything is founded on Liṅga. Hence if the Liṅga is installed, everything is installed. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
ब्रह्मणा विष्णुना वापि रुद्रेणान्येन केन वा ।
लिङ्गप्रतिष्ठामुत्सृज्य क्रियते स्वपदस्थितिः ॥ ३॥

English
It is only by resorting to the installation of the Liṅga that Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra or other deities maintain their splendour. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
किमन्यदिह वक्तव्यं प्रतिष्ठां प्रति कारणम् ।
पर्तिष्ठितं शिवेनापि लिङ्गं वैश्वेश्वरं यतः ॥ ४॥

English
What more reason can be advanced for the installation of Liṅga than that Liṅga of Viśveśvara has been installed by Śiva too? (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वप्रयत्नेन परत्रेह च शर्मणे ।
स्थापयेत्परमेशस्य लिङ्गं बेरमथापि वा ॥ ५॥

English
Hence by all means one shall instal the phallic or the bodily image of Śiva for his welfare here and hereafter. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
श्रीकृष्ण उवाच ।
किमिदं लिङ्गमाख्यातं कथं लिङ्गी महेश्वरः ।
कथं च लिङ्गभावोऽस्य कस्मादस्मिञ्छिवोऽर्च्यते ॥ ६॥

English
Śrī Kṛṣṇa said :—  What is a Liṅga? How is lord Śiva a Liṅgin? How did he have the status of a Liṅga? Why is he worshipped in it? (6.)

---

Sanskrit
उपमन्युरुवाच ।
अव्यक्तं लिङ्गमाख्यातं त्रिगुणप्रभवाप्ययम् ।
अनाद्यनन्तं विश्वस्य यदुपादानकारणम् ॥ ७॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  The unmanifest is called the Liṅga. It is the source of attributes as well as that wherein the universe merges and dissolves. It has neither beginning nor end. It is the material cause of the universe. (7.)

---

Sanskrit
तदेव मूलप्रकृतिर्माया च गगनात्मिका ।
तत एव समुत्पन्नं जगदेतच्चराचरम् ॥ ८॥

English
It is the Māyā, the Mūlaprakṛti as extensive as the firmament. This universe including the mobile and immobile beings is born of that. (8.)

---

Sanskrit
अशुद्धं चैव शुद्धं यच्छुद्धाशुद्धं च तत्त्रिधा ।
ततः शिवो महेशश्च रुद्रो विष्णुः पितामहः ॥ ९॥

English
The universe is of three types: the impure, pure and the pure-impure. From the Liṅga are born Śiva, Maheśa, Rudra, Viṣṇu and Brahmā. (9.)

---

Sanskrit
भूतानि चेन्द्रियैर्जाता लीयन्तेऽत्र शिवाज्ञया ।
अत एव शिवो लिङ्गो लिङ्गमाज्ञापयेद्यतः ॥ १०॥

English
The Bhūtas with the sense-organs merge into this at the bidding of Śiva. He is Liṅgin since he commands the Liṅga. (10.)

---

Sanskrit
यतो न तदनाज्ञातं कार्याय प्रभवेत्स्वतः ।
ततो जातस्य विश्वस्य तत्रैव विलयो यतः ॥ ११॥

अनेन लिङ्गता तस्य भवेन्नान्येन केनचित् ।
लिङ्गं च शिवयोर्देहस्ताभ्यां यस्मादधिष्ठितम् ॥ १२॥

English
Since the Liṅga can do nothing by itself without being commanded by Śiva, since the dissolution of the universe born of it is only within it, this constitutes the state of being Liṅga in Śiva and not due to any other reason. The Liṅga is the body of Śiva and Śivā because it is presided over by them. (11-12.)

---

Sanskrit
अतस्तत्र शिवः साम्बो नित्यमेव समर्चयेत् ।
लिङ्गवेदी महादेवी लिङ्गं साक्षान्महेश्वरः ॥ १३॥

English
Hence Śiva is worshipped therein for ever along with Śivā. The goddess is the pedestal of the Liṅga and Liṅga is lord Śiva himself. (13.)

---

Sanskrit
तयोः सम्पूजनादेव स च सा च समर्चितौ ।
न तयोर्लिङ्गदेहत्वं विद्यते परमार्थतः ॥ १४॥

English
By the worship of Liṅga alone, Śiva and Śivā are worshipped. Their having the Liṅga for their body is not the ultimate reality. (14.)

---

Sanskrit
यतस्त्वेतौ विशुद्धौ तौ देहस्तदुपचारतः ।
तदेव परमा शक्तिः शिवस्य परमात्मनः ॥ १५॥

शक्तिराज्ञां यदादत्ते प्रसूते तच्चराचरम् ।
न तस्य महिमा शक्यो वक्तुं वर्षशतैरपि ॥ १६॥

English
Since they are pure it is their body only in a secondary sense. That is the great Śakti of Śiva the great Ātman. Śakti at the behest of Śiva gives birth to mobile and immobile beings. One cannot describe the glory of the Liṅga, even in hundreds of years, by which Brahmā and Viṣṇu were deluded at the outset. (15-16.)

---

Sanskrit
येनादौ मोहितौ स्यातां ब्रह्मनारायणावपि ।
पुरा त्रिभुवनस्यास्य प्रलये समुपस्थिते ॥ १७॥

वारिशय्यागतो विष्णुः सुष्वापानाकुलः सुखम् ।
यदृच्छया गतस्तत्र ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः ॥ १८॥

ददर्श पुण्डरीकाक्षं स्वपन्तं तमनाकुलम् ।
मायया मोहितः शम्भोर्विष्णुमाह पितामहः ॥ १९॥

English
Formerly when the universe was in the state of dissolution, Viṣṇu lying on his couch in the waters went into deep slumber. He lay comfortably asleep. Brahmā the grandfather of the worlds casually went there. He saw the lotus-eyed Viṣṇu sleeping undisturbedly. Deluded by the Māyā of Śiva, Brahmā said to Viṣṇu. (17-19.)

---

Sanskrit
कस्त्वं वदेत्यमर्षेण प्रहृत्योत्थाप्य माधवम् ।
स तु हस्तप्रहारेण तीव्रेणाभिहतः क्षणात् ॥ २०॥

प्रबुद्धोत्थाय शयनाद्ददर्श परमेष्ठिनम् ।
तमाह चान्तः सङ्क्रुद्धः स्वयमक्रुद्धवद्धरिः ॥ २१॥

English
“Who are you? Tell me”. O saying he hit Viṣṇu angrily and wakened him. Struck by the blow of his hand Viṣṇu woke up in a trice and got up from his bed. He saw Brahmā there. Viṣṇu though infuriated within addressed him politely. (20-21.)

---

Sanskrit
कुतस्त्वमागतो वत्स कस्मात्त्वं व्याकुलो वद ।
इति विष्णुवचः श्रुत्वा प्रभुत्वगुणसूचकम् ॥ २२॥

रजसा बद्धवैरस्तं ब्रह्मा पुनरभाषत ।
वत्सेति मां कुतो ब्रूषे गुरुः शिष्यमिवात्मनः ॥ २३॥

मां न जानासि किं नाथं प्रपञ्चो यस्य मे कृतिः ।
त्रिधात्मानं विभज्येदं सृष्ट्वाथ परिपाल्यते ॥ २४॥

संहरामि नमे कश्चित्स्रष्टा जगति विद्यते ।
इत्युक्ते सति सोऽप्याह ब्रह्माणं विष्णुरव्ययः ॥ २५॥

English
“O dear son, whence have you come? Why are you agitated? Tell me.” On hearing the words of Viṣṇu, indicative of his lordly attitude Brahmā felt irritated due to his Rājasic qualities and spoke again. “Why do you address me as ‘dear son’ like a preceptor calling his disciple? Don’t you know that I am the lord. This universe is my creation. After dividing myself into three, I create, protect and annihilate. There is none in the universe who has created me.” When thus addressed Viṣṇu said to Brahmā. (22-25.)

---

Sanskrit
अहमेवादिकर्तास्य हर्ता च परिपालकः ।
भवानपि ममैवाङ्गादवतीर्णः पुराव्ययात् ॥ २६॥

मन्नियोगात्त्वमात्मानं त्रिधा कृत्वा जगत्त्रयम् ।
सृजस्यवसि चान्ते तत्पुनः प्रतिसृजस्यपि ॥ २७॥

विस्मृतोऽसि जगन्नाथं नारायणमनामयम् ।
तवापि जनकं साक्षान्मामेवमवमन्यसे ॥ २८॥

तवापराधो नास्त्यत्र भ्रान्तोऽसि मम मायया ।
मत्प्रसादादियं भ्रान्तिरपैष्यति तवाचिरात् ॥ २९॥

श‍ृणु सत्यं चतुर्वक्त्र सर्वदेवेश्वरो ह्यहम् ।
कर्ता भर्ता च हर्ता च न मयास्ति समो विभुः ॥ ३०॥

English
“I am the primordial creator of this universe, the protector and the annihilator. You too, O sir, were born of me formerly. At my unthwartable bidding you divided yourself into three and began creation. You create the three worlds, protect, dissolve and re-create it. You forget Viṣṇu the lord of the universe free from ailments. You insult even me, your father. It is not your fault. You have been deluded by my Māyā. Due to my favour this delusion of yours will disappear ere long. O Brahmā, listen to the truth. I am the lord of all gods. I am the creator, sustainer and annihilator. There is no other lord equal to me." (26-30.)

---

Sanskrit
एवमेव विवादोऽभूद् ब्रह्मविष्ण्वोः परस्परम् ।
अभवच्च महायुद्धं भैरवं रोमहर्षणम् ॥ ३१॥

English
A verbal dispute between Brahma and Visnu arose thus. Thereafter a terrible fight ensued causing horripilation. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
मुष्टिभिर्न्निघ्नतोस्तीव्रं रजसा बद्धवैरयोः ।
तयोर्दर्पापहाराय प्रबोधाय च देवयोः ॥ ३२॥

मध्ये समाविरभवल्लिङ्गमैश्वरमद्भुतम् ।
ज्वालामालासहस्राढ्यमप्रमेयमनौपमम् ॥ ३३॥

क्षयवृद्धिविनिर्मुक्तमादिमध्यान्तवर्जितम् ।
तस्य ज्वालासहस्रेण ब्रह्मविष्णू विमोहितौ ॥ ३४॥

विसृज्य युद्धं किं त्वेतदित्यचिन्तयतां तदा ।
न तयोस्तस्य याथात्म्यं प्रबुद्धमभवद्यदा ॥ ३५॥

तदा समुद्यतौ स्यातां तस्याद्यन्तं परीक्षितुम् ॥ ३६॥

English
Due to Rajasic quality they fought and hit each other with fists. In order to dispel their arrogance and to enlighten them, the wonderful Linga of the lord appeared in between them. It had thousands of flames. It was incomprehensible and incomparable. It did not increase or decrease. It had no beginning, no middle, no end. Brahma and Visnu were enchanted by thousands of flames. Ceasing from their fight they began to think "What is this?" When they could not understand the reality they attempted to survey its beginning and end. (32-36.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र हंसाकृतिर्ब्रह्मा विश्वतः पक्षसंयुतः ।
मनोऽनिलजवो भूत्वा गतस्तूर्ध्वं प्रयत्नतः ॥ ३७॥

English
Brahma took the form of a swan with wings all round and went up assiduously with the speed of the mind or wind. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
नारायणोऽपि विश्वात्मा नीलाञ्जनचयोपमम् ।
वाराहममितं रूपमास्थाय गतवानधः ॥ ३८॥

English
Visnu, the Atman of the universe, assumed the form of a Boar resembling a mass of collyrium and went downwards. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
एवं वर्षसहस्रं तु त्वरन् विष्णुरधोगतः ।
नापश्यदल्पमप्यस्य मूलं लिङ्गस्य सूकरः ॥ ३९॥

English
Thus hastening down for a thousand years he did not have even a glimpse of the root of Linga. (39.)

---

Sanskrit
तावत्कालं गतश्चोर्ध्वं तस्यान्तं ज्ञातुमिच्छया ।
श्रान्तोऽत्यन्तमदृष्ट्वान्तं पापताधः पितामहः ॥ ४०॥

English
Meanwhile, Brahma too was going up to know his top. But unable to see the end he became exhausted and fell down. (40.)

---

Sanskrit
तथैव भगवान् विष्णुः श्रान्तः संविग्नलोचनः ।
क्लेशेन महता तूर्णमधस्तादुत्थितोऽभवत् ॥ ४१॥

English
Similarly the weary Visnu with dejected mind due to the great distress quickly rose up from below. (41.)

---

Sanskrit
समागतावथान्योन्यं विस्मयस्मेरवीक्षणौ ।
मायया मोहितौ शम्भोः कृत्याकृत्यं न जग्मतुः ॥ ४२॥

English
When they met each other again they were completely perplexed and stared at each other with a smile of shame. Deluded by the Maya of Siva they did not know what to do or what not to do. (42.)

---

Sanskrit
पृष्ठतः पार्श्वतस्तस्य चाग्रतश्च स्थितावुभौ ।
प्रणिपत्य किमात्मेदमित्यचिन्तयतां तदा ॥ ४३॥

English
They stood in front, at the back and on either side of it and bowed. They began to think “Of what nature is this ?” (43.)
