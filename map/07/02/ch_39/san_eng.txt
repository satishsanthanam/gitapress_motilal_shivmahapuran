### **CHAPTER THIRTYNINE - (The Śaivite Yoga)**

---

Sanskrit
३९

उपमन्युरुवाच ।
श्रीकण्ठनाथं स्मरतां सद्यः सर्वार्थसिद्धयः ।
प्रसिध्यन्तीति मत्वैके तं वै ध्यायन्ति योगिनः ॥ १॥

English
Upamanyu said :—  Some Yogins perform meditation with the full conviction that Siddhis are immediately acquired by those who remember the lord. (1.)

---

Sanskrit
स्थित्यर्थं मनसः केचित्स्थूलध्यानं प्रकुर्वते ।
स्थूले तु निश्चलं चेतो भवेत्सूक्ष्मे तु तत्स्थिरम् ॥ २॥

English
In order to steady the mind some perform meditation on the gross form. The mind that is fixed on the gross form becomes stable in the subtle form. (2.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवे तु चिन्तिते साक्षात्सर्वाः सिध्यन्ति सिद्धयः ।
मूर्त्यन्तरेषु ध्यातेषु शिवरूपं विचिन्तयेत् ॥ ३॥

English
When Śiva is directly meditated upon, Siddhis are achieved. Even when the other forms are meditated upon the devotee shall think of the form of Śiva also. (3.)

---

Sanskrit
लक्षयेन्मनसः स्थैर्यं तत्तद्ध्यायेत्पुनः पुनः ।
ध्यानमादौ सविषयं ततो निर्विषयं जगुः ॥ ४॥

English
He shall observe the steadiness of the mind and frequently meditate. This meditation, they say, has at the outset a specific object. Thereafter it becomes devoid of any specific object. (4.)

---

Sanskrit
तत्र निर्विषयं ध्यानं नास्तीत्येव सतां मतम् ।
बुद्धेर्हि सन्ततिः काचिद्ध्यानमित्यभिधीयते ॥ ५॥

English
Philosophers say that there is no Nirviṣaya meditation. They hold that a series of intellectual vision is called meditation. (5.)

---

Sanskrit
तेन निर्विषया बुद्धिः केवलेह प्रवर्तते ।
तस्मात्सविषयं ध्यानं बालार्ककिरणाश्रयम् ॥ ६॥

सूक्ष्माश्रयं निर्विषयं नापरं परमार्थतः ।
यद्वा सविषयं ध्यानं तत्साकारसमाश्रयम् ॥ ७॥

निराकारात्मसंवित्तिर्ध्यानं निर्विषयं मतम् ।
निर्बीजं च सबीजं च तदेव ध्यानमुच्यते ॥ ८॥

निराकारश्रयत्वेन साकाराश्रयतस्तथा ।
तस्मात्सविषयं ध्यानमादौ कृत्वा सबीजकम् ॥ ९॥

English
But the intellectual vision by itself without an object shall also function. Therefore the fact is—the Saviṣaya meditation is on the lord who is conceived as brilliant as the sun. The meditation on the subtle form is Nirviṣaya. Saviṣaya meditation has a definite form in view. Meditation of the formless is the Nirviṣaya meditation. The two are called Nirbija and Sabija also. Hence the practiser shall at the outset perform the Saviṣaya or Sabija meditation and in the end perform Nirbija or Nirviṣaya meditation. (6-9.)

---

Sanskrit
अन्ते निर्विषयं कुर्यान्निर्बीजं सर्वसिद्धये ।
प्राणायामेन सिध्यन्ति देव्याः शान्त्यादयः क्रमात् ॥ १०॥

शान्तिः प्रशान्तिर्दीप्तिश्च प्रसादश्च ततः परम् ।
शमः सर्वापदां चैव शान्तिरित्यभिधीयते ॥ ११॥

तमसोऽन्तर्बहिर्नाशः प्रशान्तिः परिगीयते ।
बहिरन्तःप्रकाशो यो दीप्तिरित्यभिधीयते ॥ १२॥

स्वस्थता या तु सा बुद्धेः प्रसादः परिकीर्तितः ।
कारणानि च सर्वाणि सबाह्याभ्यन्तराणि च ॥ १३॥

English
The benefits derived from Prāṇayāma are Śānti, Praśānti, Dipti and Prasāda. When adversities subside it is called Śānti. Praśānti is the destruction of ignorance both external and internal. The external and internal illumination is called Dipti. The normal and the healthy state of the intellect is called Prasāda. When the intellect is in healthy normal state the internal and external sense-organs too acquire healthy and normal state. (10-13.)

---

Sanskrit
बुद्धेः प्रसादतः क्षिप्रं प्रसन्नानि भवन्त्युत ।
ध्याता ध्यानं तथा ध्येयं यद्वा ध्यानप्रयोजनम् ।
एतच्चतुष्टयं ज्ञात्वा ध्याता ध्यानं समाचरेत् ॥ १४॥

ज्ञानवैराग्यसम्पन्नो नित्यमव्यग्रमानसः ।
श्रद्दधानः प्रसन्नात्मा ध्याता सद्भिरुदाहृतः ॥ १५॥

ध्यै चिन्तायां स्मृतो धातुः शिवचिन्ता मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ १६॥

योगाभ्यासस्तथाल्पोऽपि यथा पापं विनाशयेत् ।
ध्यायतः क्षणमात्रं वा श्रद्धया परमेश्वरम् ॥ १७॥

अव्याक्षिप्तेन मनसा ध्यानमित्यभिधीयते ॥ १८॥

English
The meditator shall perform meditation after realising the four : viz. the meditator, meditation, the object of meditation and the benefits of meditation. The meditator as defined by good men shall be richly endowed with knowledge and detachment. His mind shall never be excited. He shall have faith and his Ātman shall remain delighted. The root Dhyai means to contemplate. Frequent contemplation on Śiva with even a little practice of Yoga shall quell sins of the person who meditates on the lord with faith and unexcited mind. (14-18.)

---

Sanskrit
बुद्धिप्रवाहरूपस्य ध्यानस्यास्यावलम्बनम् ।
ध्येयमित्युच्यते सद्भिस्तच्च साम्बः स्वयं शिवः ॥ १९॥

English
The object of meditation on the form of intellectual visions is Dhyeya and that is Śiva himself accompanied by Śivā. (19.)

---

Sanskrit
विमुक्तिप्रत्ययं पूर्णमैश्वर्यं चाणिमादिकम् ।
शिवध्यानस्य पूर्णस्य साक्षादुक्तं प्रयोजनम् ॥ २०॥

English
The experience of salvation and the perfect Aṇimā etc. is the direct fruit of meditation on Śiva. (20.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मात्सौख्यं च मोक्षं च ध्यानादुभयमाप्नुयात् ।
तस्मात्सर्वं परित्यज्य ध्यानयुक्तो भवेन्नरः ॥ २१॥

English
Man shall eschew everything and be engaged in meditation since he will be having both happiness and salvation from the practice of meditation. (21.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्ति ध्यानं विना ज्ञानं नास्ति ध्यानमयोगिनः ।
ध्यानं ज्ञानं च यस्यास्ति तीर्णस्तेन भवार्णवः ॥ २२॥

English
Knowledge cannot be attained without meditation. A non-yogin cannot have meditation. The ocean of worldly existence is crossed by the person who has both meditation and knowledge. (22.)

---

Sanskrit
ज्ञानं प्रसन्नमेकाग्रमशेषोपाधिवर्जितम् ।
योगाभ्यासेन युक्तस्य योगिनस्वेव सिध्यति ॥ २३॥

English
The clear and single-centred knowledge devoid of all conditioning factors can be achieved only by a Yogin who regularly practises Yoga. (23.)

---

Sanskrit
प्रक्षीणाशेषपापानां ज्ञाने ध्याने भवेन्मतिः ।
पापोपहतबुद्धीनां तद्वार्तापि सुदुर्लभा ॥ २४॥

English
The minds of only those whose sins have been entirely quelled become inclined towards knowledge and meditation. Those whose intellects have been defiled by sins find it wholly inaccessible. (24.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा वह्निर्महादीप्तः शुष्कमार्द्रं च निर्दहेत् ।
तथा शुभाशुभं कर्म ध्यानाग्निर्दहते क्षणात् ॥ २५॥

English
Just as the blazing fire burns both the dry and the wet twigs, so also the fire of meditation burns in a trice both the auspicious and the inauspicious Karmans. (25.)

---

Sanskrit
अत्यल्पोऽपि यथा दीपः सुमहन्नाशयेत्तमः ।
योगाभ्यासस्तथाल्पोऽपि महापापं विनाशयेत् ॥ २६॥

English
Just as even a modicum of light dispels darkness so also even the slightest practice of Yoga destroys great sins. (26.)

---

Sanskrit
ध्यायतः क्षणमात्रं वा श्रद्धया परमेश्वरम् ।
यद्भवेत् सुमहच्छ्रेयस्तस्यान्तो नैव विद्यते ॥ २७॥

English
There is no limit to the benefits acquired by one who meditates on the lord with faith even for a moment. (27.)

---

Sanskrit
नास्ति ध्यानसमं तीर्थं नास्ति ध्यानसमं तपः ।
नास्ति ध्यानसमो यज्ञस्तस्माद्ध्यानं समाचरेत् ॥ २८॥

English
No holy centre is so efficacious as meditation; no penance, no sacrifice is equal to it. Hence one shall perform meditation strenuously. (28.)

---

Sanskrit
तीर्थानि तोयपूर्णानि देवान्पाषाणमृण्मयान् ।
योगिनो न प्रपद्यन्ते स्वात्मप्रत्ययकारणात् ॥ २९॥

English
Yogins do not resort to holy centres full of waters nor to deities made of stone or clay because they have belief only in their Ātmans. (29.)

---

Sanskrit
योगिनां च वपुः सूक्ष्मं भवेत्प्रत्यक्षमैश्वरम् ।
यथा स्थूलमयुक्तानां मृत्काष्ठाद्यैः प्रकल्पितम् ॥ ३०॥

English
Just as the gross form of the lord fashioned out of clay or wood is observed by the non-yogins so also his subtle form can be perceived by the Yogins. (30.)

---

Sanskrit
यथेहान्तश्चरा राज्ञः प्रियाः स्युर्न बहिश्चराः ।
तथान्तर्ध्याननिरताः प्रियाश्शम्भोर्न कर्मिणः ॥ ३१॥

English
Just as in the Royal household, the interior officials not the workers outside are the favourites of kings so also those who are engaged in inner meditation are the favourites of lord Śiva and not those who perform holy rites. (31.)

---

Sanskrit
बहिष्करा यथा लोके नातीव फलभोगिनः ।
दृष्ट्वा नरेन्द्रभवने तद्वदत्रापि कर्मिणः ॥ ३२॥

English
Just as the exterior workers do not enjoy the pleasures in the royal palace, the same is the case with the Karmins. (32.)

---

Sanskrit
यद्यन्तरा विपद्यन्ते ज्ञानयोगार्थमुद्यतः ।
योगस्योद्योगमात्रेण रुद्रलोकं गमिष्यति ॥ ३३॥

English
If a person in his attempt for knowledge and Yoga were to die in the middle he shall go to Rudraloka even due to his mere endeavour for yoga. (33.)

---

Sanskrit
अनुभूय सुखं तत्र स जातो योगिनां कुले ।
ज्ञानयोगं पुनर्लब्ध्वा संसारमतिवर्तते ॥ ३४॥

English
He enjoys happiness here and is reborn in the family of a Yogin. Attaining knowledge and Yoga or the path of knowledge he transcends the worldly existence. (34.)

---

Sanskrit
जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य यां गतिं लभते नरः ।
न तां गतिमवाप्नोति सर्वैरपि महामखैः ॥ ३५॥

English
Even by performing sacrifices, that goal is not obtained which a man with the desire for the knowledge of Yoga attains. (35.)

---

Sanskrit
द्विजानां वेदविदुषां कोटिं सम्पूज्य यत्फलम् ।
भिक्षामात्रप्रदानेन तत्फलं शिवयोगिने ॥ ३६॥

English
The fruit derivable by worshipping a crore of brahmins can be attained by giving alms alone to a Siva Yogin. (36.)

---

Sanskrit
यज्ञाग्निहोत्रदानेषु तीर्थहोमेषु यत्फलम् ।
योगिनामन्नदानेन तत्समस्तं फलं लभेत् ॥ ३७॥

English
By giving cooked rice to him the benefits of sacrifices, Agnihotras, charitable gifts and pilgrimages can be secured. (37.)

---

Sanskrit
ये चापवादं कुर्वन्ति विमूढाः शिवयोगिनाम् ।
श्रोतृभिस्ते प्रपद्यन्ते नरकेष्वामहीक्षयात् ॥ ३८॥

English
Those who disparage Sivayogins under delusion undergo sufferings in hells along with those who listen, till the dissolution of the world. (38.)

---

Sanskrit
सति श्रोतरि वक्ता स्यादपवादस्य योगिनाम् ।
तस्माच्छ्रोता च पापीयान्दण्ड्यः सुमहतां मतः ।
ये पुनः सततं भक्त्या भजन्ति शवयोगिनः ॥ ३९॥

ते विदन्ति महाभोगानन्ते योगं च शाङ्करम् ।
भोगार्थिभिर्नरैस्तस्मात्सम्पूज्याः शिवयोगिनः ॥ ४०॥

प्रतिश्रयान्नपानाद्यैः शय्याप्रावरणादिभिः ।
योगधर्मः ससारत्वादभेद्यः पापमुद्गरैः ॥ ४१॥

वज्रतन्दुलवज्ज्ञेयं तथा पापेन योगिनः ।
न लिप्यन्ते च तापौघैः पद्मपत्रं यथाम्भसा ॥ ४२॥

English
Only when there is some listener, does a person disparage the Yogin. Hence the listener too is a sinner. Those who worship Sivayogins attain pleasures here and salvation hereafter. Hence, Sivayogins shall be honoured and revered by those who seek worldly pleasures, giving them asylum, food stuffs and drinks, beds and blankets. The Yogic virtue cannot be smashed by the iron clubs of sins. It is very strong and shall be considered to possess adamantine fibres. Yogins are not smeared by sins like the lotus leaf not affected by water. (39-42.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्मिन्देशे वसेन्नित्यं शिवयोगरतो मुनिः ।
सोऽपि देशो भवेत्पूतः स पूत इति किं पुनः ॥ ४३॥

English
Even the land where the sage engaged in Sivayoga resides is hallowed and sacred, let alone Sivayogin himself. (43.)

---

Sanskrit
तस्मात्सर्वं परित्यज्य कृत्यमन्यद्विचक्षणः ।
सर्वदुःखप्रहाणाय शिवयोगं समभ्यसेत् ॥ ४४॥

English
Hence a shrewd and efficient man shall eschew all activities and practise Sivayoga in order to quell miseries. (44.)

---

Sanskrit
सिद्धयोगफलो योगी लोकानां हितकाम्यया ।
भोगान्भुक्त्वा यथाकामं विहरेद्वात्र वर्तताम् ॥ ४५॥

English
A Yogin who has achieved the fruits of Yoga may sport about after enjoying the pleasures as he wishes or shall remain here performing the requisite services. (45.)

---

Sanskrit
अथवा क्षुद्रमित्येव मत्वा वैषयिकं सुखम् ।
त्यक्त्वा विरागयोगेन स्वेच्छया कर्म मुच्यताम् ॥ ४६॥

English
Or let him consider worldly pleasures worthless and eschew them. Due to detachment let him abandon rites and be liberated. (46.)

---

Sanskrit
यस्त्वासन्नां मृतिं मर्त्यो दृष्ट्वारिष्टं च भूयसा ।
स योगारम्भनिरतः शिवक्षेत्रं समाश्रयेत् ॥ ४७॥

English
Or seeing evil portents and realising death as imminent the Yogin engaged in the practice of Yoga shall resort to a Śaivite holy centre or temple.$^{303}$ (47.)

---

Sanskrit
स तत्र निवसन्नेव यदि धीरमना नरः ।
प्राणान् विनापि रोगाद्यैः स्वयमेव परित्यजेत् ॥ ४८॥

English
If he has courage enough he shall abandon his life there voluntarily even without ailments. (48.)

---

Sanskrit
कृत्वाप्यनशनं चैव हुत्वा चाङ्गं शिवानले ।
क्षिप्त्वा वा शिवतीर्थेषु स्वदेहमवगाहनात् ॥ ४९॥

शिवशास्त्रोक्तविधिवत्प्राणान्यस्तु परित्यजेत् ।
सद्य एव विमुच्येत नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥ ५०॥

English
He who voluntarily forsakes his life as prescribed in Śaivite scriptures, by observing fast, or by consigning his body to Śiva-fire, or by plunging into Śaivite holy rivers, shall immediately be liberated. (49-50.)

---

Sanskrit
रोगाद्यैर्वाथ विवशः शिवक्षेत्रं समाश्रितः ।
म्रियते यदि सोऽप्येवं मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ५१॥

English
Even if he is afflicted by ailments and dies after resorting to Śaivite holy centres he shall be liberated. (51.)

---

Sanskrit
यथा हि मरणं श्रेष्ठमुशन्त्यनशनादिभिः ।
शास्त्रविश्रम्भधीरेण मनसा क्रियते यतः ॥ ५२॥

English
Since voluntary death by means of fasts etc. is sought with a mind full of confidence and devotion, they say that this death is commendable. (52.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनिन्दारतं हत्वा पीडितः स्वयमेव वा ।
यस्त्यजेद्दुस्त्यजान्प्राणान्न स भूयः प्रजायते ॥ ५३॥

English
After killing a person engaged in disparaging Śiva or being afflicted himself, if a devotee eschews his life, not ordinarily possible to forsake, he is not reborn. (53.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवनिन्दारतं हन्तुमशक्तो यः स्वयं मृतः ।
सद्य एव प्रमुच्येत त्रिःसप्तकुलसंयुतः ॥ ५४॥

English
He who dies after fighting being incapable of killing a disparager of Śiva shall be liberated with the members of his family for twentyone generations. (54.)

---

Sanskrit
शिवार्थे यस्त्यजेत्प्राणान् शिवभक्तार्थमेव वा ।
न तेन सदृशः कश्चिन्मुक्तिमार्गस्थितो नरः ॥ ५५॥

English
No man treading the path of salvation is equal to one who eschews his life for Śiva or for a devotee of Śiva. (55.)

Sanskrit
तस्माच्छीघ्रतरा मुक्तिस्तस्य संसारमण्डलात् ।
एतेष्वन्यतमोपायं कथमप्यवलम्ब्य वा ॥ ५६॥

षडध्वशुद्धिं विधिवत्प्राप्तो वा म्रियते यदि ।
पशूनामिव तस्येह न कुर्यादौर्ध्वदैहिकम् ॥ ५७॥

English
Hence his liberation from the worldly sphere becomes speedier. If a Śivayogin dies after resorting to one of the means cited before or after attaining the purity of the six pathways, obsequies shall not be performed as they are done for the layman. (56-57.)

Sanskrit
नैवाशौचं प्रपद्येत तत्पुत्रादिर्विशेषतः ।
शिवचारार्थमथवा शिवविद्यार्थमेव वा ।
खनेद्वा भुवि तद्देहं दहेद्वा शुचिनाग्निना ॥ ५८॥

क्षिपेद्वाप्सु शिवास्वेव त्यजेद्वा काष्ठलोष्टवत् ।
अथैनमपि चोद्दिश्य कर्म चेत्कर्तुमीप्सितम् ॥ ५९॥

कल्याणमेव कुर्वीत शक्त्या भक्तांश्च तर्पयेत् ।
धनं तस्य भजेच्छैवः शैवी चेत्तस्य सन्ततिः ।
नास्ति चेत्तच्छिवे दद्यान्नदद्यात्पशुसन्ततिः ॥ ६०॥

English
His descendants shall not observe post-mortem pollution. His body shall be buried under ground or burnt in fire, or cast off in Śaivite holy waters or left abandoned like a log of wood or a clod of clay. Or if at all some post-mortem holy rite has to be performed let it be some auspicious rite. The descendant shall propitiate devotees. Only a devotee of Śiva shall inherit his wealth. If his children are not initiated in Śaivite cult the wealth shall be handed over to Śiva. No child shall take it. (58-60.)
